AL-QADHDHAFI DISCUSSES COMMUNISM, RELIGION FES meA 1 

Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1915 GMT 14 May 76 IN 

(Text] British television channel 2 has shown an interview with Revolution Command 
Council Chairman Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi; British journalist (Richard 
Carshug) interviewed the brother colonel. The brother colonel answered several 
questions in this interview dealing with national, religious, political, and social 
issues, 

The brother colonel affirmed that religion and nationalism are the prime motivating 
factors in history. The economic factor and other factors are of Secondary importance, 
He pointed out that wars and disputes have broken out over national and religious issues. 

The brother colonel affirmed his rejection of communism if it means a State monopoly 
of property. He said: We reject monopolies, whether by the state of by capitalism. 
If, philosophically, communism means atheism, then we reject this atheism because we 
believe in God. 

The brother colonel also affirmed his rejection of capitalism because it means the 
monopoly of property by capitalists, including companies. He said: We reject this 
because the socialism we believe in raises the Slogan of partners and not the hired. 
In political practice, we find that those who own capital dominate the policies of 
Society. This is contradictory to democracy, which means that power snould be for all 
or the people. 

Asked a question about Islan and its universality, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that 
Islam is an international religion and that the Koran addresses people everywhere and 
is not just for the Arabs alone. He said that Islam calls for belief in the one God. 

The brother colonel said that he Stands on the side of the faithful in the international 
struggle, whether they are Moslems or Christians. He said that his attack on the policies 
of the Western states is due to their imperialist policies, and is not based on their 
Christian religion. He pointed out that Islam is close to Christianity because both 
believe in the one God.

The brother colonel affirmed to the British journalist the Libyan Arab Republic's 
rejection of imperialism regardless of its source. 

Thre British journalist asked the brother colonel about his concept of nationalism. In 
this connection, the brother colonel pointed out that the nation is [words indistinct] 
of civilization forming a common culture and giving birth to a joint future and fate 
and supreme interests for an entire people. 

When the British journalist asked the brother colonel about Jewish nationalism, he said: 
We believe, in accordance with the Third World theory, that history is moved by national 
and religious struggle. [words indistinct] all nationalities and religions should be 
respected. However, the so-called Jewish nationalism is an aggressive movement which 
seeks to go beyond its boundaries and attack all nations and nationalities. Therefore, 

we are against it. 

In the course of his comments about so-called Jewish nationalism, Colonel Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that the entity based on repression and aggression should be 
toppled and that the people who were expelled from Palestine must return to their home- 

land --Palestine. 

In reply to a question about the future of the Jews who are living now in Palestine, 
the colonel explained that the Jews who came from other countries must return to those 
countries, and those who are from Palestine must stay on in Palestine. 

In reply to another question on Arab unity, the brother colonel said: There are 
obstacles in the way of unity. If it were not for these obstacles, we would achieve 
unity much faster. He affirmed that unity is as necessary for the Arabs as it is for 

any other nation. The colonel pointed out the reasons for the failure of the efforts 

at unity which the LAR has made with certain Arab countries. He said he believed that 

the failure to achieve unity was due to regional vestiges which have accumulated 
throughout history and left separatist tendencies in the minds of traditional politicians. 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi stated that President as-Sadat let Egypt down by signing the Sinai 
agreement despite the fact that he did not believe in this agreement. 

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi spoke about the LAR's support for the world liberation movement, 
He said: Our stand is clear and we support the struggle for liberation. He added: 
Our support for the Irish people stems from our belief that they are struggling for 
independence from Britain. He added that if Ireland were independent, British soldiers 
would not be present in Northern Ireland to colonize part of it. He said that the 
conflict in Ireland is an embodiment of the religious and national disputes which are 

the prime motivating factors in history. 

Speaking about his impressions of the British, Al-Qadhdhafi expressed his admiration for 
the British people who live in the countryside. He said that these British people are 
good and [words indistinct] and that amity and respect prevail among the members of 

the family. In the cities, degeneration and disintegration prevail among the people. 

The British journalist said at the end of the interview that he had noticed that the 
masses were enthusiastic about Brother Colonel Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi. He asked: Isn't 
there a danger that you will become a leader in this country who is adored as a hero? 
The brother colonel replied by saying: Our patriotic democratic experiment will save 

us from this danger.
