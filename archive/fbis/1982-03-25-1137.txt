LIBYA

ENVOY'S CLAIM OF LIBYAN PRESENCE IN GHANA DENIED 


LD242330 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 24 Mar 82 


[Text] Lagos, 24 Mar (JANA) -- Alhaji Youssif Patty, Ghanaian high commissioner in 
Lagos during the era of deposed President Limann, alleged there were Libyan military 
personnel at Kotoka international airport, the Accra radio building and Burma Camp. 


Those empty allegations clearly show that the former Ghanaian official is one of the 
agent elements exposed by the Ghanaian people and deprived of their position and their 
privileges. It is also indicative of his treachery and his hostility toward the Libyan 
Arab people and the Ghanaian people, who are united in the struggle against imperialism 
and reaction. 


The allegations ofthe escaped Ghanaian high commissioner are fabrications aimed at 


serving sinister objectives to destroy the relations of struggle between the jamahiriyah 
and Ghana. 


AL-'UBAYDI RECEIVES CYPRIOT FOREIGN MINISTER 


LD242032 Trivoli JANA in English 1633 GMT 24 Mar 82 


{(Text] Tripoli, 24 Mar (JANA) -- Mr ‘Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, the secretary of the 
people's committee of the foreign liaison affairs, this morning met with Mr Nikolaos 
Rolandhis, the Cypriot foreign minister. 


The meeting discussed enhancing and promoting cooperation between Cyprus and the 
jamahiriyah and issues of common interests. The Cypriot official arrived in the 
jamahiriyah yesterday. 


Nicosia Report 
NC241905 Nicosia Domestic Service in Greek 1730 GMT 24 Mar 82 


[Text] Foreign Minister Rolandhis, currently in Tripoli, Libya, met today with the 
secretary of the general people's committee for foreign liason, ‘Abd al-‘Ati al-'Ubaydi. 
They discussed the Cyprus problem and bilateral relations, with special emphasis on 
trade exchanges. 


In connection with the Cyprus problem, Al-'Ubaydi reaffirmed his country's support for 
a solution that will safeguard the sovereignty, independence, territorial integrity, 
unity and nonalignment of the Republic of Cyprus. On trade exchanges, it was agreed in 
principle that a Cypriot trade deiegation will visit Libya during the first 2 weeks of 
April to definitely resolve all existing issues. 


During their meeting, Rolandhis and Al-'Ubaydi also discussed the Middle East, African 
questions, the situation in the eastern Mediterranean and other international issues 
within the framework of the cooperation between the two countries in the nonaligned 
movement. 


Al-'Ubaydi accepted an invitation to visit Cyprus. The date of the visit will be fixed 
later through diplomatic channels.
