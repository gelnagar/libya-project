Summary (AI): A 1982 CIA memo records a meeting of the MacFarlane-chaired group on Libya, where officials discussed policy options against Qadhafi, including a possible U.S. oil boycott and severing economic ties. The NEA Bureau favored slowing the oil boycott idea because it was linked to a Libyan withdrawal from Chad.
• Omnibus paper on political, military and economic options vis-a-vis Libya to be circulated to principals by about 12 November 1982.
• A SIG meeting was planned for the week of 16 November 1982 to consider the policy paper, before presentation to the NSC.
• Options raised: a U.S. oil boycott against Libya and/or complete severance of U.S.-Libya economic relations.
• U.S. objectives: exploit Qadhafi's vulnerabilities, isolate him, and improve the ability of threatened states to defend themselves.
• 1982-11-06: MacFarlane-chaired group on Libya meets (1715-1745 hours) to review draft political, military, economic and informational policy options vis-a-vis Libya
