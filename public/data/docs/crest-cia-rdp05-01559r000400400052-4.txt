Algerian-Maltese Parties Issue Joint Communique 
LD281302 Algiers Domestic Service in French 
2200 GMT 27 Jul 85 

[Text] Algerian-Maltese relations: the National Liberation 
Front and the Malta Labor Party intend to reinforce relations. 
This was expressed in a joint communique that took stock of 
relations between the two delegations led on the Algerian side by 
Mr Abada Mohamed, member of the Permanent Secretariat of 
the Central Committee, and for the Malta Labor Party by its 
president, Dr (Alfred Sant). This meeting was the occasion for an 
extensive review of important international issues as well as those 
related to the current situation in the Mediterranean. Both 
parties strongly condemned the redeployment of foreign forces 
in this area and also agreed to continue with their efforts to create 
a climate of real peace. With regard to this aim the Malta Labor 
Party and the National Liberation Front hail the coming meeting 
in Tunis of the conference of progressive Mediterranean forces 
and will apply themselves to working for its success. On the 
subject of the Palestinian issue both parties reiterate their unre- 
served support for the struggle of the Palestinian people under 
the leadership of their sole legitimate representative, the PLO, 
for the recovery of their occupied territories. 

Finally, both delegations repeated their wholehearted support for 
the progressive national Lebanese forces, who are fighting for the 
preservation of the integrity of the Lebanon and respect for its 
sovereignty. The Malta Labor Party and the National Liberation 
Front also renew their solidarity with the struggle of the Cypriot 
people for the recovery of their independence. 

Finally, with regard to the situation in northwest Africa, and in 
particular the Saharan issue, the two delegations reiterate their 
indestructible support for the struggle of the Saharan people for 
its right to self-determination.
