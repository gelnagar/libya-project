LIBYA

OFFICIAL COMMENTS ON MILITARIZING OIL EMPLOYEES 


LD191536 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 19 Jan 82 


[Text] The commander of the militarization of towns, Maj al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi, 
ended his meeting with the secretaries of the people's committees for oil, which began 
on Saturday, 16 January, at the command headquarters of the militarization of towns in 
Tripoli. 


Concluding this meeting, Brother Maj al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi stressed the concern for 
the success of work in the oil sector to realize the victory of the revolution for all 
Libyans in this sphere and in an appropriate manner for carrying out arms training. He 
called on the participants to practice the revolution in a real and practical manner by 
being concerned, truthful and faithful, as the oil battle is the point of [word indis- 
tinct] on which we depend for strategic success in all other sectors. He said it is 
inevitable that we should militarize all oil employees in order to secure freedom, 
dignity, popular democracy and the revolution and that our concern for the establishment 
of the armed people and for weapons to be carried by all the people arises not from our 
fear of the people but from our fear for the people, particularly in view of the U.S. 
imperialist challenges and their efforts to dominate the progressive revolutionary 
forces. He reaffirmed that the question of defending honor, dignity and pride cannot 
be bought for money and that the people's authority arising from our revolutionary 
convictions make it inevitable for us to stress, continuously and strongly, these 
convictions and to incite the people. 


In conclusion, he stressed the need for coordination in weapon training to be carried 
out in a manner that achieves success in production and readiness to defend our 


revolutionary gains made thanks to the revolution. 


U.S. FAILS TO SUBSTANTIATE HIT SQUAD CLAIMS 


LD191946 Tripoli JANA in English 1812 GMT 19 Jan 82 


[Text] London, 19 Jan (JANA) -- Informed American sources said that the joint 
committee of the F.B.I. and C.I.A. formed 3 months ago following a personal order from 
Reagan has failed in its mission to substantiate Reagan's allegations that Libya has 
sent an assassination squad to the U.S.A. to kill top U.S. officials. 


These sources confirmed to the London-based MIDDLE EAST magazine that despite strict 
instruction from Reagan to forbid the circulation of any information about this joint 
committee, the scandal of U.S. allegations regarding the Libyan hit squad will be 
historically remembered as he failed a test balloon that no one believed. 


The paper affirmed that (?this) committee has not reached any conclusion or material 
proofs to satisfy international and American public opinion and substantiate Reagan's 
allegations. Thus, the committee decided to end its mission and informed the government 
about its decision to stop.

Joint Communique Issued 
LD141522 Tripoli Domestic Servicein Arabic 1330 GMT 14 Jan 82 


[Text] A joint communique was released today in Tripoli and Moroni on the visit to the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah by the president of the Federal Islamic Republic 
of the Comoros, Ahmed Abdallah. The communique refers to the meeting between the brother 
leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and President Ahmed Abdallah, during which they exchanged 
viewpoints and discussed the future of relations between the two countries. There were 

also talks during the visit on bilateral relations and the current international situa- 
tion. The two sides expressed the desire to promote and consolidate bilateral relations 

in the interest of both countries and to consolidate the position of Muslims in the world. 
They stressed determination to spread Islam and the Arabic language. They also stressed 
the importance of coordination, cooperation and solidarity among the Muslim states. 


They noted with much anxiety the complex situation in the Arab region due to the savage 
imperialist-Zionist attack on the Arab nation. In this respect, they stressed that peace 
in the region can be achieved only through realizing the legitimate and established rights 
of the Palestinian people, including their right to return to their homeland and to set 

up their independent state on their territory. The two sides also stressed that the PLO 
is the legitimate representative of the Palestinian people. They strongly condemned the 
expansionist aims of the Zionist enemy, particularly the decision by the Israel Knesset 

to declare Jerusalem the eternal capital of Israel and also its recent decision to annex 
the Golan Heights. 


The two sides stressed their adherence to OAU aims and principles which form a sound 
foundation for tackling developments on the African Continent. They again expressed 
support for the OAU resolutions. 


Regarding the question of the Comoros island of Mayotte, the two sides expressed support 
for the resolutions of the seventh committee of the OAU and those of the United Nations 
and the nonalignment conference and the Islamic Congress Organization, stipulating that 
a speedy solution should be found to ensure the return of Mayotte to Comoros.

Regarding the Chad issue, the two sides hoped the OAU will continue its efforts to 
establish peace and stability in Chad. They expressed concern over the deterioration of 
the situation in Chad. They also paid tribute to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah's role which put an end to the civil war and contributed to the establishment 
of security and peace in this African country. 


The two sides expressed adherence to the principles of the right of self-determination in 
accordance with the OAU resolutions. The two sides saluted the heroic struggle of the 
people of Namibia under the leadership of SWAPO for independence. The condemnei attempts 
by imperialism to create disagreement on issues relating to southern Africa. They 
expressed the hope that any solution to the Namibia issue will be on the basis of Security 
Council Resolution 435. They also condemned the policy of racial suppression by the 

South African regime and its frequent military aggressions on Angola and Mozambique and 
other independent countries in the region. 


When reviewing the international situation, they affirmed their full support for the 
positive and important role played by the Nonaligned Movement in the struggle for 
preserving world peace and security and also their support for the struggle of the peoples 
for liberation and progress. They regard the Nonaligned Movement as an important factor 
in the stability of the international situation. The two sides noted the complicated 
international situation due to the escalation of imperialist activity which aims at 
aggravating tension and at putting an end to peaceful coexistence and international 
detente. They also supported the efforts being made by the peaceloving forces in the 
Arabian Gulf and the Indian Ocean and Europe, seeking to end tension and to stop the arms 
race and also to preserve peaceful coexistence and international detente. In this 
respect, they renew support for the efforts to make the Indian Ocean and the Mediterranean 
region areas of security and peace. 


The Comoros side expressed deep satisfaction for the achievements of the Great 1 September 
Revolution. At the end of the visit, President Ahmed Abdallah extended an invitation to 
his brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to visit the Islamic Federal Republic of the 
Comoros. The invitation was accepted, its date to be fixed later.
