LIBYA

POSSIBLE FRENCH ROLE IN MAURITANIAN AFFAIR NOTED 


LD181755 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1300 GMT 18 Mar 81 


[Text] Paris, 18 Mar (JANA)--The JANA Paris correspondent says that political observers 
in France are wondering about the dimensions of the role which France played in the 
abortive coup in Mauritania. The correspondent adds that these observers recall the 
statements made by the king of Morocco during his visit to France, in particular those 
relating to taking measures against Mauritania because of its stance in support of the 
struggle of the Saharan people. These observers believe that France had a hand in this 
attempt, especially because those who staged the coup came from the territory of Senegal, 
which is pro-French. 


Nouakchott, 18 Mar (JANA)--A few hours before the abortive coup which was engineered and 
financed by the Moroccan regime against Mauritania, the Mauritanian authorities dis- 
covered a French spy plane; its crew was captured. Responsible sources in Nouakchott 
said that following interrogation of the French spy plane's crew it became clear that 

the plane was given the task of supporting those involved in the coup, who were hirelings 
of the Moroccan regime. 


V. 19 Mar 81 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


AL-QADHDHAFI TO MAKE OFFICIAL VISIT TO USSR 


LD182350 Tripoli JANA in English 1909 GMT 18 Mar 81 
{"Libyan-Soviet Press Statement"--JANA headline! 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Mar (JANA)--The following statement was issued today in Tripoli and 
Moscow: 


"At the invitation of the Central Committee of the Soviet Communist Party, the Presidium 
of the Supreme Soviet and the Soviet Government, the leader will pay an official cordial 
visit to the Soviet Union. This visit comes because of the desire of both sides to con- 
solidate and develop the friendly relations between the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah and the U.S.S.R.". 


It is worth noting that the fourth meeting of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front's 
leaders, which was held in Tripoli from 26 to 29 Jamadi al-Oula 1389 (12-15 April 1980), 
had decided that Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great al-Fatah Revolution, would 
contact the Soviet Union on the front's behalf in order to increase the military and 
political backing for the front, redress the military and political balance of forces 

in the region, and strengthen and deepen relations between the Soviet Union and the front.
