Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a Tripoli radio broadcast reporting Qadhdhafi's cable to King Hassan II of Morocco on the Western Sahara dispute. It covers Libyan arms support for the Polisario front and Libya's opposition to forced annexation. Nothing in it concerns places or events in Tripoli itself.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya had opened an office for the Western Sahara front in Tripoli in 1973 and supplied it with arms.
• The cable says Libya would not oppose Morocco if the Sahara people had not been deprived of their choice, and calls the annexation forced.
• Qadhdhafi says Sahrawi refugees were crowding Tindouf near the Algerian border.
• 1976-02-26: Qadhdhafi sent a cable to King Hassan II of Morocco on the Western Sahara dispute (broadcast by Tripoli Arna on 27 Feb 1976)
