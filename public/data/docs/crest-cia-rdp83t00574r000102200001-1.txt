Summary (AI): A 1982 NPIC imagery note reports the first identification of a Soviet-style BIG CAP B jammer system in Libya, at an electronics countermeasures site on the northwest edge of Tripoli next to Umm Aitiqah airfield. It also says related KING PIN and TUB BRICK jammers moved to the site from a temporary location 1.8 nm to the west.
• The BIG CAP B is a two-vehicle system: a DOG CART trailer (KING PIN) and a BIG CAP B truck based on a Ural-375 with an environmental cover and two generator mufflers.
• Coordinates given: 32-54-00N 013-19-05E.
• Related KING PIN and TUB BRICK jammers reportedly relocated to the site from a temporary site 1.8 nm to the west.
• The document is a sanitized NPIC attachment dated 22 September 1982.
• 1982-09-22: BIG CAP B jammer system newly identified at the site on imagery; first identification of this system in Libya and outside the Soviet Union and Warsaw Pact.
