LIBYA

JALLUD' RECEIVES LJUBICIC; VIEWS EXCHANGED ON RELATIONS 
LD302101Y Belgrade TANJUG in English 1921 GMT 30 Mar 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, March 30 (TANJUG)--Secretary of the Secretariat of the General People's 
Congress of the Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 'Abd as-Salam Jallud this evening in Tripoli 
received Yugoslav Federal Secretary for National Defense, General of the Army Nikola 
Ljubicic. Ina brief, friendly talk, views were exchanged on the current international 
situation and Yugoslav-Libyan relations. In the course of General Ljubicic's stay in 
the Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, successful and open talks were held on the further pro- 
motion of miiitary cooperation between the two countries, it has been reported. 


LIBYA, YUGOSLAVIA SIGN MILITARY COOPERATION PACT 
LD310440Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 30 Mar 78 LD 


[Text] A military cooperation agreement was signed on 30 March by the Jamahiriyah and 
Yugoslavia. Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, the commander in chief of the Libyan armed 
forces, on the evening of the same day attended a dinner given by the Yugoslav ambassador 
in honor of Nikola Ljubicic, the federal secretary for national defense of Yugoslavia, 
and his accompanying delegation now on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


Later in the evening the Yugoslav guest visited the ancient town of Libdah and the 
agricultural project at Tajura’. 


V. 31 Mar 78 NORTH AFRICA 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES CABLE FROM OAU CHAIRMAN 
LD301920Y Tripoli JANA in English 1544 GMT 30 Mar 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, March 30 JANA--On the oceasion of the reconciliation achieved between the 
Chad Government and FROLINAT, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi received the following cable 
from the president of Gabon, who is the current chairman of the OAU: 

"I was very pleased to hear of the outcome of the agreement reached between the Chad Military 
Council and the United Chad Liberation Front in Benghazi." "I have no doubt that this 
agreement was reached thanks to your efforts and endeavors which you have always exerted 

in order to bring peace to Africa in general and to find a solution to the Chad problem, 
through negotiations, in particular, and today we can see that the reconciliation is 
possible, for which Africa will always be grateful to you. Please accept my best regards 

and respect." 


Similarly, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi received another cable of congratulations from 
the president of the Front for the Liberation of Moro on the 8th anniversary of the 
evacuation of the British troops from the Jamahiriyah. 


AT-TURAYKI RECEIVES MONGOLIAN, MOROCCAN OFFICIALS 
LD301342yY Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 30 Mar 78 LD 


[Text] Foreign Secretary Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki received at noon today the 
deputy foreign minister of People's Mongolia, who is now visiting the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. JANA has learned that the deputy foreign minister of Mongolia 
nanded Dr at-Turayki a written message addressed to brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi from 
the president of the People's Republic of Mongolia. 


Bilateral relations and the strengthening of cooperation between the Jamahiriyah and the 
People's Republic of Mongolia were discussed at this meeting, which was also attended 

by the acting under secretary of the Foreign Secretariat, by the assistant under secretary 
for technical affairs and by the director of the Asian Department at the Foreign Secretariat. 


The foreign secretary also received at noon today Driss El Fallah, Moroccan ambassador 
to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. JANA has learned that the Moroccan 
ambassador handed At-Turayki a written message addressed to him from M'hamed Boucevtta, 
Moroccan foreign minister.
