MINUTES SIGNED ON LIBYAN=DPRK JOINT COMMITTEE MEETING 
LD160904Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0730 GMT 16 Aug 78 LD 


[Text] Pyongyang--The day before yesterday the Libyan and Korean sides signed the minutes 
of agreement on the first meeting of the joint committee of the Jamahiriyah and the DPRK, 
This took place on the occasion of the visit to the DPRK by Staff Major Jallud between 

11 and 14 Ausust. The minutes dealt with several spheres of economic, technical and 
cultural cooperation between the two countries.
