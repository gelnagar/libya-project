JANA: POLICE REPORTEDLY OCCUPY TUNIS UNIVERSITY 
LD211626 Tripoli JANA in English 1503 GMT 21 Feb 80 LD 


(Text ] Tunis, February 21, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The oppressive police of the 
Tunisian puppet regime cordoned off the University of Tunis and prevented students from 
entering. 


A Tunisian student said that the police have occupied the university. This led the 
students to call for maintaining the strike and [words indistinct] popular uprising in 
Tunisia. 


The Tunisian regime tried to bring friction between the students and their lecturers 
when Hedi Nouira received representatives of the university staff who were forced to 
see him in an attempt to calm the students! rage. 


The regime's mass media falsely reported a statement on behalf of the lecturers indi- 
cating that they strongly denounce the students uprising.

1D211954 Tripoli JANA in English 1700 GMT 21 Feb 80 LD 


(Text ] Banghazi, February 21, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Basic People's Congress of 
Banghazi central held an emergency session last night at the people's theatre, and 
issued the following resolutions: 


Within the context of the unionist Libyan Arab People's commitments towards their 
brethren in the Arab nation in general, and more particularly in Tunisia, which is 
currently the scene of a popular revolution by freemen amongst this Arab people against 
the decaying regime, the Basic People’s Congress of Banghazi central held an extraordi- 
nary session on the evening of Wednesday, 20 February 1980, to discuss the false accusa- 
tions made by the reactionaries of the puppet regime ruling the Tunisian protectorate 
against the Jamahiriyah and the leader of its revolution and to consider the open French 
invasion of Tunisia to protect the falling regime. The congress has passed the follow- 
ing resolutions:

1. To fully condemn the ailing Tunisian regime and its desperate attempts to 
suppress the people's revolution which sparked off in Qafsah. 


2. To condemn the open French invasion of Tunisia and warn for [as received] an 
immediate withdrawal, stressing that the uprising is genuine and that there is 
no justification for the presence of French, American and Moroccan forces in 
Tunisia. 


3- To confirm our full support to the revolutionary forces in Tunisia financially 
and militarily until the freedom of the brotherly Tunisian people is achieved. 


4, To confine our. warning to France, for the time being at least, to a demand 

for the withdrawal of her troops from Tunisian territories and an end to her siding 
with one party against the other in the conflict which is an internal affair 

that does not affect France, stressing that her interests in the area will be 
threatened if she went as far as declaring a war against her to defend the 

security of the Jamahiriyah. 


5. To open our borders before the Tunisian workers. 


6. To emphasize that the popular uprising in Qafsah and other uprisings and revolu- 
tions in other parts of the world is a success for the third universal theory laid 
down by the grand Al-Fatah revolution for humanity as a final emancipation from 

all dictatorial ruling tools and unjust laws in the socioeconomic sectors. This 
theory never meant a direct intervention by the Jamahiriyah as alleged by the 
trumpets of reaction and colonialism. 


7e To confirm the statement made by Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 
grand Al-Fatah revolution, to the French news agency, A.F.P., which revealed the 
facts of events in the protectorate of Tunisia. 


8. To call for a merger between the Jamahiriyah and Algeria as the nucleus of the 
Islamic Arab unity. 


9. To place the Libyan Arab mass media under the disposal of the Tunisians in the 
Jamahiriyah and refrain from answering recriminations of the Tunisian regime as we 
have a revolutionary culture, but to continue the deterrent scientific reply. 


10. To open our borders before the Tunisian popular resistance against the puppet regime. 
11. No reconciliation with the Tunisian puppet regime. 


12. To send popular delegations to the Arab and African countries as well as France 
and non-aligned countries to explain the seriousness of the French intervention in 
Tunisia and the grave effects which might arise from such an act and expose the bad 
intentions of the U.S.-French coordination with certain puppet regimes in the region, 
such a plan which is designed to divide the region, control it and restore the 19th 
century situation. 


13. To be committed to immediately implement the resolutions of the communique of the 
third meeting of the revolutionary committees. 


14. To renew allegiance to the leader of the grand Al-Fatah revolution, Colonel 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi as an answer to the fabrications of the Tunisian regime's 
propaganda, and pledge our support to him as leader and struggler for man's freedom.
