Summary (AI): A June 1985 FBIS London correction to a Tripoli TV broadcast of Qadhdhafi's Evacuation Day address, in which he urged Arab brothers to stop accusing each other of plots, bombings and newspaper attacks. It is a wire correction with no specific places or casualties tied to Tripoli.
• The item is a correction (take 15) to a Tripoli Television Arabic broadcast dated 1985-06-12.
• The corrected passage concerns Arab states accusing each other of plotting, time bombs, booby-trapped cars and newspaper attacks.
• The message was filed by FBIS London on 12 June 1985 (120748Z JUN 85).
• 1985-06-12: Tripoli TV broadcast of Qadhdhafi's Evacuation Day address; FBIS London issued a correction to the Arabic text
