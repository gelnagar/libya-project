Summary (AI): A 1957 CIA background brief says Libya, under King Idriss, was politically unstable, aligned with the West and dependent on foreign aid, especially from the US and UK. It describes Egyptian subversion and a weak economy. Nothing specific about Tripoli sites.
• Libya's annual British subsidy was to be cut in 1958 from $11.2 million to $3.5 million.
• US aid to Libya totalled about $87 million a year, including $4 million for military base rights.
• Britain announced it would withdraw its military forces from Libya.
• More than 90% of Libya is desert, and less than half of one percent is under cultivation.
• More than 460 Egyptian school teachers in Libya were available to spread Egyptian propaganda.
