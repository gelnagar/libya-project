CLANDESTINE GROUP APPEALS TO FAMILIES OF WAR-DEAD 
London IRAN PRESS SERVICE in English No 74, 3 Jun 82 p 7 


[Text] A group calling itself Irandin has called on the members of the families 
of the war dead to take Khomeyni to task for having sent their sons, brothers, 
or fathers to death so indiscriminately. 


In a pamphlet distributed in Tehran the group has said: "We will not let 
Khomeyni capitalise on the blood of our brothers, fathers and husbands. We 
will not let this bloodsucking old dog build his imaginary empire on the blood 
of our dearest ones. We will take this criminal to task. He will tell us why 
he sent children to explode mines, why he so treacherously destroyed Iran's 
mighty army to leave the country defenceless and then send so many people to 
serve as cannon fodder; and why he encouraged Saddam to attack Iran after he 
had mutilated the army." 


The group said the mullahs must not be allowed to take the credit for sacri- 
fices which the Iranian people made to save their motherland. It said Khomeyni 
must not spill Iranian blood to bolster the killer regimes of Hafez Assad in 
Syria and Qadhdhafi in Libya, or to engage in inter-Arab politics. 


"Iranians have no mandate to liberate Palestine,'' it said. "We also have no 

desire to be crusaders in an Islamic war because as Shias most of us Iranians 
are not even accepted by the bulk of the Muslims. We are Iranians first and 

foremost and our primary task is to build up our country fromthe ruins of an 

Islamic theocracy." 


Informed sources said the new group must comprise factions of the Pan-Iranists 
who have defected from Daryoush Forouhar's party as well as elements of the 
royalists. The style of Persian used in the pamphlet resembles that of a 
famous Iranian thinker, Ahmad Kasravi, who founded the Pakdinan Society in the 
1940's. He was virulently anti-Shia and an advocate of basic reforms in Islam. 
He was murdered by gunmen of the Fedayeen-e-Eslam in 1946. At that time 
Khomeyni was a small time preacher sympathising with the Fedayeen-e-Eslam.

MINISTER ANALYZES FOURTH PLAN 
Main Characteristics 
Nouakchott CHAAB in French 29 Apr 82 p 3 


[Excerpt] The minister gave CHAAB an exclusive presentation of the plan and 
the main programs which will be pursued during the plan period in the various 
sectors of national life. 


Today we are publishing the first part of this presentation. It is, among 
other things, a question in this first part of the basic characteristics of the 
fourth plan which will henceforth take into account the regrettable failure of 
those which preceded it and the prospects for the development of the Mauritian 
economy. 


Working on the basis of a rather realistic diagnosis of the Mauritian economy, 
taking into account the encouraging results of the recovery plan established by 
the national leadership as of 1979, but also the path remaining to be covered 

in order to reestablish the major balances in the Mauritanian economy, so harm- 
ful to it were the joint effects in the 1979 decade of such natural catastrophes 
as the drought, the war and certain economic policy errors committed, the 
fourth plan was designed as a plan for recovery, consolidation and restruc- 
turing. 


I. The Basic Characteristics of the Fourth Plan 





In the course of the period between 1959 and 1980, the base of the national 
economy has gradually shifted from the primary sector (mines excluded) to the 
tertiary sector.
