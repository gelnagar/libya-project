Summary (AI): A 1982 AP story in the Chicago Tribune says U.S. officials remained vigilant against a reported Libyan assassination team allegedly sent to target President Reagan, though no team had been found and publicity had faded. It has no specific places in or near Tripoli.
• A Libyan assassination team was reported in late 1981 to be stalking President Reagan; no attacks or arrests followed.
• Reagan's motorcade reportedly used two limousines and a counterassault team in a van.
• Secret Service protection for James A. Baker, Edwin Meese and Michael K. Deaver began in early December 1981.
• FBI said there was much less information coming in than a month earlier and no Libyan hit team had been established in the U.S.
