ECEVIT ENDS VISIT; COOPERATION AGREEMENT SIGNED 
LD301732 Tripoli JANA in @nglish 1442 GMT 30 Jan 79 LD 


(Text] Tripoli, January 30, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--An agreement was signed for the 
promotion of cooperation between the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and 
Turkey in the economic, scientific and technical fields and in joint investments. 


A protocol has been also signed to follow up the implementation of previous cooperation 
agreements within the works of the Libyan Arab-Turkish joint committee on bilateral coopera- 
tion. The agreema@mt and the protocol were signed at the end of the visit paid by Turkish 
Prime Minister Bulent Ecevit to the Jamahiriyah. 


Ecevit, Jallud Discuss Visit 
LD302248 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 30 Jan 79 LD 


{Excerpts ] Turkish Prime Minister Bulent Ecevit has emphasized that his visit to the 
Jamahiriyah and the talks he conducted during the visit have yielded very good results 
that will deepen the historic bonds of fraternity between the Libyan Arab and Turkish 
peoples. In a statement to the press prior his departure from Tripoli, the Turkish 

prime minister said his meeting with the great thinker, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 

is a historic meeting. He also said that the Arab Libyan-Turkish cooperation agreements 
Signed during this visit, which cover all economic and social spheres, have established a 
wide and firm foundation in the structure of the historic, fraternal relations between 
the Arab Libyan and Turkish peoples. 


Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud stressed in turn that the Jamahiriyah does not view 
the relations between both peoples as being ordinary, but as eternal relations that ought 
to be studied in depth. It is in this spirit that we were able to achieve progressive 
results in the interests of both peoples and to lay down a framework for cooperation that 
befits these eternal relations. 


Staff Major Jallud said that the Jamahiriyah is pleased with the results reached. We 
are confident that everyone will implement everything that has been agreed upon and that 
we have no choice but to cooperate.

This is because cooperation is the yardstick of our freedom and independence, and the 
duty of Islamic fraternity makes it incumbent upon us to stand by our brothers in Turkey 
to resist any outside pressures. 


He said: We consider the need for the Turkish Muslim people to overcome their problems, 
particularly economic ones, as a duty dictated by the Islamic fraternity. He also noted 
that the need for Turkey to emerge from these problems strong is in our interest and that 
of Turkey and the Islamic world. He said: We stand by Turkey to prevent any economic 
pressures on it because we have found in the Turkish People's Party and Mr Ecevit a spirit 
of independence and concern for Islam that is in conformity with the philosophy of our 
Islamic faith and the Third World theory we advocate. 


He added in his statement: We, from the position of Islamic fraternity, are committed and 
shall work to help our brothers in Turkey solve their problems because we consider this 
issue our issue. 


Staff Major Jallud said the Turkish people are strong in their material potential, faith 
and history; it is our responsibility to make the Turkish people capable of using their 
creative potential in building themselves. 


Speaking about relations between the Jamahiriyah and Turkey, Staff Major Jallud emphasized 
that we are reassured more about the development of these relations, pointing out that 
this is the responsibility of both peoples. Moreover, this visit was an opportunity to 
assert Islamic fraternity and Arab-Turkish fraternity and to underline the need for joint 
action to strengthen the Islamic and developing worlds and project them as a political 
and military force. 


Staff Major Jallud pointed out that this visit made it possible to lay down everything 
that is of interest to both peoples in agreements and documents. Staff Major Jallud said 
the Arab Libyan and the Turkish peoples shoulder the responsibility of implementing these 
agreements. This visit was also an opportunity to hold deep intellectual and political 
meetings between Bulent Ecevit and Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, which helped to create 
a good climate for the development of relations. 


Staff Major Jallud expressed his pleasure at Bulent Ecevit's visit to the Jamahiriyah and 
praised his effective role in the building of firm relations between both peoples. 


Joint Statement Issued 
LD302347 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 30 Jan 79 ID 
[Text] The following joint statement was issued today in Tripoli and Ankara: 


In answer to the invitation extended by brother Staf Maj 'Abad as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, 
member of the General Secretariat of the General People's Congress, Bulent Ecevit, prime 
minister of the Republic of Turkey, heading a senior delegation, paid an official visit 
to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 26-29 January. 


Bulent Ecevit received a warm reception, which expressed the fraternal relations linking 
the Libyan Arab and Turkish peoples. Col Mi'mmar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 

1 September Revolution, reeeived Bulent Ecevit. They exchanged views on bilateral re- 
lations, the means of strengthening them, the situation in the region and current inter- 
national issues.

During his visit, Bulent Ecevit held official talks with Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad 
Jallud. The delegations of both countries took part in these talks, which dealt with bi- 
lateral relations and international issues of interest to the Arab and Islamic worlds. 


In the sphere of bilateral relations both sides affirmed the fraternal and historic links 
joining their peoples, and expressed their satisfaction with the development of these re- 
lations. They agreed to adopt the necessary measures to realize further progress in the 
mutually beneficial development of these relations. 


Both sides reviewed the achievements of bilateral cooperation in the economic and trade 
fields. They expressed their satisfaction at the positive steps taken in the past. They 
also agreed to adopt all measures to insure an increased volume of trade during 1979 
and the coming years, following the positive steps of 1978. 


Both sides stressed the importance of bilateral industrial cooperation. They took measures 
for quick development in light of the initiatives taken in this field. Both sides agreed 
to promote cooperation in planning, preparation of projects, engineering, services and 
construction contracts. They also expressed the particular importance both countries 

give to cooperation with the Third World. 


At the end of the talks, both sides signed a cooperation agreement, which is aimed at 
creating the widest possible scope for bilateral relations in all fields and at preparing 
programs for joint projects in agriculture, industry, technical and scientific cooperation, 
communications, manpower and infrastructure projects. 


The Libyan side expressed its readiness to contribute to the efforts of the Turkish Re- 
public to overcome its current economic problems. 


Both sides stressed the extreme importance of the Islamic fraternity and Arab-Turkish 
fraternity. They expressed their belief that Islamic solidarity and overall cooperation 
are clear indicators of the realization of Islamic unity in the world as a contribution 
to achieving the peace and prosperity for humanity. In the Islamic sphere, both sides 
affirmed their support of and adherence to the resolutions of the Islamic Congress on the 
cause of the Muslims in the Philippines, on Muslim minorities and on all other Islamic 
issues. 


Both sides discussed with great interest the explosive situation in the Middle East re- 
sulting from the challenge facing the Arab world. They expressed their support for the 
Palestinian people in their effort to obtain their full rights. They also stressed the 
need for withdrawal from all occupied Arab territories, a move that will help to reach 

an overall, lasting and just solution in the region. Both sides also stressed that.any 
sOlution not taking into account all the legitimate rights of the Palestinians, including 
their right to establish their national state, will not lead to a lasting peace in the 
region. They once again stressed that the PLO is the only legitimate representative of 
the Palestinians. In this connection, Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud expressed his 
satisfaction with the progress in the PLO's arragements to open an office in Turkey. 


Both sides declared their strong opposition to any measure likely to alter the Arab- 
Islamic character of the holy city of Jerusalem,

The Turkish prime minister explained the recent developments connected with the Cyprus 
problem, Both sides stressed the need to expedite negotiations between the Turkish and 
Greek communities in Cyprus as equals on the basis of the Denktash-Makaries agreement 
of 12 February 1977. They also agreed on the need to stop the attempts to impose an 
economic ban and sanctions against the Turkish community in connection with contacts and 
travel, so there might be a favorable atmosphere in which to advance the effort to bring 
about a nonalined, sovereign federal republic for both communities and regions. 


Both sides discussed the situation in Africa ana expressed their support for the 

African liberation movements in their struggle against racism, They strongly condemned 
foreign interference in the affairs of the African Continent, and are confident in the 
ability of the African peoples and the OAU to find the correct solutions to the problems. 


Both sides expressed their total support of the United Nations and the principles of its 
charter, reaffirming the importance of whe reformation of the representation system at the 
United Nations and declaring their firm desire to work for the development of their 
relations with the developing countries, which are facing the same economic and social 
problems, They also pointed to the need to strengthen their solidarity with these 
countries, 


Both sides expressed the need to find a new world economic system to bring about equality 
(?in) economic relations between developing and economically advanced countries, They 
declared their support for all the efforts to close the gap between the two groups. In 
this connection, they expressed their conviction that the North-South dialog must 
continue and receive a new impetus within the United Nations, noting that world economic 
cooperation founded on equality and mutual interest would contribute to the development 
and social progress of humanity. Both sides stressed the legitimate right of all 

peoples to tighten their control over their natural resources, 


During the talks, which took place in an atmosphere of understanding and in the spirit 
of Islamic fraternity, the Turkish prime minister expressed his profound admiration of 
the great achievements of the Great 1 September Revolution aimed at providing prosperity 
and the establishment of social justice in the Jamahiriyah,. 


At the end of the visit, the Turkish prime minister expressed his profound appreciation 
for the extensive welcome and kind hospitality that he and the accompanying delegation 
received during their stay in the Jamahiriyah. 


Bulent Ecevit extended an official invitation to his brother Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad 
Jallud, member of the General Secretariat of the General People's Congress, to pay a 

visit to the Republic of Turkey. The invitation was accepted. The date will be fixed 
later through diplomatic channels.
