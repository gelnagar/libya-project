(Text ] Tripoli, 11 October, JANA--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi called on America to 
review its aggressive policy towards the Arabs and a large number of the peoples of this 
world, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "We, the Arabs in this dialogue, or on any other occasion 
have no duty to solve the puzzle of America's hostility toward the Arabs, because this 
is the responsibility of the Americans who have to solve their own puzzle, acquit 
themselves of the accusation and to work for the awakening of the American conscience 
So that it appreciates the aggressiveness of their policies towards a large number of 
the peoples of this world. 


In his talk at today's morning session of the Arab-American popular dialog, Colonel 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi announced that he is the first who is enthusiastic about presenting 
the question of international terrorism to the United Nations. He said that the world 
has to face this phenomenon and to find the solution to it. In this connection he 
emphasized the necessity of defining the real meaning of terrorism. He emphasized 

that terrorism in its real meaning is the presence of the military bases and war vessels 
in the territorial waters of the states, the manufacturing of atomic bombs and the 
Starvation of peoples and fighting them economically. He called on the states of the 
world to define the question of terrorism, beginning with the hijacking of aeroplanes 
and ending with the manufacturing of atomic bombs, blacklisting all of them because 

that is terrorism, 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi emphasized that it was the Zionist policy, which is 

hostile to America itself and to the Arabs in the first instance, that has poisoned 

the American culture and influenced the American policies so that it concerted them 

into hostile policies towards the Arab nation and to many peoples of this world as 

well. He said that the Zionist policies, which has nested within the American society, 
has employed the American policies in the service of racism, imperialism and Zionism, 

and that the American interests are the first victim of this. America found itself in 
the end being hostile to peoples between whom and America there are no problems, foremost 
of which was the great Arab nation. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi indicated that America is being hostile to the Arabs without there 
being an Arab-American problem and without there being an Arab territory occupied by 
America, or an American territory being occupied by the Arabs. Moreover, there are no 
common boundaries between the Arabs and the Americans that might result in a boundary 
problem between them, 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi wondered about the reason why the Americans are always 
Screaming about the aeroplane hijacking at the time they forget that their fleets, bases, 
atomic bombs, intercontinental ballistic missiles and starvation police are the source 

of serious terrorism to the peoples. 


He added that the Americans are forgetful of the fact that the Palestinians are displaced 
from their land and that America assists the occupier in holding to its occupation of 
this land.

Arab-American Dialog Concludes 


LD122110Y Tripoli JANA in English 1920 GMT 12 Oct 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 12 October JANA--The first Arab-American peoples! dialogue which was held 
from 9-13 October, 1978 in Tripoli, the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, con- 
cluded its final meeting today by issuing the following statement: 


The dialogue was held with the participation of a group of Americans from many of the U.S. 
states amd a group of Arabs from the majority of Arab countries. The dialogue was frank, 
constructive and fully comprehensive. It included discussion of issues relating to 
political, economic, cultural and informational relations and the discussion considered 
among other points: 


1. Relations between peoples and states should be established on the basis of equality, 
mutual respect for their rights and interests. 


2. Non-utilization of force to solve international conflicts, and the necessity of finding 
solutions to such conflicts on the basis of the recognition of the peoples! rights to 
sovereignty, independence and self-determination. 


3, Economic relations should be promoted and strengthened on the basis of equality and 
respect for common interests.

American people, in order to contribute to understanding and close cooperation between
