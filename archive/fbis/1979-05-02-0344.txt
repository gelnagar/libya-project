STATEMENT DENOUNCING NEW CHADIAN GOVERNMENT ISSUED 
LDO12012 Tripoli JANA in English 1943 GMT 1 May 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 1 May, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Foreign Secretariat of the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah issued a statement on the formation of a 
new government in Chad, reading as follows: "The news agency reported that a new 
government was formed in N'Djamena and the Foreign Secretariat in the Jamahiriyah 
affirms that the formation of such a government is a violation of the Kano agreement, 
In view of its commitment and responsibility towards the crisis which the people of 
Chad are facing, the Jamahiriyah announces the following: 


1--What was alleged by the group imposed on the people of Chad, the formation of a 

new government, is insignificant and of no importance, for this government is illegal 
and the one who formed it has no legality, and it contradicts the Kano agreement which 
stipulates the formation of a transitional national unity government from all parties 
which signed the agreement and the parties which agreed on the items of the agreement, 
so the government only represents itself, and no government could be formed in Chad, 
except in accordance with the Kano agreement. 


2--The Jahairiyah cannot recognize the fake government, affirming its confidence that 
the countries involved will not be deceived by this manoeuvre which aims at monopolizing 
power by a certain group which will also aggravate the situation in the country and 
cause a bloodshed and fighting among brothers, 


3--The Jamahiriyah iscontinuously incontact with all the neighbouring countries to 
Chad in order to hold a conference with all Chadian parties attending it, and then to 
form a government according to the Kano agreement, in order to enable the Chadian 
people with all its sects to possess the initiative and not to give chance to the 
adventurers to dominate power and fiddle with the people's destiny.

ALLEGATIONS OF CHADIAN, EGYPTIAN INCIDENTS DENIED 
LD011722 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1600 GMT 1 May 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 1 May--An Arab Libyan source has denied allegations by AFP that an 

Arab Libyan Dakota DC-3 aircraft carrying weapons for rebels in Chad crashed in central 
Chad on 25 April. The source said that this report was groundless, and that it was a 
fabrication by hostile Western propaganda organs designed to divide the Arab Libyan people 
in the Jamahiriyah and the fraternal Chadian people. The same source pointed out that 
since April, borders have been closed between the Jamahiriyah and Chad, and there have 
been no air or ground communications between the two countries. Consequently, no Libyan 
plane has crossed Chadian airspace. 


The Arab Libyan source also denied news agency reports about “an incident" between an 

Egyptian submarine and a Libyan Navy unit ff As-Sallum. The source said that the in- 
formation media of the defeated president has been spreading such rumors for some time 
as a prelude for aggression against the Arab Libyan people, so the defeated president 

could cover up the explosive situation in Egypt following his disgraceful capitulation 
and signing of a treaty with the enemy. 


The source stressed that these kinds of rumors are simply aimed at creating a climate 
of tension along the border between Egypt and Libya. 


AS-SADAT ENVOY'S ‘ABORTIVE! MISSION IN AFRICA REPORTED 


LD011538 Tripoli JANA in English 1430 GMT 1 May 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, May 1 JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The envoy of the defeated Egyptian president, 
Mamduh Salim, is currently on a tour to some of the African countries in an abortive 
attempt to persuade the countries that had severed relations with (Israel) to resume them. 


The defeated Egyptian president is attempting to prevent African countries from severing 
relations with his regime, in solidarity with the Arab countries that had already done so. 
This hereby tightens the international grip of boycott against his isolated regime, es- 
pecially as several African countries have refused to receive the Egyptian emissary in 
solidarity with the Arab countries. Amongst those countries are the Cameroon, Upper 
Volta, Mali and the Niger. 


STATEMENT RELEASED ON SIERRA LEONE MINISTER'S VISIT 

LD012032 Tripoli JANA in English 1714 GMT 1 May 79 LD 

[Text] Tripoli, 1 May, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The following press statement was released 
today in Tripoli and Freetown on the visit of Mr. Abdulai Conteh, minister of foreign 


affairs of Sierra Leone, to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah from 1-4 Jumadi 
al Thani, corresponding to April 28th-May 1, 1979. 


V. 2 May 79 I 6 NORTH AFRICA 


The foreign minister of Sierra Leone, Dr. Abdulai Conteh, paid anofficial visit to the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in the period from 1-4 Jumadi al Thani, 1388, 
corresponding to April 28th-May 1, 1979. 


During this visit he conducted talks with Dr. 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, the foreign 
secretary, and both reviewed the bilateral relations, the situation in the African Con- 
tinent, the Arab causes and the current international issues. 


With regard to bilateral relations, the two sides expressed satisfaction to the extent 
the relations have reached. They expressed their determination consolidate these brotherly 
relations in all fields to realize the goals the two brotherly people aspire for. 


In the African field the two sides observed that the African Continent has been exposed 
to a number of problems and secessions which pave the way to international imperialism 
and monopolies to intervene and to inflict harm on the relations between its peoples 

and government in a bid to re-control their resources and capabilities. The two sides 
called upon all African states to adhere to their unity and to solve their problems in 
an African spirit within the framework of the Organization of African Unity--OAU. They 
affirmed that the African wisdom is capable of finding suitable solutions to all the 
problems which hinder the establishment of strong brotherly relations between the states 
of the continent. 


The two sides affirmed their belief that armed struggle against imperialism and racism 

is the only way for the liberation of the African people. In this respect the two sides 
denounce the close cooperation between the racist regimes in southern Africa and occupied 
Palestine and consider that this cooperation is directed against the human values and the 
people's rights to selfe-determination, freedom and justice. 


With regard to the question of the Western Sahara, the two sides affirm their full ad- 
herence to the principle of self-determination for the brotherly people of the Sahara 
in accordance with the resolutions of the UN and OAU. 


The two sides denounced the imperialist attempts which work for the creation of military 
pacts and alliances between them and some African states, which threaten the seqmrity and 
peace and lead to the partition of these states. The two sides urged all the African 
states to refrain from joining such military pacts in accordance with the principles of 
the nonalined movement. 


And in this respect, the two sides express their deep concern towards the dangerous 
developments between Tanzania and Uganda which have resulted in the overthrow of the 
Ugandan regime, 


The two sides also condemn any foreign intervention in the internal affairs of any inde- 
pendent state. The two sides called for an Arab-African unity and consolidation affirming 
the importance of the joint role carried out by the Arab-African community towards the 
progress of civilization and world economy to be free and independent from domination 

and imperialist monopolies. The joint cooperation between the two communities will bene- 
fit the peoples of the Third World, and through the historical and geographical relations 
between the Arab and African communities they will be able to play a practical and effective 
role in the world arena in different fields. 


After studying the Palestinian's cause carefully, the two sides expressed their deep 
regret towards the role played by Arab reactionaries cooperating with Zionism and im- 
perialism aiming at liquidating this cause.

So, the two sides regard any agreement which excludes the Palestinian people under the 
leadership of PLO as null and void, The two sides affirm their will and determination 

to support the struggle of the Palestinian people for the return of their land and usurped 
rights. 


The two sides affirm their unconditional support to all UN, Organization of African 
Unity and the Arab League resolutions and recommendations on the Palestinian cause, On 
the international level, the two sides affirm their commitment towards the charter and 
principles of the United Nations, and observe the need for amending the UN Charter 

to guarantee justice and equality amongst the member countries to abolish the veto and 
to implement a new world economic system to reserve the rights of the countries of the 
Third World, of sovereignty on their natural resources which guarantee world equity and 
justice, 


During the visit, Dr *Ali tAbd at-Turayki showed the foreign minister of Sierra Leone 
the democracy practised in the Jamahiriyah in which the people possess wealth, power and 
weapons and what the revolution has achieved within and outside the country. 


The foreign minister of Sierra Leone expressed his strong admiration for the great 
achievements taking place in the Jamahiriyah since the Great First of September 
Revolution; he also expressed his great thanks and appreciation for the brotherly 
reception he and the accompanying delegation were given in the Jamahiriyah. And 
finally, he invited Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, the foreign secretary, to visit 
Sierra Leone, and the latter accepted the invitation, the date of which will be assigned 
later.
