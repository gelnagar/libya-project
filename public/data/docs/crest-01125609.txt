TRAVEL OF LIBYAN EXTERNAL SECURITY ORGANIZATION OFFICERS THROUGH MALTA IN DECEMBER 1988
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/01125609

Summary (AI): A December 1988 CIA cable reports that two Libyan External Security Organization officers, Abdelbaset Ali al-Megrahi and Mas'ud Abu Aqilah, travelled through Malta on 7–8 and 17 December 1988, and that the source judged them to be communications technical experts possibly carrying intelligence-gathering equipment.
• Both ESO officers were described by the source as technical communications experts.
• Source comment: it is likely Megrahi was carrying technical intelligence-gathering equipment.
• Megrahi departed Malta airport without Maltese customs processing.
• Both officers stayed overnight at the Holiday Inn in Malta.
• 1988-12-07: ESO officer Abdelbaset Ali (al-Megrahi) arrived in Malta on a Libyan Arab Airlines flight from Tripoli under an alias, met by former LAA station manager; ESO officer Mas'ud Abu Aqilah arrived on an Air Malta flight the same day.
• 1988-12-08: Both officers departed Malta together on a Swissair flight to Zurich.
• 1988-12-17: Both ESO officers returned to Malta and departed for Tripoli the same evening.
