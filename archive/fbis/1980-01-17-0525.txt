NC170806 (Clandestine) Voice of Palestine in Arabic to the Arab World 0530 GMT

[Text] Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi has issued an order to the Libyan Air Force commander

JANA EDITOR DENIES EXISTENCE OF LIBYAN, FATAH RIFT 
ID162234 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 16 Jan 80 ID 


[Text] A JANA political editor has denied a London radio report that the meeting of the 
foreign ministers of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front was held to eliminate the 
disagreement between the Jamahiriyah and the Fatah movement, Concerning this, the poli- 
tical editor pointed to the statement made by the leader of the revolution at the press 
conference held in Benghazi, attended by a number of reporters of leading Arab and world 
newspapers, There he emphasized that there is no disagreement between the Jamahiriyah and 
Fatah, He said that the disagreement comes from one side, Fatah, and it is up to Fatah 
to remove this disagreement, 


The political editor stressed that thismeeting, for which preparations and cont ts 
were made, was held to discuss the Arab situation, especially since the betrayal of 
the Arab nation and its central issue--Palestine--by the defeated Egyptian president,
