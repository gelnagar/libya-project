That  does  not  mean  that  Egypt  prevents  our  workers  from  traveling  to  work

there  are  about  3  million  Egyptian  workers  in  Arab  and  foreign  countries  help¬ 
ing  to  bring  about  development  through  their  expertise  and  strength.  Among

countries,  and  about  50,000  workers  in  European  countries,  Syria,  Lebanon  and 
other  Arab  states.
