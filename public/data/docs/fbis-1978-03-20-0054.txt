AL-QADHDHAFI GIVES SPEECH TO LEBANESE PEOPLE, PLO 
LD190939¥ Tripoli JANA in English 0830 3M? 19 Mar 78 LD 


(Text ] Tripoli, March 18, JANA--Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great First of 
September Revolution, addressed the following speech [as received] to the Lebanese Arab 
people and to the Palestine Liberation Organization (Pi). 


Cclonel al-Qadhdhafi said: At this time which our Lebanese and Palestinian Arab masses 
face the barbaric invasion launched by Zionist forces against Lebanon, the Lebanese Arab 
people stand steadfast against this attack. The Libyan Arab masses, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi 
said, record this heroic struggle of the Lebanese people in the great history of the 

Arab nation. 


Whatever the Lebanese people lost, but the history record forms a strong revolutionary 
stance, there is no Arab land suffered for the Arab cause more than Lebanon, who gave 

everything he owned to this cause by the national Lebanese leadership, which was born 

as a national movement of the origin people the movement that was born in the national 
street. [paragraph as received] 


It is a great honor for the Lebanese fighter to die beside his brother the Palestinian, 
defending ou: Arab nation's honor and future. 


Lebanon, who approves these great sacrifices and these heavy weights, certainly links 
his future with the Palestine people future and expresses the heart of the Arab nation, 
because history of struggle doesn't account any price. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added that Lebanon gave his Arab nation more than any other Arab 
country and more than any other Arab people. Lebanon expresses the consciousness and 
the pride of the Arab nation. Lebanon today represents the Arab honor and its pride. 


Lebanon now must have the congratulations of the Libyan Arab people, for Lebanon stands 
steady with great patience. The history of the great Arab nation will include in its 
pages the heroic steadfastness of the Lebanese and Palestinian fighters. 


It is our great duty in the Libyan Jamahiriyah and in the Arab homeland that the whole 
Arab people must stand side by side with their brothers in Lebanon who assert victory 
for the Arab nation at the end, al-Qadhdhafi concluded.
