Summary (AI): A February 1983 NPIC briefing note reports that the second of four Soviet-supplied Nanuchka-II missile patrol boats for Libya had arrived at the naval base within the port facilities at Benghazi (spelled "Banghaz" in the document). Two Natya minesweepers were also reported en route. This concerns Libyan military procurement far from Tripoli.
• Nanuchka-II is the export version of the Soviet Nanuchka guided missile patrol combatant.
• Each Nanuchka-II is armed with four SS-N-2C antiship cruise missiles, two SA-N-4 surface-to-air missile launchers, and one twin-mounted 57mm cannon.
• Four Nanuchka-II boats are to be delivered to Libya in total.
• The two Natya minesweepers are the third and fourth Natya minesweepers for Libya.
• 1981-10: First Nanuchka-II patrol boat delivered to Libya from the USSR
• 1981-03: First two Libyan Natya minesweepers delivered and berthed at Tubruq
• 1983-02-04: Second Nanuchka-II patrol boat arrives at the port

1. (SWN) SIGNIFICANCE: THE SECOND NANUCHKA-II GUIDED MISSILE 

PATROL COMBATANT (PGG) TO BE DELIVERED TO LIBYA HAS ARRIVED AT 
THIS PORT 

(SWN) REMARKS: THE SECOND OF FOUR NANUCHKA-II PGG THAT WILL 
EVENTUALLY BE DELIVERED TO LIBYA FROM THE USSR IS BERTHED AT 
THE NAVAL BASE WITHIN THE PORT FACILITIES. THE FIRST PGG WAS 
DELIVERED IN OCTOBER 1981. THE NANUCHKA-II PGG IS THE EXPORT 
VERSION OF THE SOVIET NANUCHKA PGG AND IS ARMED WITH FOUR 
SS-N-2C ANTISHIP CRUISE MISSILES, TWO SA-N-4 SURFACE-TO-AIR. 
MISSILE LAUNCHERS, AND ONE TWIN-MOUNTED 57MM CANNON.
