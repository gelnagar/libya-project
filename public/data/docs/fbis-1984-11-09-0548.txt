Tripoli on U.S. Threat to Nicaragua 
LD091016 Tripoli Voice af Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 0230 GMT 9 Nov 84 


[Unattributed commentary: “The Peace- and Freedom-Loving 
Peoples Sand Side by Side With the Nicaraguan People” | 


[Text] The Reagan administration has begun a huge propaganda 
campaign against Nicaragua, alleging that it has been receiving 
enormous military aid from the USSR including long-range 
supersonic military aircraft. The Reagan administration claims 
that the alleged arms deal will disturb the balance in the region 
and will encourage Nicaragua to make war upon its neighbor 
countries. 


There is no doubt that through this propaganda campaign the 
Reagan administration is preparing to launch a major aggression 
against Nicaragua in a mean attempt to topple the Sandinist 
revolutionary regime there. 


While launching this unparalleled campaign against Nicaragua 
and accusing it of receiving sophisticated weapons, the Reagan 
administration totally ignores the sophisticated military weapons 
and hardware and protection from popular revolutions it gives to 
agent governments subservient to it, such as the agent regime of 
Napoleon Duarte in El Salvador to help him repress the rev- 
olution in that country. The United States is trying by this means 
to impose its hegemony over the peoples of that region. The 
Reagan administration also ignores the aid it offers to the Zionist 
gangs, including military aid used to repress the Palestinian Arab 
people and the Arab people and the Arab strugglers in southern 
Lebanon. The huge aid given by the United States to fascist

tion champions terrorism in the world to dominate small nations. 


The US. allegations that sophisticated weapons given to Nica-

imperialist propaganda trumpets, are no more than an attempt
