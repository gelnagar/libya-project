LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SPEAKS ON CAVALRY, ‘EVACUATION DAY' 


LD011738 Tripoli JANA in English 0910 GMT 1 Apr 81 


[Text] Banghazi, 1 Apr (JANA)--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi announced the formation of 
the cavalry division inside the Urban Militarization Command. In a speech he delivered 
at the grand equestrian show held in Banghazi last night within the celebrations of the 
llth anniversary of evacuation of British bases from the Libyan soil, he said that the 
people's cavalry force is a step forward towards the establishment of the armed people, 
a new important force of skillful efficiency in war and a force of a command annexed to 
the urban militarization body. 


"Horsemen all over the Jamahiriyah will be formed into regular battalions within the 
urban militarization concept," he explained. "The people's cavalry force is not less 
effective than the militarization of educational institutions which brought nearer the 
establishment of the armed people. Horsemanship, through the revolutionary action, has 
moved from the equestrian nature to the actual action of the battle." 


"Those equestrian performers who are displaying and showing their art on the national days 
in the Jamahiriyah, will shortly be organized into battle units to be trained on various 
weapons according to age, ability and area." He said, addressing the cavalry force, "You 
will be trained on different kinds of weapons according to your age, capability, and 
region for which certain type of weapons are needed. But this should not change the 
fantastic performance of the equestrian force. The equestrian nature of the cavalry force 
is also important, for it preserves authenticity and creates enthusiasm and high spirits 
among the horse riders. 


The horse rider cannot be a coward, for horse riding creates courage, and a horse rider 
does not retreat or betray, because of his high spirit and the nature of his profession. 
He always goes forward whe: he is riding his horse despite all the difficulties he may 
face. Therefore this work should be preserved. 


The people's cavalry force, from now on, should be transformed into an organized fighting 
force, armed with all kinds of weapons. This means more arms are to be possessed by the 
masses who were disarmed and attacked from inside and outside. People's freedom and 
sovereignty will always be threatened, as long as arms are not in the hands of the people. 
The formation of this cavalry force, means that an important weapon is in the hands of 

the masses. More masses, in addition to those militarily organized in the educational 
institutions, factories and other production sites, will join this force when it possesses 
the arms," he added. 


"This will increase the fighting capability of the people and reinforce their freedom, 
after they saw themselves gradually possessing the arms following their possession of 
authority through the people's congresses and committees."

"When the great revolution of the workers abolished exploitation and gave wealth to the 
people, the freedom of the Libyans was consolidated. This freedom which turned into a 
serious daily exercise of the Libyans, free from hypocricy, deception and demagogy." 


"The world will feel and see that the historic achievements on the Libyan soil are not a 
force and propaganda, neither is it a demagogy just to create enthusiasm among the ~2ople. 
But by transferring arms, wealth and authority to the masses, the whole issue becomes a 
serious exercise and the people then will feel they are free and masters of themselves." 


The leader said: "When authority is transferred into the hands of the people, they then 
become more aware of their responsibility. When the people are prepared to face all the 
consequences of carrying arms and holding powers, they become more responsible towards 
production and development, since the wealth is in their hands." 


"As we celebrate evacuation day and victory over foreign colonial forces, we are also 
celebrating a new achievement and that is the establishment of a new armed people's 
cavalry force, which makes us very proud because it promises bravery, heroism and idealism 
and brings closer the establishment of the armed people." 


"This festival which bring here horsemen from all over the Jamahiriyah is unique. This 
:an happen perhaps once in the history of this country. It is taking place in Banghazi, 
the city of the first declaration and the first historic headquarters of the free union- 
ist officers. The implications of this festival are great since it coincides with evacua- 
tion day, through which we want our horsemen to feel their new strength." 


"We would like to organize the horsemen within our new force, so that they feel they are 
a genuine people's force taking part in establishing an armed people." 


"The arming of horsemen will show that we are serious in handing weapons over to the 
people. It is a result of this revolutionary process, all the arms, authority and wealth 
will be transferred into the hands of the people. Consequently the state in its tradi- 
tional form, the regular army, the fascist army, all the repressive instruments and the 
exploitative class will all disappear." 


"These will be replaced by the people who then resolve everything in their own interest 
after being deprived of freedom and dignity for so long. After being oppressed in its own 
land by internal and external enemies. This people can tear asunder the ranks of treachery, 
greed and looting." 


"Those bourgeois greedy traitors were trying to split the ranks of our heroic people when 
they were confronting the Italians, allowed the invaders to exploit our people and control 
this land. They then arranged deals to establish American and British bases, which were 
meant to stay indefinitely." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi explained that without the will of this people, that produced the 
free unionist officers movement which led the revolution and destroyed the monarchy, 
there would still be bases in Libya which would for ever ;emain an occupied country. The 
people achieved victory because the revolution was led by their sons. They were the sons 
of the suppressed, overworked masses that suffered tremendously from slavery inside and 
outside their country," he said. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that the Libyan people are beginning to look after their own 
interests to revenge their martyrs and raise their voices in challenge to the world and 
its super powers. "This nation has a right to be on this earth and under the sun. So 
that its voice can be heard, this nation must be armed and in control of its own resources."

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi declared that arms will be given to the new people's cavalry and 
that a large section of the people will be armed very soon, so restoring to them the 
right to bear arms. 


He concluded by calling members of the cavalry to train hard on their arms so as to 
defend the revolution and bring nearer the day of the ‘armed people.’ 


Spectators warmly welcomed the leader when he arrived at the equestrian show. The masses 
raised banners supporting the revolution and their comnitment to the goals of the Great 
Fateh Revolution. Guests at the show included Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud and the 
visiting Chadian people's delegation. 


The governors of Faya, province in Chad, said in a speech that the destiny of the two 
brotherly peoples were one. He also praised the Humanitarian help given by Libya to the 
Chad people in bringing victory for their revolution and security and stability in Chad 
territories. The governor confirmed that the Libyans were the only people who extended 
continuous, unlimited aid. "Libyans fought with their brotherly Chad people to achieve 
their goals against the French colonialism and its agents." 


"The Chad people won their freedom and victory over their enemies because of the help of 
Libyan Arab people and their great revolution which put fear into the enemy and contin- 
uously stands with military oppressed peoples and for their freedom throughout the world," 
he said. 


The support of the principles of the Great Fateh Revolution that Africa is for the 
Africans and no alliances for Africans except with each other, "we are one land with one 
destiny and one path." [sentence as received] 


The president of the Chad Women's Union expressed the appreciation and gratitude of the 
Chad people for the revolution and its leader towards the Chad people and their victory 
for freedom. She said, "Unity between the Libyan and Chadian people would bring full 


stability, security and freedom for our Chad people." She said the Chad Women's Union 
strongly condemned the defamation campaigns of reactionaries and imperialists against the 
Great Fateh Revolution, for its continuous support for the Chad revolution. 


AL-HUMAYDI ADDRESSES GRADUATING MILITIAMEN 


LD021046 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 2 Mar 81 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 2 Apr (JANA)--Maj al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi, commander of the people's 
militia, last night attended a ceremony observed by the popular masses in Al-Jamil Dis- 
trict to mark the llth anniversary of the eviction of British bases from the homeland. 
During the ceremony a new batch of male and female recruits in the people's militia units 
was graduated. Brother Maj al-Khuwayldi al-Hjmaydi delivered a speech at the ceremony. 


Major al-Khuwayldi said today we are celebrating, in the midst of this great occasion, by 
realizing another great achievement: The graduation of new units of the people's militia 
in order to prove to the world that our daily celebrations are always new achievements in 
the defense of freedom, the Jamahiriyah and the authority of the people. He added that 
today we celebrate the building of new stores for weapons and ammunition to ease the task 
of depending the homeland and its dignity and actively participate in cementing true free- 
dom in order to achieve equality between male and female Libyans in authority, resources 
and weapons. 


The new graduating units have been named "25 January" to glorify that great day when our 
masses displayed their support for the revolution and the eviction.
