LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES ARAB PEOPLES" CONGRESS 


LD202120 Tripoli JANA in English 1644 GMT 20 Jan 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 Jan (JANA)--Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the great Al-Fatah revolu- 
tion has affirmed that "Libyan soil is the base of mass popular action". In a speech to 
the final session of the Arab People's Congress last night, the leader informed the dele- 
gations present that they could "rely on Libya because it is suitable for mass action. 

For Libya has been transformed into a jamahiriyah where conflict has been resolved in the 
interest of the masses and where people's power, and none other, has been established. 
Therefore it is more suitable than other place to become a base for mass popular action". 


The leader continued "the Arab People's Congress, which groups all the political and 
vocational forces and movements, represents a genuine start of people's political Arab 
nationalist action. It can be transformed into a progressive Arab nationalist people's 
front. This front can adopt socialist unionist action and its programme is based on 
liberation, socialism and unity. Thus, this grouping becomes transformed into a revolu- 
tionary grouping in opposition to the Arab right wing, reaction, regionalism and imperialism. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, added "There is no contradiction among the forces forming the Arab 
People's Congress despite ideological differences which must wither away through demo- 
cratic mass interaction". 


The leader expressed the desire for the Arab forces to move towards the goal of liberation 
and ridding the Arab nation from regionalism, exploitation and imperialism. He added 

"the establishment of the Arab People's Congress is hopeful because it was born in con- 
ditions of setback and despair". 


Concerning unity between Libya and Syria, the leader asserted that "It is inevitably 
coming. There is unfamiliar seriousness to realize this unity, for unity is not just to 
save Libya but to save the entire Arab nation". 


The leader said "we must go into counter attack, confront the enemy, reorganize ourselves 
and realize a change in the balance of forces in order to regain Palestine, the Golan 
Heights and the occupied Arab territories."

Referring to Chad, the leader said "Libya has intervened militarily in Chad in response 
to a request from the Chad Liberation Front (Frolinat), ending the war which lasted many 
years and resolving the conflict in Chad in the interests of the friendly forces and the 
people's forces represented by Frolinat which led the armed struggle for twenty years and 
defeated the racist and reactionary puppet forces and imperialism backing them for the 
secession led by Habre had behind it As-Sadat, his stooge Numayri and the French agents 
in Africa". 


The leader declared "The Libyan and Chadian forces have captured the arms depots in Chad. 
These depots contained (Israeli), Egyptian and Sudanese weapons because America instigated 
its agents in Chad, and the war in Chad was against the Jamahiriyah". Colonel al-Qadhdhafi 
affirmed "We have crossed the border in self-defence when an act of force was used to 
impose a pro-imperialist regime hostile to the Chadian people". He added "As long as they 
resorted to force, we responded in kind to impose a friendly regime hostile to imperialism 
and representative of the Chadian people". The leader explained "the complete mass unity 
between the two countries, based on people's power as stipulated in the joint Libyan- 
Chadian statement, is not like the familiar constitutional political unity. For there is 
a very old historical interaction between the Libyan and Chadian peoples and between the 
Chadian people and the Arab nation, and the borders are open between the two countries. 


The leader of the revolution added "The Chadian people are similar to the Sudanese people 
in make up. For there is a large proportion of Arabs among the Chadian people. Eastern 
and south eastern Chad is entirely Arab and a large section of the Chadian people are Arab 
by origin and race, while the overwhelming number are Moslem, and Arab culture is the 
prevailing culture in Chad. The Chadian people have a specificity very different from all 
biack African peoples. A very large number of Libyans emigrated to Chad, settled there 
and became Chadians, similarly a very large number of Chadian people are at present in 
Libya". 


The leader said that "the main and most pressing issue is to achieve Arab unity from the 
Atlantic to the Gulf, or at least unity between several Arab countries such as Algeria, 
Libya, Egypt and Sudan, then work can be done to achieve unity with Chad". The leader also 
explained that "the Africans who objected to the unity between the Jamjahiriyah and Chad 
and did not look at it from the point of view of Arab nationalism, but rather with a racist 
view". 


He added: "What prevents the unity between two African people? And how can we make a 
distinction between Zanzibar and Tanganyika's unity, which form Tanzania and the unity 
between the Libyan and Chadian peoples? This is a racist view dictated by imperialism 
which indicates ignorance, superficiality, and total submission to imperialist will and 
points also to racism which harmed the African presidents who protested against this unity”. 
The leader also asked "why did not these people protest against what was declared in Kampala 
yesterday concerning the intention of Kenya? And why, they do not also protest against the 
proposed unity between Ghana, Mali and Guinea, while they are protesting against the unity 
between Libya and Chad?" He added that this was a racist view desired by imperialism. 


The revolution's leader went on "We fight alongside Africa because we are Africans and 
members of the organization of African unity which calls for unity in Africa. We join the 
Africans in their struggle against South Africa and we boycott it, and we combat, along 
with them, apartheid. 


I can excuse the African leaders who are protesting against the Libyan-Chadian unity be- 
cause they are not expressing their free will but they are merely saying what imperialism 
suggests to them."

Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi the leader of the revolution affirmed that "Libya is 
neutral and will remain neutral and the Americans realise this, We do not have any 
Soviet bases and we are not agents of the Soviet Union. If As-Sadat raves day and night 
that there are Soviet bases in Libya, the Americans would laugh at what he says because 
the Americans possess spy satellites and know what is going out of or into the Soviet 
Union. The Americans cannot say that there are bases in Libya or say that the Libyans 
presence in Chad is a Soviet extension. 


The Americans realize that the Libyan presence in Chad is purely Libyan and they know 
the reasons behind it. They also know that Libya is an independent and neutral state, 
neither Marxist nor communist and it is not under the Soviet Union's influence, and that 
there are no Soviet bases on Libyan soil. 


They also know that Libya is anti-American and anti-imperialist and that it endeavours 

to liberate Palestine and this is the secret of the enmity existing between Libya and America. As-Sadat 
claims this enmity arises from the fact that Libya has become an agent of the Soviet Union and that 
there are Soviet bases on Libyan soil, and also that the Libyan presence in Chad is a Soviet expansion, 
which of course is not true. The Americans know that what As-Sadat says is purely nonsensical 
because they certainly know that Libya is sincere in its historic orientations and fundamental 
choices, and it is anti-American because America is an imperialist country and because 

America supports the (Israelis) who usurped Palestine. 


They also know that Libya wants to unify the Arab nation, and this will lead to the 
emergence of a third or fourth force in the world which will affect imperialist 
interests, and put an end to (Israeli) existence, and which will have the veto right 
in the Security Council. America does not want to see the emergence of any new force, 
particularly on the Libyan model, anti-imperialist and aligned to freedom and to the 
peoples". 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said "America realizes the nature of the relations between Libya and 
the Soviet Union and knows that we are friends of the Soviet Union because we are 
convinced of its friendship and committed to it as we share common objectives. Both 

we and the Soviet Union are enemies of America, enemies of imperialism and Zionism. The 
Soviet Union stands with the Palestinian people and the Arab nation, it is also arming the 
Arab nation. While America blacklists Libya, the Soviet Union sends us long-range 
missiles and strategic planes". 


The leader of the revolution maintained: "We are continuing in our agitation to carry 
on the counter attack against the American aggression in the Arab homeland, which started 
by invading Egypt, Somalia and Oman." 


Concerning the new American administration, the leader said: "From the political point of 
view we must give a chance to the new American administration which changes hands from the 
Democrats to the Republicans, and which may result after two or three years, and not 

in the very near future, in a change of American policy, such as an American withdrawal 
from Somalia or Egypt, the scrapping of the swift intervention force or the lifting of 

the arms embargo on an Arab country." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added: "This is from a political point of view and not from a 
revolutionary one. Politics require us to give a chance to the new American administra- 
tion in order to know how dangerous it could be, but President Reagan cannot shift 
America from supporting the (Israelis) to supporting the Arabs, or shift it from the 
right to the left because America will remain the way it is for generations unless a 
miracle happens as it happened in tsarist Russia, Iran under the shah, or Libya under 
the Senoussi royal family."

He went on to say: "The political wait and see attitude does not exclude agitation to 
prepare for a counter-attack against the invasion spreading in the Arab world. The 
Arab People's Congress must adopt a programme for preparing the nation's counter attack 
against all its enemies, for we will attack Zionism, imperialism, and the reactionary 
forces throughout the Arab homeland without exception, but if the enemy withdraws from 
its bases in Somalia, for instance, we will, in this case, cancel from our programme 

an attack on Berbera base." 


The leader ended his speech at the closing session of the Arab People's Congress, by 
clarifying the kind of Arab unity for which the Arab citizen is becoming ready to fight 
and get martyred. He affirmed "It is the mass unity in which the conflict will be 
resolved in the masses’ interest and not a unity to repress the masses, not a fascist 
unity or unity consolidating feudalism and capitalism." 


The leader of the great Al-Fatah revolution put the responsibility of clarifying the 
nature of mass Arab unity and instigating the Arab masses to struggle and die for its 
realization on the Arab People's Congress. 


ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS ISSUES POLITICAL DECLARATION 


LD201118 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 GMT 20 Jan 81 


[Political declaration issued by the second ordinary session of the Arab People's 
Congress in Tripoli on 19 January 1981] 


[Text] At this important and grave stage in the history of the Arab struggle, when the 
Arab masses and the national, pan-Arab and progressive regimes and forces are fighting 
against imperialism and Zionism and reaction, and in response to the requirements of 


confrontation, the Arab People's Congress held its second session from 15-19 January 
1981. 


The session reviewed the most important political developments in the Arab and inter- 
national arenas. The session acquired special importance due to the increased steadfast- 
ness of the masses of our Arab nation and its forces and national progressive Arab regimes 
in face of the ferocious offensive by the imperialists, Zionists, and reactionaries. 
These forces seek to impose capitulationist solutions onour Arab nation and liquidate 

the Palestine question through the imposition of the Camp David agreements, complementary 
plans, and alternative options to the establishment of an independent Palestine state, 
and reimposition of American domination over the Arab homeland through the Camp David 
accords and intensification of the imperialist presence through the establishment of 
American military bases in Egypt, Somalia, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, the waterways, 
the oil communication lines in the Arab Gulf, and by bolstering the role of the Arab 
reactionary regimes in serving imperialism and Arabizing the conflict in the region. 


The Camp David accords epitomize the Zionist-imperialist-reactionary alliance and provide 
them means for defending the common interests of all members of this alliance against the 
Arab national liberation movement and its national and pan-Arab progressive regimes which 
constitute the Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation. The front epitomizes Arab 
steadfastness and the escalation of the Palestine Arab people's struggle in the occupied 
territory against the Zionist occupation authorities, thus defying the suppression, 
terror, detention, torture, oppression, assassination, and deportation of national 
leaders, as well as the UNRWA plots against members of our Palestinian people which are 
part of the imperialist scheme to liquidate the Palestine question. The resistance has 
intensified and expanded into the occupied territory against the occupation authorities. 


In collaboration with steadfast Syria and the struggling National Lebanese Movement, 
the Palestine revolution has continued to check the Israeli aggressions against 
southern Lebanon and the sectarian isolationist plan, and all attempts which strike at 
the Palestine revolution and exhaust the National Lebanese Movement.

Steadfast Syria has bravely and valiantly confronted the Israeli aggressions on southern 
Lebanon and stood fast in the face of all internal and external plots which seek to subdue 
it and weaken its resistance to the American imperialist regimes in the region. 


Relations between the various members of the Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation 
became stronger and their ability to face up to the imperialist-reactionary-Zionist 
offensive increased. 


The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah bravely faced the plots of imperialism 

and As-Sadat against its eastern borders and in Africa. It condemned the French military 
provocations and called for confrontations of the direct American imperialist offensive 
in all areas. 


Algeria aborted the attempts at internal subversion and external provocation against the 
borders of the Arab Maghreb. 


The PDRY is boldly facing the threats of imperialism and reaction and the hostile military 
moves on its borders and within its territorial waters, and the attempts by Saudi Arabia 
through its agents to obstruct the unity of the two parts of Yemen. 


The National Egyptian Movement also intensified its struggle to topple the regime of 
agent As-Sadat inside and outside Egypt. 


The struggle movements and the armed struggle in the Peninsula, the Gulf and Western 
Sahara and in all the fields of confrontation against the reactionary regimes which are 
in league with imperialism also intensified. 


The unity decision between Syria and Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah affirmed 
the importance of the unity struggle for the liberation of Palestine and realization 
of the objectives of the Arab struggle. 


In order to place the Arab masses face to face with their responsibilities, the Congress 
decided the following: 


The struggle tasks of the coming stage: 


1. To abort the Camp David accords, the autonomous rule plan, and all the capitulationist 
solutions and alternative options imposed by imperialism, Zionism and reaction, 
particularly the so-called Jordanian option which aims at detracting from the legitimacy 
of the PLO and liquidating the Palestine cause. 


2. To increase the struggle and combat capabilities of the Palestine revolution in all 
fields of confrontation with the enemy under the leadership of the PLO--the sole legi- 
timate representative of the Palestine people--and also to provide all forms of support 
for the struggle of our rising masses inside the occupied territory. 


3. To open all confrontation fronts before the Arab fedayeen action to escalate con- 
frontation with the Zionist enemy and enable the Palestine revolution to play its 
political and struggle role on the Jordanian arena. 


4. To bolster the steadfastness of Syria, the advance stronghold of confrontation against 
the Zionist enemy, and to consolidate with it against all internal and external plots 
hatched against it, and to provide it with all military and financial aid to enable it 

to achieve military balance with the Zionist enemy. 


5. To bolster the National Lebanese Movement and its cohesion with revolutionary Syria 
and the Palestine revolution in defense of Lebanon's unity and Arab character, against 
the isolationist plot and Israeli aggressions in southern Lebanon. All these 
constitute a pan~Arab responsibility.

6. To back the struggle of our Arab people in Egypt, and their national, pan-Arab and 
progressive forces to overthrow the Camp David agreements and to overthrow the agent regime 
of As-Sadat, and to restore Egypt to its natural position in the pan-Arab struggle. The 
conference stresses the need to work for strengthening the unity of the factions of the 
National Egyptian Movement opposed to the regime of As-Sadat and the imperialist-Zionist- 
reactionary plans. 


7. To mobilize the Arab masses to wage continuous struggle to liquidate the imperialist 
military bases in all parts of the Arab homeland and to strike at American imperialist 
interests, to include the use of the weapons of oil and other assets in our pan-Arab battle 
against the enemies of the Arab nation. 


8. To work to escalate the struggle of the masses for Arab unity and to speed up the 
realization of the fighting unity between Syria, the revolution [as heard] and the 
revolution of the great First September, and to realize a pan-Arab unionist tide which 
will speed up the rise of unity and will protect it against the plot of its enemies. 


9. To realize basic freedoms in all parts of the Arab homeland as a basic condition for 
the Arab mass upsurge with which the Arab masses can restore their effective role in 
realising the targets of Arab struggle. 


10. To call for direct dialogue between the Ethiopian regime and the Eritrean revolution 
to find a peaceful solution which would ensure the right of self-determination for the 
Eritrean people and preserve the interests of both people, and thwart imperialist and 
reactionary maneuvers in the region. 


11. To eall for an end to thewar raging in the Western Sahara and to grant the Saharan 
people the right to self-determination on their own national territory through direct 
dialogue, similar to that which took place with Mauritania. 


12. To intensify the struggle against the imperialist onslaught against Africa, especially 
the [word indistinct] assault, and to back the struggles of the progressive forces in it, 
and to support the Socialist Peoples’ Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in Chad, and to stand against 
the American imperialist plans in east Africa and French colonialism in west Africa. 


13. To build the progressive pan-Arab popular front which mobilizes, organizes and leads 
the struggle of the Arab masses to achieve their targets in liberating all parts of the 
occupied Arab territories, and for unity, liberation and progress. 


14. To strengthen the fighting relations between the Arab revolutionary forces and the 
forces of liberation and progress in the Islamic and the nonaligned countries. 


15. To strengthen the alliance of the Arab National Liberation Movement with the World 
Biberatiton Movement and the democratic forces in the capitalist world, and the liberation 
and progressive movements in the world, and the community of socialist countries headed 
by the Soviet Union. 


16. To strengthen the links of liberation and progressive struggle with the revolutionary 
forces which are escalating their struggle in Latin America and the Caribbean, and to 

support the revolution in El Salvador and Guatemala to enable them to abort the military 
dictatorial regime in their countries. 


At the end of its work the Arab Peoples’ Congress salutes the people of the Socialist 
Peoples' Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and its revolutionary leadership, headed by the brother 
struggling leader Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, for the facilities provided to convene the 
conference and the success of its work.

The congress also values the guiding address with which Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam 
Jallud opened the congress and the tasks for which it is responsible. 


The congress values the great effort exerted by the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab 
People's Congress, especially General-Secretary Brother ‘Umar al-Hamdi, and the 
achievements made in the past 3 years. It also values the effort exerted in preparation 
of the documents of this congress and the serious preparations which contributed to the 
success of its work. 


ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS SENDS CABLE TO AL-QADHDHAFI 


LD201854 Tripoli JANA in English 1420 GMT 20 Jan 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 Jan (JANA)--At the conclusion of the second ordinary session, the Arab 
People's Congress sent acable to Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi leader of the great 
Al-Fatah revolution. It reads as follows: 


Brother Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdafi leader of the great Al-Fatah revolution the [word 
indistinct] who attended the second ordinary session of the Arab People's Congress which 
was also attended by the various factions of the Arab liberation movement would like to 
pledge to you their allegiance to the revolutionary struggle in an effort to achieve 

the aspirations of the Arab nation in terms of unity and liberation. 


To this effect, the Congress gratefully evaluates your decisive revolutionary effort in 
confronting the vicious attack against the Arab nation. 


The Congress strongly condemns the French military provocations the purpose of which 
is to regain control over the African Continent. 


The Congress appreciates the role played by the Jamahiriyah in helping the Chadian people 
to sescure their freedom and unity. 


At the local level, the Congress appreciates the socio-economic transformation drawn by the 
great Al-Fatah revolution on the road to a final emancipation of mankind. 


On this occasion the Congress takes this opportunity to praise your efforts towards 
the achievement of pan-Arab unity and the merger with Syria. This merger is a decisive 
reply to the deteriorating situation in the Arab region. 


Finally, the Congress is greatly thankful to the Libyan people for their support for the 
Arab People's Congress and its Permanent Secretariat. The Congress appeals to you to keep 
your revolutionary work up until progressive revolutions are established all over the 

Arab homeland.
