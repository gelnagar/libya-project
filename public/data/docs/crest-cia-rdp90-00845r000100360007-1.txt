Summary (AI): A 1980 press clipping (Daily Telegraph, reprinted in CIA reading room) describes Billy Carter's paid relationship with Libya's Gaddafi government, alleged Libyan covert action and terrorism support, and Libya's ties to the Soviet Union and East Germany. It names no Tripoli sites or local killings.
• Billy Carter's first visit to Tripoli was reportedly arranged via Libyan contacts in Italy.
• Libya reportedly supplied Sam-7 missiles used to bring down two civilian planes in Rhodesia.
• Libya reportedly awarded Carlos $2,000,000 for the 1975 OPEC kidnapping in Vienna.
• Libya's arms deal with the USSR was reportedly concluded in 1976 and valued at up to $12 billion.
• 1978: Libyan embassy in Rome reportedly held Aldo Moro after an Italian police tip, per the article's account of the Moro investigation
• 1980-04: Murder in London of two of Gaddafi's exiled opponents by an invasion-style hit team (2 killed)
• 1980-01: Billy Carter received about $220,000 (about £95,000) in cash from Libyans
