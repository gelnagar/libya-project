LIBYA

TURKISH DEFENSE MINISTER AKMANDOR DEPARTS 25 JUL 

LD260552 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 25 Jul 79 LD 

[Text ] Neset Akmandor, Turkish defense minister, left Tripoli today after a visit 

of several days to the Jamahiriyah during which he had talks with Brig Abu Bakr 

Yunis Jabir, commander in Chief of the armed forces, He also met Staff Maj 'Abd 

as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and secretaries of the General People's Committee for Housing and 

Planning. He was seen off by Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, a number of army officers 

and the ambassador of Turkey to the Jamahiriyah.
