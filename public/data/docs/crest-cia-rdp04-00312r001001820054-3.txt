Summary (AI): A 1981 press article (OCR-garbled) about Americans, including a former Green Beret, who allegedly trained and supplied Libyan terrorism and military operations from 1976 through at least 1979. It describes a small American munitions factory about 20 miles south of a luxury seaside compound in Libya, and says some involved believed the U.S. government knew of the work.
• The article says that from 1976 through at least 1979 the operation reportedly provided training and supplies to Libyan government forces.
• Americans involved reportedly included a Green Beret given leave of absence by his superiors, who had been at Fort Bragg, N.C. less than 10 days before.
• The operation reportedly also involved Palestinian groups, Italy's Red Brigades, and the Irish Republican Army as terrorist-linked groups Qaddafi financed.
• According to State Department intelligence, more than a dozen, and perhaps as many as 50, Americans were connected to the Libyan project and some may still be in Libya in 1981.
