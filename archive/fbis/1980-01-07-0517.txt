LIBYA

COOPERATION, TRADE RELATIONS WITH PRC CUT 
1D061604 Tripoli JANA in English 1430 GMT 6 Jan 80 ID 


[Text] Tripoli, Jan 6, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY=--The General People's Congress today adopted 
a majority recommendation made by local people's congresses calling for cutting off coe 
operation and trade relations with China. 


The decision was taken in view of China's hostile policies toward the Arab nation, the 
Palestinian cause, the Jamahiriyah and the arms supplies provided to the defeated Egyptian 
regime after its alliance with Israel and imperialism and the redeployment of its troops 
on the borders of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah.
