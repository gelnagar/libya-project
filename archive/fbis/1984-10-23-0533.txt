LIBYA

Reportage on Visit of Lebanon’s President al-Jumayyil 


Al-Jumayyil Arrives im Tripoli 
LD221907 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1835 GMT 22 Oct 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 22 Oct (JANA) — Lebanese President Amin 
al-Jumayyil arrived in Tripoli this evening on a visit to the 


Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. Staff Major “Abd 
al-Salam Ahmad Jallud was at the head of those who welcomed 
him. Also taking part in the welcome were members of the 
General People’s Committee and the secretary and members of 
the General People’s Committee for Tripoli Municipality and the 
Lebanese ambassador to the Jamahiriyah and a number of 
officers of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces. 


President Amin al-Jumayyil made a statement to JANA in 
which he lauded the cordial reiations between the Jamahiriyah 
and Lebanon. He said: When we visit Libya, our second home- 
land, we (?strengthen) real cordial and fraternal relations 
between Lebanon and Libya 


He added that this visit affirms the links between Lebanon and 
Libya — links of real strength and sincere cooperation for the 
sake of our joint issues, particularly at this crucial stage of the 
history of our Arab struggle, which requires more joint efforts 
and work in the interests of Lebanon, Libya, and all Arab 
brothers

JANA Claims Plane Almost Hit by U.S. Rocket 
LD221945 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1915 GMT 22 Oct 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 22 Oct (JANA) — A plane belonging to Libyan 
Arab Airlines this evening was exposed to an abortive attack 
while landing at Athens International Airport, in Greece. It was 
the target of a rocket coming from the direction of a US. military 
air base. The captain of the plane said that he saw the rocket 
when it was very close to the plane. However, he managed to 
evade it and land his plane safely 


V. 23 Oct 84 


The captain of the plane gave a detailed report on the attempt to 
the airport control tower at Athens Airport. In it he explained 
the circumstances connected with this attempt from the moment 
of discovering it to the time of reporting it, noting that during the 
attempted attack, the plane was at an altitude of 250 meters and 
about 6 km to the left of runway 15. 


Kurdish Party Statement on Turkish Operations 
LD192011 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
1815 GMT 19 Oct 84 


[Text] The Kurdistan Democratic Party has urged the United 
Nations and peoples of the world to take steps to stop the military 
operations carried out by the Turkish Armed Forces, in collusion 
with Saddam Husayn’s regime, against the Kurds in northern 
Iraq. In a statement published yesterday evening in Paris, the 
party urged countries of the world and international organiza- 
tions to take steps to effect an immediate withdrawal of Turkish 
forces and stop Turkey’s interference in the Iraqi Kurds’ prob- 
lem. It is worth noting that Saddam Husayn’s regime in Baghdad 
has allowed the Turkish Army to enter Iraqi territories along the 
border between the two countries in the last 2 days to pursue 
Kurds. 


JANA: Opposition Forces Kill 8 Sudanese Soldiers 
LD211032 Tripoli JANA in English 0930 GMT 21 Oct 84 


[Text] Nairobi, Muharram 26, October 21, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The Sudanese National Liberation Army 
announced that it attacked a military garrison for the stooge 
Numayri forces in the Junea region, in southern Sudan, before 
dawn yesterday. The Sudanese National Liberation Army radio 
station revealed in a report that the attack resulted in the death 
of eight Sudanese soldiers, and the destruction of a Buffalo-type 
plane.
