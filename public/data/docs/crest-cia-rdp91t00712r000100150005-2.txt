Summary (AI): An NPIC briefing note dated 8 December 1983 reports the first identification of a probable drone support facility in Libya at Abu Kammash, on the northwest coast about 11 nm east of the Libya/Tunisia border, roughly 150 km from Tripoli. It describes a walled, double-fenced compound with two hangars, a helicopter pad and a possible ground launch position, plus an Italian-built Andromeda drone system.
• Facility is wall and double-fence secured, with two large hangar-type buildings, one helicopter landing pad and one possible ground launch position.
• A probable Agusta A-109 helicopter, likely the launch/recovery vehicle, was present.
• Collateral information said the system had been in Libya since 1979.
• The system is the Italian-built Andromeda remotely piloted vehicle, with Mirach 70 and Mirach 100 drones.
• Coordinates given: 33-04-28N 011-44-16E.
• 1983-12-08: NPIC reports first identification of a probable drone support facility in Libya.
