{Text} Tripoli 17 Ramadan-29 JAMAHIRIYA NEWS AGENCY--The "Voice of Germany" 
radio program in Cologne broadcast on Thursday evening 24 July the text of 
the interview with the head of the revolution, in which he spoke of the 
teachings and theories of the three parts of the Green Book, as a road that 
leads humanity to free itself permanently from all traditional forms o. gov- 
ernment. 


The head of the revolution stated that the direct democracy adopted by the 
Libyan people was different from the traditional forms of representation as 
manifested by the deputies' assemblies. 


He stated that direct popular democracy is made concrete by the congresses 
and the popular committees, stating also that although the congresses are 
not elected bodies like the deputies' assemblies, nevertheless they under- 
stand all the masses of the people in whose hands the government rests. 


He added that the popular congresses are the final outcome of the people's 
struggle for democracy. 


Mentioning the solution to the economic problem, such as established by the 
Third World theory, he stated that the theory is a consecration of democracy, 
which makes it a genuine democracy and a realization of justice and legality 
between all citizens, where no one is subject to another where his life or 
wages are concerned. 


The head of the revolution referred in that regard to the watchwords, 
"Partners and not Salaried Workers," "The House belongs to the Person who 
Occupies it,"The Land is the Property of no one Person," which in Libya have 
freed men from exploitation. 


The colonel asserted that the regimes that govern the world at present are 
dictatorial regimes because they subsctitute themselves for representation in 
the government by the masses, whereas in truth the people are unable to be 
represented and all representation is an illusion. He asked why a repre- 
sentation since the masses are there, and why candidates, since the masses
