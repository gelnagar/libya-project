LIBYA

STATEMENT VIEWS RELATIONS WITH FRANCE, CHAD 
LD161726 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1520 GMT 16 Jul 81 
[Statement issued by Libyan People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison in Tripoli 16 July] 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 Jul (JANA)--The People’s Bureau [for Foreign Liaison] has been acquainted 
with the statement made by the French Foreign Ministry regarding Libyan-French relations 

and the prospects of cooperation between the two countries. The bureau has noted with 
interest the positive points in that statement, particularly with regard to the French 
Government's affirmation that it will adhere to the implementation of the cooperation 
agreements between the two countries and its readiness to widen the prospects of such 

a cooperation. While welcoming the positive French attitude, the People's Bureau would 

like to reaffirm its readiness to strengthen and develop the relations between the two 
countries in various fields. 


As for the question of Libyan-Chadian relations, the bureau would like to emphasize that 
this question concerns two countries between which there are agreements which regulate 
their bilateral relations in harmony with the spirit and letter of the OAU Charter. The 
Libyan Arab role in supporting Chad's security and stability was discussed and highly 
praised by the African states at the Nairobi summit. The resolutions which were made at 
this conference on the question of Chad are the concern of the Chadian Government and the 
OAU alone, and any third party which involves itself forcibly in this question is directly 
interferring in the affairs of a sovereign and independent state. 


Consequently, the question of the presence of Libyan forces in Chad is an issue which 
concerns the Chadian Goverament alone and the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahirivah 
is quite willing to withdraw its forces from Chad, but this matter is left entirely to 
the legitimate Chadian Government.
