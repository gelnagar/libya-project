JANA CALLS EGYPTIAN REFERENDUM 'WORST FORGERY' IN HISTORY 


LD201811 Tripoli JANA in English 1740 GMT 20 Apr 79 LD 


(Text) Tripoli, April 20, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The worst forgery in history took 
place in Egypt yesterday, a forgery which will misrepresent the will of the Arab people 

in Egypt once the results are declared of the farce of the referendum on the document of 
the defeated Egyptian president's capitulation to the Zionist enemy and on the dissolution 
of the so-caiied People's Assembly. 


Observers have already predicted that the referendum results will be similar to all acts 

of forgery. The defeated president has himself asserted that the results will be more than 
99 percent. AGENCE FRANCE PRESSE last night reported that the results of the referendum 

in Egypt do not require any experts or computers. 


Ve 23 Apr 79 I 4 NORTH AFRICA 


The political editor of the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY reported that the aim behind a 
referendum in Egypt is the attempt to persuade the world that the Egyptian people 
agrees to capitulation to the enemy, the relinquishment of Arab rights in Palestine, 
giving up the Arab occupied territories and accepting Zionist infiltration in Egypt. 
This in addition to the isolation imposed on Egypt by Arab and other progressive and 
Third World countries as a result of all that, 


The editor affirmed that the defeated president does not really respect the will of the 
Egyptian people, not even that represented in the result of the referendum The Egyptian 
people had voted "yes" to the Federation of Arab Republics, nevertheless one day he 
resolved to freeze the matter, This irony insults millions of Egyptians who had gone 

to the poll with unprecedented enthusiasm in order to raise the flag of unity. The 
editor went on to say that after the defeated Egyptian president found himself drowning 
in the swamp of treason, he thought that he could escape the Egyptian people's revolu- 
tion by usurping their consent through misrepresenting their will in a referendum farce 
on the most serious cause of destiny. In conclusion the editor affirmed that the 
people's revolution in Egypt will burn the Egyptian president and with him all the docu. 
ments of capitulation he signed in prostration before the racist Zionist enemy, in the 
presence of the leader of world imperialism, Jimmy Carter, The Egyptian people will 
remain as ever before, rejecting defeat and insisting on the restoration by force of 

the usurped rights, 


TRIPOLI RADIO SAYS U,S, 'MISSILE GROUPS! STATIONED ON BORDER 
LD221315 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 22 Apr 79 LD 


[Text] JANA has learned that a meeting of officials of the U.S, military intelligence 
was held on 16 March 1979 during which it was decided to send U.S. missile groups [ar 
firaq] to be stationed along the border with the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah, The commander of these groups received a message before his departure for 
the Egyptian capital from the head of the U.S, military intelligence telling him: We 
are at present unable to guarantee As-Sadat being in our hands completely because his 
problems with other Arab states are growing, Despite this, there is still hope, You 
and your group should make the necessary effort, Furthermore, the head of the ninth 
American missile group was ordered to leave for the Egyptian border with Libya to train 
Egyptian forces there, He took with him two missile groups from U.S, forces in Italy 
and West Germany, The leader of the group asked in his report to U.S. intelligence to 
make available a quantity of U.S. weapons in the region, Reports say that a group of 
these weapons was indeed transferred to the border with Libya, It includes bombs of 

the type 83 MK and 88 MK, air to air missiles and surface to air missiles type 10 MAU 
and 68 MAU and chemical bombs type ACB. JANA also has learned that on 12 April 1979 the 
American military groups moved to Egypt with the aim of carrying out reconnaissance 
tasks, One group came from Karlsruhe U.S, air base, another from the U.S. naval base of 
Bremerhaven and a third from the (Arding) base, A senior U.S, officer who took part in 
battles in Vietnam and Cambodia was chosen to lead the U.S, air delegation present in 
the region of the Egyptian-Libyan border,

[Text] The Libyan Foreign Secretariat issued a statement on the claims made by the body 
Which calls itself the state council in Chad. Here is the text of the statement: 


Within the framework of the informtion campaign against Lilya and its people, the in- 
formation media 2 days ago carried a statement issued by a group calling itself "State 
Council of Chad" containing lies and malicious fabrications attributed to that group

The body which calls itself the Chad revolutionary council does not exist and it has

ridicule and it does not deserve a reply. The Foreign Secretariat, while astonished

quests in the name of the Jamahiriyah that those who want to should make their own 
investigation and visit the areas referred to in order to find out about this imaginary 
story. The Jamahiriyah, which is linked to the Chadian people by historical, spiritual 
and cultural relations, rer-ets that such claims are made--claims which cannot be anything 
else except a fictional s’ ry fapricated by some elements which were once supported by 
the Jamahiriyah, and which realize that the reason for their existence now in Ndjamena 

is due to the Jamahiriyah. While Libya affirms its stand alongside the Chadian people, 
at the same time it affirms that sole legality is something which the third conference of 
the Chadian national liberation front, FROLINAT, will produce. Libya also affirms that 
no one can speak in the name of the Chadian people who are torn by the struggle for 
power by some adventurers who are working to isolate this struggling people from their 
neighbors and the rest of the African peoples.

[Text] The people killed in the terrorist attack in Nahariya will be buried tomorrow, 
The police sergeant, Eliyahu Shahar, 24, will be buried at 1400 tomorrow, in the plot 
allocated to military casualties in the Ma'alot cemetery. The funeral of Dani Haran, 
28, and his daughters 'Eynat, 4, and Ya'el, 2, will leave Tiv'on tomorrow for the 
cemetery in Nahariya,. 


Three of those wounded in the attack are hospitalized in the Nahariya hospital. The 
hospital manager defined their condition as satisfactory, 


At noon the IDF's spokesman made an announcement summing up the progression of the 
terrorist attack: 


It is stated in the announcement that at nearly 0300 in the morning four terrorists 
reached Nahariya by sea. They took over an apartment in an apartment builing and there 
one of the Haran family daughters was killed. Two terrorists took the father of the 
family and the other daughter with them and tried to escape and return to their boat on 
shore, After they murdered the two hostages they fell into the hands of our forces, 
The two other terrorists of the group were killed in exchanges of fire with the security 
forces reaching the incident sites, One of them was killed by a citizen's shots and 
the other by the fire of the security forces, In the exchanges of fire a policeman was 
killed and four people were wounded, The IDF spokesman adds that the terrorist group 
left Tyre on a rubber boat and its members belong to the Palestinian Liberation Front 
acting in Lebanon with Iraqi support,

I. is interesting that there are countries which every year have a feast that shows their

the fact that it is a year when one of the most important and most basic processes-- 

of signing the Israeli-Egyptian peace treaty--has happened, I compare this to one of the 
most basic chapters of the foundation and strengthening of the State of Israel,

[Answer] There are different emphases now, The emphasis should now be, to our regret, 
on the eastern and northern fronts. I wish we could say we could beat our swords into

[Answer] I do not view the UN force as a negative thing. I think that in some cases one
