LIBYA

Senegal—Ibrahim Muhammad al-Bishari, secretary of the 
Libyan General People’s Committee for External Liaison 
and International Cooperation, met Senegalese President 
Abdou Diouf on 11 December. Diouf praised Libya for its 
support for Islam and Muslims and thanked Libya for its 
support for holding the summit in Senegal. (Tripoli Libyan 
Television Network in Arabic 1930 GMT 11 Dec) 


Mauritania—Al-Bishari met the Mauritanian foreign 
minister on 11 December. (Tripoli JANA in English 
0809 GMT 12 Dec) 


Guinea-Bissau—Al-Bishari met Guinea-Bissau Presi- 
dent Joao Bernardo Vieira on 11 December on bilateral 
cooperation between the two countries. (Tripoli JANA 
in English 0808 GMT 12 Dec)
