LIBYA

OFFICIAL DISMISSES REPORTS OF LIBYAN SOLIDERS IN IRAQ 
LD051820 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1615 GMT 5 Oct 80 
[Text] Tripoli, 5 Oct (JANA)--The secretary general of the Foreign Secretariat today 
received the Iraqi ambassador to Libya and informed him of his strong condemnation of 
the lies reiterated by the Iraqi defense minister, who alleged that some Libyans were 
taken prisoner in the battles against Iraq. 
He also advised of the stand of the Libyan Foreign Secretariat which is that if a Libyan 
soldier was taken prisoner; we demand of you before Arab and world public opinion that 
you do whatever you like with him and make a sandwich out of him.
