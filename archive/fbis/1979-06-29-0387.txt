LIBYA

ARAB PEOPLE'S COUNCIL CABLES AL-ASAD ON ISRAELI AIR BATTLE 
LD281224 Tripoli JANA in English 0943 GMT 28 Jun 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, June 28, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab 
People's Congress sent a cable yesterday to President liafiz al-Asad of Syria which reads 
as follows: 


"President Hafiz al-Asad of the Syrian Arab Kepublic. 


The confrontation by Palestinian Arab eagles of the Zionist raid against our Palestinian 
and L’ banese people embodies the Arab will which rejects defeat and believes in strength, 
the language to be used in dealing with the enemy. It is an expression of the national 
solidarity, our Arab nation's masses and of steadfastness in the face of all the 
imperialist-Zionist-Sadatist conspiracies and schemes, 


While following with concern reports of the air battle which took place in the Lebanese 
airspace, the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress considers with esteem 
your courageous decision to confront the enemy and hails the Syrian people and their 
heroic eagles. 


The Permanent Secretariat declares its stand by the Syrian people and its readiness as 
well as that of the Arab masses to offer everything within their means to ensure support 
for the confrontation battle with the Zionist enemy through the Arab nation's steadfast- 
ness and the solidarity of its revolutionary forces. This alliance will never succeed 

in driving the Arab nation to surrender, "
