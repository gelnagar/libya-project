LIBYA

BRIEFS 
AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES CABLE -- Tripoli, Rabi' Al-Awwal 7, Dec 22, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- 
The revolutionary force in Poland has strongly condemned the U.S. provocation against 
the Libvan people. They also expressed their readiness to defend the grand achievements 
of the Libvan people. The cable expressed the forces admiration of the Jamahiriyah's 
ceaseless efforts to make the 19th African summit a real success. The cable has further 
hailed the African progressive countries which stood up to the challenges of the U.S. 
Administration. [Text] [Tripoli JANA in English 1745 GMT 22 Dec 82 LD]
