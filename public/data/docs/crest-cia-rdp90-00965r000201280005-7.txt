Summary (AI): A 19 March 1986 Baltimore Sun article reports that the U.S. was deploying three carrier task forces in the Mediterranean north of Libya for flight operations over two days, amid a war of nerves against Qaddafi's Libya. It concerns the Gulf of Sidra standoff rather than Tripoli itself.
• Three carrier task forces: Saratoga, America (scheduled relief), and Coral Sea.
• Force could put as many as 245 aircraft in the air, including A-6, A-7, F/A-18 and F-14 aircraft.
• Soviets reportedly had about half a dozen warships in the Mediterranean; a submarine tender acting as an eavesdropping ship was reported off Libya.
• The naval force included equipment for detecting and destroying Soviet-supplied SAM sites.
• 1986-03-19: U.S. announced three carrier task forces (Saratoga, America, Coral Sea) deployed in the Mediterranean north of Libya for flight operations over two days, with about 30 warships on scene.
