LIBYA

OIL MINISTER STRESSES ECONOMIC INTERDEPENDENCE OF STATES 
DW021236 Hamburg ARD Television Network in German 2100 GMT 29 Jun 79 DW 


[Correspondent Seibert interview with Libyan Minister for Petroleum Izz ad-Din al-Mabruk, 


apparently in Geneva, original language unknown--recorded ] 


[Text ] [Question] May we say that the times of modest prices are past? 


[Answer ] No doubt about it, and the consumer countries should note that prices only go 
up, that the era of cheap energy is over for good, 


[Question] Speaking in terms of the world economy, what do you anticipate from such a 
price policy? 


[Answer ] We, the OPEC countries, always adapt to conditions. So far we have been very 
moderate and acted responsibly in world-economic respect, for we are part of the world 
economy, and a recession would hit us, too, We want to adapt moderately, but we are 
always described as those culpable, the scoundrels of the world economy, This simply is 
not true. We have shown this again here, 


[Question] Are you not fearing any negative consequences such as a price increase for 
industrial goods? 


[Answer] Yes, this is the vicious circle, We jack up our oil prices, the others hike 
prices for their industrial goods and thus again swallow our extra proceeds, But one 
thing is definite: All of us are interdependent. We need the industrial countries to 
develop, and they need us, This problem can be solved only in such a way that all 
countries sit down at a table and jointly discuss the structure of the world economy, 


[Question] But are you not enlarging the gap with the have-nots, the poor countries of 
the Third and Fourth Worlds? 


[Answer ] We are aware that they ere badly off. Basically we belong to them. The only 
difference is that we are slightly more lucky, having our good oil.
