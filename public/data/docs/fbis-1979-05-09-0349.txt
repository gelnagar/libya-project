LIBYA

CHOICE OF STRAUSS AS U.S. REPRESENTATIVE DENOUNCED 


LDO71944 Tripoli JANA in English 1811 GMT 7 May 79 LD 


("Carter Appoints a Jew for Al-'Arish Talks"--JANA headline] 


[Text] London, 7 May, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The American President Jimmy Carter 
announced that he had appointed Robert Strauss, who is of Jewish origin, as the main 
American negotiator on self-rule issue on West Bank and Gaza Strip, in the negotiations 
which will be held in Al-'Arish between the enemy and the defeated Egyptian regime, 


The observers believe that the appointment of Strauss the Jew for the talks is a true 
indication of the intentions of America in supporting the enemy's position and its 
complete alignment. 


The observers pointed out that Carter has inflicted a new insult to the defeated presi- 
dent when he enlisted an American Jew to the enemy's delegation in these talks.
