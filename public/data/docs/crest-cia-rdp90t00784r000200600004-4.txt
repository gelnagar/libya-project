Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing note reports Soviet arms deliveries and SA-2 surface-to-air missile transshipment activity at Tripoli port. It describes helicopter containers and armored vehicles offloaded from a Soviet ship and SA-2 equipment waiting on the quay for transfer to a Libyan ro-ro ship.
• Six HIP helicopter fuselage containers and at least 21 HIP component containers offloaded from a Soviet Leninskaya Gvardiya-class merchant ship.
• Three armored vehicles placed on 60-ton heavy-lift transporters, possibly part of an arms delivery.
• Five SA-2 missile launchers (not annotated) and at least 12 SA-2 missile transporters remained on the quay awaiting transshipment.
• Transshipment was to the Libyan El Temsah-class roll-on/roll-off ship.
• Coordinates given: 32-54-25N 013-11-15E.
• 1983-02: Offloading of six HIP helicopter fuselage containers and at least 21 HIP component containers from a Soviet Leninskaya Gvardiya-class merchantman; three armored vehicles moved on heavy-lift transporters; SA-2 transshipment to a Libyan El Temsah-class ro-ro ship continuing, with five SA-2 launchers and at least 12 transporters awaiting transfer on the quay.
