Armed Forces Commander Arrives in Prague 
LD271839 Tripoli JANA in English 1747 GMT 26 Oct 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Muharram 20, Oct 26, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY —— Commander in chief of the Libyan Arab Armed 
Forces, Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, has arrived in Prague 
on a visit to Czechoslovakia to discuss the policy of friendship 
and cooperation between the two countries. Brigadier Abu Bakr 
Yunis was met by Major General Marten Tazur, the 
Czechoslovakian defence minister. Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis 
was met by deputies of the defence minister, the heads of forces, 
and the members of the People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau in 
Crechoslovakia
