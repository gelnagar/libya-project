Summary (AI): A April 1986 CIA assessment of who might succeed Qadhafi if he were ousted, covering power struggles among factions, the armed forces' alienation after the US strikes, and possible Soviet influence. It mentions no specific Tripoli sites beyond a general reference to Tripoli's climate and an Air Force commander's arrest.
• Soviet military advisory contingent in Libya is put at 1,500-2,000 men.
• Last fall's infighting pitted the Revolutionary Committees against the Qadhafa tribe.
• Qadhafi established a Peoples Militia as a counterweight to the armed forces.
• Some East Germans reportedly trained Libyan intelligence officers, but Qadhafi limited Soviet Bloc involvement in his security services.
• 1986-04-15: US airstrikes on Libya; the Air Force Commander was reportedly arrested afterward for the poor Libyan showing on 15 April.
• 1984-05: Exiled Libyan dissidents made a first attempt to establish a network inside Libya capable of attacking Qadhafi.
