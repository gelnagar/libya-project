[Question] During your talks did you find tangible elements allowing you to think 
that the West Sahara conflict can not be settled on a military basis and that it 


is the hour for diplomacy? 


[Answer] I do not see that I should substantially change the terms of my reply just 
because you change the terms of the question. I shall simply tell you that in the 

talks I had on this subject of West Sahara I heard our Moroccan partners’ viewpoint, 
and I expressed France's viewpoint, a viewpoint moreover which I have rendered public. 
I shall not go beyond this declaration.

Concerning a question on the situation in Chad after the Libyan intervention, Mr 
Barre declared: “When you follow events for a certain duration you notice that what 
people call observers are alvays more responsive to what they call imprecisions than 
to what appear to be the invariables. That is perhaps because the positions of one 
and the other are not the same. A government carries out a certain policy whose 
duration responds to certain principles; it does not carry out a policy to respond 
to emotions, to moods which moreover can be quite changeable according to circum- 
stances. So far as Chad is concerned, France's position has been set forth clearly. 
We have tried for a long time to see to it that a political solution could be found 
in Chad. 


We were asked to withdraw our forces from Chad; we do not impose ourselves on any 
country. We withdrew our forces. It is interesting--not to use another expression-- 
to hear those who asked us to withdraw our forces now tell us that we ought to have 
let them stay. As I said a moment ago, if two countries in Africa wish France to 
bring them aid, France will be there. But France does not impose itself on a 
country that does not wish to receive France's economic, financial and military 
assistance. Respect for the independence of all countries is the principle of our 


policy.

SYRIAN REGIME'S TORTURE METHODS DESCRIBED 
Cairo AL-DA'WAH in Arabic Dec 80 pp 14,15 


[Article: "Syrian Authorities Use the Jews' Terror Methods; Beating With an Axe, 
Instead of a Club, When Frustrated over Extracting Confessions!") 


[Text] AL-DA'WAH, with (Allah) God's help, has been able to meet with one of 
the mujahedin brethren who left Aleppo a week ago. From him, it obtained a ser- 
ious report about what is happening inside that heroic and beleagured city. 


He began by talking about al-Ma'mun secondary school, the largest secondary school 
in Aleppo. He said: 


"Al-Ma'mun is considered the biggest secondary school in Aleppo. The number of 
students last year, before the Syrian Islamic revolution escalated, was 1800. As 

a result of the escalation of mujahedin efforts against the despotic, voracious and 
barbarous Nusayri regime, because of the cohorts of the client regime, the number 
of students this year was only 800. Among the remaining 1000 students are those 
who have joined the mujahedin, pursued by the regime. 


Among those who were martyred, for example: Haytham Hanzani, a student, obtained 
his bachelor's degree and Islam Abu Hamidah and Aysar Abu Hamidah, Palestinians 
from al-Nayrab camp in Aleppo. Their third borther, Ahmad Abu Hamidah, was also 
martyred, along with Mazen al-Qadhi, Fares Hamwi, Yusef al-Misri, Fadhel al-Hajj 
Yusef, Nafe' Quds, and other school students. Another group is in prison and 
detention camps; for instance, Bukri Shawa', ‘Abd al-‘Alim Khiyatah, ‘Adil Qurrah 
Hamud, and Maher Najjar. As for the names of those who joined the ranks of the mu- 
jahedin, they are known to God!"
