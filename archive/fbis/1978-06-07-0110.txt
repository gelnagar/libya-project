JANA REPORTS LIBYAN-ALGERIAN JOINT COMMUNIQUE 


LD062212Y Tripoli JANA in English1823 amt 6 Jun 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 June, JANA--The following joint communique was issued in Tripoli and 
Algiers today at the conclusion of an official visit by Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to 
Algeria. 


Vv. 7 Jun 78 IT2 NORTH AFRICA 


Within the framework of regular meetings between the two political leaderships, and 

out of the strategic alliance of the two revolutions, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, secre- 
tary general of the General People's Congress of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah, paid a visit to the People's Democratic Republic of Algeria during the 
period 30th May to 6th Jure 1978. 


On the Libyan side tne talks were attended by the brothers Maj al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi, 
member of the General Secretariat of the General People's Congress; Jadallah 'Azzuz 
at-Talhi, industry secretary; Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, foreign secretary; and 
Musa Ahmad Abu ruraywah, planning secretary. 


From the Algerian side the following were present: Mohamed Salah Yahiaoui, member of 
the Revolutionary Council and executive official of the party organ; Mohammed Benahmed 
Abdelghani, member of the Revolutionary Council and minister of the interior; Ahmed 
Draia, member of the Revolutionary Council and minister of transport; Mohamed Tayebi 
Larbi, member of the Revolutionary Council and minister of agriculture and agrarian 
reform; and Dr Ahmed Talel) tbrahimi, minister counselor to the president. 


In this meeting experiences of the two sisterly countries regarding socialist appli- 
cation and popular democracy were reviewed with a view to mutual enrichment and deep- 
ening of their revolutionary values. The different aspects of bilateral cooperation 

in various fields and the ways of increasing and developing them were also considered. 
It was agreed to take the necessary steps to strengthen them to meet the aspirations of 
the two peoples to realize prosperity, self-sufficiency and unity. 


The two delegations considered the situation in the Arab homeland and affirmed their 
national commitment to the resolutions of the Tripoli and Algiers cmferences of stead- 
fastness and confrontation, and their condemnation of the policy of capitulation and 
subservience to Zionism and imperialism. The two sides affirm their complete rejection 
of any Arab meeting under the policy of capitulation and abandonment of the nationalist 
and patriotic rights, because Arab solidarity must be against and not with the enemy. 


The two sides declare their absolute support for, and pledge their assistance to, the 
Palestinian revolution and declare their resolute stand against the schemes aimed at 
liquidating the Palestinian cause and «essignment of the national rights of the Pales- 
tinian people and their right to complete liberation of their soil, seif-determination 
and the return to their usurped land. 


The two sides hail the steadfastness of Syria and the Palestinian revolution, and who 
are standing firmly and bravely at the front line of the national struggle against 
Zionism and imperialism, as an impregnable fortress in the face of the liquidationist 
schemes. 


The two sides condemn the Zionist aggression against Lebanon and condemm the isolation- 
ist forces which have made themselves allies of Zionism, and greet the Lebanese Arab 
people, who sacrificed, without hesitation, for the Arab struggle. 


The two sides believe that the role of the UN forces must be limited to guarantee the 
withdrawal of the Zionist invasion forces from southern Lebanon. 


The two sides hail the steadfastness of the Egyptian people and their patriotic and pro- 
gressive forces in facing pressure, repression, terror and starvation. The two sides 
call on the patriotic and progressive forces in the Arab nation to stand by the Egyptian 
people in their ordeal.

On the other hand, the two sides considered the situation in Africa and the savage 
imperialist onslaught on the African Continent and express their belief that the African 
peoples possess the capability to confront this onslaught, and that resolution of African 
problems must we accomplished by Africans themselves, without any foreign intervention, 
out of their belief that Africa has no pact but with itself and that the true and 
necessary alliance is that of the progressive forces in the continent against imperialism 
and its accomplices. 


The two sides affirn rejection of the pacts being set up by the neocolonialist forces 

in order to bring back their [passage indistinct] the military intervention of neo- 
colonialism in the Western Sahara, Shaba Province and Chad, and their determination to 
resolutely withstand this intervention. The policy of using paratroopers and airplanes 
is nothing but a modern version of the antiquated gunboat diplomacy which history has 
judged to be futile, as it has judged that malice will always rebound on the malicious 
and that peoples cannot be fooled by the imperialist justifications which are used to 
loot the wealth of peoples and to trample on their humanity. The pretexts of protecting 
nationals, interests and the implementation of agreements imposed by the occupiers are 
nothing but a vain imperialist tactic which has been proved futile. 


In this connection the two sides declare their support for and pledge their assistance 
to the African liberation movements in their struggle for liberation and freedom from 
imperialist control and the racist regimes. 


The complete liberation of the African Continent will be a decisive factor in establish- 
ing the new world economic order. The two sides affirm the.right of the developing 
peoples to control their resources and national wealth and demand a reassessment of the 
basis of the international economy in order to eliminate all types cf ecmomic and 
social oppression and exploitation between the developing countries and the developed 
ones. 


The two sides reaffirm their adherence to the principles of nonalignment, as they are 
a positive and decisive factor in the international struggle for the victory of the 
peoples and for the establishment of international peace and security. 


The two sides considered the situation in the Mediterranean and agree on the necessity 
of removing all factors of tension caused by the foreign presence in this sea, in order 
to transform it into a lake of peace and cooperation to serve the peoples of the region. 


The two sides express their satisfaction with the positive results achieved by this 
visit, and agree to continue contacts at all levels. 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the secretary general of the General People's Congress, 
expresced his thanks for the warm and brotherly welcome accorded to him by President 
Houari Boumediene, the National Liberation Front and the government and people of the 
People's Democratic Republic of Algeria. 

PFLP-GC LEADER MAKES STATEMENT ON ARRIVAL

[Text] Tripoli, June 6, JANA--The secretary general of the (PLFP-GC), the Poptlar 
Front for the Liberation of Palestine [General Command], Mr Ahmad Jibril, yesterday 
arrived in Tripoli on a few days visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


Mr Jibril was received at Tripoli International Airport by a number of officials of the 
Arab Liaison Office at the General Secretariat of the General Peopie's Congress,

In a statement to the JANA correspondent, Mr Jibril said that his visit came at a time 
when the imperialist, Zionist and reactionary attacks on the Arab nomeland were being 
stepped up and the people of the Arab east were exposed to particularly fierce military 
attacks led by the Zionist enemy. 


The secretary general of the (PLFP-GC) referred to colonial intervention in the African 
Continent, saying that the imperialist forces were trying by all means possible to return 
to their old policies--direct intervention among the African peoples in the western 
desert, in Chad end the Shaba Province. 


Mr Jibril affirmed that the Arab homeland and its national and progressive forces had no 
other alternative except a real and positive confrontation, particularly after the enemy's 
plots against the Arab and African countries were exposed. 


Mr Jibril also called for the setting up of an Arab policy (?of) action supported by the 
world's progressive forces to draw up plans to confront the enemy. He referred to the 
al-Fatah Revolution, the Palestinian resistance and the countries of the steadfastness 
front who are assigned with the task of foiling the enemy's plots.

Meets With Municipalities Secretary 
LDO61646Y Tripoli JANA in English 1542 GMT 6 Jun 78 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, June 6, JANA--Mr Abu Zayd 'Umar Durdah, the municipalities secretary, 
received this morning the Bulgarian deputy chairman, Mr Andrey Lukanov, and the delegation 
accompanying him. The meeting was attended by the under-secretary of municipalities and 
the head of the construction and public works corporation. 


The meeting dealt with the aspects of cooperation between the Bulgarian authorities and 
the municipalities sector, particularly the tenders presented by Bulgaria for refrigeration 
and shopping centres in a number of municipalities. 


Agreement was reached on a visit by a Bulgarian technical delegation to the Jamahiriyah to 
obtain the necessary data for plans and studies of complete projects of public utilities 
in more than 30 cities, including the setting up of a joint company for drilling drinking 
water wells. Instructions were given to the authorities concerned to investigate the 
details of the project.
