Summary (AI): A June 1985 FBIS transcript of a Tripoli TV broadcast of Qadhdhafi's Evacuation Day address threatens Egypt over its relations with Israel and US-backed radio stations, and refers to Sudan's Numayri. It contains no specific facts about Tripoli places, events or people.
• The speech was broadcast by Tripoli Television in Arabic on 11 June 1985 (Evacuation Day).
• Qadhdhafi threatened Egypt and said the popular congresses would be asked to debate confrontation if Egypt did not reverse course.
• He demanded that Egypt revoke the Camp David treaties.
• He referred to Israel's strike on an atomic reactor in Baghdad as a model.
