POLISH MILITARY OFFICIAL ARRIVES 20 APRIL 


LD202120 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 20 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 Apr (JANA) -- Gen Florian Siwicki, Polish deputy minister of national 
defense, member of the Military Council of National Salvation and chief of staff of the 
armed forces in the Polish People's Republic arrived in Tripoli this evening. 


At Tripoli airport, the Polish guest was met by Brig Gen Mustafa al-Kharrubi, chief of 
the general staff of the armed forces, and by a number of officers of the Libyan Arab 
Armed Forces. 


In a statement to JANA, Gen Florian Siwicki lauded the development of relations between the 
jamahiriyah and Poland. General Siwicki expressed his hope that this visit will contribute 


to the strengthening of these relations in the interest of the two friendly peoples. 


AL-Q ADHDHAFI RECEIVES BOURGUIBA MESSAGE 


LD180828 Tunis Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 18 Apr 82 


[Text] Social Affairs Minister Mohamed Naceur last night had a meeting with Col Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi and conveyed to him greetings from President Habib Bourguiba. In turn, Col 
al-Qadhdhafi asked the minister of social affairs to convey his greetings to the great 
combatant. 


A working meeting was held yesterday afternoon between the members of the Tunisian and 
Libyan delegations, during which the position of the Tunisian community and aspects of 
economic and social cooperation between Tunisia and the Libyan Jamahiriyah were discussed. 
Following the meeting, two committees were set up, one to deal with the position of the 
Tunisian community in the jamahiriyah and the other concerned with commercial and 
industrial exchange and their [word indistinct] between the two countries. This work falls 
within the framework of contacts taking place in preparation for a (?future) meeting of 
the higher committee.

UN REPRESENTATIVE MEETS WITH PEREZ DE CUELLAR 


LD171447 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 17 Apr 82 


[Text] UN Secretary General Javier Perez de Cuellar has expressed appreciation for the 
prominent role played by the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahriiyah in resolving 
international issues and for Libya's effective contribution to the work of the UN agencies. 
During a meeting with Dr ‘Ali at-Turayki, the jamahiriyah's permanent UN representative, 
Perez de Cuellar also expressed his pleasure at taking part in the work of the African 
summit conference to be held in the jamahiriyah this August. He said that this visit w‘1l 
give him the opportunity to see the achievements of the Libyan Arab people as a result 

of the Great al-Fatih Revolution and to meet Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi.
