BOUMEDIENE CABLES AL-QADHDHAFI ON EVACUATION ANNIVERSARY 
LD12i000Y Tripoli JANA in English 0952 GMT 12 Jun 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, June 12, JANA--Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, secretary general of the General 
People’s Congress, has received a telegram from the Algerian president, Boumediene. 
Following is its full text: 


I am happy, while the brotherly Libyan people are celebrating the 8th amiversary of the 
evacuation of the American forces from the national soil, to express to you in the name 
of the Algerian people and in my own name, the warmest congratulations and the sincerest 
brotherly feelings on this great occasion which we regard as a turning point in the his- 
tory of the Arab nation. 


The evacuation of the foreign forces from the land of brotherly Libya was the crowning 
of the bitter struggle, waged by the Libyan people, for realizing their dignity and at- 
taining their goals of freedom and sovereignty. The current conditions in our region 
demand from us increased cooperation and coordination and the exertion of efforts to 
confront the imperialist invasion and the plots hatched against our common causes of 
destiny. 


I wish you good health and wish the brotherly Libyan people continued progress and 
prosperity. 


PFLP Congratulations 
LD112324Y Tripoli JANA in English 1822 GMT 11 Jun 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, June 11, JANA--Colonel Mu'ammar Qadhdhafi, the leader of the Great 
Al-Fatah Revolution has received a cable from the PFLP [Popular Front for the Libera- 
tion of Palestine] congratulating him on the occasion of the evacuation of the American 
forces from the land of the Jamahiriyah.

The cable said: "On the 8th anniversary of the evacuation of the American forces from 
the land of the great Al-Fatah, the General Command of the PFLP congratulates the 
Jamahiriyah's people and the leadership of the Great Al-Fathah Revolution on this 
immortal day which symbolizes the determination of the Al-Fathah revolution to give 
the land its true identity from the first moment cf its birth. 


The cable added that it was not strange to see the Al-Fatah revolution reducing distances 
and time in order to build and firmly establish the pillars of progress in all fields, 
and to reach a more advanced stage of development in which the people possess the 

wealth and the power to act. The cable pointed to the firm and progressive stands of 
the Great Al-Fatah Revolution towards the Palestinian revolution and towards all the 
liberation movements throughout the world. 


JANA SCORES FORTHCOMING VISIT OF EGYPT'S KAMIL TO ZAIRE 
LD112326Y Tripoli JANA in English 1817 GMT 11 Jun 78 LD 


[Text] Egypt, June 11, JANA--The Egyptian foreign minister starts a visit tomorrow to 
Kinshasa to conduct negotiations described by the Egyptian mass media as important. The 
importance of such negotiations stems from the fact that the Egyptian president is 
offering military assistance to Mobutu's regime in crushing the Zairian revolutionaries. 
It is not surprising that As-Sadat's mass media considers such an act a fulfillment of 
As-Sadat's obligations towards Africa, because one of his obligations after deserting 
the Arab and African line was to repress every liberation movement in Africa. That 

is why he sent a military mission and military equipment to Zaire at a time when 
Egyptian lands are still under Zionist occupation.
