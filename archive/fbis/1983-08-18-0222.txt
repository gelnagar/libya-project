LIBYA

JANA: Chad’s Goukouni Cables Arab League 
LD172025 Tripoli JANA in English 1847 GMT 17 Aug 83 


[Text] Bardai, Dhu al-Qa’dah 9, August 17, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — President Goukouni Oueddei, head of the 
legitimate Government of National Unity in Chad, today sent a 
cable message to Chedli Klibi, secretary general of the Arab 
League, in which he exposed the colonialist conspiracy facing his 
country as a result of imperialist, U.S., French and reactionary 
intervention in the internal affairs of his country and the suffer- 
ing of the Chadian people as a result of this. 


President Goukouni said the following in his letter: “This gov- 
ernment, formed as a result of a historic agreement between 
various factions in Chad, had put an end to the hardships of our 
people, in order to establish permanent peace. The agreement 
was reached with the help of the OAU and Chad’s neighbours 
which provided guarantees. From here the well known Lagos 
agreement was born and is considered the only legitimate 
framework for my country now.” 


The letter went on “The agent Habre, who held the post of 
minister of defence in the government formed on the basis of the 
agreement, decided to adopt armed rebellion, to topple the 
legitimate government to satisfy his ambitions for power. He was 
supported, encouraged and armed by the world imperialism 
Zionism and their agents in Africa such as Mobutu of Zaire. 


President Goukouni said, “It is in the face of such threat which 
puts the hard earned independence and sovereignty of our people 
at jeopardy that I would like to draw the attention of the 
secretariat and members of the Arab League to the just cause for 
which the Chadian people is struggling and to the new dimensions 
developed lately in the problem.” 


The president of the legitimate National Unity Government 
confirmed that the USA and France and their agents in Africa, 
having supplied the rebel Habre in Ndjamena with all means of 
destruction, topped it all by sending combat forces to fight 
alongside him and by doing so they bear the responsibility for 
internationalising the problem.” 


He said that the present turning point of the events does not only 
threaten Chad but it threatens the Socialist People’s Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah, a member of the Arab League, and the 
security of the region which includes other members of your 
organisation, fully aware that this blatant aggression to which 
we are victim [words indistinct] not just by pure chance but the 
result of a plan to weaken Arab resistance wherever that may be.
