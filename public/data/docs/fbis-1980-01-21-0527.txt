The symposium, which convened today in Tripoli, is being organized by the International 
Secretariat for Solidarity with the Arab People and their central issue, Palestine, in 
conjunction with the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress, 


The symposium was declared open today in Tripoli by the secretary general of the Arab 
People's Congress Permanent Committee, and the head of the International Secretariat. 
This is the International Secretariat's first activity Since its formation last November. 


Speakers at today!s meeting discussed the meaning of solidarity with the Egypt ian people. 
They said such solidarity is an important duty if American imperialist and Zionist 
conspiracies are to be confronted, and their use of the defeated Egyptian regime as a 
spearhead against the Palestinian and Egyptian people and the Arab nation is foiled. 


At this morning!s session, which started at nine o'clock in the morning at the headquarters 
of the nation's unionist council, speeches were made by heads of delegations represent ing 
the patriotic and progressive Egyptian movement, Syria, the Palestine Liberation Organi- 
sation, the Soviet Union, the Arab-Italian Friendship Society, the secretary general of 
the popular bloc in Jordan, and Mr Ahmad Shahati in his capacity as member of the World 
Peace Council's governing body and head of the General People's Congress Foreign Liaison 
bureau in the Jamahiriyah. 


The delegations have already resumed discussion of agendas for solidarity with the Egyptian 
people. On the other hand, a political rally is being organised in Tripoli this evening. 
The rally will be attended by all delegations participating in the symposium,

Burg Rejects Egyptian Demands 
TA181217 Tel Aviv MA'ARIV in Hebrew 18 Jan 80 p 3 TA 
[By Avraham Rotem] 


[Text] Interior Minister and Chairman of the Ministerial Committee on Autonomy 
Dr Yosef Burg reacted yesterday at a meeting of the NRP center to Egypt's demand 
to apply autonomy to the eastern part of Jerusalem, 


He said: "In Cairo we submitted a model of how Israel viewed the functions of the 
administrative council, We said that if we talk about ideology this would only 
create a rift with Egypt. Therefore, to bring about a rapprochement between the 
parties one should supply a solution, It is for this reason that we submitted the 
model in detail with interpretations, 


"Yesterday the Egyptian Government said that it viewed our proposals as unacceptable, 
Today they submitted their proposals that part of Jerusalem be subject to the 
autonomy rule, while it was only last week that the prime minister reiterated in 
Aswan that Jerusalem is neither negotiable nor partitionable, 


"The Egyptian premise that everything that was previously under Jordanian rule 
should be under the autonomy is paradoxical, Throughout thousands of years and in 
all periods in Eretz Yisra'el Jerusalem was one city." 


Dr Burg said that yesterday morning the Egyptian demand on Jerusalem was again 
made, and he could only use the same words that the Egyptians used a day earlier 
in rejecting Israel's autonomy model: "This is totally and clearly unacceptable," 
The interior minister added that "the fact that they totally refuse to discuss it 
pushes us back at least 6 months if not more," 


Further on, Dr Burg said that "stability in this region at this time, against the 
background of events in Iran and Afghanistan, is more important than ever and 
anyone who imagines that opposing our proposals will bring stability to our area 
more quickly is wrong. I think that the kings, shaykhs and emirs in our region 
have to do a lot of soul searching and show greater fear of communist ideology. 
Their throne is at greater risk from communism than from us," 


Dr Burg said that one can only wait until the end of the month when Sol Linowitz 
arrives, and then "we'll meet and see what happens," According to him, the free 
world does not appreciate the size of Israel's sacrifice to avoid jeopardizing the 
signing of the Camp David agreement,

Cabinet Rejects As-Sadat's Proposals 
TA201155 Jerusalem Domestic Service in Hebrew 1105 GMT 20 Jan 80 TA 


[Text] Now to the prime minister*s office where the weekly Cabinet meeting ended a few 
minutes ago. Here is our political correspondent Shim'on Schiffer. 


[Schiffer] The Cabinet discussed the autonomy negotiations today and this is what was 
resolved. In the future Israel will stand by the positions presented last week in Cairo 
by the working group for the autonomy talks. The Israeli model for the structure and 
powers of the autonomy were presented to the Cabinet in detail. 


In the deliberations the prime minister said that the Egyptian positions on the autonomy 
issue were a total deviation and complete aberration of the Camp David agreements. In view 
of the recent developments in the region, including the Islamic revolution in Iran and the 
Soviet invasion of Afghanistan, it should be made clear to everybody that Israel will 
continue to stand by its positions and will oppose the establishment of a Palestinian state 
in the territories. These were the prime minister's remarks. 


Cabinet Secretary Arye Na'tor analyzed the position expressed by the cabinet as follows: 


[Begin Na'tor recording] Israel's position is that the latest events in the Middle East 
prove the justice and firmness of the Israeli positon. That is to say, if it has been 
proved, as is the case in Afghanistan, that at the invitation of a soecalled friendly 
government, a Soviet army can go any place reachable by air from the Soviet Union, then it 
must be concluded that if the right of self<jetermination were granted to the Arabs of 
Judaea, Samaria and Gaza and if the autonomy could be changed into a state--which contrae 
dicts the Camp David agreementse-there is a danger that such a Soviet army would be invited 
by a so-called friendly government. If it were invited it would come, and if it came 

this would pose a danger to the state of Israel. But this will also be a danger to 
American interests. Therefore, it is our position that not only are we totally right on 
this issue but there are no objective foundations for a dispute with the United States. 
{end recording] 


[Schiffer] The chairman of the Ministerial Committee on Autonomy Affairs negotiations, 
Dr Yosef Burg, said that the hardening of the Egyptian position in the negotiations has 
to be viewed against the background of remarks coming from the United States about the 
urgent need to solve the Palestinian problem. Minister Yitzhaq Moda'i asked, What next? 
what will Israel's next moves be in view of the deadlock in the negotiations? 


Yigatel Hurvitz proposed establishing a think tank to try to examine and assess how Israel

Professor Yigatel Yadin again raised the possibility that in the next few months there

These were the main points in the deliberations and, as has been said, the Cabinet came out
