NC 150850 

TRIPOLI SDOMESTIC SERVICE IN ARABIC 0500 GMT 15 MAY 85 

((TEXT)) PRESIDENT JEAN-BAPTISTE BAGAZA OF BURUNDI HELD A 

DINNER IN HONOR OF THE BROTHER LEADER OF THE REVOLUTION AND HIS 

ACCOMPANYING DELEGATION LAST NIGHT. THE DINNER WAS ATTENDED BY 

MEMBERS OF THE CABINET AND OFFICIALS FROM THE UPRONA PARTY. IT 

WAS ALSO ATTENDED BY THE AMBASSADORS OF ARAB AND FOREIGN STATES 

ACCREDITED TO BURUNDI. 

THE BROTHER LEADER OF THE REVOLUTION AND THE PRESIDENT OF 

BURUNDI EXCHANGED ‘SPEECHES. THE FOLLOWING IS THE TEXT OF 

THE SPEECH DELIVERED BY THE BROTHER LEADER: 

IN THE NAME OF GOD. PRESIDENT AND MADAME BAGAZA, 

GOVERNMENT AND PARTY OFFICIALS IN BURUNDI, MEMBERS OF THE 

DIPLOMATIC CORPS, AND BURUNDIAN FRIENDS: I AND MY ACCOMPANYING 

DELEGATION WOULD LIKE TO EXPRESS OUR DEEP GRATITUDE FOR THE WARM 

WELCOME AND THE OFFICIAL AND POPULAR RECEPTION ACCORDED TO US IN 

YOUR COUNTRY, AND THROUGH WHICH THE BURUNDIAN PEOPLE HAVE EXPRESSED 

THEIR SENTIMENTS TOWARD THE LIBYAN ARAB PEOPLE. I WAS ALSO 

MOVED BY THELENGTHY PARAGRAPHS CONCERNING OUR COUNTRY WHICH YOU 

MENTIONED IN YOUR SPEECH. I AGREE WITH YOUR ANALYSIS OF THE 

AFRICAN AND INTERNATIONAL ISSUES. 

OUR MEETING TODAY IN BURUNDI IS AN IMPORTANT STEP FOR 

CONSOLIDATING COOPERATION BETWEEN OUR TWO COUNTRIES AND FOR 

SUPPORTING PROGRESSIVE ATTITUDES. IT ALSO CONTITUTES AN AFRICAN 

RESPONSE TO THE AGENTS OF IMPERIALISM AND ZIONISM, WHO ARE 

MEETING TODAY WITH THEIR RACIST MASTERS. ONE EXAMPLE IS THE 

CURRENT MEETING BETWEEN THE DIRTY AGENT MOBUTU AND HIS 

RACIST ISRAELI MASTERS IN OCCUPIED PALESTINE. HOWEVER, AGENTS 

NEVER LEARN THEIR LESSON. AGENT MOBUTU WILL PAY A HIGH PRICE FOR 

HIS ALLIANCE WITH ZIONISM AND RACISM, THE STAUNCH ENEMIES OF 

AFRICA AND THE ARAB NATION. PEOPLE LIKE MOBUTU AND THE 

LIBERIAN PRESIDENT, WHO HAVE STRENGTHENED THEIR RELATIONS WITH THE 

RACISTS, WILL PAY A PRICE LIKE THAT PAID BY THE SHAH OF IRAN, 

AL~SADAT, AND NUMAYRI. AGENTS SHOULD LEARN A LESSON FROM THE 

FATE OF THESE AGENTS OF ZIONISM, RACISM, AND IMPERIALISM. 

WE MUST WARN THE DIRTY AGENT MOBUTU THAT THE ISRAELIS CANNOT 

SAVE HIM. IF THEY WERE ABLE TO DO SO, THEY WOULD HAVE SAVED THEIR 

NUMBER ONE AGENT, AL-ASAD. SPEAKING HERE ON THE BORDERS OF HIS 

COUNTRY, I DEFY HIM AND I DEFY HIS MASTERS TO SAVE HIM IN THE 

FUTURE IF THEY CAN. MOBUTU HAS THROWN HIMSELF INTO THE 

LAP OF ZIONISM. THIS WILL NOT BENEFIT HIM; IT WILL ONLY SERVE 

TO SHORTEN HIS POLITICAL LIFE. 

IN AN ACT OF CONSPIRACY AGAINST THE PALESTINIAN PEOPLE, 

THE AGENT NUMAYRI RECENTLY SMUGGLED FALASHA JEWS TO OCCUPIED 

PALESTINE. ACCORDING TO THE ARAB PROVERB, THIS WAS THE STRAW 

THAT BROKE THE CAMEL'S BACK. 

THE PUNISHMENT WHICH MOBUTU WILL RECEIVE WILL NOT BE 

WHAT THE TENDENTIOUS IMPERIALISTS WILL INTERPRET FROM THESE WORDS

WE ALSO WANT THIS MEETING TO BE AN INCENTIVE TO AWAKEN

THE TRADITION OF CONVENING AN ANNUAL SUMMIT CONFERENCE OF

NOW IT NO LONGER EXISTS ON THE MAP, HAVING BEEN REPLACED BY

DISAPPEAR AND THE ARAB STATE OF POEESTINE WILL BE 
ESTABLISHED ON ITS RUINS. 
AS THE ARAB PROVERB SAYS: SOME GOOD DOES COME FROM HARMFUL 
THINGS. ALTHOUGH THE PRESENCE OF ZIONISM IN PALESTINE 
AND RACISM IN SOUTH AFRICA IS AN EVIL THING, IT HAS UNIFIED 
THE STRUGGLE OF THE ARAB NATION AND THE AFRICANS AGAINST THE 
RACISTS IN PALESTINE AND SOUTH AFRICA. THIS IS ESPECIALLY 
TRUE SINCE THE COOPERATION BETWEEN THESE TWO RACIST REGIMES HAS 
REACHED A DEGREE INVOLVING THE PRODUCTION OF ATOMIC 
WEAPONS. THE ISRAELIS HAVE DECLARED THAT THANKS TO COOPERATION 
WITH THE RACISTS IN SOUTH AFRICA, THEY HAVE BEEN ABLE TO PRODUCE 
ATOMIC WEAPONS AND HAVE ACTUALLY INSTALLED NUCLEAR MISSILES ON THE 
OCCUPIED SYRIAN GOLAN HEIGHTS AND IN THE NEGEV DESERT. 
EMPIRES HAVE DISAPPEARED, BUT PEOPLE REMAIN. IMPERIALISM, 
RACISM, AND ZIONISM WILL DISAPPEAR JUST AS FASCISM AND NAZISM 
HAVE DISAPPEARED FROM THE HISTORY OF THE WORLD. 
WE KNOW THAT IMPERIALISM AND ZIONISM ARE A NEW 
RESURRECTION OF FASCISM AND NAZISM, BUT THEIR FATE WILL BE THE 
SAME AS THAT OF THE FASCIST AND NAZI EMPIRES. WE HAVE FAITH IN 
THE STRUGGLE OF SMALL PEOPLES AND WE BELIEVE IN THEIR RIGHT TO LIVE 
A WORTHY LIFE ON THIS EARTH AND UNDER THE SUN.

((TEXT)) GOD, AND NOT THE UNITED STATES, CREATED PEOPLE. 

THE NUMERICALLY SMALL LEBANESE PEOPLE HAVE DEFEATED THE UNITED 

STATES AND ITS FLEETS. ONLY THE SEA SAVED THE AMERICANS, 

JUST LIKE THE WHALES. THE AMRERICANS FLED TO THE SEA ON FOOT, 

BELIEVING OUT OF FEAR THAT THEIR FEET WOULD BE QUICKER THAN THEIR 

VEHICLES. THIS ACTUALLY HAPPENED LAST YEAR AS A RESULT OF FIERCE 

LEBANESE POPULAR RESISTANCE. THE ISRAELI INVADERS ARE NOW 

FROM SOUTH LEBANON UNDER THE BLOWS OF THE HEROIC 

LEBANESE PEOPLE'S POPULAR RESISTANCE. WE ARE URGING THE 

FIGHTING TO CONTINUE FROM SOUTH LEBANON TO SOUTH PALESTINE. WE 

APPLAUDED, BROTHER, WHEN YOU DECLARED IN YOUR SPEECH YOUR 

REAL SUPPORT FOR THE PALESTINIAN PEOPLE AND THEIR 

LEGITIMATE STRUGGLE TO REGAIN THEIR LAND. THE PALESTINIANS WILL 

BE VICTORIOUS BECAUSE THEY HAVE THE RIGHT TO RETURN TO THEIR 

TERRITORIES FROM WHICH THEY WERE EXPELLED IN 1948. THE 

PALESTINIANS ARE FORCED TO FIGHT TO RETURN TO THEIR LAND BECAUSE 

NOW THEY HAVE NO LAND OF THEIR OWN. THEY ARE LIVING IN REFUGEE 

CAMPS IN ARAB COUNTRIES WHICH BORDER.OCCUPIED PALESTINE. 

IF THE UNITED STATES RESPECTS ITS POSITION AS A SUPERPOWER, 

THEN IT MUST BECOME ALIGNED WITH THE PERSECUTED AND DISPERSED 

PALESTINIANS. THE UNITED STATES MUST NOT ASSIST THE 

AGGRESSIVE FORCES WHO ARE EVEN PURSUING THE PALESTINIANS INSIDE THE 

REFUGEE CAMPS. IT IS USELESS TO REACH AN UNDERSTANDING WITH THE 

UNITED STATES OR TO DEPEND ON ITS INFLUENCE TO PROMOTE THE ISSUE OF 

PEACE AND JUSTICE BECAUSE THE UNITED STATES SUFFERS FROM 

MEGALOMANIA AND A LACK OF MORALS. THE SMALL PEOPLE, WHO ARE THE 

TARGETS OF IMPERIALISM AND RACISM PARTICULARLY IN THE ARAB 

HOMELAND AND AFRICA, HAVE NO CHOICE BUT TO UNITE AND RESIST THE 

ENEMY. 

OUR MEETING TODAY CONSTITUTES A STEP TOWARD AFRICAN UNITY AND 

AN IMPLEMENTATION OF ((THE OAU ~- FBIS)) CHARTER. ANY 

COOPERATION BETWEEN OUR TWO COUNTRIES IS A STEP TOWARD ACHIEVING 

THE UNITY OF THE AFRICAN PEOPLE. AS YOU HAVE SAID, WE, AT THIS 

MEETING, MUST SAVE AFRICA FROM THE RETURN OF COLONIALISM. WE 

MUST DESTROY COLONIALISM'S OLD POSITIONS IN SOUTH AFRICA AND 

NAMIBIA. THE LIBERATION FORCES IN INDEPENDENT AFRICA SHOULD 

DOUBLE THEIR SUPPORT FOR THE NAMIBIAN PEOPLE WHOSE INDEPENDENCE 

IS BEING THREATENED. THIS THREAT IS HEADED BY THE UNITED STATES 

AND THE WEST. WE RECOGNIZE ONLY SWAPO WHICH IS LEADING THE 

STRUGGLE FOR THE LIBERATION OF NAMIBIA. WE SHOWED ALSO DOUBLE 

OUR SUPPORT FOR THE LIBERATION MOVEMENTS IN SOUTH AFRICA. WE 

DECLARE OUR ABSOLUTE SUPPORT FOR THE BLACK PEOPLE'S ORGANIZED 

UPRISING IN SOUTH AFRICA. WE HAIL THE VICTIMS WHO HAVE 

FALLEN FOR THE SAKE OF FREEDOM THERE. 

WE DEPEND ON OUR FRIEND BAGAZA AND THE NATIONAL UNITY 

MOVEMENT FOR PROGRESS IN BURUNDI TO STAND WITH US IN THE STRUGGLE 

TO LIBERATE AND UNITE AFRICA, AND TO DEFEAT ZIONISM IN
