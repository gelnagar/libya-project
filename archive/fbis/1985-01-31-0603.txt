Bulgaria’s Filipov Arrives in Tripoli for Talks 
LD302337 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1920 GMT 30 Jan 85 


[Text] Tripoli, 30 Jan [JANA] — Grisha Filipov, member of the 
Bulgarian Communist Party Central Committee Politburo and 
chairman of the Council of Ministers, arrived in Tripoli today at 
the head of a delegation on a 4-day visit to the Jamahiriyah. They 
were welcomed at the airport by Staff Maj ‘Abd al-Salam 
Ahmad Jallud, a number of secretaries of the General People’s 
Committee, heads of the political missions in the Jamahiriyah, 
and a number of armed officers. 


In a statement to JANA on arrival, Filipov affirmed the depth 
of relations of friendship and cooperation which bind the Libyan 
Arab and Bulgarian peoples together, relations whose foundation 
was laid by Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi and Chairman 
Zhivkov. 


He pointed out that his visit to Jamahiriyah will center on the 
bolstering of cooperation in the various fie!4s. He added that

‘Angry’ Demonstrations Against U.S. Maneuvers 
LD301744 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
30 Jan 85 


[Text] An angry popular demonstration took place this morning 
in Tripoli against the provocative exercises by the U.S. 6th Fleet 
in Egyptian territorial waters opposite the shores of the Jamahir- 
iyah. 


Huge crowds marched in this demonstration shouting slogans 
which express the Libyan people’s will in defiance of all 
provocations, and opposition to all those who want to harm the 
people’s authority and the revolutionary achievements realized 
in the Jamahiriyah thanks to the Great al-Fatih Revolution and 
its leader. The demonstrations raised slogans denouncing the 
U.S. Government and the collusion of the Egyptian hireling 
regime with that government in carrying out these provocative 
exercises. 


The angry demonstration marched to the Belgian Embassy, 
which looks after U.S. interests in the Jamahiriyah, where a 
statement was read outside the embassy strongly attacking the 
Reagan administration, affirming that it is practicing terrorism 
and that the Libyan Arab people will not be intimidated by 
threats and provocations, and will decisively deter all who want 
to harm them and all their enemies. 


The demonstrators denounced the stance taken by the agent 
Egyptian regime, in collusion with the U.S. Government, and 
affirmed that the Egyptian people will not be lenient with this 
hollow regime, which has squandred Sinai and Tabah and sub- 
mitted to the Zionist enemy, and has become a pawn in the hands 
of the politicians of Washington. 


The statement affirmed that the Libyan people will not be alone 
in the trench fighting the enemies, and will defend their freedom 
to the end, side by side with all living revolutionary forces. The 
statement said that the U.S. fleets will not intimidate us, and we 
will not be intimidated by the voice of the coward Reagan; we 
will not abandon our weapons and will not abandon our help to 
those struggling for freedom. 


Advance to punish the traitors.

and an arrow which penetrates the heart of Zionism. 


An angry popular demonstration was also staged in the town of 
(Furman) this morning, which denounced and condemned the 
provocative U.S. exercises being carried out by the U.S. 6th 
Fleet. Masses of the basic popular congresses in the town partici- 
pated in this demonstration. The demonstrators issued a 
statement denouncing the Reagan government and its terroristic 
policy against the peoples struggling for freedom and who reject

General Giap, the Vietnamese vice premier, participated in the

Popular angry demonstrations were staged also yesterday in 
Sabha and Ujaylat, denouncing the provocative exercises by the 
U.S. 6th Fleet inside the Egyptian territorial waters, northeast 
of Tubrug. The demonstrators carried slogans denouncing the 
acts of terrorism being carried out by the U.S. Government, and 
affirming the readiness of the Libyan Arab people to confront all 
hostile actions. 


The demonstrators affirmed that the U.S. Government is 
spreading terrorism at an extensive level to threaten the freedom 
and stability of peaceful peoples. The masses of the basic popular 
congresses in Sabha and Ujaylat, in statements issued following 
the angry demonstrations, reaffirmed their permanent readiness 
to carry out the death order in defense of their freedom and 
popular authority and achievements, which were realized thanks 
to the Great al-Fatih Revolution in all walks of life.
