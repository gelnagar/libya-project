LIBYA

People’s Congresses Discuss Foreign Policy 
LDI91509 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 19 Dec 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Dec(JANA) — The Basic People’s Congresses, 
the highest authority in Libya, have begun their new session in 
preparation for the upcoming meeting of the General People’s 
Congress, which will formulate the resolutions adopted by the 
Basic Congresses 


A number of extremely important issues at the local, Arab, 
African, and international levels are being discussed 


The agenda to be studied by the Basic Congresses contains a 
number of important foreign policy issues on which decisive 
resolutions will be adopted. The following are the foreign policy 
issues 


1. Consideration of the report submitted by the General People’s 
Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison with 
regard to the following 


A. Changing the UN Charter; 
B. Abolishing the right of veto; 


C. Halting the buildup of nuclear arms, which threatens mankind 
with annthilation 


2. Consideration of the question of the deployment of US 
missiles in southern Italy 


3. Consideration of the events in Lebanon and the definition of 
the Libyan Arab people's position regarding them 


4. Consideration of steps for the achievement of the unity of the 
Arab Maghreb 


5. Formulation of the attitude to be adopted by the Socialist 
People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah toward those African coun- 
tries which have restored relations with the Zionist enemy and 
those contemplating such a move 


6. The question of the failure of the General People’s Committee 
of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison to circulate any 
reports to the Popular Congresses through the General Secretar- 
iat of the General People’s Congress on the following matters
