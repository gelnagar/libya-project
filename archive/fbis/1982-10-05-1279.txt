LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ON AFRICAN, MIDEAST ISSUES 


LD041954 Tripoli JANA in English 1619 GMT 4 Oct 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulhijja 17, October 4, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The press delegation 


accompanying President Saye Zerbo of Upper Volta has held an interview with the 
revolution's leader, Colonel Al-Qadhdhafi, on a number of African, Arab and international 
affairs. 


Answering a question on President Zerbo's visit to Jamahiriyah, Colonel Al-Qadhdhafi 
said, "Meetings between Upper Volta and Libya, similarly to those with all African 
brothers, are always important. Considering what currently faces the OAU, it was 
imperative to meet President Zerbo in order to overcome this impasse. We benefited 
from the exchange of views.... I believe that we have found the solution, which will 
be put forward to the African presidents in the next few days". 


Concerning Libyan-Chadian relations, the leader said, “Regretably, no government has yet 
been stabilized in Chad so as we lay the basis for dealing with it. [as received] for 

a year ago we thought that Goukouni's regime became stable but it collapsed and a few 
months ago we thought Habre's regime became stable but the civil war erupted anew.... 
Until now fierce battles are raging in Ounianga Province, eastern Chad...and we hear 
that there are preparations for other battles. We fear that peace will totally collapse 
in Chad. We regret that Goukouni's regime collapsed after it became stable...and we now 
witness the beginning of the collapse of Habre's regime after we thought that it became 
stable. In any case, our stand is neutral towards this conflict". 


On a question concerning Libya's participation in the Kinshasa Africa-French conference, 
the leader said, "From outset, we are against the idea of grouping the French speaking 
countries, because such a grouping is a traditional continuation of French colonialism 
and a continuation of French hegemony over the French speaking African countries.... 

We strongly oppose it and regard it as a continuation of colonial hegemony, and it is 
contrary to Libya's dignity and independence to attend this meeting or to have any 
relations with it.

In his answer to a question on the Fes summit results, Colonel Al-Qadhdhafi said "It is 
crystal clear to the whole world now, that the Arab countries’ security contradicts with 
the Israeli existence and precisely with Israel as an armed force. Without the disarming 
of Israel, we cannot talk about peace in the region, and despite the Arab recognition of 
Israel, the latter refused to recognise the Arabs". 


Answering a question on the savage Zionist invasion of Lebanon, Colonel Al-Qadhdhafi said, 
"The Israelis are now working towards the transformation of Lebanon into another Israeli 
colony after Palestine". 


On the relations between the Jamahiriyah and USA, the leader said, "We are a neutral 
country and play a leading role in the Non-aligned Movement. We would like to have equal 
relations with the big powers, but unfortunately, America wants us to be her slaves and 
not friends. She only has either slaves or enemies, and we refuse to be her slaves and 
therefore she classifies us as an enemy. This is the American decision". 


Finally, one of the journalists asked the leader saying, "In spite of our short stay in 
your country, we were enchanted by your country. What are the secrets behind such great 


achievements?" Colonel Mu'amar ai-Qadhdhafi answered him saying, "It is the revolution". 


JALLUD RETURNS FROM VISIT TO ETHIOPIA, PDRY 


LD041016 Tripoli JANA in English 0928 GMT 4 Oct 82 


[Text] Tripola, Dhulhijja 17, October 4, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Staff Major ‘Abd 
as-Salam Ahmad Jallud last night arrived here after concluding his visits to Ethiopia 
and Democratic Yemen. 


In Addis Ababa, Staff Major Jallud attended the six-membered liaison committee set up by 
the great African summit held in Jamahiriyah last August. He also handed important letters 


from the revolution's leader, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi to President Julius Nyerere of Tanzania, 
President Mengistu Haile Mariam of socialist Ethiopia, President Denis Sassou-Nguesso of 
the People's Republic of Congo, and President Kenneth Kaunda of Zambia. 


In Aden, Staff Major Jallud held talks with ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, president and prime 
minister of Democratic Yemen. They discussed measures to be taken to confront the Zionist 
scheme, which is threatening the Arab nation's security and existence.
