AL-QADHDHAFI ON PEOPLE'S CONGRESS AGENDA 
JANA ACCUSES REUTER OF 'FABRICATING' AL-QADHDHAFI SPEECH 
LDO4 1608 
TRIPOLI JANA IN ARABIC 1445 GMT 4 JAN 88 
((TEXT)) TRIPOLI. 4 JAN (JANA)--REUTER HAS REPORTED A STATEMENT 
WHICH, IT CLAIMED, WAS INCLUDED IN THE SPEECH BY THE BROTHER 
LEADER OF THE REVOLUTION ON THE OCCASION OF THE START OF THE 
CONVENING OF PEOPLE'S BASIC CONGRESSES IN THE GREAT JAMAHIRIYAH. 
THE AGENCY RESORTED TO FABRICATING CLAUSES AND FALSELY ATTRIBUTING 
THEM TO THE BROTHER LEADER OF THE REVOLUTION. FOR EXAMPLE, IT 
SAID IN A REPORT ISSUED ON SUNDAY, 3 JANUARY, AND ENTITLED 
"AL-QADHDHAFI CALLS ON LIBYANS TO TURN THEIR WEAPONS AGAINST 
ARABS WHO RECOGNIZE ISRAEL" THAT THE BROTHER LEADER SAID: 
LET THE WORLD GO TO HELL IF WE CANNOT SECURE OUR FOOD...AND SO ON. 
WE AT JANA ARE UNABLE TO INTERPRET REUTER'S BEHAVIOR AS OTHER THAN 
ILL-INTENTIONED AND AS PART OF THE CAMPAIGNS OF DECEPTION AND 
INTRIGUES AIMED AT DISTORTING THE BROTHER LEADER OF THE REVOLUTION'S 
IMAGE AND HARMING THE GREAT JAMAHIRIYAH'S STANDS AND INTERNATIONAL 
POLICY. 
(ENDALL) 041445 BRADY/PG 04/1714Z JAN
