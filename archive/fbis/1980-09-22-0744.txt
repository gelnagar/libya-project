LIBYA

TROOPS REPORTEDLY GOING TO SYRIA ‘WITHIN 2 WEEKS' 
NC220744 Paris AFP in English 0743 GMT 22 Sep 80 
[Text] Beirut, 22 Sep (AFP)--Libyan military units with full equipment will take up posi- 
ions along the Syrian border with Israel within two weeks and a squadron of Libyan Phantoms 


[as received] will’move to a Syrian airbase at the same time, the Lebanese daily AL-BAYRAQ 
reported today. 


Quoting a well-informed Arab source, the paper said the moves followed an agreement of 
principle between the respective military commands following the announcement of union 
between the two countries made earlier this month. 


AL-BAYRAQ did not give details of how many Libyan troops would go to Syria, but said 
they would be bringing sophisticated weapons with them.
