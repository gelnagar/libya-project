LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ADVISES KING HUSAYN TO FIGHT ISRAEL 


LD292242 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 29 Jan 82 


[Text] Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great September Revolution made 
a special statement to JANA in which he said: We thought the call for volunteers by 
King Husayn of Jordan today would be for the liberation of Palestine and holy Jerusalem 
which can be seen from the house of King Husayn and not for the purpose of fighting 
against Iran. 


The leader went on to say: Our analysis of Arab conditions is that the United States 
is riding the Arab horse and it has begun to lead it in its chosen direction to serve 
its own and Zionist aims. 


The brother leader noted in his statement that Arab countries whose existence is 
threatened by the Zionist entity have begun to exchange the real enemy for another enemy. 
They have begun to turn their backs on the real enemy of the Arab nation and they con- 
tinue to publicize this serious matter which serves U.S. and Israeli aims. 


The leader added: This demagogy reveals che historical farces being enacted in the 
lean Arab theater. The proper thing is for all Arab rifles to point at the Zionist 
enemy, which is threatening Arab existence, and to leave Iran alone. At the same time, 
we have our stand on this war between two Islamic states, Iraq and Iran. 


The leader said: From the start, we were against this war and we are also against the 
occupation of even one foot of Iraqi territory. We are also against the enslavement 

of any Iraqi by Iran. Iran knows this and we do not think it is thinking about doing 

this. We are not with Iran in any attempt on its part to occupy even one foot of Arab 
territory or to enslave any number of Iraqi individuals. We also are against Iraq occupying 
any Iranian territory. 


The leader explained that our efforts will always be directed toward ending this war 
and resorting to wisdom and reason. We declare our responsibility because we are ready 
to stop this war if the Arabs decide to fight the Israeli enemy -- the real common 
enemy -- to liberate their occupied territory. 


COMMENTARY CRITICIZES HAIG'S MIDDLE EAST TOUR 


LD300416 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 29 Jan 82 


{Station commentary ] 


[Excerpts] U.S. Secretary of State Alexander Haig returned to the Arab region in con- 
tinuation of his previous tour. During Haig's previous visit, he toured a number of 
Arab states in addition to the Zionist entity where he unexpectedly cut short his visit 
and returned to Washington. Haig has returned to the Arab area. He began his tour with 
a visit to occupied Palestine where he met with leaders of the Zionist enemy including 
the terrorist Menahem Begin.

What attracts attention is the fact that the tours of U.S. officials have intensified since 
the execution of As-Sadat. It becomes clear from this tour that the U.S. administration -- 
which has been and still is the major side to the treasonous Camp David agreements and all 
the resulting liquidationist schemes such as the autonomy conspiracy -- is eager to main- 
tain these agreements because they perpetuate the Zionist presence, liquidate the Palestinian 
issue in favor of the Zionist enemy and maintain U.S. interests in the Arab region. 


The tours of U.S. administration officials have become so numerous because this administra- 
tion insists on marching along the path of the Camp David agreements until it realizes its 
purpose and objective at the expense of the Arab nation and the Arab nation's legitimate 
rights. The new Haig tour comes at a time when there is much talk about the possibility 

of the Zionist enemy committing an aggression against south Lebanon, which might cover a 
target beyond the south, namely, the liquidation of the Palestinian revolution and the 
imposition of new conditions on the entire Arab region. These new conditions would fully 
favor the enemy especially now that his appetite has become more insatiable with the annex- 
ation of the Golan Heights which received the blessings of the U.S. administration. 


The new Haig tour, observers believe, is another link in the chain of U.S.-Zionist con- 
spiracies against the Arab nation. One can look at this tour from the U.S. angle to see 
that Haig is participating in the Zionist expansionist steps to annex the West Bank and 
the Gaza Strip under the guise of the autonomy conspiracy now that the U.S. administration 
has used the veto to stop penalizing it. 


Haig has come to the area to encourage the Zionist enemy toward further occupation and toward 
making such an occupation a fait accompli. Haig also has come to give his blessing to the 
annexation of the Golan; this is a new attempt to establish U.S. domination over the Arab 
region. This domination is based on a strategic alliance with the Zionist enemy and 
(?blackmailing) some Arab states while intimidating others. The Zionist enemy and the 

U.S. administration are two sides of the same coin. If Haig's mission is to promote this 
coin in the Arab region, the Arabs will have to prove to the world that this currency is 
co.uterfeit and is unlawful for circulation. 


The current state in which U.S. officials tours of the region have become too frequent is, 
in the opinion of observers, the most serious thing that the Arab nation has had to face in 
its contemporary history or rather the greatest thing that this nation has ever had to face 
in its long history. The danger arises from the Zionist enemy and his U.S. ally as well as 
from the plundering of Arab rights. This Zionist state is a direct daily threat and there 
is no one who can belittle its enormity except those who are traitors to the Arab nation. 
There is no one who can deny that Haig's tour, which ended today with talks in Cairo, is 
playing the role other U.S. officials have played, namely, defining the tasks assigned to 
the Zionist state in addition to what is happening as a result of separating the Arab East 
from the Arab West, standing in the way of achieving the fateful Arab unity, working with 
other hostile imperialist and colonialist forces to destroy the Arab entity and preventing 
the Arab nation's liberation and progressive advance.

MUBARAK CAUTIONS NUMAYRI ON RELATIONS WITH U.S. 


LD311908 Tripoli JANA in English 1800 GMT 31 Jan 82 


{Text] Beirut, 31 Jan (JANA) -- The Lebanese newspaper AN-NAHRI said today that the 
Egyptian President Husni Mubarak has advised the Sudanese ruler Ja'far Numayri to 
exercise caution in his dealings with the United States in view of the current situation 
through which the Sudan is passing. Mubarak is believed to have told Numayri to exercise 
caution especially in providing unlimited military facilities to the U.S. 


The Lebanese paper said the Egyptian president promised Numayri that he will bring up 
the subject of aid for the Sudan in talks with Western European leaders. The paper 

said Mubarak apparently told Numayri during their meeting in Aswan that Egypt will 

send oil supplies to Sudan to cover the serious shortfall in the country's fuel supplies.

CORRESPONDENT SAYS CHAD FORCES FOIL REBEL ATTACK 


LD311516 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 31 Jan 82 


[Text] Ndjamena, 31 Jan (JANA) -- Chad Government forces have checked the Northern 
Forces and foiled an attempt they made to occupy Fada. A JANA correspondent reported 
that the government forces succeeded in checking the Northern Forces following a grinding 
battle and fierce fighting. The Chad forces have been able to recapture the Mangalme 
area from the Northern Forces who had occupied the area last week.
