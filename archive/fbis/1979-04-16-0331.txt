LIBYA

CUBAN DELEGATION ARRIVES FOR TALKS ON COOPERATION 


LD140940 Tripoli JANA in English 0815 GMT 14 Apr 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, April 14, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Mr. Hector Rodriguez Llompart, 

Cuba's chairman of the State Committee for Economic Cooperation, arrived here yesterday 

at the head of a delegation on an official visit of a few days to the Jamahiriyah. He will 
hold talks on promoting bilateral cooperation, 


The Cuban delegation was received at Tripoli International Airport by Abu Bakar 
ash-Sharif, secretary of the General People's Committee for Economy, a number of directors 
at the Foreign Secretariat, a representative from the Protocol Department and the Cuban 
ambassador to the Jamahiriyah.
