BOMBING OF LIBYAN BOEING 727 FLYING FROM BENGHAZI TO TRIPOLI IN DECEMBER 1992
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/0000218771

Summary (AI): A March 1994 CIA information report says that on 21 December 1992 a regularly scheduled Libyan Airlines Boeing 727 flying from Benghazi to Tripoli collided with a military aircraft while landing at Tripoli International Airport, with no survivors aboard the airliner. Qadhafi publicly suggested America or the Irish Republican Army might be responsible. Much of the text is redacted.
• The collision occurred on 21 December 1992, the anniversary of the Pan Am 103 bombing.
• The Boeing 727 carried 150 to 156 passengers and a number of crew members.
• The report is an information report, not finally evaluated intelligence; the document is dated 4 March 1994 and the DQI period runs to 12 October 1993.
• Parts of the text are redacted.
• 1992-12-21: Regularly scheduled Libyan Airlines Boeing 727 from Benghazi collided with a military aircraft on landing approach; both aircraft exploded and were destroyed; no survivors aboard the 727, which carried 150 to 156 passengers and crew; two crew members ejected safely (partly redacted).
