LIBYA

PRESENCE OF NORTH KOREAN PILOTS, SOVIET 'ADVISERS! REPORTED 
Paris DEFENSE INTERARMEES in French Sep 79 p 9 WA 


[Text] Two squadrons of the Libyan Air Force based at Banbah which are equipped with 
MIG-21's have been made available to North Korean personnel who are acting not only as 
pilots for these aircraft but also taking care of their maintenance. It should also 
be noted that the number of Soviet "advisers" in Libya is soon to be increased from 
2,000 to 5,500.
