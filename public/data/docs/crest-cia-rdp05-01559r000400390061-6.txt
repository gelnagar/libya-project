((TEXT)) AT PRESENT, TUNISIA, LIBYA, ALGERIA, MOROCCO, 

SUDAN, IRAQ, AND EGYPT -- INDEED, EVERY STATE -- CANOT 

SOLVE ITS OWN PROBLEMS OF DEVELOPMENT WITHIN AN ARAB 

FRAMEWORK. WE CAN HARDLY ACHIEVE SUCCESSES IN THIS 

FIELD IN THE PRESENT AGE. 

THE INCOME OF THE ENTIRE ARAB NATION, OF ALL THE ARAB 

COUNTRIES, IS LESS THAN THAT OF IRALY. BUT THEY DECEIVE 

THE PEOPLE, THESE NAIVE ONES WHOSE CLOTHES ARE 

SMEARED WITH BLACK OIL. THEY TELLING THEM: YOU ARE RICH, 

YOU ARE AMONG THE BIG POWES IN THE WORLD AND YOU ARE RICH. 

BUT THEY ARE POOR AND THE OTHER COUNTRIES ONLY TRICK THEM. 

ALL THE OVERBLOWN INCOMES OF THE ARABS, INCLUDING THE BLACK 

OIL, IF BROUGHT TOGETHER, BECOME LESS THAN THE INCOME 

OF ITALY. 

REVENUE TODAY IS IN THE HANDS OF A HANDFUL OF RULERS WHO 

USE IT TO BUY PRIVATE PLANES, PRIVATE PALACES, PRIVATE 

TRIPS, AND PRIVATE CARS. AND SO THEY APPEAR TO BE RICH -- EVEN 

RICHER THAN THE [TALIANS AND GERMANS. BUT IN FACT, THE NATIONAL 

INCOME OF ALL THE ARABS PUT TOGETHER IS LESS THAN THE NATIONAL 

INCOME OF ITALY. 

AS A MATTER OF FACT, THE ISSUE OF UNITY IS ONE OF LIFE AND 

DEATH AS FAR AS THE ARABS ARE CONCERNED. THEREFORE WE MUST 

FIGHT FOR THE SAKE OF ARAB UNITY IN ORDER TO GUARANTEE THE 

FREEDOM OF THE ARABS OVER THEIR LAND AND TO GUARANTEE AN ADVANCED 

LIFE FOR THEM. WHATEVER WE DO, WITHOUT UNITY WE WOULD BE 

PLOUGHING THE SEA. 

SAUDI ARABIA, THE RICHEST OIL COUNTRY, IS NOW ZERO IN 

EVERYTHING. IF IT DOES PRODUCE AGRICULTURAL PRODUCE, IT IS 

PRODUCED AT MANY TIMES ITS PRICE. IF THE WORLD PRICE OF A 

KILO OF ORANGES IS 20 PIASTERS, IT IS PRODUCED OR 

SOLD IN SAUDI ARABIA FOR 5 DINARS, FOR EXAMPLE. THIS IS A 

LOSING DEAL. PRODUCTION SHOULD BE ((WORDS INDISTINCT)), 

IT SHOULD BE LESS THAN 20 PIASTERS, NOT 1 DINAR -- OR 2, OR 3, 

OR 4. THIS IS A LOSING OPERATION. 

TRAINING, HOWEVER, IS CONTINUING IN ORDER TO ENHANCE ARAB 

CONSCIOUSNESS. THE PROGRAM OF ACENTRY, DIVISION, TREASON, 

THE DISRUPTION OF THE ARAB NATION'S CAPABILITIES, CONTINUES 

TO SERVE THE INTERESTS OF THE FORCES OPPOSED TO THIS NATION WHICH 

HOPE TO TRANSFORM THIS STRATEGIC REGION INTO A REGION 

TO SERVE THEIR INTERNATIONAL CONFLICT AND TO EXPLOIT THESE 

CAPABILITIES FOR THE GOOD OF THE PROGRAMS OF THE COUNTRIES HOSTILE 

TO THIS NATION. FURTHERMORE, THE ARAB NATION IS TODAY BEING 

SUBJECTED TO BALKANIZATION. 

THESE ARE DISEASES WHICH HAVE BEEN AFFLICTING THIS BODY. 

NO ONE COULD RAISE THE IDEA OF PARTITIONING LEBANON INTO SATELETS, 

EGYPT INTO A COPTIC STATE, AN ISLAMIC STATE, AN UPPER EGYPT STATE, 

A LOWER EGYPT STATE OR A PHAROAH STATE, OR CCNTEMPLATE DIVIDING 

SYRIA, IRAQ, OR ALGERIA IF IT WERE NOT FOR THE WEAKNESS OF 

ARAB NATIONALISM AND THE WEAKNESS OF THE NATIONALIST TIDE. 

THE REVOLUTION HAS BEEN HALTED. THE ARAB BODY HAS BEEN DRUGGED 

AND IS INCAPICITATED: SYSTEMS OF OPENNESS, DIVISION, 

ARTIFICIAL CONFLICTS, AND FALSE DEMOCRACIES PREVAIL.

THE MASSES HAVE BEEN STRIPPED OF ALL THEIR POTENTIAL. 

THESE POTENTIALS ARE NOW IN THE HANDS OF THOSE FORCES WHICH CONSIDER

DOMINATE THE ARAB MASSES AND CONSEQUENTLY THEY ARE ENEMIES OF THESE 
MASSES. THIS IS BECAUSE THEY WANT TO DOMINATE THE MASSES. 

IN ORDER TO CONTINUE IN POWER, THEY MUST WITHDRAW ALL THE 

POTENTIAL SO AS TO KEEP THE MASSES WEAK. THEY HAVE THE POWER 

AND CONSEQUENTLY THE WITHDRAWING OF ARMS FROM THE MASSES 

CONTINUES AND SO DOES THE WITHDRAWAL OF WEALTH AND POWER. 

THESE ARE USED AGAINST THE MASSES TO CONSOLIDATE THESE REGIMES. 

IN FACT, THE ARAB REGIMES HAVE NOW LOST ALL JUSTIFICATION FOR 

FOR THEIR EXISTENCE, BECAUSE THEY ARE BASED ON A CORRUPT THEORY, 
THE THEORY OF REPRESSION AND EXPLOITATION. THEY ARE BASED ON THE 
THEORY OF GOVERNMENT WHICH HAS BEEN BYPASSED BY THE AGE OF THE 
MASSES. 

THE ARAB RULERS TODAY HAVE NO EXCUSE OR ARGUMENT IN THEIR 

FAVOR. THIS IS BECAUSE ALL THEIR THEORIES SPIRAL ((LAWLABIYAH) ); 
CAPITULATIONIST, OPEN-DOOR, REPRESSIVE, AND FASCIST -- ALL HAVE 
BEEN TRIED AND TESTED BUT THEY COULD NOT SOLVE THE PROBLEMS OF 
PROGRESS, PROSPERITY, AND DEVELOPMENT NOR DID THEY LIBERATE 

THE ARAB LAND. 

THE ARABS HAVE BEEN DEFEATED, THE PEOPLE HAVE BECOME 

SHACKLED, AND THE RULERS ARE PROSTRATE BEFORE AMERICA. THE 

RULERS RUN TO THE AMERICAN WHITE HOUSE TO OFFER ALLEGIANCE TO EVERY 
NEW AMERICAN PRESIDENT ACROSS THE ATLANTIC, ACROSS THE SEA OF 
DARKNESS, AND TO DISGRACEFULLY BEG FOR SOLUTIONS FROM 

HIM.
