LIBYA

INA: IRAQI EMBASSY EMPLOYEE IN TRIPOLI SLAIN 
JN171727Y Baghdad INA in Arabic 171% GMT 17 Aug 78 JN 


[Text] Tripoli, 17 August--An employee at the Iraqi Embassy in Tripoli, Libya, was the 
target of a treacherous, cowardly attack as he was entering the embassy building. 


The employee, Husayn Mihammad 'Ali, was hit by three bullets this morning and succumbed 
to his wounds a few minutes after entering a hospital. 


Libyan citizens, in cooperation with the embassy staff, managed to capture the killer. 
was handed over to Libyan security authorities, who have not yet given any information 
about the killer or his identity.
