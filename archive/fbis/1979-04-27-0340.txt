LIBYA

PROTOCOL SIGNED WITH MAURITANIA DETAILED 


LD261620 Tripoli JANA in English 1423 GMT 26 Apr 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, April 26, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Our correspondent learnt that the 
Libyan Arab-Mauritanian protocol signed at the end of the recent visit of Ahmed Ould 
Abdallah, the Mauritanian foreign minister, to the Jamahiriyah, dealt with the differ- 
ent aspects of cooperation between the two countries and the setting up of several 
joint projects. 


The text of the protocol included Mauritania's undertaking to hand the part of the 
Sahara it occupied back to the liberation front of Saghij Hamra and Rio de Oro, 


Both sides discussed the Sahara issue in detail. The Mauritanian side reaffirmed its 
country's open stand regarding Mauritania's recognition of the right of the Saharan 
people to self-determination and its readiness to hold negotiations with the West 
Saharan liberation front in Tripoli. 


The Mauritanian side expressed its country's intentions and concern to establish good 
and stable relations with Morocco and Algeria, stressing that Mauritania is not in 
alliance with Morocco against Algeria and vice versa, 


Both sides had agreed to hold negotiations between Mauritania and the liberation front 
of Saghij Hamra and Rio de Oro in Tripoli on 26 May 1979 in order to sign a peace 
treaty that ensures a permanent peace between them and lay down the methods to put it 
into force,

Both sides expressed their intentions to continue consultations and strengthen rela- 
tions between them for the mutual interests of both peoples as well as discussing 
national and African issues, 


The protocol affirmed the stand of both countries against all imperialist alliances 
in the area and condemned such alliances, They also stressed their commitment to im- 
plement the resolutions of the conference of Arab foreign and economic ministers held 
recently in Baghdad, 


Both sides have agreed to hold a meeting for the joint committee in Nouakchott at the 
end of next June,

BULGARIAN METALLURGY, MINERAL RESOURCES MINISTER LEAVES 


LD261728 Tripoli JANA in English 0828 GMT 26 Apr 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 26 April, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Bulgarian minister of mineral 
resources, Mr Stamen Stamenov, ended his visit to the Jamahiriyah which lasted a few 
days yesterday. 


He was seen off at Tripoli International Airport by the secretary of heavy industries, 
Mr 'Umar al-Muntasir, the Bulgarian ambassador to the Jamahiriyah and a representative 
from the Protocol Department at the Foreign Secretariat, 


In a statement to our correspondent, Mr Stamenov expressed his pleasure at visiting 
the Jamahiriyah and said that during his visit the two countries had decided to pro- 
mote bilateral cooperation in industry as well as to set up a joint electricity corpo-
