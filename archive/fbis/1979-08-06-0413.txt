LIBYA

ZIMBABWE PATRIOTIC FRONT'S MUGABE CONCLUDES VISIT 
LD042112 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1925 GMT 4 Aug 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 4 Aug--Robert Mugabe, cochairman of the Patriotic Front for the Libera- 
tion of Zimbabwe, left Tripoli this afternoon after a visit to the Jamahiriyah lasting 
several days, during which he discussed ways of insuring escalation of the armed struggle 
against the racist regimes in southern Africa as the sole road to liberation, Mr 
Mugabe's talks also dealt with discussing the means that insure standing up to all the 
capitulationist solutions devised by colonialism and reaction for the sake of perpetuat- 
ing minority rule in southern Africa, 


Mr Mugabe praised the courageous and firm stands of the Great First of September Revolu- 
tion toward the just causes and issues of the peoples struggle against colonialism and for 
freedom, noting the great appreciation of the Zimbabwe African national union fighters for 
the noble stands of the Libyan Arab people toward the cause of liberating Africa and 
defeating colonialism, 


In a statement to JANA he said that the African peoples today are in dire need of unity 
under the leadership of their progressive and revolutionary forces to destroy their common 
enemy embodied in imperialism and racism, 


Mr Mugabe affirmed the full support of the people of Zimbabwe for the Arab people of 
Palestine in their struggle for the recovery of their land and establishment of an inde- 
pendent state on the whole of their land, He affirmed that the Arab and African struggle 
is one struggle against one enemy who is supported by the imperialist forces headed by 
the United States, 


Mr Mugabe praised the prominent and effective roie played by the Great First of September 
Revolution in supporting the national liberation movements which are fighting for libera- 
tion and independence, He said that the Jamahiriyah may be far from Zimbabwe geographi- 
cally, but it is the nearest to us from the revolutionary viewpoint, It constitutes an 
important part of the human struggle to liquidate evil, aggression and the enemies of man, 


In conclusion, Mr Mugabe affirmed the stand of all free men and revolutionaries in the 
world alongside the Great First of September Revolution against the imperialist and 
reactionary forces which are trying in vain to strike the progressive and revolutionary 
forces in the area, encircle and abort them, 


Mr Robert Mugabe was seen off at Tripoli International Airport by the acting chairman 
of the Foreign Liaison Bureau,
