LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES KHOMEYNI REPLY 


LD092304 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 9 Mar 83 


[Text] The brother commander of the revolution has received a message of thanks and 
appreciation from the imam, Ayatollah Khomeyni, in reply to the message the brother 
commander sent on the occasion of the anniversary of the victory of the Iranian Islamic 
Revolution. In his message, Ayatollah Khomeyni affirmed that the Iranian Islamic Revolu- 
tion, which was based on the masses, has been the target of enemies who hatch all sorts 
of plots against it and who provide all the necessary help and facilities needed for 
harming it. He said that Saddam's imposed war is the best proof of this. Ayatollah 
Khomeyni added that, by supplying constant material and military aid to the collapsing 
Iraqi regime, these enemies have been preventing the ending of the war and preventing 

the rescuing of the Iraqi people from the evils of this bloody regime. 


EGYPT'S CALL FOR LIBYAN SUSPENSION RIDICULED 


LDO91543 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 9 Mar 83 


[Text] The Egyptian regime has failed to find an excuse for continuing its membership 
in the Nonaligned Movement and has had to resort to hallucinations to escape the firm 
stand being taken for dismissing it from the movement. The Egyptian Government's MENA 
said that the Egyptian regime has asked the seventh summit conference of the nonaligned 
countries in New Delhi to consider suspending Libya's membership in the movement. This 
request by the Egyptian regime was met with continuous laughter in the conference hall 
as it reflects the conviction of the nonaligned countries that the Egyptian regime is 
an aligned regime and that this regime, with its connections with the racist Zionist 
entity, and its alliance with the U.S. Administration, has lost its presence in this 
movement which was originally established on the basis of rejecting alliances and 
spreading prosperity and peace.

Mubarak's regime, as numerous delegations participating in the conference stated, is 
aligned to the United States. Egypt, because of its clear alignment to the United 
States, is no longer a nonaligned country. 


While the Egyptian request was met with ridicule because it was -- from the nonaligned 
countries’ point of view -- an expression of the Egyptian regime's misunderstanding of 
the principles and aims of the movement, there is no doubt that Libya is considered a 
unique example in nonalignment as it is not bound by any subordinate relation with any 
major state as is the case with the Egyptian regime. Libya is an independent state, 
and it is considered to be an example for the independent states in the world. 


Political observers in the Indian capital think that the Egyptian regime has entered a 
new vortex of political hallucinations and that it does not understand the first thing 
about political concepts. It cannot distinguish between alignment and nonalignment.
