He called for an Arab summit in Tripoli, criticised U.S. withholding of Libyan-purchased aircraft, and praised Soviet support. Nothing here is located in Tripoli itself.
• Qadhdhafi said the U.S. was holding Libyan military transport aircraft paid for by Libya and manufactured for Libya, and called for their delivery.
• Qadhdhafi said the U.S. refused to hand over goods Libya had paid for, while the Soviet Union sold goods on installment terms.
• Qadhdhafi said America had no justification for not accepting a Libyan ambassador.
• Libya and Tunisia signed an agreement referring the continental shelf dispute to the International Court of Justice.
• 1977-06-11: Qadhdhafi speech in Darnah marking the seventh anniversary of the evacuation of U.S. forces; called for an Arab summit in Tripoli with a five-point agenda.
