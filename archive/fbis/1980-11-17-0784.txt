LIBYA

GENERAL STAFF COMMAND ISSUFS CALL FOR VOLUNTEERS 
LD141914 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 14 Nov 
{[fext] Here is en announcement concerning the acceptance of Arab volunteers: 


Out of the revolutionary unity role undertaken by the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah in its capacity as the nucleus of unity, the state of all Arabs, in affirmation 
of the right of every free Arab to have the honor of defending tne Arab nation, liberating 
its soil and saving its sanctities from the desecration of the imperialist presence and 
out of the belief of the great 1 September revolution that the hour of deliverance has 
drawn near and that it is time for this nation to waken from its slumber and rise in the 
face of the intruding foreigner, the General Staff command calls on all Arab airmen and 
technicians whose military service has been terminated for one reason or another to 
volunteer or service in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah Armed Forces. 

They will have the opportunity of receiving sophisticated and advanced training in 
preparing for the major battle of the Arabs, the battle of freedom, honor and dignity. 


The call of the jihad has been issued. The day of victory is near. Heed the call of duty 
and rise to defend Arab honor and Arab dignity. 


SECRETARIAT FORMULATES NEW ARAB CITIZENSHIP LAW 

LD152140 Tripoli JANA in English 1800 GMT 15 Nov 80 

[Text] Tripoli, 15 Nov (JANA)--In implementation of the resolutions and recommendations 
of people's congresses which held their extraordinary session between 23-30 shawwal 
1389, coinciding 2-9 September 1980, the General Secretariat of the General People's 
Congress has drawn up the following citizenship law: 

Law No 18, 1980 regarding citizenship. General People's Congress. In implementation of 
the resolutions and recommendations made by people's congresses during the extraordinary 
session held between 23-30 shawwal 1389, coinciding September 2-9, 1980, the following 
law has been formulated: 


First article: 


The Arab nationality is that to which citizens of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah belong. 


Second article: 

The Arab nationality is the right of any Arab entering Libyan territories and wishing to 
obtain this citizenship. Those considered as being Arabs for the purpose of the applica- 
tion of this law are the following: 

A) Holders of citizenship of any Arab country. 


B) Those whose adherence or parents' adherence to the Arab nation is proven. 


Third article 


This law does not apply to Palestinian Arabs.

Fourth article: 


Arabs who obtain Ar7b citizenship according to this law will enjoy the rights of 
citizens of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, be responsible for the 
same responsibilities in accordance with laws of the Jamahiriyah. 


Fifth article: 


It is possible to grant Arab citizenship under conditions and situations stated by 
the bylaws of individual categories listed below: 


A) Those who have Arab mothers married to citizens not of the Socialist Peoples Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah. 


B) Highly specialised scientists with experience in fields needed by the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


C) Alien women married to Arabs holding Arab citizenships, on condition their foreign 
citizenship is dropped. The secretary of the General People's Commictee for Justice 
can prevent the granting of Arab citizenship for justifiable reasons. He also has 
the right to withdraw the Arab citizenship from those who have not been married for 

a minimum period of two years. 


Such women do not lose their Arab citizenship at the conclusion of their marriages 
unless they get married to foreigners, made their normal residence abroad, or regained 
their foreign citizenship. 


Children of alien women holding Arab citizenship by virtue of their marriages to 
citizens of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah from other marriages 
will not be granted Arab citizenship. 


Sixth article: 


A woman with Arab citizenship who marries an alien can retain her Arab citizenship 
unless she desires to take up her husband's citizenship. 


Any woman experiencing this particular situation can, however, regain Arab citizenship 
should her marriage be, for any reason, terminated, by informing the General People's 
Committee secretary of justice of her desire, provided that she drops her alien 
citizenship. 


Seventh article: 


The Arab citizenship of Libyan citizens is ascertained upon producing a valied certificate 


of citizenship. The granting of Arab citizenship to other individuals is made upon 
a decision taken by the General People's Committee upon the recommendation of the 
General People's Committee secretary of justice. 


Arab citizenship is only granted after the applicant forgoes his previous citizenship. 
Eighth article: 


Anyone holding an Arab citizehship loses his citizenship if he decides to acquire 
a foreign citizenship (2unless) permitted to do so by the General People's Committee 
for Justice. 


If the father of a family loses his Arab citizenship all his children who are under 
legal age likewise lose their citizenship. However, they can regain their Arab 
citizenship by notifying the secretary of the General People's Committee for Justice 
the year they reach legal age.
