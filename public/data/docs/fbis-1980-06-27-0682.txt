LIBYA

HAWATIMAH'S MESSAGE TO AL-QADHDHAFI DENOUNCES AS-SADAT 
LD231724 Tripoli JANA in English 1430 GMT 23 Jun 80 


[Text] Tripoli, June 23, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Gadhdhafi, 
leader of the Great First of September Revolution, received a message from Nayif 
Hawatimah, secretary general of Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine, 
denouncing the Egyptian president's act of imposing a state of emergency along the 
borders with the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


Mr Hawatimah pledged the firm support and solidarity of the front and Palestinian fighters 
with the Libyan Arab people and their great revolution against any hostile attempt by the 
Egyptian regime. 


The messeag added: “The Egyptian move clearly shows the real crisis of this treacherous 
regime which is trying to distract the Egyptian people away from their real struggle and 
internal problems on the one hand, and the deadlock of the U.S.-Zionist plots and schemes 
designed in the Camp David accords on the other. 


He concluded: "We in the Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine and the 
Palestinian revolution will fight side by side with our Libyan Arab people and their 
revolution against any conspiracy". 


TENSION INCREASES ALONG LIBYAN-EGYPIIAN BORDER 
LD261326 Kuwait KUNA in English 1117 GMf 26 Jun 80 


[Text] Kuwait, June 26 (KUNA)--The commander of the Libyan forces on the front with 
Egypt said that Libya would not be the first to launch war but he warned that his 
country had enough power to repulse any Egyptian attack. 


The unidentified officer told AR-RA'Y AL-!AMM in an interview published here Thursday 
that Libya would strongly counter any attack by the Egyptian forces and would inflict 
on them heavy losses as Libya owns weapons capable of causing total destruction. 


Tension has been increasing along the Egyptian-Libyan borders since President Anwar 
as-Sadat annourced last week deployment of troops on the western front. Libya accused 
As-Sadat of planning to launch war against it in implemertation of a U.S.-Israel 
scheme and to divert the attention of the Egyptian public from internal problems. 


The Libyan officer said that Egypt had deployed large forces comprising six troops 
divisions, six armoured divisions, three paratroop battalions, three divisions for 
air defence supported by Mirage, Sokhoi and MiG-21 war planes, in addition to a 
number of helicopters and 40 naval units. 


The commander refuted Egyptian claims that the Egyptian forces were deployed in defensive 
positions against any Libyan attack and wondered as how could it be that those well- 
equipped forces of 12 divisions were deployed for defence and not for attack. 


The newspaper correspondent in Tripoli reported that he watched what he called war 
preparations being undertaken along the Libyan-Egyptian borders. He said that daily 
reinforcements, including tanks, armoured cars and personnel carriers, could be seen 
moving to the front and that the Libyan national airline is helping the air force in 
transporting troops to the border. 


He added that hospitals in the capital were evacuated for emergency cases in the event 
war erupts.
