LIBYA

NIGER, TOGOLESE FOREIGN MINISTERS ARRIVE IN TRIPOLI 


LD151.736Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 CMT 15 Jun 78 LD 


{[Summary] The Niger foreign minister arrived in Tripoli this afternoon on a visit. He 
was welcomed by Foreign Secretary Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki and the Niger ambassador 
to the Jamahiriyah. 


The Foreign Minister of Togo also arrived in Tripoli this afternoon. He was also wel- 


comed by the foreign secretary and the Togolese ambassador to the Jamahiriyah. The 
Togolese minister is accompanied by the Togolese presidential adviser on Arab affairs. 


Received by Al-Qadhdhafi 
LD150112Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 15 Jun 78 LD
