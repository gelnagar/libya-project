LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Calis for Lebanese ‘People’s Army’ 
LD081447 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1418 GMT 8 Dec 83 


(Text} Tripoli, 8 Dec (JANA) — Colonel Mu‘ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi, commander of the Great | September Revolution, 
has called for the formatios of a people’s army to resist the 
invaders in Lebanon and to destroy the Zionist entity that has 
been implanted in the heart of the Arab homeland. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi made this remark while speaking to a 
group of secondary school students in Banghazi yesterday. He 
declared that it is our duty to create a people’s resistance army 
in Lebanon composed of the Lebanese and Arab masses. 


He noted that the purpose of this army would be to fight village 
by village and town by town until the occupation is destroyed, the 
invaders of Lebanon routed, and the usurping entity in occupied 
Palestine expelled. 


We as Arabs are not oppressors, he said, and we do not want to 
cross the seas and go to Europe and the United States to fight. 
But as a nation that has been the victim of aggression and in 
whose land a foreign body has been implanted, we Arabs must 
destroy the enemy and liberate the Arab homeland. 


He added that the world will wake up to this fact and realize our 
right to defend ourselves and to expel the usurping enemy that is 
continuously confronting us with war and aggression, destroying 
our capitals, and killing the sons of our nation.
