LIBYA

Further on British Hostages’ Release 
LDO071729 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1712 GMT 
7 Feb 85 


[Text] Terry Waite, personal envoy of the Anglican Church, 
accompanied by the four Britons who were released in compli- 
ance with the resolutions of the basic people’s congresses during 
their third ordinary session for 1985, have left Tripoli. 


In a statement to a JANA correspondent at the airport prior to 
his departure with the four Britons, Terry Waite expressed his 
appreciation of this humane gesture by the basic people's con- 
gresses -- the release of the four Britons who were imprisoned in 
Libya on charges connected with security and the undermining 
of the economy. 


He said that this is a victory for mercy and understanding. He 
added: We hope the spirit of understanding that has been built 
during the past months will continue to grow.

in the past month and said: We look forward to the future. 


Joint Solidarity Groups Hold Tripoli Meeting 
LDO71233 Tripoli JANA in English 0935 GM7 7 Feb 85 


{Text} Tripoli, Jumada Awal 16, Feb 7, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY Yesterday evening in Tripoli, a joint 
meeting was held between the Libyan Arab Afro-Asian Solidar- 
ity Committee (L.A.A.A.S.C.)and the delegation of the Egyptian 
Arab Solidarity Committee (E.A.S.C.) 


A member of (L.A.A.A.S.C.) gave a speech at this meeting at the 
beginning of which he said: Today we receive you because we 
see in you vanguards and participants in the 23d of July Rev- 
olution and witnesses to its national liberation role, some of you 
who took part with the late leader Jamal ‘Abd al-Nasir in 
preparing and carrying out of the revolution. Some of you have 
carried with him the burdens after the success of the revolution 
in its confrontation with imperialism, reaction and Zionism 


He added that the July revolution was the real backer of the Arab 
revolutions in Algeria, Democratic Yemen, Iraq and Syria. It was 
also the vanguard of the first Arab unity between Egypt and 
Syria and one of the founders of the Non-alignment Movement 
which confronted imperialism, the areas of influence and pacts 
in the Arab region, Africa, Asia and Latin America. 


He added: The Great Al Fateh Revolution came out carrying 
the banner of Arab nationalism, Arab unity and confrontation of 
imperialism, Zionism and reactioiu, and to emphasize peoples’ 
liberty and possession of authority, wealth and weapons. What is 
taking place today in the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jama- 
hiriyah is the free direct democratic practice through the general 
people’s congresses and people's committees. 


He went on to say, “We would like to know: What is the role 
of the Egyptian people in destroying the policy of normalization 
and coexistence with the enemy and its recognition. What is the 
role of the progressive forces in Egypt in frustrating the 
treacherous Camp David accords and in moving Egypt away 
from the spheres of influence and imperialist bases.” 


The delegation of the Egyptian Solidarity Committee strongly 
praised the brave and resolute stands of Al Fateh Revolution in 
resisting all forms of conspiracies that American imperialism 
design against the Arab nation. They confirmed that the Egyp- 
tian people strongly reject and resist the Camp David treaties. 


The program of the Afro-Asian solidarity committee includes: 
review of national struggle by the Arab masses for liberation and 
unity, study on the American military presence in the Arab 
homeland, its danger on the Arab nation’s present and future and 
the Camp David accords, the conspiracies that followed it and 
the Reagan plan of conspiracy. It will also include cooperation 
within the Afro-Asian organization on bilateral levels as well as 
on the level of strengthening the struggle of the peoples in Africa 
and Asia.

Egypt Continues ‘Conspiracies’ Against Chad 
LD081013 Tripoli JANA in English 0844 GMT 8 Feb 85 


[Text] Cairo, Jumada Awal 17, February 8, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The hireling Egyptian regime continues 
its conspiracies against the Chadian people, its unity, security 
and stability, instigated by its role as an instrument for the service 
of U.S. imperialism. 


In the midst of these conspiracies a meeting was held yesterday 
between Butrus Ghali, state minister for foreign affairs, and 
Ismail Muhammad, information minister [as received] of the 
so-called Habre’s government. Ghali reiterated the same reac- 
tionary imperialist dispositions aimed at causing instability in 
Chad.
