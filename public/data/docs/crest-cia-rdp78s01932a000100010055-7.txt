Summary (AI): A garbled CIA cable reports that Tunisia and Libya signed a declaration to unite into one republic with a single president, announced over Libyan radio as the Islamic Arab Republic. The text was not yet available, and the cable notes the announcement may be an agreement in principle rather than an immediate union.
• Libyan official announcement was broadcast over Libyan radio and said the federation would be called the Islamic Arab Republic.
• The cable says a timetable for establishing the union was to be arranged later, and that the announcement may prove to be an agreement in principle subject to popular ratification.
• Tunisian press reportedly characterized a Libyan visit to Tunisia as a 'private visit'.
• The cable reports that Tunisia's leader bluntly rejected the Libyan proposal and conceded only that an agreement had been achieved after years of cooperation.
• 1974-01-12: Tunisia and Libya announce a declaration to unite into one state with a single president; Libyan radio names it the Islamic Arab Republic
