Summary (AI): A Washington Post article reports that President Reagan said the US has evidence Libya sent assassins to kill senior US officials, and that the administration weighed economic options such as an oil boycott. Nothing here is located in Tripoli itself.
• A defector reportedly walked into a US diplomatic mission in Western Europe within the month and described 10 men, most of them Libyans, said to make up hit teams.
• About 1,500 US citizens, mostly oil workers and their families, remain in Libya.
• Six US oil companies operate in Libya; the State Department advised US citizens to leave Libya the previous summer.
• Economic options discussed include an oil boycott and withdrawal of American and British oil technicians, which would need British cooperation.
• 1981-12: Reagan says the US has evidence Libya sent assassination teams to the US; NSC meets on economic options against Libya
• 1981-09: Ambush attack near Heidelberg on Gen. Frederick J. Kroesen's armored car, linked to West German terrorists; Kroesen not seriously injured
