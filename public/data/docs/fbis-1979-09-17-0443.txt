LIBYA

SPOKESMAN DENIES CONFISCATION OF U.S, TROOP CARRIERS 
LD161526 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1500 GMT 16 Sep 79 LD 
[Text] Tripoli, 16 Sep--Some news agencies have quoted the official spokesman of the 
White House in Washington as claiming that the Libyan authorities have confiscated four 


armored troop carriers that were on their way to Lebanon on board the U.S, vessel 
("Dorcilla").

An official spokesman of the Benghazi port has denied this report, saying it is base- 
less and has no shred of truth at all, The spokesman added that the vessel ("Dorcilla") 
was carrying nothing but fodder, a quantity of which was unloaded at the Tripoli port 
and the rest at Benghazi. 

REVOLUTIONARY COMMITTEES TO BE FORMED FOR MILITARY 

LD161150 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 GMT 16 Sep 79 LD 

[Text] Tripoli, 16 September, JANA--Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi announced last 
night during a meeting with new graduate officers of the land, air, navy and air defense 
forces that revolutionary committees for the armed forces would soon be organized as 
part of the implementation of the decision in this respect adopted by the conference 

of Free Unionist Officers held in Benghazi on 9 April 1979. Among the tasks of these 
committees=--a new phenomenon in the armed forces--are the following: 

1. To assume revolutionary direction of the armed forces; 

2. To confirm the new theses heralded by the Great First of September Revolution; 


3- To shoulder the historic tasks of achieving Arab and Islamic unity and spreading 
the new civilization, 


4, To crush any counterrevolutionary attempt immediately; 
5. To exercise revolutionary control; 


6. To educate the armed forces concerning Permanent Order No, 1 with all its sayings 
and instructions; 


7- To prompt the armed forces to hasten the creation of an armed people; 
8, To urge the masses to carry arms; 


9. To train the popular masses and command them and push the armed forces to proceed 
quickly with this task; 


10. To create a cultural revolution in education that will bring military sciences 
into the educational institutions; 


11, To confirm the people's authority; 


12. To call for revolution and to prepare propaganda for the revolution and to herald 


13. To bring cohesion to the revolutionary committees, 


The leader of the revolution pointed to the tremendous development since 1977 of the 
defensive capability of the Libyan Armed Forces, during which the quantity of the land 
naval and air weapons of the Jamahiriyah doubled. He also noted that various huge 

and devastating weapons have been received since that date, 


The brother colonel pointed to the task of the armed forces after the people received 
authority and wealth,

He said that the urgent task of the armed forces is to implement the saying "the armed 
people" and to raise the people's fighting capability and bring them to the level of 
the regular army. 


The leader said that the implementation of this new thesis has begun through general 
military training, popular resistance, mjahidin battalions, the militarization [ar 
tajyish] of boys and girls schools, and the militarization of cities, 


The leader of the revolution confirmed that with the creation of the armed forces 
there would be no chance for defeating this people, Defeat, he said, can be dealt to 
regular armies, but an armed people cannot be defeated at all and cannot be eliminated, 
He explained that, even if the armed people were defeated, the enemy would not be able 
to reap the fruit of victory. 


The meeting was attended by Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the Libyan 
Armed Forces, and the chiefs of staff of the four armies, 


The brother colonel expressed his thanks to the USSR and the socialist countries for 
enabling Libya to build up its defense force and to reach its present level of naval, 
air and air defense capabilities. He affirmed that these forces are fully capable of 
defending the homeland and the giant achievements of the revolution--the giant revolu- 
tionary achievements made by the Libyan Arab people, 


In this regard, the brother colonel referred to the attitude of the United States, 
which placed Jamahiriyah on its black list in both the civilian and military fields, 
so much so that it refused to deliver to the Jamahiriyah the civilian aircraft that 
Libya had contracted to purchase. 


The brother commander declared that he has received a letter from Lebanese President 

Ilyas Sarkis concerning the deteriorating situation in Lebanon due to Israel's daily 

aggression against Lebanese territory and the need to hold an Arab summit conference 

to enable the Arab nation as a whole to shoulder its full pan-Arab military responsi- 
bility in the face of the enemy, 


The brother colonel affirmed the need for an Arab military presence in Lebanon to 
check the enemy, since Lebanon is an Arab state facing the most flagrant aggressions 
by the enemy. He said that the presence of the Arab forces in Lebanon was a matter 
provided for in the joint Arab defense pact, 


The brother colonel asserted that Lebanon should not be left alone as it has now 
reached a stage in which it no longer can defend its soil and protect its territory. 
He added that the holding of an Arab summit conference in response to the appeal of 
Lebanese President Ilyas Sarkis was an urgent matter as far as saving Lebanon is con- 
cerned, 


The brother colonel told the graduating officers that what is taking place in Lebanon 
was a true war in the full sense of air, naval and land wars, He added that the Arab 
states have the right to intervene in order to check the Zionist enemy in Lebanon, 
This, he said, is in keeping with the provisions of the Arab League charter and the 
joint Arab defense pact, 


The graduation ceremony was attended by Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief 
of the Libyan Armed Forces, as well as the chiefs of staff of the four services: the 
land, naval, air and air defense forces,
