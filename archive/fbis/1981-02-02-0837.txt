LIBYA

SYRIA'S KHADDAM VISITS, CONVEYS MESSAGE 1 FEB 


JNO10859 Damascus Domestic Service in Arabic 1215 GMT 1 Feb 81 


[Text] ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam, deputy prime minister and foreign minister, arrived in 
Tripoli at noon today on a short visit to the Libyan Jamahiriyah during which he will 
deliver a message from President Hafiz al-Asad to Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. 


Khaddam was received at the airport by ‘Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the People's 
Bureau for Foreign Liaison; the official in charge of the Syrian public relations office 
in Tripoli; and members of the office. 


In an arrival statement, Khaddam said that he will convey a message from President Hafiz 
al-Asad to his brother leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi on several issues of common interest. 
Khaddam affirmed that the special relations between the two countries necessitate an 
exchange of viewpoints and consultations on all joint issues. 


1 Feb Departure 
LD012348 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 1 Feb 81 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution yesterday evening [1 February] received Syrian 
Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam, who arrived in Tripoli 
yesterday morning on a short visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 
Brother Khaddam conveyed a message to the leader of the revolution from Syrian President 
Hafiz al-Asad. Brother ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam left Tripoli yesterday evening after con- 
cluding his visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


CHADIAN FOREIGN MINISTER ATTACKS LIBYA'S CRITICS 


LD302058 Tripoli JANA in English 1840 GMT 30 Jan 81 


{Text] Rome, 30 Jan (JANA)--Ahmat Acyl, the Chadian foreign minister, made a sharp 
attack on the European countries which manifest a hostile position towards the great 
Al-Fatah revolution and which strive to sabotage its increasing world influence. 


The Chadian foreign minister told "IL GIORNALI D'ITALIA" that these countries oppose the 
Al-Fatah revolution for the significant role it plays in various areas and for its ability 
to confront all sorts of imperialist trends and designs. Europeans know fully, he added, 
that Al-Qadhdhafi is a revolutionary and that we are and remain his friends regardless 

of all consequences. 


Mr. Acyl condemned the French position as contrary to the interests of the African Con- 
tinent. He added that France, which had invaded his country in the sixties, is now 
waging a campaign of noise against Chad. Denouncing European and reactionary reactions 
against the declaration of merger with Libya, he asked: Why was it that nobody raised a 
voice against us when we merged with Zaire?

CHADIAN PRESIDENT DEPARTS AFTER VISIT 31 JAN 


LD311924 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 31 Jan 81 


[Text] Oueddei Goukouni, president of Chad, at midday today left Tripoli following a 
visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad 
Jallud saw the president of Chad off at Tripoli's international airport. The secretary 
of the General People's Congress and some members of general people's committees also 
saw the president off.
