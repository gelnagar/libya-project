LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Receives OPEC Ministerial Delegation 
LD151956 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1830 GMT 15 Aug 84 


[Text] Tripoli 15 Aug (JANA) — The brother leader of the 
revolution today received the OPEC ministerial delegation which 
includes Dr Mani‘ Sa‘id al-‘Utaybah, UAE minister of petro- 
leum and natural resources; Belkacem Nabi, the Algerian min- 
ister of energy and petrochemical industries; and the Venezuelan 
minister of energy and mines, Dr Arturo Hernandez Grisanti.
