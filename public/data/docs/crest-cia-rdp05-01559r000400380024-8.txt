Summary (AI): A 1979 CIA-released transcript of Oriana Fallaci's interview with Muammar el-Qaddafi, conducted in his Tripoli compound during the Iran hostage crisis. Qaddafi discusses Iran, oil as a political weapon, Idi Amin, Chad, and Israel.
• Qaddafi was 38 years old and had come to power on Sept. 1, 1969 by overthrowing the monarchy.
• Fallaci claimed Idi Amin lived in a villa with a park and swimming pool near Tripoli with two wives and 10 children; Qaddafi replied Amin was not there.
• Qaddafi said Libya was seriously studying replacing the dollar in oil payments and possibly withdrawing deposits from American banks.
• Qaddafi threatened to use oil as a political weapon, including refusing to supply petroleum to America.
• Libya's population was given as some 2.5 million.
• 1979-11-23: Oriana Fallaci began a two-day interview with Qaddafi in his Tripoli compound, 19 days after the seizure of hostages at the US Embassy in Tehran.
