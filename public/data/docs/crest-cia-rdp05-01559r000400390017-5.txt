AL-QADHDHAFI ON LEBANON, U.S. MANEUVERS , REAGAN 
AU211557 Vienna PROFIL in German No 25, 21 Jun 82 pp 24-25 

[Report on Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi's interview with a visiting Austrian "political 
scientists delegation" at his "headquarters" in Libya -- date not given] 

[Excerpts] On the war in Lebanon: 

"Libya is wondering why the Americans and Europeans continue to support Zionism, 
considering that it has been proved that Zionism is a shocking factor for world peace, 
and that the Israelis are barbarians and demagogues, against world peace and against 
mankind in general. As we know, they have now shown their true face, because in their 
latest attacks they have not distinguished between military and civilian targets. They 
have bombed civilians, killed children and old people and razed to the ground culture 
and civilization. Ultimately, this certainly will lead to a third world war as the 
Israelis caused World Wars I and II. However, I would very much like to know how Europe 
and America would react if an Arab nation had committed these barbaric acts. Europeans 
and Americans still have not realized that the Israelis are an evil to the whole world 
and that they will certainly touch off a world catastrophe. They have no morals and are 
barbarians. They want to destroy the whole world so as to remain alone." 

On Libya's role in the Lebanon conflict: 

“We will be in solidarity with the Palestinian people until they have regained their 
rights. However, God willing, the Europeans and Americans will pay the price, the price 
for their support of Israel. They will pay this price because the Israelis are conjur— 
ing up a third world war.... Hence, Europe and America must simply dissociate them- 
selves from these barbarians and cut off any military and economic support for Israel." 
On the U.S. intention to again hold maneuvers in the Gulf of Sidra in September: 

"That would mean world war..and it is certainly possible that a second world power would 
also intervene in this war -- the Soviet Union...we will offer resistance to aggressors, 
colonialists and conquerers..." 

On chances for Arab unity: 

"As long as the currently existing regimes are in power, I. don't see any chance what- 
ever. Only when all these systems are abolished will Arab unity become real." 

On the Libyan model as the foundation for Arab unity: 

"The system of the Jamahiriyah is designed not only for the Arab states, but is world- 
wide." 

On his current assessment of U.S. President Ronald Reagan: 

"He has changed a little, no doubt. At first, he was a 100-percent ignoramus as far as 
international relations are concerned. Today, he grasps at least 25 percent of the 
world problems." 

On his visit to Austria in March 1982: 

“The visit to Austria was highly successful and paved the way for our future visits to 

other Western countries... In this context, I am highly satisfied with the endeavors of 
the Austrian chancellor to this end."
