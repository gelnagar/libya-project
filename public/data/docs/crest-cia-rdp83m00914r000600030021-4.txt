November 30, 1982 

MEMORANDUM FOR MR. WILLIAM P. CLARK 
THE WHITE HOUSE 

. SUBJECT: Report of SIG on Libya: Approval and 
Implementation of the Stair Step Plan 

- The SIG on Libya convened on November 29 to consider the 
attached IG review. The SIG approved the Stair Step Plan as an 
overall program for assertion of rights. The SIG also approved 
the steps planned for early December. 

The SIG accepted a proposal by the NSC to insert substitute 
language in the IG review document. The language stipulates 
use of JRC channels for interagency clearance of steps 6-8. 

State opposed a proposal by JCS that action steps 1-3 in 
the future be cleared with the Secretary of Defense only. It 
was the Chairman's view that all activities in the Stair Step 
Plan require political review and should be cleared by the SIG 
agencies beforehand. 

As modified, the IG review will stipulate that all further | 
activities in the series between steps 1 and 5 inclusive will 
be cleared in advance with State, using normal notification 
procedures for operations in politically sensitive areas. 
Activities at step 6 and above will be cleared through the SIG 
agencies using the JRC procedure. > * a
