DFLP’s Hawatimah Statement on Tripoli 
LD101607 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1535 GMT 10 May 87 


[Text] Tripoli, 10 May (JANA) — DFLP S-cretary General 
Nayif Hawatimah and members of his delegation left Tripoli at 
noon today 


In a statement to JANA before leaving Tripoli International 
Airport, Hawatimah said that PLO unity and the PNC meeting's 
positive results have disturbed the Zionist, U.S., Jordanian, and 
Egyptian reactionary circles, because the results are marvelous 
and serve the interests of the Palestinian people and the Arab 
nation. The results have won the admiration and appreciation of 
the revolutionary Arab forces headed by the great Jamahiriyah. 


Hawatimah stressed the cohesion in the struggle between the 
great | September Revolution and the Palestinian revolution, 
pointing out that this cohesion has constantly been growing 
deeper and stronger for the good of the Palestinian people's cause. 
He also noted that the land of the Great Jamahiriyah embraces 
all the groups of the Palestinian revolution and that the PLO 
office and the offices of Palestinian groups, within and outside 
the PLO, have been playing their normal role in bolstering the 
revolutionary cohesion between the two revolutions and have 
been functioning normally for the realization of the ambitions of 
the Palestinian people. These offices, he said, work to facilitate 
the Palestinian people in the Great Jamahiriyah in assuming 
their natural role within the Palestine revolution, that being the 
rear guard for bolstering the frontline of the Palestinian rev- 
olution

[Text] Cairo, 8 May (MENA) — MENA has learned that Libya 
has succeeded in circumventing the U.S. embargo by concluding 


a deal to purchase three C-| 30 aircraft and a large quantity of 
spare parts for them through the German (Gmacs) company 
based in Frankfurt 


The planes, which currently are in Portugal, are ready for 
delivery according to company representative “Abd al-Qadir 
‘Abbas Hilal, a Sudanese national. He added that the company 
also offers prospective customers Western arms and computers 


JANA Comments on South Pacific, U.S. ‘Terrorism’ 
LD091708 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1505 GMT 9 May 87 


[Text] Tripoli, 9 May (JANA) — Commenting on the American 
campaign against the so-called Libyan role in tne Pacific Ocean, 
the JANA international! affairs editor writes 


Australian officials have stated that the Australian Government 
will send a special envoy to the South Pacific islands to explain 
what they called their profound concern over the continued 
Libyan presence in the region An Australian Foreign Ministry 
spokesman has stated that the Libyan question will head the 
topics to be discussed. The Australian Government spokesman 
stated that the envoy will affirm that Australia has no plans to 
interfere in the islands’ affairs but that Libya's intervention in 
the area is a cause of concern 


It seems that the U.S. Administration nas moved from the stage 
of inciting its agents to wage a fierce political and information 
campaign over what they call the threat of the Libyan role in the 
Pacific, to the point of actually assuming officially the leadership 
of this campaign under the pretext of concern and fear at the 
threat of the Libyan presence and its effect in inflaming the spirit 
of revolution in that region 


The U.S. Administration is shedding crocodile tears over what it 
calls the expected fate of the Pacific Ocean region because of the 
Libyan presence. The U.S. Administration knows that nothing 
threatens any area in the world more than the terrorist line it 
pursues in dealing with peoples and countries — even those in its 
own region — starting with the use of its monopolist tentacles to 
rob the people of their wealth, to the placing of its military bases 
and the deployment of its naval fleets, the militarization of space, 
pushing the world to the brink of a nuclear war, and to spreading 
its germ terror throughout the world 


It suffices for me to mention that the United States has resorted 
to the use of military force 250 times to interfere in the affairs of 
other countries, blackmailing them and bringing pressure to bear 
on them. Of these instances of interference, 55 were during 
Reagan's tenure as head of the U.S. Administration. Should | 
recall the crimes of the American wars in Vietnam and Korea, 
and the blockade of Cuba a quarter of a century ago, the blockade 
of Nicaragua, and the invasion of Grenada and Lebanon? Or 
should I talk about America’s insistence on continuing its nuclear 
tests and its innovation in manufacturing weapons for 
transferring its devastating war into space? Or should | talk 


about the CIA's responsibility for inventing AIDS, the plague of 
the 20th century? Or should I talk about the U.S. Administra- 
tion's sponsorship and protection of racist entities to set them 
against peaceful peoples, as has happened in occupied Palestine 
and southern Africa? 


Has the world forgotten Hiroshima and Nagasaki? Shall | reca!! 
something even more recent, when the U.S. Administration 
amassed scores of aircraft and used its stationary bases and 
artificial satellites to pour hundreds of sophisticated bombs and 
missiles in a barbaric attempt — the first of its kind in modern 
times — to assassinate the leader of the Great | September 
Revolution, thereby destroying scores of houses over the heads of 
their inhabitants — Libyan women, the aged, and children — 
while they slept. 


The international community, which has incriminated the US. 
Administration on more than one occasion, and unanimously 
condemned it as a country that adopts a policy of official orga- 
nized terrorism when it launched its failed NATO barbaric 
aggression against the Great Jamahiriyah, fully realizes the real 
danger threatening the security and future of this world. Equally, 
the peoples of the Pacific Ocean region, whose waters have been 
transformed into fields for devastating nuclear and atomic tests 
and anchorages for nuclear fleets, and whose land has been 
transferred into fertile pastures for the Western monopolist 
companies and the military bases serving the objectives of these 
companies, are also fully aware of what is the real danger 


These peoples, some of whom are still living under the yoke of 
Western colonialism, are fully aware of what is taking place 
around them and of the danger facing them. They can distinguish 
between the danger that came to rob them of their will and 
destinies and to turn their region into a testing ground for 
weapons of annihiliation, and those who extend help and aid to 
them, bolster their rights to struggle for a free life and their 
building a future away from the snares of subservience and the 
spirit of domination and hegemony. 


It remains for me to point with astonishment at the insolence 
which characterizes those in charge of the U.S. Administration. 
They feel no shame or disgrace in warning the peoples of the 
Pacific region against the threat of terrorism that faces them, or 
of reminding them of terrorism’s victims and calling on them to 
face up to terrorism at a time when the U.S. Administration 
knows better than anyone that they are the last to have the right 
to talk about terrorism and its victims. The U.S. Administration's 
name has become for the world a synonym for terrorism and a 
center for crime whose record of victims is as long as the history 
of the United States itself. If there are people in this world who 
suffer, it is American terrorism that is the direct cause of their 
suffering. 


If we were to probe through this ugly American record, we would 
need as many books and comments as there are days in a year, 
or even more.
