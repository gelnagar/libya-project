LIBYA

FRG REPORTEDLY AGREES TO 'CONSIDER' WAR REPARATIONS 
LD270734 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 27 Oct 80 


[Text] The FRG ambassador has informed the Foreign Liaison Bureau of the Jamahiriyah 

of the German government's agreement to consider the question of war reparations demanded 
by the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah for the huge losses caused to the Libyan 
Arab people as a result of the World War II. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES POLISARIO SECRETARY GENERAL 
LD270746 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0245 GMT 27 Oct 80 


[Text] The brother commander yesterday received Brother Mohammed Abdelaziz, the Polisario 
secretary general who is on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah.
