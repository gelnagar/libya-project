LIBYA

FRENCH REPORTEDLY WANT AL-QADHDHAFI TRIP CANCELED 


LD281257 Kuwait AR-RA'Y AL-'AMM in Arabic 26 Jan 81 p 23 


{'Adnan Nasrawin report: "French Pressures To Cancel Al-Qadhdhafi's Visit To Italy and 
Germany" ] 


[Excerpt] Paris--Well-informed French sources have said that France has informed both Italy 
and West Germany that it wishes Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qzdhdhafi's visit to Rome and Bonn 
during the first quarter of this year to be canceled. 


The sources explained that France made no official request to this effect to the West 
German or the Italian Governments but only expressed the hope that Al-Qadhdhafi's visits 

to the two capitals will be canceled because of Libya's stand on Chad. 

Unofficial contacts have taken place between Paris and Bonn and Rome on this subject within 
the framework of the continuous consultations between EC member states. 


French official sources have made it clear that the French Government does not wish any 
visit by any Libyan official to the EC countries unless it is extremely necessary. Well- 
informed sources in the Common Market said that the question of the proposed merger-unity 
between Libya and Chad has been the subject of intensive contacts in the past 2 days 
between the European Common Market countries.
