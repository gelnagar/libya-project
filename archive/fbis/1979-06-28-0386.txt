LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI INTERVIEWED ON MEDITERRANEAN, MIDEAST ISSUES 
LD271215 Rome L'EXPRESSO in Italian 24 Jun 79 pp 44-45 LD 


(Dina Nascetti undated interview in Tripoli with Secretary General of the Libyan General 
People's Congress Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi: "My Sicilian Brothers" ] 


[Text] Tripoli--My appointment with Col Mu-ammar al-Qadhdhafi was at 1230 hrs, I was taken 
by car to the "Kayada" (command) headquarters by a pretty young Libyan woman, Saida, whom 
the guards knew well and greeted in a friendly manner, Later Al-Qadhdhafi himself told me 
that Saidi is regarded as an excellent revolutionary. Here in Kayada the colonel spends 
most of his day and receives official delegations, ‘The headquarters in surrounded by a wall 
3-meters high stretching as far as the eye can see, The entrance, which gives onto a small 
circular area, is flanked by four large semi-entrenched, but clearly visible, large tanks, 
topped by as many guns, The interview took place in a rectangular room, whose walls are 
lined by some 15 Italian-made chairs, 


It was obligatory to ask the colonel about the "third universal theory" (his political 

Koran, after capitalism and communism) as spread through the Green Book, Al-Qadhdhafi dreams 
of a peaceful society without poverty, without intermediaries between the people and the 
government, a direct and absolute democracy, He interprets the Koran in socialist and 
egalitarian terms, but rejects the idea of Islamic socialism,because, he says, there is 

only one socialism, I asked him whether it is true, as it is being asserted, that he has 

in fact abandoned political power, "For us power from above is over," Al-Qadhdhafi replied. 
but added that he is the head of the revolutionary committees and leader of the revolution, 
precisely in order to assert the people's authority. 


Next we came to the number one topic for which we asked to meet with him: the Mediterranean, 
The colonel believes that the Mediterranean concerns all the peoples who live round it and 
that it is up to them to control it. It is indispensable, therefore, to eliminate the 
foreign military bases to avoid the danger of possible international conflicts and to 
transform this sea into an area of peace, This is why Libya promoted the conference of 
Mediterranean progressive and socialist parties, whose third meeting ended recently in 
Athens, 


[Question] Does not this message of peace and cooperation conflict with the fact that Libya 
has become an outright arsenal of some of the most sophisticated weapons and that, according 
to many reports, it has grantéd military bases to the USSR? 


[Answer] Libya cannot grant military bases to any foreign country. We fought colonialism 
and imperialism for 60 years and eliminated the British and American bases from our terri- 
tory and we do not want to replace them with other bases, For 10 years, that is, since 
the revolution began, we have not even granted maritime facilities to foreign ships, 
American or Soviet. Nevertheless, if we were forced to do so by events, we could turn to 
a friendly country. [answer ends] 


And yet a few hours before our meeting Al-Qadhdhafi granted an interview to REUTERS, in 
which he had stated that Libya was prepared to grant military bases to the USSR, Why these 
two conflicting, and at the same time similar, assertions?

Was it a way of addressing the United States at a time when Egypt is amassing troops on 
the Libyan border and at the same time the Mediterranean European countries, which 
Al-Qadhdhafi would like to see freed from the presence of military bases? 


"The sophisticated weapons that we have purchesed," Al-Qadhdhafi continued, "are for the 
defense of the Libyan state, But I want to stress proudly that weapons have been distributed 
to the entire people, who make up the popular militia," 


[Question] Let us return to the Mediterranean, How realistic and truthful are your pro- 
posals to transform the Mediterranean into a sea of peace, when on 31 March, on the 
occasion of the celebration of the departure of the British troops from Malta, you yourself 
said: "There is no place for Malta in Europe?" 


[Answer] I have frequently defended Malta's neutrality and I did not say that it should 
break its ties with Europe, Following the evacuation of the last foreign troops, I con- 
sider Malta's neutrality a further step forward, after Libya's toward stability and inter- 
national peace and toward the transformation of the Mediterranean into an area of peace, I 
hope that soon Italy itself and the otherMediterranean countries can follow Malta's example 
and leave the Atlantic Treaty. 


[Question] Why did the plan for Italy and France, on the one hand, and Libya and Algeria, 
on the other, to guarantee Malta's neutrality fail, or why, at least, was it shelved? 


[Answer] Because Italy and France did not want to grant economic aid to the Maltese people, 
since they were keen for the island to remain linked to NATO, while Libya felt dutybound to 
help Malta defend its neutrality and independence, [answer ends] 


But why then did the Maltese premier state, during Marshall Tito's recent visit, that: 
"Libya has been making fun of Malta for 3 years on the issue of the demarcation of terri- 
torial waters?" From Malta we passed on to Sicily, another island in which Libya has shown 
great interest, also prompting several polemics, 


[Question] Some people accuse you of expansionist aims, The reports that you have bought 
large areas of the island of Pantelleria and land in Sicily have created a sensation, Are 
the reports true? And what are the reasons for the operation? 


[Answer] We have not bought any land. We have simply made some investments within the 
context of the mutual cooperation which is the basis of our principles, which means that 
prospects for prosperity have been created for the Sicilians, 


[Question] Why so much interest in Sicily on the part of the Arab world in general and 
Libya in particular? 


[Answer] Two reasons: one historical and the other geographical, Our interest is one of 
friendship and cooperation, while we are worried by the interest of the United States, which 
would like to transform Sicily into a major military base, Because of its geographical 
position and its historical precedents, Sicily is for us a natural bridge in relations of 
cooperation with Italy. It must not be forgotten that a large proportion of Sicilians are 
of Arabic origin: In fact the Arabs governed Sicily for several centuries, giving rise to 

a vigorous civilization, of which there are many signs, 


[Question] Let us pass on to another subject.

What is your verdict on the Iranian revolution and what similarities and differences are 
there between Khomeyni's revolution and the Libyan revolution? 


[Answer] The Iranian revolution is the continuation of the Libyan revolution, At present 
neocolonialism and imperialism are organizing several conspiracies, but if the Iranian 
revolution manages to thwart them it will fully become a "third theory" revolution, 


[Question] But the West is worried about the present Islamic revival, which is often 
described as a mederate form of holy war, 


[Answer] That is the usual blindness of the West. Since the Iranian revolution is based 
on the laws of the Koran, the West regards it as reactionary, But we regard this denigratory 
propaganda as a crusade by the West against the Iranian revolution, [answer ends] 


Al-Qadhdhafi also has a recipe for resolving the energy crisis: "The cause is the West 
itself, which is doing its utmost to prevent the oil-producing countries from becoming 
industrialized, by disproportionately increasing the prices of their technology, which the 
emerging countries need, It is also the West, with its policy, that is creating world 
inflation, recession and unemployment, If the workers and working people succeed in gaining 
control of the monopolies and in managing the enterprises themselves, many things will 
change, 


[Question] Following the signing of the Israeli-Egyptian peace treaty, there is talk of the 
possibility of another oil embargo. 


[Answer] Israel's aggressiveness with respect to the Arab world is undoubtedly influencing 
oil prices and relations between Europe and the Arab countries; it is therefore influencing 
world peace, The West's support for Israel creates many problems and the West will eventually 
pay the price for it, 


[Question] Be that as it may, how can the Palestinian problem be resolved? Are you opposed 
to negotiations? Do you believe that the final decision can only result from a war? 


[Answer] Every time negotiations have been mentioned the Palestinians have always been 
excluded as a result of Israeli pressure, Therefore, what negotiations are we talking 

about if the principal interested party is being ignored? [Is it not perhaps true that 
Israel has even refused to observe the UN resolutions in its own favor? Since the so-called 
peace treaty was signed the fedayeen have doubled their actions in theooccupied territories, 
This is the only way to halt the aggression, 


FOREIGN SECRETARIAT DENIES INVOLVEMENT IN CHAD FIGHTING 
LD271604 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 27 Jun 79 LD 


[Text] An official source at the Foreign Secretariat has refuted the claims of the ruling 
group in Ndjamena that fighting is taking place between Libyan and Chadian forces, He 
stated: This claim is without any true foundation, He pointed out that the border between 
the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and Chad was finally closed a long time ago, 
He said: The whole world knows that fighting has been taking place continuously in Chad 
for some time between the various Chadians factions and the ruling group,

The source described the claims of the ruling group in Ndjamena as false and fabricated, 

and as an attempt to distract the attention of the world from the real problems and 
tragedies being suffered by the people of Chad as a result of the tyranny of this group 

and its rejection of national reconciliation and as a result of the operations of oppression 
and annihilation it is carrying out against the people of Chad. 


The source concluded by reiterating the Jamahiruyah's definite concern for the interests of 
the people of the Chad and its continued efforts to find a solution to the Chadian question 
according to the Kano agreements, which provide for the setting-up of a national government 
of all factions in Chad,
