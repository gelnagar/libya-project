LIBYA

Maltese Foreign Affairs Minister Visits Tripoli 


Arrives 26 May 
LD261902 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1818 GMT 26 May 85 


[Text] Tripoli, 26 May (JANA) — Maltese Minister of Foreign 
Affairs and Culture Alex Sceberras and his accompanying del- 
egation arrived in Tripoli this afternoon on a visit to the Socialist 
People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 


Meets Foreign Liaison Secretary 
LD271636 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 
1230 GMT 27 May 85 


[Text] The secretary of the General People’s Committee of the 
People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison conferred this morning with 
Dr Alex Sceberras Trigona, the Maltese minister of foreign 
affairs and culture, who is visiting the Socialist People’s Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah. 


During the meeting they reviewed the cooperation between the 
two countries in all fields and discussed the means to consolidate 
these relations in order to fulfill joint interests and consolidate 
the activities of joint companies. They also discussed making the 
Mediterranean region into a zone of peace and security, and 
excluding it from international conflicts. 


During the meeting, they reviewed cooperation among the coun- 
tries of the Mediterranean region and implementation of res- 
olutions adopted by the Mediterranean nonaligned foreign 
ministers conference, held in Valetta last year, and also interna- 
tional issues of joint interest.
