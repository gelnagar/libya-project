Summary (AI): A New York Times article from December 1981 reports that Libyan leader Muammar el-Qaddafi denied plotting to assassinate President Reagan or anyone else, while the State Department said it had evidence of such plans. It also covers Qaddafi's remarks on Exxon, U.S. relations, and reactions from senators.
• State Department said five terrorists trained in Libya had come to the United States, per reports; FBI and Secret Service were put on alert.
• Qaddafi said Exxon had decided to end operations in Libya under pressure from the Reagan administration.
• Qaddafi was interviewed by Corriere della Sera (Milan) and denied Libya had helped the Red Brigades.
• Senator Moynihan said of Qaddafi, 'that man was lying to us.'
• 1981-12-06: Qaddafi denies in a televised interview from his Tripoli office that he plotted to assassinate President Reagan; State Department says it has strong evidence of planned murders of American officials
