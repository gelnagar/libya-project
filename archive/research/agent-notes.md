
# Agent notes: NGO / UN / Libyan legal sources (added 2026-10-17)

## Abu Salim prison
- Up to about 1,200 prisoners were reportedly killed on 28-30 June 1996, out of 1,600-1,700 held; Libyan groups abroad gave the toll, HRW could not verify it. Gaddafi acknowledged killings in April 2004. https://www.hrw.org/news/2006/06/27/libya-june-1996-killings-abu-salim-prison
- Amnesty's contemporary 1996 releases dated the mutiny to about 5 July 1996 and spoke of "scores" dead; later Amnesty (1997) cited 30-40 reported killed. Dates and tolls differ between sources. https://www.amnesty.org/ar/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/mde190051996en.pdf and https://www.amnesty.org/ar/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/mde190081997en.pdf
- Amnesty (1997) says interrogation and torture reportedly used underground rooms beneath the administration building. https://www.amnesty.org/ar/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/mde190081997en.pdf
- Amnesty (1994 annual report) says five Islamic Liberation Party prisoners arrested in 1973 were serving life terms there. https://www.refworld.org/node/24059
- HRC views in El Hassy v. Libya describe a "Military Unit" and "Central Unit" at Abu Salim; special forces stormed the Central Unit in June 1996. https://www.icaed.org/fileadmin/user_upload/El_Hassy_v_Libya.pdf
- In December 2009 the Internal Security chief told HRW the agency held 330 prisoners at Abu Salim whose sentences had ended or who had been acquitted; on 24 March 2010 Saif al-Islam said 409 remained. https://www.hrw.org/news/2010/03/25/libya-202-prisoners-released-hundreds-still-held-arbitrarily
- Authorities announced plans in October 2009 to demolish the prison; families feared loss of evidence (Amnesty annual entry). https://www.amnestyusa.org/reports/annual-report-libya-2010/
- A reported grave of about 1,200 prisoners was said on 25 September 2011 to lie in a field outside the prison wall. https://www.hrw.org/news/2011/09/26/libya-use-forensic-experts-exhume-prison-grave
- 77 detainees linked to the 1996 killings were held at al-Hadba on 17 May 2017 and later confirmed at Ain Zara in November 2017 (OHCHR/UNSMIL). https://www.ecoi.net/en/file/local/2004491/AbuseBehindBarsArbitraryUnlawful_EN.pdf
- 2022: a Tripoli court referred the 1996 massacre case to a military tribunal (AFP). https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-91/Africa/Libyan-court-passes-jail-massacre-case-to-military-tribunals-18127
- May-June 2025: reports of 10 charred bodies at the SSA headquarters in Abu Salim and 67 bodies in hospital fridges; ICJ/ACHPR cite mass-grave reports. https://unsmil.unmissions.org/en/node/56475 and https://www.icj.org/libya-investigate-unidentified-bodies-and-reported-mass-graves-in-abu-salim/

## Abu Salim zoo
- Missing-persons authority reported on 15 May 2025 information about mass graves at the Abu Salim zoo; UN reported a burial site at the SSA-run Tripoli Zoo. https://www.icj.org/libya-investigate-unidentified-bodies-and-reported-mass-graves-in-abu-salim/

## Ain Zara prison
- Amnesty delegates visited Ain Zara in May 2009; the Internal Security Agency controlled it and Abu Salim (HRW 2009). https://www.amnesty.org/zh-hant/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/mde190032009eng.pdf and https://www.hrw.org/news/2009/12/12/libya-repressive-atmosphere-pockets-improvement
- Amnesty (2004) calls it "on the outskirts of Tripoli"; Fathi al-Jahmi was reportedly moved there during its February 2004 visit. https://www.amnesty.org/en/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/mde190022004en.pdf
- UN Fact-Finding Mission (2022) cites 35 mine locations near Ain Zara listed on a tablet left by Wagner personnel. https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4022642/files/A_HRC_50_63-EN.pdf

## Jdeida / al-Jadida prison
- HRW 2006: a death-row inmate said about 180 people were sentenced to death across ten units. https://www.hrw.org/reports/2006/libya0106/
- Amnesty 1997 places "al-Jdayda" at al-Farnaj outside Tripoli, holding 24 students tortured and sentenced in 1995. https://www.amnesty.org/ar/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/mde190081997en.pdf
- OHCHR/UNSMIL 2018 describes al-Jdeida women's prison. https://www.ecoi.net/en/file/local/2004491/AbuseBehindBarsArbitraryUnlawful_EN.pdf

## Hadba prison
- 15 September 2015: HRW met Saadi Gaddafi and three others at al-Hadba; car bomb outside its wall on 9 September 2015. https://www.hrw.org/news/2015/10/26/libya-gaddafi-son-speaks-jail
- August 2015 videos allegedly showed abuse at al-Hadba, controlled by a former Deputy Minister of Defence allied with Libya Dawn (Amnesty). https://www.amnesty.org/fr/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/MDE1923102015ENGLISH.pdf
- March 2026: a grave with two bodies found in the "al-Hadba project area" in Abu Salim (single outlet). https://libyasecuritymonitor.com/two-bodies-discovered-in-mass-grave-in-abu-salim/

## Mitiga (former Wheelus Air Base)
- HRW 2006 identifies Mitiga airport as the former Wheelus Air Base. https://www.hrw.org/reports/2006/libya0106/
- Late February 2011: Alkarama (unverified) said abductees might be held at Maitiga Air Force base; about 400 Air Force Academy students were reportedly confined. https://www.alkarama.org/en/node/19901
- 2016-2022: held about 1,700 (2016), about 2,600 (Nov 2017), up to 5,000 (FFM 2022). https://www.right-docs.org/doc/a-hrc-34-42/ and https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4022642/files/A_HRC_50_63-EN.pdf
- ICC: El Hishri alleged senior role at Mitiga prison 1 May 2014 to 30 June 2020; charges confirmed 16 July 2026 per ECCHR. https://www.ecchr.eu/en/case/crimes-against-humanity-and-war-crimes-in-libya-finally-before-the-icc/

## Yarmouk camp / Salah al-Din
- Warehouse beside the Yarmouk base (Khalida Ferjan, Salahaddin): about 45 bodies counted by HRW on 27 August 2011; UN COI says 157 detainees, 51 survivors confirmed. https://www.hrw.org/news/2011/08/29/libya-evidence-suggests-khamis-brigade-killed-45-detainees and https://right-docs.org/doc/a-hrc-19-68

## Bab al-Aziziya / Gargur
- 26 August 2011: HRW inspected 18 bodies in a riverbed between Gargur and Bab al-Aziziya and 29 more near a field clinic outside the compound. https://www.hrw.org/news/2011/08/28/libya-gaddafi-forces-suspected-executing-detainees
- Al-Amal al-Akhdar building (Internal Security) near Gargur: 17 bodies taken to Matiga hospital on 24 August 2011 (same release).
- May 1984 attack on Bab al-Aziziya: most people arrested afterwards believed held at Abu Salim (Amnesty 1992). https://www.amnesty.org/ar/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/mde190031992en.pdf

## Tripoli city centre / Green Square / Fashloum / Tajoura (2011)
- 20-22 February 2011: at least 62 bodies in two hospitals (HRW), over 200 at morgues on 20-21 February (UN COI). https://www.hrw.org/news/2011/02/22/libya-commanders-should-face-justice-killings and https://right-docs.org/doc/a-hrc-19-68
- 4 March 2011: about 150 detained at a mosque on Mizran Street (Amnesty UA 104/11). https://www.amnesty.org/en/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/mde190162011en.pdf

## Gharghour / Tripoli protest (15 November 2013)
- 43-46+ killed and 460-500 wounded when Misrata militias fired on a march toward Gharghour; 18 bodies at Abu Salim Trauma Hospital. https://www.hrw.org/news/2013/11/17/libya-militias-kill-unarmed-protesters

## Janzour and Tawergha camps
- 6 February 2012: Janzur (Naval Academy) camp raided, seven killed; four Tripoli camps (Janzur, Airport Road, al-Fallah, Sarraj). https://www.hrw.org/news/2013/03/20/libya-stop-revenge-crimes-against-displaced-persons

## Tajoura
- 2 July 2019: airstrike on Tajoura detention centre killed at least 44; it held at least 600. https://www.hrw.org/news/2019/07/03/libya-deadly-attack-highlights-disregard-civilians

## Al-Ruwaimi prison
- June 2016: 12 released former Qadhafi-associated prisoners found dead about 24 hours after release. https://unsmil.unmissions.org/en/node/54952

## Legal framework
- Law 87 of 1974 (state-security procedure: 7 days to prosecution, 45-day extensions) https://security-legislation.ly/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/1442-Law-No.-87-of-1974_EN.pdf
- Law 47 of 1975 on prisons https://security-legislation.ly/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/334-Law-No.-47-of-1975_EN_0-1.pdf
- Law 50 of 2012 compensation: 8,000 LYD per month of detention 1 Sept 1969 to 15 Feb 2011 https://security-legislation.ly/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/309-Law-No.50-of-2012_EN-1.pdf
- Decree 89 of 2013 created the missing-persons authority (HQ Tripoli) https://security-legislation.ly/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/719-Decree-No.-89-of-2013_EN.pdf
