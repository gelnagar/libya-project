an interview with AGENCE FRANCE-PRESSE, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that the confronta- 
tion has involved big parts of North Africa, maybe the whole continent and maybe all 
the Near and Middle East. 


He said that President a'Estaing brought back to France the policy of direct 
colonialism and the sending of intervention forces, [word indistinct] the policy 
of past centuries of colonialism, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi described this policy as one which will inevitably be defeated 
because it clashes with the will of the peoples and their new aWakening,

primitive are now inspired by the Spirit of the revolution, of consciousness and of 
(words indistinct].

confidence of his electorate because all the French people are more €ager to safe-

keep d'Estaing, 


He pointed out that if France resolved to Sacrifice its interests, its prestige and

But if it is more interested to develop useful relations With the peoples, with the 
Arab nation and Africa, then President d'Estaing would have proved that he is in-

The leader of the grand Al-Fatah revolution affirmed that despite the bad policy

He pointed out in ease this confrontation takes Place, we would [word indistinct]

He pointed out that the regimes France wants to protect through this intervention, 
like the Tunisian, will fall victims of this action. The Tunisian regime will be

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that this intervention Will greatly shorten the rule of 
the Tunisian regime, He explained that the prompt French invasion of Tunisia, across 
air and naval bridges, was designed to strike at Qafsah, and not the Libyan, Algerian

He pointed out that this is an indication that France is Still dreaming about

It is the ally of the peoples struggling against imperialism, dictatorship and 
exploitation. This is a clear basic and principled stand in our policy." 


He stated that France is not a policeman in Africa, It is not responsible for the 
continent. It should instead take care of the European Common Market, of the NATO 
alliance and of the balance between leftists and rightists in France where President 
a'Estaing obtained only 51 percent of the votes, 


Colonel Mu'tammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the grand Al-Fatah revolution, affirmed

He Said, "We in Africa, the Arab nation and the Tunisian people had fought colonialism

have the capabilities of exerting pressure on the world. All these are weapons which 
we will use in the battle of liberation," 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi noted that the French president with his intervention in Tunisia

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi expressed his belief that resistance and revolution in Qafsah

Tunisian Government except to ask for help from the French army. 


On the political and trade relations with France, he pointed out that they have not 
yet been affected and I do not think there are intentions by the people's administra- 
tion in Libya to this effect. 


He expressed that the withdrawal of the French ambassador to Libya and several embassy 
Staff came a surprise to the Foreign Liaison Bureau, the secretariat of the General 
People's Congress, and the people's congresses in Libya. Colonal al-Qadhdhafi added 
that there is no intention by the Libyan side to take similar measures despite the 
fact that the Libyan People's Bureau in France does not have a diplomatic status. It 
was better for the French ambassador to remain in Libya in order to maintain our con- 
tacts and control the Situation, 


It would have been wiser not to recall the ambassador, he added, to allow a direct 
and continuous dialogue on the bases of mutual respect, and to avoid any serious 
deterioration in the situation. 


He referred to Libya's relations with France which had been very good since the 
eruption of the former!s revolution, noting that he had praised France in several 
Arab and international circles. He added that the average trade exchange between 
the two countries had increased and that fruitful cooperation was existing in the 
form of many major commercial deals which were beneficial to France and several 
companies, These deals included the various fields ranging from arms to agriculture 
and industry, etc, 


He Saidgs "We were encouraged by the fact that France wanted to follow a policy inde- 
pendent from America, For this reason we prompted cooperation and expanded trade 
exchange with it", 


Answering a query about the allegations made by the regime ruling the Tunisian pro- 
tectorate, which suggests that Libya had sent Fedayeen groups to Qafsah, Colonal al- 
Qadhdhafi denied the Tunisian claims and said that until now there was no material 
evidence of any Libyan involvement in the event which took Place in Qafsah, a town

LIBYA PROTESTS HARRASSMENT OF ARAB LEAGUE DELEGATION 


LD1i21440 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 12 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] The Libyan delegation taking part in the 28th session of the Arab Economic and 
Social Council has been exposed to harrassment by the regime of the Tunisian pro- 
vectorate, Elements from the Tunisian intelligence service and the police took the 
delegation members from the airport waiting room to a nearby room where their hand 
baggage was searched, thus completely ignoring all international norms and customs and 
violating the charters and traditions of the Arab League, 


For long hours the authorities of the Tunisian protectorate deliberately delayed the 
Libyan delegation's Passport formalities at the airport, Moreover, rancour prevented 
them from returning the Libyan Arab delegation's Passports. 


The delegation has Submitted a memorandum to the General Secretariat of the Arab 
League condemning these Police measures which reflect a backward, tyrannical mentality. 
The memorandum includes the following points: 


1. The Libyan delegation has come to the headquarters of the Arab League to take part 
positively in the meeting of the Arab Economic and Social Council and has not come to 
visit the government of the Tunisian protectorate, What the delegation was exposed to 
is considered a direct insult to the Arab League and to the Arab Economie and Social 
Council, 


2. The attention of the government, which is host to Arab League headquarters, is 
drawn to the necessity of respecting the Arab League, its councils and all its agencies, 


Se The delegation has requested the circulation of this memorandum to the brother 
heads and members of the participating delegations,

[Text] The embassy of the Tunisian protectorate in Jordan [as heard] has refused to 

grant an entry visa to the permanent member of the Libyan delegation to the Arab Economic 
Unity Council in his Capacity as a member of the council taking part in the present 

Session of the Arab Economic and Social Council, This action by the regime of the Tunisian 
protectorate comes at a time when the regime boasts that it is civilized, However, this 
action shows that the regime does not understand the meaning of being civilized, 


On the other hand it has been learned that the government of the Tunisian protectorate 

is considering moving the Arab League headquarters from its capital if the other Arab 
countries do not Support the French invasion of Tunisia, Libya has called for the convening 
of an extraordinary meeting of the foreign ministers of Islamic countries to consider 

the French invasion of Tunisia, A message in this connection has been circulated to the 
foreign ministers of Islamic countries by the secretariat of the Islamic conference, It 

is known that Tunisia, a member of this conference, is currently being exposed to a 

blatant French invasion.
