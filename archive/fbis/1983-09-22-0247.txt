LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Syrian Armed Forces Chief 
LD212048 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1905 GMT 21 Sep 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 Sep (JANA) — The brother leader of the 
revolution today received General Hikmat ash-Shihabi, chief of 
the General Staff of the Arab Syrian Armed Forces, who handed 
him an urgent message from President Hafiz al-Asad concerning 
the grave situation resulting from U.S. aggression in the region. 


Armed Forces Chief Departs 
LD220848 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 22 Sep 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 22 Sep (JANA) — Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis 
Jabir, commander of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, received 
General Hikmat ash-Shihabi, chief of the General Staff of the 
Syrian Arab Armed Forces. Gen Hikmat ash-Shihabi left Tripoli 
yesterday evening after a short visit to the Jamahiriyah. 
Brigadier Mustafa al-Kharrubi, chief of the General Staff of the 
Libyan Arab Armed Forces, saw Gen ash-Shihabi off. Gen 
ash-Shihabi arrived here yesterday morning and was welcomed 
on arrival by Brigadier al-Kharrubi. 


Vv. 22 Sep 83 


Official Begins Tour of Maghreb Nations 
LD211318 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
21 Sep 83 


[Text] Muhammad Abu al-Qasim az-Zuwayy, secretary of the 
General People’s Committee for Justice, yesterday arrived in 
Rabat on orders from the brother leader of the revolution at the 
start of a tour which will include Algeria, Mauritania, and 
Tunisia. He will be delivering a very important verbal message 
from the brother leader to King Hassan II of Morocco, President 
Chadli Bendjedid of the Republic of Algeria, Lieutenant Colonel 
Mohamed Khouna Ould Haydala, president of the Military 
Committee for National Salvation and head of state of the 
Islamic Republic of Mauritania, and President Habib Bour- 
guiba, president of the Republic of Tunisia. JANA has learned 
that the message from the brother leader of the revolution is 
connected with the U.S. armed aggression on Arab territory, the 
continuous shelling by the U.S. 6th Fleet against the Muslims 
and the nationalist forces in Lebanon, and with the need to adopt 
a decisive Arab posture in facing this aggression. 


Reportage on Goukouni’s Press Conference 


Removes Conditions for Negotiations 
AB211720 Paris AFP in French 1551 GMT 21 Sep 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 Sep (AFP) — Transitional National Union 
Government (GUNT) President Goukouni Oueddei on Wednes- 
day afternoon in Tripoli brushed aside any precondition for the 
opening of direct negotiations with Hissein Habre. The GUNT 
president said in a press conference that he was ready to relegate 
to the background the precondition for the withdrawal of French 
troops if this would block meeting with Hissein Habre or other 
parties in the conflict. 


Arriving from the “interior” very late Tuesday night, the GUNT 
president, who is fighting against the Ndjamena forces, gave a 
press conference for the first time in the Libyan capital in the 
presence of some journalists invited by Tripoli. “I want to re- 
affirm the desire of the GUNT, and especially of the martyred 
people of Chad, to resolve the crisis, which we call the Chadian 
tragedy, through negotiations and not by arms,” stressed Mr 
Goukouni, who was wearing a dark three-piece suit and was 
surrounded by his Chadian collaborators and members of the 
Libyan information and press service in the hall of the Grand 
Hotel of the capital. 


Mr Goukouni also made an appeal to African countries and 
France before the coming Franco-African summit at Vittel 
(beginning of October), indicating that he was prepared to attend 
if “President Francois Mitterrand wanted clarifications.” “My 
intention is to make African countries and, in particular, France, 
which will receive on its territory a number of African heads of 
state, understand that they must be objective and must not allow 
themselves to be trapped by the United States,”” Mr Goukouni 
stated.
