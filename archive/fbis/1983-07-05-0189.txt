Tripoli on Visit 
LD020923 Tripoli Voice af Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2015 GMT I Jul 83 
[Excerpts] The leader of the revolution’s pan-Arab working visit 
to the fraternal Moroccan Arab country is an extension of the 
new pan-Arab task the Jamahiriyah has taken up in oider to 
expand the framework of united pan-Arab action so that it will 
become comprehensive and serious in its defense of the Arab 
existence in the face of the aggressive Zionist-imperialist march, 
which is backed by the force of U.S. power. This danger does not 
differentiate between progressive and reactionary regimes, 
between civilians and military, or between men and women so 
that the Zionist empire can be built on its debris. 


On this basis the Jamahiriyah’s [word indistinct] and pan-Arab 
effort for comprehensive pan-Arab mobilization has centered on 
providing a pan-Arab base and solid ground that will endure the 
burdens of the counterrevolutionary attack by providing the 
widest positive pan-Arab participation by all Arab countries that 
are concerned about defending the Arab national existence, 
Lebanon, Palestine, and Arab unity. This excludes those regimes 
that have crossed the red lines in their alliances with the Zionist 
imperialist enemy and turned their backs on the Arab nation and 
the countries that threaten its existence — such as the Egyptian 
regime, which has sunk in the mud of the Camp David card — 
which is a treacherous crime — and is involved in a conspiracy 
to annihilate the Arab existence: This regime must be banished 
from the ranks of the Arab nation. 


The Jamahiriyah’s aim is to unite all Arab effort, energy, and 
concern and concentrate them on the main danger threatening 
the Arab national existence, namely, the danger of the aggressive 
Zionist march. This brave pan-Arab initiative received every 
encouragement and appreciation from the Moroccan brothers. 
King Hassan II sent a special envoy to the Jamahiriyah. This 
envoy was received by the brother leader. The brother leader's 
visit to Morocco creates the ground for mobilized, comprehensive 
pan-Arab action and [word indistinct] joint pan-Arab strategy, 
which emphasizes and consolidates the Arab concern and efforts 
aimed at repelling and (?destroying) the Zionist death train and 
prepares the way for the pan-Arab counterattack. 


This is especially necessary now that the warnings of war in the 
Al-Biga’ are escalating and the Zionist enemy is refusirg all 
capitulatory solutions, whether Arab or American in identity, 
and is not accepting a substitute for the destruction of Arab 
existence. Thus, the Arabs as a whole have no alternative but to 
persistently strive to deepen the trend toward pan-Arab unity, 


which deserves all Arab capabilities and energy within the 
framework of pan-Arab mobilization to face the danger of the 
aggressive Zionist march. 


It must be pointed out that this historic pan-Arab initiative by 
the Great 1 September Revolution cannot be at the expense of 
damaging or delaying the historic path of the popular revolution 
in the Arab homeland, because the revolution is an internal issue 
and not a commodity that can be exported or imported. Every 
revolutionary action with mass essence is bound to impose its 
legitimate presence and be sanctified and respected by world 
revolutionaries. The popular revolutionary movement has an 
individual and distinguishing logic that neither submits to nor is 
affected by political changes, because it draws the picture of the 
essential and final solution to all problems of Arab reality and its 
requirements.

JANA Denies Palestinians Asked To Leave Libya 
LD020816 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 2 Jul 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 2 Jul (JANA) — The Arabic-Language Al-Fajr 
published in occupied Jerusalem has published a false report 
leaked by hostile quarters that Libya has notified 12,000 Pal- 
estinians residing there to leave Libyan territory before 12 July. 


What has been published by the paper appears to be completely 
void of the truth because this report falls in line with the rotten 
intrigue against Libya. Libya is carrying out a unique pan-Arab 
role in the struggle for the freedom of the Palestinian people, their 
dignity, and honor. 


Such cheap machinations are totally rejected and no honest 
person can accept them. The instigators, who have had enough 
of carrying out the struggle, want to distort the pan-Arab posture 
of the Jamahiriyah, the vanguard of the Arab movement. 


Al-’Ubaydi Says Egypt Violating OAU Principles 
LD041913 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
4 Jul 83 


[Text]’Abd al-’Ati al-’Ubaydi, secretary of the People’s Commit- 
tee of the People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau, has sent a message 
to the OAU secretary general informing him that Egypt's mili- 
tary occupation of Sudan is a blatant violation of OAU principles. 
Furthermore, it is completely against the interests of the Suda- 
nese people and encourages the symbols of the ruling regime in 
Khartoum to repress the people and falsify their will. He 
remarked that Egyptian political and military officials have in 
numerous statements admitted the presence of Egyptian military 


forces in Sudan, and that the commander of the An-Nasir 
Military Academy, Lieutenant General Ahmad Fakliri, in an 
official statement, considered these territories as an extension of 
Egyptian territory. 


In his message Brother "Abd al-’Ati Al-’Ubaydi explained that 
Egypt's exploitation of the terror and fear — which dominates 
Sudanese officials as a result of overwhelming popular rancor, 
which has been taking the form of tumultuous demonstrations in 
Sudan’s streets, and of the deteriorating economic situation — 
its exploitation of the disadvantageous conditions that Sudan is 
experiencing, is clearly blatant interference in the internal affairs 
of an OAU member-state and constitutes a precedent that should 
be noted. 


He stated that Egyptian officials’ repeated statements on what 
they term a state of imbalance in the economic and military 
balance of forces of the countries bordering Egypt as justification 
for occupying Sudan is a false claim; it also does not justify the 
violation of the most important OAU principle. The fundamental 
OAU principle is the principle of nonalignment toward all blocs. 
The presence of Egyptian forces in Sudan is nothing more than 
a cover for the U.S. military presence through the so-called 
Rapid Deployment Force. This pesence is a blatant violation and 
a grave breach of the most important of the OAU’s principles. 
The secretary of the People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau expressed 
in conclusion his extreme concern about these grave develop- 
ments and the blatant violation of the OAU’s principles and 
charter, indicating the necessity to intensify efforts for work 
toward the withdrawal of the Egyptian occupation forces from 
Sudan. 


JANA: Numayri Disarms Troops in Southern Sudan 
LD042151 Tripoli JANA in English 1615 GMT 4 Jul 83 


[Text] Khartoum, Ramadan 24, July 4, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — It was learnt here today that the Sudanese 
forces in southern Sudan’s northern province have been disarmed 
under the pretext of “no loyalty and posing a danger”. Many of 
the soldiers in these units are locals of the area. 


The move is part of large scale changes in the armed forces 
carried out by agent Numayri following the rebellion in the Ist 
division in the south. 


Meanwhile, informed Sudanese sources said that homes of Suda- 
nese officers near Numayri’s palace (?were) destroyed and his 
guards replaced by Egyptian officers and soldiers.
