Shuffling and Arranging

preoccupied with counting its supporters and organizing its lines. But 
observers who are well informed with the secrets of Moroccan politics 

expect the king to use the "national unity" and "defending the unity of 

the soil" card to emerge victorious in the elections, even if his prime 
minister's party fails. The bridges that have begun to open between Algeria 
and Morocco, the numerous mediations and the talk about the possibility of

restoration of the 1975 "national unity" formula. The palace hopes that
