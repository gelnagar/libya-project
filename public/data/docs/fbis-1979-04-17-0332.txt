LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI MEETS WITH COMMITTEE SECRETARIES 


ID171026 Tripoli JANA in English 0845 GMT 17 Apr 79 LD 


[Text] Benghazi, 17 April JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu! ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
leader of the Great First of September Revolution, met yesterday morning the secretaries 
of the General People's Committees for Land Reclamation and Agrarian Reform, Public 
Service, Light Industries, Electricity, Information, Treasury and Economy and Planning 
at Gar Younis University at Benghazi. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi during this meeting made a number of remarks on the role of the 
qualitative people committees in consolidating the authority of the people and the 
handling of various public affairs at the municipality level, through the realization 
of the General People's Committee to the citizens! needs and working for meeting these 
needs, 


He affirmed that the system of the General People's Committee at the Jamahiriyah level 
cancels the system of the individual rule in order that the people's administration 
dominate at all the levels, 


With regard to the sector of land reclamation and agrarian reform, the leader of the 
revolution clarified that the duty of the General People's Committee is to reclaim 

land, develop the agricultural schemes and livestock, construct dams, promote ploughing 
and cultivation and store crops, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi stressed on the necessity that 
every qualitative committee ir this field should work to achieve self-sufficiency in its 
region in condition that all this should be directed in accordance with the people's 
decision, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed the necessity of giving concern to livestock in every 
municipality and develop the pastures necessary for this, 


He pointed out that the revolutionary committees should increase their role in the 
(?information) of the people!s congresses and committees in their capacity as revolu- 
tionary tools characterized by the awareness of the march of peoplets authority. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi dealt also with the sector of public service clarifying that it 
covers anyone who renders public service to the society. He noted that everyone 
entrusted for a public work should feel his responsibility and be devoted to it until 
he achieves the highest levels of success and perfection, He said that those who are 
not needed in this sector should be transferred to productive positions, 


He urged for the necessity of purging the administrative sector and of creating an 
organisation for public service characterised by perfection, activity and by the 
appointment of the right man in the right place,

He explained the duties of the General People!s Committee for Economy after the former 
Secretariate of Trade, Nutrition and Sea Wealth have been added to it, 


He said that among the responsibilities of this committee are the public markets in 
order to eradicate the free trade as it is considered an exploiting phenomenon, and 
to create a new system which sells the commodities at cost prices, 


He called for a revolution in the field of food in order to make available the full 
nutrition to the citizen by specifying the value and the quantity of the types of 

food in the markets, "and also," he added, "the importance of imported food should 

be reconsidered," With regard to the tourism sector Colonel al-Qadhdhafi stressed that 
it is a right of every Libyan without any discrimination to enjoy the beauty God gave 
to this land, He called for encouraging the local fairs at the level of the 
Jamahiriyah in order to contribute in the improvement of production, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi also dealt with the duties of the General People's Committee for 
Planning affirming that the importance of planning should stem from the broad bases of 
the masses instead of being a monopoly of an individual or one council because the 
masses know better their needs and allocation of money and because they are most able 
to follow up their project's implementation, 


He affirmed that the People's Committee for Planning in every municipality is respon- 
sible to follow up every project within its region and to assist the masses in the 
people's congresses to estimate the municipality budget and to show them the best 
method to satisfy needs, 


He dealt with the sector of light industries urging for: the establishment of factories 

for everything which can be manufactured in the municipalities, He also urged that the 
committees which operate the factories now should work for the improvement of work and 

increase of production, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi also stressed the necessity bo eradicate all forms of monopoly, 
"because," he added, "the sources are owned by all the Libyans and free business is 
permitted as long as it is productive and does not use others," 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi stressed on the necessity of investing all kinds of sea wealth 
in every coastal municipality. He encouraged the establishment of factories in the 
municipalities in the field of investing in the sea wealth, 


He dealt with the information sector affirming the necessity that all its activities 
should cover the transformation taking place in the Jamahiriyah politically, economically 
and socially. 


In conclusion, the leader of the Great First of September Revolution dealt with the 
electricity sector, pointing out that the duty of the People's Committees is the 
implementation of the local projects for each municipality, but the qgestions of 
operating, generating and producing will be the duty of the General People's Committee 
until the network which will cover all the Jamahiriyah regions is unified, 


The remarks of Colonel al-adhdhafi were interrupted by cheers: Long Live the Great 
First of September Revolution, A Masses People!s Revolution, and Long Live the Leader 
of the Revolution, a Philosopher, a Thinker and a Teacher, 


The meeting was attended by the secretary general and some members of the General 
People's Committee of the Jamahiriyah,

CUBAN MINISTER ENDS VISIT TO LIBYA 16 AR 
LD170010 Tripoli JANA in English 1658 GMT 16 Apr 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, April 16, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Cuban minister for scientific 
and technical cooperation, Mr Hector Rodriguez Llompart, left Tripoli this morning for 
Cuba after ending a several day visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


The Cuban minister had made contacts during his visit to the Jamahiriyah with officials 
in connection with the bilateral relations and methods of bolstering these relations, 
He also discussed coordination between the Jamahiriyah and Cuba for the non-aligned 
conference due to be held in Havana next September, 


JALLUD RECEIVES DELEGATION OF CHADIAN TRIBESMEN 
LD162335 Tripoli Vice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2013 GMT 16 Apr 79 LD 


{Text] Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud met this morning with a delegation of the 
(At-Tabu) and (Al-Bur'tan) tribes from the (Loir), (Baradi), (Faru), (Chada) and (Taya) 
areas. The delegation affirmed to Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Jallud their tribes! fateful 
and close union with the Libyan Arab people, who gave greatly in aiding the Chadian people, 
The delegation also affirmed that the victory of the Chadian National Liberation Front 
{FROLINAT] and the triumphant (?entry) of the people of Chad into Ndjamena was a result 

of the strong stand and limitless support of the Libyan Arab people, 


The delegation affirmed that any person who disapproves of or harms the eternal relations 
between the two Muslim peoples represents no one but himself, The delegation said that 
the people of Chad, who struggled for more than 13 years and sacrificed thousands of 
martyrs, are capable of (?reorganizing) themselves and standing up to the conspiracies. 
They call for a national conference, representing all sections of the people of Chad, to 
proclaim a new revolutionary council, to expel the agents and traitors from among the 
people and to confirm the final victory by establishing a genuine popular authority equal 
to the great sacrifices the people of Chad have given,
