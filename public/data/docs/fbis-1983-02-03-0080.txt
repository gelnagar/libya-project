AL-QADHDHAFI SPEECH AT OPPOSITION MOVEMENTS MEETING 


LDO21040 Tripoli JANA in English 0810 GMT 2 Feb 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Thani 18, Feb 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The revolution's 
leader last night attended at the people's hall in Tripoli the opening session of 

the national meeting of the opposition movements and popular organizations in the 

Arab world that include armed struggle movements. The meeting convened in response to 
the national initiative of the leader of the Great Al-Fatih Revolution in order to 
formulate a new struggle strategy capable of‘confronting the Arab defeatist reality, 
psychologically and militarily. 


The opening session was also attended by ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, Sec-Gen of the Central 
Committee and head of the supreme Presidium of the People's Council and prime 
minister of the Democratic Republic of Yemen, and Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad 
Jallud. 


The revolution's leader Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi addressed the opening session 
speaking about the Arab declared and underground opposition, its definition and 
various activities noting that the call to attend the meeting was basically made to 
the constitutional and legitimate opposition so that the meeting is constitutional 

and legitimate and so as to allow the participating delegations to legally exercise 
the measures to be taken without being legally liable in their home countries. He 
said: "I consider this as a gathering of the Arab political opposition allowed to 
operate in their countries. The Egyptian opposition, for example, is a legal and 
recognised opposition and therefore should not be chased or obstructed by the authori- 
ties which if they did, then they would have breached the constitution and law.

"This isalso applicable in the case of the opposition in Morocco, Jordan, Sudan, Kuwait, 
Tunisia and Lebanon." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that "undoubtedly there is secret opposition in the 
Arab world, but this is a different issue. We announce to the world that the struggle 
for rescuing the Arab nation has neither limits, restrictions, reservations nor 
courtesy. Should there be any secret revolutionary force in the Arab world, then we 
should not abandon it as we, day and night, call for wider revolutionary opposition 

in the Arab world. The revolutionary opposition which can not go out in the street 
should direct its attention to underground action so that the ground will move from 
underneath the hostile Arab force. 


"Our meeting here with the official and legal opposition does not mean we have abandoned 
the secret Arab revolutionary force and no one can stop any meeting or arrangement with 
it." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi stressed the need for such a national meeting as a political 
opposition force in the Arab world which operates legally from the ocean to the Gulf 
for a national cause important for the Arab nation. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added: "Every opposition movement which works in the open is often 
formed to serve national interests. But certain political opposition forces in the Arab 
world do not carry a national or unionist identity and are regrettably confined t! em- 
selves to a narrow regional and separatist activities. Yet there are few of the 

forces which have been formed basically for unionist and national objectives." The 
revolution's leader noted that certain constitutional political opposition in the 

Arab world have been useful for the regime they oppose, as it was the case with As-Sadat 
when he divided the house of representatives at the Egyptian People's Assembly into 

two: One he called the ruling National Party and the second the opposition. And from 
there started the Egyptian opposition by a decree from As-Sadat who in fact created 

the opposition. "But this has opened the door wide for the genuine opposition forces." 


The revolution's leader repeated that certain Arab regimes have benefited from the 
opposition and the presence of an overt opposition has prolonged the life of the regimes 
and gave them the false democratic identity. Even As-Sadat, the enemy of the people and 
the historic traitor of this nation who committed the ultimate crime and sin which 
cannot be forgiven by recognising and visiting the Zionist state over [word indistinct] 
Palestinian Arab land and by prostrating himself before the Israeli flag, has boasted 
one day and said Egypt is the state of democratic [traditions] which has an opposition 
and there is no opposition in Libya. As-Sadat said these words with pride and with 
ignorance too because the opposition in Libya might have existed if there has been an 
authority ruling the people, because the people has the right to oppose this authority 
through peaceful means or by force, whether overtly or covertly. But when the struggle 
develops and takes the people to power, when they (?form) people's congresses which is 
the end of the road of the people's struggle for democracy, where the government dis- 
appears and the people take over all the government's functions, then the opposition 
disappears totally and radically and there would be no more grounds for the opposition 
because the whole opposing side had taken over power. 


He added: "Any opposition after that is considered an opposition against the people 
and the people's authority; while the current Arab opposition has an absolute context 
of opposing the authority which controls the people, as it is natural that the people 
produces forces opposed to the authority which rules it so as to create enough pressure 
to make the authority lift or reduce its authoritarian measures or in some cases 
express the wishes of the people through available means which if non-existant, the 
people resort to underground activities.
