• The cable thanks France for voting in favour of a UN Security Council resolution supporting Palestinian rights, after an American veto.
• The cable describes Libya as the first to build bridges of friendship and cooperation with France.
• The message is signed Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and dated 2 Safar 1396 Hegira, 2 February 1976.
• 1976-02-02: Qadhdhafi sends thank-you cable to French President Valery Giscard d'Estaing over France's UN Security Council vote on Palestinian rights; broadcast by Tripoli Domestic Service on 2 February 1976.
