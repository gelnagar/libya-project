Jallud Speech at Banquet 
LDO11028 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0720 GMT 1 Feb 85 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, | Feb (JANA) Major “Abd al-Salam 
Ahmad Jallud gave a banquet yesterday evening in honor of 
Grisha Filipov, member of the Bulgarian Communist Party 
Central Committee Politburo and premier, and members of his 
delegation. The banquet was attended by the secretaries of the 
General People’s Committee and heads of political missions in 
the Jamahiriyah. 


During the banquet Major Jallud delivered a speech in which he 
welcomed Grisha Filipov and the accompanying delegation. He 
said: The exchange of visits between the Jamahiriyah and 
Bulgaria has become a tradition performed on all levels. We hold 
these visits in high esteem. Our talks today have been very 
fruitful, he added. He referred to the exchange of visits between 
Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi and Todor Zhivkov which laid 
the basis and opened the scope for strong friendship and cooper- 
ation between the two countries. Jallud said: Our talks today 
have proven once more that our views, analyses, and attitudes 
toward the issues that concern us and the world at large are 
identical. 


Major Jallud pointed out that cooperation between Bulgaria and 
the Jamahiriyah does not aim at the immediate interests of 
Bulgaria and Libya but at increasing our struggle and rev- 
olutionary capabilities. 


Major Jallud said that like the Jamahiriyah, Bulgaria is exposed 
to attack from several sides because of its steadfast attitude 
toward the liberation movements and progressive forces. He 
affirmed the importance of meetings between the two countries 
which enabled them to review the many issues in which the 
Jamahiriyah and Bulgaria play prominent roles due to their 
commitment to the causes of freedom, peace, and progress 


Jallud said the Jamahiriyah and Bulgaria have morale and 
struggle capabilities which can play a major role — a role even 
the imperialist powers must reckon with. He added that might is 
not a matter of material resources, but of strong faith and firm 
principles, and adherence to these principles. 


During the past 4 years, he said, the world has seen problems 
which genuinely threaten the existence of mankind, and it 
appears that the next 4 years will be an extension and an 
escalation of these problems.
