LIBYA

11 OFFICERS REPORTEDLY EXECUTED BY GOVERNMENT 

NC250829 Cairo MENA in Arabic 0732 GMT 25 Apr 79 NC 


[Text] Cairo, 25 April--Citing diplomatic sources in Tripoli, today's edition of the 
weekly AKHIR SA'AH says that in recent weeks, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's intelligence 
service has uncovered several attempts by Libyan officers to assassinate Al-Qadhdhafi 
and overthrow his regime; the officers were dissatisfied with his irresponsible 
behavior and unsuccessful policies, 


The magazine says that the Libyan intelligence service arrested a number of Libyan 
pilots who were about to take off in their planes to strike the colonel's headquarters 
with rockets, It adds that 11 other Libyan officers were executed on the charge of 
Placing explosives in Al-Qadhdhafi's car.

The attempt was discovered at the last moment when an officer taking part in the 
operation broke down and informed the authorities about the explosives in the car, 
All the officers were arrested and executed immediately without trial, including the 
officer who exposed the plan, 


The magazine says Al-Qadhdhafi watched the execution by hanging. It adds that Al-Qadhdhafi 
is in a constant state of fear after the crushing defeat of his forces in Uganda. He 

has distributed weapons to the loyal tribes of Surt to protect him against any further 

coup or assassination attempts, 


JANA EDITOR DENOUNCES EGYPTIAN ALLEGATIONS ABOUT CHAD 
ID210903 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 21 Apr 79 ID 


[Text ] Tripoli--JANA's political editor has denounced the Egyptian Government!s 
condemnation of what it described as Libyan aggression in northern Chad, The editor 
said that the allegations of the Egyptian Government merely serve to expose the 

Egyptian regime's premeditated intention to carry our some action against the Jamahiriyah 
now that the regime has admitted defeat at the hands of the Zionist enemy and has become 
a willing tool of U.S. imperialism and its puppet, Israel. 


The political editor warned that any Egyptian military intervention in Chad would be 
met with a decisive intervention in Sudan which would completely eliminate Egyptian 
influence there, 


The editor said that the Egyptian Government should at once stop talking about (?questions) 
of peoples struggling for their freedom now that it has compromised the freedom of its 
land and the sovereignty of its people and after agreeing to surrender Palestine and 
Jerusalem to the Zionist and racist colonialists, thus disowning the most sacred Arab 

and Islamic cause, 


TALKS BETWEEN AT-TURAYKI, SUDANESE ENVOY REPORTED 


1D221656 Tripoli JANA in English 1440 GMT 22 Apr 79 ID 


[Text ] Tripoli, April 22, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The foreign secretary, Dr. ‘Ali 
tAbd as-Salam at-Turayki, this morning received the Sudanese ambassador to the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, Mr. ‘Abd al-Majid Bashir al-Ahmadi,. 


Upon instructions from his government, Mr. Ahmadi clarified the Sudanese position 
regarding the capitulation treaty signed by the defeated Egyptian president and Zionist 
enemy, 


He said the Government of Sudan opposed the treaty and adhered to the liberation of all 
occupied Arab lands including Jerusalem and the Palestine people's right of establishing 
an independent state over its own land and determining its own future, 


The Sudanese ambassador told Dr. at-Turayki that his country had from the beginning 
opposed the general framework of the "Stable David" accords.

MAURITANIAN FOREIGN MINISTER ARRIVES, COMMENTS 


LD221630 Tripoli JANA in English 1428 GMT 22 apr 79 LD 


(Text ] Tripoli, April 22, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Mauritanian foreign minister, Mr 
Ahmed Ould Abdallah, arrived in Tripoli yesterday evening on a short visit to the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, 


Present at the Mauritanian foreign minister's arrival in Tripoli International Airport 
were the foreign secretary, Dr. 'tAli 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, the deputy undersecretary 
for political affairs, directorate heads of the Foreign Secretariat, the Jamahiriyah's 
ambassador to Mauritania, and the Mauritanian ambassador to the Jamahiriyah, 


Mr Ould Abdallah expressed pleasure for visiting the Socialist People's Libyan arab 
Jamahiriyah, 


In a statement to the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY's correspondent, he said his visit fell 
within the context of exchanging views regarding relations between the Jamahiriyah and 
Mauritania, and the situation in the Arab region. He said he will endeavour through 
this visit to bolster relations between both countries, 


Commenting on the efforts being exerted by the Islamic Republic of Mauritania for the 
implementation of the Baghdad Arab foreign and economic ministers! conference resolutions 
boycotting the defeated Egyptian regime, Mr, Ould Abdallah confirmed that his country 
had already started the implementation of these resolutions by the withdrawal of its 
ambassador from Cairo, 


He said Mauritania is totally committed to the Arab foreign and economic ministers! 
conference resolutions, 


Commenting on the efforts being exerted for finding a peaceful solution to the Western 
Sahara problem, the Mauritanian foreign minister said his country has always been ready to 
participate in finding a peaceful solution to this problem and other problems in the Arab 
nation. 


Ends Visit 23 April 
LD232104 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1809 GMT 23 apr 79 LD 


(Text ] Tripoli--Mauritanian Foreign Minister Ahmed Ould Abdallah left Tripoli this 
afternoon following a 2-day visit to Libya. 


The Mauritanian minister was seen off from Tripoli International Airport by the assistant 
undersecretary for political affairs at the Foreign Secretariat, the head of administra- 
tion at the Foreign Secretariat, the Jamahiriyah ambassador to Mauritania and the 
Mauritanian ambassador to the Jamahiriyah, 


In a statement to the JANA correspondent, the Mauritanian minister praised relations 
between the two fraternal peoples and lauded the results of his visit.
