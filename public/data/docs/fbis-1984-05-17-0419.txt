LIBYA

Tunisia’s Recall of Envoy Prompts ‘Extreme Regret’ 
LD162031 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1951 GMT 16 May 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 May (JANA) — The People’s Committee of 
the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison this evening issued a 
statement concerning the Tunisian Government's decision to 
recall its commissioner general from the Jamahiriyah. The text 
of the statement follows: 


The People’s Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison affirmed in an earlier statement its anxiety to safeguard 
relations between the Jamahiriyah and fraternal Tunisia and to 
protect these relations from anything that might endanger them. 


But unfortunately, as customary in the Arab homeland, when an 
error is committed, instead rectifying it when tackling it, other 
errors are committed while defending it. In the Arab homeland, 
we have become accustomed to severing relations and to recalling 
representatives. 


It is known that relations among neighbors always lead to 
questions, and when unity and integration become subject to such 
interactions, no progress can be made toward that end. 


Accusing the Libyan Arab people in the exercise of their author- 
ity with unethical actions is unacceptable. Such an accusation is 
in itself unethical. 


Arab rulers have become used to not heeding the views of the 
ordinary Arab citizen or respecting his opinion. But what Lib- 
yans say at their People’s Conference exercising their authority 
is an expression of their views directly, and not through false 
parliaments or ruling parties. 


This concerns the Libyan Arab people and nobody else. If the 
Tunisiar officials, who possess authority, can recall envoys and 
sever relations, the People’s Committee of the People’s Bureau 
for Foreign Liaison possesses no such authority, which is vested 
only in the Basic People’s Conferences. The People’s Committee 
of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison can only express 
regret at this. 


While expressing its extreme regret at the Tunisian Govern- 
ment’s decision to recall its envoy, the People’s Committee of the 
People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison affirms this action will not 
serve the Tunisian people, and can serve only a specific group 
who seek to create problems and to harm the relations between 
the two fraternal peoples. These elements will one day pay the 


price. 


At-Turayki Receives Arab League Official ‘Imran 
LD161805 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1725 GMT 16 May 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 May (JANA) — Dr ‘Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at- 
Turayki, secretary of the People’s Committee of the People’s 
Bureau for Foreign Liaison, this morning received ‘Adnan 
‘Imran, Arab League assistant secretary general. 


Discussions during the meeting covered the latest developments 
in the Arab area and ways to bolster Arab-African cooperation 
in all fields. 


Further Report on 16 May Banghazi Demonstration 
LD161902 Tripoli JANA in English 1730 GMT 16 May 84 


[Text] Al Bayan al Awwal, Sha‘ban 16, May 16, JAMAHIR- 
IYAH NEWS AGENCY Huge masses of People’s Con- 
gresses in the city of Al Bayan al Awwal (Banghazi) went out to 
the streets this morning, in an angry demonstration challenging 
the plotters and Muslim Brotherhood cliques, agents of America, 
party members, stray traitors and scoundrels and showing 
readiness and vigilance to confront and die in defence of people's 
authority, great achievements and gigantic revolutionary accom- 
plishments achieved by the Great al-Fatih Revolution and its 
leader Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi. 


The participating masses marched carrying banners calling for 


Our lives sacrifice for the people's authority 

Death to the enemy of the people's authority 

Immediate execution to the agent terrorists. 

We will become suicidal groups to deter the enemies 

Physical liquidation for the traitors and renegades. 

Alliance with the Sudanese people to overthrow the agent bloody 
regime. 

Ignition of the revolution against the plotters and wiping out of 
terrorist cells. 

Conspiracies of imperialists and their aids will not frighten us. 


Thousands of participants in the demonstrations of anger and 
challenge chanted revolutionary slogans stressing the eternal 
alliance with the Great al-Fatih Revolution and its leader and 
committment to the Green Thought, people’s authority and to 
die for the victory of the people’s will. 


Demonstrators stressed in their slogans and placards that the 
people who have control over their destinies in power, wealth and 
arms will not be defeated and teach the enemies an unforgettable 
lesson that will bring about their total defeat and their end one 
by one, in the same way as the conspiring terrorists who infil- 
trated to the Jamahiriyah for sabotage against the people's 
establishments and civilized achievements of the Great al-Fatih 
Revolution. Their fate was doom, the same inevitable for every 
agent and traitor. 


Slogans and banners raised by the demonstrators reflect the 
wicked targets of the Muslim Brotherhood cliques, the fascist 
means and dirty ways of sabotage, assassination and treason. 


The slogans reveal the nature of this agent and traitor clique 
affirming that the so-called Front for Salvation of Libya is 
nothing more than a cover-up to Muslim Brotherhood, America’s 
agents who were eliminated inside. Everyone saw their fate on 
the hands of the people. Only a very few number of them are still 
at large abroad and are wanted by the Interpol for forging and 
stealing people’s money.

Speeches were delivered by the Basic People’s and Arab Con- 
gresses on the demonstration of anger and challenge confirming 
collaboration of the Arab people with the Libyan Arab people 
and great revolution and thinker, revolutionary leader Colonel 
Mu‘ammar Al-Qadhdhafi as well as continuous readiness to die 
in defence of aims and principles of Great al-Fatih Revolution. 


The Palestinian Arab masses confirmed in their speech that the 
Arab nation will not forget the black history of colonial countries 
in Sabra, Shatila, Kafar Gassim and Gulf of Sidra and fascist 
massacres in our deserts which grew human principles and ideals. 


The Palestinian Arab masses declared that according to the deep 
rooted and strong relations with the Libyan Arab people and their 
historical leadership they renew their support to the Libyan Arab 
people and their leader, who brought out the Jamahira era and 
are ready continuously to die on the path of the Green March 
and in defence of the Arab nation. 


The Sudanese Arab masses who participated in the demonstra- 
tion of anger and challenge have condemned the terrorist and 
sabotage actions of the Muslim Brotherhood cliques, agents of 
America, against the Libyan Arab people. 


The Sudanese revolution force in the Jamahiryah declared and 
confirmed their eternal collaboration with the Libyan Arab 
people and its great revolution and thinker and revolutionary 
leader Colonel! Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi against all imperialist, 
Zionist reactionary Conspiracies and firm determination to esca- 
late the people’s revolution in the Sudan to overthrow the fascist 
hireling Numayri who transformed the Sudan into an imperialist 
colony polluted by American forces. 


The Egyptian Arab masses participating in the demonstration of 
anger and challenge have confirmed their collaboration with 
Libyan Arab people and historical leadership in their brave 
confrontation of conspiracies of terror by hired partisans, traitors 
and American agents. 


The Egyptian masses confirmed in their speech that the masses 
era is the only way to liberate the human being for ultimate 
salvation from slavery to rule himself by himself and self- 
determination. 


The Egyptian masses explained that thanks to the Great al-Fatih 
Revolution and Green Thought and human approaches which 
spread all over the world, the American British terror and the 
agent regime in the Sudan will not undermine these 
achievements. 


The speech of the Basic People’s Congress of the Egyptian Arab 
people confirmed their constant readiness for confrontation and 
death to liquidate this rotten gang of stray dogs and reactionary 
destructive forces which lost their Arabic and Islamic affiliation. 


The Egyptian masses in the city of Al Bayan al Awwal declared 
their challenge of American and British imperialism and agent 
Arab regimes which had financed and trained the Muslim 
Brotherhood clique, agent of America as well as their total 
readiness to hit American and British interests wherever they 
are.
