LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Returns From African Tour 1 May 
LD020935 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 2 May 83 


{Text] Tripoli, 2 May (JANA) The brother leader of the 
revolution last night concluded a working visit to Africa which 
included visits to the Federal Republic of Nigeria, the Popular 
Republic of Benin, and the Republic of Upper Volta. The brother 
leader was accorded great popular and official welcomes during 
this visit. Popular masses in these states took to the streets and 
squares and rushed to the airports in order to greet the leader of 


3 May 83 


the Great | September Revolution and to express their joy at 
welcoming the African revolutionary 


The leader of the revolution arrived in Ash-Shararah al-Ula from 
Ouagadougou, and a huge crowd was at the airport to welcome 
him. The masses mobbed the brother leader of the revolution 
while chanting revolutionary slogans that stressed their faith in 
and attachment to the Great | September Revolution 


The brother leader left Ouagadougou airport at 2030 last evening 
Jamahiriyah time. The leader was accorded a grand popular and 
official send-off at Ouagadougou airport by Major Dr Jean- 
Baptiste Ouedraogo, president of the People’s Salvation Council 
[CSP], and Captain Thomas Sankara, prime minister 


Members of the CSP and government, the chief of siaff of the 
armed forces, senior officers of the Upper Volta Armed Forces, 
the secretary and staff of the Jamahiriyah mission in 
Ouagadougou, the heads of political missons accredited to Upper 
Volta and a huge crowd participated in the leader's send-off 
Immense crowds of Ouagadougou residents lined the street from 
the leader's residence to the airport, greeting the brother leader 
and chanting slogans which stressed the need for African unity, 
integration. and cooperation 


They also chanted slogans about the need to step up the struggle 
in order to counter imperialism and eliminate racism and colo- 
nialism in the dark continent 


Al-Qadhdhafi Telegram Thanks Upper Volta Leader 
LD020949 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 2 May 83 


[Text] Ouagadougou, 2 May (JANA) — The brother leader of 
the revolution sent a telegram to Major Dr Jean-Baptiste 
Ouedraogo, president of the People’s Salvation Council [CSP] of 
Upper Volta, on leaving Ouagadougou last night. Here is the text 
of the telegram 


Brother President Jean-Baptiste, it pleases me while leaving your 
friendly country to voice my thanks and gratitude for the splendid 
fraternal welcome accorded to us by Your Excellency and also 
by members of the CSP and Government of Upper Volta. The 
splendid popular welcome given to us by the friendly people of 
Upper Volta stressed the depth of ties between our two African 
peoples 


Brother president, the African and international issues we dis- 
cussed required coordination between our two countries so that 
they will be able to perform their role in securing the progress of 
Africa and the triumph of its causes of liberation, and to affirm 
their steadfastness in the face of the conspiracies of imperialism, 
Zionism, and reaction 


Brother President, finally | express my gratitude for the results 
achieved by the delegations of our two countries, which are likely 
to bring about integration and cooperation between our two 
countries in various domains 


Forward. the struggle continues
