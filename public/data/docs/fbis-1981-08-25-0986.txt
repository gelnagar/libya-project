LIBYA

SYRIAN CHIEF OF STAFF VISITS, MEETS OFFICIALS 


Jallud, Jabir Meetings 
JN231714 Damascus Domestic Service in Arabic 1615 GMT 23 Aug 81 


[Text] Tripoli--Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud today received Gen Hikmat ash-Shihabi, chief of 
the General Staff of the Army and the Armed Forces in the Syrian Arab Republic. Last night, 
a meeting was held between General ash-Shihabi and Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander 
in chief of the Libyan Armed Forces. The meeting was attended by members of the Syrian 

and Libyan delegations. 


During the meeting Ash-Shihabi stressed the importance of the fraternal relations and ties 
between the two countries and said: We have come here at the proper time in order to 
affirm our profound relations of struggle and joint pan-Arab commitment. Ash-Shihabi added: 
Your steadfastness and ours will be bolstered through the establishment of the unity of 
struggle so that all plots and conspiracies will be foiled, whether within the framework 

of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front or through close cooperation between us. 


General ash-Shihabi praised the steadfastness of the Libyan people in the face of the 
aggressors and said: Our presence among our brothers in these circumstances bears great 
significance. We look upon the Jamahiriyah as a revolutionary pioneer in the Arab homeland. 
He pointed out that the imperialist offensive launched against the Libyan people by the 
leader of terrorism, the United States, is also launched against Syria by agents both from 
within and without. 


24 Aug Departure 
LD241322 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 24 Aug 81 
[Text] Chief of the Syrian General Staff Maj GenHikmat ash-Shihabi and the accompanying 
delegation left Libya this morning at the end of a visit to the Jamahiriyah. He was seen 
off by Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces' 


officers. 


PFLO MESSAGE TO AL-QADHDHAFI ON SIDRA INCIDENT 


LD241946 Aden Voice of PFLO in Arabic 1545 GMT 24 Aug 81 


[Message from the PFLO Central Executive Committee to Libyan leader Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi; 
date not given] 


[Text] Esteemed Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 1 September Revolu- 
tion: In the name of the central leadership of the PFLO and in the name of the front's 
fighters and the masses of our militant people, we condemn the American aggression against 
the Jamahiriyah and its heroic people. American maneuvers and provocations near your 
borders which recently culminated in attacks against your aircraft in Libyan territory and 
over Libyan territorial waters have been the object of denunciation by the militant peoples 
of the world. What happened was a flagrant aggression against the UN principles and charter 
and a provocation against the Arab nation as a whole. 


Dear militant brother, as we condemn this aggression we appeal to all the sons of our Arab 
nation to stand in one rank against the United States and its reactionary supporters in the 
Arab region and to teach it lessons by striking at its interests and boycott it economically 
and politically until it (?realizes) that this nation cannot be downtrodden and that it has 
creative capabilities and resources for struggle to defend national sovereignty, however 
mighty the imperialist onslaught. 


V. 25 Aug 81 Q2 NORTH AFRICA 


In order to defeat the onslaught and its formations, the masses of our struggling nation 
must be mobilized and thrown into an intensive battle against imperialism, Zionism and 
reaction. 

We hail your steadfastness and your heroic stands. We are with you in the same trench, 
assuring you that we shall not hesitate for a moment to contribute to the persevering 
struggle to defeat arrogant America and its supporters. 


Dear militant brother, we declare our full solidarity with you and place all our potentials 
at your disposal for the protection of revolutionary and steadfast Libya. 


From your brothers, the PFLO Central Executive Committee
