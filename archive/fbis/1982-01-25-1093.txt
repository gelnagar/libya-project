20 Jan Working Session 
LD201500 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 20 Jan 82 


[Text] A working session was held this morning between the commander in chief of the armed 
forces, Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, and the Yugoslav federal secretary of national defense, Gen 
Nikola Ljubicic. After this session, plenary talks began between Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir 
and Gen Nikola Luubicic, in which the delegation accompanying Gen Ljubicic and some officers 
of the Libyan Armed Forces took part. 


The commander of urban militarization, Maj Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi, today at noon received at 
his office the Yugoslav federal secretary for national defense, who currently is visiting the 
Jamahiriyah. Humaydi explained fully to the guest the armed people's experiment which cannot 
be defeated and which is being implemented on the land of the Great Fatih and also the 
insistence by the revolution under the leadership of Col al-Qadhdhafi that the people must 
possess authority, resources and arms in support and defense of freedom and dignity, now 

that these have been realized thanks to the Great Fatih Revolution. Gen Luubicic expressed 
admiration and appreciation of what is taking place in the Jamahiriyah in connection with the 
realization of establishing the armed people which cannot be defeated in accordance to the 
prepared program. The guest stressed the importance of exchanging experience in militarization 
by way of exchanged visits between the two countries. This morning, the guest laid a wreath 
on the monument of the martyrs of the battle of Al-Hani.

AL-QADHDHAFI OPENS NEW MARKET COMPLEX IN TRIPOLI 


NC151152 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 15 Jan 82 


[Excerpt] The revolution leader yesterday opened a people's supermarket complex in the 
Al-Jum'ah market in Tripoli within the framework of the giant accomplishments made, as part 
of the socialist changes, by the Libyan Arab people in the course of the Great 1 September 
Revolution. 


The leader wrote the following message in the market's register after the opening: 


In the name of God, the opening of this new people's market in the Al-Jum'ah market area 
is another step toward establishing the people's society -- the new society free of 
exploitation, extortion, stealing money and robbing their efforts in the name of profit 
which is the principal justification for exploitation.

AL-QADHDHAFI DISCUSSES ISLAM AT KORAN SYMPOSIUM 


LD170130 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0015 GMT 17 Jan 82 


[Text] The leader of the revolution yesterday evening attended the third international 
Koran symposium held in Tripoli. Contestants in learning and reciting the Koran from 36 
Arab, Islamic and African states and Islamic minorities from various continents took part. 


Those present and taking part warmly welcomed the brother leader. They chanted slogans 
Praising the stands of the Great 1 September Revolution toward Islam, strengthening the 
Status of Muslims and strengthening their influence. 


The leader explained that if Islam is purely a spiritual cause concerning the belief in 
almighty God, his divine books, Prophets, angels and the day of judgment, its obedience 
leads to heaven and its violation leads to hell. In this case, it is a purely personal 
matter between the individual and God directly and that there is no connection in such a 
situation between one Muslim and another. 


As for the relationship in which the individual needs a fellow individual of the nation 
needs another nation and the group needs another group, this becomes a case of (?affiliation) 
and it is an internal issue and it has no (?influence), [sentence as heard] The brother 
leader called for (?entrenching) Islam so that it can have a political meaning. He said 


that we stand together as one force not only for the sake of the faith but also to confront 
the enemy and defend our freedom. The nations adopting Islam as their faith should benefit 
from one another and they should fight united and solve the problems they face among themselves. 


it becomes a political league of the people with a common religion, so that we support one 
another and all ourselves against the common enemy, he said. The Islamic world means that 
its 1 tions should be allied and united; they have common economic, political and military 
interests; and they have a common enemy. 


In conclusion, the leader affirmed that we have to take advantage of Islam so that we can 
give ourselves a common identify and exploit this benefit politically, €conomically and 
militarily. The brother leader explained that the West European powers despite the fact 
that they are not linked by ties, nevertheless, give themselves one identity, namely, the 
NATO organization. They exploit it politically, economically and militarily. They made 
themselves one bloc to oppose other blocs.
