LIBYA

JANA ASSAILS NUMAYRI'S 'RECOGNITION' OF ISRAEL 


LD240850 Tripoli JANA in English 0812 GMT 24 Sep 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulhijja 5, Sept 23, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Numayri has today declared 
himself to be unequivocally moving along the path charted out by the traitor As-Sadat and 
is, therefore, ready to acknowledge the existence of the enemy, grant it recognition and 
conclude treaties of defeat with it. 


In a speech delivered yesterday following his meeting with Yasir ‘Arafat he declared that 
the grave mistake committed by the Arabs in the past was their refusal to recognize the 
adversary they have been fighting, especially as it is a recognized state and a member in 
the U.N. : 


Numayri's statement which was published by the official Sudanese News Agency yesterday 
added that Article 7 of the Fes proposal regarding the guarantee of the security of the 
countries of the region, including a Palestinian state, does not constitute a recognition 
of Israel but is actually a "de facto" admission. 


JANA's political editor commented on this stand saying: 


"Throughout As-Sadat's reign, the hireling Numayri has piped As-Sadat's tune and championed 
the treacherous Camp David accords to gain the goodwill of the Pentagon's pirates. This 
stand also qualified him to be his master's voice with complete disregard to any sense of 
values and in contradiction of Arab character. He has descended to the level of hypocrites, 
pawns, and political hirelings. 


Furthermore, it is not surprising that Numayri may now emerge from covert dealings with the 
"peace" callers to a deeper and overt involvement with those urging a speedy settlement 
with the Zionist enemy.
