LIBYA

JANA ON 'REJOICING' OVER AL-JUMAYYIL KILLING 


LD161310 Tripoli JANA in English 0829 GMT 16 Sep 82 


{Text] Tripoli, Dhulqidah 28 September 16, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The dynamic forces 
within the Arab nation executed yesterday another As-Sadat who had committed high 
treason against its existence. 


Traitor Bashir al-Jumayyil met his end at the hands of this honourable force which rejects 
treason and treachery and which is committed to rooting out Israeli agents and those 
who recognise (?Israel) or deal with it. 


Like As-Sadat's, Al-Jumayyil's death is something to rejoice over for the Arab masses. Days 
to come will witness the end of all traitors who betrayed their nation and who publicly 
announced their desire in having relations with the Zionist enemy and with American 
imperialism. 


This rejoicing is explained by the fact that Al-Jumayyil had betrayed this (?nation) and had 
toed the American line and had established dealings with the Israelis and had made the plans 
to push the Zionist death wagon into further Arab positions. 


The killing of Bashir al-Jumayyil, after convicting him of betraying the nation, emphasizes 
the determination of the Arab people to continue the struggle against all traitors, all 
renegades and all the traitors who gathered in Fes. 

The dynamic force within the Arab nation asserted its effectiveness despite the psychological 
defeats which hit the weak and defeatists who raised the banners of recognising the Zionist 
enemy. 


The end of all these will be at the hands of the honourable elements within this nation. 


FOREIGN SECRETARY RECEIVES TANZANIAN COUNTERPART 


LD161734 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 16 Sep 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 Sep (JANA) -- The secretary of the General People's Committee for 
Foreign Liaison conferred this morning in Tripoli with the Tanzanian foreign minister, Salim 
Ahmad Salim, and the accompanying delegation. , 


The secretary of the General People's Committee for Foreign Liaison delivered a speech 

in which he expressed his appreciation for Tanzania's stances and its backing of the 

Arab causes, chiefly the number one Arab cause, Palestine, and for the role of Tanzania and 
President Julius Nyerere in consolidating the independence of the African Continent and 
backing its liberation movements. 


He said: We in the Jamahiriyah consider ourselves and Tanzania to be marching in the same 
direction with a view to achieving genuine independence for Africa and endeavoring to halt 
the imperialist-Zionist tide in this continent. 


The secretary of the General People's Committee for Foreign Liaison stressed that 
imperialism is trying in earnest to stop the wheels of progress in Africa in an attempt 
to impose its influence on this continent. He said that without a collective struggle we 
will be under political and economic pressures aimed at subjugating us and at destroying 
us economically. He explained that unless there is African unity, both racist regimes 

in South Africa and occupied Palestine will be able to achieve their strategic goals 

in the African Continent and in the Arab homeland.

The secretary of the General People's Committee for Foreign Liaison emphasized in his 
speech that the visit of the Tanzanian foreign minister and the accompanying delegation 
gives new prospects of true cooperation between the Jamahiriyah and Tanzania. 


For his part, the Tanzanian foreign minister stressed his country's desire to develop and 
consolidate cooperation with the Jamahiriyah, particularly in confronting the imperialist- 
Zionist plots aimed against the freedom and unity of the African Continent and at the 
destruction of the Arab nation. He said we are struggling against and resisting these 
plots together and we have all the reasons for achieving exemplary cooperation between 

the Jamahiriyah and Tanzania in view of the fact that we are sincere Africans who extend 

a helping hand to the liberation movements -- to support their struggle to achieve their 
freedom. 


In his speech, the Tanzanian foreign minister referred to the fruitful talks which Julius 
Nyerere conducted with the brother leader of the revolution in Tripoli and stressed that 
these talks were extremely important in interpreting the spirit of understanding prevailing 
in their relations. He said his country appreciates the role of the Jamahiriyah in the 
struggle for the freedom and unity of Africa and the Arab nation and that it is proud of 
the pioneering stances of the Great 1 September Revolution and the sacrifices made by the 
Libyan Arab people in support of the causes of their brothers in the African Continent.
