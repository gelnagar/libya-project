Summary (AI): An AP report from Fort Collins, Colorado says a jury could not reach a verdict in the trial of ex-Green Beret Eugene Tafoya, accused of shooting Libyan dissident Faisal Zagallai in October 1980. Prosecutors linked the shooting to fugitive ex-CIA agent Edwin Wilson, and the case has no Tripoli location.
• Prosecutors alleged Tafoya was paid about $8,600 to kill Zagallai because Zagallai criticized Qaddafi's regime.
• The jury deliberated 54 hours before failing to reach a verdict.
• Possible sentences ranged from two to 24 years.
• Mohammed Shabata was sentenced to five years to life in Utah for the death of Libyan student Nabi Mansour.
• 1980-10-14: Faisal Zagallai was shot twice in the head with Tafoya's .22-cal. revolver; he survived but lost an eye.
