Summary (AI): An NPIC briefing note from February 1982 reports Soviet naval vessels at Tubruq (Tobruk) port facilities in eastern Libya, including a Moskva-class cruiser, a modified Kashin destroyer and a submarine. Tobruk is far from Tripoli, so this is background on Soviet naval presence in Libya.
• First major Soviet port call of 1982 at Tubruq.
• One Moskva guided missile aviation cruiser (CHG), one modified Kashin guided missile destroyer (DDG) and one attack submarine (SS) were at Tubruq.
• The previous Soviet port call to Tubruq had one modified Kashin DDG, one Kashin DDG and one submarine present.
• Coordinates given: 32-02-00N 023-59-20E.
• 1982-02: Soviet port call at Tubruq with a Moskva-class aviation cruiser, a modified Kashin destroyer and a submarine; described as the first major Soviet port call of 1982.
