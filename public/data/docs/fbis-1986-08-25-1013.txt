Syria’s Al-Asad Arrives, Meets With Al-Qadhdhafi 
LD242037 Tripoli Voice af Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 2015 GMT 24 Aug 86 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution and Syrian President 
Hafiz al-Asad held a working session this evening. It was 
attended by Staff Major ‘Abd al-Salam Ahmad Jallud on the 
Libyan side, and by brother ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam, the Syrian 
vice president on the Syrian side. 


The president of the Syrian Arab Republic, Hafiz al-Asad, 
arrived in the Great Jamahiriyah this evening. He was accorded 
a popular and official welcome and the brother leader of the 
revolution was at the forefront of those who welcomed the Syrian 
president and the delegation accompanying him. Also taking part 
in the welcome were Staff Major ‘Abd al-Salam Ahmad Jallud, 
secretaries of people’s congresses and committees, and large 
masses of citizens. When President Hafiz al-Asad came down 
from the plane, he was embraced by the brother leader of the 
revolution. The Jamahiriyah and Syrian national anthems were 
played. The Syrian president is accompanied on this visit by 
brother ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam, the vice president; brother 
Rashid Akhtirini, member of the regional command of the Ba‘th 
Party; Dawud Ni‘mih, member of the Central Leadership of the 
National Progressive Front; and ‘Isam al-Na‘ib, minister of state 
for foreign affairs. 


Syrian President Hafiz al-Asad made a statement to pressmen 
on his arrival, in which he said: [read by announcer] I am 
pleased to be in the land of the Jamahiriyah that is steadfast in 
confronting imperialist schemes which are not only directed 
against Libya but against the Arab nation as well. It is well 
known that we meet on and exchange views from time to time 
and there are continual and uninterrupted contacts exist. We 
think that these direct contacts with brother Colonel Mu‘ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi contribute to bolstering joint action between us in 
a way that would make them more effective in confronting 
imperialism. On the occasion of my presence in Libya I would 
like to address a greeting of love to our Libyan Arab people and 
affirm to them that Syrian masses are standing with all their 
strength and unhesitantly in the same trench and same position. 
Any harm inflicted on Libya is inflicted on Syria, and any attack 
on Libya is an attack on Syria and the Arab nation. Syria will 
shoulder its responsibilities in confronting any attack on Libya 
in the same way as it would if the attack was on Syria. 


He said we view the steadfastness manifested by the fraternal 
Libyan people with appreciation and we stress our great appre- 
ciation and respect for this stand. Syria will remain in this 
position with Libya, as it always has, and will confront with it 
anything that would harm either of the two countries or any Arab 
country. He added: Weconsider the Libyan masses’ position as 
a pan-Arabist Arab position and indeed epitomizes the defense 
of the Arab cause. Therefore, this position is receiving the 
appreciation of the Arab masses.

KUNA Reports Talks With Tunisia in Switzerland 
LD241056 Kuwait KUNA in English 
1006 GMT 24 Aug 86 


[Text] Kuwait, 24 Aug (KUNA) — Kuwait A/-Watan today said 
that official unannounced negotiations have commenced in Swit- 
zerland since last Tuesday between the Tunisian and Libyan 
Governments in order to ease tension in the relations between the 
two North African Arab countries. 


The newspaper attributed to Tunisian sources, in a news item 
from Tunisia, that these negotiations concentrate on the means 
to resume economic relations, especially trade exchanges, 
between the two countries, and the basis for compensating Tuni- 
sian industrialists who exported their products to Libya and have 
not received their money because of the political situation 
between the two countries. 


Al-Watan attributed to Tunisian economic sources that Tunisia, 
because of the situation, has lost at least 150 million Tunisian 
dinars, that is, about $112 million. 


Al-Watan said that the two sides have nearly reached a final 
agreement on restoring the assets of Tunisian Airlines, which 
were frozen when air traffic between the two countries was 


suspended. 


It added that the land routes between Tripoli and the Tunisian 
south have been reopened since the second week of August, which 
is considered as an additional indicator toward reconciliation. 
Al-Watan attributed to well-informced sources that Arab 
League Secretary General Chadli Klibi has played an important 
role in this reconciliation. 


Tripoli Cites AL-WATAN 
LD241453 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 
1230 GMT 24 Aug 86 


[Text] Citing official Tunisian sources, the Kuwaiti paper Al- 
Watan reports today that there is a steady improvement in 
Tunisian-Libyan political and economic relations. The sources 


say that Tunisia has lost as a result of the policy pursued by 
dismissed Prime Minister Mohamed Mzali toward the Great 
Jamahiriyah. The sources say that the roads between the two 
countries have been reopened and that economic cooperation is 
being studied as a matter of priority.

Attends Military School Graduation 
LD241525 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 
1230 GMT 24 Aug 86 


[Excerpts] As part of the popular celebration of the 17th anniver- 
sary of the Great 1 September Revolution and on the eve of the 
people’s armed uprising, the fifth group of cadets at the Air 
Defense Technical School graduated today. The graduation cere- 
mony was held at the Air Defense Technical School in Tripoli 
and was attended by Major al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi; Paul 
Victor Obeng, member of Ghana's Provisional National Defense 
Council and coordinating secretary; and the delegation accom- 
pany him. Armed Forces officers and cadets’ parents also 
attended. [passage omitted] 


Major al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi distributed certificates to dis- 
tinguished students. Paul Victor Obeng, member of Ghana's 
Provisional National Defense Council; Armed forces officers; 
parents of graduate cadets; and members of the teaching staff at 
the air Defense Technical School took part in awarding cer- 
tificates. A graduate cadet took the oath that was repeated by 
other cadets. The graduates then chanted revolutionary slogans 
that underiined their commitment to the objectives and principles 
of the Great 1 September Revolution and their constant readiness 
to confront and crush all those who may think of transgressing 
on the Green Thought, the thought of complete emancipation 
from slavery and exploitation.
