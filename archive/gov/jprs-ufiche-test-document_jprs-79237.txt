LIBYA

RETIRED PAKISTANI MILITARY PERSONNEL IN SECURITY JOBS 
Paris AFRICA AFP in inglish No 2830, 22 Sep 81 pp 12-13 


[Text] Islamabad, 22 Sep* 2mber--Pakistan has asked Libya to supply information 
about thousands of ex-servicemen recruited for security jobs in that country, 
it was announced here Monday. 


According to reports, some 3,500 retired Army personnel, were recruited by a 
Libyan-based firm this year to work as security guards and supervisers. 


Endorsement of the plan was given by Pakistan's Labour Attache at Tripoli who 
provided that the emigrants would get training in Libya but would not be used 
in military, para-military or other defence organisations. 


Rumours had circulated that Libya had sent the trained Pakistanis to Chad for 
active fighting and their relatives, hearing nothing from them for six months, 
approached the authorities. 


Newspapers here also reported that the Pakistani emigrants were subjected to 
“maltreatment and were not accorded facilities" stipulated in the service agree- 
ment. 


Desert Training 


Official sources said the Government was fully aware of the situation and was 
trying to find out the correct state of affairs and “ameliorate the difficul- 
ties" faced by Pakistani emigrants. 


According to recruiting agents, letters received from the workers said that 
they were being trained in handling Libyan arms in the desert and this prevented 
them from writing home. 


Authoritative sources said that Pakistan had expressed "deep concern" with 


Libyan authorities and had asked Tripoli to allow Pakistani officials to visit 
the camps to check on the reports of maltreatment. (A.F.P.)
