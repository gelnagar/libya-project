TRIPOLI DOMESTIC SERVICE IN ARABIC 1652 GMT 28 MAR 85 

((EXCERPTS)) ((PASSAGE OMITTED)) THE IRANIAN PEOPLE RESPONDED 

TO THE CALL AND STAGED THE REVOLUTION; WE CANNOT BETRAY THIS 

REVOLUTION SINCE WE CALLED FOR WHATEVER HAPPENED. THESE PERSIANS 

SAY WE ARE ARABS, BUT THIS IS A REVOLUTION. WE CANNOT BETRAY THE 

REVOLUTION. 

HOW CAN WE FIGHT THE REVOLUTION WHICH LIBERATED IRAN FROM 

AMERICAN DOMINATION? IT IS A CRIME. THIS REVOLUTION HAS THROWN THE 

ENTIRE WEIGHT OF THE IRANIAN PEOPLE ON THE SIDE OF THE PALESTINE 

PEOPLE AND THE ARAB NATION AGAINST THE ISRAELIS AND AGAINST THE 

RACISTS OF SOUTH AFRICA. IT TOPPLED ALL THE POLICIES OF THE 

UNITED STATES AND THE SHAH IN THE REGION. 

HOW CAN WE FIGHT SUCH A REVOLUTION? IF THE IRANIANS WERE TO 

OCCUPY IRAQ, COLONIZE IT AND ANNEX IT, THEN THEY WOULD STOP BEING 

A REVOLUTIONARIES AND BECOME COLONIZERS. THEN WE WILL NO LONGER 

SAY AN [RANIAN REVOLUTION BUT A PERSIAN COLONIZATION. ( (APPLAUSE) ) 

WE ARE ON THE SIDE OF IRAN BECAUSE IT IS A REVOLUTION. BUT WHEN 

IT TURNS INTO A COLONIZATION WE WILL STAND AGAINST IT. THIS IS A 

WELL-KNOWN STAND AND WE REPEATEDLY TOLD THE IRANIANS: DO NOT BECOME 

COLONIZERS, FOR THEN WE WILL FIGHT YOU AND CONSIDER YOU 

IMPERIALISTS AND COLONIALISTS. WHEN YOU ARE REVOLUTIONARIES WE 

WILL BE REVOLUTIONARIES ON YOUR SIDE. THIS IS A NATURAL ALLIANCE 

AMONG REVOLUTIONARIES. ((APPLAUSE) ) 

WE DOUBT THAT THE REVOLUTION WOULD TURN INTO IMPERIALISM OR 

SECTARIANISM. IF IRAN TURNED INTO SECTARIANISM AND PLACED THE 

SHIITES ABOVE THE SUNNIS -- IF THERE ARE STILL ANY OF THIS 

HERITAGE -- AND IF IT OCCUPIED, COLONIZED AND MADE IRAQ SUBSERVIENT 

TO IT, IT MEANS THAT THE REVOLUTION WHICH WE SUPPORTED ONCE AND 

WHICH OCCURRED IN IRAN IS AT AN END. THEN IRAN IS NO LONGER 

REVOLUTIONARY IRAN BUT A PERSIAN RACIST SECTARIAN AND COLONIZER 

OF THE ARAB HOMELAND, AND WE WILL FIGHT THIS COLONIALISM. 

HOWEVER, THE REVOLUTION WOULD NEVER DO THIS, UNLESS THE REVOLUTION 

CEASES IN IRAN. IT IS ONLY A POSSIBILITY THAT THE REVOLUTION COULD 

BE ABORTED. WAS THERE NOT A REVOLUTION IN EGYPT WHICH ENDED? 

WAS NOT INDONESIA A COUNTRY OPPOSED TO IMPERIALISM AND THE 

NONALIGNED? NOW IT IS SUBSERVIENT TO AMERICA. WAS NOT GUINEA 

FIGHTING IMPERIALISM? IT IS NOW SUBSERVIENT TO THE ISRAELIS. 

THE REVOLUTION IN IRAN COULD BE ABORTED AND END, AND IRAN BECOME 

SECTARIAN, RACIST AND PERSIAN. THEN IT BECOMES COLONIALIST. OUR 

ATTITUDE TOWARD COLONIALISM IS CLEAR. IT NEEDS NO ARGUMENT. THERE 

IS NO NEED TO ASK US WHAT OUR POSITION WILL BE WHEN IRAN BECOMES 

COLONIALIST. OURS IS A PRINCIPLED ATTITUDE. 

ON THIS OCCASION, WHAT PAINS US IS THAT THE WAR HAS MOVED 

TO THE CITIES. IN FACT, I AM EXTREMELY DISAPPOINTED THAT THE WAR 

CHANGED FROM BRAVERY TO COWARDICE. BRAVE MEN FIGHT &ACH OTHER 

ON THE BATTLEFIELD. HOWEVER, TO RESORT TO CITIES, THIS IS A WAR 

FOR COWARDS. 

KHOMEYNI ((AS HEARD)) SENT ME A MESSAGE SAYING I AGREE THAT YOUR 

ANALYSIS WAS CORRECT AND THAT THIS IS A WAR OF COWARDS AND THAT 

WE ARE AGAINST STRIKING AT CITIES. I ONLY RESORTED TO STRIKING

CITIES BECAUSE IRAQ BEGAN TO STRIKE AT CITIES. IF IRAQ STOPS 

STRIKING CITIES, 1 AM PREPARED TO DO THE SAME. THIS WAS IN 

A WRITTEN MESSAGE. 

THE [RAQI REPLY CAME. IT SAID [ WILL NOT STOP STRIKING AT CITIES 

UNTIL IRAN AGREES TO NEGOTIATE TO END THE WAR. THIS IS VERY 

STRANGE. FOR THE FIRST TIME, STRIKING AT CIVILIAN TARGETS BECOMES 

A CONDITION FOR NEGOTIATION AND ENDING WAR. 

I AM REALLY SURPRISED. DO THE BROTHERS RULING IRAQ HAVE NO PITY 

FOR BAGHDAD? BAGHDAD IS OUR CITY AND ITS PEOPLE ARE OUR PEOPLE. 

INDEED, WE HAVE TOO MUCH PITY FOR IT. I BELIEVE THAT WHAT THEY 

ATTRIBUTE TO SADDAM HUSAYN -- THAT HE WILL NOT SURRENDER IRAQ UNTIL 

THERE ARE NO MORE PEOPLE IN IT -- IS NOW BEING IMPLEMENTED IN DEED. 

HE HAS BEGUN TO DRIVE THE IRAQI PEOPLE IN FRONT OF HIM SO THEY ARE 
' ANNIHILATED AND THEN IF HE SURRENDERS IRAQ, HE WILL SURRENDER IT 

WITHOUT PEOPLE. IT SEEMS THIS STATEMENT IS COMING TRUE. 

THIS IS EVIDENT. IT IS EVIDENT NOW THAT THE IRAQI PEOPLE ARE 

BEING SQUANDERED AND THAT THERE IS A DECISION TO DESTROY BAGHDAD 

BY THE IRAQI OFFICIALS THEMSELVES. 

KHOMEYNI HAS SAID I AM READY TO STOP STRIKING BAGHDAD 

AND THESE CITIES, PROVIDED THE IRAQIS STOP STRIKING MY TOWNS. 

BUT OUR BROTHERS IN IRAQ SAY WE WILL NOT STOP STRIKING CITIES 

UNLESS THE WHOLE WAR ENDS. THIS MEANS IRAN GOES AHEAD AND CONTINUES 

TO STRIKE BAGHDAD, BASRA AND OTHER CITIES UNTIL THE IRAQI PEOPLE 

ARE ANNIHILATED. THIS MEANS THAT THE IRAQI OFFICIALS BELIEVE IN 

FACT THAT IRAQ WILL SURRENDER AND SINCE IT IS GOING TO SURRENDER, 

THEN THE SAYING MUST BE IMPLEMENTED THAT IT BE SURRENDERED WITHOUT 

PEOPLE. THIS IS EVIDENT. 

NOBODY CAN DEFAME THE IRAQI ARMY OR ACCUSE IT OF NOT BEING BRAVE, 

OR NOBLE. IT IS AN ARAB NATIONALIST BRAVE AND NOBLE ARMY. THE 

IRAQI PILOTS ARE BRAVE PILOTS. 

THE IRAQIS FOUGHT IN OCTOBER BETWEEN THE ISRAELI AND EGYPTIAN 

FIRE. WHEN THEY RETURNED, EGYPTIAN MISSILES USED TO HIT THEM. 

WHEN THEY ATTACKED THE LAND OCCUPIED IN 1973, ISRAELI MISSILES FIRED 

AT THEM. AN IRAQI SQUADRON WAS SUBJECTED TO ISRAELI MISSILES 

AS IT ATTACKED THE OCCUPIED TERRITORY, BUT IT KNEW THAT THE ISRAELIS 

WERE RESISTING IT. ON ITS WAY BACK, I[T KNEW THAT THE EGYPTIANS 

WOULD FIRE AT IT. THEY -- THE EGYPTIANS -- FIRED AT ANY 

PLANE AND NOT ONLY THE IRAQI PLANES, BUT ANY PLANE AT ALL. IN 

FACT, EGYPT LOST HALF OF ITS OWN AIRCRAFT THIS WAY. AN 

IRAQI HAWK-HUNTER SQUADRON WAS ANNIHILATED THIS WAY. THEY WERE 

CENTERED IN THE DELTA. THE IRAQI PILOTS WERE BRAVE. THEY DID NOT 

COMPLAIN, SAYING WE GO TO THE ENEMY AND HE FIRES ON US AND WE 

RETURN TO YOU AND YOU FIRE ON US.
