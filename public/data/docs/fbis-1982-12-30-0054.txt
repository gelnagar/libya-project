LIBYA

JANA EDITOR ON U.S. ROLE IN LEBANON TALKS 
LD292306 Tripoli JANA in English 1740 GMT 29 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Awal 14, Dec 29, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- JANA political 
editor commented on the U.S. participation in the negotiations between the Lebanese 
authorities and Zionist enemy. 

He wrote: "The U.S. Administration is repeating its misleading and deceitful role 
which it played ten years ago at the 101-km negotiations to achieve strategic gains 
in the area for the Zionist enemy and its security. This role appears again in the 
negotiations which were imposed on Lebanon by Israeli occupation. The U.S. 
Administration is carrying out political manoeuvres and applying pressures on the 
Lebanese Government so as to achieve the objectives the Zionist failed to achieve 
through the military piracy and the mass killings in Sabra and Shatila. The 
participation of Reagan's administration in these negotiations can only mean one 
thing, which is to force the Lebanese authorities to sign a treaty similar to Camp 
David through the meanest methods that enabled it to impose these negotiations away 
from U.N. presence and international law consultations. All this clearly indicates 
that Reagan's administration is eager to secure its political objectives in the area 
and achieve Israel's aims in expansion and occupation. 


One should remember that the U.S. Administration has never been trustworthy in any 
negotiations or attempts to find just solutions to any issues disputed. All trust 
and integrity has disappeared through the ways adopted by U.S. administrations 

past and present, starting by Cisco and ending with Habib and Draper. Accordingly, 
one should imagine what would the Lebanese people benefit from negotiations in which 
the U.S. Administration is a party, especially when the third party is the Zionist 
enemy, which enters the negotiations from a strong position -- that of the occupier, 
who imposes his own conditions with the force of arms and the threat of the U.S. 
warship, anchored off Khaldah. Undoubtedly, the outcome will be tragic for the 
Lebanese people. 


Therefore, the Lebanese people, driven by their strong will power, nationalist and 
patriotic spirit to defend their freedom, dignity and Arabism, have no alternative 
but to launch a popular resistance that makes the Zionist occupation soldiers leave 
in coffins. Definitely, the dynamic forces in the Arab nation will rush to the 
support of this people and fight on its side against the occupation. 


The Libyan Arab people, which leads the battle of escalating the national struggle, 
will be in the front line with its brothers in Lebanon to drive out the usurpers and 


foil all their aggressive plots against the Arab nation.
