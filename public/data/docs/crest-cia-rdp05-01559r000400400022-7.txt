TAKE 1 OF 2 -- SUNA ON AL-QADHDHAFI VISIT, STATEMENT 
JN18 1832 
KHARTOUM SUNA IN ARABIC 1738 GMT 18 MAY 85 
((TEXT)) KHARTOUM, 18 MAY (SUNA) -- LIBYAN PRESIDENT 
COLONEL MU'AMMAR AL-QADHDHAFI, LEADER OF THE 1 SEPTEMBER 
REVOLUTION, MADE A STOPOVER AT KHARTOUM AIRPORT THIS AFTERNOON, 
WHERE HE MET WITH HIS BROTHER GENERAL ‘ABD AL-RAHMAN MUHAMMAD 
HASAN SIWAR AL-DHAHAB, CHAIRMAN OF THE TRANSITIONAL MILITARY 
COUNCIL. HIS EXCELLENCY HELD A 2-HOUR TALKS WITH GEN SIWAR 
AL-DHAHAB IN THE VIP ROOM AT THE AIRPORT. 
IN A STATEMENT TO THE PRESS FOLLOWING HIS ARRIVAL, COL 
AL-QADHDHAFI SAID THAT HE CAME HERE AS PART OF AN AFRICAN TOUR 
TO CONGRATULATE THE SUDANESE LEADERS AND HEROIC PEOPLE ON THE 
VICTORY OF THEIR POPULAR REVOLUTION AND THE PEOPLE'S ARMED 
FORCES' BIAS TOWARD THE PEOPLE TO AVERT BLOODSHED. HIS EXCELLENCY 
DESCRIBED THE PEOPLE'S ARMED FORCES' POSITION AS HISTORIC, 
NATIONALIST, CIVILIZED, REVOLUTIONARY, AND COURAGEOUS. 
PRESIDENT AL-QADHDHAFI ALSO CONGRATULATED THE HEROIC SUDANESE 
PEOPLE, WHO DEVELOPED OPPOSITION AGAINST THE REGIME TO THE DEGREE 
OF CIVIL DISOBDIENCE AND REBELLION. HE ADDED: I CALL ON THE 
OTHER ARAB PEOPLES TO FOLLOW SUDAN'S EXAMPLE AND LIQUIDATE THE 
FORCES OF REACTIONARIES AND DEFEATISTS IN THE SAME WAY THE SUDANESE 
PEOPLE AND THEIR ARMED FORCES LIQUIDATED .THE NUMAYRI REGIME. 
AL-QADHDHAFI ALSO CALLED ON ARMED FORCES IN THE ARAB COUNTRIES 
TO LIQUIDATE REACTIONARIES AND DEFEATISTS SO THAT THE ARAB NATION 
CAN UNIFY AND LIBERATE ITSELF AND SAFEGAURD ITS HERITAGE. HIS 
EXCELLENCY STRESSED THAT THE POPULAR REVOLUTION WILL MOVE ACROSS 
IMAGINARY BORDERS. NO ATTEMTPS BY ANY RULER CAN PREVENT IT, 
BECAUSE REVOLUTION DOES NOT RECOGNIZE IMAGINARY BORDERS. 
THE LIBYAN LEADER SAID THAT THE SUDANESE PEOPLE CARRIED OUT 
AN UNPRECEDENTED REVOLUTION WHICH AFFIRMED THAT POPULAR REVOLUTIONS 
CAN TOPPLE ARROGANT, VAIN GOVERNMENTS. THE SUDANESE PEOPLE'S 
ARMED FORCES PROVIDED A GREAT REVOLUTIONARY MODEL BY SIDING WITH 
THE NATIONAL FORCES AT CRITICAL MOMENTS. 
REFERRING TO NEWS AGENCY REPORTS ABOUT DEMONSTRATIONS IN 
EGYPT AGAINST THE ARAB NATION'S ENEMY, ISRAEL, AL-QADHDHAFI 
SAID THAT THE ARBA REVOLUTIONARY FORCES IN THE ARAB HOMELAND 
HAVE PROPOSED TO MAKE SUDAN THEIR GENERAL HEADQUARTERS FOR THE 
ARAB REVOLUTION'S FACTIONS FOR APPROVAL. HE SAID: WE ARE 
HONORED TO MAKE SUDAN A HEADQUARTERS FOR THE REVOLUTIONARY COMMAND 
IN THE ARAB HOMELAND. 
GEN SIWAR AL-DHAHAB AND THE GREAT GUEST, COL AL-QADHDHAFI, 
DROVE IN AN OPEN CAR IN THE OUTER YARD OF THE AIRPORT. THE 
MASSES RECEIVED THEM WITH SLOGANS PRAISING THE ARAB NATION, 
UNITY, AND SOLIDARITY.
