[Speech by Libyan leader Col Mu!'ammar al-Qadhdhafi on 1 September at a popular rally held 
at Al-Khadra! Square in Tripoli, marking the 10th anniversary of the 1 September 
revolution--recorded ] 


{Excerpts] We would like to say a few words regarding the domestic situation. You saw 
the military parade today. In fact, it was the biggest military parade. It showed the 
strength ane the dangerous nature of the weapons you now own. This requires that all these 
weapons should be in the hands of the people. The popular masses should have control over 
these weapons, because this formidable military might will be turned against ‘he popular 
masses unless the latter have control over it. If this might is not controlled by the 
popular masses it would kill freedom. The shah in Iran used the vast power of the Iranian 
Army to supress freedom in Iran and divide the sons of the Iranian Army to supress freedom 
in Iran and divide the sons of the Iranian people, who were calling for freedom and 
liberation from U.S. domination, In other words, the Iranian Army was used against the 
Iranian people. Why? Because during the shah!s era the army was not under the control 

of the Iranian people, It was controlled by the shah, the government, and fascist, anti- 
freedom and antipopular elements. Now, praise be to God, the Iranian Army is in the 

hands of the Iranian people. Therefore this army will neither be used for killing 
freedom in Iran nor for dividing the sons of the Iranian people. It will be used for 

the protection of the (?wealth) of the Iranian people and their freedom. This is why 

the enemies of the Iranian people want to strike at the freedom of Iran and the Iranian 


people.

The Libyan military force, at least at the Libyan level, is regarded as a formidable force. 
It is therefore required that the popular masses should be in control of this force. 

It is inevitable that we should speed up the establishment of the armed people so that 
tanks, artillery guns, armored vehicles and all military installations are run by the 
popular masses and so that the latter can use them in self-defense and in the defense of 
their gains. Otherwise, the equipment will be used against you one day. [sentence 
indistinct] [applause and cheers] The solution is to speed up the establishment of the 
armed people so that the people will become armed and the weapons will be in the hands of 
the people and not in the hands of any other (?group). This is in order to guarantee that 
neo one will use these weapons against the popular masses. Freedom is still at the mercy 
of the winds. Everything that is feared by the citizen should inevitably be in the hands 
of the citizens--the masses--so that there will be no more fear and so that freedom can be 
achieved. tIrrespective of the authority in power, so long as these weapons are not in your 
hands there is a possibility that they will be used against you. 


Starting next year, as of the scholastic year, we will begin militarization [tajyish], the 
militarization of the schools. Today (?we saw) the vanguard of the armed people. We 

saw students from secondary schools driving 2 missile-carrying vehicles. They are capable 
of using these. [word indistinct] the secondary school student was fired on by weapons such 
(?such as these) [words indistinct]. Other [word indistinct] countries of the world, 
students were killed by the bullets of the government. Today, this very day, here, the 
secondary school students who used to fear the weapons because they were used against them 
are being trained on these weapons and are driving the missileecarrying vehicles. They - 
are the ones who have the weapons in their hands. This is a serious change. [cheering] 


Every student, every father who has a student among his sons, and every citizen whose pvrother 
is a student, or one of his relatives is a student, should work to bring about the success 

of the militarization of the schools. This is in order to avoid the introduction of 

national service and compel this student to perform this service after graduation. If he 

is trained on these weapons there is no need for him to perform military service. We want 
the weapons to be in the hands of the people, so that we can defeat our enemy and so that 
these weapons are not used against us or against our freedom. 


The militarization of the towns should inevitably be speeded up. Every citizen, irrespective 
of his work, should inevitably belong to a garrison in his town. He should carry an 
identity card with his number and a weapon on which he should receive training continuously. 
He should continue with his training on this weapon as he continuously attends tohis 

other work.

The question concerns freedom. If you live in a rented house you are not free. It is 
inevitable that the house should belong to the person who is living init. Naturally, the 
person who owns more than one house has no right to do so because the second house should 
be owned by the other person who is living in it. The question here concerns the completion 
of freedom first and foremost. You should own the house you are living in, so that you 

and your family live in security. How can the people be free if they are living in rented 
houses? How can the people be free if there are some living in rented houses and some 
offer houses they own for rent? In other words, how can we have some people owning more 
than one house each and some people who have none and therefore have to rent? It is a 
question of freedom. The popular congresses issued Law No 4 supporting a slogan 

{from Al-Qadhdhafi's Green Book] [words indistinct]. Even the work carried out by the 
revolutionary committees to implement this slogan is a source of pride to all of us. 

The measures taken by the revolutionary committees and the other popular committees whose 
duty is to implement Law No 4 have been completed. But there remains one thing--one problem 
which we have not solved; this will be solved as of now. Revolutionary measures [words 
indistinct] the co xing years [words indistinct]. [applause] Any person living in a rented 
house will be regarded, as of now, as being the owner of that house. This house is his. 


As of today, any person living in a rented house will be deemed the owner of that house. 
He must (?register) the house in his own name in the real estate department register. He 
should register that this house, number so and so, on such and such street and this or 
that town or villse in which he resides, now belongs to him. [Words indistinct] if the

The general budget will pay compensation on behalf of the new owners by paying the price 
of the house. As of now, in order to put an end to the problem, any person living in a 
rented house will be deemed to be the ownercf that house in order to realize the slogan: 
the house belongs to the person who lives in it. [applause and cheers] 


The second point--we have said that we are concerned about your freedom, the freedom of 
everyone of you, the freedom of every individual and his family. If we have freedom, 
there is no need for revolution. Revolution is against subjugation, injustice, and 
oppression. Why did we stage the revolution? We did so in order to eliminate injustice 
and exploitation as well as class distinctions and class differences. There should be 

no person serving another. Every individual should serve the whole community. No person 
should serve another; no person should be served by others. 


For example, the question of employing servants, including employment of people to drive 
your car for you--this is a [word indistinct] subject. Vehicles used for transport and 
the carrying of goods--we still regard transport and the carrying of goods on vehicles as 
productive work. We still regard transport as productive work. 


For special considerations transport is productive work, but in actual fact it is not. 
Therefore, the vehicles used for public transport and the carrying of goods are not 

{words indistinct] like the privately owner car. The person who is currently driving 

a vehicle is regarded as being a productive worker, the same as the farmer and the worker.
