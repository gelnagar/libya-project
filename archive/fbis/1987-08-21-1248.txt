Large Numbers of Rebels Reportedly Surrender 
JN201127 Khartoum SUNA in Arabic 
1040 GMT 20 Aug 87 


[Text] Khartoum, 20 Aug (SUNA) — The newspaper Sawt 
al-Ummah, mouthpiece of the Al-Ummah Party, today said 
large numbers of rebels laid down their arms and surrendered to 
the Armed Forces after the latter had taken full control of the 
rebel areas and also as a result of a growing desire for peace, 
which prompted the government to allocate a special budget to 
meet the subsistence and settlement of the returning fighters. 


The paper also said reports from the eastern borders affirm that 
Karbino [name as received] the fourth-in- command in the 
Garang Movement, and William Noon [name as received] the 
third-in-command in the Movement, split from Garang after 
killing four Ethiopian intelligence officers and entering Sudanese 
territory. 


Al-Ummah said the increased Armed Forces’ efforts as well as 
the growing desire for peace by southerners who had joined the 
rebellion, are the major reason for the fragmentation of the rebel 
movement. 


National Unionist Party To Make SPLM Contacts 
JN201142 Khartoum SUNA in English 
1025 GMT 70 Aug 87 


[Text] Khartoum, Aug 20 (SUNA) — The National Unionist 
Party decided to make immediate contacts with the Sudanese 
People Liberation Movement “SPLM” of Col. John Garang, the 
daily Al-Sudani published here today quoted the party's 
Chairman ‘Ali Muhamud Hasanayn as saying. 


Hasanayn accused the government of leading the country to a 
deadlock saying that it speaks sometimes about the peaceful 
solution for the southern Sudan problem and sometimes about 
the military option for solution of the said question without really 
leaning to any one of the two options. 


Hasanayn further went on to say that the current coalition 
government is trying to solve the country's main problems by 
theory without any practical steps. 


Reports on Anya Nya II-SPLA Contacts ‘Unfounded’ 
JN201826 Khartoum SUNA in English 
1717 GMT 20 Aug 87 


[Text] Khartoum, Aug. 20, SUNA — Recent reports, mediation 
between the rebel movement of Sudan People’s Liberation Army 
(SPLA) and the pro-government forces of Anya Nya-Two have 
been described by the latter as “unfounded”. [sentence as 
received] 


Deputy commander of the Anya Nya-Two forces, better known 
as “The Friendly Forces”, Brigadier Fawlino Matyb Nial [name 
as received] has indicated that his movement has no contact or 
mediation with the SPLA.
