LIBYA

FOREIGN SECRETARY RECEIVES KENYAN, ZIMBABWE OFFICIALS 
LD1L62230¥ Tripoli Voice of the Arabic Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 26 Feb 78 LD 


[Text] At noon todey Foreign Secretary 'Ali at-Turayki received the Kenyan deputy 
foreign minister, who informed him of the Egyptian president's interference in domestic 
frican affairs. He also informed him that Kenya forced down an Egyptian plane carrying 
tons of arms and ammunition and that Egypt has detained two Kenyan civilian planes while 
on normal flights between Kenya and Europe. 


Dr at-Turayki also received the head of the Algerian delegation to the meetings of the 
frican liberation cocrdination committee. This afternnon Dr at-Turayki, foreign 
secretary and current chairman of the African liberation coordination committee, met with 
Robert Mugabi, the cochairman of the Patriotic Front for the Liberation of Zimbabwe and 
chief of Zimbabwe delegates to the committee meetings. 


A JANA correspondent has learned that discussion during the meeting covered the latest 
developments in Zimbabwe and the role of the African foreign ministers conference in 
bolstering the African struggle in this area against the racists.
