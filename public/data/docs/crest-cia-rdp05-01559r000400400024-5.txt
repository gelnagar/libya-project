Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a Tripoli domestic radio broadcast reports that Qadhafi and his delegation performed minor pilgrimage rites at Mecca and prayed inside the Kaaba, then led crowds in slogans against the United States, Jordan's King Husayn and Egypt's Husni Mubarak. It has no specific Tripoli locations.
• Broadcast by Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic, 1000 GMT, 19 May 1985
• Slogans included 'down with the United States, the enemy of Islam' and praise for the 1 September Revolution
• Source is an FBIS Nicosia message, 19 May 1985
• 1985-05-18: Qadhafi and accompanying delegation performed minor pilgrimage (umrah) rites and prayed inside the Holy Kaaba; crowds shouted anti-US, anti-Husayn and anti-Mubarak slogans

AL-QADHDHAFI SHOUTS ANTI-MUBARAK, HUSAYN SLOGANS IN MECCA 

NC 191026 

TRIPOLI DOMESTIC SERVICE IN ARABIC 1000 GMT 19 MAY 85 

((TEXT)) THE BROTHER LEADER OF THE REVOLUTION AND HIS 
ACCOMPANYING DELEGATION PERFORMED THE MINOR PILGRIMAGE RITES LAST 
NIGHT. THEY ALSO SAID A PRAYER INSIDE THE HOLY KAABA. AFTER 
FINISHING THE MINOR PILGRIMAGE RITES, THE BROTHER LEADER AND THE 
MASSES CROWDING THE PLACE SHOUTED: GOD IS GREAT; VICTORY FOR THE 
ARAB NATION; DOWN WITH THE UNITED STATES, THE ENEMY OF ISLAM; 
DOWN WITH HUSAYN, THE ENEMY OF GOD AND THE AGENT OF ZIONISM; 

AND DOWN WITH HUSNI MUBARAK, ENEMY OF GOD AND ZIONIST AGENT. 
CHEERS WERE ALSO RAISED STRESSING THE ROLE PLAYED BY THE 1 
SEPTEMBER REVOLUTION IN SPREADING ISLAM AND THE WORD OF GOD 
THROUGHOUT THE WORLD. 

(ENDALL) 191000 NG/GIMIGLIANO/HK 19/1031Z MAY
