# Research notes (working file; facts with sources, for area profiles and records)

## Tekbali camp (معسكر التكبالي), Salah al-Din, ~32.798,13.220
- LANA (Libyan state news agency), 1 Jul 2013: "Khalifa al-Takbali camp" (معسكر المرحوم خليفة التكبالي) in Tripoli handed by the Third Support Brigade (فرقة الإسناد الثالثة), which had guarded it since Tripoli's "liberation from Gaddafi's brigades", to the Libyan National Army; ceremony attended by GNC president Nouri Abusahmain and Interior Minister Mohamed al-Sheikh. https://lana.gov.ly/art.php?id=25973&lang=ar

## Military College / academy, Hadaba (الكلية العسكرية), ~32.839,13.2025
- Al Jazeera 5 Jan 2020: on Sat 4 Jan 2020 an air strike on a military academy/"military camp at Al-Hadhba", Tripoli, killed at least 30 cadets (mostly aged 18–22) gathering on the parade ground, 33 wounded; GNA blamed Haftar's LNA, LNA denied. https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2020/1/5/dozens-killed-in-attack-on-military-academy-in-libyas-tripoli

## Yarmouk camp (معسكر اليرموك), Hamza camp (معسكر حمزة), al-Sawarikh (missile) camp — south Tripoli
- Al Jazeera 23 May 2020: GNA said it retook Yarmouk, al-Sawarikh and Hamza camps from Haftar's LNA; Yarmouk had served as LNA central command for the southern Tripoli front; reporter said Wagner mercenaries were there. https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2020/5/23/libya-gna-retakes-military-camps-near-tripoli

## Naam camp, Tajoura
- Libya Security Monitor: 10 Jul 2022 Naam camp (Tajoura) passed from 92 Infantry Brigade (Haithem Tajouri) to 51 Infantry Brigade (Bashir Khalaf Allah). https://libyasecuritymonitor.com/bashaaghas-influence-in-tripoli-weakened-as-tajouri-loses-control-of-military-camp/

## Women's military academy ("Amazons"), between port and city centre
- Kuwait Times/AFP 20 Jun 2021: former academy of Gaddafi's female bodyguards, between the port and the city centre; most buildings demolished c.2017; opened 2021 as a public park; control tower at old gate remains. https://kuwaittimes.com/libyans-chill-at-ex-barracks-for-gaddafis-amazons/

## Libyan legal records (current, public)
- DCAF Libya security-sector legislation database (security-legislation.ly; © DCAF, link + summary only). Law No. 71 of 1972 "on criminalising partisanship": RCC law, signed by Gaddafi as PM; Gazette No. 29, 3 Jul 1972, pp. 1338–41; Arab Socialist Union the only permitted organisation; Art. 3 death penalty for founding/joining/funding any group opposing the revolution; Art. 4 ≥10 years for not reporting; Art. 7 special courts under RCC; repealed by Law No. 2 of 2012. https://security-legislation.ly/?p=32378
- Libya Analysis substack, 20 May 2026: 18 May 2026 Tripoli Court of Appeal acquitted Abdullah al-Senussi (and Mansour Dhao, Saif al-Islam) in the 2011 crackdown case No. 630; Abu Salim 1996 case still under judicial review, no final judgment; ICC: al-Senussi case inadmissible; ICC confirmation hearing 19–21 May 2026 for Khaled al-Hishri (Mitiga prison 2014–2020 crimes). https://libyaanalysis.substack.com/p/20-may-2026-tripoli-court-sentences
- ICJ, 27 May 2025: >50 unidentified bodies found in Abu Salim hospital morgue (reported 19 May 2025); General Authority for the Search and Identification of Missing Persons received information on mass graves at the Abu Salim zoo (15 May 2025); ICJ called for investigation under the Minnesota Protocol. https://www.icj.org/libya-investigate-unidentified-bodies-and-reported-mass-graves-in-abu-salim/
