LIBYA

GOVERNMENT SEEKS UAE IMPOUNDMENT OF GREEK FREIGHTER 
JN241021 Doha QNA in Arabic 0935 GMT 24 Sep 79 JN 


[Text] Abu Dhabi, 24 September--The Libyan Embassy has asked the responsible UAE 
authorities to impound the Greek freighter (Casto), The freighter, which docked at 
Sharjah port with a shipment of tea belonging to the Libyan Government, left India 
last March on its way to Benghazi, but changed its course and proceeded toward Gulf 
ports, This raised doubts in the Libyan Government, which informed port authorities 
in Arab Gulf and European countries of the incident, 


In its memorandum to the UAE Foreign Ministry, the Libyan Government asked that the UAE 
insure the arrival of the shipments --<comprising 577 tons of tea valued at 165,000 pounds 
sterling--in Benghazi, as it belongs to the Libyan Government, 


The Sharjah port authorities opened an investigation of the incident today, in light of 
documents submitted by the Libyan Embassy, These include the bill of lading, the certifi- 
cate of insurance and other documents that prove that all duties and charges were settled 
before the ship left India, 


A Libyan Embassy source stated that this incident is one of the acts of piracy Greek 
freighters have recently begun to practice against Arab countries, 


MALDIVES PRESIDENT ENDS VISIT, DEPARTS 22 SEP 
LD231108 Tripoli JANA in English 0950 GMT 23 Sep 79 LD 


[Text] Maldives, 23 Sep, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The president of Maldives, Dr Maumoon 
Abdul Gayoom, left the Jamahiriyah yesterday after having ended his three-week tour which 
included the Jamahiriyah, Kuwait, America and India, During his visit to the Jamahiriyah 
he attended celebrations marking the 10th anniversary of the First of September Revolution. 
He also attended the non-aligned summit held in Havana,
