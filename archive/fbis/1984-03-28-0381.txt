Romanian Vice President Manescu Visits Tripoli

Committee of the Central Committee of the Romanian Commu-

Socialist Republic of Romania, and the delegation accompany-

Egyptian ‘Fears’ over Situation in Sudan Portrayed 
LD241614 Tripoli JANA in English 1510 GMT 24 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada Thani 20, March 24, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The current situation in Sudan has had an 
effect on the Egyptian regime as the latter began adopting a 
series of military measures to protect what a high-ranking Egyp- 
tian official called the national security of Egypt. 


The head of the Egyptian regime himself did not conceal the 
nature of this effect on his regime and has began to fear the 
widespread of [as received] these repercussions on him, to a point 
that impelled him to call in America as U.S. AWACS and other 
support aircraft arrived from the U.S. to Egypt. The head of the 
Egyptian regime announced that the AWACS aircraft will con- 
tinue to use Egypt’s air space, in light of the development of the 
situation in Sudan. 


In light of the atmosphere of tension which the Egyptian regime 
is undergoing came [phrase as received] Thursday’s meeting 
between Husni Mubarak and Donald Rumsfeld, U.S. envoy to 
the region, a meeting which was attended on the Egyptian side 
by Kamal Hassan ‘Ali, the foreign minister, Usamah al-Baz, the 
first deputy at the Foreign Ministry, while on the American side 
it was attended by Ambassador Silverman, the U.S. foreign 
department (adviser) and by the U.S. ambassador to Cairo. 


The talks (? according) to information from the Egyptian capital, 
concentrated on the Egyptian regime’s fears in light of the 
current situation in Sudan as well as ways to facilitate a wider 
U.S.-Egyptian military intervention in Sudanese territory.
