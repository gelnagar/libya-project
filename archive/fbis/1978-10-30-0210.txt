LIBYA

FOREIGN SECRETARY LEAVES FOR BAGHDAD CONFERENCE 
LD291504Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 29 Oct 738 LD 
[text] Foreign secretary 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki left for Baghdad this morning heading 
a Libyan delegation to attend the Arab foreign ministers conference beginning tomorrow 
morning in the Iraqi capital in preparation for the Are’ Summit conference scheduled to 
convene on 2 November. The delegation includes the assis ant under secretary for political 
affairs and some directors of the Foreign Secretariat. 
MALTESE PRIME MINISTER MINTOFF ARRIVES 
LD291502Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 29 Oct 78 LD 
[Text] Maltese Prime Minister Dom Mintorf arrived at the Tripoli international airport 
this morning on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. He was welcomed at the airport by brother 
Mansur Badr, the maritime transport secretary, chief of protocol and head of the Arat 


desk at the Foreign Secretariat, and the Maltese charge d'affaires in the Jamahiriyah.
