LIBYA

JANA Says French Readying Military Move in Chad 
LD300203 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
0015 GMT 30 Jan 84 


[Excerpt] JANA has learned that France has prepared 67 mili- 
tary vehicles carrying French soldiers — which will be supported 
by helicopters — for a move to occupy the Fardjia zone in Chad. 
This French move reveals the hidden aims of the French Govern- 
ment in Chad, and its efforts to internationalize the struggle in 
the Chadian arena and strike at the people of Chad. 


Chad Asks Libyan Support 
LD291945 Tripoli JANA in English 1747 GMT 29 Jan 84 


[Text] Paris, Rabi Thani 24, January 29, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The legitimate, unified Chadian Govern- 
ment has threatened that it will take appropriate action against 
France because of the recent escalation of military activity by 
the French Government. 


The legitimate, unified Chadian Government’s spokesman in 
Paris, Mr Abdul Rahman Moussa, said France should expect 
appropriate reactions from Chadian citizens Struggling for their 
independence. Moussa said in his Statement in Paris that the 
legitimate unified government has appealed to Libya to stand 


beside the National Liberation Army in accordance with joint 
defence agreements signed by both sides. 


Moussa accused the French Government of pouring oil over a 
raging fire by bolstering its armed forces and overstepping the 
red line which it had earlier drawn for itself. 


Al-Qadhdhafi, Saudi Envoy Discuss Egypt, ICO 
LD300145 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
0041 GMT 30 Jan 84 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution yesterday received 
Shaykh Ali Musallam, special adviser to King Fahd ibn ‘Abd 
al-‘Aziz, the Saudi monarch. 


During this meeting, Shaykh Ali Musallam conveyed to the 
brother leader of the revolution a verbal message from King Fahd 
ibn ‘Abd al-‘Aziz, which included the king’s assurance that the 
return of Egypt to the ICO will never happen unless it agrees to 
remove the reasons which caused the Suspension of its ICO 
membership in 1979. 


Army’s Jallud, Jabir Receive Fata Dissidents 
LD271542 Tripoli JANA in English 1412 GMT 27 Jan 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Thani 22, Jan 27, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and 
Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the 
Libyan Armed Forces, yesterday received Abu Salih [Nimr 
Salih}, Abu Musa [Sa‘id Musa Muragha], Qadri, Abu Khalid 
al-‘Amlah and Abu Mahdi, members of the provisional com- 
mand of the new revolutionary movement within Fatah, in 
addition to Talal Najji, assistant secretary of the PFLP-GC, all 
arrived in Tripoli yesterday. [sentence as received] 


Petroleum Secretary Confers With Soviet Official 
LD282128 Tripoli JANA in English 1850 GMT 28 Jan 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Thani 23, January 28, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The petroleum secretary, Mr ‘Abd al- 
Majid al-Qa‘ud, this afternoon conferred with the deputy 
chairman of the Soviet State Committee for Foreign Economic 
Relations and chairman of the Soviet side of the joint Libyan 
Arab-Russian Committee. 


Discussions during the meeting centered on methods of bolster- 
ing bilateral cooperation.
