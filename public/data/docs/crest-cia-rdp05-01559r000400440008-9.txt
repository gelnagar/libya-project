• Broadcast by Jamahiriyah News Agency (JANA) in English, 1800 GMT 11 April 1990, distributed by FBIS London.
• Qadhdhafi argues party politics, vanguard parties and religious groups cannot rule; only People's Congresses and People's Committees are valid.
• He cited Hungary's 1990 election, where the top party won about a quarter of the vote, as proof no majority existed.

((TEXT)) TRIPOLI, AL-TAYR ((APRIL)) 11, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY 
-- COL. MU'AMMAR AL-QADHDHAFI THE LEADER OF THE GREAT AL-FATAH 
REVOLUTION ASSERTED THE ERA OF THE MASSES IS KNOCKING ON ALL DOORS 
AND NO ONE CAN STAND IN THE FACE OF THE MASSES, WHICH WANTS TO TAKE

((SENTENCE AS RECEIVED)) RELIGIOUS PARTIES AND THE TYRANTS HAD ALL

NOW IN FRONT OF THE MASSES' BLOWS AND ANGER. 
DURING HIS MEETING WITH THE STUDENTS OF THE SPORTS EDUCATION 
DEPARTMENT AT AL-FATAH UNIVERSITY COMPLEX, HE SAID "THE MASSES ARE

EVERY STATE AS A STEP ON THE ROAD TO THE BASIC PEOPLE'S CONGRESSES 
DESPITE THEM KNOWING THAT. HE POINTED OUT THAT THE GREEN BOOK HAS 
NOT ARRIVED TO THEM YET AND THEY WERE STILL CONSIDERING THE JAMAHIRI 
THEORY AS A NEW THEORY TO THEM AND IF THEY KNEW THAT THERE IS A NEW 
FORMULA BY THE NAME OF PEOPLE'S CONGRESSES AND PEOPLE'S COMMITTEES 
THEY WOULD HAVE LEFT THE PARTIES AND CHANGED INTO THE JAMAHIRI 
REGIME.
