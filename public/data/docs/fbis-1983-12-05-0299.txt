LIBYA

Military Official Ends Visit to Hungary, Poland 
LDO21043 Tripoli JANA in English 0915 GMT 2 Dec 83 


{Text} Tripoli, Safar 27, Dec 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — Brigadier Mustafa al-Kharrubi chief-of-staff of 
the Libyan Arab Armed Forces ended his visit to the People’s 
Republic of Hungary and the Socialist Republic of Poland and 
returned to Jamahiriyah last night with his accompanying del- 
egation. 


During his visit Brigadier Mustafa al-Kharrubi held a series of 
talks with a number of officials in both friendly countries related 
to the development of cooperation and friendship ties between 
Jamahiriyah and both Hungary and Poland. 


In the Hungarian capital, Budapest, Brigadier al-Kharrubi, 
chief-of-staff of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces met with the 
Hungarian president of the Presidential Council and member of 
the Political Bureau, Pal Losonczi and with Ferenc Havasi, 
member of Political Bureau and the Political Bureau of the 
Hungarian Socialist Workers Party. He also held meetings with 
the Hungarian minister of defence, his deputy and the chief-of- 
staff of the Hungarian Armed Forces during which discussions 
were held about bolstering cooperation in all fields between 
Jamahiriyah and Hungary. During his visit to Hungary 
Brigadier al-Kharrubi visited a number of Hungarian technical 
establishments and factories where he familiarized himself with 
the methods used in these establishments. 


Later in Warsaw, Brigadier al-Kharrubi met with General 
Jaruzelski, chairman of the Council of Ministers, and delivered 
a verbal message to him from the leader of the revolution, in 
relation to the cooperation between the two friendly countries 
Al-Kharrubi, also met with General Florian Siwicki, the minister 
of national defence, the deputy minister of defence and the chief 
of staff of Armed Forces and discussed with them the relations 
between the two countries and the means of bolstering them 
Brigadier al-Kharrubi during his visit to the Polish Socialist 
Republic visited a number of arms production factories, and 
Polish establishments specialised in producing and developing 
military and technical equipment. 


Economic Agreements Signed With Tunisia 
LDO022143 Tripoli JANA in English 1426 GMT 2 Dec 83 


[Text] Tunis, Safar 27, Dec 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The minutes of the meeting of the joint Libyan 
Arab-Tunisian economic affairs committee were signed yester- 
day evening in the framework of achieving economic and social 
unity between the two countries 


The minutes of the agreement included the following: 


In the field of planning — coordination in planning programmes 
and exchange of experiments and experiences in the field of 
planning and preparation of annual budgets in industry. 


The two sides showed readiness to establish joint industrial 
information as well as the development and exchange of raw 
material used in industry in both countries. The two sides also 
agreed to study industrial projects in shoe making, ready made 
clothes, weaving and some parts for refrigerators. They also 
agreed to study the possibilities of integration in the fields of 
heavy industries, energy and investments. 


Both parties agreed to establish a joint Arab Tunisian-Libyan 
bank for development and external trade. It was also agreed to 
establish a bank to deal with non-residents as a subsidiary of joint 
Arab Tunisian-Libyan bank for development and external trade 
to be named north African international bank for agriculture. 


The two sides also discussed future cooperation between their 
countries in the fields of water, land weathering and deser- 
tification. They also studied the establishment of joint schemes 
in the sphere of agriculture and trade. 


The two sides reached a new trade and customs agreement, and 
in this aspect a joint technical committee was formed to discuss 
the unification of customs checkpoints. 


Fawzi al-Shakshuki, the secretary of planning; and Ismail Khalil, 
the Tunisian minister of planning, signed the context of the 
agreement. Salih Mbarka, the Tunisian minister of finance, and 
the Libyan and the Tunisian general commissioners attended the 
signing ceremony
