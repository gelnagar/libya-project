LIBYA

Charge Refutes Jordanian Allegations on Embassy Attack 
LD282347 Tripoli JANA in English 1412 GMT 28 Feb 84 


[Text] Damascus, Jumada al Ulla 24, Feb 28, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY The Jamahiriyah’s charge d'affaires in 
Jordan held a press conference here yesterday in which he talked 
about the Jamahiriyah’s stand toward the Jordanian decision to 
sever unilaterally diplomatic and political relations with Libya. 
He refuted Jordan’s allegations saying that what happened was 
merely an angry reaction by Palestinian and Arab masses on the 
Great al Fateh soil who demonstrated outside the Jordanian 
Embassy in Tripoli. Answering reporters, he said the ties between 
the Libyan and Jordanian people still existed and this issue will 
be referred to the masses of the basic people’s congresses to take 
their decision, being the decisionmaking machine in the Jama- 
hiriyah’s mission in Amman have left Jordan at the instructions 
of the Jordan regime’s authorities who alone are responsible for 
this conduct. A statement was distributed by the People’s Com- 
mittee for Foreign Liaison Bureau on the Jordanian decision to 
unilaterally sever ties with the Jamahiriyah.
