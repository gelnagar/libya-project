LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Returns to Tripoli From Arab Tour 
LD261748 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
1715 GMT 26 Jul 83 


{Text] The brother leader of the revolution arrived at Tripoli 
International Airport this afternoon from Algeria at the end of 
his working visit to Mauritania and Algeria. This visit came 
within the pan-Arab move of the Great 1 September Revolution 
to amass and unify the energies of the Arab nation in order to 
confront the Zionist death train driven by American energy. A 
sendoff ceremony was held for the brother leader at Houarie 
Boumedienne Airport, which was led by Algerian President 
Chadli Bendjedid. The speaker of the Algerian People’s Assem- 
bly, members of the FLN [National Liberation Front] Political 
Bureau, party and government officials and members and the 
secretary of the Libyan Fraternity Bureau in Algiers were also 
present. 


Egyptian Revolution Anniversary Marked 
LD240239 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2200 GMT 23 Jul 83 


{Text] Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud yesterday 
evening attended the great mass rally held by the revolutionary 
force in commemoration of the 31st anniversary of the immortal 
23 July Revolution which was set off by the free officers led by 
the immortal Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir. The rally was also attended 
by a member of the pan-Arab leadership of the Syrian Arab 
Socialist Bath Party, Tawfiq Salhah, and a member of the 
Political Bureau of the Popular Front for the Liberation of 
Palestine [as heard], Taysir Qubba’h, who are visiting the Jama- 
hiriyah. The rally was also attended by members of the Perma- 
nent Secretariat of the Arab People’s Congress and huge masses 
of citizens and Arab brothers in the Jamahiriyah. 


V. 27 Jul 83 


Brother Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud gave a speech at the 
rally, at the beginning of which he saluted the 23 July Revolution, 
which set off revolutionary awareness in the Arab nation. He 
said: Your celebration of the 23 July Revolution at this particular 
time emphasizes the height of loyalty of this nation on the one 
hand, and on the other hand it emphasizes the continuation of 
the will to defy embodied in Jamal ‘Abd an- Nasir and the 23 July 
Revolution.
