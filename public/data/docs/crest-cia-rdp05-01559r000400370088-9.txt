Summary (AI): An FBIS wire relays a 24 July 1976 Tripoli radio broadcast in which Qadhdhafi attacks Egyptian President Sadat and Sudanese President Numayri and comments on Syria in Lebanon. It concerns Libya's foreign policy and does not describe any place in Tripoli.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya would ask Saudi Arabia whether it had been involved in a military pact against Libya.
• Qadhdhafi said Numayri had recalled 8,000 Sudanese workers from Libya and was replacing them with Egyptian workers.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya was ready to reopen its embassy in Sudan, which had been closed at Sudan's request.
• 1976-07-23: Qadhdhafi addressed the final session of the Arab Nasserist Unionist Student League conference and said Sadat's stance would be referred to the People's Basic Congresses next month.
