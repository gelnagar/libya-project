JANA REPORTS ON 22 JAN OIL CONFERENCE IN TRIPOLI 


LD231852Y¥ Tripoli JANA in English 1507 GMT 23 Jan 78 LD 


fText] Tripoli, January 23, JANA--The conference of Arab oil exporting countries 
yesterday recommended an increase in the price of oil to a limit which makes exploitation 
of alternative energy resources economical, to put an end to the extravagance in 
consumption of oil and gas, subsequentiy to benefit from processing them, 


The conference, the tenth of its kind, and attended by all the Arab oil exporting 
countries and representatives of OPEC and OAPEC, also recommended that the price of gas 
should be linked to the price of crude oil on the basis of calorie contents and other 
specifications, 


It said the Arab countries should coordinate their plans when conducting a dialogue 
with the industrialised countries to strengthen their position in negotiations to 
enable them to acquire oil technology on reasonable terms in the shortest possible time, 


The conference also recommended the necessity of reviewing the present averages of oil 
production in the light of circumstances related to the world market, in order to 
realise a revenue in line with the development requirements and preservation of oil 
wealth, 


The conference asserted the importance of linking oil prices with the prices of imports 
from oil consuming countries and with the conditions for the transfer of technolgoy, as 
it is apparent that Arab petroleum exporting countries, after the elapse of a period 
from the rectification of prices have not realised substantial benefits because of the 
lack of such linkage. 


Whatever the technical regulations proposed for this linkage or means of payment, the 
conference recommended the establishment of the mechanical system safeguarding 
continuous compensation to Arab oil producing countrics for matters affecting their 
revenues from realising the genuine and convenient prices ensuring the realisation of 
economic development of Arab countries. 


The conference stressed the necessity to produce as much oil as possible to raise the 
standard of the Arab citizen, in all fields especially technology.

The conference observed that the determination of an alternative for oil as an 
energy source should not be confined to the consuming industrialized countries. Arab 
scientists must accordingly be encouraged to do researches on other energy resources 
such as solar energy. 


The conference has reviewed the latest economical development since the niuth Arab oil 
conference, in connection with promoting the relations between oil exporting and 
consuming countries. 


The conference stressed the necessity of strengthening the relations between developing 
countries and the work for a new world economic order. 


The conference expressed its satisfaction about the aids offered by the oil exporting 
countries to the developing countries to promote solidarity between the two sides, It 
called on industrialized countries to moderate their intransigent stand toward the just 
demands of the developing countries and to offer more aid to them especially in the field 
of technology. 


It deplored the methods used by the industrialized countries in confronting the oil 
exporting countries so as to (?weaken) them, 


The conference reviewed the ceaseless efforts in the field of cooperation between the 
Arab oil producing countries, and observed that the Arab countries should work as an 
integral unit in the field of training research and scientific and applied research for 
oil industries. 


The Arab countries should also promote the Arab technical capabilities in the field of 
construction, engineering, planning and designs for the oil projects. These countries 
should also start manufacturing oil equipment, 


The conference called for the formationofa permanent technical committee of experts in 
the Arab countries to compile an Arabic oil technical encyclopedia. 


The conference observed that something should be done to stop the immigration of Arab 
minds and encourage them to return home, 


It called for increase in information and studies exchanges and holding more scientific 
seminars and urged Arab oil companies to exchange and second more cadres. 


The conference stressed the necessity of the continuation of supporting the oil 
specialized departments whether in the Arab League or in the Organisation of Arab Oil 
Exporting Countries and the continuation of financing joint Arab projects. 


It stressed the necessity of devoting the Arab oil for the service of Arab issues, topped 
by the liberation of occupied territories and the restoration of the rights of the 
Palestinian peoples,

SOVIET DEPUTY AGRICULTURAL MINISTER ENDS VISIT 
LD221756Y Tripoli JANA in English 0946 GMT 22 Jan 78 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, January 22, JANA--The deputy agriculture minister of the Soviet Union, 
Boris Runov, left here yesterday after a several day visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


During his stay he held talks with Jamahiriyah officials on strengthening cooperation 
between the two countries in the agricultural field, specially technical cooperation, 


He was seen off by officials of the agriculture secretariat and members of the Soviet 
Embassy in Tripoli.

TRIPOLI-CUBAN AGREEMENT--Havana, January 5, JANA--Several agreements, covering trade, 
economic and cultural relations between the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 

and Cuba were signed at the Foreign Ministry here yesterday. The agreements were drawn 

up in March last year during the visit of Quban President Fidel Castro to the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. Ina joint statement the Cuban foreign minister and 

the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah ambassador to Cuba praised the bilateral 
relations between the two countries. [Text] [Tripoli JANA in English 0909 GMT 5 Jan 78 LD]
