Summary (AI): A badly OCR-scanned 1975 CIA-reading-room communique on Soviet Premier Aleksey Kosygin's visit to Libya, where he met Col. Muammar Qadhafi and Abd al-Salam Jallud. It covers bilateral cooperation, anti-imperialist and Palestinian positions, and disarmament, and mentions the removal of imperialist military bases from Libya. It names no Tripoli sites, prisons, or repression events.
• The communique says the two sides discussed bilateral relations and cooperation, and reaffirmed determination to expand it.
• It states that the removal of imperialist military bases in Libya was an important victory for the anti-imperialist struggle.
• It mentions an invitation for Qadhafi to pay an official visit to the USSR, and for Soviet leaders to visit Libya.
• The document is heavily garbled by OCR; many passages are illegible and no reliable dates of events in Tripoli are given.
