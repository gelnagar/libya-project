LIBYA

LEBANESE NATIONALISTS, SOCIALISTS VISIT TRIPOLI 


Arrival Report 
LD152348 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 15 Jul 81 


[Text] Walid Junblatt, chairman of the Political Bureau of the Lebanese National Movement 
and leader of the Lebanese Progressive Socialist Party, Muhsin Ibrahim, executive secretary 
of the Lebanese National Movement, and Muhsin Naloub, deputy leader of the Lebanese 
Progressive Socialist Party, arrived in Tripoli this evening for a visit to the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


In a statement to JANA, Mr Walid Junblatt said that the visit is within the framework 
of discussing the latest developments in the Lebanese and Arab arena, so as to promote 
the move to confront the colonialist-Zionist challenges in the Arab region. 


Jabir Reception 
LD160846 Tripoli JANA in English 0823 GMT 16 Jul 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 Jul (JANA)--Brigadier Abu Bakir Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of 
the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, last night received Mr. Walid Junblatt, president of the 
Political Council of the Lebanese National Movement, L.N.M., and leader of the Socialist 
Progressive Party, S.P.P., in Lebanon; Mr. Muhsin Ibrahim, L.N.M. executive secretary; 
and Mr. Muhsin Daloub, deputy leader of the S.P.P.
