LIBYA

AI,-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES SAUDI EDUCATION MINISTER 


LD190048Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 18 Jan 78 LD 


[Text] Brother Col Mu'tammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 1 September Revolution, 
this afternoon received Dr 'Abd al-'Aziz al-Khuwaytir, Saudi minister of education and 
personal envoy of King Khalid. The Saudi envoy handed brother Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi a 
private message from King Khalid. The meeting was atitended by the liaison secretary, 
the education secretary, and the Saudi ambassador in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah., The Saudi minister of education and personal envoy of King Khalid left 
Tripoli International Airport this afternoon. 


AT-TURAYKI RETURNS TO TRIPOLI AFTER AFRICAN TOUR 
LD182312Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 18 Jan 78 LD 


[Summary] The foreign secretary, Dr 'Ali !Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, returned to Tripoli 
this afternoon after visits to Benin, Togo and Niger, where he had talks with officials 
on the consolidation of relations between the two countries.
