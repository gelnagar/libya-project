LIBYA

JANA: 12 Sudanese Government Troops Killed 
LD231109 Tripoli JANA in English 0901 GMT 23 Feb 84 


[Text] Khartoum, Jamada al Ulla 19, Feb 23, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The revolution fighters in the south of 
Sudan killed 12 soldiers of the government forces in the Upper 
Nile region. 


Major Muhammad Mustafa, military commander of the Upper 
Nile told local papers that the 12 people were killed when a land 
mine went off under their [words indistinct]. According to the 
official version, the operation was carried out on 6 February. 
Major Mustafa added that this operation was carried out by 
fighters belonging to the Sudanese People’s Liberation Army. 


JANA: Secret U.S.-Egypt-Sudan Meetings Held 
LD221702 Tripoli JANA in English 1426 GMT 22 Feb 84 


[Text] Khartoum, Jumada al Ulla 18, Feb 22, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Recently, a secret meeting took place in 
Khartoum between Field Marshal ‘Abd al-Halim Abu Ghaza- 
lah, Egypt’s defence minister; the U.S. Air Force commander; 
and the deputy director of the U.S. Central Intelligence Agency 
for African affairs. This meeting was preceded by many others 
at various stages. 


Hireling Numayri and ‘Abd al-Halim Abu Ghazalah met and 
(?discussed) the situation on the Sudanese-Ethiopian border and 
Ethiopia’s alleged role in supporting the Sudanese opposition in 
the south. They also discussed the siege by the Sudanese opposi- 
tion forces of 600 Sudanese soldiers in the eastern region of 
Sudan. 


During the meeting, hireling Numayri asked for Egyptian mili- 
tary support in the form of Egyptian forces to be sent to the 
southern region. Abu Ghazalah has agreed on the intervention 
of Egyptian forces for the defence of the main cities in Sudan, 
and to move south to face the opposition forces. It was also agreed 
between both sides that the Egyptian airforce would intervene 
and be based at Wadi Sidna base, north of Khartoum, to take 
part in any possible operation. 


This meeting was followed by another between General Umar 
Muhammad at-Tayyib, first deputy to hireling Numayri, and 
head of the High Committee of National Security and the deputy 
director of U.S. Central Intelligence Agency for East African 
affairs. During this meeting, discussions centered on the opera- 
tion to stir up confrontation with Ethiopia. The implementation 
of this operation was worked out during the meeting. 


Meanwhile, another meeting took place between hireling 
Numayri and General at-Tayyib, Marshal Abu Ghazalah and 
the commander of the U.S. Air Force, and the deputy director of 
the U.S. Central Intelligence Agency for East African affairs. 
During this meeting, it was decided that the U.S. will send some 
fighter planes fully equipped until the military base in east Sudan 
is ready. This base is to be delivered during this month and will 
be operational next April. It was also decided that surface to 
surface and surface to air missile launchers will be set up in the 
Sannar region east of Sudan. It was also decided during the 
meeting to form a coordinating military committee including 
members from Sudan, Egypt and the U.S. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Speech Scores U.S. ‘Intimidation’ 
LD221313 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 22 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli 21 Feb (JANA) — The brother leader of the 
revolution this morning met the participants in the first rev- 
olutionary meeting of Al-Fatih arms [sawa‘id] being held at the 
Ideological Training Center in Tripoli under the slogan of Al- 
Fatih arms: “New Blood for the Revolutionary Committees 
Movement.” 


The brother leader spoke during this meeting. He said: This 
rising new generation must actually be a generation of anger 
from the start, and must shoulder its responsibility, create for 
itself a new creature and sever its link with the current generation 
of submission, the generation of exploitation and the generation 
of tyranny which exploits others, is pleased by exploiting others 
and accepts tyranny from others. 


The leader explained that this generation and those that 
preceeded it are prepared to accept the exploitation of others and 
the tyranny of others. Severing the link with the generation of 
exploitation and tyranny does not mean breaking off the link with 
the glorious past, and the immortal heritage and supreme ideals. 
These we must inherit from them and must not waste. This also 
means that we must not belittle the past generation and all these 
values, but we must get rid of all the existing superstitions and 
fairy tales left to this generation by the Ottomans, Italian colo- 
nialism and the defunct monarchy, in order to submit to authority 
and colonialism. 


The brother leader, addressing the participants at the meeting, 
said: If you area generation of anger against all that is happen- 
ing now, you must get free, break away and get rid of all these 
superstitions, and break all these shackles and free yourselves in 
order to be able to free your nation and land. 


The leader of the revolution affirmed the determination to build 
a new revolutionary movement which will be historically free, 
and will decide the battle on the international level, not only on

the Libyan or Arab level. He explained that the building process 
of this movement must start from the elementary and secondary 
stages where the vestiges of the past — selfishness and egotism 
— have to be eliminated. 


The brother leader of the revolution, addressing the participants 
at this meeting, went on to add: You are the new generation 
which must not fear America, or Israel, or the atomic bomb. If 
this angry generation shows that it is not afraid of these things 
for any reason then it will inflict defeat on the enemy and on 
America. 


He stressed that the liberation of man from within constitutes a 
mighty power that does not know fear. In this respect, he specifi- 
cally pointed out that when people who did not know fear 
appeared in Lebanon, the forces of NATO were forced to flee, 
forces for whom the New Jersey and the thousands of soldiers 
were of no use. This happened previously in Vietnam when the 
Vietnamese people forged ahead and scored a victory against the 
American forces. 


The brother leader of the revolution also stressed that this 
generation must get rid of the complex of fearing America 
because they have a right on Earth (?without which) there is only 
death, and they must not worry about what America does and 
about its fleets. We are people with a right. America comes to 
our homes and lands, spoiling our living for us and wanting us to 
live every day under the intimidation of the American fleet and 
American aircraft, while the Israelis are expanding and 
destroying our homes and land daily. 


He stressed that all this must arouse anger within us and lead us 
to destroy the ships of the American fleet by any means. He 
explained that no one could achieve this other than a fearless 
angry generation.
