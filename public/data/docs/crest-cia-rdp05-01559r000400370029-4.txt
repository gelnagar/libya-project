Summary (AI): An FBIS London wire relays a Tripoli Arab Revolution radio report (17 January 1976) that Qadhdhafi told the General Popular Congress the US and Zionists accuse Libya of financing terrorism. He defended support for "liberation movements" and dismissed claims that Libyan funds went to Africa.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya wanted to reveal the backing it extends to liberation movements, to see whether they are terrorist or peoples' movements demanding freedom.
• He said claims that Libyan funds went to Africa were tendentious and that people visit Libya only to take Libyan money.
• 1976-01-17: Qadhdhafi speech to the General Popular Congress denying that Libya finances terrorism and defending support for liberation movements
