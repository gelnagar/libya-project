LIBYA

CHAD SECRETARY GENERAL ARRIVES TO EXPLAIN SITUATION 
LD100845 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 10 Apr 80 LD 
[Text] Tripoli--Secretary general of the provisional government of Chad Mahamat Nour 
Adam arrived in Tripoli this morning on a several-day visit to the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah. Ina statement to a JANA correspondent on arrival at Tripoli airport 
he explained that he has been entrusted by the provisional government of Chad with the task 
of explaining to the Libyan Arab pecples the latest developments of the conditions in which 
the people of Chad are living. 


LIBYAN, AFGHAN, ALGERIAN FOREIGN MINISTERS HOLD MEETING 


LD100044 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 9 Apr 80 LD 


[Text] A tripartite meeting was held today in Tripoli in which the foreign secretary 

and the foreign ministers of Algeria and Afghanistan took part. They discussed the situa- 
tion in the Arab and the Islamic regions. They also agreed to coordinate the attitudes 

of the progressive Islamic forces against colonialism. 


AFGHAN'S DOST COMMENTS ON REACTION TO REVOLUTION 
LDO91400 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 9 Apr 80 LD 


[Text] Shah Mohammad Dost, Afghan foreign minister, has stressed that the colonial and 
reactionary states are exploiting the situation in Afghanistan in order to achieve their 
aims and designs in the region. He pointed out that these states made no moves toward 

the Afghan people when they were suffering tribulations and tragedies before the revolution 
of last December. At a press conference held in Tripoli today, Shah Mohammad Dost said 
that the revolution in Afghanistan is a progressive, Islamic revolution applying genuine 
Islam and not the Islam advocated and called for by colonialism and reaction.

The Afghan foreign minister explained that the colonial and reactionary forces were 
exploiting the question of Islam in Afghanistan as an excuse for intervention in the 
region, to divert the attention of Muslims from their cause of destiny in occupied 
Palestine and in other regions where the Muslims are suffering repression. He said that 
these forces, under the leadership of American colonialism, were trying to distort the 
Afghan revolution by injections of venom and false fabrications. 


The minister stressed Afghanistan's stand against the colonial and reactionary forces 

and its support for the causes of freedom and liberation in the world. At the end of 

his statement Shah Mohammad Dost praised bilateral relations between the Libyan and 
Afghan peoples. He underlined that this visit will contribute to strengthening and 
developing these relations. He also praised the Islamic revolution in Iran and described 
it as a progressive Islamic revolution.
