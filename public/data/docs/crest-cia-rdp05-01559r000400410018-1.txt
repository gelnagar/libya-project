Al-Qadhdhafi Threatens Installation of USSR Missiles 
AUI11531 Rome ANSA in English 1518 GMT 11 Feb 86 

[Text](ANSA) Rome, February 11 — Libyan leader Mu‘ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi today urged the Italians to “get it into the 
Americans’ heads” that, as long as “justice is not given to the 
Palestinian people, not only will there never be. peace in the 
Middle East, but world peace itself will always be in danger”. 

In an interview published by the Turin daily, LA STAMPA, Al- 
Qadhdhafi said that, if the situation with the United States 
should “precipitate”, “we would have what one might call a state 
of necessity” in which he would be forced to make a formal 
military alliance with the Soviet Union. 

“The result would be the installation in Libya of very modern 
long-range nuclear missiles”, the Libyan leader said. “If these 
lethal weapons were installed, :all of Western Europe’s. missile 
arsenal would be automatically placed out of play”. 

Al-Qadhdhafi also called once more for a summit between 
himself and Italy’s prime minister, Bettino Craxi, a Socialist 
Party member. “You tell me that Craxi, a Socialist, might have 
difficulty in meeting me”, he said. “But I think that, in order to 
make history, you sorietimes have to have the courage not to pay 
too much attention to the day-to-day news” 

The interviewer reminded the Libyan leader that, after the 
Palestinian attacks which left 20 dead at Vienna and Rome’s 
Fiumicino Airport on December 27, Craxi could hardly meet him 
unless Al-Qadhdhafi himself made some concrete gesture to pave 
the way, for example by urging the Palestinians to desist from 
actions of this kind against friendly countries. 

“I am against actions like Fiumicino and Vienna” he replied. 
“Among other things, they are meaningless. But I must remind 
that who are justly indignant (over the attacks) that... it is truly 
difficult to put oneself in the shoes of people chased from their 
own land, of young people who have seen their parents and little 
brothers murdered, guilty only of being Palestinians”. 

“Those young people cannot make certain distinctions as the rest 
of us do. Who can proble their infinite desperation? However that 
may be, | will try to convince them to fight only in Palestine. But 
you Italians, you Europeans, should give me a hand...”.
