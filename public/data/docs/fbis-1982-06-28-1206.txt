LIBYA

TRIPOLI ON HAIG RESIGNATION, SHULTZ APPOINTMENT 


LD261540 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 26 Jun 82 


[Text] U.S. President Ronald Reagan yesterday accepted the resignation of his secretary of 
state, Alexander Haig. Former Treasury Secretary George Shultz has been appointed as his 
successor. Regarding this news, Al-Jamahiriyah radio's political editor has written the 
following analysis: 


The resignation of U.S. Secretary of State Alexander Haig yesterday evening removes the 
mask from the blundering and haphazardness of Reagan's administraion and exposes the extent 
of the effect of personal conflicts and private interests on political decisionmaking 

in the United States.
