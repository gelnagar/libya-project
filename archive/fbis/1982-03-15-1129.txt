LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RETURNS AFTER MALTA STOPOVER 


LD132014 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1950 GMT 13 Mar 82 


[Excerpt] Tripoli, 12 Mar (JANA) -- The brother commander of the revolution concluded his 
4-day official and cordial visit to Austria at the invitation of Austrian Chancellor Dr 
Bruno Kreisky. The brother commander returned home at 1900 today. 


The brother commander was welcomed at Tripoli International Airport by Commander in Chief 
of the Armed Forces Brig ‘Abu Yunis Jabir, Chief of Staff of the Libyan Armed Forces Brig 
Mustafa al-Kharrubi, and Commander of Urban Militarization Maj al-Khuwayldi al-Humaydi. 


Statement on Return 
LD140810 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 14 Mar 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 14 Mar (JANA) -- The brother leader of the revolution on his arrival 
last night in Tripoli at the conclusion of his 4-day visit to Austria made a statement 
to JANA. He said: 


My visit to Austria was extremely positive. It made it possible to explain viewpoints on 
various international issues which used to be distorted by the Zionist, imperialist and 
reactionary media. During this visit I was able to convince the Austrian people and 
officials of the correctness of our position and the justice of our cause. 


The leader said that the comprehension, vitality and courage of the Austrian chancellor 
effectively influenced the success of this visit and also helped the majority of the 
official sectors of Western public opinion appreciate that our posture and policy are 
greatly misunderstood. 


He added that this visit confirmed that dialogue and direct meetings are the best means to 
settle international issues and to establish world understanding which strengthens world 
peace. 


The leader of the revolution, in his statement to JANA, stressed that we are hopeful direct 
meetings like the one which took place in Austria will take place with all the Western 
countries. He explained that this is the way to understand each other and to avoid much 
misunderstanding and counter propaganda. 


The leader explained that he did not discount making other successful visits to Western 
Europe to serve the cause of peace in Europe, the Mediterranean Sea and Africa and the 
development of economic and political cooperation among these countries. 


He said: I do not discount that this will take place even with America. 


About his stop in Malta this evening [13 March] the brother leader explained that his stop 
was the result of the intervention of Chancellor Kreisky who had an important role in 
bringing about an understanding with Malta. 


He said the stop in Valetta gave him the chance to meet with Prime Minister Dom Mintoff, 
members of his government and the president of the republic. The talks he held restored 
matters to their normal course and it was agreed to submit the question of the continental 
shelf to the International Court of Justice, as was done between Libya and Tunisia. 


He added that his stop in Valetta led to a complete understanding with Dom Mintoff and his 
government and was received with a warm fraternal spirit. He stressed that this meeting 
opened the door for further cordial understandings to settle many of the problems in the 
region and in the world.

U.S. REACTION TO TRIP ‘CRUDE INTERFERENCE’ 


LD131744 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 13 Mar 82 


[Excerpts] The U.S. administration has expressed great concern over the visit to 
Austria by the leader of the revolution and its positive results and their effect on 
U.S. policy toward the European countries which rejected subservience to the United 
States and adopted independent stands. In a press statement issued yesterday in 
Washington, a U.S. State Department spokesman said the department has informed Austria 
of its concern and reservations regarding the visit and its possible consequences. 


Following the U.S. State Department spokesman's remarks, the JANA political analyst 
said the U.S. administration's attempt to notify the Austrian Government of the concern 
preoccupying that administration because of the leader's visit to Austria is seen as 

2 blatant and crude interference in the affairs of an independent country which does 
not need anyone to tell it what it should do. The interference proved a devastating 
failure in its attempt to pressure the European countries into making them follow its 
aggressive campaign against Libya purely for the sake of U.S. interests.

For ‘Revenge Measures’ 
LD122322 Tripoli JANA in English 1918 GMT 12 Mar 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 12 Mar (JANA) -- AL-JAMAHIRIYAH daily, the organ of the revolutionary 
committees, accused the Saudi Kingdom of taking revenge measures against the people of 
the jamahiriyah, following the Saudi's setback at Fes summit. 


The paper added that by glutting the oil market, Saudi Arabia was acting on orders from 
the Reagan's administration which considers the jamahiriyah as a threat to its designs 
in the Arab and African regions. 


"The Saudi position of revenge against the people of the jamahiriyah follows the policy 
review made by the Reagan administration in the light of the escalating effectiveness 

of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front and the role it is playing against 
American, Zionist and reactionary designs", the paper continued. 


The paper attributed the Saudi hostility toward the jamahiriyah to the dialectical 
contradiction between the surrogate elements of evil and the revolutionary forces which 
will be solved in favour of the revolution. 


AL-QADHDHAFI SAID TO REDUCE AID TO LEBANESE 


PM150821 London AL-MAJALLAH in Arabic 13-19 Mar 82 p 11 
[From "AL-MAJALLAH Correspondents" feature] 
[Text] According to informed Lebanese sources, Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has 


reduced financial aid to Lebanese political organizations by as much as 75 percent. 
All attempts to persuade Al-Qadhdhafi to change his mind have failed. 


Sources close to Libya say this step was taken because these Lebanese organizations 
"have not played the role required of them." Other sources maintain that the real 
reason for the move is Libya's serious liquidity problem.
