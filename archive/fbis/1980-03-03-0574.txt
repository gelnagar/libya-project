EGYPTIAN REVOLUTIONARY COMMITTEES* TASKS OUTLINED 


LD222044 Tripoli JANA in English 1425 GMT 22 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, February 22, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--In response to the unionist 
nationalist march in Tripoli yesterday and considering the dicta "no resolution outside 
the revolutionary committees," "committees everywhere," the Egyptian revolutionary forces 
from various parts of the Jamahiriyah have met and formed revolutionary committees. On 
this occasion communiques were issued which define their revolutionary tasks: 


Communique of the Egyptian revolutionary force in Tripoli in response to the unionist 
march in Tripoli on 21st February 1980. Regarding the setting up of Egyptian revolu- 
tionary committees in order to carry out a popular revolution to topple the defeated 
Egyptian regime as well as all the Arab reactionary regimes, the Egyptien forces in 
Tripoli convened and created a revolutionary committee. This followed their awareness 
that revolutionary committees are the means to secure a popular revolution. 


The General Secretariat of the Libyan-Egyptian Friendship Society was used as its office. 
The new revolutionary committee passed the following resolutions: 


l--To incite the Egyptian Arab masses to undertake a people's revolution against the 
ruling regime in Egypt and to toe the line of the committee. 


2--To train in using arms so as to prepare an armed people's struggle in order to bring 
down the defeated As-Sadat regime. 


3--To act towards undermining American and Zionist interests in any part of the world. 


4Ye-=-To act in complete and constant harmony with the revolutionary committees in the 
Jamahiriyah. 


5--To consistently believe in the third universal theory as the way to get rid of all 
forms of despotic governments. 


6--To call on all the sincere elements to adhere to it in the proper style and to pro- 
claim that there is no meaning to a revolution outside revolutionary committees. 


Al-Fatah forever. Committees everywhere. 

The Egyptian Revolutionary Committee, Tripoli. 

Communique of the Egyptian Revolutionary Committee in the city of Bayda. 

Revolution is a popular progressive action all through Arab history. The revolutionary 


spirit which pervades the hearts of the Arab masses cannot be repressed and cannot be 
doubtful in the eyes of the collaborators and reactionaries.

The rise of the loathsome Zionist flag in the sky of the Egyptian capital represents the 
motive which has sparked off the revolutionary spirit and determination in the hearts of 
the masses which had raised high a million Palestinian flags in the sky of Egypt. Fur- 
thermore, the Egyptian forces in the Jamahiriyah have organized and unified their ranks 
and set up a revolutionary committee in the city of Bayda in application of the dictum 
"no revolution outside revolutionary committees." The regional secretariat of the 
Libyan-Egyptian Friendship Society was used as its office. Its tasks are the following: 


l--To incite Egyptian masses within and without Egypt to adhere to it in order to launch 
a people's revolution. 


2--To act towards toppling the defeated humiliated As-Sadat regime. 
3--To practise revolutionary monitoring and revolutionary violence. 
4--To work in harmony with the Libyan Arab revolutionary committees. 


5--To protect the grand Al-Fatah revolution against all the conspiracies organized against 
it. 


6-=-To work in harmony with the Tunisian revolutionary committees and to struggle against 
the French invasion. 


7--To call upon the Egyptian Arab masses to take up arms to restore the honour and pride 
which have been trodden upon. 


"Al-Fatah forever, committees everywhere." 

The Egyptian Revolutionary Committee, Bayda. 

Communique of Egyptian Revolutionary Committee in Derna. 

The Egyptian Arab masses in the land of the grand Al-Fatah revolution, while witnessing 
and becoming fully convinced that the organized revolutionary action carried out in the 
Jamahiriyah is the best example to follow, and in response to the dictum "no meaning to 
a revolution outside the revolutionary committees" have calléd upon the Egyptian Arab 


revolutionary force in Derna to rally and form a revolutionary committee. 


This committee will be based at the branch secretariat of the Egyptian-Libyan Brotherhood 
Society in Derna. Its main tasks will be: 


l1--To incite the Egyptian Arab masses in and out of Egypt to topple the defeatist reac- 
tionary regime and to restcre Egypt's image as part of the Arab nation. 


2--To call on the Egyptian masses to work in solidarity with the Egyptian revolutionary 
committees and to start an organized revolutionary action. 


3--To protect the grand Al-Fatah revolution, the hope of the Arab masses aspiring to 
unity and liberation. 


4e-To preach the third universal theory, the way to final emancipation from all forms of 
dictatorial regimes. 


5--To act in complete unity with the revolutionary committees of the Jamahiriyah, Iran 
and Tunisia. 


6=--To take up arms to realize the armed revolutionary people. 
Allah is great. Al-Fatah forever! Committees everywhere. 
The Egyptian Revolutionary Committee in Derna.
