LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Asks Lebanon's Al-Wazzan To Resign 
LDO11542 Tripoli JANA in English 1500 GMT | Sep 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulgidah 24, Sept 1, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The leader of the revolution today sent a message 
to Shafiq al-Wazzan in which he urged him to resign his post so 
as to avoid a massacre of the Moslem masses in Lebanon by the 
crusade launched by the army of the Phalangist authority and 
the French and American forces. The message reads as follows: 


Shafiq al-Wazzan, it became quite clear that the authority in 
Lebanon is that of the isolationist and fascist Phalange Party and 
not representative of the whole of Lebanon and your presence as 
the head of the dummy government is a protective cover and gives 
legality to the fascist Phalangist authority in Lebanon. 


The crusade waged by the Phalangists, the army of the isolation- 
ist Phalangist authority and the American and French troops 
against the Moslems and the nationalist forces in Lebanon, make 
your stay in your post no longer justified as it forms a protective 
cover for this undeclared crusade. From the position of 
brotherhood I call on you to resign so that the Moslem masses in 
Lebanon are not massacred in the name of legitimacy and in 
order you do not become the cover of this crusade. It is a historic 
moment that needs a historic stand from your side. 


[signed] Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi, Ist September 1983.
