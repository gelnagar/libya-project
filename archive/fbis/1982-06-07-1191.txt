LIBYA

JAMAHIRIYAH AGREES TO ARAB EMERGENCY MEETING 


LDU71310 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 7 Jun 82 


[Text] The people's committee for the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison has informed 
the Arab League of the jamahiriyah's agreement to an emergency meeting of the joint 

Arab defense council, a meeting called for by the Lebanese Government and the PLO to 

adopt a unified Arab action toward the Zionist enemy and the terrorist invasion operations 
he is carrying out in southern Lebanon. 


A source at the people's committee for the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison made a 
statement to JANA today, in which he stressed that the Socialist People's Libyan 

Arab Jamahiriyah is fully prepared to defend Lebanon and the Palestinian Revolution 

and to resist Zionist aggression. He said: The jamahiriyah affirms its readiness to 
carry out this pan-Arab duty. It reaffirms its previous commitment to provide cover for 
Lebanon in facing repeated Zionist aggression. 


COMMENTARY ASSAILS SYRIA'S POSITION ON LEBANON 


LD062318 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 6 Jun 82 
[Station commentary ] 


[Text] The Arab masses from the ocean to the Gulf are asking why Syria has not yet 
entered the war to repel the Zionist aggression against Lebanon. Why have the missiles 
stationed on the peaks of Sannin not been unleashed to hunt the raiding Israeli aircraft? 
Why aren't the Syrian deterrent forces embarking on the battle of honor and heroism at 
least to defend itself at the side of the joint forces? Why hasn't the order to 

fight been issued to the Syrian forces which have been provoked by the crossing of the 
Zionist forces on the (Jabal ash-Shaykh) axis, within their sight and within the range 

of their weapons? Why hasn't the order been issued which would satisfy the Syrian forces 
thirst, the thirst to fight not only to regain the Golan and protect the Palestinian 
Resistance, but even the thirst to defend themselves? 


These questions occupy the minds of the masses of this nation following the events of the 
zionist aggression. This nation bows its head to the glory of the fedayeen of the 
Palestinian-Lebanese joint forces who regretably have been engaged on their own, for 3 
days and nights, in ferocious battles facing 20,000 Zionist troops deployed on three 

axes in the direction of (Jabal ash-Shaykh), the Lebanese coastline and the middle region 
and backed up by the Zionist air and naval forces. 


The masses are right in asking these questions because they have known Syria as a 
fortress of steadfastness and confrontation, and have known Syria always to refuse 
surrender, always to challenge the stable David path, always to protect the Palestinian 
Resistance and to safeguard the welfare of Lebanon and the security of its nation. They 
have known Syria as the light of Arab hope amidst the darkness of defeat and surrender 
and as the Arab front that has been the [word indistinct] the integrity and dignity of 
this nation amidst the fall of other Arab fronts at the hands of the enemy. Our masses 
also have known the heroic Syrian Army as an example of Arab military courage, boldness 
and faith, which has what it takes in military techniques and the most modern weapons 

to tilt the balance of the war in the interest of this nation. 


the political leadership in steadfast Syria has a historic and favorable opportunity to 
destroy the aggression, liberate the Golan and regain its dignity which was (?violated) 
when the annexation of the Golan was announced. It has complete international legitimacy 
in entering the battie with the whole of Syrian military might and with the Arab nation, 
with the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front behind it; because at least it would 

be exercising the lawful right of self-defense of the Syrian positions themselves in 
(Jabal ash-Shaykh) and of the Syrian deterrent forces.

The Zionist aircraft are flying with complete freedom over the positions of the Syrian 
missiles in the Sannin Mountains. The Zionist objective is not only the physical liquidation 
of the Palestinian Resistance but also aims at surrounding and destroying Syria because Syria 
is an Arab force taken seriously by the enemy. Thus, the political leadership in Damascus is 
dutybound to drop all Arab and international reservations because it has been internationally 
proven that the objective of the United States and of the Zionist entity is the occupation of 
the whole region. 


At a time when we declare the Arab national demand of offering sacrifices and pointing the 
Syrian bullet toward the Golan to repel the aggression, the masses of the occupied territory 
are also called upon to immediately set the fire of their overwhelming uprising, which has 
earned the admiration of the whole world. This is the right time for the most violent 
uprising to ease the military pressure and Zionists' weight on the joint forces in Lebanon, 
to force the enemy to disperse and scatter its forces on many fronts instead of concentrating 
them on one front and to disturb the enemy's security from within. Upsetting the enemy's 
internal front undoubtedly will affect the enemy's external front in Lebanon. The nearly 

1 million Palestinians inside the occupied territory could give the enemy a violent struggle 
even by using stones, Molotov cocktails and locally manufactured weapons or by capturing the 
enemy's weapons and arsenals. The 1 million Palestinians inside the occupied territory, 
which has demonstrated its heroism, vitality and revolutionary activity to the whole world, 
must not take the role of a spectator regarding the savage massacre committed by the enemy 

in Lebanon. The Palestinian and Jordanian masses must not remain observers and spectators 

of the events in Lebanon. They must become the effective strategic depth for the Palestinian 
popular uprising inside the occupied territory and provide it with reinforcements and fighting 
capabilities and must destroy all obstacles to reaching the heart of Palestine. The masses 
of this nation also must overcome (?regional) obstacles, chains and barbed wire to reach 
Palestine and Lebanon at any cost. 


The fifth Arab-Zionist war is actually exposing the true intentions of this enemy which sees 
no security and stability for itself except on the debris of this nation. The time has come 
for those masses to settle their accounts with all the reactionary poison and rotten elements 
on Arab territory, with those who have appointed themselves as guards of the enemy's security 
for 34 years. Arab towns must be transformed into Tyre, Sidon, An-Nabatiyah and (Qal'at 
Shaqia). It is shameful that 15 projectiles should be dropped every minute on An-Nabatiyah 
alone while Arab capitals sleep under lights with the noise of drunkards and the activities 

of merchants and brokers and of those taking pleasure in false worldly activities. Therefore, 
everyone who has an iota of Arab dignity and honor must move. Let that roaring popular 
volcano erupt to sweep from its path the piles of reactionary traitorous scum in the Arab 
land. To die in the battle of honor and heroism is better and more glorious than living in 
degenerate humility, drinking the bitterness of humiliation with every new day, being trampled 
underfoot and violated. 


MALTESE FOREIGN MINISTER ARRIVES 5 JUNE 


LD052224 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1930 GMT 5 Jun 82 


[Text] Maltese Foreign Minister Sceberra Trigona has praised the jamahiriyah's position 

with regard to the Maltese people. The Maltese official, upon arrival at Tripoli international 
airport this evening, at the head of a delegation, told a JANA correspondent that the position 
of the Libyan Arab people toward the Maltese people has enabled Malta to attain its independence 
and freedom.

He expressed the hope that relations between the two friendly peoples will see a further 
development toward strengthening cooperation in all fields in the interest of both friendly 
countries. 


He said his country supports the efforts to ensure the neutrality of the Mediterranean, 
driving out foreign fleets and making it a sea of security and peace. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Reception 

LD061732 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1705 GMT 6 Jun 82 
[Text] Tripoli, 6 Jun (JANA) -- Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has received the Maltese foreign 
minister, Sceberra Trigona, who conveyed to him a message from Maltese Prime Minister Dom 
Mintoff. 
The visit falls within the framework of bolstering cooperation between the jamahiriya 
and Malta and the contacts that took place during Al-Qadhdhafi's stopover in Malta while 
enroute home from Austria.
