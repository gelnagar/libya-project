LIBYA

PLO’s Al-Wazir Discusses PNC With Al-Qadhdhafi 
LD042103 Tripoli Television Service in Arabic 
2030 GMT 4 May 87 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has received Brother 
Khalil al-Wazir, alias Abu Jihad, member of the Fatah Centra! 
Committee and deputy commander in chief of the Palestinian 
Revolution Forces. Abu Jihad briefed the brother leader on the 
results of the PNC meetings in Algiers, and on the Palestinian 
situation in general. 


Opposes Conference, Departs 
LD052125 Tripoli Television Service in Arabic 
2000 GMT 5 May 87 


[Text] Brother Khalil al-Wazir, also known as Abu Jihad, mem- 
ber of the Fatah Central Committee and deputy commander in 
chief of the Palestinian Revolution Forces, has high praise for the 
honest efforts made by the brother leader of the revolution to 
unite the Palestinian detachments at the PNC meetings in 
Algiers. This unity had an effect on the results achieved by the 
PNC at the close of its meetings. 


In a statement to JANA before leaving Tripoli International 
airport this afternoon, Brother Khalil al-Wazir expressed his 
happiness at meeting the brother leader yesterday. He said: 
We reviewed the results of the PNC meetings and the Palestinian 
situation in general. 


In his comments, Brother Khalil al-Wazir said the current 
Palestinian situation dictates unity in Arab policy in support of 
the Palestinian Arab people’s fight. The armed struggle against 
the enemy must be stepped up, and the Palestinian rifle pre- 
served. 


At the end of his remarks, Brother Khalil al-Wazir talked about 
the so-called international conference. He said: This con- 
ference is a conspiracy against the Palestinian cause. Its strings

PFLP’s Habash Thanks Al-Qadhdhafi for Support 
LDO51759 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 
1600 GMT 5 May 87 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has received a message 
from PFLP Secretary General George Habash. Habash had high 
praise for the efforts of the brother leader of the revolution, which 
led to Palestinian unity on the basis of progressive principles. 
Habash informed the brother leader of the results of the recent 
PNC session in Algeria
