It has no specific Tripoli sites or killings.
• The CIA outlined a multimillion-dollar Libyan effort to influence US policy and opinion, as described to the Justice Department.
• One organizer said he spent more than $225,000 on air fare to bring 125 Americans to Libya.
• Libyan offers included brokerage deals for peanuts, soybeans and wheat, and oil deals.
• Sen. Birch Bayh's panel of nine senators would decide after Oct. 4 whether to widen the inquiry.
• The Justice Department sent a letter of inquiry to Rev. Jesse Jackson.
• 1978-10: Richard Shadyac's group took about 125 Americans to Libya under an 'Arab-American dialog' seminar label, shortly after Billy Carter's visit to Libya.
