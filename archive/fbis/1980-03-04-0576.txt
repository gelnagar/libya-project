LIBYA

PDRY FOREIGN MINISTER DEPARTS LIBYA 3 MAR 
LDO32000 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 3 Mar 80 LD 
[Summary] Tripoli--PDRY Foreign Minister Salim Salih Muhammad left Tripoli this after- 
noon at the end of his visit to Libya, He was seen off at the airport by the assistant 
secretary general for political affairs at the Foreign Secretariat, by the 
ambassadors of the PDRY, the YAR and others,
