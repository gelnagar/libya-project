AL-QADHDHAFI ORDERS CURTAILMENT OF EXECUTIONS 


ID112016 Tripoli Voice of the Arabs Homeland in Arabic 1733 GMT 11 Jun 80 


[Speech by Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, secretary general of the Libyan General People's 
Congress, at celebrations being held at Ra's Lanuf on the occasion of the 10th annivers- 
ary of the evacuation of U.S, bases--live ] 


[Excerpts] Brothers, we are today marking the 10th anniversary of the imperialist U.S. 
forces from our land, We are celebrating this 10th anniversary at a place which used 
to be one of the U.S, military bases in Libya, 


At this place where we are gathered here today there used to be a Coast Guard base 
which used to direot U.S. ships in the Mediterranean, 


When the Libyan revolution was staged, there used to be more than one U.S, base in 
Libya; in addition to Wheelus field, there used to be four other bases, 


Had it not been for the revolution which was staged by the army, we would not have 

been able to remove the U.S. bases from Libyan territory, The Libyan people only then 
realized the extent of the U.S. presence in Libya, But many peoples now have U.S. 
imperialism on their land; they are suffering as the Libyan people did in the past, We 
believe that these peoples can only rid themselves of imperialist domination by a 
revolution,

Now you have become convinced that these states which enjoy false independence are still 
in fact colonized. They think that by having a king or a president they are independent; 
they are mistaken. It has become apparent to you and to history that these states which 
are satisfied with this limited independence are still being colonized. But, brothers, 
despite the victory which the Libyan people scored by their revolution over the forces of 
colonialism this imperialism, regrettably, has not gone away far enough. 


The Libyan people expelled imperialism in 1970; the Libyans expelled U.S, imperialism in 1970 
from this land. But, regrettably, it returned in 1980 to the land of Egypt. The bases 
which left Libya in 1970 returned to Egypt, along the Libyan-Egyptian border in 1980. 


For every step forward taken by the free and honorable forces in this Arab world, lackey 
forces took another step backward to cancel the forward step. Thus, contradictions in the 
Arab homeland are aborting each other. If a progressive step is taken in an Arab area, ree 
action and agentse=the agents of imperialismeehave taken a step backward. Evidence of this 
can be seen here--while we are today celebrating the 10th anniversary of the eviction of the 
U.S. forces, We feel sad and pained because these forces are not far from us; they left 
Libyan territory but now they can be found on Egyptian territory. Therefore, the battle 
against the United States, against imperialism, as far as the Arab nation is concerned, and 
not only as far as the Libyan people are concerned, is still on; it has not ended. It 

will end when imperialism is expelled from the entire Arab homeland. The freedom of the 
Libyan people is threatened so long as U.S, imperialism is on Egyptian territory. But the 
responsibility for overthrowing treason in Egypt is not confined to the Libyan people alone. 
In the first place it is in the hands of the Egyptian people and the Egyptian Army. It is 
also the responsibility of the Arab nation. Egypt has become an aggressive base (?against ) 
the Arab nation. But today (?we declare) our alliance with the Egyptian people and army in 
order to topple treason in Egypt. [shouts and cheers] 


We have to tell the Egyptian Army that we protect its rear--the Egyptian Army concentrated 
along the Libyan border--and we want to tell it before the whole world that we will protect 
its rear if it wants to topple treason in Egypt. We cannot direct our rifles against the 
Egyptian Army if it moves to the Egyptian capital to overthrow treason. But we will be 
compelled to train our guns on the Egyptian Army if the latter carries out the orders of 
agent As-Sadat by attacking Libyan territory. [shouts and cheers; anti-As-Sadat slogans] 


The overthrow of treachery in Egypt is not longer only a task for the Arab nation; it is 
also a task for the Islamic nation. 


Thousands of Iranians who were formerly soldiers of the shah and members of SAVAK gangs, who 
returned from the United States and who fled from the onslaught of the heroic Iranian people, 
are now in Egypt, where they are being trained in the use of arms to subvert the Islamic 
people's revolution in Iran. Therefore, the Egyptian regime has become the catspaw of 

world imperialism led by U.S. imperialism. It now represents a real danger to the Islamic 
nation, That is why it is a holy jihad task for Muslims, especially the Iranian masses, to 
bring down the traitorous regime in Egypt. 


In this month, in which we celebrate our triumph over the U.S. forces, the sultan of the 
donkey's shed, the sultan of Muscat and Oman, decided to sign with the United States an 
imperialist treaty which will restore to it an imperialist base in a strategic area which 
it had lost earlier in Libya after the revolution.

Sultan Qabus is giving Arab territory to the United States to build military bases and 
this month established a military alliance with the United States, thus violating the 
Arab League charter and the resolutions of Arab summits. He also broke his own pre- 
vious agreement to the Arab charter, in which it is stated that no foreign force should 
be allowed in Arab territory. 


He thus allowed U.S. imperialism to penetrate into a very important and strategic re- 
gion to the Arab nation and the world: the Gulf region. 


If a big power such as the United States entered militarily into the Gulf region, this 
would lead to another power--opposed to the United States--also entering. We would 

thus open up Libyan territory to international conflicts. [sentence as heard] Therefore, 
this dirty agent Qabus should be expelled from the Arab League. He should be subjected 
to the sanctions which were applied to the agent of the Egyptian regime who violated 

the Arab League charter and all Arab conventions, Arab summit resolutions and Arab 
unanimity, defied the Arab will, recognized the Israeli enemy, [word indistinct] the 
United States, and opened up his country to U.S.-Israeli bases, 


This is a grave matter which threatens the independence of the Arab nation and on which 
we can not remain silent. This we put before the Arab political quarters. As to the 
evolutionary aspect, it is necessary to rebuild, organize, rearm and refinance the 
liberation front of Oman so that it can undertake the revolutionary task of bringing 
down this agent regime, 


At this time, when we feel that we are free on our free land, at this time when we feel 
we are happy because we are celebrating one of the days of the strengtheing of freedom 
and triumph on Libyan soil, there are another Arab people who, at these very hours, are 
being subjected to aggression; they are being subjected to Israeli raids, being sub- 
jected to [word indistinct] every day. They are the fraternal Lebanese people. 


While we are happy here, the people there are grieving. We are happy because we tri- 
umphed over colonialism on Libyan soil, but the Lebanese people are being subjected 
daily to aggressive raids on their land. Towns are destroyed from the land, sea and 
air. The skies, the land and the sea of Lebanon are being violated before the eyes of 
the Arab nation which is not lifting a finger. 


The Lebanese territory and the Lebanese people have endured more than any other Arab 
territory has endured [word indistinct] the Arab cause and the Palestinian issue. The 
Lebanese territory is now the only territory which endures the Palestinian resistance, 
In return, the Lebanese people pay the price. They pay it in blood and lives in return 
for enduring the Palestinian resistance, 


Why doesn't the Arab nation appreciate this pan-Arab historic stand of the Lebanese 
people? Why are the Lebanese people alone being subjected to aggression without the Arab 
nation taking collective action to repel the Israeli aggression on Arab Lebanon? How 
can the Lebanese people alone shoulder the responsibility of protecting the Palestinian 
resistance? The Palestinian issue is an Arab issue; all the Arabs must shoulder their 
responsibility. 


Lebanon is a member of the Arab League and is being subjected to foreign aggression. In 
accordance with the Arab League charter, the Arab nation must collectively repel this 
aggression against this Arab country which is a member of the Arab League. But the 
aggression is taking place every day and the Arab nation is not carrying out its duty. 
This fact highlights the need for a summit-level meeting--if any results can be achieved 
from such a summit meeting and if the resolutions of the summit are respected, especially 
for Lebanon, 


Vv. 12 Jun 80 NORTH AFRICA 


Through their popular conferences, the Libyan people will shoulder their responsibility 

as one of the people of the Arab nation toward another Arab people, namely the Lebanese 

people whose towns are being destroyed every day by Israeli bombs from the sea, land and 
air. Lives are lost every day as a result of this Israeli aggression and the continuous 
Israeli raids. In spite of all this, they still embrace the Palestinian resistance and 

allocate for it a part of its land. 


The Arab nation must act collectively for the success of the plan of national political 
reconciliation in Lebanon so that a national government can be set up in Lebanon and so 
that harmony can be restored among the communities of the Lebanese people. 


In return, the Arab nation must inevitably shoulder total responsibility with the Lebanese 
people. It must also guarantee the presence of the Palestinian resistance on the Lebanese 
territory. Many fronts were closed to the resistance which you are well aware of. 


As to the Jordanian front, which the resistance lost, we hope it will reopen again to 
the Palestinian resistance. This is a pan-Arab duty for Jordan from which there is no 
escape, even if it means that it would result in losses for Jordan just as is the case 
now with regard to Lebanon. The Arab nation, including the Libyan people, must shoulder 
its share in these losses in return for the protection of the Palestinian resistance. 


On this occasion we greet the people of the occupied land, the steadfast people confronting 
at the same time the enemy, and which deals blows from (?inside) against the Zionist entity 
at all times. We must back this people and consolidate its struggle and steadfastness in 
the occupied land. ‘his is a sacred duty of the Libyan people as it is the sacred duty 

of all Arab peoples. 


I do not believe that even among the traditional regimes there is one that does not accept 
Arab agreement on taking collective defensive measures against the enemy in relation to 
Lebanese territory, collective Arab measures for the reconstruction of Lebanon, and collec- 
tive Arab measures to assist in solving the Lebanese and Palestinian problems and in the 
consolidation of the positions of Palestinian resistance in Lebanon and in the consolidation 
of the unity of the Lebanese people and the affirmation of the Arabism of the Lebanese 
people. 


At the same time, we must (?admit) that there is (?extremism) that may take place in the 
Lebanese arena by several Arab sides and, in Lebanon, it is headed by local Lebanese sides. 


The whole Arab nation has contributed to this and this is the extreme [word indistinct] 
against some Lebanese sects, especially the Christian one--a matter which drove it to seek 
alliance with the enemy. But those who crossed the red line and allied themselves with the 
enemy regardless of all the causes must be eliminated by all means, and first of all the 
dirty agent Sa'ad Haddad, who split off a part of the Lebanese Army and joined the Israeli 
Army and then started to shell Lebanese Arab villages and towns from the occupied land. 
This dirty agent, Sa'ad Haddad, and those who are with him; there can be no meeting with 
them. [masses chant for about 5 minutes; slogans indistinct] 


Revolutionarily, it is the duty of the Arab revolutionary force to tackle those who 
cooperate with the Israeli enemy, those who cooperate with the U.S. enemy, those who con- 
tinue to have dealings with the regime of Qabus, the sultan of the donkey's shed, and those 
who have failed to fully boycott the agent Egyptian regime.

The Arab revolutionary force concerned in this matter is that which is headed by the 
heroic Palestinian resistance, whose interest demands the toppling of ail the forces 
impeding the liberation march and the destruction of all the forces which ally themselves 
with imperialism, the enemy of the Palestine people, in the first place. 


In addition to the traditional political action which saves whatever can be saved of the 
deteriorating Arab conditions, the revolutionary action cannot look backward or stop for 
one single day and look on. The revolutionary work is creative work. Revolutionary work 
is the decisive work. The revolutionary force is the living force of the Arab nation. 

It is the one that makes history and the future. [applause and cheers ] 


The revolutionary committees have confirmed that the arm of the revolution is long and 
strong, and that they can reach any place in the world to strike at the enemies of the 
revolution. [cheers] 


After the revolutionary committees confirmed their capability to operate anywhere in the 
world and that they were capable of striking with bravery and with a steel fist everywhere, 
and after the enemies of the revolution became convinced that the hotels of London, the 
brothels of Italy, and the winehouses of Beirut could not protect them from the verdict 
passed on them by the revolutionary committees--when all this has been asserted, I want 

to ask the revolutionary committees something. 


But before stating these points I want to observe that hundreds of Libyans who fled from 
the popular revolution since its beginning and until now, have now returned following 

the ultimatum addressed to them. Others began preparing for their return and all the base 
attempts by imperialism and agents of imperialism in the region to lure some (?misled 
people) to cooperate with them have failed. This spy [not named] who fled the country 
and lost his Libyan nationality for his treason is today a resident of mental hospitals; 
he has totally collapsed. From the (?Bahnan) hospital in Egypt, he went to the military 
hospital in Kuwait, and then to the (?Mannuba) mental hospital in Tunis. 


I want to ask the revolutionary committees the following: First: To stop now all the 
fedayeen operations in all parts of the world. [shouts] 


Second: Not to carry out the death sentences of the revolutionary committees except on 
those who are convicted by a revolutionary court. Exceptions to this are those who are 
proved, in any way--even if this is not by a revolutionary court--to have had dealings 
with the following: the Egyptian, Israeli, and U.S. authorities. Those who deal with 
these three authorities commit high treason and lose every protection and deserve death 
everywhere. 


The last attempt by the imperialist circles which have lost the security in their own 
countries to stand against the revolutionary forces was made by the UK police today-- 
officially. A very strongly worded protest will be presented by the Foreign Secretaria 
to the UK Government for carrying out this official action against the People's Bureau 
in London. 


The UK police today transported some people--14 in number--in a vehicle and exploited 
today--being a holiday--when the bureau was closed and staged a pseudodemonstration. 

Ten of them were Indians and Pakistanis who were misled; the remaining four were Libyans, 
the sons of (?Tighayit) who committed suicide because he was caught redhanded running 

a factory for the manufacture of alcohol in his house in Tripoli, and after that he was 
convicted of dealing with a foreign country, receiving money from the latter. When he 
was arrested by the revolutionary committees, he committed suicide.

The sons of this man--the 4 of them--were gathered by the UK police with the other 10 
foreigners and it was alleged that they were Libyan students staging a demonstration. 
This indicates the absurdity of the counterrevolutionary attempts. 


This indicates how trivial these attempts are. This shows that there is no force capable 
of opposing the revolution. The last of these absurd attempts is what the UK police did 
today: It shows that the revolutionary aétion by the revolutionary committees has tri- 
umphed everywhere throughout the world--at home as well as abroad. [cheers] 


I would like also to ask the Students! Popular Conferences and the General Congress of 
the Libyan Students Union to reconsider the resolution they adopted with regard to the 
necessity for the return to Libya of Libyan students studying abroad at their own expense. 
They are asked to reconsider this resolution to allow such students to continue their 
studies at the expense of society or at their own expense, after having ascertained that 
the masses of the students are strong and capable of toppling the counterrevolutionary 
elements. [applause and cheers] 


In conclusion, I wish to greet the delegations which are sharing with us now the commemo- 
ration of the 10th anniversary of the expulsion of the U.S. imperialist forces from our 
territory. They are: 


Brother Mohamed Lamine, the Saharan Democratic Arab Republic [SDAR] prime ministers--the 
SDAR in which popular conferences and popular committees have been formed and in which 
the popular authority will be asserted when it succeeds in liberating all the territories 
of Saquia El Hamra and Rio de Oro. [applause] 


We also greet the Iranian popular delegations [applause], particularly the delegation of 


the Iranian Consultative Council. [applause] We declare our stand by the Iranian Islamic 
Revolution in the face of all imperialist challenges. [applause] At the same time as 

we affirm our all-out stand, we also wish to affirm our full stand by the side of the 
Iranian popular revolution against the imperialist attempts, particularly the U.S. provo- 
cations--these aggressive provocations which are directed against the Iranian people, 

and we express our regret at the present confrontation which is taking place now between 
an Arab fraternal country, which is Iraq, and the Iranian Revolution. 


We hope this confrontation among the brothers will end so that the Islamic unity between 
the Persians and the Arabs will be restored under the banners of Islam and a new civiliza- 
tion will be born for the world, as we produced a world civilization when the Arabs and 
Persians united under the banners of Islam in the middle ages, which is the basis of the 
civilization of the present modern age. [applause] 


We also greet the chief of staff of the Chadian forces which are visiting us. [applause] 
He has come at the head of a delegation and is now with us here. We wish to congratulate 
them on the evacuation of the French forces from Chadian territory. [applause] At the 
same time, we regret to see civil war and fighting taking place among the Chadians them- 
selves, their various factions. 


We say that Libya has taken a neutral stand in this conflict because it does not wish to 
side with one Chadian side against another. But from the start and even before the estab- 
lishment of the people's authority, the Libyan stand was on the side of the whole Chadian 
people, with all its races, sects and factions, against imperialism and backwardness.

This, too, is our attitude throughout Africa. We have supported the Chadian people against 
the imperialist forces andthe imperialist forces have now left. This is a victory which 
was realized by the Libyan people and the Chadian people against the imperialist forces. 
[applause] The Chadian people and their armed forces stood on the side of the Chadian 
national liberation front--FROLINAT--against the fascist regime in Ndjamena. Through 

this alliance, it was possible to topple the fascist regime in Ndjamena, FROLINAT thus 
attained power in Ndjamena. [applause] 


But as you know the true situation in Chad well, it is regretted that some time ago, 
fighting began among the various Chadian factions which belong to FROLINAT. In this case, 
we decided to adopt a neutral stand, It was necessary that we should take a neutral stand, 
and we Will not interfere in the Chadian internal affairs. We will not interfere in the 
Chadian war between the Chadian sides. Our stand is essentially with the Chadian people 
and with FROLINAT--the vanguard of the Chadian people against imperialism, and against 
fascism in Africa and in Chad. 


It is the duty of the Libyan people not to intervene materially in the strife in Chad except 
in accordance with an official treaty to be signed by the two sides--the Chadian and the 
Libyan--and to be deposited at the headquarters of the OAU and the United Nations, so 

that the Libyan material action in Chad will be supported by international law and will 

be legitimate, 


If the Chadian and Libyan sides work out a legal action of this type, a contractual-action, 
this will help them to work together to end the war in Chad and establish security and 
rebuild Chad. 


But without a treaty that permits the Libyans to work with the Chadians in tackling this 
internal problem, then the Libyans must take a neutral stand. 


We also greet the chief of staff of the Burundian Armed Forces, [applause] He is also 
present with us here at this celebration, 


We also greet the delegation of Bulgarian Muslims, [applause] 


We also greet the head of the Muslim League in India, We also greet the Arab and foreign 
diplomatic corps in Libya who are attending the celebration with us. 


As much as we rejoice and celebrate the victory of freedom in Libyan territory, we must not 
stick our heads in the sand and ignore the threats to freedom around us. We must realize 
that freedom in Libya is in danger as long as the enemy threatens an Arab territory, any 
Arab territory. As far as the Libyans are concerned, the mission has not ended. It has 
not ended nationally [Ala Mustawa al-Watan] in terms of national liberation. 


The enemy is still lurking in wait for the Arab nation, still scoring victories over the 
Arab nation, We still have pan-Arab tasks [(qawmiyah], represented in the liberation of 

the Arab territory and the unity of the Arab nation, and the glory of the Islamic nation, 
March forward! [applause and cheers ] 

IRANIAN DELEGATION LEADER LAUDS LIBYAN SOLIDARITY 

LD120940 Tripoli JANA in English 0535 GMT 12 Jun 80 

[Excerpt] Ra's al-Unuif, June 12, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The head of the Iranian Consulta- 
tive Council affirmed the revolutionary solidarity between the Libyan Arab and Iranian 


peoples indicating thas it is one of the outstanding factors of this era, of the Muslim 
revolution, 


Vv. 12 Jun 80 I 8 NORTH AFRICA 


In a speech at the massive public celebration at Ra's Al-Unuf on the occasion of the 
tenth anniversary of the expulsion of U.S, bases from the homeland, he pointed that the 
era of the masses is full of movement, warmth, deep revolution and radical change 
affirming that this era has witnessed the greatest solidarity movement in history 
between the two revolutions in Libya and Iran, 


He indicated that the Great Al-Fatah Revolution and the Islamic revolution in Iran have 
their effects and their organic ties. They also have their spiritual, moral and revol- 
utionary dimensions throughout history. He pointed to the depth of religious and 
fraternal ties binding the two brotherly peoples the Libyan Arab and the Iranian peoples 
who united inconfronting the spirit of evil and the schemes of American conspiracy. 


He said, "Wehave all lived the misery, tragedies and oppression of the U.S, imperialism 
and felt together the deep bitterness of living under its slavery and malice." He 
added, "You started several years ahead of us to evacuate colonialism and its puppets 
as well as all its factors within the country, and God gave you success." 


We feel proud today when we participate with you [in] your celebrations of evacuating 
the U.S, colonialist bases and all its factors from the Libyan Arab soil. 


In his speech the head of delegation carried the greetings of Imam Ayatollah Khomeyni, 
leader of the Islamic revolution in Iran, the greetings of the Iranian Islamic Consulta- 
tive Council and the greetings. of the Muslim people in Iran to the Muslim Arab people in 
Libya and their historic leadership.
