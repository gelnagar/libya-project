LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI INTERVIEWED BY ITALIAN TV 
AU120959Y Rome ANSA in English 0815 GMT 12 Apr 78 AU 


{Text] Rome, April 12 (ANSA)--Italian Television last night broadcast an interview 
with Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, during which the man responsible for trans- 
forming the country into the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah pinpointed his 
role in the Arab world and his policy's objectives. 


The first questions of the four Italian Television newsmen--who interviewed Al-Qadhdhafi 
in a tent near Benghazi--concerned terrorism. The Libyan leader was asked the reasons 
behind suspicions that the Libyan secret services may be implicated in terrorism in 
Italy. 


Al-Qadhdhafi replied that the rumors are part of an anti-Palestinian campaign organized 
by Zionists together with their ally, American colonialism, because Jamahiriyah is an 
active supporter of the Palestinian people and that these charges are a link in the long 
chain of the international conspiracy against the Palestinian people, even though it 
appears aimed at the Libyans. 


In reply to a question on why Jamahiriyyth did not intervene in favor of the Palestinians 
in the recent Israeli attack in southern Lebanon, the colonel said the Palestinians are 
considered guests in a land which is not theirs, in a state presumed to be independent. 
All these factors created difficulties for a direct intervention. Otherwise such an 
intervention would have been a duty for all Arab countries, Al-Qadhdhafi added, 


During the interview the Libyan leader reconfirmed his total intransigence towards 
Israel and said that he is convinced that "political phenomena and racist colonial 
entities such as Israel or Rhodesia will disappear and the peoples who originally owned 
the land will win, the people of Palestine and the people of Zimbabwe." 


"We do not recognize anything," the colonel added, "called Israel. The world knows 
only Palestine," 


Al-Qadhdhafi explaine? his clash with Egyptian President Anwar as-Sadat--leader of a 
country with which Jamahiriyah is still seeking union--"because the two people are 
actually one. ‘The two lands with a single destiny are one single territory."

As-Sadat, al-Qadhdhafi continued, wants to see the formation of a Palestinian state next 
to Israel while Al-Qadhdhafi wants to see a Palestinian state which is not a state of 
Arab Palestinians but also of Jewish Palestinians. according to the Libyan leader, the 
other Jews, those "invaders who emigrated from various nations and arrived after 1948, 
will have to return to their nations of origin." 


Some of the last questions concerned relations between the Libyan Jamahiriyah and the 
Soviet Union which, Al-Qadhdhafi said, are good because the two countries have the same 
point of view towards the progress of exploited people and opposition towards colonialism. 
The Soviet Union supports, the colonel added, Jamahiriyah's positive non-alignment, the 
liberation of people and emancipation from exploitation. 


In reply to the last question, which concerned Italian colonization of Libya, the colonel 
said it was a "black page in our joint history, a page to be forgotten.” 


MORO FRONT LEADER PRAISES LIBYA'S ATTITUDE 
LD131947Y Tripoli JANA in English 1810 GMT 13 Apr 78 LD 


{Text] Kuwait, April 13, JAMA--Mr Nour Meswari, the chairman of the Moro Liberation Front, 
praised the attitude being adopted by the Jamahiriyah and Colonel al-Qadhdhafi towards 
the Moslem revolutionaries in the south Philippines in their just struggle. 


Mr Nour Meswari was reported by the JANA correspondent here to have declared that the 
Philippines authorities did violate the peace agreement concluded in Tripoli with the 
aim of ending the war in the Philippines. He also declared that the war in the south 
Philippines had fanned out to other areas and that the Philippines Government had 
recruited over 100,000 soldiers to take part in the fighting. 


Mr. Nour Meswari added that so far he has visited Algeria, Saudi Arabia and Kuwait as 
part of his tour of a number of Arab and Islamic countries. 


Received by At-Turayki 
LD131948Y Tripoli JANA in English 1836 GMT 13 Apr 78 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, 13 April, JANA--Dr. 'Ali 'Abd al-Salam at-Turayki, foreign secretary 
and chairman of the current session of the Foreign Ministers Council for the Islamic 
countries, today received Mr. Nour Meswari, chairman of the Moro Liberation Front, 
currently visiting tne Jamahiriyah. 


The meeting, which was attended by the head of the Department of Asian Affairs at the 
Foreign Secretariat, covered the recent developments in the south Philippines, the 
persecution to which the Moslems there were subjected and the ways of putting into 
practice the Tripoli agreement regarding the concession of self-rule to people in the 
south Philippines.
