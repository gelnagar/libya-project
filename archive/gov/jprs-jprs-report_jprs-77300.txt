[Report by Badi' Hasan: "Following Success of Shihatah's Talks in Rome, 
Al-Qadhdhafi, Expected To Visit Italy in Spring") 


[Excerpts] The Libyan chief of state will pay an official visit to Italy 

as an indication of the desire to improve relations between Rome and Tripoli. 
This has come as a result of official talks which Ahmad Shihatah, in charge 
of the External Relations Bureau, has had with his Italian counterpart 
Emilio Colombo. 


Shihatah held official talks with a number of Italian ministers and political 
personalities, including Andreotti and Italian Socialist Party Secretary 
General Craxi, it was agreed that Italian Foreign Trade Minister Enrico Manca 
will pay a visit to Tripoli. Italian President Sandro Pertini also expressed 
the desire to visit Tripoli. This desire was expressed during a meeting with 
Shihatah who gave the president a check for $6 million as aid from the Libyan 
Government for the victims of the earthquake that struck southern Italy last 
month. 


Shihatah also held economic talks with his Italian counterpart during which 
discussion dealt with the possibility of the Italian Government contributing 
to the Libyan 5-year plan (1981-1985) for which $80 billion has been allocated. 


On the political level the discussions between both sides dealt with the 
question of the 11 Italians detained in Libya on various charges. (The 
Italian Government asked that the charges against them should be specified 
and that consular contacts with them be facilitated. The talks also dealt 
with Italy's guarantee of Malta's neutrality (which includes Maltese obliga- 
tions toward Libya) and the oil prospecting question. In this regard Shihatah 
said that Libya and Malta will seek the arbitration by the Hague International 
Court of Justice in order to decide who has the right of sovereignty over the 
disputed area and, consequently, the right to extract oil from it. 


Shihatah praised the attitude of Italy and the Common Market countries toward 
the Palestine problem but reiterated that no peace can be reached without a 





comprehensive solution, including the establishment of a Palestinian atate in 
which all Palestinians, Jews and Muslim and Christian Arabs, can coexist, 
Failing this a third world war becomes a possibility. He also reiterated 
accusations against the United States=-that it is an imperialist country and 
that it is likely to threaten the oil routes between Libya and Europe. 


Speaking on Italian television about the Chad problem, Shihatah said: In no 
way did we intervene in that war. All that we did was to give technical aid 
to the legitimate government there and we will continue to give such aid as 

long a8 we find it necessary. We have obligations toward Chad since it 

is 4 sisterly and neighborly country and we will not hesitate to give it any 
aid it asks from us,
