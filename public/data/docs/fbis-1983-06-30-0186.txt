JANA: Egypt’s Mubarak ‘Conspiring’ Chad 
LD291655 Tripoli JANA in English 1441 GMT 29 Jun 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Ramadan 19, June 29 (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY) -- Another stage of the conspiracy against Chadian 
people and their national government led by Goukouni Oueddei 
has been started. The head of the Egyptian regime Mubarak and 
his stooge Numayri are leading this new plot in lieu of the U.S. 
imperialism to destablise Chad so as to maintain areas of tension 
and flash points. Within these reactionary movements, Mubarak 
received the envoy of the Zairian president in Alexandria who 
delivered a message from President Mobutu of Zaire who 
restored close relations with Israel. REUTER news agency said 
the Egyptian regime started to participate increasingly in back- 
ing Hissein Habre. It was noted that Mubarak gave a particular 
importance to the Zairian envoy’s visit to show Egypt’s observa- 
tion of O.A.U. resolutions, but in the same time indicators show 
that the head of the Egyptian regime is preparing to intervene in 
Chad in collaboration and coordination with his friends Numayri 
and Mobutu Sese Seko. 


JANA: CIA Responsible for 


Chad Regime 
LD291702 Tripoli JANA in English 1436 GMT 29 Jun 83 


[Text] Washington, Ramadan 19, June 29, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The American television network CBS 
held the CIA responsible for the overthrow of President Gou- 
kouni Oueddei, head of the Provisional National Unity Govern- 
ment in Chad, by a plot carried out last year. 


In a long report last night, it said that the C.I.A. involvement in 
the plot against President Goukouni was revealed in a secret 
testimony given in Washington in closed door briefings to the 
Intellegence Committee of the House of Representatives. It 
pointed out that the U.S.A. has spent 10 million dollars to carry 
out this conspiracy against Goukouni government. 


This information is considered very grave as it reveals the extent 
of U.S. involvement in Chad and hot pursuit to destroy the unity 


of Chad and impose a bunch of criminals to implement its hostile 
plots against the Jamahiriyah and other progressive African 
countries. 


The information also indicates the continuation of American 
conspiracy against the national legitimate government which was 
recognized by the Organization of African Unity and Lagos 
resolutions. The conspiracy is not aimed at President Goukouni 
Queddei alone but also at the African countries which reject U.S. 
hegemony in the area. 


Current events confirm that the U.S. intelligence sees that stooge 
Numayri and President Mubarak are in their perfect places in 
line with U.S. stand which is aimed to destabilise Chad and back 
gangs led by Hissein Habre. 


UN Representative Refutes Habre Allegations 
LD291701 Tripoli JANA in English 1445 GMT 29 Jun 83 


[Text] New York, Ramadan 19, June 29, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Jamahiriyah’s permanent representative 
at the U.N. has addressed a letter to the president of the Security 
Council refuting allegations made by rebel Habre in his letter to 
the council. 


The representative referred, in his letter dated 24 June and 
registered in document Number 15843, to the internal situation 
in Chad and to other letters drawing the attention of the Security 
Council, the General Assembly and international public opinion 
to repeated allegations spread by Hissein Habre, the representa- 
tive of the so-called northern army backed by a power hostile to 
the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and aimed at 
harming Jamahiriyah’s reputation and dragging it into problems 
she is not involved in. 


He categorically denied all allegations made by Habre. He said, 
“As a neighboring country of Chad, the Libyan Jamahiriyah is 
keener than any other country on attaining security and stability 
in Chad. She affirmed this through direct action within the 
framework of sisterly neighboring countries’ endeavors or 
through regional and international organizations”. 


He added, “Current events in Chad are the direct result of a 
rebellion by only one of the eleven sides, which signed the Lagos 
agreement, against the legitimate government that sprang from 
that agreement and is recognized by O.A.U. The conflict in Chad 
today is between Chadians and on Chadian territories”. 


He stressed, “A resolution of the Chadian problem could not be 
achieved by covering up events in Chad from the world’s atten- 
tion or through the spreading of lies and allegations and accusing 
some countries neighboring Chad. The solution could, however, 
be realized through the attainment of national reconciliation 
among all Chadians, regardless of their beliefs or regions...” 


The Jamahiriyah is fully ready to work alongside sisterly African 
countries to realize such a national reconciliation and resolve the 
Chadian problem on the basis of the Lagos agreement's princi- 
ples.

Djibouti Eavoy 
LD300110 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2315 GMT 29 Jun 83 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution received a message 
from President Hassan Gouled, president of the republic of 
Djibouti, in which he pointed out the dangers which surround the 
Arab nation, stressing that the current situation can lead to a 
disaster for the Arabs, and history and the next generations will 
never forgive us any complacency in combating these dangers. 


“Because of your strong will and insistence on unifying the Arab 
action, through your important and noble role in the Arab arena, 
I ask you, dear brother, to make every effort to stabilize the 
situation of our Palestinian brothers in Al-Biqa’ in order to lead 
this situation to a satisfactory result, in order to avoid a war 
between the brothers.” 


President Hassan Gouled expressed his trust and confidence in 
the leading role which the brother leader of the revolution plays 
for the good of the Arab nation and the Islamic world.
