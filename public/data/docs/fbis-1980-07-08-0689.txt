LIBYA

ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS SECRETARIAT ISSUES STATEMENT 


LD040852 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 3 Jul 80 


[Text] Thursday, The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress ended the 
meetings of its fourth regular session, held 1-3 July in Tripoli. The secretariat has 
issued a political statement, a number of resolutions and the draft charter of the 
pan-Arab Progressive Popular Front [Al-Jabhah al-Sha'biyah al-Qawmiyah al-Taqaddumiyah]. 


The political statement says that the Permanent Secretariat has reviewed political 
conditions in the Arab area and has noted developments in the political situation. 
The most important of these developments are the following: 


The Camp David alliance, having failed to impose autonomous rule over our Arab Palestinian 
people in the ocuppied territory, resorted to new offensive tactics, indicated by the 
following: 


A. The continued racist colonization campaigns and the waging of a criminal terrorist 
campaign against the masses of the occupied territory and their national vanguards. 

The attempts on the lives of strugglers Bassam al-Shak'ah and Karim Khalaf, after the 
deportation of strugglers Muhammad Milhim and Fahd al-Qawasimah and Shayh Rajab al-Tamimi, 
were the climax of the Zionist crime to force the Palestinian Arab people in the occupied 
territory to accept false autonomous rule. 


Be. The escalation of military operations against Lebanon, particularly southern Lebanon, 
within the framework of a continuous war of attrition. 


Ce. The intensification of sabotage against Syria in an attempt to break up its domestic 
front and bring it to its knees. 


De The preparation for military aggression by the As-Sadat regime against the Jamahiriyah, 
with a view to paralyzing the pan-Arab support which the Jamahiriyah provides to the 
front of confrontation against the Zionist enemy. 


The statement adds: The United States is exerting concerted efforts, directly and 
through Western Europe and Romania, to put forward alternative plans for the Camp 
David policy which do not conflict in essence with the aims of Camp David, with a view 
to drawing other Arab quarters into the capitulation talks. 


The statement adds that the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Gongress has 
noticed that some Arab countries and some responsible Arab officials have made serious 
statements which meet in practice with the American plan to transform Camp David 

from an Egyptian Camp David into an Arab Camp David. 


The statement of the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress asks the Baghdad 
summit member countries to abide by the resolutions providing for the boycott of the 
Egyptian regime and rejection of the Camp David policy, otherwise the Permanent 
Secretariat will expose to the eyes of the Arab masses the attitudes of the countries 
which have departed from the Baghdad summit resolutions.

The statement affirms that the ruling regime in Egypt is practicing a still graver policy, 
with a view to deepening Egypt's isolation from its Arab nation and to turning it into a 
base for American imperialism, either by granting it military bases and facilities, by 
taking joint stands with it or by joint action against the Iranian revolution or by 
threatening Ethiopia or by preparing for new aggression against the Jamahiriyah. At the 
same time it works for shattering the unity of the Egyptian Arab people by creating 
sectarian sedition and by suppression and by the promulgation of further suppressive laws 
and measures, the last of which were the law of shame and the law of suspicion, with a 
view to striking at the struggle by great Egyptian people. 


The statement says that the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress, while 
calling on the Arab masses and their progressive and national forces to show greater 
vigilance and to exert greater struggle against the new offensive by the American-Zionist- 
reactionary alliance, notes with pride and appreciation the struggle of our Arab masses 

in all the arenas of our greater homeland, 


The statement adds that the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress greets the 
struggle of the Palestinian Arab people led by the PLO, the sole legitimate representative 
of the Palestine people inside and outside the occupied territory--that struggle which 
constitutes a formidable wall in the face of the Camp David scheme. It also greets the 
struggle of the Lebanese Arab people led by their national movement--the Lebanese people 
who are steadfastly shouldering the cost of the pan-Arab battle on their soil and offering 
the blood of their martyrs and the blood of the martyrs of the Palestine revolution, 


The Permanent Secretariat also greets the struggle of Egyptian Arab people led by ti.cw 
national forces, foremost of which are the national progressive unionist grouping and the 
Egyptian National Front--that struggle which is broadening resistance to the regime of 
agent As-Sadat and which is asserting its pan-Arab character and its loyalty to the 
objectives of the pan-Arab liberation struggle. 


The secretariat also greets the struggle of Arab revolutionaries and fighters in all the 
Arab countries--revolutionaries and fighters for democracy, the freeing of political and 
trade unionist prisoners and detainees. 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress calls on the Arab masses to 
confront the enemy alliance with a practical mass program embodying the following points: 


A. Observance of 23 July as a pan-Arab day of Arab wrath and peaceful march against the 
U.S. and Egyptian regime embassies abroad for the submission of notes of protest and 
denunciation, 


B. Starting a popular boycott of American goods, 


C. Launching a worldwide campaign against Zionist crimes against our Palestinian Arab 
people in the occupied territory and against southern Lebanon. 


D. Bolstering the Palestinian revolution militarily and materially and opening the 
borders before them to face the enemy, as well as bolstering the steadfastness of the masses 
of the occupied territory. 


E. Bolstering Syria's steadfastness and showing solidarity with it against the plots and 
sabotage, exposing the saboteurs and paralyzing those Arab quarters which feed the plots 
against Syria.

F. Implementing the world program of solidarity with the Lebanese Arab people and 
their national movement. 


Ge Bolstering and supporting the pan-Arab Front of Steadfastcness and Confrontation 
and the assertion of solidarity with the national regime in Algeria against the 
Moroccan provocations and imperialist plots, and also with the PDRY against American 
and reactionary pressures and threats. 


He. Expressing solidarity with the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah against 
aggression by the As-Sadat regime and transforming the battle from one by the 
Jamahiriyah against the agent As-Sadat regime into one of pan-Arab comprehensive battle, 
and asking the Arab League, the international organizations, the OAU, the nonalign- 
ment countries and the Islamic Conference to shoulder their responsibilities. 


I. The struggle for securing political and trade unionist freedom and also securing 
the release of Arab strugglers in Arab prisoners and detention camps. 


Je Bolstering and supporting the Saharan people's struggle, led by Polisario, against 
the war of annihilation waged by the royalist Moroccan regime, and also the Eritrean 
people's struggle for self-determination within the framework of the unity of the 
African Horn against reaction and imperialism. 


K. Bolstering the Arab people's struggle in the Arabian Peninsula and the Arab Gulf 
against imperialism and reaction and for complete independence and democracy and the 
enjoyment of natural wealth, and the liquidation of imperialist military bases and 
alliances. 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress condemns the American-Qabus 
agreement which turned Oman into an American base threatening the Arab nation and the 
neighboring peoples. It asks the Arab countries to secure the expulsion of the Qabus 
regime from the Arab League and the cancellation of its membership because in its 
national andpan-Arab betrayal, it does not differ from that of the AseSadat regime. 
It also calls on them to increase support for the Popular Front for the Liberation 
of Oman so that it may topple the traitorous regime.
