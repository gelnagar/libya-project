{Unattributed article: “The Army: A Revolutionary Establish- 
ment”’] 


[Text] Self criticism and self appraisal are fundamental to 
revolutionary action. They serve as the basis of all creative 
revolutionary work and therefore no one can become a god who 
is above criticism 


Organisations which have failed to observe this revolutionary 
rule within their own ranks have become dull or rigid bureau- 
cratic establishments because they lacked one of the most impor- 
tant elements necessary for their development 


This rule occupies a special place among the rules of the 
Revolution in mass ideological thinking. Therefore, the leader- 
ship of the Revolution continues to incite and urge the rev- 
olutionary force to practise that rule in a courageous and 
responsible way, so as to provoke thought and thereby increase 
the awareness of the masses. 


Two weeks ago I read an article in AL-ZAHAF AL-AKHDAR 
entitled “The Army: Hashish and Foolishness,” which was writ- 
ten by our young men. Mass thinking perceives an ultimate vision 
of information for the masses in the form of wall posters for which 
no one could write an article such as that published in AL- 
ZAHAF AL-AKHDAR because representation is alien to the 


ideology of the masses and because no one can express another's. 


opinion, with the result that an article would only reflect the ideas 
of its author 


Mass Information 


Despite the exceptional stage reached by mass information, | 
found it necessary to clearly point this out so that there can be 
no misunderstanding about the true nature of mass information. 
The author of this article committed a great polemical mistake 
by demanding that the Army as an establishment be destroyed
