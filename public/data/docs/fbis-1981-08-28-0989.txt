JOINT LIBYA-PDRY-ETHIOPIA COMMUNIQUE ON ADEN TALKS 


LD280255 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 27 Aug 81 


[Joint communique issued on 27 August in Tripoli, Aden and Addis Ababa on the 16-19 August 
summit meeting held in Aden by Libya's Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the PDRY's ‘Ali Nasir 
Muhammad and Ethiopia's Mengistu Haile Mariam] 


[Text] Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, commander of the Great 1 September Revolution of 

the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah [SPLAJ]; ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, secretary 

general of the YSP Central Committee, chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme People's 
Council and prime minister of the PDRY; and Mengistu Haile Mariam, chairman of the provisional 
Military Administrative Council [PMAC] and the Commission for Organizing the Party of the 
Workers of Ethiopia [COPWE] and commander of the revolutionary army of socialist Fthiopia, 
held a summit conference in Aden. 


In an atmosphere of cordiality and mutual understanding, the leaders of the three countries 
held expanded official talks covering relations of brotherhood and struggle among the 

three countries. They also reviewed political developments in the Arab, African and 
international arenas. 


On reviewing the relations of brotherhood and struggle among their countries, the three 
leaders expressed their utmost pelasure at the high level of cooperation among the three 
states and affirmed the need to strengthen and develop it in all fields for the good 

of the common interests of their countries and in order to raise the material and spiritual 
levels of their peoples, strengthen the revolutionary cohesion of their three countries and 
advance several steps forward the heroic struggle against imperialism, Zionism, racism 

and reaction. 


Out of their deep faith in the importance of developing and strengthening relations among 

the three countries and out of their realization of the need to unify their stands against 
imperialism, Zionism, reaction and racism and to bolster solidarity and cooperation 

in all fields, the leaders of the SPLAJ, the PDRY and socialist Ethiopia agreed to sign 

a treaty of friendship and cooperation among their three countries with a view to coordinating 
their relations in the various political, economic and other fields and to setp up a 

higher council of the leaders of the three countries and ministerial committees to follow 

up the implementation of the provisions of the treaty of friendship and cooperation. 


A number of agreements were signed within the framework of developing economic and 
technical cooperation among the three countries. 


During their stay, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and Mengistu Haile Mariam acquainted themselves 
with some of the historic landmarks and economic and cultural achievements of the PDRY. 

They expressed their utmost admiration for the great accomplishment of the Yemeni people 
under the leadership of the YSP. They also expressed their appreciation for the 

efforts exerted by the workers of the PDRY to consolidate and develop these achievements 

and defend them.

The two cides--Libya and Ethiopia--condemned all the plots hatched by the imperialist and 
reactionary circles, denounced Oman's military provocations and aggression against the PDRY 
and declared their firm stand on the side of the Yemeni people and their support for their 
just struggle to safeguard their national sovereignty and build a new life. 


The two sides--Libya and Ethiopia--supported the efforts exerted by the PDRY for the reuni- 
fication of the two parts of Yemen by peaceful and democratic means. They praised the 
positive steps already taken in this regard. They affirmed that the realization of Yemeni 
unity constitutes an important factor for the realization of the ambitions and aspirations 
of the Yemeni people for development and progress and an effective factor in guaranteeing 
the stability and security of the region. 


The two sides--Libya and the PDRY--praised the political, economic and social accomplishments 
of the Ethiopian revolution under the revolutionary leadership of Chairman Mengistu Haile 
Mariam, chairman of the PMAC and of the COPWE and commander in chief of the revolutionary 
army of socialist Ethiopia. They also expressed their support for the sound policy pursued 
by socialist Ethiopia to rid itself of the repressive feudalist system. They affirmed their 
stand on the side of the Ethiopian people in their struggle to consolidate their revolution, 
safeguard their sovereignty and protect their territorial integrity and insure its safety. 
They condemned all the plots hatched by the imperialist and reactionary forces with a view 
to detracting from the revolutionary achievements of the Ethiopian people. 


The Ethiopian and Yemeni sides expressed their deep appreciation for the achievements of the 
Great Al-Fatah Revolution in the political, economic, social and cultural spheres. They 
condemned the aggressive campaigns launched by the imperialist and reactionary circles which 
are aimed at obstructing and slandering the march of the Libyan people and their historic 
and revolutionary leadership. They announced their total support for the SPLAJ in its 
struggle to foil all imperialist, Zionist and reactionary conspiracies, especially those 
hatched by the defeatist regime ‘in Egypt and its reactionary allies. 


The three leaders condemned the aggressive policy purused by the United States against the 
progressive regimes and liberation forces in the world and its conspiracies to physically 
liquidate their leaders. The leaders strongly condemned the recently revealed U.S. plot 
on the life of the leader of the Great al-Fatah Revolution. 


Reviewing the situations in the Arabian Peninsula and the Gulf and the Indian Ocean, the 
three leaders expressed concern about the latest crucial developments in those areas brought 
about by the escalation by the imperialist circles' activities aimed at consolidating and 
expanding their influence in these areas in order to control their wealth and harm the 
sovereignty, independence, security and unity of the countries of the regions. The leaders 
of the three countries condemned the aggressive policy of U.S. imperialism manifested by 

the presence of military bases and facilities in Oman, Somalia, Egypt and Diego Garcia 

and by the intensification of the military posture in these regions. They also expressed 
their condemnation of the U.S. imperialist policy aimed at escalating tension in the regions 
and transforming them into huge warehouses forthe products of the U.S. imperialist military 
monopolies. 


The leaders of the three countries supported the efforts made to end the war between Iraq 
and Iran. They emphasized that the continuation of the war would severly harm the capabil- 
ities of Iraq and Iran, greatly endanger the security and stability of the region and give 
imperialism an opportunity to increase its military presence in the area. 


The three leaders expressed their concern about [word indistinct] and the spread of U.S. 
fleets in the Mediterranean, the Red Sea and the Indian Ocean to frighten the peoples and 
countries of the regions and threaten their independence and security and world peace.

They supported the efforts made by the international community to make the Indian Ocean, 
the Red Sea and the Mediterranean regions zones of peace and security. 


Reviewing the development of the situations in the Arab region, they reiterated their 

total rejection of the stable [Camp] David capitulatory agreements and all initiatives aimed 
at violating the established rights of the Palestinian people. They condemned the Israeli 
military attacks on the Lebanese and Palestinian peoples aimed at liquidating the 
Palestinian revolution and dividing Lebanon. They praised the period steadfastness of 

the Palestinian resistance and the Lebanese National Movement. They condemned the United 
States for its unlimited imperialist support for the Zionist entity and for (?supplying) 

it with the latest military equipment to escalate the aggression against the Arab 

countries and peoples. 


The leaders of the three countries stressed their total support for the just struggle of 
the Palestinian people under the leadership of the PLO, the legitimate and sole represen- 
tative of the Palestinian people, to establish an independent state on their territory and 
exercise their right to return to their homeland. 


The leaders of the three countries stated their support for the struggle of the Lebanese 
people and their national movement for the unity, national independence, and Arabism 
of Lebanon. 


They praised the heroic steadfastness of the Syrian people in confronting the imperialist 
and Zionist plans and repulsing the Zionist threats and U.S. provocations. 


They praised the distinguished role played by the Confrontation and Steadfastness Front 
in the Arab struggle to confront the stable David capitulatory agreements. 


In discussing the situation on the African Continent, they praised the proposals reached 
by the African summit held in Nairobi as a correct basis for facing developments on the 
continent. They reiterated their support for the proposals of the OAU made at the Nairobi 
summit concerning the dispute in the Horn of Africa and condemned the expansionist and 
imperialist U.S. conspiracies against the Ethiopian revolution and the unity of its 
territories--plots which are aimed at escalating tension in the region by establishing 
aggressive military bases in Somali. 


The leaders of the three countries declared their support for the legitimate government in 
Chad under the leadership of soukouni Oueddei and welcomed the (?establishment) of peace 
and stability in Chad. They praised the Libyan role in ending the bloodletting to which 
the Chadian people were exposed as a result of the activities of reactionary and imperial- 
ist conspirators and their lackeys in Chad. They also condemned the conspiracies aimed 

at dividing the Chadian people and obstructing their social progress. 


They supported the struggle of the Saharan people, under the leadership of Polisario, for 
self-determination and called for direct negotiations between the Saharan Democratic 
Arab Republic and Morocco. 


The three leaders saluted the heroic independence struggle of the Namibian people under 

the leadership of the SWAPO. They also condemned the attempts made by imperialism to 
divide and weaken Africa by instigating conflict over the South Africa/Namibia issue. 

They condemned the role played by the so-called contact group comprised of five Western 
states in obstructing the Security Council and the UN General Assembly, especially Security 
Council resolution [figure indistinct] (?condemning) the racist occupation of Namibia. 


They appealed to the next special session of the UN General Assembly on Namibia to take 
active steps to ensure the speedy implementation of the UN proposals and to impose 
sanctions on the racist regime in South Africa.

They condemned the racist repressive policy of the South African regime, its repeated 
military attacks on Angola, Mozambique and the other independent states in the region 
and its continued occupation of Namibia. They also strongly supported the struggle of 
the people of Soutn Africa to topple the apartheid regime. 


The three leaders affirmed the utmost importance of the solidarity and cohesion of the 
progressive regimes and forces in the Arab region and Africa in routing the imperialist, 
Zionist, reactionary and racist plots and designs against the region's peoples. 


Reviewing the conditions in Latin America, they condemend the American imperialist policy, 
which supports the fascist and dictatorial regimes. They also declared their support for 
the heroic struggle of the national liberation movements in Latin America. They declared 
their firm support for the revolutions in Cuba, Nicaragua and Grenada. 


Dealing with the development of conditions in Asia, they declared their support for the 
Kampuchean and Afghan revolutions and denounced the imperialist and reactionary inter- 
ference in their internal affairs. 


Reviewing the international situation, they affirmed their full support for the important 
positive role played by the Nonaligned Movement in the struggle to safeguard international 
peace and security and appreciation for its support of the struggle of the peoples 
fighting for liberation and progress. They considered nonalignment an important element 
for the stability of the international situation. 


The leaders of the three countries noted with concern the complications in the international 
situation caused by the growing imperialist activity aimed at escalating tension and doing 
away with peaceful coexistence and international detente by the production of the neutron 
bomb and the setting up of a missile network in Europe and by the escalation of the arms 
race, the establishment and expansion of military bases and the exercise of an aggressive 
terrorist policy against the peoples. 


They declared their condemnation of the grave imperialist policy which expose international 
peace and security to great danger. 


They also declared their support for the efforts exerted by the peace-loving forces, 
particularly the various initiatives made by the USSR, in the Gulf, the Indian Ocean and 
Europe with a view to ending tension, halting the arms race and securing peaceful coexistence 
and international detente. 


The leaders of the three countries declared their appreciation for the firm principled 
stands of the socialist countries which support the struggle of peoples seeki g liberation 
and progress, in particular the struggles of the Palestinian people and the peoples of 
Namibia and South Africa. 


They also affirmed the importance of deepening and developing strategic relations with 
the countries of the socialist bloc and the progressive countries and forces in the world. 


The leaders of the three countries condemned the attempts by the industrial capitalist 
countries to impose economic hegemony over the developing countries with a view to 
frustrating the development plans of these countries and preventing them from achieving 
any economic revival for their peoples and from obtaining fair prices for their national 
products. In this regard, they affirmed the importance of a new world economic system 
based on economic justice for all peoples, thereby ensuring security, welfare and social 
progress and guaranteeing balanced economic and social growth all countries. 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and Chairman Mengistu Haile Mariam expressed their deep gratitude 
to the leadership and people of the PDRY for the warm fraternal hospitality and welcome 
they accorded them and their delegations during their stay in beautiful Aden.

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES CABLES ON U.S. ‘AGGRESSION’ 


Soviet Trade Union Cable 


LD271946 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1910 GMT 27 Aug 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 27 Aug (JANA)--The All-Union Central Council of Trade Unions [AUCCTU] of 
the USSR denounced, in the name of the millions of Soviet workers, the U.S. imperialist 
aggression against the people of the Jamahiriyah and condemned the imperialist-reactionary 
attempts to halt the progressive revolutionary tide of the Great 1 September Revolution. 


In a cable to the brother commander today, the AUCCTU affirmed the solidarity of all Soviet 

workers with the Jamahiriyah people and trade and vocational unions against all American 

imperialist designs and the lackeys of American imperialism: Egypt, Sudan and Somalia. 
SWAPO Cable 

LD272138 Tripoli JANA in English 1916 GMT 27 Aug 81 

[Text] Tripoli, 27 Aug 81 (JANA)--The South West Africa People's Organization (SWAPO) has 

condemned the American imperialist aggression on Jamahiriyah people. This came in a cable 

sent by Sam Nujoma, SWAPO's president, to the leader. Following is its full text: 

Dear Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Libyan revolution: 

The South West Africa People's Organization (SWAPO) of Namibia has learnt of the unjusti- 

fiable aggression by American imperialism against the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 

Jamahiriyah's Air Force, which is carrying its duties within the airspace and territorial 

waters of the Jamahiriyah. 

The African liberation movements strongly condemn the American imperialist vile aggression 

against the peace loving Jamahiriyah. Let the Jamahiriyah people rest assured of SWAPO's and 

the Namibian people's total support for its struggle to defend the safety of its airspace 

and territories. 

Dear Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, please accept our absolute revolutionary support". 

[Signed] Sam Nujoma, president of SWAPO

AL-QADHDHAFI CABLES SUPPORT TO ANGOLAN LEADER 


LD271554 Tripoli JANA in English 0945 GMT 27 Aug 81 


[Text ] Tripoli, 27 Aug (JANA)--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of 

the Great al-Fatah Revolution, yesterday sent a message to President Jose Eduardo dos 
Santos of the People's Republic of Angola denouncing the racist and fascist aggression of 
South African troops against Angola and its friendly people. The message reads as follows: 


Dear Friend Jose Eduardo dos Santos, president of the People's Republic of Angola: 
We heard the news of the repeated barbaric and terrorist attacks by the racists of South 
Africa backed by U.S. imperialism and international Zionism against your friendly people 
and country. We strongly condemn these barbaric attacks and re-pledge [word indistinct] of 
the Libyan Arab people with all their resources for your friendly people and government and 
they are ready to fight on your side to push back the imperialist racist aggressor. 
Finally, I greet the steadfastness and courage of the militant Angolan people through their 
legitimate defence of their independence, the sovereignty of their territories and support 
for the heroic Namibian people struggling against opperssion by the racist and imperialist 
forces and for their self-determination. 
Please accept my best wishes for you personally and your friendly people. 
[Signed] Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi
