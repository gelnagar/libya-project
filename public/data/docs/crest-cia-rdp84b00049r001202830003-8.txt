Summary (AI): A 1982 CIA National Intelligence Council memo on rising Libyan military activity in the Aozou Strip of northern Chad, including recruitment of Chadians in Nigeria and training at Sabha in southern Libya. Nothing about Tripoli itself.
• A campaign of harassment of Habre's forces by Chadians trained in Libya was planned, with no evidence yet of a large-scale attack using Libyan forces.
• The memo is from the National Intelligence Officer for Africa, NIC #8867-82, dated 2 November 1982.
• 1982-11: Libya recruiting Chadians in Maiduguri, shipping them via Lagos and Cotonou to Sabha for training before return to northern Chad.
