LIBYA

PDRY PRESIDENT ARRIVES, MAKES STATEMENT 


LDO51938 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1830 GMT 5 Sep 82 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 5 Sep (JANA) -- Brother ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, president of the PDRY, 
arrived in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah this evening. The brother leader 
of the revolution welcomed Brother ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad and his accompanying delegation. 


Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in 
chief of the armed forces, took part in welcoming the PDRY president. 


V. 8 Sep 82 Q 2 NORTH AFRICA 


The Yemeni president told JANA his visit to the Jamahiriyah comes within the scope of con- 
tinuous meetings with the brothers in the Jamahiriyah for deliberations and consultations 
on pan-Arab destiny and of means of bolstering the solid fraternal relations between our 
two peoples and countries. 


At the end of his statement, he expressed confidence that the talks in the Jamahiriyah will 
bolster cooperation in supporting the struggle of our Arab nation, which has been subjected 
to the most ferocious imperialist, Zionist and reactionary attack.

[Text] Tripoli, Dhulqidah 17, September 5, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The revolution's 
leader and supreme commander of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, Colonel Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, yesterday evening attended the graduation ceremony of a new batch of students 
from the [word indistinct] defence college [word indistinct] popular celebrations marking 
the 13th anniversary of the Great Al Fateh Revolution. 


The graduation was also attended by the general commander of the Libyan Armed Forces, 
Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, the chief of staff, Brigadier Mustafa al-Kharrubi, the air 
force, army, navy and air defense chiefs of staff and a large gathering of citizens. 


The ceremony was inaugurated by readings from the Holy Koran [words indistinct] the air 
defence school commander. In it he said [words indistinct] the present and previous [word 
indistinct] make the realisation of an armed society a [word indistinct] reality. He said 
these graduates will defend the gains made by the Libyan people in all fields under the 
Great Al Fateh Revolution. 


The graduated students then held a [word indistinct] in which the traditional handing over 
of [words indistinct] between graduating and other students took place, after which the 
revolution's leader and supreme commander of the armed forces presented diplomas to the top 
ten graduating students. 


Citizens and students present at the ceremony chanted slogans affirming their solidarity 
with the revolution's leader, stressed their commitment to the defence of the Great 

Al Fateh Revolution and its giant accomplishments and pledged to challenge all imperialist 
Zionist and reactionary conspiracies that attempt to rob the Arab nation of its freedom. 


JALLUD ADDRESSES 'PRODUCERS' 3 SEPTEMBER 


LD041016 Tripoli JANA in English 0805 GMT 4 Sep 82 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, Dhulqidah 16, September 4, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Staff Major 
"Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud yesterday night met with the producers in the sector of produc- 
tion, economy and services. He told them the producers have the full rights to celebrate 
the Great Al Fateh day, for it means to them the final emancipation and the end of wrath 
and oppression. 


He added: "The lst September 69 Revolution led to the 1978 producers revolution which 
brought about political, material and moral achievement for the producers who became 
partners instead of wage workers. This revolution put an end to the bureaucratic adminis- 
tration in the public sector and the exploitation in the private sector”. 


Staff Major Jallud said that the revolutionary command relies a great deal on the masses 
of the producers for inciting Arab workers to achieve victory and join the producers in 
the Jamahiriyah so as they all shoulder the responsibility for stopping the Zionist death 
train and defeating it and realize victory for millions of workers in the Arab homeland, 
because their victory will bring about that of the Arab nation.
