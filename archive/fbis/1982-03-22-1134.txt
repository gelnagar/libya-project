LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI, JANA COMMENT ON SAUDI OIL CUTBACK 


Al-Qadhdhafi Statement 
LD220106 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2300 GMT 21 Mar 82 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution made a statement to JANA yesterday following 
the Saudi rulers' claims of having decided to lower their oil production to 7 million 
barrels per day. In his statement the brother leader explained that the Saudi family did 
not put forward any positive action at the Vienna meeting which proved their complete sub- 
servience to the orders issued by Washington and Tel Aviv. 


The leader stressed there is no justification for producing more than 7 million barrels per 
day except for the need cf the U.S. [word indistinct] for supplying the Israelis with the 
latest means of destruction directed at destroying the Arabs and Muslims. He said the un- 
real and impudent personal expenditure of the Saudi family -- even if it surpassed phenomenal 
limits -- could not exceed the income from 2 million barrels per day. The leader said, at 
the end of his statement to JANA, that the world, except for the Americans and the Israelis 
is asking questions in astonishment about the income from the other 5 million. 


JANA Commentator 
LD202312 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 20 Mar 82 


[Text] The Saudi oil minister announced today that Saudi Arabia will cut its oil production 
from 7.5 million to 7 million barrels per day as of 1 April. 


Commenting on the announcement, the JANA political commentator says: Saudi Arabia has 
definitely lost its credibility as lies have become adailymeans by which to distract atten- 
tion from its efforts to serve U.S. strategy and to bolster the oil stockpiling of the U.S. 
administration. This new announcement is another lie that Saudi Arabia has added to its 
series of lies about the so-called cutback. The announcement is not true at all. The 

Saudi princes are selling oil privately to increase their personal incomes. 


The political editor explains that the Saudi decision to increase production to the current 
level was in response to U.S. demands and is indicative of Saudi subservience to the United 
States. Saudi exports exceeded the currently declared figures. 


TRIPOLI RADIO CRITICIZES SAUDI COUNCIL OF ULEMA 


LD202010 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 20 Mar 82 


[Text] Saudi Arabia's so-called Council of Senior Ulema issued a statement attacking the 
leader of the revolution, discrediting the correctness of the faith and beliefs of the 

Libyan people and using a common style and vulgar expressions in an attack on the Jamahiriyah 
and its people. The council demonstrates to the world that it is a despicable instrument 
used to strike at the unity of Muslims and to achieve the aims of imperialism and reaction 
and that it hides under the veil of Islam and the call to carry the banner of Islam. 


This body of imposter ulema in Saudi Arabia consists of a bunch of adulating functionaries 
and corrupt and corrupting elements uttering falsehoods and engaging in debauchery, cowardly 
elements uttering falsehoods and engaging in debauchery, cowardly covering up crimes of 
morality, dishonor and depravity, practicing abominations and turning a blind eye to homo- 
sexual orgies and the debauchery of theprinces who drink their goblets full of wine in the 
palace corridors.

What comes from the mouths of those adulating functionaries is inspired by and on the 
orders of their masters, who pay and manipulate them to make the "haram" [what is 
prohibited] into "halal" {what is allowed] and vice versa. That is the trait of 
those atheists and receivers of bribes who trade in the almighty. They have become 
instruments for aborting the hopes of the people and corrupting their dreams. They 
decorate what is false for the devils of the palaces and work for other than the 
religion of God and the glory of His messenger. 


Where are Islam and Muslims in this body of senior Islamic ulema, this group that keeps 
quiet in face of the facts and does not utter a word or take any stand that could be 
construed as meritorious, only stands of hypocrisy, deception and fraud. 


Where was that bunch of adulating functionaries when the land of Islam was burning? 
How far away are those clowns, who are so enthusiastic about satisfying the sultans, 
from the fundamental issues for which our Arab nation and our Islamic world is struggling? 


We know those cowards with their trivial abilities and empty education. We know they 
trade in religion and sell licenses to practice what is prohibited to the Saudi ruler 
whose abominations, lies and betrayal of Islam and Muslims abound throughout history. 


In case that bunch of quacks has forgotten, we remind them of what exists and what is 
real. Their stand is cowardly and insignificant. We condemn their deliberate silence 
regarding the disgusting crimes committed by the debauched Saudi princes against the Arab 
people living on the Arabian Peninsula under the force of tyranny, injustice and the most 
severe kind of despotism. Why do those exhalted ulema keep silent? Why don't they say 
what is allowed is obvious and that what is prohibited also is obvious? Why don't they 
expose what is happening in the palaces of the Sa'ud family and condemn that family's 
allegiance to the United States and Israel? They know the real answer. They know they 
are merely slaves and a corrupt clique, filthy instruments, sinful subnormals, a 
conniving, feeble gang and a flock of lost ostriches drowned in their cowardice. Shame 

on those who kiss dirty hands and smelly noses and woe unto those laying themselves at 

the doorsteps of the rulers and clamoring for the tyranny of those who idolize themselves. 
To hell with the ulema who have sold their conscience, treaded on their beliefs, corrupted 
themselves, sold what was left of their honor and stained what was left of their dignity. 


There is nothing to link those corrupt on earth with Islam. They have no religion. They 
try to incite our people and the leader of our revolution because they have become a part 
of the mob and common people, bewitched by the dinar and the dollar. They are the apostles 
of blasphemy and the instigators against Islam. Our Arab nation must expose those trivial 
people haunting the palaces' wine cellars, singing the praise of the tyrants, and the 
ignoramuses of the lost and debauched Sa'ud family, a family disowned by the Arab nation 
and the Islamic world because it is God's enemy and the enemy of this great nation.
