JANA: TUNISIAN STUDENTS PROTEST AGAINST GOVERNMENT 
LD181012 Tripoli JANA in English 0832 GMT 18 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Tunis, February 18, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Tunisian suppressive forces 
and militia continue their siege of the university precincts in Tunis. The students 
had staged a sit-in two days ago inside the university compound, announced a strike 
and chanted slogans of support hailing the grand al-Fatah revolution and the downfall 
of the ruling idols propped up by imperialism to control the capabilities of the 
Tunisian peoples, According to news from the protectorate, study has not returned to 
normal in the schools in spite of the allegations of the mass media to the contrary. 


Students Continue Strike 
LD201054 Tripoli JANA in English 0945 GMT 20 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Paris, February 20, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--It is reported that the students! 
strike in Tunisian universities and colleges is still going on despite the repressive 
campaign launched against them by the Tunisian protectorate's regime, The involvement 
of the repressive forces led to violent clashes with the students protesting against 
the French invasion of Tunisia, One law student was killed in these clashes last 
Monday. 


Purthermore, the students at Qafsah colleges organized a demonstration last Saturday 
against the French invasion, which involved the participation of local people, The 
demonstration which became impressive frightened the French and Tunisian forces which 
have been there since the outbreak of the Tunisian popular uprising, 


JANA: ‘BLOODY CLASHES! IN TUNISIAN CAPITAL 15 FEB 

LD201536 Tripoli JANA in English 1409 GMT 20 Feb 80 LD 

[Text] Paris, February 20, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Bloody clashes took place in the 
Tunisian capital last Friday between the regime's national guards and a group of 
militants intending to occupy the TV and radio stations. In the face of this con- 
centrated attack, the authorities were forced to call for more military reinforcements 
from the city of Quranbaliyah, 

BULGARIAN FOREIGN TRADE MINISTER ARRIVES 20 FEB 

LD201810 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 20 Feb 80 LD 


[Excerpts] Tripoli--Bulgarian Foreign Trade Minister Khristo Khristov arrived in 
Tripoli at noon today for a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


In a statement to JANA the guest expressed his happiness and that of the accompanying 
delegation at being in Libya, pointing out that the visit falls within the framework 

of consolidating mutual cooperation between the two peoples who stand firmly against 

all colonialist attempts in the world.
