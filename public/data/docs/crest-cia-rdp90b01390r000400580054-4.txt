MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD 

SUBJECT: Briefing for SSCI Staffers on Middle East Terrorism 
and Libya 

On 3 January at 1530 hours in Room 7&32 Headquarters, SSCI 
staffers Jim Dykstra, Carmen Battaglia, and Bruce Berkowitz 
were briefed by representatives of the DI/NESA and DI/OGI under 
the chairmanship of the National Intelligence Officer for Near 
East South Asia, Graham Fuller. | Liaison 25X1 
Division, OLL, attended the brie ing. The briefing consisted 
largely of a current situation report regarding the recent 
bombings in the Rome and Vienna airports. The briefers 
described the Abu Nidal group and its past history. They also 
discussed Libya, its likely reactions to various Us Or Israeli 
courses of action, and its recent acquisition of Soviet SA5s. 
Prospects for terrorist actions in Western Europe were also 
discussed. No follow up actions were noted. (C) 

25X1 
Deputy Chief, Liaison Division
