• Qadhdhafi said English had been removed from the school curriculum in Libyan schools (as stated by the interviewer).
• 1986-07-28: Asahi Television interview with Qadhdhafi broadcast; he says liberation by armed force is the only way to settle Middle East problems and that he will not attend an Arab summit in Saudi Arabia.
• 1986-08-04: Brazilian Bandeirantes TV interview (take 2 of 3) in which Qadhdhafi calls Reagan a madman and says he would spit in his face if they met.

TOKYO ASAHI SHIMBUN IN JAPANESE 29 JUL 86 MORNING EDITION P 7 
((TEXT)) ASAHI TELEVISION NETWORK BROADCAST AN INTERVIEW WITH 
LIBYA'S SUPREME LEADER, COL AL-QADHDHAFI, ON 28 JULY. REFERRING 
TO THE RECENT TALKS HELD BETWEEN ISRAELI PRIME MINISTER PERES AND 
MOROCCO'S KING HASSAN II, AL-QADHDHAFI SAID IN THE INTERVIEW: 
"NO RESULTS WERE ACHIEVED. THE TALKS HAVE CONFIRMED THAT LIBERATION 
BY ARMED FORCE IS THE ONLY WAY TO SETTLE MIDDLE EAST PROBLEMS." 
MOREOVER, HE STATED THAT HE HAD NO INTENTION OF ATTENDCNG AN 
ARAB SUMMIT EVEN IF IT WERE HELD. 
A SUMMARY OF THE INTERVIEW WITH COL AL-QADHDHAFI FOLLOWS: 
((QUESTION)) WHAT DO YOU THINK OF THE RESULTS OF THE TALKS 
HELD BETWEEN KING HASSAN AND PRIME MINISTER PERES? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) THERE WERE NO RESULTS. (ISRAEL) WANTS 
TO SUBORDINATE THE ARAB STATES. MOREOVER, (ISRAEL) HAS OCCUPIED 
THE MIDDLE EAST AND REJECTED THE RIGHTS OF PALESTINIANS AS WELL AS 
THE PLO, THEIR ONLY LEGITIMATE REPRESENTATIVE. (IN VIEW OF KING 
HASSAN'S ACTIONS), I THINK EUROPE AND THE UNITED STATES WANT THE 
ARABS TO SURRENDER. 
((QUESTION)) AFTER THE HASSAN-PERES TALKS, WHAT WILL LIBYA'S 
POSITION AND STRATEGY BE CONCERNING PEACE IN THE MIDDLE EAST? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) THE TALKS HAVE CONFIRMED THAT THE ONLY WAY TO 
SETTLE THE MIDDLE EAST PROBLEMS IS LIBERATION BY ARMED FORCE.

BY FANATISM? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) ((LAUGHS)) I BELEIVE THAT THE PEAK OF
