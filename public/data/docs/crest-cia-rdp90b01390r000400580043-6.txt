MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD 

SUBJECT: Briefing for SSCI Staffers on the Middle East, 
Terrorism, and Libya 

On 9 January at 1630 in Room 7D32 Headquarters, SSCI 
staffers Eric Newsom, George Tenet, and Jim Dykstra were 
briefed by Charles Allen, NIO/Counterterrorism, 

DI/NESA, and DI/OGI. Liaison 25X11 
Division, OLL attended the briefing, which was at the Top 
Secret Codeword level. Mr. Allen opened the briefing by 
providing examples of the kinds of terrorist incidents that 
have been thwarted since October 1984, according to Agency 
records. The briefers discussed various Middle Eastern 
terrorist groups, particularly the Abu Nidal group, as well as 
Libyan state support for terrorist activities. They also 
discussed probable Libyan reaction to a variety of possible US 
responses to terrorist actions. No follow-up actions were 
noted. The briefing was a lively and useful exchange, and 

Mr. Newsom commented particularly favorably on the 
knowledgeability of the briefers. (C) 

25X1 
Deputy Chief, Liaison Division
