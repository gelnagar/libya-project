MAURITANIAN MINISTER ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 
LD172037Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1700 GMT 17 Jun 78 LD 


{Text] Tripoli--Brother Abdallah Ould Boye, minister of party affairs in the Islamic 
Republic of Mauritania, arrived in Tripoli this evening for a few days visit to the 
Jamahiriyah 


The Mauritanian minister was received at Tripoli International Airport by Brother 
Muhammad at-Tahir al-Mahjub, secretary for labor and civil service; a delegate from 

the Arab administration in the Secretariat of Foreign Affairs; and the Mauritanian charge 
d'affaires in the Jamahiriyah. 


Brother Ould Boye told JANA that during his visit he will hold talks with officials in 
the Jamahiriyah concerning consolidation of cooperation and brotherly relations between 
the Mauritanian and Libyan Arab peoples. 


TOGOLESE FOREIGN MINISTER ENDS VISIT TO JAMAHIRIYAH 


LD161334Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0915 GMT 16 Jun 78 LD 

[Text] Tripoli--Edem Kodjo, Togolese minister of foreign affairs and cooperation, left 
Tripoli this morning at the end of his brief visit to the Jamahiriyah. During the visit, 
he gave Brother Col Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi a message from Togolese President General Eyadema 
expressing Eyadema's admiration for the brother colonel and his desire to develop 

relations between the two countries. 


Foreign Secretary Dr 'Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki met the Togolese minister yesterday and 
liscussed bilateral relations and ways of developing cooperation between the Jamahiriyah 
and Togo in all fields.
