LIBYA TO PARTICIPATE IN AFRO=-ARAB COOPERATION MEETING 
LD300944Y Tripoli JANA in English 0803 GMT 30 Nov 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Nov 30, JANA--The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah will 
participate in the meetings of the third session for the committee of Afro-Arab coopera- 
tion which commences next Saturday in Kuwait to study the consolidation of Afro-Arab 
cooperation in all fields. 


During the meeting the committee will discuss the report of the Afro-Arab organizations 
concerning the implementation of the resolutions and commendations of the third session 
which was held in Niger last June and the establishment of sub-committees specialised 
in all activities. 


The committee will prepare the agenda and fix the date of the fourth session meetings for 
the committee of Afro-Asian cooperation which will be held in Kuwait. It will be attended 
by forty-six African and Arab foreign ministers, and will also fix the date of the meetings 
of the Arab and African ministers of information and work.

AL@-QADHDHAFI MESSAGE TO WALDHEIM ON SOLIDARITY WITH PALESTINIANS 
LD292028Y Tripoli JANA in English 1756 GMT 29 Nov 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, November 29, JANA--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the great 
lst September revolution and secretary general of the General People's Congress sent the 
following message to Dr. Kurt Waldheim, the UN secretary-general to mark the declaration 
of the 29th November as a day of international solidarity with the Palestinian people: 


Secretary-general, 


The (?devotion) of a day this year to the solidarity with the Palestinian people is an 
evidence of the recognition by the nations of the world included in this organization, 
that one of the peoples of this world, namely the Palestinian people, who have been driven 
out of their homes, whose land is occupied, deserve to return, Otherwise, why should 

the UN devote a day for the solidarity with the Palestinian people? How did these nations 
decide to stand in solidarity with the Palestinian peoplvw? They have decided to stand in 
solidarity with them through their tragedy, with respect to their fight to return to their 
occupied homeland, through their struggle, and as regards to the establishment of their 
own independent state on the whole of the Palestinian soil. 


It is truly regrettable that this should be the attitude of the nations of the world 
towards these people while these same people are ignored outside the United Nations. 
Although this is regrettable, it is also natural. Because those who conspire outside 
the UN also conspire against the UN against the civilization of the world, against the 
peace and the peoples they are dominating though their parties, their monies, or their 
swords, 


This solidarity day by the nations of the globe is an international revelation about the 
attitudes of those conspirators who ignore the Palestinian people, which minimizes the 
relevance of their obvious manoeuvres, 


I am addressing you as an Arab on the one hand, and as a man whose country is a member of 
the UN on the other hand. Therefore I am entitled to speak about the Palestinians, to 

whom I am related, and about the UN as one of its members, stressing that the international 
solidarity is a historic testimonial of the justice of the Palestinian cause, 


It will be seen as an international support for the struggle of the Palestinian people 
until they achieve victory over the racist Zionism the enemy of humanity and of peace. 


I greet you, secretary-general, and congratulate you for such a political and historic 
awareness which was reached by the UN during you era, 


My best regards and my appreciation. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi also sent a similar message to this day's celebrations committee in 
yeneva.

AteTurayki Cable 
LD292029Y Tripoli JANA in English 1810 GMT 29 Nov 78 LI 


[Text] Tripoli, November e7, JANA--The foreign secretary, Dr. 'Ali Abd as-Salam at-Turayki 
called upon the peace-loving peoples to assume full responsibility with regard to the rights 
of the Palestinian people and the implementation of the United Nations resolutions, until 
the Palestinian people restore their full rights and until they can live in peace like any 
other people, in their homeland, 


In cables sent to the secretaries-general of the United Nations, the Arab League, the 
Jrganization of African Unity, and the Islamic Conference, the chairman of the non-a ligned 
countries as well as foreign ministers of the world's countries, the foreign secretary 
Stressed that the Jamahiriyah affirms her adherence to all the United Nations resolutions 


which secure the full rights of the Palestinian Arab people, and her cooperation for restoring 


the full rights of the Palestinian people fighting the racist regimes and confronting the 
imperialist manoeuvers, by all means. 


In his cable on the international day of sOlidarity with the Palestinian people, the 
foreign secretary stated that the peoples of the world are witnessing the continuous 
intransigeance reflected in the stands of the racist Zionist entity who ignores the 
resolutions of the United Nations and of the international organizations and continue: 
to occupy the Arab territories by force, carelessly defying all values and ideals. 
Accordingly neither this entity nor any other racist regime qualify to be members of the 
United Nations. 


Dr, at-Turayki pointed out, in his cable to the sufferances and tragedies experienced by 
the Palestinian people in the past thirty years and to the conspiracies waged against 
them, the most recent of which are the "Stable David" agreements. He affirmed that the 
resolutions stipulated by these agreements, have completely ignored the rights of the 
Palestinian people. 


The foreign secretary affirmed that these rights are nonalienable. They are the rights 
which were stipulated in the United Nations Resolution No 32236 which was issued on 22d 
November 1974, They are rights which should be defended until the Palestinian people 
restore their self-determination, and establish their own state on the whole of Palestine.
