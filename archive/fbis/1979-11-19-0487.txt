LIBYA

ATTURAYKI CONDEMNS FREEZING OF IRAN'S ASSETS 
LD171600 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 17 Nov 79 ID 


[Text] Parise-Fereign Secretary Dr ‘Ali *Abd as-Salam at-Turayki has condemned the decision 
to freeze Iranian deposits in American banks and stressed that it is "a provocative, 
international terrorist act" against Iran. 


In an interview published today by the French LE MATIN, the foreign secretary added that 
what the United States has done is considered a very serious precedent and unjustified 
behavior, raising doubts about American policy, which used to claim that it respected the 
freedom of exchange. 


The foreign secretary warned other countries against the consequences of this American 
measure and pointed out that the freedom of the movement of deposits is no longer guaranteed 
and that it is possible that similar steps might be taken against other countries. 


He added: "Freezing the deposits of the Iranian people in the United States and asking 
some other countries not to import Iranian oil is an international terrorist act against 
Iran. This question must be debated at the next OPEC meeting in Caracas." 


He explained that this question was raised at the Tunis meeting because it affects the Arab 
OPEC member countries. He stated that the decisions adopted by the United States must be 
debated at this conference. The brother foreign secretary said: We stand with the Iranian 
people in their demand that the shah be tried for the crimes he committed against them. He 
pointed out that the Arab foreign min sters showed their anxiety about these American 
actions and considered them unjustifiable. 


JANA EDITOR CITED ON EGYPTIAN AIR FORCE EXERCISES 
1D161326 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 16 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] Yesterday the Egyptian Air Force carried out its largest exercise with live 
ammunition in the western desert near the borders with the Jamahiriyah. Various types of 
aircraft were used in this exercise, which was attended by the defense minister of the 
defeated president. Missiles and bombs were used. 


The JANA political editor explained in his comment on this exercise that it is not by chance 
that the defeated president should carry out such obvious provocations against the people 
of the Jamahiriyah. Indeed, this move falls within the framework of the aggressive con- 
spiracy against the Libyan Arab people which the defeated president is carrying out on the 
orders of his imperialist and Zionist masters to threaten the security of the Jamahiriyah, 
which firmly and solidly stands in the face of all challenges which the Arab region is 
facing and plays a pioneering role in the Arab nation toward the Arab-Zionist conflict. 
The editor pointed that it is nothing new for the regime of the defeated president to 
carry out such exercises and provocations against the Libyan Arab people. Indeed, since 
his capitulation to the Zionists, he has brought all his forces from the front with the 
enemy to the borders with the Jamahiriyah.
