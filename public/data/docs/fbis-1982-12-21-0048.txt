LIBYA

OPPOSITION, POPULAR GROUPS INVITED TO CONVENE 


LD171928 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1855 GMT 17 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 17 Dec (JANA) -- JANA has learned that opposition movements and popular 
formations [tadhimat] in the Arab homeland have been invited to convene an urgent 
national meeting in the Jamahiriyah to discuss the current situation and its reper- 
cussions, to find the most suitable means and ways of preventing the collapse and 
deterioration of the situation in the Arab world, to strive to stop the death train 
threatening the Arab existence, and to face up to Zionism and imperialism. 


BULGARIA'S KIRIL ZAREV INTERVIEWED UPON ARRIVAL 


LD191446 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 19 Dec 82 


[Text} Kiril Zarev, deputy chairman of the Council of Ministers of Bulgaria and 
secretary of the Communist Party Central Committee, and Khristo Khristov, minister 
of foreign trade, and an accompanying delegation arrived in Tripoli this morning. 


In a statement to JANA at Tripoli International Airport, Zarev said: “We have a mutual 
ambition in developing the economies of our two countries. We work jointly for eco- 
nomic and agricultural progress and we hope that our cooperation will widen and deepen 
in various fields." Zarev hailed the strong relations between the Jamahiriyah and 
Bulgaria in the field of trade, and the efforts exerted to achieve the aspirations 

for progress of the Arab Libyan and Bulgarian peoples.
