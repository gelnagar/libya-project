LN Envoy Addresses Nonaligned Group Meeting

[Text] New York, Dhulhyah 28, Oct 5, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY The resident Jamahiriyah delegate at the 
LN said that the Non-Aligned Movement 1s passing a critical 
stage pomtir ut that in the past recent years there has been a 
wide campaign by imperialist forces against the movements 
countries in orders to abort their independence and non- 


alignment 


In a speech at a meeting of the U.N. non-aligned group, he said 
the colonialist campaign against the non-aligned countries 
started by attacking the regional organization and groups in an 
attempt to disperse and undermine them 


He said the direct intervention of the forces of evil against the 
peoples of Latin America and Africa by sending the warships to 
the Middie East and Mediterranean in order to threaten and 
blockade the non-aligned countries 


These threats and military exercises are mainly aimed at creating 
more tension and undermining detente and peaceful coexistence 


He pointed out that the policy of non-alignment does not mean 
to be neutral! in the confrontation of colomialist powers because 
then non-alignment loses its grounds as anti-colonialist and 


anti-racist force 


He questioned how could the movement be neutral towards the 
issue of Palestine and [passage indistinct] among the non-aligned 
countries especially those producing raw materials is essential 
and stressed the need for a constructive dialogue between the 
North and South based on the interests of the Non-aligned 
Movement's nations. He added that the attempts to undermine 
and paralyze the role of the U.N. is very grave pointing out that 
the imperialist forces ignore the U.N. role and try to impose 
solutions by force through the Multi-national Forces, Rapid 
Deployment Forces and despatching warships. He stressed that 
the Jamahiriyah believes in the non-aligned policy and the 
importance of the movement as well as the necessity to increase 
its efficiency in order to become a powerful movement to achieve 
just peace
