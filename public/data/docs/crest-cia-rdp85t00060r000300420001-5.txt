Summary (AI): NPIC imagery note on an arms transshipment in progress at the Tarabulus (Tripoli) port transshipment mole, involving Hind helicopter fuselage and tail crates alongside a Soviet ship, plus probable BRDM-2 armored scout cars. It also mentions possibly related activity at Umm Aitiqah airfield.
• Three Hind helicopter fuselage crates (Type III) and two Hind tail assembly crates were on the transshipment mole.
• Soviet Slavyansk-class merchant ship had been at the mole since the Hind crates arrived; the crates were not seen as deck cargo.
• A Libyan Derna-class roll-on/roll-off ship had moved to the quay next to the Soviet ship but was not positioned to load through its stern door.
• Six probable BRDM-2 armored scout cars on the mole may be destined for shipment out of country.
• Document coordinates given as 32-54-25N 013-11-15E.
• 1984-11: Arms transshipment in progress; three Hind fuselage crates and two Hind tail assembly crates on the mole near a Soviet Slavyansk-class merchant ship; six probable BRDM-2 armored scout cars on the mole may be removed for shipment out of country.
