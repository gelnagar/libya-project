[Excerpts] Khartoum, 5 Jul (SUNA)--President Ja'far Numayri thie morning chaired

ments with fraternal and friendly countries. It approved a financial protocol 
between France and Sudan for 1981 under which France will give Sudan about 

200 million france to purchase French commodities. 


The cabinet approved in principle a draft law on the additional loan agreement 
between Sudan and the Abu Dhabi economic development fund to finance the 1981

spinning mill at a cost of $35 million. It also approved a draft law on the 
$ll-million loan agreement between the Sudanese Government and the U.S. fund 
for international development to boost the 1981 balance of payments. 


The cabinet approved in principle the trade protocol bill between Sudan and 
Egypt for 1981 amounting to $124.08 million. The cabinet also approved the 
contract for the establishment of a joint bank between the two countries to 
facilitate trade exchange and boost economic development between the two 
countries.
