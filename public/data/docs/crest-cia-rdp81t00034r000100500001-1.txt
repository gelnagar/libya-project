TACTICAL SURFACE-TO-SURFACE MISSILE EQUIPMENT IN LIBYA (S) 

1. (S/D) This report provides information on tactical surface-to-surface missile equipment/forces in 
Libya. The analysis is based on imagery acquired through The report includes a location 25X1 
map and three photographs. 

2. (S/D) Since the spring of 1976, Libya has acquired both the Soviet SCUD and FROG tactical 
surface-to-surface missile (TAC SSM) systems. The SCUD system was first identified in Libya in July 1976 

at Al Aziziyah Ammunition Storage Depot The initial identification of the FROG 25X11
