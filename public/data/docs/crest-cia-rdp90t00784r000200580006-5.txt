Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC attachment reports that a Soviet Kapitan Kushnarenko-class merchant ship delivered four Wood Bine satellite communications vehicles at the Tarabulus (Tripoli) port facilities in Libya, the first such delivery outside the Soviet bloc and Soviet forces in Afghanistan. Small-scale imagery prevented identification of the vehicles at Tripoli.
• Four Wood Bine SATCOM vehicles were delivered to Libya by a Soviet Kapitan Kushnarenko-class merchant ship.
• This was the first delivery of Wood Bine SATCOM vehicles outside the Soviet bloc and Soviet forces in Afghanistan.
• Small-scale imagery precluded identification of the vehicles at Tarabulus.
• Coordinates given: 32-54-25N, 013-11-15E.
• 1983-02: Soviet Kapitan Kushnarenko-class merchant ship offloaded four Wood Bine SATCOM vehicles; the first delivery of this equipment outside the Soviet bloc and Soviet forces in Afghanistan.
