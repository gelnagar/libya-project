Summary (AI): A 1981-82 CIA Office of Security memo, with attached press clippings, discusses U.S. media reports that the CIA planned to kill or overthrow Qaddafi. It describes a Newsweek story on a multiphase plan to remove him and a Jack Anderson column on poison-ring schemes, but no places in Tripoli are named.
• The memo states that since August 1981 three instances of U.S. media reports that the CIA intended to kill Qaddafi were totally false.
• The Newsweek plan reportedly included a disinformation program, a counter-government, and an escalating paramilitary campaign to blow up bridges.
• Newsweek ran a television promotion saying the CIA had marked Qaddafi for possible death.
• 1981-08-03: Newsweek story reports a CIA proposal, presented to the House Intelligence Committee, to remove Qaddafi from power by force; the committee sent a protest letter to President Reagan.
• 1981-08-25: Jack Anderson column reports CIA schemes to kill Qaddafi, including a poison ring and a dart disguised as a black fly.
