JANA REPORTS ON AL-QADHDHAFI'S VISIT TO MOSCOW 
Religious Activities 


LD282326 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 28 Apr 81 


[Text] Moscow, 28 Apr (JANA)--The brother leader of the revolution and accompanying 
delegation today performed noon prayers at Moscow's grand mosque. JANA reports that today, 
and for the first time, the call to prayers echoed over loudspeakers, as the call "God 

is the greatest'’ was repeated loudly in Moscow. 

Worshippers received the leader of the revolution by acclamation, expressing appreciation 
for and pride in the leader's courageous and honorable stands in support of Islam and 

the banner of Islam, as well as the leader's support for Muslims and their causes all 
over the world, in addition to his siding with the causes of right and freedom. 


The leader heard explanations about the condition of Muslims in the Soviet Union. 


The leader and his delegation also visited the Muslim cemetery in Moscow, where he read 
Koranic verses. 


Moscow Radio Coverage 
LD281712 Tripoli JANA in English 1526 GMT 28 Apr 81 


ANTA 


{"Radio Moscow Highlights Al-Qadhdhafi's Visit"--JANA headline] 


[Text] Moscow, 28 Apr (JANA)--Radio Moscow described Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's visit to the 
Soviet Union as another step on the road to furthering the relations of friendship and 
cooperation between the Libyan and Soviet peoples since the outbreak of the Great al-Fateh 
Revolution which opened new horizons before the Libyan people. 


The radio reviewed the courageous stands of Al-Fateh Revolution and pointed out its historic 
role in evacuating the colonialist bases from the Libyan soil and mentioned the gains and 
achievements accomplished through the Great Al-Fateh Revolution and its leader, Colonel 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi.

Al-Fateh Revolution, it added, has put the oil resources for service of the people and 
employed its revenues in the economic construction and important transformations. 


The efforts of the Libyan people, it confirmed, are now concentrated in reviving agriculture, 
industry and education. Libya's voice became strong and confident in the Arab and inter- 
national arena and the Libyan people has played a significant part in the consolidation 

and unity of all nationalist forces in the Arab world against the conspiracies of imperialism 
and its satellites. It added that the Libyan people strongly reject Camp David accords. 


The Jamahiriyah, it said, is playing a prominent role in the Steadfastness and Confronta- 
tion Front which is leading the struggle against Camp David treaty and the parties concerned 
who are carrying out a vicious propaganda attack against Libya. 


The radio condemned As-Sadat's suspicious attempts against the Jamahiriyah and the Egyptian 
military buildup along Libyan borders. It also condemned the unholy alliance of U.S. 
imperialism, Egypt, Sudan and Morocco which is aimed at dealing a blow to the progressive 
Arab forces and serving the U.S. interests in the region. 


Finally the radio said, "The Soviet people wholeheartedly welcome the leader of the Great 
lst September Revolution, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, who's current visit is an important 
step towards strengthening the Soviet and Libyan Arab peoples’ ties and cooperation". 


COMMUNIQUE ON MALAGASY FOREIGN MINISTER'S VISIT 


LD282356 Tripoli JANA in English 1816 GMT 28 apr 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 28 Apr, (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--A press communique on the visit of the 
Malagasy foreign minister to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah was issed in 
both Tripoli and Antananarivo. The communique stressed the identity of views between the 
Jamahiriyah and Madagascar on international issues of mutual interest, especially the 
situation in Africa and the Arab homeland. 


The communique also expressed the two sides determination to continue the fight for the 
freedom and independence of oppressed peoples and the final elimination of imperialism 
racism and zionism. 


It warmly greeted the brave positions adopted by the Jamahiriyah to help the Chadian 
people preserve its unity and independence, rebuild its national economy and put an end 
to the war fuelled by imperialist and reactionary forces. The two sides also condemned 
the attempts made by imperialism and colonialism to set up bases on the African Continent 
in view of dominating its peoples. 


The communique stressed the need to reinforce solidarity between African progressive 
forces, and in this respect, the two sides expressed their total support for the Libyan 
proposal to convene a meeting of African progressive states in order to discuss matters 
of security and cooperation between them. They greeted the victory of the Zimbabwean 
people and condemned the plots aimed at delaying Namibia's independence. They also 
reiterated their material, moral and political support for the fight of the Namibian 
people against the racist regime in Pretoria. 


They expressed their strong condemnation of the conspiracies aimed at delaying the 
acceptance of the Democratic Saharan Republic as a member of the Organization of African 
Unity. 


The communique also reiterated the two sides’ support for the right of the Palestinian 
people to create their independent state on their soil, and their strong condemnation of 
the Camp David accords and the imperialist and racist plots aimed at prolonging the 
suffering of the Palestinian people. They condemned all capitulatory attempts aimed at 
continuing the Zionist occupation of Palestine.

They stressed support for a U.™. conference in Colombia to turn the Indian Ocean into a 
peace zone, and Madagascar's Cali to hold a similar conference in Antananarivo. They 
demanded the urgent creation of a new international economic order to confront the world's 
economic crises, stressing the need to make efforts to promote cooperation between 
developing countries, which have to be self-reliant. Onthe bilateral level, the two 

sides decided to develop cooperation between the Jamahiriyah and Madagascar in every 

field of economic development, and they expressed their deep satisfaction with the impor- 
tant decisions reached during the fourth session of the Joint Libyan-Malagasy Commission. 
The Malagasy side praised the political, economic and social achievements made by the 
Libyan Arab people guided by the Great al-Fateh Revolution.
