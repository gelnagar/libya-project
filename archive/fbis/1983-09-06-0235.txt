LIBYA

JANA Reports on Events Related to Chad 


French Planning ‘Expansionist tions’ 
LD031109 Tripoli JANA in English 0855 GMT 3 Sep 83 


[Text] Ndjamena, Dhulgidah 26, September 3, Jamahiriyah 
News Agency — Reports from here revealed that French forces 
here are preparing for wide scale expansionist operations in Chad 
$0 as to occupy new parts of the country. 


In this respect, the reports specified that French troops have been 
reinforced with new weapons and war equipment in addition to 
the despatch of more French soldiers and mercenary forces. 


Imperialist warmongers at the Elysee are intent on fuelling the 
civil war in Chad by their military escalation in coordination with 
US. military policies. 


The size of French troops in Chad together with their unusual 
moves and French intentions are becoming a source of worry and 
suspicion. France has in fact decided to recolonise Chad, not help 
the Chadian people to build their unity. 


French Shelling Kills 50 
LDO51849 Tripoli JANA in English 1800 GMT 5 Sep 83 


[Text] Brussels, Dhulgidah 28, Sept 5, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — A spokesman for the Chadian legitimate united 
government here said that the civilians in Oum Chalouba have 
been greatly harmed when the French planes shelled the positions 
of the popular revolutionary forces there. 


50 people have been killed as a result including 15 women and 
several children. On the other hand AGENCE FRANCE - 
PRESSE reported a heavy explosion in Ndjamena but gave no 
further details. 


The French magazine Le Monde Diplomatique commented that 
rebel Habre would have never reached power if he did not get 10 
million dollars and arms from the United States. 


It added that the main objective of the U.S. Administration by 
helping rebel Habre is to extend the U.S. influence in Africa. °
