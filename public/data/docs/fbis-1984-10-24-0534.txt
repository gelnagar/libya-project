LIBYA

Al-Jumayyil Speech at Dinner Given by Jallud 
LD231544 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
23 Oct 84 


[Text] Staff Major ‘Abd al-Salam Ahmad Jallud last night held 
a dinner banquet in honor of the president of Lebanon, Amin 
al-Jumayyil, and delivered a speech welcoming the president and 
his accompanying delegation. 


The president of Lebanon, Amin al-Jumayyil, also delivered a 
speech at the dinner banquet. He said my visit to sister Libya is 
a natural one. We are here with brothers — suffering together 
those tragedies and meeting together those challenges which face 
us all. Our meetings have been very fruitful. They have been an 
opportunity to thrash out all subjects which confront us. 


He said that Lebanon today is paying a very high price for the 
plots hatched against its Arabism, a matter which resulted in 
further destruction, deve’ ‘on, and killing and for which the 
Lebanese Arabs paid a very dear price. 


He added that now, through cooperation with our Arab brethren, 
we seek a halt to the circle of violence and destruction, and seek 
the unity of Lebanon. He stressed that the Arab will and the 
determination of the Lebanese Arab people are strong and enable 
the Lebanese Arab people to confront all Zionist and imperialist 
plots. 


He stressed that his visit to Jamahiriyah and his meeting with 
the brother leader of the Revolution confirm the resistance to 
Zionist and imperialist plots, and confirm the coincidence in 
views regarding the unification of the Arab nation at this stage 
in particular. 


The dinner banquet held in honor of the president of Lebanon 
and his accompanying delegation was attended by the secretaries 
of the General Peoples’ Committee and heads of the diplomatic 
missions accredited to Jamahiriyah.

Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Iraqi Kurdish Leader 
LD241041 Tripoli JANA in English 0954 GMT 24 Oct 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Muharram 29, Oct 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The leader of the revolution received Mas‘ud 
al-Barzani, leader of the Kurdish Democratic Party in which he 
highlighted the operations of genocide the Kurds are being 
subjected to asking him to intervene with the concerned states to 
put an end to this act of aggression against the Kurdish Muslim 
people by Muslim brothers. 


Replying to this message the leader of the revolution recalled the 
principled and firm stand which he expressed several times 
affirming the right of the Kurdish nation to freedom, indepen- 
dence, unity and to live in peace with the sisterly Arab and 
Turkish nations. 


In his reply to the message of Mas‘ud Al-Barzani, the leader of 
the revolution condemned the acts of genocide against the Kurds 
and the alliances set up against them reasserting that Libya 
would no effort [as received] in working to see that the concerned 
states stop their hostilities against unarmed Kurds in Northern 
Iraq. For they did not commit any crime and all they wanted is 
their natural rights as a strong Muslim grouping with a distinct 
cultural identity and an historical role in the face of the attempts 
aimed at the region against the invading crusaders and vihc7s. 


Habre Reportedly Receives ‘North African’ Aid 
LD241026 Tripoli JANA in English 0934 GMT 24 Oct 54 


[Text] Ndjamena, Muharam 29, Oct 24, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — News from Ndjamena revealed that rene- 
gade Habre has recently received large assistance from a country 
in North Africa. Sixty fuel tankers have already arrived at 
Ndjamena, news added. 


Habre Forces Defect From ‘Ruling Clique’ 
LD241029 Tripoli JANA in English 0936 GMT 24 Oct 84 


[Text] Bardai, Muharram 29, Oct 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — A large number of soldiers of renegade Habre have 
recently joined the forces of the national government with their 
equipment and weapons. Recently, more than 150 soldiers have 
defected from the clique of oppression ruling in Ndjamena, news 
said.
