LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi on Libya’s Revolutionary Role 


LD241527 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
24 Aug 83 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has stressed the 
importance of building the revolutionary movement both inside 
and outside Libya, explaining that without this movement Libya 
will not have the distinguished status which we hope for in the 
future. He said during his meeting last night with participants in 
the second session of the sixth meeting of the revolutionary 
committee which began its work yesterday morning in Sabha 
that Libya is now at the top. It is the creator of revolutionary 
events which have contributed to the victory of the freedom cause 
in the Arab homeland,in Africa, and all over the world. 


He added that Libya, because of its leading prominent role in 
creating the events and in reinforcing the struggle for the cause 
of freedom, has become a target for an enemy of international 
imperialism, whether it is America or Zionism, because they 
know that Libya is serious and means what it says. Libya has the 
determining role in the battle against Zionism and imperialism 
and reaction. 


The leader stressed that the prominence which Libya enjoys, its 
determining role in the freedom struggle, its rejection of subjuga- 
tion, and its present international status — all these are tied to 
its continued building of a revolutionary ideological movement. 


He said that the immortality and historic value of the Libyan 
Arab people come from building an ideaological revolutionary 
movement whose origin is in Libya which changes the world and 


achieves victory for the Jamahiriyah theory. The leader of the 
revolution stressed the need to build an ideological revolutionary 
movement which preserves Libya’s importance and continues the 
effectiveness of its leading role in defending freedom. He said 
that without this movement Libya will lose its role in initiating 
events, and it will also lose the interest of other nations. He said 
that when the United States challenged libya, as it did recently, 
the attention of the whole world was attracted. 


The freedom-fighting peoples believe that in Libya they have a 
supporter which is explicitly threatened by imperialism. They 
stand by the Libyan Arab people, all of them, in Central Amer- 
ica, Latin America, other continents, liberation movements, and 
all individuals struggling for freedom. All the people and all 
progressive countries stand by us because losing Libya would be 
a great loss for them. It is in the interest of the peoples to defend 
Libya against aggression because if they lose Libya, they will lose 
a strong ally. 


He said the origin and source of all this is Libya’s revolutionary 
strength. 


Iranian Foreign Minister Concludes Visit 


Meeting With Al-Qadhdhafi 
LD 241239 Tehran IRNA in English 1016 GMT 24 Aug 83 


[T:x.} Banghazi, Aug. 24 IRNA — Libyan leader Col. Mu’am- 
maz al-Qadhdhafi tuesday announced support of his country and 
people for the Islamic Revolution of Iran and described the 
revolution as a move against U.S. interests. Addressing the 
visiting Iranian Foreign Minister ‘Ali Akbar Velayati who con- 
ferred with the Libyan leader here on Tuesday night, Al- 
Qadhdhafi stressed that the Islamic Revolution of Iran was 
backed by all Islamic progressive countries. 


Iran’s foreign minister, outlining the Islamic Republic’s stance 
vis-a-vis the Iraqi imposed war, emphasised keeping pace with 
the struggles of the Palestinian people. 


During the meeting, also attended by the Libyan foreign minister 
and the two countries’ ambassadors, Velayati presented Col 
al-Qadhdhafi a written letter from President Seyyed ‘Ali 
Khamene’i.

[Text] The minutes of a joint cooperation agreement were signed 
between the Jamahiriyah and Iran today in Tripoli. The minutes 
were signed by the brother secretary of the People’s Foreign 
Liaison Bureau and for the Iranian side by Dr ‘Ali Akbar 
Velayati, Iranian minister of foreign affairs. The include 
increasing cooperation in agriculture, transport, communica- 
tions, investments, trade, oil, and cultural, information, and 
technical cooperation. 


Memorandum of U aderstanding 
GF241700 Tehran IRNA in English 1634 GMT 24 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Aug. 24, IRNA — A memorandum of under- 
standing was signed here today with Libya on the concluding 
three day visit of a ranking Iranian politico-economic cultural 
delegation headed by Foreign Minister ‘Ali Akbar Velayati. The 
memorandum was signed by the foreign ministers of Iran and 
Libya. 


The memorandum called for the cooperation between the two 
countries in the fields of agriculture, air transport, investment, 
oil, trade and culture. The two parties also discussed the related 
changes pertaining to cooperations as stipulated by an earlier 
agreement in the above mentioned fields. 


In the memorandum signed earlier the two parties had 
announced their interest to further strengthen and expand 
bilateral cooperation. Bilateral cooperation in the field of agricul- 
ture as agreed upon in the memorandum, will involve the des- 
patching of a delegation from the agricultural research centre of 
Libya to establish a joint agricultural sub-committee in Iran, 
exporting of sample seeds by Iran and providing Iranian expertise 
in the stablisation of sand dunes to Libya. 


In the field of air transport, the two parties finalised the signing 
of an earlier memorandum and also announced the visit of Libya 
experts to Iran to discuss cooperation in civil aviation affairs. 


The two parties while expressing satisfaction over trade 
exchanges in the past, agreed to study the new proposals with a 
view to making known goods to be exchanged. Also the providing 
of facilities for possible setting up of trade exhibitions by private 
firms in each other's country were also a point noted in the 
memoranda 


The two parties also expressed interest in expanding cooperation 
in the field of oil 


In the memorandum it was furthermore agreed by both parties 
to open cultural centres in each other's country. Also the Libyan 
side agreed upon opening of an ISLAMIC REPUBLIC NEWS 
AGENCY (IRNA) branch office in Tripoli as soon as possible. 


The Islamic Republic's delegation is scheduled to leave for 
Damascus, Syria this evening.
