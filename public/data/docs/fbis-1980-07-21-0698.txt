LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI DEMANDS REPARATIONS FROM FRG, UK, ITALY 
LD201502 Hamburg DPA in German 1750 GMT 20 Jul 80 


[Excerpt] Hamburg--The Libyan head of state, Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, has reiterated his 
demand that the Germans, British and Italians pay his country reparations "in the 
billions" for the damage they did in Libya during World War II. In an interview given 
to DER SPIEGEL, Al-Qadhdhafi again threatened to seize the assets of the three countries 
in Libya should the demands for reparations not be met through negotiations. In May 
Al-Qadhdhafi made similar demands through the Libyan news agency JANA. 


AL-QADHDHAFI MESSAGE TO COPENHAGEN WOMEN'S CONFERENCE 


LD171006 Tripoli JANA in English 0825 GMT 17 Jul 80 


[Text] April, July 17, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel al-Qadndafi, the leader of the 
great Fatah revolution sent a message to the world conference of women which is being 
held in Copenhagen. The message reads as follows: 


We warmly support this conference and other similar conferences of women, because we 
believe that women are exploited throughout the world. Perhaps such attempts as your 
conference will urge women to liberate themselves from the bad conditions they are in. 


The woman in the Middle East is a commodity, while in the West, she is a productive 
element and an economic value. Therefore, she is neither recognized as a woman in the 
West, nor is she recognized as a human in the East. 


In the industrialized countries, she does the hardest job and sometimes the dirtiest, 

in order to earn her living, regardless of her status, being a woman, a wife and a 

mother. This kind of job destroys her femininity, beauty and nature, which also destroys 
civilization. The human race might perish as a result of changing the natural role of the 
woman and the collapse of the family and the emergence of non-family committed persons. 

On the other hand, in the East women are not even recognized yet as human beings. 


In the East women are some sort of slaves, or pieces of furniture in the houses of 

‘sultans! and of the rich. The solution, therefore, is that women should be respected: 

Their natural role should not be abused. Their femininity and beauty should not be 
undermined. They should not be treated as slaves. They are equal as humans--as referred 

to in the Green Book. 

Therefore, women and men are equal as regards all their rights. But it is unfair to ask 
women for duties that age calculated to abuse them. All societies treat women as no more 
than a commodity. The East treats them as commodities subject to sale and the West 

treats them as if they were not feminine, as referred to in the third part on the Green Book, 


The real freedom of women cannot be realised except through revolution, as the enslavers 
of woman in the materialistic industrial society or the Oriential society, the society of 
slavery, would not come to their senses spontaneously. Therefore, we call on the 

women to rebel continuously against this situation in which they find themselves, in 
order to lift the yoke of injustice being subjected to as females. This is the only road 
towards a happy society. 


V. 21 Jul 80 NORTH AFRICA 


A society where a human being feels that his or her dignity is respected.... And his 

or her rights and duties commensurate with mould [as received] as a human being.... A 
society where a human being, be it aman or woman, feels that he or she belongto a family 
with a father and a mother. 


Therefore, I favoured an invitation to form covert or overt female revolutionary committees 
in order to work for lifting the yoke of injustice to which woman is subjected, and to urge 
her to live in a happy society. 


I wish you success in your conference. 
March forward. 
Colonel Mu*ammar al-Qadhdhafi.
