LIBYA

Algerian Industries Minister Arrives 
LD140059 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 13 Sep 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 Sep (JANA) — Member of the Central Com- 
mittee and Algerian minister of light industries, Said ait- 
Messaoudene, and his accompanying delegation, arrived this 
evening in Tripoli on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. The Algerian 
minister of light industries expressed his pleasure at visiting the 
Jamahiriyah. In a statement to a JANA correspondent after his 
arrival at Tripoli International Airport, he stated that this visit 
came within the sphere of the proceedings of the Libyan-Algerian 
joint committee, which was convened between the Algerian 
Council of Ministers and the Libyan General People’s Commit- 
tee. He added that this visit would enable us to discuss the issue 
of increasing joint cooperation and laying down a plan of work 
in the economic fields in general and the industrial field in 
particular in a way that would realize the joint objectives and 
interests of the two brotherly peoples.
