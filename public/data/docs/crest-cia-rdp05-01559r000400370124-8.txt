NEW INDUSTRIAL WORKERS UNION ESTABLISHED 
Addis Ababa Domestic Service in Amharic 1700 GMT 9 Oct 76 LD/EA 

{Text] Representatives of 60,000 factory workers today established an industrial 

workers union after a week-long meeting held at the national parliament building. The 
decision to establish the industrial workers union was reached after a lengthy discussion 
on the rules and regulations of the union, which were approved by all representatives 
participating in the meeting. During the vote to establish the industrial workers union, 
nine members of an implementation committee were selected in a democratic election. 

The formation of the industrial workers union was witnessed by Ato Tefari Haile Selassie, 
minister of labor and social affairs; Ato Getnet Zewde, permanent representative of the 
ministry; and the heads of ministry departments.
