AL-QADHDHAFI GOES TO MALTA FOR SHORT VISIT

[Text] Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and the delegation accompanying him arrived 
Malta at 1645 today on a few days! state visit. He was welcomed at the airport by the 
Maltese president and ministers, Arab and foreign diplomats in Malta, members of the 
Jamahiriyah Embassy in Malta and scores of thousands of Maltese citizens. 


Meets Mintoff 2 July 
LD022116Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 2 Jul 78 LD 


[Text] Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and Dom Mintoff held a closed meeting yesterday 

lasting 30 minutes. In the evening, Al-Qadhdhafi received Mintoff in the presence of 
Mustafa al-Kharrubi, Foreign Secretary 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, and the Libyan 
ambassador to Malta. Present on the Maltese side were the deputy prime minister, the 
foreign minister and the justice, lands, housing and parliamentary affairs minister. 
During this meeting a gift was presented to Al-Qadhdhafi representing Malteste traditional 


crafts. 


'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, Libyan foreign secretary, and Joseph Cassar, Maltese deputy 
prime minister and foreign minister [as heard], held a meeting this morning at the 
Maltese Foreign Ministry. It was attended on the Libyan side by Husni Sha'ban, 

assistant under secretary for political affairs at the Foreign Secretariat, and other 
members of the Libyan delegation. On the Maltese side it was attended by a number of 
ministers. The directors of economic and trade affairs at the Maltese Foreign 

Ministry were also present. The meeting dealt with questions relating to the 
strengthening of cooperation between the two countries in the industrial and economic 
fields.

Communique on Visit 
LD052253Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1700 GMT 3 Jul 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--This morning the following joint communique was issued in Valletta 
and Tripoli on the visit to the Republic of Malta by brother Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
secretary general of the General People's Congress, which was concluded today, 


At the invitation of Anton Buttigieg, president of the republic of Malta, and Dom Mintoff, 
the prime minister, brother Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 

September Revolution and secretary general of the General People's Congress 

Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, paid an official friendly visit

sides dealing with bilateral relations, mtual cooperation, and Arab and internation

The two sides expressed their satisfaction at the good standard which bilatera!

The Libyan Arab side expressed appreciation for the strides the Republic of Malt

The two sides confirmed the importance of liquidating all foreign military bases. They 
agreed to increase their joint efforts to avert international conflict in the Mediterranem 
region and to turn it into a lake of peace in the service of the peoples of the region 

and the entire world. To that end the Jamahiriyah promised to render its full support

Talks between the two sides also dealt with the explosive situation in the Arab area. 
They strongly condemned Zionist aggression against the Palestinian people and the 
Arab nation. They confirmed support for the just struggle being waged by the 
Palestinian Arab people for the right to return to their homeland and establish 
democratic state.

falks also dealt with the situation in Africa. Both sides condemned the racial regimes
