Summary (AI): A 1982 NPIC briefing note reports the first identification in Libya of the Soviet-origin BIG CAP B jammer system at the Umm Aitiqah ECM site, northwest of Umm Aitiqah airfield. The site is far from Tripoli.
• BIG CAP B is a two-vehicle jammer system: a DOG CART trailer (modified KING PIN) and a BIG CAP B truck (Ural-375 with a 1.6 m environmental cover and two generator mufflers).
• The system probably works with KING PIN and TUB BRICK jammers that relocated to the site from a temporary site 1.8 NM to the west.
• Coordinates given: 32-54-00N 013-19-05E.
• 1982-09-22: BIG CAP B jammer system newly identified at the site; first identification in Libya and outside the Soviet Union and Warsaw Pact.
