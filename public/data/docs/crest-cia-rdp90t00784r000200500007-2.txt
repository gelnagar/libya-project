Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery note from January 1983 reports a Soviet-type roll-on/roll-off ship carrying Libya-bound HAZE A antisubmarine/search-rescue helicopter shipping containers arriving offshore Tubruq (Tobruk). The containers were likely to be moved on to Misratah port and the helicopters probably to Ghurdabiyah airfield. It is about military arms shipments, not Tripoli itself.
• Deck cargo included eight HAZE A helicopter fuselage shipping containers and 16 HAZE A-associated component containers.
• HAZE A is an antisubmarine warfare/search-rescue helicopter operated outside the Warsaw Pact only by Syria and Libya.
• Coordinates given: 32-04-42N 023-58-50E (Tubruq area).
• 1983-01: Soviet-class RO/RO ship with HAZE A helicopter shipping containers arrived offshore; helicopters expected to be unloaded in Libya.
