TRIPOLI REPORTS ARRESTS AMONG SUDAN WOMEN'S UNION 


NC230820 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic ©4v0 GMT 23 Jan 82 


[Text] Reports arriving in Beirut from the Sudanese capital yesterday said that authori- 
ties of (?agent) Numayri launched a campaign of arrests in the last few days against 
members of the Sudanese Women's Federation for their effective participation in the 
uprising being witnessed in Sudan against the Numayri regime's terrorist policy. 


Reports say that among those detained are Fatimah Ahmad Ibrahim, chairman of the Sudanese 
Women's Federation; Al-Khansa ‘Umar, member of the Sudanese Women's Federation executive 
body; and Ni'mat Malik, member of the Sudanese Women's Federation Executive Council. 

The reports spoke of the bad conditions the patriot detainees are suffering. These 
reports stressed the detainees are placed in solitary dungeons, they are deprived of 
medicine and medical services and their health conditions have deteriorated seriously. 
Sources of [words indistinct] expressed their concern and fear about the health of 
struggler Fatimah Ibrahim who is suffering from several diseases that need constant care. 


ANTI-NUMAYRI_ SEMINAR HELD IN BEIRUT 23 JAN 


NC240707 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 24 Jan 82 


[Text] A seminar was held in western Beirut yesterday in solidarity with the uprising 

of the Arab people in Sudan against the regime of [word indistinct] Numayri. The seminar 
was attended by some of the leaders of the Lebanese National Movement and the Palestinian 
resistance and officials of some parties and Arab and student organizations. Addresses 
were delivered at the seminar affirming support for the uprising of the Sudanese masses.
