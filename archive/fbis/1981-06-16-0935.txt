LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI VISIT TO NIGERIA REPORTED POSTPONED 


LD152150 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1730 GMT 15 Jun 81 


[Text] Tripoli, JANA--An informed source at the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison has 
stated that the official and friendly visit which Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 
Great 1 September Revolution, was scheduled to pay to the Nigerian Federal Republic at the 
invitation of President Shehu Shagari has been postponed to an early date to be agreed 
upon by both sides. The postponement, which was with the agreement of both countries, is 
due to the busy schedules of both the revolution leader and President Shagari at this 
particular time. It is recalled that a meeting between Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and Presi- 
dent Shagari was recently held in Ndjamena, that the two countries have strong relations 
and closer cooperation and that they maintain constant coordination and consultation on 
international, African and oil issues. 


COMMENTARY RAILS AGAINST U.S.-ISRAELI COLLUSION 


LD160910 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 16 Jun 81 


[Unattributed commentary ] 


[Excerpts] Only the naive, foolish and ignorant believed the false American gesture of 
postponing delivery of the four American aircraft to the Israeli enemy over the incident 
of the Iraqi nuclear reactor, and they did so only to save face with the Arab masses. 
But these masses, thanks to their sense of nationalism and their suffering and agonies, 
know that the United States is truly their enemy and that the Israelis are no more than 
a gang gathered from all corners of the world to implement the terrorist expansionist 
plan directed against the Arab nation.

These Israelis would not have been able to bluster so freely, violating the sanctity of 
the Arab skies, land and water--indeed, they would not have been able to set up their 
terrorist racist state in Palestine--had it not been for American protection, support and 
aid. In all the wars they have fought, the Arabs were actually fighting not the Israelis 
so much as the Americans, who formed the principal party in the Arab-Zionist conflict. 
This is what caused the defeat of the Arab nation. 


The United States is the leader of world terrorism, and it provides protection under its 
terrorist umbrella to all the economic and political dictatorships to enable them to 
continue committing abominable crimes against the people, as can be clearly seen from 
the events in Vietnam, Kampuchea, Laos, Iran, Ethiopia, Nicaragua, Cuba and El Salvador. 
All these events prove the United States is the leader of international terrorism. 


As for the attack on the Iraqi nuclear reactor, the fingers of accusation point to the 
United States: The planes which attacked the Iraqi reactor were American; the pilots 
themselves could also be American by virtue of dual citizenship between the United States 
and the Zionist enemy. Observers affirm that the AWACS aircraft must have provided the 
Israelis with all the information they needed about the Iraqi nuclear reactor. One 
should not belittle the role of the U.S. bases and fleets in the execution of this filthy 
terrorist operation. 


However, we now have the news which unmasks the truth about U.S. collusion with the 
Zionists in striking at the Iraqi nuclear reactor: The U.S. Government has threatened 
the Arab delegations that not only will it veto any resolution put forward with a view 
to imposing sanctions against the Zionists, but it will go further and rescind its de- 
cision to refrain at present from delivering the four U.S. planes to the Zionists. 


Furthermore, Begin is fully confident that the United States will provide him with the 
political cover he needs to protect himself from any international confrontation of em- 
barrassment. This farce, which the Arab citizens have been witnessing, has caused them 
to lose all hope about America, the UN Security Council and all the international resolu- 
tions as long as they stand impotent and incapable of facing up to the international 
terrorism led by America. 


The Arab nation must not pin any hopes on the UN Security Council, for in fact it is the 
road to which the weak resort. The Arab nation must deal double blows and reiy only on 
its strength in this world, which has no respect except for the strong and for their 
rights. This element of strength can only be achieved through the elimination of all 
inter-Arab conflicts and differences, the establishment of Arab unity and a fusion of 
the capabilities and resources of the Arab masses within the framework of the pan-Arab 
character of the battle and of fedayeen action.
