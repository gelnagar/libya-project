Summary (AI): Qadhafi gave a live speech at the People's Hall in Tripoli on 28 March 1985, marking the anniversary of the British evacuation from Libya. He argued that British and American bases had kept Libya dependent, and that the US forces expelled from Wheelus had moved to Egypt.
• The speech was broadcast by Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic at 1652 GMT on 28 March 1985.
• Qadhafi said the American forces expelled from Wheelus were now at Ra's Banas on the Red Sea, and that the British had taken part in manoeuvres near Alexandria, Marsa Matruh and al-Sallum.
• The speech declared Libya the cornerstone of a new era of Jamahiris.
• 1985-03-28: Qadhafi delivered a live speech on the anniversary of the British evacuation from Libya, which was interrupted by applause and chants.

TRIPOLI DOMESTIC SERVICE IN ARABIC 1652 GMT 28 MAR 85 

((COLONEL MU'AMMAR AL-QADHDHAFI'S SPEECH ON THE 

ANNIVERSARY OF THE BRITISH EVACUATION FROM LIBYA AT THE PEOPLE'S 

HALL IN TRIPOLI -- LIVE)) 

((TEXT)) IN THE NAME OF GOD. BROTHERS, TODAY WE STAND 

HERE TO COMMEMORATE THE ANNIVERSARY OF THE EXPULSION OF THE BRITISH 

FORCES AND TO GREET THE BROTHERS AND FRIENDS WHO ARE PRESENT 

WITH US TODAY TO SHARE OUR COMMEMORATION OF THIS ANNIVERSARY. 

((APPLAUSE AND CHANTS PREVENTING AL-QADHDHAFI FROM SPEAKING) ) 

HAD IT NOT BEEN FOR THE REVOLUTION, THE BRITISH BASES, THE 

AMERICAN BASES, AND PERHAPS AIR BASES OF OTHER IMPERIALIST 

POWERS WOULD HAVE BEEN HERE UNTIL TODAY. 

AFTER A LONG TIME, THE LIBYAN PEOPLE, THE ENTIRE ARAB 

PEOPLE, AND OTHER PEOPLE IN SIMILAR SITUATIONS DISCOVERED 

THAT THE INDEPENDENCE GAINED BY MOST OF THESE COUNTRIES 

WAS INDEPENDENCE IN NAME ONLY AND WAS FALSE. THERE ARE 

STILL OTHER PEOPLE WHO MISTAKENLY BELIEVE THEY ARE INDEPENDENT 

BECAUSE THEY HAVE A FLAG AND A NATIONAL ANTHEM AND ARE RULED BY 

PEOPLE OF THEIR OWN. WE ARE SORRY TO SEE THE PEOPLE DECEIVED 

IN THIS WAY UNTIL THE PRESENT STAGE WHEN, THROUGH CONSTANT 

STRUGGLE, MANY PEOPLE HAVE ADVANCED THROUGH IMPORTANT STAGES . 

OF POLITICAL DEVELOPMENT, AS KINGDOMS AND REPUBLICS. NOW WE ARE 

REACHING THE THRESHHOLD OF A NEW ERA: THE ERA OF THE MASSES, 

THE ERA OF JAMAHIRIS, OF WHICH LIBYA IS THE CORNERSTONE. 

((APPLAUSE AND CHANTS)) 

UNFORTUNATELY, THERE ARE STILL SOME ARAB PEOPLES, IF SUCH AN 

EXPRESS HOLDS, WHO DECEIVE THE EOPLE AND MAKE THEM BELIEVE THEY 

ARE INDEPENDENT, AS THE LIBYAN PEOPLE WERE DECEIVED IN THE PAST 

FROM THE DAY OF THE PROCLAMATION OF THEIR FALSE INDEPENDENCE 

UNTIL THE PROCLAMATION OF THE REVOLUTION. 

IT WAS THE REVOLUTION ALONE THAT EXPOSED THE TRUTH ABOUT THE 

FALSE INDEPENDENCE. IT WAS THE REVOLUTION THAT EXPOSED THE TRUTH 

ABOUT THE REGIME THAT EXISTED. IT WAS THE REVOLUTION THAT EXPOSED 

THE TRUTH THAT LIBYA WAS AN AMERICAN, A BRITISH, AND AN 

ITALIAN COLONY. ITALIAN COLONIALIST RULE, DISGUISED IN CIVILIAN 

CLOTHING, DOMINATED EVERYTHING. ITALIAN COLONIALISM ACHIEVED ITS 

OBJECTIVES IN LIBYA AND DOMINATED A LARGE PART OF ITS 

DESTINY, ALONGSIDE THE AMERICAN AND BRITISH BASES WHICH TURNED 

THE WHOLE OF LIBYA INTO A SERVANT FOR NATO AND IMPERIALIST 

INTERESTS. 

THE JAMAL ‘ABD AL-NAZIR BASE, WHERE TODAY WE ARE CELEBRATING 

THE ANNIVERSARY OF THE EXPULSION OF THE BRITISH, WAS CALLED 

AL-'ADEM BASE EVEN AFTER THE REVOLUTION. FROM THIS BASE RAIDS 

WERE LAUNCHED ON EGYPT IN 1956. WHAT I WANT TO SAY IS THAT 

IMPERIALISM LEFT EGYPT AND CONCENTRATED IN LIBYA. SOME OF THE 

MILITARY EXPELLED FROM THE SUEZ CANAL AFTER THE REVOLUTION 

IN EGYPT LED BY JAMAL 'ABD AL-NASIR, WERE DISCOVERED TO HAVE

NAMELY LIBYA, COULD WELCOME THESE FORCES WHICH CONCENTRATED IN

TODAY, WE ARE CELEBRATING THE DAY OF FREEDOM AND THE COMPLETE

BUT THE TRUTH IS THAT WHILE WE REJOICE TODAY, WE ALSO FEEL

OTHER EGYPTIAN BASES. PERHAPS THE BASE ON THE RED SEA IS A PURELY 
AMERICAN BASE. 

THE AMERICANS WE EXPELLED FROM LIBYA ARE NOW IN ECYPT. THE 
BRITISH WE EXPELLED FROM AL-'ADEM BASE 15 YEARS AGO, TOOK PART 

IN MANEUVERS THE DAY BEFORE YESTERDAY ON THE SHORES OF 

ALEXANDRIA, MARSA MATRUH, AND AL-SALLUM -- ONLY A SHORT 

DISTANCE AWAY FROM THE AL-'ADEM BASE FROM WHICH THEY WERE EXPELLED. 
WHAT CONCLUSION CAN WE DRAW FROM THIS? THE CONCLUSION IS THAT 

THE LIBERATION OPERATION WAS ALSO AN ACT OF DECEPTION -- JUST LIKE 
THE INDEPENDENCE OPERATION. IF WE BELIEVE THAT, OUR LIBERATION 

OF PART OF THE HOMELAND -- OUR CROUCHING IN IT AND OUR DRAWING OF 
BORDERS FOR IT WHICH ONLY CONSOLIDATE THE IMPERIALIST DIVISION 

OF THE ARAB HOMELAND ~- IT MEANS THAT THE FALSEHOOD CONTINUES, 
JUST AS THE FALSEHOOD OF INDEPENDENCE AND FREEDOM BECOMES 

A FARCE JUST LIKE THE FARCE OF INDEPENDENCE. 

IN FACT, FREEDOM CAN ONLY TRIUMPH AND WE FEEL CONFIDENT THAT 

WE HAVE LIBERATED, PURGED, AND RETRIEVED OUR LAND, WHEN THE WHOLE 
OF THE ARAB HOMELAND IS SUCCESSFULLY LIBERATED. OTHERWISE, 

THERE WILL BE NO USE. WE HAVE EXPELLED THE BRITISH FROM LIBYA 

AND WE HAVE EXPELLED THE AMERICANS FROM LIBYA BUT THEY HAVE GONE 
TO EGYPT. BEFORE US, 'ABD AL-NASIR EXPELLED THE BRITISH FROM EGYPT, 
BUT THEY CAME TO LIBYA AND STRUCK AT HIM FROM LIBYA. IT MAKES NO 
DIFFERENCE WHETHER THEY ARE AT TOBRUK OR AT SUEZ.
