LIBYA

JALLUD: CHAD FREE TO SEEK AID ANYWHERE 


AB290900 Paris AFP in French 0844 GMT 29 Oct 81 


[Text] Ndjamena, 29 Oct (AFP) -- In an interview with the Chadian national radio, Maj 
"Abd al-Salam Jallud, Libya's number two man, described France's decision to impose a 
neutral peace-keeping force on Chad as interference in Chad's internal affairs. 


Major Jallud, who held talks in Ndjamena with Chadian President Goukouni Oueddei on 
Tuesday and Wednesday [27 and 28 October], added in particular: If we are there (in 
Chad), it is with the agreement of the Chadian Government. The day it tells us to leave, 
we will leave. 


The Chadian people are free and sovereign to make their own decision on this issue. He 
added: For Tripoli, the Chadian Government is free to call on any country for aid. 


After stressing that his visit falls within the framework of periodic meetings established 
by the two brotherly countries, the number two Libyan once again stated: Chad needs to 
be rebuilt and Libya is ready to assist it. 


Nothing was leaked from the meeting between President Goukouni and Major Jallud. On 
20 October, the Chadian head of state received Major Mas'oud a special envoy of Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi in Ndjamena. 


JANA QUESTIONS FRENCH REACTION TO CHAD SITUATION 


LD290806 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0715 GMT 29 Oct 81 


[Text] In an urgent dispatch from Ndjamena last night, JANA correspondent said that the 
situation in Chad was completely calm, under cohesive national unity, and that there 

was no truth in the reports disseminated by AFP yesterday to the effect that there was 
an alleged deterioration of the situation in Chad. 


The correspondent affirmed that life was normal and that general activities in the 
country were also normal. AFP last night circulated reports alleging that Libyan forces 
had entered Ndjamena. The agency portrayed the situation in Chad as unstable, and 
circulated claims and allegations to convey a false impression of the unity and co- 
hesiveness of the various parties under the national government headed by Goukouni 
Oueddei. 


The agency also reported a statement by an informed French military source which said 
the French forces stationed in the Central African Republic had been put on a state of 
alert due to the allegedly unstable situation in Chad. 


Commenting on the false reports disseminated by AFP, a JANA political editor said the 
essence of what was disseminated by the French news agency involved ill-intentions 

aimed at deception and giving a false impressicn of the stable situation in Chad. This 
in fact accords with a number of claims and fabrications disseminated by the imperialist 
circles and the hireling reactionary regimes. 


He said the circulation and dissemination of such reports, and their attribution to 
responsible French sources had a clear meaning, which is: France wants to reverse 

facts by conveying a false idea abrut the situation in Chad. We cannot see any justifi- 
cation for the French military move in the Central African Republic based on the 

logical and true point of view emanating from the natural and stable situation in 

Chad except that France wants to occupy Ci.ad after having occupied the Central African 
Republic, if what was circulated by AFP in this respect is true.

AL-QADHDHAFI DENIES MILITARY PLAN AGAINST SUDAN 


LD282345 Tripoli JANA in English 1710 GMT 28 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 28 Oct (JANA) -- The revolution's leader has sent a letter to Daniel arap 
Moi, president of the Republic of Kenya and current chairman of the Organization of African 
Unity, (OAU), in which he reaffirmed that the Jamahiriyah has no intention to carry out 
military acts against Sudan and has no military concentrations along the borders with Sudan. 


The leader also informed the OAU chairman of the continuous Sudanese aggressions launched by 
the Sudanese Armed Forces on the Chadian cities and villages in an attempt to undermine 
security and stability in Chad. This is in addition to the fact that the Sudanese Government 
is harbouring the remnants of rebel Hissene Habre's followers and backing them by all means 
which makes Chad in a continuous state of tension. 


The leader stressed that Africa must direct its efforts towards economic development and the 
realization of social progress. This is what the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
has done for the Chadian people, giving it assistance to enable it to realise and build its 
future. 


The leader acquainted the OAU chairman of the repeated aggressions carried out by Sudan 
which contributed to undermining the OAU decisions. 


He also acquainted him with the irresponsible statements issued by Sudan's ruler containing 
blatant threats to commit aggression on the Libyan people and conduct assassination and sabotage 
operations within the Jamahiriyah. 


SUDANESE URGED NOT TO UNLOAD, HANDLE U.S. ARMS 


LD290012 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1950 GMT 28 Oct 81 
{From the "Sudan of the Revolution" program] 


[Text] Valiant masses of the Sudanese people, dockers, port workers in the warehouses, 
customs officials and crane operators: Today the United States is sending its weapons to Port Sudan in 
order to colonize Sudan and use these weapons to strike at your movement which is against 
agent Numayri. What are you going to do about it? Will you unload these weapons from ships? 
Will you load these weapons on the railroad cars? Will you, customs officials, clear these 
weapons? Will you be a tool that helps to transport these weapons and equipment inside Sudan? 
These weapons will be trained either on our people who are against the agent Numayri or 
against the Afro-Arab neighboring peoples. We remind you of your heroic stands in Port 

Sudan in 1967 when you refused to accept British, U.S. and French ships, and refused to load 
or unload them because they were enemy ships. We would like to remind you of your revolution- 
ary stand when you expelled the senior British pilot from Port Sudan. 


The Sudanese people have always stood against the U.S. and British invasion against the Arab 
people. We would like to remind you of the stand taken by employees at Sudanese airports who 
refused to service the aircraft heading for the Arab south to strike at the movement of our 
brothers who were conducting an armed struggle against British colonialism. The Sudanese people today 
are facing the same U.S. colonialist march which wishes to enter our country with its arms 

and soldiers. Our people should bar the way before the U.S. soldiers. 


Our workers: Do not unload U.S. weapons, do not load them onto railroad cars, do not deal 
with these weapons in any form. Register your opposition and rejection of marching U.S. 
colonialism. Stop this march with the strength of your own arms! 


Masses of our struggling people: Today is your day. Prove to the whole wide world that 
you are the people of October [Sudanese October revolution] and that you are the people of 
Sudan.
