• Reagan said the US has evidence Libya sent assassins to murder senior US officials; no public evidence was released.
• A defector reportedly walked into a US diplomatic mission in Western Europe within the last month and described 10 hit-team members, most of them Libyans.
• Economic options discussed at NSC meeting included an oil boycott, which would need agreement from Britain, France, Italy and Japan.
• Only British cooperation would be needed to close Libyan oil fields temporarily by withdrawing American and British technicians.
• The State Department last summer advised US citizens to leave Libya; about 1,500 US citizens, mostly oil workers and dependents, remained.
• Six US oil companies operated in Libya.
