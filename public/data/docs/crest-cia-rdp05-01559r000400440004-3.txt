((TEXT)) IBRAHIM NAFI': IT MIGHT BE SUITABLE AND LOGICAL, 
BROTHER COLONEL, TO BEGIN OUR DIALOGUE FROM LAST MONTH'S TUNIS 
MEETING, WHICH WAS THE LATEST MAJOR ARAB EVENT. WHAT IS YOUR 
ASSESSMENT OF THE POLITICAL AND ECONOMIC PERFORMANCE OF THE ARAB 
MAGHREB UNION, AND HOW DO YOU VIEW THE MECHANISM BY WHICH FULL 
COORDINATION AND COOPERATION CAN BE ACHIEVED AMONG THE THREE ARAB 
GROUPINGS? 

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: YOU KNOW THAT WE ORIGINALLY PROCEED FROM THE 
CALL FOR A COMPREHENSIVE ARAB UNITY AND YOU KNOW THAT IN THE PAST, 
COLONIALIST SCHEMES WERE PROPOSED FOR THE REGION SUCH AS THE UNION 
OF THE FERTILE CRESCENT AND THE NILE VALLEY, BUT THE REVOLUTIONARIES 
AND UNIONIST ARABS RESISTED THESE SCHEMES AT THAT TIME AND

AMENDMENT PROGRAM. IT WAS ADOPTED BY THE CASABLANCA SUMMIT 
CONFERENCE AND BY PRESIDENT MUBARAK WHO ATTENDED THE SUMMIT. BY 
THIS PLAN I ATTEMPTED TO GROUP THE THREE COUNCILS TOGETHER, IN 
ADDITON TO THE ARAB COUNTRIES WHICH ARE NOT MEMBERS TO ANY OF THEM. 
THIS IS AIMED AT TURNING THE SUMMIT CONFERENCES INTO SPECIALIZED 
PRESIDENCY COUNCILS, THAT IS, A COUNCIL FOR THE FOREIGN MINISTERS, A 
COUNCIL FOR INDUSTRY AND AGRICULTURE, AND SO FORTH. THESE COUNCILS 
WOULD BE GIVEN AN ECONOMIC CONTENT WITHIN THE POLITICAL FRAMEWORK-- 
AND THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT THING--THROUGH THE ESTABLISHMENT OF 
AN ARAB AUTHORITY FOR BORDERS, AN ARAB AUTHORITY FOR WATER, GAS, AND 
ELECTRICITY, AND SO FORTH. ECONOMIC UNITY WOULD TAKE PLACE WITHIN 
THIS FORMULA. WE ARE, HOWEVER, AT THE BEGINNING AND IT IS DIFFICULT 
WO PREDICT HOW THE PICTURE WILL BE TOMORROW. BUT WE HAVE TO DRAW 
THE PICTURE WE WANT AND ATTEMPT TO MOVE THE GIVEN FACTS IN THE 
DIRECTION OF THE REQUIRED PICTURE WHICH FULFILLS THE NATION'S HOPE 
FOR UNITY.

WERE MUCH BETTER THAN THE EUROPEAN AGREEMENTS UNDER THE EEC. BUT

((TEXT)) IBRAHIM NAFI': BROTHER COLONEL, I BELIEVE THAT THE MOST 
SERIOUS ISSUE WITH WHICH THE ARABS ARE PREOCCUPIED NOW IS THE SEARCH 
FOR THE BEST METHOD TO EMBARK ON A COMPREHENSIVE ARAB MOVE TO FACE 
THE SOVIET JEWS' IMMIGRATION TO PALESTINE. IN YOUR OPINION, WHAT IS 
THE PRACTICAL WAY TO AVERT THIS DANGER WHILE MAINTAINING THE PEACE 
PROCESS? 

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: THIS IS A VERY IMPORTANT QUESTION. THERE IS 
NO DOUBT THIS EMIGRATION CONSTITUTES A GRAVE THREAT TO THE 
PALESTINIAN PEOPLE AND THE ARAB NATION. THE DIRECT CONSEQUENCES ARE 
REPRESENTED BY SEIZING THE REST OF THE ARAB LAND AND EXPELLING MORE 
PALESTINIANS FROM PALESTINE. THIS MIGRATION COULD ALSO LEAD TO WAR, 
BECAUSE THE PRESENCE OF JEWS IN GREATER NUMBERS ON THIS LAND, 
PALESTINE, WILL ENTICE THEM TO EXPAND AT THE EXPENSE OF THE ARABS, 
TO ESTABLISH THEIR GREATER STATE, AND TO SEEK THE REALIZATION OF 
THEIR HISTORICAL DREAM OF A GREATER STATE EXTENDING FROM THE 
EUPHRATES TO THE NILE.

IBRAHIM NAFI': IN THE VIEW OF COL. AL-QADHDHAFI, HOW CAN THE 
LARGEST DEGREE OF ARAB-AFRICAN COOPERATION BE ACHIEVED TO CONFRONT 
THE PROBLEMS FACING THE ARAB AND AFRICAN STATES TOGETHER? 

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: CERTAINLY, THE ARABS AND THE AFRICANS ARE 
VERY CLOSE TO EACH OTHER, ESPECIALLY SINCE HALF OF THE ARABS ARE 
AFRICANS. HOWEVER, THERE IS A COLONIALIST PLOT AGAINST THIS 
COOPERATION. AMONG THE LINKS OF THIS CONSPIRACY HAS BEEN THE 
CREATION OF A LIBYAN-CHADIAN PROBLEM, THE SOUTHERN SUDAN PROBLEM, 
AND THE CONFLICT BETWEEN MAURITANIA AND SENEGAL AND BETWEEN SOMALIA 
AND ETHIOPIA. THE CONFRONTATION BETWEEN THE ARABS AND AFRICANS HAS 
BEGUN TO EXTEND FROM SOMALIA TO MAURITANIA. THIS IS A GRAVE 
DEVELOPMENT THAT IS NOT IN THE INTEREST OF THE ARABS, ESPECIALLY 
SINCE SENEGAL, MALI, NIGER, CHAD, TANZANIA, AND ETHIOPIA HAVE AN 
ISLAMIC MAJORITY INFLUENCED BY ARAB CULTURE AND THE ARABIC LANGUAGE. 
EVEN RACIALLY, THESE NATIONS HAVE ARAB BLOOD RUNNING IN THEIR VEINS. 
THEY CANNOT AVOID MORE COHESION AND THE ESTABLISHMENT OF AN ARAB- 
AFRICAN UNION. FOR EXAMPLE, MALI, NIGER, AND CHAD MAY JOIN THE ARAB 
MAGHREB UNION. THIS IS A CALL WHICH I RAISE. HOWEVER, THERE IS 
CURRENTLY AN EXTREMELY SERIOUS PLOT THAT HAS PROVED SUCCESSFUL TO A 
GREAT EXTENT. THIS PLOT IS AIMED AT EFFECTING A RIFT BETWEEN THE 
BLACK AFRICANS AND THE ARABS OR BETWEEN THE AFRICANS AND THE ARAB 
AFRICANS. COLONIALISM SYMPATHIZED WITH OTHERS AGAINST LIBYA, 
MAURITANIA, SUDAN, AND SOMALIA. EVEN THE ISLAMIC AFRICAN PEOPLES-- 
THE CLOSEST TO THE ARABS AND MUSLIMS--HAVE BEEN DECEIVED BY THIS 
STAND. THE U.S. PEACE GROUPS HAVE PLAYED A MAJOR DESTRUCTIVE ROLE 
IN THIS PLOT. | 

IBRAHIM NAFI': THEREFORE, SHOULD WE CONTINUE TO TRACK THIS PLOT? 

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: DEFINITELY. WE MUST ASSERT THAT ARAB-AFRICAN 
UNITY IS A FATEFUL UNITY. WE MUST EXPOSE THE HIDDEN CONSPIRACY 
BEHIND EVERY DISPUTE, SUCH AS THE SENEGAL-MAURITANIA AND THE CHAD- 
LIBYA DISPUTES. ALL THESE DISPUTES MUST BE RESOLVED THROUGH 
FRIENDLY MEANS. 

IBRAHIM NAFI': THIS LEADS TO A SPONTANEOUS QUESTION ABOUT COL. 
AL-QADHDHAFI'S VIEW OF THE SITUATION IN CHAD, LIBYA, AND SUDAN. 

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: REGARDING CHAD, I SAY THIS COUNTRY HAS FALLEN 
IN THE INTERNATIONAL GAME. THEREFORE, IT WILL CONTINUE TO BE 
HARMED, UNLESS IT DEPARTS THIS INTERNATIONAL GAME. THE CHADIAN 
PROBLEM HAS BEEN EXPLOITED AND CHAD HAS BEEN TURNED INTO A BASE. 
THEREFORE, IT IS MEANT TO BE KEPT IN A WHIRLPOOL SO AS TO HAVE A 
JUSTIFICATION FOR INTERFERENCE IN ITS AFFAIRS. THOSE BEING HARMED 
ARE THE CHADIAN PEOPLE. THEY HAVE BEEN ENDURING CIVIL WARS FOR 30 
YEARS.
