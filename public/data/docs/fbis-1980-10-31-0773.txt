LIBYA

COMMENTARY ATTACKS ‘AGENT REGIMES,’ SEVERANCE OF TIES 
LD310055 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland 2315 GMT 30 Oct 80 
[Unattributed commentary ] 


[Excerpts] The reactionary agents in the Arab homeland are now facing the hour which 
they tried at all costs to avoid. Not only have they been exposed, but their inevitable 
doom is drawing near. They have been fully exposed and their records of disgrace and 
betrayals have been revealed, and have been denounced by the masses whom they disre- 
garded and with whose fates they played havoc. 


The reactionary agents are trying to prop up their collapsing wall with the bases and 
the forces of their master: the imperialist United States. Thus we see the United 
States establishing such a large number of American military bases in Arab territory. 


Naturally the oppressed popular masses are not the ones who asked the United States to 
set up the military bases or send its fleets to Arab waters. 


It is not unprecedented for the reactionary agents who stand against the aims and aspira- 
tions of the Arab nation to make decisions behind the backs of the masses with a view to 
dividing ranks and casting suspicions. Their black history abounds with such decisions 
and plots which aim at sowing the seeds of alienation among the Arab masses. 


When the reactionary agents announce the severance of relations with another Arab people, 
they have no proof that one single member of the popular masses agrees with their deci- 
sion or wishes it or knew of it in advance. 


The reactionary regime which declares severance of relations with an Arab people only 
sentences itself to further choking and killing withdrawal. This makes the popular 
masses realize more fully and deeply the true subversive role played by the reactionary 
agent regimes which make decisions behind the backs of the masses to divide ranks, to 
separate, and to consolidate the artificial regional borders at a time when the popular 
masses look forward to unity and to the blowing up of the borders.

These foolish precedents of reaction and its agents prove more fully and deeply that 
relations must exist between the peoples themselves over the debris of the traditional 
political relations between the dictatorial regimes which rule in the name of the people. 


Who was the one who decided to sever relations with the Jamahiriyah people, those free 
people who possess the wealth, authority and arms and who rule themselves by themselves 
and have destroyed once and for all the traditional governmental forms, concepts and 
oppressive relations? 


Those who decided to sever relations are the reactionary regimes whose relations are in 
fact severed with the suppressed peoples in their own countries. These same agent 
regimes which had previously decided to present the Arab territory to the United States 
to build on it military bases to threaten the entire Arab nation and to try to occupy 
the entire Arab homeland are the ones that decided to sever relations. 


The decision to sever relations was made by these same reactionary agent regimes which 
did nothing for the peoples except suppression, illiteracy, poverty and sickness. 


They are the same reactionary agent regimes that colluded with Zionism and imperialism 
so that Palestine may be occupied and the cause of the Arab nation in Palestine be liquidated. 


These reactionary agent regimes cannot delay for one single hour the march of the popular 

masses throughout the Arab homeland, that pan-Arab unionist march which will crush 

reaction and establish on the ruins of the agent regimes and their dictatorial exploitative 

relations, the new socialist society--the society of the people's authority and the age 

of the masses, the masses who alone possess the authority to make and implement decisions.
