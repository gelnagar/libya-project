Summary (AI): A Washington Times/AP article reports that West German and Italian officials said Libyan intelligence had in past years tipped off European authorities about terrorist strikes, but that this help apparently stopped in 1984 or 1985. The piece places this within debate over European reluctance to join U.S. sanctions against Qaddafi's Libya.
• German and Italian officials said Libyan intelligence agents tipped off European authorities in past years about terrorists planning strikes.
• An Italian source said the Libyan help stopped in 1984 or 1985: 'It seems they have taken a different attitude.'
• Col. Qaddafi's agents may have informed European authorities about movements of Palestinian group members out of favor in Libya.
• The article describes a claim that Qaddafi had intelligence relations with the CIA from 1969-74 and used the arrangement to prevent coups against him.
