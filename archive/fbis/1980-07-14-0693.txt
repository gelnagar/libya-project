LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI FRIDAY SERMON ON RELIGIOUS MATTERS 
LD121238 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 12 Jul 80 


[Excerpts] Tripoli--Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi said that the religious revolution, which 
was begun by the September revolution, aimed at correcting the Islamic practices which 
were contrary to the faith. For example, mosques should not be exploited for personal 
reasons or reasons of creed in the name of religion because this was not in the nature 
of the mosques, which belong to God. In his Friday sermon, at Jaddu mosque yesterday, 
he went on to say that mosques were not meant for bustling activities and dealing with 
temporal matters because if they were turned into places for discussing economic, social 


and political questions, they would not remain mosques but become a sort of administrative 
center. 


Referring to creeds he explained that the Koran was against creeds, such as the Maliki, 
Shafi'i, Abazi and others, for Islam was not supposed to have creeds, adding that creeds 
were copied from Christians, who tried to introduce schism among Muslims by fostering 
different allegiances and creeds in order to destroy the Islamic religion. 


He explained that the creeds and groups which emerged during and after the life of the 
Prophet were no more than political parties which had nothing to do with religion but 
were involved in a struggle for power unconnected with either religion or the Koran.

V. 14 Jul 80 NORTH AFRICA 


13 July Meeting 


LD131516 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 13 Jul 80 


[Text] Tripoli--The acting secretary of the Foreign Liaison Office today received 
Joshua Nkomo, minister of home affairs in Zimbabwe, who is presently visiting Libya. 

The two sides discussed the means of developing bilateral relations and the role resting 
on the shoulders of the two friendly people in African and world affairs.
