LIBYA

Iranian Ambassador Meets With C. ongress Secretary 
GF102028 Tehran International Service in Arabic 
1430 GMT 10 Apr 84 


[Text] Brother Mahmud Mahdi Shamlu, ambassador of the 
Islamic Republic of Iran to Libya, met in Tripoli yesterday with 
the general secretary of the General People’s Congress of the 
Libyan Jamahiriyah, who is considered to be the chairman of the 
legislative body in Libya. 


During the meeting, he delivered a message from Brother 
Hashemi-Rafsanjani, speaker of the Islamic Majlis, on the new 
crime of the Zionist regime in Iraq of using chemical weapons 
against the Islamic soldiers. 


The general secretary of the General People’s Congress affirmed 
his country’s support for the Islamic revolution in Iran and for 
rightful and legitimate stands of Islamic Iran on the imposed war. 
He expressed his hope that the regime ruling Iraq will fail very 
soon. He also expressed his personal desire to expand the friendly 
relations between the two countries. 


Egyptian Border Actions Show ‘Dictatorial’ Nature 
LD101939 Tripoli JANA in English 1759 GMT 10 Apr 84 


[Text] Ams‘aad, Rajab 10, April 10, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The Egyptian frontier authorities stopped today 
10 Egyptian families entering Egypt through the land road 
between Jamahiriyah and Egypt and refused them entry after 
suffering a degrading treatment by the authorities. Further, the 
Egyptian authorities refused to help the families to have some 
rest in front of the Egyptian gate, the children accompaning the 
families met the same treatment. Egyptian nationals banned by 
the Egyptian authorities from entering Egypt condemned Husni 
Mubarak policy of repressing the sons of Egypt and denying them 
their natural right to visit their country and relatives. They 
affirmed, in statements to the JA MAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY correspondent in Ams‘aad, [that] the Egyptian 
regime’s refusal and banning of the Egyptians working in Jama- 
hiriyah from entering Egypt confirm the dictatorial nature of the 
regime in Egypt and expose the falsehood of slogan it raises.
