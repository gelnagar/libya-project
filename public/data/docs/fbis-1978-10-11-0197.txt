MINISTRY RELEASES COMMUNIQUE ON BOUMEDIENE'S LETTER 


LDO70034Y Rabat Domestic Service in Arabic 2000 GMT 6 oct 78 LD 


[Excerpts] As is known, His Majesty King Hassan II sent a letter of protest to the Algerian 
president in the wake of the aggression against the region of Hassi Tilemsi. The Algerian 
president sent a reply to his majesty the king. In this respect, the Ministry of Information 
released a communique containing a few remarks on the letter from the Algerian president. 
Here is the text of the communique: 


The message through which President Houari Boumediene replied to his majesty's letter prompts 
the following basic remarks: 


First, President Boumediene wonders whether it is still possible to establish direct 
contact between Morocco and Algeria in order to clear up the political atmosphere in the 
Arab Maghreb region after the recent positions taken by the two sides respectively con- 
cerning the Palestinian question. President Boumediene questions this possibility as if, 
with regard to the Palestinian question, an insurmountable wall stands between Morocco 
and Algeria, which will necessarily lead to a stalemate in any dialog between them on 
the problem of tension in the Arab Maghreb; 


Morocco's position is clear: Morocco stands by the resolutions of the 1974 Rabat con- 
ference which, contrary to the 1967 Khartoum conference, opened the door for initiatives 
for a peace based on justice, and gave the green light for action toward an inclusive 
settlement of the problem of the Arab-Israeli conflict, on the basis of justice for the 
legitimate rights of the people; Morocco has never swerved from this line, unlike Algeria, 
which has considered that it has the right to adhere to rejection, thus adopting the posi- 
tion of a minority of member of the Arab League, while the majority of the league's 

member states still adopt Morocco's position, loyal to the Rabat summit resolutions, 


Nonetheless, Morocco's desire for peace leads it to believe in tackling the tension 
prevailing in the region in a positive spirit, unaffected by differences of attitudes 
to the Arab issue. 


Second, the Algerian president's message reaffirms the attachment of Algeria to the Ifren 
treaty, and to the 1972 conventions which, in fact, constitute a framework for an inclusive 
settlement of the border disputes and lay the foundations for overcoming them in the con- 
text of comprehensive economic cooperation. At that time, Morocco obtained all guarantees 
and affirmations from Algeria that the latter would help it achieve its territorial unity 
and retrieve the rest of its usurped parts, foremost of which is the Western Sahara,

Why, after that, did Algeria go back on its position? What made Algeria brandish other 
slogans that contradict national territorial unity, after blessing the intention of Morocco 
and Mauritania to liberate the Sahara at the Nouadhibou conference and at subsequent meetings? 
Algeria's president declared at the Rabat conference that he blessed the Moroccan-Mauri- 
tanian agreement, and that Algeria had no ambitions regarding the Sahara, regarding what 

the soil and subsoil of the Sahara contain--as he put it. 


Third, Morocco is delighted to see that the message of President Boumediene lauds Moroccan- 
Algerian cooperation and its achievements in the past, in the interest of the two peoples 
and to see that the message contained nostalgia for the period of cooperation between the 
two countries, a period distinguished by mutual understanding and coordination of efforts. 
However, we are entitled to wonder; Does this cooperation accord with Algeria expelling 
40,000 Moroccan citizens recently, citizens who coexisted with their Algerian brothers on 
the soil of Algeria, for just one crime: their being Moroccan nationals? That came at a 
time when Morocco was host to an important Algerian colony, enabling them to earn a living 
in Morocco and treating Algerians in a manner reflecting the true spirit of brotherhood which 
Morocco nurses toward the brotherly people of Algeria, Furthermore, does this cooperation 
accord with organizing gangs, and equipping them in order to attack Morocco at home and 
kill innocent people in their own land and territory? Not content with this, Algeria 
launches its regular army into attacks against Moroccan territory, which is not disputed 
even by Algeria, in accordance with the 1972 conventions to which Boumediene adheres~-as 

he said, 


Fourth, we agree with the Algerian president that the Saharan problem must be solved through 
political means, But the Algerian president, in these conditions, should choose between 
political means and the means of encouraging aggression, and even taking part in ager ssion, 
so that he can be logical with himself and loyal to his conceptions. 


Lastly, we are open to dialog, we accept any mediation and we welcome the forthcoming out- 
come of the action of the African wise men [African Wise Men Committee, charged by the OAU 
to look into the Sahara problem) regarding the question of existing tension, 
But like the Algerian president, we have principles too, to which we are attached, and to 
which we give priority in our political options, The most sacred of these principles is 
the defense of the integrity of our national territory and our sovereignty.
