18 March 1985 

MEMORANDUM FOR: National Intelligence Officer for NESA 

FROM: Director of Central Intelligence 
SUBJECT: SNIE 30/2-85: Iran, Libya, Syria: Prospects 

for Increased Radical Cooperation 

I sent you a note this morning saying I liked the Key Judgments 
in your estimate on Iran/Syria. I have since read the comments of the 
Senior Review Panel and the comments in 4a. and 4b. may deserve your 
attention. I am always inclined to favor and reflect on important 
elements in discussion in the Key Judgments which may be all our larger 
customers ever read. 

William J. Casey 

20X11
