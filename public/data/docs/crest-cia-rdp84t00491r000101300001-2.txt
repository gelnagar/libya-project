Summary (AI): A 1984 NPIC imagery note reports severe burn and blast damage at the Al Abyar ammunition storage depot in eastern Libya, near Benghazi, apparently from multiple explosions. The location is far from Tripoli, so it has no direct bearing on the Tripoli map.
• Al Abyar lies about 25 nm ENE of Benghazi and about 250 nm west of the Egyptian border.
• Elements of a BMP battalion normally housed at the depot were parked just outside the fenceline.
• About 150 personnel were in a troop formation on the parade apron in the support area.
• No damage or unusual activity was seen at Al Abyar about 2 nm west of the depot.
• Coordinates given: 32-12-30N 020-36-20E.
• 1984-04-03: Multiple explosions caused large burn and blast marks and debris across open storage areas and destroyed several buildings; clean-up with four front-end loaders was under way.
