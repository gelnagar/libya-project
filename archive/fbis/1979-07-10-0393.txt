LIBYA

PEOPIE'S CONGRESS IEZADER RECEIVES SDAR PRIME MINISTER 
ID092048 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1530 GMT 9 Jul 79 ID 
[Text ] Tripoli, 9 July--tAbd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary general of the General People's 
Congress, received at his office this morning Mohamed Lamine Ould Ahmed, prime minister 


of the Sahran Democratic Arab Republic who is currently onavisit to the Jamahiriyah. 


The meeting was attended by the head of the Arab Liaison Office at the General Secretariat 
of the General People's Congress.
