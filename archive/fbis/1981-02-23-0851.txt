LIBYA

PDRY LEADER ARRIVES ON OFFICIAL VISIT 20 FEB 


LD201804 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1655 GMT 20 Feb 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 Feb (JANA)--At noon today Brother ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, secretary general 
of the YSP, chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme People's Council and prime minister 

of the PDRY, arrived here for a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
[SPLAJ]. Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud welcomed ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad and the delegation 
accompanying him at Tripoli international airport. The brother secretaries of the general 
people's committees and the heads of diplomatic missions in the Jamahiriyah were also 

there to welcome him. 


Brother ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad made a statement to JANA in which he expressed happiness at 
being in the SPLAJ, pointing out that he would exploit his visit for fraternal talks on 
the development of relations between the two countries. He said that .is visit was an 
extension of previously exchanged visits and expressed the hope and confidence that it 
would contribute to the consolidation of the struggle and links between the Yemeni and 
Libyan revolutions, and would open a wide scope for the development of these links in the 
future. 


Brother ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad confirmed that his country fully supported the Jamahiriyah 
against the intrigues of imperialist and reactionary forces in the region and against 

the provocations of the ruling regime in Egypt. He said we highly appreciate the great 
changes achieved by the Libyan people. He explained that during the visit he would hold 
consultations on the current Arab situation and in particular on the dangers resulting 
from Camp David and the role of the steadfastness and confrontation in aborting this plot. 
He will also discuss the recovery of the occupied Arab territories, support for the 
Palestinian struggle for self-determination, and the setting up of its own state. 


He pointed out that among the vital problems which he would be discussing during this 

visit were ways of confronting U.S. colonialist moves and checking the dangers of U.S. 
bases in Oman, Somalia, Egypt and the Indian Ocean; the importance of the bringing together 
of our two countries' efforts aimed at the liquidation of colonialist military presence, 
and creating conditions suitable for making the region one of peace, stability and 
cooperation among its peoples. 


Joint Communique on Visit 
LD220000 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 2015 GMT 21 Feb 81 


[Joint communique issued 21 February on the 20-21 February visit to the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah by ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, YSP Central Committee secretary general 
and chairman of the Supreme People's Council Presidium and of the Council of Ministers of 
the PDRY] 


[Text] At the invitation of brother Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 1 September 
Revolution, Brother ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, secretary general of the YSP Central Committee, 
chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme People's Council and chairman of the Council of 
Ministers of the PDRY, paid a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah from 

20 to 21 February 1981. The visit comes within the framework of the meetings and consul- 
tations carried out by the leaderships in the two sister countries in confirmation of 

their ties of fraternity and joint struggle, strengthening the fraternal relations between 
the two sister countires and the service of their joint objectives and interests, as well 

as in the cause of defending just issues of the Arab nation and maintaining its dignity 

and realizing the aims of its masses.

Brothers Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of 1 September Revolution, and ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, 
secretary general of the YSP Central Committee, chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme 
People's Council and chairman of the Council of Ministers, held talks in an atmosphere 
marked by a spirit of fraternity and cordiality. The talks dealt with bilateral relations, 
Arab issues and new developments on the international level. 


They expressed their satisfaction with the firm fraternal relations in all spheres between 
the two sister countries and with the growth of cooperation in accordance with the agree- 
ments concluded between the two countries. These agreements respond to the aspirations 

of the two peoples in realizing progress and prosperity. 


They confirmed their determination to expand domains of cooperation and to establish ways 
to reinforce and consolidate them in order to reach the aspired objectives and to consoli- 
date the ties of struggle which bring together the Libyan and Yemeni revolutions in the 
service of the supreme interests of the Arab nation. 


Reviewing the situation in the Arab homeland, the two sides agreed that the current phase 
through which the Arab nation is passing is one of the most serious phases of struggle 

in its life against the old and neocolonialism, Zionism and reaction. They stressed the 
need to continue the struggle against the Camp David accords. They also expressed their 
principled and full support to rendering all forms of help to consolidate the victory of 
the just cause of the Palestinian Arab people, especially their right to the return, 
self-determination and establishment of their own independent state on their national soil 
under the leadership of the PLO, their sole, legitimate representative. In that respect 
the two sides confirmed the need to consolidate the role of the National Front for Stead- 
fastness and Confrontation and to develop relations of struggle among its members. They 
backed the Arab people's congress as well as the strengthening of the Arab struggle 
against colonialism and Zionism. 


The two sides are of the opinion that it is important to hold a summit conference of the 
National Front for Steadfastness and Confrontation as soon as possible. 


The two sides stressed the importance of awakening and mustering the capabilities of the 
Arab liberation forces and of reinforcing the alliance among their various units to oppose 
the fierce colonialist and Zionist assault, and to work to liquidate the U.S.' aggressive, 
military bases in Egypt, Oman and Somalia. 


The two sides fully supported the struggle of the Egyptian people, as well as their national 
and progressive forces, to topple the regime of agent As-Sadat. They also fully supported 
the struggle of the Omani people under the leadership of the National Front for the Libera- 
tion of Oman in order to establish a national regime that contributes toward the battles 

of destiny of the Arab nation, to the fore of which is the armed struggle to liberate the 
occupied Arab lands and to achieve victory in the Palestine issue. 


The two sides strongly condemned the United States' joint maneuvers in Egypt and Oman as 
constituting a serious threat to the independence and sovereignty of the Arab countries. 
Brother leader Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi expressed his support for the initiative of Brother 
"Ali Nasir Muhammad to call a summit conference for the states of the Gulf area, the 
Arabian Peninsula and the Horn of Africa to discuss the liquidation of foreign military 
presence as being a positive step toward bringing security and stability in those regions. 


The two sides dealt with the situation in Lebanon. They expressed their solidarity with 
sister Lebanon. They confirmed their care for its independence, national sovereignty and 
integrity, which is being threatened by the Zionist enemy.

They stressed their full support for sister Syria in foiling the internal and external 
maneuvers aimed at harming its steadfastness. 


They also expressed their great anxiety over the situation in the Western Sahara, the 
result of foreign occupation. They renewed their support for and consolidation with the 
struggle of the Saharan people under the leadership its sole legitimate representative, 
the Polisario Front, in the cause of exercising their right to self-determination and 
independence. 


Brothers Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and President ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad saluted the Saharan 
Democratic Arab Republic's fifth anniversary. 


The two sides supported the Soviet Union's initiative, announced by Leonid Brezhnev in 
India. It comprises a call for rendering the Gulf and Indian Ocean as areas free from 
any foreign military presence, as well as not involving those areas in the establishment 
of alliances and activities that may lead to disturbing security and peace. 


The two sides expressed their endearment for the Ethiopian people's support for the strug- 
gle of the Arab peoples. They confirmed their solidarity with the Ethiopian revolution. 
The two sides called for the need for coordination and cooperation between the National 
Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation as well as the Arab peoples and the Ethiopian 
revolution in the cause of reinforcing the liberation struggle against Zionism, racism, 
and colonialism. 


The two sides expressed their serious concern at the endeavors being made by colonialist 
and reactionary forces to divide the African states, which are aggravating the situation 

in Africa and hindering the efficiency of the OAU. The two sides condemned those endeavors 
as well as any other direct or indirect interference on the part of those forces in the 
internal affairs of the African independent states. In that respect the two sides urged 
those colonialist and reactionary forces to halt flagrant interference in African affairs, 
not because the interference constitutes violation of the OAU and UN Charters, but because 
it forms a maneuver which aims at hindering the great progress the OAU has achieved in 

the course of the last 18 years in resolving African issues. 


In that respect the two sides condemned the flagrant French and U.S. interference in the 
affairs of the African Continent. 


The two sides stressed their consolidation with and support for the legitimate Chad Govern- 
ment of National Union under President Goukouni Oueddei. They confirmed their condemnation 
of the colonialist and reactionary forces which are trying to harm the victories being 
achieved by the people of Chad. They warned those states of the consequences of any 
aggression against the Republic of Chad. 


The two sides also stressed anew their full support for the national liberation movements 
in South Africa. They condemned racist South Africa and its colonialist allies on account 
of the failure of the Geneva talks. They strongly demanded an end to the illegitimate 
occupation of Namibia and the withdrawal of the forces of the racist regime of South Africa 
from Namibia, in accordance with the UN resolutions in that respect, and the granting of 
true independence to the country under the leadership of SWAPO, the sole legitimate repre- 
sentative of the Namibian people. 


Brother Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 1 September Revolution, affirmed the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah's continued consolidation and support for the people in 
the PDRY in building their new life and confronting all fo ms of conspiracies and threats 
of colonialism and reaction and for the cause of bringing about the unity of the two parts 
of Yemen by peaceful means.

Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi lauded the efforts being exerted by the PDRY in that direction. 


The two sides expressed their satisfaction with the positive results reached in the course 
of the visit. They stressed the importance of continued meetings between the political 
leaderships of the two sister countries. 


Brother ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad expressed his gratitude and great appreciation for the brotherly 
welcome accorded him and the delegation accompanying him. He extended an invitation to 

his brother Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 1 September Revolution, to visit 
the PDRY. He accepted the invitation with thanks. The date will be fixed later. 


READINESS, CONDITIONS FOR SOVIET TREATY REPORTED 


NC221800 Paris AFP in English 1747 GMT 22 Feb 81 


[Text] Damascus, 22 Feb (AFP)--Libya is prepared to sign a treaty of friendship and co- 
operation with Moscow similar to the Syrian-Soviet accord if the treaty guaranteed Libya's 
independence and sovereignty, Libyan Justice Minister Muhammad al-Qasim az-Zvwayy [as received] 
said here today. 


In a statement to AGENCE FRANCE-PRESS, Mr Zwai also said that Tripoli might sign a treaty 
with Moscow if Libya was faced with conditions similar to those that confronted Syria 
prior to the signing of its accord with the Soviet Union. He did not elaborate. On 
February 15, Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi said Libya would subscribe to the Syrian- 
Soviet treaty after a union between Libya and Syria had been established. 


CHAD REPORTEDLY REJECTS LIBYAN WITHDRAWAL OFFER 


LD201224 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 20 Feb 81 


[Text] Algiers, 20 Feb (JANA)--The Chadian foreign minister, Acyl Ahmat, has said: the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has proposed to the Chadian national government 
to withdraw its forces from Chadian territory, but we refused to accept such a proposal 
because the freedom of our people is threatened. 


In an interview with the Algerian EL MOUDJAHID published yesterday he said: Thanks to the 
Libyan assistance we were able to put an end to the routed rebellious forces. He pointed 
out that the Jamahiriyah was the only country which responded to our call for help to put 
an end to the mutiny which was backed by the As-Sadat regime. He stressed that there is a 
big difference between the Libyan assistance, which we had asked for, and the colonialist 
forces which are stationed in certain African countries for the purpose of disturbing 
Stability. The Chadian minister stressed that Chad will have the Libyan forces remain on 
its territory so long as its security remains threatened. 


JANA REAFFIRMS SUDANESE-LIBYAN LETTER EXCHANGE 


LD221940 Tripoli JANA in English 1542 GMT 22 Feb 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 22 Feb (JANA)--The Sudanese minister of state for external affairs said 
reports that the Sudanese President Ja'far Numayri received a letter from Colonel al-Qadhdhafi 
on the rearming of the Sudanese Army, land reclamation aid and the opening of new vistas 

of cooperation with the Sudan, are untrue. 


The JAMAHRIYAH NEWS AGENCY once again reaffirms that letters were directly exchanged with 
President Numayri and agreement was reached via the Sudanese ambassador in Tripoli on the 
reorganization and rearmament of the Sudanese Armed Forces, and the Jamahiriyah's contribu- 
tion to Sudan's land reclamation programmes and the opening of new horizons of cooperation 
between both countries.

"REVOLUTIONARY FORCE' TAKES OVER JANA PARIS OFFICE 


LD220900 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 22 Feb 81 


[Statement by the "revolutionary force in France in control of the JANA office in Paris"; 
date of issue not given] 


[Text] In accordance with the wishes of the commander of the great 1 September Revolu- 
tion concerning control of the information media by the forces of the vigilant revolu- 
tion, the revolutionary forces in France have rallied together and have taken control of 
the JANA office in Paris. The Jamahiriyah's revolutionary information has become an 
urgent and imperitive necessity for keeping up with the movement of the revolution and 
for enriching the revolutionary information experiment. The revolutionary forces in 
France have accepted the law of the revolution and its legitimacy and regard the call 
and propagation of the objectives of the great 1 September Revolution outlined in the 
speech of the commander of the revolution on the llth anniversary of the great 

1 September Revolution as an objective guide. Therefore, the revolutionary force has 
decided the following: 


1. The information experiment led by the revolution force internally must also be 
reflected in the information centers abroad. 

2. The information centers abroad must exercise the popular aspect of information. 

3. One of the brothers has been assigned to run the JANA office. The revolution force 
will continue to mobilize itself in serving the center until it achieves the kind of 
information that will keep abreast of the revolution march internally and externally. 
Forward. The great 1 September Revolution will always prevail. 

[Signed] The revolution force in France in control of the JANA office in Paris.
