SOVIET TECHNICIANS REPORTEDLY WILL GO TO LIBYA
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp78s01932a000100090033-3
https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-cia-rdp78s01932a000100090033-3

Summary (AI): A September 1974 CIA operations cable reports that Soviet technicians would soon go to Libya, mainly to train Libyans to use Soviet-supplied missiles, tanks and air defence equipment. It estimates the figure of 3,000 Soviet personnel is too high, and says Tripoli set up a special police unit to watch them.
• A substantial Soviet-Libyan arms deal was signed in spring 1974.
• Estimate of about 3,000 Soviet personnel is considered too high; comparison: about 1,200 Soviet military technicians and advisers in Syria and 800 in Iraq.
• The withdrawal of the Egyptian military advisory mission may have prompted Tripoli to accept more Soviet personnel.
• Libyans reportedly told Moscow that the technicians would be under surveillance.
• 1974-09-24: Reported arrival of Soviet military technicians in Libya for training Libyans on Soviet-supplied air defence missiles and tanks; technicians to be rotated every three months and watched by a new Tripoli special police unit.
