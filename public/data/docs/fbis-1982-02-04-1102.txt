LIBYA

GOVERNMENT PROTESTS U.S. ‘AIR PIRACY ACT' 


LDO31900 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1745 GMT 3 Feb 82 


[Text] The U.S. administration has perpetrated an aggressive terrorist incident against 
a passenger airliner belonging to the Libyan Arab Airlines Company as part of its provocative 
aggressive designs against the Libyan Arab people. 


Two U.S. Phantom-14's carried out an air piracy act last Sunday evening. They intercepted 
the Libyan Arab airliner inside Greek airspace, precisely 30 miles southeast of Athens, 
while on routine flight No 153 from Athens to Tripoli. 


The two planes took off from one of the American aircraft carriers in the area. They 
carried out provocative acts and flew over the Libyan airliner and chased it for more than 
7 miles. 


The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah protested the barbaric act by the U.S. administra- 
tion by summoning the Belgian ambassador -- the Belgian Embassy looks after U.S. interests -- 
and handing him a strongly worded protest memorandum to be conveyed to the U.S. administration. 
The general secretary for communications at the Peoples Bureau for Foreign Liaison informed 

the Belgian ambassador that the barbaric terrorist act carried out by U.S. aircraft against 

the Libyan civilian passenger aircraft is regarded as a provocative act and is an open threat 

to the safety of civil aviation as well as contrary to international aviation rules. 


The Greek charge d'affaires today was summoned and informed of the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah's strong protest against the piratic act carried out by the two U.S. 
military aircraft against the Libyan passenger aircraft inside Greek airspace and, in that 
connection, a reminder was made of the dangers posed by foreign military bases in the region 
on the security and safety of countries neighboring Greece and the Mediterranean littoral 
countries. 


The Peoples Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison today summoned the UAE 
charge d'affaires -- the UAE looks after the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
interests in the United States -- where the general secretary for communications handed 
him a copy of the protest memorandum to be submitted to the U.S. administration. 


The Socialist People's Libyan Jamahiriyah today informed the UN Security Council and the 
United Nations of the incident which the American administration planned against Libyan 
Arab Airlines and sent them a memorandum containing a strong protest against the U.S. 
administration's flagrant violation of international law and against the acts of terrorism 
which this administration is carrying out against the Jamahiriyah. The memorandum stated 
that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah regards the action to which the Libyan 
passenger aircraft was subjected as a hostile act directed against it and against the 
safety of civil aviation, a fact which constitutes a xiolation of the principles and spirit 
of international agreements.

The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah asked the chairman of the UN Security 
Council and the UN secretary general to regard its memorandum as one of the documents 

the Security Council and the UN General Assembly distributes among member countries, and 
to inform them of the danger which the U.S. administration's terrorist course represents 
and the continued attempts of that administration to escalate its aggressive campaigns 
against the Jamahiriyah people. [In an earlier, briefer report, Tripoli Domestic Service 
in Arabic at 1330 GMT 3 February says Libya "asked the council to condemn these terrorist 
practices" of the U.S. administration. ] 


The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has also protested to the International Air 
Transport Association [IATA] about the terrorist act by the American fighter planes 
against a Libyan civilan aircraft and asked for the adoption of a firm stand denouncing 
American terrorism. 


JANA EDITOR SCORES U.S. OFFICIAL'S STATEMENTS 


LD031952 Tripoli JANA in English 1441 GMT 3 Feb 82 


[Text] Washington, 3 Feb (JANA) -- The Voice of America yesterday quoted a statement made 
by a U.S. official on Husni Mubarak's visit to Washington in which he said that Mubarak's 
talks will concentrate on several issues such as security in the Middle East, the 
Jamahiriyah's role in the Organisation of African Unity, the situation in Sudan and Chad, 
the friendship treaty signed by the Jamahiriyah, Democratic Yemen and Ethiopia, and what 
was described as the latest Libyan activities. 


JANA's political editor commented on these statements saying that "the political impli- 
cation behind these statements is quite clear and does not need any elaboration if we 
consider the U.S. objectives reflected in her renewed intentions to launch an aggression 
on Libya. 


"The U.S. administration would like to make of Egypt a tool for its expansionist policy 
the way it did during the rule of As-Sadat who acted according to U.S. instructions and 
directives in reinforcing the hostile alliance with the Numayri regime against the 
Jamahiriyah, Chad and Africa in general. 


"Through these open statements, the U.S. administration would like to reveal the continuation 
of pressures on Egypt after the death of As-Sadat and to direct Egypt to maintain flash 
points in the region and make Egypt isolated from her Arab sisters. 


"The statements of the U.S. official deviously imply to contain [as received] Egypt and 
push her in As-Sadat's course. Such open U.S. blackmail represents a flagrant inter- 
vention in Egypt's internal affairs and at the same time gives an example of ten [as 
received] premeditated hostile intentions of U.S. administration against the Libyan 
Arab people and their revolution.
