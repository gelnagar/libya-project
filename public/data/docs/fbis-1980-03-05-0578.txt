LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SPEAKS ON ANNIVERSARY OF JAMAHIRIYAH 


LD031800. Tripoli JANA in English 0810 Q@MT 3 Mar 80 LD 


("Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's Third Amiversary Speech"-—-JANA headline] 


[Text] Tripoli, March 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader 
of the grand al-Fatah revolution, said the Libyan Arab peoples who were becoming armed 
are well aware of the legal aspect for the use of arms against others in self defence. 
When they feel besieged, he added, they will assume their legislative responsibility of 
defence relying on all the historic precedents, international law and the aspects of 
aggression. This was part of Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's speech to thousands of Libyans who 
rallied tonight in Green Square, Tripoli, in commemoration of March 2, the third anniver- 
sary of the declaration of the Jamahiriyah and the establishment of people's authority. 


He added: "When any Arab territory is exposed to foreign aggression, it is the duty of 
Libyans, as part of the Arab nation, to use all the forces in defence of Arab territory, 
whether it is requested or not. In such a case the slogan becomes the national spirit 
of the battle and the reality becomes the reality of the Arab nation. Hence, there can 
be no consideration of artificial boundaries set up by imperialism." 


Colonel Mu'tammar al-Qadhdhafi discussed the Soviet Union's position regarding the Arab 
cause. He said the USSR recognises the Palestinians, yet does not extend similar recog- 
nition to the (Israelis). He said they provide arms to the Arabs, yet withhold the supply 
of weapons to (Israel). Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the Soviet Union, as a friend of the 
Arabs, is prepared to supply all Arabs with arms. 


In his speech delivered on the third anniversary of the declaration of people's authority 
and proclamation of the Jamahiriyah, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said intervention in the Arab 
world is not a specialty of the Soviet Union, but is more of a practice in which the 
United States is conversant. After all, America has recently declared the formation of 

a 100-thousand-strong rapid deployment force. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the function of the above-mentioned force is intervention 
in foreign territories which are not America's, and the first region in which the United 
States contemplates intervention is purportedly the Arabian Gulf. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi wondered under what right does the United States intend to intervene 
in this particular region. Who says the Arabian Gulf is threatened? He said it is 
America alone which claims that the region is threatened by the Soviet Union. 


He added that any Arabian Gulf state which asks for American intervention in the region 
is an enemy of the Arab nation, the Palestinians and all other peoples, because anyone 

asking for such action to be taken is in fact calling for (Israeli) intervention. Any 

ally of the United States is an ally of (Israel).

He said that through our own will we shall seek alliance with whomsoever we desire. 
Danger now exists for the Palestinian cause andthe fate of the Arab nation. He said 
anyone who seeks alliance with America casts great danger on the fate of the Arab nation. 


He added: "The greatest mistake is for the friends and allies of America to believe 
that they are against (Israel). No friend of the United States could by definition be 
an enemy of (Israel). 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi then dealt with the positions of the United States and the Soviet 
Union regarding the Palestinian cause. 


He said the Soviet Union recognizes the Palestinians where as it does not extend this 
recognition to (Israel). On the other hand, America considers negotiations with the 
Palestinians as unthinkable, yet the Palestinians are free at any time to seek aid from 
the Soviet Union. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the USSR arms the Arabs and boycotts the flow of arms to (Israel). 
Americs has commenced the supply of arms to As-Sadat efter it has assured itself of his 
anti-A: ab credentials. 


He said America arms As-Sadat who is considered a hireling and enemy of the Arabs, but it 
desists from arming the Egyptians for fear that they might inadvertently support thou- 
sands of future 'Abd an-Nasir's who would turn out to be staunch enemies of American 
colonialism and (Israel). 


He said: "Our enmity towards America does not impinge on her reputation, but comes as 

a result of her enmity towards the Arabs. America has constantly armed (Israel), 
fought the Arabs, exhausted their oil, totally plundered their wealth and trod upon the 
Arabs with its absolute alignment with the (Israelis), who own neither oil nor wealth. 
It took no account of the Arabs who supply America with oil and funds, and whose markets 
are wide open to American products." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the United States refuses to supply Arabs with arms at a time 
when it is doing exactly this with (Israel). 


Regarding this point Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed to the Soviet Union's position vis-a- 
vis the Arabs, he said the USSR is a friend of the Arabs while America is their enemy. 
This is clear, because the Soviet Union is willing to supply all Arabs with arms. 


He said the USSR is not the ally of (Israel). America is the true ally, defender and 
mentor of (Israel). 


He said intervention in the Arab world is not the specialty of the Soviet Union but 
something in which the United States is more conversant, for it has just announced the 
formation of a 100-thousand-strong rapid deployment force. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi wondered aboyt the nature of Islam which America seeks to protect 
and respect, pointing tc Carter's statement which notes his readiness to defend the 
Muslim faith. 


He said: "God, according to America's friends is not the one and almighty, but appears 
to be President Carter. Their 'kaba! is not the one we know of in Mecca, but the White 
House of Washington."

"Libyans are a master people because they possess authority, wealth and arms,"’ the leader 
of the grand Al-Fatah Revolution Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi declared,

They do not know that the Jamahiriyah is the authority of the masses (jamahir), and the

He stressed that what is happening in Libya now is a practical expression of the use 
of the authority's potential by the masses, the same potentials which were previously 
owned by the government. 


He said that after the establishment of the people's authority, authority fell into 
the hands of the people as the masses themselves are exploiting their potentials and 
using them to reinforce their authorities. 


He said that the governments which try to solve their presence in various ways, are 
using the potentials o’ their authority to serve and glorify themselves. He pointed 
out that the idea of giving governments the potential to protect the masses and es- 
tablish public security is a superstition which does not stand for it and was surpassed 
by the historic and amazing development of the masses in this world. He added that 

the potentials of all the authorities which were established in Libya since the Ottoman 
era up to after the false independence were today restored to the Libyan Arab people 

so that they may inherit from them, from one generation to another. 


He pointed out that the police repressive measures imposed on the peoples are shameful 
and dirty. "Every time the people's masses launch a peopie's revolution and gain access 
to new economic, political and defence capabilities, we feel that the freedom is re- 
inforced," he said. The leader of the grand Al-Fatah Revolution, Colonel Mu'ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi, said that the Libyan Arab people, who are becoming an armed people, know the 
legitimate aspect of using force against someone else. 


If they feel that they are besieged, they have the right to use force to liberate thean- 
selves. In this case they would be performing the duty of self-defence. 


If the Libyan people feel they are besieged, they have the right to perform the duty 
of legitimate self-defence, on the basis of the historic precedents and the force of 
international law. Under any circumstances can this be seen as aggression? 


He went on to say that when an Arab land is exposed, at any time, to a foreig aggres- 
sion, it becomes the duty of Libyans who are part of the Arab nation to use all bases 
to defend the Arab land, whether they are asked to do so or not. 


Then they would be acting under the slogan of the national battle, this reality would 
be that of the Arab nation. 


Then no consideration would be given to the artificial boundaries which were set up 
by imperialism. 


He stressed that Arab capabilities must be devoted to confront any aggression and that 
the Libyan Arab force cannot hesitate in engaging in the national battle to defend the 
Arab land. 


He said that the question of revolution in these states was not one of the tasks of the 
Libyan people, because the latter does not export revolution, and does not fight on 
behalf of other peoples. 


Peoples who expect armed Libyans to enter their territories and liberate them are people 
who do not deserve to live but to always suffer the domination of their despots. 


He said that allegations that Libyans are going to invade Tunisia, Egypt, or Chad are 
lies and fairy tales.

When the Egyptian people carried out revenge operations in 1976 and 1977, operations

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that the reason behind these allegations and fabrications

admit these popular uprisings are internal. 


Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that any foreign threat to or occupation of

Atlantic to the Gulf. The Libyan people are part of the Arab people and the freedom

The Arab nation is suffering from the absence of freedom, he said, and also from humil- 
iation as a result of their suppression of the (?freedom) of one of its peoples, the 
Palestinians, and because a part of the Arab world, Palestine, is occupied by a foreign 
force, 


We are ready for a confrontation with colonialism, he added and we will not stand idle 
in the face of any colonialist intervention in any Arab territory. Whether asked or 
not, we shall act to help any Arab country. If we become the martyrs of Arab national- 
ism and territories, we would have fulfilled our wishes. 


The defence of the Arab nationalism and the Arab land is an act of self-defence, Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi went on, and the colonialists agents should be reminded of that if they 
think they can rule, backed by the force of colonialism. 


He again stressed that in case of any foreig intervention in an Arab land, it becomes

how can foreign forces intervene and fight in certain Arab territories while denying 
this right to Arab forces, on Arab territories? 


Colonel Mu!'ammar al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that the responsibility for the revolution

where they work=-through a popular worker's administration which would enable them to

He pointed out that it is the hired worker who feels the necessity for the revolution

it is inconceivable for Libyans to revolt on behalf of the masses, against the despots, 
and to give them their freedom, because responsibility for the revolution remains that 
of those peoples, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi expressed confidence that these masses would be capable of revolu- 
tion and emancipation from their despots and bloodsuckers.

He stressed that the blood of the revolutionaries will not be in vain and that nothing

He emphasized that this day is imminent. It is nearer than the day of resurrection.

The leader of the grand al-Fatah revolution said, "We leave the responsibility of the

The Arab nation today faces one common enemy, he said: The (Israeli) enemy which

The Arab masses will get to know that their rulers who befriend America and ally with

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that those who invest in Egyptian banks are supplying
