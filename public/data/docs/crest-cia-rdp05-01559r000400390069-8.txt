Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a Tripoli JANA report from 31 March 1985 on Qadhdhafi's speech at the final session of the Pan-Arab Command of Arab Revolutionary Forces. The speech is a broad anti-Israel and anti-US address with no specific Tripoli sites, though it touches on Libya's arms purchases and the regional situation.
• The speech called for revolutionary violence against Israel and what it described as a tripartite enemy alliance backed by the United States.
• Qadhdhafi said progressive Arab states such as Libya and Syria still purchase arms from abroad and cannot manufacture these weapons.
• The speech referenced the Sabra and Shatila, Sidon, Beirut and southern Lebanon events, and the Bahr al-Baqr school bombing in Egypt.
• 1985-03-31: Qadhdhafi delivers speech at final session of the Pan-Arab Command of the Arab Revolutionary Forces, where the declaration, organizational statute and Decision No. 1 were read out

TRIPOLI JANA IN ARABIC 1850 GMT 31 MAR 85 
((TEXT)) TRIPOLI, 31 MAR (JANA) -- THE BROTHER LEADER OF THE 
REVOLUTION DELIVERED A SPEECH AT THE FINAL SESSION OF THE MEETING 
OF THE PAN-ARAB COMMAND OF THE ARAB REVOLUTIONARY FORCES IN WHICH 
THE DECLARATION AND THE ORGANIZATIONAL STATUTE AND DECISION 
NO. 1 OF THE PAN-ARAB COMMAND WERE READ OUT. THE BROTHER LEADER 
SAID IN HIS ADDRESS: 
WE ALL BELIEVE THAT FOR THE FIRST TIME IN THE MODERN HISTORY OF 
OUR NATION THIS NATION'S REVOLUTIONARIES AND STRUGGLERS MEET AT A 
PAN-ARAB LEVEL SUCH AS THIS IN ORDER TO DECIDE TO FIGHT AND PURSUE 
AND PRACTICE REVOLUTIONARY VIOLENCE IN ORDER TO LIBERATE AND UNITE 
THIS NATION. I FEEL THAT THIS MEETING DID NOT COME ABOUT AS A 
RESULT OF SENTIMENTAL REASONS OR AS A REACTION TO SOMETHING, BUT 
WAS AN INEVITABLE RESULT OF THIS NATION'S PAINS AND THE DIFFICULT 
PERIQD OF LABOR IT EXPERIENCED PRIOR TO GIVING BIRTH TO THIS ACTION. 
THE BROTHER LEADER ADDED: I BELIEVE THAT WE ARE MOTIVATED BY 
AN (2INNER) FEELING ALTHOUGH WE DO NOT REALIZE HOW DEEP IT IS. 
THE ENEMY HAS FINALLY CONVINCED US OF THE NECESSITY OF MEETING, 
OF TAKING ACTION, AND OF THE NECESSITY OF VIOLENCE. THIS IS 
BECAUSE THE PAINS ENDURED BY OUR NATION ARE VERY BITTER INDEED. 
SO MUCH SO THAT THIS NATION HAS BEEN HUMILIATED BEFORE ALL NATIONS 
OF THE WORLD. THE ENEMY HAS KILLED, MADE HOMELESS, AND CAPTURED 
MANY, UP TO TODAY ((WORD INDISTINCT)) 31 MARCH 1985. THE BROTHER 
LEADER REAFFIRMED THAT THE ENEMY'S ARROGANCE WILL CONTINUE AND WILL 
NOT STOP AT ANY LIMIT UNLESS WE PUT AN END TO IT. DO NOT BELIEVE 
THAT THE ENEMY WILL BE SATISFIED WITH HIS OPPRESSION OF THIS 
NATION, AND THE FACT HE IS TEARING IT ASUNDER; HE WILL NOT BE 
SATISFIED WITH HIS CONTEMPT FOR THIS NATION. HE WILL NOT BE 
SATISFIED AND WILL NOT DECLARED A HALT ON HIS OWN. THE FACT IS 
THAT THIS ENEMY IS NOT ONE ENEMY BUT A (?TRIPARTITE) ALLIANCE. 
THESE THREE ENEMIES WILL NOT STOP FEEDING ON THE FLESH OF THIS 
NATION AND WILL NOT STOP REGARDING THIS NATION AS PREY UNDER THEIR 
FEET UNLESS WE STOP THEM. IN ACTUAL FACT THE CONFRONTATION 
BETWEEN US AND THIS ENEMY HAS GONE BEYOND (?THE VARIOUS) METHODS, 
AND THERE IS NOTHING LEFT FOR US EXCEPT VIOLENCE. THIS IS BECAUSE 
THE ENEMY HAS ESCALATED HIS ACTIVITIES AGAINST US TO THIS DANGEROUS 
DEGREE. THE ENEMY NOW USES MASS ANNIHILATION; IN SABRA, SHATILA, 
SIDON, BEIRUT, SOUTHERN LEBANON, AT BAHR AL-BAQR SCHOOL ( (SCHOOL 
IN EGYPT WHICH THE ISRAELIS BOMBED IN THE WAR OF ATTRITION DURING 
THE TIME OF ABD AL-NASIR -- FBIS)) IT IS QUITE CLEAR ON EVERY FRONT 
THAT THE ENEMY HAS DECIDED TO ANNIHILATE THIS NATION. HES METHOD 
HAS BECOME THE METHOD OF ANNIHILATION. [T 1S NOT THE METHOD OF 
COLONIALISM WHICH SEEKS DOMINATION ONLY. WE HAVE BEEN EVICTED 
FROM OUR HOMES IN PALESTINE AND THE ENEMY NOW WANTS TO EVICT US 
FROM LEBANON, SYRIA AND JORDAN. HE IS CONTINUING TO DO SO. THE 
ZIONIST ENEMY WHO WORKS WITH ASSURANCE UNDER THE AMERICAN UMBRELLA 
[S NOW ENTERING INTO A BATTLE AGAINST US IN ORDER TO REMOVE US 
FROM OUR POSITIONS IN ORDER TO CONTROL OUR WATER RESOURCES. 
OUR BATTLE AGAINST THE ISRAELIS [S A BATTLE TO CONTROL THE WATER 
RESOURCES -- THE RESOURCES OF LIFE. HE WANTS TO OCCUPY THE ARAB 
RIVERS IN ORDER TO BRING MORE JEWS TO THE ARAB REGION. THIS CAN

THE BROTHER LEADER ALSO SAID THAT AFTER THIS BATTLE THE ENEMY 
WILL BEGIN A BATTLE AGAINST US IN ORDER TO CONTROL OUR SOURCES OF 
ENERGY. DURING THIS BATTLE THE ENEMY WILL ACHIEVE DOMINATION

THE BROTHER LEADER POINTED OUT THAT THE ENEMY USES AGAINST US 
A STRATEGIC DRAWN-OUT TACTIC WHICH USUALLY RESULTS IN EXHAUSTION. 
IT IS KNOWN IN MILITARY TERMS AS THE (?ARTICHOKE) TACTIC AND 
INVOLVES CONTINUING ((THE FIGHT -- FBIS)) UNTIL WE ARE TIRED AND 
FATIGUED. IN ACTUAL FACT THIS HAS BEEN ACCOMPLISHED WITH REGARD 
TO SOME (?COUNTRIES) WHICH HAVE BECOME TIRED OF CONFRONTATION 
AND HAVE BEGAN TO SEARCH FOR CAPITULATIONIST SOLUTIONS WITH THE 
ENEMY. THIS IS WHAT HAS ACTUALLY HAPPENED TO EGYPT; IN OTHER WORDS 
EGYPT FELL VICTIM TO THE TACTIC OF ATTRITION. THE ENEMY WANTS TO 
APPLY THE SAME THING TO SYRIA. IN YEARS HE WILL BE ABLE TO USURP 
SYRIA BY MAKING IT TIRED AND FATIGUED AND ULTIMATELY FORCING IT 
TO ASK FOR NEGOTIATIONS OVER THE GOLAN, AS HAPPENED AT STABLE DAVID. 
THE BROTHER LEADER SAID THAT THIS MAKES US THINK THAT IT IS 
NECESSARY TO CONSOLIDATE SYRIA'S STEADFASTNESS AND RAISE ITS 
MORALE AND INCREASE ITS MATERIAL CAPABILITY SO THAT IT WILL NOT 
BECOME FATIGUED AND SO THAT THIS ARTICHOKE TACTIC WILL NOT BE 
SUCCESSFUL. THE LEADER SAID THAT THE ENEMY WANTS TO CREATE 
CONTINUOUS DISTURBANCE AND COLLAPSE WITHIN THE INTERNAL ARAB 
FRONT BY INCITING SECTARIANISM WHICH NOW CONSTITUTES A DANGER THAT 
FACILITATES THE TASK OF THE ENEMY. THE LEADER ADDED THAT THE 
GOVERNMENTS HAVE FAILED IN THEIR CONFRONTATION; THE TRADITIONAL 
ARMIES HAVE FAILED; THE THEORIES OF OPPRESSION, EXPLOITATION, 
COMPULSORY MILITARY SERVICE, AND THE USE OF MERCENARIES HAVE 
FAILED. HE SAID WHAT MADE US MEET IN THIS HALL IS THE FAILURE 
OF THE THEORY OF OPPRESSION AND EXPLOITATION AND THE ENEMY'S 
‘METHOD WHICH REPRESENTS SUCH A DEGREE OF DANGER TO THE FUTURE OF 
THE ARAB NATION. EVEN THE INDIVIDUAL SUICIDAL OPERATIONS WHICH 
TOOK PLACE AGAINST THE AMERICAN, FRENCH AND ISRAELI FORCES IN 
LEBANON ARE IN FACT AN EXPRESSION OF THE FACT THAT THERE ARE NO 
CAPABILITIES AND METHODS OTHER THAN SUICIDE AND DEATH. IT WAS 
AN EXPRESSION OF SOME PEOPLE WHO WANTED TO AWAKEN THE SLEEPING 
SPIRIT OF THIS NATION. WHEN FIDA'I FROM THE JAPANESE RED ARMY 
CAME AND CARRIED OUT THE LOD OPERATION ((IN ISRAEL -- FBIS)) 
HE SAID THAT HE WANTED TO TEACH THE ARABS THE SPIRIT OF SACRIFICE. 
BY THAT COURAGEOUS OPERATION THAT JAPANESE SAW THAT THE SPIRIT 
OF SACRIFICE OF THE ARABS WAS LACKING AND ASLEEP AND WANTED TO 
AWAKEN IT. 
THE BROTHER LEADER POINTED OUT THAT THE INDIVIDUAL SUICIDAL 
OPERATIONS WHICH WERE CARRIED OUT BY OUR BROTHERS WERE AN 
EXPRESSION OF THIS METHOD AND THAT WE HAVE REACHED THE STAGE 
WHEREBY WE ARE READY TO DIE AS THESE INDIVIDUALS MINED THEMSELVES 
AND DIED. HE SAID THAT WE DO NOT SEE THESE AS ONLY HEROIC
