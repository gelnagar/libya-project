OFFICIAL EXPLAINS STATUS OF WASHINGTON 'PEOPLE'S OFFICE' PERSONNEL 
LD071314 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0980 GMT 7 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli--the secretary of the foreign liaison office has sent a cable to U.S. 
President Carter. Here is the text: 


President Jimmy Carter, President of the United States of America, Washington, 


In my capacity as secretary of the foreign liaison office and in charge of this task by 
the General People!s Congress, the assembly of people's congresses, people's committees 
and vocational congresses, representative of the people!s authority in the Jamahiriyah 
which has finally ended the government and all instruments of dictatorship and repression, 
I would like to inform you that the People's Committee at the People's Office in 
Washington is an extension of the political experiment applied in the Jamahiriyah, The 
members of the people!s committee are ordinary Libyan citizens, without diplomatic status, 
working for the development of good relations between the Libyan people and the American 
people. At this time, when members of the People's Office are carrying out their duties, 
we find that American police are cordoning off the People's Office and members of the 
People’s Committee who have committed no crime, 


In order to maintain relations between the people of the Jamahiriyah and your people we 
ask Your Excellency the President to immediately intervene and issue orders to the 
authorities concerned to lift the cordon from the Libyan People!s Office and its menbers 
so that they may be enabled to carry out their duties, Otherwise this will affect reia- 
tions between the two countries, 


ve 8 May 80 ia NORTH AFRICA 


[Signed] Ahmad ash-Shahhati, secretary of the Foreign Liaison Office at the Secretariat 
of the General People!s Congress, 


JANA: U.S, ESTABLISHES NEW MIDEAST INTELLIGENCE SERVICE 
LD071853 Tripoli JANA in English 1810 GMT 7 May 80 


[Text] Beirut, May 7, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--It has been revealed in the U.S. that a 
new American intelligence service has been set up in Lebanon and other Arab countries to 
replace the C.I.A. there. 


The objective of the new service is to safeguard American interests in the Arab region 
mainly in oil exporting countries, 


In 1976.the American ambassador to Beirut began the setting up of this apparatus which is 
directly connected to the Pentagon in collaboration with an American officer, 


The two men recruited Arab youths, doctors, university professors and businessmen, Main 
branches have been set up in Beirut, Sidon, the Lebanese towns of Tinjoh and Zahala, Abu 
Dhabi, Dubai, Oman, Tehran, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait and Pakistan, Huge sums have been put 
at the disposal of these branches, 


JANA REFUTES ALLEGED PRESENCE OF LIBYAN TROOPS IN CHAD 
ID071510 Tripoli JANA in English 1434 GMT 7 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli, May 7, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--JANA political editor has refuted allega- 
tions by the French newspaper LE QUOTIDIEN DE PARIS that Libyan forces are present in Chad, 


These allegations, he said, are false and groundless, Libyan forces have not entered Chad 
at all and Libya will always remain a country calling for the establishment of peace and 
security in Chad and for ending foreign intervention in Africa, Moreover, Libya's borders 
with Chad have been closed since the beginning of the conflict in Chad,
