Summary (AI): A August 1976 FBIS-relayed Tripoli radio text reports Qadhafi's speech at the Colombo nonaligned summit, where he called for a definition of terrorism and said Libya would accept the label for supporting liberation struggles. It concerns foreign policy and has no specific Tripoli places or people in Libya.
• Qadhafi said the Libyan Arab Republic was the only state to promulgate a law against terrorism.
• He said Libya rejects, opposes and resists terrorism, and that the Zionists and America consider African revolutionaries terrorists.
• He said Libya would support the Palestinian struggle and accept the terrorism charge as an honour.
• He stated Libya was prepared to pay the price for its support of just causes.
• 1976-08-18: Qadhafi addresses the Nonaligned Summit Conference in Colombo and calls for a definition of terrorism
