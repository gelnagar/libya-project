LIBYA

JANA Urges Rebellions Against Pro-U.S. Leaders 
LDO71057 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0740 GMT 7 Jul 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Jul (JANA) — The American Rapid Deploy- 
ment Force is due to carry out more military terrorist maneuvers 
in the Arab region and East Africa late next month. More than 
7,000 U.S. officers and men will be taking part in these maneu- 
vers to test their fighting capability in both regions. These forces 
will conduct naval maneuvers in Egypt and amphibious landings 
in Somalia. They will also conduct [word indistinct] against those 
whom the U.S. Administration has described as guerrillas in 
Sudan, and mock air raids in the Sultanate of Oman. 


These fresh maneuvers by the Rapid Deployment Force are of 
an aggressive nature and involve strategic expansionist objectives 
planned by the Pentagon, which has charged the hireling regimes 
in Egypt, Sudan, Somalia, and Oman with participating in them, 
now that these regimes have completely surrendered and joined 
the bandwagon of blind subservience to America. The provoca- 
tive terrorist maneuvers by the Rapid Deployment Force in 
Egypt, Sudan Somalia, and Oman constitute a direct threat to 
the freedom of peoples in the Arab homeland and Africa, and it 
is a dirty task these regimes have accepted to undertake, as a 
token of their alliance with America and to prove the extent of 
their loyalty to imperialism and Zionism. 


These regimes have openly and publicly joined the conspiratory 
American schemes and mobilized themselves to be a bridge for 
the American forces. But the people of Egypt and the peoples of 
Sudan, Somalia, and Oman will not accept such collusions and 
will not forgive the traitorous heads of these regimes, who have 
permitted the American invasion forces to enter the Arab land 
and use it as a springboard for aggression against the peoples who 
reject American domination. 


We believe that national duty makes it imperative for the free 
and noble men to revolt against these regimes and topple them. 
It would be an infamy if such regimes continue in power, prac- 
ticing treason, and believing in subservience and loyalty to Amer- 
ica, which is leading the new crusade against the Arab homeland 
and Africa. It would be a disgrace, too, if these regimes were to 
continue to open up Arab lands to the American and Zionist 
forces under the pretext of training and improving fighting 
capability. 


The collusion of Husni Mubarak, Sultan Qabus, sidekicks 
Numayri and Siad Barre and their open siding with America 
mean only that these regimes are plotting against their own 
peoples and defrauding the correct pan-Arab positions. We 
would never believe that our people in Egypt, in Somalia, in 
Oman, and in Sudan would accept the unconvincing pretexts put 
forward by those plotting heads of state. 


America is trying to deceive public opinion and is disregarding 
the feelings of the Arab people. It is using these impotent regimes 
as a springboard to realize its aggressive designs. For how can 
one believe that the aim of these maneuvers is training? Ameri-
