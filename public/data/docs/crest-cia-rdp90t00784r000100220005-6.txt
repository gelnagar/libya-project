Summary (AI): A 7 May 1982 NPIC imagery note reports two Libyan Air Force crashes: a probable Mirage III/V at Sebha airfield in central Libya (apparently during takeoff) and a possible Fitter at Ghurdabiyah airfield in north-central Libya (apparently during landing). Neither site is in Tripoli.
• The Libyan Air Force recently lost a probable Mirage III/V and a possible Fitter in two separate crashes.
• Sebha airfield is in central Libya.
• Ghurdabiyah airfield is 9 nm south of Surt on the Mediterranean coast.
• Document is classified SECRET and was prepared by the National Photographic Interpretation Center.
• 1982-04-21: Possible Fitter crashed and burned while apparently landing (title gives 21 April 1982 for this accident)
• 1982-05-04: Probable Mirage III/V crashed, apparently during takeoff (title gives 4 May 1982)
