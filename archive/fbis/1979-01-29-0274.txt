LIBYA

TURKEY'S ECEVIT ARRIVES 26 JAN, RECEIVED BY JALLUD 
1D261944 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1850 GMT 26 Jan 79 ID 
[Excerpts] Tripoli, 26 Jan--Turkey's Prime Minister Bulent Ecevit arrived in Tripoli 
this afternoon for a visit to the Jamahiriyah, during which he will hold talks on the 


development of relations between the Jamahiriyah and Turkey. 


There was an official and a popular welcome for Mr Ecevit, and Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam 
Jallud was in the forefront of those who welcomed him. 


In a statement to newsmen, Mr Ecevit expressed his Joy at visiting the Jamahiriyah and 
lauded relations between the two fraternal peoples. 


Bilateral Talks Begin 
LD261947 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1850 GMT 26 Jan 79 ID 


{Excerpt ] Tripoli, 26 Jan--The first round of the talks between the Jamahiriyah and 
Turkey began here this evening at the headquarters of the Foreign Secretariat in Tripoli. 


The Libyan side to the talks was chaired by Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and 
the Turkish delegation was led by Bulent Ecevit.
