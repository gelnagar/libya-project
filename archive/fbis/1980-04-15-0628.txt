LIBYA

JOINT COMMUNIQUE ISSUED ON AFGHAN FOREIGN MINISTER'S VISIT 
LD ‘1126 Tripoli JANA in English 0915 GMT 11 Apr 80 


[Text] Tripoli, April 11, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--A joint communique was issued yes- 
terday in Tripoli and Kabul, on the visit of Mr. Shah Muhammad Dost, Afghanistan's 
foreign minister, to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


Important talks were held between the Afghan minister and the Libyan Arab Foreign Secre- 
tary. Both stressed the solid ties binding their countries. They condemned the U,S,A, 
and reaction for using Islam to mask their schemes and impose their hegemony over the 
Islamic world as well as diverting attention from the cause of Palestine, the basic one, 
a matter which contradicts with real Islam, The two sides considered current develop- 
ments in the Arab world and reiterated their condemnation of the "Stable David" agree- 
ments which are a conspiracy against the Palestine cause, 


They voiced their denunciation of the capitulatory measures designed to liquidate this 
cause, The two sides reprobated the normalization of relations between the defeated 
Egyptian regime and the racist Zionist entity. 


They expressed support for the struggle of the Palestinian people for the restoration of 
their usurped homeland and the establishment of their own independent state over the 
whole of their national soil, They asserted their solidarity with the just struggle 

of the Saharan people, for self-determination and independence, 


Mr. Dost commended the important role of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
and the National Steadfastness and Confrontation Front in countering the reactionary 
imperialist Zionist alliance endeavouring to impose a capitulatory settlement on the 
Arab nation. 


The two sides denounced the attempts, by imperialist countries to exploit the Afghan 
situation in order to restore their influence and impose their control over Islamic and 
Arab countries. They asserted their adherence to the principles of the non-alignment 
movement which is positively contributing to international peace and security.
