Summary (AI): A 1982 NPIC imagery note says 16 Soviet-made T-54/55 tanks and MAZ-537 heavy-lift trailers probably shipped from Libya via Mers el Kebir were newly seen at a Polisario camp in Algeria, indicating continued Libyan support. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• Document is a 16 September 1982 NPIC briefing note (attachment to 2-15888/82).
• Equipment at Mers El Kebir: 16 T-54/55 medium tanks and four MAZ-537 transporter trailers, each carrying a multiple rocket launcher, offloaded from a Libyan RO-RO cargo ship.
• Oued Tatrat is 620 nm southwest of Mers El Kebir and 33 nm east of the Algerian-Western Sahara border.
• 1982-09: Sixteen medium tanks and three MAZ-537 heavy-lift trailers were newly identified at the camp, bringing its tank total to 43.
