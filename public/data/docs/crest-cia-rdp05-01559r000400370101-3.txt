Summary (AI): An FBIS Athens translation of Qadhdhafi's 1 September 1976 speech at Martyrs' Square in Tripoli, broadcast on Tripoli domestic radio. He defends Libya's relations with Egypt, criticizes Sadat, and says no Libyan soldier will move to the Egyptian border.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya supplied Egypt in the 1973 October War with 12,000 artillery electronic devices, 107 tanks and 30 Mirage planes.
• Qadhdhafi said that if the decision were his alone he would have severed all relations with Egypt, but most popular conferences had decided against severing relations.
• The speech marked the seventh anniversary of the First of September Revolution.
• 1976-09-01: Qadhdhafi gave a speech at Martyrs' Square on the seventh anniversary of the First of September Revolution, calling for popular democracy and criticizing Egypt and Sadat.
