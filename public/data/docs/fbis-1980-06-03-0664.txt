LIBYA

LIBYA PREPARING TO EXPEL BRITISH DIPLOMATS, CITIZENS 
LD021010 Tripoli JANA in English 0816 GMT 2 Jun 80 
[Text] Tripoli, June 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--‘he General People's Committee for 
Justice in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah is currently taking measures 
for the expulsion of a group of foreigners carrying the British nationality, for 
contravening the original conditions of their employment in Libya. Among those against 
whom these measures are contemplated are three British diplomats.
