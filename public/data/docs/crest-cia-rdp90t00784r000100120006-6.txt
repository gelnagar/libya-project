• Probable Libyan C-130 transport identified at Gondar airfield, Ethiopia, reported 21 October 1982.
• Camouflage paint pattern resembled that seen on C-130s in Libya.
• Aircraft may have supported Sudanese dissidents reportedly trained by Libyan personnel in the Gondar area.
• Coordinates given: 12-30-52N, 037-26-44E.
• Three cargo trucks were parked near the aircraft.
