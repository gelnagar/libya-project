Summary (AI): A January 1976 FBIS-relayed Tripoli radio report says the General People's Congress debated the 1976 administrative and development budgets, with Jallud presenting the development plan basis and Qadhdhafi speaking on foreign workers and Arab unity. It gives no specific Tripoli locations.
• Proposed general administrative budget for fiscal 1976 was 485.845 million dinars; one Libyan dinar was about 3.4 US dollars.
• Congress voted by an overwhelming majority to raise the administrative budget while keeping tax levels unchanged.
• Qadhdhafi said there were tens of thousands of non-Libyan workers in the Libyan Arab Republic, with Egyptians totalling about 250,000 by his count (a quarter of a million).
• Development plan covered 1976-1980 and included prospecting for minerals that could substitute for petroleum.
• 1976-01-08: General People's Congress resumed session for the fourth straight day; Congress reviewed the general administrative budget for fiscal 1976 (485.845 million dinars proposed) and discussed development budget items
