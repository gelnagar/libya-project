LIBYA

‘Right’ To Defend Against ‘U.S. Base’ in Chad Cited 
LD270919 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 0115 GMT 2786 


[Text] Ata press conference in Washington yesterday, the official 
spokesman of the American black house, Larry Speakes, touched 
on the situation in Chad and said: We have cooperated with 
Chad and have strengthened its Armed Forces. 


Question: What have you done to help Chad? 

Answer: I believe we have continued the sales of arms. From 
the point of view of supplies; we have provided assistance from 
time to time. We have always reported that. 


Question: Can you say anything about coordination of action 
with the French in Chad? 

Answer: We havealways consulted the French about Chad, but 
I do not wish to comment on this. 


The JANA political editor, commenting on what Larry Speakes 
said about Chad, has declared: Thus it becomes clear to the 
world in general and all African peoples in particular, that Chad 
has become a U.S. military base from which the Americans 
disturb the stability of the peoples of the continent and strike at 
the liberation movements of the continent. Proceeding from the 
legitimate right to self-defense, the Great Jamahiriyah declares 
its right to defend itself to confront this U.S. base, which will be 
a dangerous military springboard near the Jamahiriyah’s 
southern border.
