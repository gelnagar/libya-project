LIBYA

AL-'UBAYDI VIEWS TALKS IN, RELATIONS WITH FRANCE 


PM291448 Beirut AS-SAFIR in Arabic 25 Jul 81 pp 1, 7 


[Hamidah Na'na' report: "Al-Ubaydi: France Agreed To Implement Military and Economic 
Contracts With Jamahiriyah"] 


[Excerpts] Paris--Secretary of the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison ‘Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi concluded his visit to France the day before yesterday and 
left for Tunis to attend meetings of the Arab Defense Council. 


Before his departure from Paris Al-'Ubaydi told AS-SAFIR that the visit was positive: It 
was the first official contact, but it was prepared for by several preliminary contacts. 
He added: During the meetings many issues which were the cause of differences between 
Libya and France were explained. 


Asked whether he has noticed any profound change in French policy toward the Libyan 
Jamahiriyah and Africa, he said: "What we regard as positive is the fact that the relations 
between the two countries have emerged from the stalemate that existed during the regime 

of President Giscard d'Estaing. We found the Socialist government responsive to our 
initiatives, whereas under the previous president Franco-Libyan relations were characterized 
by an unjustifiable escalation of tension."" He added: Another positive point in the visit 
is that it showed France's commitment to fulfill all contracts, including military contracts, 
and to carry on with economic cooperation in general. 


With regard to Chad, the secretary said: We underlined several facts including the fact 
that the problem of Chad does not concern a third party: It concerns only the Jamahiriyah 
and Chad, on the grounds that our aid to Chad was in accordance with an agreement between 
the two countries and with the joint defense agreement. 


He said: We pointed out that the Chadian Government has not complained that its territories 
are occupied by Libya. Even the Nairobi summit did not suggest either directly or in- 
directly that the presence of the Libyan forces in Chad is illegitimate. Rather, it con- 
firmed the legitimacy of the present government and stressed the need to build Chad and 
safeguard its stability and security. He added: For its part the French side affirmed 

that it is not involved in the question of Chad and its cooperation with Libya. France 

has historical ties with Chad and is eager for the question between Libya and Chad to be 
dealt with within the OAU. 


Continuing, he said: The Libyan side affirmed that the United States is trying to play 
a role in Africa, and particularly in Chad, through its agents. We explained to the 
French that this should be understood and told them that we find ourselves confronting 
some of the African states which the United States is trving to set up as a barrier 
against us. We found the French side understanding: It asserted to us that France does 
not entirely agree with all the U.S. pelicies in Africa. 


Speaking about the military and economic outcome of the visit and its results in terms 
of concluding new military deals, he said that he has left this matter to be discussed 
by those concerned, while it was agreed to continue the political dialogue. 


He said: We assured them that we agree with the Socialists on many issues, which was the 
reason why Libya welcomed President Mitterrand's victory. Among these are the issues 

of social justice, condemning racial discrimination, respecting the will of Africa and 
keeping out of the struggle for influence.
