RESISTANCE COUNCIL CALLS FOR STRIKES--Paris, 19 Sep (AFP)--The Paris-based Iranian 
National Resistance Council has called on supporters in Iran to “organize strikes" 
to oppose the "dictatorship" of Ayatollah Ruh»ilah Khomeyni and obtain the respect 
of national and popular rights, the group said here today. In a communique, the 
council said that the strike call had been distributed in Iran during the past 
week by the Peoples Mojahedin, the major opposition movement to the Iranian Islamic 
regime. [Text] [NC191240 Paris AFP in English 1229 GMT 19 Sep 81] 


GUNBOATS IN INDIAN OCEAN--Algiers, 22 Sep (AFP)--Three French-built Iranian 
gunboats, attacked off the Spanish coast in August by an anti-Khomeyni commando, 
are now in the Indian Ocean, sailing toward iran, Western diplomatic sources 
reported here today. The three vessels reached the Indian Ocean via the Suez 
Canal and the Red Sea after a brief stop in Tripoli, the sources said. The 
Khanjar, Meyseh and Tabarzin left military dockyards in Cherbourg, western France,
