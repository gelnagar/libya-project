Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery analysis note dated 15 January 1982 reports a new jet-capable airfield under construction at Al Wigh in southwestern Libya, far from Tripoli. The analyst judged it would add air defence in Libya's southern district and could serve as a staging base into sub-Saharan Africa.
• Main runway 4,316 x 72 m (14,160 x 236 ft), NW/SE orientation.
• Secondary runway 1,805 x 45 m (5,922 x 148 ft), NE/SW orientation.
• Construction/support camp adjacent to east side of airfield with one large vehicle storage building and at least 50 housing trailers.
• Coordinates given: 24-05-02N 014-31-52E.
• 1982-01-15: Imagery shows new airfield in mid-stage construction, with runways, taxiways and apron being graded and rolled; a construction camp with at least 50 housing trailers is adjacent.
