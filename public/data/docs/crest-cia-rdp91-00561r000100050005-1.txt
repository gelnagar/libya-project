Summary (AI): A December 1981 Washington Post article reports that the Reagan administration warned Qaddafi through a third country that he faces "the most serious consequences" if Libyan-linked "hit teams" strike against U.S. leaders, and that officials were weighing economic and political measures.
• Reagan sent Qaddafi a message through a third nation, including evidence about Libyan 'hit teams' reportedly in the United States.
• Officials said the administration was considering economic and political, not military, measures against Libya.
• CBS reported 'hit squad' members had been identified and were being interviewed in Mexico; Mexican and US diplomatic officials said they had no knowledge of a hit team in Mexico.
• Reagan chaired National Security Council meetings on Monday and Tuesday dealing with the Libyan question.
