Summary (AI): A 1988 intelligence report says two Libyan External Security Organization officers, Abdelbaset Ali al-Megrahi and Mas'ud Abu Aqilah, travelled from Tripoli through Malta in December 1987 and stayed at the Holiday Inn there. The source notes they were likely doing technical intelligence work.
• Megrahi travelled on a Libyan passport under the alias Abd al Baset Ali; Abu Aqilah used the alias Abu Aqilah.
• Megrahi arrived on a Libyan Arab Airlines flight and Abu Aqilah on an Air Malta flight, both from Tripoli.
• Megrahi carried one bag onboard and left the airport without Maltese customs processing.
• Both officers left Malta for Zurich on a Swissair flight on 8 December and returned on 17 December.
• Source comment: the officers were likely carrying technical intelligence-gathering equipment.
• 1987-12: ESO officers Abdelbaset Ali al-Megrahi and Mas'ud Abu Aqilah arrived in Malta from Tripoli on separate flights, were met by former LAA station manager Al-Amin Khalifa, stayed at the Holiday Inn, and left for Zurich on 8 December.
• 1987-12-17: Both ESO officers returned to Malta and departed for Tripoli the same evening.
