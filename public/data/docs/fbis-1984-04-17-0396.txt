LIBYA

British Police ‘Open Fire’ on People’s Bureau 
LD171249 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1235 GMT 
17 Apr 84 


[Text] The Libyan Arab People’s Bureau in London was this 
morning subjected to a most horrible terrorist action in violation 
of all international laws and traditions on the part of the British 
Government. The British police, supported by a helicopter and 
Libyan and other foreign agents of the British intelligence, 
stormed the Libyan Arab People’s Bureau in the British capital 
and opened fire on members of the bureau and arrested a number 
of people working in it. 


In the face of this planned terrorist operation, the workers of the 
Libyan Arab People’s Bureau had to defend themselves against 
the armed aggression by the British police and British agents and 
defend the Libyan Arab People’s Bureau, which represents a part 
of the Libyan Arab territory.
