Summary (AI): A January 1984 CIA imagery briefing (NPIC) on Libyan and Libyan-backed forces in northern Chad and southern Libya, listing unit and aircraft deployments at Fada, Faya Largeau, Ounianga Kebir, Gouro, Ogoui, Aozou, Kufra, Sebha and Matan as Sarra. Nothing about Tripoli itself.
• Libyan ground force deployments at Fada, Faya Largeau, Ounianga Kebir and Gouro in northern Chad and Sebha and Kufra in southern Libya each contained a combined arms force.
• Air defence assets in northern Chad included two SA-6 batteries, two SA-9 batteries and at least four ZSU-23/4 guns.
• Libyan combat aircraft in northern Chad during the week included three Mirage F-1, 13 Fitter (Su-22), 16 SF.260 and four Hind helicopters.
• Around 400 trucks and 58 Land Rover-type vehicles were observed at six installations in the Sebha area supporting operations in Chad.
• 1984-01: First deployment of Flogger aircraft to Matan as Sarra airfield in southern Libya; mechanized infantry unit moved from Kufra to Matan as Sarra area.
