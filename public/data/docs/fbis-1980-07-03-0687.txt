LIBYA

POLISH MINISTER'S VISIT CONTINUES WITH MEETINGS, TOUR 


Cooperation Talks 


LD021158 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1030 GMT 2 Jul 80 


[Text] Tripoli--Brother Jadullah 'Azzu at-Talhi, secretary of the General People's 
Committee, last night conferred with Wojciech Jaruzelski, member of the Polish United 
Workers Party Politburo and national defense minister of the Polish People's Republic, 
and the accompanying delegation who are currently on a visit to the Libyan Arab Jamahiri- 
yah. The meeting, which took place at the committee's headquarters, reviewed the results 
of economic, scientific and technical cooperation. The guest minister expressed satis-
