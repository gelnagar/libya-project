[Text] Tripoli, 14 Aug (JANA) -<Commenting on the allegation published by a journalist 
called Graff Brockdorffe-the London correspondent of the German paper DIE WELT, who said 
that an attempted coup was foiled in Libya 6 days ago when an officer tried to seize the 
transmitting station in Tobrug e-the political editor of JANA stressed that this report is 
fabricated, a mere figment of the imagination and totally unfounded,
