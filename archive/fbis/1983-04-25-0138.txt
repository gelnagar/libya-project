LIBYA

AL-MAJALLAH: Government Seeking SAM-5’s 
PM221559 London AL-MAJALLAH in Arabic 23-29 Apr 83 p 9 


[From the “AL-MAJALLAH Envoys” Feature] 


[Text] Well-informed Arab diplomatic sources have said that 
Libya is making overtures to the Soviet leadership in order to 
obtain SAM-5’s. Libya has justified its request for these missiles 
with the claim that there is a U.S. threat to its territories and 
coasts and that some aircraft of the U.S. fleet violate its airspace. 
Libya submitted this request following the agreement in principle 
with the Soviet Union on concluding a treaty of friendship and 
cooperation between the two countries. It is understood that 
Syria is the only Arab country which has obtained the Soviet 
long-range SAM-5’s.
