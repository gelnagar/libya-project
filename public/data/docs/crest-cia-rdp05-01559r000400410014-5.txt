Link With Austrian Arms Supplys to Iran Denied 
LD281823 Kuwait KUNA in English 1613 GMT 28 Jan 86 

[Text] Vienna, Jan 28 (KUNA) — The Libyan Embassy in 
Vienna Tuesday categorically denied any involvement of Libya 
in alleged Austrian weapons deals with Iran. 

The embassy was commenting on claims made this week by the 
Austrian magazines BASTA and PROFIL which maintain that 
Austrian weapons have been delivered to Iran via Libya. Austria 
has a law banning the supplying of weapons and military 
equipment to countries involved in armed conflict. 

Libya had nothing to do with alleged weapons deals by Austrian 
nationalised steel corporation ‘Voest-Alpine.’ Nor had it ever 
played the role of ‘middle man’ in any such transactions, the 
embassy stated.
