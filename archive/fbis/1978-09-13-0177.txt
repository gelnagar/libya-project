PRESS ASKED NOT TO PUBLISH NAMES OF ACCUSED 
LD120952Y Tehran Domestic Service in Persian 0500 GMT 12 Sep 78 LD 


[Excerpts] During a conversation with a correspondent from the National Iranian Radio 

and Television Organization [NIRTO], the minister of justice requested the following of 
all press writers and correspondents: Unless the charges against accused persons have 
been proved in court, their names should not be published. Dr Baheri, the justice minister 
said: The press is a basic pillar of democracy, and the primary goal of democracy is to 
protect human dignity and individual rights. For this reason, until a charge against a 
person has been proved, his name or what he is accused of ought not to be revealed. 


During his telephone conversation with the NIRTO correspondent, Dr Baheri repeated his 
request to the mass media and asked the press to refrain from mentioning the names of 
persons arrested on various accusations until these accusations are proved in court. He 
said it is possible that some of the accused may be innocent, and thus a hardship may be 
inflicted on them and their families, or their honor and human dignity may be tarnished. 


The minister of justice said: I have made this request to newspaper publishers many times. 
Nevertheless, we have seen that some newspapers publish the names of persons arrested on 
various charges, thereby causing discomfort for them and their families. This is 

against morality, human honor, conscience and human rights.

Communique No 7 
LD1OOS4U8Y Tehran Domestic Service in Persian 0756 GMT 10 Sep 78 LD 
[Communique No 7 by the Military Governorate of Tehran and district ] 


[Text ] In order to provide for the welfare of the respected citizens and facilitate 
movement by the public, the curfew hours, which had been announced in Communique No 3 
as from 2100 to 0500, will be changed to 2200 to 0500. We expect the citizens to 
observe the regulations and thus cooperate in securing more comfort for the public, 


[Signed] Military governor of Tehran and district, Gen Gholam-Ali Oveysi 
Communique No 6 

LDO91218Y Tehran Domestic Service in Persian 1000 GMT 9 Sep 78 LD 

[Communique No 6 by the Military Governorate of Tehran and district ] 


[Text ] The public was informed in Communique No 4 by the Military Governorate of Tehran 
and district that from yesterday morning, despite the previous government declaration 
pertaining to the necessity of obtaining permission for assemblies and also the 
declaration on setting up the Military Governorate of Tehran and district; and 
drawing the attention of the people to the need to preserve order and cooperate 

with security officers; a number of adventurist and subversive elements, who have 
under no circumstances believed in or been concerned with [words indistinct] of the 
realm and order and security and for whom the question of the welfare and comfort 

of the dear compatriots is of no interest, embarked on onslaughts against pubiic 
rights and committed acts of arson with the aim--in some parts of the capital--of 
making political capital out of fomenting uncertainty and paving the ground for im- 
plementing designs by foreigners. These people, who do not wish to see the State of 
Iran secure and the Iranian people prosperous, set fire to public places, means of 
transportation, vehicles, and buildings and did not pay heed to the fact that the 
owners of these cars and buildings were their compatriots and brothers in religion. 
In these circumstances the security officers, after giving previous warnings and 
drawing the public's attention to the need to preserve security and public welfare, 
had no option but to carry out their lawful duty. Since the independence of the 
homeland and the defense of public lives, property and security are at stake, clashes 
are inevitable in such incidents, and a mumber of people were wounded and kiZled. 

The wounded were immediately dispatched to a hospital. 


Reports in hand about those wounded seriously say a mumber have died. The final 
statistics on the number of those who died will be made public after accurate in- 
formation has been collected, 


The Military Governorate, while regretting the course of events brought about by 
rioters and the enemies of religion and the realm, has no option but to draw the 
attention of the public once again to the need to preserve order and cooperate with 
security officers, and hopes that the roots of insecurity and riots, backed by foreign 
agents in this country, will shrivel up with the cooperation and assistance of the 
God-fearing, religious and patriotic people of this country. 


[Signed] The military governor of Tehran and district, Gen Gholam-Ali Oveysi
