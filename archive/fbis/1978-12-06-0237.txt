LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI CONVENES GENERAL PEOPLE'S CONGRESS 16 DEC 
LDO51324Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 5 Dec 78 LD 
[Text] Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, secretary general of the General People's 
Congress, has issued a decision inviting the General People's Congress to convene. 
This is the text of the decision: 


The secretary general of the General People's Congress has decided: 


Article 1--The General People's Congress is hereby invited to convene on Saturday, 
16 December 1978 at 1000 in at the People's Hall in Tripoli. 


Article 2--This decision shall be published in the official gazette and shall be imple- 
mented as from its date of issuance. 


Signed: Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, secretary general of the General People's Congress, 
5 December 1978. 


AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS MESSAGE TO JORDAN'S HUSAYN 


LDO51548Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 5 Dec 78 LD 


[Text] Recently brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi sent a message to King Husayn of Jordan 
dealing with the recent developments between the Jordanian and Palestiniar peoples and 
the support enjoyed by the Palestinian resistance from Jordan, which was manifested 

at the meetings and in the dialog held recently between Jordanian officials and a 
delegation from the Palestinian resistance. 


JANA has learned that brother ‘Abd al-Hamid Sharaf, chief of the Royal Jordanian Court, is 
expected in Tripoli tomorrow carrying a personal message to brother Col Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi from King Husayn of Jordan.
