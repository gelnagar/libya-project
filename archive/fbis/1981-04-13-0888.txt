LIBYA

OFFICIAL EXPLAINS BOYCOTT OF REFUGEE CONFERENCE 


LD120930 Tripoli JANA in English 0720 GMT 12 Apr 81 


[Text] Geneva, 12 Apr (JANA)--The Socialist Peoples Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah ‘as boycotted 
the international conference held to discuss aid to the African refugees, which began 

its work in Geneva last Thursday, because of the participation of the Zionist enemy. 

The brother secretary of the popular committee for the Popular Bureau for Foreign Liaison 
yesterday held a press conference at which he explained the Jamahiriyah's attitude and 

its reasons for boycotting the conference. 


He said we have explained to our African friends that it is illogical for a conference 
discussing the humanitarian problems affecting refugees to permit the Zionist enemy, 
which is responsible for the biggest refugee problem in the world, to attend. 


He added that the Jamahiriyah did not attend the conference because it felt the conference 
was not important or because it was not keen on helping the Africans to solve their 
problems, for it is already fully performing its role in this regard, but it did not 
attend because of the participation of the Zionist enemy, he said. He told the pressmen 
that he was surprised to see the Zionist enemy had been invited, for throughout contacts 
with the OAU and with the secretariat preparing the conference, there was no mention 
whatsoever that the Zionist enemy would be invited to attend. 


JALLUD MEETS EAST GERMAN OFFICIALS IN BERLIN 


LD120900 Tripoli JANA in English 0825 GMT 12 Apr 81 


[Text] Berlin, 12 April (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud 
yesterday met President Erich Honecker of the German Democratic Republic and secretary 
general of the Unified German Socialist Party. Staff Major Jallud conveyed Colonel al- 
Qadhdhafi's greetings to the German leader. 


The meeting was attended by Willi Stoph, vice chairman of the Council of State and 
member of the Politburo of the Socialist Unity Party Central Committee, Guenter Mittag, 
member of the political bureau andAlfred Steiger, member of the Central Committee and 
minister of electrical and electonic tehnology.
