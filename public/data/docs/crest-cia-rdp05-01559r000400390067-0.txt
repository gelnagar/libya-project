Summary (AI): An FBIS transcript (take 2 of 4) of a March 1985 speech by Muammar al-Qadhdhafi to a newly formed Pan-Arab Command, urging its detachments to incite the masses and calling the body the true all-embracing command. It is ideological rhetoric with no specific Tripoli sites, events or casualty figures.
• Speech broadcast by Tripoli JANA in Arabic, dated 31 March 1985, addressing the Pan-Arab Command.
• Speaker said the Pan-Arab Command detachments should become a revolutionary force that incites the masses rather than relying on party and front members.
• Speech referred to the entry of Portugal and Spain into the EEC and declared the Arab nation 'born again' on 31 March 1985.

AN ARMY OF TANKS, AIRCRAFT AND ARTILLERY -- A CLASSICAL ARMY.

BOOBY TRAPPED CAR AGAINST THEM. THEY FEAR A MAN WHO TAKES 
HIS GUN AND GOES TO FIGHT THEM WITHOUT TAKING ORDERS FROM ANYONE.
