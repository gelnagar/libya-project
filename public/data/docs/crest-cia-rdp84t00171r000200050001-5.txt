Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing on Libyan resupply of Chadian dissidents at Faya Largeau, Chad, also notes Libyan armour and artillery staging at Al Kufrah Barracks in southeastern Libya, far from Tripoli. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• Four armoured vehicles were next to the hangar at the Faya Largeau airfield, and four 122mm howitzers were deployed near the west end of the runway.
• At Al Kufrah Barracks, nine BTR-60 APCs (four on heavy-lift transporters) and three empty heavy-lift transporters were staging from a concrete pad.
• Al Kufrah Barracks houses a mechanized infantry battalion and is about 220 nm north of the Libya-Chad border.
• Coordinates given: 17-55-00N 019-07-00E and 24-13-10N 023-18-13E.
• 1983-07: Imagery shows Libyan armour, 122mm howitzers, reinforcements and trucks arriving to resupply Chadian dissidents; Libyan F.27 transport departed.

LIBYAN/DISSIDENT ACTIVITY 
FAYA LARGEAU, CHAD 

AL ee as 25X1 

SIGNIFICANCE: LIBYAN RESUPPLY OF CHADIAN DISSIDENTS CONTINUES 

AS ARMOR, ARTILLERY, AND ADDITIONAL PERSONNEL HAVE ARRIVED 

SINCE (SWN) 25X1 

REMARKS: FOUR ARMORED VEHICLES ARE NEXT TO THE HANGAR 

(GRAPHIC 1), AND FOUR 122MM HOWITZERS (GRAPHIC 2), WITH GUN 

TUBES ORIENTED SOUTHEAST TOWARD ABECHE, ARE DEPLOYED NEAR THE 
WEST END OF THE RUNWAY. THE LIBYAN F.27 MEDIUM TRANSPORT WITH 
CIVILIAN MARKINGS, HERE ON HAS DEPARTED. ONE TWIN 25X1 
OTTER LIGHT TRANSPORT REMAINS PARKED NEAR THE HANGAR. JUST 
WEST OF THE AIRFIELD, ONE PROBABLE 76/122MM HOWITZER IS 
ORIENTED TO THE SOUTH. REINFORCEMENTS ARE ARRIVING IN THE TOWN 
(GRAPHIC 3). NINE TRUCKS AND AT LEAST 10 LAND ROVERS ARE 
ASSEMBLING ON THE WEST EDGE OF TOWN. A LARGE GROUP OF 
PERSONNEL 1S IN THE AREA, AND ADDITIONAL PERSONNEL ARE 
DISEMBARKING FROM THE TRUCKS. AT LEAST SEVEN ADDITIONAL 
TRUCKS, MOST PROBABLY CARRYING PERSONNEL, ARE APPROACHING THE 
TOWN FROM THE WEST. INSIDE THE TOWN OF FAYA LARGEAU, ONE 
POSSIBLE MULTIPLE ROCKET LAUNCHER AND ONE PROBABLE 122MM 
HOWITZER D-30 ARE NEAR THE CENTER OF THE TOWN. AT LEAST 75 
LAND ROVERS AND TRUCKS REMAIN DISPERSED IN AND AROUND THE 

TOWN. (SWN) 

AT AL KUFRAH BARRACKS IN LIBYA (GRAPHIC 4), NINE BTR-60 ARMORED 
PERSONNEL CARRIERS (FOUR ON HEAVY-LIFT TRANSPORTERS) AND THREE 

EMPTY HEAVY-LIFT TRANSPORTERS ARE STAGING FROM A CONCRETE PAD 

IN THE CENTER OF THE INSTALLATION. ELEVEN BTR-60 APC WERE HERE 

ON PROBABLE SUPPORT ELEMENTS CONSISTING OF AT LEAST 25X1 
SEVEN TRUCKS AND 19 UTILITY TRUCKS/VEHICLES ARE IN CONVOY 

IMMEDIATELY SOUTH OF THE INSTALLATION. (SWN) 

AL KUFRAH BARRACKS, WHICH HOUSES A MECHANIZED INFANTRY 
BATTALION, IS APPROXIMATELY 220 NM NORTH OF THE LIBYA-CHAD 
BORDER. (SWN)
