Summary (AI): A Washington Post clipping from December 1981 reports that President Reagan warned Qaddafi through a third country of "the most serious consequences" if reported Libyan assassination teams act against U.S. officials. It also covers White House deliberations on economic and political (not military) measures, and Secretary Haig's objections to leaks. Nothing here is located in Tripoli.
• Reagan chaired NSC meetings on the Libyan question on Monday and Tuesday; no NSC meeting was held on the day of the article.
• White House officials were committed to notifying congressional leaders of their intentions by the night of the article.
• Officials said the response under consideration was economic and political, not military.
• Haig said he was 'appalled' by leaks about retaliatory steps against Libya being contemplated.
• A recommendation to US oil companies doing business in Libya was expected before the end of the week.
• 1981-12: Reagan sends message via a third nation to Qaddafi warning of serious consequences if Libyan hit teams strike U.S. officials
• 1981-12: CBS reports five 'hit squad' members identified and interviewed in Mexico; Mexican and US diplomats in Mexico City say they had no knowledge of such a team
