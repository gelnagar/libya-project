Forces, and his accompanying delegation arrived in Tripoli this

Colonel Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the Libyan

because the Greater Maghreb has great influence on all Arabs.

imperialism, Zionism, and colonialism do not want the Arabs to 
become united. On the contrary, they would like to see Arabs 
disunified and separated from each other. We are now proceed- 
ing on the path of unity, but not just between Algeria and the 
Jamahiriyah. Our unity will be a stepping stone toward compre- 
hensive Arab unity, if God is willing.

Iran Urged To Separate Politics From Pilgrimage 
EA082057 Omdurman Domestic Service in Arabic 
1930 GMT 8 Aug 87 


[Text] Prime Minister al-Sadiq al-Mahdi has sent the following 
cable to the custodian of the two holy mosques, King Fahd ibn 
‘Abd al-‘Aziz: 


We are sorry to hear of the Mecca events and appeal to everyone 
to abide by the regulations for the safety of the pilgrims and to 
observe the sanctity of the holy mosques. May Allah bless them 
to serve the Almighty’s guests and ensure their safety. 


His excellency also sent the following cable to President ‘Ali 
Khamenei, president of the Islamic Republic of Iran: 


We are sorry to hear of the Mecca events. May God have mercy 
on the souls of the dead. We appeal to you to accept a compromise 
between the freedom of pilgrimage and the obligation of the 
pilgrims to observe the regulations of the country acting as their 
host to guarantee everyone's safety. We appeal to you to cooper- 
ate in isolating the pilgrimage from political and factional differ- 
ences and to cooperate in avoiding an expansion of conflict. We 
appeal to you, instead, to work for the resolution of problems 
among Muslims by peaceful means. 


Iranian Deputy Foreign Minister Visits Country 


Confers With Al-Mahdi on Mecca 
LD092150 Tehran IRNA in English 1718 GMT 9 Aug 87 


[Text] Tehran, Aug. 9, IRNA — Sudanese Prime Minister 
al-Sadiq al-Mahdi said in Khartoum Saturday that world Mus- 
lims have a right to know the truth about the bloody Mecca 
catastrophe and Iran is justified in calling on the Muslim world 
to investigate the issue. 


Al-Sadiq al-Mahdi made the remarks in a meeting with Iranian 
Deputy Foreign Minister for Political Affairs Hoseyn Sheykh 
ol-Eslam in the Sudanese capital Khartoum, where he said that 
performing an uninhibited hajj ceremony is a prime and divine 
right for all Muslims. 


On the situation in Persian Gulf the Sudanese prime minister 
said his country believes that foreign powers should withdraw 
from all important and strategic points of the world including the 
Persian Gulf. No Muslim country should allow the superpowers 
to exert hegemony over the region, he stressed. In reply, Sheykh 
ol-Eslam said the prime motive behind the Mecca massacre was 
to overshadow the humiliating U.S. set back in the Persian Gulf. 


Referring to the Saudi's deliberate delay in treating the wounded 
pilgrims as well as their stalling of efforts to deliver the bodies of 
martyrs, Sheykh ol-Eslam said they are withholding bodies of 
Iranian pilgrims in a bid to erase the signs of their crimes which 
are evident on the bodies. Saudi rulers are not qualified to 
administer the two holy sanctuaries and Muslim ulema and 
scholais are obliged to take necessary measures in this regard, 
stressed Sheykh ol-Eslam.

5th Member of Head of States Council Selected 
EA082049 Omdurman Domestic Service in Arabic 
1700 GMT 8 Aug 87 


[Text] At 1500 today Prime Minister al-Sadiq al-Mahdi held a 
press conference at the General Secretariat of the Council of 
Ministers. To begin the press conference his excellency made a 
statement in which he pointed out that the Constituent Assembly 
had selected Mirghani al-Nasri, the advocate, as the fifth mem- 
ber of the Head of States Council. He then congratulated Mir- 
ghani al-Nasri. 


His excellency said that the fifth member should have been 
nominated by the Democratic Unionist Party [DUP] following 
the resignation of Muhammad al-Hasan Abdullah Yasin in 
accordance with the agreement governing relations between the 
Ummah Party and the DUP. The DUP nominated Ahmad 
al-Sayyid Hamad, but the Ummah Party rejected this nomina- 
tion because, among other things, the memorandum of under- 
Standing the parties signed in June 1987 stipulated that 
“Al-Sadin” should be kept out of office in order to eliminate the 
vestiges of the May regime [Numayri’s revolution], The memo- 
randum defines “Al-Sadin” as a person who has exploited his 
position to annoy others, gained an undeserved promotion, 
worked for the state security organ, taken part in the persecution 
or harming of citizens, tortured members of the national opposi- 
tion, and enriched himself as a result of favoritism. 


Mr Ahmad al-Sayyid Hamad held a prominent political position 
during the May regime. He never ceased to violently attack that 
regime's opposition, thus landing himself within the first provi- 
sion of the definition. Moreover Ahmad al-Sayyid Hamad cen- 
tinued to attack the Ummah Party and the alliance between the 
Ummah Party and the DUP. 


For this reason, the Ummah Party opposed his nomination and 
demandes that another person be selected. However, the DUP 
reiterated its support for Ahmad al-Sayyid Hamad and resolved 
to expel a.zy other nominee who presented himself in his name. 


The Ummah Party resolved that if the DUP submitted the name 
of any other person, it would support him, provided he did not 
involve himself in intrigues to divide the DUP on this matter. But 
since the DUP refused to submit the name of another qualified 
person, the Ummah Party had to nominate a patriotic and 
nationalist personality for the seat. This is what happened. Mir- 
ghani al-Nasri was nominated on the basis of his being a national 
personality.
