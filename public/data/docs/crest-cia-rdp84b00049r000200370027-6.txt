MEMORANDUM FOR: Director of Central Intelligence 
FROM : SA/IA 
SUBJECT : NSC Meeting on Poland and Libya, 4 February 1982 

1. As you know, tomorrow afternoon's NSC meeting will address both Poland 
(i.e. sanctions) and Libya. Because of the amount of material for the Polish 
portion of the meeting, I have divided the material into two books. Book 1 
contains the material on Poland, and Book 2 contains the material on Libya. 

Poland 

2. We have received two papers from the NSC for tomorrow's discussion on 
Poland. The first focuses on the issue of extending sanctions against the Soviet 
Union beyond our own borders. The paper you signed off on today for distribution 
to NSC members is a fine wrap-up of the issue. I am confident that the conclusions 
of that paper will figure prominently in tomorrow's discussion. 

 - has prepared a very useful and comprehensive memorandum 25X1 
outlining the various aspects of the sanctions problem (see Tab C). It would be 
well worth your while to read his comments carefully. He mentioned also that 
he had given you some information orally. 

4, For backup and reference on the pipeline issue, I have included a number 
of memoranda and cables reporting on various aspects of pipeline construction and 
development. In addition, Harry Rowen drafted a memorandum for the record covering 
the SIG meeting he attended last Friday. Finally, I included the information 
from covering estimated net Soviet receipts from gas and oi] sales in 25X1 
the late 1980s (see Tab J). 

5. The second NSC paper deals with possible additional steps against Poland 
and the Soviet Union, including the option to refuse to reschedule Poland's 1982 
debt. has provided comments on this paper as well (see Tab I). 25X1 

25X11 

6. Please note that the NSC Staff issued two versions of this second paper, 
a Top Secret version which you have, and a Secret version.

7. Should you be asked to comment at the meeting on the Polish internal

Libya 

8. This will be the second agenda item, assuming there is adequate time 
to get to it. 

9, has provided a short memorandum outlining the key 25X11 
issues in the NSC paper entitled “Next Steps on Libya." The focus of the 
discussion will be whether to revalidate and then activate the next steps 
toward Libya discussed at the December NSC meeting. Further, the discussion 
will focus on whether and when to institute an embargo on importsof Libyan 
oil and an embargo on exports to Libya. Discussion will also include military 
measures against the Libyan threat, including advisability and timing of another 
U.S. naval exercise in the Gulf of Sidra. 

10. Please note that I have included an update on Libyan terrorist planning 
from and some recent cables that focus on reports that Libya intends 20X1 
to attack the U.S. Sixth Fleet. If these reports are true, another U.S. exercise 
in the Gulf of Sidra could very well turn into a fairly major confrontation. 

11. Because your schedule will not permit you to spend any time at Head- 
quarters tomorrow, I will send you via your OEOB office any further inputs for 
your meeting. Please let me know in the morning if there is anything else you 
wish to have.
