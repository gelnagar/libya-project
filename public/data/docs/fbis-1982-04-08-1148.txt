LIBYA

STUDENTS MARK SIXTH ANNIVERSARY OF UPRISING 


JANA Views Students' Role 
LDO70914 Tripoli JANA in English 0813 GMT 7 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Apr (JANA) -- The revolutionary student force in jamahiriyah is rallying 
to wage a new battle, escalating the people's revolution and widening the revolutionary 
committees’ movement. It will also take new steps to resolve the conflict in the interest 
of people's authority and freedom and to herald the humanitarian dicta, destroy reactionary 
culture and ideas of the old society and crush all obstacles, [and] obstructive persons. 


The revolutionary force, amidst celebrations today marking sixth anniversary of the students 
uprising, will undertake a distinguished militant role in realising new revolutionary 

tasks affirming the inevitable victory for the revolutionary force and consolidating the 
people's authority. 


People's committees at some colleges will be selected and self administration at some 
secondary schools and some colleges will be established. There will also be a march on 
the media organs so as to create a nationalist revolutionary media serving the Arab 
nation's causes, confronting imperialism and Zionism, and exposing the vile traitors 
and their claims, capitulation and shameful subservience. 


For the first time, the revolutionary-unionist-nationalist force will join the revolutionary 
student force in the revolutionary march on the media in order to transform it into 

a nationalist unionist media of the masses which heralds emancipation and agitates for 
people's revolution confronting all regionalist and reactionary trends. 


Media 'Marched On' 


LDO71400 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1300 GMT 7 Apr 82 


[Text] The student revolutionary force in the jamahiriyah today accomplished supreme 
revolutionary tasks at the peak of its continuous battle to escalate the popular 
revolution and extend the growth of the popular revolutionary committees’ movement 
during celebrations held today on the occasion of the sixth anniversary of the students' 
7 April revolution against the reactionary rightwing forces and the remnants of the 
rotten partisans and bourgeoisie. 


V. 8 Apr 82 Q 2 NORTH AFRICA 


The student masses broke out into the arena of serious revolutionary action, forming 
popular committees in the university colleges and proclaiming the establishment of 
self-management at some colleges, institutes and secondary schools. The revolutionary 
students’ force, together with the committed revolutionary unionist pan-Arab force, 
marched on information media, transforming them into committed, inciting revolutionary 
pan-Arab jamahiri information media to expose colonialism and its pillars, to uncover 
the agents and the reactionaries, to burn the rubbish on the Arab arena, to incite 
popular revolution and unity and to herald the final break from slavery and all forms 
of domination, repression and exploitation. 


Fatih University in Tripoli observed the sixth anniversary of the students’ revolution 
this morning. It organized a large rally in which the students of the university, the 
secondary schools and military institute and other Arab students took part. 


A review of the students, male and female, in military uniform, took place during the 
rally. An equestrian show also was held in which the students took part. 


The secretary of the student congress at Fatih University delivered a speech at the 
rally. At the beginning of the speech, he stressed that the 7 April Revolution, which 
came in response to the call of the leader in 1976, has been able to cleanse the 
university of the reactionary rotten rightwing and remnants of bourgeois and defunct 
styles of culture of the old society. He pointed out that before 7 April 1976 the 
rotten bourgeois reactionary rightwing used the university to spread its defunct 
reactionary venom and ideas to mislead the student masses, to throw them into positions 
hostile to their revolution and to (?divert) them from the revolutionary march and the 
masses, the true owners of the interest of the revolution. 


The secretary of the higher popular committee of Fatih University then delivered a 
speech in which he praised the role of the student masses, who scored victory against 
the rotten reactionary rightwing in the university on 7 April 1976, for uprooting 
reactionaries and agents from the education establishment. He said the students of the 
Great Fatih will always remain the shield of the revolution, proudly standing and 
challenging in the face of all enemies and difficulties. The secretary of the higher 
popular committee of the university saluted the struggle of the Palestinian people and 
their raging uprising against the Zionist enemy in the occupied land. 


Students Issue Statement 
LDO72016 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1600 GMT 7 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Apr (JANA) -- After marching on the information media today, the 
student revolutionary forces issued the following statement: 


While they salute the sixth anniversary of the students’ revolution, which was a climax 
of the Arab and world student struggle, the students announce their march on the 
information media so that it may become an information service of the masses and a 
platform for inciting popular revolution to destroy the partitioned regional reality 
and to sweep away the disintegrating traditional regimes, be they despotic rule, 
tribalism, party rule, factional or class rule; to herald the advent of the power of 
popular congresses and popular committees, to expose the oppressive and exploitative 
forms of relations, wages, rents and trade; and to herald the age of partnership and 
liberation of the essentials which should not be exploited. 


The students, by virtue of their impartial composition and because they have bypassed 
the party-oriented education system which instills fear and terror and checks 
creativeness and because they are able to absorb the contents of the "Green Book" which 
draws the picture of final deliverance and freedom from all forms of suppression and 
enslavement prevailing in the world today, are a living and effective force capable of 
leading the popular and masses revolution to build the jamahiriyah to embrace all 
Arabs. 


8 Apr 82 NORTH AFRICA 


Encouraged by the student revolutionary forces, a number of Pan-Arab progressive forces 
from various Arab arenas have taken part in the students’ march from different 
informational positions and in affirmation of the pan-Arab role of the information 
media of the masses, which will be the students’ effective weapon in exposing defeated, 
partioned Arab reality which is centered on tribalism, sectarianism and party 
affiliation, to raise this information to the level of pan-Arab allegiance to the Arab 
nation in confronting the imperialist and Zionist invasion which is advancing daily at 
the expense of the Arab nation. 


To stress the students’ persistance in continuing the daily struggle against all 
obstacles of the revolutionary train, to destroy all the various mockeries and those 
who advocate them and those lying in wait for the revolution, they declare their 
liquidation of all that hinders the pan-Arab mass information media so that progressive, 
pan-Arab thought may reach all the oppressed and czortured in the Arab homeland and the 
world. Let us go forward and the struggle will continue.

AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES STUDENT GROUPS 3 APR 


LDO32002 Tripoli JANA in English 1812 GMT 3 Apr 82 


[Text] Benghazi, 3 Apr (JAMAHIRIYAN NEWS AGENCY) -- Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader 
of the revolution, met today with the students of Al-Hawari Institute for female teachers, 
January Martyrs secondary school and the Institute of Applied Engineering. He said that 
the students represent the strategic reserve and the big hope for the historical process. 
The revolutionary movement should be extended in order to play a greater role. 


He said that the revolutionary forces should organize themselves in revolutionary 

platforms and that such platforms should be the ultimate aim for the members of revolutionary 
committees. He added: "The revolutionary forces which are the new movement, have a signifi- 
cant presence in the world and have become a living force protecting the new political revo- 

lutionary movement." He said: "The committees should win the world's respect because 

they are an historical movement and a model for international revolutionary politics. 

From this students medium, the revolutionary movement should come into being and incite 

the masses to confront the enemies." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi then spoke of the current Arab situation saying that the reason for 
this present situation is the absence of the Arab masses’ will. He said that in future 
the masses should assert their will in response to the agitation of the revolutionary 
forces. He added that there is no way to stand up to the imperialist Zionist challenges 
except through mobilising the masses’ potentials to achieve victory: The masses should 
play an effective role and assume this natural position. 


8 Apr 82 Q 6 NORTH AFRICA 


He said all political, economic and military establishments in the world have proved to be 
bankrupt and have failed to solve their problems. "This affirms our new dicta which we 
preach to the world. The world has tried all political and economic regimes, and there 
has been no progress, because of the nature of such establishments. The only establish- 
ments which cannot go bankrupt are the masses' establishments. 


"The revolutionary committees movement should be built on historical understanding because 
their mission is an historical one. All of you shoulder this responsibility and the 
revolutionary committees should defeat all obstacles that hinder the flourishing of the 
revolutionary movement. This generation of students is one qualified for this historical 
mission," he concluded. 


GREEK COMMUNIST PARTY HEAD ARRIVES 5 APR 


LDO51900 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 5 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 5 Apr (JANA) -- The secretary general of the Greek Communist Party, 
Kharilaos Florakis, and the delegation accompanying him arrived in Tripoli this evening. 

In a statement to JANA, Florakis said this visit affirms the Greek Communist Party's 
solidarity with the struggle of the Libyan Arab people in their confrontation with colonial- 
ism and imperialism. Moreover, it will enable the delegation to familiarize itself with 

the democratic experience applied in the jamahiriyah and with the country's achievements. 

He stressed that this visit will further consolidate bilateral relations and increase 

mutual cooperation. 


ROMANIA'S PATAN SET TO DISCUSS COOPERATION 


LDO72130 Tripoli JANA in English 1744 GMT 7 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Apr (JANA) -- Mr Ioan Patan, member of the Central Committee of the 
Romanian Communist Party, deputy prime minister and chairman of the Libyan Arab-Romanian 
economic cooperation committee, and his accompanying delegation arrived here this morning. 


In a statement to a JANA correspondent, he acclaimed relations between the two peoples and 
said the visit was aimed at strengthening and discussing aspects of technical and economic 


cooperation between the two friendly countries.
