AL JUFRAH AIRFIELD OPERATIONAL FOR ADVANCED JET AIRCRAFT AL JUFRAH AIRFIELD, LIBYA (SANITIZED)
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp91t00712r000100160004-2
https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-cia-rdp91t00712r000100160004-2

Summary (AI): A December 1983 NPIC briefing attachment says Al Jufrah Airfield in central Libya, reportedly built by the Soviets, had become operational and capable of handling advanced jet aircraft. It lists new radar, bunker, hangar and storage construction. The airfield is about 260 nautical miles southeast of Tripoli, so it is not in the city itself.
• A ground control intercept (GCI) site and a ground control approach (GCA) site were identified at the airfield.
• Construction included 12 Foxbat-capable aircraft bunkers in the WNW fighter dispersal area.
• Construction included 12 medium bomber revetments in the bomber dispersal area.
• Two large aircraft hangars were nearing completion near the center of the airfield complex.
• Analysts said GCI sites are not previously identified at Libyan airfields but are common at Warsaw Pact airfields, which they took as evidence of Soviet involvement.
• Coordinates given: 29-13-00N 016-00-00E.
• 1983-12-20: NPIC issued briefing notes assessing Al Jufrah Airfield as operational for advanced jet aircraft
