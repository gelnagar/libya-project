Summary (AI): FBIS translations of Libyan radio reports from 25 October 1975 say Qadhafi announced arms would be distributed to the masses to defend the revolution, and warned against an attempted coup by a military figure. The rally was held in front of the Revolution Command Council headquarters in Tripoli.
• Qadhafi said arms would be distributed to the masses in Libya to defend the revolution.
• He said up to 100 persons a day could be sacrificed to prevent any fascist from imposing himself by force of arms.
• The reports refer to an attempted coup by an officer who allegedly tried to use the broadcasting station to seize power.
• Qadhafi claimed the plotters had believed they could ignore the Libyan people and suggested some had contacts with foreign or American intelligence.
• 1975-10-25: Qadhafi addressed a night-long rally at the Revolution Command Council headquarters, announcing arms would be distributed to the masses and referring to a failed coup attempt against the regime.
