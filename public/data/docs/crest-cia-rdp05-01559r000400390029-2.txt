AL-QADHDHAFI ON TIES WITH U.S., PRC, PLO, JAPAN 

0W070615 Tokyo NHK Television Network in Japanese 2250 GMT 5 Dec 82 

[Interview with Libyan head of state Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi by three Japanese 
reporters, including NHK Banghazi correspondent Kuwae, at a farm on the outskirts of 
Banghazi on 4 December -- recorded; interview conducted in English with Japanese 
translations appearing in subtitles and simultaneous voice translation] i 
[Text] [Question] What was the main cause of the OAU's failure to convene a summit 
meeting on two occasions? The uncompromising attitude of Jamahiriyah was the main 
cause of the failure... 

[Answer, interrupts] No, that is not true. 

[Question] What do you think was the major reason for the second failure of the OAU 
summit? 

[Answer] It is very clear: the intervention of the United States and France in the 
domestic affairs of some African countries. Because of this, those countries did not 
participate in the summit. Those African countries are not independent in that respect. 
We are willing to establish good relations with the United States as with ‘any other 
country in the world.. Libya has tried time and again to improve relations ‘with the 
United States. However, the United States, particularly the Reagan administration, has 
refused to improve relations with Libya. The United States shows no sign of trying to 
improve relations. 

oe 

[Question] Could you tell us why you visited China recently? 

[Answer] Why not? China is a friendly country and an important country for the Third 
World. Our relations with China have been good thus far. Therefore, my recent visit 
to China is not of particular significance. On my part, I intend to maintain our pre- 
sent relations. 

Our relations with the PLO are also good. The Palestinians are our brothers, and their. 

unit [tani] is also our unit. When they were withdrawing from Beirut, I sent a 

telegram to Chairman ‘Arafat encouraging him to fight to the last drop of their blood. 

But I did not mean to tell them to commit suicide. I think you can understand what I = 
am saying if you look at the massacre that occurred at the refugee camps after the PLO 

withdrew from Beirut. 

We consider Japan a friendly country. Historically, we have had no bad relations and 
at present there are no problems that might hamper the improvement of cooperation bet- 
ween the two sides. There are possibilities for our two countries to cooperate with 
each other in all fields and improve relations. This is my message to the Japanese 

people.
