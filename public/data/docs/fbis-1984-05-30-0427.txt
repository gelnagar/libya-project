LIBYA

Castro Greets Al-Qahadhafi on Africa Day 
LD291312 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
29 May 84 


[Text] The brother commander of the revolution has received a 
cable from the president of Cuba’s Council of State and Council 
of Ministers, President Fidel Castro, on the 21st anniversary of 
the OAU and on Africa Day. He expressed on his own behalf and 
on behalf of the people and Government of the Republic of Cuba 
his warmest congratulations on this occasion, which epitomizes 
the unity of will of the African people against imperialism and 
the enemies of of freedom, progress, and peace. In his cable, 
Castro said the anniversary is celebrated amid repeated threats 
and offensives by imperialism against the peoples struggling for 
freedom, progress, and genuine peace and against the unity of 
the African Continent, which is one of the principal targets of 
these threats. In his cable, President Castro said Cuba wishes to 
reiterate its full solidarity with steadfast Africa, confident in 
Africa’s final victory over imperialism, Zionism, and racism. 


Australian Movement Cables Al-Qadhdhafi 
LD292102 Tripoli JANA in English 1821 GMT 29 May 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Shaaban 29, May 29, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The independent Nasirite movement in Australia 
has condemned the dirty terrorist act a few hired people and 
traitors, agent of American intelligence, the Muslim 
brotherhood, attempted to carry out. 


It called in a cable sent to the leader of the revolution, for hitting 
by the fist of iron all those who attempt to violate the giant 
civilized achievements realized by the people of the Jamahiryah 
and the gains of the Great Al-Fatih Revolution. The movement 
also announced in the cable its total support for the people of the 
Jamahiryah in confronting all terrorist attempts devised by the 
American imperialism against them. 


The masses of Basic People’s Islamic Congress have deplored in 
a cable sent to the leader of the revolution all dirty terrorist 
attempts which the Muslim brotherhood clique, enemies of free- 
dom and progress, tried to carryout against the achievements of 
the Libyan Arab people. They also declared their total solidarity 
with the people of the Jamahiryah in confronting these dirty 
terrorist plots. 


Arrested ‘Terrorists’ on ‘Hostile’ Radio Stations 
LD300857 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0758 GMT 30 May 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 30 May (JANA) — The Muslin Brotherhood 
terrorists who are under arrest here have made detailed 
statements about the broadcasting stations that are hostile to the 
Libyan people, their locations, their funding, the states that 
purchased them, to whom they were handed, and the role of the 
Khartoum government in installing and protecting these hostile 
radios on its territory.
