(Text ] Tripoli--The foreign secretiary at noon today received Robert Mugabe, co-leader 
of the Zimbabwe liberation front, who is currently on a visit to the Socialist People's 
Iibyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


During this meeting, attended by the director of the African Department at the Foreign 
Secretariat, the means by which armed struggle can be escalated against the racist 
regimes in southern Africa--considering it!s the only way to liberate the soil and men 
on the African Continent--were discussed, The means to challenge all capitulationist 
solutions being hatched by colonialism, reaction and the traitors in order to perpetuate 
minority rule in south of the continent were also discussed, 


The foreign secretary stressed to Mr Mugabe the Jamahiriyah's full and unrestricted 
support for the struggle of the people of Zimbabwe to achieve freedom and full inde- 
pendence on their land. 


Mr Mugabe expressed the gratitude and appreciation of the people of Zimbabwe for the 
support extended by the Jamahiriyah, He said that this backing confirms the clear and 
sound line followed by the Great First of September Revolution in its support for 
African liberation causes and in supporting armed struggle against racist regimes in 
the south of the continent,
