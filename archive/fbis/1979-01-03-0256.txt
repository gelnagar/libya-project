Speeches at Tripoli Dinner 
LDO21100Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 2 Jan 79 LD 


{Text ] Tripoli, 2 January--Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud gave a banquet last 

night in honor of DPRK Vice President Pak Song-chol and members of the accompanying 

delegation. The banquet was held at the guest palace in Tripoli and was attended by 
the brother secretaries and department heads at the Foreign Secretariat. 


Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud made a speech, which he began by welcoming the 
guest of the Jamahiriyah and the accompanying delegation. He said: It is not the 

first time we have welcomed Pak Song-chol. We welcomed him before and he knows the 
Jamahiriyah and its officials. 


He affirmed that the visit will provide a good opportunity for strengthening and 
consolidating bilateral relations and also an opportunity to exchange views on the most 
important international issues. 


Staff Major Jallud explained that the talks that friend Pak Song-chol has had during 
this visit have shown that there is a jcint desire to increase the ties of cooperation 
and friendship between the Libyan Arab and Korean peoples. 


Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud stressed that there has been a big development in 
Libyan Arab-Korean relations since the Great 1 September Revolution. This development, 
he said, oan be seen in the exchange of visits between both countries and in the results 
achieved with the Korean leaders, particularly the leader Kim Il-song. 


He said that the people of the Jamahiriyah have great respect for the Korean people and

[Text] Tripoli--This morning in the People's Palace Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud 
received Pak Song-chol, vice president of the DPRK, who is currently on a visit to the 
Jamahiriyah. 


The meeting was attended by Dr ‘Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, foreign secretary, and

SAHARAN, MALAGASY, SOMALI OFFICIALS ARRIVE IN TRIPOLI 
LDO22228Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 2 Jan 79 LD 


[Summary] Foreign Minister Ibrahim Hakim of the Saharan Democratic Arab Republic arrived 
in Tripoli this evening for a visit that will last for several days. Ina statement to 
JANA, Hakim said: "To us--the Saharan people--the Jamahiriyah is our second country; it 
is also the mecca of all revelutionaries in the world. In our struggle, we proceed from 
the thoughts of the leader of the 1 September Revolution as well as of our own leader. 

He stresses the need for the solidarity of all progressive forces with the Algerian people 
following the tragic loss of the struggler Houari Boumediene." 


Malagasy Foreign Minister Jalil [as heard] also arrived in Tripoli for a visit during which 
he will have talks or consolidating mutual cooperation. He expressed his happiness to 

be in the Jamahiriyah, and he conveyed greetings from his people to the people of 
Jamahiriyah. 


"Col Ahmad Sahal, member of the Somali Revolutionary Socialist Party Central Committee 
and head of the party's foreign affairs office, also arrived in Tripoli for a visit of 
several days."
