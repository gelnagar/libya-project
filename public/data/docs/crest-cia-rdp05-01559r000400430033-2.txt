Summary (AI): A May 1988 Libyan TV interview with Qadhdhafi, translated by FBIS, in which he recalls his pre-revolution years as a student in Sabha, Misratah and at the military academy, and discusses the 1969 revolution and the release of prisoners. Nothing specific to Tripoli sites is named beyond passing references.
• Qadhdhafi says he was arrested twice in Sabha as a student and the court called him a small innocent student.
• He says none of those arrested after the revolution had any role in founding the Free Unionist movement in 1959.
• He says he intervened to stop death sentences of some conspirators, and that a former police officer who conspired was freed.
• Interview recorded with two prisoners recently freed by Qadhdhafi; date and place not given.
• 1969-09: Dawn of September 1969 cited as the end of the '4,000 days' of struggle leading to the revolution; an arrest order against Qadhdhafi was reportedly issued in March-September 1969 but not carried out.
