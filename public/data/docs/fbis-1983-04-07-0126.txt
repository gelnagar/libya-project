GREEN BOOK CONFERENCE PREPARATIONS UNDER WAY 


LD062002 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 6 Apr 83 


[Excerpts] Banghazi, € Apr (JANA) -- The first international conference on the thought 
of Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the Green Book, will open tomorrow morning in Banghazi under 
the patronage and with the participation of the’thinker and teacher Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi. It will be attended by leaders and political personalities in their 
intellectual capacities, around 1,000 participants and 100 journalists from all over 
the world, and 50 intellectuals and researchers from the Jamahiriyah. 


This international conference will open at 0900 tomorrow in Qar Yunis University. All 
the preparations have been completed. Two conference sessions will be held each day: a 
morning session from 0900-1300, and an evening session from 1700-2100. Three separate 
halls have been allocated for listening to the submitted papers, and 20 minutes has been 
allocated for the reading of each paper at the rate of 6 papers per session. The rest 
of the time in the sessions is left for discussing the submitted papers. 


IDEOLOGICAL DIALOGUE WITH LEBANESE VIEWED 


LD061720 Tripoli JANA in English 1625 GMT 6 Apr 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada Thani 23, Apr 6, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- An extensive ideo- 
logical dialogue was yesterday conducted between the revolution's leader, Colonel

al-Qadhdhafi, and lecturers at Lebanon's universities who have arrived in Jamahiriyah 
as adherents to Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi's civilizational thought. 


The dialogue centred on the third international theory formulated by the revolutionary 
thinker to resolve the political, economic and social problems, and to realise human- 

ity's happiness in a new era, that of the jamahiriyah (mass) societies. Reference 

was made to the correct concepts of the revolution's green ideology to achieve freedom 
and destroy oppression. This ideology provides radical solutions to all social preb- 

lems and is a genuine expression of the peoples' aspirations. 


A major part of the dialogue was related to Arab conditions, whereby Lebanon is regarded 
as an active arena in which Arab interaction takes place. The Lebanese people play a 
major role in the Arab nation's struggle against the Zionist enemy and imperialist 
forces. 


This is the second meeting between the revolution's leader and Lebanese university lec- 
turers. Last year, he had an ideological dialogue with 64 such lecturers. He affirmed 
that Lebanon "is passing through a tragic period for the Arab nation's sake, while the 
Arabs are behaving like bystanders, as though the Zionist invasion, occupation and 
attempts to force the surrender of Lebanon does not concern the Arabs." 


MOROCCO'S BOUCETTA ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 6 APRIL 


LD061659 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1626 GMT 6 Apr 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 Apr (JANA) -- Moroccan Minister of State for Foreign Affairs M'hamed 
Boucetta arrived in Tripoli this afternoon for a visit to the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


He told JANA at Tripoli International Airport that he was happy to visit the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. He said: I bear a message from King Hassan II to his 
brother Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great Al-Fatih Revolution, in 
connection with the current Arab situation and issues of common concern. Brother 
Boucetta stressed in his statement that there was a pressing need for such consultations 
and exchange of viewpoints, especially at this stage through which our Arab nation is 
passing; there was also a need for serious and unified action for our sacred Arab cause, 
namely the Palestinian cause and the liberation of the occupied Arab territories.
