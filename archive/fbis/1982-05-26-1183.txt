LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI CONDEMNS ZAIRE FOR ISRAELI TIES 


LD251950 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1850 GMT 25 May 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 25 May (JANA) -- Col mu'Ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the revolution, 
has denounced Zaire's restoration of relations with the Zionist enemy. At his meeting 

with the ambassadors of the African states this morning in Tripoli, he stressed that this 

act had rendered a cheap service to our racist enemies in occupied Palestine and South Africa. 


He added the position of the Government of Zaire has become hostile to the Arab nation 
and all the African peoples and this is a great disappointment to us. He said: Today, 
when we are celebrating the 19th anniversary of the OAU, we express our great regret 
that an African state, a member of the organization, has left our ranks and completely 
joined the ranks of the racists, the enemies of Africa. 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi stressed that we cannot but condemn this att, which is a cheap 
service to racists in Africa and occupied Palestine. 


The brother leader told the African ambassadors the fact that the Government of Egypt 
has recognized the Zionist enemy should not be a justification for an African government 
like Zaire to take the step of restoring its relations with the racist entity in 
Palestine, because Egypt was forced into this illegitimate and illegal recognition. 

Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi said this recognition would certainly be abrogated by the 
Egyptian people some day as proven by the fact that the head of the Egyptian regime that 
signed this recognition was killed by the Egyptians themselves. He added: The Africans 
who are celebrating the anniversary of the establishment of the organization all 
strongly urge Zaire immediately to reconsider its decision to restore relations with the 
Zionist entity. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi announced we will not stand idly by in the face of any attempt to 
render service to the racists in occupied Palestine or South Africa. He warned that 

any African state following the example of Zaire would most certainly find itself on the 
losing side; all assistance to it would be cut off, any support to it would stop and it 
would be fought economically and politically. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES AFRICAN AMBASSADORS 25 MAY 


LD251958 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1805 GMT 25 May 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 25 May (JANA) -- Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has emphasized Libya is 
determined to play an active and effective role in resolving the urgent issues facing the 
OAU. During his meeting with the African ambassadors this morning in Tripoli, on the 
occasion of Africa Day, he said we must intensify our efforts for the people of Namibia so 
they can gain their independence and return the Diego Garcia Archipelago to the mother 
land, the African continent. 

The leader of the Great 1 September Revolution emphasized to the African Ambassadors that 
the CAU came into being as a result of the independent African will in 1963, and its 
proclamation marked the culmination of African efforts and conferences to secure a 
regional organization for Africans that would work for the realization of African unity. 
The leader also explained to the African ambassadors on the occasion of African Liberation 
Day on 25 May that this work will remain the strategic objective of the OAU. 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi said the gains won by the African Continent were not achieved 
easily but were realized thanks to sacrifices and continuous struggle against colonialism. 
The leader voiced the hope of seeing the people of South Africa gain their independence 
{words indistinct] the apartheid regime.

More on Remarks 
LD252015 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1857 GMT 25 May 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 25 May (JANA) -- Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has declared that the forth- 
coming OAU summit will find a solution to the Sahara problem now facing the OAU, and 
therefore, he said, presence at such a conference is an absolute necessity. That came 
during his meeting today in Tripoli with the African ambassadors. 


He warned any paralysis that might beset the forthcoming African summit would harm Africa 
alone and would make its enemies rejoice, stressing that the issue is not that of the 
summit's venue, because this is an ordinary session affecting all the organization's 
members and the issue is that of Africa and its organization. 


Colonel Qadhdhafi, leader of the revolution, expressed his full confidence in Africa's 
ability to resolve all its problems, and its ability to secure its rights and overcome 
the racists and the imperialists. 


He said the forthcoming OAU summit will be where all the outstanding African problems 
will be resolved and where the foundation stone will be laid for economic integration and 
strategic cooperation between African states, both rich and poor. 


IMPERIALISM SEEN AS CONTROLLING IRAQI DEFEAT 


LD251516 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 25 May 82 


[Text] International news agencies and radios have for the past 24 hours been reporting 
the Iranian forces’ capture of the Khorramshahr area and the reinstating of Iranian control 
over it. This followed the withdrawal and retreat of the Iraqi forces after more than 18 
months since the latter's invasion of Iranian territory in that area which has been the 
strategic battlefield in the Iraq-Iran war throughout recent months. Regarding the 


recent development of the Iraq-Iran war and its dimensions, our political affairs com- 
mentator wrote the following analysis: 


The Iraqi forces' withdrawal from Khorramshahr and their retreat to the international 
border between Iraq and Iran was no surprise to political observers, particularly during 
the past few months. [as heard] The general development of the Iraq-Iran war recently 
has recorded the military supremacy of the Iranian forces over the Iraqi forces. But there 
is more than one tactical and strategic element behind the defeat of the Saddam regime and 
its military and political downfall in this war. These elements may not be understood by. 
the general public because they are hidden behind a number of political events and facts. 


Before dealing with these elements, it should be pointed out here that the lack of balance 
in Baghdad between the factors or strong motivations and the causes of the war [as heard] 
was behind the defeat of Saddam Husayn, even inside the Iraqi Army itself, while this 
element did not exist in Tehran. The opposite is true. The Iranian masses rallied with 
all their strength around their leadership, in harmony with this leadership's aims, 

to achieve victory from the point of self-defense -- an attitude which was imposed by the 
political leadership in Baghdad not only on the Iranian people but also on the fraternal 
people in Iraq, who throughout the war period, now passing through its final stages, 
remained dissatisfied and rejected the war. Even when the reactionary regimes, under the 
leadership of the Saudi regime, tried to support and shore up the Iraqi regime, they were 
incapable of doing so despite the arms and funds which poured into Baghdad.

This failure to save the Iraqi position in the war was clearly manifested at the recent 
meeting in Kuwait of the GCC, which is under the domination of the Saudi rulers. Des- 
pite the fact that the Iraqi regime's lack of any pan-Arab or nationalist support was 
not declared, there are several elements prominent in the development of events in the 
region. They are part of the theatrical play of the Iraq-Iran, or the Gulf war as ti.e 
imperialist circles like to call it. 


During recent days, international news agencies and radios have reported a number of events 
and political developments, among them the visits by U.S. Secretary of Defense Weinberger 
to Riyadh, Amman and Oman; the Gulf shuttletours by King Husayn of Jordan to a number of 
Arab countries and tours by YAR President ‘Ali "Abdallah Salih to Oman, Riyadh, Amman 

and Baghdad, the countries of the reactionary-imperialist axis in the war. 


On the conclusion of these U.S.-Jordanian-YAR visits and tours, the media reported 
statements to the effect that a military force is being prepared to play a future role 
in the Gulf region, a force which is to be the Trojan horse with a U.S.-Arab appearance, 
but totally foreign in everything else, from head to hooves. . 


Time has revealed the identity of the (?sides) behind the establishment of such a force -- 
the Egyptian army, which no longer has a pan-Arab role following Egypt's fall and 

which has become a pawn moved by the whims of the rulers of the policies of their imper- 
ialist allies. In view of the formation of an Egyptian-Jordanian-YAR force undertaking 
to play the role of a policeman in the Gulf area from forward positions in Shatt al-Arab, 
the Iraqi forces’ role, which they were compelled to play by Saddam Husayn, will come to 
an end in accordance with a studied plan prepared by circles in Washington. Observers are 
of the opinion that this acceleration of the end of Saddam Husayn's involvement in the 
Iraq-Iran war may be due to the fact that he is being given the opportunity to prepare 
for the forthcoming summit conference of the nonaligned countries to be held in Baghdad 
in September. 


It is in harmony with such events and the Iraqi regime's role in them that one can see 
Baghdad's declaration to move toward the resumption of relations with Cairo, so the first 
meeting between the head of the Egyptian regime and the biggest of international gather- 
ings since the boycott and isolation of the Egyptian regime following its signing of the 
treasonous Camp David agreements can take place in an Arab capital. 


Thus, the Iraqi rorces' withdrawal from the war with Iran cannot be explained as being 
a mere defeat for the Saddam Husayn regime but a role which has come to an end in an 
imperialist plan which has ulterior aims.
