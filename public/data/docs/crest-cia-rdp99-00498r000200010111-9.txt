Summary (AI): A Baltimore Sun article reports National Security Adviser Zbigniew Brzezinski defending, before a Senate subcommittee, his use of Billy Carter to seek Libyan support during the Iranian hostage crisis. It also covers Billy Carter's ties to the Libyan government and a meeting with the Libyan People's Bureau secretary in Washington.
• Brzezinski said he called Billy Carter on November 20, 1979, and Carter came to Washington the same day.
• Brzezinski said the strategy was to isolate Iran by gaining Libyan support.
• Investigators focused on calls Billy Carter made on December 6 to Charter Oil in Jacksonville and to the Libyan embassy.
• Calls were placed from Billy Carter's office in the Best Western Motel in Americus, Georgia, to Libya.
• 1979-11-27: Billy Carter meeting with Ali el-Houdari, secretary of the Libyan People's Bureau in Washington, arranged at Brzezinski's request
