LIBYA

UK ANTI-LIBYAN ‘FABRICATION’ PROTESTED 


LD111608 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 11 Dec 82 


{Text] The People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison submitted a strongly worded protest to 
the British Government about the British Foreign Office's leakage of fabricated 

news against the Jamahiriyah to British newspapers. This leak was coordinated with the 
Moroccan regime with the aim of sabotaging the 19th African summit conference in 
Triopli. 


The People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison summoned the British ambassador to the 
Jamahiriyah yesterday and handed him a memorandum regarding this, stressing that such 
attempts absolutely and directly affect relations between the two countries and peoples. 
Furthermore, they contain intentions that do not serve the future of these relations. 


A responsible source at the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison has stressed that what 
was published by some of the British newspapers, in coordination with the British 
Foreign Office and the Moroccan regime, is considered to be part of a campaign of worn 
out fabrications and intrigues against the Libyan Arab people and their revolution. 

He explained that the purpose behind the publication of such fabricated news also aimed 
at instigating disturbances in the African Continent and preventing the convocation of 
the 19th African summit conference in Tripoli.

He stressed that the British newspapers that have published this host of fabricated and 
false allegations have lost the trust and respect of their readers, What has been 
published has been received with laughter and mockery by the Libyans, because the 
Libyans do not have a government: The people in Libya rule and decide through the 
basic people's congress and people's committees and, consequently, challenge everything 
that might harm what they have attained. He warned that persistence in leaking and 
publishing such allegations will hinder relations between the two countries and make 
the Libyan Arab people take a firm and strong stand toward anything that spreads 

chaos or harms the civilizational attainments and transformations in the mass society. 


In its latest issue the London weekly magazine NEW STATESMAN revealed that the British 
Foreign Office leaked false news about the Jamahiriyah with the aid of the diplomatic 
editors of THE GUARDIAN, THE DAILY TELEGRAPH and THE DAILY MAIL at a time when efforts 
were being made to convoke the 19th African summit conference. (Alan George) wrote 

in THE NEW STATESMAN that the British Foreign Officer has been actively involved in the 
fabrication of false reports about the Jamahiriyah and that the diplomatic editors of 
the aforementioned paper have played a major role in this regard. The published 
fabricated reports were blatantly similar and what was published by the three major 
papers had no basis in truth. 


He said the timing of the publication of such fabricated reports expressed the politi- 
cal purpose hoped for from their publication: namely, the prevention of the 
convocation of the African summit in the Jamahiriyah. (Alan George) showed the extent 
of coordination between the British Foreign Office and the diplomatic editors of the 
British papers in the publication of fabricated and misleading news about Libya. He 
stressed that Libya has been a permanent target for campaigns of misleading informat- 
ion. The British Foreign Office fabricated false reports used in this campaign in a 
meeting held in the afternoon of 15 November.
