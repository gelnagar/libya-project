Summary (AI): A 29 March 1979 DCI Alert Memorandum to the National Security Council warns that border tension between Egypt and Libya raises the risk of war, by deliberate attack or escalation of border incidents such as the July 1977 clash. It lists adverse consequences for US interests, including Arab condemnation of Egypt and possible Soviet and Syrian/Iraqi involvement. Nothing here concerns Tripoli specifically.
• The memorandum was issued 29 March 1979 as an alert memorandum to the National Security Council.
• A conflict could prompt Syria and Iraq to send troops to Libya in solidarity, per the assessment.
• Unless Libya provoked the conflict, Arabs might suspect the US (and perhaps Israel) of sanctioning Egyptian military moves.
• 1977-07: Border escalation incident between Egypt and Libya
