LIBYA

ECONOMIC COOPERATION TALKS WITH BULGARIA BEGIN 
LD111857Y Tripoli JANA in English 1804 amt 1l Jan 79 LD 
{Text] Tripoli, January 11, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The ninth session of the Libyan 
Arab-Bulgarian Committee for Economic and Social Cooperation opened at the Foreign 


Secretariat in Tripoli today. 


The meetings are led by the Jamahiriyah trade secretary, Abu Bakr asheSharif, and 
Bulgarian Minister of Building and Building Materials Ivan Sakarev. 


The committee will discuss bilateral cooperation and the drawing up of a programme to 
consolidate relations between the two countries as well as reviewing the implementation 
of the resolutions and recommendations passed during the committee's second session.

GAMBIAN EXTERNAL AFFAIRS MINISTER CONCLUDES VISIT 

LD120000Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 11 Jan 79 LD 

{Text ] Gambian External Affairs Minister Kiti Jabang left Tripoli yesterday, Thursday, 
at the end of a few days visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah during 
which he discussed the bolstering of relations between the two friendly countries. 

The minutes of the meeting of the joint Gambian-Libyan Arab Committee on Cultural and 


Economic Affairs were also signed. It was decided that the two ceuntries would set up 
a joint agricultural committee.
