|] Tripoli, Shawal 14, August 3, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The revolution's 
leader, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, held a banquet last night in honour of President Samora 
Machel of Mozambique. The leader delivered a speech at the beginning of which he wel- 
comed the Mozambican guest. He said: "It pleases me to warmly greet our friend and 
colleague in revolutionary struggle, 'President Samora Machel, and his accompanying 
delegation.'' He also welcomed foreign ministers of brotherly African countries who were 
attending the banquet. 


The leader added: "This visit honours the Jamahiriyah people who always look forward 
to meet their African friends on our land." He stressed: ''Mozambique and Jamahiriyah 
carry a big burden in the responsibility to liberate and unite the African Continent.... 
The African peoples look forward to the results of this meeting and expect a great deal 
from the meeting of progressive revolutionary forces.... This visit is regarded as a 
prelude for the presence of the African presidents on the Libyan African land." 


He added: "In this meeting of ours we affirm our certain determination to unite the 
African Continent and to accomplish the task of liberating it. The meeting of Africans, 
particularly in the present circumstances, is of extreme importance because of the grave 
issues facing Africa at various levels -- political, economic, social and security levels." 


He affirmed: "This meeting would certainly endeavour to solve these problems and open 
the way for resolving the problems which we were unable to resolve in a single meeting 
or a single summit." 


He went on to say: "Those who are trying to confine the task of the African summit to 
yne single political issue are, on the one hand, deluded and on the other hand are 
conspiring against the continent's unity and its supreme causes." He pointed out: "The 
basic problem facing our continent is the economic problem, which has not yet been resolved. 
This does not mean that Africa is*poor and in need for others. Africa has abundance of 
huge resources which the others are ambitious to rob and squander. Africa needs the 

ree and united will of its people. This, we believe, is embodied in the African summit, 
in order to resolve this serious problem." 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added that some African countries are well off and could greatly 
contribute to the development and progress of the less developed African countries. 
"This can only be done through the forthcoming summit." 


"The world around Africa is being regrouped and in the first place developed economically. 
Those who put forward political and side issues to replace this grave problem are in 
actual fact retarding the African Continent. We should at least emulate Europe's example 
of economic unity. Africa should stand on equal footing with Europe and not under its 
influence. Libya for example is a rich African country and has, in addition to the 
natural wealth, a liquidity ready for investment in Africa. Mozambique too is a rich 
country and even she is short of liquidity. She would still have natural resources 

which are also ready for exploitation. This is a good example of an economic integration 
and between two African countries, Libya and Mozambique. One has the liquidity and the 
other has the natural resources that need such liquidity. This integration and equity 
and joint investment programmes between the two friendly countries can be easily worked 
out.

"The capitalist and exploitative forces are trying to sidetrack the African summits

He added: "The confrontation with the racist regimes, which are backed by imperialism, 

has naturally united the Arabs and their African friends in this historic battle.

Machel Delivers Speech 
LD031820 Tripoli JANA in English 1658 GMT 3 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 14, August 3, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- President Samora Machel 
of the Republic of Mozambique delivered a speech at the banquet and greeted the leader of 
the revolution and the Libyan Arab people. 


He said that his visit to the Jamahiriyah is a friendly one for solidarity with the Libyan 
people, and for exchanging the struggle experience of different African peoples. 


"We are visiting Libya to deepen and increase our understanding of the problems facing 

the continent and to closely inspect the great achievement of the Libyan revolution at the 
different social, scientific and technical levels. Such achievements are genuine 
revolutionary developments that brought glory back to the Libyan Arab people. 


The Great lst September Revolution is the revolution for the African revolutionary and & 


progressive countries.... It is the revolution for the African Continent's dignity.... 
It is for the world and humanity as a whole." 


Mr Machel has also praised the role of the Jamahiriyah and that of Colonel al-Qadhdhafi in 
exploiting the continent's natural resources for the benefit of the African peoples. 

"People should be educated and revolutionized in order to allow them to absorb the 
developments that are taking place in Africa especially in countries previously colonialized. 
It is not enough to drive out colonialism. People should be educated for the benefit of 

the African Continent in order to enable them to contribute to the process of wiping out 

the effects of old colonial rule." 


Mr Machel has also expressed his country's willingness to increase cooperation with the 
Janahiriyah and consequently to increase the continent's economic development and 
integration. "We should wage a new battle in this respect and to work towards establishing 
an economic and political integration and consequently a new African economic order. This 
is not an easy task but the African struggle forced colonialism to leave the continent. 

Of course sacrifices are very necessary for our freedom and independence." 


He pointed out that Africa too wants peace and is concerned about world security. 


He added: "We should wage a fierce battle against imperialism. Imperialists are now 
conspiring against the Africans and their economy. The imperialists are even intervening 
in Africa militarily to deal a blow to the independent African countries in order to bring 
the continent back under its direct rule." 


The president condemned the colonial policy in Namibia and South Africa and attacked the 
repeated attacks on Angola noting that the world is not yet aware of the real nature of 
these attacks.

On the question of Chad, he said this is another ailing part of the continent. 


Commenting on the role of the OAU President Machel said the organisation poses a challenge 
to imperialism and is most certainly a force to be reckoned with. However, he advised 
that the OAU should devote its efforts to solving crucial issues rather than preoccupy 
itself with side problems. He added: "The task before the OAU is to end imperialism 

in all its forms and it is important to bear in mind that neither imperialism nor 
colonialism can solve the problems of Africa." 


President Machel moved on to the Arab crisis and said the Palestinians and Arabs should 
not be annihilated in a war of genocide. He deplored the Zionist brutal assault on 
Lebanon and Beirut and criticised the silent reaction of the international community to 
these barbaric crimes against the Palestinian and Lebanese people and which contravene 
recognized human rights. 


President Machel affirmed support fro the just struggle of the oppressed African and Latin 
American people. He concluded by expressing his confidence that his 

bilateral relations between the Libyan Arab and Mozambican people and will certainly 
promote economic relations between the two countries.

AL-"UBAYDI RECEIVES GHANAIAN ENVOY'S CREDENTIALS 


ABO031956 Accra Domestic Service in English 1800 GMT 3 Aug 82 


[Text] Ghana's ambassador to Libya, Col Jeff Asmah, has declared that the events of 
31 December demand that Ghana and Libya should pocl their resources to block all the 
loopholes that tend to invite foreign domination. 


He was speaking at a ceremony at which he presented his credentials to the Libyan foreign 
minister, 'Abd al-'Ubaydi, in Tripoli today. He said Ghana and Libya have been and 

will continue to be the targets of imperialist designs and intrigues. This is because 

their detractors see in their principles and common desire for progress a challenge 

to their neocolonialist interests. They have therefore sworn to use all means at their 
disposal to frustrate the aspirations of the two countries. This situation, Colonel = 


Asmah said, calls for maximum vigilance on the part of the two countries. 


Colonel Asmah expressed the country's appreciation for the financial and other assistance 
which Libya has extended to Ghana since the revolution. He assured Libya that the 
Provisional National Defense Council, the workers, and indeed, the entire population 

of Ghana will do everything to stand against any desperate attempt to frustrate their 
legitimate rights. 


Replying, the Libyan foreign minister, Mr al-'Ubaydi, said Colonel Asmah represents 

a country and a leader whom every Libyan knows is closest to their country and its 
people. He said the Libyan leadership views Ghana as a pioneer in the fight for African 
liberation. Every Libyan also knows that Ghana's leadership is taking the right step 

to ensure justice and social transformation as well as power-sharing. We are aware of 
the position of the imperialists to your efforts, but the Libyan people are with you 

in your confrontations with the enemy. 


On the OAU summit, the Libyan foreign minister said everything is set for the meeting 
to begin on Thursday. He said the heads of state of the OAU member states who would 


be in Libya would be able to take certain policy initiatives to save African unity.
