ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS EMERGENCY SESSION 1-2 JUL 


Secretariat Issues Appeal 
LD021304 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 2 Jul 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 2 Jul (JANA) -- The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 
has addressed a call to the Arab masses at the opening of its extraordinary session in 
Tripoli yesterday. It reads: 


Representatives of Arab political and trade union organizations meeting in Tripoli, 
Socialist People's Libyan Jamahiriyah, today, 1 July, so that the Arab masses may shoulder 
their responsibility in confronting the Zionist-American invasion of Lebanon, confirm: 


First, heroic resistance by the fighters of the joint Lebanese-Palestinian forces against 
the invasion is continuing and the will to fight is strong and unbeatable; morale is 
high because it rests on right, justice and legitimate defense of the homeland and the 
people, and the Arab masses are requested to join this heroic struggle immediately; the 
pan-Arab decision is: victory or martyrdom, and capitulation will not be imposed on our 
great nation. 


Second, the official Arab stand continues to be shameful, exposing the inability and 
colusion of the Arab reactionary regimes and their betrayal of the pan-Arab interests of 
the Arab people and of the supreme national interests of the Lebanese Arab people and 

of all obligations and commitments within the Arab League. It also exposes the weakness 
of relations among the nationalist regimes. 


Third, the Arab masses are ready to defend the Palestinian Revolution and the independence 
and Arabism of Lebanon and to volunteer to fight and donate blood, money and materiel. 

The times confirm that only the Arab masses are capable of performing a mircale, of 
protecting Beirut and supporting the continued existence of the Palestinian Revolution. 
This conference is devoted to giving an impetus to practical plans for battle. In the 
name of [words indistinct] we pray for the souls of the martyrs who fell in Lebanon and 
Palestine. 


Four, the Arab masses throughout the great Arab homeland are called upon to intensify their 
struggle in order to: 


1 -- Secure continuation of the volunteer movement in the ranks of the joint Lebanese- 
Palestinian forces, in affirmation of the glorious popular war which the Palestinian and 
Lebanese heroes have been heroically waging for 4 weeks, inflicting serious damage on 
the enemy and winning the admiration of the world. 


2 -- Boycott the United States economically and diplomatically and blacklist it; boycott 
its goods, ships and aircraft; withdraw Arab deposits in American banks; halt the sale 
of Arab oil to the United States and hit at U.S. interests, using all forms and methods. 


3 -- Intensify the struggle to bring down agent and reactionary Arab regimes which ally 
themselves with the United States and with the enemy and which prevent the Arab masses 
from participating in their liberation battle. 


The battle continues and the victory of the masses is certain. So let us continue the 
struggle in support of the Palestinian Revolution and the Lebanese Nationalist Movement 
and of Syria. Let the masses take to the streets and let every Arab capital become a rock 
of solidarity with Beirut in defense of Arab honor and dignity.

Various Delegates Speak 
LD021042 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 2 Jul 82 


{[Text] Tripoli, 2 Jul (JANA) -- The Arab People's Congress emergency session meetings 
to confront the Zionist-American invasion of Lebanon continued in Tripoli last night. 


At its first session the congress heard speeches by a number of delegates taking part in 
the work of the emergency session. The congress heard speeches by PLO member Abu Arab; 
the representative of the National Movement of the Peninsula and the Gulf; the Union of 
Arab Women secretary general; the representative of the Sudanese Socialist People's Front 
and the Sudanese National Front; [representative of] the Southern Lebanon Cultural 
Council; the secretary of the National Federation of Lebanese Workers Trade Unions; the 
{representative of the] Tunisian Communist Party; the secretary general of the Union of 
Arab Writers and Authors, and the secretary of the Federation of Arab Workers. 


The speakers stressed the need for and the importance of a quick Arab move to stop the 
Zionist invasion of Lebanon and to stop the wholesale massacres of the Lebanese masses 
and the Palestinian Resistance. 


The speakers pointed out that the seriousness of the stage and the [word indistinct] stand 
facing the Palestinian Revolution and the Nationalist Movement in Lebanon calls for an 
immediate Arab move in which all the military, economic and political resources in the 
various areas are used to stop the invasion, the massacre and the liquidation of the 
Palestinian Resistance and the Paiestinian people. 


The speakers emphasized that the burning of Beirut and the liquidation of the Lebanese 
resistance is a new prelude and a main staging point for the Zionist enemy to move toward 
other Arab capitals. The speakers stressed the need for war and the continuation of fighting 
through a total mobilization of the masses and the Arab forces to confront the Zionist- 
American arsenal and the betrayals of Arab reaction. 


The speakers also underlined the importance of placing the American administration on the 
blacklist and of striking at its interests in the Arab homeland by means of mobilizing 
the Arab masses in all areas and of kindling the vehement popular revolution against the 
weak and capitulating Arab reactionary regime. 


The speakers stressed that popular liberation war and striking at the Zionist enemy 
everywhere is the correct and sound way to achieve final victory over the enemy. 


The speakers pointed to the need for mass mobilization of the entire Arab people to 
participate in the extensive war of liberation by means cf volunteers to fight in the battle- 
fields and by collecting donations, supplies, medicine and foodstuffs for the fighters on 
the battlefields. 

Document Issued 


LDO32016 Tripoli JANA in English 1826 GMT 3 Jul 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Ramadan 12, July 3, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The Arab People's Congress 
ended the meetings of its emergency session held between July lst and 2nd in the Jamahiriyah. 
The congress discussed the agenda prepared by its Permanent Secretariat under the title 
“Arab Masses’ Nationalist Responsibility in Confronting Zionist Invasion of Lebanon." 


A document was issued which includes an agenda for the continuation of fighting in a 
popular war of liberation against Zionist-U.S. imperialist invasion of Lebanon. Following 
is its full text: 


Vv. 7 Jul 82 Q 6 NORTH AFRICA 


According to the Arab People's Congress Charter which confirms the right of Arab masses 
to take over the management of their own affairs, to fight with all means, primarily 
through armed struggle so as to liberate every inch of occupied Arab lands, 


And according to what the current stage of the history of Arab struggle has proved, i.e., 
the failure of official governments to confront imperialist, Zionist and reaction [as 
received] alone and away from effective popular participation, 


Also in response to imperialist Zionist challenges against Lebanon and the enemy's siege 
of Beirut with the objective of destroying the Palestinian Revolution and Lebanese 
National Movement, and subjugating Syria, steadfast in the face of imperialist schemes, 


At this crucial stage in the history of Arab-Zionist conflict, the Arab People's Congress 
(APC) held an emergency session in Tripoli, Jamahiriyah on July lst and 2nd 1982, and 
discussed its Permanent Secretariat's agenda entitled “Arab Masses' Nationalist 
Responsibility in Confronting Zionist Invasion of Lebanon," the APC confirms the following: 


1. The determination of Arab masses to mobilize all their energies and capacities to 
wage a popular war of liberation against U.S. imperialist Zionist [as received] and their 
agent reactionary regimes. 


2. The continuation of the struggle to protect the Palestinian Revolution led by the 
Palestine Liberation Organisation so as to enable it to continue its militant armed 
struggle to regain Palestinian people's legitimate and unalienable rights, primarily that 
of returning to their homeland, self-determination and re-establishment of their 
independent country over their national land without any conditions. 


3. APC's total solidarity with the struggle of the Lebanese National Movement and national 
forces in Lebanon so that this country may remain free, united, Arab and democratic. 


4. Its total adherence to Syria's continued steadfastness in the face of all conspiracies 
as a fortress for a comprehensive Arab steadfastness in confronting U.S. imperialist 
Zionist comprehensive onslaught against the Arab liberation movement, Arab reactionary 
regimes’ plan for hegemony together with the capitulationist line these regimes are trying 
to impose on the entire Arab region. 


In order to realise the slogan of the nationalisation of the struggle and because of the 
necessity of Arab masses’ solidarity with joint Palestinian and Lebanese forces and the 
Syrian Army, bravely confronting Zionist invasion troops for nearly a month, and 
challenging with rare courage the enemy 3 weeks' siege of Beirut, the eternal Arab capital, 
also because of the APC's firm belief in the continuation of fighting, steadfastness, 
heroism and confrontation of the invaders (?as) the only means to realise victory, the 
Arab People's Congress has decided the following: 


1. Call upon Arab masses to wage a popular war of liberation against U.S. imperialist 
Zionist invasion of Lebanon and prevent the enemy from imposing its conditions on Lebanon 
which destiny is today threatened by this enemy. 


2. Urge steadfastness and confrontation countries, Iran and all Arab nationalist countries 
to send military units to break up the siege of Beirut and take part in the fighting until 
Zionist occupation of Lebanon is ended. 


3. A call to Steadfastness and Confrontation Front members countries to earmark some of 
its military forces to fight alongside the joint forces and Syrian Army in Lebanon in a 
war of popular liberation. The above-mentioned countries should also guarantee the supply 
of arms and munitions. 


4. The escalation and organisation of the recruitment of Arab, Muslim and other volunteers 
wishing to fight in Lebanon to defeat the Zionist invasion and foil the U.S.-Zionist- 
reactionary scheme.

5. The formation of a national popular committee for jihad (holy war) and volunteering 
to assume the following responsibilities: 


A. To call for jihad and struggle against the imperialism and Zionism and the liberation 
of every inch of the occupied Arab lands. 


B. The reception, training and arming of volunteers and their transportation to the 
battlefront. 


C. The opening of military training camps in the Jamahiriyah, Algeria and the Democratic 
Yemen Republic uvder the supervision of the national people's committee for jihad. 


D. A call to open all fronts, especially the Jordanian front with Israel, for operations 
by Arab freedom-fighters seeking the liberation of Palestine, Lebanon and all occupied 
Arab lands. 


E. The committee will supervise the activities of the following field commands: 
a) The popular liberation war command operations against the enemy on all battlefronts. 


b) The command of operations against U.S. imperialist interests and the liquidation of 
all traitors and hirelings. 


6. The formation of national volunteer recruitment committees throughout the Arab nation 
and any Islamic nation willing to participate in this people's war. These recruitment 
committees are to be attached to the national popular committee for jihad and shall supply 
it with mujahedeen in accordance with a programme to be set later. 


7. The national people's committee for mujahedeen shall consist of representatives of 
the Palestinian Revolution, the Lebanese National Movement, Syria, the Jamahiriyah and 
any other parties ready to fight. The permanent secretariat will further formulate the 
committee's byelaws and all necessary programmes. 


8. A call to the Arab masses to make financial donations to the Arab popular struggle 
fund which is affiliated to the Arab People's Congress. The committee is empowered to 
make the necessary disbursements and open branch offices in the Arab world and abroad. 


9. The Arab People's Congress affirms the need to: 


A. Use oil and financial assets as a weapon on the battle. An oil embargo should be 
imposed on the U.S. and Arab assets withdrawn from American banks. 


B. The opening of all fronts for operations by the fedayeen and mijahedeen, and calling 
upon Jordan to open its front with Israel for Palestinian and Arab fedayeen operations. 
The congress further urges the Arab masses to immediately take action to implement the 
above-stated proposal. 


10. Empowering the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress to follow up work 
and take necessary measures to implement the above decisions and to expose all those who 
waver from implementing these decisions.... 


11. The congress has also decided to send a high-level delegation representing the 
General Feople's Congress to the Soviet Union. The delegation will enter into dialogue 
on the enhancement of relations and the necessity of offering continued political and 
military backing capable of foiling the aggression against Lebanon by Israel backed by 
advanced U.S. weapons of destruction. 


The congress is confident in the fighting will of the Arab masses and, in its awareness 
of the dimensions of the battle against imperialism, Zionism and reaction, calls for 
revolutionary awareness and the realisation of victory which is no doubt assured because 
of our accumulated experience and those of the peoples of Algeria, Vietnam, Iran and 
others in fighting colonialism.
