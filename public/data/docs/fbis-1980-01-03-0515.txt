PLO GROUP SENDS MESSAGE TO GENERAL PEOPLE!S CONGRESS 
LDO21028 Tripoli JANA in English 0816 GMT 2 Jan 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Jan 1, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The PIO Political Committee in the 
Jamahiriyah sent a cable to the General People's Congress reading: 


On the occasion of holding the fifth ordtuary session of the General People's Congress in 
the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah the PLO Political Committee presents its 
warm struggle regards and true wishes that the works of the congress be completely suc- 
cessful for reinforcing struggle consolidation between the two revolutions and among all 
Arab national and progressive forces. 


Freedom fighters... [ellipsis as received] 


Your congress is being convened at a time when our Arab nation is waging one of its most 
honorable battles against the unholy trilateral Carter-Begin-As-Sadat alliance and its 
aggressive plans which attempt to impose defeat on our nation on imperialist-Zionist- 
reactionary terms in order to gain control over our region, 


But our nation's patriotic and progressive freedom-fighting forces will foil the schemes 
of this alliance after the passing of [word indistinct] one year after the signing of the 
treacherous accords by the defeated Egyptian president and (Israel) and the treasonous 
visit to Jerusalem. The unholy alliance has not yet been able to impose capitulation on 
our nation, thanks to the steadfastness of the masses and the heroic struggle of patriotic 
and progressive forces,

ALGERIAN UNDER SECRETARY DEPARTS AFTER TALKS 
LDO12014 Tripoli JANA in English 1710 GMT 1 Jan 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Jan 1, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Mr Mohammad al-Saleh Dambous, under secre- 
tary for foreign affairs in Algeria, left Tripoli this afternoon after a visit for the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah during which discussions took place of what had 
been agreed upon between the foreign secretary and the Algerian minister of foreign affairs 
regarding contacts of coordination between the two countries. 


In a statement to the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY correspondent before his departure, the 
Algerian under secretary expressed his great satisfaction about the results achieved with 
his accompanying delegation during this visit. He pointed out that agreement has been 
reached between the two countries on general secretariats level in the JAMAHIRIYAH and 
Algerian ministries on African, Arab, Asian and European departments level to exchange view- 
points, to deepen unified action and to present a unified policy affirming that cooperation 
toward Africa should be consolidated. 


He also stressed the encouragement of development plan between our two peoples in different 
fields, especially education, transport, information and economic sectors,
