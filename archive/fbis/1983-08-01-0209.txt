LIBYA

Foreign Liaison Source Refutes Habre Claims 
LD300237 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2315 GMT 29 Jul 83 


[Excerpts] It was learned yesterday at UN headquarters in New 
York that the clique of the renegade Habre sent a message to the 
Security Council president protesting against what it termed 
recent Libyan announcements concerning Libyan sovereignty 
over the Aozou Strip and the right to defend its territorial 
integrity, security and safety. 


A source at the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison made the 
following statement about the claims of renegade Habre concern- 
ing Aozou. 


Statements were made by the renegade Habre and the officials 
in his regime regarding claims in SPLAJ [Socialist People’s 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah] territories. Through their false claims 
at international forums, they attempt to emphasize that Aozou 
is a Chadian territory. 


The People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison would like to draw 
everyone’s attention to the fact that these groundless claims 
constitute an interference in the internal affairs of the SPLAJ. 
They also constitute a violation of the Arab Libyan people's 
sovereignty over their territories. If there occurs an internal 
struggle for power in Chad, the SPLAJ has declared her neutral- 
ity vis-a-vis this struggle since such a struggle concerns the 
Chadian people, who should be allowed to settle their internal 
problems free from any foreign intervention. The Jamahiriyah 
also supports the statement by the OAU 19th session bureau 
regarding the situation in Chad and stresses that the OAU should 
stop the series of foreign interventions in Chad. 


The People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison again stresses the 
zravity of the conspiratory role being implenented by the rene- 
yade Habre, which involves foreign powers led by the United 
States and which threatens the Jamahiriyah’s security and her 
territorial integrity, a matter which will be firmly confronted by 
the Libyan Arab people and their revolutionary leadership. 


Spokesman Denies Forces Fighting in Northern Chad 
LD311516 Tripoli JANA in English 1423 GMT 31 Jul 83 


{Text} Tripoli, Shawal 21, July 31, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — A spokesman for the Foreign Liaison Bureau 
today categorically denied the involvement of the Libyan Arab 
Forces in the current fighting between the forces of the unified 
legitimate government and the forces of renegade Hissein Habre 
in northern Chad. 


The spokesman said the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jama- 
hiriyah is carrying out aerial reconnaissance flights inside its 
border and is monitoring the situation especially after the entry 
of renegade Hissein Habre’s forces to the town of Faya Largeau.
