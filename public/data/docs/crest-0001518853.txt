Summary (AI): A 1989 CIA cable reports a source's account that Abdalbasit Ali al-Magrahi traveled to Malta in late September 1989 with former Libyan Arab Airlines manager Lamin Fihma, meeting Maltese citizen Vincent Vassallo and an ESO chief in Malta over a jointly owned travel agency. This concerns Libyan intelligence activity abroad before the Pan Am bombing and has no Tripoli-specific locations.
• Al-Magrahi and Vassallo jointly owned MedTourist Services, a travel agency in Malta.
• Vassallo was recruited by the LAA station manager to help the ESO spot potential recruitment targets.
• The source obtained the information directly from Vassallo; the source had no further information on al-Magrahi.
• The document is marked SECRET and was reviewed in October 1989.
• 1989-09: Abdalbasit Ali al-Magrahi, under the alias 'Abd al Baset Ali, traveled to Malta with former LAA manager Lamin Fihma and met Vincent Vassallo and the ESO chief in Malta; they departed after two or three days.

M. ABU ((AQILA)) AKA 'ABD-ALBA ( (AL-MAGRAHT)), WITH 

(P/1) EVENING 16 OCT 89. P/1 IDENTIFIED AL-MAGRAHI AS 
TRAVELING UNDER ALIAS 'ABD AL BASET ((ALI) TO MALTA IN LATE SEP 89 
WITH FORMER LIBYAN ARAB AIRLINES (LAA) MANAGER IN VALLETTA LAMIN 
((FIHMA)). THEY MET WITH MALTESE CITIZEN VINCENT ((VASSALLO) ) WITH 
WHOM THEY JOINTLY OWN "MEDTOURIST SERVICES," A TRAVEL AGENCY _IN 
MALTA. AL-MAGRAHI AND FIHMA ALSO MET WITH ESO CHIEF/MALTA 

WHO HAS AN INTEREST IN MEDTOURIST AS WELL (NFI) AND 

DEPARTED AFTER TWO OR THREE DAYS. P/1 OBTAINED THE INFORMATION 
FROM VASSALLO DIRECTLY. NOTE THAT P/1 PREVIOUSLY REPORTED THAT 
VASSALLO WHO WORKS AT THE LUQA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT CAFETERIA WAS 
RECRUITED BY THE LAA STATION MANAGER (NEI) TO ASSIST THE ESO IN 
SPOTTING POTENTIAL RECRUITMENT TARGETS. 

2. P/1 HAD NO FURTHER INFORMATION REGARDING AL-MAGRAHI. P/1 

BELIEVES FIHMA WAS A REGULAR LAA EMPLOYEE WHILE IN MALTA WHO ALSO 
SERVED AS AN ESO COOPTEE.
