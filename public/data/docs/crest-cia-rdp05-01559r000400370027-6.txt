Summary (AI): An FBIS London cable (9 January 1976) reports that the Tripoli paper Al-Jihad published serialized excerpts from Qadhdhafi's Green Book, Chapter One, rejecting parliaments, political parties and plebiscites in favour of "popular congresses" and direct popular democracy. It contains no places, events or casualty figures.
• Al-Jihad (Tripoli) published the excerpts on 2, 4 and 5 January 1976, described as Chapter One of the Green Book, to be published shortly.
• The excerpts argue that plebiscites are a mockery of democracy, that parties are an abortion of democracy, and that parliaments are a forgery of democracy.
• The excerpts state that democracy means the people's control of itself, that popular congresses are the only means of popular democracy, and that there can be no proxy for the people.
