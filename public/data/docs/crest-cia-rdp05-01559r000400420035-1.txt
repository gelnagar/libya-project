[Excerpt] Cairo, 21 Dec (MENA)—Egypt and the Euro- 
pean Economic Community signed today a joint project 
agreement on agricultural financing, whereby the EEC 
will offer 49.5 million dollars to the project, 2,200,000 
dollars of which is in the form of a non-repayable grant 
and the rest: to be refunded in the form of four-year 
installments at only a one-per-cent interest, following a 
ten-year period of grace. The agreement was signed by 
Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Agriculture and 
Land Reclamation Dr. Yusuf Wali while the EEC repre- 
sentative here signed for the European Community.
