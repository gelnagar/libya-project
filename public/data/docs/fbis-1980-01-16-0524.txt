LIBYA

IRANIAN AMBASSADOR TO KUWAIT ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 
LD132306 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 13 Jan 80 LD 


[Text] The Iranian ambassador to Kuwait, Dr 'Ali Shams Ardakani, arrived in Tripoli 
this evening on a visit to Libya. 


Dr Ardakani made a statement to a JANA correspondent at the airport in which he said 
that his visit is within the framework of strengthening and bolstering fraternal 
relations between the Libyan Arab and Iranian peoples. 


He explained that there are numerous contacts between the two revolutions which require 
cooperation and bonds in various fields. He added that any mutual cooperation between 
the two Muslim countries, the Jamahiriyah and Iran, is a thorn in the side of the 
traitors and opportunists.
