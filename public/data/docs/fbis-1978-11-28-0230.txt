LIBYA

JALLUD RECEIVES FROLINAT DELEGATION 
LD252120Y Tripoli JANA in English 1801 GMT 25 Nov 78 LD 
[Text] Tripoli, November 25, JANA--Staff-Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud received this 
morning at the headquarters of the General Secretariat of the General People's Congress 
a delegation from Chad Liberation Movement (FROLINAT) headed by Mr. Goukouni Quaddeimi.
