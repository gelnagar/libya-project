LIBYA

JANA CRITICIZES SAUDI ROYAL FAMILY, POLICY 


Details Backing of U.S. 
LD240054 Tripoli JANA in English 1806 GMT 23 Mar 82 


[Text] Washington, 23 Mar (JANA) -- The Saudi reactionary ruling family is totally 
subservient to U.S. policies. An expert in finance and economy summed up the essential 
Saudi backing for the U.S. as follows: 


1. Financing of U.S. Government budgets. 

2. Backing of U.S. dollar. 

3. Financing of U.S. banks and companies and extending investment fields for their 
benefit. 

4. Backing scientific and technological progress in the U.S.A. and promotion of 
U.S. economic and military capacities of invasion. 

5. Depriving Saudi Arabia of the use of these assets unless America agrees to it 
only following tedious and severe conditions. [as received] 


The Saudi government oil company "Petromine" financed some U.S. companies to establish 
a refinery in Houston, Texas. These were Pioneer National Gas, Oklahoma National Gas and 
Philips Petroleum. 


It has become obvious to conclude that the Saudi policies based on oil squandering has 
led to a fantastic accumulation of money which Saudi Arabia cannot utilize. The Saudi 
Oil Minister Zaki Yamani admitted that the kingdom is making stupendous amounts of money 
from its oil and that these revenues surpass its [word indistinct]. 


(?Another) discovery of oil reserves in Saudi Arabia capable of supplying Western 
countries and U.S. markets needs for oil, together with the enormous profits reaped by 
all their monopolies have directed U.S. policies towards such projects as building 
enough American military bases to protect what it calls its interests in the region. 


Thus America built Adahran Airbase close to the most important oil source in the 
peninsula and all the rest [words indistinct] wells monopolised by European and U.S. 
companies throughout the Arab Gulf. In Dammam, another sea base was built to protect 
imperialist interests. A military land route connecting both bases was also set up. 
About a hundred kilometres from the Jordan border. They also built Tabuk base directly 
supervised by U.S. experts. Khamis Mashit base, 100 km away from the Yemeni border is 
equipped with Hawk missiles and the most sophisticated military equipment in tne Middle 
East area and run by more than 900 U.S. officers. Jiddah base has the most 
sophisticated de-coding electronic equipment supervised by American military experts. 
‘Al-Batin' base is part of a network which covers various parts of the Arab [word 
indistinct] was set up to protect the Saudi regime and protects all U.S. oil monopolies, 
continuing thus the domination and exploitation of oil fields and threatening all 
regimes which might oppose oil monopolies in the region. 


Criticizes 'Wicked Line' 
LD231902 Tripoli JANA in English 1752 GMT 23 Mar 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 23 Mar (JANA) -- JANA's political editor has made the following 
comment: "The Saudi ruling family is following a blatant path of complicity in 
assassinating nationalist hopes and aspirations. The agent cliques in Riyadh are 
spending huge sums of money in feeding imperialism and Zionism and enabling them to 
persecute the Arab nation. These rulers are bent on entrenching this wicked line and 
sheltering under its umbrella so as to repress the voice of freedom and deal a blow to 
the popular movements aspiring to salvation from despotism. These rulers deploy their 
reactionary stands and policies to thwart the Arab masses' struggle and steadfastness 
and to sow discord and division within the Arab nation.

Scores Marcos’ Visit 
LD221918 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1815 GMT 22 Mar 82 


[Text] President Ferdinand Marcos of the Philippines presently is visiting the Saudi family 
for talks while his forces continue to slaughter Muslims in the south of the Philippines 
and to murder the women and children of these Muslims. 


JANA political editor exposes the truth about Marcos’ current visit with the Saudi rulers: 
The Saudi rulers have sold out the Philippine Muslims and have abandoned all commitment 
to the struggle these Muslims have been waging against the agent regime. They are doing 
so in line with their policy and in complete subservience to the U.S. administration. 


Marcos has come to meet with the Saudi rulers to thank them for helping to complete the 
deal providing his authorities with a favorable climate to commit further crimes and 
massacres against the Muslims fighting for freedom in the southern Philippines. 


To enable Marcos’ forces to inflict harm on and to murder the Muslims of the Philippines, 
the Saudis have been supporting Marcos with money and oil, giving him a license to kill 
Muslims without mercy. 


Thus, these traitorous rulers have unmasked the truth about themselves and have shown their 
true face as enemies of Islam and the cause of Islam. All the talk about their being cus- 
todians of Islam is no more than falsehoods and lies.
