Summary (AI): A April 1976 CIA-reading-room cable (heavily garbled OCR) reports Qadhdhafi's speech to Benghazi University students, in which he urged loyalty to the 1 September Revolution, called for purging opponents from the university, and said the revolution would not be lenient toward "traitors." Nothing here concerns Tripoli specifically.
• Speech given in Benghazi, not Tripoli; reported in a cable dated April 1976 and a follow-up cable dated 16 April 1976.
• Qadhdhafi reportedly said the students and teaching staff 'must undergo a purge' and that the liquidation should be carried out 'precisely'.
• Qadhdhafi reportedly said that the revolution would not be lenient toward traitors and reactionaries.
• The follow-up cable says Qadhdhafi expounded on the aims of the 'Green Book' style program, including building an industrial base and promoting agrarian reform.
• 1976-04-04: Qadhdhafi addressed Benghazi University students and faculty; the meeting reportedly included calls for a purge of the faculty and student body and for support of the 1 September Revolution.
