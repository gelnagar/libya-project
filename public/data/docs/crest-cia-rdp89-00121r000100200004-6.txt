Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery briefing note from January 1982 reports construction at Al Jufrah airfield in north-central Libya, about 260 nautical miles southeast of Tripoli, which appears designed to support military operations. The main runway and a parallel auxiliary runway/taxiway were under construction, along with hangarettes resembling Soviet types.
• Main runway measures 4,207 by 61 meters, about 70 percent complete, with azimuths of 140/320 degrees.
• Parallel auxiliary runway/taxiway measures 4,656 by 46 meters and is less than 50 percent complete.
• At least four fighter-sized hangarettes under construction at the northwest end of the airfield, similar to Soviet hangarettes.
• A probable barracks/administration area and possible POL storage area are under construction just east of the airfield.
• Coordinates given as 29-10-00N 016-00-00E.
• 1982-01: Construction activity at Al Jufrah airfield revealed that the field would support military operations; first identified in the early period noted in the document.
