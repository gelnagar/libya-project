AL-QDHDHAFI: LIBYA WOULD CUT OIL PRICES IF INDUSTRIAL PRODUCTS ALSO CUT

Summary (AI): A 1975 FBIS translation of a Tripoli domestic radio broadcast and an ANSA report in which Libyan leader Muammar al-Qadhdhafi says oil-consuming industrial nations should blame themselves for the 10 percent OPEC oil price rise, and that Libya would cut oil prices if industrial goods prices were cut. The document concerns oil pricing policy and contains no places in Tripoli or repression details.
• Qadhdhafi's statement was broadcast by Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic on 29 September 1975 and was reported by ANSA from Rome on 15 October 1975.
• The OPEC decision raised oil prices by 10 percent.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya is prepared to reduce the price of its oil in return for reductions by oil-consuming countries in the prices of their industrial exports.
