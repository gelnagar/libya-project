LIBYA

JANA: Al-Qadhdhafi Replies to OAL Message oa Chad 
LD280908 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 28 Jun 83 

{Text] Tripoli, 28 Jun (JANA) ~ In a reply message to Mengistu 
Haile Mariam, president of Socialist Ethiopia and OAU 
chairman, the brother commander of the Great | September 
Revolution, Colonel Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi “expressed con- 
cern over developments in Chad, Libya's neighbor.” In his mes- 
sage Brother Col al-Qadhdhafi affirmed the Socialist People’s 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah’scontinued readiness to hold consulta- 
tions with the OAU on all matters leading to stability in Africa 


He said that Libya wishes to inform the OAU chairman that it 
has not interfered in Chad and that it maintains neutrality in the 
fight for power in Chad. He said that current developments in 
Chad are purely internal Chadian matters and that Libya is 
adhering to its neutrality and noninterference in the Chadian 
problem and asks everyone to do the same. It shares the Chadian 
people's desire to affirm their right to determine their future in 
the manner they choose. 


Brother Col Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi asked the OAU officially 
to form an investigation committee to come to Faya and ascertain 
for itself how everything that is being disseminated by African 
countries and imperialist circles about an alleged Libyan inter- 
ference is no more than a pretext to pave the way for and tojustify 
their intervention in Chad's internal affairs. 


The brother commander Col Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi affirmed 
that if any foreign power intervenes in Chad, Libya will find itself 
obliged to intervene too. He added that Libya is currently adher- 
ing to its neutrality and nonintervention in the Chadian problem 
and asks all others to do the same, and that it shares the Chadian 


people's desire to affirm their right to determine their future as 


they choose. 


Brother al-Qadhdhafi had received a message from Mengistu 
Haile Mariam, current OAU chairman, dealing with Chad. 


KEYHAN Carries Al-Qadhdhafi Interview 14 June 
GF 281340 Tehran Keyhan in Persian 14 Jun 83 p 6 


[Text of interview with Colonel Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi by 
unidentified Keyhan correspondent in Banghazi — date not 
given) 

{Excerpts} [Correspondent] Brother al-Qadhdhafi, as a rev- 
olutionary leader what is your opinion about the aims of US. 
imperialism in the Middle East and the means it is resorting to 
to achieve them? Have these goals been reached or not? 


[Al-Qadhdhafi] The U.S. imperialist strategy is trying to move 
near the Soviet Union and surround it. In this way should a world 
war occur, the American forces will be able to win the war to the 
advantage of capitalism. The important and dangerous issue in 
such a strategy is that the Third World countries — if the 
assumption is correct — and the Middle and Near East countries 


which are near the Soviet borders will be coopted into becoming 
agents of U.S. policies. 


In order to oppose the Warsaw Pact the United States is trying 
to bring Libya under its control to be able to control the southern 
Mediterranean and North Africa. They thus will be able to 
maintain a link between North Africa and southern Europe. 
Similarly, the U.S. strategy wants to keep a watch on the 
movement of oil from Iran, the Arab states, and Latin America 
and the passage of raw materials from South Africa across the 
Mediterranean. 


The Soviet Union also wishes closer ties with countries in order 
to oppose U.S. policies. The Iran of the shah’s era had become a 
vital base to the United States against the Soviet Union in an 
effort to keep a watch on the Soviet Union. The Iranian and 
Libyan revolutions thus were of immense service to the Soviet 
Union since they moved such U.S. bases away from Soviet soil. 
This should make the Soviet Union honor bound to these rev- 
lutions. 


We, of course, revolted to reach our goals but a by-product was 
the service to the Soviet Union when we expelled forces active 
against them from our countries. 


[Correspondent] in your important speech delivered on the occa- 
sion of the March celebrations you stated that the masscs 
announce their unity and support for the Islamic revolution in 
Iran and warned the reactionary Arab countries to refrain from 
acting against this revolution. What are the fruits of such support 
and cooperation now and in the future? 


{Al-Qadhdhafi] It is very obvious that the war imposed by 
Saddam against the Islamic Republic is a result of the support 
of reactionary Arab countries of the region for Saddam and an 
attempt to destroy the Islamic revolution in Iran. Saddam 
imposed the war on Iran as a surrogate of Israel and the United 
States since both Israel and the United States wish to destroy the 
Islamic revolution of Iran. They have given this mission to 
Saddam and the reactionary countries in the region. A country 
such as Iraq which should be a progressive Muslim country 
supporting the Islamic revolution in Iran, Syria, the nationalist 
forces in Lebanon, and the Palestinian and Libyan resistance in 
a revolutionary and united front against Zionism, imperialism, 
and reaction has unfortunately involved itself with the war and 
has suffered irreparable losses. 


{Correspondent] What are the U.S. plans for opposing the rev- 
olutionary forces in Africa and what has the OAU done to 
counter these plans’ 


[Al-Qadhdhafi] Over 50 percent of the members of the OAU are 
reactionaries, U.S. er Zionist mercenaries or puppets of racist 
African regimes. However, there is a progressive faction in the 
OAU headed by Libya. We in the faction support the rev- 
olutionary forces in Africa and invite them to revolt to rid Africa 
of the mercenary regimes that are working for imperialist goals. 
Regimes such as those of Egypt, Sudan, Somalia, Zaire, 
Morocco, Gabon, Senegal, Ivory Coast and Djibouti have only a 
superficial independance and are idols built by the imperialists 


and Zionists. The revolutionary forces in Africa should first of 
all deal heavy blows against such mercenary regimes. 


[Correspondent] in your opinion, what factors are responsible for 
the worldwide oil crisis and how do you think the oil-exporting 
countries should resolve these crises? 


[Al-Qadhdhafi] Unfortunately there are no revolutionary coun- 
tries in OPEC except Iran and Libya. The problems encountered 
by OPEC during the past few years had resulted from the wrong 
policies of the reactionary oil-producing regimes on the orders of 
US. imperialism. The reactionary regimes which followed US. 
policies and its attempts to change OPEC policies resulted in 
crises and differences which we are trying to resolve even today. 


They have realized that their actions have been to their own 
detriment and to the benefit of their mentor, the United States. 
In fact even some of the reactionary regimes of the area have 
become aware of this. They have also realized that unquestioning 
obedience to the United States is to their disadvantage and this 
may motivate them to revise their attitude on the implementation 
of U.S. orders. But most of the OPEC countries are reactionary. 
Because they are reactionary, a crisis and even salvation from the 
present crisis will cost them heavily and they will barely be able 
to come through unscathed. Since most of them are reactionary 
they are not able to have a correct national perspective which 
would be in the interests of their nations. The mutual ties between 
Iran and Libya have had some useful repercussions, but we rally 
other countries such as Algeria and some other countries in 
OPEC around us and make a stronger front within OPEC and 
the difficulties will be resolved with our unity and stance. 


{Correspondent} What are your views on the international con- 
ference on the Green Book which was attended by numerous 
thinkers and scholars of the Third World and what has been the 
effect on the Third World and Africa? 


[Al-Qadhdhafi] This conference was a forum for Muslims ,uni- 
versity professors, and enlightened people to discuss the spread 
of this fresh and new doctrine , the first of its kind for the mastes 
of the world. This theory and the conference on the Green Book 
are based on the tenets of the .rue religion of Islam. 


I had hoped similar symposia would be held in Iran even before 
they were held in Libya. But Iran delayed this for various reasons. 
The initial difficulties of the revolution, the reformation of a 
society left behind by the shah, the Mojahedin-e Khalq, counter- 
revolutionary groups, and other general problems caused the 
delay. 


We hope that these problems will be resolved as soon as possible 
and the situation will be under control so that you may be able 
to continue your revolutionary performance for the benefit of the 
masses. The Iranian revolution is a people’s revolution in the 
complete sense of the word which has been explained in the 
theory of the Green Book and is a revolutionary power which 
invites other masses to stage a revolution. The masses du not need 
armaments such as tanks and machineguns for the success of a 
revolution. The people of Iran are living proof of this. They 
attained success without any arms, especially when the cannon, 
airplanes, and machineguns were in the shah’s hands. 


At the Green Book conference, problems, particularly pertaining 
to the Third World countries were discussed in detail. A com- 
parative study of the theories of Marxism, capitalism, and the 
theory propounded in the Green Book was made. The Green 
theory [as published] came out with flying colors in this compari- 
son. The devout Muslim youth of Iran should particiapte in the 
dissemination of Islamic thought especially because you are part 
of a system based on the masses, a revolution of the people of 
Islam. Under this revolution, it is impossible for a bourgeoisie 
government to come to power.

[Text] Tripoli, 28 Jun (JANA) — The People’s Foreign Liaison 
Bureau has replied to statements of the Sudanese, Egyptian,and 
French Governments, which refer to theirbeing troubled and 
anxious about what is going on in Chad and claiming directly or 
indirectly of Libyan intervention in Chad andthese governments 
commitment to extend military support to Habre. 


The People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau expresses astonishment at 
such irresponsible statements and would like to point out the 
following: 


1: The Jamahiriyah affirms its complete neutrality in the 
struggle now taking place in Chad and invites all to make certain 
of this and [words indistinct] from outside and inside the con- 
tinent to be committed to this neutrality. 


2: These fabricated statements and claims against the Jama- 
hiriyah by both France and Sudan make clear the covert 
intention of these states to intervene directly in the struggle for 
power taking place in Chad between Habre’s faction and the 
forces of the Transitional National Union Government. They 
also make clear the plan involving these states which aims at 
upsetting(?hope) and stability in the African Continent and 
consequently threatens the security and safety of the peop'e of 
the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


3: The Jamahiriyah stresses that any direct outside interven- 
tion in the struggle taking place in Chad will present a threat to 
Libya’s security and safety, something which will absolve the 


Jamzhiriyah from any commitment to neutrality and subse- 
quently force it to intervene.
