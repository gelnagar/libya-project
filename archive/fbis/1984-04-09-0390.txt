LIBYA

Reportage on 2-Day Official Visit by Indira Gandhi 
Met by Jallud 


LDO71628 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1545 GMT 7 Apr 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Apr (JANA) — Mrs Indira Gandhi, prime 
minister of India and current chairman of the Nonaligned Move- 
ment, arrived in Tripoli this afternoon for a visit to the Socialist 
People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. An official welcoming cere- 
mony was held for her led by the brother leader of the revolution, 
Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud. Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis 
Jabir, general commander of the Armed Forces; Brigadier 
Mustafa al-Kharrubi, chief of staff of the Armed Forces; the 
brother assistant secretary of the General People’s Congress; 
secretaries of the popular committees, trade unions and feder- 
ations, professional associations at the General People’s Con- 
gress; the secretary and staff of the people’s committee of the 
municipality of Tripoli; a number of Army officers; heads of the 
diplomatic missions accredited to the Jamahiriyah, and members 
of the Indian expatriate community were also present to welcome 
Mrs gandhi. Mrs Gandhi made a statement to JANA in which 
she confirmed the depth of bilateral relations between the two 
countries and said that she is pleased to visit the Jamahiriyah at 
this time to discuss many issues of international importance and 
of common interest. Mrs Gandhi is accompanied by Mr Ramesh 
Bhandari, secretary at the Foreign Ministry; Mr Sharada 
Prasad, information advisor to Mrs Gandhi; Mr Gharekhani, 
secretary at the prime minister’s office; Mr Dhawan, private 
assistant to Mrs Gandhi; and Mr (Mathur), assistant secretary 
at the Ministry of Home Affairs. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Stresses Ties 
LD071634 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1548 GMT 7 Apr 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Apr (JANA) — Colonel Mu‘ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great Al-Fatih Revolution, made a 
statement to the information delegation which is accompanying 
Mrs Indira Gandhi, Indian prime minister, who today started a 
visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, in which 
he expressed his happiness and that of all Libyans for this visit. 
He stated that this visit is a good impulse for beginning a new 
stage of development in relations. He stressed the importance of 
developing the relations between the Jamahiriyah and India, and 
between the Arabs and India in general. 


At the end of his statement Col Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
thanked Mrs Gandhi for carrying out this visit despite the 
difficult circumstances in her country.
