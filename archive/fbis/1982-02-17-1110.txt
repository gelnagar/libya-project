LIBYA

PDRY LEADER SATISFIED WIT AL-QADHDHAFI MEETING 


NC170746 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 17 Feb 82 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has received a cable from Brother ‘Ali Nasir 
Muhammad, secretary general of the Central Committee of the Socialist Party in the Yemeni 
Democratic Republic, on the occasion of his departure from the Jamahiriyah, in which he 
expressed his profound appreciation of and satisfaction with the outcome of his meeting 
with the brother leader. 


Brother 'Ali Nasir Muhammad siad: We affirm to you that we will remain together in one 
trench for the victory of the Arab nation's struggle and for achieving the goals of this 


nation. 


TOGO PRESIDENT ARRIVES FOR VISIT 12 FEB 


LD121418 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 12 Feb 82 


[Text] The president to Togo, Gnassingbe Eyadema, arrived in Tripoli this afternoon for a 
visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. A big welcome was staged for the 
guest at Tripoli international airport. It was led by Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, 
members of the General People's Congress and members of the General People's Committee, a 
number of officers of the armed forces, and heads of diplomatic missions accredited to the 
Jamahiriyah, 


The guest is accompanied by a delegation including Mouassa Barque, member of the party 
Political Bureau and minister of public works, Koffi Walla, minister of commerce and 
transport and member of the party Central Committee, and a number of Togolese officials. 


The president to Togo told JANA that his visit to the Jamahiriyah fell within the context 
of consolidating good relations between the two countries. He said it is natural to meet 
the brothers in the Jamahiriyah every now and then to discuss cooperation and to study 
problems of interest to our African Continent and the world. 


13 Feb Departure 


LD131624 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 13 Feb 82 


[Text] The brother leader yesterday received Togolese President Gnassingbe Eyadema. President 
Eyadema left Tripoli this morning. The sendoff staged for the guest president at Tripoli 
international airport was headed by Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, members of the 
General People's Committee and heads of diplomatic missions accredited to the Jamahiriyah.
