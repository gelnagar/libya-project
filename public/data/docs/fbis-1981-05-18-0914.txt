LIBYA

RALLY DENOUNCES U.S. CLOSURE OF PEOPLE'S BUREAU 


LD161510 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 16 May 81 


[Text] The Benghazi masses today angrily demonstrated in denunciation of the U.S. 
Government's decision to close down the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
People's Bureau in Washington and to deport its staff. The demonstrators expressed our 
people's determination to face up to the imperialist challenges and to achieve Arab unity 
and liberate the occupied territories. 


The brother secretary of the Benghazi Municipal People's Congress addressed the demonstra- 
tors. He affirmed our Libyan Arab people's ability to reply to all forms of American 
imperialist terrorism and to face up to the Zionist aggression, which is supported by the 
United States and Arab reaction. 


The masses of the Benghazi area, who demonstrated today in protest against the U.S. 
Government's decision to close down the Libyan Arab People's Bureau in Washington and deport 
its officials, have addressed a cable to the brother commander saying: 


Brother Commander of the Great 1 September Revolution: The Benghazi masses who have come 
out in an angry, defiant march wish to declare their support for you along the road of the 
revolution and Arab unity--the road of the popular revolution which marches to eradicate 
the symbols of reaction in Egypt, Sudan, Morocco and the Arabian Peninsula. They declare 
they will remain true loyal soldiers of "Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the commander and symbol 
on the road to final emancipation. 


At the end of the march, the following statement was read: 


Our struggling Arab masses, out of their belief in the aims and objectives of the Great 

1 September Revolution and out of their realization of the great responsibilities which 
lie on the shoulders of the living forces of the Arab nation, in facing all the imperial- 
ist, Zionist and reactionary challenges--the masses which gather today in this great 
popular rally in Benghazi to denounce the U.S. imperialist circles’ decision to close down 
the Libyan Arab People's Bureau in Washington and to deport all those working in it--wish 
to affirm the foi ing: 


1. This provocative action on the part of the American administration constitutes an act 
of aggression against the Libyan Arab people who have chosen to totally control their own 
freedom, to be their own masters and to build their relations with other peoples of the 
world on popular bases divorced from all forms of traditional diplomacy. For this 
reason, the Libyan Arab people have the full right to take all the steps they deem neces- 
sary to reply to these acts of aggression. 


2. This act of aggression is only a link in the chain of acts of terrorism which successive 
U.S. administrations have been committing against the Arab nation and other peoples 

calling for liberation. The production of nuclear weapons, the establishment of military 
bases, the deployment of naval fleets and interference in the internal affairs of the other 
peoples are all components of that terrorism. 


3. The liberation of Palestine, the struggle against imperialism, Zionism and reaction and 
the providing of support to Arab and world liberation movements constitute established 
principles for the Libyan Arab people, from which there can be no retreat and in regard to 
which there can be no slackening, regardless of imperialist pressures and practices. 


4, We strongly denounce all the lagging Arab regimes aligned with the United States, the 
archterrorist, for their stands which are hostile to the masses of the Arab nation. As 
Arab masses who have seized authority, wealth and arms, we declare our rejection of all the 
abominable imperialist plots which are being hatched against the Arab nation, foremost of 
which is the Jamahiriyah, the land of all the Arabs.

The forces of American imperialism, Arab reaction and racist Zionism are all trying to 
besiege the Jamahiriyah. 


5. We declare to the parties to the imperialist-Zionist-reactionary alliance that 
confrontation will not be with the Libyan Arab people alone but with all the progressive 
forces which are allies of the Libyan people, who declare at this popular rally their 
unlimited readiness to check the ferocious imperialist offensive. 


6. The masses of the Arab nation are today called upon, more than at any time in the 
past, to comprehend the dimensions of the American imperialist-Zionist-reactionary plan 
which aims at dividing the Arab nation, sowing the seeds of despair in it and imposing 
surrender and submission on the Arab people and forcing them to squander their wealth 
and march in the caravan of imperialism and its agents. For this reason, the Arab 
masses are called upon to rebel against all the agent reactionary regimes which are in 
league with imperialism and Zionism. They are also called upon to bolster the heroic 
steadfastness of Syria, the Palestinian resistance and the Lebanese National Movement. 


7. We affirm that our relations with the American people are marred by no ambiguity. 
This is because we realize that the American people's will is not their own, due to 
exploitation, oppression and suppression by the American capitalist administration, 
which in turn is governed by the Zionist influence. 


8. The popular masses in this part of the Arab homeland affirm that no part of their 
duty to defend the freedom, sovereignty, rights, achievements, gains and wealth can be 
relinquished. For this reason, they call upon all the honorable men of the nation to 
participate in the struggle against American imperialism, Zionism and Arab reaction. 


9. We declare our stand on the side of the Palestinian and Lebanese peoples who stand 
firmly in the face oc the Zionist arrogance. We also declare our stand on the side of 
the heroic Syrian people who reject capitulationist, defeatist solutions. 


10. American imperialism and its lackeys want to suppress the peoples, starve them and 
enslave them. The time has come for these peoples to reply to the American imperialist 
challenge, to prove that free peoples are stronger than atom bombs, AWACS and naval 
fleets. 


Our struggling Arab masses: We want to tell America through this popular rally that its 
fleets which roam the seas and watch our territorial waters will not frighten us. 
Indeed, they will push us yet forward on the road to revolution. We wish to say to 
America that the bases it is establishinp will not divert us from doing what we have 
determined to do. Indeed, they will increase our resolve. 


We wish to say to America that there is no future for treason and that our Arab nation 
will sooner or later topple the tools of treason in Egypt, Sudan, Morocco and the 
Arabian Peninsula. 


Let America know that we have chosen the road of popular authority. No force can divert 
us from that road. Let America also know that we have chosen socialism. We will not 
allow its filthy capitalists to cancel our choice. Let America know that Palestine is 
Arab and that the foreign body it has planted in the body of the Arab nation will not be 
able to stand in the way of the growing Arab force. Let America know that we have 
decided to live as free men and that no force in the world can divert us from this. 


Greetings to all the free men in the world. Greetings to the Jamahiriyah's students in 
the United States, who rushed to the People's Bureau and formed a new people's committee 
to run it, thereby defying the U.S. administration. 


The Great 1 September Revolution will always be there. Go forward. The struggle will 
continue.
