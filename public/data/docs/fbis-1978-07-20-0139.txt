LIBYA

IRANIAN TROOPS POSITIONED ON PDRY-OMAN BORDER 
LD191704Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 18 Jul 78 LD 
[Text] The shah of Iran has dispatched two units of his armed forces to the Sultanate of 
Oman. One of those two units has taken up positions close to South Yemen's border with 
Oman. Observers have pointed out that, by taking up such positions, the Iranian forces 
mean to prepare for direct military intervention by exploiting the recent events in Yemen.
