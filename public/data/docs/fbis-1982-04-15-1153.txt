LIBYA

SAUDIS COMMIT 'VILE ACT,' CLOSE AIRSPACE 


LD141320 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 14 Apr 82 


[Text] The Saudi regime has committed a vile act conflicting with Arab traditions and 
Islamic values. Saudi Arabia has closed its airspace which is a bridge between Tripoli 
and Aden used to ferry equipment and supplies for the relief of the Yemeni flood victims. 


COMMENTARY CRITICIZES SAUDI STRIKE CALL 


LD142324 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 14 Apr 82 
[Commentary by JANA "political editor" 


[Excerpt] The U.S. imperialist plan to make the Arab masses despair in their struggle against 
the Zionist enemy receives increasing support from the reactionary Saudi regime, which 

sings out about false glories and heroism, and raises imaginary banners calling for holy 

war and prayer in Jerusalem. 


In the course of this support by the regime -- which attempts, with disgusting determination, 
to affirm its reactionism and agentry -- comes its call today for a strike, a call designed 
to steal the limelight in an attempt to get it out of its isolation. It believes that with 
this strike it has liberated Jerusalem, bolstered the struggle of the Palestinian 
revolution, liberated the usurped land from those who seized it; that it has taken revenge 
on the killers of a Palestinian child; that it has brought back respect to those praying 

in the 'Umar Mosque who were mowed down by bullets of treachery and deceit. 


The ruling regime in the peninsula wants to devote itself to misleading, deceiving and 
hanging on to foolish justifications and excuses -- as has been its position through a 

series of disgraceful stands, wavering and cowardice. Yet by doing this it wants every- 

one to thank it for this initiative and to be praised by all -- incense holders in Khartoum, 
custodians of oppression in Muscat and the faltering ruling family in Mogadishu. 


VV. IS Apr &2 Q2 NORTH AFRICA 


What has this strike done and what has it realized for the Palestinian people? From our 
pan-Arab struggling view, it is without content and effectiveness. However, the servants 
of imperialism in Riyadh have greatly relied on it. They claim that it was more effective 
than the stones used to wrest freedom in the West Bank and it aroused more reaction than 
the statements made by the stands of the Palestinian strugglers. It is also claimed to 

be an indication of conquering land, liberating Jerusalem and raising the Palestinian flag; 
a stand dictated by Islamic law and a message in the service of the Islamic world. Wonders 
will never cease. Aren't those Saudi rulers, who called for a strike in support of the 
Palestinian people's struggle in the bank and in Gaza, the ones who took a disgraceful 
stand in support of the Balfour promise and bolstered measures for its implementation. 
Aren't they the ones who in 1948 declared that they were appealing to the Palestinian 
people to be patient and have self-restraint? 


PDRY DEPUTY PRIME MINISTER DEPARTS 14 APR 


LD140944 Tripoli JANA in English 0840 GMT 14 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 14 Apr (JANA) -- ‘Ali Antar, member of the Political Bureau of the Socialist 
Party and first deputy prime minister of Democratic Yemen, and kis accompanying delegation 
left here tonight after a visit to jamahiriyah. Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander- 
in-chief of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, the ambassador of Democratic Yemen to jamahiriyah 
and the North Yemeni charge d'affaires were present to bid farewell to the guests. 


Brigadier Antar expressed in a statement to JANA's correspondent at the airport the Yemini 
people's appreciation and gratitude for the Libyan Arab people's assistance and aid to the 
Yemeni people helping it overcome the difficulties arising for the floods, which had swept 
some regions of Democratic Yemen. He stressed that this aid would remain as a symbol of 
fraternity binding the two brotherly peoples and consolidating bilateral militant relations.

MAURITANIAN OFFICIAL AFFIRMS SUPPORT FOR LIBYANS 


LD150900 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 15 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 15 Apr (JANA) -- Diop Moustapha Limine, assistant permanent secretary of 
the Military Committee of National Salvation and Mauritanian information minister, arrived 
last night in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


V. 15 Apr 82 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


In a statement to JANA, Diop said the Mauritanian people stand by the fraternal Libyan 
Arab people who are facing U.S. plots. We will back them because we are confident of 
the principles and firm stand of the Al-Fatah Revolution in supporting the peoples' 
struggle for their freedom. 


Regarding the brother leader's call to turn the Palestinian people's uprising in the 
occupied Arab territories into a popular revolution, Diop said Mauritania supports this 
call because it can see that the aggression against the Palestinian people is directed 
against the entire free world. We must stand with strength and firmness by the side of 
the Palestinian and African peoples to face U.S. imperialism in the Arab homeland and on 
the African Continent. 


As for the Al-Sa'ud plot to flood the world market with oil, Diop Moustapha Limine called 
for the Arab countries to stand with their Arab brothers exposed to an economic blockade 
aimed at turning them away from their stand and principles.
