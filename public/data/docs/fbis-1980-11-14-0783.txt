LIBYA

STUDENTS TO TRANSFORM INSTITUTES INTO MILITARY BARRACKS 
LD132026 Tripoli JANA in English 1753 GMT 13 Nov 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 Nov (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The student's congress at (Ahmad al- 
Na'eb) institute for teachers in Terhouna, today held an emergency meeting in which the 
students decided to transform their institute into a barracks in response to the leader's 
call in his meeting with the students of Sabha. 


In a message they sent to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi at the end of their congress, the students 
expressed their readiness to defend the great Al-Fatah revolution and to die for the 
victory of the Arab nation's causes as well as the liberation of the Arab world from 
colonialism, Zionism and reaction. 


Basic student congresses held emergency meetings today at three other secondary schools, 
namely (Talayia Alwohda) in Tripoli, (Al Shouala) and (Al Fateh Alzeem) school of 
Ajdabiyah, in response to the leader's call at his meeting with the student forces of 
Sabha, of the significance of total mobilization against the impending imperialist 
threat facing the Arab world. 


The student congresses decided to transform three other schools into military barracks 
and the readiness of the student movement to stand in defence of the great Al-Fatah 
revolution. [as received] 


The student's congress at Ghar Yunis University this morning held an emergency meeting 
in response to the leader's call during his meeting with the students of Sabha in which 
he called for overall mobilization of schools, institutes and colleges. 


The students unanimously agreed to transform the college of medicine into barracks for 
training on various kinds of weapons. 


The students of the college of medicine stressed the importance of militarization of 
universities to enable the students to participate efficiently in the battle of libera- 
tion and final emancipation of Arab man.
