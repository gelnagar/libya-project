Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing on Libyan military deployments in northern Chad and southern Libya (Faya Largeau, Ouniananga Kebir, Kufra, Maatan as Sarra). It concerns Libyan foreign military activity far from Tripoli and contains no Tripoli-specific sites.
• Libyan forces were consolidating in the Faya Largeau area and reinforcing Ouniananga Kebir with armored units.
• At Ouniananga Kebir, aircraft included six SF.260, one Twin Otter transport and two Hind helicopters, with two Hip helicopters arriving.
• Libya moved long-range artillery to the border near Maatan as Sarra and mechanized infantry to Kufra.
• Logistical activity supporting Libyan and dissident forces continued at key bases in southern Libya and northern Chad.
• 1983-08: Libyan armored battalion elements (about 24 armored vehicles, including some tanks) arrived and took defensive positions at the airfield.
• 1983-08: Two Libyan artillery units, including three 130mm M-46 field guns and multiple rocket launchers, arrived near the military camp.
• 1983-08: Ten BMP APCs and three probable Cascavel armored scout cars were at the Kufra military installation.
