• Soviet SA-5 long-range antiaircraft missiles were being installed in at least two Libyan locations; the US protested to Moscow and was rebuffed, per administration officials.
• SA-5 reach is about 150 miles with a ceiling of about 95,000 feet; reports of the number of missiles to be deployed ranged from 36 to 54.

• 1983-10-23: Suicide truck bomb at US Marine headquarters in Beirut killed 241 US servicemen (cited as background). (241 killed)
