Summary (AI): A 1982 NPIC imagery note reports that a Soviet Pula-class merchant ship unloaded SA-2 missile launchers, transporters and electronics vans (for Fan Song radar) at the military transshipment quay in the port of Tripoli, Libya. The shipment included components for at least two SA-2 batteries.
• Shipment included components for at least two SA-2 surface-to-air missile batteries.
• Eleven SA-2 launchers offloaded; the document's transporter count reads 'IINE' (likely nine) by OCR.
• Ten electronics vans associated with the Fan Song tracking and guidance radar were offloaded.
• Coordinates given: 32-54-25N 013-11-15E.
• 1982-10: Offloading of Soviet arms shipment: 11 SA-2 launchers, 9 SA-2 missile transporters and 10 Fan Song electronics vans; 4 more transporters remained in the hold and about 25 prime movers, 3 truck-mounted cranes and 3 cargo trucks were on deck.
