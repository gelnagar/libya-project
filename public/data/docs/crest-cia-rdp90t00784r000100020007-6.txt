Summary (AI): A 1982 NPIC imagery note reports 32 Flogger fighter aircraft parked at the newly built Al Khuwaymat airfield near the Libya-Egypt border, about 370 nm southeast of Benina. The site is far from Tripoli, so it has no direct Tripoli relevance.
• Runway is northwest/southeast (14/32 magnetic), asphalt, with parallel taxiway, high-speed exit ramps, and two concrete parking aprons, all complete.
• Control tower and POL storage were still under construction; no radar or significant electronics were observed.
• Coordinates given as 27-15-53N 021-36-57E.
• Document is an attachment to NPIC memo Z-11685/82, marked SECRET.
• 1982-11: Flogger interceptor aircraft observed for the first time at the newly completed airfield; 32 Flogger (probable E models) parked on two aprons, mostly partially sheeted, suggesting storage. Aircraft may have deployed from Benina, where the Flogger count had been low since February 1982.

1. (SWN) SIGNIFICANCE: FLOGGER INTERCEPTOR AIRCRAFT ARE OBSERVED

PARKED IN ROWS ON BOTH PARKING APRONS (GRAPHICS 1 AND 2). MOST

IN STORAGE. THE FLOGGER MAY HAVE DEPLOYED HERE FROM BENINA 

. AIRFIELD WHERE THE FLOGGER COUNT HAS BEEN LOW 
SINCE FEBRUARY 1982. 
(SWN) AL KHUWAYMAT AIRFIELD WAS IN THE 

EARLY STAGES OF CONSTRUCTION. A NORTHWEST/SOUTHEAST 

(14/32 MAGNETIC) ASPHALT RUNWAY, A PARALLEL TAXIWAY, TWO
