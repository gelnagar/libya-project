LIBYA

Jallud Receives Delegation of Fatah Dissidents 
LD152023 Tripoli JANA in English 1936 GMT 15 Aug 83 


[Nemes in parentheses spelled as received] 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulqgada 7, August 15, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Staff Major Abd ’as-Salam Jallud this 
afternoon received a delegation from the new revolutionary 
movement within Fatah which included Abu Salih [Nimr Salim}, 
member of the Command Committee, Qadri, member of the 
Command Committee, Colonel (Abu Majdi), member of the 
Revolutionary Council, Lt Colonel (‘Abu Jabir), member of the 
Military Council, Mahmud Labadi, official in charge of foreign 
media in the movement, (Abdullah Hassan), deputy secretary 
general of the Palestinian Teachers Union and (Sakher Darwish), 
official responsible for organisations in south Lebanon. 


Nimr Salih Press Conference 
LD151648 Tripoli JANA in English 1451 GMT 15 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulqgada 7, August 15, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Mr Abu Salih [Nimr Salih] member of the 
Command Committee of Fatah revolutionary movement, held a 
press conference in Tripoli yesterday in which he talked about 
the uprising in Fatah and other matters concerning the current 
Arab situation. He said that the uprising within Fatah came to 
protect the continuity of the Palestinian armed resistance and 
that, “our visit to the Jamahiriyah falls within our effort to review 
jointly with our Libyan brother the situation and the means 
needed to ensure the continuity of the armed struggle against the 
imperialist designs on the Arab nation”. 


“The courageous Libyan stands and the steadfastness of Syria 
enables us in Fatah revolutionary movement to carry out our 
duties effectively”. 


Abu Salih added in his press conference that the uprising in 
Fatah is receiving the full backing of the different Palestinian 
factions and the Palestinian people as well as the progressive 
Arab countries. “This was the source of our strength at both Arab 
and international level”. 


He went on to say that this revolutionary movement had given 
rise to the fighting spirit of the fighters and strengthened their 
confidence after they had been led by ’Arafat towards a capitula- 
tory American settlement. 


“This revolutionary movement guarantees the safety of the Pal- 
estinian revolution and ensures its unity during the Palestinian 
people's struggle for their legitimate rights”. 


He added that Fatah revolutionary movement would continue to 
resist the Zionist occupation and expose the US objectives in the 
area which contradict the Palestinian people’s aspiration to 
establish their independent state. He also expressed his satisfac- 
tions towards the Soviet support for the Palestinian people's 
struggle.
