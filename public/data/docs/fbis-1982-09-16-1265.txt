TRIPOLI COMPARES AL-JUMAYYIL TO AS-SADAT 


LD151332 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 15 Sep 82 


[Excerpts] Dispatches from Beirut report that the isolationist Al-Jumayyil will be buried 
this afternoon in his own town. 


The Arab nation's free people today and tomorrow are juibilant about the liquidation at 

the hands of the sons of the heroic Lebanese Arab people of a new As-Sadat, this isola- 
tionist Bashir al-Jumayil and the gang of traitors who were with him. The fate of Bashir 
al-Jumayyil, and before him As-Sadat, is the fate of those who think of betraying the 

Arab nation by negotiating with the enemy and recognizing him. Thus history confirms that 
the Arab people will have no mercy on traitors who betray their cause and will destroy them.

TANZANIAN FOREIGN MINISTER ARRIVES 15 SEPTEMBER 


LD160930 Tripoli JANA in English 0826 GMT 16 Sep 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulqidah 28, September 16, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Salim Ahmad Salim, 
foreign minister of Tanzania, arrived here last night. 


He was quoted by our correspondent as saying that his visit is in the context of strengthen- 
ing relations between the Jamahiriyah and Tanzania and for exchange of opinions about 
African problems. 


He said Africa is now presently faced with many difficulties and problems that need coordina- 
tion and unification of efforts within the countries of the continent in order to fend off 
imperialist plots against African unity. 


The foreign minister of Tanzania added that his visit to Tripoli is part of the intensive 
contacts being made concerning the holding of the 19th session of the OAU as Tanzania 
is one of the African country [as received] assigned to conduct such contacts.
