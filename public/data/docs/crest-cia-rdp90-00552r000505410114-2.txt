Summary (AI): A 1981 Washington Post item reports NBC claims that Libyan leader Qaddafi ordered teams of former Green Berets to assassinate President Reagan after two Libyan jets were shot down in August 1981. A Secret Service spokesman called the report erroneous and said there was no investigation of such a plot.
• NBC Magazine show reported CIA agents said Qaddafi ordered the assassination after the August 19 jet incident.
• A Secret Service spokesman said the reports were erroneous and that no investigation of such a plot existed.
• Reagan was reportedly not attending Sadat's funeral because of the reports.
• 1981-08-19: Two Libyan jets shot down by U.S. planes (as cited by NBC report)
