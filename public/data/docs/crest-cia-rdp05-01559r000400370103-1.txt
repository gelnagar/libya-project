Summary (AI): Excerpts of Muammar al-Qadhdhafi's recorded speech at a 1 September 1976 rally in Martyrs' Square in Tripoli marking the seventh anniversary of the 1969 revolution. He argues the revolution began as a military act but must move beyond military rule to mass participation, describing a "second stage" that began with the Zuwarah speech.
• The speech was broadcast by Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland on 2 September 1976 and recorded for broadcast.
• Qadhdhafi says the revolution's first stage was military, and that a second stage began with the Zuwarah speech, when mass participation took over institutions.
• Qadhdhafi says officials appointed by Revolution Command Council decrees fell to popular pressure, including governors, directors and mayors.
• 1976-09-01: Qadhdhafi addresses rally marking seventh anniversary of the 1 September 1969 revolution
