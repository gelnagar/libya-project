LIBYA

Protest Made to Tunisia Over ‘Arafat Groups 
LD312025 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1945 GMT 31 Jan 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 31 Jan (JANA) — Today the People’s Bureau for 
External Liaison in Tripoli summoned the charge d'affaires of 
the Tunisian High Commission in the Jamahiriyah and informed 
him of the displeasure of the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah with the fact that Tunisia is turning into a center 
for the activities of the gangs formed by Yasir “Arafat to assas- 
sinate Libyan elements working abroad, in revenge for the prin- 
cipled and firm stance of the Jamahiriyah in confronting all the 
capitulatory tendencies that contradict the aspirations and hopes 
of Arab nationalism. 


The charge d'affaires of the Tunisian High Commission was told 
that in the light of the distinguished relations between the two 
countries, the Jamahiriyah finds it unacceptable that Tunisia is 
a center for the subversive activities that Yasir ‘Arafat and his 
clique are engineering against the Libyans.
