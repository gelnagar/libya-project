[Unattributed commentary ] 


[Excerpts] The revolution carried out by the Tunisian people against corruption, the 
dictatorial and oppressive regime, injustice and exploitation, the protection of the 
French, and humiliating subservience is a comprehensive one embracing the masses in 
Qafsah, Safqis, Susah, Qabis, Naftah and in all the Tunisian towns and villages, The 
comprehensiveness of the popular revolution carried out by the Tunisian masses is 
coupled with a continuity, because the revolution definitely has the guarantees of 
continuity, It has finance, equipment and popular mass support, In addition to the 
comprehensiveness which characterized the popular revolution and its certain capabilities 
and ability to continue, the popular revolution against the agent regime of the Tunisian 
protectorate possesses a third element which can be described as being a strategic 
element, because whoever has this element in any struggle will be the victor in the end, 
By this strategic element, we mean that the popular revolution in the Tunisian 
protectorate is fighting on its own ground, 


With the fourth element available to the revolution, namely, the support of the world 
liberation forces for the popular revolution, in addition to the three other elements 
available to it, namely, the comprehensiveness, continuity and the struggle of the 
revolution on its own grounds, we reach the conclusion that the popular revolution will 
settle the current struggle in the Tunisian protectorate in favor of the popular masses 
and the triumph of freedom 


Reliable information indicates that one could say with certainty that there are wide 
areas in the Tunisian protectorate which are now considered liberated areas controlled 
by the people and the brave popular resistance, The fortified areas where the forces 
of the popular revolution are concentrated, which neither the forces of the regime nor 
the French have dared to approach, are areas which are definitely controlled by the 
revolution, From these liberated areas, the forces of the revolution are carrying out 
their brave operations against the camps of the French forces and the military patrols 
which drive through the streets of the Tunisian capital,

On the destructive power struggle witnessed in some countries of the world, President 
Herrera said; "The reason for power struggle has always been economic domima tion, 
There are people who are attempting to assert their independence and identity and their 
right to selfadetermination, But they clash with the interests of the Super powers who 
had alliances and force their hegemony on them," 


President Herrera affirmed the need for the developing countries to defend their political 
and economic independence and work for an independent economy, a self.[word indistinct] 
culture and refrain from being attached with those alliances, 


He pointed out that the price of oil depends on a number of factors, amongst which is 
the world market, the quality of oil and the social conditions for the productions, He 
added that although the situations differ for the different oil producing countries, 

we are attempting to find a system for unified prices, This will encourage us to 
establish a new international system in collaboration with the Third World countries 
and overcome the problems which endanger peace as a result of the intervention of great 
powers, 


He said that his visit reflects the stands or Venezuela and the Indies countries which 
desire to carry out dialogue with OPEC, He affirmed that his visit gave positive results 
particularly in the field or bilateral relations between the Jamahiriyah and Venezuela and 
the consolidation of joint stands in addition to the issue o1 the development agency 
which will unite the Third World, not only in the oil field, but for the search for 

ther energy sources, 


About the limit of oil production in Venezuela, he said that every oil producing country 
takes into consideration the development of the market and its needs to determine the 
level of production, Venezuela started to determine its production level particularly 
at the beginning ot this year
