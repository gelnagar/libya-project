LIBYA

AFGHAN FOREIGN MINISTER MEETS WITH AT-TURAYKI 
LD081452 Tripoli JANA in English 1405 cmt 8 Apr 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, April 8, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Shah Mohammad Dost, the Afghan 

minister of foreign affairs, praised the Jamahiriyah for her assistance to Afghanistan 

and support for Muslims throughout the world. In his meeting with the foreign secretary, 
Mr Dost affirmed the Afghan people's support for the Arab nation in her struggle for the 
liberation of Palestine. He condemned the defeated Egyptian president for his capitulatory 
course and submission to the enemies. 


MASS RALLY IN TRIPOLI SUPPORTS AL-QADHDHAFI 


LD081514 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 qmT 8 Apr 80 LD 


[Text] Hundreds of thousands of Libyan men and women have marched to and gathered in 
Tripoli in an angry march against the enemies of the people, exploiters, dictators, the 
bourgeoisie, reactionaries and their agents. Hundreds of thousands of the people's masses 
have expressed their anger against the society of exploitation and dictatorship. They 
have declared their categorical determination to eradicate, liquidate and use the strongest 
forms of violence against the enemies in support of freedom, They have declared that the 
people's authority is an irreversible choice, that an armed people is an irreversible 
choice, that socialism is an irreversible choice, that the building of the society of 
freedom is an irreversible choice, that the final settlement and the assault will be on 
all fronts. 


Hundreds of thousands of Libyan men and women--producers, peasants, students, soldiers-- 
took a pledge to destroy the enemies of the people's authority. They have decided that 
11 june is the last day for closing the door of repentence in the face of those plotting 
against the people's authority--traitors, exploiter and reactionaries. 


This unprecedented popular march, in which the masses decided to stand in the rain for 
many hours chanting and shouting, is proof of the determination of the Libyan people to 
be their own master. 


Hundreds of thousands have decided to fight the enemies, Israelis, Americans and reactionaries, 
They have declared themselves determined to defend Islam as brought forward by Prophet 
Muhammad and as it came in the Koran, and not Islam defended by Carter and exploited by the 
reaction. Hundreds of thousands have decided to learn a lesson from history and from the 
revolutionaries who made truces with the reaction and were then liquidated by the reaction, 
such as the 23 July revolution, They decided to eradicate the society of exploitation 

and reaction and not to make a truce with it, and to use revolutionary violence against it. 


Hundreds of thousands of masses, who today flocked from various parts of the Jamahiriyah 

to Tripoli, raised hundreds of placards stressing that the will of the masses is unbeatable, 
and that the masses are determined to go on with the purge until the socialist society, the 
society of freedom, is laid down, that Fatah is the day of freedom, that general military 
training brings closer the day of the armed people, and that there is no path for 
strengthening freedom except through crushing reaction.

These placards also declared the Libyan people's acceptance of the challenge and their 
readiness to continue to storm and attack along all fronts, The masses shouted: God 
is great, a devastating storm against reaction, On their placards the masses also 
wrote a pledge to destroy the enemies of the authority of the people. A document of 
pledge and fealty was read out from the popular masses marshaled in the 8 April center 
in Tripoli, Here is the text of the document: [Begin recording by unknown speaker] A 
document of pledge and fealty from the popular marshaled masses; 


1, We promise our revolutionary thinker and leader, our mentor Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
a covenant of loyal men, a covenant that we will not betray, to march forward to action, 
to glory, under his leadership and guided along his way, towards progress and victory. 

We will sweep away anyone who stands in the way, We promise that we are with him body 

and spirit, always, 


2. We declare that there is no difference or distinction between our leader and our 
revolution, There is no difference or distinction between the revolution and the revo- 
lutionary masses, The Libyan people are Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, and Mu'ammar alQadhdhafi 
is the Libyan people, 


3. We the masses assert our final resolve to crush all the instruments of exploitation, 
tyranny and retardation; we are determined to rid ourselves forever of the relics of sub- 
jugation, We are resolved to free our society from exploitation and to win the final 
victory for the new socialist society, the free, happy society in which no one is oppressed 
or exploited, in which there is no lord and no slave, 


4, We declare our complete readiness to march forward toward the new phases of the revo- 
lution, with all its new revolutionary tasks [words indistinct]. 


5. We the masses declare that we accept the challenge of AsSadat, the Americans and the 
Hebrews, We shall face it with a determination that knows no retreat, no defeat, no hesi-e 
tation, a determination that knows only a destroying forward march against all bases of 
treachery and oppression, 


6. We declare our determination, ability and readiness to turn any aggressive attempt 

by the defeated leader and his alliance into a war of liberation and unification in 

which the masses of the Arab people of Egypt will join; they will contribute their living 
revolutionary force; they will (?declare) a sweeping popular revolution, and declare a 
"green" Arab unity. Long live 1 September, Long live Mu'ammar the leader, thinker and
