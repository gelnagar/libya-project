[Excerpt] [Question] chief of Staff, over the last 2 weeks a new chapter has been 
written in the war between Israel and the terrorists, in the Misgav ‘Am affair and 

the penetration into Lebanon, the IDF penetration of Lebanon that came in its wake. 

Is the claim made by the commander of the Northern Command that over the last 6 months 
the IDF has been limited in fighting the terrorists because of political restrictions 
true? 


[Answer] The defense minister mentioned these remarks by the commander of the Northern 
Conmand, and the defense minister confirmed that there are indeed external political 
pressures restricting our freedom of action, in this case, against the terrorists. This 
does not mean that we have not taken the initiative against terrorists, at short range, 
outside the borders of our country. It is difficult to estimate, in this activity, the 
extert of the deterrence achieved by offensive actions, but in fighting against terror, 
initiative is a very important element.

[Question] In a situation of being bound by political restrictions in everything that 
involves the war against the terrorists, do you still feel that you are capable of 
guaranteeing the security of the country's inhabitants against infiltration by terrorists, 
to the degree that you would like to do this? 


[Answer] Even if we had complete freedom of action in every country, at any depth and 
against any target, we could not guarantee that no penetration whatsoever would be 
permitted via one of the borders, in one of the sectors. 


[Question] With regard to the terrorist action in Misgav 'Am and the way in which the 
IDF acted: Do you consider this to be a part of the defensive system you spoke about, 
which is the main factor causing terrorist losses? 


[Answer] When the squad infiltrated and we reacted, you can describe this affair as 
defensive activity. Let's put it this way. Im all modesty, perhaps it was a rescue 
operation--of hostages--the most complicated we have ever had. I do not want to draw 
comparisons with Entebbe, since this was a completely different situation. However, 
from all the incidents we have experienced here, in Israel, this was the most compli- 
cated action. Allow me to give the IDF credit, since whoever had to operate, operated 
in the most perfect way and gained the objective completely. That is, the release of 
the hostages, the rescue of the hostages and the elmination of the terrorists. Only 
we have such soldiers, and such commanders, who can plan and put into operation such 
an activity, such an action.

importance of Elon More. Do all the settlements in Judaea and Samaria have security

[Answer] Security importance for settlements? This can be classified in two parts: 
direct contribution to security and contribution to the renewal of the Jewish colony 
in Eretz Yisra'el. The importance of Elon More, from the security aspect, was given 
and explained in 1973 when the progran for settlement in Judaea and Samaria was for- 
mulated, at exactly the same place and also with the same name.

[Answer] Yes. The answer is positive, like settlement everywhere else in Eretz 
Yisra'el, from before the war of independence and to this day for Judaea and Sanaria 
were sparsely populated before the war of independence: Gush 'Ezyon, Bet Ha'arava, 
'Atarot, Neve Ya'aqov, these were settlements established then. There were difficulties 
of foreigm rule that prevented us from having freedom to settle, but the aim, the under- 
standing, that through settlement we would be able to establish a home in Eretz Yisra'el 
existed many years ago and with very great efforts, with very many sacrifices. 

They knew how to realize this. 


[Question] Do you feel that the integrity of the country and settlements throughout 
all parts of the country have a purely security value? 


[Answer] You speak to me about security, so the answer is positive. When we examine 
what modern war is, geography and topography have supreme importance. I shudder to 
think of what would have happened had the 1973 war broken out along the 1967 border.
