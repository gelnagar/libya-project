LIBYA

ANTI-IMPERIALIST GROUP CABLES AL-QADHDHAFTI 


LD241254 Tripoli JANA in English 0915 GMT 24 Feb 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Feb (JANA) -- The preparatory committee of the International Platform 
To Resist Imperialism, Zionism, Racism and Reaction sent a cable to the leader. The fol- 
lowing is the full text of the cable: 


Brother Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great al-Fatah Revolution: The delegations 
participating in the meetings of the preparatory committee of the International Platform 
To Resist Imperialism, Zionism, Racism and Reaction wishes to renew to you and through 
you to the masses of the Libyan Arab people their total support and backing for your 
brave struggle in confronting imperialist conspiracies and those of their followers. 


International imperialist endeavours led by the United States of America, to threaten 
the jamahiriyah and encircle it are but proof of their fear of danger threatening their 
interests in the entire region. 


The establishment of the international platform is a signal for an important struggle 
at the same (?time) as it is an appreciation from all the nations of the world and its 
dynamic forces for the leading role of the jamahiriyah in the service of international 
struggle opposed to imperialism, as well as recognition of its revolutionary role in 
support of the struggles of national liberation movements and all (?developing) nations. 


We firmly believe in the ability of the leadership of the Al-Fatah Revolution and its 
fighters to carry out the tasks of this struggle with success and enthusiasm.

We are certain that they will make of this platform an important and basic centre to 
consolidate and organise the struggle, to promote its effectiveness successfully 
together with the other centres, forces and political institutions of the entire 
international struggle against imperialism, Zionism, racism and reaction, in the service 
of the freedom, security, sovereignty, democracy, social progress of all the nations and 
the peace and security of the entire world. 


We chose this important occasion to renew our total belief in your great Arab people in 
the jamahiriyah led by their noble revolution and your revolutionary leadership and 
their capacity to crush all conspiracies and aggressions and to realise ambitions and 
revolutionary choice. 


We also want to renew the pledge to remain faithful to the objectives of the masses, 
their ambitions and wishes throughout the world. We pledge that our mass struggle will 
continue and build up in order to realise these objectives, ambitions and wishes. We 
reaffirm our pledge to you and the Arab peoples of the jamahiriyah that we will always 
be ready to contribute with all means to the defence of the jamahiriyah territory, its 
people and revolution. 


Death and defeat to imperialism, Zionism, racism and reaction, victory for jamahiriyah 
struggles. 


LIBYA-GHANA COMMITTEE HOLDS FIRST MEETING 


LD210258 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 2015 GMT 20 Feb 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 Feb (JANA) -- The first meeting of the Libyan Arab-Ghanaian 
committee was held this evening at a conference hall at the Al-Wahat Hotel in Tripoli. 
The brother secretary of the General Popular Committee for Justice and Chris Bukari 
Achibi, a member of the Provisional National Defence Council [PNDC] in Ghana, took part. 


The brother secretary of the General Popular Committee for Justice made a speech in 
which he said that the revolution in Ghana is special to the revolutionaries in the 
world. He noted that the emergence of the popular revolution in Ghana at this time in 
particular is significant for many reasons. 


He expressed the readiness of the jamahiriyah to stand alongside and aid the Ghanaian 
people. He stated that the cause of revolution and freedom in the world is indivisible. 


Mr Chris Bukari Achibi, member of the PNDC in Ghana also spoke. He lauded the 
pioneering role played by the jamahiriyah in confronting imperialism and its 
conspiracies in Africa. 


He expressed the gratitude of the Ghanian people for the support and backing they 
received from the Libyan Arab people and he affirmed the depth of the ties and friend- 
ship which link the people of the jamahiriyah and Ghana. Mr Chris Bukari Achibi 
stressed that the Hilla Limann regime enslaved the Ghanaian people and overburdened the 
country with foreign debts. 


In conclusion Chris Bukari Achibi stressed that the Ghanaian people have resolved to 
live free and to be ready to stand up to all the imperialist and reactionary conspiracies 
in Africa. 


Cooperation Treaty Signed 


LD242232 Tripoli JANA in English 1823 GMT 24 Feb 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Feb (JANA) -- A treaty of cooperation was today signed between the 
jamahiriyah and the Republic of Ghana. 


V. 25 Feb 82 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


The treaty was signed by the jamahiriyah Secretary of the General People's Committee 
for Justice, Mr Muhammad Abu al-Qasim az-Zuwayy and Ghanian member of the National 
Defence Council, Mr Chris Bukari Achibi. 


The treaty stipulates cooperation in the fields of culture, information, health and 
economy between the jamahiriyah and the Republic of Ghana.
