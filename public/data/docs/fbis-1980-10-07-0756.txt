LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI REPORTEDLY TO VISIT USSR WITH AL-ASAD 
LDO70920 Kuwait KUNA in Arabic 0746 GMT 7 Oct 80 


{Text] Kuwait, 7 Oct (KUNA)--The Kuwaiti newspaper AL-ANBA today says that the Libyan 
leader Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi may visit the Soviet Union during the stay of Syrian 
President Hafiz al-Asad there later this month. 


The paper quotes informed sources as saying that the purpose of the visit is for Libya and 
Syria to coordinate with the Soviet Union the formation of a joint plan to confront the 
Egyptian-Israeli-U.S. Camp David agreements and the steps that followed them. 


Syrian President al-Asad is expected to visit Moscow before the middle of this month, but 
the date of the visit was not anncunced.
