LIBYA

PDRY PRESIDENT ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI FOR VISIT 
LD231702 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 23 May 79 LD 


[Text] 'Abd al-Fattah Isma'il, president of the Presidium of the PDRY, arrived at 
Tripoli international airport at noon today for a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah. He is heading an official delegation which includes the foreign, 
industry and agriculture ministers and a number of officials of the Presidium, An 
official welcome was accorded to the Yemeni president, with staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam 
Ahmad Jallud heading the welcoming party. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Guest 
LD24O445 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 23 May 79 LD 
[Text] Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdafi, leader of the revolution, this evening received 'Abd 
al-Fattah Isma'il, president of the Presidium of the PDRY, The meeting was attended by 
staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and Muhammad Salih Muti', PDRY foreign minister. 
Jallud, Isma'il Hold Talks 
LD240448 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 23 May 79 LD 
[Text] Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and 'Abd al-Fattah Isma'il, president of 
the Presidium of the PDRY, this evening held a meeting at the guest palace in Tripoli. 
They discussed aspects of cooperation between the two sister countries in various 


fields, the Arab situation in particular and the international situation in general. 
At an earlier meeting they discussed some Arab questions of common interest.
