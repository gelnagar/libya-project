The text names Tripoli only in passing, so it has little specific place information.
• The interview was published in Abu Dhabi newspaper Al-Ittihad, 2 May 1986 (CREST document text date 1986-05-01).
• Qadhdhafi calls the U.S. raids the 'tenth crusade' and refers to the Gulf of Sirte mining of Grenada and the U.S. presence off Lebanon.
• Qadhdhafi says the tent 'remained erect and vanquished the NATO fleets', and that Libya will not join the Warsaw Pact.
• Qadhdhafi says the Soviet stand should dispel doubts and mentions article 9 of the Warsaw Pact document in discussion.
• He states that unity with Algeria, Syria, Yemen and Sudan continues.
• 1986-04: U.S. air raids on Libya, which Qadhdhafi describes as bombing of cities, universities and hospitals; he says his adopted daughter Hana was killed and his sons Khamis and Sayf were wounded.
