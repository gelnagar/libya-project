LIBYA

JALLUD RECEIVES HUNGARIAN MINISTER 21 MAY 
LD220910 Tripoli JANA in English 0822 GMT 22 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli, May 21, JAMANIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--At the People's Palace in Tripoli this 
morning, Staff Major 'Abd as-Salah Ahmad Jallud received Gyula Szeker, Hungarian deputy 

prime minister currently visiting the Jamahiriyah. The meeting was attended by Abu Zayd 
‘Umar Durdah, secretary of general people's committee for economy. 


JALLUD RECEIVES YAR ARMY CHIEF 22 MAY 
LD230920 Tripoli JANA in English 0816 GMT 23 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli, May 23, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Staff Major Jallui received last night 
Lieutenant Colonel 'Abd al-'Aziz al-Boti, the chief of staff of the North Yemeni Armed 
Forces visiting Libya. The meeting took place in Sha'b Palace in Tripoli. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES GREETINGS FROM CASTRO 
LD221940 Tripoli JANA in English 1832 GMT 22 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli, May 22, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader 
of the grand Al-Fatah revolution received the following message from the Cuban President 
Fidel Castro on the occasion of Africa Day: 


I take the opportunity of the Day of Africa to express to the peoples of the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and yourself our brotherly regards in the name of the 
people and Government of the Republic of Cuba. 


The Cuban people, who are linked with Africa with dcep historic eternal roots, affirm 
today their solidarity with the African peoples against imperialism, colonialism and 
racism including Zionism and racial discrimination. They also affirm their solidarity 
with the full economic political and social independence of the African countries. 


We are confident that the joint struggle of the Cuban and Libyan peoples will continue to 
develop the established friendly ties between them. I take this opportunity to reaffirm 
my great appreciation. 


{[Signed] Castro, president of the Councils of State and Ministers in the Republic of Cuba.
