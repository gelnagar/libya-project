SFRY’s Culafic Signs Security Accord, Ends Visit 
LD201514 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
20 Feb 85 


{Text]A protocol of cooperation in security and justice was signed 
this morning by the Secretariat of the General People’s Commit- 
tee for Justice in the Jamahiriyah and the Federal Secretariat 
for Internal Affairs in Yugoslavia within the context of cooper- 
ation between the two friendly countries. It was signed by the 
secretary of the General People’s Committee for Justice and on 
the Yugoslav side by Dobroslav Culafic, member of the Federal 
Executive Council and federal secretary for internal affairs. The 
protocol provides for the consolidation of cooperation in justice 
and security, the exchange of visits and the organizing of training 
courses. 


Dobroslav Culafic expressed satisfaction at the results of his visit 
to the Jamahiriyah and said after the signing ceremony that the 
visit has given a new strong impetus for strengthening the ties of 
friendship and developing bilateral relations between the two 
friendly people. Finally he praised the great achievements and 
changes achieved by the people of the Jamahiriyah in various 
spheres owing to the Great al-Fatih Revolution. 


Dobroslay Culafic left Tripoli after a few days’ visit to the 
Jamahiriyah.
