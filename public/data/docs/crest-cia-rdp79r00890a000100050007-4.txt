Summary (AI): A 1953 CIA document assesses Libya as weak and at risk of breaking apart, citing Cyrenaican separatism, British financial dependence, and Egyptian and Italian pressures. It also discusses the US effort to secure a base agreement for Wheelus Field outside Tripoli.
• Libyan unity, established by the UN, was threatened by Cyrenaican separatism.
• Libya was largely dependent on British financial assistance.
• Egypt reportedly financed terrorist cells to forestall Libyan parliament approval of base agreements with the West.
• A conflict between the prime minister and the king was reportedly resolved.
• The US offered $1,000,000 for use of the field, though no law required it, to strengthen the pro-Western prime minister against the King.
