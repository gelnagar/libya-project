LIBYA

TRIPOLI DEMONSTRATION AGAINST BEIRUT MASSACRE 


LD241826 Tripoli JANA in Ar oic 1555 GMT 24 Sep 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Sep (JANA) -- Tripoli at noon today witnessed a tumultuous demonstration 
of anger and denunciation against the horrible massacres committed by Zionist terrorist 
gangs against old people, women and children at the Sabra and Shatila camps in West Beirut 
and against the weakness of Arab reaction and its disregard for the feelings of the Arab 
masses. re 


The Arab masses that participated in the demonstration affirmed their determination to 
face up to the enemy's barbaric arrogance and to destroy the Zionist train of death that 
is fueled by American imperialism with a view to destroying Arab existence. 


The Arab masses affirmed their determination to storm all dens of Arab reaction which 
deeply persisted in their collusion with imperialism and Zionism in a manner which 
exceeds all imagination. 


The Arab masses who marched through the streets of Tripoli carried placards expressing 
their determination to escalate confrontation with the Zionist fascist enemy until 

the achievement of final victory and the mobilization of all the capabilities of the 
Arab masses for this inevitable confrontation and for avenging the innocent souls that 
were lost in this abominable massacre, which is unparalleled in contemporary history. 


Abu Tariq, PLO bureau chief in Tripoli, addressed the masses participating in the 
demonstration. He affirmed the continuation of the fighting against the oppression 

of the indiscriminate fascist Zionists and the escalation of the confrontation against 
the Arab reactionary regimes which plotted against the will of the Arab masses. 


He affirmed that the will of the Arab masses will triumph and will destroy all the 
imperialist and Zionist plots directed against their very existence and will destroy 
the reactionary dens in the Arab homeland which plot against their freedom and prevent 
them from attaining their ambitions. 


Afterwards, the demonstrators proceeded to the Mawlay Muhammad Mosque in Tripoli, 
where they prayed for the souls of the departed, the martyrs of the brutal massacres 
carried out by the Zionists in West Beirut.
