Summary (AI): A August 1986 CIA reference aid profiling Qadhafi's inner circle, key aides, Revolutionary Committee radicals, front men and exiled opposition figures. It describes their roles in security, military, intelligence and foreign operations, with few specifics tied to Tripoli sites.
• Qadhafi's Revolutionary Command Council of 12 was abolished in 1977.
• Abd al-Rahman al-Sayyid commanded Libyan forces in Chad 1983 to April 1986.
• Muhammad al-Majdhub directed the takeover of the Rome People's Bureau by Libyan radicals in 1984.
• Assassinations of anti-Qadhafi exiles were planned from the RCB Operations Room.
• Muhammad Yusuf al-Magaryaf's group was responsible for a May 1984 clash with regime forces in Tripoli.
