LIBYA

SOVIET MINISTER RYZHOV ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 7 DEC 


LDO71854 Tripoli JANAin Arabic 1830 GMT 7 Dec 82 


[Text] The Soviet deputy foreign minister, Nikita Ryzhov, arrived in Tripoli this 
afternoon on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


BRIEFS 


LIBYAN-POLISH COMMITTEE MEETS -- Tripoli, Safar 19, Dec. 5, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- 
The meetings of the 4th session of Libyan Arab-Polish joint Economic and Social Cooper- 
ation Committee began here this morning at the Foreign Liaison Bureau to study and 
review aspects of bilateral cooperation in all fields. The meetings were attended 

by the General People's Committee's secretary for public utilities, a number of 

experts and technicians from different secretariats and by Mr. Tadeusz Hupalowski, 
Poland's minister of economy and environment as well as a number of Polish experts 

and technicians. [Text] [Tripoli JANA in English 1757 GMT 5 Dec 82 LD] 


L-TALHI MEETS POLISH OFFICIAL -- Tripoli, Safar 19, Dec. 5, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- 
Mr Jadallah ‘Aziz at-Talhi, the secretary of the General People's Committee met this 
morning with Poland's Minister of Administration, Local Economy and Preservation of 
Environment General Tadeusz Hupalowski who is currently visiting the Jamahiriyah 

to attend the meetings of the joint Libyan-Polish Committee for Economic and Social 
Cooperation which began here this morning. [Text] [Tripoli JANA in English 1814 GMT 
5 Dec 82 LD]
