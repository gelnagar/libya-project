LIBYA

AFP REPORTS ATTEMPT TO IMPOSE TREATY ON CHAD 


AB101419 Paris AFP in French 1238 GMT 10 Mar 83 


[Text] Paris, 10 Mar (AFP) -- It was learned from an African diplomatic source in 
Paris on Thursday that Libya wants to impose on Hissein Habre's government the signing 
of a treaty proclaiming the Arabic character of Chad and providing for a "strategic 
alliance" between the two countries as well as Ndjamena's recognition of the "historical 
border" between Chad and Libya. 


The same source explained that the proposed treaty -- which constitutes a condition for 
Libya's official recognition of President Habre's government -- was submitted to a 
Chadian delegation which visited Tripoli last week. The delegation's visit to the 
Libyan capital followed the visit to Ndjamena by a Libyan mission led by Colonel Radwan, 
former commander of the Libyan expeditionary corps in Chad until November 1981. 


The same sources added that the Chadian authorities consider the Libyan proposals a 
"veritable diktat" which for them means: "You either agree with us or we will declare 
war on you.” 


In this connection, observers recalled that the authorities in Ndjamena have for the 
past few weeks been denouncing “Libya's military preparations" against their country. 


According to this African diplomatic source, Chad's reply to Tripoli's proposals con- 
sisted -- as far as the first point was concerned -- in demonstrating that although 
Chad is situated between white and black Africa and has a majority of Muslims, it 

is not an Arab state. 


It is not in Chad's interest to tie itself to any country whatsoever unless it is 
through treaties of goodneighborliness and nonaggression, the Chadian authorities 
replied, according to the same source. The authorities also wondered about the real 
position of the "historical border" between Chad and Libya. During the discussions in 
Tripoli, the Chadian delegation is said to have demanded the evacuation of the Libyan 
troops from the advanced posts at Ogou, Miski, Gouro, and Tibesti in the extreme 

north of Chad. According to the same source, the Chadian-Libyan negotiations were sus- 
pended last Saturday, but the contacts do not seem to have been broken. 


Questioned by AFP, the Chadian charge d'affaires in Paris, Allam Ahmen, considered spe- 
culation on the Chadian-Libyan discussions inopportune at a time when, he said, "The 
Chadian and Libyan Governments have begun direct dialogue for the first time."

"The desire for peace expressed by these contacts must not be compromised," continued 
Mr Allam-mi. The real problem of Chad's ills has finally been posed; it is the problem 
of peaceful coexistence between two neighboring and brotherly countries and peoples who 
share a common history and Arab-Islamic culture." 


After denouncing "the false Chadian-Libyan problems created and maintained," according 
to him, by "a Chadian-Libyan mafia with the blessing of the regime in Tripoli," Mr 
Allam-mi stressed the Chadian Government's desire to "explore all peaceful avenues to 
achieve the normalization of relations between the two countries." 


It is for Tripoli, he said, "to stop all aggression against Chad and to withdraw its 
forces now stationed in the interior of Chad. In the absence of a diplomatic solution, 
Chad would know how to defend itself and find friends to help it," Mr Allami declared. 


AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS MESSAGE TO PDRY CHAIRMAN 


GF100930 Aden Domestic Service in Arabic 1900 GMT 9 Mar 83 


("Text ' of message sent by Libya's Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to PDRY Chairman ‘Ali Nasir 
Muhammad in reply to the cable of support -- date not given] 


[Text] We highly appreciate and express gratitude for receiving your cable in which 
you expressed your support and the support of your people for the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in light of the U.S. military threats. The U.S. provocative 
actions -- which ignored all international laws and which represented a direct threat 
to Libyan coasts by intruding into the terrorial airspace and waters of the 
Jamahiriyah -- constitute a serious danger to peace and security in the region and in 
the world. In the past the United States deployed its aircraft carrier and ships near 
the Arab-Libyan coasts under the pretext of carrying out military maneuvers. 


Today the [United States] unveiled its aggressive nature and admitted to the world that 
it deployed its naval force this time to the Libyan coasts in order to terrify and 
scare the Arab Libyan people. The policy of power and threats will not frighten the 
Arab Libyan people, nor will it make them deviate from the principles in which they 
believed. The Jamahiriyah will continue to support the national liberation movements 
and to fight U.S. imperialism, in addition to divulging its plans which are aimed at 
enslaving the people and denying them their freedom. 


Despite the announcement that the aircraft carrier and the ships that protect it have 
been withdrawn, the U.S. officials have yet to stop issuing statements that are anta- 
gonist to the Jamahiriyah and its policies. By doing so, they are preparing the world 
public opinion for further violations ard new aggression. The U.S. officials adopted 
the policy of distortion in order to justify their aggressive policies and their 
military plans. 


Mr Chairman, the official and popular information media have denounced the United 
States use of power policy and its military extortion against the Jamahiriyah, With 
the support and the backing of the national and revolutionary powers and the voices of 
justice everywhere, we will powerfully confront any new attempt aimed at violating our 
territories, our territorial airspace, and our territorial waters, no matter what the 
source of aggression and how powerful it may be. Any attempt to pressure us and to 
force us to change our anticolonialism, anti-U.S. imperialism policy, and our support 
of worldwide liberation movements will be defeated. We are sure that national and 
revolutionary powers which defend justice and rights will be victorous while colonial- 
ism and imperialism which supports aggression will he defeated.

I hope, Mr Chairman, that you will accept my profound thanks and appreciation and the 
appreciation of people the Socialist People's Libyan Jamahiriyah for your initiative in 
supporting and backing the people of the Jamahiriyah vis-a-vis this imperialist 
feverish aggression. We stress our conviction that your profound solidarity with us 
has reinforced our confidence in the importance of jointly struggling on the road of 
honorable struggle to preserve our freedom and the safety of our country. 


My best fraternal regards.

ASH-SHARQ AL-AWSAT DISMISSES DANGER TO SUDAN 


PMO081101 London ASH-SHARQ AL-AWSAT in Arabic 3 Mar 83 p 7 
[Ahmad Baha' ad-Din column "Diary": "Libya, the United States and Sudan"] 


[Text] There is no doubt that the story of the Libyan danger to Egypt was a pure 
American invention. 


First, who would believe that Libya, even if given all the arms in the world, could be 
a danger? It is true that Libya has a great quantity of arms but they are stockpiled. 
Arms alone cannot fight. What Al-Qadhdhafi acutely lacks is soldiers. Also, Egypt is 
not Chad. It was strange that Egypt and Sudan should deny the report [about the 
danger] while the United States was disseminating it. The United States simply wanted 
to demonstrate its military muscle and find an excuse to dispatch the aircraft carrier 
Nimitz close to the Libyan waters so as to say "we are here!" 


But why does the United States resort to such a foolish action? 
Probably in order to distract attention for some time from the faltering Israeli- 


Lebanese talks in Lebanon, for the whole world blames this faltering on the United 
States because of its inability to exercise any pressure on Israel.

Probably because Chadian President General Habre, who returned to Chad and expelled 
General Oueddei, Al-Qadhdhafi's man, is now consolidating his power and, realizing that 
Al-Qadhdhafi will try to undermine the Chadian regime as he did before, the United 
States wanted to warn him before he gets involved in a new Chadian venture. Probably 
because the United States from time to time demonstrates its muscle in the Mediterranean 
as a message to the Soviet Union within the context or the chess game between the super- 
powers. Probably because of a wrong intelligence report on maybe in order to create a 
psychological atmosphere both in Egypt and Libya that would turn the imagined tension 
into real tension. 


President Reagan has proved that he takes rumors too seriously, which calls to mind the 
story of Libyan elements infiltrating the United States in order to storm the White 
House and assassinate the president. 


On that day the world laughed but the U.S. President placed troops and trained marks- 
men on the White House roof in order to shoot any Libyan trying to penetrate to the 
White House. 


Finally, it is probably because the United States, in view of its diminishing moral 
influence as a result of Israeli intransigence, wanted to draw to everybody's attention 
that it is the protector of the area. This would have been logical had it protected 
the area from within as well, that it is the protector of the area. This would have 
been logical had it protected the area from within as well, that is from Israel which 
is devouring Arab lands and Arab flesh, while the United States, with its nuclear- 
powered aircraft carrier, is helpless and unable to respond to the Arabs' call for 
help. 


But if one is naive and believes that there was really a Libyan move against Sudan that 
calls for intervention by foreigners in this manner, then this is something regrettable. 
It is high time that we realized that people are responsible for changing what they 
want to change and that no change can come from abroad.
