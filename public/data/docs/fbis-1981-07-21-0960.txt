LIBYA

LIBYA TO COMPENSATE PALESTINIANS, LEBANESE 


LD201016 Tripoli JANA in English 0833 GMT 20 Jul 81 

[Text] Tripoli, 20 Jul (JANA)--Reliable sources at the People's Committee of the Foreign 
Liaison Bureau said that the Jamahiriyah is intending to compensate the Palestinian 
resistance, the Lebanese National Movement and the Lebanese people for all the financial 
and military losses caused by the Israeli raids on Lebanon.
