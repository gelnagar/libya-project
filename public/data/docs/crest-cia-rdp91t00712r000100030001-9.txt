Summary (AI): A September 1983 NPIC briefing attachment lists Libyan and Libyan-backed forces deployed in northern Chad (Fada, Faya Largeau, Ounianga Kebir, Gouro, Ogoui, Aozou), with ground and air order of battle. Nothing here concerns Tripoli or domestic repression.
• Combat aircraft in northern Chad included six Mirage F-1, 23 Fitter (Su-22), 15 SF.260 light-strike aircraft and five Hind helicopters.
• Air defence assets included two deployed SA-6 batteries, one probable SA-9 battery and three ZSU-23/4 (two probable).
• Ground forces included elements of four mechanized infantry battalions, one motorized infantry battalion, two tank companies, four small tank units, and elements of six heavy-lift battalions.
• The document is an NPIC/IEG briefing attachment dated 2 September 1983 with control number 2-13967/83.
• 1983-09-02: NPIC briefing notes report Libyan force dispositions across northern Chad, including new deployments at Fada and changes at Faya Largeau, Ounianga Kebir and Aozou.
