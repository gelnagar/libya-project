LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES ALGERIAN COUNCIL MEMBER 
LD032152Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 3 Oct 78 LD 
[Text] The leader of the revolution, Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, this evening 
received Ahmed Bencherif, member of the Algerian Council of the Revolution and minister 
of irrigation and land development, who is currently on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. 
The meeting was attended by the Jamahiriyah ambassador to Algeria.
