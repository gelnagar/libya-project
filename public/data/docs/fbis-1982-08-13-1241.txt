LIBYA

LIBYA READY TO HOST NONALIGNED CONFERENCE 


LD121504 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 12 Aug 82 

[Text] Tripoli, 12 Aug (JANA) -- JANA has learned that Libya has informed the 
organization of nonaligned states of its readiness to host the forthcoming nonaligned 
summit conference that was scheduled to be held in Baghdad.

AL-QADHDHAFI URGES 'ANNULMENT' OF PLO DECISION : 


LD121634 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 12 Aug 82 


[Text] Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, brother leader of the revolution, has sent a cable 
to the leaders of the Palestinian Resistance and the Lebanese and Arab sides that have 
participated in the U.S.-Israel conspiracy against the Palestinian cause. Here is the 
text: 


Despite our total conviction that the agreement to the departure and the distribution is 
a betrayal of the martyrs, of the holy cause and of the Palestinian people, and that this 
should not have happened following the great sacrifices and the heroic resistance, and 

in view of the lunatic annihilation air raids since the acceptance of the plan was 
announced, all these sides should immediately declare their annulment of the conspiracy 
of departure and distribution. The annihilating Israeli air raids have presented the 
world with sufficient proof that there is nothing to depart from and nothing can be dis- 
tributed. 


V. 13 Aug 82 NORTH AFRICA 


JANA EDITOR: PALESTINIAN CAUSE BETRAYED 


LD121114 Tripoli JANA in English 0930 GMT 12 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 23, August 12 —- Jamahiriyah News Agency —- JANA political editor 
said in his commentary: "Those who are willing to receive the Palestinian fighers (who 
were forced to leave by the Zionist military machine), with the exception of the front 
line countries -- Lebanon, Syria and Jordan, have betrayed the Palestinian people's cause 
and are part of the Zionist-U.S. schemes against this Arab people. "Is it not the 
Zionists' wish -- the dispersal of the Palestinians in different Arab countries so the 
land becomes empty? Is not the Zionist - invasion of the Lebanon intended to achieve 
this aim? Is it not the American imperialists’ plan to settle the Palestinians in 
various Arab countries, even in the USA itself?" 


The editor went on to say that those who take such a decision will have their hands 

stained with the blood of the martyrs and contribute to the dispersion of the Palestinians 

and liquidation of this cause in spite of the long years of struggle and suffering. 

He concluded his commentary saying: "By which logic will those who compete to take the 

Palestinians (who were forced by terrorist Begin to leave after a heroic resistance) 

into their country justify to history and the generations to come their decision?"
