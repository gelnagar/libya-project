LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RETURNS FROM 2-WEEK FOREIGN TOUR 


LD061816 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1755 GMT 6 Nov 82 


[Excerpt] Tripoli, 6 Nov (JANA) -- The leader of the revolution has ended a 14-day working 
visit to the People's Republic of China, the People's Democratic Republic of Korea and the 
Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia. Al-Qadhdhafi returned to the Jamahiriyah from 
Belgrade this afternoon,
