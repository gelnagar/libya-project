LIBYA

Political Editor on ‘Shameful’ Egyptian Policies 
LDO51109 Tripoli JANA in English 1009 GMT 5 Jan 84 


[“JANA’s Political Editor Calls for Increased Opposition to 
Capitulationist Line” — JANA Headline] 


{Text} Tripoli, Al Ahram |, Jan 5, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — Usama al-Baz, the first undersecretary at the 
Foreign Affairs Ministry of the Egyptian regime and Husni 
Mubarak’s special envoy to the other two capitulationist and 
compromising countries, Iraq and Jordan, said that the next step 
will concentrate on compromise and consolidation of relations 
with the Zionist enemy within the context of Camp David. 


In a statement made after his return from Baghdad and Amman, 
he said that the Egyptian regime will be a party in the nego- 
tiations between Yasir ‘Arafat and King Husayn, in preparation 
for direct negotiations with Israel. The JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY’s political editor commented on the statement 
by the Egyptian official, which is the basis of the Egyptian 
regime’s capitulationist and shameful policies and said that Arab 
commitment to a strategy to defeat the Zionist enemy and its 
allies has become a meaningless statement without any value 
because some treacherous leaders removed the masks from their 
faces, showing their true, ugly, treacherous character and sub- 
missiveness to the Israelis and the U.S. 


Husni Mubarak officially announced yesterday that a war 
against Israel is of no use... 


And Egypt has decided to take the path of compromise after it 
tried wars, saying that every war must eventually end up on the 
negotiating table. King Husayn preceded him in announcing his 
keen readiness to negotiate with the Zionists with a Palestinian 
mandate for coexistence — with the Israelis, the enemies of the 
Arab nation. He was supported in Baghdad by the president of 
the Iraqi regime, who blessed the current moves and preparations 
to complete the new rings in the treacherous Camp David chain. 
What next after this shameful subservience and after this race 
to give in and bow to the Zionist flag? 


These treacherous leaders no longer have legitimacy, no 
boundaries, no constitutions, no laws. They have all surpassed 
those legitimacies. Their plans should be faced with widespread 
anger, so that the Arab soil would burn under the feet of those 
defeatists who undermine the Arab masses. 


Maltese Prime Minister Concludes Visit 


Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Guests

5 Jan 84 


[Excerpts] The brother leader of the revolution received at noon 
today the Maltese prime minister, who was accompanied by 
Foreign Minister Alex Sceberra Trigona, Minister of Health Dr 
Vincent Moran, and Major General Cachia, chief of the Maltese 


Armed Forces. Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in 
chief of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, also received Major 
General Cachia, chief of the Maltese Armed Forces, today. 


Maltese Visitors Depart 
LDO52212 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2115 GMT 5 Jan 84 


[Text] Prime Minister of Malta Dominic Mintoff and the del- 
egation accompanying him left Tripoli this evening after a short 
visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


Before leaving Tripoli International Airport he made a statement 
to JANA in which he expressed his extreme pleasure and com- 
plete satisfaction with the results of his visit. He confirmed 
Malta’s desire and determination to consolidate the relations of 
friendship and cooperation between the two countries in various 
fields. He confirmed the common determination of Malta and 
the Jamahiriyah to achieve peace and security. 


The prime minister praised the steps made by the Jammahiriyah 
in building a better future for the Libyan Arab people. He also 
praised the measures and arrangements made by the Jama- 
hiriyah in confronting the international economic crisis. 


Concerning the situation ir Lebanon and the presence of Zionist 
invasion forces backed by the imperialists, Mintoff once again 
strongly condemned the invasion of Lebanon by the Zionist 
enemy. He said that Malta condemns what Israel is doing in 
Lebanon, as theyhave clearly stated at the UN and other inter- 
national gatherings.
