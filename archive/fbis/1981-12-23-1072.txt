LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI MAY MAKE PRIVATE FRG VISIT IN 1982 


NC221557 Paris AFP in English 1532 GMT 22 Dec 81 


[Text] Bonn, 22 Dec (AFP) -- Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi will probably make a 
private visit to West Germany some time next year, liberal (Free Democrat Party-FDP) 
Member of Parliament Juergen Moellemann said here today. 


Mr Moellemann, who is FPD spokesman for foreign affairs and security, said on his return 
from a visit to Tripoli that Colonel al-Qadhdhafi had accepted an invitation from the 
German-Arab Association. 


The politician, who is the association's chairman, also urged Chancellor Helmut Schmidt 
to use his good offices to encourage talks between Libya and the United States, which 


recently ordered U.S. citizens to leave Libya. 


SRV VICE PREMIER ARRIVES, MAKES STATEMENT 


LD222314 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 22 Dec 81 


[Text] Pham Hung, SRV Council of Ministers vice premier, arrived in Tripoli this afternoon 
accompanied by a delegation which includes a number of senior officials. 


Pham Hung made a statement to the JANA reporter on arrival at Tripoli International Airport. 
He praised the struggle of the Libyan Arab people and their opposition to the imperialist 
onslaught. He said the SRV has recorded with appreciation and pride the strides made by 
the Libyan Arab peoples as a result of the revolution, along the path of progress and 
modern construction.

He added that the Vietnamese people highly appreciate the firm revolutionary stand of the 
Great September Revolution toward the just Arab issues and he lauded the role that the 
Jahamiriyah plays in the national Steadfastness and Confrontation Front, for realizing 
the aims of the Palestinian Arab people. 


He strongly denounced the terrorist campaign of the U.S. administration against Libya and 
in this connection he affirmed the solidarity of the Vietnamese people with the Libyan Arab 
people in their determined opposition to all forms of imperialist-reactionary conspiring. 
He said the Vietnamese people are very proud of the courageous stand which the Libyan Arab 
people took in support of the Vietnamese people against the U.S. imperialists during the 
Vietnamese war. 


PAPER REPLIES TO U.S. TERRORISM CHARGES 


PM221447 Tripoli AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR in Arabic 15 Dec 81 p 1 
[Editorial: ''These Are the Real Reasons" 


[Excerpts] It is a testimony of which the Great 1 September Revolution, the Arab nation 

and all the peoples struggling for freedom are proud -- namely the announcement by the 

U.S. administration that Libya is seeking to undermine U.S. interests, which mean aggression, 
destruction and plundering of people's wealth. 


By this statement the U.S. administration has attributed to the Jamahiriyah the noblest 
and most precious values, which are the basis of the Jamahiriyah's revolutionary presence 
in the pan-Arab national arena and the international arena. 


All the world's peoples realize that what the Reagan administration alleges about U.S. 
interests and what the U.S. officials and leaders are saying is evidence of aggressive 
expansionist designs and of a desire to subject the world peoples to U.S. tutelage and 
influence and to the greed of the exploiter monopolies, so that they can suck the peoples' 
blood and wealth. 


The ideal model and yardstick for service to U.S. interests as seen by the United States 
is what the buried As-Sadat did and what Numayri, Siad Barre, and Oman's Qabus are doing. 


The United States wants to regard these mean and despicable examples as the standard for 
its foreign relations with peoples and for defining its interests in world countries. 


The Great 1 September Revolution refused to be part of this dirty crew or to join the list 
of those who kowtow to U.S. arrogance and has insisted on pursuing an independent course, 
free from tutelage and subservience to any world power. It dealt a severe blow to U.S. 
imperialist influence by ousting five U.S. bases following the outbreak of the Great 

1 September Revolution. It continues to expose and reveal the reality of the aggressive 
U.S. military presence in the Arab area as represented by the U.S. fleets, Rapid Deployment 
Force and aggressive maneuvers. It has also exposed and uncovered the naive and ignorant 
agents who have appointed themselves watchdogs over U.S. interests at the expense of the 
Arab nation's higher interests. The 1 September Revolution has also sided and always sides 
with the Palestinian Fedayeen for the liberation of Palestine and the repatriation of the 
Palestinian people. 


Because the 1 September Revolution has done all this and because of all these factors --- 
of which the revolution, the Arab nation and all the world's free peoples are proud -- 
U.S. imperialism has adopted its present attitude toward the Jamahiriyah people and the 
revolutionary leader and has clearly and openly declared that the Great 1 September 
Revolution is seeking to undermine U.S. interests.

Therefore the question, as the U.S. administration itself admits, is not one of terror 
or alleged assassinations committed by the Libyans but of the 1 September Revolution's 
rejection of U.S. imperialist interests, of the Jamahiriyah people's revolt against the 
shackles of humiliation and subjugation and U.S. tutelage and containment, and of the 
Jamahiriyah people's independence and support for the cause of freedom and of all the 
wronged, the persecuted and the underdogs in the world. 


This is the reality and the essence of the current conflict between the Jamahiriyah people 
and the U.S. administration. The issue is not one of terror or assassination. It has 
become clear to U.S. public opinion and to the whole world that Reagan and his administra- 
tion are the terrorists and killers. 


We challenge the U.S. administration and President Reagan to cite evidence to prove their 
allegations. 


We challenge the Reagan administration to prove that Americans in Libya are not safe and, 
at the same time, we call on the U.S. Congress and leaders of U.S. public opinion to see 
for themselves that Americans in Libya lead the same normal life as Libyans do. 


We assure the whole world that Americans in Libya enjoy greater safety and security than 
they do in the United States, where life is unstable because of the atmosphere of terror 
that prevails in U.S. society. : 


The U.S. administration, by bringing up what it calls the Libyan issue at every inter- 
national council or conference, suggesting to its European allies that they take retalia- 
tory measures against Libya, hysterically demanding the imposition of economic sanctions 
against it and going so far in its malice as to commit aggression in the Gulf of Sitre, 

is in fact reflecting the alarm and fear it feels at the bleak future awaiting its 
interests in the area if the Great 1 September Revolution continues to adhere to its 
principled and firm stand of resisting and opposing all colonialist and exploiter con- 
cepts and domination, which is what the term U.S. interests means.
