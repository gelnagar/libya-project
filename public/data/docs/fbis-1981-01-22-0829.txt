LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES MUSLIM CEREMONY ON ISLAM 


LD211220 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 21 Jan 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 Jan (JANA)--The leader of the revolution stated that the genuine 
Muslim individuals who sensed their self-motivated responsibilities toward the liberation 
of the land from tyrants are those who began the revolution in the modern era to destroy 
tyrants in the world. This came in a speech delivered by the leader in a solemn religious 
ceremony attended by a large number of Muslims from all parts of the Islamic world. 

The leader added that Idris as-Sanusi was one of those tyrants who fell on 

1 September 1969 and used to hide behind Islam to serve his interests and that of 
colonialism, and non-Islamic interests. 


The shah of Iran, who also hid behind Islam while in fact he used to worship America and 
the Israelis, was toppled, and by his downfall the Islamic revolution in Iran triumphed. 
He said that the Iranians, after the triumph of their revolution, are waging a real 
jihad. They have challenged the United States and trod on its dignity; she is begging 
them in front of the world without their being frightened by her fleets. The Iranian 
revolution has gained respect; it gave values to the Muslims and Islam by the power of 
faith it showed which frightened the major powers. 


The leader of the revolution underlined the need for the Muslims to carry out jihad to 
liberate Jerusalem in a popular war and to rid it of the Zionist occupiers. 


The leader of the revolution called on the Islamic people to be liberated from the 
hegemony of non-Islamic states. He said that Islamic peoples from now on must not remain 
under the hegemony of the British Commonwealth and must not remain under the hegemony of 
the francophonic group. There is no Islam dominated by France, no Islam dominated by 
America, no Islam dominated by Britain. He said that the blessed Koran urges jihad and 
is opposed to Islamic people being under [non-] Islamic hegemony. 


The leader of the revolution urged the Muslims to adopt the Arabic language, the language 
of the Koran. He said: The Islamic masses must turn to the Arabic language without 
delay and impose it upon themselves and upon the coming generations. He added: As true 
and sincere Muslims who memorise the holy Koran, you should learn the Arabic language to 
understand the meaning of the Koran. There is no Muslim without the Arabic language. 


The leader of the revolution addressed the huge numbers of Muslims who attended the 
religious ceremony on the importance of the holy Koran in the lives of Arabs and Muslims. 
He explained that after the establishment of the people's authority the holy Koran has 
become a source of legislation for the Jamahiriyah society. He said that in order for 
action to be genuine and just, the Koran must be the legislation for the society. He 
stressed that the holy Koran is not just a blessing or something to memorize--it is to 

be applied. The leader of the revolution said that we as Muslims must work, and legislate 
democratically without producing any legislation contrary to the holy Koran. 


TRIPOLI SAYS AS-SADAT HAS DESIGNS ON SUDAN 


LD211406 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 21 Jan 81 


[Text] Despite the foul expressions uttered by the ruler of Egypt in which he attacked 
the Libyan Arab people it is important that the Libyan Arab people learn the rancor 
and threats being uttered by As-Sadat. 


V. 22 Jan 81 NORTH AFRICA 


He said: Egypt will enter Sudan after the Libyan intervention in Chad. As-Sadat went on 
to say: Egypt is in constant contact with Sudan and it will enter the battle whatever 
its nature, 


At a political meeting he held in Aswan town yesterday As-Sadat mentioned that the Libyan 
presence in Chad is threatening Sudan's security and that Egypt rejects what has happened 
in Chad and will confront it. Thus, As-Sadat has completed the military occupation of the 
Sudan. This Egyptian military occupation of Sudan threatens the outbreak of war in the 
region and is threatening the security and the stability of peace in Africa. This flagrant 
Egyptian occupation of Sudan demands from the African presidents a speedy move and the 
adoption of a clear and decisive posture vis-a-vis this military occupation announced and 
implemented by As-Sadat in Sudan. 


It would have been more fitting for the Sudanese Government to put its forces on alert 
and rapidly move to face this Egyptian occupation of its territory and protest against 
the French military occupation of the Central African Republic, instead of joining the 
Egyptian regime in its tendentious propaganda against the Libyan Arab people for the 
humane aid they extend to the fraternal people of Chad. 


Thus the Libyan Arab people, who took the decision to cancel the confrontation with the 
Egyptian Army, should know about As-Sadat's aggressive intentions and the colonial plans 
hostile to the Libyan people so that As-Sadat's actions can be a lesson for the people's 
congresses as they make their decisions, so that these decisions can be mature and objective.

[Excerpt] President Goukouni Oueddei of Chad arrived in Tripoli this evening on a visit

Al-Qadhdhafi Meeting 


LD211930 Tripoli JANA in English 1845 GMT 21 Jan 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 Jan (JANA)--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the great Al-Fatah 
revolution, met with President Goukouni Oueddei of the Chadian Transitional Provisional 
Government for two hours in Tripoli this morning. The Chadian delegation of imams accom- 
paying Goukouni expressed their thanks for the Libyan assistance to Chad especially for 
the assistance for the reconstruction of the mosques in the country which were destroyed 
during the war. 


AL-QADHDHAFI DENIES NUCLEAR DEAL WITH PAKISTAN 


BK211604 Delhi Domestic Service in English 1530 GMT 21 Jan 81 


[Text] Libyan leader Colonel al-Qadhdhafi has said that he considers India to be a 
brotherly country. In an interview with the edition of the Bombay WEEKLY CURRENT in 
Tripoli, he said he has high esteem for Mrs Gandhi and is keen to visit India. Cclonel 
al-Qadhdhafi denied that his country is helping Pakistan to produce a nuclear bomb. 
Describing the suggestion as a big joke, he said the question of this kind of collabora- 
tion between Libya and Pakistan does not arise.

CENTRAL AFRICAN LEADER TERMED ‘IMPERIALIST AGENT’ 


LD212018 Tripoli JANA in English 1836 GMT 21 Jan 81 


[Text] Paris, 15 Jan (JANA)--David Dacko, the imperialist agent whom France appointed 
ruler of the Central African Republic, said on French television that he called upon the 
French troops to protect his country and maintain its independence and the unity of its 
soil. With this meaningless statement, the agent Dacko ignored the heroic African people 
who sacrificed scores of martyrs and fought for the liberty of Central Africa. Imperialist 
agent Dacko may have meant that he called upon the French troops to protect his very regime 
which the French forces of oppression set up for him on the corpses and skulls of Central 
Africa's people.
