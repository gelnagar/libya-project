LIBYA

Attempt on Life of Iraq's Saddam Husayn Reported 
LD211412 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
21 Oct 83 


[Text] Iraqi President Saddam Husayn miraculously escaped an 
attempt on his life last night. A state of emergency has been 
declared in Baghdad and the city is now under general alert. 
Armored cars have been seen in the streets. According to a 
dispatch published by the DAILY MAIL, a group of senior Iraqi 
Army officers has been placed under house arrest. The chief of 
Iraqi intelligence, Major Barzan Ibrahim at-Tikriti, has also 
been dismissed and placed under arrest.
