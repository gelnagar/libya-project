LIBYA

FOREIGN SECRETARY SENDS CABLE TO YASIR 'ARAFAT 


LD161042yY Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 16 Mar 78 LD 


[Excerpt] Tripoli--Dr Ali''Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, foreign secretary and chairman of the 
OAU foreign ministers council's current session, has sent the following cable to brother 


Yasir ‘Arafat: 


Vv. 17 Mar 78 NORTH AFRICA 


Struggling brother Yasir ‘Arafat, PLO chairman and commander in chief of the armed forces 
of the Palestine revolution; greetings: The continuation of the racist, Zionist aggres- 
sion on the Palestinian and Lebanese peoples proves categorically that the racist, Zionist 
entity is founded on usurpation, terrorism and the shedding of innocent blood, the fact 
that threatens the peace and security of the region and the entire world. This requires 
from all of us the adoption of all decisive means and measures to check and confront it. 


On behalf of the OAU foreign ministerial council, while saluting the heroism and stead- 
fastness of the Palestinian people and their courageous forces which are defending the 
sacred right against the usurpers and racists, I stress that Africa and the forces which 
cherish justice and freedom are following your struggle and sacrifices with appreciation 
and admiration and condemn this treacherous aggression and stand by you, supporting you 
in your legitimate struggle till victory.
