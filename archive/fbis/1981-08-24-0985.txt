LIBYA

NOTE CLAIMING GULF OF SIDRA SOVEREIGNTY REPRINTED 


LD211930 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1810 GMT 21 Aug 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 Aug (JANA)--A U.N. spokesman confirmed today that the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah told the United Nations in 1973 that the Gulf of 
Sidra was an indivisible part oi Libya's territorial waters and that it was under total 
Libyan sovereignty. The document was officially published in 1976. 


The following is a JANA reprinting of the document. It is intended to inform public 
opinion in the Arab world and throughout the rest of the world of the aggressive 
intentions of America against the people of the Jamahiriyah. Text of the document put 
before the United Nations in October, 1973: 


The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah declares that the Gulf of Sidra, which 
lies within its territory, whose eastern, southern and western land boundaries, bounded 
by a latitude 32 degrees 30 seconds due north, must be considered an indivisible part 
of its territory and falls under its complete sovereignty; it is regarded as Libya's 
internal [dakhiliyyah] waters, beyond which lie Libya's territorial waters. 


This is because of the fact that it is embedded within Libya's territory, and because Libya, 
as its owner, has for many years exercised undisputed sovereignty over it. In addition, 
it is vital to the security of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. Absolute 
Libyan control of the gulf is necessary to safeguard the security and peace of the 
country, given its geographical position, which overlooks the heart of that country. 


In view of the above, the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah declares that the 
Gulf of Sidra, whose borders are delineated above, is under Libya's complete national 
sovereignty from the legal, legislative and administrative points of view. This juris- 
diction applies to any ships or persons in the gulf. No foreign ships, publicly or 
privately owned, may enter the gulf without prior permission from the Libyan authorities, 
and in accordance with their regulations. The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
reserves the rights of fishing in the Gulf of Sidra for its own nationals, and will 
generally practice all sovereign rights over the gulf, as it does over any part of its 
territory.

UN DELEGATE DETAILS, CONDEMNS U.S. ‘AGGRESSION' 


LD211850 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 21 Aug 81 


[Text] Geneva, 21 Aug (JANA)--The Libyan UN delegate gave a press conference yesterday 

at the UN palace in Geneva which was attended by reporters from various countries. The 
Libyan delegate gave details of the American aggression against the two Libyan aircraft 
inside Libya's territorial waters and airspace. He confirmed that this aggression 
constituted a terrorist act and was within the context of the actions which America, the 
leader of international terrorism, has become accustomed to carrying out against peoples’ 
freedom. He explained that by this aggressive action America has confirmed its belittling 
of international laws using in this the logic of force and the heavy stick. 


The Libyan delegate pointed out that the American mass media had prepared for the planned 
aggression and intentionally obscured the right of the Libyan Arab people to control its 
airspace, land and waters with the intention of concealing the facts from American public 
opinion. In this context he confirmed that the Libyan people are able to respond violently 
to any attempt at violating its right to control its territorial waters and airspace.
