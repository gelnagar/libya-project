Summary (AI): A 1981 Daily Telegraph article reports a former Green Beret, Luke Thompson, saying he met about half a dozen British mercenaries working for Gaddafi's forces, recruited by former CIA agent Edwin Wilson. It describes a July 1977 Tripoli meeting at the Ministry of Trade with intelligence chief Abdullah Sanussi.
• Mercenaries were reportedly paid $6,500 a month plus a $10,000 bonus at the end of a year.
• Wilson's London company office was in Hans Road, Knightsbridge, and Thompson stayed at the Berkeley Hotel.
• Wilson safe houses were reportedly in Sussex.
• Thompson said the mercenaries made their own whisky because alcohol was banned in Libya.
• 1977-07: About 17 recruited mercenaries met at the Ministry of Trade with Libyan intelligence chief Abdullah Sanussi, who placed them under the operational control of a Capt. Hadjazi and had them list their military experience.
