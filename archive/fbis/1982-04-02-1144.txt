LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI MEETS WITH STUDENTS 1 APR 


LD012212 Tripoli JANA in English 1750 GMT 1 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 1 Apr (JANA) -- The revolution's leader today met with student masses of 
Bayan al-Awal Institutes for Girls, Al-Alwiya Al-Khadra's Secondary School for Girls, the 
Electricity Institute, lst September School and Economic Science Institute in Benghazi as 
part of the leader's meetings to urge the masses to escalate revolutionary work and wage 
battles against their enemies. 


The leader reaffirmed: "The students are the strategic reserve for revolutionary action... 
we are in state of continuous strategic confrontation on all levels." He stressed the 
necessity of strengthening the revolutionary force and expanding its scope of action. 

He said: "Through these meetings, action must be mounted from the revolutionary platforms 
and revolutionary work must be escalated so as to build the force which activates the masses 
in accordance with their historical choice, for everything is for the masses and through 
them. Therefore, since the masses would be waging the battles, the dynamic force, which 

is the revolutionary force, should work for inciting the masses and backing their struggle 
in confronting their enemies." 


He stressed: "American imperialism's interests are opposed to our nationalism; thus it 
views the Arab nation's interests as an obstacle to its own interests. This is the imperia- 
list countries’ logic." He pointed out: "The imperialist acts of aggression, such as 

those by America, arise because of the absence of an Arab strategic force. Therefore, a 
nationalist force in the Arab homeland must be created to deter these acts of aggression." 


He affirmed: "Libya is the dynamic force's revolutionary launch-pad. Libyan land is 
ablaze, burning all the reactionary dicta and culture of the old society." 


CHAD'S KAMOUGUE CALLS FOR GOVERNMENT CHANGE 


LDO11654 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1600 GMT 1 Apr 82 

[Text] Ndjamena, i Apr (JANA) -- According to reports in Ndjamena, the defense minister 
{as heard] in the transitional Government of Chad, Abdel Kader Kamougue, has withdrawn 
his forces from the battle with Northern Forces led by Hissein Habre. 


The reports confirm that Kamougue went to the south where he declared that a meeting with 
Hissein Habre is necessary following the loss of control of the situation by the Ndjamena 
government. Kamougue also called for changing the present government under Goukouni 
Oueddei.
