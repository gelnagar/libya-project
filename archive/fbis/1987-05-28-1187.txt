LIBYA

USSR’s Katushev Delivers Gorbachev Message 
LD270900 Tripoli Voice af Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 0115 GMT 27 May 87 


[Text] At noon yesterday the secretary of the General People’s 
Committee met with Konstantin Katushev, chairman of the 
USSR State Committee for Foreign Economic Relations, and 
the delegation accompanying him, who are currently visiting the 
Great Jamahiriyah. 


Katushev handed the brother secretary of the General People’s 
Committee a message to the brother leader of the revolution from 
Mikhail Gorbachev, general secretary of the CPSU Central 
Committee, relating to bolstering relations between the two 
countries.
