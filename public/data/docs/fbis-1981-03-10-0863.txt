LIBYA

LDO91529 Kuwait AS-SIYASAH in Arabic 5 Mar 81 


[Report on press conference held by Libyan Secretary of the General People's Committee 
for Justice Muhammad Abu al-Qasim az-Zuwayy in Kuwait--date not specified] 


[Excerpts] Libyan Secretary of the General People's Committee for Justice Muhammad Qasim 
az-Zuwayy has welcomed the establishment of the Council for Gulf Cooperation but said 
that he has reservations regarding the Sultanate of Oman's participationin this council. 


10 Mar 81 , NORTH AFRICA 


He said that he has acquainted Foreign Minister Shaykh Sabah al-Ahmad with this reserva- 
tion during the talks he had with him. He expressed the hope that the council will lead 

to Oman once again regaining its independence and will strengthen the freedom of the entire 
area. He said that Shaykh Sabah told him that the council membership is open to all 

Arab countries from the Atlantic to the Gulf. Az-Zuwayy said he hopes the council will 

be the nucleaus of Arab unity. 


Az-Zuwayy was answering questions by AS-SIYASAH on the Gulf Cooperation Council during 
a press conference he held in Kuwait. 


Before arriving in Kuwait Az-Zuwayy visited the UAE, where he delivered a message to 
UAE President Shaykh Zayid ibn Sultan from Col Mu'ammar Al-Qadhdhafi. He also visited 
Aden, where he attended the recent conference of Arab ministers of justice. 


The Libyan official condemned the state of lethargy in the Arab countries and warned 
that direct colonialism will return to the area if this state of affairs continues. He 
pointed to the deteriorating situation in Somalia and the Sultanate of Oman. Apparently 
the Somali president is pleased with the process of switching from the extreme left to 
the extreme right in return for a few dollars, he said. 


Speaking about the Sultanate of Oman, he said, "Thereis open talk there about joint 
U.S.-Omani maneuvers." 


The Libyan official wondered when the Arabs will no longer remain silent and said that 
"Libya is persistently working to urge the Arabs to launch a counterattack against what 
is happening in the Arab arena at present." He added: "This probably explains the 
reason for the onslaught which colonialism and the United States are waging against 
Libya. They want us to bow our heads, something which we refuse to do and over which 
we will not remain silent.” 


PFLP'S HABASH ENDS VISIT, MAKES STATEMENT 9 MAR 


LD092124 Tripoli Voice of Arcb Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 9 Mar 81 


[Text] PFLP Secretary General George Habash left Tripoli this morning at the end of his 
visit to the Jamahiriyah. Habash expressed appreciation for the Great ist September 
Revolution, emphasizing the depth of the bonds of the joint struggle between it and the 
Palestinian revolution. In a statement to a JANA correspondent, he said that his 

visit to the Jamahiriyah was an opportunity to discuss the major issues of concern to 
the Arab nation and to the struggle of the Palestinian people. He pointed out that the 
views of the two sides are in agreement on all issues. Habash confirmed that the PFLP 
stands on the side of the Libyan Arab people in confronting imperialist and reactionary 
plans.
