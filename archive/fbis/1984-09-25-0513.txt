LIBYA

Further on Visit of Greek Prime Minister 


Makes Dinner Speech 
LD241028 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 24 Sep 84 


[Excerpt] Tripoli, 24 Sep (JANA) — Prime Minister Papandreou 
of Greece delivered a speech at last night’s dinner banquet which 


\ 
he started by saying: This visit is exceptionally important. As 


Staff Major ‘Abd al-Salam Ahmad Jallud has stated, bonds with 
Greece go back many centuries. 


Papandreou said: ‘Ye have the greatest respect for the Libyan 
Arab people and for their leader Col Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
we appreciate the struggle of this people and their success in 
achieving their freedom, and we highly value the decision made 
in Libya through the Libyan Arab people in their own interest. 


He added that after 1945 progressive forces began to wage long 
and serious national liberation struggles which led to the search 
for an alternative to colonialism, an alternative stronger and 
more practical than colonialism and founded on the new eco- 
nomic and technical power. 


He stressed that today’s new crises in Latin American, repre- 
sented in the exports to the north, are closely tied with the interest 
rates and deficit in the United States. This represents only part 
of the problem, however. What is worse is that new power centers, 
such as multinational companies, are causing gaps between the 
rich and poor. 


Regarding the conflict between East and West, Papandreou 
stressed that with the Greek Government and people, there is no 
retreat from the struggle for freedom and independence. The 
Greek people’s right of sovereignty means that the country does 
not depend on foreign power centers or any agitating force inside 
the country. 


He added that Greece is trying to extend its contribution to 
achieve peace and social and economic progress. He stressed that 
these qualities of struggle for freedom, national sovereignty, and 
social and economic progress constitute a common factor 
between Greece and the Jamahiriyah. He said: I was very 
happy to listen to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, to his ideas and hopes. 
The contacts which existed between Athens and the ancient 
Greek cities exist in Libya as a model for social structure. 


Papandreou added: With regard to peace, we have stated 
within NATO and the Common Market that the deployment of 
Pershing II and cruise missiles will not push the Soviet Union to 
the negotiating table, rather the opposite will happen, which will 
lead to the introduction of SS-20’s. 


The Greek prime minister said that politicians and senior mili- 
tary leaders are dealing with many complex problems; however, 
they are not giving enough attention to the major problems 
represented in the survival of living humanity. 


He pointed out that there is starvation in the South and death 
from starvation, and there is prosperity in the North, which takes 
a turning point remote from our biological origins. 


Andreas Papandreou stressed that Greece has made an effort in 
the Balkan peninsula to create a Balkan nuclear-free zone. He 
said: We shall remove all nuclear weapons from Greek terri- 
tory. At the same time our belief that a sensible decision by the 
Soviet Union not to set up missiles in Bulgaria or Romania will 
give the inhabitants of the Balkans an opportunity similar to that 
of the Scandinavian countries, and subsequently the creation of 


a nuclear-free zone could be extended to include both parts of 
Germany, which will help detente and survival. 


Greek Prime Minister Papandreou at the end of his speech 
stressed that Greece does not move within outmoded 
frameworks, and this is one of the qualities of our Libyan friends. 
Hesaid: I would like to salute Col al-Qadhdhafi and the Libyan 
people on this wonderful occasion and discuss some of the com- 
mon problems such as peace in the Mediterranean without 
foreign bases and without foreign fleets. 


Draft Cooperation Agreement Signed 
LD241941 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1855 GMT 24 Sep 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Sep (JANA) — A draft agreement on joint 
cooperation in various fields between the Socialist People’s Lib- 
yan Arab Jamahiriyah and Greece was signed this evening. The 
draft agreement was signed by Staff Major ‘Abd al-Salam 
Ahmad Jallud and by Prime Minister Andreas Papandreou on 
behalf of the Greek side. 


Papandreou Concludes Visit 
LD241942 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1910 GMT 24 Sep 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Sep (JANA) — Greek Prime Minister Andreas 
Papandreou left Tripoli this evening at the end of an official and 
friendly visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 
The visit was aimed at strengthening friendship and cooperation 
between the friendly Libyan Arab and Greek peoples. 


Andreas Papandreou was given a big send off at Tripoli Interna- 
tional Airport by Staff Major ‘Abd al-Salam Ahmad Jallud and 
others. Also present at the send-off were the brother secretaries 
of the General People’s Committee, and the People’s Committee 
of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison, several Armed Forces 
officers, and the head of foreign diplomatic missions accredited 
in the Jamahiriyah. 


Statement on Papandreou Visit 
LD242027 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1900 GMT 24 Sep 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Sep (JANA) — A press statement was released 
today both in Tripoli and Athens on the official and friendly visit 
of Greek Prime Minister Andreas Papandreou to the Jama- 
hiriyah from 23 to 24 September 1984. Here is the text: 


At the Jamahiriyah’s official invitation, the prime minister of the 
Republic of Greece, Mr Andreas Papandreou, paid an official 
and friendly visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahir- 
iyah from 27 to 28 Dhu al-Hijjah 1393 after the death of the 
prophet, corresponding to 23 to 24 September 1984. During this 
visit, which reflects the excellent relations between the two 
countries, Mr Papandreou was received by Colonel Mu‘ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great Al-Fatih (1 September) Rev- 
olution. Matters of utmost importance were discussed.
