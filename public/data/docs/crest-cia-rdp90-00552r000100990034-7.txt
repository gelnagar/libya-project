Summary (AI): A Baltimore Sun article reports that National Security Adviser Brzezinski told a Senate subcommittee that using Billy Carter to seek Libyan support during the Iranian hostage crisis was a mistake in hindsight, though he defended the idea. It concerns US-Libya contacts in Washington, not Tripoli itself.
• Brzezinski told a Senate subcommittee that Billy Carter was used because the Libyans knew him and had friendly relations with him.
• The strategy was to isolate Iran by gaining Libyan support.
• Turner told Brzezinski that Billy Carter was seeking increased Libyan oil allocation for a Jacksonville firm, Charter Oil.
• Calls were made from Billy Carter's office in the Best Western Motel in Americus, Ga., to Libya.
• 1980-11-20: Brzezinski called Billy Carter to ask for help seeking Libyan support for the release of the US hostages in Tehran
• 1980-11-27: Billy Carter arranged a meeting with Ali el-Houdari, secretary of the Libyan People's Bureau in Washington
• 1980-12-06: Billy Carter placed calls to Charter Oil Company in Jacksonville and the Libyan embassy
