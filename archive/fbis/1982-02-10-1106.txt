LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI MEETS ACADEMICS, DISCUSSES LEBANON 


LD100406 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0015 GMT 10 Feb 82 


[Text] The leader of the revolution yesterday evening met with 64 faculty members of various 
Lebanese universities. The brother leader affirmed that Lebanon is an arena embodying all 
forms of Arab interaction. If reflects the truth of Arab reality. The Lebanese people 
represent the vanguard of the Arab nation. They are experiencing the greatest of suffering 
and are struggling valiantly against the enemy. The brother leader said this raises our 
hope in the effectiveness of the Arab nation and its enormous ability to confront the diffi- 
culties and challenges it has been facing. With its resources and abilities, this nation 

is capable of confrontation and triumph. 


The leaders explained that Lebanon today is passing through a tragic stage for the sake of 
the entire Arab nation. It stands on the frontline of confrontation with the evemy and is 
making sacrifices for the sake of the Arab nation. The leader said: The Lebanese people 

are fishing and paying a high price for the sake of the Palestinian cause, whereas the 

Arabs are standing around like spectators, as if what is happening in Lebanon doesn't concerm 
the Arab nation. He said: Those just standing around are engaging in treason by virtue 

of their defeatist attitude. The leader of the revolution expressed his great appreciation 
for the heroic struggle being waged by the Lebanese people for the sake of the Arab nation 
and its freedom. 


During the meeting, gifts symbolizing the internationalism of the Great 1 September Revolution 
and the internationalism of its ideology were presented to the leader. The academics affirmed 
to the leader of the revolution that they are loyal soldiers of the ideas of the Great 1 
September Revolution and emissaries committed to spreading this civilizing ideology, which 
gives mankind the correct solution to all issues and problems, serves humanity and truly 
expresses the aspirations and hopes of the peoples. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES NICARAGUA'S WHEELOCK 9 FEB 


LD100320 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0015 GMT 10 Feb 82 


[Text] The leader of the revolution yesterday evening received Jaime Wheelock, member of 
the junta of the Government of National Reconstruction and minister of agriculture, develop- 
ment and agrarian reform of the Republic of Nicaragua, and his delegation now on a visit 
to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


V. 10 Feb 82 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


The Nicaraguan delegation affirmed that the third solution is the one being applied in 
all regions of Latin America to get rid of enslavement and to proceed quickly toward the 
broad horizons of freedom and final deliverance so as to enable the nations of this 
continent to confront the plots of imperialism which seeks to dominate their resources 
and aspiration.
