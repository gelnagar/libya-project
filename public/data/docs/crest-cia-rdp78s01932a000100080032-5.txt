Summary (AI): A 1974 CIA cable reports that Libya's official news agency was ordered to drop all references and pictures of Qadhafi, apparently a temporary blackout, with one passing mention allowed. It suggests this may be Qadhafi's own move to threaten resignation or withdraw from the Revolutionary Command Council, and notes the RCC's fifth anniversary on 1 September as a possible occasion for announcements.
• The order was to drop all references and pictures of the President from the Official Libyan News Agency.
• Qadhafi had imposed similar media directives in the past, sometimes preceding his threats to resign.
• The Revolutionary Command Council's fifth anniversary falls on 1 September.
• Qadhafi has favored a presidential system, but his RCC colleagues have repeatedly vetoed moves to dismantle the military council.
• 1974-08-23: Libyan news agency ordered to drop all references and pictures of Qadhafi; one passing reference allowed in an article about his talks with Sadat
