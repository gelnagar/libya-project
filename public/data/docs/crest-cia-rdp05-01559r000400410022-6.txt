Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a Le Figaro Magazine interview with Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, in which he discusses the Chad conflict, a Tripoli meeting of Palestinian and other radical groups, and terrorism. Nothing specific to Tripoli sites is described beyond the city being the meeting place.
• Qadhdhafi said about 2,000 Americans, 1,000 French, 8,000 Italians and 12,000 Soviet advisers were living in Libya.
• Qadhdhafi said US aircraft carriers Coral Sea and Saratoga were patrolling off the Libyan coast.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya would not use its military forces in the Chadian civil war if French forces did not intervene.
• Qadhdhafi condemned bombs in Kuwait and a recent bombing in Paris as acts of terrorism.
• 1986-02: Qadhdhafi chaired a meeting of the Arab nation's revolutionary high command, attended by 22 extreme organizations including Palestinian groups.
