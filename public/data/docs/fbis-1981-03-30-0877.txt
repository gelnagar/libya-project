LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES PUBLIC RALLY IN TOBRUK 


LD282024 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1300 GMT 28 Mar 81 


[Speech by Libyan leader Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi at 28 March public rally in Tobruk 
to mark the anniversary of the evacuation of British bases from Libya--recorded] 


[Excerpts] Free brothers: It is indeed a great honor for us to celebrate on three 
separate occasions each year the evacuation of foreign forces from our land. [chants 
and applause] However, we must deepen the significance of the evacuation of foreign 
forces from our land, because this will consolidate our freedom in our land and will 
bolster our determination against imperialism. It also provides a categorical reply 

to those liars who try to threaten our dignity and accuse us before the world of 
permitting foreign bases to be established on our soil, at a time when we are extremely 
proud of being able to celebrate, on three occasions each year, the anniversaries of 
the evacuation of foreign forces that were on our soil. [applause and chants] 


The Libyan people, who fought for scores of years to evict the foreign presence from 
their territory, can never surrender that land, drenched in their blood, to foreign 
powers again. [chants] Under no circumstances will the people who fought for scores 
of years to evict the foreign forces from their territory be prepared to surrender 
their land, which they drenched with their blood, to foreign forces once again. This, 
brothers, is our reply to the group of agents who clamor around us and who, every day, 
try to threaten the dignity of the Libyan people, stabbing their pride by saying that 
Libyan territory has been opened to Soviet bases. Soviet-Libyan friendship is being 
established in fact, and we cherish it and hold to it. But we can never open our lands 
to foreign bases whatever they be. [applause and chants] 


Brothers, those who level these accusations against us are the ones who have opened 
their lands to foreign powers. American bases are being established in Egypt, Sudan, 
Morocco and Saudi Arabia. The Arab territories are now open before the imperialist 
forces which are opposed to the Arab nation and to the cause of freedom everywhere. 

They are the ones who level charges against us. But how can they level charges against 
us when they bow their heads before their American masters in Egypt, Sudan, Saudi Arabia 
and Morocco? Those agents must feel ashamed, must keep silent and must stop encroaching 
against the free land and people who now possess authority, wealth, and arms and who 
stand up with heads held high before America and the superpowers. [applause and chants] 


The Libyan Arab people have asserted their freedom in their land by evicting the 
foreign bases, and today they are celebrating one of the anniversaries of the eviction 
of these bases. We must always realize, brothers, that our freedom becomes stronger 
only when the freedom of all peoples becomes strong. Our presence in our land becomes 
stronger only when final defeat is inflicted on imperialism everywhere. 


With us today are delegations who do not represent kings, presidents and princes, nor 
do they represent agent governments or despotic governments which subjugate their 
peoples. With us are delegations whose presence with us today we cherish, because 
their presence with us on this day and on this platform has great significance--the 
alliance of the Libyan people with struggling peoples against imperialism. This 
presence means support to the Jamahiriyah from these peoples at a time when the 
governments which subjugate peoples are adopting hostile stands against us. These 
governments know that we fight on the side of the peoples against all dictatorships 
and all forms of oppression and exploitation.

At the same time, politically it appears to the superficial and naive that we are losing 
much in the field of international politics. But, in fact, we enjoy the support of those 
to whom the future belongs and who will triumph. We enjoy the support of the masses, thie 
peoples and the revolutionary forces which make the future of the world and which write 
the true history of the world. [applause] 


If the United States, as a government, an imperialist government, were to stand against 
us, then the peoples of Latin America who are struggling against America would stand 

on the side of the Libyan people and fight on the side of the Libyan people. The Libyan 
people would fight on the side of the Latin American peoples against the United States, 
its hegemony, arrogance and despotism. 


Today we have with us one of the founders of the Sandinist Revolutionary Front. With us 
today we have one of the leaders of the Nicaraguan revolution. With us today we have one of 
the leaders of the Nicaraguanrevolution against the United States. That revolution has 
triumphed despite the fleets of America, the power of America, the arrogance of America 

and the arms thrown into the battle by America. With us is the leader, military commander 
and revolutionary Tomas Borge--one of the founders of the Sandinist Front. He is one of 
the commanders of the revolution of the Nicaraguan people. [applause] He has come at the 
head of a delegation to affirm to the United States and the world that the Nicaraguan 
people stand on your side, the side of the Libyan Arab people. This is true support. This 
is the ally on whom one can rely: the peoples and not the governments, which daily fall 
under the feet of the masses, the masses which march to seize authority. 


It is sufficient for us, brothers, to have on our side the Latin American people, who stand 
against the United States--foremost the Nicaraguan people, who have come to declare their 
support for the Libyan people, their alliance with the Libyan people and to express their 
readiness, as stated by Brother Tomas, to fight on the side of the Libyan people. [applause] 
This is the true gain the Great 1 September Revolution is making in the field of international 
politics and in the international field. It does not seek the friendship of governments which 
fall daily like autumn leaves in the face of popular revolutions everywhere. We do not seek 
the friendship of kings and presidents who plead to the masses to prolong their political 

life day by day and month by month. We, brothers, win the friendship of the peoples, to 

whom the future and tomorrow belongs. With us, brothers, are allied the living revolutionary 
forces which create the future of the world. With us we have the Latin American peoples, 
whose alliance with us against the aggression of the United States we cherish--against its 
arrogance, its looking out for its interests in the Arab homeland and its complete alignment 
against the Arab nation. This is a true friend whose friendship we cherish and the true 

prop onwhich we, brothers, rely. 


Also with us we have yet another popular delegation. It is the delegation of the fraternal 
Iranian people who stand on your side just as you stand on their side. This is the friendship 
we cherish. This is the holy alliance we are forging now between our people and struggling 
people everywhere, peoples who are fighting imperialism, reaction and hegemony. It suffices 
for us to have the Iranian people with us now. If the Iraqi Government takes a hostile 
stand against us--the Iraqi Government which is fighting the Iranian people on behalf of 

the United States--the great Iranian people have established friendship with us. The great 
Iranian people have carried out a miraculous revolution. They destroyed the shahinshah 
regime. They destroyed America's arrogance. They took the United States lightly, its 
fleets and all the might it possesses, to prove their existence on their land and under 

the sun. 


You know, brothers, that the Government of Iraq has taken a hostile stand against you with 
no justification. It has done so prompted by rancor. This is what prompted it to fight 

a Muslim people who threw all its weight on the side of the Palestine question following 
the triumph of the revolution in Iran.

They threw all their weight behind the Arab nation. But the Government of Iraq is fighting 
these people on behalf of the United States and Israel. 


If that government takes a hostile stand against us, we have the Iraqi people, the Iranian 
people and the government of the Iranian people [applause and chants] headed by Imam Khomeyni 
on our side. [prolonged chants] 


We have yet another popular delegation. It is the delegation of the Chadian people. [chants] 
In their presence among us and in the words uttered by the head of the Chadian delegation 

lies a convincing reply to the enemies of the Libyan and Chadian peoples, to the agents of 
imperialism in Africa, the Arab homeland and the world who believe that Libya has annexed 

Chad by force. Here are the Chadian people replying to these lies. From this platform the 
Chadian people declare their support for unity between the Chadian and Libyan peoples. 
[applause and chants] 


Yesterday the first units of the Libyan forces which fought in Chad returned home. They 
returned to their original places--the places from which they moved out a few months ago. 

This proves to the world that the Libyan forces are forces of liberation. They fight to 
uphold freedom and then leave the liberated land for its owners and return home. But the 
imperialist forces, when they liberate--or rather occupy--a land, they never leave it but 
occupy it and colonize it forever. The French historical record in Africa, and the historical 
record of the imperialist countries in Africa, Libya, the Arab countries, Latin America and 
everywhere in the world testify to that. The armies of the imperialist countries go to 

occupy the land and stay there and try to stay there forever, to exploit them in the worst 
possible manner for the good of the imperialist countries. 


At this moment, brothers, we want the ambassadors of foreign countries and of African coun- 
tries to hear the voice of the Chadian people who are calling for unity and who declare 
unity, and who welcomed the Libyan forces as liberators and not as imperialists. The Chadian 
people resisted the imperialist forces. But they welcomed the Libyan forces with roses and 
bid them farewell with roses because they are forces of liberation. The Libyan forces will 
be gradually reduced until they are completely withdrawn. We hope that this will happen in 
the shortest possible time. But we hold the imperialist countries and reaction in African 
responsible for threatening peace in Chad. [applause] The withdrawal of Libyan forces 

could be impeded if it becomes evident that Chad's neighbors, at the instigation of European 
imperialism, are trying to exploit the withdrawal of Libyan forces to return war and destruc- 
tion to Chad and to occupy that country. If it becomes evident that there are some who want 
to exploit the withdrawal of the Libyan forces from Chad then the withdrawal of these forces 
will be halted--I say this openly before the world. [applause] 


At the same time the forces which were withdrawn and which have returned and those which 

are being withdrawn are fully prepared to return immediately to fight in Chad as they did 
before. [applause and chants] Listen to the Chadian people. Let them [as heard] listen 

to what the Chadian people say. [words indistinct] Let certain foreign states listen to 
(?Chadians) calling for death to As-Sadat and Numayri. [chants of death to As-Sadat and 
Numayri] At the same time, brothers, we want to warn those who believe that we are not privy 
to the perfidies and maneuvers they concoct that we are aware of the maneuvers they are 
conducting. 


To those who believe that they can open an illusionary front on our southeastern flank across 
the hadian-Sudanese borders so that thev can concentrate their main military thrust on our 
northwestern flank, we say: We are fore clever than you in military maneuvers. We were the 
ones who offered them [as heard] new views on mobilization, (?marching) battle and principles. 
{applause and chants]

We were the ones who succeeded before the whole world in staging the biggest [word 
indistinct] maneuver across the great Sahara and the borders and jungles. We have proved 
our high administrative and combat efficiency. We wishtotell them that we know of their 
tricks and are aware of their maneuvers. We are able to meet these threats and to in- 
flict defeat on those perpetrating them. 


For years we have been celebrating in Tobruk without a military parade. This is because 
in past years military confrontation was imposed on us. It was a cheap military confron- 
tation which we did not want. It was imposed on us by an Arab army which we trained and 
armed. Egyptian pilots graduated from the Jamal ‘Abd an'Nasir Base. After we liberated 
the base from the British we opened it to the Arab forces and to Egypt. In 1956 British 
aircraft flew from that base to strike at Egypt and at the Egyptian people. We, in 
defiance of imperialism and to spite it, opened the Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir Base to the 
Egyptian Air Force. The Egyptian pilots who took part in the 1973 war graduated from the 
Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir Base. 


Unfortunately, the Egyptians were forced against their consciences in 1977 to carry out 
provocations and destruction. They tried to destroy the same base which had trained them 
to fight the enemy of the Arab nation. This was a mean attempt to destroy their own Arab 
consciences and to direct their aircraft against the base from which they graduated. 


Those who made the Egyptian pilots, against their consciences, raid the Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir 
Base where they had trained wanted to show to the world that they were suffering from the 
disease of ingratitude and that they were totally devoid of gratitude. This, brothers, 

is enough as far as they are concerned. 


Years after that undignified military confrontation had been imposed upon us by a fraternal 
army we celebrate today the llth anniversary of the expulsion of the British Forces from 
the Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir Base, formerely the Al-Adem Base. [applause] We celebrate and 

the situation from our view point is completely different. We celebrate today at the same 
time that we are ending the military confrontation at the border with Egypt. In all parts 
of the Jamahiriyah, the popular congresses have decided to abolish the military confrontation 
with the Egyptian Army. We are now celebrating in Tobruk in which there are no military 
forces in accordance with the decisions of the poular congresses. This means that on this 
llth anniversary of the expulsion of the British forces from the Al-Adem Base, the Jamal 
‘Abd an-Nasir Base, we celebrate the ending of the military confrontation imposed on us 

by the Egyptian regime and imposed on the Egyptian Army and on the Egyptian people. This 
regime imposed a dirty confrontation, an unexpected confrontation on two fraternal peoples 
sharing the same land, origin and fate and on two peoples with a common enemy, that is, 
the Israeli enemy backed by the colonialist forces which are the enemies of the unity and 
dignity of the Arab nation. 


Therefore, while celebrating the llth anniversary of the evacuation, we also celebrate 

the ending of the military confrontation on our eastern borders. At the same time, while 
we were here, waves of jet fighters and bombers flew over the podium. This means that the 
command of the air [applause] will belong to Libyan jet fighters and bombers from any 
base. [applause] Thismeans that the vigilance and caution recommended by the popular 
masses has been taken into consideration in abolishing the military confrontation on our 
eastern borders. As you can now see the command of the air belongs to the air force over 
the border and it does not matter from which base the aircraft take off. But there are 
no military forces in this region. We warn, however, that there is caution. There is 
also seriousness in abolishing the confrontation but seriousness also in facing up to 
confrontation if it is imposed on us. [applause] The regular forces have been replaced 
by a new historical manifestation, that of the armed people, an armed people that cannot 
be defeated or surrounded, that does not surrender. [applause aad chants]

The armed people have replaced the traditional military forces. But this does not mean that 
the military forces will not come back. If confrontation is imposed on us the military 
forces will come back to the previous situation. But we now announce the evacuation of 
regular troops from these military positions and as you see the camps which were in the 
Tobruk region have been turned into education centers, schools, institutes and universities. 
[applause] We are trying to replace barbed wire with trees and trenches with siios. 
[applause] But we should not trust reaction, which has sold itself to the devil. We must 
always be vigilant and capable of carrying the battle into the heart of the land of reaction 
if a battle is imposed on us. The silos which are now being filled with water we will not 
hesitate for a moment to fill with blood if necessary. [applause] 


On the occasion of the abolition of the confrontation with the Egyptian army we address 
ourselves and launch an appeal to the Egyptian Army to direct its weapons toward reaction 

in order to liberate Egypt; to direct its arms toward Palestine in order to liberate it from 
Zionism. [applause] If this happens the Egyptian Army will find us with it, supporting 

it, backing it and more, taking part in the battle side by side with it. [applause] Brothers, 
the people in this region must shoulder the responsibility of defense against any attack, 

any treacherous surprise attack that reaction might launch across the border, the reaction 
that has allied itself with Zionism and colonialism. 


In every village there must be unswerving resistance; in every village there must be a 
weapons depot and in the hands of men and women there must be arms. [applause] In every 
inch of land there must be fighting. The withdrawal of regular forces from the Tobruk 
region must not be a gamble that might expose Tobruk to a treacherous attack at any time. 
But the armed people must be the strong citadel to smash any attempt at aggression. [chants] 


Brothers, the thousands of armed people living in the Tobruk region are stronger than any 
regular military units that were stationed in Tobruk before the end of the confrontation. 
[chants] Those who think that they can fool us because of the ending of the military 
confrontation should be aware that the regular forces that have withdrawn have left behind 

a monolithic and huge citadel; namely, the armed masses in this region who are capable of 
defeating any reactionary army. They have also left behind a forest of antiaircraft missiles 
at the firm bases of the Tobruk region [applause] which will remain in these positions. 


Brothers, while we celebrate the llth anniversary of the expulsion of foreign troops, 
we feel that our freedom is becoming stronger and our presence firmer every year. 


So brothers, at a time when we strengthen freedom on Libyan territory, strengthen national 
dignity and honor and urge our Arab nation to liberate the Arab land, unite the Arab land 
and defeat the enemies of the Arab nation; at a time when we strengthen our own forces on 
which we count in the first place, together with our material and moral potential, at this 
time we strengthen our position in the world by standing by the peoples who are struggling 
against colonialism and who are trying to consolidate their freedom and dignity. We have 
succeeded in this international battle, brothers, because we have joined peoples who fight 
colonialism, revolutionary peoples. We have gained a mounting power, a fresh power. We 
have with us now allied peoples, allied masses, revolutionary forces. We have allies in 
Africa, in Asia and in Latin America. This is the friendship, brothers, as I told you at the 
beginning, on which we rely and which we cherish. This is the holy alliance built by the 
Jamahiriyah with the peoples of the world. This is the support which is never depleted, 
the strong ally who is never defeated, the friend who never betrays. [applause] 


Brothers, as I told you at the beginning, the superficial and naive analysts, the ignorant 
and the tendentious, are the ones who believe that we have lost the friendship of some 
states and that we have lost in international politics.

I want to tell them: You superficial people; your analyses are wrong. The presence of the 
Jamahiriyah is stronger and its relations with peoples have grown firmer. 


Brothers, I greet you on the llth anniversary of the explusion of the foreign forces which 
corrupted and destroyed our land and people. We are still asking for compensation from 
the forces of Britain, Germany, Italy and the colonialist states which corrupted our land 
and inflicted losses and destruction on us and several years of backwardness. 


Brothers, the people still have the historic right to ask for their rights from those 

states which entered our country in a war in which we had no involvement. They turned this 
land into a scene for international military operations and destroyed our people and 

material potential. What we mean, brothers, is that we shall never abandon the right of 

the Libyan people to the colonialist states which oppressed our country, made it backward and 
inflicted destruction on it. We announce that we have obtained international support for 

our right with regard to the colonialist states which held our people in contempt and trampled 
our people under their dirty feet. The colonialist states that moved west and east 

[words indistinct] they trampled our clean land under their dirty feet. After the revolution, 
brothers, we avenged ourselves; we destroyed the bases of colonialism. We destroyed five 
American bases and the British Al-Adem Base. We destroyed their camps. We drove out the 
Italian colonialist settlement which was like a cancer infiltrating our country. [applause] 


After the revolution we drove them out and defeated them before the world. We drove them out 
in a short period, which they never expected because they dreamed that their bases would 
exist for 99 years. This was the contract signed between the American and British Govern- 
ments. (?They wanted Libya) to remain colonized forever. But colonialism went wrong as 

well as reaction. We will (?fight colonialism until we defeat it). The struggle goes on 
and forward. [applause]
