LIBYA

AL-AHALI Interviews Chief of Staff Yunis 
PM151616 Cairo AL-AHALI in Arabic 7 Mar 84 p7 


{Interview with “Chief of Staff’ Abu Bakr Yunis, by ‘Aydah 
al-‘Azab Musa in Tripoli — date not given} 


[Text] It was, of course, imperative that we begin the interview 
with a question on Chad, the most important African issue as far 
as Libya is concerned. I asked him about the claim that the 
Libyan forces moved 100 km northward after the downing of a 
French aircraft by the forces of Goukouni Oueddei. I also asked 
about the French foreign minister's visit to Libya and whether 
there is some kind of partition in Chad. 


Yunis: The French forces did not advance even | meter. In 
view of the difficulties their forces are facing in Chad, the threat 
of death to the French soldiers and the possible reaction in France 
itself, the French have come up with the idea of a third alter- 
native; that is, having a third person substitute for Habre and 
Oueddei. The Libyan masses approve of this idea, provided it 
results in the withdrawal of the French forces from Chad. As for 
Cheysson’s visit, he did not bring ready-made solutions or even 
specific proposals. We reject any French intervention, but if the 
OAU achieves a solution we will accept it.
