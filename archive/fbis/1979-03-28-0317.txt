LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS MESSAGES ON AS=SADAT'S TREACHERY 
LD272110 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 27 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the great 1 September 
revolution, has addressed a message to all Arab kings and presidents following the signing 
by the defeated Egyptian president of the agreements and treaties of humiliation and 
capitulation with the racist Zionist enemy in Washington yesterday under the supervision 
and blessing of leader of imperialism, Carter. Here is the text of this message; 


Praise be to Gods Only God is praised when a calamity takes place. The vision and 
political predictions of the Libyan revolution have come true. 


We have frequently and repeatedly told you that As-Sadat is a traitor. You said: The 
source of such charges is your bilateral differences. We told you that he would be closer 
to the Israelis than the Arabs. You laughed and said: This is also due to your bilateral 
differences. We told you that he is fighting us because he has decided not to fight the 
enemy at all. You said; Never, you are fighting a man whose main concern is to ~.ght 

the enemy to liberate Palestine. This time we laughed. 


Praise be to God who confirmed all that we said. The charges are now being turned into 
embarrassing facts relayed by satellites, and the world bears witness to the predictions 
of the Libyan revolution. 


Praise be to God that the matter has come to light. There is nothing before us in the 
Arab homeland except black or white. But in order that we should not make the same mis- 
takes, against which we warned, we must not henceforth accept those whose color changes 
like the chameleon--those who though living among us have their heart with the enemy. 

We tell you agains: They have to keep only to one color; either white or black. How 
could the chameleon maintain one color? We must do to them what Jiha did to his donkeys. 
This is wisdom of deep significance, which our heritage preserves for us in spite of its 
humoristic nature. He began to hit the tied-up donkey and wanted to kill him, leaving 
the free donkey eating the crop. When asked why he was doing this, he replied that the 
tied-up donkey was the cause. The one which was free has eaten whatever he could; we 
must punish the other one so that it does not go on and eat the rest. 


Thus, you are now facing a traitor whe has committed his treason and is now with the 
enemy; you are now facing hypocrites who change color and who, if left alone, will 
destroy what is left. They are the true cause of As-Sadat's treachery.

They are the ones who finance and feed the treachery. This is on the one hand. On 
the other hand, there could be no aggressive high tide unless there were pan-Arab low 
tides. All of us, except the unionists, are partners in the crime through the anti-Arab 
unity, separatist attitudes. There is no decisive reply, now that the Pharaonic-Hebrew- 
American alliance against the Arab nation has been established, save the establishment 

of the revolutionary Jamahiriyah unity among Syria, Iraq, Libya, Algeria, the Palestinian 
resistance and Jordan, this operation being strategic entrenching on both sides of the 
grave strategic penetration which has already taken place, then the acceration of unity 
between North and South Yemen and consequently the unity of the whole of the Arabian 
Peninsula by any means. 


There is still danger in ignoring the predictions of the Libyan revolution, whose correct- 
ness time has shown. This is because the Libyan revolution is the conscience and the true 
vanguard of the Arab nation; the vanguard does not lie to his people. These are 
established facts; this is the right direction; and this is the role of the Libyan 
revolution; It is a conscience which rebukes for the sake of right and good, regardless 
of what arrogant people might do. This is but a story. Peace and the blessing of God 

be with you. [Signed] Your brother, Col Mu'amnar al-Qadhdhafi. 


DEMONSTRATORS ISSUE STATEMENT ON AS-SADAT'S 'CRIME! 

LD271612 Tripoli JANA in English 1420 GMT 27 Mar 79 LD 

[Text] Tripoli, March 27, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The masses who participated in the 
demonstrations of denunciation which took place in Tripoli yesterday released a statement, 


in which the following points are most important: 


1. Full rejection by the masses of the defeated Egyptian president's practices against 
our Arab nation's struggle, particularly his signing of the treasonous treaty. 


2. It is high time that every Arab should shoulder his national responsibilities by 
taking action against this crime to foil its implications at Arab and international levels. 


3. The first Arab action is to unite in confronting this crime. 
4, To fight American economic interests in the Arab world. 


5. The belief of our Arab nation in the inevitability of victory against all forces 

of injustice, exploitation and dictatorships stems from the masses! revolutionary will which 
has beer for ten years embodied ideologically and practically in the great first of 
September unionist revolution taking place on the Libyan Arab arena. It shoulders the 
responsibilities of our Arab nation's leadership to achieve liberation and unity, as all 
honourable revolutionaries and true free men do,
