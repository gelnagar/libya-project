Program of Urgent Tasks 
LD212323 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1850 GMT 21 Nov 81 


{Program of urgent tasks of the Arab liberation movement for tackling the imperialist- 
reactionary plans on the Arab arena" issued 21 November by the emergency session of the 
Arab People's Congress held in Tripoli on 19-21 November; statement read by Dia' Haj ‘Ali, 
secretary general of the Arab Peasants Federation -- live] 


[Text] As we approve this program, we must not forget for one single moment the aim for 
which the emergency session of the Arab People's Congress was convened. The agenda of the 
session and its basic political topics, reflected by the political report, fall generally 
under two main headings: 


- How to restore Egypt to its leading and natural position in the Arab homeland after 
the execution of traitor As-Sadat. 
2. The plan of the Saudi Crown Prince Fahd, its political background, its consequences 
and the dangers that will result from it, and the national need to foil and frustrate 
the imperialist-reactionary moves that rely on it. 


In addition to the above two items, we have the evidence and political changes which surround 
the above two items and which have been extensively summarized and objectively correlated by 
the political report. The program of tasks must do the same thing as the political report so 
that it may become a true interpretation of our political view of the current conditions 
through which our Arab nation is passing, particularly the new conditions, for the sake of 
which the emergency session of the Arab People's Congress was convened. 


By affirming this matter in presenting this program, we mean to affi -m what the political 
report attached to the importance of fixing our objectives clearly im our moves in the 
current stage and the need for adopting a unified decision by which all of us would abide 
with regard to the way of achieving our objective without getting lost in labyrinths and 
details which would preoccupy us from attending to the urgent tasks laid upon the shoulders 
of the Arab national liberation movement, so that we may accomplish the aim for the sake 

of which the emergency session of the Arab People's Congress was held. [sentence as heard] 


We wish to add that this program of tasks treats how to deal with the new developments in 
the Arab arena following the holding of the second regular session of the Arab People's 
Congress. These new developments on the Arab arena underline the extreme importance of the 
need to implement the political program [as heard] of the Arab People's Congress. 


The program of urgent tasks of the Arab national liberation movement, which is now in our 
hands, contains practical steps for the application of the political program's [as heard] 
provisions in tackling the new imperialist-reactionary plans. It is based completely on the 
political program of the Arab People's Congress and on the charter and the broad political 
lines of the congress, particularly those of the second session held 15-16 January. 


The program before us is only a very small part of our battles of struggle throughout the 
Arab arena for the sake of liberation, unity and progress. This means, in the first place, 
the existence of an urgent need for the unity of the detachments of the Arab liberation 
movement and their unity of struggle within a direct democratic framework. This is one of 
the basic conditions for the success of the program of tasks.

The adoption of a unified decision by all members of the Arab People's Congress at this 
emergency session is not sufficient by itself, despite its importance. To implement our 
decision, we must couple it with a revolutionary struggle. This is our basic task after 
leaving the congress meeting hall. 


Great efforts must be exerted at this stage. The responsibility lies squarely on the 
shoulders of us all, each in his field and arena. These efforts must be exerted urgently 
in two fields: addressing ourselves to our popular bases in order to mobilize them and 
influencing the ruling circles in the various Arab countries and their political decisions. 
This is our basic method of accomplishing the urgent tasks specified by this program as 
follows: 


The urgent tasks of the Arab liberation movement: 


1. To pursue the struggle for the final downing of the Camp David accords and the liquida- 
tion of their traces inside and outside Egypt on all Arab international spheres. The carrying 
out of the death sentence against As-Sadat has dealt a decisive blow to these accords, but has 
not destroyed them completely. 


2. To work for the restoration of Egypt to its leading natural position in the Arab homeland. 
This can only be accomplished through the realization of two conditions: the abandonment by 
the Egyptian regime of the Camp David accords and the liquidation of their traces inside and 
outside Egypt; and the unleashing of political and trade unionist democratic freedoms inside 
Egypt and the release of the political and trade unionist detainees, the lifting of all 
pressures brought to bear on the Egyptian national progressive movement so as to enable it 

to move freely, the setting up of a guarantee for national policy inside Egypt to prevent any 
relapse, and the rejection of any attempt by Arab reaction to make peace with the Egyptian 
regime prior to the realization of these two conditions. 


3. To work for the toppling of the Arab regimes which are in alliance with the Egyptian 
regime, American imperialism and Zionism, despite the existence of the Camp David accords, 
and which play their role most insolently within the framework of the imperialist schemes

in the area. In this regard, we specifically mention Sudan, Somalia, Oman and Morocco, 


5. [mumber as heard] To contain and foil the Saudi move and, in particular, Prince Fahd's 
initiative and those accepting it. To expose the Saudi policies and their link with the 
American strategy in the Arab arena. To struggle for the mobilization of the Arab masses 
against this initiative, to destroy it and to reject any excuse or justification put forward 
for accepting Fahd's initiative. 


6. To escalate the struggle for the consecration of the principles connected with the 
Palestine question, To affirm that no party has the right to unilaterally submit solutions 
or make initiatives in connection with the Palestine question, which is the principal cause 

of the Arab nation. To uphold the no's of the Khartoum summit: no peace, no negotiations and 
no recognition of the Zionist enemy and no squandering of the rights of the Palestinian Arab 
people. To affirm that the PLO is the sole legitimate representative of the Palestine people. 
To abort the policy of relying on the United States, its allies or its agents for the sake of 
achieving a just, lasting peace in the Arab area. 


7. To intensify the efforts to lift the pressures exerted against the PLO on the Arab and 
international levels, thereby bolstering the efforts to check the Saudi initiative, through 
granting broad political and material support to the Palestinian gun to enable it to escalate 
the armed struggle; through concentrated Arab action to tip the balance of power in the 
Lebanese arena in favor of the Palestine revolution, the national Lebanese movement and Syria, 
and thereby bolster the struggle of the Arab nation; through drawing up a program of joint 
struggle with the cooperation of the PLO and supporting and bolstering the PLO on Arab and 
international fields in a manner which will strengthen the struggle of the Palestine people 
and the Arab liberation movement.

3, fo exchange visits and delegations and communicate among the parties of the Arab 
liberation movement for the purpose of consulting one another and coordinating and 
exchanging viewpoints concerning the methods used to achieve those tasks. 


4. To carry out the most universal Arab campaign for the sake of increasing material and 
moral support for the Palestinian revolution and the Lebanese patriotic movement in their 
struggle for tipping the balance of force in the Lebanese arena in favor of the Arab 
struggle by way of complete coordination with the Lebanese patriotic movement, the 
Palestinian revolution and Syria. 


5. To act immediately to form an executive committee of the popular pan-Arab struggle 
fund and to form masses’ committees in every Arab arena to collect contributions to the 
fund. 


6. To provide all means of material, political and moral support for the Egyptian 
progressive patriotic movement for the sake of making it achieve its aims: the release 
of democratic freedoms in Egypt and bringing about the downfall of the treasonous line 

of As-Sadat with all its symbols and remains and the return of Egypt to its vanguard role 
in the Arab homeland. 


7. To carry out campaigns of mobilization inside the Arab homeland alongside the Arab 
countries and the Arab liberation movements who are under an increasingly serious threat 
from U.S. imperialism, such as the Libyan Jamahiriyah, and to assert solidarity with its 
people against U.S. aggression and provocations along its borders. 


8. To mobilize the Arab masses and to entice them inside every Arab country to strike 
the political, economic and military imperialist interests by all means beginning with 
popular demonstrations and including the various forms of boycott, even the use of 
armed violence. 


9. The need to implement the decision of the permanent committee to hold seminars on 
the basic freedoms. The freedom of the Arab man is the basic guarantee of real confrontation 
with all U.S. imperialist plans in the Arab area and of foiling all the schemes of settlement. 


10. To call on the masses to stage general strikes and popular demonstrations on 
25 November as their expression of rejecting all the capitulationist schemes and 
particularly Prince Fahd's scheme. 


In regard to appealing to world public opinion, we must also, as part of the militant 
battle we are waging, look to our friends and to the democratic and progressive circles 
abroad to gain more support for the program of urgent tasks of the Arab liberation 
movement. 


l. To call on the parties to the pan-Arab Steadfastness and Confrontation Front to 
reject the Fahd initiative to foil the chance of Arab reaction's raising this initiative 
at the Fes summit and to pressure the other Arab regimes to reject the initiative. 


2. To prevent the restoration of (?normal) relations with the Egyptian regime until the 
policy of treason is liquidated. 


3. To pressure the Arab regimes and governments to make them provide more support for 
the PLO and to adhere strictly to the national rights of the Palestinian people. 


4. To stand firmly in the face of the Arab governments, foremost Saudi Arabia, who call 
for strategic alliance with the United States of America, because such an alliance is 
considered an alliance with the Zionist enemy.
