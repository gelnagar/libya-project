LIBYA

Official in Damascus on Jordan Embassy Incident 
JN271819 Damascus Domestic Service in Arabic 1615 GMT 
27 Feb 84 


[Text] Damascus — As-Salhin al-Jawhari, Libyan charge d’af- 
faires in Amman, holds the Jordanian Government responsible 
for severing the diplomatic and political relations with the 
Jamahiryah. At a news conference he held today at the Libyan 
Embassy in Damascus, Al-Jawhari asserted that these relations, 
which were severed unilateraily, will continue with the fraternal 
Jordanian people, and that these relations still exist. 


Al-Jawhari categorically denied the Jordanian authorities’ accu- 
sations that the Jamahiriyah was responsible for the Jordanian 
Embassy incident in Tripoli. He said that the Libyans are not in 
any way connected with what took place, and that the incident 
was not prepared by Libyan hands. Al-Jawhari condemned the 
‘Arafat-Husayn meeting, noting that their meeting falls within 
the framework of peddling capitulatory solutions with the Zionist 
enemy. He also asserted Libya’s rejection of these solutions 


Jallud Holds Press Conference in Nigeria 
LD241229 Tripoli JANA in English 0923 GMT 24 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada al Ulla 19, Feb 24, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Staff Major Ahmad Jallud announced 
that the Jamahiriyah and Nigeria had agreed to work together 
to support the Organisation of African Unity (OAV) in achieving 
peace and stability in Chad and in encouraging the new initiative 
by the organisation to mediate between the Chadian factions on 
the basis that the Chad problem is an African issue 


Staff Major Jallud in a press conference held in Lagos at the end 
of his visit to Nigeria added that both the Jamahiriyah and 
Nigeria are neighbours to Chad and influenced by what goes on 
there. Hence, it is their duty to aid the Chadian people to achieve 
peace and to keep the influence of the foreign powers away from 
this African country. 


He then said: “The new regime in Nigeria headed by Major 
Mohammadu Buhari and members of the Supreme Military 
Council believe in the role of people and are determined to solve 
the country’s problems and pay greut attention to Nigeria’s role 
in African problems”. 


He then added “We will willingly cooperate with this regime. 
The Libyan Arab and the Nigerian people are bound together 
with many historical ties. There are many fields for mutual 
cooperation based on respect and noninterference in internal 
affairs of others and for the good of Africa”. He then expressed 
his optimism about the future of the cooperation existing between 
the two countries, noting that the agreements signed during the 
visit of the revolution’s leader to Nigeria makes a solid base which 
could be developed. 


Staff Major Jallud expressed his satisfaction with the talks he 
held in Nigeria with Major Mohammadu Buhari and Brigadier

Al-‘Ubaydi Receives USSR Official, Ambassador 
LD260801 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 26 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 26 Feb (JANA) — The brother secretary of the 
Popular Committee for the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison 
last night received Shvedov, director of the First African Depart- 
ment of the Foreign Ministry of the Soviet Union. Viewpoints 
about many international issues and the situation in the Arab 
region and Africa were exchanged during the meeting, which was 
attended by the Soviet ambassador to the Jamahiriyah.
