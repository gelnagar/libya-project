Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a Tripoli Domestic Service broadcast (2 January 1987) of Qadhdhafi's recorded 1 January 1987 speech to Tripoli municipal officials. He says central government institutions were moved out of Tripoli, giving the city local people's power, and complains that armed forces and camps stationed in the city had caused congestion.
• Speech recorded at a 1 January 1987 meeting in Tripoli with members of the People's Committee of Tripoli Municipality and the Secretariat of Tripoli's People's Congress.
• Qadhdhafi said central general institutions were transferred out of Tripoli for the first time in 2,000 years.
• He said armed forces and their camps stationed in Tripoli had caused overcrowding and congestion in the city.
• Broadcast by Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic, 1330 GMT, 2 January 1987.
• 1987-01-01: Qadhdhafi meets Tripoli People's Committee and People's Congress secretariat members; announces that central institutions have been moved out of Tripoli and the city is responsible for itself.
