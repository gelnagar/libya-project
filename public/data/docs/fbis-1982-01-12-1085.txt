LIBYA

AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR ASSAILS PRO-U.S. ARAB LEADERS 


PM081611 Tripoli AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR in Arabic 28 Dec 81 p 1 
[Editorial: "Possibilities of Aggression Still Exist"] 


[Excerpts] A revolutionary armed clash with U.S. imperialism, which is invading the 
Arab homeland, will remain likely at any moment as long as this imperialism pursues its 
policy of escalating aggression through U.S. bases, the U.S. Rapid Deployment Force 
[RDF] and U.S. fleets provocatively deployed along the Arab coasts and as long as it 
continues to implement the Zionist aggressive plan to achieve a so-called greater 
Israel, from the Nile to the Euphrates. It will remain likely as long as U.S. 
imperialism ignores the right of an entire people -- the Palestinian people -- to exist 
and to live on their own soil and pays no attention to or attaches any importance to the 
dignity of the Arab nation and the feelings of its masses. 


It will remain so as long as U.S. imperialism continues to rally around it naive, 
ignorant and stupid people among the Arab leaders, who are used as bridgeheads to invade 
the Arab homeland and turn it into a large U.S. protectorate or colony. 


It is wrong to believe that the execution of As-Sadat at the hands of revolutionary 
forces has curbed U.S. aggressive expansionism or lessened the likelihood of a U.S. 
military attack on the Jamahiriyah. It is true that As-Sadat was one of the more 
malicious, uglier and more fascist figures in the area, as well as one of the more 
bloodthirsty and more anxious to start war with the Jamahiriyah, and was the one inciting 
and instigating imperialism to step up its aggression against the Jamahiriyah. However, 
it so happens that there is more than one As-Sadat in the area, ready and willing to 
crawl on hands and knees to lick the boots of the U.S. imperialists. As-Sadat's death 
has only caused them to plunge deeper into the quagmire of treason and treachery and to 
become further involved in the U.S. aggressive plan. 


At the same time, As-Sadat's death has heightened their fascism against their own people 
and their malice against the forces of revolution, unity and progress, which take the 
Jamahiriyah as their bastion and launching point for carrying out their revolutionary 
activities. 


The existence of such malicious, naive and ignorant rulers is a burden on the Arab 
masses, their national security, the independence and sanctity of their soil and the 
future of their generations. This is because these rulers have inherited from 

As-Sadat his bloody malice against the masses and his open hostility against the 
revolutionary forces which take the Jamahiriyah as their haven. They also inherited his 
persistence in introducing U.S. imperialism into the area and driving the U.S. 
colonialists toward waging an aggressive war against the Jamahiriyah.

These malicious reactionaries are, in fact, nothing but agents of aggression and packhorses 
who will transport U.S. tools of death and destruction to suppress this nation's revolution 
so that their reactionary, appeasing and subservient logic can prevail and they can subject 
the area and its people to the madness of U.S. arrogance. 


Woe to the reactionaries and to their sick minds, which make them see wrong as right, and 
woe to all their philosophies and claims, which justify defeat and humiliating submission 
under U.S. feet. They will be severely brought to account by the masses. And when this 
happens, neither the United States nor its bases nor its fleets and RDF will be of any use 
to them, just as in the case of executed As-Sadat. 


As our people affirm their revolutionary commitment to incessantly incite popular revolu- 
tion to topple these lackeys and decadent figures, they once again renew their readiness 
and thirst to fight a holy war against U.S. imperialism, a war that will be fought by 

the armed people and revolutionary forces in the Arab homeland and throughout the world. 


BRIEFS 


NEW CARGO VESSEL -- Benghazi, 11 Jan (JANA) -- The new general cargo vessel Ibn Hawqal 
joined the Libyan Arab commercial fleet at a ceremony held at Benghazi port this morning. 
The 9,400-ton vessel is one of three being built in the GDR according to a contract signed 
in 1979. The third vessel, 27,000 tons, will be delivered to Libya early in March.
