JANA: ‘Rumors’ of Goukouni Disappearance Denied 
LD181154 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 18 Aug 83 


[Text] An explanation by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and 
cooperation about the rumors of the disappearance of Brother 
Goukouni Oueddei, head of the legitimate Union Government of 
Chad: 


Bardai — The legitimate Transitional National Union Govern- 
ment of Chad strongly denies the tendentious, false claims fab- 
ricated by the imperialist circles about the disappearance of 
Brother Goukouni Oueddei, head of the legitimate National 
Union Government of Chad, since the battle of Faya Largeau on 
30 July 1983. 


We reject these insolent lies which are pure imagination and 
which aim at diverting national and world public opinion from 
the Franco-American-Zairin aggression against the oppressed 
people of Chad. 


After leading the battle of Faya Largeau and triumphing over 
the assault carried out by the enemy forces, Brother Goukouni 
Oueddei is now to be found among the ranks of the valiant 
strugglers on the battlefield. 


JANA on Increased French Presence in Chad 
LD182137 Tripoli JANA in English 2023 GMT 18 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhu al-Qa’dah 10, August 18, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — France has doubled the number of its 
military forces in Chad after its troops sent to Ndjamena cap- 
tured four main cities and established a defence zone around 
‘Ndjamena. 


It was rumoured in political circles today that the French Gov- 
ernment has taken advantage of the period of calm during which 
discussions [word indistinct] are centred on initiatives to realise 
peace, to send more forces, military equipment and experts and 
missiles to [words indistinct} Thus, France has secured a total 
military presence which can be explained in terms of military and 
political considerations as a dangerous presence which does not 
help accepting Mitterrand’s government's justifications claiming 
that France’s objective was to help backing the unity of the 
Chadian people and realising peaceful initiatives in Chad. 


Although French military sources pointed out that the number 
of French forces in Chad will increase to 3000 soldiers the actual 
number of French forces which will be positioned in Chad and 
which will form the total of French military presence in Chad 
will remain secret for the time being. This was announced this 
morning by the French head of the Defence Committee of the 
Socialist Parliamentary National Council following his meeting 
with the French Defence Minister Charles Hernu. 


The French official went on to say that the new French military 
operations include professional elements and have been named 
Manta II instead of Manta I. He also said that a general will be
