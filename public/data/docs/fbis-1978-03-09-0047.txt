LIBYA

EFFECTS OF FALL OF DOLLAR ASSAILED IN COMMENTARY 
LDO81520Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 8 Mar 78 LD 
[Unattributed commentary] 


[Excerpts] Because they are linked to the American dollar, presently collapsing in the 
world currency markets, the assets of the oil-producing states, including the Arab oil 
revenues, are slumping, continually losing ground. This disgraceful situation must be 
reviewed so as to retrieve our oil revenues, the most important and biggest material 
potential of our Arab nation, from servitude and exploitation by the American dollar, 
which survives at the expense of their value. That the Arab oil revenues--more, the 
revenues of all other oil-producing countries--are linked to the American dollar is 
illogical and in contradiction with the simplest economic norms.

The OPEC member countries had better hasten to take practical measures to save the 
revenues of their national resources, threatened by total collapse if not by 
bankruptcy owing to the (?dwindling) U.S. assets and the pressure of the sick U.S. 
currency. 


The fall of the American dollar is no longer a manifestation of normal stock exchange 
fluctuations. The dollar is today gravely sick, an incurable currency the recovery 
of which no remedy can assure. 


The devil take America and its sick dollar. 
CHADLAN GOVERNMENT DELEGATION ARRIVES FOR TALKS 
LDO31530Y Tripoli JANA in English 1432 GMT 3 Mar 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, March 3, JANA--Djime Ngakinar Mamari, vice president of the Supreme 
Military Council of Chad arrived here yesterday at the head of a Chadian delegation for 
a visit lasting several days. The Chad delegation was welcomed by Lt Col M. al-Kharrubi 
and the secretaries of communications and health. Also present was a representative 
from the Secretariat of Foreign Affairs and the Chad delegate to the OAU conference. 


Official Departs 
LDO71932Y Tripoli JANA in English 1836 GMT 6 Mar 78 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, March 7, JANA--The deputy chairman of the Chad Military Council left 
Tripoli yesterday at the end of his short visit to the Jamahiriyah. He was seen off by 
Maj al-Khuwaylidi at-Humaydi and a number of officials.
