[Text) Under the title “Nuclear Danger in the Mediterranean Basin, Libya and 
Algeria Are For Atomic Bomb", the Spanish journalist Vicente Talon has recently 
written a long article devoted to the nuclear threat menacing all the countries 
bordering the Mediterranean. 


"In fact", the Spanish journalist says, “Libya and Algeria, with their great re- 
serves of uranium, are very favorably disposed to the manufacture of the atomic 
bomb". Here are several extracts from Vicente Talon's article. 


“Libya is presently receiving aid toward that end from India and the Soviet 
Union, and perheps even from Argentina. It is certain that while Iraq or Brazil

from different countries and then put them together. Presently, it is trying to 
manufacture plutonium out of uranium imported recently from Niger and Chad. 
There are plans, thus, for the establishment of a nuclear research center 
equipped with two reactors, one for training purposes and the other for testing.

power plant, even if for the time being the names of the suppliers of the reac- 
tor and the necessary uranium are still unknown.
