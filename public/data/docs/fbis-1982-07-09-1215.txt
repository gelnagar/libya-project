LIBYA

JANA QUESTIONS MOTIVE FOR PLANNED U.S. EXERCISES 


LD090830 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 9 Jul 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 9 Jul (JANA) -- With the participation of U.S. aircraft carriers in the 
Mediterranean, the U.S. 6th Fleet will carry out an exercise from 0200 on 10 July to 
0200 on 11 July in Tripoli's airspace zone. 


JANA's political editor says: Why is this provocative exercise being staged at this 
particular time and in the waters situated in the Tripoli airspace zone? What does this 
exercise which was suddenly announced mean? Is it a complement to the American-Zionist 
invasion of Lebanon -- the invasion which resulted in the barbarous mass annihilation of 
both Lebanese and Palestinian peoples with a view to breaking the will of the resistance 
of the Arab people? Does it not force us to say that it is an extension of the 
American-Zionist-Saudi reaction conspiracy as a whole, aimed at destabilizing the Arab 
region, at exercising organized international terrorism against the peaceful peoples 

and at enforcing the American strategy which is aimed at humiliating the Arab peoples, 
at seizing their land and their spiritual, oil and water resources? 


The political editor added that this exercise is part of all the aforementioned, parti- 
cularly since it comes at this time and in an unexpected manner.
