Al-Qadhdhafi on Possibility of Another U.S. Raid 
PM161442 Paris PARIS MATCH in French 17 Oct 86 
pp 38,39 - FOR OFFICIAL USE ONLY 

[Interview with Libyan leader Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi by 
Pierre Hurel — date and place not given] 

[Text] Paris Match: At the beginning of September the immi- 
nence of another U.S. attack on Libya was front-page news in 
the American press. At the same time General Walters, Presi- 
dent Reagan’s special envoy, visited the European governments 
to find out about their attitude to such an eventuality. Do you 
think that Europe, and particularly France, would take part in 
another raid? 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: I do not think so. The first Ameri- 
can raid was a fiendish act and the whole world condemned 
Reagan. The European governments, aside from Britain, were 
not pleased with the American initiative. None of them would 
want to take part in another attack by the White House thug. 

Paris Match: If such an attack was perpetrated, how would 
you react? : 
Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: If the United States uses its mili- 
tary strength once again, I will be forced to look outside Libya 
and raise an army on a world scale — an army of fedayeen and 
commandos to crush the American empire. American fleets, 
intercontinental missiles, and nuclear bombs will be unable to 
combat this invisible army. There would be tens of thousands of 
clandestine warriors,each wearing the cloak of danger. No reg- 
ular army can fight against a secret army. It is always secret 
armies that have destroyed empires. This statement is my final 
warning. I will not hesitate. 

Paris Match: Do you mean that attacks will be perpetrated on 
American territory? 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: You should not ask me this ques- 
tion. We have not perpetrated any acts of terrorism in Europe or 
the United States. But if I decide to do so, no border will be 
inviolable. 

Paris Match: And yet no terrorist commando group has yet 
struck inside the United States. It is Europe which is experienc- 
ing the violence.... 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: It is the United States that wants 
the violence to be transferred to Europe. The United States is 
prepared to sacrifice Europe to defend itself. That is why it has 
deployed its medium-range missiles in Europe. It wants World 

War III to be fought in your countries! The Europeans are 
unaware of what is happening or are obedient to the Americans. 

Paris Match: If there is another US. attack on Libya, how 
would the Soviet Union react? 
Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: Thatis the Soviet Union’s business. 

Paris Match: What was Gorbachev’s answer to your recent 
proposal to join the Warsaw Pact? 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: I called for the creation of a pact 
against imperialism that would be broader than the Warsaw 
Pact. It will be created. 

Paris Match. What do you think of the forthcoming Reagan- 
Gorbachev summit? 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: It will be a failure, like all the 
previous summits. The Soviet Union wants to halt the arms race 
for the sake of its own interests. The United States wants to 
extend its lebensraum over the whole earth. Peace does not 
interest the Americans, Reagan has told his friends that this 
summit is a historic opportunity for the United States to win a 
strategic victory over the USSR. Gorbachev will realize one day 
that he has been betrayed. 

Paris Match:

What was your reaction to the recent terrorist 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: I expressed my sympathy to the 
French Government over the victims of these acts. We were 
surprised at this wave of violence in France. I do not know what 
France has done to be the scene of such violence. Perhaps there 
are things about which we do not know.... The cause of all this is 
that France is involved in the Middle East conflict. And, just as 
France and other European countries train terrorist groups, the 
Middle East countries have their terrorist groups. Europe, as a 
democratic continent, gives asylum to Middle East oppositionists 
who are in fact terrorists. This is what has brought violence from 
the Middle East to European territory. This violence will not end 
until the Middle East conflict has been solved. 

Paris Match: Do you know [Lebanese Revolutionary Armed 
Forces leader] Georges Ibrahim ‘Abdallah? 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: This is the first time I have heard 
this name, Nobody is sure that it is his real name. And nobody 
is sure that a person known by this name exists. 

Paris Match: What is your reaction to[French Prime Minister] 
Jacques Chirac’s statements at the United Nations about Libyan 
interference in Chad?

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: What did he say? 

Paris Match: He condemned Libya for “its attempts at 
destabilization, its occupation of part of the territory, and, 
finally, its open military aggression...” 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: But it is France that is responsible 
for the destabilization of Chad! It is France that must withdraw 
its troops! France is responsible for the destabilization of numer- 
ous African countries. It has organized coups in the Central 
African Republic, in Chad, in Congo, in Guinea, and in Niger. 
All former French colonies have experienced and will experience 
coups staged by France. This is because France wants certain 
kinds of regime and is prepared to sacrifice a country’s stability 
to achieve this. But we will oppose this. If a French force returns 
to Chad, we will enter Chad. If France fights in Chad, we will 
fight. We will do this because our proximity puts us in the best 
position to guarantee Chad’s peace and stability. 

Paris Match: Do you want the partition of Chad? 
Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: We are opposed to the partition of 
Chad. The law is on the side of the National Unity Transition 
Government. The Ndjamena regime is illegal, it is a minority 
government protected by France. There is no government in 
Ndjamena. : 

Paris Match: Could you intervene personally to secure the 
release of the seven [French] hostages in Lebanon? 
Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: Who is holding these hostages? 

Paris Match: 1 personally do not know... You are in a better 
position to know than I am! 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: So many hostages have been 
released and so many have been killed that I do not have a very 
clear picture of the current situation in Lebanon. But if I could 
help secure their release I would not hesitate to do so. 

Paris Match: You said recently with regard to the U.S. 
raid: “The Libyan people repulsed the 10th crusade.” Do you 
regard yourself as the leader of the Arab people’s historic revenge 
against the West? 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: Crusader-style hatred of the Arabs 
is being revived in the West; it is ancestral hatred revived by the 
alliance with Israel. We must enlighten the world and reveal the 
existence of this religious pact against Islam — this racial pact 
against the Arabs. The crusades are not over and this revival is 
a disaster for civilization. We thought that the West had gone 
beyond the stage of religious fundamentalism and racial hatred. 
It is now reviving them. 

Paris Match. The French in general tend to think that you are 
the aggressor... 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: This is because I am one of the first 
to defend the Arabs against the West. The West thinks that the 
earth belongs to it and that men are its slaves. When the slaves 
struggle, the West regards them as enemies, insurgents, barbar-

Paris Match: | Western espionage experts state that the U.S.

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: I have not noticed anything. 

Paris Match: Has nothing changed? 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: Nothing. 
Paris Match: And yet there is talk of a plot hatched inside the 
armed forces. Is this true? Has anybody been punished? 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: The United States has trained ter- 
rorists that it has infiltrated into Libya, This has happened 
before, back in 1984: We found them in an apartment block 
manufacturing explosives and plotting attacks. They were all 
arrested. I know that the West and the United States are 
continuing to train terrorists to infiltrate them into Libya. The 
West is guilty of official terrorism against Libya and Syria. The 
terrorists who have perpetrated attacks against civilians in Syria 
were trained in the West. They are now in the FRG and Britain. 

Paris Match. Do the French have reason to fear the Islamic 
Revolution — that of Libya, Iran, and Syria? 

Mu‘ammaral-Qadhdhafi: The three cases are not comparable. 
The Libyan revolution is a national revolution that does not 
concern France. We would have carried out this revolution 
whether or not we had been Muslims. We are not living in the 
days of the wars of religion. The conflict is not between Muslim 
and Christian, but between the colonizer and the colonized. 

Paris Match: Will you continue as Libyan leader for long or 
do you intend to retire one day? 

Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi: This is one of the things you do not 
understand: I have no official post. This is a Jamahiriyah 
(literally “of the people”), not a republic. 

And yet you are called “the leader’! And it is 
your face which can be seen on posters throughout Tripoli. 

Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi: That is because I represent the 
Libyan people. The Libyan people are behind me and this makes 
me stronger than ever.
