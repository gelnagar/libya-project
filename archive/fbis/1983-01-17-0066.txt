LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES PDRY DEFENSE MINISTER 


LD171028 Tripoli JANA in English 0907 GMT 17 Jan 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Thani 3, Jan 17, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The leader of the 
revolution received yesterday Mr Muslih Qasim, Democratic Yemen's Defence Minister, 

who handed him a letter from President ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad on Democratic Yemen's agree- 
ment to hold the conference of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front called for by 
the leader. 


Al-'Ubaydi Confers With Qasim 
LD162100 Tripoli JANA in English 1828 GMT 16 Jan 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Thani 2, Jan 16, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The Foreign Liaison 
Secretary 'Abd al-"Ati al-Ubaydi this morning conferred with Mr Salih Muslih Qasim, 
member of the Central Committee and defence minister of South Yemen who is currently 
visiting the Jamahiriyah. 


Discussions centred on methods of enhancing fraternal relations and cooperation between 
the Libyan Arab and Yemeni peoples in all fields, and ways of confronting imperialist- 
Zionist-reactionary schemes to undermine the Arab nation's freedom. 


17 Jan 83 Q 2 NORTH AFRICA 


Mr Muslih Qasim affirmed South Yemen's positive response to the leader's call for 
holding a meeting in Tripoli at the earliest possible date of the leaders of the 
National Front for Steadfastness and Confrontation to face current conditions and to 
agree on a comprehensive strategy capable of dealing with the current situation through 
which the Arab nation is passing. 


LD161353 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 16 Jan 83 


[Report on speech by Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Jallud, Revolution Command Council 
member, at ceremony to mark the birthday of Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir, at the Egyptian Front 
offices in Tripoli on 15 January] 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 16 Jan (JANA) -- Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud said that our 
celebration at this place in particular has more than one meaning. This building was 
once a church and one of the colonialist instruments of the Christian imperialist 
culture against the Arab nation. It used to form one of the links of the capitalist, 
Christian cultural invasion of the Arab nation and Africa. 


He added: Today we celebrate this place, as a result of the Great Fatih Revolution, 
being turned into a movement of enlightenment for Islamic missionaries and a bastion 
of Islamic enlightenment, which gives Islam its true meaning and projects it as a 
revolution in the full meaning of the word. This alone honors ‘Abd an-Nasir, who 
revolutionized Al-Azhar and destroyed this charlatanism and dervishism in it and turned 
it into a progressive Arab-Islamic cultural center of enlightenment. He also banned 
any meeting in it with the Zionists. 


Staff Maj Jallud exposed the posture of the Iraqi regime. He asked: Where is the 
Ba'ath Party that outbid ‘Abd an-Nasir in the Rodgers plan? Where is the Ba'th Party 
in Iraq, which recently has become an instrument used by America to fight the Islamic 
Revolution in Iran and to betray the struggles of the Iraqi people and their national 
forces? Saddam took part in Fes and was among the Arab rulers who recognized the enemy 
in Fes. Moreover, the Iraqi information media are wooing America and are boasting and 
saying that we are against your strategy and that Iraq is within the American strategy. 
Staff Maj Jallud added that the Iraqi regime has stated that there is no Arab ruler in 
existence who does not believe in the existence of the State of Israel and that 
security guarantees must be given to the State of Israel. He explained that they have 
the right, since there is no Arab ruler who does not believe in the existence of the 
State of Israel. 


He said that we in Libya are ashamed for Baghdad, pan-Arab Baghdad, revolutionary 
Baghdad, rejectionist Baghdad. He explained that today Baghdad is an instrument in the 
hand of reaction and America. He pointed out that Iraq's resources are being used now 
to stike at the Arab masses after the plots against Iraq destroyed Iraq's decisions. 


Staff Maj Jallud explained that the problem in the Arab homeland is not one of weapons 
‘or manpower, rather it is the defeated Arab political will represented in the rulers. 
The Arab is defeated by bourgeoise Western liberal cultures through the Arab informa- 
tion media, which writes in Arabic letters and serves the Zionist-imperialist- 
reactionary aims. He said: Then the problem lies in the psychological defeat. At 
the fifth meeting of the Revolutionary Committee the defeated mentality was analyzed. 
Psychology explains the reasons for this defeat. The problem is not that of ability; 
it is in the lack of will to fight and to be martyred. He pointed out that the Libyan 
people had fought the Italians because martyrdom was a must, the way the Algerian 
people fought the French for love of martyrdom.

This is also the case with the Lebanese and the Iranians. He stressed that our theory 
is love for death and martyrdom and our mobilization project is to drive the masses 
toward the love of death. 


He said that what happened in Beirut is a crisis and that the problems of all our 
defeats since 1948 until now is ideological and the natural result of the defeated 
political will of the rulers and the will of the defeated Arab citizen through counter- 
cultures and counter-information media and through grilling, exploitation, and repres- 
sion to which they are exposed. 


He said that we in Jamahiriyah believe that there is a real train of death swallowing 
the Arab homeland country by country and that this train wants to end all our existence 
and wants us to accept living like the Red Indians. 


He said that by mobilizing, arming and training the people, men and women in Libya, 
Syria, the Palestinian camps, Lebanon and Cairo are bound to turn every house into a 
trench of resistance. He pointed out that, unfortunately, long after the Zionist 
storming of Lebanon the leaderships and organizations maintain the same behavior and 
the same attitudes as if nothing had happened in Lebanon. He said that we in 
Jamahiriyah, and all those who share with us this conviction, are determined to find an 
historic solution. As for those who do not share this conviction, they will repeat the 
failure and will practice the failure. In years to come we will find another earthquake 
taking place somewhere other than in Beirut. 


He explained that the Arabs now have surrendered and have recognized the enemy in Fes. 
He stressed that we believe that our existence conflicts with the Zionist existence. 

It is impossible for the two nationalities to exist -- either an Arab nationality or a 
racist nationality. Staff Maj Jallud denounced the Fes plan. He stressed that it is a 
capitulationist plan through which Arab reaction gave cover to the entry of Israeli 
forces into Lebanon. 


Staff Maj "Abd as-Salam Jallud stressed that the Lebanese link is one of the links of 
the Zionist train of death designed to annex and to swallow other Arab countries. He 
pointed out that the problem is not the Palestinian question. Subsequently, we call 
for the commencement of a real resistance and war in Lebanon. He said we are prepared 
to back the popular resistance in Lebanon with all the power we have to fight the 
Israelis. He added that every Muslim, every progressive person, every unionist, and 
every patriot, and even the bourgeoise must fight the Zionist enemy. 


Staff Maj Jallud noted that despite the end of As-Sadat who betrayed Arab cause, the 
treacherous Camp David accords, which he signed, still exist in Egypt. Subsequently, 
Egypt is still capitulating and tied to these humiliating agreements. He called on the 
Egyptian people to get rid of the fact that they are living in the shadow of the Camp 
David accords and to rebel against them and to rid Egypt of Zionist and imperialist 
ties and to resist the Jews who soiled the land of Egypt.
