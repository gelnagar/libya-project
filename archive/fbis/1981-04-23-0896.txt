LIBYA

HUNGARIAN DEFENSE MINISTER ARRIVES FOR VISIT 


LD221044 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0930 GMT 22 Apr 81 


{Text] Tripoli, 22 Apr (JANA)--The Hungarian Defense Minister, Army Gen Lajos Czinege, 
arrived in Tripoli yesterday evening on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah where he was met at Tripoli airport by Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander 
in chief of the Libyan Armed Forces, and a number of senior Libyan officers. 


On his arrival the Hungarian minister made a statement to JANA in which he said that 

his visit falls within the framework of meetings among friends and will provide an op- 
portunity for the further development of relations between our two peoples. The minister 
praised the achievements of the Libyan Arab people and their great accomplishments in 
their land. He also praised Libya's principled stands against the plots of imperialism 
and reaction. He denounced the imperialist endeavors directed against the achievements 
of the Libyan people. 


In conclusion he said that there were some who are perturbed by the development and 
progress Jamahiriyah has made because the Great 1 September Revolution has always taken 
bold stands and placed the interests of the Libyan and Arab peoples in general and their 
ambitions above everything else. 


Jabir Reception 
LD222350 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 22 Apr 81 
[Text] Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, general commander of the armed forces, this evening 
received the Hungarian Defense Minister, Gen Lajos Czinege. During the meeting, talks 
on the consolidation and development of cooperation between the two friendly peoples 


were discussed. 


ROMANIA-LIBYA COMMISSION TALKS BEGIN 22 APR 


AU221909 Bucharest AGERPRES in English 1800 GMT 22 Apr 81 


{Text] Tripoli, 22 Apr (AGERPRES)--The eighth session of the Joint Romanian-Libyan 
Commission on Economic and Technical Cooperation started in Tripoli. Ion Patan, deputy 
prime minister and minister of technico-material supply and control of fixed assets 
administration, chairman for Romania of the joint commission, had meetings on the 
occasion with 'Abd as-Salam az-Zaq'ar, secretary of the General People's Committee on 
Petroleum, chairman for Libya of the joint commission, as well as with executives of 
other Libyan economic departments. Questions of development of the economic and 
technical cooperation between Romania and Libya were discussed on the occasion.

CSSR'S STEPAN RECEIVED BY AL-QADHDHAFI 17 APR 


AU211729 Prague RUDE PRAVO in Czech 17 Apr 81 p 1l 


{[CTK report: "Contribution of Students Movement to the Struggle for Peace"] 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 Apr (CTK)--In Tripoli on Thursday Libya's highest representative, 
Mu’ ammar al-Qadhdhafi, received Miroslav Stepan, chairman of the International Students 
Union. Al-Qadhdhafi highly valued the contribution of the progressive international 
revolutionary students movement to the world struggle against imperialism, to the 
development of the revolutionary process in various parts of the world and to the 
struggle for lasting peace and social progress. At the close of its stay in Libya, the 
delegation of the International Students Union assessed, with the General Union of 
Libyan Students, a joint project of student actions and the preparation of an inter- 
national students conference on just utilization of natural resources in the developing 
countries.
