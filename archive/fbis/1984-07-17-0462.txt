LIBYA

Envoy on Al-Qadhdhafi Message to Egypt, Plans 
LD161632 Kuwait KUNA in Arabic 1516 GMT 16 Jul 84 


[Text] Rabat 16 Jul (KUNA) The Libyan leader’s envoy Mr 
Muhamad Abu al-Qasim Az-Zuwayy confirmed that Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi’s messages to [words indistinct]. He added that the 
messages to the Sudan and the PLO were handled through the 
Arab League. The message to Egypt was handed through its UN 
representative. 


A diplomatic source in Rabat told KUNA’s correspondent of this 
contact with Egypt, the first since the severance of diplomatic 
relations in 1974. 


In a mini press conference he held today in Rabat, Mr Az- 
Zuwayy said the colonel’s messages carry practical suggestions 
for joint Arab action and concern two subjects: how toestablish 
Arab unity, and the future of the Palestinian cause. He said that 
Col al-Qadhdhafi proposed finding a possible program which 
would be agreed upon through the Arab League and the forma- 
tion of a special committee.
