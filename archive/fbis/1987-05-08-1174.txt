LIBYA

Paris Radio on Vanuatu’s Expulsion of 2 Libyans 
LD072145 Paris International Service in French 
2030 GMT 7 May 87 


[Excerpt] It is not only in Africa that Libyan activities are causing 
concern. Even Vanuatu, in the Pacific, which had decided to 
establish diplomatic relations with Tripoli, has expelled two 
Libyans. These two officials are said to have violated diplomatic 
norms. This will reassure Australia and New Zealand, which are 
both worried about Libyan activities in the area and particularly 
about Soviet penetration of the region. [passage omitted]
