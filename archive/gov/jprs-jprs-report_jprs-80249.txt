POLISARIO DEFECTOR OUTLINES LIBYAN, ALGERIAN AID 
NC211414 Paris AFP in English 1354 GMT 21 Feb 82 


[Text] Rabat, 21 Feb (AFP)--A defector from the Polisario Front guerrillas, fight- 
ing Morocco for control over the Western Sahara, has claimed that he was drugged, 
taken against his will to Algeria and forced by the Algerian army to read a state- 
ment to the international press attacking Morocco and Mauritania. 


Mbarek Ould Mayshem, in a special interview published by Morocco's news agency map 
this weekend, also claimed that he participated in attacks against the Moroccan 
army after being trained by the Algerian army and promoted to the rank of lieuten- 
ant. 


Claiming to have been wounded in action, Lt Ould Mayshem said he was taken to 
Algiers and Tripoli where he was treated by "Yugoslav, Egyptian, Palestinian and 
Indian specialists." 


He later left for Europe and joined the revolutionary Saharan popular front, which 
he called an "authentic Saharan movement" that owes no allegiance to Algeria. 


Lt Ould Mayshem also said that there was widespread suffering in the Polisario 
camp at Tindouf, in Algeria, where "death is presented as a glory." 


"A mother who cries for the death of her son is immediately put in prison," he 
said. 


The dominant ideology in the camp is communist-based, and “one cannot recite the 
Koran in public or say prayers in front of other people,” he said. 


Lt Ould Mayshem said that Vietnamese, South Yeminis and about 100 Cubans are at the 
camp but that Algerians were in charge of military training and operations. 


He claimed that Libya provides Soviet-made Kalashnikov guns, SAM-6 and SAM-8 missiles 


and armoured vehicles and that 250 guerrillas have received military training, 
including naval training, in Libya.
