LIBYA

DELEGATION RETURNS FROM U.S. VISIT 24 FEB 
LD250008 Tripoli JANA in English 1418 GMT 24 Feb 79 LD 


(Text ] Tripoli, February 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Libyan Arab peoplé's dele- 
gation under the leadership of the head of the foreign liaison office, Mr. Ahmed 
ash-Shihati, returned to Tripoli. The delegation had been on a visit to the United 
States in response to an invitation made by Mr. Billy Carter and the American people's 
delegation which participated in the Arab-American dialogue. 


Mr. ash-Shihati made a statement to the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY's correspondent in 
which he said the people's delegation succeeded in breaking through the mass-media and 
psychological barriers which Zionism is trying to impose in order to falsify the Libyan 
Arab people's stands for the Palestinian people's cause and liberation causes in Africa, 
Asia and latin America. 


He said Libyan Arab-American people's dialogue was based on mutual respect and added that 
he felt a definite desire by the American people to establish close links with the Libyan 
Arab people. Mr. ash-Shihati said the visit changed to a great extent the prevalent 
American adverse view of the Jamahiriyah created by Zionist propaganda. 
Mr. ash-Shihati affirmed the Arab communities and American people's understanding of the 
Great First of September Revolution's humanistic principles based upon the freedom and 
independence of man everywhere.
