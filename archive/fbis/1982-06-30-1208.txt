PAPER ON ARAB ‘COMPLICITY’ IN ISRAELI AGGRESSION 


LD291710 Tripoli JANA in English 1434 GMT 29 


[Text] Tripoli, Ramadan 8, Jun 29, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The newspaper AZ-ZAHF 
AL-AKHDAR said in an article today that the barbaric Zionist onslaught against Lebanon 
has two basic aims. 


1 -- Total liquidation of the Palestinian Resistance movement in Lebanon. Such action 
would secure Israel's safety from any Arab confrontation, because Palestinian armed 
resistance represents the Arabs' last effort for steadfastness and challenge. It would 
further open the door for reactionary Arab regimes to participate in the Camp David 
process. 


2 -- The complete occupation of Lebanon and the signing of a capitulation treaty with it. 
This is one of the dreams of the Zionist movement and Israel's ruling rightist coalition 
that invaded Palestine and Lebanon, annexed the Golan Heights, spoiled the lands and 
skies of more than one Arab country, occupied Egypt and is still fighting on all fronts, 
plunging the [words indistinct] wars that will ultimately lead to their doom unless they 
return to their countries of origin. 


The paper also condemned the treachery and complicity of all Arab governments without 
exception for not acting against the mass annihilation of the Lebanese and Palestinian 
people that is still taking place with violent fervour. The paper accused these govern- 
ments of making it possible for the racist-Zionist entity to continue occupying Arab 
lands through protecting it. 


The magazine strongly condemned the stand of the international forces who are friendly 
with Arabs and claim to be progressive and lead the anti-imperialist and anti-Zionist 
struggle. 


The paper wondered where are the Arab armies and the high-ranking officers with medals 
and decorations? Are these armies specially built for military parades and to terrorize 
the Arab masses? Is their chalienge limited to receiving the executives who carry with 
them the American proposals? The Zionist enemy is fully supported by the U.S.A. who 
protects this enemy and even prevents international condemnation of its atrocities. By 
doing so the U.S.A. relies very much on her Arab allies who support her with millions of 
oil barrels and military bases. Foremost of those, the Lebanese tragedy has shown the 
true forces who are fighting the enemies and exposed the opportunists and those who only 
raise the revolutionary slogans as a disguise, and further exposed the forces who 
protect Israel and encourage her in her aggression against the Lebanon, Syria and the 
Palestinian Resistance. 


The paper said: "The Arab rulers are putting pressure on the Palestinian Resistance in 
order to silence its guns and consequently force it to take part in the Camp David 
capitulation. All this is being don y fake promises and financial privileges for some 
of its leaders. lany of the Arab rulers are behaving as if terrorist Begin, Sharon, 


Weizman, and Peres are their true friends and allies.’ 


V. 30 Jun 82 NORTH AFRICA 


The paper pointed to the rulers rejection of an Arab summit to discuss the Zionist 
invasion of the Lebanon. Instead, the paper said, 7 Arab rulers have decided to send 
their foreign ministers for a meeting. Those foreign ministers are known as CIA agents 
and very much adore the dollar more than the Arab cause. The foreign ministers do not 
have the power to take any decision even if the decision concerns their private lives 
and their wives. They are more faithful to Reagan and Begin than to their nation and 
home land. 


The meeting, the paper said, is aimed at serving the Zivsist ambitions and tightening 
the conspiracy on the Palestinian Resistance and consequently allowing more Arab rulers 
to join Camp David. The paper said the revolutionary forces will expose the rulers 

and ministers’ involvement in the conspiracies and the money they received for their 
part. The revolutionary forces, the paper said, challenge the Arab rulers to meet in 
an Arab summit and transform their palaces into command headquarters and to open their 
hearts for the armed struggle. 


The paper concluded tis article hailing the heroes who gave their lives in Palestine and 
Lebanon in defence of Arab homeland and Islam.
