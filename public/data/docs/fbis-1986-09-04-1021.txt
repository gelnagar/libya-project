LIBYA

Jallud, Rafiqdust Mark Links, Seek Saddam’s Demise 
LD031751 Tehran IRNA in English 
1737 GMT 3 Sep 86 


[Text] Tripoli, Sept 3, IRNA — Iranian minister of the Islamic 
Revolution’s Guards Corps (IRGC) Mohsen Rafiqdust told 
member of Libya’s leadership council Major ‘Abd al-Salam 
Jallud here Tuesday [2 Sep] that Libya will certainly emerge 
victorious in its fight with the U.S. 


Rafiqdust, here at the head of a delegation to participate in 
ceremonies marking the 17th anniversary of the victory of the 
Libyan revoiution, stressed that Iran will continue to stand by 
Libya’s side against U.S. threats. 


The IRGC minister also briefed Jallud on the successful Jranian 
offensives in the northern sector of the Persian Gulf and in Hajj 
‘Imran and said that Iran’s defensive war against the agents of 
imperialism and Zionism, and among them Saddam Husayn of 
Iraq, is not separate from Libya’s fight with the U.S. 


In response, the Libyan official said that Libya considers the 
Islamic Revolution as well as the Iranian struggle against impe- 
rialism as its own. He noted that the Tripoli government has been 
and will continue to be a staunch ally of the Islamic Revolution. 
Jallud described Iraqi ruler Saddam Husayn as the cause of 
tension in the Persian Gulf and called for a quick toppling of the 
Iraqi regime. He noted that the elimination of Saddam Husayn 
will be in the interest of all revolutionary people and countries in 
the region.

Rafiqdust 
LD031903 Tripoli JANA in English 
1805 GMT 3 Sep 86 


[Text] Tripoli, Dulhija 29, Shahr al-Fatih 3, Jamahiriyah News 
Agency — Mohsen Rafiqdust, the Iranian minister of the Islamic 
Revolution Corps, and his accompanying delegation left Tripoli 
this afternoon. 


The Iranian minister gave a statement praising the deep-rooted 
relations that bind the two peoples of Great Jamahiriyah and 
Islamic Iran. 


In his statement, he indicated that he and his accompanying 
delegation have come to Great Jamahiriyah as envoys of the 
Iranian people and government to join the Libyan Arab People 
in its celebrations of the 17th anniversary of the Great Al-Fatih 
Revolution. 


He also praised the courageous confrontation of the Libyan Arab 
people to the U.S. imperialist attack confirming the standing of 
the Iranian people by the side of its brothers in Great Jama- 
hiriyah to repulse any new imperialist aggression. 


Jallud Receives Sudan’s Al-Banna, Delegation 
LD031924 Tripoli JANA in English 
1744 GMT 3 Sep 86 


[Text] Tripoli, Dulhija 29, Shahr al-Fatih 3, Jamahiriyah News 
Agency — Staff Major ‘Abd al-Salam Ahmad Jallud, received 
today Idris al-Banna, the vice president of the State Council in 
the Republic of Sudan and his accompanying delegation who are 
joining the Libyan Arab people in its celebrations of the 17th 
anniversary of the Great al-Fatih Revolution. The reception was 
attended by the Sudanese ambassador to Great Jamahiriyah. 


Staff Major ‘Abd al-Salam Ahmad Jallud also received the 
popular Sudanese delegation which is joining the celebrations of 
the people of Great Jamahiriyah of the 17th anniversary of the 
Great al-Fatih Revolution. The head of the Sudanese popular 
delegation affirmed Sudan’s standing with Great Jamahiriyah’s 
people against the imperialist threats of the U.S. terrorist admin- 
istration. He greeted the brave standing of the Libyan Arab 
people in the face of the colonial plots, confirming that the Great 
Jamahiriyah’s people is building the Arab nation’s glory.

People’s Committee Seeks Proof of U.S. ‘Claims’ 
LD301348 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 
1230 GMT 30 Aug 86 


[Statement issued by the People’s Committee of the People’s 
Bureau for Foreign Liaison; date not given — announcer-read] 


[Text] Within the framework of its series of aggressive and 
terroristic actions against the Libyan Arab people and its rev- 
olutionary command, the U.S. Administration has been trying to 
delude world public opinion with flimsy pretexts aiming at 
influencing it and creating favorable conditions for aggression 
against the Great Jamahiriyah. It claims that it has definite proof 
and information about terrorist actions which Libya will carry 
out against U.S. citizens and targets. 


The People’s Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison categorically denies these false claims. It also wishes to 
make clear to the international contmeaity that the Jamahiriyah 
has no information or knowledge of persons carrying out or 
intending to carry such actions. 


We ask the U.S. Administration to present the proofs and 
evidence and name the persons the U.S. Administration claims 
to be entrusted with carrying out such actions, their places of stay 
and any other specific and true information it may have so that 
they may be exposed and arrested and their identities determined 
and thereby abort their actions before they commit them and 
bring them to trial in front of world public opinion. 


With our proposal, we are providing the world with an opportu- 
nity to abort the terroristic actions alleged by the United States 
and at the same time save the world from the dangers of the 
destructive U.S. aggression which threatens world peace and 
security and that of the Mediterranean. 


Out of its responsibility as a permanent member of the UN 
Security Council for the safeguarding of international peace and 
security, the United States should cooperate with the interna- 
tional community to achieve this objective. 


In this regard, the People’s Committee of the People’s Bureau for 
Foreign Liaison is anxious to show world public opinion that the 
Jamahiriyah rejects terrorism outright, on tk asis that the 
Libyan Arab people are the first victims of terror n, particularly 
state terrorism, and that the human losses and devastation they 
suffered as a result of these terrorist practices give them a vital, 
direct interest in the elimination of all forms of terrorism, includ- 
ing the state terrorism.
