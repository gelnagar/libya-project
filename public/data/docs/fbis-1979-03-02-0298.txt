LIBYA

AL -QADHDHAFI OPENS GENERAL PEOPLE'S CONGRESS SESSION 


LDO11534 Tripoli JANA in English 1444 GMT 1 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, March 1, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--At 10:40 this morning ( Jamahiriyah 
local time), the secretary-general of the General People's Congress, Colonel Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, inaugurated the opening session of the General People's Congress in order to 
select the new general secretariat of the General People's Congress and the new General 
People's Committee on the level of the Jamahiriyah as the last stage of selection. 


At the beginning of the session, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi made some remarks about 
the selection process, expressing his appreciation of the democratic style used in the 
selection process and the quality of the people selected as secretaries of qualitative 
committees and heads of municipalities' committees. 


In today's morning session the General People's Congress selected Mr. 'Ali ‘Abu al-Khayir 
secretary for congresses' affairs, Mr. Abdallah Zahmul secretary for people's committees' 
affairs and Mukhtar Qurbu!' secretary for trade unions and vocational associations! affairs. 


It is worth mentioning that Mr. 'Abd al'Ati al-'Ubaydi, who has been selected to secretary- 
general of the General People's Congress, previously held the position of head of the 
General People's Committee.

Mr. Muhammad az-Zuwayy, who has been selected under secretary of the congress, previous- 
ly held the post of information and culture secretary. 


As to Mr. 'Ali 'Abu al-Khayir, he was the secretary of the People's Congress in Benghazi 
municipality while Mr, Abdallah Zahmul was the secretary of the People's Congress in 
Tripoli municipality and Mr, Mukhtar Qurbu!' was the secretary of the producers union. 
Mr. Muhammad ash-Shuwayhdi was selected information and culture secretary through a 
process of direct free selection by the members of the General People's Congress. Mr. 
ash-Shuwayhdi is now holding the post of chief-editor of the Arab culture magazine which 
is widely read in the Arab homeland. 


AL-QADHDHAFI ON SEPARATION OF AUTHORITY, REVOLUTION 
LDO20045 Tripoli JANA in English 1633 GMT 1 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 1 March, JANA--The leader of the great First of September Revolution, 
Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, referred to the separation of the authority from the revolu- 
tion. He stressed that everybody, in the country and abroad, is now convinced that 
revolution is something while authority is something else, for the revolution will re- 
main and is not a question of election, selection or appointment. Like faith, it comes 
from within and is an initiative, even a risk in its beginning. On the eve of the great 
First September Revolution, we have risked our lives and those of thousands of soldiers 
and innocent civilians, 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi stressed that the revolution is not a post but a principle. A 
person remains revolutionary until he loses his revolutionary fervour, like a person who 
remains faithful until he loses his faith. No one can restore faith. Similarly if a 
revolutionary loses his revolutionary fervour no one can restore it to him. Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi added that many friends, comrades and people dear to us dropped out and 
lost their revolutionary fervour and all our attempts and prayers failed to restore it 
to them. 


He affirmed that no one could prevent others from practising the revolution, for the 
revolution cannot be described in an administrative, functional or legal concept, nor 
is it an attribute one can adopt by decision, law or vote. He said that as long as we 
are revolutionaries our mission is to defend the revolution, assert all the dicta it 
introduced, establish the law of the revolution, ensure the success of the comprehen- 
sive revolutionary transformation, achieve the revolution's aims and let the revolution 
spread from one people to another, 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that the revolution has a historic mission beyond the 
secretariat of the congress, any other people's committee or post. He said that the 
leadership of the revolution shall not be involved in any post secretariat or people's 
committees, but will join the forces of the revolution and work for it, adding that 

this path is open to any one present, or absent. 


FOREIGN SECRETARIAT ASKS ABOUT DIPLOMAT HELD BY SAUDIS 

LD272100 Tripoli JANA in English 1635 GMT 27 Feb 79 LD 

[Text] Tripoli, February 27, JAMAHIRIYAH NEW AGENCY--The Jamahiriyah's Foreign Secre- 
tariat made contacts with the Saudi Government through its embassy in Tripoli to enquire 


about the fate of the Libyan Arab diplomat Abdussalaam Bashir Butweirat [name as received] 
who is detained by the Saudi authorities.

Mr Butweirat is employed at the Jamahiriyah!'s Embassy in Yemen Arab Republic and not in 
Yemen People's Democratic Republic as previously reported by us. Mr, Butweirat left Aden 
on 17th October 1978 on Saudi Airlines flight No 795 on his way to the Jamahiriyah via 
Jidda where he was detained by the Saudi authorities.
