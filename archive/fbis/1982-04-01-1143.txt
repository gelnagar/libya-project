LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES SYRIAN, PLO OFFICIALS 


LD3i2200 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 31 Mar 82 


[Text] The leader of the revolution today received Brother ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam, Syrian 
deputy prime minister and foreign minister, and brother ‘Abd al-Muhsin Abu Mayzar, member 
of the Executive Committee and the official PLO spokesman.
