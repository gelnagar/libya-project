[Text ] Nouakchott, 1 Nov (AFP)--Mauritanian head of state Mustapha Ould Saleck was 
promoted today to the rank of full colonel, it was announced here, 


V. 2 Nov 78 NORTH AFRICA 

As a lieutenant colonel, Saleck seized power from President Moktar Ould Daddah in a coup 
dtetat last July. 

Meanwhile it was announced here that Col Saleck will hold talks in Paris next Tuesday 
with President Valery Giscard D'Estaing. The Mauritanian head of state's visit to Paris, 
originally scheduled for tomorrow, was postponed to enable him to attend the Arab summit 
in Baghdad.
