JANA POLITICAL EDITOR COMMENTS ON SABHA SUMMIT 
LD261820Y Tripoli JANA in English 1420 GMT 26 Feb 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, February 26, JANA--The summit of four African countries in Sabha has 
proved to the world once again that Africans can solve their problems themselves, through 
positive dialogue, without any foreign intervention. 


Tne political editor of the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY in a commentary on the summit 
communique said the leaders of the Jamahiryah, Chad, Sudan and Niger not only exerted 
their efforts to reach an agreement but they considered it necessary to commit themselves 
to implement the agreement to close the door in the face of all challengers and to 
prevent intervention from any source, except the four signatory countries. 


The political editor said the mere holding of the Sabha summit was a triumph for the 
African will against the cclonialist enemies of Africa and against the treacherous 
hireling agents, who exerted every possible effort to frustrate the summit and prevent 
it from achieving positive results for solving the problem of our brothers in Chad, 
halting blood shed and providing a convenient atmosphere to build and reconstruct the 
African brotherly region, on the basis of national unity and co-operation with African 
brothers and neighbors. 


The editor referred to the Sabha summit decision to fix next March 21 as the date for 
holding a meeting in Sabha between representatives of the Chadian Government and various 
detachments of the Chadian Liberation Movement [FORLINAT], in support of efforts to 
realize national reconciliation in the brotherly Republic of Chad. 


It has also been agreed to set up a Libyan-Niger military committee to remain in touch 
with the circumstances of conflicting parties in Chad until national reconciliation is 
achieved. Regarding bilateral relations between the Jamahiriyah and Chad, the Chadian 
Government has decided to normalize diplomatic relations. The text and the spirit 

of the summit communique clearly indicate that Africa is determined to maintain its 
sovereignty and dignity and act for the realization of its unity, at any cost and 
sacrifice. It asserts that the African will, when united and concentrated towards 
finding solutions, will tolerate nothing obstructing its path and aspirations. 


The solution of the Chadian problem shal” be the first step of the unified African 
action, which will be an example for solving all the continent's problems and for 
reinforcing unified African action for the progress of the continent and the happiness 
of the African man. Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the revolution, clearly 
expressed this fact when he asserted that African countries are trying to solve the 
problem of Chad and they will be able to solve ali other African problems, whether in 
Zimbabwe, Namibia, Western Sahara or in any other part of the African Continent.

Will the colonialist powers, which have been living on looting the wealth of Africa, 
respect the African will embodied and expressed by the conference? Will these powers 
acknowledge that Africa is for Africans, including its land, wealth, thought and 
leadership and that African problems could only be solved by Africans themselves? 

Or have these colonialist forces deemed convenient to intervene in the continent's 
affairs, to plunder its wealth, to violate its sovereignty and spread sedition among 
its people and countries? 


If the imperialist forces and the foreign countries in general have absorbed the meaning 
and recognized the Africans' right to self-determination, running their own affairs and 
solving their problems through dialog, then a bright future is expected for cooperation 
between the black Continent and all world countries, to the interest of world peace, 
security and prosperity. But if the colonialists continue their obstinacy and their 
aggressive acts against Africa, which expelled colonialism despite the great differences 
in material and military potentials, then the African people will be able to give the 
enemies further lessons and smash the colonialist intentions of aggression, plunder 

and exploitation, through the strength of African unity, African brotherhood and 
African will. Those who are in doubt of this should try it and they will be sure that 
the oppressor will be defeated.

AL=QADHDHAFI HOLDS TALKS WITH KENYA'S FOREIGN MINISTER 
LD232055Y¥ Nairobi Domestic Service in English 1600 GMr 23 Feb 78 LD/EA 


(Text ] The Libyan leader, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, has spelled out his stand on 
Somalia's aggression against Kenya and Ethiopia and said Libya does not support any 
country which tries to acquire territory from other states by the use of force. 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi was receiving in Tripoli Kenya's minister for foreign affairs, 
Dr Waiyaki, who is in the Libya capital for an OAU ministerial meeting. 


The Libyan leader hailed Kenya and Uganda for their move to strengthen relations be- 
tween them. He said there was no need for Kenya and Uganda to quarrel as they were 
sister-states. He promised to do everything possible to strengthen further the 
improving Kenya=-Uganda relations. Colonel al-Qadhdhafi instructed his ministers to 
grant scholarships to Kenyans to study different courses in Libyan institutions. 


On aviation, the Libyan head of state revealed that Kenya Airways could start oper- 
ating flights through Benghazi and Tripoli airports with immediate effect. He 
proposed Kenya and Libya to establish a joint shipping line to facilitate the 
development of comnerce between the two sister African states.
