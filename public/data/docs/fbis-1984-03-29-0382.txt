LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Warns U.S. To Withdraw From Region 
NC281247 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0952 GMT 
28 Mar 84 


[Speech delivered by Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi during a 
celebration held at the Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir base in Tobrug to 
mark the 14th anniversary of the expulsion of the British troops 
and the closure of their bases in Libya — live] 


[Text] Brothers, we stand inside the Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir base 
to commemorate the 14th anniversary of the expulsion of the 
imperialist British forces from the land of Tobruq. This base was 
called the British Adem base. It is now called the Jamal ‘Abd 
an-Nasir base. 


Brothers, it is this base which graduated the Ramadan [October 
1973] war pilots who bombed and destroyed the Bar-Lev line. 
This base was a symbol of Arab unity and a symbol of the unity 
of the Libyan and Egyptian Armed Forces. It is the base which 
prepared the October heroic pilots, whom As-Sadat sold at a 
cheap price to Zionism and imperialism. Husni Mubarak, the 
present president of the Arab Republic of Egypt, testified that 
when he was commander of the Egyptian Air Force this base was 
completely placed at the disposal of his Air Force. Squadrons of 
Egyptian pilots graduated from this base. They fought against 
the Zionist occupation and destroyed Zionist positions on the 
canal, on the Bar-Lev line, and in Sinai. 


Vv. 29 Mar 84 


Brothers, this base was not a Libyan base; it was an Arab base. 
This base played a role in liberating Egypt and Sinai and in 
defeating Zionism. When we liberated this base from the British, 
we did not exploit it in the interests of Libya or the Libyan people 
alone. It was turned into a national base bearing the name of the 
great ‘Abd an- Nasir — ‘Abd an-Nasir, the symbol of Arab unity; 
‘Abd an-Nasir, the hero of Arab nationalism. [cheers and 


applause} 


The buried As-Sadat, who paid the price of national treason — 
every traitor of this Arab nation will pay the price — committed 
the abominable crime for which he was sentenced to death when, 
in 1977, he ordered the pilots who had graduated from the Jamal 
‘Abd an-Nasir base to try to destroy it. However, these pilots, 
who were ordered to do this by the accursed buried traitor 
As-Sadat, refused to destroy the base which turned out highly 
efficient pilots. They refused to destroy the base from which they 
had graduated and which had turned them into highly efficient 
pilots. They refused to destroy the base on which they had 
depended in their honorable battle against Zionism. They drop- 
ped their bombs far from this base. They carried out several raids 
against their will and upon the orders of this traitor. However, 
they refused to destroy this base. Our greetings to the October 
pilots and to the Egyptian pilots who graduated from the Jamal 
‘Abd an-Nasir base and who disobeyed the orders of the accursed 
traitor As-Sadat. 


Brothers, we declare today that the Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir base 
continues to be the Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir base in the service of 
Arab nationalism. It supports the Egyptian people in their 
forthcoming battle against reaction, stable David [Camp David 
accords}, and Zionism and for the liberation of the Sinai, which 
has not yet been liberated. Its liberation was a fake and sham 
liberation. The proof is that the Egyptian Army and the Egyptian 
Air Force cannot fly in the skies of the Sinai. The Egyptian 
Ground Forces cannot enter the Sinai. The Sinai is still under 
Zionist domination and under the foreign control of the multina- 
tional forces that are hostile to the Egyptian people. 


Brothers, we declare that the Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir base is still 
ready to receive the honest Egyptian pilots. 


We are still ready to place this base once again at the service of 
the Egyptian people, the liberation battle of the Egyptian people, 
and the liberation battle of the Arab nation. This base which 
bears the name of Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir cannot be a regional 
base. It can only be faithful to Jamal ‘Abd an- Nasir, whose name 
it bears. We called it the Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir base because in 
1956, British aircraft flew from it to participate in the tripartite 
aggression against fraternal Egypt. From this base an aggression 
was launched against Egypt and against ‘Abd an-Nasir, as well 
as against the 23 July revolution. For this reason we liberated 
this base in 1970 from British control. We called it the Jamal 
‘Abd an-Nasir base in order to vex the colonialists who did not 
believe this base could be freed from their domination or bear the 
name of Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir, whom they wanted to destroy 
from this base. They launched air raids against him from this 
base. This base was liberated by the revolution. Jamal ‘Abd 
an-Nasir’s name, brothers, was given to it despite the British. The
