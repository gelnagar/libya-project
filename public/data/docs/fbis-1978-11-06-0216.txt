LIBYA

AI-QADHDHAFI CALLS ON AMIN, NYERERE TO EXERCISE RESTRAINT 
LDO41440Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 4 Nov 78 LD 


[Text] Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi sent cables to Presidents Nyerere of Tanzania and 

Idi Amin of Uganda calling on them to exercise restraint and to try to solve the problems 
between their two countries by peaceful means, explaining that Africa is facing a fierce 
battle against colonialism and racism and that Africans should direct all their energies 
and potentials toward winning the battle against colonialism and racism. 


This morning Secretary of Information Muhammad Abu al-Qasim az-Zuwayy left Tripoli on a 
special mission from Al-Qadhdhafi to the two presidents. This is within the framework 
of Libya's moves to repair the rift between the two sister African countries. 


Peace Mission Progresses 
LDO51840yY Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 gmt 5 Nov 78 LD 


[Text] JANA, Dar es Salaam, 5 Nov--Muhamnad Abu al-Qasim az-Zuwayy, Libyan information 
and culture secretary, is due to arrive in Dar es Salaam today from Uganda, in the 
framework of Arab Libyan moves designed to heal the rift between the African countries. 
Az-Zuwayy arrived in Kampala yesterday, dispatched by Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, and 
held talks with President Idi Amin of Uganda. Muhammad az-Zuwayy is due to meet with 
President Julius Nyerere of Tanzania today. 


Comments on Baghdad Summit 


LD051634Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 4 Nov 78 LD 


[Excerpts] Now Arab brothers, we present a recording of an interview our correspondent 
had with the commander of the revolution, brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, on the 
occasion of the cultivation season at a project in Southwest Benghazi. 


[Begin recording] [Unidentified correspondent] Brother Compatriots: We are now with 
the leader of the great 1 September revolution at one of the production sites where he 

is personally directing the heavy machinery used to remove all buildings and walls which 
blocked the agricultural lands and prevented their exploitation. In this short interview, 
brothers, we will try to use the opportunity to meet the leader and to isk him to clarify 
some points for us pertaining this work and other important issues. 


The Arab summit conference is holding discussions in Baghdad. The entire Arab nation 
expected you to attend. We see, however, that you are here today and for the last few 
days, standing amid hundreds of peasants and urging them to work hard. What are the 
reasons behind your absence from the conference? 


[Al-Qadhdhafi] The conference is called a summit conference but we do not have a summit 
or a base. Though some countries still have a top and a bottom, we here are all at the 
summit. We do not have a president to attend a summit and a people on the bottom. The 
entire people are at the summit. Therefore any Libyan can do the job, particularly if 
he is the foreign secretary. Brig Abu Bakr Jabir Yunis, the commander in chief of the 
armed forces, went to participate in these meetings, which are called summit meetings.

That is one point. The other point is that the question under discussion at the 
conference in Iraq is that of financial aid to the confrontation states. Even if that 
was not decided upon and even if the summit was net held in Baghdad, the question is

and have expressed our pleasure at this rapprochement. 


As to the question of Egypt, it was proposed that it be given aid if it retreats from 

the steps it has taken. If everybody betrays and then says he will retreat, and we give 
him money, we will be setting a serious precedent. Then it becomes a matter of business; 
it is no longer a question of a nation, a sacred question and a question of struggle 
against the enemy. Besides, as far as we are concerned, we do not need to discuss this 
matter in Baghdad. We have finished dealing with the matter and have placed Egypt on the 
blacklist, for agreeing with the enemy. Any Arab country that deals with Egypt following 
the signing of the treaty with the enemy will also be placed on the blacklist and the

PALESTINIAN COUNCIL MEMBER ADDRESSES LOCAL ASSEMBLY 


LD051352Y Tripoli JANA in English 0805 GMT 5 Nov 78 LD 


[Textj Tajura, 5 Nov JANA--"Abu Mazin, member of the Palestine National Council, attended 
yesterday the discussions of the basic people's congress in Tajura. In his speech at the 
congress, 'Abu Mazin praised the part played by the people's congresses and committees as 
the true agency through which all the people could attain their rights and ensure their 
progress. He also mentioned the support, which the Jamahiriya gave to the confrontation 
countries, which continued to oppose colonial plans and solutions of surrender. 


‘Abu Mazin outlined the difficulties through which the Palestinian people were passing, 
and the Zionist persecution of the Arabs in the occupied land. He also stressed the 
necessity of increasing the solidarity with the Palestinians in view of the plots the 
reactionary imperialist forces and Zionism hatched against them. The Palestinians, he 
emphasized, knew no way other than the gun, by which they will regain their rights and 
their land.
