Summary (AI): A 1959 memorandum from Sherman Kent, Assistant Director of National Estimates, comments on Ambassador Jones' views about US-Libyan negotiations. It says US base position in Libya depends on the pro-Western King's survival, and that the US would likely be in trouble in trying to keep unrestricted use of Wheelus Field if the King were dissatisfied with aid terms.
• The memo estimates the stability of the US base position in Libya depended on the survival of the pro-Western King.
• Analysts said a successor regime would be at least as anti-US as the King, and probably considerably more so, especially if his fall were related to the issue of US bases and compensation levels.
• The memo advises that the question of US bases should be kept out of the Libyan public political arena.
