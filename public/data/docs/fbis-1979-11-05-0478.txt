LIBYA

RAUL CASTRO RUZ ARRIVES FOR OFFICIAL VISIT 
LD031909 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 3 Nov 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli--Raul Castro Ruz, commander of the army, second secretary of the Cuban 
Communist Party Central Committee, first vice president of the Councils of State and 
Ministers, and minister of the revolutionary armed forces, arrived in Tripoli this 
evening accompanied by Minister of Construction (Labib Habib) as well as two members of 
the party Politburo in the Republic of Cuba on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah. 


The Cuban guest was accorded an official welcome at Tripoli international airport. He 
was met by Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir and Maj al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi, and by the chiefs 
of staff of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, the Cuban ambassador to the Jamahiriyah and 
heads of the diplomatic missions in the Jamahiriyah. 


In a statement to a JANA correspondent, Castro expressed his pleasure at visiting the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, pointing out that he is bringing with him 
greetings from President Fidel Castro and the Cuban people to the Libyan Arab people and 
the leader of the revolution. In his statement the Cuban guest condemned the attempts 
and plots being hatched by colonialism and its collaborators to control the destinies 

of the peoples and exhaust their wealth. 


Vietnam's Giap Arrives 
LDO31930 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1750 GMT 3 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--Major General Giap, Vietnamese deputy premier and minister of defense, 
arrived in Tripoli this evening on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah. 


He was accorded an official reception at Tripoli internati nal airport, where he was met 
by Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir and Maj al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydji as well as by the chiefs 

of staff of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, several officers of the armed forces and by heads 
of the diplomatic missions accredited in the Jamahiriyah. 


In a statement to a JANA correspondent, the Vietnamese deputy premier and minister of 
defense expressed his great pleasure at visiting the Jamahiriyah and said he brings 
the greetings of the Vietnamese people and their president to the Libyan Arab people 
and the brother leader of the revolution, 


Major General Giap lauded the support and backing given by the socialist and African 
states, led by the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, to the Vietnamese people 
during their struggle against colonialism. He pointed out that the triumph of the 
Vietnamese people over French colonialism and American imperialism had been certain, 
because the Vietnamese people were struggling for a just cause. 


Major General Giap denounced the agreements of treason and disgrace signed by the 
vanquished Egyptian president at the 'Stable David! with the terrorist Begin with 

the blessings and under the supervision of the United States, the leader of the imperialist 
world, and described the act as a betrayal of the Arab nation.

He affirmed that all progressive forces in the world seek true peace based on justice, 
freedom and independence. He spoke of the struggle of the heroic Vietnamese people 
against French and American imperialism for the sake of freedom, peace and stability in 
the world--a struggle which lasted 30 years. 


Major General Giap pointed out that his visit to the Jamahiriyah is a clear indication 
of the friendship and trust that exists between the two countries. It is also definite 
proof of the support of the Vietnamese people, which stems from the fact that Vietnam 
and the Jamahiriyah are both fighting in one trench against the forces of evil and the 
enemies of man and freedom. 


Major General Giap reiterated his greetings and those of the Vietnamese people and 
government to the Libyan Arab people and the brother commander of the revolution, and said 
that the welcome and hospitality shown to him will further consolidate the ties between 
the two friendly peoples, who should work side by side to bolster this friendship.

Visit Former Airbase 
LDO42324 Tripoli JANA in English 1734 GMT 4 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, November 4, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS ACENCY--The Vietnamese vice premier and 
defence minister General Vo Nguyen Giap and Raul Castro, second secretary of the Come 
munist Party Central Committee, deputy leader of the Council of State and minister of 
the armed forces of the Republic of Cuba, this morning visited the 'Uqbah Bin Nafi 
Airbase, General Giap and Mr, Castro were received upon their arrival in the base by 
Brigadier Abu Bakr Yinis Jabir and the commanders of the Libyan Arab air, land, sea 
and air defence forces and a number of air force officers, 


The air force chief of staff welcomed General Giap and Mr, Castro and explained that 
during the former monarchical regime this base was used to threaten the security of 
the Libyan Arab people, Arab peoples, the people of the Mediterranean and Africa, 

But following the Great First of September Revolution and through the realization of 
the danger of such a base, the revolution responded to the people's will and evacuated 
the base within the first six months of its birth, 


The two guests, accompanied by Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, chiefs of staff and 
members of the Cuban and Vietnamese delegations, toured the base and listened to a 
detailed explanation of the function of the departments they visited, They also 
witnessed an air display carried out by Libyan Arab airforce officers, 


General Giap and Mr, Castro expressed admiration for what they saw during the visit 
and praised the high standards attained by the Libyan Arab airforce during the era 
of the revolution, They also lauded the efforts exerted to free 'Uqbah Bin Nafi base 
from the claws of American colonialism, 


At the end of the visit, the commander of the Libyan Arab airforce presented symbolic 
gifts to General Giap and Mr, Castro,
