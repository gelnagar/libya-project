LIBYA

QAFSAH UPRISING "BEGINNING OF END' OF TUNISIAN REGIME 
LD191044 Tripoli JANA in English 0955 GMT 19 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Paris, February 19, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY=-Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that the 
recent popular uprising of the Tunisian people is a purely internal revolution. He added: 
“Je challenge any proof, if only one, that Libya has anything to do with it, specifying 
that the Tunisian Government did know that it was facing an internal revolution, and so 
did France." 


In an interview with [words indistinct] daily "LIBERATION," he explained that the regime 
in the Tunisian protectorate decided to accuse a neighbouring country and hold it re- 
sponsible for the uprising because it realized it was not in its interest to recognize 
that it was an internal revolution. He continued that at the beginning the regime hesi- 
tated about whether to accuse Algeria or Libya but that King Hassan of Morocco advised 
him to accuse Libya. This is because the regime knew, he explained, that if it accused 
the Algerians, Libya would express its solidarity with them, which would be dangerous for 
the Moroccan regime itself. He went on to say that by accusing the Jamahiriyah the regime 
wanted to isolate Libya from Algeria by setting it against both Tunisia and France. In 
this respect he specified that the attempt to polarize and flatter Algeria aimed at 
creating differences between Algeria and Libya.

He went on to say that the king of Morocco is seeking presence east and West of Algeria 
so as to confront it while it cannot have Libyan support. These, he said, are the calcu- 
lations of Morocco to isolate Algeria. The leader of the revolution stressed that the 
popular revolution which started in Qafsah and spread to all the other cities and places 
of Tunisia is the beginning of the end of the ruling regime in the Tunisian protectorate. 


Asked whether the time has come for the Tunisian people to drop political struggle and 
start armed struggle, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi answered that it was up to the Tunisian people 
to decide whether or not to take up arms. He said that the Libyans will always take a 
stand with the Tunisian people. He said that the traditional opponents of the Tunisian 
regime have decided to change their mind and join the popular revolution. 


He continued: "Our opposition to the Tunisian regime has become a reality and will 
remain so." About the ruling regime's attempt to exploit the people's revolution to 
firmly establish its territorial policy under the so-called national unity, Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi said that the spirit of regionalism is no more than superficial bubbles 
covering the reactions of Arab unionists. He went on to say that whenever these bubbles 
increase, the feelings for Arab unity increase too. This is evident with the appearance 
of a new generation of unionists. 


He stated that when he visited Tunisia he was welcomed by unionist demonstrations in 
several Tunisian cities including Tunis, Nabil, Safagqis, (Kerouan) and Qafsah. In this 
respect he specified: "I actually met a new generation of unionists who wrote unionist 
Slogans on slates so as to erase them afterwards lest should the police see them." 


Concerning the French invasion of Tunisia, he said: "This led us to realize, for the 
first time, that there could be a wide-ranging confrontation which is likely to spread 
to other places of North Africa, maybe to all Africa the Middle East and Far East, and 
we will act to spread it to other countries." 


He warned that: "If the situation deteriorated we will use all our arms in this confron- 
tation, adding: "We have fought colonialism in Africa, and in the Arab world at a time 
when we were in a bad situation. So what about today when we possess energy and means 

of pressure on the world?" 


IRANIAN AMBASSADOR RECEIVED BY FOREIGN SECRETARY 
LD161952 Tripoli JANA in English 1808 GMT 16 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Feb 16, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The foreign secretary, Dr. ‘Ali at-Turayki, 
today received the Iranian ambassador accredited to Kuwait, Dr. 'Ali Shams Ardakani, and 

the delegation accompanying him. Their discussion covered the consolidation of bilateral 
relations and the unifying of efforts in the service of Muslim causes. 


The Iranian ambassador praised the solidarity the grand al-Fatah revolution has shown 
towards the Iranian Revolution and its positive stand regarding the ousted shah and his 
repressive measures against the Iranian people. Dr. Ardakani is currently visiting the 
Jamahiriyah to finalise preparations for the opening of an embassy of the Irangan Islamic 
Republic in Libya.

FOREIGN SECRETARIAT APPROVES PAN-ARAB CHARTER 
LD181100 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 GMT 18 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 February--The Libyan Foreign Secretariat has expressed Libya's 
Satisfaction at the pan-Arab charter announced by Iraq. The charter rejects the 
presence of any foreign military forces or armies in the Arab homeland or the granting 
to them of facilities in any form or under any pretext, It also provides for checking 
any aggression or violation by foreign party of the sovereignty of any Arab country
