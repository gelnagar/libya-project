LIBYA

JANA REPORTS REAGAN'S POST-ELECTION PRESS CONFERENCE 
LD081306 Tripoli JANA in English 0815 GMT 8 Nov 80 


[Text] Los Angeles, 8 Nov (JANA)--The U.S. President-elect Ronald Reagan said his 
first statements characterized by intransigence at a press conference in Los Angeles 
and announced that he considered Palestine Liberation Organization as a terrorist 
group. He also touched upon the issue of American hostages and Iran's stand and warned 
the Iranian authorities from extending the period of keeping the hostages in Iran. 


The new U.S. president touched upon American relations with the Soviet Union and in 
particular the talks on limitation of arms, and said that the future of these talks 
will depend on new conditions. 


MORE EMBASSIES CONVERTED INTO PEOPLE'S, BROTHERHOOD BUREAUS 

Panama People's Bureau 
LDO72322 Tripoli JANA in English 1906 GMT 7 Nov 80 
[Text] Tripoli, 7 Nov (JANA)--As a way to escalate the people's revolution, and as a 
confirmation that genuine relations among people are achieved directly and not through 
ambassadors and envoys, the Libyan Arab masses in Panama, Central America, gathered 


and marched this morning on the Libyan Embassy in Panama, and transformed it into a 
People's Bureau and formed a people's committee to run it. 


The members of the people's committee of the Libyan Arab People's Bureau, met with the 


press in Panama who attended the march, and expounded to them at length the direct 
popular democracy being adopted and put into practice by the Libyan Arab people through 
their people's congresses and people's committees. 


Aden Brotherhood Bureau 
LDO91534 Aden Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 9 Nov 80 EA 


[Text] A press conference was held this afternoon at the building of the Libyan Arab 
brotherhood bureau in Aden. It was attended by a number of local news media 
representatives and Arab and foreign diplomats accredited to Aden. 


The brother officials of the bureau explained what had motivated them to convert the 
Libyan Embassy in Aden into a Libyan Arab brotherhood bureau and said that other Libyan 
embassies were also being converted into Libyan Arab brotherhood bureaus. 


A statement issued by the popular committee of the Libyan Arab brotherhood bureau in 
Aden said that the conversion was part of the escalation of the popular revolution 
being waged by the fraternal Libyan Arab people to give expression to popular autho- 
rity. The statement said that in affirmation of the fraternal bonds between the 
fraternal peoples of Democratic Yemen and Libya, a popular committee had been estab- 
lished to manage the bureau so as to promote the ties of brotherhood between the two 
peoples. 


It will be recalled that the Democratic Yemeni Foreign Ministry last Thursday received 
a memorandum from the popular committee of the Libyan Arab brotherhood bureau in Aden, 
pointing out that the embassy had been converted into a Libyan Arab brotherhood bureau 
on the basis of experience gained by the Libyan people at home and abroad. 


V. 10 Nov 80 NORTH AFRICA 


RALLY SUPPORTS AL-QADHDHAFI ON TRANSFORMING SCHOOLS 
LD091924 Tripoli JANA in English 1833 GMT 9 Nov 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 9 Nov, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--A huge student procession was staged 
in Tripoli today supporting the leader's speech to secondary schools, colleges and the 
Sabha colleges of education. The leader's speech referred to transforming schools and 
educational institutes into military camps for preparing students to fend off all 
imperialist and reactionary plots directed against the honour and national pride of the 
Arab nation. 


The students chanted slogans calling for total mobilization, and for transforming 
educational establishments into military training camps. The slogans denounced the 
imperialist-reactionary moves taken by some Arab rulers in making their countries 
imperialist bases for America, the enemy of all Arabs. 


The students issued a statement asserting their adherence to the leader's speech and 
denouncing reactionary attempts and imperialist domination to which the Arab nation 
has been exposed with the aim of bringing the colonisers back again to halt Arab unity. 
The statement added that the student masses are intent on continuing efforts for the 
much aspired Arab unity and for speeding up the day of final liberation from all forms 
of repressive, unjust dictatorial regimes to which the Arab citizen is subjected today, 
and the time when power, wealth and weapons will be in the hands of every Arab. 


The students re-affirmed that they will be the warriors of true Islam, the revolution 
which stands against humiliation, surrender and capitulation. They will call for jihad 
in the name of God and come into direct open confrontation with those who have taken 
the faith of American Islam and others who still see things with eyes of the Middle 
Ages using religion as a cover. The statement concluded by asserting the belief of 

the student movement in the historic humanitarian dicta heralded by the third universal 
theory, and their commitment to advocate its causes.
