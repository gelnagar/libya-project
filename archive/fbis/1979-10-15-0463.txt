JOINT PALESTINIAN-JORDANIAN DELEGATION ARRIVES 
LD130140 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 12 Oct 79 LD 


[Excerpt] A joint Palestinian-Jordanian delegation arrived in Tripoli yesterday evening. 
The delegation has been sent by the Jordanian-Palestinian committee in support of the 
Palestinian people's steadfastness on usurped soil. The delegation includes Jordanian 
Information Minister "Adnan Abu *Awdah; Abu Mazin, member of the Fatah movement Central 
Committee; and Shawkat Muhammad, director of the department of usurped soil affairs.
