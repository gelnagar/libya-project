LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Sends Message to UN on Grenada 
LD252237 Tripoli JANA in English 1922 GMT 25 Oct 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Muharram 19, Oct 25, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY Colonel] Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 
Great Ist of September Revolution, sent an urgent message to 
the chairman and members of the UN Security Council, UN 
secretary general, the current chairman of the UN General 
Assembly, and heads of state of the world on the armed invasion 
staged by the United States and six other countries against 
Grenada. It reads as follows 


The current events in Grenada have killed any hope of the smaller 
nations like Grenada to live free on this earth. What is happening 
now in Grenada did not kill the freedom of the people of Grenada 
only but also the civilisation of the 20th century and exposed it 
as fraud 


The arrival of people like Reagan to power in an aggressive 
country like the USA, is an indicator to the defeat of humanity 
and a retreat to the era of barbarism, law of the jungle, and the 
illogical. Nothing can rescue humanity and civilisation which are 
now being buried but an international alliance to invade the 
United States itself and impose the principles of humanity and 
freedom and justice as well as purge the spirit of evil and Nazism 
in the United States which has become a threat to the freedom 
of all the smaller nations and world peace 


There are no terms available in all the languages of the world 
that can describe this intervention. It is the most critical 
precedents and violations [as received] of both sovereignty and 
internal affairs. To bear the horror of a third world war is much 
easier than to bear the responsibility of an ugly violation and 
international madness against what is left of human values which 
are being slaughtered today in the tiny island of Grenada as they 
are being slaughtered in Palestine, Lebanon, Namibia, Ireland, 
and Azania [Southern Africa] and tomorrow comes the turn of 
the rest of the helpless peaceful peoples such as the people of 
Grenada. 


The international community with all its organisations, institu- 
tions, and material and moral resources, should make a move and 
take an action that rescues the island of Grenada and repulse the 
barbaric military invasion staged by the United States and some 
antifreedom and fascist governments of Latin American states 
Let the new Hitler of the world understand that in the end, Hitler 
had fallen to his actions and the people were victorious. Death to 
the new power-obsessed Naziists, glory to the people of Grenada, 
the sacred victim, and victory to the people. 


Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi
