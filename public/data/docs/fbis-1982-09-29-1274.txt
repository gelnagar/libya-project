LIBYA

PEOPLE'S CONGRESS LAUDS JAMAL ‘ABD AN-NASIR 


LD290030 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 28 Sep 82 


[Text] The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress has issued a statement 

to mark the anniversary of the martyrdom of the Arab leader Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir. The 
statement affirms that Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir devoted his life to the battles of liberation, 
independence, unity and the Palestinian Revolution for 18 years. The secret of Jamal ‘Abd 
an-Nasir's (?greatness) was in his awareness of the (?basic) facts of the revolutionary 
struggle. In the middle of the siege of Al-Falujah he realized that the defeat of the 
Arab armies before the Zionist gangs was not due to the strength of these gangs and the 
weakness of the Arab armies but to the betrayals and the collusions of the Arab reaction- 
ary regimes and their connections with U.S. imperialism, which was planning to impose 
domination over the Arab homeland. 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress called on the Arab masses to 
have self-confidence and confidence in the continuation of the struggle. It demanded 
from the Arab masses, in conclusion, that they take charge of their own affairs and 
shake the foundations of the thrones of underdevelopment, subservience and (?impotence), 
through working in accordance with the pan-Arab revolutionary and liberation struggle, 
whose outlines were drawn up by Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir.
