LIBYA

ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS MEETINGS BEGIN 17 NOV 


LD181544 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 18 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Nov (JANA) -- The meetings of the Permament Secretariat of the 
Arab People's Congress began last night. 


The General secretary of the Permament Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 
delivered.a speech at the opening meeting. He stressed that the progressive forces in 
the Arab homeland had entered a decisive battle against the United States, the Zionist 
enemy and Arab reaction. He pointed out that the Arab nation is now going through its 
most dangerous stages, especially after America entered into a strategic alliance with 
the Zionist enemy and Arab reaction. The general secretary urged the progressive and 
patriotic forces in the Arab homeland to move rapidly to challenge all plots and plans 
being hatched by world imperialism in cooperation with reactionary regimes. 


The participants at the meeting condemned Fahd's plan and described it as an attempt to 
sell out the Arab cause to the enemy of the Arab nation. It also is considered a 
relinquishment of the legitimate ri~.ts of the Palestinian Arab people. 


The secretariat will study the results of the emergency meeting held by the secretariat 
last October after the death sentence carried out on the traitor As-Sadat; will prepare 
the emergency session of the Arab People's Congress to be held in Tripoli this Thursday; 
will prepare the Arab-African conference and ensure its success; and will review the 
political situation in the Arab and international spheres. 


Message to Aden Meeting 


LD182058 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GYT 18 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Nov (JANA) -- The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 
sent a message to the foreign ministers of the Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation, 
who began their meetings this morning in Aden. The message said that the hope we attach 
to the pan-Arab Steadfastness and Confrontation Front has prompted us to address the 
following statement: 


The execution of the death sentence on As-Sadat crowned the campaigns of mobilization and 
the national and pan-Arab struggle both inside and outside Egypt against the policies of 
As-Sadat and the Stable David agreements. During this period, we must use the oppor- 
tunity to follow up our efforts and concentrate them on the complete overthrow of the 
Stable David agreements and the destruction of all its effects inside and outside Egypt. 


We believe that the resumption of normal relations between Egypt and the other Arab 
countries must be based on two ideological conditions: 


1. The complete overthrow of the Stable David agreements and the final elimination of 
all its effects inside and outside Egypt. 


2. The reintroduction of the democratic freedoms in Egypt, the release of political and 
trade unionist prisoners, the removal of (?pressure) from the Egyptian National Movement 
so that it can work and create a seafeguard for national politics in Egypt, and the 
refraining from (?infringing) on them in the future.

At this moment in particular, the National Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation has 
an historical responsibility to eliminate any opportunity for plots by the U.S. adminis- 
tration and Arab reaction aimed at consolidating their gains won from the Stable David 
agreements and at substituting plans which would lead to more disunity inside the Arab 
homeland and among the Palestinian people themselves. 


Within that context, the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress believes 
that Saudi Crown Prince Fahd's "initiative" is a project for adopting American plans at 
the expense of our Arab nation and the Palestinian issue. We believe that the main duty 
of the pan-Arab Steadfastness and Confrontation Front now is to confront that project 
and its political background and hoped for objectives and in particular to confront the 
project at the Arab summit conference which will be held in Fes in the next few days. 


The attempts to impose that project at the official Arab level through the Fes summit 
will complicate matters very much in the Arab homeland and create massive confusion at 
the official and popular levels. We, for our part, as the Permanent Secretariat of the 
Arab People's Congress, will send a high-level delegation to Fes during the conference to 
communicate with all the Arab delegations and to express our open views on the project. 


It is natural that we request your help with that move as partners in the national 
struggle. At this very critical juncture for our Arab nation and our Arab region, we 
believe that the only option is to unite our efforts as an Arab liberation movement in 
order to confront these dangers and to stand fast in the face of our enemies and their 
plans. We are looking to your meeting with great hope.

TRIPOLI CRITICIZES U.S. POLICY TOWARD LIBYA 


LD181448 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 18 Nov 81 


[Text] U.S. State Department spokesman Dean Fischer said the government of the American 
President regrets, according to him, the lawless behavior of the Jamahiriyah which he 
claims includes international terrorism and subversion against other governments. Fischer 
said America is studying the possible boycott of Libyan oil and this subject is among 

the options being studied within the framework of reviewing Washington's policy toward 
Libya. 


V. 19 Nov 81 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


Official American insinuations have come within the framework of the series of claims 

and falsifications aimed at attaching a label of terrorism against the Jamahiriyah and 

at intensifying misleading information campaigns against it. Without any doubt, the 
hostile American move against Libya, which is taking numerous forms and is being manipu- 
lated by various means, is dependent on a display of force and on carrying out provoca- 
tive actions and can only be considered international terrorism deliberately exercised by 
the American administration and can only be considered the cornerstone of the American 
policy pursued by the American President, Reagan, which fundamentally aims at worsening 
the situation in the region. 


The method pursued by the American administration toward the Jamahiriyah -- attaching to 
it the label of terrorism -- is one of the vilest methods now taking a prominent place 
in American policy. 


The Jamahiriyah has been holding and will continue to hold aloft its humane principles, 
will amass all its resources in the service of human aims and will take its natural place 
in supporting the cause of right, justice and peace and in backing the oppressed and the 
downt rodden. 


The essence of the statements made by the American spokesman is an example of the twisted 
logic with which America deals with peoples. Referring to the possibility of imposing a 
ban on Libyan oil clearly reflects this bad logic in international relations. As for 
Libyan oil, America does not have the power of decision over it for it is the property of 
the Libyan people and is available for the service of every national and pan-Arab aim.

[Excerpts] The current military maneuvers near the Jamahiriyah's border reveal the ugly 
face of the colonialist U.S. policy. This policy is dictated by strategic and economic 
interests that have no connection with the peoples of the region or their interests. This 
policy proceeds in the first place to establish dependency, impose control and spread 
imperialist influence over the map of the Arab homeland. 


The U.S. administration wants through maneuvers in Egypt to establish a permanent military 
presence in the region which will terrorize the Arabs and deter [words indistinct] with 
the aim of preventing the hateful stand toward U.S. policy from reaching its objective. 

In other words, U.S. policy vis-a-vis the Arab homeland wants to achieve two main objec- 
tives after which the United States would become [word indistinct] on the Arab homeland's 
map and the director of its destiny, if a destiny remained. 


The first objective is to safeguard the current and future supply of Arab oil and to 
strike at any popular or even [words indistinct] which might threaten these supplies. The 
second objective is the liquidation of the Palestinian issue. 


The question facing the Arab nation now that the Arab summit conference is so near is: 
What is the Arab stand toward the U.S. disregard for the Arab nation and the U.S. defiance 
toward its basic existence. The Arab summit's reply has almost been announced, that is, 
submission to the will of America and surrender to its domination in the shape of the 
explicit or tacit agreement to the escapist and capitulatory Fahd project. However, there 
remains the reply of the Arab nation and its active forces which is not just a demon- 
stration of denunciation and condemnation.

ARAB SOLIDARITY STATEMENT DENOUNCES U.S. THREATS 


LD181000 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 18 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Nov (JANA) -- The delegation of the international secretariat for 
solidarity with the Arab people and its central cause, Palestine, yesterday issued a 
statement. It says: 


Under the conditions of direct and continuous American military plots and threats against 
the people of the Jamahiriyah and their revolutionary leadership, represented in its most 
dangerous form by the extensive American military maneuvers now taking place along the 
borders of the Jamahiriyah and in the Mediterranean; 


To implement the resolution of the international conference for solidarity with the people 
of the Jamahiriyah held in Tripoli from the 28 to 31 August, 1981; 


To implement the resolution of the international secretarat for solidarity with the Arab 
people and their central cause, Palestine; 


The delegation of the international secretariat for solidarity with the Arab people and 
its central cause, Palestine, met and decided the following: 


To convey the solidarity of all anti-imperialist democratic forces in the world parties 
organizations, trade union, vocational and political bodies and liberation movements, with 
the people of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in the face of American 
intrigue and threats; 


To convey the appreciation of these forces for the progressive and liberation role played 
by the revolution of 1 September under the leadership of Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
against imperialism, Zionism and reaction, and to express their approval for the material 
and political assistance it extends in support of the liberation movements and the peoples 
struggling in the world; 


To convey the denunciation and condemnation by all democratic and peace-loving quarters 

in the world for the American military maneuvers currently taking place in the region, 
which are directed against progressive Arab regimes and forces, in particular the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


The delegation expresses its high esteem for the struggling role played by the Jamahiriyah 
in supporting the Arab liberation movement and in particular supporting the PLO. 


The delegation expresses its profound appreciation for the humane role played by the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah to establish peace in Chad, the role which 
actually was proved -- after the withdrawal of Libyan forces from Chad in agreement with 
the legitimate government in Chad -- to be honorable without any personal design, contrary 
to the claims of the imperialist information media. 


V. 19 Nov 81 Q5 NORTH AFRICA 


Viewpoints were exchanged at the joint meeting with the Libyan brethren concerning the 
implementation of the resolution adopted by the international conference for solidarity 
with the people of the Jamahiriyah with regard to making the Jamahiriyah the international 
center to resist imperialism, Zionism, reaction and racism. Agreement on a proposal was 
reached to put this resolution into force. 


Agreement was also reached on the necessity of carrying out a world information campaign 
in which all democratic and peace-loving forces in the world take part to confront the 
imperialist campaigns of lies and propaganda directed against the Jamahiriyah and its 
revolutionary leadership. 


The delegation of the international secretariat decided to send cables of solidarity with 
the people of the Jamahiriyah, condemning and denouncing the imperialist plans and American 
military threats against the Jamahiriyah and to demand the immediate withdrawal of American 
military forces from the Arab region. Cables were sent to the following: the general 
secretary of the United Nations, members of the Security Council, the nonaligned states 
group, the OAU, the league of Arab states, and to the Islamic Conference. 


The delegation also decided to send a strongly worded message of protest to the American 
administration, condemning the aggressive American policies and the continuous threats 
against the Jamahiriyah, also asking it to listen to world public opinion and all the 
democratic and peace-loving forces which stand with the Jamahiriyah and which condemn the 
American policies hostile to the peoples' freedom and world peace. 


JANA REPORTS KHARTOUM UNIVERSITY ‘UNDER SEIGE' 


LD181842 Tripoli JANA in English 1750 GMT 18 Nov 81 


[Text] Khartoum, 18 Nov (JANA) -- The University of Khartoum is still under siege by 

the police since last Monday following the clashes between the students and the police. 

A large number of students who took part in the demonstrations, were reported to have been 
arrested. 


Diplomatic sources said that the situation is deteriorating and that dramatic incidents 
will be witnessed in the country in the near future, especially the inevitable full of 

the regime. The same sources said that the popular uprising which spread at a wide-scale 
in the country is only the preparation for a real revolutionary uprising which will result 
in the overthrow of the regime.
