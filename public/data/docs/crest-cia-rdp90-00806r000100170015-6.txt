Summary (AI): A 1981 New York Times article, reproduced in a CIA reading-room copy, reports that American pilots and mechanics were recruited to fly and maintain aircraft for the Libyan Air Force. It links the recruitment to former CIA and Naval Intelligence officer Edwin Wilson, whose network was tied to explosives shipments to Libya.
• The Libyan Air Force reportedly had eight C-130 transports, Chinook helicopters, 10 727s, nine C-47s and one 707.
• Justice Department officials said involvement of Americans in Libya does not appear to violate American law, since neutrality laws apparently do not cover this.
• Wilson later shifted operations to the London office of Brithurst Ltd., a British company.
• Recruiting reportedly involved American pilots and mechanics; the article says it was unofficial.
• After the assassination of Sadat in October 1981, the Reagan administration sent two AWACS aircraft to Egypt to monitor Libyan air activity.
