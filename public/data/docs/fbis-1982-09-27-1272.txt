LIBYA

TRIPOLI RADIO ON U.S.-MOROCCAN ACTIVITIES 


LD251324 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 25 Sep 82 


[Text] AFRIQUE-ASIE published yesterday discloses that Hassan II, king of Morocco, handed 
the full text of the Fes summit reports to an adviser of the U.S. intelligence service in 
Rabat. The periodical states that Hassan II personally pledged to Reagan, during the 
visit to Washington, to strengthen relations -- which were not at all severed -- between 
the Moroccan regime and the U.S. intelligence center. The periodical points out that 

many members of the U.S. intelligence service and the Zionist intelligence service, Mossad, 
have visited Rabat since that time.
