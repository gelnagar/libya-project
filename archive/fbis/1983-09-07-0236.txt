Mauritania’s Haydala at Festivities in Tripoli 
ABO21555 Nouakchott Domestic Service in French 1430 GMT 
2 Sep 83 


[Text] Lieutenant Colonel Mohamed Khouna Ould Haydala, the 
chairman of the Military Council for National Salvation and 
head of state, yesterday afternoon attended a parade mounted by 
the various units of the Libyan Army in Tripoli. The parade was 
organized within the framework of the festivities marking the 
14th anniversary of the | September Revolution. 


The presence of the head of state at the various ceremonial 
activities testifies to the friendship and solidarity between the two 
brotherly people. Moreover, this solidarity was cemented by the 
participation in the festivities of our special force commandos of 
the 3d Military Region led by its commander. The red helmets 
were impressive and enriched the parade with the order and 
discipline they demonstrated. [as heard] 


Apart from these festivities, the head of state on Wednesday 
evening held talks with the Malagasy president who also came to 
attend the celebrations. The internalization of the Chadian con- 
flict and the Western Saharan question in light of the discussions 
at the last summit in Addis Ababa were discussed by the two 
heads of state.

[Text] Tripoli, 3 Sep (SANA)— President Didier Ratsiraka of

the Libyan Arab people of the 14th anniversary of the Great

Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud was at the front of 
those who saw President Didier Ratsiraka off at Tripoli interna-
