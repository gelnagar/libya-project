LIBYA

OFFICIALS HOLD MEETINGS WITH FRANCE'S CHEYSSON 


Al-'Ubaydi Dinner 
LD011026 Tripoli JANA in English 0920 GMT 1 Jun 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 1 Jun (JANA) -- The secretary of the People's Foreign Liaison Bureau, "Abd 
al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, last night held a dinner banquet in honour of Claude Cheysson, the 
French foreign minister, who is currently visiting the jamahiriyah. The banquet was 
attended by a number of secretaries and members of the diplomatic corps accredited to the 
jamahiriyah. 


Jallud Reception 
LD011418 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 1 Jun 82 


[Text] Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud this morning received Claude Cheysson, French 
external relations minister, and his accompanying delegation. The meeting was attended by 
the secretary of the People's Foreign Liaison Bureau and the secretary of the Libyan 
People's Bureau in France. 


Al-'Ubaydi, Cheysson Speeches 
LD011434 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 1 Jun 82 


[Text] Libyan-French talks continued this morning. A plenary session was held between 
"Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the people's committee for the People's Bureau for 
Foreign Liaison, and Claude Cheysson, French external relations minister. 


‘Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi delibered a speech at the start of this session, in which he 
expressed his hope for the development of bilateral relations and the strengthening of 
bilateral cooperation. He stressed the need for security and stability in the Mediterranean 
basin and for the sea to be a region of peace and security free of foreign bases. He 
explained the Jamahiriyah feels its neutrality is being threatened as long as bases exist 
along the shores of the Mediterranean, which are being used to intimidate, threaten and 
aggress against the Libyan Arab people. He said bases and missiles in countries the 
jamahiriyah threaten our security and the security of all countries on the Mediterranean 
Sea. He also said our anxiety is heightened and the correctness of our course confirmed 
by the American aggression against our territorial waters in the Gulf of Sidra, which 
sprang from bases in countries in the Mediterranean basin. Hence, the neutrality of 
countries is threatened as long as major powers exploit bases in the Mediterranean. 


Regarding Africa, he pointed out that the jamahiriyah considers the OAU to be the framework 
defining relations of cooperation among African states and affirming the freedom of Africans. 
Accordingly, we stress to all our friends that we in the jamahiriyah do not accept that 
relations among African states can be constituted through any framework other than that of 
the OAU. The jamahiriyah will resist the attempt of any world power to impose its 
guardianship on African relations. There are no reservations about relations on mutual 
respect. 


Regarding the Palestinians, the secretary of the people's committee for the People's 
Bureau for Foreign Liaison stressed that the jamahiriyah considers the right to self- 
determination and the establishment of a Palestinian state led by the PLO to be the 
basic issue in resolving this question.

Any initiative put forward and [word indistinct] the posture of the Palestinian revolution, 
the posture of Syria and the posture of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front will not 
succeed in any way. He stressed the need to halt all assistance to and backing of South 
Africa and the need to back the struggle of the people of South Africa. 


Regarding bilateral relations, he stressed the jamahiriyah is prepared to bolster existing 
cooperation and this cooperation must cover cultural, technical, economic and all other 
spheres. 


Claude Cheysson delivered a speech in which he expressed France's complete readiness to 
develop its relations with the jamahiriyah and to bolster without restrictions all coopera- 
tion. He said the French Government is encouraging French companies and organizations to 
strengthen their relations with the jamahiriyah. He said the best proof of this is that 
when we were asked to evacuate our citizens from the jamahiriyah, we refused. Instead 
France encouraged the strengthening of cooperation and did not put any travel restrictions 
on its citizens to or from the jamahiriyah. 


The French external relations minister said French-Libyan relations went through a certain 
phase as a result of differences of opinion on many African issues. I admit this and now 
regret it. It will not be repeated in the future because we will have direct discussion of 
issues that concern us. He stressed France unreservedly supports the right to self-deter- 
mination of the Palestinian people in establishing a state on their soil and is prepared to 
develop its relations with the PLO, which it considers the legitimate representative of the 
Palestinian people. 


Cheysson Departure 
LD011832 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1805 GMT 1 Jun 82 
[Text] Tripoli, 1 Jun (JANA) -- French External Relations Minister Claude Cheysson left 
Tripoli this evening at the end of his brief visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jahamiriyah, during which he held talks aimed at bolstering relations between the two 


countries. 


The talks covered stability and security of the Mediterranean basin, both countries’ policies 
on Africa, cooperation in all fields and world issues of common interest. 


HABRE FORCES CLAIM CAPTURE OF CHADIAN TOWN 


LD282038 Tripoli JANA in English 1746 GMT 28 May 82 


[Text] Paris, 28 May (JANA) -- The representative of the Northern Forces of Hissein Habre 
in Europe announced that Habre forces yesterday captured Massakory, 46 km north east of 
Ndjamena. 


In a statement published in Paris, he said Habre's forces are able to take over Ndjamena, 
it is not a military problem. He added Habre's forces will soon take over Doujia [as 
received] and Massaguet villages, 80 km from the capital. 


AL-QADHDHAFI GREETS ARGENTINA'S GALTIERI 


LD281118 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 26 May 82 


["Text" of telegram from Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to Argentine President Leopoldo Galtieri] 


[Text] On the occasion of the Argentine people's National Day, on which Argentina achieved 
its independence and its own identity and severed its subvervience to the colonialist king- 
doms of Europe, we are honored to congratulate it on the anniversary of this day and to 
laud the struggle of the great Argentine people to reaffirm freedom and sovereignty.

We are convinced that this great people once again will triumph over the forces of British 
colonialism that moved from the most remote northern point of the globe to impose obsolete 
colonialist concepts on the southern hemisphere and to encroach upon territorial lands and 
waters to which Britain has no right whatsoever and which belong to the Argentine people 
from the point of view of extension of territory, proximity and legitimate 

rights, in accordance with the values of logic, equity and civilization. 


We also express our full support for the Argentine people in their struggle and loudly 
declare that we side with them in their struggle and just cause against a barbaric invasion 
that revives an outdated, disfigured, deformed image of defaced, ugly colonialism with 

its armadas and pirates, that senselessly and irresponsibly lays open to danger the 
security of the peoples of Latin America and poses a threat to world peace, just for the 
sake of spurious illusions of a pseudosovereignty, reminiscences of glorious men of bygone 
piracy, that ended and disappeared with the end and sunset of the empire forever and until 
eternity. 


I greet the heroic struggle waged by the Argentine people and its army with bravery and 
steadfastness and we greet the martyrs fallen in defense of the land, waters and skies of 
the homeland against the barbarism of the 20th century. 


[Signed] Col Mu‘ammar Al-Qadhdhafi
