JALLUD DISCUSSES LEBANON WITH SOVIET ENVOY 


LD100900 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0846 GMT 10 Jun 82 


{Text] Tripoli, 10 Jun (JANA) -- Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud last night received 
the Soviet ambassador to the jamahiriyah. 


During the meeting, Staff Major Jallud pointed out to the Soviet ambassador the dimensions 
of the U.S. imperialist-Zionist-reactionary conspiracy and the dangers of the Zionist 
invasion of Lebanon and its clear aims -- to liquidate the Palestinian people and put an 
end to Lebanon and to obstruct the growing role of the progressive states in resisting the 
imperialist attack on the Arab homeland and the dangers this poses to world peace.

EUROPEAN ENVOYS SUMMONED ON LEBANON CRISIS 


LD091526 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 9 Jun 82 


[Text] The People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison this morning summoned the West European 
ambassadors in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and informed them of the 
dimensions of the grave plot being hatched against Lebanon and the Palestinian Revolution 
through the barbaric Zionist aggression against Lebanese territory. The secretary of the 
people's committee for the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison stressed that this plot 
falls within the framework of the large-scale aggressive plan carried out by the Zionist 
gangs backed by the American administration to liquidate the Palestinians and finish off 
their cause and to work for the withdrawal of the Syrian forces from Lebanon in order 

to establish a regime in Lebanon that would work for the «ims and designs of the Zionists. 


The brother secretary of the people's committee for the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison 
urged the countries of West Europe to adopt firm positions against the treacherous Zionist 
invasion aimed at the Palestinian Revolution and the Lebanese people. He asked the ambas- 
sadors to inform their governmments of the dimensions of this grave plot and the barbaric 
Zionist assault against southern Lebanon. He stressed the need for all countries to rally 
to defend the principles and charter of the United Nations.

The People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison this morning also summoned the ambassadors of 
the East European countries. They were informed of the gravity of the situation result- 
ing from the barbaric Zionist invasion of Lebanon, which aims to destroy the Palestinian 
Revolution and the Lebanese people. The brother secretary of the people's committee for 
the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison urged the ambassadors of East European countries 
to consider the gravity of what is happening in Lebanon. He stressed that the Zionist 
invasion is aimed primarily at totally annihilating the Palestinian Revolution and 
exposing to danger the freedom, security and independence of Lebanon. He informed the 
ambassadors that under the present circumstances the Palestinian Revolution needs not 
only political support but also urgent military support from its friends in East Europe 
in order to face the treacherous Zionist invasion. 


JANA EDITOR ON ISRAELI ACTIONS, U.S. VETO 


LD091726 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 9 Jun 82 


[Text] A JANA political editor comments on the racist Zionist invasion of the Lebanese 
Arab territory to wipe out the Arab Lebanese and Palestinian peoples: 


The decision by the U.S. administration to use its veto at the Security Council, to 
block a resolution calling for a cease-fire in Lebanon in the war of annihilation being 
waged by the racist Zionists against the Palestinian Revolution ard the sovereignty 

of the Lebanese territory and people [words indistinct]. This American stand clearly 
indicates: 


First, the Reagan administration has come to consider the armed invasion of the territory 
of a sovereign state -- a member of the United Nations -- as a legitimate act, an act 

the condemnation of which it prevented. Moreover, the United States gave the invasion 
its stamp of legality. In the case of the barbaric Zionist invasion now taking palce 
against the Lebanese and Palestinian people, the United States is considered a full 


partner in organizing, planning, preparing and executing the operation. We can clearly 
say that the one who is waging the current invasion against Lebanon and the Palestinian 
Revolution is the U.S. administration itself in alliance with the Zionists, occupiers of 
Palestinian land. They are acting with U.S. aid, U.S. weapons and with international 
protection from the United States. 


Second, this invasion is part of a joint U.S. plot, the parties to which are the United 
States, the Israelis and the Arabs in the United States. It is the third stage of an 
American strategy aimed at capturing the Arab homeland, dominating Arab territory through 
the deployment of American military bases, controlling the sources of Arab oil and 
seizing the reins of Arab political decisionmaking. The first stage in this hostile 
strategy, the strategy of containment and bringing the Arab homeland under the U.S. 
umbrella, was the policy of small steps by which the Zionist Kissinger guided the 
executed As-Sadat to Camp David and the signing of the capitulation agreement. The 
second stage was to turn Arab oil from an Arab potential into an instrument used by the 
United States against the Arabs. This second stage was carried out by the agent Saudi 
family who reduced the prices of oil and increased the production levels. In turn, this 
Saudi oil fueled Zionist aircraft and tanks which burned and destroyed everything facing 
them. The second stage was also the so-called Fahd plan, the attempt to bring Egypt 
back into Arab ranks and the U.S.-Saudi military agreement. The purpose of the second 
stage was to complete the U.S.-Zionist strategic alliance using the Arabs in the United 
States.

As for the third stage, it seems the total annihilation of the Palestinian Revolution 
and to escalate the psychological, political and economic pressure on the will of the 
Arab Resistance expressed by the Arab revolutionary forces and the progressive forces 
the region, to give an opportunity to the dishonorable and spineless Arabs in America 
continue to live although they are going against the tide of history. 


Third, the U.S.-Zionist aggression has prevented the rising momentum of the Iranian 
revolution against Saddam's defeated regime -- the agent regimes in the region realize 
that its downfall means the start of their downfall. 


Now we ask the questions which should be asked especially at this time. America's 
allies, America's friends, the Arabs in America, those who boast about Arab solidarity, 
those who march behind the American illusion, what is left of them? What is left for 
them other than disgrace, shame, humiliation and insults? Wasn't it their ally who 
arranged and agreed to the aggression taking place against the Palestinian people and 
the Lebanese people? Where did their calls for the support of the cause and the libera- 
tion of Jerusalem go? Where are the illusionary plans, Fahd's plans, or the plans of 
international legality in facing invasion, killing and annihilation of the elderly, 
women and children from the Palestinian and Lebanese peoples? All of them are Arabs. 
Arabs in America are deaf and dumb, trembling and cowardly, and will be swept by the 
rising tide of the revolution in the direction of affirming the Arab challenge to the 
enemy. This popular resistance confronting the enemy is the largest evidence that the 
will for freedom will remain over Arab territory and that America's plans, America's 
decisions, the [word indistinct] by Arabs in America will be destroyed under the feet 
of the free will of the Arab masses -- makers of victory and revolution.
