LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi, Junblatt Have ‘Telephone Contact’ 
LD090735 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 9 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli 9 Feb (JANA) There has been a telephone 
contact between the brother leader of the revolution and Brother 
Walid Junblatt, chairman of the Lebanese National Salvation 
Front. This is part of an effort to follow up the march of pan-Arab 
fighting in Lebanon and unify efforts between the Arab rev- 
olutionary forces, in order to complete the liberation of Lebanon 
and stand up to any counterattack from the NATO forces, which 
were ousted by force yesterday from Beirut and fled in fear to 
their ships off the coast. 


JANA has learned that these contacts are continuing in order to 
unify efforts and to escalate the struggle until final victory. 


JANA Political Editor on Mubarak Morocco Visit 
LDO81759 Tripoli JANA in English 1647 GMT 8 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada al Ulla 5, Feb 8, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY The political editor of the JAMAHIR- 
I1YAH NEWS AGENCY commented on the visit made by the 
head of the Egyptian regime to Morocco. 


He said: The visit of the head of the Egyptian puppet regime to 
the sisterly country of Morocco forms a new, tragic event in the 
Arab series of shame. The visit itself, and its acceptance, is 
considered by all standards a walk away from Arab unanimity, 
destruction of all what had remained of the Arab League, an end 
to Baghdad’s resolutions, as well as a permission, from now on, 
not to submit to Arab will, the resolutions of its summits, and the 
charter of its league. 


All attempts to justify this event that it comes in the context 
of the Islamic Conference Organization and had nothing to do 
with the Arab cause ~~ cannot by any means annul the outcome 
of this incident and limit its grave repercussions. What a mockery 
and what a humiliation! This excuse is much worse than the guilt 
itself. It is, in fact, an insult to Arab intelligence, belittling it as 
if these justifications are addressed were a child who might be 
overwhelmed into believing the tricks of the magician, and is 
taken aback by the innocence of their display of lies and slander. 
They justify their shameful action by falsehood as if the Arab 
world is a world of small children who will believe what they say 
and accept what they do. 


To extend the invitation to the head of a regime which betrayed 
the cause and welcome him in an Arab capital creates a contra- 
diction which is hard to believe and impossible to justify. That 
person who recognized the enemy, submitted his country to 
the will of the occupier, hoisted the Zionist flag over the Egyptian 
capital, and made Cairo the defeated capital — well deserves to 
be beaten up and cursed, and not to be received with hospitality 
and respect. 


The Arab who recognizes the enemy after half a century or more 
of continuous struggle, and after scores of thousands of victims
