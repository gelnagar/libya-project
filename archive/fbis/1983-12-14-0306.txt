LIBYA

Reagan’s ‘Defender of Peace’ Claims ‘Destroyed’ 

LD! 31658 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 

13 Dec 83 


[Station commentary] 


[Text] In New York yesterday, the chief of the U.S. Administra- 
tion, Ronald Reagan, addressed a society which awards medals 


Vv. 14 Dec 83 


to servicemen. He declared that what he called America’s days 
of weakness have passed, and that from this point we must let 
weapons be the judge that defends LS. interests throughout the 
world 


In this connection, he praised the role of the US. Army. He 
said) From Grenada to Lebanon, and from the Demilitarized 
Zone in Korea to the NATO line in Europe, the U.S. Army is 
fulfilling its duty in the best way 


What the chief of the U.S. Administration said 1s a serious 
matter. What makes it more serious is that sharp tone which he 
used when speaking on this subyect. His was a tone which springs 
from the inherent evil that has distinguishes Reagan's pulic: 
since coming to power in the U.S. Administration. He has taken 
it upon himself to act on the political stage in much the same 
manner he once used on the public stage. However, he is now 
using 4 more insane and frightening manner to make the world 
agree, tremble, and to be brow-beaten. Reagan's power madness 
is not accidental; it stems from the very core of his general 
behavior. He who heads a major power, one which possesses tons 
of nuclear and hydrogen bombs, biological weapons, fleets of 
ships, and thousands of aircraft squadrons, he who heads a power 
capable of destroying the greater part of the world, says with 
hateful rancor that the days of America’s weakness have passed 
These words of his mean that America is no longer concerned 
with peace or any degree of stable security 


According to Reagan's statement, the United States has taken 
on another role, that is, of crossing the borders of small countries 
and imposing hegemony over them. This is exactly what is meant 
by the saying) rom Grenada to Lebanon, and from the Demili- 
tarized Zone in Korea to the NATO line in Europe, the US 
Armed Forces are fulfilling their duty in the best way. In return 
for this, Reagan asks all small countries which aspire to peace, 
look forward to the future, and seek to bolster their freedom 
through the possession of (?their own) defensive power in order 
to live in peace, to totally stop arming themselves and controlling 
their own resources. They must remain like the mild lamb until 
they are swallowed by the ravenous American wolf. In Reagan's 
view, all the weapons of destruction, devastation, and anni- 
hilation are not enough. In his view, both the capacity and range 
of US. destructive power must be increased. Isn't this precisely 
what is meant by organized world terrorism? Moreover, doesn't 
this mean that terrorism has come to be on a par with the policy 
of the U.S. Administration? All the answers are yes. Reagan's 
statements are the best proof of this. He speaks the language of 
war, threatens peace, and works for the wrecking of the people's 
freedom and the destruction of their aspirations and hopes for 
freedom. This means more than US. arrogance. It reflects a 
crusading behavior nurtured by military tyranny 


One thing must be said: These statements by Reagan destroyed 
forever all claims that the U.S. ts a defender of peace and a 
protector of human rights. They have shown the entire world the 
truth: The United States is a tyrannical nation hostile to the 
peoples of the world, good at war and no good at peace.
