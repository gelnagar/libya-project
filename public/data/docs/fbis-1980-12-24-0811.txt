ARAB NATIONALS CALLED ON TO JOIN LIBYAN ARMY 


JN232003 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1918 CMT 23 Dec 80 
{Station announcement ] 


[Text] Announcement on the acceptance of Arab volunteers: 


V. 24 Dec 80 aa NORTH AFRICA 


In view of the ‘ocialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah's unionist revolutionary role 
as the unionis. state and the state for all Arabs, and in affirmation of the right of 
every free Arab to have the honor of defending the Arab nation, liberating its soil and 
delivering its shrines from the filth of the imperialist presence, and in view of the 

1 September revolution's belief that the hour of deliverance has come and that it is 
time for this nation to awake from its slumber and arise in the face of the aliens, the 
command of the General Staff calls on all the Arab pilots and technicians who have 
ended their military service for any reason whatsoever, to enlist in the service of the 
Libyan Arab Armed Forces, where they will be given the opportunity to get advanced 
training and prepare themselves to wage the Arabs’ great battle for freedom, honor and 
dignity. The call for jihad is sounded and the day of victory is approaching. Heed the 
call of duty and arise to defend Arab honor and dignity. 


ARAB NATIONALS TO BE ACCEPTED IN MILITARY SCHOOLS 


JN232049 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1924 GMT 23 Dec 80 
[Station announcement ] 


[Text] Announcement on the acceptance of Arab students in the military colleges of the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah: 


In view of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah's unionist revolutionary role 
as the unionist state and the state for all the Arabs, and in affirmation of the right 
of every free Arab to get the proper training and tc have the honor of defending the 
Arab nation, liberating its shrines, delivering it from all forms of alien imperialist 
presence and working for its unity, and in order to bring closer the day of creation of 
the armed Arab people, the command of the General Staff of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces 
announces that the door is now open for Arab students to join the following military 
colleges: 


The military college, the naval college, the air force college, the air defense college 
and the girls military college. 


The applicant must meet the following requirements: 


He must be an Arab, must be between 17 and 25 years of age, must have a general secondary 
certificate or any other equivalent certificate, should pass the prescribed medical 
examination and must not have been convicted of a crime or felony concerning matters of 
honor. 


The applicant must submit a written application with the following documents: 


Certificate of the level of his education; certificate of non-conviction; a photograph 
of his passport, travel document or identity card; and his full address. 


The student will be given a monthly allowance for the duration of his study. He will be 
granted the rank of second lieutenant at graduation. He will be subjected to all the 
military rules and regulations applied in the armed forces. If he finds any difficulty 
in obtaining some of the required documents, he may submit the application now, provided 
he pledges to bring those documents later. 


The applications must be sent to the command of the General Staff the nearest military 
camp or any Libyan Embassy or People's Bureau abroad. For further explanations, the 
officials in the previous paragraph may be contacted. 


[Signed] The command of the General Staff 


V. 24 Dec 80 NORTH AFRICA 


IRAQI MILITARY INTELLIGENCE CHIEF REPORTEDLY KILLED 


LD232238 Tripoli JANA in English 1926 GMT 23 Dec 80 

[Text] Baghdad, -3 Dec (JANA)--Reports from Baghdad say that Colonel Raed Sadoun, chief 
of Iraqi military intelligence, has been killed in Basrah by “opponents of the regime". 
Some military installations are also reported to have been destroyed in the city.
