Summary (AI): A 1981 Los Angeles Times article reports testimony in the trials of former CIA operatives Edwin Wilson and Eugene Tafoya about links to Libyan leader Qaddafi's terrorist and assassination activities. Tafoya was accused of shooting Libyan dissident Faisal Zagallai in Fort Collins, Colorado, in October 1976 (OCR reads "1989" but context indicates 1976).
• A CIA spokesman declined to elaborate on the agency's September 1976 contact with Wilson and repeated an earlier denial of involvement by the agency in illegal activities.
• Tafoya testified he went to Zagallai's apartment to deliver a message from the CIA that Zagallai should curb his political activities.
• Tafoya said he shot Zagallai in self-defense after Zagallai pulled a gun he was authorized to carry because the FBI had warned that his name was on an international hit list of Kadafi opponents.
• The jury in the Tafoya trial began deliberations on a Wednesday.
• 1976-09: Edwin Wilson and Frank Terpil allegedly plotted to murder a Libyan dissident.
• 1976-10-14: Faisal Zagallai, an opponent of Qaddafi, was shot twice in the head by Eugene Tafoya in Zagallai's living room during a struggle.
