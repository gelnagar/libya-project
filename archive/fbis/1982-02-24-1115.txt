LIBYA

ANTI-IMPERIALIST COMMITTEE ISSUES STATEMENT 


LD231644 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 23 Feb 82 


[Text] The preparatory committee for the general conference of the International 
Forum To Fight Imperialism, Zionism, Racism and Reaction concluded its work at the 
conference hall in the Ash~-Shati Hotel in Tripoli at noon today. The following 
statement was issued by the committee: 


In implementation of the resolutions of the International Conference for Solidarity 
With the People of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah held in Tripoli from 
21 to 23 August, 1981, calling for the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah to be 
considered as an international platform for resisting imperialism, Zionism, racism and 
reaction and in appreciation for the struggles of the Libyan Arab people and as an 
evaluation for the stances of the principled Great 1 September Revolution with regard 

to the people's struggles and their liberation which are embodied in [words indistinct] 
struggle for freedom and the unity of confronting imperialism, Zionism, racism and 
reaction, the preparatory committee for the International Forum To Fight Imperialism, 
Zionism, Racism and Reaction held its first meeting in Tripoli from 21 to 23 February, 1982, 
to adopt the executive measures for the general conference of the international forum. 
It stresses that the international forum is the living expression of the people's con- 
science the genuine embodiment of their will for liberation and breaking out of bondage, 
the revolutionary (?rejuvenation) of their struggle against imperialism, Zionism, racism 
and reaction, the uplifting of their political, military, economic and social effective- 
ness at a time when the features of the assault of world imperialism, under the leader- 
ship of the United States have become apparent. Their plots against the peoples and the 
plans to steal the freedom of the peoples, to threaten their independence, to destroy 
their choices through a blatant imperialist intrigue, to threaten peace and security and 
world detente, to engulf the world and to politically dominate it by extending their 
aggressive alliance with reactionary, fascist, dictatorial, racist and Zionist forces, 
and to militarily dominate them by (?deploying) military bases and by widening their 
scope and by increasing military provocations against the people's security and safety, 
and to dominate them economically by increasing the exploitation of the people's 
resources, to enhance the growth of economic monopolies and to extend the scope of the 
multinational companies and to dominate them culturally by destroying the cultural 
independence and identity of the peoples -- a matter which demands a revival of the 
people's struggle to create a new world molded by the living people's forces determined 
to realize their choices, to preserve their unity and to protect their democratic 
freedom and deepen their political, economic and social liberation -- demand the 
strengthening of the unified struggle of these living forces to inflict a final defeat 
on colonialism, reaction, and racism and to liquidate all aspects of their political 
encirclement, military domination and economic monopolies. 


The preparatory committee, while confirming the inevitable defeat of the imperialist 
forces under the leadership of the United States and its reactionary, fascist and 
dictatorial tools, expresses its confidence that the struggle for freedom is an 
indivisible. unit, the responsibility for which is shouldered by the living forces of 
the peoples and to which all the revolutionary and progressive forces in the world are 
committed in order to realize a world in which freedom, progress and peace can prevail. 
Thus, the committed stresses:

First, The establishment of a international forum for resisting imperialism, Zionism, 
racism and reaction in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah is an embodiment 
of the unity of struggle of the peoples exposed to aggression by imperialism and its 
racist and reactionary tools, and it gains new legality from the collective 
responsibility of the peoples to confront all aspects of imperialist hegemony over any 
arena and on every front and in every place through all political and military means. 
The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah is a revolutionary and struggle center 
working in coordination and integration with all the revolutionary political and social 
centers, forces and organizations opposed to imperialism. 


Second, the establishment of the international forum in the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah confirms the inevitability of confronting the Zionist-Imperialist- 
reactionary intrigue and the inevitability of challenging it by all oppressed peoples 
participating in this forum. Resisting imperialism and its tools will be the 
responsibility of the peoples and their revolutionary, progressive and democratic 
forces in order to confirm the depth of their faith in the inevitability of the victory 
of the peoples and the victory of their will and national choices. 


Third, the legality of the struggle of the peoples to preserve their firm rights and to 
liberate their land and to defend their independence and to regain their firm national, 
political, economic and cultural rights is a human responsibility to whose unity, 
integration and escalation the living forces of the peoples are committed. 


Fourth, the liquidation of monopolist imperialism and all forms of economic and cultural 
hegemony demands backing the struggle of the people to eliminate economic subservience 
and to liberate their resources from the domination of others and to help them implement 
their development programs to serve their national interests, and demands economic 
integration among peoples to back these programs and to collectively confront all 
attempts at subversion and economic blockade. 


Fifth, stress is placed on the need to struggle to liquidate all aspects of racism and 
Zionism and all forms of racial discrimination and all inhuman practices against the 
minorities in order to reach a new world which preserves man's dignity and develops all 
aspects of creativeness and ingenuity without any distinction as regards color, race or 
religion. 


Sixth, stress is placed on the need to support and back the world liberation movements 
in their political and armed struggle for the liberation of their peoples and to resist 
the imperialist projects, allies and military bases which threaten the security and the 
safety of the peoples and to resist all aspects of fascism, dictatorship, domination and 
terrorism led by rightist, reactionary and racist forces. 


The preparatory committee, while stressing these principles which express the faith of 
the peoples in freedom and confirm their determination to collectively struggle to 
realize them in order to reach a new world in which freedom is consolidated and which 
confirms the masses’ presence and control over their political, social and economic and 
cultural potentials, calls on all forces struggling for freedom and naturally opposed to 
world imperialism and its racist, reactionary, fascist, dictatorial and Zionist tools, 
to adopt practical steps for the establishment of an international platform for resist- 
ing imperialism, Zionism, racism and reaction on the soil of the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in order to bring about the unity of the struggle of the peoples 
to strengthen their efforts and to cement their national and progressive choices in the 
framework of the integration and tightening of struggle action with the political and 
social establishment opposed to world imperialism and its attempted intrigues.

The preparatory committee for the International Forum To Fight Imperialism, Zionism, 
Racism and Reaction has considered the speech by Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the 
leader of the Great 1 September Revolution, and one of its official documents and 
appreciated his effective role in backing human struggle and in fighting world imperialism 
under the leadership of the United States. The preparatory committee has issued the 
following organizational decisions to set up the International Forum To Fight Imperialism, 
Zionism, Racism and Reaction. 


First, decision No 1 approves the aims of the International Forum To Fight Imperialism, 
Zionism, Racism and Reaction. 


Second, the preparatory committee listed the world liberation movements, the national fronts, 
parties and political organizations and world international organizations to be invited 

to attend the general conference of the international forum, in the Socialism People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


Third, the preparatory committee fixed the date for convening the general conference for 
the International Forum To Fight Imperialism, Zionism, Racism and Reaction for 15 through 
18 May in Tripoli. 


Fourth, the preparatory committee has chcsen from among its members a working committee 
to organize the political or organizational preparations needed for convening the 
conference, to be submitted to the preparatory committee at its next meeting. 


Fifth, the preparatory committee fixed the date of its next meeting in Tripoli for 12 
through 14 May 1982, to study the arrangements prepared by the working committee to be 
submitted to the meeting of the general conference of the international platform. 


RADIO NOTES SECRETARY HAIG'S ‘SECRET REMARKS' 


NC210826 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 21 Feb 82 


[Text] The despot General Haig, the U.S. secretary of state, has disclosed another 
aspect of the hostile U.S. plots against the Jamahiriyah and provided evidence of the 
terrorism the U.S. administration is exercising against the Libyan Arab people. 


The Washington POST has published remarks by the U.S. secretary of state -- which Haig 
had made in secret notes -- regarding the U.S. plot against the Libyan Arab people. 
These secret remarks, which were dated 18 January, said inter alia: It seems that the 
time has come to put into effect the second phase against Libya. At the beginning of 
February the Americans will be out of Libya. Therefore, the situation is suitable for 
carrying out another action.
