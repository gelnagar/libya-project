LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Urges Libyan-Syrian-Algerian Summit 
JN271620 Abu Dhabi AL-ITTIHAD in Arabic 
26 Aug 86 pp 1, 8 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, Rabat, News Agencies — Responsible Libyan 
sources have said that Libyan President Mu‘ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi suggested to Syrian President Hafiz al-Asad, during 
their talks in Tripoli yesterday, the holding of a tripartite summit 
which would include, in addition to the two of them, the Algerian 
president, on confronting the U.S.-Zionist challenges. These 


sources disclosed the existence of Syrian-Libyan-Algerian 
coordination to prevent the expansion of the Iraq-Iran war. 
[passage omitted] 


Responsible Libyan sources told Al-Jttihad Tripoli puts great 
importance on convening a tripartite summit among Syria, 
Libya, and Algeria. The sources added if the summit convenes it 
will focus discussion on a number of urgent matters, particularly 
the escalation of the Gulf war — there is Syrian-Libyan-Algerian 
coordination to this effect — Algerian efforts to unite Palestinian 
factions in light of Syria’s desire to postpone a reconciliation 
meeting and Fatah’s readiness to abrogate the Amman accord, 
the challenges facing the Arab nation in light of the possibility 
Libya may be exposed to new aggression, the U.S.-Egyptian 
maneuvers in the Mediterranean, and the continuation of the 
Israeli occupation of a part of southern Lebanon, in addition to 
continued Israeli threats to Syria. 


Syrian sources in Tripoli said President al-Asad discussed with 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi Libya’s defense needs and development of 
the new missile system which Libya recently received from the 
USSR and which will be partly manned by Syrian military 
experts. The sources added the Syrian president is holding con- 
tacts to reinforce the Arab front which rejects capitulatory 
solutions following the Ifrane meeting and that he will visit 
Algeria for this purpose. [passage omitted] 


Central Command Said Moved Outside of Tripoli

[Text] According to diplomatic sources in Tripoli, the Libyan 
Government has moved its central command [qiyadataha al- 
markaziyah] from Tripoli to the town of (Hun), 400 km east of 
Tripoli.
