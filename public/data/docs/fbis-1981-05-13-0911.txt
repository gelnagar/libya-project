DEFECTIONS FROM CHAD GOVERNMENT ARMY REPORTED 
IN121152 Khartoum SUNA in Arabic 1052 GMT 12 May 81 
thartoum, 12 May (SUNA)--The Chadian Northern Armed Forces of Hissein Habre have 
announced that large numbers of forces belonging to Goukouni Oueddei and his interior 
minister Mahamat Abba have begun to escape from Ndjamena and join the northern forces. 

A statement issued by the northern forces today welcomed the strugglers who had joined 
them to fight against the Libyan occupation forces. 


The statement held the Libyan invasion forces responsible for the recent events in Abeche 
ind Mao, during which large numbers of the sons of the Chadian people were killed. It 
iffirmed that the Chadian fighters are suffering from Libyan provocations and harassment. 
The statement accused the invading forces of carrying out large-scale assassinations among 
the ranks of the Chadian forces. It appealed to the honest sons of Chad to return to their 


senses and not be deceived by their leaders Goukouni Oueddei and Mahamat Abba. It also 


appealed to them to join their brothers and carry arms against the Libyan occupation forces.
