He defends new people's security and arrest rules, calls for killing opponents, and attacks the U.S., Egypt, Algeria, Jordan and Arab unity. No specific Tripoli sites or events are named beyond a passing reference to a block of flats in Tripoli.
• Qadhdhafi said the new people's security law meant there was no traditional police, and every Libyan could serve in people's security in his street or district.
• He said revolutionary committees and people's congresses should arrest suspected traitors, and that 'an entire people liquidating its opponents inside and abroad' was legitimate.
• He described Muslim Brotherhood members as sent by an American intelligence officer and given dollars and sent to 'a block of flats in Tripoli'.
• He declared that any Arab could enter Libya with only an identity card, without a passport, and said Maltese would enjoy the same terms.
• He referred to the Ghat air base and Algerian air training over Libyan skies.
