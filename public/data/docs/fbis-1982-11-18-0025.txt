LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI DISCUSSES 'ZIONIST' PLANS FOR AREA 


NC171130 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 17 Nov 82 


[Excerpts] The brother leader of the revolution [Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi] last night 
opened the first session of the Arab housing and development ministerial council in 
Al-Bayan al-Awwal city [in Banghazi]. 


In his opening speech, the brother leader of the revolution dealt with several questions 
and subjects relating to development planning in Arab cities and the need for making 
radical changes in the existing housing and development studies in line with the 
citizens’ environmental and social circumstances.

At the outset of his speech, the brother leader outlined that he was opening the meeting 
not in his capacity as head of state because the government inthe Jamahiriyah is abol- 
ished and there is no head of state. The people have replaced the head of state, the 
president of the republic and the government and created instead people's conferences 
and people's committees. He said: I am leading the revolution through the activity of 
the revolutionary committees, which incite the people's masses to exercise and affirm 
the authority. We believe that the whole world will turn toward the Jamahiriyah's 
system in the future because it is the ultimate phase in the struggle for solving the 
problems of democracy and power. 


The brother leader emphasized the importance of this Arab meeting held in the 
Jamahiriyah being one form of the unitary grouping at the Arab homeland's level. 


In this respect, he said, he feels the existence of a real danger to the unity of the 
Arab nation through foreign attempts against Arab nationalism, drawing attention to the 
need for resisting this danger. He said: If the deterioration continues and the enemy 
attempt to attain gains in every round is met by disgraceful Arab retreat, we will 

face the danger of the Arab nation's disappearance. 


The brother leader of the revolution warned in this connection of the consequences of 
the hostile Israeli advance, which aims at the material liquidation of the Arab home- 
land. The seriousness of this advance is that it is taking place under the big 
umbrella of the United States, which is driving the Zionist enemy to achieve its goals 
against the Arab nation. 


The leader of the revolution emphasized the historical fact that the Arab-Zionist con- 
flict is one of survival, not of borders, and that Arab existence is incompatible with 
Zionist existence. He said that either the Zionist existence or the Arab existence 
would survive. He stressed that we cannot meet at any peaceful ground with the 
Israelis, because they are an entity opposed to the Arabs' existence. 


The leader noted that the Zionist enemy is immigrating 12 million Jews to the Arab land 
to perpetuate its occupation of Arab territory and to liquidate the Arab existence the 
there. 


The brother leader outlined that the Zionists are planning to increase their numbers to 
15 million, which is more than four Arab countries, while now they are less than the 
population of any one of the Arab countries. The Negev, Sinai and other areas of 
Jordan and Lebanon will be developed to accommodate them. Subsequently, expansion will 
start, the population will be increased, the Arabs will be evacuated from their homes 
and Jews will be immigrated to the Arab land from other countries. 


He said that the Zionists’ goal now is to reach the sources of water. They are seek- 
ing to control the Jordan, Al-Yarmuk, Litani and Nile Rivers and later the Tigris and 
Euphrates Rivers. 


The leader added that their first strategic goal is not the question of the Palestinian 
Resistance or the threat to the settlements but, after reaching the sources of water, 
to control the holy places and the energy sources. Of the holy places, they have 
occupied Jerusalem and made it, as they say, the eternal capital of the Jewish state. 
Their objective now is Medina, Khaybar, Tabuk and Mecca. 


As to the energy sources, this question is subject to an agreement between the 
Americans and the Israelis. If the United States sees that the Israelis are better 
suited to manage the energy sources in the interests of the United States, it will 
allow them to control them in the Arabian Peninsula, the Gulf and Libya. If the 
United States sees that the Arabs are better suited at this phase to manage these 
sources in its interests, then this would hinder the Israelis in reaching this goal.

Concluding his speech, the brother leader greeted the conferees and said: I 

highly appraise this meeting and I am grateful to those who created this conference 
and the annual meeting. It indicates that this nation is still good. He expressed 
regret that the Arab League now calls only for the recognition of the Zionist enemy 
and meets only to sell honor and land, emphasizing that the Arab League Charter is 
obsolete, ludicrous and dates back to Imam al-Badr and Imam Ahmad [of Yemen]. 


The brother leader called on the conferees to safeguard and maintain the conference 
annually and to achieve further positive results in the interests of the Arab citizen. 


100 ARRESTED IN FOILED ATTACK ON AL-QADHDHAFI 


NC171047 Paris AFP in English 0945 GMT 17 Nov 82 


[Text] Paris, Nov 17 (AFP) -- Some 100 Libyan military and security officers were 
reportedly arrested early this month as they were preparing to attack a plane 
carrying Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, a London-based Arabic language news- 
paper reported today. 


The paper, ASH-SHARQ A’ AWSAT, said the attack, planned for November 4 when Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi was scheduled to return from a visit to Yugoslavia, was foiled when one 
of the officers alerted loyalist security forces who captured the plotters hours 
before the plane was due to arrive. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's return was reportedly postponed two days as a precautionary 
measure. 


Most of the officers involved, who came from the army, the air force and Libyan 
security units, were young, according to the paper. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi was said to have been wounded in the shoulder by one of his 
own bodyguards in an attack in Banghazi in June, 1980 and rumors of other plots 


against him surfaced several months earlier.
