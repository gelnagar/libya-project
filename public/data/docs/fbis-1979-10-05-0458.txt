AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES TRIPOLI 'GREEN BOOK! SYMPOSIUM 
LDO41524 Tripoli JANA in English 1417 GMT 4 Oct 79 LD 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, October 4, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel al-Qadhdhafi took part in last 
night's session of the International Symposium on the Green Book. He made a number of remarks 
about the content of some of the papers delivered in the symposium, saying that the Green 
Book had not been written in a traditional warlike story, a novel, or a research work of 

the usual kind, 


He explained that the Green Book was the upshot of what mankind has attained as it deals 
with the various problemsof life, The dicta of the Green Book embodies the interaction 
between man and his problems, that is to say, the dialectical relation between man and 
these problems. He underlined that any free and daring opinion will ultimately reach the 
same conclusions as those of the Green Book. 


Referring to the different papers delivered, he said that some of them had no connection 
with the Green Book, while others supported the Green Book in a superficial way. He 
remarked that some of the studies have shown that those who delivered them have not 
consulted the ureen Book because of some prejudice, and because they do not wish to be 
acquainted with ideas which refute their own.

Colonel alQadhdhafi went on to say that the Green Book put forward solutions to over- 
come violence, oppression and depotism against the peoples of the world, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi classified the papers presented to the seminar into three main 
topics, according to their handling of the subjects: First, the parliamentary experi- 
ment; secondly, the party experiment, and thirdly, socialism, 


He said: "The papers dealing with the first topic of parliamentary experiment based 
their assessment on a philosophy of two centuries ago, so they are out of date from 

the onset, It was condemned by scholars at a time when the masses were incapable of 
understanding the situation, Those scholars who had condemned this philosophy were 

very few in the 17th and 18th century refuted the theory representation". [sentence 
as received] 


He added: "The elements of dictatorship found a way out of this situation," Thus, 

for instance, Hitler, Mussolini and the shah of Iran had parliamentary council, Even 

As Sadat has a parliamentary council, This means all corrupt regimes have, or had, such 
a representation, The Green Book says that the strongest dictatorships were represented 
by parliamentary councils, 


From the very beginning, thinkers and philosophers have drawn attention to the falsity 
of parliamentary councils saying that "individual will and freedom cannot be conceded, 
This means that man either exercises his own rule or lives under a dictatorship, There 
is no representation in individual will and independence, but nowadays all the world 
constitutions talk about people's sovereignty, while this is exercised on their behalf", 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi elaborated: "We finally conclude the representation has been 
rejected from the beginning, Concerning the party system, a political conflict now 
exists, despite the one-party and multi-party systems, such as religious and secular 
parties, the so-called people's parties and fronts formed by a party coalition, Despite 
the existing party systems, the political crisis of the world has reached its climax", 


Concerning the third topic of the papers, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out: "Everyone 
agrees with us that a worker is exploited by the employers, the servant by the house 
master, the tenant by the landlord, the people by the governments, a state by another 
one and a continent by another continent, Inflation and price increase prove that 
humanity is suffering of exploitation, The world lives on the brink of a volcano, due 
to the anger of those exploited, They will rise to burn up the elements of exploitation 
as they had risen in Libya, I hope they will succeed in Iran, too, The revolution will 
continue until it destroys the world of exploitation and suppression," 


The leader of the Great First of September Revolution, Colonel Muammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
referred to one of the studies presented uné?r the title of: "Freedom in an Oppressed 
World", he said; "This topic deserves to he theme of this symposium, as it 
summarizes the state of the world today, ti °ch for freedom in an oppressed world, 
immaterial of the content of this study, 


"There is always someone oppressing others, a government oppressing another country, To 
this moment, as I stand before you, I do not confirm the inevitability of the Green Book, 
But this Green Book is the most recent of solutions presented following all previous 
experiences of capitalism, communism, Trotskyism and materialism, And the whole world 
agrees that there is an unestablished solution to be found, which is the search for 
freedom in an oppressed world, The strikes, sit-ins, assassinations and coups are 
evidence of a rejection of something established and a search for a new solution",

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi wondered about the nature of this solution and whether it had been 
incorporated into one of the previous systems. He replied: "I do not believe it has been, 
because these systems have proved to be failures. And we in the Jamahiriyah, have this 
book. So let us search in it to find out whether or not it contains the solution. 

The implementation of the dicta of this book shows whether it is ideal". 


He pointed out saying that there was a real difference among us on the factors which 

create history. "The -eason of this difference is our ignorance of such factors. Through 
my review of human history, I have established that the social factor, as the third part of 
the Green Book asserts, is the maker of history on the international level, which stems from 
the national factor, Another factor is the religious one, which always fails in contrast 
with the national factor. Many empires have been established on the basis of religion-- 
such as the Abassid, Ottoman, Austrian, Pakistani, Indian and Bengali--but all failed to 
exist. This happens on the international level, whereas on the internal level, the struggle 
is always governed by the political and economic factors. In the first factor we find the 
people's authority and wonder, do the people want the authority in their hands or in the 
hands of others? The correct answer would be the people's authority. Ina kingdom we see 
people controlled by the king, who has his heir apparent; In the republic, the authority 

is given to those elected, but in the jamahiriyah, the masses do not choose their ruler 

but rule themselves by themselves, Thus, authority in the Jamahiriyah system is in the hands 
of the masses. This is the summary of the Green Book: ‘Authority is in the hands of the 
masses. No representation and no concession,...and the masses are always aligned with 
authority!", 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi elaborated on the second part of the Green Book on socialism saying: 
"What is this socialism? Is this the socialism of Western Europe, Eastern Europe, or the 
Third World? It is the socialism of the emancipation of man's needs from any domination and 
monopoly, which once achieved, freedom would then be attained. This is when workers become 
partners, houses belong to their occupiers, land becomes public property, when servants are 
emancipated, wages abolished together with rents and profits. Then exploitation would be 
eradicated and freedom achieved". 


He added: "The Green Book explains to us the conducive conditions for the prosperity of 
freedom when it discusses women, education and blacks. It states that the black issue is a 
human issue, but it questions whether the problem can be resolved by segregating the white 
and the blacks in America, or by leaving the Africans to live in jungles like monkeys at 

a time when America is draining out African resources while claiming Africa to be 
prosperous. Are oppression, ignorance and war solutions to the problem?" Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi retorted that these were foolish and ridiculous means. 


On the subjects of journalism and education he noted that both were complementary to 
freedom. He asked: "Who of us objects to the dicta of the Green Book concerning these 
subjects as long as they are in our favour and that of freedom?" 


"The people will assume all the duties which had been carried out by the dictatorship. 
The security apparatus, for example, is imposed by dictatorship. The security apparatus, 
for example, is imposed by dictatorships. But in a Jamahiriyah society the police force 
is non-existent. Defence and protection are authoritarian forms, as the shah of Iran 
had pretended that Iran was being threatened when he built a strong army with which he 
terrorized the Iranian people. Does this mean that anyone concerned about security and 
defence should carry out such a mission?"

The leader of the Great First of September Revolution concluded his debate by referring 
te the peoples which were in a better position to choose the new solution, He said: 
"The American peoples are in a very bad condition as they are confronted by several 
obstacles--mainly the Pentagon, C.I.A., the House of Representatives, the Senate and 
the bombs--which are all elements of oppression directed against the American people, 
The space and nuclear programmes as well drain out the resources. The American peoples 
could be friends with the Russians and thus make nuclear bombs needed no more, But the 
people's will is not the world's instigator. The U.N. consists of 150 delegates each 
representing one state, yet this symposium includes several times the number of those in 
the United Nations. 


There are peoples, he added, who think they are in a worse condition than others, But in 
fact, they are better, This is the case of the Libyan people, who, until recently, used to 
believe they were the worst peoples, even in comparison with their neighbours, the Egyptian 
people. But, the windmills hanging over the Egyptian people prevented them from attaining 
freedom. Whereas the Libyan people, being in a better position, have been able to achieve 
their freedom. 


Therefore the Latin American and Arab peoples are more likely to achieve freedom than the 
U.S. people. This is despite the fact that they are exploited.and backward, with some 

of their land occupied. Nonetheless, they are in a better condition and Libya has proved 
it. The Arab countries might find themselves in the same situation as Libyan people and the 
Iranians by way of a popular revolution, And the Latin American peoples might achieve 
victory overnight", 


Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi concluded his debate by referring to a great number of the 
studies and papers submitted to the symposium which will be annexed as explanations to 
the Green Book, 


AT-TURAYKI MEETS WITH TURKISH, GUYANESE COUNTERPARTS AT UN 
LDO42034 Tripoli JANA in English 1908 GMI 4 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] New York, Oct 4, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Dr, 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, the 
Jamahiriyah foreign secretary, conducted contacts with other foreign ministers and heads 
of delegation taking part in the meeting of the 34th session of the U.N. General Assembly. 


Dr. at-Turayki held a meeting yesterday with Mr, Gunduz Okcun, the Turkish foreign minister, 
during which they discussed bilateral relations, the current [word indistinct] situation 
and the French invasion of Central Africa, The Turkish minister condemned foreign inter- 
ference in African affairs. 


Dr. at-Turayki held another meeting with the foreign minister of Guyana, Mr. Frourik Rolo, 
during which they discussed bilateral relations, the French invasion of Central Africa 
and matters of common interest. Mr. Rolo said that his country strongly condemns the 
French invasion of Central Africa, which it considers sets a dangerous precedent that
