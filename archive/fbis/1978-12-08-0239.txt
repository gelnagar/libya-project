LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI CALLS FOR ELIMINATION OF EXPLOITATION IN TRADE 
1D071528Y Tripoli JANA in English 1430 GMT 7 Dec 78 ID 
[Text] Tripoli, December 7, JANA--Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the lst 
of September revolution and secretary-general of the General People's Congress, pointed 


out that trade is an exploitation phenomenon, not only in the Jamahiriyah, but also 
throughout the world since traders are considered consumers and not producers, 


ve. 8 Dec 78 NORTH AFRICA 


Such a phenomenon is doomed, 


He said that this is a historic decision to opt for the revolution, In time it will be 
the option of the whole world, now that a humanitarian and progress revolution is spread- 
ing, reaching Iran where portraits of the shah, an outdated symbol of dictatorship, 
domination and exploitation, are stamped in the streets, 


This statement came during the forum at the People's Palace in Tripoli last night in 
which took part the leader of the lst of September revolution and members of ‘the chambers 
of commerce and industry union, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that any attempt against the revolutionary option is doomed, 
for its stands against freedom and alignment with slavery. He explained that the revolu- 
tion's new directives are irreversible and the guarantee for its victory and continuity 

is represented by the revolutionary forces throughout the country and even outside the 
Jamahiriyah, for these directives are getting wide responses from the revolutionary forces 
in the world, who are determined to realize their aspirations through a popular revolu- 
tion. He asserted that the directives of this revolution, which is a sound radical revolu- 
tion of civilization, are neither inspired by a feeling of hatred against a certain 

group nor an eagerness to defend anybody's personal interest. They are inspired by faith 
in the freedom of man as a general cause. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "Fortunately, the developments of the revolution!'s in the 
Jamahiriyah are gradual and all radical changes were achieved peacefully and in appro- 
priate circumstances," 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi referred to the revolution of the production workers and the workers! 
seizure of factories, firms and production sites which took place in this connection, He 
said that the workers believed in the new dicta revealed in the GREEN BOOK and realized 
the unjust relations which prevailed between workers and employers. Consequently they 
revolted against these relations and destroyed them, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi explained that if trade was allowed in the past andno blame was put 
on whoever practised it, it is now the task of the revolution to detect the faults and 
exploitation element in trade, in order to enable the masses to eliminate them, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that the traders and contractors themselves will come to realize 
that their activities are consuming and not productive, being based on the efforts of 
others. Most certainly they will take part in eliminating these phenomena of exploitation, 
in order to build the new society free from all forms of exploitation, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi explained that the free trade, similarly to renting and hiring, is 

an exploitation phenomenon that should be eliminated, not an act of revenge or hatred, but 
for creating a society in which all citizens are free and equal and not, as in the past, 
masters and slaves of their wages. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi then referred to the resolution of the people's congresses regarding 
the increase of the production capacity. He defined it by saying that anybody working 

in consumption will change production, as a step towards eliminating backwardness through 
the mobilization of all resources, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi explained that the chambers of commerce and industry union could 
play a major role in directing the traders sector towards productive jobs, with complete 
free will,

POLAND'S JABLONSKI CABIES GREETINGS ON LEAVING LIBYA 


ID071026Y Tripoli JANA in English 0813 GMT 7 Dec 78 ID 


[Text] Benghazi, Dec 7, JANA--Henryk Jablonski, president of the Council of State in the 
People's Republic of Poland sent the following cable to Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
leader of the great lst of September revolution and secretary general of the General 
People's Congress: 


Colonel Mu'tammar al-Qadhdhafi, secretary general of the General People's Congress of 
the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, Tripoli: 


As I leave your beautiful country I would like to extend to you in my name and the names 
of those who are accompanying me, our sincere thanks for your hospitality with which we 
were received in your country on our way from the People's Republic of Angola, the fact 
that reflects the existing friendship relations between our countries and our peoples, 


I want to take this opportunity to express again my best wishes to you and to all the 
Libyan people, wishing you more achievements in the development of your country. I 
think that the relations between our two countries will develop continuously for the 
interest of our peoples and the interest of peace and progress.
