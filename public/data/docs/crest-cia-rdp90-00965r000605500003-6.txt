Summary (AI): A 1985 New York Post article reports on a book, The Specialist by "Gayle Rivers," which describes a purported 1982 Libyan plot to bomb a New York subway train in midtown Manhattan. The Sunday Times of London disputes the book's claims and says the author is actually Raymond Brooks. The piece is about a publishing controversy, with little concrete information on Libya itself.
• Book The Specialist to be published by Sidgwick & Jackson in Britain and Stein and Day in the US, with $60,000 promotion budget and a $16.95 hardcover price.
• The book claims the Libyan plot would have used Puerto Rican Rastafarians, the Jewish Defense League, the Black Liberation Army and the Islamic League as explosives-smuggling groups.
• The Sunday Times said the author had failed to make the grade as an SAS territorial reservist and was now an arms dealer.
• 1982: Alleged Libyan plan, described in the book The Specialist, to assemble explosives on a New York subway train for detonation in midtown Manhattan (disputed by the Sunday Times)
