LIBYA

KHALIL, HIGH OFFICIALS REPORTEDLY FLEE SUDAN 


LD211408 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 21 Jun 81 


[Text] Senior Sudanese officials and personalities have begun to flee the country follow- 
ing the rapid and fierce escalation of the Sudanese popular uprising now sweeping the 
country. A large number of responsible Sudanese officials, including Numayri's 

first vice president, 'Abd al-Majid Khalil, have already fled. 


COMMENTARY PREDICTS SUCCESSFUL SUDAN REVOLUTION 


LD220454 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 21 Jun 81 
{[Unattributed commentary ] 


[Excerpts] The strength of the Arab revolution and all its progressive and revolutionary 
groups in the Arab homeland from the ocean to the Gulf are today witnessing with great 
pride the militant events and magnificent battles which the heroic Sudanese people are 
waging against their corrupt and sick regime, which has become a rotten fruit about 

to fall. 


While engaging in this heroic battle the Arab masses in Sudan are fully aware of the 
conspiracies that are being hatched secretly and openly by reactionary international 
imperialism and by the open U.S. intervention which Numayri has sought as protection 
against the wrath of the masses; cf the secret meetings the U.S. ambassador has been 
holding with Numayri, who has been written off by the Sudanese Arab people, and of the 
visit the U.S. deputy secretary of defense intends to pay to Khartoum soon. 


The Arab masses in Sudan are aware of all this and they are asking a thousand questions 
about what U.S. imperialism intends to do to liquidate their giant uprising, encircle 
their popular and gallant revolution and allow the new American replacement to emerge to 
take the place of Numayri, who has become completely bankrupt politically, militarily 
and economically and who has become incapable of controlling the development of events 
in Sudan. 


The awakened Arab masses in Sudan fully realize that the United States is trying to play 
its cards to protect its interests and its bases and to exploit and exhaust the 
important strategic position of Sudan to suppress the movements of revolution, libera- 
tion and progress in the African Continent. In order to do this, the United States has 
set up military bases so that it can suppress the will of the Arab people in gallant 
Sudan and the will of the Arab and African nations. 


The United States knows best the nature of the Arab masses in Sudan. It knows best that 
the blessed and pure Sudanese blood which was shed at the hands of the reactionary and 
falling regime in Sudan will not be in vain but will be taken into account on the day of 
reckoning. The popular uprising in the Sudanese Arab arena is a prelude to a tumultuous 
popular revolution to restore the image of free Sudan, Arab Sudan, Muslim Sudan and 
independent Sudan. 


The Arab masses in Sudan fully understand and sense all the intrigues and conspiracies 
of imperialism, Zionism and reaction which are trying to control this uprising if they 
cannot abort or liquidate it. But alas, the militant revolutionary is enlightened, 
vigilant and alert enough to be able to complete this state of steadfastness and allow 
the erupting revolution to reach the shore of safety and the dawn of freedom. 


The masses of the Arab people in Sudan are resolved to tighten their control over all 
the outlets. They firmly and strongly reject all the middle solutions and all the U.S. 
imperialist tricks. They will deepen the roots of the popular revolution which rejects 
all impurities. The masses will never allow any traitor to infiltrate and reap the 
fruits of their brilliant victories.
