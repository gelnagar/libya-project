• The article says Qaddafi was 39 years old at the time and had been in power for about a decade.
• Six previous assassination attempts on Qaddafi were reported over the past decade, according to intelligence sources.
• Qaddafi drives a Fiat 128 and lives in a small, sparsely furnished house; he is building another home in his birthplace area and accepted a farm from the township of El-Marj.
• He has been known to shut down broadcasts he dislikes by phoning the station.
• Qaddafi reportedly wandered Libyan streets and marketplaces incognito in early years of rule to hear public opinion.
• 1981-late: Army assassination attempt on Qaddafi, wounded in the arm, shoulder, stomach and legs; CIA confirmed it, per the article
