AL-QADHDHAFI OPENS ARAB CULTURE MINISTERS! MEETING 10 FEB 


LD111706 Tripoli JANA in English 1444 GMT 10 Feb 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, February 10, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The leader of the Great First 
of September Revolution, Colonel Mu!lammar al-Qadhdhafi, this morning opened the second 
Arab conference of culture ministers in Tripoli's Beach Hotel, 


The opening ceremony was attended by the secretaries of education, labour, civil service, 
and planning, some undersecretaries of various secretariats, and diplomats accredited 
to the Jamahiriyah, 


Participating in this conference are 17 Arab countries, the director general of the 
Arab educational, scientific and cultural organization, and a representative of the 
United Nations Education, Scientific and Cultural Organisation (U.N.E.S.C.0.). 


Ve. 14 Feb 79 NORTH AFRICA 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great First of September Revolution, delivered a 
speech in which he welcomed the ministers and delegations participating in this Arab 
meeting. He stressed the significance of the meetings of the Arab family in order to 
study their problems and affairs to find solutions for them through such meetings, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi referred to the fact that the Arab nation unfortunately was in a 
stage of deterioration in the political, economic and cultural fields. Nevertheless, 
the Arab nation was equipped with vast material capabilities and a great human legacy. 


He said that the Arab nation was subjected to foreign invasion at earlier stages and to 
colonial alliances which were enslaving parts of it after independence, but it managed 

to overcome and destroy them, The Arab nation destroyed the bases which were in its 
territories and destroyed the American and British alliances and the Italian settlers 

in the Jamahiriyah after the Great First of September Revolution, and the French settlers 
in Algeria. 


The Egyptian people destroyed British colonial existence, as was colonialism destroyed 
in Sudan, Tunisia, Syria, the Arab Peninsula, the gulf and Iraq. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi clarified that in spite of the fact that the Arab nation had 
been freed from such alliances and bases, unfortunately its conditions were not improved, 
Furthermore, they were getting worse, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that the reason behind this was unfortunately the responsibility 
of this generation, because it was saturated with the colonial culture and fads imported 
as a result of colonial remnants which formed a greater danger to the Arab nation and 

to its freedom, dignity, progress and unity than imperialism itself because it was 
imperialism which caused the division of this nation into reactionary and progressive 
forces, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi clarified that when we search for the reason behind this division, 
we find it in the existence of an agent Arab and a free honest Arab--and this is due to 
culture, education and the remnants of colonialism, and that imported colonialist 
cultural and educational curricula were the cause of the creation of this character 
which made it either reactionary or progressive, 


He said; "Because of this, if we succeed to solve this very difficult problem and 
drink from one source after we purify it, we can create in the future one new Arab 
mentality and this pure source will create a liberated progressive mentality," 


The leader of the Great First of September Revolution pointed out that fads, tastes 
and cultures cannot be imported and that we should learn our original Arab culture 
as the Arab nation is imbued with a great heritage and culture, 


He said; "We should reconsider education and its curricula to be able to destroy con- 
tradictions in the Arab world and find radical solutions and cultural and scientific 
grounds regardless of short-term treatments carried out at present," 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi urged the attendants to search and find the solutions and methods 
to wipe out imported Western culture which wiped out and denigrated Arab culture in 
the Arab world, in order to put an end to the foreign mentalities and imported cultures 
in the Arab world,

He said that this could be done by the creation of a cultural revolution in the Arab 
world which will wipe out these imported cultures and the restoration of our original 
Arabic culture, He said Western culture, which was introduced by some Egyptian writers, 
was the cause of denigrating the Arab mentality and distroying Arab culture because 
these Egyptian intellectuals had studied and absorbed the colonialist Western culture, 
and the culture now presented is foreign even to Egyptian culture itself. 


The revolution's leader went on to say in the speech he delivered before the second 
conference of Arab culture ministers that the original Egyptian artist who sang to the 
traditional musical instrument and ballads was not adopted gy the state which instead 
adopted those who sang to imported Western misical instruments foreign to Arab music, 


The same applied to the directors who were molded by Western culture and foreign edu- 
cation and who influenced with their distorted production the Arab countries. This led 
to the rise of contradictions in the Arab mentality and created agents and puppets, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi referred to the contagion which was transferred to some Arab writers 
and artists through those Egyptian writers who were saturated with colonial Western 
culture. The Arab nation should therefore get rid of foreign culture and intellectuals 
who are foreign to Arab culture. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi clarified in his speech that it was possible to import education 
from any source in the world in order to learn technology, sciences and modern industries, 
but it was not possible under any circumstances to import culture, tastes and fads, 
because each nation had its own particular tastes, fads and culture, 


He said, addressing the participants "as ministers of culture, you are now responsible 
for this tragedy, because you approve and encourage this production which works daily 
on Wiping and spoiling the Arab character," 


Concluding his speech, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out to the 4th article of the conference 
agenda concerning the celebration of the occasion of 14 centuries since the Holy Prophet's 
Hijira. He said: "It is a great event that we celebrate this occasion, because the 

Hijira is an important event in Islamic history. We in the Socialist Peoples Libyan 

Arab Jamahiriyah, from now onwards, and with conviction, will start recording from the 

date of the Prophet's death, because we see the Prophet's death as the most important 

event, not only to Islam but to all humanity as the Prophet Muhammad was the last of all 
prophets and messengers and by his death, contact between heaven and earth ceased, 


After Colonel al-Qadhdhafi concluded his speech, Mr. Mohieddine Sabir, chairman of Arab 
Organization for Education, Culture and Sciences delivered a speech in which he thanked 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great First of September Revolution, for opening this 
great cultural meeting. He said that Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's speech to the conference 
indicated his interest in the national causes which were being dealt with by the con- 
ference, and that such interest was an indication of his personal support and the support 
of the Great First of September Revolution for the aims, programmes and national schemes 
of the Arab organization, 


The chairman of the Arab Organization for Education, Culture and Sciences praised the 
Jamahiriyah's participation in deepening and developing the strategy of Arab education 
and culture. He said that the Jamahiriyah had offered direct and decisive contributions 
in implementing the foreign policy of Arab culture, particularly in spreading the Arabic 
language and Islamic culture in Africa. 


He added that the organization was striving to initiate joint national action in the 
fields of culture, education, sciences and building the Arab man on a national basis. 


Vv. 14 Feb 79 NORTH AFRICA 


He said conflicts and disputes tearing the world apart today were due to cultural battles 
and civilizational conflicts, 


In concluding his speech Mr, Mohieddine Sabir stressed the creative nature of Arab 
culture which is an open culture which did not honour either fanaticism or isolation, 
This culture, he said, is a tolerant culture which gives to others from a powerful 
position and receives selectively, 


Following the speech of the director of the Arab Organisation for Education, Culture and 
Sciences, Mr. Muhammad Abdel Kader Ould Sidi, minister of culture and information in the 
Islamic Republic of Mauritania delivered a speech on behalf of the delegations expressing 
his deep appreciation to Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi for opening the conference, 
clarifying that "the national, Islamic and humanitarian stands led him to open this Arab 
cultural conference, and lay down his intellectual writings before the conference, and 
he is the man whose close and direct contacts with the people's masses made out of his 
writings and thoughts a reference to the sons of this nation," 


He said: "our salvation is connected with the restoration of our culture which is the 
basis of our original glories, This human message cannot be established unless efforts 
are unified by leaders, thinkers and historians to go back to the sources of our 
civilization and great Arab heritage," 


Concluding his speech, he thanked the people of the Jamahiriyah for hosting this great 
cultural meeting. 


After the end of the speeches, the second conference of Arab ministers of culture held its 
first session, The participants chose Mr, Muhammad Abu al-Qasim az-Zuwayy, secretary of 
information and culture, to head the conference,

[Text] Tripoli, February 11, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The second conference of Arab 
ministers of information resumed its sessions Saturday evening under the chairmanship of 
Mr, Mohammad Abu al-Qasim az-Zuwayy, the secretary of information and culture, 


The session started with heads of delegations exchanging speeches, with the Tunisian 
minister of information, Mr, Mohamed Yawali, who stressed the importance of exchanging 
books between Arab countries, the preservation of copyrights for Arab authors and 
artists, and the importance of preserving Arab heritage and culture, 


For his part, Mr, Rashid Muhammad, the information minister of the Yemen Democratic 
Republic, praised the part played by the Jamahiriyah for the efforts made in aiding the 
Arab Organization for Education, Culture and Science, and the role it is playing in the 
field of information, He further added that the Jamahiriyah's hosting of the conference 
clearly reflects the participation of the Great First of September Revolution's pioneering 
Arab role which highlights the authenticity of Arab culture, 


He stressed the importance of preserving cultural identity in facing colonial plots that 
are striving for the destruction of Arab culture, 


He called for the necessity of taking measures to protect the Arab world from the invasion 
of foreign cultures,

The head of the Algerian delegation's speech followed, The speech concentrated on the 
human, economic and cultural requirements of the Arab cultural entity for which planning, 
organization, financing and the solution of related problems are essential for reaching 
cultural goals, 


In his speech, the Algerian representative said culture should not only look at its 
past, but should also no doubt face the present with responsibility and firmess, and 
to the dissemination of a bright future, 


The representative of U.N,E.S.C,0,'s director-general made a speech in which he praised 
efforts made by the information and culture secretariat for organising the culture con- 
ference, He said cultural development is of great importance and constitutes an 
essential part of total development aimed at creating the Arab individual, He pointed 
in this connection to the efforts exerted by U.N.E,S.C,0, in the form of special pro. 
grammes, 


He said that U.N,E.S.C.0., in agreement with the Jamahiriyah, will participate in the 
administration of a project for constructing a national museum in Tripoli. The project 
is considered one of the biggest international ventures of its kind in which total 
overall costs are expected to reach 25 million dollars, 


Ending his speech, the U.N,E,S.C,0, representative said his organization was working 
for the revival and maintainance of individual cultural identity and heritage which was 
highlighted in U.N.E,S,C,0.'s program for the protection of cultural property and the 
organization of international campaigns to save Arab antiquities, 


The U.N.E.S.C,0, representative then read the report made by the organizations! 
director-general which dealt with the implementation of the first Arab culture minister 
conference and permanent committee recommendations, The report also dealt with the 


organization's activities during 1976 and 1977, and preparations for celebrations 
marking 14 centuries since the birth of the last prophet, the great Mohammed bin 
Abdulla, The report finally dealt with Arab cultural development abroad, Arab cultural 
security and the national Arab nature of knowledge, 


The director-general of the Arab organization clarified in his report that the replies 
received by the organization from Arab countries on the recommendations of the first 
conference's reports which were compiled in three documents, are now submitted to the 
second conference, They are related to the cultural situation in the Arab world and 
the Arab countries! reports on the implemented recommendations of the first conference 
and their views of the five-year Arab cultural plan, He said that if these reports 
were unanimous in their faith regarding cultural development!s basic objectives, yet 
different in defining a clear strategy for serving these objectives, and agreed again 
regarding the implementation of these projects then differed on the quantity and the 
quality of such objectives, then such a situation confirms that the second conference 
is moving towards a strategy for Arab culture, which is a natural step, linking aspeets 
of cultural policy agreed upon in the first conference with transitional plans to be 
defined by this conference, 


At the end of his report, he referred to the significance of bilateral cultural 
agreements concluded between Arab countries and the organization's eagerness to 
obtain copies of such agreements, to prepare cultural projects and to organise its 
cultural relations with other countries, and to coordinate the Arab cultural movement 
within the framework of Arab development abroad,

AL-QADHDHAFI INTERVIEWED ON GOALS OF LIBYAN REVOLUTION 


LD130632 Tripoli JANA in English 1625 GMT 12 Feb 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, 12 February, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The television of the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiryah made an interview with the leader of the Great lst September 
Revolution and secretary general of the General People's Congress, Colonel Mutammar al- 
Qadhdhafi, during which he answered many questions on the day to day revolutionary movement 
in the new socialist democratic society. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi tackled several issues of interest to the Libyan Arab citizen and 
defined the task of the historic leadership of the Great lst of September Revolution after 
the next extraordinary session of the Arab people's congress when power will be separated 
from the revolution. In this respect Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that the major task 
of the revolutionary leadership is the leadership of the revolution which is more important 
and more comprehensive than any steps it took to achieve the people's power, 


The revolution, he said, is the handing over of power to the people through many changes 

and procedures, some adopted by the masses themselves, as for example the producers! seizure 
of the production establishment. The people's possession of arms will crown the process 

of establishing the power of the armed people through several steps, namely the general 
military training, the military conscription, and the militarization of cities and schools. 


On the role of the revolutionary committee in the transformation witnessed in the Jamahiriyah, 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that the revolution considers it is the right of the 
revolutionary forces and the masses to practise power themselves. All revolutionary forces 
are always the favorite choice being an example of the man who should be included in the 
secretariats of the people's congresses and committees. [sentence as received] 


He pointed out that the revolutionary committees differed from the traditional political 
party which is formed especially to practise power on behalf of the masses, while the 
revolutionary committees were not formed to serve such a purpose, but to enable the masses 
to assume power and continue to practise it until they got used to it. 


Ve. 14 Feb 79 ) NORTH AFRICA 


He stressed that from the very beginning, the Great lst of September Revolution aimed 
at creating a radical revolution where all the masses assume power, The workers brought 
together, with all other citizens, in people's congresses, form the people's power, 
They were chosen with the other members of the people's congresses and committees which 
carry out their resolutions in the people's congresses. Finally they havetheir own 
trade unions. 


They seized the production establishments in order to run them by themselves and obtain 
their own share of production. Thus no sector will control the people as power is for 
the people, a term which includes workers, students, farmers and the population, 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi pointed out the producers' revolution is sensible and con- 
vincing. It is organized revolutionary; therefore workers revolution need not be feared; 
the final power is that of the people, 


In the interview Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi tackled the issue of free trading as an 
aspect of exploitation which he said means consumption without production, It is seen 
as the act of buying and selling while raising the price of a commodity by the owner to 
make a profit. This so-called profit comes from the consumer's pocket, i.e., that of the 
people as a whole, in whose interest the abolition of free trade is. In this respect, 
Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that free trading as an aspect of exploitation 
will be completely eliminated and all exploiters transferred to production, 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhefi stated that the existing 700 cooperatives will be adapted 
to the new system and turned into a people's store selling to its owners at cost price 
without making a profit. They would obtain goods, the value of which is equivalent to 
the capital they have placed and such big stores will be administrated by a few people 
only. 


He also spoke about the fate of trade once free trading is abolished as an aspect of 
exploitations, affirming that the tradesmen are the sons of the people who care for 
production as much as all Libyans and their sons because production is ultimately for 
the benefit of the Libyan people, 


He announced that he will lead the tradesmen's revolution as he led the unionist free 
officers through the Great Ist of September Revolution and the workers through the 
workers! revolution, 


He added that the traders' sector could be involved in a revolutionary action for 
turning consumption into production, and achieving the victory of trade over exploita- 
tion by traders who could hold honourable positions in the new society. 


He explained that the situation of the tradesmen was not very good but a revolution 
could break out. He affirmed that when the time is ripe he would lead the tradesmen in 
a revolution, thus confirming to the people's sectors that the tradesmen are free and 
capable of a revolution for radical changes which could lead to an agricultural revolu- 
tion, for new land could be found where every tradesman could own a farm and reclaim 
its land. 


He affirmed that the Great 1st of September Revolution is responsible for the tradesmen. 
If the revolution is responsible for the Muslims of the Philippines, the Arab unity and 
the liberation of Palestine, shouldn't the tradesmen who are part of the Libyan people 
living in the same country and having suffered a society of exploitation in the past be 
given special concern.

In the speech, Colonel Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi dealt with the Jamahiriyah's import policy 
which, he said, should be sound, conscious and organized being governed by our political 

and economic relations with the countries of the world. He affirmed that such policy should 
take in consideration trade exchange and cooperation relations between the Jamahiriyah and 
the countries which contribute in the developing taking place in the Jamahiriyah in all 
fields. 


Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that trade in the past was not governed by any 
sound strategy as it was only based on exploitation, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi then referred to the definition of the farmer, who, he said is the 
person wholly devoted to farming, as those who work in other fields in addition to 
agriculture are not considered farmers. The farmer of the future will run his farm himself. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi then pointed out the role of women in the society of the revolution, 
He said that his views on women will be expressed in the third part of the Green Book. He 
added that some women were not occupying their proper place in the world. In one part they 
were considered as a commodity; in another they were not women at all. Both views were 
wrong. Such views can only be corrected by revolution and not by reformed resolutions 

such as giving women posts in social work as in some traditional countries. This change, 
he confirmed, will be through a real revolution by the women's masses. 


A revolutionary tool must be used to place them in the right position. Up till now women 
are not happy and do not feel their position and future as secure. He confirmed that the 
oppressed and persecuted women still exist. They are an ideal soil for breeding the 
revolution which only needs the revolutionary moving force, and this will be provided 

in the third part of the Green Book which will give a full explanation of the issue. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi referred to information as a tool of the revolution "as people know 
that the revolution is for them, that it is their life and future and as the people 

will now take over the information systems, it is logical that the people employ the 
information systems to serve the revolution and nothing else.
