[Interview with 'Abd al-Muhsin Abu Mayzar, Official Spokesman of the PLO, by

[Text] ‘Abd al-Muhsin Abu Mayzar, official spokesman of the PLO, says that the 
only alternative to the Camp David accords is the complete and final defeat of the 
accords and that the Palestinian revolution has succeeded in extracting a resolu- 
tion from the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) declaring that the Camp David 
accords are invalid and illegitimate insofar as the Palestine question is con- 
cerned.

[Question] The West European powers--France in particular--have tried several 
times to undertake initiatives to bring about peace in Lebanon. But they have 
failed. Why have they failed in your view? 


[Answer] Those powers have succumbed to Palestinian "intimidation and terrorism" 
and oil “blackmail," and have failed to take account of the steadfastness of the 
Christians in Lebanon and their determination to defend their freedom, security 
and dignity. I believe that our steadfastness and endurance have begun to make 
those nations feel that they have to review their positions and make sound and 
proper initiatives which would lead to the reestablishment of national sovereignty 
in all of Lebanon and to ending the domination imposed on the Lebanese. 


[Question] Do you believe that the Iraqi-Iranian war will have any particular 
effects on Lebanon, and if so what kind of effects? 


[Answer] I do not believe that the Iraqi-Iranian war will have any negative effects 
on Lebanon. 


[Question] What are the effects of the Syrian-Soviet treaty on Lebanon, in par- 
ticular, and the Middle East, in general? 


[Answer] The Syrian-Soviet treaty has formalized a situation that existed in 
effect before that. It has not added anything new to the political scene in the 
area, or at least this is what appears to us to be the case.

HEAVY INDUSTRIES PROJECT--The secretary of heavy industries concluded an agreement 
with an international company yesterday for construction of a main electrical 
distribution plant and auxiliary plants at the Muserata iron and steel complex, 
connection of the powerplant with 220-kilowatt high-tension wires, maintenance 
shops and a water purification plant for the complex' system. The secretary also 
ratified an agreement with an international company entrusted with the construction 
of a dessalinization plant for the iron and steel complex and is planning the 
training of national personnel to be in charge of the management and operation of 
the complex. [Text] [Paris BULLETIN QUOTIDIEN DE L'AGENCE JAMAHIRIYA-PRESSE in 
French 1 Feb 81 p 7] 11,464 





AFFORESTATION PROJECT--The afforestation project is well underway in the region 
of Amsaad on the eastern border of the republic. Producers from the Secretariat 
of Sport and citizens groups in Amsaad have participated in the project, which 
is the result of resolutions of People's Congresses concerning the conversion of 
the border area into forest so as to avoid any confrontation with the Egyptian 
Army. [Text] [Paris BULLETIN QUOTIDIEN DE L'AGENCE JAMAHIRIYA-PRESSE in French 
3 Feb 81 p 3) 11,464 


SAVINGS, INVESTMENT BANK ESTABLISHED--In accordance with the resolutions of the 
rank-and-file People's Congresses at their third regular session for the year 89/90 
following the death of the Prophet, that is, 1980-1981 on the Christian calendar, 
the General People's Congress has passed a law stipulating the establishment of a 
savings and investment bank. The law also provides for establishment of a Libyan 
profit-sharing company called the Savings and Investment Bank having its own 

legal status and attached to the Office of Secretary of Housing. Its main office 
will be in Tripoli. This bank may set up other branches in the country depending 
on the activities it is called upon to perform. The law defines the bank's pre- 
rogatives: support to construction and city planning activities, while encouraging 
savings and investment and granting terms aimed at furnishing the necessary housing. 
Within the framework of the plan and considering the country's general policy, the 
law stipulates that capital will total 100 million dinars. It also provides for 
eliminatior of the investment portion of the Libyan Industrial and Investment 

Bank and the dissolution of two national investment and land companies. Naturally, 
the assets of the land portion of the industrial bank and those of the companies 

in question must revert to the capital of the savings bank. Likewise, funds from 
the land banks needed by the public treasury and commercial banks must return to 
the Savings and Investment Bank, which must publish the documents of these banks 
equivalent to the value of the funds coming to it. [Text] [Paris BULLETIN QUOTI- 
DIEN DE L'AGENCE JAMAHIRIYA=-PRESSE in French 3 Feb 81 p 4] 11,464
