LIBYA

Further Reporting on Museveni Visit 


Minutes of Agreement Signed 
LD201336 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
20 Aug 86 


[Text] Staff Major “Abd al-Salam Ahmad Jallud and Ugandan 
President Yoweri Museveni at noon today attended the ceremony 
of signing the minutes of an agreement between the Great 
Jamahiriyah and Uganda in economic, technical, and investment 
cooperation. 


The minutes of the agreement were signed by the brother secre- 
tary of the General People’s Committee for Planning on the 
Libyan side, and Professor Mulema, the [Ugandan] finance 
minister. 


Minutes of an agreement in connection with cooperation in the 
political and cultural field and popular organization between the 
two friendly countries were also signed. The minutes of the 
agreement were signed by the brother secretary of the People’s 
Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison on the 
Libyan side and Foreign Minister Ibrahim Mukiibi on the Ugan- 
dan sid. 


Addresses Mini-Ministerial Meeting 
EA201757 Kampala Domestic Service in English 1400 GMT 
20 Aug 86 


[Excerpt] Reports from Tripoli say President Yoweri Museveni 
has held talks with his host, President Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
of Libya. According to the reports both sides agreed to strengthen 
relations between the two countries, especially in the field of 
agriculture. 


Addressing a mini-ministeria! meeting composed of members of 
the Libyan and Ugandan delegations at the Secretariat of the 
General Popular Congress in Tripoli, the president said the 
discussions held between the Libyan and Ugandan Governments 
during his visit are part and parcel of the organizational 
agreements provided for in the 10-point program of the NRM 
[National Resistance Movement} He disclosed that the two 
countries had identified many possible priorities, and stressed the 
need for the negotiations to be followed by experts who are able 
to translate the views and principles into reality for the benefit 
of their people. 


President Museveni appealed to African leaders ever to bear in 
mind the industrial, defensive, and inferiority concepts which can 
easily be demoralized by wrong ideologies. [sentence as heard] 
He spoke of the need for African countries to identify their real 
interests and avoid promoting interests of other countries. 
Uganda, he said, has already started fighting such ideologies by 
creating political schools for the mobilization of population com- 
mittees, so that they can know their own interests instead of 
others. 


The president told the meeting that the NRM took up arms to 
create a new era of a unified and dignified life, tampered with by 
past regimes. The people’s human, political, and economic poten- 
tials were not exploited for the benefit of the nation and Africa 
as a whole. The revolution in Uganda, therefore, he declared, was 
launched to create a new chance in tackling the south-to-south 
cooperation among the African states. He stated that the south- 
to-south cooperation is not a new idea in Africa, but the people 
only lacked the political will to follow it up, thus leading to 
irrational, expensive, and nonbeneficial arrangements. 


During the same meeting the Libyan Staff Major ‘Abd al-Salam 
Ahmad Jallud expressed the hope that since Libya and Uganda 
share the same aspirations, the economic strength of the two 
countries will grow stronger, adding that with the political and 
economic will the available resources of both countries have the 
potential for development. He urged both sides to create a 
feasibility study to view the economic stand of the two countries 
with the aim of laying the foundation for long-term plans.

Joint Communique 
LD201946 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1855 GMT 20 Aug 86 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 Aug (JANA) — A joint communique was 
released today both in Tripoli and Kampala following the visit of 
the Ugandan President Yoweri Museveni to the Great Jamahir- 
iyah. Here is the text of this communique: 


At the invitation of the brother, Colonel Mu‘ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great al-Fatih Revolution, President 
Yoweri Museveni paid an official visit to the great Socialist 
People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in the period from 13 to 15 
Dhu al-Hijjah 1395 corresponding to 18 to 20 August 1986. 


The Ugandan president and his accompanying delegation were 
accorded a warm welcome which expressed the depth of the 
fraternal ties which link the two fraternal peoples. 


During this visit President Yoweri Museveni laid a wreath at the 
monument that commemorates that martyrs of the battle of 
Al-Hani. 


President Yoweri Museveni during this visit had cordial and 
fraternal meetings and talks with Brother Col al-Qadhdhafi, 
leader of the Great al-Fatih Revolution, dealing with a number 
of local and international issues, particularly the struggle against 
colonialism, racism, and Zionism. The Brother Col al-Qadhdhafi 
acquainted President Yoweri Museveni with the consequences of 
the savage, barbaric, and terrorist aggression to which the Great 
Jamahiriyah was exposed by the imperialist U.S. Administration 
and the NATO alliance, noting the military policy of the U.S. 
Administration, and the policy of domination and hegemony it 
pursues — two things that work for increasing world tension 
especially in Africa and the Mediterranean. 


President Yoweri Museveni strongly condemned the aggressive 
actions of the U.S. Administration against the Jamahiriyah and 
he described them as being one aspect of the policy of state- 
sponsered terrorism.

He also expressed the solidarity of the Ugandan leadership and 
people with the fraternal people of the Jamahiriyah in their 
standing up to the savage U.S. aggression and the economic 
blockade imposed on the Jamahiriyah, and also the right of the 
Libyan Arab people to defend their land with all available means, 
and to defend their rights to the Gulf of Sidra in accordance with 
the declaration released by the Jamahiriyah on this gulf in 1973. 


President Yoweri Museveni has also highly appreciated the spirit 
of bravery and sacrifice showed by the Libyan Arab people in 
standing up to aggression, under the leadership of the Great 
al-Fatih Revolution led by Col Qadhdhafi who was personally a 
target of this aggression. 


The Ugandan president stressed that the barbaric attack against 
the Jamahiriyah was a criminal act, a violation of laws and ethics, 
and constituted a great threat to international peace and security. 


On his part, the brother colonel greeted the struggle of the 
Ugandan people under the leadership of the resistance movement 
which had eliminated despotism and put an end to the mass 
killing of the sons of the people of Uganda. 


During the visit, there were several discussions and meetings at 
a high level between the delegations of the two countries, cover- 
ing bilateral relations as well as the African, Arab, and interna- 
tional situation. Taking part in them on the Ugandan side 
were: Foreign Minister Ibrahim Mukiibi, Finance Minister 
Ponsiano Mulema, and Energy Minister Andrew Kayiira. On the 
Libyan side, taking part were: Brother Qasim Muhammad 
Sharlala, secretary of the General People’s Committee for the 
Treasury, Brother Fawzi Ahmad al-Shakshuki, secretary of the 
General People’s Committee for Planning, and Brother Kamil 
Hasan al-Maqhur, [sometimes called Al-Mansur, but here 
transmitted as Al-Maqhur, his original surname] secretary of the 
General People’s Committee for Foreign Liaison. 


After reviewing the current situation in the African Continent, 
the two sides stressed their firm determination to work for the 
total liberation of Africa and the commitment to the achievment 
of African unity in accordance with the OAU Charter and its 
humanitarian principles. In this context, the two sides strongly 
condemned imperialist interference in the African Continent, 
denounced foreign presence in it, and expressed their conviction 
that defending the African Continent cannot be achieved except 
by African peoples themselves. 


Regarding South Africa, the two sides condemned the hated 
racist regime which is considered a crime against humanity as a 
whole, and they declared their determination to use all cap- 
abilities to destroy this plague, bring forth the day of final victory, 
end of apartheid, and the declaration of a democratic state in 
South Africa. 


They also stressed their determination to support African liber- 
ation movements materially and morally. Concerning Namibia, 
the two sides reaffirmed their full support for the just struggle of 
the Namibian people under the leadership of the SWAPO, and 
are asking the United Nations, and the Security Council in
