LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES CONFEDERATION PRIME MINISTER 
JNO40615Y Damascus Domestic Service in Arabic 0415 qmT 4 Jan 78 JN 


[Text] Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi received Ahmad al-Khatib, the prime minister 
of the Confederation of Arab Republics, in Tripoli yesterday. 


In a statement to the SANA Tripoli correspondent foilowing the meeting, Al-Khatib said 
that he conveyed President aleAsad's greetings to Brother Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and to 
the Libyan people, Al-Khatib said that he told the Libyan leader that President al-Asad 


regards Libya's security, prosperity and progress just as he regards the security, prosperity 


and progress of Syria. He said that President al-Asad considers them as one cause, 


Al-Khatib said that he discussed with the Libyan leader matters pertaining to the confedera- 


tion in light of the new reality, which has dictated his transfer to Tripoli following 
As-Sadat's direct dealing with the Zionist enemy and his relinquishment of his commitments 
toward the confederation and the Arab nation and its basic issue--the Palestinian cause, 
He also discussed the issue of completing the formation of the confederation government, 
opening the confederation National Assembly, defining the confederation budget and insuring 
all that is necessary for the confederation's progress, 


Al-Khatib said that the Libyan leader expressed his high admiration for the heroic stcad- 
fastness of the Syrian people under President Hafiz al-Asad vis-a-vis the enemy's plot 
against the Arab nation's fateful issues,
