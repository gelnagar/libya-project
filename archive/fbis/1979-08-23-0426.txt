LIBYA

AT-TURAYKT RETURNS FROM LAGOS CONFERENCE 
LD221704 Tripoli JANA in English 1432 GMT 22 Aug 79 LD 


(Text] Tripoli, Aug 22, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Foreign Secretary Dr 'Ali 'Abd al-Salam 
at-Turayki arrived home last night, having attended the conference on national recon- 
ciliation in Chad which wound up in Lagos at noon yesterday, with an agreement on 
national reconciliation in Chad. 


Dr at-Turayki asserted that the Socialist People!s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, having stood 
by the Chadian people as they struggled for independence and the abolition of imperialism, 
would maintain the same stand while they build their country. Speaking at the conference's 
closing session, Dr at-Turayki pointed out that the agreement reached was not the victory 
of a party over the other but that of the whole Chadian people, Africa and African will. 
He said that Africa is represented by Chad!s neighbouring countries. He stressed that 
Africa was capable of solving its own problems and assume its full responsibilities and 
that this should serve as an example and a lesson to the enemy of Africa: imperialism. 


He remarked that the conference for national reconciliation in Chad has proved that when 
wmited, Africa was capable of achieving many of its goals. He said: "It was the whole 
Chadian people, all its sectors and strata, which was triumphant today after long years 
of misery and torture. The Chadian people with its unified forces can build Africa, 
side by side with its brethren, a united, strong Africa liberated from all forms of 
military, political and economic colonialism." He pointed out that a strong Chad would 
help the African struggle against racism and imperialism in the continent and against 
racist Zionism and world imperialism, 


Dr at-Turayki stressed that what was achieved so far was only a beginning. He called 
upon the Chadian people to take a united stand and take care, for more difficult tasks 
were awaiting them. 


ALGERIA'S YAHIAOUIL RECEIVED BY AL-QADHDHAFI, DEPARTS 
LD221829 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 22 Aug 79 LD 


(Text ] The commander of the revolution yesterday evening received Mohamed Salah Yahiaoui, 
member of the Political Bureau and coordinator of the National Liberation Front Party 

{of Algeria] who arrived in Tripoli yesterday evening where he was welcomed by Maj 
al-Khuwayzidi al-Humaydi, the Algerian ambassador to the Socialist People's Libyan 
Jamahiriyah, and a representative of the Protocol Department at the Foreign Secretariat. 


Brother Yahiaoui left Tripoli this morning. He said to a JANA correspoment that he was 
happy to visit the Jamahiriyah, whichis one of the strongholds of struggle and a 
revolutionary base.
