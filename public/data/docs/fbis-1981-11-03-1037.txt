PALESTINIAN RALLY MARKS BALFOUR DECLARATION 


NCO33759 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 3 Nov 81 


[Text] The Palestinian Arab masses last night in Tripoli held a popular rally to mark 
the 64th anniversary of the ill-omened Balfour declaration. 


Speaking on the occasion, the PLO representative in the Jamairiyah aff!rmed that the 
Palestinian Arab people are determined to oppose all imperialist and Ziouist plots and 

to maintain the struggle for repatriation and for establishment of their own state in 
their homeland. He stressed that the Palestirian revolution, which has attained with 

the blood of martyrs many victories and achievements, cannot give up these victories under 
any circumstances. 


The PLO representative in the Jamahiriyah declared that the PLO will not accept any capitu- 
lationist bargainings or initiatives of any kind. He affirmed that the Palestinian people 
definitely reject any bargaining over their legitimate rights to return to their land. 


The brother secretary general of the Permanent Secretariat o; the Arab People's Congress 
also spoke on the occasion. He noted that the anniversary comes when the Arab nation is 
continuing to resist the ugliest forms of colonialist and Zionist challenges aimed at 

the progressive forces in the Arab homeland. He said: By marking the Balfour declaration 
today, we do not mean to review the sacrifices or lament our lost glories, but to learn 

a lesson so that we can resist all Zionist and imperialist plots concocted against the 
revolutionary and progressive forces in the Arab homeland. 


PALESTINIAN LEADER JIBRIL ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 


LDO10105 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2300 GMT 31 Oct 81 


[Text] Secretary general of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine-General 
Command, brother Ahmad Jibril, arrived in Tripoli Saturday evening. In a statement to JANA, 
he said his visit's purpose is to study the current Arab situation and new developments 
following the Egyptian Arab people's execution of the traitor As-Sadat.
