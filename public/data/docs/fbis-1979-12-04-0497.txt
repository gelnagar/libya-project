AL-QADHDHAFI WARNS OF BAN ON OIL EXPORTS TO U.S. 
NCO31713 Beirut Domestic Service in Arabic 1630 GMT 3 Dec 79 NC 


[Text] In an interview published in an Italian newspaper, Libyan leader Col Mu!ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi has said that he might ban oil exports to the United States if his country's 
assets in the United States are frozen. 


STUDENTS DEMONSTRATE IN AS=SUWANI 2 DEC 
LDO31536 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1455 GMT 3 Dec 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--The town of As-Suwani yesterday morning witnessed a huge student demon 
stration in which students from As-Suwani preparatory school and the As-Suwani teacher- 
training school for girls took part. The demonstrators roamed the streets of the town 
in solidarity with the Palestinian Arab people and the Iranian people and their Islamic 
Revolution. The participants in the march issued a statement stressing their solidarity 
with the Iranian people in facing U.S. imperialism, 


MAURITANIAN PRIME MINISTER ARRIVES FOR TALKS 1 DEC 
LDO11840 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabie 1715 GMT 1 Dec 79 LD 


[Text] Mohamed Khouna Ould Heydalla, first deputy chairman of the Military Committee of 
National Salvation and prime minister of Islamic Republic of Mauritania, arrived in 
Tripoli this evening on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, 
during which he will hold talks on the promotion of the fraternal relations and cooper- 
ation between the two fraternal peoples in various fields. The Mauritanian guest was 
accorded a great welcome at Tripoli International Airport where Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam 
Ahmad Jallud was at the head of those who came to welcome him. The following also took 
part in the welcome: members of the General People's Committee, the Mauritanian ambas- 
sador to the Jamahiriyah, the Jamahiriyah ambassador to Mauritania and directors of 
departments at the Foreign Secretariat. 


Mohamed Khouna Ould Heydalla made a statement to the JANA reporter in which he expressed 
happiness at his visit to Libya. He said that the visit will be a good opportunity to 
learn about the achievements of the Great 1 September Revolution. He also expressed the 
hope that the visit will contribute to strengthen the ties of fraternity and relations 
of cooperation between the two fraternal peoples, bring the unity of the Arab nation 
closer and defend our Islamic faith. 


The first deputy chairman of the Military Committee of National Salvation and prime 
minister is accompanied by a delegation including Moulaye Ould Boukaraiss, member of 
the Military Committee of National Salvation and planning minister, and Foreigm Minister 
Ahmedou Ould Abdallah,
