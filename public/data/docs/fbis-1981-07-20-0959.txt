AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS MESSAGE TO ARAB LEADERS 


LD141838 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 19 Jul 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Jul (JANA)--The brother leader has sent the following message to the 
Arab kings and presidents: 


It is a bad occasion which calls for me to address you once again in connection with the 
same issue, as it is not appropriate on the part of the Americans and the Israelis to go 
even further in their humilitation and contempt of your majesties, your excellencies and 
your highnesses by directly destroying the Arab existence with U.S. weapons--as is happening 
today in Lebanon where wholesale massacres of Lebanese Arabs and Palestinians are carried 
out at the hands of Israel, the spoiled foster child of America, at the expense of the 
Arabs: It is no longer appropriate on the part of the Americans and the Israelis to be 
inconsiderate to your majesties, your excellencies and your highnesses, despite your 
unbounded friendship with America and despite the millions of barrels of oil which the 
generous Arabs have been presenting to Israel via America andas their contribution toward 
the great Israeli war effort. The Americans and the Israelis should have taken into 
consideration the value of the friendship and the density of the oil which forms a link 
between them and your majesties, your excellencies and your highnesses. 


I hope you will reexamine your all: ance with the enemy of the Arab nation and the 
cheap and deep-flowing supplies of oil which only meets the requirements of the enemy, 
because when all the scientific calculations are completed you do not need them. 


Forgive me if my message resembles an irresponsible article. This is because I do not 

carry the responsibility of a majesty, nor an excellency or a highness, and because I 

am not responsible to the United States, -s I am not an employee of the United States nor 

do I have the rank of a king, president or prince in the Arab region. Secondly, this is 
because of a lack of responsibility in the Arab homeland; every day that passes by affirms 
that there is no person responsible for the Arab dignity or principles except the Palestinian 
fedayeen. I would like to stress this: the fedayeen alone. 


I would like to give you the glad tidings that the Arab revolutionaries will be compelled 
in the end to make a serious decision. If the Arab world is destined to sink and go under 
then it should do so by virtue of our own decision and not by a decision by our enemies. 
God be my witness that I have given warning, 


[Signed] Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 


LEBANESE NATIONAL MOVEMENT LEADERS DEPART 18 JUL 


LD181012 Tripoli JANA in English 0815 GMT 18 Jul 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 July (JANA)--Walid Jumblatt, head of the Political Council of the 
Lebanese Nationalist Movement and chairman of the Socialist Progressive Lebanese Party, 
and Muhsin Ibrahim, executive secretary general of the Lebanese Nationalist Movement, left 
Tripoli yesterday on their way to Algiers as part of the mission assigned to them by the 
leader with the aim of mobilizing Arab efforts against Israel.

MAURITANTA 


FOREIGN AFFAIRS MINISTRY CONDEMNS ISRAELI ACTIONS 


AB171350 Paris AFP in French 1041 GMT 17 Jul 81 


[Text] Nouakchott, 17 Jul (AFP)--In a statement published by the Mauritanian Ministry 
of Foreign Affairs in Nouakchott on Friday, the Mauritanian Government strongly condemns 
Israel's thoughtless and irresponsible acts in southern Lebanon. 


The communique adds: It is obvious that true to its usual policy of blackmail, intimidation 
and aggression, which has so far gone unpunished, the Zionist entity hoped, on one hand, 

to undermine the achievements of all the efforts made--and particularly those of the 
quadripartite Arab committee--toward establishing national harmony and unity in southern 
Lebanon, and, on the other hand, after its heinous crime involving the Tammuz nuclear 

plant, to prove to the Arab nation and international public opinion that it was still 
determined to use brute force to accomplish its aims of supremacy and domination. 


The government, which feels that the threat of war in that area is greater than ever, 

calls on international public opinion and the United Nations to assume their responsibilities 
in order to rapidly put an end to the escalation of violence and hate which Israel is 
inflicting upon the entire Middle East and for which it alone shall assume full 
responsibility.
