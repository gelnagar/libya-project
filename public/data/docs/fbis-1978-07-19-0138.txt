LIBYA

MALTA, LIBYA SIGN JOINT FISHING AGREEMENT 


LD181808Y Tripoli JANA in English 1441 GMT 18 Jul 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 July, JANA--A Maltese-Libyan agreement for the establishment of 
a joint fishing company was signed here yesterday. Mr Muhammad Azazarruq Rajab, 
treasury secretary, signed the agreement on behalf of the Jamahiriyah. Mr. Danny 
Cremona, the Maltese minister of industry. fisheries and agriculture, signed the 
agreement for Malta. 


Both sides signed a draft agreement with the aim of promoting bilateral relations in 
the fields of commerce, agriculture and industry and strengthening cooperation in 
connection with the establishment of joint companies. Mr Cremona and his accompanying 
delegation left Tripoli last night, winding up their short visit to the Jamahiriyah. 
They were seen off at Tripoli International Airport by Mr. Rajab and Mr. Maurice Branou, 
the Maltese ambassador to the Jamahiriyah.
