AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES RALLY IN MALTA 2 JULY 
LD022238Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 2 Jul 78 ip 


[Text] This evening Col Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader ot the Great 1 September Revolution, 
addressed a major popular rally of Maltese attended by Dom Mitoff, prime minister and 
leader of the Maltese Socialist Labor Party, and by Joseph Cassar, deputy prime minister. 
The rally was held about 12 kilometers from the capital of Valetta. Amid acclaim for the 

1 September Revolution and its leader and for Maltese-Libyan Arab friendship, Colonel 
ai-Qadhdhafi delivered his address. 


After greeting the Maltese masses and Prime Minister Dom Mintoff, he said: I would like 
to say that we are on the side of the people of Malta until they wrest their freedom, 

We are with you until you achieve the evacuation [of the British bases] and until the 
freedom of the people of Malta is attained. We are with the Maltese people in overcoming 
their economic problems. 


The leader of the revolution stressed that this stance toward the Maltese pecple is firm 
and strategic and cannot change. He emphasized that the security of Libya is tied to the 
security of Malta, and vice versa. 


He pointed out that the Maltese Socialist Labor Party wants to achieve gains for all 
Maltese people. It is on this basis that we give our support to the party, because we 
want freedom for all Maltese and we back the gains of all Maltese, 


He added: I agree with Dom Mintoff when he says that Europeans are alien to Malta and 
stresses that Malta is not European. In this connection he referred to what Malta has 
endured at the hands of European colonialists, to its use by them as a bridge to achieve 
their colonial ambitions in North Africa and to their coritempt and neglect of the people 
of Malta and their rights. He emphasized tiiat the people of Malta are the people of 
Malta and will always remain so.

He explained to the Maltese masses: We do not want to set up bases in Malta, neither do 
we wish to use Malta as a bridge as the Europeans had used it. What we want is the 
freedom and neutrality of Malta and its liberation from foreign bases. 


The leader of the revolution declared the Jamahiriyah's unqualified and unlimited support 
for the consolidation of the independence and neutrality of Malta and the liberation of 
its economy from foreign domination and its territory from foreign bases. He said: 

We are ready to defend, by your side, your gains. It is our duty to cooperate with you 
and to help you until your economy and your territory are liberated from foreign imperi- 
alist bases and until this region is transformed into an island of security and peace, 


Leader of the revolution Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi, referring to the historic ties between 
the Maltese and Arab peoples, said: The Phoenicians were your and our forefathers, 

and it was they who established the great civilization in the Mediterranean basin. For 
this reason, Malta historically has not been in isolation. 


Addressing the Maltese masses, he said: You are part of the great east and you must take 
pride in your ancestors. He explained that the Romans learned their civilization from 
the Phoenicians, Therefore, we have been together in the past, are together in the 
present and will continue to be together in the future, 


After the speech Dom Mintoff thanked the 1 September Revolution for its brave stance, 
its support and help for all the oppressed and its alliance with those struggling for 
freedom. 


Joseph Cassar, Maltese deputy prime minister, also spoke at the popular rally explaining 
the role of the Maltese Socialist Labor Party and its achievements for the people of 
Malta. 


In his address, Dom Mintoff, prime minister and leader of the Maltese Socialist Labor 
Party, welcomed leader of the revolution Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi and praised the strong 
relations between the Jamahiriyah and Malta. He explained the role of his party in this 
respect. He also explained the efforts of the Maltese Government to improve the living 
standard of all sections of the people of Maita and the developments through which Malta 
has passed in all epochs. 


Mintoff stressed the importance of the evacuation of the British imperialist bases from 
Malta, saying these bases would be wound up and the full freedom of the people of Malta 
achieved before the end. of next year. 


He said Malta has chosen the road of freedom, is nonalined and will work with friendly 
states to make the Mediterranean a "lake of peace." He called on the Maltese people to 
work for protecting and developing Malta and its future. In this connection he paid 
tribute to the help being given by the Jamahiriyah to the friendly people of Malta in 
achieving their progress and prosperity. 


CHADIAN FOREIGN MINISTER ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 


LDO21948Y Tripoli Voice cf the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 2 Jul 78 Lb 


[Text] The Chadian foreign minister arrived in Tripoli this afternoon to lead his 
government's delegation to the reconciliation talks which will be resumed in Tripoli 
tomorrow. 


The Chadian minister was welcomed by Muhammad at-Tahir al-Mahjub, secretary of labor and 
civil service. The Chadian delegation includes four ministers and some advisers at the 
Chadian Foreign Ministry.
