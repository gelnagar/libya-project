[Text] The 41st ordinary session of the OAU Foreign Ministers

INTER-AFRICAN AFFAIRS 

In his message, the comrade secretary general said that in order 
to contain the drought problem in Africa and to look for a lasting 
solution, African countries should implement the Lagos plan of 
action by taking individual and joint action. He added that 
African countries must make great efforts to contain their prob- 
lems. He stressed that the problem had become so complex, both 
quantitatively and qualitatively, that it required international 
cooperation and support. In his message, our comrade rev- 
olutionary leader noted that, although the actions taken by the 
international community are encouraging, some countries are 
nevertheless trying to use the drought and famine problem as a 
political weapon in pursuit of their political objectives. To curb 
this, African countries should further strengthen their cooper- 
ation.
