LIBYA

JALLUD RECEIVES LEBANESE NATIONALIST MOVEMENT DELEGATION 
LD24231L4Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 24 May 78 LD 
{[Summarv] Tripoli--Maj 'Abd as-Salam Jallud this morning received a delegation of the 
Lebanese Nationalist Movement led by Walid Junblatt. They reviewed the Arab situation 
in general and the situation in Lebanon in particular, They also discussed the role 
of the Lebanese Nationalist Movement in the stage through which the Arab nation is 
passing.
