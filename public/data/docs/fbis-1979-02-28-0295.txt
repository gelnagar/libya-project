LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI, JABIR ATTEND MISSILE TESTS 25 FEB 


LD252122 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1855 GMT 25 Feb 79 LD 


(Text] Tripoli, 25 February--Brothers Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and Brig Gen Abu Bakr 
Yunir Jabir attended this morning the launching of volleys of missiles capable of crossing 
borders, carried out by our armed forces at the Al-Hamadah al-Hamra', The volleys of 
missiles hit their designated targets, which were hundreds of kilometers away from the 
launching site, and destroyed them completely--something which confirms our armed forces! 
capability to use advanced weapons with high skill. 


V. 28 Feb 79 NORTH AFRICA 


AUSTRIAN AMBASSADOR ASKED TO EXPLAIN COOPERATION WITH ISRAEL 


LD271638 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 27 Feb 79 LD 


[Text] The Libyan Foreign Secretariat has summoned the Austrian ambassador to ask 
him for a clarification regarding the reports about Austria's intention to manufacture 
a military aircraft in partnership with the Zionist racist enemy. 


The Foreign Secretariat made clear to the Austrian ambassador the consequences to 
Arab-Austrian relations resulting from Austria's cooperation with the Zionist enemy. 


AT-TURAYKI RETURNS FROM ETHIOPIA; COOPERATION DISCUSSED 

LD272050 Tripoli JANA in English 1815 GMT 27 Feb 79 LD 

[Text] Tripoli, February 27, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Foreign Secretary Dr. 'Ali ‘Abd 
as-Salam at-Turayki returned to Tripoli this afternoon after he led the Jamahiriyah's 
delegation to the foreign ministers' meetings of the 32nd regular session of the 


Organization of African Unity which opened in Nairobi lately. 


Dr. at-Turayki also visited Ethiopia where he conducted talks with officials there 
on the reinforcement of relations between the two countries in all fields. 


Our correspondent reported that the Libyan Arab-Ethiopian joint committee has recently 
met in Addis Ababa and agreed on founding joint ventures in the fields of agriculture, 


metallurgy, and sea transport. 


The committee also drew up a programme of cultural and trade exchange between the two 
countries during 1979.
