Summary (AI): A declassified Washington Post article (24 April 1986) reprinted in the CIA reading room says Qaddafi was living in fear and vulnerable to a coup, and that the Soviets preferred his No. 2, Abdul Salam Jalloud, and had shipped over $20 billion in military hardware to Libya. It is about Soviet-Libyan relations and Libyan leadership politics, with no Tripoli sites named.
• The article says the Soviets had shipped more than $20 billion worth of military hardware to Libya.
• A top-secret CIA analysis quoted in the article says Qaddafi had almost certainly not softened his basic distrust of the Soviets.
• The article says Libya had been turned into a forward logistical base for Soviet control of North Africa.
• The article says Qaddafi and Jalloud were in dispute over Soviet ties, with Jalloud more responsible for the Moscow-armed confrontationist state.
