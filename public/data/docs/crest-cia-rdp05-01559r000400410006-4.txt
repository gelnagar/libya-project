DPA: AL-QADHDHAFI ON ECONOMIC SANCTIONS, TERRORIST SQUADS 

LD1609 10 

HAMBURG DPA IN GERMAN 0219 GMT 16 JAN 86 

((TEXT)) HAMBURG, 16 JAN (DPA) -~ THE LIBYAN REVOLUTIONARY 
LEADER, COLONEL AL-QAHDHDAFI, HAS WARNED THE EUROPEANS ABOUT TAKING 
PART IN THE ECONOMIC SANCTIONS IMPOSED ON HIS COUNTRY BY THE 
UNITED STATES. IN AN INTERVIEW WITH THE ARD ((FIRST GERMAN 
TELEVISION -- FBIS)) PROGRAM "TAGESTHEMEN," AL-QADHDHAFI SAID ON 
WEDNESDAY NIGHT THAT IF THEY DID “THEN THEY WOULD PUNISH THEMSELVES, 
BECAUSE THEY HAVE MAJOR INTERESTS IN LIBYA." 

IN RESPONSE TO A QUESTION ON WHETHER SEVERAL THOUSAND 
GERMANS CURRENTLY WORKING IN THE COUNTRY WOULD BE ALLOWED TO 
LEAVE AT ANY TIME, AL-QADHDHAFI SAID THAT THEY COULD OF COURSE DO 
THAT. BUT GERMAN INTERESTS WOULD SUFFER, HE ADDED. 

ABOUT THE THREATENED USE OF LIBYAN TERRORIST GROUPS IN THE 
UNITED STATES AL-QAHDHDAFI SAID: "IF THE UNITED STATES ATTACKS US 
WE WILL DEFEND OURSELVES. BUT SINCE WE ARE A SMALL COUNTRY AND 
CANNOT REACH THE UNITED STATEES, WE WILL SEND PEOPLE THERE WHO 
ARE PREPARED TO SACRIFICE THEMSELVES." IN HIS DEFENSE, AL-QADHDHAFI 
CITED THE INTERNATIONAL RIGHT TO DEFEND ONESELF. "WE WILL DESTROY 
THE UNITED STATES FROM WITHIN," HE SAID. 

COMMENTING ON THE REPORTS ABOUT THE EXISTENCE OF TRAINING 
CAMPS FOR PALESTINIAN COMMANDOS IN LIBYA, AL-QAHDHAFI SAID, 

"THESE CAMPS ARE NOT IN LIBYA ‘AT THE MOMENT'." BUT HE ADDED THAT 
IF THE "LIBERATION OF PALESTINE" MADE IT NECESSARY TO ESTABLISH 
THEM IN LIBYA, THEN IT WOULD BE "OUR NATIONAL DUTY" T DO THAT. 

THE INTERVIEW, GIVEN IN TRIPOLI, BEGAN WITH THE QUESTION ON 
WHETHER AL-QADHDHAFI REGRETTED THE BLOODY ATTACKS ON ROME AND 
VIENNA AIRPORTS. THE LIBYAN HEAD OF STATE AVOIDED GIVING A STRAIGHT 
ANSWER BY SAYING THAT IN THAT CASE OTHER BLOODBATHS WOULD ALSO 
HAVE TO BE REGRETTED AND REFERRED, AMONG OTHERS, TO U.S. 
INTERVENTION IN GRNEADA, SOUTH AFRICA, AND THE MASSACRE AT 
PALESTINIAN CAMPS IN BEIRUT.
