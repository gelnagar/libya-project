LIBYA

REPORTAGE ON VISIT BY, TALKS WITH CHAD'S GOUKOUNI 


2 Jan Arrival 
LD022020 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 2 Jan 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 2 Jan (JANA)--Brother Goukouni Oueddei, president of the Transitional 
National Union Government and head of state of Chad, arrived this evening in Tripoli 

on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. Brother Goukouni was 
received at Tripoli airport by Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud, members of the secretariat 
of General People's Congress, members of the General People's Committee, officers of the 
armed forces and members of the diplomatic corps in Libya. 


JANA Interview 
LD031004 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0936 GMT 3 Jan 81 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 3 Jan (JANA)--Goukouni Oueddei, chairman of the Transitional National 
Government, and president of state of Chad has arrived in Tripoli last night on a visit 
to Jamahiriyah. 


President Goukouni Oueddei gave an interview to JANA, in which he greatly praised Jamahiriyah's 
efforts for the security, stability and unity of the Chadian people and said: 


"This visit comes within the framework of consultations without brothers in Jamahiriyah, 
with which we are linked by religion, geography and struggle against imperialism and 
against those who seek to return back the wheel of ' story, following the triumph of 

the Chadians people's revolution, and their success in achieving the unity and safety 

of their territory." 


He said that following the triumph of the revolution and the defeat of the remnants of 
rebel Habre, we appeal to the Libyan Arab people, who gallantly stood by us in our IlIciy 
struggle, to continue their support and aid to the Chadian people to enable them to 
develop their country and reconstruct it. We do not suspect for a moment that the Libyan 
Arab people will fail to respond to this appeal, as we know about them and their leaders’ 
radical progressive stands in the cause of mankind and the dignity of men everywhere, 
particularly in the African Continent. 


President Goukouni stressed that continuation of struggle against reaction and that the 
Chadian people will not rest until all dens of reaction which seek to bring back chaos 

to Chad and dissipate its unity, are completely eradicated and annihilated in Africa. 
President Goukouni Oueddei condemned the reationary ruling regimes in Africa, particularly 
those of Sudan and Egypt, who seek to destroy and tear the unity of the Chadian peopie 
through their support to rebel Habre, affirming that the Chadian people possess the full 
capacity and capability to defeat such attempt and actions and will continue to build a 
progressive, radical state in Chad. 


Goukouni Address 


LDO31536 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 3 Jan 81 


[Excerpts] The General People's Congress, a gathering of representative of people's 
congresses, trade unions, federations and associations, began its sixth session at 1000 
this morning at the People's Hall in Tripoli, to consider the decisions of the basic 
people's congresses in their 1980 sessions. 


V. 5 Jan 81 NORTH AFRICA 


The chairman of the Transitional National Union Government and president of Chad, 
Goukouni Oueddei, attended the opening meeting of the General People's Congress. Presi- 
dent Goukouni Oueddi addressed the congress and expressed his great joy and that of the 
accompanying delegation at being able to attend the opening of the General People's 
Congress. He conveyed the greetings of the people cf Chad to the fraternal Libyan people 
and to the leader of their sreat revolution. 


He said: Our attendance at this congress, which embodies the direct popular authority in 
the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, expressed the depth of solidarity between 
our two fraternal peoples and the depth of our cultural, spiritual and geographical re- 
lations in addition to our joint struggle against colonialism and reaction. He said: This 
struggle went on for long years and was crowned with the solidarity and unity of the two 
revolutions and two peoples, who often were exposed to occupation, repression and 
exploitation. 


The chairman of the Transitional National Union Government and president of Chad praised 
the material and moral support extended by the people of the Jamahiriyah to the fraternal 
people of Chad in their long struggle against colonialism and domination. He said: During 
the recert war through which we lived, the biggest, most barbaric and most devastating 

war, the Jamahiriyah was first to stand by our stand from the African states. He added 
that the support of the Jamahiriyah led to the OAU decision to support the provisional 

Chad Government in its quest for peace and security in Chad. 


Goukouni Oueddi denounced the rebel Habre and his separatist supporters. He stressed that 
the victory achieved by the legitimate government against the rebels emobided the aspira- 
tions of the people of Chad and their revolution. He also strongly denounced the colonial, 
Zionist and reactionary plots aimed against the freedom of the people of Chad. He pointed 
out that the events which occurred in occupied Palestine and in South Africa were pointed 
out that there was firm evidence of the backing given by the colonial, African and re- 
actionary Arab states to Habre's mutinous group. He said that during the battles which 

our people waged against rebel Habre and his group, various weapons, ammunition and 
materials were captured as well as mercenaries from the As-Sadat and Numaryi regimes, and 
also from the Israeli enemy, who had passed through Sudanese territory. President Goukouni 
accused these reactionary agent regimes of trying to establish a base for world colonialism 
against the peoples struggling for their freedom. He said that these regimes, in the 
service of the agent Habre, did not stop in their attempts and maneuvres to cast doubt 
against the legitimacy of the government in an attempt to impose Habre upon the people 

of Chad. 


President Goukouni Oueddi said that we support the right of the African peoples to self- 
determination and we want the best relations with these peoples, especially our fraternal 
neighbors, within the framework of mutual respect. President Goukouni stressed that the 
people of Chad will not allow the colonial forces and their agents to interfere in the in- 
ternal affairs of the people of Chad. He said that we paid dearly for the price of freedom 
and peace, and the martyrs who fell are the best testimony of this, and we cannot abandon 
the enormous sacrifices we made. We also will not allow agents like Hissein Habre to be 
masters over the people of Chad. He said that the people of Chad, despite the harsh 
conditions imposed upon them as a result of the dastardly intrigue, have come out strong 
and united and more determined to maintain their unity, safety and gains. He said that 

the people of Chad will pursue the international criminal Hissein Habre, who left behind 
many corpses, and will inflict on him the punishment he deserves for the crimes he committed 
against the people of Chad, especially during the past 9 months.
