Fundamentalists Reported Fleeing Country 

90P40054 London AL-DUSTUR in Arabic 
21 May 90 p 7 

[Text] Libyan circles have stated that a number of 
Libyan fundamentalists have tried in the last few weeks 
to get out of Libya and into the Arab Maghreb countries. 
These circles indicated that the Libyan authorities may 
have conducted a campaign of arrests among them.
