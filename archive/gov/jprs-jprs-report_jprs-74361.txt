Just one year has elapsed since the kidnapping of Emam Musa Sadr, the lead-

his message to Qadhdhafi and shortly after that dispatched one of his

release of Emam Sadr to Libya. 


After returning to Iran, Emam Khomeyni addressed a group sent by al- 
Qadhdhafi which had come to Tehran to obtain permission for the latter to

Musa Sadr is a violation and infringement of the limits of Islam and, un-

tion of relations between our genuine Islamic revolution and the leaders 
of Libya will not be possible." At that point he refused to grant al- 
Qadhdhafi permission to be received.
