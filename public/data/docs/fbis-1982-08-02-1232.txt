LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES MESSAGE FROM HAWATIMAH 


LDO11302 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 1 Aug 82 


[Text] The brother leader has received the following cable from Nayif Hawatimah, general 
secretary of the Democratic Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine [DPFLP]. Here 
is text of the cable: 


The invaders are trying to occupy the axes of southern Beirut in preparation for a wider 
assualt. The defense front has been inflamed in the face of the invaders since dawn today 
and has destroyed the vanguards of their tanks. The enemy is continuing his aggression in 
order to turn patriotic Beirut into hell. The city is burning and thousands of people are 
standing fast. We appeal for support for the fighting front, which is defending the Arab 
nation's honor and ask you to take an initiative to convene an Arab summit to adopt practical 
measures against the invaders and their protectors. Glory for the resistance. Defeat for 
the invaders. 


[Signed] Nayif Hawatimah 


AL-QADHDHAFI GIVES ‘URGENT’ MESSAGE TO USSR ENVOY 


LDO11256 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 1 Aug 82 
[Text ] The brother leader of the revolution received the Soviet ambassador to the 
Jamahiriyah today and gave him an urgent message for the Soviet leaders connected with the 


current grave international situation.
