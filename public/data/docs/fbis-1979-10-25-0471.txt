JAMAICAN PRIME MINISTER ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 
LD230924 Tripoli JANA in English 0848 GMT 23 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, October 23, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Jamaican Prime Minister, 
Mr Michael Manley, arrived in Tripoli yesterday evening on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. 
A big reception was given to the guest at Tripoli international airport. 


He was met there by Mr Jadallah Azzuz Talhi, secretary general of the General People's 
Committee, some members of the committee, the undersecretaries, heads of protocol and 
head of administrative departments at the foreign secretariat. 


The Jamaican premier made a statement to our correspondent upon his arrival in the air- 
port. He expressed his happiness at visiting the Jamahiriyah and said: 


"Tt is a great honour for me to be today a guest of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah". 


He went on saying: "The Jamaican people fully appreciate the giant achievement accomplished 


by the Great First of September Revolution in all fields". 


The Jamaican premier praised the pioneer role led by the Great First of September Revolu- 
tion against all forms of colonialism, and in purging and supporting the non-alignment 

movement, affirming that this visit would give both countries the opportunity to exchange 
their viewpoints and would lead to an increase in the volume of trade between them for the 
common interest, and welfare of both friendly peoples. 


Concluding his statement, the Jamaican premier expressed hope that the visit woula give 
him the opportunity of witnessing the giant achievements of the Libyan Arab people, under 
the Great First of September Revolution and its heralds, the establishment of people's 
power and of the first Jamahiriyah in history.

Joint Statement 
LD241745 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 24 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] Tripolie-A joint statement was issued today in Tripoli on the visit of the Prime 
Minister of Jamaica, Michael Manley, to the Jamahiriyah. Here is the text: 


At the invitation of Jadallah Azzuz ateTalhi, secretary general of the General People's 
Committee, the Prime Minister of Jamaica, Michael Manley, accompanied by an important 
delegation, paid an official and friendly visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah between 22 and 2" October. 

The delegation was accorded a warm reception that expressed the good relations between the 
two countries and the two friendly peoples. The prime minister was also received by the 
brother leader of the revolution, when they exchanged views on international topics of 
common interest. 


During this visit talks were held between the two sides. These talks, which took place in 
an atmosphere of friendship and understanding, dealt with various aspects of cooperation 
between the wuwo countries, the review of bilateral relations between them and the search 
for the best way to consolidate these relations and promote them in the interest of the 
two friendly peoples. The two sides expressed their determination to consolidate the 
relations and develop cooperation between the two friendly countries. In this connection 
the two sides agreed to establish diplomatic relations between the two countries at 
ambassadorial level. They also agreed that experts from the two countries meet in Tripoli 
at the beginning of January 1980 to study the potentials for cooperation in the fields of 
trade and joint projects and other fields. 


The two sides exchanged views on a number of current international issues, particularly 
the situation in the Arab region, in Africa and Latin America and the activities of the 
nonalined group. The two sides showed particular interest in the developments in the Arab 
region and agreed that the solving of the Palestinian issue was necessary and a must for 
achieving a just and durable peace in this region. They also firmly stressed that any 
solution must fully recognize the inalienable rights of the Palestinian people to self- 
determination, independence and national sovereignty, and also secure the retrieval of all 
the occupied Arab territories in accordance with the UN resolutions. 


The two sides affirmed that the treaties signed at "Stable David" do not respect these 
rights and therefore they are not considered a solution to the problem. In this connection 
the two sides affirmed their absolute support for the just struggle which the Palestinian 
people are waging under the leadership of the Palestine Liberation Organization (PLO), 

the sole legitimate representative of the Palestinian people. 


On reviewing the current situation in Africa, the two sides noted that the African countries 
were subject to strong pressures and interference in their internal affairs and attacks 

by colonialist and imperialist forces. The two sides expressed the need for total respect 
of the sovereignty of all peoples in the African Continent and the preservation of their 
freedom, independence and territorial integrity without any outside interference. They also 
declared their absolute support for the struggle of the peoples of Zimbabwe, Namibia and 
South Africa against colonialism, both old and neocolonialism, and against racism and 

racial discrimination.

The two sides called for total commitment to the UN resolutions with regard to sanctions 
against South Africa and Rhodesia, They condemn all colonialist and imperialist attempts 
which aim at imposing specific solutions on the inhabitants of this part of the African 
Continent. The two sides also affirmed their support to the struggle of the people of 
Western Sahara and their right to self-determination in accordance with the resolutions 
of the United Nations and the OAU. 


The two sides reaffirmed their commitment to support the liberation movements in Latin 
America, and in this connection, they condemned the policy of domination and the attempts 
of containment pursued by the imperialist and reactionary forces toward the countries 

of this region, which are eagerly trying to complete the control of their destinies 

and invest their resources to achieve the ambitions and legal aspirations of their 
peoples for progress and prosperity. 


The two sides also condemned the dictatorial regime in Chile and affirmed their firm 
solidarity with the struggle which the people of Chile are waging against repression and 
tyranny being pursued by the repressive fascist regime. The two sides expressed their 
support for the rights of the people of Belize to sovereignty, self-determination and 
independence together with territorial integrity. 


On this occasion the two sides saluted the triumph of the victorious popular revolution 

in Nicaragua under the Sandinist National Liberation Front which ousted the reactionary 
fascist regime of Somoza. The two sides noted with profound satisfaction that the 
nonalined movement had become an influential and effective force in international relations 
and constitutes an important element in the world's stability and the positive changes 
which take place in the worlds; and that the principles of the policy of nonalinement 

has proved its ability and importance in the struggle to make the international, the 
economic and the political relations more democratic and to consolidate cooperation, 

peace and security in the world. 


The two sides pointed out that it was important and necessary to all the nonalined 
states to work constantly to strengthen the cooperation and solidarity and unity of 
action of the nonalined movement, so as to enable this movement to continue the struggle 
successfully to achieve the independence and the liberation of countries and peoples 

of the world and total liberation from colonialism and imperialism and all forms of 
racism and other forms of domination and exploitation. The two sides hailed the 

success of the sixth summit conference of the countries of the movement in Havana 

last September. The two sides discussed the positive role played by the nonalined group 
for the establishment of new international economic order aimed at getting rid of back- 
wardmess and at achieving prosperity and economic stability in the whole world. 


In this connection the two sides affirmed the need for the people's control of their 
natural resources and their investment so as to secure the realization of their national 
aims, prosperity and progress. In this regard the two sides noted with concern that the 
efforts being made to establish this desired economic order were unsuccessful because of 
the inflexible stands adopted by a number of the advanced states and their failure 

to meet the just demands of the developing states. 


The two sides affirmed anew the importance of carrying out a basic structural change in 
the present international and economic relations by setting up a new international 
economic order that would offer new scope to international cooperation and contribute 
to strengthen international peace and security.

ARRIVAL OF CONGOLESE FOREIGN MINISTER REPORTED 
LD230910 Tripoli JANA in English 0836 GMT 23 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, October 23, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Mr Pierre Nze, the foreign minister 
of Congo arrived in Tripoli yesterday on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan frab 
Jamahiriyah. 


He and his delegation were met at Tripoli international airport by Foreign Secretary 
Dr 'Ali ‘Abd al-Salam at-Turayki, the head of the African and Cooperation Departments 
and a representative of the protocol department at the Foreign Secretariat, 


In a statement to our reporter at Tripoli international airport, the visiting minister 
expressed his pleasure as he landed in the Jamahiriyah, remarking that his visit comes 
within the context of the reinforcement of bilateral relations between the Libyan Arab 
and Congolese peoples, and to coordinate the stands of the two friendly countries in 
the face of the imperialist conspiracies plotted against Africa. 


He said the African states should protect their independence and never allow any 
opportunity of imperialist foreign intervention in its affairs, to restrict their 
freedom and violate their independence, 


In his statement, Mr P, Nze condemned the French invasion of Central Africa. "Our 
people have the right and the ability to determine their own fate", he said.
