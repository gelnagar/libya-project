LIBYA

EMERGENCY ARAB LEAGUE MEETING REQUESTED 
JN201355 Baghdad INA in Arabic 1335 GMT 20 Oct 81 


[Text] Tunis, 20 Oct (INA) -- Libya has requested an emergency meeting of the Arab League 
Council to discuss the critical situation on its border with Sudan. This was stated by 
informed sources at the General Secretariat. The sources added that a Libyan memorandum 
has been distributed on the member states regarding this meeting, which is expected to 

be held within the next 3 days. 


The INA correspondent says that Arab League Secretary General Chedli Klibi will leave 

for Tripoli tomorrow on a brief visit to Libya. He will discuss with senior Libyan officials 
the developments of the situation on the Libyan-Sudanese border. During the last few days, 
Klibi held a series of meetings with the representatives of the concerned countries and 
expressed the Arab League's worry about the fast development of current events between 

the two countries.

W. EUROPEAN ENVOYS BRIEFED ON SUDAN'S AGGRESSION 


LD202156 Tripoli JANA in English 1918 GMT 20 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 Oct (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY) -- The People's Foreign Liaison Bureau 
this morning summoned the Western European ambassadors accredited to Jamahiriyah as 

part of the effort to acquaint sisterly and friendly countries with the aggressive path 
followed by Sudan's ruler towards Jamahiriyah, his blatant threats to shift the battle 

to Jamahiriyah and his declared determination to conduct terrorist operations against the 
Libyan people.

"Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the People's Committee of the Foreign Liaison 
Bureau, explained to the ambassadors Jamahiriyah's stance towards Sudan and the 
Sudanese regime's attampt to undermine security and stability in the region, and his 
schemes to harm the Chadian people's freedom. 


In this respect, the ambassadors were acquainted with Sudanese regime's ill intentions 
towards the Libyan Arab people and the series of attacks and threats against the 
Jamahiriyah people and its revolution's leader which reached the stage of blatantly 
threatening assassination. 


Al-'Ubaydi stressed "the Jamahiriyah has no aggressive intentions towards Sudan or any 
neighbouring country. The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, faced with these 
threats from the Sudanese regime and the dangers they pose to its people's freedom and 
achievements, will find itself obliged to take a decisive measure which deters any 
attempt or any terrorist act by the Sudanese regime". 


He said, "America is misleading Sudan and Egypt into believing the presence of an 
alleged danger from Libya. America's objective is to guarantee its hegemony over the 
Sudanese regime and keep Egypt and Sudan within its sphere of influence". 


He affirmed, "The Libyan Arab people is ready to confront America if the latter attempts 
to harm its security and safety". 


He added, "The Sudanese regime is overtly backing the butcher Habre which is a clear 
violation of African decision. The Jahamiriyah's stand in backing and assisting the 
Chadian people and consolidating its freedom is not to the Sudanese ruler's liking, 
because he blatantly backs the butcher Habre and prompts him to commit aggressive acts 
to undermine security and stability realized in that country’). He stressed the 
Jamahiriyah's concern for the Chadian people's freedom and its refusal to let any side 
to harm this freedom. He said, "Jamahiriyah will not stand with folded arms in the face 
of these attempts by Sudan's ruler against Chad". 


He condemned the Sudanese ruler's statements flagrantly threatening Libya. He pointed 
out, "Sudan's ruler is facing a difficult situation and is in the grip of despair after 
the execution of traitor As-Sadat". 


He reaffirmed to the Western European ambassadors that Jamahiriyah “has no aggressive 
plans in Egypt or Sudan. Jamahiriyah's concerns are focused on the safety and freedom 
of its people and the building of its happy socialist society. If these two countries 
(Egypt and Sudan) have internal difficulties and problems, then this is not Libya's 
responsibility". 


Al-'Ubaydi asked, "What would America, which accuses us of terrorism, say now? Isn't 
this terrorism itself?" 


He added, "We have the right to attack any terrorist camp which we believe is threaten- 
ing our security and safety, because the principle of self-defence is a necessity". 


He pointed out, "The second disagreement with Sudan's ruler is his support for Camp 
David. His recent meetings with Begin is perhaps a prelude for creating a new 
As-Sadat. This will not be accepted by Jamahiriyah under any circumstances. We will 
confront it, because it threatens our security and the Palestinian people's rights, 
and gives America the opportunity for further infiltration of the region". 


E. European Envoys Briefed 


LD202202 Tripoli JANA in English 1940 GMT 20 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 Oct (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY) -- The People's Committee of the 
Foreign Liaison Bureau this morning summoned the Eastern European ambassadors accredited 
to Jamahiriyah. They were informed of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
stand towards the Sudanese regime's aggressive stand and the Sudanese ruler's statements 
threatening to commit aggression on Libya.

"Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the People's Committee of the Foreign Liaison 
Bureau, pointed out to the ambassadors that Jamahiriyah has no plans against Sudan. 
Jamahiriyah, he affirmed, stemming from the principle of self-defence, would resolutely 
confront any terrorist attempt by Sudan's ruler against Jamahiriyah. 


He referred to the Sudanese ruler's aggressive statements against the Libyan people and 
the state of despair this ruler is in after the execution of traitor As-Sadat. 


He said, "These statements affirm the terrorist and aggressive intentions of Sudan's 
ruler against Jamahiriyah people." 


He explained that the Sudanese regime's backing for the butcher Habre and the support 

of Sudan's ruler for the Camp David accords were the two points of disagreement with that 
regime. He stressed, "Jamahiriyah will resolutely confront any attempt aimed against 

the Chadian people or its freedom. The backing for murderer Habre is a violation of 
African decisions, which stressed backing for the legitimate government in Chad. The 
Sudanese ruler's support for the capitulatory agreements is also a violation of Arab 
unanimity and is an affirmation of treason." 


He pointed out, "America is trying to exploit As-Sadat's death to increase its influence 
in the region. This makes it imperative for the nonaligned countries to confront such 

a step, because America is trying to enforce the continuation of capitulatory policy 
followed by As-Sadat. The giving of weapons to Egypt and Sudan and the arrival of 

AWACS spy planes are but a proof of this."
