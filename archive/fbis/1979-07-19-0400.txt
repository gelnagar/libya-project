LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS INDEPENDENCE DAY MESSAGE TO CARTER 
1D181940 Tripoli JANA in English 1805 GMT 18 Jul 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, July 18, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The leader of the Great First of Sep- 
tember Revolution, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, sent a cable to American President Carter 
on America's 203rd independence day. The message reads as follows: 


President Jimmy Carter of the United States of America, I congratulate you and the Amer- 
ican people in the 203rd anniversary of your country's independence. The commemoration 

of America's independence inspires many historic reflections and meditations. On the one 
hand there is Britain, who once colonized America, now several times smaller than her. 

On the other hand there is America, natural ally and protector of Britain. How could this 
happen? One wonders! The American people gained their independence after a bitter struggle. 
Without this struggle they would not have won their freedom. America's independence from 
Britain made it possible to establish, intime, not only correct relations but also friend- 
ly ones between the two countries, otherwise the hostile relationship would have continued 
as is the case between Britain and Northern Ireland. Mr President, I would like to remind 
you that, for their independence, the American people used every possible means of struggle. 
For example, the unloading of the British cargo at sea by a group of masked Americans was 
one link in the chain of struggle operations for independence. I recall these events now 
because I believe America, or at least America's high-level policy, denies other peoples 
the same path. America asks the peoples of colonized countries to establish cordial re- 
lations with their colonizers and deplores the armed struggle for freedom. This is ob- 
vious in America's attitude towards the struggle of the Palestinian Arab people, and re- 
cently, towards that of the Vietnamese people. Nevertheless, we never despair and never 
have any fears, for yesterday's giants are today's dwarfs and today's giants are tomorrow's 
dwarfs. Thus, history repeats itself. Peace is upon those who follow the path of right- 
eousness. 


With my compliments, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi.
