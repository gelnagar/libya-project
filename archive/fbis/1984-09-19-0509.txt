Al--Turayki Meets With Goukouni 
LD180958 Tripoli JANA in English 0821 GMT 18 Sep 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhu al-Hijjah 23, September 18, JAMAHIR- 
IYAH NEWS AGENCY — Dr ‘Ali ‘Abd al-Salam al-Turayki, 
the secretary of the People’s Committee for the Foreign Liaison 
Bureau has met with President Goukouni Oueddei of the Chad- 
ian National Unity Government and informed him of Jama- 
hiriyah and France’s decision to begin the reciprocal and total 
withdrawal of French troops and Libyan elements backing the 
Chadian National Unity Government. President Goukouni 
expressed his government's support for the decision and pointed 
out that the withdrawal of French troops from Chad was an old 
wish whez< implementation will help solve the Chadian problem.
