LIBYA

GOUKOUNI HOLDS PRESS CONFERENCE, THANKS LIBYA FOR SUPPORT 
LD062240 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 4S Nov 80 


[Excerpts] The head of the Chadian national government, Goukouni Queddei, has praised the 
stand of the great 1 September revolution and the Libyan people's leading role in supporting 
the struggle of peoples against imperialism and colonialisn. 


Goukouni was speaking at a press conference he held at Sabhah yesterday evening, which was 
attended by representatives of a number of world news agencies and papers. 


He said that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has constantly supplied unlimited 
aid to Frolinat. He added that the Jamahiriyah's aid consists of food, medicines and military 
experts. This, he said, is legitimate and is in accordance with the resolutions of the 

recent African summit conference held at Freetown to bolster the legitimate government in 

Chad and to ensure the restoration of peace, security and stability to it. Furthermore, he 
affirmed that the legitimate Chadian Government has the right to seek the help of any African 
country to put an end to the rebellion, and to restore security and rebuild the country. 


He said that the military situation will end soon in favor of the legitimate government and 
that the Chadian people have the will and determination to continue the struggle for their 
unity and territorial integrity. 


He strongly denounced As-Sadat's terrorist regime, which he said is behind Habre's mutiny and 
provides it with econes... and military aid. He added that the Chadian Government has strong 
evidence proving the Egyptian regime's participation in supporting rebel Habre. The government 
forces have seized Egyptian-made arms in the liberated areas of (Oubreco), Oum Chalouba, 
(Klaid) and Faya. These arms, he added, came by way of Sudanese territory. Goukouni 
Oueddei said that Egyptian planes land at Abeche carrying arms and equipment to mutineer Habre. 
These planes come by way of Sudanese territory, he said. 


There is also evidence of the presence of Egyptian and Sudanese experts working alongside 
rebel Habre in the Abeche area. He added that the terrorist As-Sadat regime has set up a 
diplomatic mission equipped with communications equipment led by one Muhammad, a former 
consul in Chad. 


He referred to the tour which mutineer Habre made to Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Sudan and occupied 
Palestine. He said they confirm the bonds that bind these regimes to mutineer Habre. 


He declared that the transitional government will hand over authority to the Chadian people 
to rule themselves by themselves, and will entrench the concepts of popular authority. 


He welcomed aid from the countries of the world. He said we are in need of medical and food 
supplies. He added that the Chadian people will appreciate all aid offered now. 


He demanded support for the legitimate government in Chad and for the implementation of the 
Lagos agreement as this will make an effective contribution toward the unity of the 

Chadian people. He affirmed that the suppert provided by some of the reactionary regimes 
in Africa to mutineer Habre only increases »loodshed in Chad.

JANA Commentary 
LD062348 Tripoli JANA in English 1815 GMT 6 Nov 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 Nov (JANA)--JANA's political editor commented on the dazzling victories 
won by the legitimate national government of Chad against the forces of rebel Hissein 
Habre. He said: At last the legitimate national forces in Chad were able to put an end 
to the tragic suffering of the brotherly Chadian people, from a grinding and ferocious 
war perpetrated by the renegade Hissein Habre in intermittent battles, which petered 

out only to rekindle again.

By recovering and liberating the capital of the north the legitimate national government 
forces, enabled the Chadian people to enjoy the stability in various areas of the country, 
extending to north of Ndjamena to Asteb north of Abeche across several towns and villages 
which were direct target to Hissein Habre [words indistinct] attacks. But the forces 

of goodness have at last prevailed. This victory of the legitimate national forces in 
Chad is a victory to all revolutionary forces in the area and in various parts of the 
African Continent, which regard the standing of the Chadian people and its legitimate 
goverment in the face of renegade Habre and his Zionist allies of lackey regimes a natural 
extension to their steadfastness and strategic stands towards the causes of freedom, 
security and peace in the African Continent and the whole world. There is no doubt that 
the ability of the legal forces to impose their full control and hegemony over the northern 
parts of Chad, including the [word indistinct] north and then Barkou Ennedi, Ennedi-Tibesti, 
is considered one of the pillars of the decisive and great victory which Chad took forward 
and which is about to be achieved by the final downfall of rebel Habre in one of his last 
surrounded pockets near Abeche. This is the final showdown between the two sides and the 
Habre is completely trapped with nowhere to escape. 


He added that on the light of this situation in Chad caused by flagrant interventions 

by those regimes which were exposed against the victories of the Chadian people and their 
legitimate forces, President Goukouni Oueddei of Chad visited the Jamahiriyah this week 
heading a large delegation from the Transitional National Unity Government and met with 
the leader of the great Al-Fatah revolution. 


Both sides strongly condemmed the regimes which are increasing sectarian strife among 
the Chadian people and condemned their intervention which coincides with the (Israeli) 
support and backing to rebel Habre threatening the security of the Chadian people and 
peace in the whole area. 


JANA's political editor went on to say that if the tragedy of the Chadian people and 
their suffering are caused by that support and those flagrant interventions by Zionists 
and regimes considered as African and non-aligned, then the most serious of all is the 
collaboration of certain African states and their involvement in offering access and 
facilities for the military aid and supplies to rebel Habre. Such stand was condemned 
by the historic leadership of the great Al-Fatah revolution and of the Transitional 
National Unity Government. 


The communique issued at the end of the visit made by the head of state and the national 
government of Chad came as direct address to the African countries to take a clear stand 
towards this intervention and work to put an end to this serious precedent which encourages 
rebels inside African states, the thing which shatters security and peace in the continent. 


JANA political editor concluded that OAU is responsible to the Chadian people who are now 
threatened in their security, independence and sovereignty, to seek a collective African 
stand against this serious breach of international relations and norms. 


The Chadian people retain their heroic stand against the enemies of their existence 

and freedom. They have their revolutionary committment towards their brothers who 

were and still have their distinctive stand in the support of the Chadian people through- 
out their sufferings, a point which the Chadian delegation was keen to emphasize with 
pride and gratitude towards the stand of the great Al-Fatah revolution.

MORE EMBASSIES CONVERTED TO ‘BROTHERHOOD BUREAUS' 
Doha Embassy 
LD061318 Kuwait KUNA in English 1130 GMT 6 Nov 80 


[Text] Doha, 6 Nov (KUNA)--A group of Libyan citizens took over their country's embassy 
here Thursday and turned it into an office for "Arab brotherhood.” 


A group spokesman told KUNA that eight Libyans had "marched" on the embassy and trans- 
ferred it into the new status at instructions from Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. 


Qatar Embassy 
LD062356 Tripoli JANA in English 1701 GMT 6 Nov 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 Nov (JANA)--A bid calculated to escalate the popular revolution, and 
in confirmation of the brotherhood bonds between the Arab people, the revolutionary 
force and the Jamahiriyah masses in Arab countries are continuing their take-over of 
Libyan Embassies in Arab countries, transforming them to Arab brotherhood bureaus. 


Today, they took over the Libyan Embassy in Qatar transforming it to a brotherhood bureau 
to be run by a people's committee. In a statement the people's committee of the brother- 
hood bureau in Qatar asserted that the mass control of embassies and their transformation 
into Arab brotherhood bureaus leads to strengthening the brotherhood ties between the 
Arab people and to speeding up of their comprehensive unity. Arab Libyan masses have 
already taken over the Libyan Embassies in Sudan, Algeria, Mauritania, Lebanon and U.A.E., 
transforming them into brotherhood bureaus.
