FOREIGN MINISTER AT-TURAYKI ARRIVES IN NEW YORK 
LDO4I904Y Tripoli JANA in English 1721 GMT 4 Oct 78 LD 


[Text] New York, 4 October, JANA--Dr at-Turayki, the foreign secretary, arrived this 
morning in New York heading the Jamahiriyah delegation to the 33rd U.N, session, and will 
deliver the Jamahiriyah speech on 6 October. Receiving the foreign secretary in New York 
was the assistant secretary-general of the United Nations, the Jamahiriyah permanent 
representative at the U.N, and members of the Jamahiriyah delegation. 


JANA's envoy to the U.N, mentioned that the foreign secretary would have contacts and 
discussions with many of the foreign ministers of brotherly and friendly countries which 
were participating in the United Nation's meetings, in order to consolidate bilateral 
relations between the Jamahiriyah and these countries, and would discuss issues of
