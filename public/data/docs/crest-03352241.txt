Summary (AI): A CIA cable from the morning of 1 September 1969 reports that an army group attempting to seize power in Tripoli has announced itself as the "Committee of Sovereignty for the Revolution," led by Colonel Sa'd al-Din Abu Shuwayrib. It also reports a radio-announced curfew, closure of the airport and ports, and a ban on movement and on entry to or exit from Libya.
• The group called itself the 'Committee of Sovereignty for the Revolution' in a public communique.
• Abu Shuwayrib had retired from the army some time earlier and become a notary; the Revolutionary Council reportedly asked him to return to head it.
• A long-time leading opponent of the government claimed ignorance of the coup beyond public information, saying his uncle had telephoned earlier to warn him that troops were taking over the city.
• Radio announced a curfew starting at 2200 hours (OCR reads '8808'), with all movement prohibited, airport and ports closed, and no entry or exit from Libya until further notice.
• 1969-09-01: Army group attempts to seize power in Tripoli and announces the 'Committee of Sovereignty for the Revolution' under Colonel Sa'd al-Din Abu Shuwayrib; radio announces curfew, airport and ports closed.
