LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI WELCOMED BY THOUSANDS UPON RETURN 
ID122200 Tripoli JANA in English 2024 qmT 12 Jul 79 ID 


[Excerpt] Benghazi, July 12, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
leader of the Great First of September Revolution, arrived back home this evening ending 
a twenty-day national action tour to the following Arab countries: Syria, Jordan, Iraq, 
Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Yemen Arab Republic and Yemen 
People's Democratic Republic. 


VY. Db da NORTH AFRICA 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great First of September Revolution, was 
received upon his arrival at Ibinina Airport in Benghazi by It Col Mustafa al-Kharrubi, 
the secretary-general of the General People's Congress, the secretaries of people's con- 
gresses affairs, trade unions, and vocational association affairs at the General Secre- 
tariat of the General People's Congress, members of the General People's Committee, 
secretaries of people's congresses and committees and a huge crowd. 


Thousands of Libyans, carrying green flags, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's pictures and slogans 
expressing their commitment to their historical leadership, gathered in the airport and 
lined the road heading to the city of Benghazi early this morning to welcome him back. 


The moment Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's plane landed the crowds cheered him and shouted slogans 
for the unity of the Arab nation. The following are some of the slogans reiterated by 
the masses: Welcome, Welcome Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, You Are Our Sole Leader! Welcome, 
Your People Salute You! Welcome, We Are Eager To See You! The masses followed Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi in a huge march to the centre of Benghazi city. Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's tour 
of the Arab countries is of great importance due to the current critical circumstances 

of the Arab nation following the surrender and submission of the defeated Egyptian 
president to the enemy and its allies. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi was accompanied by a delegation which included; Major al-Khuwaylidi 
al-Humaydi, the assistant secretary general of the General People's Congress; Muhammad 
‘Abd al-Kassem Zuwi; the secretary of planning; 'Abd al-Majid Go'oud; the foreign 
secretary, Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki; and the secretary of the General People's 
Committee for Municipalities, Abu Zayd Durdah.
