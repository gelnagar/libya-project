LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Responds to BBC on U.S. Threat 
BK291253 London BBC World Service in English 
1200 GMT 29 Aug 86 


[From ‘Radio Newsreel’ Program] 


[Text] The Libyan leader, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, has said he is 
not perturbed about the latest warnings by the United States 
toward his country. It was his first comments since the recent 
allegations from Washington against his regime and it was made 
to our reporter Kerin Cook while Colonel al-Qadhdhafi was 
touring the Libyan coastal area.
