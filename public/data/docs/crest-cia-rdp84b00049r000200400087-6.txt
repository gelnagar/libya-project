Summary (AI): An internal CIA memo from February 1982 asks a deputy chief in the Near East Division to pull together material for the Director ahead of a 10 February NSC meeting on Libya. It asks for a detailed account of Qadhafi's activities abroad (terrorism, insurgency support, funding such as Pakistani nuclear efforts) and his underlying strategy.
• Libya was on the agenda of an NSC meeting held on 4 February 1982 but was not thoroughly discussed.
• The Director wanted the package by noon Tuesday, 9 February 1982.
• Requested coverage includes Qadhafi's activities in Africa, the Middle East and South Asia, including funding of Pakistani nuclear efforts.
• The memo is addressed to the Deputy Chief, Near East Division, DDO, and copied to C/NIC, DDI, NIO/NESA and D/NESA/DDI.
• 1982-02-10: NSC meeting scheduled at 9:30 to discuss Libya
