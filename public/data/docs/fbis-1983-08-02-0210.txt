JANA Claims Chad’s Habre Killed in Shelling 
LDO021426 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1414 GMT 2 Aug 83 


[Text] 2 Aug (JANA/BARDAI) [dateline as received] — It has 
become certain that Habre has been killed as a result of shelling 
by guns of the forces of the legitimate government that encircle 
Faya Largeau 


Government Urges OAU Mediation in Chad 
LDO11457 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT | Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, | Aug (JANA) — JANA has learned that Libya 
has contacted the OAU chairman and requested that he cooper- 
ate with it to find a peaceful solution among the disputing 
Chadian sides and stop the civil war in Chad. 


Libya has also asked the OAU chairman that the OAU should 
use its authority and influence to prevent foreign intervention in 
Chad and permit peaceful action for reconciliation and an end to 
the war there 


Statement Denies ‘Cheap’ U.S. Accusations 
LDO11557 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT | Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli | Aug (JANA) — The People’s Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison in the Socialist Peoples Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah issued 
a statement today concerning the remarks of the American 
Administration on the so-called Libyan intervention in Chad 
Here is the text 


While the American Administration allows itself into parts of 
Africa, and uses the territories of African countries in an attempt 
of show off, thus destabilizing peace and security in the African 
Continent and threatening its peoples, at the same time the 
American Administration issues a statement in which it accuses 
the Socialist Peoples Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah of interfering in 
Chad, especially in the battles of Faya Largeau 


The People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison vehemently voices the 
Jamahiriyah’s protest against the accusation of committing an 
action which it did not commit, and which has been fabricated 
in the imagination of the American Administration in order to 
distort the Libyan role and its announcement of neutrality in this 
power conflict among the factions of the people of Chad. It also 
protests the attempt to [word indistinct] against the continuing 
consultations between the Jamahiriyah and the OAU and its 
current chairman to find a peaceful solution to end the current 
fighting in Chad. 


The People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison demands that the 
American Administration withdraw this cheap accusation and 
warns the international public against its dangers. At the same 
time it stresses its firm denial of this accustion, by which the 
Reagan administration seeks to bring about direct military inter- 
ference in the Chad war and to push its agents towards further 
intervention, thus threatening the peace and security of the 
Jamahiriyah and the unity of its territory. The People’s Bureau 
for Foreign Liaison stresses that the transfer of the conflict to 
Faya Largeau through American intervention and French weap- 
ons is a dangerous sign threatening the peace and territorial 
security of the Jamahiriyah. This necessitates that the Libyan 
Arab people closely follow these battles, not because of any desire 
for direct intervention, but to protect Libyan soil against any 
aggression, especially after the statements by officials in Habre’s 
regime concerning some so-called territorial claims in the area 
of Aozou, which is an integral part of Libyan territory 


The People’s Bureau for Foreign Laison considers that the 
United States must withdraw its forces and stop supporting the 
regime of the rebel Habre, so that the OAU and its member- 
countries might find a peaceful solution to the Chad problem. It 
also calls on the American Administration to withdraw its forces 
which are deployed in Latin and Central America, Africa and 
the rest of the regions of the world; to stop terrorizing the peoples 
of the world; to order its Zionists agent in occupied Palestine and 
the Golan to give back all the Arab territories to their owners; 
and to make its allies in Pretoria grant the peoples of South 
Africa and Namibia the legitimate right of independence. 


Al-‘Ubaydi Meets Soviet, French Envoys

AGENCY Mr ‘Abd al-‘Ati al-‘Ubaydi, secretary of the
