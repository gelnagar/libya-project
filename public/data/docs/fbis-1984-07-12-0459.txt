LIBYA

Cooperation Protocol With Cuba Signed 
LD1I11027 Tripoli JANA in English 0955 GMT 11 Jul 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 Shawal, 11 July, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — At the end of the meetings of the Joint Libyan- 
Cuban Committee, a protocol of mutual cooperation between 
Jamahiriyah and Cuba was signed. 


The protocol was signed by Muhammad ‘Ubayd ash-Shukri, 
secretary of the General People’s Committee for Public Utilities 
and in charge of housing and Ricardo Cabrizas Ruiz, the Cuban 
foreign trade minister. The protocol of cooperation provides for 
cooperation in the fields of economy, housing, transport, youth, 
sports and the promotion of cooperation in cultural and scientific 
spheres. 


The Cuban minister, in a speech he delivered on the occasion, 
saluted the great Ist September revolution and its revolutionary 
leader and praised the existing relations of cooperation between 
the Jamahiriyah and Cuba. 


The Cuban minister further affirmed the Cuban people’s support 
and solidarity with the Jamahiriyah people in their struggle 
against imperialism, Zionism and reaction asserting his country’s 
desire to strengthen and foster the existing relations of cooper- 
ation between the two countries for the mutual interests of the 
two friendly peoples. 


Cuban Minister Departs 
LD112035 Tripoli JANA in English 1748 GMT 11 Jun 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 Shawal, 11 July, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The Cuban Minister for Foreign Trade Ricardo 
Cabrizas Ruiz, and the delegation accompanying him, left Trip- 
oli this afternoon after an official visit to the Socialist People’s 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah lasting several days following which 
a protocol of bilateral cooperation was signed covering the fields 
of economy, housing, transport, youth, sports and promotion of 
cooperation in cultural and scientific spheres for the mutual 
interests of the peoples of Libya and Cuba.

Kuwaiti Official Ends Visit to Tripoli 
LD112024 Tripoli JANA in English 1755 GMT 11 Jul 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 Shawal, 11 July, JAMAHIRYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — State Minister for the Cabinet Affairs in Kuwait 
‘Abd al-‘Aziz Husayn this afternoon left Tripoli at the end of a 
visit to the SPLAJ [Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah] 
which lasted for several days. In an announcement toa JANA 
reporter the Kuwait minister praised the Jamahiriyah for her 
outstanding role in all Arab issues and appreciated the 
achievements carried out by the Libyan Arab people in various 
fields. 


Husayn pointed out that during the visit he discussed the 
strengthening and promotion of cooperation relations existing 
between the two sister nations. He then expressed his pleasure 
with cooperative attitude shown by the brothers in the Jamahir- 
iyah to support and to develop mutual cooperation relations in all 
fields. 


Lebanese CP’s Hawi Arrives, Makes Statement 
LD112002 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1848 GMT 11 Jul 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 11 Jul (JANA)— George Hawi, secretary general 
of the Lebanese Communist Party, arrived in Tripoli this eve- 
ning. 


In a statement to JANA at Tripoli International Airport he said 
the visit he and members of his delegation are paying to the 
Jamahiriyah comes in the wake of historic victories achieved in 
Lebanon represented by the expulsion of U.S. and NATO inva- 
sion forces, the abrogation of the 17 May agreement, and the 
placing of the country on the road toward asserting its Arab 
identity. He added that the visit is also taking place at a time 
when the fighting is intensifying against Zionist occupation 
forces in the south, where forces of the Lebanese national resis- 
tance are pursuing the enemy. 


We have come to Jamahiriyah at this precise time, he said, to 
exchange views on the tasks of continuing the struggle in the 
Lebanese and other Arab arenas. Consultations, he said, have 
always been the means for defining the objectives for which 
Lebanon is struggling. These are pan-Arab objectives that con- 
cern the entire Arab nation and all those fighting for the good of 
this nation headed by the Great | September Revolution and its 
commander, brother Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi. 


Hawi added: We have come to express our solidarity with the 
Libyan Arab people against all provocations and plots being 
hatched by U.S. imperialism and reaction, to affirm our stand at 
the side of the Great | September Revolution, and our readiness 
to join the Libyan Arab people in defending the achievements of 
the Great | September with all our capabilities, because the 
Great | September Revolution is our revolution in Lebanon too. 
It is the revolution of the national progressive Lebanese forces, 
the revolution of the true revolutionaries of Palestine and of all 
strugglers and workers for complete liberation of our nation, its 
unity and advancement.
