TRIPOLI REPORTS NUMAYRI SHOT, CONFLICT IN SUDAN 


LD161442 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 16 Jan 82 


[Excerpts] According to an urgent dispatch from Khartoum, the ruler of Sudan yesterday 
was fired upon and hit by two bullets. 


The situation in Khartoum seems more tense today, following the escalation of conflict = 
started yesterday within the ranks of the army. According to some reports, certain 
elements have exchanged fire sporadically. 


An observer in Sudan, particularly recently, can only find one interpretation for all these 
developments: the deterioration of conditions in Sudan to such a serious level that the 
Sudanese people are no longer able to tolerate them. In addition to this, there is the 
fall of the lackey regime into the abyss and also the fall of Numayri to the level of the 
defunct agent As-Sadat. All the people in Sudan are now aware of the causes of the 
developments in these decisive days when the Sudanese masses have reached a high pitch of 
brewing and revolution from which they will not retract until Numayri, the head of agentry 
and subservience in the country, falls. 


Today, with the reports of Numayri's being hit by two bullets and of the escalation of 
disturbances within the ranks of the Sudanese Armed Forces to the extent of exchanging 
fire between the commands, the fate of the symbol of agentry in Sudan has been decided by 
the rebellious masses of Sudan and the true countdown for the end of the agent has begun. 
The masses together with the freemen in the armed forces will fix the hour to be rid of 
Numayri. 


JANA: ‘Civil Mutiny’ 
LD152000 Tripoli JANA in English 1855 GMT 15 Jan 82 


[Text] Khartoum, 15 Jan (JANA) -- Reports from Khartoum said that the railway tracks 
between Khartoum and Atbarah has been blown up this afternoon bringing the railway 
traffic to a halt. On the other hand, ten people have been reported killed in the 
violent clashes yesterday during the funeral of a martyr in Wad Medani, capital of the 
central province. Clashes began when a policeman opened fire on the mourning crowds.

AL-KIFAH AL-'ARABI: U.S. BEHIND SUDAN COUP 


NC170715 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 17 Jan 82 


[Text] In its issue published yesterday, the AL-KIFAH AL-'ARABI magazine quoted reliable 
sources as saying that the United States has put the final touches to a coup plan aimed 
at replacing the present faces in Sudan with others. 


The magazine said specialists in Sudanese affairs stress that the problem in Sudan has 
several causes. Some of these causes are the increasing opposition to the Numayri regime 

and the Zionist occupation of Sudan, which has become evident recently in light of informa- 
tion that Zionist officers are training Sudanese units and that a senior Zionist officer 
named (Shlomo Asafi) is supervising the Sudanese military intelligence. The magazine says 
that another reason for the problem is the escalating campaign of liquidation of the educated 
people, students, workers and teachers. This campaign also affected army ranks where many 
officers have disappeared under mysterious circumstances. Basing its report on exclusive 
information, the magazine said that well over 1,000 Sudanese officers recently were 
liquidated. 


ESSO-NATIONAL OIL ESTABLISHMENT AGREEMENT 


LD141600 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 14 Jan 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 14 Jan (JANA) -- The national oil establishment in the Socialist People's 
Libyan Jamahiriyah, Esso Standard and Esso Sidra Inc Companies have signed an agreement 
under which the two companies will sell and relinquish all their rights and assets connected 
with their activities in the Jamahiriyah to the national oil establishment. 


The national oil establishment has issued a press statement. Here is the text: 


On 17 December 1981, an agreement was reached between the national oil establishment and 
the two companies Esso Standard and Esso Sidra Inc, under which the two companies will sell 
and relinquish all their rights, stocks and assets connected with their activities in the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah to the national oil establishment. 


It has been decided that this agreement is effective as of 1 December 1981. The agreement 
also provides for a transition period during which the handing over and receiving process 
will take place in an organized manner and in accordance with defined measures to ensure 
the continuity of the oil operations without any confusion. 


The signing of the aforementioned agreement occurred after lengthy negotiations between 
the two sides which lasted more than 2 months. All parties wanted to enable the two Esso 
companies to terminate their contractual and legal commitments in accordance with the law 
governing these relations. Under the concession contracts granted to the two companies, 
the national oil establishment shareholding was 51 percent, but under this agreement, the 
establishment has become the sole holder of those concessions, except for a very small 
percentage which is still held by G.E.R.S. Oil Company. 


In accordance with the aforementioned, the national oil establishment has charged Sidra 
Oil Company -- which is an operational company totally owned by the national oil est- 
ablishment -- with the task of running and managing operations in all oil areas pre- 
viously run by the two Esso companies.

OFFICIAL URGES MILITARIZATION OF OIL SECTOR @ 


LD161426 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 16 Jan 82 


[Text] The commander of urban militarization, Maj al-Kuwaylidi al-Humaydi, this morning 
met with the secretaries of the People's Committees for the oil sector in the presence 
of the secretary for oil to discuss the question of militarization of the oil sector in 
accordance with a well-studied training program which would ensure that militarization 
will not affect work in the oil fields and installations. 


Maj al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi expresses his appreciation of the efforts exerted by this 
sector and urged all the national elements to continue to exert efforts and to offer 
sacrifices to face the challenges to the Libyan’Arab people and to their revolution. He 
affirmed that the imperialist challenges aim at impeding the march of the revolution and 
starving the Libyan Arab people. 


Addressing the secretaries, he said that by their faith in their revolution, our people 
can frustrate the seige the American imperialist circles and their agents are working to 
impose on the Libyan people. He affirmed the need for the militarization of the oil 
sector and the creation of national technical capabilities as they are the only guarantee 
for solving the problems created by the hostile imperialist circles in the oil sector 

in a desperate attempt to force the Libyan people to relinquish their freedom, dignity 
and achievements.
