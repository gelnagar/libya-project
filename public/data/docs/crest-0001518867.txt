Summary (AI): A 1989 CIA cable reports, citing a Libyan businessman, that Libyan External Security Organization (ESO) and military intelligence planned to significantly expand their presence in Malta in 1989. It names several officers, business fronts, and a planned $5 million intelligence fund, but nothing about Tripoli sites.
• ESO officer Ali Muhammad al-Sami'i was assigned to the World Islamic Call Society office in Malta in early 1989.
• A Maltese vendor at Luqa Airport was recruited by ESO and reported to ESO headquarters in Tripoli via a Libyan Arab Airlines courier.
• The partnership of Shuqayfa, Farhat and Huwaysa reportedly had access to a foreign currency account containing five million USD for intelligence-gathering.
• Musbah al-Warfalli planned an oilfield services company in Malta in 1989 to provide cover for ESO officers.
• Document date: 2 March 1989; ACO stated as mid-April 1989.
• 1989-03: Planned significant increase in Libyan intelligence presence in Malta during 1989, including ESO and military intelligence personnel.

SUBJECT: PLANNED SIGNIFICANT INCREASE IN THE LIBYAN INTELLIGENCE 
PRESENCE IN MALTA IN 1989 

TEXT: 1. ACCORDING TO A LIBYAN BUSINESSMAN WITH CONTACTS IN THE 
LIBYAN EXTERNAL SECURITY ORGANIZATION (ESO), THE LIBYAN 
INTELLIGENCE PRESENCE IN MALTA WILL INCREASE SIGNIFICANTLY IN 1989. 
THE INCREASE IS EXPECTED TO INCLUDE PERSONNEL WITHIN BOTH THE ESO

AND THE LIBYAN MILITARY INTELLIGENCE SERVICE. IT IS A DELIBERATE 
EFFORT TO ENLARGE THE LIBYAN INTELLIGENCE PRESENCE IN MALTA TO 
IMPROVE THE QUALITY AND QUANTITY OF INFORMATION COLLECTED, AND TO 
INCREASE THE NUMBER OF INTELLIGENCE-GATHERING SOURCES. 

2. AS PART OF THIS PLAN THE ESO HAS ALREADY EXPANDED ITS 
PRESENCE IN MALTA. ESO OFFICER ALI MUHAMMAD AL- ((SAMI'I)) WAS 
ASSIGNED TO THE WORLD ISLAMIC CALL SOCIETY OFFICE IN MALTA IN EARLY 
1989. A SECOND ESO OFFICER, KHALIFA ((SANUSSI)), WAS ASSIGNED AS 
THE ASSISTANT STATION MANAGER AT THE LIBYAN ARAB AIRLINES OFFICE IN 
MALTA. MOREOVER, THE ESO RECENTLY RECRUITED A MALTESE NATIONAL 
EMPLOYED AS A VENDOR AT LUQA AIRPORT TO ASSIST IN ACQUIRING 
INFORMATION ON PERSONS OF INTEREST TO THE LIBYAN SERVICE. THIS 
INDIVIDUAL PREPARES BIOGRAPHIC AND ASSESSMENT INFORMATION ON 
PERSONNEL OF INTEREST TO THE ESO AND FORWARDS HIS REPORTS TO ESO 
HEDQUARTERS IN TRIPOLI VIA A LIBYAN ARAB AIRLINES COURIER. 

3. MUSTAFA ((SHUQAYFA)), A LIBYAN MILITARY INTELLIGENCE 
OFFICER IN MALTA WHO WAS FORMERLY ASSIGNED TO THE LIBYAN ARAB 
MALTESE FISHING COMPANY, RECENTLY FORMED A BUSINESS PARTNERSHIP IN 
MALTA WITH TWO OTHER LIBYAN NATIONALS, FARID ((FARHAT)), THE 
LAFITRADE REPRESENTATIVE IN MALTA, AND NURI ((HUWAYSA)). THE 
PARTNERSHIP HAS ACCESS TO A FOREIGN CURRENCY ACCOUNT (NFI) WHICH 
CONTAINS FIVE MILLION USD FOR USE IN INTELLIGENCE-GATHERING 
ACTIVITIES. (SOURCE COMMENT: HUWAYSA IS A VERY CLOSE FRIEND OF 
KHUWAYLIDI AL- ((HUMAYDI)), THE CHIEF OF THE LIBYAN MILITARY 
INTELLIGENCE SERVICE. ) 

4. SOMETIME IN 1989, SALAH ABU (('AYSHA)), THE HEAD OF THE 
AFRICA COMPANY, AN ESO-FUNDED LIBYAN FRONT ORGANIZATION, WILL OPEN 
AN ARABIC TRANSLATION BUSINESS IN MALTA. THE COMPANY WILL BE 
OPERATED BY FNU AL-((TURKI)) WHO IS CURRENTLY EMPLOYED AS AN 
ADVISOR BY THE AFRICA COMPANY. IN AN EFFORT TO GENERATE PERSONAL 
PROFITS AND TO PROVIDE COVER FOR ESO OFFICERS, THE ESO 
REPRESENTATIVE IN MALTA PLANS TO PARTICIPATE IN THE FORMATION OF 
THIS AND SEVERAL OTHER BUSINESS VENTURES IN MALTA IN 1989. 
MOREOVER, MUSBAH AL-((WARFALLI)), THE-HEAD OF THE NEUTRON S.A.R.L. 
COMPANY LTD IN TRIPOLI, PLANS TO FORM AN OILFIELD SERVICES COMPANY 
IN MALTA IN 1989. THIS COMPANY WILL ATTEMPT TO GENERATE PROFITS 
WHILE PROVIDING COVER FOR ESO OFFICERS. (SOURCE COMMENT: 
WARFALLI, A SENIOR ESO OFFICER, WAS EXPELLED FROM ITALY SEVERAL 
YEARS AGO FOR HIS ROLE IN TARGETTING EFFORTS AGAINST LIBYAN 
CITIZENS IN ITALY WHO WERE OPPOSED TO THE REGIME OF LIBYAN LEADER 
COLONEL MU'AMMAR AL-((QADHAFI)). HE LATER LIVED IN MALTA. IT IS 
NOT KNOWN WHETHER WARFALLI PLANS TO RESIDE IN MALTA AND OPERATE 
THIS BUSINESS HIMSELF.) 

ACO: (MID-APRIL 1989).
