AL=QADHDHAFI COMMENTS ON INSIGNIFICANCE OF MAY DAY 
LD021502 Tripoli JANAin English 1412 GMT 2 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli, May 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--In a statement to the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY on the lst May which is called "Worker's Day," Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader 
of the Great First of September Revolution, pointed out "the lst of May has lost its 
Significance because the workers throughout the world nowadays are living under the slavery 
of being wage-workers and suffer from exploitation. No matter how improved their wages 
are, workers are still a kind of slave. As the wage-worker is like a slave for the master, 
he is in fact a temporary slave as long as he is doing his job in return for a wage from 
the employer." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added: "The real liberation of workers is the day they are free from 
the slavery of being wage-workers and rid of employers whether individuals or governments 
which suck the blood of the workers of the world, exploit their efforts in production and 
force them to give up their share ef production for a certain wage." "Those who boast 
every year of defending the workers and their interests, are in reality exploiting and 
enslaving the workers as well as stealing their share of production. For this particular 
reason, the .st May has lost its meaning and has turned into a day when exploiters and 
bourgeoisie repeat empty slogans to deceive the workers inorder to contime exploiting 
them. Workers from now on should not listen to such hypocrisy and deceptive speeches and 
therefore they should replace this day by another which represents their real liberation, 
such as the First of September 1978 when the workers in Libya took over the establishments, 
factories and production units liberating themselves from the slavery of being wage-workers 
and employers and becoming the sole owners forever. By doing this, they have destroyed 
the exploitationist management of the private sector and the bureaucratic management of the 
public sector and set up the people's management, that of the workers, that of the producers, 
and got their full share of production which they improved as they became partners and not 
wage-workers ." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi concluded: "The First of September 1979, the day when the workers 
formed their people's committees and within their production units they set up production 
congresses comprising all producers who became partners for control and decision-making, 
that day is the real one which we should call: ‘International Workers! Liberation Day."

ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS APPEALS TO EUROPE ON IRAN CRISIS 


LD061856 Tripoli JANA in English 1635 GMT 06 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli, May 6, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab 
People's Congress has made an appeal to all European peace-loving democratic forces, in 
which it explained that the USA is trying to force Western countries to take the same 

stand against the Iranian people and by exerting increasing pressure on them to impose 
economic sanctions on Iran. The Iranian-American conflict is quieter and easy to understand. 


It began with Iran's demand for {?extradition) to put him on trial for the crimes he had 
committed against the Iranian people and its demand for thereturn of the [words indistinct ] 
out by the shah to America. His crimes have been recognized by the whole world including 
the U.S. Government which had to refuse the shah asylum when he was on his way to 
Washington. The shah was shocked at the U.S, decision and expressed his disappointment 

in President Carter. The secretariat's appeal added that far from understanding the 
Iranians! just demands, the U.S. administration met these demands by moving its fleet in 
the region, setting up a task force, using threats and freezing Iranian assets in American 
banks ranging between 4.5 and 6.5millian dolla’s as well as imposing economic sanctions 
against Iran and calling upon Western Europe and Japan to follow suit. 


As for the issue of the Americans held hostage in Tehran, the appeal said this could be 
understood within the context of the conflict between Iran and the USA. A different course 
could have been taken to solve it had the U.S, not been so intransigent and determined to 
deal a blow to the Islamic revolution in Iran. 


On the other hand the Carter administration undertook the abortive military landing 
operation, done on the pretence of freeing the American hostages. 


The absurdity of this adventure is now quite evident: Not only has it ridiculed the 
reputation of America and the American militarism, but it has ended up in American losses 
totalling 9 dead, scores of wounded and a number of destroyed aircrafts as well. It is 
also evident that America is calling Western Europe to follow the same adventurous self- 
destructive way.

The permanent secretariat call went on further to urge the pecples of West Europe to take 
their responsibilities in stopping America from dragging their governments into these 
ever-doomed adventures. It added that America is the only country who stands to benefit 
from an increased Middle Eastern and world tension. This way its monopolies can make away 
with soley dominating the wealth of the peoples starting with the oil of the Arab Gulf and 
Iran. And for the purpose of sustaining the insane profits of its monopolies, the Carter 
administration is pushing Western Europe into a battle out of which Western Europe can get no 
gains. The interest of Western Europe is fully tied to the security and peaceful flow of 
trade and economic exchange with the Middle East including Iran, and with the world at large. 


At the end of its call, the Permanent Secretariat went on to say that as representative of 
150 political, trade and professional organisations [from] all the organized forces in the 
Arab world, it calls similar organisations in Western Europe to confront the American 
pressure that is dragging their countries to forge measures antagonistic to the Iranian 
people. This should hopefully be done not just in solidarity with the Iranian people, but 
in the interests of the people of Western Europe themselves.

The two sides affirmed that the imperialist-Zionist-reactionary design against the Arab
