[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has sent a telegram 
to the world heads of state on the subject of transferring the UN 
headquarters from New York. Here is the text of the telegram: 

Ata time when we are on the threshold of the 40th anniversary 
of the United Nations, | consider it appropriate to send you this 
letter, which will perhaps produce a response from you and 
contribute, through what we might undertake in the way of 
collective measures, to the consolidation of this organization, 
which the peoples of the world pinned such great hopes on when 
it was founded. 

The hopes of our peoples, particularly those of the Third World, 
have been disappointed because of the lack of vigor -- not to say 
the complete paralysis -- that has beset this organization over 
recent years, rendering it incabable of assuming its responsibil- 
ities in resolving major international problems, especially those 
concerning the right of peoples to freedom, independence, and 
self-determination. 

The presence of the United Nations in the United States is the 
cause of the paralysis in its efficiency. Moreover, the United 
Nations is now witnessing several attempts to use it as a tool of 
US. policy and its colonialist aims. This can be seen in the 
withdrawal of the United States from UNESCO and its recent 
decision to cut down its contribution to the UN budget from 
1987.

Security Council and preventing the adoption of resolutions to 
punish aggressors and return rights to the oppressed, the United 
States violates its obligations and continues taking repressive 
measures against UN officials and delegations from a number of 
UN member countries to prevent them from exercising their 
duties effectively. This contradicts the letter and spirit of the 
agreement of the country where the headquarters of the organi- 
zation is located.

acts of aggression against nonaligned, small countries. Worse 
still, it has occupied one of these states by force to the knowledge

[Text] Londoh, Muharam 24, October 8, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY -- A Tunisian source close to military circles 
disclosed that a group of U.S. Embassy personnel was seen in 
Hamam al-Shatt Village a day before the Israeli bombing raid 
among whom was the deputy of the military attache. Also, a car 
with diplomatic numbers belonging to U.S. Embassy was seen in 
Al-Manzah area near the PLO office. 

The above source added that the Tunisian authorities were 
informed about the frequent attempts conducted by U.S. diplo- 
mats to place PLO offices under surveillance. Further, he said 
that the operation of Tunisian radars was tampered with from 
9.55 to 10.30 which was the exact period of time of the flight of 
the Isracli fighter aircraft and also the appearance of some of the 
warships of U.S. Sixth Fleet near Bizerte Seaport.

[Text] The Netherlands has decided to remove its soldiers from
