LIBYA

CHAD'S GOUKOUNI OUEDDEI DEPARTS 24 MAY 


LD241314 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 24 May 82 


[Text] Guokouni Oueddei, president of Chad and head of the Transitional National Union 
Government, left Tripoli today. He was seen off by Brig ‘Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander 
in chief of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, members of the general people's committee and a 
number of army officers. 


"NEUTRAL ATTITUDE’ TOWARD CHAD CONFIRMED 


LD241834 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 24 May 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 May (JANA) -- The People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison stressed that the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah is committed to the neutral attitude it has 

adopted toward Chad. A responsible source in the bureau stated that in accordance with its 
neutral attitude, the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah is committed not to interfere 
in the struggle taking place in Chad and will not send any forces there. The source added 
that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah is committed to the resolutions adopted 

by the basic people's congresses regarding social and economic aid to the fraternal people 

of Chad.
