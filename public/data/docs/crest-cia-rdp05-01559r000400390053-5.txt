Summary (AI): An FBIS translation (March 1985) of a Beirut Al-Safir interview with Muammar al-Qadhdhafi covering Iran, Soviet leadership, relations with France and Morocco, Chad, and aid to Lebanon. It contains no places, sites, or events in or near Tripoli.
• Qadhdhafi said he refused secret meetings and that Morocco's King Hassan proposed a secret meeting, which Qadhdhafi rejected in favour of Oujda.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya provides weapons and trains people for the liberation of southern Lebanon.
• Document is part of an FBIS series, take 8 of 8, from Beirut Al-Safir of 20 March 1985.

THE NEW SOVIET LEADERSHIP ((SUBHEAD) ) 

QUESTION: GORBACHEV IS THE NEW SOVIET LEADER. WHAT ARE YOUR 

EXPECTATIONS AS TO HIS ACTION'S TOWARD THE ARAB AREA, HIS VIEWS 

OF SOVIET-LIBYAN RELATIONS, AND HIS POLICIES TOWARD THE TENSE 

INTERNATIONAN SITUATION? 

AL-QADHDHAFI: I DON'T THINK THAT THERE WILL BE ANY CHANGES 

IN THE SOVIET STAND ON THE ARAB AND INTERNATIONAL SITUATIONS. 

SOVIET POLICY IS FIRM, REGARDLESS OF CHANGES IN LEADERSHIP. 

QUESTION: BUT WHEN ANDROPOV CAME TO POWER THERE WAS STRONG 

SOVIET SUPPORT FOR THE ARAB CAUSE, PARTICULARLY SYRIA. 

AL-QADHDHAFI: I DON'T THINK THAT THERE WILL BE ANY CHANGES. 

THE SOVIET STAND IS NOT THAT OF BREZHNEV, OR ANDROPOV, OR 

CHERNENKO OR GORBACHEV. IT IS THAT OF THE SOVIET 

UNION. I BELIEVE THAT ALL SOVIET LEADERS PURSUE THE SAME 

POSITION. IT IS NOT A QUESTION OF INDIVIDUALS. 

QUESTION: WHAT ABOUT YOUR RELATIONS WITH FRANCE? 

AL-QADHDHAFI: THEY ARE GOOD. 

QUESTION: DID YOU MEET WITH PRESIDENT MITTERRAND IN MOROCCO? 

AL-QADHDHAFI: NO, I MET HIM IN CRETE FOR THE FIRST TIME. 

QUESTION: IT HAS BEEN SAID THAT YOU HELD A SECRET MEETING 

IN MOROCCO. 

AL-QADHDHAFI (INTERRUPTING): NO. FIRST OF ALL, I 

REFUSE TO HOLD SECRET MEETINGS. IT IS NOT TRUE THAT THIS HAPPENED, 

BECAUSE I REJECT SECRET MEETINGS OR NEGOTIATIONS. WE ARE NOT 

THUGS. KING HASSAN PROPOSED THAT WE MEET IN FRANCE OR MALTA 

TO DISCUSS THE QUESTION OF UNITY. I TOLD HIM THAT IT WOULD NOT 

BE APPROPRIATE FOR US TO SIGN AN ACCORD ON ARAB UNITY IN A FOREIGN 

COUNTRY. IS OUR LAND OCCUPIED? IF YOU CANNOT COME TO LIBYA, 

I CAN GO TO MOROCCO. I PROPOSED THAT WE HOLD THE MEETING IN 

OUJDA. HE EVEN PROPOSED THAT THE MEETING BE SECRET. I SAID 

NO, IF WE HAVE TO MEET WE SHOULD DO IT PUBLICLY. I DO NOT MEET 

SECRETLY WITH ANY HEAD OF STATE. AL+SADAT WENT SECRETLY TO MOSCOW 

AND HE ANNOUNCED IT ONLY WHEN HE RETURNED. 

QUESTION: WHAT ARE THE DEVELOPMENTS IN THE CHADIAN SITUATION, 

FOLLOWING THE FRENCH TROOPS' WITHDRAWAL AND THE ESCALATION OF THE 

OPPOSITION? 

AL-~QADHDHAFI: HABRE WILL CERTAINLY FALL.
