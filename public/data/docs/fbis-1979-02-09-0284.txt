LIBYA

CSSR'S STROUGAL TALKS OF FRANK, OBJECTIVE MEETINGS 


LDO081904 Tripoli JANA in English 1438 GmT 8 Feb 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 8 Feb, JANA--The prime minister of the Czechoslovak Socialist Republic, 
Lubomir Strougal, left Tripoli yesterday evening after ending this three-day visit to the 
Jamahiriyah,. 


There was an official send off for Strougal at Tripoli International Airport, headed by 
staff Major 'Abd as-Salaam Ahmad Jallud, Present were some of the secretaries, the under 
secretary of the Foreign Secretariat, directors of departments, director of protocol at the 
Foreign Secretariat, heads of foreign missions accredited to Jamahiriyah, and the Czech 
ambassador to Jamahiriyah. 


The Czech prime minister gave a statement to a JANAcorrespondent in which he expressed his 
pleasure at the positive results achieved by his visit to Jamahiriyah. He said, "We have 
held negotiations in Jamahiriyah dealing with matters of common interest."

He pointed out that these negotiations were characterized by frankness and objectivity in 
strengthening bilateral cooperation in the light of the results achieved by the historic 
visit of Colonel Multammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great lst of September Revolution and 
secretary-general of the General People!s Congress to Prague last year. He said: "We 

have agreed to carefully implement what Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and President Gustav 
Husak had agreed upon." 


Strougal described the Libyan Arab-Czech relations as excellent and that the two sides will 
work towards strengthening these relations for what serves the two friendly people, the 
Arab cause and the liberation causes in the world. He made clear that the continuous meet- 
ings of the two sides will ensve with further positive results. 


Concerning a question about the defeatist stands and capitulatory initiatives of the defeated 
Egyptian president, Strougal said: "We denounce and strongly condemn these stands. The 
Egyptian president could not have done worse than this." Strougal added, "We completely 
support the progressive Arab countries and have great hopes in the Steadfastness and 
Confrontation Front." 


At the end of his statement, the Czech prime minister stressed that his country supports the 
just cause of the Palestinian people. He said, “We believe that this cause will triumph and 
we are prepared to offer all aid to the Palestinian Arab people in their struggle against 
Zionism."
