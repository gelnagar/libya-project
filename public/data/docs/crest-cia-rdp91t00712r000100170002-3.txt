PROBABLE MISSILE-~READY BUNKERS 
AL JUFRAH AIRFIELD, LIBYA (SWN). 

25X1 

1. SIGNIFICANCE: SIX PROBABLE MISSILE-READY BUNKERS, CAPABLE OF 
HANDLING ATR-TO-SURFACE MISSILES (ASM), HAVE BEEN CONSTRUCTED 
AT AL JUFRAH AIRFIELD IN LIBYA. (SWN) . 

2. REMARKS: CONTINUING ANALYSIS OF 25X11 
TMAGERY REVEALED THAT SIX PROBABLE MISSILE-READY BUNKERS (ONE 
NOT ON GRAPHIC), HAVE BEEN CONSTRUCTED IN THE MEDIUM BOMBER 
DISPERSAL AREA. THE BUNKERS ARE BEHIND, OR IN THE IMMEDIATE 
VICINITY OF 12 REVETTED HARDSTANDS (ONE NOT ON GRAPHIC), WHICH 
ARE CAPABLE OF HOUSING ADVANCED, MEDIUM, ASM-CAPABLE BOMBERS. 
A VEHICLE TURN-AROUND AREA IS IN FRONT OF EACH BUNKER, AND AN 
ACCESS ROAD CONNECTS EACH BUNKER WITH THE REAR ENTRANCE TO THE 
REVETTED HARDSTANDS. OTHER FACILITIES IN THIS AREA INCLUDED 
SIX PERSONNEL BUNKERS AND SIX PROBABLE THREE-BAY REVETMENTS 
(ONE NOT ANNOTATED ON GRAPHIC) THAT WILL PROBABLY BE USED FOR 
GROUND SUPPORT EQUIPMENT. THE DISPERSAL AREA HAS BEEN 
CONSTRUCTED SO THAT ONE PROBABLE MISSILE-READY BUNKER, ONE 
PERSONNEL BUNKER AND ONE THREE-~BAY REVETMENT CAN PROVIDE 
IMMEDIATE SUPPORT FOR AIRCRAFT OPERATIONS IN EACH PAIR OF 
BOMBER REVETMENTS. A MISSILE STORAGE/SUPPORT FACILITY (NOT ON 
GRAPHIC) IS UNDER CONSTRUCTION APPROXIMATELY 1 NM EAST OF THE 
WEST-NORTHWEST END OF THE RUNWAY. (SWN) 

ANALYST COMMENTS: CURRENTLY, LIBYA DOES NOT HAVE A BOMBER 
ASSOCIATED ASM CAPABILITY AND AMONG THE WARSAW PACT COUNTRIES, 
ONLY THE SOVIET UNION POSSESSES THIS CAPABILITY. IT APPEARS 
THAT THE BOMBER DISPERSAL AREA HAS BEEN CONSTRUCTED TO SUPPORT 
RAPID-RESPONSE AIRCRAFT OPERATIONS, A CAPABILITY THAT HAS NOT 
BEEN IDENTIFIED AT MEDIUM BOMBER AIRFIELDS IN THE SOVIET 
UNION. THE SOVIETS ARE REPORTEDLY INVOLVED IN THE 
CONSTRUCTION OF AL JUFRAH AIRFIELD, 260 NM SOUTHEAST OF 
Baie (TRIPOLI) AND 120 NM SOUTH OF THE GULF OF SIDRA.
