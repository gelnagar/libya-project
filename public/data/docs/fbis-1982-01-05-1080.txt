Al-'Ubaydi Address 
LD041448 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1100 GMT 4 Jan 82 


[Speech by ‘Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the Popular Committee of the People's 
Bureau for Foreign Liaison, to the seventh session of the General People's Congress at 
the Al-Amal Hall in Tripoli -- live] 


[Text] Before I speak about foreign policy, I wish to make some remarks concerning the 
report of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison, as approved by the people's basic con- 
ferences. I must point out that the realization of the popular work or objectives in 
regard to our foreign relations was completed last January following the meeting of the 
General People's Congress: the popular masses marched on the Foreign Secretariat and 
formed a People's Committee for the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison. This people's 
committee is no different from any of the other people's committees formed after the 
popular revolution of 1973. 


In pursuing our foreign policy, we have been guided by the decisions of the people's 
congresses and by the tasks set forth in resolution No 1356 of the General People's 
Committee for 1981, pertaining to the organization of the People's Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison. In our work, we have constantly taken into consideration the norms and guide- 
lines laid down by the Great 1 September Revolution, since its beginning, for our actions 
in Arab, African, Islamic, international and nonaligned affairs. 


As for the report, I am anxious to refer to the current Arab situation. The past year 

saw a series of Zionist challenges to the Arab nation directed against its entity and 
existence and affirming the so-called theory of Israeli security which aims at imposing 
guar‘ianship on the Arabs and their progress and even on their reactions to any aggression. 
The past year also witnessed and affirmed open American-Zionist aggression against our 
Arab nation. In short, there was constant aggression against Lebanon and the Palestine 
resistance. There was also a constant threat to Syria, particularly after it moved 
missiles to the Biqa'. There was also the aggression against the Iraqi nuclear reactor 
and there were constant violations of Saudi airspace and recently the Iraqi airspace. 


What has been the Arab reaction to this aggression and challenge? The Arab nation's reply, 
or rather the official one at least in terms of the Arab League, has not equaled the 
challenges and threats facing our Arab nation, nor has it equaled the ambitions of the 
peoples of the Arab nation. 


We always received the blows and then called for meetings which came out with cheap state- 
ments. We used to receive expressions of solidarity and support from the international 
community as though all the Arab nation need«? [was] -- and [as though] all its objectives 
were -- to receive the blows and then welcome support and sympathy, whereas the real work 
for repelling any aggression involves serious military work that is carried out by the 
Arab nation as a whole. 


We all have witnessed this phenomenon. The last Zionist action was the annexation of the 
Syrian Golan Heights after the Zionist enemy, supported by the United States, came to 
realize that Syria is the stronghold of steadfastness that prevents the imposition of any 
capitulationist solutions. Undoubtedly, the aim of this was to carry out the enemy's 
expansionist policy and to draw Syria by force into solutions which neither Syria nor the 
Syrian people have accepted or will ever accept. The reply to this annexation decision 
did not even extend to statements of support. We are waiting to see what decisions will 
be made by the UN Security Council. It is certain that the United States will veto the 
imposition of any sanctions against the Zionist enemy. We will then wait and see what 
the Arab League and the Arab peoples will do after such a decision, particularly Arab 
League has been summoned to an emergency meeting.

This is the deteroirating Arab situation which prompted the commander of the Great 1 September 


Revolution, Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi [prolonged applause], to state during the 
celebration of the 11 June anniversary of the American forces [presumably referring to 
the date when U.S. bases in Libya were closed] that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah was prepared to forget its differences with the Arab brothers for the sake 
of achieving a unified Arab action against the common enemy. Thus we have resumed 
relations with fraternal Morocco, Iraq and Saudi Arabia. The Libyan leadership and Arab 
Libyan people have made only one stipulation for the restoration of these relations, the 
break up of which they have not been responsible for: that the Arab nation adopts a 
serious stand against the constant U.S.-Zionist offensive against our Arab nation. 


As to the African field, I shall be brief. The Jamahiriyah played a role in supporting the 
people and Government of Chad. There was a feverish reactionary campaign against our 
country which went so far as to accuse us of occupying and colonizing Chad. The Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has always affirmed its adherence to its agreement with 
the Chadian Government for defending the country and leaving it whenever its government 
wished. We have proved to the world the truthfulness of the Jamahiriyah's claim, that 

we went [to Chad] only to help the fraternal people and to affirm African solidarity and 
the African ability to rely on itself. We have disproved to the world the allegations 

made by imperialism and reaction. Our armed forces have now returned home after having 
played this great humanitarian role in Africa. 


There were also attempts by reactionary quarters in Africa instigated by the United States 
to prevent the Jamahiriyah from acting as a venue for the forthcoming African summit 
conference. Yet Africa has proved its ability to act freely and to make its own decisions. 
The Africans have proved that the Jamahiriyah is in the forefront of the African countries 
which are anxious to safeguard Africa's freedom and independence. Thus it was decided to 
hold the conference in our country. 


Other prominent events of the year were the attempts by some reactionary quarters in 
Africa to restore relations between some of the African countries and the Zionist enemy 
after the OAU had decided to boycott the Zionist regime as being an imperialist racist 
regime which is no different from South Africa. We can affirm that the United States is 
behind such attempts, in addition to the agents in the continent. But the Jamahiriyah 
and other sincere Africans are working to halt such a dangerous line through cordial 
contacts with all the African brothers. 


This is what I wanted to say briefly with respect to the report. The efforts exerted in 
the foreign field are in fact collective efforts .conducted by the popular foreign bureau 


and the Popular Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison and our people's bureaus 


abroad. 


As to the agenda item pertaining to the meaning of nonalignment, I must make frank 
references to the background upon which the item is based. 


Since the Reagan administration assumed office in the United States, it has been conducting 
an organized, continuous campaign against the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
and its leadership. It has levelled a series of accusations which have no foundation in 
truth whatsoever. On the other hand, the Reagan administration accuses the Jamahiriyah 

and its leadership of threatening neighboring African countries and of being a spearhead 
for Soviet influence in the African continent.

This political campaign has been coupled with a concerted information campaign, an 
intensive campaign, which has reached the point of trying to make the African and even 
the Libyan people believe that the commander of the revolution is behind all these 
accusations. Indeed, their imagination and blind rancor have led them even to hatch 
plots to liquidate the commander of the revolution. This is nothing new for the American 
administration. It has liquidated other progressive African leaders. 


Then came the U.S. maneuvers in waters which have been declared Libyan territorial 
waters since 1973, by this I mean the Gulf of Sidra. This resulted in a confrontation 
last August between our planes and the U.S. planes. 


It was evident here that the Libyan Arab people were exposed to a genuine threat by 
a superpower which was supposed to work with other superpowers to safeguard security 
and peace in the world. 


All this led to a review of the concept of nonalignment during the celebrations of the 
Great 1 September Revolution last year. In raising this point, the leadership had a 
number of facts before it: if there are any differences between us and the United 
States over waters which we insist are our territorial waters that form part of our 
country, we do not believe dealing with such a matter necessitates the dispatch of 
fleets to the gulf. It was evident that the aim behind dispatching the fleets was to 
provoke the Jamahiriyah and clash with it as a pretext for wider military operations. 


It was evident to the leadership and the Libyan Arab people that these planes took off 
from aircraft carriers that did not leave from the United States but from bases in 
neighboring countries which are members of NATO, which in turn is led by the United 
States. Hence the clear statement by the leadership that the United States was not in 
this alone but was a member of NATO, and had it not been for the U.S. bases and facili- 
ties in Europe, which is a member of NATO [as heard], the United States would not have 
been able to stay in the Mediterranean. 


In his clarifications during the celebrations of the Great 1 September Revolution, the 
commander affirmed that there cannot be neutrality should a third world war break out. 


There was yet another clear fact: The Libyan Arab people will remain neutral and will 
uphold nonalignment as long as it is kept away from U.S. pressures, challenges and pro- 
vocations. The Libyan Arab people do not seek war or join axes. It was the United 
States that forced the Libyans to reconsider their neutral status. What I mean is that 
the constant aggressions and threats were what prompted the leadership to raise this 
matter and what prompted the popular conferences to place it on their agendas. 


It is evident that our membership in the nonaligned bloc lays upon our shoulders certain 
commitments. It also lays counter commitments on the shoulders of the nonaligned group. 

In as much as we in the Jamahiriyah are anxious to protect our neutrality and nonalign- 
ment and ensure that our country is free from any bases whatsoever, particularly as the 
Libyan Arab people have closed down five bases, of which you all are aware, and therefore 
will not be happy to have any alternative bases if they have a choice and are kept away 
from dangers -- the nonaligned group is expected to play an effective political role in 
protecting its members and checking such aggression by a superpower against the Jamahiriyah. 


Another point: The neighboring countries, which are members of NATO, are required not 
to allow their lands and bases -- perhaps against the will of their peoples -- to be 
used as a springboard for any aggression against the Jamahiriyah.

Once we believe that aggressions against the Jamahiriyah are checked, that the Nonaligned 
Mcvement protects its members, and that the countries neighboring the Jamahiriyah or any other non- 
aligned country will not allow their territories and ports to be used as springboards 

for aggression, then there will be no need for the people of the Socialist People's 

Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah to reconsider its neutrality. But if there is aggression and 

threats to the Libyan Arab people and their achievements, then we have no alternative 

but to ally ourselves with the forces which stand by us and by the liberation movements. 

These forces are well known to us all, and we have no alternative but to ally ourselves 

with the enemy of our enemy, and our enemy in this case is the United States of America. 


It is evident that the United States seems to overlook several facts: that by its 
hostile activities against the Jamahiriyah it has made Europe feel the dangers inherent 
in its policy and thus take a different stand in support of the Jamahiriyah in its 
conflict with the United States. Most certainly, the Libyan Arab people appreciate 
this stand, and will always prompt Libya to pursue a policy toward these peoples and 
governments compatible with these stands. The more they stand against the aggressions, 
the more we stand by these peoples and deal with them, and the more we all ensure that 
our Mediterranean region remains free from dangers, wars and threats. 


There is yet another fact evident to us all: When the United States says that the 
commander of the revolution is behind all the false things it accuses him of, it forgets 
that the Libyan Arab people, with its popular conferences and committees, and with its 
unions and federations and revolutionary committees, and with its armed people, have 

all affirmed that they all defend the commander of the revolution and support his 
thoughts and political lines in the Arab, African and international fields. 


One more fact: It has been proved that alliance with the United States and running 
after U.S. imperialist policies means the end of many regimes. For example, the 
imperial regime in Iran collapsed before the very eyes of U.S. imperialism. President 
as-Sadat was assassinated before the very eyes of the American CIA. Somoza in Nicaragua 
also fell before the will of his people. 


We in the Jamahiriyah are confident that these U.S. approaches and this U.S. terrorism 
will not bother us and will only help to prove our views. 


All that we wish to emphasize is that the international groups, that is, the United 
Nations, the Nonaligned Movement, or the European countries will put an end to this 
aggression and arrogance by the United States. Our Arab people are anxious to safeguard 
their neutrality and nonalignment. This is the conclusion I have come out with by 
following the resolution of the basic popular conferences. Thank you.

[Speech by Tawfiq as-Salhah, member of the Regional Command of the Syrian Ba'th Party, 
at seventh session of General People's Congress at the Al-Amal Hall in Tripoli -- live] 


[Excerpts] Brother strugglers, members of the congress, allow me to thank you for the 
invitation to take part in your meetings. 


The two experiments in the two sister regions have great and many common denominators. 
There is a unionist inclination, and there is one socialist inclination, and there 

is one political line of hostility to imperialism, Zionism and the agent reaction linked 
to them. There is the deep belief of the two revolutions in strengthening, deepening 

and developing relations with the forces of liberation and progress in the world and 

with the socialist camp, headed by the Soviet Union, which is friendly to our Arab people.

Moreover, there is the great common denominator that we in both regions absolutely 

believe that power lies in the hands of the people, and that the people are the source 

of everything. This is -ufficient to realize the unity between us. In any event, whether 
the unity comes now or if it is delayed, every citizen should know that we are in one 
trench and that we are one army. When there is an aggression against the sister Libyan 
region, the enemies will find the Syrian valiant armed forces fighting on revolutionary 
Libyan territory. [applause] Also, the enemies will find the Libyan Armed Forces 
fighting on Syrian territory. This has been decided. We are in one trench fighting 
against the Arab nation's enemies wherever they are. 


We all must know, regardless of what some people say, that we brothers maintain our 
positive neutrality and that we belong to the nonaligned group. This is what we believe 
in. We differentiate between enemies and the friends. 


America should know that it is the number one enemy of the Arab nation, that it is 
responsible for the destruction, the destitution, the occupation, and for every drop of 
blood being shed in our Arab homeland. There is no friendship for America in our Arab 
homeland, it is only from the agents. Our friend is the Soviet Union, with whose weapons 
we are defending ourselves, and which stands by our just stances and on the side of 

our national and pan-Arab causes, and the repressed struggling peoples. 


The U.S. plot will not succeed in our Arab region, regardless of the number of American 
bases, the forces it sets up or the agents it finds. The U.S. plot will not succeed. 
We know this will cost us very dearly. We also know that the price of freedom and 
dignity is also very costly. We have decided to stand fast and to fight to the last 
drop of our blood, regardless of how great the plot, or how many its parties and 
(?choices) become. 


However much America and its agents try to pressure us, they will be mistaken. Either 
we live with our heads held high with dignity, or we die also with our heads held high 
and with dignity. [applause] 


If America thinks that revolutionary Syria, or revolutionary Libya or the Palestinian 
revolution is an easy prey, then it is also mistaken. It will find all in one trench 
fighting it, and triumphing behind them the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front, all 
the Arab masses and all the forces of progress and liberation in the world, and the 
socialist camp headed by the friendly Soviet Union. The treaty of friendship between 
us and the Soviet Union is to achieve a strategic balance in the region, especially, 
as Brother ‘Abd al-'Ati mentioned, in view of the strategic cooperation agreement 
between America and the Zionist entity in occupied Palestine. 


If they have taken an American-Zionist decision to annex the Golan in reply to our 
national and pan-Arab stand, this will not affect our steadfastness. The Golan terri- 
tory to us is like the Sinai territory, the West Bank and every occupied inch of our 
Arab territory. We shall go to battle at the appropriate time to liberate all the Arab 
territory. 


Let America and its agent know that there will be no peace in the region other than the 
peace we, the masters of the rifle, know, represented in unconditional, full Israeli 
withdrawal from occupied Arab territories, the restoration of firm rights to our 
Palestinian Arab people, including the establishment of their independent state on 
their national territory. No peace, no negotiations and no surrender of one inch of 
territory! America and its agent must know this. 


He who signed the Camp David agreements was killed. The traitor Anwar as-Sadat was 
killed despite the imperialist and reactionary protection accorded to him. Let Husni 
Mubarak and all other agents know that their fate will be no different from that of 
As-Sadat because the path of traitors is death, and life is only for the honorable.

FBI FAILS TO PROVE HIT TEAMS SENT TO U.S. 


LD041532 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 4 Jan 82 


[Text] The U.S. FBI Director William Webster acknowledged the failure of the U.S. 
administration to produce any evidence to confirm its allegations about the existence 

of an alleged Libyan hit squad to assassinate President Reagan and some of his aides. 
Last night, AFP reported a statement by the FBI director made to the ABC television 
network, in which he said: We have never been able to get any evidence concerning 

the existence of an assassination squad directed from Libya to assassinate the President 
and some of his aides. 


The JANA political commentator made the following comment: The U.S. administration has 
flagrantly and intentionally spread fabrications and false allegations against the 
Jamahiriyah in the course of its conspiracies against and hostility toward the Libyan 
Arab people. The U.S. administration's attempt to prove that the Jamahiriyah has sent 
an alleged assassination squad to America to assassinate President Reagan has met with 
failure. 


The commentator adds that the acknowledgement of the FBI director has come to confirm 
this failure and to show piblic opinion that the U.S. administration is unable to face 
the American people and iniernational public opinion with facts and evidence, which the 
Jamahiriyah has asked for en insisted upon. The FBI director's statement reveals the 
falsehood of President Reagan's administration, and his statement to ABC is considered 
as evidence that the allegations which this administration has spread through the CIA 
and the American press are false, testifying to President Reagan's foolishmess and 
clearly proving his lying and superficial mentality.
