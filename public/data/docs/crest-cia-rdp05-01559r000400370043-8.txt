Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a 12 March 1976 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland broadcast reports Qadhdhafi's rally speech in Tripoli, marking the burning of the last shanty in Tripoli and boasting of housing construction. He also attacked Egyptian and American intelligence over the recruitment of journalist Al-Muhayshi (OCR is heavily degraded).
• The speech said 75 million dinars were allocated for housing in the 6 years before the revolution and over 1 billion dinars in the 6 years after, with more than 100,000 houses built.
• At the start of the revolution there were more than 90,000 shanties and tents in Libya, per the speech.
• Last year (1975) the last shanty in Benghazi was reportedly removed.
• 1976-03-12: Popular rally marking the burning of the last shanty in Tripoli; Qadhdhafi gave a speech attacking Egyptian and US intelligence over the recruitment of Al-Muhayshi.
