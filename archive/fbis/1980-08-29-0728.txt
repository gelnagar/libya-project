LIBYA

JANA EDITOR ATTACKS AS-SADAT!S CHAD POLICY 
LD261556 Tripoli JANA in English 1416 iMT 5 Aug 50 
2? I > =) 


[Text] Tripoli, 28 Aug, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The political editor of JANA said that 
As#Sadat has become a source of danger to the independence and inity of Chad following 
his role as a policeman for colonialism in Africa and the Arab region and open door 
policy which allows American bases in Egypt from which attacks can be launched against 
the freedom of the Arab nation, the progressive forces, and the African people, 


The editor added that As-Sadat is aggravating the situation in Africa by sending arms and 
money for more bloodshed in Chad. He has even started training mercenaries led by 
Bishara Showayk who according to Prolinat sources had been expelled for theft, 


As-Sadat has harbored Showayk and asked him to train a certain number of mercenaries to 
extend the war in Chad. He has done this according to the colonial saying "divide and 
rule," As-Sadat has even ordered Showayk to make anti-Libya statements to let the new 
As-Sadat plan come through. But the Jamahiriyah which has always stood with the people 
of Chad and initiated conferences for national conciliation in Chad will not listen to 
the lies of agents but will continue to Support the friendly people of Chad to put an end 
to the civil war there and to enable the Chadians to achieve progress and prosperity.
