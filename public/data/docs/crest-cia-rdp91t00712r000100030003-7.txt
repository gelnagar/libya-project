Summary (AI): A 31 August 1983 NPIC imagery note reports five Czechoslovak 152mm M-1978 self-propelled guns/howitzers newly observed at a Tripoli army barracks at Prinn, probably delivered through Tripoli port by a Krakow-class ship. The document says the barracks is about 5 nm southeast of the Tripoli port facilities.
• The Czechoslovak 152mm self-propelled gun/howitzer M-1978 was newly observed in Libya.
• Five guns were in front of a vehicle storage shed at the Tripoli barracks.
• Five guns on railcars were also observed at Ploce port, Yugoslavia, where a Krakow-class ship was berthed.
• Coordinates given: 32-50-46N 013-14-13E.
• 1983-08-31: Imagery observation of five Czechoslovak 152mm M-1978 self-propelled guns/howitzers, first observed in Libya and first outside Czechoslovakia.
