Summary (AI): A 1981 Washington Post Magazine profile of Kevin Mulcahy, a former associate of ex-CIA operatives Edwin Wilson and Frank Terpil, who became a source for reporters on Libya-related deals. It covers his media role and the U.S. prosecutors' displeasure, with no specific Tripoli sites or events.
• Alleged 1970s deals included secret shipments of explosives from the United States to Libya and alleged training of Libyan terrorists by Green Berets.
• Mulcahy said he quit in 1976 after deciding his bosses' business was distasteful.
• A U.S. Attorney's office said Mulcahy led reporters in London to a safe house sometimes used by Wilson, which was under British police observation.
• Mulcahy reportedly flies under an assumed name and says he has been compensated by CBS, The New York Times (travel expenses), Time, Newsweek, BBC and CBC.
