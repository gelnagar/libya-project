Borge Leads Nicaraguan Delegation 
LD301716 Tripoli JANA in English 1408 GMT 30 Aug 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulhijja 3, August 30, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Commander Tomas Borge, revolutionary 
leader and minister of the interior in Nicaragua accompanied by 
(Mrs Dorsy Betty Griona), assistant minister of interior and 
(Arno Fawarina), vice chairman of international relations of the 
Sandinista Front have arrived in Tripoli to participate in the 
celebrations of the Libyan Arab people commemorating the | 5th 
anniversary of the Great Al-Fatih revolution. 


Tomas Borge explained in a press release to JANA correspon- 
dent that this visit came to confirm their depth of relations and 
friendship between the Libyan Arab and Nicaraguan peoples and 
to confirm their stand side by side with the Libyan Arab people 
in their confrontation of imperialist plots conspired against their 
security and peace.
