JANA DENOUNCES HAIG'S FALKLAND ISLANDS MISSION 


LD191634 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 19 Apr 82 


[Text] U.S. Secretary of State Alexander Haig was concluded his latest round of talks 
with the president and foreign minister of Argentina. These talks went on for 12 hours 
during the past 3 days in an attempt to find a diplomatic formula for an understanding 
between Buenos Aires and London on the Falkland Islands. JANA's political editor 
comments on the U.S. secretary of state's mission between Argentina and Britain: 


The U.S. secretary's shuttle, despite its motives and dimensions, exposes a fact to the 
Arabs of America, those who rely on it, (?finance) it and refrain from any pan-Arab action 
to deter the Zionist enemy. This fact is that the central cause of the Arabs does not 

and will not fall within the scope of interest of the U.S. administration; it lies outside 
these interests. Moreover, it is also a means used by the U.S. administration to black- 
mail and to bolster its interest and to find agents to serve as lackeys to promote these 
interests and these aims, which involve weakening the cause and lengthening the Zionist 
arm to achieve harmony with reaction in order to perpetuate occupation and domination 

and to (?stretch out) in our Arab homeland. 


The JANA political editor adds that the U.S. administration, whose main concern is its 
own interests and the interests of its allies, has assigned its tyrannical secretary 

of state to express these concerns in this manner, while it has done nothing worth 
mentioning for the cause of the Arabs, other than to dispatch elements of the agents of 
politics and blackmail and full [word indistinct] to the enemy to convince some regimes, 
which revolve in America's orbit, that they should rely on the de facto hegemony and 
occupation and to continue to call for capitulation, submission and apathy. 


Prince Fahd, a graduate of the elementary schools for teaching the Koran, beseeches 

God to guide the Palestinians on the West Bank and in the Gaza Strip to resort to common 
sense and to be patient. The sultan of Muscat openly invites America and Britain to 
secretly protect the sultanate from communism. Field Marshal Numayri in Khartoum secretly 
secretly meets with the terrorist Shamir and banks on the success of a group of acrobats. 
Moreover, somebody from the ruling family in Mogadishu belches and turns away from the 
worries of the Palestinians and the issues of the Arab homeland. Meanwhile, America uses 
its veto at the Security Council against Arab rights and sends Philip Habib to pressure 
and threaten the princes, to meet the Lebanese to intimidate them and to meet with 
journalists and lie to them. 


How do they expect America, whose virtues they praises, or its imperialist administration, 
to be partial toward Arab rights? What kind of tragegy are we in! Among us are those 
who have descrated Mecca and advocated capitulation and ones who have recruited themselves 
for circus groups and lied to the people with feeble preaching. America is fully 
confident in their presence, hence its interest in the Falklands and in places of 
occupation in Muscat and Khartoum and the oil sources in the peninsula. Moreover, it 

is confident in seeing government as a toy ideally serving U.S. interests, better even 
than the Zionist Kissinger, the soldier Haig and Nixon, the man of the Watergate scandal, 
served those interests. 


NEW OPPOSITION FRONT FORMED TO OVERTHROW REGIME 


PM191331 London ASH-SHARQ AL-AWSAT in Arabic 16 Apr 82 p 3 


[Unattributed report] 


[Text] London -- An organization opposed to the regime of Libyan chairman Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi was recently formed in Europe. It includes a number of prominent Libyan 
figures, some of whom are officials close to Al-Qadhdhafi.

This front is working secretly, in Libya and abroad, to bring down the regime, according 
to Ahmad Ibrahim Ahwas, an official of the front who was once charge d'affaires at the 
Libyan Embassy in Guyana before resigning his post "in protest of Al-Qadhahafi's 
malpractices and his subversive role in a number of Arab and African states." 


Ahwas told ASH-SHARQ AL-AWSAT the opposition front is now making preparations for a number 
of steps it intends to take with the aim of toppling the present regime and promoting 

an alternative regime satisfactory and acceptable to the Libyan people. He added: The 
method of dialogue is no longer workable and we must bring the regime down in some other 
way. He also said: "The National Front for the Salvation of Libya" is now working its 
way toward that objective through its leaders, branches and offices in various countries. 
It is now making preparations for large-scale demonstrations to be followed by other 
measures in light of future developments. 


Ahwas described Al-Qadhdhafi as an “evil man with no real desire to raise the standards 

of the Libyan people or even give them the opportunity to improve their conditions." 

He added: "Al-Qadhdhafi imposed himself on the people and is ruling the country through 
a clique which humiliates the people in a state where there are no government organs, 

no projects and no departments to look after the people and serve public interests. He 

continued: Al-Qadhdhafi is now isolated at home. Because of his practices there is no 

real loyalty to him. 


The front, he said, is mobilizing people and resources against the regime at all levels, 
in order to expose and isolate the regime and enable the people to destroy it. He added: 
The present stage is very crucial and we do not believe that Al-Qadhdahfi will be able 

to last mich longer. With my own eyes I have seen the economic ruin, moral degeneration, 
ideological deviation, administrative chaos, political confusion and popular resentment 
in all sectors. My views are in fact the views of the vast majority of the afflicted 
Libyan people who are doing their best to get rid of this terrible nightmare. 


He also said: I was deeply pained by Al-Qadhdhafi's foolish acts and his disregard for 
all sanctities and values. I was also surprised by those who adopted and propagated his 
false ideas. I used to wonder about what was happening and how to confront it. I knew 
that I had to play a positive role myseif. This feeling grew stronger within me as 
Al-Qadhdhafi's arrogant regime persisted more and more in its flagrant exercise of all 
kinds of despotism and injustice which has led to more and more voices being raised 
against him at home and abroad. In my opinion, silence can no longer be justified on any 
grounds.
