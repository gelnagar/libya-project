LIBYA

FOREIGN SECRETARIAT ON HOSTAGE SITUATION IN IRAN 
LD221435 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1415 GMT 22 Nov 79 LD 
[Report on statement by spokesman of the foreign secretariat--date not given] 
[Text] The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah does not support any action against 
diplomatic missions and their staff members, and in principle it is against any action 
directed against these missions and the holding of their staff members as hostages. 
The spokesman added that what is happening in Iran now is a matter that concerns the 
Iranians alone, They are better placed to assess it. In our view the hostages should 
be released, 
As to the language of using force and the threat to use it against the Iranian people, 
this is a matter which we reject. We will stand by Iran against any aggression, because the 
Great 1 September revolution is a natural ally of the Islamic people!s revolution in Iran, 


The spokesman urged the Arab nation and the Islamic world to stand by Iran in the event it 
is subjected to any aggression.
