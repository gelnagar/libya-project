LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Liberation Eavoy 
L£D021532 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1500 GMT 2 May 87 


[Text] Tripoli, 2 May — The brother leader of the revolution has 
received the coordinator of the Popular Union for the Liberation 
of Guadeloupe [PULG], who expounded to him the sufferings of 
the Guadeloupe people due to the French colonization of their 
country. 


The coordinator of the PULG asked the brother leader to support 
and bolster the cause of the Guadeloupe people by supporting it 
[the PULG] in international organizations and conferences. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Reunion Liberation Official 
LD041315 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 1130 GMT 4 May 87 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has received the 
secretary general of the Liberation Movement of Reunion 
[MLR], who has explained the suffering of the Reunionese people 
due to the French colonization of its [Reunion’s] territories. 


The secretary general of the Reunion mo<ment conveyed to the 
brother leader of the revolution a tormenting picture of the 
dreadful danger resulting from the French nuclear tests carried 
out on Pacific Ocean and Indian Ocean islands. He said the 
masses on these islands live in terror and are threatened with 
extermination due to the great number of nuclear tests carried 
out by French colonialism. He also informed the brother leader 
that France has carried out more than 60 nuclear tests on one of 
the islands situated in the ocean and that this has led to the 
sinking of this island. 


The MLR secretary general asked the brother leader of the 
revolution to stimulate support by world states and to warn them 
at all international meetings against the permanent danger 
threatening these islands due to the arrogance of French colo- 
nialism and the fact that it [France] continues to carry out nuclear 
tests.
