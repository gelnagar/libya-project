• Qadhdhafi says Libya opens its camps and arsenals to liberation fighters in South Africa, Namibia, Palestine and Central and South America.

I have come, brothers, basically to salute the fraternal people of

Robert Mugabe. I have come to declare my happiness at the fact

strugglers of the ZAPU [Zimbabwe African People’s Union) and

I have also come to meet with the fighters of South Africa,
