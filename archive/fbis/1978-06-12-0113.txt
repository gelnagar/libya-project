You must understand that freedom has its price, However, as Maj 'Abd as-Salam Jallud 

once said: I am sorry that the evacuation was achieved without half of us being killed 
fighting for it. Half of us should have been killed before the Americans left so that we 
might appreciate the value of striking at the imperialist forces and the foreign troops and 
consequently appreciate the value of freedom, I consider this historical bad luck: to be 
able to evict the five American bases without a fight and also to evict the British base 

on Tobruk without a fight; this is a negative factor, If half of you had been killed in 
this land you are now standing cn, then you would appreciate the value of this land,

Lidya possess, Before us we see long-range strategic bombers. They are extremely 
dangerous aireraft. Before us we have fighter planes, interceptors and all types of

USSR possesses. These areonly token units, They are only part of the hundreds of fighters,

have and about which I spoke, 


As I said, freedom has its price. These planes need pilots. But should we consider 
those who smuggle their children out to London and who go to London during the month of 
Ramadan Libyans or Britons? 


When the air academy asked for students to train as pilots in the air force, they began 
to smuggle their children out; some dressed them as girls and others dressed them as 
(?wolves) and smuggled them out to Germany, the United States and Britain. Should we 
bring them to account or not? 


These people enriched themselves in Libya before the revolution and after it, But now 
they are smuggling their children cut. They want to keep the planes grounded. These 
planes belong to you Libyans whether you have heard of them or not, 


You can decide your own fate, The decision rests with the Libyan people through the 
people's congresses, These planes belong to you. If you want them to fly, let them 
fly. If you just want to display and watch them every year, then do so and put incense 
on them as you would on a sacred idol, 


Today we greet those who volunteered, because up to now it has not been compulsory; the 
young men who volunteered have become pilots and now flythemost modern Eastern and 
Western aircraft. They are being flown by Libyans, sons of peasants and workers, sons of 
the good earth and loyal to this land. They are not flown by those who rob the land 

and live in other countries but by the sons of peasants, workers and toilers, These 
young men are the ones who volunteer every year and fill the air academy and they now 

fly hundreds of aircraft. However, there are hundreds of aircraft and more still need 
pilots, 


Do you want to bring in mercenaries? Then we will draw up a budget for them and give 
half of it to the mercenaries [words indistinct], There are mercenaries in the world 
ready to fly aircraft. You can decide this issue at popular conferences. You must 
make the decision, What is right and what is wrong is clear,
