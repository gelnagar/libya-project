LIBYA

EMBASSY COMMUNIQUE CALLS FOR FALKLANDS SOLUTION 


PY121809 Buenos Aires NOTICIAS ARGENTINAS in Spanish 1720 GMT 12 Apr 82 


[Text] Buenos Aires, 12 Apr (NA) -- The Libyan Government officially stated today that 
the Malvinas Islands "are not British territory." In so doing, the Libyan Government 
has officially expressed support for the Argentine position regarding the Argentine-UK 
conflict. 


In a statement issued today at noon, the Libyan Government also called on all nonaligned 
countries to seek a negotiated solution to the crisis. The text of the communique 
delivered by the Libyan Embassy: 


l. The British presence on the Malvinas is a remainder of the colonialist policy that 
prevailed in the 19th century and as a result of an agreement in which spheres of 
influence were divided among the colonial powers. Consequently, since the jamahiriyah 
has expressed its opposition to any aggression perpetrated against an independent terri- 
tory, we, the jamahiriyah, do not consider the Malvinas Islands to be part of British 
territory. 


Y The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah expresses the need for respecting 
the feelings of the residents of the Malvinas Islands and their right to live in free- 
dom on the islands and to go abroad. 


oe The jamahiriyah expresses the need for resolving all international disputes through 
peaceful means. 


4. The jamahiriyah calls for a halt to the hostilities between Argentina and the UK 
ane on all nonaligned countries to adopt a common position that will lead to the solution 
of this conflict through negotiations. 


The communique is signed by Al-Barrani al-Yarushi, charge d'affaires of the Libyan 
Embassy in Argentina.
