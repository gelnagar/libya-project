Summary (AI): A 1988 CIA cable reports a Libyan walk-in, Abdul Magid (codename Giaka), born in Tripoli in 1960, who had worked for Jamahiriya Security and then as assistant manager of Libyan Arab Airlines in Malta. He defected to the US side in Malta, fearing arrest if recalled to Tripoli over a dispute at the Luqa airport ticket counter.
• Subject born 1 June 1960 in Tripoli, Libya; Libyan passport holder.
• Subject worked for Jamahiriya Security 1984-86 and claimed to be in the secret files section and a security collection group.
• Subject said he received training from KGB instructors in Tripoli.
• Subject has been assistant manager of the LAA office in Malta since March 1986.
• Subject was living at an apartment hotel near the airport in Malta at the time of the report.
• 1988-08-10: Abdul Magid (Giaka) walked into a US contact point in Malta seeking relocation in exchange for information on Libya.
• 1988-08-12: Libyan Transport Minister ordered Giaka to return to Tripoli to discuss the Luqa incident.
