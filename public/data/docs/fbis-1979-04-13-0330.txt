LIBYA

FREE UNIONIST OFFICERS CONGRESS ISSUES STATEMENT 


LD121146 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 12 Apr 79 LD 


[Text] Benghazi, 11 April--The first historic congress of the Free Unionist Officers 
Movement has condemned the capitulatory treaty signed by the defeated Egyptian presi- 
dent with the Zionist enemy "Israel," 


In a statement issued at the conclusion of their congress this evening, the Free 
Unionist Officers affirmed the contin -lon of their close contact with the unionist 
officers in the Egyptian armed forces to undermine for good the ruling Egyptian regime, 


The statement pointed out that this regime sold our fateful cause at the American 
black house in alliance with colonialism and Zionism and with disregard for the sac- 
rifices of the Arab nation for its freedom and dignity. 


The Free Unionist Officers Movement stressed that its alliance with the revolutionary 
forces of the free officers in the Egyptian armed forces is in conformity with its 
unionist identity. 


The statement said that the revolutionary forces in the Egyptian armed forces, with 
whom they are working to join together in the unionist march on Jerusalem, will 
strike at the agency, reaction and renegade which the ruling Egyptian regime now 
represents. It added that the unionist officers will not set their mind at rest 
and will not cease in their efforts until this agent regime is undermined for good 
and until the black pages entered in the history of our Arab nation are erased. 


In a statement they issued today at the end of their historic congress, the Libyan 

Free Unionist Officers called on all the Free Unionist Officers in th Arab Armies 

to topple the reactionary regimes which hinder the march of freedom and unity of 

the Arab nation, They called on all the Free Unionist Officers in the Arab armies 

to join in combat with the Free Unionist Officers in Libya to storm the road to achieve- 
ment of the Jamahjrityah unity of the Arab peoples.
