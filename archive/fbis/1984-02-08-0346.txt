LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi on Chad; Italian Restitution 
LDO71119 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
0215 GMT 7 Feb 84 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution held a news con- 
ference yesterday with Italian and French information media 
and a number of world news agencies and newspaper correspon- 
dents. During this meeting, the brother leader spoke about a 
number of current Arab and international issues. 


About his meeting with Claude Cheysson, the French minister 
of foreign relations, and its results, the brother leader made it 
clear that this meeting had positive results, and that they were in 
agreement on the goals. However, we have not discovered a 
suitable means to achieve it. The leader added that it became 
clear from the viewpoints of all the African and other sides that 
Habre is the obstacle on the path of peace and the negotiations, 
and on the path of stability in Chad and in Africa in general. He 
pointed out that all the parties agree that Habre is sacrificing 
peace and stability for the sake of power, and relentlessly seeks 
to sacrifice Libyan-French friendship. 


The brother leader of the revolution said that Habre could lead 
Libya and France into a conflict on his behalf, as he wants to 
sacrifice the Fench interests in Libya for his own personal 
interest. He stressed that the French people should be aware of 
this. He made it clear that the entire world is certain that Habre 
is a murderer, and the ugliest form of murder is that of his aide 
Idriss Miskine. The French people must not support such a 
murderer. 


The brother leader of the revolution stressed in this regard that 
from a principled point of view, we do not recognize the right of 
any foreign power from outside Africa to be in Africa, and that 
our struggle is continuing to liberate Africa inch by inch, not 
through military power — the way it could be cunningly inter- 
preted — but through revolution. He added that we do not 
recognize any commitments by France toward its former colo- 
nies, because this is neocolonialism and a form of imperialism 
which we must resist. He explained that Libya supports the 
legitimate unity government in Chad which represents the over- 
whelming majority. He stressed that Libya’s security is based 
also on Chad's security, and that the security and the borders of 
France are not connected with Chad. 


Concerning Libyan-Italian relations after the visit of Italian 
Foreign Minister Andreotti to the Jamahiriyah, especially in 
connection with the compensation demanded by the Jamahiriyah 
as a result of the Italian colonization of Libya, the leader said: | 
do not believe that relations between the two countries will be 
corrected unless the question of compensation for the colonial 
damage is settled, and that the future of Italian economic rela- 
tions with Libya will lose out unless this question is resolved. 


He added that Italy appreciates this, that there is mutual under- 
standing about this subject, and that some openings for solutions 
have been found. He added that the question of compensation for 
Italian colonial damage needs to be discussed between the two 
sides and that Italy acknowledges its responsibility. 


There is a precedent for this matter, which is the German 
reparations for what Hitler did. The brother leader stressed that 
the demand of the Jamahiriyah in this case is not a question of 
amounts but rather a moral issue which should be based in 
international law and which will prevent colonialism recurring in 
the future. He said that it should be clear to the Italian and world 
public that Libya seeks in its demands for compensation to rescue 
the colonized countries, however long it may take, in order that 
countries in the future refrain from colonizing each other. 


Regarding Italy’s joining the so-called Multinational Force in 
Lebanon, the brother leader stressed that we consider the Multi- 
national Forces to be colonialist forces and we will continue to 
resist them until they depart, because no one has the right to 
intervene between a government and its people. The leader said 
that the problem of Lebanon is an Arab one concerning the Arabs 
alone. He noted the insolence of the imperialist intervention. The 
leader added in this connection: We are working for Arab 
forces to replace the Multinational Forces in Lebanon. 


Regarding his view of an international UN force in Lebanon in 
place of these forces, the brother leader stressed that this force 
is acceptable if it is there to supervise the Zionist forces’ with- 
drawal from Lebanon. 


JANA: ‘Arafat Group Planning Murder of Envoy 
LD071932 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1910 GMT 7 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Feb (JANA) — Fresh information has become 
available to informed circles concerning the attempted assassina- 
tion of the secretary of the Libyan Arab People’s Bureau in Rome 
last January, since it was learned that Salah Khalaf — Abu lyad 
— was the person behind the execution of this treacherous 
attempt by a person called Zakariyyah Ba‘lushah. 


Informed circles have learned that ‘Arafat’s cliques are now 
preparing to carry out another operation against the secretary of 
the Libyan Arab People’s Bureau in Athens. According to accu- 
rate information, two Palestinian youths have gone to Cyprus 
from where they will proceed to the Greek capital under a false 
passport for this purpose.
