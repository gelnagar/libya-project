Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing on Libyan and Libyan-backed forces deployed in northern Chad, listing ground, air and air-defence units at places such as Fada, Faya Largeau, Ounianga Kebir, Gouro, Ogoui and Aozou. It has no connection to Tripoli or to sites inside Libya.
• Libyan forces in northern Chad included elements of five mechanized infantry battalions, one motorized infantry battalion, three tank companies, four small tank units, and three BM-21 MRL batteries (as of 16 September 1983).
• Air defence assets included two deployed SA-6 batteries, one SA-9 battery, and two ZSU-23/4 SPAAG.
• Libyan combat aircraft in northern Chad included six Mirage F-1 and 24 Fitter (SU-22) fighter/bombers, and 14 SF.260 light-strike aircraft.

AT OUNIANGA KEBIR -- 

NO SIGNIFICANT CHANGE HAS BEEN OBSERVED IN THE GROUND 
FORCES IN THE OUNIANGA KEBIR AREA. CHANGES THAT HAVE BEEN 
OBSERVED INCLUDE A DECREASE IN THE NUMBER OF HEAVY-LIFT 
TRANSPORTERS IN THE AREA. IN ADDITION, THE BTR-60 COMPANY 

THAT HAD BEEN OBSERVED IN THIS AREA COULD NOT BE 

IDENTIFIED FROM AVAILABLE IMAGERY. CHANGES IN THE AIR 
ORDER-OF-BATTLE INCLUDED THE DEPARTURE OF THE FOUR SF.260 

LIGHT-STRIKE AIRCRAFT THAT HAD BEEN DEPLOYED AT THIS 
AIRFIELD, A FLUCTUATION IN THE NUMBER OF HELICOPTERS
