Summary (AI): This is a 1985 FBIS translation (part 8 of 8) of a Qadhafi anniversary speech broadcast by Tripoli domestic radio. It covers Libya's foreign policy positions on Lebanon, the Polisario, Iran-Iraq war, Syria, and the United States, with no specific Tripoli sites or local repression described.
• Qadhafi urged Libya to send arms and fighters to southern Lebanon and said Libya had already provided arms to the Lebanese resistance.
• Qadhafi praised the Polisario as brave and honest and criticized the approximately 80,000 Moroccan soldiers facing Polisario fighters.
• The speech was broadcast by Tripoli Domestic Arabic radio on 28 March 1985 and transmitted by FBIS London on 29 March 1985.
• The speech criticized Saddam Hussein for the 1975 Treaty of Algiers and said Libya had broadcast to Iranians against the Shah.
