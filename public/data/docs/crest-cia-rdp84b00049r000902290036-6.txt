16 October 1981 

MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD: 

SUBJECT: Countering the Threat from Libya 

‘Sar 1. On 14 October 1981 the undersigned attended a meeting 
in the office of State Department Counselor Robert C. 
McFarlane, who was interested in exploring additional measures 

- which might be taken against Libya in the wake of the | 
Tripartite Summit in Aden and the assassination of President 
Sadat. A working paper which McFarlane presented to the 

“meeting is attached. (The participants at the meeting are 
listed in the working paper.) 

2. As can be seen from the attached paper, consideration 
is being given to imposing a U.S. embargo of Libyan oil. Also 
discussed at the meeting was the idea of pulling out U.S. oil. 
advisors and technicians from Libya. As a backdrop to the 
decision making process on these two issues, McFarlane would 
like to have from the Agency an analysis of Libyan 
capabilities, intentions, and vulnerabilities, specifically 
a) what are Qadhafi's economic vulnerabilities; b) what would 

be the effect of a U.S. embargo of Libyan oil; and c) what > 
would: be the effect of a pullout of U.S. oil personnel from 
Libya. , 

3. As can be seen from the attached paper, actions 
contemplated vis-a-vis Libya are broken down into four main 
categories: exploiting Libyan vulnerabilities (chiefly an 
embargo on Libyan oil); improving local defenses (i.e., 
enhanced security assistance to threatened states around 
Libya); U.S. military strategy against Libya; and, an 
information campaign to achieve public, congressional and 
foreign support. Action in these four areas is as follows: 

State/NEA/AF together with CIA (Vulnerabilities); 
State/PM and DoD/OSD (Local-Defense) ; 
DoD/JCS/OSG (Military Strategy); and, 
State/SP and State/NEA/AF (Information Support). 

Action Required: Will pass on to Director/NFAC and NIO/NESA 
McFarlane's above request for an analysis of Libyan 
capabilities, intentions and vulnerabilities.
