LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Speaks on 7th Anniversary of Jamahiriyah 
LD022241 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
1820 GMT 2 Mar 84 


[Speech by Colone] Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi on the occasion of 
the seventh anniversary of the creation of the people’s authority 
and the proclamation of the birth of the Jamahiriyah in Libya, 
at the People’s Hall in Tripoli — live] 


[Excerpts] Today we are celebrating the seventh anniversary of 
the creation of the people’s authority and the proclamation of the 
Jamahiriyah. [applause] So far, 7 years of revolutionary work 
have passed on the road of the historic choice to replace all forms 
of government by the people’s authority through the popular 
conference and popular committees. [applause] 


The establishment of a jamahiriyah in the world is a grave matter 
facing no small challenges, from the borders of Libya to the end 
of the world. This new system meets with surprise, indignation, 
and challenges because the whole world is governed by the theory 
of oppression and exploitation. All peoples are dominated by 
governments and armies that came to power through coups and 
by kings who falsely and unjustly inherited the earth and all that 
is on it. All peoples are now subjugated by the tools of govern- 
ment, oppression, and exploitation at the hands of the exploitive 
forces which often create tools of government to dominate the 
masses. 


The true and wide-scale strategic alliance and support we receive 
comes not from the ruling regimes in the world but from the base, 
from the wide masses and the peoples who struggle everywhere 
for emanicipation, deliverance, and freedom. Libya has become 
a model for the people of these countries. All the peoples 
struggling for emanicipation, deliverance, and freedom now are 
seeking to achieve the form of social and political life as is 
happening in Libya. 


This places on the Libyan men and women a grave responsibility 
before the world peoples. This is because in revolutionary and 
extremely rare conditions and exceptional circumstances, you 
have set up a jamahiriyah in the heart of the Arab homeland — 
a homeland dominated by kingdoms and princes and life 
presidents and the forces of exploitation. It is even dominated by 
tribalism and family control. Families continue to dominate 
entire Arab peoples. 


There are still people who claim to own the earth and everything 
on it. The Arab land is still privately owned by some families and 
by some kings. Even those who declared other nonroyal forms of 
government, have also allowed themselves to inherit the earth 
and all that is on it through life-presidencies and amirates. 


The Arab homeland is governed by kingdoms, princes, kings, life 
presidents and fascists and is dominated by the exploitive classes 
which exploit the toiling masses for their own ends. 


This Arab homeland is backward and divided and colonized. It 
is only independent in form, even those Arab countries whose

foreign forces entered their territories without permission as is 
the case with the evacuation from Sinai. It is only evacuation in 
form. Sinai is still in the hands of the Israelis who return to it 
anytime they wish. Furthermore, the Egyptians cannot militarily

However, in the midst of such an Arab homeland, a popular and 
a totally popular system was set up. A system of popular confer- 
ences and popular committees. Thanks be to God, everyone 
should realize that what was declared in 1977 was no political lie 
or act of deception. It is no historical lie. It is a fact. 


It has proven that the people can be fully liberated by the 
revolutionary force to exercise authority deservedly. [applause 
and chants] 


The establishment of the Jamahiriyah in the midst of such an 
Arab homeland that is defeated, vanquished, suppressed, and 
dominated by tools of government, an Arab homeland that is 
inherited by families, was not an easy task. 


But this was a historical lesson and true emancipation from which 
there can be no retraction because of the difficult conditions 
surrounding us. 


Thus we have no alternative but to march forward in developing 
the jamahiri society so that the jamahiri society will overcome 
the conditions surrounding it and so that the Jamahiriyah will 
become a beacon by which the peoples around it can be guided 
so that there will be a popular revolution. [applause and chants] 


We have no alternative then to march forward in the development 
of the jamahiri society and the affirmation of the authority of the 
people, so that peoples can realize that it is possible to establish 
an authority for the peoples through the people’s conferences and 
people’s committees. 


The struggle in every part of the world is not for the improvement 
of conditions of government nor for the replacement of the tool 
of government by another. When the Jamahiriyah example 
becomes evident to the masses and peoples — I mean the Libyan 
Jamahiriyah example — the struggle will turn to demanding the 
destruction of the tools of government and of the parties which 
dominate peoples and the classes which exploit the peoples, and 
to crushing the tools of government, both civilian and military, 
which oppress the masses. 


Then the objective of the masses’ struggle becomes clear: the 
establishment of the system of rule of the masses, and the 
establishment of Jamahiriyahs, and the settlement of accounts 
for the good of the popular masses. 


This demands that the picture of the Jamahiriyah become clear 
and the authority of the people asserted, so that the other peoples 
will follow this example set by the Libyan masses. [applause and 
chants] The democratic practices in Libya are not only reflected 
on the Libyans, but also on the peoples who are watching this 
new experiment.

The peoples which demonstrated for bread in the Arab world this 
year — do not the merchants, contractors, rulers, exploiters, 
employers, and brokers, do they not have stores full of food-stuff 
which they sell for high prices? [words indistinct] the sky does not 
rain flour and does not rain bread, and money does not reproduce 
itself. Nobody can put a dinar in his pocket and see it give birth 
to another dinar. It can never become two dinars except if he 
takes the other from a consumer. 


This circumstance makes us more committed to the immortal 
idea that authority, wealth and arms must be in the hands of the 
people. 


Weapons are now used against the masses, and the masses are 
being put down by them. The masses must (?obtain) them. It will 
be a shame if half the people retreat, or if a reactionary or 
obstructive person comes and tries to deprive half the Libyan 
people of weapons. The women must choose on this. According 
to the resolutions of the People’s Conference — and (7it is a pity) 
that they come together, the establishment of the people’s author- 
ity, which was established in the announcement of the peoples 
authority, the announcement which was writien by all Libyan 
men and women in the people's conferences, and which says that 
defending the homeland is the responsibility of every man and 
woman of the citizens — tomorrow, Saturday, 3 March, military 
training for women in schools and colleges will end, and will 
remain only in the male institutions. 


The solution does not rest in compulsory channeling [ar tawjih]. 
We live in a democratic society. Authority is in the hands of the 
people. It is impossible for anything to be issued unless it comes 
from the popular conferences. But if there is a fault in the popular 
conferences it must be corrected and make democracy sound. 
The revolutionary force discovers the reactionary one and the 
masses attack the reactionary forces. The staunchest enemies of 
democracy are the reactionaries. The staunchest enemies of the 
toiling popular masses are the reactionary forces. The agents of 
colonialism, are the reactionaries. Those who sold Palestine are 
the reactionaries. The gaurds of Israel now are the Arab reacti- 
onaries. Those who are in alliance with America against the Arab 
nation are the Arab reactionaries. Reaction has brought (?our 
homeland) to its present state. The reaction which Jamal ‘Abd 
an-Nasir did not liquidate is the one which killed the 23 July 
Revolution and it is the one which [words indistinct]. 


No one is trying to deceive himself and the popular masses and 
claim that the Libyan people is a revolutioanry one. Libyan 
society is a reactionary one, like the region in which it lives. There 
is, however, a revolutionary force inside this reactionary society. 
There are masses, broad masses, which have an interest in the 
revolution. The revolutionary force may succeed in awakening 
hese masses in order to enable them to make their way to achieve 
heir interests and not relinquish them; and the reactionary force 
till remaining in Libya may succeed in misleading and deceiving 
hese masses and make them adopt resolutions against their 
terests and in the interest of backwardness. The rectionary 
rce means a force which goes backward, a backward force 
hich hates progress, hates everything new.
