Summary (AI): A December 1983 NPIC imagery note reports limited fighter operations at Matan as Sarra airfield in remote southeast Libya, near the Chad border. It describes a Flogger fighter-bomber, Hind helicopters, a tent camp and a communications site at the airfield, which was still under construction.
• One Flogger F fighter-bomber with wing-mounted fuel tanks arrived and was parked on the partially completed crossover link near a Hind attack helicopter; first jet fighter sighting at the airfield.
• A probable air-to-ground communications site lies about 500 m NNE of the crossover, with four mast-mounted antennas, two generator vans and a command vehicle.
• Arresting gear was under construction at each end of the runway.
• Coordinates given: 21-41-25N 021-49-56E.
• 1983-12-20: Tent camp first observed at the airfield, not present when a C-130 was parked nearby.
