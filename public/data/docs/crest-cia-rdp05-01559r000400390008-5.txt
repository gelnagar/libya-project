Summary (AI): Qadhdhafi's 11 June 1981 speech at the 'Uqbah ibn Nafi air base (former Wheelus) marking the anniversary of the U.S. base evacuation from Libya. He denounced U.S. "terrorism," NATO mobilization against Libya, and the Israeli raid on Iraq's nuclear facility, and announced the base would be renamed.
• The speech was broadcast live by Tripoli Domestic Service on 11 June 1981.
• Qadhdhafi said the base would be renamed in honour of a child said to have been killed by Americans in 1958, and that the 11 June base would be named 'Uqbah ibn Nafi.
• He said the U.S. was mobilizing NATO against Libya and had sent letters to heads of state in Western Europe and Africa/Asia.
• He cited Italian, West German, French and British economic interests and workers in Libya, and said Libya would not lose.
• He condemned the Israeli raid that destroyed Iraq's nuclear establishment, which he said used U.S. aircraft.
• 1981-06-11: Qadhdhafi speech marking the anniversary of the U.S. bases' evacuation from Libya; he announced that the base would be renamed and that the 11 June base would be named 'Uqbah ibn Nafi.

{Text] Beirut, 11 Jun (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The Libyan Arab volunteers in Lebanon 
have sent the following message to the leader of the revolution on the occasion of the 
eleventh anniversary of the eviction of U.S. troops from Libyan soil: 

Brother Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
Leader of the Great al-Fatah Revolution. 

With blood we write the name of the revolution and deepen its meaning, and with blood 
alone we will map the spirit of the green thought and embody in practice the Lebanese 
arena the motto of the battle's pan-Arab character in which the Great al-Fatah 
Revolution believed and raised its banner aloft. 

Dear Leader, we the Libyan Arab volunteers in Lebanon affirm to you that we will fight 
side by side till the last shot in order to liberate the Arab land and to become martyrs. 
What a glorious martyrdom we aspire for while we are present in the front trenches of 

the Palestinian revolution. 

Dear Leader, just as al-Fatah's cry resound shattering the suspicious Arab silence and 

dealing a blow to the citadels of America's Arab agents, we the Libyan Arab volunteers, 
after the blood of our comrades merged with that of our Palestinian brothers, we pledge 
to take the sacred revenge for the blood of the freemen shed on the Lebanese soil 

in defence of their Arabism and the Arab nation. 

With the blood of successive martyrs who believed in the aims and principles you 
instilled in us, we will carry the rifle in one hand and the Green Book in the other. 

While the masses of Al-Fatah are celebrating one of our days of freedom, the day of the 
eviction of U.S. bases from our soil, we cannot but greet the souls of our martyrs who 
are today pointing to the horizons of freedom. Those martyrs who fell fighting for 
freedom when they resisted these bases which trampled upon our land. 

With rifles Al-Fatah will remain. 
With blood we will write its thought and with revolutionary practice we will stay with 
the leader of the green march. :
