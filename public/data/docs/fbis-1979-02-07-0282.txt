LIBYA

TRIPOLI MARCHERS VOICE SOLIDARITY WITH IRANIAN REVOLUTION 
LDO061445 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 6 Feb 79 LD 


{Text] This morning Tripoli witnessed huge popular marches backing the Great 1 September 
Revolution and its leader, brother Col Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi, and supporting the uprising 
of the Muslim Iranian people. The participants in the march, in which various groups of 
people took part, began in Al-Khadra Square and proceeded to the People's Palace, carrying 
national flags, pictures of the leader of the revolution and banners expressing the abso- 
lute support of the masses for the Great 1 September Revolution. The people also voiced 
their determination to apply what was in the Green Book in its two parts--the first and 
the second--and reaffirmed their support and backing for the Iranian people's revolution 
against the regime of the agent shah. The people shouted the following slogans: Al-Fatih 
Is a Popular Revolution! Al-Fatah Is a (?Socialist) Revolution! Partners, not Hired 
Hands! No, No to Capitalism! Khomeyni Forward, Forward, This Is the Era of the Masses! 
Egyptian People, Oh Volcano, [as heard] Heed the Lesson of Iran! 


In a statement at the end of the march, the masses stressed their full support for the 
uprising of the Iranian Muslim people and the steps of their leader and the symbol of their 
struggle, Ayatollah Khomeyni, toward realizing the masses! authority and the proclamation 
of the Islamic republic,
