LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI CALLS FOR SUPPORT FOR W. BANK 


LD271004 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0920 GMT 27 Mar 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 27 Mar (JANA) The brother leader of the revolution sent a cable to Arab 
presidents and kings. The following is the text of the cable: 


What is happening on the West Bank of the Jordan River in occupied Palestine against 
unarmed Arab citizens, and the acts of mistreatment and eviction which have reached the 
point of massacres, is another insult to be added to a series of degrading insults by 
Israel to the submissive Arab nation. 


It would be frustrating to the will of the Arab masses in the occupied territory to hear 
from us more expressions of denunciation and condemnation which have no meaning except 
to be part of an Arab comedy. 


It is a duty and a defense of honor before the world that we transform this daring uprising 
into a popular liberation war; that we break the barriers that are separating us from the 
Arab masses in the occupied territory and build bridges across all the Arab borders to the 
occupied territory so that we can join with them physically. 


If the Arab rulers are unable to carry out their duty, as is certainly the case, then 

we have to, at least, give our material capabilities to the Palestinian resistance to 
enable it to help the residents of the West Bank who are facing U.S. and Israeli terror- 
ism and their racist crusader savagery and barbarism. 


AL-QADHDHAFI ATTENDS NAVAL COMMISSIONING 


LD281944 Tripoli JANA in English 1914 GMT 28 Mar 82 


[Text] Gulf of Sidra, 28 Mar (JANA) -- The revolution's leader and supreme commander 

of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi today attended a ceremony 

by the Libyan Navy in the Gulf of Sidra in which new naval vessels joined the fleet as 
part of festivities marking the 12th anniversary of the evacuation of British bases from 
Libya. The ceremony was also attended by the Libyan Armed Forces Chief of Staff brigadier 
Mustapha Kharrubi, the navy chief of staff, and other officers. 


The naval forces saluted Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and chanted revolutionary slogans affirming 
the Libyan people's readiness to defend their revolution and its giant accomplishments. 


After the inspection of the new naval vessels by Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, a giant march was 
organised in support of the Great Al Fateh Revolution and its accomplishments and to 


affirm the historic character of the revolution. 


NICARAGUA'S ORTEGA SENDS MESSAGE, ASKS SUPPORT 


LD261944 Tripoli JANA in English 1749 GMT 26 Mar 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 26 Mar (JANA) -- Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of Al-Fatah 
Revolution, has received a message from Mr. Daniel Ortega, member of the Sandinista 
Command Council and coordinator of the National Salvation Council. He said that in 

case the U.N. Security Councils fails to take firm measures against the U.S. provocations 
then Nicaragua will strongly fight back any attack on its territories.

He appealed to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi to contact the non-aligned countries to mobilize 
solidarity with Nicaragua. 


FARMERS HOLD MEETING WITH POLISH DELEGATION 


LD281048 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0815 GMT 28 Mar 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 28 Mar (JANA) -- The general secretariat of the farmers trade union 
federation in the jamahiriyah held a meeting last night with a delegation from the 

farmers union of the Peoples Republic of Poland, at the premises of the farmers federation 
in Tripoli. 


The meeting was devoted to discussions on the possibilities of cooperation between the 
farmers trade union federation of the jamahiriyah and the Polish farmers union, and to 
working out programs for the future aimed at strengthening cooperation in the service 
of the common interests of the two friendly countries. 


The brother secretary general of the jamahiriyah farmers federation briefed the guest 
delegation in detail on the achievements in the domain of agriculture thanks to the 
great 1 September Revolution.
