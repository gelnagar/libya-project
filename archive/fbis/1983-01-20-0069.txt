LIBYA

JANA ATTACKS HABIB ROLE IN MIDDLE EAST 


LD191018 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 19 Jan 83 
[Unattributed commentary ] 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Jan (JANA) -- The stray waif Philip Habib adopts the posture of the 
utmost hostility and hatred for the Arabs. His movements and meetings reflect his 
behavior which rejects Arab rights and which blatantly favors the Zionist enemy. 


Despite his Arab origin he is vexed by the Arabs and has turned his face away from them; 
moreover he has been intent on offending them and insulting their attitudes. He prefers 
to be a Zionist at heart and an American hypocrite, flatterer, and plotter. 


His statements and advice to the Zionists have shown his true nature as the best friend 
of our enemies. He marches to the (?orders) of his Zionist friends and carries out 
their plans in the manner they desire and prefer. In full view of us he closes his eyes 
to all Arab facts and disregards the legitimacy of our causes and even treads on the 
corpses of our martyrs in Sabra, Shatila, Khaldah, and Al-Khalisah to achieve the 
Zionists’ aims. 


He does not serve peace and the causes of peace as the claim, but he is paving the way 
to war and aggression. A look at the dates of his visits to the region is enough to 
tell us how they were precisely timed with the Zionist enemy's aggressions, invasions, 
and massacres. 


What we must be convinced of, which also commits us to respond, is that this stray waif, 
adopted by Reagan and the terrorist Begin, is plotting against us and denying our rights. 


V. 20 Jan 83 NORTH AFRICA 


JALLUD, HABASH, HAWATIMAH ADDRESS RALLY 


LD141448 Tripoli JANA in English 0823 GMT 14 Jan 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Awal 30, Jan 14, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The student masses 
yesterday celebrated the 19th anniversary of the 13-14 Jan 1964 student uprising 
against the royalist police state, which was struck down with one blow by the Libyan 
Arab people in the Great lst Sept 1969 Revolution. 


The students gathered in Tripoli yesterday afternoon and staged a big march and rally 
in commemoration of this historic uprising and of its martyrs who were treacherously 
killed by the forces of repression. 


They assembled near the people's palace where they were greeted by Staff Major ‘Abd 
as-Salam Jallud and Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander-in-chief of the armed 
forces. Staff Major Jallud delivered a speech marking the anniversary and acclaiming 
the students’ struggle against the defunct regime and their brave confrontation with 
its forces of repression. 


He pointed out that the commemoration of the student uprising of January 13 and 14 of 
1964 today coincide with the meeting here of the leaders of the rejectionist 
Palestinian organizations. This, he said, will reinforce their stand and help them to 
continue carrying the banner of a liberated Palestine, from the River Jordan to the 
Mediterranean Sea. 


Staff Major Jallud said: "The deafening cries for liberation, armed struggle and 
rejection of capitulatory settlements spur the Palestinian fighter and the resistance 


leaders materially and morally. 


"The students’ mass cry in Libya roars for the armed struggle as they now livein a free 


jamahiri (mass) society which alone enables them to freely express themselves." 


He added: "The free student masses in this jamahiri society, the society of people's 
congresses and committees, are themselves capable of going to the field to make their 
rejection heard. The roaring cry of the student masses in the Jamahiriyah is only to 
be expected since man has become free here and possesses authority, wealth and arms." 


Staff Major Jallud noted that the student masses that were treacherously fired at in 
1966 by the fascist reactionary regime are today freely training in the use of the 
most advanced arms. 


He continued: "If you have been liberated by the student revolution led by the pupil 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi in the fifties in the city of the first spark, and by the student 
uprising of 1964, then you must be proud to be the only students in the Arab homeland 
and the only young men who are consolidating a free and socialist society and fighting 
for unity. 


"The road of struggle is long and arduous in front of you and involves liberating the 
Arab will from the gulf to the ocean. If you have been liberated your brothers are 
still in prisons in the reactionary states. You have brothers in Moroccan prisons 
simply because they reject capitulation and reaction." 


Staff Major Jallud commented also on how the students uprising of 1964 paved the way 
for the revolution and the setting up of people's authority.

Staff Major Jallud said: "The student youth of Jamahiriyah are training on arms and the 
most moder weapons, planes, tanks and missiles. Thus, they are consolidating the 
society of freedom, where authority, wealth and weapons are possessed by the people. 
They are bringing nearer the day of liberating Palestine from the River Jordan to the 
Mediterranean Sea, and the day of unity. 


"These students are not raising empty slogans but are practising, by their sweat and 
blood, daily struggles for consolidating the people's mastery of authority, the auth- 
ority of People's Congresses and Committees. They are daily practising revolution and 
mass work, which is daily growing." 


He stressed: "Marking this uprising should become an occasion for students to be loyal 
to revolutionary nationalist programme, exert efforts to train on arms, increase produc- 
tion and build a moral, economic and military force." 


He urged them to work harder for greater production and realization of the revolution- 
ary nationalist programme to build a force affirming faith in all the revolutionary 
dicta, slogans and programmes. 


He added: "This magnificent rally is being held today in this square, which was a mono- 
poly for the king and his clique while today it has become a popular square for revo- 
lutionary action and demonstrations."' He called on the students to conselidate people's 
power and the masses' era as a pledge of loyalty to the martyrs who fell in 1964 by 

the bullets of reaction, fascism and dictatorship. 


He added, "Currently we are noting that Arab political will is being defeated. It is 
even more grave that it is [word indistinct] to defeat Arab human will. However, the 
most potent response is that this generation of revolution, renewal, unity and masses’ 
era, is the generation most determined to hold on to the rifle." 


He went on: "How wonderful it is to see brigades and divisions of students carrying 
logistical manoeuvres using tanks and missiles. How wonderful it is to see our students 
in Tripoli and Benghazi unloading goods to increase production. We in the revolutionary 
leadership find in the students’ stands the best backing for us to continue the struggle. 
Our students have always been loyal to revolutionary ethics." 


He added: "The generation of rejection should be incited to destroy all false slogans 
and efforts to deceive the masses." 


He concluded by saying: "Your thundering voice today is heard by the Arab students and 
masses from the Atlantic to the Gulf. They will fight against the Arab nation's enemies. 
Let us give our pledge here today to remain loyal to the revolutionary nationalist pro- 
gramme, which will stop the Zionist death wagon being propelled by American energy." 


Dr George Habash, the secretary general of the Popular Front for the Liberation of 
Palestine (P.F.L.P.), addressed the student march saying that the Zionist-imperialist- 
reactionary attack has been viciously escalated against the Arab nation and all the 
national, progressive and revolutionary elements. 


"Most of the Arab rulers have surrendered and therefore the revolutionary forces of the 
Arab nation should stand up to the imperialist schemes and confront the enemies with 
serious fighting and comprehensive mass mobilization and not just words." 


He added that the history of the Arab nation is full of challenges and that the 
Jamahiriyah is the vanguard of the Arab struggle against Zionism, imperialism and Arab 
reaction.

Mr Nayif Hawatimah, the secretary general of the Democratic Front for the Liberation of 
Palestine (D.F.L.P.), too, greeted the Jamahiriyah students on this anniversary saying 
that the 1964 student uprising inspired the Libyan Arab people for the revolution and 
today they are carrying the arms defending the Arab nation's causes especially the 
Palestinian cause. He added that the Libyan people who are carrying arms in one hand 
and working with the other, will not be defeated and that all hostile attempts will be 
foiled as a result of their determination and strong will to build socialism and achieve 
unity. 


Mr Hawatimah has also praised the struggle of the Palestinian and Lebanese peoples and 
their heroic resistance against the invading Zionist force. He said that the 
Palestinian and Lebanese fighters waged the most honourable and heroic offensive against 
the Zionist forces in Lebanon while most of the Arab regimes stood idle and impotent 
towards the invasion. 


He added: "We have great trust in the Jamahiriyah students and the Great al-Fatih 
Revolution, who are carrying arms, to always stand on the side of the Palestinian 
people."
