IRANIAN DELEGATION STOPS OVER IN TRIPOLI ON WAY TO TUNIS 
LD210045 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 20 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] An Iranian delegation representing the Revolution Council and the Iranian Islamic 
government passed through Tripoli international airport en route to Tunis. The Iranian 
delegation, speaking to a JANA correspondent, praised the courageous and honorable attitudes 
of the Great 1 September Revolution toward the issues of liberation in the world. He 

said that the backing and support of the Jamahiriyah for the Iranian people in their 
struggle against U.S. imperialism is definite proof of how important are these attitudes, 
which spring from the desire to establish firm reiations with all Islamic nations and 
peace-loving peoples and to stand united against the colonialist conspiracies which are 
aimed at the Arab and Islamic nation and the Third World, 


The delegation said that they will visit Arab countries to explain the political stands 
of the Iranian revolution and to meet with Arab brothers to exchange viewpoints on all 
issues of common interest between the Iranian revolution and the Arab countries. The 
delegation renewed its country's demand to the U.S. Government to return the deposed 
shah so that he may stand trial for the crimes he committed against the Iranian people, 
The delegation denounced the invitation of the defeated Egyptian president to his partner 
in treason and crime to be his guest in the oppressed Egyptian capital. They said that 
this suspect gesture by the defeated president revealed the extent of his treason and 
conspiracy against the Muslim Iranian people and the Arab nation and its fateful issues.

[Text] Tripoli, November 20, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Last night Foreign Secretary Dr. 
at-Turayki met Mr. 'Abd al-Fattah Isma'il, the secretary-general of the Yemeni Socialist 
Party and president of the Higher People’s Council in the Yemen Democratic Republic. 

The two officials reviewed the agenda of the 10th summit conference and the means of 
strengthening the Arab position inthe face of the conspiracies of capitulatZon and of 
reinforcing the struggle of the Palestinian people for the liberation of its usurped land. 


They exchanged views about the coordination of their respective countries! stance 
during the sessions of the Arab summit conference which is opening in Tunis today.
