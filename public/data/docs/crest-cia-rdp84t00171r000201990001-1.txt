Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing note reports large-scale Libyan ground forces training exercises involving 22 maneuver battalions in three areas of the Western Military District, the largest at Jadu Mizdah training area. Most units were thought to come from garrisons around Tripoli, Az Zawiyah, Tarhunah and Gharyan; the document gives no Tripoli-city site.
• 22 major maneuver battalions observed in three training areas of the Western Military District.
• Primary Jadu Mizdah units included six probable tank battalions, two probable mechanized infantry battalions, five self-propelled artillery battalions, two MRL battalions, one FROG battalion and one towed artillery battalion.
• Analysts suggest at least two armor brigades and one artillery brigade may have participated, the first observed Libyan brigade-level training.
• Collateral reference: DIA Ground Order of Battle: North Africa States, November 1982.
• 1983-09: Large-scale ground forces training exercises observed in three areas of Libya's Western Military District, involving 22 major maneuver battalions; largest in Jadu Mizdah training area.
