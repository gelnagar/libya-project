ARTILLERY PURCHASE--Iraq has ordered $660m worth of 155mm artillery guns from 
France. The contract provides for the supply of 85 guns built by the Societe 
Francaise de Materiels d'Armement (Sofma), The guns, which have a range of 24 km 
and can fire eight shots a minute, will be mounted on AMX30 tanks. Iraq is the 
second foreign client after Saudi Arabia to buy the guns. Last year Saudi 

Arabia lent Iraq six 155mm artillery guns, reportedly for use in the Gulf war. 
Although Iraq receives the bulk of its military equipment from the Soviet Union-- 
including MiG 21 fighters, Llyushin bombers, and SAM missiles--it has recently 
turned to France for more planes and helicopters, Last year Iraq bought $1.6bn 
worth of Roland missile systems to bolster its air defences. The Franco-German 
Roland weapuns-system is designed to protect airfields, ammunition depots and 
other key military installations against low-flying supersonic aircraft, [Text]
