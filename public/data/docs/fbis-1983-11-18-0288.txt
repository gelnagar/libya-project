LIBYA

JANA Reacts to Ban on Flights in Chadian Airspace 
LDI71146 Tripoli JANA in English 0930 GMT 17 Nov 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Safar 12, Nov 17, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The rebel clique in Ndjamena gave itself the right 
to take a decision on behalf of the Chadian people and its 
legitimate government led by Goukouni Oueddei banning the 
flights of Libyan aircraft inside Chadian airspace. According to 
AFP, a statement was issued to this effect and was broadcast by 
Habre’s radio yesterday. 


It is obvious that it is easier for some to take decisions yet it is 
harder to take the responsibility and repercussions of this decision 
especially if those people are a bunch of thieves who based their 
decision on a dream which tickled their fancy. The Ndjamena 
thieves involved themselves too much in their dream which is 
based on mirage and imagination. It seems that their decision to 
ban the flights of Libyan planes in Chadian airspace is an 
expression of hostile intention accumulated by their biased devo- 
tion to the enemies of the Chadian people. The decision would 
have been considered seriously and a certain stand would have 
been taken, should it come from a legitimate sovereign state.
