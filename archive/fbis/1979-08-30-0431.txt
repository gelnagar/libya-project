LIBYA

PDRY LEADER ARRIVES TO ATTEND CELEBRATIONS 
LD291848 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 29 Aug 79 LD 


[Excerpt] ‘Abd al-Fattah Isma'il, chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme People's 
Council of the PDRY, this afternoon arrived in Tripoli to attend the celebrations 

by the Libyan Arab People of the Great First of September Revolution. He was given 
an official and popular welcome at Tripoli International Airport where Col Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi led those who came to welcome him. Others who were there: Maj 'Abd 
as-Salam Jallud, Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir and Maj al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi. 


JANA Statement 
LD292250 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 29 Aug 79 LD 


[Excerpts] Ina statement to JANA, 'Abd al+Fattah Isma'il emphasized the appreciation 
and respect of the Arab people of Yemen for the outstanding and effective role played 
by the Great First of September Revolution in the liberation struggle against world 
imperialism, Zionism and the enemies of man, freedom and progress. 


He stressed that his visit would afford an opportunity to discuss the question of 

the development of relations of cooperation and the common struggle between the two 
fraternal countries, especially in these circumstances in which our Arab nation was 

faced with a series of imperialist plots threatening its sovereignty and dignity 

following the signing by the defeatist Egyptian president of the treaty of treachery, 
shame and capitulation with the enemy with the blessing of the leader of world imperialism, 
the United States. 


He added: These circumstances make it urgently necessary for us to strengthen Arab 
solidarity and the unity of the struggle of the Arab progressive revolutionary forces 
to frustrate the aims of the treacherous Camp David agreement, to liberate our Arab 
land and to enable the fighting Arab people of Palestine to return to their homeland, 
to determine their future and to establish their own national state in all their land 
under the leadership of the PLO, the sole lawful representative of the Palestinian 
people,

BILLY CARTER, ROMANIA!S PATAN ARRIVE FOR CELEBRATIONS 
ID291410 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 29 Aug 79 ID 


[Excerpts] The delegations of sister and friendly countries, as well as progressive organi- 
zations and parties, have started arriving in the Jamahiriyah to attend the celebrations 
marking the 10th anniversary of the First of September Revolution. 


This morning, Ion Patan, Romanian deputy prime minister, arrived in Tripoli leading a 
delegation. 


Last night, Billy Carter, leading a U.S. popular delegation, arrived in Benghazi to attend 
the celebrations of the Libyan Arab people. In a statement to the JANA correspondent, Billy 
said: My first visit to the Jamahiriyah, which took place in October last year, enabled me 
to contact the Libyan Arab people. The achievements I saw during that visit, and which 

have been brought about in the years of the revolution, have proved that the people of the 
Jamahiriyah are capable of realizing all they aspire toward.
