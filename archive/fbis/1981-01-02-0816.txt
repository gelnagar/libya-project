("Commentary by JANA Political Editor'--JANA headline] 


(Text] Tripoli, 31 Dec (JANA)--Leopold-Sedar Senghor has fallen. And with his fall he 
has chosen the word "resigned" to cover his downfall. As for 20 years Senghor ruthlessly 
ruled his people who (?had) mobilized themselves in a [word indistinct] opposition move- 
ment and he therefore, found no way out but to step down.

Because the revolution against him has materialized and become a fact. Today has proved 
that time is with the people who are determined to get their freedom and possess their 
authority and that hirelings do not live for long. 


Senghor has spent his life in an attempt to dope the Senegalese people, helped colonialism 
to dig deep in Senegal and fought all liberation. He was an obedient tool in the hands of

maintain oppression of the Chadians who were bent on conirontation. 


He was brought down following a similar fate to that of his ally, the insurgent Habre, 
under the blows of the Chadian people. And so anothe: colonialist pawn disappears from the 
African Continent and with him is discredited the theory of "local agents" which French 
colonialists have worked to implement for over two centuries. Accordingly the people are 
{word indistinct] the beat of their ongoing march growing louder and louder while the 
puppets search for new means to cover up their downfall. 


MOROCCO REPORTEDLY BUILDING SAHARA FORTIFICATIONS 
LDO11646 Tripoli JANA in English 1453 GMT 1 Jan 81 


[Text] Rabat, 1 Jan (JANA)--Moroccan forces are strengthening the defences of their 
positions in uthern Morocco and the disputed Western Sahara in the fa of repeated 


attacks fror ylisario guerrillas. 


Reports here say t..at the Moroccans are building a fortified line between Tan-Tan and Smara. 
Moroccan troops are building a sandwall, which is topped with barbed wire and sown witii 
land mires. Radar stations are also being installed. 


The commander of the sixth Moroccan battalion, General Fajrani, has admitted that 
guerrilla attacks on Moroccan positions are meeting with success. 


Polisario (Popular Front for the Liberation of the Saguia El Hamra and the Rio De Oro) 
is fighting for an independent state in the former Spanish colonies, which were split up 
and given to Morocco and Mauritania when Spain quit the area in 1975.
