Summary (AI): A January 1988 FBIS translation of a Qadhdhafi speech to students at Al-'Aziziyah, urging a "generation of anger" in support of the Palestinian uprising and attacking sports, tourism, and religious tomb visits. It also includes a short Paris radio item on Libya's stance on a postponed OAU committee meeting about Chad. Nothing specific to Tripoli sites or repression.
• Qadhdhafi said the 'generation of anger' in occupied Palestine had begun, and claimed Israeli radio linked Palestinian uprising elements to Libya.
• Qadhdhafi attacked sports, tourism, and visits to holy men's tombs as distractions, and criticized Libyans seeking treatment in Switzerland and the US.
• Qadhdhafi said a government had built a dome over the tomb of an Italian, and that the Libyan government had closed mausoleums and arrested charlatans.
• The Paris item reported that the OAU ad hoc committee meeting on Chad was postponed from February to April 1988, and Libya said it was not a member of the committee.
• 1988-01-27: Qadhdhafi speech to students, broadcast by Tripoli Television on 27 January 1988 (recording date not given)

[Speech by leader Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi to

[Text] Here is the anger in occupied Palestine. The 
members of this generation, they themselves raise slo-

anger. Even Israeli radio said there are elements among 
the uprising of the Palestinian people which follow 
Libya. What does the phrase Libyan elements mean? It 
means Palestinian elements which follow Libya. See the 
interpretation of enemies who distort everything. Ele-

our enemies and our land is occupied. They heard them 
say—we are the generation of anger—and they know that 
this cry came from Libya. This generation of anger has 
bypassed Yasir ‘Arafat and all these traditional leaders 
because it seems that they are not angry. There is 
someone who has a rifle but he does not fight with it. 
However, this one, out of anger, fought with stones. The 
generation of anger has begun to prove its existence; the 
vanguards of the generation of anger have appeared in 
the arena. The revolution of stones and bullets should 
not be the only one in the arena. Anger should include all 
the fields. [crowd chants] 

Anger should prevail in the field and be led by this new 
generation because of your effectiveness, the effective- 
ness of each one of you, each class, each [word indis-

Palestine, depends on the salvation of the future of this

Deceit, brainwashing, duping, soccer, cards, alcohol, 
tourism? Why are they spreading them? In order to 
distract people’s minds from thinking about their future. 
Hashish, by the order of the king, should be distributed 
on a large scale in order to numb all the youth. These 
governments show concern for one issue, in addition to 
their servitude and loyalty to outsiders: soccer and all 
these games, which distract all people, distract the youth: 

Do not listen to this tasteless and unreasonable encour- 
agement of this ball game, this group, and that of the 
president himself, the king himself attended the game,
