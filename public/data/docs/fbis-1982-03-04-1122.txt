LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI: TO HELL WITH U.S., SAUDI ARABIA 


LDO21808 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1455 GMT 2 Mar 82 


[Speech by Col Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi at Tripoli's international conference center 
during 2 March rally celebrating fifth anniversary of the establishment of the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah -- live or recorded] 


[Text] Free brothers, there is no greater day for the masses than the day they achieve 
emancipation and liberation and assume reponsibility for their own destinies. There 

is no greater anniversary for the Libyan masses than that which we are celebrating today 
the anniversary of the proclamation of the jamahiriyah and the establishment of the 
people's authority. [chants] 


This is the anniversary of the day, brothers, on which the Libyan people became masters 
and free men in their own land. It is your right, brothers, to raise high your voices 
and your heads because you are the only people who are masters in their own land. You 
are the only free people. [chants] 


Brothers, your voices here tell the shackled millions in the greater Arab homeland that 
it is possible for people to exist without a government and to run their own affairs 
by themselves in a jamahiri manner, without any proxy or mediation. 


Your voices today, brothers, echo and resound throughout the greater Arab homeland from 
the ocean to the Gulf, declaring the establishment of the jamahiriyah and the era of 
the peoples, declaring the triumph of the popular will over a part of the Arab homeland 
indeed, in the heart of the Arab homeland, which Libya represents. Libya is the heart 
of the Arab homeland. The Libyan people are the pulsating heart of the Arab homeland. 


Brothers, the establishment of the jamahiriyah and of the people's authority constitutes 
one of the most important changes in modern times. For the first time, the masses have 
liberated themselves from those who shackled them. The masses have liberated them- 
selves from all shackles. 


As we celebrate the fifth anniversary of the establishment of the people's authority and 
the founding of the jamahiriyah today [words indistinct]. 


This free land used to suffer under the weight of foreign bases built on it. Until 
recently, the people were forced to bow their heads before reactionary dictatorship, 
its whips and swords which hung over the necks of the masses. 


Brothers, a government's actions against the masses which march to seize authority are 
acts of injustice and are wrong and illegitimate. The laws promulgated by Arab governments 
against the Arab peoples to subdue the masses to their authority are unjust laws. They 
constitute injustice in the full sense of the word. The prisons in which the masses 

are crammed on the orders of governments must be destroyed by the masses, just as the 
masses destroyed them on the great 1 September. 


Brothers, the laws with which the Arab governments arm themselves against the masses 
with a view to subduing them [words indistinct]. 

Brothers throughout the Arab homeland: These days a flagrant challenge to the popular 
masses exists. Brothers, under the umbrella of the U.S. and Israeli march against the 
Arab nation, the Arab reaction has been behaving like a lion toward the masses and has 
been confronting them everywhere in an insolent and clear challenge. These reactionary 
dictatorial regimes are the agents of imperialism and Zionism in the Arab homeland.

Brothers, the progressive revolutionary forces in the Arab homeland are facing challenges 
and provocations from the reactionary forces, which have begun to feel like a lion and to 
raise their voices under the umbrella of the United States and the Israeli forces" march 
against the Arab nation. 


But, brothers, we declare from here the rise of the voice of challenge to the Arab 
reactionary regimes, the agents of imperialism. We challenge the Arab reactionary regimes 
and regionalism and those who persecute our Arab masses, the Palestinian masses, the 
Lebanese masses and the Arab masses everywhere. [chants] 


Brothers, today we declare -- on this historic day in the life of the Libyan people -- that 
we challenge the reactionary and (?regionalist) regimes shackling our Arab masses and 
preventing them from achieving liberation, deliverance and from taking part in the battle 
of dignity and fate against the Zionist enemy and against America, the biggest enemy of the 
Arab nation and the peoples struggling for freedom. 


We declare our defiance, brothers! We declare our strong defiance against the reactionary, 
police, regionalist, corrupt regimes. And from here, brothers, we declare in a loud, fearless 
voice, in a loud voice that fears no one, we declare our complete backing with all our 
capabilities for the Arab revolutionary forces now being savagedly treated everywhere. 
Brothers, we declare our complete backing with all our potential for the revolutionary Arab 
forces facing savage treatment from the ocean to the Gulf. 


Brothers, reaction, which collaborates with Zionism and imperialism and which is an agent 

of imperialism, wants to liquidate the revolutionary forces, the progressive forces, the 
unionist forces, so that the Zionist and American forces can attain complete control over 
the Arab homeland. Brothers, we are now fighting in self-defense, in defense of Arab 
existence, of Arab dignity, of the Arab land on which the American armies are advancing 
everywhere without resistance; from our east and from our west the American forces are 
advancing on the chaste Arab land without any resistance, brothers. But we will declare the 
resistance and declare the mobilization of the masses of the Arab nation; and we are with 
them; we are behind them. We will fight along side all free men. You Arab revolutionaries, 
from the ocean to the Gulf, we will not abandon you, you revolutionaries now suffering in 
jails and being chased in the streets. They are trying to liquidate the hopes you embody, 
the hopes of this nation to secure unity, to liberate itself, to build socialism and progress 
and to hold aloft the banner of honor and dignity, oh brothers! 


Brothers, these days, Arab reaction expresses an historical concept hostile to the masses 
and forever and fatefully allied to colonialism and Zionism. Today, Saudi Arabia, on U.S. 


orders, declares on you a war of starvation and an economic war against the Libyan people, 


the Algerian people, the Kuwaiti people and [words indistinct]. Today, Arab reaction 
expresses its true identity and is totally biased in a flagrant challenge to the Arab nation. 
It is biased in favor of America and the Israeli brothers. Saudi Arabia, implementing U.S. 
orders, has decided to deprive the Libyan people of their sole source of income: oil. Saudi 
Arabia, dear brothers, unnecessarily, needlessly and without any national economic justifica- 
tion is flooding the world market with cheap oil and is selling its oil at cheap prices. 
Saudi Arabia, brothers, today is declaring its open alliance with Israel and the United 
States against the Libyan people, against the Algerian people, against the Kuwaiti people, 
against the Nigerian people and against the peoples who rely on oil. Saudi Arabia decides, 
in implementing U.S. desires, to flood the markets with cheap oil to sell its oil at rock- 
bottom prices so as to deprive the Libyan people, the Algerian people and the Kuwaiti 

people from selling their oil.
