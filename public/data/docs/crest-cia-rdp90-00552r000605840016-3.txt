Summary (AI): A 1982 London Daily Telegraph article reports that a third man, Douglas Schlachter, cooperated with the FBI in the investigation of ex-CIA agents Edwin Wilson and Frank Terpil over their activities for Libya. Schlachter pleaded guilty to charges tied to his work in Libya for Wilson, and his lawyer said he had briefed two former CIA officials about Wilson's Libya dealings.
• Schlachter, 39, surrendered to federal authorities in December 1981 and had been indicted in August 1981 for illegally shipping explosives from the US to Libya.
• Schlachter's lawyer said he briefed two high-ranking CIA officials in Washington in 1976 and 1977 about Wilson's activities for Gaddafi.
• Two further charges against Schlachter were to be dropped in return for his cooperation.
• Clines retired from the CIA in 1978; Shackley left in 1979.
