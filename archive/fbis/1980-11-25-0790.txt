LIBYA

SECRETARIAT ISSUES STATEMENT ON U.S. ‘INTERVENTION’ 
LD232326 Tripoli JANA in English 1647 GMT 23 Nov 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 23 Nov (JANA)--The permanent secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 
yesterday issued the following statement: American intervention in the Arab region is 
growing bolder and more dangerous. This intervention is part of the colonialist- 
Zionist-reactionary scheme aimed at imposing the Stable David accords over the entire 
region. This intervention has taken the form of a direct U.S. military presence 
especially in the wake of the Iran-Iraq war. 


The military colonialist presence is part and parcel of the capitulatory policy of Arab 
reaction. 


On the other hand, Arab reaction is covering up this military presence and giving it 
legitimacy through official calls for a U.S. presence. 


This explains Arab reaction's political and propagandistic silence on this aggressive 
American presence. Joint manoeuvres are in fact at this moment taking place on the 
Jamahiriyah's eastern borders, with the U.S. rapid deployment forces and As-Sadat's 
forces participating. 


It is worth noting that these same Rapid Deployment Forces carried out manoeuvres two 
years ago in the Nevada desert. Amongst the orders given then to these forces was the 
destruction of the 'Libyan enemy’. This proves that the current manoeuvres are intended 
to deliver a military blow against the Libyan Arab people in a bid to impose the Stable 
David conspiracy.

The colonialist-Zionist-reactionary scheme, enshrined by the Stable David accords, aims 
at casting doubts over the principled revolutionary stands of the Jamahiriyah. To this 
end, Arab and reactionary puppets began moving according to plan so as to facilitate 

a military blow against the Jamahiriyah from the east where current American-Egyptian 
manoeuvres are taking place. 


It is therefore inperative that Arab public opinion should be warned of the danger of 
American colonialist intervention and the necessity of Arab progressive solidarity with 
the Jamahiriyah in the battle for honour and dignity. 


The implementation of the absolutely capitulatory Stable David accords are aimed at 
drawing the Arab regimes one after the other behind As-Sadat under the banner of U.S. 
imperialism and international Zionism. 


Thanks to the Palestinian peoples and the Arab nation's struggle, the policy was foiled. 
Consequently, hostilities increased and several As~-Sadat-like moves appeared in suspicious 
statements by Arab reactionaries about the possibility of reconciliation with (Israel). 


Then, the Iraqi-lranian war broke out to initiate the second part of the Stable David 
designs by: crushing the Arab nation's alliances, upsetting the priorities in its 

struggle strategy, imposing divisions on the vital issue of discrimination between friends 
and foes, imposing an isolation on the Arab nation and depriving it of the support of 

the Muslim peoples, depriving it from the joint strength of the Iraqi and Iranian peoples, 
camouflaging the imperialist policy in the region, demoting the Palestinian cause from a 
place of prominent and urgent concern in the Middle East conflict, escalating (Israel's) 
land, air and naval aggression, (Israeli) annexation of more land and coordination with 
all the Arab nation's enemies to crush the Palestinian resistance movement and the Lebanese 
national movement. 


Not surprisingly, under such conditions the voices of reaction integrated in an open 

attempt to deal a blow to the Libyan Arab people and the revolutionary leadership to prevent 
the Jamahiriyah from proceeding with its national policy by making Libya the land for 

all freedom loving Arabs. 

Hence, the increased U.S. imperialist intervention in the Arab nation and its occupation 

of several bases in Egypt, Somalia and Oman, and the hostile manoeuvres by the rapid 
deployment forces on the eastern border of the Jamahiriyah in collusion with Egyptian 

forces rules out silence and neutrality. The outcome is either acceptance or rejection 

of this flagrant U.S. imperialist aggression. 

The permanent secretariat of the Arab Peoples' Congress calls on the masses of the Arab 
nation to shoulder their historic and freedom-fighting attack aimed at crushing the 
Palestinian revolution, the Lebanese national movement, all the progressive popular forces 
and the countries of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front. 


The freedom, dignity, and future of the Arab nation, as well as its right to live and thrive, 
are at stake unless this attack is confronted. Victory is in our hands once oppression, 


hesitation and irresponsibility are abandoned. 


Our country is capable of facing defiance no matter how great, no matter at what price. 


The permanent secretariat of the Arab People's Congress

Khaddam was received at the Tripoli International Airport by Dr ‘Ali ‘Abd as-Salam 
at-Turayki, Libyan foreign secretary; the charge d'affaires of the Syrian relations office 
in Tripoli and members of the office; and the Algerian ambassador in Tripoli, 


Upon arrival, Khaddam made a statement to journalists. He said: You understand that the 
Arab nation is suffering an abnormal situation as a result of the state of disputes exist- 
ing in the Arab arena. These disputes are the normal outcome of the Camp David policy, 
which has played a major role in creating division in the Arab situation. He added: Our 
opinion has been that we must confront the dangers facing the Arab nation, particularly 
the Zionist and imperialist dangers, to eliminate these abnormal conditions and to rectify 
the Arab situation's course. Khaddam explained: For that, we have called for the post- 
ponement of the Arab summit conference for a limited time, during which the Arab situation 
can be dealt with. But some Arab countries, for reasons of their own, rejected the idea 
of the postponement. Consequently, there has been a determination to ignore this Arab 
Situation. He added: The stands of the Steadfastness Front have been uniform and identi- 
cal, whether in their view of what is taking place or in their assessment of the current 
situation. 


Khaddam concluded his statement Saying: My visit to Libya comes within the framework of 
existing relations between Libya and Syria. President Hafiz al-Asad has asked me to con- 
vey a message to his brother, the leader of the 1 September revolution, Col Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, dealing with the Arab Sstituation, the stand on the Arab summit conference 
and views on the situation.
