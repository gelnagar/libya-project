LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SPEAKS ON CHAD, YEMEN, CARTER MISSION 


LDO90110 Tripoli JANA in English 2005 avr 8 Mar 79 LD 


(Text ] Bangazi, Mar 8, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of 
the Great lst of September Revolution, greeted the 8th of March revolution in Syria, which 
was waged against secession in Syria and was an attempt to restore unity between Egypt 

and Syria.

9 Mar 79 a3 NORTH AFRICA 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi indicated that the Ba'th Party had a great role in this 
revolution and also had an effect in enhancing national unionist consciousness in the 
Arab world, At the massive revolutionary rally in Banghazi this evening Colonel 

Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi called upon the Ba'th Party youth in the Arab world to transform 
into revolutionary committees and to lead the ranks of the people's masses to achieve 
people's authority, to form together with the revolutionary committees in the Jamahiriyah 
a sole revolutionary, political movement in the Arab world which is the movement of 

the revolutionary committees, the tool of people's revolution, 


The leader of the Great lst September Revolution referred to the stage when the Arab 
unionist and national forces organized themselves into parties as this was their only 
means of assembling themselves, He pointed out that now that the era of the jamahiriyahs 
has emerged and that the jamahiriyahs have started to form a new type of international 
units, it became evident that the party system is no more the appropriate means to the 
era of the masses and the establishment of the people's authority, for the party is a 
ruling tool while the era of the masses does not recognize any such tool, but only 
recognizes the people's authority. 


The time is now ripe for the Arab unionist national forces in all parts of the Arab 
homeland to transform themselves into revolutionary committees and struggle in order to 
set up the people's authority and abolish all the ruling tools which rule on behalf of 
the Arab people, 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great lst of September Revolution, affirmed 
in his speech delivered today at the revolutionary rally organised in Banghazi, the 
need for the transformation of the political parties in the Arab world into revolutionary 


committees, He said: "The true revolutionary unionists have no choice but to transform 
into revolutionary committees and to struggle for the establishment of the people's 
authority." 


He added: Starting from now, the revolutionary committees in the Jamahiriyah will call 
for the dissolution of all political parties in the Arab arena and the abolition of all 
the authoritarian forms which contradict the people's authority, because the people's 
authority has only one method and one means through which it can be achieved, that is 
the people's congresses and people's committees, All other forms are dictatorial even 
if loyal to the masses because they rule on behalf of the people. 


The leader of the Great Ist of September Revolution, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, said 
that from now on the revolutionary committees will devote themselves to the real 
revolutionary tasks including the call for achieving Arab unity through people's 
revolution, through people's committees, Any attempt to achieve Arab unity other than 
through the people's authority is doomed to failure, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 

said. He pointed out that the aspired Arab unity is that of the people's masses which 
abolishes exploitation, reaction, feudalism, capitalism and fascist forces which 
contradict life amd history. 


The leader of the Great lst September Revolution asserted that the people's Arab unity 
will achieve victory for the benefit of the Arab masses as wage-workers will become 
partners, the house will belong to its occupier and the people will be the masters, 


This was part of the speech given by Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the 
Great lst September Revolution, during the revolutionary rally organized in Banghazi 
tonight. 


V. 9 Mar 79 NORTH AFRICA 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great lst of September Revolution, affirmed 
that the great Al-Fateh revolution stands by the Chad National Liberation Front 
(FROLINAT) in its struggle to the end, He said that that stems from the nature of our 
revolutionary movement, 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that there are French and Egyptian troops 
fighting the Chadian people with Felix Malloum's regime, 


In his revolutionary statement today Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 
lst of September Revolution, touched upon the current fighting between South Yemen and 
North Yemen and said, "We are against this fighting and are taking actions with those who 
are endeavouring to halt it through the bodies assigned by the people's congresses to deal 
with foreign issues, However if what is going on is a revolution then it concerns us 

and the matter does not concern the Foreign Secretariat which was assigned by the General 
People's Congress and decided upon by the people's congresses to deal with foreign issues, 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi said that if there were a revolution in Yemen, then these 
matters concern us, the forces of revolution in the Jamahiriyah, 


Our message, we, the revolutionary committees, is revolution until the masses are 
victorious and until the false borders between the peoples of the Arab nation disappear 
and until the peoples are victorious over their floggers and oppressors, 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi declared the support of the Jamahiriyah's revolutionary 
forces for the Democratic Front of North Yemen, He said: Should the front request us 
to fight by its side, the revolutionary committees would positively respond to this 
request for we stand by the peoples and their revolutions, Similarly if a people's 
revolution takes place in Yemen, we together with the revolutionary forces would support 
it and this applies anywhere in Morocco and Egypt. 


We stand by the people's revolution, the peoples and the masses and no one else, This is 
a national and revolutionary principle which emanates from the core of our cause, for 
revolution is our main preoccupation as it inspires life and makes history, and we, 

the forces which create life and oppose the worn out agonizing forces hostile to the 
masses, reject reaction in Morocco, Egypt, Yemen or anywhere else, We will fight 
(?side) by side with the revolutionary forces against the reactionary forces hostile 

to us and which obstruct our course everyday and collaborate with imperialism to strike 
the Arab nation, 


At the revolutionary rally in Banghazi this evening Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader 
of the Great lst of September Revolution touched upon the suspicious visit by the 
American President to the Egyptian capital, He said; Masses in Egypt have been made 

to applaud the leader of world imperialism, Carter, who arrived in Egypt today, 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi indicated that this is a farce which is meant to hint 
that an Apab people loves the American President at a time when ordinary Egyptian 
citizens do not even know the location of America in the world, How then will they 
love the American President, Carter in particular, while they do not know how to 
pronounce his name? 


He said, "If the Egyptian masses employed to receive Carter today were asked about 
America they will not even know its location on the map," 


V. 9 Mar 79 | NORTH AFRICA 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that this farce which is now played in the Arab arena 
in the absence of organized revolutionary forces, incites people to revolt, Which is 
more powerful, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi questioned, the Egyptian Government or the masses? 


Answering this question he described the Egyptian masses as "an ocean that drowns 
thousands of governments in one day." But these masses, he added, need revolutionary 
committees to incite them openly, 


He went on to say: "We represent the will-power of the revolution, the challenge, the 

new revolutionary tide and the conscious forces which know well the law of revolution, 
analyse the international facts and are aware of the reality of imperialism, reaction 
and the rule which links them together, as well as the inevitability of people's victory 
in the end, We can foresee tomorrow's events since the revolutionary forces are the 
foreteller of the masses, We tell them that the streets which are now washed clean by 
water for the American President, will in the future be washed by blood to wipe away 

the traces of the car of the American President and whoever brought him there," 


He said, "We tell the American President that if he wants to wage a campaign for his 
presidency, the term of which expires in 1980, he should not use the Arab nation as a 
scene for his campaign, for the American presidency is a matter which concerns the 
Americans alone, It should take place in America not on an Arab territory." 


He said, "We, the conscious revolutionary forces, loyal to the masses and growing out 
of them define the American President's visit as his presidential campaign, The Arab 
nation has no relation whatsoever with the election of an American President," 


He went on to say: "The American President wants to prove to his people that he found 
a settlement to the Middle East problem and signed a peace treaty between the Arabs 

and the Zionists, in order to be reelected, However we say that peace is not as wished 
by the American President or people, nor is it associated with the American elections, 
Peace is as wished by the Arab nation, regardless of any agreements signed by the 
American President or treaties of capitulation and humiliation signed by the Egyptian 
president, 


He stressed that only the Arab people know what peace is to the Arab nation, how true 
peace documentc are signed and how they are written...when the Arab nation and the 
Palestinian people achieve victory over the aggression and Palestine is fully 
liberated," 


He said, "Our history knows nothing but Palestine, It knows nothing called "Israel", 
That is a racist phenomenon which emerged after World War II, The American President, 
who is not concerned with the causes of the peoples but with growing cocoa [as 
received], trading in it, raising cattle, is ignorant of this fact, 


Colonel Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi added: We say to the American President from the position 
of the revolution and not of authority, because the revolution knows no limits, we say 
to him, we are more entitled to express the Arab cause than the American President, 

We are more entitled to intervene than an American President coming from overseas, we 
are more entitled to solve it, we are those whose voice should be raised and heard 
because it is the voice of the owners of the cause and the voice of the animate force 

in the Arab nation which will create the future,

He added, addressing the American President: Any treaty you conclude with the Zionists 
and the Egyptian Government may well be benefitial for you in the next presidential 
election, and you will be the winner and Egypt will be the loser and consequently the 
Arab nation, However any treaty of this kind to be reached will be thrown in the dustbin, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that the Arab nation will not recognize any treaty signed 
by an agent retreating traitor and the leader of imperialism, The Arab nation will 
bring about peace in its lands in the way it sees proper, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi addressed the American President by saying: "We do not object to 
supplying the Egyptian Army with American fighters and weapons as we are quite certain 
such arms will be against the Egyptian President himself, his regime, and against 
Zionists and the American presence in the Arab area, 


In this respect, he pointed out that the United States armed the shah as a person and as 
a dictatorial reactionary regime which is friendly with the imperialist and racist forces 
in South Africa and occupied Palestine, But overnight, the shah of Iran fled with his 
wife leaving the American weapons behind in the hands of the free Iranian people, 


He asserted that the capitulatory treaty that might be reached by the Egyptian president, 
the American President and the (Israeli) enemy, would be blown off by the same American 
weapons to be supplied to the Egyptian regime, This, with God's will, is not very far, 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great lst of September Revolution, touched 
upon the hostile campaign waged by Western imperialist media in coordination with the 
Egyptian governmental media against the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah because 
of her principled revolutionary stands towards the causes of the world, 


In this respect Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi pointed to what had been reiterated by 
Western media, and in particular the British broadcasting service, the West German radio 
of lies and allegations published by Egyptian governmental media, 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi wondered what motives make the hostile Western media 
report these false and groundless allegations, He said, "The reason is clear: The 
official Egyptian press now is only a mouthpiece for imperialist circles and the news 
which it publishes conforms exactly with imperialist policies which are hostile to the 
Arab nation." 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi also wondered about the reason why these Western media do 
not publish the true news reported by JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY about what is going on in 
Egypt such as the workers! uprising in Hulwan in the last two days, the students! 
demonstrations in Egypt against the American President's visit, and the assault waged 
by revolutionary groups against Egyptian police, 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi said that this behaviour does not reflect the objectivity 
of these media but their enmity to the Libyan people, This is obvious because the 
published false news which have no foundation quoting a mass medium, [word indistinct] 
but financed by imperialism, This shows that Western mass media act against the 
Libyan people, spreading anti-Libyan propaganda.

and answer back, Britain, West Germany, France and America...each of these countries 
has sensitive points and suffers from open wounds, They know that if they wish to be

AL-QADHDHAFI COMMENTS ON RELATIONS WITH TURKEY 


NCO082118 Ankara Domestic Television Service in Turkish 1950 GMT 8 Mar 79 NC 


(TRT interview with Libyan leader Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi presumably in Libya; date 
not given--recorded in Arabic with superimposed Turkish translation] 


[Excerpt] [Question] Following the recent visit paid by Prime Minister Bulent Ecevit 
to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, an agreement and two documents were 
signed, Thus, ties of friendship and brotherhood between the two countries were 
strengthened through economic and technical cooperation, Could you give TRT your views 
on this issue, particularly on the present state and the future of Turkish-Libyan 
relations? 


[Answer] I am pleased that relations between Turkey and Libya have developed in such a 
short period of time, After the revolution in Libya we exerted major efforts and 
established a bridge of brotnehood and cooperation between the Arabs and the Turks, 

We can never forget that we received support from the Turks during our struggle against 
{word indistinct] and that we defended our land together. The Turks, too, do not forget 
that Arabs fell as martyrs by their side, We must remember with gratitude those who 

fell together under the banner of Islam and we must revitalize this brotherhood, We have 
started this process in Turkish-Libyan relations in particular, We drew up joint plans 
in several fields with an eye to enabling Turkey to overcome its current economic 

crisis. These plans cover the economic field in particular, 


[Question] According to news agency reports, Libyan oil sales to foreign countries 
have been restricted through a declaration of [words indistinct], Will this restriction 
affect Turkey? 


[Answer] No, it will have no effect. We are implementing a special procedure for oil 
exports to Turkey; we are providing special facilities, 


[Announcer] The Libyan President [as heard] said in reply to another question that he 
has been invited to Turkey and that he will be pleased to visit Turkey at a suitable 
time,
