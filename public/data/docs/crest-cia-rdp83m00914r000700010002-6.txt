2. As I mentioned to you in my memo on Monday, the Law of the Sea

The first three strategies outline ways to implement Option 1 of the basic 
Memorandum to the President--to withdraw from the Law of the Sea Conference 
because an acceptable deep seabed regime is unattainable and further nego- 
tiations on the existing text would reflect tacit acquiesence to the un- 
realistic goals of the LDCs. Strategies 4 through 6 outline three approaches

negotiations with a goal of improving the deap seabeds provisions, while 
simultaneously safeguarding the non-seabed provisions, particularly those 
that protect U.S. military and commercial navigational freedoms.
