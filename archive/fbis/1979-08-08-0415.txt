ARAB PEOPLE‘S CONGRESS ISSUES STATEMENT ON MEETINGS 
LDO41340 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GM? 4 Aug 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People’s Congress last night con- 
cluded its meetings which began in Tripoli on 31 July 1979. At the final meeting it 
issued a statement, the text of which is as follows: 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People!s Congeess has concluded its fourth ordinary 
session held in Tripoli from 31 July to 3 August 1979, during which time it carefully 
studied the development of political events in the Arab region, and related world de- 
velopments, It followed the escalation and ferocity of the imperialist onslaught against 
it. It considers that the U,S,-Zionist-As-Sadat. alliance has now moved into the phase 
of action to implement conspiratorial and liquidatory plans against the Arab nation and 
the rights of the Palestinian people, in coordination with world imperialism and its tools 
and puppets in the Arab homeland. The aim is to perpetuate and heighten tension, to 
shatter Arab strength, to incite Arab forces against one another and to foment internal 
conflicts and side-issues in order to divert attention from this alliance and to dissi- 
pate the Arab strength which could be directed against it. 


The moves and pressure are being effected through a number of axes to weaken Arab stead- 
fastness and to strike at its cohesion, 


In the Arab arena the following is being clearly noticed: continuation and escalation of 
aggression by the enemy and its agents in southern Lebanon to make the people there accept 
occupation within the state of traitor Sa'd Haddad out of desperation; continued plotting 
to exclude theSyrian Arab Army from Lebanon, to leave the PLO alone in the field, and to 
bring about thedivision of the Zionization of Lebanon; continued fomenting of sectarian 
conflicts and attempts to spread them to other Arab countries, to create dissent and to 
incite agents and puppets to perpetrate massacres in order to speed up the kindling of 
these conflicts.

The latest example of this was implemented in the artillery academy in Aleppo; continued 
implementation of the assassination plan against the leadership of the Palestinian resis- 
tence, of the self-government plot and the normalization of relations between Egypt and 
the enemy. 


The parties of the alliance are trying to create alternatives to the PLO in the West Bank 
and the Gaza sector to complete the stages of the liquidation of the problem and to goad 
Jordan into joining the Camp David agreements. 


While all this takes place in the arena of confrontation with the Zionist enemy, the 
Egyptian president's regime for its part masses troops on the borders of the Jamahiriyah 
and helps the imperialist agent regime of Qabus to countenance the Omani revolution, 
thus completing its role as the policeman of imperialism in the Gulf, the Red Sea and 
Africa, 


The agent Egyptian president, while continuing his treason against the nation, is prac- 
tising the biggest falsification operation of the will of our Arab people in Egypt and 
is daily taking further strangling measures against the Egyptian national progressive 
movement which is bravely fighting a fierce battle against his regime and treason. 


Imperialist and reactionary pressure against the Arab region is increasing and widening 

to include many fronts and areas, most serious of which is the engineering of the oil 
crisis and holding the Arabs responsible for it and inciting the world against them. 
Imperialist pressure is making itself felt in various ways, beginning with the direct 
threat of occupation--in particular, occupation of the oilfields for which a Franco-U%S, 
force has been set up under the banner of protecting interests--(?and ending with) secret 
Plotting, putting on pressure, and the creation of obstacles to all forms of progress 

and unity between Arab countries, most prominent of which is the example of the two Yemens. 


World imperialism, which is going through a general crisis--most evidently witnessed by 
the economic crisis, some of whosemanifestations have been witnessed recently in the U,S.-- 
strives to solve its problems at the expense of poor and weak peoples, and in particular 

at the expense of our Arab people, Meetings at the imperialist Tokyo summit, and the 
European move within the EEC and the attempts to present papers to save the reputation 

of the stable David agreements and to pump new blood into them, and also moves by the 
Socialist International, are all prominent signs and indications of this trend. 


Our Arab people, however, who have been subjected to successive concentrated attacks 

for a long time, are proving at every stage their vitality and endless ability to strug- 
gle, to stand fast and to confront. The Arab arena witnesses honorable forms of con- 
frontation, foremost of which are: the steadfastness of our Arab people in the occupied 
land before the plot of self-rule and the continued attack on it to abort it. 


Prominent examples of this confrontation are: the Jerusalem conference of 20-22 July 
1979; the successful operations of the Palestinian resistance in the heart of the occu- 
pied land, which prove daily the revival of the spirit of struggle of our Palestinian 
Arab people; the steadfastness of our Lebanese Arab people in southern Lebanon and their 
challenging of the enemy, his stooges and conspiracies, despite the poor condition in 
which they live and despite the great sacrifices they have made;

The Syrian Arab Air Force confrontation of the enemy!s air force over southern Lebanon 
with all the national implications that this has, despite the presence of enemy concen- 
trations along the Syrian front and the threat that the enemy presents with the aid of 

its agents in Lebanon to strategically sensitive regions in Syria; the strategic meeting 
between the leadership of the Libyan and Palestinian revolutions which confirmed support 
of the Palestinian revolutions and the Lebanese progressive national movement; the working 
tour of Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi to Arab countries which came as a reinforcement of the 
role of the steadfastness and confrontation front and to the full pledgeto implement the 
Baghdad summit resolutions, 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress, which works to kindle the spirit 
of struggle, steadfastness and confrontation in the Arab masses in praising the stead- 
fastness of our masses and their confrontation of all forms of conspiracy against them 
and their rights, urges the Jamahiriyah and progressive Arab regimes to give priority 

to the requirements of the Arab national struggle and place the central cause of the 
modern Arab struggle--the Palestine problem--above all causes, and make the main dispute 
between our nation and the enemy above any other dispute, 


For this reason the secretariat, in the name of the Arab masses, stressed the need to 
implement in full the resolutions of the Baghdad conference: strengthening the role of 
the front of steadfastness and confrontation and making it more effective in shaping 
events and in confrontation and calling on Iraq to join it; accomplishing the unity steps 
between Syria and Iraq in order to translate into reality the joint national action 
charter; removing the existing obstacles in the path of the unity of both parts of Yemen; 
supporting the unionist steps between the Jamahiriyah and Algeria; continuing every form 
of backing for Syria, the Palestine revolution and the Lebanese national and patriotic 
movement; supporting the struggle of the Arab people in Egypt in its resistance to the 
regime of the defeatist Egyptian president to bring Egypt back to its natural place in 
the Arab struggle; giving support to the armed liberation movements in the Arab homeland 
so that they can intensify their fight for liberation and self-determination, especially 
the revolutionary movements in Oman, the Sahara and in Eritrea; struggling for unity as 

a strategic weapon in our battle against Zionism and imperialism; considering democracy 
as the foundation stone for the upsurge and liberation of Arab man. 


Our confidence in the ability and vitality of our people, in their fighters and their 
sincerity, in our lawful rights and in the strength and solidity of our friendship with 
the forces of liberation in the world lead us to be more certain in continuing the strug- 
gle until the Arab nation achieves an honorable victory, regains its usurped rights, 
determines future and builds its unity in freedom and dignity befitting its history, men 
and struggle. 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress, believing in the serious nature 
of the phase and of the need to move expeditiously to countenance it, will send delega- 
tions to the Arab countries to consult on ways to continue the struggle and to stand 

fast in the face of imperialist designs in a spirit imbued with brotherhood and democracy, 
common serious action and strengthening the mational people's struggle fund. 


The secretariat also issued several other documents.
