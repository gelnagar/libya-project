Summary (AI): An FBIS transcript of a 6 September 1988 Tripoli TV broadcast of an interview Qadhdhafi gave to Algerian television on 31 August 1988. He dismisses the effect of a Western economic blockade, praises self-sufficiency, and comments on Maghreb unity and the Western Sahara settlement. It describes no specific Tripoli places or events.
• Interview recorded in Tripoli on 31 August 1988 by unidentified Algerian television correspondents.
• Qadhdhafi says Libya is not afraid of an economic blockade and that the blockade benefited Libya by pushing it toward production and self-sufficiency.
• Qadhdhafi says the Western Sahara process via the UN has harmed Arab and Maghreb solutions.
• The transcript mentions the 1 September revolution anniversary and the Green Book as a constitution-like document.

//(CINTERVIEW WITH COLONEL MU'AMMAR AL-QADHDHAFI BY 
ALGERIAN TELEVISION IN TRIPOLI ON 31 AUGUST; QUESTIONS ARE 
ASKED BY UNIDENTIFIED ALGERIAN TELEVISION CORRESPONDENTS -- 
RECORDED) ) 

((EXCERPTS)) ((PASSAGE OMITTED: INTRODUCTION TO THE PRESS 
CONFERENCE THANKING AL-QADHDHAFI FOR THIS OPPORTUNITY TO 
QUESTION HIM; QUESTION ON THE EXTENT TO WHICH THE 1 
SEPTEMBER REVOLUTION'S OBJECTIVES HAVE BEEN ACHIEVED; 
AL-QADHDHAFI'S ANSWER SAYING THAT LIBYA HAS BECOME THE FIRST 
JAMAHIRIYAH ON EARTH WHICH HAS, MOREOVER, GIVEN AUTHORITY TO 
THE PEOPLE AND WHICH HAS SUBSTITUTED PARTNERSHIPS IN PLACE 
OF THE SYSTEM OF WAGE-EARNERS; QUESTION ON WHETHER THE 
RECENT GREEN DOCUMENT AMOUNTS TO A CONSTITUTION; AL- 
QADHDHAFI'S ANSWER SAYING THAT WHAT HAS HAPPENED TS MERELY 
AN IMPLEMENTATION OF THE GREEN BOOK, WHICH PROVIDES ANSWERS 
TO ALL INJUSTICES) ) 

((QUESTION)) IT IS KNOWN THAT THE WESTERN STATES TRIED, 
THROUGH THE DIRECT U.S. AGGRESSION ON THE SOVERIEGNTY OF THE 
JAMAHIRIYAH, TO IMPOSE AN ECONOMIC BLOCKADE AGAINST IT. HOW 
DID THE REVOLUTION COPE WITH THE REPERCUSSIONS OF THAT 
BLOCKADE? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) AS FAR AS WE ARE CONCERNED -- LIBYA, 
ALGERIA, ALL THESE STATES -- WE ARE NOT AFRAID OF AN | 
ECONOMIC BLOCKADE. THE ISSUE, IF YOU WOULD EXCUSE ME, OF 
SPEAKING ABOUT AN ECONOMIC BLOCKADE IS SILLY. WE ARE STATES 
THAT COULD NOT LOSE. - HOW COULD A BLOCKADE AFFECT US -- WILL 
ALL THESE ELECTRONIC VEHICLES THAT WE HAVE CEASE TO WORK, OR 
WILL THE EXPORT TO THEIR MARKETS...((AL-QADHDHAFI CHANGES 
THOUGHT) ) WE ARE CONSUMERS. THIS BLOCKADE BENEFITTED US A 
LOT. THEY ASK FOR CONSUMER THINGS, USELESS THINGS LIKE 
THOSE IN FRONT OF US, AND THE PEOPLE ASK WHERE ARE THEY? 
THEY HAVE PROHIBITED THEM, THOSE STATES WHICH MANUFACTURE 
THEM. GOOD, ((WORDS INDISTINCT)). WE LAUGHED AT THEM WHEN 
THEY SPOKE OF A BLOCKADE. ((WORDS INDISTINCT) ) 

THE OIL IS BEING SOLD, AND THIS IS THE ONLY THING WHICH 
WE AND YOU EXPORT. THIS OIL IS BEING SOLD, AND THE 
REMAINDER IS WHAT WE IMPORT. IF THEY WITHHOLD THE CONSUMER 
MATERIAL THAT WE IMPORT FROM THEM, THAT WOULD BE IN OUR 
INTERESTS. INDEED, IT BENEFITTED US VERY MUCH. DURING 
THOSE YEARS, WE TURNED TOWARD PRODUCTION AND 
SELF-SUFFICIENCY. WE DID NOT LOSE ANYTHING. WHAT? DO WE 
HAVE CAR FACTORIES AND EXPORT HUNDREDS OF THOUSANDS OF CARS 
TO THEIR MARKETS AND, AS A RESULT OF THEIR BLOCKADE, HAVE WE 
ACCUMULATED CARS? DO WE MAKE PLANES AND SHIPS, WHICH WE 
EXPORT TO THEM, AND WHICH THEY BLOCKADED US BY NOT 
PURCHASING FROM US? NO, WE DO NOT EXPORT, WE CONSUME. IF 
THEY BLOCKADE JAPAN, PERHAPS IT WOULD BE HARMED. IF THEY 
BLOCKADE WEST GERMANY, PERHAPS IT WOULD BE HARMED. BUT WHEN 
THEY BLOCKADE COUNTRIES LIKE LIBYA AND ALGERIA, WE DO NOT 
EVEN FEEL...((AL-QADHDHAFI CHANGES THOUGHT)) THIS IS A 
FAILED AND STUPID POLICY WITH WHICH THEY COOL THEMSELVES

((QUESTION)) POLITICAL OBSERVERS AGREE THAT THE CHANCES 
AND OPPORTUNITIES FOR HTE ACHIEVEMENT OF THE MAGHREB'S UNITY 
PROJECT ARE NOW BETTER THAN EVER, ESPECIALLY AFTER THE AIR 
WAS CLEARED BETWEEN THE JAMAHIRIYAH, TUNISIA, ALGERIA, AND 
MOROCCO; THE NORMALIZATION OF THEIR BILATERAL RELATIONS; AND 
FOLLOWING THE CURRENT EFFORTS BEING MADE UNDER THE AUSPICES 
OF THE UN SECRETARY GENERAL TO IMPLEMENT THE OAU AND UN 
RESOLUTIONS REGARDING THE CONFLICT OF THE WESTERN SAHARA. 
THESE FACTORS REPRESENT POSITIVE BASES FOR THE CREATION OF A 
NEW DYNAMIC IN THE MAGHREB. BROTHER COLONEL, WHAT IS YOUR 
ASSESSMENT OF THESE FACTORS AND THIS DYNAMIC? AND WHAT ARE 
YOUR VIEWS ON THE SETTLEMENT OF THE PROBLEM OF THE WESTERN 
SAHARA WITHIN THE EFFORTS BEING MADE TO FIND A LASTING AND 
JUST SOLUTION TO THIS PROBLEM? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) WE ARE GLAD TO SEE THAT SERIOUS MOVES 
ARE BEING MADE THESE DAYS TO FIND A SOLUTION TO THE PROBLEM 
OF THE SAHARA. I WAS HOPING TO SEE AN ARAB, AND EVEN A 
MAGHREBIAN SOLUTION TO BE REACHED AMONG OURSELVES. THE FACT 
THAT THIS IS TAKING PLACE VIA THE UN HAS HARMED US IN FACT. 
WHY IS IT THAT IT IS THE UN WHICH SOLVES A PROBLEM | 
CONCERNING ARABS. HOWEVER, MAY GOD FORGIVE OUR BROTHERS IN 
THE REGION WHO DID NOT GIVE THEIR BROTHERS THE OPPORTUNITY 
TO SOLVE THIS PROBLEM IN THE FAMILY AND REACH AN ARAB, PAN- 
ARAB, AND MAGHREBIAN SOLUTION. .IN ANY CASE, WE ARE GLAD 
THAT THINGS ARE MOVING THIS WAY, AREN'T WE?
