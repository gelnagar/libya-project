LIBYA

JANA REPORTS NUMAYRI REVEALS HOSTILE INTENTIONS 


LD181552 Tripoli JANA in English 1503 GMT 18 Jun 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Jun (JANA)--President Ja'far Numayri of Sudan has delivered a speech at 
the opening session of the Regional People's Assembly of Kurdufan. He said addressing 
Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi that Libya's borders with Sudan would be a graveyard for the 
Libyans. 


Thus, Numayri has shown his hostile intentions and disclosed hostile plans he is engineering 
against Libya and has confirmed reports published in London on a military plan involving 
U.S.A., Egypt, Sudan and Israel to attack Libya. 20 Israeli military advisors drew up the 
plan, which includes a military offensive involving 120 planes, including 100 bombers, and 
tank divisions and artillery batteries. 


President Numayri made a statement to the HERALD TRIBUNE on the lst of April in which he 
said: "We should do something to overthrow this man (Al-Qadhdhafi) by any means whether 
war or assassination. 


On 16 May, he repeated to the West German news agency D.P.A. that his strategic goal was 
to get rid of Al-Qadhdhafi and topple his regime. 


In the light of these hostile plots announced by Numayri against Libya, which he repeatedly 
affirmed, in coordination and collaboration with the U.S. administration, the Israelis and 
the Egyptian regime, Libya has briefed the United Nations, the organization of African unity 
(OAU) and the Arab League on this plot in order for these organizations to shoulder their 
responsibility in facing Numayri's attempt which is aimed at destabilizing peace and security 
in Africa and the Middle East. 


JANA's political editor comments that Numayri has announced that he was preparing for a war 
in Africa and an attack against Chad and that 90,000 Sudanese soldiers were being deployed 
along Chad's eastern borders to back the insurgent gangs there. He is also preparing an 
attack against Libya as part of his policy which is being drawn by the U.S.A. for execution 
by Sudan, Egypt and Israel. Therefore, Numayri should bear the responsibility of his actions 
especially after he revealed to the whole world his hostile plans and intentions. 


NEW SUDANESE OPPOSITION GROUP'S STATEMENT CITED 


LD181524 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 18 Jun 81 
[East African correspondent dispatch] 


[Text] The birth of an anti-Sudanese regime military organization called the Organization 
of the Officers and the People's Armed Forces for the Salvation of the Homeland has been 
announced in Sudan. The London office of the Sudanese organization has issued a statement 
on the occasion. The statement says: 


Our country has been fated to have an agent regime squatting on its chest--a regime which 
has been created and planted by the intelligence services which hid behind doors and in 
embassies, which is based on lies and deception and which trifles with the dignity and 
humanity of the Sudanese people. This has prompted the patriotic sons of the people to 
oppose this regime in a series of heroic battles in which the cream of the Sudanese people, 
peasants, students, workers and intellectuals, were martyred.

The statement adds: It has become evident to us, the armed forces, that this regime is 
isolated from the masses of the Sudanese people. This has resulted in the collapse of 
Sudan's economy and in Sudan's dependence on the crumbs from the masters. It has also 
resulted in Sudan's forfeiting its independence and territory and in its being turned 
into a base where the American armies of occupation are stationed. 


The statement adds that these circumstances have prompted us to play our historic role 
toward the Arab nation, the safety of its territory and safeguarding its independence. 
We, as protectors of the people, declare on our territory and publically the birth our 
organization: The Organization of the Officers and the People's Armed Forces for the 
Salvation of the Homeland. This is a true embodiment of the will of the patriotic 
officers, NCO's and soldiers. 


Our first and last goal, the statement adds, is to rescue our country and [word indistinct] 
and blind subservience, and also to stand up to the danger threatening our country. 


In conclusion the statement says: We must call upon our people, intellectuals, workers, 
businessman, students and peasants, to be vigilant and reject the tricks of this dubious 
regime and to prepare the ranks for the day of deliverance--a day which they see as far 
off, but which we see it as very close. 


"SOURCES' REPORT ISRAELI PLANES LAND IN SUDAN 


LD182344 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 18 Jun 81 


[Text] Sudanese sources in London said that a group of Israeli military aircraft landed 

the day before yesterday at a Sudanese military base. The sources said that the landing 
of the Israeli military aircraft had been shrouded in complete secrecy, and that all the 
Sudanese junior officers and those whose allegiance to the regime was in doubt had been 

evacuated from the base. The sources pointed out that these planes could well have been 
sent in to launch an aggression against an Arab confrontation.
