LIBYA

NIGER FOREIGN, MINES MINISTERS ARRIVE FOR TALKS 
LD081826 Tripoli JANA in English 1742 GMT 8 Aug 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, Aug 8, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Niger minister of foreign affairs 
and cooperation, Maj Moumouni Adamou Djermakoye, the minister of mines, Mounkeila 
Arouna, and a number of officials arrived here this afternoon on a visit to the 
Jamahiriyah lasting for a few days. The Niger delegation will hold talks on relations 
between the two countries. 


The delegation was received at Tripoli International Airport by the foreign secretary, 
the secretary of the General People's Committee for Electricity, the Niger ambassador 
to the Jamahiriyah and a number of directors at the Foreign Secretariat. Ina statement 
to our correspondent, Mr Djermakoye said he was pleased to visit the Jamahiriyah and 
said his visit came within the context of promoting Niger-Jamahiriyah consultations 

and cooperation. 


He said his country together with the Jamahiriyah will exert joint efforts to bring 
about national conciliation and peace to Chad in connection with other neighbouring 
countries, 


He commended the Jamahiriyah's endeavours to this effect. 


V. 10 Aug 79 a NORTH AFRICA 


YAR FOREIGN MINISTER AL-MAKKI ENDS VISIT 9 AUG 
LDO91130 Tripoli JANA in English 0805 GMT 9 Aug 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, 9 August, JANA--Hasan al-Makki, deputy prime minister and minister of 
foreign affairs in the Yemen Arab Republic, left Tripoli yesterday after a short visit 
to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah during which he delivered a message 
from 'Abdallah Salih, the Yemeni president, to Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader 
of the Great First of September Revolution. 


Al-Makki was seen off at Tripoli International Airport by the foreign secretary, 
secretary of the General People's Committee for Electricity, the ambassadors of Yemen 
Arab Republic and Yemen Democratic Republic to the Jamahiriyah, and representatives 
from the Foreign Secretariat. 


In a statement to JANA, Hasan al-Makki expressed his pleasure at his visit which came 
within the context of the cooperation between the two countries, and praised the 
courageous stands of the leader of the First of September Revolution towards the causes 
of the Arab natione He said: "The stands of the Jamahiriyah and the leader of the 
revolution are known for their steadfastness and confrontation and their defence of 
causes of destiny of the Arab nation and Third World, inclucing the freedom of people 
still struggling for liberation." 


Hasan al-Makki affirmed that the Great First of September Revolution is another landmark 
in the struggle of the Arab nation. Supporting its stands and proving the nation's 
capability for steadfastness and confrontation. 


He pointed out that his visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah came 
within the framework of bilateral talks serving the Arab cause and international issues 
of common interest.
