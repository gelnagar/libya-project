CANADIAN AUTOBUS DEAL--The first shipment of Canadian autobuses will arrive 
soon in Baghdad. They were ordered by the Public Transportation Board in the 
Iraqi capital to be put into passenger service in Baghdad. This shipment re- 
presents part of a 42 million pounds sterling deal, which the Iraqi authorities 
signed with a branch of the Canadian Bombardiyah Company, which operates an 
autobus factory in the city of Limerick, Ireland. In accordance with the deal, 
Baghdad will receive 500 double-decker busses from Ireland, equipped with 
British Rolls Royce engines. It is worth pointing out that the municipality 

of Baghdad is considered one of the largest spenders for growth and development 
projects in Iraq. However, foreign companies have recently reported that they 
must offer the required financing for projects to be implemented in the Iraqi 
capital, for fearthat their projects will be subject to a freeze. This request 
comes in the wake of measures of governmental expenditure pressure, to which 
Iraq is resorting due to the reduction of foreign cash reserves to about $8 
billion. [Text] [Paris AL-MUSTAQBAL in Arabic No 305, 25 Dec 82 p 37] 7005
