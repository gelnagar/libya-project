Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery briefing note dated 14 December 1983 on the Tripoli area of northern Lebanon (Tarabulus), not Libya's capital. It describes pro- and anti-Arafat PLO forces, Syrian artillery, and damage to the port and refinery. It has no connection to Tripoli, Libya.
• The document covers Tripoli in northern Lebanon, not Tripoli, Libya.
• Anti-Arafat forces had tanks overlooking the city, including about six probable T-54/55 tanks.
• Syrian forces deployed one field artillery battalion (18 M-46 130mm guns) and one BM-21 MRL battalion in support of anti-Arafat forces.
• At the port, two warehouses were destroyed and a freighter was sunk; at the refinery, 21 of about 33 POL tanks were destroyed.
• 1983-12: Pro-Arafat PLO forces occupied Tripoli, Lebanon, and Al Baddawi camp; anti-Arafat forces backed by Syrian artillery surrounded the city.
