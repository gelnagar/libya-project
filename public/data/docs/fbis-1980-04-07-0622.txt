AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES MESSAGE ON CHAD FROM OAU CHARIMAN 


LD051902 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arahic 1600 GMT 5 Apr 80 LD 


[Text] The leader has received a telegram from William Tolbert, president of Liberia 

and current chairman of the OAU, In his telegram, the Liberian president underlines the 
present tragedy of the Chadian people, due to a destructive power struggle now taking place 
in Chad. He also reviews OAU efforts to establish security and peace in Chad. He says 
that given the intractability of some elements, the desired security and peace could not 

be brought about. President Tolbert urges the brother leader to do his utmost to find a 
rapid solution for the Chadian problem, which will insure the establishment of peace and 
the enforcement of the Lagos agreement,

CHADIAN ENVOYS APPEAL FOR AID ON GOUKOUNI'S BEHALF 


LDO41930 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1630 GMT 5 Apr 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 5 April 1980, JANA--The Chadian delegation sent by the Chadian Provision- 
al Government has held a press conference in Tripoli in the presence of a number of corres- 
pondents of various international information media. At the beginning of the conference, 
Youssef Siad, representing President Goukouni, read a communique in which he said: 


As you have heard in the news, particularly in connection with Ndjamena, we are now fight- 
ing a war against a member of the government, namely Hissein Habre, We deeply regret this 
war, which has been fueled by the colonialist forces. 


After the Lagos agreement, he added, the government found itself unable to discharge its 
duties, due particularly to Hissein Habre, who has impeded the carrying out of any pro- 
vision of the Lagos agreement by obstructing the evacuation of the French forces and the 
implementation of the cease-fire mentioned in the agreement. 


Goukouni's representative explained that Ndjamena was now experiencing fierce fighting 
waged by the popular armed forces against Hissein Habre and his followers. This dissident 
criminal, backed by imperialism, constitutes a danger to our country. He has a hand in 
the prevailing instability which has forced citizens to flee from Ndjamena. 


He said: Chad is now experiencing a very difficult period, and this is why the head of 
the provisional government has charged me with delivering a letter calling for help to 

Col Mutammar aleQadhdhafi so that he can help us overcome this problem and this impasse 
and help the population of Ndjamena to enjoy stability and quiet. 


To a question from the German television correspondent on whether he had asked for assist- 
ance from the Libyan armed forces, Goukouni!s representative said: I have brought a letter 
to the brother Colonel in which we ask for the he_p of the Jamahiriyah, because we know 
that the Jamahiriyah will not remain with its arms folded when its brothers in Chad are 
under the threat of death, 


To a question from the correspondent of the British GUARDIAN about the reason for the 
call for help to Libya, he said: Libya, one of the participants in the Lagos conference, 
is under an obligation to observe it. Furthermore, Libya will not remain with its arms 
folded in connection with its brothers. Libyan help for us can be explained from this 
fraternal point of view. 


To a question from the JANA correspondent on the kind of aid the Egyptian regime is giv- 
ing to Hissein Habre, he said: We are certain that the Egyptian regime is giving arms and 
ammunition to Habre through one of our neighbor countries. We have evidence to this 
effect, and there will be a day when we will disclose this evidence and name this country. 
The world, and particularly the African peoples, should shoulder their responsibilities 

as regards the Egyptian intervention in our country. 


To a question from the correspondent of Japanese radio about the countries which supply 
military aid to Hissein Habre, the Chadian emissary said Hissien Habre has turned his back 
on legitimacy and the Lagos agreements. The Egyptian regime is giving Habre equipment and 
arms, and the colonial forces which have been there, and whose evacuation was opposed by 
Habre, are also helping him in indirect ways.

To a question from the correspondent of INTERPRESS on the situation in Ndjamena, 

Mr Adam Belal, representative of the Chadian interior minister, said: The next few 
hours are going to be decisive because we know that the forces siding with Habre are 
few and will not be able to resist before our popular armed forces. He added that 
Goukouni's call for help from the Jamahiriyah is motivated by humanitarian and 
strategic reasons, as relations between the Jamahiriyah and Chad do not date back to 
today. The Jamahiriyah has helped us before. I can assure you that without the aid of 
the Jamahiriyah, FROLINAT would not be what it is now. Besides, Habre himself would 
not have existed without Libyan aid in the past, before he became a dissident, As 
revolutionaries, he said, we are not ashamed of asking the Jamahiriyah for aid since 
this is for the sake of our freedom and existence, 


SUPPORT OF LAGOS AGREEMENT ON CHAD AFFIRMED 
LDO70123 Tgipoli Domestic Service in Arabic 2315 GMT 6 Apr 80 LD 


["Text" of statement issued by Foreign Secretariat on "recent events in Chad"--read by 
announcer ] 


[Text] Since the signing of the Lagos agreement on national reconciliation in Chad on 
22 August 1979 and the forming of the Transitional National Union Government of Chad 
in accordance with this agreement, it has become clear that elements connected with 
foreign forces which seek control and power have been using all means to prevent the 
implementation of this agreement and the realization of peace and security in Chad. 
last of these attempts was the armed rebellion by the defense minister of the tran- 
sitional government against this government, with the support of foreign forces, a 
matter that has led to wholesale massacres and bloodshed and the flight of scores of 
thousands of Chadians, 


Since the Transitional National Union Government (?attempted to end) this rebellion, 
serious attempts and initiatives have appeared which, in the end, have been con- 
spiracies against the fraternal Chadian people and against Africa. The Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah Foreign Secretariat would like to draw the attention 

of African and international public opinion to the gravity of this conspiracy, affirming 
that the only solution lies in supporting the lawful government which sprang from the 
Lagos agreement to end this rebellion, and this is for the sake of achieving peace and 
security in Chad, 


The Foreign Secretariat also affirms that the solution of the Chad problem lies in the 
implementation of the Lagos agreement, In this connection and ever since the be- 
ginning of the armed rebellion the Jamahiriyah and Niger--two neighboring countries of 
Chad and members of the Lagos conference--have called for the holding of a new session 
of the Lagos conference on national reconciliation in Chad in order to stop the blood- 
shed and put an end to this armed rebellion that is backed by foreign forces, 


The Foreign Secretariat affirms that any attempt to keep out the states neighboring Chad 
and participants in the Lagos conference is nothing but a conspiracy whose aim is con- 
tinuation of the rebellion and hindrance to all the efforts that have been made to 
realize peace in Chad--efforts that began with the conferences of Sabha, Benghazi and 
Tripoli to the conferences of Kano and Lagos--and that states neighboring Chad that 
attended the Lagos agreement conference deem it their responsibility to support the 
transitional government to end this armed rebellion and that this is in implementation 
of the Lagos agreement,

The Foreign Secretariat affirms that it does not agree to any steps or attempts aimed 
at frustrating the Lagos agreement, Also, it will not be bound by any agreement other 
than the Lagos agreement. It furthermore affirms that the Jamahiriyah is committed to 
supporting and backing the transitional government so that it can fulfill its 
commitments and implement the Lagos agreement in letter and spirit. Also, it heeds 
the appeal made by the head of the government of the Transitional National Union and 
the request made by the head and members of the Chadian Government to the Jamahiriyah. 


Accordingly, the Jamahiriyah will not stand with arms folded in the face of this grave 
international conspiracy against the people of Chad and the fraternal peoples of the 
African Continent.
