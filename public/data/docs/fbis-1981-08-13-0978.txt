LIBYA

BULGARIAN CHIEF OF STAFF LEAVES TRIPOLI 12 AUG 


LD1220009 Tripoli JANA in English 1901 GMT 12 Aug 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 12 Aug (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The first deputy defence minister and 
chief of staff in the Socialist [as received] Republic of Bulgaria left Tripoli this 
evening after a visit to Jamahiriyah. He was seen off by Brigadier Jabir, commander-in- 
chief of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces. He told JANA's correspondent that his visit was 
aimed at discussing bilateral relations and ways of increasing cooperation in various 
fields as well as deepening friendship bonds between the two friendly peoples. He also 
praised the giant achievements realized in Libya guided by the Great Al-Fatah Revolution.
