ARAB CONGRESS CONDEMNS U.S., CALLS FOR SUPPORT FOR IRAN 
1D041726 Tripoli JANA in English 1615 GMT 4 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli, May 4, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The permanent secretariat of the Arab 
People's Congress issued a statement to world opinion aimed at supporting the Iranian 
revolution against U.S. aggression, It stressed the following: -Despite the failure 
of the recent U.S. military invasion of Iranian territory, the U.S, administration 
remains openly and unashamedly adamant at continuing its hostility and conspiracy a- 
gainst the Iranian people and their revolution, American imperialism exerts pressure 
and incites its allies and agents to mobilise their resources and back hostile policies 
against the Iranian popular revolution on all levels: economic, political and military. 


The statement elaborated: It can be clearly seen that the American administration is 
attempting to conceal its first abortive military aggression against Iran by preparing 
world and American public opinion, especially in West Europe, for mounting another 
military operation of a wider scope. This time the USA will be supported by wide eco- 
nomic sanctions against the Iranian Revolution as the measures adopted by the European 
Economic Community demonstrate, 


Furthermore, by its blatant hostile measures, imperialist America has revealed its 
wide-scale aggressive plots against the Gulf region in general in order to impose its 
political and economic hegemony on the region, and by the use of force if necessary. 


The statement said the overt and aggressive American policy requires all world democratic 
and progressive forces and anti-colonialist forces to unify their ranks against the 
colonialist U.S, and its allies (world Zionism, Israel, and hireling reactionary regimes, 
especially the defeated Egyptian regime). It called upon these forces to stand side by 
side with the popular Islamic revolution in Iran, defend it in world public forums and 
expose the aggressive goals of U.S, policy towards Iran and its people. 


The statement called for convening a popular international conference in solidarity with 
the popular Islamic revolution in Iran, It said: ‘We are certain that the call for 
such a conference will be positively received by progressive, democratic and anti- 
imperialist circles throughout the world.!

SWAPO OFFICIAL HOLDS TRIPOLI PRESS CONFERENCE 
1LD042206 Tripoli JANA in English 1814 GMT 4 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli, May 4, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The representative [as received] of the 
South-West African People's Organization (SWAPO), Mr, Nashandi, praised the material, 
moral and political support provided by the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
for liberation movements in Africa, Asia and Latin America in their struggle against 
imperialism, Zionism and racism, 


Speaking at a press conference held at the foreign liaison bureau in Tripoli this after- 
noon, Mr, Nashandi affirmed that the people of Namibia stand with the Arab nation in its 
confrontation of the treasonous Stable David conspiracy and imperialism which is aimed 
at subjugating people, especially the Palestinian peoples. He pointed out that SWAPO 
was r ady to enter into negotiations with the Organisation of African Unity (OAU) and 
the nonaligned Movement to find a solution for the problem of Namibia, He also stressed 
SWAPO's rejection of all solutions which are contrary to the interests of the Namibian 
people,
