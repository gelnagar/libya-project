AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES SYRIAN DEPUTY PRIME MINISTER 
LD150048 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 14 Oct 80 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has received Brother ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam, 
the Syrian deputy prime minister and minister of foreign affairs. 


LIBYA DENIES SENDING WEAPONS TO IRAN 
LD110857 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0702 GMT 11 Oct 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 11 Oct (JANA)--The Foreign Secretariat of the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah has denied the tendentious reports spread by the radio of the Zionist 
enemy about the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah ferrying weapons by air to Iran. 
The Foreign Secretariat has described these reports as tendentious. 


MOURNING PERIOD DECLARED FOR ALGERIAN EARTHQUAKE VICTIMS 
LD121124 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 12 Oct 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 12 Oct (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--JANA has learned that the Jamahiriyah 
has declared a 7-day mourning for victims of the earthquake in the Algerian town of 
Al-Asnam which claimed the lives of thousands of people and destroyed a large section of 
the town.
