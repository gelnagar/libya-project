LIBYA TO ESTABLISH RADIO STATION OFF LEBANESE COAST 
LD301429 London AL-MAJALLAH in Arabic 27 Sep-3 Oct 80 p 10 
{From "From AL-MAJALLAH Ambassadors" feature] 
[Text] Libya is making preparations to set up a floating radio station off the Lebanese 
coast following the rejection by Cyprus of a Libyan request to set up such a station 


on its territory. 


COMMENTARY SCORES U.S. INVOLVEMENT IN GULF REGION 


LDO11602 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 1 Oct 80 


{[Unattributed commentary] 


[Excerpts] With or without occasion American terrorism continuously demonstrates and 
clearly confirms how hostile this American administration is to the freedom of people, 
how it crushes their will, lies in wait for their movement, plunders their wealth and 
exploits their resources under the pressures of terrorism and piracy; how it pursues 

a policy of muscle-flexing and exploitation of opportunities to penetrate the strategic 
positions of various parts of the world. The American administration continues to set 
up its bases and espionage equipment, plant its radar networks, let its aircraft and 
fleets roam, intent on subjugating and repressing the people and on encircling the 
liberation tide which rejects its ugly plans. 


This aggressive terrorist action has recently intensified, exploiting the bloody events 
among the brothers and brethren which it hatched and planned, so that it might spring 
up afterward to exploit these events in the interest of its aims in the region.

The terrorist aggressive tide is embodied in confirmed reports that the American ad- 
ministration has issued its terrorist orders for four spy aircraft--equipped with sophis- 
ticated radar networks--to head for the Arabian Gulf region to play their terrorist 
espionage role and to watch events at close range to provide the American spying 
machinery with more information in order to enable it to implement terrorist aggressive 
policies in the region and to dominate by force the resources and wealth of the peoples 
of the region. At the same time the American administration is not satisfied with 

mere spying. Through its aircraft, which are equipped with the most sophisticated 
surveillance, monitoring and spying equipment, at the same time it moves its naval 
forces to back the air aggression in the Arabian Gulf region. Informed quarters have 
confirmed that the American President has issued orders to a number of units of the 
terrorist American fleet to head also to the gulf region. 


The recent escalation of American terrorism, specifically in the Arabian region, places 

a historic responsibility on the peoples of the region to challenge this vicious terror- 
ist assault which is first of all aimed at striking the revolutionary tide movement, 
dominating the resources and the wealth of the peoples of that region and encircling 

the revolution movement. It is aimed subsequently at protecting its exploiting interests 
at the expense of the progress and freedom of the region. 


The escalation of American terrorism is also aimed at exploiting the bloody events 

among the brothers and brethren who are supposed to marshal their energies and potentials 
to challenge the colonialist plans in the region, to strike at themand to liquidate 

them in order to foil all their intrigue and to defeat all their terrorist crimes. 


When the revolutionaries cry to the masses of the region, urging them to take a decisive 
stand against American terrorism in the region, it is because the masses recognize 

the agency of the regimes and the way they throw themselves in the laps of American 
colonialism, and because the ruling regimes are interested in American presence to con- 
solidate their hold over the minds and hearts of the masses. When the regimes hide and 
seek the protection of the colonialist quarters and arsenals, it is because they have 
lost all their links with the masses, as well as with the pan-Arab aims and aspirations 
which the masses are fighting to realize and to cement in the soil of pan-Arab struggle 
action. Since the popular masses in the region recognize the extent of the agency of 
the regimes and their links with the American colonialists, they alone; that is, the 
masses, are historically fit to take charge of their struggle duties against all the 
hostile colonialist plans against the liberation struggle march. 


The American terrorist assault in the region requires a strong mass stand to foil the 
aggressive intentions of colonialism in the region.
