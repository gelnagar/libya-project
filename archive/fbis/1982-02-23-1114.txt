LIBYA

COOPERATION AGREEMENTS SIGNED WITH IRAN 


LD192100 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 19 Feb 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Feb (JANA) -- Secretary of the People's Committee of the People's Bureau 
for Foreign Liaison, ‘Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi and the Iranian foreign minister, ‘Ali Akbar 
Velayati, signedan agreement on bilateral cooperation in various fields between the 
jamahiriyah and Iran. Two agreements were signed, one for cultural cooperation and the 
other for economic cooperation between the two countries as well as an agreement to form 

a Libyan Arab-Iranian joint committee for bolstering and developing bilateral cooperation 
between the jamahiriyah and Iran. 


Iranian Delegation Departs 
LD202244 Tripoli JANA in English 1443 GMT 20 Feb 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 Feb (JANA) -- The Iranian Foreign Minister 'Ali Akbar Velayati and his 
accompanying delegation left Tripoli this morning. Before his departure, Velayati made a 
statement to our correspondent at Tripoli Airport in which he said his visit was positive 
and that it will open new horizons for the development of relations between both countries. 
He said the visit affirms Iran's support for the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front. 


He said the Iranian Islamic revolution highly values the unflinching stands of the 
Steadfastness Front in confronting all imperialist and Zionist conspiracies. 


He said the Iranian Islamic revolution is well aware of the conspiracies perpetrated by 
the U.S. and has consquently broken off relations with American, Israel and the racist 
South African regime. It has, he added, stopped all oil supplies to these countries 
and handed over the Israeli Embassy in Tehran to the Palestine Liberation Organisation 
for use as its headquarters. 


He said the Palestinian issue forms the pivot of Iran's foreign policy and views all those 
betraying it as enemies, and as agents of America, Zionism and reaction.

ANTI-IMPERIALIST GROUP DISCUSSES FOLLOWUP WORK 


LD230948 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0215 GMT 23 Feb 82 


[Text] The preparatory committee of the International Forum To Fight Imperialism, 
Zionism, Racism and Reaction met at 1800 Monday to discuss the final agenda item: the 
study of plans for a general conference of the international forum. The meeting 

ended with the formation of a 15-member committee to fix the meeting times of the 
preparatory committee's action committee which will follow up work, set the times 

for meetings of the committee preparing for the international forum's general conference, 
and fix the dates for the general conference. 


The preparatory committee of the International Forum To Fight Imperialism, Zionism, 
Racism and Reaction will hold its final meeting Tuesday morning to announce its resolutions 


and recommendations. 


JANA BLAMES U.S. FOR OIL GLUT, URGES SUMMIT 


LD221044 Tripoli JANA in English 0910 GMT 22 Feb 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 22 Feb (JANA) -- U.S. imperialist policies and methods of an aggressive 
nature have led to a deterioration of the situation on the international oil market, 
resulting in a world oil glut. 


The glut is a direct result of former U.S. President Jimmy Carter's proposal to Congress 
in January 1980 to use force if necessary as a means of securing American interests. 


It is furthermore the result of Reagan's insistance on continuing this role by enhancing 

it through the establishment of the Rapid Deployment Force. This policy has guaranteed the 
U.S. Administration cheap oil supplies and caused harm to the interests of producer 
countries. This dangerous policy's strategy depended on several factors, one such factor 
was the encouragement the United States gave to some oil countries to increase production 
by astronomical rates. Saudi Arabia's production has risen from 4 to 12 million barrels 

a day. This increase was planned by the United States in advance in a bid to confront 
other producing countries and paralyse their economic activities. 


By doing so, the United States confused countries fighting for their freedom and investing 
their oil revenues in a bid to raise the living standard of their peoples. 


The U.S. administration has clearly benefitted from the prevailing glut on the world 
market. The glut has enabled it to stockpile 250 million barrels in 1980, and it is now 
seriously working for raising its strategic stockpile to one billion barrels by 1985. 


The United States has also been able to carry out its premeditated designs by lessening 
the strategic importance of oil. JANA news agency political editor said the U.S. 
administration is striving for its continued and guaranteed access to the Arabian Gulf 
oil fields so that oil is not used as a strategic weapon against either Israel or itself. 


He said the dangerous aspects of the continued world oil glut is the serious damage it 
has caused to peoples of producer countries in Africa, Central America and the Arabian 
Gulf. 


He said that if a summit is not convened to deal with this dangerous situation, a more 
dangerous period of confrontation shall begin. The holding of such a summit meeting 
seems more urgent following the failure of earlier conferences held at ministerial level 
in realising desired goals, i.e. ending the oil glut which has been artificially created 
by increased Saudi oil production.

African, Arabian Gulf and North African peoples have started to sense the serious threat 
that has been inflicted on them as a result of the stampede to buy cheap Saudi oil. 
These peoples feel that the oil glut has paralysed their activities and threatened them 
with starvation as a result of their own reduced oil production. 


These peoples have been left no alternative but to employ the same means used by the 
American administration of confrontation whatever the price. Hunger knows no barrier 
and the hungry shall always be prepared for confrontation until death. 


The political editor said that Saudi Arabia carries some of the responsibility for this. 

ts duty necessitates awareness that flooding the international market with crude oil 
has caused the starvation of peoples in other producing countries. Saudi Arabia is 
therefore called upon to take necessary measures through OPEC and OAPEC to avert the 
possibility of confrontation between these peoples and their enemies. 


This call, translated into reality, means that Saudi Arabia should reduce its maximum 
ceiling for oil production to 4 million barrels per day. 


EVIDENCE OF U.S. ‘PRESSURE’ ON AFRICA OBTAINED 


LD191518 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1440 GMT 19 Feb 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Feb (JANA) -- The U.S. administration is bringing pressure to bear 
on the African countries to persuade them not to attend the forthcoming OAU summit 
conference scheduled to be held in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in 
order to attain its imperialist interests in Africa. The U.S. administration has 
threatened some African countries that they will be exposed to economic sanctions from 
the United States if they fail to submit to the pressure and attend the conference. 


While reporting these facts, obtained from responsible African sources, JANA might find 
it necessary to publish letters addressed to some African countries in which the U.S. 
administration asks them not to attend the said conference. 


JANA also will publish recordings of meetings that have been held by U.S. ambassadors in 
some of the African countries and some American officials who visited these countries 
recently in which they ask the ambassadors to exert pressure on these governments to 
force them not to attend the African summit conference.
