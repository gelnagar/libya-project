LIBYA

BENGHAZI DEMONSTRATORS DENOUNCE U.S. 'PLOTS' 
LD231510 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 23 Dec 81 


[Text] The Benghazi masses today came out in a big demonstration of anger and defiance to 
denounce the plots being hatched by U.S. imperialism against the Libyan Arab people and 
their historic leadership. The demonstrators affirmed their absolute adherence to the 
principles and objectives of the Great 1 September Revolution and their readiness to fight 
until death in defense of the great cultural changes that have been brought about by the 
Great 1 September Revolution and its commander. 


The demonstrators also declared that the Libyan Arab people will not be intimidated by 
the U.S. fleets nor by its terroristic plots and provocations. They will rise with utmost 
courage to check the U.S. administration's arrogance and terrorism. 


The thousands of demonstrators who filled the streets of Benghazi carried placards 
affirming the Libyan Arab people's ability and readiness to die for the sake of their 
revolution and its commander and its great achievements.

They chanted revolutionary slogans which included "God is great," "The storm will uproot 
reaction," "All my country's youth are ready to sacrifice themselves for the principles 
of the 1 September Revolution." The slogans also included: "Oh America, the 1 September 
Revolution is a colossal force" and "Cowboy Reagan, we will die rather than let you get 
to Mu'ammar." 


As the demonstrators arrived at the Square of the Popular Congress of the Benghazi 
municipality, they set fire to an effigy of Reagan, the head of the U.S. administration, 
as an expression of their anger and wrath at the policy of terror and aggression being 
pursued against the freedom of the peoples and the obstruction and frustration of their 
ambitions and hopes. 


The angry masses participating in this big demonstration issued a statement which read 
as follows: 


The masses of the Great 1 September Revolution and their living revolutionary forces, 
which are confident of the inevitable victory of the people, today declares to all the 
freemen and honorable men of theworld that the United States, the leader of world terror- 
ism, wants our people to die. It also wants our nation to be annihilated. It wants to 
destroy our beliefs and Islamic and pan-Arab values. 


The United States has come to defy and challenge our small people because they call for 
the freedom of man and for equality among peoples and because they say no to the usurpa- 
tion of land of others and their occupation by force. The United States wants to starve 
our people because we consider it an honor to be martyrs in fighting the aggressors and 
usurpers. The United States is the enemy of mankind. It wants to place our people in 
subservience and to humiliate and subdue them, so that it can pass its capitulationist 
solutions against the Arabs and so that it can carry out its terroristic strategy against 
the Arab homeland. 


The United States is the aggressor and usurper. It is behind all attempts to humiliate 
and insult our Arab people. It does not want us to live on the face of the earth. It 
does not want us to build up our strength and to take a neutral stand and to refrain from 
giving support to the causes of liberation and from siding with the small and oppressed 
peoples. It is trying to intimidate us. It stands against us. It mobilizes all its 
capabilities in order to make us submit to its wishes. 


This is the U.S. administration, the administration of terror and arrogance. It is the 
murderer of peoples and the one that committed inhuman crimes at Hiroshima, in Vietnam, 
Latin America, Palestine, Iran, Egypt, Syria, Lebanon and all parts of the world. 


Our people today take a stand of defiance and pride against the Reagan administration 

and its agent symbols. They are capable of resisting all the pressures. Thanks to their 
living revolutionary forces, our people are prepared to die and be martyred in order that 
the Jamahiriyah can remain proud and dignified, so that our people can remain proud and 
great and so that our nation will not submit to the defeatist and capitulationist 
solutions. 


The statement affirms the following: 


1. Our people, who resisted the invading fascist Italians over 30 years ago, without any 
oil resources or capabilities, declare their complete readiness to abort any attempt aimed 
at detracting from their dignity and pride. They will counter challenge with challenge 
and will refuse everything except dying while standing in the battlefield of honor and 
martyrdom.

2. As they declare their defiance and their readiness to fight and be martyred, our 
people realize the dimensions of the plot, starting with the defunct As-Sadat aggression, 
through the aggression of the U.S. Sixth Fleet, against the Gulf of Sidra to the attempts 
at economic blockade and the recall of U.S. citizens. 


3. Our people are fully aware of the plots and attempts being perpetrated by the hidden 
fingers of the Reagan administration to use its symbols and agents to accomplish its 
aggressive objectives against the people of the Jamahiriyah who will cut off all criminal 
hands. 

4. By their faith, our people will crush the plots around them, will impose their 
existence on their soil whatever the challenges, and will affirm their readiness to 
crush all attempts opposed to the authority of the people. 


5. Our proud people declare to the whole world that they will continue to adhere to 
their principled strategic stands. 


6. Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi constitutes the conscience of the Arab nation. He is the 
expression of its will and the symbol of the revolution. He is the face of the revolu- 
tion that never dies. 


The statement affirmed that the forces of the revolution, as it comes out in this popular 
demonstration, assert their revolutionary adherence to all the principles and immortal 
ideas and declare that our struggling people will remain the deterrent force in the arena 
of confrontation between the Arab nation, on the one hand, and the imperialist forces and 
their feeble symbols. 


The masses participating in the demonstration of wrath and defiance in the Benghazi 
municipal area, cabled the brother commander saying: The masses of the Benghazi municipal 
area, who have come out today in a demonstration of anger and defiance, having realized 

the dimensions of the filthy plot being hatched by U.S. imperialism and its agents, declare 
to you that they will remain, as always, faithful to the Great September Revolution and its 
commander and principles. They declare that they accept the challenges and will not be 
intimidated by the threats and plots of America. They are fully ready to fight and defeat 
the enemies. They also declare their adherence to the authority of the people and their 
determination to continue to defend it until death and to march forward with the commander 
in order to raise high the banners of freedom and bolster the pan-Arab struggle and build 
the socialist society. 


JANA INTERVIEWS UN OFFICIAL ON U.S. POLICY 


LD231916 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1640 GMT 23 Dec 81 


[Excerpts] Washington, 23 Dec (JANA) -- Current UN General Assembly Chairman 'Ismat 
Kittani has expressed astonishment at the hysterical campaign which the Reagan adminis- 
tration has been launching against the Jamahiriyah and the lies and rumors which the American 
information media have been fabricating against the people of the Jamahiriyah. 


In an interview with the JANA correspondent at the United Nations, Kitanni said that he 
does not know the purpose of this unjust hysterical campaign against the Jamahiriyah. 
Indeed, he said, U.S. public opinion itself does not understand the purpose for which the 
administration has been waging this campaign.

Kittani also spoke of the role the U.S. administration has been playing in obstructing 
the implementation of the UN resolutions against the Zionist enemy and its role in pre- 
venting the international community from adopting deterrent measures against the enemy. 
In this regard he spoke of the U.S. delegate's stand against the imposition of deterrent 
sanctions against the Zionists after their attack on the Iraqi nuclear reactors, thus 
forcing the UN Security Council to content itself with condemnation of the attack only. 
The American administration's exploitation of the veto to obstruct UN resolutions is 
frustrating. The United States, he added, will continue to use the veto and threaten 

to use it to prevent the adoption of any resolution against the Zionist enemy until the 
policy of veto is changed. 


LIBYAN-SRV TALKS BEGIN; OPENING STATEMENTS MADE 


LD241016 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 24 Dec 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Dec (JANA) -- The Libyan-Vietnamese meetings began yesterday evening 
at the Foreign Liaison Office in Tripoli and were attended by the secretary of the 
People's Committee for Public Services, Vietnamese Vice Chairman Pham Hung and a number 
of experts and technicians. 


At the beginning of the meeting, the secretary of the People's Committee for Public 
Services welcomed the guests. He paid tribute to the role of the friendly Vietnamese 
people in the struggle against U.S. imperialism and their decisive victory over colonial- 
ism. He also paid tribute to Vietnam's firm stands in support of the Arab nation and its 
just causes. 


The Vietnamese vice chairman denounced the terroristic campaign being waged by the 
Reagan administration against the Jamahiriyah aimed at harming this people's gains and 
achievements which were realized through the Great Fatih Revolution. He paid tribute 
to cooperation between the two countries which is making positive steps forward. He 
said the Vietnamese people admire and appreciate all aid given by the friendly Libyan 


people. He also expressed admiration for the progress being made in the Jamahiriyah 
in various fields. 


It was decided that this meeting should mark the beginning of the first session of the 
Libyan-Vietnamese mixed committee. Technical committees were set up to study and discuss 
the strengthening of cooperation in economic, technical and scientific fields in the 
common interest.
