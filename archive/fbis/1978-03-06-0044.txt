OFFICIAL SPOKESMAN DENOUNCES U.S, ARMS POLICY 
LDO41330Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 4 Mar 78 LD 


[Text] An official Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah spokesman, commenting 
on the decision of the American Government to ban the sale of spares for the C-130 
military aircraft and the sale of the commercial Boeing airliners to the Jamahiriyah, 
said: The American policy as far as the Jamahiriyah is concerned has lost its self- 
respect because it has been proved to us that there is no such thing as a rational 
American policy, but an American [word indistinct] policy. Furthermore the American 
policy cannot be anything but the product of a community of capitalist exploitation, 
the community of slaves and of Jewish hatred of Semitism [as heard], Islam and Arabs. 
The American ruling group has allied itself with Judaism against Islam. For these 
reasons American policy has always been unrepresentative of the interests of the 
Americans as confirmed by the dictation of its attitude against the wishes of the 
American people, an attitude which does not express the view of the American citizen 
whom we have seen and with whom we are familiar, whether as a student, a congressman, 
an intellectual or a farmworkers. The Jamahiriyah has good relations with the various 
groups of the American people with whom we have been able to maintain contacts and a 
dialog, something which has resulted in good popular relations with us because they 
became convinced of the soundness of the attitude and policy of the Jamahiriyah and the 
justice of the causes it supports. 


We declare our regret that the policy of a big power should be so childish and that it 
proclaims its stances to the international community without these stances being based 
on proper knowledge or on dialog with the side concerned. The step taken by the 
American Government toward the Jamahiriyah and the steps it takes against the small 
nations are liable to make these nations hostile to the United States and line up with 
the enemies of the United States. This is the outcome of the foolish American policy. 
The American Government declared that its decisions were taken owing to the Jamahiriyah's 
attitude in obstructing a settlement to the Middle East problem and to what it called 
the question of international terrorism. We consider that this reasoning is completely 
false. Even if for argument!'s sake we assumed it were true, the American Government 

is not the policeman of the world. Every state is free to exercise its policy. 


The official spokesman concluded his statement by saying that the American decisions 
cannot make us cease supporting the struggle of the Palestinian people until victory. 
Indeed the Jamahiriyah will continue supporting this struggle against the Zionists and 
the enemies of Semitism and Islam. As to the reference in the American decision to 
international terrorism, we know the sort of terrorism which America means. It considers 
that anyone who stands on the side of the oppressed peoples and anyone who supports 

the questions of liberation, justice and peace is a terrorist. 


SPOKESMAN TELLS CARTER TO KEEP OUT OF AFRICA 


LD051625Y Tripoli JANA in English 1517 GMT 5 Mar 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, March 5, JANA--Following President Carter's statement about the situ- 
ation in the Horn of Africa, an official spokesman in the Jamahiriyah had this to say: 
Africa doesn't need American advice and if Carter has any wisdom he should use it to 
solve his internal problems and to face the strikes, racism and the deteriorating 


value of the dollar.

The spokesman went on to say that if advice is inevitable, then we should advise 
America not to interfere in African problems and to take its hands off Africa. 
America ought to know that the Jamahiriyah and other African states are on their 
way to resolve the conflict between the two African neighbors. The spokesman added 
that America complicates any problem in which it interferes. 


Finally the spokesman accused the United States of deepening the differences between 
the African brothers and of continually trying to stop them from solving their own 
problems. The African Continent realized the destructive role of America and wouldn't 
allow Carter or anyone else to destroy the African efforts to solve the problem in 

the Horn of Africa.
