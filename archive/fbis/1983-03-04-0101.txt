LIBYA

AIRLINER INTERCEPTED BY ITALIAN FIGHTER 


LD031507 Tripoli JANA in English 1410 GMT 3 Mar 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada 19, March 3, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- A Libyan airliner, 
flight Number 102 Tripoli-London, was intercepted by an Italian fighter over the 
Mediterranean on February 27th. The Libyan airline authority said that the Italian 
fighter F-104 was equipped with air-to-air missiles. The source added that the 
fighter was flying close to the Libyan airliner for 35 miles which a violation of the 
international aviation regulation. The Libyan captain contacted the Italian air 
traffic controller and reported the incident but the Italian authorities denied to 
have any information about the fighter. 


Q4 NORTH AFRICA 


IRAN'S PRESIDENT SENDS SUPPORT MESSAGE TO LEADER 


LDO32049 Tripoli JANA in English 1813 GMT 3 Mar 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada 19, Mar 3, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- President Ali Khamene'i 
of the Islamic Republic of Iran has sent the following cable to the revolution's leader 
affirming Iran's support for the Libyan people in bravely confronting U.S. imperialist 
plots. 


"Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great Az-Fatih Revolution, peace and God's 
mercy be upon you. 


By sending its fleet to Libyan coastline waters and threatening the integrity of your 
country’s waters and skies, American imperialism has further revealed its aggressive 
nature. This new move is a new link in the chain of provocations by the greater Satan 
against the struggling peoples and those demanding independence in the world. In every 
region where a people rise up for gaining their rights, freedom and independence, and in 
every corner where a voice is raised against oppression and injustice, the American mili- 
tary machine, with all its satanic force, mobilises its might against them. 


America's latest provocations against Jamahiriyah were executed under the pretext of 
defending their allies and deserve some contemplation. They come on the eve of the 
weakening of the foundations of the terrified regime, imposed on Iraq, and amidst the 
danger facing America's strategic interests. They are a reaction against Jamahiriyah's 
continued and uninterrupted defence of the fightout stand of the Islamic Republic 
towards the war imposed on it. 


With this satanic move, America wanted to show its muscles before the region's progres- 
sive and revolutionary countries, and to raise the tottering morale of her agents and 
hated mercenaries who are in a permanent state of fear from the anger of their peoples. 


Stemming from the noble Islamic teachings and from its belief in defending the sover- 
eignty of the Islamic world and that of the Islamic and progressive countries as one of 
its primary duties, the Islamic Republic of Iran, in view of Jamahiriyah's revolutionary 
stands opposed to the oppressers, declares its backing and support for brotherly Libya 
in confronting any aggressive provocation by the greater Satan. It calls on all Muslim 
masses in the region to be vigilant and steadfast in the face of America's conspiracies 
and provocations so as Islamic solidarity in the region gets strengthened and the 
Islamic masses’ struggle destroys the big powers’ domination over Islamic lands. 


Seyyed Ali Khamene'i, president of the Islamic Republic of Iran".
