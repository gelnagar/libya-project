Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing attachment on Libyan military deployments in northern Chad at Faya Largeau, Ounianga Kebir, Gouro, Ogoui and Aozou. It has no connection to Tripoli.
• Libyan forces in northern Chad included elements of four mechanized infantry battalions, two probable motorized infantry battalions, two tank companies and three tank platoons.
• Air defence included two SA-6 batteries (not operational), three SA-9 launchers and three ZSU-23/4 guns.
• Libyan combat aircraft in northern Chad included six Mirage F-1s, 23 Fitters (Su-22), 19 SF.260s and six Hind helicopters.
• Forces were deployed to five locations: Faya Largeau, Ounianga Kebir, Gouro, Ogoui and Aozou.
