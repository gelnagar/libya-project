LIBYA

Tripoli Reports Al-Qadhdhafi Remarks on German TV 
LD1I71203 Tripoli Television Service in Arabic 2000 GMT 
16 Jan 86 


[Text] German television's Channel One last nighi conducted an 
interview with the brother commander of the revolution concern- 
ing the recent American-Zionist threats to the people of the 
Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


In his interview, the brother commander answered a number of 
questions pertaining to the American-Zionist threats to the 
Libyan Arab people and the measures Reagan has taken to freeze 
Libyan property and bank assets in the United States 


He pointed out that all friendly and fraternal peoples have 
declared support for the Libyan Arab people against these 
threats. 


[Begin recording] [Question in English fading into superimposed 
Arabic translation] Are you sorry for the incidents in Rome and 
Vienna, despite the fact that you said you had no connection with 
them? 


{Al-Qadhdhafi} If we were to begin expressing regret, then we 
must begin by expressing regret about the Dayr Yasin massacres 
committed by Menahem Begin against the Palestinian people, 
and the massacres now taking place in South Africa against the 
innocent black citizens, and the massacres in Grenada comm:tied 
by the regular American Armed Forces, and the massacres in 
Sabra and Shatilaz committed by the terrorist and butcher Sharon 
who is still a minister in the Israeli Government. We must also 
feel sorry for the victims on the Libyan airplane which on 23 
February 1973, with 99 men, women, and children, was shot 
down by an Israeli plane over the Sinai. We should be sorry for 
the massacres in southern Lebanon. We are also sorry about the 
Rome and Vienna operations. [end recording] 


Jallud Prepared ‘To Die’ for Al-Qadhdhafi’s Ideas 
LD162112 Tripoli Television Service in Arabic 2000 GM1 
16 Jan 86 


[Text] Staff Major ‘Abd al-Salam Ahmad Jallud held a news 
conference this morning which was attended by correspondants 
from numerous international radio stations, newspapers, and 
news agencies. 


{Unidentified speaker in English] The second point [context not 
specified] that | would like to stress is that since you have been 
here, you have witnessed and acquainted yourselves with the 
situation and come to the conclusion that the confrontation [word 
indistinct] is not with Colonel al-Qadhdhafi; it is the the Libyan 
people who are all facing this struggle and facing this confronta 
tion 


{Begin Jallud recording in Arabic] Personally, I believe in all the 
ideas stated by the brother colonel, and I [am ready] to die for all 
these ideas. Reagan and the terrorist Reagan administration 
(?say that the battle is against al-Qadhdhafi) but nobody will 
believe this. Col Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi represents [the feel- 
ings] of young people in the Arab world, Africa, the world, and 
even in the United States itself, and represents a mass rising to 
combat the United States and U.S. arrogance. [end recording] 


Reportage on Basic People’s Congress Meetings 


Tarhuna Congress Urges Action 
LD161923 Tripoli Television Service in Arabic 1800 GMT 
16 Jan 86 


[Text] Here are the final resolutions of the Basic People’s Con- 
gress meeting in Tarhuna: 


1. Hastening the establishment of an armed people, and imme- 
diate training to handle weapons 


2. Setting up suicide squads to combat the United States 
everywhere 


3. Registering [for arms training] will begin tomorrow, so that 
the municipality of Tarhuna can be the first municipality to 
undertake such an action 


4. Withdrawing Arab assets from U.S. banks 


5. Urging the brother leader, Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
to immediately get in touch with the leaders of Arab states to 
unify Arab ranks [sound of slogans being chanted: One Arab 
People! One Arab Army! followed by applause] and to direct the 
Jamahiri arms against U.S. colonialism and Zionism 


6. Urging the brother colonel to begin contacts with Iraq and 
South Yemen in order to halt the fighting between brothers and 
to direct the rifle at the hearts of Zionism and colonialism. [sound 
of slogans being chanted: One People, Not Two Peoples! fol- 
lowed by applause]. 


17 Jan 86 


7. Beginning an economic boycott of the United States and 


stopping Arab oil supplies to the United States, given that 

a strong weapon in battle, and closing its embassies in all the 
Arab states 

8. Arab workers must not cooperate with U.S. ships, fleets, and 
U.S. aircraft in Arab ports, and must not supply fuel to then 
They must also refrain from extending assistance to them, and 
guiding them at sea and in the air. [sound of slogans being 
chanted: We Say to Hell With You and Your Fleet, O Reagan! 
followed by applause] 


9. Doing without the U.S. dollar; setting up an alternative Arab 
banking system and creating a unified inter-Arab fund 


10. Calling on Arab leaders to awaken from their deep sleep to 
the threat surging toward them and toward the Arab nation 
{sound of slogans being chanted: Palestine Is Arab! No to 
Capitulation Solutions! followed by applause] 


11. Effectively participating in strengthening production and 
reinforcing it by working on Fridays in any production enter- 
prises inside the municipality, and also working an extra hour 
each day in various fields and public establishments 


12. Setting up an ad hoc committee to correct trends, so that these 
trends may be formulated in accordance with the current sit- 
uation inside the Arab homeland 


13. The Arab revolutionary committees must get in touch with 
all the Arab masses at the level of the Arab world, and mobilize 
them in the service of the pan-Arab cause 


14. Removing geographical borders in the Arab homeland, and 
considering a unified Arab passport as the passport of the Arab 
homeland [sound of applause and slogans being chanted: One 
People Not Two Peoples From Marrakech to Bahiain'} 


Al-Wakhim on Suicide Squads 
LD162050 Tripoli Television Service in Arabic 1907 GMI 
16 Jan 86 


[“Text” of the resolution of the Basic People’s Congress [BPC] at 
Al-Wakhim in Tripoli read by unidentified member on the 
rostrum following the discussions — live] 


Text] The Basic People’s Congress at Al-Wakhim in Tripoli has 
discussed the U.S. threats to the people of the Jamahiriyah and 
the U.S. economic boycott declared by the insignificant comic 
actor Reagan and has decided the following 


1. The congress recommends upgrading alliances with all ant 
U.S. imperialist influence movements which are seeking liber- 
ation and emancipation and the achievement of a national 
identity beginning with the Palestinian revolution, the African 
liberation movements, the Greens movement in Europe, and 
other world movements
