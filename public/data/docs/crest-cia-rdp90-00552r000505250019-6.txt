Summary (AI): An AP item in the Washington Post (2 December 1985) reports that the ACLU asked the Senate Intelligence Committee to determine whether President Reagan had secretly suspended the ban on assassinations in a reported CIA covert campaign against Libyan leader Muammar Qaddafi. The ACLU also urged steps to prevent Qaddafi's killing.
• The ACLU asked the oversight committee to ensure 'effective steps' are taken to prevent the killing of Qaddafi.
• According to the Post report, committee leaders had expressed concern that the CIA operation could end in Qaddafi's assassination.
• 1985-11-03: Washington Post reported that Reagan had approved a campaign against Qaddafi (cited by the ACLU)
