LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SPEAKS AT 25 DEC TRIPOLI DEBATE 


LD261032 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 26 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 26 Dec (JANA) -- The brother leader of the revolution last night 
participated in a democratic debate held at the Basic People's Congress in Tripoli, 
the Great March Center, on the first article of the agenda of the Basic People's 
Congresses concerning the revolutionary mobilization progran. 


The leader of the revolution stressed the need for the masses to assume responsibility 
for the consequences of their decisions regarding agenda articles. In this respect 

the leader urged the masses of the Basic People's Congresses to make decisions according 
to their free will, for all the popular decisions will be implemented by the People's 
Committees. This must be taken into account in making any decision by the masses of 

the People's Congresses. 


He said that once the People's Congresses have entrusted the People's Committees with 
the task of carrying out their decisions on any article on the agenda, there should be 
no objection or criticism concerning the means of implementing the decisions of the 
masses of the congresses. 


The leader underlined that the People's Committees, in their implementation of the 
decisions of the Basic People's Congresses, may resort to the use of any means, 

including harsh ones. By so doing they would not be practicing dictatorship but carrying 
out popular democracy in accordance with the power of the people and revolutionary 
violence. 


He said that for the people to be able t. force their way toward their objective they 
must exercise revolutionary violence against impeding elements and open up prospects 
before the holy march of the masses proceeding toward a better future. 


The leader explained that this fundamentally covers the decisions made by the Basic 
People's Congresses concerning the realization of military and economic power, 
confrontation of the Zionist death train and colonialism, the projects of change, 
the growth of production, and other serious. questions and issues included in the 
agenda of the third session of the People's Congresses. 


V. 27 Dec 82 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


The leader stress the absolute freedom of the masses of the People's Congresses in their 
decisions. He said that the masses are free to decide what they deem fit, to introduce 

the substitutions they want, and to cancel what they want. They are, for example, free 

to replace plants or part thereof with roads, or to stop farming or a type of farming in 
a region. So one imposes his opinion on them and they are not subjected to patronage 

or instigation from anyone. 


He said: I do not object to any decision and it is not my responsibility. My task is 
to help and to explain, so that I may open the door before you, so you are free to 
make decisions or rejections. It is your right to say no or to say yes, and to trace 
the right path in order to achieve what we aspire to as a people exercising freedom. 


The leader concluded his explanatory talk and observations by stressing that despite 
the popular trust, he contended he could not make decisions alone because this would be 
against the will of the people, against the power of the people, and the power of the 
people is bound to stay firmly entrencehd. 


Further Remarks 
LD270946 Tripoli JANA in English 0856 GMT 27 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi" al-Awwal 12, Dec 27, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The revolution's 
leader yesterday made further comments and clarifications over points made in the 
current debate of the Basic People's Congresses (BPC's) third session. 


In clarifications over points made in the debate of the Bourka BPC, the leader said it 
is the right of every citizen to freely make his point without interruptions by 

anyone whatever the nature of the views expressed. He said such interruptions would be 
tantamount to the practice of dictatorship. 


On the attempts to belittle the seriousness of the threat of the U.S.-backed Zionist 
death wagon, the leader said the Zionist death wagon (?is a) great danger to the Arab 
nation's fate. He said the Zionists occupied Palestine, killed more than 30,000 
Arabs in Lebanon, occupied Sinai, the West Bank, the Golan Heights and Gaza Strip 

in 1967, and imposed capitulation on the traitor As-Sadat. 


The leader said that this threat cannot be countered by a defeatist spirit and policy, 
but by the total mobilisation of Arab forces. 


The leader also made further clarifications regarding the basic military training of 
preparatory school students. 


Finally, the leader reaffirmed the necessity of providing the opportunity for every 
citizen to freely and frankly express his view at his Basic People's Congress, thus 
practicing his natural right to authority. 


JANA ON PLANNED ISRAELI-LEBANESE TALKS 


LD261524 Tripoli JANA in English 1416 GMT 26 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi* al-Awwal 10, Dec 82, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Following the 
approval of the Lebanese cabinet last Wednesday, negotiations are imminent between the 
Lebanese authorities and the Israelis over the withdrawal of Zionist forces from 
Lebanese territories. 


A report from Beirut this morning said the timing of the first session in these 
negotiations has not yet been agreed, but is will be held in the Lebanon beach hotel 
in the coastal town of Khaldah south of Beirut's international airport. 


JANA's political editor published the following commentary on the involvement of the 
Lebanese Government in negotiations with the enemy. 


27 Dec 82 Q4 NORTH AFRICA 


Negotiations between the Lebanese authorities and the Israelis imply grave dangers 
that will inevitably force the Lebanese people to once again pay a high price over 
their existence and the erosion of their freedom and dignity. 


Involvement in such negotiations, planned by the U.S. Administration, will be of 

no use for the Lebanese people. For the Zionist occupation will not be uprooted and the 
field will remain wide open for U.S. imperialism to reap some of the results of its 
strategic alliance with the Zionist enemy. This alliance is basically dedicated to 
imposed hegemony and enable Israel to expand, pillage and usurp. 


In order to appreciate the true meaning of the dangers looming for Lebanon, it is 
essential to realise that these negotiations will lead to the imposition of Zionist 
conditions on Lebanon, something that the U.S. envoys to the region have been trying 
hard to achieve... It is because of this that the Lebanese should abort these designs 
against their freedom, existence an Arab character. 


Acquiescence to Israeli-American demands will tie Lebanon to occupation and make it 
a hostage of the U.S. Administration. Lebanon should therefore demand the unconditional 
expulsion the occupiers without the holding of negotiations with the Zionist enemy. 


The most effective remedy for removing the Zionist cancer from Lebanon neither lies 
in the political negotiations nor shuttle missions of Philip Habib and his assistant 
Draper. It does not also lie in acquiescence to marginal extortions or bargaining, 
but in confronting the occupiers and resisting them. 


The Lebanese people should thus carry arms and confront the occupying enemy through 
a people's war that will only remove the Israelis from Lebanon in coffins. 


The Libyan Arab people, who are mobilising their resources to confront the Zionist 

death wagon, are completely prepared for mobilising their human and material resources 
to enter Lebanon's popular battle and to fight the usurping Lebanon so that the Lebanese 
people may remain Arab and free.
