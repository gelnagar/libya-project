“The fall in prices on the oil market put Libya in difficulties,” 
Muammar al-Qadhdhafi said 


The country’s economy has been greatly affected. Some projects 
have already had to be interrupted. And when Prime Minister 
Jallud was in the Soviet Union in February to settle some arms 
supplies, he did so on credit for the first time 


The oil price reduction after the OPEC meeting in London 6 
weeks ago has by itself led to a loss of income of almost $2 billion 
for Libya. To this must be added a reduction in the production 
quota and inflation in Europe. This means a reduction in oil 
income which totals 20 percent at its lowest and almost 60 
percent at its highest for a 3-year period 


Question: Is OPEC still a useful organization, despite the fact 
that Saudi Arabian dominance has led to elements of deteriora- 
tion for Libya? 


Answer: OPEC is still a useful organization for the oil-producing 
countries. There should be similar organizations for countries 
which produce other important raw materials. The majority of 
these countries belong to the Third World and are exploited by 
the industrialized nations 


But al! international organizations are dominated by a few 
countries. This is true of the UN Security Council and it is also 
true of OPEC. OPEC is dominated by countries which produce 
more and have greater reserves 


Members of international organizations should be equal. Other- 
wise those of us who are dominated must abandon our member- 
ship and allow those who are dominant to dominate each other 
This could mean all small states’ withdrawing from the United 
Nations, from the association of nonaligned nations and from 
OPEC. If we were to do that, the dominant states would be 
isolated — and then they would be the ones to lose out 


Question: Have you considered withdrawing from OPEC? 


Answer: | would not hesitate to do so the day the problem of 
domination becomes clear and OPEC become useless. [answer 
ends]
