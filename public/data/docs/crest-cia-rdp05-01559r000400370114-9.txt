Summary (AI): An FBIS-transmitted correction of a 3 September 1976 Qadhdhafi speech, in which he attacks Egyptian President Sadat over the Sinai agreement and Egypt's use of Libyan-supplied arms, and recalls Nasser's advice. It is Libyan foreign policy with no specific Tripoli places or events.
• Qadhdhafi says Libya provided Egypt with tanks, guns and 30 Mirage planes, and 32 MiG planes for the Syrian front.
• Qadhdhafi says Libya has decided not to mass troops on the Libyan-Egyptian border or send soldiers to the borders.
• The speech refers to Umm Sa'd village and an air base to be renamed al-Qadhdhafi base.
• 1976-09-03: Qadhdhafi speech (corrected FBIS transmission) denouncing Sadat, the Sinai agreement and Libya's arms supplies to Egypt
