COMMUNIQUE ISSUED FOLLOWING BADRAN'S VISIT 


LD061925Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1700 GMT 6 Apr 78 LD 


[Text] Benghazi, 6 Apr, JANA--The following press communique was issued this afternoon 
on the visit of brother Mudar Badran, prime minister of the Hashemite Jordanian Kingdom, 
to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah: 


At the invitation of brother 'Abd al-!Ati al-'Ubaydi, chairman of the General People's 
Congress of the Jamahiriyah, brother Mudar Badran, prime minister of the Hashemite 
Jordanian Kingdom, heading a delegation consisting of brother Hasan Ibrahim, minister 
of state for foreign affairs, and a mumber of officials, paid an official visit to the 
jJamahiriyah on 4 April 1978 that lasted 3 days. 


During this visit brother Mudar Badran handed brother Col Mu! ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader 
of the Great 1 September Revolution, a message from his brother King Husayn. 


During this visit, the Jordanian delegation met with brother officials in the Jamahiriyah, 
among whom were brother Col Mu!ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 1 September 
Revolution; brother 'Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, chairman of the General People's Congress; 
brother Dr "Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, foreign secretary; and other senior cfficiais. 


In an atmosphere of fraternity, talks were held between th: Jordanian side, headed by 
Prime Minister brother Mudar Badran, and the Ayab Libyan side, headed by brother 'Abd 
al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, chairman of the General People's Congress, The talks covered the 
current situation in the Arab world, latest developments in the Arab region, Jordan's 
concept of the strengthening of a unified Arab nationalist action in the face of ex- 
pansionist Israeli scheme;, the utilization of Arab potential in the service of the 
common destiny, and urgent moves to face up to the future.

Te two sides condemn the continuous Zionist aggression against the Arab nation; they 
also strongly condemn the Israeli aggression against southern Lebanon, which is a link 
in the chain of the Zionist-colonialist conspiracy aimed at further expansion at the 
expense of the Arab territories and at eliminating the Palestinian resistance, 


The two sides reaffirm the need for the Palestinian veople to continue exercising their 
right to struggle for the liberation of their usurpea land, Both sides reaffirm their 
support for the PLO, the sole legitimate representative of the Arab Palestinian people, 
in its struggle for liberation, return and self-determination, including establishing 

a Palestinian state in Palestine, The two sides firmly stand up against any attempt 

to relinquish Palestinian national rights and adhere to the need to liberate all the 
Arab occupied territories, 


Both sides greet the streadfastness and struggle of the Palestinian people in the occupied 
territories, and strongly denounce all the Zionist methods of repression and terror, 

Te two sides call for the mobilization of the military, political and economic potential 
of the Arab nation for the battle of liberation and destiny. 


The Arab Libyan side expressed its appreciation for Jordan's steadfastness and for 
bearing its pan-Arab responsibilities, The Jordanian side expressed its appreciation 
for the role played by the Great 1 September Revolution in the Arab liberation movement, 


During the visit there was also a review of relations between the two fraternal 
countries, The two sides expressed satisfaction at the continuous growth of the 
fruitful relations between the two countries and agreed to the need to further expand 
the scope of this cooperatior in a way that consolidates the fraternal ties between the 
two fraternal peoples in various fields and to continue their contacts tn order to 
realize these objectives. 


During this visit two agreements were signed: One is an agreement on economic, technical 
and scientific cooperation; the second is an agreement on cultural cooperation, 


The Jordanian prime minister expressed his appreciation to the Jamahiriyah and its 
leader for the warm fraternal welcome accorded to him and to the delegation accompanying 
him, He also extended an invitation to his brother 'Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, chairman 

of the General People's Congress, and to brother Dr ‘Ali tAbd as-Salam at-Turayki, 
foreign secretary, to visit the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan, The invitation was 
accepted; a date will be fixed later, 


Written in Benghazi, 6 April 1978.

DETAILS OF LIBYAN-JORDANIAN AGREEMENTS REPORTED 

LDO62040Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1915 GMT 6 Apr 78 LD 

[Text] This evening the Jamahiriyah and Jordan signed a general agreement on cooperation 
in the economic, technical and scientific fields. For the Jamahiriyah it was signed by 
Dr "Ali tAbd as-Salam at-Turayki, foreign secretary, and for Jorden by Hasan Ibrahim, 
minister of state for foreign affairs. 

The agreement aims at the consolidation and development of cooperation between the two 
countries, especially in the fields of construction, agriculture, agrarian reform and 
manpower, and the exchange of teachers and professors inthe economic and technical fields. 
In confirmation of the national ties between the two Arak peoples an agreement on cultural 
cooperation for the exchange of scientists, professors, teachers and trainees, cultural 
and sport delegations was signed. This agreement contributes to the consolidation of 
cooperation between tne radio and television organizations and translation and publishing 
work in both countries.
