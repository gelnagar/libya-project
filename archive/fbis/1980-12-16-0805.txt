LIBYA

French Attitude 
LD151014 Tripoli JANA in English 0901 GMT 15 Dec 80 


[Text] Yaounde, 15 Dec (Jamahiriyah News Agency)--The Government of Chadian National 
Unity headed by Queddei Goukouni expressed regret at the French statement over so-called 
foreign intervention in Chad. A spokesman for the government told the French news 
agency that Chad cannot understand this French attitude, especially that the civil war 
in Chad is drawing to a close, The spokesman warned against French intervention in 
Chad's internal affairs pointing to the presence of French troops in the neighbouring 
countries.
