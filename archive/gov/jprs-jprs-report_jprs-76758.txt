Algiers EL MOUDJAHID in French 18 Sep 80 p 1 


[Text] Two years ago, on 18 September 1978, Anwar Sadat, along with Begin and 
Carter, signed the first pact of high treason that an Arab head of state ever 
set his name to in the history of our nation. For Sadat, Camp David was the 
crowning glory of his “political escapade" at occupied El-Qods (Jerusalem), on 
19 November of lest year. The "Pax Americana,"’ long seen by the Arabs as a 
serious danger, found a much-heralded beginning in Sadat's Egypt. From east to 
west, the Arab nation has reacted against the crime of the "cursed son of Mit 
Aboul Koum." The Arab nation is even ready to take the offensive. 


The Arab rejection front, which includes Algeria, Syria, Libya, Democratic Yemen 
and the PLO, has formulated a platform of action to oppose the danger of Sadat. 
The Arab masses, very sensitive to the reaction of these progressive nations, 
enthusiastically show their support and their satisfaction. Denunciations of 
Camp David are heard from Kuwait to Algiers. 


Two months after the Camp David accords were concluded, the Arab kings and heads 
of state unanimously decided at the Baghdad summit to join in retaliatory measures 
advocated several months earlier by the Arab rejection front. 


The political, diplomatic, economic and cultural boycott against Egypt today 
ensures her isolation. 


Meanwhile, Tel Aviv and Washington are seeking to exploit this breach in the Arab 
world. How? 


1) With Egypt: the establishment of diplomatic relations and exchange of 
ambassadors in Tel Aviv and Cairo. 


The reopening of borders between the two countries was followed by commercial 
exchanges and official visits to the two capitals.

Begin refuses to accept total Israeli withdrawal from the Sinai. Unfortunately 
for Sadat, the withdrawal will be gradual and spread out cver several years in 
order “not to endanger Israel's security."

PHALANGE ORGAN RIDICULES ARAB NATIONALIST MOVES IN NATION 
Beirut AL-'AMAL in Arabic 13 Sep 80 p 2 


/article:; "The Comedy of Perseverance and Steadfastness on Lebanese Soil--They 
Recommend That Libya Take Part in the Deterrent Forces" 


/Text/ Does the Libyan government have the right to send ite military forces to 
Lebanon, following the announcement of the merger union between it and Syria? 


About a month ago the Syrian government determined on the party and official levels 
to "stand fast against Israeli aggressions launched against Lebanon, against the 
south and against che Palestinian resistance." 


Syria carried this resolution out when it moved its air force to counterattack 
againet Israeli warplanes over Lebanon. One Syrian air force plane was downed, and 
those calling for steadfastness applauded because a Syrian pilot managed to break 
the terrible wall of silence which the whole Arab world has wrapped and is wrapping 
around what happened and is happening in and through Lebanon concerning Palestinian 
and Israeli operations. After this resolution it was observed that the Syrian gov- 
ernment demanded, and still is demanding, that all Arab governments and armies take 
part in deterring Israeli aggressions and defending Lebanon and the Palestinian re- 
sistance. It was also observed that the Palestinians applauded and welcomed this 
step, considering any Arab hesitation in coordinating with the Syrian movement and 
strategy to be neglect and conspiracy. 


Following the Syrian resolution and the Israeli reaction, the Libyan government pro- 
claimed its position, in Tripoli specifically, claiming that it was necessary to 
open the borders of the front-line states to Palestinian guerrilla activity on the 
one hand and have Syria participate militarily, materially and morally in defending 
Lebanon, the south and the Palestinian resistance against the Israeli aggressions. 


The Syrian army is present in Lebanon, in implementation of the resolutions of the 
Riyadh and Cairo summits to preserve security and stability, prevent fighting and 
thus defend Lebanese territory; this army is totally subject to the personal command 
of the Lebanese president and its continued presence on Lebanese territory in turn 
is subject to a request presented by the Lebanese president to the Arab League 
council through his ambassador, accompanied by causative reasons, because the Arab 
countries which are participating materially or morally in the Arab deterrent forces 
must be convinced in advance of the justifications the Lebanese president presents

The Libyan military presence in Lebanon must be in accordance with a request from 
the legitimate Lebanese authorities, and these authorities, which appreciate the 
position and gravity of adopting a decision like that, certainly will hesitate to 
adopt it, whatever the pressures. 


However, advocates and enthusiasts of the proposal found the legal formla, or what

Lebanese territory. They recommended to Libyan officials that their presence in 
Lebanon lie within the context of the Arab deterrent forces. Since the Libyan and 
Syrian states will become a single state after the unity merger, their armies will 
become a single army, their political and militery decisions will become united, 

and the presence of the Libyan army on Lebanese territory will in turn become legit- 
imate and acquire a legal covering through the reselutions of the Council of the 
League of Arab States. 


Information reaching certain Lebanese circles from Tripoli states that the Libyan

about it at the outset but requested more time to conduct an expanded study and ob-

country and in the past 2 days have been the subject of an evaluation in official 
and political circles, lest Lebanon awake to new steps which will not be in its 
interests at all.
