LIBYA

AL-'UBAYDI URGES DISCUSSION OF U.S. MANEUVERS 


LD050026 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2300 GMT 4 Nov 81 


[Text] The secretary of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison, Brother ‘Abd al-'Ati 
al-'Ubaydi, met with brother ambassadors of the Steadfastness and Confrontation [Front] 
countries in Tripoli yesterday evening. The brother secretary, during his meeting with 

the brother ambassadors, explained that the imperialist defiances confronting the Arab 
nation require a meeting of the political committee of the Steadfastness and Confrontation 
Front, which would include foreign ministers, to discuss the aggressive U.S. maneuvers 

aimed at the Arab nation. He said that these maneuvers, to be carried out on Arab territory, 
over its skies and around its territorial waters, are not considered in the Jamahiriyah as 

a passing event, but in reality as a U.S. invasion of the Arab homeland and an attempt to 
occupy it -- reinstituting it into an area of U.S. influence, domination and imperialism. 


He added that the flights of the strategic B-52 aircraft in the Arab skies and their 
violation of our airspace and the entry of the U.S. Rapid Deployment Forces into Arab 
territories required from us, as a national front for steadfastness and confrontation, 
a serious stand; it also requires us to meet to discuss this dangerous situation and to 
take a firm stand regarding it. 


COMMENT ON U.S. PLAN TO ASSASSINATE AL-QADHDHAFI 


LD041640 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 4 Nov 81 


[Excerpts] Confirmed information published by a number of news agencies and by Arab and 
foreign radio and press...disclosed that the Pentagon in cooperation with the U.S. Central 
Intelligence Agency is striving to implement a plan aimed at the leader of the revolution 
during the period 10-15 [Nov] -~ which coincides with the time fixed for the provocative 
military maneuvers by the American forces in western Egypt. 


The editor of the political programs section in the radio services has written the 
following analysis: 


The American plan to carry out an operation directed against the leader of the revolution 
is, right from the beginning, nothing new in the American stand toward the Great al-Fatih 
Revolution. America's attempt to assassinate the leader, an attempt directed against the 
revolutionary reforms of the Jamahiriyah and the gains of its masses, is not new in as much 
as it is -- in the manner recently exposed by news reports -- a development of American 
plots against the revolution and its achievements. The CIA is no longer alone in the 

task of implementing the attempt. The Pentagon, in all its agencies and military bodies, 
has become party to the implementation of this dirty colonial plan.

SOMALI POLITICAL OFFICIALS ASK FOR ASYLUM 


LD050158 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0015 GMT 5 Nov 81 


[Text] Brother: Umar Milad Ilmi, acting deputy of the general secretary of the ruling 
Somali Revolutionary Socialist Party, chairman of the party's central bureau for adminis- 
trative affairs, member of the Central Committee and member of the Somali People's Assembly; 
Abdallah Muhammad Hasan, chairman of the party's bureau for cadres affairs, member of the 
Central Committee, member of the Somali People's Assembly and a former minister of informa- 
tion and national guidance; Uthman [words indistinct] Salih, an official of the party's 
Central Committee, member of the Somali Revolutionary Youth Union Central Committee and 

the Union's former general secretary for foreign relations; Ahmad Abbas Ahmad, a lecturer 
at the Somali national university, and Sister Zuhrah Abdallah Faraj, an official of the 
party's Central Committee and member of the Somali Revolutionary Youth Union Central 
Committee, arrived in Tripoli and asked for political asylum in the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


These brothers held a press conference in Tripoli yesterday evening, in which they revealed 
the repressive dictatorial practices of the agent dictator. Siad Barre pursues a repres- 
sive policy hostile te the Somali people, allies himself to imperialism and persecutes the 
opposition national forces. 


The brothers denounced the support of the Siad Barre regime for the stable of David 
accords, its violation of the Arab League character through its open support of those 
accords and its call for the Americans to build military bases on Somali territory -- in 
Barbarah, Kismabu and Mogadishu, to protect the moribund regime. 


The brothers strongly denounced the attack by the United States on the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and on the Arab nation, Africa and the world as a whole. They 
also denounced the participation of the Siad Barre regime in the land, sea and air 
maneuvers conducted by U.S. imperialism and Arab reaction. 


In reply to a question about their motives for coming to the Jamahiriyah and asking for 
political asylum, one of the brothers said the Jamahiriyah embraces revolutionaries and 
is is the source of revolutions. He said it supports liberation movements everywhere and 
takes the position that Siad Barre's decision to sever relations with the Libyan Arab 
people did not represent the will of the Somali epople. We are here to confirm the 
{words indistinct] the relations betwen the Libyan Arab people and the Somali people and 
to explain that the permanent relations are between the peoples and not between the 
governments.
