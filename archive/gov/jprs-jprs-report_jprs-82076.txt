[text] Tunis, October 2--The Tunisian Government acknowledged this week 
that anti-semitic incidents took place in the southern Tunisian towns of 
Zarzis and Ben Gardane after three days of silence, despite widespread 
talk here of the attacks, 





The incidents reportedly occurred Monday but the government statement was 
not released until Thursday. Meanwhile, residents of Tunis deplored the 
incidents though no one was sure exactly what happened in these two towns 
near the Libyan border. 


The government communique said that "several isolated altercations which. 
caused neither victims nor property damage" took place recently in Zarzis 
and Ben Gardane. '"Minor incidents" took place between Jews and demon- 
strators, it said,
