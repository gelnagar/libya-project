LIBYA

AS-SADAT LOSES ALL RESPECT AS RESULT OF LARNACA 
LD201208Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1025 GMT 20 Feb 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 February--The JANA political editor has commented on the recent 
Egyptian violation of the sanctity and sovereignty of Cyprus. He writes: Not a single 
day passes without As-Sadat providing new proof that he is a madman who has lost all 
his senses, Daily he creates acrobatic dramas that confirm his madness, aimed at 
diverting attention from his failure both internally and externally. 


The political editor affirms that the aggression against the sovereignty and sanctity 
of Cyprus, that small country with friendly people who have supported and continue to 
support the Arab stands at all international conferences, is an unparalleled crime. 

In fact, this incident would have taken place in another airport or another country had 
the Cypriot plane landed there. This is because President as-Sadat now sees in the 
Israelis his sublime ideal and he emulates them after having capitulated completely to 
them. It was no accident that Israeli television was the first to carry reports of 

the mad adventure of the As-Sadat government before all the world information media. 
This can or“y mean that the operation was carried out in complete coordination between 
the Israelis and the government of As-Sadat. 


One cannot think how As-Sadat will explain and justify this scandal which is added to 
his continuous flood of scandals. What will he say to the Egyptian people to justify 
the killing of their soldiers and sons whom he pledged not to send abroad until the 
liberation of Sinai but whom he sent as aggressors against the Arab and African 
brothers, and whom he now sent to Cyrpus to die in an act of aggression which is a sign 
of madness, irresponsibility and the loss by As-Sadat and his government of all respect 


and prestige in the Egyptian, Arab and international fields?

V. 21 Feb 78 NORTH AFRICA 


ASH-SHAHATI: AS-SADAT WILL BRING WAR TO HORN 
LD191536Y Tripoli JANA in English 1427 GMT 19 Feb 78 LD 


[Text] Paris, February 19, JANA--Head of the Foreign Liaison Bureau Ahmad ash-Shahati 
said here yesterday that the policies of Egyptian President as-Sadat will lead to war 
on the African Continent. He told a press conference, attended by Arab and French 
journalists, As-Sadat's intervention in the Horn of Africa was adding fuel to fire. 

He strongly denounced this intervention. Ash-Shahati stressed that, as result of 
As-Sadat'!s failure to achieve peace in the Middle East, he is asking for American arms 
to further subdue the African Continent.
