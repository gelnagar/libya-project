Summary (AI): A 1982 National Photographic Interpretation Center briefing note reports that Libya received another six probable HAZE A antisubmarine/search-rescue helicopters, shipped in containers via the transshipment mole at Misratah port. The containers were probably bound for Ghurdabiyah airfield, not Tripoli.
• Libya received an additional delivery of six probable HAZE A antisubmarine warfare/search-rescue helicopters.
• Outside the Warsaw Pact, only Syria and Libya operated HAZE A helicopters.
• Thirteen HAZE A helicopters were observed at Ghurdabiyah airfield.
• Coordinates given: 32-22-27N 015-12-51E (Misratah port area).
• 1982-12: Six probable HAZE A helicopter fuselage containers observed on the transshipment mole, likely delivered by a Soviet Ivan Skuridin-class roll-on/roll-off ship.
