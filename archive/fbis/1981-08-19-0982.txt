FOREIGN LIASON BUREAU STATEMENT ON U.S. MANEUVERS 


LD182353 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 18 Aug 81 


[Statement issued 18 August by the People's Committee of the People's Foreign Liaison 
Bureau } 


[Text] As part of the campaign of terror and U.S. provocations, the U.S. 6th Fleet in 

the Mediterranean yesterday, 17 August 1981, began military maneuvers off the shores of 
Sidra which will continue until 23 August. According to the confirmed information it 
appears that a part of the area of maneuvers fallsinside the territorial waters of the 
socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, in the Gulf of Sidra, by about 38 km. Also, 
part of the area of maneuvers which falls inside the Gulf of Sidra also falls within the 
area of [words indistinct] of the Libyan Arab Air Forces. The last part of the maneuvers, 
which will take place in international waters, falls within a dangerous area, HLB-6 

[as heard] of the Libyan Arab Air Force. 


Holding these U.S. maneuvers in the mentioned area contradicts the Libyan declaration of 
9 October 1973, which said that the Gulf of Sidra falls inLibyan territory and that its 
eastern, southern and western land borders and its northern limit, latitude 32 degrees 
20 minutes north, is considered an intergral part of the Libyan region and comes under 
its full sovereignty, being domestic waters, after which begins its regional sea, which 
comes under complete national sovereignty and jurisdiction. This declaration was, at 
the time, notified to all states and international organizations. 


This U.S. action is considered a violation of national sovereignty and uncalled for 
interference and provocation against the Jamahiriyah. It exposes international peace 

and security to danger and it contradicts the principles and aims of the United Nations. 
The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah reserves the right of taking all measures 
which it sees necessary to safeguard its legitimate rights in its airspace and territorial 
waters. 


COMMENTARIES ATTACK U.S. 6TH FLEET MANEUVERS 


JANA Commentary 


LD181946 Tripoli JANA in English 1848 GMT 18 Aug 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Aug (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--NEWSWEEK magazine of yesterday pointed 

out that in response to instructions from President Reagan the defence secretary, Caspar 
Weinberger, had ordered the U.S. Sixth Fleet to carry out manoeuvres during this week inside 
the Libyan Arab coastal waters, in Sidra Gulf, to coincide with large scale land maneuvers 
organized by the Egyptian regime along the Libyan borders.

The magazine, which has close links with the State Department and the White House, said 
marine units and planes would take part in the manoeuvres. The American Navy has 
warned ships and planes passing through the region that the firing range would include 
an area of 3,200 square miles. 


After months of debating how to assassinate Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, the magazine said, the 
American administration would offer its first direct challenge to the Libyan revolution 
through these manoeuvres. 


JANA's political editor has made the following comment on the American Government's 
decision to conduct these terrorist provocative manoeuvres inside Libyan territorial 
waters: 


"The American manoeuvres to be conducted by the American Sixth Fleet during this week in 
the Gulf of Sidra, which is an indivisible part of our sovereign territorial waters, 
expose the nature and reality of American aggressive intentions and affirms that the 
bloodthirsty terrorists are, through their subversive activities and provocative 
terrorist acts, consolidating their terrorist programmes to commit aggression on the 
Libyan people and attempting to strike at its gains and undermine the civilizational 
transformations realized on Libyan soil using agent instruments and figures with whom 

it indulges in executing the criminal terrorist schemes." 


He added: "The joint challenge thrown by the American administration and its agent, 
As-Sadat, in the region, exemplified by the manoeuvres to be conducted by the American 
fleet and the Egyptian regime's land forces, is another link in the chain of 
imperialism, the search for war and aggression against the peoples and their freedom". 

affirmed "the Libyan Arab people, faced with this provocative armed stance against 
its revolution and leader, will fight by all means for its territorial waters and any 
inch of its land or air space whoever is the aggressor party, whether it is America or 
atty other force."

LD182202 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 18 Aug 81 
{Commentary by Tripoli radio's political editor] 


[Text] The American maneuvers which are to take place opposite the Gulf of Sidra, close 
to our territorial waters, are not the first of their kind in the series of U.S. 
provocations against the Jamahiriyah. It is the same line which the U.S. consistently 
takes in its dealings with other nations. 


In view of this American action, characterized by increasing hostility, an action whose 
links have been exposed and whose scope became particularly clear since the terrorist 
plot against the leader of the revolution--in view of this action it becomes clear that 
the Libyan people's achievementments and their revolution, which produced these achieve- 
ments and which is the source of life on Earth, have greatly troubled imperialist 
American circles. It is certain that imperialist circles resort to such military moves 
from time to time in order to terrorize people and threaten their freedom, future and 
very existence. They want to threaten the path chosen by people, the way they wish to 
live and the way they want to conduct their national and international affairs. 


When the CIA plot failed to eliminate the revolutionary leadership of the Libyan 
revolution, when it became clear to the U.S. that such elimination could not be 
accomplished without a sea of blood, involving hundreds of thousands of Libyan Arabs 
who would have fought to defend their leader and their achievements--when all that 
became clear, the maneuvers opposite the Gulf of Sidra have come along as yet another 
link in the chain of imperialist plots, warm »ngering, aggression against nations and 
threats to their freedom.

If we follow the series of American maneuvers which threatened the Jamahiriya's people, 
we find, as we have already said, that this round of maneuvers is not the first of its 
kind. American and Egyptian maneuvers have taken place near the Jamahiriya's eastern 
frontier. Seventy (?M-8) aircraft, (?Cobra-l1 helicopters) and Mirages participated in

Congress approved Reagan's military budget, which amounts to $222 billion and reflects

As for the American military presence in the Mediterranean, the eastern Mediterranean 
is crowded with American warships: There are over 30 American warships there. The 
American presence in the Mediterranean is also represented by reactivating 26 American

budget for 1981 thus reached its highest figure, $222 billion. 


Finally, American maneuvers carried out near our shores from time to time show that the

American imperialist circles. Those who are today conducting the maneuvers and their

inch of their airspace, regardless of who the aggressor may be, America or any other
