LIBYAN-IRANIAN ANTI-OMAN PLAN--A Voice of Iran correspondent reports that 
following efforts by the Khomeyni regime and the Libyan Government to 
further expand relations between Tehran and Tripoli, the Libyan prime 
minister is to arrive in Tehran soon to hold talks with officials of 
Khomeyni's regime. The Voice of Iran political commentator stresses that this 
latest joint plan compiled by the regimes of al-Qadhdhafi and Khomeyni is 
to train 600 terrorists who would be sent to Oran to create disturbances 
and riots in the sultanate. Meanwhile, we have received reports from Oman 
stating that the sultanate's security officials have taken the necessary 
measures to confront the possible arrival of terrorists and saboteurs sent 
by Khomeyni and al-Qadhdhafi. [Text] [NC210956 (Clandestine) Voice of 
Iran in Persian 1805 GMT 20 Apr 82] 


DEMOCRATIC PARTY OFFICIAL SURRENDERS--IRNA reports that the defection of 

a large number of members of minigroups to the forces of Islam, two people 

from the defunct Democratic Party surrendered to the forces of Islam and turned 
in their G-3 rifles and [words indistinct]. They were granted safe conduct. 
The report adds that one of the persons who surrendered was a responsible 
official of the defunct Democratic Party. [Text] [LD212052 Tehran Domestic 
Service in Persian 1842 GMT 21 Apr 82] 





ARGENTINE ACCUSED IN COUP--An Argentine bus:nessman who is a resident of 
Paris has been implicated in Qotbzadeh's radio and television interview of 
being an agent for purchasing arms and weapons for the attempted coup and 
having contact with the CIA. He has called these allegations extraordinary, 
shameful and false. He further said that it is apparently a sign of the 
shakiness of Khomeyni's regime. [Text] [GF261325 (Clandestine) Radio Iran 
in Persian to Iran 1330 GMT 21 Apr 82]
