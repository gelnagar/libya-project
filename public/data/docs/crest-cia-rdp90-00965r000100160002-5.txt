Summary (AI): A Human Events article argues that U.S. media reports of CIA plots, including a Jack Anderson column on a poisoning scheme, may have provoked Qaddafi's alleged plans to kill President Reagan. It also notes Soviet TASS and Radio Moscow repeating the stories. Nothing specific to Tripoli sites.
• Qaddafi's alleged desire to kill President Reagan was linked by some to two Libyan aircraft shot down by American forces in August 1981.
• Anderson's column claimed CIA plotters planned to use a deadly poison squirted into Qaddafi's food or injected through a dart.
• Rep. Boland said a July 28 statement noted an Executive Order prohibits assassination by U.S. intelligence agencies.
• Anderson cited a reported top-secret CIA planning document dated May 9, 1981, titled Draft Covert Operations Planning Document: Africa-Middle East.
• 1981-08-25: Jack Anderson column alleges a CIA scheme to poison Qaddafi, which appeared in many U.S. newspapers
• 1981-12-06: Qaddafi tells ABC he believes the U.S. government is preparing to assassinate him and poison his food
