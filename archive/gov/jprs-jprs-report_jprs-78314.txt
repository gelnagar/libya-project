LIBYA

PHOPLE'S CONGRESS DECREE ON FREEDOM--Tripoli, 26 May (JAMAHIRIYAH NEW AGENCY)~--The 
General People's Congress endorsed decree No 11 of 1981 on supporting the liberation 
movements of the world, in implementation of the resolutions of the third regular 
session of the people's congresses formulated and endorsed by the General People's 
Congress in ite sixth regular session. The text reads as follows: Article l--In 
implementationof the declaration of the people's authority and the establishment of 
the Jamahiriyah, the people of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah is 
committed to support the causes of freedom in the world, materially and morally, to 
ensure the freedom and integrity of individuals and communities and end all forms 

of exploitation, domination and injustice. Article 2--The people of the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah rejects and condemns all forms of terrorism and 
commits itself to back all humane and just causes of individuals and communities 
over its soil and in any part of the world, and takes the responsibility to protect 
and defend those asking its help and enabling them to defend their humane and na- 
tional causes with every possible moral and financial means without any segregation 
among colors, races and religious beliefs. Article 3--The decree is to be published 
in the official newspaper and is effective as from the date of issue. General Peo- 
ple's Congress 13 May 1981. [Text] [LD262044 Tripoli JANA in English 1449 GMT 

26 May 81]
