LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI MESSAGES ATTACK RDF, U.S. BASES 


LDO51732 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 5 May 81 


[Excerpt] , The brother leader in his capacity as chairman of the present session of the 
National Front for Steadfastness and Confrontation has sent messages, the first to the UN 
secretary general and the other to the chairman of the UN Security Council, about the Rapid 
Deployment Forces being formed by America and other Western countries and about the spread 
of American bases in the Arab region, especially in Egypt, Sudan, Somalia and Oman. 


The leader told the UN secretary general and the Security Council chairman that the Rapid 
Deployment Force is not directed against the West but is directed against the nonaligned 
states, and it constitutes a threat to the Third World countries. He pointed out that the 
presence of American bases in Arab countries, which are thousands of miles away from America, 
is considered a violation of the UN Charter and indicates a great interference in the 

affairs of small countries. The leader asked the United Nations and the Security Council 

to quickly intervene to halt this danger, which constitutes a dangerous precedent threatening 
peace and security in the world. 


The leader sent messages with the same meaning to the heads of the nonaligned states. In 
them he points out the Egyptian Government and the Sudanese Government granting the United 
States bases and military facilities in their countries. He stated that these bases and 
facilities encourage the other countries to follow this path, an action which constitutes 
the start of the collapse of the Nonaligned Movement and which conflicts with its aims and 
principles. In his messages the leader of the revolution called for the expulsion of the 
Egyptian and Sudanese regimes from the Nonaligned Movement.
