Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery analysis report from September 1980 describes damage from an explosion at Al Kufrah Barracks in Libya, which destroyed four buildings and damaged others, along with tanks and armored vehicles. The site is in southeastern Libya, far from Tripoli, and most of the report is redacted.
• Three tanks and three BTR-60PB armored personnel carriers parked near the vehicle storage building were destroyed.
• Four tanks, 14 BTR-60PB, and 16 ZU-23 antiaircraft guns were either destroyed or damaged.
• One building was approximately 150 meters (492 feet) from the point of the explosion.
• Report IAR-A216/80, prepared by NPIC Third World Forces Division, Imagery Exploitation Group.
• 1980-09: Explosion just outside the eastern perimeter security wall between a vehicle storage building and a storage building; four buildings destroyed and at least five other large buildings damaged.
