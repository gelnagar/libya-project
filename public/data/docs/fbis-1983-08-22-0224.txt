LIBYA

JANA: Goukouni Contacts Islamic Conference 
LD191022 Tripoli JANA in English 0909 GMT 19 Aug 83 


[Text] Bardai, Dhulgidah 11, August 19, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — President Goukouni Oueddei, of the 
Legitimate Government of National Unity of Chad sent a letter 
to the secretary general of the Islamic Conference outlining the 
aggressive U.S.-French imperialist policy of intervention in 
Chad's internal affairs. This hostility by the U.S., France and 
their agents against the Chadian people which threatens the 
independence and sovereignty of Chad, for which the Chadian 
people have paid dearly, President Goukouni stated, can only be 
repulsed. 


President Goukouni affirmed further that the U.S., France their 
agents who have supplied [word indistinct] Habre’s clique in 
Ndjamena with all possible arms and equipment are entirely 
responsible for internationalising the conflict in Chad. He added: 
“This stand does not endanger Chad alone but it also threatens 
the Jamahiriyah’s security and the peace of the region.” 


In conclusion the Chadian leader said “Our people expect peace 
and security and we have no wish to wage war for war’s sake. 
This is why we have welcomed the OAU'’s call for peaceful 
settlement. We are ready to discuss the ways and means to 
achieve permanent peace.” 


Tripoli: French Order Units To Capture Abeche 
LD211407 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
21 Aug 83 


[Text] Four French Jaguar aircraft arrived this morning at 
Ndjamena Airport from the Central African Republic. These 
four aircraft are accompanied by a KC-135 air refuelling air- 
craft. 


The French military command in Ndjamena has ordered units 
from its Foreign Legion to capture the town of Abeche. These 
units are being ferried by air to Chad from their post in the 
Central African Republic.
