Summary (AI): A 1981 Los Angeles Times article (OCR-garbled) reports a federal investigation into about 20 tons of C4 explosives shipped from the US to Libya, allegedly to equip a terrorist training center in Tripoli set up in 1978 and staffed by former US Green Berets and bomb experts. The Spotlight and Washington Post pieces in the same file cover CIA internal controversy and CIA director Casey.
• About 7,500 pounds of C4 plastique were loaded in Louisiana under state trooper escort toward the Texas border.
• The explosives were amassed at a cost of about $300,000 from private stocks of military surplus explosive in the US and Canada.
• A federal grand jury was to hear testimony the following week on the scheme.
• The article says the operation involved former CIA agents and other Americans working for the Libyan regime.
• 1978: Terrorist center and training school set up in Tripoli, staffed by American ex-Green Berets and retired U.S. government bomb experts.
• 1980: A bomb expert told a Washington grand jury he made a bomb to be hidden in a box of tea.
• 1981-08: About 20 tons of C4 airlifted to Libya from Houston aboard a chartered DC-8, about two months after the Pomona dealer's accumulation.
