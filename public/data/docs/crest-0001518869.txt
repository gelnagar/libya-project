Summary (AI): A heavily redacted 1989 CIA cable about a Libyan External Security Organization (ESO) officer based in Malta who is being recalled to Tripoli. He wants a faked surgery to avoid conscription and remain in Malta, and offers to keep passing ESO information to the station.
• The source wanted sham surgery to document a physical disability so he could resign and avoid being drafted into the Libyan armed forces.
• A Maltese physician had already given the source documents attesting to back problems.
• The source said Libyans of his age were being drafted at a steady pace.
• The station proposed sending a case officer to Malta the week of 17 April to try to convince the source to return to Tripoli as a penetration of the ESO.
• 1989-04-11: Evening meeting with the source, triggered by his news that he had received a telex ordering him to return to Tripoli.
• 1989-04-15: Next scheduled meeting with the source, at which he expected a response on the request for sham surgery.
• 1989-05-01: Source's scheduled recall date to Tripoli.
