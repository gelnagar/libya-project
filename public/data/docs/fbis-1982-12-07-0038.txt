LIBYA

AL-FAYSAL BANK OPERATIONS IN SUDAN EXPOSED 


LD061058 Tripoli JANA in English 0818 GMT 6 Dec 82 


[Text] Khartoum, Safar 20, Dec 6, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The merchants of politics in the 
ruling Saudi family have exploited the Sudanese people's difficulties by carrying out a 
massive looting operation, approved by hireling Numayri, under the guise of establishing 
the "Islamic Development Company.’ 


The company, an investment concern, is headed by Prince Muhammad al-Faysal who also 
heads the board of directors of the ‘Al-Faysal Bank" in Khartoum. This bank is known to 
have channeled money to Numayri, his brother, Mustapha and other members of his clique, 
to establish joint companies whose profits are reaped by the ruling Saudi family. 


The ‘Al-Faysal Bank’ in Khartoum is also known for its funding of sabotage operations in 
the region and is directly involved in Sudanese property speculation. 


The official Sudanese News Agency said Muhammad al-Faysal will head the first meeting of 
the Islamic Development Company in London on December 14th. The company, with a proposed 
capital of one billion dollars, will concentrate its activities on agriculture, services, 
public utilities, trade and import-export. These activities were earlier recommended in 
a plan prepared by American experts who visited the Sudan lately. 


Al-Faysal also met in Khartoum with hireling Numayri and the U.S. ambassador there.
