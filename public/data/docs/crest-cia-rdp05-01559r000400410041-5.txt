Rome Daily Carries Al-Qadhdhafi Interview on Raid 
PM301128 Rome LA REPUBBLICA in Italian 
27-28 Apr 86 p 3 

{Unattributed report on interview with Libyan Chief of State 
Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi by Saed Naqvi of the New Delhi 
WORLD REPORT at Bab al-‘Aziziyah; date not given] 

[Text] [Saqvi} Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, the entire world would like 
to know exactly what happened on the night of 15 April when the. . 
Americans bombed Tripoli and your home. 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] I was asleep at home with my family. I could not 
have imagined that in this era there were barbarians planning to 
attack my family in their sleep. There was no reason of any kind 
— there was no direct war between the United States and Libya, 
no reason for the United States to do what it did, no reason for a 
major power to use its might against a small country.

[Naqvi] Let us talk about the political aspects later. First tell me 
how you escaped. Did the bombs hit the room you were in or were 
you far away? Did you see them explode? 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] The house collapsed into a pile of masonry. I 
rushed to save the children; it was difficult to make headway 
amid the bombs and the flames. There were many difficulties 
that night also because my wife was confined to bed witha slipped 
disk. The attack was perpetrated by 33 aircraft and centered on 
my home and tent. You can take a look for yourself. [Al- 
Qadhdhafi ends] This is the start of the interview with Al- 
Qadhdhafi granted to the Indian journalist Saed Naqvi, who met 
with the colonel at Bab Al-‘Aziziyah. It is the first exclusive 
interview granted by the Libyan leader since the crisis. We 
reprint it in full. Naqvi writes: “Libya may be forced to join 
the socialist bloc as a result of the American initiatives. Al- 
Qadhdhafi told me: ‘The Americans forced Cuba to become a 
communist country. Reagan could force Libya in the same 
direction.’ He said that if Libya’s neutrality is not observed, ‘then 
we will choose the socialist camp against the imperialist 
camp.’Al-Qadhdhafi also told me that the Americans know that 
we [Libyans] have nothing to do with terrorism but that they 
conduct propaganda against Libya because Libya supports the 
Palestinian liberation movement. Al-Qadhdhafi also condemned 
the bomb explosion at the British Airways office in London. 
Naqvi continues: “I was taken to the hotel at Bab Al- 
‘Aziziyah, the large complex enclosed by high walls where Al- 
Qadhdhafi usually lives and where his offices are. It was 
Al-Qadhdhafi’s house in this complex that was hardest hit by the 
American bombs that killed Hana, his 16 month old adopted 
daughter, and wounded the other children. At the gates there 
were two tanks sunk into the ground. There were soldiers every- 
where. I was taken to a large room furnished with leather sofas 
and two modern candelabra. The foreign minister led me to a 
Staircase leading to another room well below ground level. In the 
middle there stood a table tennis table, and a dozen soldiers 
armed with submachine guns were watching television and smok- 
ing. 

“After passing through several more rooms I reached an austere 
low-ceilinged rectangular room with white walls and a table in 
the middle — apparently a makeshift headquarters. Al- 
Qadhdhafi wore a dark gray pilot’s uniform, with an embroidered 
black cape over his shoulders. He was standing behind the table 
and greeted me with a wave. If he was tense he did not show it: 
He was pensive, but smiled at times.” 

[Naqvi] How do you intend to respond to the U. S. act of 
aggression. 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] By continuing mankind’s struggle, the struggle 
against imperialism and for every peoples’ right to be free and to 
a place in the sun. We must make every effort to maintain world 
peace. 

[Naqvi] As a statesman and politician, how do you explain 
Reagan’s actions? Why did he do it? For domestic reasons? To 
alter the political situation in the Middle East? 

{Al-Qadhdhafi] There are profound psychological reasons.

[Naqvi] Namely? 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] The crusading spirit against Islam, an anti- 
Semitic spirit against the Arabs, because the Arabs are Semites. 
The Arabs are the real Semites. Moreover, he hates revolution, 
he hates socialism, he hates liberation struggles. He is a capitalist 
and a fanatic. He is a failed actor who has become president of 
a major power and wants to show that he can set big war 
machines in motion. He is suffering from old age and cancer. He 
wants the world to end at the same time as his own life is coming 
to an end. Why should it endure after him? I have studied 
psychology, so my analysis is well-founded. Reagan has a special 
relationship with Mrs Thatcher: He wants to show her his real 
worth. 

(Naqvi] His worth as a man? 

{Al-Qadhdhafi] Yes, exactly. These are the psychological reasons 
for what he did. Then there are the political reasons. And here 
his advisers bear the greater share of responsibility: They 
believe that it is important and necessary to fill the vacuum in 
the Mediterranean before there are any changes. 

[Naqvi] What vacuum? 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] His advisers believe that the Soviet Union does 
not yet have a strong presence in the Mediterranean, which is 
why they want to gain control of the entire area before that 
happens. He wants to fill the area before the first Russian aircraft 
carrier reaches the Mediterranean. 

[Naqvi] Do you mean that the first Russian aircraft carrier is 
about to enter the Mediterranean? 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] Reagan wants to fill the area before that happens 
so that the Soviets have no chance of entering the Mediterranean 
on a large scale. The Russians are constructing two further 
aircraft carriers. The Americans are afraid of changes in the 
region that would hinder their presence. 

[Naqvi] What developments, what changes? 

(Al-Qadhdhafi] The attainment of Arab unity, revolutions in the 
Mediterranean area, the victory of the ‘Greens’ in West Ger- 
many and Europe. NATO’s attempt to disrupt the balances 
achieved after World War II is a very dangerous initiative. The 
experiment of disrupting the balances is starting with Libya. 
They believe they have found the right time to alter the balance 
of power. 

[Naqvi] How? 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] Through their attack, their testing of new sophis- 
ticated weapons, the intervention in the area: In the final 
analysis it is all directed against the Soviet Union. Libya was an 
obstacle. Libya has repelled the attack: This is why it deserves 
to be called the Great Jamahiriyah. 

[Naqvi] Hijacked aircraft and innocent people killed at air- 
ports: This is what we call terrorism. Why is your name 
associated with such incidents?

liberation movement. They find an excuse for hitting the liber- 
ation movements. So when there are incidents such as an aircraft 
hijacking, they exploit them and carry out large-scale propa- 
ganda to shift attention away from the Palestinians’ struggle. 

[Naqvi] But do you deplore acts of terrorism such as the Lae 
attack on the British Airways office? ; 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] We are clearly and definitely opposed to terror- 
ism. Hijacking and killing innocent people... we do not accept 
such methods. The Americans know that we are not the guilty 
parties but they want to put an end to the struggle of all peoples 
for their liberation. But they cannot. Empires always come to an 
end and the peoples triumph. Violence existed in this world before 
I carried out the revolution in Libya. Terrorism existed and was 
carried out by the Americans and Israelis before I was born — 
in 1936, 1940, and 1948. Those were the years of Israeli terrorism 
against the Arabs. 

There are problems in the world that must be resolved before 
violence will end — the Palestinian issue, the problem in Ireland, 
the world’s minorities, South Africa, Namibia, the problem of 
freedom in Latin America, racism, exploitation, colonialism: 
These are the causes of violence. New Caledonia is a cause of 
violence, so is Martinique. Violence cannot be eliminated by 
means of a raid on Libya or on my tent. And this raid on my tent 
will not end the freedom struggle. 

[Naqvi] If Reagan does not heed the nonaligned countries and 

the United Nations and has the military strength to do what he 
wants, what will happen? 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] He will create a worldwide imbalance between 
the NATO and Warsaw Pact countries, Then he will find himself 
having to deal with the Warsaw Pact countries. His action could 
lead to World War II]. But his actions are also hastening the 
advent of the Arab people’s unity. 

[Naqvi] Will he drive you into the communist camp? 

(Al-Qadhdhafi] Whoever comes after me could be driven into the 
communist camp. So could I. 

[Naqvi] Why are you not a communist? 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] Reagan is trying to show that we cannot be 
neutral. If there can be no neutrality then we choose to be in the 
socialist camp against the imperialist camp. 

[Naqvi] Has the USSR helped you in the past few days? 
[Al-Qadhdhafi] Certainly. 

[Naqvi]So there will be no more American bombings of Libya.... 

{Al-Qadhdhafi] The reasons why Reagan attacked us still exist 
and he could continue along his path.
