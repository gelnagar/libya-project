Summary (AI): A May 1976 cable relays a message from Libyan leader Muammar al-Qadhdhafi, sent to the Islamic Conference of foreign ministers meeting in Istanbul. It praises Turkey's role in the Islamic world and welcomes the conference to Turkish soil. It has no Tripoli-specific content.
• The message was relayed by the Amman embassy and sent to multiple US posts, including Tripoli, in a cable dated 13 May 1976.
• Qadhdhafi's message praised Turkey's historic role in spreading and defending Islam and expressed appreciation for the sacrifices of the Turkish people.
• The cable's source text was broadcast by Tripoli domestic services on 13 May 1976 at 1731 GMT.
• 1976-05-13: Islamic Conference (foreign ministers) convened in Istanbul; Qadhdhafi sent a message of support
