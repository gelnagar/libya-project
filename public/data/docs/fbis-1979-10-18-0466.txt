LIBYA

OFFICIAL RECEIVES U.S. CHAMBER OF COMMERCE DELEGATION 


LD161020 Tripoli JANA in English 0951 GMT 16 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, October 16 JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY=--The deputy head of the foreign liaison 
bureau at the general secretariat of the General People's Congress met with an American 
popular delegation headed by Mr. William Bonham, chairman of the American Chamber of Commerce 
in New York. 


At the meeting they discussed the role of popular organizations in the Jamahiriyah and 
America in developing popular relations. The Jamihiriyah official gave the delegation an 
elaborate explanation on the experiment of direct democracy carried out by the Jamahiriyah 
(?and its) people to ensure their authority. He also explained some to the Green Book dicta. 


The American delegation expressed the eagerness of American popular organizations to set 
up relations with Libyan Arab people in particular, and the Arabs in general. 


The meeting was attended by the chairman of the Chambers of Commerce in the Jamahiriyah and 
a number of foreign liaison bureau members.

[Text] Tripoli, 17 Oct, JANA--Last night Colonel al-Qadhdhafi received the Maltese Prime 
Minister Mr Dominic Mintoff. The meeting was attended by Liaison Secretary 'Abd al-Majid 
al-Qa'ud. 

Prime Minister Departs 
LD171752 Tripoli JANA in English 1436 GMT 17 Oct 79 LD 
[Text] Tripoli, October 17, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Maltese Premier Dom Mintoff left 
Tripoli last night, ending a short visit to the Socialist Peoples Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
during which he was received by the leader of the Great First of September Revolution 
Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi and Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud. 


CEAUSESCU'S SPECIAL ENVOY ARRIVES FOR VISIT 


LD171748 Tripoli JANA in English 1622 GMT 17 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, October 17, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Mr. Vassel Boukan, the advisor and 
special envoy of President Nicolae Ceausescu of Romania, a member of the Central Committee 
of the Communist Party, arrived in Tripoli this morning on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. 

He was met at Tripoli international airport by the head of the European department of the 
foreign secretariat, the Romanian charge d'affaires to the Jamahiriyah and a representative 
of the protocol department at the Foreign Secretariat.
