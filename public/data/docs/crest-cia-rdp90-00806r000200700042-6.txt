Summary (AI): A 1981 New York Magazine article reports that ex-CIA agent Edwin Wilson was reportedly working for Qaddafi and had contacts with an associate of Egypt's Hussain al-Toukhamy, linked to Libyan dissident groups. It is about foreign intrigue against Qaddafi and has no specific Tripoli locations.
• Published reports placed Wilson in Qaddafi's camp, negotiating to sell Libya restricted arms.
• Sources said the National Link, a Libyan dissident group, was apparently set up as a conduit for arms, money, and personnel to groups working to overthrow Qaddafi.
• One source said the National Link was in a direct working relationship with the US Defense Intelligence Agency; the DIA declined to comment.
• Other reports speculated the CIA unofficially encouraged Wilson's activities to gain intelligence.
