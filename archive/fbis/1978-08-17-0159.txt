LIBYA

FINNISH FIRM, USSR TO BUIID NUCIEAR PIANT IN LIBYA 
ID151034Y Helsinki Domestic Service in Finnish 0500 GMT 15 Aug 78 LD 


[Text] Imartan Voima and Finnish industry will deliver--in cooperation with the UJSSR-- 

a big nuclear power plant of the Ioviisa type to Libya. The USSR has already concluded 
an agreement with Libya concerning the delivery of the plant. Imatran Voima was offered 
cooperation with the USSR last spring and the cooperation agreement was signed in June, 
The deliveries of equipment for the nuclear power plant, which may well be built in coop- 
eration with the USSR, and the share in the project, will be apportioned according to the 
same principles which applied in nuclear power plant projects carried out in Finland, 
Imatran Voima will reeeive the basic information needed for the planning of the Libyan 
project in the next few months,
