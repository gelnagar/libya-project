Summary (AI): An FBIS London cable (September 1977) reprints a Le Monde interview in which Qadhdhafi discusses the Western Sahara dispute and his proposal for a Maghreb confederation. It says nothing about Tripoli or domestic repression.
• Qadhdhafi proposed settling the Western Sahara dispute within a Maghreb confederation, with the disputed territory as a political entity within it.
• He said he would tour Spain, Morocco, Mauritania and Tunisia within 3 or 4 months.
• Asked why Libya had not recognized the Polisario-formed government, he replied that wisdom and the need for compromise required caution.
• Interview published in Le Monde, Paris, 26 September 1977.
