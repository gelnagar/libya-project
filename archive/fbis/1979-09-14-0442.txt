PRESS STATEMENT ISSUED ON IRANIAN PARTY VISIT 
ID131428 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1230 GMT 13 Sep 79 LD 


[Text] Text of the press statement published in Tripoli today on the visit of the delee 
gation of the Islamic Republican Party of Iran: 


At the invitation of the Foreign Liaison Office at the General Secretariat of the General *y 
People*s Congress and within the framework of constant consultations between the 

revolutions of the Great First of September and theIslamic revolution of Iran, a delegation 
of the Islamic Republican Party of Iran paid a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah where it attended the celebrations of the 10th anniversary of the Great First 

of September Revolution. 


The delegation was (?warmly) received by Col Mu*ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the revolution, 
and by Major *Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud. The delegation was also received by Muhammad 
Bilqasim Suwayy, assistant secretary general of the General People's Congress. During these 
meetings the delegation conducted lengthy and substantive talks characterized by a spirit 

of fraternity, cordiality and full understanding of all topics discussed. Among the main 
topics was the liberation of all the Palestinian territory and all the Islamic territories, 
chiefly holy Jerusalem, and the return of the Palestinian people to their homeland, the 
establishment of their independent legitimate authority and their national sovereignty 

over the whole of Palestine. 


The two sides also agreed on the need to achieve comprehensive Islamic unity as it is the 
only way for the liberation and dignity of all Muslims and for the utilization of their 
human and material resources and their Islamic civilization in the service of the freedom of 
man everywhere on earth and the guaranteeing of world peace, its security and happiness. 


The two sides agreed to exchange experiences, visits, and popular delegations and to set 

up a joint committee between the Islamic Republican Party and the General Secretariat of the 
General People*s Congress to coordinate the actions of the masses between the two fraternal 
people. The committee is to hold periodic meetings alternately in the two countries. The 
Islamic Party of Iran expressed great admiration for the grand military parade which it 
attended on the occasion of the 10th anniversary of the Great First of September Revolution. 
It emphasized that the military power of the Jamahiriyah was a power for the Islamic nation 
and the Third World and the peoples struggling for their freedom and independence. The 
delegation also praised the agricultural devel .ent and the great progress in housing, 
education and health achieved by the Great First of September Revolution, and the experience 
of direct people's democracy which is being waged by the people of the Jamahiriyah and 
preached to the peoples of the world to achieve genuine democracy and social justice. 


The Libyan side praised the great Islamic revolution which broke out in Iran under the 
leadership of the great struggler Imam Ayatollah Khomeyni--the revolution which is cone 
sidered a true Islamic popular revolution in this era and which together with the Great 
First of September Revolution constitutes an example and the beginning of an Islamic 
awakening that embraces the entire Islamic world and places the full authority in the hands 
of the Islamic masses.
