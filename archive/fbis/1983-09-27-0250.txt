LIBYA

Armed Forcés Chief Meets With YAR President 
LD270933 Tripoli JANA in English 0843 GMT 27 Sep 83 


[Text] San’a’, Dhulhijah 20, Sept 27, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — Colonel ’Ali "Abdallah Salih, president of the 
Yemen Arab Republic, met in San‘a’ last night with Brigadier 
Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the Libyan Arab 
Armed Forces. The meeting focused on bilateral relations. It was 
attended by the chief of staff of the Yemeni Armed Forces and 
the secretary of the Brotherhood Bureau in San‘a’. 


JANA Reports Explosion at Chadian Radio Station 
LD271012 Tripoli JANA in English 0839 GMT 27 Sep 83 


[Text] Ndjamena, Dhulhijah 20, Sept 27 JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — A radio station near Ndjamena was last 
night attacked. Eye-witnesses said that an explosive charges went 
off just outside the radio station in Tamrida[JANA spelling] near 
Ndjamena causing extensive damages.
