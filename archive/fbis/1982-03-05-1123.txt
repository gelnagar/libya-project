Congress at the ideological orientation center in Tripoli -- live] 


[Excerpts] Proceeding from the (?principle) that revolutionary force is the building of 
the popular will until it reaches a level that will enable it to exercise authority 
without the need for inciters, I have come here to explain some points I raised 
yesterday and another regarding the people's committees.

Due to its excess oil production and its policy, which is under U.S. influence, Saudi 
Arabia constitutes a real danger to the economy of the Libyan people, the Algerian 
people, the Arab people in the Gulf and also Nigeria and a number of oil exporting 
countries which rely only on oil. Yesterday, I was speaking in a square filled with a 
million people; there was no calm and one could not dot the I's or cross the T's. 

But today we are in a hall and are able to speak calmly about the issue. The oil file 
was supposed to be clear before the people's congresses. 


What is happening is that the oil market has been flooded. There is a glut. Oil tankers 
are loaded with oil but no one wants to buy. This is due to the increase in Saudi 
oil production which has reached 8.5 million barrels a day. 


Saudi Arabia is selling its oil cheaply, not at the posted price. Saudi Arabia is lying. 
Saudi oil is owned by princes, 5,000 of them, each owning an oil well, a share in the 
oil. Each of them wants to invest in companies, hotels, places of entertainment, palaces, 
drinking places, private planes and private businesses. Each of the Saudi princes spends 
what an entire people could spend. Saudi oil, therefore, is owned by princes. How is 

it possible to control 5,000 princes through OPEC? How can they maintain prices; they 
look after their private interests not state interests. In Libya, the state, the people, 
own the oil. The Libyan state owns the oil, while in Saudi Arabia oil is the property 

of individuals, of the princes. Saudi Arabia, on U.S. order, produces so much oil. 

Every now and then an oil well dries up. This being the case, Saudi Arabia will become 
bankrupt and its oil exhausted in the near future. The United States is taking Saudi 

oil and storing it. The princes are selling oil and receiving the money to put it in 
banks. [words indistinct]. Every now and then an oil well dries up in Saudi Arabia. 

The Americans lie to the Saudis and say to them: You have a vast reserve of oil which 
will last for years. And when an oil well dries up, the Americans say it has dried up 
{words indistinct]. Oil will be exhausted in a short time. 


We, on the other hand, have our oil in our land and it will last for years. This is 
important. If one has money or any other material which people do not want to buy at 
the moment, one is not regarded as poor. You have gold and no one wants to buy it, but 
you are not poor. There will come a time when this gold is wanted. But for one to 
(20versell oil and gas), this is the road to becoming poor. One can decide not to 
export oil or to reduce exports to the minimum so that this oil will last scores of 
years and will not be exhausted quickly [words indistinct]. 


It is true that the Libyan oil is in the ground. You should not regard Libya as a poor 
country at all; it is rich with oil and gas and there is wealth beneath our feet; we 

have not squandered it like they do in Saudi Arabia. But now, as a result of the surplus 
of Saudi oil, we cannot sell the quantity which we decided to sell because those who 
would buy from us will say that they are buying from Saudi Arabia. This is the policy 

of Saudi Arabia. Those people whom I condemned yesterday were condemned because we are 
fed up with them. There is no friendly and fraternal method that we have not tried. We 
have met with and told them: Brothers, this is a serious matter; you should not produce 
8.5 million barrels a day. You are harming the peoples of Kuwait, Libya, the Gulf, 
Algeria, Nigeria and others; you are inflicting real harm on them. In Kuwait the amir 

is shouting and saying that Saudi Arabia is killing me. He says: Saudi Arabia wants 

me to submit. The people are not buying my oil but are buying the cheap oil from Saudi 
Arabia which has glutted the market with oil in order to make us submit. The Gulf people 
feel that Saudi Arabia has replaced them in the oil market so that they will submit. 

In this manner, Saudi Arabia gathered them together and established the Gulf Cooperation 
Council and a security pact. What is the meaning of a security pact? It means that Saudi 
Arabia has become dominant over Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait and the UAE.

Saudi Arabia is expanding so as to dominate the whole of the peninsula. It is now trying

JANA: SAUDI ARABIA LEADS OIL PRICE REDUCTION 


LD041932 Tripoli JANA in English 1838 GMT 4 Mar 82 


[Text] Kuwait, 4 Mar (JANA) -- Saudi press reports have stressed that Fahd Ibn ‘Abd 
al-'Aziz had made it clear that he would personally lead an international quest to reduce 
oil prices in the world down to 25 dollars per barrel. 


In a recent meeting with proprietors and editors of Saudi papers and magazines, he 
alleged that anybody who endeavours to reduce oil prices is working to destroy 20th century 
civilization. 


Meanwhile, informed sources said Fahd issued a warning to journalists demanding from them 
to keep fully in line with the ruling family's policy whether regarding oil matters or 
politics or even art and literature. These sources added that Fahd was extremely tense 
during the meeting.
