TITO CABLES AL-QADHDHAFI FOLLOWING 4 JUN DEPARTURE 
LDO41536 Tripoli JANA in English 1445 GMT 4 Jur 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, June 4, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The leader of the Great First of 
September Revolution, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, received the following cable from 
President Josip Broz Tito of Yugoslavia, following his departure from the 'Uqba bin 
Nafit air bases: 


"As we depart from the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, we would like to thank 
you for your kind hospitality which I and the delegation accompanying me have received 
during our stay in your beautiful country. As was the case with our previous meetings, 

I am sure this visit will strengthen the cooperation bonds between our non-aligned 
countries and reinforce the friendship tying both peoples. 


"Tamconvinced that the high degree of mutual understanding which exists between us as 
well as our points of view on the several issues discussed will contribute to the imple- 
mentation of the principles and objectives of the non-aligned policy in particular an 
will serve the interest of international cooperation and world peace, in general. 


"On this occasion, I catch the opportunity to convey to you and the friendly Libyan people 
our best wishes for constant progress and prosperity." 


AT-TURAYKI DEPARTS WITH BENIN FOREIGN MINISTER FOR COLOMBO 
LDO42204 Tripoli JANA in English 1840 amr 4 Jun 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, June 4, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY-- The foreign secretary, Dr 'Ali 'Abd 

as-Salam at-Turayki, left Tripoli this afternoon for Colombo to participate in the meetings 
of the Coordination Bureau for the conference of foreign ministers from non-aliged countries. 
The bureau will be making the preparations for the sixth summit conference for the non- 
aligned countries, due to be held in Havana in September. 


The meetings of the bureau which commenced today will discuss the situation in the Arab 
region, particularly the issue of the Palestinian people following the signing of the 
treacherous treaty with (Israel). 


The conference will also discuss the suspension of Egypt's membership in the organiza- 
tion of non-aligned countries due to its complete alignment with (Israel) and its falling 
into the laps of American imperialism,

Other prominent issues that wil be discussed include that of the Western Sahara, 
liberation issues in South Africa, as well as economic cooperation within the non- 
aligned movement. In a statement to our agency before leaving Tripoli, the foreign 
secretary affirmed that the visit by the Yugosiav president, Josip Broz Tito, has sup- 
ported the non-alignment movement, constructively. 


He added: "Talks between Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and President Tito and the joint commun- 
ique issued following President Tito's visit to the Jamahiriyah have asserted the impor- 
tance of the non-aligned movement and their efforts to assist it so that it would have 

a major contribution to the struggle against colonialism and Zionisn. 


On board of the same plane was the Benin foreign minister on his way to Colombo to 
take part in the conference, 


The Benin foreign minister told our representative that his visit to the Jamahiriyah was 
successful and fruitful. He said: "The talks I have held with my brothers in the 
Jamahiriyah dealt with promoting economic, scientific and technical cooperation between 
the two countries." 


He then thanked the Jamahiriyah for the warm welcome and hospitality he was given during 
his stay and praised the strong relations between the Libyan-Arab and Benin people. 


ARAB SPACE COMMUNICATIONS OFFICIAL ENDS VISIT 
LD051255 Tripoli "ANA in English 0920 GMT 5 Jun 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, June 5, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Dr Ali al-Mashat, the director-general 
of Arab space communications, left Tripoli yesterday after visiting the Jamahiriyah for 
several days upon the invitation from the General People's Committee for Communications 
and Marine Transport. Dr al-Mashat held talks with those in charge on matters relating 

to the Arab statellite and the question of setting up the necessary communication stations 
for communal reception. 


Dr al-Mashat met 'Abd al-!'Ati al-'Ubaydi, the secretary-general of the General People!s 
Jongress [title as received], the secretary of the General People's Committee for Communi- 
cation and Marine Transport, and those in charge of telecommunications and the manu- 
facturing of sophisticated instruments at the Secretariat of Light Industry. 


Dr al-Mashat expressed admiration for the tangible development in the field of tele- 
communication in the Jamahiriyah and noted the concern shown by the general directorate 


for the post and telecommunication in the Arab space communication project. 


It is worth mentioning that the Jamahiriyah is a member in the administrative council of 
the Arab space communications organization.
