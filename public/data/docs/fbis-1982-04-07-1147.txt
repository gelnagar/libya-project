LIBYA

RTS ON SAUDI ARABIA RULING 


FAMILY 


Financing Chad Forces 


LDO21502 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1426 GMT 2 Apr 82 

[Text] Paris, 2 Apr (JANA) -- An information official in the Chadian Frolinat has 
revealed that the Northern Forces, led by Hissein Habre, have received financial 
assistance totalling $180 million from the ruling family in Saudi Arabia. The money 
is a first payment. The official added that this information was obtained from 


documents found on captives from the Northern Forces.
