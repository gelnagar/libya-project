LIBYA

Green Book Conference Final Statement Issued 
LD132250 Tripoli JANA in English 2045 GMT 13 Apr 83 
[Text] Al-Bayan Al-Awwal, April 13, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — Dr Ahmad Saiman Ahmad, member of prepara- 
tory committee, delivered the final communique of the first 
international conference on the ideology of Mu’ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi,the Green Book. The text follows of the “Final Com- 
munique of the First International Conference on Mu’ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi’s Ideology -- the Green Book”: 


The world is dominated by political and economic oppression. It 
is witnessing acute social ruptures. Man and people are being 
subjected to the most savage forms of domination, subjugation, 
terrorism and exploitation. These dangers are being posed to 
peace and security by imperialism, Zionism, racism reaction and 
fascism, which are running amock in the international arena, 
threatening humanity with extinction. 


The world is passing through a stifling crisis, and a deterioration 
at the political, economic, and social levels. It is characterised by 
ideological bankruptcy and failure of all prevalent theories and 
regimes in finding solutions to man’s problems so as to realise his 
freedom and sovereignty. 


For all these prevailing reasons above, the first international 
Green Book conference on Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi's ideology 
in theory and practice was held at Qar Yunis University in 
Al-Bayan Al-Awwal city from 7-13 April. The conierence was 
the crowning point of all the seminars and hearings held at local 
and international levels on the Green Book. The conference was 
held under the auspices of the Interntional Green Book Research 
Centre and was attended by more than 1,000 lecturers, writers, 
journalists and a number of revolutionary and political leaders 
together with freedom fighters from all corners of the world. 
They came to study the radical solutions propounded by the 
Green Book to various political, economic and social problems 
which the prevailing regimes and theories have failed to resolve. 


The participants at the conference hail the thinker and teacher 
Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi, and express appreciation for his active 
involvement in the conference’s proceedings, his patronage and 
his elucidations and interpretations of the Green Book’s dicta

which have enlightened humanity’s path towards freedom and 
emancipation from all the chains of oppression, injustice and 
exploitation. The participants further acclaim him as the leading 
ideologue of jamahiri (mass) thought. 


The participants studied a number of research papers dealing 
with humanity's ideological problems as heralded in the Green 
Book. They were democratically, seriously and objectively dis- 
cussed. The participants affirm the following: 


1. The Green Book represents a new revolutionary civilizational 
and humanitarian orientation capable of resolving humanity's 
problems and their devastating effect. 


2. The Green Book's dicta are the product of humanity's thought 
and the people’s struggle throughout history. They have compre- 
hensive orientiation and are not an ideological product of an 
individual or localised or nationalist character. 


3. The solutions contained in the Green Book are radical, prac- 
tical and capable of application regardless of the geographic, 
democratic, cultural, national and religious characteristics. 


4. Examining and explaining this ideology together with studying 
and heralding it represent a humanitarian task to be shouldered 
by all peoples struggling for freedom and happiness. 


5. The attempts to suppress the Green Book’s ideology by the 
imperialist-Zionist-reactionary-racist-fascist forces requires the 


waging of an international cultural revolution launched by the 
believeres in the masses and the inevitability of victory over all 
the foundations of injustice and exploitation. 


6. The Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah represents a 
revolutionary citadel and an international platform for combat- 
ing imperialism, Zionism, racism, reaction and fascism. Freedom 
seekers and peace loving forces should support her legitimate 
struggle against the imperialist threats and provocations. 


7. It has become necessary to form an international committee 
composed of lecturers and writers to set up a programme to assist 
the International Green Book Research Centre in implementing 
this conference's recommendations and in preparing for the 
conference on Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi’s ideology (the Green 
Book). The programme should include the following: 


A) The endeavour to launch an international cultural revolution 
creating a new mentality to contribute to setting civilisation on 
the right path. 


B) The endeavour to create forms of cooperation between scienti- 
fic institutions in the Jamahiriyah and their counterparts in other 
countries with the aim of acquiring a deeper understanding of 
jamahiri ideology as set in the Green Book and of enriching it 
with studies and elaborations. 


C) Work to form joint research teams for the same purpose as 
above.
