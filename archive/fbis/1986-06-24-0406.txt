Popular Revolution Urged Against Jordanian Regime 
LD231441 Tripoli Voice af Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 0115 GMT 23 Jun 86 


[Station commentary] 


[Text] Arab masses in Jordan: From here, the bastion of Arab 
revolution, from the voice of the revolutionary committees, the 
voice of the greater Arab homeland, we appeal to you and invite 
you to struggle and to stage a revolution against the lackey 
regime — the rotten monarchical regime in Jordan. Free broth- 
ers; the monarchical regime is a lackey regime. It is a reactionary 
and traitorous regime. Free brothers; Arab workers in Jordan; 
students, peasants, soldiers, and Arab masses in genera! in Jor- 
dan; we are now calling on you to strike at this rotten regime. 
Free brothers, this regime, which has thrown itself into the laps 
of world imperialism, particularly British and U.S. imperialism, 
and Zionism, is selling out the Arabs and the Arab east. 


Free brothers, we all have to live through this deteriorating 
situation in Jordan. Who has the power in his hands? It is not the 
Arab people in Jordan, but a royal, reactionary, and rotten 
family. This royal family controls authority, and the Arab people 
in Jordan are helpless. Proof of this can be seen in the submission 
by this traitor lackey King Husayn. Had there been a people’s 
authority in Jordan it would not have allowed a submission to 
imperialism or a sell-out of the Arab cause, which is an insult to 
Arab honor and dignity and the Arab nation. But in the absence 
of the Arab people’s authority in Jordan we expect this sick and 
rotten political reality in Jordan. The latest result of such a 
reality was the visit of agent Husayn to the U.S. Black House. 


24 Jun 86 


From the economic point of view there is much wealth in this part 
of the Arab homeland, but who benefits from this wealth? Is it 
the Arab people in Jordan? No, not at all. The royal retinue and 
those who are close to it — exploiters, merchants, brokers, and 
businessmen who are linked to world imperialism and foreign 
capitalism — are the only ones who benefit from this Arab 
wealth, while the Jordanian citizen is poor, and endures unem- 
ployment, poverty and deprivation, misery, and poverty. On the 
other hand, theroyal family enjoys pomp. All this indicates that 
the economic system in Jordan is rotten. This calls for a strike 
against this subjugating, exploiting regime by the masses of 
workers, peasants, students and all Arab people in Jordan in 
order to destroy this regime and build the alternative, which 
entails prosperity for all. 


Our free brothers, students, peasants, soldiers, and workers: You 
should rectify this deteriorating situation, politically, econom- 
ically, militarily, and socially. It is a shame and a thousand 
shames from the political point of view to have the authority 
vested in a handful of people, who are the ruling family in Jordan. 
It is a shame and a thousand shames that Arab wealth in Jordan 
is in the hands of a handful of exploiters, merchants, and brokers 
__ the servants of world imperialism — while the Arab people in 
Jordan are poor, deprived, and unemployed. It is a shame and a 
thousand shames that a certain group in Jordan — the royal 
retinue and their close associates — should remain masters, while 
the remainder of the Arab people are their slaves. So long as this 
is our situation in Jordan, and as long as there is a dictatorship 
in Jordan and political autocracy — that is to say, a minority of 
people monopolizing power, which is the ruling family, while the 
Arab people in Jordan are helpless; and as long as a minority, 
from the economic point of view, controls and monopolizes 
wealth, and exploits the remainder of the people while the latter 
are deprived, exploited, and unemployed; and as long as, from 
the social point of view, this handful of people who have power, 
wealth and arms in their hands in Jordan is the most powerful, 
while the remainder of the people are weak and subjugated, then 
this is a deteriorating situation; it is a diseased situation, it is a 
rotten situation. 


The question which is now being asked is: What is the solution? 
Or what is the remedy for these political, economic, military, and 
social diseases? In simple terms the remedy lies in a popular 
revolution. There is no remedy except popular revolution. But 
popular revolution does not mean carrying out repair work, 
patching up, or a military coup. Military coups are one thing, 
while revolution is something else. Popular revolution means that 
all workers, students, peasants, and free soldiers in our free Arab 
army in Jordan should march and strike at this lackey regime 
and remove it, and then build the jamahiri revolutionary alter- 
native — the jamahiri revolutionary regime, in which the inter- 
ests of all the people lie, the interests of all factions — workers, 
students, peasants and soldiers. Revolution is different from a 
military coup. The latter means that a group of militarists seize 
power through the use of arms. But the regime which they 
establish may be worse than the regime they have toppled. The 
popular revolution is always the best alternative, which is in the 
‘nterests of all the masses of the people and all the factions I have 
mentioned.
