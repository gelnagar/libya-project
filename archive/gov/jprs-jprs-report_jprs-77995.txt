JOINT OLL REFINERY--AL-ADWA' has learned that a giant Gulf project will soon be 
implemented in Bahrain. The project will cost $600 million and will be financed 
and owned by Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Bahrain. The project consists of the eatab- 
lishment of a giant refinery of a special type to convert heavy petroleum products 
into Light products. During a brief meeting between the chief editor and Minister 
of Development and Industry Yusif Ahmad ash-Shirawi, the chief editor learned 

that the Ministries of Development and Industry of the three states have made good 
progress in studying this giant project and that the three countries will very 
soon endorse the final steps for its implementation. The chief editor has also 
learned that this project will increase the price of heavy petroleum by no less 
than $15 per barrel after converting it to light petroleum at the refinery. The 
price will go up by several thousands of dollars following the processing of this 
light petroleum to other chemical products. [Excerpt] [GF111604 Bahrain AL-ADWA'

Ashraf Continues Campaign--Warns of Soviet Threat 


A warning of the threat of a communist takeover and subsequent Soviet inter- 
vention in Iran was given by Princess Ashraf Pahlavi, twin sister of the late 
Shah, when she addressed a gathering of about 400 senior U.S. military figures 
and some prominent politicians at a New Jersey dinner last week. 


"In today's Iran, according to all observers, ...e marxists are the only well- 
organised and disciplined force," she said. "Once in power they will call for 
the Soviet Union to come to their rescue, just as they did in Afghanistan. 
Already the red flag flies in several northern provinces (of Iran)." 


The Princess said the Libyan leader Moammar Khaddafi, the Palestine Liberation 
Organisation and even misguided liberals in the West were knowingly or 
unknowingly working to the advantage of Moscow, and the Soviets 'mderstood this. 
The chaos and anarchy in Iran could only profit them and they were taking 
full advantage of this. The communist danger there was imminent. 


She also told the gathering that Iran had warned former U.S. president 

Jimmy Carter that a communist takeover in Afghanistan was imminent at a CENTO 
meeting in April, 1977. Yet, Carter was astonished when the Russians moved 
into the country. 


"Yet, as early as February, 1976, in his report to the Communist party 
congress, Mr Brezhnev stated, and I quote: ‘We do not hide the fact that 

for us detente enables favorable conditions to be established for socialist 
and communist build-up,'" the Princess said. "Fortunately, your new president 
knows better, for he did not hesitate to state that detente should not be a 
one-way street.”
