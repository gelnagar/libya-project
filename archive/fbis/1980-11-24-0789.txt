LIBYA

IRAN'S RAFSANJANI VISITS TRIPOLI, SPEAKS, SEES LEADERS 
Meeting With Jallud 
LD211828 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 21 Nov 80 


[Text] Brother Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud this morning received Hojjat ol-Eslam 
Hashemi Rafsanjani, the speaker of the Iranian Islamic Consultative Assembly, in the 
presence of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamariyah ambassador to Iran and the charge 
of the Iranian Embassy in the Jamahiriyah. 


Speeches at Friday Prayers 
LD221202 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 21 Nov 80 


[Text] Brother Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud made a speech at Friday prayers at 

[Mullah Muhammad] Mosque in Tripoli. During the speech, he greeted Brother Hashemi 
Rafsanjani, speaker of the Majlis, the brother members of the Majlis and brother leaders 

of the revolutionary guards in Iran. He pointed out that the meeting of brethren consti- 
tutes true strength and confirms the victory of Islam. He added: The 1 September revolution 
was alone in fending for Islam and Muslims in all corners of the world, supporting those 

who are persecuted and tormented on earth. But today we feel greater strength as the Muslim 
masses of Iran, led by Imam Khomeyni, have joined us. We appreciate true Islam as it was 
presented by our prophet Muhammad, may the blessings and prayers of God be upon him. For 
the Koran is the law of society. Ordinary law is laid down by individuals, but the law of 
heavens no one can change or cancel. 


He went on to say: If the prophet, may the blessings and prayers of God be upon him, 
attached idols made of stone, today in the arab homeland and the islamic world there are 
idols made of people who glorify colonialism and pray for imperialism, conspiring against 
Islam and Muslims. He pointed out that the poor and the deprived were those who supported 
the prophet, may the blessings and prayers of God be upon him, and spread hismessage. But 
the rich of Mecca fought him and conspired against him. From this concept we [word 
indistinct] the Islam of catastrophies, polygamy, possession of large palaces and hoarding 
of jewels, gold and silver, and take the side of the Islam which sanctifies labor, be- 
friends the poor and honors and values of justice and equality. 


He stressed that it was the 1 September revolution alone which had a clear, defined stand 
toward the shah because he corspired against Islam and Muslims. When the majority of 
Muslim and Arab heads of state took part in the shah's ceremonies in 1972 and licked his 
shoes, the 1 September revolution was waging kindling war against the shah's policy, making 
alliance with the Muslim masses in Iran and Imam Khomeyni, giving them every support and 
backing. He added the true Koran and Islam have made "mankind successors in the land" 
[phrase from Koran] for they are against governments and bourgeois regimes but for the 
masses, for the people's authority, for shura and for the realization of justice. 


Staff Major "Abd as-Salam Jallud concluded his speech by saying: The Muslim does not fear 
anybody, nor does he bow his head for America or anybody else. He only bows for God. Thus 
he is a continuous call for Jihad and permanent awareness for the creation of the strong 
Muslim, Hojjat ol-Eslam Hashemi Rafsanjani, speaker of the Islamic Majlis in Iran, 
stressed the significance of the cohesion and unity of the Muslims in repelling the coloni- 
alist, fierce crusade on the Islamic world, and in order to spread the banner of truth 

and justice.

In his speech at the Friday prayers, he confirmed that the victory of the Islamic revolu- 
tion in Iran and the 1 September is considered a victory for Islam and Muslims all over 
the globe. The speaker of the Iranian Majlis lauded the courageous, sincere stands of 
the 1 September revolution in the cause of Supporting the causes of truth and justice 

as well as freedom everywhere. He stressed that the Muslim Iranian people consider that 
the occupation by the Zionist enemy of the Palestinian Arab lands is the cause of all 
Islamic peoples and that the Iranian people will dedicate all their potentialities to 
cooperating with sister Islamic countries in order to liberate the occupied Arab lands. 
In his speech, he explained that the Iraqi-Iranian war--which has been imposed on the 
Iranian people--only serves the Zionist-imperialist alliance and Arab reaction, confirm- 
ing that the funds wasted could have been devoted to the liberation of the occupied Arab 
lands. 


Meeting With Al-Qadhdhafi 
LD221358 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 22 Nov 80 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution this morning received the Iranian delegation 
on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, headed by Hojjat ol-Eslam 
Hashemi Rafsanjani, The brother leader talked to the Iranian delegation, which included 
a number of the leaders of the revolutionary guard, about the people's revolution, which 
became a reality, sweeping over all parts of the world. At this meeting the leader also 
expounded the Third World theory which outlines the path of salvation and final release 
for humanity.

SYRIAN FOREIGN MINISTER MEETS AL-QADHDHAFI 
LD232105 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 23 Nov 80 


[Text] A telephone conversation took place today between the leader of the revolution 
and Syrian President Hafiz al-Asad. 


The brother leader received in audience ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam, Syrian deputy prime minis- 
ter and foreign minister, who arrived in Tripoli this evening for a visit to the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and who will be having contacts and talks om the current 
Arab situation, He was welcomed at the airport by the foreign secretary. Brother ‘Abd 
al-Halim Khaddam told JANA that his visit is within the framework of continued consul- 
tation between Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and President al-Asad on the current Arab situa- 
tion and on what the Arab nation is experiencing. He also explained that he bears a 
message to the leader from President al-Asad within the framework of fraternal relations 
between them, He stressed that the Syrian viewpoint is represented in postponing the 
summit conference until the differences and conflicts which exist now in the Arab nation 
are removed so as to confront the dangers threatening the Arab struggle and, in particular, 
the Zionist threat and colonialism as well as to correct the course of the Arab situation 
in the interest of our Arab nation in steadfastness and confrontation.

ARAB PEOPLE'S CONFERENCE STATEMENT ON ARAB SUMMIT 
LD230918 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 22 Nov 80 


[Excerpts] The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Conference, which embodies all 
the political, trade union and professional associations in the Arab homeland, and, which 
has taken upon itself to place the facts of the situation in front of the Arab masses, 
the first and foremost beneficiaries of the areas's resources, presents the following 
observations: 


1. Summit conferences in general, where there are regimes of different ideological ten- 
dencies, accomplish little except small frameworks of joint Arab action. They unfor- 
tunately have not, after 12 previous summit conferences, turned their resolutions into 
something tangible. 


2. Between the summit of Baghdad and the present time many events have taken place that 
have left their impact on the Arab Situation. The Afghanistan problems emerged and 
American colonialism exploited it, not in the confrontation between itself and the Soviet 
Union but to divide the Arabs and create a confrontation between one part and another. 
The Arab masses were Surprised to see the Arab regimes forget that the Arabs' main battle 
was against Camp David, against U.S. colonialism, Zionism and the As-Sadat regime, which 
in fact signed a joint military alliance against the Palestinian and Arab people. These 


same regimes convened in Baghdad in order to confront them, and then Afghanistan Was substi- 


tuted for Camp David and Kabul for Jerusalem. Those conservative regimes entered the Same 
front with the As-Sadat regime, U.S. colonialism and Israel. 


3. The Iraqi-Iranian war took place without any acceptable justification and in complete 
contradiction with our national interests. Iraq, through the outbreak of war, proceeded 
with its military power toward something else than the main Arab battle against the 
Zionist enemy, who usurped Palestine and occupied the Syrian Golan, Egyptian Sinai and 
southern Lebanon, 


The U.S. military presence has increased in the area. As-Sadat called the U.S. Rapid 
Deployment Force [RDF] to carry out maneuvers on the Egyptian western desert on the bor- 
der of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah after he had granted the United 
States four military bases on Egyptian soil. 


Arab reaction is increasingly deepening its relations with U.S. colonialism and asking 
its military protection. Thus, the RDF is carrying out its maneuvers in the Feypt of 
As-Sadat, while what is required is to stand against the Camp David alliance, in particu- 
lar against the leadership of that alliance embodied in colonialism with its growing 
serious military presence in the area. 


In these disturbed circumstances, what results can ensue from an Arab summit conference? 
The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Conference calls the Arab masses and their 
political and trade unions to be aware and block the path in front of reactionary 
conspiracy.
