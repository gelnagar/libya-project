Summary (AI): A 29 March 1979 DCI alert memorandum to the National Security Council warns that Egypt and Libya were building up forces along their common border, raising the risk of war by deliberate attack or escalation of border incidents. It lists consequences such as Arab backlash against Egypt and possible Syrian and Iraqi troop deployments. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• Egypt and Libya were augmenting their forces along their common border as of March 1979.
• The memorandum was an interagency Alert Memorandum coordinated at working level by CIA, DIA, and NSA.
• A conflict was assessed as likely to draw condemnation of Egypt from most Arabs and could prompt Syria and Iraq to send troops in solidarity.
• 1977-07: Border incident between Egypt and Libya (cited as precedent for unpremeditated escalation)
