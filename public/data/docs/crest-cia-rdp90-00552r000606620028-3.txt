Summary (AI): A 1981 Washington Post wire story reports that retired Master Sgt. Luke Thompson said he and other ex-Green Berets were recruited around 1977 by a man posing as a CIA contact to train terrorists in Libya. The recruits were allegedly promised large pay and were flown via Zurich, where they learned their employer was the Libyan government.
• Thompson said he was asked to recruit four or five others with Special Forces A Team expertise.
• Recruiters offered $6,500 a month, a $10,000 bonus and $250,000 in life insurance.
• Thompson said he was told the cover was an aircraft-industry job in Indonesia before the Libya destination was revealed.
• Thompson said he was ordered to testify before a federal grand jury about his involvement.
• The article is dated 27 August 1981 and datelined Honolulu, 26 August (AP).
• 1977-07: Recruits met by a man named Wilson, offered $6,500 a month plus a $10,000 bonus and $250,000 life insurance, and learned their employer was the Libyan government.
