STATEMENT ISSUED ON VISIT OF CHAD'S GOUKOUNI 


LD231415 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 23 Jan 81 


[Text] A press statement was issued today on the visit Chad President Goukouni Oueddei 
has paid to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah [SPLAJ]. Here is the text: 


Accompanied by an important delegation, President Goukouni Oyveddei paid a fraternal and 
friendly visit to the SPLAJ during which he conducted fraternal talks with brother Col 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the great 1 September revolution. 


The two sides--the Libyan Arab and the Chadian sides--also conducted talks stemming from 
the depth of friendship and fraternal alliance between the Libyan Arab and Chadian peoples 
that centered on the Libyan Arab people's backing for their brother Chadian people during 
the state of rebuilding Chad. 


The two sides have agreed to increase economic and technical cooperation and to cooperate 
in the fields of education and culture. 


The situation on the African Continent and in the world was also discussed. 


The two sides affirmed their belief in the importance of acliieving security, peace and 
stability in the region and stated their pledgeto work for the realization of this goal. 
They also affirmed their conviction in the policy of good-neighbor relations, mutual respect 
and noninterference in the internai affairs of other states and their respect for the 
political choices of the African states. They asserted respect for the sovereignty and 
territorial integrity of these states, while at the same time they warn against any inter- 
ference in the internal affairs of Chad or any conspiracy against the Chad people and 
their lawful government. They place responsibility on the neighboring states of Chad for 
any acts of conspiracy or sabotage that originate in these states against the sovereignty 
and security of Chad. They also affirm that the limited Libyan military presence in Chad 
is legitimate and its role is restricted to helping the lawful government of Chad in 
achieving security and stability. It does not constitute any threat to Chad's neighboring 
states. This question is the concern of the SPLAJ and the Republic of Chad. 


The two sides expressed concern over the French military occupation of a number of African 
states, which constitutes a neocolonialist return to Africa, particularly the concentration 
of the French forces in Central Africa near Chadian borders. They demand the immediate 
withdrawal of the French forces from Africa and call upon the heads of African states to 
join them in demanding the withdrawal of the French forces. They also call for convening 

a conference of African heads of state to adopt a decision on the withdrawal of French 
forces from Africa. 


On behalf of the people of Chad and the Chadian Government, President Goukouni Oueddei 
conveyed thanks to brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and to the Libyan people for the 
courageous stand which the Libyan people have taken towards their brother Chadian people. 


President Goukouni Oueddei has extended an invitation to the brother colonel to visit 
Chad. The brother colonel has accepted this invitation with thanks, and the visit's date 
will be set later.

AL-'UBAYDI COMMENTS ON CHAD MERGER, REFERENDUM 


LD261053 Lusaka Domestic Service in English 1800 GMT 24 Jan 81 CA 


[Text] Libyan Foreign Minister [as heard] Mr ‘Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi has said that steps 
have not been taken yet towards his country's merger with Chad. Speaking to reporters at 
Lusaka International Airport today just before his departure, Mr Ubaydi said the proposed 
merger with Chad will be implemented after a referendum has been conducted in Chad aimed 
at determining the will of the people. He said since the joint communique on the merger 
was signed about 2 weeks ago between the Libyan and Chadian leaders, Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
and Goukouni Oueddei respectively, the steps have not been taken to implement the merger 

as the general consensus of the people of Chad has not yet been determined. 


Mr Ubaydi, who delivered a special messagefrom President al-Qadhdhafi to President Kaunda 
at State House this morning onthe current situation in his country, hoped that the people 
of Chad will be given the chance of carrying out the decision so that the two countries 
can work together in unity. He said his country was in support of a referendum in Chad 
and pledged his country's support for the aspirations of the African people. 


MOROCCO REPORTEDLY TO ‘COOPERATE’ WITH FRANCE 


LD261020 Tripoli JANA in English 0804 GMT 26 Jan 81 


[Text] Paris, 26 Jan (JANA)--King Hassan of Morocco announced his readiness to cooperate 
in widescale military operations in Africa should France request it. 


The French newspaper "“L'HUMANITE" said King Hassan has informed the French authorities of 
his pesition, reminding them of his past services to France, USA and Zaire. The paper 
added that France welcomed the proposal especially as its colonial intervention in Central 
Africa and elsewhere are being met with African and international condemnation. 


Our own correspondent in Paris said that French Premier Barre's visit to Morocco falls 
within the French effort to recruit its agents to serve its colonial interests in Africa.

ARAB CONGRESS RESTRUCTURED IN FINAL SESSION 


LD231113 Tripoli AL-FAJR AL-JADID in Arabic 20 Jan 81 p 2 


[Unattributed report: "Arab People's Congress Concludes Second Ordinary Session Last 
Night and Issues Final Statement and Number of Important Resolutions and Documents" ] 


[Excerpt] The Arab People's Congress last night held the final meetings of its second 
session. At the meeting members of the Congress Permanent Secretariat were selected. 
These are the [representatives of] the Libyan General People's Congress, the Algerian 
FLN, the Syrian Arab Ba'th Party, the International Confederation of Arab Trade Unions 
[ICATU], the PLO, the Federation of Arab Peasants, the Lebanese Nationalist Movement, 
the Egyptian Nationalist Movement, the Yemeni Socialist Party [YSP], the Nationalist 
Movement in the Peninsula and the Gulf and the Polisario. 


The congress issued a resolution at this meeting discontinuing the membership of the 
ruling party in Baghdad from the People's Congress and its Permanent Secretariat while 
preserving a place in both for the Iraqi people. The congress also denounced the 
ruling party in Iraq and its policy which does not serve the Arab nation's interests 
as is evident in its war against the Islamic revolution in Iran and its hostile stands 
toward the Arab liberation movement represented by the Arab People's Congress. 


The congress decided to convene a symposium on political freedoms and charged the 
Permanent Secretariat to take the necessary steps to this end. It also decided to set 
up a preparatory committee to prepare for such a symposium.
