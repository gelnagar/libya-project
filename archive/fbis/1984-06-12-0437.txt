LIBYA

Anniversary of U.S. Bases’ Eviction Celebrated 
LD1I11528 Tripoli JANA in English 1415 GMT 11 Jun 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Ramadan 12, June 11, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY Celebrations and popular rallies started 
throughout Jamahiriyah last night, in commemoration of the 
14th anniversary of the eviction of the American colonial military 


bases from the Libyan Arab territory in Tripoli. The torch of 


victory was lighted at Algiers Square declaring the start of huge 
celebration to commemorate this historical event realized by our 
people’s free will, thanks to the Great First September and its 
thinker and revolutionary, Colonel Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi. 


The victory torch lighting process began from Umm ‘Atigah Air 
Force Base amid huge popular rallies in which the masses of the 
Basic People’s Congresses took part. Amidst these festivities, 
multi-coloured missiles were launched and ships docking at 
Tripoli sounded their horns in salute to this great event. More- 
over, folk dance rings were organized, as well as lines of cars, 
green flag holders, torch carriers, groups of militias, people from 
youth and sports centre, producers, scouts, cadets, arms of the 
al-Fatih and group of folk arts 


However, the participants in the festivals affirmed their determi- 
nation to continue to work for more victories declaring their 
challenge to imperialist plots and resistance of the arrogance of 
the aggressors calling for the elimination of all treacherous and 
agent gangs. On this great occasion, evening entertainments were 
held in which folk song groups and the team of Chinese acrobats 
took part 


In all Al-Bayan al-Awwal, huge popular rallies celebrating the 
14th anniversary of the eviction of the American colonial military 
bases which were occupying the Arab Libyan territory were held 


A march went through the streets of the town including people 
carrying torches, popular cavaliers, the generation of anger 
amongst the cadets and arms of Al-Fatih, scouts and the masses 
of the Basic People’s Congresses at Al-Bayan al-Awwal Munici- 
pality 


The participants in the march carried banners reaffirming the 
victory of freedom on the Libyan Arab soil and reasserting the 
authority of the people
