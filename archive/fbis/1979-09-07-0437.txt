LIBYA

MORE ON AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESS TO 'SYMBOLIC UNITS! 
LD062040 Tripoli JANA in English 1405 GMT 6 Sep 79 LD 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, Sep 6, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The leader of the Great First 
September Revolution, Col Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi, delivered a speech yesterday during his 
meeting with the symbolic units of Arab armies. The address reads as follows; 


"I came to personally present you with the Badge of the Great First September, a token of 
my appreciation, that of your brethren the members of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces and 

the Libyan people, men and women who deemed that you deserved appreciation and honour. 

For this reason I have come to present you with the Badge of the Great First September. We 
feel that during these celebrations, at this particular time, we are actually one people, 
with one homeland and one army, despite the fact that we raise different flags, perform 
different anthems and wear different uniforms. But all these are artificial and 

irrelevant formalities which do not alter the truth. The truth is that the Arab nation 

is one nation and the Arab people one people. 


The establishment of one Arab army is also possible and all the regional differences 
which now seem to characterize every group are not here to remain. They are not real. 
The reality as I said is that of the one nation, one people and one army. 


Then it is neither far fetched nor impossible with Arab determination that these regional 
formalities which divide the Arab nation should disappear. It is neither far fetched nor 
impossible that this nation rises beyond regionalism and that we find one day that this 
uniform which marks the difference between us disappears while we wear one national 
costume and weave out of these various flags one Arab flag. We might find the differences 
which divide between us eliminated by the one nation. Then we would have achieved the 
true Arab unity, our dream and hope. 


We have recorded your presence with us, in the 10th pages of the record of the Great 

First of September Revolution as well as in the hearts of the Libyan people. We do not 
have material things worth giving to you and which can last to remind us of this day exoept 
the Medal of the Great First of September which we have just awarded to you. 


We feel that you are by the side of the Libyan people in the battle of challenge in which 
they face the enemy of the Arab nation's enemy, represented in imperialist, Zionist and 
reactionary forces, positioned on the borders of Libya. 


We affirm to you that the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, the units of which you have seen in 
the parade, are also at your disposition. It is your force and a force for the country 
and a force for you at your disposition to fight by your side. The tanks, the modern 
planes, the guns are all yours and the soldiers are your brothers. In this national 
military parade and this gathering the unity of our Arab nation and the unity of the 
Slogans we raise is embodied. The slogans include: one Arab army, chanted by Arabism 
everywhere. 


The possibility of the establishment of one Arab army is not ruled out. In the parade 

you showed the whole world that the Arab nation was one nation and at the same time it 
proved Arab unity, and fastness of getting together from the Gulf to the ocean on the 

day of the Great Al-Fatah. The national parade was a challenge to the world which might 
think that Arabs were not strong and incapable of getting together and to have understanding.

The leader said: "We wanted to challenge the world, (Israel) and America, We want to 
show the whole world that the Arab nation is one nation and its national festivals are one, 
Your participation on this military and political level proved that Al-fatah does not 
belong to the Libyans only but to the whole Arab nation, The same thing applies to the 
Palestinian problem, because it belongs to the whole Arab nation Palestine is the 

stolen homeland of the Arabs, Therefore Al-Fatah is the feast of all Arabs, the million 
Algerian martyrs are the war of the whole Arab nation in its immoralized battle, 


So Al-Fatah is your day of celebration and your revolution, the revolution of all the 
Arabs, similarly the Arab Gulf, the UAE, Qatar, (?Biahrain) and Kuwait is for all the 
Arabs their wall and shield, What happens to it happens to us, (?It) harms this wall 
it harms us all and its strength is our strength as much as Al-Fatah is your nominal 
day. The steadfastness of Jordan is the steadfastness of the Arab nation and the long 
front guarded by Jordan (?is) the front of the Arab nation, For instance what does the 
name "Umm Qais" which is carried by Libyan tanks during the parade mean to you? This 
means that "Umm Qais" height is for all the [word indistinct] as a rock and a defence 
position against the enemy, 


Syria and Egypt were the forces of confrontation in the north and the south, but after 
As Sadat had pulled out, an Arab position has fallen, this affected the Arab nation and 
we all lost this position, 


The steadfastness of Syria in the northern front is an advanced position for the Arab 
nation, 


We wanted the south and the north of Yemen to be united, because this unity is for the 
Arab nation as much as it is for the Yemeni people and a step on the path of unity. 


As much as the above mentioned places possessed by the Arab and all are national matters, 
AlFatah with your presence became yours and a national day of the Arab nation, 


The presence of these symbolic units shows the Arab strength and unity. So we want to 
reply to the world that we are not ina weak position, but why did As-Sadat surrender? 
Are we short of strength? No, we are not, We inspected the Arab force during the 
celebration of 10th anniversary of the Great First of September Revolution, Again, are 
we short of man or material forces? No, we are not, But we also possess oil and 
strength so there is no justification for capitulation and why should the nation 
surrender? This is the Arab strength and unity, 


In 1948, 1967 and 1973 all the Arabs from the [word indistinct] to the Gulf took part 
in the wars, from Kuwait, Iraq, [word indistinct] Algeria and Libya, were the tanks, 
fighters, cannons and soldiers, The gunboats and the 155-mm cannons crossing the Suez 
Canal were Libyan and the first artillary to fire was Libyan too, 


So the Arabs are capable of mobilizing such force when it is needed, hence what is 
the justification for the capitulation? Is there any reason for one country or more 
than one to surrender?

The Palestinian people's struggle is escalating day after day, the (?Israelis) are living 
in fear because of the fedayeen. The Arab nation possesses will for sacrifice, and has 
girls like Dalal Mughrabi who sacrificed her life. The (?Israelis) are also afraid of 
the coaches because they think that a Palestinian has implemented in it an explosive 
device. So the enemies are not in a stronger position to make us surrender like As-Sadat. 
They are living in fear because of the Palestinian fedayeen, any move by the Palestinian 
frightens them. They are even frightened of their shadows, thinking it is a Palestinian. 


Hence this nation has no justification whatsoever for capitulation. It also rejects it. 
It carries out heroic actions and sacrifices martyrs as mich as it had sacrificed one 
million martyrs in Algeria. This nation that sacrificed one million martyrs in a part 
of its territories will never submit, kneel or be beaten by the enemies. 


The Yemen was part of Britain and they believed she will remain, but look today where 
Britain is, and Yemen is democratic, popular and side by side with the Arab armies. She 
had also sacrificed many martyrs in order to remain an Arab country. 


The battle in Libya lasted for twenty years, during which three-quarters of a million 
were sacrificed as martyrs. 


In the past, how many invaders we defeated such as the Crusaders, Mongolians, and the 
Tartars. Then a man like As-Sadat came along preaching for capitulation. But the 
Palestinians are capable and have the will to fight and liberate the Arab nation which 
is backing them. So what is the justification for capitulation. 


We have fedayeen who drink blood, so why should we surrender. I am saying this to the 

Arab armed forces, the vanguard of the Arab masses, implying that there is no justification 
for capitulation, neither on the Jordanian front nor on the Syrian, the Lebanese fronts 
nor in the occupied West Bank of Gaza Strip, even in Egypt there is no justification for 
capitulation. 


We are very optimistic about the future of this nation due to our potential of which we 
built the civilisation in the past. We are also optimistic about the liberation, unity, 

the fall of the defeated prophets and the liberation of Egypt in order to take her position 
among the Arab nation and finally the return of our beloved Palestine. This optimism is 

not emotional but based on material facts possessed by the Arab nation for unity, liberation 
and the defeat of the enemy. 


Our presence in this celebration of the 10th anniversary of the Great First of September 
Revolution is a materialistic proof of the Arab nation's capability for unity. Forward. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES BULGARIAN DEFENSE MINISTER 


1D051056 Tripoli JANA in English 0929 GMI 5 Sep 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, September 5, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader 
of the Great First of September Revolution, received yesterday the Bulgarian minister of 
national defence, currently visiting the Jamahiriyah at the head of his country's 
delegation to the Libyan Arab people's celebrations of the 10th anniversary of the 

Great First of September Revolution. The meeting was attended by Brigadier Abu Bakr 
Yunis Jabir.
