LIBYA

LIAISON BUREAU REFUSES U.S. PROTEST NOTE ON DOGFIGHT 


LD210020 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 20 Aug 81 


[Text] The Libyan People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison refused to receive a so-called 
protest note delivered by the ambassador of the Belgian Kingdom to the Jamahiriyah on 
behalf of the U.S. Administration, since the Belgian Embassy represents American interests 
in Libya. 


The bureau refused to accept the note on the grounds that such a protest is totally 
rejected and is open to no discussion because the party committing the aggression against 
the freedom of the Libyan people is the United States, whose 6th Fleet committed a 
flagrant aggression against two Libyan planes carrying out routine reconnaissance flights 
within the Libyan Arab airspace and over its territorial waters, 


The JANA political editor writes: It is impossible to accept any excuse from an aggressor, 
particularly since this aggressor is the United States, which has proven by its aggression 
against the two Libyan planes that it practices organized international terrorism and 
carries out subversive activities which shake the foundation of peace and undermine the 
future of the peoples struggling for freedom. 


The JANA political editor affirms that the attempt at camouflaging the facts cannot 
convince us or world public opinion that the aggressor is a docile lamb. Thus, it is 
only logical to reject thisprotest by which the United States is trying to cover up its 
open and condemned aggression against Libya. 


INVOLVED PILOTS' 'INTERVIEW' ON 19 AUG DOGFIGHT 


LD201404 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 20 Aug 81 


{"Interview" with two Libyan pilots and a Libyan military official involved in the 19 
August Gulf of Sidra incident, by a JANA correspondent on 19 August; all participants 
unidentif ied--recorded ] 


{Text] [First pilot] At 0712 we were carrying out a reconnaissance mission as usual inside 
our territorial waters, in order to protect our shores and to be on the lookout for any 
hostile forces. We carried out the operation under the supervision of the officer in 

charge of the air defense, continuing along our ordinary route over our territorial waters 

in the Gulf of Sidra. Suddenly we were informed by the officer in charge of the air defense 
that there were air, naturally military, targets moving toward us. In actual fact, we got 
ready for these targets in the direction of 030 from the Sidra Town. We were able to observe 
the targets: They were U.S. F-14 aircraft, Tomcats. 


When these targets reached us they engaged us suddenly, despite the fact that they were 
inside our territorial waters. I retaliated to this act of aggression and downed one of 
the U.S. aircraft--as we said, an F-14. I fired a missile--naturally an air-to-air missile-- 
at the aircraft. The aircraft went down. I was certain of its downing, as it was on fire 
and I saw it crash into the sea. As I have pointed out, this indicates that the American 
aircraft were bent on carrying out an act of aggression on the Jamahiriyah. They inter- 
cepted us while we were carrying out a reconnaissance mission. They intercepted us inside 
our territorial waters, and they did not stop at that: They attacked the formation. One 
of the pilots in the American formation, which consisted of eight aircraft--two aircraft 
dived on us and the rest, six aircraft, guarded them at various altitudes. [sentence as 
heard] As Libyan pilots, we replied to this aggression so that we could prove to the U.S. 
that it is nothing and that the Libyan people and pilots can check any U.S. aggression and 
stand as equals against the U.S. pilots and U.S. fleets which fill all parts of the world.

REACTION ON DOGFIGHT WITH U.S. PLANES REPORTED 


Envoy in UAE . 
JN201443 Abu Dhabi EMIRATES NEWS AGENCY in Arabic 1345 GMT 30 Aug 81 


[Text] Abu Dhabi, 20 Aug (ENA)--A Libyan official has said his country will use the oil 
weapon against the United States in response to its policy, which he described as seeking 
to undermine the revolution in the Libyan Jamahiriyah. 


Muhammad al-Qammudi, secretary of the Libyan Brotherhood Bureau in the UAE, said at a 
press conference he held here this afternoon that the confrontation of two Libyan aircraft 
by the U.S. Air Force [as received] yesterday was part of a U.S. plan to plant military 
bases in the Arab homeland and undermine the role of Libya, which is opposing this plan. 
He said that the U.S. plan also seeks to bring the Sidra region, which is the most 
geographically productive oil location, under U.S. control. 


He said that part of the U.S. maneuvers took place inside Libyan territorial waters and that 
this constitutes a violation of Libyan national sovereignty and a provocation against 

the Libyan Jamahiriyah. Such a provocation endangers international peace and security and 
contravenes the principles of the United Nations. 


V. 21 Aug 81 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


He affirmed that his country reserves the right to take all measures that it deems necessary 
to safeguard its legitimate rights. 


The secretary of the Libyan Brotherhood Bureau emphasized his country's rights over the 
territorial waters, extending 200 miles. He explained that part of the maneuvers took 
place approximately 38 km inside the Sidra Gulf and that another part of the maneuvers 
took place within the Sidra Gulf inside the restricted training area belonging to the 
Libyan Air Force. 


He added that the conflict on this issue is not new and that his country had submitted a 
protest to the United Nations on this issue 3 years ago. 


He said that the U.S. maneuvers contravene the Libyan declaration issued on 19 October 

1973, stating that the Sidra Gulf, located within Libyan territory, and its eastern, western 
and southern land boundaries and its northern boundary at a latitude of 32 degrees are 
considered an inseparable part of Libyan territory, and that the gulf is under its complete 
sovereignty because it is considered internal waters. He said that the declaration was 
conveyed to all countries and international organizations at the time. 


Al-Qammudi revealed that the United States had officially asked his country to stop sup- 
porting the PLO and the other liberation movements in the world.

LD210555 [Editorial Report] Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic at 2205 GMT on 20 
August carriers an l-hour program entitled "The Meeting of the Week" in which 3 prominent 
Libyans are invited to talk on the question of U.S.-Libyan relations. The three men 

are Dr Muhammad al-Jarrari, of the Libyan jihad center; Dr Muhammad al-Madani al-Hudayri, 
secretary of the Popular Committee at the Pedagogical College at Sabhah; and Dr Khalifah 
al-'Azzabi, member of the Administrative Committee for Revolutionary Information and 
secretary of the External Information Branch. 


Al-Jarrari spoke on the history of relations between the United States and Libya since 
the 19th century. 


Al-Hudayri spoke about the roots of the conflict between the United States and the Libyan 
Arab people. He said that because of their very nature, the two had to be hostile to 
each other. He said that "in view of this nature of relations between the United States 
and Libya, we expect the United States to use all means at its disposal and take all this 
into consideration. We are fully prepared to face up to the United States." He added 
that the United States began by attacking Al-Qadhdhafi, thinking that he was one thing 
and the Libyan people were another who care nothing about him. But the Libyans are not 
like the Americans--Reagan enjoys a link with the American people only proportional to 
the number of votes he obtained. 


Al-Hudayri adds: "If we were to measure the level of conflict between the United States 
and the Jamahiriyah, we could not measure it by the level of military power. For if 

we were to view things from this angle, it would be impossible to compare the 

military power of the United States with that of the Libyan Arab people. The measure, 
then, should be that of the principle of people's self-defense. The Libyan people act 
in support of a principle. They are motivated by their right to freedom, justice and 
the freedom to support freedom everywhere." 


He said that though the United States has begun with Libya, if it were left to continue 
it would attack others, and it aims at destroying ali sparks of revolution in the Arab 
and African worlds and elsewhere in the world. 


"By their faith, the justice of their cause and their right to stand honorably against all 
those who try to subjugate them to their domination," he added, "the Libyan Arab people 
see no bogey in the United States, even if it is headed by Reagan, who is stubborn. For 
if Reagan's stubbornness is made of iron, the firmness of our faith is made of steel. 

Let the United States make a move and it will see in front of it steadfastness and con- 
frontation based on faith, the people and values of struggle, whichare not deterred by 
military hoardes, whoever they may be." 


Al-'Azzabi talks about the follies committed by the United States and its resort to 
plotting and assassination. He says the conflict between the United States and Libya 
is an ideological one. The United States dislikes Libya's support for the struggle 
in Latin America. He says that there are 21 Zionist advisers in the State Department, 
8 in the Defense Department and 5 in the National Security Council, and thus Zionism 
dominates the United States.
