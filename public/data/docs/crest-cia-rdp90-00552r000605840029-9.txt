Summary (AI): A Washington Post article from 30 November 1981 reports that Douglas Schlacter, an aide to former CIA agents Edwin Wilson and Frank Terpil, surrendered at Dulles Airport after being indicted for allegedly coordinating explosives shipments and terrorist training for Qaddafi's Libya. The story is about U.S. prosecutions, with little on Libya itself.
• Wilson and Terpil were indicted in April 1980 on charges of exporting explosives, delayed-action timers and night vision equipment to Libya.
• Schlacter reportedly ran an air freight service in Bujumbura, Burundi.
• Schlacter could face more than 25 years in prison if convicted.
• CIA officials maintained the agency neither knew nor approved of Wilson's and Terpil's operations in Libya.
