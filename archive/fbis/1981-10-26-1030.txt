LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ON RELATIONS WITH U.S., ITALY, SUDAN 


LD232050 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 23 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 23 Oct (JANA) -- Italian television, Channel One, has conducted an 
interview with the leader of the revolution which it will show tonight. In the interview 
the leader spoke about Libyan-U.S. relations and the hostile U.S. stand toward the people 
of the Jamahiriyah and its constant threats to harm the freedom of our people. He 
explained it was not Libya which decided on hostility toward the United States or to 
boycott it; it is the United States which does not want Libya to be a free state. 

To the United States, peoples are either slaves or enemies; it does not accept the 
existence of friends or neutrals; you are its slave and under its foot or you become 

its enemy if you refuse to accept slavery. We refuse to be agents of the United States 
or of anybody else. The United States has classified us as enemies because we have 
rejected its bondage. We have frequently and repeatedly declared that we are prepared 

to accept normal and sound relations with the United States, just as with any other 
country in the world -- relations that bring about ideal benefits for the two countries 
and which are based on mutual respect. If the United States wants to establish relations 
with us, it must accept us with our views, principles and attitudes unchanged. We 

reject any conditional relationship which alters our policy and direction or harms our 
choices and convictions. We are sure we are right and our policy is the right policy, 
but the United States is an imperialist country which does not like our policy and wants 
to colonize the world and us -- rather it wants to enslave us, and we reject this 
categorically. 


He said we adhere to our policy and beliefs and we believe there is a possibility for 
dialogue, there being many areas in which differences could be resolved, but not by this 
stupid method or terrorist style which U.S. President Reagan is pursuing, which depends 
on threats of force and the parading of fleets and on the forcing of others to become 
slaves for the United States, or woe unto them. 


The leader described this imperialist course which Reagan is imposing on international 
relations as a wrong course representing a childish policy whose main protagonist 
suffers from megalomania. The leader renewed the readiness to open a dialogue with the 
United States and said we are prepared to enter into a dialogue and discussion with the 
United States and also to exchange diplomatic representation with it and to maintain 
economic interests of mutual benefit to both peoples, in which there would be mutual 
respect. With these words we challenge the United States before Italian, European, 
American and world public opinion to accept sound relations free of threats of force 
and without harming our policy and also without having to agree to change this policy. 


The United States wants only to enslave others and if the latter refuse they are 
classified as enemies; this is what happened in our case. Anyway we are prepared to 
enter into a dialogue with the United States on several issues: the Mediterranean, 
the Middle East, the bilateral problem, the United Nations, the International Court 
of Justice and the Law of the Sea Conference. 


He added that using force to settle disputes should be a remote thing because it is a 
dangerous venture which threatens all the civilization which mankind has built in 
modern times, and it is bound to take the new world back to the age of barbarism and 
the jungle, where mankind would lose the civilization and the culture it has built. 
The United States will be responsible for this if it insists on using force to 

settle disputes. 


The leader said the world has only to compare my words on these subjects and the issues 
which we express with conviction and the U.S. policy, which is based on the threat 

of force and imposition of domination, to know who the terrorist is and who threatens 
peace and who is barbaric. He stressed that should the United States confront us 
militarily we are determined to confront it.
