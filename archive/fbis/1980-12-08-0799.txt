LIBYA

TRIPOLI REPORTS ON U.S. REPLY TO PROTEST 


LD061322 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 6 Dec 80 


[Text] The Arab Libyan People's Bureau in Washington has received a reply from the U.S. 
State Department to the protest memorandum submitted by the foreign liaison office about 
the U.S.-Egyptian maneuvers which took place on the borders of the Jamahiriyah last 
November. The U.S. reply says that the Egyptian president a number of times sought 
permission from the U.S. government to attack Libya but the United States in each instance 
was in no position to give permission to the Egyptian president to launch an attack against 
Libya. The U.S. State Department has admitted, in its reply to the protest memorandum, 
that U.S. aircraft on a number of accasions violated Libyan airspace during the maneuvers 
in the zone marked by latitudes 30 to 32 degrees. 


JANA REPORTS EMERGENCY IN SOMALI ARMED FORCES 


LD051612 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 5 Dec 80 


[Text] Mogadishu, 5 Dec (JANA)--Reports coming from Somalia have stated that a state of 
emergency has been declared within the ranks of the Somali Armed Forces and that an 
extensive campaign of arrests took place in the ranks of the army after a mutinous move- 
ment within the ranks of the Somali Air Force was uncovered 1 day before the October 
celebrations. These reports have stressed that this movement which is supported by a 
number of other Somali military units, sought to overthrow the regime of Siad Barre. 

The reports speak of sharp differences between Siad Barre and his defense minister after 
this movement was uncovered and the measures that followed it. 


It is known that Somalia is currently living in a state of increasing popular turmoil 
against Barre's regime, which has introduced American military bases to the country. 

A number of popular uprisings occurred in the country against this regime led by the 
National Salvation Front and its revolutionary committees spread out through all sectors 
and localities of the country. 


AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES STUDENTS ON 'MOBILIZATION' 


LD0O71640 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 7 Dec 80 


[Text] The brother leader has reaffirmed that the real solution in confronting the 
imperialist onslaught against the Arab nation is the realization of immediate unity and 
the establishment of a military force to confront the military interference in the Arab 
land. 


At his meeting this morning with the students of the Al-Fatih and Qar Yunis Universities 
by means of closed circuit, the leader reaffirmed that the Arab nation should mobilize 
its forces in confronting its enemy in order to defeat the United States and regain the 
Arab nation's prestige. He said that the true direction for this mobilization is to 
introduce military training in the schools, institutes and colleges because students 

are a living force capable of fighting. The leader pointed out that the introduction of 
military training in schools does not mean the halting of education and science. But we 
have to have the textbook in one hand and the weapon in the other in order to strike a 
happy medium between the textbook and the weapon. 


The leader reaffirmed anew that the introduction of military training in schools and 
colleges is to make the people armed in readiness to launch the counterattack against 
U.S. bases. The leader added that a comprehensive mobilization of all living forces 
underway to face the interference, and that the battle is the battle of the entire Arab 
nation because imperialism aims at all the Arabs. The brother leader also met this 
morning with Arab students studying at Qar Yunis University. 


Vv. 8 Dec 80 ISRAEL Nl 


DECISION ON WEST BANK MAYORS, REACTION REPORTED 


TA051238 Jerusalem Domestic Service in Hebrew 1100 GMT 5 Dec 80 


[Text] We will now go over to the prime minister's office, where the prime minister, with 
a ministerial team and others have been consulting for more than 3 hours about the Supreme 
Court's decision to rediscuss the affair of the deportees. We will go over to Shim'on 
Schiffer and the news conference now beginning: 


[Schiffer] The ministerial consultation headed by the prime minister ended less than an 
hour ago and now we are here, in the conference room of the prime minister's office. 

Justice Minister Moshe Nisim will bring us the decisions from the ministerial consultations. 
Mr Justice Minister, please: 


{[Nisim] Well, gentlemen, as you know, yesterday the high court of justice issued its 
decision on the deportation of the Hebron and Halhul mayors. The court, by a majority de- 
cision, judged that it had not found any legal fault in what had been recommended and what 
had been decided. Thus, the deportation order, of course, is valid. 


At the end of presiding Judge Landau's verdict, there was a recommendation to the 
political echleons--which are capable of considering the question from all sides as the 
verdict said--to reexamine the decision. The prime minister summoned a meeting of the 
ministerial committee for security affairs for today to discuss the Supreme Court 
recommendation because of the respect the government accords this important institution. 


The ministerial committee for security affairs discussed the court's verdict which was, as 

I said, handed down yesterday, regarding the deportation order issued against those two 
mayors. The committee heard the opinions of the defense elements and, after the committee 
had considered all the aspects of security and public safety, it came to the conclusion that 
the deportation order was to be implemented. I would like to add that by doing this, by 
meeting today to discuss the Supreme Court recommendation, the government accorded the 
recommendation full respect. I might add that it was a prolonged, in-depth and thorough 
discussion. There was no alternative for the ministerial committee for security affairs 
other than to make the decisionit did because it was faced with important security and public 
safety considerations, issues that were the basis of the calculation when it was decided-- 
first, to issue the deportation order and, second, to maintain the order following the 
appearance of the two mayors before the recommendations committee. As you know, the com- 
mander of the Judaea and Samaria area decided to implement the order in accordance with the 
advisory committee's recommendations. 


[Schiffer] Mr Justice Minister, when will the deportation order be implemented? When will 
the mayors be expelled from Israel and were political factors and data, which could affect 
this order, also taken into account in today's debate? 


[Nisim] The implementation is in the hands of the area commander. The ministerial com- 
mittee did not discuss a specific moment or any time frame and this did not enter into the 
discussions at all. All it had to decide was whether to implement the order or not. The 
date, the time--all this--is handed over to the commander of Judaea and Samaria. As for 
your second question, the State of Israel is in a situation in which its security con- 
siderations must be decisive and must also take precedence over political considerations. 

We felt among ourselves that this issue of the deportation of the two mayors was by way 

of being a necessity to maintain security. I am convinced that this is also for the benefit 
of the population, when the ability to incite it to actions against the public security 

will undoubtedly be weaker without the presence of those two mayors.
