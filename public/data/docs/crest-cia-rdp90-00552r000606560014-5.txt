Summary (AI): A 1982 Washington Post article reports that a federal grand jury in Alexandria indicted Robert Manina, a Canadian business associate of former CIA agent Edwin Wilson, for illegally exporting night vision equipment to Libya. Sources say the equipment was used by the Libyan military.
• 1976: Company won a contract to supply night vision equipment to Libya, which Manina said was for civilian protection of oil fields.
• 1977-08: Low-light-level television camera with silicon intensifier tube shipped to Canada and then to Libya without required US export licenses.
• 1979-05: Manina's Jaguar car was blown up in the driveway of his home in Kitchener, Ontario.
• 1980-10: Assault on an exiled Qaddafi opponent in Fort Collins, Colorado.
