LIBYA

Al-OQadhdhafi Message Condemns Vittel Summit 
LD171534 Tripoli JANA in English 1422 GMT 17 Oct 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Muharram | 1, Oct 17, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY The leader of the revolution sent messages to a 
number of African heads of state who took part in Vittel summit 
conference in France. The text of the messages is as follows 


“While I laud the African peoples in general and we are one 
and praise the struggle history of your people for freedom and 
liberation from colonialism... | would like to refer again to the 
meetings called on by France and gather the French speaking 
people, the intruding language, and language of the colonialists 
who imposed it on the expense of the local African languages and 
the African civilization, culture, and heritage, and through it, 
colonialists tried to partition Africa into French speaking, Eng- 
lish speaking, Spanish speaking, and even Portuguese speaking 
nations. We in the Libyan Jamahiriyah still have unshaken belief 
that such meetings are of no benefit to Africa which gets nothing 
out of it but new restrictions and new times which put it, willingly 
or unwillingly, in the colonialist fold 


The direct colonialist role of France and its dark history are still 
remembered by the past generation and are still evident in the 
backwardness, poverty, illiteracy, and diseases left behind by the 
colonialists. The meetings of African heads of state with the 
French head of state, who sees himself no friend to any but on 
the contrary he sees his self as master, emperor, and big chief, 
are meaningless and of no value except to France and the least 
damage they can do to us Africans is dividing Africa. Mitterrand 
himself admits that one of the main objectives of the conference 
was to bolster France's political image in Africa and the world 
and not, as a result, for the interest of Africz and the Africans 
When such summit achieves nothing to her, France stari un- 
ashamedly calling it through its media “boring discussions” and 
the “summut for nothing
