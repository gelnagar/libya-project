• Churba said Qaddafi's oil wealth helped finance a new airstrip on Grenada.
• Churba said Qaddafi had become a destabilizing force in Latin America and Africa and the Mideast.
• Asked about assassination, Churba said it should definitely be considered as one of many alternatives, while denying he meant a CIA assassination.
• Churba called the 1983 policy shift the work of long-time bureaucrats who had 'made book on Libya' for 20 to 25 years.
• 1971: Libyan officers reportedly approached the American Embassy seeking support for an overthrow of Qaddafi; the information was reportedly handed to Qaddafi and the men were eliminated (per Churba's claim).
