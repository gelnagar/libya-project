LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI CONDEMNS FRENCH 'COLONIALISM! IN AFRICA 
LD252222Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1800 GMT 25 May 78 LD 


[Text] Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi met hundreds of officers and cadets of the military 
academy this morning on the occasion of the 15th anniversary of the OAU. Addressing 
them on this anniversary and on its significance to Africa, he said the anniversary comes 
while the African Continent is being subjected to an armed imperialist onslaught by 
foreign armies, 


He referred to the meeting of the heads of French-speaking African states in Paris with 
President Giscard d'Estaing, stressing that the meeting was held at a time when the 
anniversary of the OAU is being observed in the continent--half of which still consists 
of French colonies. Referring to the statement which followed the meeting and which 
proclaimed the emergence of an alliance of French-speaking countries led by France, he 
emphasized that this concept will be resisted by the African people with all the strength 
they possess, because the alliance means that half of Africa will become an open field 
for French hegemony. It also means the return of French colonialism to its former 
colonies, He said that the allianze has gone beyond economic and political colonialism 
to reach military colonialism effected by the forces of France, Al-Qadhdhafi added that 
the alliance, if it materializes, will split Africa into two parts, one colonized and 
the other free, He stressed that this idea will be met by a military alliance by the 
free progressive and uncolonized states in the continent, 


Al-Qadhdhafi explained that the alliance will not achieve security and stability for the 
continent as alleged in the Paris statement. Indeed, it will insure one thing, the 
reestablishment of French colonialism in the African Continent, which will undermine the 
security and peace cf the continent. Therefore, it will be necessary to destroy this 
alliance and reliberate Africa. To achieve liberaticn, generations of Africans will 
offer themselves for this cause, 


Commenting on the Jamahiriyah's attitude towards Egypt, Al-Qadhdhafi said that the 
Jamahiriyah is still awaiting Egypt's reply to the call made in his speech in Tobruk, 
on the occasion of the commemoration of the withdrawal of the British forces, for a 
meeting between the commanders of the armed forces of the two countries to draw up a 
plan for the liberation of Sinai and the occupied Arab territory. 


He emphasized that any other meeting without Egypt's return to the path of struggle will 
be impossible, He added that for many reasons Egypt and the Jamahiriyah need no third 
party to intervene between them, tecause they quarrel directly and reach agreement directly.
