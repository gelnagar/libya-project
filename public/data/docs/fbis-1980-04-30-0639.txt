LIBYA

TRIPOLI LAUDS NEW TUNISIAN GOVERNMENT LED BY MZALI 
LD252041 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 25 Apr 80 


{Excerpts] The formation of the new government announced in Tunis yesterday has stimulated 
attention by observers. The most important thing in this formation is the final fall of 
Hedi Nouira, the fallofhis government and his supporters. Observers have noted the 

fall of Hedi Nouira, Mohamed Ali Souissi, Dhaoui Hanablia, Mustafa Zaanouni, Amor Rourou, 
Slaheddine Ben Mbarek and Moncef Ben Za'wan [last name as heard]. Moreover the removal 

of Mohamed Sayah, the minister delegate to the prime minister and director of the party, 

is considered by observers as an affirmation of the desire to turn a page of a past 
characterized by violence and conflict, and he has been replaced by Mongi Kooli. 


Commenting on this change, the JANA political editor states: Libya hopes that the new 
government of Mohamed Mzali, which rose on the ruins of the collapsed government of Hedi 
Nouira, will work for the restoration of the good relations with its neighbors, particularly 
with the Socialist People!s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, and that this government will make 
efforts to bury the rancor created, nurtured and caused by Hedi Nouira, who is considered 

a sumbol of hatred and who dishonored the signature of President Habib Bourguiba on the 
Djerba Declaration and cancelled measures for holding a referendum to enforce the 

provisions of this declaration as a result of his blind hatred which knows no limit. 


The political editor went on to state that the arrival of Mohamed Mzali, who enjoys 
appreciation and backing from all the various currents in Tunisia and who is a pioneer 
in the policy of Arabization, a writer and a thinker, opens a new era which we hope will 
contribute to the restoration of tranquillity between the two countries--tranquillity 
shattered by the policy of Nouira--and to putting the Djerba Declaration into force. 


JALLUD RECEIVES PAKISTAN'S AGHA SHAHI 


LD262044 Tripoli JANA in Mmglish 1620 GMT 26 Apr 80 


(Text ] Tripoli, April 26, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Staff-Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud 
received Agha Shahi, the personal envoy and the Pakistani president's adviser on foreign 
affairs. The meeting, which took place at Al-Chaab Palace, was attended by the foreign 
secretary. Mr Shahi arrived in Tripoli yesterday on a visit to the Jamahiriyah.
