Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery note dated 22 January 1982 reports the first identification of SA-8 surface-to-air missile equipment in Libya, with three SA-8 transporter-erector-launcher-radars parked at a SAM training facility on the eastern outskirts of Tripoli. The document gives coordinates 32-51-45N 013-25-00E.
• SA-8 SAM equipment was identified for the first time in Libya at the Tarabulus military installation.
• Three SA-8 transporter-erector-launcher-radars (TELAR) were parked at the SAM training facility.
• The equipment is probably part of 16 SA-8 TELAR and six SA-8 resupply vehicles observed at Nikolayev port facilities, USSR.
• Part of this shipment possibly went to Syria, where three SA-8 TELAR were reportedly observed on the Lattakia-Tartus road.
• Coordinates: 32-51-45N 013-25-00E.
• 1982-01-16: SA-8 equipment identified for the first time in Libya at Tarabulus military installation, east-southeast of the city
