LIBYA

COMMUNIQUE ON U.S. PLOT PROMISES FEDAYEEN ACTION 


LD132146 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1855 GMT 13 Aug 81 
("Important communique" issued by the Free Unionist Officers on 13 August] 


{Text] Tripoli, 13 Aug (JANA)--At this time when the complete truth about American 
terrorism, which has derived its history from the pools of blood and the corpses of the 
victims of humanity and the free people, is being unveiled; and at a time when American 
policy appears naked with the announcement of the conspiracy against the leader of the 
Great 1 September Revolution as a final solution to its enduring complex caused 

by this revolution and its leader and masses; and during these moments in which the 
triumph of the era of the masses is being confirmed, the Free Union Officers have rallied 
from the premise of their revolutionary responsibilities and in confirmation of their 
strength and firmness, derived from the leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, and declared the 
following: 


1. Thereaching of the leader and thinker Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi means very precisely 
the penetration of a huge wall of our bodies and skulls, and plotting against his life 
means plotting against all the elements of good and freedom. 


2. The leader does not stand alone in the field of challenge; we are all Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, the great hope that has risen in the life of humanity to pave its road in 
confronting the forces of evil represented by the leader of world terrorism, America. 


3. This intrigue thrown by the United States as the final card in its mad game against 
the Socialist People's LibyanArab Jamahiriyah is very primitive and came very late as ~ 
we have taken him as a symbol of our struggle and a leader of our march toward good, 
development and love and because he is a glow in the hearts of the toiling and struggling 
masses on earth and because Mu'ammat is not the soul and body; rather he is [words 
indistinct] and dogma absorbed by the depths of humanity since the dawn of the Great 1 
September Revolution. 


4. The truth of this revolutionary is stronger than all conspiracies and it is capable, 
in all conditions, to turn upside down the calculations of all computers and all the 
hellish assessments which America adopts to contain the world. It is very stupid 
indeed for America to resort to such trivial thinking now in particular, after 12 years 
of revolutionary cohesion between the leader and his masses who believe in his thought 
as they now know that Mu'ammar is not a mere Libyan; rather he is thought to be the 
value of the struggle and the revelutionary seed growing forever, despite all storms 
and intrigues. 


5. By announcing this plot, America has openly exposed its leadership of worid terror, 
thereby setting a serious precedent which gives us the right to respond in the same style 
unless it retreats and apologizes officially. 


6. Despite the impossibility of implementing this crime, America shall be held responsible 
for the decision of death which we shall take in the biggest fedayeen operation, during 
which we shall hit at U.S. interests anywhere and undertake the physical liquidation 

of anyone who permits himself merely to think of harming the life of the leader of the 
Great 1 September Revolution, beginning with Ronald Reagan and ending with the 

smallest agent, whether in the Jamahiriyah or abroad. By confirming this openly we 

are adopting a historic stand in confronting all agent forces which think, -even if from 

a distance, of joining the banner of American terrorism.

7. We consider this plot to be a confirmation of the purity of the Great 1 September 
Revolution and its leader, of its pursuing the correct revolutionary line, and of the 
danger it constitutes to the forces of colonialism and reaction, and we consider it a 
direct condemnation of the weak who control the fate of the Arab people, and above 

all we consider it a badge of honor and a unique certificate of exoneration to be added 
to the record of the Great 1 September Revolution. 


God is great, the struggle continues, and the Great 1 September Revolution will last 
forever. 


[Signed] The Free Unionist Officers
