LIBYA

Government States Position on U.S. ‘Scheme’ 
LD220826 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 22 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli 22 Mar (JANA) — The People’s Committee of the 
People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison yesterday summoned the 
Arab ambassadors accredited to the Jamahiriyah. The commit- 
tee informed them that the Jamahiriyah strongly condemned the 
American military intervention in the Arab region and the 
language of muscle flexing and intimidation which is represented 
by sending the advanced warning and fighter aircraft to the Arab 
region. 


The committee also informed the Arab ambassadors that the 
Jamahiriyah rejects the aggressive statements by the officials of 
the American Administration and the statements of the heads of 
the Egyptian and Sudanese regimes, and it also strongly con- 
demns the military, political and information coordination 
between the American Administration and the Egyptian and 
Sudanese regimes. 


The People’s Committee for the People’s Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison explained the Arab ambassadors that the Jamahiriyah 
has called for the convening of the UN Security Council to look 
into the American aggressive scheme against the Jamahiriyah, 
stressingthat the Jamahiriyah will adopt all the necessary mea- 
sures to defend itself and that the Jamahiriyah has firmly 
rejected the American threatening memorandum. 


The committee pointed out in its explanation that what happened 
in Omdurman is nothing but an excuse for intervention in the 
Sudan by the colonialist countries in order to make it a forward 
base for the aggression against the Libyan Arab people. The 
People’s Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison 
called on the Arab countries and the Arab peoples to shoulder 
their full responsibilities in combating the American aggression 
in order to preserve the security, freedom and dignity of the Arab 
nation.

Tripoli Conference Reviews Sudan Situation 
LD220753 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0710 GMT 22 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli 22 Mar (JANA) — At the Permanent Secretariat 
of the Arab People’s Conference in Tripoli, a meeting has been 
held attended by the squads of the Sudanese patriotic movement 
and the members of the Arab People’s Conference from the 
political and trade union organizations and the liberation move- 
ments. The situation in the Sudan was reviewed. The conferees 
examined the deterioration of the internal situation in the Sudan 
and the ways in which the Numayri regime commits aggression 
against the Sudanese people and its ancient democratic and 
patriotic traditions and against its Arab and African relations by 
throwing itself into the arms of imperialism, the stable David 
policy, and by making Sudan a (?station) for imperialism and 
reaction. 


JANA: Shultz Issues ‘Flagrant Threat’ 
LD220959 Tripoli JANA in English 0844 GMT 22 Mar 84 


[Text] Washington, Jumada Thani 18, Mar 22, JAMAHIR- 
IYAH NEWS AGENCY — George Shultz, the American 
secretary of state, sent a flagrant threat with aggression against 
Libya in the context of his frantic decision of sending AWACS 
planes to Egypt in addition to other support planes. He 
announced his desire in a press conference held in Washington 
yesterday to help America’s friends and allies in the Middle East 
and the Horn of Africa and to counteract Libya. The American 
secretary of state expressed the American Administration’s 
alarm because of the mounting Libyan role in rejecting American 
domination and described Libyan behavior as falling outside any 
recognizable norms. The American secretary of state's 
announcements bore naked threats to flex American military 
muscles against Libya following America’s admission of failure 
of its foreign policy and its defeat in the Lebanon. 


Government Refuses To Accept U.S. Message 
LD211540 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1505 GMT 21 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 Mar (SANA) — The People’s Committee of 
People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau has categorically refused to 
accept a(?verbal) memorandum from the U.S. State Department 
conveyed by the Belgian ambassador, who looks after U.S. 
interests in the Jamahiriyah, to the committee today. The com- 
mittee regards this memorandum as a threat by the United States 
to declare war on the Jamahiriyah on behalf of the Governments 
of Egypt and Sudan, which are incapable and have assigned the 
United States to look after their affairs. The committee requested 
the Belgian ambassador to convey to the U.S. Administration 
that the Jamahiriyah rejects the language of threats, use of force 
and muscle flexing, and alerts the world that actions among Arab 
countries, whatever their nature, are considered to be internal 
matters, that the artificial borders between Arab countries are 
the making of colonialism and that such a situation does not 
provide a pretext for any kind of interference in the region or in 
Arab affairs. The committee stressed that Libya has the right, 
on the basis of all international laws, to take any political and 
military measures to defend its security and secure its safety 
against any danger.
