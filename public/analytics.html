What is counted, and what is not

These are totals only. The Atlas does not record names, addresses, accounts or what anyone typed into a search box. A visitor is one device on one connection, counted by a scrambled code that cannot be turned back into an address; the same person on two devices counts twice, and two people sharing a connection and browser count once. Anything opened by fewer than five different people is left out, so a small number never points at one person. Pages about individual people and family reports are never listed. Sign-ins by the family are not counted.
