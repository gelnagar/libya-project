Summary (AI): A Washington Post column (17 February 1983) reports that Qaddafi made a secret phone call to Egyptian Defense Minister Abu Ghazala suggesting Libya and Egypt go to war against Israel, and that Ghazala rebuffed him. It presents Qaddafi as still seeking to destroy Israel and unite the Arab world, despite reports he was mellowing. Little about Tripoli itself.
• Ghazala was military attache at the Egyptian embassy in Washington from 1976 to 1980.
• Egypt and Libya fought a four-day border war in 1977.
• Qaddafi reportedly lived for a time in Sadat's home when they were close.
• Qaddafi reportedly indulged in public exultation after Sadat's assassination.
• 1983-02: Qaddafi secretly telephones Egyptian Defense Minister Abu Ghazala suggesting joint Libyan-Egyptian war against Israel; Ghazala refuses, saying Egypt has a peace treaty with Israel
• 1981-10-06: Assassination of Egyptian President Anwar Sadat; Ghazala was at Sadat's side and a grenade bounced off his head without exploding
