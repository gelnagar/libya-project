LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS LETTER ON NORTHERN IRELAND 


LD101034 Tripoli JANA in English 0945 GMT 10 Aug 81 


{Letter sent by Libyan Leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to UN Secretary General Kurt Waldheim; 
date not given] 


[Text] Tripoli, 10 Aug (JANA)--Mr. Waldheim, U.N. secretary general: 


I very much regret to draw your attention to a painful human tragedy. But it seems that 
we live in a world whose conscience is dead due to the repeated violent incidents, other- 
wise we could have said that the world [word indistinct] is shaken for the tragedy. But 
in fact it is not. 


I would like to draw your attention to the slow, heroic and tragic death series which every 
sixty days takes the life of the most heroic people of northern Ireland as a result of the 
British arrogance. 


Britain refuses to treat them as political prisoners although their status should be con- 
sidered so, because they are fighting for a just holy cause for the freedom of their people. 
Their people is one of the small peoples of the world, but they have a place in the sun and 
on Earth as they were created by God. 


In spite of the good relation between Britain and my country, I, as human being and a 
revolutionary and not as a politician, because I do not hold any political or administra- 
tive post, call upon you and urge you to intervene, and through you, I address the con- 
science of the United Nations in order to stop this tragic death series. Otherwise the 
price will be paid by all adamant and the onlookers and might be dearer and more painful. 
If we could not stop this tragedy, then history, the next generations and the conscience 
of humanity when it awakens, will curse the civilisation of today and consider it as a 
period of the dark ages and more racist and backward still. 


I, Mr Secretary General, raise my voice high in [word indistinct] protest against the 
British non-humanitarian code of conduct and condemn the world's indifference towards this 
humane issue irrespective of its just political aspect. I believe I voice the opinion of 
the unheard millions filing a document among UN documents for next generals that spares me 
personally and my country the condemnation of history. This is a document in defence of a 
group of human beings who are led to death at the rate of one every sixty days similar to 
the sad and horrible human sacrifices in the dark ages with the world raises but silence. 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 


BULGARIAN DEPUTY DEFENSE MINISTER ARRIVES 10 AUG

General Staff of the Bulgarian Army arrived in Tripoli at noon today. He was welcomed
