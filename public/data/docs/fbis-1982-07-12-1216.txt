LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RETURNS FROM TUNISIA VISIT 


LD102224 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 10 Jul 82 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution returned to Tripoli yesterday after concluding 
a working visit to Tunisia, during which he held talks with President Habib Bourguiba 
concerning the grave situation in Lebanon. 


The prcether leader and the Tunisian president affirmed the need to continue efforts to back 
the Palestinian Revolution and to stand alongside the Palestinian and Lebanese peoples in 
their struggle and opposition to the invasion. They affirmed the need for joint Arab action 
to defeat the aggression and that intensive efforts were being made in this connection. 


AL-QADHDHAFI: ZIONIST EXISTENCE ENDANGERS ARABS 


LD100942 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 10 Jul 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 10 Jul (JANA) -- The brother leader last Wednesday evening met with the 
male and female cadets of various military academies. He explained the dimensions of the 
current Arab situation and stressed its seriousness. He pointed out that the struggle 
against the Zionist enemy is a religious and pan-Arab struggle. The brother leader drew 
attention to the fact that the enemy is striving to seize the Arab homeland in stages, 
that the Arab nation is a force on this earth and that confronting the enemy is the only 
way to assert the Arab right to regain Palestine and to evict the Zionist invaders from 
Lebanon. 


The leader added that to preserve our existence we must prepare ourselves for all dangers. 
He said Zionist existence [wujud] endangers our own existence and therefore we should 

take action to surprise and destroy it before the eyes of the whole world because the 
Zionist existence [wujud] is taking our nation by surprise and the Zionists are striking 
at our targets, claiming they endanger their so-called security. Therefore, it is our 
right to strike at and destroy their targets.

The leader pointed out that unless we destroy the Zionist danger it will put an end to us. 
He stressed that this requires that the revolutionary forces in the Arab homeland should 
open the road leading to Palestine because this is the real strategic action that guarantees 
the liquidation of the Zionist danger. The leader reaffirmed the necessity of arming 
ourselves with all scientific and other means to consolidate our capabilities and strengthen 
our march to put an end to Zionist existence. He pointed out that the current situation in 
view of the Zionist existence's [wujjud] frenzied attack and its posing a growing danger to 
the Arab nation has become very serious and, therefore, we must inevitably wage a decisive 
historic battle in which we either perish or emerge victorious. The leader called for the 
realization of the fact that the continuation of Arab existence requires the elimination of 
regionalism, partnership, tribalism and the desire to stay in the seats of power. Power 
should be given to the masses because they are the only ones who sacrifice and die. 


He said it is inevitable that we should seriously think from now on about curbing all 
attitudes that prevent the unleashing of a hellish Arab action that accepts all challenges 
and dangers to save the Arab nation. We have every justification for carrying out such an 
action because the enemy wants to put an end to us and we must inevitably put an end to him 
before he puts an end to us. The leader pointed out that this requires a comprehensive 
mobilization of all our forces. The leader pointed out that the current Arab situation has 
encouraged the enemy to develop his expansionist plans and arrive at what he has arrived at 
so far. Even in his invasion of Lebanon, the enemy did not dream that he would be able to 
reach Beirut and throw a siege around it. The leader said the Zionists' aim is to subjugate 
the Arab nation and to make it recognize them and to impose their existence by force because 
they realize there is no possibility for them to reach an understanding with the Arabs and 
to make the latter recognize them on Arab land. Therefore, they have adopted the use of 
force as a means for compelling the Arabs to recognize them and to impose their existence 

on the Arabs. 


JANA CRITICIZES EGYPTIAN FOREIGN MINISTER 


LD100916 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 10 Jul 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 10 Jul (JANA) -- Whenever Libya is mentioned or something is said about 
Libya, the ugly voice of Butrus Ghali, foreign minister of the executed As-Sadat, descendant 
of a traitor and crusader who hates everything that is Arab and Muslim, can be heard uttering 
phrases full of hatred and malice against the Libyan Arab people, the Arab nation and 
Muslims everywhere. This crusader was one of the people who called for the capitulation 
that made Egypt kneel before and be biased in favor of the enemy; he negotiated with the 
enemy, to whom he belongs, against the Arab nations’ interests. He is still carrying out 
the same role without having learned a lesson from the fate of his treacherous grandfather 
who was executed by the Egyptian people or from the fate of his master As-Sadat who was 
publicly executed as punishment for his treason, to which Butrus Ghali contributed by 
planning and propagating it. However, we are confident this vagabond does not express the 
will of the Egyptian people and his fate will be similar to the fate of his grandfather and 
his master the traitor As-Sadat. We will show him.
