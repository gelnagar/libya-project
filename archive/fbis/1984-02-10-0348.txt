LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Urges Attack on U.S. Fleet 
LD091958 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1935 GMT 9 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 9 Feb (JANA) The brother leader of the 
revolution has sent a cable to Syrian President Hafiz Al-Asad 
and to Walid Junblatt, leader of the Lebanese National Salvation 
Front. It read: 


Unless the blatant American aggression against the Arab home- 
land comes to a halt, if the Security Council fails to shoulder its 
responsibilities, and if the American people prove incapable of 
curbing the madness of actor Reagan, we must all shoulder our 
responsibility to future generations and the world and use all the 
means of war that we possess to destroy the U.S. fleet in the 
Mediterranean, in self defense. 


JANA has learned that copies of this cable have been sent to all 
the serving Arab rulers. Copies have also been sent to the 
President and members of the Security Council, and to the U.S. 
Congress.

The Reagan administration is trying with gunboat policies to get 
out of the astounding defeat that the Lebanese nationalist forces 
have inflicted upon it in the Arab land of Lebanon. This appears 
to be the first time that an imperialist superpower seeks to 
disguise its defeat and the failure of its policies and aggressive 
plans by a cowardly and swift flight to sea. 


In order to save face over its flight from land to sea under the 
blows of strong resistance, the Reagan administration, as a state 
accustomed to war, has resorted to an intensive use of warships 
to strike from a distance the positions of the nationalist forces 
that have foiled all American calculations and strategies, and 
brought most of the Lebanese territory under its control. 


In the face of the heroic response to the American fleet, America 
has resorted to directing the guns of the New Jersey, the heaviest 
warship in the world, against unarmed civilians, killing and 
wounding dozens of women, children and old men.
