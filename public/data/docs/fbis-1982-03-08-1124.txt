LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SPEECH AT TRIPOLI STADIUM 5 MAR 


LD052126 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1835 GMT 5 Mar 82 


[Speech by Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhchafi at the opening of the 13th African soccer games at 
the 11 June Stadium in Tripoli on 5 March -- recorded] 


[Excerpts] I welcome you, African brothers, to Libya, the northern gate of Africa. 
[applause] Libya is the defender of Africa. It raises the slogans “Africa for the 
Africans" and “Africa has no alliance except with itself." Libya fights against 
imperialism and racism in defense of Africa. It is the one that brought peace to Chad. 
[applause] It is the one that fights shoulder to shoulder with the African liberation 
forces against the racist regime and against neocolonialism. [applause] 


We are extremely sorry to see civil war return to Chad. Blood has been flowing 
profusely and division has returned to Chad after the return home from there of the 
Libyan forves. 


Today, we challenge the enemies of Africa and the enemies of Chad, who have deceived 
Chad, to restore peace to Chad and to restore unity to shattered Chad, to restore life 
to hundreds and thousands of children who today are dying from starvati and sickness. 
It is a tragedy. But at the same time it confirms by its tragic episode. the truth 
about the Libyan role, which bolsters peace and freedom in the African Continent. 


We are the ones interested in peace in Chad. We are the ones interested in the 
triumph of freedom in Africa because we are an extension of Chad and Chad is an 
extention of us. We are Africans and we are part of Africa. Africa is our continent. 
Peace in Africa is our concern and the triumph of freedom in Africa also concerns us. 
[chants ]

Thus, and while we meet as Africans on Afro-Arab Libyan soil today at this friendly 
sporting event, we declare our challenge to the forces of imperialism and racism which 

are hostile to Africa and which today intervene between Libya and Africa and between Libya 
and the dearest and nearest of its African neighbors. 


We challenge them by saying that Africa is ours and we are Africans. When we fight for 
the freedom of Africa, we are actually defending ourselves. When we bolster peace in 
Africa, we will be bolstering our peace. When we fight alongside our African brothers, 
we do so because we, too, are Africans. 


But what excuse has a country like France to intervene or even speak about the problems 
of the African Continent. The problems of the African Continent, dear brothers, and 
guests... [sentence unfinished] We are happy to play host to you today on Libyan soil 
and your Libyan brothers are happy to meet you in order to express our sincerity toward 
our African cause and our African Continent. We unmask the truth about colonialism which 
is lying in wait for you and which is trying to disunite us. Brothers, the problems of 
Africa were caused by French colonialism, British colonialism and U.S. interference and 
the racist regimes which were planted in Africa. 


The problems from which Chad is suffering were caused by detested French colonialism. 

The problems from which Africa is suffering were caused by French and British colonialism. 
The military face of colonialism vanished, but colonialism has not yet completely vanished. 
The church in Africa is not an African church. It is a colonialist church. (?Its) 
language is not an African national language. It is a colonialist language. Economic 
activity in Africa is not African nationalist economic activity but instead colonialist, 
exploitationist and capitalist economic activity. 


France considers Africa its own plantation from where 75 percent of your resources go to 
feed the factories of France and allow the French people to live a comfortable life, whereas 
you, brother Africans, live in wretched hardship and terrible underdevelopment. This is 

the French vision of Africa, as a plantation, a mine for France. 


As for the United States, it regards Africa as a legacy left by the French after D'Estaing. 
It is trying today to dig in its poisoned claws and the U.S. (?dragon) wants to come to 
suck the blood of Africa. 


From here, we are prepared to resist this new and old evil because we are Africans and 
because we work for the unity of Africa and the liberation of Africa. The unity of 

Africa cannot be realized until the will of Africa is liberated. Its will cannot be 
realized except in the heart of the battle to liberate Africa. From here, brothers, we 
stand rigidly to defend Africa. We are the defenders of Africa. We are the ones who 
defend it. We are the gateway to Africa. [applause] We shoulder this responsibility with 
capability, no matter how difficult and heavy it may be. We destroy from the base all the 
U.S. and European justifications, which we do not believe in, by which they permit 
themselves to interfere in Africa. 


The French, the Americans and the racists must take their hands off Africa. Africa is 
for the Africans. They are filthy colonialists. They have kept Africa backward. They 
tore up Africa. They monopolized Africa. They caused all the political, economic 

and social problems from which Africa is suffering. We welcome you brothers, as Africans 
and brothers from our continent where there is a conflict between the racist regimes 
wanting to establish their foothold forever on the ruins of the black, indigenous 
inhabitants of Africa, and the vast majority of blacks despised by the racist regimes and 
regarded as slaves and guinea pigson whom they carry out medical experiments. In Africa, 
the conflict is taking place between the enemies of freedom and the peoples claiming the 
right to live freely in Africa.

Today, there is a conflict going on in Sudan between the Sudanese people and the hireling 
military and fascist regime which is proracist and colonialist. I regret very much that 
this regime imposes a blackout on a popular revolution waged by an African people, the 
Sudanese people, the racist services, the reactionary organs and the colonialist organs 
imposed a blackout on the revolution of the gallant Sudanese people whose sons are falling 
every day as an offering on the altar of deliverance from the military, fascist, dicta- 
torial, proracist and colonialist regime. But at this international gathering, we declare 
in the name of the Sudanese people that the revolution in Sudan will continue until the 
fascist regime falls, as the fascist regime which preceded it fell, under the feet of 

the Sudanese people. [applause] 


If the organs owned by the forces of colonialism, racism and reaction which are aligned 
to the fascist regime because it is a hireling and an antipeople regime in Sudan impose 
a blackout on the revolution of the Sudanese people, it is our duty to inform the world 
by announcing the details of the revolution of the Sudanese people, in which they are 
engaging today and tomorrow until the fascism and racism, which is procolonialist, and 
the proracist hirelings have fallen. 


We declare, brothers, that any intervention on the side of the collapsing fascist govern- 
ment in Sudan will force us to exercise our duty of intervening on the side of the Sudanese 
people. Any military, economic or political intervention on the side of the fascist 
government, which is hostile to the Sudanese people, to enable it to triumph over the 
Sudanese people will force us to exercise our duty of military, economic and political 
intervention on the side of the Sudanese people. This is a warning we address to the 
enemies of the Africa peoples, to the enemies of Africa, to those who intervene to defeat 
the Sudanese people who are daily offering sacrifices on the altar of salvation. [applause] 


We address a warning to Arab reaction and Saudi reaction particularly. The Saudi reaction 
today has allied itself with the fascist regime which is hostile to the Sudanese people 
with a view to overpowering the Sudanese people. We address a warning to this reaction: 
Unless they restrain and curb themselves and contain themselves to their filthy artificial 
borders, we will put an end to this intervention which is hostile to the Sudanese people. 
This is because this matter concerms us and because the security of the Arab and African 
peoples is one and their freedom is indivisible. Their battle is one and their enemy is 
common. [chants] 


We welcome you, brothers from Africa, of which we are a part. A struggle is raging close 
to the borders of this land on which you are meeting today -- this, in addition to the 
struggle on its eastem borders in Sudan, between the people of Sudan and fascism, which 
is loyal to racism and colonialism. 


On the southern borders of this country, there is a struggle raging for the sake of the 
Unity of the Chadian people and peace and stability in Chad. It is a struggle between 
the forces that advocate peace, stability, freedom and unity of Chad and the forces which 
oppose these popular aspirations. 


But I say to you once more: Those who have deceived Chad and caused it to be divided are 
the ones today blacking out the news of the bitter bloody struggle now raging in Chad. 
Ndjamena receives scores, indeed hundreds, of the dead and wounded on all fronts every 
day. Blood is flowing in every part of Chad. Peace totally collapsed in Chad after the 
returm home of the Libyan forces. 


This is what the French colonialists, the United States and the reactionaries and racists 
want. They deceived the Government of Chad and persuaded it to ask for the return of the 
Libyan forces to their bases. A horrible civil war is now raging in Chad. Scores, in- 
deed hundreds, of dead are daily falling in Chad. We are fully aware of this fact. 

We are sorry to see that after establishment of peace and the ending of bloodshed, every- 
thing in Chad has collapsed, after the return home of the Libyan forces.

We want to show the world how the Libyan forces were the safety valve in Chad. They 
established peace and brought about unity. We challenge them to restore peace and 
stability to Chad and to put an end to bloodshed in Chad. We challenge them but we know 
they will fail. This is because all of them are enemies of Chad. We are sincere about 
Chad because Chad is part of us and we are part of it. Peace in Chad is our peace and 
the security of Chad is ours. 


As for France, the United States, the reactionaries and racists, their security is not 
dependent on the security of Chad. Their interest lies in the spread of instability in 
Chad and Africa. Their interests lie in the shattering of Chad and in the disappearance 
of the state of Chad from the map. They are the enemies of Chad while we are the friends 
and brothers of Chad. They are the enemies of Africa but we are Africans and the Africans 
are our brothers and we are brothers to them. 


Africa, brothers, is witnessing a struggle between racism in its south and the forces of 
liberation in South Africa -- and also between the people of Namibia and the racist forces 
centered in South Africa. 


But we, brothers, herald the good tidings: The end of the imperialists will not be far 
off. The end of the racist regimes is certainly coming. This is because we have decided 
to place all our capabilities at the disposal of the African people for the sake of the 
liberation of the African Continent and for the defeat of racism in Africa and colonialism. 


Colonialism has decided to turn us into a farm, a mine and slaves. But we have decided 
to seize our national wealth and exploit it ourselves. We have decided to be free. 


This is the truth of the situation today between us and the forces of imperialism and its 
racist and reactionary allies. 


Brother Africans, you live in a darkness while your wealth is used by the peoples of 
North America and France. They turn it into electricity and into atoms, but you live in 
darkness, naked and barefooted. But your wealth is stolen by France and America. 


This outcry, African brothers, is not new. But we will continue to incite revolution 
in Africa and against the forces of exploitation, the forces of imperialism, the racist 
regimes, until imperialism is vanquished in spite of itself and until Africa triumps in 
spite of imperialism and racism. [applause] 


It is extremely important, African brothers, that we realize that the struggle is now in 
full swing between France and the United States over the new colonization of Africa. The 
European imperialist banners which fall in France ‘are being picked up by U.S. imperialism 
in order to hoist them anew as U.S. banners in the African Continent. 


It is for this, brothers, that America now intervenes most insolently and with no due 

respect for the national independence of the African peoples and issues orders to them, 

both written and verbal. We have copies of such orders sent to a number of heads of 

African countries, warning them against agreeing to the holding of the OAU summit conference 
in Libya because this will mean bringing the end of imperialism and racial discrimination 
nearer. This means that Africa might enter a new era of unity, solidarity, cooperation 

and joint exploitation of its wealth, depriving America, France and the enemies of Africa 

of Africa'swealth, resources and the use of Africa's resources for the good of their peoples. 


But the African heads of state loyal to their peoples and to Africa have informed us of 
these orders and that they have rejected America's orders. This is because America's 
orders to prevent the holding of the summit conference in Africa will mean the realization 
of the interests of America, the triumph of the U.S. will, the defeat of the African will 
and that Africa acts on the orders of America -- a disgrace which would be exceeded by 

no other disgrace for the national independence of Africancountries.

This is the truth about the U.S. and French policies which are totally hostile to our 
African Continent. They are true enemies and we must fight them. They possess no argument 
to stop us from fighting them anywhere in Africa for we are Africans and this is our con- 
tinent. But they are racists, foreigners and colonialists. 


From a purely ideological viewpoint, I would say that a sporting occasion is when we explain 
to those gathered here and elsewhere in the countries watching us now and those involved in 
these social affairs, that I am totally biased toward sports. The claims of the enemies are 
not true. [applause] In fact, I regard sports as essential. This is my belief. It is 
essential materially and morally to the individual. If Zionist and U.S. propaganda turns 

my revolutionary actions to terrorist action, I am not that person. [sentence as heard] 

I am a sporting man. I know this myself even if circumstances do not permit. [applause] 

I say that sport is necessary materially and morally to the individual. I encourage every 
individual to participate in sports. I advocate sports to the masses. All the masses 
should play sports. Sports concern every individual. 


Sports are necessary for everyone. I call for the destruction of monopolies of sports in 
the same way I called for the destruction of the mcnopoly of power, wealth and arms. All 
monopolies should be destroyed. 


Recently, a team came here from another country. I talked to it about its state and the 
improvement of relations and what benefit this team could give us while we discussed in 

a sporting spirit all that can be of benefit to the two countries. But we discovered that 
the team in question did not belong to the country whose flag it was representing and for 
whom was playing. We found it to be a team of mercenaries -- individuals who had been 
bought by that country which possesses oil. That country turned those sports heroes into 
slaves. It falsified their sporting heroism. They became mercenaries and false heroes. 


Only recently, there was a crisis between Libya and its neighbor and friend, Tunisia, as 

a result of a trivial sporting incident at a football match. I therefore expressed my 
fears when I heard the Libyan and Tunisian teams were scheduledto meet. I was worried that 
this meeting might have a negative effect on the good and fraternal relations built between 
the two countries and strengthened by the economic integration whose foundations were laid 
during my recent visit to Tunisia. I am talking from an ideological standpoint. I am sure 
of its truth. I speak from experience which cannot be refuted. I am not making a conjec- 
ture. 


I have seen in the schedule of these games that the Libyan team will meet the Ghanaian team. 
We are allies of the revolution in Ghana. We stand with all our potentials alongside Ghana, 
the new Ghana, revolutionary Ghana, the anti-imperialist, the antireactionary and the anti- 
racist Ghana. I hope that the sporting event between the Libyan and Ghanaian teams will 

not have a negative effect on revolutionary and fraternal relations between the Libyan and 
Ghanaian peoples. 


At any rate, I am very pleased that we meet as Africans and talk about our troubles and 
problems and focus on the path to the future -- the path of deliverance from colonialism, 
racism and underdevelopment. I thank you brother Africans who provided us with the oppor- 
tunity, and the opportunity you have provided for these thousands of sons of the Libyan 
people who are enjoying themselves in the traditional way -- as spectators lazily occupying 
the terraces of the stadium. I hope they will not produce any adverse results on the 
competitions. 


I wish you success and I welcome you.
