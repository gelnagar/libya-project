LIBYA

Vv. 3 Aug 87 


President Receives Saudi Envoy on Mecca Marches 
LDO21350 Tunis Domestic Service in Arabic 
1100 GMT 2 Aug 87 


[Text] President Habib Bourguiba received Ibrahim al-Sa‘d al- 
Ibrahimi, the ambassador of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabi to 
Tunis, in the presence of Minister Amor Chadli, director of the 
Presidential Office. 


Following this meeting, the Saudi ambassador issued the follow- 
ing statement: I had the honor of an audience with President 
Habib Bourguiba. His Excellency expressed his denunciation 
and condemnation of the marches and demonstrations carried 
out by a large number of Iranians in holy Mecca. These actions 
were disturbing to the pilgrims to God's holy house. In this 
context the supreme combatant entrusted me with a message fer 
his brother, the custodian of the two holy mosques. 


Sends Condolences on Incident 
LD030208 Tunis Domestic Service in Arabic 
2200 GMT 2 Aug 87 


[Excerpts] Premier Rachid Sfar has presented his excellency, the 
supreme combatant President Habib Bourguiba, with a full 
report on his talks with Kuwaiti officials. The Kuwaiti officials 
had asked him to convey their affection and esteem to the great 
fighter for lis iirm and clear stands supporting fraternal Kuwait 
during this period when it has faced challenges and increasing 
threats against its security and stability. [passage omitted] 


Those attending the meeting reviewed the situation in the Gulf 
region. President Habib Bourguiba has followed with full interest 
the incidents which have occurred in the holy city of Mecca as a 
result of the behavior and serious excesses of groups of Iranians. 
Such actions completely contradict the teachings of the tolerant 
Islamic faith. 


The grat fighter stressed his complete support for the steps taken 
by the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and the Gulf states te confront 
these conditions. In this connection, his excellency sent a message 
of condolence and solidavity to the custodian of the two holy 
mosques, King Fahd Ibn ‘Abd al-‘Aziz.
