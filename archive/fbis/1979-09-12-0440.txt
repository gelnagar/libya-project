AL-QADHDHAFI ABOLISHES CERTAIN JUDICIARY POSTS 
1D101528 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 10 Sep 79 LD 


[Summary] Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi this morning attended a meeting of the General 
People's Committee for Justice, He stressed the need for consolidation of peuple's 
authority, which is now fully in the hands of the people in the Jamahiriyah, This way, 
he said, the people will be saved from domination by one individual or group of people, 
He said that control by popular committees should not mean interference with sentences 
or the work of courts, Should a popular committee anger the masses, it should expect a 
popular revolution against it, as happened to the defunct regime on the First of 
September Revolution, 


"The brother colonel announced the abolition of the posts of the public attorney and 
public advocate, and the higher judiciary body and the higher police council, He 
called for the replacement of these by popular committees," 


"Speaking of the task of the lawyer, the leader said that the lawyer is part of the 
court and must be a public servant just like the judge and the policeman and other court 
officials, Thus the present conditions--in which only someone with money is able to 
appoint a lawyer to defend him--must end,"

AT-TURAYKI RETURNS FROM HAVANA, REMARKS ON SUMMIT 
LD112118 Tripoli JANA in English 1850 GMT 11 Sep 79 LD 


(Text] Tripoli, 11 Sep, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY=-Foreign Secretary Dr 'Ali 'Abd as.Salam 
at-Turayki and his delegation were back in Tripoli last night after they attended the 
meeting of the 6th summit conference of nonaligned countries which was held in Havana 
between 3 and 9 September current, 


In a statement to the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY the foreign secretary pointed out that 
the conference had achieved great success and could be considered one [as received] 
in the solidarity with the Arab nation and the Palestinian people, 


It had been attended by 94 delegations and several observers, The delegates strongly 
condemned the treaty of capitulation, the "stable David" accords and the Egyptian 
regime's policy, The foreign secretary pointed out that the measures taken by the 
Egyptian president are considered a violation of the principles of the nonaligned 
movement. 


On this basis, the conference denied the Egyptian regime any legitimate representation 
of Egypt in the nonaligned organization and formed an ad-hoc committee to study the 
necessary measures for implementing this resolution and submitting suggestions to the 
next foreign ministers conference for approval, 


The foreign secretary added that the conference had unanimously supported the Falestinian 
people's and the Arab nation's cause, He considered the conference an international 
referendum on the policy of the Egyptian regime, considering it aligned to colonialism, 
Zionism and racism, 


The foreign secretary indicated that the conference was dominated by the spirit of 
collective responsibility for the triumph of people's causes in the world in general, 
the Palestinian cause and liberation causes in particular,

This conference, which was held for the first time in Latin America, is also considered 
an internatZonal phenomenon as well as an international support for Latin American's 
peoples and the revolution which has inflamed there against America. For the first 
time, Latin American countries participated after their people have become victorious 
against hireling reactionary regimes such as Grenada and Nicaragua. 


Dr at-Turayki said thet the conference strongly stressed that the nonaligned countries 
are maintaining their independent policy and unity and standing on one side against 
imperialism, cokonialism, ragism and Zionism, Moreover they are determined to play 
their role in an international policy governed by democracy. 


The foreign secretary said that the unanimous approval of the motion tabled by the 
Jamahiriyah on abolishing the veto at tne Security Council is a first step towards 
justice in the United Nations. With respect to the economic issues, the conference 
considered the current economic order inappropriate«since it was created by colonialist 
countries and international monopolies and directed against the peoples of the Third 
World. Dr at-Turayki pointed out the conference affirmed it was time to achieve 
cooperation among the nonaligned community and prevent the colonialist countries from 
exploiting the raw materials and resources of the developing countries. The conference 
expressed determination to create a new economic order for theinterest of humanity.

He was received at Tripoli International Airport by Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahm.4

to the people of Upper Volta, particularly during the famine which engulfed western :

Communique on Visit 
1D110412 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 10 Sep 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli--A joint commumique was issued here this evening at the end of the visit 
to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah by Upper Voltan President Aboubacar 
Lamizana, The communique states that during their meetings, President Aboubacar 
Lamizana and the leader of the September revolution, Col Mu'ammar alQadhdhafi, dis- 
cussed bilateral relations, the political situation in Africa and the Arab region, and 
also international issues. 


The Libyan side, led by Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Jallud and the Upper Volta side, led 

by President Aboubacar Lamizana, conducted talks which dealt with bilateral relations, 

the situation in the Arab region and Africa and international issues of common interest, 
The two sides reiterated their intention to consolidate the historic relations of friend. 
ship and cooperation between the two peoples and they undertook to develop these relations 
so as to make them ideal relations, in realization of the trust that exists between the 
two leaders and peoples, . 


The two sides observe that the African Continent has become a goal for the ambitions of 
the great powers and for their occasional interference in the continent's internal 
affairs, When reviewing this existing situation they decided to address an appeal to 
all African peoples and governments to adhere to their unity and to try and resolve 
their problems in an African spirit within the framework of the OAU, stressing that 
African wisdom is capable of finding the appropriate solutions to these problems, 


They also expressed the belief that [word indistinct] the armed struggle against 
colonialism and racism in Africa must continue, it being the only way to the free“om 
of the African peoples, The two sides strongly condemm the aggressions being carried 
out by the racist regime in southern Africa and Palestine, regarding these aggressions 
as being directed against human values and against the people's rights to freedom and 
justice, 


Regarding the Western Sahara issue, the two sides stressed their absolute support for 
the right of the Saharan people to self determination in accordance with the principles 
and resolutions of the United Nations and the OAU. 


The two sides condemm attempts by the imperialist forces to create military blocs and 
alliances with some countries on the continent, regarding this as constituting a 
threat to the security of the African peoples and seeking to divide the continent, 
They appeal to all the African countries not to join such military blocs, in accordance 
with the basic principles of the nonalined countries, 


The two sides also condem the military invasion of Uganda and regard this invasion as 
a serious precedence in the field of relations and especially among the African states, 
many of which have border disputes and ideological differences with their neighbors, 


Regarding the situation in Chad, the two sides encourage national reconciliatim in 
accordance with the results achieved at the recent Lagos conference; they declare their 
full support for these results, They also call on ail parties concerned to respect the 
resolutions of the conference, so as to restore security and peace to this sister 
African country. In this connection, they demand the withdrawal of the foreign forces, 
in accordance with the spirit of this conference,

On the Arab and African plane, the two sides call for Arab and African solidarity and 
unity and they stress the importance of the joint role played by the Arab-African group 
for world cultural and economic progress, in order to liberate it from imperialist 
subservience, domination and exploitation, The two sides are also convinced of the 
importance of Arab-African cooperation in world economic development in general and in 
the Third World in particular, 


When reviewing the Palestinian issue, the two sides condemned the role being played by 
reaction, which is in alliance with colonialism and imperialism for the liquidation of 
the Palestinian cause in the interest of Zionism, The two sides once more underline 
the established and legitimate right of the Palestinian people to self-determination and 
to set up their own independent state on their territory, In this respect they stress 
that no just and lasting solution can be reached without the direct participation of 
the real representatives of the Palestinian people under the leadership of the PLO, The 
two sides condemn any agreement which has been signed or will be signed which does not 
take these facts into consideration, 


The two sides stress their adherence to the UN Charter and principles and they advocate 
the need to amend the charter of this organization so as to achieve justice and equality 
among member states, They also advocate a new world economic system that recognizes 

the rights of the Third World countries on a basis of justice and equality. The two 
sides regard the natural resources of every country as a natural and legitimate right 
of that country; no other country has the right to interfere so as to take over these 
resources, 


At the end of the visit, President Aboubacar Sangoule Lamizana expressed admiration of 
the great achievements realized by the Great First of September Revolution which aims 
at the realization of prosperity and social justice in the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah, He also reaffirmed that the third international theory which is 
being implemented in the Jamahiriyah is the best means for solving the problems faced 
by mankind in the political, economic and social spheres, 


He also expressed his thanks and gratitude for the warm and fraternal welcome he and 
the delegation accompanying him received in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah, President Lamizana extended an invitation to his brother, Col Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the Great First of September Revolution, to visit the 
Republic of Upper Volta, This invitation has been accepted and its date will be fixed 
in due course,
