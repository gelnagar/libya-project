LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SPEAKS ON EDUCATION, MINORITIES 


LD120916 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 12 Apr 81 


[Text] The brother commander of the revolution met last evening with the delegations of 
the Arab and world student organizations, federations and unions which joined the students 
of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in their celebration of the fifth anni- 
versary of the 7 April students’ uprising. The commander greeted the delegations of 
students and teachers and expounded on some of the topics dealt with at the Ar Unis 
University debate on the democratization of education, nationalities and minorities. 


Education in the world today, he said, is compulsory, and the curricula are drawn up by 

the governments ruling the world. The societies from which governments spring take 

the necessary steps to insure the continuation of their rule. A government in a capitalist 
society takes steps to insure its continued domination and exploitation for as long as 
possible. The same applies to other governments. 


The brother commander affirmed that the curricula being taught at present throughout the 
world, though differing from one country to another, are alike in that they are all 

drawn up by the government ruliug the society. This, he affirmed, is contrary to freedom. 
The "Green Book" is intended to be a guideline for people on how to rid themselves of domi- 
nation and create a free, happy world, he said. The solution lies in giving everyone the 
freedom to learn whatever he wants in the manner that suits him, he added. 


The commander gave a number of examples. He said that the entire Christian world is 
ignorant about Islam, because the Christian governments do not want people to learn the 
truth about it. The same, he added, applies to the majority of traditional Muslims, who 
consider the Christians as atheists, which is incorrect. This is because of anti-Christian 
teaching. This has led to catastrophes and tragedies, of which a prime example is the 

wars of the crusades. So that these catastrophes and tragedies do not happen again, he 
said, freedom must triumph and man must learn the truth in the manner best suited to him 
and as it is. 


The brother commander pointed out that man must acquire knowledge so that he can practice 
freedom. The present curricula in the world do not enable man to acquire knowledge; con- 
sequently, man cannot be free or happy, he said. The jamahiri society abolishes govern- 
ments and vests authority in the popular conferences and committees; thus there is no 
reason to keep any knowledge from the masses, as governments do now, he affirmed. 


Speaking of the technological curricula, the brother commander said that this is a matter 
special to the Socialist People's Libyan Jamahiriyah, because of its need for technology 
to solve its problems. We will implement the provisions of the "Green Book" in this regard. 


On nationalism and minorities, the brother commander said that minorities today are the 
cause of many headaches for the world, and world wars stem from such a problem. Wars 
might take place in the future because of the problem of minorities, he said, and this 
proves that history is affected by the concept of nationalism more than by any other 
factor. The Green Book provides a solution which would end the headache sweeping the 
world: Nobody has the right to dominate someone else, regardless of the reason, and 

no nationality has the right to dominate another. Likewise, independent nations have no 
right to crush their minorities. 


He pointed out this should not rule out the existence of minorities within nations, be- 
cause minorities are the byproduct of social interaction. Some of the present nations 
might become minorities in the future, he added.

The brother commander said that there are two kinds of minorities. First there are the 
minorities without a homeland or a nation, such as the Gypsies. They exist in a number 
of European countries but so far have no fixed homeland or nation to bring them together. 
However, it is not justified to crush them just because they are a minority and have no 
homeland or nation. 


The solution to the political and economic problems of the minorities, he said, lies in 
the creation of a jamahiri society where everyone belongs to a popular conference, re- 
gardless of whether he belongs to a majority or minority group, and where there are no 
elections or representation. The popular committees are elected on the basis of adminis- 
trative capability, he said, and in a jamahiri society the land belongs to all, the house 
belongs to its occupant and there are no servants but partners, and only those who produce 
have the right to consume. It does not matter at all whether one is a member of a 
minority or a majority. 


Socially, nobody has the right to prevent a minority from speaking its own language if it 
has one, he said, and it should practice its culture, traditions and ethics. It is unfair 
to interfere in such matters, he affirmed. 


The second kind of minority, he pointed out, consists those with a homeland and a nation 
but who at present live within another nation. The Kurds in the Near East, for example, 
cause headaches and wars, revolutions and rebellions all the time. They also cause 
disobedience and work both covertly and openly. This is because they live in a number 
of the countries of the area. The same applies to the Armenians. The solution lies in 
the repatriation of these minorities to their original homelands and in their reunifi- 
cation. Unless this solution is applied, he said, the headache will continue and peace 
will never be realized. 


The brother commander said that the realistic order of classification as far as mankind 

is concerned is: man, family, tribe, nation and world. Just as no individual can dissolve 
into another, no nation can dissolve into another, he said. Adding that attempts had been 
made at one time to unite two nations on the basis of religious factors but that such a 
union did not last and secession took place. He cited as an example attempts to unite 

the Arab, Persian and Turkish nations. He also cited several attempts in Europe. 


The brother commander said that all the conflicts in the world today are based on 
nationalism. The celebrations of independence anniversaries, he said, are celebrations 

of the independence of one nationality from the domination of another. It is impossible 
to create one state in the world, as some believe, but it is possible to create a jamahiri 
world in which governments, parties, classes, sects and armies disappear. 


In conclusion, the brother commander pointed out that some nationalities continue to 
dominate others in the world today. This demands an overall struggle by the masses and 

the staging of a popular revolution for which the ground should be paved by means of a 
cultural revolution. The commander thanked the professors and students of the Madrid 
Independent University and the other delegations attending the celebrations of the students’ 
revolution.
