LIBYA

KUWAIT: LIBYAN EMBASSY DENIES REPORT OF ATTEMPTED COUP 


LD220954Y Kuwait KUNA in Arabic 0700 GMT 22 Nov 78 LD 


[Text] Kuwait--In Kuwait today tne Libyan Jamahiriyah Embassy denied a Kuwait press 
report which speaks of an attempted coup against President Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
at the beginning of this month.
