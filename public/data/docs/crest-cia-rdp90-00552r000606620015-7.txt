Summary (AI): A Boston Globe article from 30 September 1981 reports that in July 1977 Green Beret master sergeant Luke F. Thompson led retired Special Forces soldiers to Libya to train Libyan commandos in counterinsurgency, and that the authorization question remained unresolved. It also describes a wider pattern of retired US soldiers and ordnance technicians training Libyan commandos and making explosives from 1976 through 1979.
• Thompson's team was believed to be one of the first of dozens of retired Green Berets and Army ordnance technicians who went to Libya from 1976 through at least 1979.
• The team was to train Libyan commandos and manufacture explosives under a contract with Libya.
• Justice Department said training Libyan terrorists was not illegal because the Green Berets did not enlist in the Libyan army or commit hostilities against the US.
