• Wilson, age 53, was a former CIA agent described as an alleged supplier of airplane mechanics and pilots for the Libyan Air Force.
• A four-count federal indictment handed down in Washington the previous year charged Wilson and Frank Terpil with supplying explosives to Qaddafi's government in violation of a federal arms shipment statute.
• The indictment also accused them of conspiring with Cuban exiles to assassinate an enemy of Qaddafi.
• The State Department called Wilson's mission to Libya 'reprehensible'.
• Wilson denied all charges, saying he was innocent.
