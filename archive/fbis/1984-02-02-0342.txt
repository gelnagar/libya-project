LIBYA

JANA Cites Bardai on GUNT Repelling Habre 
LDO20818 Tri: oli JANA in Arabic 0730 GMT 2 Feb 84 


[Text] Bardai, 2 Feb (JANA) Bardai radio, organ of the 
Transitional National Unity Government quoting a GUNT mili- 
tary spokesman, said that forces of the rebel Habre, backed by 
French forces, have launched an attack against the positions of 
the GUNT in the region of Toko, and that the forces of the 
legitimate government have confronted them and inflicted heavy 
losses in lives and equipmeni 


The radio said that the losses suffered by the rebel Habre forces 
had reached 354 killed in addition to four French soldiers 
killed, who were driving intelligence vehicles — and hundreds of 
wounded. Bardai radio added that the GUNT forces had 
destroyed four enemy half-tracks, seven vehicles of various types, 
five jeep-mounted 106-caliber guns, 10 truck-mounted 14.5 mm 
machine guns, and a French-made machine gun 


The forces of GUNT are still chasing the fleeing hordes of the 
rebel Habre and his collaborators
