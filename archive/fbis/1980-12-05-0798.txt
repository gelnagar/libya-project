LIBYA

LIBERIAN DELEGATION EXPLAINS VISIT POSTPONEMENT 


LD050954 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 5 Dec 80 


[Text] The leader of the revolution has received an important delegation from the 
Republic of Liberia led by the Liberian minister of foreign affairs, Gabriel Matthews, 
and including as a member the minister of planning and presidential affairs. The 
delegation was sent to the Jamahiriyah by the head of state and chairman of the 
People's Redemption Council in the Republic of Liberia to convey apologies for the 
postponement of the visit by the Liberian head of state to the Jamahiriyah which had 
been scheduled to take place on 30 November. The delegation explained to the brother 
leader the circumstanees which Liberia hac witnessed during the past days and which 
were the reason for the postponement. The delegation conveyed the regret and apologies 
of the Liberian head of state for the postponement of the visit. The delegation handed 
the brother leader a written message from the Liberian head of state.
