LIBYA

Sri Lanka Warned Over ‘Zionist’ Office in Colombo 
LD101452 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 10 Jun 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 10 Jun (SANA) — Dr Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at- 
Turayki, secretary of the People’s Committee of the People’s 
Bureau for Foreign Liaison, has conveyed the profound dis- 
pleasure of the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah with 
the measure taken by the Government of Sri Lanka in opening 
an office to look after Zionist interests in the capital, Colombo. 


The brother secretary of the People’s Committee of the People’s 
Bureau for Foreign Liaison pointed out in a message to the Sri 
Lankan foreign minister that the justifications offered by his 
government are absolutely unconvincing, that the Sri Lankan 
Government's continued implementation of this measure will 
force the Jamahiriyah to reconsider its relations with Sri Lanka, 
and that this action does not at all aid developing relations 
between the Islamic world and Sri Lanka. 


Muslim Brotherhood Member Executed in Zuwarah 
LD102229 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2200 GMT 10 Jun 84 


[Text] The masses of the Zuwarah Basic People’s Conference 
have sent a cable to the brother leader. Here is the text: 


Brother Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 
Al-Fatih Revolution: The masses of the Zuwarah Basic Peo- 
ples Conference after an extraordinary session today to carry out 
the death sentence against one of the Muslim Brotherhood gang’s 
conspiring agents, (Farahat ‘Ammar Jlab), confirm to you their 
complete readiness to carry out the death order and the liquida- 
tion of the enemies of people’s authority everywhere in the world. 
Forward, Fatih forever, and the revolutionary struggle continues.
