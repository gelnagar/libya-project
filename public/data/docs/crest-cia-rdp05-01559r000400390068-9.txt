Summary (AI): An FBIS wire transmission carries part of a March 1985 speech by Qadhafi marking the revolution anniversary, attacking Arab regimes, praising Lebanon's popular resistance against Israel, and criticizing the 17 May Agreement and the United States. It contains no places in or near Tripoli.
• The speech was broadcast on Tripoli domestic Arabic radio and monitored by FBIS London on 29 March 1985.
• Qadhafi argued that all Arab regimes founded on governments, parties, sultanates, tribalism or inheritance had failed and called for a pan-Arab popular revolution.
• Qadhafi praised Lebanon's popular resistance as the model of the Jamahiriya, claiming it forced Israel back without a regular army.
• Qadhafi criticized the 17 May Agreement and said the Americans fought to preserve it.
