Summary (AI): A 1981 New York Times Magazine article by Seymour Hersh, reproduced in a CIA reading room file, says former CIA operatives Edwin Wilson and Frank Terpil made a business deal with Qaddafi to sell him intelligence contacts and expertise. It alleges they helped Libya set up a plant making assassination weapons, recruited former Green Berets to train Libyan soldiers and terrorists, and illegally shipped arms and explosives to Libya.
• Wilson and Terpil allegedly helped Libya set up a plant producing assassination weapons.
• They allegedly recruited dozens of former Green Berets to teach Libyan soldiers and Arab terrorists how to handle explosives.
• Government has known of the Qaddafi connection since fall 1978, when Mulcahy approached the CIA and FBI.
• Article published in New York Times Magazine, 14 June 1981.
