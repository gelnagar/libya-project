LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI: EXPELLED AMERICANS HAD TERRORIST CONNECTIONS 


LD191334 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 19 May 80 


[Text] The brother commander has affirmed that now the people of the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah rule themselves by themselves and that their relations with other 
peoples mist be a direct popular one through the people's bureaus and not through traditional 
governmental embassies. 


In an interview with the U.S. ABC television network, the brother commander said that members 
of the Libyan people's bureaus are unlike members of the diplomatic missions. They are free 
citizens and most of them are Libyan students studying in the countries where the bureaus 
are. These students have marched on the embassies and turned them into people's bureaus 

and imposed the will of the masses. 


We, in Libya, no longer deal with diplomatic matters in the traditional manner, he said. 

We have popular conferences which have authority. Abroad, we have people's bureaus which 
are considered as links between the popular conferences and the other peoples of the world. 
There is also a people's bureau which is responsible for the people's bureaus abroad. That 
bureau is, in turn, responsible to the popular conferences. 


The brother referred to the message sent by the brother secretary of the Foreign Liaison 
Office to President Carter, in which he told him that the secretary of the Foreign Liaison 
Office was authorized by the popular conferences to be in charge of the people's treaus 
abroad. 


The brother commander affirmed that those opposed to the people's authority and who have 
absconded abroad have been collaborating with the intelligence media of foreign countries 
such as those of the Egyptian regime, Israel and the United States. The Libyan people have 
repeatedly pardoned them and have given them numerous opportunities to return home. But 
they have allied themselves with the enemies abroad. This, the revolutionary committees 
decided to liquidate them. 


He said that the battle is being waged between the people and their enemies. He pointed 
out that responsibility for this battle rests with the revolutionary committees which have 
taken the decision and the people's bureaus have nothing to do with the matter. 


Asked about the expulsion of some Americans from Libya, the commander pointed out that 
the popular committees of justice and the popular security quarters have established that 
these people belong to the intelligence media and have connections with some terrorist 
organizations. 


The full text ofthe interview will be broadcast during our radio and television transmissions 
in English and French later today. 


‘TRADITIONAL? DIPLOMACY IS A THING OF THE PAST 


1D191555 Tripoli AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR in Arabic 12 May 80 pp 1, 2 


{[Editorial: "Traditional Diplomacy On Scrap Heap of History"] 


[Excerpts] In implementation of the third session of the revolutionary committees! 
meeting, a march will be staged on Monday to take over 10 [Libyan] Embassies in 10 
different countries as a means of consolidating relations between the Libyan Arab people 
and the world peoples and ending the traditional relations inherited from the era of 
dictatorship, tyranny and domination in international relations. 


V. 20 May 80 NORTH AFRICA 


The history of embassies is the history of colonialism. The "high commissioner" is 
the messenger of dictatorship from one country to another. Since the Jamahiriyah is 
eliminating all means of domination and arbitrary rule in society, it also rejects 
domination, guardianship and proxy in international relations, Indeed, it is working 
to establish equal and direct relations between peoples and not governments, 


What is happening here in the Jamahiriyah is genuine democracy, because there can be 

no democracy without people's congresses and the establishment of revolutionary commit- 
tees everywhere. What the Libyan Arab people have achieved in relation to the world 
peoples is similar to winning a race; once it is determined who is the winner, the race 
is over. Therefore, issues of democracy have been resolved, and there is no longer any 
reason for further search, 


The United States, which is ruled by unjust domestic laws, where the capitalists ex- 
ploit the workers in heavy, mechanical production, where man is exploited like a machine, 
and where the citizen is bought and sold in election campaigns--that United States is 
committing injustice on the international level and seeking to dominate. Its police and 
intelligence organs and its monopolist companies play with other peoples! destinies. 

Its embassies have turned into espionage dens, Its ambassadors play the role of high 
commissioners who dictate orders to clients of the United States, which despises the 
peoples. The United States does not hesitate to exterminate peoples if they try to 
resist, as has happened in Kampuchea, Vietnam and Latin America. These barbaric actions 
are the fruits of the philosophy of ruling and governing. It can only produce war, 
conspiracies and international terror, 


By escalating popular action in these embassies and turning them into people's bureaus 
we aim to build a bridge for our masses to meet the world masses without middlemen or 
representatives, in order to establish good relations and strong ties.

MA'ARIV COMMENTS ON EGYPTIAN POSITION 
TA191200 Tel Aviv MA'ARIV in Hebrew 19 May 80 p 5 
[Editorial: "Is Egypt Allowed To?"] 


[Text] One of the Egyptian pretexts for suspending the autonomy talks is that last week 
the Knesset voted in favor of the first reading of Ge'ula Kohen's private draft proposal 
intended to reaffirm Jerusalem's status as the eternal capital of the state of Israel, 
Cairo is now counting this act as one of the obstacles Israel has set on the negotiations 
road, 


V. 20 May 80 


Indeed, there is apparently no limit to Egypt's insolence., Ms Kohen's proposal came as 
a direct reaction to the resolution passed by the Egyptian People's Assembly on 1 April 
at the initiative of the government of Egypt, which says that Egypt has commitments to 
the Arab character of East Jerusalem and it regards it as an inseparable part of the 
West Bank and as the capital of the autonomy which is to be established, 


That resolution aroused dissatisfaction in Jerusalem and political sources noted that 

it did not bring peace any nearer and could cause the autonomy talks to fail. However, 
nebody in Jerusalem thought that such a unilateral resolution could be sufficient to 
justify suspending the talks; after expressing its dissatisfaction, Israel continued with 
the deliberations, as though nothing had happened, 


Now Cairo is claiming that it is Israel that is holding up the talks because the 
majority of the Knesset voted in favor of a draft proposal not submitted by the govern- 
ment, but by one member of the Knesset. What the Egyptian Parliament did, in its 
opinion, is only natural; what the Knesset does foils attempts to reach an agreement on 
autonomy.... 


It seems that we, too, are not without guilt if we agree to the creation of a situation 
in which our arguments are by way of being a sin and an obstacle, and only the Egyptian 
claims can be made out loud without disturbing the correct process of the negotiations. 


The time has come for a renewal of symmetry in the talks, The time has come to explain 
to Egypt that its tactics are absolutely transparent and it is clear to everyone that 
they are looking for excuses to delay the negotiations and, if this is what they want, 
they should understand that the Israeli enthusiasm is no greater than that of As-Sadat 
and his advisers, 


MA'ARIV: U.S. TO TAKE INITIATIVE ON TALKS 


TA191230 Tel Aviv MA'ARIV in Hebrew 19 May 80 ppl, 2 


[By Yosef Wachsman and 'Odded Granot ] 


[Excerpts] The initiative for the resumption of the autonomy talks will not come from 
Jerusalem, but it may be assumed that such an initiative will be taken by the United 
States--the prime minister's confidants said this morning, They added that there might 
be contacts on this issue even today, It has been reported from Jerusalem that 
President as-Sadat is not proposing any date for the resumption of the talks in his 
letter, simply noting the importance of their being resumed, 


As has been reported, the tone of the letter is "respectful and courteous" and one of 
the prime minister's confidants even jescribed the tone as "friendly, as in the past," 
It has also been reported that the letter contains no ultimatum and the request for 

a "gesture" is only hinted at, 


Up to now the letter has been read by Ministers Dr Yosef Burg and Yitzhaq Shamir and 
today it will also be given for study to the other ministers in the autonomy committee, 


Today the prime minister will text his reply to President Jimmy Carter's letter which 
was received with satisfaction in Jerusalem because it contains two paragraphs 
identical to the Israeli position--concerning the granting of personal autonomy to the 
inhabitants of Judaea and Samaria and the Gaza Strip and contradicting the Egyptian 
interpretation that the autonomy should be for the territories--and also because of 
President Carter's comment that the autonomy should guarantee Israel's full security. 


20 May 80 


The chairman of the ministerial autenomy committee, Dr Yosef Burg, refused to refer to 
the letter, but did say about Jerusalem that it was surprising that President as-Sadat 
announced the cessation of the talks for the first time on 8 May, while MK Ge'ula Kohen's 
proposal concerning Jerusalem was approved by the Knesset only on 14 May. "I do not 
understand how people are taking a later reason for an earlier decision," he said.

[Text] The economic minicabinet has decided not to amend the state budget for the next 
6 months. Our correspondent Gid'on Zelinger explains that this means a real cut in 

the budget as well as further difficulties in [receiving] the credit. The decision may 
result in increased interest rates. The minicabinet has also decided to freeze new

and freeze the credit ceiling for local activity. By the end of the month, a decision 
will be made on which items in the budget will be frozen and which will be approved. 


Economic circles in Jerusalem have expressed skepticism as to the ability of the Finance 
Ministry to enforce its resolution on the Cabinet ministries. 


Minister Hurvitz has said that the decision was intended to align government expenditures 
with income and to force the economy to live according to its capability. His ministry 
will not allow an increase in real wages, but at this point he does not think there is 
room for further taxation or amending the vates of the existing taxes.

PUBLIC FIGURES CONDEMN BEGIN INTERPRETATION OF ZIONISM 


TA191828 Tel Aviv HA'ARETZ in Hebrew 19 May 80 p 3 


[Text] Two hundred and fifty people, including IDF reserve generals, former ministers, 
Knesset members from various parties, members of the Peace Now movement, writers, kibbutz 
members from the three kibbutz movements, professors from institutes of higher education, 
industrialists and personalities from the theater signed a manifesto this week headlined: 
"Their Path Is Not Our Path." 


Those who signed the manifesto strongly condemned the warped interpretation given to 
Zionism by nationalist extremists within the public and the government. The signers 
demanded that a dam be constructed against the wave of nationalism and that there be a 
return to the golden path of Zionism which strives for peace and security through a 
compromise and partitioning of Eretz Yisra'el. 


Among those who signed: Former Chief of Staff Lt Gen (Res) Hayim Bar-Lev; Major Generals 
(Res) Avraham Adan, Tzvi Zamir, El'ad Peled and Yehoshofat Harkabi; Thelma El'azar and 
Annette Dulzin. Former ministers added their names to the manifesto, including MK Gao 
Ya'aqobi, Hayim Gvati, Aharon Yadlin and Victor Shemtov; Ambassadors Gid'on Refa'el and 
Ya'aqov Tzur; writers Yehuda 'Amihay and ‘Amos '0Oz; professor Arye Dvorzki, Shmu'el 
Eisenstadt, Ya'aqov Talmon, Hayim Bargay, Yeshoshu'a Praver, Shim'on Shamir, Dan Patenkin, 
Yoram Ben-Porat, Asaf Razin, Mikha'el Bruno, 'Imanu'el Sivan, Shlomo Simonson, Eytan 
Berglas and others. 


Over 20 Knesset members also signed the manifesto, including MK Amnon Rubinstein, 'Uzi 
Bar'am, Mikha Harish and Me'ir Talmi. Council leaders and other public figures are also 
on the list. 


Among other things, the manifesto says that the path of the extremists is endangering 
friends and isolating Israel, leaving dissension among the Jewish people, alienating 
friends and strengthening the extremists among the enemies and that peace is required to 
fully realize the Zionist vision.

HIGH COURT SEEKS EXPLANATION OF EXPULSION ORDER 


TA200818 Jerusalem Domestic Service in Hebrew 0800 GMT 20 May 80 


(Text ] This morning the High Court of Justice issued an order nisi for the state to ex- 
plain within 48 days why the expulsion order issued against the three Hebron Hills 
personalities will not be revoked, seeing that they were not given the proper oppor- 
tunity to submit their reservations about the deportation. 


In the course of the debate, State Attorney Gavri'el Bach admitted that the expulsion 
had indeed taken place in a manner that made it impossible for the deportees to appeal 
to the appeals committee. Mr Bach explained it as stemming from the immediate security 
need to expel them. At the same time, the state attorney emphasized that the law ought 
to be respected and, therefore, lessons will be learned in the future, 


Justice Hayim Kohen expressed his astonishment at the fact that the law stipulating 

that immediately after the deportation order is submitted the person involved is at once 
informed of his right to appeal to an appeals committee was not fulfilled. Justice 
Kohen noted that as early as 32 years ago, the Supreme Court decided that deportation 
orders should not be carried out if the appeals committee is not functioning, 


V. 20 May 80 N 6 


The state attorney explained the rush in deporting the men, saying that the Hebron 
massacre was a traumatic and brutal event, to which Justice Kohen replied that although 
what happened in Hebron was traumatic, one should not gather from it that the depor- 
tation, too, should be a traumatic experience,
