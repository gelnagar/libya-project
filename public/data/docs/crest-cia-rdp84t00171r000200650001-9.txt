Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery briefing attachment (August 1983) on Libyan forces resupplying and deploying in Libya and northern Chad in support of Chadian dissidents. It describes deployments around Fada, Ogoui and Biltine, and aircraft at Kufra and Sebha; none of it is in or near Tripoli.
• Fada is approximately 135 NM northeast of the probable government/French-controlled town of Arada.
• Libyan equipment at Fada includes at least 14 BTR-60 APCs, four T-54/55 medium tanks and two probable light antiaircraft guns.
• Air order-of-battle at Ogoui included five SF.260 light-attack aircraft, one CH-47 and two Hind attack helicopters.
• The document is a secret NPIC briefing note with no coordinates provided.
• 1983-08: Libyan combat manoeuvre elements deployed near Fada airfield, including BTR-60 APCs, T-54/55 tanks and heavy-lift transporters.
