SUDAN'S BAKR ADDRESSES SUDANESE EXILES AT CAMP 
LDO/123 Omdurman Domestic Service in Arabic 0430 GMT / May 79 LD/EA 


[Text] A report by a SUNA mobile unit in Tripoli says that Ar-Rashid at-Tahri Bakr, vice 
president of the republic and foreign minister, met at 2200 yesterday the masses of Al-Ansar 
[Sudanese exiles who support former Sudanese Premier al-Mahdi] at the 7 April Camp, Tripoli. 


His excellency said the bond of religion and the homeland imposes great responsibilities 

on us towards our religion and homeland, He called on them to work together under one 
banner--the banner of the Islamic religion, He told the masses of AleAnsar: You have 
lived for a long time outside the homeland, It!s God's will that we meet today under the 
banner of religion and the homeland to turn over a page of past history with all the spites 
and sectarian loyalty it contains, We ofter you our hand, clean and without evil, to begin 
a new stage in the history ot our homeland, 


Ar-Rashid at-Tahir pointed out the major part played by President Numayri's initiative in 
achieving national reconciliation and the role of As-Sadiq al-Mahdi in responding to it, 
since there was unity and one will whose truth they saw. He stressed that the homeland and 
faith are above all selfish interests, and this involves reunion and solidarity for the sake 
of the Sudan so as to turn over a new leaf for the future. His excellency told the masses 
of AleAnsar: We are not afrrid of responsibility. Today you are in Tripoli and tomorrow 
you will be with us in Khartoum to form a march of brotherhood, build the homeland and face 
challenges from within and without the country. 


In conclusion his excellency conveyed to the masses of Al-Ansar greetings from President 
Numayri and the people of the Sudan, He also greeted them in the name of the May revolution 
and in the name of the homeland, Ar-Rashid at-Tahir Bakr'!s speech made a good impression 
and had an obvious effect on the Al-Ansar masses. 


The meeting was attended by As-Sadiq al-Mahdi; Dr 'Abd al-Hamid Salih; and ‘abd al-Majid 
al-Ahmadi, the Sudan's ambassador to the Jamahiriyah,. 


At the same time Maj Gen Umar Muhammad at-Tayyib, head of the Sudanese state security organ, 
declared at his meeting with the Al-Ansar masses at the 7 April Camp in Tripoli yesterday 
evening that Al-Ansar's return to the Sudan will begin on 16 May and last until 20 May and 
that arrangements for their return have been completed with Libyan officials. The number 
of Al-Ansar at the camp is 350 citizens,

LD072326 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 7 May 79 LD 


{Text ] Today, Sudan has dealt a blow to the defeated Egyptian president by approving 
the resolutions of the Baghdad conference strengthening the violation of the 
defeated Egyptian president, who has always tried to provoke the fraternal Sudanese 
people. 


This was stated in the Libyan Arah-Sudanese communique published this evening at the 
end of the visit to Libya by brother Ar-Rashid at-Tahir Bakr, Sudanese vice president 
amd foreign minister. 


The communique affii.nred the political and military alliance between the two fraternal 
peoples. It also called for giving aid and establishing economic integration between 
the two fraternal covntries and the construction of joint projects. [passage indistinct] 


They affirmed their decisive opposition to all the Zionist amd colonialist plans which 
seek to perpetuate Zionist occupation and (?divide) the Arab nation. 


The two sides affirmed their support for the armed struggle of the Palestinian people 
against the racist Zionist state. They expressed their absolute support for the struggle 
of the Palestinian people under the leadership of the PLO, their sole legitimate repre- 
sentative, for liberation, return and self-determination and the establishment of the 
Palestinian state on the soil of Palestine. 


They affirmed the right of the Saharan people to self-determination in accordance 
with UN amd OAU resolutions. They affirmed their support for the OAU efforts in this 
framework. The two sides studied the situation inthe African Continent, reaffirmed 
their support for the African liberation movements and strongly comiemned the racist 
regimes and the methods of oppression, exploitation and racial discrimination. They 
affirmed their unchanging stand alongside the struggle of the African peoples to work 
for the liquidation of military alliances in the African Continent. 


The two sides supported the UN resolution adopted at its 30th general session regarding 
Zionism as a form of racial discrimination. They also supported the contimation of 

the two countries! efforts to bring about a national reconciliation between the brothers 
in Chad. They affirmed the need to solve the problem in light of the basic points agreed 
upon at the first Kano conference on Chadian national reconciliation. 


The two sides agreed to continue consultations within this framework in order to achieve 
this. 


The two sides reviewed the situation in Uganda expressing their concern about the serious 
situation in this fraternal region and their adherence to the principle of rejecting and 
condemning intervention by any state in the affairs of another state. The two countries 
demand the withdrawal of the foreign troops from Uganda and appeal to all fraternal 

and friendly states to work for putting an em to bloodshed am foreign intervention 

in Uganda and giving aid to the Ugandan refugees. 


The two sides reviewed the situation in the Horn of Af_ica and they agreed to contimue 
to make joint efforts to bind a peaceful and just solution to the Eritrean issue.

ment and their deep conviction in the effective role nonalined States play in internatioml

The two sides expressed their adherence to the aims and principles of the United Nations
