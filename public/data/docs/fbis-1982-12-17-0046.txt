LIBYA

ARAB STUDENT ORGANIZATIONS END MEETING 


LD151444 Tripoli JANA in English 0824 GMT 15 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Safar 29, Dec 15, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The emergency session of 
the Central Council of the Arab Youth Union and the General Union of Arab Students has 
approved the following programme at the end of its meetings in Tripoli last night: 


] -- Contact the leaders of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front countries and 
assign the general secretariat and executive committee to follow it up within two months 
as from the end of this conference. 


2 -— Open all Arab fronts before the Palestinian armed struggle. 


3 -- Appeal to the Arab youth and students everywhere to boycott all American goods 
and strike against the U.S. interests in the Arab world. 


4 -- Issue regular information bulletins in several languages to be distributed to 
world's youth and students organisations so as to expose the American imperialisn, 
Zionism expansionism and Arab reaction. 


5 -- Organize cultural weeks, photograph exhibitions and documentary films on the 
Zionist imperialist invasion into the Lebanese Arab territories. 


6 — Work locally and nationally to prepare the masses militarily and open military 
training centres to counter the imperialist Zionist and reactionary threat as from the 
summer of 1983. 


7 -- Form joint voluntary working teams for Arab youth students for construction and 
public services the schedule and sites to be determined by the general secretariat and 
the executive committee. 


8 -- Work towards forming international youth and student organisations in cooperation 
with the international students and the international democratic youth union in Europe 
so as to expose the Zionist invasion and its expansionist aggressive nature. 


9 -- Work towards organizing meetings of Arab youth and students living outside the 
Arab homeland to be held from summer 1983. 


10 -- Establishing effective solidarity with the Palestinian and Lebanese people directed 
towards exposing the enemy's barbaric crimes during the Shatila and Sabra massacre and 
to commemorate this in traditional annual student and youth rallies.

11 -- Guarantee chances of admission to schools, universities and institutes for 
students exiled from the occupied lands and for Palestinian and Lebanese students 
whose education was interrupted as a result of the Zionist invasion as well as grant- 
ing them scholarships. The general union of Palestinian students and the Lebanese 
progressive national forces should present proposals on these issues to the execu- 
tive committee and general secretariat within a 2 months period of their issue. 


12 -- Youth and student organizations should take charge of the children of 
Palestinian and Lebanese martyrs. This should be organized by the general union 

of Palestinian students and Lebanese progressive national forces in coordination with 
the general secretariat and the executive committee. 


The emergency central council also issued a message of gratitude to the revolutionary 
leader Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi which said: 


"The emergency central council expresses its highest esteem to the general congress 
of the Jamahiriyah students for their invitation and hosting of this emergency 
meeting and greets the leadership, people, students and youth of the Jamahiriyah. 
It also pledges total solidarity with them until final victory and liberation." 


JANA: © KILLED IN MOROCCAN STUDENT CLASHES 


LD162206 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 16 Dec 82 


[Text] Rabat, 16 Dec (JANA) -- Six people were killed and a number of others were 
wounded and a large number of students were arrested during bloody clashes in the 
last 2 days between the students of Oujda University and the royal Moroccan oppres- 
sive forces. 


These clashes occurred when the royal regime's security forces entered the university 
to disperse a large angry demonstration staged by the students against the ruling 


regime and its suppressive practices. 


The students have been on strike since 24 November protesting bad living and study- 
ing conditions.
