Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery briefing note dated August 1983 reports Libyan SA-6 air defence batteries and other units deployed at Aozou airfield in northern Chad, about 50 nautical miles south of the Libya-Chad border. The site is far from Tripoli, so it has no direct Tripoli link.
• Partial coverage showed seven SA-6 transporter-erector-launchers, two Straight Flush radars, one SA-6 transloader and at least 12 support vehicles.
• Other air defence equipment at the airfield included an SA-9 unit and a ZSU-23/4 unit.
• Other Libyan units at Aozou included a tank company and an artillery battery at a support area north of the runway.
• 1983-08: Imagery shows at least two Libyan SA-6 batteries deployed northwest and southeast of the airfield, the furthest south Libyan SA-6 units have deployed in recent operations.
