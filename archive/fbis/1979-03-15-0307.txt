AMBASSADOR TO CHAD LEAVES ABOARD FRENCH PIANE 


JN121616 Khartoum SUNA in Arabic 1420 GMT 12 Mar 79 JN 


[Text ] N'djamena, 12 March--SUNA has learned that the Libyan ambassador here has contacted 
the French ambassador and asked him to provide the Libyan mission with an aircraft to 
transport himself and Libyan Embassy officials out of Chad, The French ambassador agreed 
to the request by his Libyan counterpart. The Libyan diplomats were flown to the 

capital of Gabon from where they will proceed to Libya. 


During the war in Chad, the Libyan ambassador remained a prisoner in his house as a 
result of the shooting, 


On the other hand, the Libyan ambassador remained a guest of the Sudanese until he left 
Chad. [sentence as received]

ROMANIAN DEPUTY PREMIER ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 
LD141510 Tripoli JANA in English 0827 GMT 14 Mar 79 LD 
[Text] Tripoli, 14 March, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Romanian deputy prime minister, Ion 
Patan, arrived in Tripoli yesterday evening on a visit to the Jamahiriyah due to last 
several days. 
Mr. Patanwas met at Tripoli International Airport by the oil secretary, 'Izz ad-Din al-Mabruk, 
the Romanian ambassador to the Jamahiriyah and a representative from the protocol department 
at the Foreign Secretariat. 
In a statement to the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY correspondent, Mr. Patan said that his visit 
to the Jamahiriyah comes within the context of the development of bilaterai relations between 
the Jamahiriyah and Romania, in the economic and technical fields, for the interests of the 
two peoples. He also praised the existing relations between the two countries.
