Summary (AI): NPIC imagery briefing note on Sebha airfield in southern Libya, dated August 1983, reporting Libyan heavy-lift transporters carrying armored personnel carriers assembling north of the field as resupply for Libyan-backed Chadian dissidents. Sebha is far from Tripoli.
• Resupply efforts to Libyan-backed Chadian dissident forces continued, per the significance statement.
• Twelve BTR-60 APCs were reported, 11 of them on heavy-lift transporters.
• Coordinates given as 26-59-36N 014-28-01E.
• 1983-08-24: Libyan heavy-lift transporters carrying mechanized APCs assembled north of the airfield; twelve BTR-60 APCs (11 on transporters), four empty transporters and one transporter with a possible water tank were present.
