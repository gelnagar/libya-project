Summary (AI): A May 1976 CIA reading-room item reproduces a Libyan news report that Colonel Muammar al-Qadhafi's Revolution Command Council chairman arrived in Malta on an official visit, was welcomed at the airport by Maltese officials, and spoke about historical Arab-Maltese ties and the Maltese language. It has no direct bearing on Tripoli sites.
• The report was carried by the Libyan news agency (JANA) and dated Valletta, 22 May 1976.
• Qadhafi said Libya would help Malta strengthen historical ties and cited Arabic words in the Maltese language, which he said are about 20 per cent of its vocabulary.
• The document is an OCR-degraded translation with garbled text.
• 1976-05-21: Colonel Muammar al-Qadhafi, chairman of the Revolution Command Council, arrived in Malta for an official visit and was welcomed by Maltese President Anthony Pace and officials.
