LIBYA

THIRD BATCH OF SOLDIERS RETURNS FROM CHAD 23 JUN 


LD232240 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 23 Jun 81 


[Excerpt] Benghazi, 23 Jun (JANA)--More units of the Libyan Armed Forces returned to 
Libya today after having helped achieved peace in Chad, and the Chadian people triumph 

in their struggle for freedom. These units are the third batch of Libyan Armed Forces 
which successfully and courageously carried out its mission to restore peace and security 
to Chad. 


A mass popular reception was held for these units this evening in the Martyrs Street in 
Benghazi's Al-Abyar District. The reception was attended by Maj al-Khuwayldi al-Humaydi, 
commander of urban militarization. Thousands of citizens from the basic people's con- 
ferences in Al-Fatah municipality shouted revolutionary slogans for the brave soldiers. 
These soldiers executed the death order of the brother leader by going to Chad to carry 
out the task of liberation alongside the Chadian people and to decide the battle in favor 
of that people and in the interests of the Libyan people and peace and freedom in Africa.
