It covers the Libyan-Moroccan union treaty, a rebuke to French minister Cheysson, and Qadhdhafi's calls to demolish borders and confront the US and Israel.
• The General People's Congress unanimously agreed to the Arab-African union treaty between Libya (SPLAJ) and Morocco, and sent a telegram to King Hassan II.
• Qadhdhafi said the parade was a demonstration of power and that Libyan strike forces were built to liberate Palestine.
• Qadhdhafi said the parade started from the west and moved east, which he interpreted optimistically.
• Qadhdhafi said the Libyan and Moroccan popular conferences were to vote on the federation the same day, and he referred to a Moroccan referendum in which he said 7 million people voted yes.
• Qadhdhafi made an appeal to Libyan pilgrims in Saudi Arabia to cooperate with Saudi authorities and not disturb security in the holy places.
• 1984-09-01: Military parade in honour of the 15th anniversary of the 1 September Revolution, at which Qadhdhafi gave a live speech calling for the demolition of borders and the liberation of Palestine.
