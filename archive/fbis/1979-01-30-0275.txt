LIBYA

ECEVIT ADDRESSES TURKISH WORKERS IN TRIPOLI, BENGHAZI 
TA291614 Ankara ANATOLIA in Faglish 1550 GMT 29 Jan 79 TA 


[Text] Tripoli, Jan 29, A.A. (Special to the ANATOLIA Agency)--Ecevit's scheduled return 
to Turkey from Libya today was postponed for a few hourg to permit technicians to work 
out the details of the long-term economic cooperation agreement between Turkey and 

Libya, it was announced today. 


Ecevit flew to Benghazi this morning to talk to Turkish workers. He is scheduled to 
return to Tripoli in the afternoon. According to the tentative programme, he will fly 
from Tripoli to Turkey either late tonight or early Tuesday morning. 


The premier Sunday addressed a group of Turkish workers in Tripoli and when a small 
crowd tried to interrupt his speech with slogans demanding freedom to the Turkish "peoples,' 
Ecevit told them that he would never permit slogans aimed at dividing the Turkish nation. 


Ecevit said the integrity of Turkey could not be sacrificed to divisive slogans. 


Responding to shouts that the only way out for Turkey was a revolution, Ecevit said every 
country had its own special conditions. In Turkey, he said, several problems had been 
solved in the past through revolutionary action and reforms. But revolution was not 

the only solution. 


"No one can force Turkey to accept something it refuses to accept. Whatever you say, 
nobody will be able to push Turkey down a path which is alien to the Turkish nation. 
There are no peoples in Turkey, but only a single monolithic people," Ecevit said. 


In response to further slogans that Turkey should say no to foreign bases on her soil, 
Ecevit said Turkey would not fall into the trap set up by imperialism. He said it was 
evident that foreign forces, aware that they could never trample Turkey from outside, 
were provoking incidents inside the country andwere bringing brother against brother to 
foster their sinister aims. 


During the same meeting, Ecevit told Turkish workers that Turkish teachers would be sent 
to Libya to teach the children of expatriates there.

LIBYA REPORTEDLY DISPLEASED WITH IRAQI-SYRIAN UNITY 


LD291229 Lonmion AL-HAWADITH in Arabic 26 Jan 79 p 27 LD 


["Damasous bureau" report: "Libya Withdraws Syrian Arms Finance in Protest Against 
Unity With Iraq"] 


[Excerpts] From the Sheraton Hotel in Damascus the Iraqi delegation to the unity talks 
with Syria maintains 24-hour contact with Baghdad by hotline, Iraqi Revolution Command 
Council member Tariq ‘Aziz is shuttling between Damascus ani Baghdad, 


The fantastic speed with which agreement was reached between the two countries was ex- 
plained by an Iraqi official as follows: There can be no lukewarm relations between 
Baghdad and Damascus; relations are either fierce or ardently friendly. There is no 
middle of the road, 


In the past the two countries have failed to agree on a joint railroad project with 
90-percent financial contribution by Iraq and 10 percent from Syria, despite the pro- 
posal to deposit the money in a foreign bank as a guarantee against political ups and 
downs, But now a joint "national charter" has been concluded between the two countries, 
and the process of unification during the past 3 months has been going on smoothly, 
This is probably the reason why the optimists expect the declaration of an immediate 
unity merger, similar to that of 1958, during President Ahmad Hasan al-Bakr!s forth- 
coming visit to Damascus, But Tariq ‘Aziz said; "This political unity will not be 
declared during the visit, although a review of what has been achieved will be made," 
This will be followed by the establishment of a new program for completing the declara- 
tion of unity which, according to an Iraqi official, will take place in 6 weeks! time. 


The official said that this will be followed by a new summit between Al-Bakr and Al-Asad, 
at the end of which a constitutional declaration and a party declaration will be issued, 
The two draft declarations have in fact been prepared, 


A Syrian official stressed that the declaration of "Iraqi-Syrian unity" will not be a 
decision from above, 


The official added that the unity of the two ruling Arab Ba'th parties is inevitable, 
Since no party elements in either country will erect obstacles to achieving party 
unity. Veteran Ba'th Party members who ruled in the past in Syria and Iraq will be 
reinstated on the grounds that they are part of the party history and the political 
history of both countries, 


The Syrian official said: The "Al-Asad style" of removing certain human obstacles to 

the achievement of unity is the same as his style in implementing the campaign against 
"illicit profiteering," that is, punishment of the party or government official concerned, 
following a deadline and meted out in a normal and calm manner, 


Question; What is the attitude of this unity toward the two giants? 


An Iraqi official replied: It will constitute an iniependent national Arab stand by 
the United States and the Soviet Union, 


He added: Libya has not been pleased with the Syrian-Iraqi rapprochement. After 
informing Moscow that it was ready to finance the arming of Syria, following the declara- 
tion of the steadfastness and confrontation front--which included Syria, Algeria, Libya, 
South Yemen and the PLO--it changed its mind and informed the Soviet capital that it 
retracted its pledge in light of Syria and Iraq!s plan to establish unity.
