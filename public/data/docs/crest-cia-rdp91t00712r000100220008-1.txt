Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC graphic briefing on the Libya-Chad conflict describes Libyan forces resupplying and deploying in Libya and northern Chad in support of Chadian dissidents, including armor and aircraft at Fada, Ogoui and Kufra. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• Fada is about 135 nm northeast of Arada, a probable government/French-controlled town.
• Libyan forces near Fada included at least 14 BTR-60 APCs, four T-54/55 tanks and 15 heavy-lift transporters.
• Libyan air order of battle at Ogoui included five SF.260 light-attack aircraft, one CH-47 and two Hind helicopters.
• Libyan forces were at Faya, the southernmost confirmed deployment, while French/government forces deployed at Biltine.
• 1983-08: Libyan mechanized infantry battalion elements, a tank company and support units deployed north of Fada airfield.
