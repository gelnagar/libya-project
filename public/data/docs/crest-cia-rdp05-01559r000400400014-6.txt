Summary (AI): A Tripoli Domestic Service broadcast (25 April 1985) carries excerpts of a speech by Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to the Tripoli Municipality People's Congress, on tribal allocation of posts, revolutionary committees, and the role of local popular security. It also includes a brief Algerian item on Casbah protests.
• Qadhdhafi said the congresses of Tripoli, Banghazi, Surt and Ghdamis were ideal, and that selection in 13 provinces was discredited and would be reelected.
• He said the traditional police would disappear and be replaced by people's security wearing civilian attire, present at all meetings.
• Each basic popular conference would nominate its own member of each sector, replacing the joint vocational committees.
• He said anyone who forms a party after the revolution must be crushed.
• 1985-04-24: Session of the Tripoli Municipality People's Congress addressed by Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, broadcast 25 April 1985.

{Text] The inhabitants of the Casbah quarter in the Algerian 
capital staged a demonstration today in protest against the lack 
of security and shortage of water. AFP quoting eyewitnesses said 
that the demonstrators proceeded to the center of the city and 
the headquarters of the ruling party in Algeria — the only party 
in the country. The police used tear gas to disperse the dem- 
onstrators many of whom have been injured and others were 
arrested. The agency added that elements of the security forces 
surrounded the Casbah quarter this afternoon and began to 
dispersc groups of people and surrounded the headquarters of the 
only ruling party in Algeria in order to protect it against renewed 
demonstrations.
