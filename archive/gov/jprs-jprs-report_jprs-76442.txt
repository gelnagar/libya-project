LDO11108 Tripoli JANA in English 0827 GMT 1 Sep 80 


[Text] Tripoli, September 1 (Jamahiriyah News Agency)--President ‘Ali 
Nasir of the Yemen People's Democratic Republic sent a letter of greeting 
to the World Conference of Solidarity with the Libyan Arab people. The 
letter reads as follows: 


"I would like to convey my fraternal greetings to all the conference 
participants whose presence is an expression of the unity of the peoples 
and of tie struggle of the liberation and progressive peoples against 
imperialism and Zionism. The national progressive source of the Libyan 
regime and its role in the anti-imperialist Arab struggle is met with 
the support and solidarity of the Arab and world liberation movements 
and progressive forces. The traitor al-Sadat's regime threat to the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah is not only aimed at harming 
the national progressive role of the Libyan Arab people, but is also 
directed against the Arab liberation movement as well as against the 
steadfastness and confrontation countries which are playing the role of 
exposing the Camp David accords and foiling the capitulatory course. 


Dear friends, the continuous conspiracies and the aggressive threats 
against the Jamahiriyah are only part of the imperialist-Zionist designs 
which have also been escalating against the Lebanese patriotic movements 
and the Palestinian resistance. These designs are represented in different 
forms of obstacles against the progressive regimes of Syria and Algeria, 
as well as the obstruction of any revolutionary changes by our progressive 
regime in South Yemen. The conspiracy being designed against the 
Palestinian people and the continuation of the so-called self-rule nego- 
tiations and the preparation for other initiatives which ignore the 
national rights of the Palestinian people for self-determination and to 
establish their independent state. [Sentence as received] 


The increased military presence of the U.S.A. in the Arab region, such 
as the new facilities given by the regime of traitor Qabus and that of 
Siad Barre, threatens the Arab liberation movements in the Red Sea and 
Indian Ocean region, and encircle our progressive regime of South Yemen 
and the anti-imperialist course of the Ethiopian revolution. 























Dear friends, U.S. colonialism is a direct threat to the peace and 
security of the Gulf region and the Arab peninsula. Our Yemeni Socialist 
Party pledges the necessity of liquidating the U.S. military presence 

in the region as well as of escalating the struggle against the Camp 

David accords and the capitulatory course which are aimed at harming the 
Palestinian people's rights for ectablishing their independent state 

under the leadership of the Palestine Liberation Organization (P.L.0.). 
The Steadfastness and Confrontation Front should play an important role 

to foil the U.S.-Zionist-Sadatist alliance and to confront the imperialist 
attack on the Arab region. 


Dear friends, we re-affirm our support for the Libyan Arab people in 
their struggle against the different conspiracies as a result of their 
anti-imperialist stand and the national progressive role in supporting 
the Liberation forces of the world. 


We finally wish you all the success in your conference, peace upon you."

LIBYAN ENVOY TO BAHRAIN ON LIBYAN-ARAB RELATIONS 
JNO20857 Manama Gulf News Agency in Arabic 0635 GMT 2 Sep 80 


[Excerpte| Manama, 2 Sep (GNA)--Muhammad Ramadan al-Ru'ubi, Libyan 
ambassador to Bahrain, has praised hie country's relations with the 

Arab Gulf estates. He said: Libyan-Gulf relations have prospered since 
the 1973 war. There is no alternative to Arab unity, especially now that 
imperialism and Zioniem have succeeded in removing the biggest Arab 

power from the Pan-Arab arena. 


Speaking to the Gulf News Agency, the Libyan ambassador added that Libya 
and the Arab Gulf states have tecome one front and that Col Mu‘ ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi's tour of the Gulf last year further consolidates these 
relations. 


On the Saudi crown prince's call for holy war to liberate the occupied 
Arab territories, Ambassador al-Ru'ubi said: Libya supports every 
sincere thought and faithful opinion. We consider Prince Fahd's call 
as an extension to the appeal made by Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi before the 
1973 war for the Pan-Arabiem of the battle and fedayeen action. 


On Libyan-Egyptian relations following the Camp David accords, Ambassador 
al-Ru'ubi said: Libya has warned against the results of these accords. 
Developments have now borne out what we expected and feared. Here is 
al-Sadat now, surrounding the Arabian peninsula with U.S. bases. Egypt, 
which at one time was the springboard for Arab action, has now become a 
frontline base for aggression against the Arab nation. 


The ambassador added: The bases situated west of Cairo and Qina in 
Egypt, Berbera and Kenya have placed the Arabian peninsula between 
pincers. The bases are there to threaten and intimidate the Arab states 
and their peoples and leaders, so that they will take no action against 
U.S. and Zionist interests and so that they will be drawn into the 
negotiations process. I am confident that the nation's leaders will not 
be deceived by this trick.

Ambassador al-Ru'ubi disclosed that Libya is currently using its good 
offices to mediate between Iraq and Syria, in order to achieve Arab 

unity, especially in the current phase, in which the Arab nation needs 

the support of every Arab effort. He said: It is incumbent upon us to 

put forth every Arab effort for the battle on the eastern front, especially 
since the Camp David partners, led by the United States and Zionism, 

are doing their best to keep this front weak by sowing discord and by 
driving a wedge between Iraq and Syria, to prevent any rapprochement 
between them. 


On Libyan-Iranian relations and Iranian accusations against Libya, the 
Libyan ambassador said: Zionism, imperialism, those who begrudge the 

1 September revolution in particular and the Arab nation in general, 

and the states which began concluding treaties and agreements with them 
after the 1973 war are trying to provoke sedition and division within the 
Arab and Islamic nation. 


Ambassador al-Ru'ubi asserted that Libya's primary objective has always 
been to uphold Islam, to support Muslims and to restore the past glories 
of the Islamic nation and of Arab power. 


The Libyan ambassador said that his country's relations with Tunisia are 
based on love and sincerity, in view of the historic and cultural ties 
binding the two countries. He asserted that anything that affects the 
Tunisian people alsc affects the Libyan people. Libya, he reiterated, 
cannot dispense with any additional Arab efforts for the cause of the 
Pan-Arab battle. He added: We cannot dispense with Iraq or Syria or 
with any part of the Arab homeland. Our primary goal is to pool the 
resources of the Arab nation.
