• Document IAC-D-57/37, dated 25 June 1956, is a post-mortem on NIE 36.5-56 (The Outlook for US Interests in Libya, dated 19 June 1956).
• The chief estimative problem was the outcome and implications for US interests of the political struggle likely to follow the death of the king, described as speculative judgment rather than a specific intelligence deficiency.
• The appropriate IAC agencies were asked to levy requirements in their fields to overcome the stated deficiencies.
