LIBYA

JANA Reports Bombing of Omdurman Radio 
LDI61739 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1708 GMT 16 Mar 84 


(“The Destruction of the Sudanese Radio and TV Station” — 
JANA headline] 


[Text] Cairo, 16 Mar (JANA) — The Egyptian MENA and the 
ASSOCIATED PRESS have reported that a state of emergency 
was declared in Sudan Friday afternoon, while indications point 


NORTH AFRICA Q1 


to an attempted revolution by the Sudanese Air Force against the 
regime of the lackey Numayri. 


According to the official statement issued by the Khartoum 
government, which broadcasts using the facilities of Radio Lon- 
don [presumably the BBC] and the ASSOCIATED PRESS, the 
Omdurman radio station was bombed from the air at 1130 
Friday morning, 16 March. The situation is still unclear in 
Sudan. 


The news about the destruction of Omdurman radio comes at a 
time when the popular revolution is escalating in the south of 
Sudan, increasing the dangers to the regime of the lackey 
Numayri. 


Sudanese Minister Cited 
LDI72115 Tripoli JANA in English 1903 GMT 17 Mar 84 


[Text] Khartoum, Jumada Thani 13, March 17, JAMAHIR- 
IYAH NEWS AGENCY — The information minister of hire- 
ling Numayri’s regime, Muhammad Salihin, officially admitted 
today that the Omdurman television and radio station have 
stopped functioning after being destroyed by an aircraft belong- 
ing to the Sudanese Air Force yesterday morning. 


In an interview broadcast by the Sudanese Government’s news 
agency this afternoon, he said that programs are broadcast by 
the so-called Wadi Nil [Nile valley] radio which broadcasts its 
programs through Sawt al-‘Arab [Voice of the Arabs] radio 
waves in Egypt. He also said that the Egyptian regime’s radio 
stations and newspapers since yesterday took over the 
broadcasting of the programs and information prepared by the 
Sudanese Government, following the destruction of the Omdur- 
man radio and television station. 


JANA Cites BBC Report 
LDI71944 Tripoli JANA in English 1741 GMT 17 Mar 84 


[Text] Khartoum, Jumada Thani 13, March 17, JAMAHIR- 
IYAH NEWS AGENCY — The British Broadcasting 
Corporation, quoting its correspondent in East Africa, reported 
this morning that there was evidence proving that the airplane 
which bombed the radio and television stations belonged to the 
Sudanese Air Force, and the person who piloted it was one of the 
pilots of Numayri’s regime. 


London Group ‘Confirms’ Bombing 
LD162358 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 2300 GMT 16 Mar 84 


[Excerpt] The Sudanese Democratic Union in London has con- 
firmed the bombing by the Sudanese Air Force of the Sudanese 
radio and television station in Omdurman. A union source has 
stated that this is an integral part of the escalating popular 
revolution against the Numayri regime. He appealed to the 
Sudanese people’s masses to be cohesive in order to destroy this 
collapsing regime.

Numayri Allegations on Bombing Raid Refuted 
LD172102 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1937 GMT 17 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 17 March — The popular committee of the 
General People’s Committee for Foreign Liaison today issued a 
statement refuting the allegation of the ruler of Sudan that the 
Jamahiriyah was behind the air raid on the Omdurman 
broadcasting station. Here is the text of the statement: 


The Popular Committee of the General People’s Committee for 
Foreign Liaison categorically refutes the lies made by the ruler 
of Sudan who said that the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah is behind the raid on the Omdurman broadcasting 
station that took place on the evening of 16 March 1984. It 
provides the following clarification of the situation: 


It has become customary for regimes which face popular rev- 
olution and which are exposed to blows from their people to try 
and accuse the Jamahiriyah, because it is a revolutionary state 
that supports revolution and aligns itself on the side of the cause 
of freedom everywhere. The events taking place in Sudan today 
are no more than the result of the popular revolution which 
embraced all popular sections, and this includes the Sudanese 
Armed Forces. Consequently, it is not surprising that the air raid 
on the Omdurman broadcasting station was the action of the 
revolutionary elements in the Sudanese Air Force. 


The Khartoum ruler has persisted in blaming the revolutionary 
Jamahiriyah whenever the Sudanese people tighten the grip. The 
Jamahiriyah challenges the Khartoum ruler to provide any evi- 
dence of truth in his false, contradictory charges. Is it conceivable 
that the movement of a foreign plane can be monitored, its course 
followed, and its identity and make known without an effort to 
intercept and chase it, and open fire on it? This is a farcical 
matter which calls for mockery and ridicule, and if not farcical 
it points out that the Sudanese Armed Forces in Khartoum — 
particularly the air defense and the Air Force — had full 
knowledge of this and gave assistance for the raid to reach its 
target in the heart of Omdurman. 


The Sudanese people know the Khartoum ruler has long made 
false allegations about Sudan being exposed to external aggres- 
sion as a pretext for gaining the sympathy of the U.S. Admin- 
istration and the colonialist forces, to justify granting them bases 
and military facilities and asking them to intervene to prop up 
his regime, which is about to fall. As the Khartoum ruler has 
failed to confront the popular revolution which reached the 
outskirts of his palace, he is trying to involve Egypt in a war with 
the Jamahiriyyah and Ethiopia, and with the GUNT [Transi- 
tional Government of National Unity] Government in Chad, and 
to let it drown with him in the Sudanese jungle. One is bewildered 
that the Egyptian regime would respond to the absurdities of the 
Khartoum ruler, a regime which took Egypt out of the confronta- 
tion with the Zionist enemy on the pretext that it does not want 
war. 


Finally, the popular committee of the General People’s Bureau 
for Foreign Liaison is fully convinced that the Khartoum ruler is 
reaping what he has sown against his people and his neighbors, 
and that if he continues sowing the wind we warn him that he
