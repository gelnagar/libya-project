JALLUD SPEAKS AT 6 FEB DINNER FOR CSSR!S STROUGAL 
LD071622 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 GMI 7 Feb 79 LD 


[Exceyvpts ] Tripoli--Staff Major 'abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud held a dinner banquet last 
night in honor of Lubomir Strougal, prime minister of the CSSR, and the accompanying 
delegation, The banquet was attended by some brother secretaries and heads of the dip- 
lomatic missions accredited to the Jamahiriyah,. 


Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Jallud delivered a speech at the beginning, welcoming Mr 
Strougal and the accompanying delegation on his second visit to Libya. He pointed out 
that although Libyan Arab-Czechoslovak relations began after the rise of the Great 

1 September Revolution, Arab-Czechoslovak relations began in the sixties, 


He pointed out that the Libyan Arab people take a deep interest in friendship with the 
Czechoslovak people and the socialist countries and feel the need to develop these 
relations, He said that the development of these relations and the realization of 
effective cooperation are the joint responsibility of both sides, 


He said that Libya fully believes in peace because our Islamic faith and our new theory 
call for peace, affection and happiness, We believe in harmony, as we believe in 
disarmament because we do not want to pay one dinar to purchase weapons because we need 
to spend it in the sphere of achieving progress and prosperity. 


He went on to say: However, in the present circumstances and conditions which the Arab 
nation is experiencing, the Arab people in general believe that the rifle is more 
important than a loaf of bread--not for the love of the rifle but to defend the loaf 

of bread, life and existence, 


Staff Major Jallud affirmed the Jamahiriyah!s support for all liberation movements in 
the world because the freedom of the Jamahiriyah is part of the freedom of the struggling 
people, He pointed out that the Arab revolution forces stand entrenched with the 
socialist countries against exploitation, colonialism, and reactionary and Zionist mono- 
polies, He said that the states following the path of development have no choice but 

to struggle to realize genuine economic and cultural independence, instead of a sham of 
political independence, with the aid of the revolutionary forces in the world and the 
socialist countries and by controlling their economic resources, Staff Major Jallud 
stressed that the peoples of the Third World cannot develop their independence in the 
shadow of an unjust world economic and monetary system, which is the natural byproduct 
of the colonial stage and which confirms the exploitation of the poor by the rich,

find a new world econom‘c system, because their presence would strengthen the stance of 
the developing countries, He pointed to the need to hold a dialog between the socialist 
and capitalist countries in order to develop the economy of the poor states and to enable 
them to control their own natural resources, 


Joint Communique 
1D072224 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1900 GMT 7 Feb 79 ID 


[Text ] Tripoli--A joint communique has been issued in Tripoli and Prague following the 
visit of Czechoslovak Premier Lubomir Strougal to the Jamahiriyah from 5 to 7 February. 
Here is the text: 


At the official invitation of staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, member of the General 
Secretariat of the General People's Congress in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah, the premier of the Czechoslovak Socialist Republic, Lubomir Strougal, paid 
an official and friendly visit to Libya from 5 to 7 February 1979. 


The leader of the Great 1 September Revolution, brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
received the Czechoslovak republic, and exchanged with him views on the strengthening 
of bilateral relations and on the current international situation, 


Talks were held between staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, member of the General 
Secretariat of the General People's Congress, and Czechoslovak Premie lIubomir Strougal in 
an atmosphere of friendship and mutual understanding, The two sides es “anged information 
regarding the development of their respective countries, The premier of Czechoslovakia 
witnessed the achievements of the Great 1 September Revolution in the fields of economic 
and social development and in the sphere of the people's authority, The premier of 
Czechoslovakia praised these achievements, 


The two sides exchanged views on the development of bilateral relations and in this 
connection praised the historic visit of brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader 
of the Great 1 September Revolution, to Czechoslovakia--the visit which laid down the 
deep and wide foundation for the strengthening and increasing cooperation between the 
two countries in the political, economic, scientific and cultural fields, They 
expressed their satisfaction with the positive progress made in these fields, They 
noted with satisfaction that relations are proceeding according to the plan which was 
formulated during that visit. 


The two sides exchanged views on the current international situation and noted that 
their views were identical with regard to the issues they discussed, 


The two sides discussed the dangerous situation in the Middle East and strongly 
condemned in this connection the Camp David agreements which are regarded as being an 
extension of the colonialist-Zionist conspiracy against the Arab nation and the question 
of Palestine, The two sides condemned all subsequent results of the Camp David 
agreements, 


The two sides condemned also the repeated Israeli acts of aggression on southern 
Lebanon, and the Israeli shelling of the villages and the Palestinian refugee camps 
with the aim of liquidating the Palestinian resistance movement and its heroic struggle 
and of occupying more Arab land, 


Vv. 8 Feb 79 NORTH AFRICA 


They reaffirmed their countries! solidarity with the struggle of the Palestinian people 
under the leadership of the Palestine Liberation Organization, the legitimate and sole 
representative of that people, 


The Czechoslovak side highly appreciated the efforts being made by the Resistance and 
Steadfastness Front to frustrate the attempts of colonialism, reaction and Zionism to 
bring about the unilateral capitulationist solutions, and lauded the role being played 
by Libya in this connection, 


The Czechoslovak side highly appreciated the results of the Ninth Arab Summit Conference 
which was held in Baghdad and its subsequent results in reviving Arab solidarity against 
the Camp David capytulationist agreements which constitute a serious threat to the Arab 
peoples! vital interests, 


The two sides emphasize’ the importance of cooperation and friendship between the Arab 
people and the peoples of the socialist canmmunity, They expressed the usefulness of 
deepening this cooperation and friendship to achieve joint aims in the service of the 
just Arab cause, progress and peace, 


The two sides declared that a just and lasting peace in the Arab area cannot be achieved 
except by the liberation of all the occupied territories and the guaranteeing of the 
legitimate rights of the Palestinian people, including their right to return to their 
homeland and determine their own destiny and set up their own independent state, 


The two sides discussed the situation in the African Continent. They affirmed their 
support for the liberation movements throughout the continent, particularly in Zimbabwe, 
Namibia and South Africa, The two sides emphasized that the only solution to these 
issues is to grant full independence by means of recognizing the Patriotic Front and 
SWAPO--the two sole legitimate representatives of their peoples, They condemned the 
colonialist and reactionary attempts aimed at interfering in the affairs of the continent 
and at hindering and isolating the progressive forces and the national revolutions there. 


The two sides also asserted the right of the Saharan people to self-determination in 
accordance with the UN resolutions, 


The two sides stressed the need for noninterference by imperialism in the African 
Continent and for letting the Africans solve their own problems by themselves in accordance 
with the UN Charter and the Organization of African Unity (OAU) Charter. 


The two sides concemned the attempts by imperialism which wishes to impose its domination 
anew over the African Continent and asserted their determination to assist and support 

the African people who are struggling for the liquidation of the foreign imperialist bases 
from the continent and for their freedom and independence, 


The two sides noted that the nations! struggle against aggression and the policy of 
repression, for peace and accord, for halting the arms race, for the development of 
(?international) cooperation based on mutual respect, and for national independence and 
sovereignty and noninterference in internal affairs is gaining strength in the present 
international situation,

The two sides are jointly convinced that the continuation of the arms race is one of the 
main dangers threatening world peace and international security, and that the urgent 
task for guaranteeing world peace lies in the ending of the arms race and in transforming 
the latter into a total and comprehensive disarmament, They consider the efforts being 
made in this connection for the convening of an international conference on disarmament 
in which all states will participate on an equal footing as being the most important task 
of international politics at the present time. At the same time they reaffirmed the 
peoples' right to defend their freedom and to struggle against reaction, colonialism 

and racism, 


The two sides noted the significance of the European security conference and stressed 
the reed for the continuations of the implementation of the resolutions of the Helsinki 
Conference, They expressed their support for these resolutions which have helped to 
secure permanent peace in Europe, They reaffirmed that European security is strongly 
linked with the security in the Mediterranean and the Arab region, and therefore have 
called for the liquidation of the colonialist military bases in the Mediterranean so 
that the latter will become a lake of peace for the good of its own peoples as well as 
for the peoples of the world, Libya appreciates the positive and effective role which 
the Czechoslovak Socialist Republic is playing in implementing the conference's final 
resolutions in order to strengthen peace and develop international cooperation, 


The two sides expressed deep satisfaction with the progress of the nonalined movement 
and the efforts this movement is making to consolidate peace and stability throughout the 
world and to support the national liberation movements and the issues which interest all 
of mankind, 


In this cmnection they stressed the significance of the sixth summit conference of the 
heads of state and government of the nonalined countries which will be held in Havana 
this year, 


The two sides also praised the positive role being played by the nonalined group in laying 
down a new international economic system based on justice, equality and the peoples! right 
to control and administer their own natural resources in a manner that will bring about 
their economic development, The Czechoslovak Socialist Republic appreciated the construc- 
tive role which Libya is playing within the nonalined movement in an attempt to consolidate 
its anticolonial policy, 


The two sides exchanged views regarding the current situation in Iran and strongly con- 
demned all forms of pressure and colonialist interference in the internal affairs of this 
country which are directed against the interests of the Iranian people, 


The two sides reaffirmed their adherence to the UN principles and aims and their apprec- 
iation for the role which this organization is playing to consolidate world peace and 
international security. 


The two sides expressed their satisfaction with the development of economic, scientific and 
technical cooperation between their countries which is proceeding in a spirit of mutual 
understanding, 


The joint Libyan-Czechoslovak C ammittee discussed various aspects of economic, commercial 
and technical cooperation as well as cooperation in the fields of energy and health and 
agriculture, and the exchange of specialists, vocational training, technical education 
and communications, The two sides agreed that there are new horizons for the development 
of such cooperation in the interest of the two countries, 


At the end of the talks of the joint committee an agreement was signed,

Strougal and with the results of his talks with the Libyan leaders, The Czechoslovak 
premier expressed his thanks for the hospitality which he was given during the visit and 
extended an invitation to staff Maj 'aAbd as-Salam Jallud, member of the General Secre-
