LIBYA

POLISH MINISTER MEETS WITH LIBYAN OFFICIALS 
LDO32004Y Tripoli JANA in English 1833 GMT 3 Jun 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, June 3, JANA=-Mr ‘Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, chairman of the General Popular 
Committee, received this morning Mrs Maria Milczarek, Polish minister of local economy and 
preservation of environment. Mr Abu Zayd Durdah, secretary of municipalities, attended 
the meeting. 


Technical Protocol Signed 


LDO32005Y Tripoli JANA in English 1844 GMT 3 Jun 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, June 3, JANA--A protocol for a common working programme in the scientific 
and technical fields was signed today between the Jamahiriyah and Poland. The protocol 
was signed by Mr Abu Zayd 'Umar Durdah, the municipalities secretary, and the Polish 
minister of local economy, administration and environment protection, Mrs Maria Milczarek. 


In a speech delivered during the signing ceremony, the municipalities secretary said 
that the agreement gives a new boost to cooperation between the two countries. Talking 
about the future setting up of a Libyan-Polish friendly society, he said that it will be 
a new step towards increased cooperation and reinforced relations between the two 
friendly peoples. 


Under the working programme protocol, Poland will provide tenders for the following 
projects: 


1. Surveying and map drawing, and preparation of pians and technical specifications for 
public utilities in the Jamahiriyah's towns and villages, a list of which is to be sup- 
plied within a month of the signing of the agreement. 


2. Studies and schedules for municipal public utilities projects, including, 
A. Assessment and evaluation of the projects executed. 
B. Arrangement of data for plans. 


C. Treining of five Libyan Arab data-processing technicians in the application of this 
technique. 


3. Poland will also train Libyans in the field of surveying anu data processing. 
Training will take place in the Jamahiriyah and Poland as well. 


4, Carry out the necessary studies and make suggestions for long-term training in the 
field of municipal utilities. 


The Polish side will submit its tenders concerning the training one month after receiving 
the work requirement from the Libyan side. The two siues see the possibility of setting 
up a joint venture to carry out the transformational plan projects in the sector of 
municipalities, including planning. After the necessary provisional studies have been 
carried out the Libyan Arab side will invite Polish firms and companies to submit their 
tenders for consultancy and execution of works in the field of municipalities.

The Polish side will present a proposal for the research system in the field of environmen. 
protection and control, including the specifications of the system. The Libyan Arab 

side will ¢noose the subjects in the field of environment research and its control that 
concern it. The Polish side will submit its tenders in this respect. 


The Polish side stressed its wish to cooperate with the Libyan Arab side by delegating 
Polish specialists in the sector of municipalities, The Libyan Arab side expressed its 
wish to obtain technical expertise from Poland, namely 139 engineers with the required 
experience, The Polish side expressed its readiness to delegate 40 engineers in accordance 
with its capacity in 1978. 


The Libyan Arab side will invite mayors to visit the heads of people's municipalities in 
Misurata, Durna, and Al-Zawiya. The Polish side will invite three Libyan Arab specialists 
so that they can become acquainted with the achievements of the Polish People's Republic 
in the fields stipulated in the items of the agreement, and the Libyan Arab side will 
determine the subjects of talks. 


In fulfillment of their wish to develop cooperation between the two sides and to put into 
effect the agreement concluded for this purpose, the two sides have agreed to communicate 
with regard to the implementation of the agreement through the Bureau of Foreign Affairs 
at the Ministry of Administration, Local Economy and Enviroment Protection in the Polish 
People's Republic and the General Administration for Planning and Followup at the 
Municipalities Secretariat. 


In view of the huge size of the joint scientific and tecinical work stipulated in the 
agreement, this agreement has been made to be extended at execution, in order to prepare 
for the draft programme of 1980, It has been decided to hold a meeting by the end of the 
second quarter of 1979 in Warsaw which will be attended by the representatives of the
