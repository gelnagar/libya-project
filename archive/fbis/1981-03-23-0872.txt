LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI COMMENTS ON FOREIGN AFFAIRS ISSUES 


NC221954 Paris AFP in English 1852 22 Mar 81 
(Quotation marks as received] 


[Text] Paris, 23 Mar (AFP)--Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi said in an interview to 
be broadcast here today that "for now" he did not wish to see a change in "the balance of 
forces between the two super-powers in the Middle East and the Mediterranean." 


In his comments to interviewer Christian Malard of Radio-TV Luxembourg (RTP), Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi emphasized his country's "neutrality." 


"If Libya was allied with the Soviet Union," he said, "this would provoke a major change 
in the balance of forces" between the USSR and the United States. He added that it was 
unfortunate that the U.S.-USSR confrontation should have spread to Africa. 


"The blind support by the Americans of the Israelis could lead to a confrontation between 
the super-powers," he said, adding, "I fear that the Jews could lead the world into a 
third world war, as they led it into the Second World War." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi also stressed the "dangers" that he saw growing out of the "installa- 
tion of American bases in Somalia, Oman, Egypt and Saudi Arabia."" He said, too, that there 
were similar risks in the “installation of medium range missiles in Europe, the impasse 

in disa: iament talks and French attempts to return to Africa." 


In respect to the situation in Chad, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that he feared that the 
withdrawal of Libyan troops "would be impeded by the concentration of Egyptian and Sudanese 
troops at the Chad-Sudan frontier." He also said that the "resurgence of banditry in Chad 
and the recruitment of mercenaries in the Sudan could undercut Chad's security." 


The colonel said that he considered "the making of the atomic bomb a crime against humanity. 
We do not intend to make this bomb," he said, and supported the "destruction of all nuclear 
weapons in the world" and the cancellation of all nuclear military programs." 


However, he said, "all countries have the right to use nuclear science for peaceful ends." 


Noting that relations between France and Libya have recently been strained, Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi said that he "did not believe that France and Libya were on the verge of a 
total break "because of the French embargo on the gunboats ordered three years ago." He 
said that the embargo harmed France's image in the world. But he said that he "always 
favored a French-Libyan entente and a conference at a high level." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that, while French President Valery Giscard d'Estaing is a 
“gentleman, certain interests of the French people had suffered" during his seven years 
of office. He said that Mr Giscard d'Estaing had committed "several errors in respect to 
France's interests in Africa and the Arab world." 


The Libyan leader mentioned the case of ousted Central African emperor Bokassa and said 
that he, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, knew some "very dangerous personal secrets of his (the 
ex-monarch's) relations" with Mr Giscard d'Estaing. However, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said 
"we do not wish to become involved in this affair. We have no intention of deriving any 
profit from it."

JANA SCORES REPORTED SUDANESE PROVINCES PLAN 


LD220954 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 22 Mar 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 22 Mar (JANA)--According to reports received from Khartoum, Sudan is 
about to witness sharp internal developments and the regime is about to face hard times 
because of popular wrath. 


In mid-April the regime will implement a suspicious plan which aims at dividing northern 
Sudan into six provinces, with each province having its own nationality and flag and its 
own independent constitutional status. 


With the implementation of this plan, the ruling reactionary regime will complete the 
preparations for the reorganization of Sudan's internal conditions in a manner which 
would make it possible to break up the unity of the Sudanese people and destroy their 
heritage and revolutionary struggle traditions. With the formation of these provinces and 
the demarcation of their borders, it will become easier for Sudan to accept the establish- 
ment of military bases throughout Sudan by the American-Zionist-Egyptian military alliance. 


Arab diplomatic sources in Khartoum affirm that the new plan, from a scientific and 
geopolitical aspect, means the transformation of Sudan from a political entity to a mere 


geographical term. 


JANA ATTACKS EGYPTIAN-SUDANESE NORMALIZATION 


LD220920 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 22 Mar 81 


{Text] Tripoli, 22 Mar (JANA)--The JANA political editorhas commented on yesterday's 
announcement of the resumption of full diplomatic relations between Sudan and Egypt, 
affirming that it exposes the Sudanese regime and proves it has fallen in the lap of 
imperialism and is riding on the bandwagon of the policy of the Egyptian Government, which 
is in league with imperialism, reaction and Zionism. 


The exchange of ambassadors between the two regimes, the political editor added, was an 
act of clinging together by the frightened, the defeated and those isolated from their 
peoples. He affirmed that Numayri's attempt to seek the protection of the defeated will 
not save him from the clutches of the Sudanese people. 


This rallying and open falling into the lap of imperialism by reactionary forces places 

on the shoulders of the living revolutionary forces in the Arab homeland grave tasks and 
responsibilities, so that they may break the links of the reactionary alliance chain 

which is supported by the United States and the imperialist and reactionary countries. 

It also makes it necessary for us to be on the alert and to be able to confront and secure 
victory for the Palestinian cause. 


The political editor pointed out that the resumption of relations between the Egyptian 
and Sudanese agent regimes proves the soundness of the vision of the Great 1 September 
Revolution, which has persistently warned against the organic cohesion between these two 
reactionary regimes and against the threat they pose to the Arab nation and its causes. 


The political editor asks: Will the revolutionary forces move now to check this rallying 
and to seek revenge for the Arab dignity and to achieve victory for Palestine, or will 
they just sit and watch the imperialist design demolishing the foundation of the Arab 
edifice brick by brick, roaming through the Arab land and besmirching Arab dignity and 
keeping the dagger planted in our hearts?
