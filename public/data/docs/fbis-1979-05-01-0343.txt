LIBYA

AT-TURAYKI ADDRESSES JOINT COMMITTEE WITH SIERRA LEONE 
LD291750 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 amT 29 Apr 79 Li 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 29 April--The meeting of the Libyan-Sierra Leone joint committee for 
economic, technical and cultural cooperation has begun at the Libyan Foreign Secretariat 
premises in Tripoli, The meeting is presided over by Libyan Fore igen Secretary ‘Abd as- 
Salam at-Turayki and Sierra Leone Foreign Minister Abdulai Conteh. 


At the beginning of the meeting, Dr at-Turayki delivered a speech welcoming the Sierra 
Leone delegation. Dr at-Turayki said; We are facing @ racist enemy supported by world 
imperialism in South Africa as well as in Zimbabwe, where imperialism has used mock 
elections to tamper with the will of the African people, We are facing the same problem 
in the Arab world, There is a racist enemy supported by America, and there is direct 
intervention on the part of the imperialist forces which are trying to impose their dom- 
ination on the continent--such as Prench imperialism's interference in the African Cone 
tinent in order to divide it and create alliances to serve its interests in Africa, 


We categorically reject this interference, he added, Prench imperialism constantly trie 

to create such alliances, As African peoples, we must be alert to counter this imper ial- 
ist interference for the sake of preserving the unity of the continent and out of respect 
for the sovereignty of the African countries, 


Dr at-Turayki said that recent developments in East Africa, where Tanzania invaded Uganda, 
constitute a serious precedence in Africa and an infringement of the OAU Charter, A: 
African peoples we must not permit such interventions, he said, As African countries, 

he said, it is our duty to strike at the foreign monopolies on the continent and liberate 
the African economy.
