LIBYA

‘Traitor Recruited’ By U.S. Intelligence Hanged 
LD041 302 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GM1 
4 Jun 8&4 


[Text] Confirming the people’s authority and realizing direct 
democracy, the Basic People’s Congress of Tanzim held an 
emergency session this morning, Monday, 4 June, to implement 
the decision of the Basic People’s Congress of Tanzim dated 14 
May 1984 to physically destroy the terrorists of the Muslim 
Brotherhood, agents of America. During the session the confes- 
sions of the criminal traitor Muhammad Sa’‘id ash-Shabani were 
read to the congress. The death sentence against him was ratified 


Members of the Basic People’s Congress of Tanzim carried out 
the execution of the criminal traitor Muhammad Sa‘id ash- 
Shabani by hanging until death. He joined the Muslim 
Brotherhood party and was recruited by the American intel- 
ligence service to carry Out subversive acts against the people's 
authority in the Jamahiriyah, to implement a terrorist plan 
calling for the assassination of a number of Libyan citizens, to 
destroy popular markets, socialist establishments, and fuel sta- 
tions, to poison water reservoirs, and to carry out subversive acts 
against the gains of the Libyan Arab people realized as a result 
of the Great Al-Fatih Revolution
