U.S. ADMINISTRATION ANNOUNCES OIL IMPORT BAN 


LD111400 TripoliDomestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 11 Mar 82 


[Text] The U.S. administration adopted a decision to impose a complete ban on its imports 
of Libyan oil; it also ordered a halt to oil technology exports to the jamahiriyah. These 
measures were announced yesterday afternoon at a press conference by a U.S. State Depart- 
ment spokesman, who justified the Reagan administration's decisions by alleging that the 
jamahiriyah is spreading terrorism in the world and is adopting hostile stands toward the 
pro-American regimes in Somalia and Sudan. 


These U.S. decisions against the people of the jamahiriyah fall within the framework of 
the U.S. deep-rooted aggressive course and the aims of the crusade-like stand President 
Reagan is adopting against the Libyan Arab people with the aim of making them submit and 
of depriving them of the money and arms by which they feed the Palestinian revolution and 
consolidate the struggle of the peoples struggling for freedom. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES LEBANESE COMMUNIST 


LDO71516 Tripoli JANA in English 1408 GMT 7 Mar 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Mar (JANA) -- The revolution's leader Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
yesterday received the secretary general of the Lebanese Communist Party George Hawi and 
members of the party's Politburo.
