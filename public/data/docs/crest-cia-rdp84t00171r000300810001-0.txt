Summary (AI): A December 1983 National Photographic Interpretation Center imagery note on Tripoli in northern Lebanon (not Libya), describing pro-Arafat PLO positions, anti-Arafat forces backed by Syrian artillery, and damage to the port and refinery. It has no bearing on Libya's Tripoli.
• The document concerns Tripoli, Lebanon (Tarabulus), not Tripoli, Libya.
• Anti-Arafat forces were supported by one Syrian field artillery battalion (18 130mm M-46 guns) and one Syrian MRL battalion (18 BM-21 launchers).
• At the Tripoli port, two warehouses were destroyed and a freighter sunk; at the refinery, at least 21 of about 33 storage tanks were destroyed.
