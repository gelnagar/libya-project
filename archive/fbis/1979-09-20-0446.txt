LIBYA

JALLUD SPEAKS ON ACHIEVEMENTS, ISLAMIC TIES WITH IRAN 
LD200612 Tripoli JANA in English 1405 GMT 19 Sep 79 LD 


[Text] Zeintan, 19 Sep, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Jallud last 
night laid the foundation stone of the new Zeintan town, amid the Libyan Arab people's 
celebrations of the Great First of September Revolution, 


A big people's celebration took place to mark the event in the presence of the secre- 
taries for congresses and committees affairs at the General People's Congress and of 
people's congresses and committees in the area as well as by masses of citizens. 


Staff Major Jallud laid the foundation stone of new Zeintan town while the masses 
shouted for the life of the Great First of September Revolution and its leader Colonel 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. 


Then he recorded the following notes: "In the name of Allah, most gracious, most 
merciful: Amidst the celebrations of the 10th anniversary of the Great First of 
September Revolution, I had the honor to participate in the celebrations of the people 
of Zeintan on this day, the day commemorating the end of the oppression suffered by our 
masses who were gulled and suppressed, the same masses which displayed their courage 
and offered sacrifices and martyrs to defeat the fascist Italians.

"We feel happy as we lay the foundation stone of new Zeintan town for we feel that we 
are nearly fulfilling the commitment pledged by the revolution to revenge for those who 
refused to co-exist with the Italians and left their homes to defend their honor, land, 
Arabism and Islam. For this they were punished by the clique in power then, who on the 
other hand gave their blessings to those who embraced the Italians and co-existed with 
them. 


"While rewriting the story of the struggle which had been written in blood, the revolution 
is committed to revive the memory of those who stood with such a courage, 


"The value of the town of Zeintan is not the material one, although that also is great. 
The revolution considers it a sign of gratitude and loyalty to those who sacrificed for 
Libya's sake, they are the ones who deserve to enjoy its prosperity. 


"We greet the masses of Zeintan which led the struggle, joined the revolution, applied 
all its dicta, contributed to the success of the revolution and brought nearer the final 
victory. " 


Staff Major Jallud also inaugurated the exhibition organized at the traditional craft 
center. Staff Major Jallud was shown round the exhibition. He wrote a note in the 
guests record book expressing his admiration for the exhibits and complimenting the 
local youths for the initiatives which revealed their creative talents and showed that 
they belong to a thoroughbred popular movement. 


Staff Major Jallud delivered a speech on the occasion referring to the 10th anniversary 
of the Great First of September Revolution, which, he said, is the celebration.of the 
giant achievements in this historic event. Every day in the past 10 years has recorded 
a new great achievement. 


He said: "We celebrate the 10th anniversary while we are even more confident about the 
future. Laying this foundation stone is like the revenge of backwardness and of the 
men who fought Italian fascists." 


He went on to say: "By building scores of new towns we affirm the values and loyalty 
as well as the competence of the Libyans who fought the fascists." 


"With the same determination, the Libyan Arab people are defeating backwardness, re- 
claiming lands and erecting new towns and factories." 


He added that the Great First of September Revolution considers the struggle of our 
ancestors against the Italians as a great credit which defies the deposed rulers. There- 
fore it fells committed, materially and morally for the sake of the victims of oppression 
and backwardness and for those who fought the fascist Italian invasion, to carry out 
these tremendous social changes. 


He went on to say: "While we declare our commitment to carry out these changes for the 
benefit of the masses which fell below the poverty line, the leadership of the 
revolution, particularly Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, have travelled across the 
deserts, oases and valleys to consider the situation on the spot. As a result, a daring 
revolutionary programme was set up to cover the desert, the oases, villages and towns 

as well as the mountains, to reclaim the valleys, erect housing blocks, build factories, 
construct roads and set up power networks." 


He said: "We can confidently, and proudly say that on the 10th anniversary we have 
achieved many changes, which will help abolish backwardness and achieve the goals of 
our ancestors who gave their lives for this end."

He went on: "We have the potential as well as the moral principles and values, the 
confidence and determination, Therefore, with the help of Allah, we can change the 
situation for the benefit of the masses at the political, social, ecmomic, philosophical 
and ideological level." 


He added: "In ten years we have managed to achieve great things, but now after building 
an effective revolutionary force from the masses, which have developed a consciousness, 

building educational institutions and within the next ten years will hope to accomplish 

even more than our past achievements." [sentence as received] 


Staff Major Jallud said: This morning we have laid the foundation stone for the Misurata 
steel and iron factory. Tonight we are laying the foundation stone of the new town of 
Zeintan. Tomorrow we will do the same and lay the foundation of the biggest oil refinery 
in the world and the [words indistinct]. Within the next few days we will lay the 
foundation stone of several new towns such as New Qawarsha, New Majinin and others, 


He said: "By considering these [word indistinct ] huge achievements and our moral values one 
truth becomes obvious, that the blood of honorable men which was shed on this land and the 
sweat which has poured and will do so in the future, has now brought its reward. As we 

are now confident that we are on the right path and that, with thehelp of God, will know 
where we are going and will not have our peace of mind until we defeat backwardness and 
create a new society where the people!s authority will be confirmed." 


Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Jallud said: "The Libyan Arab people is entitled to feel 
proud of its achievements which seemed impossible and surprised all observers." 


He said: “After the First of September Revolution, and thanks to this revolution, Libya 
has become the Arabs! centre of attraction and their hope for liberation and progress, The 
meeting of Arab leaders in the Jamahiriyah on the 10th anniversary [of the] revolution 
indicated the Arabs! appreciation for the pioneer national role of the Libyan Arab people." 


He explained that the Arab nation, from the ocean to the Gulf, rely on the Libyan Arab 
people who are not in a leading position, He said [the revolution] gave the Libyans 
a historic responsibility. 


Staff Major Jallud affirmed that the Jamahiriyah became the fortress of Islam and centre of 
attraction. He referred to the Iranian-Libyan joint statement issued after the visit of the 
Iranian Islamic Republic Party which also affirmed the necessity for the unity of the two 
revolutions, the Libyan Arab and the Iranian, the nucleus of a greater Islamic unity. 


Staff Major Jallud praised the pioneer role of the Great First of September Revolution 
in Africa which made the Jamahiriyah the pillar of the African struggle and the Mecca 
of the African peoples struggle. He also pointed out the participation of the African 
military symbolic contingents of the armies and African liberation organizations which 
stressed the prestige enjoyed by the Jamahiriyah in Africa. [passage indistinct ] 


PEOPLE'S BUREAU TO STRENGTHEN TIES WITH AMERICANS 
LD200528 Tripoli JANA in English 1420 GMT 19 Sep 79 LD 


[Text] Washington, 19 Sep, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The secretary of the People's 
Committee of the Jamahiriyah People's Bureau said that the students! take-over of the 
embassies in Washington and other European countries came in response to the call 
addressed by Colonel Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the Great First of September 
Revolution, during his speech at the Green Square on the 10th anniversary of the Great 
First of September Revolution,

In a television debate on Channel Five, which was attended by a number of journalists, 
the secretary of the People's Bureau said that the conversion of the embassies into 
people!s bureaus administrated by the selected people's committee is a step which runs 
parallel with the real democratic people's rule practised in the Jamahiriyah since 2nd 
March 1977. 


He went on to say: "The embassies represent a king, head of state or a government. 

Because the traditional government had disappeared from the Jamahiriyah, and the people 
are ruling the country through their congresses while [as received] and the resolutions 
are implemented by the people's committees, the revolutionary step of seizing the embassies 
and turning them into people's bureaus reflects the democratic process taking place in 

the country. Therefore, this bureau will strengthen relations between the peoples of the 
Jamahiriyah and America," 


During the programme, Mr. Iskia Mohammad, a black journalist, favourably spoke about his 
visit to the Jamahiriyah and his participation in the American-Arab dialogue in October 

last year. He also praised the achievements of the Great First of September Revolution which 
he was able to observe during the visit, as well as the stands adopted by the revolution 

in support of the people's struggle and of the African liberation movements for the 
liberation of Africa from colonialism and racism, 


The participants said that the American mass media are controlled by Zionist influence 
propagating a false and fake image of the Jamahiriyah.
