Summary (AI): A Washington Post article reports that Libyan dissident Faisal Zagallai, in Fort Collins, Colorado, identified former Green Beret Eugene Tafoya as the man who shot him twice in the head in October 1980. Prosecutors call it a regime-directed assassination attempt; the defense says Zagallai pulled a gun and Tafoya fired in self-defense. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• Zagallai said Qaddafi ordered all Libyan students to return or face execution in February 1980.
• Zagallai said six students were hanged after a 1973 student conference in Libya.
• Zagallai said he took over the Libyan Embassy in the United States in 1976.
• The CIA has denied that Tafoya worked for it.
• Three witnesses testified they heard three shots rather than two.
• 1980-10-19: Faisal Zagallai was shot twice in the head by Eugene Tafoya in Zagallai's apartment, per trial testimony; the shooting occurred the evening after an Oct. 18, 1980 call offering a job interview.
