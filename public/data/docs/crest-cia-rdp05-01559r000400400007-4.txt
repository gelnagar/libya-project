Summary (AI): An FBIS transcript (take 5 of 6) of Qadhdhafi's 10 April 1985 press conference, in which he defends Libya's ties and criticises the US, Mubarak, Numayri, Thatcher and Israel. It contains no specific facts about Tripoli places, prisons or security sites.
• Qadhdhafi said the USSR decision to halt deployment of medium-range missiles was a positive step for world peace.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya's People's Committee for Foreign Liaison handled contacts with Britain, which he said he did not know about.
• Qadhdhafi said he was against Zionism but not against Judaism.
• The transcript was filed by FBIS London and sent to multiple US agencies and embassies.

((TEXT)) ((AL-QADHD CO )) AMERICA DID NOT PREVENT THE 
IRANIAN PEOPLE FROM EXPELLING THE SHAH AND IT WAS NOT ABLE TO DO 
ANYTHING FOR THE SHAH. IT DID NOT PREVENT THE ETHIOPIAN PEOPLE 
FROM TOPPLING HAILE SELASSIE, AND IT DID NOT DO ANYTHING TO SAVE 
HIM. IT DID NOT DO ANYTHING FOR IDRIS AL-SENUSSI WHO OPENED UP 
LIBYA AS A BASE FOR AMERICA -- A COLONY FOR AMERICA. AND BATISTA 
IN CUBA, SOMOZA IN NICARAGUA LON NOL IN CAMBODIA, NUMAYRI IN 
THE SUDAN -- ALL THESE ARE LESSONS LEARNED FROM RELATIONS WITH 
AMERICA. ON THE CONTRARY, IT HAS BECOME CLEAR THAT THOSE WHO 
FORM TIES UP WITH AMERICA SPEED UP THEIR 
DOWNFALL. THEY FALL CURSED. NUMAYRI WHO FELL IN AMERICA'S 
EMBRACE AND HANDED SUDAN TO AMERICA SPEEDED UP HIS OWN FALL. 
HUSNI MUBARAK WILL FALL LIKE NUMAYRI AND THE REST OF THE AGENTS 
IN THE ARAB REGION WHO DEPEND ON AMERICA. WE HERALD THEIR SPEEDY 
FALL ALL THE TIME THEY (?STRENGTHEN) TIES WITH AMERICA AND THEIR 
DEPENDENCE ON IT. 
((QUESTION)) YOUR EXCELLENCY THE LEADER: THE SUDANESE PEOPLE AS 
A WHOLE ROSE TO EXPEL NUMAYRI JUST AS THEY EXPELLED THE DESPOT 
(IBRAHIM ABBUD) BEFORE. DOES THIS FIT YOUR THEORY THAT THE MASSES 
CONTRL EVENTS AND THAT THE ERA OF THE MASSES HAS COME? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) OF COURSE THIS IS CLEAR, THERE IS NO DOUBT. 
((QUESTION)) DO YOU BELIEVE THAT THE FORTHCOMING VISIT BY THE 
ALGERIAN PRESIDENT TO WASHINGTON WILL HAVE A POSITIVE EFFECT IN 
RELATION TO THE ARAB HOMELAND? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) IT WILL HAVE A BAD EFFECT. 
((QUESTION)) MR LEADER, WITH RESPECT AND I APOLOGISE FOR GOING 
BACK TO MY ORIGINAL QUESTION IT'S JUST THAT I DID NOT UNDERSTAND 
YOUR STATEMENT ON THE NATIOAL COMMAND. ARE YOU TELLING ME THAT THE 
NATIONAL COMMAND, IF IT DECIDED THAT THE UNITED STATES, FOR EXAMPLE, 
HAD LAUNCHED AN ACTION OR TAKEN AN ACTION IN AN ARAB NATION 
((WORDS INDISTINCT)), THE NATIONAL COMMAND WOULD BE PREPARED TO 
TAKE MILITARY OR PUNITIVE ACTION INSIDE THE UNITED STATES? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) WE BEFRIEND THOSE WHO BEFRIEND US, AND WE 
ARE HOSTILE TO THOSE WHO ARE HOSTILE TO US. 
((QUESTION)) TASS CORRESPONDENT: BROTHER LEADER OF THE REVOLUTION, 
A FEW DAYS AGO MIKHAIL GORBACHEV, CPSU CENTRAL COMMITTEE GENERAL 
SECRETARY, CAME UP WITH A NEW PEACE INITIATIVE -- THE USSR 
DECISION TO HALT THE DEPLOYMENT OF MEDIUM-RANGE MISSILES. WHAT 
IS THE ATTITUDE OF YOUR EXCELLENCY TO THIS INITIATIVE? 
THANK YOU. 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) THIS INITIATIVE IS A POSITIVE ONE IN THE 
SERVICE OF WORLD PEACE. AND THIS IS THE BEST PRESENT WHICH 
FRIEND GORBACHEV COULD GIVE THE WORLD ON THE OCCASION OF HIS 
ASUMPTION OF POWER IN (?USSR). REGRETTABLY IT DID NOT RECEIVE 
A POSITIVE REACTION FROM IMPERIALISM. OF COURSE THE IMPERIALISTS 
ARE PUSHING THE WORLD TOWARD WAR WHILE THIS IS A STEP TOWARD PEACE. 
((QUESTION)) BBC CORRESPONDENT: I HAVE LEARNT RECENTLY THAT THERE 
WAS CONTACT AT A HIGH LEVEL BETWEEN THE LIBYANS AND THE BRITISH 
IN ROME WITH A VIEW TO NORMALIZING RELATIONS BETWEEN THE TWO 
COUNTRIES. HOW CLOSE TO NORMALIZATION OR HOW FAR FROM 
NORMALIZTION ARE YOU AT THIS POINT?

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) I HAVE NO KNOWLEDGE ON THIS SUBJECT; THE 
PEOPLE'S COMMITTEE FOR FOREIGN LIAISON IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THIS AND 
THE BASIC CONFERENCES. 
((QUESTION)) WHAT ARE YOUR PERSONAL FEELINGS ABOUT IT? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) I FEEL ANGRY ABOUT THE BAD BRITISH REACTION 
TO THE GOOD ACTIONS OF THE POPULAR CONFERENCES. IT CAUSED ME 
TO DOUBT THE CREDIBILITY OF THATCHER AND QUESTION BRITISH MORALITY 
IN GENERAL. 
((QUESTION)) I WOULD LIKE TO RETURN TO THE SUBJECT OF 
SUDAN, MR AL-QADHDHAFI, WHOM DO YOU SUPPORT NOW: THE MASSES IN THE 
SUDAN OR THE CHRISTIANS OF (?JONGLEI)? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) THIS QUESTION IS PREMATURE. 
((QUESTION)) LAST WEEK TUNISIA ACCUSED LIBYA OF BROADCASTING 
A PROGRAM INCITING THE KILLING OF JEWS WHO LIVE IN MOROCCO, 
TUNISIA, AND ALGERIA. IS THIS CORRECT? AND IS THERE ANY CHANGE 
IN THE JAMAHIRIYAH POLICY WHICH HAS ALWAYS DISTINGUISHED BETWEEN 
JEWS AND THE ISRAELIS WHO LIVE IN THE OCCUPIED TERRITORIES? 
ALSO SOME MAGAZINES PRINTED IN THIS COUNTRY JUSTIFIED THE KILLING 
OF JEWS WHEREEVER THEY ARE. 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) FIRST, I DID NOT HEAR THIS ACCUSATION AND I 
DID NOT HEAR THIS RADIO STATION. BUT I HEARD THAT THERE IS A 
RADIO STATION BEAMED TO THE WHOLE ARAB MAGHREB, INCLUDING LIBYA. 
WE BELIEVE THAT IT TRANSMITS FROM SOUTHERN LEBANON AND IT IS BEING 
SAID THAT IT EXPOSES THE MASSACRES BEING CARRIED OUT BY THE 
ISRAELIS IN SOUTHERN LEBANON AND IT DESCRIBED HOW THE ISRAELI 
TANKS CRUSHED CIVILIAN CARS WHILE FULL OF LEBANESE PASSENGERS, 
FAMILIES, AND CHILDREN. IT SEEMS THAT THIS RADIO STATION BELONGS 
TO THE SOUTHERN LEBANESE WHO SEE THE ISRAELIS COMMITTING 
INDISCRIMINATE MASSACRES. THUS IT IS THEIR RIGHT TO RESPOND TO 
THEM IN THE SAME WAY THE ISRAELI SOLDIERS TREAT US. HOW CAN YOU 
ALLOW THE SPILLING OF BLOOD OF ARAB AND LEBANESE FAMILIES? 
ISRAEL DID NOT DENY THIS, THEY WROTE ON THE WALL AND THE WHOLE 
WORLD READ IT, THAT THIS WAS THE REVENGE OF THE ISRAELI ARMY. 
YOU ALL -- THE WHOLE WORLD PRESS -- STUDIED THIS EXPRESSION, THE 
WRITING OF THE ISRAELI ARMY ON A WALL IN SOUTHERN LEBANON. 
AS REGARDS THE LEBANESE, THEY HAVE THE RIGHT TO SET UP A STATION 
AND CALL FOR REVENGE TO REMOVE THE ISRAELI KNIFE FROM THE NECKS 
OF THE CIVILIANS AND FAMILIES. WHY DOES THE WORLD IGNORE THESE 
MASSACRES COMMITTED BY THE ISRAELIS AND YET PROTESTS AT WORDS 
BROADCAST FROM SOUTHERN LEBANON? PRIORITY IN PROTEST: SHOULD IT 
BE AT MASSACRES OR RADIO TALK? AMERICA SUPPORTS THESE MASSACRES 
AND USES THE VETO RIGHT TO PREVEN THEIR CONDEMNATION. SO WHY 
DO THEY PROTEST AT HITLER8S MASSACRES SINCE YOU SUPPORT MASSACRES 
NOW AND ARE BIASED IN THEIR FAVOR AND USE THE VETO RIGHT 
ON THEIR SIDE? IN THIS CASE HITLER IS INNOCENT. YOU SUPPORT THE

PALESTINE AND LEBANON TO BEAR THE RESPONSIBILITY FOR THE ACTIONS 
OF HITLER OR WEST GERMANY? WHAT KIND OF LOGIC RULES THE WORLD

CAPACITY TO MAKE THEIR VOICE HEARD IN THE WORLD AND THIS STATION 
IS BEAMED FROM SOUTHERN LEBANON. IT ONLY MAKES THE WORLD HEAR 
THE CRY OF THOSE WHO WERE MASSACRED IN LEBANON; AND THOSE WHO
