LIBYA

FEDERAL COUNCIL ISSUES STATEMENT ON ISRAELI-EGYPTIAN TREATY 
LD281530 Tripoli JANA in English 1419 GMT 28 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, March 28, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The nation's Federal Council issued a 
statement today following the signing of the capitulatory treaty between the defeated 
Egyptian president and the Zionist enemy in which it called for the implementation of the 
resolutions of the Baghdad summit, the convening of a joint Islamic-Arab summit for the 
defence of the Arab and Islamic nation and that of an emergency African summit for con- 
fronting the violation of the organization's charter. 


The council called upon the UN secretary-general to condemn the Egyptian-(Israeli) treaty 
which poses a threat to world peace and violates UN resolutions. It demanded that on 26 
March of every year flags in the Arab and Islamic world should be lowered since that day is 
Similar to the one in which Balfour promised to give Palestine to the Zionists. 


The statement pointed out that this treaty was a document of treason deviating from all 
norms, laws and resolutions whether Arab, African, Islamic or international. 


This treaty, the statement added,is detrimental to Egypt's political and economic independence, 
bringing a mandate to the country by which (Israel) would exploit her economically, corrupt 
her society, disrupt her army, humiliate her people who will be used to carry out imperialist- 
Zionist schemes in Egypt and the Arab world. 


The council said that the defeated Egyptian president insisted on signing the document which 
secures Zionist usurpation, ensures dissident conspiracies and imperialist interests in the 
Arab region. Thus the treaty is a flagrant violation of all resolutions stipulating the 
liberation of Arab territories, the recognition of the Palestinian Arab people's national 
rights and of the PLO as their sole legitimate representative and the refusal to sign any 
separate agreement with the enemy, as well as stipulating that Jerusalem is an Arab city. 


Moreover, the defeated Egyptian president hasdeviated from the resolutions of African 
summits and Islamic states and the charter of their organization, thus insulting the Islamic 
world.

He also violated the UN Charter and resolutions endorsed by all the countries of the world 
except by America and the Zionist enemy concerning the liberation of Arab territories, the 
Palestinian people's rights, the Arabism of Jerusalem and considering the Zionist enemy a 
racist regime similar to that of South Africa. 


The statement pointed out that he has betrayed the council's constitution which stipulated 
that no efforts should be spared to liberate the occupied Arab territories, that no con- 
cessions or negotiations should be made to give up not even one inch of these territories, 
that the Palestinian cause should not be abandoned or bargained over. As-Sadat has broken 
his oath to the council.
