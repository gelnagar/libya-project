JALLUD DENOUNCES U.S. MANEUVERS AT 9 NOV RALLY 


LD091334 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1121 GMT 9 Nov 81 


[Speech by Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, member of the Revolution Command Council, 
at 9 November Tripoli raily to protest U.S. military maneuvers ~-- live] 


[Excerpt] Free brothers, you have come out on this angry march to represent the will of 
challenge of the Arab nation, after this will was (?stabbed) and Saudi Arabia and by the 
press of dictatorship, reaction and opportunism. 


V. 10 Nov 81 Y NORTH AFRICA 


Free brothers, only a free people can (?fight) and challenge because slaves have no will; 
the free have free will. And with free will, the masses can challenge and fight. Today, 
we are reaping the fruits of the people's revolution. 


These angry , spontaneous marches today are being held because of the masses’ sense of 
responsibility. Here in the Jamahiriyah, the free masses who are in authority and who 
hold the wealth and weapons are the ones who are in charge and they do not wait for an 
order to come out. 


Brothers, the masses who came out today did not do so to protest against the maneuvers, but 
to declare the fight against the American forces which have come to occupy the Arab home- 
land in the form of maneuvers; these are not maneuvers, but a direct occupation and a viola- 
tion of the Arab homeland by America and by the American forces. [applause] 


The masses cannot be deceived that these are maneuvers. There is a direct occupation and 

a violation of the Arab homeland taking place. It is up to the Arab nation, the masses of 
the Arab nation and its living [word indistinct] forces to declare hostilities against these 
invading forces. 


They are invading and occupation forces and they are not holding maneuvers. America is 
vast, Europe is vast, so why does America insist on testing its weapons and its military 
tactics in the heart of the Arab homeland. It is a trick which does not deceive the Arab 
masses. It is a direct occupation. The forces which will land in Oman, Somalia, Sudan 
and Egypt will not depart. They will continue the occupation of those countries. This is 
a fact (?0bvious) to the Arab masses and to the revolutionary forces. 


However, the result will certainly be opposite to that intended. [applause] This will incite 
the Arab masses. It will force them to take up arms to fight these invading forces which 
have violated the Arab homeland. This will also speed up the revolution. The results of 
this direct military occupation will boomerang against the interests of America, reaction 
and the [word indistinct] reactionary forces. 


This direct occupation will have two results. First, it will convince the Arab masses to 
fight against these forces which came to occupy the Arab homeland. Second, these forces 
will be [words indistinct] and will incite revolt and be a new instigator of revolution by 
the masses and by the revolutionary and patriotic forces. 


The occupation of the Arab homeland, particularly those countries neighboring the Jamahiriyah, 
is intended, in addition to the direct occupation of these countries, to intimidate the 
Libyan masses, the Libyan Arab masses, because there is [word indistinct] plot and the cause 
is being sold out. Colonialism and reaction know that the free Libyan masses alone are 
capable of challenging the treachery and processes of selling out the Palestine question. 


Free brothers, revolutionary brothers, why did the Arab masses sentence As-Sadat? Because 
he betrayed the Palestinian question and recognized Israel and the Zionists. Now another 
treacherous plan is being put forward, the intention of which is to recognize the Zionists 
in Palestine and to sell out the Palestinian question. 


Brothers, there is no Saudi plan. It is an American plan. Saudi Arabia is incapable. 

Fahd is incapable of putting forward a planto settle the Palestinian question. (?Fahd and 
others) are busy with gambling, women and making money. We must know that it is an Amer- 
ican plan. American assent to the so-called AWACS deal, to be implemented in 1985 after 
the cause is sold out, is the price for letting through the plan of selling the Palestinian 
question. 


V. 10 Nov 81 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


Brothers, I must say that before the Arab nation reaches an unenviable situation, the 
Saudis must withdraw their plan or, rather, America must withdraw its plan which it put 
forward in a Saudi form. Fahd is not authorized to speak for the Palestinian question. 

It is enough for Fahd to attend to his own affairs. Those calling for recognition of the 
Zionist enemy, (?disregarding) the Arab masses, are adopting the same posture as As-Sadat. 


Now there is plan, a new Camp David. Now there is a wide call to (?relinquish) Palestine 
and to sell out the Palestinian question and recognize the enemy. The forthcoming Fes 
conference is being held to sell out the question. The Arab masses, the revolutionary 
forces, must regard the same treacherous conditions which applied to As-Sadat as 
applicable to those who will take part in the Fes conference. 


The Arab nation and its masses and its living forces must disown the Fes conference and 
its resolution because the Fes conference is devoted to a sellout. 


What are the contents of the Saudi plan, the American plan? To recognize the enemy. Now 
all the Arabs are being asked for a collective recognition since As-Sadat recognized 

(?the enemy). The plan can be summed up as relinquishing Palestine and as recognizing 
the enemy. Relinquish Palestine for the enemy. Hence, the Arab masses must consider 
those taking part in the Fes conference in Morocco as being on the same level as As-Sadat. 
The conference is to sell out the question. This must be known to the Palestinian masses, 
to the Palestinian fighters in their trenches. This must be known to the students in 
Beir Zeit, the West Bank and Gaza. 


We must not miss this opportunity to ensure that the Arab nation is not split up once 
again and to ensure that the Arab homeland enters war against America -- a war which will 
devastate everything both valuable and worthless belonging to those who have been given 
the task of implementing the American plan. They must withdraw their plan and remain 
within their own limits. 


The Arab masses, and particularly the Arab masses in the countries concerned, and the 
patriotic, unionist and national and revolutionary forces must know that this is a direct 
occupation and invasion of the Arab homeland and that the forces to land in Oman, Somalia, 
Egypt and Sudan are occupation forces. They only want to deceive the masses and to 
camouflage this direct occupation as a maneuver. 


But this should not discourage the Arab and revolutionary masses. Boldy and courageously, 
they must begin counteroperations against the direct American occupation of the Arab home- 
land. The challenge must be accepted. 


The Arab masses and the revolutionary forces, brothers, must not confine themselves to 
protests and statements. They must take up arms to fight these invading forces. 


This occupation will, in fact, help to speed up revolutionary operations. It will act as 
an incentive for staging revolutions against the agents and the reactionaries. Thus, it 
is possible for the Arab masses and the revolutionary forces to utilize the occupation for 
positive ends: to rally their strength, to raise their struggle capabilities, to resolve 
to face up to the occupation with force and to carry out bold Fedayeen operations against 
the American bases and these American forces. 


The revolutionary forces in the Arab homeland must use this direct occupation as an 
opportunity to mobilize the people's masses, the students, workers, peasants and revolu- 
tionary intelligensia in a noble people's action and a broad people's operation to uproot 
apostasy, treason and agentry and also to uproot American interests. 


V. 10 Nov 81 Q 4 NORTH AFRICA 


Specific tasks must be drawn up for the Arab masses and their revolutionary forces. They 
must firmly resolve to resist the invading forces which have besmirched the Arab homeland. 
We must also speed up revolutions as the sole road to the liberation of Palestine. 
Palestine will never be freed except with the liberation of the Arab from all elements of 
oppression, suppression and backwardness. Then, brothers, we must be fully determined. 
Our means must be through the export of people's revolutions so that the roots of agentry, 
treason and reaction are removed. We must mobilize the masses toward the accomplishment 
of specific objectives. 


We must shoulder our responsibilities and not bow our heads. The great Libyan people 
have proven that the United States can be resisted, defied, challenged and defeated. The 
Libyan people defeated America in the Gulf of Sidra as they defeated the Italian empire 
before. 


Free brothers, Arab brothers, America would not have cut short its maneuvers which were 
scheduled to continue until the 22d [of August], curtailing them on the 19th [of August], 
but for the determination of the Libyan people to resist the American presence in the Gulf 
of Sidra. The Gulf of Sidra was to be a gulf of blood, a red gulf, as the brother colonel 
called it. [applause] 


With all its might and greatness, the United States found itself and its vessels and planes 
beseiged by Libyan naval and air forces. 


The Libyan people, brothers, have provided the masses of the Arab nation from the Gulf 

to the ocean -- in Sudan, Oman, Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the Gulf and [word indistinct] -- 
with proof that the people's willpower cam defeat the F-15's and F-16's and the armies 
and might of America. 


The Libyans do not march in the streets or issue statements of protest against the opening 
of the Arab homeland to the American forces. The Libyan people, with them masses of the 
Arab nation, are determined to resist the aggression and this flagrant direct occupation 
of the Arab homeland. 


Brothers, free brothers, this tumultuous demonstration will be followed by deeds and by 
struggle and blood and martyrdom. We will prove the truth of what we say. We must prove 
that we are prepared to die in defense of what we believe in and all of life's values 
which we uphold. 


Brothers, we must prove that we are capable of translating all our words into deeds, 
material deeds. You are capable of giving and dying and becoming martyrs for the sake 
of our beliefs and those of our Arab masses from the ocean to the Gulf. 


The Libyan people are capable of everything. They are capable of implementing all that 
they say. Brothers, the Libyan people have moved out in the largest military strategic 
action across thousands of kilometers to defeat America, France's Giscard d'Estaing, 
As-Sadat and the agent Numayri. The Chadian people have triumphed. The arm of the Libyan 
people is long and can reach its objectives wherever they are. [applause] 


The Libyan people's stick is hard and their arm is long. They can teach the reactionaries, 
the enemies of the Libyan people, the fascists, America and reaction a lesson they can 
never forget. Let them think of the lesson we have already taught them in Chad. 

{words indistinct] 


Brothers, free brothers, the Libyan people have proven themselves to be a rough fighting 
people. The Libyans possess the weapon of faith. We love martyrdom, brothers, just as 
Al-Mukhtar and our fathers and forefathers, when they fought against the Italians. 
[applause }

The Libyan people have their worth in life. They have become a respected people. The 
Libyan people have won respect in the world and their voices have become heard. Suffice 
it to say in this regard that the U.S. administration and the Pentagon are now fully pre- 
occupied with the Libyan people. This is sufficient proof. [applause] 


Today we see women joining in these demonstrations of thousands. This is a proof that the 
women and girls of Libya now occupy their place in Jamahiriyah society. [applause] 
Libyans are now able to teach lessons to their enemies. I say their arms are long and 
their sticks are big and ready for use against anyone who wants to defy and challenge the 
Libyan people. God is great. May God grant you success. March forward. 


TRIPOLI DEMONSTRATORS ISSUE 5-POINT DECLARATION 


LD091554 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1204 GMT 9 Nov 81 


[Statement issued by demonstrators in Tripoli 9 November to protest the U.S. joint maneuvers; 
read by Muhammad Abu al-Futuh -- live] 


[Excerpts] In the name of God, the merciful and compassionate, this is the statement of 
the pan-Arab march against the American maneuvers: 


The Arab nation today is being exposed to the American maneuvers, to a new episode in the 
serial of American terrorism, aggression and challenge to the will of this nation and its 
march toward a free honorable life. 


In a few days and for a whole month, the United States will be staging provocative military 
maneuvers in the heart of the Arab homeland, in the Egyptian western desert close to the 
Jamahirivah's borders. The Egyptian, Sudanese, Somali and Omani armed forces are to 
participate in these maneuvers which constitute a serious aggression against the dignity 

of the Arab nation and a grave threat to its security and safety. Today, it faces American 
terrorism and threats, recalls the pages of the black American annals which register 
America's hostile stand and plotting against the Arab homeland from the start of the 
escalation of the Arab revolutionary movement in the 1950's and up to this day. The 

United States has always adopted a stand of animosity and plotting against the Arab homeland 
and has tried to contain, shatter and humiliate it and to bleed it and sap its strength. 


It is evident that by these maneuvers which it is staging with agent governments, the 
United States, leader of world imperialism and international terrorism, seeks to accomplish 
the following: 


1. To protect the agent regimes in the area and to let the Arab people and their 
revolutionary forces know that these agent regimes are under U.S. protection and hegemony. 


2. To show its readiness to use American military force against the masses of the Arah 
nation if they try to change their deteriorating conditions and to liquidate the regimes 
of agentry and treason. 


3. To intimidate the Arab people of Egypt and to stop their revolutionary tide; to prevent 
them from completing their revolutionary march against the Sadatite regime after the 
toppling of the chief of this regime. 

4. To consolidate and complete the remaining episodes of the treacherous Camp David agree- 
ment which suffered a violent shock with the execution of the traitor ‘As-Sadat; to bring 

in other Arab countries, to widen the scope of Camp David and to justify the capitulationary 
attempts which advocate recognition of Israel. 

5. 1. intimidate the Arab revolutionary forces in general and to threaten the Jamahiriyah 
in particular. 


V. 10 Nov 81 Q 6 NORTH AFRICA 


6. To obstruct and impede the course of the Arab revolution and the Arab nation toward 
their objectives of freedom, socialism and unity. 


In the face of these objectives, of imperialist American designs and of the muscle flexing 
and American terrorism epitomized by the American military maneuvers in the heart of the 
Arab homeland, the Arab masses participating in this great pan-Arab march decide and 
declare in the name of the entire Arab nation from the Gulf to the ocean, the following: 


1. To call for greater unity among the forces of Arab revolution [words indistinct] 
in the face of the ferocious imperialist offensive [words indistinct]. 


2. To offer full support and complete cohesion with the Arab people of the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in their staunch and firm stand against threats, manuevers 
and aggressions by the United States. 


3. To challenge American imperialism and terrorism and to strike at American interests 
in the area in reply to the challenge, disrespect and aggression represented by the 
American military maneuvers in the heart of the Arab homeland. 


4. To adopt the course of armed struggle and overall revolution against imperialism and 
its agents in the Arab homeland. 


5. To reject any attempt at liquidating the Palestinian question, enlarging Camp David 
and realizing complete conciliation with the Zionist entity which has usurped the sacred 
land of Arab Palestine. 


Long live the struggle of the Arab nation! Down with America, leader of imperialism and 
terrorism! Victory for the Arab struggling masses! 


"THOUSANDS' DEMONSTRATE AGAINST U.S. MANEUVERS 


LD091456 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1025 GMT 9 Nov 81 


[Excerpt] Huge popular demonstrations swept the towns and villages of the Jamahiriyah 
this morning, protesting the aggressive military maneuvers which the American forces plan 
to stage in Egypt's western regions adjoining Libyan Arab borders, with the participation 
of Egyptian, Sudanese, Somali and Omani forces. The demonstrations affirm the Libyan Arab 
people's rejection of all political and military pressures on the Jamahiriyah by American 
imperialism. 


Thousands of the sons of the Libyan Arab people and masses of the Arab people in the 
Jamahiriyah and the Arab and African liberation movements participated in these great 
popular demonstrations, which expressed the challenge and defiance of the masses against 
the policy of oppression and international terrorism pursued by the U.S. administration 
against the Great i September Revolution and its commander. 


The demonstrators in this morning's largest ever demonstration held in Tripoli denounced 
the American military maneuvers against the people of the Jamahiriyah and the entire Arab 
nation. 


AL-QADHDHAFI COMMISSIONS MISSILE PATROL BOAT 


LDO81740 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1705 GMT 8 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 8 Nov (JANA) -- Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, supreme commander of 
the armed forces, and Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the armed forces, 
took part in a ceremony held by the Arab Libyan Naval Forces this afternoon to mark the 
commissioning of the new missile patrol boat Tariq ibn Ziyad, one of a batch of advanced 
patrol boats being supplied to the Arab Libyan Naval Forces. 


V. 10 Nov 81 Q7 NORTH AFRICA 


The missile patrol boat Tariq ibn Ziyad, added today to the Arab Libyan Naval Fleet, is 
considered to be one of the most modern naval craft in the world. It is equipped with 
surface-to-surface and ground-to-air missiles.
