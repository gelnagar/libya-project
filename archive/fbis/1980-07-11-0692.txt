LIBYA

MILITARY COMMANDER MEETS WITH SEYCHELLES, SFRY OFFICIALS 
LD101922 Tripoli JANA in English 1750 GMT 10 Jul 80 
[Text] Tripoli, July 10, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY*-Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, the 
general commander of the Libyan Armed Forces, held a meeting today with the defence minister 
of the Seychelles Republic, who is currently visiting the Jamahiriyah. Brigadier Abu Bakir 
Yunis also met the Yugoslav deputy minister of defence and commander of the air force, Mr. 
Anwav Jamado [name and title as received]. 
Both meetings dealt with the cooperation between the Jamahiriyah and two respective countries,
