LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SPEECH AT ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS 


LD192030 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1726 GMT 19 Nov 81 


[Speech by Libyan chief of state Col Mu'Ammar al-Qadhdhafi at opening of "extraordinary" 
session of Arab People's Congress in Tripoli -- live] 


[Text] Brothers, I greet you, through the Arab people's Congress at this extraordinary 
session. I congratulate the Arab People's Congress on the carrying out of the death 
sentence pronounced by the Arab people's court [applause] on traitor Anwar as-Sadat, the 
former president of Egypt -- the one who paid the visit of disgrace and shame, the one 
who signed the dirty Camp David treaty, recognized the Israeli enemy now oppressively 
occupying the Arab land of Palestine, and normalized relations with this enemy. 


The carrying out of the death sentence on that traitor was the ineluctable result of 

the sentence of the Arab people -- to execute treachery and its symbols at any time 

and anywhere. The carrying out of the death sentence on that traitor was the ineluctable 
result of the sentence passed by the Arab people -- to execute treachery and its symbols 
any time and anywhere in the Arab homeland. 


Before him, the Arab nation carried out the death sentences on (former Prime Minister of 
Jordan} Wasfi at-tall; King ‘Abdallah [of Jordan]; and Butrus Pasha [former foreign 
minister of Egypt, assassinated in 1910]. It also carried sentences out, in a grand 
revolution, on Faysal II [of Iraq, killed in 1958]; ‘Abd al-Ilah [former regent of Iraq] 
and Nuri as-Sa'id [prime minister of Iraq under Faysal]. Through revolution, the 

Arab people overthrew a generation of reactionary traitors who had given up the right of 
the Arab nation and traded its future and its hopes -- traitors who had shown negligence 
in defending its dignity and its territories, such as Faruq [of Egypt] on 23 July; Idriss 
as-Sanusi [of Libya] on the Great 1 September; Faysal II on 14 July [applause]; Al-Badr 
[former Yemeni leader]; the secessionist clique on 8 March [reference to a Syrian regime] 
the fascist clique in Sudan on 21 October; and the sultans of south Yemen on 14 October. 
[applause] 


Through this angry revolution, the Arab people fought whole groups and families because 
they were swamps of treachery. Anger and revolution did not stop at just the toppling 
of rulers. It also eliminated the political and social existence of these families and 
groups, such as the corrupt, deviationist and traitorous As-Sanusi family and the other 
families leaning on it, and also the agent, cowardly Hashemite family in Iraq. A 
generation of sultans were wiped out in south Yemen; so was the outsider Mohammad 

‘Ali family. 


We must proceed with the revolutionary, daring liberation in order to liquidate the 
rest of the reactionary and corrupt family rule -- families that are the allies of 
colonialism and that fail to confront the Israeli enemy. The objective is to make the 
Arab revolution one revolution, and to weld the links of this revolution from the dates 
of 23 July, to 1 November 1954, [reference to Algeria] and 14 July 1958 [reference to 
Iraq], and 26 September, 8 March, 21 October, and the great 1 September 1969. 
[applause ] 


We must stress that these revolutions, from 23 July, to 1 September 1969, were volcanoes 
that erupted through one cataclysm, the historic revolution of the Arab people against 
the era of the Sharifian families [reference to royalists in Jordan, Iraq and Libya], 
the dervish groups, the partisan military and facist cliques, and against colonialism, 
so that the nation can be unified, combat and emerge victorious.

We must carry on with the revolutionary and daring liberation in order to liquidate the 
remaining police regimes which lean on fascist parties [word indistinct], or on fascist 
militarymen. The failure of Arab regimes has been proved. We must expose their failure 
each day. They have lost justification for their existence. They have lost all the 
military battles with the enemy; they have failed to avert its impugning blows in Beirut, 
Baghdad and Saudi Arabia. They have failed in steadfastness and confrontation toward 
all the enemies. Worse still, they have allied themselves with them against the masses. 
They have failed to confront underdevelopment or to resolve its economic and political 
problems. Therefore, they have lost all linkswith progress, and they have been histori- 
cally condemned to fall. 


We must most courageously expose the Arab reality; we must lay it naked without shame. 
We must [words indistinct] of the Arab masses on confronting their traitor rulers. Must 
we deliberately widen the gap between the masses and the ruling regimes? Any sympathy 
between the masses and the regimes is the sympathy of a slave and his master, the 
sympathy of a she-camel to her butchers. We must deepen the awareness of the masses and 
increase the level of their intelligence. 


We must deepen the awareness of the masses and increase their enlightenment. We must 
highlight the inevitable contradiction between them and their rulers. 


The execution of the death sentence on As-Sadat can be used to prove the inevitability 

of the fate of the traitors, and the ability of the ordinary Arab man to perform miracles. 
It can be used to prove that the deed carried out by an Arab citizen, such as the 
assassination of Anwar as-Sadat, represents the will of the Arab people as a whole. It 
was decided and instigated by the Arab people and carried out by an unknown, ordinary 
Arab citizen and was supported after it was carried out. 


The greatest and noblest of men get assassinated. Even prophets get assassinated. But 
the difference between the assassination of one great and one low lies in the will. 
Several attempts were made against the life of ‘Abd an-Nasir. Had he been assassinated, 
his assassination would have weakened the nation and its willpower and would have 
saddened it, just as his natural death saddened it -- perhaps even more. But As-Sadat's 
assassination has strengthened the determination and strength of the nation and has 
increased its confidence in its willpower. 


Thus the killing of a great man is the result of an individual will, a reckless, sick or 
enemy will. The act of assassination is soon forgotten after it is carried out, but 

the assassination of traitors is the result of the will of nations which become stronger 
after the carrying out of the act of assassination. 


To highlight the popular Arab willpower and transform the results for the good of the 
masses, the Arab People's Congress should become the byproduct of basic Arab popular 
congresses throughout the Arab homeland and it should consist, in the future, of delegates 
bringing resolutions from their respective, basic popular congresses. It should be a 
superior structure. 


Brothers, the carrying out of the death sentence on As-Sadat has made the Arab People's 
Congress an Arab political, national and effective entity. It has made the Arab People's 
court, too, and Arab political, legal, national and effective entity. It has also made 
the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front a political and national effective entity. It 
has made Syria's steadfastness under the leadership of President Hafiz al-Asad and the 
Ba'th Party a factor directly affecting the As-Sadat regime in Egypt because of As-Sadat's 
direct betrayal of Syria in the battle. It has also highlighted the effective role of 

the Lebanese National Movement and the escalation of its role.

We must work to escalate still further that role without any cessation. But the decisive 
factor is the categorical rejection by the Palestine resistance of As-Sadat's actions 
starting with his visit to shame and disgrace and ending with the Stable David agreement. 


Brothers, we must rely only on the factor of the masses, with the masses' actions, we 
must contain the plots of the traitors and their cowardly policies and frustrate them. 


We must not proceed to Fes but rather get a fes [play on words since fes means axe in Arabic] 
to destroy idols with it. [applause] 


I declare, brothers, before you that I will not take part in the Fes summit conference. 
First, because I am neither a king nor a president, and second, because the conferees at 
Fes have not been authorized by the Arab people to sell out the cause and recognize 

the enemy. To sit side-by-side with those who recognized the enemy and met with Begin 
and walked with him shoulder-to-shoulder at the funeral of traitor As-Sadat -- those who 
meet with these traitors and take part in discussing the agenda topics must be charged 

by the Arab people with high treason and must be tried as As-Sadat was tried. [applause] 


The Arab governments should have intervened, by force if necessary, to stop the U.S. 
invasion of Egypt, Sudan, Oman and Somalia. But they did not make a single move, is this 
not an act of treason? Is not this submission? The Arab governments should have inter- 
vened on the side on Libya when it was attacked by the U.S. fleet inside its territorial 
waters and airspace. The Arab governments should have intervened to defend Lebanon, 
Baghdad and Tabuk [Saudi Arabia]. 


Thus the treason has not ended yet. It will not come to an end with the end of As-Sadat. 
As-Sadat was not the last of the traitors. As-Sadat was only one of the Arab traitors 
but he was the most brave and most foolish of them. 


Hence we are still faced with traitors who are both cowards and clever. The Arab people 
and this congress of theirs must affirm that there will be no truce with the cowardly, 
traitorous rulers who still control the destinies of the Arab masses; there will be no 
meeting with Egypt on the path of recognition of the enemy and the squandering of Palestine. 
Conversely, all capabilities must be pliced at its disposal if Egypt returns to the Arab 
fold and resumes its leading role in the battle of liberation and unity. 


Any attempt to go along with the plicy of recognizing the enemy and normalizing relations 
with him must be exploded immediately. We must be determined that the fate As-Sadat 

met will be the fate of all those who continue to deal with the enemy whether in Egypt 

or elsewhere. It should be the fate of anyone who attempts to deal with the enemy. 


We still consider the continued exchanges of visits with the enemies and the shaking 
of hands with them...[Al-Qadhdhafi does not complete sentence] 


We consider the U.S. military maneuvers, which are in reality an invasion of the Arab 
homeland, to be traitorous, filthy and dangerous aftereffects of As-Sadat's existence. 
It is he alone, to the exclusion of all other Egyptians, whom we hold responsible for 
the crime of recognizing the enemy and removing Egypt, until this very moment, from the 
Arab confrontation and from the Arab nation, and placing it within the ranks of its 
enemies without any justification or need. 


We must stand firm on the side of the Egyptian people and the honorable Egyptian 
opposition. We must place all capabilities at their disposal. We must resist, most 
forcefully, all the attempts at intervention in Egypt and ensure that Egypt is left, 

in the present stage, for its sons. This means full alignment with the Egyptian national 
popular forces which are working to bring Egypt back into the Arab fold against the 
Israeli and U.S. enemy.

After the execution of the death sentence on its agent As-Sadat, the United States suffered 
hysteria. It began to act with unlimited recklessness against the willpower of the Arab and 
Egyptian peoples and the sanctity of their territory. It is determined to inherit As-Sadat's 
legacy directly. Its venomous mouth waters over Egypt and it is overwhelmed by a strong 
desire to swallow it. It is now trying to eat it up most hungrily and ferociously. But we 
must oppose it and ally ourselves with those in Egypt who decide to resist America. We look 
forward to the day when Egypt returns and resumes its leading position in the front of the 
Arab ranks and when closed borders become open, closed roads open and when the Egyptians and 
their brothers embrace each other on the day of return which we hope will be soon. Forward, 
and the struggle will continue. [applause]

delegates [as received] to the emergency session of the Arab People's Congress (APC) due

The meeting discussed the programme of the urgent tasks of the Arab liberation movement 
so as to confront the imperialist and reactionary plots on the Arab arena. It is to be 
submitted to the APC emergency session. The programme included two main items: 


1. Way to restore Egypt [to] its natural and leading place in the Arab world following 
the death of As-Sadat. 


Fahd plan and the political background and the future dangers resulting from this plan 
and the national necessity to confront and foil the imperialist and reactionary moves. 


TRIPOLI REPORTS GOUKOUNI FEARS OF EXPLOITATION 
LD1L90251 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0015 GMT 19 Nov 81 


[Text] Chadian President Goukouni Oueddei said that the Libyan Arab forces played a decisive 
role in ending the civil war which plagued the Chadian people and that the people would 
remember with appreciation that role which was carried out by the Libyan forces with dynamism 
and courage. President Goukouni added in a television talk in Zaire yesterday evening that 
the Libyan presence in Chad was beneficial to Chad and its people and actively contributed 

to safeguarding security and to strengthening the liberty of the Chadian people. He expressed 
his fear that the situation in Chad would be exploited against the interest of the Chadian 
people after the withdrawal of the Libyan forces, remarking on the operations of invasion 

and occupation carried out by the Sudanese forces and the forces of Hissein Habre, which 

have completed their occupation of four towns in eastern Chad. 


JANA: _HABRE, SUDANESE FORCES CAPTURE CHAD TOWN 


LD191954 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1855 GMT 19 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Nov (JANA) -- Abeche, the largest town in eastern Chad, fell today to 
the Sudanese forces and the forces of Hissein Habre. These forces are now continuing their 
advance on remaining Chadian towns and regions. Their major objective will be the fall of 
the capital, Ndjamena. Commenting on this, the JANA political editor says that the whole 
world is now sure and fully convinced that Libya is the protector of peace and holds aloft 
the flags of peace. Libya offered many sacrifices so that security, peace and stability 
could prevail in the heart of the African continent. The world is convinced that the 
Libyan forces are peace forces and the guarantee of security in this region. 


V. 20 Nov 81 ie NORTH AFRICA 


As a test to this honest categorical assertion, as soon as the Libyan forces withdrew 
from Chad, the situation there collapsed, chaos prevailed and trouble became rife. It 
has now become easy for the Sudanese forces and the forces of Hissein Habre to occupy 
Chad. 


The whole world also has become clearly convinced that the African and European states 
which were insistingly putting pressure on President Goukouni Queddei of Chad and his 
government to ask for the withdrawal of the Libyan forces tricked the Chadian president 
and his government. In fact, while putting pressure on him, they were plotting against 
Chad and its security. Praise be to God that we live and witness the difference between 
right and wrong.
