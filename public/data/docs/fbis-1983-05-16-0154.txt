LIBYA

JANA Reports Rebellion Among Palestinian Forces 
LD131113 Tripoli JANA in English 0820 GMT 
13 May 83 


[Text] Beirut, Rajab 31, May 13, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — A wide scale rebellion broke out amongst Palestin- 
ian forces stationed in Al-Biqa’Vvalley. The rebellions forces 
were the Yarmouk and other units led by Abu Musa, this erupted 
into a revolution led by Abu-Salih and Abu Musa who took 
control of Fatah forces in the area and controlled the whole of 
Al-Biga’ Valley and the road leading to Syria. Former leaders 
were disarmed and most of them were arrested. Palestinian 
brigades were purged of their right wing factions while some of 
them escaped. Abu Jihad [Khalil al-Wazir] was trapped in Tasa 
Laba’ [as received] and Colonel Abu-Musa replaced him as 
leader of the Palestinian forces in Al-Biga’ Valley. 


Palestinian revolutionary forces hailed this as the true Fatah 
revolution till victory, and not Fatah of hesitation and surrender. 


Abu-Salih refused all attempts of reconciliation made by right 
wing Palestinian factions and rejected any dialogue before 
bringing to trial those responsible for the Beirut war and those 
who left the camps defenceless until they fell to the enemy who 
occupied them and destroyed them and those who conspired 
against the resistance and drove it out of Lebanon. 


The Palestinian factions hope that the camp would be a peaceful 
and bloodless one. 


Meanwhile Abu “Ammar [Yasir ‘Arafat] retaliated by arresting 
six Fedayeen belonging to Abu- Nidal [Sabri al-Banna] group and 
executed them. Their bodies were mutilated and thrown in the 
streets, a act which caused anger, disgust, and mutiny among the 
factions of all the Palestinian resistance 


Vv. 16 May 83
