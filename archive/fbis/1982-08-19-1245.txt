LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES ISLAMIC CONFERENCE 


LD181424 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 18 Aug 82 


[Text] The brother leader today spoke at the moming session of the second Islamic con- 
ference that is meeting in Tripoli. Hundreds of ulema and Muslim scholars are attending 

the conference. At this session, the leader dealt with several essential issues of interest 
to Islam, Muslims and their fateful issues. In his speech, the brother leader called for 
the adoption of the call for the liberation of God's holy places and for the struggle so 
that the holy places will not be under any authority. The brother leader stressed the need 
for the holy lands to be open to all Muslims, who will not need any visa from an embassy to 
visit these lands and who should not be subjected to any inspection or questions when in 
these lands. 


The leader also called for setting up an international assembly for Islam, consisting of 

all Islamic organizations and societies. He also called for the formation of a political 
association of all Muslim peoples to replace the Muslim World League, which carries out 

the orders of the Saudi princes and legalizes what is forbidden for the Saudis. 


The brother leader stressed the importance of the adoption of Islam without any creeds in 
the call of those who call for Islam in the world because this is regarded as a return to 
the original source and a return to Islam which came down from God to Muhammad -- may God's 
prayers and peace be upon him. 


The brother leader pointed out that Libya has become the center of hope for oppressed 
Muslims everywhere and a symbol for the liberation of the Arab masses. It is also a symbol 
for the gathering of Muslims everywhere, reaffirming that there is no contradiction between 
the revolution and religion. 


In this connection, the leader referred to the reactionary presentation of Islam adopted 

by the Al Sa'ud family, reaffirming that this example is the worst example on earth as the 
Sa'uds are the most cynical, immoral and backward people. He said tne United States is 
leading a 10th crusade against the Muslims with Saudi oil. He pointed out that the Sa'ud 
princes obey U.S. orders and they agreed to convene a summit to disperse the palestinians 

and to put an end to the Palestinian Resistance after having been ordered by the United 

States to do so. In this connection, he said the Great al-Fatih Revolution is against the 
conducting of negotiations between the Palestinians and the Zionist enemy because negotiations 
with the Zionist enemy have led to the destruction and invasion of Lebanese towns. 


The brother leader drew attention to the empty slogans with which the Saudi rulers boast 
with regard to the pan-Arab issues, saying: We challenge these rulers to stop the oil 
supply to the United States, withdraw their credits from U.S. and Zionist banks and sever 
their political relations with it. We challenge them to send a military division to parti- 
cipate in the eviction of the Zionist enemy in accordance with the Libyan Arab proposal. 


JANA REPORTS ON OFFICIALS' TALKS IN PRC 


LD172204 Tripoli JANA in English 1637 GMT 17 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 28, August 17 -- Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Mr Jadallah ‘Azzuz 
at-Talhi, secretary of the General People's Committee, and Mr Zhao Ziyang, chairman of the 
State Council of the People's Republic of China, this morning held their first round of 
talks during whick _ney discussed world affairs of mutual interest, bilateral relations, 
and development of bilateral economic, trade and technical cooperation.

Also present on the Libyan side were the secretaries of communication and marine transport, 
economy and light industry and a number of experts from various secretariats. On the 
Chinese side also present were the foreign, industry and trade ministers and a number of 
officials from other ministries. 


Our correspondent in Beijing reports that during these talks joint committees were 
established on economic, trade and technical affairs. They are given the task of laying 
the foundation for strengthening and developing bilateral relations. 


The Chinese side expressed during these talks its full desire and readiness to develop 
cooperation between the two peoples. 


Premier Zhao Ziyang opened the talks with a speech welcoming the Libyan Arab delegation 
and stressing his country's desire to develop and strengthen bilateral relations in all fields.
