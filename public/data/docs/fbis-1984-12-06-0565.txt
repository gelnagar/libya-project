LIBYA

U.S. Statement on Chad Pullout Criticized 
LDO51840 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2300 GMT 4 Dec 84 


[Political talk by unidentified commentator] 


[Text] U.S. allegations that Libyan support elements have not 
withdrawn from Chad come as part of the hostile actions by the 
terrorist Ronald Reagan admisistration against the Jamahir- 
iyah, for the purpose of discrediting the role of the Libyan Arab 
people on the African Continent. The statements by U:S. officials 
were made in this connection after Reagan's reelection. They 
once more point out that the terrorist administration in Washing- 
ton is preparing for a hostile action against the Jamahiriyah for 
the purpose of silencing the resounding voice of revolution which 
now has a wide and effective echo in Africa and the world. 


The Reagan government declared that it has three objectives — 
liquidating communism in the Caribbean, solving the Middle 
East issue, and ending the disturbances in North Africa. This

declaration, defined by the terrorist Reagan, comes as the best 
proof of the U.S. Administration’s intention to carry out a 
premeditated aggression. 


The disturbances in North Africa to which Reagan referred, are 
nothing but the results of the revolutionary stands of the Great 
Al-Fatih Revoluton toward the causes of freedom and their 
defense in the world. These stands have created terror in the 
circles of imperialism hostile to the rights to freedom and inde- 
pendence of the people who aspire to be free of subservience and 
hegemony. Such stands also became a worry for the master of 
the U.S. black house. 


Washington’s policy toward the Jamahiriyah and the Great 
Al-Fatih Revolution became more inflexible and pompous after 
the reelection of the terrorist Reagan, and with it the U.S. threats 
and the hostile provocations increased. Thus we can see why the 
U.S. Administration persists in reopening the question of the 
withdrawal of the French forces and the Libyan support elements 
to the GUNT after the dossier was closed with the release of the 
Libyan-French communique on Chad. That communique con- 
firmed the end of the withdrawal operations in accordance with 
the agreed plan. 


We cannot understand why this subject has been reopened except 
from the angle of another of the hostile and provocative attempts 
by the the terrorist Reagan administraion against the Jama- 
hiriyah people. The allegation that the question has not ended is 
a U.S. imperialist fabrication meant to be used as pressure on the 
French Government to involve it in an issue which ended in 
accordance with the terms agreed upon in the Libyan-French 
communique on Chad. The U.S. Government has made the 
allegation to find an excuse to force France and its right wing 
forces and the rebel Habre to return to Chad. 


We once again say and stress that the Jamahiriyah has, in 
accordance with the terms of the agreement, withdrawn the 
Libyan support elements from Chad and has not violated the 
agreement. On the contrary, it has remained committed to the 
agreement and has declared its readiness to cooperate with 
France in finding a political solution. However, before everything 
else, the Jamahiriyah is prepared to fight in the north, the east, 
and the south.
