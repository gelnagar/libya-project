AL-QADHDHAFI DENIES SOVIETS HAVE 'RIGHTS' TO BASES IN LIBYA
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp05-01559r000400370082-5
https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-cia-rdp05-01559r000400370082-5

Summary (AI): A June 1970 interview, reported from Hamburg by a news agency, in which Libyan Revolution Command Council member Muammar al-Qadhdhafi denies that the Soviet Union has rights to bases in Libya. He says Libya buys arms from the USSR and other countries for defence, and discusses the Lebanon crisis, Israel, and relations with West Germany. The OCR is heavily garbled, so many passages are uncertain.
• Qadhdhafi says the USSR has never asked for facilities in Libyan ports for its Mediterranean fleet.
• He says Libya buys aircraft, tanks and other weapons for defence from the Soviet Union and other countries.
• He states that about 2.65 million Libyans live in Libya (OCR reads '265 million', likely a typo).
• He says the Lebanese civil war is an internal affair and that there is no Israel, only Palestine occupied by Israelis.
• He says Libya has always had close economic and political relations with West Germany, which could help build the Libyan economy.
• 1970-06-07: Qadhdhafi denies that the Soviet Union has rights to bases in Libya and says Libya never asked the USSR for shorts or ports for its Mediterranean fleet.
