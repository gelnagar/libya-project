Summary (AI): A Washington Post column from December 1981 says U.S. intelligence has evidence linking Qaddafi's oil money to terrorist groups such as the PFLP, Baader-Meinhof, the IRA and the Red Brigades. It also covers a U.S. Air Force OSI report on terrorism and the 1970 Son Tay raid in Vietnam. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• Qaddafi denied sending a 'hit squad' to assassinate President Reagan.
• Qaddafi reportedly gave the PFLP, Baader-Meinhof Gang, IRA and Red Brigades money, weapons and training, according to the column.
• The column says there is no direct link yet between Dozier's kidnaping and Qaddafi's campaign.
• The Son Tay raid took place on Nov. 21, 1970, about 23 miles west of Hanoi.
