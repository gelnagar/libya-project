Summary (AI): A February 1976 Baghdad radio broadcast, relayed by FBIS, reports Libyan leader Muammar al-Qadhdhafi criticizing the recent settlement of the Lebanese crisis and calling for the elimination of the feudal, tribal and sectarian system in Lebanon. It concerns Libyan foreign policy only and has no Tripoli-specific content.
• Qadhdhafi said the recent settlement of the Lebanese crisis achieved nothing and the present cease-fire would not last.
• He called for eliminating the feudal, tribal and sectarian regime to achieve final peace in Lebanon.
• He said religion must be separated from state affairs and criticized the redistribution of parliamentary seats between Moslems and Christians.
• 1976-02-11: Qadhdhafi criticizes the Lebanese settlement in a statement broadcast by Baghdad Voice of Palestine
