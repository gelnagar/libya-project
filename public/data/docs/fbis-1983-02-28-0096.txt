FOREIGN LIAISON BUREAU REJECTS SCHULTZ" 'LIES' 


LD261648 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1530 GMT 26 Feb 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 26 Feb (JANA) -- The Foreign Liaison Bureau in the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah issued a statement today in which it refutes the allegations 
and the lies which United States Secretary of State George Shultz repeats against the 
Jamahiriyah, and in it lists several facts which refute the latter.

The Foreign Liaison Bureau noted the continued allegations and fabrications by U.S. 
Secretary of State Shultz concerning the Jamahiriyah and its attitudes toward its 
neighboring sister countries, despite the worldwide condemnation of the American pro- 
vocations and threats addressed to the Jamahiriyah. The Foreign Liaison Bureau, while 
pointing in particular to the latest statements by the United States secretary of state 
at his press conference in Atlanta, Georgia, wants to point out the following facts: 


The statements by Shultz confirm the intention of the Reagan administration to impose 
the American hegemonist plans on the whole Arab region. And seeing that the Jamahiriyah 
is the bastion of resistance aimed at aborting these plans, it has begun fabricating 
justifications in order to shake the steadfastness of this citadel. The Libyan people, 
however, confident of the justice of its cause and the firmness of its stand will not 

be scared by the American military provocations or the statements by Shultz, the mis- 
guided dog barking on the sidelines of events. 


The Libyan people, having paid a high price for the affirmation of their freedom and 
sovereignty, do not accept any American intrusion [tattafful] designed to shake their 
solidarity inclinations or to affect their relations with heir Arab brothers, with 
whom they are linked by the same estiny and with whom they work to achieve the same 
ambitions. And on this bases, they consider any American attempt to interfere in their 
relations with their brothers in Egypt, the Sudan, or others, as interference in an 
internal Arab affair on a par with interference [in the relations] between the American 
states f Alaska and California or between the Yugoslav republics Serbia and Croatia. 
The continuation by America of the policy of arrogance and threats to use force confirm 
that the American Administration has forgotten the lesson of Vietnam with its defeats 
and harshness. We say that unless this administration works to rectify the dangers of 
the policies of force and stops playing the role of gendarme in the Arab region, the 
Libyan people's will to challenge is capable of putting an end to this American 
onslaught. 


LEADER RECEIVES SOLIDARITY CABLE FROM ETHIOPIA 


LD251754 Tripoli JANA in English 1842 GMT 24 Feb 83 


{Text ] Tripoli, Jumada 12, Feb 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The revolution's leader 
has received the following cable from Mengistu Haile Mariam, president of the Military 
Council and president of the Executive Committee of the working people's party of 
socialist Ethiopia. 


"Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 1 Sept Revolution: We in socialist 

Ethiopia are closely following developments concerning acts of provocation and aggres- 
sion by U.S. imperialism's armed forces, in cooperation with some reactionary regimes 
in the region, against the Jamahiriyah people. We are fully confident that the fra- 
ternal Libyan people under your brave leadership will, as in the past, foil the 
American conspiracies. I affirm to you that the people and government of socialist 
Ethiopia are in solidarity with their Libyan brothers and sisters in resisting American 
imperialist terrorism and arrogance. 


Revolutionary greetings. Mengistu Haile Mariam."
