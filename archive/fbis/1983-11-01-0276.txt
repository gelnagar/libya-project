LIBYA

Al-OQadhdhafi Warns of ‘Violent Reaction’ in 1984 
LD311612 Tripoli JANA in English 1448 GMT 31 Oct 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Muharram 25, Oct 31, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The leader of the revolution made a statement to 
the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY in which he said: 
“Unless practical and concrete results are achieved by the joint 
Arab action during 1983 in the interest of the Arab unity and the 


ordinary Arab citizen and against the enemy, there are fears of 
4 violent reaction during 1984 and after that will not leave those 
defeatist regimes any chance for manocuvering because they will 
not find any argument to defend themselves.” 


Jabir Meets Admiral Mamula in Belgrade 
LDO10342 Tripoli JANA in English 1910 GMT 31 Oct 83 


(Text] Belgrade, Muharram 25, Oct 31, JAMAHIRIVAH 
NEWS AGENCY Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, com- 
mander in chief of the Libyan Armed Forces, met this morning 
with Admiral Branko Mamula the secretary general of the 
Federal Defence Secretary [as received] in Yugoslavia. The 
meeting which was attended by the Arab Libyan delegation and 
the chief of staffs of the Yugoslav Armed Forces focused on 
mutual cooperation existing between the two countries and the 
ways and means to develop it further 


Returns From CSSR, SFRY 
LDO10052 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
OOISGMT I Nov 83 


[E-xcerpt| Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief 
of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces has concluded his visit to the 
CSSR and the SFRY and returned to Tripoli last night. During 
these visits Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir conducted talks and 
held meetings with the officials in the two friendly countries, 
concentrating on the bolstering of cooperation existing between 
the Jamahiriyah and Czechoslovakia and Y ugosiavia in all fields, 
and the means of developing this cooperation in a manner that 
would achieve the interests of the Libyan Arab people and the 
Czechoslovak and the Yugoslav peoples 


Paper on British Forces’ Return to Egypt 
LDO10325 Tripoli JANA in English 1842 GMT 31 Oct 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Muharram 25, Oct 31, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY The Libyan Arab paper Az-Zahf al-Akhdar 
{words indistinct] revolutionary committee wrote on the return of 
the British colonialist forces to Egypt. The paper said that “after 
the death of ‘Abd al-Nasir and the establishment of a hireling 
regime, British colonialist forces are returning to Egypt, thus 
challenging the will and dignity of the Egyptian masses and the 
Arab nation that views this treacherous act as 4 grave insult to 
the principles of the July 23 revolution [words indistinct] persis- 
tent struggle against all forms of colonialism in the Arab nation.” 
The paper referred to the changes that have taken place in Egypt 
following the execution of the traitor As-Sadat who opened 
Egypt's doors wide open to Zionism and (7U.S.) imperialism and 
transformed the country into a U.S.-Zionist base. Thus 
threatening the existence of the Arab nation. [punctuation as 
received| 


It added: “It has becomne clear that (’traitor) Husni Mubar- 
ak’s regime is a continuation of As-Sadat’s. It is continuing the 
process of transforming Egypt into an imperialist Zionist base 
hostile to the Arab nation and to the nation's progressive and 
revolutionary regimes. In fact, Mubarak’s role is more dangerous
