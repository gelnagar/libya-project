LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI REPIACED AS HEAD OF PEOPLE'S GENERAL CONGRESS 
TA011213 Paris AFP in English 1210 GMT 1 Mar 79 TA 


[Text ] Paris, March 1 (AFP) --'Abd al-'ati al-'Ubaydi replaced Colonel Mu!ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi today as head of Libya's People's General Congress, Libya's official 
news agency reported in a broadcast monitored here, 


Mr al-'Ubaydi was "freely and democratically" elected secretary general of the congress 
in a unanimous vote at a special session, the agency said. Secretary (Minister) of 
Information Muhammad az-Zuwayy was elected deputy secretary general, Mr al-'Ubaydi was 
previously chairman of the People's Committee (Cabinet). Col al-Qadhdhafi announced 
last Dec 19 that he would give up leadership of the congress to devote himself to 
revolutionary action, That did not mean he was withdrawing "into my tent," he said 
then, The former ruling party, the Arab Socialist Union, was transformed into the 
People's General Congress in 1976. 


AR-RA'Y: AL-QADHDHAFI CANCELS VISIT TO IRAN 
JNO10645 Amman AR-RA'Y in Arabic 1 Mar 79 p 1 JN 


[Text ] Tehran, It has been learned here from well-informed sources that an expected 

visit to Iran by Libyan leader Mufammar al-Qadhdhafi has been canceled due to differences 
over the disappearance of Imam Musa as-Sadr, leader of the Shilites in Lebanon, Imam 
Musa as-Sadr has not been seen for months, following a visit to Libya.
