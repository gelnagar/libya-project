LIBYA

Al-Ondhdhafi Warns of New Crusade Against Arabs 
LD1I 31004 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
OOISGMT 13 Oct 43 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has sent a message to 
Arab presidents and kings concerning the British maneuvers 
scheduled for 24 October which are part of the crusade in the 
Arab homeland which the U.S. Administration has began to fan 
in Lebanon. Here is the teat of the message 


It appears that we are on the threshold of a new stage timewise, 
but a very old stage in so far as its action is concerned. We are 
about to witness occupation of the Arab homeland by the same 
Western Christian forces which had never got rid of and will 
never get rid of the complex of hating the East — primarily the 
Arab nation and Arab nationalism —— and rancor against Islam 
and its prophet Muhammad. They reject his prophethood and 
infinitely and unlawfully covet the resources of this homeland 
and its geographic position 


The naval concentrations against Lebanon and Syria by the 
Christian Western powers, supported by the Phalangists, are the 
same concentrations which were a prelude to the crusade wars 
and the same fleets with which these states occupied the Arab 
countries during the collapse of the Islamic Ottoman Empire 
with the same pretexts and justifications 


The repeated maneuvers of the LU S fleets in Egyptian territories 
and at Libya's borders are clearly a prelude to occupation. In the 
past | have addressed you about those threats directly leveled 
against Syria, Lebanon, and Libya. But today | address you again
