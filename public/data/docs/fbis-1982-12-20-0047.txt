LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES NATION 19 DECEMBER 


LD200144 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0015 GMT 20 Dec 82 


[Text] The brother leader explained yesterday evening in a live radio and TV broadcast 
the agenda of the third session of the Basic People's Congresses. The agenda includes 
serious issues which demand that every citizen attend, express his opinion, and decide 
his fate by issuing the decisions and laws concerning his fate and that of his family. 
No one will decide for the ones who do not attend. 


The brother leader explained that 1983 will be eventful and we, as the masses, will 
make serious decisions concerning the agenda of the third session, decisions which will 
concern our future and relations with others and all that concerns the life of the 
Libyan citizen. 


The brother leader confirmed the need for the masses to shoulder their responsibility 
concerning every decision that they make about all the serious issues on the agenda, a 
matter which confirms the popular exercise of popular authority. He said we must 
remember that we can decide to become strong by the decisions which we will make during 
this session, to become rich, needing no one, and not to be weak before others. The 
leader added we can make decisions which are harsh on us in order to have military 
strength with which to deter others [words indistinct]. 


The brother leader pointed out that the revolutionary program for comprehensive 
mobilization approved by the fifth meeting of the revolutionary committees heads 

the agenda. He said that this program has been drawn up by the revolutionary force 
which is requested to be present at the basic popular congresses when they begin 
discussion of the agenda in order to play their inciting [ah tahridi] role and in order 
to make the msses understand this plan [words indistinct]. He said that it is the 
responsibility of the masses to approve this revolutionary program for comprehensive 
mobilization which is aimed at achieving the minimum level of food so that we may 
become strong. If we succeed in the implementation of this program we will be 
breaking the psychological barrier of Arab weakness and we will be able to face up 
to our dangers and enemies. 


The brother confirmed that strength is in our hands and under our feet and we can with 
the free will of the Libyan people, decide on what can make the strength to firmly 
confront our enemies available and what can become an example to be followed. The 
leader confirmed that exhausted determination does not promote decisiveness, confirming 
that the determination of the Libyan people is not exhausted and we scorntte enemies, 
Israel, the United States, reaction and all these forces,

We do not consider them fate and divine decree. We consider the Israelis much weaker 
than us and destined to pass away in the Arab region. The brother leader confirmed we 
feel strong before our enemies and we shall surpass them and defeat them. We shall 
recover our usurped land, pointing out that the capabilities of our enemies are limited 
and they are the ones who depend for their living on the United States and who are in 
constant need of it. We can do without the United States but the Israelis cannot live 
without the United States. 


The brother leader added that the Arab nation can do without the United States should 

it decide this someday. Then the brother leader went on to the article concerning the 
definition of the attitude of the Libyan people toward their enemies abroad and explained 
that we all should take a united attitude toward those misguided dogs who fled abroad 
after they plundered the resources of the people. The Arab Libyan people should decide 
the measures which should be taken against tuose [word indistinct]. 


The brother leader confirmed that the Great 1 September Revolution is a strong revolu- 
tion and fears no one. Its hand is long and must eliminate those who encroach upon it 
anywhere, and it confronts the United States and all misguided dogs. 


Further on Speech 
LD201014 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 20 Dec 82 


[Excerpt] Tripoli, 20 Dec (JANA) -- The brother leader of the revolution met at 2000 
yesterday with the masses on radio and television when he explained the agenda of the 
third session of the Basic People's Congress due to begin today. 


The brother leader of the revolution referred to the foreign policy item on the agenda 
and noted that in discussing this item use could be made of the report prepared by the 
People's Foreign Liaison Bureau, and that as regards the agreements concluded by the 
Jamahiriyah with a number of fraternal and friendly countries, these will not be valid 
until they are ratified by the Basic People's Congresses. 


The brother leader spoke of the importance of deciding through the People's Congresses 
with which countries better relations should be established, which countries are hostile, 
and which are neutral, referring in this regard to the French stance vis-a-vis the con- 
vocation of the last African summit in Tripoli. He explained that France had apologized 
twice and at several levels and had denied that it took part in preventing a number of 
African countries from participating in the summit. However, these countries received 
orders from America or received large sums of money [amwal] from an Arab quarter for not 
attending the conference. : 


The brother leader pointed out that since France has apologized, we politically and 
diplomatically accept the apology and consider it innocent. It has expressed its will- 
ingness to maintain good relations with Libya, which is a new stand. 


Furthermore, the brother leader referred to China and the transformations which have 
occurred in its policy toward the Jamahiriyah, something which we must take into con- 
sideration when we deal with the topic of our relations with other countries. 


The brother leader spoke of America's stand vis-a-vis the Jamahiriyah, saying: "America 
persists in its obstinacy and does not wish to maintain relations with Libya because 
Libya refuses to be subservient to it. [quotation mark as received] He affirmed that 
we will not submit to America and that it is the Arab Libyan people who say that. He 
explained that it is an honor for the Libyan people that America considers them its 
enemy. This is a testimony to innocence and honor before the Arabs and the world; the 
support which we gained from the peoples is because we oppose America and because we 
are a revolutionary country which has principles and values and which adores freedom 
and peace.

Concluding his speech, the brother leader stressed the importance of the items on the 
agenda, saying that the year 1983 will be a qualitative jump, in various spheres for 
the masses, and that the role of the revolutionary committees will be crucial in this 
period for the masses to shoulder their responsibilities toward understanding the 
revolutionary program for full mobilization. 


AL-QADHDHAFI CABLES PALESTINIANS, LEBANESE 


LD171948 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1915 GMT 17 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 17 Dec (JANA) -- The brother leader of the revolution has sent a tele- 
gram to leaders of Palestinian Resistance movement detachments and to leaders of the 
Lebanese Nationalist Movement detachments urging them to hold an urgent meeting that 
would bring them all together in the Jamahiriyah as soon as possible. 


In his telegram the leader stressed that the period after the invasion of Lebanon 
requires more than at any time before the strengthening of the alliance and cohesion 
of the detachments of the Palestinian Resistance and the Lebanese Nationalist Movement 
through the adoption of new plans for struggle at the Palestin_. 1, Lebanese, and Arab 
levels. 


The leader noted that any delay in holding such a meeting will enable the enemy to 
overcome the unity of the Palestinian Resistance and the Lebanese Nationalist Movement 
units, stressing that the Arab Libyan people and its revolution stand in deeds on the 
side of the Palestinian and Lebanese revolutionaries in the darkest circumstances and 
hardest times. 


Cables Steadfastness Front Leaders 
LD172012 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1920 GMT 17 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 17 Dec (JANA) -- The brother leader of the revolution has sent a tele- 
gram to leaders of the Arab Steadfastness and Confrontation Front member countries, 
inviting them to meet in the Jamahiriyah at the earliest date to discuss the current 
situation and to agree on a comprehensive strategy that would meet the circumstances 
the Arab nation is experiencing. 


In his telegram the brother leader stressed that the situation facing the Arab nation 
now is no less grave — on the contrary -- than when the front was set up. He said we 
are still marking time outside the sphere of effective action against attempts and 
conspiracies aimed at turning military defeat into political defeat. 


The brother leader underlined that in the absence of the spirit and action of stead- 
fastness and confrontation, the phases of the enemy scheme are clearly and tangibly 
following one another without any si ficant Arab resistance.

The leader concluded his telegram to the leaders of the Arab Steadfastness and 
Confrontation Front by expressing his conviction that much could still be done and 
the honor of steadfastness will always remain the most honorable and useful option. 


ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS SESSION ENDS 


LD192002 Tripoli JANA in English 1832 GMT 19 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi' al-Awwal 3, Dec. 19, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The fourth 
session of the Arab People's Congress (APC) Permanent Secretariat ended today in 
Tripoli's Al-Shati hotel. 


The secretariat issued a statement at the end of its proceedings praising the 

stands of world national liberation movements and democratic and socialist forces, 

and their backing of Arab rights and effective solidarity with the Palestinian revolu- 
tion and Lebanese people. 


The secretariat condemned all capitulationist plans, especially the Reagan plan and 

the Fes summit's policy. It also saluted the Palestinian people’s struggle and their 
heroic resistance against the elimination of Arab identity from the occupied Arab 

lands and the expansion of Zionist settlements there. It also lauded the heroic strug- 
gle of the Lebanese patriotic forces against Zionist occupation. 

In its statement, the secretariat affirmed the necessity of holding an emergency 
meeting of the leaders of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front's member-countries 
to confront the current situation to the Arab nation. 

Several documents were also issued at the end of the meeting, including: 


1. A political report on the current situation in the Arab nation and the responsi- 
bilities of the Arab liberation movement. 


2. A critical analysis of official and popular Arab action. 
3. Total popular mobilisation in a bid to seize the initiative from the enemy. 
4. The secretariat's programme for 1983. 


The APC's Permanent Secretariat also decided on calling an emergency meeting of the 
APC from February 1-5, 1983.
