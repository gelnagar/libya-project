Summary (AI): A 1981 New York Times Magazine article (as reproduced in a CIA reading-room copy) describes former CIA men Edwin Wilson and Frank Terpil allegedly helping Col. Qaddafi build an assassination-weapons plant, recruit ex-Green Berets to train Libyan and terrorist forces, and ship arms illegally. It is mainly about US-based intrigue, with little about Tripoli itself.
• Information about the Qaddafi connection had been known to the government since fall 1976, when Mulcahy approached the CIA and FBI.
• Wilson and Terpil are said to have helped Libya set up a plant producing assassination weapons.
• They allegedly recruited dozens of former Green Berets to teach Libyan soldiers and Arab terrorists to handle explosives.
• The two Americans were indicted for wrongdoing, according to Federal investigators.
• Qaddafi boasted of supporting terrorism in the Middle East, Europe and Africa.
