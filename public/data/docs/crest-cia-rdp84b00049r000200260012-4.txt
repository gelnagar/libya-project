CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE ILLEGIB 

N, D. C. 20505 

7 December 198] 

NOTE FOR: Deputy Director of Central Intelligence 

FROM: Charles E. Waterman, NIO/NESA 

SUBJECT: Requirements on Libyan Question 

1. I would appreciate guidance on what is desired from the intelligence 
community on the Libyan question. I understand that the questions revolve 
around two issues: 

a. What we know and do not know about Libyan terrorist 
threats in this country, 

b. The implications of various policy options currently 
under consideration. 

2. It is my impression that intelligence community consideration of 
Morn i these items would, at present, be redundant. Nearly all evidence 
of the threat in this country is included in the list of DDO reports contained 
at the end of the policy paper under consideration. To submit this to 
community review would, in effect, be engaging in a counter intelligence 
analysis of these reports. Given knowledge of sources, etc., I would 
think this would be better done by the DDO itself. 

3. If we, at this relatively late date, are to consider the impact 
of policy alternatives now under consideration, I would like to do so in 
a way which does not merely duplicate the current NSC deliberations at 
the analytical level. Perhaps the most useful input the intelligence 
community could now make would be to analyze in depth the likely reactions 
to and implications of policies which are adopted. K 

« Charles E. Waterman 

This Note is classified 
SECRET in its entirety.
