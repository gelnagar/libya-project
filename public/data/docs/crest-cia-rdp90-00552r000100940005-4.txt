Qaddafi denied ordering plots against President Reagan and other US officials. No specific Tripoli locations are mentioned.
• INS headquarters sent a seven-page memo to border and airport offices listing names, ages, aliases and sketches of five members of a team believed led by Carlos.
• The memo described the second team as including three Syrians and three Libyans, and reportedly two Iranians, a Palestinian, a Lebanese and an East German.
• A key informer's credibility had not been determined, according to one source.
• The Los Angeles Times reported Israeli intelligence may have been a major source and that Israel may be trying to build US support for a strike against Qaddafi.
• 1981-12: INS memo with suspected hit-squad identities was found posted on the lunchroom bulletin board at the San Ysidro border-crossing station
