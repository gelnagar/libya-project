LIBYA

Bardai Radio Says Shelling of Faya-Largeau Stops 
LD050731 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0708 GMT 5 Aug 83 


[Text] Bardai, 5 Aug (JANA) -- Head of the Legitimate Govern- 
ment of National Unity in Chad, Goukouni Oueddei, has 
announced the stopping of the artillery shelling of Faya-Largeau 
Bardai Radio, which broadcast this report, said Goukouni is 
giving Habre -- if he is still alive -- a good opportunity to 
surrender to the Legitimate Government of National Unity in 
Chad. The radio also said that Goukouni Oueddei also gave the 
followers of Habre another chance to surrender or return to their 
kinsfolk 


Opportunity for Talks Welcomed 
LD050736 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0715 GMT 5 Aug 83 


{Text] Tripoli 5 Aug (JANA) — The Socialist People’s Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah has welcomed the decision of the Legitimate 
Government of National Unity of Chad to stop the artillery 
shelling of the positions of the rebels in Faya-Largeau. 


This decision by the Legitimate Government of National Unity 
in Chad opens the opportunity for negotiations to end the conflict 
over authority in Chad in the event Habre was still alive 


Chadian Defense, Youth Ministers Captured 
LD041934 Tripoli JANA in English 1736 GMT 4 Aug 83 


[Text] Bardai, Shawa 25, August 4, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY The Chadian National Unity Government has 
announced that the forces have captured some of Habre’s top 
officials, including the deputy minister of youth, the defence 
minister and a number of Army officers and soldiers 


The defence minister was said to have escaped but the forces are 
still searching for him in Oum Chalouba. 


These officials and Army officers were captured during the fierce 
fighting in Oum Chalouba which fell in the hands of the forces 
of the National Unity Government. The statement said Baradi 
radio broadcast last night that the forces of the National Unity 
Government had captured the minister of labour in the so-called 
Habre government who confirmed reports that Hissein Habre 
had been killed in Faya-Largeau.

Arab League Cited on U.S. ‘Provocative’ Actions 
LD050947 Tripoli JANA in English 0840 GMT § Aug 83 


[Text] Tunis, Shawal 26, August 5, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY The General Secretariat of the Arab League 
condemned U.S.A.’s aggressive stands by using its right of vet 
against a resolution condemning the oppressive actions and set- 
tlement policies of the Zionist enemy and also condemned the 
U.S.provocative actions carried out by American planes against 
Libya. 


This was expressed in a statement put out by the Arab League's 
General Secretariat yesterday 


The statement said that the position adopted by U.S. Administra 
tion by using its right of veto in the Security Council against a 
resolution voted for by 13 countries representing the United 
Nation’s member countries. [sentence as received] This type of 
action in general harms the standing of the U.N. and its message 
which aims at ending aggression and occupation to achieve peace 
and justice among the nations of the world 


The statement added that U.S. support of Israel in its racist 
expansionist settlement policy is pushing it to use its right to veto 


This stand represents a reversal of the previous U.S. stands as 
the U.N. representative of the U.S. alleged that the dismantling 
of the settlements despite its illegitimacy is unrealistic 


The statement also said that this negative stand by US. in the 
Security Council is parallel to another situation which increases 
the tension in the Mediterranean through the action of the LS 
fleet and U:S. aircraft which are provocative to the Arab Libyan 
shores, a situation which can only be considered as a threat

JANA Cites Omani Foreign Minister on Israel 
LDO032133 Tripoli JANA in English 1743 GMT 3 Aug 83 


[Text] Kuwait, Shawal 24, August 3, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY The Omani minister of state for foreign affairs 
called in Kuwait yesterday for Arab reconciliation with the 
Zionist enemy and to end the state of hostility with it once and 
for all. Sultan Qabus had earlier resorted to this treacherou 
stand during his visit to Washington. The Omani minister als 
added that whether now or after 10 or 15 years, a reconciliatior
