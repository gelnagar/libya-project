AL-QADHDHAFI INTERVIEWED ON JAMAHIRIYAH CONCEPTS 
LD281836 Tripoli JANA in English 1538 GMT 28 May 79 ID 


{Text] Tripoli, May 28, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--In an interview with REUTER, Colonel 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the First of September Revolution, said, "The concepts 
according to which the Jamahiriyah has been set up are considered unique in relation 

to current world political situations. However they rely on the natural basis of human 
life. The third international theory is the basis of the revolution which has been 
brought about by this theory. I believe that this theory will be that of the future 
throughout the world and will be put into practice by future generations. The concept 
of enabling the people to possess authority, wealth and arms will impose itself in the 
future, for no one has the right to decide these issues but the masses themselves." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi went on, "Defending the homeland is the responsibility of all the 
masses. When these masses are able to carry arms, the regular army will be dissolved 
leaving the people in charge of defending themselves and their land in an organized way 
while military cadres from the masses will remain to train these masses," 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out, "Without people's congresses there could be no real 
people's democracy."

In the Jamahiriyah, he added, the people are the sole decisionmakers following the im- 
plementation of people's democracy through basic people's congresses and are the only ones 
to implement resolutions through people's committees. All these draw up a plan for the 
country's overall policy which they draft for a period of one year during the General 
People's Congress. Then the pian is carried out by people's committees under the 
supervision of people's congresses. 


The secretaries of qualitative people's committees in the municipalities are members of 
specialized general people's committee such as education, electricity, and transport at 
the Jamahiriyah level. The secretary and members of these people's committees are re- 
sponsible before the general people's committee in addition to qualitative people's 
committees being responsbile before their people's congress at the municipal level. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: Countries without people's congresses are countries without 
democracy where the people is absent and a party, a class, tribe, a council or sect is in 
power. This proves the falseness of a democracy founded on these bases. Masses all over 
the world will inevitably take over power as they are eager to do so and are struggling 

for this aim. Concerning his role as a leader of the revolution, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said 
that his role and that of his colleagues in revolutionary committees spread everywhere 
consists of establishing the people's authority and that of people's congresses. He 
explained that the world which still lives according to a traditional outlook believes 

that there is a ruling regime in the Jamahiriyah and cannot conceive of a people ruling 
itself by itself. 


He added, "I do not lead authority and I cannot pass laws or resolutions, but I work to 
enable the people to issue them." He said, "My role concentrates on affirming people's 
authority as well as the role of the revolutionary committees, inciting the masses to 
practise authority and to guide them to take sound resolutions which meet their interests 
and needs." 


He pointed out that the existing traditional ruling tools in the world at present are a 
provisional status which will be eliminated in the future by the peoples who will rule 
themselves by themselves. 


In respect of preparation of people's military cadres within the framework of implementing 
the dictum of the armed people, the leader of the Great First of September Revolution 
pointed out that experiments have started to prepare reserve pilots who train to fly 
military planes in addition to their other normal tasks. He said, "We called for reserve 
pilots whom we trained two years ago and they flew their planes successfully." He affirmed 
that all these specialities will be run in the future by the people. An example for this 
is the purer stations [as received] the radio and television stations, early warning 
system, antiaircraft resistance, missiles and others. 


On the revolution of workers and their transformation from wage workers into partners, 
the leader of the Great First of September Revolution said, "We have made a big leap in 
different fields of progress," pointing to the increase of industrial production after 
the control of workers over factories and the end of governmental administration and 
private sector management. He said that progress will be the inevitable result of the 
implementation of the dictum "partners and not wage workers" because the masses are 
building up themselves and their progress.

On the relation of Islam with technical progress, the leader of the Great First of 
September Revolution indicated that Islam is a progressive revolution in the history of 
humanity. 


He said that the West has achieved industrial progress and we need to import technology 
to achieve technological progress, 


On the loss of some nations of their originality and personality because of their use of 
Western technology, the leader of the Great First of September Revolution affirmed that 
this is a social deviation and problem for which the third universal theory and especially 
the third part of the Green Book will present radical solution and will present the true 
example on how to use Western technology while retaining originality and social values 

of the society. 


AT-TURAYKI RETURNS FROM LAGOS, ATTACKS FRENCH ROLE IN CHAD 

1D291052 Tripoli JANA in English 0925 GMT 29 May 79 ID 

[Test] Tripoli, May 29 JAMAHIRIYAN NEWS AGENCY--The Jamahiriyan foreign secretary ree 
turned to Tripoli yesterday after participating in the national reconciliation confer- 
ence on Chad held lately in Lagos, the capital of Nigeria. 

The Jamahiriyah, Niger, Nigeria, Central Africa, Cameroon, Sudan and all Chadian parties 
except the fascist gang that is ruling Chad at present had participated in the confer- 


ence, 


The foreign secretary stated to our correspondent that the reconciliation conference 
adopted several resolutions including the illegality of the so-called N'djamena present 


government, the existance of which is considered a violation of Kano conference resolu- 
tion, The participants also held Habre and Goukouni responsible for the failure of the 
national reconcilation in Chad, 


The foreign secretary pointed out that the conference had discussed French intervention 
in Chad and support for the Habre and Goukouni fascist gangs. The participants unani- 
mously agreed that the presence of French forces is the main reason for the failure of 
the national reconciliation in Chad, 


He also pointed out that Lagos conference assigned Nigeria to withdraw her forces from 
Chad because of the provocative acts carried out by Habre and Goukouni gangs against 
the African-Nigerian peace-keeping forces, 


The foreign secretary stated that the Chadian parties which cid not attend the Kano 
conference had actually signed the resolutions of the conference during their attendance 
of the Lagos conference. They formed a national unity and a national world charter 
which the conference recognized, 


Thos parties, he added, have condemned France for its ridiculous intervention in the 
internal affairs of Chad and for its support of the fascist Habre and Goukouni gang. 
He said: "The conference has shown a great awareness of African responsibility and has 
urged Africans to solve their on problems." He asserted that if the efforts of the 
African states have not been fruitful in achieving peace, this is due to French inter- 
vention in Chad and their support of the fascist Habre and Goukouni gang, which the 
members of the Lagos conference unanimously agreed to be the central cause for this 
failure,

At the conclusion of his statement, the foreign secretary declared that the conference 
has given the fascists in Ndjamena time, until June 2, to dissolve themselves and to 
agree with the Chadian parties to find a solution for Chad. 


He said: "Granting this opportunity is a sign of the eagerness of the participating 
African countries in the conference to conclude a real permanent national reconciliation 
in Chad," 


JANA CONDEMNS U,S. DECISION NOT TO DELIVER PLANES 
1D302002 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1900 GMT 30 May 79 ID 


[Text] Tripoli--The United States has suspended the process of handing over the Boeing 
aircraft which the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah had contracted to purchase 
for its civilian airfleet. 


Commenting on this action by the United States, the political editor of JANA described 

it as childish and absurd behavior, The editor stressed that this behavior is bound to 
brand the American administration as foolish, We said that by such behavior the American 
administration is overlooking the fact that civil aircraft are sold and bought and that 
the states can buy what civil aircraft they require of various sizes and types. 


The political editor of JANA warns the American administration that if it persists in 
ignoring its commitments, the policy of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahir iyah 

is clear and known: It is based on the principle of: we befriend whoever befriends us and 
we are hostile to anyone who is hostile to us,
