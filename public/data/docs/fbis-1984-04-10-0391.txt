LIBYA

Bulgarian Defense Minister Dzhurov Visits Tripoli 


Welcomed by Brig Jabir 
LD071903 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1815 GMT 7 Apr 84 


{Text} Tripoli, 7 Apr (JANA) — Army General Dobri Dzhurov, 
member of the Bulgarian Communist Party Central Committee 
Politburo and Bulgarian minister of defense, along with his 
accompanying delegation arrived in Tripoli this evening on a visit 
to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the 
Libyan Arab Armed Forces, welcomed the Bulgarian guest and 
his delegation at Tripoli International Airport. 


Also taking part in the welcoming ceremony were some Libyan 
Arab Armed Forces officers, the Bulgarian ambassador to Libya, 
and members from the Bulgarian Embassy staff. 


Army General Dobri Dzhurov made a statement to the JANA 
reporter in which he expressed his pride and the pride of his 
delegation at visiting the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jama- 
hiriyah. He also explained that the visit is part of continuous 
contacts and consultations for the sake of strengthening and 
developing relations between the two friendly countries in all 
fields.
