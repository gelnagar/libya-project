7 December 1981 

MEMORANDUM FOR: Director, NESA/NFAC 
FROM: Chief, Interdepartmental Affairs Staff, OPP 
SUBJECT: Additional Material on Libya for DCI 

1. This note confirms our phone conversation of a while ago regarding 
additional material requested by the DCI for his NSC deliberations on Libya. 
He would like the following points amplified from the talking points provided 
him this morning: 

a. Would it be best to issue a private demarche first before taking 
steps to get Americans out or the reverse? 

b. He would like an intelligence assessment on steps Qadhafi 
would likely take to impede the exit of Americans following a private 
demarche as well as before a private demarche. Would there be any 
difference? 

c. What steps would he likely take in any event and what would 
be the considerations he would weigh? 

2. As I mentioned to you, the NSC reconvenes tomorrow morning at 1015. 
Please shoot for having the material to me by 0900 tomorrow. 

3. I have advised Charlie Waterman, who will be available to review 
your responses. Good luck: 

cc: NIO/NESA
