FOREIGN SECRETARIAT ISSUES STATEMENT ON TUNISIAN CHARGES 


LDO11000 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 1 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--Th2 Foreign Secretariat has issued a statement about the official 
statements made by Tunisian Government officials with regard to the events taking place 
in Tunisia, Here is the text of the statement of the Foreign Secretariat: 


The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah was surprised by the cfficial statements 
made by the Tunisian Government officials, particularly the statement by the prime 
minister, Hedi Nouira, on Wednesday 40 January 1980, which contained a flagrant attack 
on the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and its revolutionary leadership 
seeking to involve it in the events now taking place in Tunisia, 


The Foreign Secretariat, while condemning these attacks and the tendentious information 
campaigns, emphasizes the following: 


1. The Sosialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has no direct or indirect connection 
with the events which are taking place in the town of Qafsah, and the attempts by the 
Tunisian Government to blame the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah for these 
events are aimed at preoccupying the Tunisian public with its interna’. problems and 
distracting it from learning the real motives which have led in the past, and still 
lead, to events and popular uprisings, 


The Tunisian regime has been accustomed, in an attempt to avoid the truth, to blame 
the Libyan people for any popular uprising or popular discontent, when the Livyan 
people are innocent and have nothing to do with such uprisings, If they are not 
innocent, their only guilt is the eruption of a historic popular revo.ution, and if by 
Libyan interference the Tunisian officials mean the transfer of the ideas of the 
revolution to the Tunisian masses, then the Libyan people are not responsible for this, 
Moreover, neither the Libyan people nor the Tunisian authorities can prevent human 
ideas from being transferred because ideas cannot be suppressed or impriscned., The 
Great 1 September revolution gives out its own ideological radiations which influence 
the people's movement. The closure of the Libyan Cultural Center in Tunis is the best 
example of this, 


2. The Tunisian prime minister has announced a number of measures by the Tunisian 
Government harming the fraternal relations existing between the two fraternai peoples, 
While regretting such actions, the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah Foreign 
Secretariat lays the responsibility with the Tunisian Government alone for the creation 
of division and discord between the two fraternal peoples and for destroying their 
interests, We cannot but warn the two fraternal peoples to be vigilant and not to 

give any opportunity to their enemies,

3. The Tunisian prime minister was not content to implicate the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in the Qafsah events, but he pointed to the possibility of in- 
volving it in wnat could happen in Tunisia in the future with regard to popular up- 
risings when he forecast in his statement that there is a premeditated intention of 
leveling charges at the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah whenever the Tunisian 
regime is subjected to a popular uprising. This in itself exposes the falseness of the 
claims made by the Tunisian prime minister and the plan he is drawing up to harm the 
relations between the two fraternal peoples and undermine the fraternal relations 
between them, 


4. The formal charges made by the Tunisian regime pointed to the seizure of Libyan 
passports in the possession of certain elements. The Foreign Secretariat wishes on 
this occasion to explain that the Tunisian Consulate in Tripoli is in the habit of 
using tne methods of hiding and smuggling the passports of Libyan Arab nationals who 
apply for entry visas to Tunisia for the purpose of spreading such charges,

Libyan passports, the first, No 371831 issued on 31 July 1977, and the second, 

No 163494 issued on 13 February 1974+, were seized from a member of the Tunisian 
Commissioner's Office, Muhammad as-Sadigq, who was driving a car with diplomatic plate 

No 13/57. The two Tunisian officials were on their way to Tunisia when the above-

fabrications whether with regard to any Libyan collaboration in the popular uprising 
in Qafsah or any other material evidence proving that the Libyan people were involved 
in the uprising, 


5. The reference to "the friends" from whom the Tunisian prime minister said he is 
seeking assistance confirms that the Tunisian Government has the intention of resorting 
to the foreign imperialist forces with the aim of threatening the interests of the two 
fraternal peoples and of exposing the peace and security of the region to danger and 
foreign intervention, This is rejected by the two fraternal peoples who are linked with 
a common striggle against the imperialist enemy, not forgetting their fraternal and 
neighborly relations and common destiny. Moreover the seeking of the assistance of the 
French air and naval forces constitutes an affront to the fraternal Tunisian people, 


6. While reiterating its profound regret at the fabrications and allegations ef the 
Tunisian officials, the Foreign Secretariat affirms its concern for the fraternal re- 
lations and the deep ties and the nobility of the joint national aspirations of the 

two fraternal peoples and points out that there is still an opportunity for the Tunisian 
regime to reconsider its stand and not to be drawn into a scheme that serves no one but 
the enemies of the two peoples, and to refrain from dragging a foreign force into the 
region,

[Text] Paris, January 31, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The French daily L! IMPRESSION 
reports, quoting a correspondent who was able to reach the Tunisian city of Qafsah, 

that shooting is still heard in the city where curfew is still enforced, 

According to L'IMPRESSION, many sources confirmed that the people of Qafsah encouraged 
the insurgents, On the same subject LE MATIN today described the situation in Qafsah 

as still very tense with the Tunisian authorities cordoning off the city and stopping 
the traffic of citizens in and out, 

The daily's reporter quoted an officer in the Tunisian Army whose name was not disclosed 
as saying that the army was now facing a difficult task due to the cooperation of Qafsah 


citizens with the insurgents, 


Helicopters are circling over the city's suburbs while armoured vehicles are centered 
in the city and additional reinforcement was brought in, LE MATIN also reported,
