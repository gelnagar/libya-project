LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI MEETS WITH OFFICERS IN TOBRUK 


NC301024 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 30 Mar 83 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution [Al-Qadhdhafi] met with officers of the 

Arab Libyan Armed Forces in Tobruk and exchanged good wishes with them on the 13th 
anniversary of the ousting of the British colonialist forces, which marks a great historic 
action and a splendid national victory scored by the will of the free people through 

the Great 1 September Revolution and its leader Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadtdhafi. 


During the meeting, which was attended by Brigadier General Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, the 
commander in chief of the Armed Forces, the leader spoke to the officers about several 
important questions in the Arab and the international arenas and the effective 
revolutionary resistance by the Great 1 September Revolution against the savage 
imperialist-Zionist onslaught. 


He also spoke about the revolutionary march which set out from the struggle trench in 
the Jamahiriyah to confront all plots and terrorist provocations. He affirmed that the 
Libyan Arab people, who possess the authority, resources, and arms, are the master 
people and a free people who cannot be coerced or defeated. 


In their meeting with the leader, the officers affirmed their adherence to the 
revolutionary principles and ideas laid down by revolutionary thinker Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi and their heroic adherence to the authority of the people who have the 
sole authority. They affirmed their readiness to carry out the order of death in 
defending every grain of sand in the Libyan Arab territory -- the staunch revolutionary 
territory of all free men and strugglers for freedom and emancipation. They also 
affirmed their readiness to offer their lives to resist the Zionist death wagon and to 
repel all aggressors and plotters against the Arab nation. 


The brother leader attended a reception given by the officers in Tobruk to mark the 13th 
anniversary of ousting the colonialist bases. This historical event carries deep signi- 
ficance in affirming the courageous vanguard role undertaken by the brother leader of 
the revolution to consolidate the Libyan Arab people's freedom and to constantly urge the 
masses" action in rejecting slavery, domination, coercion, defeat, and surrender. 


FOREIGN LIAISON BUREAU CONDEMNS U.S. ON GRENADA 


LD260934 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 26 Mar 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 26 Mar (JANA) -- The People's Committee of the People's Foreign Liaison 
Bureau has condemned the terrorist provocations and interferences carried out by 
Reagan's administration against the people and the revolution of Grenada. It stresses 
that the Libyan Arab peoples stand with and support the people of Grenada and all 
friendly people in Latin America. 


V. 31 Mar 83 Q4 NORTH AFRICA 


The People's Committee of the People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau has issued a statement 
regarding the terrorist provocations against the people of Grenada which says: 


The foreign minister of Grenada summoned the heads of the political missions accredited 
to his country, including the brother secretary of the People's Committee of the 
People's Bureau in Grenada, and informed them of the aggressive American threats 
against his country that began in December 1982 when Reagan's vice president launched 
an attack against Grenada during a press conference in Miami, which was followed by 

a speech by the U.S. President on 10 March in which he claimed that Grenada constituted 
a threat to the security and safety of his country. 


The foreign minister of Grenada went on to say that the United States intended to 
take military action against his country, especially after recent developments in 
Nicaragua and the naval exercises that took place in [words indistinct]. 


The People's Foreign Liaison Bureau, informing the General People's Congress of these 
blatant American threats against the defenseless people of Grenada who refuse to 
submit to American domination, expresses its profound anxiety regarding these 
American threats and strongly condemns and denounces the aggressive course that has 
become the clear distinctive mark of the reactionary American Administration, which 
basically is built on aggression against every people that rejects American pomposity, 
arrogance, and hegemony. 


The People's Foreign Liaison Bureau recalls the continuing aggressions and provoca- 
tions carried out and being carried out by American imperialism against the Libyan 
Arab people, which included the attack against Libyan aircraft in our territorial 
waters in the Gulf of Sidra during August 1981, the violation of our territorial 
waters and airspace in February 1983, imposing an economic blockade against the 
Jamahiriyah, and the U.S. President's personal applications of racial discrimination 
against the Libyan Arab students studying in the United States, preventing them 

from continuing their studies there without any justification, attempting to sow 
sedition in the Arab region by inciting the brothers to fight among themselves, 

and other similar hostile actions. 


Added to this are the American imperialist reactionary acts against the people of 
Nicaragua and its revolutionary leadership, its continuous encouragement of 
mercenaries, arming and training them to commit aggression against Nicaragua, and 
also its recent muscle-flexing provocations in the "Team Spirit" exercises close 
to the borders of North Korea. 


All the hostile American actions deserve strong condemnation by the international 
community and the marshalling of the efforts of all progressive forces in the world 


to deter American arrogance. 


APPOINTMENT OF U.S. AMBASSADOR TO CHAD ATTACKED 


NC311224 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1100 GMT 31 Mar 83 


[Text] An official U.S. statement says that U.S. President Reagan yesterday appointed 
lay P. Moffat as U.S. ambassador in Ndjamena. Moffat has been helping Hissein Habre 
ind his clique since January 1982 to kill the Chadians who reject Habre. The appoint- 
ment of Moffat as U.S. ambassador in Ndjamena comes within the framework of plans 
engineered by the Reagan administration to strangle the Chadian people and shake 
security and stability in the area. At the same time, this reveals the Reagan 
administration's aggressive intentions to fan the hysteria of provocation against 

the Libyan Arab people.

SOVIET GROUPS GREET AL-QADHDHAFI ON ANNIVERSARY 


LD281136 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0915 GMT 28 Mar 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 28 Mar (JANA) -- The Union of Soviet Societies for Friendship and 
Cultural Relations with Foreign Countries, the Society for Friendship and Cultural Ties 
with Arab Countries, and the USSR-Libya Friendship Society have sent congratulations to 
the brother leader of the revolution on the anniversary of the removal of British bases 
from Libyan Arab soil. 


In their cables, the Soviet societies denounce the aggressive provocations of American 
imperialism against the people of the Jamahiriyah, and affirmed their full solidarity 

with the Arab Libyan people in their struggle against these aggressive designs. They 

also emphasized their intention to continue working for the development of cooperation 
between the Jamahiriyah's people and the Soviet people. 


The Soviet friendship societies also expressed their concern over the dangerous situa- 

tion in the Arab arena due to the aggressive, expansionist Zionist intentions supported 

by American imperialism, and demanded the withdrawal of the Zionist forces and the 

achievement of the legitimate rights of the Palestinian people to self-determination.
