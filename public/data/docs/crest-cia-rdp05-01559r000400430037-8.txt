Summary (AI): An FBIS London transcript (6 September 1988) of an Algiers TV interview with Qadhafi, in which he discusses the Polisario and Western Sahara, Maghreb and Arab unity, the EEC, and the Berbers. It has no specific places, sites, or events in or near Tripoli.
• Qadhafi says the Polisario was supported initially as a revolutionary movement but its independence demand for a 21st small state is not encouraging to Libya.
• Qadhafi says the EEC may become a single state by 1992 and that this has pushed the Arabs toward greater unity.
• Qadhafi argues the Berbers are pre-Islam Arabs and should not be considered a minority.

((EXCERPTS)) ((AL-QADHDHAFI CONTINUES)) IN FACT, THE 
BLOODLETTING AND INFIGHTING AMONG ARABS IS AN EMBARRASSMENT 
BEFORE THE WORLD AND IS USELESS. WE HAVE NOT EVEN SEEN 
SERIOUS ORIENTATIONS FOR WHICH EVERYONE WOULD MAKE 
SACRIFICES. IF THE POLISARIO WAS A REVOLUTION AIMED AT 
CHANGING THE BORDERS, THE ECONOMIC SYSTEM, AND THE POLITICAL 
SYSTEM IN THE ENTIRE REGION AND AT MOBILIZING THE POWERS IN 
THE REGION TO DIRECT THEM TOWARD PALESTINE OR AT ACHIEVING 
ARAB UNITY, FAIR ENOUGH; IT WOULD HAVE HAD THE SUPPORT OF 
EVEN THE MOROCCAN, LIBYAN, AND ALGERIAN YOUTH AND IT WOULD 
HAVE BECOME WIDER. 

WE SUPPORTED THE POLISARIO IN THE BEGINNING BECAUSE IT 
WAS A REVOLUTIONARY EXPLOSION BUT WHEN IT BECAME A MOVEMENT 
FOR THE INDEPENDENCE OF TENS OF THOUSANDS OF PEOPLE IN ORDER 
TO ADD A NEW MINI-ARAB STATE, THIS DEMAND IS NOT ENCOURAGING 
FOR US NOR FOR THE ENTIRE NATION. WITH 20 STATES, THERE IS 
NO NEED TO ADD A 21ST STATE, ANOTHER MINI-STATE. WE ARE 
PROTESTING AGAINST THE EXISTENCE OF ALL THESE STATELETS, 
WHICH SHOULD BE ONE SINGLE STATE. SO HOW CAN WE ACCEPT THE 
ADDITION OF ANOTHER SMALL STATE? 

HAVING SAID THAT, THE INHABITANTS OF SAQIA AL-HAMRA AND 
RIO DE ORO ARE HUMAN BEINGS AND THEY ARE A PEOPLE WHOSE WILL 
MUST BE RESPECTED. WE MUST NOT INVADE THEM OR ATTACK THEM 
AS WE WOULD ATTACK A FLOCK OF SHEEP OR WILD ANIMALS. THIS 
IS THE REASON WHY WE IN FACT SUPPORTED THE STANDS OF THE 
POLISARIO ((WORDS INDISTINCT)) BECAUSE THE WILL OF THIS 
PEOPLE MUST BE RESPECTED, REGARDLESS OF WHETHER IT IS BIG OR 

SMALL. THE HUMAN WILL AND THE ASPIRATIONS ARE NOT MEASURED 

IN NUMBERS; THESE THINGS ARE MORAL AND NOT MATERIAL. THE 
SAHRAWI PEOPLE MUST HAVE THE RIGHT FOR SELF-DETERMINATION 
AND IN THIS WE AGREE WITH THE ALGERIAN LEADERSHIP AND WE 
AGREE ON THE NEED FOR THE SAHRAWI PEOPLE TO HAVE SELF- 
DETERMINATION. I BELIEVE THAT THE CURRENT MEASURES ARE 
AIMED AT GIVING THE SAHRAWI PEOPLE THE OPPORTUNITY TO DECIDE 

' ITS FUTURE WITH A YES OR A NO. THIS IS THE RIGHT THING TO 

DO. ALL THE EFFORTS MUST BE NOW MADE TO ENSURE THE SUCCESS 
OF THE REFERENDUM, REGARDLESS OF WHETHER THEY SAY YES OR NO. 
THIS IS THE RIGHT THING TO DO IN FACT. 

WITH REGARD TO THE MAGHREBIAN TRENDS, WE ARE INDEED VERY 
GLAD, BECAUSE THE ARABS' AWARENESS HAS REACHED A STAGE WHERE 
THEY KNOW THAT UNITY IS A NECESSITY. WE HAVE BEEN RAISING 
THIS SLOGAN FOR THE PAST 20 YEARS, AND WE HAVE BEEN SAYING 
THATUNITY WAS NECESSARY AND THAT THESE MINI-STATES COULD 
NOT SURVIVE HENCEFORTH. THE WORLD HAS NOW TURNED INTO GREAT 
BLOCS. LUCKILY A GREAT POLITICAL, ECONOMIC, AND MILITARY 
BLOC HAS EMERGED BESIDE US -- NAMELY THE EEC THIS BLOC MIGHT 
BECOME A SINGLE STATE IN 1992. THIS HAS PROVOKED US AND HAS 
BROUGHT HOME TO THE ARABS THAT THE WORLD HAD INDEED TURNED 
INTO GREAT BLOCS. ((PASSAGE OMITTED REITERATING THEME, ) ) 

IF THE UNITY OF THE MAGHREB IS NOT A STEP TOWARD ARAB 
UNITY, IT WIL BE INCOMPLETE, AND IT MIGHT EVEN HARM THE ARAB

UNITY AS A NATION, BECAUSE THE COLONIALISTS ARE TRYING TO 
_PREVENT THE ARAB NATION FROM BEING THE SAME NATION. FOR 
TIME THE COLONIALISTS HAD BEEN TALKING ABOUT THE UNITY OF 
THE FERTILE CRESCENT, THE UNITY OF THE NILE VALLEY, THE 
UNITY OF THE MAGHREB IN ORDER TO INCITE THE POPULISTS WHO 
DID NOT BELIEVE THAT THEY WERE ARABS. THEY HOPED TO SEE A 
POPULIST MOVEMENT SET UP A NON-ARAB STATE IN NORTH AFRICA, 
ANOTHER NON-ARAB STATE IN THE NILE VALLEY, AND A THIRD STATE 
IN THE FERTILE CRESCENT. THE OBJECTIVE OF ALL THESE UNITIES 
WAS TO SEE THE POPULIST NON-ARAB DEFEAT THE ARAB. OBVIOUSLY 
THERE IS A BIG FALLACY WITH REGARD TO THE BERBERS IN THE 
ARAB HOMELAND; THEY ARE ARABS, ESPECIALLY IN NORTH AFRICA. 
WE DO NOT CONSIDER THEM A MINORITY. I DO NOT KNOW HOW THIS 
TOPIC IS DEALT WITH IN ALGERIA. WE DO NOT CONSIDER THE 
BERBERS A MINORITY. THE MINORITY IS THE ONE THAT DOES NOT 
BELONG TO THIS NATION; IT IS A MINORITY OF A DIFFERENT RACE, 
SUCH AS THE KURDS OR THE ARMENIANS -- THEY ARE MINORITIES. 
AS FOR THE BERBERS, THEY ARE PRE-ISLAM ARABS, CONSEQUENTLY 
WE CANNOT CONSIDER THEM A MINORITY AT ALL. THE HISTORICAL 
EVIDENCE AND TESTIMONIES CONFIRM THE BERBERS’ ARABISM.
