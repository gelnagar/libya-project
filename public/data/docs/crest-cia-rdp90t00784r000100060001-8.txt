Summary (AI): A December 1982 NPIC briefing note reports that four Libyan Foxbat interceptors were armed with AA-6 air-to-air missiles at Sebha Airfield, with 11 of 14 Foxbats there part of an original deployment. Sebha is in central Libya, about 510 nautical miles southeast of Tripoli, so this is not a Tripoli site.
• Four Foxbat aircraft armed with AA-6 air-to-air missiles; Libyan Foxbats are normally not observed armed.
• The 11 Foxbats present are part of an original deployment of 14 first observed at the airfield.
• An RSBN-4N short-range navigational system was operationally deployed just south of the northwest/southeast runway.
• Coordinates given as 26-59-36N 014-28-01E.
• 1982-12: Four Libyan Foxbat interceptors observed armed with AA-6 air-to-air missiles; three on the south parking apron, one on a hangar apron at the east end; seven others dispersed in parking areas.
