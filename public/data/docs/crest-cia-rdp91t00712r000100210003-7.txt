Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing note on the Libya-Chad conflict, focused on N'Djamena airfield in Chad. It describes Libyan and dissident forces holding northern Chad and French and government forces holding the south, with French combat aircraft arriving at N'Djamena. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• Libyan/dissident forces control northern Chad; status of central Chad undetermined; French/government forces control southern Chad.
• French aircraft at N'Djamena include Mirage F.1 and Jaguar fighters, a C-135F, and Transall C-160 transports, some armed with Matra Magic AAMs.
• A newly deployed mobile Crotale SAM battery supports the French force at N'Djamena.
• No Libyan/dissident forces were detected south of Faya Largeau; a small mechanized infantry unit on heavy-lift transporters left the area.
• Coordinates given: 12-08-05N 015-02-06E (N'Djamena area).
• 1983-08: French air contingent of four Mirage F.1 fighters, six Jaguar strike fighters and a C-135F tanker arrived at N'Djamena airfield; six Transall C-160 transports also arrived or were present.
