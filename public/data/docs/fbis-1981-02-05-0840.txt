LIBYA

POLAND'S JAGIELSKI VISITS, HOLDS TALKS, DEPARTS 


Committee Meetings 
LD041616 Tripoli JANA in English 1514 GMT 4 Feb 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 4 Feb (JANA)--The Libyan-Polish joint committee this morning held a meeting 
at the Secretariat of the People's Committee for Land Reclamation and Agrarian Reform. 


Mr Bashir Jawda, the secretary of the People's Committee for Land Reclamation and Agrarian 
Reform, attended the meeting on the Libyan side, and Dr Mieczyslaw Jagielski, deputy prime 
minister and member of the Politburo of the Polish United Workers’ Party, on the Polish 
side. The meeting reviewed the resolutions of the 3rd session of the Joint Committee for 
Economic, Scientific and Technical Cooperation held in Warsaw on 12th of July 1980. Both 
sides expressed their satisfaction with the positive results they reached which are con- 
sidered a concrete base for promoting and developing this cooperation. 


Jallud Meeting 


LD050940 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 5 Feb 81 


{Text] Tripoli, 5 Feb (JANA)--Yesterday morning Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud received 
the deputy chairman of the Polish council of state and member of the PZPR political bureau, 
M. Jagielski. Present at the meeting was the secretary of the General People's Committee 
for Agrarian Reform and Land Reclamation. 


4 Feb Departure 
LD050944 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 5 Feb 81 


{Text] Tripoli, 4 Feb (JANA)--The deputy chairman of the Polish council of state and 
member of the PZPR political bureau, M. Jagielski, left Tripoli last night at the end of 
his visit to the Jamahiriyah during which he attended the meetings of the joint Libyan- 
Polish committee. Yesterday evening the secretary of the General People's Committee for 
Agrarian Reform and Land Reclamation and the deputy chairman of the Polish Council of 

State signed the minutes of the third session of the joint Libyan-Polish committee for 
cooperation in the economic, scientific, technical, agricultural, building and construction 
fields.
