LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Leaders of ‘New Fatah’ 


LD270052 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arabic Homeland in Arabic 
0015 GMT 27 Jan 84 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution tonight received 
Brother Abu Salih, Brother Qadri, Brother Khalid al-‘Amlah, 
Brother Abu Musa, and Brother Abu ‘Ali Mahdi. They are 
members of the revolutionary leadership of the new Fatah move- 
ment. A working session was held and attended by Brother Talal 
Naji, member of the PLO Executive Committee and assistant 
secretary general of the Popular Front for the Liberation of 
Palestine — General Command. 


JANA Rejects French Criticism on Chad 
LD271055 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 27 Jan 84 


[Text] Paris, 27 Jan (JANA) A spokesman for the French 
Ministry of Foreign Affairs has made a statement about the 
events of 2 days ago in Chad by the forces of the legitimate 
National Unity Government headed by Goukouni Oueddei 
which led to a number of French aircraft being shot down. The 
spokesman claimed that Libya is possibly responsible for the 
events 


It is certain here that the French Government more than anyone 
else realizes well that what is happening in Chad is an internal 
struggle concerning the Chadian peopic themselves and that 
Libya has continually stressed and again stresses to the world 
that there are no Libyan forces in Chad. Therefore, it refuses to 
be accused of something it has not committed. 


Moreover, the French Government realizes that the forces of the 
legitimate National Unity Government in Chad were responsible 
for challenging the French air raid against their position and no 
one has the right to prevent them from doing so. At this point we 
must ask: Whatare French Jaguar aircraft doing in Chad? Are 
they defending French territory or are they there for another 
purpose? 


The case of Chad concerns the people of Chad themselves, and 
neither France nor anyone else should concern itself with Chad's 
internal affairs, nor should they settle matters among themselves 
or be partial to one side. In the final analysis it is the affair of 
Africa and the concern of the OAU. 


It is worth mentioning here that when the Jamahirijah sent its 
forces to Chad in 1980 it did so at the request of the legitimate 
government, and when they withdrew it was also at the request 
of the legitimate government in Chad.
