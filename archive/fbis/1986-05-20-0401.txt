LIBYA

JANA Political Editor on U.S.-Saudi Arms Sale ‘Farce’ 
LD221737 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 
1600 GMT 22 May 86 


[JANA political editor commentary] 


[Text] The Jew George Shultz, in a meeting with Republican 
members of the U.S. Senate, announced that the sale of Stinger 
missiles to Saudi Arabia has been halted at the initiative of the 
Saudi Government. This move came as a response to the orders 
of the Zionist lobby, which objected to the completion of this sale. 


Commenting on the subservience and capitulation of the Saudi 
Government when confronted by the conditions of the Zionist 
lobby, the JANA political editor said: 


This staged battle between Saudi Arabia and the Zionist lobby 
has become a two-act farce. The first act was played by the Saudi 
rulers when their impotence and subservience to the conditions 
of the Zionist lobby, which is headed by the Jew Shultz, were 
revealed. 


The second act was played by the Zionist Mosad’s mad dog 
Reagan, when he announced to a gathering of the Zionist lobby 
that the Saudi Government has officially informed him that it is 
abandoning this sale. However, Reagan returned to play an 
additional act in the same farce when he used the veto after he 
was satisfied that the situation was suitable for the demands of 
the Zionists, on the one hand, and to the (?members) of his 
administration, on the other hand. 


The Zionist Mosad’s mad dog Reagan based his approval of the 
sale of Stinger missiles on Saudi Arabia's approval of the failure 
of the emergency Arab summit which was called for in the wake 
of the failed crusading aggression against the people of the Great 
Jamahiriyah. 


The JANA political editor stresses that while the Saudis have 
remained silent about the repeated U.S. accusations over the past 
2 weeks, the Zionist lobby has continued to issue orders and 
instructions which are immediately accepted by the Saudi rulers. 


JANA Reports Teaching of Russian Instead of English 
LD221059 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0739 GMT 22 May 86 


[Text] Tripoli, 22 May (JANA)— JANA has learned that Libya 
has ended the teaching of the English language at all educational 
Stages and has begun teaching the Russian language instead.
