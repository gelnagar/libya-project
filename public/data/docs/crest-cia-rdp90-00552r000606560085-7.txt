Summary (AI): A Washington Post column (13 October 1981) reports that Qaddafi allegedly vowed to have President Reagan assassinated after US jets downed two Libyan jets, and describes Libyan-linked killings of anti-Qaddafi exiles. The CIA denies planning any assassination of Qaddafi.
• Wilson made a secret visit to Rome in July 1981 to meet US officials, reportedly about the criminal charges against him.
• A telephone call by Qaddafi to Mengistu, intercepted by the National Security Agency, reportedly discussed assassinating Reagan.
• A Libyan group called 'Free Unionist Officers' publicly vowed revenge on US interests after the jet shootdown.
• The CIA has denied planning or discussing an assassination attempt on Qaddafi and says a standing executive order prohibits it from engaging in assassination.
• 1981-08: US fliers shot down two Libyan jets in the Mediterranean; Qaddafi reportedly vowed to go ahead with plans to have Reagan assassinated.
• 1980-04: Qaddafi warned Libyan expatriates to come home or suffer 'physical elimination'; at least 10 anti-Qaddafi exiles were reportedly murdered in Italy, West Germany, Britain, Greece and the United States since then. (10 killed)
