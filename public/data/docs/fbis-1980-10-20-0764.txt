LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI URGES JIHAD TO LIBERATE MECCA 


LD191428 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 19 Oct 80 


[Excerpts] The brother leader this morning led the prayers of the holy ‘Id in the Zawilah 
region. The leader delivered the sermon of the ‘Id prayers in which he said: We are per- 


forming the ‘Id prayers in the Zawilah village which was conquered by a companion of the 
prophet of God, namely, ‘Amr ibd al-'‘As. 


The leader said that this Islamic gathering coincided with the sacrifice day and came at 
a time when the holy Ka'ba was moaning under American occupation; and yesterday, he said, 
the pilgrims’ prayers to God on Mount ‘Arafat mixed with the buzzing of American planes. 
He stressed that this never happened before and that it did away with any meaning of 
pilgrimage this year. 


The leader said: Let Muslims everywhere know that Mecca, the holy Ka'ba, holy Al Madinah, 
the prophet's tomb and the holy Mount ‘Arafat are now under the yoke of American occupation. 
The leader affirmed that the first Islamic duty is to wage a jihad to liberate Mecca. Until 
recently, he said, we were urging Muslims to launch a holy Islamic invasion to liberate 
Jerusalem, but today, and after the Jews have occupied Jerusalem, we are surprised to see 
Christians occupy the sky of Mecca, the sky of holy Al Madinah and the sky of Mount ‘Arafat.

The leader called on the Islamic masses everywhere to take to arms, abandon the quest 
for pleasures of this world, engage in a search for death for the sake of God, and turn 
to rifles, guns and planes. He stressed that the situation is now intolerable because 
the skies of the holy places are occupied and there is no longer an excuse for any true 
Muslim to be dissuaded from the jihad. 


Also in the sermon of the holy ‘Id this morning, the brother leader called for a halt 

in the fight between Iraqi and Iranian Muslims, urging Arabs and non-Arabs jointly under 
the banner of Islam to embark on liberating the Islamic sanctities which have been pro- 
faned and to engage in liberating Jerusalem and Palestine, which have been occupied. 


He said that we feel the crusade wars have not ended since a new crusade has been 
launched by means of American planes and ships and is entrenching itself in Egypt, 
Somalia, Muscat, Oman, Saudi Arabia and Palestine. He stressed that 2 days ago 
crusaders, Jews and Christians managed to inflict heavy casualties on Muslims in south- 
ern Lebanon. 


The leader underlined the need for us here to assume our responsibility and to go from 
this place straight to military training camps and to wage a jihad to secure martyrdom. 


FORMER STUDENT IN U.S. WOUNDED AS '‘SPY' 
LD172104 Tripoli JANA in English 1920 GMT 17 Oct 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 17 October--In affirmation of the fact that physical liquidation is 

the final stage in the revolutionary conflict dialogue which decides it once and for 

all when a separation from economic, political and social weapons fail to put an end to 
the activities of the counterforces, a member of the world revolutionary committee tried 
to liquidate one Faysal az-Zaqla'i, who was seriously wounded. Az-Zaqla'i studied at 
the expense of the [Libyan] community for 10 years at Colorado University in the United 
States to prepare for a legal degree and a doctorate. But instead of returning to serve 
his country and people, he became an agent and a spy for American intelligence, 
supplying it with information about his country.
