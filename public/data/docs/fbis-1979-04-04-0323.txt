LIBYA

SPOKESMAN DENIES PRESENCE OF LIBYAN TROOPS IN UGANDA 
LD031852 Tripoli JANA in Inglish 1712 GMT 3 Apr 79 LD 
[Text] Tripoli, April 3, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--A Foreign Secretariat spokesman in the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah said that Libya did not send any Libyan troops 
to Uganda but sent several Libyan instructors, and supplied Uganda with some of the fuel 
it needs. 
The reason for not supporting Amin, the spokesman added, is that Libya is not sure yet 
whether Uganda is fighting a regular war with Tanzania or Ugandans are fighting each 
other, 
NYERERE CHARGED WITH MASSACRE OF MUSLIMS IN UGANDA 
ID030940 Tripoli JANA in English 0844 GMT 3 Apr 79 LD 
({"Nyerere Continues War of Extermination Against Muslims in Uganda"--JANA headline] 
[Text] Dar es Salaam, April, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--According to news from Dar es Salaam, 
wide-scale arrests are being carried out against Muslims in Tanzania who have expressed 


objections against the horrific massacres of Ugandan Muslims by Nyerere farces. 


Sources indicate that groups of Muslims in the city of Tinja shouted anti-Nyerere slogans 
after last Friday prayers describing him to be an enemy of Islam and Muslims. 


The Muslims in “anzania called on all Muslims in the world to declare "holy jihad", in 
support of the Muslims in Uganda in confronting the crusader's onslaught to which Nyerere's 
regime is exposing them, 


JIBRIL, PFLP DELEGATION ARRIVE IN TRIPOLI 
LD031724 Tripoli JANA in English 1405 GMT 3 Apr 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, April 3, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Ahmad Jibril, secretary-general of the 
Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine--General Command, arrived in Tripoli 
yesterday heading a delegation from the front's Political Bureau on a visit due to last 
for several days in the Jamahiriyah. 


ie was received at Tripoli International Airport by a representative from the Arab Liaison 
Bureau and the Front's representative in the Jamahiriyah. Mr, Jibril said that his visit to 
the Jamahiriyah comes within the framework of consultations between the revolutionary 
progressive forces to confront the capitulatory conspiracy and the hostile pact formed 
between Carter, the leader of imperialism, assassin terrorist Begin and the defeated 
Egyptian president against our Arab nation, 


In a statement to our correspondent, Mr Jibril stressed that Arab progressive forces should 
speed up their action to contain and foil the imperialist attack and prove the ability of 
our masses in steadfastness and confrontation.
