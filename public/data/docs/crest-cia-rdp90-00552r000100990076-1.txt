Summary (AI): A 1980 Washington Star article reports on the upcoming trial of former CIA employee Francis E. Terpil, charged with acting as an unregistered foreign agent for Libya and conspiracy to murder, tied to an alleged plot to kill Libyan defector Umar Muhayshi. The article also discusses a photo linking Terpil to Billy Carter at a Libyan revolution anniversary celebration.
• Terpil and Edwin P. Wilson were indicted in April 1980 in Washington on charges including acting as unregistered foreign agents for Libya, conspiracy to commit murder, and unlawful export of U.S. munitions.
• The federal indictment alleged the conspiracy aimed to supply the government of Libya covertly with personnel, explosives and goods, including to make explosive devices and train others in a terrorist training project.
• Terpil denies being the man seated next to Billy Carter in a Time magazine photo of the reviewing stand at the Libyan revolution anniversary celebration in 1979.
• Terpil was arrested in New York.
• 1980-11-05: Trial date set for Francis E. Terpil in U.S. District Court (Judge John J. Pratt); the trial was later delayed from early October
