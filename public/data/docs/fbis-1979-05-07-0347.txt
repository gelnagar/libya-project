LIBYA

SUDANESE VICE PRESIDENT BAKR ARRIVES, MAKES STATEMENT 
JNO51131 Khartoum SUNA in Arabic 1030 GMT 5 May 79 JN 


(Text ] Tripoli, 5 May--Vice President and Foreign Minister ar-Rashid at-Tahir has con- 
firmed Sudan's firm stand and adherence to the Algiers and Rabat summit resolutions and 
the Arab nation's unanimous objectives of bringing about Israel's total withdrawal from 
all the territories occupied in 1967 and regaining the struggling Palestinian people's 
rights to return to their land and establish their state, as well as our right as Arabs 
and Muslims in Holy Jerusalem. 


In a statement upon his arrival in Tripoli yesterday, At-Tahir added: We closely adhere 
to and struggle for these objectives, which we cannot relinquish. He affirmed that 
despite all the conspiracies and wounds inflicted upon it, our Arab nation is capable 

of healing its wounds, mobilizing its resources and regaining all its rights. 


Vice President and Foreign Minister ar-Rashid at-Tahir announced that Egypt has asked 
Sudan to look after its interests in certain countries. He said that this matter is 
currently under study in Khartoum, 


Referring to his visit to the Libyan Jamahiriyah, At-Tahir said: The visit is part of 

a move that should take place and continue. The fraternal sentiments that link the 
Libyan and Sudanese peoples, and the bonds of blood and religion that have been consoli- 
dated over the ages, make it incumbent upon our generation to utilize its potentials and 
means to promote these bonds and safeguard them in the interests of the two countries 
and the Arab and Islamic nations. 


At-Tahir added that one of the subjects he will discuss during his current visit to the 
Jamahiriyah is the current situation in the African region, which is passing through 
difficult circumstances and is being subjected to conspiracies against its unity, 
security and independence. He will also discuss the convocation of the next Kano 
conference. 

Meeting With Al-Qadhdhafi

(Text ] Tripoli, May 6, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi yester- 
day received the Sudanese vice president and foreign minister, Ar-Rashid at-Tahir Bakr, 
who delivered a message from President Ja'far Numayr, of Sudan. 
The meeting was attended by the foreign, communications and oil secretaries. 


Talks With At-Turayki 


LD051450 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 5 May 79 LD 


(Text ] Libya:u-Sudanese talks began this morning at the Foreign Secretariat. The talks 
are headed by Dr 'Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, Libyan foreign secretary, and Brother 
ar-Rashid at-Tahir Bakr, Sudanese vice president and foreign minister. During this 
meeting bilateral relations and ways to strengthen them in the common interests of the 
fraternal peoples were discussed.

Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, at the beginning of the meeting, welcomed Brother 
ar-Rashid at-Tahir Bakr and the accompanying delegation. He stressed the importance 

of the visit in strengthening relations between the two sister countries. He said: 

This is not the first meeting between the two revolutions. There have been many meetings 
between them. These meetings began with the Great First of September Revolution and the 
25 May Revolution, to bring back the prestige of the Arab nation after the forces of 
tyranny and evil had attacked the existence and the entity of this nation. 


Dr at-Turayki stressed that the coincidence between the Great First of September revo- 
lution is an important historic event for the Arab nation and the African continent. 

He pointed out that the forces of evil which fought the Arab nation were working to see 
that Libyan-Sudanese unity should not continue. He stressed that the treaty of betrayal 
and capitulation signed by the defeated president of the Egyptian regime was a serious 

act against pan-Arab issues and the fate of all Arab people. Dr at-Turayki said: We 
believe that we have a historic duty represented first of all in unifying our efforts, 
because the alliance between the agent Egyptian regime and the Zionist enemy is unnatural. 
It is aimed at the fraternal Egyptian people, who are destined to live this tragedy, and 
at the Arab cause, the just cause of the Palestinian Arab people in particular. 


He said: Joint cooperation between us will bolster the forces of good in the African 
continent--represented by Islam. Dr at-Turayki stressed that the meeting between Libya 
and Sudan is a historic and important one, which plays an important role in Islamic and 
African issues. Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki praised Brother ar-Rashid at-Tahir Bakr 
and said: We appreciate his personal efforts to strengthen bilateral relations and the 
cause of the Arab nation. At the end of his speech the foreign secretary expressed hope 
about consolidating the bonds of cooperation and (?integration) to reach unity between the 
two countries, which is the aspiration of the peoples of Libya and Sudan, 


Brother ar-Rashid at-Tahir Bakr delivered a speech in which he stressed that the Great 
First of September Revolution and the 25 May revolution have a common background because 
they were a direct reaction by our Arab nation to the events in June 1967. He said: 
The Arab nation is capable of marshalling its potential to reply to the colonialist- 
Zionist assault despite the difficult conditions it is going through. He added: This 
meeting is taking place at a time when our nation is being exposed to a new colonial 
assault aimed at our existence and our causes of destiny. He stressed that the Great 
First of September Revolution and the Sudanese 25 May Revolution agree on the time, the 
targets and the struggle for democracy and freedom. He said: We have opened the doors 
wide for the two peoples to meet and to place their potential in the service of common 
causes of destiny. He said: ‘Where have been some difficulties on the path of the two 
revolutions. There were some problems and some coolness, but the two sincere peoples 
have believed that this was merely a cloud. 


At the end of his speech Brother ar-Rashid at-Tahir Bakr expressed joy at visiting Libya. 
He said: This visit will effectively contribute to the consolidation of the bonds of 
cooperation and solidarity linking the two peoples. 


Dr at-Turayki also met earlier with Brother ar-Rashid at-Tahir Bakr. Bilateral relations 
and the means of strengthening them were discussed at the meeting, which was attended by 
members of the two delegations. The most important current international issues were 
also discussed at this meeting.
