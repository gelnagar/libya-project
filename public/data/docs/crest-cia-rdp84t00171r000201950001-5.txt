Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery analysis note reports the first identification of Libyan Air Force Flogger B interceptors armed with Apex air-to-air missiles, seen at Al Bumbah airfield North on the northeast coast, about 48 nautical miles northwest of Tubruq. It is far from Tripoli.
• Flogger B interceptors armed with AA-7 (Apex) air-to-air missiles were confirmed in Libya for the first time.
• Thirteen Flogger B aircraft were seen, six armed with Apex; not all were on the graphic.
• The Flogger E is currently the primary Libyan Air Force interceptor; the Flogger B has improved avionics and infrared subsystems.
• Apex maximum effective range is up to 14 NM, versus 4 NM for the primary Flogger missile, the Atoll.
• Coordinates given as 32-27-13N 023-07-10E.
• Collateral reference: DIA DST-1320S-105-82, 5 April 1982.
• 1983-09-23: NPIC imagery analysis reports first confirmation of Flogger B interceptors armed with Apex AAM in Libya, with 13 Flogger B aircraft (six armed with Apex) on the apron and taxiways.

SIGNIFICANCE: THE FIRST IDENTIFICATION OF THE FLOGGER B AND AA7 
(APEX) AIR-TO-AIR MISSILES .(AAM) IN LIBYA. (SWN) . 

REMARKS: FLOGGER B INTERCEPTORS, ARM WITH APEX AAM WERE 
CONFIRMED FOR THE FIRST TIME IN LIBYA THIRTEEN 
FLOGGER B (SIX ARMED WITH APEX) WERE ON THE MAIN PARKING APRON 
AND TAXIWAYS AT AL BUMBAH AIRFIELD NORTH (NOT ALL ON GRAPHIC). 
AL BUMBAH AIRFIELD NORTH IS 48 NM NORTHWEST OF TUBRUQ, ON THE 
NORTHEAST COAST OF LIBYA. (SWN) .
