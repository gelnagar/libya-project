LIBYA

TRIPOLI ON U.S. MANEUVERS, AL-QADHDHAFI STATEMENT 


NC180730 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0655 GMT 18 Feb 83 


[Text] U.S. radio stations simultaneously announced at dawn yesterday, Jamahiriyah time, 
that the United States has dispatched four units of the U.S. 6th Fleet to the middle of 
the Mediterranean and ordered them to move toward the Libyan coast. The units include 
the nuclear-powered aircraft carrier Nimitz. 


The United States has also dispatched four AWACS aircraft to Egypt. The radio stations 
reported that this U.S. military move toward Libya and the dispatch of the AWACS 

to Egypt came at the personal request of President Husni Mubarak. While the U.S. 
President claimed at a press conference that the movement of the U.S. units is connected 
with U.S.-Egyptian joint maneuvers currently being held, a U.S. White House official 
affirmed after U.S. President Ronald Reagan's press conference that maneuvers have been 
carried out off the Libyan coast in the Mediterranean in which the aircraft carrier 
Nimitz took part. 


On the other hand, an Egyptian official spokesman has said that the AWACS aircraft and 
the U.S. aircraft carrier Nimitz have not been participating in joint maneuvers with 
Egypt, as President Reagan said. 


The news of the U.S. military movement toward the Libyan coast has been the lead story 
in all world and Arab information media. The world news agencies, radios, and newspa- 
pers published yesterday evening carried the announcement by the brother leader of the 
revolution before the General People's Congress yesterday that the Libyan people will 
not allow the United States or any other state to enter the territorial waters in the 
Gulf of Sidra, and that the Libyan people are fully prepared to turn this gulf into 

a red gulf in defense of our waters, land, and air. 


TRIPOLI COMMENT ON REAGAN MESSAGE TO NUMAYRI 
NC180810 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 18 Feb 83 


[Text] The lackey Numayri yesterday received a message from U.S. President Reagan 
which, reportedly, is connected with the close relations existing between the lackey's 
government and the U.S. Government. The message, which was delivered by a U.S. envoy 
who arrived in Khartoum yesterday, affirmed the U.S. Administration's support of the 
Sudanese regime, which has turned the life of the Sudanese people into misery and 
deprivation. 


It was also reported, on the basis of a report carried by the official SUDANESE NEWS 
AGENCY, that the message also included an affirmation of the humiliating U.S. position 
and an attempt to draw the Sudan -- land and people -- by coercion to the imperialist 
circles. 


JANA: CHAD REFUTES ALLEGATIONS OF INVASION PLANS 
LD171603 Tripoli JANA in English 1400 GMT 17 Feb 83 


[Text] Bardai, Jumada 5, Feb 17, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The information secre- 
tariat of the Chadian National Unity Government issued a statement here yesterday 
refuting allegations circulated by the imperialist and colonialist circles against the 
Chadian Provisional National Government, led by Goukouni Oueddei. It also refuted 


allegations by the Western press that Libya was preparing for an invasion of Chad. 


The statement said: "We remind international public opinion that Libya intervened in 
the past in Chad at the request of the legitimate government, recognised by the African 
countries in the Lagos agreement.

Similarly, the Chadian Provisional Government of National Unity asked the Libyan 
forces to leave Chadian territory. The Libyan forces withdrew after helping in the 
enforcement of security in Chad. The last Libyan soldier left Chad in November 1981. 


It stressed: The Chadian people will intensify the struggle against the Habre clique 
until it overthrows this corrupt gang, which bro ght destruction and poverty to the 
Chadian people. 


The statement cited several operations carried out by the national liberation army, 
throughout the country, against Habre's gangs. On 5 January a group of fighters carried 
out a successful operation in Sar in the south setting ablaze an ammunition dump and 
killing its guards. Last October, the liberation army downed an American C-130 plane, 
25 km from Mongo in the central regions. The plane was carrying weapons and ammunition. 
The Army also captured Casra city [spelling as received] for 15 days last December. 


In town Birko-Indi Tibsti [spelling as received], the liberation army repulsed an 
attack by rebel Habre forces inflicting heavy damage, including 120 killed and large 


quantities of U.S. and Zionist-made weapons and transport carriers captured.
