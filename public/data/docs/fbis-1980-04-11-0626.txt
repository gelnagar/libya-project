The foreign secretary said in his opening speech that this conference, taking place following

president has done will not go by unpunished. He added that the Arab nation which rejects 
capitulation and defeat is determined to achieve liberation through struggle.

AMBASSADOR INTERVIEWED ON GULF ISLANDS, IRAN RELATIONS 


LDO080334 Tehran Domestic Service in Persian 1630 GMT 8 Apr 80 LD 


[Text] In an interview with PARS, the Libyan ambassador in Iran commented on Iranian- 
Libyan relations, the outlook for relations between the two countries and the economic 
future of Iran and Libya following the victory of the Islamic revolution of Iran. In 

this interview, the Libyan ambassador, commenting on the Iraqi foreign minister!s re- 

marks regarding Iranian sovereignty over the three islands--Lesser Tunb, Greater Tunb 

and Abu-Musa--in the Persian Gulf, said: The raising of such issues can only benefit 

the enemies of Islam, world imperialism and the colonialists. 


At the outset of this interview, he pointed out that the Libyan Embassy in the Islamic 
Republic of Iran was opened about the time of the Iranian New Year and that the two 
countries! relations are continuing at the embassy level at present. The ambassadors 
will soon present his credentials to the Iranian authorities. 


Continuing his remarks to PARS, the Libyan ambassador emphasized that the relations 
existing between Iran and Libya are very cordial and effort will be made to make them 
exemplary and reflect the understanding between the Iranian and Libyan nations. 


He added: At present about half a million foreign workers are working in Libya and 
following the victory of the Iranian Islamic Revolution brother Al-Qadhdhafi has ex- 
pressed the desire for Iranian experts, too, to participate in the construction of the 
new Libya. Iranian workers and experts are Muslims and are regarded as our brothers in 
religion; we will properly welcome them in Libya. 


Continuing his PARS interview, the Libyan ambassador pointed out: The Libyan press, 

radio and television, in their news and other programs, spare no effort in order to 
introduce the Islamic revolution of Iran. When the Muslim student followers of the 

imam's policy occupied the U.S. Embassy, the Libyan press--more than any other country!'s-- 
published material on it. 


The Libyan ambassador added: We are pleased by the establishment of diplomatic relations 
with Iran. Imam Khomeyni is not only the leader of Iran, but is the leader of all the 
Muslims of the world. Iran and Libya are like one country and we regard any form of 
aggression against Iran and the Iranian Revolution as aggression against our own country.

CHAD OFFICIAL EXPRESSES GRATITUDE FOR LIBYAN SUPPORT 


LD101320 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 10 Apr 80 LD 


[Text] Mahamat Nour Adam, secretary general of the Chad Sovernment led by Oueddei Goukouni, 
underlined his government's determination to put an end to the armed rebellion in Chad 

led by Hissein Habre and backed by the imperialist states and the reactionary forces in 
Chad. The Chad Government secretary general expressed the gratitude of the people of 

Chad to the Libyan Arab people for backing them and constantly standing by them in their 
struggle for freedom and the establishment of peace in Chad. He explained that the Chad 
Government categorically rejects any conference for reconciliation in Chad other than the 
Lagos conference, He said that his visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
is aimed at conveying the latest developments of the situation in Chad and the consequences 
arising from the rebellion of the traitor Hissein Habre. Mahamat Nour Adam, who arrived 

in Tripoli today, has conferred with the foreign secretary. 


PRESS RELEASE ISSUED ON AFGHAN MINISTER'S VISIT 
LD110138 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 10 Apr 80 LD 


[Text] A press release was published Thursday in Tripoli and Kabul on the visit of 

Shah Mohamed Dost, foreign minister of Afghanistan, to Libya. The press release stresses 
the strong ties linking the two countries and condemns the use of Islam by colonialism, 

and particularly the United States and reaction, as a screen for carrying out their schemes 
and imposing their hegemony on the Islamic world and for diverting its attention from 

the basic question in Palestine, a matter that is at variance with true Islam, 


The two sides condemn the Stables David agreements, which are a conspiracy against the 
Palestinian problem, and all capitulatory moves aimed at doing away with this problem, 
They both expressed support for the struggle of the Palestinian people for the recovery 
of their usurped homeland and the setting up of their own indpendent state on all their 
national territory. They reaffirm their backing for the just struggle of the Saharan 
people for self-determination,
