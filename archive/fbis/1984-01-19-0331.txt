LIBYA

‘Arafat Visit to Egypt Scored by Congress 
LD181756 Tripoli JANA in English 1419 GMT 18 Jan 84 


{Text} Tripoli, Rabi Thani 14, Jan 18, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People’s 
Congress (APC) issued a statement at the end of its first session 
for 1984, in which it condemned Yasir ‘Arafat's visit to Egypt, 
calling it a stab and a blow to the Palestinian struggle in 
particular and the national struggle in general, and that it is a 
breach of the Palestinian national charter and the resolutions of 
the Baghdad summit and Arab People’s conference charter. 
‘Arafat's visit, it added, gave a boost to the As-Sadat-Mubarak 
regime, giving it an Arab approval and dealing a blow to the 
Palestinian national unity. 


The participants greeted the historical steadfastness of the Pal- 
estinian people especially those inside the occupied Arab 
territories and their rejection of capitulatory solutions, and 
praised the organizations which rejected Abu ‘Ammar’s line and 
his visit to Cairo 


The statement said that the members of the APC Permanent 
Secretariat have studied the situation in Lebanon, it being the 
arena of national struggle against the Zionist enemy, imperialism 
and reactionaryism. The arena reflects directly on the Palestin- 
ian cause, Syria and the situation in the Arab world as a whole. 
In this respect, the statement called for concentrating all efforts 
on the current events in Lebanon and the necessity of giving all 
forms of political, financial and military support to the Lebanese 
national forces through their struggle against the Zionist occupa- 
tion and the U.S. military presence. 


The conferencees [as received] lauded the significant role of the 


Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in its backing of the 
Lebanese national forces, the Palestinian revolution and Syria, 
politically, financially and militarily, and considered its stand 
evidence of the sincerity of the national tendency of the Jama- 
hiriyah and its revolutionary leadership which made of the 
Palestinian cause and the national cause a direct responsibility 
and commitment of the Arab masses in Libya. 


The final statement of the Permanent Secretariat put an empha- 
sis on the need for national popular general mobilization to 
confront the common enemy in the light of the increasing impe- 
rialist, Zionist and reactionary challenges. 


The secretariat also studied the suggested program for 1984 and 
endorsed it in its final formula, and discussed means of improving 
and developing the Arab People’s Congress. The members also 
agreed to support the international secretariat in solidarity with 
the Arab people and its core cause Palestine in order to

enhance the international popular action in the service of the 
Arab cause, based on aims of the national struggle which drops 
all capttulatory tendencies 


The A.P.C. Permanent Secretariat also issued a number of 
resolutions and recommendations, including the merger of sev- 
eral student and women’s organizations in the Arab world, in 
order to expand the membership of the A.P.C. Permanent Sec- 
retariat. The resolutions also stressed that it is essential for 
Secretariat members to be committed to their responsibilities and 
be honest and respect their decisions, and do their best to carry 
them out in the proper manner, and the need to convey the 
resolutions to the basic popular units. The responsibility of 
implementing these resolutions lies with the members of the 
Permanent Secretariat and not its bureau 


The secretariat decided to organize action among the popular 
masses in every Arab arena in order to face the Zionist- 
imperialist enemy by creating a formula that allows for the 
injection of efforts by all concerned parties at the Arab People’s 
Congress, in order to improve quality through concentrating 
these efforts in Lebanon against the Zionist enemy and creating 
a national formula for armed struggle

U.S. ‘False Concern’ Over Chad Criticized 
LDI81611 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
18 Jan 84 


[Text] The Reagan administration has expressed mock anxiety 
regarding the situation in Chad, after it sought the failure of the 
National Reconciliation Conference held recently in Addis 
Ababa. An official spokesman for the U.S. State Department 
expressed this false concern at his daily news conference yester- 
day. He said: The American Administration is still anxious 
about the situation in Chad, as it is also anxious about what Libya 
intends to do in Chad

official American designs and convictions about the need to
