LIBYA

Ab-’Ubaydi Meets With Iran’s Velayati 23 Aug 
LD231426 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
3 Aug 83 


[Text] Meetings of the joint Libyan-lranian committee opened 
this morning at the meeting hall in Al-Kabir Hotel in Tripoli to 
(identify) aspects of cooperation between Jamahiriyah and Iran. 
The meeting was attended by ‘Abd al-’Ati al-’Ubaydi, secretary 
of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison, and on the Iranian 
side by Dr ‘Ali Akbar Velayati, Iranian foreign minister. 


IRNA Report 
LD232127 Tehran IRNA in English 2004 GMT 23 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Aug. 23, irna The Iranian Foreign Affairs 
Minister Dr. "Ali Akbar Velayati who is currently here heading 
a high ranking delegation, met and conferred with Libyan For- 
eign Liaison and Oil Secretary "Abd al-’Ati al-'Ubaydi and 
Kamil Hasan al-Maghur, here today. Dr. Velayati who arrived 
here yesterday in a meeting with the Libyan oil secretary, 
discussed issues related to OPEC. During the meeting, which was 
also attended by the Iranian Oil Ministry's deputy, Dr. 
Honardust it was decided that the Libyan oil secretary would 
travel to Iran in a near future to follow up the discussions. 


Meanwhile the Iranian foreign affairs minister and his accom- 
panying delegation held talks with the Libyan Foreign Liaison 
this morning during which both sides stressed the need for greater 
unity amongst Muslims in order to counter the global infidelity. 
Al-'Ubaydi, commenting on the Islamic Revolution of Iran said 
it has continued to stand on the side of oppressed countries and 
its Muslim allies, despite the unjust war and numerous plots of 
imperialism 


Dr. Velayati told his Libyan counterpart that at a time while the 
world blasphemy was standing against Islam and Muslims with 
the objective of weakening them, the Muslim countries should 
use their efforts to revive glory of Islam. Furthermore, he added, 
the Muslim countries should further unite their ranks in dealing 
with the plots of the Zionists. As for the issue of Palestine, he 
said it was the biggest problem of the Muslims. The Zionist 
regime, he said, by attacking south Lebanon, wanted to shatter 
the resistance showed by the member countries of the
