Cables Al-Qadhdhafi on Departure 
LD141049 Tripoli JANA in English 0822 GMT 14 Oct 83 


{Teat] Tripoli, Muharram 8 Oct 14, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY ~~ Lt Colonel Mohamed Khouna Ould Haydala sent 
the following cable to the leader of the revolution following his 
departure at midday yesterday 


Brother Colone! Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, while | am leaving 
the beloved city of Tripoli | would like to express thanks and 
fratitude on my behalf and on behalf of my accompanying 
delegation for the warm reception and hospitality we met through 
our stay in the Jamahiriyah. | am quite confident that our talks 
will have positive results in bolstering our fraternal relations for 
the interest of both our brotherly peoples. | also hope our talks 
will contribute to uniting the Arab nation and consolidating 
cooperation and solidarity among the Araband African countries 
so as to confront all the challenges facing the Arab and Islamic 
nation as well as the African peoples. Please accept my highest 
fraternal respects. Your brother, Colonel Khouna Ould Haydala, 
Chairman of the Military Committee for National Salvation and 
president of the Islamic Republic of Mauritania 


Al-Qadhdhafi Clarifies View of Italian Relations 
LD1I51607 Rome Domestic Service in Italian 2300 GMT 
14 Oct 83 


[Text] Libyan leader Al-Qadhdhafi is demanding war repara- 
tions from Italy and affirms that this is the only obstacle to the 
development of friendly relations between the two countries 
Here is a dispatch from Tripoli by Leopoldo Innocenti 


[Innocenti] If we do not solve together and in a friendly way the 
dispute of the past Italian colonialism in our country, there can 
be serious consequences. A few hours ago, Libyan leader Al- 
Qadhdhafi uttered these words in Tripoli in a calm but firm voice 
in the tent that is now part of the official show. Before the major 
representatives of the Italian papers, the colonel spoke of the 35 
years of colonization of his country, pointing out that the crimes 
of the Italians were seen worse than those committed by Hitler 
against the Jews 


This is in brief the views of the Libyan leader at the press 
conference. On one hand, he makes on behalf of the Libyan 
people precise demands for reparations, but on the other, leaves 
the door open to a friendly and constructive dialogue with a 
partner such as Italy, who is regarded by Al-Qadhdhafi as 
extremely important 


It was said that 20,000 Italians live and work in Libya and, 
therefore, it 1s in the interests of the two countries to put into 
action the new historical and friendly relations between the two 
countries This is what Al-Qadhdhafi told us
