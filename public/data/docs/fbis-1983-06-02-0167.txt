LIBYA

SANA: Al-Asad, Al-Qadhdhafi Hold Talks 1 June 
JNO10950 Damascus SANA in Arabic 0855 GMT | Jun 83 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, | Jun(SANA)— President Hafiz al-Asad and 
Colonel Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi held a third round of talks at 
midnight last night, which continued until 0200 today. 


The talks revolved around issues pertinent to the general sit- 
uation in the region and the need to confront the imperialist- 
Zionist onslaught on the Arab homeland and to undermine the 
contract of submission which is meant to be imposed on Lebanon.

[Text] Tripoli, 31 May,(JANA)— Brother Mohamed Abdelaziz,
