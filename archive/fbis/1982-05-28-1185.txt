LIBYA

ARGENTINA'S GALTIERI SENDS MESSAGE TO AL-QADHDHAFI 


LD272306 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 27 May 82 


["Text" of message from Argentine President Lt Gen Leopoldo Galtieri to 
Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi -- date not given] 


[Text] To Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, commander of the great 1 September Revolution: 


Our people's struggle for liberation today urgently requires the solidarity of spiritual 
forces which alone is capable of confronting the heretical imperialism and materialism 
which is destroying our civilization. In the name of the Argentine nation I greet 
through Your Excellency the Libyan people who know how to wage the battle of faith and 
belief to save the religious and spiritual values and morals of Islamic society from 
certain destruction. There is nothing more important and honorable in the life of men 
than the battles they wage for the salvation and protection of their dignity from all 
forms of [word indistinct] subjugation and colonialism. 


I express to you our admiration for your great revolution, which is inspired by the 
glorious Koran and the esteemed Islamic religion and which fulfilled the national will 
and accomplished the serious task which providence had wished for it -- to be a lasting 
example and model for the entire Arab world and all the free peoples in the world. 


At these fateful moments in the history of the Argentine nation and of the whole of 
Latin America, which decided years ago to secure-and consolidate its spiritual character 
in confronting aggressive imperialism, I affirm to Your Excellency our determination to 
confront with bravery and faith the forces of treason and aggression. Our state has 
suffered from the treacherous and cunning aggression of the heretical colonialist forces 
which are hostile to the values of Christianity, which Your Excellency understands very 
well. I want to thank you for your sincere and brave attitude toward the unjust war to 
which we are subjected and whose ordeals we suffer, and we are confident that our 
steadfastness and struggle has the same meaning and values as the holy war in Islam. 


We beseech Almighty God and His prophet Muhammad to inspire you and strengthen you so 
you may complete your sacred and inevitable task for the glory of Islam and Muslims and 
unite us with him in faith and belief and support us with His glory for the triumph of 
our just cause and for the liberation and sovereignty of our people and homeland. 


May God, hisprophet and the Virgin Mary protect you on the raad of right and jihad. 


[Signed] Lieutenant General Galtieri
