LIBYA

EGYPTIAN MANEUVERS NEAR BORDER REPORTED 
ID141399 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 14 May 79 LD 


[Text] It was announced in the Egyptian capital today that the Egyptian Armed Forces 
stationed at the Libyan borders began military maneuvers this morning in the area of 
Sidi al-Barrani, 


It is recalled that an enemy military delegation consisting of the chief of staff and 
chief of intelligence is now visiting the Egyptian capital, in addition to an American 
military delegation, According to observers, the talks among the three military dele- 
gations included discussions of launching aggression against Libya. 


AT-TURAYKI PROPOSES FORCE TO DEFEND MUSLIM DIGNITY 
ID120945 Tripoli JANA in English 09¢) GMT 12 May 79 ID 


[Excerpt] Fez, 12 May, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Dr 'Ali at-Turayki, the foreign 
secretary, affirmed that the time is ripe for reaching a true practical solution which 
guarantees the full liberation of Jerusalem, 


Dr at-Turayki, who was talking to the press yesterday in Fez, said that the liberation 
of Jerusalem is an Islamic duty which concerns all Muslims in the world, 


He said that the Jamahiriyah has suggested the formation of a military force to @fend 
the Muslims and their dignity. He pointed out that Muslims are subjected to wars of 
mass eradication in different parts of the world, particularly in Palestine and 
occupied Jerusalem,

The foreign secretary pointed out that if a Zionist person or a person from the countries 
of big military pacts is arrested, the world turns upside down, while not a finger is 
lifted in the face of eradication campaigns against Muslims, Should seriously think of 
this suggestion. [sentence as received] 


He affirmed the importance and the necessity of this suggestion in the present world. 


On the other hand, he affirmed that there is no trusteeship over the Palestinian people 
and that the PLO is the sole legitimate representative of the Palestinians, He said 

that the conference has condemned the defeated Egyptian president because he held negotia- 
tions with the enemy on behalf of the Palestinians without possessing any right to do so, 
He said that the expulsion of Egypt from the Islamic conference and all its international 
organizations and associations is a reaction from the Islamic nation to the capitulatory 
Egyptian policy. 


With regard to Sudan's stand, the foreign secretary pointed out that Sudan affirmed in a 
joint communique with the Jamahiriyah last week its support for Baghdad's summit resolu- 
tions and its condemnation of the capitulatory solutions with the enemy and its stand 
with the Palestinian people and their just cause. With regard to the suspension of 
Egypt's membership in the Organization of African Unity, OAU, and the non-alignment 
movement, the foreign secretary said that the subject will be presented before the two 
organizations, because Egypt had violated their resolutions and became an ally to the 
Zionist enemy. He also affirmed in his statement that the Jamahiriyah was the first 
voice to announce after the kilometre-101 meeting that As-Sadat is a traitor and 
surrenderer, [as received] but the Arabs laughed and said that this is because of 

personal disagreements between the two countries, but the time has proved the farsighted-

regime which (?implemented) the national treason according to a precise [word indistinct ] 
at the time we said that the agreement will lead to the Arab nation and the Islamic 
nation to disaster,

and the defeated Egyptian regime is about the Palestinian cause and the Palestinian people 
which we decided to fully support, but the defeated Egyptian president decided to sell out 
their cause and to give it up to the Zionists. He affirmed that the Arabs have one choice: 
either to be with or against the enemy.
