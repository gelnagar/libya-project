Makes Statement, Departs 
LD281505Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 28 May 78 LD 
[Text] This morning Spanish Foreign Minister Marcelino Oreja left Tripoli at the end of 
his visit to Libya during which he handed a message from King Juan Carlos to Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi and renewed the king's invitation to visit Spain. 
The Spanish minister was seen off at the airport by Foreign Secretary Dr Ali Abd as-Salam 


at-Turayki, the assistant under secretary for political affairs, heads of Foreign Ministry 
departments and the Spanish ambassador to the Jamahiriyah. 


The Spanish foreign minister said his country will not establish relations of any kind with 
the Zionist enemy, especially diplomatic ones. He said Spain will not recognize Zionism 
and has not had any dialog or meeting with it. He confirmed that Spain stands by the 
Palestinian people's right to self-determination. He said: The historic relations between 
Spain and the Arab countries make us stand by the legitimate right of the Palestinian 
people and we will always adhere to this position.

PRESIDENT OF RWANDA ARRIVES FOR VISIT 


LD282246Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 28 May 78 LD 


[Text] Juvenal Habyarimana, president of the Republic of Rwanda, arrived in Tripoli 
this evening on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. He was given an official welcome at Tripoli 
international airport, where he was received by Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, 
Secretaries, under-secretaries, heads of diplomatic missions and senior officers of the 
armed forces also took part. 


President Habyarimana, in a statement to JANA, described his visit to the Jamahiriyah 
as being a visit of friendship and fraternity. He said: I will use the opportunity 
of my visit to discuss with my colleagues topics relating to the strengthening of 
relations between the Jamahiriyah and Rwanda.

JALLUD RECEIVES CONGOLESE PRESIDENT'S ADVISER 
LD301L514Y Tripoli JANA in English 1425 GMT 30 May 78 LD 


{Text} Tripoli, May 30, JANA--Staff-Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud, member of the Permanent 
Secretariat of the General People's Congress, met yesterday the political adviser of the 
Congolese president. The envoy handed him a letter to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, secretary- 
general of the General People's Congress, from Mr. Joachim Yhombi-Opango, president of 
the People's Republic of Congo. 


The meeting, which was attended by the director of the African Department at the Foreign 
Secretariat, dealt with bilateral cooperation, the situation in Africa and questions of 
mutual interest. 


LEBANON'S WALID JUNBLATT, DELEGATION END VISIT 

LD260956Y Tripoli JANA in English 0831 GMT 26 May 78 LD 

[Text] Tripoli, May 26, JANA--Mr Walid Junblatt, the leader of the Lebanese Progressive 
Socialist Party and the Lebanese National Movement, left here yesterday after visiting 
the Jamahiriyah at the head of a Lebanese delegation. 

Mr Junblatt expressed his satisfaction at the results of his visit during which he had 
met Colonel Mu'tammar al-Qadhdhafi and Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and held 
official talks. 


In a statement to the JANA correspondent, Mr Junblatt commended the Jamahiriyah's role 
in supporting liberation movements and in fighting colonialism in all its forms. 


Mr Junblatt reaffirmed that the Jamahiriyah and the Lebanese National Movemeut agreed 
upon the need to consolidate their cooperation in order to liberate iue Arab people as 
well as the peoples in Africa, Asia, and all over the world from the oppression of 
reaction and colonialism. 


He added that what was taking place in the Jamahiriyah could be taken as an example by 
the whole world of striving towards a better society.

[Text] Kuwait, 27 May--The Kuwaiti newspaper AL-ANBA!' reports here today that the Libyan

this is the biggest deal ever reached between the two countries, but it does not report 
on the amount of the deal or the time of delivery to Libya.
