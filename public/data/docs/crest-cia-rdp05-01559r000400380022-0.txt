Summary (AI): A March 1979 cable from the US embassy in Tripoli reports Qadhafi's complaint, in a Paris magazine interview, that the US had blocked eight Lockheed C-130 aircraft Libya had paid for and had not refunded the money. The embassy suggests a sale-and-refund settlement. Nothing here concerns specific places in Tripoli.
• Libya had paid for eight Lockheed C-130 aircraft, with the full purchase price paid in 1973.
• Qadhafi said the US government had refused to send the planes and refused to refund payments, calling it 'highway robbery'.
• The embassy suggested offering to help facilitate sale of the eight C-130s with proceeds returned to Libya as purchase price plus interest.
• Embassy noted the inflationary rise in value of the planes could permit a sale covering the original price plus interest.
• 1979-03-19: Qadhafi interview with Afrique-Asie magazine, carried by JANA on 19 March, complaining the US blocked paid-for C-130 aircraft and did not refund payments
