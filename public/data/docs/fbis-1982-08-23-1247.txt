JANA Editorial 
LD221524 Tripoli JANA in English 1426 GMT 22 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Dul Qidah 2, August 22 -- Jamahirayah News Agency -- In a commentary 
on the tragedy of evacuating the Palestinian Resistance from Beirut and the hosting by 
some reactionary Arab regimes of the Palestinian fighters forced to leave the Lebanese 
capital, the political editor of the Jamahiriyah News Agency published the following 
editorial: 


Yesterday, Saturday, was a day of Arab catastrophe. For that day signalled the beginning 
of a conspiratorial series of events whose heroes are those conspiring Arab rulers who 
are implementing a treasonous scheme under the smoke-screen of nationalism and of meeting 
the Palestinian Resistance's request. These shameless Arab regimes repeating their offer 
of receiving the Palestinians who were forced to leave Beirut under Zionist shelling 
supported by American imperialism. 


Everyone knows, including the regimes involved, that these regimes are obeying American 
and Zionist orders, and have the price for implementing America's orders. The events 

in Beirut, the tragic dispersal of the Palestinians watched by the entire world and the 
statements made by Begin and Sharon following the beginning of the Resistance's evacuation, 
throws light on the backstage of events and reveals the common position of all occupiers. 


This course of events places all Palestinians before an undisputed reality: that their 
only choice lies between martyrdom or joining the bandwagon of treasons which many 
leftists and rightist Arabs are party to. All masks have finally been removed and 

the true face of treason is now revealed. 


With the exception of the front-line states, any regimes receiving the Palestinians 
forcefully evacuated by the Zionists from Beirut, are treasonous regimes that are 
fulfilling Zionist and American goals. 


The final analysis of the summary of these events confirms the belief that the dispersal 
of the Resistance, and the death and destruction caused by the Zionists were made possible 
by the Camp David accords. 


The commital by the Egyptian regime of its greatest sin of capitulation to Israel has 
enabled the Zionists to strike at the Palestinian Resistance and all other Arab positions. 
The Egyptian regime is the architect of defeat and a tool in the hands of the Zionists 
and America. Yet in spite of its treason, it appears now more patriotic than the 

rest of the reactionary regimes which are implementing the enemy's plans in broad 
daylight under the smoke-screen of false allegations.

ISLAMIC CONFERENCE ENDS, ISSUES RESOLUTIONS 


LD201730 Tripoli JANA in English 1450 GMT 20 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Aug 20 -- Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The 2nd Islamic call conference 
which included hundreds of Muslim scholars from all parts of the world ended here last 
night. 


A number of resolutions and recommendations were issued by the conference which include 
the following: 


- To consider Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's speech an official document of the conference. 


- To set up an international Islamic call council which would act as an executive coun- 
cil for this Islamic call conference. The council would be made up of personalities who 
have shown moral support for the holding of an Islamic call conference. The council 
members should be 36 in number to be chosen from members of the general conference of 
the Islamic call which holds its general meeting every 4 years. 


- To make the headquarters of the Islamic Call Society in Tripoli, Jamahiriyah, as the 
main center of the international Islamic call council. 


- It is possible for the council to meet at any other venue and the secretary of the 
Islamic Call Society may act as a secretary general of the council and the 
administrative section of the society will be in charge of the council's general 
administrative affairs. 


- The international Islamic call council meets upon the invitation of its secretary 
general at least once a year to carry out the resolutions and recommendations passed by 
the general conference and thereby fulfill the objectives of the Islamic call and 
implement its constitution. It will also endeavour to unite the efforts of all the 
Muslims to promote life in the Muslim community as ordained by God through his revela- 
tion to the prophet Mohammad (peace be upon him). The council will also urge the 
Muslims to be guided by the prophet's message, combat oppression and tyranny and join a 
"jihad," which would spread the word of God and purge the lands of the Muslims from 
imperialism, Zionism and crusade. 


The conference approved the election of the members of the international Islamic call 
couneil. 


V. 23 Aug 82 ie NORTH AFRICA 


The conference appealed to popular Islamic organizations to intensify their efforts in 
projecting a true picture of the atrocities committed by the Zionist enemy, which has 
been supported by a U.S. crusade, against the Muslim Palestinian Arabs. The massacres 
committed against these people should be told to the world at large. 


The conference condemned suspicious initiatives by those who desired to liquidate the 
Palestinian cause and recognise the Zionist enemy. It called on the Islamic countries 
to withdraw their deposits from American-Zionist crusading banks and to deploy the oil 
weapon in backing the Palestinian and [words indistinct] peoples and regaining the 
sacred shrines. 


It called on all Muslims to mobilise their potentialities and declare jihad against 
Zionism and the crusade on the Lebanese Muslim people aimed at liquidating the 
Palestinian Resistance. It stressed that only through jihad could the Muslims regain 
the dignity of their nation, purify their sacred shrines and back their brothers. Only 
through jihad could they purify their societies from elements of weakness, despair and 
subservience, and destroy the idols of hypocrisy and the humiliation thrones, which are 
in complicity with the Zionists and crusaders and are protected with the bayonets of 
those who fight our religion and expel us from our homes. 


It affirmed that the vile crusade on Jerusalem was a reason for the loss of Islam's 
shrines there and for Muslims not being able to visit their first Mecca and the ascen- 
sion place of their prophet (peace be upon him). 


The conference pointed out: This has materialised because the Muslim masses abdicated 
these sacred shrines and delegated responsibility for them to kings, individuals, 
presidents and governments rather than taking charge of them by themselves ir accordance 
with God's wisdom: "Their affairs by mutual consultation." 


It also expressed apprehension that not only the remainder of Jslam's sacred shrines 


would be lost but also the entire land of Islam, if the Muslims slackened their resolve 
and did not take charge of their affairs and self reliantly rid themselves of evil. 


It called on the Islamic peoples to address the problem and liberate Palestine and 
Jerusalem, and drew attention to the sacred shrines in Mecca and Medina, which could 
also face the fate of Jerusalem. 


The conference considered the liberation of Mecca and the Al-'Aqsa Mosque as the respon- 
sibility of all Muslims. It reminded Muslims that Mecca should be accessible to everyone 
and no authority should ever obstruct the path of a Muslim to holy Mecca. 


- The conference declared solidarity with all the Muslim people struggling to gain their 
freedom. It calls on all Muslim countries and people to overlook their differences, 
stop fratricidal wars and rally their ranks in the campaign against the enemies of 

Islam and the Muslims. 


- The conference condemned the injustices faced by some Muslim minorities in certain 
countries. Muslim masses everywhere, it stressed, are legally responsible for overcoming 
tyranny and aggression, rid the land of evil and ensure the respect, honour and veneration 
due to their sanctities no matter how potent the force of oppression is, because for 
Muslims none can be greater than the God Almighty who dispenses with all matters. 


- The conference stressed the need to treat with concern members of the Islamic call who 
should receive all assistance possible from the universities and higher education and 
Muslim establishments. 


V. 23 Aug 82 Q 6 NORTH AFRICA 


- The conference recommended that Islam should be adopted in its pure form as revealed by 
God and expounded by the prophet (peace be upon him), without any sects or groups. This 
would reunite the Muslims and purify the religion to its original form. 


- The conference urged that the secretary generals of the Islamic Call Society and the 
international Islamic call conference to communicate the resolutions and recommendations 
passed at its 2nd conference in Tripoli, August 14-19, to the 13th conference of the Islamic 
foreign ministers conference to be held in Niamey, Niger. 


The secretary general of the Islamic Call Society delivered a speech in which he acclaimed 
the results reached and which promote the interests of Muslims and Islam everywhere. He 
said these results open the way of righteousness. He noted that the conference helped to 
define problems facing Muslims, especially those of minority Muslim communities in various 
parts of the world. In conclusion, he highlighted the importance of these gatherings in 
consolidating the progress of Islam and the spread of its message in its true form.
