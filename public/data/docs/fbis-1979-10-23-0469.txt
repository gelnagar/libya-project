LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES LEBANESE SOCIALIST UNION LEADER 
LD192006 Tripoli JANA in English 1910 GMT 19 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Oct 19, JAMAHZRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 

leader of the Great First of September Revolution today received Mr Abdurrahim Murad, 
secretary general of the Lebanese Socialist Union, who is currently visiting the Jamahiriyah. 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi discussed with Mr Murad the latest developments in the Arab region 

and the continued Israeli aggressions against south Lebanon.
