• Qadhdhafi says the US raid targeted him and his family directly and says he was not killed.
• He says the Harare nonaligned conference condemned the NATO-American aggression against Libya.
• He announces the idea of an international army to fight America anywhere in the world, if US provocations continue.
• JANA claims the UN vote was the first time in UN history that political and public defeat was inflicted on the United States.
• 1986-11-23: Tripoli Domestic Service broadcasts text of Qadhdhafi interview with Swedish paper Aftonbladet; he says the U.S. raid came after midnight and targeted his family, and calls for Reagan and Thatcher to be tried.

[Text] Question: Reagan has been trying to justify the recent 

attack on the Jamahiriyah by saying that it was in reply to 
terrorism, which he affirms you were behind. What do you say 
about this? 

Answer: Weare against terrorism and we have no connection 
with terrorist groups. We are against the hijacking of planes and 
the placing of bombs at airports and such actions. We are a small 
country which wants to live in peace, exactly like Sweden. We 
support freedom. The U.S. CIA is the one that hatches plots 
against us. But we do not consider the PLO a collection of 
terrorist groups at all. The Palestinians are fighting for their 
freedom and their right to set up their independent state. We 
should never be held responsible for the actions they carry out or 
for the methods they pursue. 

Questions: What was your feeling at the time of the raid on 
Tripoli? 
Answer: 1 was completely surprised, for I never at all expected 

to see superpowers attack my family after midnight. For the 
Americans to attack military targets was something we had 
expected. But we never expected them to attack my family 
directly. Evidently, Reagan sought to kill me. It was for this that 
he committed a crime which conflicts with the U.S. Constitution 
which prohibits the assassination of foreign heads of state. The 
United States has also violated international law by attacking a 
country which is not in a state of war with it. 

Question: Would you desire to retaliate in the face of such a 
situation? Do you hate Reagan? 

Answer: Ido not hate anyone. But I feel sorry for Reagan and 
for the American people who are ruled by such a person. I also 
believe that Reagan is a dangerous man because he is 100 percent 
mad. I am not exaggerating. I believe that Reagan is physically 
and mentally mad. Furthermore, he suffers from old age and 
cancer, Indeed, his aides and those working with him have begun 
to lose patience with him and to have doubts about his health. 
The CIA chief, William Casey, has told his friends that Reagan 
is mad, Weare aware that Reagan daily gets in touch with Casey 
and asks him: Has anything happened to Al-Qadhdhafi? Reagan 
must have a tumor in his brain. Those working with him are 
awaiting his death soon. 

Question: How does Libya envisage confronting the American 
assault? 
Answer: The American people must bring Reagan and 

Thatcher to trial as both of them are accused of murdering 
children. They must be tried. Failing this, relatives of Libyan 
families whose sons were martyred will seek a means of 
responding to this act. These people have the right to act, and I 
am not responsible for the way they will act now or later on. They 
have the right to act and I cannot prevent them from this. 

Question: 

But how would Reagan and Thatcher be tried in 
practice? . 

Answer: The same way that Nixon was tried and then dis- 
missed from office. 

Question: You say that Libya is not against the American 
people, and that Reagan and his government are your enemies. 
Is this true? 

Answer: People all over the world support us. We received 
many letters from America and Sweden after the U.S. raid. 
These letters express support for us. Truly, the American people 
is not an enemy to us. Our enemy is always Reagan and his group 
and we will endeavor to fight them. 

Question: Will this be accomplished through the international 
secret army which you talked about during the Harare con- 
ference a while ago? 

Answer: If America continues its provocations and hostile acts 
against the Great Jamahiriyah and other small countries I will 
find myself obliged to leave my country and my people to form 
an international army to fight America anywhere in the world. 

Question: Do you think that the world will consider this army 
a terrorist group? 

Answer: We are against terrorism, but if America continues 
its terrorist policy we will be forced to take deterrent measures. 
If no other solutions are found to put an end to the international 
terrorism conducted by a major power like America, we will seek 
a solution to fight back against American aggression. America 
was the one which attacked Libya and not the opposite; America, 
with its missiles and bombs, will not be able to stand in the way 
of the international army, but I hope that we will not find 
ourselves compelled to use such a method. I hope that Reagan 
will get what he deserves at the hands of the American people. 

Question: If everything falls on Reagan’s shoulders, will 
American-Libyan relations change from what they are at the 
moment if Reagan does not hold power anymore? 

Answer: Yes. I am fully confident of this. Our relations were 
excellent during Carter’s term of office. Reagan was the one who 
implicated his country in this bad situation. 

Question: How do you see Reagan’s designs against the Jama- 
hiriyah? What does he hold in his quiver? 

Answer: Reagan is an actor. His hobby is to write a scenario 
about international politics. At present he is busy writing a new 
scenario about Libya. It is likely that the CIA will embark on a 
number of acts of terrorism and then accuse Libya of them. Or 
the United States might try to distort Libya’s image in the press. 

Question: In your speech at the Harare nonaligned conference, 
you described the member-countries as dummies. Yet the con- 
ference adopted a resolution condemning the NATO-American 
aggression on the Jamahiriyah. Is the Nonaligned Movement a 
really useless movement and does it have no meaning?
