LIBYAN, SOVIET AMBASSADORS CONFER IN BUCHAREST 
LD022134 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 2 Apr 79 LD 
[Text] The ambassador of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in Bucharest 
received the Soviet ambassador, Doyen of the diplomatic corps in Romania, today. 
During the meeting they discussed Libyan Arab-Soviet relations and the current situation 
in the Arab region, The talks also dealt particularly with the reports of troops 


massing on the Libyan borders, 


DECISION OF PEOPLES CONGRESS DELEGATES COUNCIL REPORTED 


LD021738 Tripoli JANA in English 1615 GMT 2 Apr 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, April 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The permanent Delegates Council 
held a meeting yesterday at the Arab People's Congress permanent secretariat headquarters 
in Tripoli, 


Ve 3 Apr 79 NORTH AFRICA 


The meeting was for following up the resolutions of the secretariat's emergency meeting 
held recently in Beirut upon the invitation of the Palestine Liberation Organization 
for confronting the capitulation treaty signed by the defeated Egyptian president and 
the terrorist Begin under his master's supervision, Jimmy Carter, 


During its meeting, the council decided on the formation of a central and a national 
follow-up committee which will monitor popular reaction against the As-Sadat-JZionist- 
imperialist alliance, It will also study the boycott of ships, planes and American 
merchandise, the isolation of hireling As-Sadat's regime and support for the Palestine 
revolution with all possible means, 


The delegates sent a letter to the Arab Press Union's meeting currently convened in 
Baghdad, and a memorandum to the Arab Union of Trade Syndicates concerning the imple- 
mentation of the Arab People's Congress resolutions in order to confront the treasonous 
treaty and escalate popular struggle against it, 


PEOPLE'S CONGRESS ISSUES STATEMENT ON PALESTINIAN DAY 
LDO10044 Tripoli JANA in English 1702 GMT 31 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Mar 31, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Permanent Secretariat of the 
Arab People's Congress issued a statement addressed to the Palestine Arab people in 
the occupied land in commemoration of the "Day of the Land," The statement is as 
follows; 


"Fellow Palestinian Arabs in the occupied land, This year whilst commemorating the 

Day of the Land, our Arab nation and progressive world forces appeal to you, with great 
hope and confidence, to decisively confront the new conspiracy against our Palestine 
people and their giant revolution, which has become an objective reality for world 
public opinion to see, 


Your continuous movement against the conspiracy, aided by all nationalist forces in the 
Arab nation, will prove to both friend and foe that no peace could be achieved without 
guaranteeing the Palestine people's unshakeable national rights and that the defeated 
Egyptian president's treachery will not hinder the Palestine people's resistance, " 


The statement added: "We are today called upon more than any other time to escalate 
the struggle against the enemy within and outside the occupied lands, in order to 
entrench our rights and defend our lands, Only through this can our Arab people 
declare its position, carry out its refusal of the defeated president's treason and 
reaffirm its capability for confrontation and steadfastness against the American- 
Zionist defeated Egyptian president conspiracy, " 


The statement said; "Imperialist, Zionist and reactionary schemes aim at bolstering the 
American military presence in the Arab region and tying it to a series of military 
alliances designed to protect Arab reactionary regimes aligned with world imperialism 
and eager for capitulationist solutions,.." 


The statement added: "Such schemes also aim at reformulating the Arab region's poli- 
tical, economic and social policies to suit the interest of world imperialism mono- 
polies and their brokers in the Arab nation, We are confident of ourselves and our 
just struggle no matter how intense the enemy's aggression is..."

The statement affirmed: "Our Palestinian people is not alone in its battle, Jur Arab 
nation, with all its popular forces and progressive, nationalist regimes, is waging 
the battle with Palestinians, aided by all world progressive forces, especially 
friendly socialist countries..." 


Ending its statement, the secretariat affirmed that the whole world viewed with admi- 
ration our people's struggle in the occupied land and looked forward to similar added 


moves for foiling the conspiracy aimed at liquidating the Palestine people's revolution, 


TRIPOLI REPORTS 'BESTIAL OFFENSIVE! AGAINST UGANDA 


LD021926 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 2 Apr 79 LD 


[Text] Uganda is now witnessing massacres and operations of annihilitation against 
Muslims by the Tanzanian forces which have been for some days waging a fierce attack 
against Ugandan territory. Reports from the battlefield confirm that the bestial 
offensive by the invading Tanzanian forces is primarily directed at striking at Muslims 
and their property. The reports point out that unless there is intervention to save 
these Muslims from the fate into which they are being driven, their condition will 
worsen every hour, 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Islamic Conference has demanded the withdrawal of 
the Tanzanian forces from Uganda in its message to UN Secretary General Kurt Waldheim,
