JALLUD HOLDS PRESS CONFERENCE ON TOBRUK INCIDENT 


LD221236 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 22 Aug 80 


{Text ] Tripoli, 22 Aug (JANA)--Maj Abd as-Salam Jallud yesterday afternoon met foreign 
journalists and news agency correspondents in the people!s palace, Tripoli. He began by 
welcoming the journalists to Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and then said that 
he intended to reveal the truth and nothing but the truth, "We are revolutionaries who 
struggle to reveal the truth, unmindful of lies and fabrications," 


He went on; "Only a few days separate us from the celebrations of the llth anniversary 
of the Great 1 September Revolution, The occasion will find us with the same determina- 
tion and vigor as when the revolution came into being, yet reinforced by the experience 
and achievements of the past 10 years," 


He said that when we were in the unionist officers! movement we numbered a few hundred 
but now the revolutionary movement has grown and is stronger and more capable in its 
efforts to attain greater achievements, 


Replying to a question from a REUTER correspondent about the false reports recently circu- 
lated by the media of Western imperialism and by the defeatist hireling Egyptian president 
reporting that a mutiny occured in the army garrison of Tobruk, Maj Jallud stressed that 
those lies were completely unfounded as there was neither a mutiny nor any conflict. He 
pointed out that what hac ha ~ened in Tobruk was no more than an act of espionage which was 
contained and that thos ponsible were arrested, 


Jallud explained that an i alian, working for a company engaged in a military camp in 
Tobruk, was able to enlist the services of a noncommissioned officer and two civilians by 
bribing them with wine. The [talian was kept under watch, He left for Rome, where he met 
the secretary of the Egyptian ambassador and informed him of what he had done in the 

Tobruk camp, On returning to Tobruk he was arrested, He has been in detention for 10 days, 
he confessed everything and we have taped the account which took place between him and the 
noncommissioned o-“ficer and the two civilians, 


Major Jallud said that the camp commander ordered the arrest of the noncommissioned 
officer, He at‘%empted to escape from the military police but he and the two civilians 
were finally arrested and are now under investigation, That is all that happened and was 
no more than a simple espionage operation as happens in other countries, 


Major Jallud said that were it not for the fact that the operations had taken place 
inside a military camp, it would have been possible to allow the Italian longer time 
before his arrest so that more information could have been obtained about the purpose of 
the operation, 


Major Jallud said that the lie circulated by the media of the defeatist regime and im- 
perialism is but one of the lies which the regime usually disseminates, He recalled that 
the defeatist Egyptian president once claimed in the 1977 aggression against the Jamahiriyah 
that he had destroyed Jamal 'Abd an-Nasir base in Tobruk, but more than 80 journalists 
visiting Tobruk on the same day had lunch on the base,

Replying to a question from one of the journalists on the reaction of the Jamahiriyah 

to the decision of the defeatist Egyptian president to declare a state of emergency and 
mass his troops on the borders of the Jamahiriyah, Jallud said there is no doubt that the 
state of emergency in itself represented an act of aggression, but the defeatist Egyptian 
president is incapable of waging aggression unless he requests assistance from 
imperialist quarters. 


Jallud said that the presence of Arab Libyan forces in the frontier region is normal and 
that Libya has not amassed its forces. The Libyan people are now armed, and we certainly 
closely observe the movements of the Egyptian forces. We are ever watchful, he said. 


We have great confidence in the Egyptian people and in the Egyptian Army, Jallud said. 
If the defeated leader commits aggression against the Jamahiriyah, the Egyptian peopl 
and the Egyptian Army will close ranks with their brothers, the L&byan people, in a 
united front against the As-Sadat regime. 


Concerning foreign military experts present in the Jamahiriyah, Major Jallud emphasized 
that Libya needs no one to defend it. He said that Libya buys arms from the Soviet 
Union, Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia, France and Italy. Libya also has technical experts 
from those countries in various institutions giving instruction regarding these arms, 
but they are civilians and are not in any fighting units. 


Major Jallud said that there are no East German military experts in the Jamahiriyah, 
there is no military cooperation between Libya and East Germany. 


At the end of his press conference, Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud said: I want to make 
clear that there has not, and there cannot be, mutiny in the Socialist Pecpie's Libys 
Arab Jamahiriyah, for two reasons. First, there is no possibility of a mutiny 
because the armed forces are controlled by free unionist officers. Secondly, the peo 
are no longer the pecple who used to applaud before the 1969 revolution. They are now 
an armed nation. The popular forces are trained and armed on the same level and with 
same arms as the armed forces. During the era of the Jamahiriyah and the power of the 
people, no one has the courage to rebel against the power of the people. Coups and 
mutinies ended with the advent of the power of the people.

FOREIGN LIAISON OFFICE 'ANXIOUS! OVER CARTER STATEMENT 


LD231721 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1450 GMT 23 Aug 80 


{Text] Tripoli--Here is a statement issued by the Foreign Liaison Office of the secretariat 
of the general people's conference: 


The Foreign Liaison Office has been following the U.S. President's statements regarding 
the Arabian Guif area, and wishes to express the great anxiety of the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah over the aggressive intentions and the imperialist outlook 
expressed by the United States and which concerns a vital region of the Arab homeland, the 
Arabian Gulf; it is an area which must be regarded as indivisible from the Arab nation 
whose inhabitants are greatly concerned about its peace and security. 


Such action on the part of America is, in our opinion, a blatant interference in the 
internal affairs of the Arab nation. It is, indeed, an act of defiance which disregards 
the sovereignty of the Arab nation over its territory; for on the Iranian side, the culr 
is protected by Iran, and on the Arab side, it is defended by the Arab nation. The 
security of the gulf is not the responsibility of the East or the West; it is first and 
foremost the responsibility of the nations of the region.
