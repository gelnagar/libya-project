Even  the  establishment  of  a  Soviet  base  in  Tartus,  he

ground  forces  in  Europe,  which  has  increased  the  impor¬ 
tance  of  the  sea  platform.  Tartus  was  intended  to  track 
Western  military  activity  in  the  area  and  not  to  expand 
Soviet  power. 

“In  the  final  analysis,”  the  researcher  admits,  “the  USSR

position  as  a  superpower.  The  base  and  the  few  planes 
supplied  to  Libya  and  Syria  are  a  response  to  the  West  in 
the  Middle  East.”
