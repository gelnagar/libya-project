LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Message From Soviet Ambassador 
LDI51954 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1900 GMT 15 Sep 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Sep (JANA) The brother leader of the 
revolution today received the ambassador of the Soviet Union to 
the Jamahiriyah who handed him a written message from Soviet 
leaders regarding the current grave international situation. The 
brother leader, on his part, entrusted him with an important 
message to the Soviet leaders concerning the present situation in 
Lebanon and Chad, and the crusade-type imperialist assault on 
these two countries in order to bring them into the zone of 
imperialist influence. The message said that the so-called peace- 
keeping forces and the multinational forces are nothing other 
than a perfidious trick to justify colonialism and the new crusade 


Benin President Sends Letter of Support 
LDI51005 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2315 GMT 14 Sep 83 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution received a message 
from President of the People’s Republic of Benin Mathieu 
Kerekou in which he expressed the Beninese people's backing and 
support to the Libyan Arab people in their struggle against 
imperialism and for safeguarding their freedom and protecting 
the higher principles of peace and justice in the world. In his 
message, President Kerekou said: In implementation of its 
strategy of controlling and ruling our great continent, Africa, 
international, repressive, exploitative, and hateful imperialism is
