LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI DECORATES, ADDRESSES NEWLY TRAINED SOLDIERS 
ID181142 Tripoli JANA in English 0942 GmT 18.Sep 79 ID 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 September, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The leader of the Great First 

of September Revolution, Colonel Mu!'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, greeted myriads of citizens who 
have completed their general military training course and awarded them the Badge of 

the Great Al-Fatah Revolution. Colonel al-Qadhdhafi personally presented the tops of 
these batches with the award. He declared it was in appreciation of their enrollment 

of the general military training and of their quick conversion from civilians to soldiers 
capable of carrying arms to defend the revolution and its gigantic achievements. 


Addressing the newly trained soldiers, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "You are an ideal 
example in terms of nationalism and readiness to defend your nation. By joining the 
general military training course you have implemented one of the revolution's dicta, 
namely the necessity for an armed people." He added that the military training is 

quickly building up the armed people, a guarantee to freedom, a confirmation of their 
authority and a cornerstone of the Jamahiriyah's society which possesses authority, wealth 
and arms. He went on . say that the authority which used to frighten the masses was 
handed to them through people's congresses and committees. 


Another source of fear for the masses was the wealth, which led to their exploitation, 
the control of their possessions and the sucking of their sweat and blood, [but] has 
now become a free for all, since the wage-workers have become partners, the land a 
general property and the houses the possession of their tenants. 


"Arms which were the most dangerous means of terrorizing the people were gradually 
placed in the hands of the masses." He said authority, wealth and arms are no more 
a source of fear but are now controlled by the masses. 


AL-QADHDHAFI REPORTEDLY EXPELS 1,000 PALESTINIAN TEACHERS 
NC182209 Cairo MENA in Arabic 2148 qmT 18 Sep 79 NC 


[Text] London, 18 September--AL-JIHAD, the organ of the Libyan National Democratic 
Movement, published in London, reports that Libya's Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has 
ordered the expulsion and immediate deportation of 1,000 Palestinian teachers working 
in Libya. The paper wonders why Al-Qadhdhafi wants to add to the pains and aifficulties 
of the Palestinians, saying: Is this how Al-Qadhdhafi wants to liberate Palestine? 

The paper points out that there are approximately 250,000 foreigners working in Libya, 
saying: Has the colonel become tired of 1,000 Palestinian teachers who earn their 
living by their work?
