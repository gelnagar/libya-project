Summary (AI): A 1980s New York Times clipping (the catalog date says 2011, but the text reads as January 1980) reports that Douglas Schlachter, alleged supervisor of a Libyan terrorist training project, told prosecutors two senior CIA officials endorsed it. It also says Edwin Wilson and Frank Terpil signed a 1976 contract with Col. Muammar el-Qaddafi and that Wilson lived in Tripoli.
• Schlachter's lawyer said Schlachter was briefed on Wilson's activities by two CIA officials in 1976 and 1977.
• The alleged Libyan tasks included trying to obtain Soviet military equipment, including a surface-to-air missile and radar components.
• Schlachter was indicted in August for illegally shipping explosives to Libya.
• Judge John B. Pratt set January 15 as the date for Schlachter's guilty plea.
• The CIA said that some employees supported Wilson and Terpil without official agency sanction.
• 1976: Wilson and Terpil reportedly signed a contract with Col. Muammar el-Qaddafi to train Libyans, including in bomb-making, and to supply intelligence and military equipment. Schlachter was reportedly briefed on the operation and asked to gather intelligence in Libya.
