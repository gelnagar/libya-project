Summary (AI): A 1982 New York Times article (reprinted in CIA reading room) reports that Douglas Schlachter, accused of supervising a Libyan terrorist training project run by Edwin Wilson, claims two senior CIA officials endorsed it. It concerns US intelligence links to Libya, with little specific detail about Tripoli itself.
• Wilson and Terpil reportedly signed a 1976 contract with Qaddafi to sell expertise in intelligence and military matters.
• Schlachter was indicted in August 1981 on charges of illegally shipping explosives to Libya.
• Wilson lives in Tripoli, according to the article.
• Schlachter was to enter a guilty plea on two counts on Jan. 18, 1982.
• Alleged Libyan tasks included obtaining a Soviet surface-to-air missile and Soviet-made radar electronic components.
