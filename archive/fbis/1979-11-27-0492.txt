JOINT STATEMENT ISSUED FOLLOWING IRANIAN-LIBYAN TALKS 
LD261338 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 26 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] The following joint statement was issued today on the talks between the 
delegation of the Iranian Revolution Council and the foreign liaison office at the 
secretariat of the People's General Congress: 


In the period from 4 Muharram 1389, after the death of the Prophet equivalent to 

23 November 1979, to 7 Muharram 1389 after the death of ithe Prophet equivalent to 

26 November 1979, a delegation representing the Iranian Islamic Revolution Council 

and the Iranian Foreign Ministry visited the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, 
The delegation consisted of Brother Mohamad !Ali Hadi, Hojjat ol-Eslam Hadi Madrasi and 
Mohamad Saleh Hoseyn. During the visit the delegation members were received by the 
brother commander of the Great 1 September revolution, the secretariat of the People's 
General Congress and the revolutionary committees. The delegation held lengthy and 
deep discussion which were characterized by a spirit of fraternity, cordiality and 
complete understanding on all topics raised, 


The most important topics discussed were; The entrenchment of strategic relations 
between the two revolutions in the Jamahiriyah and Iran, the Zionist and imperialist 
threats directed against the Islamic revolution in Iran because of the firm stand 
adopted by the Iranian Muslim people toward imperialist designs and their demand for 
the surrender of the agent shah, who is using the United States as a haven, 


The fraternal delegation also heard a statement from the brother commander of the 
revolution fully explaining the concept of the Great 1 September revolution and the 
people's authority principle which is in force in the Jamahiriyah, as expounded by the 
Green Book, The two sides affirm the following points: 


1. The two progressive Islamic revolutions in the Jamahiriyah and Iran are allied 
naturally and strategically. 


2. The two sides affirm the importance of the liberation of Palestine from Zionism, 
which usurped Palestinian territory, and from imperialism and foreign monopolies, They 
also affirm the need for the return of the Palestine people to their land and homes, 


3. The Jamahiriyah considers any American or other military aggression against the 
Islamic Iranian republic is an aggression against the Jemahiriyah itself. 


4, The two sides affirm the Arab-Iranian fraternity and the need for the consolidation 
of this fraternity and its development for the good of the two nations: Arab and Iranian, 


5. The two sides consider any aggression against the Iranian revolution as aggression 
against all Islamic peoples and forces of the revolution in the world. 


6. The two sides strongly denounce the Camp David agreements and all their consequences. 
They ask the Egyptian people to act to topple the defeated Egyptian regime and to 

return Egypt to the Arab arena in the conflict against world Zionism, colonialism and 
imperialism, 


7. The two sides affirm that the two revolutions in Iran and Jamahiriyah constitute a 
firm ground for an Islamic awakening which will embrace the entire Islamic world and 
Place full authority in the hands of the Islamic masses, which will act as a tool for 
the realization of comprehensive unity. 


8. The two sides affirm their support for the Islamic and Arab world liberation 
movements in their struggle against colonialism, imperialism and Zionism.

Delegation Press Conference 
LD261603 Tripoli JANA in English 1428 GMT 26 Nov 79 LD 


(Text] Tripoli, November 26, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Iranian delegation currently 
visiting the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah praised goed relations binding 
the two Islamic peoples in the Jamahiriyah and Iran, and the current positive development 
of relations. 


The delegation also lauded the Great First of September Revolution's courageous policy 
vis-a-vis the Iranian people and their Islamic Revolution which ousted the hirelings of 
imperialism, the shah, and delivered Iran from the rule of reaction. 


The head of the Iranian delegation said ina press conference held yesterday evening at 
the Foreign Liaison Bureau in Tripoli that the delegition's meetings in the Jamahiriyah, 
especially with Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and Staff-Major [words indistinct] affirmed 
the solidarity of the Great First of September Revolution with the Iranian people and 
their Islamic Revclution. 


He said genuine revolutions inevitably agree on the principle of freedom. Such agreement 
existed between the Al-Fatah and Iranian Islamic revolutions even before the latter's 
victory, because the causes of freedom and justice are inseparable. 


The head of the Iranian delegation pointed out that the shah's lackey regime had been 
backed by American imperialism and imposed on the Muslim people of Iran, He declared 
that the struggle against American imperialism and the revelation of its intentions 
against the Islamic world before the eruption of the Islamic Revolution in Iran was 
directed against its treasonous puppet, the shah. 


He said that American policy relied on threats and terrorism because it was based on the 
logic of force even in its relations with other countries. He stressed that the threats 
directed against the Iranian people were quite normal, coming from imperialism. He 
insisted that such threats and show of force would not intimidate the Iranian people 

who are prepared to give their life for the principles of the victorious revolution and 
for freedom and dignity. 


The Iranian delegation warned all peoples against the usage of the dollar in their dealings 
with America for she is using it to affect their economy and stability. The Iranian 
delegation said: "The real cause of the conflict existing between us and America is that 
we want to try the shah in order to show the world the crimes this despot committed 
against the Islamic peoples in general and the Iranians in particular. It is not at all 
our aim to hold hostages." 


The delegation pledged the continuous support of the Iranian people and Islamic Revolution 
to the Arab people and its just cause: Palestine. To this effect they referred to Iran's 
break-off of relations with the enemy and the establishment of brotherly strategic relations 
with the Palestinian revolution in order to confront Zionism and imperialism. 


To conclude the press conference, the delegation expressed the Iranian people's support 
for the Lebanese, for freedom for the full liberation of the Lebanese soil and for the 
independence and unity of Lebanon.

Delegation's Departure 
LD271020 Tripoli JANA in English 0800 GMT 27 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Nov. 27, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Iranian delegation, led by Dr. Mohammad 
'Ali Hadi, left Tripoli yesterday evening after a brief visit to the Jamahiriyah during 
which he held official talks on promoting bilateral cooperation. 


In a statement to our correspondent, Mr. 'Ali Hadi expressed his satisfaction at the visit 
which, he said, has allowed the delegation to exchange views on many issues concerning 
the Islamic nation, to explain the Iranian Islamic Revolution's conflict with American 
imperialism and to meet Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the lst of September 
Revolution and Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Jallud. 


Referring to the joint communique issued by the Iranian delegationad the Foreign Liaison 
Bureau of the General Secretariat of the General People's Congress, the head of the 
Iranian delegation said that the communique affirmed the unity of views between the 
Jamahiriyah and Iran on all current problems. The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab 
People's Congress issued a statement after a meeting between the members of the Iranian 
delegation, resident members of the Permanent Secretariat and representatives of the 
liberation movements, inthe Jamahiriyah. The statement affirmed the solidarity [word 
indistinct] between the Arab and Islamic revolutions. 


The head of the Iranian delegation, Dr. Mohammad 'Ali Hadi, made a statement in which he 
commended the Islamic humanitarian revival and development in the Jamahiriyah in all fields. 
The members of the Iranian delegation were seen off at Tripoli International Airport 

by the head of the Foreign Liaison Bureau, the undersecretary for municipalities, head 

of the Office for Ideological Education and a representative from the Protocol Department.

[Text] The victory of the popular revolution in Iran is a victory for Islam, both in 
its revolutionary and progressive framework, a framework which is hostile to dictatorial 


tyranny, capitalist exploitation and the reactionary forces and their alliance with 
international colonialism.

Through the popular revolution in Iran, Islam has manifested its effective revolutionary 
presence as a world force which embodies for nations the meaning of freedom, justice 
and equality. 


Destroying the links of injustice within Iranian society and demolishing the Peacock 
Throne, which was the greatest structure symbolizing colonialist subservience, dependence 
and protecting imperialist interests in the area, could not have taken place except 
through the inciting instrument called for by Islam, namely, the belief in sacred jihad 
as an expression of revolution and rejection of injustice, inactivity and exploitation 
and a confirmation of the independence of the Iranian people and their right to be free 
to exploit their revenues in realizing welfare. This confirms that the essence of revo- 
lution and rebellion against the corrupt state of affairs and an incitement of the masses 
to stage a popular revolution and confront colonialist challenges, however strong they 
may be. 


International colonialism has seen in the triumph of the Iranian revolution a new revo- 
lutionary upsurge of the Islamic nations, and this means the beginning of the end of 
colonialist monopoly interests, which bleed the resources of the Muslims at the expense 
of their underdevelopment and poverty. Muslims in international society come within the 
framework of Third World nations, which have suffered from underdevelopment and colonial- 
ist domination for many centuries. That this section of Third World countries is turning 
into a flaming revolution in implementing the proper concepts of Islam that seek the 
realization of political, economic and social freedoms, comes as a signal that the star 
of international colonialism is about to be eclipsed, The colonialist blockade of the 
Iranian popular revolution and the conspiracies against this revolution in order to ex- 
tinguish the flame of Islam in Iran are motivated by the collapsing star of international 
colonialism, which nowadays is headed by U.S, imperialism. 


The United States was not content with bleeding the resources of the Muslim Iranian 
people during the years of the defunct regime of the shah, but today conspires against 
the revolution of the Iranian people by sheltering the criminal of the age, who is con- 
sidered a terrorist, a murderer and a fugitive. One of the most basic rights and human 
ethics acknowledged by international agreements impels the United States to hand over 
this terrorist to the Muslim Iranian people. But U.S, imperialism!s stubbornness and 
determination to provoke the feelings of the Iranian people and not submit to their 
right to have the deposed shah handed over to them gives rise to suspicions about the 
hostile intentions of U.S, imperialism. It is not surprising for the United States to 
resort to the use of armed force in order to interfere in the internal affairs of Iran 
and try and overthrow the popular Iranian revolution in order to once again restore the 
deposed shah to the Peacock Throne, particularly when there is a serious U.S, precedent 
in this connection. This was when it intervened with its intelligence armies in Iran, 
toppled the Mossadeq revolution and returned the deposed shah to Iran from his Italian 
exile, 


The United States is making a show of force off Iranian shores and massing aircraft 
carriers off the Iranian coast. It places the NATO alliance forces ina state of alert 
and readiness to intervene in the event the United States decides to embark on military 
action against Iran. If the United States persists in its stubbornness and insists on 
military intervention in Iran, the Iranian people will not be alone on the battlefield 
because the Iranian people's revolution, as we said, is a prop for the masses of Muslims. 
The Muslims will make sure that its flame will remain constantly ablaze.

The Great 1 September Revolution is linked to the Iranian people's revolution by anun- 
changing principled and strategic relationship, They are brought together by the unity

Based on these principled and firm relations which link the Great 1 September Revolution
