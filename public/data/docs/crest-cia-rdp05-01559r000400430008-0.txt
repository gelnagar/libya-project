[Mahmud Bakri Report: “Major U.S. Embassy Report

[Text] AL-SHA‘B has obtained the contents of a report 
the U.S. Embassy in Cairo prepared and sent to the U.S. 
Defense Department some time ago whose full details 
the Egyptian authorities succeeded in obtaining. The 
report deals with the situation in the Egyptian Armed 
Forces and Egyptian Army strategy after the signing of 
the peace treaty with Israel. The report indicates that the 
Egyptian Army strategy, especially since President Husni 
Mubarak assumed power after Al-Sadat’s assassination, 
has been to introduce advanced weapons to the art of 
military combat, and that Egyptian arms in this regard 
have not been confined to those imported from the 
United States, since Egyptian military officials and their 
political leadership realize that the United States would 
not allow the Egyptian Army’s modernization except 
within a framework that the U.S. Administration would 
allow, and which in turn would not affect Israel’s secu- 
rity. The Egyptian Army disapproves utterly of the 
special and distinct military relationship between the 
"United States and Israel. 

The report goes on to say: In view of this, Egypt has 
decided to seek assistance from the European states, 
both Western and Eastern; it has also initiated military 
cooperation with the Soviet Union. 

The report stresses that cooperation will rapidly double 
within the coming years. The report therefore warned the 
U.S. Government about the consequences of allowing 
Soviet-Egyptian cooperation to reach its maximum with- 
out any clear U.S. intervention to obstruct this form of 
military cooperation! 

The report reviewed the strategies of the Egyptian Army 
at this stage, indicating that Egypt has turned to the 
system of integrated military cities, a kind of modern- 
ization designed to give a secret nature to military 
training and to maintain the continuity of military lines, 
as is now happening in the two cities of Junayfah and 
Shallufah. This is in addition to a building plan being 
prepared by the Egyptian Defense Ministry covering the 
areas of Al-‘Amiriyah, Dahshur, Inshas, and Al-Hayk-

streets.

groups is against the regime in Egypt. He said that they 
are trying to control the professional unions in order to 
achieve their objectives. He affirmed that the mission of 
university professors is educating the youths. He denied 
demanding the cancellation of university academic staff 
clubs, but he demanded that the board of directors of 
four academic clubs be dissolved for violating the laws. 
They are the academic staff clubs of the Cairo, Alexan- 
dria, Al-Mansurah, and Asyut Universities. 

The minister said that the opposition forces want to win 
the support of national establishments and unions in 
order to use them to achieve their objectives of causing 
incitement and casting doubts. He said that party activ- 
ity has its own sectors, and that nobody has the right to 
penetrate the national institutions to distract them from 
their national mission. 

The minister expressed appreciation for the university 
professors. He said he holds the greatest respect for 
them. He said he blames some of them for encouraging 
students to stage demonstrations outside universities. 
This disturbs security and order, although they know 
that the demonstrating students will be firmly and 
strongly confronted. 

The minister urged the professors to teach the students 
the right way to express their opinions through the 
legitiamte channels. 

Concerning the implementation of the emergency law, 
the minister said that it made it possible to arrest drug 
dealers who annually purchase $6 billion worth of drugs 
to destroy citizens’ lives. The minister responded to a 
request by the Al-Mansurah University academic staff to 
establish a services unit inside the campus, including a 
unit for car licenses and passports. [passage omitted]
