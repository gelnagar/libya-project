Summary (AI): Qadhdhafi addresses the Tunisian Chamber of Deputies in Tunis on 9 December 1988, speaking on colonialism, Arab unity with Libya and Algeria, Israel and the Palestinians, and the 1986 U.S. raid on Libya. The speech is foreign policy and rhetoric, with no Tripoli-specific sites or events.
• Qadhdhafi said the 1881 Bardo Treaty spirit still exists and claimed Libya and Algeria were vetoed from uniting.
• He said Libyan aircraft were being blocked from flying to Nicaragua, Mauritania and Morocco.
• He referred to missiles with a 4,000 km range allegedly targeted at Tunisia and Libya, and to U.S. lifting an embargo on cluster bomb sales to Israel.
• He said Libya has Soviet-made arms, radar, missiles and aircraft, and that the Libyan population is about 3 million.
• 1986: Qadhdhafi refers to a U.S. aggression against Libya with 178 planes, which he says Libya repulsed; no Tripoli site is named.
