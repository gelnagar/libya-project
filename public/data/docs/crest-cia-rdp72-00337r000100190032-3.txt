2 February 1970 

MEMORANDUM FOR: Director of Current Intelligence 
SUBJECT : Foreknowledge of Libyan Coup 

REFERENCE : Mr. Maury's Memorandum of 23 January 
(OLC 70-0061) 

1. We have searched our files and found no reference 
to an Iranian warning of the September Libyan coup. This 
does not necessarily mean that some such warning was not 
passed on, but in any event it did not reach us. The Shah 
has frequently alluded to his concern for the future of 
conservative Arab leaders but has never been specific on 
threats to them. 

2. The number of Soviet naval ships in the Mediter- 
ranean was at an all time high just before the coup. They 
were, however, engaged in a large scale exercise for the 
benefit of senior Soviet naval commanders and there still 
appears to have been no connection at all with events in 
Libya. 

3. In our post-mortem on the coup we found no infor- 
mation which even in hindsight could have served to indicate 
that it was coming. Any judgments at the time on a possible 
turnover would have centered on the possible abdication of 
the King or on some move by the Shahli brothers to seize 
control. 

4, It is our understanding (via the DIA Libyan cur- 
rent analyst) that DIA, which has received the same query 
from Mr. Blandford (similarly unspecific), will probably 
provide the same response. 

5. Nor are we aware of any Iranian warning in the 
hands of our counterparts in State, INR.
