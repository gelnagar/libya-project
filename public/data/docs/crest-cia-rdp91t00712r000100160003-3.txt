Summary (AI): A December 1983 NPIC imagery note reports increased security at Qadhafi's headquarters and living complex at Tarabulus Barracks Brigade and Battalion Headquarters in northwest Tripoli. Roadblocks, barriers, SA-8 missile launchers, a ZSU-23/4 and tanks were deployed there.
• At least six barriers containing filled barrels blocked roads into the compound.
• Large concrete blocks were placed in maze fashion along interior roads at four major entrances.
• Units garrisoned included one T-72 tank battalion, one BM-21 rocket launcher battalion, one BMP mechanized infantry company, and an air defence unit with SA-8, SA-9 and antiaircraft guns.
• Coordinates given: 32-52-28N 013-10-07E.
• 1983-11-24: One of the roadblocks was already present; none were present on 8 November 1983.
• 1983-12-16: NPIC reported two deployed SA-8 launchers, one ZSU-23/4, several ZPU-4 guns, and at least nine tanks and APCs at the facility; SA-8s had been there since mid-summer 1983.

“1. SIGNIFICANCE: INCREASED SECURITY MEASURES WERE OBSERVED AT 
COL. MOHAMMAR QADHAFI'S HEADQUARTERS IN TARABULUS, LIBYA. 
(SWN) 

2. REMARKS: INCREASED PHYSICAL SECURITY MEASURES WERE OBSERVED 
‘ KT COL. MOHAMMAR QADHAFI'S HEADQUARTERS AND LIVING COMPLEX AT 
TARABULUS BARRACKS Co AND BATTALION HEADQUARTERS IN 
NORTHWEST TARABULUS ROADBLOCKS AND 25X1 
BARRIERS HAVE BEEN PLACED ACROSS ALL ROADS LEADING INTO THE 
FACILITY AND SEVERAL ARMORED VEHICLES AND AIR DEFENSE WEAPONS 
WERE DISPERSED THROUGHOUT THE FACILITY. (SWN) 

AT LEAST SIX BARRIERS (GRAPHIC 1) CONTAINING WHAT APPEARS TO 
BE FILLED BARRELS BLOCKED VARIOUS ROADS LEADING INTO THE 
COMPOUND AND LARGE, PROBABLE CONCRETE BLOCKS HAD BEEN PLACED 
IN MAZE FASHION ALONG INTERIOR ROADS AT FOUR MAJOR ENTRANCES 
(GRAPHIC 2, TWO NOT ON GRAPHIC). EQUIPMENT. OBSERVED INCLUDE | 
TWO DEPLOYED SA-8 SURFACE-TO-AIR MISSILE TLARS (GRAPHIC lL) 
ONE ZSU 23/4 SELF-PROPELLED ANTIAIRCRAFT GUN, SEVERAL ZPU-4 
ANTIAIRCRAFT GUNS, AND AT LEAST NINE TANKS AND ARMORED 
PERSONNEL CARRIERS. ONE OF THE ROADBLOCKS WAS PRESENT AS 
EARLY AS 24 NOVEMBER. HOWEVER, NONE WERE PRESENT ON 8 
NOVEMBER. THE SA-8 TLARS HAVE BEEN DEPLOYED IN THE FACILITY 
SINCE MID-SUMMER 1983. (SWN) 

TARABULUS BARRACKS BRIGADE AND BATTALION HEADQUARTERS IS A 
HEAVILY FORTIFIED GARRISON WITH NUMEROUS TANK BUNKERS, 
UNDERGROUND STORAGE FACILITIES, AND GROUND FORCE UNITS. 

UNITS GARRISONED AT THE INSTALLATION INCLUDED ONE T~72 TANK 
BATTALION, ONE BM~21 MULTIPLE ROCKET LAUNCHER BATTALION, ONE 
BMP-EQUIPPED MECHANIZED INFANTRY COMPANY, AND ONE AIR DEFENSE 
UNIT CONTAINING SA-8, SA-9, AND: ANTIAIRCRAFT GUNS. (SWN)
