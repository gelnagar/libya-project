Summary (AI): A Jack Anderson radio broadcast from May 1982 says Libya's southern neighbour Chad is split by factions, that Chad's president ordered 14,000 Libyan troops out the previous year, and predicts Qaddafi will send troops back. It concerns Libyan foreign policy, not Tripoli itself.
• Chad's president reportedly ordered 14,000 Libyan troops out of Chad in 1981.
• Chad's president has been secretly meeting with Libyan leaders because of internal rivalry, according to the broadcast.

DATE May 31, 1982 9:00 AM civ Washington, DC 

SUBJECT Libya and Chad 

JACK ANDERSON: Libya's southern neighbor Chad is split 
by two different violent factions. The country's president 
ordered 14,000 Libyan troops out of Chad last year. But because 
of internal rivalry the president has been secretly meeting with 
Libyan leaders. 

I predict Libyan leader Qaddafi will seize this opportu- 
nity to send his troops back to Chad.
