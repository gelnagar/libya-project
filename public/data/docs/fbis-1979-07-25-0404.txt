LIBYA

BULGARIA'S LUKANOV MAKES STATEMENT ON DEPARTURE 
LD242337 Tripoli JANA in English 1810 GMT 2!; Jul 79 LD 
[Text] Tripoli, July 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Mr Andrey Lukanov, the Bulgarian depu- 
ty chairman of the Council of Ministers left Tripoli this morning after a four-day visit 
to the Jamahiriyah. 
The Bulgarian guest made a statement to our correspondent in which he praised the speedy 
development and giant achievements realized at all levels by the Great First of September 
Revolution. He also expressed his pleasure at the positive outcome of his visit affirm- 
ing that the talks helped further cooperation and bilateral relations.
