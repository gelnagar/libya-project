LIBYA MAY HALT OIL, WITHDRAW DEPOSITS FROM U.S., UK 
LDO90742 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 9 May 80 
[Text] Tripoli--Brother Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi stated that Libya is seriously 
thinking of stopping oil to Britain and America as well as withdrawing its deposits 
from the two countries, 
In a statement made today he said that Libya will ask for compensation of hundreds of 
millions of dollars from Britain, West Germany and Italy because of the great losses 
which Libya sustained then they turned Libyan territory into an arena for international 
terrorism during World War II for their own interest without Libya having any interest 
or anything to do with it. 
The brother colonel emphasized that unless the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah obtains such compensation through negotiations, we will lay our hands on 
anything that will compensate for the losses we sustained,
