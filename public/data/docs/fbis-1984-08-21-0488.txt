Joint Committee Protocol With GDR Signed 
LD201535 Tripoli JANA in English 1407 GMT 20 Aug 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 23 Dhulgidah, 20 August, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The protocol of the agreement of the sixth 
session of the meetings of the Libyan Arab-German Joint Com- 
mittee was signed last night in Tripoli. The protocol was signed 
by a Werner Buschmann, light industry minister of the German 
Democratic Republic and the secretary of the General People’s 
Committee for Planning. 


The protocol stipulates on strengthening technical, economic and 
scientific cooperation between the two countries and a cooper- 
ation agreement about agriculture, industrial, communications 
and naval transport, and railways in addition to exchange of 
technical and training expertise in different fields. 


The secretary of the General People’s Committee for Planning 
delivered a speech in which he expressed gratitude for reaching 
these outcomes which are considered a serious step along the path 
of the strong relations between the two friendly countries. 


Werner Buschmann, light industry minister of the German 
Democratic Republic delivered a speech praising the develop- 
ment of relations between the two friendly countries. He said, 
“The signature of the protocol of the agreement coincides with 
our celebrations of the 35th anniversary of the estabiishment of 
the German Democratic Republic and the 15th anniversary of 
the Great Al-Fatih Revolution. These are historical occasions by 
which a lot of progress has been achieved to the peoples of the 
two countries”. 


He went on to say that “we moved forward visible steps for 
strengthening cooperation between us through the meetings of 
the Joint Committee along the path of the joint struggle”. 


Buschmanan Expresses Satisfaction 
LD201545 Tripoli JANA in English 1411 GMT 20 Aug 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 23 Dhulgidah, 20 August, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Werner Buschmann, light industry min- 
ister of the German Democratic Republic, and the head of the 
German side in the Libyan Arab-German Democratic Joint 
Committee has expressed satisfaction over the outcome achieved 
by the Joint Committee for Technical, Economic and Scientific 
Cooperation between the Jamahiriyah and the German Demo- 
cratic Republic which ended its meetings, last night. He added 
that, “We agreed on developing cooperation between our coun- 
tries in economic, scientific and technical fields and taking 
suitable measures to implement this programme for the common 
benefit of the two peoples”. 


He confirmed in a statement to pressmen, the readiness of his 
country to extend the scope of cooperation with the Jamahiriyah 
in cultural, scientific, and economic fields. He also referred to 
the success of talks he carried out in Tripoli. 


Werner Buschmann, pointed out the importance of developing 
cooperation and strengthening political dialogue, especially after 
the deterioration of the tense international situation due to the 
policy of confrontation and threats followed by the American 
Government and allies. 


He confirmed on the needs of coordination between countries to 
avoid dangers caused by imperialist threats and contributing 
towards laying the basis of international peace. He concluded his 
press conference by expressing thanks and the accompanied 
delegation for the generous hospitality and reception they 
received and wishes for the Libyan Arab people for achieving 
more progress.

fi Proposes Reconciliation With Sudan 
GF201640 Dubayy AL-BAYAN in Arabic 20 Aug 84 P 1 


[Qassi al-Darwish dispatch from Paris} 


[Text] AL-BAYAN has learned from reliable sources that Libyan 
President Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi has expressed a readiness for 
reconciliation with Sudan on condition that Khartoum closes 
down the opposition radio directed at Libya and stops training 
Libyan opposition forces in its camps. The Libyan president's 
envoy has conveyed these terms to Sudanese President Ja‘far 
Numayri in the past few days. According to these sources this is 
within the framework of a radical change in Libyan foreign 


policy and perhaps domestic policy too which began to take form 
after the unsuccessful mutiny attempt which Tripoli witnessed 2 
months ago.
