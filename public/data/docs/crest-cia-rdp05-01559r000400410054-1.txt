LIBYA 

Al-Qadhdhafi ‘Greatly Angered’ by Attack on Kurds 
LD181711 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 
18 Aug 86 

[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has sent a message to 
the president and prime minister of Turkey. Here is the text: 

The operation carried out by the Turkish Air Force to pursue and 
kill Kurds inside the territory of Iraq is not acceptable to us, and 
all the people of the world denounce and reject it because it 
constitutes a form of annihilation. The operation has also resulted 
in the destruction of the Kurds’ homes and villages, and the death 
of their women and children, like the annihilation of the Arab 
and the African peoples being carried out by Zionism and the 
racist ruling regime in South Africa. 

The Kurds are members of the Pan-Arab Command for the 
Revolutionary Forces on the one hand, and on the other they 
constitute a nation that should be respected and that has a right 
to unity and independence. It is a sister nation to the Turkish 
nation, the Arab nation, and the Iranian nation, and the denial 
of its national freedom deprives us of the right to defend our 
pan-Arabism, and the denial of its freedom denies us the right to 
defend our freedom. I was greatly angered by this action which 
I hope will not be repeated. 

However, if it were a case of ordinary crimes committed by 
ordinary individuals, it is unreasonable that the response should 
be thus. I regret that the Turkish Government has become 
involved in a conspiracy hatched by the Iraqi Government in 
order to maltreat [ar tankil] our Kurdish brothers. This is a 
shameful and unprecedented collusion with the Iraqi Govern- 
ment.

Red Cross Suspends Mercy Flights in South 
LD181254 Kuwait KUNA in English 1220 GMT 18 Aug 86 

[Text] Geneva, Aug 18 (KUNA) — The International of the Red 
Cross Committee has suspended mercy flights in southern 
Sudan, a spokesman for the Swiss humanitarian agency said 
Monday. 

In particular, an airlift of emergency food supplies for the town 
of Waw had been stopped, after only one day of operation. 

The (2spokesman) said that the decision to stop the flights had 
been taken on Friday, before the shooting down of the Sudan Air 
Fokker friendship plane. The Red Cross cited “the state of 
insecurity in the region” as the reason for the cancellation. 

In the short time that the airlift had been operating the ICRC 
had managed to bring in 44 tonnes of maize from Uganda to feed 
the townspeople and thousands of displaced people in the area.
