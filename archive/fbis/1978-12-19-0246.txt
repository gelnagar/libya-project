{Excerpts ] Tripoli, 16 Dec, JANA--The General People's Congress commenced its fourth 
regular session at 10 o!clock this morning to draft the resolutions and recummendations 
adopted by the basic popular congresses after detailed and thorough discussions on 17 
items on their agenda. 


Upon arrival of Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the lst of September revolution 
and secretary general of the General People's Congress, [words indistinct] (God is great) 
anthem after which verses of the holy Koran were read, 


Then Mr al-Knarratti Ahmad delivered a speech on behalf of the National Union of the 
Moroccan Popular Forces, at the beginning of which he greeted the leader of the revolution 
and the Jamahiriyah people on behalf of the Moroccan freedom fighters and popular forces, 


Mr, al-Kharratti referred to the efforts being made by these forces in the way of realizing 
an Arab socialist homeland, and said that the national union was struggling in difficult 
circumstances but was destined to attain victory against opportunism, and the deception 

of the masses, 


Mr al-Kharratti called for the consolidation of cooperation among the Arab liberation 
organizations, the consolidation of their programmes of struggle, the liberation of the 
general Arab situation, and agreement on a unified strategy with the progressive move- 
ments in the world, 


Concluding his speech, Mr al-Kharratti affirmed the Moroccan popular forces! rejection of 
all capitulationist solutions whichever source they came from, 


Some of the people!s congresses called for the abolition of compulsory military service 
and adoption of urban militarisation and the armed masses, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi noted 
that the basic peoples congresses had themselves decided on compulsory conscription 
and that later the idea of urban militarisation had appeared, calling for the armed 
masses! swift emergence and the ending of compulsory conscription and the regular army, 


He said: "It is not appn priate that you decide on abolishing compulsory military 
conscription and the regular army bearing in mind that you have not yet militarised 
your urban centers," 


However, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that compulsory conscription and the regular army 
would end automatically when urban militarisation and the armed masses were established, 


He said: "We will see....during the next sessions whether the (?popular congresses!) will 
approve urban militarisation," 


He affirmed that the question was one which involved the concept of self-defense, defense 
of the family, farm and the nation,

He said: "Compulsory military service is in the interest of all Libyans, since it allows 
them to [word indistinct] themselves and their interests, and the evasion of military service 
is a result of abhorrent past history when Italy enslaved this country and forcibly con- 
scripted its men to use them in fighting Ethiopians within the context of the Italian 
State's colonial interests, But now compulsory conscription is in the interest of every 
citizen for it would enable him to defend himself, his family, house, village and also his

the citizen tries to escape it and adopts a false birthdate, so as to avoid being 
conscripted, but it is rather a national duty and the responsibility of every citizen," 


In this connection Colonel al-Qadhdhafi indicated that "the Zionists have almost approached

to defend his homeland should raise the white flag up and accept to become a slave," 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi emphasized that "the Libyans should succeed in mobilizing armies in the 
cities before they decide to terminate compulsory conscription",
