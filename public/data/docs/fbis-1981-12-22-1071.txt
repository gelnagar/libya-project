PAPER URGES VIGILANCE AGAINST "CHALLENGES '

LD212224 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1815 GMT 21 Dec 81 

[Text] In a frontpage article under the caption "A Warning," the newspaper AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR 
writes: One of the basic responsibilities resulting from the enjoyment of freedom and the 
exercise of politics and sovereignty in the society of popular authority is that the 
Jamahiriyah citizen who, thanks to the revolution, now has authority, wealth and arms, 
should be vigilant and alert in the face of the growing signs of external challenges. 

He should fully understand their causes, motives and objectives. He also should monitor 
their development so that as soon as they take place he can make his revolutionary decision 
and take deterrent action on a level compatible to that of the gravity of the escalating 
aggressiveness of the challenges which aim to abort the prevalent climate of freedom, 

to detract from the popular authority and to obstruct the wheels of the historic cultural 
development taking place in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


The society of popular authority has crushed the walls of the monopolization of politics 
forever. Matters of politics and sovereignty are no longer discussed in the palaces behind 
closed doors. They are now discussed in the open inside the basic popular congresses where 
once more the model is being formulated of the free man who is capable of shouldering his 
responsibilities in defense of his authority, wealth and arms. 


As a result of our careful analysis of the signs of external challenges and to put things 
in their true perspective, we must say that the Jamahiriyah people are facing a real war. 
It is not necessary for the tools of wars to be arms alone. They also cover all the 
economic, propaganda, political and psychological weapons which together pave the way 

for the armed battle with the enemy. 


So far, our people have been faced with economic weapons, such as the U.S. imperialist 
decision to recall the U.S. companies and expertise, to impose economic pressure and a 
blockade and to instigate the NATO member countries to adopt similar economic decisions. 


There also is the reactionary group which also brandishes the weapon of economic pressure 
by glutting the world markets with oil and cutting the oil prices, a matter which helps 
to enchance the U.S. imperialist economy and to harm the economic interests of the 
oil-producing peoples. All this takes place under the umbrella of a declared economic 
war imposed by imperialism against the people of the Jamahiriyah. 


There also are the venomous propaganda weapons whose rancorous pictures we see in the 
press of the imperialists and their lackeys in the world who seek to defame the people of 
the Jamahiriyah and the commander of the revolution and to level false charges and lies 
against them. This is some kind of black propaganda to which people usually resort to 
create pretexts and to prepare the ground for imposing an armed battle. 


The question, however, is why the war and what are its objectives and dimensions? To 
answer this question, we say that had it not been for the pioneering applications of the 
cultural principles of the Green Book, the establishment of the people's authority and the 
liquidation of the areas of exploitation and the corrupt reactionary rightist cells; 

had it not been for the Jamahiriyan's support for the causes of freedom and liberation -- 
the Jamahiriyah becoming a haven for international resistance against imperialism: had it 
not been for the Great 1 September Revolution's championing of the causes of Palestine, 
Arab unity and the popular revolution; had it not been for its instigation of others 

to stand up against the U.S.-Zionist aggressive offensive, to strike at the monopolist 
interests and U.S. bases and to force the foreign fleets to remain away from Arab 

shores, thereby safeguarding national security and consolidating Arab independence -- 

had it not been for all these matters, we would not have had any problem with the 

United States.

But the United States wants to turn us into a subservient lackey just like the other 
agent regimes in the Arab homeland. It refuses to let the people's choose their own 
political, economic and social systems in complete freedom, to affirm their true 
freedom to exercise popular authority and take control of their wealth and to liquidate 
the pillars of exploitation. 


Hence, it is essential for the people of the Jamahiriyah to choose one of two options: 
either to abandon all their principles to avoid clashingwith the United States or to 
uphold their principles and accept th2 challenge in the face of the staunchest imperialist 
power, even if that means their total annihiliation, just for the sake of their honor and 
glory and dignity. 


The Jamahiriyah people have chosen to tie their fate and existence to the continuity of 
their principles and to affirm their constant perpetuation, even at the cost of fighting 
the whole world or death. We prefer to die rather than live as slaves underthe feet 

of the United States. 


In conclusion, AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR writes: As long as the people of the Jamahiriyah are 
people of principles, values and sublime ideals, one of the priorities of facing up to 

the U.S. challenge is for us to exert further pressure on the wound which pains U.S. 
imperialism and lead it to inevitable doom. We must affirm our revolutionary choices 
symbolized in the consolidation of the popular resistance and promote them both internally 
and externally. We also must continue our attacks against the elements of exploitation 
and the remnants of the rotten reactionary right and speed up the implementation of the 
concept of armed people and the society of free partners. We also must uphold the 
defense of freedom in the international field. These are the essential duties of the 
revolutionary committees. 


We must not overlook the fact that there is a wide front of brothers and friends who 
were provoked by the aggressive U.S. imperialist designs against the Jamahiriyah and 
thus rushed to stand by us, aiding and supporting us. Undoubtedly, we will triumph with 
own own power and that of the peoples supporting us. 


We address a strong warning to the agent traitors, the naive and unimportant, the 
friends of the United States. Woe unto them. Death for imperialism and victory for us. 


The struggle will continue. 


KUWAITI PRIME MINISTER ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 


LD192142 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1700 GMT 19 Dec 81 


[Excerpts] Kuwait heir apparent and Prime Minister Shaykh Sa'd al-Abdallah as-Sabah 
arrived in Tripoli this afternoon for a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiryah during a tour that includes Tunisia, Algeria, Morocco and Mauritania. 
There was a big welcome at Tripoli International Airport and Staff Maj Abd as-Salam 
Jallud was there to welcome him. 


In a statement to newsmen on arrival, Shayk Sa'd al-Abdallah as-Sabah said: I and the 
members of the delegation accompanying me are happy to visit the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and to meet our brothers in this steadfast country. We in 
Kuwait are aware of the degree of dangers and the size of the challenges and pressures 
faced by every Arab country. We think it is time for us to work together to consolidate 
Arab solidarity and to stand in one rank to face the enemies of the Arab nation.

Jallud Welcomes Visit 
LD201506 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1815 GMT 19 Dec 81 


[Text] Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud said the visit of Shaykh Sa'd al-'Abdallah 
as-Sabah to the Jamahiriyah assumes special significance at this time as the dimensions 

of the imperialist challenges to the Arab nation are increasing. Major Jallud told 

newsmen after the arrival of Shaykh Sa'd at Tripoli International Airport that this visit 
will be an opportunity to exchange views with our brother Kuwaitis on the need to understand 
Arab reality and accept the challenges in a much more decisive and serious way. 


We continue: The Arab people in the Jamahiriyah and Kuwait are championing the path of 
unity of Arab action against the enemy of the Arab nation which is threatening its 
(?independence and existence). He added that this visit will afford a good opportunity 
to talk about issues important to our nation because the Arab future is one and Arab 
responsibility is one. He said: We must set aside definitions and statements and 
confront challenges with action. He stressed that these challenges are aimed at the 
existence of the Arab nation, that this nation from the ocean to the Gulf can exist only 
under Arab nationalism and that the illusions championed by the capitulationists about 
the possibility of coexisting with the enemy are meant to make this nation submit and 
bring it to its knees. 


Staff Maj Brother ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud explained that in the Jamahiriyah we follow 
with interest the (?role) of our brothers in Kuwait and their activities.

Joint Communique Issued 
LD211642 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 21 Dec 81 


["Text" of joint communique issued at the end of the 19-21 December visit to Libya of 
Kuwaiti Crown Prince and Prime Minister Shaykh Sa'd al-'Abdallah as-Salim as-Sabah] 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 Dec (JANA) -- In response to an official invitation from Staff Maj 
"Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, his highness the crown prince and prime minister of Kuwait, 
Shaykh Sa'd al-'Abdallah as-Salim as-Sabah, paid an official visit to the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyan from 19-21 December 1981. His highness the guest and 
the accompanying delegation were accorded a fraternal and official welcome demonstrating 
the depth of the fraternal ties between the two countries and their fraternal peoples. 


On this occasion, Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 1 September 
Revolution, received in audience His Highness Shaykh Sa'd al-'Abdallah as-Sabah. His highness 
conveyed to Brother Colonel al-Qadhdafi a verbal message from his brother His Highness 

Shaykh Jabir al-Ahmad as-Sabah, amir of the State of Kuwait, containing expressions 

of cordiality and true fraternity and the desire to consolidate cooperation between the 

two fraternal countries. 


Talks chaired on the Libyan side by Brother Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and on 

the Kuwaiti side by Crown Prince and Prince and Prime Minister Shaykh Sa'd al-'Abdallah 
as-Sabah were held in an atmosphere of understanding. The two sides reviewed the strong 
bilateral ties between the two fraternal countries and expressed their complete satis- 
faction with what has been achieved through joint action and continuous cooperation. 

They reaffirmed their determination to take further steps to expand the scope of this 
cooperation in the economic, oil, investment, information, education and cultural domains 
and invarious other fields to realize all the constructive aspirations of the two countries 
and their fraternal peoples. 


The two sides discussed the current situation in the Arab region, particularly the 
Palestinian question, the continuing Zionist occupation of Arab land and the Zionist 
enemy's expansionist schemes and criminal practices against the Arab nation both inside 
and outside the occupied territory. In this respect, the two sides voiced their pride 
at the growing popular uprising in the occupied and land stressed the need for action 

to strengthen the resistance of the Arab Palestinian people and their struggle under the 
leadership of the PLO, their sole legitimate representative, to recover their land and 
from their own independent state on Palestinian national territory. 


The two sides denounced the recent Zionist declaration on the annexation of the Golan 

as revealing the expansionist nature of the Zionist enemy and constituting a manifestation 
of the continuous American support for the Zionist enemy. This support has encouraged 

the enemy in further pursuing its expansionist and aggressive policies against the Arab 
nation. To face up to these schemes, the Arab nation is now dutybound to act toward 

the establishment of a unified strategy that will continue to affirm unified Arab action 
and through which the Arab nation will mobilize its military, economic and political 
potentials to confront the common enemy. 


In reviewing the situation in Lebanon, the two sides emphasized the Arab character and 
unity of Lebanon and their support for the Arab efforts to restore security and stability 
to this country. The two sides also reviewed the joint Arab action and discussed why it 
is stumbling. In this respect, they stressed the need for unified joint Arab action in 
facing up to the enemy and for action to remove all traces of marginal Arab disputes 

and the need to settle all these disputes in a constructive fraternal spirit to devote 
ourselves exclusively to facing up to the fateful dangers looming over the Arab nation 

in this delicate stage of its history.

The two sides discussed the American assault against the Jamahiriyah and the economic 
and technological blockade imposed by the American administration against it. As an 
expression of the unity of Arab action, Kuwait reaffirmed its stand in solidarity with 
the Jamahiriyah, and the Arab Libyan side expressed its appreciation for the position 
adopted by Kuwait and the latter's denunciation of these American measures. 


In discussing the situation in the Arab Gulf, the two sides expressed their eagerness to 
keep the area free from international conflicts and their opposition to any form of 
foreign presence of military intervention in that region. The Kuwaiti side noted the 
measures and decisions made in the framework of the Gulf Cooperation Council. The Arab 
Libyan side expressed its support for all the sincere efforts aimed at using the poten- 
tials and assets of the region in service of national and pan-Arab causes. 


The two sides expressed their deep concern over the continuation of the Iraq-Iran war 
because of the dangers it poses to the two warring countries and to the security of the 
region and given the waste of Arab and Islamic potentials it involves. The two sides 
emphasized their support for efforts undertaken to rapidly end this war. 


In reviewing the situation in Africa, the two sides came out in support for the struggle 
of the liberation movements in the continent, particularly the struggles of the people of 
Namibia and the people of South Africa to achieve freedom and independence. 


The Kuwaiti side lauded the Arab Libyan role in Chad and its role in restoring security 
and stability to this African country. 


The two sides voiced their deep satisfaction with the results of the visit and stressed 
the importance of the continuity of the exchange of friendly visits by officials of the 
two countries. 


His Highness Shaykh Sa'd al-'Abdallah as-Sabah expressed his profound gratitude for the 
warm welcome and hospitality accorded to him and the accompanying delegation. His high- 
ness also hailed the achievements of the Great 1 September Revolution and recorded his 
appreciation for these achievements. 


His Highness extended an invitation to Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud to visit his 
second country, Kuwait. His excellency accepted the invitation. The date of the visit 
will be fixed later.

FOREIGN TEACHERS IN LIBYA REJECT REAGAN CLAIMS 


LD180948 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 18 Dec 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Dec (JANA) -- American and British members of the teaching staff of the 
new Tripoli college sent a message to the commander of the revolution, in which they expressed 
their categorical rejection of the allegations being propagated by the current U.S. 
administration in an attempt to consolidate the charges that the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah was working for the assassination of President Reagan and some of his aides. 


The American and British teachers pointed out that Reagan's decision to force American 
citizens to leave Libya was based on fallacies since President Reagan's claims are un- 
founded. Under no circumstances would these allegations be accepted, they said. 


The message said American citizens working in the Jamahiriyah enjoy all respect and a 
prosperous life among the Libyan Arab people and have complete freedom of movement throughout 
the country, enjoying the protection of Libyan laws. 


American President Reagan's allegations that Americans working in Libya are living under 
insecure conditions were mere fabrications and distortion of facts, which are refuted by 
reality. 


The American and British teachers added: We enjoy full security and so do all foreigners 

in Libya. We affirm that we are able to practice our religions in an atmosphere of 
reassurance and psycological comfort. We will corroborate our statements with a film showing 
how we enjoy freedom and how respectfully the Libyan people treat us. Undoubtedly, we will 
show this film for the benefit of the American people so that together we will show Reagan's 
allegations to be a source of mockery. 


TRIPOLI REPORTS ON DELEGATION TALKS IN BENIN 


NC220836 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 22 Dec 81 


[Text] The meetings held between a delegation from the force of the revolution in the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the Committees for the Defense of the 
revolution in the People's Republic of Benin concluded yesterday in Cotonou, capital of the 
People's Republic of Benin. The meetings were convened within the context of strengthening 
relations between the Libyan Arab people and the people of Benin. 


In concluding their meetings, the members of the two delegations affirmed their readiness 
to resist the U.S. imperialist onslaught on the peoples of the area, first and foremost 
the Libyan Arab people who have proclaimed the emergence of the people's authority, who 
have announced their limitless support for the struggling peoples and who stand firmly 
against the forces of terrorism and aggression. 


The Committees for the Defense of the Revolution in Benin louded the role of the force of 
the revolution in the Jamahiriyah and its diligent work in the Arab, African and inter- 
national domains -- a work done by preaching the humanitarian reforms contained in the Green 
Book. These reforms champion the causes of liberty, emancipation from slavery and freedom 
from imperialism. These reforms also call for inducing the masses to wield their authority, 
revolution and arms.
