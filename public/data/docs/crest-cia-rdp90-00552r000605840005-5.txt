• Schlachter, 39, was indicted with Wilson in August 1981 on four conspiracy and export charges and pleaded guilty to two in November 1981.
• Wilson was convicted in Alexandria in November 1982 and sentenced to 15 years in prison.
• Wilson faced a subsequent Washington trial on charges including a conspiracy to assassinate a prominent Libyan exile.
• The CIA consistently denied knowing of or condoning Wilson's Libyan operation.
