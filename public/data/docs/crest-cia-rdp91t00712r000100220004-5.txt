Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing note on the Libya-Chad conflict, describing resupply and force deployments at Chadian and Libyan-held sites in northern and central Chad. Nothing here concerns Tripoli or Libya's domestic repression.
• Libyan forces near Ounianga Kebir old airfield included elements of two mechanized infantry battalions and one tank company.
• Aozou airfield air order of battle included 23 Fitter, six Mirage F-1, seven SF.260 aircraft, two Hip helicopters and one Alouette.
• Three ZSU-23/4 and three SA-9 systems were deployed defending Aozou airfield.
• Libyan forces at Gouro included at least 17 BMP APCs, two tanks and a 130mm M-46 battery.
• 1983-08: Resupply efforts to Libyan-backed Chadian dissidents and French-backed Chadian government forces continued, with Libyan forces deployed at several Chadian sites.
