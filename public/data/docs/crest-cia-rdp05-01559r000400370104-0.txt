Summary (AI): A September 1976 Tripoli radio broadcast excerpt of a Revolution Command Council rally speech, presenting Libya's "people's authority" system (basic popular congresses, committees, trade unions, General People's Congress) and rejecting the label "Al-Qadhdhafi regime." It contains no specific places, casualties or facilities in or near Tripoli.
• Speech refers to the Zuwarah speech and to three stages of the 1 September Revolution, with the third stage establishing the General People's Congress.
• The broadcast is attributed to Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland, dated 2 September 1976 (020200 local time).
• The speech states that the regime is not to be called a military regime or the regime of any specific person.
