• The speech was reported by Tripoli JANA in Arabic and relayed by FBIS London on 1 April 1985 as part 4 of 4.
• Qadhafi pledged full support for Kurdish independence and territorial integrity.
• Qadhafi said Libyan oil and arms belong to the Arab revolutionary forces.

NATION DECIDES TO DIE, THIS IS A DECISION WHICH THE UNITED STATES

AMERICA, WITH ALL ITS MIGHT, WITH 50 WARSHIPS CLOSE TO LEBANON'S 
SHORES, COULD NOT PREVENT THIS. A PERSON PLANTED BOMBS ON HIMSELF 
AND SAID I HAVE DECIDED TO DIE IN EXCHANGE FOR 300 AMERICANS. HE 
BLEW HIMSELF UP AND WITH HIM 300 AMERICANS. THIS IS THE DECISTON 
WHICH THE ENEMY CAN NOT PREVENT. IF I SAID [ WANT TO TAKE WATER

THIS ROAD TO SIDON TODAY, THE ENEMY COULD SAY NO. BUT IF YOU SAID 
I WANT TO DIE IT IS IMPOSSIBLE FOR HIM TO SAY NO TO YOU BECAUSE 
THIS DECISION IS YOURS. 
THE LEADER SAID: WE WANT TO RESURRECT THIS SPIRIT IN THIS NATION, 
THE SPIRIT OF MARTYRDOM. WE WANT EVERY ONE OF US TO SAY: I HAVE 
DECIDED TO DIE JUST TO SPITE AMERICA, BECAUSE THIS DECISION IS ONE 
WHICH AMERICAN CAN NOT VETO; NO, I WANT TO DIE AND THIS IS SOMETHING 
I AM CAPABLE OF. IF WE COULD BRING THIS NATION TO THE POINT WHERE 
IT POSESSES THIS DETERMINATION IT WILL DEFINITELY WIN. 
THE BROTHER LEADER OF THE REVOLUTION ADDED THAT THIS MEETING 
CONSTITUTES FOR THE ARAB NATION, IN ADDITION TO WHAT I HAD 
ALREADY SAID, A NEW MOMENTUM FOR ANOTHER TORN AND AGONIZED NATION, 
THE BROTHERLY KURDISH NATION, SOME OF WHOSE SONS ARE UNDER THE 
BANNER OF THIS LEADERSHIP. EVEN THOSE KURDISH FIGHTERS WHO ARE NOT 
WITH US FEEL THAT SOMETHING NEW HAS COME ABOUT WHICH SERVES THEIR 
INTERESTS: LIBERATING THE ARAB NATION AND UNIFYING IT ON 
PROGRESSIVE AND POPULAR BASES. THIS MEANS ALIGNMENT, THE ALIGNMENT 
OF THE ARAB NATION WITH THE FIGHT OF THE GLORIOUS KURDISH NATION. 
FROM NOW ON THE STRUGGLE OF THE ARAB NATION AND THE STRUGGLE OF 
THE KURDISH NATION WILL BE CONSIDERED AS SUPPORTING EACH OTHER.
