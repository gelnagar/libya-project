SFRY Official Planinc Arrives for Visit 
LD201846 Tripoli JANA in English 1803 GMT 20 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada al Ulla 16, Feb 20, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Milka Planinc, president of the Federal 
Executive Council, arrived in Tripoli this morning on a three day 
visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


The visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah falls 
within the strategic cooperation, the foundation of which v. as laid 
by late President Tito and the revolution’s leader. The guest was 
received at Tripoli International Airport by Muhammad az- 
Zarruq Rajab, secretary of the General People’s Committee and 
other secretaries and the heads of the diplomatic missions of the 
socialist countries. 


Mrs Planinc is accompanied on her visit by a high level del- 
egation. The delegation includes Zivorad Kovacevic, member of 
the Federal Executive Council; Mirko Ostojic, under secretary 
for foreign affairs; and the Yugoslav ambassador to the Jama- 
hiriyah and some advisers and experts. 


A JANA reporter said that the official talks between the two 
sides are expected to open later today. 


Planinc Arrival Statement 
LD201441 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
20 Feb 84 


[Excerpts] Milka Planinc, president of the Yugoslav Federal 
Executive Council, arrived in Tripoli this morning for a 3-day 
visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, in the 
framework of the relations of strategic cooperation between the 
two friendly countries. 


Milka Planinc made a statement to JANA in which she expressed 
her pleasure at visiting the Jamahiriyah, voicing confidence that 
this visit will contribute to an exchange of views and to 


strengthening intensified cooperation and consolidating the stra- 
tegic relations between the two countries, the foundations of 
which were laid by the brother leader of the revolution and the 
late leader Josef Broz Tito. 


She said: We (?strive for) the establishment of equal relations 
between peoples and international cooperation for peace. The 
Jamahiriyah and Yugoslavia, she said, are linked by strong 
relations that strengthen the objectives and principles of the 
Nonaligned Movement, of which are both members. 


At the conclusion of her statement, Milka Planinc said: Our 
presence here is an emphasis on continuous cooperation between 
the two countries in all fields, and exchanging visits and talks 
between us is a sound way to consolidate relations of cooperation.
