LIBYA

Envoy Recalled; Lebanese Envoy Asked To Leave 
LD171959 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1935 GMT 17 May 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 17 May (JANA) — JANA has learned that the 
popular committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison 
has decided to withdraw the secretary of the Arab Libyan 
People’s Bureau in Lebanon in protest against the Lebanese 
Government's signing today of the agreement of shame and 
humiliation with the Zionist enemy. 


The People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison has asked the Lebanese 
ambassador in Tripoli to leave the Jamahiriyah. 


The People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaisor will submit the Lebanon 
file to the basic people’s congresses so they may make any 
decision they want. 


The agency also learned that the popular bureau for foreign 
liaison has submitted a memorandum to the Arab League asking 
it to carry out against the Lebanese Govornment the same 
measures as those implemented against the defunct As-Sadat for 
his committing the highest wrongdoing against the Arab nation.
