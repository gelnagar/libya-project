LIBYA

Reports on Tunisian Prime Minister's Visit 


Arrives for Meeting 
LDI91555 Tripoli JANA in English 1410 GMT 19 Jul 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Shawwal 9, July 19, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — Mr Mohamed Maali, the Tunisian prime minister, 
arrived in Tripoli at 10:30 p.m. today heading a high-level 
delegation to take part in the proposed meeting between the 
Tunisian Cabinet and the General People’s Committee. 


The delegation was given a warm reception which expressed the 
strong ties between the two peoples. Mr Mzali was received at 
Tripoli airport by Mr ‘Aziz at-Talhi, secretary of the General 
People’s Committee, members of the General People’s Commit- 
tee, the Jamahiriyah's [word indistinct] envoy to Tunis, the 
Tunisian envoy to Tripoli and the head of Arab diplomatic 
missions to the Jamahiriyah and some Libyan Armed Forces 
officers 


Premier Mzali made a statement to JANA in which he said: 
Before all, | would like to express my happiness and pleasure to 
visit Jamahiriyah along with a large number of my colleagues to 
attend meetings of the higher joint committee, which was decided 
upon following Brother Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi's visit to Tuni- 
sia in February 1982. | would also like to address my greetings 
of brotherhood to Libya, its leader and masses. Tunisia’s presi- 
dent, government and people are determined to march along the 
path of genuine solidarity, organic integration and adherence to 
the unionist programme in our future relations so as to guarantee 
for our two countries and the Arab nation greater strength, self 
sufficiency and prosperity” 


Vv. 20 Jul 83 


He added: “President Habib Bourguiba and his Brother Colonel 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi have charted the path for us in the 
General People’s Committee and the Tunisian Government to 
materialise the decisions taken during Colonel Al-Qadhdhafi's 
visit to Tunisia and which were contained in the official joint 
statement on 27 Feb 1982.” 


Answering a question on the effectiveness of economic integra- 
tion as an advanced step on the road of comprehensive Arab 
unity, he said: “Comprehensive Arab unity is our sacred goal and 
could be arrived at through economic integration, unification of 
educational curricula, linking the media organs, uniting cultural 
and social policy. The economy is extremely important and vital 
It needs unionist and a nationalist outlook. Such a unionist 
outlook is best formed through educational, cultural and media 
programmes. For if our young generation starts to acquire 
umionist culture and education this will be a great gain for our 
generation” 


He said: “I personally rely on economic integration and believe 
that some positive steps were already taken and others will 
undoubtedly emerge from our meetings. | also believe in educa- 
tional and media work. | visited Libya ten years ago as a minister 
of national education to lay the foundations and principles for an 
integral Libyan- Tunisian educationa! endeavour’ 


In conclusion he expressed his gratitude for the warm reception 
he was accorded. He said: “This session will be followed by others 
in Tums and Tripoli every six months. Their results will be 
fruitful, thanks to the determination of the two leaders and that 
of us all to realise our peoples’ common destiny” 


The Tunisian delegation comprising the foreign minister, Mr 
Bey: Caid es-Sebsi; minister of interior, Driss Guiga; minister of 
transport and communications, Mr Sodok Ben Jomaa: minister 
of culture, Mr Bechir Ben Slama; minister of agriculture, Mr 
Lassad Ben Osman; minister of social affairs, Mr Mohamed 
Ennaceur; minister of education, Mr Fredj Chedli; minister of 
housing, Mr Moncef Bel Hadj Amor; minister of information, 
Mr. Abd ar-Razzaq al-Kafi; and the governor of the Tunisian 
Centra! Bank, Mr Moncef Belkhodja 


Al-Qadhdhafi Opens Meeting

19 Jul 83 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution today opened the joint 
meeting of the Jamahiriyah Generai People’s Committee and the 
Council of Ministers of the sister Republic of Tunisia to discuss 
further means of strengthening and developing integration 
between the two countries. The first step toward achieving full 
unity between the two sister countries was laid by the brother 
leader and the president of Tunisia, Habib Bourguiba 


The brother leader of the revolution spoke at the opening meeting 
about the firm relations of cooperation between the two sister 
countries and the future prospects of this cooperation, which 
constitutes a pan-Arab unionist act for the benefit of the Arab 
nation
