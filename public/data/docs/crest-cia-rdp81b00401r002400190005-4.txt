Summary (AI): A December 1977 CIA briefing outline on the Tripoli conference of rejectionist Arab states and Palestinian groups opposed to Sadat's peace initiatives. It lists the participating groups, their militia sizes and leaders, and the communique signed by the PLO, Syria, Libya, South Yemen and Algeria, with Iraq walking out.
• PFLP: independent radical, 500 to 1,000 militia.
• PFLP-GC: radical, close to Syria, 100 to 200 militia.
• Front for the Liberation of Palestine: radical, close to Iraq, recent offshoot of PFLP-GC, 100 to 200 militia.
• Arab Liberation Front: controlled by Iraq, 250 militia plus 500 Iraqi militiamen in Lebanon.
• Popular Struggle Front and Black June: controlled by Iraq, each less than 200 militia.
• Rejectionist states listed: Libya, Iraq, South Yemen, Algeria.
• Iraq withdrew from the communique arguing UN Resolutions 242 and 338 should have been explicitly rejected.
• 1977-12: Tripoli conference of rejectionist states and Palestinian groups called to oppose Sadat's peace initiatives; communique calling for a 'pan-Arab front for resistance and confrontation' signed by PLO, Syria, Libya, South Yemen and Algeria; Iraq withdrew and did not sign.
