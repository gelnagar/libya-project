AT-TURAYKI ON RESULTS OF AL-QADHDHAFI VISIT TO NIGER 
LD252036Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1700 GMT 25 Jan 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--Dr 'Ali at-Turayki, foreign secretary, has described the results of the 
visit of Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to the Republic of Niger as important. He says that 
it is an important historic event not only for the two countries but for the African 
Continent, and for the economic cooperation between the two countries. Dr at-Turayki 
explained that the Jamahiriyah is to reclaim agricultural land for Niger and is to 

give Niger loans to enable it to reclaim additional land.

Dr at-Turayki has pointed out that Col Mu!ammar al-Qadhdhafi held lengthy talks with 

Lt Col Seyni Kountche which dealt with strengthening bilateral relations between the 
two friendly countries and strengthening the unity and solidarity of the African 
peoples. He declared that the joint statement about the visit and the talks between 
the Arab Libyan and Niger sides are considered an important event and a turning 

point in international relations because they stress the unity and solidarity of the 
peoples of the continent and their stand in the face of foreign intervention and the 
restoration cf their control over their natural resources. The joint statement has 
made it clear that if there were to be a policy of pacts it should take place among the 
people of the continent who have a mutual destiny and common interests, 


Dr at-Turayki pointed out that the talks held by the leader of the revolution with 
William Eteki, general secretary of the OAU, dealt with the restoration of relations 
between the Jamahiriyah and Chad, (?This may produce results) since the talks between 
the leader of the revolution and the OAU general secretary have stressed the sense 

of responsibility of the two peoples of the Jamahiriyah and Chad and that the efforts 
exerted may result in restoring relations to normal, 


At the end of his statement the foreign secretary noted the historic, geographical 

and spiritual relations which link the Arab Libyan and Chadian peoples. He has 

stated that there are efforts being made to restore relations between the two fraternal 
people and that it is essential that cooperation should take place between the two 
countries to realize the interests of the Arab Libyan and Chad peoples, 


COMMUNIQUE ISSUED ON AL-QADHDHAFI VISIT TO NIGER 
LD252346Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1800 GMT 25 Jan 78 LD 


[Text] A joint communique was issued today in Tripoli and Niamey on the visit of Col 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the great 1 September Revolution, to the Republic 
of the Niger. Here is the text: 


Within the framework of fraternal periodical meetings between the two sister countries 
and at the invitation of his friend Lt Col Seyni Kountche, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
president of the Supreme Military Council, head of state, secretary general of the 
General People's Congress and leader of the great 1 September Revolution, paid en 
orficial visit to the Republic of Niger from 25 to 25 January 1978. Col Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi was given a warm official and popular welcome expressing the feeling of 
brotherhood and friendship between the two peoples and countries. 


During the visit the two leaders reviewed bilateral relations and the Arab, African 
and international situation during their meetings, Official talks took place between 
the two sides and were attended by, for Libya; Lt Col Mustafa al-Kharrubi, member of 
the General Secretariat of the General People's Congress; Muhammad Al‘ Tabu, secretary 
for agriculture; Taha Sharif ibn Amir, liaison secretary; Jaddalah Azzuz al-Talhi, 
secretary for industry; Dr 'Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, foreign secretary; Muhammad 
al-Bkhari Hammudah, Jamahiriyah ambassador to Niger; and a number of senior officials, 
Or. the Niger side were: Maj Moumouni Adamou Djermakoye, minister of foreign affairs 
and cooperation; Maj Manga Boulama, minister of rural development; Annou Mahaman, 
planning minister; Brahim Mamane, state secretary for rural development; Abu Bakr 
Bello, ambassador of Niger to the Jamahiriyah and a number of sendor officials.

In an armosphere of friendship and brothsrnood the two sides reviewed questions of 
interest to them, Viewpoints were identical in all fields, Concerning bilateral 
relations, the two leaders paid tribube to the firm relations between the Libyan Arab 
and Niger peoples characterized by friendship and good neighborliness, The two leaders 
expressed full satisfaction with the development of relations, solidarity and coopera- 
tion which have besn reflected in extensive economic and cultural sectors, They 
committed themselves to do all they can to make this cooperation a true model for 
emulation by the African states, a matter which requires continued strengthering of 
this existing cooperation which has been the direct result of the success of the 

two countries in attaining liberation from all forms of foréign domination, 


On Africa, the two sides utress their full adherence to the principle of self- 
determination by the Saharen people, The two sides hope the OAU emergency summit 
will succeed in reaching a peaceful, just and final solution, They declare their 
full support for the struggle of the African liberation movements in Nemibia, Zimbabwe 
and the southern part of the continent, affirming their intention to continue their 
help and support for all the African peoples struggling for liberation from ell forms 
of colonialism and racism, In this connection the two sides expressed their full 
support for the conference of the African liberation committee of coordination due 

to be held in the capital of the Jamahiriyah during Feburary. The two sides condemn 
the attempts by imperialism to creste alliances and blocs in Africa to serve its 
interests, something which threatens the security, safety and unity of the African 
Continent. Africa should have only such alliances which serve its own interests and 
between neighbors in accordance with the principles of nonalinement. 


While strongly condemning imperialist foreign interference in the affairs of Africa 
Which aims at internationalizing African problems and taking tne continent back to 


the era of colonialism and domination, the two sides stress the ability of the African 
peoples and the OAU to find proper solutions to these problems, 


In the Arab field, the two sides call for Afro-Arab cohesion and unity and stress 

the importance of the common role which can be played by the African-Arab group in 
achieving cultural and economic progress and defeating coloniélism, The cooperation 
between these two groups is to the benefit of the peoples of the Third World generally. 
By virtue of their historical and geographical relations, the Arab and African groups 
will be able to play an effective role on the international level and in various 
fields. Therefore the two sides stress their commitment to strengthen this cooperation 
in all sectors, 


in considering the Palestinian issue which has resulted from the arrogance and intransi- 
gence of the racist Zionists, the two sides affirmed their determination to back and 
help the Palestinian people in their efforts to regain their land and all their usurped 
rights, In this connection the two sides denounce any initiative aimed at excluding 
the Palestinian peoples from the solution of their problem, 


In the international field, the two sides stressed their commitment to the charter 
and principle: of the United Nations and their belief in the need to amend the 

UN Charter in such a way as to achieve justice and equality between states, big and 
small, and to call for the abrogation of the right of veto, They also called for 
creating a new international economic order recognizing the right of the Third World 
countries to participate in it on basis of justice and equality.

During theSe talks “ol Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi explained the principles of the great 
September Revolution and the democratic achievements, represented in the delegation, 
of power to the reople. He also spoke about the victories achieved by the Libyan 
people against monopolies and imperialism and stressed the continued call by the 
Jamahiriyah people for the unity of the Arab homeland, the peoples of Africa and

Seyni Kountche expressed confidence that the Jamahiriyah enjoyed the appreciation and

for progress. He acquainted his brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi with the great changes

ad control over the national resources, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi expressed admiration 
at the progress realized in the economic, social and cultural fields in the 

interest of the well-being of the Niger people. 


In concluding his visit, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi expresses his deep gratitude to Lt 
Col Seyni Kountche, the Niger Government and people for the warm welcome accorded to 
him and his delegation, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi addressed an official invitation to 
Lt Col Seyni Kountche to visit the Jamahiriyah. The invitation was gratefully accepted 
and its date will be fixed later.
