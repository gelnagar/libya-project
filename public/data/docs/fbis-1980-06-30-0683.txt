FOREIGN SECRETARIAT: AS-SADAT PREPARES FOR WAR 
ID272010 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1845 GMT 27 Jun 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 27 June--The Egyptian authorities have commented on the statements 

made by the Saudi foreign minister, who said that the escalation of the current situa- 
tion is a grave matter which cannot be justified and that efforts must be concentrated 
in the present on facing the true danger which continues to plot against the Arab nation 
and which takes the form of the Zionist enemy which is thieatening us and whose threats 
are directed against all the Arab countries, and not egainst one of them alone, 


In their comment the Egyptian authorities have said that Egypt has not been the one to 
intensify tension in the border area with Libya and "has not sent o: > single soldier to 
the western region," All Egypt did was to declare a state of emergency in that region 
in view of the threats alleged to have been made by brother Col Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi 
in his 1i June speech, the Egyptian authorities said, 


In view of this Egyptian comment the Libyan Foreign Seeretariat wishes to state the 
following: 


On theseventh anniversary of the Great 1 September Revolution, the brother colonel 
ordered that provisions, food and water be supplied to the officers and men of the 
Egyptian Army whom the Egyptian president had moved from the line of confrontation with 
the enemy to the area of the borders with the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
along the eastern desert with a view to launching a war against the Libyan people, 


The brother commander asserted then that the Egyptian soldiers amassed on our borders 
were sons of the Egyptian people and consequently were our brothers. Their cause was 
not on our borders, That was our attitude, But what as the attitude of As-Sadat? 

He launched a war against us in July 1977. That aggression was part of his prepara- 
tions for his initiative, which started with his visit to Jerusalem and then continued 
until it ended in the signing of the treaty with the Zionist enemy and Egypt's 
becoming a U.S. base ready to be used against the Arab and Islamic nation,

The Egyptian president has defined his position regarding that aggression. He said that 
he wanted to teach the Libyan people a lesson they would not forget. But events have 
proven that the president wanted to teach all the Arab countries a lesson for opposing 
his agreements and his normalization of relations with the Zionist enemy. 


As-Sadat is now going back to the same old policy of aggression, threats and fallacies. 
He has amassed his forces along the eastern borders of Libya. The Egyptian military 
leaders continue to utter statements, and war preparations have been completed with the 
declaration of the state of emergency, which was actually declared previously in secrecy 
on the borders with Libya. This is the attitude of the Egyptian president: threats of 
war and preparation for war. 


In the face of this, the Foreign Secretariat wishes to affirm here what the commander 
said in his speech on 11 June: We feel pained and sorry because the U.S. bases which 

were expelled from Libya in 1970 are now being established on Egyptian territory. 

The bettle is the battle of the Arab nation against imperialism. The freedom of the 

Tibyen people will always be in danger as long as U.S. imperialsm continues to crouch 
in the Egyptian territory and turn Egypt into a base of aggression. 


But the task of overthrowing the treason in Egypt is not the Libyan people's. It is in 
the first place the responsibility of the Egyptian people and-the Egyptian Army. It is 
also the responsibility of the Arab nation now that Egypt has become a base for aggression 
against the Arab nation and for terrorizing the Arab nation and threatening it. 


Today we declare our alliance with the Egyptian people and army. We cannot direct our 
guns against the Egyptian Army but we will be forced to do so if the Egyptian Army 
carries out the orders of As-Sadat and attacks the Libyan territory. 


This definition of our stand should be sufficient to block the way in the face of all 
falsifications. The moves and policy of As-Sadat, as declared by him, provethat he 
has appointed himself [word indistinct] on the Arab and Islamic nations. But the 
Arab and Islamic worlds ard world public opinion have realized this anc this condemn 
and reject the aggressive Egyptian steps. 


The Saudi statement represents one of the Arab, Islamic and world stands which have 
expressed regret at As-Sadat's grave steps, which can have no justification. But 

the Egyptian authorities persist with their policy of fallacies with a view to distorting 
the facts and misleading the people. It seems As-Sadat and his media believe in their 
ability to deceive all the people, and thus they have tried to lie even about the Saudi 
Arabian Government. They tried to lie about it as they did with the Egyptian people. 

We are not surprised to see the Egyptian president adopt the principle of lies after 
having adopted the principle of treason. 


AL-QADHDHAFI SPEECH PROMPTS EMBASSY CLOSING IN SENEGAL 


LD271932 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1910 GMT 27 Jun 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 27 June--The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has decided 

to close down its embassy in Dakar, the capital of Senegal, and has asked a member of 
the Libyan People's Bureau in Paris to look after the Jamahiriyah's interests in Senegal, 
JANA has learned. 


Senegal is one of the French-speaking African countries which is totally subservient to 
French hegemony. 


30 Jun 80 NORTH AFRICA 


In his speech to the African ambassadors on 25 May on the occasion of Africa Day; 

the brother colonel had pointed out that the Libyan Arab people would define their 
position toward the continued subservience of some French-speaking African countries 

to France, and that this continuation of subservience would prompt us to consider 

shifting the Libyan representation from these countries toFrance, where it naturally belongs.
