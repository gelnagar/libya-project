Corrections and removals

This record is assembled from published documents, and some of it will be wrong. If an entry misstates a fact, misplaces a site, or names someone who should not be named, say so here. The editor reads every request, and none is shown on the site.

What we will do

  • Correct a fact where a source shows the entry is wrong, and note the correction on the entry.
  • Remove the name of a victim at the family's request, even where a published source names them.
  • Remove any private person named as a perpetrator, informer or member of a security body. The record does not name such people.
  • Answer each request that leaves a way to reply.
