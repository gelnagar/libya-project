LIBYA

DISPUTES, CONFLICT AMONG SAUDI PRINCES INCREASE 


LD221858 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1815 GMT 22 Mar 82 


[Text] According to reports reaching Beirut, disputes and conflicts among the princes 
of the Saudi royal family are on the increase and are no longer a secret. The reports 
say Prince Fahd's travels abroad and his over-20-day absence from Saudi Arabia is a 
clear sign of such conflicts. 


According to one report, Prince Fahd and his brother, Prince Salman, the governor of 
Riyadh, have been launching fierce attacks against the minister of petroleum and mineral 
affairs, Ahmad Zaki Yamani, and trying to replace him with Prince Sa'ud al-Faysal. At 
that point, Prince Fahd would assume the Foreign Ministry portfolio now occupied by 
Prince Sa'ud.

According to the reports, the conflict among the princes of the Saudi family has reached 
illogical proportions and is affected by personal whims. Other reports speak of the 
winds of revolution sweeping the area and say these winds and the changes that have fol- 
lowed the fall of many of the pro-U.S. regimes have led to a growing spirit of rebellion 
among the youth of the Arabian Peninsula against the princes and those in power there. 
This has prompted the princes to take measures at home to absorb the rage of the youth 
and their pan-Arab and Islamic activities. 


AL-'UBAYDI VISITS MALTA; DISPUTE TO GO TO HAGUE 


LD230044 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0015 GMT 23 Mar 82 


[Text] Following the brother commander of the revolution's stopover in Malta en route 

to the jamahiriyah from Austria and his meeting with Maltese Prime Minister Dom Mintoff 
and members of his government when agreement was reached on the settlement of all pending 
issues between the jamahiriyah and Malta, Brother Abd al Ati-al-'Ubaydi, secretary of 

the people's committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison, paid a visit to Malta 
during the last 2 days. He held talks with Maltese Prime Minister Dom Mintoff and members 
of his government. Agreement was reached during the talks on a number of issues including 
an agreement to refer the dispute over the continental shelf between the two countries to 
the International Court of Justice. 


AL-"UBAYDI MEETS SAO TOME FOREIGN MINISTER 


LD221954 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 22 Mar 82 


[Text] Brother Abd al-‘Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the people's committee of the People's 
Bureau for Foreign Liaison, this morning met Mrs Maria de Nascimento, minister of foreign 
affairs of Sao Tome and Principe. During the meeting, they discussed the strengthening 
and developing of bilateral cooperation between the two friendly countries and issues of 
mutual interest.
