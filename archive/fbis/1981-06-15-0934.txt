LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI COMMENTS TO PRESS ON ISRAEL, U.S. 


LD150142 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 14 Jun 81 


[Text] The brother leader has stressed that it is the Arabs' right now to destroy the 
Israeli nuclear reactor following the enemy's grave precedence of attacking the Iraqi 
nuclear reactor. At his meeting with a large number of Arab and foreign newsmen who 
attended the celebrations of the llth anniversary of the expulsion of the U.S. bases, 
the leader said that it was the Arabs' right to destroy the enemy's nuclear reactor 
because it produces atom bombs with which it seeks to destroy the Arab homeland. 


The leader explained that the world will take less severe measures against the Arabs in 
the event they attack the Israeli reactor than the measures which the world might take 
now against the enemy, since the Arabs would be reacting to a precedent set by the 
enemy. Regarding the reaction of the Western states to the attack on the Iraqi reactor 
and the American pressures being put on these states, the leader said that this was a 
matter which is connected with how much these states value their interests. We are not 
concerned whether these states’ attitude is positive or negative, as we are not seeking 
interests but are now facing a destruction of the Arab entity. The brother leader 
stressed that the danger now goes beyond all borders, as it has as its goal the entire 
Arab entity. Iraq is not in the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front, but despite this 
the Israelis struck at it. The Arabs, the leader added, must settle their differences 
and must unite for the sake of their existence. 


The leader stressed that it is the Arabs who would put an end to Israel [yada'un 
haddan], which now constitutes a danger to the security and existence of the Arab 
nation. It has become clear that there are no limits to Israel's security and, 
consequently, Israel has become a danger to the Arabs. Noting in this connection that 
Israel has determined that it is either Israel or the Arabs, he said: From now on, the 
aim should be to remove Israel. He added: We have decided not to raise any Arab 
differences until Israel is finished. 


Concerning the danger to the Arab nation of the British and U.S. bases in Cyprus, the 

leader affirmed that foreign bases in any place constitute a danger to peace and above 
all they pose a great danger to the countries in which they are based. He called for 

the need to continue the struggle for the expulsion of all foreign bases and said they 
should go home whether these bases are in Cyprus, Germany or any other place. 


In reply to a question concerning the attitude of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah toward the U.S. administration, which expelled the members of the Libyan 
Arab people's bureau, the leader explained: We have not lost anything by this act which 
the United States committed. He said that the basic popular congresses, when convened 
at the end of this year, will look into the relations between Libya and the United 
States unless the United States reconsiders its unjustifiably hostile policy toward the 
Libyan people.

He added: We believe that Reagan followed (?in the footsteps of Carter) [words indis- 
tinct]. I believe that the American people will reject him as they rejected Carter 
because the stupid policy destroys the U.S. interests in the world. 


In answer to a question about the results of his visit to Moscow, the brother leader 
explained that the visit has increased the promotion of cooperation between the 
Jamahiriyah and the Soviet Union and it strengthened the friendship between the Libyan 
Arab and Soviet peoples. 


Regarding the hostile terror campaign led by the United States against the Libyan Arab 
people, the brother leader said: We are confident in ourselves. We exercise our right 
to live. We are ready to confront those who commit aggression against us. The threats 
of enemies do not frighten us. The leader emphasized the U.S. terror against small 
nations, including the Libyan Arab people. He said: We will work for mobilizing the 
small nations in the world against the United States and its terror. We will win this 
battle because all the people hate the United States and its terrorist practices. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES PLO'S ‘ARAFAT, OTHERS 


LD122326 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2915 GMT 12 Jun 81 


[Text] The brother leader today received Brother Yasir ‘Arafat, PLO Executive Committee 
chairman, in the role which was entrusted to him by the brother leader of stopping the 
war between Iraq and Iran, so that the Iranian and Iraqi forces may be directed toward 
the true enemy of the Arabs and Muslims, and mustering an Arab force that would wipe out 
the disgrace and destroy aggression which is aimed at Arab existence itself. 


Following this, Brother Yasir ‘Arafat left Tripoli for Rabat and from there to Riyadh, 
Tehran and Baghdad. 


The brother leader also received today Brother Muhsin Ibrahim, the executive director of 
the Lebanese National Movement, in the role which was entrusted to him by the brother 
leader. He also received Walid Junblatt, leader of the Lebanese National Movement, 

in his role of contacting the capitals of the steadfastness and confrontation states to 
find an Arab solution to the Lebanese problem which would protect the Palestinian 
resistance, Lebanese sovereignty and preserve the unity of Lebanon and keep far away 
from Lebanese territory all potential explosive situations. 


The brother leader also received today Muhammad ash-Sharif Musa'idiyyah, member of the 
Central Committee and the official in charge of the Permanent Committee of the Central 
Committee in Algeria. He also received Brother Muhammad al-Amin, prime minister of 
SDAR. 


SECRETARY DEMANDS ARAB LEAGUE EXPULSION OF SUDAN 


LD142108 Tripoli JANA in English 1714 GMT 14 Jun 81 


[Text] Tunis, 14 Jun (JANA)--The Libyan Arab secretary for revolutionary information 
asked for the expulsion of the Sudanese regime from the Arab League due to its repeated 
acts of treason against the Arab nation. Speaking yesterday at the last session of the 
Tunis conference of Arab information ministers, he said the Sudanese regime has opened 
Sudanese territory to American colonialist bases. 


He referred to the leader's speech, delivered on the 11th anniversary of the evacuation 
of U.S. bases from Libya, in which he describes the state of the Arab nation in the 
aftermath of the recent Israeli onslaught and American terrorism against the Arabs. The 
leader had drawn up a precise plan for unifying Arab resources and capabilities to 
confront the enemy and U.S. imperialism, which constitutes a serious threat to the 
security of the Arab nation.

The secretary quoted the leader's statement that the Arab nation is in need of a good 
strategy to retaliate against the clearly premeditated hostility of Israel and America. 


The secretary for revolutionary information noted American oppression against peace-lov- 
ing people and the way the U.S. directed its blow against Arab dignity and existence. 

It set up its military bases on Arab territory and thus approached the very core of Arab 
existence, he said. The repeated barbaric Israeli attacks against south Lebanon, aimed 

at the Palestinians and the progressive Lebanese National Movement, is a case in point, 

he added. 


On the recent Israeli destruction of the Iraqi nuclear reactor, which he described as a 
blatant violation of all international laws, he said: "The raid would not have been 
possible had it not been supported militarily, financially and morally by the USA." The 
secretary affirmed the Jamahiriyah's unreserved support for Iraq on this issue. 


In conclusion, he urged an end to the Iraqi-Iranian war so that Arab guns might be aimed 
at the joint enemy, Israel. He also called for the confrontation of American 
imperialism, which is Israel's backbone, providing it with all the military, financial 
and moral support needed. 


TRIPCLI REPORTS FEAR OF POPULAR UPRISING IN SUDAN 


LD141456 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 14 Jun 81 


[Text] Units of the Sudanese Army and police were placed on alert last night for fear 
of a popular uprising due to worsening internal conditions and the continued all-out 
strikes by railway workers, farmers, doctors and lawyers. 


Kuwait's AL-ANBA' reports that leaflets have been distributed in the streets of Khartoum 
and some other Sudanese towns calling for revolution against the regime and for toppling 
it. The paper adds that commercial life has come to a standstill in the country, basic 
commodities have vanished from shops and a broad wave of indignation is now rife in 
various popular sectors, a matter which threatens to explode any time. 


Last Tuesday, Khartoum witnessed a demonstration by thousands of Sudanese in protest 
against deteriorating conditions in the country. The demonstration began at midnight 
after 3,000 Sudanese destroyed the seats in a cinema--the Coliseum. According to 
reports, the angry masses marched toward the main market in Khartoum, where they were 
joined by thousands more. A number of incidents have taken place and a number of cars 
have been either destroyed or set afire. 


The Jamahiriyah correspondent in Djibouti has reported that units of the central 
security forces intercepted the angry demonstrations. The correspondent affirms that 
the Sudanese capital and a number of other Sudanese towns have been engulfed in darkness 
during the past few nights as a result of power cuts. 


Quoting diplomatic sources, the correspondent said that a political strike was now being 
staged in Sudan. Work has come to a complete standstill in a number of government 
departments. Civil service attendance has dropped to 30 percent in other departments 

as a result of the railwaymen's strike. 


Scores of cars can now be seen on the sides of the main roads as a result of fuel 
shortages. Furthermore, a large number of trucks have been left abandoned in the 
streets, blocking traffic. 


Quoting its Khartoum correspondent, the Abu Dhabi paper Al-Wahdah reports that today 

the committee formed by the ruler of Sudan to negotiate an agreement with Sudanese 
railwaymen failed to achieve anything after the workers refused to negotiate for lack of 
confidence in the regime's ability to solve their chronic problems.

The paper adds that the committee's chairman, Musa Mattah, has joined the ranks of workers 
and expressed support for their demands and decided not to return to Khartoum. He also 
issued a statement denouncing the ruling regime's policy. Over 4,C00 railwaymen are still 
surrounding the house of their trade union's leader, ‘Abbas Khadr, while in a state of 
constant readiness to check any attempt to arrest him. 


While conditions in Sudan are deteriorating because of the regime's deviation, new 
indications manifesting themselves incriminate the regime and prove its abject impotence 
in finding solutions to the problems of the Sudanese people. 


Mustafa Hassan, the Numayri regime's minister of public transport, has issued a statement 
in which he intimates that full responsibility for what is happening falls on the shoulders 
of the ruler of Sudan. He has implicity admitted that the ruling regime was deceiving 
workers by dodging its previous promises and refusing to respond to their demands. 


The London magazine THE MIDDLE EAST has reported that over 20,000 Sudanese textile workers 
have been arrested and that the remaining textile workers have declared an all-out strike 
in protest against the arrest of their colleagues and in expression of their denunciation 
of the Sudanese ruler's regime. 


Airports in northern Sudanese provinces were closed to all air traffic yesterday. The 
reason for this has not yet been discovered. 


The Abu Dhabi paper AL-WAHDAH yesterday disclosed that a secret meeting took place to 
the south of Khartoum between Numayri and the U.S. ambassador. Discussions at the 
meeting covered finding for the Sudanese regime ways out of the popular anger and worker 
and student strikes which have beset the regime.
