Al-Qadhdhafi Statement on Chadian Conflict 
LD200050 Tripoli Voice af Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 2315 GMT 19 Aug 87 

[“Statement” to JANA by leader Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi; date 
and place not given — read by announcer] 

[Text] The brother leader of the revolution made a statement to 
JANA in which he asserted: There is no enmity between Lib- 

-yans and Chadians. On the contrary, they are brothers and. 

neighbors more related than any other peoples in the region. 
Chad will remain forever in need of Libya. In fact, the enmity is 
between the imperialist forces on the one hand, and the Libyans 
and Chadians on the other. These imperialist forces have appro- 
priated Chad and set it against Libya against its will. Chad is 
now only a tool for imperialism to implement its aim against the 
Libyan revolution and the Arab nation. However, it is Chad that 
will lose in the end. This is what the imperialists want. 

The review yesterday, Wednesday, of the forces at Istresbase by 
the French president and his defense minister unveiled the 
French stand to the world. France has become completely naked. 
France has demonstrated to the world that the confrontation is 
not between Libyans and Chadians, but with imperialist France. 
Peace will prevail in the region if French colonialism returns to 
its own country and if the Chadians who are fighting each other 
for power are able to reconcile themselves and establish national 
unity. Any conflict that ever existed between Libya and Chad 
could be resolved by the OAU. However, if France and the 
United States continue, then some unexpected critical develop- 
ments will probably take place in Africa. 

Foreign Liaison Statement 
LD192112 Tripoli Television Service in Arabic 
1930 GMT 19 Aug 87 

[Text] A spokesman for the People’s Committee of the People’s 
Bureau for Foreign Liaison has made the following statement: 
French President Mitterrand’s visit to a base for military inter- 
vention in Chad in the south of France, as well as his review of 
the French arms and forces which participate in the occupation 
of Chad, underscore the aggressive stand which France has 
adopted for many years. France has exerted extensive military, 
political, and propaganda efforts to secure the protection and 
support of the agent ‘Ibri [Libyan name for Chadian President 
Hissein Habre; "Ibri means Hebrew] to consolidate his aggression 
against the Great Jamahiriyah. 

Despite all the justifications supplied by the French Government 
to veil its support for ‘Ibri and its direct participation in the 
aggression on the southern borders of the Great Jamahiriyah, the 
French president’s review of the forces of intervention in Chad 
confirms the same old French policy and supports the same 
aggressive French aims to consecrate French occupation of Chad 
and threaten the security of the African Continent. 

The presence of French forces in Chad constitutes a direct threat 
to the security of the Great Jamahiriyah, as well as a danger for 
the entire region. The agent ‘Ibri, urider the military protection 
of France, is free to launch his aggression against neighboring 

states. What happened on 8 August 1987 confirms this reality. 
Chad has become, in a more determined way, a base for military 
aggression at the service of U.S. and French interests and the 
imperialist tools in the region. The Great Jamahiriyah empha- 
sizes its principled stand to repel the aggression and eliminate its 
source. It calls on the OAU, through the message sent to its 
president, to shoulder its responsibility to achieve the withdrawal 
of the French forces from Chad and thereby remove the factors 
of tension and conflict and achieve stability and peace in the 
region.

Hudhayri has handed in a written message to Dr. Kenneth 
Kaunda, chairman of the Organisation of African Unity (OAU) 
concerning the withdrawal of French troops from Chad and 
holding the OAU responsible from bringing about this with- 
drawal in that these troops pose a threat that escalates the tension

Al-Qadhdhafi, Kaunda Discuss Chad 
£D200956 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1020 GMT 20 Aug 87 

[Text] Tripoli, 20 Aug (JANA) — A telephone conversation has 
taken place between the brother leader of the revolution and 
Zambian President Kenneth Kaunda, the present chairman of 
the OAU. 

Their discussion focused on the dangerous French intervention 
in Chad and its potential risk to the African Continent, as well 
as the concern which Africans have begun to feel about their 
security as a result of this intervention. 

The conversation also dealt with the demonstration of power 
staged by President Mitterrand at the French base of Istres 
yesterday and the need for France to withdraw its forces from 
Chad in particular and from the African Continent in general.

[Text] Tripoli, Hannibal [August] 19, Jamahiriyah News Agency

Revolutionary Council has announced its firm supportive stand

aggression carried out by imperialism and its stooges represents 
the conspiracy hatched against Great Jamahiriyah and its role 
and firmness in standing resolutely in the face of the imperialist- 
reactionary schemes at all levels.
