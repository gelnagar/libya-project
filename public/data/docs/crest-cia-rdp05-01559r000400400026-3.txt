aGhdhafil Discusses Africa, Palestinians, Lebanon 

1260049 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 26 May 85 

[Text] Tripoli, 26 May (JANA) — Yesterday evening the brother 
leader of the revolution received heads of the African diplomatic 
missions accredited in Libya on the occasion of Africa Day. 

Ina brief address to the heads of the African diplomatic missions 
the leader spoke about his recent visit to Central Africa which 
included Rwanda, Burundi, and Sudan and he apologized for not 
being able to accept the invitation from the president of Gabon, 
El Hadj Omar Bongo. 

The leader noted that the countries of Central Africa do not 
suffer from drought and that they have vast unutilized water 
resources. The leader said: It is regrettable that at the time | 
was visiting these countries to discuss African issues, the presi- 
dent of Zaire was in occupied Palestine meeting with the racist 
Zionists — allies of the white racists in South Africa. 

The leader pointed out that both the presidents of Rwanda and 
Burundi have pointed to the need for the Arabs to contact 
Mobutu before adopting any definite stance against him for 
restoring his relations with the Zionist occupiers; this is as a result 
of a misunderstanding since Mobutu believed that the Arabs 
began to accept the establishment of relations with the Zionist 
enemy in the wake of Egypt’s submission and its defeat during 
the Ramadan war and the forcibly imposed reocgnition of it at 
the tent of shame 101 [Kilometer 101}, He also pointed to the 
statements made by the foreign minister of Zaire who said that 
Mobutu went to occupied Palestine to defend the Arab cause and 
the minister has requested people not to be hasty in judging Zaire. 
The leader said that on the advice of the brother presidents of 
Rwanda and Burundi, the Arab League secretary general will 
visit Zaire and we hope that this Arab endeavor will receive a 
response form the president of Zaire to reconsider his decision to 
restore relations with the Zionist enemy. The leader has asserted 
that as Arabs we are confronting an enemy who threatens our 
existence and we shall not accept from any regime — particularly 
from our African brothers — the establishment of relations with 
the Zionist enemy; we cannot compromise on this question, 

The leader then said that this year Africa Day comes when the 
Palestinian people are facing a new tragedy. What the Zionists 
have failed to achieve is now being achieved by Arabs against 
Palestinians. At a time when the world is still affected by the 
massacres of Sabra and Shatila at the hands of Sharon, and 
before this wound has healed, these massacres are being repeated 
by Arab Sharonites.

The leader said: The Palestine Salvation Front has assured me 
that the justifications that were cited by those responsible for 
these massacres, which are being committed now against the 
Palestinian camps, are untrue and that what is being said is no 
more than a justification for commiting this ugly crime. The 
leader went on tosay: This has prompted us to wonder if Yasir 
‘Arafat was not right in his search for a land to establish a 
Palestinian state on any part of Palestine even if it is on the

where. The leader has stressed that this does not mean that we 
accept the Zionist presence on the Arab land. Even if a Palestin- 
jan state is established and recognizes the Zionist entity, we will 
not recognize this entity. The leader said that in addition to the 
impossibility of the coexistence of a Palestinian state and a 
Zionist entity, the war between the Arabs and the Zionists is a 
war of existence particularly after the Zionists armed themselves 
with the atom bomb and deployed nuclear missiles in the Negev 
and on the Golan. 

The leader added: 1! cannot but side with the Palestinians 
whether they are justified or not. This stance is based on principle 
and the masses of the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jama- 
hiriyah have no option in the face of what is taking place but to 
stand by the Palestinians and incite them to resist and not 
surrender the camps. In his address the leader called on the 
Palestinians to stay on Libyan soil with their camps and detach- 
ments and to continue fighting from Libya to liberate Palestine. 
The leader added: Let it be known that Libya supports the 
annexation of Lebanon to Syria so that the two become one 
country, but this should not be realized over the corpses of 
Palestinians. 

In conclusion the leader asked the heads of the African diplo- 
matic missions to contact their leaders to stand by the Palestin- 
ians in the tribulations they are facing these days.

spoke on the telephone last night with both the president of

the Palestinian camps have been exposed to at the hands of Amal 
movement and the necessity to halt this plot which is targeted at 
the Palestine rifle and the Palestinian existence, and moreover 
the whole Arab steadfastness. 

The brother leader informed the leaders of the Lebanese national 
movements of the need for firm confrontation of this plot and to 
stand by the side of the Palestinian resistance.

HA‘ARETZ on Proposed Egyptian Ambassador 
TA240936 Tel Aviv HA‘ARETZ in Hebrew 24 May 85 p ! 

[““Exclusive” report from the United States by Tvzi Bar‘el] 

[Text] Washington — Egyptian sources have told HA‘ARETZ 
that ‘Izzat ‘Abd al-Latif, who until recently served as Egypt’s 

ambassador to Sudan, has been named the candidate for ambas- 

sador to Israel. 

In the past, ‘Abd al-Latif had also served as his country’s envoy 
to Jordan and as the head of the Egyptian delegation to the 
Israeli-Egyptian disengagement talks. 

The sources added that upon his return from Sudan, ‘Abd 
al-Latif was asked “to study and read material about Israel, and 
look forward to his next assignment there,” but could not tell us 
when a decision to dispatch him to Israel would be made. 
Egyptian diplomatic sources attribute great importance to the 
talks that will be held on Monday between the two delegations, 
during which the Israeli delegation is expected to present the 
Cabinet’s position on the Egyptian ideas [about Tabah]. “If we 
get positive responses, the appointment of an Egyptian ambassa- 
dor may well be expected to take place within a short period of 
time —- say a month or a bit longer.” Nevertheless, the possibility 
that the incumbent Egyptian representative, Muhammad Bas 
yuni, will be promoted and appointed ambassador has still not 
been totally ruled out. :
