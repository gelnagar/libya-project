LIBYA

Tripoli Comments on Saudi Crown Prince Visit 
LD061648 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
6 Jun 83 


[Text] Prince "Abdallah ibn ‘Abd al-’Aziz, Saudi crown prince 
and deputy prime minister, paid an official visit to the Jamahir- 
iyah during which he delivered a message from King Fahd to the 
brother leader of the revolution. The message concerns the 
deteriorating Arab situation and joint Arab action to save the 
Arab nation and halt the Zionist death train in these difficult 
circumstances in which the Zionist plot against the Arab nation 
is growing stronger. After the Zionist occupation of Lebanon a 
year ago and the destruction, killing, and homelessness that 
accompanied it, the Zionist entity and the United States have 
been able to drag the Lebanese Government to sign an agreement 
which serves their objectives, which is considered another stage 
of the capitulation agreement signed by the Egyptian regime with 
the Zionist enemy and which achieves their objective of sep- 
arating Lebanon from the Arab world. This agreement is also 
regarded as an inevitable stage in the achievement of the U.S. 
colonialist plan presented under the name of the Reagan plan, 
which can become a reality only after eliminating the Palestinian 
resistance, neutralizing Lebanon, and forcing Syria to accept the 
agreement signed by the Lebanese Government with the Zionist 
enemy
