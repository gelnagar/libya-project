Summary (AI): An FBIS wire (May 1985) reports Sudanese officials' statements after Qadhafi's stopover in Sudan, where he congratulated Sudan on the uprising that removed Numayri and said he had stopped aid to John Garang's rebels. It concerns Libyan foreign policy, not Tripoli itself.
• Qadhafi said Numayri's regime caused the deterioration of relations between Libya and Sudan.
• Qadhafi reportedly told Sudanese officials he had stopped all assistance to John Garang.
• 1985-05-18: Qadhafi stopover in Sudan; talks with Gen. Siwar al-Dhahab, chairman of the Transitional Military Council, who said Qadhafi pledged to stop assistance to John Garang

((TEXT)) ON ANOTHER LEVEL, GEN SIWAR AL-DHAHAB, CHAIRMAN 

OF THE TRANSITIONAL MILITARY COUNCIL, ANNOUNCED THAT DURING THE 
TALKS COL AL-QADHDHAFI MADE IT CLEAR. THAT HE IS CONCERNED ABOUT 
ENDING THE FIGHTING THAT JOHN GARANG IS LEADING BECAUSE IT HAS 
NO PURPOSE NOW THAT NUMAYRI, AGAINST WHOM THE FIGHTING WAS 
WAGED, HAS BEEN OVERTHROWN BY THE SUDANESE PEOPLE. 

IN HIS STATEMENT TO REPORTERS AT THE ,CONCLUSION OF THE TALKS 
BETWEEN HIM AND AL-QADHDHAFI, SIWAR AL-DHAHAB SAID THAT COL 
AL-QADHDHAFI'S VISIT WAS AN AFFIRMATION OF THE GOOD RELATIONS 
BETWEEN THE TWO PEOPLES AND THAT AL-QADHDHAFI'S STOPOVER IN 
SUDAN REFLECTS THESE RELATIONS. HE POINTED OUT THAT 
AL-QADHDHAFI'S STOPOVER WAS AN EXPRESSION OF HIS FEELINGS TOWARD 
THE SUDANESE PEOPLE AND THEIR LEADERSHIP AND A MEANS TO 

EXPRESS HIS CONGRATULATIONS ON THE POPULAR UPRISING AND THE 
PEOPLE'S ARMED FORCES' SUPPORT FOR THE SUDANESE MASSES. 

GEN SIWAR AL-DHAHAB DESCRIBED THIS MEETING AS GOOD AND AS 

HAVING DEALT WITH VARIOUS ISSUES IN THE LOCAL, REGIONAL, AND 
ARAB ARENAS. HE SAID THAT COL AL-QADHDHAFI STRESSED THAT HE 

HAD STOPPED ALL ASSISTANCE TO JOHN GARANG. ASKED WHETHER 
ETHIOPIA INTENDS TO HALT AID TO JOHN GARANG, HIS EXCELLENCY 

SAID THAT COL AL-QADHDHAFI DEALT WITH THIS ISSUE DURING THE TALKS. 
AT THE BEGINNING OF HIS MEETING. WITH COL AL-QADHDHAFI, 

GEN SIWAR AL-DHAHAB DELIVERED A SPEECH, WELCOMING THE LIBYAN 
LEADER AND STRESSING SUDAN'S CONCERN ABOUT MAINTAINING STRONG 
RELATIONS WITH ITS NEIGHBORS AND THE ARAB COUNTRIES. 

COL AL-QADHDHAFI DELIVERED A SPEECH IN WHICH HE SAID THAT HIS 
STOPOVER IN SUDAN IS AIMED AT CONGRATULATING THE SUDANESE 

PEOPLE ON THEIR POPULAR REVOLUTION, NOTING THAT NUMAYRI'S 

REGIME WAS THE CAUSE OF THE DETERIORATION OF RELATIONS BETWEEN 
THE TWO COUNTRIES AND THAT RELATIONS WILL DEVELOP AND STRENGTHEN 
FOLLOWING NUMAYRI'S REMOVAL. 

PRIME MINSITER AL-JAZULI DAF'ALLAH ALSO DELIVERED A SPEECH, 
DECLARING THAT THE SUDANESE PEOPLE ARE CONCERNED ABOUT MAINTAINING 
BROTHERHOOD AND LOVE WITH THE LIBYAN PEOPLE AND THAT BROTHERHOOD 
AND LOVE COME BEFORE AID. HE AFFIRMED THAT THE SUDANESE 
REVOLUTION IS A GENUINE ONE.
