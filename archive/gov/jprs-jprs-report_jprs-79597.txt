The ministerial committee on Jerusalem, headed in the last government by Interior 
Minister Yosef Brug, was dismantled along with all ministerial committees when the 
new government took office 4 months ago and has not yet been reconstituted. The 
committee is responsible for coordinating development plans for the city. 


The ministerial committee on settlement, however, has been reconstituted under the 
chairmanship of Agriculture Minister Simha Ehrlich and has been busy drawing up 
plans in recent months for Jerusalem's periphery, including suburban settlement at 
the radar west of Jerusalem, adjoining kibbutz Ma'ale Hahamisha. 


Officials concerned with Jerusalem's development warned this week that by drawing 
off limited manpower resources to these suburban satellites, the settlement comnit- 
tee was endangering the Jewish character of Jerusalem itself. 

The officials urged the speedy reconstitution of the ministerial committee on 


Jerusalem and the establishment of coordination with the settlement committee on 
development of the Jerusalem metropolitan area.

general budget, deputies objected to an item in this budget that called 
for the appropriation of 12 million Lebanese pounds to overhaul Lebanon's

was required was the sale of these airplanes and not their overhaul because

the 1967 war and the 1973 war--without Lebanon's participation. In fact,

Lebanese government's asking price. Deputy Lahud said that Libya offered to 
purchase these airplanes because, on the one hand, it has Mirage airplanes

standpoint these airplanes are non-usable. A small country like Lebanon no 
longer needs this kind of military aircraft. In fact, small countries like 
Lebanon find it useful to use missile air defense bases which are mili-

Lahud cited as an example of this what happened in Israel as soon as Syria
