SOVIET DEPUTY AGRICULTURE MINISTER ARRIVES FOR VISIT 

LD172150Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 17 Jan 78 LD 

[Text] The Soviet deputy minister of agriculture arrived in Tripoli at noon today on 
a visit of a few days to the Jamahiriyah. He is to hold talks with officials in the 
agriculture secretariat connected with cooperation and the exchange of agriculture ex- 


perience between the Jamahiriyah and the Soviet Union.
