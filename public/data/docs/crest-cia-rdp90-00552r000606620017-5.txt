OFFICIALS SAY GREEN BERETS AIDED IN LIBYAN TERRORIST TRAINING PLAN

Summary (AI): A 1981 New York Times article reports that Army Special Forces (Green Berets) let at least 10 former soldiers train terrorists in Libya in 1977, tied to Edwin P. Wilson's deal with Qaddafi. The article discusses whether the CIA sanctioned the operation; no Tripoli sites are named.
• At least 10 men trained as Green Berets travelled to Libya in 1977 to help train terrorists, according to participants and Federal investigators.
• Edwin P. Wilson agreed in 1978 to sell his intelligence and military expertise to Qaddafi for training terrorists, according to Federal investigators.
• The CIA denied official involvement; an internal investigation led to the dismissal of two officials found to be assisting Wilson.
• The House Select Committee on Intelligence was to open an investigation of the Wilson case.
• 1977-07: Former Green Beret Luke F. Thompson and others travelled to Libya in late July 1977 to help train terrorists under Edwin P. Wilson's arrangement.
