Summary (AI): A Reuters item dated 20 April 1983 reports that Brazil ordered unloading of arms and explosives found on four Libyan cargo planes (three Soviet-built Ilyushins and a Hercules) that had stopped to refuel in Brazil en route to Nicaragua. Qaddafi expressed regret over the incident and blamed imperialist and Zionist exploitation. The document has no Tripoli-specific sites.
• Four Libyan planes: three Soviet-built Ilyushins and one U.S.-built Hercules
• Brazil lodged an official protest alleging a breach of trust, as Libya had cited medical supplies
• Brazil's National Security Council decided on the unloading at the Presidential Palace in Brasilia
• Arms were to be returned to Libya, possibly by sea
• Nicaragua was the planes' intended destination; Colombia had received a request for overflight
• 1983-04-18: Brazil seized four Libyan cargo planes after inspecting cargoes on the evening of the stop; the cargo reportedly contained arms and explosives, and Libya had requested transit for ostensibly medical supplies
