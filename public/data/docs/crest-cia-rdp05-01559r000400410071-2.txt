Al-Oadhdhafi on U.S. ‘Intervention’ in Chad. 
NC220809 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 
0600 GMT 22 Dec 86 

[Text] The brother leader of the revolution attended the 
graduation ceremony for the third and fourth classes of the 
Command and Staff College yesterday. Colonel(Mustafa al- 
KharrubjJand a number of Armed Forces officers were also 
present on the occasion. 

After the graduation ceremony and the supreme commander’s 
decree granting the graduates their staff insignia and ranks were 
read, the brother leader of the revolution handed out graduation 
certificates to the newly graduated staff officers. The leader then 
congratulated the graduates and spoke to them about the grave 
situation in Chad resulting from the U.S.-French military inter- 
vention. , 

The leader said that the United States hopes to turn Chad into a 
military base, which it would use to consolidate its imperialistic 
presence in Africa and to threaten Libya and, subsequently, 
Sudan and the rest of the Arab world. France, the leader added, 
is also eager to consolidate its long-standing imperialistic pres- 
ence in Africa, particularly in Chad. We do not countenance their 
presence. They are imperialists and criminals, because they have 
power.

The leader said that Habre’s forces are waging war on the

However, these countries are incapable of prolonged confronta- 
tion with the Chadian people in a desert war.
