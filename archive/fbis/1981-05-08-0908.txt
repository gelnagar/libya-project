LIBYA

STATEMENT DENOUNCES U.S. EXPULSION OF DIPLOMATS 


LDO71956 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1900 GMT 7 May 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 May (JANA)--The U.S. Government has asked the staff of the Libyan Arab 
People's Bureau in Washington to leave the United States on the pretext that th= Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah supports international terrorism and export: revol:tion. 

The People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison has issued a statement refuting the U.S. a).legations 
and charges. The statement clarifies the Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah's position toward and its 
condemnation of international terrorism as can be seen from the stands it has adopted and 
international agreements it has signed in this regard. The foilowing is the tex: of the 
statement: 


Yesterday, the administration of U.S. President Reagan requested the closing down of the 
Libyan Arab People's Bureau in Washington and the deportation of all its staff. It gave as 
an excuse for this closure and the deportation what it claimed was Libyan support for inter- 
national terrorism and the export of the revolution. 


The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah wishes to point out that the U.S. administra- 
tion's decision comes as a surprise to no one. The U.S. administration has accustomed every- 
one to its position of taking a hostile stand against all voices that rise to oppose its 
imperialist policy and which stand in the path of its ambitions in the Arab area and other 
parts of the world, paying no attention to the growing vigilance of the peoples of the Third 
World and their desire to determine their own future by themselves without America's 
custodianship and domination. 


In deciding to close down the bureau and expel its staff, the U.S. administration resorted 

to feeble and unfounded pretexts which have nothing to do with the good relations which the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has been trying to bolster with all world peoples, 
including the U.S. people, with a view to realizing these peoples’ ambitions for the establish- 
ment of international peace and security on the basis of justice and freedom. 


The attitude of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah toward international terrorism 
is clear and has been reiterated on more than one occasion and at more than one time. It 
condemns international terrorism which does away with the lives of innocent people. Further- 
more, Libya is a party to all international agreements on civil aviation protection and safety. 


Before trying to level charges of international terrorism against others, the U.S. administra- 
tion must remember the acts of international terrorism it has committed in Vietnam, El Salvador 
and Nicaragua, when its aircraft bombed thousands of innocent people, destroying houses and 
bridges and the basic infrastructures of these countries. The U.S. administration has also 
forgotten the policy of starvation and blackmail which it practices against the peoples of 
Nicaragua and other struggling peoples by imposing an embargo on the sale of grain to these 
peoples. 


The U.S. administration must also remember that the production of nuclear weapons, the estab- 
lishment of military bases and the stationing of U.S. fleets in the Mediterranean are all 
forms of international terrorism. 


There is a difference between acts of international terrorism pursued by the United States 

and the support given to peoples suffering under the yoke of imperialism and foreign domina- 
tion. The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah does not conceal its support and bolster- 
ing of these peoples and does not see this as an act of international terrorism as the U.S. 
administration claims: Indeed, it sees it as a humanitarian duty toward these peoples.

The question of international terrorism is a matter which concerns the whole world and 
consequently its definition and determination should not be confined to the United States 
alone. Furthermore, the United States is not a custodian of the world so that it can 
pass sentences haphazardly and evaluate the actions and behavior of countries. 


As to the U.S. administration's claim of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah's 
exportation of revolution, this is a flimsy allegation since thoughts and principles know 
no borders. If there are some peoples that have adopted the thoughts and principles of 
the Great 1 September Revolution, this is a matter that concerns these peoples alone. 

We do not believe this is a matter contrary to international law or any international 
tradition that concerns the United States. 


The real reason behind the decision to deport members of the people's bureau does not lie 
in the feeble pretexts claimed by the U.S. administration. It is due to the U.S. admin- 
istration's inability to acknowledge the growing vigilance of the peoples of the region 
and their desire to control their own destinies and rid themselves of all custodianship 
which is imposed on them, 


If the U.S. administration persists with its aggressive policy against people of the 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, their revolution and plans, then the Libyan Arab people will, 


through their basic congresses, adopt stands which can confront this aggressive policy. 


AL-QADHDHAFI SPEAKS ON DEFENSE OF ARAB NATION 


LD072316 Tripoli JANA in English 1515 GMT 7 May 81 
[All quotation marks as received] 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 May (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--Colonel Mu‘'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader 
of the Great al-Fateh Revolution, addressed the cadets of the military colleges. He re- 
ferred to the grave deteriorating situation of the Arab world and said that if this 
situation continues to worsen, the Arab nation will face a fatal defeat and will probably 
become like the red Indians. He added that most of the Arab regimes are contributing to 
the Arabs' tragic crisis by their defeatism in face of the colonialist offensive on the 
Arab world. 


"The American terrorism exercised on the Arab nation forms a strategic umbrella under 
which Zionism is gaining new grounds stage after stage, so that it achieves its targets 
in favour of a zionist empire to replace the Arab nation in the future." 


"Jerusalem, in the near past, was made a permanent capital of the Israelis' after thirty 
years of the making of 'Israel." This shows that the enemy has strategic aims trying to 
reach stage afrer stage to achieve a greater stage in the most important and strategic 
areas of the Arab world. These areas namely the Arab east, where you find oil fields, 
holy places, strategic sites, water resources of the Nile, Tigris, Euphrates, Yarmuk and 
Litani, partly usurped by the 'Israelis' and on their way to capture the rest.” 


"There is no barrier between the enemy and the oilfields in the Gulf, as the strategic 
areas of the Suez Canal, the Red Sea and Bab al-Mandeb Strait, are partly in the hands 

of the ‘Israelis’ in the process to have control over the rest,'' Colonel al-Qadhdhafi 
stressed, ''From the economic aspect, what was used to be called as the fertile crescent, 
is now a target of the ‘Israeli’ enemy." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added, "The occupied West Bank and Gaza Strip became under the 
‘Israeli’ occupation and Jerusalem has been annexed to become the ‘Israeli’ eternal 
capital. The Jordanian army is now fighting against the Iranian revolution on behalf 
of the Americans. Iraq has exerted all her efforts economically and militarily, on 
the wrong front that is not hostile to the Arab nation."

"When Iran was under the late shah's rule, who used to supply ‘Israel’ with oil, Saddam's 
regime signed a treaty in Algiers with the shah and as a result he gave up Arab rights 

in Shatt al-Arab and settled all existing problems then with Iran. But following the 
downfall of the shah regime the vicious enemy of Arab nationalism and the main oil- 
supplier to racist South Africa, and the emergence of the Iranian revolution, the new 
ally of the Arab nation and the Palestinian people, we see Saddam's regime building its 
forces along Iranian borders and fighting the revolution on behalf of the Americans." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said "It is very unlikely that Iran and Iraq will constitute an 
effective threat to the (Israeli) enemy in the near future." 


On the grave Lebanese situation, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that the enemy is trying to 
create a de facto situation under (Israeli) control, stressed by the daily (Israeli) 
sorties in the Lebanese skies, totally ignoring the United Nation, the Security Council 
and the Arab nation. "When a foreign aircraft violates another country's air space, all 
the countries of the world, the Security Council, and the Non-Aligned Movement protest 
against this, but now they are used to see Lebanese skies violated by (Israeli) air- 
crafts. We are also used to this, and to us, it sounds as if some died of over eating 
in the Arab Peninsula." [sentence as received] 


He said "South Lebanon is not any more part of Lebanon. It is under traitor Haddad's 
forces control, and the (Israeli) troops. Practically it has been annexed to the occupied 
territories. This is a reality because the lands controlled by Haddad's forces is 
actually occupied by the (Israelis). 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added: "The Palestinian resistance is now in the process of liquid- 
ation and destruction in Lebanon to be able to create a Maronite mini state as flank to 
the "Israeli'."” Their objective remains the same: striking a siege around Syria and 
destroying her totally. And if she fell, God forbid, then U.S. and Israeli sort of 
peace will be imposed on the Arabs. "The Syrian front is the only line of confrontation 
with Israel, and should it fall, there would be no other ditch to fight from. Because 
Egypt has fallen now open to the "Israelis" Jordan can do nothing against the advance 

of the Israelis. Moreover, Iraq has replaced her Palestinian cause by a war against the 
Iranian revolution on behalf of the United States. 


"The conspiracy is growing against Syria and the main objectives of the military opera- 
tions in Lebanon are to Syria and deal a blow to her resistance and steadfastness as 
Lebanon is no obstruction for the Israelis to implement their aforesaid plans."

He said "There is a great threat to the Palestinians because they are confined in a 
limited area at a time when they should be deployed all along the Arab confrontation line 
with the enemy. The failure of the Arabs to stand up to the colonialist and Zionist 
invasion, will encourage the (Israelis) to continue implementing the plot which will lead 
to a Zionist empire and Arab weakness". 


Recently Western countries’ officials of Britain, Germany, France and the U.S.--increased 
their visits to the Gulf region as a result of the latter's oil resources, in order to 
drain out the areas wealth. 


The leader referred to the situation in North African by saying "If we exclude Libya and 
Algeria, there is nothing positive in this wide arena. Morocco's historic mission today 
is to destroy the Sahara's people and annex the Sahara as though it wants to liberate 
Palestine. Morocco is in alliance with all the Arab nation's enemies in order to crush 
the Polisario's revolution. As for Mauritania, it is seeking salvation, but conspiracy 
will increase against it to take it back to a condition of subservience to Morocco's 
king, France, America and all the forces hostile to the Arab nation". 


He added "The Arab nation's part overlooking the ocean [as received] is being prevented 
from advancing despite the existence of a positive factor in Mauritania, namely the 
growth of a unionist revolutionary force in the Mauritanian streets which will settle 
Mauritania's future".

"The Lebanese National Movement should be supported and backed by volunteers and arms,

"The Steadfastness and Confrontation Front should change to a c«ounter-offensive position

He said: "The traditional systems make the military an instrument of obediance as they

tion which are the allies and the agents of imperialist forces and their extension. 


In the Jamahiriyah the government which deploys all means to stay in power has ended

AL-QADHDHAFI PHONES BACKING TO SYRIA'S AL~ASAD 


LDO72314 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1917 GMT 7 May 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 May (JANA)--A telephone conversation took place this evening between 

the commander of the revolution and Syrian President Hafiz al-Asad. The two men discussed 
the treacherous Zionist aggression against Lebanon, the Palestinian revolution and the 
enemy, challenge, which has gone beyond all reasonable limits. The enemy hag gone to the 
extent of disregarding the Arabs, violating the sanctity of their territories and threaten- 
ing to strike at Syrian forces inside the Arab territories--and then asking the Arabs not 
to reply to their actions, as though they had no right to defend themselves. The telephone 
conversation also covered the Arab cause in general and the need for all the Arabs to 
counter the aggression with all the might they possess, since keeping silent about the 
Zionist aggression against the Arab nation has become a disgrace to the nation. 


The brother commander affirmed to President Hafiz al-Asad the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah's firm and principled stand alongside fraternal Syria and the Palestinian 
resistance, the Lebanese National Movement and the Lebanese people. The brother commander 
also stressed the need for continued contacts between Libya and Syria, and the other Arab 
countries, and for Arab action which would put an end to this Israeli bluster. 


LIBYAN STANCE ON MOROCCO EXPLAINED TO ARAB ENVOYS 


LD071452 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 7 May 81 


[Text] The brother secretary of the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison met at noon today with the brother ambassadors of Arab countries to the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. The brother secretary explained to the brother Arab 
ambassadors that the object of this meeting is to explain the stance of the Jamahiriyah 
toward the Moroccan regime and the political caupaign which (?intends to carry out) in a 
large number of the countries of the world in an attempt to escalate its stance, which is 
hostile to the people of the Jamahiriyah and their prominent pan-Arab stances. 


The brother secretary of the Foreign Liaison Bureau affirmed in this respect that the 
Jamahiriyah considers the current Arab situation graver and more important than any 
bilateral differences, and that on the basis of its pan-Arab concept it considers that 
such conduct shows that there are Arab rulers who do not think of or see other than their 
own interest so that they might perpetuate their stay in power. 


The brother secretary of the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison 
pointed out that the leader's cable addressed to Arab presidents and kings has met with a 
good response, since loyal Arab officials have begun their contacts with a view to con- 
fronting and checking the Zionist aggression against the Lebanese people, the Palestinian 
resistance and Syria, and against the Arab nation and its ambitions--whereas the king of 
Morocco is charging his government's envoys with the task of touring the countries of the 
world on the pretext that there are dangers threatening North Africa from the Jamahiriyah, 
which stands by the side of the cause of the Saharan people in their self-determination 
and which supports the Polisario Front politically and materially. 


V. 8 May 81 Q 6 NORTH AFRICA 


He stressed that the proposal of the brother leader regarding the establishment of unity 
between Mauritania and the Saharan Republic and the establishment of an alliance between 
Mauritania, Algeria and Libya stem from the pan-Arab concept of the Social People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah, which is aware of the dangers confronting the region of North Africa 
from the agent Moroccan regime, since its role has become clear as a result of its threats 
to Mauritania, the Jamahiriyah and Algeria and its repeated aggression against the Saharan 
people. 


He said that the reason that the king of Morocco launched this suspicious political 

campaign was the exposure made by the Jamahiriyah delegation at the meeting of the 
Jerusalem Committee in Fes with regard to the role of the agent king of Morocco, who 
betrayed the Arab cause, and his role in paving the way for the visit of the ruler of 

Egypt to Israel, as well as his successive meetings with both Moshe Dayan and Yitzhaq Rabin. 


The brother secretary of the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison 
asked: How can a ruler who has contacts with the Zionist enemy, worked for the departure 
of Egypt from its pan-Arab responsibility and participated in the reaching of the Camp 
David agreement chair the Jerusalem Committee of the Islamic Conference? He said the 
Jamahiriyah challenges the king of Morocco to liberate Jerusalem. He explained that the 
difference between the Jamahiriyah and Morocco is a difference of principles and a 
difference in stances, and that there are no differences between the two peoples, and that 
the king of Morocco is considered one who supplements the role of the ruler of Egypt.
