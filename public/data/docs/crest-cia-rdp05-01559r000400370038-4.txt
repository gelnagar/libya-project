Summary (AI): An FBIS wire relays a Tripoli radio broadcast in which Colonel Qadhdhafi says Libya and Algeria are undertaking a joint action to advance Arab interests, after talks with Algerian President Houari Boumediene. It is foreign policy rhetoric with no specific Tripoli sites or local events.
• The statement was broadcast by Voice of the Arab Homeland from Tripoli at 2315 GMT on 13 February 1976.
• Qadhdhafi said the joint action would be of special significance given the threats from imperialist forces abroad and reactionary forces at home.
• Asked whether this meant shifting from defensive to offensive, he replied that this was the correct and required conclusion.
• 1976-02-13: Qadhdhafi ends talks with Algerian President Houari Boumediene in Tripoli and announces a joint Libyan-Algerian action
