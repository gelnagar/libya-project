FOREIGN SECRETARIAT ISSUES STATEMENT ON NAMIBIA 


LD122004¥Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 12 Dec 78 LD 


(Text ] The Foreign Secretariat today issued a statement on the unlawful measures being 
taken by the authorities of South Africa in connection with the elections in Namibia. 
The statement said that the Jamahiriyah, which is among the vanguard of the forces of 
liberation resisting racism in all its forms, strongly condemns those sham elections in 
which deception and violence are being used to forge the people!s will in Namibia. 


The statement added that the Jamahiriyah denounces this action through which the regime 
of the racist minority in the southern continent is defying the will of the people of 
Namibia and the peoples of the entire world, and at the same time the Jamahiriyah reminds 
the international community that these deceitful and reckless actions prove the existence 
of the contempt for human rights and the right of peoples to freedom and a dignified 

life. The statement affirmed anew the absolute support of the Jamahiriyah to the struggle 
of the people of Namibia under the leadership of the South-West African People!s Organi- 
zation. 


INDIAN DELEGATION HOLDS TALKS ON ECONOMIC COOPERATION 
BK121111y Delhi ISI in English 0917 GMT 12 Dec 78 BK 


[Text ] New areas of economic and technical cooperation between India and Libya have been 
identified at the first meeting of the Indo-Libyan joint commission that began in Tripoli, 
Libya, on 10 December, 


The Indian delegation to the meeting is being led by George Fernandes, union minister 

of industry, who is chairman of the commission, Fernandes arrived in Tripoli on 9 December 
and was warmly welcomed by the Libyan cochairman, At the plenary session of the commis- 
sion on 10 December, progress in the implementation of the protocol signed between India 
and Libya at the conclusion of the visit of the Libyan vice president, Staff Major tabd 
as-Salam Jallud, to India last July was reviewed and ways of further cooperation were 
explored, 


Fernandes also had separate meeticrs with President Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and the vice 
president, Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud, He informed them about the new policy and 
programme initiatives taken by India during the past 20 months and explained to them the 
all-round economic progress made by the country during this period.

He told the Libyan leaders that India was willing to share her technical expertise 
with other developing countries and assured them of the determination of the Government
