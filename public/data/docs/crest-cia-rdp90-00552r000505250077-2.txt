Summary (AI): A March 1982 NBC Today Show segment reports Chris Wallace's interview with Colonel Muammar Qaddafi in Libya. Qaddafi rejects a U.S. ban on Libyan oil imports, vows to defend the country against U.S. forces, and accuses President Reagan of distracting Americans from economic problems.
• Qaddafi said there was no justification for a U.S. ban on Libyan oil imports.
• Qaddafi said Saudi Arabia should cut oil production by two to three million barrels a day, or OPEC might be destroyed.
• Qaddafi said Libya would defend its country 'till the last drop of our blood'.
• The report references two U.S. planes shooting down two Libyan jets over the Gulf of Sidra the previous summer.
• 1982-03-06: Chris Wallace interviews Qaddafi for about an hour in Libya on Saturday night (reported as the weekend before 8 March 1982)
