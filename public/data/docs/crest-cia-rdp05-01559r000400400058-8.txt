SUBJECT: MEDIA REACTION: U.S.-LIBYA. 
UNDER HEADLINE "THE U.S. IS RESPONSIBLE," ARABIC WEEKLY 
AL BAYANE (CLOSE TO CHAMBER OF COMMERCE) REPORTED 
SEPTEMBER 16 THAT "AFTER QADHAFI'S CONTINUING CLAIM 
THAT HE IS NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR THE EXPULSION OF 
TUNISIAN AND EGYPTIAN WORKERS FROM LIBYA, AND STATING 
THAT THIS WAS 'THE RESULT OF A DECISION TAKEN BY THE 
PEOPLE'S COMMITTEES,' HE RECENTLY BLAMED THE U.S. FOR 
THIS MASS EXPULSION. HE EXPLAINED THAT THESE MEASURES 
WERE IMPOSED BECAUSE OF BUDGET RESTRICTIONS THAT ARE 
THE RESULT OF WHAT HE DESCRIBED AS THE "U.S. ECONOMIC 
BLOCKADE AGAINST LIBYA." THE PAPER ASKED "WHERE DOES 
THE TRUTH LIE?" THE PAPER ADDED THAT "COMMENTING ON 
THE U.S. DECISION NOT TO EXPORT TO LIBYA ANY HIGHLY 
SOPHISTICATED TECHNICAL PRODUCTS QADHAFI STATED IN HIS 
LATEST SPEECH THAT 'THE LIBYAN PEOPLE CAN DO WITHOUT 
THE BANANAS, THE SWEETS AND ALL THE PRODUCTS SENT BY 
THE U.S. AND THAT AIM AT DESTROYING THE PEOPLE OF 
DEVELOPING COUNTRIES'." ITEM. SCHOONOVER
