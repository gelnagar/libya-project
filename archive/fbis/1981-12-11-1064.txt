LIBYA

TRIPOLI REACTS TO CLARK STATEMENT ON LIBYA 


LD110038 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2300 GMT 10 Dec 81 


[Text] William Clark, acting U.S. secretary of state, yesterday evening made a statement 
in which he discussed the measures that President Reagan has decided to take regarding U.S. 
citizens in Libya or their travel to Libya. Here is the text of the statement as it was 
broadcast yesterday evening by the Voice of America. 


On behalf of President Reagan, I met with leaders of the Congress today and contacted the 
high directors of the U.S. companies which have officials in Libya. I have discussed the 
Libyan efforts, which have been known for many years, to destroy the butresses of the U.S. 
interests and the interests of our friends. During the last 6 months, Libya has expanded 

the scope of its efforts and the speed of these efforts -- aimed at destroying the buttresses 
of the neighboring states and working against the interests of the United States -- have in- 
creased. Consequently, the prevailing security atmosphere in relation to U.S. citizens in 
Libya has worsened. Owing to the danger posed by the Libyan regime against the U.S. citizens, 
President Reagan calls upon all Americans there to leave Libya as soon as possible. In light 
of the increasing danger, it has been decided immediately to cancel travel to Libya for all 
those holding U.S. passports. President Reagar is also ready to take whatever legal measures 
are necessary to make Americans leave Libya if that becomes necessary. The United States, 
while aware of the gravity of these measures, is convinced that Libyan actions are forcing 

it to these measures, as it would be irresponsible for the United States to hesitate to take 
them. 


The political commentator commented on the statement which included the U.S. measures against 
the Jamahiriyah and which was announced on behalf of President Reagan, saying: It is the 
first time the Reagan administration has spoken the complete truth -- that Libya has been 
working for years, since the Great Fatih Revolution began, to destroy U.S. interests. 
Therefore, the assassinations and terrorism of which it accuses Libya are nothing but 
superstition. We are proud, and all Arabs feel proud with us, that the United States is 
taking such a hostile stance toward the Great Fatih Revolution and its leader. The official 
statement declared that Libya has destroyed U.S. interests. This is absolutely clear as U.S. 
interests contradict the Great Fatih Revolution interests, the pan-Arab interests and the 
interests of all peoples struggling against U.S. imperialism. The U.S. interests, as is 
known, are achieved at the expense of others’. 


We are proud of destroying U.S. interests. For the first time, the U.S. administration's 
stances show that the issue of the alleged assassinations and terrorism is a fabrication 
with no truth to it. It now appears to the world that it is the United States who is 
practicing terrorism in a flagrant manner.

JANA DENIES ALLEGATIONS ON ASSASSINATION SQUADS 


LD101944 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 10 Dec 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 10 Dec (JANA) -- The Jamahiriyah UN mission today issued the following 
statement about the U.S. administration's allegations and accusations against the Jamahiriyah 
regarding the sending of groups to assassinate Reagan. 


President Ronald Reagan's administration continues to reiterate the false allegations 
about Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah sending groups to assassinate President 
Reagan and some officials of the U.S. administration. The Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah categorically denies such allegations. On 10 December 1981, the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah reasserted this categorical denial to the Belgian ambas- 
sador who looks after Libyan interests in the United States. On 23 November 1981, the 
Jamahiriyah also reaffirmed to the UN secretary general and to the president of the Security 
Council that there is no foundation at all for these allegations. The Jamahiriyah showed 
readiness to receive a commission from the Security Council to investigate the truth of 
these allegations. Through the leader of the revolution's 7 December interview with the 
American television network, the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah stressed 

that we reject these allegations and challenge Reagan's administration to produce a single 
piece of evidence to support the truth of these allegations. 


In letters he addressed on 8 December to the leaders of the U.S. Senate and House of 
Representatives, the secretary of the General People's Congress in the Jamahiriyah stressed 
that the Jamahiriyah is prepared to receive a factfinding committee to establish the false- 
hood of these allegations. 


It appears that these American practices have become a part of the policy of terrorizing 
people, a policy that is being exercised by President Reagan's administration against the 
Jamahiriyah in particular. 


The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah UN mission affirms that the Jamahiriyah is 
prepared to receive a factfinding committee from the UN Security Council or from the U.S. 


Congress to establish the falsehood of the allegations of President Ronald Reagan.

[Text] [Question] Good evening, sir. Does the prospect of assassination worry you at 
all or do you ignore that concern? 


[Answer] Pardon? 


[Question] Does the possibility of an attack on you, maybe by the people here or people 
outside, does that worry you? 


[Answer -- in English] No, I'm not afraid. I'm not afraid of such a thing. 


[Question] Even when the greatest power on earth, the U.S. has -- or many of its 
senior officials have -- declared that they would like to see you finished?

[Answer] We just see that as part of American terrorist poiicies, policies which are 
hostile to the people. 


[Question] You are confronting Western interests at every turn, 


[Answer] This is propaganda, the size of Libya and its capabilities simply do not match 
such accusations. They are merely justifications for aggression against Libya, prompted 
in the first place by the wealth of Libya and its strategic position and also to pre- 
vent the Arab nations from uniting and making progress. 


[Question] Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, you know that the accusation, or allegation, against you, 
your government, is that you support -- through money, through weapons and through 
training in Libya -- groups of terrorists ranging from the IRA in our own country to 
the Italian Red Brigades and so on. What's your response to that? 


[Answer--in English] It's just propaganda and allegations without evidence. 


[Question] What about the terror groups in Europe who are said to have training facilities 
here in Libya? Is that true or not? 


{[Answer--in English] It is not true at all. 
[Question] No European groups are receiving any such training, for instance? 


[Answer] We have no relations with such groups at all, and we are absolutely against 
terrorism and these terrorist groups in Europe. 


[Question] Can I turn to the pursuit of Libyan exiles, therefore, who are opponents of 
yours on the streets of London, the killing of opponents of your regime? Now what's 
your reaction to that? 


[Answer] This is something which is clearly understood. It was carried out by 
revolutionary committees abroad on their own responsibility. It was considered a 
revolutionary act, not a government one. 


[Question] You spoke at the time in a way that seemed to justify the acts of your 
revolutionary committees when they killed people on the streets of London, when they 
killed people on the streets of Rome, when apparently still they are killing people in 
the U.S.? 


[Answer] People get killed and we only hear about it from the news agencies. This 
indicates that it is not a government decision at all. I cannot possibly control the 
work of revolutionary committees abroad. I don't know them or even the names of those 
people who get killed. 


[Question] But they are revolutionary committees, Colonel Al-Qadhdhafi, of your 
revolution, aren't they? And they are pursuing your opponents in Europe and in the 
U.S. 


[Answer] There are revoluntionaries in every corner of the world and they cannot be 
controlled. It is not a political act. Libya as a state cannot accept that responsibility. 


[Question] You're saying that you are opposed to any pursuit, to any action against 
exiles, against Libyans living abroad? 


[Answer [ am against assassination, against any [word indistinct] any action, any 
system. It is against my belief. 


[Question] Which guerrilla groups received Libyan assistance?

[Answer] We trained the Palestinian people to fight and that is our national duty. This 
is a just cause and we are prepared to accept the full consequences. We have trained 
armed forces from Zimbabwe. We trained Angolan forces and forces from Namibia. These 
are just causes. These are people who are fighting for freedom. This training is public 
knowledge and we broadcast news of it every day. 


[Question] You're saying that you would support any opposition movement against any 
government that was pro-West. 


[Answer] Certainly we would support the opposition to any government which was a puppet 
of the West. But at the same time, it must be clearly understood that we do not oppose 
such governments directly. We support the opposition to them, just as the colonialists 
support governments which are loyal to them, so we support popular movements against such 
government. 


[Question] How far are you prepared to go in supporting these opposition movements? 
You send them arms? You give them training in Libya? Is that legitimate? 


[Answer -- in English] No, I speak politically, but when you ask about instruments and 
arms, something like this, it is silly questions. I speak politically, and I support 
politically. I don't speak about materials and instruments, how they do and where they 
do. It is their responsibility. 


[Question] I take the distinction you are making between material and political support, 
but the allegation against you and your regime is that you give material support to the 


opponents of Western movements. But is it true? 


[Answer] It is America and the Western powers who are giving military support to the 
governments involved. 


[Question] But does that justify you, therefore, in giving military equipment or military 
training to all those who oppose Western interests? 


[Answer] This is a lie. 


[Question] May I ask what was your personal reaction to the news of President as-Sadat's 
assassination? 


[Answer] He was not assassinated, he was executed. Everyone throughout the Arab world 
was happy about it. 


[Question] But for some years you were quite friendly with As-Sadat. You had good 
relations, you got to know the man well. 


[Answer] Well, I didn't answer your first question in a personal capacity. I answered 
it politically. 


[Question] Did you have any personal reaction? 


[Answer] Of course, as an individual, as a human being, I cannot possibly be pleased 
at the killing, at the death of someone I knew. But from a political point of view, I 
was quite happy. 


[Question] What is your response to U.S. pressure on your personally and your regime
