Summary (AI): A 1978 CIA DCI briefing note assessing Libya's recent moderation in terrorism, Soviet relations and support for regional dissidents, along with Qadhafi's domestic position and Libya's arms inventory. It has no specific Tripoli locations, but it gives national-level figures on Libyan forces and weapons.
• Soviet-bloc weapons contracts estimated at $2.6 billion, 1974-1978.
• Libyan armed forces about 45,000 personnel versus about 500,000 in Egypt.
• Libya had about 500 medium tanks and 600 APCs versus Egypt's 2,200 tanks and 2,400 APCs.
• Soviet military advisers in Libya increased by 1,500-1,700 since the Egypt attack; presence doubled since July 1977.
• Estimate that Qadhafi survived no fewer than 5 assassination attempts in recent years.
• Libya's oil accounted for about 98% of exports and 55% of GDP, per the note.
• Libya had never acceded to Soviet requests for naval bases.
