Summary (AI): A State Department cable relays a July 1982 New York Times report, based on a West Berlin newspaper, that Qadhafi met West European antinuclear and Green-linked activists in Tripoli and urged them to close US bases in Europe through protests and cutting off water and supplies. It concerns Libyan foreign policy rather than events inside Tripoli.
• The New York Times report, datelined Bonn, July 26, 1982, relayed a West Berlin newspaper report (Die Tageszeitung).
• Qadhafi reportedly said US bases in Western Europe must be dismantled and that Fedayeen groups would be founded again to reach this goal.
• Qadhafi reportedly called for a boycott of the United States and Israel and for an end to immigration to Israel.
• The Green Party executive did not know in advance of the visit by its members, according to a party spokesman.
• 1982-07: Qadhafi met representatives of West European antinuclear groups, including German Greens members, and proposed protests at US bases in Europe.
