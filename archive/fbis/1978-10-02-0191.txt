LIBYA

AT-TURAYKI CONTINUES BRIEFINGS ON CAMP DAVID 
LD291016Y Tripoli JANA in English 0910 GMT 29 Sep 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, September 29, JANA--The Foreign Secretariat is continuing to conduct 
its contacts with friendly and sisterly states to brief them on the outcome of the 3rd 
summit of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front which was held in Damascus last week 
and to explain the Jamahiriyah's rejectionist stand towards the "Stable David" accords 
and the Egyptian president's concessions, 


Dr, ‘Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, the foreign secretary, yesterday met with the Arab 
ambassadors to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and informed them of the 
results of the third summit of the National Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation, as 
well as the current situation in the Arab homeland, 


The foreign secretary explained that the Arab nation rejects such agreements as those 
signed at "Stable David" and emphasized that the Arab nation is undergoing a grave stage 
which necessitates surpassing the stage of issuing statements to the stage of taking 
practical measures to affront the conspiracy to which the Arab nation is being subjugated. 


Dr, at-Turayki said that the Jamahiriyah had drawn attention to the seriousness of the 
visit by the Egyptian president to Jerusalem and its grave consequences. The accords 
signed at "Stable David" were one of these consequences, 


The foreign secretary re-affirmed to Arab ambassadors that the current stage the Arab 
nation is undergoing necessitates thatcollective or individual practical steps be taken 
to frustrate the conspiracy and eliminate its consequences, In this connection, the 
foreign secretary referred to the need for overriding differences and unifying efforts 
to face the new grave situation in the Arab homeland, which constitutes a threat to the 
very existence of the Arab nation,

TRIPOLI STATION CITES BEIRUT REPORT ON SHI'ITE IMAM 
LD502112Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 30 Sep 78 LD 


[Text] In a dispatch from Beirut citing information being circulated among persons 
close to the Supreme Shi'ite Council in Lebanon, Kuwait's AL-ANBA' reported today that 
it had been learned that a room in one of Rome's grand hotels had been booked in the 
name of Imam Musa as-Sadr and that his bag and passport were found in the room, Another 
room was found to contain the bags and clothes of his companion, Shaykh Muhammad Ya'qub, 
Both their passports were found to have been stamped by Rome airport authorities, in- 
dicating that the two men had entered Italy by way of Rome airport. 


PEOPLE'S CONGRESS DELEGATION LEAVES FOR MOSCOW 
LD281120Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 amT 28 Sep 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--A delegation of the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 
left Tripoli International Airport this morning for Moscow, The delegation is led by 
'Umar al-Hamidi, secretary general of the Permanent Secretariat, At the airport the 
delegation was seen off by members of the General People's Congress, the GDR ambassador 
and the charge d'affaires of the Soviet Embassy in Tripoli. 


In a statement to a JANA correspondent, the president of the public prosecutor's office 
of the Arab People's Court and member of the delegation said: We are leaving Tripoli 
for the Soviet Union and then for the GDR and Poland to hold meetings and consultations 
with our friends in these countries to strengthen the bonds of friendship between our 
Arab people and the peoples of those countries, We will also acquaint them with our 
stand toward what is currently happening in the Arab arena with regard to conspiracies 
against the Arab cause, 


He expressed his hope that the visit will yield good results that will contribute toward 
the further strengthening of cohesion among all progressive and patriotic forces in 
these countries,
