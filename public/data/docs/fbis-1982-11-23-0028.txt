LIBYA

SOVIET AIR MARSHAL SKORIKOV ARRIVES IN LIBYA 


LD221626 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 22 Nov 82 

[Text] Tripoli, 22 Nov (JANA) -- Marshal of Aviation Skorikov, chief of the Main 
Staff of the Soviet Air Forces, arrived in Tripoli at noon today for a visit to the 
Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


UN SECRETARY GENERAL ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 


LD201350 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 20 Nov 82 


[Text] UN Secretary General Perez de Cuellar arrived in Tripoli at noon today on a 
visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. De Cuellar made a statement 
to a JANA representative in which he expressed his joy at visiting the Jamahiriyah. 
He praised the effective role of the Jamahiriyah in various UN agencies. 


Meets Government Officals 
LD211650 Tripoli JANA in English 1610 GMT 21 Nov 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Safar 5, Nov 21, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The Foreign Liaison 
Secretary Mr. ‘Abd al-Ati al-'Ubaydi this morning conferred with the United Nations 
Secretary General Javier Perez de Cuellar who is currently visiting the Jamahiriyah. 
The meeting was also attended by the Jamahiriyah's permanent representative at the 
United Nations Dr. ‘Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki. 


Discussions centred on several world issues, cooperation between the Jamahiriyah and 
the U.N. and its specialised agencies, the international role of the Non-Aligned Move- 
ment and the necessity of the North-South dialogue serving the interests of developing 
nations. 


The U.N. secretary general also expressed appreciation for the efforts exerted by the 
Jamahiriyah in various U.N. bodies to promote world peace. De Cuellar said the 
Jamahiriyah's contributions to the U.N.'s specialised agencies have contributed to 
their smooth functioning in providing humanitarian aid. He said the U.N. and its 
agencies will try to increase this cooperation in the interests of all peoples. 

De Cuellar further affirmed the Jamahiriyah's effective role in debating world issues 
tabled at the U.N. and added that the Jamahiriyah have effectively contributed to the 
service of mankind. ‘
