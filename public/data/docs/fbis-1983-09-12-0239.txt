Party of Benin, and in the name of the Executive Council and the 
people of Benin, affirmed the complete support for the Great 
\l-Fatuh Revolution. President Kerekou also affirmed the com-

ind his firm stance against the imperialism which ts taking the

Libya as the progressive country in Africa, and because Libya 
constitutes an obstacle against the imperialist expansion

America and the colonialist countries are looking for the chance 
for a completely local conflict, and they have rushed to take
