separating the revolution from the authority issued by the General People's Congress in 
the Jamahiriyah places us before great and serious responsibilities. It also holds the 
masses responsible for preaching and calling for this throughout the world, until the

{Text ] lyadh, 5 March, SNA--Cheikhna Ould Mohamed Laghdaf, the Mauritanian foreign minister, 


has affirmed his country's readiness to hold talks with all parties concerned with the 


sanaran question at any time and at any place in order to reach a peaceful solution.

But, he added, the present conditions are not favorable and we are awaiting the formation 
of the new Algerian Cabinet. 


In a statement to the newspaper AL-MADINAH, published here today, he said Mauritania 
had called for a plebiscite in the Saharan part of Mauritania but the POLISARIO refused. 


Laghdaf declared that he is optimistic about the solution of the Saharan problem. 
He added that the situation is now at a standstill and that no mediation is taking 


place, 


In this regard it was reported yesterday that France is planning to make an initiative 
to solve the dispute over the Western Sahara. Laghdaf said that the formation of the 
new Algerian Cabinet will serve as a catalyst in the quest for peace in the Arab 
Maghreb. 


Speaking of his country's relations with Morocco and Algeria, he said we will shortly 
begin to lay the foundation for diplomatic relations with Algeria as a first step toward 
the improvement of relations between Mauritania and Algeria and the paving of the way 
for a meeting between the Mauritanian and Algerian presidents. s for Mauritania's 
relations with Moroceo, he said they were excellent and were improving further. He 
said there were no differences of opinion between the two countries on the issues under 
discussion. Laghdaf pointed out that military coordination between his country and 
Morocco was continuing and that the Moroccan forces will gradually withdraw following 
the completion of the training of the Mauritanian forces which will replace them. He 
expected the withdrawal of the Moroccan forces from Mauritania to be comple%ed within 
the current year. 


Speaking of the situation in Mauritania, he denied strongly that there were any 
internal troubles there, adding that there is nothing to cause concern over the unity 
of Mauritani and the unity of the factions which comprise its people.
