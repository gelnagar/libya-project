LIBYA

SYRIAN BA'TH PARTY, ARAB DELEGATIONS ARRIVE 


LD272110 Tripoli JANA in English 1901 GMT 27 Aug 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Aug 27 (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--A delegation from the Syrian Socialist 
Arab Ba'th Party arrived in Tripoli this afternoon headed by the undersecretary of the 
Ministry of Culture and chairman of the Syrian Arab Writers Union. 


Vv. 28 Aug 79 I2 NORTH AFRICA 


The delegation will attend the world conference sponsored by the Permanent Secretariat 


of the Arab People's Congress on reactionary, Zionist and imperialist schemes against 
the Arab nation, 


Similarly the assistant secretary-general of the international confederation of Arab 
trade unions arrived here at the head of a delegation from the confederation to the 
conference, The two delegations will also participate in the tenth anniversary of the 
First of September Revolution, 


They were met at Tripoli International Airport by representatives from the Permanent 
Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress and the general union of producers and the 
higher committee for celebrations. 


USSR'S DEMCHENKO ARRIVES FOR ANNIVERSARY CELEBRATION 
LD271907 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1800 GMT 27 Aug 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--Pavel Demchenko, member of the Supreme Soviet and representative of the 
Soviet committee for Afro-Asian solidarity, has referred to the importance of the letter 
sent to President Carter by President al-Qadhdhafi. He said that it is an important 
initiative on the part of Colonel al-Qadhdhafi to enlighten world public opinion on 

the problem of the Palestinians, 


Mr Demchenko said the above in a statement to a JANA reporter on arrival at Tripoli 
International Airport yesterday evening, to share with the Libyan people their celebrations 
on thel10th anniversary of the First of September Revolution, and to attend the meetings 

of the preparatory committee for solidarity with the Arabs and their central problem-- 

that of Palestine. 


The member of the Supreme Soviet said that American imperialism leans wholeheartedly 
toward Zionism; it is therefore necessary that all progressive forces throughout the 
world should unite to overcome racist Zionism and its allies. 


Mr Demchenko said that it is no coincidence that the meeting of the international 
preparatory committee for solidarity with the Arabs should take place at the same time 
as the Libyan people are celebrating the 10th anniversary of the Great First of 
September Revolution. It is confirmation and recognition of the fact that the Libyan 
Jamahiriyah stands in the forefront of the struggle against international imperialism 
in the Arab world and throughout the world,
