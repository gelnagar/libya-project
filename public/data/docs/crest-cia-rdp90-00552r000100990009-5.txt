Summary (AI): A reprinted New York Times text of the final Justice Department report on Billy Carter's dealings with Libya. It covers his trips to Libya in 1978 and 1979, oil negotiations, and the Libyan agent Michele Papa, and concludes no government officials were shown to have engaged in illegal conduct. Nothing in it concerns places in Tripoli.
• Billy Carter made two trips to Libya, in 1978 and 1979.
• The State Department approved the release of two 727 aircraft to Libya on foreign policy grounds, according to the report.
• Billy Carter was reportedly seeking a loan from Libyan officials and was in oil negotiations with Libya, which became public in 1980.
• The Justice Department report was dated April 1981, and the final interview with Papa took place on 12 February 1981.
