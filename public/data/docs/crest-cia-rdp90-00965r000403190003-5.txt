Summary (AI): A 1985 New York Post article reports on a book, The Specialist (pseudonym Gayle Rivers), which describes an alleged 1982 Libyan plot to plant explosives on a New York subway train, a claim the Sunday Times disputed. The article does not concern Tripoli itself.
• The book The Specialist was to be published by Sidgwick & Jackson in Britain and Stein and Day in the US, with 50,000 copies and a $60,000 promotion budget.
• The alleged plot involved groups including Puerto Rican Rastafarians, the Jewish Defense League and the Black Liberation Army.
• The Sunday Times reported the author was Raymond Brooks, a failed SAS territorial reservist under police investigation.
• 1982: Alleged Libyan plan, described in the book The Specialist, to assemble explosives on a New York City subway train; disputed by the Sunday Times of London
