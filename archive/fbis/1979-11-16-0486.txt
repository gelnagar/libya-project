LIBYA TO CALL SPECIAL OPEC CONFERENCE TO SUPPORT IRAN 
1D151400 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 15 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] Secretary of Petroleum 'Izz ad-Din al-Mabruk has emphasized that Libyan oil 
will not replace Iranian oil, In a statement to JANA today, he said: We stand by the 
Islamic revolution in Iran and all the oil and financial measures it has taken to 
confront the attempts of domination and challenges it is facing, 


Al -Mabruk condemned the American act of piracy by freezing Iranian assets in American 
banks, He pointed out that Iran, as a member of the oil-exporting countries, has the 
right to ask the other member states to support it in confronting this flagrant viola- 
tion by one of the states that consumes the oil of this group, He stressed that the 
importance of the organization lies in its ability to check such unlawful transgressions 
on one of the members of the group,

AT-TURAYKI REMARKS IN TUNIS ON CARTER IRAN DECISION 
1D152106 Tripoli JANA in English 1854 omT 15 Nov 79 ID 


[Text] Tunis, Nov 15, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Dr, 'Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, the 
foreign secretary, said the Arab nation is capable of foiling the capitulatory plan 
carried out by the Egyptian defeated president. For, he added, the Arab nation has 
unanimously decided to expel the Egyptian regime from the Arab League, 


In a statement to the Tunisian newspaper AS-SABAH, Dr. at-Turayki said the Arab nation 
has condemned the capitulatory treaty and has unified its stance at conferences against 
the Egyptian regime such as the conferences of Monrovia and Havana where a committee was 
set up to suspend Egypt's membership in the nonaligned movement, 


He added that joint action should be developed in the Arab foreign ministers conference 
currently held in Tunis so as to foil the treachorous agreement which poses a threat to 
the Arab nation's cause, 


Dr. at-Turayki went on to say that other measures should be taken against the Egyptian 
regime in order to enhance its isolation and to stop any Arab investments inside Egypt 
as well as halting air travels and tourist excursions to Egypt. 


He explained that by collaborating with (Israel) immediately, the Egyptian regime has 
played a dangerous role against the Arab nation, [words indistinct] struggling people. 


This regime, he pointed out, would be doomed if Arab and African countries tightened 
their boycott, 


Concerning the Palestinian resistance presence in Lebanon, the foreign secretary affirmed 
the need for the Palestinian presence not only in Lebanon but on the whole of the Arab 
arena as well as for the need of Arab cooperation to solve this problem which should 

not be left to the Lebanese people alone, 


He stressed the Jamahiriyah's readiness to support the Lebanese people in solving their 
problem and to confront the continued Zionist aggressions against the south. 


He went cn to say that the Lebanese problem is a Zionist attempt to create such a prob- 
lem in the Arab region which is one of the outcomes of the capitulatory treaty signed 
by the defeated Egyptian president and the Zionist enemy, 


He added that the unity and authority of Lebanon is necessary. As for the Iranian 

Islamic revolution, the foreign secretary affirmed that it is a historic event on the 
international and Islamic level, For during the shah's period Iran cooperated with the 
(Israel1) enemy and American imperialism, whereas after the revolution, Iran backed the 
Arab nation's struggle against (Israel), thus becoming a real support to the Arab nation. 
Because of this particular stance, he added, we notice that imperialism began to engineer 
plots and create problems for the Iranian people, The collaboration of the U.S. Government 
and the shah is a hostile action against the Iranian Islamic revolution, Dr, at-Turayki 
described Carter's decision to freeze the Iranian assets in the United States as a serious 
precedent in international relations,

The foreign secretary said that this action reminds us of what the U.S, had done towards 
Mosaddeq when he tried to nationalize Iranian oil industry in favour of the Iranian people, 
and thus America was able to postpone the Iranian revolution. 


He said that President Carter's decision is a terrorist act practised by a great power 
against a people who have rid themselves of a fascist regime which is hireling to 
imperialism. He added that the freezing of Iranian funds is a dangerous phenomenon to 
which smaller nations and the Arab nation's attention in particular should be drawn, 
as the issue is not that of hostages but one of the chains to suppress the Iranian 
revolution and to starve the Iranian people. 


The foreign secretary demanded the Arab nation to support the Iranian people and 

Iranian revolution. Dr. at-Turayki said that any country which responds to American threat 
to boycott Iran and not to import Iranian oil then the Arab nation has to take an action 
towards that state. 


He added that the Arabs have to consider the fate of their investments and properties 
within America as a result of jungle law practised by the United States against smaller 
nations. 
Dr. at-Turayki said that Arab countries solidarity with Iran is required at present. 
At the end of his statement the foreign secretary urged the need to adhere to summit 
conferences resolutions and for the Arab countries to state their stand from that 
solidarity. 
SYRIAN PILOTS REPORTEDLY QOUNTER EGYPT BUILDUP 
LD151603 London AD-DUSTUR in Arabic 12-18 Nov 79 p 6 LD 
(Unattributed report: "Syrian Pilots in Jamahiriyah?")] 
{Text] Well-informed sources have asserted that there is at present a large number of 
Syrian pilots in Libya and that their total number is equivalent to that of two Syrian 
Air Force squadrons. 
These sources say that the presence of this number of pilots in the Jamahiriyah is due to 
fear of a possible adventure by As-Sadat's regime to invade the Jamahiriyah and seize 
control of its oil resources. These sources believe that the boundaries of confrontation 
with Camp David are now the Jamahiriyah border, particularly since As-Sadat is now stationing 
no less than 200,000 soldiers on his western border.
