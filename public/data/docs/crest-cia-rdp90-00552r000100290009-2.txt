• The White House banned travel by Americans to Libya the week before and advised Americans living there to leave.
• Federal agencies circulated composite sketches of five terrorists to senior officials.
• A Africa-based informer said in mid-November 1981 that he had trained in assassination teams in Libya; he passed lie detector tests.
• Officials said many sources gave misleading tips to the FBI.
• 1981-09: Early September: first indication Libya might dispatch assassination teams, shortly after American pilots shot down two Libyan jets off Libya.
• 1981-10: A Libyan team was reported to target U.S. Ambassador to Italy Maxwell M. Rabb in October.
• 1981-11: An American diplomat in Paris barely escaped an attack in November that officials thought involved Libya.
• 1981-12-17: Reagan news conference defended the threat assessment, saying it was based on several sources.
