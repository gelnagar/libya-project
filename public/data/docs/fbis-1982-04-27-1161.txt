LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI MEETS WITH SYRIAN CHIEF OF STAFF 


LD262000 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1908 GMT 26 Apr 82 

[Text] Tripoli, 26 Apr (JANA) -- The leader of the revolution today received Gen Hikmat 
ash-Shihabi, deputy minister of defense and Syrian chief of staff, who conveyed a verbal 
message from Syrian President Hafiz al-Asad. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES MESSAGE FROM OBOTE 


LD262158 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 26 Apr 82 


[Text] The brother commander of the revolution received a written message from Ugandan 
President Milton Obote, in which he affirmed the depth of relations binding the two friendly 
countries and the bridges of cooperation between the Libyan Arab and Ugandan peoples. 


He expressed his country's appreciation for the development in economic relations between 
it and Libya and for Libya's support in developing Uganda's economic resources. He said 
thanks to this stand, the Ugandan people have succeeded in developing their society and 
in building a strong foundation for their national economy. 


President Obote denounced the prejudiced propaganda and clamor campzigns raised by imper- 
ialism, Zionism and reaction, which seek to cast doubts about the ho'ding of the forth- 
coming African summit conference in Libya. In this respect, he pointed out that these 
propaganda campaigns fall in line with the U.S. administration's obje:tive of preventing 
the convocation of this conference in the jamahiriyah. He said these campaigns are false 
and aim at breaking up the unity of Africa, which Libya plays a firm role in bolstering. 


In his message to the commander of the revolution, Brother President Obote affirmed that, 
on numerous occasions, Libya has proven its effective support for the African peoples and 
has sought to bolster their struggle for freedom. In this respect, he said, we recall 
the role played by the Great 1 September Revolution in restoring peace in Chad. 


President Obote also recalled the role Libya played in securing the eviction of the Zionists 
from Africa. He said Libya was behind this eviction which served the cause of the freedom 
of the African peoples. 


President Obote conveyed to the brother commander of the revolution his complete pleasure 
at the convocation of the forthcoming African summit conference in the jamahiriyah to 
achieve further results serving the interests of the African peoples. He said the Republic 
of Uganda will attend this conference and will play a role in it for the sake of attaining 
the desired objectives.
