Jallud Speech at African Youth Festival 
LD222254 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
1834 GMT 22 Aug 83 


[Speech by Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salaam Jallud at second African 
youth festival in Tripoli — live] 


[Excerpts] In the name of God. I would like to begin by greeting 
delegation heads and this manifestation of African youth. I 
would like to greet the youth organizations from Latin America, 
Asia, Europe — the delegations that have come to share with the 
African youth their joy today over this manifestation which has 
more than one significance and more than one meaning. I regret 
that it is not possible to have a simultaneous translation; so as to 
save time, some brothers will try to summarize my speech when 
I finish. However, the speech will be translated into various 
languages and distributed to you. 


Brothers, the role of youth in the Third World is not like tht of 
the capitalist European world. Consequently, for African youth 
to copy youth in the Western capitalist countries is a grave plot 
against the future of Africa and against the future of African 
youth. The future is for the young. Consequently, reactionary 
forces, rightwing forces, bourgeois forces, colonialist forces, and 
lackey forces are trying to hypnotize the young and thus divert 
them from their real duty by saying to them that the young were 
created for physical exercise. Physical exercise, brothers, is done 
$0 as to enable us to think more and to struggle more. Sports are 
not an end by themselves but a means
