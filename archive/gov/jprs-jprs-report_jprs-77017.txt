A Program for the South 


[Answer] 1 am opposed to financial support for the south at the present time be- 
cause it will be pocketed by feudalists and ministers and because the poor resident 
of south Lebanon who has suffered damages will not benefit from this support at all. 


[Question] What if the [financial] support were to come to the National Movement? 


[Answer] This would be possible, We did carry out a program for south Lebanon, 
and we received a 10 million dollar subsidy from Libya. 


We formed a committee for this matter, and the fund is open. 


We intend to carry out special projects that are not merely confined to building 
a house that was torn down [here] or compensating a person (there) for damages 

he had suffered. We will carry out projects such as digging artesian wells and 
building orphanages and hospitals, The purpose of these projects is to stabilize 
the residents of south Lebanon in their cities and in their villages. 


What is required, for example, is the transformation of some south Lebanon villages 
into something that resembles the Israeli kibbutzes so they can defend themselves. 
The construction of orphanages is also particularly required. 


The Palestinian Resistance did build orphanages, but what it did build is still 
far short of what is needed. 


We Want Guns 

[Question] You are proposing a dialogue with the Phalangists while Bashir makes 
military preparations end proposes the liberation of all Lebanon. He is making 
Statements to that effect daily. 


[Answer] Let the Arabs, instead of giving us statements and talks about moral 
support. give us guns so we can confront Bashir al-Jumayyil. 


I am prepared to confront al-Jumayyil, but let the Arabs give me the guns.

[Question] Do you mean the settlement of Palestinians? 


[Answer] Yes, There is spontaneous settlement, but there are those who are

[Answer] No. Is it reasonable or acceptable that there be no dealings between
