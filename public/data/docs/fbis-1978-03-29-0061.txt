AT-TURAYKI COMMENTS ON RELATIONS WITH CHAD, SUDAN 
241150Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 GMT 24 Mar 78 LD 


{Text ] Sabha--Foreign Secretary Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki has affirmed that the 
Tamahiriyah will continue to devote all of its efforts, as it has in the past, to see 
that peace and harmony prevail in Chad, which has fraternal and neighborly relations 
with the Jamahiriyah. This will strengthen our relations with Chad on the basis of 
cooperation, mutual respect and neighborliness. 


Dr at-Turayki pointed out that the Jamahiriyah will do its utmost to achieve national 
reconciliation within the African framework. He added that one of the results of the 
success achieved by the historic Sabha conference is that the shedding of African blood 
has ended in Chad and peace has prevailed in the interests of the people of Chad. We 
need every drop of blood for the liberation of our continent from the racist regimes which 
are linked with colonialism, and for the liberation of Palestine from the racist Zionist 
regime, he said. 


Speaking at a press conference he held last night in the town of Al-Qahirah in Sabha, 

Dr ‘Ali at-Turayki explained that a good spirit has prevailed during the negotiations 
between the Chad Government and the Chad National Liberation Front, and that the nego- 
tiations have been proceeding very well. He added that such negotiations are not easy, 
and we do not expect instant miracles. Nevertheless, we shall make great efforts, to- 
gether with our brothers, to achieve the joint goal and help our brothers in the Chad 
Tovernment and the Chad National Liberation Front. He said tat there is a great hope 

of achieving the results which the people of Chad and all people, particularly the people 
of Africa who cherish peace and justice, desire to see.

The foreign secretary pointed out that the negotiations are connected with an important 
issue linked to the future of the people of Chad and the peoples of Africa. He said that 
the Jamahiriyah, proceeding from its clear principles on liberation, is prepared to make 
every effort for the success of these negotiations and for reaching the goal which we 
are seeking to achieve through these negotiations. 


Dr at-Turayki explained that the morning session of the negotiations yesterday discussed 
the core of the issue and that there was a full response from all parties in the meeting-- 
a fact which gives us great hopes of achieving the results we are seeking. Dr at-Turayki 
pointed out that the topics submitted ty the national front were discussed. The question 
of the front's recognition, which had been dealt with by the quadripartite summit, was 
discussed, and just the fact that the present meeting is taking place means that the 
issue has been resolved. Recognition is thus inevitable. 


As for the question of French presence, the leaders discussed this question and condemned 
all foreign intervention in the African Continent. The question of the French pres: nce 
concerms the people of Chad, who alone decide with whom they want tc cooperate. We as 
Africans do not agree to the presence of foreign forces in an African country. More- 
over, this contravenenes the OAU Charter and principles of the nonalined countries to 
which Chad belongs. 


Speaking about Arab Libyan-Sudanese relations, the foreign secretary affirmed that the 
recent steps taken were important steps for the strengthening of relations and the 
joint struggle for our pan-Arab issues and for the joint struggle in Africa against our 
common enemy: racism. 


Dr at-Turayki pointed out that what is happening now in the Arab homeland ‘s a conflict 
against Zionism, reaction and capitulation that requires from the May and Great 1 Septem- 
ber revolutions greater efforts to achieve the aspirations of the Arab nation and 
achieve victory in our struggle against the enemy--Zionism and capitulation. He said 
that the process of the development of the good relations between the Jamahiriyah and 
Sudan had met difficulties from reaction, the capitulatory regimes and colonialism, 

but we shall continue our struggle in order to achieve our goals of standing up to 
colonialism and Zionism, so that we can give new drive to the Arab nation. There is 
great hope of achieving all of the aims and principles of both the May and Great 1 
September revolutions and of liberating the land and strengthening our joint struggle 
for freedom and progress.
