LIBYA

DOWNED U.S. F-14 REPORTEDLY FELL NEAR SARDINIA 


LD260826 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 26 Aug 81 


[Text] Fresh information was revealed yesterday about the U.S. F-14 aircraft shot down 
by the Libyan Arab eagles of the sky during the flagrant American aggression against the 
Jamahiriyah. Italian officials in Rome have stated that a 2-seater F-14 fell during the 
past few days off the Sardinian shores at a distance of 10 miles to the west of (Poisano). 
One of the pilots was killed immediately while the other suffered serious injury. 


According to the Italian information, the F-14 was hit by a missile during alleged maneuvers 
by NATO off the Sardinian coast. 


The United States has concealed the news of the shooting down of the plane by the Libyan 
Arab Air Force and has asked its allies in NATO to announce that several days after the 
flagrant aggression a German plane was downed during NATO maneuvers a long way from the 
U.S. 6th Fleet maneuvers in the Gulf of Sidra. 


All Western press analyses and reports have been unanimous in saying that the F-14, which 
was claimed to be a German plane, was the same F-14 plane of the U.S. 6th Fleet shot down 
by the Libyan Arab Air Force over the Gulf of Sidra. 


FOREIGN LIAISON BUREAU DENIES DANGER TO AMERICANS 


LD260804 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 26 Aug 81 


[Text] The people's Bureau for Foreign Liaison yesterday evening issued a statement 
denouncing the call made by the U.S. Administration on the Americans working in the 
Jamahiriyah to leave the country on the grounds that their security and safety are 
exposed to alleged dangers. The statement affirmed that the American community in the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah will continue to enjoy the protection and 
security enjoyed by all in the Jamahiriyah and that the Libyan Arab people are keen on 
establishing good cordial relations with all peoples, including the people of the United 
States. 


The people's bureau urged members of the American community not to be taken in by the lies 
and allegations of the Ronald Reagan administration which are designed to foment suspicion 
and hatred against the Jamahiriyah and its people, with a view to undermining the relations 
of friendship and respect between them and the American people.

OFFICIAL IN ROME COMMENTS ON SIDRA GULF INCIDENT 


AU251625 Rome ANSA in English 1510 Gmt 25 Aug 81 


[Text] Rome, 25 Aug (ANSA)--A representative of the Libyan Jamahiriyah people's committee 
for information, ‘Ali Ati'ya, declared in Rome today that "American arrogance must be 
halted even at the cost of causing a war" in a statement of the Tripoli government view 

of the clash between Libyan and American fighters over the Gulf of Sidra last Wednesday. 


The decision of the Libyan representative to call a press conference here came after the 
commander of the U.S. Navy Sixth Fleet, Vice Admiral William Rowden,and the pilots of the 
two American F-14's who shot down the Libyan warplanes provided reporters with their 
account of the incident on Monday. The American fleet commander and pilots held their 
press conference abroad the aircraft carrier USS Nimitz, which dropped anchor in the Bay 
of Naples on Monday. 


In substance, Ati'ya today reiterated the version of the air clash presented earlier by 
Libyan Leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi when he said "an American F-14 failed to heed the 
warning of the two SU-22 Libyan aircraft flying over Libyan territorial waters and was 
subsequently shot down. 


"After this," the Libyan representative continued, "eight F-14's took off (from the 
Nimitz) and shot down two Libyan planes." 


Ali Ati'ya went on to predict that "other incidents like the one which occurred over the 
Gulf of Sidra will almost certainly take place in the future. This is a struggle between 
good and evil and evil will try again", he said. 


"The problem is not one of territorial waters. The problem is political. The Reagan 
administration wants to attack Libya, to put an end to our revolution but we are prepared 
to accept the challenge", the representative insisted. 


"Now that we know what freedom is, we will defend it. Our hope is that the United States 
learned its lesson. If it failed to understand, we will have a way to make it understand 
in the future", ‘Ali Ati'ya declared. 


The Libyan Government spokesman then returned to the issue of territorial waters and the 
protest filed by the U.S. with the UN Security Council in 1973 challenging Libya's unilateral 
decision to extend territorial waters out to include the Gulf of Sidra, the area of the 
Mediterranean picked for the Sixth Fleet missile-firing maneuvers. 


"If the Libyan decision can be discussed with any one", the Tripoli official said, "it 
would be only with those countries having a Mediterranean coastline. To admit the principle 
of American intervention would be a defeat for them". 


When asked about the prospect of installihg Soviet military bases in Libyan territory 
as a result of the air clash, the Tripoli government representative said "for the time 
being there are no Soviet bases in our country. Possible decisions in this area will 
have to be made by the Libyan people. The problem, however, is not current one. 


"If there is a NATO base in Sicily, or an American installation, which is the same thing, 
we must ask ourselves what the targets of these missiles might be", Ati'ya continued, 
"and no one can stop us from agreeing to give a base to the Warsaw Pact." 


Speaking about the likelihood of a visit to Italy by Al-Qadhdhafi in October, At'ya said 
"there is an invitation. It is up to Italy to choose a date”.

and the United States if Al-Qadhdhafi's visit comes off, the representative said "it 
(the visit) would provide an historic occasion, very useful on both the bilateral and 
multilateral levels". 


ENVOYS IN TURKEY BRIEFED ON U.S. ‘AGGRESSION' 


LD260810 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 26 Aug 81 


[Text ] The secretary of the People's Committee of the People's Bureau of Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in Turkey met with the ambassadors of the Islamic and 
African countries there yesterday afternoon. He acquainted themwiththe dimensions of 
the treacherous American aggression against the Libyan Arab people. During the meeting, 
the ambassadors affirmed to the secretary of the People's Committee of the People's 
Bureau their strong denunciation of America's flagrant aggression against the Jamahiriyah. 


With the aid of a map, the ambassadors were acquainted with the extent of the Libyan 
territorial waters in the Gulf of Sidra. The secretary also spoke of America's terrorist 
plots to assassinate the brother commander and undermine the achievements of the Great 

1 September Revolution. 


ARAB UN BLOC'S CONDEMNATION OF U.S. REPORTED 


LD251022 Tripoli JANA in English 0818 GMT 25 Aug 81 


[Text] New York, 25 Aug (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The Arab bloc of countries at the 
United Nations today issued a statement condemning the U.S. attack against the Jamahiriyah. 


The head of the Arab bloc sent the statement to the Security Council chairman and the U.N. 
secretary and it will be further distributed as an official Security Council document.. 
The text of the statement is as follows: 


"The United States' aggression against the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
follows a series of threats widely documented by the American news media. 


"The Arab bloc of countries at the United Nations condemns this attack that fallows in the 
wake of the lifting of the American embargo and delivery of warplanes to Israel, coming 
as some sort of an indication that the American Administration is intent on challenging 
the feelings, security and rights of the Arab nation. 


"The provocative military manoeuvers carried out by the Sixth Fleet in Libyan waters, the 
repeated military attacks against parts of the Arab nation, and threats issued by the 
parties to the Camp David accords are a continuing chain of actions directed against the 
independence, sovereignty and security of Arab countries--especially the Palestinian 
people's right to self-determination--and also causing increased international tension 
and threatening world peace. 


"The Arab bloc of countries in the United Nations expressed its total patriotic solidarity 
with the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and appeals to all world groupings, 
especially the Non-aligned Movement, to unite against these provocations and threats, 

which constitute a dangerous precedent in international relations, especially since they 
come from a superpower that a permanent member of the U.N. Security Council who is supposed 
to be carrying out a distinct responsibility in maintaining world peace and security".
