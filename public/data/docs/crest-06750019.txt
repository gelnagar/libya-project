VISIT OF LIBYAN REVOLUTIONARY COMMAND COUNCIL CHAIRMAN QADHAFI TO CAIRO
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/06750019
https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-06750019

Summary (AI): A December 1969 CIA cable reports that Libyan RCC Chairman Muammar Qadhafi secretly visited Cairo from 30 November to 4 December 1969 to meet Egyptian President Nasser, who advised him on Soviet ties, the private sector, and two ministers allegedly communist. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• Qadhafi stayed the entire visit at Nasser's residence to avoid publicity.
• Nasser advised Qadhafi not to go too far in relations with the Soviet Union.
• Nasser advised Qadhafi not to nationalise the private sector of the Libyan economy.
• Nasser urged Qadhafi to replace two ministers whom the UAR believed were communists.
• 1969-11-30: Qadhafi begins a secret visit to Cairo to see UAR President Nasser; the visit runs to 4 December 1969.
