Summary (AI): A 1981 compilation of press clippings about CIA Director William Casey and reports that former CIA agents (Edwin Wilson, Frank Terpil) and retired Green Berets helped train terrorists in Libya. The material is mainly about US intelligence management, with little specific information about Tripoli itself.
• The New York Times (26 Aug 1981) reported that about 10 Green Berets went to Libya in 1977 to train terrorists under a program allegedly organized by Edwin P. Wilson.
• Wilson was indicted in 1983 (OCR reads 1988) by a federal grand jury for illegally exporting explosives to Libya and was reportedly in Tripoli.
• Recruits were reportedly promised $4,500 a month plus bonuses.
• Casey ordered a review of agency policies on former agents transferring technology to the Soviet Union and Libya.
• 1977-07-21: Former Green Beret Luke Thompson was recruited by phone to a mission to train terrorists for Qaddafi's Libya; a sergeant later went to Tripoli, met Libya's intelligence chief, was shown an explosives factory, and returned to the US.
