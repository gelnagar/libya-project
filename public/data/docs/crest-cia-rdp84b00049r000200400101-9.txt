DRAFT NATIONAL SECURITY DECISION DIRECTIVE ON ECONOMIC DECISIONS FOR LIBYA
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp84b00049r000200400101-9
https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-cia-rdp84b00049r000200400101-9

Summary (AI): A March 1982 NSC staff routing document with a draft National Security Decision Directive on economic measures against Libya. It proposes banning U.S. imports of Libyan crude oil and requiring validated licenses for most U.S. exports to Libya, with exceptions for medicine, medical supplies and agricultural goods. No Tripoli-specific sites are named.
• Draft NSDD of 2 March 1982 would prohibit US import of Libyan crude oil.
• Validated licences would be required for all US exports of goods and technology to Libya, except medicine, medical supplies and agricultural commodities.
• Policy would deny licences for national-security-controlled goods and for oil and gas technology not readily available outside the US.
• Comments on the draft were due by close of business 3 March 1982.
• A Task Force on Libya was to be co-chaired by the Secretary of State and the Assistant to the President for National Security Affairs.
• 1982-02-25: NSC meeting at which recommendations on US policy toward Libya were made to the President (as stated in the cover memo)
