((EXCERPTS) ) ((QUESTION)) BROTHER COLONEL, AFTER THE 
CONVENING OF THE FIRST MAGHREB SUMMIT IN ALGIERS AND THE 
MEETING OF THE HIGHER POLITICAL COMMITTEE, THE JAMAHIRIYAH 
IS HOSTING THE PROCEEDINGS OF THE SUBCOMMITTEE GIVEN THE 
TASK ((AS HEARD)) OF MAGHREB CONSTITUENT BODIES. BROTHER 
COLONEL, WHAT IS IN YOUR OPINION THE MOST USEFUL STRUCTURAL 
FRAMEWORK WHICH WOULD RESPOND IDEALLY TO THE TOTAL POLITICAL 
AND ECONOMIC REQUIREMENTS FOR THE BUILDING OF THE ARAB 
MAGHREB? : ; 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) I HAVE 19 YEARS OF INTENSIVE AND SERIOUS 
EXPERIENCE IN MAMMOTH UNIONIST WORK. I HAVE REACHED THE 
CONCLUSION THAT THE IMPORTANT THING IS TO FIND A POLITICAL 
FRAMEWORK WHICH CAN EXPLOIT THE ECONOMIC CAPABILITIES TO 
CREATE PROGRESS AND PROSPERITY FOR THE ARAB CITIZEN WITHIN 
THIS POLITICAL FRAMEWORK, AND THEN TO BUILD UP THE DEFENSE 
CAPABILITIES TO PROTECT THE CITIZEN AND HIS HOMELAND FROM 
EXTERNAL THREATS. AFTER THAT, IT DOES NOT MATTER WHETHER 
THIS COUNTRY IS A JAMAHIRIYAH, ANOTHER A REPUBLIC, ANOTHER A 
KINGDOM, AND ANOTHER A SULTANATE. IT DOES NOT MATTER. ALL 
OBSTACLES TO THE MOVEMENT OF CITIZENS, CAPITAL, AND 
EVERYTHING THAT EXISTS ON LAND -- EVEN THE MOVEMENT OF 
ANIMALS FOR PASTURE, SELLING, OR TRADING -- AND TO FISHING 
AND OTHERS AMONG THESE COUNTRIES MUST BE REMOVED. THIS SEA 
AND THIS LAND MUST BECOME THE PROPERTY OF ALL THE CITIZENS 
OF THESE COUNTRIES. THERE MAY BE A SULTAN, A KING, AN AMIR 
OR PRESIDENT. THIS DOES NOT MATTER IN RELATION TO 
EXPERIENCE WHICH I TRULY RESPECT ((WORDS INDISTINCT)). IT 
IS THE FRAMEWORK THAT MUST BRING TOGETHER THE ECONOMIC 
CAPABILITIES WHICH CREATE PROGRESS AND PROSPERITY FOR THE 
CITIZEN AND MUST BRING TOGETHER THE OTHER MATERIAL 
CAPABILITIES TO DEFEND THIS HOMELAND AND THIS CITIZEN. IN 
FACT, THIS IS WHAT CONCERNS, WHS TEVER HAPPENS AFTER THAT. 

( (SENTENCE AS HEARD) ) 

. PERSONALLY, I WILL NOT BE PRESIDENT AND WILL NOT RETURN 
TO A POLITICAL OR ADMINISTRATIVE POSITION BECAUSE I HAVE 
REJECTED THIS EVEN IN LIBYA. I WORK FROM A REVOLUTIONARY 
POSITION. I LEAD THE REVOLUTION AND INCITE THE MASSES. 

THIS MATTER IS DECIDED NOT BY EDICT OR BY APPOINTMENT. 

I DO NOT CARE WHO BECOMES PRESIDENT OR RULER. I DO NOT 
EVEN CARE IF IT IS A KINGDOM, SULTANATE, OR EMPIRE. THESE 
ARE NAMES AND ((WORD INDISTINCT)) HAS AFFIRMED THAT THEY ARE 
FORMS. WE MUST NOT INTERFERE IN THE AFFAIRS OF ANY OF THESE 
COUNTRIES. IF WORKERS WERE OPPRESSED, I MEAN EXPLOITED, IN 
A COUNTRY, THEY WILL REVOLT AGAINST THEIR EXPLOITERS. IF 
THEIR CONDITION IS GOOD, FINE. IF PEOPLE ARE OPPRESSED, 
THEY WILL RESIST ((WORD INDISTINCT)). CONSEQUENTLY, THERE 
IS NO NEED FOR US TO SAY THAT WORKERS MUST BE PARTNERS IN 
THIS STATE. IT IS CLEAR THAT PARTNERS, NOT WAGE-EARNERS IS 
THE FUNDAMENTAL SOLUTION. FORTUNATELY THERE IS SIMILARITY 
IN THE PROGRESS OF THE TWO COUNTRIES. 

THE POLITICAL AND ECONOMIC BASES IN THE TWO COUNTRIES
