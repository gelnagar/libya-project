LIBYA

Radio Reports ‘Confirm’ Habre Killed in Faya 
LDO21816 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1755 GMT 2 Aug 83 


[Text] Lome, 2 Aug (JANA) Radio Bardai, monitored in 
Lome this afternoon, has confirmed the death of Hissein Habre 
in the town of Faya, which is besieged by the forces of the 
legitimate government, led by Goukouni Oueddei 


The radio said in a statement that it has definite information 
about the death of Habre and a number of his aides. The radio 
said that a statement will be broadcast later containing detailed 
information about Habre’s death. 


The French radio, which transmits from Libreville, has also 
confirmed the report of the killing of Hissein Habre in Faya 


Statement Denies U.S. Carrier in Gulf of Sidra 
LD030914 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0855 GMT 3 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 3 Aug (JANA) -- A People’s Foreign Liaison 
Bureau source made a statement today denying the U.S. Defense 
Department's claims that the U.S. aircraft carrier Eisenhower is 
currently patrolling the Gulf of Sidra. The statement said that 
the U.S. aircraft carrier Eisenhower has not drawn close to the 
Gulf of Sidra. It adds that if the Eisenhower enters the Gulf it 
will be confronted, because the Gulf of Sidra is an indivisable 
part of Libyan territorial waters, and that orders have been issued 
to the Libyan Arab Air Force to destroy any target that enters 
the Gulf of Sidra. 


Al-‘Ubaydi on Hope for OAU Study of Role in Chad 
LDO021524 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1412 GMT 2 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 2 Aug (JANA) — The brother secretary of the 
People’s Committee of the People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau 
affirmed today here that the Jamahiriyah is ready to receive an 
OAU fact-finding mission that could see for itself the falseness 
of the rumours and fabricated allegations circulated by imperial

ist and reactionary circles over a so-called Libyan intervention in 
Chad. He reaffirmed that Libya is still committed to its neutral 
position on the internal struggle for power in Chad, expressing 
the hope that such a mission will see the facts for itself, and put 
to rest all anti-Libyan claims. 


The brother secretary of the Popular Bureau for Foreign Liaison 
mentioned the message sent by the brother leader of the rev- 
olution to President Mengistu, head of the Socialist Republic of 
Ethiopia and chairman of the OAU, about the present situation 
in Chad, late last month. He recalled that in the message the 
brother leader of the revolution had underlined that Libya has 
not intervened in Chad and that it is adopting a position of 
neutrality on the struggle for power between Chadian factions, 
and that what is now going on is a purely Chadian domestic 
matter 


In his message to the chairman of the OAU, the brother leader 
also stressed that Libya officially asked the organization to set 
up an investigation commission and send it to Faya [Largeau] so 
that it may see for itself that all that has been circulated by 
certain African states and colonialist circles about a so-called 
Libyan intervention is just preparation for and justification of 
their interference in Chad's domestic affairs.
