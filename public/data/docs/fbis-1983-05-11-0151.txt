LIBYA

Fatah Member ‘Recruited’ To Kill Al-Oadhdhafi 


U.S. Charged With Plot 
LDIOIOS8 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0909 GMT 10 May 83 


{Text} A person identified as a Palestinian national was arrested 
in Tripoli. He is called “Abd al-Qadir Musa al-Khatib. He was 
recruited by the U.S. CIA to carry out a filthy terrorist plot 
aimed at assassinating the brother leader 


This person has confessed that he was recruited by the U.S. CIA 
to carry out this base deed. In his confessions, he said that he 
belongs to the Palestinian Fatah Movement and that he has 
begun to implement the plan which was prepared for him under 
the guise of this affiliation. He added that he relayed the informa- 
tion he obtained to a “Fatah” office in Tripoli, which in turn 
transmitted it to the Fatah headquarters in Tunisia. He also 
received instructions about operations, implementing tasks, and 
gathering required information from the “Fatah” office in Trip- 
oli through other persons in the office, among them Shawai Abu 
Shu’ayrah who is a student at the petroleum engineering college 
at Fatih University, and from Mahmud Shahin 


He added that the operation was for the benefit of America and 
the Fatah movement, which opposes military action in solving the 
issue. He disclosed that there are existing secret contacts between 
the Fatah movement and America. 


He said that the operation to assassinate Colonel al-Qadhdhafi 
was a political operation aimed at destroying the only voice of 
opposition, namely the voice of Col Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi; 
thus this operation was for the benefit of America and a matter 
that Fatah accepted to carry out. He added that there was direct 
agreement between the Fatah movement and America that the 
operation to assassinate Al-Qadhdhafi was conceived in order to 
strengthen a peaceful solution and to enable America to draw 
closer to Fatah. 


He explained that it was decided to commence the operation 
during the Zionist invasion of Lebanon. He also disclosed the 
existence of contacts between the Fatah movement and Zionist 
organizations and Israeli personalities who are trying to find 
rapprochement and coexistence with the Fatah movement. 


His confessions also stated that he began to carry out the 
operation by infiltrating the Palestinian revolution camp close to 
the command camp in Tripoli, where he attacked and killed one 
of the guards. However he failed to escape and was arrested. 


He added that his mission was to gather information, to learn the 
kinds of weapons in the camp and the method by which the 
assassination plan could be carried out, and also to discover 
locations and roads leading to the command camp. 


“Abd al-Qadir Musa al-Khatib has lived in the Jamahiriyah since 
1975 with his family, obtained a university degree from the Fatih 
University in Tripoli, and has worked as computer programmer

Students Protest Attempt 
LDIO21S1 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2015 GMT 10 May 83 


[Text] The students of Misratah municipality have staged a noisy 
and angry demonstration protesting the base act of the Palestin- 
ian agent who was assigned by the CIA the filthy task of 
assassinating the brother leader of the revolution. 


The student masses affirmed their undying unity with the brother 
leader of the revolution, the living symbol of the conscience of 
the Arab nation. They denounced the agents and the attitudes of 
complacency, treason, and subservience to US. imperialism, the 
enemy of the Arab nation
