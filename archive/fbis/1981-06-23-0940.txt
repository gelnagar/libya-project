LIBYA

MOROCCAN ENVOY ARRIVES, MEETS AL-QADHDHAFI 


22 Jun Arrival 
LD221730 Tripoli JANA in English 1638 GMT 22 Jun 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 22 Jun (JANA)--Ahmed Redha Ghdirah, the adviser and envoy of King Hassan II 
of Morocco, arrived in Tripoli this afternoon to deliver an official message from the 
Moroccan monarch to the leader. This comes in response to the call made by the leader of 
the revolution in the important speech he delivered on the 11th anniversary of the evacua- 
tion of U.S. bases to eliminate the differences and mobilize Arab resources and direct them 
against the real enemy of the nation. 


In a statement to JANA's correspondent, the Moroccan envoy said, "We listened with interest 

to the call made by Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to eliminate Arab differences and 

mobilize all resources to confront the common enemy of our Arab nation and we have responded 
to this call." He added, "We appreciate the determination and will included in the 

colonel's call to mobilize Arab resources in order tc confront the common enemy." He went on 
to say: "We are at this very moment in a greater need to unify and strengthen the Arab 
nation and we, in Morocco, are determined to realize all that could unite Arabs, strengthen 
their unity and achieve their objectives." 


Al-Qadhdhafi Reception 
LD222006 Tripoli JANA in English 1830 GMT 22 Jun 81 
[Text] Tripoli, 22 Jun (JANA)--The revolution's leader, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, this 
evening received Redha Ghdirah, the adviser and personal envoy of King Hassan of Morocco. 
Mr Ghdirah delivered a letter to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi from King Hassan. The letter is 


believed to deal with the concentrated efforts being carried out by the Great al-Fatah 
Revolution to unify Arab ranks in a bid to confront the Zionist enemy. 


JANA REPORTS SITUATION IN SUDAN 'VOLATILE' 


LD221952 Tripoli JANA in English 1644 GMT 22 Jun 81 
["Situation Sudan Near Explosive"--JANA headline] 


[Text] Khartoum, 22 Jun (JANA)--Diplomatic sources here today said that the situation in 
Sudan was very volatile and massive demonstrations have roamed the cities and villages 

of Sudan calling for the overthrow of Numayri's regime. Heavily armed police have failed 
in restoring order and some of these forces joined the demonstrators. 


A statement by the Sudanese revolutionary forces said that angry demonstrations erupted 
yesterday in (Jenina), (Jabal Mura) Fashir, (Teckina), and the masses of (Malka), Juba, 


(Dengala), Shandi and Bahr al-Ghazal were organized in a huge popular march against the 
bloody regime in Sudan.
