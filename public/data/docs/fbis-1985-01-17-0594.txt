LIBYA

People’s Congresses Cable Tunisian, Algerian Leaders 
LD162111 Tripoli JANA in English 180! GMT 16 Jan 85 


[Text] Tripoli, 26 Rabi Thani, 16 Jan, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The masses of the Basic People’s Congresses which 
numbers nearly 2,000 congresses convening now in their 3rd 
normal session for 1984-85 sent cables to Habib Bourguiba the 
president of the Tunisian Republic confirming the!r commit- 
ments to the historic unionist declaration and demanding the 
implementing of what this declaration stated regarding the 
establishment of unity between the Jamahiriyah and Tunisia 


The masses of the Basic People’s Congresses, also sent similar 
cables to President of Algeria Chadli Benjedid the secretary 
general of the Central Committee for the Algerian Liberation 
Front and the president of the Democratic and Popular Republic 
of Algeria demanding the implementation of the unionist Hasi 
Masoud agreement between the two brotherly countries confirm- 
ing that the Arab unity establishment is an inevitability to 
confront the hostile challenges facing the Arab nation
