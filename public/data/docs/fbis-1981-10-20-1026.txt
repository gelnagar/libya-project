LIBYA

FOREIGN LIAISON BUREAU SUMMONS ARAB AMBASSADORS 


LD191456 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 19 Oct 81 


[Text] The People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison this morning summoned the ambassadors of 
the fraternal Arab countries accredited to the Jamahiriyah. The secretary of the People's 
Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison, ‘Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, affirmed 

to the brother Arab ambassadors that the Jamahirivah has no designs on Sudan or on any 
neighbouring country, but that it was against any foreign interference in these countries 
and any interference which would limit the freedom and ability of the people of those 
countries to assert their will. 


Al-'Ubaydi referred to the hostile statements made these days by the ruler of Sudan 
against the Jamahiriyah people and their historic leadership, statements which prove 

that the ruler of Sudan has been living in a state of despair since the death sentence 
against traitor As-Sadat was carried out. He pointed out that the Jamahiriyah has no 
differences with Sudan other than the fact that the ruler and the regime of Sudan have 
been supporting the butcher Seyni Habre. The Jamahiriyah considers this an act of 
aggression and a violation of the recent OAU summit resolutions, which affirmed the 
legitimacy of the Chadian Government and the need to back and bolster it. That, he 
added, was the stand the Jamahiriyah is committed to implement in accordance with African 
resolutions. Furthermore, he said that we differ with the Sudanese regime because of its 
alignment with the stable David accords and because it throws itself completely in the 
lap of the United States and As-Sadat -- a matter which violates Arab unanimity and 
squanders Arab rights. The Jamahiriyah cannot condone this at all and will check it by 
all means and methods. 


He pointed out that the Jamahiriyah is being attacked by the Sudanese ruler and that this 
attack has escalated in an aggressive manner after As-Sadat's death sentence was carried 
out, after the increase in U.S. penetration into the Arab area and after the expansion of 
the influence of the United States, whic: openly supplies the Egypgian and Sudanese 
regimes with arms to assert its course o: preserving As-Sadat's traitorous line and to 
give everyone the impression it will never accept any change in the policy of capitulation 
pursued by the traitor As-Sadat. 


Brother al-'Ubyadi affirmed that he who severs all links with his people and his Arab 
brothers can never survive; neither the United States nor anyone else can protect and 
support him. The fate which As-Sadat has met with is the best proof of this. He pointed 
out that in the face of these flagrant aggressive threats, the Jamahiriyahwill be forced 
to assert its right to self-defense.

The secretary general for technical affairs at the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison 
also met this morning with the ambassadors of the Islamic countries accredited to the 
Jamahiriyah and explained to them the country's attitude toward Sudan. He affirmed that 
the Jamahiriyah had no designs against Sudan or any neighboring country. He explained 

the Sudanese ruler's aggressive intentions against the Jamahiriyah people and its historic 
leadership. He also spoke to them about the statements and attacks currently being waged 
by the Sudanese ruler against the Libyan Arab people. 


He affirmed to the brother ambassador of the Islamic countries that the Jamahiriyah was 
opposed to any foreign interference in the neighboring countries and was against any 
foreign interference that would limit the freedom of their peoples. 


He said the reason for the conflict with Sudan was that its ruler has been supporting but- 
her Hussein Habre, a matter which constitutes a departure from the African resolutions 
which the Jamahiriyah upholds and is committed to implement. Another reason for the 
conflict was the Sudanese ruler'’s attitude toward stable David and his squandering of 
the legitimate rights of the Palestinian people. He also referred to the repeated state- 
ments by the ruler of Sudan and his bragging about moving the battle to Tripoli and 
Benghazi. He affirmed that the Jamahiriyah will stop these terrorist acts out of its 
right to self-defense. 


[RIPOLI RADIO NOTES U.S. REPORT ON USE OF AWACS 
LD192130 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 19 Oct 81 


[Excerpt] Tripoli, 19 Oct (JANA) -- An American report obtained by AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR, 
published in Tripoli by the Revolutionary Committee, makes it clear that the AWACS and 
the F-15, stationed in U.S. bases in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Somalia and Sudan could 
play an important role in protecting "Israel" against any outside attack. They could 
also play a role in intelligence operations and gather information about the progressive 
regimes in the Arab homeland, the Horn of Africa and the regions of the Red Sea and the 
Arabian Gulf. 

The report, “which had a very limited distribution in Washington" stresses that the 
AWACS aircraft would be of absolutely no use for the Arabs in any new war they wage 
against "Israel." 


The report says that the presence of these espionage aircraft in Saudi Arabia would 
constitute a vital aid for the supplying, massing and supporting of U.S. forces in the 
region against any progressive regime hostile to U.S. policy and against the popular 
revolutions expected to take place there. 


The first part of the report, which was prepared by the Center for International Strategic 
Studies at Georgetown University, says that the AWACS deal with Saudi Arabia, which is 
linked with America, will serve the broad aims of American policy and those of the racist 
Zionis* enemy, in particular in light of the strategic alliance recently concluded between 
America and the racist Zionists. 


HABASH PLEDGES PFLP SUPPORT TO AL-—QADHDHAFI 


LD191700 Tripoli JANA in English 1450 GMT 19 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Oct (JANA) -- Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 
Al Fatah Revolution, received a cable yesterday from Dr. George Habash, secretary 
general of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine, who pledged his front's 
readiness to fight side by side with the people of the Jamahiriyah against any force 
that tries to harm their achievements. The cable reads as follows: 


Dear Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great First of September Revolution: 

On behalf of the P.F.L.P. Central Committee and its cadets and fighters and me personally, 
I strongly condemn the imperialist-Zionist-Reactionary schemes against the people of the 
Jamahiriyah and its leadership.
