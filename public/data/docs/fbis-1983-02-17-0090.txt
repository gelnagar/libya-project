LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ON U.S. SIXTH FLEET ACTIVITIES 


LD171351 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0930 GMT 17 Feb 83 


[Speech by Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi at meeting of General People's Congress 
in Tripoli -- live] 


[Excerpt] At dawn today the United States sent a unit from the American 6th Fleet 
in the direction of Libyan shores. It has also sent spy planes, four AWACS planes to 


Egypt. 


The entire world has been saying that the American naval units were sailing toward the 
Libyan shores for some specific reason. The American President found it necessary to 
hold a press conference and to deny that any naval units had been ordered to move 
toward Libyan shores.

However, he admitted that he ordered the AWACS planes to proceed to Egypt because of 
American-Egyptian joint maneuvers. 


By saying this I mean to show that you have to defend yourselves. This is a matter of 

necessity because of the challenges that exist, whether you like it or not. None of us 
has been the cause of it. All we are doing is trying to live freely and work to unify 

the nation. We do not seek to colonize America. (7?All we want is for) America not to 

colonize us. 


Have we massacred the Israelis in Germany or anywhere else -- or in Russia -—- so that 
they come and massacre us? Let them go slaughter the West Germans or anyone else who 
massacred them. We Arabs did not massacre them. The eastern Jews have been living 
with us as brothers for 4,000 years. Let them go to Europe, which caused the massacres, 
and set up a state for themselves in East Germany, a state for all Jews, to avenge 
Hitler, for example. 


[Words indistinct] to establish a Zionist stage in the Arab homeland. They said there 
is nowhere else to establish their state. They drive the people out, colonize their 
land, and massacre them. 


Is this something we caused or provoked? Did we ask the world to bring the Israelis to 
set up a state in our midst to destroy us? Did we ask America to supply Israel with 
destructive weapons so that the Israelis could use them to destroy us? We are refugees 
(?and have no arms) but it freely gives them arms. 


Did we go to the gulf of (?Houston) in America and interfere there? Or was it the 
United States that came to the Gulf of Sidra, inside Libyan territory, between Misratah 
and Banghazi? [Words indistinct] anyone who enters the Gulf of Sidra will have 
Misratah and Banghazi behind him. He enters Libyan territory. Anyone who enters the 
sea actually enters the land. If America enters the Gulf of Sidra that means it is 
entering Libya. But we would not allow it to do so. [Words indistinct] to enter the 
Gulf of Sidra, only America which is a foreign power. [applause] We will not allow 
any foreign power to enter it. 


GENERAL PEOPLE'S CONGRESS RALLY BEGINS 12 FEB 


LD121514 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 12 Feb 83 


[Excerpts] The General People's Congress -- The rally of the popular congresses, 
popular committees, unions, trade unions, and vocational leagues -- began its eighth 
ordinary session this morning at the people's hall in Tripoli to collate and redraft 
the resolutions submitted by the third ordinary sessions of the Basic People's Con- 
gresses for 1983. The opening session was attended by delegations from fraternal 
Syria, Tunisia, and the SDAR, as well as by the secretaries of the fraternal bureaus 
and of the people's bureaus abroad and members of the diplomatic corps accredited to 
the Jamahiriyah. 


The brother secretary of the General People's Congress opened the session with a speech 
in which he affirmed that the mass attendance of the meetings of the Basic People's 
Congresses, which lasted for a whole wonth, constitutes proof of the people's faith in 
the authority of the people and in the thoughts of the commander thinker Colonel 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. He pointed out that the meeting is being held to draft resolu- 
tions based on those adopted by the masses of the Basic People's Congresses during 
their month of meetings -- a process which epitomizes the idea of popular authority. 

He said that we teach the world around us how democracy can be practiced directly 
through true exercise of the people's authority.

He pointed out that all those who have come to our country and shared the exerpeince 
have affirmed their praise for it and for the fact that this was the first time any 
people have exercised direct democracy. 


He greeted the fraternal delegations that have come to attend meetings of the current 
session of the General People's Congress: the delegations of Syria, Tunisia, and the 
SDAR. 


After the brother secretary general of the General People's Congress concluded his 
speech, the meeting began to review the agenda. The first item dealt with a review of 
the resolutions adopted by the Basic People's Congresses which had been drafted by the 
General People's Congress for the year 1982. One hundred fourteen basic congresses 
expressed satisfaction with what has been implemented so far and conveyed the remarks 
of their congresses. The congress also heard the remarks made by the general students 
congress. 


Following a recess, the meeting began to discuss paragraph two of item one of the agenda 
which deals with following up the deveiopment budget and the administrative budget for 
1982. 


The morning session adjourned at 1330 and will be resumed at 1700. 
Al-Qadhdhafi Attends Session 
LD141228 Tripoli JANA in English 1004 GMT 14 Feb 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi' Thani 30, Feb 14, (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY) -- The revolution's 
leader yesterday attended part of the evening session of the General People's Congress 
and listened to the comments of some Basic People's Congresses on the article regarding 
reviewing general people's committees without referring to the congresses. 


Moreover, stemming from his role as agitator for the exercise of people's authority, 
the leader made some comments and clarifications on the article. 


The leader said decisions are made by Basic People's Congresses which have authority. 
They alone can decide, whereas people's committees implement the congresses’ resolu- 
tions. 


The leader pointed to the warning that was spelled out during the fourth conference of 
revolutionary committees that if the people's congresses do not exercise authority, 
then this will encourage, even impel, the commiftt*es to exercise authority. This 
cannot be... 


He said the interpretation that affirms people's authority and explains the relation- 
ship between people's committees and congresses should always be remembered by the 
people's congresses in all circumstances. 


The leader said the basis of people's authority is that people's committees have no 
right to arrive at any decisions. Their only prerogative is to implement the decisions 
of the Basic People's Congresses. 


The leader said some decisions made by people's committees are not legal and do not 
come as part of their prerogatives. In this respect, he pointed to the decision of 
stopping car imports, raising electricity prices, including patients seeking medical 
(?treatment) abroad on public expense without referring to medical committees, [word 
indistinct] contracting by establishments, and abolishing consumer societies.

Speaks on Foreign Policy 
LD161947 Tripoli JANA in English 1818 GMT 16 Feb 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada 4, Feb 16, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The revolution's leader, 
Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, attended part of this morning's meeting of the General 
People's Congress. He listened to the comments made by a number of Basic People's 
Congresses regarding the agenda's fourth article on the Jamahiriyah's foreign policy 
during 1982. 


As part of his guidance and agitation role in the practice of people's authority and its 
entrenchment, the leader noted in his contributions that the Basic People's Congresses 
should have assessed their 1982 decisions on foreign policy and what has been applied 

of those decisions. 


He said, "During the BPC's discussions, I did not note any review of their decisions on 
foreign policy which should have been followed by the people's Foreign Liaison Bureau, 
so as the BPC's are able to assess what has been implemented of their decisions. 


I fear that there might not be a clear foreign policy for 1983 which is drawn by the 
Libyan Arab people and adopted and carried out by the people's Foreign Liaison Bureau, 
and so as by the end of the year the people's congresses know what has and hasn't been 
implemented of their decisions and the reasons which prevented implementation". 


The leader also noted that the “formulations arrived at appear to be relatively general 
and resemble recommendations issued by a non-responsible congress". He stressed that 
the people's congresses in Libya “are responsible for deciding the country's policy”. 


He added, “The people's congresses did not arrive at a formulation according to which a 
clear foreign policy could be drawn. This could be due to the question of 
consciousness". 


The leader expressed his fear that the people's Bureau of Foreign Liaison might not be 
able to know the policy drawn up for 1983 by the Libyan people concerning the relations 
with each individual country. 


He added that any failure in carrying out the foreign policy will be the responsibility 
of the people's congresses which are the only decision making body. The congresses 
should look into the Jamahiriyah's relations with each country and the decision should 
be carried out by the people's Bureau for Foreign Liaison. 


"The Basic People's Congresses should, for instance specify the foreign aid which the 
Foreign Liaison Bureau could spend during 1983. It can then be questioned if it 
exceeded its limit. But it can also move within these limits in case of an emergency”. 
The leader added that during 1982 some countries took a negative attitude towards the 
Jamahiriyah. Our relations towards these countries should be clearly decided because 
we have the means to punish them and reward those countries who took positive stands 
towards us and stood on our side”. 


He went on to say "the people's Bureau of Foreign Liaison will brief the people's 
congresses of its 1983 activities, step by step. If it carried out certain activities 
on foreign policy, this should be recorded by the congresses’ secretariats and put 
before the basic people's congresses meeting by the end of the year. This of course 
would mean that the congresses’ secretariats can by themselves explain to the BPC's 
masses the different activities without referring to the Foreign Liaison Bureau". 


He concluded by saying, "These are the broad lines within which you can manoeuvre but 
of course at the end of the congress this can be rephrased in a much clearer way".
