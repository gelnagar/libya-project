LIBYA

COMMENTARY VIEWS U.S. 'CAMPAIGN' AGAINST LIBYA 


LD090842 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 9 May 81 
[Unattributed commentary ] 


[Text] The American imperialist campaign against the Libyan Arab people, with the support 

of Arab reaction, and the timing of this campaign are all part of an imperialist-reactionary 
plan to insure implementation of the imperialist designs in Africa and the Arab area. This 
campaign is being waged against the Jamahiriyah under the pretext of an alleged Libyan threat. 
However, the fact of the matter is totally different: The imperialist campaign has other 
dimensions which are currently being coordinated against the Jamahiriyah, but which cover 

the entire Arab region. Briefly, the dimensions of this campaign can be stated as follows: 


1. To beseige the Great 1 September Revolution politically and militarily through military 
alliance with the imperialist-Zionist-Arab reactionary countries. This proves a premeditated 
intention to launch a military operation against the Jamahiriyah people, probably using 

Arab reaction as one of its tools. 


2. To counter the call by the brother commander for other Arab progressive forces to 
join the Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation. This call has prompted King Hassan II 
of Morocco to send out over 90 envoys to countries throughout the world to express his 
fears of the effects of the Great 1 September Revolution and its call for toppling his 
agent reactionary regime. 


These, in brief, are some of the conclusions one can come up with from the present American 
campaign against the Libyan Arab people and its decision to close down the Libyan People's 
Arab Bureau in Washington. The U.S. State Department has summoned the brother head of 

the bureau and asked him and his colleagues to leave the United States within 5 days. 


The U.S. decision has occupied a prominent place amid the most important international 
issues of the hour. The observers believe that the decision by the U.S. administration 

to close down the people's bureau was prompted by the bold and effective role played 

by the Jamahiriyah in exposing American imperialism and its agents in the Arab area as 
well as in the world at large. They also believe that it is prompted by the Jamahiriyah's 
aid and support for liberation revolutions in the world, particularly in the Arab homeland 
and Africa. 


Circles in Washington have pointed out that Reagan's decision was adopted a week after the 
visit of the commander of the revolution to Moscow. 


The American-Arab reactionary plot has thus been exposed. Hence, Arab peoples everywhere 
are called upon to abort the imperialist plot against the Arab nation represented in the 
Great 1 September Revolution, the great bastion of the Arabs which fights against imperialism 
and its designs. The Arab people are also called upon, at the present time, to expose 

all the corrupt regimes and to revolt against them so that they may collapse and so that 

all the imperialist designs which are implemented with the support of Arab and other 
reactionary elements may topple. 


Thus the United States proves to the world that it adopts aggressive designs not only 
against the liberation movements but also against all the progressive countries which do 
not pursue a policy compatible with its terrorist policy. Otherwise, why should the 
Reagan administration decide to close down the Libyan Arab People's Bureau in Washington 
believing that, by doing so, it can divide the Arab peoples from the American people? 


The Libyan Arab people, contrary to what the U.S. administration believes, establishes 
its relations with the peoples and not with government regimes. The Jamahiriyah has no 
diplomatic mission in the United States for the Reagan administration to expel. There is 
only a people's committee, which is there to bolster relations between the two peoples.
