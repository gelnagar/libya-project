LIBYA

Congolese President Arrives, Interviewed on Ties 
LD282309 Tripoli JANA in English 1822 GMT 28 Sep 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulhijjah 21, Sept 28, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — President Col Denis Sassou- Nguesso of 
the People’s Republic of Congo arrived in Tripoli this afternoon 
on a visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. At 
Tripoli International Airport a warm welcoming ceremony took 
place for President Sassou- Nguesso and his accompanying del- 
egation where he was met by Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad 
Jallud at the head of the welcoming delegation which included 
secretary and members of the General People’s Congress, mem- 
bers of the General People’s Committee, secretary of the Libyan 
Arab People’s Bureau in the Congolese People’s Republic, Con- 
go's accredited ambassador in Jamahiriyah who resides in Alge- 
ria, and a number of officers in the Armed Forces and heads of 
African diplomatic delegations in Jamahiriyah. 


The Congolese president is accompanied by Pierre Nze, the 
foreign minister, Camille Bonfou, general secretary of the Cen- 
tral Committee of Parti Congolais Du Travail, Basil Acobe, 
diplomatic advisor of the president, Christian Gilbert Bambai, 
communications advisor, and a number of officials in the Congo- 
lese Government. The president of People’s Republic of Congo 
in a statement to JANA's correspondent confirmed that ties 
between the Libyan Arab people and the Congolese people are 
of brotherhood and friendship nature and are relations which the 
Congolese people build with all African people struggling for the 
liberation of the African Continent, foremost the Libyan Arab 
people, the forerunner of these people. He praised the existing 
relations between the Jamahriyah and the Congo and said that 
his visit falls in the framework of further developing these 
relations and to discuss means of bolstering bilateral relations as 
well as African and internal issues. In his answer to a question 
about the colonialist attack and the war of annihiliation waged 
against the Chadian people, by the U.S. and French forces, he 
said that this is a very important matter which will be discussed 
at length during my visit in Jamahiriyah.
