Summary (AI): A 1980s-era press article (OCR-garbled) describing how former CIA employees Edwin Wilson and Frank Terpil, with American explosives makers, supplied explosives and trained personnel for Libya under Qaddafi in 1976-1979. It also mentions Libyan intelligence chief Abdul Senussi and a Libyan palace complex where bombs were built.
• Libyan commandos were believed responsible for the 1980 execution of 11 Libyan exiles living in Europe (as stated in the article).
• Terpil and Wilson were recruiting American explosives experts and a Green Beret for training Libyan commandos.
• The article says the Libyan operation was ongoing until late December 1979, more than three years after the FBI investigation began.
• Brower's company was in California; the recruits included engineers from the China Lake Naval Weapons Station.
• The source describes the Libyan intelligence chief Abdul Senussi suggesting a gas attack on Egyptian infiltrators.
• 1976-08-13: Terpil, Wilson, Brower and others met at the offices of Interarms Technology Inc. and allegedly agreed to ship explosives to Libya without State Department approval.
• 1976: Brower's company in California shipped the first load of explosives to Tripoli, reportedly in drums marked 'Industrial Solvent'.
