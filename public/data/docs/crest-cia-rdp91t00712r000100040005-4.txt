Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery analysis dated 28 September 1983 reports large-scale ground forces training exercises involving 22 maneuver battalions in Libya's Western Military District, observed on 13 August. The main exercise was at Jadu Mizdah training area, about 75 nautical miles southwest of Tarabulus; most units were probably from garrisons in the Tripoli, Az Zawiyah, Tarhunah and Gharyan areas.
• 22 major maneuver battalions observed in three areas of the Western Military District.
• Jadu Mizdah exercise included six probable tank battalions, two probable mechanized infantry battalions, five self-propelled artillery battalions, two MRL battalions, one FROG battalion and one towed artillery battalion.
• Analysts suggest at least two armor brigades and one artillery brigade may have participated, the first observed Libyan brigade-level training.
• Most units probably came from garrisons in Tarabulus, Az Zawiyah, Tarhunah and Gharyan.
• 1983-08-13: Large-scale ground forces training exercises with 22 major maneuver battalions observed in three areas; possibly brigade-level training.

GROUND FORCES TRAINING ACTIVITY 

WESTERN MILITARY DISTRICT, LIBYA (SWN) 5X4 

1. SIGNIFICANCE: LARGE-SCALE GROUND FORCES TRAINING 
EXERCISES, ONE POSSIBLY AT THE BRIGADE LEVEL, WERE 
OBSERVED IN LIBYA'S WESTERN MILITARY DISTRICT ON 13 
AUGUST. THE SIZE AND COMPOSITION OF THIS ACTIVITY IS 
WITHOUT PRECEDENT IN LIBYA. (SWN) 

2. REMARKS: LARGE-SCALE TRAINING EXERCISES INVOLVING 22 
MAJOR MANEUVER BATTALIONS (BNS) WERE OBSERVED IN THREE 
AREAS OF THE WESTERN MILITARY DISTRICT. THE LARGEST OF 
THE THREE EXERCISES WAS IN THE JADU MIZDAH TRAINING AREA 
A NEWLY IDENTIFIED AREA 75 NM SOUTHWEST 25X1 
OF TARABULUS. THE PRIMARY UNITS PARTICIPATING IN THIS 
EXERCISE INCLUDED SIX PROBABLE TANK BNS, TWO PROBABLE 
MECHANIZED (MECH) INFANTRY (INF) BNS, FIVE SELF-PROPELLED | 
ARTILLERY BNS, TWO MULTIPLE ROCKET LAUNCHER (MRL) BNS, 
ONE FROG BN, AND ONE TOWED ARTILLERY BN. THE FIVE 
REMAINING UNITS INVOLVED IN EXERCISES WERE TRAINING IN 
THE EASTERN PORTION OF THE WESTERN MILITARY DISTRICT. 
TWO MECH INF BNS, ONE TANK BN, AND ONE MRL BN WERE 
DEPLOYED 66 NM SOUTHEAST OF TARABULUS AND 17.5 NM 
NORTHWEST OF BANI WALID AT 31-59-00N 013-47-15E. ONE 
TOWED ARTILLERY BN WAS DEPLOYED 120 NM SOUTHEAST OF 
TARABULUS AND 52 NM EAST OF BANI WALID AT 31-42-10N 
015-01-00E. (SWN) 

ANALYST COMMENTS: THE UNITS IN THE JADU MIZDAH AREA MAY 
HAVE BEEN INVOLVED IN BRIGADE LEVEL TRAINING. ALTHOUGH 
NO DISTINGUISHABLE BRIGADE PARKING PATTERNS OR BRIGADE 
MANUEVERS WERE OBSERVED, THE TYPES AND NUMBER OF UNITS 
THAT WERE PRESENT IN THIS AREA SUGGEST THAT AT LEAST TWO 
ARMOR BRIGADES AND ONE ARTILLERY BRIGADE MAY HAVE BEEN 
PARTICIPATING. THIS WOULD BE THE FIRST TIME LIBYAN UNITS 
HAVE BEEN OBSERVED TRAINING WITHIN A BRIGADE STRUCTURE.
