Al-Qadhdhafi Calls for ‘Holy War’ 
LD1910i7 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 19 Nov 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Nov (JANA) — The brother leader of the 
revolution granted a private interview to AFP recently, which 
was transmitted by the press agency last night. In it, he concen- 
trated on the French raid against Shi'ite positions in Lebanon, 
and called for an immediate end to the Iran-Iraq war. He also 
affirmed that the liberation of Lebanon from NATO and Israeli 
forces has become a national, pan-Arab, and Islamic duty. Here 
is text of the statement 


The French raid against Shi'ite positions, launched in the wake 
of Israeli raids against the same positions, stirs boundless anger 
in anyone with either conscience or honor 


These raids are proof that the Christian countries have actually 
begun to implement the program of the tenth crusade under 
America’s leadership. These countries, including the Israelis, 
lack even the slightest amount oi international responsibility 
Their behavior in the Malvinas, on Grenada, and now in Lebanon 
has been inhuman, unlawful, and irresponsible 


The Arab nation and the other Islamic nations, particularly 
revolutionary Iran, must now be prepared for a new holy war 
against the Western nations, which have begun to confirm their 
barbarism on the one hand, and their dormant rancor against the
