LIBYA

ANGOLAN PRESIDENT MAKES TRIPOLI ARRIVAL STATEMENT 


LD302020 Tripoli JANA in English 1846 GMT 30 Sep 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 30 Sep, (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY) -- President Jose Eduardo Dos Santos, 
Chairman of the Party of Labour and President of the People's Republic of Angola, arrived 
in Tripoli this evening on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 
Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud was at the head of the party welcoming the Angolan 
president and the delegation accompanying him on their arrival at Tripoli International 
Airport. 


Present at the airport were also the members of the General Secretariat of the General 
People's Congress, members of the General People's Committee, heads of diplomatic missions 
accredited to the Jamahiriyah, and several officers of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces. 


The Angolan President was accompanied by a top-level delegation including a member of the 
Central Committee who is the party's foreign affairs secretary, Paulo Teixeira Jorge, 
member of the Central Committee and the foreign minister, Artur Vidal Gomes, member of 
the Central Committee and agriculture minister, Alberto Do Carmo Bento Ribeiro, the 
industry minister, and several officials in the Angolan Government. 


President Jose Eduardo Dos Santos made a statement to the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY in 
which he praised the support of the Jamahiriyah people and the leader of its revolution 
for the struggle waged by the Angolan people against the racist regime in South Africa. 


He also praised the stands of the great Al-Fatih Revolution towards liberation movements 
in Africa. 


The Angolan president said: "I am carrying the greetings of the Angolan people to the 
leader of the Jamahiriyah affirming the unity in struggle existing between the people of 
the Jamahiriyah and the Angolan people in confronting imperialism and colonialism." 


The Angolan president also condemned the racist aggression on his country and said: "Every- 
day we expect an attack from the racist regime in South Africa against our country and our 
people." 

He indicated that Angola was the victim of aracist invasion on August 23 and added: "The 
Angolan people is steadfastly confronting and deterring aggressors."' He also expressed his 
satisfaction with the solidarity of several African’ countries with the People's Republic of 
Angola inits struggle against the racist regime in South Africa." 


He affirmed that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah stood firmly by the side 
of the Angolan people." 


At the end of his statement, he said: "The battles we are waging against the racist 
regime in South Africa are still continuing."
