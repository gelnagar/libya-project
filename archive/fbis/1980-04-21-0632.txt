[Text] Tripoli, April 18, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Revolutionary Command of the 
Liberation of Tunisia released a communique affirming that the oppressive Tunisian 
regime's brutal executions on Thursday April 17 of 13 of the revolutionaries who 
revolted against terror and repression was onby a chain in the series of crimes committed 
by the dictator ruling party>of Pwmisia.. Brutal oppression is characteristic of this 
bloddy regime whose list of crimes include: collaboration with the French colonialism 
to assassinate the leader Farhat Hashad; mass killing in the mid- ifties, the killing 
of struggler Saleh Ben Yousef, the execution of the Arab Mujahideen following the up- 
rising of 1962, the shootinz of innocent citizens in Wardanin, the massacre of January 
1978 and now the execution or the free revoluticnaires who led the popular uprising in 
Gafsah. 


The commumique went on to say that the people's forces will not give up struggle or fear 
oppression as they believe that their pathis long and difficult. The movement states 

in its commmique: "We vow to the masses before Allah and the martyrs that we will 
retaliate for the death of the freedom fighters and react firmly to the regime!" 


Those who oppress will sooner or later pay for it. The blood of the creators of the 
regime and of any person supporting it is to be shed. Every person in our cowtry 

should retaliate to avenge the martyrs! pride and dignity. At this critical stage of 

our Tunisian people, who are struggling for full liberation from the claws of oppression, 
and dependence, we call upon the Arab nation, foremost the Steadfastmess and Confrontation 
Front, and the Islamic nation led by the Islamic revolution in Iran and all the revolu- 
tionary and progressive regime, to take a firm stand with the Arab people of Tunisia 

and to stop all assistance to the oppressive regime, because the people do not benefit 
from such assistance, which goes straight to the pockets of the regime's men which 

makes it stronger.
