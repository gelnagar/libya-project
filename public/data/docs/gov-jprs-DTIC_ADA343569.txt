Tarty System Phenomenon’ Criticized 

45040016b Tripoli AL-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR 
in Arabic 19 Oct 87 p 5 

[Article by Muhammad Salim al-Shalamani: “The Party 
System Phenomenon” 

[Text] The party system phenomenon, which is prevalent 
now in the world, is not democratic at all for the 
following reasons: 

1 . A political party represents a segment of the people, 
and the people’s authority is indivisible. 

2. A political party rules on behalf of the people, but 
what is proper is that no one can act on behalf of the 
people. 

3. A partisan system aborts democracy.
