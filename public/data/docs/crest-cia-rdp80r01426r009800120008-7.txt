Summary (AI): A declassified CIA/NIS veterinary survey of Libya (c. 1959) describing weak animal health services, livestock diseases and export limits. It has only background value for a map of Tripoli, with one passing mention of Tripoli's slaughter facilities.
• Animals and animal products accounted for 30 percent of the value of Libya's agricultural exports.
• Libya had no national or federal Ministry of Agriculture; Tripolitanian and Cyrenaican veterinary departments were run by Nazirates of Agriculture.
• In 1957 the Tripolitanian veterinary service employed six Italian veterinarians; the Cyrenaican service employed seven Egyptian veterinarians.
• A ban on cattle movement from Tripolitania to Cyrenaica followed a 1958 foot-and-mouth disease outbreak.
• Libya had no veterinary training center and no professional veterinary organization.
