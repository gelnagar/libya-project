Summary (AI): A 1976 CIA cable (OCR-garbled) reports statements by Libyan leader Muammar al-Qadhdhafi and Prime Minister Abd al-Salam Jallud to a Jordanian newspaper about restoring relations with Jordan and Syria and the Palestinian cause. It concerns foreign policy and has no specific Tripoli sites.
• Qadhdhafi said official resumption of relations and exchange of ambassadors would follow after Libya named its diplomatic corps.
• Jallud said Libya had restored relations with Jordan and would cooperate with Jordan in working for the Palestinian cause under King Husayn's leadership.
• Jallud said Jordan's approval of the Arab summit resolutions recognised the PLO as the Palestinians' legitimate representative.
• Jallud praised the establishment of a confederation with Syria as a nucleus for a general Arab union.
• 1976-05-06: Qadhdhafi and Jallud make statements to Jordanian paper Al-Ra'y (reported from Amman, dated 6 May 1976) on restored Libyan-Jordanian relations
