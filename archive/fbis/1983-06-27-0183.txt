LIBYA

Jamahiriyah ‘Completely Neutral’ Toward Chad 
LD250753 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 25 Jun 83 


[Text] Paris 25 Jun(JANA)— The Libyan Arab People’s Bureau 
in Paris has stressed that Jamahiriyah is completely neutral 
towards the struggle now taking place in Chad; however it will 
find itself forced to intervene if any outside parties attempted to 
on their part to intervene in Chad. 


A statement distributed to journalists yesterday at the press 
conference held by the brother secretary of the Libyan Arab 
People’s Bureau in Paris said that the battles currently taking 
place in Chad are between Chadian opposing groups and that 
Libya has not intervened at all in these battles. 


It stressed that the battles are taking place in regions more than 
1,000 km from the southern border of the Jamahiriyah with Chad 
and any international observers could confirm for himself what 
is taking place inside Chad. The People’s Bureau, in a statement 
accused the Reagan Administration of working to international- 
ize the situation in Chad. 


It stated that the American Administration is involved militarily, 
politically and economically in Africa and that we accuse the 
Reagan Administration of internationalizing the Chad problem 
and confirm that Chad's independence and security are linked to 
the security and stability of the Jamahiriyah. 


It pointed out that there are other parties, neighbors of Chad, 
involved in what is happening in Chad, foremost the regime of 
the lackey Numayri.
