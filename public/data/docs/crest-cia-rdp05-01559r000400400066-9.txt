Algerian-Tunisian Financial Agreement Signed 
LD131148 Tunis Domestic Service 
in Arabic 1600 GMT 13 Nov 85 

[Excerpts] In culmination of the visits between President Habib 
Bourguiba and Chadli Bendjedid, an agreement on financial 
cooperation between the Algerian and Tunisian Governments 
was signed in Algiers today. It was signed for Tunisia by Noured- 
dine Hached in his capacity as Tunisian ambassador to Algeria 
and for Algeria by Mohamed Bardan, secretary general of the 
Algerian Ministry of Finance. 

The agreement deals with loans granted by Algeria to Tunisia 
with easy terms. This is the first time such a thing has taken place 
in the history of economic relations between the two countries. 
The bases of this agreement were laid during the last visit that 
Mohamed Maali paid to Algiers. 

The first loan, amounting to $10 million, will be used to finance 
industrial projects in Tunisia, while the second loan, amounting 
to $20 million, will be used to finance a number of agricultural 
projects in southern Tunisia. 

Yesterday Hatched paid a number of farewell visits to Boualem 
Benhamouda, Algerian finance minister, and Larbi Sfar, secre- 
tary general of the Algerian [word indistinct], on the occasion of 
his completion of his accreditation to Algeria and his return to 
Tunisia.

Budapest TV Interviews Al-Qadhdhafi on U.S. 
LD142237 Budapest Television Service 
in Hungarian 1945 GMT 14 Nov 85 

[From ae Week” program: Interview with Colonel Mu‘am- 
mar al afi by correspondent Alajos Chrudinak, in Tripoli 
on ovember; Al-Qadhdhafi’s answers in Arabic with super- 
imposed translation — recorded] 

[Text] [Chrudinak] U.S. President Reagan and even President 
Carter before him have alleged that Libya is a terrorist state, and 

dangerous man on earth, someone who threatens peace, and so 
forth. In your opinion, what is the reason for this attack on Libya 
and on you, personally? 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] America has greedy dreams, and now it imagines 
that it can attain these in the Arab region. But we are fighting 
to stop it achieving its aims. America is carrying out a compre- 
hensive strategic move in order to encircle the socialist camp and 
the Soviet Union. It wants to annex Africa, Asia, Western 
Europe; it wants to gain control over the Red Sea, the Mediterra- 
nean and the Indian Ocean, as well as the Pacific. And now it is 
militarizing space. It is pursuing an imperialist policy; it wants 
to swallow the entire world. 

America’s final aim is to place the socialist countries under a 
blockade and to annihilate the Soviet Union. America views the 
Arab countries as one of the spheres of interest it can control — 
where it can establish military bases and take the natural 
resources and use them in its imperialist wars. However, we are 
keeping an eye on America, and every time it interferes in the 
affairs of an Arab country we condemn it, attack it, and agitate 
against it. When the Libyan revolution began, we stopped Amer- 
ica from ‘plundering Libya, we took from it approximately 2 
million square km of Libyan territory that earlier had been at the 
disposal of the American Armed Forces. We took the Libyan 
shore, which is about 2,000 km long, from it. 

{Chrudinak] Along the Mediterranean. 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] Yes, we took the Mediterranean shore of Libya, 
which had been under American control, from the Americans. 
Naturally, America does not like this, and this is why America 
regards us as its number one enemy. This is because we are 
opposing it while America is working against the Soviet Union. 
We often say: If someone is endeavoring to reach a great goal 
but meets resistance along the way, however small that resistance 
may be, it regards this as its number one enemy, the one it must 
defeat before it can progress toward the great goal. When 
America proceeds toward the great target, the socialist camp and 
the Soviet Union, it finds that Libya is one of the points of 
resistance in its path. This is how we have become public enemy 
number one: Because America can advance toward its goal 
only if it eliminates resistance along the way. 

Look, I would not like to see the Arab countries trodden upon by 
the American boot while America was advancing toward the 
Soviet Union and the socialist countries. In other words, we are 
hindering America in its imperialist policy aimed at the Soviet 
Union. We are doing a service to the Soviet Union and the 
socialist countries, as well as opposing imperialism. Our national 
interests dictate that we should oppose it, because we do not want 
to become an American colony. 

(Chrudinak] This is an interest we hold in common. 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] At the same time, we are doing a service to the 
Soviet Union, bécause if the Soviet Union succeeds in overcoming 
imperialism, this will have a favorable effect on our issues of 
national fate, because imperialism is the common enemy.

[Chrudinak] You are accused of giving assistance to the liber- 
ation movements, to international terrorism. It is said that ter- 
rorists are trained here. What can you say to these accusations? 

{Al-Qadhdhafi] Naturally, I oppose terrorism. In my opinion it 
is precisely America that is carrying out terrorist actions, and 
here in the Middle East it is the Israelis. This is the real terrorism, 
because we are supporting the peoples’ struggle for indepen- 
dence, but the imperialists, Zionists, and racists do not want these 
peoples to gain their freedom. They are even accusing the PLO 
of being a terrorist organization, yet it is a movement for national 
independence. Thus, when we help liberation organizations we 
are accused of supporting terrorism: They say that Libya 
supports terrorism because we are giving help to the national 
liberation movements. The matter is extremely simple: They 
call the struggle for independence, which the world regards as a 
just fight of humanity — its sacred cause — terrorism. 

(Chrudinak] Would you be willing to take part in an international 
conference dealing with the fight against terrorism? 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] Yes. 

[Chrudinak] What sort of recommendations would you put for- 
ward there? 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] What I would like is for this international con- 
ference to come together in order to discuss the concept of 
terrorism, in order to define the meaning of terrorism. Then, 
those who support the activities that the world has determined to 
be terrorism should be declared terrorists. I am sure that the 
conference, after determining the meaning of the word terrorism, 
would condemn America, because terrorism is what is done by 
America and the Israelis.

from Dr. Samir Ghawshah, secretary general of the Palestinian 
Popular Struggle Front. It reads: 

“On behalf of the leadership, cadres, fighters of the Popular 
Struggle Front and also on behalf of myself, we hereby announce 
that we firmly stand with your revolution, people and yourself 
against the imperialist threats and against the conspiracies 
hatched by the CIA which not only aim against you personally 
but also against the Great al-Fateh Revolution, its achievements 
and our Arab nation. 

These conspiracies represent a violation and flagrant intervention 
in the rights of our nation and its national integrity. 

Imperialism has always threatened the Arab people and has 
become even more arrogant when Reagan assumed office to take 
the form of direct and indirect military intervention. It still 
escalates its aggression policy against our Arab nation. Its latest 
scheme was this conspiracy against the Libyan Arab people. 

O brother, we take the pledge while having our fingers on the 
trigger, that any aggression against the Great al-Fateh Rey- 
olution represents an aggression against the Palestinian rev- 
olution. We will use all forms of struggle to combat against any 
USS. imperialist aggression against the Great al-Fateh Arab 
Revolution. 

The right of our Arab nation and people is sacred that should not 
be violated. no flagrant threats of plots could frighten us, for we 
will stand up against any plot that aims at wrecking al- 
Jamahiriyah and the Arab nation. 

We reiterate to you that we will firmly carry on struggling 
against the enemies of our nation and will remain with you in one 
trench.”
