LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Sends Message to Iran’s Khomeyni 
LD180913 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 18 Jun 83 


[Text] Tripoli 18 Jun (JANA) — JANA has learned that Brother 
Colonel Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great | Septem- 
ber Revolution, in the past few days sent a message to his holiness 
Imam Ayatollah Khomeyni imploring him to personally inter- 
vene over internal events in Iran, the reactions to which has 
pleased imperialism, primarily America. These events are to all 
appearances achieving one blow after another which imperialism 
wants against the progressive forces. 


The leader in his message stressed that we fear that behind what 
has happened there are forces hostile to the Islamic Revolution 
want to split its ranks from within and demolish the revolutionary 
forces which stood by it at times of difficulty. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Addresses Military Academy Cadets 
LD201025 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 GMT 20 Jun 83 


{Text} Tripoli, 20 Jun [JANA] — The commander of the rev- 
olution yesterday evening met with cadets of the military acad- 
emy and explained to them the current Arab situation following 
his recent tour of a number of Arab countries and that there is 
no hope except in the new Palestine resistance and Syria's 
steadfastness. 


The commander affirmed that the historical role of Libya was to 
instigate and amass all the potentials of the Arab nation to face 
up to the Zionist death train which is threatening the Arab nation 
from the Gulf to the ocean. 


After the brother commander ended his speech, the cadets staged 
a huge march affirming their commitment to and adherance and 
readiness to defend the Great | September Revolution and to 
block the death train.
