Summary (AI): A 1988 CIA cable reports that Libyan External Security Organization officers travelled from Tripoli to Malta in October 1988 on Libyan Arab Airlines flights, met at a rented apartment in Valletta, and tried to book onward travel to Chad before being told to stand down by ESO official Abdallah Sanussi.
• The four ESO officers were in Malta as a group at the direction of senior ESO officer Abdallah Muhammad Sanussi.
• Source comment says the purpose of the mission is unknown but it appears to have been a special operation.
• Massud travelled on Libyan passport number 835004.
• Ashur travelled under the alias Nasr Salim with Libyan passport number 689008.
• Maraby travelled on Libyan passport number 422898.
• 1988-10-08: Libyan ESO officers Abd al-Basat el-Megri and Abu Agela Massud arrived in Malta from Tripoli on a Libyan Arab Airlines flight and met other group members at a Valletta apartment.
• 1988-10-10: Senior ESO officer Nasr Ashur arrived in Malta under the alias Nasr Salim, followed on the same flight by ESO officer Abd al-Salam el-Maraby; el-Megri left for Rome and the others returned to Libya.
