LIBYA

JANA CRITICIZES GUINEA FOR NOT ATTENDING OAU SUMMIT 


LD271536 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1455 GMT 27 Jul 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 27 Jul (JANA) -- A strong section within OAU circles believes it will 
be difficult to hold the next African summit, scheduled for next year in Guinea, because 
of Guinea's failure to attend thecurrent OAU conference in Tripoli. 


This view is based on the premise that the attitude now taken by the Guinean Government 

is a violation of African unity and solidarity, particularly since Guinea is one the OAU's 
founding members and its president, Sekou Toure, has always adhered to the OAU Charter 
and has been anxious at most times to ensure the organization's coherence. 


Several questions hang over the Guinean Government's failure to attend this African 
meeting, which brings together several African men of responsibility. 


This attitude does not serve the interests of Guinea and the efforts it has exerted to 
ensure the holding of the next conference on its territory. 


JANA CITES SAUDI-IRAQI MILITARY COOPERATION 


LD271544 Tripoli JANA in English 1419 GMT 27 Jul 82 


[Text ] Paris, Shawal 7, July 27, Jamahiriyah News Agency —- Informed Arab sources in the 
Gulf have pointed out that the Saudi ruling family and Saddam Husayn's regime have reached 
a detailed agreement on developing joint military cooperation against Iran. 


The sources said the Saudi rulers, over the past ten days, have given the Iraqi regime a 
big quantity of weapons, free of charge, to save the Iraqi regime from collapse in its war 
against Iran. The sources added Prince ‘Abdallah's recent visit to Baghdad was within 
that framework. 


Saddam Husayn has already admitted that he had received 20 billion dollars from the 
Saudi family and Gulf states to continue his war against the Iranian revolution. 


OPPOSITION LEADER ON AL-QADHDHAFI'S ‘HMADNESS' 


PM271507 Cairo AL-AKHBAR in Arabic 25 Jul 82 p 6 


["Adil Rida report] 


[Excerpts] Dr Muhammad Yusuf al-Maqaryiaf, secretary general of the National Front for 
the Salvation of Libya, has made an exclusive statement to AL-AKHBAR, in which he praised 
Egypt's current wise and responsible action to find an acceptable solution to the Lebanese 
crisis that will ensure the attainment of the demands of the Palestinian and the Lebanese 
peoples. He said the Palestinian Resistance is bound to triumph in its just struggle 
regardless of time and difficulties. 


Al-Magaryiaf denounced the defeatist stand of certain Arab countries that only serve the 
great powers hostile to the Arab struggle. He particularly denounced Libya's call to the 
Resistance to commit collective suicide, saying that such a call is new confirmation of 
Al-Qadhdhafi's treason and madness and his shirking of national responsibilities in 
critical times. 


V. 28 Jul 82 ie NORTH AFRICA 


Al-Maqaryiaf said Al-Qadhdhafi's attitude toward Iraq in its war with Iran is similar 

to that of Israel. He has provided Iran with arms and funds in its war against a Muslim 
Arab people. Instead of joining Arab and Islamic efforts to stop the fighting between 
brothers in the Gulf war, he went on inciting and inflaming the conflict. 


Moreover, Al-Qadhdhafi has been pursuing the policy of exporting conspiracies and terror 
and sowing sedition on the African Continent. Not a single Arab or African country has 
been spared the abuse of Al-Qadhdhafi or his conspiracies and crimes. Examples of such 
actions are evident in Egypt, Sudan, Chad and elsewhere. 


Al-Maqaryiaf said: Our Libyan Arab and Muslim people are shocked by the squandering 

of their wealth and resources in foolish and malicious battles. They sincerely apologize 
to our Arab nation and to the African Continent for keeping Al-Qadhdhafi in power all 
these years. They affirm, and may God be their witness, that they will not rest until 
this pervert and mad ruier is eliminated so that they can rejoin the Arab and Islamic 
caravan and be an added strength to the Arab struggle and to Arab-African cooperation 

and Islamic solidarity. 


In conclusion, Al-Maqaryiaf affirmed the inevitability of the triumph of the Libyan 
people's will against Al-Qadhdhafi and his tyranny. He called upon Arab and African 
countries not to be deceived by Al-Qadhdhafi and his pledges nor to break the isolation 
in which he lives. He urged Arab and African countries to boycott the African summit 
if it is held in Libya, because this would only further his conspiracies against the 
African Continent and be a blow to Arab-African relations.
