LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Meets With Saudi King’s Envoy 
LD061006 Tripoli JANA in English 0824 GMT 06 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada al Ulla 3, February 6, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The leader of the revolution met Saturday 
evening with Shaykh ‘Ali Musallam, King Fahd’s envoy, who 
conveyed to the leader King Fahd’s confirmation that the return 
of the Egyptian regime to the Islamic Conference Organisation 
is not possible because of the Egyptian regime’s non- 
reconciliation between its commitment to the 1981 Islamic sum- 
mit resolutions in At-Ta‘if which stipulated the liberation of 
Palestine, the regaining of sacred Jerusalem and the return of the 
Palestinian people to their usurped land, and its commitment to 
the Camp David agreement 


Nigerian Military Chief of Staff Visits 


Arrival in Tripoli 
LD060313 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1830 GMT 5 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 5 Feb (JANA) Brigadier Mohammad Jado, 
member of the Nigerian Supreme Military Council and chief of 
staff, arrived in Tripoli this evening. 


Brigadier Mustafa al-Kharrubi, chief of the General Staff of the 
Libyan Arab Armed Forces, was at Tripoli International Airport 
to welcome Brigadier Mohammed Magoro and his accompany- 
ing delegation 


Received by Al-Qadhdhafi 
LD062047 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 6 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 Feb (JANA) The brother leader of the 
revolution today received Brigadier Mohammad Jado, member 
of the Nigerian Supreme Military Council and chief of staff, who 
conveyed to the brother leader a message from Major General 
Mohammad Buhari, chairman of the Nigerian Supreme Mili- 
tary Council. 


Nigerian Visitor Departs 
LD062051 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 6 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 Feb (JANA) Brigadier Mohammad Jado, mem- 
ber of the Nigerian Supreme Military Council and chief of staff, 
left Tripoli this evening together with his accompanying del- 
egation at the end of his visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah. 


He was seen off at Tripoli airport by Brigadier Mustafa al- 
Kharrubi, chief of the general staff of the Libyan Arab Armed 
Forces.
