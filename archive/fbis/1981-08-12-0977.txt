LIBYA

JABIR HOSTS CHADIAN, SAHARAN, BULGARIAN MINISTERS 


LD111004 Tripoli JANA in English 0855 GMT 11 Aug 81 


{Text] Tripoli, 1 Aug (JANA)--Brigadier ‘Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the 
Libyan Arab Armed Forces, last’ night held a banquet attended by Mr Adam Tojo, Chadian 
defense minister; MrHamad Abu Said, Chadian interior minister; Mr Ibrahim Gali, defence 
minister of the Saharan Arab Republic; and the first deputy minister of defence and chief 
of the Socialist People's Republic of Bulgaria.
