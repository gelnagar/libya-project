Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery analysis note reports heavy-lift transporter activity at Tajura Barracks and a vehicle storage area in Libya, linked possibly to Libyan operations in Chad, and tanks and artillery moving to Ounianga Kebir airfield in Chad. The Tajura site is in the Tripoli area.
• At least 34 empty heavy-lift transporters (21 in convoy) remain in the northern section of the Tajura installation.
• Five SF.260 light-strike aircraft and three Hip helicopters are present at Ounianga Kebir.
• Coordinates given for Tajura: 34-49-50N 013-27-30E.
• Coordinates given for Ounianga Kebir: 19-02-34N 020-27-40E.
• 1983-08: At least five probable tanks loaded onto heavy-lift transporters; activity may be related to Libyan operations in Chad.
• 1983-08: At least ten 130mm M-46 field guns arrived; BMP-equipped mechanized infantry battalion and armored unit deployed to defend the airfield; at least 39 armored vehicles discernible.

1. SIGNIFICANCE: A HIGH LEVEL OF HEAVY-LIFT TRANSPORTER ACTIVITY 
CONTINUES AT TAJURA BARRACKS AND VEHICLE STORAGE AREA IN 
LIBYA. IN CHAD, AN INCREASE IN DEFENSIVE MEASURES IS OBSERVED 
AT OUNIANGA KEBIR OLD AIRFIELD. (SWN)

NOT IMAGED. THIS ACTIVITY MAY BE RELATED TO THE ONGOING LIBYAN 
OPERATIONS IN CHAD. (SWN)
