LIBYA

JALLUD ADDRESSES ARAB CONGRESS SECRETARIAT MEMBERS 
LDO92140Y Tripoli JANA in English 0943 GMT 9 Jan 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli,Jan 9, Jamahiriyah News Agency--Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud 
met yesterday members of the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress at the 
People's Palace in Tripoli. 


He called for the necessity of doubling the activities of the Permanent Secretariat of 
the Arab People's Congress and the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front to fulfill its 
unity message of the national sphere and to work for moving the Arab man to make him feel 
his important role being the owner of the cause, 


Staff-Major Jallud clarified that the Arab man enjoys all the national factors in spite 

of the psychological struggles he experiences when expose him to the attempts of inner 
destruction to the extent that his thought almost became paralysed and indifferent. 
Staff-Major Jallud affirmed that the Arab People's Congress constitutes a unity prelude 
for the Arab nation. He said that the colonialist and Zionist forces believed that they 
were in political circumstances wich enable them to hinder the march but the Steadfastness 
and Confrontation Front constituted a very important factor in constricting these attempts. 


He indicated that imperialism and Arab reaction exerted desperate attempts to put an end 
to the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front so that it will not create progressive forces 
as that constitutes danger to their interests, and as the [words indistinct] the true 
path for the liberation of Palestine and an historic and national start for our Arab 
nation's struggle. 


Staff-Major Jallud demanded the necessity of continuation of meetings and conferences 
organized by the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress in order to transfer 
the Arab citizen from the dictating process to the process of thinking and initiative, 
and inorder to become a prelude for an Arab, national and people's movement supporting 
progressive regimes, 


Staff-Major Jallud said that our interest in the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front 
is a national interest and not that of [words indistinct].

HUNGARIAN FOREIGN MINISTER ENDS 3-DAY VISIT 
LDO91415Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 9 Jan 79 LD 


[Text] Frigyes Puja, the Hungarian foreign minister, this morning left Tripoli following 
a 3-day visit to the Jamahiriyah during which he had talks about strengthening lateral 
cooperation relations between the Jamahiriyah and Hungary. 


Frigyes Puja made a statement to the JANA representative in which he praised the achieve- 
ments he saw in the Jamahiriyah, He said: These achievements confirm the ability of 

the Libyan Arab people to build themselves and to realize their aspirations. He stressed 
that his visit to the Jamahiriyah will give a strong impetus to bilateral relations be- 
cause direct contacts are an important factor in strengthening relations and cooperation 
between the two countries, At the end of his statement he expressed his gratitude and 
appreciation for the extensive hospitality with which he and his delegation were received 
throughout their visit to the Jamahiriyah, 


The Hungarian minister was seen off at Tripoli International Airport by Dr 'Ali ‘Abd 
as-Salam at-Turayki, the foreim secretary, the assistant secretary for political affairs, 
directors of the departments at th» Foreign Secretariat and the ambassadors of the socialist 
countries to the Jamahiriyah, 


Press Stotement on Visit 
LDO91540Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 9 Jan 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--A press statement was published today on the visit of the Hungarian Foreigm 
Minister Frigyes Puja, to the Jamahiriyah, 


The statement emphasized the need to increase and deepen the continuing cooperation between 
the two countries in the economic, technical and scientific fields, 


Views were exchanged on the situation in the Arab region. They emphasized that it consti- 
tutes a threat to international security and peace. They agreed that the attempts which 
are being made by colonialism, Zionism and reaction, such as the agreements of Camp David, 
cannot possibly lead to the solution of the explosive situation in the region, but are 
aimed at pushing through the capitulatory and separatist solutions to liquidate the issues 
of the Palestinian people. To strike at their just struggle for the liberation of their 
land and for a return to the homeland, to determine their own fate and to establish their 
own state in Palestine. 


The two sides affirmed the importance placed on the shoulders of the Front of Steadfastness 
and Confrontation in foiling the attempts and designs aimed at liquidating the Palestinian 
issue. 


They also underlined the importance of the resolutions of the Conference of Steadfastness 
and Confrontation and the ninth Arab summit cmference in Baghdad in foiling the capitu- 
latory and separate political designs which constitute a threat to the interests of the 
Arab people .

The two sides agreed to make further efforts to support the liberation movements which 
struggle for the elimination of racism and colonialism in Zimbabwe, Namibia and South 
Africa, They condemned the colonialist [word indistinct] in Africa, 


They supported the convening of a world conference for disarmament as soon as possible, 
and asserted the peoples! right to defend their freedom against imperialism and racism, 
They also underlined the need to liquidate colonialist military bases and the withdrawal 
of the armed forces from the Mediterranean region, 


The two sides highly appreciated the constructive role which the nonalined countries are 
playing in upholding the causes of struggle, justice and right. 


They agreed to find a new international economic order founded on a just and equal basis 
that will enable all the peoples to control their own natural resources, 


The two sides expressed their satisfaction with the friendly atmosphere in which the talks 

were held--(?the atmosphere) which expressed the spirit of firm relations between the 

two countries in particular, and the spirit of cooperation existing between the progressive 

forces in the Arab homeland and the countries of the socialist community in general,
