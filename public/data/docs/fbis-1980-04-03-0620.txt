LIBYA

'IMPORTANCE OF STEADFASTNESS SUMMIT RECOGNIZED! 


LD030918 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 3 Apr 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--The JANA political editor has denied the report put out by QATARI NEWS 
AGENCY (QNA) on Monday that Libya informed Syria that it would not attend the steadfast- 
ness and confrontation summit or any other mesting of the states of the Tripoli charter 
called by the PLO. The editor said that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
firmly recognizes the importance of the Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation and 
regards it as a struggling organization which expresses the aspirations of the Arab 
masses in their struggle against capitulation and defeat, the American solutions and 

the Zionist aggression, 


fhe editor added that Libya is an Arab country which embraces all Arabs, and from this 


principle it does not believe that the arrival of any Arab constitutes a problem. Hence 
it welcomes the arrival of Yasir tArafat, in his capacity as an Arab, at any time. 


ALGERIAN PRESIDENT CONCLUDES VISIT, DEPARTS 
LD021623 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1545 GMT 2 Apr 80 LD 
{Text] Tripoli--Algerian President Chadli Bendjedid and the delegation accompanying 


him left Tripoli this afternoon at the end of his visit to the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. The Algerian president was given a popular sendoff.
