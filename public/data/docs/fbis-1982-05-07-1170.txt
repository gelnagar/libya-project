LIBYA

OFFICIAL MEETS WITH ENVOYS ON MOROCCAN PLANS 


LD062114 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 6 May 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 May (JANA) -- The brother secretary general of the People's Foreign 
Liaison Bureau conferred this evening with heads of fraternal Arab diplomatic missions 
in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. He handed them a note including the 
text of the statement made by the brother leader of the revolution to JANA, concerning 
the visit the Moroccan minister of state for foreign affairs plans to make to the 
Egyptian capital and his attempt to restore Moroccan-Egyptian relations. 


The brother secretary general stressed what the brother leader's statement said, that 
is, if this step is carried out, it will mean a retraction from the Baghdad summit's 
resolutions stipulating the blacklisting of the Egyptian regime. It also is a breach of 
the Arab League's Charter and will insult the Palestinian people who are being killed 
and mistreated every day. 


He warned any attempt at rapprochement with the Egyptian regime or even the idea of 
restoring relations with it in the shadow of its present connections with the enemy will, 
by necessity, be dealing with treachery and the perpetuation of its consequences and 
also an acknowledgement of the enemy through Egypt; it also will mean bartering 
Palestine for a fictitious sovereignty over the Sinai. 


At the end of the meeting, the brother secretary general asked the heads of the Arab 
diplomatic missions to convey the text of the leader's statement to their governments 
as the nature of the situation requires the definition of stances in accordance with 
respect for the bases of pan-Arab action vis-a-vis the great fateful causes.
