Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC briefing note reports six Libyan Air Force FLOGGER B interceptors armed with APEX air-to-air missiles at Al Bumbah Airfield North, on Libya's northeast coast, 48 nm northwest of Tubruq. The site is far from Tripoli, so it has no direct bearing on the Tripoli map.
• The FLOGGER B has improved avionics and infrared subsystems.
• The APEX AAM has a maximum effective range of up to 14 nm, versus 4 nm for the primary FLOGGER AAM, the ATOLL.
• The FLOGGER E is currently the primary FLOGGER interceptor of the Libyan Air Force.
• Collateral reference: DIA DST-1320S-105-82, 5 April 1982 (SECRET).
• Document is an NPIC briefing note, control no. 7-13990/83, dated 23 September 1983.
• 1983-09-23: Six FLOGGER B interceptors armed with APEX air-to-air missiles observed on the apron and taxiways; first confirmation of this combination in Libya.
