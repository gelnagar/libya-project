LIBYA

PALESTINIAN LEADERS ARRIVE IN TRIPOLI 


LD111528 Tripoli JANA in English 1420 GMT 11 Jan 83 


{[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Awal 27, Jan 11, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- A number of 
Palestinian leaders arrived here last night including Ahmad Jibril, secretary general 
of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine (General Command), Samir Ghaw- 
shah, secretary general of the Palestinian Popular Struggle Front (P.P.S.F.), ‘'Isam 
al-Qadi, secretary general of the vanguards of the Popular War of Liberation (As-Sa' 
iqah), Fadl Sharur, member of the Politburo of the Popular Front for the Liberation 
of Palestine and Haytham, ‘Abd al-Qadir, member of the Politburo of the P.P.S.F. 


They made statements to our correspondent stressing the escalation of armed struggle 
against the Zionist enemy as part of their full conviction in nationalist responsibil- 
ity to liberate the Arab occupied land and halt the Zionist death wagon, which 
embodies Zionist domination and that of U.S. imperialism's ally, Arab reaction. 

They called for drawing a clear-cut programme of struggle, at Palestinian and Arab 
levels, to confront the current stage's dangers. This should be fully carried out 
through the Arab masses participation which had been absent from the struggle against 
the Zionist enemy and its U.S. imperialist ally.
