AL-QADHDHAFI SPEECH AT ISLAMIC CONFERENCE 


LD191044 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 2044 GMT 18 Aug 82 


{[Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi speech to the morning session of the second Islamic call conference 
in Tripoli on 18 August -- recorded] 


[Excerpts] In the name of God, it gives me pleasure to welcome you to your country and 
among your brothers. We, the believers, are brothers. I do not consider you strangers 

to this country, because (?the people of Libya) defend Islam and use all their material and 
moral capabilities for the protection of oppressed Muslims everywhere who are making 
historic cultural inroads to create a new civilization based on the values of Islam and its 
lofty ideals.

I am going to talk very briefly, in order to allow time for discussion of your program. 
I take this opportunity to talk to you about Islam from my point of view. I believe 

it is worthwhile for me to stand before this conference and submit to it my ideas about 
the universality of Islam and about the political and revolutionary thesis of Islam. 


I want you to understand that an important and unusual feature in the Arab region and 
the Islamic world is that a progressive, socialist revolution can take place and, at the 
same time, be Islamic. We were accustomed to the types of coups that used to take place 
in the Arab and Islamic worlds. These revolutions claimed to be progressive, socialist 
ones and viewed themselves as contradictory to Islam. We did not see a socialist or 
progressive call in the Arab and Islamic world spring from Islam or rely on it. The 
fact is that before Hafiz al-Asad came to power in Syria, the Syrian Ba'th Party [words 
indistinct]. 


We are now at the end of a stage in which we have reaped the results of this serious 
thesis. We are now at the end of a stage that has enabled us to draw lessons from the 
experiences of the past and the serious attempts of the past. Before us are two very 
clear (?pictures) or lines, especially in the Arab world. One direction is that which 
exploits nationalism and considers it contradictory to religion. The nationalist direc- 
tion was adopted primarily by the B'athist movement in the Arab world and the pan-Arab 
movement. These pan-Arab parties beleive in a secular state built only on nationalism. 
This direction is now in its death throe and faces a deadend. The pan-Arab movement 

has disappeared forever and the Ba'th Party is now in its death throes, as is the case 
in Iraq. 


This direction led to the emergence of another direction contradictory to it -- each is 
a reaction to the others. This is the direction that has adopted religion and fights 
nationalism which it considers it [words indistinct]. This is tribal solidarity of the 
type that preceded Islam. It is an ideal which rejects nationalism and which its 
adherents think solves the problem. This direction is led by the Muslim brothers. 


What have been the results of the two directions? The secular direction (?destroyed) 

the movements that adopted it. We also see today the end of the movements that adopted 
the second direction. The brothers fight Arab nationalism, for instance, [words 
indistinct]. They thus are also fighting against the unity of the Arab nation and are 

in harmony with colonialism and Zionism, which work to prevent the creation of Arab unity 
because this would mean the emergence of a force that would deprive colonialism of its 
ability to exploit and colonize this homeland. This is what the Muslim brothers movement 
and the Islamic Liberation Party, an Islamic movement hostile to nationalism, have in 
common with colonialism and why they have been adopted by colonialism. America fights 
Arab unity, the Israelis fight Arab unity, the Muslim brothers fight Arab unity and the 
Islamic Liberation Party fightsArab unity. Thus, these four groups are two groups: one 
calls itself Muslim and the other includes Zionism and colonialism. 


The movements which claim to be Muslim and exploit Islam [words indistinct] Muslim brothers 
have also adopted the struggle against socialism. They link socialism with communism, con- 
sidering communism an heretical theory preaching scientific socialism. So these movements 
are hostile to socialism, (?relying) on this incorrect interpretation. Thus, the movements 
claiming to be Islamic, through their fighting against progress and socialism, came to terms 
with capitalism, which is headed by the United States and which, naturally, is hostile to 
socialism and [word indistinct] progress and wants to (?establish) the exploitative 
capitalist ideology.

The movements that claim to be Islamic [words indistinct] fight the relationship between 
the Arab nation and the socialist countries. This also is encouraged by the United States 
and the Israelis since they are interested in the Arabs having no allies or friends and, 
in this way, can make them (?submit). What has been the result of this trend which has 
warred against nationalism and which has adopted this course? The outcome has been that 
they have thrown themselves into the arms of colonialism and Zionism, 


It is regrettable that the headquarters and leadership of the Muslim brothers have 
completely moved to the United States for protection. You know that they are waging a 
war against the Fatah Revolution, against Arab unity and against the people's authority 
from rostrums in the United States. Those who have been recruited by the United States 
to fight against us are (?protected) by U.S. intelligence. What Islam is this, which 
the United States is protecting? What kind of person is he who calls for Islam and is 
protected by U.S. intelligence? These are people whom we all know. The Muslim brothers 
[words indistinct] and others have their headquarters in the United States. The United 
States protects them, supports them and finances them so as to succeed in achieving its 
goals, namely, preventing the establishment of Arab unity, the paving of the road to 
progress and socialism and the establishment of friendship between the socialist countries 
on the one hand and the Arab revolution and the Arab revolutionary forces on the other. 
This is treason. This movement that claims to be Islamic has met with Zionism. It has 
lost its Islamic meaning and has become a vehicle for imperialism and Zionism to invade 
the Islamic and Arab homeland. 


We act on the basis of the Arab nation becoming united and on the assumption that this 
unity would be based on Arab nationalism, on account of the Arab nation being one nation. 
Its spirit, however, must be Islam. The Arab nation should be united through a strong 
Islamic revival that makes it aware of its responsibility as a nation and as the nation 
of Islam and of its responsibility toward Muslims everywhere. We in Libya are like a 
miniature Arab nation. We act on the basis of Arab nationalism and urge the Arab masses 
from the ocean to the Gulf to encourage pan-Arab nationalism. We also strengthen the spirit 
of Islam and are leading it in a strong, new revival. We have become a model of the Arab 
nation so that when it becomes united in the future, it will be on the basis of religion 
and nationalism and on a revolutionary progressive basis. This is why Libya after the 
revolution became more concerned about the conditions of Muslims than any other Islamic 
or Arab state and than prerevolutionary Libya. 


The point I want to draw to your attention is that, as a rule, when a revolution breaks 
out in the Arab and Islamic world, we have been accustomed to seeing it separating itself 
from Islam and the spiritual issues and championing purely material issues. They fail 
because they stand on one leg or in a body without a spirit. However, we have learned 
lessons because the Libyan revolution took place at the end of a stage that permitted 

us to gather the lessons learned from the experiences of the past. We drew lessons from 
then. 


Libya is concerned about the conditions of Muslims. Libya has become a symbol of hope 
for Muslims persecuted everywhere. At the same time, Libya is a symbol of unity and 
liberation to the Arab masses. From this pan-Arab angle, we bring together the Arab 
masses. We are a symbol of unity. The evidence of this is in your acceptance of this 
invitation, coming from every corner and region. Your acceptance indicates that you 
have great confidence in Libya and that there is an Islamic call in Libya; you got 
together and spontaneously expressed your feelings toward Libya.

No one has said that the revolution in Libya contradicts Islam. It is clear that 
revolution cannot contradict religion. We have found that there is no contradiction 
between revolution and religion. On the contrary, they are body and soul. (?This was 
lacking before.) Nct one of you has (?objected to the fact) that there is socialism in 
Libya and that this is considered a form of disbelief and contradicts Islam. 


I would like to say that the reactionary trend in the countries that embrace Islam has 
harmed Islam and [words indistinct] for example, Saudi Arabia is considered by non-Arab 
Muslims to be a religious state and the defender of the house of God. They feel that 
anyone who wants to learn about Islam should go to this religious state. The Saudi 
Arabian model has caused great historic harm to Islam. The Saudi model is a reactionary 
one. As it is a reactionary model, then it must be contrary to Islam. The shock comes 
when the Muslims discover that the religious state of Saudi Arabia is the worse example 
on earth and that it is the most immoral and sinful of all countries and the most 
(?backward). 


Malicious people accuse Islam of being reactionary, but Islam is not reactionary. 
Unfortunately, Islam is found in areas which had been colonized and became 
underdeveloped and which after becoming underdeveloped were exploited and turned into 
reactionary countries. This situation reflects on Islam. The Europeans, particularly 
the rancorous ones, the anti-Islam fanatics and crusaders, have exploited this «nd said 
that Islam means palaces, concubines and harems. When we ask them where they learned 
this, they say: From Saudi Arabia. They think Islam permits drinking and gambling and 
squandering of wealth. We say to them: Islam combats these things. They answer: No, 
this exists in Saudi Arabia. The princes and kings of Saudi Arabia, against God's will, 
gamble and drink alcohol and commit immoral acts and squander money on things He 
prohibited in the places of amusement in Europe. They see these things themselves every 
night. And you know that, in the United States and Europe, the Saudi princes travel in 
private planes overnight to gamble in Europe and return home before daybreak. [Sentence 
- dndistinct] The Europeans and the crusaders, the enemies of Islam, exploit this and say 
Islam is synonymous with gambling, drinking, waste, palaces, gold, silver and concubines. 
Yet Islam, in the Koran, is against this. 


The Saudi princes divide the oil between them; Saudi Arabia sells 7 million barrels a 
day. The United States takes whatever it wishes and the rest is divided among the 
princes. Each prince owns an oilfield and a number of barrels. Is this Islam? This 
reflects on Islam. Therefore, any of you who cherishes Islam must surely declare jihad 
against this bad example that harms Islam. 


The United States currently is leading a modern crusade. The U.S. 6th Fleet is on a 
10th crusade, or near enough. We regard the U.S. 6th Fleet as a 10th crusade, no 
different from the crusades against the East and against Islam. Saudi Arabia finances 
the fuel that powers the 6th Fleet. The United States is leading the modern crusade. 
The United States drips with hatred against Islam and the East. It encourages the 
Israelis to destroy Islam, the East and its civilization, its revolutions and revival 
and then seize its wealth. The United States will help them one day to reach Mecca, 
Al-Madinah and even the oil of Saudi Arabia and the Gulf. One day, it will be in the 
hands of the Israelis on the orders of America. When America sees that its interests 
require that these places be in the hands of the Israelis and not the Arabs, it will 
let that happen. However, the United States has not decided on this yet. It prefers 
that they be in the hands of the Arabs until it decides when to let the Israelis take 
over. Then, the question depends on agreement between the United States and the 
Israelis on who controls Mecca and who controls the oil of the Gulf. 


Why does the United States help them? It is because it harbors hatred against the East 
and against Islam and the Arabs. Who finances the United States? It is Saudi Arabia. 
Saudi Arabia gives billions of dollars to the United States. The friends of the United 
States are the friends of Israel. On this basis, then, Saudi Arabia is a friend of 
Israel.

We as Muslims -- unless we are afraid or have been bribed -- cannot keep quiet about

This man, who embraced Islam thanks to us, has now been bought by Saudi Arabia in the

evening with Arab ambassadors accredited to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah.

which has been confirmed between the Government of Iraq and the Egyptian regime, blacklisting

brother secretary told the brother Arab ambassadors that this act might lead to the 

abolition of the Arab League, to the violation of its charter and to a disrespect for or 
abidance with any resolutions any Arab summit might pass in the future. 


The brother:'secretary asked the brother ambassadors to inform the Arab presidents and 
kings of this suggestion and said that the Jamahiriyah cautions anew against the gravity of 
keeping silent about this act and demands that it be exposed and denounced.
