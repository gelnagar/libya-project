LIBYA

Comment, Reportage on Developments in Chad 


Warning to France 
LD112019 Tripoli JANA in English 1820 GMT 11 Jul 83 


[Text] Paris, Shawwal |, July 11, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The secretary of the Libyan Arab People’s Bureau 
here affirmed that the despatch of French forces to Chad will be 
considered equivalent to a military action against the Jama- 
hiriyah which gives the right to intervene. 


He added at an interview with the French newspaper “La Croix” 
today that France is not alone in Chad “but the U.S., the Zionist 
entity, the regimes of Egypt, Sudan and Zaire all helping and 
interfering by sending forces, technicians, experts and aircraft of 
various description to help rebel Habre. The intervention by these 
foreign countries constitute a direct threat to the security and 
stability of the Jamahiriyah. However, the Jamahiriyah is con- 
cerned that its relations with France should remain normal”. The 
secretary pointed out the geographical, linguistic and historical 
link between the Libyan Arab and Chadian people which make 
the stability and security of the two countries to be interlinked. 
He wondered why the Jamahiriyah cannot have the right to 
safeguard its border. He said “this of course rules out the 
possibility of the presence of a political regime in Chad which is 
opposed to the Jamahiriyah and uses Chad territory as a base 
from where to launch direct operations against it”. 


Further on Warning 
NCI11709 Paris AFP in English 1659 GMT I1 Jul 83 


{Text} Paris, July 11 (AFP) — The despatch of French troops to 
Chad would be considered in Tripoli as “an act of war against 
Libya”, giving it the right to intervene in its turn, Said (Hafiana,) 
the Libyan ambassador to France, warned in an interview pub- 
lished here today. 


Mr (Hafiana) told the daily La Croix that France, the United 
States, Israel, Egypt, Sudan and Zaire were helping the Ndja- 
mena government of Hissein Habre against his ousted Libyan- 
backed predecessor Goukouni Oueddei.
