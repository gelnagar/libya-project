Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing note on the Libya-Chad conflict reports French and Chadian government air and supply activity at N'Djamena and Biltine airfields in Chad. It does not concern Tripoli or Libya directly.
• Two Dassault-Breguet Atlantic (probable ATL2) aircraft arrived at N'Djamena airfield.
• Military area order of battle at N'Djamena includes one Zairian Mirage III/5, eight C-160s, four SEPECAT Jaguars and four Mirage F-1s.
• At Biltine, one C-160 and at least nine vehicles/equipment were at the eastern end of the runway; a probable AML-90 scout car was noted east of the field.
• The note says French presence in Chad is further indicated in areas held by the Chadian government and French forces.
• 1983-08: French and Chadian government resupply and transshipment activity observed at N'Djamena and Biltine airfields in Chad.
