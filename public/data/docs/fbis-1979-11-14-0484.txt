STUDENTS ISSUE STATEMENT SUPPORTING IRANIAN STUDENTS 
LD101401 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 10 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] Jamahiriyah students have affirmed their stand with their brethren of Iran 
against the colonialist challenges led by the leader of world imperialism, the United 
States, represented in the moves of its fleets in the Indian Ocean bent on terrorizing 
the Iranian people in an attempt to abort their revolution. 


The Jamahiriyah students in a statement issued by the General Union of Jamahiriyah 
Students have expressed their full support for the demands of the Iranian students for 
the handing over of the shah. The statenent points out that the students of the 
Janahiriyah, on whose land freedom has been completely realized through the rise of the 
people's authority, stress their readiness together with the students of Iran to defend 
their Iranian popular revolution, which stems from the historic declaration announcing 
the rise of the people's authority in which the Libyan Arab people in all their groups 
Pledge to defend freedom anywhere in the world. 


The statement stresses that to abort the revolutionary students movement in Iran would 

be a direct blow to the progressive student force in the world. Free students in the 
world should shoulder their historic responsibilities in supporting the Iranian students. 
The statement emphasises that what the Iranian students are going through today is a real 
test for the progressive student force in the world and its ability to face the imperialist 
challenge and all forces hostile to the freedom of the peoples until it achieves its 

final victory against exploitation, repression and backwardness through the rise of the 
society of the era of the masses, which will be heralded by the student masses in the 
world.

AT-TURAYKI MEETS WITH JORDANIAN MINISTER OF STATE 
LD132144 Tripoli JANA in English 1656 GMT 13 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Novenber 13, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Foreign Secretary Dr. ‘Ali 
‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki this morning met with Hasan Ibrahim, the Jordanian minister of 
state for foreign affairs, currently visiting the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah., The meeting reviewed the latest developments in the Arab situation and 
coordination between the Jamahiriyah and Jordan as well as issues of mutual interest. 
They also discussed ways of reinforcing and promoting cooperation in all fields to reach 
the aspirations of the Arab peoples in both countries. 


ARAB SOLIDARITY SECRETARIAT TO CONTINUE IN EXISTENCE 
LD102034 Tripoli JANA in English 1646 GMT 19 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] Paris, November 10, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The preparatory secretariat of the 
world conference for solidarity with the Arab people and Palestinian cause has decided 
not to dissolve itself. The secretariat also held a meeting in which it discussed methods 
of expanding its administrative body to include other world regions, in response to a 
desire shown by many participating delegations in the Lisbon conference held between the 
2nd and 6th of Novenber. The Maltese Prime Minister Dom Mintoff told the preparatory 
secretariat that his country is prepared to host the secretariat's forthcoming meeting 
as a sign of Malta's solidarity with the Arab nation and Palestinian people's struggle 
against Zionism. It is worth noting that the preparatory secretariet has decided to 
send a delegation to the U.N. General Assembly to deliver resolutions made by the Lisbon 
conference to U.N. secretary, Dr. Kurt Waldheim, as the Palestinian issue is currently 
being debated in the General Assembly. 


AL-QADHDHAFI TSSUES DECISION ON RAISING TOWN ARMIES 
LD102216 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 10 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] The supreme commander of the armed forces has issued a decision laying down the 
bases for implementing the act of raising town armies. Here is the text of the decision: 


In implementation of the decisions and directives of the popular congresses and to attain 
the fulfillment of the slogan the armed people, the supreme commander of the arned forces 
has decided: 


Article 1. The raising of town armies is the true instrument leading to the emergence of 
the armed people. It is a right and a duty of every citizen--male and female--capable of 
bearing arms. 


Article 2. The raising of town armies will be based on the following groups: A. Those 
who are exempted from compulsory military service; B. Students of colleges, institutes 
and secondary schools, C. The rest of the people in accordance with the selection 
priorities to be defined by the commander in chief of the armed forces.

Article 3. Exemption from doing compulsory military service is canceled in the event 
of failing to join the units and formatious of town armies.
