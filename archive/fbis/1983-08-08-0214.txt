LIBYA

Air Force Issued Orders on AWACS Planes 
LD081506 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1443 GMT 8 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 8 Aug (JANA) — JANA has learned that orders 
have been given to the Libyan Arab Air Force to strike at the 
American AWACS planes whereever they are if the Libyan 
Arab Air Force is certain that these planes could have any effect 
on Libyan Arab territory. 


Statement on U.S. ‘Aggressive Intentions’ 
LDO071947 Tripoli JANA in English 1740 GMT 7 Aug 83 


[“Statement by Foreign Liaison Bureau on U.S. aggressive inten- 
tions against Libya” — JANA headline} 


[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 28, August 7, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY -— The People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau has issued a 
statement in which it exposed U.S. aggressive intentions and 
provocations against the Libyan Arab people. It described the 
false and unjust accusations by the U.S. Administration concern- 
ing a Libyan intervention in Chad as an insult to U.S. and 
international public opinion stressing that the U.S. Administra- 
tion is fully aware that Libya is far away from the Chadian 
conflict currently taking place in Chad. 


The people’s Foreign Liaison Bureau said in its statement that 
the U.S. Administration’s objective behind these accusations are: 


1. To cover up for U.S. direct intervention in Chad and turn this 
African country into a zone of influence and military bases. 


2. An excuse for aggression against the Libyan Arab people. 


This is clearly seen in U.S. Sixth Fleet war vessels’ moves 
including aircraft carriers across Libyan shores and their contin- 
ued provocations as well as America’s despatching of AWACS 
spying aircraft, fighter jets and thousands of soldiers equipped 
with various weapons to Egypt and Sudan which share the same 
borders with the Jamahiriyah allegedly to hold joint manoeuvres 
in the region. 


3. To cover up for the occupation of the Arab homeland under 
the guise of wide-scale U.S. manoeuvres 


Therefore the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah is 
appealing to international public opinion to shoulder its respon- 
sibility so as to put an end to this flagrant American threat 
against Libya. It also appeals to the U.N. Security Council to

Official Briefs USSR Ambassador on U.S. ‘Threats’ 
LDO51436 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 5 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 5 Aug (JANA) — Brother ‘Abd al-‘Ati al- 
‘Ubaydi, secretary for foreign liaison, received this morning the 
Soviet ambassador to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jama- 
hiriyah. During the meeting the ambassador was informed of 
developments in the current situation in light of the American 
Administration's escalation of its threats and provocations 
against the Libyan Arab people. The Soviet Union has been 
informed of the situation and of the explicit American threat to 
the Jamahiriyah, which endangers peace and security. 


Military Delegation Leaves for USSR 7 Aug 
LDO071454 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 7 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Aug (JANA) — JANA has learned that a 
Libyan military delegation left today for the Soviet Union. The 
delegation includes the Air Force chief of staff, the Air Defense 
chief of staff, the chief of staff of the Navy, and the director of 
military industrialization in the Jamahiriyah. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Cables Arabs on U.S. Military Maneuvers 
LDO52125 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2015 GMT 5 Aug 83 


{(“Text” of message sent by Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi to Arab 
kings and presidents — date not given] 


[text] The landing of U.S. forces in Egyptian, Somali, Sudanese, 
and Omani territory constitutes, frorn all aspects, occupation of 
Arab territory. Even if they withdraw or appear to withdraw in 
the future, it constitutes preparation for future American occu- 
pation of these Arab territories. This in fact is another in a series 
of occupations of the Arab homeland, which started in 1948 with 
the occupation of Palestine and continued in 1982 with the 
occupation of Lebanon. Furthermore, it constitutes a serious 
insult to the Arab nation’s dignity and a grave violation of its 
independence. 


We, brothers, have to align ourselves with the peoples of the Arab 
nation in Egypt, Sudan, Somalia, and Oman in resisting the 
invasion by the American forces of these Arab countries, made 
under the pretext of defending these countries. Who has granted 
America the right to defend these countries, and against whom?
