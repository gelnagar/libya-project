TITO MESSAGE DELIVERED TO COLONEL AL-QADHDHAFI 
LD300052Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 29 Mar 78 LD 


[Text] Dr 'Ali 'abd as-Salam at-Turayki, the foreign secretary, received this morning 

at the Foreign Secretariat in Tripoli the ambassador of Yugoslavia in the Jamahiriyah.

a message to brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi from President Tito of Yugoslavia.

afternoon the naval exercise in the Mediterranean and which was 7? .unched from the Tobruk
