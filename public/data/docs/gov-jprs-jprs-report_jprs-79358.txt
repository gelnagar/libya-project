JUSTICE SECRETARY HOLDS PRESS CONFERENCE IN KUWAIT 
LD191856 Tripoli JANA in English 1412 GMT 19 Oct 81 
[Quotation marks as received] 


[Text] Kuwait, 19 Oct (JANA)--Mr Muhammad al-Zuwayy, the secretary of the general 
people's committee for justice, said that Kuwait and the Jamahiriyah's viewpoints 
conceruing the current Arab situation and the latest developments in Egypt, are | 
identical. Both countries strongly condemn the U.S.-imperialist-Zionist attack on 
Egypt and to completely pull her out of the Arab-Zionist conflict. 


Mr al-Zuwayy added in his press conference held in Kuwait yesterday, that U.S. 
imperialism is now trying to use Egypt as its new strategic base in addition to its 
main base-~-Israel--and to strike at any progressive Arab tendency aimed at unifying 
the Arab nation and exerting national efforts to regain usurped rights. 


He added, "We are agreed on the necessity to help Egypt gain her natural position 
in the Arab nation. This means that the new president should give up all treacher- 
ous policies carried out previously by al-Sadat. These policies were aimed at 
isolating Egypt from the rest of the Arab world and putting her under direct U.S. 
imperialist domination. 


Mr al-Zuwayy said, "During my meeting with the Kuwaiti amir, we both agreed that 

the Numaryi-Begin meeting which took place after al-Sadat's funeral procession, 

came as a natural result to the Numayri policies which placed Sudan under imperialist 
domination just like what al-Sadat had cone." 


He added, “The amir of Kuwait responded positively to our view on U.S. provocations 
against the Libyan people which are aimed at silencing the progressive Arab voice." 


On the hysteric provocations of Numayri against Libya, Mr al-Zuwayy said: "There 
is no problem between the Jamahiriyah and Sudan, it is only Numayri's attempts to 
impose the murderer Habri on the Chadian people." 


He pointed out that the Jamahiriyah considered the Numayri-Begin meeting as high 
treason and that the Arab nation should strongly oppose it and deal with Numayri 
in the same way it had previously dealt with al~Sadat. 


He added: "We believe that as a result of al-Sadat's execution, Numayri was gripped 
by hysteria making him lose his senses."

He reminded his audience of Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's speech on October 5 in Sebha, 
one day before al-Sadat's death. In it, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi had said that Numayri 
is a sick man who is relying on a dead man. 


No wonder, he added, that Numayri has been stricken by hysteria: "He is trying all 
in his power to maintain his regime in Sudan. We challenge him in maintaining his 
tottering regime.” 


On the Aden treaty, Mr al-Zuwayy said the treaty is designed to enhance the ability 
of progressive forces in confronting the savage U.S. imperialist onslaught against 
the Arab nation, and Zionist infiltration into Africa. 


He said only Arab unity can guarantee the liquidation of the U.S. colonialist pres- 
ence and Zionism. This, he added, is what revolutionaries in the Jamahiriyah are 
Striving for and what the U.S. is seeking to abort by armed force...." 


Mr al-Zuwayy added: "It is our right to call on the new regime in Egypt to bury all 
previous treacherous agreements and treaties and to bring Egypt back to her natural 
position. It will then be its right to get our support to back it with all our 
resources.'' Our call on Egypt to retain her position is clear. We do not want 
Egypt to come to us as' a Trojan horse carrying imperialist-Zionist seeds. Egypt 
which we are inviting is the Egypt of Arabism and struggle and national liberation. 


Mr al-Zuwayy added that the colonialist achievement has resulted in the setting up 
of a pact which includes Egypt, Sudan, Somalia and Oman. This pact is aimed at the 
Arab nation and not just only at the steadfastness and confrontation front and the 
treaty of Aden as some people like to put it. 


He added that Aden treaty came to consolidate the capabilities of the progressive 
forces and to confront the U.S. imperialist attack on the Arab people and also to 
confront Zionist infiltration in Africa. 


He added, "The U.S. does not take anybody as a friend unless he becomes her agent." 


He went on to say that the only solution to all the dangers that are facing the 
Arab nation is Arab unity. This unity means the end of the U.S.-Zionist colonial- 
ist presence. This is what revolutionary in the Jamahiriyah are fighting for and 
the U.S. is trying to abort by force. 


He said: "We are a progressive revolutionary force that believes theie is no 
excuse for us not to confront those who oppose our nation and its expectations.... 
We are ready to die in defence of our nation's honour and its civilizational mes- 
sage that calls for the creation of a new world of peace and love, free of hatred 
and envy.... This is the message of Islam...." 


He added: "American is fighting us with all its political, economic and military 
capabilities.... It has recently started issuing threats to oil companies not to 
buy or market Libyan oil and to impose an economic embargo on our country. 


This proves that we are a source of worry for America and that we are an obstacle to 
its designs in the region.... Libyan oil is not, if used alone, an effective weapon 
against America. But Arab oil is an economic weapon in our possession that we can 
use in fighting America...."

Mr al-Zuwayy expressed regret that some Arab countries are fighting Libya by proxy 
for America and Israel. He said this is being done by the freezing of oil prices 
and creating a glut on the world oil market. 


On the attitude of the steadfastness and confrontation front vis-a-vis provocations 
against the Jamahiriyah, Mr al-—Zuwayy said any aggression on Libya is considered an 
attack on other steadfastness front countries and vice versa. He said he is certain 
that even other Arab countries will support the Jamahiriyah, because they know that 
aggression on Libya can only come from the U.S. or one of its hireling puppet re- 
gimes. 


On calls for inviting the Egypt [as received] to the forthcoming Arab summit con- 
ference, Mr al-Zuwayy said Egypt's invitation to the conference, with the country's 
continued adherence to previous commitments, is unacceptable, because it not only 
lets down the Arab nation but the Egyptian people also, who totally reject the Camp 
David accords.... 


He added: "We trust the Egyptian people who are fired by the spirit of the revolu- 
tion and positive action, in spite of efforts to belittle their capabilities.... 

We revolutionaries have good reason to be optimistic in placing our faith in the 
peops. Victory is always the ally of peoples...." 


Regarding unity between the Jamahiriyah and Syria, Mr al-Zuwayy saidi "This unity 
is going ahead as planned and there is complete coordination between us on various 
issues...." 


On Colone1]Mu' ammar al-Qadhdhafi's national call for exerting all Arab efforts, 

Mr al-Zuwayy said that "We are actively working to put an end to attrition between 
Morocco and the Polisario Front. The Jamahiriyah did not supply the front with wea- 
pons against Morocco. Our Moroccan brothers are aware of that as we have direct 
contacts with them." 


The aim of Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's national call to concentrate Arab national re- 
sources for our battle against the Zionists and the Americans, he added: 


"We have contacted our brothers in Iran and Iraq in order to put an end to their 
armed confrontation and to solve their differences through peaceful means. This 
war is a great loss for the Arab nation. We are now trying to stop this attrition 
and to reach an agreement and national reconciliation." 


Finally, Mr al-Zuwayy said that the return of Egypt to the Arab nation will put the 
fire out. This fire was first started by al-Sadat's capitulation. 


He finally expressed confidence that the Egyptian brothers are capable of reinte- 
gration with the Arab nation.
