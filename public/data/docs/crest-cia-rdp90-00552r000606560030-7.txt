Summary (AI): A Washington Post article reports that former Green Beret Eugene Tafoya was convicted of misdemeanor assault and conspiracy in the October 1980 shooting of Libyan dissident Faisal Zagallai in Fort Collins, Colorado, and acquitted of attempted murder. Prosecutors had alleged he was a hit man for the Libyan government working for former CIA agent Edwin P. Wilson.
• Conviction carries sentence of six months to two years and a fine of $500 to $5,000 for assault; conspiracy count punishable by up to six months and a $50 fine.
• Tafoya was released on $5,000 property bond until sentencing.
• Tafoya claimed he went to deliver a message from the CIA, which CIA agents denied; the Libyan allegedly pulled a gun from under a sofa cushion.
• Tafoya had earlier been linked to a May 1979 firebombing of a car belonging to a former Wilson business associate in Canada, per a tape.
• 1980-10-14: Tafoya shot Libyan dissident Faisal Zagallai at his apartment; Tafoya claimed self-defense.
• 1981-12-04: Tafoya convicted of misdemeanor assault and conspiracy; acquitted of attempted murder.
