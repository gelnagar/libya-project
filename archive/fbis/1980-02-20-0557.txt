LIBYA

DIPLOMATIC BREAK WITH FIVE ARAB COUNTRIES THREATENED 


LD201002 Kuwait KUNA in Arabic 0700 GMT 20 Feb 80 LD 


{Excerpt] Kuwait--The Kuwaiti paper AS-SIYASAH today reports that Libya has threatened 
to break off relations with five other Arab countries if they receive French President 
Valery Giscard d'Estaing, who intends to visit these countries next month. 


Quoting sources in Tripoli that it describes as senior, it says that the Libyan Foreign 
Ministry has sent messages to leaders of these five governments informing them that 
Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has decided to withdraw ambassadors and break off 
diplomatic relations with them if they receive President Giscard d'Estaing,. 


The paper says that the countries--Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, the UAE and Jordan--were 
surprised and stunned by Colonel ai-Qadhdhafi's decision, However, they have not yet 
given their replies,

JANA CITES 'GREEN MARCH! ON FRENCH IMPERIALISM 
LD191022 Tripoli JANA in English 0935 GMT 19 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Feb 19, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The newspaper "GREEN MARCH” yesterday 
published an article in which it dealt with the emergency meetings held by the Basic 
People's Congress throughout the Jamahiriyah and the repercussion of the flagrant French 
invasion of Tunisia in the wake of the Qafsah uprising, 


Under the title heading "The Ugly Face of France" the newspaper dealt with the recurring 
role unsuccessfully played once again by imperialist France after previous setbacks in 
Africa, 


The paper said France over the last ten years has carried out barbaric campaigns 
against the peoples of Congo and Shaba Province and in Central Africa and Chad, French 
imperialism has also committed the most heinous of crimes against the people of Saguia 
Hamra and Rio de Oro, using the most modern weapons of destruction, The paper added: 
"Today the same game is being played once again in staunch Tunisia people which rejects 
the ruling fascist clique and is struggling to overthrow it, thus ridding the people 
of oppression," [as received] 


The convening of people's congresses at this delicate stage affirms the mss solidarity 
between the Libyan Arab and Tunisian peoples in the quest for freedom, throughout the 
world, as stipulated by the historic document which declared the people's authority. 
(?A11) congresses were unanimous in their readiness to confront French imperialism 
through all means available, including war and the disruption of French interests 
throughout the world," 


The article expressed belief that the Tunisian people's revolution draws its strength 
from all those who believe in it, thus transforming the country's soil into flames for 
all those who have captured the land and rendered Tunisia a base for imperialist 
aggression against neighbouring countries, especially the Jamahiriyah. 


All we have which remains to be said is that the Tunisian revolutionary forces should 
exercise caution and vigilance, Let this revolution follow its path, with the help 
of the strong determination of the freedom-fighting people so that the Tunisian msses 
and the Arab nation might feel proud again and so that Tunisia may rid itself from the 
hireling regime and having to share borders with the invading French troops,

Dzur Departs 
1D152050 Tripoli JANA in English 1817 GMT 15 Feb 80 ID 


{Text] Tripoli, February 15, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Gen Martin Dzur, the Czech defence 
minister and member of the Central Committee of the Communist Party, left Tripoli today 
after a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah., He has been given an 
official send off at Tripoli international airport headed by Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis 
Jabir, general commander of the Libyan armed forces, a number of officers, the Czech 
ambassador to Jamahiriyah, the airport director and a representative of the military 
Protocol Department. 


In a statement to our correspondent Gen Dzur praised the great achievements realized by the 
grand Al-Fatah revolution in all fields. He also expressed his admiration for the Libyan 
armed forces standard both in arms and training. He praised the good ties between the 
friendly people of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and Czechoslovakia hoping 
for these relations to grow and visits to be exchanged.

MINISTER STRESSES ALGERIA'S READINESS TO UNITE WITH LIBYA 


LD191526 Tripoli JANA in English 1436 GMT 19 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Feb 19, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Mr Abu Allam Hammouda, the member of 
the Political Bureau and interior minister of Algeria left Tripoli international air- 
port last night after a short visit to the Jamahiriyah during which he reviewed the 
Algerian-Libyan Arab viewpoints. Mr Hammouda was seen off by Mr Muhammad Ali Jaddi, 
the secretary of the General People's Committee for Justice, the Algerian ambassador 
to the Jamahiriyah, his counterpart in Algiers and the head of the Protocol 
Department, 


Mr Abu Allam Ben Hammouda gave a statement to JANA's reporter, expressing his 
satisfaction at his visit to the Jamahiriyah, Mr Hammouda announced that the joint 
committee would meet soon to define stages for achieving the unity both countries are 
looking forward to and stressed Algeria's readiness to unite with Libya, Mr Hammouda 
disclosed that a top-level meeting would be held in the Jamahiriyah to consider ways 
of achieving unity, 


In his statement, Mr Hammouda said that viewpoints were identical in all the matters 
discussed, particularly on the situation in the Arab region, He also stressed that 
both parties have condemned the French-American invasion of the Arab region,
