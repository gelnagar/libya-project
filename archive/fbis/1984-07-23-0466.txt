LIBYA

U.S.-UK Base in Oman Threat to Africa, Asia 


LD212110 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
21 Jul 84 


[Unattributed commentary] 


[Text] The American-British agreement on developing their 
Masirah base aids the formation of Rapid Deployment Forces 
which threaten regions in Asia and Africa, and in a direct 
manner, the Arab homeland. We offer the following comment: 


Oman, situated in the east of the Arab homeland, occupies an 
important strategic position, something which attracts the atten- 
tion of the colonialist forces, primarily the United States. 
Through the stationing of naval forces in this region, the Arab 
Gulf and the Arabian Peninsula are being threatened, and many 
regions in the Pacific [as heard] Ocean are being controlled. The 
United States, exploiting the events of the Gulf War, has esca- 
lated its military presence in this important region of the Arab 
homeland by reinforcing its naval forces. The United States has 
allocated $62 million for developing the Masirah base in Oman, 
one of the main American bases on Arab soil. It is also preparing 
two air defense projects, due to be completed in 1988, at a cost 
of $400 million. 


At a time when liberated countries are striving to liquidate 
military bases and colonialist hegemony in its many forms, the 
agent Qabus has begun in an intensive way, especially since 1980, 
to extend U.S. military bases in Oman and to give the United 
States more control and powers, thus transforming Oman into an 
arsenal of advanced American weaponry and soldiers. Asa result 
of the growth of this weaponry, which includes land weapons such 
as M-60 tanks, 105 mm artillery and amphibious vehicles, Amer- 
ica has embarked on extending its military bases in Oman, 


particularly Masirah, so as to absorb these weapons, as well as 
radar stations and military airfields. 


Informed sources have disclosed that the United States and 
Britain have agreed to strengthen the military base in Diego 
Garcia for use by NATO forces, thus making it one of the most 
important bases in the Pacific [as heard] Ocean, serving U.S. 
military tyranny in this region. 


Masirah base was developed to include 15 fighter plane 
squadrons — 5 of which are British; 12 bomber squadrons — 4 
of which are British; 4 AWACS spy planes; the establishment of 
a radio communications base covering the Arabian Peninsula, 
Southeast Asia and East Africa is also planned. This base 
coordinates with a similar one being built in western Sudan. 


The extension of the Masirah base in Oman comes within the 
context of the formation of Rapid Deployment Forces. This 
American base is being developed in an Arab country to accept 
Pershing and Cruise nuclear missiles to be aimed at Arab and 
African countries. The Pentagon has allocated $30 billion for 
American-British military expansion in Oman, a huge sum, 
earmarked only for military purposes, which threatens world 
peace and security. Joint American-British expenditure does not 
conceal both sides’ intentions — to consolidate their military 
presence in the Arab Gulf and the Pacific [as heard] Ocean. 


The linking of the Masirah base in Oman with the American 
aggressive military base in Sudan confirms that America and its 
allies are preparing to threaten the Arab homeland and harm the 
interests of the Arab nation. America and Britain strive to 
achieve all that is contrary to the Arabs’ interests. This colonialist 
policy falls in the context of the policy of vacuum filling, adopted 
by American imperialism to impose its control and hegemony 
over the Arab homeland and the Third World. The intensive 
American-British presence at Masirah base in Oman confirms 
this. Masirah is a bridgehead for threatening aggression in Asia 
and Africa, foremost, of course, the Arab homeland. 


Egyptian Group’s Statement Criticizes Cairo Regime 
LD221649 Tripoli JANA in English 1452 GMT 22 Jul 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 24, July 22, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The Egyptian National Front in Jamahiriyah has 
released a statement on the 32nd anniversary of the rise of the 
July 23rd Revolution. The statement said: The liberation of 
Cairo on the morning of July 23rd, 1952 was the beginning of 
the only road which has no alternative, as the suffering of Jamal 
‘Abd an-Nasir during Al-Faluga siege showed the reality that 
the battle with Zionism, colonialism and reaction is one and 
inseparable. 


The Statement also said: If the July 23rd Revolution had 
succeeded in paving the difficult path amidst a weak Arab reality 
over which the shadow of colonialism allied with its reactionary 
and treacherous agents was hovering and to realise for Egypt and 
the Arabs a great deal of achievements, it was only natural to see 
all those whose interests collided with the objectives of July 23rd 
Revolution awaiting to undermine it. It is also normal to see that 
the revolution’s struggle against them had been, from its very 


beginning, a fierce and continuous one. If in the absence of ‘Abd 
an-Nasir, As-Sadat had been able to turn his back on the 
revolution, using all the implements of destruction against its 
achievements and opening the door to the Zionists, imperialists 
and reactionaries to gain from Egypt and its people what had 
been lost during ‘Abd an-Nasir’s era, the successors of the 
As-Sadatist regime in Egypt are still following the same path. 


It further confirmed that since the passing away of Jamal ‘Abd 
an-Nasir, Egypt has been following a downward and deteriorat- 
ing path and will not resume the uphill journey unless it returns 
to the 23rd July An-Nasirite nationalist and liberational rev- 
olutionary path and rids itself from the humiliating Camp David 
agreements. As-Sadat’s actions against Egypt were aimed 
against all Arab masses. The only option before these masses, 
their organizations and arenas of struggle is to unite so as to 
topple the Camp David path which is in the process of being 
passed in different Arab arenas under new names. Victory how- 
ever will inevitably be on the side of the masses.
