Summary (AI): A February 1982 cover memo to the Director of Central Intelligence transmitting a package of materials on Libyan activities abroad, including terrorism, anti-Qadhafi opposition groups, and Libyan activity in the Middle East, South Asia and Sub-Saharan Africa. It contains no specific facts about Tripoli.
• The package includes a rundown of Libyan terrorist activities, a chronology of reports on Libyan activities against U.S. officials, and a rundown of anti-Qadhafi opposition leaders and groups.
• The package includes an item on Soviet assistance to Libyan efforts to counter Western intelligence.
• A National Security Council meeting on Libya was cancelled in part because Task Force work was not ready for high-level review.
