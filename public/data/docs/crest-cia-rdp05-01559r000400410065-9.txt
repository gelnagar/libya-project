Summary (AI): A DIA cable reports that Qadhafi, speaking in Surt on 7 October 1986 for the anniversary of the end of Italian rule, urged mining the coast, fortifying the country, and threatened terrorism against the US if attacked again. It also records calls for Italian compensation and revenge if the issues are not resolved.
• Qadhafi called on people to mine the coast and fortify the country with trenches and barbed wire.
• He threatened to use terrorism against the US if Washington attacks Libya again, despite denying past use of it.
• He urged Italians to pressure their government over NATO bases used by the US against Libya and demanded Italian compensation for colonial-era crimes.
• The cable comments that the size and reaction of the audience is undetermined and that Qadhafi retains full control.
• 1986-10-07: Qadhafi speech commemorating the 16th anniversary of the end of Italian authority in Libya, calling for mining the coast, trenches and barbed wire, and threatening terrorism against the US if attacked again.

TEXT: 1. (U) LIBYAN LEADER COL QADHAFI, DURING A SPEECH 

COMMEMORATING THE 16TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE OFFICIAL END OF ITALIAN 

AUTHORITY. IN LIBYA, CALLED AGAIN FOR DEFENSIVE MEASURES AGAINST A US 

ATTACK AND THREATENED THE USE OF TERRORISM. 

2. (U) QADHAFI AND HIS DEPUTY, JALLUD, WERE IN SURT ON 7 OCTOBER 

TO MARK THE COMMEMORATION. THE LIBYAN LEADER CALLED ON HIS PEOPLE 

TO MINE THE COAST AND TO FORTIFY THE COUNTRY WITH TRENCHES AND 

BARBED WIRE. MOREOVER, HE THREATENED, DESPITE HIS DENIAL OF ITS USE 

IN THE PAST, TO EMPLOY TERRORISM AGAINST THE US IF WASHINGTON 

ATTACKS HIS COUNTRY AGAIN. 

3. (U) QADHAFI ALSO SPOKE OF AN INTERNATIONAL ARMY WITH HIMSELF AS 

ITS HEAD TO "FINISH OFF" THE US. HE SAID SUCH AN EFFORT IS 

NECESSARY BECAUSE EVEN MOSCOW WILL NOT CONFRONT WASHINGTON BECAUSE 

IT FEARS A WORLD WAR. QADHAFI CLAIMED THE US WANTS TO OCCUPY LIBYA 

AS A BASE AGAINST THE USSR. HE AGAIN CALLED ON ROME TO PAY 

COMPENSATION FOR "CRIMES" DURING THE COLONIAL ERA, CITING GERMANY'S 

PAYMENTS TO ISRAEL AFTER THE NAZI PERIOD AS A PRECEDENT. QADHAFI 

URGED ITALIANS TO TAKE THEIR GOVERNMENT TO TASK FOR ALLOWING NATO
