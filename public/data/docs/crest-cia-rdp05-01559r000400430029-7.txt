[Text] Tripoli, 30 Apr (JANA)—The People’s Commit- 
tee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison issued a 
statement today about the maneuvers being carried out 
by NATO in the Mediterranean which said: 

NATO is carrying out military maneuvers in the Medi- 
terranean until 18 May 1988. With regard to this, the 
People’s Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison stressed the following: 

These maneuvers, taking place in one of the most sensi- 
tive areas of the world, and exactly opposite the coasts of 
a number of nonaligned countries, stress the provocative 
intentions of the participating countries toward the 
peoples of the region and is a straightforward expression 
of their defiance of the will of the international commu- 
nity and their disregard for the right of the peoples of the 
region to live in peace and security. 

These maneuvers are a direct threat to the security, inde- 
pendence, and sovereignty of the countries of the region, 
and expose to danger air and maritime navigation and the 
lanes of civilian passengers and international trade. They 
are also an alarming indication of events which cannot be 
controlled and which can cause disastrous consequences to 
international peace and security. 

The Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, while 
recalling United Nations and nonaligned resolutions 
with regard to security and peace in the Mediterranean, 
would like to point in particular to the fact that the 
eighth conference of the Nonaligned Movement in 
Harare clearly condemned such military maneuvers in 
the Mediterranean and any other aspects of muscle- 
flexing because they lead to extremely dangerous events 
and threaten the sovereignty and independence of the 
nonaligned countries in the region. This position was

Comments on Gulf War 
LD292139 Tripoli Television Service in Arabic 
2030 GMT 29 Apr 88 

[Text] The Paris-based radio station Al-Sharq had an 
interview with the brother leader of the revolution in 
which he spoke about a number of pan-Arab and inter- 
national issues. In his interview the brother leader of the 
revolution touched upon the Iraq-Iran war and its inter- 
actions in the Arabian Gulf region, stressing the Great 
Jamahiriyah’s clear stand that: We have been against the 
war since its beginning, and our efforts to stop it are 
continuous. We consider it an aimless war that serves 
only imperialism and Zionism, and we consider it stupid 
to continue with it. 

The brother leader explained that this is our stand: 
Namely, to work to stop this war without allowing 
ourselves to fight either of the two warring sides. This is 
because one is Muslim and revolutionary and the other is 
Muslim and Arab, and there is no question of fighting 
either side. However, there is a possibility for mediation 
between them, and this is what the Jamahiriyah is doing. 
She will continue her efforts until the war ends, God 
willing.

Makes May Day Statement 
LDOI 0902 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0750 GMT 
I May 88 

[Text] Tripoli, 1 May (JANA}—On May Day, the 
brother leader of the revolution made the following 
statement to JANA: 

The international day of deceit of the world’s wage 
earners, 1 May, comes this year with millions of wage 
earners still being exploited. They have not been freed 
from the slavery of earning salaries, which are used 
against them by the rulers and factory owners to steal 
their efforts and sweat. 

The emancipation of millions of wage earners in the 
world lies in the abolition of salaries and the freedom of 
workers from slavery. Workers should turn this day, a 
day of deceit, into a day of struggle against this lie and 
into a confrontation against the forces of exploitation by 
marching on factories, production sectors, and adminis- 
trations. They should claim their whole share of produc- 
tion and abolish wages forever. 

The current fallacy in the world these days indicates that 
the freedom of the workers is incomplete. In fact, it is 
nonexistent. It can be achieved only if millions are freed 
from slavery to wages, [words indistinct}. If workers 
agree among themselves, then the factory owners will 
have to accept the fait accompli, and that will be the end 
of them. 

The real day for workers is | September 1978, when 
wage earners of the soil of the Jamahiriyah were freed. 
They destroyed the dens of exploitation everywhere; 
they took control of factories and other production 
sectors; they became partners. 

The Green Book, the third international theory, depicts 
the unique way to liberate mankind from oppression and 
exploitation and paves the way for the final emancipa- 
tion of all the world’s workers. Therefore, workers 
should seek advice from the Green Book to gain their 
freedom and put an end to slavery to wages. 

Wage earners, no matter how their salaries improve, are 
in fact a category of slaves. The wage earner is a slave to 
the master who hires him. His slavery is judged accord- 
ing to his type of job, in return for [word indistinct] from 
the factory owner. 

All the speeches and statements delivered during such a 
day do not incite the workers to free themselves from 
slavery to wages. These speeches and statements deeply 
encourage exploitation and the workers should not listen 
to them at all.
