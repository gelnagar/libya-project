[Text] Tripoli, 1 Apr(JANA)— At noon today the brother leader 
of the revolution met the secretaries of the municipalities’ peo- 
ple’s committees. The brother leader, in a comprehensive guiding 
speech, confirmed that the secretaries of the municipalities peo- 
ple’s committees should work night and day for the implementa- 
tion of the resolutions of the basic people’s conferences. The 
brother leader warned the secretaries that all the executive 
measures being taken by the people’s committees would be illegal 
and totally. void if they are not in accordance to the resolutions 
of the conferences. 

The brother leader added that the people’s committees — if they 
see that a matter needs implementation and the conferences has 
not adopted any resolutions concerning it — should hasten to 
submit it to the basic people’s conferences. The brother leader 
confirmed that the secretaries’ work should be coordinated with 
their committees, and that any attempt to bypass a committee 
would costitutue a violation of the people’s authority. 

The brother leader reviewed developments in the oil market and 
recalled that he has warned repeatedly against the danger of 
reliance on oil alone. He explained that we should use the 
abundant funds made available by oil to build a productive 
society free from reliance on one source of income. He added that 
although we have gone a long way in this field, we still need to 
redouble our efforts. The leader stressed the need for us all to 
turn to production and to understand that the dinar does not 
reproduce; rather, those that reproduce and regenerate them-
