Summary (AI): A January 1986 FBIS-style transcript of a Qadhdhafi speech at the Basic People's Congress of Shari' al-Zawiyah in Tripoli, calling for mobilization of air force, navy and air defence conscripts and an economic battle against the United States after U.S. sanctions. It mentions the Gulf of Sidra confrontation and his labelling of Libyan exiles as "stray dogs" collaborating with U.S. intelligence.
• Qadhdhafi ordered that conscripts in the Air Force, naval forces and air defences be ready for a sea or air battle against the U.S.
• He said the U.S. president's January 1986 presidential decisions declared a national emergency citing the Libyan danger.
• He called on Libyans to pay utility bills, deposit salaries in banks and join mobilization as an economic weapon against America.
• He proposed rotating groups of 50,000 to 1 million Libyans through training in holding weapons.
• He said 500 girl students directed to military colleges had failed to join.
