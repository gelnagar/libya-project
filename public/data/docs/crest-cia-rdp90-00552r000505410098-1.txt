No decisions had been reached.
• Policy review of economic and political measures against Libya had been under way since summer 1981.
• Measures under study included an embargo on Libyan oil imports and a ban on American travel to Libya.
• The State Department said it had strong evidence Qaddafi had been planning the murder of American officials at home and overseas.
• Libyan oil imports to the U.S. had reportedly dropped to about 600,000 barrels a day.
• Libya was withdrawing troops from Chad at the time, which Haig cited as a reason for deferring a decision.
• 1980: Mob burned the American Embassy; American diplomats left Tripoli, the capital.
• 1981-05: Libyan diplomats were expelled from the United States.
