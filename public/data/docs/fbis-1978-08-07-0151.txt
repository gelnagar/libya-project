ARABS MUST BE AWARE OF THREAT CAUSED BY VANCE VISIT 
LDO42130Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 4 Aug 78 LD 
{Unattributed commentary] 
{Excerpt] The U.S, secretary of state is due to begin his visit to the region today with 
a visit to the Zionist enemy. Then he is to visit some Arab capitals. Observers of de- 
velopments in our Arab region may ask what is new to be brought to the region by the U,S. 
secretary of state. 
In view of the numerous visits to the region by U.S, officials without registering a single 


step forward along the road toward a solution even from a purely U.S, standpoint, observers 
feel inclined to discount any importance which may be attached to such visits or moves.

But since states, and especially big powers, believe time is too precious to waste, we 
once again wonder about the reasons and motives of these U.S. moves, Are they merely to 
cool down tempers or to sort out some confidential matters that are too difficult for 
ordinary people to understand? 


It is said that it is the result that counts. But what are the renal effective results 
secured by the Arab region from the total of U.S. moves? We do not want at present to 
enumerate the pitiful results reaped by Egyptian President Anwar as-Sadat from his U.S. 
policy, but we wonder about the fate of the Arab region which has been battered in an 
unprecedented manner by U.S. moves. 


Let us merely cast a glance at the latest U.S. attitude toward the region. We see, for 
example, that the United States staged three successive steps which involved antagonism 
and evil for the Arab nation. In this it does not discriminate between friend and foe, 


For instance, we see that it has advised its nationals residing in Lebanon to leave at 

once, This is a clear U.S. sign suggesting that the civil war in Lebanon will continue 

to blaze. This has led to the departure of many Lebanese from their own country to various 
parts of the world, By taking this step the United States has given everyone the impression 
of its actual involvement in the Lebanese tragedy--a tragedy which only serves the in- 
terests of the enemies of the Arabs, especially the Zionist enemy. 


The second step by the United States is reflected in its demonstration with its friend 
President as-Sadat for saying that it would be difficult for him to continue the negotia- 
tions with Israel if Israel did not offer anything new which would encourage him to do so, 
This U.S. displeasure following the mere expression of a statement is disturbing and leads 
one to wonder, This is at a time when the U.S. administration has maintained silence about 
serious developments in the region, and this exposes its absolute prejudice against the 
Arabs, including those loyal to it. 


The third step is represented in a decision taken last night by the U.S. Congress suspending 
financial aid, which is not more than $100 million, to Syria, Evidently the reason was 
Syria's attitude in Lebanon, The suspension of the aid is meant as a punishment for this 
attitude. Furthermore, an American official has said that Syria is acting in Lebanon in- 
dependently of the U.S. position. 


It is significant that these three steps precede the arrival of the secretary of state in 
the region by a few days. Are they meant as an act of brandishing the American big stick 
once again? 


The Arabs have gone to the farthest limit in moderation; so much so that they have become 
proverbial for this throughout the world, after a time when they used to be described as 
extremists, But no one has come a step forward to reward the Arabs for what they call 

their moderation. This is at a time when the Zionist enemy reaches the height of ext- 
remism without anyone in the world brandishing a stick in his face or addressing a criticism 
or warning to him, 


Therefore the question, as the facts show, is not so much one of moderation or extremicm 

as it is of the existence of an international collusion against the Arabs under the leader- 
ship of the United States, Its aim is to force them to accept colonialist settlement and 
its geopolitical expansionism and to hurl the Arabs into a new enslavement of bigger dimen- 
sions than the old enslavement,

AT-TURAYKI INTERVIEW WARNS OF AMERICAN THREATS 


LpO6111¢CY Tripoli JANA in English 0938 GMT 6 Aug 78 LD 


[Text] “uwait, August 6, JANA--The foreign secretary, Dr. 'Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, 
stressed that all the Arabs who attended the Belgrade conference agree that the policy 
adopted by the Egyptian regime vis-a-vis the Zionist enemy is mistaken, and that the only 
way to end the Arab disputes is to revoke the Egyptian president's initiative and to break 
any liaison with the Zionist enemy. 


In an interview with the Kuwaiti newspaper AL-QABAS published today, Dr. at-Turayki said 
that tne Jamahiriyah deems that there is no hope of solving the Arab problems through 
negotiations with the enemy neither at a Geneva conference nor at any other conference, 
and that the only solution is to struggle against the usurper enemy. 


Dr. at-Turayki declared that the real danger confronting the Arab Gulf region lies in the 
American threats and that any attitude ignoring this fact would merely distract the states 
of this region from their cause of destiny and would create illusions which have no 
foundation. In his interview »ublished in AL-QABAS, Dr. at-Turayki stressed that the 
Jamahiriyah stands for Arab nationalism and the independence of the Arab Gulf. He addec 
"we should not be blind to the real danger threatening peace and security in the Arab 
Gulf."
