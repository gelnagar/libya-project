LIAISON BUREAU DEMANDS U.S. END MIDEAST ACTIONS 


LD151906 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1810 GMT 15 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 15 (JANA) -- The Popular Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison today used the following statement: 


16 Oct 81 ) NORTH AFRICA 


The United States is trying to complete its occupation and domination of Egypt with a 
view to turning the country into a center for influencing the area and serving American 
Strategy and its imperialist objectives, caring nothing about the area's security and 
independence. The United States is doing this to serve its aggressive policy and to 
drag the area into the arena of international conflict in a manner which would serve 
American interests. 


The United States knows better than anyone else that there was no threat to either 
Egypt or Sudan from Libya and that there were no Libyan military concentrations on 
the borders of the two countries. However, the United States is seeking to create 
justifications for launching an aggression by making these countries believe that 
this fabricated and baseless danger exists. The real danger lies in America's over- 
whelming desire to dominate these two countries to serve its imperialist strategy and 
objectives. 


These imperialist strategies and objectives are epitomized by America flexing its 
muscles in the Mediterranean and close to the Arab shores, by the deployment of the 
Rapid Deployment Forces and the concentration of its forces in Egypt under the pretext 
of joint maneuvers, and finally by the dispatch of the AWACS. spy planes to Egypt. 


All of these things and their consequences constitute a challenge and a provocation 
to the people of the area and a true threat to their security and safety. 


In view of this, the Popular Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison 
wishes to aiert Arab public opinion to the gravity of the American game and warns the 
United States of the consequences of its imperialist policy in the area. While 
demanding that the United States lift its hands from the area and leave it alone to 
solve its own problems by itself in accordance with the will of the area's people, 
the Popular Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison makes the following 
demands: 


. Cancellation of the aggressive provocative maneuvers; 


1 
2. Withdrawal of the AWACS spy aircraft; 
3. Ending the 6th Fleet's muscle flexing and keeping it away from Arab shores. 


MISSION AT_UN DENIES ANY ATTACK ON SUDAN 
LD160836 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 16 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 Oct (JANA) -- The Jamahiriyah's mission at the United Nations 

has declared that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah categorically rejects 
reactionary imperialist allegations that the Jamahiriyah has attacked the Sudan or 
that it has threatened to carry out such an attack. 


In a statement issued in New York yesterday the mission explained that the U.S. 
information media and the U.S. Administration have organized a campaign of hatred against 
the Jamahiriyah based on unfounded allegations which are aimed at using these allegations 
to justify the dispatch of weapons to Egypt and Sudan and to consolidate the U.S. 
presence in the region, and consequently to encourage Egypt and Sudan to attack the 
Jamahiriyah and impose on it the treaty of capitulation. 


The mission appealed to U.S. public opinion to be aware of the U.S. plans to interfere in 
the internal affairs of Libya and its attempt to impose hegemony on the Arab region. 


GREEK PAPER CITES AL-QADHDHAFI ON U.S. BASES 


NC151555 Athens AKROPOLIS in Greek 15 Oct 81 p 10 


[Text] Yesterday Al-Qadhdhafi reiterated his threat to strike at U.S. bases in Greece -- 
and in any other Mediterranean NATO country -- if he is subjected to an attack which -- 
he alleges -- the Americans are preparing in collaboration with the Egyptian and the 
Sudanese. 


V. 16 Oct 81 Q 6 NORTH AFRICA 


The threatening new warning by Al-Qadhdhafi is incorporated in a statement issued by the 
Libyan Embassy in Athens (the "People's Bureau of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah.") This statement clearly reiterates Al-Qadhdhafi's threat "to destroy all the 
bases which will collaborate with America or with any other country in an attack against 
Libya." In an effort to leave no doubt that the threat is directed at Greece and specifi- 
cally at Kriti, the Libyan statement mentions that the Libyan leader has already "warned" 
that he will strike. 


Yesterday's Libyan provocation, as expressed verbatim in the statement from the Libyan 
Embassy is as follows: 


"You may have observed on the basis of developments that both the statements of the Egyptian 
and Sudanese officials are well as those by the Western information media headed by the 
United States of America -- made after As-Sadat's murder and ater the disturbances which 
broke out in Asyut and Alexandria -- and in the midst of international tension are aimed 

at justifying some forthcoming military operation against the Jamahiriyah. 


"The people's bureau also draws attention to the premeditated intent of the United States 
and its leaders, who have lost all sense of logic after As-Sadat's death, to launch an 
attack against the Jamahiriyah. They have already started preparations for this attack in 
the Mediterranean with suspicious moves by the U.S. 6th Fleet with the assistance of the 
Egyptian Navy, creating provocations along the shores of the Jamahiriyah and within its 
territorial waters or through concentrations of Egyptian and Sudanese Armed Forces along 
the frontiers of the Jamahiriyah so that the invasion may take place on a number of fronts. 
It must be noted that the Egyptian forces have been transferred from the Egyptian frontiers 
with Israel. In addition, large quantities of ammunition and hundreds of experts are 
arriving in Egypt and Sudan to prepare the ground for the invasion. 


"The Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah is certain that the use of U.S. bases in NATO countries in 
the Mediterranean, within the framework of this planned aggression, will expose these 
countries to defensive counteraction by the Jamahiriyah. 


"The leader of the Jamahiriyah, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, has already warned that in 
self-defense Libya will be forced to destroy all the bases which will take part, together 
with America or any other country, in an attack against the Jamahiriyah. 


"The people's bureau, in recalling the warning of the leader of Libya, is certain that the 

friendly Greek people and the Greek information media, which have never ceased to staunchly 
oppose the plots which are hatched in secret, will not, once again, hesitate to dynamically 
neutralize the planned aggression before the area is set on fire through a third world war."

Decides on Executive Program 
LD151636 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1450 GMT 15 Oct 81 
[Text] Tripoli, 15 Oct (JANA) -- The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 


decided at its emergency meeting which ended in Tripoli this morning on an executive program 
to consolidate the struggle of the Egyptian people: 


I. Effective and full support on all fronts for the Egyptian people's struggle which -- from 
the urgent tactical aspect -- aims at overthrowing the agent As-Sadat - Mubarak regime. This 
requires: 


A. The Arab liberation movement and the pan-Arab Steadfastness and Confrontation Front to 
adopt this end politically, and support for it in its national, Pan-Arab and international 
activities as well as standing up to any imperialists-Zionists-reactionary maneuvers which 
seek to obliterate or surround it. 


B. The information media of the Arab liberation movement and the Pan-Arab Steadfastness 
and Confrontation Front should enable the Egyptian national information activity to 
cooperate with the pan-Arab information in bringing down the agent As-Sadat-Mubarak regime 
as well as raising the degree of awareness of the Egyptian popular movement and its 
organization in a manner that enables it to overthrow the regime and set up a democratic 
and national regime in its place. In this respect: 


1. Providing radio which expresses the voice of the Egyptian people and which is administered 
by the leadership ofthe Egyptian national information has become a pressing matter. 
Responsibilities for this must be shouldered by member states of the Pan-Arab Steadfastness 
and Confrontation Front. 


2. Furthermore the publication of an Egyptian national paper has become an equally urgent 
matter. 


II. Full, effective and practical support for the Egyptian national movement:

Egypt and in particular the Unionist Progressive National Grouping Party as well as the

activities. 


C. Giving the opportunity to its units abroad to act among the Egyptian communities
