Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery note from June 1983 reports four BTR-60PB armored personnel carriers and driver training by a probable dissident force at a facility near Sebha, interpreted as Libyan support for Chadian or other dissident forces. The location is in southern Libya, far from Tripoli.
• First observation of BTR-60PB armored personnel carriers at this dissident training facility; earlier vehicles seen there were mainly Landrovers and jeeps.
• Training suggests a Libyan intention to provide more sophisticated military equipment to Chadian or other dissident forces.
• Coordinates given as 27-01-30N 014-30-00E.
• 1983-06-01: Imagery shows two BTR-60PB APCs used for driver training by probable dissidents, with a group of personnel watching; four more BTR-60PB were parked within the facility.
