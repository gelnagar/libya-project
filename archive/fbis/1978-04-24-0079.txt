AL-QADHDHAFI: SHIPMENT FOR PALESTINIANS EXPLODES 


JN222048y Baghdad Voice of Palestine in Arabic 1830 GMT 22 Apr 78 JN 


[Excerpt] Libyan President Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has announced that a plane loaded with 
Libyan arms bound for the Palestinian revolution was made to explode in the air while 
passing over Syrian airspace. In an exclusive interview with the magazine AL-WATAN 
AL-'ARABI, Al-Qadhdhafi did not reveal who was responsible for the explosion but said 
that the plane was not Libyan and that it was loaded with arms to aid the Palestinian 
revolution during the recent Zionist aggression against Arab Lebanon. 


V. 24 Apr 78 NORTH AFRICA 


SUDAN OFFICIAL ARRIVES FOR TALKS ON CHAD 


LD232250Y Tripoli Voice of the Arabs in Arabic 2115 GMT 23 Apr 78 LD 


[Excerpts] 'Abu al-Qasim Muhammad Ibrahim, the Sudanese first vice president, arrived 
at Tripoli International Airport this evening. Maj Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud was at 
the head of the official reception. 


In a statement to JANA 'Abu al-Qasim Muhammad Ibrahim said: I am visiting Tripoli today 
to meet the brothers in Libya in order to implement the first practical step to realize 
the national reconciliation in Chad. This step was agreed upon at the meeting in Sabha 
and Benghazi. He said he would discuss with Libyan officials the current Arab situation 
and action to confront the challenges facing the Arab nation. 


SAO TOME AND PRINCIPE FOREIGN MINISTER ARRIVES 
LD192316Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 19 Apr 78 LD 


{Summary] The foreign minister of SaO Tome and Principe arrived in fripoli tonight.

Principe to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi. 


Received by Foreign Minister 
LD211530Y Tripoli JANs in English 1415 GMT 21 Apr 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 April, JANA--Dr 'Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, the foreign secretary 
and the chairman of the current session of the (OAU) Foreign Ministers Council, yesterday 
received Mr. Leonel Mario d'Alva, the foreign minister of Sao Tome, currently visiting 
the dJamahiriyah. 


Both sides reviewed current developments in Sao Tome following the recent incursion by 
the mercenaries on Sao Tome and Principe. Dr at-Turayki confirmed the vested interests 
of the Organization of African Unity (OAU) in counteracting the mercenaries in supporting 
the African peoples of Sag Tome and Principe.
