Summary (AI): A 1981 newspaper article reports that federal investigators concluded Libya "loaned" Billy Carter $200,000 plus $20,000 for expenses, and that the NSA reportedly knew of a further $200,000 payment. It also covers Carter's dealings with CIA renegades Frank Terpil and Edwin Wilson. Nothing here concerns places in Tripoli.
• Investigators determined the Libyans 'loaned' Billy Carter $200,000 and gave him $20,000 more for expenses tied to a Libyan delegation's US visit.
• The article alleges the NSA knew in May 1980 of an additional $200,000 Libyan payment to Carter, withheld on national security grounds.
• The ban on US military exports to Libya was never lifted, per the article.
• Terpil and Carter allegedly discussed machine guns and transport aircraft for Libya; lobbying for a foreign power without registration is illegal.
• 1978: Billy Carter visits Libya (first visit)
• 1979: Billy Carter's second Libya visit; meets Frank Terpil at a dinner and is photographed with him watching a parade
• 1980-01: Billy Carter tells Justice Department official Joel Lisker and an FBI agent that Terpil had contacted him about buying machine guns for Libya
