AUTONOMY DELEGATION DEPARTS; BURG INTERVIEWED 
TA181205 Jerusalem Domestic Service in Hebrew 1105 GMT 18 Dec 79 TA 


[Text] The autonomy delegation is now leaving forGiza, which is near Cairo where its 
members will meet with the heads of the Egyptian and U.S. delegations. This will 

be a contimation of the talks Dr Burg and Ambassador Linowitz held in Cairo about a 
week ago. Our correspondent Dan Eshel reports from Ben-Gurion Airport. 


{Eshel--live] The head of the Israeli delegation, Dr Yosef Burg, emerged first. He 
was the only one who approached the reporters, [begin recording] 


[Burg] We have beforeus the mimtes with the working team's recommendations about the 
elections. In my opinion this has to be approved, and some of the other disputed points 
should be clarified. We will also have to begin clarifications on how we will set up 

the administrative council of the autonomy. This is not a simple undertaking and certainly 
cannot becompleted in one session, but I hope we will be able to make progress. 


[Question] Dr Burg, how do you regard Egyptian Prime Minister Mustafa Khalil's statement 
that Jerusalem is a part of the West Bank? 


[Answer] There is nothing new about this, since at Camp David, too, the Egyptians said 
what they had to say and we said what we had to say. This is one of the things on which 
we definitely differ. I hope I will have the opportunity to have personal talks and 

also clarify the meaning of some of the statements made here last week. 


[Question] Dr Burg, can one assume that in these deliberations you will already begin 
shaping the model of the administrative council? 


[Answer] We would like to build together what we call a model. 
[Question] How is it to operate? 


[Answer] We think that if we argue over the matters of principle, we are rather far away 
(from reaching on agreement], but we believe that if we discuss the practical matters, 
we can be close. [end recording] 


GHALI REQUESTS LIAISON OFFICES IN TERRITORIES 
TA182026 Jerusalem Domestic Service in Hebrew 2000 GMT 18 Dec 79 TA 


{Text] Egyptian Minister of State Butrus Ghali asked for Israel's agreement today to 
open Egyptian liaison offices in Judaea, Samaria and the Gaza Strip following the 
establishment of diplomatic relations between Israel and Egypt in 2 months. Ghali 

made the request during a 2-hour meeting in Cairo with the director of the prime minister's 
office, Eliyahu Ben-Elisar, and with Foreign Ministry Director General Yosef Ciechanover. 
According to him, these liaison offices will for the first time allow direct contact 
between Egypt and the Palestinians. These liaison offices would play an important role 

in persuading the Palestinians to join the negotiations on the autonomy. The Israeli 
delegation promised to pass the request on to the Israeli Government, Ghali said to 
correspondents in Cairo.
