LIBYA

KHARTOUM REPORTS SAY TANKS ROAMING STREETS 


LD0O11756 Tripoli JANA in English 1636 GMT 1 Feb 82 


[Text] Khartoum, 1 Feb (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY) -- Urgent reports from Khartoum said that 
tanks are roaming the streets of most of the Sudanese cities and that the masses once again 
moved into the radio and television buildings in Omdurman and Atbarah headquarters and 
occupied the premises for several hours. 


The Sudanese masses also marched into the railway stations, thus preventing trains from 
running in service of the regime's police forces. 


The number of people arrested during these incidents rose to 4,000 in addition to those 
arrested earlier. 


Political analysts confirm a rapid sequence of events against the Numayri regime and the 
popular revolution is gathering momentum despite the represssive measures of the shaking 
regime. The Sudanese masses went out to the streets chanting slogans reflecting their 
aspirations in the authority, wealth and arms to be in the hands of the people. 


More on Demonstrations 
LD011804 Tripoli JANA in English 1639 GMT 1 Feb 82 
[Text] Athens, 1 Feb (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY) -- According to passengers arriving here 


from Sudan yesterday, Khartoum is living under a state of extreme tension with military 
check points set up at all the approaches to the capital. 


Our correspondent was told that all trade unions are meeting constantly and they are expected 


within the next two days to ask all their members to stage a massive strike against the 
dictatorial authorities. 


On the other hand, demonstrations continue to be widespread in Al-Jazirah Province and its 
capital Wad Madani. The people of this province are demanding the resignation of Numayri 
as a condition for stopping the demonstrations. 


Anti-Numayri rallies continue in the south of Sudan as well and the authorities appear to 
have lost control.

GREEK AMBASSADORIAL APPOINTMENT UNACCEPTABLE 


NC311028 Athens I KATHIMERINI in Greek 30 Jan 82 p 1 


[Text] Libya will not accept the appointment of Manos Kafetzopoulos, a PASOK [Panhellenic 
Socialist Movement] cadre, as Greek ambassador to Tripoli instead of a career diplomat. 
According to reports, a demarche already has been made to the Greek Government. 


Libya maintains that the appointment of a new diplomat will downgrade the Greek diplomatic 
representation at the Jamahiriyah. The reports further say that Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's 
government has asked that Ambassador Petrounakos [as published] not be transferred from 
Tripoli. 


It appears the Greek Government's relations with Libya are not as warm as believed. 
Libya appears to be dissatisfied with the Arab policy followed by the PASOK government, 
which in practice does not consider the tense differences among the Arab states. 


MEDITERRANEAN GROUP OFFICIAL ON U.S. PRESENCE 


NC291730 Athens AKROPOLIS in Greek 29 Jan 82 p 8 


[Text] "We Libyans have the right to self-defense in case of attack. For this reason, 
we can use the about 10 missiles installed in our country against all the bases -- both 
in Greece and Italy -- from where the 6th Fleet comes." This was stressed in a press 
interview yesterday by Ahmad ash-Shahatah, sccretary general of the Permanent Secretariat 
of Mediterranean Socialist Parties. 


In another part of his talk, Ash-Shahatah said "many planes involved in incidents at the 
Libyan borders had taken off from NATO bases," hinting at Greece. 


Furthermore, referring to the Cyprus issue, Ash-Shahatah said that the "Permanent 
Secretariat will undertake an initiative for a dialogue between the interested parties 

so that a solution can be found." However, he avoided describing the Turkish attack on 
Cyprus as an invasion, something that is contrary to PASOK [Panhettenic Socialist Movement ] 
positions. It should be noted that PASOK has been a founding member of the secretariat 
since 1967. 


In connection with the disputes between Turkey and Greece, Ash-Shahatah said: "The United 
Steres is plotting a war between the two countries." 


"PASOK, continued Ash-Shahatah, is considered by the Americans to be a hostile power in 
the Mediterranean and, for this reason, the situation will be forced with the result 
that a war will take place." 


Ash-Shahatah fired "volleys" against the U.S. policy, claiming that it oppresses people 
with dictatorships.

However, in reply to a question by AKROPOLIS about his opinion on the situation in Poland 
and the Jaruzelski regime, he said: "The Polish issue has not been fully clarified. It

Ash-Shahatah supported the Soviet viewpoint on the issue of the establishment of the two
