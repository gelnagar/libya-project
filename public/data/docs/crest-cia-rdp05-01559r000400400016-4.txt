Summary (AI): An FBIS London translation (excerpts, part 4 of 5) of Qadhafi's address to the General People's Congress, in which he criticizes local People's Committees, imported foreign labour and goods, and the General People's Committee's accountability. It contains no places, units or facilities in or near Tripoli.
• Qadhafi said the General People's Committee would stand accused until proven innocent and that, if proven guilty, it would not last more than 7 months.
• Fifty members from municipal people's conferences and committees were to oversee the General People's Committee under the General People's Congress Secretariat until the next session.
• Qadhafi said former US presidents Kissinger, Carter and Nixon could have been hired as advisers for money.
• The speech was broadcast by Tripoli Voice of the Greater Arab Homeland and transmitted by FBIS London on 7 May 1985.

TKE 4 OF 5 -- AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESS TO GENERAL PEOPLE'S CONGRESS 

LD072346 

((EXCERPTS)) HOW I WISH THAT ALL THE LOCAL PEOPLE'S COMMITTEES 

WOULD ASK THE GENERAL PEOPLE'S COMMITTEE TO ACCOUNT FOR ITSELF ON 

THE BASIS OF ITS ACHIEVEMENTS SO THAT THOSE PEOPLE WHOM YOU KICK 

OUT ARE KICKED OUT FOR VALID REASONS AND ARE CONDEMNED FOR 

IMPORTANT THINGS. BUT, SO FAR, I REGARD THE MATTER AS A STRONG 

REACTION ON THE PART OF THOSE WHO CALLED FOR THE DOWNFALL OF THE 

COMMITTEE. ULTIMATELY, THE RESPONSIBILITY LIES WITH ALL LIBYANS. 

ANY LIBYAN WHO RECEIVES WAGES FOR WORK HE SHOULD CARRY OUT 

AND WHO REFUSES TO DO SO SHOULD BE KICKED OUT, AND NOT THE FOREIGNER. 

ANY LIBYAN WHO GETS TRANSFERRED FROM ONE PLACE TO ANOTHER AND SAYS: 

I AM NOT GOING THERE; ANY LIBYAN WHO IS TOLD OT LEAVE HIS JOB AND 

BE TRAINED FOR ANOTHER AND THEN REFUSES AND SAYS: NO, I AM STAYING 

IN MY PLACE, SHOULD BE KICKED OUT. THIS IS BECAUSE SUCH A PERSON 

IS AN OBSTACLE AND A REACTIONARY; HE IS BACKWARD AND AN ENEMY 

OF PROGRESS AND FREEDOM. HE HELPS COLONIALISM, BECAUSE AS LONG 

AS WE ARE BACKWARD WE DEPEND ON THE FOREIGNERS. 

IF YOU TELL SOME LIBYANS: COME AND LET US BUILD SOME RAODS AND 

THEY ANSWER: NO, WE WILL NOT -- THEN YOU HAVE TO BRING IN A

THE WHOLE WORLD OUTSIDE DEPENDS ON MONEY AND CAN BE BOUGHT FOR 

MONEY. WE MUST EXERT PRESSURE ON THE PEOPLE'S COMMITTEES NOT TO PAY 

MORE MONEY TO THE FOREIGNERS -- IT IS NO GOOD THAT 

EVERYBODY SHOULD KEEP ASKING FOR MORE AND MORE MONEY. THE RIGHT 

THING IS TO TAKE TO TASK THE COMMITTEE WHICH CONTINUES TO BRING 

PEOPLE IN FROM ABROAD. IF THERE IS SOMETHING WE NEED, THEN 

WELL AND GOOD -- WE WOULD BE HAPPY TO IMPORT IT. IF THERE ARE 

NO CLOTHES, THEN GOOD, BRING THEM FROM ABROAD. BUT IF WE HAVE 

CLOTHES, THEN WE SHOULD NOT IMPORT THEM. WE SHOULD 

BUILD FACTORIES TO PRODUCE OUR OWN CLOTHES. IF WE HAVE NO SHOES 

SOME PEOPLE WILL SAY: WE WILL GO BAREFOOT UNLESS YOU IMPORT 

SHOES FOR US FROM ABROAD. BUT IF YOU IMPORT THINGS THAT YOU CAN 

MAKE IT MEANS THAT YOU ARE PAYING FOREIGNERS WITH HARD CURRENCY. 

THE OPPOSITE MEANS THAT YOU ARE SAVING THIS HARD CURRENCY AND 

KEEPING IT INSIDE THE COUNTRY. I SEE THAT DURING THE CURRENT 

FINANCIAL YEAR THE RESERVE IS INCREASING BECAUSE THERE IS A 

RESTRICTIOIN ON THE CURRENCY BEING PAID ABROAD. ((PASSAGE OMITTED

YOU SHOULD NOT REJOICE AT SEEING THINGS BEING IMPORTED 

FROM ABROAD. YOU SAY: BRING US BUSES, TELEVISION PLAYS AA ESPECIALLY 

THOSE WHICH WERE BROUGHT TO BE SCREENED DURING RAMADAN LAST YEAR; 

THEN YOU COMPALIN: WHERE IS THE BUS, ETC. THIS BY GOD, IS 

BACKWARDNESS; IT IS A LIMITED VISION| IF BUSES HAVE TO BE IMPORTED 

FROM ABROAD THEN THERE SHOULD BE NO BUSES. BUSES ARE IMPORTED, 

THEIR DRIVERS ARE BROUGHT INTO THE COUNTRY FROM ABROAD, AND 

THEIR SPARE PARTS FROM ABROAD -- IF THIS IS THE CASE THEN WE SHOULD 

GO ON THE BACKS OF DONKEYS IN TH ESTREETS. THIS IS NOT SHAMEFUL. 

THE SHAME IS WHEN FOREIGNERS LAUGH AT US UND TRICK US IN 

ORDER TO GET OUR MONEY AS THEY DO WITH THE PEOPLE OF THE GULF AND 

THE OTHER ARAB OIL-PRODUCING STATES +-- THEY ARE A LAUGHING 

STOCK. THE FOREIGNERS LAUGH AT THEM AND TAKE THEIR MONEY AND 

IN RETURN GIVE THEM THINGS WHICH BREAK DOWN AFTER 2 DAYS SO THAT THE 

FOREIGNERS CAN GET MORE MONEY. THOSE STATES WHICH ARE REGARDED 

AS RICH ARE SUFFERING FROM THE BIGGEST CRISES. ((PASSAGE OMITTED 

REPEATING THE ABOVE) ) 

THE GENERAL PEOPLE'S COMMITTEE IS ACTUALLY AT A CROSS-ROAD

HEARD. CRITICISM AGAINST ANY COMMITTEE OR ANY SECRETARY SHOULD 

BE DONE IN TH EPROPER MANNER. TO FACILITATE THIS TASK WE SHOULD 

NOT BE SEATED IN THREE HALLS AND TALK AS WE DO AND SAY THAT WE HAVE
