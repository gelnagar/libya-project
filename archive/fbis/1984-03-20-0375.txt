LIBYA

Air Force ‘Capable of Destroying’ U.S. AWACS 
LD192044 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1955 GMT 19 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Mar (JANA) — A spokesman of the Libyan 
Arab Armed Forces has stated the following: 


1. It appears that the succession of events connected to the 
bombing operation, of which the Omdurman radio station was a 
target, seems to have been fabricated, and that it took place with 
the knowledge and the encouragement of the United States for 
the purpose of finding an excuse to force Egypt and Sudan to 
allow U.S. strategic spying. This is considered to be flagrant 
aggression against the Arab nation, violation of its airspace, and 


a threat to its independence. The Arab nation is called upon to 
resist this flagrant aggression, which is trying to return through 
the window after being kicked out the door. 


These agents want to make amends to their master, Reagan, for 
his great and shameful loss in Lebanon, at the expense of the 
honor and independence of Egypt and Sudan. Seeking the assis- 
tance of the United States and its aircraft on every occasion fully 
exposes the incompetence of the Egyptian and Sudanese regimes 
to defend themselves. It also constitutes an insolent provocation 
against the dignity of the Egyptian and Sudanese officers and 
men, the Egyptian and Sudanese peoples, and consequently the 
entire Arab nation. 


2. It is not ruled out that the return of U.S. AWACS spy planes 
to the area seeks, among other things, aggression against Libya. 
If it is proved that these planes have returned to facilitate, pave 
the way, cover or protect in any form, aggression on Libya, we 
warn that the Libyan Arab Air Force is capable of reaching them 
and destroying them. Reagan should do his figuring again, 
figuring based on tampering with world stability and peace and 
on his agents flinging themselves to his bosom. We also remind 
the agents in Egypt and Sudan of the utter failure of the United 
States in protecting Haile Selassie Idris as-Sunussi; the shah; 
Lon Nol; and As-Sadat — people who sought U.S. protection. 
This protection led to a shortening of their lives. He who gives a 
warning is excused.
