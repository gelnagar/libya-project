Summary (AI): A 1982 CIA assessment says Libyan activity in West Africa was low-level compared with its efforts elsewhere in Africa, with Ghana the main exception. It describes Libyan subversion and support for dissidents in several neighbouring countries. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• Libyan cargo planes to Ghana have averaged about two a week since the coup, ostensibly carrying foodstuffs and medical supplies but possibly military equipment.
• Libya opened a People's Bureau in Ghana after the new government re-established diplomatic relations.
• Benin hosts a Libyan People's Bureau of almost 100 persons and serves as a transit and recruiting point for West Africans training in Libya.
• Libya encouraged Tuareg rebellion in Niger and offered haven and support to a leading oppositionist in Senegal.
• Libya bribed Nigerian officials and supported political and religious groups there.
• Libya encouraged rebellion by ethnic minorities in Mali.
• In Mauritania, Libyan support for pro-Libyan elements was so blatant that the opposing military faction moved quickly to change the leadership.
