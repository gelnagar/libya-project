LIBYA

People’s Congresses Discuss Foreign Relations 
LD291535 Tripoli JANA in English 1419 GMT 29 Dec 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi al Awal 24, Dec 29, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Some of the Basic People’s Congresses 
have started discussing Libya’s relations with foreign countries 
on the basis of these countries’ political stands on Arab national 
issues, as well as their attitude toward the Jamahiriyah’s inter- 
national role. 


The BPC masses realized the importance of this subject because 
of the colonialist countries’ repeated attempts to undermine the 
Libyan Arab people’s stands. Therefore, the existing cooperation 
with these countries, in the light of new attitudes, should be 
reviewed and decided upon decisively. 


The masses participating in these congresses discussed the 
aggressive U.S. and French stands towards the Libyan Arab 
people and analyzed the Jamahirtyai’s present relations with the 
two countries, particularly economic and trade relations, and 
arms purchases from these countries. 


The masses participating in the discussion suggested boycotting 
Western arms and finding substitutes for such a market. 


The congresses also discussed the diplomatic relations which 
have recently been established with the Zionist entity and sug- 
gested that Libya's relations with these countries should be 
severed.
