AL-USBU' AS-SIYASI ON YOUNG'S 'FORCED' RESIGNATION 
LD191813 Tripoli JANA in English 1804 GMT 18 Aug 79 LD 


{Text ] Tripoli, Aug 18, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Yesterday's issue of AL-USBU' AS- 
SIYASI magazine published here commented on the forced resignation of Mr Andrew Young, 
the U.S. permanent representative to the United Nations. 


In an article written by the editor under the title "A Lesson the Arabs Will Fail To 
Learn", The magazine focused on the background and the consequences of this topic, its 
relation to the Arab cause and the influence of the Zionist lobby dominating the American 
political system. 


The magazine said that a calm, sensible dialogue with a Palestinian Arab citizen ended 
the career of a senior American official, the result of which was the wrath of the entire 
unjust American machinery. On the other hand, the complete meeting of interests between 
America and (Israel) has not resulted in anything but open treason by As-Sadat, the con- 
spiracy of silence practiced by many people, and the complete failure to take any Arab 
stand to counter clear American hostility against the Arab nation, its freedom and 
ambitions. 


The text of the article is as follows: 


The American representative to the UN, Mr Andrew Young, was neither a close friend of 
the Arabs nor a defender of their causes, but he was a close frined of President Carter 
and a defender of the American Government, its stands and aggressions, therefore there 
is no reason for joy or despair because of his expulsion and his forced resignation, 


The incident should be a lesson to Arab leaders and the Arab nation. Although we know in 
advance that that the Arab leaders are incapable of absorbing the lesson and the Arab 
nation incapable of doing anything despite its capability of absorbing the lesson and 

the reasons for this are well known. 


"Wheat did Andrew Young commit to receive such insults and open condemnation from the U.S. 
secretary of state and to be hurriedly summoned by the President who makes him choose 
either to resign or to be sacked? Poor Young had to resign to save his dignity. Young 
committee a crime against liberty in the land of professed "liberty" and therefore had 
to be punished. 


Maybe this resignation is the first of a series of similar (?moves). The black American 
ambassador found enough courage to meet with the PLO observer in the UN and exchange 
ideas on possible amendments to Resolution 242, 


Not a single Arab believes that the amendment of Resolution 242 will satisfy anything 
but the interests of (Israel), recognition for its occupation of Arab lands and the 
capitulation treaty signed by the defeated Egyptian president. 


Andrew Young was practically putting out diplomatic feelers and exerting efforts as 

part of a multiparty clique including Ceausescu, Kreisky, Brandt, Giscard d'Estaing and 
other Arab and non-Arab mediators. All of them are manipulated by the Zionist Brzezinski 
who hides behind the false image of an American religious man known as Jimmy Carter.

In fact, Andrew Young has done no more than imagine that he was actually the man of 
freedom who could do what he wanted, particularly that the man he met is an accredited 
representative given the legitimate status of observer by a clear and definite United 
Nations resolution. But no, Mr At-Tarazi is Palestinian and represents the Palestine 
Liberation Organization and the Palestine Liberation Organization is the enemy of Zionism

Andrew Young is very lucky in that he was not sent to the electric chair because of the

What is the lesson which we believe Arab leaders will not heed--the lesson of dealing 
with the enemy? Let us explain. 


Zionists in Palestine are enemies of the Arab nation and successive American administra- 
tions have been staunch allies of (Israel). 


Logically, this equation may not seem sound, but in reality this is the existing formula 
of relations between the above-mentioned parties. 


All what Andrew Young did was hold a secret meeting (which was later uncovered) with a 
PLO representative. He did not denounce the Zionists for occupying the Arab lands; 
neither did he talk about the rights of Palestinians to restore their land and practice 
their sovereignty. He did not even call upon his country to reconsider its stand towards 
the Palestinian cause. 


Despite this he was considered traitor and enemy of America and its friends. He was 
publicly condemned, blamed, threatened and forced to resign... and that is exactly what 
happened to William Antony who dared to suggest a neutral stand by Nixon's administration 
towards the Palestinian problem and the Middle East as a whole, 


Let all Arab friends of America be warned that the U.S. supplies (Israel) with all its 
basic necessities, guns and butter. 


Was not America one of the first countries to recognize (Israel)? Does not America issue 
daily declarations of support to (Israel's) existence and didn't the U.S. fight alongside 
Israel in 1967 and 1973? Doesn't America call your Palestinian brethren "terrorists" 
because they are defending their right of regaining usurped land? Doesn't the U.S, 
encourage; finance and arm (Israel) to continue the destruction of southern Lebanon which 
is not considered even remotely aggressive but is branded as self-defense? Doesn't 
America find itself always ready to make issue if (?anyone) is not allowed to emigrate 
from the Soviet Union to occupied Palestine? 


Sixty percent of all Soviet Jewish emigrants go to the United States or any other 
country except Palestine. 5s 


Yet on the other hand its seems odd that America constantly denies the right of 
Palestinians to return to their rightful homeland... 


Doesn't America threaten you, her Arab friends, with occupation of your lands and 
resources and doesn't it threaten employing its hegemony on your sovereignty?

JANA ON EXTENT OF ISRAELI INTELLIGENCE ACTIVITIES IN U.S, 
1D181036 Tripoli JANA in English 0910 GMT 18 Aug 79 LD 


{Text ] Washington, Aug 18, JAMAHRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--American Government sources have 
admitted that (Israeli) intelligence agenvs are considered most active in the United 
States of America. 


According to these sources, the Zionists have concentrated spying activities in the U.S. 
and are helped by some members of the American Zionist communities and sometimes by 
American agencies. 


Mr Andrew Young, the American ambassador to the UN who offered his resignation on 
Wednesday following a campaign of condemnation and reprobation launched against him by 
the American administration and due to Zionist pressures which were imposed because of 
his meeting with the PLO representative at the UN, was asked if electronic equipment 
was used by the Zionists to bug the talks. He replied: "In diplomatic circles tech- 
nical equipment was way ahead of the ability of people to communicate," 


The sources doubt the U.S. ability to know the full extent of the activities of Zionist 
intelligence in the U.S. They stated that no reliable estimate is available of the 
Zionist activities which they thought would never be detected. 


The sources said that evidence to the intense spying by Zionist agents against members of 
the Arab community in America, is the detention of American youths of Arab origin who 
were held as soon as they arrived in occupied Palestine because they had met Palestinians. 


Their immediate detention indicated that Zionist authorities had been notified before 
their arrival. The general belief is that the American Federal Bureau of Investigation 
is collaborating with Zionist agents in watching the Arab students organizations in the 
U.S.
