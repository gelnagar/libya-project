Summary (AI): A 1985 CIA reading-room article says the CIA backed the exile group NFSL, whose 8 May 1984 attempt to assassinate Qaddafi failed. Two days after the leader was killed on 6 May, a five-hour gun battle at the Bab Aziziya barracks ended in the death or capture of the rebels.
• The NFSL was formed in 1981; its first public proclamation was dated 7 October 1981.
• Saudi Arabia reportedly provided at least $7 million to the NFSL.
• After the failed attempt, Qaddafi reportedly executed perhaps as many as 200 dissidents and imprisoned thousands more.
• CIA agents reportedly trained NFSL recruits in Western Europe, Sudan and Morocco.
• 1984-05-06: Ahmed Ibrahim Ahwas, leader of the NFSL military wing, was caught and killed trying to infiltrate Libya from Tunisia. (1 killed)
• 1984-05-08: Five-hour gun battle at the Bab Aziziya barracks ended in the death or capture of NFSL rebels in the assassination attempt on Qaddafi.
