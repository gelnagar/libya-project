LIBYAN FOREIGN POLICY 

Some observers of Libyan activity have reported signs that President . 
Qadhafi's foreign policy approach may be in transition. Although the evidence 
is far from conclusive at this time, the signs point toward the possibility of 
somewhat more responsible international behavior, and improvement in Libya's 
relations with the West. If these perceptions prove accurate, they presage 
changes in Mideast political, economic and military dynamics which may provide 
opportunities for policy initiatives in the West. : 

Since a transition, however subtle or restrained, in Libyan international 
affairs can have serious and profound consequences, it seems prudent to assess 
collection capabilities against Libyan foreign policy and, where gaps or remediable 
shortcomings appear, to recommend changes which will enhance finished intelligence 
analysis. The attached "Objectives" are those topics of concern through which
