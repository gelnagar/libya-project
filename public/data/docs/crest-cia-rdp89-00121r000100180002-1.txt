Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery briefing note reports SA-3 air defence equipment deployed at Labraq Airfield in eastern Libya, suggesting an upgrade from the previously deployed SA-6. Labraq is far from Tripoli, so it has no direct Tripoli relevance.
• An SA-3 firing battery (three SA-3/GOA four-rail launchers) was observed in travel mode at Labraq Airfield.
• One low blow SA-3 guidance control radar in travel mode was observed.
• At least 11 probable SA-3 missile transloaders, one SA-3 computer van and two electronics trailers were seen in the airfield support area.
• SA-3 equipment is normally not in the Labraq area, which suggests an upgrade from the previously deployed SA-6/Gainful air defence.
• Coordinates given: 32-47-39N 021-57-50E.
