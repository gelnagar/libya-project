AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES SYRIA'S KHADDAM 8 MAY 


LD082144 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 8 May 81 


[Excerpt] This evening the brother leader received Brother ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam, the 
Syrian deputy prime minister and foreign minister, who conveyed to the brother leader 

a message from Syrian President Hafiz al-Asad within the context of the continuing con- 
tacts between the Jamahiriyah and Syria concerning the provocations against the Arab 
nation by the Zionist enemy, backed by U.S. imperialism. Brother ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam 
acquainted the leader of the revolution with the latest developments in the Lebanese 
arena and with matters concerning the Arab issues. 


JANA CLAIMS LIBYAN INFLUENCE ON FRENCH ELECTION 


LD111032 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 11 May 81 


[Text] The results of the French elections which toppled Giscard d'Estaing confirm a 
number of facts. The influence of the Great 1 September Revolution on the French elec- 
tion is a continuation of its powerful and steady influence on the course of serious 
international events. The leader of the revolution, interviewed by the French Channel 

1 on 21 January 1981, called on the French people not to elect Valery Giscard d'Estaing 
because he harmed France's prestige and what was achieved by De Gaulle and Pompidou. 

The brother leader, interviewed by AFP, uncovered the truth about the French policy pur- 
sued by Giscard d'Estaing and the harm it had on the French people. In this interview 
the brother leader said: "Since d'Estaing took the helm in France, French policy has 
adopted a very backward colonialist policy, a matter noticed by the French and by the 
whole world." The leader went on to say: "If France is concerned about building fruit- 
ful relations with the peoples, the Arab nation and Africa, then Giscard d'Estaing has 
proved that he is unable to realize this." 


The downfall of Giscard d'Estaing confirms the continuing world influence of the 1 
September Revolution. Before Giscard d'Estaing the influence of the Jamahiriyah was 
great in the downfall of Jimmy Carter. The brother leader of the revolution, inter- 
viewed by NEWSWEEK, called on the American people not to reelect Carter because of his 
hostile actions against the Arab nation and his role in signing the Camp David agreement. 
Carter was toppled when most observers expected him to win. 


Before Giscard d'Estaing and Carter, the 1 September Revolution influenced the overthrow 
of Haile Selassie, who owned Ethiopia and all that was in it and who turned it into an 
American-Zionist base. The role of the 1 September Revolution in the overthrow of 
Emperor Haile Selassie is confirmed by the whole world. The shah of Iran was overthrown 
by the Iranian people in a sweeping popular revolution. When asked by British television 
about those who were behind his overthrow, he said that the hero of the action was 
Al-Qadhdhafi. The 1 September Revolution was the sole voice and the sole factor, in 
addition to the voice and effect of the Iranian people, which attacked the shah and led 
to his overthrow. Leopold Sedar Senghor "fell, collapsed, and the Senegalese people got 
rid of his authority and his agency." The 1 September Revolution was the basis for his 
overthrow. 


Today Giscard d'Estaing, in whose hand Senghor was an instrument in Africa, fell. The 
colonialists and capitalists and their agents continue to fall. Today, Giscard d'Estaing 
fell, the rest will follow. 


OFFICIAL TELLS ENVOYS OF MOROCCAN EXPANSIONISM 


LD091614 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 9 May 81 


[Text] The brother secretary of the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for 
Foreign Liaison this morning met with the African ambassadors accredited to Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


V. 12 May 81 Q2 NORTH AFRICA 


Discussions during the meeting covered the current developments on the African Continent 
and the Moroccan regime's role in disturbing the peace and stability of the continent. 


In this regard, the brother secretary of the Foreign Liaison Bureau warned about the 
role being played by the ruling regime in Morocco and its expansionist policy. He said 
that the regime is launching a political campaign to convince other countries that there 
are problems and instability in Africa, claiming that the Jamahiriyah and Algeria are 
behind it. 


The secretary of the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison added 
that the policy of the ruling regime in Morocco is unprincipled and aims at fostering 
instability in Africa. It pursues a policy which had been condemned by the OAU and the 
United Nations. The latest action taken by the Moroccan regime in this regard was the 
attempt to overthrow the regime in Mauritania by means of terroristic means, which re- 
veals the true nature of that regime. 


He pointed out that the Moroccan regime called for Mauritania's annexation prior to its 
independence. It now regards the Western Sahara as an area under its domination. 


He affirmed the Jamahiriyah's stand on the side of the Saharan people and said that the 
Polisario is a front fighting for the liberation of the Saharan people from the domina- 
tion of the Moroccan regime. He pointed out that the Saharan people's right to self- 
determination was endorsed by the United Nations and the OAU and that the Saharan Govern- 
ment has won recognition by 46 countries, of which 26 are African countries. This proves 
that the support accorded by the Jamahiriyah and by African and other countries to the 
Saharan people is based on the resolutions of world organizations. 


He said that the king of Morocco is attempting to escape the isolation in which he finds 
himself, now that the Jamahiriyah has exposed his suspect role and his contacts with the 
Zionist commanders during the past years. It was he who arranged the visit by the 
Egyptian ruler to the Zionists and his meeting with the terrorist Begin, which led 
eventually to the stable David accords, which are in turn contrary to the resolutions of 
the United Nations, the OAU and the Arab and Islamic summit conferences. He added that 
it was not strange that the Moroccan king contacted the racist regime in South Africa, 
because the Moroccan king does not care about principles in his political activity. 


The brother secretary of the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison 
added that the Jamahiriyah's stand against the Moroccan regime is a principled one which 
is upheld by all free men in Africa and the world. He also said that the call made by 

the commander of the Great 1 September Revolution for the establishment of unity between 
the Sahara and Mauritania and the creation of an alliance among Mauritania, the Saharan 
Government, Algeria and the Jamahiriyah has perturbed the Moroccan regime, which sees 

in this a step directed against the role played by that regime in the service of im- 
perialist interests in Africa, at a time when the United States has provided it with arms 
to use in threatening the countries neighboring Morocco and in continuing to kill the 
Saharan people and render them destitute. 


In conclusion, he warned that the African ambassadors should realize these facts, adding 
that we are confident that the Arab people in Morocco are capable of putting an end to 


these traitorous plots of the Moroccan king against the Arab nation and Africa.
