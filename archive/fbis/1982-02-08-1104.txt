LIBYA

GOVERNMENT INFORMS OAU OF U.S. PRESENCE IN CHAD 


LD061512 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 6 Feb 82 


[Text] The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah informed the current chairman and 
the secretary general of the OAU about the presence of U.S. Armed Forces elements among 
some units of the African peace-keeping forces in Chad and informed them that the U.S. 
military presence in Chad is considered a violation of OAU resolutions. At the same time, 
that presence constitutes a danger to security and stability in Chad and, subsequently, to 
the security and safety of Africa.

NICARAGUAN DELEGATION ARRIVES 6 FEB FOR VISIT 


LDO71034 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 7 Feb 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Feb (JANA) -- Jaime Wheelock, member of the Nicaraguan Sandinista National 
Front Command and minister of agricultural development agrarian reform; Foreign Minister 
Miguel d'Escoto; and Finance Minister Joaquim Cuadra, arrived in Tripoli last night for a 
visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah within the framework of efforts 

aimed at strengthening relations of friendship and cooperation between the Libyan Arab and 
the Nicaraguan peoples. 


Joint Committee Meets 
NCO80816 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 8 Feb 82 


[Text] The joint Libyan-Nicaraguan committee began its meetings yesterday at the meeting 
hall in the people's office for foreign liaison. Participating in the meetings on the 
Libyan side were the secretary of the general people's committee for justice, the 

secretary of the general people's committee for agrarian reform and land reclamation, and 
the secretary of the people's office for foreign liaison; on the Nicaraguan side were 

Jaime Wheelock, member of the Sandinist National Front Command and minister of agricultural 
development and agrarian reform, and the foreign and finance ministers. 


At the outset of the meeting, the member of the Sandinist National Front Command delivered 

a speech lauding the courageous stands of the Great al-Fatih Revolution and its confrontation 
with all the imperialist American plots and provocations. He emphasized Nicaragua's 
solidarity with the Libyan people in confronting imperialism and its agents. 


The secretary of the general people's committee for justice welcomed the Nicaraguan revolution 
delegation and said the Libyan Arab people and the Nicaraguan people will confront all the 
plots and provocations carried out by the U.S. administration, emphasizing that the [words 
indistinct] augur the end of imperialism and [word indistinct]. 


TRIPOLI 7 FEB REPORTS ON SITUATION IN SUDAN 


Guards’ Attack 


NCO70705 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 7 Feb 82 


[Text] Arrivals from Khartoum have stated that a group of (?agent) Numayri's guards, led 

by a captain, yesterday attacked the entrance to the presidential headquarters in Khartoum. 
The collaborators of Deputy Defense Minister Gen Fathi Abu al-Hasan attacked these soldiers 
from the rear, seriously wounding three of them; the three soldiers have probably died as 

a result of their wounds. Two other soldiers were arrested. Numayri's authorities blockaded 
the area following the incident and launched a wide-scale search of the house and citizens.

This daring operation is regarded as clear confirmation that Numayri's regime is now 
living its last moments and that the Arab people in Sudam are now more determined than 
at any time before to overthrow this traitor. 


Attackers’ Executed 
LD071354 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 7 Feb 82 


[Text] The repressive authorities in Sudan have executed by firing squad five soldiers 
and three officers of various ranks, who were arrested yesterday after storming into 

the presidential headquarters in Khartoum and firing at the guards in an attempt directed 
against the ruler of Sudan. 


According to an urgent dispatch from Khartoum, the death sentence was carried out against 
these eight men at dawn today in one of the military barracks in the outskirts of the 
capital. 


According to other reports, the repressive regime in Sudan earlier last week executed 
more than 13 officers and men on charges of rebellion and the circulation of leaflets 
opposed to the ruling bloody regime. 


Numayri Loses Support 
Lv071432 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 7 Feb 82 


[Text] Sources in close touch with Sudanese affairs have affirmed that the ruler of 
Sudan is living his last days now that he has lost all ability to rule and has been 
exposed and robbed of military and popular support. 


According to these sources, a meeting of all the leaders of national action in Sudan 
was convened 17-21 January this year. It transpired at the meeting that all the elements 
on whom Numayri relied -- be they ministers, members of the Political Bureau of members 
of the Sudanese Socialist Union, or members of the so-called popular regional councils 

or regional commanders - opposed Numayri's decisions and actions. 


Numayri, who attended the final session of the meeting, becamse hysterical and poured 
his wrath on the conferees. He abused them and described them as cowards. According 
to these same sources, Numayri affirmed at the conference that he is determined to 
continue with his so-called economic revival program by force. 


The conferees asked that Numayri's powers be reduced, the Sudanese Socialist Union dissolved 
and its members put on trial. They also accused Numayri of isolating Sudan from the 
Arab nation and of approving the American presence in the country. 


The sources pointed out that just as Numayri found himself rejected by the popular leaders, 
he has also found himself rejected by the entire army. Junior officers have declared 
their rejection of the regime's institutions and have asked Numayri to resign. They 

asked to meet with him and tell him openly of their posi ‘on. But he refused to meet 

them. He decided instead to meet with the senior commanas of the army. 


The sources pointed out that the senior army commanders actually met with Numayri on 26 
January when he was shocked by the officers reports of the disgraceful acts of the 

regime and the corruption Numayri's henchmen have been committing even inside the 
presidential palace itself, under the leadership of Baha’ ad-Din Muhammad Idris, minister 
in charge of presidential affairs. 


The supreme commanders of the Sudanese Army affirmed to the ruler of Sudan during the 
meeting that the army is not prepared to act against the Sudanese people and that it 

is not happy with the repressive methods used by the regime against the Sudanese people's 
ursurge. The army also refused categorically to be subservient to any foreign quarter.

According to these sources, following this stormy meeting at which Numayri was asked 

to resign because he was sick and incapable of ruling, Lt Gen ‘Abd al-Majid Muhammad 
Khalil issued a statement on the results of the meeting in which he affirmed that the 
Sudanese Army fully agrees on all issues facing Sudan and that its position toward them 
is firm and cannot be subject to bargaining. 


The sources pointed out that such a stand by the army is extremely serious and proves that 
the ruler of Sudan has lost his last support. 


Army Officers Dismissed 
LD071400 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 7 Feb 82 


[Text] According to reports received from Khartoum, 26 Sudanese Army officers have been 
relieved of their command duties by the ruler of Sudan. 


These include Chief of Staff Lt Gen 'Izz ad-Din ‘Ali Malik and five officers of major 
general rank including the commander of the airborne forces, Maj Gen Badawi al-Mubashshir, 
the commander of the Khartoum garrison, Maj Gen Nur ad-Din Mubarak and the commander of 
military intelligence, Maj Gen Ahmad Yahya ‘Imran. They also include commanders of the 
transport corps, the training academy and directors of military operations and armed forces 
accounts departments. This is in addition to 13 brigadiers and 5 colonels.
