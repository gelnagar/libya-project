LIBYA

PROBLEMS IN CHAD ATTRIBUTED TO 'FANATICAL' MALLOUM 
LD251708 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 25 Jan 79 LD 
(Unattributed commentary ] 


{Excerpt] The current critical situation in Chad, typified by crippling economic 
difficulties and numerous strikes which are about to bring the country to an economic 
standstill, along with the danger of the country being torn apart, is the sole and direct 
responsibility of Felix Malloum, the fanatical fascist who seized power in the country 

in order to use it to implement later on a plan to reimpose colonial domination on the 
African Continent and to impose colonial military bases on Chadian territory--bases 

which colonialism uses to strike at the forces of revolution and liberation in Chad itself 
and on the African Continent in general. 


News from Chad speaks of an explosive situation within the present leadership which, if 
it is not resolved--and it will not be except through Felix Malloum's downfall--will lead 
to a bloody conflict and terrible massacres.whose victims will be new generations of 

the Chadian people, who are living in poverty and under the colonial yoke and who suffer 
from the disgraceful fanatical measures carried out by Felix Malloum's hired gang of 
colonialism, The deteriorating situation suffered by the Chadian masses has reached 

the point of overriding gravity; this crisis has befallen the oppressed people of Chad 
only since Felix Malloum seized power and appointed himself a fascist dictator, imple- 
menting the colonial plot against the masses in Chad and against the African Continent 
and the movements of liberation and progress on the continent. He opened up the country 
to colonial military bases, imagining that [words indistinct] are able to protect his 
agent regime and to immobilize the revolutionary movement in Chad and on the African
