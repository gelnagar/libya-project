U.S. "PROVOCATIVE TERRORIST MANEUVERS' NOTED 


LD181505 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 18 Jan 83 


[Text] The U.S. 6th Fleet today is carrying out procative terrorist maneuvers off the 
Libyan coast, (?according to) aviation sources in Tripoli. These maneuvers are to 
continue until the 26th of this month. Aircraft and naval vessels are taking part in 
them. 


Thus the American Administration and the pirates of the Pentagon once again confirm the 
hostile course and the ugliest American penchants for intimidation, threats, and 
blackmail against peoples who reject American hegemony.
