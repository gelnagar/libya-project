Summary (AI): A Washington Post clipping reports (via Newsweek) that CIA Director William Casey approved an agency plan to depose Libyan leader Muammar Qaddafi, which the House Intelligence Committee protested. The plan reportedly included disinformation, a counter-government, and a paramilitary campaign.
• Newsweek reported the CIA plan called for a disinformation campaign to embarrass Qaddafi, creation of a counter-government to challenge his authority, and a paramilitary campaign.
• The House Intelligence Committee reportedly was outraged and Newsweek said it sent a strong letter of protest to President Reagan.
• An administration spokesman said Reagan had not yet seen the letter because it was going through the regular clearance process.
