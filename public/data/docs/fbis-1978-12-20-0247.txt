LIBYA PROMISES TO DETAIN, EXTRADITE FRG TERRORISTS 
LD191348yY Hamburg DPA in German 1214 GMT 19 Dec 78 LD 


[Excerpt] Tripoli--Libya will detain and extradite immediately to the Federal Republic 
German terrorists who make a stopover there, This was the promise mace by Libya's secre- 
tary of the interior, Yunis Abu al-Qasim, to German journalists in Tripoli. He stated 
that all border control posts and police authorities have been supplied with search data 
from the Federal Criminal Investigation Office (BKA) in Wiesbaden, Abu al-Qasim went 

on to say that the BKA and the Libyan security authorities are exchanging information on 
a permanent basis, 


The Libyan secretary of the interior stressed that his country passes on to the Federal 
German authorities immediately information on the movements of German terrorists in the 
Arab world, He announced that Libya will endeavor to win over other Arab states to this 
form of cooperation, He will raise the subject at the next conference of Arab interior 
ministers, Abu al-Qasim underlined that the cooperation between the security authorities 
of the two countries is being carried out with the express approval of head of state 
Al-Qadhdhafi, 


Closer Ties With West 


LD191600Y Hamburg DPA in German 1449 GMT 19 Dec 78 ID 


[Excerpt] Tripoli--Libya, one of the world's most important suppliers of oil, apparently 


intends to loosen its hitherto one-sided political links with the Eastern Bloc and is 
striving for improved relations with the West. A sure sign of this change of course by 
head of state Al-Qadhdhafi is that the process of drawing closer to the FRG should come 
about in spite of the latter's good relations with Israel, The Libyan head of state 
declared in an interview with German journalists in Tripoli: "This does ; not prevent 

the further deepening of economic relations and cooperation in the area of security policy. 


Head of state Al-Qadhdhafi also made a clear distinction between terrorists and liberation 
organizations: "One group want to free an occupied country, but terror serves to achieve 
personal gain, or is only an end in itself," stated Al-Qadhdhafi, who considers terrorists 
to be "far removed from reality,"

ID191030Y Tripoli JANA English 0800 GMT 19 Dec 78 ID 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, Dec 19 JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY-After the evening prayer's intermis- 
sion, the congress resumed, The secretary general of the congress referred to the reso- 
lutions of the people's congresses in respect to the coastal line where 170 congresses 
approved the memorandum in question, 


Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great lst of September Revolution and secretary 
general of the General People's Congress, said that "he cannot change the contents of 
resolutions made by the people's congresses, as opinions are for the masses to submit 
and not for individuals."

The congress then moved to review the item of authorities and capacities assigned to 
the General Secretariat where the chairman of the drafting committee read the resolu- 
tions of the people's congresses in this respect and said that 179 people's congresses 
resolved that General People's Congress should appoint the president of the Supreme 
Court and its judges and the public prosecutor, that is a percentage of 95.72, while 
two congresses said that the People's Committee to appoint them, one congress suggested 
that the Justice Supreme Council is responsible for their appointment, and one congress 
assigned this job to Justice Secretariat, 


The secretary general of the General People's Congress noted: "What has been said in 
this memorandum concerns administrative matters which the Revolutionary Command Council 
had earlier seen fit to deal with, But when the people had commenced the exercise of 
power these matters were not within the people's field of specialisation," 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed: "As of now, the General Secretariat will divert its 
efforts to the practice of revolution, As for other administrative affairs, these 
will be shared out between the General People's Congress and the People's Committee," 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added: "Concerning the Jamahiriyah Security Organisation, the 
suggestion is the abolition of this organization because the investigation police and 
security organs will in future become people's organs, It was therefore the duty of 
every individual inthe Jamahiriyah, and people's power gains," 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added: "These organs were earlier used to protect the government'ts 
status and maintain the Pegime, whether monarchical or republical, but now, power lay 

in the hands of the people, and they will create these organs from within their ranks, 
Every person in the Jamahiriyah will find himself obliged to protect himself, his 
property and his gains from the enemy who is spying on the country." 


The session then adjourned at 8:30 p.m, (Jamahiriyah local time) to be resumed tomorrow 
morning, 


19 Dec Morning Session

[Text] Tripoli, 19 Dec, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The congress then moved to the discussion 
of the item concerning agreements ratified with friendly and sisterly countries, 


The chairman of the drafting committee explained that 178 people's congresses recommended 
endorsement and ratification of those agreements, Some congresses also demanded an ex- 
pansion in the signing of such agreements to expand the aspects of cooperation between 
the Libyan Arab people and friendly and brotherly peoples, 


The congress also reviewed the item concerning assistance to the African struggle. The 
chairman of drafting committee pointed out that 186 people's congresses, equivalent to 
{word indistinct] had recommended assisting African struggle.

Commenting on these points, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi explained that the reason why the 
subject was brought up before the people's committees was based on the activity of the lst 
of September Revolution in Africa. For, he said, the African countries have become the 
Arab nation's ally by severing their diplomatic relations with the Zionist enemy, hence 
achieving what Arab diplomacy failed to do over twenty years. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that following the defeated Egyptian president's treason 
and his recognition of the enemy, attempts would be made by a number of other capitulationist 
treacherous Arabs to restore the African's recognition of the enemy. Colonel al-Qadhdhafi 
said that this meant a relaunching of the struggle once more to safeguard the Arab nation's 
and Islam's gains in Africa. He remarked that on the other hand, Africa was facing the 
aggression of racist regimes in southern Africa just as the Arab people were facing 

racism and Zionism in occupied Palestine. These racist regimes, he went on, constituted 

the racist Zionists! main ally. Accordingly Colonel al-Qadhdhafi raised the issue of 

having the Jamahiriyah join the five African countries of confrontation against the 

racist enemy in Palestine and southern Africa, 


The General People's Congress discussed the item on employing teachers during summer 
holidarys in order to use their services in all sectors. Another item concerning 
revolutionary agricultural and medical advisors was also discussed, 


Dr Muftah al-Usta 'Umar, the health secretary, elaborated on the achievements of the 
Great lst of September Revolution in the medical sector which constitutes a model to 
a great many countries. 


Resuming its meeting after a short recess, the congress reviewed the recommendations of 

the people's congresses concerning the memo on foreign policy dating from the third regular 
session of the General People's Congress of 1977 until its fourth session of 1978. The 
head of the drafting committee pointed out that 187 people's congresses had agreed upon 
the memo with a few additional comments, 


Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great lst September Revolution and secretary- 
general of the General People's Congress, enquired from the secretaries of the congresses 
about some of the comments made by the people about their congresses. The session was 
adjourned at 1.15 P.M. to be resumed at 5.00 P.M. Jamahiriyah local time. 


19 Dec Evening Session 
ID192224Y Tripoli JANA in English 1850 GMT 19 Dec 78 ID 


{Text] Tripoli, Dec 19, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The General People's Congress resumed 
its sessions at 5.15 P.M. this evening at the People's Hall in Tripoli, by reviewing the 
seventh item on the people's congresses agenda with regard to the memorandum related to 
re-organizing the information and culture sector. It was indicated that 183 congresses 
have approved the contents of the memorandum concerning the organization of this sector. 
Reference was also made to the observations and recommendations presented by the masses 

of the basic people's congresses. They include the formation of a people's committee 
which would undertake supervision of information sectors instead of an information and 
culture secretariat, and the press institution, the formation of a people's committees 

for information at municipalities level from which representatives would be coopted to 

the higher people's committee for information. The congresses also recommended that the 
information plan should be basically derived from the spirit of the Holy Koran and Islamic 
values as well as the interest in mobile cultural units, cultural centres financial support 
for information [as received] and the establishment of information directorates in 
municipalities.

The congresses recommended concentration on foreign information, the establishment of 
specialized institutions for information, and the formation of competent committees 
for domestic and foreign information. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, secretary general of the General People's Congress, talked on 
this topic clarifying the prevailing world situation of the mass media. He said: "It 
is either government-controlled, opposition-controlled, or a commercially-oriented 

mass media, but our situation in the Jamahiriyah differs from the rest of the world, 
because the government does not need propaganda any more, nor does any ruler have any 
personal interest in broadcasting or the press.... If any propaganda should be had, it 
should be propaganda for the people, it s people's authority, its principles and its 
stands," 


"Therefore, it is the right of every citizen to participate in this activity and present 
his point of view on its operating methods... because money spent in this field is the 
people's money." 


The session was adjourned for dusk prayers. 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great lst of September Revolution and secretary 
general of the General People's Congress, indicated at the end of reviewing the items 

om the congresses! agenda: "The illness of President Houari Boumediene and the provo- 
cations which Algeria faces has an effect on the congress! sessions which are character- 
ised by hastiness." 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi announced: "Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir has headed for 
Aigiers because what goes on in the Jamahiriyah concerns Algeria, and what goes on in 
Algeria concerns the Jamahiriyah." 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi affirmed "the Jamahiriyah enjoys an excellent world-wide 
reputation."
