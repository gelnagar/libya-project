Summary (AI): An FBIS transcript of an excerpted Qadhdhafi rally speech, broadcast from Tripoli on 2 September 1976, in which he describes the planned General People's Congress, rejects constitutions, presidents and a traditional army, and calls for the whole people to defend the country. It names no specific Tripoli sites, prisons or casualties.
• Speech says the General People's Congress will meet before next May and will select ministers-equivalent citizens for agriculture, industry, foreign affairs, interior affairs and other sectors.
• Speaker says the Koran must be the source of all legislation.
• Speaker says the responsibility for defence belongs to all the people rather than a traditional military, which he calls a worn-out theory.
• Speech marks the seventh anniversary of the first stage of the revolution (1 September 1969).
