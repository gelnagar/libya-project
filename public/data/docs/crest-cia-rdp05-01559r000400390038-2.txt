Summary (AI): A June 1982 CIA reading-room compilation of Arab press items. Its Libya item reports Qadhdhafi addressing hundreds of armed forces officers on the 12th anniversary of the US forces' evacuation from Libya, after recent naval maneuvers in the Gulf of Sidra, and discussing the Lebanon situation. Other items cover Libyan meetings with foreign visitors.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya could not take direct military action on the northern (Lebanon) front for geographical reasons.
• Qadhdhafi reportedly said Libya's potential was available only to confrontation states adjacent to Palestine: Egypt, Jordan, Saudi Arabia, Syria and Lebanon.
• Qadhdhafi hailed the heroic role of the Syrian air and naval forces.
• The Gulf of Sidra maneuvers by Libyan armed forces took place two days before the 14 June 1982 speech.
• 1982-06-14: Qadhdhafi addressed hundreds of armed forces officers on the 12th anniversary of the evacuation of American forces from Libyan soil, discussing the Lebanon situation and the Gulf of Sidra maneuvers
