LIBYA

Jabir Told Al-Asad ‘Rapidly Recovering’ 
LD160944 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
0015 GMT 16 Nov 8&3 


[Text] Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of 
the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, has telephoned Brother ‘Abd 
al-Halim Khaddam, Syrian deputy prime minister and foreign 
minister, to inquire about President Hafiz al-Asad’s health. 
Brother ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam assured Brig Abu Bakr Yunis 
Jabir about President Hafiz al-Asad’s health and informed him 
that he is rapidly recovering and that he is now in good health.

The prime minister of the enemy meant what he said in the 
statement, although he deliberately avoided clarifying and

1981, the alliance which emerged to crown the development of 
the physical relationship between America and the Zionist 
enemy and to translate the reality of the coincidence of interests 
and aims for which the Camp David accords paved a suitable 
launching pad in the Arab homeland. 


More than once attention has been drawn to the seriousness of 
this agreement, and it has been stressed that it not only raises the 
military and economic capability of the enemy but it goes beyond 
this to involving the enemy in the American military strategic 
plans implemented by Reagan’s administration to impose hege- 
mony through the use of force. 


Despite this, that is, despite the proclamation of the military 
alliance between America, a major power, and the racist entity 
which is in a state of war with the Arab nation, it is deeply 
regretted that some have not yet realized that America is an 
active ally of the Zionist enemy and that some still do gamble on 
American policy and alliance with America without consciously 
realizing that America has taken into account its alliance [with 
Israel] in achieving its strategic aims of dominating the Arab 
homeland and fully turning it into a zone of American influence, 
without considering or distinguishing between those who have 
made these aims possible for it or those who oppose and chal- 
lenged them, that is, that the American- Zionist strategic alliance 
requires the imposition of the precept of realizing the mutual 
interests and aims of the two parties and of serving their major 
interests.

mon aims in Lebanon. This signal does not only apply to this Arab
