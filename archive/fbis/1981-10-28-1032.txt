LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI WARNS OF ACTION AGAINST AWACS 


LD272126 Hamburg DPA in German 1819 GMT 27 Oct 81 


[Text] Hamburg, 27 Oct (DPA) -- The leader of the Libyan revolution, Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
believes that the United States wants to occupy Egypt. In an interview in the Hamburg 
magazine STERN, published on Thursday, Al-Qadhdhafi states: "Under no circumstances will 

we allow American AWACS planes to photograph Libyan territory. If they do so and are within 
range of our missiles, we will not hesitate to use them. I do not believe it is the 
Egyptians who want to attack us. It is the Americans who have aggressive intentions, not 
only against us but against the whole region...." 


Asked about his attitude toward terrorism, Al-Qadhdhafi replied: "We have always been 
against terrorism. We hate it. But the United States is terrorizing us.... It is 
threatening us militarily, politically and economically. That's terrorism, isn't it?" 
Al-Qadhdhafi said about Ronald Reagan's administration: I think Reagan is the most stupid 
and the craziest man in the world. Carter is an angel by comparison." 


Al-Qadhdhafi called new Egyptian President Husni Mubarak a "very good officer", but denied 
he is a good politican. He said the late President as-Sadat had led Mubarak "into a trap 
when he made him vice president." Al-Qadhdhafi described accusations by Sudanese President 
Muhammad Numayri that the Libyan Air Force had bombed Sudanese border villages as "rubbish." 
There is "no reason" for a war with Sudan, Al-Qadhadhafi said. 


MEETING OF LIBYAN, EGYPTIAN OFFICIALS DENIED 


LD271530 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 27 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 27 Oct (JANA) -- The QATARI NEWS AGENCY and KUNA on 26 October cited a 
report in AL-KHALIJ, which is published in Ash-Shariqah, about an alleged meeting which took 
place recently in a Western capital between a Libyan official and First Deputy of the 
Egyptian Foreign Ministry 'Usamah al-Baz. 


A source at the People's Office for Foreign Liaison told JANA today that this report is 
without foundation and is untrue. He also said Libya categorically rejects any such meeting 
with the like of 'Usamah al-Baz who has stained his name by his frequent visits to occupied 
Palestine and his frequent meetings with the Zionist terrorists and who has made a big 
contribution to the betrayal of the Arab nation. Today in all his positions he has become 
more Zionist than the Zionists themselves. 


28 Oct 81 ! NORTH AFRICA 


CPSU'S VORONIN, DELEGATION ARRIVE IN TIPOLI 


LD271848 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 27 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 27 Oct (JANA) -- Lev Vornonin, member of the CPSU Central Committee and 
first deputy chairman of the State Planning Committee, together with a delegation 
arrived in Tripoli this evening on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah. 


In a statement to a JANA correspondent, Voronin lauded the good relations of friendship 
and cooperation between the Jamahiriyah and the Soviet Union. He pointed out that the 
visit of his delegation will also contribute to the development of friendly relations 
between the two friendly countries.
