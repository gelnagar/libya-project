Summary (AI): A December 1981 New York Times article reports that President Reagan and his advisers were weighing punitive economic and political actions against Libya over an alleged assassination plot, and that about 1,000 of 2,500 Americans in Libya had left since May. It names no Tripoli sites.
• Administration cited 'serious evidence' of an assassination plot by Libya.
• Democrats in the Senate sought a nonbinding resolution backing a boycott of Libyan oil.
• Concern that Libya might hold Americans hostage if an oil embargo were announced.
• About 1,000 of the 2,500 Americans in Libya had left since May 1981.
• Senate Foreign Relations Committee hearings scheduled for Wednesday were canceled.
