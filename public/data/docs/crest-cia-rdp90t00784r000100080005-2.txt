Summary (AI): A December 1982 NPIC briefing board reports a major delivery of Soviet-made HIP medium transport helicopters to Tripoli (Tarabulus) Airfield, with 15 fuselage containers and at least 20 component containers in the military transport support area. The helicopters were first seen at Tripoli port facilities earlier.
• The HIP is a Soviet medium-lift helicopter for short-range transport of passengers and cargo; in the assault role it can carry up to 24 armed troops.
• Libya's existing helicopter force included Super Frelon, CH-47 and Hind types.
• Tarabulus Airfield coordinates given as 32-39-45N 013-09-35E.
• 1982-12: Major delivery of HIP medium transport helicopters; 15 fuselage shipping containers and at least 20 component containers in the military transport support area.
• First sighting in Libya of HIP helicopters: three fuselage containers and at least three component containers observed.
