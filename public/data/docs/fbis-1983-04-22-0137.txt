LIBYA

Support to Nicaragua Challenge to U.S. 
LD211508 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 
1230 GMT 21 Apr 83 


[Text] The U.S. Administration, its President in the White 
House, the State Department, and U.S. ambassadors in Latin 
Ameica are in a state of uncertainty as a result of the assistance 
provided by the Libyan Arab people to the revolution in Nicara- 
gua in order to confront U.S. colonialist aggressions. 


White House spokesman Larry Speakes said that President 
Reagan may soon deliver a speech about U:S. policy in Central 
America to win congressional approval of additional aid to 
pro-U.S. regimes in Latin America. The U.S. Administration has 
waged a wide campaign against the Jamahiriyah’s support to the 
revolution in Nicaragua following the stopover in Brazil of 
Libyan Arab planes on their way to Nicaragua carrying assis- 
tance from the Jamahiriyah to the people of Nicaragua, who are 
confronting U.S. aggression. 


In its turn the United States plays the role of supporting aggres- 
sion and facism, and it will not be able to stop Libyan aid to 
revolutionaries anywhere in the world despite its agents, spies, 
and terrorist apparatus. The Libyan Arab people, with its inter- 
national humanitarian revolution, today challenges the United 
States in the Caribbean and Latin America, which the United 
States has always believed to be merely a banana plantation 
belonging to it. The Libyan Arab people, which the U.S. Admin- 
istration challenged on the Sidra coast, are now returning the 
challenge to America and the Caribbean, which the United 
States considers to be its back yard
