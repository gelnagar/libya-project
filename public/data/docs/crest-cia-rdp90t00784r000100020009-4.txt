Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery note from November 1982 describes a large aircraft display and review area at Umm Aitiqah airfield, six nautical miles east of Tripoli, apparently tied to an Organization of African Unity meeting planned for Tripoli. It lists the aircraft present, including Flogger, Super Frelon, Foxbat, L-39 and Mirage types.
• Display included 16 Flogger, four Super Frelon, two probable Haze A, five Candid, 23 Foxbat, 15 L-39 and nine Mirage III/V aircraft.
• Transient apron held two Blinder, one Soviet Crusty, one Jetstar, one Falcon 50, three C-130, one L-100 and two Curl aircraft.
• Twelve Fitter aircraft were parked on the taxiway at the northwest end of the ESE/WNW runway; the document notes Fitters are not normally there.
• Coordinates given: 32-53-46N 013-16-32E.
• 1982-11: Large aircraft display and reviewing area set up at Umm Aitiqah, probably for the Organization of African Unity meeting in Tripoli.
