• The Supreme Islamic Council announced that Ramadan 1406 H begins on Saturday, 10 May 1986.
• Qadhdhafi's speech calls for challenging masses who come out under bombing and attacks the U.S. 6th Fleet as an American-Israeli fleet.
• The speech warns that any Arab country granting facilities to the U.S. 6th Fleet is considered in confrontation with Libya.
• The speech mentions Tunisian workers and the Tunisian prisoner Al-Mistiri.
• 1986-05-08: Qadhdhafi addressed a mass public rally in Banghazi, after a route of popular demonstrations from Thulug and Nawwagiyah.
• 1986-05-08: Qadhdhafi said U.S. aircraft were downed and pilots killed in the raids on Tripoli and Banghazi; the claim is his own and is unverified.

ambiguous situation in which people judged states’ policies on 
the basis of the atittudes adopted by individuals. Indeed do we 
not see virtually every day ministers embracing each other 
fraternally when their two countries are virtually at war.
