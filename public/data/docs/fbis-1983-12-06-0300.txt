LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi: Arafat's Exit To End Tripoli Attack 
LDO52325 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2300 GMT 5 Dec 83 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution made a statement to 
JANA yesterday concerning the situation in northern Lebanon 
In it, he said: The fighting taking place in Tripoli, Lebanon, 
will end with Yasir ‘Arafat's exit from Tripoli. As a result of the 
many and detailed meetings which have taken place between the 
parties concerned, | can guarantee that the town of Tripoli will 
not be attacked or stormed by force following Yasir “Arafat's 
departure. The brother leader added: Libya will accept Yasir 
‘Arafat and those accompanying him should they decide to seek 
refuge here

LDO5S1632 Tripoli JANA in English 1413 GMT 5 Dec 43 
(“JANA Editorial on US. Air Raids” — JANA headline] 

[Text] Tripoli, Safar 30, Dec 5, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY The political editor of the JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY issued a commentary on the US. air raids 
launched yesterday morning by 2% aircraft and by direct orders 


from President Reagan on Syrian and progressive forces positions 
in Lebanon. 


He said “these concentrated air raids have come only days after 
the signing of the U_S -Isracli strategic cooperation protocol, and 
ate seen as the concrete result of the aggressive intentions 
underlying this agreement in a bid to enable the imperialist- 
Zionist alliance to impose total control over Lebanon and to 
transform the country to a launch pad for aggression against the 
Arab nation. 


Following the Zionist raid on Syrian positions in Lebanon, 
American aircraft carried out similar raid thus practically 
translating the imperialist-Zionist alliance by exerting military 
pressure on Syria to force it into withdrawing its forces from 
Lebanon and leaving this Arab country clear for U.S. and Zionist 
forces. 


US. policy has now dangerously raised the threat of war in the 
region. The dangerous military aspect of this policy of escalation 
means that America has taken it upon itself to fight directly 
instead of leaving the fighting to its Zionist ally. This has created 
a new cauldron of tension in the region and will have devastating 
effects on the region's peoples and dangerous repercussions for 
world peace and security 


Confrontation between the Arab nation on one hand and the U.S 
Administration and its Zionist ally on the other, has left the 
Arabs without choice but to engage in mortal self-defence and to 
utilize all resources in confronting imperialist- Zionist aggression 
which is being escalated with intent day by day. 


Whatever its might, America can be defeated if confronted. The 
downing of three of its military aircraft by Syrian air defence is 
a witness, and more American aircraft can be shot down and 
America’s face can be bloodied. 


Weare facing a dangerous form of aggression in which advanced 
weapons of warfare are being used. We should be clearly aware 
that this aggression is being directed at Syria and the Arab 
nation, and it is therefore the Arab nation’s duty to stand beside
