[Text] Riyadh, 9 May (SPA)--An official Saudi source has confirmed taat the 
reports published by the Libyan-financed Lebanese newspaper AL-KIFAH =btout the 
Saudi Acabian kingdom's attitude toward the Palestine question are no more than 
lies and fabrications akin to those propagated by the Libyan information media. 


In reply to an SPA question the source said in commenting on these reports 
that despite the clarity of the kingdom's policy and its repeated affirmations 
through Crown Prince Fahd Ibn ‘Abd al-‘Aziz to the effect that the Palestine

the source said, affirming that since its foundation the kingdom has pursued 
a clear policy toward the Palestine question, and the soundness and clarity 
of its policy need no proof since it is supported by the Palestinians them-

SAUDI PAPER DESCRIBES AL-QADHDHAFI AS ‘INSANE’ 
LDO71506 Riyadh SPA in English 1224 GMT 7 May 82 


[Text] Riyadh, 7 May (SPA)--The Jidda-based newspaper AL-MADINA today accused 
Liv»yan leader Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi of planning to incense war in all 
parts of Africa and send troops to “occupy” Chad again. 


Describing al-Qadhdhafi as insane, criminal and atheist, the paper said "it 
is circumstance alone which now protect al-Qadhdhafi from the just punishment 
he deserves. 


"However, the countdown has begun and punishment is inevitable," it added. 


It went on to say that "as part of his conspiracies, al-Qadhdhafi is preparing 
to incense the conflict in all parts of Africa, and the latest development is 
that al-Qadhdhafi will send his oppressed army to occupy Chad once more." 


The paper said al-Qadhdhafi could continue to embark on new moves "until God 
saves the Libyan people form this mishap, which in fact is also that of the 
whole (Arab) nation." 


In a front-paged report, the same paper quoted “authoritative African sources" 
in London as saying that Libyan troops were preparing to return to Chad. 


It said that Chad President Goukouni Oueddei had a meeting recently with 
al-Qadhdhafi's chief aid Major ‘Abd al-Salam Jallud to discuss "ways and means 
of ensuring the return of Libyans" to his country. 


According to the same sources, the conflicting groups in Chad had begun seek- 
ing foreign support in view of the impending withdrawal of African peace forces 
from Chad in mid-June. 


The sources said Goukouni would not have resorted to the new move had he still 
depended on French assistance. However, in the wake of military operations 

by the former Defence Minister Hussein Habre and his threat to the Chad 
capital Djamena, France was now more keen to conclude a deal between Habre and 
Goukouni to prevent another eruption of the civil war, the sources added.

RIYADH VIEWS AL-QADHDHAFI CRIMES IN LIBYA 
LD290838 Riyadh Domestic Service in Arabic 2000 GMT 28 Apr 82 
[Unattributed commentary] 


[Text] The national front for the salvation of Libya has ca!led on the Libyan 
people to get rid of al-Qadhdhafi by holding a political trial for him and his 
regime in view of the crimes his regime is committing against innocent citizens 
and against the Arab and Islamic nation and against human rights and justice. 


The front described Colonel al-Qadhdhafi as a war criminal. It urged the Lib- 
yan people to end this nightmare and to try him as a criminal inside Libya so 
that he would later be referred to an international criminal trial for crimes 
he committed against all mankind, as was the case with his counterparts-- 
criminals of World War II. 


In its statement the front produced a number of charges convicting al-Qadhdhafi 
for his violations and exposing his actions inside Libya. It said that al- 
Qadhdhafi is transgressing on the sanctities of the nation represented by its 
religion and faith in various forms and by distorting its history and cultural 
heritage. He also worked to destroy the judicial system by flagrantly inter- 
fering in its independence and by ending due legislative process of all safe- 
guards of justice and the abolition of the task of the lawyers. 





The national front for the salvation of Libya added that al-Cadhdhafi has 
also left his country without a constitution for the past 12 years. Moreover 
he changed the education curricula and programs so that it would be in line 
with his aims and serve the objectives of his masters in the Kremlin. 


The front said tinat al-Qadhdhafi's unethical practices inside Libya and the 
transgression on the basic rights of all Libyan citizens and the suppression 
of their freedom, chiefly the freedom of thought, meeting and expression, was 
embodied in the opening of hundreds of detention places and in the exercising 
of the ugliest forms of torture from which not a single home inside Muslim 
Libya has escaped. 


The national front for the salvation of Libya concluded its statement by call- 
ing on the Libyan people te end this rule which practiced destruction, terror- 
ism and killed its own people, its neighbours and worked against the African 
continent and against all mankind. It described the present state in Libya 

by comparing it with the evils of World War II and indeed it might be more 
serious because of al-Qadhdhafi's threats to the world security, thought and 
stability.
