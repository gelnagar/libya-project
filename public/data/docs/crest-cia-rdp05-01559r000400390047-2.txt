[Text] The brother leader of the revolution hosted a-banquet last 
night in honor of Nicolae Ceausescu, secretary general of the 
Communist Party of Romania and president of the Socialist 
Republic of Romania, and his accompanying delegation. In 
attendance were Brigadier General Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, com- 
mander in chief of the Libyan Armed Forces; Major al- 
Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi; the secretary and members of the 
General People’s Committee; and the heads of diplomatic mis- 
sions accredited to the Jamahiriyah. 

The brother leader of the revolution delivered a speech in which 
he greeted President Ceausescu and his accompanying del- 
egation. He said: It gives me pleasure to warmly welcome our 
great friend and guest, President Nicolae Ceausescu, his wife and 
the high-ranking delegation accompanying him. We deeply 
appreciate this visit and hope that it will boost our bilateral 
cooperation. I would like to express my gratitude for the devel- 
opment of our bilateral relations, which have made splendid 
progress in all areas. 

The brother leader added: I thank our friend Ceausescu and his 
wife for accepting our invitation and making this visit. Relations 
between our two countries have made tangible progress as a 
result of reciprocal summit-level visits. In addition, our 
friendship has been strengthened. We hope that this friendship 
will be further strengthened by this visit, which will permit us to 
exchange views on complicated international issues. 

The Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, which plays an 
important role in the Nonaligned Movement, and Romania, 
which is noted for its outstanding international activity in world 
policy, can only benefit the general international situation when 
they meet to allay tension and contribute to the resolution of 
current grave international problems, the brother leader said. 

The brother leader continued: We certainly share a common 
concern over the serious deterioration in international relations 
resulting from the imperialist policy pursued by the capitalist 
camp. There is the problem of the serious arms race, which has 
now reached the point of involving weapons in space. We must 
all participate in ensuring the success of the upcoming talks 
between the great powers, which are seeking to place weapons in 
space. 

The brother leader said that the Gulf war, the Middle East war, 
and the Malvinas war are indications of the deterioration pre- 
viously noted. The people in these areas are living in a state of 
war anid are suffering from the resulting ordeals. There is also 
the war in East Asia. Thus, there is no peace. The racist policy 
in Africa and the racist Zionist policy in the Middle East have 
created constant wars, from which the people, who seek to build 
their economies in a stable atmosphere, are suffering. 

The brother leader noted that the most volatile areas are the 
Middle East and the Gulf. He said that the imperialist forces are 
responsible for these wars. He said: We believe that the attempts 
now being made to achieve peace in the Middle East are doomed
