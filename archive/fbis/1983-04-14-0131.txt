LIBYA

Oil Secretary Ends Visit to USSR 12 April 
LD132019 Tripoli JANA in English 1810 GMT 13 Apr 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada Thani 30, Apr 13, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY— Mr Kamil Hasan al-Maghur, the secretary 
of oil, yesterday ended a visit to the Soviet Union during which 
he held talks with the Soviet minister of foreign trade centered 
on furthering bilateral cooperation. 


Armed Forces Chief Receives Greek Delegation 
LD1 2452 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 
1230 GMT 12 Apr 83 


[Texi] Maj al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi, deputy chief of the Gen- 
eral Staff of the Armed Forces, has received the director of the 
Greek naval staff college and the accompanying delegation. 


During this meeting the major stressed the need to exchange and 
to continue the visits to strengthen the friendship linking the 
Libyan Arab people and the Greek people, and expressed his 
welcome of this visit. He explained to the Greek militiary del- 
egation about the Jamahiriyah military establishment, saying: 
We have chosen to build our might through the revolution to 
bolster our freedom. The Libyan Arab people, through their men 
and women, are training in arms, are capable of proving their 
existence, and are ready to defend their territory and honor and 
to defend freedom-loving people that are being (? oppressed) and 
dominated by imperialist power struck by power madness 


14 Apr 83 


He added: We refuse to be browbeaten or to surrender to 
America because we are revolutionaries; we have rebelled for the 
sake of freedom and have expelled the foreign bases that were on 
our territory. We decided to live in dignity or die with honor 


Maj al-Humaydi pointed to the presence of foreign bases on the 
territory of many countries. The peoples of these countries are 
robbed of their will and lack of freedom. [Words indistinct] some 
units of the U.S. fleet set sail to provoke the Libyan Arab people 
from some of these bases tn the countries bordering the Mediter- 
ranean in an endeavor by the United States to create a spirit of 
hostility between the Libyan Arab people and these peoples. He 
added: We salute the enormous popular awakening of some of 
the peoples who are demanding the eviction of the American 
bases from their territories. This worldwide popular hostility 
against U.S. military presence is nothing but proof of America’s 
[word indistinct} the hatred of the peoples of the world for it 


The major also said: The provocative U.S. attempts against our 
people in the Mediterranean and the attempts at economic and 
scientific blockade only increase our conviction that we are 
proceeding on the correct path. Let the terrorists learn that the 
Gulf of Sidra is death, because it is part of the land mass of the 
Libyan Arab territory, and that we are ready for confrontation 
He went on: We are against any capitulation attempts by reac- 
tionary Arab countries on Arab territory. This does not weaken 
our resolve because we are determined to confront the enemy 
anywhere to back freedom and free people and to face the Zionist 
death train backed by the United States
