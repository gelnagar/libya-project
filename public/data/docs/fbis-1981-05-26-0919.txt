AL-QADHDHAFI, “ARAFAT MEET, ISSUE STATEMENT 


LD242058 Triroli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 24 May 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 May (JANA)--A statement was issued this evening in Sebha following 
the meeting between brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and Chairman of the PLO Executive 
Committee Yasir ‘Arafat, on President Anwar as-Sadat's visit to Sudan. The statement 


says: 


At these historic moments, when our Arab nation and national fate are being exposed to 

the most severe imperialist and Zionist dangers and threats directed against Arab stead- 
fastness; at a time when the Zionist enemy, with the direct backing and support of the 
United States, is escalating the war of annihilation against the Lebanese and Palestinian 
peoples, and when the threat of aggression are continuing against sister Syria and against 
the positive pan-Arab role played by the Arab Deterrent Forces in Lebanon; at a time when 
the imperialist and Zionist adventurous policy in the Arab region constitutes a threat 

to the security and national honor of the Arab region and also a threat to world peace and 
security, agent Anwar as-Sadat visited his comrade-agent Ja'far Numayri in an attempt to 
perform another act in the serial of treason against their two peoples and against the 
higher interest of the Arab nation. Brother Mu'‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 

1 September Revolution, and Yasir ‘Arafat, chairman of the PLO Executive Committee and 
commander in chief of the Palestinian revolution forces, studied the dangers of this 

visit and its consequences. The two leaders also studied the Arab situation and the 
latest developments of Palestinian, Arab and international events. They agreed on the 
following: 


1. To consider Nemayri's invitation to As-Sadat to visit Khartoum as a flagrant violation 
of the resolutions of the Baghdad and Tunis summits, and also an open defiance of the 
feelings of the people in both Sudan and Egypt and in all the countries of the Arab home- 
land, in a view of the negative consequences which this invitation and this visit con- 
stitute to the Palestinian cause in particular and the Arab cause in general. 


2. Numayri's invitation to As-Sadat and As-Sadat's visit to Khartoum constitute a falsi- 
fication by Numayri of the will of the struggling fraternal Sudanese people, since wel- 
coming an agent like As-Sadat on Sudan's pure soil is tantamount to an endorsement by 
Numayri and his regime of the pan-Arab crimes which As-Sadat is exercising against the 
Palestinian people and their sacred cause and also against the Arab nation. 


3. The gravity of this visit lies in the fact that it constitutes a deviation and a 
tacit recognition by Numayri of dealing with the arrogant Zionisi enemy, which conflicts 
with the real interests of Sudan and also the higher interests of all the Arabs. 


4. Numayri's reception of As-Sadat and his continuing to deal with him requires imple- 
mentation of the resolutions of the Baghdad summit and imposition of the sanctions which 
were imposed on As-Sadat's regime, including the suspension of Sudan's membership in the 
Arab league, severing diplomatic and political relations with Sudan and applying the rules 
of the Arab boycott to Sudan. 


5. Numayri's reception of As-Sadat, who has betrayed Islamic [word indistinct] and sanc- 
tities--primarily holy Jerusalem--is a provocative action against the feelings of all 
Arabs and Muslims. Therefore, it is our Islamic duty to apply that which was agreed upon 
at the Islamic congress against As-Sadat to Numayri's regime. 


6. The Great 1 September Revolution and the Palestinian revolution stress their determin- 
ation to firmly and practically oppose this new effort to execute the Camp David plot and 
the Israeli-Egyptian treaty. They declare their full support and solidarity with the 
fraternal Sudanese people and their armed patriotic forces which cannot remain silent about 
this criminal visit; they are convinced of their ability to inflict the appropriate punish- 
ment on those committing this crime.

In the light of the dangers the Palestinian revolution and the Arab region are experi- 
encing, the two sides stress the following: 


1. To call on all Arab states to extend facilities to the Palestinians who are working 
in these countries and to whom the resolution of the Palestinian National Council 
regarding general mobilization applies, to give them the necessary facilities to join 
the forces of the Palestinian revolution and also to ensure their rights in coucdination 
between these sta _es and the PLO. 


2. To open all the Arab fronts for the fighters of the Palestinian revolution and to 
extend all facilities for the consolidation of the armed struggle inside the occupied 
Palestinian territory, and also to permit Arabs to volunteer to join the Palestinian 

revolution and its armed forces. 


3. The United States of America must take full responsibility for the aggressive 

Israeli practices against the Palestinian revolution, against the Lebanese and Palestinian 
peoples and against sister Syria, in which the most modern U.S. weapons are being used, 
including those which are banned internationally, against the Palestinian and Lebanese 
people and with the open support of the U.S. administration. In this respect, the Arab 
states must form a firm, practical and insistent stand against the United States and its 
interests in the Arab region, and to treat the United States on the basis that it is a 
partner of the Zionist enemy in its attacks on the Palestinian and Lebanese people and 

the Arab nation. 


4. Absolute support for sister Syria in its steadfast stand against the Israeli threats 
and attacks, which are being carried out with the support and encouragement of the United 
States, and in implementation of its policy in the Arab region. To underline the full 
support for the Arab Deterrent Forces and their positive pan-Arab role in Lebanon in the 
interest of Lebanon, its unity, sovereignty and independence and also in the interest of 
protecting the Palestinian revolution. 


5. To call on the Arab states to adopt a practical stand--materially, politically and 
militarily--on the side of the Palestinian revolution and Syria in their opposition to 
the threats and the war of annhilation which the Israeli enemy is practising against 
them; to invoke the joint defense treaty in order to support them and to participate with 
them in the defense of the honor and dignity of the Arab nation, and to send their armed 
forces to the battlefields. 


6. The adventurous American-Zionist policy in the Arab region is a practical realization 
of the Camp David policy and accords and of the Egyptian-Israeli treaty which the Arab 
nation has rejected. It has expressed full determination to oppose their results and 
effects until they are foiled.
