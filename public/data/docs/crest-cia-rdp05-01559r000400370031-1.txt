Summary (AI): FBIS translations of Libyan media reports from the General People's Congress in Tripoli (18 January 1976). Qadhdhafi announced that popular committees would continue for at least three more years, a military draft to fill armed forces shortfalls in 1976, the rehiring of retired civil servants, expanded foreign investment, and an extension of Revolutionary Command Council law-making powers until the next congress session.
• Popular committees to continue for at least 3 more years, per Qadhdhafi at the General People's Congress in Tripoli on 18 January 1976
• Congress approved national defence clause and a draft to fill armed forces shortage, with training in 1976 for weapons to be imported under the coming five-year plan
• Retired civil service officials to be considered for reinstatement
• Congress voted unanimously to study amending the constitutional declaration to give the Congress legislative powers
• 1976-01-18: General People's Congress session: Qadhdhafi says popular committees will continue at least 3 years; military draft approved for 1976; RCC authorised to keep enacting laws until next session
