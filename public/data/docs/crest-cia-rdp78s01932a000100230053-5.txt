Summary (AI): A April 1974 White House Situation Room message reports that Libyan troops were airlifted to Kampala, Uganda, to protect Idi Amin from army units of doubtful loyalty, and that Libyan and Ugandan soldiers clashed. It has no connection to Tripoli itself.
• Message dated 22 April 1974, item WH-S, White House Situation Room.
• Reports said the Libyan contingent was airlifted to Kampala on about 8 April and its size was reported as growing.
• Uganda Radio broadcast on about 11 April said Amin had denied the troop presence.
• Serious friction reportedly occurred between Libyan and Ugandan soldiers.
• Context: tensions in the Ugandan army since late 1972, when Amin was coping with an exile invasion from Tanzania.
• 1974-04-08: Libyan soldiers airlifted to Kampala to provide protection for Idi Amin against army units of doubtful loyalty
