LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Message From Junblatt 
LD151712 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 
15 Apr 84 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution received a message 
from Brother Walid Junblatt, chairman of the Progressive 
Socialist Party in Lebanon, which says: 


The relations of brotherhood and joint struggle that link the 
Jamahiriyah and the Progressive Socialist Party are pan-Arab 
and include destiny strengthened by embarking on the common 
battle in defense of the integrity of his nation and taking respon- 
sibility for its protection. In our opinion, the pan-Arab battle is 
an undivided whole and Arab blood is the same wherever it flows, 
as long it protects Arab honor. As a revolutionary who has fought 
imperialism and paid the price for the integrity and dignity of 
your country and people, you undoubtedly appreciate its value. 


The victories achieved in Lebanon with the blood of its sons are 
largely a result of the absolute political, moral, and material 
support offered by the Jamahiriyah under your courageous rev- 
olutionary leadership, to which the most brilliant pages of con- 
temporary Arab history will be devoted, and which will not be 
forgotten by the coming Arab generations when they feel the 
taste of freedom, dignity, and peace. In the profusion of our 
struggle on this front, where the blood of thousands of martyrs 
has flown, we stress to you that we are following up your struggle 
in confronting imperialist and reactionary forces and we are at 
constant and absolute readiness to join the battie beside you. 


Egyptian Defense Minister’s ‘Braggery’ Hides Fear 
LD141646 Tripoli JANA in English 1458 GMT 14 Apr 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Rajab 14, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY 

- The Egyptian Defence Minister Abu Ghazalah has boasted 
that Egypt has enough forces in the western desert to secure its 
borders with Libya. 


At the same time, Abu Ghazalah admitted to press correspon- 
dents who were covering the U.S.-Egyptian joint military 
manoeuvres near the Suez Canal that Egypt has hundreds of 
troops in Sudan. 


Abu Ghazalah’s braggery over the Egyptian regime's possession 
of enough troops on the Egyptian-Libyan border can only be 
justified by the fact that the Egyptian regime fully realizes the 
truth of the rising revolutionary tide in Libya to confront the 
disgraceful situation arising from the conclusion of the treaty of 
shame and dishonour between regime and the Zionist enemy and 
all subservient policies adopted by pro-American regimes. 


Thus, the Egyptian regime’s military mobilization is an expres- 
sion of its fear and terror.
