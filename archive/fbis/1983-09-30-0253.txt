Habre Forces Moving North, Clash Expected 


LD291742 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1650 GMT 29 Sep 83 


{Text} Information coming from Chad has confirmed that the 
forces of rebel Hissein Habre are now moving from the region of 
Maou northwards in order to cut off the road between Zouaou 
and Faya. It was learned that, in order to face up to this military 
development resulting from the movement of rebel Habre, the 
forces of the legitimate government of national unity led by 
Goukouni Ouedde: have begun to prepare for the battle to 
confront the forces of rebel Habre and foil their dubious move 
ment. Political observers affirm that the situation which has been 
calm recently has deteriorated again due to the movement of the 
forces of the rebel in Ndjamena exploiting the current calm 
period in order to fulfill military objectives. Observers do not rule 
out a fresh explosion of the situation at any time
