AL-QADHDHAFI LINKED WITH SOMALI OGADEN WITHDRAWAL 
AU210905Y Rome ANSA in English 0845 GMT 21 Mar 78 AU 


[Text] Taormina, March 21 (ANSA)--Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi reportedly had 

a hand in Somalia's move to withdraw its troops from the Ogaden, according to Jamahiriyah 
Foreign Information Chief Hibrahim El Dishai [name as received]. The assertion came 
during a news conference in this Sicilian city yesterday in conjunction with the press 
release of the Italian edition of part two of Al-Qadhdhafi's "green book" displaying 

the Libyan leader's political doctrine. 


According to Dishai, Somali President Siad Barre decided to pull his country's troops 

out of the Ethiopian region and to announce the move publicly during a meeting with 
Al-Qadhdhafi in Tripoli at the beginning of the month. Restating his government's stand 
on the Horn of Africa, the Libyan spokesman urged a negotiated settlement between the 

two sides within the realm of the Organization of African Unity [OAU]. By the same 
token, Libya would like to see the Eritrean question settled peac2fully with negotiations 
between the two sides that led to a settlement that took the right of Eritreans to decide 
their own destiny into account. 


Dishai said that Libya had supported the Eritrean cause for years, when "the secessionist 
struggle was a progressive battle against the imperialist Selassie regime." "Now we 

are maintaining contact with both sides and we believe in a negotiated settlement," he 
added, asserting that the decision between independence and regional autonomy could only 
come from direct talks. 


In a reference to the Middle East conflict, the Libyan spokesman charged that Egyptian 
President Anwar as-Sadat, "a traitor to the Arab cause," was involved in "an imperialist 
plot" and deplored the massacres allegedly waged by Israeli troops in southern Lebanon. 
Dishai added that as things now stand, the only path for solving the Palestine problem 
was that of armed struggle.

{Text] Tripoli, March 16, JANA--A statement was issued by the preparatory committee 
for the symposium on the Arab-American talks on the popular level to be held in Tripoli. 


The statement announced that Zionism had taken the opportunity of the Arab absence from 
the American arena and remoteness from the American people to spread depravity.. It had 
alsc found the opportunity first to distort the Arab people's image in the eye of the 
American public opinion “hrough its control over the American media, picturing the 
American society as inimical to the just causes of the Arab nation, and second, to 
create a barrier preventing any contact between the Arab and American people in order 
to serve the colonialist and racist interests of Zionism. 


The statement stressed that popular movements should overcome all obstacles and run the 
blockade imposed by Zionist.and colonialist institutions so that they could convey their 
aims to the nations of the world without fanaticism or a sense of inferiority. 


Therefore, since the Jamahiriyah is the pioneer of the era of the masses, based on the 
principles of the Fatah revolution of September and fully confident of its power and 
determination to over come the obstacles hindering the contact of the Arab people with 
the other nations of the world regardless of the official policies of the governments 

of these nations. The Jamahiriyah will spare no effort in contacting all nations either 
in the West or East in order to build bridges on the popular level based on mutual 
respect, peace and justice. 


The statement added that through the contacts on the popular level which had taken place 
between the Libyan and American delegations, it was crystal clear that there were great 
possibilities to establish useful Arab-American relations on the popular level in 
political, economic and cultural fields, to the benefit of the joint interest of the 
Arab and American people as well as to that of peace and justice all over the world. 
Accordingly it has been agreed to hold a symposium on talks on popular level which 

will be attended by a number of American and Arab eminent persons. At this symposium 
political, economic, cultural and information issues will be discussed in order to lay 
don the foundation for Arab-American relations based on mutual respect and joint 
interests. 


The preparatory committee held its first session in Tripoli on 15 and 16 March to or- 
ganise the symposium due to open in Tripoli next October. "We hold these talks 

with the American people because our struggle against Zionism and the enemies of the 
Arab nations is not restricted to one area but should be conducted on all fronts and 
levels; so that this just struggle will be crowned with the victory of peace based 

on justice which will achieve freedom to all the people in struggle."
