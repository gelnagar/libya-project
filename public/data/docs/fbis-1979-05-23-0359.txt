JANA DENIES REPORTED MEETING BETWEEN LIBYAN, SUDANESE FFICIALS 
LD220844 Tripoli ANA in English 1749 GMT 21 May 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, May 21, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCYe-Quoting Foreign Secretariat sources 
JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY stated that the news reported by the IRAQI NEWS AGENCY about a 
meeting between the foreign secretary and Mr. ar-Rashid at-Tahir Bakr, the Sudanese vicee 
president and minister of foreign affairs, was groundless. 


The sources of the Foreign Secretariat also denied the arrival of Mr. ar-Rashid at-Tahir 
Bakr in the Jamahiriyah and said that he left the Jamahiriyah on 13th May after a two-day 
visit. 


PRC ISLAMIC DELEGATION CONCLUDES VISIT, DEPARTS 
LD221136 Tripoli JANA in English 0810 GMT 22 May 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, May 22, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Islamic Chinese delegation left 
Tripoli yesterday after a visit to the Jamahiriyah which lasted several days. Some members 
of the Islamic Call Society in the Jamahiriyah and the charge d'affaires at the Chinese 
Embassy saw the delegation off. 


The deputy chairman of the Islamic Chinese Society and member of the People's Council and 
head of the Islamic Chinese delegation made a statement to our correspondent praising the 
role played by the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and by the leader of the 
Great lst of September Revolution, Colonel Mu'ammar aleQadhdhafi, in supporting Islam and 
Muslims all over the world and upholding their cause. 


He stressed the importance of increased acquaintance and cooperation between the Chinese 
Muslims and people and the Muslim Arab people of the Jamahiriyah.

set up in the form of the illegal fascist government imposed on the people of Chad.

waddeimi and Hissene Habre to have been driven by ambition to take over power and control
