TEXT OF LIBYA-CHAD COMMUNIQUE ON GOUKOUNI VISIT 


LD061852 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 6 Jan 81 


{"Text" of joint communique on visit to Libya of Goukouni Oueddei, president of Chad, 
issued in Tripoli on 6 January] 


[Text] At the invitation of the brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the great 
Fatah revolution, the head of the Provisional National Unity Government of Chad and head 
of state, President Goukouni Oueddei, paid an official visit to the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah between 26-30 Safar 1390 since the death of the prophet, 
corresponding to 2-6 January 1981, heading an important government delegation. 


The guest and accompanying delegation were accorded a great, fraternal welcome which 
expressed the depth of fraternal ties between the two fraternal peoples. 


President Goukouni Oueddei to’k part in the opening session of the third session of the 
General People's Congress. He made an important speech dealing with the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah's pioneering role in supporting legality in Chad, and exposing the 
role of reaction and some agent regimes in fanning the flames of sedition among the ranks 
of the people of Chad. On the occasion, he denounced the dubious role of As-Sadat's regime 
and that of Numayri against the will of the Chadian people, when they sided with the rebel 
Habre and supported him militarily and materially, stressing that stand was an applica- 
tion of the plans of colonialism. This being the case, he stressed that harboring the 
rebel Habre in any place is regarded as an act of aggression against the Chadian people. 


Proceeding from the historic, cultural, human, racial, geographical and spiritual rela- 
tions between the two fraternal Arab Libyan and Chadian peoples, and out of their belief 
in the unity of the common fate of the two peoples and so as to embody the eternal 
principles of the great Fatah revolution and the Frolinat revolution, the two sides stress 
that relations between the two peoples have their roots in these historic, geographical 
and spiritual ties: both peoples fought colonialism in Libya and in Chad side by side; 
their blood was shed for the same cause. The two peoples have mixed together over a long 
period of history. Tens of thousands of Chadian people have lived in Libya and have 
become Libyans; tens of thousands of Libyans have lived in Chad and have become Chadians. 


Within the framework of the strategic alliance between the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah and Chad, important talks took place between the leaderships of both countries. 


During these talks, it was agreed: 
1. To work for the realization of complete unity between the two countries--a jamahiri 


[masses] unity in which authority, arms and resources are in the hands of the people; its 
(?foundation) is the people's congresses and committees. 


2. To support the strategic and fateful alliance between the two countries, and to regard 
any aggression on either as an aggression on the other; each country is prepared to fight 
on the side of the other in case of an aggression on either of them.

3. The two sides decided to continue to support the struggle of the peoples struggling 
for their freedom, and to escalate the struggle against colonialism and Zionism and 
reaction in Africa and the Arab homeland, as everywhere else in the world. 


4. The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah stresses the continuatior f its support 
for the fraternal Republic of Chad in order to ensure the freedom and independence of its 
people and to eliminate the remnants of agent reaction which cooperates with colonialism, 


inside and outside. 


5. Proceeding from the joint defense treaty signed between the two countries n 2 Sha in 
1389 since the death of the prophet, corresponding to 15 June 1980, and in accordance with

peace, which have been realized by the ending of the civil war, and also ti ssist in the 


rebuilding of a Chadian National Army and Chadian security forces 


6. Both sides stress their complete condemnation of reactionary, imperialist and Zionist 
attempts, which are supported by colonialism, in the region, and in particular the tw 
agent regimes in Egypt and Sudan, which seek to do harm to the victori« : red by tl 
Chadian people and to rekindle the flames of war and sedition in Chad. 


The activities which are being practiced by the Egyptian and Sudanese regime ind the 
Sudanese president's statement that war has now begun in Chad are regarded as an embarking 
on aggression against the Chadian people, and are preparatory agent measur for gression 
on Chad. What the two regimes are doing violates the principles of the OAU Charter and 

the Lagos agreement. In this connection, the two sides warn the Sudanese regime against 


the serious consequences of any aggressive act against Chad from Sucanese territory. The 
Sudanese regime shall be held responsible for the consequences. The Chadian people will 
not remain indifferent to what can be regarded as a threat to its security and safety. 


7. The Jamahiriyah stresses the continuation of and the increase in its economic support 
for sister Chad in order to rebuild Chad and realize prosperity and progress for the fraternal 
Chadian people. 


8. The two sides express full satisfaction with cooperation between the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and Chad, a matter which effectively contributed to the realization 
of peace and security in the sister Republic of Chad. 


9. The two sides express pleasure at the positive results which were achieved by th 
recent Lagos conference on the Chadian issue, stressing their commitment to the resolutions 
which were adopted by the conference and which stress without a shadow of doubt the ability 
of the Africans to resolve their problems by themselves without any foreign and colonialist 
intervention. 


In this regard, the two sides express thanks and appreciation for the valuable efforts 
which were exerted by the sister Nigerian Federal Republic and its President Almaji Shehu 
Shagari to realize peace in Chad, and also their appreciation of the current OAU chairman 
and Sierra Leone President, Siaka Stevens. 


10. The Chadian side highly appreciates the aid which the Jamahiriyah has given the Chad 
people, thanks to which the Chadian people were able to put an end to the rebels and to 
realize peace and security in Chad. 


1l. The two sides have decided to open the borders between the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah and the Chadian republic sc as to enable Libyan and Chadian citizens to 
move with full freedom and without any restrictions in order to realize cohesion and 
interaction between the two fraternal peoples.

12. The two sides stress the importance of supporting the legitimate government in Chad, 
it being the sole legitimate authority in Chad. They call on the African states and the 
international community to support this government, so as to enable it to rebuild Chad, 
and also to give it aid and to completely refrain from dealing with any side or sides out- 
side this government. 


13. The two sides stress their commitment to work to preserve security and stability in 
the region, and also to respect the political choices of the countries of the region and

The Chadian Government expresses the desire to promote its relations with all states,

CHAD PRESIDENT HOLDS PRESS CONFERENCE, DEPARTS 
LD061350 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 6 Jan 81 


[Text] The chairman of the Transitional National Union Government and president of the 
Republic of Chad, Goukouni Oueddei, and the delegation accompanying him left Tripoli this 
morning following a visit to the Jamahiriyah. President Goukouni was seen off at Tripoli 
international airport by Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud. The foreign secretary, 
the secretary of the general people's committee for reclamation of land and members of 
the political bodies accredited to the Jamahiriyah were at the airport to see him off. 


President Goukouni Oueddei has stressed that the people of Chad and the Libyan Arab people 
are linked by firm relations and that they are determined to stand side by side to con- 
front all imperialist intrigues hatched by the reactionary agents. At a press conference 
at Tripoli international airport this morning before he left the Jamahiriyah, Goukouni 
expressed his profound appreciation for the backing and support extended by the Libyan 
people to the fraternal people of Chad in rebuilding their national unity. 


In reply to a question by one of the journalists, the president said that the people of 
Chad, under the leadership of the FROLINAT, sacrificed thousands of their sons when France 
helped former reactionary governments which ruled Chad before the government of Tombalbaye 
and Felix Maloum. He explained that the people of Chad fiercefully fought against the 
French presence and that they do not and will not permit the French to use their country 
as a launching base and will not allow French forces in Chad after the victory of the 
revolution. 


The president of Chad expressed his satisfaction with the good results achieved by the 
recent Lagos conference. He said: I salute the stance of the Libyan Arab people and 
their stand by the side of the people of Chad for the liberation of their land and the 
eviction of the forces backed by colonialism and reaction which lit the fire of sedition 
in Chad and are trying to ignite Africa. President Goukouni Oueddei also saluted the 
stance of the people of Nigeria. The president of Chad said the rebel Habre, before all 
of his remnants were driven off, had rejected all agreements and reconciliation in order 
to restore unity to Chad, which has now been achieved thanks to our complete victory.

The General People's Congress secretary proposed the formation of a new secretariat for 

an important sector, namely of atomic energy for peaceful purposes. The secretary ex-

Syrian Official's Address 
JNO62134 Damascus Domestic Service in Arabic 1615 GMT 6 Jan 81 


[Text] Comrade Tawfiq Salihah, Ba'th Party Regional Command Member and head of the party's 
delegation to the General People's Congress, delivered a speech in today's session which 
began by conveying greetings from the Arab Socialist Ba'th Party and its secretary gen- 
eral, President Hafiz al-Asad, to the conferees, the fraternal Libyan people and leader 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. 


Comrade Salihah reviewed the circumstances in which the party was established, the stages 
through which it has passed and its role in the 1958 unity between fraternal Syria and 
Egypt. He also talked about the stage the party passed through after the Syrian revolu- 
tion, particularly after the corrective movement under President Hafiz al-Asad's leader- 
ship, and about the party's determination and struggle for Arab unity and for any unitary 
Arab meeting. 


Tawfiq also spoke about Syria's steadfastness and confrontation of the imperialist designs 
in the region, particularly the Camp David process and its agent tools. He asserted that 
the Arab masses in fraternal Libya and Syria hope to consolidate the Steadfastness and 
Confrontation Front and are determined to achieve unity, which will be the first step 
toward the greater unity and a firm reply to the imperialist and Zionist designs.
