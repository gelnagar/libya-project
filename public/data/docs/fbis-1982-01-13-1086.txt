LIBYA, IRAN SIGN OIL COOPERATION AGREEMENT 


LD121538 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 12 Jan 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 12 Jan (JANA) -- The agreement for oil cooperation between the national 
oil establishment and the Iranian oil company was signed at noon today at the national 

oil establishment. The agreement stipulates the development of technical and technological 
cooperation in the field of oil between the national oil establishment and the Iranian 

oil company. 


The Iranian delegation, which is currently visiting the Jamahiriyah, visited a number 
of oil installations, hailed the Libyan Arab technical ability in oil and praised the 
achievements realized by the Libyan Arab people in this field.

STUDENT RALLY TO COMMEMORATE 1964 UPRISING 


LD130500 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0015 GMT 13 Jan 82 


[Text] Libyan students today will mark the 18th anniversary of the events of 13 and 14 
January 1964, when the students of universities and colleges faced the bullets of treachery 
and treason and dmonstrated against the defunct reactionary regime and its treasonous atti- 
tudes toward our Arab nation and its destiny. A number of martyrs were killed that day in 
1964 at the hands of the corrupt royalist clique which, at the behest of its imperialist 
masters, ordered its police force to strike at the revolutionary student forces. The 
students’ tumultuous uprising reflected the nobility of the Libyan Arab people, their 
pan-Arab awareness and their total rejection of all the reactionary attempts by the defunct 
royalist regime to put them on the imperialist bandwagon and to haul them away from the 
Arab nation. 


The 7 October Square will witness a great student rally to mark this occasion.
