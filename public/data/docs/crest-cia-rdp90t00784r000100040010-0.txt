Summary (AI): A December 1982 NPIC briefing note reports a newly identified field-deployed SA-2 surface-to-air missile site near Sebha, in central Libya, probably defending Sebha airfield about 3 nautical miles away. The site has six SA-2 launchers, a Fan Song guidance radar and a Spoon Rest early-warning radar. Sebha is far from Tripoli, so this is only indirectly relevant to the Tripoli map.
• Site has six SA-2 launchers, one Fan Song guidance control radar with associated vans, and one Spoon Rest early warning radar.
• Two SA-3 SAM sites were already deployed in the Sebha area.
• The first operational SA-2 site identified near Sebha.
• Document is a National Photographic Interpretation Center briefing attachment dated December 1982.
• 1981-08: SA-2 equipment first present in the Sebha area

1. (SWN) SIGNIFICANCE: A FIELD-DEPLOYED SA-2 SURFACE-TO-AIR 
MISSILE (SAM) SITE IS NEWLY IDENTIFIED IN THE SEBHA AREA AT 
27-00-50N 014-31-45E AND IS THE FIRST OPERATIONAL SA-2 SITE TO 
BE IDENTIFIED NEAR SEBHA. 

2. (SWN) REMARKS: THE SITE IS PROBABLY DEPLOYED IN DEFENSE OF 
SEBHA AIRFIELD APPROXIMATELY 3 NM TO THE 25X1

SONG GUIDANCE CONTROL RADAR AND ASSOCIATED VANS, AND ONE SPOON 
REST EARLY WARNING RADAR. SA-2 EQUIPMENT HAS BEEN PRESENT IN 
THE SEBHA AREA SINCE AUGUST 1981.
