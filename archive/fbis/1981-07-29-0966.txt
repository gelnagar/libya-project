LIBYA

U.S. 'OPERATION' AGAINST AL-QADHDHAFI REPORTED 


LD290900 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 29 Jul 81 


[Text] The U.S. Government implicitly admitted the existence of a secret American terroristic 
subversive plan aimed at toppling the commander of the revolution. Larry Speakes, official 
spokesman of the American black [as heard] house stated that the explanatory message which 
members of the Intelligence Committee of the U.S. House of Representatives recently sent to 
President Reagan was not connected with the plot which the CIA was reported to be hatching 
against the leadership of the Great 1 September Revolution. 


Speakes' remarks provide an implicit confirmation of the truth in reports carric2 by NEWSWEEK 
magazine--which is closely connected with the U.S. Government--in its latest edition on 

the existence of a secret American plan directed against the commander of the Great 1 
September Revolution. 


In its report, the magazine points out that the secret operation, which was revealed by 
former head of CIA secret services Hugel, won the approval of CIA chief William Casey 
himself. According to the magazine, American espionage circles may actually have begun 
to implement this secret terrorist subversive operation. Hugel, head of the CIA secret 
services, recently resigned because he opposed the American plau against the commander of 
the revolution. 


The CIA is one of the largest and most dangerous centers of intimidation and terrorism in 
the world. In its plans, it relies on bloodshed, treachery, assassination and the most 
abominable methods in fighting the struggling peoples and all the leaders and commands 

who refuse to bow their heads to the American terroristic provocations and their agent 
lackeys in the region. In the past, the CIA committed serious crimes against a large 

number of leaders and commanders who struggled for the freedom of their peoples. The 

CIA committed a treacherous act against Dr Kwame Nkrumah and then assassinated him. It also 
perpetrated an abominable assassination against Dr Salvador Allende. The CIA also hatched 
plots and terroristic intrigues against late President Jamal ‘Abd An-Nasir. 


Today, the CIA continues with its plots and open aggression against the Jamahiriyah people, 
the Nicaraguan people, the Palestinian people and the Lebanese people and against all the 
peoples who are determined to break the fetters and shackles and have decided to start a 
counteroffensive against the ferocious American imperialist offensive and its traitorous 
agents in the region, such as As-Sadat and Numayri. Hence, it is not strange at all for 
the world to see the United States adopt a treacherous stand against the commander of the 
revolution, who today is driving the Arab nation toward final emancipation and who is urging 
the Arab masses everywhere to stand up to the United States and abort all its plotting 
designs. 


The United States is exposed before the Free World, showing its true face as a terrorist 
country which adopts a criminal course contrary to international law and human relations. 


TASS Account Cited 
LD281738 Tripoli JANA in English 1440 GMT 28 Jul 81 


[Text] London, 28 July (JANA)--The Soviet news agency TASS said that the CIA has put a 

plan aimed at overthrowing Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great al-Fatah 
Revolution and liquidating him physically. The plan details of the CIA sabotage operation 

were published in the well-informed weekly magazine NEWSWEEK. It said, quoting Congress 
sources that this conspiracy consists of several stages aiming at overthrowing Colonel Mu-ammar 


al-Qadhdhafi.

It pointed out that this secret operation has been met with the approval of William Casey, 
C.I.A. director, and the White House. The plan is to spread fabricated lies and 
organising sabotage operations against Al-Qadhdhafi. 


Newsweek reported that the C.I.A. is engineering a conspiracy against the Jamahiriyah, 
and that C.I.A. officials expressed their deep concern towards Al-Qadhdhafi's independent 
foreign policy which is aimed at achieving unity among progressive countries. 

The magazine added that new C.I.A. officials refused to comment on the C.I.A. plan 
against Libya. The NEWSWEEK does not exclude the possibility that the C.I.A. has already 
started its operations. In fact, Washington has started a wide-scale propaganda campaign 
by spreading unfounded allegations under the cover of combatting international terrorism. 


The U.S. administration's aim is to mislead the American people and other countries in order 
to finance terrorist operations against the Libyan leadership. 


REUTER, on the other hand, said that William Casey, C.I.A. director has approved the plot 
of overthrowing the leader of the revolution. Thus, the U.S. hostile stand towards the 
Jamahiriyah is revealed and the public opinion is now well aware of the details of the 
U.S. imperialist plot against the achievements of the Libyan Arab people. 


The NEWSWEEK magazine, which has close links with U.S. administration, uncovers the 

dimensions of the U.S. terrorist policy and its hostility towards the anti-colonialist 
progressive countries and expose the official U.S. stand against the Great al-Fatah Revolution 
and its leader. 


EGYPT, SUDAN HOSTILITY, SAUDI OIL POLICY PROTESTED 


LD282229 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 28 Jul 81 


[Text] At his meeting the day before yesterday with military cadets studying abroad, 


the leader stressed that we are compelled to raise the degree of our friendship with 
friendly forces in the region to the level of alliance, in order to confront the 
aggressive U.S.-Israeli plan which is being implemented by their lackeys Numayri and 
As-Sadat within the framework of the U.S. aggressive plan aimed against the Arab nation, 
particularly the people of the Jamahiriyah. Numayri revealed with utmost stupidity 

this secret plan at the end of his talks with (?criminal) As-Sadat at a press conference 
reported by the Egyptian Government-owned news agency. The People's Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison will send notes to the Arab League, the OAU, and the UN General Assembly on the 
hostile attitudes of the Egyptian and Sudanese regimes to the people of the 

Jamahiriyah. 


The brother leader also pointed out the great economic harm inflicted by Saudi Arabia 
on the Libyan, Algerian, Iraqi and Nigerian peoples by dumping Saudi oil on the oil 
market and selling Saudi oil at very reduced prices, a matter which serves only the 
interests of the U.S., which uses this oil against the Arab nation. 


TRIPOLI DENOUNCES EGYPT, SUDAN 'SERVICE' TO U.S. 


LD282007 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 28 Jul 81 


[Text] The two lackey regimes in Egypt and Sudan have provided new evidence of their 
treachery and their submission to the United States: They have devoted their regimes 
to the service of the U.S. aggressive terrorist aims against the Arab nation and Africa. 


The ruler of Egypt last night openly revealed that he and his partner in treason and 
subservience, the ruler of Sudan, have officially agreed to provide the United States with 
parts of Egypt and Sudan for establishment of military bases. In a speech in 

Alexandria the ruler of Egypt said that he would inform U.S. President Ronald Reagan 

of this agreement during their meeting on 4 August.
