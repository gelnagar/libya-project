PAPER CONDEMNS U.S.-OMANI MILITARY AGREEMENT 
LD052020 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 05 Jul 80 


[Excerpts] JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY carries an article about the Sultanate of Oman and 
its ties with American imperialism. It says: 


Four days before the Omani people celebrated the 15th anniversary of the firing of the 
first shot by the Omani revolution against British colonialism, the traitor Qabus fired 
a bullet into the heart of the Arab nation when he announced on 5 June the establishment 
of a military alliance between the sultanate and the United States. Thus, Qabus has 
legitimized American movements in the Arabian Gulf region. 


The paper adds: In the sea off Omani shores and the Masirah Island base, there are 
nearly 30 American warships, including landing vessels, rocket-carrying destroyers, 
frigates and minesweepers. Moreover, there are in Oman itself more than 170 American 
military aircraft and nuclear weapons guarded by 12,000 American Marines.

The paper asks: What do these suspect moves in Oman and also on the Libyan borders 
mean? All reports indicate that the moves are made under joint coordination in accor- 
dance with the Camp David plans to place the Arab nation and in particular the oil 
regions under the threat of arms and direct invasion and thus secure their acceptance 
of the second stage of the Camp David conspiracy by the force of arms and blockading 
the progressive states in the region. 


Sa'd Haddad in southern Lebanon, As-Sadat on the Libyan borders and Qabus in Oman re- 
present three forward bases in the American war front against the Arab nation. The 
ultimate target of these open military moves is to facilitate other secret American 
moves to liquidate the Palestinian question and to defuse the Palestinian bomb which 
is too close to the Arab oilfields. This is the focus of U.S. attention, the basis 
of its strategy in the region and its chief motivator. 

The paper concludes: That is why the principled attitude of the Jamahiriyah toward 
this danger is clear and decisive. It calls for the expulsion of Qabus from the Arab 
League, for the application of boycott decisions similar to those which are in force 
against his colleague in subservience As-Sadat and his regime, and for supporting the 
Popular Front for the Liberation of Oman, which leads the struggle movement of the Oman 
Arab people.

FRIENDSHIP AGREEMENT WITH GDR--Tripoli, July 3, JAMAHIRTIYAH NEWS AGENCY--An agreement 
on a joint action between the Libyan Arab and Democratic German friendship societies 
has been signed at the Foreign Liaison Bureau last night. The agreement stipulates 
closer ties between both peoples in all sectors especially in culture and science. 
Both sides will exert all efforts to reinforce this friendship for the interest of 
the common objectives in support of all liberation causes and the oppressed in the 
world, side-by-side in one front against the colonialist plots. At the end of the 
meeting the Democratic German Friendship Society denounced the Egyptian military 
build up along the eastern borders of the Jamahiriyah, and which is considered as 
threat to the people's right in steadfastness and confrontation of the colonialist 
offensive. [Text] [Tripoli JANA in Mmglish 0900 GMT 3 Jul 80 LD]
