UK ‘Rancorous Crusade’ Hinders Britons’ Release 
EDO32031 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1930 GMT 3 Feb 85 


[Text] London, 3 Feb (JANA)-— A JANA correspondent in the 
British capital has reported, quoting the section which is looking 
after Libyan interests in Britain, the rancorous crusade act 
carried out by British Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher 
unveiling the so-called memorial plaque for the British 
policewoman — might hinder arrangements for the release of the 
four Britons detained in Libya. 


The section which ts looking after the Libyan interests added that 
this act 1s also considered counterproductive to the humanitarian 
efforts and endeavors which the Anglican Church in Britain 1s 
making for the release of the detained Britons.

LDO41!1S5!1 London PRESS ASSOCIATION in English 
1107 GMT 4 Feb 85 


[Text] The four Britons held in Libya must wait at least another 
24 hours for their freedom. The Libyan authorities today 
announced that the release has been delayed for 24 hours due to 
“unforseen circumstances,” the British consul in Tripoli Mr 
Hugh Dunnachie, told the PRESS ASSOCIATION by tele- 
phone 


The four men — teacher Mr Alan Russell, engineers Mr Robin 
Plummer and Mr Maicom Anderson and lecturer Mr Michael 
Berdinner had been expected to be released into the custody 
of the Archbishop of Canterbury's special envoy Mr Terry
