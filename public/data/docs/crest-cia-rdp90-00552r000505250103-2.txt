Summary (AI): A Wall Street Journal editorial (9 October 1981) argues that Qadhafi's Libya is a Soviet-backed threat behind terrorism and destabilization, and that the U.S. should consider covert action and deterrence against him. It mentions Sadat's assassination and Libya's role in it only by implication, and it has no specific facts about Tripoli.
• The editorial claims the Soviets provided Libya with several times the military equipment its army could use, Russian pilots, and East German bodyguards for Qadhafi.
• It attributes to Libya assassination squads sent after Sadat, support for terrorism, the invasion and conquest of Chad, and attacks on the Sudan.
• It mentions Libya as a nation of only two million people, as quoted from Kissinger.
• It recommends covert action in support of coups, military aid to the Sudan, and guarantees against Soviet retaliation if Egypt moves against Libya.
