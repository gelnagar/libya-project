JANA CRITICIZES SAUDI CALL FOR SUMMIT, FES AIM 


LD081428 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 8 Aug 82 


[Text] An invitation to convene an Arab summit conference, the venue and date of which 
have not been set, is in progress. The secretary general of the Arab League told the 
Saudi paper 'UKAZ that most Arab states will attend this summit. 


The JANA political editor comments on this summit: 


Talk about an Arab summit is still heard after Zionist artillery, tanks and ships have 
swept over Beirut and other parts of Lebanon, after the American war machine used by 

the Zionists has dealt a big blow to the Palestinian Resistance and after many countries 
did not respond to the summit call when the Zionist aggression was growing in strength 
and when these calls for a summit were practical calls for fighting. This introduction 
exposes the true aims toward which the American agent Fahd is moving by his talk now of 
an Arab summit. After the terrorist Begin carried out the military role assigned to 
him by America -- tostrike militarily at-the Palestinian Resistance -- now comes the 
role of the other U.S. tool, agent Fahd -- to strike at the Palestinian Resistance 
politically and to turn the Palestinian people into groups of destitute refugees.

It is futile and ridiculous to summon an Arab meeting to debate removing the Resistance 
instead of meeting to take practical measures to drive out the Zionist aggressor. Any 

Arab meeting that is not a meeting for confrontation and fighting will only make palatable 
and legitimize the killing and destruction of Palestinians and Arabs by the Zionist enemy. 
This is the height of treachery. The Arab masses are grown up and are maturely aware of 
all forms of' intrigue, trickery and misrepresentation. The main aim of the Fes summit about 
which they are talking is solely to impose the plan of the traitor Fahd, the pig, which 

was cursed by the Arab masses and whose author was cursed. Fahd considers the time right 

to carry throughhis plan, after the terrorist Begin has hit the Resistance and paved the 
way for the cortege of capitulation led by Fahd. 


This confirms one basic fact: Begin and Fahd are twins, Reagan is the horse pulling the 
cart of the destruction of the Arab nation and the Palestinian question and Fahd and Begin 
are the wheels of this aggressive cart. 


We say one final word with which we challenge Fahd and those who walk behind him: We say, 
come to a manly Arab fighting meeting, the aim of which is to put out land and air power 
into motion to repulse the Zionist aggression and to drive it our forcefully from the 
territory it occupies. If the rulers go to Fes to endorse his Zionist deed at the expense 
of the Palestinian Resistance in Beirut, the Arab masses will march to Fes to destroy the 
idols of atheism. 


BRIEFS 


AGREEMENT WITH GUINEA-BISSAU -- An agreement was signed in Tripoli today for setting up a 
joint holding company between the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and Guinea- 
Bissau, within the framework of consolidating relations and promoting cooperation between 
the two countries, stemming from the OAU Charter. One of the important aims of this 
company is to utilize the natural resources in Guinea-Bissau in a manner that will assist 
its economic development. Minutes of an agreement for economic, financial and cultural 
cooperation and for increasing cooperation in various fields also was signed. The agree- 
ment stipulates the setting up of a joint commission to meet periodically to follow up 
joint cooperation to develop relations between the two friendly states in various fields. 
The minutes of the agreement were signed by the secretary of the People's Committee of the 
People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison and the foreign minister of Guinea-Bissau. [Text]
