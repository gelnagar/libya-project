LIBYA

US. ‘Orders’ Sudan To Send More Troops to Chad 
LD271954 Tripoli JANA in English 1655 GMT 27 Jul 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 17, July 27, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The US. Administration has ordered its surrogate 
ruler Numayri to extend the scope of his military intervention in 
Chad by sending more Sudanese troops to help rebel Habre’s 
clique in Ndjamena. 


The official SUDANESE NEWS AGENCY reported that 
stooge Numayri today decreed a state of alert within the Army 
and appealed to African countries to follow his example and give 
effective aid to rebel Habre in his war against the legitimate 
national government led by Goukouni Oueddei. 


This blatant call by Numayri, who is executing the orders of his 
master Reagan, exposes his provocative intervention in Chad's 
internal affairs and his subservience to U.S. and Zionist imperial- 
ism in Chad aimed against the progressive countries and liber- 
ation movements in the continent. 


Accused of ‘Terrorist Ambitica’ 
LD272028 Tripoli JANA in English 1818 GMT 27 Jul 83 


[Text] Washington, Shawal 17, July 27, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — An American military official yesterday 
said that American military supplies to Chad were intended as a 
warning to Libya and Colone' Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi and that 
any increase in Libyan aid to the Chadian Government of 
national unity, led by Goukouni Oueddei, would be met by 
increased U.S. military assistance to rebel Habre. 


Another senior U.S. official said he believed that US. aid to 
Habre, valued at 10 million dollars, would be sufficient to put an 
end to Libyan aid to Goukouni. He said that American aid 
already earmarked for rebel Habre did not rule out further future 
assistance, adding “we do not know what Al-Qadhdhafi will do 
in the future”. 


These statements reaffirm the U.S. Administration's aggressive 
stance towards the Great Al Fatih Revolution and its leader, 
Colonel Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi. Reagan's administration and 
its satellites have decided to escalate their aggression and 


provocation against the Libyan Arab people. This time, however, 
they are carrying them out not only by violating Libyan air space 
and territorial waters but also by threatening this people from its 
southern borders through the rebel clique, which is rejected by 
the people in Ndjamena. This clique is being deployed to provoke 
Jamahiriyah, deny the Chadian people’s freedom and interfere 
in its internal affairs. 


These hostile American statements against the Libyan Arab 
people also affirm the U.S. Administration’s scheme to turn 
Chad into a military base opposed to Jamahiriyah and an impe- 
rialist base for striking at the African peoples. The U.S. Admin- 
istration’s terrorist ambitions recognize no bounds. Neither do 
they recognize the peoples’ right to liberty. This administration 
is constantly poking its finger into al! affairs, thus asserting its 
aggressive nature.
