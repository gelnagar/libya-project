Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery briefing board from January 1982 reports that Foxbat high-speed interceptor and reconnaissance aircraft deployed to Sebha airfield in southern Libya, probably from Okba Ibn Nafa airfield near Tripoli, which houses the only Foxbat units in Libya.
• Foxbat units in Libya are housed only at Okba Ibn Nafa airfield, per the document.
• Four Foxbat aircraft were seen at Sebha: two Foxbat A interceptors, one Foxbat A/C interceptor/trainer, and one Foxbat B/D reconnaissance aircraft.
• One POL truck with trailer was next to one of the Foxbats.
• Coordinates given in the document: 27-00-05N 014-27-50E.
• 1982-01-18: Two Foxbat A interceptors, one Foxbat A/C (interceptor/trainer) and one Foxbat B/D reconnaissance aircraft observed on the transient cargo apron on the west side of the airfield; first identification of Foxbat at Sebha.
