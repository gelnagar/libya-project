The note cites earlier arms-shipment activity at Tripoli port and Ghurdabiyah airfield.
• The note's significance: an Iranian merchant ship and cargo aircraft in Libya suggests continued Libyan military support to Iran.
• The merchant ship was an Arya Nur-class vessel, probably the Iran Nehad.
• Tarabulus coordinates given as 32-54-25N 013-11-15E; Tripoli airfield as 32-39-45N 013-09-35E.
• Similar cargo activity in 1980 and 1981 was associated with major arms shipments to Iran.
• 1980-10: Iranian civil and military 747 aircraft observed at Ghurdabiyah airfield.
• 1981-06: Iranian merchant ship Tran Ershad loaded T-54/55 tanks and 130mm M-46 field guns.
• 1982-04-07: Iranian 747 heavy-lift cargo aircraft observed parked on a taxiway; no military cargo activity discernible.
