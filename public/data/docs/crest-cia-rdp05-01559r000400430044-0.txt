((EXCERPTS)) ((QUESTION)) BROTHER COLONEL, REGARDING THE 
UNIONIST ACTION BETWEEN ALGERIA AND THE JAMAHIRIYAH AND THEN 
THE AGREEMENT DURING THE MEETING OF THE SUPREME 
LIBYAN-ALGERIAN COMMITTEE ON DRAWING UP A TIMETABLE FOR HTE 
PREPARATION OF A UNIONIST PLAN, CAN YOU TALK TO US ABOUT THE 
EXPECTED STEPS TO ACHIEVE THIS UNIONIST PLAN? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) THE STEPS ACCORDING TO THE AGREEMENTS 
ARE: THE PLAN WILL BE SUBMITTED TO THE MASSES FOR 
DISCUSSION, THEN A REFERENDUM WILL BE HELD ON IT IN THE 
FOLLOWING PERIOD. IF THE REFERENDUM IS SUCCESSFUL -- THAT 
IS A YES VOTE -- THEN THIS STATE WILL BE SET UP. (INDEED 
WHEN YOU OPEN THE MAP AND SEE THAT ALGERIA AND LIBYA WILL 
BECOME ONE STATE -- THIS WILL BE AN EXTREMELY IMPORTANT 
EVENT IN THE WHOLE WORLD -- INDEED A SUPERPOWER, THANKS TO 
YOUR EFFORTS, GOD WILLING, AND YOUR DETERMINATION, INSPIRED 
BY THE SOULS OF THE MARTYRS, THE 1 AND 1/2 MILLION MARTYRS 
AND THEIR PURE BLOOD ((WORDS INDISTINCT)) THEN GOD WILLING, 
SUCCESS WILL BE THE FATE OF THIS GREAT HISTORIC ACTION. 
((PASSAGE OMITTED: TRIBUTE TO ALGERIAN SACRIFICES AND 
REFERENCE TO ALGERIA'S COLONIAL PAST)) 

((QUESTION) ) BROTHER LEADER, PRESIDENT CHADLI BENDJEDID'S 
INITIATIVE IN CALLING FOR THE CONVENING OF THE UPRISING 
CONFERENCE IN ALGERIA HAD A POSITIVE IMPACT IN CLEARING THE 
ARAB AIR AND ASSITING THE STRUGGLE OF THE PALESTINIAN 
PEOPLE. WHAT IS YOUR ASSESSMENT OF THE RESULTS OF THE 
SUMMIT, AND WHAT IS ITS EFFECT ON THE UPRISING AND THE ARAB 
STRUGGLE? MOREOVER, WHAT ARE THE PRACTICAL STEPS THAT HAVE 
BEEN TAKEN BY THE BROTHER COLONEL TO IMPLEMENT THE DECISIONS 
OF THIS SUMMIT? THANK YOU. 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) I EXPRESSED MYSELF ABOUT THIS IN THE 
CLOSING SPEECH OF THE ALGIERS SUMMIT CONFERENCE. HOWEVER, 
THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THESE DECISIONS IS UP TO THE ARAB 
RULERS WHO DECIDED UPON THIS. I GIVE MY BROTHER STRUGGLER 
CHADLI BENDJEDID THE RESPONSIBILITY OF OVERSEEING THESE 
DECISIONS CONCERNING THE UPRISING BECAUSE HE NOW HEADS THESE 
EFFORTS. THE SUMMIT WAS HELD IN ALGERIA UNDER HIS 
CHAIRMANSHIP. THIS NATIONAL RESPONSIBILITY THEREFORE FALLS 
ON HIM. WE WILL HELP AND ASSIST HIM IN ((WORDS 
INDISTINCT)). © 

((QUESTION)) THE PRESENCE OF THE CURRENT OAU CHAIRMAN AT 
THE DELIBERATIONS OF THE UPRISING CONFERENCE IN ALGERIA IS A 
CONFIRMATION OF A CLEAR POLITICAL WILL WHICH AIMS AT 
CONSOLIDATING THE AFRO-ARAB RELATIONS OF COOPERATION? WHAT 
WAYS DO YOU SEE, BROTHER COLONEL, FOR CONSOLIDATING THIS 
COOPERATION? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) THE ARABS SHOULD GIVE MORE TO THE 
AFRICANS BECAUSE THEY ARE UNDERDEVELOPED, BECAUSE THEY WERE 
COLONIZED, BECAUSE WESTERN IMPERIALISM: TRIED TO EXPLOIT AND 
RECRUIT THEM AGAINST THE ARABS AND TO INSPIRE IN THEM A 
RACIST SPIRIT IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE PRINCIPLE OF DIVIDE AND 
RULE, THAT WICKED IMPERIALIST PRINCIPLE. CONSEQUENTLY, WE

AND ACHEIKH IBN OMAR WILL FIGHT; AND THE DAY AFTER ACHEIKH
