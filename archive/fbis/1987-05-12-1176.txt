LIBYA

Army Hands Tibesti Positions to Goukouni Forces 
LD111844 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1750 GMT 11 May 87 


[Text] Tripoli, 11 May (JANA) — JANA has learned that the 
Libyan Arab Armed Forces have handed over their positions in 
the Tibesti region to the troops of Goukouni Oueddei as pari of 
the return of the natural alliance between the Libyan Arab and 
Chadian revolutions.
