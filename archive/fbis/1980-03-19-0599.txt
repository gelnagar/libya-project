LIBYA

ARMY CHIEF RECEIVES BENIN MILITARY DELEGATION 


LD151912 Tripoli JANA in English 1830 GMT 15 Mar 80 ID 


[Text] Tripoli, March 15, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY=--The general commander of the Libyan 
armed forces Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir today conferred with the chief of staff of the 
armed forces of Benin and his accompanying delegation, currently visiting the Jamahiriyah. 
The meeting was also attended by the Libyan Arab ambassador in Benin.
