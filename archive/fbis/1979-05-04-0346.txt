[Text] Tripoli, 3 May, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The general secretary of Chad 
liberation front FROLINAT, Abba Siddick, described the present Chadian Government as 
a fascist and illegal government and its presence is considered as a violation of the 
agreement of Kano conference in Nigeria. 


In a statement to JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY correspondent, Abba Siddick affirmed that 
the present government in Chad is in close contact with the so-called (Israel). 


He said that Goukouni Ouaddeimi sent one of his assistants to contact the enemy's 
embassy in Paris and Goukouni had earlier admitted in a statement in Nigeria that he 
was ready to forge an alliance with the enemy.

Abba Siddick said that Goukouni Ouaddeimi only seeks to control power as he allied 
himself with Hissein Habre, and both are from one tribe, ignoring all other tribes 
and parties of national reconciliation in Chad. 


This is the same method which was pursued by Tombalbaye and Felix Malloum who tried 
to suppress the Chadian people by implementing tribal rule and depriving the other 
sections of the people from participating in the construction of Chad. 


Abba Siddick affirmed that the so-called Goukouni and Habre government cannot by any 
means achieve national unity and cannot provide effective solutions for the political 
economic and social problems from which the country is suffering. 


He added by saying: "The so-called present government does not exist and does not 
rely on any legal support. We can say that it is considered as a violation of the 
agreement of Kano conference and that the alleged state council is considered as 
non-existent as from ist April, 1979. This means that as from lst April, 1979, there 
is not a state council but two wings which are the northern armed forces of Hissein 
Habre and the forces of Goukouni Ouaddeimi who falsely claims that they represent the 
forces of FROLINAT. 


He went on to say tliat Goukouni and Habre violated the first Kano agreement, the 
agreement which stipulated the withdrawal of all armed elements from the capital 
Ndjamena to a distance of 100 kilometers and [word indistinct] the capital from any 
military presence, but they did not withdraw their forces from the capital and instead 
changed the military uniform into civilian clothes, and this violates Kano agreement. 


Abba Siddick added: the second violation was the refusal of Goukouni and Habre to 
discuss with any party for finding a political solution with the other Chadian parties 
which participated on the second congress of Kano, 


Answering the question coneerning the reasons of the failure of Kano second congress, 
Abba Siddick said that he knew from the start that this congress would not lead to 

any positive results because the state council--formed from the four parties: Goukouni, 
Habre, Abakar Abderahman and a representative from the Chadian Armed Forces--was 

formed to fill the political vacuum during the period from 23 March to April 1. Starting 
from the lst of last April this council is automatically considered dissolved and its 
constituent parties should become ordinary factions which should join the other factions 
equally in Kano's second [as received] congress. 


He added that during the term of this council, the group of Goukouni and Habre (?found) 
aids and support from foreign countries and were established as an authority due to its 
presence in the capital and it refused to take part in Kano second congress so its intra- 
sigence was due to the external aids. 


He went on to say: During Kano second congress a member of the neighbouring countries 
delegations participating in that congress told me that it was not logical that we ask 
to take part in power with those who acquired it with the power of arms. 


He meant by this the military existence of Goukouni and Habre. 


Ye said: In Kano third congress force should be resorted to to make those who want to 
govern the country by force understand.

With regard to his opinion on the intervention or the imperialist forces in Chad in an 
attempt to partition it into north and south, Abba Siddick said; In general, imperiaism 
and external power want to repartition the African Continent to weaken the african countries 
in order to exploit this continent and transfer it into a source from which they draw raw 
materials. In this respect Chad is not an exception. 


He added that the existence of a real power in Chad does not enable imperialism to achieve 
its projects and interests in partitioning Chad, and we consider the frontiers that divide 
the African countries as colonialist frontiers, so we should all fight to consolidate all 
efforts to keep the geographical existence that imperialism wants to abolish. Answering 4 
question concerning his stand towards the new government in Chad, he said: "At present 

we represent a popular and military force, and we represent the people's opinion, whe ther 
the original FROLINAT, the revolutionary democratic council, the Chadian Armed Forces, or 
people's liberation forces." 


He added: "We should organize ourselves to be one voice and will in order to impose a 
political and military solution, because it is unacceptable that a group representing a 
minority of the Chadian people should impose its power on the whole of Chadian people. 


At the end of his speech, Dr Abba Siddick praised the role of the Jamahiriyah in supporting 
the Chadian revolution, saying: "The Jamahiriyah has not concealed her support for the 
Chadian revolution to achieve its aims and to set up an independent state including economic 
independence from any foreign influence. The Jamahiriyah has also tried to find a solution 
by coordinating with other neighbouring countries participating in Kano conference." He 
added: "There are historical and human relations that link the two countries together so 
we, the Chadians together with the Libyans, should not get trapped by the ambush of 
imperialism and its agents." He went on to says "we should know that the imperialism 
puppet regime's strategy is to strike the progressive regimes whether in Africa or in the 
Third World countries, 50 it is in their interest to create problems among the neighbouring 
countries in order to exploit these countries." 


IMPERIALIST ACTS OF ‘TERROR, SUPPRESSION! CONDEMNED 
LD030950 Tripoli JANA in English 0827 GMT 3 May 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 3 May, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah delegation has condemned all acts of terror and suppression faced by the Third 
World nations by imperialist countries with the aim of harming their freedom and security. 


During the conference held in Geneva on the formulation of an international agreement on 
terrorism and holding hostages, the Libyan delegation reaffirmed condemnation of all acts 
that harm [word indistinct] and their freedom. 


The Jamahiriyah delegation distinguished between people's struggle for their freedom and 
self-determination such as the struggle of the Palestinian people to return to their 
usurped homeland and the struggle of the African people in confronting the racist regimes 
which step upon the freedom and dignity of the African human being and the terror practised 
py the imperialist countries [words indistinct] military force, strategic weapons, and by 
economic pressureSe 


The Jamahiriyah delegation pointed out the efforts of the Jamahiriyah in this field, at 
individual and peoples level.

For instance, the Jamahiriyah with her efforts managed to release Mrs and Mr Kloster 
and Priest Birera. And at peoples level, the Jamahiriyah supported the national libera- 
tion movements struggling for freedom and return to their lands. 


On behalf of non-alignment countries, the Jamahiriyah delegation rejected the motion 
put forward by the Western countries, because it lacks the principles of sovereignty 
and territorial security.
