LIBYA

Talks Between Reagan, ‘Hireling’ Numayri Reported 
LD222103 Tripoli JANA in English 1926 GMT 22 Nov 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Safar 17, Nov 22, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY The head of the US. Administration and the 
hireling Numayri last night in Washington held a meeting of an 
extremely provocative nature against the Arab Libyan people. 
To justify U.S. military intervention in Sudan and to find an 
excuse for making the country a base for Washington's aggres- 
sion on Libya, Numayri repeated coward allegations during the 
meeting. He claimed that Libya threatens his regime, Sudan's 
unity and stability in the whole continent of Africa
