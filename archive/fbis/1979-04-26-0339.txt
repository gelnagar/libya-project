LIBYA

ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS CALLS FOR 1 MAY RESPONSE TO TREATY 


LD251750 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 25 apr 79 LD 


{(Excerpts] Tripoli, April 25--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 
issued a statement in Tripoli yesterday calling for 1 May 1979 to be the day for reply to 
the imperialist treaty signed by Carter, the terrorist Begin and the defeatist traitor. 
The statement says: 


On 1 May the arab workers from the ocean to the gulf will declare their rejection of the 
betrayal treaty in a practical way by boycotting U.S. land, sea and air transportation 
and all the establishments of the ruling regime in Egypt, as an implementation of the 
decisions of the Central Council of the International Confederation of Arab Trade Unions 
in its emergency session in Damascus on 6 and 7 april. 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress has called on all national and 
progressive forces and members of the Arab People's Congress in all countries of the 
greater Arab homeland to increase the struggle and the Arab boycott of the United States 
and the region of the defeated president with all possible participation, assistance, 
support and solidarity. 


V. 26 Apr 79 NORTH AFRICA 


The Secretariat has stressed that this is everyone's battle, and the Arab workers are 
the vanguard of the Arab struggle against imperialism, Zionism and their defeated traitor 
agent. 


At the end of its statement, the Permanent Secretariat warned any Arab regime against 
the consequences of obstructing the struggle of the Arab workers who are now operating 
as a pan-Arab, national and official entity, in light of the decisions of the Arab 
People's Congress and its Permanent Secretariat and the conferences of the steadfastness 
front and the Baghdad conference, 


The decision has been made that 1 May 1979 is to be the day of starting the greater battle 
to implement the slogan of striking at American interests, against the treaty of the 
imperialist Carter, the terrorist Begin and the defeated traitor, and against the entire 
imper ialist-Zionist-reactionary plan. 


Long live the struggle of the Arab people! Long live the workers as a struggling van- 
guard! Defeat to Carter, Begin and the defeated alliance!
