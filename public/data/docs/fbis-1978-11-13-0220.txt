LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI URGES SPREAD OF ISLAMIC REVOLUTION 


LD1LO2525Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1410 GMT 10 Nov 78 LD 


[Excerpts] Tripoli--Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi this morning led 'Id al-Adha prayer in 
Martyrs Square, Tripoli, in accordance with the traditions of the Prophet. He delivered 
the 'Id sermon in which he explained, quoting from the Koran in support of his argument, 
religious and temporal matters which stress the progressive character of the religion and 
refute the assertions by renegades who try to label Islam as backward, 


He asked: Why do we see the Islamic nation backward while other nations have enough 
military power +» threaten our own nation and have such scientific potential as to enable 
them to unravel che secrets of life which have been harnessed for the good of man? Purthermore, 
the economic power of the nations around us is much more than ours, Why is the Islamic 
nation backward to such an extent that it does not even possess a nuclear weapon, inter- 
continental ballistic missiles, or the necessary technology and science? 


Al-Qadhdhafi said such questions have aroused doubt in many of the young people of this 
nation and that the superficial Moslems regara this backwardness in civilization as the 
result of Islam and therefore they have renounced it,

Explaining that, he said there are reasons we would like to mention at this Islamic 
gathering and convey to other Moslems in every corner of the globe because they show the 
real causes of backwardness experienced by the Islamic nation. One of the reasons is 

that Islamic peoples and Islam itself was subjected to colonialism which dominated the 
Islamic regions with its crusading hatred of Moslems. He said Islam stands for the truth, 
but that was misrepresented by alien mentalities which caused the Moslems to lag behind 
and which described all scientific discoveries as taboo. Moslems imagined that socialism 
was taboo, revolution was taboo and progress was toboo. He stressed that Islam was the 
opposite of all those retrograde allegations. It is revolution which calls for freedom, 
progress and building. 


Another reason for the backwardness of the Islamic nation, he explained, is the fact that 
the mission of Islam which was to bring prosperity, well-being, guidance for the good of 

men, blessings and revolution, was exploited by rulers who wished to control their peoples 
in order to enslave them, Those rulers began to tell their people that they were God's 
shadow on earth and that revolt against them would be taboo. They wanted their sons to 
inherit them so that their peoples might continue to be ruled by them. They exploited Islam 
and alleged that hereditary dynasty was the law of God. Then there were the business people 
who wanted to be worshipped so that they might preserve their wealth and the people might 
remain in need of them, If you were to revolt against them, they would say: God proscribes 
that, may you ask for forgiveness. He said what was in fact proscribed was capitalist 
wealth, squeezed from the poor by making them work 10 hours a day while paying them wages 
for 5 hours. They become rich and the workers poor, 


Al-Qadhdhafi said that Moslems from Tangier to Indonesia have been subjected to successive 
hateful and destructive colonial waves resulting in Moslems remaining behind the times, 
He said that the political-religious crusade was hostile to Islam and Moslems were the 


target merely because they were Moslems and their vulnerability stemmed from the failure 
to understand the truth of Islam, However, the backwardness experienced by the Islamic 
nation is not because of its being Islamic but because it languished under colonialism 
because backwardness was imposed upon it, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi called on Moslems throughout the world to rebel against the backward 
position foisted on them, In his sermon in Tripoli he said that Moslems now have the duty 

to wage a holy war for liberation from all vestiges of colonialism, the cause of backwardness 
of the Islamic nation and the reason why it does not possess atomic know-how, scientific 
knowledge, technology and science. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that the revolution in Iran is being waged to bring that 
country back to the correct Islamic line and the revolution must spread to liberate Moslems 
in that region from backwardness, diseases, tyranny, ignorance and enslavement, In his 

Id al-Adha sermon this morning Al-Qadhdhafi said that neither the shah nor the monarchy 

is a divine imposition and that no one can claim he is God's shadow on earth, 


He said the Islamic revolution ought to spread the revolution of justice, socialism, science 
and well-being to all parts of the world because if it does not spread, then Islam will 

be held as the cause of backwardness and the class system, He stressed that Moslems in 
Libya have woken up and waged the Islamic revolution which has achieved socialism, shattered 
the shackles of exploitation and enslavement, achieved equality, once again raised to a high 
level the name of God, and swept away from Islam the impurities and accumulation of sediment 
so that it may appear in its true form,

LEBANESE MAGAZINE CITES AT-TURAYKI ON WAR, OIL PRICES 


JN121635Y Amman Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 12 Nov 78 JIN 


[Text] Libyan Foreign Secretary 'Ali 'Abd as -Salam at Turayki asserted that the Arab 
nation wants peace based on Israel's general withdrawal from the occupied Arab territories. 
At Turayki warned in a statement to be published in tomorrow's issue of the Lebanese 
magazine MONDAY MORNING that a new war could break out in the Middle East because of 
Israel's intransigence and refusal to honor UN resolutions concerning occupied Arab terri- 
tories. 


At-Turayki said that his country supports the establishment of relations with the United 
States on the basis of mutual respect. Regarding oil prices, the Libyan foreign secretary 
referred to the need to take measures to increase the price of oil in order to offset losses 
suffered by the oil-producing countries as a result of the drop in the value of the dollar. 
Commenting on the subject of replacing the dollar as an oil currency, At -Turayki said that 
the Libyan Jamahiriyah will carry out any decision made by OPEC on that subject.
