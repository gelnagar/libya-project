BENIN PRESIDENT CONDEMNS U.S. ANTI-LIBYAN PLAN 


LD140928 Tripoli JANA in English 0815 GMT 14 Aug 81 


[Text] Cotonou, 14 Aug (JANA)--The president of the People's Republic of Benin, Mathieu 
Kerekou, has condemned the American administration's terrorist plan to assassinate the 
revolution's leader, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi. He informed the Libyan People's Bureau here 
of his stance during a meeting at the presidential palace. 


He said: "We are not surprised by this criminal scheme engineered by the U.S. administra- 
tion, which has, during the rule of all American presidents, practiced terrorist acts and 
dirty means, particularly since the advent of the Great Al-Fatah Revolution. 


He added: "The hostile line followed by the American administration against the Libyan 
Arab people will not weaken this people. The American administration fully comprehends 
that the Great Al-Fatah Revolution has become a growing force in confronting America's 
ugly face." 


He stressed: "History has taught us of how the Libyans. fought against the fascists, how 
this people's will triumphed over those invaders and how that struggle was crowned with 
a revolution on the great Al-Fatah day." 


President Mathieu Kerekou added that the victory of the Great Al-Fatah Revolution 
contributed in speeding up the advent of the revolution in the People's Republic of Benin. 
He went on to say "I and the people of Benin condemn all the U.S. imperialist attempts 
and stands against the people of the Jamahiriyah and our friend, the leader, Colonel 
Mu'Ammar al—Qadhdhafi." 


He added: "I affirm that the terrorist enemies cannot undermine the freedom of the Libyan 
people and the leader of its revolution, and that the people of Benin, despite its limited 
capacities, will stand by the people of the Jamahiriyah and by the leader of its revolution, 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and we in the same trench." 


The president of Benin told the secretary of the People's Bureau: "I hope you will affirm 
to the Libyan Arab people that the people of Benin and its revolution stand together by 

it and that the victory of the Great Al-Fatah Revolution is a victory for us and the 

enemy knows it." 


CHADIAN MINISTERS THANK LIBYAN SOLDIERS FOR AID 


LD161200 Tripoli JANA In English 0948 GMT 16 Aug 81 


{[Text] Tripoli, 16 Aug (JANA)--The Chadian defence and interior ministers yesterday 
inspected armed forces units in the central region, who participated in defeated [as 
received] the renegade forces of the butcher Hissein Habre, and who helped bring about 
freedom and peace to the country. 


The armed forces units received the two Frolinat members with loud cheers and revolutionary 
slogans affirming their readiness to defend the Libyan Arab and Chadian people against 
all those seeking to curtail their freedom. 


The Chadian defence minister also delivered a speech in which he saluted Colonel al- 
Qadhdhafi and praised his heroic stands for freedom, peace and justice. He said: "We 
highly appreciate the role played by the Great Al-Fatah Revolution and its leader towards 
our people and freedom." 


He told the army units: "Your heroic, humanitarian and bold mission has not yet ended, 
for you have to continue the struggle to liberate Arabs and Africans, to entrench 
security, stability and peace and to realise revolutionary goals throughout the world."
