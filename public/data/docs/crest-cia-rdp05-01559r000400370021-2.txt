Summary (AI): An FBIS-relayed Tripoli radio report (13 November 1975) says Qadhdhafi sent cables to heads of state of the 72 countries that voted for the UN resolution equating Zionism with racism, calling it a triumph of human rights. He also criticized the US president's objections and called for abolishing the veto of big states. The document has no specific places in or near Tripoli.
• Cables were sent to heads of state of the 72 countries that voted for the UN resolution equating Zionism with racism.
• Qadhdhafi said the UN had been like a chess board with players only from big imperialist countries, but a new era had started.
• Qadhdhafi called for abolishing the so-called veto of big states, comparing it to the divine rights of kings of the Middle Ages.
