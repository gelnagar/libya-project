Wilson, who was living in Libya, to the October 1980 shooting of Libyan student Faisal Zagallai in Colorado. • Zagallai was shot in October 1980 at Colorado State University; Libyan authorities took responsibility for the attack.
• Wilson and Terpil were indicted in April 1980 for allegedly offering $1 million to assassinate Umar Muhayshi.
