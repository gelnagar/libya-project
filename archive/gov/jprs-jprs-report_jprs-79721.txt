CSO: 


LIBYA 


°*A new Libyan cultural centre has been opened in Kuala Lumpur. The 
ceremony held for the occasion was attended by Muhiddin Mohammed 
Yassin, Parliamentary Secretary for Foreign Affairs in Malaysia, party 
leaders, members of the People’s Bureau in Malaysia, university chancellors 
and academic dignitaries, publishers and members of the diplomatic corps in 


Malaysia. 


The secretary of the Peo- 
ple’s Bureau delivered a 
speech on the occasion in 
which he said the opening of 
a number of cultural centres 
is part of the Jamahiriya’s 
belief in promoting relations 
with friendly nations. He 
said the centre will provide 
the Malaysian people and 
others with an opportunity 
to learn Arabic, the language 
of the Koran, and to use 
various Other books. 

He added that the most 
important service the centre 
will be performing will be to 
introduce the right inter- 
pretations of the concept of 
the Koran which form the 
basic legislature of the 
Jamahiriya society. He said: 
**The Islamic world is facing 
various challenges to its 
beliefs and we are called 
upon to deal with this threat 
and revise our political, 
economic and social con- 
cepts in accordance with the 
Green Book.”’
