Tass and Algeria's news agency also reacted to the Washington Post report that Reagan authorized covert CIA operations against Qaddafi.
• The Washington Post reported on a Sunday (around 3 November 1985) that Reagan authorized the CIA to support covert operations against Qaddafi.
• The alleged plan was to draw Qaddafi into a foreign adventure or terrorist exploit so a neighbouring foe such as Algeria or Egypt could strike at him.
• Reagan reportedly ordered an investigation to determine who leaked the documents.
• Algeria's news agency said cooperating in such an action was neither ethical nor political within its morals, and that Algeria's ties with Libya remain close.
• 1985-11-04: Libya's official JANA news agency and government denounced the reported CIA plan to undermine Qaddafi's government as US terrorist sabotage.
