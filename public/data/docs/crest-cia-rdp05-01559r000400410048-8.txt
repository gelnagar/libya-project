Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a Tripoli domestic radio broadcast reports Qadhdhafi's 10 June 1986 speech in Darnah, in which he calls the United States a historic enemy, warns of renewed attack, and orders Darnah turned into an armed city. The speech does not concern Tripoli itself.
• Speech broadcast by Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic, 0500 GMT 11 June 1986.
• Qadhdhafi refers to US warships threatening Tripoli and Benghazi, and says the same ships threatened Darnah in 1805.
• He says Darnah's mountains should be fortified with naval, air and ground defenses.
• Speech was translated by FBIS Nicosia and distributed to US embassies in Khartoum, Tunis, Rabat and Niamey.
• 1986-06-10: Qadhdhafi delivers speech in Darnah calling for the city to be made an armed city against possible US occupation.
• 1986-06-11: Tripoli domestic service broadcasts the speech in Arabic at 0500 GMT on 11 June 1986.

((TEXT)) BROTHERS. I HAIL THE WELCOME GIVEN ME BY THE SONS 

OF THE HISTORIC CITY OF DARNAH. THIS CITY WAS THE TARGET OF 

U.S. AGGRESSION IN APRIL 1805. IT WAS IN THAT SAME MONTH, 

APRIL 1805, THAT THE CITIES OF TRIPOLI AND BANGHAZI WERE 

TARGETED ((AS HEARD)). THUS, U.S. AGGRESSION HAS BEEN REPEATED, 

AND IT IS CLEAR THAT THE UNITED STATES IS A HISTORIC ENEMY 

OF THIS NATION. IT IS OUR TRADITIONAL ENEMY, WHO HAS ALWAYS 

SOUGHT TO CONTROL THIS LONG COASTLINE ON THE MEDITERRANEAN 

OCCUPIED BY <><>LIBYA<><>, 

THE WARSHIPS THAT NOW THREATEN TRIPOLI AND BANGHAZI ARE THE 

SAME ONES THAT THREATENED DARNAH IN 1805. IT WAS IN APRIL THAT 

THEY SHELLED TRIPOLI AND BANGHAZI, AND IT WAS IN THAT SAME 

MONTH IN 1805 THAT THEY BOMBARDED DARNAH. THE UNITED STATES 

WAS DEFEATED HERE DESPITE THE FACT THAT IT HAD MOBILIZED ITS FLEET 

AT THAT TIME AND HAD MASSED FOUR FRIGATES -- HUGE DESTROYERS 

GUARDED BY THREE OTHER WARSHIPS -- OFF THE COAST OF DARNAH. 

THESE FOUR FRIGATES SHELLED THIS CITY SAVAGELY, WITH THE SAME 

SAVAGERY AND COWBOY MENTALITY, AND WITH THE SAME HATRED AND SPIRIT, 

WHICH IS VERY HOSTILE TO THIS NATION, THAT THEY DISPLAYED IN THEIR 

BOMBARDMENT OF BANGHAZI AND TRIPOLI. BUT HERE IS THE CITY OF 

DARNAH, FREE, PROUD, AND PEACEFUL. WHERE ARE THOSE U.S. 

WARSHIPS, WHOSE CREWS THOUGHT THEY HAD DESTROYED IT IN APRIL 1805? 

THE AMERICANS WERE FORCED TO WITHDRAW THEN. BY COINCIDENCE,

END OF THE 18TH CENTURY. CONSEQUENTLY, I TELL YOU TODAY, AND 
THESE WORDS MUST BE TAKEN SERIOUSLY, THAT DARNAH IS AMONG THE 
TARGETS THAT THE UNITED STATES IS EAGER TO OCCUPY. IT SEES 
DARNAH AS AN ISOLATED AND QUITE IMPORTANT CITY IN THE JAMAHIRIYAH, 
ONE WITH A STRATEGIC POSITION, AND AS A PLACE WHERE THE UNITED 
STATES COULD GAIN A FOOTHOLD. THUS, WE MUST FACE THIS POSSIBILITY 
NOT WITH SLOGANS AND WORDS ALONE, BUT WITH A TRAINED, ARMED, 

AND PRODUCTIVE CITY, DETERMINED TO DEFEAT THE UNITED STATES 

EVEN NOW, JUST AS IT DID IN APRIL, MAY, AND JUNE OF 1805.
