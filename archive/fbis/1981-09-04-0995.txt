LIBYAN LEADER SPEECH TO ARMY GRADUATES 3 SEP 


NCO41014 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0800 GMT 4 Sep 81 


[Text] Yesterday evening, the leader of the revolution and the supreme commander of the 
armed forces met with graduates of various military academies who graduated this year and 
joined the Arab Libyan Armed Forces to bolster the capabilities of these forces. 


The graduates received the leader of the revolution with enthusiastic revolutionary 
slogans which embodied the will of the armed masses and their intent to fight the enemies 
and foil the various U.S., Zionist and reactionary conspiracies and plots. 


During the meeting, the brother leader affirmed that the popular authority is the end of 
the road for the long and bitter struggle throughout the history of humanity to attain 
power. He added that all the efforts exerted by humanity to attain power--whether this 
refers to individuals, parties or vanguards--are merely preludes and paradoxes by means of 
which the masses will discover the road to popular power. 


The brother leader said that the Libyan people have passed this phase and attained power 

by means of their popular rallies. The brother leader also affirmed that in the popular 
rallies the masses are equal in their social and economic status because economic relations 
among them are on an equal footing, that is: There is no boss and worker and there is no 
landlord and tenant and there is no governor and no one is governed. He added that the 
unjust relations in 'ibyan society have been smashed, that the workers have become partners 
and the administration has been put in popular hands, while land has become the property 

of all the Libyans. The one who produces is the one that consumes. 


The brother leader affirmed the need to expedite the formation of an armed people so that 
they can practice revolutionary supervision, educate the armed forces by means of Standing 
Order No. 1, exhort the people into carrying weapons, receiving training in them, pushing 
the armed forces into expediting this task and creating an educational revolution in the 
curricula so as to transfer military sience to the educational institutions to affirm the 
power of the armed masses, to advocate revolution and practice it.
