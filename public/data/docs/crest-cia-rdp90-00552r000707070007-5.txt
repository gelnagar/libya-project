Summary (AI): A 1981 article summarizing Seymour Hersh's New York Times Magazine story says former CIA agents Edwin Wilson and Frank Terpil trained and armed Gaddafi's terrorists, including supplying explosives and timers. It mentions a laboratory near Tripoli for making assassination devices disguised as household items.
• About 40,000 lbs of RDX explosive were reportedly flown to Libya in drums marked 'industrial solvent'.
• Gaddafi reportedly wanted thousands of timers to set off explosives at a specified hour.
• Terpil reportedly had a $3.2 million contract to supply arms, explosives and torture devices to Uganda.
• The article says Gaddafi's agents sought to buy a Redeye missile, which Mulcahy feared could be fitted with a nuclear warhead.
• The article is dated 25 June 1981 and describes the story as confirmed in essential details by TIME correspondents.
• 1981-06: Reagan Administration ordered Libya's diplomatic mission in Washington to close, according to the article.
• 1976: Kevin Mulcahy was coaxed into exporting high-speed communications and computer gear through Interarms/International Technology, Inc. with Wilson and Terpil.
