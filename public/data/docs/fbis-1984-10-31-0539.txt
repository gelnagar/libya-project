LIBYA

Habre Regime Blamed for Failure of Chad Talks 
LD311106 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 31 Oct 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 31 Oct (JANA) — The People’s Committee of the 
People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison has blamed rebel Hissein 
Habre and his clique for the failure of the preparatory meeting 
for national reconciliation in Chad held recently in Brazzaville. 


This came in telegrams from the People’s Committee of the 
People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison to the secretary gencral of 
the OAU, the Ethiopian foreign minister, as Ethiopia is the 
current chairman of the organisation, and to the Congolese 
Foreign minister, as the Congo is the state sponsoring the pre- 
paratory meeting for national recorviliation in Chad 


In its telegrams, the committee stresses that what has happened 
in Brazzaville is a repetition of what happened in Addis Ababa, 
and that all the attempts made for achieving national reconcili- 
ation in this African country have met with the rejection and 
intransigence of rebel Habre and his clique 


In its telegrams the committee urges these circles to fulfill their 
duty towards this African country by notifying African and 
international public opinion that the onus for the failure of the 
recent meeting for national reconciliation in Chad, which is now 
clear, is borne by rebel Hissein Habre and his clique
