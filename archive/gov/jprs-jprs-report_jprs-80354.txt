Question: What about the freedom and commitment Najib Mahfuz possesses? 


Najib Mahfuz: Artists always aspire to freedom, but freedom entails an intrinsic 
responsibility which is not to be taken lightly, of course, and this is embodied 
in the writer's free commitment to national, ideolozical or human issues. I do 
not think that a work of mine exists which is lacking in one or another of these 
issues, 


Question: Now that you have reached the age of 70, do you look back in anger or 
contentment? What about the future? 


Najib Mahfuz: There is no total contentment behind me, but I do foresee good 
things over the long range. I am content to find serious sincere work and good 
models, Here I can continue to be patient until the good is achieved, and not 
rush it. 


The features of change which Najib Mahfuz portrays with his pen through his writ- 
ings are a truthful expression of the inner pulse of a writer who has steeped 
himself in Egyptian society. His most prominent characteristics have been simplic- 
ity, humility, and an aversion to formality, imitation or pretentiousness. It is 
up to Najib Mahfuz to talk about this era's performance through its crises and con- 
tradictions, but salvation, in his view, lies in a sense of belonging and in 
balance.

NEW MISSILE BOAT AFTER LAVI--The next military project after the Lavi aircraft 
will involve the manufacture of a new missile boat for the navy. This was 
started today by Defense Minister Ari'el Sharon. Our military correspondent, 
Shmu'el Tal, reports that the ship will be larger than the ships currently in 
service. It will be able to stay on the high seas for a prolonged period of 
time and move the scene of the action far away from the Israeli coasts, up to 
Libya and the mouth of the Mediterranean. It will be able to carry long-range 
missiles as well as sophisticated arms systems in accordance with future needs. 
Such a ship will be capable of preventing the blockade of the civilian Israeli 
passageways even when a war is being waged far away from its waters. The plan- 
ning work for the ship is being carried out by the Israel Shipyards and the 
Israel Aircraft Industries from their own resources. The cabinet had approved 
the plan to build the new missile boats some time ago. [Text] [TA211909
