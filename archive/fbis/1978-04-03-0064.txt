YUGOSLAV DEFENSE SECRETARY CONCLUDES VISIT 
LD311510Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1158 GMT 31 Mar 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--Yugoslav Federal Secretary for National Defense Ljubicic left Tripoli 
at 1000 today together with the delegation accompanying him at the end of a 3-day visit 
to the Jamahiriyah, 


In a statement he made to a JANA correspondent, the Yugoslav federal secretary for 
national defense, said: I have visited the Jamahiriyah frequently. I can compare the 
progress achieved with what I have seen in the past. I am happy and pleased with the 
progress your country has achieved in the fields of industry, health, education and 
agriculture. The progress achieved in all these fields emphasizes the great development 
which the masses have achieved in all these domains. 


The Yugoslav federal secretary for national defense said that relations between the 
Jamahiriyah and Yugoslavia in the military domain are progressing significantly in the 
interest of the two friendly countries--the Jamahiriyah and Yugoslavia. These relations 
are founded on firm friendship, identity of views and attitudes between the Jamahiriyah 
and Yugoslavia. We must work to develop and strengthen our military potentials. The 
talks were held in a friendly atmosphere and a sincere spirit to strive to achieve peace 
in this part of the world. 


I really mean what I say about the progress your country has achieved. I thank you for 
the affection and appreciation, which together with the accompanying delegation, I have 
sensed from all those we met, led by Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of 
the armed forces, He said that cooperation is being developed significantly in all 
fields and visits are being exchanged between the Libyan Arab side and the Yugoslav side 
and that through these visits views are exchanged and agreements are reached for the 
development of relations and strengthening cooperation between our two countries. 


Brig Abu Bakr Yunis, commander in chief of the armed forces, hailed Arab Libyan-Yugoslav 
relations, describing them as excellent. In a statement to a JANA correspondent he said 
that the Libyan Arab-Yugoslav meetings on various levels--where complete understanding 
prevails--certainly embody the strengthening of the positive cooperation between us in 
various fields in a positive and effective way.

MONGOLIAN DEPUTY FOREIGN MINISTER ENDS VISIT 


LDOL2204Y Tripoli JANA in English 1826 GMT 1 Apr 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, April lst JANA--Mr. Jamblin Panzer, deputy for ‘gn minister of the 
Republic of Mongolia and the accompanying delegation, left Tripoli this morning after a 
3-day official visit to the Jamahiriyah. They were seen off at the airport by Mr. Ahmad 
al-Atrash, assistant under secretary for technical affairs and the director of the Asian 
Department at the Foreign Secretariat. A press release was issued following this visit, 
the text of which is as follows: 


A delegation from the Peoples Republic of Mongolia headed by Mr. Jamblin Panzer, deputy 
foreign minister, paid an official visit to the Jamahiriyah during the period from 20/23 
Rabi Thani 1398 A.H., corresponding to 29 March to 1 April 1978. During this period 

the deputy foreign minister of Mongolia and the accompanying delegation were received by 
Dr. 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, foreign secretary of the Socialist Peoples' Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah, and they conveyed a message from Mr. Jumjaagiyn Tsedenbal, president of 
the Peoples Republic of Mongolia to Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, secretary general of 
the General Peoples Congress. 


During the meeting with the foreign secretary the situation in the Arab native land, the 
means of development and promotion of relations and cooperation between the two countries 
were discussed. In this connection it was agreed to exchange ambassadors. 


In a friendly atmosphere, the negotiations were conducted between the Mongolian and the 
Libyan Arab sides headed by Ahmad al-Atrash, assistant under secretary for technical affairs 
in the Foreign Secretariat. The two sides expressed their desire to devebop and sustain 
the bilateral relations in the various fields in the interest of the two parties. The 
assistant under-secretary for technical affairs in the Foreign Secretariat acquainted 

the Mongolian delegation with the most important achievements since the September revolution 
in the economic, social and political fields. He also acquainted him with the role of the 
Jamahiriyah in the Arab and African fields and the non-alignment movement. The Mongolian 
delegation acquainted the under-secretary for technical affairs and the foreign secre- 
tariat with the aspects of economic and social development in their country and the high- 
lights of the Mongolian foreign policy. The two parties reviewed the current international 
situation and showed special interest in the explosive situation in the Arab native land. 
They expressed their confidence that true peace and security can only be realized in 

the Arab native land when all the Arab territories are liberated and only when the legitimiate 
rights of the Palestinian people, including their right to establish their national state, 
are observed. The two parties strongly condemned imperialism, the capitalist policies and 
initiatives of their allies which are only geared to the surrender of the rights of the 
Arab people and liquidating the Palestinian cause. The two parties confirmed that the 
recent Zionist aggression on south Lebanon reflects again the expansionist policy of Israel 
and its determination to liquidate the Palestinian resistance. They further confirmed 
their support of the just Palestinian struggle and the Lebanese progressive movement in 
their stand against this aggressive Zionist policy.

The Mongolian delegation commended the vanguard role of the Jamahiriyah in realizing 
the front of steadfastness and expressed Mongolia's solidarity with this front. 


The two parties asserted the necessity of sustaining friendship relationships between 
the Jamahiriyah and the socialist countries. 


Reviewing the current situation in South Africa, the two parties expressed solidarity 
with the peoples of Namibia, Zimbabwe and South Africa in their struggle for freedom 
from colonialism and imperialism as well as liquidation of racist regimes to enable 
these peoples to exercise self determination and independence. In this connection the 
two parties condemned the racist complicity between the two racist regimes in South 
Africa and occupied Palestine. 


The two parties asserted the need for peace and security in Asia. At the end of these 
talks, the Mongolian delegation expressed gratitude for the cordial reception and 
generous hospitality accorded to them in the Jamahiriyah ard wished the friendly Libyan 
Arab people ever success and progress. 


TRIPOLI: AS-SADAT DECEIVED ABOUT WEIZMAN'S VISIT 


LD312359Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1915 GMT 31 Mar 78 LD 


[Unattributed commentary: "How Long Will the Deception Continue?" ] 


[Excerpts] Today we heard the reactions to the enemy defense minister's visit to the 
Arab Republic of Egypt. We have also followed the comments of the press published in 
Washington, London and Cairo. All of them are unanimous in the view that the enemy 

defense minister's visit and his meetings with President Anwar as-Sadat and his aides 
have not led to any worthwhile results. Indeed, some Western papers went so far as to 
say that the Egyptian president strongly believed that he had been deceived. But how? 


The Egyptian president was under the impression that the enemy defense minister was 
bringing something new with him, particularly since the defense minister's visit came 
in the wake of three important developments. The first of these was the Zionist enemy's 
fifth war, this time against Lebanon and the Palestinian revolution. Then there was 
Menahem Begin's visit to Washington and his talks with U.S. President Carter in which 
news agencies say the American and Zionist leaders failed to bring the so-called peace- 
ful solution in the Middle East any nearer. Finally, there was the Zionist enemy's 
cabinet meeting following Begin's return from Washington and its adoption of a unanimous 
decision in support of Begin's current policy. This put an end to all the lies about 
rifts among the leaders of the Zionist Israeli Cabinet over Begin's so-called 
inflexibility in regard to the so-called peaceful solution. 


In spite of such results and in such an atmosphere the Egyptian president expected the 
Zionist minister to come with new proposals. But no sooner had the visit ended than 
he discovered that he had been deceived and that the Israeli minister had brought 
nothing worthwhile. This prompted the world press to ask what the meaning of this 
visit had been. 


However, the question is how long the Egyptian president will continue to be deceived 
like a child. Or is the Egyptian president fond of the policy of deceiving and being 
deceived? Begin can deceive As-Sadat, who claims to be mature, but why does As-Sadat 
allow himself to be deceived time and again?
