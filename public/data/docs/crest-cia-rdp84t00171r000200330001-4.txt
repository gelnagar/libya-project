Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing on Libyan military logistics tied to the Libya-Chad conflict. It reports heavy-lift and logistics activity at Tajura barracks and vehicle storage area near Tripoli, plus sites in southern Libya (Sebha, Kufra) and a probable Chadian resupply at Abeche.
• Tajura barracks area: 27 heavy-lift transporters in columns; at least one tank battalion and one mechanized infantry battalion parked outside vehicle storage buildings.
• Sebha airfield aircraft order of battle includes 3 Blinder, 18 Foxbat, 9 SF.260, 1 Candid, 1 C-130, 2 G.222T, 4 camouflage-painted Curl, 3 F.27, 1 Twin Otter, 1 CH-47.
• Gharyan area MRL battalion equipment: 12 BM-21 MRL and about 20 support vehicles.
• Document is a partly declassified NPIC briefing attachment; coordinates not given (N/A).
• 1983-08: High level of heavy-lift transport activity; one tank company (12 tanks) probably departed on transporters.
• 1983-08: High logistics activity with cargo trucks forming columns.
• 1983-08: Probable Chadian resupply flights arrived.
