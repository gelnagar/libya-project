IRANIAN FOREIGN MINISTER ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 


LD171400 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 17 Feb 82 


[Text] Brother ‘Ali Akbar Velayati, foreign minister of the Islamic Republic of Iran, 
arrived in Tripoli at noon today on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah. 


In a statement to JANA, Brother Velayati stressed the importance of the bonds and fraternal 
relations between the Libyan Arab and the Iranian peoples. He said our relations with the 
Libyan Arab people have been good since the beginning of the triumph of the Islamic 
revolution in Iran and that these relations are growing stronger day after day. He added: 
We are determined to widen and consolidate these relations in all fields. 


Brother ‘Ali Akbar Velayati is accompanied by a delegation of senior officials of the Iranian 
Foreign Ministry. 


Talks Begin 
LD180752 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0215 GMT 18 Feb 82 


[Text] Libyan Arab-Iranian talks began last night at the headquarters of the People's Bureau 
for Foreign Liaison. 'Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the Popular Committee of the 
People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison, and a nuwher of officials of various secretariats 

took part in these talks. Iranian Foreign Minister Dr ‘Ali Akbar Velayati and the delegation 
accompanying him represented the Iranian side. 


At the beginning of the meeting, Brother ‘Abd al-'‘Ati al-'Ubaydi delivered a speech in which 
he welcomed the Iranian guests, underlining the importance of this visit in consolidating 
the bonds of fraternity between the two fraternal peoples. He also emphasized the importance 
of meetings between the Great September Revolution and the Islamic revolution to confront 
the imperialist plans that are aimed at striking at the progressive forces and at imposing 
hegemony on peoples. He explained that the Great 1 September Revolution is earnestly 
seeking to form an Islamic force that could gather the Muslims and create out of them a 
force that could stand up to the states that are trying to dominate and impose hegemony on 
the destinies of the weak nations. He affirmed the stand of the Libyan Arab people on the 
side of the Iranian Muslim people to enable them to play their natural role and take part 

in the liberation of Palestine and the holy territories. 


The Iranian foreign minister delivered a speech in which he asserted the interest of the 
republic of the Islamic revolution in Iran in consolidating ties with the people of the 
Jamahiriyah, who have defended their country and achieved freedom and happiness for 
themselves. He affirmed that the Great 1 September Revolution and the Islamic revolution 
in Iran stand in the face of imperialism and its agents and that they support all the 
progressive forces in the world.

The Iranian foreign minister pointed out that resisting imperialism and Zionism will be 
accomplished by the unified action of the progressive forces in the Arab homeland and 
the Islamic world. He reaffirmed the backing of the Islamic revolution in Iran for the 
states of the steadfastness and confrontation front with all the resources available to 
the Iranian people. 


Afterwards a number of technical committees for cooperation in various fields were formed.

GOUKOUNI ON PEACEKEEPING FORCE, LIBYA ALLIANCE 


LD172354 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 17 Feb 82 


[Text] Ndjamena, 17 Feb (JANA) -- Chad President Goukouni Oueddei has declared for the 
first time that non-African forces were serving in the ranks of the African forces in Chad 
[words indistinct]. The Chad president was referring to the American forces which are 
operating within the peacekeeping forces of some African countries in Chad. 


The declaration of Chad President Goukouni Oueddei about the existence of U.S. forces 
came in a speech he made 2 days ago during the popular demonstrations which swept through 
Ndjamena to denounce the resolution of the recent Nairobi conference which called on Chad 
to enter into negotiations with the northern forces and announced the withdrawal of the 
African forces next June. 


Regarding relations between the Jamahiriyah and Chad and the historic role played by the 
Libyan forces in strengthening the freedom of the Chad people and in realizing peace in 
Chad, President Goukouni stressed in his speech that the security of Chad concerns us and 
that the security of the Jamahiriyah is linked to Chad's security. He said: We have 

always made certain not to provide the opportunity and excuse to colonialism and its 

agents to attack the Jamahiriyah and this is what had prompted us to ask our Libyan brothers 
to withdraw [from Chad]. 


He said Chad would never abandon its strategic alliance with Libya and that this is the 
logic of history. He said Chad will continue to express its gratitude and deep apprecia- 
tion to the Jamahiriyah for its valuable aid to its people which saved them from exhausting 
wars. 


He added in his speech to the demonstrators: Some African states want to prepare for the 
return of colonialism to Chad and for the return of civil war. 


He noted that the recent conference which was held in Nairobi was unlawful and that his 
government considers its resolutions as null and void. He said they [the participants 
of the conference] seek to establish colonialist domination over Chad. 


President Goukouni strongly criticized the attitude of some African countries which brag 
about democracy and call for its realization. He asked: How can these states demand 
democracy when their people are enduring oppression, humiliation and degradation?
