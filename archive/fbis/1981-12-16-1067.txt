During the review of relations between the two countries and peoples, the two sides 
recorded with appreciation the depth of the expanded and cooperative relations reached in 
various spheres, which stem from bilateral agreements signed between the two countries. 
The two sides expressed their appreciation for the high level of enthusiasm and progress 
achieved by these relations which reflect the solidarity, struggle and commitment of the 
two countries and the two peoples to oppose imperialism, colonialism, Zionism and racism 
and to achieve peace, justice, freedom and democracy. 


The two sides expressed their deep conviction that all conditions are available to strengthen 
and to expand cooperation in the interest of both countries. The two sides stressed that 
they attach great importance to the continuation of contacts and the exchange of informa- 
tion and experiences in all fields of mutual interest, especially in the political, economic, 
cultural and technical spheres. 


Fikre-Selassie praised the popular experiment applied in the Jamahiriyah and founded on 
the basis of the people's congresses and people's committees. Fikre-Selassie confirmed 
the solidarity of the revolutionary people of socialist Ethiopia and their revolutionary 
leadership with the people of the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in their 
struggle to build a new, just society. He gave a detailed explanation to Staff Maj ‘Abd 
as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and the accompanying delegation about the developments and gains of 
the Ethiopian revolution. In this connection, Fikre-Selassie expressed the appreciation 
and gratitude of the government and people of socialist Ethiopia for the full support and 
backing extended by the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and its leadership to 
the Ethiopian revolution in its struggle for economic and social reforms on a socialist 
basis. 


Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, for his part, praised the people and the leadership 
of socialist Ethiopia for the great reforms achieved in the spheres of economic and social 
development. He stressed the full support of the Jamahiriyah and its revolution for 
socialist Ethiopia in its struggle against imperialism and reaction and in reaching out 

to achieve freedom, peace, stability and social justice. 


When they reviewed the prevailing conditions on the regional and world level, the two 
delegations expressed their profound anxiety for the continuing increase in hostile 
imperialist assaults, spearheaded by the United States, and for the continued intervention 
in the internal affairs of Africa and the Arab region. They also expressed their anxiety 
about the escalation of military activity by American imperialism in the region. They 
condemned the provocative military maneuvers in the Mediterranean Sea, the Red Sea, the 
Indian Ocean and the Arabian Gulf region. They stressed that the actions of imperialism 
and its agents in the region are directed against the region's progressive forces and 
liberation movements in order to intimidate and to encircle them. 


The two sides expressed their appreciation for the unified stance taken by the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, socialist Ethiopia and Democratic Yemen during the meeting 
of the political committee set up by the Aden treaty and held in Addis Ababa in November 1981. 
The two sides noted that the statement issued by the committee at the end of its meetings 
clearly stressed the determination of the three revolutionary countries to defend their 
sovereignty and unity and the integrity of their territory and to confront all threats 
directed against them by imperialism. In this connection, they once again stressed their 
condemnation of the presence and the intervention of American forces in a number of African 
and Arab countries. They stressed the gravity of the presence of these forces, which are 
linked by the so-called strategic alliance between Reagan's administration and the Zionist 
regime in Palestine and the racist regime in Pretoria, and the danger it represents to 

peace and security in the region.

The Ethiopian side praised the decisive role played by the Jamahiriyah in reestablishing 
security and stability in Chad. When reviewing the struggle of the liberation movements 
in Africa and the Arab region, the two sides reaffirmed their total support for SWAPO, 
the legitimate and sole representative of the people of Namibia, and the opposition to 
the illegal occupation of its country by the hated Pretoria regime in collusion with its 
imperialist allies. In this regard, the two sides called on the international community 
to give total support to SWAPO in demanding the immediate withdrawal of the South 
African racist regime's forces and the full implementation of Security Council Resolution 
No 435. The two sides also declared their solidarity with the people of South Africa in 
their struggle for the liquidation of the racist facist regime. For this reason, the 
two sides appeal to all states, particularly Western states, to immediately halt their 
constant cooperation with the Pretoria regime and to strictly adhere to the OAU and UN 
resolutions with regard to sanctions against the facist regime in South Africa. 


The two sides reaffirmed their support for the confrontation states which have become 
the target of aggression by the racist regime in South Africa. They appealed to the 
peace--loving states to render every possible aid, particularly to Angola and Mozambique 
in their struggle against repeated colonialist-racist invasion. The two sides support 
the struggle of the Saharan people under the leadership of the Polisario and call on 
the sides concerned to speed up the implementation of the special measures to enable the 
Saharan people to gain self-determination in accordance with the OAU resolutions. The 
two sides strongly condemn the flagrant aggression by mercenary forces of the racist 
regime in South Africa against the sovereignty, independence and territorial integrity 
of the Republic of Seychelles in collusion with the imperialist forces. They regard 
this aggression as part of the imperialist plan to topple the progressive regimes in 
Africa. 


The two sides reviewed the situation in the Arab region and strongly condemned the con- 
tinued Zionist aggression against the Arab countries in the region. They rejected all 
agreemerts and propcSais not recognizing the firm right of the Palestinian Arab people 

to return to their own land, to practice self-determination and to establish their own 
independent state in their own land. The two sides reaffirmed their support for and full 
solidarity with the just struggle of the Palestinian Arab people under the leadership 

of the PLO, its sole legicvimate representative. 


The two sides reviewed issues relating to international peace and security and reaffirmed 
the need to consolidate solidarity among the progressive countries and peace-loving 

forces to realize victory in the struggle against imperialist reaction, racism and 
Zionism. In this connection, they strongly condemn the irresponsible policy of inter- 
ference, provocation and aggression being pursued by the Reagan administration in col- 
lusion with Zionism and reactionary forces against the Jamahiriyah, Ethiopia and the PDRY. 
The two sides condemn the unethical practices of the U.S. administration against the 
Jamahiriyah and its leadership and the oppressive political, economic and military 
measures taken by the Reagan administration against the Jamahiriyah aimed at obstructing 
the march of the Libyan Arab people in their struggle to control their own natural 
resources and to consolidate their people's authority.
