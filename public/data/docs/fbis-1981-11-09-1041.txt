ARAB PEOPLE'S COURT TO TRY NUMAYRI ‘SHORTLY' 


LD062036 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1700 GMT 6 Nov 81 


[Text] The chief of prosecution of the Arab People's Court has summoned the prosecution 
board to meet shortly to implement the decision made by the permanent secretariat of the 
Arab People's Congress during its emergency session on 12 October. The decision provides 
for the trial of agent Numayri, who has kneeled before America. 


CONGRESS, DEMONSTRATION PROTEST U.S. MANEUVERS 


Cable to Conference 
LD081510 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 8 Nov 81 


[Text] The permanent secretariat of the Arab People's Congress today sent a cable to the 
members of the International Conference for Solidarity With the People of the Jamahiriyah. 
It draws attention to the gravity of the aggressive U.S. maneuvers to be held in the Arab 
region in partnership with the agent regimes of Sudan, Somalia and Oman. 


The secretariat stresses that these aggressive maneuvers will make the situation more 

tense in the Arab region and in the world and will directly threaten the security and 
safety of the peoples of the region. It points out that by these maneuvers the U.S. admin- 
istration is challenging the will of world public opinion, recently manifested in the world 
peoples’ rejection of the U.S. military base and arms race policy. 


The cable explained that more than other people the people of the Jamahiriyahwill be 
exposed to the dangers of this maneuver. This is because a large part of the maneuvers 
will take place in the region close to the Arab Libyan territorial airspace and waters. 
It is reminiscent of the aggression committed by the 6th Fleet last August against the 
Gulf of Sidra and che repeated provocations by the U.S. administration against the 
Jamahiriyah. It calls on congress members to campaign for world condemnation of these 
plots and to condemn the terrorist policy of the U.S. administration against the people 
of the Jamahiriyah.

TRIPOLI ASKS ARAB TROOPS TO RESIST U.S. MANEUVERS 


LD070032 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 6 Nov 81 


(Unattributed commentary ] 


[Excerpts] Just as every Arab citizen is aware that the U.S. military maneuvers on Arab 
land are actually a direct flagrant invasion of the Arab homeland, every soldier in the 
Arab armed forces is also aware of this fact. Just as the agents and tools of imperialism 
resort to terrorism, persecution and suppression to prevent the masses from opposing the 
U.S. invasion, these same agents and tools resort to the same suppressive methods against 
the Arab officers and men to create a favorable opportunity for the U.S. forces to carry 
out the invasion plan which aims at occupying the Arab homeland.

The agents and tools are now busy selecting particular divisions from among the Arab mili- 
tary forces for participation side by side with the U.S. forces in the military maneuvers. 
The selection operation is based on the assumption by these agents and tools that there are 
among the Arab armed forces officers and men who support them and who would consequently be 
ready to participate in these maneuvers, which are in fact no less than a direct invasion 
of the Arab land. 


The agents and tools are exerting tireless efforts to select officers and men favorable to 
them, who would take part in maneuvers or rather invasions of the Arab homeland without 
clashing with the U.S. officers and men. There are numerous precedents for such clashes, 
about which the agents and tools are worried. The imperialist United States itself fears 
their repetition. On several occasions in the past, Arab officers and men have shot down 
U.S. aircraft and executed U.S. officers and men who were participating with them in mili- 
tary maneuvers. 


All the divisions entrusted by the tools of government in a number of Arab countries with 

the task of participation in the U.S. military maneuvers must know that they are being driven 
to perform duties contradictory to military duty and the honor of fighting soldiers. They 
must know that their selection by the agent tools casts doubts and suspicions on their 
officers and men and makes them the center of suspicion by other members of the forces not 
invited to participate in the maneuvers. They must know that if these U.S. military 
maneuvers, which are actually acts of direct invasion of the Arab homeland, take place 
according to the plan prepared by Washington, the suspicions and doubts about these officers 
and men would then be justified. 


Every Arab officer and soldier selected to participate in the U.S. invasion of the Arab 
homeland should ask himself: Why did I join the armed forces? Why did I choose this 
honorable career? When the Arab soldier finds the answer to these questions, he will 
readily discover that the agent ruling tools that ordered him to participate in the 
maneuvers alongside the U.S. forces have in fact ordered him to become an enemy of his 
nation and a traitor to his people and homeland. 


Let the Arab officers and men remember that the fierce battles fought by the Arab nation 
since the beginning of this century have been against imperialism and Zionism and that the 
true enemies of the Arab nation are imperialism and Zionism. It is disgraceful for one to 
close his eyes to the danger threatening the Arabs. Furthermore, it is disgraceful for 
the soldier, whose main task is to defend his homeland, to become a party alongside the 
invader to the attack on his homeland. 


Should any soldier attempt to justify his participation in the invasion by claiming that he 
was deceived, taken unaware and did not realize the dimensions of the plot, as of today 
there can be no justification for any soldier trying to justify his participation with such 
an excuse. 


God is our witness; we have alerted you. The right way is now clear. It is better for us 
all to see things clearly today rather than tomorrow. 


The regime of the assassinated As-Sadat used to exhaust itself in selecting forces for 
participation in U.S. maneuvers which took place in sensitive vital spots inside Cairo and 
other cities. Zionist and U.S. intelligence helped the regime of agent As-Sadat in this 
selection. Yet, in spite of all this, the U.S. planes that crashed during the preparations 
and past U.S. maneuvers, before As-Sadat was executed, were shot down by the fire of 
Egyptian soldiers. The ruling regime imagined that these soldiers were favorable to it 

and were agreeable to participating in the maneuvers with the U.S. soldiers. But neither 
U.S. nor Israeli intelligence succeeded in foiling the plots and designs of the Egyptian 
Arab officers and men who pledged to exonerate themselves and their Arab Egyptian armed 
forces from the charge of subservience to the agents and imperialists.

In our Arab armed forces, there are unknown men who are no less dedicated to martyrdom 
than their brothers who executed the death sentence on As-Sadat. These men will not 
hesitate to oppose the U.S. forces. They will never hesitate to join the masses of their 
Arab nation to check the U.S. imperialist invasion, which is being implemented at present 
under the guise of joint maneuvers. 


Instead of history recording that they were used to carry out duties conflicting with 
their military honor, history will most certainly record that the officers and men 
selected to participate in the maneuver settled the account with the invading U.S. forces 
and joined ranks with the masses of their nation against the U.S. imperialist 
aggression.
