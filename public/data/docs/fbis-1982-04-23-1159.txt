CONVENING OF JOINT ARAB DEFENSE COUNCIL PROPOSED 


LD222030 Tripoli JANA in English 1755 GMT 22 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 22 Apr (JANA) -- The under-secretary for technical affairs at Foreign 
Liaison Bureau today met with Arab ambassadors accredited to jamahiriyah as part of 
jamahiriyah's nationalist endeavour to mobilise Arab potentialities and direct them against 
the Arab nation's real enemy as to deter it and repulse its criminal aggression. 


He informed the ambassadors of jamahiriyah's proposal to convene the Arab joint defence 
council, comprised of foreign and defence ministers, in an urgent session in order to 
approve a comprehensive Arab strategy to repulse the Zionist aggression, send urgent 
military backing and provide urgent financial backing to consolidate Palestinian people's 
steadfastness. 


He also stressed the necessity of opening the borders to fedayeen operations and placing 
part of Arab countries’ forces under disposal of the Palestine Liberation Organisation and 
Lebanese National Movement to confront repeated aggression on the Palestinian revolution 
and Lebanese people.

JANA CRITICIZES ISRAEL'S ACTIONS, 'U.S. ALLIES' 


LD221442 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0915 GMT 22 Apr 82 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 22 Apr (JANA) -- Commenting on the Zionist aggression in Lebanon yes- 
terday, the JANA political editor writes: 


While Israeli aircraft are engaged in concentrated raids on the Lebanese territories in 
flagrant defiance of the Arab nation, while Israeli shelling is destroying peaceful Arab 
towns and villages, while Israeli bullets are killing Palestinian and Lebanese Arabs, 

while Israel is revealing its colonialist aspiration in a provocative and inhuman entity 
that rejects the divine message and does not recognize faiths, as a state of racist and 
vagabond gangs engaged in daily arrogance and repression of the true owners of the land, while 
all this is taking place, the Saudi regime which is plotting against the Arab nation is 
waving the so-called peace plans and filthy reactionary hands are extending to declare 

their alliance with colonialism, at the fore of which is the leader of international 
terrorism, the United States. Arab oil is turning into strategic credit in the interest 

of the enemy within the framework of escalating the campaigns of reactionary and imperialism 
plotting against the jamahiriyah, engineered by the princes of shame, disgrace and humilia- 
tion of the base Saudi regime. 


Let the allies of the United States in the region know that their denunciations and their 
strikes and their cables, previous and future ones, will not be able to restore one drop 
of the Arab blood. This is a lesson of history, they will learn.

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES MESSAGE FROM GDR LEADER 


LD201804 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 20 Apr 82 


[Text] The brother commander of the revolution has received a written message from Erich 
Honecker, chairman of the GDR Council of State, in which he praised the brave stands taken 
by the Great 1 September Revolution against all imperialist and reactionary designs and 
plots. He affirmed that the unscrupulous policy of escalating the arms race pursued by 
the U.S. administration and NATO in their efforts [words indistinct] on the socialist 

and nonaligned countries threatens the peace of mankind more than at any time since 

World War II. He pointed out that the provocative aggressive policy of the American 
administration in the Mediterranean region and in the Middle East poses a grave threat 

to world peace and aims at robbing the independent countries of their natural wealth. 

He affirmed his country's adherence to peace and security in the Middle East on the basis 
of the complete withdrawal of the Zionist enemy from all the occupied Arab territories 
and the safeguarding of the rights of the Palestinian people, including their right to 
return to their homeland, to determine their fate and to set up their own independent 
Palestinian state. 


In his message to the brother commander, Honecker said the safeguarding of peace and 
security in the Mediterranean region, where the Continents of Africa, Europe and Asia meet, 
is of special importance and is strongly and closely linked with peace and security in 
Europe. Hence, I agree with you about the existence of the great dangers posed by the 
American military presence and the provocative maneuvers in the region.
