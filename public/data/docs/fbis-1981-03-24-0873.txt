LIBYA

LEBANESE FRONT OFFICIAL PRATSES AIR COVER OFFER 


LD232042 Tripoli JANA in English 1505 GMT 23 Mar 81 


[Text] Beirut, 23 Mar (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--Mr. Tariq Alamili, member of the 
Lebanese Patriotic Movement [L.P.N.] in south Lebanon, said that the Jamahiriyah's offer 
to provide air cover for south Lebanon proves the goodwill and genuineness of the slogans 
raised by the Great Al-Fatah Revolution. In a statement to JANA's correspondent in the 
Lebanese south, he said that the Lebanese Government's rejection of the offer proves 

that it is not willing to take an active role in the Arab-(Israeli) conflict although 
Lebanon has suffered the Zionist occupation of her lands as a direct result of this 
conflict. 


Mr. Alamili added that the Lebanese authorities must take a decisive stand on this issue, 
put all of its efforts into this battle against the Zionist enemy and accept the Libyan 
offer. He also praised the Jamahiriyah's assistance to south Lebanon saying that it has 
greatly contributed to the steadfastness of the south.
