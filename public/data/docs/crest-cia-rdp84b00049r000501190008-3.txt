SUBJECT: Restricted SIG Meeting: Libya Stair Step Plan 

Attached is a memorandum from NSC Staff which recommends 
that Steps 6-8 of the Stair Step Plan be reviewed through the 
Joint Reconnaissance Center (JRC). This proposal will be dis- 
cussed at the scheduled SIG, Monday, 29 November 1982, at 3:45 
P.M. 

Also received, from the Joint Staff, is a modified 
Operations Summary sheet (Tab D of the Interagency Review). 
This has been forwarded by CINCEUR and is concurred in by DIA. 
It should replace the present sheet, previously forwarded with 
the Review, and will be the basis for the JCS SIG briefing 
(with the addition of CINCEUR's proposed next steps).
