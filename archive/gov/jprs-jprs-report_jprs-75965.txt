The Central Committee hall witnessed last week a student conference which 
included the university student leaderships and which was attended by a 
large crowd of students. The National Party leaders attending the confer- 
ence were: Fikri Makram ‘Ubayd, the National Party secretary general; 
Hasan Mansur, the minister of information; Nabawi Isma'il, the minister 
of interior, and Hilmi ‘Abd-al-Akhir, the minister of state for People's 
Assembly affairs. 


Attending from the teaching faculties were: Dr Hasan Humam, the leader 
of the student unions; Dr Mahir Mahran, the head of the gynecology section 
of ‘Ayn Shams University; Dr Hamdi 'Abd-al-Rahman, the professor of civil 
law at ‘Ayn Shams University.

The conference opened with an address delivered by Fikri Makram 'Ubayd 
who welcomed the students on behalf of President al-Sadat. He then 
presented a quick review of che domestic and external political condition 
of Egypt's policy [sic]. 





Nabawi Iema'il, the minister of interior, talked about the recent incidents 
in Asyut and Alexandria governorates and referred to the regretable role 
played by the communist organizations, by the rejectionist front in Syria, 
South Yemen, Libya and Iraq and by the Soviet communist organizations in 

an attempt to fragment our domestic front. He then stated that they have 
failed to detonate their explosives in the installations and they are now 
trying to sow the seeds of sectarian sedition. All of us, both Christians 
and Muslims, must confront these attempts so as to preserve our domestic 
front which has lived and will continue to live calmly. 


The student dialogue then began to raise questions regarding Israel's 
intransigence on the issue of the settlements. 


Hilmi 'Abd-al-Akhir replied that the initiative is proceeding according 
to its timetable and that it has even moved ahead of the timetable, 
considering that evacuation from the Sinai was scheduled to take place 

in 3 years and that it will now take place in 24 months. He added that 
the guarantees of the initiative are Egypt's strength which is well known 
to Israel. 


Another student asked about Egypt's role in the Palestinian self-rule. 


Hilmi 'Abd-al-Akhir answered that Egypt expects the self-rule problem to 
end on 26 May and that then comes the role of the Palestinians in deter- 
mining their future. He also said that the Palestinians must sit with 
the other party around the negotiations table and that Egypt will have 
thus put the foot of the Palestinians at the beginning of the road.
