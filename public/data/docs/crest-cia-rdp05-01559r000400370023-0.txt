Summary (AI): A Kampala radio broadcast reports that Libyan leader Muammar al-Qadhdhafi sent a message to Ugandan President Idi Amin, then OAU chairman, opposing an OAU heads-of-state summit on Angola because it would divide Africa. This is Libyan foreign policy with no link to Tripoli sites.
• Qadhdhafi said an OAU heads-of-state and government extraordinary summit on Angola would divide Africa and should not be held at all.
• The message was broadcast by Kampala Domestic Service on 3 December 1975.
• The document is an FBIS London transmission.
• 1975-12-03: Libyan chargé d'affaires in Uganda delivered a special message from Qadhdhafi to Idi Amin rejecting an OAU heads-of-state summit on Angola
