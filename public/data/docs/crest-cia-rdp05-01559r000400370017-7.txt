Summary (AI): FBIS translation of a live 7 October 1975 speech by Qadhdhafi at a rally in Ujaylah, marking the fifth anniversary of the expulsion of Italians from Libya. He denounces the United States, praises the 1970 expulsion, and announces the Basic Popular Conference and General National Congress structure. Nothing here is specific to Tripoli.
• Speech broadcast live by Tripoli Domestic Service at 1730 GMT on 7 October 1975.
• Qadhdhafi said the Italians numbered about 20,000 and that Libya could have carried out a massacre in Tripoli but did not.
• Speech announced a new structure of Basic Popular Conferences (about 370) feeding into a General National Congress.
• 1970-10-07: Expulsion of Italians from Libya, anniversary marked in the 1975 speech; Qadhdhafi said about 20,000 Italians were held and pardoned rather than killed.
