• The CIA was stepping up support for dissident military officers, businessmen and technocrats inside Libya and for Libyan exiles.
• US options included closing Libya's remaining Peoples Bureaus, shutting Libyan Arab Airlines offices, and boycotting Libyan oil.
• Libyan military units reportedly mutinied after the April raid, and the air force used hired Syrian pilots to crush the rebellions.

The memorandum is for your information and requires no action. The 
attached article attributes to senior US officials and intelligence officials 
statements that Libya has resumed preparation for terrorist acts and discusses 
current US strategy for dealing with Libyan intentions. The article has the 
earmarks of an official disclosure intended to inform Qadhafi that the Reagan 
administration is prepared to teach him another lesson. UDAC has checked with 
C/NE, A/NIO/NESA, and EO/NESA and the consensus jis that while the Walcott 
Story discusses policy, there appear to be no disclosures of classified 

intelligence, per se.
