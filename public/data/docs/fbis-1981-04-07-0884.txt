LIBYA

SUDANESE PREPARATIONS FOR CHAD ‘INVASION’ REPORTED 


LD051356 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 5 Apr 81 


[Text] Khartoum--The Saudi Government has provided the Sudanese regime with huge 
quantities of arms and military hardware and other military equipment, including 
anti-aircraft guns, heavy artillery ammunition, mobile heavy guns, machineguns, radio 
telecommunications equipment and a large number of armored vehicles, in addition to 
huge quantitiesof tents and military uniforms. All these have now been sent to the 
Sudanese border areas adjoining Chad in preparation for the invasion of Chad and the 
occupation of the Abeche area, and then handing these arms over to rebel Hissein Habre. 


It is worth mentioning that the Egyptian forces at present occupy the Sudanese 
territories and control all strategic positions. The Egyptian forces entered the 
Sudanese territories with the approval of the Sudanese president and against the will 
of the Sudanese Army on the pretext of the presence of problems on the Chadian border. 
Military sources in Khartoum said today that Sudanese Army forces have left their 
positions in vital spots and have been moved to the Sudanese-Chadian border to justify 
placing Egyptian forces in the positions they have left. 


NUMAYRI'S COMMENTS AGAINST AL-QADHDHAFI ATTACKED 


LD060850 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 6 Apr 81 
[Unattributed commentary] 


[Text] The American HERALD TRIBUNE has published some statements attributed to the 
Sudanese ruler Numayri. When we in the Libyan revolutionary information media learned 
of them, we refrained from broadcasting them at the time because we could not believe 
that they could be uttered by a rational person or a ruler running the affairs of a 
country and people, whatever our opinions of him might be. This is simply because 

these statements reflect a lack of ability, self-confidence and respect and are contrary 
to international usage and indicate resentment against the people of the Jamahiriyah. 


To verify these statements, we asked to be supplied with the text of these statements. 
It transpired that they have actually been made by the Sudanese ruler Numayri, and 

what wretched statements and what wretched logic they were. The ruler of Sudan 
remained silent for a long time, only to come out and utter such ridiculous statements. 
For he said that he is in a state of war with Col Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi. He reproached 
the West for not checking Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, adding that he must be got rid of, even 
if this means killing him. The Sudanese ruler said that the is personally ready to 
help anyone who intends to carry of such a task. 


Thus Numayri unmasks his true face before the world to show that he is a criminal, a 
butcher and an instigator of crime. He also shows that he has lost all qualifications 
and his self-confidence and respect. Bv his statements Numayri has expressed his 
genuine fear from the revolutionary model presented by the Great 1 September Revolution 
and revealed the hidden resentment he harbors against it because of its historical 

role in Africa and the world. While freedom is daily becoming consolidated in Libya, 
Numayri has been selling Sudan outright and has flung its doors wide open to the 
Egyptian Army and the American bases, thereby flouting the honor of the free Sudanese 
people who have long fought to rid themselves of imperialism and reaction. 


The Arab masses in Sudan and indeed all the Arab masses fully realize that the call 
for murder made by the dictator of Sudan was a foolish, naive ani small-minded cali. 
What is taking place in the Jamahiriyah is a historic, genuine revolution supported 
by all the Arab masses from the Gulf to the ocean and by all the living forces in 
the world. In Libya there is no venal president or ruler governing on behalf of 
the people.

In Libya there is only a people who became free by their revolution and took control 
of their wealth and arms and assumed their own authority. Such feverish cries as 
uttered by the ruler of Sudan will not save him from the revolution of the Sudanese 
people and will not relieve him of his political impotence and economic paralysis 

and social collapse. They will not save him from the grip of the Sudanese people, who 
are bound to their nation by their objectives, destiny and fate. 


The secretary of the Popular Committee of the Popular Bureau for Foreign Liaison yester- 
day evening met with the ambassadors of the Arab, African and Islamic countries 
individ.lly. During these meetings he pointed out the dangers inherent in the resumption 
of re).ations between the Sudanese and Egyptian regimes, particularly as the Egyptian 
regim: is in league with the Zionist entity and American imperialism. He said that 

the statements made by the Sudanese pygmy regime and its call on the Arab nation to 
restore relations with the Egyptian regime confirm the fears which we and every Arab 
citizen have in his regard, particularly as the Arab reaction is not yet equal to the 
level of this development. 


In his statements to the ambassadors, the secretary of the Popular Committee touched 
on the statements made by the head of the Sudanese regime to the HEARLD TRIBUNE in 
recent days. He said that we could not believe these statements and refrained from 
commenting on them until we verified that they were actually uttered by him. He added 
that these statements said that the Sudanese ruler is in a state of war with Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, that he believes that the world should try to rid itself of the brother 
colonel by any means, even by killing him if need be, and that he personally, that 

is Numayri, is personally prepared to offer help to anyone who plans to carry out such 
a killing. 


The secretary of the Foreign Liaison Bureau affirmed that as far as the Jam-hiriyah 

is concerned, he regards such behavior as a departure from Arab values and echics and 
also a departure from international usage and defamatory to the Arab nation. especially 
as it is the behavior of the head of an Arab state. He affirmed that the head of 

the Sudanese regime is working according to a clearly defined plan: to throw himself 
in the lap of American imperialism. Thus he has departed from the resolutions of the 
Arab and Islamic summit conferences. 


In conclusion, he affirmed that if this is Numayri'’s choice, then we are prepared to 
face up to these stands.
