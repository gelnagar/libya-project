• Wilson was suspected of recruiting former Green Berets to train for the Libyan government and of organizing American pilots to fly for the Libyan air force.

• Newsweek reported in July 1981 an alleged hit list from a Truth or Consequences, N.M. residence.
• 1980-10: Tafoya allegedly shot Libyan dissident Faisal Zagallai twice in the head; Zagallai survived but lost vision in one eye.
• 1979-05-28: Jaguar belonging to Robert Manina was firebombed at about 2 a.m.; Manina was not hurt.
