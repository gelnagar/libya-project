LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES PEOPLE'S CONGRESS 9 OCT 


LD102022 Tripoli JANA in English 1841 GMT 10 Oct 82 
[Quotation marks as received] 


[Text] Al-Bayyan al Awwal, Dhulhijja 24, Oct, 10, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- As part of 
the continuous inciting for the strengthening of people's authority and people's democratic 
process, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the revolution, yesterday attended the 
lst session of the Basic People's Congress (BPC) in its 3rd regular session at Al-Bayyan 

al Awwal city. 


The leader in his speech addressed the BPC's which are holding their 3rd regular session 
prior to the General People's Congress (GPC), through Al-Bayyan al Awwal's (BPC). 


In his speech, the leader, stressed two main points. First, the democratic way in which 
the BPC puts the items for discussion on the agenda especially the general agenda by which 
the masses of the BPC decide the democratic and foreign policies. 


In this respect, the leader said that from now on the masses will put forward the agenda 
in order to further stress that the Libyan people are free and the masters of themselves 
without a ruler or a government. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the people's committees will be one of the sources for formulating 
the agenda through the files which are to be tabled to the people's congresses in all fields. 
He said the people's committees will assume such a role because as they are also responsible 
for implementing the resolutions of the people's congresses, these congresses might ask them 
to issue laws or certain rules that would help in implementing the resolutions of the 


people's congresses. 


The leader said the role of the people's congresses secretariats is to table the files, 
observations and requests of the people's committees to members of the Basic People's 
Congresses in the Jamahiriyah so as to clarify its views regarding the issues dealt with 
in these files and observations. 


Dealing with the method in which future internal issues are to be included on the general 
agenda, the leader said: "Any internal issue adopted by any Basic People's Congress shall 
be referred to the General Secretariat of the General People's Congress, which shall in 
turn circulate it to all Basic People's Congresses throughout the Jamahiriyah for debate 
in a true democratic method. 


The function of the General People's Congress would be to unify points of view and formulate 
the decisions of the Basic People's Congresses. 


Regarding the second point expressed by masses in demonstrations and public gatherings 
regarding issues imposed on us by the enemy, the leader said: "We should decide on how to 
face death and our fate. We should prepare for making sacrifices and showing resoluteness 
to stop the Zionist death wagon that is intent on destroying the Arab nation." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed the seriousness of such issues and the responsibility of the 
people towards them, adding that the Zionist death wagon that destroyed Palestine, Lebanon 
and Egypt will overrun Syria and the rest of the Arab nation and that the people should 

seriously consider confrontation because they will be responsible for all gains or losses. 


He said: "In the era of the masses, there is no hero who can single-handedly take such a 
grave decision. Only the entire body of the masses can decide their fate and take the 
decision that can safeguard it."

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: “Arab rulers cannot decide the fate of the Arab nation because 
the Arab masses are the true target of the enemy. There was no president or king among 
these who were killed in Lebanon. The decision affects every Arab citizen. It is the 
masses which the Zionist enemy seeks to destroy. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi also dealt with the revolutionary programme that is to be formulated 
by the people's congresses for mass mobilisation to resist the inevitable approach of the 
Zionist death wagon. Colonel al-Qadhdhafi reminded his audience that the Zionists issued 
an appeal to 12 million Jews. This appeal falls within their expansionist plan that later 
calls for the settlement of Jews in the Arab nation, replacing the Arabs with a Zionist 
empire through which America would control the Arab nation, in implementation of the 
imperialist-Zionist schemes for annihilating the Arabs. 


He said: "The train of Zionist death will continue if the Zionist advance remains at its 
current pace. We cannot wait for our annihilation. We are forced to confront our fate 
and stop this death wagon. Otherwise, our lives will be at the mercy of America. Such 
action of confrontation on our part requires total mobilisation which provides all means 
that will guarantee the defeat of the enemy." 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has pointed out the Revolutionary Committee's role concerning 
the revolutionary programme saying that their role is to educate the masses but not to con- 
vince them, because the masses have the freedom of choice. The leader said: "We are 
facing a dangerous war with America and the Israelis as well as local reaction. This 
danger should be shouldered by the people when you say that you have decided to live on 
bread and water only. This is also a serious decision because nobody can force you to 

live on bread and water unless you decide this yourself." 


The leader also warned the Arab masses that the Zionist enemy with full U.S. backing is 
determined to destroy the Arabs and will inevitably try to do so. They have forced Egypt 
out of the battle and occupied Lebanon and are trying to destroy Syria. They will also 
come back to Egypt, and transform it into a colony and then expel the Moslems from there 
in order to put the Israelis there instead or leave the Copts on their own." 


"The expansionist plans will continue because the Zionist enemy believes that the exis- 
tence of the Arab nation contradicts its existence. The enemy has been entangled because 
of the establishment of their state in the heart of the Arab world. They will not give it 
up. They have no other alternative but to destroy the Arab nation, because its existence, 
even in the future, is a danger to their existence." 


He went on to say, “the Zionists are determined to slaughter the Arabs even if they raise 
white flags. They very much fear the Arab race because it will destroy them. Therefore, 
the Zionists are trying every possible means to exterminate the Arabs. He added, "The 
revolutionary programme is the programme that includes the overall mobilization with high 
morale and determination for challenge. The Arab nation's situation is quite dangerous 
because of continuous Zionist expansionism which should be confronted. The world has 
become convinced that we have the right to move and destroy Israel because we are being 
subjected to a war of genocide. 


The leader said, "The barbaric Zionist invasion of Lebanon and mass murders they carried 
out i. Beirut has clearly uncovered the true face of the Zionists, America and the world. 
It has been proven that when a people are subjected to aggression, no one, not the 
Security Council, the Red Cross or any humanitarian organization in the world can come to 
their aid. The world can only condemn, but cannot do anything more.”

This has been confirmed by events following the Zionist invasion of Lebanon. He said 
only force can stop the death wagon and that the Arabs are left with no choice but to 
face death by the use of force. 


Regarding the revolutionary programme for providing the minimum level of food security 
and maximum level of armament, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "We need to increase the 
national product to a maximum with which we will haveto provide the minimum level of 
food security. Everything else will go to armament in order to confront the enemy and 
deliver blows that will avenge Arab honour." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added: "The Arabs have a rightful claim to Palestine because it is 
their land. They need power, however. The Arabs, moreover, have the human and material 
resources and we can form an army stronger than the Israelis. But we are in need of 
total mobilisation so that these capabilities are utilised for realising power. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said all energies and capabilities should be concentrated and reminded 
his audience that the children of Palestine and Lebanese women were killed before their 

own eyes. He continued: "Not one of us can accept such a fate. But the Zionists will 

not differentiate between man, woman or child and it is therefore our duty to arm ourselves." 


In this respect, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed the importance of training and arming women 
so that they would fall as easy prey to enemies. [as received] 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "The gangs and scums of treachery and subservience to America, 
Israel and Arab reaction should be exposed. Such people are wanted for justice and giving 
Libya a bad reputation because of what they smuggled abroad. We will not allow any 
trivial person to give Libya a bad reputation abroad. Such people are charged with high 
treason because for them collaboration with the Israelis and Americans. [sentence as 
received] They should be killed not because they constitute any danger, but because of 
their high treason. 


He said, "It is the Libyan people's responsibility to liquidate such scums who are distorting 
Libya's image. The decision to liquidate them or not, and their return or not, should 

be decided by the Libyan people before the peoples of the world. Because, what will 

happen afterwards expresses the people's will. If the people decide to liquidate its 

enemy then the people cannot be described as terrorist." 


He elaborated saying, "U.S. policy is a terrorist one, and so its government and intelli- 
gence. To say that the 200 million Americans are terrorists is something unacceptable.
