JANA: Sudan Rebels Advancing Toward Malakal 
LD242033 Tripoli JANA in English 1856 GMT 24 Feb 84 


[Text] London, Jumada al Ulla 20, February 24, JAMAHIR- 
IYAH NEWS AGENCY — A statement issued today by the 
Sudan Liberation Front said that Sudanese revolution fighters 


V. 27 Feb 84 


are advancing towards Malakal in the Upper Nile Province. The 
statement said the fighters are shelling government forces in the 
city and that they have destroyed two of their armored vehicles. 


London radio said that on their advance towards the city, the 
fighters attacked two armored vehicles belonging to the govern- 
ment army. 


JANA: Paris Radio Says Sudan Regime Collapsing 
LD241316 Tripoli JANA in English 0840 GMT 24 Feb 84 


[Text] Paris, Jumada al Ulla 20, Feb 24, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The France inter radio [Paris Domestic 
Service] confirmed that the regime of hireling Numayri is col- 
lapsing due to its economic and the political difficulties, as well 
as the armed uprising in the south. The radio's correspondent in 
Sudan reported that Numayri is facing two battles: a political 
one in Khartoum and a military one in the south jungles. 


The radio ridiculed allegations by Numayri describing the rev- 
olution against his regime as hostile action by Libya and Ethi- 
opia, and his false classification of the revolutionary fighters as 
“pirates”. In contrast to Numayri’s claims, the radio went on to 
say the revolution in the south everyday gains new fighters from 
various parts of the country. 


The morale of the army has become very low, the radio said. 
Moreover, the economy is suffering particularly after the 
freezing of oil exploration and the Junglei canal project, the paper 
[as received] pointed out. Faced with increasing uprising, 
Numayri resorted to throwing innocent people into jail, the paper 
said. 


Radio Bardai Cited on Chad 
LD260733 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 0015 GMT 26 Feb 84 


[Text] Radio Bardai, which speaks for the GUNT [Transitional 
National Union Government], confirmed 2 days ago that fierce 
battles have taken place between the GUNT forces headed by 
Goukouni Oueddei and the forces of the mutineer Hissein Habre, 
which caused 80 deaths and scores of injuries among the forces 
of the rebellion clique in Ndjamena. The radio, which was picked 
up in Lome, explained that these violent battles, which had lasted 
for several days as of 10 February, took place 400 kilometers 
south of latitude 16 ina valley, 150 kilometers away from Abache 
in eastern Chad. 


The radio stressed that the GUNT forces scored a crushing 
victory during these battles against the forces of the rebel Habre, 
affirming that they will continue the struggle in Chad against the 
French and Zairean occupation. The radio added that the GUNT 
forces have captured weapons and large trucks loaded with 
ammunition. 


The radio affirmed that French imperialism will never score a 
victory over the Chadian revolution.
