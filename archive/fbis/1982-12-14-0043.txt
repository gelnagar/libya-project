LIBYA

JANA: U.S. PRESSES BRAZIL TO STOP ARMS TO LIBYA 


LD131130 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMf 13 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 Dec (JANA) -- Informed political sources in Latin America have 
reported that during his visit to Brazil President Reagan classified the states hostile 
to U.S. policy. On the list drawn up by the U.S. delegation to the talks with Brazilian 
officials were the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, Angola, and Nicaragua. 


The U.S. delegation asked Brazil not to have dealings with these states, and specifi- 
cally to reduce the volume of relations with them. 


These sources mention that U.S. Secretary of State George Shultz tried to pressure the 
Brazilian Government to halt the sale of weapons to the Jamahiriyah, considering it a 
state unfriendly to the United States. 


The political editor of JANA has commented on these reports, saying: This U.S. demand 
is considered new evidence of the hostile U.S. policy carried out by the Reagan 
administration against the liberated peoples, a policy pursued regularly and increas- 
ingly by U.S. President Reagan against all those who reject U.S. imperialism, injustice, 
oppression, and hegemony. However, it is in fact a policy incapable of comprehending 
the logic of history and the will for freedom, and an indication of aggression and the 
desire to shake international stability, security, and peace.
