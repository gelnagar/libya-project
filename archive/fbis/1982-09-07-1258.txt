REAGAN SPEECH ON MIDDLE EAST CRITICIZED 


LDO31516 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 3 Sep 82 


[Text] Voice of America has broadcast the text of a speech by U.S. President Reagan. 
Voice of America also said this speech contains new American initiatives with regard 
to the so-called peace process in the Arab region. 


In his speech, Reagan said his pian was presented to the Israelis, the Egyptian regime, 
Jordan and the Saudi kingdom. 


When reviewing the speech, it appears that it does not at all go beyond the traitorous 
stable David agreements, which drive the Israeli death train from Tel Aviv to kill 
the Arabs one by one. Reagan said: These agreements are still the basis of our policy. 


In his speech, Reagan said the question now is how to reconcile between what he calls 

the security demands of the Israelis and the legitimate rights of the Palestinians. 

He added that to reach this requires concessions. This is a clear call to the Palestinians 
to surrender and to yield to the Zionist demands. Reagan has called upon the Palestinian 
people to recognize that their political aspirations are inextricably bound to a 
recognition of what he calls the right of the Zionist entity to a secure future — the 
security that knows no limit, but only knows expansion and domination for the establishment 
of a Zionist empire, which would embrace vast parts of the Arab homeland. 


The U.S. President also called on Arab states to accept the Zionist entity as a reality 
although he admitted that the legitimacy of this entity has only been recognized by a 
small number of states. Reagan pointed out that it is the right of this entity to 

demand that his neighbors recognize these facts, namely, according to the American and the 
Zionist logic, with regard to the use of the logic of force, as has always been the 

case in the Israeli wars against the Arabs in 1948, 1967, 1973 and 1982, that is, to 
surrender under the threat of force. 


Reagan asked the Arab stdtes to make further land concessions in return for what he 
calls peace. In his plan, Reagan categorically rejected the establishment of a 
Palestinian state in the occupied West Baik and the Gaza Strip. Moreover, he asked 
that occupied Jerusalem remain an undivided city, namely, a capital for the Israelis. 


Reagan pointed out that the strategic importance of this region prompted him to present 
such a plan. He said: There is an absolute moral duty behind my plan. This is the 
moral duty that made him consolidate the killing of the innocent in Lebanon by the 
thousands. This is the moral duty that made him back the Zionist invasion and use 

the veto in the Security Council with a view to preventing the condemnation of this 
invasion. Reagan then boasted that he has always supported what he calls the heroic 
fighting of the Zionists and that the security of this entity is his personal 
commitment. 


This plan is the second phase of the invasion of Lebanon and an unsuccessful attempt 
to improve the image of America. It is a new American assertion of widening the plot 
against the Arab nation and of backing the Israeli death train, which is proceeding 
toward the Arab homeland in new forms and methods. The Zionist rejection of this plan 
is a farce aimed at creating a fuss in order to provide the opportunity for the 
capitulating regimes to accept it. This was asserted by Reagan himself when he said 
that he has presented this plan in agreement with the Zionists, the Egyptian regime, 
Jordan and the Saudi kingdom.

U.S. Proposals Assailed 


LD040432 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 3 Sep 82 


[Station commentary] 


[Excerpts] The simplest way to describe the Reagan proposal for solving the so-called 
Middle East crisis is to brand it a new U.S. aggression against the Arab nation and a 
culmination of the harrowing calamities that have befallen this nation. U.S. imperialism 
directly or indirectly lent support to the planning and implementation of these calamities. 


This proposal has come as a political harvest of the military defeat suffered by the Arabs 
in the Beirut war. It comes as a conquerer dictating his terms to the vanquished or the 
strong to the weak. It comes also as an exploitation of the psychological and moral 
breakdown and collapse of the Arab nation to make it cling unconsciously to the provisions 
of this U.S. liquidationist-capitulationist solution. It also comes only a few day before 
the convening of the summit of garbage in Morocco so that the Arab rulers may consider and 
pass it for implementation. 


It also comes while the basic and direct part of the scheme, namely, the Palestinian side, 
suffered collapse and destruction under the effect of the Zionist military (?machine) that 
used various deadly weapons, internationally banned and imported from U.S. imperialism, to 
scatter the Palestinians and drive them out of Lebanon by force until they eventually 
submit to U.S. demands and solutions which came as if imposed on them by brute military 
forces. The proposal comes also as an attempt to rescue the U.S. agents among the Arab 
rulers from the delicate position in which they found themselves versus the revolutionary 
wrath of the Arab masses, which pointed to a possible outbreak of a popular revolution as 
a result of their collusion with the Zionist enemy in physically destroying the revolu- 
tionary Palestinian presence in Lebanon. 


Finally, the scheme comes as a naive attempt to whitewash the U.S. image before the Arab 
masses after the explosion of the anger of the Arab masses as a result of the U.S. stand 
and U.S. political, military and economic support for the barbarous Zionist invasion of 
Lebanon. 


Everyone today is talking about the positive aspects in the proposal of the terrorist 
Reagan. For example, they are talking about freezing the building of Zionist settlement. 
However, they have not asked about the fate of 120 settlements built so far in the West 
Bank and Gaza Strip. They talk about the return of the West Bank and the Gaza Strip to 
Arab sovereignty through a weak, dwarfed and disfigured entity called self-rule which 
will be linked to the royalist regime in Jordan. However, they did not ask whether there 
was a reference to the PLO in the proposal made by Reagan who still regards it as a 
terrorist organization. Did he refer to the millions of Palestinians who are homeless 
and scattered in all parts of the world? Does the solution concern the Palestinians' 
resettlement or redressing the injustice especially when the West Bank cannot absorb 

4 million Palestinians or is the self-rule meant to concern only the citizens of the West 
Bank and the Gaza Strip and will the Palestinians there have complete sovereignty over 
that area in the shadow of the settlements already in existence, which Reagan did not 
mention in his scheme? Is it conceivable that self-rule is going to be free of Zionist 
security and political and military control? 


The question of self-rule in the Reagan proposal in essence does not go beyond the Begin 
proposal contained in the Camp David agreements. The Reagan proposal does not provide 
anything more than what actually exists at present.
