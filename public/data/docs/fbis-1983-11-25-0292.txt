LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Receives King Fahd’s Envoy 
LD250738 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 25 Nov 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 25 Nov (JANA) — The brother leader of the 
revolution received Ash-Shaykh ‘Ali Musallam, the personal 
envoy of King Fahd ibn ‘Abd al-*Aziz, king of Saudi Arabia, 
yesterday within the framework of Arab consultations on the 
current situation. Ash-Shaykh ‘Ali Musallam handed the leader 
a message from King Fahd which dealt with the situation in the 
Arab arena and with the crusade assault to which the Arab entity 
is being subjected. 


JANA Quotes Radio Criticism of Husayn Remarks 
LD241032 Tripoli JANA in English 0845 GMT 24 Nov 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Safar 19, Nov 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The voice of the Greater Arab Nation, Jama- 
hiriyah radio in one of its commentaries refuted King Husayn’s 
attack on Jamahiriyah and Syria with regard to the Palestinian 
revolution. The commentary read as follows. 


The Jordanian regime is now shedding crocodile tears by pre- 
tending to talk in favour of the Palestinians. One of his allega- 
tions, this time, is that the revolutionary movement in Fatah has 


been initiated by external elements and that those who support 
it aim to destroy the Palestinian resistance 


It is ridiculous that a regime like Jordan would talk about the 
Palestinians when it has got the worse record of crimes against 
them. The Jordanian regime, we all remember, abandoned the 
West Bank as an easy target for the Zionists by refusing to arm 
the Feddayeen and by arresting them when they were on the way 
back from the occupied land 


When the resistance movement became strong to the extent of 
controlling the occupied land and threatening the Zionist entity, 
the Americans and the Zionists did not find any regime but King 
Husayn’s to hit the revolution. Thus, he committed the massacre 
of Black September in 1970 where more than 40,000 Palestinian 
were killed. One year later in 1971 the Jordanian regime hit the 
rest of the Palestinian bases in Jarash. It is the Jordanian regime 
which intends to destroy the resistance by dragging it to submis- 
sive solutions and agreements 


The revolutionary movement in Fatah is a rebirth for the Pales- 
tinian resistance and a call for fight and confrontation, something 
against King Husayn’'s wish of closing the file of Palestine. No 
wonder the Jordanian regime is plotting, together with other, to 
stab this movement from behind 


We believe Jordanian regime criticism to the movernent is a 
credit in its favour. It also proves, among other things the 
conspiracy of the revisionist right Palestinian elements against 
the revolution. By defending ‘Arafat and his clique King Husayn 
desperately attempts to save his own regime since the rev- 
olutionary movement indicates the amalgamation of all the 
revolutionary masses against reactionary regimes 


We have got the right to speak about the Palestinian question in 
terms of revolution and armed struggle since nobody would beat 
the Jamahirtyah in aiding the Palestinian resistance not only in 
money and weapons but by offering the blood of its sons who 
fought together with the Palestinians. the Lebanese and the 
Syrians. It must be known that people of the Jamahiriyah will 
stop any plot and conspiracy against the Palestinian cause
