PAPER SAYS U.S. HINDERING LAW OF SEA AGREEMENT 


LD121359 Tripoli AL-FAJR AL-JADID in Arabic 5 Mar 81 p 1 
{Unattributed report: "America Seeking To Hinder Law of Sea Agreement"] 


[Text] The U.S. State Department announced the day before yesterday that it will oppose 
the conclusion of an agreement on the law of the sea during the coming session of the UN 
conference. The U.S. State Department said that the U.S. delegation has been given 
instructions to seek to prevent the success of the negotiations during the coming 
conference session. 


It is worth noting that the Law of the Sea Conference has been holding discussions for 
7 years. Next week the conference will hold a session that will last 6 weeks which many 
observers hope will be the last before an international agreement is concluded. 


The U.S. administration's aim behind hindering this conference is to maintain its 
control and exploitation of the sea's wealth. 
IRAQI PURGES, ARREST OF AHMAD AL-BAKR REPORTED 


LD121920 Tripoli JANA in English 1814 GMT 12 Mar 8] 


[Text] Damascus, 12 Mar (JANA)--The Syrian newspaper AL-BA'TH today wrote that the 

Iraqi authorities had put the former President Ahmad Hassan al-Bakr under house arrest. 

The newspaper added that informed circles indicated that Saddam Husayn was worried about 
Al-Bakr's links with Iraqi high ranking officers. The newspaper also writes that observers 
did not rule out the possibility that Al-Bakr had his own opinion on the Iraqi-Iranian 

war. 


Meanwhile, the newspaper quoted news from Iraq as saying that Saddam Husayn began doubting 
everybody around him and that Iraqi security officers were attending the meetings of the 
Revolutionary Command Council fully armed for fear of any assassination attempt. 


The newspaper indicated that the regime was carrying out daily purges against anyone 
suspected of having a different or opposing opinion to that of Saddam. It also said 
that Barazan at-Tikriti, Saddam's brother, recently killed a high ranking army officer-- 
a relative of the Iraqi ambassador in Paris, who was subsequently recalled.

[Text] Tehran, 13 Mar (JANA)--The Iranian prime minister's office yesterday issued a 
statement warning of increasing internal chaos and tension. It said that current 
conditions were similar to those in Iran on the eve of the 1953 coup d'etat. It stressed 
that the government was determined to foil, at any cost, the conspiracy of the revolution's 
enemies and that it would practice its revolutionary responsibility, for the enemies were 
preparing for a new round for which a date had already been set.

Joint Statement 
LD121508 Tripoli Domestic Servic in Arabic 1230 GMT 12 Mar 81 


{Libyan-Nicaraguan "joint statement" on the visit to Libya by Nicaraguan Foreign Affairs 
Minister Miguel D'Escoto] 


[Text] At the invitation of the brother secretary of the People's Committee of the 
People's Bureau for External Liaison, Nicaraguan Minister of Foreign Affairs Miguel 
d'Escoto paid a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah [SPLAJ] from 8 
through 11 March 1981. The guest delegation was received by our brother, Col Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 1 September Revolution, and conveyed to him the greet- 
ings and appreciation of the people of Nicaragua and the leaders of the Sandinista 
National Liberation Front for supporting their struggle for the triumph of their popular 
revolution which removed the regime of the fascist clique which used to exercise domina- 
tion and terrorism against the people of Nicaragua. It affirmed that, having achieved 
its freedom, Nicaragua is marching toward the realization of the masses system and that 
the Third World theory is now being taught in Nicaragua carefully and profoundly by 
members of the Sandinista Front. The guest delegation acquainted itself with certain 
aspects of economic and social development, and expressed its great admiration and 
pleasure at the achievements realized by the people of SPLAJ in all fields, thanks to the 
Great 1 September Revolution. 

In an atmosphere ‘mbued with friendship, extensive talks took place between the two 
delegations. Views were exchanged on bilateral relations and the means of consolidating 
and promoting ther so as to achieve the aspirations and ambitions of the two friendly 
peoples. In this domain, an agreement was signed for agricultural cooperation. The 

two sides also agreed to conclude other agreements for financial, oil, trade, scientific 
and cultura’ cooperation and a program for mutual visits with the aim of strengthening 
and promoting the political, economic, cultural and scientific relations between the 

two friendly countries. 


The two sides reviewed the situation in the Arab homeland, Africa and Latin America. 
The two sides affirmed anew the substance of the two joint statements issued in Tripoli 
on 30 October 1979 and in Managua on 5 December 1979. 


Concerning the latest developments of the Arab situation, the two sides know that the 
imperialist plot led by the United States against the people of Palestine and the Arab 
nation has assumed a grave dimension threatening the interests of the Arab nation and 
the interests of world peace, following the declaration by the U.S. Government of its 
plans to establish permanent American military bases in the region to serve as a staging 
point for aggression and provocation and as a hotbed of tension. While strongly con- 
demning these aggressive plans, they also strongly condemn the collusion of the Arab 
reactionary regimes, particularly the As-Sadat regime in Egypt which betrayed the cause 
of the people of Palestine and the causes of the just struggle of the Arab nation by 
signing the Stable David agreements and by turning Egypt's Arab land into a political 
and military sphere of influence for American imperialism and Zionism. 


The two sides affirm anew their firm conviction that a settlement of the Palestine issue 
cannot be achieved except by total recognition of the inalienable national rights of the 
Palestinian Arab people, including their right to self-determination and to the estab- 
lishment of their own state and exercising their sovereignty over their national soil 
under the leadership of the PLO. 


With regard to the development of recent events in the African Continent, the two sides 
expressed their absolute support for the struggle of the African people against colonial- 
ism, neocclonialism and imperialism. They hailed the steadfastness and the struggle of 
the people of South Africa against racism and apartheid. They stress the need for the 
enforcement of the UN resolutions regarding the application of sanctions against ra-cist 
regime of Pretoria and the need for the people of Namibia to attain their independence 
and sovereignty under the leadership of SWAPO, its legitimate and sole represent tive.

They also affirmed their support for the struggle of the people of Western Sahara for 
their right to self-determination in accordance with UN and OAU resolutions. 


The Nicaraguan side expressed its high esteem for the role of the people of SPLAJ and 

the leader of their revolution, brother Col Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi, in helping and 

backing the liberation movements and the peoples struggling for their freedom and inde- 
pendence, including their backing of the people of Nicaragua, whose victorious revolution 
triumphed under the leadership of the Sandinista National Liberation Front, as well as 
their backing to the people of Chad until they surmounted the plots of imperialism and 
its agents and restored security and stability and peace to their country. 


In this connection, the Nicaraguan delegation affirmed its country's solidarity with the 
people of SPLAJ in confronting the provocations being carried out by imperialism and 
reaction along their borders and their violation of their skies and territorial waters. 


The two sides showed concern over the recent developments in Central America and the 
Caribbean. They expressed their strong condemnation of the American imperialist inter- 
ference in the internal affairs of the peoples of this region. In this connection, the 
Libyan Arab side expressed its solidarity with the Sandinista revolution, which is 
coming under economic pressures, concern at the armed attacks and attempts to ensnare 
Nicaragua in the entanglements which American imperialism is creating in El Salvador 
through continuous propaganda campaigns--campaigns which have failed. 


The two sides affirm the right of these peoples to self-determination and their right 
to exercise their sovereignty and freedom without any outside interference. 


Reviewing the international situation, the views of the two sides were generally identical 
with regard to the need to strengthen the Nonaligned Movement and their commitment to 

its aims and principles; they expressed their support for its resolutions. They also 
affirm their solidarity in the various international lobbies, particularly the Group of 77 
for the formulation of a new international economic order based on equality and justice 

so as to ensure the realization of democracy in international life and the stability of 
world security and peace. 


The Nicaraguan delegation expressed its thanks for the hospitality afforded to it and 

for the atmosphere of fraternity and friendship which pervaded the talks and for the 
positive results achieved in the interest of the two friendly peoples. On this occasion 
it addressed an invitation to brother secretary of the People's Committee of the People's 
Bureau for External Liaison to visit Managua. The brother secretary accepted the 
invitation whose date will be fixed later. 


[Signed] Tripoli, 12 March 1981 


PAPER OUTLINES TASKS OF REVOLUTIONARY COMMITTEES 


LD121009 London AL-ZAHF AL~-AKHDAR in English 6 Mar 81 p 15 


[Unattributed article: "Inciting the Masses"] 


[Text] The essence of the Libyan Jamahirayah is the concept of the authority of the 
people, whereby the masses have a direct say in their country and are directly responsible 
for the security, wealth and development of the nation. The people are armed and in this 
they will assume responsiblity for the defence of the nation and the revolution. But it 
is to the revolutionary committees that the overall responsibility of safeguarding the 
revolution must be devolved, so that with their incitement the masses can then move to 
assert all aspects of their authority.

The serious historical duty of the revolutionary committees is to incite the masses and 
enlighten them about the many critical stages that the Arab nation is passing through, as 
well as the many challenges that it faces. This educational thrust must be aimed at 

the masses in an attempt to raise their critical awareness of the events and ideologies 
that impinge on their nation and revolution. Thus for the Arab nation it means an 
understanding of the imperialist, Zionist and reactionary forces that are constantly on 
the offensive and which recently have been occupying parts of the Arab homeland thus 
hoping to encircle it. 


There must be no illusions about this threat and the revolutionary committees have a 
specific responsibility to enlighten the masses on this matter and point the way to its 
defeat. Because of the wealth and strategic value of the Arab homeland, the imperialists 
in concert with the Zionists have made it a crusade to see that all these benefits are 
reserved exclusively for them. Any departure from this position would mean consistent 
attacks on the nation that breaks the line. That is the reason for the determination 

of the Americans to maintein a highly overt presence in the area so under the pretext of 
protecting "her vital incerests" and aided by traitors among us, the imperialists can 
assert their dominance. 


Carrying Arms 


The revolutionary committees must bear the responsibility of also enlightening the masses 
about their destiny and future as well as inciting them to carry arms in preparation 

for the inevitable conflict with the enemies of freedom and unity and, specifically, the 
enemies of the Arab nation. Even a cursory examination of the situation would show 

that the enemies are increasing in number and becoming bolder in their initiatives each 
year. In fact the major imperialist power, the United States, has advanced militarily 
deeper into the Arab homeland. This is proven by the fact that the latest move occurred 
in Oman with a joint U.S.-Omani military exercise code-named Accurate Test '81 which will 
involve the test of communications capabilities. 


No territory is safe from attack as the Americans test their so-called Rapid Deployment 
Force, and Accurate Test ‘81 is one of many tests undertaken recently to determine the 
potential of this force acting in concert with those regimes that have abandoned their 
historic role to keep the Arab homeland free of foreign domination. But the main surro- 
gate of the imperialists in the area is the traitorous Egyptian leader, Anwar as-Sadat. 
His treachery has transformed Egypt into an aggressive imperialist base against the Arab 
nation and her progressive forces. 


People's Authority 


Resistance to these encroachments in the region must be mounted and it is the responsi- 
bility of the revolutionary committees to incite the masses to embrace their authority, 
so that a concerted and powerful attack can be made on the many forces that are seeking 
to hinder the many stages of revolutionary transformation. The committees must also 
define the enemy and make known to the masses all the ploys and subtleties of their 
power, so that in fighting them we do not expend our energies on diversions while leaving 
the real enemy free to operate under his many guises. 


Who would have thought that the Zionists would have reached as far as the Jamahiriyah's 
border, threatening the nation of the Libyan masses not only by their presence but also 
by their potential to initiate hostilities with the full support of the As-Sadat regime. 
The masses must be made aware of their destiny so that they can be prepared for the 
inevitable conflict. Where there is treachery within, it must be exposed lest despon- 
dency sets in and the masses accept defeat. The enemy is not invincible and so revolu- 
tionary committees must educate the masses on the weaknesses of the enemy as well as 

the means to defeat it.

The revolutionary committes also have a responsibility to speed up the process whereby 
the masses are armed. Because the authority resides in the people it is also their 
responsibility in exercising that authority to defend themselves, nation and homeland. 
The people thus need to be trained in the use of arms so that the whole nation is the 
army with the task of repelling all invaders. 


Meeting Places 


This is a very important task which cannot be shirked as the whole future of the revolu- 
tion depends on the effective working of the committees. These committees should be 
made up of conscious individuals who are aware fully of all dimensions of the struggle. 
This revolutionary consciousness is the essential pre-requisite for any member of the 
revolutionary committee as it determines success with the masses. 


Such committees must have their own meeting places which will become the source of all 
revolutionary activities. These centres must be staffed by members and offer revolution- 
ary culture to the masses and act as exemplars in behaviour, seriousness and commitment 

to all the ideals of the revolution. The whole ethos of the place must be towards creat- 
ing a positive response to the many facets of the exercise of the people's authority 
which will enable the masses to be goaded into revolutionary action and resist representa- 
tion.
