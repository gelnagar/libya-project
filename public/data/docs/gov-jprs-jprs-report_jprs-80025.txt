[Article: "Official Television Obstructs Revolutionary Press!"] 


[Text] Is it the right of daily programs which review the press to ignore what is 
published by the newspapers AL-ZAHF and AL-.JAMAHIRIYAH or to shirk from or diminish 
the value of the subjects they publish?! 


First we must ask, if they were to have this right, have the television stations 
become "official" and gotten rid of their membership in the revolutionary committees?! 


Or don't those responsible for the "programs" know that the two newspapers are issued 
by the revolutionary committees?! 


In order to reach a definitive answer to this question, we will discuss the programs, 
at least from the "technical" aspect. 

These programs are well-known in all Arab and international broadcasting circles for 
giving preference to a number of newspapers and magazines which serve a specific 
cause or a subject which they desire to raise through the programs. 


It is assumed that this choice in this country takes place according to the 
instigating position of the revolutionary media and to the nature of the programs,

It is true that the choice is a judgmental question, but it is controlled by objective 
considerations derived from the nature of the existing regime and its interest and 

the nature of information at this stage. These considerations are supposed to be 
adhered to because we are not living amid uncontrolled dissemination of information!! 


For example, it is technically impossible for us to review other newspapers whatever 
their affiliation, and we do not review with the same enthusiasm the newspapers 
AL-JAMAHIRIYAH and AL-ZAHF, regardless of their nature as the papers of the 
revolutionary committees. 


We must concentrate and choose one or more issues so that the viewer's mind does not 
wander and he can derive intellectual and mental enjoyment from the program and gain 
condensed information. However, the programs must not be mere repetition of the 
newscast or a commentary on the news, but rather an in-depth analysis which 
contributes something. 


The period alloted for the program and the time it is broadcast allow those who 
prepare this program to correct all of these shortcomings. The most dangerous are 
personal considerations in important programs which draw the attention of thousands 
of viewers! 


Less serious is the danger of turning to mere clippings and scattered headlines and 
topics without any effort to differentiate between what is reactionary or progressive. 
If so, we lead the masses astray!
