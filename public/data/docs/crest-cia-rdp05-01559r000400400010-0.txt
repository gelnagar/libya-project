It contains foreign policy rhetoric only, with nothing about places or events in Tripoli itself.
• The press conference was held in Tripoli on 10 April 1985 and was reported in take 3 of 6 of an FBIS London transcript.
• Qadhdhafi announced the Pan-Arab Command of the Revolutionary Forces, which he presented as a response to failing Arab regimes.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya had apologized for not hosting a joint conference of Arab and African foreign ministers that had been scheduled for that month.

NOW. WE ARE WITH EGYPT AND WE SUPPORT IT. BUT WHEN THE EGYPTIAN

EVEN IF THERE IS A TRADITIONAL RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN EGYPT AND THE 

SUDAN. WHEN EGYPT BETRAYS AND GOES OVER TO THE ENEMY AND ENTERS 

THE STABLE, STABLE DAVID, THE SUDANESE PEOPLE ARE NOT OBLIGED TO 

BETRAY AND ENTER THE STABLE BY VIRTUE OF THIS RELATIONSHIP. 

THE SUDANESE PEOPLE WILL NOT PUT AN END TO THIS CONDESCENSION TOWARD 

SUDAN. HUSNI MUBARAK'S STATEMENTS CONSTITUTE A CRUDE INTERFERENCE 

IN THE INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF THE SUDANESE PEOPLE. HE IS PROVING 

THAT HE IS INSINCERE AND THAT HE CANNOT BE RELIED UPON. YESTERDAY 

HE WAS AN ALLY OF NUMAYRI AND USED TO LIE TO NUMAYRI, TELLING HIM: 

I WILL NOT ABANDON YOU AND EGYPT'S CAPABILITIES ARE AT YOUR DISPOSAL. 

HERE HE ((NUMAYRI -- FBIS)) IS IN EGYPT. WHY DOES HE NOT PLACE 

EGYPT'S CAPABILITIES AT THE DISPOSAL OF NUMAYRI. HE 

((MUBARAK -- FBIS)) HAS BEGUN TO FLIRT WITH SIWAR AL-DHAHAB. 

SIWAR AL-DHAHAB KNOWS THAT HUSNI MUBARAK, WHILE DOING SO, 

WILL NOT STAND BY HIM IF SOMEONE TOPPLES SIWAR AL-DHAHAB. THIS 

RECENT STATEMENT HAS REVEALED THE TRUE EGYPTIAN ATTITUDE TOWARD 

SUDAN. AT ANY RATE, THIS CONDESCENSION TOWARD SUDAN BY 

EGYPT -- THE ATTITUDE OF A MASTER TO HIS SLAVE -- SHOULD SHAME THE 

EGYPTIAN RULERS. SUDAN IS AN INDEPENDENT ARAB STATE WHICH HAS 

EQUAL OR EVEN GREATER RESOURCES THAN EGYPT. SIWAR AL-DHAHAB, 

DID NOT CONSULT HUSNI MUBARAK UPON ASSUMING POWER. HE DID NOT 

CONSULT REAGAN. WHEN THE SUDANESE PEOPLE STAGED THE REVOLUTION THEY 

DID NOT CONSULT MUBARAK; THEY DID NOT ASK HIM FOR HELP. IN FACT, 

THEY WERE AGAINST MUBARAK, NUMAYRI, AND AMERICA. THE SLOGANS 

VOICED RIGHT FROM THE VERY BEGINNING AND UNTIL NOW SAID SO. 

SIWAR AL-DHAHAB IS RESPONSIBLE TO THE SUDANESE PEOPLE AND THE 

ARAB NATION. HE ESTABLISHES RELATIONS WITH WHOEVER HE WANTS 

ACCORDING TO WHAT THE SUDANESE PEOPLE DICTATE, AND NOT ACCORDING

((ALQADHDHAFI)) IN ACTUAL FACT THERE ARE OUTSIDE INFLUENCES WHICH
