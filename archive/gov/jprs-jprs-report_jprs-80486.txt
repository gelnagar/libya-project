TEHRAN REACTS SWIFTLY AFTER OTHER PRODUCERS TURN DOWN OIL PRICE OVERTURES 
London IRAN PRESS SERVICE in English No 61, 4 Mar 82 pp 4-5 


[Text] Iran's theocratic rulers tried last month to urge Libya, Algeria and 
possibly Indonesia to join Iran in a united front within OPEC to force Saudi 
Arabia and the United Arab Emirates to limit their crude oil exports, reliable 
informants told IPS last week. 


[t was their failure to rally the support of those countries that led them to 
cut prices of their own crude drastically to boost sales, the informants said. 
Iran's action now threatens to destroy the unity of the oil-producing countries. 


Sources close to the Iranian administration told IPS that during this visit to 
Libya and Algeria, foreign minister Ali Akbar Velayati argued with the leaders 
of these two oil exporting countries to "gang up" with Iran to force Saudi 
Arabia to cut its own exports to below the 5 million barrels a day mark. 


Velayati was reported to have said that with their $150 billion or so re- 
serves Saudi Arabia could afford to cut down on its exports in a show of good- 
will to allow other more populous OPEC member countries to boost their own 
exports. He is believed to have told Libyans and Algerians that if the Saudis 
went to flood the already glutted market, Iran wouldn't hesitate to wreck OPEC 
unity. 


A cut in Saudi exports would be welcomed by both Algeria and Libya, but it 

seems they felt they were not in a position to influence the Saudis. They 

were also reported to have told Velayati that the mullahs would be making a 
grave mistake if they did something which led to serious harm for OPEC. 


The Lranian mullahs, however, seem to have ignored pleas by their Arab friends 
when subsequently cutting the price of their best crude by 6 dollars a barrel 
in four weeks’ time. Whether they can still export enough to earn an esti- 
inated $1.5 billion a month remains to be seen. 


The sources also said that a majlis deputy, Mullah Emami-Kashani, was sent to 
indonesia to seek that country's support for a confrontation with Saudi 
\rabia, but he failed in his mission. He was reported to have behaved so 
badly during his visit that the Indonesians had to ask him politely to leave 
the country.
