LIBYA

LIBYAN REACTION ON U.S.-LIBYAN DOGFIGHT REPORTED 


LD191954 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1910 GMT 19 Aug 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Aug (JANA)--The People's Bureau for External Liaison this evening 
summoned the Belgian ambassador in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
[SPLAJ] because the Belgian Embassy looks after the interests of the United States in 
the SPLAJ. The People's Bureau for external liaison handed him a very strongly worded 
protest against the U.S. planes' interception of two Libyan planes and firing on them 
when they were carrying out ordinary reconnaisance missions in territorial airspace 
and over territorial waters in the Gulf of Sidra this morning. 


In the note it was stated that in continuing U.S. provocations against the SPLAJ and 
constant violation of Libyan territorial waters and airspace, U.S. naval and air forces, 
carrying out personal instructions from President Reagan, staged naval maneuvers inside 
the Libyan territorial waters in the Gulf of Sidra. 


The People's Bureau for External Liaison stressed that it regards the attack to which 
the two Libyan planes were exposed this morning, by the plans of the U.S. 6th Fleet 
inside Libyan waters, as international terrorism practiced by the United States against 
the Jamahiriyah. In its aggressions the United States has relied on the law of the 
jungle and the logic of cowboys: This is the only language the United States knows. 


The People's bureau affirmed that these barbarous U.S. acts are considered a flagrant 
violation of all internationally recognized laws. 


JANA has learned that the SPLAJ has submitted a note to the Arab League, the United 
Nations, the OAU and the Nonaligned Movement explaining the U.S. attacks on the 
Jamahiriyah.

Masses’ Statement 
NC200705 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0500 GMT 20 Aug 81 


[Text] The popular masses in Tripoli and Bengasi last night staged a demonstration of 
anger and defiance against the aggression that the U.S. aircraft carried out against 
two Libyan aircraft. 


In their slogans the masses affirmed their determination to confront these aggressions and 
their readiness to fight for every inch of their land and their gains, irrespective of the 
sacrifices. They also affirmed their readiness to respond to the order to die to defend 
the Great 1 September Revolution and to stand firm against all terrorist plots being 
hatched against it--plots that are aimed at undermining the gains the people have 

achieved through their great revolution. 


The popular masses in Tripoli and Bengasi issued a statement affirming the determination 
of the Libyan Arab people to resist U.S. aggression and to deter the aggressors with all 
available strength. , The statement also affirms that the aggression the U.S. aircraft 
carried out against the two Libyan aircraft within Libyan airspace and waters exposes 
the true aggressive intentions of U.S. imperialism and expresses the reality of U.S. 
terrorism. 


The statement denounces the dirty alliance between U.S. imperialism and its agents in 
Egypt and Sudan, noting that agreement in timing between the U.S. aggression against 

the Libyan Arab territorial waters and the Egyptian military maneuvers on the 
Jamahiriyah's borders exposes the reality of the aggression being planned against our 
people and our achievements. The statement says that the Libyan Arab people will resist 
unt death in order to defend their revolution and repulse the oppressive aggressors. 


The statement declares that the masses of the Arab people in Libya are aware of the reality 
of the leader of international terrorism and that they will face all challenges and destroy 
U.S. interests everywhere. 


The statement points out that the masses of our people are strong in their revolution 
and are able and accept the challenge in all its forms, their slogan being victory or 
martyrdom. The statement also affirms the readiness of the Libyan Arab people to carry 
out the order to die in order to create a free and dignified life and their readiness 
to sacrifice martyrs for the firm and immortal principles in the society of happiness 
and freedom, the society of the era of the masses. 


London Spokesman's Comments 
LD191407 London PRESS ASSOCIATION in English 1347 GMT 19 Aug 81 
[By Tom McMullan, PA diplomatic correspondent ] 
[Excerpts] The United States, assisted by Egypt, is attempting to destabilise the Libyan 
Government of Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, according to Dr (Omar Sodani) [name as received], 
chief spokesman at the Libyan Peoples Bureau in London. Dr (Sodani) was commenting 
on the shooting down of two Libyan aircraft by American jets in the Mediterranean today. 
Dr (Sodani) said: "The Americans have been manoeuvring in our territorial waters for 


some time. American behavior has been provocative, including statements by officials 
that they want to get rid of Al-Qadhdhafi and destabilise the regime in Libya. 


"They heve said they will give military support to anyone who will carry out military 
action against Libya. 


"The Egyptians are manoeuvring on our eastern frontier. This action has taken place since 
President as-Sadat's visit to Washington."

Solidarity Group's Statement 
LD201031 Tripoli JANA in English 0941 GMT 20 Aug 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 August (JANA)--Representatives of the Permanent Secretariat for 
Solidarity with the Arab People and its Central Cause--Palestine--held an emergency 
meeting yesterday to discuss American imperialist provocations and threat of direct 
aggression against the Jamahiriyah people. 


The two secretariats issued a statement denouncing the treacherous American aggression 
and declaring their solidarity with the Jamahiriyah people. They also called on all 
Arab and international progressive forces to take an immediate initiative and all 
necessary measures. 


They also sent cables of protest and denunciation to the UN secretary general, the 

Security Council president, the Arab League secretary general, the Organization of 

African Unity, the president of the Non-aligned Movement countries and the chairman 
of the Islamic Conference. Similar cables were sent to national and international 

organizations throughout the world. 


‘CONTRADICTIONS’ IN U.S. STATEMENTS REPORTED 


NC200752 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0500 GMT 20 Aug 81 


[Text] Larry Hamilton, the official spokesman of the U.S. war ministry [as heard], 
has stated that he does not know whether the U.S. F-14 aircraft were hit or whether 
the U.S. pilots were injured. This statement by the U.S. war ministry's spokesman 
is considered a confirmation and admission of the doéming of a U.S. warplane, which 
has been announced by the military spokesman of the Libyan Jamahiriyah. 


A clear contradiction has emerged in the official statements made by U.S. administration 
and war ministry officials. This clearly confirms that the maneuvers carried out by 

the U.S. 6th Fleet were indeed carried out inside Libyan territorial waters and that 

the U.S. aircraft had begun the aggression against the two Libyan aircraft which were 

on a routine reconnaissance flight within Libyan waters and airspace. 


While the U.S. war ministry's official spokesman, Hamilton, stated that the U.S. air- 
craft were parked aboard the aircraft carrier Nimitz, which is taking part in the 
maneuvers, the U.S. war minister announced in his press conference yesterday at the 
war ministry that the two Libyan aircraft did not fly over the fleet's units. Mean- 
while, a U.S. war ministry official has told correspondents that the U.S. aircraft 
were on patrol duty.

In reply to a question as to whether the two Libyan aircraft had threatened the U.S. 
aircraft carrier Nimitz, the U.S. war minister in his press conference said: No. 


This flagrant contradiction between the U.S. officials constitutes an explicit admission 
that: 


1. The U.S. aircraft were not, as the U.S. officials claimed, parked aboard 
the aircraft carrier, but were carrying out provocative flights, and 
that they were the first to open fire--something that was confirmed by 
a U.S. war ministry official, who admitted that the U.S. aircraft were 
not aboard the aircraft carrier but were carrying out what he described 
as patrol duties. 


The two Libyan aircraft were carrying out routine reconnaissance and the 
the U.S. aircraft, as the U.S. officials have admitted, initiated the 
attack. 


A U.S. White House spokesman has admitted that two warships attached to the U.S. 6th Fleet 
were inside Libyan territorial waters in the Gulf of Sidra when the two Libyan aircraft 
were attacked by the U.S. aircraft. The official spokesman revealed that U.S. President 
Reagan personally ordered that the U.S. 6th Fleet hold maneuvers inside Libyan waters. 

The statements of the U.S. White House spokesman reveal the clear contradiction in the 
official statements issued by the U.S. administration and confirm that the U.S. 6th Fleet 
carried out its maneuvers inside Libyan territorial waters in an open and provocative 
manner. 


Meanwhile, an official spokesman of the U.S. war ministry has announced that the U.S. 
6th Fleet's maneuvers in Libyan waters in the Gulf of Sidra concluded at 1700 GMT, or 1900 
Libyan time, yesterday. The units of the U.S. 6th Fleet which took part in these 
maneuvers have withdrawn outside the Libyan territorial waters.

MILITARY SPOKESMAN REPORTS U.S. F-14 DOWNED 


LD191734 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1709 GMT 19 Aug 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Aug (JANA)--A military spokesman m: a statement this afternoon in 
which he confirmed the downing of an American military F-14 aircraft when this morning 
eight American aircraft of the American 6th Fleet intercepted two Libyan aircraft and 
opened fire on them while they were carrying out a routine reconnaissance operation 

in the Libyan skies and territorial waters in the Gulf of Sidra. Helicopters belonging 
to the American airfleet were seen trying to rescue the American pilot, whose aircraft 
fell into the sea. 


Wreckage Reportedly Found 
LD191904 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1840 GMT 19 Aug 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Aug (JANA)--A military spokesman at 1930 today announced that the 
Libyan Arab Navy has found the wreckage of the U.S. plane which was shot down this 
morning. The plane was one of eight planes belonging to the U.S. 6th Fleet which opposed 
two Libyan planes and fired on them while they were on a routine reconnaissance mission 
inside the air space of and over the territorial waters of the Gulf of Sidra.

PILOTS DISCUSS ENGAGEMENT WITH U.S. PLANES 


NC200608 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0500 GMT 20 Aug 81 


{Yext] JANA correspondents have met with the two Libyan pilots whose aircraft were sub- 
jected to an aggression by eight U.S. aircraft in Libyan airspace and territorial waters 
yesterday morning. During this meeting, which took place last night, the two pilots 
pointed out that they were carrying out a routine reconnaissance mission with Libyan air- 
space and waters at 0700 yesterday, 19 August 1981, when we [as heard] were attacked by 
eight U.S. F-14 aircraft and they opened fire on us. We returned the fire and hit one 

of them. We saw it catch fire and fall into the sea.
