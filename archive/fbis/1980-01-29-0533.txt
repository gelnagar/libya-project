LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI MEETS WITH GRENADIAN PRIME MINISTER 
LD251607 Tripoli JANA in English 1425 GMT 25 Jan 80 LD 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, January 25, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
leader of the grand Al-Fatah revolution, met last evening with the Grenadian prime 
minister, Maurice Bishop. During the meeting, they discussed the people's direct demo- 
cracy currently applied in the Jamahiriyah and the historical, political, economic and 
social changes brought about under the grand Al-Fatah revolution. 


KUWAITI PAPER: LIBYAN ENVOY TO PAKISTAN RESIGNS 
LD291120 Kuwait KUNA in Arabic 0700 GMT 29 Jan 80 LD 


[Text] Kuwait--Press reports here today say that the Libyan ambassador to Pakistan has 
resigned his post in protest against some of his government's political views on the 
Afghan issues; AR-RA'Y AL-'AMM says that Ambassador Bashir Megheirbi has submitted his 
resignation because Libya has not attended [as received] the Islamic Ministerial Conference 
being held in Pakistan, and that he has decided to give up politics and settle in 

Abu Dhabi, where he was his country's ambassador prior to his appointment in Islamabad. 


Libya is attending [as received] the conference with a delegation which is not headed 
by its foreign secretary--in contrast to the majority of the other participating states. 


AS-SIYASAH, in a dispatch it has received from Islamabad, says that Megheirbi has left 
his post and gone either to the UAE or West Germany. However, the paper has not mentioned 
the reason for his resignation.
