LIBYA

LIBYAN FORCES ATTACKED IN AL-BIQA' VALLEY 5 OCT 


LD051904 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1815 GMT 5 Oct 82 


[Text] Early today, the Zionist forces attacked Libyan forces present in the Al-Biqa' 
Valley. The attack did not cause any losses. 


LEBANESE PRESIDENT AL-JUMAYYIL GREETS AL-QADHDHAFI 


LD051608 Tripoli JANA in English 1454 GMT 5 Oct 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulhijja 18, October 5, Jamahiriyah News Agency -—- The revolution's 
leader, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, has received a message of congratulations from President 
Amin al-Jumayyil of Lebanon on the occasion of the Adha Muslim feast.
