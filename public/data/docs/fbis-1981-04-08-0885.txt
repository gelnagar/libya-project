AL-QADHDHAFI SPEAKS ON REVOLUTION IN ARAB HOMELAND 


LDO72202 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 7 Apr 81 


[Speech by head of state Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi in Tripoli on 7 April at a rally to 
mark the fifth anniversary of the 7 April 1976 student urising; intercepted in progress-- 
live or recorded] 


[Excerpts] We are entitled to say on the occasion of the fifth anniversary of the student 
revolution--7 April--that Libyan students, who today differ from the rest of the students 
in the Arab homeland by being free men and the makers of the revolution [applause]--I say 
that the Libyan students today, who, of all the other students of the Arab nation, are 
free men and makers of the revolution and who, through a revolutionary process, are con- 
solidating all the achievements which strengthen freedom and the gains of the masses, 

are called upon to embark on a wide incitement inside the Arab homeland of the student 
masses in the Arab homeland, to incite the masses to stage a revolution in the Arab 
homeland now that we have become certain of the failure of all the instruments which are 
operating in the Arab arena to realize anything for the Arab nation. [applause and chants] 


I must tell you that the existence of most Arab governments today is very important for 
Zionist expansionism and U.S. domination. The faithful guardians of these Arab govern- 
ments and the prolongers of their life and continuation are the Zionist enemy and America. 
As long as these governments continue to exist a great danger threatens the Arab nation's 
future. When I talk about these governments I do not talk about them as individuals but 
as a traitorous and reactionary system. Replacing the personalities of this system will 
not necessarily change it. The system can be changed only through revolution. 


I hope that that will not come about for us to witness, to give us cause to blame ourselves. 
{sentence as heard] There I say to you, the responsibility of the revolutionary Libyan 
student is grave because he is the only free student. He finds himself, under the re- 
volution, in a suitable position to play an historical role, to liberate the homelands 

for the popular masses and to confront the enemy. It is your responsibility to carry out 
widespread liberation, the constant concentrated liberation of the masses of students 

in the Arab homeland, so that they may in turn start popular revolutions and confront 
imperialist attacks. 


There is at present a tangible weakness in the Arab homeland, a weakness within the 
forces which could move; there is a weakness inside armies which could produce revolu- 
tionary forces and carry out revolutions; there is a weakness among workers who could be 
(?the mainstay) of the revolution; there is a weakness among the educated who could 
incite revolution. 


In most reactionary Arab states, armies are being destroyed; their attention is being 
diverted away from the national battlefront. Imaginary side issues are being invented 
to throw dust in their eyes. As-Sadat is plunging his army into the Western Sahara to 
deflect (?danger) from Cairo. In rural areas liquidation is afoot of army officers and 
men who might contemplate revolution. Every week the prisons are filled with crowds of 
officers and men from the national army, men who know that their battlefield is the 
occupied land and not a sister state. 


The Iraqi Army is being deliberately destroyed in an unnecessary battle; it is fighting 
on behalf of America and the Israelis. 


The Saudi Army is admitting foreign mercenaries from South Korea and Asia; they go by 
the name of [word indistinct]. Frankly, these will not be integrated; they will replace 
the people of the homeland.

When the revolution broke out in Libya, people thought that the Libyan Army was like the 
Saudi or the Moroccan Army, a royalist army which held royalty sacred and was loyal to 
it. But when in 1969 the revolution took off from within an army they thought was 
royalist, doubt spread among all royalist armies (?who found themselves) in a similar 
situation to that of the Libyan Army, that is, servants of the enemy. Since 1969 the 
royalist Moroccan Army and the royalist Savdi Army have been reexamined. Revolutionary 
forces which spread among them have been crushed forever. 


This is why I said to you that a considerable weakness has befallen the forces which can 
carry out the revolution--in the armies and in other popular bodies such as students, 
workers and peasants. There is now in the Arab countries [word indistinct] march to 
wear down the peasants and make them think only of filling their stomachs. 


In Sudan there are efforts to divide the students there into as many sections as possible. 
This is deliberate--a deliberate plan which was drawn up in the Sudanese universities, 

for example. All forces were allowed to recruit the students they wanted to recruit--so 
much so that in the same lecture hall you can find tens of parties. This is done so that 
they do not agree. Those in the same lecture hall cannot agree against the [word in- 
distinct] regime in Sudan, nor against this regime's link with the traitor regime in 
Egypt and [word indistinct] throwing itself in the arms of reaction and colonialism. The 
destruction of the students’ structure in Sudan is quite obvious. The same thing is now 
taking place in the rest of the reactionary Arab countries. 


This is being done to weaken the students" strength in Sudan and so that the students 
there cannot become a single force capable of taking a serious stand against the regime. 
This diabolical plan, alas, has succeeded--the antistudent and antimasses plan, the plan 
which is bound to prolong the life of the [word indistinct] regime in Sudan. Having 

made sure that the force which was capable of embarking on a revolution had been weakened, 
it clearly manifested its program. 


You are aware of the fact the Sudanese regime used to be hypocritical, keeping quiet 
about its inclinations. Now it has managed to divide the army, the workers and the 
students and to crush the peasants through feudalism. The prisons are crowded with 
officers and soldiers; we can publish lists of detained Sudanese Army men who tried to 
embark on a revolution. 


When this regime was sure that the plan had succeeded it showed its true face and re- 
stored relations openly and with every impudence with the traitor regime of Egypt. It 
also declared that Sudan was open for U.S. forces and it asked the United States to set 
up military bases in Sudan and to use these bases as it pleased. This regime made this 
public only after it had weakened the popular forces in Sudan. 


The popular forces in Egypt--the army, the students, the workers and the peasants-~are 
being destroyed every day. 


In Saudi Arabia the Arab people are being replaced by other people, people who come to 
Saudi Arabia to eat so as to live, not people who realize the purpose of the [words 
indistinct]. : 


I tell you that considerable weakness has befallen the popular forces in the Arab 
homeland. Therefore, Libyan students must take upon themselves the task of vast and 
continuous incitement through the Libyan information which must be re-taken over, be- 
cause the last takeover operation does not seem to have been carried out successfully. 
Evidence of this is the fact that information has not been able to openly carry out 
the task of incitement in the Arab homeland and even at home. Thus it is essential 
for the students, and precisely the students of the universities, to march once more 
on the information media. [prolonged applause and chants]

As I said, the responsibility laid upon your shoulders is grave. It is to instigate 

the revolution. Thus it is essential to seize the information so that serious 
instigations of the revolution can be made daily through Arab society [al-jam'iyah 
al-‘arabiyah], and particularly the students. This is because no longer can any hope be 
pinned on most of the reactionary governments in existence at present. These govern- 
ments have refused to join the Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation. This means 
that they do not wish to stand fast in the face of the enemy or confront him. 


Yes, this is their true position. These governments neither stand fast in the face of 
the enemy nor do they confront him at all. This is because they are agent governments, 
subservient to the enemy and his servants. 


We have repeatedly said that the friends of America are also the friends of Israel, for 
no Arab can ve an enemy of Israel and at the same time be a friend of America. 


When Saudi Arabia strikes up a close friendship with the United States can we believe 
that it is hostile to the Israelis? These are lies and charlatanry. We want every citizen 
in the Arabian Peninsula to hear this so that they may learn the truth about that regime 
and the other Arab regimes which are like it. At present there is an open dialogue 
between the Saudis and the Israelis. It is our duty to expose it and to instigate the 
destruction of this bridge before it is built between the Israelis and the Saudis, who 
mean to follow in the steps of As-Sadat. 


Have you not heard the invitation made now for a public dialogue between the Israelis 
and the Saudis--the invitation made by the leader of the Israeli Labor Party, who will 
be the head of government in the future? Why did he address this invitation for a 
dialogue to Saudi Arabia so publicly? This is proof that he felt confident and that the 
invitation has its preludes. 


This same Israeli has exposed the traitorous role played by King Hassan II of Morocco 

in respect to the stable David treaty. He is the one who brought As-Sadat and the 
Israelis together. As-Sadat himself has admitted this. He said that the king of 
Morocco was the one who prepared the ground for such a meeting. As I have an occupied 
territory, As-~Sadat said, I have signed the stable David agreement. But the king of 
Morocco has no occupied territory. Thus the king of Morocco did not need to meet with 
the Israelis but brought them and As-Sadat together. Today he might bring them together 
with others. 


Thus the treason committed by As -Sadat is the same treason committed by the king of 
Morocco. But the king of the Maghreb did not sign any agreements, because he has no 
territory under his [as heard] occupation. If he had an occupied territory he would 
have signed the same agreement signed by As-Sadat. How can this take place and how can 
we see it and keep quiet? There must be an instigation of the revolution from now 
onward. Otherwise, we [words indistinct] to the enemy. 


At this moment the situation in Lebanon is being aggravated in order to besiege Syria, 
liquidate the Palestinian resistance and the Lebanese patriotic movement and partition 
Lebanon and establish Israel as number two in Lebanon. This is what is happening. 
Fighting is at its climax at this hour in Lebanon. And this is by order of America and 
the Israelis. 


The counterattack about which I spoke at the llth anniversary of the revolution 
continues but the counterattack which I said we must incite the Arab nation to carry 
out has also begun. The national salvation front has begun scoring victories against 
the U.S. presence in Somalia [applause] after the 1lth anniversary of the revolution. 
Yes, this front was established and there was a meeting with it. This front was 
provided with potentials after we announced at the llth anniversary that we must 
confront the onslaught of the enemy against the Arab homeland. We are fighting in 
self-defense; we are fighting legitimately over the soil of the Arab homeland.

We do not fight America in its own territory. We did not cross the sea to fight Europe 
but we fight over the soil of the Arab homeland. We fight with our backs to the wall. 
The last fight is the fight for your own house. 


Therefore, we are not afraid at all to announce that we are fighting in Somalia; we 
are fighting the U.S. presence which is being equipped in Somalia; we are fighting 

there through the free men of Somalia, through the Somali national salvation front. 
{interruption through chanting] 


Resistance is being organized in Dhofar. This opposition will embark on a counterattack. 
We must put all possible potential for struggle at the disposal of the Oman liberation 
front so that the fight can start from Dhofar against the U.S. presence and against the 
hireling regime, which serves imperialism and on whom imperialism relies--as on a donkey-- 
to infiltrate the Peninsula from the southern end. The counterattack will start from 

the bridgehead which U.S. imperialism is now setting up in the south of the Arabian 
Peninsula. The counterattack will start soon, God willing. 


The struggle in the Sahara is now being intensified by the Polisario. The esclation 

of the armed struggle in the Sahara does not necessarily mean the creation of a new 

state, nor does it mean a secessionist war, as the king claims. All it means is a deep- 
ening and widening of the revolutionary explosion in the Arab Maghreb, particularly 

after Hassan II has proved himself to be an agent and an enemy of the Arab nation and 

that he was the one who paved the way for the disgraceful stable David accord, which 

ended the war with the enemy on the biggest Arab front, the Egyptian front. Some Israelis 
have confessed that he met with them and that, together with them, he betrayed the Arab 
nation. 


After all this, shall we preserve his regime or join those who want to set fire to that 
feudalist kingdom? To launch a counteroffensive against the enemy of the Arab nation 
and to ensure the continued existence of the Arab nation in its territory demands a 
greater widening of the revolutionary explosion in the Arab Maghreb. It also makes it 
essential these days to form a front, consisting of the Front of Steadfastness and Con- 
frontation, the Palestine resistance and the national front, to escalate the fighting 

in Lebanon so that the agents of Israel, headed by the agent Sa'd Haddad, will be liqui- 
dated. [applause and chants] 


One of the battles in the counteroffensive we fought after the llth anniversary of the 
revolution was the battle to prevent Chad, which lies on our borders, from turning into 
a base for imperialism, reaction and Zionism against the Arab nation in the heart of 
Africa. [applause and chants] 


Free student brothers, as the revolution has been escalated in Libya so it must be escalat- 
ed on the Arab level until all borders crumble and the whole Arab nation's potentialities 
are amassed in the face of the imperialist-Zionist offensive. Every day will be a day 

like 7 April. Every night will be an eve of the Great 1 September. [applause and chants]

ARAB CONGRESS LEADER CONDEMNS SUDAN'S NUMAYRI 


LD071416 Tripoli JANA in English 0943 GMT 7 Apr 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Apr (JANA)--The Arab People's Congress secretary general condemned 
yesterday the Sudanese regime for its attack on the Jamahiriyah and for encouraging 
Western countries to follow suit. 


In a press conference he held in Tripoli yesterday the A.P.C. secretary general said 
that Numayri announced that he himself will pay an award for any one who volunteers to 
assassinate Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the great revolution. 


He went on to say that Numayri's campaign is part of the U.S. hostile policy against 
the Arab nation and the progressive force in the region. He added that the Arab nation 
is now facing the most vicious imperialist attack as a way out to the Camp David 

treaty which reached a deadlock, as a result of the Palestinian people's steadfastness. 


The Palestinian people, he said, adhered to their legitimate right to self-determination 
and to establish their independent state under the P.L.O. leadership, their sole 
representative The establishment of U.S.A. bases in the Arab world and the presence 

of her fleets in the Arab waters are aimed at dominating the region and stealing the 
countries’ resources, pretending to stop the ‘Soviet influence’ in the region, he added.
