LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Takes Credit for ‘Arafat’s Safety 
LD1 21513 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1415 GMT 12 Jan 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 12 Jan (JANA) Colonel Mu‘ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi, the commander of the Great | September revolution, 
has spoken about the role played by Brother Kamal ad-Din 
Husayn, member of the Egyptian 23 July revolution Command 
Council, in saving the life of Yasir ‘Arafat and securing his safe 
departure from Tripoli in Lebanon. 


He said: Had it not been for Kamal ad-din Husayn’s efforts 
and his visit to me, a catastrophe would have taken place in 
Tripoli, Lebanon. He added: Yasir ‘Arafat is indebted to me 
and to Kamal ad-din Husayn for his safety and his still being 
alive. 


Al-Qadhdhafi was speaking during a press interview he gave 
recently to AFP, REUTERS, TASS, SANA and American TV's 
channel 6. [not further identified] He said that ‘Arafat was in an 
extremely difficult situation in Tripoli, and that he was exposed 
to annihilation. But then Kamal ad-Din Husayn came to me and 
asked me to help because he had met Yasir ‘Arafat during the 
Tripoli blockade. So I guaranteed ‘Arafat's safe departure in 
return for Kamal ad-Din Husayn’s guarantee that ‘Arafat would 
leave and would not cling to Fatah, which was fighting him. 


The brother commander added that Kamal ad-Din Husayn 
guaranteed that ‘Arafat would leave, and I, on my part, guaran- 
teed ‘Arafat's safe departure and that Tripoli would not be 
stormed by force after he left. Things went as promised. Had it
