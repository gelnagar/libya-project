Summary (AI): A January 1982 NPIC briefing sheet reports a new SA-3 surface-to-air missile site at Tajura, about 500 meters east of the existing Tarabulus SAM site, near Tripoli. It also lists new SAM deployments at Labraq, Sebha and Misratah.
• The Tajura SA-3 site is described as the sixth new SAM site identified in Libya.
• Four four-rail SA-3 launchers with missiles were present at the new Tajura site.
• New SAM equipment was seen in four areas in Libya according to the briefing.
• Coordinates given for Misratah SAM site area: 32-53-20N 013-21-00E.
• 1982-01: New SA-3 surface-to-air missile site established about 500 m east of the Tarabulus SAM site, with four SA-3 launchers, four support vans and one low blow radar; no cable scarring or squat eye radar observed.
