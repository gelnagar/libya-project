LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI MESSAGE FROM ETHIOPIAN LEADER 


NC260738 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 26 Jan 83 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution yesterday received Yusuf Ahmed, Ethiopian 
communications and transport minister and chairman of the current session of the 
African planning, transport, and communications ministers. He handed the brother 
leader a message from Mengistu Haile Mariam, chairman of the Provisional Military 
Administrative Council in the Socialist Republic of Ethiopia. 


In his message to the brother leader, the Ethiopian chairman affirmed the depth of 
relations hetween the Jamahiriyah and Ethiopia and the joint struggle on a single 


front against the imperialist and reactionary plots in Africa. 


AL-QADHDHAFI SPEECH AT ROMANIAN BANQUET 


LD260126 Tripoli JANA in English 1751 GMT 25 Jan 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi’ Thani 10, Jan 25, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The leader of the 
revolution has reaffirmed the principle stands of The Great Al-Fatih Revolution 
towards the Arab-Zionist conflict saying that the Zionist entity is a threat to world 
peace and security. 


The leader has warned in the banquet, which was given in his honour by President 
Nicolae Ceausescu, from the increasing Zionist aggressive activities in the Arab 
region saying that without the complete eradication of imperialism and Zionism, peace 
will always be threatened. He said that Zionism is the enemy of both Arabs and 

Jews alike. 


The leader has called on the Arabs, the Jews and the peace-loving nations to join 
hands together and fight Zionism. He added that the truce with imperialism is only a 
temporary one and not a radical solution to the cause of peace in the world. 


The world has become aware of the fact that the Zionist state is a base of world 
imperialism. There the fight against imperialism and against Zionism is inseparable. 
Zionism is responsible for the massacres committed against the Palestinian and 
Lebanese people in Lebanon. The leader said that Zionism has shown its aggressive 
nature to the world saying that Romania's relations with the Israelis did not stop 
them from committing the barbaric massacres on the Lebanese. The leader added that 
the so-called negotiations proved to mean the surrender of the Arabs to Zionism 

and the imposition of Zionist and imperialist conditions on the Arabs. 


The current negotiations between the Lebanese and the Zionists shows that the Israelis 
do not want only to drive the Palestinians out of their lands but also to occupy 
another Arab country and impose a unilateral treaty on it.
