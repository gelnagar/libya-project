LIBYA

GENERAL PEOPLE'S CONGRESS SENDS TELEGRAM TO PNC 
LD150314 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0015 GMT 15 Feb 83 


[Text] The General People's Congress has sent a telegram to the Palestinian National 
Congress [PNC] which is now in session in Algeria. Here is its text: 


In the name of God, to the PNC in Algiers. 


The Libyan Arab people who are meeting at the eighth session of the General People's 
Congress 1392 AH, corresponding to 1983 AD of the People's Congresses, People's 
Committees, the federations, trade unions, and vocational leagues, wish to say that 
through this congress we address this appeal to your PNC that the Zionist train of 
death -- which is supported by U.S. imperialism and Arab reaction -- may be confronted, 
that the Arab nation's aspirations may be realized in confronting this imminent danger, 
and that liberation may be achieved. The Libyan Arab people, in response to the appeal 
of the leader of the revolution, the brother Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, have 
approved in their People's Congresses the program of revolutionary action for a 
comprehensive mobilization to stop this train of death and to break the psychological 
barrier of the Arab man in order to arrive at a stage of counterattack to liberate the 
entire usurped Palestinian Arab soil. We address this appeal to your PNC confident 

in the ability of the Palestinian Arab people to make the proper decision of confront- 
ing the Zionist enemy and engaging in a struggle with him --. the struggle for existence, 
the struggle for life or death which absorbs all that has taken place in the Arab 
arena, especially after the failure of all the Arab regimes, parties and groupings, and 
the bankruptcy of their traditional means had been proved. 


The choice of the Palestinian Arab people at this juncture will undoubtedly influence 
the destiny of the entire Arab nation; indeed, it will most certainly influence the 
cause of freedom in the entire world. Your decision will be in the interest of your 
cause, your nation, and the freedom of man everywhere. You will trample on all 
attempts to subjugate you and bring you to your knees. You will breach the lines of 
siege around your cause and [word indistinct] by adhering to the rifle until victory 
ormartyrdom. Forward with you and the struggle shall continue.
