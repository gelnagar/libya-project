Summary (AI): A Washington Post article (Nov. 1981) reports on a Colorado trial of a former Green Beret, Eugene Tafoya, accused of shooting a Libyan dissident student, Faisal Zagallai, in Fort Collins in 1980. Prosecutors link the case to alleged Libyan use of ex-CIA agents Edwin Wilson and Francis Terpil. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• Tafoya, 47, of Truth or Consequences, N.M., retired after 23 years in the U.S. Army.
• Police described Tafoya as a hit man hired by Edwin P. Wilson, who had worked for the Libyan government since 1976.
• Libya's official news agency claimed responsibility for the attack three days later.
• Wilson was a fugitive in Libya; Terpil was in Beirut.
• 1980-10-14: Faisal Abdullazez Zagallai, a Libyan dissident graduate student at Colorado State University, was shot twice in the head.
• 1980-04: Edwin P. Wilson and Francis E. Terpil were indicted on charges of acting as foreign agents for Libya and of a conspiracy to murder a Qaddafi critic in Egypt.
