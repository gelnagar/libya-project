LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI VIEWS CURRENT ISSUES, PAKISTANI BOMB 


LD221440 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 22 Jan 81 


[Excerpts] The leader of the revolution said that the world should be changed into a 

new and better world where freedom and happiness would prevail, where the workers would 
have the upper hand and be partners and not hired hands, where the system of representation 
and governments would disappear and the nation and people would be governed through 
people's conferences, and where the motto would be: No democracy without people's con- 
ferences. He made these remarks to the Indian current affairs weekly (?CURRENT) in an 
interview which was published in the last issue. 


Analyzing his vision of a new world which will be governed neither by capitalism or 
Marxism, the leader of the revolutior added that wealth, authority and weapons should 
be in the hands of the people in such a world so that there would be guarantees for 
freedom. 


Referring to the development of events on the African Continent represented by the French 
imperialist interference in Africa and its occupation of Central Africa after security 
and peace had been established in Chad, the brother leader stressed to the magazine 

that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah will consolidate its forces in Chad 
as long as France continues to reinforce its forces nearby. The brother leader explained 
that the unity between Libya and Chad is the right of both fraternal peoples to conso- 
lidate their relations. 


Regarding the Iraqi-Iranian war, the brother leader pointed out that when Iran was under 
the shah's rule, it was a zone of American influence. It was against the Arab nation 

and assisted Israel and South Africa. But now that the situation has changed completely. 
Following the triumph of the Iranian revolution Iran has become a friend of the Palestinian 
people and is against America. He said that when one fights Iran, he is doing so on 
behalf of America and Israel. This is why we are helping the Iranian revolution. Iran 
deserved war when it was under the shah's rule. 


Regarding the Egyptian ruler's commitment to the Stable David [Camp David] agreements 
and the United States, the leader of the revolution emphasized that As-Sadat is now 
finished and that one must look to the future. He expressed the hope that the new 
American administration would understand the Arab issue and realize that its interests 
are not in the hands of As-Sadat.

The leader of the revolution refuted the Western imperialist rumors and allegations 

about Libyan-Pakistani cooperation for the manufacturer of a nuclear weapon. He said 
there is no cooperation between us and Pakistan in this connection and that we did 

not give Pakistan a single dirham for the manufacture of a bomb nor shall we do so in the 
future. 


The brother leader stressed the right of the small states to use nuclear energy for 
peaceful pruposes. 


In conclusion, the brother leader hailed the relations between the Jamahiriyah and India 
and the efforts being made by Indira Gandhi for the consolidation of relations of friend- 
ship and cooperation between the two peoples--the Libyan Arab and the Indian people. 


SPOKESMAN DEFENDS LIBYAN MOTIVES IN CHAD 


LD230857 Paris LE MONDE in French 22 Jan 81 p 8 


[Paul Balta interview with Libyan representative Ahmad al-Qadhdhafi in Paris on 20 
January: "It Is Ridiculous To Suggest We Intend To Annex"] 


{Text] Following the meeting of the General People's Congress (Libya's supreme legislative 
body) Colonel al-Qadhdhafi outlined his reply to his critics by explaining that the latter 
had wrongly translated and interpreted the Arabic word for "merger" with Chad, because 

it was a union between two fraternal and neighboring peoples and not organic unity between 

two states, still less annexation of the former French colony. 


At the same time the Libyan revolution "guide" has sent his nephew Ahmad al-Qadhdhafi-- 
one of his best-loved relatives--to Paris on an unofficial basis to explain things at 
the highest level if possible. 


"Why," he said to us on Tuesday 20 January, “are you making so much fuss about us? 
Our country is huge, rich and full of resources. Why would it wish to annex Chad? 
That is ridiculous. That country had been torn by war for years and we helped it to 
end the war. For the first time for ages the Chadians are no longer fighting each 
other. We should be thanked for the aid which we gave them and which we are prepared 
to give them for rebuilding their country." 


[Question] Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that Chad constituted part of Libya's vital 
"lebensraum." That is bound to stir up bad memories in Europe. 


[Answer] Those remarks were misinterpreted. Chad is our neighbor and it is largely 
populated by Muslims. We cannot be indifferent to its problems. We are Muslims and 
advocate the liberation of, and rapprochement between, Muslims. What is reprehensible 
about that? We said that we understand that France has interests in Africa. However, 
France for its part should understand that we are an African country and that we cannot 
be indiffenent to Africa's fate. We want to explain our position to France, put our 
cards on the table, explain our intentions and discuss our problems with France. However, 
France seems to have been avoiding any meeting for weeks. We do not understand that re- 
action when we have a great deal of respect for its Arab and Mediterranean policy and 
its understanding of the Palestinian people. 


{Question] That may be so but the Libyans sacked the French Embassy in Tripoli in 
February 1980. 


[Answer] We have said that we would pay for the repairs. In addition we have made many 
good-will gestures. The oil agreements reached with Mr Chalandon constitute a major 
advantage for France; that advantage was, moreover, felt acutely by the United States. 
We have also decided to import at least 30,000 Peugeot automobiles; that is considerable. 
{answer ends]

Ahmad al-Qadhdhafi then stressed that most of the African countries which accuse Libya of 
wishing to form a Saharan empire also accuse it of playing into the Soviet Union's hands. 
"However," he said, "we have good relations with the USSR because it supports the Arab 
cause. The moderate countries which criticize us refuse to see that we have good relations

CHAD'S GOUKOUNI CONDEMNS FRENCH STAND ON CHAD 


LD230030 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 22 Jan 81 


[Text] The president of Chad, Goukouni Oueddei, condemned the French threats to Chad and 

the consolidation of the French military presence in Central Africa, and said that he 
considered those threats as directed against the people of Chad and also against progressive 
peoples in the region. This came in a press conference president of Chad held in Tripoli 

in the evening in the presence of a number of Arab and international news agency correspondents. 


He said that the reaction of the people of Chad and also of the progressive states to those 
threats is clear, and it is a categorical rejection of those threats. He accused the French 
Government of adopting attitudes hostile to the transitional government in Chad and accused 
it of restoring its military presence in Africa. He pointed out to the reporters that Chad 
was facing danger when it fought the remnants of the rebels backed by France, the United 
States, Egypt and Sudan. He said that the Libyan forces intervened at the request of Chad 
in order to settle the situation and to enable the transitional government and the revolu- 
tion to restore security, tranquillity and peace to the country. 


The president of Chad condemned the biased French propaganda spread through its agents 
against Chad, in a desperate attempt to shake its attitude. (?He also condemned) the 
Egyptian-Sudanese attempts aimed at bringing back the rebel Hissein Habre, the agent who 
was defeated by the forces of the Chadian revolution, confirming that neither the ruler of 
Egypt nor his vassal Numayri can do anything. 


The Chadian president added that the Chadian people will be ready to stand in the face of 

all attempts aimed at detracting from their freedom. He pointed out that the two agent 
regimes in Egypt and Sudan face numerous internal difficulties in addition to their stands 
against the Palestine people and hence they are trying to cover up all this by their maneuvers 
against the Chadian people. But we will resist most fiercely any attempt by these two 

regimes to harm our land or dignity. 


He strongly denounced the Western and reactionary propaganda media which speak of imaginery 
disturbances inside Chad. He categorically affirmed that Chad has not witnessed any dis- 
turbances since 15 December, the day on which the revolution triumphed and inflicted heavy 
defeat on the aggressors and rebels. 


Also today Robert Mugabe, prime minister of the nationalist government in Zimbabwe, condemned 
foreign interventions in African affairs, confirming that any African problem should be solved 
by the Africans themselves. He said this morning during his meeting with brother ‘Abd 

al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the Popular Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison, that his country regards with appreciation the role which the Jamahiriyah continues 
to play for the achievement of security and peace in Chad, in cooperation with its legitimate 
government. The prime minister of Zimbabwe expressed appreciation of the valuable assistance 
rendered by the Libyan people to the people of Zimbabwe during its struggle.

IRAN, LIBYA SIGN AIR TRANSPORT AGREEMENT 


JN221902 Tehran International Service in Arabic 1730 GMT 22 Jan 81 


{Text} An agreement has been signed between the minister of roads and transport of the 
Islamic Republic of Iran and the Libyan minister of transport. Before signing the agree- 
ment, the brother Libyan minister of transport delivered a speech on strengthening coopera- 
tion between the two countries. He also expressed his country's support for the Islamic 
Republic of Iran in all fields. 


Brother Musa Kalantari, Iranian minister of roads and transport, then expressed thanks to 
the Libyan brothers and hope that the conclusion of this agreement will be a good beginning 
for future bilateral relations. 


The agreement provides mutual benefits from the Tehran-Damascus-Tripoli-Algiers air route 
for passenger transport. It also covers the provision of technical services and trans- 
portation of Libyan pilgrims on Iran Airlines next year. 


MOSCOW EMBASSY TRANSFORMED INTO PEOPLE'S BUREAU 


LD231300 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 23 Jan 81 


[Text] Moscow--The Libyan masses present in Moscow this morning marched on the Libyan 
Embassy in Moscow, turned it into a people's bureau and formed a people's committee to 

run it. The people's committee formed to run the bureau in its statement affirmed that 

the bureau will consolidate relations between the Libyan Arab and the Soviet peoples dis- 
regarding traditional diplomacy and in the belief that true and firm relations are relations 
among peoples. 


MAURITANTA 


BNEIJARA SENDS INAUGURAL CONGRATULATIONS TC REAGAN 


LD222126 Nouakchott Domestic Service in Arabic 2030 GMT 22 Jan 81 


[Text] Prime Minister Ahmed Ould Bneijara has sent the following cable of congratulations 
to His Excellency Ronals Reagan, the U.S. President: 


On the occasion of your official appointment as President of the United States, we convey 
to you, in the name of the Mauritanian Government and myself, our warmest congratulations 
and best wishes for happiness and success for you and the American people. 


I am confident that your election to lead your great country will contribute to the consoli- 
dation of world peace and the respect for human rights which the American people have always 
championed. 


I am confident that cooperation between both our countries will grow for the certain good 
of both our peoples. 


Please accept my utmost respects.
