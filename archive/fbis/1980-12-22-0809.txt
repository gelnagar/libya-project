LIBYA

SPOKESMAN REPLIES TO U.S. STATE DEPARTMENT ON CHAD 


LD191750 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1615 GMT 19 Dec 80 


{Text] Tripoli, 19 Dec (JANA)--A spokesman of the Libyan External Liaison Bureau has made 
a statement to JANA in reply to the recent statements by the U.S. State Department spokesman 
that the United States viewed with concern what he described as Lil ‘a's interference in 
Chad. The Libyan spokesman said that the United States and the imperialist powers do not 
want to see stability and security return to Chad now that the rebellion has been crushed. 
This is because the Uniced States wants to find a pretext for maneuvering to dominate Chad 
and other African countries and loot their wealth. This is evident from the fact that 
though the war has ended, the rebellion has been finally crushed and the national legiti- 
mate government forces have taken full control of the entire Chadian territory, we still 
hear the U.S. State Department spokesman making utterances through which the U.S. adminis- 
tration aims at creating confusion and sedition and at interfering in the internal affairs 
of Africa and Chad. Had it not been for the U.S. State Department statement, the spokesman 
added, Libya would not have touched on this matter at all because it does not itself wish 
to interfere nor see others interfere in the internal affairs of the African countries. 


Libya, he said, does not conceal the fact that it has been offering peaceful civil aid to 
the hungry and afflicted Chadian people and that it sends daily hundreds of tons of 
medicines, food, vaccines, baby milk and clothing to Chad at the time when the imperialist 
powers express sorrow at the ending of the war there and hope that the war will flare up 
again.

It would have been better for these countries, he said, to offer aid to the Chadian 
people instead of trying to create sedition and reopen wounds. Libya will continue to 
offer humanitarian aid, the spokesman stressed, to the fraternal Chadian people as 
dictated by its African duty. The prevalence of security in Chad is of prime importance 
to Libya before any other country, he said, and it is determined to inflict defeat on 
imperialism until it is crushed and expelled from all the areas it controls. 


We are very sorry to see such statements made by a big power which is a permanent member 
of the UN Security Council, he added. In any case, these statements are irresponsible as 
they are made by the collapsing Carter administration which is about to collect its 
scattered papers and leave office, he added. We also believe that these statements do 
not reflect the views of the new U.S. administration of Reagan which has not yet assumed 
office. 


We warn against any interference in Chad in whatever form and by whatever method, the 
spokesman said. Those who feign care for the Chadian people should help them to restore 
stability instead of creating sedition and interfering in their internal affairs. 


AL-MAJALLAH REPORTS SOVIET-LIBYAN TREATY EXPECTED 


LD220859 London AL-MAJALLAH in Arabic 20-26 Dec 80 p 10 
("From AL-MAJALLAH's Ambassadors" feature] 


[Text] Informed Arab diplomatic sources have stated that Libya will conclude a 20-year 
treaty of friendship and cooperation with the Soviet Union if Egypt launches a major 
military offensive against it. The sources pointed out that this treaty will be very 
similar to the one which Syria signed with the Soviet Union last September. The possi- 
bilities of a military confrontation between Egypt and Libya increased in the past 2 
weeks and were at the center of contacts between Cairo and Washington. If such a treaty 
is signed, Moscow would acquire an impotant strategic and political position in North 
Africa.
