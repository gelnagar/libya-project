Summary (AI): A 1974 CIA assessment of Communist and Free World arms deliveries to Egypt, Syria, Iraq and Libya. For Libya it reports Tripoli's oil-financed purchases from the West (about $300 million in the first half of 1974, including 30 Mirage F-1s) and Soviet SA-6 missiles and T-62 tanks shown at the 1 September 1974 parade in Tripoli.
• Libya bartered oil wealth for military hardware; Prime Minister Jallud concluded a significant military agreement in Moscow in May 1974.
• Libya concluded about $300 million in Western arms agreements in the first half of 1974, including 30 Mirage F-1s valued at $126 million and a $108 million contract for six missile-armed patrol boats.
• Between 1970-73 Libya bought almost $475 million in military equipment from the US and Western Europe versus $150 million from Moscow and Eastern Europe.
• The withdrawal of the Egyptian military mission from Libya prompted Tripoli to seek additional foreign assistance, including Soviet advisors and technicians.
• 1974-09-01: Parade in Tripoli commemorating Qadhafi's fifth anniversary of power displayed SA-6 surface-to-air missiles and T-62 tanks, probably bought in a Soviet military deal.
