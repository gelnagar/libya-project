Summary (AI): A 1989 CIA reading-room translation of a Libyan External Security Organisation report on Syrian national Muhammad Jamal Danura, consisting mainly of his curriculum vitae (education, oil-industry jobs, business in Malta). It has no specific Tripoli sites, and only a passing mention of Tripoli Airport as an address.
• Subject born 1935 in Lottegia, Syria; graduated from Reserve Officer's College, Aleppo, in 1960 and War College, Homs, in 1962.
• Subject held a baccalaureate from the College of Economic and Political Science, University of Cairo (1968).
• Subject worked for Abu Dhabi Oil Co. in public relations (1974-1980) and Zakum Oil Field Development Company (1980-1985).
• Subject ran a restaurant business in Malta from 1987 onward.
• The envelope was sent via Med Tours Services Ltd., Mosta, Malta.
