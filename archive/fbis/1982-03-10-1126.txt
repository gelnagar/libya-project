LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI NOT TO MEET ‘OPPONENTS' IN VIENNA 


LD092020 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1950 GMT 9 Mar 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 9 Mar (JANA) -- BBC radio reported this evening that Col Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi had invited what it described as his opponents for a reconciliation meeting 
in Vienna. 


In fact, as was pointed out, the expected meeting between Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and Libyans 
abroad is aimed at overcoming the difficulties and problems facing Libyans abroad and 

at facilitating their return to their country, the jamahiriyah. It is not to meet his 
opponents as claimed by BBC radio because Colonel al-Qadhdhafi has no opponents or enemies. 
If there are any such people, they are those absorbed by foreign intelligence and who were 
condemned by people's tribunals. Consequently, they are hostile to the people's congresses 
and people's authority which laid down the laws and passed the sentences, and not to 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi. 


UNIDENTIFIED AMERICAN WARNS OF AIR ATTACK 


LD091904 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1830 GMT 9 Mar 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 9 Mar (JANA) — An American, claiming he is from the CIA and who wants 
his identity to remain secret, contacted the security authorities in the jamahiriyah and 
told them that the Israelis soon will attack southern Lebanon and that this week or next 
U.S. aircraft bearing Israeli markings will take off from U.S. aircraft carrier and attack 
Libya. The aircraft will be piloted by Israeli or American pilots carrying Israeli 
identity cards. 


The same source said that the planning of the attack will result in the belief that the 
attack did not start from the U.S. aircraft carriers and that it was not an American 
attack. He added that a group of aircraft will fly over Egyptian territory and attack 
Libya where they will be joined by other U.S. aircraft taking off from the aircraft 
carriers to give the impression that the attack came from the occupied territory. The 
source asked that this information be reported to the highest authority in the jamahiriyah. 
He explained that the plan was drawn up by the CIA and the Pentagon and U.S. President 
Ronald Reagan, but the State Department was not informed of the plan because of the 
differences splitting the U.S. administration.

STUDENTS' CONGRESS ISSUES STATEMENT 4 MAR 


LD051848 Tripoli JANA in English 1642 GMT 5 Mar 82 


[Text] Az-Zawiyah, 5 Mar (JANA) -- The sixth general congress of the Jamahiriyah's 
students has issued the following statement at the end of its work in the city of 
4z-Zawiyah last night: 


The students of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah today end their sixth 
congress through which they exercised their real direct democracy and proved their 
strong adherence to the people's authority. They have been guided by the immortal 
dicta of the Green Book which is the guide of man towards final emancipation from all 
forms of oppression, in justice, slavery and exploitation. 


The students today feel the importance and effect of their positive role on the 
international arenas in general and the Arab [arena] in particular so as to confront 
challenges and provocations imposed by the dirty imperialism led by the U.S. 
administration and the Arab reaction headed by the rotten Saudi family. 


The Libyan Arab students stress without hesitation that they will no longer stand idly 
by before the reactionary imperialist tide which is sweeping vast areas of our Arab 
world occupying advanced positions at the expense of our existence and integrity. 


The students pledge their unlimited alliance with the dynamic revolutionary forces in 
the Arab world to wipe out the shame and humiliation and to launch a counterattack 
against the pockets of treason and symbols of defeat and capitulation. They are 
determined to deal a blow to the traitors whatever the cost and immaterial of the 
sacrifices demanded. 


We, as Libyan Arab students on this revolutionary and free soil have resolved to lead 
the unionist struggle to create a jamahiri Arab unity in which the Arab people 
possess authority, wealth and arms through which Arabs achieve freedom, integrity, 
dignity and pride. 


We also decided to erupt the volcano of anger inside our brothers, the Arab students 
and youth, so that they rebel against their executioners and topple the rotten fragile 
thrones and shaky regimes. 


The local reaction in our large Arab wourld should realise that its end is near and 

that it speeded up its own doom by joining the enemies’ camp. The flood will eventually 
uproot it sooner than it has expected where the imperialist forces of evil will help 

no more as happened to the shah of Iran, As-Sadat of Egypt, Somoza of Nicaragua, 

Idriss as-Senussiof Libya and many others. [sentence as received] 


The Libyan Arab students who are clinging to arms and who are ready to die for this 
nation will neither be scared by the mean plots nor the colonialist fleets and bases. 
They will teach the enemy an unforgettable lesson should it dare to harm the security 
and sovereignty of our territories, waters and airspace. 


The economic embargo imposed on us by the Saudi reaction, which is "a filthy pig that 
feeds from left-overs in the U.S. stable" will not reduce our determination and will 
not divert us from our revolutionary path, because our real strength does aot lie in 
our oil as some sick and ignorant people might think but in our theory, dicta and 
faith. We welcome this imperialist reactionary embargo to prove to the world our 
capability of steadfastness, confrontation, challenge and self-dependence. We, as 
students, will change into productive cadres for reclaiming land, building roads, 
setting up farms and schools, create prosperity and achieve self-sufficiency. 


We students, promise our people and leader to die in defence of the gains of our 
revolution and to adhere to its principles and objectives. 


Al Fateh forever! The struggle continues!

[Text] Az-Zawiyah, 5 Mar (JANA) -—- The sixth general congress of jamahiriyah students 
which ended here yesterday drafted the resolutions and recommendations passed by the 
basic students congresses at their sixth ordinary session. These are as follows: 


"In the name of God, the most merciful and compassionate. Resolutions and recommendations 
of the basi- students congresses drafted by the sixth general congress of jamahiriyah 
students. 


With our belief set in God, and the ideology of the Great al-Fateh Revolution taken as 
our guideline which asserts the authority of the people and recognizes no forms of 
representation or mediation, the sixth general congress of jamahiriyah students was 
held in Az-Zawiyah on February 27-March to draft the resolutions and recommendations of 
the basic students’ congresses at their sixth ordinary session. 


The congress has endeavorured to be as thorough as possible in drafting these resolutions 
and recommendations. It thanks the students’ and people's congresses and the people's 
committees in the municipality of Az-Zawiyah tor hosting the sixth general ccngress 

of jamahiriyah students and providing it with facilities which have contributed tc the 
success of its meeting. 


The congress pays tribute t. the delegations of international and Arab students unions 
and youth organizations which attended the meeting of the congress and stresses the 
importance of consolidating relations with them. The following resolutions and 
recommendations were passed by the sixth general congress of jamahiriyah students: 


1. Revision of the resolutions and recommendations of the basic students congresses 
drafted by the fifth general congress which was held in Ghar Yunis University on 


February 7-10, 1981. The basic students congresses revised the report submitted by 
the Secretariat of the general congress of jamahiriyah students which included the 
executive measures taken to implement its resolutions and recommendations. It is 
satisfied with what has been implemented and stresses the need to press ahead with the 
rest of the resolutions not yet implemented especially those concerned with: 


A. Uniting the scholarship department which includes grants, sabbatical leaves and 
supervision of students studying abroad. 


B. Preparing a bill to organise training procedures and define the relationship between 
the departments concerned for training and people's committees connected with educational 
establishments. 


C. Amending the scholarship bill and fellowing up its implementation with the basic 
people's congresses and the people's committces concerned. 


2. A. The basic students congresses considers the general people's committee responsible 
for preparation, supervision, and laying out programmes, curricula and plans that fully 
occupy students time in a good manner which leads to a scientific and guided imple- 
mentation of the students resolution on employment in coordination with the students 
general people's committee. 


B. The basic students congresses calls for creating job opportunity in the evenings 

for those students willing to take up a job so that to reduce dependence on foreign 

labour to a minimum. The work should be on productive sites subject to the controls 

laid down by the basic students congresses on productive work. The congresses also stress 
the importance of the students participation in the illiteracy campaigns.

C. To enable the people's committees concerned to carry out employment programmes, the 
basic students' congresses find it important to make the study periods flexible enough 

so that they give the students better chances to increase the rate of seasonal production 
putting into consideration the climatic conditions that enable the students to acquire 

an intensified learning during academic terms. 


D. In implementation of the unionist concept of the jamahiriyah's students as an expres- 
sion of the pan-Arab feeling created by Great Al Fateh Revolution in the students, the 
basic students congresses will set up production camps for the Arab students inside and 
outside the jamahiriyah who are willing to solve their financial problems and have better 
opportunity to continue their studies. These camps should be centres to advocate the 
new jamahiri thought so as to draw the future features of the only Arab state and achieve 
a real unity of the Arab students. 


3. The popular revolution drawn by the Green Book towards building a happy and free soci- 
ety is able to create its tools of expression in science, literature, arts and sports. 
The students, naturally, are the qualified force to take up this pioneering role. 


Accordingly, the basic students’ congresses recommend to sponsor the talents in various 
fields by developing programmes, contests, training and camps so that they achieve a wider 
participation of students, also to make available the resources and necessary equipment 
for the success of these programmes and plans within the context of the society's respect 
for man's talents, inclinations and creativity. 


4. According to the historic speech of the leader to the Great Al Fateh Revolution on 
the 5th anniversary marking the students’ revolution of 7th April and in response to his 
call on the student masses to take over the media so as to exercise the wide scale and 
continuous incitation of the Arab masses to revolt against the deteriorating regional 
reality, therefore the basic students congresses assign their revolutionary committees 

to march on the various media institutions so that they create a jamahiri media free 

from unjustified overspending, a media which is neither traditional, and inherited, nor 
addressing the symbols created by capitalist cultures, a media-which is totally free from 
archa’ style, superficial analysis and weakness in content. The basic students congresses 
decided to select the various skills and expertise in the educational institutions to be 
prepared, trained and moved to the media. 


5. The masses of students who assert their pioneering role in the construction of the 
new jamahiri society have always distinguished themselves in the education sector. They 
have continuously incited for a cultural revolution in the curricula and complete revision 
of the education system to fit the needs of the modern age of technology. They have 
endeavoured to participate in the administration and supervision of the educational insti- 
tutions through their people's committees. They therefore recommend the following: 


A. Review of the religion syllabus in accordance with the spirit of the revolution. 
Institutes of religion would thus advocate the religious revolution and assert the 
revolutionary and progressive aspect of Islam. They recommend that the curricula should 
give attention to the recitation of the Koran and rid the programmes from misconceptions 
attributed to Islam by ignorant and hostile circles. 


B. Attention to be devoted to the development of buildings of educational institutions 
in accordance with the new educational objectives. Students’ congresses should supervise 
the maintenance of their educational institutions. 


C. (I) While the basic students’ congresses consider educational grants discordant with 
a jamahiri society, they think it necessary to continue the procedure until the employment 
programmes are finalized and the academic year is divided in a way which would provide 

the students with the opportunity for productive work.

(II) Revision of grants of students studying abroad in accordance with the standard of 
living of the country concerned, and even out any unjustified differences among students 
studying in the same country. 


6. (I) The need to link all educational establishments by public transport. 


(II) Reduction of transport tickets for students travelling to their educational bases. 


7. The need to enable the general congress of students of the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah to depend on themselves in financing their activities and programmes in 
implementation of the recommendations of basic students congresses and reduce dependence 
on the general budget allocations. The basic students congresses allow the general 
students people's committee to carry out such investment porjects provided they free 
themselves of any form of exploitation and conform to the dicta of the new socialist 


society, with due consideration paid to the comments of the basic students congresses 
on this issue. 


The Libyan Arab society being transformed through revolution into the new socialist society 
is in need of all productive efforts in order to achieve their aspirations in building a 
just and independent economy. Therefore, and in (?emphasizing) the students’ role, the 
basic students’ congresses decided to adopt the principle of self-management of all their 
educational institutions and gradually get rid of the extra labour which in turn is to be 
directed to productive sites. 


9. In implementation of the dictum of the Green Book, "Those who do not produce do not 
eat, except for the elderly", the basic students congresses decided to adopt the policy 

of self-sufficiency in their educational institutions severely or collectively subject to 
circumstances in order to satisfy the needs of their individuals. The remarks of the basic 
students’ congresses in this respect will be observed. 


10. The basic students’ congresses have reviewed the report prepared by the students’ 
liaison bureau on the bilateral agreements, hosting some continental and international 
youth and student activities, the situation of the students’ organizations and unions in 
the jamahiriyah and the bases of dealings with (?them). Accordingly, the congresses have 
decided on the following: 


A. Endorse agreements with: (I) National Union of Algerian Youth; (II) National Union 
of Syrian Students. 


B. The basic students’ congresses agree to host the activities included in the report 
and point out that spending on them should be rational and economical. 


C. The basic students’ congresses endorsed the bases of dealings with the students’ 
organizations and unions in the jamahiriyah and suggest these unions allow their members 
to effectively participate in decisionmaking and implement these decisions, and work to 
create a formula for unified student action. The basic people's congresses assign the 
students liaison bureau to prepare a set of rules to this effect. 


11. The basic students’ congresses have laid down the activities programme for 1982-83 
and suggested general items for discussion in the next session of the basic students 
congresses. 


Al Fateh forever revolutionary, struggle continues, the 6th general congress of the students 
of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah.

Cables al-—Qadhdhafi 
LD051902 Tripoli JANA in English 1650 GMT 5 Mar 82 


[Text] Az-Zawiyah, 5 Mar (JANA) -- At its conclusion, the sixth general congress of 
jamahiriyah students sent the following cable to the leader of the revolution, Colonel 
Mu'ammar al-—Qadhdhafi: 


The sixth general congress of jamahiriyah students was held to draft the resolutions and 
recommendations passed by the basic people's congresses. At the same time it consolidates 
the sole authority of the people and implements the vanguard humanitarian dicta and values 
of the Green Book, which has advocated the jamahir (of the masses) era and the path for 
final emancipation for all humanity from all forms of oppression and exploitation. 


This year the congress is held at a difficult time in the history of our Arab nation with 

the imperialist onslaught against us, led by the United States and its irresponsible 
administration, becoming more intensified. This campaign has become more vicious and can 

be described as the 10th crusade, this time directed especially against the jamahiriyah due 
to the pioneering role its revolution has been playing under its courageous revolutionary 
command. The jamahiriyah has been a leader in the international revolutionary movement 
opposed to imperialism and has, in fact, become today the international centre for the 
dynamic revolutionary forces fighting imperialism and terrorism. This is a source of immense 
pride to our people.

Our congress concluded at a memorable day in our history when we became responsible for 
our own resources. For, today marks the fifth anniversary of the jamahiriyah and the 
assumption of people's authority. We should like to take this opportunity to affirm 
to you and our great people and all dynamic forces in the world, that we shall remain 
sincere and loyal advocates of the principle of final emancipation and the Green Book 
dicta. Furthermore, we assert that the terrorist practices by the United States, in 
its attempt to subjugate us to its will and force us to join the rotten reactionary 
alliance led by Fahd of Saudi Arabia only increases our defiance and adherence to our 
cause and revolution. It increases our determination to fight until death because we 
either live with honour and dignity or die in martyrdom. 


The resolutions of our students’ congresses will continue to express the student's 
responsibility and alertness since they had the honour to trigger off the revolution's 
first spark in Sebha and repeatedly opposed the practices of the former monarchical 

regime for which the martyrs of 1964 testify. The students took up the cause of the 
revolution from its first day and have remained its staunch supporters. They have also 
speeded up the assumption of people's authority by triggering off the student's revolution 
in accordance with your call to purge the Libyan universities. The students with this 
past record affirm their absolute commitment to the contents of your historic speech on 
the festival of the declaration of people's authority and your guiding speech at the 
extra-ordinary meeting of the general people's congress. 


We, furthermore, affirm commitment to the precepts of the Green Book and assure you of 
our continued support to proceed ahead along the route charted out. May you long continue 
as our leader. 


“Onward, the revolutionary struggle continues. Al Fateh Revolution shall go on forever."
