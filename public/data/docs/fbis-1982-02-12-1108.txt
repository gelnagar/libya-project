LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI URGES ‘MARCH ON INFORMATION MEDIA' 


NC111138 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 11 Feb 82 


[Text] Within the framework of affirming the people's authority and guiding the masses 

in determining their own future, the brother leader of the revolution has met with members 
of the secretariats of the student popular conferences to throw light on some questions 
included in the agenda of the student conferences. 


The meeting was attended by professors from Lebanese universities and Jaime Wheelock, 
member of the national leadership of the Sandinist Front and minister of agriculture and 
agrarian reform in Nicaragua. 


The brother leader noted that a decision was made concerning the students’ employment but 

for reasons unknown so far the decision has not been fully applied. He emphasized that 

the decision on dividing the scholastic year does not mean taking fees, but it means 
participation by this live force in building progress in the Jamahiriyah. 


Regarding the question of marching on the media, the brother leader noted that there are 
two types of world media: official information and commercial information. The information 
media under the Jamahiriyah system is substantially different from these two systems. 


The brother leader noted that the solution for the Jamahiriyah system is that the mass 
media information should serve the masses. He said the final solution is to set up a 
people's committee from all groups, such as the unions of students, engineers, women 

and other. However, in the phase of affirming the people's authority -- which is the 
period in which the revolutionary committees should incite the masses to assert the 
people's authority -- the media must be controlled by the revolutionary committees as 
they are a means for propagation. The information media at this stage are a platform for 
incitement and an instrument to incite the masses to assert and consolidate their popular 
authority and to propagate the era of the masses. 


The leader explained there are no other means for the revolutionary committees because 

they are the structure of the era of the masses. He stressed there must be a march on 

the information media through the student revolutionary committees in schools, institutes 
and universities where, at every student conference, the revolutionary committees must 
march on the information media. The student conferences must select persons who can direct 
the media together with the student revolutionary committees and a conference should be 
held, after the selection is made from the student base, to direct the march. 


On education, the leader affirmed that knowledge is the natural right of man. He pointed 
out that science must not be monopolized and no one must be deprived of it as is the 

case now where some states are being prevented from entering the atomic age by other states 
that are monopolizing the atomic age. 


The borther leader noted the present education scale and said it is not in line with the 
era of the masses because the society of the masses is the society of freedom and the 
trend of education must be toward scientific education. 


He threw light on self-management -- a new system stemming from the nature of the 

masses society -- and said the owners, and not employees, must manage all public establishments 
such as the educational institutes where self-management must be taught in class. He threw 
light on the self-sufficiency clause in that every institute, under specific terms, must be 
self-sufficient.

LD111800 Tripoli JANA in English 1431 GMT 11 Feb 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 11 Feb (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY) -- The revolution's leader, Colonel 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, last Tuesday evening conferred with the teaching staff of the 
Lebanese university (64 lecturers). 


An intellectual discussion ensued in which Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said Lebanon is an 
active theatre of Arab interaction reflecting the Arab reality. He said the Lebanese 
people are the vanguard of the Arab nation, living as they are a life of hardship and 
courageously fighting the enemy. 


He added: "This gives us hope in the effectiveness of the Arab nation and its superior 
capability in facing difficult challenges. With its resources and capabilities, this 
nation can gain victory.... 


Lebanon is currently passing through tragic times, all for the Arab nation. It is in 
the frontline, facing the enemy and presenting sacrifices for the Arab nation.... - 


"The Lebanese people are paying a high price for the Palestinian cause whilst the Arabs 
look on as spectators, as if what is happening in Lebanon is no concern of theirs...in 
fact these Arabs are exercising treachery...." 


On the third universal theory, Col al-Qadhdafi said: "The Green Book is the cornerstone 

of this theory. The theory provides for the immediate transfer from a bourgeois-dominated 
to a socialist society, eliminating the need for all transitional periods in between. 

The theory seeks to achieve happiness. It also cails for man's freedom by liberation 

from human needs...If man achieves freedom then happiness is automatically attained.... 
The third universal theory seeks to end oppression and exploitation by destroying the 
forces behind them and ultimately seeking the exercise of authority by all people.... 

The theory further calls for an end to the monopolisation of authority, wealth, arms, 
culture, sport, fashion, art and religion." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said these goals will no doubt be realised once the era of the masses 
is brought about. He said: "Through popular revolution, exploitation and oppression 
shall be eradicated and the masses shall be the wielders of arms. Through the masses‘ 
accession to authority, the struggle for power will end, as the people will exercise this 
authority directly with no intermediaries to allegedly represent them. The struggle for 
wielding control over resources and arms will also end, with the masses carrying arms.... 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed to the stages of the bitter struggle being waged for humanity's 
salvation and the creation of a mass society. He also referred to the serious attempts 
made throughout history to reach this goal, and to the sacrifices made by mankind for 
salvation and happiness. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "Peoples are now continuing these attempts and presenting 
even further sacrifices to end traditional forms of government forces and exploitation." 
He said: Peoples are in need of a clear vision to enable them to achieve success. Such 
a vision can only come through intense experiences and the continuation of struggle....

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed to the failure of traditional forms of government in 
expressing the hopes and expectations of the masses. He added: “The people can express 
their own needs properly. It is for this that the people should wield authority and 
destroy all tools of so-called representation... 


"The masses are capable of exercising authority and taking decisions that reflect their 
hopes and expectations. Throughout the world, these masses are continuing their struggle 
for liquidating all tools of traditional government in a bid to attain power and exercise 
authority directly... 


“With the establishment of the jamahiriyah (society of the masses) the struggle for power, 
wealth and arms shall end. These three weapons, that have long terrorized ordinary 
citizens, shall be then in the hands of the people, liberating them from fear, oppression 
and exploitation..." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that by the establishment of the jamahiriyah in Libya, the 
cornerstone for the era of the masses had been laid, representing the final stage in the 
masses’ long struggle to wield authority...The motto, he added, now becomes "no democracy 
with people's congresses..." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi discussed the method by which popular resolutions are passed and 
implemented. He explained the process by which the people's congresses first discuss 
their agendas and then draft their resolutions which the respective congress secretaries 
submit to the General People's Congress. The General People's Congress, which includes 
the secretaries of people's committees, secretaries of vocational congresses and unions, 
drafts the resolutions passed by the people's congresses and coordinates them. 


In this respect, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that the General Peoples Congress is 
merely a committee responsible for drafting the texts of resolutions and cannot deal with 
the resolutions passed by the people's congresses. 


He said: "The resolutions of the people's congresses, which were drafted by the General 
People's Congress, themselves determine the jamahiriyah's internal and foreign policy. 
After that, the people's committees are responsible for implementing the resolutions 
under the supervision of the people's congresses. 


"At this stage and until the people's authority is consolidated and the people become 
accustomed to the exercise of authority, there needs to be someone who can urge the people 
to exercise their authority. This body is made up of the revolutionary committees." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi underlined that the idea of revolutionary committees is part of a 
new international movement which differs completely from previous revolutionary or 
political movements because all those attempted to seek power in order to exercise it 
against the people and their interest. He said while in the past the aim had been to 
seize power, the aim of the revolutionary committees is to see the masses acquire power. 


"When this is accomplished, the revolutionary committees will continue to incite the 

masses to continue their exericse of power and see that their authority is not relinquished. 
This is the crucial difference which differentiates the revolutionary committees from any 
previous political or revolutionary movement." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi moved on to comment on the economic systems of the world and how the 
Green Book theses have surpassed any of those. He said: "The jamahiri order which 
depends on people's congresses and committees has no representation because all the people 
are present in these congresses. The people's committees do not issue anything because 
they are responsible for implementing the resolutions of the people's congresses under 

the supervision of the congresser."

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi continued: "The democracy discussed in part one of the Green Book 
remains theoretical until part two of the Green Book is implemented and which concerns a 
solution for the economic problem. When the poor and the rich sit side by side in the 
people's congress a diversity exists between them which must be eliminated if equality is 
to be achieved in the basic people's congress. To achie.2 a true people's democracy, the 
economic problem must be solved. To do this. workers must be liberated from the slavery 
of earning wages by becoming partners instead of wage-earners, for, the producer consumes 
his own production. 


"This will then eliminate the employer-class, and trade, hire and profit. The possession 
of the house moves to the occupier...The land is not the property of anyone because freedom 
is latent in need and happiness is latent in freedom...The car belongs to the driver... 
These dicta are not incorporated in present systems. Profit in trade and paying wages are 
all forms of robbery sanctioned by the law of exploitation which must be challenged by a 
revolution... 


"Once the workers are aware of their exploitation they will revolt and destroy the powers 
of exploitation... 


"The Jamahiri community has destroyed private shops and aspects of exploitation and replaced 
them by people's shops which operate at cost price. This way the people [word indistinct] 
themselves and leave no room for profit." : 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi then spoke of the type of regime adopted by Arab and most Third World 
countries that consider themselves as being progressive. He said: "This regime is reformist 
yet contrived, because it is neither capitalist nor socialist, but of a transitional nature.... 
This formula cannot last, because he who wields authority, wealth and arms shall ultimately 
attain victory... 


"In order to create the jamahiri society, the economic problem must be solved and the second 
part of the Green Book applied, freeing mankind of its needs and ending exploitation in all 
fields..." 


On Arab unity, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "The type of Arab unity we should espouse is the 
unity of the masses through which individuals can restore their crushed dignity and wield 
authority. The unity of the masses needs time so that these theses can percolate to the 
people. After this has been achieved, the masses shall no doubt reject all traditional forms 
of representation, government, employers and exploitation." 


On the confessional situation in Lebanon, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "The big mistake 
currently being perpetrated in Lebanon is that the political system has been subjugated to 
the social system... True, all sects, religions and clans should be respected. But the 
Lebanese people, regardless of their sects, should freely establish a political system that 
equitably realises the hopes of the people and which puts the interests of Lebanon over all 
other considerations... The current situation in Lebanon cannot be remedied by force of arms 
and through alliances. The sectarian problem cannot be solved by violence... the political 
situation can only be resolved by the Lebanese people..." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi called on the university lecturers to expand their efforts in disseminating 
jamahiri culture so that the falseness of existing traditional societies and of democracy is 
exposed... 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the basic cause of current inequitable relations based on cruelty 
is the concept of profit. He said: "The jamajiri society consumes what it produces only 
and there is no place for profit..." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi also called on the Lebanese university lecturers to disseminate revolu- 
tionary awareness amongst the masses there so that force is eschewed and peace restored. Only 
after this is realised, he said, can the Lebanese masses confront the true enemy and the 
Palestinian resistance movement resist Israel.

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi stressed the importance of implementing a cultural revolution in 
Lebanon and establishing revolutionary committees there. Education and Culture, he said, 
are effective weapons in society. 


During the dialogue, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi discussed the issues of religion and nationalism. 
He called for ending cultural confusion by eradicating inherited culture and carrying a 
cultural revolution that will end exploitation and help understanding... 


On the role of sexes, Colonel al-Quadhdhafi said the rights of women should be equal to 
those of men. Women, he added, should not be forced to carry out duties that are not com- 
mensurate with their nature... 


On the problems of production, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the most important problem is that 

of incentive for increasing production... He said that world's economic establishments are 
all facing this problem. Yet he added "This problem has found a_ solution in the Jamahiri 
system: When the producer is transformed from a wage-earner to a partner in production in 
his own right, then this individual will have the inner motivation to increase production 

so that his share in the fruits of his labour grows larger..." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed the importance of satisfying human needs: "These needs cannot 
be specified by others. Human needs change and develop from one generation to another..." 


On methods of increasing production, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "This problem can be solved 
by creating the proper incentives for production. The facters involved in production are 
the individual, the machine and the raw material, and if anyone of these factors is with- 
drawn, then the industrial process comes to a standstill. All these factors are of equal 
importance to production..." 


On the problem of distribution, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "The Jamahiri society has found 
a solution to this problem as enunciated in the dictum “Only he who produces, consumes."

ARRESTS FOLLOW SUDAN 'RULER' BARRACKS VISIT 


LD112112 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 11 Feb 82 


[Text] An urgent news item datelined Khartoum says widescale arrests were made before noon 
today at Umm Durman barracks. Those arrested included a large number of officers, noncom- 
missioned officers and soldiers. The arrest campaign was carried out at this barracks follow- 
ing a highly guarded visit by the ruler of Sudan. 


It was learned that the ruler of Sudan intended to give a speech during his visit. However, 
he decided at the last minute not to do that. The meeting was dispersed in minutes and the 
ruler of Sudan returned to his palace. No details of the incident are available yet.

Wheelock Departs 
LD111952 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1741 GMT 11 Jan 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 11 Feb (JANA) -- Jaime Wheelock, member of the national leadership of 

the Sandinist Front and minister of agricultural development and agrarian reform of Nicaragua, 
and his accompanying delegation left Tripoli at noon today following his attendance at 

the first meeting of the Arab Libyan-Nicaraguan joint committee for economic, technical 

and social cooperation. 


Jaime Wheelock expressed his pride at visiting the jamahiriyah and his meeting with the 
brother leader, saying: "We have made important steps in consolidating relations between 

the two revolutions and my visit to the jamahiriyah comes within the framework of consoli- 
dating these relations in political and economic spheres and in confronting imperialism and 
strengthening our revolution." He noted that this visit gave him the chance to see at close 
quarters the extent to which the al-Fatah Revolution has gone in economic and political 
development and in liberating man. 


Jaime Wheelock hailed the firm stances of the al-Fatah Revolution which is based on the 
third international theory and its fundamental solutions for man's political, economic and 
social problems. 


The Nicaraguan agriculture minister spoke of the agricultural projects visited during his 
stay in the jamahiriyah: "What we have seen of the agricultural projects will increase our 
experience along the march of agricultural development in Nicaragua."
