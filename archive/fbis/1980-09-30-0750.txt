LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES GENERAL PEOPLE'S CONGRESS 


LD291652 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 GMT 29 Sep 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 28 Sep (JANA)--The brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has affirmed that 
the convocation of the General People's Congress in a special extraordinary session 

today to draft the resolutions adopted by the various people's congresses, which decided 
to reestablish unity by the merger of Syria and Libya, is an attempt to banish the memory 
of that ill-fated day of 28 September on which the Arab nation lost the hero of unity, 
Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir.

We have begun to change this ill-fated day into an honorable 


history of the Arab nation. 


In his speech which he delivered at the end of the General 

ordinary session today, Al-Qadhdhafi greeted the Libyan peop who, according 
physical and psychological studies, have been corrupted by oil and have turned on 
themselves, shrinking into their own shell, and have turned their backs on the Arab 
nation. He said that with their noble Arab character, the Libyan people have not 
been corrupted by oil and have, by their actions, disproved ; the conclusions 
reached by those who carry out these studies. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added: We greet the pe yple who prompt ly responded and agreed 

to shoulder the gravest burdens and greatest responsibilities by entering into unity 
with Syria; people who turned Libyan territory into a land for all Arabs by agreeing 
to turn Libya into a confrontation state. Al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that these are 
grave responsibilities which impose grave commitments on the Libyans. But the Libyan 
people have agreed to shoulder them, paying no attention to the danger. 


The Libyan people, he said, are the most loyal of the Arab peoples to the cause of the 
Arab nation. He said they are the bravest people next to the peoples of the confronta- 
tion. For it is not easy for an Arab people to tie their fate to that of a people who 
are facing the Israeli and American enemy, in addition to the forces of reaction. 
Al-Qadhdhafi stated that the Libyan people have set an example that should be followed 
by the peoples of the Arab nation. They did this by overcoming selfishness and the 
notion of profit and loss and by placing dignity above materialism. 


He stated that the heroic stand of the Libyan people will have a positive effect on 
the other Arab peoples who suffer from regionalism. 


The Libyan people, he said, deserve to live and to have their place in history, for 

all the serious revolutionary changes that have taken place in Libya have taken place 
peacefully. This is owing to the nobility of the Libyan people. It is this nobility 
that prompted the Libyans to accept the revolutionary changes and to submit to the new 
socialist Jamahiriyah society without placing any obstacles in its way, because impeding 
this course would be treason. 


The Libyan people, he said, have not stopped at accepting resolutions which are binding 
on all the people. They have become part of this united stand on the confrontation 
line and turn their land into a land for all Arabs. Indeed, our records abound with 
innumerable names of Libyans who volunteered to fight on all the Arab fronts in defense 
of the Arab nation. 


These brave stands of the Libyan people, these historic stands, merit our pausing 
to highlight them and register them in history, and greet them on this great day of 
28 September. 


In the past, he said, we have faced several stands which could have placed Libya in a 


state of confrontation along its various borders. But we have saved the Libyan people 
from confrontation so that we would not be branded as adventurists. 


But when we decided to fight or to join the unity and the confrontation against the 
enemy, we did so because we felt that this was a position which we could not avoid, 
even if we had to adopt it alone. The matter then becomes a personal one.

He pointed out that unity with Syria and the turning of Libya into a frontline state 
and a state for all Arabs are risks that had to be taken. Whatever the losses of this 
historic stand, he said, it is, in the final analysis, the right historic stand. 

It is a pan-Arab gain even if it causes regional or individual losses. There is no 
escape from entering into this confrontation. 


The brother colonel said that in 1977, confrontation was imposed on us by the As-Sadat 
regime. As-Sadat had not visited Jerusalem then and had not recognized Israel, and 
had not capitulated to the Israelis or recognized them. But he imposed on us a con- 
frontation which we did all we could to avoid, and we actually succeeded. As-Sadat's 
efforts to impose an unnecessary confrontation at that time failed. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added: While we showed no aggressive intentions toward Egypt's 
borders and hoped As-Sadat would return to the fold of the Arab nation and abolish the 
Sinai agreement, we were surprised to see night raids launched against a number of our 
police posts in which several of our civilian policemen were killed or taken prisoners. 
But we did all we could to recover those abducted from inside Libyan territory. We 
even went as far as to contact other heads of states so that they may intercede with 
As-Sadat and return the soldiers and end matters before they got worse. 


The brother colonel also spoke of the confrontation imposed on the Libyan people with 
regard to Tunisia over the issue of the continental shelf which could have turned into 
a real military confrontation. He said that the confrontation was a trifling one and 
for this reason we referred the matter to the International Court of Justice and our 
rightful clairs remain in abeyance until now. 


The commander also spoke of another confrontation with Tunisia over Qafsah. He said: 
We avoided this confrontation by self-restraint until the French forces were withdrawn 
and the French president declared that there were no French soldiers in Tunisia. 


The commander also spoke of the confrontation imposed on the Libyan people in Chad, 
where the Chadian Government sought the help of French forces against the Chadian 
liberation front. He said we avoided this confrontation as well. 


The situation with regard to Chad was different. For the Chadian liberation front, 
which we supported against the government and which the French opposed, has now come to 
power in Ndjamena. The fascist government in Chad has collapsed and the French troops 
have actually left Chad. Thus, we avoided confrontation with France in Chad on several 
occasions thanks to rational actions on both sides. 


By this attitude of ours, he said, we have saved our friendship with France. Our 
relations with France are now at their best and even we can go as far as to say that 
our relations with France now are better than our relations with any other European 
country. 


Al-Qadhdhafi added: The ruling side in Chad now is one which supports us. For Goukouni, 
the chairman of the Chadian liberation front, is now the legitimate ruler of Chad. 
Al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that if the Libyan forces were to enter Chad now, their entry 
would be different from the previous times. For their entry this time will be in 
accordance with the treaty concluded between us and Chad and which is not in force, and 
copies of which have been placed with the United Nations and the OAU. They will not 

be entering with the intent of waging war but with the intent of establishing peace.
