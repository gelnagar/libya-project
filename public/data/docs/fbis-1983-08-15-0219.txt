LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi’s U.S. Television Interview 
LD132148 Tripoli JANA in English 1955 GMT 13 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulgada 5, August 13, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Millions of viewers in America yesterday 
followed the interview given by the leader to American television 
stations ABC, NBC, CBS and CNN which was transmitted live 
via satellite. 


During the interview, the leader of the revolution answered 
questions on the current situation in Chad and direct French, 
American, Zairean interventions in the internal conflict in that 
country as well as U.S. provocations against Libya launched from 
its northern and southern borders. 


In his answer to a question by ABC concerning allegations of 
Libyan intervention in Chad, the leader said that the Jama- 
hiriyah did not intervene at all in Chad and remains neutral 
vis-a-vis the civil war there. In this respect the leader expressed 
regret at the involvement of parties such as America, France and 
Zaire in this war. The leader of the revolution also refuted 
allegations claiming that French and U.S. spying equipment 
showed Libyan military activities in the region and said that 
Jamahiriyah has no aerial or land military activities there 
because its borders with Chad have been closed for a long time. 
He said no one has any proof of our intervention in the war there 
and stressed that Jamahiriyah has suggested to the O.A.U. the 
formation of a fact-finding committee to be sent to Faya-Largeau 
and other parts of Chad so as to make sure that we did not make 
any military moves in Chad. 


In his answer to a question whether Jamahiriyah supports Gou- 
kouni Oueddei’s forces to help them overthrow Habre, the leader 
said that the Jamahiriyah had in the past backed Goukouni, the 
legitimate president of Chad, and that it has stopped this backing 
a long time ago. He said “Goukouni does not need our help 
because he is strong enough and has his people on his side. He 
also possesses enough weapons from the past and does not need 
any help from Libya or any other country. | believe that he will 
win this civil war and will not need any other help because he 
represents the majority in Chad while Habre represents a minor- 
ity. Moreover, Goukouni was the president of Chad and Habre 
was his defence minister. Habre is the rebel.” 


In his answer (o a question concerning the critical situation in 
Chad the leader said “We want peace in Chad which will be 
achieved through national reconciliation between the warring 
factions and want this war to end as soon as possible. America, 
Zaire and France are trying to escalate this civil war. So instead 
of giving this people food they supply it with arms to fuel the civil 
war on a wider scale. We regret such mad actions perpetrated by 
a superpower such as America. [quote marks as received] 


Concerning rumours being circulated in the west of alleged 
Libyan attempt to spread its influence in Chad the leader said, 
“We are not looking for influence in any part of the world. This 
is part of the policy of imperialists who are trying to dominate 
and spread their influence over other parts of the world.” The

leader also expressed regret concerning Reagan's statements in 
which he said that Chad does not fall under the U.S. zones of 
influence but is part of France's zone of influence. He said “this 
is the language of imperialism and old-fashioned colonialism. 
Chad belongs to the Chadians, Africa to the Africans as much 
as Libya belongs to the Libyans. It is strange to hear the president 
of a superpower say such things about Chad. This means that the 
world is divided among imperialist countries and that no one is 
free. It means that Chad is not free and that what is taking place 
there is an imperialist issue concerning imperialist countries such 
as America and France. We are totally opposed to this. Chad is 
not part of the zones of influence of any other country but belongs 
to the Chadians.” 


In his answer to a question regarding possible confrontation 
between Libyan forces on the one hand and American and 
French forces on the other the leader said “this will not happen 
because we have no intention whatsoever to intervene in Chad. 
Moreover, we have no forces deployed along Chadian borders. 
We are still within our own borders and do not fear any confron- 
tation between us and any other party. We consider that what is 
taking place in Chad is a civil war and a struggie for power 
between Goukouni and Habre. We are joining our efforts to those 
of the Organisation of African Unity to find a peaceful solution 
to the problem and see that there is no need to use military force 
to solve this problem.” 


In his answer to a question by ABC station about Arab leaders’ 
stands vis-a-vis Libyan complaints about U.S. provocations 
against it, the leader said that he receives daily cables and 
telephone calls from Arab leaders which speak of their support 
to the Jamahiriyah against U.S. aggression from Chad and from 
the Mediterranean. 


Concerning Mobutu, Numayri and Egypt’s stands, the leader 
explained that these are a minority in Africa and are agents of 
America. The majority however supports Libya. We have made 
public the support we received from African leaders and coun- 
tries. Numayri and Mobutu are mere puppets, he added. 


In his answer to a question related to the presence of US. aircraft 
carrier “Eisenhower” across Libyan territorial waters and Lib- 
ya’s warning that it will attack the carrier if this penetrates 
Libyan territorial waters the leader said: ‘“We hope that this 
will not happen. We are trying to avoid confrontation with 
America or others but we are ready to defend our lands. We say 
that the Gulf of Sidra is part of Libya’s territorial waters and 
declare our readiness to fight for the sake of this gulf because the 
issue is related to the security of our territory and our country. 
We will not allow any war target to penetrate this gulf whether 
this target is U.S. aircraft carrier “Eisenhower” or any other 
warship belonging to any other country.” 


In his answer to a question related to the presence of U.S. spying 
aircraft and their effect on Libya, the leader said, “If this aircraft 
is deployed against Libya and affects it, we will try to destroy it 
because it is a matter of self-defence.” 


In his answer to a question concerning Jamahiriyah relations 
with its neighbours the leader said we are busy constructing the 
Arab Maghreb which will unite Libya, Tunisia, Algeria,

Morocco and Mauritania as a first step towards realising Arab 
unity. We are also aspiring towards establishing good relations 
with our African neighbours and would like peace and stability 
in Chad. We see that America and other imperialist countries 
must leave Africa because they are trying to undermine the 
stability of this part of the world. 


Regarding Jamahiriyah relations with the Soviet Union, the 
leader said “We would like to be the friends of American and 
any other country in the world and would like to bolster our 
relations with the Soviet Union especially in the light of this 
aggressive atmosphere against us created by America.” 


Concerning (?what) was circulated about peaceful initiatives 
with France to end fighting in Chad the leader answered CBS 
television station's question by saying “relations between Libya 
and France are good and there are continued contacts between 
the two countries. We hope that France helps to achieve some- 
thing in Ndjamena because no one can realise peace while 
preserving Ndjamena for himself and excluding his other com- 
rades from power. In order to realise peace in Chad Habre should 
leave Ndjamena or Ndjamena should be ruled with the help of 
other parties. French forces tried to do something last week but 
failed. The number of French forces in Ndjamena increased and 
it was believed that these would achieve something.” 


In his answer to a question related to the existence of reports 
which say that America and France could hold joint manoeuvres 
in the region using AWACS and French forces the leader said, 
“We are ready to defend our country. We are not worried 
because of these manoeuvres there or anywhere else. like the rest 
of the world we have condemned these manoeuvres in this region 
because they are a threat to world peace and security in this 
region. In any case we are not frightened and are ready to defend 
our coutnry.” 


Meets With Conference Members 
LD131045 Tripoli JANA in English 0920 GMT 13 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulgada 5, August 13, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The leader of the revolution met with the 
members of the preparatory conference of the sixth gathering of 
the revolutionary committees which ended its meetings last night 
at the people's hall in Tripoli. 


During their meeting the leader stressed the necessity and impor- 
tance of building the revolutionary committees movement at 
home and abroad and said that the mission of the sixth gathering 
must concentrate on means to create a revolutionary movement 
at home and abroad as this is the most important slogan which 
should be raised during this meeting. 


He stressed the necessity for establishing a revolutionary move- 
ment at home and abroad which believes in the revolutionary 
dicta and which would disseminate these abroad, stressing that 
Libya would have been nothing without a revolutionary move- 
ment and that it will make all the achievements realised available 
to the Libyans and to the Arab nation.
