LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Palestinian Ahmad Jibril 
LD090626 Tripoli JAN A in English 1752 GMT 8 Sep 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhuljiyah 1, Sep 8, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY The revolution’s leader, Colonel Mu'ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi, has met with Ahmad Jibril, secretary-general of the 
Popuiar Front for the Liberation of Palestine General Command, 
who took part in the Libyan people's celebration marking the 
14th anniversary of the Great Al-Fatih Revolution
