LIBYA

OFFICIAL SAYS TURKEY MUST SUPPORT PALESTINIANS 


TA280633Y Ankara Domestic Service in Turkish 2000 GMT 27 Jul 78 TA 


[Text] The chairman of the Libyan People's Councils, (Mabruk Misallati), has said that 
the Palestinian question is a common issue to all Moslem countries and that Turkey must 
also support the Palestinian liberation struggle. 


The series of meetings held in Tripoli, Libya, with the participation of some of the 
Turkish journalists visiting Libya ended today. our correspondent Erdogan Gungordu 
reports from Tripoli that at the meeting today the Libyan officials said that Libya 
will always help Turkey in connection with Cyprus and that Turkey and Libya aim at 
forming a joint Mediterranean civilization.
