LIBYA, LEBANESE WATIONAL MOVEMENT ISSUE JOINT STATEMENT 


LD262233 Tripoli JANA in English 0930 @mT 26 Jan 80 LD 


[Text] Benghazi, Jan 26, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--A delegation representing the 
Lebanese patriotic movement left Benghazi yesterday after ending a visit to the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, 


Present at the delegation's send-off at Benina airport were the secretary for local 
congress affairs at the General People's Congress and a representative of the Foreign 
Secretariat's Protocol Department. 


The secretary general of the movement's central political council and head of the 
delegation made a statement to our correspondent in which he confirmed that the visit 
achieved positive results in strengthening bilateral relations between the Lebanese 
patriotic movement and the grand (?Al-Fatah) revolution, and in coordinating efforts 
for unified patriotic confrontation of capitulationist policies, 


The delegation stressed the need for supporting the Lebanese people's struggle under 
the leadership of the patriotic movement to ascertain Lebanon's unity and Arab 
character and to protect the Palestinian revolution, 


The delegation affirmed its solidarity with the Libyan Arab people in their struggle 
for confronting imperialist and Zionist conspiracies implemented by the defeated 
Egyptian president, and expressed belief in the unity of patriotic and progressive 
Arab forces as the best means of foiling all conspiracies. 


After the end of the visit, the following statement was issued: 


A delegation representing the Lebanese patriotic movement's leadership paid a visit to 
the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah between 20-25 Jarmary 1980, 


The delegation held several meetings with the grand Al-Fatah revolution's leadership 
and the General People's Congress' Foreign Liaison Bureau, dealing with basic Arab 
problems amd the current situation in Lebanon, 


The talks resulted in agreement by both sides of the need for total Arab confrontation 
of the capitulationist stance adopted by the defeated Egyptian president and has 
resulted in the normalisation of relations with (Israel) and the establishment of an 
Egyptian-(Israeli)-American military alliance which threatens the Arab nation's future 
existence, 


The two sides reaffirmed their belief that Palestine was and still remains the Arab 
nation's core issue and the cause for confrontation against the imperialist-Zionist- 
reactionary camp. 


The two sides affirmed that the reinforcement of cunfrontation against treacherous 
capitulatory policies calls for the support of the Palestinian revolution so that 
it can continue its armed struggle for the complete liberation of Palestinian soil. 
Thus, the following should be taken into consideration:

{Text] The delegation of the national progressive movement of Lebanon left Benghazi 
this morning at the end of a visit of several days to the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah. The secretary general of the central Political Bureau of the 
movement and head of the delegation made a statement to a JANA correspondent in which 
he said that the visit has achieved important positive results in the field of con- 
solidating the relations of struggle between the national progressive movement of 
Lebanon and the Great 1 September Revolution as well as in the field of coordinating 
stand within the framework of the pan-Arab joint confrortation of the capitulationist 
course, 


He stressed the importance and urgency of bolstering the struggle of the Lebanese 
people led by their national movement for the sake of protecting Lebanon's unity and 
Arabism and protecting also the positions of the Palestine revolution, 


He also stressed his solidarity of struggle with the Libyan Arab people in the face 
of the imperialist-Zionist plots which are being implemented by the vanquished 
Egyptian president. He pointed out that the unity of the national Arab progressive 
forces is capable of thwarting all plots and designs.
