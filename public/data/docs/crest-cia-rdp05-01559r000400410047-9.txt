Summary (AI): A June 1988 FBIS transcript of Qadhdhafi's news conference in Algiers, covering the Maghreb summit, the Gulf of Sidra, US policy and a proposed Arab federation. It has nothing about places in or near Tripoli.
• The news conference was held in Algiers on 10 June 1988 for foreign correspondents and was broadcast by Algiers Television.
• Qadhdhafi said the Arab summit affirmed Libya's sovereignty over the Gulf of Sidra.
• Qadhdhafi proposed a political Arab federation with a presidential council of Arab rulers, and said it would be raised on 1 September 1989.
• Qadhdhafi described the summit as condemning US aggression against Libya and organised state terrorism.
