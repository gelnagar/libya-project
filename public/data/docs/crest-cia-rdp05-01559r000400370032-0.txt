Summary (AI): Libyan broadcast (ARNA, via FBIS London) reports Colonel Qadhdhafi announced that all persons arrested during the 1969 September First Revolution have been released. He said the revolution was not aimed at any person but at liquidating American and British bases in Libya.
• Qadhdhafi said the 1969 coup's real aim was to liquidate American and British bases in the country
• Announcement made in the General People's Congress, per ARNA report datelined Tripoli 19 January 1976
• 1976-01-18: Qadhdhafi announced in his final address to the General People's Congress that all those arrested during the staging of the September First Revolution had been released
