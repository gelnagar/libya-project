Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a 24-25 September 1987 interview with Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, published in the Gulf paper Al-Khalij, covering the Iran-Iraq war, a proposed pan-Arab union, Libya's relations with Egypt, Algeria, Jordan and Chad, and the Libyan pilots who defected to Egypt. It mentions the 1986 U.S. raid only in passing, and no Tripoli sites are named.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya had sent a memorandum to Iranian leaders and that Iran was ready to end the Iran-Iraq war if the aggressor was identified and compensated.
• Qadhdhafi proposed a pan-Arab union with rotating presidency every six months within a presidential council.
• Libya and Algeria were said to be preparing a federal state constitution to be declared in November 1987.
• Qadhdhafi said Libyan pilots who defected to Egypt would face the death penalty for treason, and that Egyptian spies had guided U.S. aircraft in the raids on Tripoli and Benghazi.
• Qadhdhafi said an emergency Arab summit had not been held after the European-U.S. aggression against Libya.

sionist intentions in the Gulf became clear and because 
he was a basic regional ally of Israel, as well as an 
American agent. We were the only Arab country that the

How can we fight a revolution which is directed against
