• Libyan preparations for possible hostile actions were observed at Kufra and Benina airfields and at Al Kufrah Barracks.
• Egyptian reaction observed at Aswan airfield, with F-4 Phantom fighters arriving.
• The document is marked as a briefing note prepared by the National Photographic Interpretation Center, with coordinates not available.
• Most of the document text is redacted (sanitized copy).
• 1983-02: Third Blinder medium-bomber deployed to Kufra airfield, probably from Umm Aitiqah airfield, amid Libyan preparations for possible hostile actions against Sudan.
• 1983-02: Camouflage-painted C-130 transport arrived at Benina airfield with vehicles indicating loading or unloading in progress.
