Summary (AI): This is a May 1983 collection of Libyan state radio items. Students in Misratah protested the Lebanese-Israeli agreement, and Tripoli radio denounced the deal and US policy toward Syria. Qadhdhafi reportedly threatened to sever relations with Lebanon if it signed the draft.
• Tripoli Voice of the Greater Arab Homeland broadcast the items on 10 May 1983.
• Radio Monte Carlo reported Qadhdhafi's statements on 1 May 1983 (dateline Paris, 1400 GMT).
• Broadcasts criticised the Lebanese-Israeli agreement as more humiliating than Camp David and urged escalating the popular war in Lebanon.
• Israeli Chief of Staff Moshe Levi reportedly said war might break out with Syria if Syria did not accept the agreement.
• 1983-05-10: Students of Misratah municipality staged a demonstration protesting the Lebanese-Israeli agreement and the alleged CIA-assigned Palestinian assassination plot against Qadhdhafi.
• 1983-05-01: Qadhdhafi told about 20 foreign reporters he had urgently summoned to Tripoli that he would sever relations with Lebanon if it signed the Lebanese-Israeli draft agreement.
