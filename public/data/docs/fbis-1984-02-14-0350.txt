LIBYA

JANA Scores Mitterrand Statements on Chad 
LD140017 Tripoli JANA in English 1835 GMT 13 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Mumada Al Ulla 10, Feb 13, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY French President Mitterrand was inter- 
viewed by French television channel one yesterday evening. 
During the interview he made false allegations regarding a 
Libyan presence in Chad. He also defended the French presence 
in Chad by alleging that Libya was the one who started the 
occupation of Chad. The French president said he is responsible 
for the French presence in the world, alleging that the French 
Government responded to a general call by black Africa to stand 
beside Habre. 


Commenting on the statements of French President Mitterand, 
the political editor of the JAMAHIRIYA NEWS AGENCY 
said the French president is trying in vain to affirm his allegations 
regarding a Libyan presence in Chad. He is taking this as an 
excuse to maintain French forces there so that they may com- 
pletely side with the renegade clique in Ndjamena, this through 
his insistance on repeating the adage that the bigger the lie, the 
easier it is believed. 


Such a method of conduct is easily discovered, as there are no 
indications of truth to Mitterrand’s allegations whatsoever. Mit- 
terrand seems to have hoped through his statements that we 
might start a slinging match with him as part of a policy of 
adventurism in a bid to black out the truth from public opinion. 
This truth affirms that the French forces are not peace-keeping 
forces and did not come to Chad for training, but have become a 
party in the war between Chadian factions. 


President Mitterrand has shrouded matters with a deep mist to 
black out this truth in an attempt to keep the struggle between 
Chadian factions going, and to continue subjecting the Chadian 
people to the dangers and horrors of civil war. From this precept, 
Mitterrand has affirmed the unchanging manifestation of 
French colonialist policy, which calls for hegemony, occupation 
and expansion. We were hoping that the socialists would change 
the course of this policy that has brought loss and catastrophe te 
the French people, so that these people would be spared further 
catastrophies.

It is essential to take into consideration the Chadian people's 
desire for security, stability and unity, together with the absence 
of colonialist forces from their territories. But President Mitter- 
rand dashed these hopes by adopting the colonialist pelicy which 
was practiced by war-mongering generals. He thought to use 
allegations that are baseless to justify the military presence in 
Chad. He even added to that by defending the allegation of the 
fascist regime in Sudan and the regime of Husni Mubarak in 
Cairo, thus covering up the true character of the French military 
presence which has in fact become a presence that is opposed to 
the desires and ambitions of the Chadian people. 


The Mitterrand government has attempted in the past to avoid 
involvement in the war between Chadian factions, but now, 
through President Mitterrand’s statement, it cannot but be 
directly involved not only in the civil war but also in placing 
obstacles before the Organisation of African Unity (OAV) so as 
to prevent it from carrying out its duty of realizing peace and 
security in Chad. 


France is now playing with fire. This is not an easy game, and 
France must realize the meaning of playing with fire in territories 
that are not hers. For France has no business in Chad and is not 
linked to it by any security interests. Her involvement in Chad 
has repercussions and dangers. France must, here, realize that 
the Chadian war has its price. For if a war breaks out, it will be 
a long one and its losses and results will be drastic. France has 
tried this and the French people have experienced the horrors of 
such a war in Algeria, some African countries and in Vietnam 
too. 


The war which France is taking in Chad will be much more 
dangerous and horrific. Even if this does not happen in our days, 
or even months later, at sometime when France must face the 
dangers of military involvement it will find that the land on which 
it is standing will have exploded, not only in Chad but in the whole 
of Africa. 


The French president is overtly seeking to evade the true posture 
with regard to the justification of the French military presence 
in Chad. He has used false allegations and sought to give the 
picture of France as a cosmetic uplift. He has further sought to 
use French prestige as a cover for (?entrenching) French troops 
in the land of others, thus ignoring historical facts which have 
affirmed that the Chadian people had never known stability 
without war until the arrival of Libyan forces [as received] several 
years ago in response to the request by the true legitimate 
government headed by Mr Oueddei. 


These facts will show that this was the first initiative realized by 
the Jamahiriya in bringing peace to Chad. The Jamahiriyah did 
nothing more than that, but France and its covert intentions 
dashed the Chadian people’s big dream for freedom and hindered 
the highly important humanitarian effort exerted by Libya to 
avert the horrors of civil war in Chad. For it is the Chadians who 
fell in the battlefield, and what is now being witnessed in Chad 
is the responsibility of colonialist France. Furthermore, France 
is now totally allied to a renegade faction led by Habre who does 
not want to step down from power, rejects peace and declines any 
negotiation. By supporting and siding with this renegade, France 
has undermined the resolution of the Lagos summit conference

and aborted the role of the OAU in realizing peace and security. 


France can no longer allege that it wants peace in Chad. Such an 
allegation has become a method of personnel propaganda and 
therefore is rejected. Through his statement, the French presi- 
dent has shown French presence in Chad has become a source of 
tension in the region and is linked to the expansionist, colonialist 
policies and designs adopted now in Chad by the government of 
President Mitterrand. 


People’s Congress Head Addresses Ninth Session 
LD112009 Tripoli JANA in English 1438 GMT 11 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada al Ulla 8, February 11, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY The General People’s Congress (GPC) 
today started the proceedings of its ninth ordinary session at the 
People’s Hall in Tripoli to formulate the resolutions passed by 
the Basic People’s Congresses at their third ordinary session in 
1983. 


The GPC’s secretary general, Mr Muhammad az-Zarrug Rajab, 
opened the congress’s proceedings with a speech in which he 
welcomed the Jamahiriyah’s guests from fraternal and friendly 
countries who have come to participate with the Libyan Arab 
people in affirming the entrenchment of people’s authority. 


Mr Rajab also welcomed other countries’ representatives and 
ambassadors in the Jamahiriyah who had attended the inaugural 
session of the congress, expressing hope that they may prove to 
be a satisfactory link between the Jamahiriyah’s people and their 
peoples, and to convey sincerely to their people what they witness 
on the land of the Al-Fatih as to the victory of people’s authority. 


Mr Rajab also conveyed the congress’s greetings and total appre- 
ciation for the leader-teacher and revolutionary-intellectual 
Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi and his free comrades who 
sacrificed themselves to the people to rescue them from the claws 
of cruelty, oppression and ignorance and to arrive with them at 
the dawn of freedom, the dawn of authority of the people who 
wield authority, wealth and arms. 


On the other hand, the congress will formulate the resolutions 
passed by the masses of the Basic People’s Congresses during 
their third ordinary session last year. These include several 
important local, Arab and international issues 


The GPC’s secretary, Mr Muhammad az-Zarrug Rajab 
added: ‘1983 has passed. It was a year full of popular accom- 
plishments, for in 1983, telex machines and the world media 
buzzed with a report that worried reactionary forces but was 
hailed by the hearts of millions of people seeking freedom... This 
report was that units of the Libyan Arab Air Force had crossed 
thousands of miles over the oceans carrying aid for the Nicara- 
guan liberation movement, in implementation of the People’s 
Congresses resolution calling for support to be extended to 
liberation movements.” 


He said: “The world’s helpless peoples saluted Mu‘ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi and the Jamahiriyah’s people for this heroic stance
