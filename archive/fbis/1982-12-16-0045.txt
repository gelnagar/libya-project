LIBYA

ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS BEGINS TRIPOLI MEETING 


LD152252 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 15 Dec 82 


[Text] The fourth session of the Permament Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 
began its meetings in Tripoli this evening. The meetings are to evaluate the activities 
of the Secretariat and to draw up a strategic plan of action for the coming year. 


At the beginning of the meeting, Secretary General ‘Umar al-Hamidi reviewed developments 
in the political arena and in the Arab liberation movement, and defined the program 

of struggle for the coming stage. He also reviewed the current political situation 

in the Arab homeland, and the responsibilities of the liberation movements in bolstering 
the national struggle and in checking the sweeping Zionist-imperialist offensive 
witnessed by the Arab region under the umbrella of the treason, submission, and 
capitulation of the reactionary regimes in the Arab homeland. These reactionary 

regimes plot against the Arab cause. 


He spoke of the need for the meeting to evaluate the positive aspects and denounce 
the negative aspects of the events that took place during the barbaric Zionist 
invasion of Lebanon. A number of members of the permanent secretariat also spoke 
during the meeting. They denounced the defeatist stands of the reactionary regimes 
in the Arab homeland.

UGANDAN MUSLIMS APPEAL TO AL-QADHDHAFI FOR AID 


LD101120 Tripoli JANA in English 0907 GMT 10 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Safar 24, Dec 10, Jamahiriyah News Ageucy -- The society of Ugandan 
Muslims for social affairs and refugee welfare has appealed to the revolution's leader 
to offer aid and assistance to the Muslims of Uganda to enable them avoid the daily 
life difficulties, stressing that the Great Al Fatah Revolution is principled on 
supporting Islam and Muslims and it will be in the front in dealing with the Ugandan 
Muslims problems so that they can take their role in spreading Islam. 


In a message he sent to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, the society's president, Khamis Mayenba 
expressed his appreciation and that of the Ugandan Muslims and all the Muslims of the 
world for the militant role of the Great Al Fatah Revolution in supporting and defend- 
ing the causes of Muslims. 


He denounced the stands of certain countries which, under the pretext of Islam's slogans, 
ignore the real issues of the Muslims by offering support for anti~Arabism and Islan, 
while the Ugandan Muslims are suffering very harsh living conditions. He added, "We 

see rich Islamic countries deny us aid and assistance and give generously te countries 
which fight Islam and opposes Muslims."

YEMENI MINISTER LEAVES TRIPOLI -- Tripoli, Safar 29, Dec 15, Jamahiriyah News Agency 
-- Mr. Rashid Muhammad Thabit, member of the Central Committee and minister of culture 
and tourism in the Democratic Yemen Republic left Tripoli this evening. He told 

our correspondent he is satisfied with the fruitful and good results of his visit 

and said practical joint steps have been taken in the field of education, culture 
information and sports. He hoped more contacts and coordination will take place 
between both countries. Mr. Thabi concluded by acclaiming the huge achievements of 
the Libyan people through the Great al-Fatih Revolution. [Text] [Tripoli JANA in 
English 1855 GMT 15 Dec 82 LD]
