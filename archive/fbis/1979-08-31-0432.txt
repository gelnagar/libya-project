LIBYA

JANA REPORTS OPENING OF GENERAL PEOPLE'S CONGRESS 
LD301632 Tripoli JANA in English 1412 GMT 30 Aug 79 LD 
[Text] Tripoli, Aug 30, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Muhammad Abu Qassim az-Zawawi, 
assistant secretary-general of the General People's Congress attended the inaugural 


session of the Tripoli conference on the imperialist-Zionist-reactionary schemes in 
the Arab world which started in Tripoli vesterday.

Az-Zawawi delivered a speech thanking all the parties and organizations taking part in the 
conference. 


He said: "It is an honour that this conference is held in the Jamahiriyah on the eve of the 
tenth anniversary of the Great First of September Revolution", He added: "The number of 
members attending the conference inspires our confidence that the honourable struggle against 
imperialism, reaction and oppression will achieve decisive and final victory very soon. 

The number of freedom fighters is on the increase every day.while the oppressors are getting 
weaker and weaker." He affirmed that if the era is that of the militant people it is also 
bound to be that of the victory of democracy. 


The reality was that the era of the masses was born two years ago. Mr az-Zawawi said: "We 
in the Jamahiriyah are happy to meet freedom fighters from all over the world, because we 
strongly believed that the cause of freedom is indivisible and its victory in the Jamahiriyah 
was oneof for freedom throughout the world." 


Mr azeZawawi affirmed thatthe downfall of agent Somoza, and the overthrow of the shah were 
victories for all honourable freedom fighters. 


He said that the generation which experienced the popular uprising against oppression and 
imperialism came to realize that the victory against imperialism and its tools was no easy 
task. Therefore we should arm ourselves with education, awareness, caution and effective 
solidarity. 


He added that we should unite our efforts to strike at imperialism, because the fall of every 
imperialist fortress is a victory for freedom throughout the world. Mr az-Zawawi addressed 

the members of the conference and said: "The Jamahiriyah's people believed that the subject 

of this conference is one which concerns all honourable peoples throughout the world. Palestine 
was the victim of the reactionary-Zionist-imperialist aggression, where all evil forces have 
united their efforts against the Palestinian people and expelled them. 


Mr az-Zawawi condemned the U.S, imperialist and reactionary forces for their help and support 
of Zionism, for this support helped to plant this racist entity in the heart of the Arab 
homeland. He said that the Zionist entity was hostile, illogical and like a knife tearing the 
Arab nation apart and restricting its role in support of freedom throughout the world. 


Mr az-Zawawi called upon the participants to study, debate and analyse the various aspects of 
the reactionary-Zionist-imperialist plots. He also called upon all honourable freedom fighters 
to join efforts to liberate this part of the world. 


Mr az-Zawawi pointed out that the Jamahiriyah was subjected to various kinds of plots and 
provocations as it endeavoured to defend freedom in the world. He said that all freedom 
fighters found themselves in one trench defending the soil on which the revolution was 
erupting to defend freedom, humanity and progress throughout the world. 


He said that the victory achieved by the Great First of September Revolution against the most 
oppressive and reactionary regime in the region was in fact that of all freedom fighters and 
that this revolution would continue to support all oppressed people until they acquire their 
freedom throughout the world.

Mr az-Zawawi said that the Great First of September Revolution realized and believed in 
these facts: "Since the time when brother Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi used to lead us in the 
schools to organize and prepare us to confront imperialism. 


Mr Omar Hamdi [not further indentified] opened the conference and delivered a speech in 
the name of the international preparatory secretariat of the international conference 
in solidarity with the Arab people and their central cause: Palestine, which will be 
held in Portugal between the 2nd and 6th of November. 


He affirmed that this conference has a special importance considering the issues to be 
discussed and the circumstances and place in which it is held. 


He said that these three dimensions make of this conference an important political event 
and create an opportunity for the representatives of millions of peoples of the world to 
express their views and work jointly to stand against these schemes which aim not only 

against the security of the Arab nation but also against the security of the whole world. 


Hamdi strongly condemned the so-called 'Camp David! accords, the Washington treaty and 
the self-rule conspiracy. He said: "It is an attempt to abolish the Palestinain 
character for it considered the Palestinians as mere refugees. He said that this was a 
ridiculous crime against a peaceful but militant people. 


Mr Hamdi submitted to the conference the draft working papers endorsed two days ago by 
the preliminary international secretariat. The draft includes: 


Firstly: The working paper to be submitted to the international conference in Portugal 
to be distributed to all the delegations invited to the next conference in Portugal to 
enable them to study the paper and its contents. 


Secondly: The Tripoli draft declaration of solidarity with the people fighting against 
the plans of imperialism, Zionism, racism and fascism. 


Thirdly: A draft message of support and solidarity with the people and leadership of 
the Jamahiriyah on the tenth anniversary of the Great First of September Revolution, 


Fourthly: A draft message to be addressed to the non-aligned countries in Havana. 


Following Mr az-Zawawi's speech, the Jamahiriyah delegation addressed the conference 
condemning the American-Zionist-Sadatist alliance. The delegation affirmed the dangerous 
deteriorating situation in the Arab region and added that the continuation of occupation 
in Palestine, the expulsion and oppression of its people, occupation of Arab lands and 
disturbance of normal conditions there, the occupation of Arab lands and disturbance of 
normal conditions there, the occupation of south Lebanon and the creation of sectarian 
entities on its border represented a daily threat to world peace and a flagrant aggression 
against human rights as defined in the charter of the United Nations. 


The delegation stressed that such attitudes reinformed the continued presence and 
policing of imperialist interests in the region.

They said that the only solution to the Palestinian cause should be that proposed by 
Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi in his message to Carter--to put an end to the migration 
to Palestine, the return of the settlers to their original countries, a halt to 
armament of the Middle East except of the occupied countries and the establishment of 
a democratic Palestinian state for the Palestinian and Jewish Arabs. He added that 
these proposals were bound to establish peace in Palestine. 


He affirmed that the establishment of the Jamahiriyah societies, "massdoms," facilitates 
the solution of the world's problems because of its principles of integration and 
equality. 


The member of the Jamahiriyah delegation stressed that socialism should be the philosophy 
of life in the world for it destroys the unjust laws which govern relations at inter- 
national levels. Through this philosophy just laws will be established. 


Then the delegations started to deliver their speeches. The secretary of the Palestine 
Liberation Organization delivered his in the morning session, representing the world 
liberation front. The representative of South Africa liberation front, representing 
Africa, the Indian representative, representing Asia while the Cuban representative 
representing Latin America also delivered their speeches. The representative of the 
international workers union gave a speech in the morning session. 


All delegations have concentrated on the importance of the struggle and cooperation 
against the imperialist, Zionist and reactionary schemes. 


The delegations praised the Jamahiriyah's role in helping, supporting and standing by 
the people in their struggle to achieve freedom and to defeat oppression and slavery. 


The conference resumed its meetings this evening with more speeches by the delegations. 
The morning session was attended by delegations of friendly countries. Among the 
attendants were the Guinean foreign minister and the chairman of the Central Committee 
of the revolutionary party of Benin as well as many ambassadors of progressive countries 
to the Jamahiriyah.
