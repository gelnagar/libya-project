Summary (AI): A 1975 CIA assessment, heavily garbled by OCR, says Qadhafi is adapting his foreign policy to Middle East realities after policy setbacks, with Foreign Minister al-Huni given more room to pursue a moderate approach. It discusses Libya's economic problems, the need for Egyptian reconciliation, Maghreb ties, and Libya's interest in improved US relations.
• Libya's oil production levels were low, and its expenditures and financial commitments were rising.
• Libya had a growing need for skilled and unskilled workers and technical experts that Egyptians were not fully meeting.
• Libyan media and officials had recently taken a more conciliatory tone toward Morocco and toward Egypt.
• Qadhafi's desire for better US-Libyan relations was linked to fear of a US invasion and a wish for international respectability.
• Members of the Arab Socialist Union reportedly grumbled about Qadhafi's handling of foreign affairs.
