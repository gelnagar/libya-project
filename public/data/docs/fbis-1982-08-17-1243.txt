Al-Qadhdhafi Proposal Praised 
LD162210 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 16 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 Aug (JANA) -- Staff Brig Gen 'Ali ‘'Antar, member of the Political 
Bureau, first deputy chairman of the Council of Ministers and minister of local gowern- 
ment of the PDRY, left Tripoli today. He was seen off by Brig Gen Abu Bakr Junis Jabir, 
commander in chief of the armed forces. 


Before his departure from the Jamahiriyah, Staff Brig Gen ‘Ali 'Antar made a statement 
to a JANA correspondent, in which he praised the proposal made by the brother leader of 
the revolution to Arab kings and presidents for the dispatch of 10 military divisions 

to Syria, supported by aircraft squadrons, to expel the enemy. He said this proposal 
has our great appreciation and deserves every welcome from us because it stems from the 
pan-Arab duty of the Great 1 September Revolution toward the fateful issues of our Arab 
nation andthe challenges imposed on it. He added that it is our duty to utilize all 

our resources and potential for unified Arab action directed against the enemy and those 
who are backing it with tools of destruction for the annihilation of both the Palestinian 
and the Lebanese people and for the dispersal of thousands of our people in steadfast 
Beirut. 


In his statement, he underlined the fact that by presenting this pan-Arab initiative, 
the Jamahiriyah constantly is asserting its eagerness for fighting in solidarity and 
for strengthening the anti-imperialist, anti-Zionist and antireaction struggle to 
firmly establish the Arab right to retrieve the usurped territories and to establish 
the legitimate right of the Palestinian people to self-determination and the building 
of an independent state under PLO leadership. He said: I would like to confirm 

the PDRY's full readiness for effective participation in every joint action to con- 
front Zionist aggression, which is backed by America, and to foil it. 

Speaking about his visit to the Jamahiriyah, he pointed out that the visit has pro- 
duced positive results. I have conveyed to Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi a 
message from the presidents of the two Yemens and I have told him that the PDRY 
welcomes the proposal he has presented for the expulsion of the Zionist enemy and 

for foiling its capitulatory schemes and the aggressive designs the U.S. administration 
is concocting against the Arab nation.
