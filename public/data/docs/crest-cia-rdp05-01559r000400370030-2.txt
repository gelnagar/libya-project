• He said demonstrations, strikes and sit-ins become unjustified where discussion is possible.

• The broadcast was from Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland, 15 January 1976, 1915 GMT.
• 1976-01-15: Qadhdhafi, speaking at a General Popular Congress session, said deviation from unions or associations would be resisted by force and without mercy, and that politics outside the Basic Popular Congresses would be suppressed.
