Summary (AI): A Washington Post article from December 9, 1981 reports that the Reagan administration was planning economic sanctions against Libya and debating their scope, alongside reports of an alleged Qaddafi-ordered assassination plot against U.S. officials. The piece covers Libyan oil imports, US trade with Libya, and Libya's counter-claims about US efforts in Europe.
• The National Security Council met for a second consecutive day on the Libya sanctions question without reaching a conclusion.
• State Department estimated US purchases from Libya at about $6-7 billion for 1981 and US sales to Libya at more than $700 million.
• Libya's JANA news agency said the CIA had sent NATO a secret report urging West European countries to counter Libyan policy.
• Alleged hit teams were said to be two five-member teams that may have entered the United States.
• Sen. Hart's resolution was withdrawn after Baker persuaded him that the White House needed more time.
