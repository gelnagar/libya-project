LIBYA

Tehran Paper Interview with 
GF141646 Tehran IRNA in English 1640 GMT 14 Jun 83 


[Text] Tehran, June 14, IRNA — Libyan leader Mu’ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi said in an interview published Tuesday that a 
revolutionary and Islamic front should be formed in order to 
stand against imperialism and Zionism. 


In an interview with the daily Persian Kayhan, Al-Qadhdhafi 
said revolutionary Muslim nations could not continue their 
struggle against their enemies without unity. 


He said it was clear that the Iraqi-imposed war was the outcome 
of collaboration among the reactionary countries in the region 
with Saddam Husayn aimed at destroying the Islamic revolution. 


Saddam imposed the war upon Iran on behalf of the U.S. and 
Israel because Israel and the US. intend to destroy the Islamic 
revolution, and passed this mission to Saddam and the reaction- 
ary Arab states, he was quoted as saying. 


Al-Qadhdhafi added however that a country such as Iraq should 
have supported the Islamic revolution of Iran, Syria, and nation- 
alist Lebanese, Palestinian and Libyan forces and create a rev- 
olutionary front against imperialism and Zionism but 
unfortunately it stepped into this war and is suffering irreparable 
damages. 


He said that the Islamic revolution had revived the spirit of Islam 
in young Muslims and restored the glory of Islam in the world. 


The hope of freedom-keeping Muslims in the leadership of the 
Islamic revolution and the results of this revolution will be felt 
among the Arab and Palestinian people...you (the Islamic rev- 
olution) showed that Islam means revolution against Zionism and 
imperialism... he said. 


On the Organisation of African Unity (OAU), Colonel Al- 
Qadhdhafi said more than 50 percent of its members were 
reactionaries and U.S. and Zionist mercenaries. He added that 
nevertheless a progressive front existed in the OAU led by Libya 
which supports the revolutionary forces in Africa and advocates 
rebellion against those countries who were serving the interests 
of the US. in this continent. 


He said the Islamic Republic, Libya and Algeria and a few other 
countries should form a front in the Organisation of Petroleum 
Exporting Countries (OPEC) in order to counter the reactionary 
countries and overcome the difficulties facing the organisation.
