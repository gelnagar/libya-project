MEMORANDUM FOR: Hugh Montgomery, Director 
Intelligence and Research, Department of State 

FROM : Chairman, National Intelligence Council 

SUBJECT : Intelligence Support for US Policy Toward Libya 

1. We have your memorandum of 29 October regarding intelligence 
Support to US policy deliberations on Libya, and agree the community 
should have been involved in providing coordinated finished assessments 
of the several factors which you highlighted. Unfortunately, our sense 
of the present state of play is that we are well beyond the point where 
such an effort will make much difference. ; 

2. As you may know, there have already been a series of ad hoc 
policy level meetings on the Libyan question. We have staffed several 
working papers for these in support of the DCI and the Secretaries of 
State and Defense. These inputs are, we understand, being melded into 
the policy process. 

3. Consequently, we feel that production of interagency memoranda 
such as you propose -- even if written, coordinated, and published on a 
fast-track basis -- would be essentially redundant. Indeed, they would 
probably merely tie down scarce analytic resources both here and in INR 
for no appreciable gain in either insight or impact. We sense and share 
your underlying unease at the manner in which intelligence support has 
been handled on this subject, and Suggest we coordinate more closely in 
the future to avoid a repetition. 

Henry S. Rowen

SUBJECT: Intelligence Support for US Policy Toward Libya 

As you know, policymakers are focusing on the threat Qadhafi's 
actions pose to our interests. Among the possible courses of US 
response are economic actions against Libya. The IG chaired by 
NEA is considering some of these measures; the NSC has mounted its 
own effort to sort out policy toward Libya. 

In the midst of these deliberations, it seems to us that the 
intelligence community can be of great service to the policymakers 
by looking into the following topics: 

(1) The reactions of US allies to US policies toward Libya, 
including possible economic sanctions; 

(2) The impact and effectiveness of an anti-Qadhafi propaganda 
campaign; 

(3) Libyan monetary reserves (including how much is in the US 
and where); and 

(4) Alternatives to Libyan oil. 

A community effort would be particularly valuable on the first 
two subjects. We are asking, therefore, that interagency intelli- 
gence memoranda be prepared on them on a priority basis. Ata 
minimum, we need the drafts by Thursday, November 5 to make the 
appropriate contributions to the policy process. 

On items three and four, we are asking for shorter, less 
formal responses--perhaps as interagency memoranda cleared at the 
working level. 

INR will be happy to work closely with your staff in refining 
the terms of reference and in contributing to the drafting process. 

Drafted by: INR/NESA:LHarris
