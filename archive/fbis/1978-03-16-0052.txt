LIBYA

AT-TURAYKI CONDEMNS ISRAELI ACTION AGAINST LEBANON 
Consults Arab Ambassadors 
LD151740Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1700 GMT 15 Mar 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--This morning, following the treacherous aggression that the forces of 
the Zionist enemy have launched against southern Lebanon, the Foreign Secretariat of the 
Jamahiriyah made urgent and intensive contacts aimed at confronting this treacherous 
aggression to which fraternal Lebanon is being subjected by the Israelis in their effort 
to deal a blow to the progressive forces and the Palestine revolution. 


This morning the foreign secretary, Dr 'Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, received the 
ambassadors of the confrontation states in the Jamahiriyah and discussed the latest 
developments with them, stressing the seriousness of the situation and the need firmly 
to confront this treacherous aggression against the Palestine revolution, Syria and 
Lebanon. 


The ambassadors emphasized that the situation was very grave and requires immediate and 
firm confrontation and that they would be in touch with their respective governments 
immediately to convey to them the Jamahiriyah's views regarding the need to confront 
this plot. 


The foreign secretary also received the Iraqi ambassador to the Jamahiriyah and discussed 
all aspects of the situation with him and the need to adopt immediate and decisive 
measures to confront the Zionist aggression, including support for the Palestine 
revolution, 


The foreign secretary, Dr ‘Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, also telephoned Yasir ‘Arafat 
this morning, chairman of the Executive Committee and commander in chief of the Palestine 
revolution forces, and brother 'Abd al-Halim Khaddam, Syrian deputy prime minister and 
foreign minister. 


Cable Sent to Waldheim 
LD160944Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 16 Mar 78 LD 
{[Text] Tripoli--Foreign Secretary Dr 'Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, has sent the 
following calbe to Kurt Waldheim, UN secretary general, in connection with the Zionist 
attacks on southern Lebanon: 
The contimation of the racist Zionist aggression on the struggling Palestinian people 


and the fraternal Lebanese people confirms the aggressive expansionist nature of the 
racist Zionist regime founded on the usurpation and the shedding of innocent blood, 


V. 16 Mar 78 I 4 NORTH AFRICA 


While the Jamahiriyah condemns this flagrant aggression, which is threatening peace 

and security in the region and the world and which is considered a flagrant violation 
of the principles and Charter of the United Nations, and confirms its support for the 
peoples of Palestine and Lebanon in their tribulation, and while urging you to halt this 
treacherous aggression and condemn the aggressor, we express to you our support for 
your efforts aimed at preserving international peace and security. Please accept my 
best regards, 


Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, foreign secretary of the Jamahiriyah, and chairman 
of the 30th session of the Organization of African Unity Council of Ministers. 


The foreign secretary also sent similar cables to the foreign ministers of the OAU 

and the Islamic Conference outlining the nature of the treacherous aggression on the 
Palestinian and Lebanese peoples launched by the racist Zionist entity on 15 March 
1978, and he called upon them to help halt this expansionist aggression and condemn the 
aggressor. 


MEETING OF UN SECURITY COUNCIL CALLED FOR 
LD151844Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1800 GMT 15 Mar 78 LD 


{Text] The PLO representative in Tripoli today handed a message for the foreign 
secretary addressed to brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi from brother Yasir ‘Arafat. 


The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah today officially has called for the 
convening of an emergency session of the UN Security council to discuss the 
brutal Zionist aggression against southern Lebanon.
