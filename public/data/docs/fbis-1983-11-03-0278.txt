the city of Janzur in protest against the provocative manoeuvres 
carried by the colomalist British forces on Egyptian territories 
The masses participating in this demonstration brought out a 
statement condemning the fascist agent regime in Egypt which 
allowed the British colonialist forces to consecrate Egyptian soil 
and dishonour Arabs Confirming that the Egyptian people who 
bore ‘Abd an-Nasir, Arabi, Mustapha Kamil and got rid of the 
traitor As-Sadat, is capable of settling accounts with the enemy 
of the masses such as Husni Mubarak and his agent regime. The 
statement said that the savage imperialist attack carried out 
against the Arab nation would not have taken place had the Arab 
nation been united. The masses confirmed their total readiness 
to defend freedom everywhere and called upon freedom loving 
Egyptians and Egypt's dynamic forces to rid Egypt of reaction- 
ary, fascist agent regime

Weekly Blames U.S. Administration for Bombings 
PM020918 Tripoli Az-Zahf Al-Akhdar in Arabic 24 Oct 83 p 1 


{Editorial “The Rest Will Follow] 

{Excerpt} Now, after the two massive explosions in the US. 
Marines’ headquarters and in the French forces’ headquarters in 
Beirut unparalleled since the Vietnam war, according to'a 
U S_ politician — the ordinary American people have every right 
to cry out loud in Reagan's face and to ask bitterly what US. 


interests were served by the flagrant and direct L S intervention 
in Lebanon. What fruit has the United States harvested” It has 
only sunk deeper into the Lebanese quagmire and has not pulled 
the Marines out of it except as corpses in coffins The American 
people must realize that their President Reagan has done every- 
thing to satisfy his arrogance of power and his lust for dom- 
ination, bloody coercion, and satanic hegemony over all the 
peoples of the world, spilling American blood for no reason other 
than his own megalomania and his empty pride complex 


After the bombing of the US. Marines headquarters in 
Lebanon,we expect the American people to demonstrate their 
fury by toppling the Reagan terrorist administration as 4 punish- 
ment for the American lives lost in Lebanon, just as they brought 
down Lyndon Johnson for his direct involvement in Vietnam 


The American people must make it clear to all future administra- 
tions that people have a right to live in peace and security and 
that people, however weak they may be, must command the 
rspect of the big and the strong. The sun does not shine only on 
the United States and the globe is not an American toy or 
satellite. If the U.S. Administrations do not understand this, then 
the peoples of the world will be compelled to use with the United 
States a language which it understands very well — the language 
of legitimate revolutionary violence. This is a people's prerog- 
ative. For example, nobody in the whole world can deny that the 
Lebanese people have a right to resist the foreign invaders of their 
territory. The U.S. Administration alone is to blame for that 
horrific explosion which has, according to initial estimates, 
claimed at least 150 lives, not to mention the hundreds wounded 
and scores missing 


Reagan, the feeble actor, international charlatan, and creator of 
hotbeds of terrorist tension in the world, and his administration 
are to blame for white American flesh being blow to pieces by 
bombs in Lebanon. The Lebanese cannot be blamed because they 
did not cross seas and oceans to go and do that kind of thing in 
the United States. If Reagan persists in his mad obstinacy and 
in his aggressive course in the region then he should expect more 
suicide operations by commandos similar to the operation 
mounted against the U.S. Marines. It is this U.S. aggressive 
course, destructive to the Arab nation, which has bred an angry 
new Arab generation which will avenge the Arab nation’s mar- 
tyrs who fell as a result of the barbaric and bestial crusader- 
Zionist assault on the Arab homeland 


Editor Views Israeli Reconnaissance Over Gulf 
LDO21655 Tripoli JANA in English 1624 GMT 2 Nov 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Muharram 27, Nov 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — JANA‘s correspondent in Beirut has reported that 
Israeli Air Force carries out continuous reconnaissance flights 
over the Arab Gulf region and the oilfield in complete coordina- 
tion with AWACS planes deployed in the area. The agency's 
correspondent has learnt that some states in the area have 
complained about the Israeli air control which puts them in an 
embarrassing situation if the presence of Israeli Air Force in their 
airspace is disclosed. The U.S. Administration used all means of 
pressure and terror available for the benefit of the Israeli enemy 
including the continued presence of enemy's Air Force in the 


airspace of that region The political editor of JANA commented 
saying 


These dangerous operations carried out by Israeli Air Force in 
the Gulf region means that this region has come under the control 
of Israel Air Force totally. After Palestine and Lebanon have 
come under the domination of the enemy and now through these 
actions the enemy 1s controlling these states by hovering over in 
their skies without any confrontation by these states who have 
lost their freedom and independence
