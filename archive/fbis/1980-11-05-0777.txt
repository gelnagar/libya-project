LIBYA

LIBYAN NATIONALS OCCUPY EMBASSY IN KHARTOUM 


LDO41142 Khartoum SUNA in English 1045 GMT 4 Nov 80 


[Text] Khartoum, 4 Nov (SUNA)--Eight Libyan nationals occupied today the country's embassy 
here. 


In a telephone conversation with SUDAN NEWS AGENCY (SUNA), the secretary general of the 
Peoples Committee here Abdallah Al-Azraa said that the occupation was undertaken in the con- 
text of President Mu'ammar Al-Qadhdhafi's directives to change headquarters of diplomatic 
missions to the peoples committees which run their country's diplomatic affairs. 

JANA REPORTS ON GOUKOUNI-HABRE CONFLICT IN CHAD 


Ndjamena Airport 


LD312119 Tripoli JANA in English 1642 GMT 31 Oct 80 


[Text] Ndjamena, 31 Oct (JANA)--The Chadian Government forces led by President Goukouni 
recorded several victories over the Hissein Habre's forces and took over many positions 
including Ndjamena airport. JANA reporter in Ndjamena said that during the past two days 
the government forces managed to recapture a number of positions after bloody fighting 
against Habre's forces. As a result the latter forces suffered heavy losses. The reporter 
added that the government forces are still advancing and are fighting with high spirits. 


Ammunition Dumps 
LD312140 Tripoli JANA in English 1652 GMT 31 Oct 80 


[Text] Ndjamena, 31 Oct (JANA)--Reports coming out from the Chadian capital today have 
revealed that government forces were able to destroy live ammunition dumps belonging to the 
rebel Hissein Habre. Such ammunition is constantly being sent to him by the Zionist terror- 
ist As-Sadat of Egypt. The reports added that Habre relies completely on such ammunition 
dumps to feed his war against the legitimate Chadian forces. 


Goukouni Comments 
LD312132 Tripoli JANA in English 1655 GMT 31 Oct 80 


[Text] Ndjamena, 31 Oct (JANA)--Oueddei Goukouni, head of the legitimate government in Chad, 
pointed out that his forces now control most vital territories in Chad. 


This will cut all supply routes used by rebel Habre. Speaking to our correspondent from the 
battlefields, Goukouni confirmed As-Sadat's and Israel's involvement in supporting rebel 
Habre in men as well as in equipment to continue the sectarian strife among the Chadian 
people. The Chadian legitimate forces have captured large quantities of light and heavy 
weapons of Egyptian and Israeli make. The remnants of the rebel Habre's forces are in very 
bad situation, he added, and they will not stand for long and their defeat will be in the 
forseeable future.
