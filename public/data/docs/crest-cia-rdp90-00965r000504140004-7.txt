The text is partly garbled by OCR.
• US intelligence sources said one of Qaddafi's personal airplanes was reportedly on the runway at a desert location.
• Several dozen assassination attempts against Qaddafi were reported over the last five years, the last in November.
• No particular group was reported moving to seize power, according to US intelligence sources.
• 1986-04-15: Reported gunfire in Tripoli streets between units loyal to Qaddafi and other military units, with reports of a mutiny and Libyan air strikes called in against Libyan army units.
• 1986-04-15: A U.S. Embassy employee was shot in the head; officials said there was no direct link to Libya, only suspicions.
