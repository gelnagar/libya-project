REVOLUTIONARY COMMITTEES MUSTBECOME 'SUICIDE SQUADS! 


LD200927 Tripoli AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR in Arabic 12 May 80 p 15 


{Muhammad al-Majdub article: "Call for Revolutionary Committees To Turn Into Suicide 
Squads" ] 


{Excerpts ] When will man be able to achieve his happiness? Man's happiness can be at- 
tained only if he is liberated from all suppression, coercion and humiliation and freed 
from need. A poor man is not free because he does not possess his daily livelihood. A 
sick man is unhappy, and so is a man who is economically, socially and politically domi- 
nated, because all aspects of such domination deprive him of freedom and therefore 
diminish his happiness. 


Therefore, in order for man to wrest the freedom that enables him to achieve happiness, 
he must destroy all the obstacles that stand in the way of achieving his happiness. 


But man has not been able to achieve his happiness. Why? The answer is because man has 
been unable to rule himself. He is entirely subject to the instrument of government. 


The party is a government tool in control of all means that coerce and enslave man. So 
are the other dictatorial tools of the various traditional forms of government. There- 
fore, the decisive solution for the achievement of man's happiness is the establishment 
of people's power. 


People's power can be achieved only through the implemertation of the principles of 
humanitarian, Third World theory, through organizing peoples in basic people's congresses, 
electing their people's ;committees to implement the decisions and resolutions of those 
basic congresses. 


The revolutionary committees are the legitimate tool for implementing the Third World 
theory and for establishing the people's power and instilling it in the mind of the 
massese The Third World theory was preceded by two ideologies--the Marxist and the 
capitalist. Communities of peoples have adopted one of the two ideologies and, as a 
result, have become divided into two groups, one inclined toward Marxism and the other 
toward capitalism. 


A new giant has emerged, namely the Third World theory. As we said before, this theory 
was preceded by two other theories. But in order for this theory to be implemented it 
must have an organized ideological power capable of materializing the humanitarian ideas 
brought forth by the Third World theory. There is no doubt that such theory needs a 
genuine instrument, to establish it and to advocate it to the world. Therefore, the 
only solution for the materialization of the Third World theory is the establishment 

of revolutionary committees. 


Revolutionary Committees Differ from the World's Political Organizatios 


The revolutionary committees are different from the world's existing political organiza- 
tionse All political organizations since Adam, with the exception of revolutionary com- 
mittees, seek power for power's sake, while revolutionary committees seek to establish 
the people's power. They do not seek the ruler's power, like all the world's other 
political organizations that claim democracy and progressiveness and exploit the people. 
The role of the revolutionary committee is to drive the masses toward positions from 
which to run their own affairs, free themselves from need and establish their own freedom.

How Do the Revolutionary Committees Enable the Masses To Rule Themselves? 


The revolutionary committees in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab J: sahiriyah are dif- 
ferent from the revolutionary committees in other parts of the world. The revolutionary 
committees in the Socialist People's Libyan Jamahiriyah are the creation of the people's 
power. They are the revolutionary means that will be able to detect centers of injustice 
and incite the masses to revolt against and destroy them and to build new and just bases 
in accordance with the Third World theory. 


The People's Revolution in the Socialist People's Libyan Jamahiriyah Has Its Own His- 
torical Roots 


In fact, the revolutionary committees in the Socialist People's Libyan Jamahiriyah were 
established before the 1 September revolution in the form of the cells of free unionist 
officers. Prior to that, the leader, when he was a student, led the people's organiza- 
tions--the equivalent of today's revolutionary committees--in Sabha and Misratah, before 
the detonation of revolution. These revolutionary organizations were the early cells 

of the revolutionary committees that came into the open after the revolution. The 

leader believes that the revolutionary committees are the genuine tool for the entrench- 
ment of the revolution. Therefore, the revolutionary committees have their own historical 
roots. 


Call for the Establishment of Revolutionary Committees in the Arab Homeland and the 
World 


The difference in time when revolutionary committees declare themselves depends on con- 
ditions and circumstances. For example, the revolutionary committees in Iran and else- 
where in the world declared themselves because conditions enabled them to do so. But 
revolutionary committees in other countries are unable to declare themselves because as 
yet they have been unable to organize society in underground people's congresses. So 
rev»lutionary committees do exist secretly in the Arab and Islamic world, each according 
to its own conditions. As a first stage they must secretly incite the masses to organize 
in secret congresses. Once this is achieved they must declare themselves, so that ruling 
governments find themselves without a base and so fall without resistance, because it is 
impossible for them to resist all the people. 


The revolutionary committees must turn into suicide squads that do not fear death, so 
as to make sacrifices for sake of the Arab nation's unity and to eliminate reactionary 
idols. Once these idols fall the achievement of unity will become certain; all the 
means tried hitherto to achieve Arab unity have evidently proved futile and ineffective, 
which makes it necessary to adopt a new method and follow a new path--the path of co- 
hesion among revolutionary forces within revolutionary committees, in order to detonate 
people's revolution.

MEDITERRANEAN SOCIALISTS CONCLUDE SESSION IN TRIPOLI 
LD192101 Tripoli JANA in English 1852 GMT 19 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli, May 19, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The permanent secretariat of the or- 
ganization of socialist and progressive parties of the Mediterranean basin concluded its 
meetings at its headquarters in Tripoli today. 


The secretariat issued a statement condemning the 'Stable David' agreements which changed 
Egypt into a military, economic and information base of aggression against the peoples 
of the Mediterranean, the Arab world and Africa. 


The statement denounced NATO military manoeuvres in the Mediterranean which are considered 
as a direct threat t= the security and stability of the region. It warned the peoples of 
the Mediterranean that the region is heading towards a critical stage as a result of the 
offensive strategies by neocolonialism in collaboration with its agents in tho region. 


The secretariat lauded the struggle of the peoples of Greece, Turkey, Italy, Morocco 
and Portugal to get rid of NATO military bases in the region and stressed the neutrality 
of Malta. 


The statement condemned all attempts by colonialism and reaction to contain the struggle 
of the Palestinian people and their national cause. 


It also condemned the Judification of Jerusalem and its transformation into the official 
capital of (Israel) as well as (Israeli) military aggression against southern Lebanon 
and U.S. military aggression against the Iranian Revolution. It pledged support for and 
solidarity with the Saharan people and condemned the aggression of the Royal Moroccan 
regime against the Saharan people. 


The secretariat also pledged support for the struggle of the workers and students in 
Western Europe for economic and social justice and denounced the repressive campaigns 
against these sectors launched by the European exploitationist regimes in alliance with 
workers monopolies. 


In this respect, the secretariat stresses its commitment to the resolutions of the 5th 
conference for control of oil monopolies held in the Jamahiriyah last March.
