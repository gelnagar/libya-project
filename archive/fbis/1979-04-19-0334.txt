AL-QADHDHAFI: PEOPLE'S AUTHORITY MUST NOT BE DISTORTED 
LD181545 Tripoli JANA in English 1405 GMT 18 Apr 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Apr, JANA--<Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great First 
of September Revolution, met the General People's Congress yesterday evening and 
stated that the Libyan Arab peoples in the Jamahiriyah have achieved the ultimate by 
way of people's congresses and committees, 


He said that there was nothing beyond the people's authority except chaos, just as there 
is nothing beyond truth but darkmess, Thus, one of the tasks of the revolution's forces 
is the protection of the people's authority which should not be at all passed over or 
distorted, 


He added: "Even if the people's authority receives a setback it will continue to be 
the supreme representative for democracy, for which the people will struggle anew in 
order to consolidate it, " 


V. 19 Apr 79 NORTH AFRICA 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that any fault or weakness which may appear is in 
fact a failure on the part of the people and are implementing the theory upon which the 
people's authority has been based within the people's congresses and committees. 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi addressed the General People's Committees and said: "You 
remain the first to be established by the people's authority. Hence, you should appre- 
ciate the historic responsibility with which you have been entrusted, not merely in 
Libya but before all people, because your failure will encourage the perpetuation of 
the dictatorial regimes. Your success will speed up the eradication of all forms of 
traditional regimes. 


"The final form of the people's power has been achieved now. It is the great target 
for which people have died every day to depose rulers, individuals, parties and certain 
classes in their age-long contest for power. The time is opportune now for the people 
to occupy those seats of power." He further pointed out the importance of preserving 
and not dissipating this great achievement because "we estimate highly the victory 

of the people's power which is the theory of tomorrow," 


The leader of the revolution then talked about the tasks of the revolutionary committees 
and pointed out that they are an integral part of the theory and any objection to them 
would in fact mean an objection to the theory and revolution themselves. The task of 
the revolutionary committees, he said, is to help the people's committees reach their 
objectives and guide the people's congresses and committees to make sound decisions. 


The people's committees exercise authority, he continued, while the revolutionary 
committees exercise revolutionary control. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi also talked about the duties of those working in the administrative 
systems. He stressed the need for everyone to devote efforts and time to carry out 

this assigned job efficiently and successfully. For any shortcoming is treason to be 
punished on earth and evil to be accounted for in the final day of reckoning because 

it kills freedom, destroys the people's authority, obstructs the process of progress 

and delays production. 


The leader of the revolution affirmed the necessity to continue the process of trans- 
formation which should be democratic involving all the masses in order to achieve the 
progress for which the greatest sacrifices should be made, 


On the administrative system, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi called for making it sound, correct, 
active and with the minimum staff since the greater the number, the greater the dependence 
and the less the responsibility which would render the system unproductive and ineffective. 
And therefore all frozen untapped resources should be transferred to production sites 

to make nearer the victory over backwardness.

PRIOR KNOWLEDGE OF AS-SADAT'S INITIATIVE DENIED 
LD182300 Rabat Domestic Service in Arabic 2000 GMT 18 Apr 79 LD 


[Text] The Israeli prime minister has been quoted as saying in one of his recent 
Statements that the visit which President Muhammad Anwar as-Sadat paid to Jerusalem 
was preceded by a meeting between the Israeli foreign minister and the Egyptian First 
Deputy Prime Minister Hasan at-Tuhami and that preparations for that visit took place 
in Morocco, 


Authoritative Moroccan sources close to the royal palace categorically denied these 
allegations and affirmed once more that the Egyptian president did not disclose his 
intention to go to occupied Jerusalem to his majesty the king. President Anwar as- 
Sadat's initiative was a complete surprise as far as his majesty was concerned, 
particularly since prior to the visit the conversation between his majesty and the 
Egyptian president was only about the Geneva conference, which at that time was the 
only appropriate framework for peace negotiations in the Middle East. Finally, the 
Moroccan sources pointed out that Mr Anwar as-Sadat himself had frequently and 
publicly stated that his initiative was purely a personal one and that he did not 
inform any Arab head of state about it in advance.

LEBANESE ARMY POSITIONS UNITS IN UNIFIL AREA 
TA181412 Jerusalem Domestic Service in Hebrew 1400 GMT 18 Apr 79 TA 


[Text] The UN spokesman in southern Lebanon announced that the first group of 150 
Lebanese soldiers arrived this morning at 0800 in seven vehicles at the UNIFIL operations 
area. The Lebanese were divided into five platoons and each one of them was stationed 

in the headquarters of one of the five battalions constituting UNIFIL: those of Senegal, 
Fiji, the Netherlands, Nigeria and Ireland. In the meantime, the rest of the Lebanese 
force also reached its destination, the UNIFIL spokesman reports. It was stated that 
militia forces in the village of An-Naqurah fired several bursts from machineguns into 
the UNIFIL headquarters yard. UNIFIL forces replied with one burst. Toward 1100, 

the UNIFIL spokesman's report says, the militiamen were seen placing explosives at the 
entrance of the Norwegian battalion's helicopter branch in An-Naqurah. UNIFIL men fired 
into the air and the militiamen retreated.

[Text] Egypt will rehabilitate and populate the town of At-Tur, along the Suez Gulf, 
with the aim of turning it into the chief oil town in the area, the Egyptian governor

Mr (Wahaba) clarified in an interview with the HA'ARETZ correspondent that the plans for

The southern Sinai governor talked about agricultural experiments being conducted in 
Egypt now, in an area with climate and soil conditions similar to those in At-Tur. He 
Claims that good results were obtained in those experiments and it was proven that the 
area is suitable for agriculture--mainly potatoes, tomatoes and fruit groves. 


Replying to the HA'ARETZ correspondent's question, Mr (Wahaba) dismissed the problem 
of water in the area and claimed that there would not be any special problems in diverting 
Nile water to the area in order to advance the local agriculture.
