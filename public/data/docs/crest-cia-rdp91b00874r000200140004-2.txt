Summary (AI): A July 1986 CIA assessment of Qadhafi's political position after the April 1986 US airstrike. It describes eroding domestic support, tighter personal security, tensions between Jallud and the military, and a reported coup plot and mutiny near Tarhuna, 30 km southeast of Tripoli.
• Assessment dated July 1986; information as of 16 July 1986.
• Qadhafi cancelled his 11 June 1986 public appearance, preferring a televised speech, likely out of fear of assassination.
• Qadhafi's survival chances through end of 1986 judged only slightly better than even.
• Revolutionary Committees reportedly arrested military personnel suspected of disloyalty after the bombing.
• Military officers were prohibited from associating in groups.
• Jallud visited Moscow; Libya reportedly offered Soviets bases in Libya during talks.
• Libya reportedly discounted crude oil by up to $1.50 per barrel below spot price of $10 per barrel; US oil companies were leaving Libya.
• 1986-04: US airstrike bombed Qadhafi's residence; funeral procession for those killed drew only several thousand marchers.
• 1986-04: Reports of coup plotting and a military mutiny near Tarhuna immediately after the US airstrike; loyalist air force reportedly suppressed an uprising.
• 1986-04: Reported mutiny of army units in Derna after the US airstrike.
• 1986-04: A military officer attempted to assassinate Qadhafi in late April 1986.
• 1985-11: Colonel Hassan Ashkal, Qadhafi's cousin and confidant, was killed (apparently executed) after violating terms of confinement.
