MINISTER ACCUSES CHAD OF IMPEDING RECONCILIATION TALKS 
JN111709Y Khartoum SUNA in Arabic 1632 GMT 11 Jul 78 JN 


{Text] Khartoum, 11 July--Dr at-Turayki [Libyan secretary of foreign affairs ] has 
announced [place and date not given] that Sudan, the Libyan Jamahiriyah and Niger will 
continue their efforts to bring about national reconciliation in Chad in the interests 
of the entire Chadian people. 


Dr at-Turayki said: For this purpose, we held the Sabha conference, which was successful, 
and reached the Benghazi agreement, which was a good one. However, we were taken by 
surprise by the French intervention, which impeded the implementation of the agreement. 

He added: Finally, we held the Tripoli conference which was clear. We were surprised 
that the Chadian Government's delegation did not want a solution. When we, Sudan and 
Niger drafted a project to bring about national reconciliation in Chad, the delegation 

of the Chadian Government refused even to discuss the project. This forced the three 
states to issue a statement putting the responsibility for the failure of the negotiations 
on Chad.

JANA CITES COMMUNIQUE ON ONSLAUGHT BY IMPERIALISM 

1D102052Y Tripoli JANA in English 1828 GMT 10 Jul 78 ID 

{Text ] Tripoli, 10 July, JANA--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress has 
issued a communique in which it affirms "the vicious imperialist onslaught against the Arab 
nation is increasing as never before." 

The first stage of onslaught was the Egyptian president's trip of shame to occupied 
Jerusalem in order to liquidate the Palestinian cause, reinforce the Zionist occupation, 


impose American proposals on the area and secure imperialist monopolization of Arab wealth, 
particularly the oil. 


Then the Zionist gang conducted the second stage of the onslaught by committing direct 


aggression against southern Lebanon, The onslaught was completed by the U.S. imposing 
on Lebanon the UN forces in which French troops played the principal role. 


Due to the great resistance with which the Palestinian revolution and the Lebanese National 
Movement confronted the Zionist aggression and the foreign presence that followed the 
aggression, imperialism and its allies took refuge in complicating the situation in Lebanon 
by creating social instability, imposing military tension throughout the country and inter - 
fering directly to prevent the Arab peacekeeping forces from facing the fascist Phalangist 
gangs which no longer hide their relationships with the Zionists and American imperialists, 


The current Zionist military movements on the Lebanese and Syrian borders indicate that the 
Zionist enemy is determined to impose capitulation upon the Arab nation. 


The statement referred to suspicious French movements, with blatant support from the U.S., 
in the Arab world where French military aircraft are attacking the Arab people in the 
Western Sahara and French troops are encircling the progressive regime in Algeria and the 
Jamahiriyah and interfering in Chad against its people's welfare and interest. 


The communique affirmed that world imperialism has provoked new instability in the south of 
the Arabian Peninsula and the Arab Gulf by inciting putsch actions in North Yemen and by 
attempting a coup against the progressive regime in South Yemen, ‘The aim of imperialism 
is to set off a border war between the two neighbouring parts of Yemen in favour of the 
interests of imperialism and Arab reaction.

The communique also stated that Arab revolutic y nationalist and progressive forces 
are requested to act with vigilance and intrepidity and to take a firm stand against the 
increasing number of conspiracies which have thwarted the pan-Arab struggle. 


It called upon the forces of the Arab progressive national movement to declare their con- 
demnation of the reactionary imperialist scheme. 


It specified that today world imperialism, headed by the United States, insistently tries 
to paralyze the movement of Arab national struggle either through coups, by means of direct 
military provocation or by devising plots, the threads of which are now being woven during 
As-Sadat's visit to Europe and his meetings with the U.S. authorities and the Zionists 

as an alternative to his capitulationist initiative which has fallen flat because of 

the Arab people's steadfastness, 


The communique emphasized the need for increased struggle on all fronts to face the 
imperialist, Zionist and reactionary aggression in the Arab West, East and South.
