LIBYA

EGYPT PLAYED ‘DANGEROUS GAME' WITH AWACS 


LD211655 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 21 Feb 83 


[Text] The Egyptian regime's request for U.S. spy planes and their presence near the 
Egyptian-Libyan borders have aroused indignant reactions against that regime and have 
been a source of public dissatisfaction and concern. JANA political editor wrote an 
analysis on the presence of these planes in the region and the strategic objectives to 
which they were devoted. He said: 


The president of the Egyptian regime played a dangerous game fraught with dire conse- 
quences when he allowed the U.S. spy planes near the Egyptian-Libyan borders. Husni 
Mubarak offered an illogical justification for giving permission to these planes; to be 
more precise his justification was ridiculous. That is he wanted to mock and ridicule 
the mentality of the Arab citizen when he claimed that these planes were requested for 
training of Egyptians. 


V. 22 Feb 83 Q 2 NORTH AFRICA 


Everyone knew the implication of dispatching these planes and the dangerous dimensions 
of their presence in Egypt; the foremost of which is that these spy planes carry out 
tasks exclusively connected with the U.S. scheme aimed at subjugating the Arab region 
to the U.S. [word indistinct] strategy. These planes will not provide any real service 
to any Arab regime, whatever the extent of its alliance with or ideal services to 

U.S. policy. 


Husni Mubarak's justifications and pretext that the Egyptians would be trained on these 
planes are totally rejected, and no one could be fooled by them. The truth is that the 
main objective behind the request for these planes was to commit an aggressive act 
against Libya and not against the Israelis. 


Hence, the truth emerges that the presence of the AWACS planes and the resumption of 
their spying activities constitute aggression against Libya and the Libyan people. 
Our people, well aware of the secrets of the game and its danger, will maintain the 
right to destroy these planes at any time they wish to do so, even if they are flying 
in Egyptian skies. These spy planes mainly serve the Pentagon's purposes and the 
U.S. and Israeli war machinery; merely by entering the region they create a situation 
of tension that will lead to the brink of war in the U.S. fashion. 


The excuse that their presence in the region was to collect information for the 
Egyptian or Sudanese regimes is deceitful and [word indistinct] excuses. The only 
information that Egypt or Numayri's lackey regime could be provided with would be 
nothing but mystifying information and false communiques which are given to the 
region's governments, which seek their help, with the aim of creating a state of 
panic and tension and with the purpose of putting these governments and regimes in a 
state of heightened [word indistinct] and war. This was the case in the past 2 days, 
when these planes flew and provided the Egyptian and Numayri's lackey regimes with 
false information in the course of a psychological campaign, making them believe that 
there was a possibility of an armed clash taking place. 


On the basis of all the firm evidence and facts, the AWACS planes will provide abso- 
lutely no facts tothe Arab nation. If this were the case, these planes would have 
provided Saudi Arabia with the correct information which it should have provided for it, 
when they were stationed in its territories, when the Zi+mist Air Force crossed Saudi 
air space and struck against the Iraqi nuclear reactor. This is a fact beyond any 
doubt, and if the Egyptian regimes choose to ignore it and hang on to playing with 
the U.S. AWACS it will only harm itself, because it will be constantly playing with 
fire. It must be well aware and bear the consequences and complications that will 
result from this dangerous game. 


UN DELEGATE'S MESSAGE ON 'GRAVE SITUATION’ 


LD201038 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 20 Feb 83 


[Text] New York, 20 Feb (JANA) -- Dr ‘Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, Jamahiriyan per- 
manent delegate to the United Nations, yesterday sent a message to the chairman of 
the Security Council. Here is the text of the message: 


I would like to draw your attention to the grave situation resulting from American 
military threats and provocations directed against the Jamahiriyah which aim at 
creating a situation likely to threaten security and peace in the region. 


America, by dispatching AWACS aircraft to one of the countries close to Jamahiriyah and 
by moving the aircraft carrier Nimitz and some other naval units close to Libyan shores, 
represent a clear provocation to international principles of law and behavior. 


V. 22 Feb 83 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


These unjustifiable actions and hostile statements by American officials and the 
intense propaganda campaign which accompanied them confirm the harbored hostile 
intentions of the American Administration toward the Jamahiriyah. 


As I inform you of this I assure you of the Jamahiriyah's striving for peace and secur- 
ity in the region and charge America with responsibility for what might happen as a 
result of its aforementioned actions. 


The continuing flights of American episonage and jamming aircraft over our borders has 
led to a grave situation and to the obstruction of civilian communications within the 
Jamahiriyah. Moreover, the presence of the U.S. 6th Fleet close to our shores could 
lead to the creation of a grave situation not only likely to threaten peace in the 
region but also to threaten international peace and security, a matter for which 
America holds full responsibility. 


The Jamahiriyah stresses that no tension exists in the region to justify this American 
provocation, and that the Jamahiriyah had and has no intention of interfering in the 
internal affairs of any neighboring or remote country, that it cares for the mainten- 
ance of peace and security in the region, and that our concern, as a small country, is 
to develop our country and its stability. 


At the same time, I would like to draw the attention of the members of the UN Security 
Council to the gravity of these provocations by one of the member states of the coun- 
cil, and we reserve our right to summon the council to convene if these American 
provocations continue or increase. We also reserve our right to defend ourselves and 
to adopt the necessary measures to maintain our freedom, the security of our 
territory, and the inviolability of our air space and territorial waters. 


At the end of delivering his message the permanent delegate asked the chairman of 
the Security Council to distribute it as a Security Council document.

[Text] Tripoli, 20 Feb (JANA) -- A prominent Palestinian official has stated that the

influencing independent Palestinian decisions. 


The political editor of JANA described the statement made by Ahmad ‘Abd ar-Rahman,

Palestinian official stating that the Jamahiriyah is an enemy of the Palestinian 
revolution, and that the condition of noninterference in the affairs of the 
Palestinian revolution has to be enforced in order to normalize relations. 


This official has forgotten, or pretends to forget, that the Jamahiriyah and the 

Great Fatah Revolution consider themselves to be in one trench with the Palestinian 
revolutionaries in the confrontation with the Zionist enemy, liberation of their 

land, establishment of their independent state on their own territory; that they are 
the only voice which called for pan-Arabism of the battle with the enemy and 
pan-Arabism of the Fida'i action; that is is the only state which rejected the process 
of withdrawing the Palestinian fighters from Lebanon who have now been dispersed 

as a result of the rejection of the Jamahiriyah's call and who are thus distanced 

from their proper position, that of facing the enemy.

ARMED FORCES WARN AGAINST TERRITORIAL VIOLATIONS 


LD191008 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0903 GMT 19 Feb 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Feb (JANA) -- The Libyan Arab Armed Forces have declared that they 
will not permit any forces or targets [abdaf] of any kind no matter who stands behind 
them, even if it is the leader of world terrorism -- the United States -- to violate 
our air, territorial waters, or land. We will turn the Gulf {of Sidra], which we con- 
sider to be a part of or territory, into fire and blood against any aggressor and we 
will implememt the resolutions of our people, our revolution, and our commander. 


In a statememt announced over the television network last night, the Libyan Arab Armed 
Forces explaiined the violations committed by the American forces in the Jamahiriyah 
skies and territorial waters. The statement declared that we have not threatened the 
security of Sudan nor did we amass troops on its borders. We do not possess planes 
that can reach deep into the Sudan so that we may strike there as the American 
President claims. 


V. 22 Feb 83 Q7 NORTH AFRICA 


The statement added that the armed forces consider all this a mere pretext intended to 
intimidate us, terrorize us, and bring us to our knees. The American President knows 
that we have no intention of carrying out an aggression against anyone, be it Egypt, 
Sudan, or Chad. 


The statement pointed out that the supreme commander of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces 
had previously said something to this effect. He said: "We declare to all those 
interested that we will carry out the will of our people and we will die in defense 
of our land and our revolution." 


The statement added that the American Administration has sent the U.S. aircraft carrier 
Nimitz and its escort ships to take up positions opposite Libyan shores on the pretext 
of protecting the Sudanese regime. 


The statement went on to say that on 13 February 1983 the American fleet began to 
approach Libyan shores. The aircraft carrier arrived opposite the Gulf of Sidra just 
outside the latitude 32 degrees and 32 minutes -- the line of Jamahiriyah territorial 
waters. 


On 16 February 1983, an aircraft violated our territorial waters and approached Banghazi 
to a distance of 80 kilometers. But our Air Forces intercepted it and forced it to flee. 


On 17 February 1983, some American naval fleet vessels approached the boundary of our 
territorial waters at a distance of 22 kilometers from the latitude 32 degrees and 30 
minutes. Our naval forces watched them closely. 


The statement pointed out that the American early warning aircrafts -- the AWACS -- 
have actually arrived in Egypt and began their provocative and aggressive operations 
along the Egyptian-Libyan border in the area extending from (Jabal al-Uwaynat) mountain 
to the (Siwah) oasis along the border line. Libyan Arab Air Forces have taken all the 
necessary measures to meet any eventuality.
