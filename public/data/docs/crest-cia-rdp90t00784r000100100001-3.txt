Summary (AI): A December 1982 NPIC imagery briefing note reports Soviet arms deliveries to Libya at the Tripoli port facilities, including SA-3 launchers, ZSU-23/4 guns, a probable flat face radar, and about 30 van-bodied trucks and trailers on the military transshipment quay. The equipment came from the Soviet ship Kapitan Kushnarenko-class vessel, with the 'Kapitan Slipko' previously calling at Tubruq and Benghazi.
• Two four-rail SA-3 launchers, 18 ZSU-23/4 self-propelled antiaircraft guns, one probable flat face radar (travel mode), one probable SA-2/3 canister transporter, and at least 30 van-bodied trucks/trailers observed on the military transshipment quay.
• Most equipment was probably present but not identifiable due to image scale.
• The Soviet ship 'Kapitan Slipko' was reported to have left the Black Sea with a deck cargo including three van-bodied trucks, one stake truck, one possible radar van, five ambulances, two utility vehicles and four trailers.
• The 'Kapitan Slipko' previously called at Tubruq and Benghazi.
• Source is an NPIC briefing board (Z-11821/82), reporting message dated 22 December 1982.
• 1982-12: Offloading of Soviet arms (two four-rail SA-3 launchers, 18 ZSU-23/4 SPAA guns, probable flat face radar, probable SA-2/3 canister transporter, at least 30 van-bodied trucks/trailers) from a Soviet Kapitan Kushnarenko-class merchant ship onto the military transshipment quay.
