LIBYA

LIBYA DENIES CHAD REQUESTED TROOP WITHDRAWAL 


LD311352 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 31 Oct 81 


[Text] Western, U.S. and reactionary information media are launching a misleading propaganda 
campaign regarding the situation in Chad. Im this connection, they are publishing 
allegations and fabrications aimed at giving a false impression about this situation. 

The Western newspapers are concentrating on an allged request made by President 

Goukouni Queddei, head of the national government, for the total and immediate withdrawal 

of Libyan forces from Chadian territory, according to their claim. These allegations 

are intended to poison the close relations between the two peoples -—- the Libyan Arab 

people and the Chadian people. 


The JANA correspondent in Ndjamena said today that the allegations of the Western and 
reactionary newspapers and radios about this so-called request fall within the 
framework of machinations and misleading campaigns aimed at shaking the strategic 
relations between the two fraternal peoples.

ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS OFFICIAL ADDRESSES RALLY 


LD312336 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1739 GMT 30 Oct 81 


[Speech delivered by Dr Ahmad as-Suwaydi, member of the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab 
People's Congress at the special ceremony held at the People's Hall in Tripoli on 31 October 
to commemorate the anniversary of the Algerian revolution and the tripartite aggression on 
Egypt in 1956 -—- live] 


[Excerpts] In our life today, we miss much of the nationalist upsurges we witnessed during 
the days of the Algerian revolution and the days of the direct confrontation with imperialism 
and its bases in the Arab homeland. In the stage of nationalist revival, Arab reaction hid 
itself waiting to see what the imperialists and U.S. imperialism would do. But today, the 
Arab reaction has moved to the offensive. On the instigation of and planning by U.S. 
imperialism, it today is waging the »attle on behalf of imperialism in the area. 


Most of the Arab rulers, princes and notables are now joining the game of the imperialists, 
sometimes wittingly and sometimes unwittingly. Saudi Arabia continued to support As-Sadat 
until it led him to Jerusalem. Saudi Arabia stood and continues to stand on the side of 
Numayri, Siad Barre and Qabus in their alliance with As-Sadat in the past and with present 
the Egyptian regime. Saudi Arabia uses oil as a weapon against the Arab nation instead of 
using it as a weapon against the enemies of the Arab nation. 


Syria supports the Palestine resistance and the National Lebanese Movement. [applause] 

The Arab homeland applauds Syria just as you did now. But this is not enough. The 
Jamahiriyah stands by the Palestine resistance and the National Lebanese Movement. [applause] 
The commander of the great September revolution recently presented a plan for national 
reconciliation in Lebanon. But instead of offering support, our Arab rulers, princes and 
shaykhs expressed their wrath and concern. They said what has Libya to do with Lebanon, 
particularly since it is so far away from Lebanon. Today we are living in an era of 

political hypocracy. 


Today, the United States violates Libyan waters and skies. When the Libyan people and the 
great 1 September revolution rise to counter the U.S. aggression, the Arab rulers begin to 
shake with fear that U.S. imperialism will get angry because of the confrontation and 
resistance put up by Libya. 


When the heroic Egyptian people topple the arch traitor of the Arab homeland and Egypt, 
Arab reaction tries to seize the opportunity to say: The one who signed the Camp David 
agreement has fallen, then let us be reconciled with the Egyptians. They want us to give 
a chance to the new ruler of Egypt. But we want to give a chance to Egypt and not to its 
rulers. We want to give the Egyptian people the chance to express their views in full 
freedom. We want to give Egypt the chance to topple once and for all the Camp David 
accords and policies. 


We reject current calls by Arab reaction for reconciliation with the new rulers of Egypt, 
despite their acceptance of the Camp David accords. We reject even more strongly the 
attempts to Arabize these accords and to replace the star of David with Fuhd's oil well. 
We reject the logic that the Camp David accords are a matter that concerns Egypt alone or 
that by his initiative, Fahd offers an alternative for these accords on behalf of the 
entire Arab nation.

We cannot abandon the thousands detained in Egypt. We cannot accept the Fahd initiative as 

an alternative for the Camp David accords -- an alternative which is welcomed by Western 
circles. The only option and alternative we accept is that of the recognition of the PLO 

as the sole legitimate representative of the Palestine people, the recognition of the national 
legitimate rights of the Palestine people foremost of which is their right to be self-determina- 
tion and to return to their land and to set up their independent national state on the soil of 
Palestine. [applause] 


As an Arab people's congress, we say there can be no alternative to the rights of the Palestine 
people and of the Arab nation and its political and economic independence. There can be 

no alternative to comprehensive Arab unity. [applause] We refuse to put ourselves at the 
disposal of a handful of princes and shaykhs. 


In the face of the developments presently taking place, we have called for an emergency 
meeting of the Arab People's Congress to be held in Tripoli from 16-19 November so that all 
factions of the Arab revolution will have the opportunity to hold consultations on how to 
deal with the new strings of the continuing plot.
