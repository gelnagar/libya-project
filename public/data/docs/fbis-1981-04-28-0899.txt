LIBYA

"ARAFAT, OTHERS VISIT, MEET WITH AL-QADHDHAFI 


LD261326 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 26 Apr 81 


[Text] The brother commander of the revolution today received brother PLO Executive Com- 
mittee Chairman Yasir ‘Arafat in the presence of brother Abu Iyad [Salah Khalaf]. The 
brother commander also received Brothers Muhsin Ibrahim, the secretary general of the 
Communist Action Organization, and George Hawi, the secretary general of the Lebanese 
Communist Party. 


Brother ‘Arafat arrived in Tripoli this morning on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah. In a statement to a JANA correspondent, ‘Arafat denounced the imperialist- 
reactionary plan which is being implemented in the Arab region. He pointed out that the 
developments in Lebanon are part of that plan. 


Brother ‘Arafat said that his visit to the Jamahiriyah is taking place under conditions of 
an intensified Zionist-reactionary offensive against the Arab nation. Imperialism has 
begun to cordon off the Arab homeland with a series of military bases in Sudan, Oman and 
the Red Sea. Brother Abu Iyad came with Brother ‘Arafat. They were on the same plane as 
brothers Muhsin Ibrahim and George Hawi.

OFFICIALS MEET WITH VISITING HUNGARIAN MINISTER 


General Committee Secretary 
LD252124 Tripoli JANA in English 1558 GMT 25 Apr 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 25 Apr (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The secretary of the General People's 
Committee met the Hungarian defence minister who is currently visiting the Jamahiriyah. 
During the meeting the Hungarian minister commended the agricultural revival in the 
Jamahiriyah. He said: 'The projects I have seen here confirm the Jamahiriyah's intention 

to reclaim desert land so that it can achieve self-sufficiency in foodstuffs.‘ The Hungarian 
minister spoke also of the fruitful results achieved by Libyan-Hungarian cooperation.

_REPORT OF EXPULSION CAMPAIGN IN SUDAN CITED 


LD281012 Tripoli JANA in English 0805 GMT 28 Apr 81 


[Text] Khartoum, 28 Apr (JANA)--According to yesterday's issue of the Kuwaiti newspaper 
AS-SIYASAH, President Numayri has expelled no less than 1400 high ranking officials from 
both the civil and military sectors.

The expulsion order was not announced publicly on the media but each person was notified 
separately for fear of a popular reprisal. The expulsion order has affected railway 
employees, the civil service, doctors, teachers, police and customs officials. 


No reason has been given why the order was taken. However, the paper did not rule out 
the possibility that Sudanese intelligence could have been at the core of the campaign 
in a bid to rounc up nationalist elements. 


JANA_RESPONDS TO AFP CRITICISM OF LIBYAN POLICY 


LD241254 Tripoli JANA in English 0815 GMT 24 Apr 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Apr (JANA)--The French news agency (AFP) has commented on the 
revolution leader's call for unity between Mauritania and the Saharan Arab Republic and 
forming an alliance including Libya, Algeria, Mauritania and the Saharan Arab Republic. 

It said that Colonel al-Qadhdhafi was facing difficulties in realizing his dream of Arab 
unity and leadership of the Arab region. It added that Colonel al-Qadhdhafi had turned 
from the stage of aiding Moslems, building mosques for them and establishing radio stations 
to that of direct interventions. 


It went to say the Libya had come to resemble the Tower of Babylon for liberation move- 
ments and international terrorism, as Americans and Westerners affirm, and that 
Al-Qadhdhafi does not hesitate in financing all jwords indistinct] international movements 
struggling against reaction whether in Somalia-or Sudan or Europe or Asia. The AFP 
enumerated the unionist attempts between Jamahiriyah and some Arab countries, and 

referred to the call for a masses unity between Libya and Chad by saying that despite the 
failure of previous attempts many African states were not concealing their anxiety toward 
Libya's policy which could undermine the fragile stability of the African Continent, which 
was now suffering from civil wars and backwardness. 


JANA's correspondent commented on the above, saying: "The AFP's comment completely 
reflects the venomous French Government's attitude towards the Arab nation, expresses 
France's hostile imperialist stand towards the Arab nation's unity and entrenches the 
trac tional colonialist understanding which works for keeping the Arab nation divided, 
weak and torn. It was more befitting for the Giscard d'Estaing government which [words 
indistinct] France's reputation, spoilt what De Gaulle and Pompidou had done, degraded 
the French republic and destroyed the French people's relations with the Arab nation and 
world peoples. It was more befitting for this agency to have warned the French people 
of reelecting d'Estaing because this would cause a disaster for them, result in a great 
loss for them and create more unemployment, racial discrimination, strikes, demonstrations 
and inflation. 


"Has it escaped the French news agency notice that the leader's constant call for Arab 
unity is a call for reverting the situation to its [words indistinct] and taking the 

Arab nation back to the situation [words indistinct] to imperialist intrigue to which 
France contributed greatly, or is it still dreaming about the return of French colon- 
ialism to Africa and aspiring to reassemble the French empire after it was torn apart by 
the revolutions of African peoples? Has the AFP forgotten that the Arab nation is one 
family and that the Jamahiriyah is part of Africa, which does not hold French 

colonialism with any esteem? 


"The image of direct French military intervention is still vivid in the minds of African 
peoples. Has it forgotten the civil wars it created, and the coups it instigated and the 
massacres that befell the African peoples at the hands of its [words indistinct] 
surrogates in Africa?

"The developing countries in Africa and the world know an [word indistinct] who is 
terrorizing them with his diverse fleets, flexing his muscles to scare them and dominate
