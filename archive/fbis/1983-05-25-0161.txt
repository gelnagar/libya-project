LIBYA

Libyan, Soviet Friendship Societies Sign Agreement 
LD250149 Tripoli JANA in English 1712 GMT 24 May 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Shaban 12, May 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The Libyan and Soviet friendship societies signed 
yesterday in Tripoli an agreement for cultural and social cooper- 
ation. 


The signing ceremony was signed by the secretary-general of the 
Libyan Arab-Soviet Friendship Society and secretary of the 
Green Book Research Centre, Mr. Ahmad Shahati, and the 
chairperson of the Soviet-Libyan Friendship Society and Soviet 
deputy premier, Mr. Vladimir Demchenko. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Receives DPRK Vice President 
LD232312 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
23 May 83 


[Text] The brother leader today received DPRK Vice President 
Pak Song-chol, who handed him a written message from DPRK 
President Kim Il-song which confirmed the Korean people's 
standing with the Libyan Arab people against U.S. provocations 
in the Gulf of Sidra and against imperialist conspiracies and 
maneuvers against the people of the Jamahiriyah. The message 
confirmed the people of Korea’s readiness to implement the 
treaty of friendship and alliance signed by the two countries, and 
also confirmed Korea's standing against imperialism and its 
creation — Israel. It condemned the Israeli aggression against 
Lebanon and confirmed the need for the Israeli aggressors’ 
unconditional withdrawal from Lebanon, and stated the belief 
that the agreement signed by the Lebanese Government with the 


Zionist entity has lost Lebanon its full freedom and enabled this 
entity to achieve its objectives. It also explained Democratic 
Korea's opinion concerning the unity of the two parts of Korea 
and the obstacles it faces at the hands of the imperialists and their 
agents in the region.

Commentary Claims ‘Arafat Discredited Among PLO 
LD241845 Tripoli JANA in English 1746 GMT 24 May 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Shaban 12, May 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — It has become almost impossible for “Arafat and 
his clique to continue giving the same impression which he falsely 
had given through his statement on readiness to confront the 
Zionist enemy. 


The image which he has drawn around himself is not any more 
acceptable to the revolutionary cadres as a symbol of respect. 


The uprising in the Al-Biga’, led by Abu Salih [Nimr Salih], Abu 
Musa [Sa’id Musa Muragha]and Qadri [Samih Quwayq] has 
again exposed ‘Arafat and his clique’s false image. The new 
revolutionary movement, which is receiving broad backing and 
support, will make its way through and emerge victorious in 
order, in the end, to replace the traditional movement which has 
been led by a group of power seekers who trivialised the rev- 
olutionary struggle and the blood of the martyrs in order to build 
their rule on a tiny land such as the West Bank and Gaza Strip. 


Abu Salih, wko is enjoying a powerful position among the 
movement's rank, has warned “we will use the big stick against 
splitters who are causing a division in the movement ranks and 
trying to find justification to pull out of the forces from Leba- 
non. 


In the face of the continued uprising in Fatah ranks ‘Arafat finds 
himself hysterical and irrational, but, of course, he will pay

dearly for his mistakes. He might resort to a childish action or 
even fabricate accusations against the new revolutionary cadres 
of Fatah. 


Mr Jihad Salih, a revolutionary cadre from Fatah, has warned 
from such moves of ‘Arafat, and said that they are aimed at 
withdrawal of forces from Lebanon. The Arab and foreign press 
have unanimously agreed, in their reporting, that ‘Arafat had lost 
his influence over the movement. “This previous support have 
been very patient within but their patience has run out when they 
realised that ‘Arafat was not going to change. His withdrawal 
from Beirut, leaving the camp behind him without defence, will 
not be forgotten or pass without punishment.” 


The emergence of Fatah revolutionary movement will furhter 
expose ‘Arafat stands and intentions before the Palestinian and 
Arab peoples. 


How could ‘Arafat have the courage to announce that all weap- 
ons found in the bunker in Beirut were Libyan? Had the weapons 
{word indistinct] brought into Beirut by Libya he would have 
destroyed these weapons instantly. 


The Palestinian fighters announced yesterday that they would 
disobey ‘Arafat orders and insisted on staying in Lebanon to fight 
side by side with the Lebanese fighters to force the Israeli forces 
out of Lebanon.
