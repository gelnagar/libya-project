Summary (AI): An AP report from 24 April 1986 says Libyan Information Minister Mohammed Sharafeddin accused the U.S. and Israel of planning terrorist attacks in Europe to blame on Libya, days after the US air raids. It also describes Libyan efforts to manage the foreign journalists allowed in after the raids.
• Sharafeddin said the alleged attacks would be similar to the West Berlin nightclub bombing and were meant to justify further attacks on Libya.
• Foreign journalists in Tripoli were taken to bomb-damaged civilian areas and staged media events to show the US raid had hit mostly civilian parts of the city.
• Khadafy held a news conference for Soviet-bloc journalists but did not meet Western journalists.
• Speakes said the White House had no information that Khadafy had lost control or was sharing power with military officers.
• 1986-04-05: Bombing of the La Belle disco in West Berlin, which killed a U.S. Army sergeant and a Turkish woman and injured 230 people; the U.S. says Libya planned it (2 killed)
• 1986-04-23: Libyan officials told some foreign journalists in Tripoli to leave the country; the order appeared to be reversed after protests
