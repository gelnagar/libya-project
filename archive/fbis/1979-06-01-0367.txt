AL-MAQSI, BENIN'S ALLADAYE-SPEAK AT START OF TRIPOLI TALKS 
1D311018 Tripoli JANA in English 0835 GMT 31 May 79 LD 
[Text] Tripoli, May 31 JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Meetings of the joint Libyan-Arab- 
Beninese committee of bilateral economic, technical and cultural cooperation opened 
here at the conference hall on the Foreign Secretariat yesterday. 
In his opening speech, Dr 'Umar Ahmad al-Maqsi, secretary of the General People's Come 
mittee for Light Industry, welcomed Major Michel Alladaye, the Beninese minister of 


foreign affairs and cooperation,who is chairing the Beninese side, 


Dr al-Maqsi saids "We, in the Jamahiriyah, are eager to promote cooperation with Benin, 
for the people of our two countries are linked by historic and cultural ties," 


He added that Major Alladye's visit would have an effective role in stréngthening 
bilateral relations under the leadership of Colonal Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and President 
Mathieu Kerekou of Benin. 

Major Alladaye expressed his pleasure at his visit which he said would realize the two 
countries common aims and his appreciation for, the noble stands of the lst of September 
revolution under the leadership of Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi towards liberation move. 


ments not only in Africa but all over the world. 


He said that the Beninese Government and people have a great admiration for these hon- 
ourable stands, 


He then concluded by thanking the Jamahiriyah people for their warm welcome and hospitality.
