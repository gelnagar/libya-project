LIBYA

AL@QADHDHAFI RECEIVES GUYANA PRIME MINISTER BURNHAM 
ID171822 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 17 Jan 80 ID 


{fext] The leader of the great 1 September Revolution this morning received Mr Forbes 
Burnham, the prime minister of Guyana. He expressed his happiness at meeting the leader 
of the revolution and explained the current situation in the Caribbean countries and the 
economic and political pressure to which the peoples of the region are subjected. He 
also proposed to the leader of the revolution various aspects of cooperation which could 
be established between the Jamahiriyah and Guyana. Brother Mu*ammar al-Qadhdhafi gave 

a full account of the third universal theory to the prime minister, explaining how the 
Libyan Arab people are exercising direct and true democracy in accordance with the proe- 
visions of the Green Book, where heralded the liberation of mankind from all domination, 
control, exploitation and enslavement practised by man against his brother under hollow 
and false slogans.

AL-WATAN: ALeQADHDHAFI PREPARING FOR EGYPTIAN AGGRESSION 
ID171641 Kuwait AL-WATAN in Arabic 15 Jan 80 pp 1, 11 WD 


[Exclusive report: "Imminent EgyptianeIsraeli Aggression Against Libya; Al-Qadhdhafi in 
Bengazi To Make Preparations and As-Sadat Is Waiting for Signal From Carter"] 


[Text] Tripoli--Wili the EgyptianeLibyan Front flare up? This questions is being asked 
by recent visitors to Libya. They base their question on the following three facts: 


1. Col Mutammar aleQadhdhafi has been absent from Tripoli and staying in Bengazi for over 
a month now. Such a long absence is unprecedented. Col al#Qadhdhafi did not participate 
in the recent People's Congress in Tripoli. According to some information, Colonel 
aleQadhdhafi will return from Bengazi today, Tuesday. Other information has it that his 
stay in Bengazi could be a long one. 


2. Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis is staying in Bengazi with Colonel al-Qadhdhafi--something 
unusual. According to available information, Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis, who is responsible 
for the armed forces, had accompanied Colonel al-Qadhdhafi to inspect the Libyan forces 
near the border with Egypt to reassure himself of their fighting capabilities and insure 
their military requirements. 


The information also says that Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis have drawn 
up a defense plan, in case the reports which say that President Anwar as#Sadat will repeat 
his aggression against Libya in the next few days prove to be ture. 


The information points out that the aggression was scheduled to take place 6 months ago, 
but President Carter did not give the goeahead for it at the time; nor did he rule it out 
as a possibility. Following the exacerbation of the hostages crisis and the Soviets!® 
opening of the Afghanistan front, President as-Sadat and Israeli Prime Minister Menahem 
Begin think that President Carter will reconsider the idea and might give the goeahead 

to Egypt to launch an aggression against Libya with Israeli support. This question was 
discussed during the As#SadateBegin meeting in Aswan a few days 8g0. 


3. In light of the report shout these possible aggressive tendencies, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi 
has decided to put fully prepared aircraft squadrons in the air and prepare for possible 
war. Meanwhile, Al-Qadhdhafi showed flexibility toward the United States during his meeting 
with American newsmen. He also fabricated the existing crisis with the PLO and Fatah in 
order to upset some international plans and distract attention from the task he has 

started in Bengazi and in the areas where the Libyan forces are present. 


Persons close to AleQadhdhafi emphasizes that his "war" with Fatah is a tactical one and 
that understanding it as a strategic contradiction has served his aim. 


In the meantime, it has been observed that Libya has not taken a stand regarding the 
Soviet invasion of Afghanistan--a matter which is inconsistent with Colonel al-Qadhdhafi'ts 
convictions. A stand has not been taken because the possibility of the EgyptianeLibyan 
front exploding will require a historic stand by the Soviet Union in support of Libya. 

For this reason, the required Libyan stand has been shelved indefinitely.
