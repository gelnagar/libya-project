Honecker Arrival Statement

(Statement by Erich Honecker, chairman of the GDR State Council, on arrival at Tripoli 
Airport on 15 February--recorded, in German followed by Arabic translation] 


{Text] I am very happy to have this cordial meeting in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah, to be met by my friend and brother Al-Qadhdhafi. We are pleased at this 
opportunity. Fraternal relations exist between our two peoples and our two countries and 
we could further deepen these relations. I believe this is the most important result of 
our visit; because in the most important international issues we have common stands in the 
struggle for peace, against neocolonialism and racism, for disarmament and also on various 
levels in many other fields. Our visit will give us the opportunity to continue to ex- 
change views, which we began 8 months ago to ow mutual benefit and for the consolidation 
of peace, I would like to take the opportunity of ow’ arrival here to greet the people of 
the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, to convey to them the warmest greetings 

of the people of the GDR, Thank you, 


Honecker, Al-Qadhdhafi Meeting 
LD152100 East Berlin ADN International Service in German 1641 GMT 15 Feb 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli (ADN correspondent) --Erich Honecker and Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi had a 
friendly meeting at the People's Palace in Tripoli this afternoon. Afterward the dele- 
gations headed by them began their official talks. At the meeting of the two statesmen 
it was noted with satisfaction that the friendly cooperation between the two states has 
continued its stable development and that the fighting alliance against imperialism, 
colonialism and racism has been consolidated. At the official talks of the two dele- 
gations, identical views on fundamental questions of international affairs were noted, 
The discussions dwelt on bilateral questions as well as on international problems, in 
particular the situation in Europe and the Middle East, 


During their exchange of views which was conducted in a friendly atmosphere, both sides 
noted identical views on fundamental questions of international affairs. During the 
official talks they expressed their resolve to strengthen and consolidate the cooperation 
and solidarity among the states of the socialist community, the nonalined countries and 
the national liberation movements still further. The two sides affirmed the need to take 
a consistent stand to strengthen peace and security of the peoples, to end the arms race 
and bring about disarmament, to liquidate the hotbeds of tension and the remnants of 
colonialism, and to promote international cooperation on terms of equality.

Dinner Toasts 


LD160406 East Berlin ADN International Service in German 2110 GMT 15 Feb 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--(ADN correspondent)--The members of the diplomatic corps accredited in 
Libya were pregented in Tripoli on Thursday to Erich Honecker, the secretary general of 
the SED Central Committee and chairman of the GDR State Council. Erich Honecker, Willi 
Stoph, Hermann Axen, Guenter Mittag and Oskar Fischer engaged in friendly conversation 
with the doyen of the diplomatic corps, the ambassador of the Italian Republic and other 
diplomats from yarious European,African and Asian countries. 


At the end of the first day of the visit, Colonel Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi gave a dinner in 
honor of Erich Honecker which was attended by the members of the GDR Party and state dele- 
gation and leading Libyan public figures. In the course of the friendly gathering, the 
Libyan and GDR heads of state paid tribute to the development of relations between the two 
countries and affirmed their common ground in the anti-imperialist struggle for peace and 
freedom of the peoples, and for social progress. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi expressed his pleasure at Erich Honecker's visit and recalled his own 
visit to the GDR last year which likewise was evidence of the friendship between the 
peoples of the two countries. The GDR and Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, 

he said proposing a toast, are among the states standing for a just peace and fruitful 
cooperation between the peoples. Both states and peoples have fought jointly against 
colonialism, racism and Zionism. 


The Libyan statesman paid tribute to the recent events in Iran as a victory of the Iranian 
people's revolution. He expressed his conviction that in Africa, too, the peoples would 
achieve victory over racism and reaction. 


In conclusion.Colonel al-Qadhdhafi described the relations between Libya and the GDR as 
an example of how the peoples could jointly fight in a single front against imperialism 
despite different social systems and ideologies. Erich Honecker's visit was a further 
important step for deepening the cooperation between the two countries. 


In his toast Erich Honecker affirmed that the friendly relations between the GDR and 
Libya are developing with stability and energy. Both countries are continuing to develop 
them in the spirit of anti-imperialist solidarity on the basis of complete equality. The 
exchange of views in Tripoli has shown once again that the GDR and Libya are allies in 
the struggle for joint aims in the building of a new society, a better world free from 
imperialist expoitation and oppression, 


Ve. 16 Feb 79 NORTH AFRICA 


Both are united in the desire to pursue the constant strengthening of the anti-imperialist 
alliance of the states of the socialist community and the progressive states of Africa and 
Asia which have achieved their national liberation. 


Concerning the Middle East problem Erich Honecker said that genuine peace can be achieved 
only if the safeguarding of the inalienable national rights of the Arab people of Palestine, 
including its right to establish a state of its own, Israel's withdrawal from all Arab 
territories occupied in 1967, and the independent existence and security of all states in 
this region are organically interlinked. This calls for collective erforts of all directly 
interested parties within the framework of the international bodies provided for this 
purpose. The GDR welcomed the basic position of the summit conferences of the Front of 
Steadfastness and Confrontation in Tripoli, Algiers and Damascus. It supported the de- 
cisions of the Baghdad summit conference of the heads of state and government of Arab 
countries. 


CONSTRUCTION COOPERATION PROTOCOL SIGNED WITH CUBA 
LD152040 Tripoli JANA in English 1655 GMT 15 Feb 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, February 15, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Mr. Muhammad al-Manqush, housing 
secretary in the Jamahiriyah and Mr. Levi Farah, the Cuban minister president of the state 
committee for construction, signed a joint protocol stipulating cooperation in various 
fields of construction and the participation of Cuban establishments in erecting housing 
units and executing some projects for roads, transportation, dams and water resources in 
the Jamahiriyah. The two sides also agreed on establishing a Libyan-Cuban company for 
construction. 


Attending the signing were the Libyan under-secretary of housing, the assistant-director 
of the National Bank of Cuba, the Cuban ambassador to the Jamahiriyah and a representative 
from the cooperation department at the Foreign Secretariat.
