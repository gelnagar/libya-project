Replying to a question on the L.A.R.'s relations with Egypt and Tunisia and claims of

without paying attention to thelr genuine will, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said.

indicated that the recognition will impose itself in the future.

"countries which own nuclear bombs also possess nuclear power unequivalent to their areas

Talking on the Palestinian issue and the Zionist presence in occupled Palestine, Colonel
