Summary (AI): A New York Times article reports a federal indictment, made public on 23 October 1981, of a Virginia businessman accused of shipping explosives to Libya and of a training project in Libya led by two former CIA agents, Edwin P. Wilson and Frank E. Terpil. The article gives no Tripoli-specific sites.
• A 10-count federal indictment was made public on 23 October 1981.
• The indictment charged the 38-year-old Virginian with shipping explosives to Libya and training, headed by two former CIA agents.
• The charges grew out of testimony of newly cooperating witnesses, according to Assistant U.S. Attorney Carol E. Bruce.
• The original April 1980 indictment of Wilson and Terpil listed an explosives shipment in August 1976.
• 1977-04: Shipment of explosives to Libya added to charges against Edwin P. Wilson and Frank E. Terpil in the new indictment
