ROULEAU ON LIBYAN SWING TO SOVIET ALLIANCE 
PM0O91553 Paris LE MONDE in French 8 Sep 81 ppl, 4 


[Dispatch by Erich Rouleau: "Colonel al-Qadhdhafi Moves Toward Alliance With 
Moscow’ ] 


[Text] Tripoli--The decision has been taken at the highest level. Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi, his closest comrades and the armed forces’ chiefs have agreed that 
Libya should propose signing a treaty of friendship and mutual defense with the 
USSR. The Jamahiriyah is prepared to grant "facilities," possibly bases, to the 
Soviet fleet, according to Libyan and foreign sources. This would, therefore, 
mark the end of the "positive neutralism" pursued by the Libyan Jamahiriyah since 
its installation barely 12 years ago. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, for his part, resigned himself to this "agonizing change," 

on the evening of 19 August a few hours after the destruction of two of his fighter 
planes by aircraft from the 6th fleet in the Gulf of Sidra. The news, which 
reached him in al-Dali in South Yem en, both humiliated and outraged hin. 

Mr 'Ali Nasir Muhammad, president of the [People's Democratic] Republic of Yemen, 
who was dining with him, remarked: "The treaty which binds us to the USSR is not 
only no embarrassment to us but has safeguarded our independence and our territorial 
integrity." This remark did not fall on deaf ears. The aerial duel over the 
Jamahiriyah's "territorial waters"' took place the same day as the signing in Aden 
of a mutual defense treaty between Libya, South Yemen and Ethiopia. Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi saw the "provocation" as an additional warning: The United States 

was determined to overthrow his regime. 


On 5 June the Libyan president had revealed his fears on this subject to Lebanese 
Communist Party Secretary General Georges Hawi. Two days previously the State 
Department spokesman had advocated "military aid'' for African countries which 
"want to oppose Colonel al-Qadhadhafi's adventurism." 


fhe Americans, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out to his interlocutor, had already 
established bases in two neighboring countries, Egypt and Sudan, which were massing 
troops on the Libyan and Chadian borders. At the same time Washington was seeking 
to "economically stifle" the Jamahiriyah. Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said he had docu- 
ments which proved that NATO members and some African states had been asked by 

the United States to boycott Libya or at least to cut their trade with Tripoli.

fhe most serious matter, the revolutionary leader went on to confide in the 
Lebanese communist leader, is that the United States had considerably reduced its 
import of Libyan "crude."" Indeed it has been confirmed that American companies 
have not renewed their purchasing contracts, forcing the Jamahiriyah to reduce 
its production from 1.6 million barrels per day to only 600,000. The estimated 
drop in income for the year from $33 to 7 billion threatens to lead to the cancella- 
tion of the new 1981-1985 5-year plan. Washington's main objective is clearly to 
neutralize the “center of international terrorism" which, in its view, the 
Jamahiriyah constitutes. Indeed the latter supports--particularly by means of 
petrodollars--a vast range of nationalist movements and leftwing parties in 
Central and South America, in Europe, Africa, Asia and particularly in the Near 
Fast. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi unwittingly bore out Washington's anxieties by stating in 

his speech on 1 September: "Libya is certainly negligible compared with the power 
of the United States. But it is the grain of sand which puts the machine out of 
order, the stone which prevents the kidney from working, the virus which threatens 
to paralyze the organism...."

A Line-up of Revolutionary Movements 


the line-up of representatives of countries and movements who took part in the 
celebration of the anniversary of the Libyan revolution on 1 September or in the 
"solidarity conference" which preceeded it, was indeed impressive: more than 

1,000 prominent people, heads of state, ministers, and political leaders came from 
85 countries to express their support for Colonel al-Qadhdhafi or their "gratitude" 
for his aid. The latter included Mr Daniel Ortega, coordinator of the Sandinist 
junta in Nicaragua, who hailed Libya as an “international center for struggle against 
imperialism and racism."" To which Colonel al-Qadhdhafi replied: "America, for 

its part, is the queen of terrorism; it terrorizes peoples who resist imperialist 
domination and who are simply seeking a place in the sun....” 


After listening to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi giving vent to his grievances against the 
United States on 5 June, the Lebanese Communist Party secretary general made him 
an unusual proposal: the signing of a defense treaty with the Marxist governments 
of South Yemen and Ethiopia, both "surrounded" by American bases (in Sudan, Somali 
and Oman) and by the 7th fleet and both "isolated" in an ocean of hostility. 

This immediately struck Colonel al-Qadhdhafi as a good idea and Mr Georges Hawi 
was entrusted with an exploratory mission. 


The coming and going between Tripoli, Aden and Addis Ababa by the communist leader 
who was accompanied by Mr Taysir Qubba-ah, a leader of the Palestine People's 
Liberation Front, and at times by South Yemen head of state Mr Nasir, took place 

in the greatest secrecy. Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's righthand man, ‘Abd al-Salam 
fallud, who visited Addis Ababa secretly initialled the text of the treaty in 
mid-July and the text took on its final form on 6 August at an equally secret 
meeting in Tripoli between the three countries’ foreign ministers. One week before, 
the American press reported a CIA plan--which the White House was to deny--to 

mount a "large-scale operation" to overthrow the Libyan regime, involving

Mr al-Qadhdhafi's assassination.

Nonetheless, the Libyan revolution leader does not regard the tripartite treaty 

as an "all-risks insurance."" In his speeches on 11 June and 1 September Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi held out his hand to Europe and to all "progress. ve or revolutionary" 
Arab countries to face the "American threat." He reestablished diplomatic relations 
with Morocco and proposed an amicable settlement with the Saharan nationalists. 

He even restored relations with Iraq, to some extent sacrificing his preferential 
relations with Imam Khomeyni's Iran and he is now trying to effect a reconciliation 
with Saudi Arabia's royal family whom he formerly doomed to the "trash cans of 
history,’ and he hopes to reach an agreement with Saudi Arabia which would make 

it possible to cut Riyadh's oil production and consequently restore the level of 
exports of Libyan "crude." 


Overtures to France 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's attempt to divide Europe from the United States was, to say 
the least, clumsy, according to a fairly widespread view in diplomatic circles 

in Tripoli. His threat, although all talk, to take reprisals by bombing the 
American bases on the Mediterranean shores, thus inflicting a "nuclear disaster" 
on the old continent certainly prompted more smiles than gnashing of teeth 

among experienced observers. 


The overtures to France were less clumsy. While Colonel al-Qadhdhafi made no 
criticism of Paris in his 1 September speech, the Libyan mass media are singing 

the praises of President Mitterrand and the "socialist government." It will be 
remembered that the latter, in a goodwill gesture, has restarted work on the 
civilian and military contracts "frozen" by the previous administration last January. 


Nonetheless Colonel al-Qadhdhafi remains intransigent on the Chadian affair, which 
increased tension between Paris and Tripoli in the wake of the backing of the 
French Embassy. The embassy is now being reconstructed at the Libyan Government's 
expense. But Colonel al-Qadhdhafi continues to justify the presence of his troops 
in Chad, and maintained once again this 1 September, that his southern neighbor 

! 


constitutes a "strategic buffer zone" for Libya. However, a compromise may not be 
impossible. 


Mr Gonkouni Oueddei, president of Chad's national unity transition government, 
who is the man most concerned in this affair, and who is due to have talks with 
the French leaders in Paris in 10 days' time, does not seem to share the Libyan 
president's views. In his speech in Tripoli on 1 September, Mr Goukouni showed 
noticeable reservations: He said nothing about geopolitical interest justifying 
the presence of Libyan troops in Chad. 


Whatever the outcome of Tripoli's diplomatic offensive toward the Arabo-African 
world and toward Europe, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi is quite clearly determined to take 
his place if not inside at least alongside the socialist camp which, in his view, 
‘onstitutes the surest guarantee of the Jamahiriyah's survival. The path he is 
preparing to take is very like the one chosen by his illustrious predecessors in 
the Third World, notably Jamal ‘Abd al-Nasir and Fidel Castro whom he makes no 
secret of emulating. The change cannot have been easy for a man who portrays 
himself as the champion of the "third way," and who went co fai as to refuse to

allow Soviet warships to make mere courtesy visits to Libyan ports. His 1 September 
speech sounded like a bitter self-criticism: "We are being forced," he stated, 

“to form a military alliance with anybody prepared to defend us against America. 
There can be no neutralism in wartime. Neutralists would then be merely cowards

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi has not announced the decision taken secretly to immediately 
open preliminary negotiations with the USSR whose name he did not even mention 
during his 3-hour speech. He will need time--his entourage maintains--to prepare 
Libyan opinion and the Libyan armed forces for this political and ideological about- 
face. He will take action after gaining the approval of a "people's congress" 
which will be convened before the end of this year.

[Excerpt] Tripoli, 9 Sep (Jamahiriyah News Agency)--The revolution's leader has 
received the ambassadors of Turkey, Greece, Italy and Spain accredited to the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


The meeting came in the wake of the escalating campaign of distortions being waged 
by the U.S. administration through its propaganda apparatus and various right wing 
and reactionary papers in order to distort the Libyan stance and to undermine and 
sabotage Libya's relations with friendly Mediterranean countries. This is being 
done by inserting lies into the revolution leader's speech during the celebration 
marking the great al-Fateh revolution's 12th anniversary, and by covering up the 
true stance followed by the al-Fateh revolution in its affirmation of its legitimate 
right to defend the Libyan Arab people's freedom to confront the American imperialist 
onslaught. These lies are part of the conspiracies and attempts by the U.S. 
administration, in cooperation with puppet regimes, to undermine and sabotage the 
Libyan people's relations with friendly peoples. 


The four friendly countries’ ambassadors were unanimous, in statements they made 
after meeting with the revolution's leader, in asserting that their countries 
rejected any aggressive stand taken by America against Jamahiriyah. 


[hey were also umanimous in stating their countries’ refusal to permit America to 
use their lands in any aggressive act against Libya.

[he ambassadors said that all officials in their respective governments appreciated

AL-QADHDHAFI ATTENDS MILITARY GRADUATION CEREMONY

(Editorial report] Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic at 0645 GMT on 30 August 
broadcast a live relay of the officers graduation ceremony at the Libyan Military 
Academy. At 0658 CMT, the announcer said that Colonel al-Qadhdhafi had arrived 
at the military academy a short while before the ceremony started. 


At 0703 GMT, medals and ribbons were awarded to the military academy's graduates 

and afterward the commander of the academy delivered a speech in which he saluted 

al-Qadhdhafi and thanked him for attending the ceremony. The commander went on 

to exhort the graduates to carry out their duties and to enhance their revolutionary 

concepts and aspirations for liberation. He concluded by reminding them that they | 
are the soldiers of the revolution and that the struggle will continue until its 
goal is attained. | 


Following the military academy commander's speech, a military parade was staged, 
and then the announcer said: 


"Let America and its stooges in the region know that the people of the Arab 
Jamahiriyah are armed and strong and will repel the attackers. Oh brothers! The 
criminal aggression carried out by American imperialism by attacking two Libyan 
aircraft is part of the American design against the people. This is a criminal 
stance." 


At 0730 GMT, the outstanding graduates were awarded prizes, after which the announcer 
said: 


“Now, a document written in blood will be handed over. This is a pledge of 
allegiance to the leader. It says: My master, the leader: We the graduates of 
the Libyan Military Academy are happy, at this perilous stage through which our 
nation is passing, to write this document in our blood. We hereby pledge to be 
loyal soldiers and honorable strugglers. [Passage indistinct] The only way to 
restore the Arab honor which has been wounded, the rights which have been usurped 
and the honor which has been trampled on is to achieve unity and be ready to die 
for it. We will strive to achieve overall unity. We are prepared to crush any 
fascist attempt against our leader. We will liquidate reactionary agents wherever 
they may be, American imperialism and treacherous Zionist gangs. We write our 
pledge in blood." 


At 0741 GMT, the graduating officers took the oath of allegiance to President 
al-Qadhdhafi and the country and then the guests attending the graduation ceremony 
left the military compound where the ceremony was held. The live relay ended at 
0748 GMT.
