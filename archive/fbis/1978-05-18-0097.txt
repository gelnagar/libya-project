LIBYA

PDRY MINISTER ARRIVES WITH MESSAGE FOR AL-QADHDHAFI 


LD171134Y Tripoli JANA in English 0850 GMI 17 May 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, May 17, JANA--Mr Mahmud ‘Abdallah 'Ushaysh, the minister of transport 
{as received] of the People's Democratic Republic of Yemen, yesterday arrived in Tripoli 
International Airport, Mr Nuri al-Fayturi al-Madani, the transport secretary, the under- 
secretary and representatives from the Arab and cooperation departments of the Foreign 
Secretariat. [sentence as received] 


V. 18 May 78 I 3 NORTH AFRICA 


The Yemeni minister has affirmed that his visit to the Jamahiriyah is within the frame- 
work of contacts and the exchange of views between the steadfastness and confrontation 
front countries. 


He praised, in a statement to the corre pondent of JANA, the relations that link the 
Jamahiriyah to democratic Yemen. He said: "They are good and solid relations embodied 
in the continued confrontation of the reactionary schemes, in the Arab region, aimed 

at harming the Palestinian revolution," 


He added that he was carrying a letter to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, secretary-general of the 
General People's Congress, from the secretary-general of the United Political Organi- 
zation--the National Front in Yemen, Mr ‘Abd al-Fattah Isma'il.
