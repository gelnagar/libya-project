LIBYA

AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR on U:S. Raid in Lebanon 
LDO71047 Tripoli JANA in English 0554 GMT 7 Dec 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Awal 2, December 7, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR, the organ of 
the Revolutionary Committees in the Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, 
condemned the widescale attack of the U.S. military planes last 
Sunday on Arab Syrian [words indistinct] and which was a 
practical translation of the strategic alliance agreement between 
US. and Zionist entity. 


The U.S. crusade and Zionist forces have begun to put to practice 
the first article of that agreement in an attempt to bring steadfast 
Syria down on its knees. 


Political observers confirm that the premeditated U.S. attacks 
against Syrian forces will be followed by an outright attack by 
the Zionist forces, in line with the crusade-Zionist plan, agreed 


upon by the [word indistinct], Reagan and Shamir, in Washing- 
ton. 


To hit Syria means to hit the last bastion of steadfastness in the 
eastern Arab world and means opening the way for the extended 
plans of U.S.-Zionist alliance which aims at controlling the Arab 
world. 


According to U.S.-Zionist plans, once Syria is out of the way, 
and by the treacherous agreement of 17 May signed by the 
Phalange authority in Lebanon with the Zionist entity, Lebanon 
will be transformed into a base for the imperialist Zionist alliance 
and the bridgehead for occupying the rest of the eastern Arab 
world. 


The active forces in the Arab world, headed by Jamahiriyah, 
when they warned against the danger of this strategic alliance 
signed between U.S. and the Zionist entity last week and said 
that this was a declaration of war against the Arab nation, were 
fully aware of what they were talking about. Thus the following 
days are proving this truth of these statements even before the 
ink with which the agreement was signed, dried. 


The U:S. and the Zionist entity are the two sides of the same coin. 
The Zionist entity, since its inception in 1948, was the bridgehead 
for the U.S. in implementing its imperialist plans in the Arab 
region. The strategic alliance between U.S. and the Zionists is 
nothing new, it has always existed. Except that under the new 
agreement, occupied Palestine will be transformed into a ware- 
house for the most advanced and dangerous strategic U.S. arms 
including nuclear arms. 


The attack against Syria came to expose to all those who believed 
in U.S. neutrality and in its role to bring so-called peace to the 
Middle East, the truth about U.S. policies. 


Syria’s heroic stand and the fact that it brought down three U.S. 
military planes confirms to the Arab world that it does possess 
the resources for confrontation and coming out of it victorious, if 
it had the willingness to fight and determination to win. The U.S. 
is not the force that cannot be defeated. It was in the past by the 
Vietnamese people and the Arab nation is not less courageous 
than the Vietnamese people. 


The AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR insisted that the U.S. attack on 
Syrian forces is the warning bell for all Arabs from the ocean to 
the Gulf.
