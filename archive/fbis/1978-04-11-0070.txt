LIBYA

PEOPLE'S CONGRESSES ANNOUNCE RESOLUTIONS 


LDO91710Y Tripoli JANA in English 1440 GMT 9 Apr 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, April 9, JANA--The General Secretariat of the People's General Congress 
has announced the People's Congress resolutions on the three issues which had been 
referred for their decision:

First: provisions regarding the ownership of buildings and land: 182 people's congresses 
approved the memorandum submitted by the General Secretariat of the People's General 
Congress (98.08 percent). 


A--Four people's congresses did not approve (01,82 percent). 47 people's congresses 
approved recompensation for the land (25.27 percent). 


B--17 people's congresses approved the recompensation for lands, real estates purchased, 
inherited and entered into the register (9.14 percent). 


C--One people's congress has decided on maintaining the present status of lands. 


D--Four people's congresses approved introduction of material penalty of confiscation 
of estates without compensation instead of the penalty of imprisonment, 


E--23 people's congresses approved the observation of numerality of wives and the size 
of family. 


F--16 people's congresses agreed upon assigning ownership to children. 
Second: conscription: 184 people's congresses approved the memorandum submitted by the 
General Secretariat of the People's General Congress (98.92 percent); two people's 


congresses did not approve (1.08 percent), 


Third: supporting Syria: Regarding the support of Syria the people's congresses 
resolutions were as follows; 


General support; 6 people's congresses conditional support; 133 people's congresses 
and the conditions of support are: 


A--To enter the war for the liberation of the land; 

B--To abide by the covenants of the countries of steadfastness and confrontation; 
C--To protect the Palestinians and support the resistance; 

D--To allow the fedayeen to operate from within Syrian territories; 


E--To allow the armies of the steadfastness nd confrontation countries to move into 
Syrian territories; 


F--To support the progressive forces in Lehanon; 

G--To withdraw the recognition of the decision of tne Security Council; 
H--To reject the presence [of] international peace keeping forces; 

I--The participation of the Libyan leadership in the planning of the battle; 


J--Criticizing Syria for its negative stand towards the latest Zionist aggiession 
against southern Lebanon; 


K--To give material and military help only when the above points are agreed to by Syria; 


3--Financial and military support were approved by 40 people's congresses. 


4j-No support: 7 people's congresses.

PRESIDENT OF CHAD ARRIVES FOR VISIT 
LDO81322Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 amt 8 apr 78 LD 


[Excerpts] The president of the Republic of Chad, Felix Malloum, and the delegation accom- 
vanying him arrived at Tripoli airport this morning on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. He 

was welcomed at the airport by Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud. President Felix 
Malloum is accompanied by the ministers of justice and agriculture. 


In a statement to a JANA representative he said: Since the historic Sabha declaration I 
am committed, together with the African people, to work for the realization of Chad's 
interest. I confirm that the Jamahiriyah and Chad are marching along a true fraternal 
course that stems from the interests of the African Continent. 


Meets With Al-Qadhdhafi 
LDOQ1L646Y Tripoli JANA in English 1452 GMT 9 Apr 78 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, April 9, JANA--A lengthy meeting was held this morning between Col. Mu'ammar 
ai-Qadhdhafi and Felix Malloum, president of Chad. In this meeting the two leaders discussed 
the progress of implementation of the national reconciliation in Chad as agreed in the 

Sabha and Benghazi meetings. 


President Malloum thanked the Jamahiriyah and Colonel al-Qadhdhafi for the great efforts 
exerted for the realization of national conciliation in Chad.

Departs 10 April 
LD101927Y Tripoli JANA in English 1825 GMT 10 Apr 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, April 10, JANA--President Felix Malloum of Chad left Tripoli this morning 
after a visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


The president was seen off at the airport by Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Jallud. At the 
airport the two national anthems were played; then the president inspected the guards. 

Before leaving, the president said to the JANA correspondent that he had expressed his thanks 
and appreciation for Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi's sincere and honorable efforts and 
sacrifices for the sake of the African Continent's prosperity. 


"We have exchanged our points of view on bilateral relations, on Africa's problems and on 
the world's problems in a friendly and fully cooperative atmosphere. I would like to take 
this opportunity while leaving the Jamahiriyah to thank my brother Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
once again and to express my appreciation to the Arab Libyan people for their warm reception 
and hospitality," said President Malloum.
