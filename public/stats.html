How these numbers are worked out

AI: every answer's token use is priced (PRICE_IN / PRICE_OUT, default $2 and $10 per million tokens, $0.01 per web search) and logged when it happens. Your Anthropic console has the exact bill. Cloudflare: on the Workers Free plan nothing is ever billed; past 100,000 Worker requests in a day, further requests fail until midnight UTC instead of costing money. Map pages, pictures and data files are served without the Worker and are free and unlimited. If you move to Workers Paid, set CF_PLAN to "paid": $5 a month, including 10 million requests, then $0.30 per million. Email: Resend's free plan covers 3,000 emails a month (100 a day). Worker request counts here are the Worker's own tally; Cloudflare's dashboard has the official figure.
