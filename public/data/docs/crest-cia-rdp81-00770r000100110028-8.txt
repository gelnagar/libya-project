Summary (AI): A September 1969 US embassy cable (Bronson/Chandlee) about Libyan state radio reception. It recommends citing "Bayda Domestic Service" as the sourceline, since Bayda is now the capital, and notes a relay station on 1394 kcs had gone silent. Nothing here concerns Tripoli directly.
• Libyan radio service identified broadcasting in parallel on 7165, 1052, 1484 and 674 kcs at various times.
• Cable recommends using 'Bayda Domestic Service' rather than the previous source line because Bayda is now the capital.
• Station relaying Libyan domestic programs on 1394 kcs had not been heard for the last two days.
