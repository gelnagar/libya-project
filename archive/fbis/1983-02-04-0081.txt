LIBYA

LE MATIN REPORTS AL-QADHDHAFI INTERVIEWS 


PMO21511 Paris LE MATIN in French 24 Jan 83 pp 14, 15 

[Excerpts from series of interviews with Libyan chief of state Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
by unnamed LE MATIN journalist, NEWSWEEK's Mark Whittaker, and JEUNE AFRIQUE's Hamid 
Barrade in Tripoli -- date not given] 


[Text] The Arab World's Deviation 


[Question] The Israeli invasion of Lebanon found a divided, impotent, and uninspired 
Arab world. That world failed to make itself felt during the war. Nor did it awaken 
afterward. Is there still a difference between "hardliners" and "moderates," "pro- 
gressives," and "reactionaries"? 


[Answer] I understand the anger of the Arab people's masses, particularly the anger 
of the Lebanese people, who have suffered so much. The Lebanese have the right to 
tear up photographs of Al-Qadhdhafi or Hafiz al-Asad (the Syrian president -- LE MATIN 
note). I understand that reaction which is directed at all Arab regimes without dis- 
tinction and does not spare Libya. Things cannot be different today. When the time 
for thought comes, people will understand that Libya's position was different from the 
others, that it has always opposed a cease-fire as long as an Arab territory is occu- 
pied, because it is our duty and right as Arabs to fight to the last man and to the 
last drop of our blood for the liberation of our land, to fight to the end to hound 
the occupier from Palestine. That is Libya's position. 


[Question] That sometimes led people in Lebanon -- and not in reputedly "reactionary" 
circles -- to say that Colonel al-Qadhdhafi was prepared for the Lebanese and 
Palestinians to fight to the last man for the cause.... 


[Answer] From the start of the Zionist invasion of Lebanon, I called for an Arab 
summit to be convened urgently so that we could find a joint response. Owing to their 
internal situations the other Arab countries were unable to respond to my appeal. 
Libyans died fighting in Lebanon. The Palestinians know how many weapons we sent them 
and how many weapons were seized by the Israelis without even being unpacked. Libya 
armed the Palestinian resistance and the Lebanese Nationalist Movement so that they 
could defend the Arab streets and Muslim streets against their enemies supported by the 
Israelis and Americans. But, in the final analysis, it was up to them alone whether or 
not they fought with our weapons. The masses will realize that one day.... 


I am with the masses; I am with the man in the street, with the oppressed, exploited, 
humiliated citizens throughout the world, with the people who have no power, arms or 
wealth. I am in the opposition ranks on a world scale. 


Africa for the Africans 


This slogan merely means that the days in which the superpowers thought they could 
freely plunder Africa, colonize it, and steal its wealth are over. The Western 
countries, Europe, and America, must realize that. But anybody who wishes to help 
Africa is welcome. We are the friends of those who come forward as friends and the 
enemies of those who show hostility. We are prepared to cooperate with France in a 
program from which Africa would benefit and even to establish a Franco-Libyan or 
Franco-Arab-Libyan policy to help Africa and ensure its recovery. What we reject is 
plunder and disdain.

[Question] What about Chad? 


[Answer] It is not true that we are planning another intervention in Chad. In 1980 

our forces entered Chad at the written request of that country's lawful government, the 
Transitional National Union Government [GUNT] headed by Goukouni Oueddei, and in keeping 
with international agreements. When Goukouni asked us to withdraw our army, we did so 
in 10 days. 


[Question] Libya still supports Goukouni.... 


[Answer] It is a fact that the only lawful government is the GUNT. Habre is merely a 
rebel minister. There are 11 Chadian factions outside the government, and they support 
Goukouni. 


[Question] So, what if Goukouni wrote to you again to request intervention? 


[Answer] He did so and we refused. I repeat that military intervention by us is very 
unlikely. We are not prepared to act as policemen or protectors for Chad. We there- 
fore do not want to intervene, but we also reject intervention by any other foreign 
power in Chad. The whole world should realize that for Libya the protection of the 
southern border is a priority security requirement. 


Liberation Movement or Terrorism 


These two notions have nothing in common, and the confusion has been deliberately main- 
tained by the Western media under Zionist influence to distract public attention from 
the peoples’ legitimate struggles for their liberation, particularly the Palestinian 
people's struggle. It was the Zionists who labeled the PLO "terrorist" and imposed 
that label on the.media. The United States parrots Zionist propaganda through its 
official channels and at the same time accuses Libya, which is supporting the peoples’ 
liberation struggles, of supporting terrorism. 


In our view, if there is an example of terrorism on an international scale, it is the 
United States, with its forces deployed throughout the world, its fleet in the 
Mediterranean, its repeated aggressions against peoples who want to liberate themselves 
from the imperialist hold in Latin America, in the Caribbean, and in Vietnam. We are 
victims of U.S. imperialism -- remember the Gulf of Sidra affair -- as the Arabs are 
victims of U.S.-armed Zionist terrorism. 


The real terrorism today is Zionism and its machinations against the Lebanese, Syrian, 
and Palestinian peoples. What are the massacres perpetrated in Sabra and Shatila if 
not terrorism? 


There is another aspect of the question -- that of the clandestine terrorist organiza- 
tions such as those which exist in Germany and Italy. It is not for me to judge those 
movements’ psychological, social, or political motives. I think it would be necessary 
to make a more detailed analysis of how they arose, how German society produced the 
Baader-Meinhof group, how Italian society was able to produce the Red Brigades. That 
did not happen by accident, and the factors which explain it should be sought in the 
structure and history of those societies. Nonetheless I completely condemn the methods 
used by those organizations -- the abductions, murders, and massacres. 


If those movements want to bring about revolution they must act openly and wage a real 
opposition struggle in their respective countries. We condemn organizations which 
indulge in terrorism and we cannot have any relations with them. They have nothing to 
do with national liberation movements. The Namibian and Palestinian peoples’ struggles 
are legitimate and just causes which we back and support.

Zionism, "Mankind's Enemy" 


Zionism's plan is to destroy or dominate the world. It is written clearlv in their 
book, they are God's chosen people and must therefore inherit the earth at the cost of 
destroying the other peoples, nations, and religions. Although they are Jews -- and it 
is known that Jews cannot convert -- some Zionists have become Christians and have 
infiltrated the clergy to dominate the church and destroy Christianity from within. 
They have done the same with Islam. From the start it was Jews converted to Islam who 
sparked off the big Muslim split between Shi'ites and Sunnis. That is a historically 
proven fact.... 


The Zionists specially educate young people in economic disciplines to undermine the 
world economy. You find Zionists everywhere in the biggest research institutes and 
the economics chairs in the biggest universities.... 


The economic crisis of the past few years, like the U.S. "black Thursday" of 1929, is 
the result of the proliferation of Zionists in treasury institutions, stock exchanges, 
oil companies, and money markets. All those sectors are dominated by Zionists. That 
is known and statistically proven. In modern language they are called Jews but they 
are Zionists and not Jews in the religious sense of the term.... 


Zionists change nationality to influence or direct the policy of the countries whose 
nationality they assume in favor of Zionism -- Kissinger for instance.... One of them 
may one day head the White House or the Kremlin, or take power in Bonn or Paris. That 
will be the beginning of the catastrophe, of the world's destruction.... 


A U.S. anti-Zionist revolution could save the United States from Zionism: if not it 
will be destroyed by it. That is Zionism's present plan, the same plan which failed 
before in Germany. In fact, although we utterly condemn Hitler for the massacre of the 
Jews, Hitler understood that plan, understood that the Zionists wanted to dominate 
Germany. [answer ends] 


Four days after Col al-Qadhdhafi made that statement, during our final interview we 
asked him whether he stood by what he had said, citing his own words about Hitler. We 
added that, despite his introductory remarks, such statements could only be read as a 
justification of the holocaust. This was his second answer: 


"I repeat that I am absolutely opposed to the massacres perpetrated by Hitler, opposed 
to any massive physical liquidation of Jewish or non-Jewish populations. I said that 
Hitler maintained that he had realized the danger wi ch the Jews represented for 
Germany -- as the Americans will one day realize the danger which Zionism poses for the 
United States -- and that he had apparently decided to sacrifice the Jews before they 
sacrificed Germany. I totally disagree with him, but I am relating a historical fact: 
We strongly condemn Hitler for what he did, but he did it because he had detected this 
danger." [answer ends] 


Zionists? Jews? Col al-Qadhdhafi's statements resembled a shortened version of the 
Protocol of the Elders of Zion and included all the European anti-Semitic propaganda 
cliches. What did he think of that? 


[Answer] The Jews, like the Arabs, are the main victims of Zionism. How could an Arab 
be anti-Semitic? Arabs and Jews are related. We have lived together for centuries. 

It was Zionism which divided us. It is the mortal enemy of both of us.... I predict 
an alliance in the future between Arabs, Americans, and Jews to combat Zionism. The 
Americans will soon realize and the Jews are already discovering that Zionism sacri- 
fices them and forces them to fight against the Arab nation against their wishes.

We support the Jewish pacifists who reject the war in Lebanon. The end of the Zionist 
state will mean the liberation of the Jews and Arabs. 


An Islamic Revolution 


Iran had fallen under U.S. domination, the country was despoiled, and the Iranian 
people were subjected to a frightful dictatorship. Revolution was inevitable. The 
people would have carried it out whether they had been Muslim or not. It was a 
revolution in an Islamic country that took place in Iran, but that does not mean that 
it is an Islamic revolution. 


[Question] That is not what Khomeyni says -- he is talking about an "Islamic Repub- 
lic", “Islamic laws," "Islamic punishments...." 


[Answer] Events in Iran must be analyzed from a revolutionary and not an Islamic 
viewpoint. People have been xecuted because they were the revolution's enemies and not 
for committing sins against Islam. We are not here to judge the Iranian revolution or 
the soundness of its actions, but, good or bad, they cannot be attributed to Islam. 


[Question] So there is no Islamic law? 


[Answer] Islamic legislation, Muslim inheritance, law, and even state are erroneous 
terms which have nothing to do with the reality of Islam. The laws which are said to 
be Islamic are civilian, temporal acts, drawn up by theologians who legislate in the 
name of or in the shadow of Islam. They are not Islamic laws but laws like those in 
any country, like the French laws. There is only one book in Islam -- it is the 
Koran.... 


We do not need any law, sect, or theologian to apply Islam: We open the Koran and 
find everything in it. The governmen and laws are matters of this world. [answer 
ends] 


The Koran is a code of morality, not a government formula, Al-Qadhdhafi said in sub- 
stance, expressing the view that there are "flagrant inconsistencies between Muslim 
legislation and the Koran." That is a rather unorthodox remark which give rise to 
irritation and grumbling in some Libyan Muslim circles. The opposition is sometimes 
demonstrated publicly and on a massive scale, as happened recently with regard to a 
proposal by Al-Qadhdhafi on a law making it possible to pronounce divorce by mutual 
consent. 


Lebanese Christians 


[Al-Qadhdhafi] "You tell me that the Lebanese Christians are fighting to safeguard 
their identity and to avoid being drowned in a Muslim entity as a deprived minority, 
That is a real feeling and I understand it. But the situation now prevailing which 
led to the tragedies which Lebanon experienced was produced by a society which should 
be analyzed from a philosophical viewpoint. This problem is the result of a fundamen- 
tal historical mistake made by a section of the Arabs. In my view the Christians 

in Lebanon and Syria are Arabs who embraced Christianity before Islam's birth. Some 
of them were later converted, others were not. That is the basic error which must 

be corrected. Arabs can only be Muslim, Jesus was a Jewish prophet who came to 
correct Moses’ law and path. His message was intended only for the sons of Israel.... 


Although the Lebanese Christians are now entitled to regard themselves as a threatened 
minority, they must understand that the solution does not lie with the Americans or 
Israelis or in the formation of militias or in a religious war. The solution is to 
embrace Islam. 


I hope that a new generation of Lebanese Christians will wake up to this shining and 
astonishing truth and will regain their true nationality by abandoning the religion of 
Jesus, which does not concern them.

Tribute to France 


When it began the French Revolution was truly Jamahiriyan. After 14 July 1789, when 
the people stormed the Bastille and the revolution spread throughout the country and 
ended the monarchy, people's congresses arose spontaneously throughout France. But 
there was a deviation, the bourgeoisie stole power, and the excesses of the terror led 
the country to the brink of dictatorship. That ended with Napoleon Bonaparte and with 
power held by one man, but France eventually became republican, thus inaugurating the 
age of republics in the world. 


In May 1968, when I myself was a student, I felt, even if you yourselves were not aware 
of it, that your movement was a return to the roots of the French Revolution. The 
Jamahiriyan principles are the same as those of the French students in 1968: power to 
the people; no democracy without People's Congresses; no representation because repre- 
sentation is deception; People's Committees everywhere. 


Those who embody Jamahiriyan thinking will eventually come together even if they are 
from different regions. Even your former President Giscard d'Estaing, although a 
rightwinger, wrote in his book "French Democracy" that the solution was government by 
everybody. Those are the French Revolution's principles. 


Up the "Greens" 


The Jamahiriyan system is the abolition of modern politics. It destroys the organs of 
pseudopolitical politics. Its aim is to bring complex societies back to their simple, 
natural bases, to their origins, in exactly the same way as you simplify a fraction or 
an equation. The world of politics is a world of hypocrisy, lies, and cynicism. The 
person who stands by moral values in that system is regarded as naive. Nonetheless, 
that is our position. We want relations with the rest of the world on a moral basis 
and always say what we think and do. We are going to destroy the world of dictator- 
ship and exploitation so that social life overtakes political life throughout the 
world. I think that is also the objective of the European pacifist, ecological, and 
alternative movements. It is a common starting point on which we can meet and discuss. 
I do not think there are any differences between the world "green movement" and the 
Libyan Jamahiriya, and moreover, that movement could have Libya as a base and even in- 
stall its headquarters in Tripoli. 


OFFICIAL NOT ‘DELEGATED’ TO MEET FRG MINISTER 


LD032125 Tripoli JANA in English 1633 GMT 3 Feb 83 


[Text] Bonn, Rabi’ Thani 19, Feb 3, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The French news agency 
quoted a statement by the West German Foreign Ministry as saying that the West German 

minister of state for foreign affairs, Jurgen Mulliman, had yesterday received Colonel 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi envoy and discussed bilateral relations with him. 


This news is groundless as the revolution's leader has not delegated anyone to meet 
any West German official. If such a meeting has taken place then it must have been 
concluded on a purely personal rather than an official level.
