Historical Background 


The Motamar Al-Alam Al~-Ielami, (World Muslim Congreas) was founded in 
Mecca Mukarrama and convened there for the firet time in 1926. The 
organizer wae Hie Eminence Haj Aminul Hussaini the Grand Mufti of Palestine 
and Mr. Servet from Turkey took part in thie meeting. King Abdul Agiz Ibn 
Saud of Saudi Arabia played host to the delegates of the conference that 
was called "World Muslim Congress" for the first time, The delegates 
decided to form a permanent organization called "World Muslim Congress" 
and authorized the Grand Mufti of Palestine to make contacts for the 


oncoming conferonces. 


The second World Muslim Motamar was held in Jerusalem in 1931. During 
thie conference, Working Rules and Regulations for the World Muslim 
Congress were drawn and the first election to the various offices of the 
Motamar and those of its Councillors wae held. Hie Eminence Haj Aminul 
Huesaini, the Grand Mufti of Palestine wae elected the President of 
Motamar. 


The Congress called the Islamic countries to cooperate in line with a 
project it prepared by pointing out the Zioniem threat that appeared 
in Palestine.
