Summary (AI): A 1981 New York Times article (reprinted in a CIA reading room file) reports that about 10 former Green Berets traveled to Libya in 1977 to help train terrorists under a deal brokered by ex-CIA agent Edwin P. Wilson with Muammar el-Qaddafi. It says the CIA and Defense Department had not authorized the operation, though some Army officials wondered whether tacit approval existed.
• The Libyan training operation was run by Edwin P. Wilson, who was indicted the previous year by a federal grand jury in New York on charges of illegally shipping explosives to Libya.
• Special Forces were based at Fort Bragg, N.C.
• The House Select Committee on Intelligence planned a full investigation of the Wilson case.
• 1977: At least 10 former Green Berets travelled to Libya in late July 1977 to help train terrorists, according to participants and Federal investigators.
