Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a 27 November 1975 interview in which Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, speaking to the Italian paper Il Tempo, discusses Palestine, Ireland, Egypt, the Soviet Union, Italy and democracy in Libya. Nothing in it concerns places or events in Tripoli.
• Qadhdhafi said the LAR supports the Palestinian resistance and that Palestinians who cannot return peacefully will return by force.
• Qadhdhafi said the LAR supports the Irish national struggle until victory.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya and Italy have one unsettled problem, referred to the United Nations, relating to the Italian-era dispute from World War II.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya would compensate for damage suffered from Italian occupation, citing hundreds of families affected and mines on farms.
• Qadhdhafi said the Italian fascists were expelled on 7 October and that there is no democracy through parliamentary chambers, only popular conferences.
