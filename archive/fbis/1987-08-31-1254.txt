Commentary Praises Aozou Victory 
LD290212 Tripoli Voice af Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 2015 GMT 28 Aug 87 


[Unattributed commentary] 


[Excerpts] The great Fatih revolution, which has faced great 
challenges since its beginning in 1969, is capable of facing more 
challenges and achieving victory. That is a fact that sometimes 
escapes the enemies of the revolution, because they live under the 
illusion of the age of force, arrogance, and conspiracy; a conspir- 
acy that has continued throughout the 18 years of the great Fatih 
revolution’s march. [passage omitted] 


The United States did not succeed in its attacks on the people of 
the Great Jamahiriyah from the north, and did not succeed in its 
repeated attacks to stop the revolutionary tide which has been 
created by the great Fatih revolution in the world. It found in 
Habre’s gang — France's old agent who is practicing terrorism 
everywhere — a fresh situation in which it could rely on new 
aggression against the Great Jamahiriyah. While hiding behind 
Habre’s gangs, it became involved in aggression against the 
southern part of the Great Jamahiriyah when it pushed these 
gangs to attack the Libyan Arab town of Aozou. It tried to impose 
a de facto policy on the Great Jamahiriyah even though the OAU 
had adopted a resolution in its recent meeting stipulating that 
African problems would be resolved within the OAU itself 


Because imperialism is imperialism, agents are agents, and the 
United States has not learned the lesson well, it believed that by 
supporting Habre’s gangs it could again curb the activities and 
aspirations of the revolution and the rallying of the world's 
peoples around it. 


The Great Jamahiriyah has repeatedly announced that it 
(wants) peace, especially because its principles stipulate that 
peace and freedom should prevail throughout the world in the 
context of humnitarian relations woven from the priciples of the 
third universal theory which brings glad tidings of freedom and 
liberation. 


When all peace efforts were exhausted, and despite repeated 
warnings, the great Fatih revolution considered it necessary, on 
the basis of defending its rights, to rout the aggression and face 
the challenge which was (?made) by the agents of imperialism 
backed by the power of arms. The day of 28 August 1987 was 
another one of the days of lofty victories at the forefront and 
another step in the great Fatih revolution’s march. 


The sons of the great Fatih revolution from the Libyan Arab 
Armed Forces marched toward the enemy destroying the mental- 
ity of arrogance, domination, and enslavement and were able in 
no more than 2 hours to rout the aggression, annihilate the 
aggressors, and turn them on their heels. The aggressors were 
burdened with [word indistinct] defeat and shame for themselves 
and their imperialist masters after they had been given another 
lesson on the great Fatih revolution’s struggle. 


V. 31 Aug 87 


The great Aozou victory took place on the road of constant and 
continuing victories. This lofty victory, which comes at a time 
when the Arab people in general and the Libyan Arab people in 
particular, are marking the 18th anniversary of the start of the 
great Fatih revolution, is a victory that will be added to the record 
of the world revolutionary forces — forces of the revolution, 
liberation, and peace movements which constitute the inter- 
national center. It reaffirms that the will of people is bound to 
triumph no matter what quantities of arms, ammunition, fleets, 
and armies are amassed by the forces of injustice, evil, and 
aggression 


Today, the day of routing and defeating aggression on the Great 
Jamahiriyah from the south, in the Aozou region, is a triumph 
for all forces of revolution and freedom throughout the world as 
much as it is a triumph for the great Fatih revolution. A salute 
to all fighters for freedom in the world and those defending the 
homeland. A salute to the great Fatih's youths and the valiant 
soldiers of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces which brought back 
to the homeland [words indistinct].

matic source has affirmed that the Arab Gulf countries refuse to

their territories

evel,” the Kuwaiti newspaper A/-Watan said that the Zionist 
obby 1s leading the biased information campaign against Kuwait

what appears ostensibly to be U.S. support for the Arab position 
n the Gulf for an Arab response to the U.S. formula for solving

The source affirmed that the US. interests in particular and 
Western interests in general — but not Arab interests — are 
what prompted the United States to embark on its current moves 
n the Arab Gulf with the support of naval units and mine-

Iranian Gunboat Fires on Kuwaiti Vessel 31 Aug 
LD311258 Kuwait KUNA in English 1231 GMT 31 Aug 87 


[Text] Kuwait, Aug 31 (KUNA) — A Kuwaiti-registered con- 
tainer ship was Monday attacked by a speeding gunboat as the 
commercial vessel sailed between two ports of the United Arab 
Emirates, the ship's owners said 


The container ship, Jabal ‘Ali, sustained damage after it came 
under rocket and machine-gun fire from the attacking gunboat, 
according to a statement by the Kuwait-based United Arab 
Shipping Company (UASC) 


Noone of Jabal ‘Ali's crew members was hurt in the attack which 
occurred at about 0400 UAE local time as the vessel was some 
seven sea miles off the Emirate of Umm al Qawayn during a 
routine voyage from Khawr Fakkan Port to the Emirate of 
Dubayy, the statement said 


The container ship later reached Al-Rashid Port in Dubayy by 
its own power and was expected to unload there in view of the 
damage it sustained, the owners said
