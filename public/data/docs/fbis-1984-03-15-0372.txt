LIBYA

JANA Editor on UK ‘Campaign of Hatred’ 
LD141537 Tripoli JANA in English 1443 GMT 14 Mar 84 


{“JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY Political Editor Con- 
demns British Campaign of Hatred Against Libya” — JANA 
headline] 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada Thani 10, March 14, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The British authorities have brought more 
invalid accusations which were raised in a provocative manner 
against some Libyans who were arrested on allegations of 
claimed charges of involvement in explosions that took place 
recently in London and Manchester. In spite of the fact that the 
British authorities have no evidence to substantiate these allega- 
tions, London radio [as received] reported this morning that a 
number of Libyans who had been interrogated by the British 
police are to appear later in court. 


The British insistence on raising unfounded accusations against 
Libyans in Britain is related to racist ways upon which the 
widespread campaign of hatred against Libyan Arab people is 
based to cover up the British authorities’ involvement in suspi- 
cious operations being prepared to make Libyan revolutionary 
forces in Britain their target. 


It is clear evidence in the campaign that these planted and 
deceptive operations led to the arrest of 23 Libyan nationals 
under very severe conditions without finding any evidence 
against them. [sentence as received] This situation confirms that 
these aggressive actions of the British Government are aimed at 
fueling the fire of hatred against Libyans which came in a wave 
of untrue accusations against our natiunals in Britain and showed 
that the British actions were well-planned and deliberate; to 
surround Libyans and paralyze their legitimate activities by 
portraying them with a character incompatible with the original 
one which takes into consideration the friendly relations between 
the Libyan Arab people and the British people.
