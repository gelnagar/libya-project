LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI URGES ARMING PEOPLE TO COUNTERATTACK U.S. 


LD072005 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1657 GMT 7 Oct 80 


[Speech by Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi at rally in Brak celebrating 10th anniver- 
sary of eviction of the Italians from Libya--live] 


[Excerpts] Today, we are standing in the face of the United States. The United State's 
might does not frighten us, no matter how big it is. This is because we have right on our 
side. We have not decided to invade the United States; it is the United States which 
decided to invade the Arab homeland and it was the United States which set up its base, 
Israel, in the Arab homeland and is supporting it with military [word indistinct] bases 
from Somalia to Egypt. 


Therefore, we will urge the Arab people to embark on a counterattack, ignoring all regimes 
and boundaries. We will urge them day and night. We urge the Arab people from the ocean to 
the gulf to embark on counterattacking U.S. bases, the U.S. presence and its main base in 
Palestine. Should the Arab regimes obstruct the counterattack of the Arab people, they 
must pay the price for this; they will be treated like the United States andthe Israelis 
will be treated. 


The Libyan people have decided that Libya shall be the state of the Arabs and the state 

of unity. With this historic decision, the borders tehind which lurk the kings, presi- 
dents and sultans to plot against their peoples and sell them as flocks of sheep in return 
for their own false personal objectives have been blown up. 


V. 8 Oct 80 NORTH AFRICA 


Thus, the Arabs from the ocean to the gulf will move toward Libya, toward the unity state. 
[applause, chants] All borders which separate sons of the one Arab people will be abolished. 


On the other hand we will accept all Arab volunteers who will be the vanguard of the 
counteroffensive against imperialism and its bases in the Arab homeland. Every Arab has 
the right to fight for the liberation of the homeland as long as the United States inter- 
feres in Arab affairs. [chants] 


We have repeatedly said that we have not aggressed against the United States. But when 
America enters the Arab homeland, it must meet an Arab resistance. [chants] 


The Libyan people have made these grave resolutions and will shoulder responsibility for 
them. Libyan Arab volunteers, or those Arabs who are in Libya and have volunteered, must 
gather immediately in the places set for them so that the arming of the people may be 
speeded up and we will be ready to face any battle that will be imposed on us from any 
quarter and at any time so that we may not be taken by surprise as happened in 1911. 


[Passage indistinct] the revolutionary change on Libyan soil inevitably cannot be delayed. 
The liquidation of the forces of exploitation must continue to the end; the liquidation 

of fascism will continue to the end; the popular advance of the counterforces will continue; 
vigilant popular control will continue and be confirmed; the advance will not stop. The 
vigilant popular control will [word indistinct] until the people are the master. Any one 
who protests against such revolutionary measures and against the sovereignty of the Libyan 
people is someone who wants to deprive the Libyan people from a possession which the people 
and no one else must have. Who is to be trodden on? The enemy of the people. Who will be 
killed? The enemy of the people. Who will be tried by revolutionary tribunals? The enemy 
of the people. Who will go to prison? The enemy of the people. Therefore, we do not lose 
[words indistinct] because they are the enemies of the people. We do not defend an authority, 
nor a president nor a king nor a heritage nor a throne nor a family [words indistinct]. We 
work for the people. 


Now, all the measures, regardless of how severe they are, even death--we cannot possibly 
fear them and we cannot possibly be ashamed of carrying them out. Why? Because they are 
for the sake of the people. [Words indistinct] The government defends its existence; a 
president defends himself; a king defends his throne; but now, defending whom? Defending 
the people, and those who are defending are the people. The permitted action now is revolu- 
tionary action. Revolutionary action is now the permitted action--the action in which no 
one can criticize. Now you can execute one, and you must never be ashamed to show it on 
television before [word indistinct], all the reporters can watch. Why? Because this one 
was against this people who possess authority, resources and weapons.

Statement on Information Media 
LD072330 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2035 GMT 7 Oct 80 


[Text] The revolution forces today marched on the various information media in the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and issued the following statement: 


In line with the first chapter of the Green Book that the democratically correct informa- 
tion is that which is directed by a popular committee and the commander of the revolu- 
tion's repeated statements that the ultimate form of information in a masses'society is 
that which is run by a popular committee and that in the stage of revolutionary trans- 
formation information must be a platform for the revolutionary forces through which they 
can instigate the masses to destroy the old society, the society of exploitation, dicta- 
torship and monopoly and to build the new society, a society of the masses, a society of 
freedom, and in response by the revolutionary forces to the call of their commander on the 
llth anniversary of the great 1 September revolution and the final session of the people's 
general congress’: emergency session ou 28 September 1980, and in furtherance of the 
resolutions of the sixth meeting of the revolutionary committees, the revolution forces 
have marched on the information media and formed a revolutionary committee in them so that 
these media will be the voice of the revolutionary committees, which through the Green 
Book discovered the falsehood of the democracy prevalent in the world and the truth about 
the society of exploitation and organized themselves in revolutionary committees to 
detonate a popular revolution, the revolution of tomorrow. 


The information media in the world are either owned by dictatorial instruments such as 
governments, parties, sects, classes or individuals, or are commercial enterprises bent 
on profit. All that mankind hears, reads or sees everywhere is nothing than the views 
of these instruments which dominate people and exploit them whatever their affiliations. 


Throughout the world peoples are being exposed to constant information campaigns aimed 
at leading them astray and keeping them ignorant about what is taking place around them 
and at consecrating the societies of exploitation, oppression and suppression. 


The information media in the stage of transformation, the stage of a new society, the 
society of freedom, will be a platform occupied by the forces of the revolution through 
which to instigate the popular masses to exercise their authority and possess their 
wealth and arms. 


Hence, from today the forces of the revolution have assumed the running of broadcasting, 
the press, the news agency, external information, the presses, cultural centers, pub- 
lications, advertisements and circulation, the theater, movies and information programs. 


Information is not an authority but a platform. Authority is vested in the masses, which 
they exercise through their popular conferences and popular committees.

The time has come for the revolutionary committees to raise their voice through all the 
information media declaring the revolution, calling for it, and instigating it so that 
the revolutionary committee may fight all the battles so that the color green will cover 
everything. 


Now that the revolutionary committees have marched on the information media, these media 
will become a voice for the popular revolution, the revolution of tomorrow on the road 
of the final emanicipation of mankind. They will be a voice for all revolutionaries in 
the world who struggle for the toppling of the societies of exploitation and oppression, 
injustice and slavery. 


The revolutionary forces in the Arab homeland and the world are called upon to offer their 
cultural, political, literary and technical assistance, which herald the new culture and 
call for national and religious unity and instigate the masses to exercise authority and 
explain the picture of the new society so that the old society may crumple and on its 
debris may rise a society of freedom--the Jamahiriyah society, which possess authority, 
the arms and the wealth. 


Forward, and the committee everywhere.
