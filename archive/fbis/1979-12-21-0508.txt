[Text] Manila, 20 Dec (AFP)--Libya today called for the ouster of Palestinian Liberation 
Organisation (PLO) Chairman Yasir ‘Arafat for allegedly deviating from the "revolutionary 
p ath. . 


"Deviation from the revolutionary path must be eliminated regardless of who is involved in 
such kind of conduct," declared Libya's Ambassador to the Philippines Moustafa Dreiza in 

a hurriedly=-called news conference. He said Libya considered ‘Arafat as following his path of 
deviation "as far as they still retain a PLO office in Cairo and they have dubious relations 
with (Egyptian President Anwar) as-Sadat." 


Asked whether he was advocating the ouster of ‘Arafat as leader of the Palestinian guerrilla 
movement, Mr Dreiza replied: "We are advocating the ouster of any elements regardless of 
any names that work against the right path of solving the Palestinian problem in the right 
way." He said, however, it was up to the Palestinians to remove their leaders because "it 
is their own problem and [word indistinct] just expressed what we believe in." 


Ambassador Dreiza denied PLO accusations that Libya was interfering in PLO affairs and had 
closed down the PLO offices in Tripoli and Benghazi, saying the offices are [word indistinct]
