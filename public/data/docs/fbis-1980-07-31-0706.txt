AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES MILITARY COLLEGE CADETS 
LD301536 Tripoli JANA in English 1423 GMT 30 Jul 80 


[Text] Tripoli, July 30 JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
leader of the great Fatah revolution met with the cadets of the military college, 


the military college for girls, air defence college and the navy college. 


The leader pointed out that:. "The armed people is quite different from the armed 
anarchy. If people, for example, carry guns on the streets or keep them at home, 
that is considered an armed anarchy. But the armed people is a scientific process, 
highly organized with precision, control and coherence. So when the people become 
armed, they must be advanced and capable of taking the responsibility to defend their 
home country". 


Colonel al-Cadhdhafi added: "When an armed jJamahiriyan is established, as it is the 
case in Libya, the regular army disappears by dividing the country into defence zones 
within military administration. Where guards are organized and patrols are weekly or 
monthly backed by a people's force which have access to the real sophisticated weaponry 
of fighters, warships, artillery, rockets, tanks, armovred carriers, machineguns and 
all military means, thus the people turn into productive elements working in agricul- 
ture and industry, attend schools and camps at specific times same as when people 
regularly go to parks and cinemas and so on", 


Colo.el al-Qadhdhafi stressed that: "The objective of the revolutionary transforma- 
tion is to take the Libyan people to progress, advanceuent and deep awareness in order 
to put all the resources in the hands of the people's masses", 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi gave Lebanon and Chad as examples to the armed anarchy in radical 
contrast to the armed people. “Although arms are found everywhere in Lebanon, yet the 
Lebanese people are incapable of repulsing the (Israeli) aggression which proves that 
Lebanon has an armed anirchy and not armed peopie, because the first has no potential 
to be up to the level of deterring the enemy and cannot be reliable in defending the 
country. On the contrary, such armed anarchy has enabled the (Israeli) enemy to in- 
filtrate and reach any part of Lebanon." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi then explained anotner case of the armed anarchy example now 
existing in Chad where every armed party tries to liquidate the other in order to 
reach power. And, as they are all armed, fighting is at its utmost in every part of 
Chad due to this armed anarchy which germiniated the fascist spirit. 


Colonel al-Qadh@hafi, asserted that "The masses which possess authority would not 
hesitate to get hold of all resources, because authority, wealth and arms are in- 
separable; the people cannot do with one without the other and should they give up 
one, they would eventually give up the other and be slaves again".
