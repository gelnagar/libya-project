https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-cia-rdp84t00171r000201860001-5

Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing set describing Libyan and Libyan-backed dissident force deployments across northern Chad (Fada, Faya Largeau, Ounianga Kebir, Gouro, Ogouï, Aozou). It concerns Libya's military intervention abroad, not Tripoli itself.
• Libyan forces in northern Chad as summarized: elements of five mechanized infantry battalions, one motorized infantry battalion, three tank companies, four small tank units, three BM-21 MRL batteries, three 122mm D-30 howitzer batteries, three 130mm M-46 batteries, and five logistics units.
• Air defence in northern Chad included two deployed SA-6 batteries, one SA-9 battery, and two ZSU-23/4 guns.
• Combat aircraft in northern Chad included six Mirage F-1, 24 Fitter (Su-22), 14 SF.260 and six Hind attack helicopters.
• Document is a 16 September 1983 NPIC/IEG briefing paper, sanitized and partly declassified.
• 1983-09-16: NPIC imagery briefing reports Libyan and Libyan-backed dissident force dispositions across northern Chad, including air and ground order of battle.
