gent  and  far-sighted  persons,  instead  of  by  persons  who

and  threats  by  the  United  States  are  like  the  early 
morning  lamp  which  glows  strongly  once  before  going 
out.  Its  shine  is  just  its  last  breath.

is  at  the  brink  of  economic  ruin.  The  U.S.  social  system

results  of  its  misdeeds.  Racial  riots,  bomb  blasts,  mur¬

now.  The  United  States,  which  a  few  years  ago  was 
known  as  the  comer  of  prosperity — and  indeed  there  was 
peace  and  harmony — is  surrounded  by  destruction 
because  of  its  own  deeds.  Its  apparent  prosperity  and 
success  are  the  result  of  its  roguish  deeds  around  the 
world,  false  boasting,  and  cheating.
