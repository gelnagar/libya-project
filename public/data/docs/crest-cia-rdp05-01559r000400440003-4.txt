WAS NOT -- AS SOME HAVE SOUGHT TO PORTRAY IT -- ONE OF DISAGREEMENT

AL-MUSAWWAR: YOUR EXCELLENCY THE COLONEL: THE MARSA MATRUH AND 
TOBRUK SESSIONS HAVE PRODUCED AGREEMENT ON A NUMBER OF PHYSICAL, 
CONCRETE MEASURES THAT DIRECTLY AFFECT THE INTERESTS OF CITIZENS IN 
THE TWO COUNTRIES. PERHAPS THERE WERE NO BANNER HEADLINES; MAYBE 
THE RESULTS DID NOT MEASURE UP TO YOUR EXPECTATIONS REGARDING THE 
UNITY ISSUE: IN THE END, THOUGH, WE NOW HAVE PRACTICAL STEPS LAYING 
DOWN SOUND FOUNDATIONS FOR THE BENEFIT OF THE TWO PEOPLES. 

THE COLONEL: THAT IS CORRECT. IN THE PAST, WE HOPED THAT 
AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE POLITICAL RANKS OF THE TWO COUNTRIES WAS THE 
RIGHT APPROACH TO BUILDING COMMON INTERESTS. THIS HAS NOT 
SUCCEEDED, HOWEVER. NOW WE ARE TRYING TO LEARN FROM THE PAST. I 
THINK THE REALISTIC START WE MADE IN MARSA MATRUH AND TOBRUK HAS PUT 
US ON THE RIGHT TACK, INSTEAD OF IGNORING REALITY. WE HAVE BEGUN 
REMOVING THE STUMBLING BLOCKS PREVENTING THE JOINING TOGETHER OF THE 
INTERESTS OF ORDINARY CITIZENS IN BOTH COUNTRIES. ONLY SUCH LINKS 
CAN BUILD THE JOINT INFRASTRUCTURE ON WHICH TO BUILD POLITICAL 
RELATIONS. I DO NOT FORESEE ANY UNDUE DIFFICULTY IN THE TWO 
COUNTRIES COMPLEMENTING EACH OTHER IN THE FUTURE. AFTER ALL, WE ARE 
ONE PEOPLE AND PART OF ONE NATION WITH SHARED INTERESTS -- NOT TO 
MENTION OUR COMMON DESTINY AND FUTURE. THE BARBED WIRE ERECTED BY 
THE ITALIANS ALONG OUR BORDER IS AN ARTIFICIAL BARRIER; WE ARE ONE 
PEOPLE AND OURS IS ONE LAND. 

I WOULD LIKE TO STRESS THAT THERE IS ABSOLUTELY NO DIFFICULTY 
WITH EGYPT. ALL WE DID IN MARSA MATRUH AND TOBRUK WAS PUT THINGS 
BACK IN THEIR PROPER PERSPECTIVE, SINCE, GEOGRAPHICALLY AND 
DEMOGRAPHICALLY, THE TWO COUNTRIES ARE IN REALITY ONE NATION. 

I DO NOT SEE ANY PROBLEM THERE -- THE MESHING OF RELATIONS 
BETWEEN THE TWO PEOPLES GOES FAR BACK INTO HISTORY. BEFORE THE 
ESTRANGEMENT IN RELATIONS, THE EGYPTIANS SHOWED PREFERENCE FOR LIBYA 
AS A PLACE OF WORK, AND LIBYAN TRAVELLERS HEADED FOR EGYPT BOTH FOR 
RECREATION AND MEDICAL TREATMENT. RELATIONS BETWEEN THE TWO PEOPLES 
WERE DEEP AND EXTENSIVE. IF WE HAD BANNED ALL THIS DURING THE 
RUPTURE IN RELATIONS, CERTAINLY IT WAS AN ARBITRARY BAN. IT WAS 
THEREFORE IMPORTANT TO MAKE THE RIGHT START. WE HAD TO START FROM 
RECOGNIZING THE RIGHT TO WORK AND THE FREEDOM OF MOVEMENT AND 
RESIDENCE. WE HAD TO PROCEED BASED ON THE JOINT INTERESTS OF THE 
TWO PEOPLES.

((TEXT)) AL-MUSAWWAR: YOUR EXCELLENCY THE COLONEL, HOW COULD 
THREE MEETINGS END AN ESTRANGEMENT THAT LASTED 16 YEARS UNLESS THERE 
WAS A MUTUAL UNDERSTANDING? WHAT ARE THE LIMITS OF THE MUTUAL 
UNDERSTANDING REACHED BETWEEN YOU AND PRESIDENT MUBARAK? 

THE COLONEL: FIRST, I HAVE KNOWN PRESIDENT MUBARAK SINCE HE WAS 
COMMANDER OF THE AIR FORCE, AND PROBABLY BEFORE THAT. HE ALSO HAS 
KNOWN ME FOR A LONG TIME. I BELIEVE THAT WE BOTH HAVE GOOD 
IMPRESSIONS ABOUT THE OTHER BECAUSE WE COOPERATED TOGETHER DURING A 
PERIOD OF BROTHERHOOD AND SYMPATHY BEFORE THE ESTRANGEMENT.. 

DURING THAT PERIOD, IN THE TIME OF PRESIDENTS 'ABD-AL-NASIR AND 
AL-SADAT, COOPERATION BETWEEN US WAS VERY STRONG. EGYPT COULD ASK 
FOR ANYTHING FROM LIBYA AND THERE WOULD BE AN IMMEDIATE RESPONSE, 
AND LIBYA COULD ASK FOR ANYTHING FROM EGYPT AND THERE WOULD BE A 
QUICK RESPONSE. PRESIDENT MUBARAK PROBABLY KNOWS BEST ABOUT THE AID 
THAT LIBY.. PROVIDED FOR THE SAKE OF THE PAN-ARAB BATTLE. 

WHEN PRESIDENT MUBARAK ASSUMED RESPONSIBILITY, UNDER CRITICAL 
EGYPTIAN AND ARAB CIRCUMSTANCES, WE HAD A SINCERE DESIRE TO END ALL 
THE PROBLEMS THAT HAD ARISEN WITH EGYPT BECAUSE OF DIFFERENCES ON 
THE PAN-ARAB CAUSE. AN INDIRECT DIALOGUE TOOK PLACE BETWEEN US 
THROUGH SEVERAL ENVOYS AND INTERMEDIARIES, BUT CIRCUMSTANCES DID NOT 
HELP IT SUCCEED. 

ON THE ONE HAND, EGYPT HAD NEW CONNECTIONS, WHICH COULD NOT BE 
IGNORED. ON THE OTHER, WE WERE LOOKING UPON EGYPT AS IF NOTHING HAD 
CHANGED AND AS IF THE NEW ADMINISTRATION WAS AN EXTENSION OF THE OLD 
ONE. THE MISTAKE WAS THAT WE LOOKED UPON EGYPT DURING MUBARAK'S 
TIME AS AN EXTENSION OF THE AL-SADAT PERIOD. THIS CONTINUED TO BE 
THE CASE UNTIL MATTERS WERE CLARIFIED AND IT BECAME OBVIOUS THAT 
UNDER PRESIDENT MUBARAK, EGYPT WAS GOING THROUGH A DIFFERENT PHASE. 

_ QUR DIRECT MEETING IN CASABLANCA HAS PAVED THE WAY FOR OUR MUTUAL 
UNDERSTANDING. THIS IS BECAUSE IT WAS IMPOSSIBLE, IN THE CLIMATE OF 
EGYPTIAN-LIBYAN RELATIONS, TO LEARN ABOUT, OR UNDERSTAND MATTERS AS 
THEY REALLY WERE THROUGH THE MEDIA, WHICH WERE THEN ENGAGED IN 
MUTUAL BATTLES. 

WE MET IN CASABLANCA, AND IN THAT DIRECT MEETING, I UNDERSTOOD 
THE MAN'S STANDING AND POSITION. I MAY EVEN SAY THAT I VALUED THAT 
POSITION HIGHLY. PRESIDENT MUBARAK BELIEVED THAT THE TIMES HAD 
CHANGED AND THAT THE ISSUE WAS NO LONGER ONE OF AN ENEMY AND ARMS. 
HE BELIEVED THAT AGREEMENT ON THE SUPREME OBJECTIVES DOES NOT 
PREVENT US FROM USING DIFFERENT METHODS TO ACHIEVE THOSE OBJECTIVES. 
HE BELIEVED THAT ARAB SOLIDARITY WAS CAPABLE OF GUARANTEEING PAN- 
ARAB RIGHTS. HE ALSO BELIEVED THAT THE CURRENT PERIOD SHOULD NOT BE 
ONE OF WAILING AND SHOUTING, BUT A PERIOD OF CULTURAL BUILDING, 
BECAUSE WITHOUT ECONOMIC, TECHNOLOGICAL, AND CULTURAL EXCELLENCE, IT 
IS DIFFICULT FOR US TO TALK ABOUT OUR ABILITY TO FACE FUTURE 
CHALLENGES. 

IN CASABLANCA, OUR VIEWS WERE IN AGREEMENT ON MEANS TO CONFRONT 
FUTURE CHALLENGES, ON THE NATURE OF THE COMING AGE, AND ON THE 
IMPORTANCE OF THE CULTURAL BATTLE IN WINNING A SECURE AND PROSPEROUS 
FUTURE FOR THE ARABS. THIS IS BECAUSE PARITY BETWEEN US AND ISRAEL 
CANNOT BE ACHIEVED BY INCREASING THE NUMBER OF SOLDIERS OR WEAPONS.

((TEXT)) AL~MUSAWWAR: YOUR EXCELLENCY THE COLONEL, OUR

THE COLONEL: IN FACT, THE GOALS WERE CORRECT. WAYS AND MEANS 
HAVE VARIED, HOWEVER. WE NOW HAVE TWO APPROACHES: 

THE FIRST ARGUES THAT UNITED POLITICAL RANKS WOULD HELP CHANGE A 
PAROCHIAL, INWARD LOOKING STATE INTO A UNIONIST ONE. THAT WAS HOW 
WE APPROACHED OUR PAST UNITY ATTEMPTS. WE THOUGHT THAT HAVING 
POLITICAL LEADERS SHOULDER THE RESPONSIBILITY FOR DECISIONS WOULD 
HELP UNIONIST STEPS FORWARD. THE PROBLEM WITH THIS APPROACH IS THAT 
THE FATE OF THE UNIONIST ENTITY WOULD BE SUBJECT TO RELATIONS AMONG
