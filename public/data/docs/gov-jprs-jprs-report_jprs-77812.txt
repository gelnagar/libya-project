[Speech by Chief of State al-Qadhdhafi in Benghasi; date not specified | 


[Text] Benghasi, ® Rabial Thani, 13 February (JAMAHIRIYA NEWS AGENCY)--The leader 
of the revolution has proclaimed the imperative need to have--during the present 
pDhase--a movement comparable to that of the revolutionary nuns: a movement able to 
adapt to the revolutionary committees and to surmount a dangerous turning point in 
history, in order to defeat America, the Israeli, the forces of exploitation, anid 
underdevelopment and thereby to do away with frontiers, regain our dignity and 
guarantee our existence. 


In the course of the important statements he made during his meeting with the pio- 
neers of the women's revolutionary formations, the leader of the revolution de- 
clared that the revolutionary nuns are like the Christian nuns who devote thenselves 
to Ghrist. He said the entire world needs them today, for they constitute an impor- 
tant movement that delivers an historic message. Revolutionary action, the Jihad, 
the religious life, and mysticism generate serenity, he declared, and added: “We 
have need of a philosophical revolutionary culture so as not to live on the side- 
of life, covered over with a heavy veil.” 


We have need of the movement of the revolutionary nuns, he declared, because we 
believe that the movement of the Christian nuns represents a challenge to Muslia 
women. 


The leader of the revolution urged the Libyan revolutionary women to take a profound 
interest in this new movement, to subscribe to it and to join it. 


He invited the heads of households, and all active elements of society, to leave no 
stone unturned in bringing this movement to fruition and to give no credence to the 
simplistic concept that views women in a superficial manner. 


The leader of the revOlution declared that these revolutionary formations were the 
first organized revolutionary activities to be spontaneously self-created as a cadre 
of action. He said they had assembled the first vanguard revolutionary elements, 
who then proceeded to operate within the framework which | omselves created. 


T believe--the colonel said--that the revolutionary formations are the first sponta- 
neous revolutionary initiative taken by the popular masses following the movement of 
the free unionist officers. He added that the elements which organized the revolu- 


tionary formations are genuinely revolutionary elements, for they expect neither 
reward nor thanks.

The action of these formations--he continued--helped to create a revolutionary 
climate which made possible the emergence of the revolutionary movement which 
preceded the advent of the movement of the revolutionary committves,. 


The pioneers of the women's revolutionary formations very early waved the flag of 
the revolution and confronted the serious social and political challenges. 


The leader of the revolution paid tribute to the role played by the revolutionary 
formations, which incited the masses to exercise power and urged thea to confront 
their enenies. 


He likewise paid tribute to the manner in which these formations helped to rouse 
the masses during the years which preceded the appearance of the revolutionary 


colonialism. The existence of these governments lesds us to believe that we have 


not yet achieved social liberation but are still in a stage of political liberation. 


This stage--the leader of the revolution declared--demanis that all forces hostile 
to colonialisa, and in particular those in the Arab fatherland, unite to achieve 
political liberation and the elimination of those governments whose existence must 
be regarded as a continuation of the colonial presence ani the bases of that 


presence. 


When we set out on the road to social liberation--the leader of the revolution 
continued--we collided with the “reclaimed” revolution, which was attempting to 
deprive the revolution of its revolutionary social content. We were successful in 


foiling this attempt, because our interests--the interests of the oppressed popular 


and vroletarian masses--compelled us to defeat this “reclaimed” force, which is 
nothing more than a substitute for colonialisa. 


The leader of the revolution invited the masses of the Arab nation to cast off .neir 


chains and do away with all pressures in order to liberate themselves by their own 
efforts. So long as the Arab nation remains in chains--he declared--the Israeli 
will march, under an American umbrella, into holy places, over the oil wells, ani

upon the strategic positions, in an explicit attempt to build a racist war-mongering 
empire in the Arab fatherland and push the Arabs back thousands of years. For our 
part, if we do not begin to act in a positive manner this situation will remain 
unchanged and only selfish and exploitative forces will profit thereby, as was the 
case when these forces exploited the Libyan people and seised everything, abandoning

In his important statements the leader of the revolution declared that the situation 
was extremely bad ani that we must continue to fight, to struggle, to sacrifice 
ourselves, to shed our sweat and blood until victory is won in this stage of our 
journey. He said the revolution rejects bourgeois thinking, ani he called attention 
to the fact that t). stage through which we are passing is not a time for harem or 
for hoarding gold or silver. It is not a time--he continued--for palaces, for big 
luxurious automobiles, or for tourism. This kind of thinking, he said, is based on 
ignorance and simplistic thinking and implies a serious lack of capacity for anal- 
ysis. 


If this nation wishes to be victorious--he declared--it must not differentiate be- 
tween men and women during this stage, for the enemy is taking aim at each and every 
one of us and we must confront him using every weapon. All of us--men and women-- 
must organize ourselves, train ourselves to use these weapons, and fight our enemy. 


The bullets--and the life-generating electronic and agricultural equipment--with 
which we combat the enemy are more precious than gold, or silver, or silk, or 
palaces. It appears that the present stage of the journey is the stage of the great 
challenge which has been hurled at the virility of every Arab citizen, for virility 
consists of fighting the enemy and carrying out the revolution so as to create at 
least one base that will enable us to have freedom of movement and combat the enemy. 


Bvery revolutionary--he said in conclusion--man or woman, must think in terms of 
this stage and of martyrdom, with the goal of liberating Palestine and the occupied 
Arad territories, which have been despoiled by the enemy and by colonialisna.
