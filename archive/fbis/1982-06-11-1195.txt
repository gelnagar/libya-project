LIBYA

JAMAHIRIYAH CALLS FOR URGENT ARAB SUMMIT 


LD110748 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0730 GMT 11 Jun 82 


{Text] Tripoli, 11 Jum (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AC NCY) -- The jamahiriyah has called for an 
urgent summit conference within 24 hours. Its agenda will consist of one item only, 
namely, the Zionist invasion and the killing and complete destruction of the Arab Lebanese 


people. 


The meeting would be held in Libya or at the Arab League headquarters in Tunis. Syria, 
Algeria, the PDRY, Kuwait and the UAE have all agreed to attend following telephone con- 
tacts between the brother leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and President Hafiz al-Asad of 
Syria, Shaykh Jabir al-Ahmad as-Sabah, the amir of Kuwait, and Shaykh Zayid Ibn Sultan 
al Nuhayyan, president of the UAE.

V. 11 Jun 82 NORTH AFRICA 


NONALIGNED AMBASSADORS SUMMONED ON LEBANON 


LD101400 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 10 Jun 82 


[Text] The People's Foreign Liaison Bureau this morning summoned separately the 
ambassadors of Cuba, Yugoslavia and India in the jamahiriya and informed them of the 
dimensions of the dangerous conspiracy being hatched against Lebanon and the Palestinian 
Revolution through the barbaric Zionist invasion of Lebanese territory. 


The secretary of the people's committee of the People's Foreign Liaison Bureau asked the 
ambassadors to inform their governments of the need for a move by the nonaligned group 
to confront this aggression and to support the Lebanese people and the Palestinian 
Revolution at this critical phase, and also that the charter of the nonaligned organiza- 
tion with regard to the defense of any member being subjected to danger should be 
implemented. The secretary of the people's committee of the People's Foreign Liaison 
Bureau pointed out that the Palestinian Revolution not only needs political backing in 
the current circumstances but it also needs urgent military support from the nonaligned 
organization to confront the Zionist invasion.

AL-QADHDHAFI CABLES EGYPTIAN OPPOSITION LEADER 


LD101400 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 10 Jun 82 


{"Text" of cable sent by Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to Khalid Muhyi ad-Din, leader of the 


Egyptian National Progressive Unionist Grouping, on 10 June] 


[Text] Brother Khalid Muhyi ad-Din: There is no party in Egypt today to address other 
than you personally and the forces of the Progressive Unionist Grouping which you lead. 
Thousands of Israeli tanks have rolled over the treaty of stable David; the bombs from 
scores of planes have been dropped on that treaty. Consequently, there is no 
justification for Egypt to adhere unilaterally to an agreement that has lost its false 
legitimacy, since it originally lacked international legitimacy as it was signed in the 
shadow of the Israeli occupation of Egyptian territory. The treaty is null and void 
since Egypt was forced to sign it after the 1973 defeat, which became a disaster. This 
is from the point of view of international law. As for integrity, the national 
integrity of a country in the same situation as Egypt does not permit compliance with 
such a treaty, regardless of what need there is for it. Adherence to this treaty has 
become an unprecedented readiness to absorb humiliation and degradation and to condone 
disrespect for national integrity. If Egypt does not take this opportunity to rid itself 
of the humiliation of the treaty of stable David, it will not exist at the national or 
pan-Arab level. If the opposition does not struggle for this goal, it will lose all 
Egyptian and Arab popular support and consequently become a component of the regime of 
capitulation that was set up by As-Sadat.

The march for peace that was heralded by the said treaty has become a laughable legend, 
since it has turned into a veritable march to war. The whole world now realizes this fact. 


What message other than this one can the opposition have? After this, what Arab future is 
there for Egypt? After this, what is the meaning of any talk about Egyptian integrity? 


[Signed] Your brother, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 


SOVIET DEPUTY CULTURE MINISTER ARRIVES 9 JUNE 


LD091746 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 9 Jun 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 9 Jun (JANA) -- The Soviet deputy minister of culture and chairman of the 
Soviet-Libyan Friendship Society and an accompanying delegation arrived in Tripoli this 
morning. The Soviet official told a JANA correspondent on his arrival that the Soviet-Libyan 
Friendship Society will organize rallies and festivals in the USSR on the occasion of the 
12th anniversary of the liquidation the U.S. bases in Libya. In his statement, he stressed 
that the Soviet people appreciate and are proud of the strong friendship linking them with 
the Libyan people.
