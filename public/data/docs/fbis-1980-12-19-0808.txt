LIBYA

CHAD PRESIDENT GOUKOUNI SAYS FICHTING HAS STOPPED 


LD182114 Tripoli JANA in English 1629 GMT 18 Dec 80 


[Text] Paris, 18 Dec (.JANA)--President Goukouni Oueddei, heau of the National Unity 
Government in Chad confirmed that all fight has stopped throughout Chad. In a letter 
addressed to the chairman of the OAU committee on Chad, urged him to continue aid to his 
country in the next stage of rebuilding Chad. {as received] 


JANA: HUMANITARIAN AID TO CHAD TO CONTINUE 


LD190836 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 19 Dec 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 Dec (JANA)--The humanitarian aid which the people of the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah have been rendering to the Chadian people to alleviate 
the suffering which they are enduring and to dress their wounds caused by war and destruc- 


tion is continuing. Rendering such humanitarian aid is an expression of our people's 
solidarity with the Chadian people and within the framework which our people have under- 
taken toward all humanitarian causes and toward all peoples who are seeking freedom, 
progress and peace.
