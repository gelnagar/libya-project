Summary (AI): A Los Angeles Times article from December 1981 reports that a former CIA operative, Edwin P. Wilson (OCR "Zawin P. Wilson"), was accused of supplying arms and terrorist training to Libyan leader Muammar Qaddafi, and that the CIA had contact with him as late as September 1976. Testimony in a Colorado trial of Eugene Tafoya, a former Green Beret accused of killing Libyan exile Dr. Ali Zagallai (OCR "Paisal Zagallai"), is also covered.
• Tafoya was accused of being a paid Libyan hit man sent to kill Zagallai, an active opponent of Qaddafi in the United States.
• Two CIA employees were reported fired for engaging in improper activities with Wilson.
• A CIA spokesman declined to comment on Wilson's 1976 contact.
• Tafoya's phone recordings were reportedly destroyed.
• 1976-10-14: Libyan exile and Qaddafi opponent Zagallai was shot in the head in his living room; Tafoya testified he shot him in self-defence.
• 1976-09: CIA's last recorded operational contact with Edwin Wilson, per testimony; Wilson and Frank Terpil allegedly plotted to murder a Libyan dissident in Egypt around the same time.
