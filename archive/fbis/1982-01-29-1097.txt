LIBYA

REAGAN ADDRESS EXPOSES ANTI-LIBYAN POLICY 


LD282322 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 28 Jan 82 


[Text] The head of the American administration has decided to express, in a flagrant 

and crude manner, his anti-Jamahiriyah and anti-Cuba policy and attitudes. Reagan yester- 
day alleged before the U.S. Congress that the Jamahiriyah and Cuba are exporting terrorism 
and he threatened he will confront the two countries. The U.S. President attempts to 
confirm, through his flagrant threat, that the U.S. imperialist move, particularly toward 
the Jamahiriyah, cannot be considered from the viewpoint of international law as interna- 
tional terrorism. He aims, primarily, at using these accusations and allegations as a 
pretext for aggression and as a cover for the course practiced by the American adminis- 
tration in supressing the people's struggle, plundering their wealth and protecting agent 
regimes. 


JANA EDITOR VIEWS U.S. AMBITIONS IN CHAD 


LD281524 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 28 Jan 82 


[Text] The U.S. Defense Department today officially admitted that it has military hard- 
ware in Chad. This was announced by the Defense Department spokesman, Col Jerry Grohowski, 
as reported by AFP. 


The JANA political editor writes: This admission reveals that Chad has become a target of 
the ambitions of the United States, which wants to turn it into an area from which to 
threaten and shake the security and safety of Africa as a whole. Furthermore, by officially 
making this admission, the U.S. administration is trying to add Chad to its sphere of 
influence with the aid and collusion of its agent in Sudan. Thus, world public opinion 

has become certain that the U.S. administration is openly working to undermine the historic 
efforts which have been exerted to end the civil war in Chad and to win freedom, peace and 
security for its people.
