LIBYAN-AFGAN COMMUNIQUE PUBLISHED FOLLOWING DAUD'S VISIT 
LD271514Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 27 Feb 78 LD 


[Joint communique issued in Tripoli and Kabul simultaneously at the end of Afgan 
President Daud's visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah] 


[Text] Within the context of the development of the fraternal relations between the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the Republic of Afganistan, Afgan Pres- 
ident Mohammad Daud paid an official, friendly visit to the Libya in the period 

between 25-27 February 1978. The guest and the delegation accompanying him were 
granted a warm reception which expressed the spirit of fraternity and affection between 
the fraternal Libyan andi Afgan peoples. In an atmosphere of fraternity and understand- 
ing, important talks were held and they were led on the Libyan side by brother col 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and on the Afgan side by President Mohammad Daud.

The two sides reviewed the development of bilateral fraternal relations and expressed 
their satisfaction with the level of these relations. They reiterated their true 

desire and intention to lead these relations for the good of the two friendly countries. 
They also affirmed tne need for joint cooperation in all fields and the opening of new 
vistas for the development of this cooperation to satisfy the ambitions of the two 
fraternal peoples.

The two sides expressed their belief in the unity of the Arab nation stressing that it 
will lead to the bolstering of solidarity of the Islamic world. During their review of 
the situation in the Arab world, the two sides strongly denounced the Zionist aggression 
against the Arab nation. They expressed their confidence that true peace and security 
cannot prevail in the Arab area without the liberation of all the occupied territories. 
The two sides affirmed their support for the struggle of the Palestinian people led by 
the PLO as being the sole legitimate representative for the retrieval of the usurped 
territories and the establishment of their independent state. 


In the African field the two sides strongly denounced the racist regimes in the African 
Continent--regimes which are in alliance with imperialism, colonialism and Zionism. 

They affirmed their support for the struggle of the African peoples in Namibia, Zimbabwe 
and South Africa for their freedom. The two sides denounced the racist collusion 
between the racist regimes in southern Africa and occupied Palestine. They expressed 
concern over the eruption of conflicts between some countries on the African Continent 
and called on the disputing parties to safeguard the continent's unity and cohesion and 
to find a solution to the problems by peaceful means. 


The two sides expressed their appreciation for the role played by the nonalined movement 

in the struggle against imperialism and colonialism and for the strengthening of inter- 
national peace and security. They consider the participation of this movement an important 
factor for the realization of good level of international relations. They affirmed the 
absolute right of the developing peoples to control their natural resources and wealth 

and the need for the consolidation of their efforts for the establishment of advanced 
international economic relations based on equality among all countries through a new and 
more equitable economic system leading to fruitful cooperation among all world peoples. 


The two sides expressed their support for the idea of transforming the Indian Ocean 

into a zone of peace, They denounce foreign naval and military rivalry in the area which 
threatens its security and stability. They declared their strong support for the efforts 
aimed at disarmament for the realization of welfare for the worid. The two sides declared 
their adherance to the principles and objectives of the United Nations and referred to the 
positive role that organization plays inthe consolidation of international peace and 
security. They reiterated their support for UN Resolution No 3379, branding Zionism 

as a form of racism. 


Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi acquainted his brother President Mohammad Daud with 

the achievements of the Great 1 September revolution in the political, economic and social 
fields. He also acquainted him with the practizal application of the principle of the 
people's exercise of direct democracy. President Mohammad Daud praised these achievements. 
President Mohammad Daud acquainted Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi with the progress 

and achievements of the July 1973 revolution of the Republic of Afghanistan. The brother 
colonel expressed his admiration for these achievements, During the visit, the minutes 

of an agreement were signed and two agreements for technical, cultural and economic were 
initialed. At the end of the visit President Mohammad Daud expressed his utmost gratitude 
to brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi for the great welcome and hospitality shown him 

and the members of his delegation during their visit to Libya. 


President Mohammad Daud invited borther Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to visit the Republic 
of Afghanistan. The invitation was gratefully accepted and a date will be fixed later,

AL-QADHDHAFI COMMENTS ON SOLUTION OF CHADIAN CONFLICT 
LD251905Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1800 GMT 25 Feb 78 LD 


[Excerpt] Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi replied to a question from the press as he saw 
Chadian President Felix Malloum and Niger President Kountche off at Sabha International 
Airport this morning about what the Chadian peopie can expect from the Sabha summit con- 
ference. He said: God willing, they Can expect peace and national unity and the rebuilding 
of Chad on the basis of equality between the north and the south and between all tribes 

and religions through the establishment of popular democracy in Chad, the elimination 

of the causes of the conflict and the realization and implementation of social justice. 

When these objectives are attained, guns will be turned into shovels for building and 
science. We have, he said, pledged with our neighbors in Chad to do all we can to help 

Chad to attain tnis dream for which the Chadian people have fought.
