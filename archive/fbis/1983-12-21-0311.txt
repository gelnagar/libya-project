LIBYA

Jallud, Al-‘Ubaydi Meet Iranian Ambassador 
LD200946 Tehran IRNA in English 0905 GMT 20 Dec 83 


[Text] Tehran, Dec 20, IRNA — Iranian ambassador in Libya, 
Hojjat ol-Eslam Seyyed Mohammad Lazem Khansari, Sunday, 
separately met with Libyan prime minister [title as received], 
‘Abd as-Salam Jallud and secretary of the Bureau for External 
Relations, “Abd al-*Ati al-‘Ubaydi. 


The Iranian envoy arrived in Tripoli recently and discussed 
matters of mutual interest with the Libyan officials. 


During these meetings, emphasis on the expansion of bilateral 
ties had been made, it was reported.
