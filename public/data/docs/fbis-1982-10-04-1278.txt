LIBYA

AL-—QADHDHAFI RECEIVES BREZHNEV MIDEAST MESSAGE 


LD021906 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1747 GMT 2 Oct 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 Oct (JANA) — The brother leader of the revolution has received 

a written message from Leonid Brezhnev, chairman of the Supreme Soviet Presidium, 
containing the Soviet Union's views on the problems of the occupation of Palestine 
and the barbaric Zionist aggression against Lebanon, and exchanging views between the 
two countries on this problem and on international politics, which are witnessing the 
conspiracy between the American President and the terrorist Begin, who have brought 
international peace to the explosion point. This is a direct result of the American- 
Zionist strategic cooperation agreement signed in Washington in November 1981.
