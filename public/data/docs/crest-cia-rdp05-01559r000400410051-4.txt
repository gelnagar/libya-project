LIBYA 

Al-Qadhdhafi: Armed Force Only Way to Liberation 

OW300451 Tokyo ASAHI SHIMBUN in Japanese 29 Jul 86 
Morning Edition p 7 — FOR OFFICIAL USE ONLY 

[Text] Asahi Television Network broadcast an interview with 
Libya’s supreme Leader, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, on 28 July. 

Referring to the recent talks held between Israeli Prime Minister- 

Peres and Morocco’s King Hassan II, Al-Qadhdhafi said in the 
interview: “No results were achieved. The talks have confirmed 
that liberation by armed force is the only way to settle Middle 
East problems.” Moreover, he stated that he had no intention of 
attending an Arab summit even if it were held. 

A summary of the interview with Col al-Qadhdhafi follows: 

[Reporter] What do you think of the results of the talks held 
between King Hassan and Prime Minister Peres? 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] There were no results. (Israel) wants to subordi- 
nate the Arab states. Moreover, (Israel) has occupied the Middle 
East and rejected the rights of Palestinians as well as the PLO, 
their sole legitimate representative. (In view of King Hassan’s 
actions), I think Europe and the United States want the Arabs to 
surrender. 

[Reporter] After the Hassan-Peres talks, what will Libya’s posi- 
tion and strategy be concerning peace in the Middle East? 

[A!-Qadhdhafi] The talks have confirmed that the only way to 
settle the Middle East problems is liberation by armed force. For 
the Palestinians, the only way to recover Palestine is war. The 
talks have brought to naught all the past efforts for peace. 

[Reporter] Will you attend the Arab summit scheduled in Saudi 
Arabia? 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] I will not attend, even if all the leaders attend. 

(Reporter] What do you think of U.S. Vice President Bush’s 
Middle East visit? 

[Al-Qadhdhafi] I do not think much of it, and I have no interest. 
Bush has been touring the Middle East as a tool of Zionism. He 
ridicules history.
