Summary (AI): A January 1986 broadcast transcript in which Qadhdhafi warns of war with the U.S. over U.S. naval maneuvers in the Mediterranean and the Gulf of Sidra, and denounces Reagan. It also reports Libyan media attacks on the U.S. 6th Fleet exercises and a UN complaint. No places in Tripoli are named.
• Qadhdhafi said that latitude 32°30' marks the start of the Gulf of Sidra and that the line joins Misratah with Benghazi.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya has no terrorist training camps and that Palestinian camps are not in Libya.
• Libyan media reported that U.S. Marines were being readied on support ships and that carriers Saratoga and Coral Sea were involved.
• 1986-01-27: Qadhdhafi gives a French Television Channel 2 interview warning that a U.S. entry into the Gulf of Sidra would mean war.
• 1986-01-30: Libyan media report continuing U.S. 6th Fleet naval and air maneuvers off the Libyan coast, which were due to end 31 January.
