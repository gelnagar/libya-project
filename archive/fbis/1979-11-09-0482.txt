LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI HOLDS MEETING WITH REVOLUTIONARY COMMITTEE 


LD072148 Tripoli JANA in English 1631 GMT 7 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, November 7, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
called upon the police force to set up a revolutionary committee similar to that 
formed by the educational institutions and the armed forces, During his meeting 
yesterday in the People's Hall in Tripoli with the revolutionary committee of the 
educational institutions and the armed forces, he said that the revolutionary forces 

in Tripoli are the nucleus which mobilize other revolutionary committees in schools, 
institutes, colleges and military units. So this revolutionary nucleus is the base 
for the revolutionary forces which propagate that the revolutionary tide inside and 
outside Libya will spread if these committees take up their responsibilities genuinely. 
This present nucleus and other revolutionary forces which are not present, will 
complete the historic revolutionary duties at world level for an international people's 
revolution where the people's authority is established and all forms of exploitation 
abolished. In this case nothing will stop the revolutionary tide. 


He stressed that a practical stage will begin, during which the revolutionary forces 
will be selected so that every revolutionary would be qualified to assume the revolu- 
tionary tasks, for these are not easy. They must be effective in order to enhance 
the spirit of revolution in the mind of the revolutionary person for revolution is 
like religion, 


He said the revolution does not mean a salary, a house, land or a promotion. It is 
a faith. Things can be lost but faith is eternal. Therefore faith in the revolution 
is not related to any material factor. 


He went on to say: "The revolution is a difficult task which requires sacrifices. 
Only a minority assume this task. The revolutionary person should engage in a 
battle with itself in order to become a true revolutionary, The revolutionary com- 
mittees should follow the course of the revolutionary lesdership which developed 
from one revolutionary committee to many, so that the faith might spread from this 
committee to the others", 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that the movement of the revolutionary forces must 
be positive, at school, at college, in the street, everywhere. It must be a daily 
practice and a phenomenon characterizing this stage. In other words, the revolution 
should be the life of every revolutionary. If the revolutionary committees practice 
the revolutionary tasks as they are practiced by the leadership of the revolution, 
but in their own positions, there is no fear for the revolution, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added: "The revolutionary committees should not feel constrained 
in their implementation of the revolution. The revolutionary should distinguish 
between revolution and the authority which lies with the masses (jamahir). The 
people's congresses themselves pass the resolutions and the people's committee them- 
selves organize, although the revolutionary committees stand behind the scenes in 
order to spur the 'jamahir! to exercise authority and to expose the malpractices of 
the people's committees",

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi confirmed that the revolutionary committees do not represent 

the masses and that it is impossible for them to exercise an administration responsi- 
bility because this is the job of the people's committees. "If the revolutionary 
committees were to exercise authority on behalf of the masses then we would become 

an abominable dominating fascist power unwanted by the masses, This is a trap into 
which all the reform movements of the world have fallen: they eventually become 
dictatorial organs used against the masses," 


He explained that the revolutionary committees in the educational institutions 
should rule out any reactionary element opposed to the revolutionary transition, 

The revolutionary committees of the armed forces should eliminate any fascists among 
their ranks, The revolutionary committees in schools should succeed in militarizing 
the schools, 


He also called upon the revolutionary committees to stand against the counterrevolu- 
tionaries and against those who attack the Jamahiriyah from outside. He said that 
the Voice of the Revolution which is heard in Libya is a reply to the cold war which 
confronts the masses! enemies at home and abroad. It also expresses the view of the 
revolution and the Jamahiriyah throughout the mass media because the mass media is 
the voice of the revolution and not the mouth-piece of authority. 


AL-QADHDHAFI CALLS ON POLICE TO DOUBLE EFFORTS 
1D072142 Pripoli JANA in English 1740 GMT 7 Nov 79 ID 


[Text] Tripoli, November 7 (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
leader of the Great First of September Revolution, praised the officers, non- 
commissioned officers and privates of the Passport Department who organized a demon- 
stration this morning in support for the call for establishing revolutionary commit- 
tees in the police force, 


He said that the police force in the era of the masses is different than the police 
force of the despotic deposed monarchy. He called upon the police force to prove to 
the masses that the police is on the side of the revolution and no more a tool of 
oppression like in the past, 


He pointed out that the army officers and privates in the past era used to kill and 
torture the people and stand against the demonstrators because they were forced to 
do so, But after the eruption of the revolution and the establishment of the 
Jamahiriyah, which realized for them all their dreams, they joined the revolution 
and expressed their solidarity like all sectors of the society. 


He pointed out that during the past era the army officers had set up an air attack 
against the late Jamal 'Abd an-Nasir, but they were forced to do so. And when the 
revolution broke out and the "air defence" abolished, the aspiration and hopes of 

the army officers were achieved like those of other sectors of society. 


The leader of the Great First of September Revolution declared that most members of 
the police force had no links with the deposed pre-revolution regime. They are 
loyal to the revolution which realized for them the same gains it realized for other 
sectors of the population.

He said: "Those superficials who think that the police will neglect their duties, 
now that they were transformed into people's committees, should be told that before 
the revolution, policemen were oppressed by their chiefs and deputy-chiefs. Now 
they (?are liberated), after the people's authority was established and the gods and 
idols who used to oppress the policemen abolished. Now there is a people's committee 
acting under the people's authority because the revolution rejects the authority of 
idols and prostrates to nobody but the one God." 


In conclusion, Colonal al-Qadhdhafi, called on the police to double their efforts 
now that they are under the people's authority, the suitable atmosphere which will 
encourage the police to work for the people, under the authority of the people, 
abandoning forever the workship of idols. 


ENRICHED URANIUM REPORTEDLY DIVERTED TOWARD LIBYA 
1D061758 Paris Domestic Service in French 1700 GMT 6 Nov 79 ID 


[Text] Several loads of enriched uranium, extracted from the air mines in northern 
Niger, are reported to have disappeared in transportation. These loads have 
reportedly been diverted toward Libya. 


The disappearance was discovered 2 months ago. One of the trucks carrying the casks 
of uranium has been found empty. It is possible to make at least two atomic bombs 
with the stolen product, so the secret services are understandably extremely busy. 


JALLUD RECEIVES VENEZUELAN OIL MINISTER 
1D081055 JANA in English 0819 GMT 8 Nov 79 ID 


[Text] Tripoli, 8 Nov, JANA--Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud yesterday 
received Venezuela's minister of petroleum and the delegation accompanying him. 
The minister delivered a special message from the president of Venezuela to Colonel 
Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great First of September Revolution. The 
meeting at the "Shatab Palace", in Tripoli, was attended by the oil secretary, 'Izz 
ad-Din al-Mabruk, and the Venezuelan ambassador accredited to the Jamshiriyah.
