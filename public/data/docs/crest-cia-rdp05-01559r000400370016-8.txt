Summary (AI): A Cairo MENA broadcast relayed by FBIS reports that Libyan leader Muammar al-Qadhdhafi sent Egyptian President Anwar al-Sadat a congratulatory cable for Id al-Fitr, and Sadat replied with thanks and good wishes. This is Libya's foreign relations with Egypt, with no Tripoli-specific content.
• Qadhdhafi's cable was sent on behalf of the Libyan Arab people for Id al-Fitr.
• Sadat's reply was broadcast by Cairo MENA on 5 October 1975.
• The document is a FBIS wire dated October 1975 and is marked for release in 2012.
