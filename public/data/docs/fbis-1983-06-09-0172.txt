LIBYA

JANA: ‘Arafat Responsible for Beirut Shooting 
LDO81832 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1615 GMT 8 Jun 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 8 Jun (JANA) — It has been confirmed by the 
specialized organizations in the Jamahiriyah that Yasir “Arafat 
was behind the criminal assassination attempt on Brother “Abd 
al-Qadir Ghawgqah, member of the former People’s Committee 
of the Brotherhood Bureau in Beirut.
