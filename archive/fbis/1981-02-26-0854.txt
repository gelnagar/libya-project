LIBYA

SYRIAN FOREIGN MINISTER KHADDAM ARRIVES 23 FEB 


LD240940 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 24 Feb 81 


[Text] Brother ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam, Syrian deputy prime minister and foreign minister, 
and Brother Faruq al-Shar', minister of state for foreign affairs, last night arrived 
in Tripoli for a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah.

24 Feb Departure 

JN241853 Damascus SANA in Arabic 1750 GMT 24 Feb 81 

[Text] Tripoli, 24 Feb (SANA)--Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi received here today 
‘Abd al-Halim Khaddan, deputy prime minister and minister of foreign affairs, and Faruq 
ash-Shar', minister of state for foreign affairs. The meeting was attended by Dr ‘Ali 
"Abd as-Salah at-Turayki, Libyan secretary of foreign affairs. 


In a statement after the meeting Khaddam said that he delivered a message from President 
Hafiz al-Asad to his brother, Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, dealing with the current Arab situation, 
the means for strengthening the Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation and bilateral 
relations. 


Khaddam and Ash-Shar' and the delegation accompanying them left Tripoli for Damascus this 
evening at the end of a 24-hour visit to Libva. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES PFLP'S GEORGE HABASH 25 FEB 


LD252358 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 25 Feb 81 


[Text] The brother leader tonight received Brother George Habash, secretary general of the 
PFLP, who is currently visiting the Jamahiriyah. Brother George Habash praised the re- 
lationship of struggle which links the Great 1 September Revolution and the Palestinian 
revolution. He aiso praised the effective role of the Jamahiriyah in the national Front 
for Steadfastness and Confrontation and in confronting colonialist and reactionary in- 
trigues. He greeted the attitudes of the Great 1 September to the causes of freedom in the 
world and its support for the cause of the Palestinian people. He expressed appreciation 
of the humanitarian attitude of the Great 1 September Revolution which had deeply touched 
him when he underwent treatment lately.
