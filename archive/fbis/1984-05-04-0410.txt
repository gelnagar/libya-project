LIBYA

Police Discover Weapons in British Embassy 
LD040820 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0710 GMT 4 May 84 


[Text] Tripoli 4 May (JANA) A source at the People’s 
Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison 
announced that the Libyan police who searched the British 
Embassy yesterday evening, following the search of the Libyan 
Arab People’s Bureau in London by the British police, discovered 
weapons inside the British Embassy safes when they were opened 
in the presence of the first counsellor of the Italian Embassy, 
Cardelli Tuarketo, and second secretary of the Italian Embassy, 
Marco Tornita. 


These weapons are as follows: 


1. Webley pistol No 787-38 

2. Walther pistol No 501243 with 32 rounds of ammunition and 
a silencer 

3. Smith pistol No 12159H 

4. Smith pistol No 120745H 

5. Smith pistol No 9110H; 14 rounds of ammunition for Smith 
pistols 

6. 20 cannisters loaded with substances and gasses believed to be 
poisonous gas or nerve gas, and about 45 gas masks, which have 
been referred to the analysis laboratories. 


The source told JANA these weapons had no licence from the 
responsible people’s authorities in the Jamahiriyah and that 
when the registers of weapons licences granted to diplomatic 
missions in the Jamahiriyah were examined, it became certain 
the British Embassy in the Jamahiriyah did not obtain licences 
for these weapons. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Raps British, U.S. ‘Barbarism’ 
LD032115 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1840 GMT 3 May 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 3 May (JANA) — The brother commander of the 
revolution has categorically refuted the fabricated British allega 
tions that the British police discovered arms and ammunition 
inside the Libyan Arab People’s Bureau in London. He stated 
these things were brought in by the police and that we do not 
accept responsibility for anything in the building, because it was 
under the British [word indistinct] authority which actually 
brought the explosives and used them in entering the bureau. 


The brother commander was speaking at a press conference 
yesterday attended by correspondents of the world press, radio 
and news agencies. He said that at the beginning the British 
police placed a rifle in the keyhole and fired several shots to make 
an opening in the door. They then blew the door open with 
explosives. Therefore as of today we no longer recognize the 
existence of anything found in the bureau, because the British 
police used arms and explosives to enter the building. What the 
police have claimed is no more than barbaric British fabrications

with no basis whatsoever. Britain has proven itself to be an 
extremely backward country from a humanitarian and social 
point of view, exactly like the United States. These societies have 
exposed themselves, have proven themselves to be barbaric and 
have lost the respect of the world. It has been proven that we are 
the civilized ones. We are the ones opposed to the terrorism 
practiced by these countries, which have proven themselves to be 
foolish and trivial 


With regard to Britain’s unilateral decision to sever relations 
with the Jamahiriyah, and whether the Jamahiriyah will recip- 
rocate, the brother commander pointed out he does not have the 
authority to make such a decision and that the same applies to 
the People’s Committee of the People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau 
The Libyan people, through their basic conferences, are the ones 
who can make such a decisions. Therefore relations will remain 
unilaterally severed until the basic conferences in the Jama- 
hiriyah convene at the end of the year and decide as they see fit. 


Replying to a question about the terroristic way in which the 
British Government has been dealing with Libyan citizens in 
Britain and about whether the Jamahiriyah will treat the British 
in Libya ina similar manner, the brother commander pointed out 
there is no room for comparison between our behavior and that 
of Britain because we are civilized people whereas Britain is a 
barbaric state, as evidenced from the fact that it was Britain who 
colonized and destroyed the world. This proves its barbaric social 
backwardness backwardness other imperialist powers that 
brought miseries to Africa and Latin America, colonized by these 
powers for long centuries. This proves that these countries have 
no respect for the borders or freedom of others 


As far as we are concerned, we have not colonized anyone 
Throughout our history we have been struggling to defend our 
independence against these sinister aggressors. Consequently our 
action cannot be the same as that of barbaric Britain. Our 
behavior is characterized by responsibility, civilization and 
respect for international norms. | do not believe the Libyan 
people in their basic conferences will act in the same way as 
Britain acted toward the Libyans. Britain is now condemned for 
its barbaric behavior toward Libyan citizens, students, tourists 
and diplomats alike. 


The brother commander of the revolution pointed out that the 
countries of the world no longer feel secure in sending their 
citizens to Britain and the United States because they become 
subjected to persecution and house searches, as the Libyans are 
now. Even children have been searched and frightened at mid- 
night by British and American policy. He affirmed that both 
international law and diplomacy are not respected in the two 
barbaric, antihumanitarian countries, and that what is now 
happening in Britain provides proof of this. 


In any case, the commander said, | ask the Libyans not to treat 
the British in Libya as the British Government is treating Lib- 
yans in Britain, because its behavior is disgraceful and barbaric, 
and Libyan behavior should not be characterized this way. | do 
not believe the Libyans will do so.
