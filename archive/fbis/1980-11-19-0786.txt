LIBYA

OFFICIAL EXPLAINS POLICY TO SOCIALIST COUNTRY ENVOYS 
LD181412 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 18 Nov 80 


[Text] This morning the Foreign Liaison Office, while receiving the heads of the political 
missions of the socialist countries, told them of the position of the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah toward the imperialist-reactionary plot aimed at many parts of the 
Arab homeland after the downfall of Egypt, after its becoming an American-Zionist colony 
and after the American military presence and its concentrations in important positions in 
the Arab b-meland. This situation demands from the Jamahiriyah, from its sense of the 
gravity of the danger and for being faithful to its pan-Arab responsibilities and its 

right to self-defense, a duty imposing upon it confrontation to defeat the hostile plans, 
whatever the size of the challenge. 


The ambassadors of the friendly countries have shown understanding of this and have prom- 
ised to transmit the true picture to their governments. 


POLISARIO LEADER THANKS AL~QADHDHAFI FOR SUPPORT 


LD181434 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 18 Nov 80 


[Text] The brother leader has received a telegram from Brother Muhammad ‘Abd al-Aziz, 
general secretary of the Polisario Front and chairman of the Revolution Command Council 

of the Saharan Democratic Arab Republic [SDAR], in which he expressed his appreciation and 
gratitude for the Jamahiriyah's support to the Saharan people and their just cause. 
Brother Muhammah ‘Abd al-Aziz praised the efforts made by the Jamahiriyah for producing 
the UN resolution concerning the right of the Saharan people to self-determination and to 
obtain their legitimate rights. He pointed out that the SDAR appreciated the Jamahiriyah's 
support for the struggle of the Saharan people which effectively contributed and is con- 
tributing to their victories. He stressed that this support clearly proves the leading 
and courageous stand of the great 1 September revolution in supporting the downtrodden 
peopies and just human causes,
