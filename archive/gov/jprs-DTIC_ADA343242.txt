External  Debt  Claimed  Growing  Rapidly 

93AS0371C  Islamabad  THE  MUSLIM  in  English 
5  Dec  92  p  II 

[Text]  Karachi,  Dec  4 — External  debt  of  Pakistan  con¬ 
tinued  to  grow  in  the  past  two  years  and  reached  22.3 
billion  dollars  at  the  end  of  1991. 

In  terms  of  composition  of  its  debt,  Pakistan  remained 
heavily  dependent  on  official  sources  of  financing:  offi¬ 
cial  debt  represented  three-fourths  of  its  total  debt  in 
1990.  In  addition,  Pakistan,  more  than  any  other 
country  in  South  Asia,  still  relies  heavily  on  short-term 
borrowing.  In  1990,  short-term  borrowing  made  up  15 
percent  of  the  country’s  external  debt.
