[Text] Algiers, 23/5/83 (APS) — The celebration of the 10th 
anniversary of the starting of the Saharan people armed struggle 
in the presence of hundreds of delegates, was another opportunity 
for the whole international community to evaluate the extent of 
SDAR audience. 

This presence is undoubtedly a new diplomatic victory added to 
numerous others and all the delegations reaffirmed their support 
and acting solidarity with this just struggle. 

In this connection, Mr Boualem Benhamouda, minister of 
finances, representing Algeria, intervened to underline the 
soundness of the Saharan people cause and call Morocco and the 
Polisario Front to start direct negotiations to put an end to the 
conflict opposing the two peoples. 

Cuba foreign minister, Mr Isidoro Malmierca, urged the inter- 
national community to back the Polisario Front led struggle and 
condemned the aggression of the Moroccan Armed Forces 
against the Saharan territory. 

As for Mr Paul Chomat [APS spelling], member of the French 
Communist ‘Party Central Committee and of the National 
Assembly, he pointed out that France could and had to use its 
traditional relations with Morocco to activate the solution of the 
conflict for a fair and lasting peace. 

For Mr Raul Weexteen {APS spelling], responsible of the 
Maghreb to the French Socialist Party, he said his party was 
deeply attached to the OAU principles and would not spare any 
effort in Africa, out of any interference. 

Many other representatives including Vietnam trade minister, 
Bolivia minister to the presidency of the republic, Afghanistan 
vice minister of justice, also intervened to reaffirm their acting 
solidarity with the Saharan people and all hailed the military 
victories won by the Saharan Liberation Army combatants. 

Beside foreign countries’ representatives, delegates of different 
international organization also attended this event. Thus, repre- 
sentatives of the pan-African organization of women and the 
World Trade Union Federation renewed their determination to 
back the legitimate combat of the Saharan people.
