Beirut AL-SAFIR in Arabic 30 Jun 80 pp 1, 14 


{Interview with Muhammad Mahmud Hijazi, Secretary of the Libyan General 
People's Committee for Information, by Ahmad Isfahani, in Damascus; date 
not given] 


[Text] Libyan information officer tells AL-SAFIR: 
"We expect an Egyptian military attack within a month. 


"We will face Sadat with a united front until we 
bring him down. 


"Al-Assad assures us that national boundaries will 
cease to exist if we are attackec." 


The secretary of the Libyan General People's Committee for Information, 
Muhammad Mahmud Hijazi, denied that Libya has built any barriers, walls, 
or fortified military lines on its borders with Egypt. He invited ob- 
servers and journalists to go to the border area to see the situation for 
themselves. 


Hijazi said in an interview with AL-SAFIR in Damascus, during his partici- 
pation in the emergency conference of Arab cultural ministers, that cur- 
rent Egyptian moves on the Egyptian-Libyan border represent the second 
phase of the Camp David agreements. A confrontation between the Libyan 
and Egyptian armies is a matter to be reckoned with, perhaps soon. 


Hijazi pointed out that Libya will not start an attack against Egypt, be- 
cause the Libyan people are committed to Arab unity. However, Libya will 
repel any act of aggression against its people in self-defense. He said 
that the issue is not at present purely Libyan, bit rather a pan-Arab one, 
and that President Hafiz al-Assad has assured him that the entire Arab 
nation will come to Libya's aid if the latter is attacked.
