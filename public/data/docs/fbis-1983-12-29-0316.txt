LIBYA

JANA Editor Scores Egyptian Diplomacy 
LD291032 Tripoli JANA in English 0852 GMT 29 Dec 83 


[“JANA Political Editor Exposes Egyptian Regime Diplomacy” 
— JANA headline] 


[Text] Cairo, Rabi al Awal 24, Dec 29, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Deputy Foreign Minister of the Egyptian 
regime Usamah al-Baz yesterday said that Israel was wrong to 
describe ‘Arafat's visit to Egypt as an obstacle in the way of 
peace. According to his words, ‘Arafat should be considered as 
the voice of justice and moderation in the Palestinian movement. 
JANA’s political editor commented on Al-Baz’s speech by say- 
ing: 


(?No) matter how ambigious it was, Al-Baz’s announcement 
reflects the true nature of the Egyptian regime’s diplomacy which 
(?is) submission and surrender. After losing its credibility as a 
consequence of signing the Camp David accords, Egyptian diplo- 
macy was left with nothing but to please the Zionist enemy by 
repeating words of apology and submission. [words indistinct] 
announcements of Husni Mubarak and other leaders of the 
present Egyptian regime have, in fact, always coincided with this 
framework. 


It is a pity that Egypt is now taking the same tactics that the 
enemy was adopting before the late As-Sadat'’s ill fated visit. The 
major objective now is to court the enemy so as not to break off 
the deformed relationship established after Camp David. 


Hence come out the appeals of Husni Mubarak and Usamah 
al-Baz to convince the Zionist terrorists that ‘Arafat's meeting 
with the Egyptian president would do no harm to the interests of 
the Zionist entity. In short, Mubarak’s regime is collaborating 
with the Zionist enemy in its conspiracy against the Arab nation. 


Austria’s Kreisky Makes Statement Before Leaving 
LD281928 Tripoli JANA in English 1813 GMT 28 Dec 83 


[Text] Tripoli’s Rabi al Awal 23, Dec 28, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The former federal chancellor of Austria, 
Bruno Kreisky, has said that he was impressed with the new 
concept of people's authority and direct democracy practiced in 
Libya. 


He said in a statement before his departure this afternoon, “I 
voluntarily attended a session of the Basic People’s Congress and 
listened with enthusiasm to the discussion conducted in that 
congress and other congresses through the TV. I sincerely say 
that | have learned a lot from this experience.” 


Chancellor Kreisky added that these congresses are practically 
a “solid base for practicing democracy, although I do not know 
what the congresses would decide later. 


“There was an obvious fact to me that all the discussions were 
conducted in a very rational and responsible way and that the 


people, unlike other countries, do not hypocritically praise their 
leaders.” He said that criticism was strong and direct, which is 
the base for proper democracy and therefore “I would like here 
to congratulate the Libyan people for adopting this safe way of 
rule.” 


Chancellor Kreisky also told a JANA reporter that the great 
canal project, which is now underway, is considered one of the 
biggest projects in our time. 


On the current political situation in the area, Kreisky said: “Il am 
pleased and satisfied with the attitude of my good friend, Colonel 
Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi, who gives great attention the Palestin- 
ian cause. 


All peace-loving peoples are concerned about the explosive sit- 
uation in the Lebanon. There is a great need to exert every 
possible effort to calm down the situation there”.
