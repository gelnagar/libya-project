LIBYA

RECOGNITION GIVEN TO NEW GOVERNMENT IN IRAN 
LD122227 Tripoli JANA in English 1701 GMT 12 Feb 79 LD 


[Text ] Tripoli, February 12, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--In an expression of the recognition 
and support of the people's congresses in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
through their continuous people!s marches for the establishment of people's authority 

in Iran, the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has announced her recognition 
today of the people!s authority set up in Iran and considering it as the sole and legiti- 
mate representative of the brotherly Iranian Muslim people, 


A statement issued today by the Foreign Secretariat affirmed the stand and support of 
the Jamahiriyah to the Iranian Muslim people for whom it wished progress and prosperity 
under the leadership of the people's authority. 


AL-'UBAYDI CONGRATULATES IRAN'S BAZARGAN 12 FEB 
LD122232 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 12 Feb 79 LD 


(Text ] Chairman of the Popular General Committee ‘abd al-'!ati al-!Ubaydi has sent a 
telegram toDr Mehdi Bazargan, prime minister of the Islamic government in Iran. Here is 
the text: 


Brother Dr Mehdi Bazargan, prime minister of the Iranian Islamic government: In the name 
of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah Popular General Committee, it gives me 
pleasure, on the basis of the popular support manifested in the marches supporting the 
fraternal Muslim Iranian people*s revolution, to convey to you most sincere congratula- 
tions on the success of your popular uprising and on your assuming the leadership of the 
first popular authority in Iran, which will have a great effect on strengthening Islamic 
ties between the two fraternal peoples, We wish the fraternal Iranian people progress 

and prosperity. 


[Signed] 'abd al-'ati al-!Ubaydi, chairman of the Popular General Committee,
