Summary (AI): A 1982 NPIC imagery note reports the first identification in Libya of an Italian-built 155mm Palmaria self-propelled howitzer, parked in travel-lock position at Tarabulus Army Barracks East. It is cited as evidence of Libya diversifying its arms purchases.
• The weapon is a 155mm Palmaria SP howitzer developed for export by the Italian company OTO-Melara.
• It is built on the OF-40 main battle tank chassis and designed for a crew of five.
• Collateral reference: Janes Armor and Artillery 1981-82.
• The document is an NPIC attachment, Z-15893/82, dated 21 September 1982.
• 1982-09-21: First identification in Libya of an Italian-produced 155mm Palmaria self-propelled howitzer, parked in travel-lock position, on imagery.

1. (SWN) SIGNIFICANCE: AN ITALIAN SELF-PROPELLED (SP) 
HOWITZER WAS IDENTIFIED FOR THE FIRST TIME IN LIBYA. 

2. (SWN) REMARKS: ONE ITALIAN-PRODUCED 155MM PALMARIA SP 
HOWITZER WAS IDENTIFIED FOR THE FIRST TIME IN LIBYA AT 
TARABULUS ARMY BARRACKS EAST ON IMAGERY 25X1- 
THIS WEAPON, WHICH WAS PARKED IN THE TRAVEL-LOCK POSITION, 
MIGHT HAVE BEEN PRESENT AS EARLY HOWEVER, IMAGE 25XK1 
QUALITY PRECLUDED ITS IDENTIFICATION. THE PALMARIA WAS 
DEVELOPED FOR EXPORT BY THE ITALIAN COMPANY. OTO-MELARA. IT 
IS BUILT ON THE OF-40 MAIN BATTLE TANK CHASSIS AND IS 
DESIGNED FOR A CREW OF FIVE. 

(SWN) THE INTRODUCTION OF THIS SP HOWITZER TO LIBYA'S 
INVENTORY PROVIDES ADDITIONAL EVIDENCE OF LIBYA'S DESIRE TO 
CONTINUE TO- DIVERSIFY ITS ARMS PURCHASES. 25X1
