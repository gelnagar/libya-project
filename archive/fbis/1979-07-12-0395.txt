LIBYA

JALLUD RECEIVES VISITING NICARAGUAN DELEGATION 
ID111522 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 11 Jul 79 ID 


[Text ] This morning at the Peoples Palace in Tripoli Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad 
Jallud received the delegation of the Nicaraguan provisional national government which 
is currently visiting Libya. 


During the meeting, which was attended by the brother head of the External Liaison 
Office at the Secretariat General of the People's Congress, the latest developments in 
Nicaragua and the struggle waged by the Sandinist front in the face of Somoza!s fascist 
rule were discussed. Staff Major Jallud confirmed to the delegation Libya's firm stand 
toward consolidating the struggle of the Nicaraguan people against the rule of agent 
Somoza and its support for the world liberation movements in their struggle against all 
sorts of despotism and suppression, 


The members of the Nicaraguan delegation expressed high appreciation to the First of 
September Revolution and its leader brother Col Mu'tammar al-Qadhdhafi for their honorable 
stand toward the Nicaraguan people under the leadership of the Sandinist front against 
fascist Somoza. 


EEC OFFICIAL [EAVES TRIPOLI AFTER 3—DAY VISIT 
1D111110 Tripoli JANA in English 06.v GMT 10 Jul 79 D}D 
[Text ] Tripoli, July 11, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Mr. Claude Sichone, a member of the EEC 
committee on developing countries left Tripoli yesterday ending his three-day visit to 
the Socialist Peoples Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, During his visit he held talks on the 
honourable stands of the Jamahiriyah which has adopted a new world economic order far 
from the control of imperialist monopolies, 
The EEC official was seen off by the secretary of the General People's Committee for 
Planning, the assistant undersecretary for political affairs and a representative from 
the European Department at the Foreign Secretariat.
