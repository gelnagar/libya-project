At the end of the national council the final motion was adopted by the entire 
assembly. Among other things it considers that only a socialist policy which 
has progressive content and guarantees socialist liberties and works for 

social justice, will save the country from its crises. The motion also calls 
for the promulgation of a general amnesty for the abolition of unconstitutional 
laws and decrees (including the press law and the laws on strikes), the 
abolition of emergency courts, and the establishment of a constitutional 
council, the task of which would be to conform laws and decrees to the 

clauses of the constitution. 


It should be noted that the National Council appealed to all progressive 
forces to unite their efforts with a view to forming a progressive front or 
rally. In this context, negotiations seem to be in progress between certain 
Leftist trends and the MUP.

The second matter has to do with marketing olive oil. 


We still have that problem. We negotiate every year, and we reach an inadequate 
solution that we think is the lesser of two evils. Therefore, we told our fellow 
Algerians and Moroccans that they will have to take a united position at the mee-

| Question | It's been noticed that relations with Algeria have been relatively 
lukewarm for almost half a year. What is the reason for that? 


[Answer] Our colleague Abdelghani was supposed to pay us a visit last February, 
but he did not come because of domestic reasons in Algeria. Cooperation exists in 
Addis Ababa. Naturally, this [situation] has its influence, but I wish there were 
no influence. Our position is neutral and fundamental. We want normal relations 
with Algeria and with Morocco. It is in the interests of Algerians and Moroccans 
that we be neutral so we can make a contribution to the effort to bring us closer 
together and to devise solutions [to our common problems |. However, I believe 
that these relations are only slightly and are temporarily lukewarm. In fact, the 
word, lukewarm, is an exaggerated description of the situation. I have constant 
telephone communicacions with my Algerian colleague. Therefore, relations are not 
lukewarm, but there are Algerian questions about our position which will become 
clearer in time.
