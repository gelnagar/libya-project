Summary (AI): A March 1976 broadcast from Tripoli reports Gaddafi's brother Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi (as printed, "Col Mu'ammar") spoke at a celebration in the Abu Salim area of Tripoli marking the end of the last shanty in Tripoli and the handover of 1,000 families into new houses. He also attacked Egypt and Tunisia over migrant workers and alleged CIA links.
• The speech was broadcast by Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland on 12 March 1976.
• Qadhdhafi claimed about 190,000 Egyptians and 40,000 Tunisians were living in Libya.
• Qadhdhafi said more than 10,000 Tunisians had entered Libya illegally.
• 1976-03-12: Celebration marking the end of the last shanty in Tripoli and the transfer of 1,000 families into new houses, attended by Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and Prime Minister Abd al-Salam Jallud.
