Al-()adhdhafi Sends Message on Solidarity Day 
LDIO19S2 Tripoli JANA in English 1750 GMT 30 Nov 83 


[Text] New York, Safar 25, Nov 30, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The revolution’s leader confirmed that the encour- 
agement given by the U.S. for aggression and expansion through 
the use of most destructive arms and its efforts to ensure the 
continued occupation of Arab land by the Zionist enemy as well 
as the enemy's total disregard to international resolutions and its 
refusal to recognize the national rights of the Palestinian people, 
is far more important and should be discussed today. 


This confirmation was made in a letter sent by the leader of the 
revolution yesterday to the world community on the international 
solidarity day with the Palestinian people in its struggle against 
the Zionist enemy. The letter was read out by Jamahiriyah’s 
representative at a celebration organized by the U.N. 


The leader of the revolution said in his letter that the Palestinian 
cause will not be solved by capitulationist initiatives and neither 
by suspicious contacts which would only divide the Palestinians 
and weaken the Palestinian revolution. The Palestinians cause 
will be solved by powerful struggle and unity and clarity of 
foresight according to revolutionary aims of clear outlines. 


The leader added that the people of Jamahiriyah, some of whom 
sacriliced themselves in defence of the Palestinian cause,. reaf- 
fizms its determination to continue its support of the Palestinian 
people, by all means, until the whole of Palestine is liberated and 
the heroic Palestinian people have recovered their legitimate 
rights. 


He added that aggression against our people because of their 
principled stands will not weaken our determination; on the 
contrary it will increase our belief and strengthen our stand 
alongside the Palestinian people and their revolution.

Statement Issued on US. Spokesman’s Remarks 
LDIO1211 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 30 Nov 83 


{Text} Tripoli 30 Nov (JANA) — The People’s Foreign Liaison 
Bureau issued a statement yesterday concerning remarks by 
White House official spokesman Larry Speakes. Here is the text: 


White House spokesman Larry Speakes, commenting on the first 
round of in-depth talks held between Israeli and the United 
States, claimed that increased cooperation between America and 
what he called Israel does not conflict with the question of 
maintaining good relations with the moderate Arab states. 
Instead, he noted that it was intended to confront future threats 
from Libya, Syria, Iran, and the Soviet Union. 


In view of the gravity of these statements, and the facts which 
expose the American role in the campaign of aggression against 
the Arab nation, making clear the short- and long-term aims of 
the hostile plan, the People’s Committee of the People’s Foreign 
Liaison Bureau would like to confirm the following points: 


The regular and routine meetings between the leaders of the 
racist Zionist entity and successive American presidents are not 
only traditional, but are well-timed. They are held for the specific 
purpose of studying joint programs and plans of aimed at accel- 
crating subsequent links in the comprehensive chain of aggres- 
sion against the Arab nation. 


If the aims of these meetings were once characterized by some 
degree of secrecy in order to gain the advantage of surprise in 
military and political operations, one can now see — given the 
current circumstances of Arab dismemberment and incapacity 

that the strategic alliance has been openly and publicly 
declared in a manner which leaves no room for doubt concerning 
the relations of full partnership which exist between the two 
allies. 


A few days before the White House spokesman made his 
remarks, the terrorist Shamir made it clear to the American 
magazine T/ME that there are joint American- Zionist aims in 
Lebanon. Regrettably, not a single Arab official made the effort 
to consider the grave implications of this statement. 


Speakes’ remarks confirm that the next phase of the aggressive 
plan seeks to drag the Arab countries -- moderate in American 
terminology, meaning those which have not yet lost confidence 
in the American role despite the ferocity of the blows — under 
the umbrella of the American- Zionist strategic alliance. These 
nations would in turn gradually become an instrument for the 
implementation of the plans, the goal of which is to perpetuate 
American-Zionist hegemony over the Arab region and its 
human, national, and material resources. 


Moreover, remarks by the official American spokesman to the 
effect that the fullest possible U.S. support and backing for the 
Zionist entity is aimed at confronting the Jamahiriyah, Syria and 
Iran — as if they were alien forces to the region —- generally 
implies an attempt to perpetuate the dismantling of the region 
and to deal with it in stages. His comments also confirm the 
existence of a plan containing a system of phased priorities, the 
purpose of which is to strike at the forces of steadfastness and 
revolution in the Arab homeland and Islamic world. This would 
serve as a prelude to the next stage, which calls for finishing off 
the entire Arab and Islamic region. This is a fact which demands 
that the Arab countrics — all of which fall within the circle of 
aggression — be alert to the reality and dimensions of this hostile 
plan, from which no one will be cxcluded 


The attempt to link the Jamahiriyah, Syria, and Iran with the 
Soviet Union in the manner employed by the statement indicates 
a new U S.-Zionist return to the scandalous game of trying to 
place the Arabs and the Zionists in one trench in confronting the 
fictitious danger of Soviet penetration. There is no need to stress 
that the revolutionary Jamahiriyah — which refuses to join or 
be subservient to the umbrella of any of the major or minor 
powers — is linked to the Soviet Union only through relations of 
friendship based on mutual respect and appreciation for the 
support rendered by the countries of the socialist community for 
the just Arab causes. 


The People’s Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison would like to conclude by asking the following major 
question: Is there any difference between America and the 
Zionist entity in the shadow of the strategic alliance and the 
declared partnership for aggression? For our part, we declare 
that there is no difference between the Zionist enemy and 
America. America is the Zionist enemy, morcover, it is the most 
seriuos dangerous facing the Arab present and future.
