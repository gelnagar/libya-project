LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI, HONECKER SPEECHES AT 15 FEB DINNER 


LD171450 Tripoli JANA in English 1456 GMT 16 Feb 79 LD 


(Text] Tripoli, 16 Feb (JANA)--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 

lst of September Revolution and secretary~-general of the General People's Congress, 
welcomed Erich Honecker, the general secretary of the Socialist Unity Party of Germany 

and chairman of the Council of State in the German Democratic Republic, and the delegation 
accompanying him, to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah.
