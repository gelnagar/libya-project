LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Press Conference 1 June 
LDO022105 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1615 GMT 2 Jun 83 


{Text] Tripoli, 2 Jun (JANA) The brother leader of the 
revolution talked to the Arab media yesterday evening at a press 
conference attended by more than 50 journalists representing 
various media organs. The leader spoke about current issues of 
the Arab homeland, the realities of the Arab situation, the U.S 
imperialist onslaught and the long-term effects of the momentum 
of the Israeli death train that is storming the Arab homeland.
