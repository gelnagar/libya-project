Summary (AI): A January 1986 CIA Directorate of Intelligence memo assesses how Qadhafi may respond to US naval forces entering the Gulf of Sidra, covering Libyan air and naval forces, the SA-5 complex, and the effect of US economic sanctions and oil-industry exposure. Nothing in it is located in Tripoli itself.
• Libyans can scramble up to 125 missile-armed fighters in 24 hours, including MIG-23MLD and MIG-25 aircraft.
• Libyan Navy has 31 missile ships and six submarines; roughly a quarter of missile ships can fire.
• US trade with Libya was about $300 million in exports, mostly available elsewhere.
• Oil exports could fall by up to 200,000 b/d if US oil companies withdrew.
• About 400 to 500 US citizens remained in Libya after the 1982 sanctions.
• 1981-08: Only previous US naval penetration of the Gulf in the seven years before 1986; Libyan ships operated close to US ships but never fired.
