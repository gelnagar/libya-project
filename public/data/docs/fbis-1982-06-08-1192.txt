LIBYA

PLO OFFICE CALLS FOR FIGHTING VOLUNTEERS 


LDO72128 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 7 Jun 82 


[Text] The PLO office in the jamahiriyah today released a communique, urging Palestinians 
and sons of the Arab nation to volunteer to fight in defense of land and honor in the face 
of the barbaric Zionist assault to which our Arab people in Lebanon are being subjected. 


The communique says that the Palestinian Revolution and the Lebanese Nationalist Movement 
are fighting fierce battles against the Zionist attack backed by American imperiatism‘against 


Lebanon in order to eliminate the revolution of the Arab Palestinian people. 
The communique underlines that the heroic epics and splendid gallantry shown by the forces 
of the Palestinian Revolution and the Lebanese Nationalist Movement are a wholehearted 
defense of Palestine and the honor of the Arab nation from the ocean to the Gulf.
