IRANIAN DELEGATION ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI FOR TALKS 
LD241914 Tripoli JANA in English 1644 GM? 24 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, November 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--An Iranian delegation arrived last 
night in Tripoli, headed by Dr Muhammad Ali Hadi. During its visit the delegation will 
hold talks on developing relations between Iran and the Jamahiriyah. The delegation was 
welcomed at Tripoli International Airport by the General People's Committee secretary of 
municipalities, the head of the Liaison Bureau, an official from the Protocol Department 
and the P.L.0O. representative in Tripoli.

The members of the delegation expressed satisfactica for this visit which takes place 
at a time when the Islamic nation is facing colonialist schemes and conspiracies which 
aim at harming its independence and capabilities. 


The head of the delegation said that the Islamic nation is going through a stage in which 
it badly needs solidarity and the exchange of views concerning its crucial causes. Dr 
Hadi expressed his country's great respect for the support given by the Jamahiriyah to the 
Iranian Islamic Revolution in its stand against all U.S. challenges. He pointed out that 
Iran is at present directly confronting American imperialism and world Zionism, as the 
golden spearhead of the Islamic nation in the face of all schemes, particularly (Israeli) 
and American plots. 


He remarked that (Israel) and Zionism were the causes for the explosion of the Islamic 
Revolution in Iran against the shah!s regime which had strong links with ( Israel). But 
the Islamic Revolution, he said, broke those diplomatic and economic relations with 

( Israel) as soon as it started and created new ones with the Palestinian revolution, 
besides cutting off of11 supplies to (Israel) and South Africa, which represented 80 
percent of their needs. He added that the Islamic Revolution in Iran boycotted companies 
which traded with (Israel), considered itself a state in confrontation with (Israel), 

and showed readiness to participate in the drawing up of a plan to fight against (Israel) 
and liberate usurped Arab lands. 


Joint Statement 
LD261338 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 26 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] The following joint statement was issued today on the talks between the dele- 
gation of the Iranian Revolution Command Council and the Foreign Liaison Office at the 
Secretariat of the People's General Congress: 


In the period from 4 Muharram 1389 after the death of the prophet, equivalent to 23 Nov- 
ember 1979, to 7 Muharram 1389 after the death of the prophet, equivalent to 26 November 
1979, a delegation representing the Iranian Islamic Revolution Command Council and the 
Iranian Foreign Ministry visited the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Janahiriyah. The 
delegation consisted of brother Mohamad 'Ali Hadi, Hojjat ol-Islam Hadi Madrasi and 
Mohamad Saleh Hoseyn. 


During the visit the delegation members were received by the brother commander of the 
Great 1 September Revolution, the Secretariat of the People's General Congress and the 
revolutionary committees. The delegation held lengthy and deep discussions which were 
characterized by a spirit of fraternity, cordiality and complete understanding on all 
topics raised, 


The most important topics discussed were: the entrenchment of strategic relations be- 
tween the two revolutions in the Jamahiriyah and Iran, the Zionist and imperialist threats 
directed against the Islamic Revolution in Iran because of the firm stand adopted by the 
Iranian Muslim people toward imperialist designs and their demand for the surrender of 

the agent shah, who is using the United States as a haven. 


The fraternal delegation also heard a statement from the brother commander of the revo- 
lution fully explaining the concept of the Great 1 September Revolution and the people's 
authority principle which is in force in the Jamahiriyah, as expounded by the Green Book. 


V. 26 Nov 79 I 3 NORTH AFRICA 


The two sides affirm the following points: 


1. The two progressive Islamic revolutions in the Jamahiriyah and Iran are allied naturally 
and strategically. 


2. The two sides affirm the importance of the liberation of Palestine from Zionism, which 
usurped Palestinian territory, and from imperialism and foreign monopolies. They also 
affirm the need for the return of the Palestinian people to their land and homes. 


3. The Jamahiriyah considers any American or other military aggression against the Iranian 
Islamic Republic is an aggression against the Jamahiriyah itself. 


4, The two sides affirm the ArabeIranian fraternity and the need for the consolidation of 
this fraternity and its development for the good of the two nations, Arab and Iranian. 


5. The two sides consider any aggression against the Iranian Revolution as aggression 
against all Islamic peoples and forces of revolution in the world. 


6. The two sides strongly denounce the Camp David agreements and all their consequences. 
They ask theEgyptian people to act to topple the defeated Egyptian regime and to return 
Egypt to the Arab arena in the conflict against world Zionism, colonialism and imperialism. 


7. The two sides affirm that the two revolutions in Iran and the Jamahiriyah constitute a 
firm ground for an Islamic awakening which will embrace the entire Islamic world and place 
full authority in the hands of the Islamic masses, which will act as a tool for the 
realization of comprehensive unity. 


8. The two sides affirm their support for the Islamic and Arab world liberation movements 
in their struggle against colonialism, imperialism and Zionism. 


IRANIAN AMBASSADOR ON RELATIONS WITH LIBYA, U.S. 
ID242004 Tripoli JANA in English 1719 GMT 24 Nov 79 LD 


{Text] Kuwait, November 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Iranian ambassador to Kuwait, Dr. 
‘Ali Shams Ardakani, affirmed that the resumption of diplomatic relations between the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the Iranian Islamic Republic would strengthen 
the joint action of the two Islamic revolutions towards preserving the unity of the Islamic 
nation and consolidating the struggle against all forms of oppression in the world. In 

an interview withoour correspondent in Kuwait, the Iranian ambassador commended the positive, 
strong and genuine support offered by the Great lst of September revolution to the Iranian 
Revolution since its inception. 


The ambassador also said: "The Iranian people will never forget the stances of the Libyan 
Arab people and their great revolution in support of the Iranian people in their struggle 
@wainst all forms of oppression and enslavement. 


Dr. Ardakani accused the international mass media, contracted by the imperialist intelligence, 
of distorting the image of the Great lst of September revolution in the eyes of international 
public opinion and of viciously attempting to create misunderstandings and intrigues between 
both Islamic revolutions, in the Jamahiriyah and Iran. 


V. 26 Nov 79 I4 NORTH AFRICA 


On the occupation, by Iranian students of the American Embassy, Dr. Ardakani pointed out 
that this action reflected the people's strong will to get hold of the deposed shah, a 
criminal who should be punished for all the crimes he committed against the Iranian people. 


He described the deposed shah as a Nazi who deserves to be punished and insisted that the 
Iranian people's demand for his extradition comes first. If America wishes to improve its 
relations with Iran, it should answer the request of the people and hand over the criminal 
shah. 


Dr. Ardakani revealed that espionage equipment existed in the American Embassy in Tehran 
which, he said, was discovered by the students after the occupation. 


He said that the Iranian Government will try the hostages for espeionage and release those 
who prove innocent. He declared that also some documents indicating that U.S.A supervised 
the escape of Shahpur Bakhtiar, the shah's last premier, were also discovered. He affirmed 
that Bakhtiar was a former C.I.A man and a British agent since 1946. He said that the 
Iranian Government holds documents which prove this. 


He described the American decision to freeze the assets and Iranian funds in America as 
hysterical and rash. He said that such a decision will be applied against other countries. 


The ambassador of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah to Kuwait, who held a 
meeting yesterday with Dr. Ardakani, affirmed the strength of ties existing between the 
Libyan and Iranian peoples. 


He described their relations as revolutionary ones directed against oppression and dictator- 
ship and not were normal traditional relations. The Libyan ambassador hailed the stands of 
the Iranian Islamic Revolution towards the Arab cause and its political economic and cul- 
tural boycott of (Israel). 


YAR PRESIDENT ENDS LIBYAN VISIT, ISSUES STATEMENT 
LD241308 Tripoli JANA in English 0912 GMT 24 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Nov, JANA--The President of the Yemen Arab Republic 'Ali ‘Abdallah 
Salih, left Tripoli this morning after ending a two-day visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


Present at the Yemeni president's send-off at Tripoli International Airport were Staff 
Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, members of the General People's Committee, some Libyan 
Arab Army and police officers and the Yemeni ambassador in the Jamahiriyah. 


Before leaving, President 'Ali 'Abdallah Salih told our correspondent that he and his ac- 
companying delegation appreciated their visit the Jamahiriyah. 


He said: "We held talks with Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great lst of 
September Revolution during the visit, which resulted in positively developing strong re- 
lations between the two countries in the fields of development and construction. The 
Yemeni president praised the great rennaisance in the Jamahiriyah accomplished by the 
Great lst of September Revolution in different political, economic, and social fields. 


He added: "The achievements we are witnessing now in our "second" home, the Jamahiriyah, 
make us very optimistic and proud, and we wish the Libyan Arab people more progress and 
prosperity under the leadership of the Great lst of September Revolution and its leader 
Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi.
