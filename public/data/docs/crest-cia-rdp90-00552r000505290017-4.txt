Summary (AI): A CBS Evening News segment from November 1981 covers the Libyan connection involving former CIA employee Edwin Wilson and ex-CIA officer Frank Terpil, who were tied to explosives sales to Libya. A former Wilson aide, Douglas Schlachter, describes his dealings with Wilson and CIA-linked figures. The transcript is fragmentary and does not place anything in Tripoli.
• The CIA had denied for six months widespread reports that it was involved in any way with Wilson and Terpil.
• Wilson and Terpil were indicted for selling explosives to Libya.
• Schlachter says he met on a regular basis with top US intelligence officials before, during, and after his involvement with Wilson and Libya.
• Schlachter first met Wilson at the Crossroads in Fairfax County, where he serviced Wilson's car.
• Schlachter later managed Wilson's farm in Upperville, Virginia.
