LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI, SIAD BARRE HOLD CLOSED SESSION 


LD282145Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 28 Feb 78 LD 


[Excerpt] Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the great 1 September revolution, and 
Siad Barre, the Somali president, held a closed session of talks this evening. Talks 
also began between the Somali and Jamahiriyah sides.
