LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES AFRICAN WOMEN'S CONFERENCE 
1D060958 Tripoli JANA in English 0824 Gut 6 Mar 80 ID 


(Text ] Tripoli, March 6, JAMAHRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader 
of the Grand al-Fatah Revolution attended yesterday's evening session of the African 
Women's Conference, currently being held in Tripoli, and delivered a message in which he 
described the conference as an important step in Africa on the road for progress and 
unity. He said that the conference is taking place at a time that the African liberation 
movement is recording a semi-final victory against imperialisn. 


He added luckily enough for the freedom movement that ZANU-PF has won the recent election 
in Zimbabwe because it is a nationalist revolutionary, progressive force. The leader 
greeted the Zimbabwean people for this victory affirming that the Jamahriyah will contime 
its support to them. He greeted the Zimbabwean martyrs who paved the way for freedom of 
Africa. 


He announced that the victory of the Zimbabweans proved that Africa will win all the 
battles in the parts colonized by the racists. He said the victory of the Zimbabwean 
revolution indicated the defeat of racism in South Africa and Palestine. He pointed out 
that the destiny is common betweer Arabs and Africans in confrontation of racism because 
the links are very strong between the racist regimes in Palestine and South Africa and 
they have a common alliance between them. 


He affirmed that the African peoples should not waste any efforts in confronting racist 
Zionism, and that the victory of the people of Zimbabwe proves that we are able to defeat 
the racist regimes in South Africa and in occupied Palestine. He elaborated that 
colonialism created puppet regimes in the independent countries to guarantee sucking the 
blood or the Africans and these puppet regimes are playing the same role of the 
colonialist. For example the Moroccan regime took the role of the Spanish colonialism 
against the people of the Sahara. He added that it is a crime committed by the Moroccan 
regime to fight the neighbouring people of the Sahara and this can not be forgiven, and 
the Africans should stand with all their strength in support for the Popular Front for 
the Liberation of the Sagia al-Hamra and Rio de Oro--POLISARIO. He also pointed out the 
alliance of the defeated Egyptian president with the racists against freedom in Africa 
by putting the Egyptian capabilities to serve the Zionist enemy in Palestine including the 
waters of the Nile.

He said that this path and alliance links the defeated Egyptian Government with the racist 
government of South Africa because the alliance with the Israelis is an alliance with the 
racists of South Africa. 


He pointed out that the racist regime recorded a great victory in East Africa but is facing 
defeats in the south of the continent. He said: To defend Africa from the racist alliance, 
the Egyptian Government should be tightly beseiged and boycotted in all fields. Strong, 
comprehensive African sanctions should be imposed on it, in order that it sets an example 
to any agent of racism in Africa. He called upon the participants to fight and break off 
any relations of their countries with the Egyptian Government and to express this in the 
conference resolutions. 


He pointed out that there are colonialist bases renewed in some African countries and 
affirmed the necessity of fighting and confronting them. He said that any country asks 

{as received] foreign forces to intervene should be considered a protectorate. It should 
abandon the record of her national struggle against colonialism and explained that the 

great imperialist powers try to divide the world. Africa has become a farm for Europe and 
amine and region of monopoly. The imperialists describe the Africans as the slaves of 
Europe. He said: Western Europe is colonialist and racist. It built its wealth by robbing 
our resources. He pointed out that Christianity was introduced in Africa through colonialism. 
It is a tool of colonialism in Africa. Language was also a tool for cultural colonialisn. 
Through church, the Africans were colonialized spiritually; through companies, Africa was 
colonialized economically; and by military forces, the continent was colonialized mili- 
tarily. In conclusion, the leader called upon the African peoples to revolt to put an 

end to all forms of colonialism in Africa, because without the eradication of these forms, 
the freedom of Africa will not be complete.
