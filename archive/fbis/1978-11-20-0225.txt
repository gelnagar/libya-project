PEOPLE'S CONGRESS ISSUES STATEMENT ON AS-SADAT'S VISIT TO ISRAEL 
LD191156Y Tripoli JANA in English 0911 GMT 19 Nov 78 LD 


{Text ] Tripoli, Nov 17, JANA--The General Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress has 
issued a statement on the first anniversary of the bad omen visit of shame which was 
made by the defeated Egyptian president to occupied Palestine, on 19 November 1977. 


Following is the full text of the statement: 


On 19 November a year would have passed since the Egyptian president's visit of shame 

to occupied Jerusalem. This year's events have proven the correctness and soundness of 
the principled analysis of the patriotic and progressive forces with Egypt and at national 
level. They have affirmed the expectations of the Arab People's Congress which was held 
to confront the visit and its consequences. 


The Arab People's Congress had clarified that the visit was a grave turning point in the 
treasonous and capitulationist policy followed by the Egyptian president in the interest 
of international imperialism led by U.S. imperialism, racist Zionism and Arab reaction 
cOlluding with them. The congress also declared that the visit marked the start of 
complete recognition of the Zionist enemy, direct negotiations and separate settlement 
on the basis of complete surrender and acquiescence to the conditions of the enemy which 
has usurped Palestine and territories of three independent Arab states. 


Events have affirmed that the defeated Egyptian president has taken his aims to their 
natural conclusion after ignoring the Arab nation's struggles and sacrifices especially 
those made by the Egyptian Arab people and army. He has also ignored all Arab League and 
summit conference resolutions from Khartoum to Rabat to Baghdad. He has placed himself 
beyond the Arab nation's will by resuming direct negotiations in Cairo, ocoupied Jerusalem, 
Ismailia, and later under the direct supervision of the President of the United States 
signed the two "stable David" accords which frankly stipulated the total liquidation of 
the Palestinian cause and the denial of the existence of the Palestine Arab people. This 
has further entrenched the Zionist enemy's usurpation of Palestinian national soil and 
finally taken Egypt out of the national struggle against the common enemy of the Arab 
nation and placed it in a camp which opposed such a struggle. This new camp inoluded the 
Zionist enemy, the Egyptian regime and is supported by American imperialism against the 
Palestinian revolution, progressive Arab regimes, African and all peace-loving peoples.

The permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress, in its continuing struggle against 
this treasonous stance and all its results, is fortified by the Arab masses! will which 
refuses any squandering of the unshakable historic national rights of our glorious nation. 
It is also fortified by the Palestine revolution's guns and its sisterly struggling or- 
ganization in all Arab fields, by the heroic struggle of our Egyptian Arab people, and the 
Arab People's Court decision nullifying the two treasonous accords and the results of 
these treasonous stands which were embodied in the 'sStable David' accords, and the support 
of all peace, justice and freedom loving forces in the world, The Permanent Secretariat 
of the Arab People's Congress affirms that the Arab nation led by progressive national 
forces will continue their just and historic struggle to abort the Carter-Begin-As-Sadat 
plot and restore the normal course in the area so that the road towards progress and 
prosperity be opened to our nation and that the development of its material and human 
resources be for the benefit of its peoples and peace and security of the world, 


The Arab masses and their progressive democratic organisations are called upon on this 
painful occasion to escalate their struggle. 


Let shame befall those who commit treason. Victory to the struggle of our glorious 
Arab nation, 


USSR REPORTEDLY SUPPLIES LIBYA WITH NEW WEAPONS 

NC180918Y Doha QNA in Arabic 0845 GMT 18 Nov 78 NC 

[Text] Kuwait, 18 November--In a dispatch from Washington citing informed U.S. sources, 
the Kuwaiti newspaper AL-ANABA' reports today that the Soviet Union supplied Libya with 


modern military weapons recently. 


These weapons include 3 submarines, 60 tanks, 2 squadrons of TU-22 aircraft, and an

NIGER PRESIDENT KOUNTCHE ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 

LD171604Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 17 Nov 78 LD 

{Excerpt] Tripoli--The president of Niger, Seyni Kountche, arrived in Tripoli this 
afternoon on a few days visit to the Jamahiriyah, President Kountche was officially 
welcomed at the airport by staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, some of the secretaries, 
the Jamahiriyah's ambassador to Niamey, Niger's ambassador to Tripoli and some members 


of the diplomatic corps accredited to the Jamahiriyah. 


Jallud, Kountche Begin Talks 


LD171836Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1746 GMT 17 Nov 78 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, 17 Nov, JANA--Official talks between representatives of the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the Republic of Niger began this evening at the guest 
palace in Tripoli. 


The Libyan side was led by staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, and Niger's side by 
President Seyni Kountche, Participating in the talks on the Libyan side were Foreign 
Secretary Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki; Interior Secretary brother Yunis Abu al-Qasim; 
and labor and civil service secretary, brother Muhammad al-Tahir al-Mahjub. Those parti- 
cipating on Niger's side were the ministers of the interior and mines and hydrology.
