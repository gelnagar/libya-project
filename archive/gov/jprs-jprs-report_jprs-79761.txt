Proceeding from this premise, I am writing you the following explanations in hopes 
that you will be kind enough to publish them: 


1. As regards our part's request to meet the president, while our party issued a 
decree to its members to vote “not in agreement" in the referendum to elect the 
President, the secretary general of the party, prior to the referendum, requested 

a meeting in which he stated the reasons for this position. These may bs summarized 
by the fact that our party was opposed to the main policies of President al-Sadat, 
out of a position of principle, and that when President Muhammad Husni Mubarak de- 
clared his full commitment to these policies and positions, our party, in harmony 
with its position in principle, voted, along with its members and friends, that it 














was not in agreement. Had it done otherwise its previous opposition would have been 
based on personal foundations, not on an objective position of principle. 


Then again after the referendum our party requested a meeting to state our views on 
the current policies in general. 


2. As regards your remark that the party issued a statement in which it welcomed 
President Mubarak and that the party said that it was comforted because President 
Mubarak did not attack the Soviet Union or Libya, which you said provoked indignation 
because the president does not attack the Soviet Union, Libya or any other country, 
we would like to point out the following: 


A. The party did not happen to issue any statement tn that effect. 


B. The last statement issued by the secretariat general of the party was the one 

in which its members and friends called for a vote of “not in agreement” in the 
referendum to elect the president. No other statement was issued by the party since 
that. 


C. The fact of the matter is that the secretary general of the party gave a number 
of press interviews in which he underlined a number of the positive aspects contained 
in the president's statements and speeches, including the fact that President Husni 
Mubarak did not take a position of hostility to the opposition or persons in it, 

even those who did not vote for him in the referendum; that the president called 

for a dialogue with the opposition; and also that he did not attack Arab countries 
and issued orders to the press not to reply to the attacks issued by Arab countries, 
mentioning specifically Libya, and would not start a military attack on any Arab 
country, specifically Libya. 


He called for a fight against corruption and laxity and said that he would settle 

the conditions of the people who had been detained and would release anyone against 
whom no act that was criminal by law had not been proved. Also he did not attack the 
Soviet Union in his speeches and statements. 


It is no secret that quoting part of the statement not only disrupts the balance of 
the truth, it also perhaps on some occasions qives an impression contrary to the 
truth, which is what happened in reaard to this incident. In any event, our party 
does not conceal its position on the need to improve relations with our brother 
Arabs, among them Libya, and the need to return to a policy of nonalignment and 
balance in international relutions, especially the two superpowers, in a manner that 
will protect Eqypt's interests. 


3- As to what you published to the effect that an official source told MAYU “Why 
does the Grouping Party single out these two countries in particular -- every 
Eqyptian's affiliation must be with Egypt and all his thinking must be on Egypt's 
behalf!" 


That statement provokes indignation because it is not a true democratic tradition 


for anyone to make himself responsible for determining to what extent Egyptians are 
loyal to their country. Perhaps the official source needs to have us remind him 
that improving relations with our brother Arabs, including Libya, is one of the 
pillars for realizing Eqypt's interests and that Egypt cannot attain its real 














interests in conflict with its Arab brothers. In addition, the policy of nonalignment 
and adoption of a balanced position vis-a-vis the two superpowers, America and the 
Soviet Union, also lie at the heart of Egypt's interests. 


How creatly our Sarty wishes that that tune which casts doubts on the loyalty of 
Eqyptians for views they hold or which considers that loyalty to Egypt is the mon- 
opoly of people with a specific position or a specific view would vanish. In any 
event, the people alone are able to issue their verdict on the loyalty of a given 
party to the cause of the nation and the people. 


4, Our party appreciates the difficulty of the present circumstances and appre- 
ciates the need to concentrate ail efforts on facing terrorism under any uise.

ASSISTANT PROSECUTOR FOLLOWS UP ON TERRORIST INVESTIGATIONS 
Cairo AL-AHRAM in Arabic 1 Nov 81 p l 


/Article: "The Office To Follow up on Investigations in the Terrorist Cases Is 
under the Supervision of the Assistant Public Prosecutor"/ 


/Text/ It has been decided to establish an office to follow up on investigations 
into the terrorist elements that were arrested recently following the discovery of 
the true dimensions of their criminal scheme to overthrow the regime. 


' This decision was taken at a meeting held by Counsellor Ahmad Samir Sami, minister 
of justice, along with Counsellor Salah al-Rashidi, the public prosecutor, with 
the objective of supervising the ongoing investigations into all the terrorist 
elements and coordinating the investigations that are being made by the office of 
the public prosecutor and the military authorities, preparatory to combining the 
disposition of both. 


Heading the office is Counsellor Safwat ‘Abbas, a public lawyer. It is made up of 
Counsellors Sahib Hafiz and Mahir al-Jindi, Muhammad ‘Abdallah Mihran, public 
lawyers /sic/, Ibrahim 'Abd-al-Mutaliby and Muhammad Hanafi, head of the public 
prosecutor's office. Supervising the office's activities is Counsellor ‘Atif Zaki, 
assistant public prosecutor.

religious groups engaged in against the Egyptian people began. This secure village 
turned into a nest for armed terrorist societies, an arsenal for storing weapons,

The people of the village were not silent about this terrorist activity which

against the members of the terrorist groups. The families to which the terrorists 
were related by blood declared that they washed their hands of them and other 
families helped the security men pursue the terrorists and gave them guidance in 
arresting them. 


After the terrorist groups had committed the latest vile crimes, the al-Zumr 
family, to which the terrorists ‘Abbud and Tariq al-Zumr belong, demanded that 
these two criminals be executed, and the family said that it would not take de-
