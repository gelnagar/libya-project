LIBYA

CONGRESS COMMITTEE PREPARES 'INDICTMENT! OF AS-SADAT 


LD231843Y Tripoli JANA in English 1630 GMT 23 Oct 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 23 October, JANA--The Work and Follow-up Committee resumed its work 
Since the end of the extraordinary session in which a decision was made to hold emergency 
sessions for the court on 28 October, in Tripoli and for the Arab congress on 15 November 
in Damascus, 


The Work Committee is currently preparing the main working paper which will be submitted 
before the court (the bill of indictment), in accordance with a study approved by the 
Permanent Secretariat in it's seventh session held on the 26th of last September and the 
statements issued by nationalist and progressive Arab sources, i.e., the statement 
released by the National Progressive Unionist Party on the 25th of last September, and 
the contribution of a number of specialists in political sciences and law. Meanwhile, 
the Work Committee has already extended invitations to the parties due to attend. 


The holding of the court emergency session is expected to be of utmost importance as it 
would define the Arab progressive national forces stands towards the treason of the defeated 
Egyptian president. 


The committee will continue its work im preparation for the Arab people's congress which 
will be an important political event formulating the will of the Arab masses and applying 
it against the conspiracy and treachery.

NEWSPAPER CALLS FOR DESTRUCTION OF SAUDI IDOLS 


LD231603¥ Tripoli AL-USBU! AS-SIYASI in Arabic 16 Oct 78 LD 


[Muhammad Ibrahim Zikri article: "Let the Idols of the Kingdom of Reaction Be Smashed ] 


[Excerpts] Since our masses decided to adopt the Book of God that can never be wrong as 

the source of legislation, reactionary media mouthpieces--particularly the Saudi press-- 

have launched a vicious campaign against this people, who have decided to restore to Islam 

its bright face and to adopt it as the launching point to build a new humanitarian civilization 
similar to that which reached its peak in the era of 'Umar Ibn al-Khattab, when Islam 

was at its high tide. 


. falsehoods and lies which the Saudi papers AL-BILAD--which no one reads --published 
on 17 August 1978 and AL-MADINAH AL-MUNAWARRAH published on 16 Ramadan 1398 Hegira and 
their poisonous attacks on our people's progress and our revolution's achievements in the 
political and economic fields and on the achievement of social justice in this good land, 
cannot be allowed to pass without a response to Saudi reaction, which oppresses the Saudi 
Arab people in Hejaz. Having read these falsehoods I must expose the real nature of Saudi 
reaction, which is up to its ears in superficialities, and tear off the figleaf with which 
it is trying to cover its shame. 


What is happening in Saudi Arabia? 


What is happening in Saudi Arabia is diametrically opposed--as a whole and in detail-- 
to Islamic laws as well as being contrary to the fundamental principles of ethics, 
human values and human rights. 


The Saudi oil prices spend millions, squander their people's wealth and prostrate 
themselves at the feet of cabaret girls in Paris, London and Monte Carlo as well as in 
roulette halls and other centers of corruption and debauchery in the West, 


Despite its denial, Saudi reaction has virtually approved of prostitution and drinking 
and even of worse things, both inside and outside the country. 


At home the palaces of the princes, their retinues and those close to them are the 
source of corruption, moral decay, debauchery and violation of Islam, 


Saudi reaction, which oppresses the people like a nightmare in Hejaz, allows the 
plundering of people's wealth contrary to Islamic tenets and on the pretext of false in- 
terpretations of such tenets by dervishes of sects that have deviated from Islam and of 
drivel spoken by fools, 


Saudi reaction is deeply sunk in the mud of U.S. imperialism to an extent that is no longer 
secret to anyhody. Its insistence on keeping foreign bases in the country to protect it 
against the winds of popular revolution is also no secret; nor is its resort tc 

physical liquidation and the suppression of any honorable voice, 


It is not therefore surprising that Saudi reaction should be hostile to us and should 

spread the venom of its black hatred against the people of the Jamahiriyah, who have adopted 
the Koranic laws as the law of society, This is because the Koran rejects all the debauched 
practices that characterize Saudi rule and constitute the base and the bulwark of this 
backward and condemned regime,
