LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Telegram to Tunisia’s Bourguiba 
LD221643 Tripoli JANA in English 1415 GMT 22 Jul 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 12, July 22/JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The leader has sent a telegram to President Habib 
Bourguiba of Tunisia in answer to a telegram received by him. 
It reads as follows: 


I was pleased to receive your telegram which was an important 
response to the joint achievement of our two brotherly people 
culminating in the plans and projects agreed upon in the first 
historic meeting. 


This advance task between our two sister countries requires your 
continued support and unrelenting guidance for it to bear fruit. 
By doing so we have laid the ground for a strong union based on 
integration until full unity is achieved. 


Your guidance to the Tunisian ministers had the effect of making 
the work of the higher committee a success. 


May you long live as a father to guide this test of unity and may 
you prosper in good health. 


Forward we march and the struggle continues until all the aims 
of the great Arab nation in freedom socialism and full unity are 
realised.
