LIBYA

NONATTENDANCE AT ISLAMIC SUMMIT EXPLAINED 


LD280032 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 27 Jan 81 


[Text] The People's Foreign Liaison Bureau spokesman told the JANA reporter that it is 

not politically possible for the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah to attend the 
Islamic Conference summit while this conference is held on the soil of a state which uni- 
laterally broke off relations with the other side. He added that the Saudi authorities' 
action of unilaterally breaking off political relations will create problems for the 
pilgrims who want to go on a pilgrimage to the sacred house of God, be they Libyan pilgrims 
or pilgrims from any other state with which Saudi Arabia has severed its political relations. 


AFRICAN LEADERS SUPPORT LIBYAN PRESENCE IN CHAD 


Rwanda President 
LD272030 Tripoli JANA in English 1825 GMT 27 Jan 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 27 Jan (JANA)--The president of the Republic of Rwanda has declared his 
country's support for the Libyan Arab presence in Chad affirming that it was a legitimate 
presence based on legal foundations Chadian Government [as received]. He said that no body 
had the right to demand withdrawing these forces from Chad but the party which had asked 
for their presence. The Rwandan president affirmed, to a Libyan popular delegation, that 
the Rwandan people stood behind the Jamahiriyah people whom they regarded as the African 
peoples’ vanguard. 


Sierra Leone Foreign Minister 
LD272010 Tripoli JANA in English 1827 GMT 27 Jan 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 27 Jan, (JANA)--Sierra Leone's foreign minister has affirmed his 
country's high regard for Jamahiriyah's role in backing African causes. The minister 
informed the General People's Committee's secretary, during their meeting in Tripoli, that 
Sierra Leone was fully convinced that Jamahiriyah played the role, which the African 
countries ought to have fulfilled, in sending armed forces to Chad to realize peace which 
has now been achieved after the Chadian revolution's victory. 


The minister expressed his regret at the different views held by some African presidents 
who took part in Lome's meetings resulting in stigmatising Libya's name, which the Sierra 
Leone's president would not desire. 


He also expressed his firm belief that Jamahiriyah would not give hostile forces the 
opportunity to interfere in the African continent's affairs. He reaffirmed that President 
Stevens of Sierra Leone and president of the Organisation of African Unity desired coopera- 
tion with Jamahiriyah in order to prevent interference in African affairs pointing to 
French military reinforcements in Central Africa. 


He added that Sierra Leone's president highly appreciated Jamahiriyah's role to realize 
peace and security in Africa, and that he appreciated her stand always demanding the resolu- 
tion of African problems by the Africans themselves. 

Cape Verde President 
LD272032 Tripoli JANA in English 1836 GMT 27 Jan 81 
[Text] Praia, 27 Jan (JANA)--President Aristides Maria Pereira, the president of Cape 


Verde islands, has affirmed his country's support for and solidarity with the brotherly 
Chadian people in their struggle for liberty and dignity.

The president denounced, in a meeting with the Jamahiriyah secretary of the Peoples Com- 
mittee of the Foreign Liaison Bureau in the capital Praia, the hostile campaign being 
launched by the imperialist circles against the Arab Libyan people because of their staunch 
support for the Chadian people. 


The president stressed that his country regarded the present government of Chad, headed 
by Goukouni Oueddei as the legitimate government in the country, which was entitled to 
seek aid from any country. 


The secretary of the People's Committee of the Foreign Liaison Bureau pointed out to 
journalists that the presence of Libyan troops in Chad was in response to a request Ly the 
Chadian legitimate government and that the presence of Libyan forces in Chad was not aimed 
against any particular neighbouring country. He added that while some countries subser- 
vient to France waged a virulent hostile attack against the humanitarian stands of 
Jamahiriyah, those same countries kept silent towards the pouring of French troops into 
Central Africa and their attempts to change the regimes in Africa as they desired. He 
added that it would be more appropriate for these countries to discuss the danger posed 

by the reinforcement of African forces in Africa [as received], because this danger 
threatened the entire continent.
