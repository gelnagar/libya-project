LIBYA

JANA: ‘Revolution’ in Fatah’s Al-Biga’ Forces 
LD121827 Tripoli JANA in English 1710 GMT 12 May 83 


[Text] Beirut, Rajab 30, May 12, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY A revolution broke out within Fatah movement in 
Al-Biga’ valley 


Abu Salih [Nimr Salih] and Abu Mousa dominated most of 
Fatah troops in Al-Biga’ valley and captured the road leading to 
Syria and circled Abu Jihad (Khalil al-Wazir) declaring that this 
is the real Fatah and Fatah’s revolution. 


Abu Salih rejected reconciliation which some forces tried to 
conduct. He called for a trial to be held for some officials 
responsible for the Beirut battle who left the refugee camps 
without defence till they were destroyed as well as those who 
conspired against the resistance and left Lebanon. 


On the other hand Abu ‘Ammar [Yasir ‘Arafat] arrested six 
commanders from Abu Nidal [Sabri al-Banna] group, executed 
them and mutilated their bodies with knives and threw them in 
the streets. This led to an atmosphere of confusion and disgust 
among the factions of the resistance
