LIBYA CLOSES PLO BENGHAZI OFFICE, EXPELS OFFICIALS 
NC162250 Paris AFP in English 2241 GmT 16 Dec 79 NC 


[Excerpt] Beirut, Dec 16 (AFP)--scalating its fight with the Palestinian Liberation 
Organization (PLO), Libya today closed the PLO office in Benghazi and ordered the 
expulsion of three top PLO leaders, the Palestinian news agency WAFA reported. 


The agency also reported that Libyan authorities have surrounded a PLO office at Tripoli 
and sent armed men into the building. 


It cited reliable Palestinian suxrces as saying that the PLO has asked certain Arab 
nations to mediate the conflict with Libya and study Libya's "suspected aggression" 
against the Palestinian people. 


Ordered expelled were Abu Tariq, Abu Sayir and Ibrahim Tubawi, assistant to the PLO 
chief in Libya who was also recently expelled, 


STUDENTS DEMONSTRATE, CONDEMN FATAH LEADERSHIP 
LD161511 Tripoli JANA in English 1403 GmT 16 Dec 79 LD 


{Text] Bani Walid, Dec 16, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The town of Bani Walid was yester- 
day the venue of a massive student demonstration in which pupils from all the region's 
schools and institutes participated. 


Students condemned "Fatah's" leadership for its ungrateful attitude and the insolent 
allegations waged against the Great 1 September Revolution, its historic leadership and 
the Libyan Arab people. 


The demonstration which started from the 7th of April school, traversed the town's 
streets chanting slogans in support of the Great 1 September Revolution which aids all 
self-respecting revolutionaries, and called for support of all fighters who believe that 
armed struggle is the only road to liberation, 


The demonstrators affirmed the Libyan Arab people's belief in the righteous cause of 
Palestine and total support for all those who believe in armed struggle as the only 
means to achieve liberation, and who reject a life of luxury. 


The demonstrators sent a cable to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirming their absolute commit- 
ment in the Great 1 September's thoughts and dicta. They also affirmed their full 
support for the speech of the leader delivered on the day of solidarity with the 
Palestinian Arab people. 


The assistant secretary of the basic people's congress of Bani Walid said that the 
masses of the congress will resume their meetings today in an extraordinary session to 
discuss the memorandum concerning the stand of Fatah's leadership towards the 

Great 1 September Revolution, its historic leadership and the Libyan people as a whole.

COMMENTARY QUESTIONS FATAH'S 'CAMPAIGN OF LIES' 
LD142105 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 14 Dec 79 LD 
{Unattributed commentary] 


[Excerpts] The Palestine revolution's strategic objective is to set up a solid, firm base 
in an Arab land which would accept becoming a base for continuing the fedayeen action. 
This strategic objective of the Palestine revolution has been achieved in the land of the 
Jamahiriyah, which the Great 1 September Revolution turned into a base for the revolution 
when it destroyed all the shackles which fettered the movements of the Palestinian masses 
and opened the doors to them, enabling them to reorganize in the manner they wished when 
it declared that the land of the Jamahiriyah was open for the movement of the fedayeen 
action toward Palestine=-even across Egypt--and when it prompted the Palestinian masses 

to formulate their own national popular unity and when it mobilized these masses for the 
escalation of their armed struggle against the Zionist enemey. 


What else does the Palestine revolution want? It has always dreamed of securing a base 
and a center for itself on Arab soil and fought the reactionary forces for this purpose. 
Now the dream has come true in the Jamahiriyah. Why then is this met with rejection and a 
feverish compaign of lies and fabrications propagated by the Fatah leadership against the 
Great 1 September Revolution and its historic leadership? 


When the Libyan people employ all their resources toward liberating Palestine and the 
escalation of the armed struggle, they are only exercising their legitimate right of 
self-defense and defense of Arabism and the very pan-Arab existence. For the Zionist 
imperialist threat is not directed against Palestine alone, but against the entire Arab 
nations. 


LIBYA SUPPORTS OIL PRICE HIKE AT OPEC MEETING 
LD161126 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1015 GMT 16 Dec 79 ID 


[Text] OPEC, 16 Dec--Jamahiriyah Oil Secretary 'Izz ad-Din al-Mabruk has expressed his 
hope that OPEC will deduct 50 cents from the price of every barrel to make up a total 
sum of $5 billion annually on the basis of the current OPEC production, which stands at 
present at 33 million barrels a day, to assist the developing countries. 


Venezuela, which will play host to the 5list OPEC conference Tuesday, supports the question 
of the growing financial subsidy which is in addition to the $500 million approved during 
the OPEC conference in Geneva last June. 


Venezuela decided Friday not to apply the $6 increase in the price of a barrel of oil, 
which came into force yesterday, against the Latin American and Caribbean countries whose 
purchases of Venezuelan oil in 1978 amounted to 156,000 barrels daily, which is half of 
their crude oil imports.

ALGERIAN FLN DELEGATION VISITING LIBYA 
LD161201 Tripoli JANA in English 0807 GMT 16 Dec 79 LD 


(Text ] Tripoli, Dec 16, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Mr Abouallem Dellouci, member of the 
Central Committee of the Algerian National Liberation Front Party arrived in Tripoli 
yesterday heading a popular delegation representing the different political and locational 
organizations in Algeria. 


In a statement to our correspondent, Mr Dellouci expressed delight for being able to visit 
the Jamahiriyah. He said his visit aims at drawing detailed projects for bolstering (?new) 
activities and for establishing strong ties between popular organisations inboth countries. 


He said specialized committees have been set up to further develop popular relations 
between the Algerian National Liberation Front Party and the General People's Congress 
secretariat. The Algerian delegation's visit cores within the context of the unification 
dialogue between the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and Algeria. 

POLISARIO DELEGATION ENDS VISIT TO LIBYA 

LD132136 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 13 Dec 79 ID 


[Text] Brother Mohamed Abdul Aziz, POLISARIO secretary general and the accompanying 
delegation left Tripoli this evening at the end of a visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


In a statement to JANA, Brother Mohamed Abdul Aziz praised the policy of the Great

as well as its confrontation with the Arab nation's enemies and the frustrating of their
