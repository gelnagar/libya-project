Summary (AI): A 1986 interagency White House memo (NSC) reactivates a working group on economic measures against Libya and sets a June 30, 1986 deadline for U.S. oil company activity in Libya to cease. It also lists the agenda and participants of a June 18, 1986 Crisis Pre-Planning Group meeting on Libya.
• The Libya Task Force sub-group on Economic Measures was to be reactivated to monitor and coordinate existing measures and follow-up to the Tokyo Economic Summit.
• The sub-group was previously co-chaired by State and NSC.
• The June 18 CPPG agenda covered intelligence on internal stability and terrorism, allied cooperation on sanctions, the refined product ban, residual supplier initiative, and Tokyo Summit financial follow-up such as closing People's bureaus.
• The memo is classified SECRET and declassifiable on OADR basis.
• 1986-06-18: Crisis Pre-Planning Group meeting on Libya to review political and economic steps toward Libya
• 1986-06-30: Deadline for cessation of U.S. oil company activity in Libya

SUBJECT: Interagency Coordination of Economic Measures 
toward Libya (S) 

Friday's 
At xpdayts CPPG meeting on Libya it was decided that the Libya 
Task Force sub-group on Economic Measures should be reactivated. 
The group would monitor and coordinate implementation of existing 
measures as well as those being undertaken as follow-up to the 
Tokyo Economic Summit. In the past, this group has been 
co-chaired by State and NSC. Elaine Morton is now the NSC action 
officer for Libya and North Africa and will represent the NSC as 
its co-chairman. (S) 

The CPPG requests that, as a first priority of business, the 
sub-group frame a public and congressional affairs strategy 
geared to the June 30, 1986 deadline for the cessation of U.S. 

oil company activity in Libya. (S)
