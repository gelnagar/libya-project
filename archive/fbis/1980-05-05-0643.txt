LIBYA

JOINT LIBYAN-PDRY STATEMENT ISSUED ON JALLUD VISIT 
LDO22246 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 2 May 80 


[Text] A joint Libyan-Yemeni statement was issued in Tripoli and Aden todaym the visit 
of brother Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud to the PDRY. The following is the text 
of the statement: 


At the invitation of brother 'Ali Nasir Muhammad, the secretary general of the Central 
Committee and chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme People's Council and prime minister 
of the PDRY, brother Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud paid a cordial visit to the PDRY 
from 15 to 17 Jumada al-Thaniyah 1389 from the death of the Prophet, corresponding to 

30 April to 2 May 1980 A.D., at the head of a Libyan Arab delegation. Brother Staff 

Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and the accompanying delegation were given a warm popular 
welcome which reflected the fraternal ties which bind the Arab people in the two countries 
together. During the visit intensive bilateral talks were held between brother 'Ali 
Nasir Muhammad, the secretary general of the Central Committee and chairman of the 

Supreme People's Council Presidium and brother Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud in 

an atmosphere of understanding and cordiality reflecting the ties of fraternity and 
struggle which exist between the two countries. The talks centered on the tasks of the 
Arab revolutionary forces in the grave stage through which the Arab nation is passing 

in order to counter reaction and the plots of imperialism, Zionism and reaction and which 
are directed against the progressive revolutionary forces and the Arab existence in 
particular. 


The tito sides reviewed the needs of the present stages and their requisites. They also 
discussed the bilateral relations of cooperation between the two countries and the fateful 
Arab issues as well as the international issues of common interest. They also discussed 
the importance of the cohesion of the great 1 September revolution and the 14 October 
revolution in the march of the national liberation struggle. 


The Libyan Arab side headed by brother Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and the PDRY 
side headed by brother 'Ali Nasir Muhammad, the secretary general of the Central Committee 
and chairman of the Supreme People's Council Presidium and prime minister held important 
talks covering the bilateral relations between them. They expressed their satisfaction 

at the development of relations between them. They also discussed the fields of coopera- 
tion between the two fraternal countries and stressed the need for their development in 

the interest of the Arab people in the Jamahiriyah and the PDRY and their progress within 
the framework of progressive changes taking place in both countries and also in the interest 
of bolstering the struggle of the Arab nation.

The two sides paid special attention to the grave situation in the Arab area and the 
developments resulting from the strategic weakness that led to the departure of Egypt 
from the struggle of its Arab nation and its joining of the American-Zionist-As-Sadat 
alliance which now threatens the Arab nation and its progress forces. 


Brother Staff Maj ‘Abd al-Salam Ahmad Jallud praised the important role played by the 
Yemeni pecple in the history of the Arab nation and Islam, stressing the ability of the 
Yemeni people to foil all the plots that seek to prevent it from regaining its effective 
historical role. 


The two sides affirmed the importance of adherence to and work for the realization of 
Yemeni unity as being a national and pan-Arab revolutionary demand, on the bases of the 
statements of Tripoli, Kuwait, San'a! and others and as being a first step toward the 
realization of a comprehensive Arab unity founded on progressive, political, economic 
and social bases, to reply to the challenges of imperialism, Zionism and Arab reaction. 


The PDRY side greeted the continued initiatives and unceasing efforts exerted by 
brother Col Mu'tammar al-Qadhdhafi, commander of the Great 1 September Revolution, for 
the realization @f any unity, including Yemeni unity. 


The Yemeni side affirmed that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah [SPLAJ] 

has now become in direct confrontation with the American-Zionist-As-Sadat alliance which 

has become a tool for striking at the progressive revolutionary forces in the Arab home- 

land. In this regard, the Yemeni people express their support for the Libyan Arab people 
in intercepting and striking at any aggressive attempt aimed at harming their unity. 


The two sides firmly declare their adherence to the principles and objectives of the 
National Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation and their work to bolster it and in- 
erease its effectiveness, to carry out its resolutions and to stress its role in the 
Arab struggle to foil the plot of capitulation. They stress the need for commitment 

to the implementation of the resolutions of the Baghdad summit conference and those of 
the Arab ministers of foreign affairs and economy. They also stress the need for the 
development of the front and for the laying down of a unified Arab strategy to face the 
challenges of imperialism and Zionism. 


The two sides affirm their faith in the Arab masses, who have the true stake in the Arab 
revolution. They also stress their commitment to work for the realization of the 

genuine effective presence of the masses in a direct challenge to their enemies internally 
and externally. 


The two sides cundemn all divisionist attempts that seek to break the ranks of the Arab 
nation represented in the Arab People's Congress and its permanent secretariat. 


The two sides affirm their determination to resist all attempts that seek to circumvent 
the Arab stand that opposes all capitulationist solutions. In this respect, they greet 
the role played by brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, commander of the Great 1 September 
Revolution, for the success of the fourth conference of the Front of Steadfastness and 
Confrontation which was held in Tripoli. 


The two sides reiterate the extreme importance of intensifying the boycott against the 
current Egyptian regime on a comprehensive basis--that regime which is trying to lead 
the Egyptian people away from their Arab nation and drive Egypt into an alliance with 
the Zionist-American enemy.

The two sides assert the importance of bolstering the struggle of the Egyptian Arab 
people with all their factions to enable them to resist the treason being committed by 
the As-Sadat regime and the Zionist American infiltration. They also reiterate the 
importance of strengthening the national and pan-Arab progressive forces in their 
struggle to topple the agent As-Sadat regime and bring Egypt back into the arena of 
pan-Arab struggle against U.S. imperialism, Zionism and reaction. 


The two sides declare their support for the armed struggle of the Palestinian people 

against the racist Zionist enemy. They affirm their firm support for the struggle of 
the Palestinian people led by the PLO for the complete liberation of their homeland, 

Palestine. 


The two sides reaffirm the right of the Palestinian people to work from all the Arab 
fronts surrounding the occupied homeland. They also affirm that the armed struggle is 
the road to the liberation of Palestine. 


The two sides affirm their support for the struggle of Lebanese Arab people for the 
safeguarding of their Arab character, unity and independence. They also reaffirm the 
Lebanese Arab people's steadfastness and support for their brothers, the Palestinian 
people, in the face of imperialism and Zionism. 


The two sides laud the steadfastness of fraternal Syria in the face of internal and 
external plots. 


The two sides denounce the repressive expansionist policy pursued by the agent Moroccan 
regime against the people of the Sahara led by the POLISARIO and the Saharan people's 
right to determine their fate and establish their independent state. 


The two sides reiterate their solidarity and support for the Iranian revolution in its 
struggle against U.S, imperialist plots. They condemn the American aggression on the 
land of Iran considering it a terrorist act and a flagrant violation of all international 
laws and usage and a threat--a direct threat to the area's security and stability. 


The two sides affirm their firm stand on the side of the Afghan revolution and condemn 
the imperialist reactionary plots against it. 


The two sides condemn all the reactionary practices which harm the essence of true Islam 
and affirm that Islam is a progressive religion that has come to help the weak on earth 
and that it is in a constant comprehensive revolution. 


The two sides affirm their support for the legitimate government in Chad which came about 
as a result of the Lagos declaration. They declare their support for it and condemn the 
imperialist conspiracy and the parties involved in it in Chad. The two sides call for 
the speedy withdrawal of all the imperialist forces and bases from Chad so that the 
Chadian people will be able to restore stability and establish progress according to 
their own will, 


The two sides affirm their support for the African liberation movements in Namibia end 
South Africa against the racist colonialist invaders. They affirm their support for all 
the liberation movements in the world which fight against imperialism and reaction.

The two sides also showed interest in the area of the Red Sea, the Indian Ocean and the 
Arab Gulf and expressed their concern over the acts leading to the escalation of tension 
in this sensitive part of the world. They called on the area's peoples to work to keep 
the area free of foreign military bases and suspect imperialist military security pacts, 
for the security of the areais the responsibility of its peoples. 


In this respect, the two sides condemned the presence of American fleets and bases and 
strongly rejected the U.S. threats to occupy the area. 


The two sides affirm the close link between the security of the Mediterranean and that 
of the Arab area. In this respect, the two sides affirm their support for the Republic 
of Malta which has declared its neutrality and evicted the imperialist forces from its 
territories. 


The two sides express their adherence to the aims and principles of the nonaligned 
movement as an important and positive element in international relations, effectively 
contributing to the bolstering of the struggle against imperialism and to the bolstering 
of the peoples seeking liberation from the yoke of imperialism and colonial hegemony. 


The two sides affirm the importance of friendship between the Arabs and the socialist 
bloc headed by the USSR, They greet the role played by the socialist bloc in bolstering 
the Arab causes. They also affirm the importance of bolstering and developing these 
relations in a manner which will serve the Arab causes in the face of the plots of 
imperialism, Zionism and reaction, as well as serving their common interests with the 
peoples of the socialist bloc. 


The two sides express their conviction that continued contacts between their two revolu- 
tions will help to promote the bilateral relations between them and strengthen their 
steadfast stands toward the causes of destiny. 


Brother Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud expressed his utmost thanks and gratitude 
for the warm welcome and hospitality given to him and members of his delegation. He 
addressed an invitation to his brother 'Ali Nasir Muhammad, the secretary general of 
the Central Committee, chairman of the Supreme People's Council Presidium and prime 
minister, from his brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the commander of the Great 1 
September Revolution to visit the SPLAJ,’ The invitation was accepted and its date will 
be fixed later.
