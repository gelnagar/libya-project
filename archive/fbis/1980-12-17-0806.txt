LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFT, AL~ASAD HOLD MERGER TALKS 16 DEC 


LD161350 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 16 Dec 80 


[Excerpt] A meeting between the leader of the revolution and brother President Hafiz 
al-Asad, president of the Syrian Arab Republic, was held this morning. Following the 
meeting Letween the brother leader of the revolution and President Hafiz al-Asad, 
president of the Syrian Arab Republic, the leaders of the Socialist People’s Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah and the Syrian Arab Republic held a working session this morning on 
the merger unity between the Arab people in the two sister regions. 


CMSN PREPARES DRAFT CONSTITUTION FOR REFERENDUM 


LD162249 Nouakchott Domestic Service in Arabic 2030 GMT 16 Dec 80 


[Excerpt] The Military Committee for National Salvation has decided in its recent 
session to begin the 3d stage of the 10 July movement, namely the restoration of 
democratic institutions. In this connection the committee has prepared a draft con- 
stitution which will be submitted for public debate before a referendum is held on it. 
The draft constitution, which takes into account the characteristics and recent history 
of our people, will guarantee basic freedoms for citizens.
