LIBYA

NICOSIA EMBASSY TRANSFORMED INTO PEOPLE'S BUREAU 


LD021554 Tripoli JANA in English 1409 GMT 2 Nov 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 2 Nov (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--As a means of advancing the popular 
revolution and in confirmation of the direct relationships between peoples and not 
through representatives or ambassadors... [JANA ellipsis] The Libyan Arab masses and 
students yesterday morning occupied the Libyan Embassy in Nicosia, Cyprus, and trans- 
formed it into a people's bureau, also forming a people's committee to run it. 


The masses occupying the embassy issued a statement affirming their adherence to 
people's authority and the era of the masses. Libyan Arab students in Cyprus also sent 
a cable to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirming their solidarity with revolutionary forces 

in Cyprus and the revolutionary dicta of the Green Book. 


EXPLOSION REPORTEDLY OCCURS IN RIYADH; 21 DEAD 
LD311150 Tripoli JANA in English 0807 GMT 31 Oct 80 


[Text] Riyadh, Oct 31 (JANA)--Saudi sources revealed yesterday that the explosion 
which destroyed a building near the grand mosque, 2 days ago, has resulted in the 

death of 21 pilgrims and the wounding of 42 others. The explosion, the cause of 

which is not known yet, greatly frightened the residents of the area and put them to 
flight. Eyewitnesses said some of the inhabitants attributed the explosion to an armed 
movement, while others thought it was the making of the ruling authorities, in order 

to justify their accusation as a prelude to their liquidation. Saudi media have tried 
to refute the news item referring to the explosion accusing Arab news media of making 
it up.
