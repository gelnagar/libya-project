LIBYA

ENVOY: FOREIGNERS HAVE NOTHING TO FEAR 


LD171616 Hamburg DPA in German 1510 GMT 17 Dec 81 


[Text] Bonn, 17 Dec (DPA) -- The head of the Libyan People's Bureau, that is to say the 
embassy, Mahdi Imbarash, has reaffirmed the guarantee of safety for all foreigners living 
in Libya, especially U.S. citizens. He told newsmen in Bonn today that so far 67 U.S. 
citizens have complied with the U.S. administration's appeal to leave Libya. 


He called U.S. accuations about alleged Libyan plans to murder President Ronald Reagan 
and about Libyan expansionist plans in Africa part of a slander campaign against his 
country, intended to provide the pretext for U.S. military action. 


The Libyan diplomat stressed that U.S. citizens and other foreigners in Libya have nothing 
to fear. But even if all Americans leave the country, the Libyan oil industry will be able 
to cope. In this connection, he praised the offers by other Arab countries, particularly 
Kuwait, to make its technological experience available for oil production in Libya. 


He welcomed the fact that European countries, he mentioned no names, are not supporting 
the American "blackmail" of Libya. Asked if the American-Libyan confrontation could strain 
relations between Bonn and Tripoli, he said: "The Federal German Republic is not America."
