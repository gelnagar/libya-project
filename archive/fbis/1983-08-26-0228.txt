LIBYA

AL-ZAHAF AL-AKHDAR Attacks U.S. ‘Megalomania’ 
PM251325 London Al-Zahaf al-Akhdar in English 

20 Aug 83 pp 1, 4 
{Unattributed article: “Defending the Arab Nation”} 


[Text] Where will American megalomania lead the US? Towards 
what fate is the Reagan administration pushing human society? 


There are American fleets armed to the teeth with the most 
modern and destructive equipment roaming the oceans of the 
world, and threatening peoples whose only offence is to long for 
freedom and final emancipation. In Central America, the US has 
been getting its ships and troops to suppress the escalating 
revolution in El Salvador, to apply pressure on the revolutionary 
regime in Nicaragua and to prop up the crumbling agent regimes 
in the region. 


For these same objectives, Reagan intends to carry out military 
exercises in the Caribbean region during the next six months, 
which will involve 22,000 troops and about 20 American naval 
vessels to terrorise the nations of the region, especially the 
revolutionary forces, and so prevent them from effecting any 
revolutionary change which would threaten American monop- 
olist interests there. 


Base for Aggression 


In Chad, the USA is attempting to establish a foothold in order 
to turn it into a base for aggression against the nations of the 
region, under the pretext of the alleged Libyan danger to Africa. 
The USA is still sending arms to Hissein Habre and is pres- 
surising agent regimes in the area to support this mercenary 
rebel. Such regimes as Egypt, Sudan and Zaire have already 
obeyed American orders in this respect. Meanwhile, the Ameri- 
can administration has been deliberating with France over the 
despatch of military groups under cover of false allegations of 
Libyan intervention in the war by the alleged bombing of Faya 
Largeau. But one should recall that Numayri’s deputy has said 
in an interview withan Arabic magazine, published in London 
recently, that the US has contacted them to take action to support 
Habre. This deputy also claimed that there are what he called 
‘French and American strategic interests in Chad’ and that both 
France and the USA are attempting to achieve these interests. 


In the Gulf of Sidra, the American Sixth fleet has been engaged 
in manoeuvres aimed at terrorising and provoking the Jama- 
hiriyah as a prelude to committing direct aggression against 
Libya, as has become manifestly clear from developments in the 
region. This is an attempt to curtail the Jamahiriyah’s interna- 
tional, African and Arab role in the national liberation move- 
ments 


In Egypt, Sudan and Somalia, the USA is preparing for large 
scale military exercises, which are an extension of those it carried 
out last year in these Arab countries. Troops of the Rapid 
Intervention Force and military aircraft including the AWACS 
spy planes have been sent to Egypt. The American administration 


itself admitted that the AWACS were sent to spy on the Jama- 
hiriyah and monitor the movements of Libyan forces. 


What sort of American hallucination is this which undermines 
the stability of the world and threatens world peace and security? 
Who gave the US the right to appoint itself international police- 
man claiming to defend freedom and human rights while in fact 
it defends its own economic and strategic interests and attempts 
to impose its hegemony over weak nations? With what right does 
the US send its fleets and armies to Central America, Chad, 
Egypt, Sudan, Somalia and Oman? None of the peoples of these 
countries has requested the US to defend it. The invitation came 
from agent reactionary regimes, installed by the American secret 
service, in these countries. The Americans have sent their fleets 
and troops to protect those regimes which submit to American 
will, not to defend the peoples of these countries who long for the 
day of release from the threat of American terror hanging over 
their heads. 


The United States of America is dealing a heavy blow tothe Arab 
peoples of Egypt, Sudan, Somalia and Oman by claiming that it 
had come to defend them, while there are men and women in 
these countries capable of defending the people without the help 
of the U.S. Is it not ironic too that the U.S. should claim that it 
has come to defend these countries while it is the big power 
aggressor threatening world stability as well as the partner of the 
zionist enemy in its crimes and acts of aggression against the 
Arab nation? 


The Arab people in Egypt, Sudan, Somalia and Oman must rise 
up to destroy the agent regimes which bring shame on the Arab 
nation. 


These reactionary regimes stand naked before the Arab masses, 
who have uncovered the role of betrayal and intrigues of these 
regimes. Such behaviour has enabled this new crusader enemy 
to control the destiny of the Arab hmeland and has allowed it to 
humiliate the Arab nation. 


American delusions of grandeur and mad terrorist actions have 
been increasing as a result of the growing revolutionary tide of 
liberation which has been spreading throughout the world where 
it threatens America’s vast interests. America’s actions in the 
Caribbean region are only a response to the growing liberation 
movements in El Salvador, Honduras and other Central Ameri- 
can countries. Likewise, the American intervention in Chad to 
save Habre is a retaliation against the growing African liberation 
movement and the continuing victories being achieved against 
the agent and reactionary forces. Are not the American move- 
ments in the Gulf of Sidra and the intended manoeuvres in Egypt, 
Sudan, Somalia and Oman in response to the growing importance 
of the leading liberation role of the Jamahiriyah? The increase 
of the Arab liberation movement in Lebanon and the occupied 
Arab territories against the Zionist enemy and in Egypt, Sudan, 
Somalia and Oman against the reactionary agent regimes there 
also needs mentioning. 


The United States’ aim behind its actions in the Gulf of Sidra, 
its military manoeuvres in Egypt, Sudan, Somalia and Oman and 
its attempts to form Chad into a base for aggression and subver- 
sion is to terrorise the Arab Libyan people and contain the 


international role of liberation by the Great Al-Fatih Revolution. 
Furthermore, Reagan aims to silence the voice of revolution and 
freedom coming out from the Jamahiriyah and to try and destroy 
its popular authority. This means directing a mortal blow at the 
world liberation movement, which finds in the Jamahiriyah its 
prime support and foundation. But Reagan’s administration 
should know that the Arab Libyan people has decided to turn the 
waters of the Gulf of Sidra into red waters in defence of its 
freedom and independence and the freedom and honour of the 
Arab nation. If that administration does not overcome its present 
state of mad aggression and remember the lesson of its defeat in 
Vietnam, then the Arab Libyan people is capable of reminding 
it of that lesson. 


While continuing its preparations for convening the sixth session 
of the Revolutionary Committee within the next few weeks, the 
revolutionary force of the Committees would like to reiterate that 
by blockading, provoking and fighting the Jamahiriyah, America 
is fighting the unity of the Arab nation. Those fools in the Arab 
homeland from the Ocean to the Gulf who believe that the battle 
is just between the Jamahiriyah and the U.S. must realise that 
this is a battle between America and the whole Arab nation. It 
is a battle directed by the Americans against the future and the 
power of the Arab nation. 


The Arab Libyans should also understand that by confronting 
the Americans they do not do so to defend a barren desert, a 
million barrels of oil a day or a small and poor people. But by 
confronting the USA with all its might, terror and continuing 
crimes, the Arab Libyan men and women would be doing so to 
defend the future of the Arab nation and the unionist and 
nationalist principles in which the Great Al-Fatih Revolution 
believes. It will be in defence of the revolutionary and progressive 
policies which the Libyans have decided in their popular con- 
gresses and in defence of the honour of the Arab nation and its 
right to unity and existence. Let the confrontation continue until 
America is defeated and the bastions of fascists, seekers of power 
and regionalists in Egypt, Sudan, Somalia and Oman are 
destroyed

conditions for a national reconciliation, for peaceful negotiations 
between the Transitional National Union Government and its 
adversary. This is also a reality
