Summary (AI): A 1988 State Department cable (outgoing message) asks that FBIS's Nicosia bureau consider regular coverage of the joint Maltese-Libyan "Voice of the Mediterranean" radio station. It concerns media monitoring logistics, with no details about Tripoli sites or events.
• Document is a CONFIDENTIAL outgoing cable dated 31 August 1988, referencing Valletta message 018974.
• FBIS was instructing its Nicosia, Cyprus bureau to consider regular coverage of the Joint Maltese-Libyan "Voice of the Mediterranean" radio station.
• Coverage depends on the Nicosia bureau's assessment of impact on monitorial resources.
• The cable was also routed to the FBIS bureau at Caversham/Reading, UK, for information relating to the BBC monitoring partner.
