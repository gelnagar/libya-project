[Text] At this stage, when U.S. imperialism is escalating its policy of consolidating its 
military forces and its political and military provocations, in addition to direct 
military agression, the United States has carried out its’ treacherous aggression against 
the Libyan Jamahiriyah in clear violation of Libyan national sovereignty, airspace and 
territorial waters. This was done on the direct instructions of U.S. President Ronald 
Reagan, as demonstrated by the insistence by the U.S. administration on holding its 

6th Fleet maneuvers in the Gulf of Sidra, which is an integral part of Libyan territory, 
as it has officially declared at the United Nations since 1973. This led to the 19 August 
attack on the two Libyan aircraft, which constituted a flagrant violation of international 
law and a direct threat to world peace and security. This grave provocation expresses 

the imperialist nature of the United States and its neocolonialist policy, which relies 

on the use of force in embarking on military attacks and which is the main source of 
international terrorism. 


It is at this stage that the international conference of solidarity with the people of the 
Jamahiriyah is convened in Tripoli. The conference comprises 58 participating members 
representing prominent political and ideological personalities, leaders and delegates of 
political parties, liberation movements, trade unions, mass organizations, committees of 
peace and solidarity and international organizations. Some 260 organizations and 86 state, 
local and international bodies are represented. 


The successful convention of the Tripoli conference at this moment clearly shows the 
increasing awareness of the world public of the gravity of imperialist, Zionist and 
reactionary plans and policies against the peoples’ national independence, in particular 
against the Libyan Arab people. The Tripoli conference is an important step and a victory 
for the development of mutual cooperation and solidarity between the world's patriotic, 
progressive democratic forces and the Great 1 September Revolution. It strengthens the 

ties of unity among the peoples in their struggle against the vicious attacks by imperialists, 
Zionists and reactionary forces. 


The conference studied the U.S. plans and the aggression against the people of the 
Jamahiriyah and the Great 1 September Revolution. It also studied the role of agent 
As-Sadat, which is seen in the information and military provocations on the eastern 
borders of the Jamahiriyah and in the granting of facilities to the American and Zionist 
military presence. The conference reached the conclusion that this constitutes a direct 
threat to peace and security. The conference regards these plans and moves as a part of 
U.S. plotting, which relies on the use of force in Africa and Latin America and which 
threatens the aspirations of the peoples and their peaceful hopes. 


U.S. administration sources have themselves admitted the existence of plans to destabilize 
and overthrow the Libyan Jamahiriyah regime and even to assassinate the historic leader 

of the Great 1 September Revolution, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, who has always stood 
firmly on the side of the struggles for freedom in all parts of the world. 


Therefore, the conference strongly condemns the U.S. military aggression and the — sae 
provocative and subversive plans of the CIA. It also condemns all the political, cae 
and economic plots of the United States and its allies in the region against the aa all 
and revolutionary leadership of the Jamahiriyah. 


The main reasons for the U.S. aggression and the current aggravation of thi. aggression

the Camp David accords and its allies, its firm and constant policy of supporting the PLO, 
the Lebanese National Movement and Syria and its struggle for liberation, independence, 
democracy, social progress and world peace. 


The conference points out, in this connection, the importance of the tripartite friendship 
treaty signed by the Libyan Jamahiriyah, the PDRY and socialist Ethiopia. It considers 
this treaty an important step in defending the independence of the peoples and in estab- 
lishing just peace in the region. 


The plots being woven by the United States and imperialism and their allies in the region 
aim at undermining the revolutionary achievements of the Libyan Arab people in the fields 
of economic independence and independent development free of the domination of inter- 
national monopolies, for the sake of achieving progress and social prosperity. The 
Tripoli international conference stresses its solidarity with the Libyan Arab people, who 
are establishing on their territory a progressive jamahiri society in which man can score 
a victory for his aim within the framework of the slogan: Power, wealth and arms are in 
the hands of the people. 


Thus, the Tripoli international conference first declares its pride in the historic role 
and achievements of the Great al-Fatah Revolution, which has liberated the Libyan Arab

withdrawal of the U.S. and British military bases--the revolution which seeks to (?realize)

dom fighters. This has made the Jamahiriyah one of the most important bases of struggle

[Text] The international conference on solidarity with the people of the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, after studying the dangers arising from the imperialist, 
Zionist and reactionary plots against Libya, and in affirmation of the need to consolidate 
the campaign of solidarity with a view to confronting and foiling these plots and plans, 
has made the following decisions: 


A. With regard to activities: 


1. To send a delegation representing the conference to the United Nations 
organization to contact the member states and to explain the dangers of 
the U.S. plot and its military provocations against the Libyan Arab 
people. The delegation is to ask the United Nations organization to 
take the necessary steps to prevent the implementation of the aggressive 
plans of the United States against Libya. The conference also approved 
the dispatch of other delegations to the Arab League, to the OAU head- 
quarters and to the nonaligned countries for the same purpose. 


To form delegations representing the democratic forces to visit a 
number of countries with a view to explaining the dangers of the plots 
which Libya is facing through meetings with representatives of politi- 
cal parties, mass organizations and press circles. 


To organize delegations from various countries to visit the 

Jamahiriyah so that representatives of public opinion in these countries 
might acquaint themselves with the achievements of the Libyan 
Jamahiriyah and its policy, which advocates freedom, progress and peace. 


To raise the question of the U.S. plots and aggressive threats against 
the Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah at all forums on both the Arab and inter- 
national levels. 


To develop relations of mutual cooperation and solidarity between the 
national and progressive forces with a view to creating an international 
movement against the subversive exercises of the United States against 
the Libyan Jamahiriyah and many other countries. 


To examine the possibility of organizing an international conference 

to discuss the political, economic, military and information conspiracy 
of the United States and its allies to create instability in countries 
struggling to strengthen their national, political and economic inde- 
pendence and to achieve peace, freedom and progress. The conference will 
adopt the best means to confront and foil these moves.

[Text] At the invitation of the International Secretariat for Solidarity With the Arab 
People and Their Central Issue--Palestine, the international conference for solidarity 
with the people of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah [SPLAJ] was held in 
Tripoli during the period 28-31 August 1981. Many organizations, popular and progressive 
democratic forces and international liberation movements attended the conference. The 
number of organizations present was 360, coming from 86 countries, while the number of 
participants amounted to more than 650 delegates. 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 1 September Revolution, delivered an 
important speech at the opening of the conference in which he thanked the participants 
for their solidarity with the people of the Jamahiriyah in its struggle against American 
imperialism and its conspiracies. He called for the necessity of consolidating the 
efforts of all liberation movements and progressive and democratic forces against these 
conspiracies, reaffirming that the Jamahiriyah was prepared to offer all means, including 
its readiness to be an international center for struggle against imperialism, Zionism and 
reaction. He proposed many practical measures for this purpose. The conference adopted 
the speech by the Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi as a basic document of the conference 
in appreciation of its political and combat value. In their speeches the participants 
reaffirmed their firm support for the struggle of the Libyan Arab people and its 
revolutionary leadership against all conspiracies being hatched by U.S. imperialism and 
the Camp David alliance. 


The conference declared its strong condemnation and denunciation of the recent U.S. 
aggression on Jamahiriyah territorial waters and airspace by the U.S. 6th Fleet in the 
Gulf of Sidra. The conference reaffirmed also its pride in the revolutionary struggling 
role being played by the Jamahiriyah in backing and being in alliance with all the 
liberation movements, organizations and the popular, democratic and progressive forces 
which are confronting U.S. imperialism and its agents. In this connection, the conference 
issued the following documents: 


1. Message to the Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the 
Great 1 September revolution, from the international conference on 
solidarity with the SPLAJ people. 


2. A program of action for solidarity with the people of the SPLAJ 
against imperialist threats by the United States. 


3. A document of resolutions of solidarity with the people of the 
SPLAJ against conspiracies and threats of an imperialist-Zionist- 
reactionary aggression. 


4. In reaffirming the common struggle of the Libyan Arab people 
with the peoples of Africa, the conference acquainted itself with the 
serious situation in southern Africa, where the Angolan people are 
facing an aggression and military invasion by the racist Pretoria 
regime. The conference issued a resolution for solidarity with the 
people and the Republic of Angola against the aggression and invasion 
by the racist regime in South Africa. 


5. A statement was issued by the conference calling for the halting 
of U.S. military buildup, for the sake of peace, liberation and 
progress in the world.

AL-—QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES GIRLS‘ ACADEMY GRADUATES 


LD311640 Tripoli JANA in English 1408 GMT 31 Aug 81 


[Text] 31 Aug (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--[Dateline as received] The revolution's leader 
and the supreme commander of the armed forces today attended at 5:30 pm [as received] 

the graduation ceremony at the girls' military academy in Tripoli. The graduating girls 
will thus join the armed people, one of the people's choices in the jamahiri system. The 
ceremony was also attended by the general commander of the armed forces, Brigadier Abu 
Bakr Yunis Jabir, the academy's commandant and armed forces officers. 


During the ceremony Colonel al-Qadhdhafi delivered a speech in which he said: "We have 
taken the correct step by totally mobilising all fighting forces in the Jamahiriyah, which 
is the precursor of the patriotic mobilisation of all the Arab nation's combat forces." 


He said: "As of this year, all air, naval, air defence academies and secondary military 
schools will be open to both boys and girls." He called on youth of both sexes who have 
graduated from high school to enroll in these academies andschools. Colonel Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi urged junior high school graduates to enroll in military high schools of all 
service branches. He said: "This will soon become a reality because proof of this is in 
front of us today.--- In the past it was impossible to have a girls military academy. 
Detractors said it is impossible for any woman to come forth and join such an academy. 
But this proved wrong, as revolutionary transformations have surpassed such mentalities 
and created a new individual." 


He continued: "We are proud that the first military academy for girls in the world is 
today graduating officers here in the Jamahiriyah. This is something novel ...whereas 

in the American Army, 150 thousand women are enrolled. This giant force is not a combat 
force and is also not respected in the American Army.... This group is used for 
recreational purposes and assigned to menial jobs as a sign of condescension towards women. 
We, the Arabs, Muslims, Orientals, we the new force and we the builders of the new 
civilisation cannot accept women being bought and sold."
