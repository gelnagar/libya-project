LIBYA

DEMONSTRATORS HAND PROTEST LETTER TO U.S, EMBASSY 


LD261446 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 26 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] In Tripoli, a demonstration that began at Green Square today contimed when 
marchers passed through Martyr Muhammad al-Mujallis Square and gathered in front of 
the U.S, Embassy chanting "Down with the traitors!" and denouncing the stand of the 
U.S. imperialist administration, which has driven its traitorous defeated agent, the 
defeated Egyptian president, to sign the agreements of capitulation and disgrace with 
racist Zionism, 


The particpants in the demonstration handed in an open letter to the U.S, people, It 
reads; The masses of the Libyan Arab people and their revolutionary vanguards, who 
came out today in demonstrations of protest and denunciation against the agreements 

of humiliation and disgrace which the defeated Egyptian president will be signing 

with the Zionist enemy, denounce the role of the U,S, administration, which instigated 
and drove the defeated agent to sign these treaties, and which has offered all economic, 
political and military support to the Zionist enemy to enable it to establish itself 

in occupied Palestine and to carry out acts of aggression from there on the Arab 
countries and to intimidate and murder the Areb people in occupied Palestine, 


The letter affirms that the masses, while denouncing the attitude of the United States 
toward this deal, warns the United States of the consequences of this for its relations 
with and interests in the Arab area as a whole, The letter affirms that the Arab 
masses throughout the Arab arena will contirmme their struggle with all their force and 
capabilities against the treacherous Zionist aggression, the like of which mankind has 
never seen before, will bring down all the capitulationist agreements and will tread 

on them and on he who has signed them, 


COMMENTARY SAYS EGYPTIAN REGIME HAS MERGED WITH ZIONISTS 
LD261552 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 26 Mar 79 LD 
{Unattributed commentary] 


[Excerpts] The defeated Egyptian regime and the traitorous Egyptian president have 
shed their last mask to prove that the treasons of the defeated Egyptian president and 
his regime are treason against the Arab nation and against the Egyptian Arab people, 
as well as against the martyrs who fell victims to the Zionist-American bombs, 


The signing of the treaty means that the defeated Egyptian regime has fully merged with 
the Zionists and has become more Zionist than the Zionists themselves, This requires 
the Arab nation to adopt a combat stand to defend itself and to foil the plot before 
its danger spreads, 


The entire Arab nation is the target of this traitorous plot, Hence any regime in the 
Arab homeland that stands by the defeated Egyptian regime, overtly or covertly, is a 
regime in collusion with Zionism, indeed a Zionist regime just like the Egyptian regime, 
Any military, political or financial aid to the Egyptian regime is tantamount to aid 

to the Zionist aggression against the Arab nation,

After the defeated Egyptian president's signing of the treaty, any weakening stand taken 
will be tantamount to stands of collusion with Zionism and imperialism, for the squander- 
ing of the Arab land in Palestine is like the squandering of the entire Arab land. 


Israel no longer occupies Palestine alone; it also occupies Egypt. Hence any Arab regime 
that supports the defeated Egyptian regime will in fact be supporting the Zionist occu- 
pation of our Arab territory in Egypt and bolstering this occupation against the. Libyan 
people, who today are standing up to Zionism on their eastern borders not to defend them- 
selves alone, but also to defend the entire Arab nation, and are standing now in the 
frontline trench on the direct line of contact with Zionism, 


Today the Arab nation has seen the truth. It now knows for sure that Libya had no personal 
feud with the defeated Egyptian regime. [passage indistinct ] 


Today we appeal to our nation, from the gulf to the ocean; Arabism is threatened. Islam 
is threatened. The traitors have unmasked their ugly faces. Libya, with the masses of 
Arabism and Islam behind it, is today standing on the line of confrontation and the line 
of direct contact with Zionism.
