Summary (AI): A 1981 Dallas Times Herald article (reprinted in the Philadelphia Inquirer) reports that former CIA agent Edwin P. Wilson, indicted over gun and explosives shipments to Libya in 1976-77, is living in Tripoli in a luxury villa near the Mediterranean and conducts business there. It also covers US-Libya tensions and the Reagan request that Americans leave Libya.
• Wilson was said to have received $1 million from Khadafy for the munitions sale.
• Reports alleged Wilson recruited American pilots and mechanics to serve the Libyan military and enlisted former Green Berets to train terrorists in Libya.
• Reagan asked all US citizens to leave Libya in December 1981; the FBI said the Libyan embassy in Washington was a source of murder plots.
• Two US jets downed two Soviet-built Libyan fighters off the North African coast in summer 1981.
• 1976-1977: Wilson and Frank Terpil allegedly arranged shipment of about 40,000 pounds of illegal explosives and timing devices to Libya.
