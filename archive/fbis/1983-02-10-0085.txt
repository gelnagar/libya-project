AL-QADHDHAFI PLEDGES ‘FULL ASSISTANCE’ TO DPRK 


SKO70539 Pyongyang KCNA in English 0524 GMT 7 Feb 83 


[Text] Pyongyang February 7 (KCNA) -- The great leader Comrade Kim I1l-song, President 
of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea, received from Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
leader of the Great September 1 Revolution of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah, a solidarity message sternly denouncing the "Team Spirit 83" joint mili- 
tary exercises being staged in South Korea by the U.S. imperialists and the South 
Korean puppet clique. 


V. 10 Feb 83 


The message dated February 3 reads: 


We are following with deep concern the "Team Spirit 83" military exercises now being 
staged against your friendly country. 


The "Team Spirit 83" is an insult to the South Korean people, an open provocation 
against your friendly people, and part of the policy of strength, threat, and blackmail 
which characterizes the U.S. hostile policy toward the Third World people standing 
firmly against its dominationist policy. 


Your excellency president, my intimate friend, bitterly denouncing these aggressive 
military exercises, we assure you that we are standing on the side of the friendly 
Korean people who are under your wise leadership and will render full assistance to 
your people resolutely opposing imperialism and its aggressive acts. With high 
considerations. 


INTERVIEW WITH AL-—QADHDHAFI ON FRENCH TELEVISION 


LD081900 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1540 GMT 8 Feb 83 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 8 Feb (JANA) -- French television channel one held an interview 
with the leader of the revolution. He explained many current international, Arab, and 
African issues, the problems suffered by the world — such as the economic crisis, 
international terrorism, energy, wars fought in some parts of the world, liberation 
issues, and liberation movements. 


On developments regarding the Palestine issue and the barbaric Zionist invasion of 
Lebanon, the leader stressed that Zionism is a racist movement hostile to the whole 
world, and it relies on a dangerous theory of remaining as God's chosen people; that 

is to say, Zionism only remaining, the destruction of the rest of the world's European, 
African, Asian, and American peoples and sucking their blood. 


He said that this underlines a call for an alliance between Arabs and Jews, because 
Jews have become the victims of the Zionist state, just as the Arabs have become 
victims of Zionism. Jews have been subjugated by Zionism to fight the Arabs. The 
leader stressed that there is no hostility between Arabs and Jews, but there is enmity 
between the Arabs, Jews, and the world on the one hand, and Zionism on the other. He 
said that America's turn would come and that some day America would wake up to the 
danger of Zionism and find out that it has sacrificed its future, its interests, and 
friends in the world as a result of Zionist deceit. 


In his reply, the leader went on to say: Therefore, the Zionist state must be wiped 
out by the Jews, Arabs, and the American people. The American people are the third 
victim, after the Arabs and the Jews: The rest of the world's people are the other 
victim. 


He stressed that Zionism would cause a third world war for the sake of its interests, 
its tyranny, and aggression and for the sake of its war policy. It will accept no 
substitute in order to destroy the world. He said that West Europe is also in danger 
as a result of its support for Zionism and for not boycotting it, and as a result of 
pouring money, economic and military aid, and political support to the Zionist state. 
The leader went on to say that there is a clear and wide difference between Zionism 
and Judaism. We Arabs have no enmity with Jews, but we have enmity with Zionism. Jews 
must become the Arabs’ allies against Zionism. He said that if Jews want to preserve 
the Zionist state, it would involve them in future wars. Jews have to be prepared, 
generation after generation, to die for Zionism in the Middle East.

Replying to a question on international terrorism and terrorist organizations, the 
leader of the revolution stressed that imperialism and Zionism are trying, through 

a psychological terroristic campaign they are waging against the Arab nation and the 
peoples of the Third World, to conceal the real terrorism they constantly practice 
in order to distract world attention and to allege that the Arab nation and the 
peoples struggling for freedom are the ones engaged in international terrorism. 


He said we never allow such accusations to be levelled without taking a stance, we 
cannot bear for long the terrorist psychological campaign being waged against us 
aimed at killing any humanitarian initiative. We can pay back twofold: We can 
bring double retaliation. He explained that the psychological campaigns being 
exercised by imperialism and Zionism, and the false accusations being levelled 
against the Arab nation and the peoples of the developing countries, are in them- 
selves a form of true terrorism against these peoples. 


He added: We openly accuse the West of engaging in historic terrorism against us, 
which may pave the way for a fresh Crusade campaign. From now on we shall begin 

to reply to this campaign because we are a suppressed people and we are under the 
terrorism of military threats, psychological war, and economic siege. The armies 

of Zionism and imperialism are destroying our homes and plundering our resources — 
and they portray us as guilty. These are facts which must be understood by French 
public opinion and world public opinion. We have become the victims of international 
terrorism: Zionist terrorism against the Arabs; American terrorism against the 
Arabs and primarily the small Libyan people; the terrorism of imperialist fleets 
that threaten peaceful peoples; the terrorism of starvation, economic boycott and 
siege, and of a vast propaganda psychological war which extends from West Europe 

to America against the developing peoples and the peoples who are working to achieve 
their freedom and independence and to live in peace. The truth is that terrorism 

is the destruction of Lebanese towns and the burning down of Beirut with American 
naval force, air force, aircraft, and rockets; also the threat by the U.S. 6th Fleet 
to the Gulf of Sidra and to Libyan territorial waters, Libyan airspace, and the 
small Libyan people. This is true terrorism; any other interpretation of terrorism 
is but lies and humbug and is misleading world public opinion. 


In his reply to a question about the Jamahiriyah being charged with terrorism, the 
brother leader of the revolution explained that the Jamahiriyah has repeatedly 
condemned all terrorist acts carried out by international terrorist organizations. 

He pointed out that terrorism by these organizations is limited when compared with 
the big international terrorism carried out by imperialism and by Zionism. Zionist- 
imperialist terrorism has caused the death of thousands of innocent people in Lebanon 
and occupied Palestine. 


In general, terrorist acts committed by the Red Brigades organization and those like 
it in Germany and Italy are small when compared with the extensive Zionist terrorism 
and American terrorism. 


The brother leader explained that the Red Brigades organization and the West German 
Bader-Meinhoff organization need psychoanalysis to discover the social, political, 
and psychological reasons for the birth of these two organizations. He pointed out 
that the emergence of these two organizations indicates the existence of a fault in 
Italian and German society and that the treatment of this terrorist phenomenon 
requires treatment of the conditions which led to their emergence. He stressed that 
it would be best for the members of these organizations to come out into the light, 
to abandon violence, and to practice opposition in a Jamahiri manner.

Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi talked about events in the island of Corsica and 
activities of the opposition movement in the island. He said: France is capable of 
defining the nature of armed orgarizations in the island, whether there is an inde- 
pendence, separatist, or terrorist movement. If France thinks Corsica is French, then 
what is taking place in Corsica is a separatist action and must be treated like any 
other separatist action from the mother soil. [sentence as received] However, if 
France knows Corsica is a nation in its own right and a people in its own right and has 
been annexed to Frande by force, then these movements are independence movements. 

In any case the French are more able to define the nature of these movements. 


The leader pointed out that he is against any act of sabotage, or explosions, or assassi- 
nation of innocent people: I condemn all these acts and our stand regarding them has 
always been clear. 


Col Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi declared that the Libyan police had no objection to cooperat- 
ing with Interpol in combatting terrorism. He said he believes the Libyan police 
complained that the authorities in France, Italy, andother European countries 
protected anti-Libyan activists, terrorists, and subversive secret action calling 

for the use of arms and violence and for planning the assassination of Libyan people. 
This planning, he said, is taking place in the capitals of West Europe and in 

America and is regrettably fully protected by the authorities. I hope these states 
and these authorities will not persist in this anti-Libyan action because if they do, 
the Libyan people, through the People's Congresses, will one day have to resort to the 
same treatment. Consequently, the Libyan people will take any action which is against 
these regimes as long as these regimes protect and assist any actions against the 
Libyan people. 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi talked about workers strikes inthe world and the reasons 
which have led to the phenomenon of demonstrations and barricades by workers which have 
been witnessed by the world. 


In his statements to French television, the leader of the revolution talked about the 
Islamic revolution in Iran and the circumstances which caused the revolution, He 
explained at the beginning of his talk that revolution in the general context is 
revolution against injustice, whether it is in an Islamic, Christian, or atheist 
world. The Iranian Revolution is a popular revolution against the tyranny of the 
shah and U.S. control of Iran. Iran lost its freedom and became a U.S. base and a 
region of U.S. influence during the shah's domination. 


The leader added: I am against fig ting the Iranian Revolution, because it is a 
popular liberation revolution which will achieve the Iranian people's liberty and the 
liberation of Iran from foreign domination. Consequently, I support the Iranian 
Revolution and the Iranian people. This does not mean I am against the Iraqi 

regime or against Saddam Husayn as a person, but I am against fighting the Iranian 
Revolution. 


The leader explained that the Iraqi-Iranian war is beyond mediation, becuase it has 
now passed the red light and it is now futile to make any attempt to stop it. He 
poiited out that the Jamahiriyah was among those who at the outset attempted to 
mediate between Iraq and Iran, who urged them to retreat and to enter into peaceful 
negotiations for the resolution of problems. He said this war has moved away from 
the original point and is now nearingits:inevitable military conclusion, which is 
much nearer than a solution through mediation or a return to the starting point.

Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has affirmed that Libya honors OPEC resolutions and that it does 
its best to preserve this organization and its unity. 


In his statement to channel one of French television, he said that there is a crisis in 
the whole world; it is not so much a crisis of oil-producing countries, or of prices, or 
production as it is a crisis of capitalism, a crisis that occurs periodically in the 
history of the world due to the exploitation of productive forces inside the capitalist 
countries, to the expansionist imperialist policy in connection with space programs, 
nuclear weapons programs, military bases, and naval fleets, and also due to the 

plundering of the developing countries" resources and the rise in the prices of industrial 
products. 


He pointed out that fuel prices at present are very reasonable and that any reduction in 
prices will harm the producing countries, the consumer countries, and the companies that 
import oil for Western industries. He explained that any reduction in current prices 
conflicts with the interests of Western countries and will have a negative impact on 

their economy. The oil surplus, he said, must be tackled so that matters can settle, 
pointing out that the Jamahiriyah put forward proposals in this regard to the oil-producing 
countries. 


In his interview to French television Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi also dealt with \ibya's 
relations with Egypt. He stressed: We reject any reconciliation with any Egyptian regime 
that recognizes the Zionist state because this is a danger to peace in the arez and a 
danger to the Arab nation, as Zionism in the Middle East is a hostile racist state that 

is anti-Semetic, and anti-Arab. Its basic aim is to annihilate the Arabs, destroy the 
Arab civilization and Arab industries, and cast a veto on the future of the Arabs. He 
added that to recognize this Zionist military camp is equivalent to recognizing the end 

of the Arab nation and the rise of a Zionist empire in this area. 


Col Mu‘ammar al-Qadidhafi commented on what has been circulating concerning the 


establishment of a Palestinian state in the West Bank and Gaza. He said: I do not have 
any faith whatsoever that there is any possibility, no matter how faint, of the estab- 
lishment of a state in the West Bank and Gaza that the Israelis may allow in any way 
the rise of a state in the West Bank and Gaza. He affirmed that the return of the Gaza 
Strip and the West Bank to the Palestinians requires battles capable of reversing all 
Palestinian battles, like the 1967 war when the West Bank and Gaza Strip were occupied. 


The brother leader affirmed that the Israelis have introduced war into the area, and 

that a solution can only be found through war and force. That the Americans support 

this logic most strongly is proved by the fact that they have been arming the Israeli 
aggressors. They are also taking part in all the talks that have been taking place as 

a result of the use of force, such as the Camp David talks and the talks now taking place 
in Lebanon, in order to bless the use of violence and force. The brother leader 
described this method as a serious precedent from a great power like America, which has 
decided that whoever uses force has the right to reap its fruits. 


Col Mu'‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi stressed that we have no intention to intervene in Chad. In 
his statement to French television he said: Our intervention in the past was lawful and 
legal and in accordance with the request of the legitimate authority in Ndjamena. He 
expalined that when this authority asked us to leave, we left immediately. Now we 

have no justification for our army entering Chad.

In his interview Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi also talked about the Jamahiriyah's relations 
with France and the United States. He said that France is not a new friend, but that 

we consider France as one of our traditional friends. Ever since the emergence of the 
revolution in Libya, friendship and strong cooperation has been established between Libya 
and France. We also contributed to the return of France to the Arab homeland, When the 
revolution emerged in Libya in 1969 France was isolated in the Arab homeland. it was 

we who built a bridge between France and the Arab homeland. 


The brother leader explained that the states which failed to attend the African summit 
in Tripoli are proteges of France. Consequently, the direct conclusion is that France 
prevented these states from attending, especially those states not considered as 
independent and those whose independence is considered a sham because they are not 
economically independent and they do not possess a strong political will, hence they 
rely on France. However, since France affirmed that it did not try to prevent these 
states from attending the Tripoli conference, and that on the contrary, it encouraged 
them to go, it became clear later that it was the United States who influenced these 
states. This was evident in the letters presented to us through certain African heads 
of state in which the United States told them officially not to attend the Tripoli 
conference. We then accepted France's explanation and began to talk in friendly tones 
once again. 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that to recognize the hostile Zionist camp would 
be to admit the end of the Arab nation and the emergence of a Zionist empire in this 
area. 


In his French television interview he said: This is what prompted us to call for an 
alliance between the Arabs and the Jews to destroy the Zionist state, because the Jews 
have become victims of the Zionist state. The alliance will be imposed on the Arabs and 
the Jews in order to abolish the Zionist state and in order to save their lives and 
rescue their generations. 


He said it should be made clear that there is a vast difference between Zionism and 
Judaism. We, the Arabs, have no animosity toward Judaism but we have animosity toward 
Zionism. The Jews c~e with the Arabs and they should be allies against Zionism. The 
Jews will not die except through the Zionist state, which will involve them in future wars. 
The Jews should be ready to die in the struggle against Zionism, one generation after 
another, in the Middle East or elsewhere. 


Col Mu'Ammar al-Qadhdhafi said the mutual gains which we share with France are important 
to us because obstacles might arise in the way of these gains, in which case the two 
sides would be deprived of them. The United States raised such obstacles and deprived 
the American people of exchanging gains with the Libyan people; it deprived the Libyan 
people of exchanging mutual benefits with the American people. 


He added that the French people benefit from their relations with Libya and the Arab- 
nation. France is interested in Africa for very well-known reasons. Libya is an African 
state and an active state in Africa. Many Africans rely on it a great deal. Therefore, 

it is important that Libya and France should have an understanding about Africa, and that 
our policies should be in harmony with one another and in harmony with African aspirations. 
It is important that there should be understanding and not collision. 


The brother leaders said: We support France's wish to be independent from American 
hegemony, unlike some other European powers such as Italy, Germany, and others who are 
under American influence to a large extent.
