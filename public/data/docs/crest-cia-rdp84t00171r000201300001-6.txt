Summary (AI): NPIC imagery note from August 1983 reporting first identification of three SF.260 light attack aircraft at Matan as Sarra airfield in far southeastern Libya, likely a staging base for operations against Chad. The site is far from Tripoli.
• Three SF.260 aircraft identified; one parked at south end of eastern parallel runway, two on graded-earth apron near centre of western parallel runway.
• Analysts assessed the airfield as a staging base inside Libya if Habre's forces threatened Aozou airfield in Chad.
• SF.260 operational radius stated as 300 NM.
• Airfield under construction since early 1980.
• 1983-08: Three SF.260 aircraft identified at the airfield, the first fixed-wing aircraft observed there, possibly using a newly marked graded-earth landing strip.

NPIC/IEG (8/83) 

FIRST IDENTIFICATION OF FIXED WING AIRCRAFT 
MATAN AS SARRA AIRFIELD, LIBYA (SWN) 

SIGNIFICANCE: THREE SF.260 AIRCRAFT WERE IDENTIFIED AT MATAN 
AS SARRA AIRFIELD IN SOUTHEASTERN LIBYA. THIS IS THE FIRST 
TIME FIXED WING AIRCRAFT HAVE BEEN OBSERVED AT THIS AIRFIELD 
AND THEY MAY HAVE UTILIZED A NEWLY DEMARCATED, GRADED-EARTH 
LANDING STRIP. (SWN) 

REMARKS: ON IMAGERY OF ONE SF.260 WAS PARKED AT THE 
SOUTH END OF THE EASTERN PARALLEL RUNWAY AND TWO SF.260 WERE 
PARKED ON A GRADED-EARTH PARKING APRON NEAR THE CENTER OF THE 
WESTERN PARALLEL RUNWAY. IN THE PAST, THE ONLY AIR ACTIVITY 

OBSERVED IN THE AREA HAD BEEN HELICOPTERS AT A LANDING PAD AT. 

MAATEN ES SARRA MILITARY CAMP » lL NM SOUTHEAST 
OF THE AIRFIELD. THE NEWLY DEMARCATED RUNWAY, AT LEAST 1,000 
METERS IN LENGTH, WITH TOUCHDOWN POINTS AND CENTERLINE WAS 
NOT PRESENT ON MATAN AS SARRA AIRFIELD HAS BEEN 

. UNDER CONSTRUCTION SINCE EARLY 1980. (SWN) 

ANALYST'S COMMENTS -- DURING 1981 AND 1982, ALTHOUGH NO 
AIRCRAFT WERE HERE, A SMALL SECTION OF THE GRADED-EARTH EAST 
RUNWAY WAS CONSIDERED OPERATIONAL FOR SMALL, LIGHT AIRCRAFT 
AND HELICOPTERS. IN EARLY 1983, THE OPERATIONAL PORTION OF 
THE RUNWAY WAS GRADED OVER AND THE MAJORITY OF THE 
CONSTRUCTION ACTIVITY SHIFTED TO THE WEST PARALLEL RUNWAY. A 
PROBABLE HARDENED SURFACE HAD BEEN LAID ON THE SOUTH 2,000 
METERS OF THE WESTERN PARALLEL RUNWAY. THE INTRODUCTION OF 
AN OPERATIONAL LANDING STRIP FOR LIGHT STRIKE AIRCRAFT AT 
MATAN AS SARRA PROVIDES THE LIBYAN'S WITH A STAGING BASE 
INSIDE LIBYA IN THE EVENT HABRE'S FORCES THREATEN AOZOU 
AIRFIELD - THE SF.260 HAS AN OPERATIONAL 

RADIUS OF 300 NM. FROM MATAN AS SARRA, THE SF.260 CAN STRIKE 

THE FAYA LARGEAU, THE BARDAI, AND THE AOZOU AREAS OF CHAD. 
(SWN) , . 

MATAN AS SARRA AIRFIELD IS IN SOUTHEASTERN LIBYA, 172 NM 
SOUTH OF KUFRA AND 275 NORTHEAST OF FAYA LARGEAU, CHAD. 
(SWN)
