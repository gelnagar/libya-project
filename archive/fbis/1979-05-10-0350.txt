LIBYA

JALWWD RECEIVES POLISARIO SECRETARY GENERAL ABDUL AZIZ 
LD092028 Tripoli JANA in English 1822 GMT 9 May 79 LD 
[Text] Tripoli, May 9, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Staff-Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud 
received today Mohamed Abdul Aziz, secretary general of Seghia al Hamra and Rio de Oro 
Popular Liberation Front [POLISARIO]. 
Abdul Aziz arrived in Tripoli last Tuesday on a several-day visit to the Jamahiriyah. 
AT-TURAYKI RECEIVES IMAM OF CHAD, CONFERENCE SECRETARY 
LD100942 Tripoli JANA in English 0840 GMT 10 May 79 LD 
[Excerpt] Fez, 10 May, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Dr. 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, 
foreign secretary, received in Fez yesterday morning the imam of the Chadian Moslems who 
is taking part in the Islamic conference, They discussed the Moslem affairs in Chad 
in confronting the tribal adventureous group that formed an illegal government in Chad. 
They also discussed the means of supporting I lam and the Moslems in Chad. 


The foreign secretary also received yesterday Mr. Ahmad Karim, the general secretary of 
the Islamic conference.
