LIBYA

REVOLUTIONARY COMMITTEES ISSUE STATEMENT 
LD121132 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 12 Sep 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 12 Sep (JANA)--The brother leader of the great 1 September revolution 
the day before yesterday attended the final session of the conference of the delegates 
of the revolutionary committees and the free unionist officers movement in the armed 
forces. At the end of its work the conference issued the following statement: 


In the name of God, the merciful, the compassionate. Statement of the meeting of the 
delegates of the revolutionary committees in the armed forces: 


After the hours of self-sacrifice and the elimination of fear, humiliation, submission 

and indignation; after the decision was made to put to death the liberation of our Arab 
homeland and the unification of the immortal Arab nation; after deciding to confront our 
enemies completely and prepare for the launching of a counter pan~Arab strategic offensive 
against the enemies of the Arab nation; and after taking forever the initiative from the 
enemy, the conference decided the following: 


l. To reorganize the revolutionary committees and increase their fighting and struggling 
and ideological and organizational preparedness. 


2. ™o devote sufficient forces to the building of the movement of the revolutionary 
committees in the armed forces. 


3. The revolutionary committees in the armed forces, while surging forward in a unitary 
popular march to destroy the regional borders and build the unitary state between Syria 
and Libya, declare that the gravity of pan-Arab responsibility has made them decide to 
destroy totally and immediately all the forces which might attempt to prevent the Libyan 
people from shouldering their historic role. 


4. The revolutionary committees in the armed forces declare their readiness to fight 
everywhere in defense of freedom and their readiness to resist the American and Israeli 
presence in the Arab homeland. 

5. The revolutionary committees in the armed forces declare their readiness to fight 
side-by-side with the Palestinian revolution for the liberation of Palestine and declare 
that upper Galilee and southern Lebanon will not be a forward trench for the Palestinians 
and the Lebanese alone. 


6. The revolutionary committees in the armed forces will act to realize the potentials 
of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces in the service of the Palestinian revolution. 


7. To address an appeal to the revolutionary forces in the Arab armies to hasten the 
revolution so that they can join the caravan of unity and liberation which begain in Libya. 


8. To immediately and ruthlessly crush any fascist attempt and any force which opposes 
the historic and strategic march which began in Libya. 


9. The revolutionary committees in the armed forces have taken the road of honor--the 
road of death, as taken by the leader in his historic speech-~and have rejected forever 
the road of indignation and humiliation. 


Fatah forever, committees everywhere. 


15 Sep 80 NORTH AFRICA 


BULGARIAN DELEGATION ARRIVES FOR COOPERATION TALKS 
LD141757 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1545 GMT 14 Sep 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 14 Sep (JANA)--Kiril Zarev, deputy chairman of the Bulgarian Council of 
Ministers and chairman of the planning committee, arrived in Tripoli today for a visit. 
Kiril Zarev, who is accompanied by a delegation, told JANA that the visit aims at achiev- 
ing cooperation between the two peoples. He praised the friendly relations between the 
Libyan and Bulgarian peoples.
