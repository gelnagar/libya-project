Summary (AI): An August 1983 CIA imagery briefing on Libyan logistics in the Chad conflict. It reports heavy-lift and tank-related activity at the Tajura barracks near Tripoli, along with activity at Sebha, Kufra and Gharyan.
• At Tajura barracks, 27 empty heavy-lift transporters were in several columns and at least one tank battalion and one mechanised infantry battalion were parked outside vehicle storage buildings.
• At Sebha airfield, aircraft observed included Blinder, Foxbat, SF.260, Twin Otter, Hip, Candid, Falcon 50 and C-130 types, with an order of battle of 3 Blinder, 18 Foxbat and 9 SF.260.
• At Kufra, 12 cargo trucks with double-axle trailers were in convoy and a heavy-lift transporter was forming columns.
• The document is a NPIC briefing graphic, marked for briefing purposes only, valid for the reporting period of its issue date.
• 1983-08: High level of heavy-lift transport activity; one tank company (12 tanks) probably departed on transporters.
• 1983-08: Probable resupply of Chadian government forces by a Chadian C-47 and DC-4.
