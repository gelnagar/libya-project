SECRET 

Senior Interagency Group No. 14 

TO : OVP - Mrs. Nancy Bearg Dyke

CIA - 

DOD - COL John Stanford

SUBJECT: Notification of SIG Meeting 

The SIG on Libya will convene at 12:00 noon on Wednesday, 
July 14, in Room 7219 of the State Department. Under Secretary 
Eagleburger will chair the meeting. 

The subject will be naval planning. A discussion paper 
will be circulated shortly. 

Attendance will be principal plus one. Addressees are 
requested to telephone the names of their representatives to 
Sheila Lopez by COB Monday, July 12. 

L. Paul Bremer, III 
Executive Secretary
