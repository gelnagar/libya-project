JANA Reports Shultz Fears for ‘Spying’ Aircraft 
LD221737 Tripoli JANA in English 1656 GMT 22 Mar 84 


[Text) Washington, Jumada Thani 18, March 21, [date as 
received] JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY — The Ameri- 
can secretary of state, Mr George Shultz, expressed the Ameri- 
can Administration fears that Libya would attack the American 
spying aircrafts, situated in the Egyptian territory, sent by 
President Reagan to protect the regimes of Egypt and Sudan 
which are subordinated to America. The American secretary of 
state expressed his fears in a press conference on Tuesday in 
Washington. He openly pointed out that the American Admin- 
istration is worried about its allies in the Middle East and the 
Horn of Africa from Libya especially after the bomb raid on 
Omduramn radio station last Friday. [sentence as received]. Mr 
George Shultz said that those fears were the reason behind 
sending the aircrafts to Egypt to protect ‘ts allies. 


U.S. Airlifts of Egyptian Material to Sudan Reported 
LD221756 Tripoli JANA in English 1734 GMT 22 Mar 84 


[Text] New York, Jumada Thani 18, March 22, JAMAHIR- 
IYAH NEWS AGENCY — The official spokesman of the 
American State Department, Alan Romberg, in a press con- 
ference yesterday acknowledged that it was the American 
Administration which had airlifted the Egyptian military 
equipment from Egypt to Sudan. The spokesman alleged that 
there was no decision taken yet with regards to sending more 
American military equipment to Sudan but he added that the 
American Administration was considering ways which would 
help Sudan in respect of its needs at the long term.

Sudanese Opposition to Numayri Cited on Raid 
LD221032 Tripoli JANA in English 0830 GMT 22 Mar 84 


[Text] Athens, Jumada Thani 18, Mar 22, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — A number of the Sudanese opposition 
front held a press conference yesterday evening in the Greek 
capital Athens in which they condemned the puppet Numayri’s 
claims that the plane raided television and radio station in 
Omdurman on Friday was a Libyan plane. They asserted that 
the Sudanese masses and the opposition know very well and have 
the proof that the attacking plane was a Sudanese and the pilot 
was a revolutionary Sudanese pilot who refused submission to 
puppets who follow the bandwagon of American imperialism and 
submit to the agent Egyptian regime. 


They added that the Sudanese opposition have got a full list of 
Sudanese Air Force officers detained after the bombing of th 

two stations which was a part of a popular uprising against 
injustice and oppression. The spreading of the uprising to 
southern Sudan is a best indication of this. It is a reoccuring event 
there watched by the whole world daily. Numayri’s claims are 
nothing but lies, the objective of which is to gain increased 
American support and to beg personal protection for himself in 
view of his mounting fear out of successive revolutionary strikes. 


Members of the opposition front as a revolutionary force asked 
world public opinion to be well aware of these Egyptian- 
American tricks aiming at recolonizing Sudan and punish its 
people and revolutionaries. They warned the agent Egyptian 
regime to refrain from interfering in Sudanese afairs and to 
direct any means it may have against the Z’onist enemy and not 
a brotherly people. They have also warned the puppet Numayri, 
the Egyptian regime, and the head of international terrorism 
America not to interfere in the Sudanese people’s affairs because 
what has happened and what may happen concerns the Sudanese 
people and no one else. They added their rejection to the sending

Further Opposition Cited

[Text] Al-Bayda, 22 Mar (JANA) — The masses of the Sudanese 
Arab people in the municipality of Al-Jabal al-‘Akhdar affirmed 
that the Sudanese masses with their popular revolution will 
struggle until the (?collapse) of the regime of Numayri in Sudan. 
The masses of the Sudanese people, in a statement issued after

commitment to the resolutions of the first national gathering, and 
that they are adopting a stand of steadfastness and struggle

imperialist, Zionist and reactionary conspiracies that are being 
plotted against the Arab nation.
