CAIRO: PALESTINIANS RESIST OFFICIAL REPRESENTATIVES 
NC061908 Cairo Domestic Service in Arabic 1830 GMT 6 Dec 79 NC 


{Text] A Palestinian source in Beirut said today that at the instigation of IZbyan Col 
Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, the Palestinian popular revolutionary committees have organized 
a rebellion against the official representatives of the Palestinian movements in Libya. 
The source added that these committees, which include Palestinians residing in Libya, 
have been surrounding the permanent headquarters of the PLO and the various other 
Palestinian organizations in Tripoli for the past few days. The source said he does not 
know whether other developments have taken place. 


Palestinian circles in Beirut have expressed strong reservations about this issue, hoping 
that the current contacts will be fruitful. 


AL=QADHDHAFI REPORTEDLY REJECTS "ARAFAT'S PROTEST 
NC062144 Cairo MENA in Arabic 2125 GMT 6 Dec 79 NC 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 6 December--Reports coming from the Libyan capital indicate that the 
Libyan militias, which receive their orders directly from Al-Qadhdhafi, have been 
besieging the Palestinian organizations! offices in Tripoli since yesterday. 


The reports indicate that Al-Qadhdhafi has rejected a protest from PLO Executive Committee 
Chairman Yasir ‘Arafat over this Libyan behavior. 


Yasir ‘Arafat is still in contact with the Libyan ambassador in Damascus in an attempt 
to lift the siege by Al-Qadhdhafi's militias on the Palestinian organizations! offices, 
all of which are located in one building in Tripoli. 


The reports indicate that the situation between Libya and the PLO has reached a high 
degree of tension as a result of Al-Qadhdhafi's behavior. 


AFP: MOROCCAN PRESS REPORTS LEBANON'S IMAM AS-SADR KILLED 
NC061428 Paris AFP in English 1357 GMT 6 Dec 79 NC 


{Text] Rabat, Dec 6 (AFP)--The Imam Musa as-Sadr, the head of the Libyan [as received] 
Shitite community who has been missing since last year, was killed along with two 
companions during a September 1978 visit to Libya, the Moroccan daily AL-MUHARRIR reported 
today. 


The imam, Shaykh Muhammad Sha'ta Ya'qub and 'Abbas Badr were executed by Lieutenant Faraj 
Bughaliyah and four under-officers who misinterpreted an order, AL-MUHARRIR said, citing 
a reliable Arab source. 


The newspaper, organ of the Socialist Union of Popular Forces, said Imam as-Sadr and his 
companions were executed on a firing range near Janzur, 30 kms (20 miles) from [fripoli.

The Libyan Government then had the travel documents of the three men falsified to make
