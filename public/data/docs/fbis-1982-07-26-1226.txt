AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS LETTER TO LEBANESE LEADERS 


LD241130 Tripoli JANA in English 0940 GMT 24 Jul 82 


[Text Tripoli, Shawal 4, July 24, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The revolution's 
leader Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi sent the following letter to: 


Ilyas Sarkis - Walid Junblatt 
Shafiq al-Wazzan - Muhsin Ibrahim 
Sa'ib Salam - Ina'm Ra'd 
Rashid Karami - Ibrahim Qulaylat 
Sulayman Franjiyah - Nabih Barri 


On the occasion of "Id al-Fitr, I congratulate you and reiterate the verse of the Arab 
poet: 


Today, compatriots, is a day of festivities. Yet true festivities mean liberation and 
forcing the Jews to succumb... 


I salute Lebanon today at a time when it is in the midst of an ordeal being experienced 
not for a Lebanese, but for an Arab cause... 


In spite of its tribulations, Lebanon's ordeal is being daily falsified so that it may 
appear not to concern the country and its people, but to be of concern to the Palestinians, 
and the Palestine Liberation Organisation in particular... Yet the tragedy has affected 
all of Lebanon and its people... The campaign of extermination is aimed at Lebanon's 
Muslim people and the country's patriotic groups, whether they be political organisations 
or ordinary citizens. 


The campaign is designed to first neutralise and finally destroy Lebanon. This is a 
clever and devious plan which is dangerous but has already been exposed... The plan has 
been revised because Lebanon is the refugee and the supporter of the Palestinian people... 
It is because of this that the enemy intentionally set upon the destruction of Lebanese 
cities inhabited by the Lebanese. It is for this that they have decided upon the 
annihilation of the people of Lebanon, especially the Muslim population... 


The enemy Jews and the world should have known that, because of their method of organisation 
and modes of struggle, the Palestinian Resistance cannot be confined to one place alone, 
and cannot congregate in large numbers... They are a ubiquitous underground movement... 
They are in every place inside occupied Palestine... They are not now in the grip 

of the enemy, and the enemy is fully aware of this... 


Therefore, the current siege is against the Lebanese people of Beirut and the present 
war is designed to exterminate the Lebanese people and their cities as revenge for the 
support they have given to the Palestinian Resistance... It is thus truly a war of 
vengeance against the Lebanese and not one of pursuit of the Palestinians... 


What I would like to make clear beyond the vengeful siege of Beirut is this: It is 
imperative that a change should take place in political events. Such events up till 
now have been intentionally falsified to that this action may not appear to the world 
as aggression and the occupation of an independent state, the annihilation of its 
people (who are not the Palestinian people) and the confrontation of Lebanese citizens 
(who do not constitute the Palestinian Resistance movement). 


Brethren, you should try and make clear to the worid that the Lebanese people are the 
ones being annihilated, because they have from the outset been the true targets of this 
campaign. Fighting is taking place between Lebanese nationalists and the barbaric 
Israeli army.

The world should understand from you that this war is being waged to seek vengeance 
from you and to annihilate your people, although the enemy is trying hard to project it 
as a war against the Palestininns... 


The enemy's attempts to falsify the situation are designed to deprive you from your right 
to resistance and your right as an independent state, upon which aggression has been 
committed, to take a position that is commensurate with your independent status. 


Unfortunately, the enemy has succeeded in this up till now: It has succeeded in neutral- 
ising the army of the country upon which aggression has been committed...How, in the 

name of God, can this army remain neutral...? Why does not Lebanon call upon the Arab 
League to implement the Arab collective defence charter...? Why doesn't it call upon the 
Security Council and the United Nations to deal with the situation as one of war between 
the Lebanese and the Israelis, and that Lebanon, an independent and sovereign state, 

is now under military occupation? 


What is being planned is not the ouster of the Palestinian Resistance from Beirut. The 
Resistance is not in Beirut. What they have there are only headquarters and offices. 

What is in fact being planned is the expulsion of the Lebanese people from their country 

so that they can be replaced by the Jews. The problem will not end with the evacuation 

of the Resistance's devastated offices and headquarters from Beirut. The presence of 

the Resistance depends on the presence of the people of Lebanon who support the Palestinian 
Resistance... What is being sought is the expulsion of the Lebanese people (especially 

the Muslim population who constitute three quarters of the Lebanese people). The 
operation is therefore designed to either exterminate or oust the Lebanese people from 
their country so that the Palestinian Resistance would no longer find support. 


The Israelis are planning to acquire a monopoly of support for themselves alone in Lebanon. 
Such support will come from the isolationist group, which has tied its fate to that of 

the [word indistinct] much as the Lebanese Muslims and other nationalist groups have tied 
theirs to the Palestinian Resistance... 


What then will the enemy do with [words indistinct] movement that resists it in Beirut, 
Zgharta and the Ash-Shuf region? Will the Israelis ignore them, fight them or forgive 
them...? Never... The fate of these Lebanese regions lies between extermination and 
evacuation of the Lebanese people from their country... 


A new political stance will (?change) the course of events in Lebanon and end the falsifi- 
cation of the country's occupation. Such a stance has become both necessary and indeed 
a duty... This will give you a very important opportunity to exercise Lebanese legiti- 
macy and patriotism... [words indistinct] capabilities to be used in the battle against 


the enemy... 


The door to negotiations over Palestinian existence and related matters should be shut 
tightly by the Lebanese and Palestinians before the entire world. One indisputable 
position should also be made clear: that the foreign invasion of an independent country 
and the attempted annihiliation of a peaceful people shall be resisted... In this 

they will have the right to ask for any help, establish any alliance and seek all legiti- 
mate methods in self-defence. Apart from this, right will anly be futile and the oppressed 
become defence and the Lebanese people will be rendered homeless... Lebanon will finally 
become Israeli... 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi

ARAB LEAGUE STATEMENT ON SOMALIA CRITICIZED 


LD242252 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 24 Jul 82 
{Statement issued 24 July by People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison] 


[Text] The Arab League secretary general issued a statement on 22 July, in which he 
denounced what he called Ethiopian military aggression against Somalia. In view of the 
fact that the statement contains misrepresentations distorting the nature of events taking 
place in Somalia and is a disservice to Arab relations with a progressive and friendly 
African state, that has long affirmed its unwavering stand alongside the Arab right, and 
as the secretary general is not entitled tc express political attitudes that do not 
emanate from Arab unanimity, the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front would like to 
clarify: 


What is taking place in Somalia these days is a comprehensive popular revolution led by 

the Democratic Front for the Salvation of Somalia against a reactionary and hireling regime. 
This regime's connections with U.S. imperialism and Zionism were established when it 
bestowed its blessings on the Camp David agreements, thus turning Somali territories into 

a U.S. military base that protects and serves U.S. interests deep in the Arab homeland and 
compliments the chain of bases spread in Sudan, Oman and Egypt. 


Thus, the statement made by the Arab League's secretary general is considered a misrepresen- 
tation of Arab public opinion. It ignores the truth about events taking place in Somalia 
today. Haphazardly accusing a friendly African state of launching aggression against 
Somalia is an insult to an African state that has always affirmed its firm stand along- 

side Arab right. The secretary general should have made a close examination before it 
issued hasty judgments. He should not have been influenced by known Arab sides into 
adopting stands opposing Arab unanimity and harming the progress of Arab-African 

cooperation -- which, particularly now, is exposed to the most serious challenges. 


Any political statement issued by the secretary general of the Arab League should express 
the viewpoints of all league members through prior consultation with them. Accordingly 
the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front considers the abovementioned statement of the 
secretary general as not expressing the viewpoint of the Arab League and a statement that, 
objectively interpreted, does not go beyond expressing the personal view of its author.
