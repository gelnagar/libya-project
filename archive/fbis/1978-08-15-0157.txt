LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFT ExPLAINS IDEOLOGICAL VACUUM IN ARAB ARENA 


LDL4YL4YLYY Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 14 Aug 78 LD 


[Excerpts] Brother Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi met with the Revolutionary Committees last night. 
Lt Col Mustafa al-Kharrubi and Maj al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi and a number of secretaries 
attended. 


Brother Colonel al-Qadhdhafi explained the important role played by the Revolutionary Com- 
mittees to insure the success of the social revolution which aims at the destruction of the 
bases of exploitation and the building of a new society founded upon justice and equality. 


He pointed out that the committees should help the masses to do away with the cppressive 
laws and replace them with just laws which enable them to achieve the slogan: All 
authority, wealth and arms must be in the hands of the people! 


[Al-Qadhdhafi--begin recording] The Revolutionary Committees do not perform the revolu- 
tion on behalf of the masses. They do not exercise the authority to make decisions in 
favor of the masses on behalf of the masses. No, they only incite the masses to perform 
the revolution. The masses are the ones to perform the ievolution; the masses are the 

ones to make the decisions. All gains are made by the masses through their popular revolu- 
tion 


In previous talks here, I have said that the Arab nationalist forces have always been 
defeated in the arena. [Words indistinct] the movement, the nationalist movement which 
was crowned with the emergence of 'Abd an-Nasir as an historical hero was defeated and 
failed to achieve Arab unity. It failed to block the way of Marxism in the Arab countries. 
Indeed, the Arab Nationalist Movement which was founded ideologically on the idea of 
nationalism and against internationalism has turned to Marxism because the nationalist 
forces always suffered defeat in the arena. Why? Why did the Arab nationalists who 
{words indistinct] to 'Abd an-Nasir turned to Marxism. Despite their love for Arab unity, 
they have failed. Why? Because there was an ideological vacuum in the area. The 
nationalist forces were not defeated by arms. Whenever an Arab nationalist met with a 
Marxist, the Arab nationalist always lost. Why? Because the Arab nationalist has no 
ideology. His attitudes are based on emotions. The defense of nationalism is emotional. 
Even our defense of Islamic religion against atheists is emotional. [end recording] 


The brother colonel explained how present theories in the world have failed to find 
radical, final solutions for the political and economic problems of man. The peoples 
are still suffering from party and capital dictatorship, which are the bitterest enemies 
of democracy. He affirmed that the only genuine solution lies in the popular revolution 
which has taken place nowhere else other than in the Jamahiriyah, where authority now 
rests with the people.

LD140922Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 13 Aug 78 LD 
[Text] Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi received brother Al-Sadiq al-Mahdi in audience. During

letter from Sudanese President Ja'far Numayri.
