Ubari Airport (IATA: QUB, ICAO: HLUB) is an airport serving Ubari, a city in the Wadi al Hayaa District of Libya.  It is mostly military, but has recently been used by Prepare2go for civil aviation flying safaris around the country.
The runway is asphalt, with a 200 metres (660 ft) concrete touchdown zone on each end.
The Ubari non-directional beacon (Ident: UBR) is located on the field.
