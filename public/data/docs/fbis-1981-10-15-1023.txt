ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS STATES POLICY ON EGYPT 


LD141540 Tripoli JANA in English 1419 GMT 14 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 14 Oct (JANA) -- The emergency session of Arab People's Congress Permanent 
Secretariat has concluded its work here yesterday. The Secretariat for this session 
following the new development in the Arab region as a result of the assassination of 
As-Sadat. [sentence as received] The session's objective is to support the Egyptian 
people's struggle to overthrow the regime and put Egypt back on its right track. 


Mr Omar al-Hamadi, secretary of the APC Permanent Secretariat, delivered his speech 
welcoming the delegations and said that the development in Egypt should be objectively 
viewed and that the death sentence carried out against As-Sadat was a genuine revolutionary 
act which expressed the will of the Arab masses inside and outside Egypt. It is the 
beginning of the end for As-Sadat's regime whose establishment should be liquidated 
completely. The death by a firing squad is the fate of all traitors such as As-Sadat, 
Numayri, Qabus and Siad Barre. 


Overthrowing the Egyptian regime is a national duty and not a purely Egyptian duty. 
Therefore other urgent missions should be carried out by Egyptian national movement and 
the Arab national liberation movement led by the Arab People's Congress. There is a real 
need for raising the consciousness and struggle of the Egyptian masses in order to foil 
any attempt by the regime to resume its previous socio-economic policies and its alignment 
with Israel and U.S. imperialism. 


He added that creating suitable conditions can be carried out at two different levels: 


1 -- Building the National Front opposed to the regime, on strong bases and on genuine 
democratic relations among its members. We hope that the Democratic National Front, 

set up recently, shall be the bridge over which the masses could cross in order to build 
their national unity for a better future. Those who are opposed to such an approach can 
only blame themselves. 


2 -- Developing the means and methods of the different factions of the National Front. 


Mr al-Hamadi added that duties of the Arab national liberation movement are equally 
important to those of the Egyptian national movement. We should pressurise the Egyptian 
regime nationally and internationally until the fall of the Camp David treaty is realised. 


He added that the regime has been vigorously shaken following As-Sadat's death. We there- 
fore should make sure that the other Arab reactionary regimes such as Numayri'’s do find 
this as a new opportunity to renew their relations with the Egyptian regime on bases 

other than those suggested by the Camp David accords. The same regimes which had relations 
with As-Sadat will continue such relations with the regime unless it is overthrown. 


He added: "We should be very cautious regarding U.S. imperialism and its Western allies’ 
Manoeuvres who are trying to make a national hero and a martyr of peace out of As-Sadat. 
They have intentionally forgotten his oppressive measures and undemocratic policies which 
were criticized by Western public opinion just after his crackdown on the opposition lately." 


Mr al-Hamadi said: "The execution of the traitor As-Sadat makes us more confident in 

the Egyptian people's ability, both at home and abroad, to confront challenges and command 
the respect of the Arab nation's enemies. Even hostile Western circles now acknowledge 
the paramount importance of the Palestinian cause in steering events in the Middle East, 
and of the necessity of offering serious solutions to the Palestinian problem."

This attitude has been clearly espoused in recent statements made by former U.S. 

Presidents Ford and Carter, who announced following their return from As-Sadat's funeral 

that the United States should establish a dialogue with the Palestine Liberation Organization 
in order to reach a lasting peace in the Middle East. These statements frankly admit 

that the Camp David Accords have now reached a dead end. However, we should exercise 
extreme caution because even though a certain position has been imposed on then, 

colonialist countries are attempting to make concessions only in a bid to serve theirs 

and international Zionism's overall interests.... 


As-Sadat's death has put an end to his treacherous excesses. It has also opened new 
vistas for the possibility of change in Egypt. His killing has come as an expression 

of the extent of danger the Egyptian people felt that As-Sadat's policies had imposed on 
them, and of the drastic and only way these policies could be stopped.... 


The execution of As-Sadat by revolutionary forces has given us renewed vigour in 
intensifying struggle within and beyond Egypt. The ultimate aim of this struggle is 
the restoration of Egypt to its natural position as vanguard of the Arab liberationist 
struggle, as it was during President , ‘Abd an-Nasir's term of office....

We are confident that we shall succeed, because our faith in the Egyptian people is by 
far stronger than any conspiracy or treachery.... 


The Permanent Secretariat is therefore called upon today to draw, after careful analysis, 
an information and political plan of action in soldiarity with the Egyptian Arab people. 
The plan should include international press conferences with Egyptian leaders to throw 
light on the Egyptian people's position following the execution of As-Sadat. The 
conference will emphasize the Egyptian people's rejection of all attempts to falsify 
their true will.... 


The secretariat should furthermore call upon the Arab People's Congress to hold an 
emergency session within a month so that the Arab masses can assume their responsibilities 
vis-a-vis Egypt at a time when imperialism, Zionism and reaction are intensifying their 
onslaught against the country.... 


An international conference for solidarity with the Egyptian people should also be 
organized in a bid to counter hostile imperialist, Zionist and reactionary propaganda 
campaigns.... 


Speeches were later delivered by Mr ‘Abd ar-Rahman Khumeisi of the Egyptian National 
Movement; Taysir Khalid of the Palestine Liberation Organization; 'Abdallah Sa'adih of 
the Lebanese patriotic movement; Mr Khaled Mjar, Central Committee member of the Algerian 
National Liberation Front Party. The speakers all affirmed the necessity for Egypt to 
return to its natural position within the Arab fold so that it could effectively 
participate in resisting the Zionist enemy. 


On the other hand, participating delegations also condemned recent U.S. colonialist 
threats and interference in Egypt's internal affairs. They said As-Sadat's death 

marks the beginning of a popular revolution in Egypt to topple the regime of the treason 
and capitulation.

EGYPTIAN REFERENDUM ON MUBARAK CALLED 'SHAM' 


LD141413 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 14 Oct 81 


[Excerpts] The security situation in Egypt is deteriorating in a manner which is con- 
fusing to and increases the fears of the authorities. Despite the declared state of 
emergency in the country and the strict police measures inside the villages and towns, 
the battles being waged by the revolutionary forces have not calmed down; they are being 
moved to the hearts of Cairo and Alexandria, where security men are being killed or 
injured in sweeping revolutionary assaults. 


News agency correspondents are unanimous that security in the country is rapidly col- 
lapsing and the popular activity against the regime is growing appreciably. 


Correspondents note that the sham referendum to appoint Hubni Mubarak as As-Sadat's suc- 
cessor took place under guard of bayonets and machineguns, and that it was popularly 
boycotted in a number of provinces, including Asyut and Marsa Matruh. Reports stress 
that the referendum took place in an abnormal atmosphere and that it had no connection 
with democracy, since it took place in the absence of the opposition and the other 
living forces. The opposition is inside the detention camps and prisons and the whole 
people are encircled by the police force. 


Nabawi Isma'il, minister of the interior in the Egyptian regime, today announced that 
Husni Mubarak has secured the presidency of the Egyptian Republic according to the 
referendum held yesterday. He said the result was 98.64 percent of the electorate 

in favor. Observers note that this officially declared percentage in the referendum 
is the same percentage which the dead As-Sadat used to declare after every referendum 
he held.

"BRIGHT STAR' MANEUVERS PREPARE TO INVADE LIBYA 


LD140922 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 14 Oct 81 


[Text] Washington, 14 Oct (JANA) -- The Reagan administration is making preparations 
for holding terrorist military maneuvers, to begin 9 November on Egyptian territory. 
The Washington POST announced that these maneuvers will be carried out close to the 
Libyan border. 


State Department officials told journalists that a decision Has been made to expand 

these maneuvers to make them an exercise for the invasion of Libya. The military maneuvers 
will cover an area estimated at thousands of square miles. The Washington POST also says 
American units will form the nucleus of the land forces taking part in the maneuvers, 
codenamed "Bright Star." 


The Soviet news agency TASS mentioned that Egyptian and Sudanese forces will play a 
secondary role in the maneuvers. This is an attempt to mislead world public opinion 
and distract it from the real aims of these military maneuvers. 


Commander of the U.S. Strategic Air Force B. Davis told U.S. television that these 
maneuvers must demonstrate the U.S. capability to face all situations in the Middle 

East and in Southeast Asia. Not long ago, Ronald Reagan affirmed that the United States 
would not take part in military operations in the Middle East. But NBC television re- 
futed this allegation, citing a statement by the Sudanese ruler Numayri that the United 
States had undertaken to give Sudan military assistance in order to confront Libya. 

The agency stressed that the military aid which the United States gives Sudan is aimed 
at preparing aggression against Libya.

Egyptian cities.
