LIBYA

Joint Statement on Iranian President’s Issued 
LD101610 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
10 Sep 84 


[Text] A Libyan Arab-Iranian joint statement was made public 
today in both Tripoli and Iran following the visit by President 
‘Ali Khamene’i, president of the Islamic Republic of Iran, to the 
Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. Here is the text of 
the statement: 


Hojjat ol-Eslam val-Moslemin Brother Seyyed ‘Ali Khamene’‘i, 
president of the Islamic Republic of Iran, paid an official and 
fraternal visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
from 8 to 9 September. 


[Tehran Domestic Service in Persian at 1630 on 10 September 
substitutes the following opening paragraph: 


“At the end of the visit to Libya by President Seyyed ‘Ali 
Khamenei, this afternoon a joint statement was issued simulta- 
neously in Tehran and Tripoli, as follows: In the name of God, 
the compassionate, the merciful. On the invitation of His Excel- 
lency Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Libyan 
Al-Fatih Revolution, His Excellency Hoajjat ol-Eslam val- 
Moslemin Seyyed ‘Ali Khamene'i, president of the Islamic 
Republic of Iran, paid an official and fraternal visit to the 
Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 8-9 September, 
1984, and was warmly welcomed by His Excellency Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi and His Excellency Major Jallud, member of the
