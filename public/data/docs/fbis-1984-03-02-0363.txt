LIBYA

Tripoli Parade Marks Birth of Jamahiriyah 
LDO012221 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1930 GMT 1 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli, | Mar (JANA) — A big popular parade marched 
through the streets of Tripoli this evening to celebrate the seventh 
anniversary of the proclamation of the people’s authority and the 
birth of the first jamahiriyah in history. The popular conferences 
joined the march which marks the start of the people s celebra- 
tions of this great occasion. 


The procession, which is the biggest ever in Tripoli, was joined 
by the bands of the girls military academy, the arts institutes, the 
industrial academy and the naval academy in addition to the 
scouts.
