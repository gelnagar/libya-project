Summary (AI): An FBIS London transmission, garbled by OCR, of the final part of a Qadhdhafi speech from September 1976. He addresses Egypt's President Sadat, offers water and relief to Egyptian soldiers stranded in the desert, and warns against Egyptian intelligence stirring trouble among Egyptians living in Libya. It concerns Libya-Egypt relations and names no places in or near Tripoli.
• Qadhdhafi says Libya will not fight Egyptian soldiers and offers them water and relief in the desert.
• He says Egyptian intelligence had infiltrated Egyptians living in Libya and enlisted them against Libya.
• He states that the armed forces shown are only selected units and that the strategic reserve is the General National Service.
• The transmission is marked as take 3 of 3 and contains many indistinct passages.
