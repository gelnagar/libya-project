LIBYA

POLISARIO FRONT SECRETARY GENERAL ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 
LD252222 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 25 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] Mohamed Abdel Aziz, secretary general of the POLISARIO Front, this evening 
arrived in Tripoli on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. Ina statement to JANA he referred 
to the grave phase through which the Western Sahara question is passing after the 
intervention of the defeatist Egyptian president on the side of the Moroccan Royal 
Forces. He said that there are more than 3,000 Egyptian soldiers taking part with the 
Moroccan forces in the war which aims at destroying the Saharan people. 


He added that what has made the question more serious was the decision taken by the 
imperialist Carter administration to send arms and actually intervene on the side of 
the Moroccan forces. He emphasized that the decision is considered clear defiance to 
the will of the Arab and African countries and the peace-loving peoples which are 
making every effort to find a peaceful solution to the 4-year war in the region, 


In his statement, Mohammad Abdul Aziz paid a tribute to the nationalist role played by 
the Great First of September Revolution in supporting and helping the liberation 
movements throughout the world, especially the POLISARI®@ Front, saying that the
