to postpone for 2 weeks the trial of Mr Mustapha Karchaoui, chief editor of 
AL-MUHARRIR, and five other political and trade union leaders, the government 
seeming intent on exerting as much pressure as possible on the main opposition 
party.

OPPOSITION LEADERS" TRIAL POSTPONED--Rabat, 11 Sep (AFP)--The trial of five leaders 
of Morocco's Socialist Union of Popular Forces (USFP) opposition party, including 

its first secretary Abderrahim Bouabid, was adjourned for 2 weeks by the high court 
here today. The five, who are being held in custody, are being prosecuted in 
connection with a party statement criticizing the terms on a proposed referendum 

in the disputed Western Sahara, where Morocco has been fighting nationalist guerrillas 
of the Polisario Front. The USFP Political Bureau statement said that people in 
Morocco should also have a say in the future of the territory, contrary to an 
Organisation of African Unity Committee proposal for a referendum only in the 
territory itself. The OAU proposal has been accepted by the Moroccan Government. 
Before the court with Mr Bouabid were four other members of the USFP Political 
Bureau-~-Casablanca MP Mohamed Mansour, Rabn MP and director of the USFP newspaper 
AL-MURHARRIR Mohamed Elyazghi, university academic Mohamed Lahbbi and Mohamed 
Forkani. The five, who were cheered at length by party supporters when they 

arrived in court, are charged with distrubing public order, peace and security, 

for which they face between 3 months and 2 years jail and possible fines if convicted.
