• Speech broadcast on Voice of the Arab Homeland, transcript dated 2 September 1976 (020200 GMT)
• Speech claims 320 factories built in less than 7 years and over a quarter of a million houses built for the people

• Speech refers to the forthcoming sessions of the General People's Congress
• Speech asserts that an entire armed people will confront any aggressor rather than a regular army
