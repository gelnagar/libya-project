Summary (AI): A April 1985 CIA-released press compilation reports the U.S. publicly warning Qaddafi he would be held responsible for Libyan terrorism, his Tripoli speech launching the National Command of the Arab Revolutionary Forces, and his April 10 news conference where he threatened President Reagan over Sudan. It has no specific Tripoli site, casualty, or prison detail.
• Qaddafi's April 1985 news conference was attended by about 30 Western reporters at his military headquarters in Tripoli.
• The newly formed National Command of the Arab Revolutionary Forces grouped small dissident groups dominated by Libya, according to diplomats.
• In 1981 two Libyan planes were shot down by an American F-14 over the Gulf of Sidra area, per the article.
• Qaddafi said Libya was a target because it defied the U.S.; the article says the U.S. had cut oil imports and severed most ties with Libya.
• 1985-04-02: State Department spokesman Bernard Kalb warned Libya would be held responsible for terrorism against Americans; Qaddafi then addressed the first meeting of the Pan-Arab National Command of the Arab Revolutionary Forces in Tripoli, praising suicide attacks on American installations.
• 1985-04-10: Qaddafi held a news conference at his military headquarters in Tripoli, warning President Reagan that his 'nose will be cut' if the U.S. interfered in Sudan.
