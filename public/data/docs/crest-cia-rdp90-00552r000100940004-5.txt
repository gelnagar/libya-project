Summary (AI): A 1984 New York Post article reports, citing intelligence sources, that the terrorist known as Carlos was hired by Libyan leader Qaddafi to lead attacks against PLO chief Arafat and to run terror operations in Europe. It also says Carlos serves as a consultant to a Libyan intelligence sabotage and terrorism school, and that a new group called "Al Barkan" was formed to replace Black September. No Tripoli-specific sites are named.
• Sources say Carlos was hired to lead Khadafy's attack against Arafat, who had vowed revenge after Khadafy branded him a traitor.
• A new terror organization called Al Barkan was reportedly formed to replace Black September, which carried out the 1972 Munich massacre.
• Carlos is reportedly a consultant for a school in sabotage and terrorism run by Libyan intelligence.
• The article says the explosions killed five people and wounded 469.
• 1984-01-01: New Year's Day bombing of a high-speed train and the Marseilles railway station, which sources attribute to Carlos (5 killed)
