LIBYA

Shultz Speech Confirms U.S. ‘Terrorist Policies’ 
LD260946 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 26 Jun 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 26 Jun (JANA) — In a speech 2 days ago, U.S. 
Secretary of State George Shultz repeated the allegations and 
slanders being composed by the U.S. Administration against the 
Jamahiriyah, the Soviet Union, Iran, and Syria — the countries 
which stand firmly in the face of American hegemony. 


The allegations raised by the U.S. secretary included claims that 
these countries practice terrorism, while he kept his eyes closed 
to the criminal deeds carried out by the U.S. Central Intelligence 
Agency on the direct order of Ronald Reagan. 


The substance of the U.S. secretary’s statements confirms the 
deceptive nature characterizing the U.S. Government's policy. 


Those in Washington are trying in vain to conceal the nature of 
this policy from the world public, which however does realize its 
nature and the dimensions and aims of the plans of the Pentagon 
pirates, who unashamedly justify their antipeoples terrorist poli- 
cies with demagogic justifications that are rejected in interna- 
tional law. 


In these statements Shultz attempted to demonstrate the extent 
of Washington's hostility toward these countries, which he men- 
tioned by name, and on the other hand to express the Reagan 
administration's alarm that these countries obstinately refuse to 
follow in the (?wake) of America and to accept its policy bent on 
imposing force and deception, a clearly uncivilized policy which 
does not reflect civilized human behavior. 


The U.S. Government allows itself a free hand in many countries 
where it spreads terrorism and carries out aggressive terrorist 
plans against their peoples — actions it refuses to call terrorist 

while stemming from the policy of deception, it is displeased, 
for example, with Libya for giving assistance and support to the 
causes of peoples freedom in South Africa, Nicaragua, and 
elsewhere, and deploys its propaganda machinery in waging 
rabid campaigns against Libya because of these legitimate 
stands. 


The methods of distortion and deception have becorne the most 
distinctive elements of Washington's aggressive policy. Using 
these methods Washington exercises the most dastardly and ugly 
deeds against the struggling peoples. 


The statements by U.S. Secretary of State George Shultz repre- 
sent the worst side of this stupid policy, which has become the 
head of organized American terrorism at state level. 


Logically, neither Shultz nor any other official in the U.S. 
Government can conceal these criminal aspects of the American 
terrorist trend, aspects which have come to form the substance 
of the Reagan administration's imperialist course.
