LIBYA

PDRY, SYRIA ACCEPT INVITATION TO TRIPOLI MEETING 


LD041924 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1905 GMT 4 Jan 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 4 Jan (JANA) -- JANA has learned that, following the invitation made 
by the brother leader to leaders of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front to attend 
a meeting in Tripoli as soon as possible in order to face the current situation and 

to agree on a comprehensive strategy concerning the circumstances in which the Arab 
nation finds itself, the PDRY and Syria have both agreed to attend this meeting. 


A reply is still awaited from Algeria and the PLO. 


JANA POLITICAL EDITOR ON ISRAELI-LEBANESE TALKS 


LD041030 Tripoli JANA in English 0824 GMT 4 Jan 83 


[Text] Beirut, Rabi Awal 20, Jan 4, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The third round of the 
shame negotiations in the occupied Beirut suburb of Khaldah between Lebanese and 
Zionist officials ended yesterday evening. It was clear from these direct negotiations 
that the Zionists are insistently demanding the signing of a capitulation treaty to 

end the state of war with the Zionists and open Lebanon to the Israelis as it was the 
case when Egypt was driven out of the Arab block by signing the Camp David capitulatory 
accords. 


The Zionist delegation has refused to agree on the agenda of these negotiations unless 
it is headed by concluding a capitualtion treaty to achieve the objective of the 
Zionists so that the Zionist wagon of death can continue to other Arab stations. 


JANA's political editor commented on these shame negotiations saying "and so follows 

the U.S.-Zionist conspiratorial series one after the other to tighten the grip on 
Lebanon and subjugate it totally to the hammering of the Zionist occupation and the U.S. 
offensive. And if the 3rd round of the shame talks had yielded nothing to the advantage 
of Lebanon and the Lebanese people's cause, then the next rounds would only follow 

suit and Lebanon in the end would be the only loser and the only victim. 


All what we say in this respect when deducing such painful results is not a matter of 
guesses or interpretations, but the clear reality which emerges as witness that the 
Zionist-American strategic alliance will not produce any other result. 


The Zionist enemy is now moving towards the imposition of its conditions as a result 
of the weak position of Lebanon that has come following occupation and massacres. 
Israeli is exploiting this situation for blackmail, with the support of the American 
negotiating partners. 


Through their presence, the Americans are working for the achievement of a dual goal 
from which both allies will benefit. 


For both Israel and the U.S. have laid dwwn their demands and are pressuring Lebanese 
officials to realise them. Lebanon cannot but emerge empty handed from these 
negotiations before these series of demands and pressures. 


America and the Zionist enemy are thus seeking to break Lebanon's ties with the Arabs 
and capitulate totally so that the Zionist death wagon may proceed to overrun other 
countries to impose the same conditions and demands.

STUDENTS IN FRG ASK END TO EMBASSY 'REPRESSIONS' 


LD041450 Hamburg DPA in German 1206 GMT 4 Jan 83 


[Excerpts] Hamburg 4 Jan (DPA) -- The "General Union of Libyan Students" -- the union 
of Libyan students abroad -- has asked the Federal Government to take action against 
the "repressions by the revolutionary committees connected with the Libyan Embassy." 
In a letter published on Tuesday in Hamburg to the federal president, the federal 
chancellor and Bundestag deputies, the students speak of maltreatment and torture of 
Libyan students recently. The aim of the violence was to learn the names of Libyan 
opposition members living abroad. 


The letter from the student organization says that on 4 September 1982 it was decided 
to establish a "command center" in Bonn to coordinate action against Libyan citizens. 
Those affected by this are exiled Libyans and students or trainees who are suspected 

of having “adopted acritical attitude toward the policies of the Libyan dictator, Al- 
Qadhdhafi."
