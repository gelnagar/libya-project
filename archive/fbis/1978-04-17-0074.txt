LIBYA

MAURITANIAN PRESIDENT OULD DADDAH ARRIVES 
LD131840Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1725 GMT 13 Apr 78 LD 


[Excerpts] Tripoli--Mokhtar Ould Daddah, president of the Islamic Republic of Mauritania, 
arrived in Tripoli this evening on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah that will last several days. An official reception was given to the 
Mauritanian president. He was welcomed by Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, 
secretaries and under secretaries, members of the diplomatic corps, heads of departments 
af the Foreign Secretariat and the Jamahiriyah ambassador to Mauritania. 


The Mauritanian president is accompanied by a delegation that includes Politburo Member 
and Minister of Party Affairs Abdallah Ould Boye, Politburo Member and Foreign Minister 
Hamdi Ould Moukmass, Presidency Secretary General Mohammad Ali ash-Sharif and representa- 
tives of various ministries. 


Meets With Al-Qadhdhafi 
LD151324Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 15 Apr 78 LD 


[Excerpts] Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the revolution, this morning had a 
meeting with Moktar Ould Daddah, president of the Islamic Republic of Mauritania. The 
meeting was attended from the Libyan side by Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, 

Maj Ai-Khuway Lidi al-Humaydi and Foreign Secretary Dr ‘Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, and 
from the Mauritanian side by Abdallah Ould Boye, member of the Politburo and minister of 
Party Affairs, and by Hamdi Ould Mounknass, member of the Politburo and foreign minister. 


President Moktar Guld Daddah, president of the Islamic Republic of Mauritania, and his 
accompanying delegation left Tripoli at noon today following a short official visit to 
the Jamahiriyah. 


The president of Mauritania made a statement to the JANA representative in which he said: 
My visit was successful, I had a meeting with my brother Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and dis- 
cussed important issues concerning the affairs of our two countries and the African Con- 
tinent. 


Brother Moktar Ould Daddah stressed that his visit to the Jamahiriyah will have a positive 
and fundamental role in consolidating fraternal relations between the two countries, 
Finally, he expressed his appreciation and graiituue to the brother colonel and the Libyan 
Arab people for their wonderful hospitality, and wished progress and prosperity for the 
people of the Jamahiriyah,

Communique Issued 


LD151812Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1600 GMT 15 Apr 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 15 April--The following joint statement was issued today on the visit 
to Libya of brother Moktar Ould Daddah, president of the Mauritanian Islamic Republic 
from 13 to 15 April 1978: 


At the official invitation of Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, commander of the Great 

1 September Revolution, His Excellency Moktar Ould Daddah, president of the Mauritanian 
Islamic Republic, visited the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah from 

13 to 15 April 1978. 


Official talks were held between Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and President Moktar Ould 
Daddah which covered bilateral relations between the two fraternal countries and Arab, 
African and international issues. 


The talks were attended from the Libyan side by Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, 

member cf the General Secretariat of the People's General Congress; Dr 'Ali ‘Abd as- 

Salam at-Turayki, foreign secretary; 'Isa al-Ba'ba', under secretary at the Foreign 

Secretariat; Ahmad al-Atrash, assis*ant under secretary for technical affairs at the 

Foreign Secretariat; Sana Sha'ban, assistant under secretary for political affairs at the ' 
Foreign Secretariat; Muhammad Ahmad al-Maqri, Jamahiriyah ambassador to Mauritania; 'Ali 

at-Tahir Abu Sarwil, director of Arab affairs; Tahit at-Tumi, acting director of 

cooperation. 


From the Mauritanian side, the talks were attended by ‘Abdallah Ould Bayyuh, minister in 
charge of national secretariat and party organizations; Hamdi Ould Mouknass, minister 

of state for foreign affairs; Muhammad ‘Ali ash-Sharif, secretary general of the Presi- 
dency; Muhammad Mahmud Ould Waddadi, Mauritanian ambassador to the Jamahiriyah; Sa'id 
Muhammad Diyagana, official in charge of missions at the Presidency of the Republic; 
Mohammad Ould Ahmad, assistant secretary at the Presidency; and al-Amin Mawlay az-Zayn, 
permanent secretary general of the Higher Council of Youth. 


The two sides reviewed the bilateral relations between the two fraternal countries in 

all fields. They affirmed their determination to bolster and develop these relations to 
achieve the joint high-minded objectives of both countries. The joint Libyan Arab- 
Mauritanian committee will give a new push to this cooperation and crystalize it so as to 
reflect the ambitions of both fraternal peoples. 


The two sides agreed to continue consultations and coordination between them to bolster 
the Arab and African causes and all the just causes in the world. 


The two sides reviewed the serious situation through which the Arab nation is passing and 
strongly denounced the continued Zionist aggression against the Arab nation--aggression 
which seeks expansion and the destruction of the Palestinian people and their victorious 
revolution. The two sides denounced the treacherous Zionist aggression against fraternal 
Lebanon, which reflects the Zionist entity's arrogance and disregard for human values and 
world public opinion. 


The two sides affirm their full and absolute support for the PLO, the sole legitimate 
representative of the Palestinian people in their just struggle for liberation and return, 
as well as for their right to self-determination ana the establishment of a Palestinian 
state on Palestinian territory.

The two sides affirm their support for the armed struggle of the Palestinian resistance 
for the achievement of these sacred objectives. 


The two sides stand firmly against any attempt to liquidate the Palestinian cause and 
to squander the (?Palestinian) rights. They also uphold the need for the liberation of 
all the occupied Arab territories. 


The two sides greet the steadfastness and struggle of the Palestinian pecple inside the 
occupied territory and strongly denounce all the Zionist methods of suppression and 
terrorism, 


As regards the African Continent, the two sides renewed their strong denunciation of the 
policy of racial discrimination in Africa. They also reaffirmed their support for the 
Just struggle of the peoples of Zimbabwe, Azania and Namibia for freedom and independence. 


As regards the Horn of Africa, the two sides expressed their hope that stability will 
return to that region so that its peoples will be able to devote their capabilities to 
overcaning backwardness in accordance with the spirit of the OAU Charter, and to solving 
the problems of Africa by Africans themselves, without any foreign interference. 


The two sides consider that the Sabha meeting attended by the Jamahiriyah, Chad, Sudan 
and Niger for national reconciliation in Chad and the clearing of the atmospheres is a 
model to be followed in solving the problems of Africa in a fraterna’™ spirit away from 
all foreign interference. 


The two sides condemn the racial collusion between the Zionist entity and the racist 
regimes in Africa as pivots of aggression against the Arab and African peoples. In this 
regard, the two sides uphold the UN resolutions which condemn the racist regimes and 

which consider Zicnism a form of racism. The two sides reaffirm the need for the bolsterin 
of Arab-African cooperation and promoting it in all fields. 


The two sides express their adherance to the principles of the nonalined movement and 
their appreciation of the role it plays in the struggle against imperialism and its 
determination to bolster and strengthen this movement to enable it to face up to the 
challenges of imperialism and colonialism. The two sides demand the establishment of 

a new international economic system which would realize the legitimate objectives of the 
developing countries. The two sides also affirm the right of all peoples to hold full 
control over their natural resources. 


The two sides affirm their adherance to the objectives and Charter of the United Nations 
and agree on the need for the revision of the UN Charter to insure equality among the 
member countries and also insure Third World representation. 


The Libyan Arab side expressed its admiration for the important role the Mauritanian 
Islamic Republic is playing for the realization of cohesion between Africa and the Arab 
homeland and the role it is playing in the Arab and African fields. The Mauritanian side 

r expressed its admiration for the leading role the Great 1 September Revolution is playing 
within the Arab liberation movement and in checking the imperialist plots. 


President Moktar Ould Daddah expressed his admiration for the great achievements of the 
Great 1 September Revolution in all fields. He also expressed his admiration for the 
direct democracy exercised by the Jamahiriyah for the realization of the people's authority.
