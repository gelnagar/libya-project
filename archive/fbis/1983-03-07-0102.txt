LIBYA

ITALY ACCUSED OF HARASSING CIVILIAN AIRLINES 


LD042128 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1957 GMT 4 Mar 83 


[Text] Tripoli 4 Mar (JANA) -- Sources in the Italian Air Force have claimed that the 
harassment by the Italian F-104 aircraft of the Libyan Arab Airlines passenger aircraft 
was nothing more than a normal watch operation by Italian air space surveillance air- 
craft. 


The sources claimed, in an explanatory statement transmitted last night on Italian 
television, channel one, that the communication and contacts made with the pilot of the 
Libyan passenger aircraft did not, according to this claim, establish that the aircraft 
had obtained a permit to cross Italian air space.

Sources at Libyan Arab Airlines today refuted these allegations, and stressed to JANA 
that the air corridor that the Libyan Arab Airlines used for this journey was a regular 
route which it has been using for regular daily journeys for 10 years, that the Libyan 
pilot had previously obtained permission to cross, and that the Italian military air- 
craft started harassing the Libyan passenger aircraft 45 minutes after it entered 
Italian airspace. The Libyan Arab Airlines sources pointed out that the Libyan passen- 
ger aircraft had been in touch with the Italian air control before leaving Maltese air- 
space, 


The Libyan Arab Airlines aircraft had been harassed by Italian F-104 aircraft equipped 
with air-to-air missiles during its normal Flight 102 from Tripoli to London, and the 
harassment continued for 35-km in violation of all international regulations.
