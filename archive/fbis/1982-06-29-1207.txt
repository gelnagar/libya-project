LIBYA

AT-TURAYKI RETURNS FROM AFRICAN TOUR 27 JUNE 


LD281052 Tripoli JANA in English 0856 GMT 28 Jun 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Ramadan 7, June 28, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Dr ‘Ali ‘Abd as-Salam 
at-Turayki yesterday ended a tour of several African countries including Tunisia, Algeria, 
Morocco, Mauritania, Mali, Togo, Ghana, Benin, Nigeria, Gabon and equatorial Guinea during 
which he delivered letters to their presidents from the revolution's leader Colonel Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi. 


The presidents of the countries included in Dr. at-Turayki's tour praised the Jamahiriyah's 
efforts for foreign security and safety and confrontation of all foreign interference in 
the continent's affairs, in accordance with the principle "Africa for the Africans". They 
also affirmed the necessity of solving African problems in the African summit conference, 
the proper place for discussing and solving all African problems. They also stressed the 
need to bolster African unity and confront all colonialist conspiracies directed at Africa. 


Discussions during Dr. at-Turayki's tour also centered on bilateral cooperation between the 
countries visited and the Jamahiriyah, and the development of this cooperation in the 
interest of the African individual....
