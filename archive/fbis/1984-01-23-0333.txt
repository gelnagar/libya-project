LIBYA

Envoy Attacked, Shot in Rome 21 January 


‘Arafat Supporters Blamed 
LD212059 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1930 GMT 21 Jan 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 Jan(JANA)— ‘Ammar at-Taqqazi, secretary 


of the Libyan Arab People’s Bureau in Rome, was the target of 


an attempted assassination when he was fired upon by unidenti- 
fied assailants at noon today outside his residence in the Italian 


capital 


It is generally believed in political cirlces in Rome that elements 
of the wing of Yasir ‘Arafat are behind the attempted assassina- 
tion of the brother secretary of the People’s Bureau. A number 
of pro- Yasir “Arafat officials recently made threats to hunt down 
Libyans in people's bureaus and fraternity bureaus and Libyan 
interests abroad. Also, ‘Arafat himself on 22 May last year, 
during a rally held at a Palestinian camp near Tripoli, northern 
Lebanon, made a threat when he said: “We will not sleep on an 
injustice. We will take revenge because Al-Qadhdhafi is support- 
ing the new movement in Fatah.” 


Previous information has shown the existence of two suicide 
squads belonging to Yasir “Arafat in the capital of Cyprus. They 
were prepared to carry out subversive activity against the inter- 
ests of the Jamahiriyah and Syria in a number of Arab and 
European capitals
