GOVERNMENT BREAKS WITH ETHIOPIA OVER POLISARIO ISSUE 
LDO91406 Rabat Domestic Service in Arabic 1325 GMT 9 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] Morocco today has broken off diplomatic relations with Ethiopia. In this connec- 
tion the Ministry of State in Charge of Foreign Affairs and cooperation has issued a 
statement, the text of which is as follows: 


Following the announcement by the Ethiopian Government of its recognition of the so-called 
POLISARIO and the so-called Saharan Government, the government of His Majesty, which con- 
tinues to respect diplomatic principles, values and norms which always have distinguished 
Moroccan-Ethiopian official relations, denounces the hostile behavior of the Ethiopian 
Government and considers it a blatant intervention in Morocco!s internal affairs. Because 
of this, Morocco has decided to break off diplomatic relations with Ethiopia. 


The spokesman of the Ministry of State in Charge of Foreign Affairs and Cooperation has 
stressed that the decision of His Majesty King Hassan's government to break off diplomatic 
relations with Ethiopia was conveyed to the Ethiopian authorities during the meeting held 
by M'thamed Boucetta, minister of state in charge of foreign affairs and cooperation, this 
morning with the charge d'affaires of the Ethiopian Embassy in Rabat. Also, Morocco!s 
ambassador in Ethiopia has been delegated to convey the government's decision to the 
Ethiopia Government. 


The ministry spokesman stressed that the Kingdom of Morocco!s representation to the General 
Secretariat of the OAU in Addis Ababa will continue, 


Boucetta Message to OAU 


LD121816 Rabat MAP in French 1530 GMT 12 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] Rabat, 12 March, MAP--M'hamed Boucetta, the Moroccan minister of state for foreign 

affairs and cooperation, today sent Edem Kodjo, OAU secretary general, a message explaining 
the reasons why Morocco has broken off diplomatic relations with the regime in Addis Ababa, 
Here is the text of this message: 


"Mr Secretary General: 


"I have the honor to inform you that the recognition by the Ethiopian Government of the 
so-called POLISARIO and the Saharan Democratic Arab Republic is a hostile act against the 
Kingdom of Morocco, which considers such a stance by a member state of our organization 

a flagrant interference in its domestic affairs. This stance is all the more provocative 
as Addis Ababa is the permanent home of our organization (OAU) which, as such, has not 
recognized the POLISARIO as a liberation movement nor its republic as an African state, 
This is why the government of the Kingdom of Morocco has decided to break off diplomatic 
relations with the government of the Provisional Military Council of Ethiopia. 


"T am asking you, Mr secretary general, to inform member states of the above. 


"Yours most sincerely."

CHAMBER OF DEPUTIES ADOPTS STATEMENT ON SAHARA POLICY 
LD092316 Rabat Domestic Service in Arabic 2000 GMT 9 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] At the conclusion of its extraordinary session this afternoon, the Chamber of 
Deputies gave a standing ovation and unanimously adopted a statement in which it announced 
the absolute support of the nation's representatives for the contents of King Hassan's 
royal address to the chamber delivered yesterday. Here is the text of that historic state- 
ment : 


[Unidentified speaker--begin recording] In the extraordinary session held on 8 and 9 March 
March 1979, the Chamber of Deputies listened to the address of his majesty the king to the 
honorable representatives. After studying the situation resulting from the repeated aggressions 
against the territory of the homeland, the Chamber of Deputies issues the following statement: 


The Chamber of Deputies greets with all respect and appreciation the policies of His Majesty 
King Hassan II--may God support him and make him triumph--as demonstrated by his continuous 
efforts to protect our recovered territories, defend the dignity of Morocco, protect its 
gains and liberate the rest of its parts. 


The Chamber of Deputies reaffirms the national consensus that the recovered Sahara has indeed 
been Moroccan territory since time immemorial, that the struggle to liberate it has never 
been interrupted since the independence of Morocco, that its recovery came through decoloni- 
zation in accordance with international laws and norms and in accordance with the terms of 
the Madrid tripartite agreement of 14 November 1975 as endorsed by the United Nations. The 
recovery of the Sahara was simply the carrying out of an historical and legal fact: that 
throughout its history the recovered Sahara has been an indivisible part of Morocco and 

that its sons have effectively taken part in the construction of Morocco's history and 
civilization and in protecting its existence and Islamic character, 


Keeping these facts in view, the chamber declares that since Morocco's recovery of its Sahara, 
it has become necessary to take firm positions at the international level, to reject the 
raising of the so-called Sahara problem, and to consider it a domestic problem. 


The chamber believes that the measures mentioned in the royal address constitute a decisive 
change in the policy conducted by our country, and that from now on it is the duty of Morocco 
to rely basically on its own means and force to confront any challenges to the defense of 

its territorial integrity. 


The chamber believes that the repeated armed aggressions organized and carried out by the 
rulers of Algeria against our country and our fellow citizens are deliberate acts of 
aggression which constitute the basis of the conflict between Morocco and Algeria. They are 
intended to destroy the territorial integrity of Morocco, to encroach upon its sovereignty 
and to start a war in the region. 


The chamber records its absolute pride in the successful efforts of the Royal Moroccan Armed 
Forces, the auxiliary forces, the royal gendarmerie and the national security forces in 
defending the national sovereignty and safety of the homeland and the security of its 
citizens. 


The chamber also records its absolute pride in the steadfastness, heroism and sacrifice of 
the Moroccan people, especially those in the regions subjected to aggression, during the 
Struggle to safeguard the territorial integrity of the country.

For all these reasons, the Chamber of Deputies declares its absolute support for His 
Majesty King Hassan's decision to set up a council for the protection of national terri- 
tory and the security of the state. The chamber reaffirms that Morocco will never accept 
any discussion over its sovereignty and territorial integrity and will never accept the 
setting up of any artificial entity in the south that would separate it from the fraternal 
state of Mauritania and threaten its security and stability. [applause] 


The chamber recommends the exercise of the right of pursuit, which is recognized as the 
right of any state that is subjected to aggression. It also recommends that retaliatery 
actions be undertaken whenever Moroccan territories are subjected to military aggression 
from outside. [applause] 


The chamber reaffirms that confronting aggression on the military level requires that 

his majesty's government adopt urgent and effective measures in the economic, social, 
diplomatic and information fields. It requires that the internal front be strengthened 

at a time when the country is bracing itself to offer more sacrifices to repel aggression 
and safeguard the security of the homeland. [applause] 


The chamber urges all Moroccan citizens to arm themselves with greater vigilance and a 
spirit of mobilization with a view to standing up to the challenge threatening the country. 
It urges all citizens once again to demonstrate their maturity and cohesion f ranks to 
protect the territorial integrity and dignity of the homeland. The chamber declares that 

it will remain leyal to its pledges to the Moroccan people and reaffirms its full confidence 
in the policy outlined by his majesty in his address to the chamber to insure the defense 
by all means of the territorial unity of the country and the protection of its security 

and stability. [prolonged applause] [end recording] 


AMBASSADOR TO SYRIA RECALLED 'FOR CONSULTATIONS! 
LDO91445 Rabat Domestic Service in Arabic 1325 GMT 9 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] Following the Syrian media's escalation of information campaigns hostile to Morocco 
and supporting the so-called POLISARIO's activities hostile to our country--which conflicts 
with the spirit of Arab solidarity and the principle of non-interference in internal affairs, 
and contradicts the deep fraternal relations which have brought the Moroccan and Syrian 
peoples together and the pure blood which mingled together in the historic Golan battles-- 
M'hamed Boucetta, minister of state in charge of foreign affairs and cooperation, this 
morning summoned the ambassador of the Syrian Arab Republic in Rabat to his office and 
conveyed to him his majesty's government's displeasure with these campaigns. He reminded 
the ambassador that this was not in line with the stand previously adopted by the Syrian 
Government in supporting Morocco's effort to regain Sahara and complete its territorial 
unity. He told the ambassador that the Moroccan Government regretfully has decided to 
recall his majesty's ambassador in Damascus for consultations.

ALGIERS SCORED OVER DECISION ON OAU COMMITTEE VISIT 
LD092318 Rabat Domestic Service in Arabic 2000 GMT 9 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] The OAU Committee of Wiseman recently decided to pay visits to the various 
capitals interested in the Sahara problem. The committee was due to begin the visits 
on 15 March 1979. Morocco welcomed the initiative, and took the necessary measures to 
receive its members; however, the Algerian Government declared that the time was not 
propitious for the visits of the committee members. This attitude of the ruler of 
Algeria came as a fresh manifestation of its policy of obstruction, procrastination 
and maneuvers regarding all the issues concerning our territorial unity.

here Friday on Ethiopia's and Vietnam's recognition of the so-called "RASD" (Arab

regimes such as Ethiopia. 


The daily outlined that the objectives of the struggle for liberty in Vietnam, and 
against feudalism in Ethiopia, were not reached. On the contrary, Ethiopian leaders 
wallowed the country in blood and increased the rate of political executions. 


The Ethiopian cynicism is evident in the case of Eritrea, where Ethiopia is destroying 
the Arab and Muslim revolution, with the help of Warsaw Pact.
