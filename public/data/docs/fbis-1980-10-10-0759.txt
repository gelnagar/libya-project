LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI CABLE TO GULF LEADERS ON SUPPORTING IRAN 
LD091914 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1730 GMT 9 Oct 80 


[Text of 9 October cable from Libyan leader Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to the Saudi king, 
UAE president and the amirs of the gulf states] 


[Text] We have followed with anxiety news of the American military advance, the possi- 
bility of widening its presence in the gulf region and the Arab Gulf, and the justifi- 
cations and claims that have accompanied this. 


Vv. 10 Oct 80 NORTH AFRICA 


We want to make clear that these colonialist justifications mean nothing other than that 
the United States wants to colonize the Arab world and in this it--that is America--has 
joined with the Zionist enemy with the objective of ending the independence of the Arab 
homeland. America is now advancing militarily in the Arab world after it has paved the 
way, for this politically and economically over many years, and in particular in the Arab 
peninsula region and the gulf. 


During recent years it has worked rapidly to prepare for its military presence in the 
Arab homeland, which has assumed practical form recently. This presence now has begun 

to advance into the Arab peninsula from Egypt and Palestine and there is no doubt that 
these columns of U.S. military forces have begun to meet with the Zionist presence in the 
Arab homeland in order to close in on the Arab peninsula and the gulf. The objective of 
this American military advance is the loss of the independence of the Arab homeland at 
the hands of America. 


If the Arabs have decided that their independence should end so that they may retain 

kings and presidents, this American military advance is guaranteed to destroy our existence. 
If we accept this, it is as though we have accepted that our independence will come to 

an end, and in our evaluation this action lacks any characteristic of friendship or an 
alliance and it has no meaning other than acceptance of the loss of our independence. 

Any other justifications are groundless. 


Among those justifications are the problems of Afghanistan, the Iraqi-Iranian war and 
the contrived energy problem, whether from the aspect of production or its price. We 
do not accept that the Arab homeland should loose its independence as a result of a 
conflict between the superpowers. If America is concerned over Afghanistan, let it go 
and fight there because when Russia entered Afghanistan, America decided to enter the 
Arab homeland in return. Now America is exploiting the Iraqi-Iranian war. Thus, it 
has brought its early warning and surveillance aircraft to the Arab peninsula and now is 
urging the gulf states to enter under this air umbrella, the umbrella of the U.S. Air 
Force. If a state in the region becomes Marxist, the United States comes and occupies 
another state in return, as it did with Egypt. For your informzcion, we will resist 
America in the Arab homeland; and if we do not find official cvoperation between us in 
resisting this danger which threatens the Arab homeland, we will resist it in alliance 
with the masses of the Arab homeland who refuse to relinquish their independence. We 
will work for the protection of the Arab homeland outside futile official actions. 


We will resist this American occupation without paying attention to artificial borders 
since America itself has not paid any attention to the Arab homeland and its countries. 
We believe that these American surveillance and early warning aircraft should leave the 
Arab skies and that America should withdraw from its military bases in Muscat, Oman, and 
Somalia and that we Arabs should then proceed to resist the American armies in Egypt and 
the armies of its ally in Palestine. This confrontation is within the context of the 
crusade existing between Muslims and Christians, between East and West, and between 

the Islamic nation and the Europeans. It is also an Islamic duty for us to be allied 
with the Muslims of Iran in this confrontation in order to face the crusade instead of 
fighting them in place of America. 


[Signed] Col Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi

COMMENTARY CLAIMS ONLY U.S. THREATENS ARABS 


LD100729 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 9 Oct 80 


{Unattributed commentary ] 


[Excerpts] It has now become clear for everyone to see that American imperialism has 
decided to invade the Arab homeland and to enter into a hot military confrontation with 
the Arab nation. This is corroborated and supported by the clear increase in the 
American military presence and hordes closing in on the Arab oil lake [as heard] in the 
gulf. This American military invasion is taking place under fallacious pretexts, justi- 
fications and excuses, such as protection of fuel supplies and energy sources for the 
Western states, protection of the security of the region against the danger from the 

left as it were, or monitoring the development of the Iraqi-Iranian war and the results 
it might lead to. Under this dense smokescreen, American columns advance with all their 
weapons: navy, air force and army. They have hiterto occupied three Arab countries: 
Egypt, Somalia and Oman. These brigdeheads are now being used to close in on civilization 
places, spiritual centers and the oil areas of this nation. In this connection, ground 
radar equipment and four AWACS planes have been sent to the Arab Gulf region. 


America is now entering into a real confrontation with the Arab nation in order to 

deprive it of what remains of its independence and to bring it back under the yoke of 
direct colonialism and direct American hegemony. All this is taking place, regrettably, 
without resistance. This brings on us Arabs shame, disgrace and humiliation in front of 
other peoples of the world. For how is it possible to see the Arab land subjected to 
direct occupation and open and blatant colonialist invasion without the Arab land ex- 
ploding into hell, flames and erupting volcanoes? Without Arab nationalist feelings being 
aroused in defense of Arab dignity and self-respect, the sanctity of the Arab land and 
Arab resources? Without this dull, dazed, doped Arab body being moved? 


Over there, in the colonialist West, they laugh at us when they say: We have come to 
your land just to defend you and to protect your security; or, to protect fuel supplies; 
or, to protect your seats against the winds of the revolution; or, and this is the worst 
disaster, we have come to protect Islam! Carter has even donned a turban and robe and 
turned into the shaykh of Islam of our times! 


Have the Arabs ever been threatened by anyone other than America? Is there a power with 
a greedy eye on Arab wealth other than America? Is there a worse enemy for Islam and 
Muslims than American imperialism? And who other than America is to blame for the 
sufferings, pain, oppression, exile and the loss of Palestine? Is there a force other 
than America that tries to impose surrender on the Arabs? 


O Arabs! America is the one who kills you every day 1,000 times; it is the one who is 
full of hatred for you, who envies you and who says: They are a people who do not deserve 
oil, because they are backward, and because oil revenues are wasted on gambling in Monte 
Carlo, to gratify pleasures and lust in haunts in Europe, and to keep up palaces, 
entourages and servants. 


What is left after all this, O Arabs? You Arab youths, men and women! Are you going to 
await death to come to you in your homes at the hands of the civilized Americans? Where 
is jihad solidarity? Where is the will to fight? Where is Islam that makes it your duty 
to fight? Islam that orders you to fight and makes fighting the duty of all able- 

bodied Muslims: "It is your duty to fight even though you are reluctant to fight." 
{Koran quotation] 


V. 10 Oct 80 NORTH AFRICA 


If you are short of ideas, means or methods, O Arab youths, the Jamahiriyah has lit the 
green light in front of you, opening its land, heart and mind for you. Those who want to 
volunteer to start the phase of the counteroffensive--the Jamahiriyah land opens its 

arms to them, because it is the land of jihad to defend pan-Arabism and Islam, and because 
it is the land of the holy fighters who humiliated Rome. This land will be the main base 
for the launch of the revolutionary march and the start of the phase of counteroffensive 
against America and against the Israelis.
