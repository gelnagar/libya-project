LIBYA

JALLUD DEFINES LIBYAN ATTITUDE TOWARD TERRORISM 
LD201000Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 20 Jul 78 LD 
[Excerpts] New Delhi--Maj 'Abd as-Salam Jallud held a press conference at the Presidential 
Palace in New Delhi. It was attended by a large number of information officials, re- 
presentatives of Indian radio and television, chief editors and correspondents of world 
news agencies in New Delhi. 
Speaking about international terrorism, Major Jallud stressed that the Jamahiriyah has 


various laws and penalties against all kinds of terrorism because our Islamic religion, which 
is the religion of affection, freedom and peace, stands against terrorism,

He also underlined that the Jamahiriyah had not been and woul? not be a refuge for 
terrorists, If imperialist and Zionist propagaria had orchestrated their trumpets to cast 
doub* on our attitude toward just causes, chiefly the Palestinian cause and the libera- 
tion movements in Africa, we stress that it is the major Western powers which are exer- 
cising terrorism, whether by their forces, fleets or bombs, or by threatening from time to 
time to suspend peoples! food supplies, Torrorism is in fact being exercised by the 
imperialist states against the small nations. The assassination of President Ngouabi, 

the recruitment of mercenaries and their dispatch to invade Benin and Guinea, the 
overthrow of national governments and the forcing of peoples in these countries to yield 
again to colonialism, precisely this is terrorism, 


Major Jallud went on to say: We continue to stand by the Palestinian people and by the 
liberation movements and uphold good and right everywhere, If this is terrorism, as 
colonialism claims, then we are with the Palestinians and all the liberation movements, 
We support the efforts of the United Nations to issue an international agreement to 
prevent terrorism, provided it contains a clause confirming that the struggle for freedom 
is not considered terrorism, 


'Abd as-Salam Jallud emphasized that the Arab nation will liberate all their territories 
and that this does not mean that it will destroy the Jews. Jews and Arabs have coexisted 
for a long time, and Palestine does not mean the Arabs alone, but it also means the Jews 
who lived there before 1948--that is, before the arrival of the racist mercenaries from 
Germany, France and America, 


AT-TURAYKI MEETS WITH AFRICAN LEADERS IN KHARTOUM 


[Editorial Report WA] Tripoli JANA in English on 19 and 20 July reports the meetings of 
Libyan Secretary of Foreign Affairs 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki with other African 
leaders during the 15th African Summit Conference deliberations in Khartoum: President 
Ahmed Sekou Toure of Guinea; President Antonio Agostinho Neto of Angola; Vice President 
'Abu al-Qasim Muhammed Ibrahim of Sudan; Executive Council Chairman Joseph Lako of Sudan; 
President Houari Boumedienne of Algeria; President William Tolbert of Liberia; President 
Idi Amin of Uganda; President Juvenal Habrarimana of Rwanda; and Mr Joshua Nkomo of the 
Zimbabwe Patriotic Front. 


AL-FIKR AS-SIYAS: U.S. INDULGES IN TERRORISM 
LDO71748Y Tripoli AL-FIKR AS-SIYASI in Arabic 26 Jun 78 pp 12, 13 LD 


[Ahmad al-Batrawi article: "U.S, Terrorism: Export of Mercenaries to the World Under 
the Guise of Businessmen and Advisers" ] 


[Excerpts] Wars are no longer confined to their old, conventional methods, For some time 
new kinds of wars have been spreading throughout the world, such as, for example, the 
kidnaping of leading officials and businessmen, This is in addition to the wars that 
mercenaries wage against progressive regimes in Asia, Africa, Latin America and elsewhere. 


The strange thing about colonialism is that it perpetrates all kinds of crimes in the 
world and yet tries, with the help of its propaganda, and its terrorist and repressive 
machines, to put the blame for all these actions on world revolutionary forces and move- 
ments in order to distort their image before the underdog peoples.
