LIBYA

UGANDA'S PRESIDENT AMIN LEAVES FOR HOME 
LD271748Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 27 Sep 78 LD 


[Text] President Idi Amin Dada left Baninah airport at noon today for home at the end 
of his 3-day official visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. President 
Idi Amin was accorded an official sendoff at Baninah airport. 


AT -TURAYKI RECEIVES VISITING SPANISH OFFICIAL 
LD271010Y Tripoli JANA in English 0807 GMT 27 Sep 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, September 27, JANA--Dr. 'Ali 'Abd as -Salam at -Turayki last night 
received at the Foreign Secretariat in Tripoli the head of the African Department in 
the Spanish Foreign Ministry, currently visiting the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah. 


The western desert problem, the developments of the Arab cause and means of consolidating 
the bilateral relations between Spain and the Jamahiriyah were investigated at the 
meeting.
