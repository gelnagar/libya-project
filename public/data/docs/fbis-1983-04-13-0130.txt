Radio Monte Carlo Interview 
JN121850 Paris Radio Monte Carlo in Arabic 
1725 GMT 2 Apr 83 


[Interview with ‘Abd al-’Ati al-’'Ubaydi, secretary of the Libyan 
General People’s Committee for Foreign Liaison, by Monte 
Carlo correspondent George Qardahi in Paris on 12 April 
recorded] 


[Text] [Question] Your Excellency, what did you discuss with 
Claude Cheysson? 


[Answer] Our discussions focused on the entire scope of the 
relations which exist between Libya and France, particularly in 
wake of Cheysson’s visit last May and the meeting of the joint 
Libyan-French committee in March. We also discussed how to 
develop these cultural and economic relations for th= better and 
to have them return to their previous level. We discussed means 
of promoting our political dialogue and consultations on issues of 
mutual interest 


[Question] What is your evaluation of current Libyan- French 
relations? 


[Answer] These relations are good and they will inevitably be 
improved. Cultral, economic and political meetings will be held 
in the future to promote these relations 


Vv. 13 Apr 83 


[Question] Will the Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah attend the 
forthcoming Arab summit called by King Hassan II? 


[Answer] We shall wait and see whether there will be a summit 
or not, because in view of the recent developments there will be 
no need to hold a summit 


[Question] How does Libya view the rapprochement in the Arab 
Maghreb? 


[Answer] We support any rapprochement among Arabs which 
takes into consideration the will of the Arab people. This applies 
to what is currently taking place in the Arab Maghreb. Aithout 
any doubt, we are against the formation of an Arab axis that 
would seek to divide the Arab homeland because we are advo- 
cates of unity and view the Arab nation as being one nation which 
possesses the means to unite in a manner that would acceptable 
to the Arab people. 


[Question] In the wake of Jallud’s visit to Moscow, there was talk 
about the conclusion in the near future of a treaty of friendship 
and cooperation between Libya and ihe USSR. When do you 
expect this treaty to be signed? 


[Answer] We are currently studying several ideas with Soviet 
friends and the treaty will be signed as soon as we ascertain that 
we share these ideas. 


[Question] What is your comment on the assassination of Dr 
‘Isam as-Sartawi? 


[Answer] We do not believe in violent methods or killings as a 
solution to any difference. This is so, irrespective of our viewpoint 
on the method and concepts adopted by the late ‘Isam as-Sartawi 
to settle the Palestinian cause.
