Summary (AI): A reprinted New York Times Magazine article by Seymour Hersh alleges that former CIA operatives Edwin Wilson and Frank Terpil sold their intelligence contacts and expertise to Libya's Colonel Qaddafi, including helping set up an assassination-weapons plant. It gives no Tripoli-specific locations.
• Wilson and Terpil allegedly helped Libya set up a manufacturing plant for assassination weapons.
• They allegedly recruited dozens of former Green Berets to teach Libyan soldiers and Arab terrorists how to handle explosives.
• They allegedly shipped arms and explosives to Libya using forged State Department export certificates.
• Government has known about the Qaddafi connection since fall 1976, when Mulcahy approached the CIA and FBI.
