LIBYA

JANA CITES IRAN'S ‘UNLIMITED SUPPORT’ FOR LIBYA 


LD081926 Tripoli JANA in English 1815 GMT 8 Apr 82 


[Text] Kuwait, 8 Apr (JANA) -- Dr ‘Ali Akbar Velayati, the Iranian foreign minister, 
reaffirmed his country's unlimited support for the jamahiriyah in its confrontation of 
U.S. ambitions. 


He told a JANA correspondent here Iran will firmly stand by the Libyan people in their 
brave confrontation of any military attack launched by Washington. He also expressed his 
country's condemnation of U.S. intention to carry out military manoeuvres in the Gulf 

of Sidra within Libyan territorial waters. 


He praised the brotherly relations between both Muslim peoples, stressing their strength 
and their good impact on the causes of independence and emancipation from imperialism. 


SOCIAL SECURITY OFFICIAL MEETS ROMANIA'S PATAN 


LD081948 Tripoli JANA in English 1846 GMT 8 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 8 Apr (JANA) -- Secretary general of people's committee of social 
security met here this morning with Ion Patan, Romanian deputy prime minister, who is 

on a visit to the jamahiriyah. They discussed aspects of bilateral cooperation and ways 
of developing and strengthening it.
