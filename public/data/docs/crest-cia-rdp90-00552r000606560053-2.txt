Summary (AI): A Washington Post article reports on the trial in Fort Collins, Colorado, of former Green Beret Eugene Tafoya, accused of attempting to assassinate Libyan dissident Faisal Zagallai in 1980 at the behest of the Libyan government. The case is US-based and does not describe places in Tripoli.
• Tafoya was a former Green Beret who served 23 years in the Army, including time in Special Forces, and was discharged in 1976.
• The prosecution alleges Tafoya was working for Edwin P. Wilson as part of a plot to liquidate opponents of Qaddafi's regime.
• Tafoya testified he was sent by an unidentified CIA agent to warn Zagallai to stop activities endangering detente between Egypt and Israel.
• The shooting reportedly occurred on Oct. 14, 1980; the article says the trial was held in Fort Collins, Colorado.
• 1980-10-14: Tafoya allegedly came to Faisal Zagallai's apartment under the pretext of a job interview, attacked him and shot him twice in the head, blinding him in one eye.
