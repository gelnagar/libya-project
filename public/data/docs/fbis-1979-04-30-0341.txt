LIBYA

RETURN OF AMBASSADOR TO UGANDA POSTPONED 
JN281508 Khartoum SUNA in Arabic 1430 GMT 28 Apr 79 JN 


[Text] Nairobi, 28 April--The Libyan ambassador's return to Kampala has been post- 
poned following a meeting between him and Mr Otima Alimadi, Uganda's new foreign 
minister, who insisted that Libya recognize the new regime in Uganda before discussing 
the subject of Libyan prisoners of war and before reopening the Libyan Embassy in 
Kampala, 


The Libyan Government had instructed its ambassador to Uganda to return to Kampala 
from Nairobi, where he went following the capture of Kampala by the Ugandan forces, 
which were supported by Tanzania, 


POLAND'S JAGIELSKI ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI FOR VISIT 
LD270854 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 26 Apr 79 LD 


[Excerpt] Mieczyslaw Jagielski, deputy chairman of the Polish Council of Ministers, 
arrived in Tripoli this evening for a 4-day visit to the Jamahiriyah, during which 
he will hold talks on ways of expanding and consolidating relations between the two 
countries,
