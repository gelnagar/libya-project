LIBYA

Interior Minister on North African Summit 
LD280002 Kuwait KUNA in English 1006 GMT 27 Sep 83 


[Text] Tunis, Sept 27 (KUNA) — The North African Arab states 
will shortly hold a summit to discuss the armed American 
intervention in Lebanon, the Libyan Interior and Justice Minis- 
ter, Muhammad Abu al-Qasim az-Zuwayy told a news con- 
ference here last night. Az-Zuwayy, who is in the last leg of a 
North African tour, described the armed American intervention 
in Lebanon as “a serious precedent that can be repeated in any 
other Arab state unless a firm Arab stand is taken.” 


He said that during his present tour, he delivered messages from 
the Libyan leader Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to the heads 
of state he visited on the need to take a united action “against 
the new crusade assault led by the United States with the support 
of some Christain European countries” which he did not name. 
“The American Administration is using the fascist Lebanese 
Phalange Party as a spearhead in its new crusade war against the 
Muslim masses in Lebanon and the Arab nation,” he went on 
saying. Az-Zuwayy called for evacuation of the four-nation 
multinational force from Lebanon and an end to Israeli occupa- 
tion of parts of that country either by implementing the United 
Nations resolutions or by war. The Libyan official defended the 
stand of the Soviet Union during the recent civil war in Lebanon, 
as opposed to the American support for the Phalange Party, 
saying Moscow offered full moral support and supplied Syria 
with the necessary weapons. “We can not possibly ask the Soviet 


Union to come and fight on our behalf,” he said. Az-Zuwayy 
expressed his country’s willingness to attend the forthcoming 
Arab summit in Riyadh “if the Arabs agree to convene it,” but 
hastened to add it might not be necessary for Colonel al- 
Qadhdhafi to head the Libyan delegation 


Chad President Says Libya Has Not ‘Intervened’ 
LD272357 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 27 Sep 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 27 Sep (JANA) President of the Unified 
Legitimate Government of Chad, Goukouni Oueddei, has 
refuted allegations that the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah has intervened in the struggles in Chad. President 
Goukouni Oueddei said that allegations about Libyan interven- 
tion are a fabrication by the Ndjamena rebel Hissein Habre to 
enable him to obtain support from the U.S. Administration, the 
French Government,and the rest of the parties backing him. 


President Goukouni Oueddei accused the U.S. Administration 
and France of slaughtering and attacking the people of Chad. He 
was interviewed by a delegate of the British supervisory body in 
Tripoli. He said that the rebel Habre is considered to be an agent 
of the U.S. Administration, which uses him as a means through 
which it can threaten the Jamahiriyah, and this is one of the 
reasons for U.S. intervention in Chad. President Goukouni Oued- 
dei affirmed that the only forces opposing the rebel Habre are 
those of the Unified Legitimate Government of Chad. He 
explained that Zionist soldiers are working with the forces which 
the Zaire regime sent to Chad to participate with Habre in the 
war he is waging against the people of Chad. This is in addition 
to the other European mercenaries hired in the Belgian capital. 


JANA: Israeli Troops Return to Lebanese Town 
LD271631 Tripoli JANA in English 1425 GMT 27 Sep 


[Text] Beirut, Dhulhijah 20, Sept 27, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY People arriving from southern Lebanon have 
reported that a Zionist contingent of 200 soldiers, 60 tanks, a 
troop carrier, 20 vehicles, and ten jeeps returned to position itself 
once again at the town of Karkha in Jazzin Province. This 
position was vacated by the Zionist forces, on the 25th of last 
month, amidst news of decreasing the number of Zionist forces 
in the area between Jazzin and (Uifon) in the south. On the other 
hand a Zionist patrol put an explosive device in Al Awgaf St. in 
Sidon, which on exploding injured a number of the inhabitants 
and caused material damages to nearby buildings and cars. 


Clashes in Somalia, Rush To Join Front Reported 
LD271342 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
27 Sep 83 


[Text] Important and sudden political changes are expected to 
occur in Somalia at any moment. The flow of thousands of 
citizens, including large numbers of the Somali Armed Forces, 
to join the Somali National Salvation Front is being noticed. 
Fierce clashes are taking place in the provinces of Galguduud, 
Hiran, and Togdheer between the agent Barre and the Somali 
National Salvation Front. Clashes and mutinies have taken place 


among units of the Somali Army in the aforementioned regions 
Observers consider it is the last hours of the Siad Barre regime
