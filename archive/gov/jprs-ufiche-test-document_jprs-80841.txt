SOVIETS COMPLETING BRIDGE OVER THE AMU DARYA RIVER 
Tashkent PRAVDA VOSTOKA in Russian 14 Apr 82 pl 


[Text] UzTAG. At first, in its upperpreaches, people crossed the Amu Darya 
River on foot; they were construction workers of the Ivan Dovbysha-Nikolay 
Kuzyakin Brigade who completed assembling the last--ninth-link of the bridge 
over this great Central Asian river. This bridge is 800 meters long and was 
assembled one and one-half years earlier than planned. It is with such 
swiftness that the 32nd Bridge Detachment of the USSR Ministry of Transportation 
Construction operates. The assigned task is to open the bridge for working 
traffic in May. Now on the passable part of the bridge, they are laying rails 
and building a roadbed for automobile traffic. 


Finishing workers have been painting the spans. The new bridge over the Amu 
Darya makes it possible for railroad and automobile traffic to move between 

the two friendly countries, the Soviet Union and Afghanistan. Shipping freight 
without transfering it onto river vessels affords the opportunity of signifi- 
cantly expediting and making exchange of goods cheaper.

Text] Whenever President Reagan gives arms to Tunisia, he assumes that these 
arms will be directed against Libya. It does not matter if Tunisia affirms, 
via its prime minister Mohamed Mzali and with full knowledge of Reagan, that

This strange attitude was manifested la t Friday during the press conference 
held by Reagan and Mzali at the end of Mzali's visit to the United States. 
Mzali affirmed that relations between the two fraternal countries, Tunisia 

and Libya, are normal. However, Reagan took the microphone to announce to 

the reporters that the United States is always ready to help Tunisia confront 
(external threats). U.S. officials promptly explained that Reagan specifically 
meant (Libyan threats).
