Arab People’s Congress on Egypt-iraq Relations 
LDO091957 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 9 Jul 83 


{Text} Tripoli, 9 Jul (JANA) — The Permanent Secretariat of 
the Arab People’s Congress has followed with deep anxiety the 
development of relations between the Iraqi and Egyptian 
regimes, which was reflected in the meeting of Iraqi Foreign 
Minister Tariq “Aziz with the foreign minister of the Egyptian 
regime and in the restoration of relations between the two 
regimes to the level prior to the 1979 Baghdad summit. 


in a press statement issued today the Permanent Secretariat of 
the Arab People’s Congress stressed that the gravity of these 
developments arises from the fact that this constitutes a complete 
abandonment of Arab summit resolutions connected with As- 


Sadat's signing of the Camp David accords, accords to which the 
Egyptian regime headed by Husni Mubarak still adheres. 


The statement made it clear that this step is the culmination of 
earlier steps of cooperation between the two regimes for more 
than 2 years. With this Iraqi action, which serves the Camp 
David policy, the secretariat draws attention to the gravity 
ofdisregard for unanimous Arab resolutions, and it demands that 
the Arab League secretary general take the measures [words 
indistinct] the league in confronting the violation of its res- 
olutions. 


In its statement the secretariat called on the Arab masses to 
denounce the stance of the Iraqi regime, which is virtually 
declaring its adherence to Camp David. 


USSR Said Pressuring Libya To Sign Treaty 
PM081549 London Al-Majallah in Arabic 9-15 Jul 83 
pi2 


[From Al-Majallah correspondents’ dispatches] 


[Text] According to informed Arab diplomatic sources, the Soviet 
Union is currently putting “various pressures” on Libya to 
persuade it to sign a friendship and cooperation treaty with the 
Soviet Union. An understanding was reached “in principle” 
regarding this step during ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud's recent visit to 
Moscow. 


The sources say that a ranking Libyan official has told a number 
of Arab circles recently that his country does not wish to sign 
such a treaty and is trying to avoid such a move, but the Soviets 
are insisting. 


Libyan Reportage on Developments in Chad 


Habre Forces Flee Abeche 
LD090903 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0805 GMT 9 Jul 83 


[Text] Ndjamena, 9 Jul (JANA) — According to urgent dis- 
patches from Ndjamena, the forces of rebel Habre abandoned 
their positions and fled from the town of Abeche yesterday 
afternoon in the face of the advance on the town by the national 
forces. Observers expect the town to fall in the hands of these 
forces, led by Goukouni Oueddei, which now control over one 
third of the country. 


Abeche Falls to Gowkouni Forces 
LD091613 Tripoli JANA in English 1515 GMT 9 Jul 83 


[Text] Paris, Ramadan 29, July 9, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — Adoum Yacoub, the provisional Government of 
National Unity’s delegate announced here today that the city of 
Abeche. 700 km away from the Chadian capital has fallen into 
the hands of Goukouni's forces. 


In a telephone conversation with the French news agency, the 
delegate also said that despite fierce resistance by Habre’s forces, 
the town is completely under control today. 


The delegate also announced the fall of Biltine 80 km north of 
Abeche.

[Text] Bardai, Shawwal |, July 11, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — Radio Bardai, voice of the Chadian National 
Unity Government headed by Goukouni Oueddei and monitored 
in Lome, has refuted the allegations of Ndjamena’s renegade 
clique that it had recaptured the city of Abeche. 


The radio said that such allegations are groundless and circulated 
by the renegade clique to cover up defeats inflicted upon them 
by the National Liberation Army forces. 


it added that the city of Abeche is still the control of the National 


Liberation Army forces which have been repeatedly striking at 
renegade Habre's clique.
