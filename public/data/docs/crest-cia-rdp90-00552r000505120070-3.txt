Summary (AI): A 1983 New York Post column by Rowland Evans and Robert Novak reports that a leak exposed U.S. AWACS surveillance of a Libyan plot against Sudan, and that the Reagan administration quietly helped Italian investigators probing a possible Libyan link to the attempted assassination of Pope John Paul II. It does not concern places in Tripoli.
• The article is dated 26 February 1983 and appeared in the New York Post.
• It says a leak revealed U.S. AWACS radar planes were sent to monitor a Libyan plot against neighboring Sudan, and that the flights were passed off publicly as 'not an unusual' event.
• It says the leak put U.S. agents at risk and that Khadafy might retaliate by finding and killing them.
• It says the Reagan administration offered discreet help to Italian police and intelligence investigating the attempted assassination of Pope John Paul II, with a suspected Libyan link.
