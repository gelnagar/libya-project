Nothing specifically located in Tripoli sites beyond the general exercise area.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya would intercept any Israeli plane flying over the Mediterranean, arguing this was in accordance with international law and reciprocity.
• Qadhdhafi called President Reagan 'the new Hitler' and said the Berlin-Rome Axis was now the Washington-Tel Aviv axis.
• The Libyan statement cited the Security Council's inability to act because of the US veto.
• Libyan statement listed Dir Yasin, Kafr Qasim, Bahr al-Bagar School, Sabra and Shatila as massacres by Israelis.
• Exercises were announced as running from 8 February to 16 February 1986 with live ammunition.
• 1986-02-07: Qadhdhafi news conference for foreign journalists on Libya's decision to intercept Israeli planes over the Mediterranean within range of Libyan air bases; he warned of a fight with the US in its homes if attacked.
• 1986-02-08: Foreign Liaison Bureau statement said the SPLAJ would continue intercepting Israeli planes and resist anyone obstructing it, citing US naval maneuvers opposite Libyan shores.
• 1986-02-08: Libyan naval, air and air defense forces began live-ammunition exercises in the Tripoli air traffic control region and economic zone, running until 16 February 1986.
