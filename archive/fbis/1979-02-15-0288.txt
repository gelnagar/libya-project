{Excerpts ] Inevitably, history will record Sunday, 11 February 1979 with letters of gold 
in the annals of the revolutions of the peoples against oppression, injustice, tyranny, 
totalitarianism, despotism, exploitation, reaction and imperialism. Inevitably, history 
will record for the valiant Iranian Muslim masses their great steadfastness, strength 

of faith, grandness of sacrifice and popular revolutionary wealth and their great 

love of sacrificing most bravely so that their will may triumph and their voice may 

rise freely and proudly above all other voices, 


Yesterday and this morning, the last of the staunchest institutions on which the shah 
founded his throne collapsed--the army, which had been trained and equipped by the shah's 
masters, the Americans, and his Zionist allies, 


As the heroic Iranian masses were magnanimous in their revo'’ution, so they have been 
great in affirming their principles and exercising full restraint in dealing with their 
enemies of yesterday,

Despite what the masses have suffered because of U.S. interference, they did not kill 
members of the U.S. Embassy staff, nor did they kill members of the staff of the agent 
As-Sadat embassy--As-Sadat who provoked the Iranian masses by playing the imperial 
anthem for the shah, the outcast of the masses and the head of corruptim, following 
the collapse of his throne once and for all, 


The whole of Iran is today celebrating the day of victory. They have the right to 
celebyate this final and crushing victory. With hearts filled with love, faith, 
solidarity and admiration we congratulate them on their day of victory. We say to 
them: Welcome, great revolutimary masses, into the process of revolutions on the 
road of the popular revolution, welcome to you in the age of the peoples and masses, 
a thousand greetings from the citadel of the masses, the citadel of the freemen, the 
citadel of the Great 1 September Revolution. 


You have triumphed. The eyes of all oppressed peoples are focused on you and will 
inevitably follow on the same road, the road of the popular revolution, the road of 
freedom, glory and victory, now that they have come to realize that he who seeks death 
receives life, and that the mightiest power, the biggest despots, the largest armies 
and the most sophisticated equipment can be defeated by the masses. 


In the recent past, the masses and the march of the popular revolutions swept away the 
throne of Haile Selassie in Ethiopia. In the near past the revolutionary popular tide 
swept out the seats of agentry and reaction in Vietnam, Camdodia and Laos. Today, 

the victory of the popular revolution in Iran comes to prove once more that arms, 
military hardware, aircraft, warships, missiles and tanks are too weak to suppress the 
outcry and wrath of the peoples, 


The United States, Westerm imperialism and Zionism have provided the outcast shah-- 

and before him the old Haile Selassie regime--with the most sophisticated and destructive 
weapons. Imperialism and Zionism provided the shah and before him Haile Selassie, Lon 
Nol, Kao Ky and others with tens of thousands of regular troops and military experts 
disguised as civilians. Imperialism mobilized for these regimes all the experience it 
possessed in the field of terrorism and annihilation. The CIA itself along with 

Zionist intelligence organized, armed and trained the intelligence systems of these 
regimes, such as the Pahlevi intelligence system, SAVAK. 


But all this might and this support, and all these forces stood wmable [words indistinct] 
the angry masses which defeated the regimes of treason, the might of the generals and 
the experience of spies and terrorists. When the masses determined to triumph or die, 
these regimes and their military and police institutions collapsed like autumn leaves, 
and were crushed by the feet of the masses with their sweeping revolutionary storm. 


There remain two points which we have to make. The first concerns a report by the 

New York TIMES that U.S. Ambassador to the United Nations Andrew Young met on Saturday 
with two representatives of Khomeyni in an attempt to win over the leader of the Iranian 
popular revolution. Earlier, the news agencies reported similar contacts, 


The thited States, which had placed all its weight in the service of consolidating the 
shah's throne over the skulls of the martyrs when it interfered in August 1953 and brought 
him back from exile in Rome after he was expelled by the masses, today refuses even to 
receive its agent. If it indicates anything, this stand indicates that the imperialist 
powers see their agents as mere (?pawns), and they only view international changes and 
political moves and their consequences in the world through their private interests--that 
is, the private interests of these powers. They always support the triumphant, whether 
he is in the right or the wrong.

As we make this remark, we cannot but note the wide difference between this attitude and 
our attitude here in the land of the Great 1 September Revolutim. From the very first 
moment, our masses rose to declare their full support for the Iranian masses. Over a 
period of 10 years, our masses have always been quick to stand by the causes of right, 
Justice and freedom and by the revolutionary masses against oppressim, suppression, 
exploitatio and subservience. All this is out of our deep belief in principles and 
our firm commitment to the causes of the masses, whether they triumph or not, 


The second point we wish to raise deals with the moves the United States is carrying 

out in the Arab area, and the American statements following the triumph of the Iranian 
people's revolution. U.S. Secretary of Defense Harold Brown is visiting the region 

and inspecting the American forces in some bases, and is declaring Washington's readiness 
to increase the supply of arms, Then too, Henry Jackson, member of the U.S. Senate, 
called last night for the speeding up of the establishmen* of a defense pact between the 
United States and the Zionist enemy, Egypt and Saudi Arabia, and to support them with 
all the arms and money they need. 


Then too, the queen of Britain intentionally wears decent clothes to carry out a tour 
of the Arabian Peninsula. Then too, the foreign minister of defeated As-Sadat calls 
almost daily for the completion of the plan of submission to the enemy. 


All this takes place while the blood of the Iranian martyrs has not dried yet and the 
cries of the triumphant Iranian masses soar into the skies. All this takes place while 
the outcast shah still lives in an Arab territory, in a state of exhaustion, suffering 
from a breakdow, seeing nightmares and cast away by even his American and Zionist allies. 


Is it not time for those midget rulers and their imperialist mentors to realize that 
all these abortive attempts in the occupied Arab homeland, against the nation in whose 
lands sprang the Great 1 September Revolution, the 23 July revolution, the Algerian and 
other revolutions and in whose lands rose heroes who defeated the invadors and the 
greedy, and who time and time again destroyed the citadels of agentry and reaction--is 
it not time for them to realize that all the attempts at disgraceful grouping and the 
setting up of alliances will be no more than a small particle in the face of the masses! 
storm and that the revolutionary deluge is inevitably coming, as brother Colonel al-
