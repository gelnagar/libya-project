JANA: AL-QADHDHAFI TALKS WITH ARAB BANKS! GOVERNORS 
LD271904 Tripoli JANA in English 1806 GMT 27 Sep 79 LD 


[Text] Benghazi, September 27, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu'ammar aleQadhdhafi, the 
leader of the Great First of September Revolution, held a meeting with members of the Governors 
Council of the Arab central banks, during which they discussed the means of using the Arab 
funds for the benefit of the Arab cause and a weapon against the countries hostile to the 

Arab destiny causes, 


Colonel Al-Qadhdhafi affirmed the necessity of implementing the Baghdad summit resolutions 
which stipulate the economic sanction measures against the Egyptian regime. 


The meeting was also attended by Dr. Hassan Zalzalah, the assistant-secretary of the economic 
affirs of the Arab League. 


AMBASSADOR TO BAHRAIN INTERVIEWED ON ARAB ISSUES 
JN271211 Manama GULF NEWS AGENCY in Arabic 0820 GMT 27 Sep 79 JN 


{Text] Manama, 27 September--Libyan Ambassador to Bahrain Ramadan al-Rubi has stated that 
the protection of the Gulf area is an Arab responsibility. He said: We will not accept any 
foreign intervention in our country, in fraternal countries, the Gulf area or in areas where 
oil sources are located. The responsibility of defending the area should be shouldered by 
our people and not by foreign countries. We will accept no one’s tutelage. 


In an interview with the Bahraini newspaper AKHBAR AL-KHALIJ, the Libyan ambassador stressed 
that the responsibility of defending oil sources is the responsibility of the Arab countries 
and not of any other party.

On the Lebanese problem, the Libyan ambassador said his country has called for an Arab 
summit conference in order to save Lebanon from the problem it faces and to enable the 
Arabs to confront Israel's expansionist designs, represented in the occupation of the water 
sources. This will enable the Zionist entity to expand the areas suitable for agriculture, 
thus facilitating the absorption of additional immigrants. 


On the issue of Lebanese Shitite leader Musa as-Sadr, the Libyan ambassador said that Imam 
Musa as-Sadr visited Libya and then left. His country has submitted evidence proving ;jthat he 
left, the ambassador added. ) 


The Libyan ambassador reiterated his country’s support for the POLISARIO, He said: We support 
the principle of the freedom of mankind in this world. Freedom was the first slogan we raised. 
We support the freedom of the individual, wherever he may be. He added that his country 

was the first Arab country to support the Westerm Saharan people during the Spanish rule and 
to offer them the necessary assistance at the time, 


The Libyan ambassador described his country's relations with the United States as relations 
between equals. He said: Our policy is clear. It seeks liberation and a return to the home- 
land. The United States wants us to go along with the situation and not tovoppose the 

current proposed solutions or its policies on the subject. To achieve this, the United States 
resorts to pressure and intimidation. The Libyam ambassador pointed to the U.S. refusal to 
sell Libya commercial materials. He said: This U.S. position constitues the beginning of 
pressure on the Libyan people, who reject the proposed U.S. solutions in the area. 


The Libyan ambassador said that the American people are a friendly people, noting that an 
American delegation participated in the 1 September Revolution anniversary. 


On Libyan-Bahraini relations, the Libyan ambassadior noted that they are strong and constantly 
developing.
