LIBYA

U.S. PREPARING 'MILITARY AGGRESSION! AGAINST ARAB WORLD 
LD301940 Tripoli JANA in English 1820 GMT 30 Apr 80 
[Text] New York, April 30, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The U.S.A. is planning large scale 
manoeuvres to be conducted by the recently formed task force. In an official announcement 
a spokesman for the American Defense Department said that the manoeuvres would take place 
between the 3rd of June and the 3rd of July in a desert area near one of the American 
bases in Nevada. 
It was further reported that the manoeuvres are to take place in an area similar to the 
Middle East. This is proof enough that America is rehearsing this force for military 
aggression against the Arab world and [passage indistinct].
