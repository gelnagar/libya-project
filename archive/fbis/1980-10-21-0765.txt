LIBYA

IRANIAN PRIME MINISTER RAJA'I ARRIVES, MEETS JALLUD 
LD201254 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 20 Oct 80 
[Text] Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud today met with Brother Mohammad ‘Ali Raja'‘i, 
the prime minister of the Islamic Republic of Iran, who arrived in Tripoli at noon today 
from Algeria en route to Iran. 
AL-QADHDHAFI REPORTEDLY TO VISIT USSR, SOLIDIFY TIES 
NC181250 Paris AFP in English 1228 GMT 18 Oct 80 
[Text] Paris, 18 Oct (AFP)--Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi will pay a visit to Moscow 
soon which will mark a "qualitative change" in Libyan-Soviet relations, the Arab-language 
weekly AL-MUSTAQBAL reported here today. 
Quoting well-informed sources in the Syrian capital, Damascus, it said the visit could 
bring the signature of a friendship treaty similar to that signed recently between Syria 
and the Soviet Union, during President Hafiz al-Asad's Moscow visit. 
AL-MUSTAQBAL said Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's proposed visit to Damascus for further dis- 
cussions on the planned unification of Syria and Libya had meanwhile been put off by 
mutual agreement, until after he returns from Moscow. Libyan sources in Paris refused 
to confirm or deny the weekly's report.
