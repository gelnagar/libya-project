Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery analysis note on Libyan military activity in Chad (Ounianga Kebir, Faya Largeau, Salal) and heavy-lift transport activity at Tajura Barracks in Libya. Little of it is about Tripoli itself beyond the Tajura vehicle storage area.
• Six of ten 130mm M-46 field guns that arrived at Ounianga Kebir are deployed north of the airfield.
• Aircraft at Ounianga Kebir include six SF.260 light-strike aircraft and two Hip helicopters.
• Faya Largeau area: one C-130 medium transport, two Hip and three Hind helicopters present.
• At Tajura, at least 70 heavy-lift transporters were in convoy on main roads at the installation entrance.
• 1983-08: Libyan forces continue improving defenses; M-46 130mm field guns deployed north of the old airfield.
• 1983-08: Force consolidation and logistics activity; first identification of probable antiaircraft guns in the area.
• 1983-08: High level of heavy-lift transport activity continues at the vehicle storage area.
