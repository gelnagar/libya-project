Speech at Second Session 
LD242002 Tripoli JANA in English 1812 GMT 24 Sep 79 LD 


{Text] Benghazi, 24 Sep ‘JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The leader of the Great First of 
September Revolution, Colouel Muammar al-Qadhdhafi, declared this morning during the 
revolutionary committee's second forum that it is the revolutionary forces which convince 
the majority and lead them to the sites where they can serve the interests of the people 
best. He said all people wish to gain access to freedom but do not have the means. 
However, the organized revolutionary forces are capable of leading the masses toward 
democracy. 


He also stressed that the revolutimary committees are a tool in the era of the masses 
and the new body organizing the revolutionary forces. Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed 

out that the tasks of the revolutimary committees differ from those of previous poli- 
tical powers which do not seek the authority sought, for example, by the parties. He 
said that was the nature of the revolutimary committees before the revolution and that 
is the way it still is now. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added: "The mly aim of the revolutionary committees is to help 
the masses seize power and urge them to practise it. Therefore it is important to have 
a revolutionary committee in every people's congress for such committees are the con- 
gresses' dynamic power." He added: "Without revolutionary committees the people's 
congresses are unreliable for they lack the awareness, the revolutionary element needed 
to urge and guide. In other words they become motionless congresses." The leader of 
the revolution called upon each people's congress without a revolutionary committee 

to join another congress which already has one. 


He referred to the revolutionary committees within the armed forces and said that such 
committees had already existed. They were the force which led the revolution and 
now they were reorganized. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi spoke about the new transformation plan which will start in 1980 
when the first five-year plan is due to be completed. He said the new plan will include 
heavier burdens and more responsibilities than the first, because it aims at the 
achievement of new and dangerous transformations which will affect every individual. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi warmed that the people's congresses might not assimilate or appre- 
ciate the importance of this plan except through the revolutimary committees which 

are capable of convincing the masses about the objectives of the transformation and the 
abolition of backwardness. Colomel al-Qadhdhafi stressed that the revolutioary trans- 
formation will continue at popular level by the masses themselves. For it the great 
transformation takes place through a dictatorship it would have many drawbacks, the most 
dangerous of which is that the masses would lose confidence in themselves and submit 

to any domination. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi insisted that the transformatio programme would be carried out 
whatever the price, by the masses themselves instead of a revolutionary command council.

OFFICIALS, AFRICAN ENVOYS DISCUSS CENTRAL AFRICA

[Text] Tripoli, 24 Sep (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The Foreign Secretariat has conducted 
contacts with Islamic and African states concerning the latest developments in Central 
Africa, 


This afternoon the ambassadors of the African cowtries to the Socialist People’s Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah were called for a meeting with the assistant under secretary for 
political affairs at the Foreign Secretariat who explained to them the Jamahiriyah's 
point of view concerning latest and [as received] developments, their dimensions and 
their real causes. He also exchanged views with the ambassadors and asked them to in- 
form their governments about the views exchanged in this meeting. It was stressed 

to the ambassadors that the real danger stemmed from the fact that a non-African state 
had led a direct military invasio against an African cowmtry to change the system and 
impose another, loyal to it. This, he said, contradicts all the international charters 
including that of the UN and of the Organizatim of African thity. 


The assistant under secretary asked: "If the OAU's Charter prohibits the intervention 
of one member state in the internal affairs of another member state, how could the 
same charter allow a non-African country to launch a direct military invasion of an 
African cowmtry and to subvert the existing system in that comtry?" 


In the meeting the: under secretary warmed that any other African or non-African cowmtry 
could be the target of a similar intervention in the future, particularly if a firm 
stand is not adopted to stop direct interventions. 


In the meeting it was affirmed that the time was ripe for one decisive African stand 
against direct and indirect imperialist interventions aimed at placing the African 
Continent under imperialist domination and to reb its resources and expertise. 


The ambassadors of Islamic cowmtries accredited to the Jamahiriyah were invited this 
afternoon to a meeting with assistant under secretariat [as received] who informed

DEMONSTRATORS PROTEST FRENCH ACTIONS IN BANGUI 
LD250934 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 25 Sep 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli--Tight security yesterday was placed on the residence of the French 
ambassador to the Hamahiriyah and on the French Embassy in Tripoli in anticipation of any 
popular reactions to the hostile measures taken by the French invading forces in Bangui 
against Libyan citizens there. 


There have been some marches in Tripoli in denunciation of the blatant French invasion of 
Central Africa and in protest against the provocations by the French invading forces in 
Bangui against Libyans in the capital of Central Africa, a matter which has aroused a 
wave of indignation in popular circles, 


Reports from Bangui mentioned that the French invading forces had detained 37 Libyan citi- 
zens among the merchants there. 


JANA: CAPTURED PLANE IN BANGUI CHARTERED, CARRYING FRUIT 
LD241538 Tripoli JANA in English 1402 GMT 24 Sep 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, 24 Sep, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The French authorities allege that the 
French force present in Bangui, Central Africa, has captured a Libyan plane carrying 
several tons of ammunition, thus trying to distract the attention away from their open 
intervention in the internal affairs of the Republic of Central Africa. 


An official source here described these allegations as untrue. The source added that the 
said plane captured by the French troops is not a Libyan airliner nor is it from the 
Libyan Arab air force but it is a chartered plane for carrying fruits, a sort of cold 
store.
