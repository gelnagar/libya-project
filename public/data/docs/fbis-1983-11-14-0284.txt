‘Arafat Agrees to Move 
LDI10045 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
OOISGMT II Nov 83 


[Text] JANA has learned that Yasir ‘Arafat has responded to the 
call addressed to him by the brother leader and has agreed to 
move to the Jamahiriyah in order to spare Palestinian blood and 
end the bloody conflict that is now taking place among brothers 
and comrades in arms in the Palestinian revolution. Consulta- 
tions are now underway to organize and secure Yasir ‘Arafat's 
arrival in the Jamahiriyah. The brother leader asked Yasir 
‘Arafat to come to the Jamahiriyah and reside here instead of 
taking refuge elsewhere, with the Jamahiriyah guaranteeing his 
safety ahd protection and his right to defend himself with regard 
to Arab or Palestinian questioning. This is part of the Jama- 
hiriyah's efforts to end the fighting among Palestinian brothers 
and to bring an end to the bloody conflict among brothers and 
comrades in arms so that all guns can be pointed at the enemy
