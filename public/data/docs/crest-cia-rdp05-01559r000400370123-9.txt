Summary (AI): An FBIS London wire relays a Libyan (JANA) report from Tripoli that Qadhafi pledged to implement the recommendations of an Arab Popular Conference supporting the Palestinian revolution. Lt-Col Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir addressed marchers at the RCC premises after the conference closed. It is foreign-policy and regime news with no specific Tripoli site detail beyond the RCC building.
• Qadhafi reportedly said the popular organisations in Libya possess the authority to decide and that Libya is committed to implementing the conference recommendations.
• Jabir called on Arab popular organisations to struggle on a pan-Arab basis rather than a tactical one.
• The report is a JANA English broadcast of 0910 GMT, 10 October 1976, relayed by FBIS London.
• 1976-10-09: Arab Popular Conference for the Support and Protection of the Palestinian Revolution closes; participants march to RCC premises where Qadhafi's commitment to implement its decisions is announced.
