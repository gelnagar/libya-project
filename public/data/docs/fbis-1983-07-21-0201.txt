[Text] The brother leader of the revolution received at noon 
yesterday Brother Mohamed Mzali, Tunisian prime minister. 
The meeting was attended by Jadallah ‘Aziz at-Talhi, chairman 
of the General People’s Committee. 


A joint communique of the joint meeting between the General 
People’s Committee and the Tunisian Cabinet was signed in 
Tripoli today. The communique was signed by Jadallah ‘Aziz 
at-Talhi, chairman of the General People’s Committee, and on 
behalf of the Tunisian side by Brother Mohamed Mzali, Tunisian 
prime minister. 


Also today at noon in Tripoli the minutes of joint cooperation 
between the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the 
fraternal Republic of Tunisia were signed by Brother Musa Abu 
Furaywah, secretary of economy and light industries, and on 
behalf of the Tunisian side by Brother Abdelaziz Lasram, min- 
ister of national economy. 


In Tripoli today at noon a program for culture between the 
Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the fraternal 
Republic of Tunisia was signed. The program was signed by 
Brother ‘Abd al-*Ati al-‘Ubaydi, secretary of foreign liaison, and 
on behalf of the Tunisian side by Brother Bechir Ben Slama, 
minister of culture. 


A meeting was held this morning in Tripoli between Jadallah 
‘Aziz at-Talhi, chairman of the General People’s Committee, and 
Brother Mohamed Mzali, Tunisian prime minister. During this 
meeting they reviewed the achievements of the preparatory 
committee, and assessed the consultations that took place last 
night between brother secretaries of the General People’s Com- 
mittee and Tunisian ministers, which concentrated on the 
achievement of economic integration between the two countries 
in light of joint meeting between the General People’s Committee 
in the Jamahiriyah and the Tunisian Cabinet. During this meet- 
ing they also reviewed the agreements that have been imple- 
mented and the joint companies which have been established 
between the two countries, and a plan has been drawn to vitalize 
the work of these companies within the framework of economic 
integration between the two countries.

[Text] Tripoli, 20 July (JANA) — A joint communique on the 
first meeting of the joint supreme committee of the General 
People’s Committee and the Tunisian Council of Ministers in the 
Socialist Peoples Libyan Jamahiriyah was issued today. Here 
follows its text 


Within the context of the historic ties between the fraternal 
peoples in the Socalist Peoples Libyan Jamahiriyah and the 
Tunisian Republic; in consolidation of the fraternal relations 
between the two fraternal countries; in implementing the 
agreement signed between them during the visit by Brother 
Colonel Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great | Septem- 
ber Revolution, to the Tunisian Republic from 23-27 February 
1982; in response to the generous invitation extended to him by 
his brother the great struggler His Excellency President Habib 
Bourguiba, president of the Tunisian Republic, the joint supreme 
committee of the General People’s Committee in the Socialist 
People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the Council of Ministers 
of the Tunisian Republic met in Tripoli from 19-20 July 1983. 


The meetings of the joint supreme committee were opened by 
Brother Col Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great | 
September Revolution. After the joint supreme committee had 
recorded its satisfaction with the achievements of the preparatory 
committee in its first and second session in Tunis and Tripoli, and 
with those of the technical committees that studied joint actions 
in the economic, cultural and social fields and ways to develop 
them; after expressing the firm will to deepen these relations, to 
strengthen them and raise them to the level of the aspirations for 
economic integration between the two countries within the 
framework of the Great Arab Maghreb of the two fraternal 
peoples, and in order to attain comprehensive Arab unity, 
agreement was reached on the following: 


First, in the field of planning: 


Both sides confirm the importance of the continued study of the 
two countries’ development plans and of coordination between 
them with the objective of achieving economic integration in 
various fields. To achieve this, both sides will exchange expertise, 
studies, documents and economic information and will submit 
any projects or economic policies capable of contributing to the 
achievement of economic integration. In the future both sides will 
study ways to coordinate the start of their plans and their 
duration. 


Second, in the field of trade: 


The joint supreme committee has noted its satisfaction with the 
growth of trade between the two countries, and in an endeavor 
to increase their development in a balanced way and in accor- 
dance with the minutes of the meeting signed on 20 January 1983 
and 29 April 1983, it has decided the following: 


1. To give priority to imports and exports of the two countries’ 
produce. 


2. To exchange competent trade delegations, information, and 
reports on production and exports possibilities in a manner that 
will increase trade exchanges between the two countries. 


3. To work for the total exemption of all national products in 
other of the two countries from customs fees. 


4. To study the establishment of a unified customs barrier with 
regard to the flow of national goods without customs restrictions

and to treat such goods as national commodities in both countries; 
also to lay down a unified external customs tariff. 


5. To unite the customs posts at the common borders. 


Third, in the field of tourism: 


A. To encourage and develop tourism between the two countries, 
and to support cooperation in the field of tourism, including 
training and the employment of manpower. 


B. To open tourist bureaus and hotel-management schools in the 
Jamahiriyah, and exploit the potential for tourism in the two 
countries. 


C. To grant special facilities to the citizens of the two countries 
on their tourist visits to enable them to learn of the various signs 
of progress achieved in both countries. 


Fourth, the industrial field: 

The two sides stress the importance of coordination and indus- 
trial cooperation to serve as a stimulus for economic integration 
between the two countries, and of ratifying the two minutes of 
the meetings dated January 1983 and 29 April 1983; the two 
sides have agreed to establish cooperation in the following fields: 


1. The chemical, petrochemical, soil, fertilizers, truck and tractor 
industries. 


2. The industry of high and low voltage electric cells; the servicing 
and the maintainance of electric transformers and motors. 


3. The metallurgical engineering works. 

4. The food industry. 

5. The ready-made clothes a: J yarn industry. 

6. The shoe and leather industries. 

7. Sea fishing equipment. 

8. All types of paper. 

9. To make use of the potentialities and to utilize industrial 
energies in the factories of the two countries to manufacture the 
products of the other's country. 

10. The medical industries and equipment. 

It authorizes the competent quarters in the two countries to 
complete the economic and technical studies of the projects 
planned to be implemented. 

Five, in the field of joint investment: 

A. Both sides confirm the importance of consolidating and devel- 
Oping existing investments in order to overcome all difficulties 


obstructing their way with the aim of giving an impetus to the 
development of cooperation in this area.
