LIBYA

New Maltese Premier Vows To Maintain Relations 
LD131127 Tripoli JANA in English 1050 GMT 13 May 87 


[Text] Valletta, Malta [May] 13, Jamahiriyah News Agency — 
The new prime minister of Malta, Mr. Eddie Fenech [Adamil, 
has stressed that his government will keep up the distinctive 
relations between Malta and Great al-Jamahiriyah. 


He points out that his government will abide by the cooperation 
and friendship treaty between the two countries. 


In his first comments after being sworn in, Mr. Fenech has 
stressed that his government will keep up the cooperation treaty 
with the Soviet Union.
