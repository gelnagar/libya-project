LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI INTERVIEWED ON ATOM BOMB, IRAQ-IRAN WAR 
LD241716 Tripoli JANA in English 1400 GMT 24 Oct 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Oct (JANA)--The Italian magazine EPOCA today carried the text of the 
interview which was conducted by the journalist Franceso Briggiri with the brother leader 
in which he spoke about a number of international and national issues. 


Replying to a question on the merger with the sister country Syria, he said, "This merger 
was inevitable for the Arab masses; all the enemies' attempts to hinder such unionist steps 
were useless. We do not care about such attempts because they only serve the enemies ' 
interests." 


On the question concerning the big powers' military presence in the Mediterranean, Colonel 
Al-Qadhdhafi said that the Mediterranean Sea should remain an area of peace and free from 
all foreign fleets. He said that if another Arab-(Israeli) war breaks out, a third world 
war will become very possible. He added if the Arabs tried to liberate Palestine from 
(Israeli) occupation and the United States intervened to stop them from doing so, then a 
third world war would be inevitable. 


He went on to say that it is the right of the Arabs to liberate Palestine, therefore a 
third world war could not be avoided unless the United States adopts a different policy 
and the Palestinian rights are restored. 


"We are very much concerned about the possibility of a third world war, but we will try 
our utmost to maintain peace in the world. We do not have the power to change the U.S. 
policy or to put an end to (Israeli) occupation in Palestine peacefully. Therefore we 

hold America and (Israel) responsible for any world conflict." 


In his answer to the question on the third universal theory and its implementation, Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi said that the dicta of the Green Book are being applied in the Jamahiriyah. 
Power, he said, is now in the hands of the people and it is made practical through the 
people's congresses, where resolutions are being approved and carried out by people's com- 
mittees. The regular army, he said, will be replaced by the armed people. Houses now 
belong to their occupiers, and factory workers have become partners and not wage workers. 
Public markets were set up and exploitation is abolished. 


He added that the whole world will soon become like the Jamahiriyah. He continued, "Fol- 
lowing the establishment of the people's power, my role now is only to incite the masses 
to consolidate our people's power and to guide the masses and lead them along the right 
track." 


On the women's issue, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that women, who constitute half of the 
world population, are being subjected to oppression. In the East, women are treated badly 
and in the West women have to strip themselves from their femininity to become like men 

in order to be able to earn their living. This also has to be abolished. 


The world, he said, should realize the nature of the feminist movement and help them to 
gain their rights. Women should not be treated like ornaments. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's answer to the question of the Fiat workers strike was to say what 
is going on in Fiat plants fully contradicts what is stated in the Greek Book. "We stand 
on the side of the workers who are threatened to be laid off. We totally disagree with 
the method used by the management in dealing with the situation despite Libya's position 
in the company as a partner." The Fiat workers, he said, should occupy all plants until 
a positive solution is reached and all other problems are solved.

On the Jamahiriyah's endeavour to possess an atomic bomb, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said his 
country does not possess such a bomb nor is it about to do so. He then asked, why can't 

the world just concern itself with the bombs that already exist for a while, instead of 
occupying itself with whether the Jamahiriyah possesses an atomic bomb. He wondered why 
world public opinion should be deceived and why all the fuss should be centered around 

an atomic bomb alleged to be in the Jamahiriyah's possession, instead of worrying about 

the bombs that the United States, Britain, Russia, France, China and India actually possess. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that Western propaganda aims at halting Third World efforts 
to acquire technology and to use nuclear power for peaceful purposes. He expressed con- 
cern over the continuing manufacture of atomic bombs by a few countries, which, he said, 
pose a threat to humanity. The Third World should get rid of these bombs that pollute 

the earth, he said. 


When asked about the existence of military training camps in Libya for terrorists, Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi said that Western allegations on this matter are groundless and that anyone 
knowing of such camps should come forward. He added: How can Libya offer military training 
when Libyans themselves are sent to get their military training in the countries supplying 
Libya with arms, 


On the continental shelf between Libya and Malta, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the issue has 
been referred to the international court. He added that oil search in the disputed region 
should (?be stopped) until a decision has been taken by the court. 


He pointed out that the Jamahiriyah is one of the first countries to guarantee Malta's 
neutrality and not Italy, for Italy being one of NATO's members cannot possibly be neutral, 
whereas Libya can, being a member state of the non-aligned movement. Hence, Libya can 
actually guarantee Malta's neutrality. 


On the Iraqi-Iranian conflict, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that the current ’':loody war be- 
tween Moslem brothers is a U.S.-(Israeli) design. "From the very beginning we have sent 
letters to both sides to bring this war to a halt. We will try again in the next Arab 
foreign ministers conference." 


On the question of the Jamahiriyah's demands for a compensation from Italy and Germany, 

for using the Libyan territories during the Second World War, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said 
that these countries should compensate the Libyan Arab people, and that an agreement should 
be reached with these countries because this is the right of our people. 


JANA SCORES SAUDI MEDIA REACTION TO AL-QADHDHAFI SERMON 
LD242048 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 24 Oct 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Oct (JANA)--The media of the Saudi ruling dynasty continue to cast 

a blind eye on the facts raised by the commander of the revolution in his sermon on the 
occasion of the blessed 'Id al-Adha feast and to preoccupy Saudi citizens with matters 

that are remote from reality. 


The media of the ruling dynasty today attacked the sermon of the commander of the revolu- 
tion but said nothing about the occupation by the American aircraft over the skies of 
Mecca and the holy places. Nor did they say anything about the brother commander's call 
on Muslims to declare a jihad against Zionism and the American forces which march to 
occupy the Arab homeland and desecrate the holy places. Instead, these media praised 

the adherence to Islam by the affluent Saudi royal dynasty and the repressive regime 
which has been amassing wealth in American coffers while leaving the people in abject 
poverty.

DEFENSE MINISTRY DENIES SUPPLYING PARTS TO IRAN 
TA261603 Tel Aviv IDF Radio in Hebrew 1510 GMT 26 Oct 80 


[Text] In reply to a question from our reporter, the Defense Ministry spokesman today 
denied a report published in the United States by columnists Evans and Novak. According 
to the report, Israel is supplying Iran with spare parts for Phantom aircraft, which have 
been shipped via Holland for more than 2 weeks now, That is what the U.S. report claimed. 
The reaction of the Defense Ministry spokesman: The report is totally unfounded. 


IRAQ REPORTEDLY SEEKING PARTS, AMMUNITION FROM EGYPT 
TA261931 Jerusale:in Nomestic Television Service in Hebrew 1900 GMT 26 Oct 80 


[Text] Iraq is holding secret contacts with Egypt for the supply of spare parts and ammuni- 
tion for the Soviet equipment in its possession. This has been learned by our correspondent 
in Egypt, Ehud Ya'ari. According to the reports, an Iraqi delegation secretly arrived in 
Egypt for this purpose. Sources inEgypt deny this report. Our correspondent in Cairo 

has also learned that an Egyptian military delegation visited the Saudi airbase at Tabuk, 
near Elat. The Egyptian interior minister, Nabawi Isma'il, returned to Egypt from a pil- 
grimage to Saudi Arabia; and the minister of state for foreign affairs, Butrus Ghali, told 
our correspondent in Cairo that contacts are underway between the two countries aimed at 
improving relations. 


PRESIDENT NAVON LEAVES FOR STATE VISIT TO EGYPT 


TA260953 [Editorial Report] Jerusalem Domestic Service and Tel Aviv IDF Radio in Hebrew 

at 0832 GMT on 26 October begin live coverage of the state farewell ceremony for President 
Yi.zhaq Navon at Ben-Gurion Airport prior to his departure for Egypt. The president's 
entourage arrives at 0836 GMT and is greeted by a trumpet fanfare. The president and his 
wife proceed to take their leave of a long line of Israeli officials and other public figures 
headed by Prime Minister Menahem Begin. At 0845 GMT President Navon makes a statement. 

After greeting those present, he says: 


"On my behalf and on behalf of my wife, Ofira, and the people accompanyiing us I want to 
express our deep gratitude for your coming here to bid us farewell upon our departure for 
Egypt in response to the kind and friendly invitation of President Muhammad Anwar as-Sadat. 
This is the first time I am leaving the boundaries of the country since I was elected 
president and I am glad that this departure is to our large neighbor, the land of the 

Nile. I am carrying with me words of peace and truth to the Egyptian people, words about 
our firm adherence to a way of friendship, fraternity and cooperation as well as words about 
[word indistinct] from the peace process and our opinion regarding the development of the 
relations stemming from it. I am glad that, together with my wife, we are given the oppor- 
tunity to meet Egyptian personalities from various social circles, first and foremost the 
Egyptian president and his wife, administration officials, members of parliament, writers 
and intellectuals, editors of newspapers and magazines, women active in various spheres, 
workers and farmers. I am not going there to negotiate or to bargain. I am not over- 
estimating my expectations from the visit. However, if Egypt's attitude toward us depends 
on the understanding of our motives, feelings and needs, perhaps this visit will bear some 
fruit. Time will tell. Perhaps, sometime in the future the words of the prophet Isaiah-- 
"Blessed be Egypt my people, and Assyria the work of my hands, and Israel my heritage"-- 
will come true. 


President Navon then proceeds to inspect the guard of honor. The national anthem is played 
amid the sounds of cannon fire. The president and his entourage board the plane and the 
airport broadcast ends at 0858 GMT.
