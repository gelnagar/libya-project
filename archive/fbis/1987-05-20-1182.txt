Qaddumi Condemns U.S. ‘Schemes’ 
LD201237 Tripoli JANA in English 1111 GMT 20 May 87 


[Text] Tripoli, Al Maa [May] 20, Jamahiriyah News Agency — 
Mr Faruq Qaddumi, the head of the Political Department of the 
PLO, and the delegation accompanying him yesterday visited the 
house of the leader of the revolution and inspected the effects of 
the aborted American Atlantic barbaric aggression to which it 
was subjected during Al Tayr month last year. 


Mr Faruq Qaddumi and the delegation accompanying him con- 
demned anew the American aggression on the people of Great 
Jamahiriyah. 


Mr Qaddumi reasserted the solidarity of the PLO with the people 
of Great Jamahiriyah in countering all terroristic schemes 
hatched by American imperialism. 


Soviet Economic Delegation Arrives in Tripoli 
LD200951 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 GMT 20 May 87 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 May (JANA) — Konstantin Katushev, 
chairman of the Soviet State Committee for Foreign Economic 
Relations, arrived in Tripoli this morning for a visit to the Great 
Jamahiriyah. 


Katushev is accompanied by an important economic delegation.
