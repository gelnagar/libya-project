Summary (AI): An FBIS London translation of a 7 October 1977 Tripoli domestic radio broadcast of Qadhdhafi's speech at a rally in Tarhunah marking the Day of Revenge, when the Italian community was expelled from Libya. The speech is mainly about Palestine, the United States, Israel and Arab unity, with some remarks on the Jamahiriya, people's congresses and the Egypt-Libya conflict.
• Broadcast by Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic at 1638 GMT on 7 October 1977.
• The speech was issued in several takes by FBIS London (MSGNO 284, 298, 301, 309, 325).
• Qadhdhafi said the Palestinians were the owners of a cause that will triumph, and called the United States the leader of world colonialism.
• He said the Libyan people had no faith in the solutions offered, such as Geneva conferences and negotiations.
• He said the people's congresses would meet on 15 October and that Libya would put the question of placing the Libyan armed forces under Egyptian command to the popular congresses if Egypt wished to liberate Sinai.
• 1977-10-07: Qadhdhafi addressed a rally marking the seventh anniversary of the Day of Revenge, when the Italian community was expelled from Libya.
