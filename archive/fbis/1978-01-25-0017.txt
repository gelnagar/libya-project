VISITING SOVIET DELEGATION HOLDS TALKS IN TRIPOLI 
LD251030Y Tripoli JANA in English 0915 GMT 25 Jan 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 25 Jan, JANA--The visiting Soviet delegation of the Libyan-Soviet 
Friendship Society yesterday held a meeting with the society members at the foreign 
liaison bureau. The two sides discussed aspects of cooperation in their bilateral 
relations and ways and means to strengthen these relations in the current year. 


They agreed to exchange visits by populer delegations, to celebrate national days of 
both countries and to organise cultural anc artistic weeks to introduce the culture 
and heritage of both countries to their people. 


The meeting also finalised the programme of the Soviet delegation during its visit to 
the Jamahiriyah. The programme includes several meetings with secretaries of various 
departments, heads of trade unions and other federations and tour of some projects 
reflecting the progress achieved by the First of September Revolution in various fields. 


The Libyan-Soviet Friendship Society will hold a reception this evening at the People's 
Palace in Tripoli to celebrate the anniversary of the October Revolution.

[Text] The Algerian leader, Mohamed Lebjaoui, has published the following statement: 


The Algerian Movement of People's Forces and of the Army for Democracy and Maghreb Union 
[MAFPADUM] has decided, on behalf of true Algeria, to denounce the operation undertaken 
by the fascist dictator Houari Boumediene in the Arab Mashriq. 


In ract, on the fallacious pretext of strengthening the cohesion of the Arab nation, the 
usurper colonel is trying to use the Arab-Moslem world's current difficulties for his own 
ends, He hopes to use these special circumstances to portray himself as the unswerving 
defender of Arab dignity while he really intends to continue his work of dividing and 
burying our unity. He has already let his mask slip by openly praising fratricidal war 
and hatred, 


The real aim of his tour cf Arab Mashriq countries is clear, In addition to his ridicu- 
lous permanent personal candidature for the leadership, Colonel Boumediene is trying to 
disguise the insoluble internal problems facing his totalitarian regime, to break out of 
his diplomatic isolation, to wage a libelous campaign against Rabat and Nouakchott over 
the Moroccan-Mauritanian Sahara and possibly to set certain Arab leaders against France 
because the latter will not fall quietly into line with the Algerian dictatorship's 
anti-Maghreb policy. 


He also wants Washington to see him as a valuable and powerful intermediary with a great 
deal of influence in the Third World and he wants Moscow to see him as a particularly 
effective satellite, Furthermore, we would like to point out that at the end of his 
tour of Arab capitals he went straight off to report to the Kremlin and to demand more 
arms to spill yet more Maghreb and Arab blood, For him, our country's growing economic 
dependence on the United States does not exclude his regime's existing ideological, 
political, diplomatic and military dependence on the Soviet Union, On the contrary, 

the higher interests of Algeria require a concerted effort by all Arab countries, parti- 
cularly the PLO, to force the United States and the USSR, who were joint founders of 

the State of Israel after the last world war and consequently bear most of the responsi- 
bility for the tragedy of the Palestinian people, to join their efforts for peace, with 
the restitution of all occupied Arab territories including Jerusalem and the right of 

a Palestinian state to exist on Palestinian soil as priorities, 


Moreover, Colonel Boumediene dares to advocate the assassination of President as-Sadat 
and a few other Arab leaders and presents his criminal plans as the only way to achieve 
Arab liberation. 


But patriots in both our countries realize, on the contrary, that only by destroying 
Boumediene's monstrous plots will we be able to fight together with all the appropriate 
means to force Menahem Begin's government to abandon its colonialist plans and its un- 
pardonable inflexibility in face of the Arab nation's legitimate demands and the desire 
of all the peoples in the region, including Israeli people, for peace with dignity and 
justice.
