Summary (AI): A CIA reading-room compilation of Foreign Broadcast Information Service translations from December 1985, mainly Algerian news, plus a long Tripoli Domestic Service excerpt of Qadhdhafi's speech at the General People's Congress on the draft agenda for the 23 December Basic People's Congresses. He argues for direct people's rule, arms training for citizens, and the abolition of the army and police, and he mentions the Basic People's Congress of Qasr Abd al-Jalil in the Al-Fatih municipality of Tripoli.
• There are 1,443 Basic People's Congresses throughout the Jamahiriyah, according to Qadhdhafi's speech.
• Qadhdhafi said that under the system he described, the army would disappear, the police would disappear, and the Secretariat for Justice would spontaneously disappear.
• Qadhdhafi said citizens could be members of the People's Security and that courts could be formed by any five people agreed upon by the people, with death penalties if they remained.
• A Basic People's Congress speaker said the agenda's problems were administrative and income-related, with most projects interrupted and lacking financing.
• Qadhdhafi cited Tubruq, which he said had become a city of 500,000 people, and criticised memorandums from Bin Jawwad and Tripoli concerning water supply.
