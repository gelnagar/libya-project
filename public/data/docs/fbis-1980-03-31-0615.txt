SYRIA'S KHADDAM ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI, MAKES STATEMENT 


JN291809 Damascus Domestic Service in Arabic 1615 GMT 29 Mar 80 JN 


‘Text] Tripoli--'Abd al-Halim Khaddam, Syrian deputy prime minister and minister of 
foreign affairs, arrived in Tripoli before noon today, He was received at the airport 
by Dr 'Ali at-Turayki, Libyan foreign secretary; Ambassador 'Ali Hasan, head of the 
Syrian relations office in Tripoli; and the Syrian relations office members, Ina 
statement to the SANA correspondent in Tripoli, 'Abd al-Halim Khaddam said: The aim 

of our visit of Libya is to exchange viewpoints with our Libyan brothers on all issues 
that concern us, He added: I am carrying a message from President Hafiz al-Asad to 

his brother Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi on the Arab situation and the circumstances facing 
the Arab nation, 


A closed meeting was held between 'Abd al-Halim Khaddam and Dr 'Ali at-Turayki at 
Tripoli airport in the presence of Mohamed El Mokhtar, Mauritanian foreign affairs 
and cooperation minister, 


The SANA correspondent has learned that during the meeting, viewpoints were exchanged 
between the conferees on the current Arab situation, ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam, !'Ali 
at-Turayki and Mohamed El Mokhtar, accompanied by the Libyan liaison secretary and 
the head of the Syrian relations office in Banghazi, then took a special Libyan plane 
to meet with Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, Khaddam is scheduled to leave 
Banghazi for home later today after delivering President Al-Asad's message to Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi,

IRAN'S KHALKHALI SPEAKS AT RALLY IN TRIPOLI 
LD282115 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 28 Mar 80 LD 


[Text] In his speech this evening at the popular rally at the Green Square in Tripoli, 
Ayatollah Khalkhali warmly saluted the leader of the great Fatah revolution and his role 
in supporting the eauses of freedom and Islam in the world, stressing the cohesion of 
the vuwo Muslim revolutions in Libya and Iran, 


The two revolutions he added, seek the defeat of colonialism and of the enemies of Islam, 
We possess the strength of faith and adherence to the Koran, In his speech Ayatollah 
Khalkhali stressed: We are determined to liberate every inch of the homeland of Islam 
from the enemy who is supported by the United States which regards this enemy as being 

a military base for itself, We, he said, will destroy this base with all our capabilities 
and potentials,

Addressing the masses at the celebrations, he said: You have driven out the English from 
your homeland, the homeland of the Muslims, We have driven out from our homeland the 
Zionists and the CIA, We dug graves for them in Iran with all our potentials which were 
not weapons or rifles but faith and the Koran and Islam, Both Libyans and Iranians, he 
pointed out, have managed to drive out the colonialists and to confiscate their assets by 
faith, 


In his speech Ayatollah Khalkhali attacked the defeated Egyptian president, the terrorist 
Begin and U.S. President Carter, Carter, he said, claims humanity, but he does not respect 
humanity, As-Sadat is protecting the thief, murderer and executioner who is known as the 
shah, The defeated president is divorced from Islam and has nothing to do with it, Con- 
cluding, he stressed that despite all the American-Zionist-Pharaonic alliance intrigues, 

we are resolved to liberate Palestine, 


KHALKHALI: LIBYA HAD NO PART IN IMAM'S DISAPPEARANCE 


LD301948 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 30 Mar 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--Leader of the Iranian Revolution Ayatollah Sadeq Khalkhali has stressed 
that Musa as-Sadr was a victim of an imperialist and Zionist plot, as were national leader 
Kamal Jumblatt, struggler Mahai Bin Barakah, Aldo Moro and Toni Franjiyah, 


He said at a press conference here this evening that Libya had nothing to do or had no fault 
in the disappearance of the Shi'a Imam Musa as-Sadr, Had the Libyans wanted to assassinate 
the imam, they could have done that in Beirut, in the south of Lebanon or at his home, 
Khalkhali pointed out that world colonialism and Zionism and the anti-Muslim forces were 
trying to divide the Muslims through fabrications,
