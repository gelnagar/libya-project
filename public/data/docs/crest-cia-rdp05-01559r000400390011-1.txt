Summary (AI): A December 1981 State Department cable from the embassy in Rome reports Qadhafi's interview with Corriere della Sera, datelined Tripoli, covering US relations, terrorism, Italy, the MFO in Sinai, Chad, and the internal situation. It mentions the dismantling of the Tripoli souk as a past measure.
• Qadhafi said Libya had no faith in the US or the Reagan government and that there was no possibility of dialogue with the US.
• Qadhafi said Libya would have to reconsider its non-aligned position if necessary, though he never said it would directly enter the Warsaw Pact.
• Qadhafi said the Fahd plan was a continuation of Camp David and was stillborn.
• The cable says delays in Libyan payments to Italian firms were due to economic difficulties.
• 1981-12: Qadhafi gave a front-page interview to Corriere della Sera, conducted by Mino Vignolo before an audience of students.
