LIBYA

Romanian Arrives 30 November 
LD301734 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 30 Nov 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 30 Nov (JANA) lon Stanescu, Romanian 
minister and chairman of the Foreign Construction Department, 
accompanied by a delegation, arrived in Tripoli this morning on 
a visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


Ministers Receive Stanescu 
LDOI 2152 Tripoli JANA in English 1822 GMT I Dec 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Safar 26, Dec 1, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — Eng. Muhammad ‘Ubayd ash-Shukri, secretary 
of public utilities and acting secretary of housing, met today in 
the morning with the Romanian minister head of the department 
of constructions abroad, lon Stanescu who is currently on a visit 
to the Jamahiriyah. The meeting focused on cooperation between 
the Jamahiriyah and Romania and on ways and means to develop 
it in the interest of the two sisterly nations. 


Ibrahim al-Fagih Hassan, secretary of social security today at 
noon met with the Romanian minister head of the department of 
constructions abroad, lon Stanescu and his accompanying del- 
egation. The meeting focused on development of cooperation 
between the two sisterly nations.
