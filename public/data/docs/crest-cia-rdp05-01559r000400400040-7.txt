Summary (AI): An FBIS London translation of a Tripoli TV broadcast of Qadhdhafi's Evacuation Day address (June 1985) recounts the 1977 Libyan-Egyptian border clashes and Libya's claims about them. It does not concern Tripoli places or local events.
• The speech claims Libyan forces destroyed Egyptian mechanised and infantry brigades at al-Sallum plateau, with Qadhdhafi putting Egyptian dead at 10,000 to 11,000.
• Qadhdhafi says Libya offered to exchange 80 Egyptian prisoners for 11 Libyan soldiers, and Egypt refused.
• The speech mentions an Egyptian air raid on Jamal Abd al-Nasir Base, which Qadhdhafi says was flown by pilots trained there.
• 1977-07-19: Libyan forces moved from Musa'id and Jaghbub and cleared the border area; Qadhdhafi says 70 Egyptian soldiers were taken prisoner and scores killed in the border fighting.
