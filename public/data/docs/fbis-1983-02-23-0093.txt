LIBYA

FURTHER ON AL-QADHDHAFI 17 FEB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS SPEECH 


LD171906 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0930 GMT 17 Feb 83 


[Speech by Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi at meeting of General People's Congress in Tripoli -—- 
live] 


[Excerpts] [passage indistinct] Although the USSR is our friend, we would not allow its 
fleet to enter the Gulf of Sidra before it makes a request to enter and asks for per- 
mission for a visit or for a unit to enter our waters, or exactly as Yugoslavia, Italy, 
Turkey, or any other country would seek permission and exactly as we would ask 
permission from them at some time or another, saying that we have a submarine or a 
warship wanting to pass through the Bosphorus, the Dardanelles, or the Aegean Sea. 


Although the Russians are our friends, their fleet cannot go beyond latitude 32, either 
in the air or by sea. 


What I am trying to say is that we are forced to defend ourselves and to mobilize men 
and women for fighting. This is something which is inarguable. 


But we forget things. We have forgotten the massacres at Dayr Yasin [1948, Palestine] 
and Bahr al-Baqar [in Egypt]. We will soon forget the massacres at Sabra and Shatila, 
and we will forget Beirut and Tyre and Sidon, as we have forgotten ‘Akko and Yafo before. 


Tomorrow they will occupy us with another matter: they will say that they want Al-Madinah 
{in Saudi Arabia] as our capital. We would forget then about Jerusalem. They made 


Jerusalem their capital against our wishes. 


We have protested and protested. But tomorrow they will make us forget, despite 
ourselves. They will distract us with another place. They will occupy Al-Madinah and 
say this is the capital for the Jews. 


This is the train of death which you have to confront with women and men. I am sorry 
indeed that such a decision has to be taken under such conditions. I wish the conditions 
were at least normal. But in the present conditions, we are hearing tlhe sound of the 
train of death. They are ruthless and barbaric people who have no hearts. 


They are permanent members of the UN Security Council. They have the right of veto. 
They are there specifically to curb force such as that used by the Israeli forces when 
they entered Lebanon. These too sat on the fence watching and laughing. They said that 
this problem concerned the Arabs, and let them go to hell. I got angry with the USSR 
and the socialist countries. I summoned their ambassadors and told them that their 
friendship was in danger and that this showed no friendship: We are burning in front 

of you. This is enough. What did we want these countries to say? Let them at least 
tell the Security Council -- this would do. Thereupon Brezhnev sent me a message. He 
said: My friend al-Qadhdhafi. If you give food to someone, he must at least open his 
mouth so that you can put the food in it. And once the morsel is in his mouth, he 

must chew it so that he can swallow it. But we cannot open your mouths and put the 
morsel in it and chew it for you. We have given you arms. As for.friendship, this is 
friendship. But we cannot liberate Beirut for you, liberate any of your positions 

for you, or liberate Palestine for you. This is a matter for the Arabs. I mean they 
gloat, even your friends! The USSR is a friend and even an ally of the Arabs by virture 
of the basic contradiction between it and the United States, which sides with the Israelis. 
Thus it is natural that the USSR should be with the Arabs. This is in addition to other 
common objectives such as the fight against imperialism, the search for peace and 
democracy, and so on. Socialism ties us to the USSR and to the socialist bloc.

Their attitude is good. There are no better stands than those of the USSR and the 
socialist bloc toward the Arabs at the present time -- toward Libya, Syria, Yemen, and 
Algeria. [applause] 


When America declared a blockade against us and stopped buying our oil, the USSR asked 
us for payment, but we had nothing but oil. The USSR said, well, we will take the oil, 
since you have nothing but oil. [words indistinct] 


What I want to say is that, despite the friendship of the USSR and the socialist bloc 
and the fact that they are the closest countries to the Arabs now in the world, they 
cannot defend you or fight for you in your country. That is your own responsibility. 
If you say it is not our responsibility or say we are not trained, you will see what 
will happen. 


The matter of foreign congresses deserves a pause. It deserves evaluation. It deserves 
saluting, when people's congresses are set up beyond the seas and oceans -- from Latin 
America, to Australia, to Europe. Their resolutions must be incorporated in your draft. 
The agenda must also be circulated among them. The agenda is not something that is 
secret. Even if it were secret, they are Arab people's congresses, after all. Each 
year's agenda must be circulated among them. 


Arab people's congresses must continue to be formed both inside and outside the Arab 
world, openly or secretly. This could be the way to pull the carpet from under the feet 
of the regional regimes which refuse to eliminate borders and bring about Arab unity. 
Ideally, we will one day see the Arab people form a general congress for the Arab 

people and see the Arab people divided into people's congresses, which could be operat- 
ing secretly and, at some time, impose their will and force the governments to [word 
indistinct] for themselves. 


Hence, we should continue to instigate the formation of people's congresses. This is 
a task for all Libyans and for all of the Arabs forming people's congresses abroad: to 
form people's congresses everywhere, both openly and secretly, both inside and outside 
the Arab homeland. This is also the task of the Libyan and Arab revolutionary 
committees, in the Arab homeland, both the open ones and the secret ones. Every 
people's congress must incorporate a revolutionary committee to instigate it to exer- 
cise authority. Thus the Arab people's congresses abroad must have revolutionary 
forces emerging from within them to form a revolutionary committee that will perform 
the tasks which the revolutionary committees perform inside Libya. 


We must tighten our belts so that we are not affected by the economic crisis and do not 
fall like others who have fallen. As I have told you before, some countries have been 
crushed and harmed. All the countries in the world have embarked on economic measures 
to deal with the crisis: laying off workers, reducing wages and budgets, increasing 
taxes, increasing customs duties, and increasing prices of commodities. 


We have so far escaped the economic crisis through which the world is passing. We are 
among the countries least affected by the world economic crisis. We must continue to 
remain so. [applause] 


There is yet another point in addition to the economic one, concerning political and 
moral aspects. The United States has plotted against us with coups and direct military 
threats, hoping they would bring about some effect inside Libya. As you might have 
heard last year, I am not sure whether it was 1982 or 1981, besides the personal flthy 
plots, [words indistinct] they assassinated. What we are familiar with from the CIA, 
that is the program of [word indistinct] the American intelligence committee. They 
were discussing the budget. They decided they could get rid of these people with that 
method.

They thought that by doing something to one person they could put an end to the revolu- 
tion or cause it to relapse. They said that when ‘Abd an-Nasir died, Egypt retreated. 
[passage indistinct] Thus they said that issues depend on personalities, so if they 
subdue certain personalities -- that is, occupy the Arab homeland, or some area, or the 
petroleum [sentence as heard] They have failed in all their attempts against us. 


{passage indistinct] All this is black propaganda. The idea is to incite the world 
against someone as was the case with Hitler. They accused Libya of being the country 
of crime, terrorism, and distribution of arms and [words indistinct] all these have in 
fact failed. 


Austria's Kreisky has told them that all of this talk about Libya -- we have read in 
history what was said against the French Revolution when it was staged -- you considered 
it an act of terrorism and subversion. But then you saw how the French Revolution be- 
came a turning point in the history of mankind. He said that what is happening in 
Libya was also a new turning point in the history of mankind, a new culture, a revolu- 
tion, a cultural revolution. [applause] 


They asked him why he welcomed Al-Qadhdhafi in Austria. He showed me the cables and 
protests he received from individuals and groups in addition to the official pressures 
from America and the Israeli Government. He told them: This is a black propaganda 

you are waging against him. It is propaganda by prejudiced people and has the stamp of 
Israel. This is similar to the propaganda campaigns waged by emperors and kings against 
the French Revolution. 


This matter needs to be understood. They have failed in their black propaganda against 
us. During the days of As-Sadat, they thought he could capture Libya and that nobody 
would protest on the pretext that Libya was a country of terrorism. America also 
throught that if its fleets were to come close to Libya, the Libyans would get fright- 
ened and [words indistinct] but they have failed [words indistinct] they no longer 
have any hope. The regime is a people's regime [words indistinct] the Libyan people 
are free. 


We must politically and morally endure so that we prove to them that such tactics do 

not work with us. We refuse to submit or yield. Either we are convinced and thereby 
accept, or we should be prepared to endure even death before submitting. Let America 
and those with it understand that all these things will be of no avail [words indistinct] 
so that they may respect us and say: We realize that you are certainly a free people 
[your own] masters who are prepared to die or to live only on bread and water, but who 
will deal with you differently. You say what you want and we say what we want, and 

then we try to reach agreement between the two wishes. 


We do not reject this and the door for this is not closed. 

this door for this is not closed. However they can enter this door only when they be- 
come convinced that we are a people who would not [word indistinct] ourselves for the 
sake of chocolate, or out of fear of losing salaries, or cars [words indistinct] and 
even whether or not we had rice. To hell with tea and coffee. Even if we eat wood and 
leather. We are people who refuse to bargain over our dignity. This is impossible. 

We must prove this to them. It is not enough to urge you to do so. We must prove this 
to them. [applause] 


We must take the necessary measures so that we do not submit. The Americans and the 
others must ultimately feel certain that they have to change their policy toward Libya, 
that they must respect it and deal with it in the manner it wants and not in the manner 
they want. 


This is what I want to say. The struggle continues. March forward.

HIJACKED PLANE REMAINS AT MALTA AIRPORT 


LD231001 Tripoli JANA in English 0707 GMT 23 Feb 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada 11, Feb 23, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The Libyan Arab Airlines 
passenger plane hijacked on an internal flight last Sunday is still at Luca airport. 
Libyan Arab-Airline sources said that negotiations are in progress between the hijackers 
and the airport's control tower with the presence of the Maltese prime minister. The 
Maltese authorities today refused to refuel the plane.
