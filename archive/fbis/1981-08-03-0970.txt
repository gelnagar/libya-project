LIBYA

"ARAFAT TALKS WITH AL-QADHDHAFI, MAKES STATEMENT 


LD022358 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 2 Aug 81 


[Text] The brother leader has received Chairman of the PLO Executive Committee Yasir 
‘arafat, who arrived in the Jamahiriyah last night. Brother Yasir ‘Arafat left Tripoli 
tonight. 


Brother Yasir 'Arafat made a statement to a JANA correspondent in which he said: I have 
informed the brother leader of the revolution, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, of the results 
of the honorable battles waged by the Palestinian-Lebanese joint forces during the 
attacks by the Zionist forces, on the orders of the United States, against the Lebanese 
and Palestinian peoples. The talks with brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi also dealt 
with a number of Arab and international issues. 


He added that he reaffirmed to the brother leader the attitude of the Palestinian 
revolution, which supports the Great Al-Fatah Revolution's stands, particularly follow- 
ing the discovery of the U.S. terrorist conspiracy aimed at the assassination of the 
brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi which was exposed by the U.S. information media. 


He also said that the Arab masses should rally round a unified Arab plan to confrent 
the Zionist enemy, particularly in these circumstances, in which colonialism is trving 
to weaken the Arab nation. What happened to the Iraqi nuclear reactor is a warning to 
every Arab country. 


Brother Yasir ‘Arafat condemned the bloody regime in the Sudan and exposed its links 
with and subservience to U.S. imperialism. He declared the Palestinian revolution's 
support for the struggle of the Sudanese revolutionary forces to topple the lackey and 
traitorous regime of Ja'far Numayri. Brother Yasir ‘Arafat also denounced the Sudanese 
regime and its hostile attitude to the Palestinian revolution and said that the 
Palestinian revolution stands by the fraternal Sudanese people in their struggle against 
the traitorous Sudanese ruler.
