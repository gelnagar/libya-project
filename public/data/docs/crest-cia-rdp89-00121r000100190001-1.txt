Summary (AI): A 1982 NPIC briefing note reports an SA-3 surface-to-air missile firing battery deployed in the support area of Labraq Airfield on Libya's northeast coast, about 11.5 nautical miles east-northeast of Al Bayda. The site is far from Tripoli, so it has no direct Tripoli link.
• Battery in the support area: one possible SA-3 four-rail launcher, one low blow guidance radar, four missile transloaders, one computer van, and 13 trucks or pieces of equipment.
• Elsewhere at the airfield support area: three SA-3 four-rail launchers, one low blow radar, at least 11 transloaders, one computer van and two trailers.
• Document is an NPIC/PEG briefing board attachment (Z-10138/82), classified SECRET and dated January 1982.
• Labraq coordinates given as 32-47-39N 021-57-50E.
• 1982-01: SA-3 firing battery deployed about 1 NM northeast of the airfield at 32-49-38N 022-00-45E, with launchers, a guidance radar, transloaders and a computer van.
