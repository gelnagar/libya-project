UNISIAN FOREIGN MINISTER ARRIVES 7 APR 


LDO71722 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 7 Apr 83 


Text] Tunisian Foreign Minister Beji Caid es-Sebsi arrived today in Banghazi. The 
Tunisian minister and the delegation accompanying him were welcomed on arrival by the 
secretary of the people's committee of the People's Foreign Liaison Bureau and the 
secretary of the general people's committee for agrarian reform and land reclamation. 
The Tunisian foreign minister in a statement to JANA praised the depth of the fraternal 
ties between the two fraternal peoples, pointing to the positive results of the visit 

the brother leader of the revolution to Tunisia in February last year, which he 
lescribed as having built strong bridges of cooperation between the two countries in 


various areas.

JALLUD RECEIVES MOROCCAN FOREIGN MINISTER 


LDO71508 Rabat MAP in English 1200 GMT 7 Apr 83 


[Excerpt] Tripoli, Apr 8 (MAP) -- Mr M'hamed Boucetta, Moroccan minister of state 
for foreign affairs and emissary of King Hassan II of Morocco, was received by 
Libyan commander ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud on Wednesday in Tripoli. 


The state minister arrived here Wednesday afternoon as part of a tour that took him 
to Algeria and Tunisia. 


In a statement to the JAMAHIRIYA NEWS AGENCY, "JANA", the Moroccan diplomat said he 

was bearing a message from the Moroccan monarch to the Libyan president Colonel Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, on the situation prevailing in the Arab world and issues of common 
interest. 


He added that consultations and exchange of viewpoints among Arab leaders are necessary 
particularly at this current turning point. 


The message also deals with the convening of an extraordinary Arab summit scheduled to 
be held in Fes (Morocco) later this month. 


While in Tunis, Mr Boucetta handed similar messages to the Tunisian President Habib 
Bourguiba and to the secretary general of the Arab League.
