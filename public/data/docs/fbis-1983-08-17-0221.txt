LIBYA

As-Sa’igah’s Al-Qadi Sends Al-Qadhdhafi Message 
LD161036 Tripoli JANA in English 0846 GMT 16 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhu al-Qa’dah 8, August 16, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The revolution’s leader received the fol- 
lowing cable from ‘Issam al-Qadi, secretary general of the Gen- 
eral Command of As-Sa’iqah (Vanguard of the Popular 
Liberation War): 


The Arab masses and their national revolutionary forces together 
with all peace-loving people in the world are following with 
extreme concern the reactionary Zionist-U.S. onslaught against 
the Jamahiriyah. 


The onslaught is coupled with desperate attempts to liquidate the 
Palestinian revolution. We are aware of the fact that the repeated 
attack against the Arab nation is aimed at subjugating it to U.S 
and Zionist hegemony. U.S. provocations against Libya in the 
north coincided with U.S. manoeuvres in [word indistinct] ter- 
ritories and increased imperialist intervention in Chad, against 
both the Chadian people and their legitimate united government 
under the leadership of President Goukouni Oueddei. The U.S 
staged all these operations by fabricating false allegations 
against the Jamahiriyah. 


This vicious onslaught, however, will not undermine the determi- 
nation of the Libyan Arab people and their revolutionary lead- 
ership for, together with the other Arab masses they are capable 
of repulsing the aggressor. 


In conclusion, the cable called on all national forces to back the 
Libyan Arab people in confronting the vicious imperialist 
onslaught against the Jamahiriyah. 


Fatah Dissident Delegation Departs 16 August 
LD162334 Tripoli JANA in English 1747 GMT 16 Aug 83 


[Excerpt] Tripoli, Dhu al-Qa’dah 8, August 16, JAMAHIR- 
IYAH NEWS AGENCY The delegation of the new rev- 
olutionary movement before noon today left Tripoli 


Abu Salih, [Nimr Salih] member of the Command Committee of 
the new revolutionary movement, in a statement to JANA’s 
correspondent at Tripoli airport before he left, said: “We feel 
confident as we leave Jamahiriyah land of the Great Al-Fatih 
Revolution for our positions, that we are able to reach our aims 
which are the aims of all Arabs, to destroy the Zionist enemy and 
defend the Palestinian cause.” 


He added “Our confidence in the strength of our Arab nation to 
achieve our ambitions for freedom and liberation, had increased, 
especially after our meeting with the leader, who is evoking 
towards the unity of all revolutions and towards achieving the 
aims of the Arab nation, to protect Lebanon and the victorious 
outcome of the Palestinian people’s cause and the right of the 
return to the homeland.”
