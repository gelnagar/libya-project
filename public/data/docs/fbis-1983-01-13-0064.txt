LIBYA

FIERCE FIGHTING CONTINUES IN CHADIAN CAPITAL 


LP131040 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1025 GMT 13 Jan 83 


[Text] Ndjamena, 13 Jan (JANA) -- Urgent news from the Chadian capital today reported 
that fierce fighting was taking place in the capital city of Ndjamena. The news said 
that the fierce fighting began last night and that it could be an attempt to overthrow 
the insubordinate Hissein Habre. News from the Chadian capital confirmed the fierce 
fighting was continuing. However, no further details of the situation or the outcome 
of the fierce fighting in Ndjamena are available. 


Other information from the Chadian capital reported that the intensity of the fierce 
fighting against the insubordinate Habre has increased, and that it seemed to be between 
Habre's forces and insurgents who have rebelled against him. 


REPORTS ON TRAINING OF AFRICAN GUERRILLAS DENIED 


LD121930 Hamburg DPA in German 1651 GMT 12 Jan 83 


[Text] Bonn, 12 Jan (DPA) -- On Wednesday, Libya denied reports on the training of 
African guerrilla fighters under the supervision of Soviet and GDR instructors. The 
Libyan Embassy in Bonn said that this false piece of information is part of a campaign 
against Libya which has been conducted for some time. The allusion to the alleged 
training of underground fighters from Chad, Mauritania, and other African countries is 
an attempt to damage relations between Libya and its neighboring states. The embassy 
stressed that Libya sets "great store by the close relations between the Arab-Libyan 
and the German people;" reports of this kind are intended to put them under strain. 


TRIPOLI COMMENTS ON U.S. POLICY TOWARD SYRIA 


LD121850 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 12 Jan 83 


[Text] The U.S. Administration is showing an artificial and deliberate annoyance at 
Syria's deployment of SAM-5 long-range missiles. The Reagan administration, in coordi- 
nation with the Zionist enemy, has staged a propaganda campaign against these missiles. 
The United States alleges that deploying such missiles threatens the security of the 
area. 


JANA's political editor says that this artificial annoyance comes within the framework 
of Washington's tricks and its banking on what it calls the weakness of the Arab mind.

It has created this storm not out of fear of these missiles, which are used lawfully 

in defense against the Zionist aggressors who are supported by the U.S. machine, but 

its has created it for the purpose of going ahead with the implementation of its plan 
devoted to serving the so-called security of Israel. 


It is strange that the Reagan administration expresses so-called concern for the 
right of Syria to defend itself and to confront the dangers posed by the Zionist 
enemy, for basiclaly it is a question of the United States conspiring, through 
expanded Zionist-U.S. armament, to dominate the Arab homeland. 


It must be stressed that Washington allows itself to spread weapons of destruction 
both in the Arab homeland and in a number of other countries. It also pushes the 
Zionist ally to occupy, to expand, and to turn occupied Palestine into an arsenal of 
U.S. destructive weapons to confront the Arab nation. 


Why was Washington not annoyed with the Zionist invasion, which took place with a 
heavy cover of U.S. aircraft, tanks, and cluster bombs sent to Tel Aviv from Washing- 
ton as a gift? Why is Washington not annoyed -- even as play-acting for public 
opinion -- with the killing of children, peasants, workers, women, and old people by 
factist dictatorships in Latin Ameirca with whom it has built permanent bridges, 
regimes, and figures which would not have been able to kill, oppress, spread terror, 
or cause terrible destruction had it not been for the U.S. weapons? 


The Reagan administration, while showing such artificial concern and false annoyance, 
is not only leveling scorn at the Arab mind, but it is also grossly holding in 
contempt the entire world public opinion, which is now fully aware of the ugly 

crimes committed against nations and of the conspiracies hatched against the progres- 
sive regimes which reject U.S. hegemony. 


If the U.S. finds it too much for the Syrian people to defend their soil and Arabism 
this gives us further proof of its attitude and policy, which has been condemned 
because it ignores Arab rights, and blatantly aligned itself to the Zionist enemy and 
encouraged him to commit aggression, stage invasions, and kill thousands of innocent 
people with U.S. weapons and money. At the same time the Reagan Adminsitration 
exposes itself and its tricks, and confirms its premedidated intentions to perpetuate 
Zionist occupation of Lebanon by way of providing absurd pretexts and stifling the 
progressive Arab attitudes. This administration first implements capitulationist 
solutions and schemes, second, guarantees the picking of the fruits of the Zionist 
aggression against Lebanon, and subsequently finds a suitable atmosphere to implement 
the U.S. plan which seeks to push the Zionist train of death to other positions in the 
Arab homeland. 


It is no secret to anyone that the U.S. Administration has been taking advantage 

of the circumstances in the Lebanese arena in order to realize its other aims: to 
strip Syrian defences naked; to take Lebanon through the current shameful negotiation 
into a position of complete submission; and to make Lebanon a passage through which 
U.S. peace can be imposed so that the meeting of Zionist and U.S. interests in other 
positions can be realized.

AL-QADHDHAFI PROPOSAL PASSED TO ARAB LEAGUE 


LD122236 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 12 Jan 83 


[Text] JANA has learned that contact has been made between the Popular Bureau for 
Foreign Liaison and the secretary of the Arab League to find out what the league's 
General Secretariat had done in the way of measures toward the proposal of the brother 
leader of the revolution concerning the formation of an Arab military force in which 
member states participate in order to stand up to the Zionist occupation of Lebanon, 
support the Palestinians, and prevent further deterioration in the ranks of the Arab 
nation. 


The Arab League secretary general said that he duly circulated the proposai to all the 
Arab member-countries. The secretariat general is awaiting the replies of the Arab 
states to this proposal.
