Summary (AI): A garbled 1976 Tripoli radio (Arabic service) report says Qadhdhafi told a popular rally near Tripoli, at Bin Ghashir, that continued mass processions and festivals throughout Libya were a weapon against reactionary and imperialist campaigns. It is a regime propaganda item with no specifics on repression.
• Source is a Tripoli radio item in Arabic, dated 2 May 1976, filed as a CIA CREST document.
• Qadhdhafi said the more campaigns against the 1 September revolution intensified, the more the Libyan people would become determined to confront them.
• He praised the 'martyr fathers' who fell defending the Libyan Arab territory.
• 1976-05-01: Qadhdhafi speech at a popular rally saying mass processions and festivals should continue to counter reactionary and imperialist statements.
