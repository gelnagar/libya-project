LIBYA

CHAD'S GOUKOUNI OUEDDEI MEETS AL-—QADHDHAFI 


LD272348 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2300 GMT 27 Nov 82 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution yesterday evening received Goukouni 
Oueddei, head of the Transitional National Government of Chad. The meeting was attend- 
ed by Adam (Tugot). 


Departs, Sends Cable 
LD292218 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 29 Nov 82 


[Text] Goukouni Oueddei, head of the Transitional National Government of Chad, sent a 
cable to the brother leader of the revolution after his departure from the Jamahiriyah 
for Chad. Here is the text: 


At a time when I am leaving for Chad, together with my accompanying delegation, we con- 
vey to you and to your people our sincere thanks for the great hospitality accorded us, 
and for the steps taken by the Jamahiriyah and the efforts it made to convene the 19th 
OAU summit. 


Your country, which is loyal to its political principles, has always taken a stance, 
without reservation, toward the Chadian people and without yielding to outside pres- 
sures; this stance is characterized by the direct backing our people received from the 
Jamahiriyah during the difficult moments they experienced. Your people contributed to 
the restoration of peace and stability; this is an historic fact acknowledged by 
African states. We are fully convinced that my government has learned from the lessons 
of the past and will work to consolidate relations between our two peoples by relying 
on the bonds of historic and cultural kinship and on the basis of our revolutionary 
principles. Moreover, we are also fully convinced that some of the countries which did 
not rise to their African responsibilities will soon realize the peril of their stance 
and ultimately the interests of the African peoples will triumph over the interests of 
imperialism so that the 19th summit will convene under the best conditions and at the 
best time. 


With profound appreciation, 
[Signed] Goukouni Oueddei 


CHAD ARRESTS, EXECUTIONS, BORDER CLOSURE REPORTED 


LD291620 Tripoli JANA in English 1451 GMT 29 Nov 82 


[Text] Ndjamena, Safar 12, Nov 29, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- It has been confirmed 
that Hissein Habre has arrested some of his top aides, including Finance Minister and 
member of the State Council Mahamat Nour Abderaman, former Minister of Communication 
and the Economy Minister Ali Salim, and the head of the Treasury. 


Habre is also reported to have executed the minister of justice in Goukouni Oueddei's 
government and the head of the military bureau. A curfew has been imposed in the 
capital Ndjamena (?and) the Chad-Cameroon border was closed to stop the Chadians from 
leaving the [word indistinct] and joining the Transitional Government of National 
Unity which is still in control of the central, northern and eastern section of Chad. 


Sources from the Cameroon state that an air link has been opened up between Zaire and 
Ndjamena to convey soldiers from Zaire and Egypt as well as military equipment into 
Chad to prevent the Habre regime from falling down the precipice.
