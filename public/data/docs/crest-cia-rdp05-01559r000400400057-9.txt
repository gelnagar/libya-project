Summary (AI): A 1985 CIA-reading-room compilation of Algerian and Libyan press items, including an Al-Tadamun interview with Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi (conducted in Tripoli on 22 August 1985) on Iran, Iraq, Sudan, and Libya's expulsion of Egyptian and Tunisian workers. It contains nothing about specific Tripoli sites, prisons, or killings.
• Interview with Qadhdhafi by Fu'ad Matar was conducted in Tripoli on 22 August 1985 and published in London in Al-Tadamun on 7 September 1985.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya might send surface-to-surface and surface-to-air missiles and aircraft to Iran, but had made no decision.
• Libya had deported Egyptian and Tunisian workers shortly before the interview, according to the interviewer's question.
• Qadhdhafi said a Mengistu deputy arrived in Tripoli on 22 August 1985 to discuss Sudan and Garang.
