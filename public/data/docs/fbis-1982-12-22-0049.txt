LIBYA

AT-TALHI, BULGARIA'S ZAREV DISCUSS COOPERATION 


LD212230 Tripoli JANA in English 1652 GMT 21 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Awal 6, Dec 21, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Mr Jadallah ‘Aziz 
at-Talhi, secretary of the General People’s Committee, has received the Bulgarian 
deputy premier and secretary of the Bulgarian Communist Party's Central Committee, 
Mr Kiril Zarev. [titles as received] They discussed the means for strengthening 
their cooperation and the implementation of agreements signed between the two 
countries. 


V. 22 Dec 82 Q 2 NORTH AFRICA 


Zarev Departs 
LD212240 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1830 GMT 21 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 Dec (JANA) -- Kiril Zarev, member of the Bulgarian Communist Party 
Central Committee and deputy prime minister, [titles as received] and Bulgarian 

Foreign Trade Minister Khristo Khristov and their accompanying delegation left Tripoli 
this evening. 


Zarev praised the positive results achieved by the visit, as well as the full under- 
standing which prevailed during the bilateral talks. 


In a statement to a JANA correspondent at Tripoli International Airport, he said: We 
have agreed on the necessity to widen and deepen cooperation in various fields, 
especially in economic, industrial, commercial, and agricultural areas. He pointed out 
that agreement was reached to deepen the ties of friendship and to strengthen bilateral 
relations. 


JANA INTERVIEWS POLISARIO SECRETARY GENERAL 


LD212114 Tripoli JANA in English 1327 GMT 21 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Awal 6, Dec 21, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Polisario secretary 
general and president of the Saharan Arab Republic, Mohamed Abdelaziz, and the minister 
of education and member of the Revolutionary Command Council, Mohamed Lamine, arrived 
here yesterday. 


In a statement to our correspondent Mir Abdelaziz expressed pride at visiting the 
Jamahiriyah, "the land of revolution", because he felt part of it. He said: "The 
visit comes at a most sensitive time for the Arab region and the African Continent in 
view of the imperialist conspiracies against the region." 


He added: "These conspiracies reached their climax in Angola and Mozambique in an 
attempt to liquidate S.W.A.P.0. and preparing to liquidate the Saharan Republic by 
imperialist and reactionary forces. To this end, Moroccan territory was opened for 
U.S. military bases, and the Moroccan Army was strengthened under U.S. supervision. 
Both reaction and imperialism are endeavouring to liquidate the revolutionary force 
in each of Algeria and the Jamahiriyah." 


Mr Abdelaziz further pointed out that the imperialist forces managed to even penetrate 
and affect the African decision at the conference of the 0.A.U., which itself repre- 
sents the independence and honour of Africa. He said that imperialism is attempting 
beyond any doubt to destroy the 0.A.U. He stressed however, that the Saharan Republic 
and its allies in Africa will counter all these conspiracies. 


He praised the tremendous achievements of the Libyan Arab people through the great 
Al Fatah Revolution. He said that this was the route for progress, dignity and freedom 
of the Arab (?man) in the Arab world and the world [words indistinct].
