LIBYA

Connection With London Explosions Denied 
LD111751 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 11 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 11 Mar (JANA) — The Popular Committee of the 
People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison has asserted that the Jama- 
hiriyah has no connection whatsoever with the explosions that 
occurred recently in the British capital. 


The committee today asked the British ambassador in Tripoli to 
submit clarifications about the statements attributed to British 
Foreign Secretary Geoffrey Howe, who pointed an accusing 
finger at the Jamahiriyah with regard to these incidents. 


The committee has noticed that recently the Jamahiriyah had 
been subjected to a deliberate, malicious and racist campaign in 
the British information media. It stressed that this campaign, 
which has intensified and escalated, is a cover-up for the British 
stance that encourages the stray dogs that have committed 
crimes against the Libyan Arab people. The committee said that 
the British authorities have refused to hand over these elements, 
“who were tried on charges of theft, economic corruption and 
carrying out criminal terrorist actions against the people’s 
authority in Libya,” according to arrest warrants issued by 
“Interpol,” the international police. 


The Popular Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison has told the British ambassador that, while affirming the 
development of relations with the British people, it will not be 
responsible for or ensure [as received] the incidents or explosions 
that take place in Britain, explaining that the British authorities 
are alone capable of preventing the occurrence of such incidents 
in Britain. 


Spokesman Denies Chad ‘Allegations’ Over Plane 
LD111758 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1725 GMT 11 Mar 84 


{Text] Tripoli, 11 Mar (JANA) — A spokesman for the People’s 
Committee of the People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau made a 
statement denying allegations by the renegade Habre’s ambas- 
sador in Paris regarding the destruction of a French aircraft at 
Ndjamena airport yesterday. 


In his statement, the spokesman said that the Socialist People’s 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah had nothing whatsoever to do with this 
incident, and while denouncing the renegade Habre’s ambas- 
sador’s allegation regarding Libya’s connection with the inci- 
dent, said: The incident confirms the deterioration of security 
in Ndjamena and the Habre regime’s inability to control matters 
in the Chadian capital and achieve security and stability, even 
within the boundaries of the capital which he controls with the 
protection of foreign troops. This incident is another indication 
of the anger of the Chadian people and their rejection of French 
military presence on Chadian territory.
