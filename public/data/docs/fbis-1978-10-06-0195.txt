LIBYA

IRAQI ENVOY BRINGS MESSAGE FOR AL-QADHDHAFI 


LD051311Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 12 


[Text] 'Adnan Husayn, member of the Revolution Command Council and minister of planning 
in fraternal Iraq, arrived in Tripoli at noon today on a visit to the Jamahiriyah, 


At Tripoli International Airport he was met by Major al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi and by the 
undersecretary of the Foreign Secretariat and by Iraq's ambassador to the Jamahiriyah, 


In a statement to a JANA correspondent, 'Adnan Husayn said that he was lessage 
from President Ahmad Hasan al-Bakr to brether Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of 
the Great 1 September Revolution, connected with the current Arat Situation. He added 
that he would carry out certain contacts with the brothers in the Jamahiriyah on the 
coordination of the joint stands to rescue the Aral nation from the tribulation it 

is experiencing.
