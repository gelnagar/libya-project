• Ghurdabiyah airfield is in north-central Libya, 9 nm south of Surt.
• Coordinates given: 26-59-36N 014-28-01E (Sebha area) and 31-03-34N 016-35-49E (Ghurdabiyah area).
• 1982-05: Probable Mirage III/V crash at the northeast end of the airfield, apparently during takeoff
