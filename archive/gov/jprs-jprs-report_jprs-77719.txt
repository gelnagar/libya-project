[Text | Al-Jabal al-Akhdar, 10 Rabii al Alkher, 15 February (JAMAHIRIYA NEWS AGENCY). 


The secretary of the People's Committee for Communications and Maritime Transport 
of the municipality of al Jabal al-Akhdar has made it clear that a study is in pro- 
gress concerning a certain number of projects in the area of roads and communication 
among which are the construction of a 150 kilometer road to connect the munici- 
pality's stores, and of an expressway °t Massa Chahat. 


There is also the possibility of the construction of a telephone exchange on the 
esplanade of El Kouf and another in the city of Hl Beidha. 


In accordance with the implementation of the resolutions and recommendations of the 
grass roots people's congresses, 50 telex cables and telephone networks will be 
built at El Paidia, Garnada, Omar El Mokhtar El Hanina, Aslatna and El Fajr El Jadid. 


Furthermore, according to the sources of the People's Committee for Agricultural 
Reform and Land Exploitation of the municipality of al-Jabal al-Akhdar, a study is 
currently in progress with regard to a certain number of agricultural projects, 

such as support for agricultural associations, the construction of «rain warehouses, 
a watering place for animals in pasture areas, the construction of 13 artesian 
wells, the establishment of a certain number of stables, of certain services concerr 
ing the agricultural project of El Wassita, and chicken raising centers intended for 
ege production.

|Report on closing session of the meeting of the General Union of Students of the 
Jama!.iriya at Benghazi, on 10 February 1981 | 


[Text | Benghazi, 6 Rabii al Akher, 11 February (JAMAHIRIYA PRESS AGENCY). 


Last night, the fifth general congress of the General Union of Students of the 
Jamahiriya ended its session which was held at the University of Garyounes in 
Berythazi, under the motto: "Students are the instrument of revolution, unity and 
prorrress." 


During the final session, which took place yesterday evening at 1700 hours (local 
time), the resolutions and recommendations adopted by the grass roots student con- 
gresses and formulated by the general congress, were read. 


These resolutions of the General Union of Students of the Jamahiriya consider’ the 
contents of the address by the head of the revolution to the congress to be the 
action guide for student congresses during the next stage, and emphasize the fact 
that the students will be the instrument of consolidation of power for the people 
and of the era of the masses, and the heralds of the people's revolution through 
their revolutionary committees. They have, as a guide, The Green Book through 
which they have discovered the vanity of modern democracies and the reality of the 
exploitation socicty. 


The student congresses also decided to reorganize the General Union of Students of 
the Jamahiriya, in such a way as to achieve the power of the people. They approved 
the establishment of a student liaison office, whose mission will consist of build- 
ing excellent and solid relationships with the Arab and world student unions and 
organizations. 


The congresses decided to revolutionize the passive traditional methods to liberate 
minds by stressing technological sciences and specialized schools, in order to 
serve the new transformation at the level of civilization and to contribute to the 
solving of the problems of the masses. 


At their congresses, the student masses emphasized the need to accelerate the im- 
plementation of higher education programs in the universities and institutes for 
higher learning in Jamahiriya, and to refrain from calling on foreign experts, ex- 
cept in highly specialized fields. The student congresses also recommended a
