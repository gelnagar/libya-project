TRIPOLI COMMENTARY CONDEMNS ISRAELI 'TERRORISM' 
LD131452Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 13 Mar 78 LD 
[Unattributed commentary] 


[Excerpts] There are thousands of proofs of the terroristic and criminal character of 
Zionism: The occupation of Palestine, the homelessness of its people, and the establishment 
of a Zionist entity on the debris of the social and political entity of the Palestinian 
Arab people is the height of terrorism, aggression and oppression, equalled only by the 
Zionists! claim that Palestine was only a land without a people and was taken over by a 
people without a land. Palestine was never a land without people and the Zionists are 

no people; even if we assume they are a people, Palestine is not the haven of this so-called 
people gathered by Zionism from all parts of the world and whose children, boys or girls, 
and men and women, were turned by Zionism into racist fighters committing acts of terrorism 
and murder against the Arab people. 


Our people have the right to fight the Zionists as a whole because they are all fighters 
and aggressors. There is no difference between their soldier at the front and the other 
which occupies an Arab house and Arab farm. At times of danger all the Zionists in the 

occupied territory can be alerted by means of signals broadcast over the enemy radio and 
all become fighters committing massacres and carnage against Arab citizens and the Arab 

territories. 


Hence the bold fedayeen operation which took place recently at Tel Aviv is in no way a 
military operation against civilians but a fedayeen military action against Zionist soldiers 
occupying Arab territory and the homes of the Arabs who had been driven from their homeland. 
It is the right of the Palestine Arab people who refuse to submit to faits accomplis to 
resort tq all ways and means to force the enemy to leave their territory. This is no act 

of terrorism at all, even if the United States appointed its President to brag about human 
rights and to claim to be resisting terrorism. 


If the United States is serious and true in its claims, then let it come even a little 
closer to the truth and let it call things by their names.

If it were to do so, then it will be found who is the terrorist to be resisted by the 
entire world community: Is he the Arab who is fighting to return to his home, farm and 
homeland, or is he that Zionist usurper who seized the home, farm and homeland of the 
Palestinians? 


The Palestinian Arab people supported by the forces of Arab revolution, the forces of 
confrontation and steadfastness do not care about being called terrorists by the terror- 
ists, or even worse names. The Arab people have taken a stand from which they will never 
retreat: to deepen their fighting practices for the retrieval of their land. They 
have decided that confrontation will not mean standing in the trenches to check aggres- 
sion but that confrontation will mean fighting, attacks and a bold war against the enemy 
positions in the depth of the occupied territory. In other words, the rejection of the 
Zionist-imperialist reactionary plot has entered the stage of combat rejection in reply 
to the Zionist-imperialist denial of the existence of the Palestinian Arab people and 

in reply to the desperate capitulatory plots being implemented by the apostate Egyptian 
regime, and to the abominable Zionist crimes which the entire world community had con- 
demned time and again. 


The Zionists will have to learn that the sole legitimate representative of the Palestinian 
people is not only the PLO but also the fighting gun as well. The guns do not beg or 

ask for mercy. The Zionists are the ones to start aggression, carnage and terrorism, 

Our nation's reply is not an aggression nor an act of terrorism. We do not fight the 
Zionists over their lands even in Tel Aviv, but we fight them over our land in Tel Aviv, 
Haifa and Nazareth, the Gaza Strip and the occupied West Bank. The Palestinian Arab 

gun is loaded. The future undoubtedly holds a great deal of the escalation of the armed 
struggle until liberation is achieved by true means and methods, 


ISRAELI CALL TO BREAK WITH PLO REJECTED 


LD131018Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 13 Mar 78 LD 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 13 March--The JANA political editor has commented on the Zionist 
enemy's call on wor)d countries with relations with the PLO to sever these relations 
following the recent fedayeen operations inside occupied Palestine. 


The editor writes: The Israelis have asked countries with relations with the PLO to 
sever these relations following the recent fedayeen operations in the occupied Arab ter- 
ritories on the pretext that these operations were abominable crimes committed against 
civilians. We must definitely accept the logic of severing relations with aggressors 
who have no respect for human life and security, the editcr adds. But we have the right 
to ask: What can we call the Zionists who come from all corners of the world to usurp 
the land of Palestine and change even its historical name, make its Palestinian people 
homeless, chase them even in their tents and shelters and attack them without mercy or 
pity? What can we call terrorist Begin, the commander of the Dayr Yasin carnage? And 
how can we describe the mass killings carried out by Israeli aircraft against the 
students of Bahr al-Baqar and the workers of Abu Za 'Bal factory? 


What is terrorism? Is man's fi; ‘t for the retrieval of his land, home and farm terrorism? 
Is the Palestinian resistance fight against the Nazi-type invasion terrorism? Was the 
Americans! fight against British colonialism an act of terrorism? Was the Vietnamese!s 
defense of their lands an act of terrorism? Were not the Americans the terrorists? 

Was the British people's fight against Nazism an act of terrorism, or were the Nazis 

the real terrorists?

These questions, the editor writes, mst be answered by world conscience, particularly 
those deceived by the pro-imperialist and pro-Zionist information media which depict 
real terrorism as the victim and the real victim as terrorism. Neither the Palestinians 
nor the Arab national should be held responsible or asked to pay the price for the 
suffering of the European Jews, 


The world should heed the call of the Israelis to sever relations with the terrorists who 
commit aggressions against the innocent. The African Continent did precisely that when it 
realized the racist, imperialist and terrorist nature of the Zionist entity and thus 
severed relations with it. 


The peoples of the civilized world are called upon to follow suit and to sever relations 
with the racist-Zionist regime, whose records abound with crime, terrorism, aggressions 

and innumerous condemnations by the United Nations and which has been branded officially 
by the world as racist. 


In conclusion, the political editor writes: A word to the Eastern Jews who came from 
Palestine and other parts of the Arab homeland: It is time you realized that the masters 
that came from Europe and the United States to colonize you and to create the only country 
in the world that oppresses the Jews in modern times will not bring you peace, security 

or stability. By their terrorist, racist and expansionist policy they will only bring 
destruction and death to yeu. Stand up in the face of the oppressors and debunk the myth 
of the foreign Zionists! supremacy. Place your hands in the hands of your cousins, the 
peoples of the area, and together build this area and enjoy its wealth with them, as you 
did for centuries until the Zionist racists and terrorists arrived. 


AL-QADHDHAFI REPORTEDLY TO VISIT JORDAN SOON 
NC130900Y Doha QNA in Arabic 0811 GMT 13 Mar 78 NC 
[Text] Kuwait, 13 March--The Kuwaiti newspaper AR-RA'Y AL-'AMM today carries a dispatch 
from Amman reporting that Libyan Chairman Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi will visit Jordan soon 
as part of a tour to several states in the area, 
The paper says that the Jordanian Government has received assurances indicating that the 
Libyan Government will resume Libyan financial aid to Jordan, The paper says that a military 
training team composed of 16 officers is currently in Libya and that the Libyan Government 
has requested that this team be increased to 200 officers.
