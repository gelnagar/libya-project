LIBYA

PALESTINIAN LEADER ARRIVES, MAKES STATEMENT 7 AUG 


LD081008 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 8 Aug 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 8 Aug (JANA)--Gasir ‘Abd Rabbuh, assistant secretary of the Palestine 
Liberation Front, last night arrived in Tripoli for a visit to the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


Vv. 10 Aug 81 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


He made a statement to a JANA correspondent in which he said that his visit would deal with 
the grave situation through which the Arab region is passing, especially after the Zionist 
aggression against Lebanon, backed by American imperialism. 


He explained that the Zionist enemy was seeking to wage a new aggression to occupy 
Lebanon afterits barbaric attacks against Beirut and southern Lebanon and before the 
steadfastness of the Palestinian and Lebanese resistance, which relied upon the support 
of the Arab nation and progressive national forces, especially the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


He stressed the need for solidarity with the people of the Jamahariyah in their confronta- 
tion with American imperialist threats. He said that all the Arab masses firmly stand by 
the side of the Libyan Arab people in their continuous struggle against the enemies of 
freedom and who are carrying out their pan-Arab duty in foiling all imperialist, Zionist 
and reactionary plots. 


In his statement he denounced the criminal terrorist plan prepared by the American 
administration to assassinate the leader of the revolution through its agents such as the 
rulers of Egypt and Sudan. He stressed that the revolutionary forces in the Arab homeland 
will firmly and resolutely stand [together] to destroy all American terrorist plans. 


TUNISIAN CHARGE WARNED ON ILLEGAL IMMIGRANTS 


LD091956 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1845 GMT 9 Aug 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 9 Aug (JANA)-—-The Tunisian charge d'affaires was summoned this morning 
to the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison and was told that large numbers of Tunisian 
citizens have recently been observed infiltrating Libyan Arab territory in a noticeable 
way, something which might compel the people's authorities in the Jamahiriyah to collate 
these numbers to study the possibility of taking advantage of the skilled elements and 
of repatriating the surplus number via the normal channels in accordance with the legal 
measures governing resident status, which have been in operation to date between the two 
fraternal countries. 


Out of a desire to maintain fraternal relations and good-neighbor relations, and in order 
to avoid any misunderstanding of these ordinary measures, the People's Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison arged upon (?the Tunisian charge d'affaires) the need toinformhis government of the 
Jamahiriyah's readiness to study any proposal which might contribute toward resolving 
the problems of infiltration, (?including) abolishing entry visas [as received] (?in the 
manner which would ensure the freedom of movement of the citizens between the two 
countries.
