Summary (AI): A Washington Star article reports that former CIA agent Francis E. Terpil, an arms dealer charged with supplying explosives and personnel to Libya for assassination and terrorist training, faces trial, and is linked to a photo with Billy Carter at a Libyan revolution anniversary celebration. Terpil denies being the man seated next to Carter.
• Terpil was charged with conspiracy to supply covertly and for profit the Libyan government with personnel, explosives, explosive material and other goods to make explosive devices and to train terrorists.
• Terpil was also charged in New York with state weapons charges and with unlawful transportation of about 5,000 bands of explosives with intent to conceal the purpose.
• Terpil's trial was expected to last about two weeks; a delay had been granted from early January.
• The alleged plot against Mu-hayshi reportedly involved a $1 million payment to kill a former Libyan Revolutionary Council member who had defected.
• Several intelligence sources were uncertain whether the man beside Billy Carter in the Time photo was Terpil; Terpil says the man is not him.
• 1980-07: Libyan revolution anniversary celebration; Billy Carter photographed in reviewing stand beside a man Time magazine identified as Terpil, which Terpil denies
