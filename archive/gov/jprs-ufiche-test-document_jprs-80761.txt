a number of important changes

Popular Committee, which per-

The new selection procedure 
will consolidate the direct demo- 
cracy practiced in Libya since it 
will accord a greater role to the 
Basie People’s Congresses. the 
local grassroots decision-making 
forums open to all citizens. In 
future, each Basic People’s 
Congress will nominate experts in 
the various sectors of the economy 
to serve on the people's com- 
mittees that administer the Libyan 
Secretariats. Under the jamahiri 
system, Secretariats carry out the 
Same functions as ministries in 
traditional countries. The people's 
committees will then choose the 
Jamahiriva’s  Seeretaries from 
their numbers. Previously. Secre- 
taries were chosen by the General 
People’s Congress.
