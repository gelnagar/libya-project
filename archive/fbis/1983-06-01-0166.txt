LIBYA

Syrian President Arrives on Visit 31 May 
LD311721 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
1600 GMT 31 May 83 


[Text] President Hafiz al-Asad, president of the Syrian Arab 
Republic, arrived in Tripoli at 1700 today. President Hafiz 
al-Asad was given a great welcome on arrival at Tripoli interna- 
tional airport. The brother leader of the revolution was at the 
head of those who welcomed him. 


The Brothers Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud; 
Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the 
Armed Forces; Brigadier Mustafa al-Kharrubi, chief of the 
General Staff of the Armed Forces; Major al-Khuwaylidi al- 
Humaydi, deputy chief of staff of the Armed Forces, also took 
part in welcoming the president. 


The secretary and members of the Secretariat of the General 
People’s Conference and members of the Popular General Com- 
mittee, »2ads of diplomatic missions accredited to the Jama- 
hiriyah, and officers of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces also took 
part in welcoming him. 


President Hafiz al-Asad is accompanied during his visit to the 
Jamahiriyah by a delegation including Brother ‘Abd al-Halim 
Khaddam, deputy prime minister and foreign minister; Brother 
Ahmad Dhiyab, member of the Regional Command of the 
Socialist Arab Ba'th Party; and Fayiz Isma’il, member of the 
Central Command of the Progressive National Front. 


Damascus Report on Arrival 
JN311751 Damascus Domestic Television Service in Arabic 
1736 GMT 31 May 83 


[Text] At 1700 today President Hafiz al-Asad arrived in the 
Libyan capital of Tripoli, accompanied by ‘Abd al-Halim Khad- 
dam, deputy prime minister and foreign minister; Ahmad Diyab, 
member of the Ba’th Party Regional Command; and Fayiz 
Isma‘il, member of the Central Command of the Progressive 
National Front. The president and his delegation were received 
at Tripoli international airport by Colonel Mu’ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi, leader of the | September Revolution; Staff Major 
‘Abd as-Salam Jallud, member of the 1 September Revolution 
Command; Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief 
of the Armed Forces; Brigadier Mustafa al-Kharrubi, chief of 
the Army Staff; the secretary general and members of the 
General People’s Congress; members of the General People’s 
Committee; heads of the diplomatic missions accredited to Libya; 
and a number of senior army officers.
