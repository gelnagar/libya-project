LIBYA

STATEMENT RELEASED ON DIPLOMATIC TIES WITH IRAN 
LD172205 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 17 Jul 79 ID 
[Text] The following press statement was released in Tripoli and Tehran: 


Following the continuous fraternal contacts between officials in the provisional govern- 
ment of the Iranian Islamic Republic and their Libyan brothers and colleagues, and 
following the recent visit of the Jamahiriyah's envoy to Iran andhis meeting and talks with 
officials of the Foreign Ministry of the provisional government of the Iranian Islamic 
Republic it has been decided that an Iranian delegation will visit Libya in the near 
future at the invitation of the Jamahiriyah to complete consultations on establishing 
diplomatic relations, and afterward, to take the necessary measures to open two diplo- 
matic missions in Tripoli and Tehran representing the two countries. 


AZ-ZAWI INTERVIEWED BY AMMAN AD-DUSTUR 


JN171347 Amman AD-DUSTUR in Arabic 16 Jul 79 pp 1,17 JN 


[Excerpts] Muhammad az-Zawi, assistant secretary general of the Libyan General People's 
Congress, left Amman yesterday at the end of a short visit to Jordan during which he 
conveyed a message to King Husayn from his brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 


In a predeparture statement, Muhammad az-Zawi, assistant secretary general of the Libyan 
General People's Congress, told an AD-DUSTUR correspondent that President al-Qadhdhafi's 
Arab tour was like a mobile Arab summit conference aimed at further developing joint 
Arab action and securing a firm Arab boycott against the Egyptian regime, Azg-Zawi 
stressed that all leaders of the states which President al-Qadhdhafi visited underlined 
the need to strengthen the joint Arab action and further isolate the Egyptian regime. 

He said that the few coming months will witness the fruits of President al-Qadhdhafi'ls 
tour. 


Regarding Libya's stand toward the Kuwaiti call to hold an Arab summit conference, Az-Zawi 
said that Libya favors the convocation of an Arab summit conference, Such a meeting is 
very necessary at present in order to further strengthen the Arab ranks and repulse 

the Camp David parties! designs. Such a meeting will also be requested to mobilize all 
Arab capabilities to serve the Arab nation's battle. He stressed that there is unanimous 
Arab agreement to hold an Arab summit conference, 


Asked about the suggested agenda of the proposed Arab summit conference, Az-Zawi said 
that if the Baghdad summit!s resolutions formed the minimum acceptable by all Arabs to 
repulse the settlement treaty between Israeli and Egypt, then the forthcoming summit 
must take initiatives and mark the beginning of an active confrontation of the plot, 
This could be done by further supporting the steadfastness front consisting of Jordan, 
Syria and the PLO.

Furthermore, the Arabs are requested to coordinate their efforts in order to abort the 
big conspiracy in southern Lebanon and, most of all, end marginal differences between 
the Arabs. 


Asked about Jordanian-Libyan relations, Az-Zawi emphasized that Libyan-Jordanian relations 
and cooperation are developing in various spheres. The Libyan revolution's relations 

with others are based on the firm principles of repulsing and confronting the imperialist- 
Zionist schemes and the ceitulatory course as well. Hence, Jordan's position, 
represented in its king, government and people, are highly appreciated by Libya and its 
leader. We highly praise Jordan's steadfastness, because this country forms the main 
gate leading to the emancipation of the occupied territories. 


Asked about the Iranian-Iraqi relations and its impact on the Arab cause and whether 
Libya is exerting any efforts to end the Iraqi-Iranian dispute, Az-Zawi said: Libya 
will spare no effort to solve the Iraqi-Iranian differences and secure good bilateral 
relations, for we are in the middle of a fateful battle and in need of Iraq's partici- 
pation in the battle. Furthermore, the Arabs will need the Iranian revolution's 
support. 


When asked about the Libyan stand regarding the missing Imam Musa as-Sadr, Az-Zawi 
answered: I would like to stress that this issue is always raised haphazardly by the 
hostile forums in order to divert attention from the Arab nation's main and basic 
issue. The issue of the imam is also used as a weapon to split the cohesion of the 
Iranian and Libyan revolutionaries. This is understandable because the cohesion 

of the two revolutions threatens U.S. imperialist interests in the area. Once again 
I would like to underline the fact that there is no disagreement between us and Imam 
as-Sadr. Imam Musa as-Sadr came to Libya at an official invitation. He was 
frequently seen in Libya particularly at the night of the September Revolution's 
celebrations. Suddenly he left on board an Italian plane. He was seen off by the 
Mauritanian ambassador to Libya as well as by other officials and by a representative 
of the Arab Affairs Department. 


Asked whether a date has been fixed for President al-Qadhdhafi's visit to Iran, Az-Zawi 
said: There is nothing that might hinder this visit. The two revolutions! viewpoints 
are identical particularly regarding the Palestinian issue and supporting Palestinian 
resistence. The two revolutions also condemn and reject the U.S. -Zionist-As-Sadat 
treaty. However, due to the current conditions in Iran and the preparations for 

the permanent constitution and elections, it will be more convenient to delay this 
visit until the end of this phase. 


Az-Zawi stressed that Libya places great hope on cooperation with Iran, with all 
Islamic states and with the nonalined countries as well in order to serve our sacred 
cause. Az-Zawl said that President al-Qadhdhafi will visit several European states after 
attending the forthcoming nonalined summit conference in Cuba.
