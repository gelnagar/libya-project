Continued Reportage on Bulgaria’s Zhivkov Visit 


Al-Qadhdhafi, Zhivkov Banquet Speeches 
LD071501 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 7 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Mar (JANA) — The brother leader of the 
revolution last night hosted a banquet in honor of Chairman 
Todor Zhivkov, general secretary of the Bulgarian Communist 
Party Central Committee and chairman of the State Council of 
the People’s Republic of Bulgaria, who yesterday began an 
official friendly visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jama- 
hiriyah. 


The banquet was attended by Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam 
Ahmad Jallud. It was also attended by the secretary and assistant 
secretary of the General People’s Congress, the secretary and 
members of the General People’s Committee, the secretary of the 
Popular Committee for the Municipality of Tripoli, members of 
the delegation accompanying the chairman and members of the 
diplomatic corps accredited to the Jamahiriyah. 


The brother leader of the revolution delivered a speech at the 
banquet. He started by saying: I have the pleasure this evening 
to welcome our dear friend Todor Zhivkov and the accompanying 
delegation. When we welcome him we welcome in his person the 
friendly Bulgarian people. We express our profound appreciation 
for the fruitful cooperation between the two peoples and I recall 
that your visit to our country a few years ago was an important 
step on the path of strengthening and developing cooperation and 
friendship between us. Also, I have fond memories of the visit 
which I paid to your friendly country last year. 


He said that these consecutive visits show our firm determination 
to develop and strengthen cooperation between our two countries. 
We are confident that cooperation between our countries will 
continue and will progress with the passage of time, especially 
sinceour two countries enjoy political and social stability, a model 
example to the world torn by political conflicts and struck by a 
world economic crisis. 


The leader dealt with the effect of the world economic crisis. He 
explained that the economic crisis has exposed the capitalist 
system and has confirmed its inevitable end, and has proved tht 
the capitalist system is the system of crises, unemployment, and 
war. Hesaid: Weare not surprised, therefore, when imperialist 
policies are directed toward war and confrontation to give a 
respite to the crisis which is strangling the capitalist system. We 
are not surprised that the deployment of medium-range nuclear 
missiles in Western Europe is accompanied by the emergence of 
the present economic crisis. What is being said about this matter 
is also being said about all the aggressive military activities which 
imperialism is carrying out in the world like the ones we are 
witnessing in Lebanon, Egypt, Sudan, Somalia, Chad, Central 
and South America. 


He added that all these activities, from which no continent in the 
world is free, and from which no sea or ocean is free, are natural 
products of the imperialist system which is facing this crisis. He 
stressed that world peace faces real dangers as long as the 
imperialist camp is still in existence on the surface of the earth. 
Consequently, we must not expect a peaceful policy from impe-

The leader said: If the American people reelect Reagan this is

sensible manner in the United States. He explained that the

therefore, Reagan could steer the American Administration once 
again in spite of this hot and bloody slap in the face. 


The leader pointed out that the economic crisis which the world 
is facing has shown the prestige and the soundness of the socialist 
course in the world. He said that the world is noticing that the 
socialist countries have not been affected to any appreciable 
extent by the economic crisis in comparison with the intense 
probleras suffered by the capitalist countries. The regimes which 
put their faith in imperialism are the ones which are being 
exposed to fierce tremors which in the future will bring about 
their end. 


The leader of the revolution explained that the regimes which do 
not follow the socialist course have been shaken by popular 
revolutions, revolutions of the hungry, the killing of scores and 
hundreds by police bullets and the imprisonment of hundreds of 
thousands because they asked for econoinic and political problem 
to be solved. He stressed that the Jamahiriyah has solved funda- 
mentally all the economic and political problems by the applica- 
tion of the socialist course according to Jamahiriyah theory. 


The leader said that Bulgaria, a member of the socialist 
community, and Libya, which solved its problems according to 
Jamahiriyah theory, enjoy a sound political and economic system 
that lets us proceed with confidence to build future and joint 
cooperation between us.

and exceed all our previous discoveries on land, something thai

wealth exceeding that of all the countries of the region and 
possibly in the world, in addition to a sea of sweet water discov- 
ered undergound which has led to the construction of the great 
man-made river which will change the face of Libya, God willing, 
regarding agriculture in a few years.
