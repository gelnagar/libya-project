LD150428 Tripoli JANA in English 1648 GMT 14 Oct 86 


[Text] Tripoli, safar 10, Al-Tomor 14, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The CIA has received of late 3 reports from its 
intelligence organs. They have participated in formulating a 
complementary plan to encounter the Soviet Union in the Medi- 
terranean Sea basin and Eastern Europe. 


The Lebanese periodical, A/-Anwar, in a report it has published 
recently says that the above plan aims at encircling the Soviet 
Union as a prelude to strike at specific targets inside it. 


These reports have placed emphasis on the importance and 
necessity of launching an aggression against Libya in a bid to 
occupy it forcibly to carry out the plan ana force the Soviets to 
retreat and get contained. 


The reports acknowledge that the aggression against Libyan will 
ensue results surpassing many times the Vietnamese war, apart 
from the violent repurcussions which will be far beyond the 
Middle East. 


The reports have stressed that this aggression will enable the 
Americans to control the resources in the Libyan Arab soil and 
to announce officially the involvements of USA in regional 
expansionist activities through offensive warfare. 


The periodical says that this plan discloses the objectives behind 
U.S. continuing concentration on Libya of late. This is of course 
goes far beyond the lie regarding sponsoring terrorism the inval- 
idity of which has been confirmed. 


The reports add that Libya is the pass-word for attacking and 
besieging Moscow in addition to the fact that this aggression will 
serve as message to the Soviets to say that the Mediterranean 
Sea is an international game.
