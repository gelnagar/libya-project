[Text] Jidda, Dhulqidah12, Aug 31, Jamahirivah News Agency -- King Fahd of Saudi Arabia 
said the Arab-Zionist conflict has reached new dimensions requiring new political 
methods, concepts and means to deal with it, following the Zionist invasion of Lebanon. 


In a speech before his Cabinet yesterday evening, he pointed out that the forthcoming 
Fes summit will concentrate on finding political means which would coordinate with the 


latest developments in Lebanon. 
t 


IANA political editor commented on his speech by saying that this Saudi proposal is 
aimed at talking the Arab masses out of the battle against the enemy and agitating for 
capitulationist plans which do not take into consideration the fact that the 
Arab-Zionist struggle is that of survival, not a struggle for borders, and that it is 
wiser to deal with the crisis through what he called the putting forward of new ideas 
and means for political action instead of fighting the enemy, evicting it from the 
occupied lands, standing against U.S. imperialism, withdrawing Arab [word indistinct] 
from U.S. banks and stopping supplies to its war machines. 


Thus, the Saudi proposal has no meaning nor content and is misleading. It is an anchor 
coming from an agent tool known for his crafty misleading methods, for his treason, for 
his imposition of the spirit of oppotunism and for his containment policies. 


Fahd's declaration that the Arab-Zionist conflict has reached new dimensions after the 
invasion of Lebanon, which he said require new political methods, concepts and means to 
deal with it, needs no further explanation. It essentially means that the core of the 
policies adopted by the pigs of the Arab peninsular from Riyadh remains the same. These 
policies are enough to emphasize their collusion with the enemies, their subservience 
and participation in the suppression of this nation and the denial of its rights.
