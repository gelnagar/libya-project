LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS CONDOLENCES ON BREZHNEV DEATH 


LD122028 Tripoli JANA in English 1945 GMT 12 Nov 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Muharram 26, Nov 12, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The leader of the 
revolution sent a message of condolences to the Central Committee of the Soviet Communist 
Party on the death of the Soviet leader Leonid Brezhnev. It reads as follows: 


I have received with great sorrow and grief the news of the death of the prominent 
Soviet leader Leonid Ilich Brezhnev. I would like to express to you, his family and 
the friendly Soviet people our deepest commiserations and those of the Libyan Arab 
people asking Almighty God to compensate the Union of the Soviet Socialist Republics 
for the loss of one of its most prominent leaders who led his country wisely and faith- 
fully throughout the past eighteen years during which he consumed all his efforts and 
energy to serve the USSR goals aimed at consolidating peace and achieving progress and 
prosperity. 


The life of the late Soviet leader Leonid Brezhnev was rich with a long list of exploits 
and humanitarian deeds whether on the Soviet, Arab or international levels. His era 
was characterized by more progress, prosperity and respect for the basic rights of the 
Soviet citizens and the persistent work towards the continuation of confidence between 
the Soviet peoples and all their various nations and beliefs. 


The Soviet leader has contributed to the development and strengthening of friendship 
ties based on a mutual respect between the Arab nation and the Soviet people and 
establishing more profound understanding inside the USSR of the Arab causes and sore 
support for the struggle of the Arab nation against colonialism, Zionism and imperialism, 


The leader Brezhnev has an important and significant role in bolstering the friendship 
between the Libyan Arab people and the people of the Union of the Soviet Socialist 
Republics, that friendship which is considered a good example for relations among 
peoples. 


The late president is considered one of the prominent Soviet leaders on the inter~ 
national arena as during his leadership, he tried with all means to consolidate world 
detente and achieve world peace and security to save humanity from mass destruction. 


And finally, repeating our most sincere condolences, I would like to convey our deepest 
grief and sorrow to the family of the deceased. 


Colonel Mu’ ammar al-Qadhdhafi 


V. 15 Nov 82 CENTRAL AFRICA
