[Text] Tehran--A 75-year-old man was executed in Kabul for carrying a picture 
of Revolutionary Leader and Founder of the Islamic Republic, Imam Khomeini on his 


person, Ayatollah Mohseni the leader of the Afghani Islamic Movement said in an 
interview. 


The Persian daily 'KAYHAN' recently carried a report on the interview with the 
Afghan Ayatollah who is also secretary general of the Afghan Liberation Front, 
but did not say when it took place. 


He said the Front which opposed the military occupation of Afghanistan by the So- 
viets was established during the reign of Zahir Shah, but had grown in stature 
since the 1978 Communist coup in Afghanistan. 


Mohseni said ‘slam had no borders and the chief concern of the Front was the pre- 
servation of the Islamic ideology. With this in view they had concentrated on 

liberating Afghanistan for Islam by undertaking guerilla attacks in areas such as 
Kabul, Kandahar, Herat, Mazhar and other cities where the Soviets were ensconced.

the farms to plant 1,000 feddans each season in corn and sesame and leave the 
remaining 500 feddans uncultivated so that its fertility can be restored. In this 
way, the fertility of a third of the cultivated area can be restored every 3 years.
