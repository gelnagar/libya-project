• The dinner banquet in Kigali was held on 16 May 1985 and reported by JANA on 17 May 1985.

TRIPOLI JANA IN ENGLISH 0950 GMT 17 MAY 85 

((TEXT)) KIGALI, 27 SHABAN, 17 MAY, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 

AGENCY -- PRESIDENT MAJOR GENERAL JUVENAL HABYARIMANA OF 

RWANDA LAST NIGHT HELD A DINNER BANQUET IN HONOUR OF THE LEADER OF 

THE REVOLUTION AND ACCOMPANYING DELEGATION. PRESENT WERE MEMBERS 

OF THE GOVERNMENT, THE PARTY AND DIPLOMATIC MISSIONS ACCREDITED 

TO RWANDA. 

THE LEADER OF THE REVOLUTION AND PRESIDENT JUVENAL HABYARIMANA 

EXCAHNGED SPEECHES DURING THE DINNER BANQUET. FOLLOWING IS THE 

LEADER'S: 

BROTHER PRESIDENT AND DEAR FRIEND JUVENAL HABYARIMANA, MRS 

PRESIDENT, DEAR FRIENDS OF RWANDA, MESSRS GUESTS PRESENT. 

I WOULD LIKE TO EXPRESS MY DEEP FEELINGS AS REGARDS THE CONTENTS 

OF YOUR SPEECH AND WOULD LIKE TO VOICE MY AGREEMENT WITH YOUR 

VIEWPOINTS CONCERNING AFRICAN AND INTERNATIONAL ISSUES WHICH YOU 

HAVE DEALT WITH IN YOUR SPEECH. I AM OVERWHELMED BY THE POPULAR 

RECEPTION FROM THE MEN AND WOMEN FREIDNS IN RWANDA EXTENDED TO 

US UPOON OUR ARRIVAL TO YOUR BEAUTIFUL COUNTRY, RECEPTION WHICH 

EXPRESSES THE WARMTH OF FRIENDSHIP BETWEEN OURSELVES AND THE DEPTH 

OF AFRICAN BROTHERHOOD LINKING THE PEOPLES OF LIBYA AND RWANDA. 

WE IN LIBYA ARE INDEBTED TO YOU BECAUSE YOU HAVE MADE SEVERAL 

FRATERNAL VISITS TO YOUR SECOND COUNTRY LIBYA WHICH UNFORTUNATELY 

WE WERE UNABLE TO RECIPROCATE IN FULL. THIS VERY VISIT IS ONLY 

A PART OF IT. YOUR VISITS WERE UNDOUBTEDLY RESPONSIBLE FOR PAVING 

THE WAY FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF COOPERATION BETWEEN THE TWO COUNTRIES 

AND THE DEEPENING OF FRIENDSHIP BETWEEN US. THE CONSOLIDATION OF 

PERSONAL RELATIONS AND THE LAYING OF COOPERATION BASIS IN FACT 

IS DUE TO YOUR VISITS AND THOSE OF YOUR WIFE TO LIBYA. I AM 

SATISFIED WITH THE DEVELOPMENT OF RELATIONS AND COOPERATION BETWEEN 

THE TWO COUNTRIES AND HOPE THIS VISIT IS A NEW FACTOR TO BOOST 

THESE RELATIONS AND COOPERATION TO A MORE ADVANCED STAGE. THIS 

MEETING BETWEEN US TODAY IN RWANDA CONSOLIDATES JOINT AFRICAN ACTION 

IN A WAY CAPABLE OF REALISING AND BRINGING CLOSER THE AFRICAN 

UNITY SOUGHT BY ALL AFRICANS. ALLOW ME TO SAY THAT I AM SORRY TO 

SEE THAT AT A TIME WHEN THE IMPERIALIST-RACIST THREATS INCREASE 

AGAINST OUR AFRICAN CONTINENT, REQUIRING UNITY OF EFFORTS BY AFRICANS 

TO CONFRONT THEM, WE FIND THAT RACISM AND ZIONISM HAVE 

REALIZED A GAIN BY INFILTRATING OUR RANKS LIKE IT IS HAPPENING NOW 

ON THE BORDERS OF THIS COUNTRY WHERE WE ARE TODAY MEETING, I MEAN 

BY THE OTHER COUNTRY ZAIRE. AS WE MEETING HERE AT AFRICAN LEVEL 

DIRTY AGENT MOBUTO IS MEETING WITH HIS RACIST MASTERS IN OCCUPIED 

PALESTINE. AFRICAN PEOPLE HOWEVER, WILL NOT LEAVE SUCH AGENTS 

WITHOUT PUNISHMENT. THE AFRICAN PEOPLE IN THE SUDAN HAVE PUNISHED 

STOOGE NUMAYRI WHEN HE COLLABORATED WITH ZIONISTS AND SMUGGLED 

FALASHAS TO OCCUPIED PALESTINE. HE COOPERATED WITH ZIONISTS HOPING

_THE LEADER WENT ON TO SAY: WE KNOW THAT COOPERATION BETWEEN THE

OF JOINT PRODUCTION OF NUCLEAR WEAPONS. THIS WAS DONE WITH THE

ITS INABILITY TO RESPECT ITS POSITION AS A WORLD SUPER POWER AND WAS 
AFFECTED BY MEGALOMANIA TO THE EXTENT IT BELIEVED IT COULD CONTROL 
ALL PEOPLES. WE HAVE HOWEVER HAVE DEALT IT A BLOW THAT HAS BROUGHT 
IT BACK TO ITS SENSES IN THE LEBANON, WHERE U.S. TROOPS FLED ON 
FOOT, IN HELICOPTERS, IN BOATS AND SWIMMING TO SEEK PROTECTION IN 
SHIPS. THEY FLED IN A RACE WITH THE FISH IN THE SEA OF LEBANON 
WHEN THE BLOWS OF THE HEROIC POPULAR RESISTANCE INTENSIFIED AND WHEN 
ONE LEBANESE DECIDED TO DIE AGAINST HUNDREDS OF AMERICAN TROOPS. 
WE HAVE LOST ONE MARTYR WHO BLEW HIMSELF UP TO KILL 300 AMERICANS 
IN THE LABANON. ANOTHER DID THE SAME AGAINST FRENCH TROOPS AND A 
THIRD AGAINST ISRAELI TROOPS IN SOUTH LEBANON. WE HAVE INFLICTED A 
HUMILIATING DEFEAT ON IMPERIALIST, AND RACIST ZIONISTS IN THE 
LEBANON, AND BEFORE THAT, THEY WERE DEFEATED BY THE SMALL VIETNAMESE 
PEOPLE. THE REVOLUTION IN NICARAGUA AND IN CUBA TRIUMPED IN 
SPITE OF AMERICA. IT WILL ALSO TRIUMPH IN EL 7SALVADOR,

FASCISM. WE PLACE OUR CAPABILITIES AT THE DISPOSAL OF RESISTANCE 
IN SOUTH AFRICA AND THE PALESTINIAN RESISTANCE IN ORDER TO INFLICT 
YET ANOTHER DEFEAT ON THE RACISTS IN PALESTINE AND IN SOUTH AFRICA. 
WE ARE DETERMINED TO DEFEAT THEM. ISRAEL WILL ULTIMATELY DISAPPEAR 
TO BE REPLACED BY PALESTINE AND SOUTH AFRICA BY AZANIA, AS DID

WHICH WAS REPLACED BY MOZAMBIQUE. YESTERDAY, FRENCH COLONIALISM
