LIBYA

PDRY'S ‘ALI NASIR ENDS TRIPOLI VISIT 6 JUL 


Departure Statement 
LD061348 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 6 Jul 81 


[Excerpt] ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, chairman of the presidium of the PDRY Supreme People's 
Council, left Tripoli this morning at the end of his visit to the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. He was seen off by Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and 
Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the Libyan Armed Forces, and others. 
He was seen off at Tripoli International Airport by members of the People's General 
Committee. 


In a statement to JANA, Chairman 'Ali Nasir Muhammad said: Our visit to the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has been successful and fruitful. I wish to express my 
complete satisfaction with the positive results of our fraternal talks with our brothers 
in the Jamahiriyah. Our views, he added, have been in agreement on these matters of 
common interest which we discussed. These include the widening of complete cooperation 
between the PDRY and the Jamahiriyah. The same applies to our evaluation of the nature 
of developments in the Arab and in the international situation. It was agreed that it 
was important that the Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation should be bolstered and 
support for the Palestine revolution, Syria and the Lebanese National Movement should be 
conti.ued, and solidarity be maintained with all the fraternal peoples and their good 
forces which stand firm in the face of imperialist plots in the area. 


He also praised the giant achievements made by the Libyan Arab people, thanks to the 

Great 1 September Revolution. He said these successes by the Jamahiriyah please all their 
friends. He added: We highly admire the Jamahiriyah's steadfastness in the face of the 
provocations of imperialism and Zionism, and we affirm our solidarity with the Libyan 
revolution and appreciate the rele it plays in bolstering and supporting the national 
liberation, Arab, and international movements. 


Joint Communique 
LD062138 Tripoli JANA in English 1820 GMT 6 Jul 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 Jul (JANA)--Following the visit to the Jamahirivah by the South Yemeni 
President ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, the following communique was issued in Tripoli and Aden: 


Upon the invitation by the leader of the Great al-Fatah Revolution, Colonel Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, President ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad of the Yemen People's Democratic Republic, 
and chairman of the Supreme People's Council and prime minister, visited the Jamahiriyah 
July 4-6, 1981 (Ramadan 2-4, 1390). 


President ‘Ali Nasir held talks with Colonel al-Qadhdhafi on current developments in the 
Arab arena and international matters of joint interest. 


Bilateral relations and ways and means of promoting them in the national interests of 
the two countries were also discussed. 


The two leaders noted with satisfaction the line bilateral relations have taken and 
affirmed the importance of co: colidating those relations. 


On the question of reuniting the two Yemens, the leaders expressed satisfaction at the 
progress made so far and affirmed the need to continue efforts along these lines until 
the noble aim of a united Yemen is achieved.

They considered the unity of Yemen to be a stabilizing factor for the people and countries 
of the region, as well as an important step towards a comprehensive Arab unity which can 
confront Zionism and colonialism. 


President Muhammad also praised the developments witnessed in Libya under the leadership 
of the Great al-Fatah Revolution's leader, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. He further 
expressed satisfaction at the role played by the Jamahiriyah in escalating the struggle 
against Zionism and colonialism, and affirmed Democratic Yemen's solidarity with Libya in 
facing colonialist, Zionist and reactionary provocations. 


President Muhammad praised Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's initiative declared at the eve of 
celebrations marking the llth anniversary of the evacuation of U.S. military bases from 
Libya. 


In it, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi had called for the mobilization of all efforts and for trans- 
cending all Arab squabbles in a bid to face American and Zionist conspiracies directed against 
the Arab nation's very existence. 


On his own part, the Great al-Fatah Revolution's leader, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
expressed admiration for the progressive transformations realized by the Democratic Yemeni 
revolution under the leadership of the Yemeni Socialist Party. He further saluted the party's 
patriotic and liberationist role carried out in support of the struggle of peoples in the 
region. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi strongly condemned the Omani sultanate's conspiracies directed against 
Democratic Yemen. Both sides therefore agreed to maintain their support for the Omani 
people's struggle under the leadership of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Oman. 


On political developments in the Arab region, both sides expressed deep concern for recent 
complications there, and attributed this to the savage colonialist-Zionist onslaught on 
the Arab nation's patriotic and progressive regimes. 


The two sides renewed their rejection of the Camp David accords and all other capitulationist 
initiatives aimed at liquidating the Palestinian revolutions. They agreed chat peace can only 
come to the Middle East by recognizing the legitimate and inalienable rights of the 
Palestinian people under the leadership of the Palestine people's sole legitimate representa~ 
tive, the Palestine Liberation Organization. 


The two sides also expressed support for the Lebanese people and patriotic movement 's 
heroic struggle against the Zionist-isolationist alliance in a bid to maintain the country's 
sovereignty, independence and Arab character. 


The two sides also praised Syria's heroic and glorious steadfastness against the Zionist 
entity and the reactionary conspiracies aimed at dealing a blow at Syria and ending its 
steadfastness. 


They declared their total support and backing for Syria in its confrontation of the Zionist 
entity and U.S. imperialism. 


The two sides renewed their total condemnation of the setting up of military bases in Egypt, 
Sudan, Somalia and Oman and stressed the need to work towards their dismantlement because 
of the great threat they pose to the Arab nation and the peoples of the region, since these 
bases ere a main starting point for aggression against the sovereignty and independence of 
the Arab nation and a military link complement ing the imperialist-Zionist military circle 
aimed at imposing hegemony and domination over the Arab homeland.

The two sides also reviewed the very dangerous consequences which might result from a 
longer Iraqi-Iranian war as it would give the U.S. imperialism the chance to step up 

its military presence in the region and it would do the utmost harm to the Iraqi and 
Iranian peoples. Therefore, they called for the need to work towards ending this war 
and solving this conflict through peaceful means to enable the Iraqi and Iranian peoples 
to contribute actively in the struggle against Zionism and imperialism. 


The two sides also reviewed the developments of the situation in the Western Sahara and 
stressed the need to hold direct negotiations between the Moroccan Kingdom and the 
Polisario Front in order to achieve self determination for the Saharan people. 


The two sides expressed support for the heroic struggle being waged by the Sudanese 
people under the leadership of its revolutionary forces against the capitulatory policy 
being pursued by the lackey Sudanese regime. 


On the question of intensifying opposition against Zionism and colonialism, the two 
sides agreed that the National Front of Confrontation and Steadfastness forms the basic 
stronghold in this matter and expressed the need to strengthen the front to enable it 
to carry its national responsibility to its optimum capacity. 


The two sides attached immense importance to Arab solidarity in the face of Zionism and 
imperialism and commended the efforts being exerted on this line. 


In their review of imperialist and Zionist conspiracies against the Arab nation, the two 
sides conceded the main enemy of the Arab nation to be the USA and the Zionist entity. 
They called on the Arab and other countries to take decisive political and economic 
sanctions against the U.S. which is collaborating with Israel in its terror campaign 
against the Arab nation. 


Regarding current events in Africa and the Yemeni side praised the Libyan Arab role in 
Chad and its contribution in restoring peace and security in the country. 


The two sides expressed support for the Chad people and its legitimate government. They 
condemned the imperialist and reactionary interventions in Chad's internal affairs. 


On the heroic struggle by the African people against racism and colonialism, the two sides 
condemned colonialist policies which are aimed at intensifying conflict amongst the coun- 
tries and people of the region in a bid to consolidate imperialist hegemony. 


The two sides expressed support for the Namibian people's struggle against racist policies 
pursued by South Africa. 


Regarding current developments in the Horn of Africa, both sides welcomed the decisions 
taken by the Organization of African Unity's summit conference in Nairobi. They also 
condemned the colonialist policy aimed at establishing military bases and interfering 
in the internal affairs of peoples of the region. 


On the current international situation, both sides condemned aggressive imperialist 
policies directed against the world's various aspiring people's and expressed deep 
concern for the imperialist escalating of tension and the arms race. 


The two sides saluted the unshakeable and principled stands taken by the socialist bloc 
in general and Soviet Union in particular vis-a-vis Arab causes and expressed support 
for the policies pursued by the socialist bloc for maintaining world peace and security.

The two sides affirmed the crucial role played by the Non-Aligned Movement in backing 
the popular struggle against colonialism, Zionism and racism, and in maintaining world 
peace and security. 


At the end of his visit, the secretary general of the Central Committee, the chairman of 
the Supreme People's Council and prime minister of the Democratic Yemen Republic, Mr ‘Ali 
Nasir Muhammad, expressed deep gratitude for the hospitality shown him and his accompanying 
delegation. 


Mr Muhammad also invited the Great al-Fatah Revolution's leader Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
to visit the Democratic Yemen Republic. Colonel al-Qadhdhafi accepted the invitation and 
will set the date of the visit at a later time. 
Issued in Tripoli on Ramadan 4, 1390, July 6, 1981
