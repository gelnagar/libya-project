Summary (AI): A January 1974 cable reports that Libyan Prime Minister Jallud planned a visit to seven European countries, including Austria, Switzerland, Belgium, Germany and Britain, to explain Libyan oil policy and discuss bilateral issues. The tour was described as part of an effort, begun during Qadhafi's trip to France and Yugoslavia, to strengthen Libya's relations with Europe.
• Countries named in the planned tour include Austria, Switzerland, Belgium, Germany and Britain; other countries unspecified.
• The primary purpose of the trip was to explain Libyan oil policy to Europeans and discuss other bilateral issues.
• The cable says Libya tried to keep its oil policy despite oil sanctions imposed by other Arabs.
• 1974-01: Libyan Prime Minister planned tour of seven European countries to explain Libyan oil policy
