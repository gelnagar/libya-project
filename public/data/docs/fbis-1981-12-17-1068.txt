HUSAYN INFORMS AL-QADHDHAFI ON IRAQ-IRAN WAR 


LD151410 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 15 Dec 81 


[Text] The brother leader received a message from President Saddam Husayn of Iraq through 
the Iraqi ambassador in Amman. In it, the Iraqi president condemns the negative stances 
of the reactionary Arab countries and informs him about the unfortunate situation existing 
between the two Islamic states, Iraq and Iran. It is known that Libya is exerting itself 
to the utmost to end the war, to halt the conflict between the two Muslim countries and 

to point all the weapons against imperialism, Zionism and reaction. It also hopes that 
its call to end hostilities between the two countries will be heeded.

NC151702 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1200 GMT 15 Dec 81 


[Text] Brig Gen Abu Bakh Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the Armed Forces of Arab Libya, 
today received PDRY Agriculture and Agrarian Reform Minister Brother Muhammad Sulayman 
Nasir, who gave him a message for the brother leader of the revolution from 'Ali Nasir 
Muhammad, secretary general of the Socialist Party, chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme 
People's Council and prime minister of the Democratic Republic of Yemen. The meeting was 
attended by the ambassador of the Democratic Republic of Yemen. 


Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir also received today YAR Agriculture and Fisheries Minister 
Dr Ahmad al-Hamdani, who gave the commander in chief of the armed forces a message for 
the leader of the revolution from YAR President ‘Ali ‘Abdallah Salih. The meeting was 
attended by the YAR ambassador to the Jamahiriyah. 


CORRESPONDENT REPORTS ON FIGHTING IN CHAD 


LDi30926 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 13 Dec 81 


[Text] The JANA correspondent in Ndjamena, in an urgent dispatch sent a short time ago, 
reported Chad Government forces repulsed the forces of Hissein Habre and Sudanese forces 
and fighting is now taking place 30 km west of Abeche. He said more wounded government 
soldiers arrived a few hours ago.
