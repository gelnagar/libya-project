Summary (AI): A 1985 Washington Post article reports the US expelled Libyan diplomat Farhat Tibar, attached to the Libyan UN mission in New York, over a Libyan-backed plot to assassinate Libyan dissidents in the US. It also notes a grand jury probe in Alexandria, Virginia, and a string of killings of Qaddafi opponents in Europe.
• The US expelled 26 Libyan diplomats and closed the Libyan People's Bureau in 1981.
• A federal grand jury in Alexandria, Virginia, subpoenaed 15 to 18 pro-Qaddafi Libyans in Virginia, Colorado, Michigan and North Carolina.
• Five Libyans were granted immunity after invoking their right against self-incrimination; no indictments had been returned.
• Four assassinations or attempts against Qaddafi opponents occurred outside the US so far in 1985.
• 1985-06-05: State Department ordered expulsion of Farhat Tibar, a Libyan diplomat attached to the Libyan UN mission in New York, declared persona non grata for activities incompatible with his status.
• 1985: Since February 1985, three Libyan opponents were killed in Europe and the former Libyan ambassador to Austria was wounded in an assassination attempt in Vienna. (3 killed)
