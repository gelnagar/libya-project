Summary (AI): Summary of a March 18, 1981 Senior Interdepartmental Group meeting on Libya, which proposed a US strategy toward Qaddafi's Libya. Actions discussed include Sixth Fleet maneuvers and reconnaissance flights in international waters, warnings to Americans against travel to Libya, pressure on US oil firms, a public campaign on Libyan excesses, and closing the Libyan People's Bureau in Washington. Nothing here is specifically about Tripoli sites.
• Meeting recommended a memo to the President requesting approval of a strategy toward Libya viewing Libya as a threat to US interests.
• Proposed immediate actions include reconnaissance flights and Sixth Fleet maneuvers in international waters and airspace claimed by Libya, preceded by a warning that hostile Libyan action would be met by force.
• Proposed closure of the Libyan People's Bureau in Washington, timing to be coordinated.
• Proposed stronger advisory notices warning Americans against travel to Libya and consultation with US oil companies to reduce American staff in Libya.
• Distribution included JCS, NSC, CIA, DOD, and DOE; document marked SECRET, eyes only.
