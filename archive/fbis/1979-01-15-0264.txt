LIBYA

WORKERS REPORTEDLY CONTINUE PRODUCTION SITE TAKEOVERS 
LD121510Y Tripoli JANA in English 1430 GMT 12 Jan 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Jenuery 12, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Workers continued to seize the 
companies, factories and production sites as urged by the leader of the great lst of 
September revolution, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadndhefi, who called upon them to liberate 
themselves from the enslavement of hire and the exploitation of employers end to apply 
the dictum "partners not wage workers," 


The working masses seized the following production sites where they formed people's 
committees: 


1.-Wadi al Zeit, contractors, Sabha; 

2-The post office, Sabha branch; 

3-Libyen Airlines, Sabha branch} 

4-Public Transport Corporation, Sabha branch; 
5-Alwahadiri Contractors, Sabha; 

6-Ali Ibrahim al-Alem Company, Sab’ia; 
7-Youssef Jarrah Quarry, Barrak ash-Shati; 
8-Kurkub Contractors, Darnah; 

9-Al-Bahr Tourist Hotel, Darnah.
