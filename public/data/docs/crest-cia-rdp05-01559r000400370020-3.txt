Summary (AI): An FBIS wire relays a Libyan (ARNA) report that Muammar al-Qadhdhafi addressed a multi-day Arab Socialist Union seminar in Tripoli on enlightenment, opened by Prime Minister Abd al-Salam Jallud. Qadhdhafi spoke on the move from revolution to state and the new democratic experiment through popular conferences and committees.
• Seminar was described as a three-day event on enlightenment for Arab Socialist Union members, with lectures on local and pan-Arab issues.
• Qadhdhafi's speech covered the transition from revolution to state and the new democratic experiment via basic popular conferences, the General People's Congress and popular committees.
• 1975-10-19: Three-day Arab Socialist Union seminar on 'enlightenment' opened by Prime Minister Abd al-Salam Jallud; Qadhdhafi spoke the previous night (19 October).
