Summary (AI): An FBIS London transmission (part 6 of 6) reports Qadhafi's 10 April 1985 press conference in Tripoli, covering his views on Jews, Zionism, Christianity, French rule in New Caledonia, a killing in Bonn, and North African workers in France. It contains no specific places in Tripoli, facilities, or casualty figures.
• Qadhafi stated Libya welcomes the return of Arab Jews to Arab countries and distinguishes opposition to Zionism from opposition to Jews.
• Qadhafi said Libya supports North African workers in France facing racial discrimination.
• Asked about a man killed in Bonn by other Libyans, Qadhafi said he did not know him and blamed Germany for him.

((TEXT)) ((AL-QADHDHAFI CONTINUES)) ON THE CONTRARY, WE WELCOME 
THE RETURN OF ARAB JEWS TO ARAB COUNTRIES. I HAVE ALWAYS DECLARE 
THAT ZIONISM HAS SACRIFICED THE JEWS AND IS NOW SACRIFYING THE 
ARABD; IT WILL SACRIFY AMERICA FOR THE SAKE OF ZIONISM, JUST AS 
THE CRUSADE'S SACRIFICED THE CHRISTIANS. WE ARE AGAINST THE 
CRUSADE SPIRIT BUT NOT AGAINST CHRISTIANITY. WE ARE AGAINST ZIONISM 
BUT NOT AGAINST THE JEWS. BUT THE JEWS SHOULD FREE THEMSELVES FROM 
ZIONISM AND THE CHRISTIANS SHOULD RID THEMSELVES OF THE CRUSADE 
SPIRIT. THANK YOU VERY MUCH. 
((QUESTION)) JANA SAYS THAT THERE IS ((WORD INDISTINCT)) TO THE 
FRENCH OCCUPATION IN NEW CALEDONIA. I BELIEVE THAT THIS IS WRONG? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) HOW CAN YOU SAY IT IS WRONG WHEN YOU KNOW THAT 
FRENCH TROOPS OCCUPY NEW CALEDONIA. THERE IS GROWING REVOLT AGAINST 
THE FRENCH PRESENCE. ((PASSAGE INDISTINCT) ) 
((QUESTION)) ONE OF YOUR ENEMIES WAS KILLED IN BONN BY OTHER 
LIBYANS. DO YOU SUPPORT SUCH AN ACTION? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) WE DO NOT KNOW HIM, WE DO NOT CONSIDER HIM 
HOSTILE, BECAUSE WE DO NOT KNOW HIM. HE IS AN UNKNOWN PERSON. 
GERMANY IS RESPONSIBLE FOR HIM. 
((QUESTION)) DO YOU HAVE ANY INFORMATION, OR DO YOU KNOW THAT 
THERE ARE PEOPLE TRAINED TO KILL THOSE WHOM YOU CALL TERRORISTS, WHO 
ARE NOW IN GERMANY? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) I HAVE NO KNOWLEDGE. 
((QUESTION)) BROTHER LEADER, I HAVE A QUESTION, WHICH I 
BELIEVE IS IMPORTANT, CONCERNING THE JAMAHIRIYAH. 
(( AL-QADHDHAFT ) ) WHAT IS IT? 
((QUESTION)) AS AN ARAB CITIZEN FROM MOROCCO, I WAS FORTUNATE 
TO SEE SOME OF THE GREAT ECONOMIC, SOCIAL AND CULTURAL ACHIEVEMENTS 
THROUGHOUT THE JAMAHIRIYAH. THEY SHOW THE EXTENT OF THE DEVELOPMENT 
OF THIS PART OF THE HOMELAND. THE GENERAL IMPRESSION I FORMED DURING 
THREE VISITS IS THAT THE AWARENESS OF THE GREAT ACHIEVEMENTS IS 
WEAK AMONG THE LIBYAN MASSES. HOW ARE YOU BUILDING THE NEW MAN WHILE 
BUILDING THE NEW SOCIETY? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) THIS REQUIRES STUDY. 
((QUESTION)) ((PASSAGE INDISTINCT)) . 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) WE DECLARE OUR SUPPORT FOR THE NORTH AFRICAN 
WORKERS, ESPECIALLY THOSE IN FRANCE, WHO ARE BEING SUBJECTED TO 
RACIAL DISCRIMINATION AND OPPRESSION. WE WELCOME OUR ALGERIAN, 
TUNISIAN AND MOROCCAN BROTHERS WHO COME TO LIBYA FROM FRANCE. IT 
IS A NATIONAL DUTY FOR LIBYA, THE STATE OF THE ARABS, TO PROTECT THEM 
AND FIND THEM WORK. I REGRET THAT THESE WORKERS DO NOT FIND 
SOMEONE TO DEFEND THEM IN THE COUNTRIES WHERE THEY WORK. 
(ENDALL) 110127 HAYMAN/JA 12/0003Z APR
