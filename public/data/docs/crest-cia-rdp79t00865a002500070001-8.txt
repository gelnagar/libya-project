Summary (AI): A 1975 CIA document, largely garbled by OCR, assesses that Qadhafi is adapting Libya's foreign policy after the Middle East setbacks, with a stated push to improve relations with Cairo and the Maghreb and a hope of regaining international respectability. It does not name any place in or near Tripoli.
• Assessment says Qadhafi's new flexibility appears born of necessity rather than conviction.
• Document says Libya's relations with Cairo were now its top priority, with ties to the Maghreb and other regions also discussed.
• Document notes Qadhafi's continued militancy toward Israel, antagonism toward the superpowers, and commitment to Arab revolution are suspected to persist.
• Document mentions a fear that the US intends to move against Libya, and a hope that diplomatic attention from Washington would help.
