LIBYA

BAR ASSOCIATION CONDEMNS TUNISIAN QAFSAH TRIALS 


LD231342 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 23 Mar 80 ID 


fText] The Libyan bar association issued a statement yesterday in which it commented 
on the attacks launched by the Tunisian public prosecutor at the court trying the 
Qafsah revolutionaries. The statement reads: 


The lawyers have followed some of the session of the Tunisian State Security Court 
through the Tunisian information media. In his address before the court, which is 
trying the Qafsah revolutionaries, the public prosecutor blamed Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the commander of the Great 1 September Revolution for 
this revolution and used them as a pretext to conduct acts of suppression, aimed at 
frightening the Tunisian people and distracting them from their chronic domestic 
problems, 


The statement affirmed that the bar association in the Jamahiriyah wishes to assert 
that the Great 1 September Revolution and the Libyan Arab people are determined tha% 
no agent Tunisian regime should rise, one that is hostile to it and on its western 
borders, They are determined not to permit attempts to besiege them from the east by 
the apostate, agent and defeatist regime, 


The collapsing Tunisian agent regime, the statement continued, as usual blames the 
Jamahiriyah for its internal problems and forcefully involves its name in any popular 
uprising by the oppressed Tunisian people, whose hands are fettered and who are 
explcited by the fascist terrorist clique whose only concern is to safeguard their 
temporary interests. 


The Jamahiriyah bar association, the statement said, feels that the pleading of the 
Tunisian public prosecutor cannot be considered legal for he has gone along with the 
trumpet of Zionist and imperialist propaganda, which have constantly condemned the 
Jamahiriyah!s national stands, intentionally disregarding the imperialist American 
invasion with a view to suppressing the revolution of the fraternal Tunisian people. 


The bar association statement affirmed that the ruling clique in Tunisia, which honors 
no pledge or promise, as can be seen from its failure to implement the Jarbah historical 
statement, which was signed by the Tunisian head of state, cannot be dealt with nor can 
the resolutions recently adopted by the Arab League concerning it be recognized. 


In conelusion, the bar association said that it believes that the Libyan people are called 
upon to work side by side with their brothers the Tunisian people to topple this agent 
regime, destroy it and prevent it from continuing in view of the danger it poses to the 
future of both fraternal peoples and the Arab nation. This must be initiated so that Tunisia 
will regain its Arab an?’ Islamic shining face within the framework of an immediate 

merger based on direct democracy represented in the peoplets authority; for the people 
possess authority, wealth and arms,
