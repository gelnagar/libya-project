LIBYA

SOMALI PRESIDENT SENDS MESSAGE TO AL-QADHDHAFI 
LDO351350Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 3 Jun 78 LD 


[Text] Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, member of the General Secretariat of the General 
People's Congress, received at the secretariat's headquarters at noon today 'Abd al-Qadir 

Ha jji Muhammad, member of the Somali Military Council. Brother Hajji brought Jallud a 
message from Somali President Mohamed Siad Barre. The meeting was attended by the assistant 
secretary for foreign affairs at the Foreign Secretariat and the charge d'affaires of the 
Somali Embassy in the Jamahiriyah. 


PFLP-GC LEADER JIBRIL ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 
LDO60720Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 5 Jun 78 LD 


[Excerpt] Ahmad Jibril, secretary general of the Popular Front for the Liberation of 
Palestine-General Command [PFLP-GC], arrived in Tripoli at noon on 5 June for a visit of 
several days to the Jamahiriyah.
