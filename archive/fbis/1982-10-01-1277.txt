LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS MESSAGE TO ARAB LEADERS 


PM301525 Tripoli AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR in Arabic 27 Sep 82 p 1 


("Text" of message sent by Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to Arab kings and heads of state -- 
date not given] 


[Text] A very bad occasion prompts me to address you once again on the same issue. The 

direct destruction of Arab existence with U.S. arms, as in the case of the mass killing 

of Lebanese and Palestinian Arabs in Lebanon by Israel, America's child epeiled at the Arehs' 
expense, no longer suits the positions of Your Majesties, Highness and Excellencies. f& 

is not right for the Americans and Israelis to persist in their insults and disregerd fer 

Your Majesties, Excellencies and Highnesses despite the unlimited Arab affection fer the 

United States and despite the million of barrels of oil supplied by the genereus Arabs to 

the Israelis as a contribution to the enormous Israeli war effort via the United States..____ 
The United States and the Israelis should have valued the friendship and massive oil 

supplies wich bind them to Your Majesties, Excellencies and Highnesses. 


I hope that you will reconsider your alliance with the Arab nation's enemy and also reeea- 
sider the question of the massive and cheap oil supplies which meet the needs of the enemy 
only because, on the basis of scientific calculations, you do not need it all. 


Forgive me if my message sounds like an irresponsible article. The reason is that I de net 
have the responsibility of a majesty, an excellency or a highness. And I am not respencible 
to the United States because I am not a U.S. employee with the grade of king, president or 
amir in the Arab region. Furthermore, responsibility is lacking in the Arab world. It is 
daily becoming more evident that nobody feels responsible for Arab honor and Arab cities 
except the Palestinian fedayeen, and only the fedayeen. 


I bring you the good news that the Arab revolutionaries will in the end be forced to make 
a serious decision. If we Arabs must sink, then we must sink by our decision and not by 
that of our enemies. By God, I have warned you. 


[Signed] Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 


JANA REPORTS PENTAGON ANNOUNCEMENT ON MARINES 


LD301858 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1835 GMT 30 Sep 82 


[Text] Washington, 30 Sep (JANA) -- The American Defense Department, the Pentagon, has 
announced that an American marine was killed today and two others were injured at Beirut 
airport. The American Defense Department spokesman did not give the real reasons behind the 
injuries of the American soldiers. However, he said, the incident might have been caused 
by a mine explosion. 


It is known now that an American detachment of 1,200 men started to land in Beirut today 
within the framework of the multinational force.
