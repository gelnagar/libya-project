An Eyewitness to al-Qadhdhafi's Thinking 


But I will linger over an important observation made by an Arab politician 
who met recently with Colonel al-Qadhdhafi in Tripoli, and who returned to 
say that he was astounded and amazed at al-Qadhdhafi's thinking, for he is 
living in a dream. 


He--that is, the colonel--believes that his thoughts will solve all the coun- 
try's problems, and that his theories will sooner or later be applied to the 
whole world. Thus he is prepared to do anything to carry out what is in his 
head--in Libya, at least, so it can be a model. He believes that his "Green 
Book" is better than anything humanity has published or known so far. In 
order to apply it, he has allocated a huge budget in petrodollars, and does 
not hesitate to use any means of removing any obstacle in his path. 


The visiting Arab political eyewitness went on to al-Qadhdhafi's thinking and 
the behavior which results from his visionary dreams, saying, "Therefore, the 
colonel lives in isolation, attacking in particular the politicians and in- 
tellectuals who do not accept his way of thinking. Therefore, he orders the 
murder and liquidation of political opponents, or those he considers to be 
opponents, among the refugees abroad, and launches concentrated operations 

to arrest lawyers, journalists and writers who have not stomached the theo- 
ries of the Green Book. 


"For example, 28 intellectuals accused of forming illegal organizations were

There is other testiomony to the resistance which has begun to break out.

The colonel is aware of all these dangers surrounding him, especially since

Libya, and no one can find out where he is.
