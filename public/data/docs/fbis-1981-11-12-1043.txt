LIBYA

FORCES TO BE 'NEUTRAL' BETWEEN CHAD FACTIONS 


LD111684 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1626 GMT 11 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 11 Nov (JANA) -- A source at the People's Foreign Liaison Bureau 
announced this evening that Arab Libyan forces have been completely evacuated from the 
towns of Iriba and Guereda in eastern Chad. Thus, Libya is no longer responsible for 

the defense of these regions, which the Libyan forces previously regained from the forces 
of Hissein Habre. Libyan forces have now completed their mission; they are adopting a 
neutral position between the Chad factions and they will not be biased on one or the 
other side. 


The People's Foreign Liaison Bureau hopes that the African forces will be able to regain 
those regions from the forces of Hissein Habre and Sudan where fierce fighting is now 
taking place between the forces of the Chad Government on the one hand and the forces of 
Hissein Habre and the Sudanese forces on the other hand. 


PAPER SAYS LIBYA IS TARGET OF U.S. AGGRESSION 


LD091614 Tripoli JANA in English 1441 GMT 9 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 9 Nov (JANA) -- The daily AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR, organ of the Revolutionary 
Committees, today denounced the violation of Libyan air space by American Sixth Fleet 
planes on 9 August last. It said, "This was no passing incident, nor was it an 
unplanned operation. Indeed, it was a basic preparatory move for a larger operation 
planned by the U.S. to render an actual and direct war with Jamahiriyah a logistically 
viable campaign." 

In its special weekly editorial entitled "Beware...America Is Coming," the paper 
reviewed American imperialist schemes starting with Vietnam and passing through the Arab 
region and regional wars in the world instigated by America, whereby the world has 
become a testing range for U.S. weapons in particular. 


The paper stressed that the world had actually entered a third global war. "The world 
today is not on the brink of war but has effectively entered such a war," it says 
referring to former President Nixon's book "The Real War," in which he pointed out that 
the world has actually started waging the third war. 


The paper went on to say, "It would not be surprising that the forthcoming joint 
maneuvres by America and its stooges would be a real prelude for a state of war. The 
AWACS deal with Saudi Arabia is nothing but a camouflage concretely agreed upon between 
the U.S., the Zionist enemy and Saudi Arabia. This was accomplished after extensive 
contacts with some Middle Eastern countries in order to out-manoeuvre the region's 
revolutionary and progressive forces." 


The paper added, "The fascist acts committed by America against the world liberation and 
revolutionary movements prove that America will not spare an effort in completing its 
fascist programme. And Jamahiriyah, in the coming days, will be a direct target of 
American aggression. This is agreed upon by all political observers of Middle Eastern 
afraizs.” 


In conclusion, the paper called on every Libyan citizen to brace himself or herself for 
a brave decision: "I am a free human being who would not let America or its agents 
impose a new colonial situation. The gravest test for all Libyans next week will be a 
test of their bravery in facing their enemies. The war is coming with its nuclear 
missiles, and America is coming next week with its arrogance." 


12 Nov 81 Q2 NORTH AFRICA 


AL-QADHDHAFI CABLES SUPPORT TO FIDEL CASTRO 


LD112224 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 11 Nov 81 


[Text] The leader of the revolution today sent a cable to President Fidel Castro, first 
secretary of the Communist Party and President of the Council of State and Council of 
Ministers of Cuba, denouncing the crude U.S. threats against the Cuban people and its 
revolution. He condemned the misleading U.S. propaganda campaigns and allegations 
against Cuba and said that the threats and the series of fabrications against Cuba are 
not new to U.S. imperialism, remarking that the United States previously surrounded 
Cuba and was defeated in the Bay of Pigs. The United States also previously threatened 
the security of the Jamahiriyah and attacked it on the Gulf of Sidra, thus violating 
the freedom of territorial waters and threatening the Libyan people. He stressed the 
Libyan Arab people's stand and support for the friendly Cuban people against the U.S. 
imperialist intrigues and plots. 


PUBLICATION NOTES ‘SPECTACULAR' NAVAL DEVELOPMENT 


NC061726 Paris AFP in English 1716 GMT 6 Nov 81 


[Text] Paris, 6 Nov (AFP) -- The "spectacular" development of Libya's Navy "modifies 
somewhat the strategic situation in the Mediterranean," according to the 1982 edition 
of the French Naval yearbook FLOTTES DE COMBAT (COMBAT FLEETS). 


The yearbook said that Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has acquired or ordered 
"very sophisticated" warships from abroad. 


Beyond four Wadi M'ragh-type missile-launching gunboats constructed in Italy and 
delivered in 1980 and 1981, the Libayn Navy has obtained a fourth Foxtrot submarine and 
two Soviet Natya minesweepers. 


Libya has also ordered 10 Combattante II missile-launching patrol boats from France, 
but delivery was postponed by the French Government. 


The yearbook said that Tripoli might also shortly receive two Koni frigates and four 
Soviet Nanushka II missile-launching gunboats. 


The author expressed doubt over the ability of Libyan personnel to operate and maintain 
the vessels "without permanent outside aid." 


Fifty-one French-made Combattante patrol boats have already been bought by the following 

foreign nations: West Germany (20), Iran (12), Greece (10), and Nigeria, Qatar and 

Tunisia (three each). The boats are armed with Harpoon, Otomat or Exocet MM38 self- 

directing missiles capable of hitting maritime targets 40-100 km (24-60 miles) away.
