LIBYA

LIBYAN, SUDANESE, CHADIAN, NIGER HEADS TO MEET 


JN210833Y Baghdad INA in Arabic 0820 GMT 21 Feb 78 

(Text ] Tripoli, 21 February--Informed sources said here today that they expect a 
quadripartite meeting to be convened among the heads of state of the Libyan Jamahiriyah, 
Sudan, Chad and Niger in Sabha city near the Libyan-Chadian border tommorow, Wednesday. 
The four heads of state--Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, Ja'far Numayri, Felix Malloum and 
Seyni Kountche--will discuss the question of settling the Libyan-Chadian differences. 


It was announced esxrlier that the meeting would be attended only by the Libyan and 
Chadian heads of state. However, the INA correspondent in Tripoli cited informed 
sources today confirming that Presidents Numayri and Kountche will attend the meeting. 
It was announced here yesterday that the fighting between the Chad rebels and govern- 
ment troops stopped yesterd-y.
