LIBYA

TRIPOLI NOTES EGYPTIAN STATE OF sMERGENCY DECREE 


LD162352 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 16 Jun 80 


[Text] The defeated Egyptian president issued a decree declaring state of emergency in 
his armed forces in the eastern desert area, Marsa Matruh, and all along the borders 

with the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. Egyptian officials in the vanquished 
capital stated that this area will be considered a military area from Wednesday on, and 
Egyptian civilians are banned from entering it, except with permits issued by the military 
authorities. The Egyptian Government news agency said that civilians who enter the area 
without permits will be tried by a military tribunal. 


POLISH ARMED FORCES CHIEF OF STAFF LEAVES 15 JUNE 
LD161102 Tripoli JANA in English 0810 GMT 16 Jun 80 


[Text] Tripoli, June 16, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The chief of staff of the Polish 

Armed Forces and member of the Central Committee of the Polish Communist Party left Tripoli 
International Airport last evening on his way back to his country on the conclusion of 

his visit to the Jamahiriyah.
