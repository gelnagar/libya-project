Summary (AI): placeholder
• The Libyan RO/RO ferry Garyounis was offshore Pointe Noire in October 1982.
• Pointe Noire is about 4,800 nautical miles from Libya's home port of Tarabulus.
• A smaller Derna-class ferry delivered medium tanks and rocket launchers to Mers el Kebir, Algeria, probably for Polisario.
• 1982-08: Garyounis reportedly resupplied Syrian or PLO forces at Tartus.
• 1982-03: Garyounis probably delivered armored personnel carriers to Ethiopia.
