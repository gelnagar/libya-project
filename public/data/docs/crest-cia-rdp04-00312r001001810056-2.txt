• Indictment charged the two men with conspiring to assassinate a Libyan defector and with teaching others how to make explosive devices, in a terrorist training project.
• Sources said the planned assassin was offered $1 million and was to make the murder appear CIA-sponsored; the American rejected the offer in Geneva after learning the US was not behind the plot.
• Several Libyans were reportedly killed and three American employees of Wilson injured in an explosion at a bomb-making training site, per the clipping.
• Wilson, 52, was believed to be out of the country; Terpil, 42, was arrested in Beltsville, Md.
• 1976: Wilson and Terpil allegedly shipped hundreds of pounds of explosives to Libya in 1976 and 1977 (per indictment).
