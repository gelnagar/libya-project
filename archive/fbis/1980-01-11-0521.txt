LIBYA

JANA EDITOR DECRIES FATAH COMMUNIQUE, SLIGHT ' ARAFAT 
ID101136 Tripoli JANA in English 0817 GMT 10 Jan 80 ID 
["JANA's Political Editor on Fatahts Communique" JANA headline] 
[Text] Tripoli, Jan 10, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCYe=Fatah leadership issued a communique in 
Beirut the 7th of January in which it attacked the Libyan Arab people and alleged that 
the Jamahiriyah had not offered any aid to Fatah movement since 1975. 
He said that had Fatah leaders been genuine revolutionaries they should have attacked 
Aswan negotiations between the defeated Egyptian president and terrorist Begin on the 
path of selling out and betraying the Palestinian cause. 
The editor said that from the first Fatah attack on the Jamahiriyah, it was clear that 
they had taken the same treacherous course as the defeated Egyptian president and that 


they have gone the way of capitulatory solutions, thus going far from the armed struggle. 


He said that now Fatah leadership has but to take up defeated AseSadat's speeches and 
the comments of his puppet press against the Jamahiriyah and sign them. 


The editor continued: We, in the Jamahiriyah, have tried hard to contain Arab popular 
rumours which aimed at naming Yasir "Arafat, Yasir Arafnak (Yasir we have known you") 
{quotation mark as received].
