Summary (AI): A 1974 White House Situation Room cable warns that Qadhafi may take anti-US action around 11 June, the fourth anniversary of the US departure from Wheelus Air Base, and may even sponsor terrorism before his scheduled public address. It also says Libyan leaders are uneasy about their troubled relations with Cairo, which may restrain him.
• Last year (1973), Qadhafi issued a bitter harangue condemning US reconnaissance flights off the Libyan coast and threatened a major showdown with American oil companies.
• The cable says Qadhafi may even sponsor terrorist action just before the anniversary celebrations to draw attention to his scheduled public address.
• Libyan leaders reportedly feared that attacking the US would further complicate their current problems with Cairo.
• 1974-06-11: Possible anti-US action by Qadhafi around the fourth anniversary of the US departure from Wheelus Air Base; the cable says 'JUNE 44' OCR, interpreted as 11 June 1974 only loosely, so treat exact day as uncertain.
