LIBYA

LEBANESE LEADER ARRIVES 3 APRIL, MAKES STATEMENT 


LD040859 Tripoli JANA in English 0807 GMT 4 Apr 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada Thani 21, April 4, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- Ibrahim Qulaylat, 
the head of the Independent Nasirite Movement-Al-Murabitun arrived last night in 
Tripoli. 


V. 5 Apr 83 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


In an interview with JANA's correspondent, Qualaylat said that the Zionist occupation 
of Lebanon undermines all the vital issues of the Arab nation, forefront of which is 
the Palestinian people's cause. 


In his statement he condemned the shameful negotiations between the Zionist enemy and 
the Lebanese Government and added that this was the result of the barbaric Zionist 
invasion of Arab territories. 


Qulaylat told JANA's correspondent that the Independent Nasirite Movement-Al-Murabitun 
is in total agreement with the position of Jamahiriyah as announced by Colonel 

Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the Great Al-Fatih Revolution, on the necessity to 
confront the Zionist occupation of Lebanon. 


QNA REPORTS POSSIBLE PURCHASE OF FRENCH AIRCRAFT 


GFO11146 Doha QNA in Arabic 1040 GMT 1 Apr 83 


[Text] Kuwait, 1 Apr (QNA) -- The Kuwaiti newspaper AS-SIYASAH reported today that 
France has started initial talks with Libya to sell it new advanced Mirage-2000 air- 
craft and other modern Etendard missile-carrying and launching aircraft. 


The newspaper added that in return, France is conducting studies and holding numerous 
contacts in preparation for negotiations on the sale of Libyan oil at very special 
prices. It said that the success of these negotiations depends on the outcome of the 
visit which "Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the General People's Committee for 
Foreign Liaison, will make to Paris next month. 


Quoting what is described as high-level diplomatic sources, the newspaper said French 
President Francois Mitterrand believes it is necessary to deal with Libya on more than 
one level despite the fact that the French president and Colonel al-Qadhdhafi hold 
dissimilar views on the Chad crisis.
