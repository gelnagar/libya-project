AL-QADHDHAFI RETURNS HOME FROM ALGIERS, TUNIS 
1D071552Y Tripoli JANA in English 1410 GMT 7 Feb 78 ID 


{(Text] Tripoli, February 7, JANA--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the 
revolution, returned to Tripoli last evening after taking part in the summit of stead- 
fastness and confrontation in Algiers and a brief stay in Tunis. 


In Algiers Colonel al-Qadhdhafi was seen off at the Algiers airport by President Houari 
Boumediene and members of the Revolutionary Council. 


In Tunis Colonel al-Qadhdhafi was received by Prime Minister Hedi Nouira. He could 
not meet President Bourguiba because of his serious illness. 


On arrival in Tunis Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said his visit was within the framework of 
consultations and cooperation between the two brotherly countries. He emphasized 
that the Jamahiriyah and Tunisia were one country, one nation and parts of the same 
body. 


AL-QADHDHAFI BLAMES CHAD FOR BREAK IN RELATIONS 
LDO71434Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 7 Feb 78 ID 


(Text ] Leader of the Great 1 September Revolution Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has sent 
messages to Gabonese President al-Hajj Omar Bongo, who is OAU chairman, Niger President 
Seyni Kountche, Togolese President Eyadema, and OAU Secretary General William Eteke. 
The messages concern the decision of the Chad government to suspend its political rela- 
tions with the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


In his messages, the brother colonel explains that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah welcomes any effort to normalize its relations with Chad, and that on the 
basis of mediation by President Kountche and President Eyadema and William Eteke, the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah had agreed to attend a meeting to be held 
between the Jamahiriyah foreign secretary and the Chad foreign minister in the presence 
of the Niger foreign minister in Niamey on 7 February. 


The Libyan Arab foreign secretary in fact arrived in Niamey on 6 February, but the Malloum 
government on the same day unexpectedly issueda decision announcing the suspension of its 
relations with the Jamahiriyah and it refused to attend the scheduled foreign ministers 
meeting. It asked the Jamahiriyah ambassador and members of the embassy to leave Chad as 
soon as possible. 


The brother colonel noted in his message that this strange behavior by the Chad Govern- 
ment shows its lack of respect of international law. It also proves its unworthiness 
as & responsible government and this action on its part is a personal insult to the 
African presidents who tried to mediate in restoring normal relations between the two 
countries,
