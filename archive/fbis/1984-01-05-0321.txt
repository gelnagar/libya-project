LIBYA

Palestinian Body Calls for National Conference 
LD041848 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1700 GMT 
4 Jan 84 


[Text] The Secretariat of the Palestinian Arab People’s General 
Congress today invited Palestinian Arab people's conferences all 
over the Jamahiriyah to hold an extraordinary session on Friday, 
6 January. 


The Secretariat explained that Yasir ‘Arafat's visit to Egypt and 
its grave consequences at all levels are the first item on the agenda 
of this extraordinary session. This is according to the resolutions 
of the Palestinian Arab people’s conferences, which were formu- 
lated by the Palestinian Arab People’s Congress which approved 
armed struggle as the only way to liberate Palestine. The meeting 
is called to confront the serious situation caused by this visit, and 
increasing public pressure to implement the people’s will against 
those who betrayed the people and the national Arab issue — 
Palestine. 


The Secretariat appealed to the Palestinian Arab people’s con- 
ferences to send recommendations and resolutions regardingthis 
session to the Secretariat of the Palestinian Arab People’s Con- 
gress within 7 days of the date of the invitation. 


French Foreign Ministry Official Visits 
LD040949 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 4 Jan 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 4 Jan (JANA) — Marc Bonnefous, minister 
plenipotentiary and director of the department of North Africa 
and the Middle East at the French Foreign Ministry, last night 
arrived in Tripoli for a visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah.

[Text] Tripoli, 4 Jan (JANA) — Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis
