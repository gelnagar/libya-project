Summary (AI): A 1979 DCI Alert Memorandum to the National Security Council assesses a possible Egyptian military incursion into eastern Libya, and its implications for Egypt, Libya, the USSR and the United States. It does not concern Tripoli or any specific Tripoli site.
• Libya expects an Egyptian attack, according to the memo.
• Libya's oil exports to the United States account for 10% of US oil imports, per the memo.
• Chances for a limited Egyptian success, such as reaching the outskirts of Tobruk, are assessed as good.
• The USSR would probably provide operational advice and logistic support to Libya and might undertake an airlift.
• Syria and Iraq possibly could send token forces in a prolonged conflict.
• 1979-08-01: Egyptian plan for a military incursion into eastern Libya reported as still on track; alert memorandum issued
