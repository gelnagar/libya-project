Summary (AI): A 1982 NPIC briefing board reports aircraft deployed at Brach Airfield 2 in west-central Libya, about 40 nautical miles north-northwest of Sebha, which is far from Tripoli. It describes seventeen L-39 trainer/ground-attack aircraft and a runway extension under construction.
• Seventeen L-39 Albatros trainer/ground-attack aircraft observed (not all shown on graphic).
• A 1,243-meter runway extension under construction at the southeast end; when complete the runway will be about 3,749 m by 60 m.
• A barracks/housing area of 100 house trailers/prefabricated buildings built about 200 m south of the aircraft parking apron.
• Brach is one of six new airfields renovated or under construction in Libya; the others are Ghadames East, Al Jufrah, Maktura, Al Wigh, Matan as Sarra (listed on graphic).
• 1982-02: Seventeen L-39 Albatros trainer/ground-attack aircraft observed deployed at the recently renovated airfield; aircraft possibly came from Okba Ibn Nafa airfield.
