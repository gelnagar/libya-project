[Text] The leader of the revolution and the Bulgarian leader, Todor Zhivkov, first secre- 
tary of the Bulgarian Communist Party and chairman of the State Council of the People's 
Republic of Bulgaria, held a long closed meeting this morning at the Peoplets Palace in 
Tripoli. Todor Zhivkov and the accompanying delegation left Tripoli this morning at the 
end of the cordial 4-day visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. At 
Tripoli international airport; he was seen off by the brother leader of the revolution 

as well as brothers Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, Lt Col Mustafa al-Kharrubi and 
Major al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi. 


At Tripoli international airport the Bulgarian chairman made a statement to the reporters 
in which he underlined the great achievements realized by the great al-Fatah revolution 
in all fields. He said: I would like to point out that we have sensed the important 
achievements which have transformed the Jamahiriyah entirely into a workshop where the 
Libyan Arab people, under the leadership of Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, are making great 
achievements and firmly establishing the people’s democracy and socialism. 


The Bulgarian chairman expressed his profound satisfaction with the warmth of the recep- 
tion accorded to him. He described the talks ani the meetings he had with the brother 
the leader of the revolution as extremely fruitful, and said the joint statement will 
reflect these successes, In conclusion Chairman Zhivkov stressed that Bulgaria will do 
its utmost for the deepening of relations of friendship and cooperation which have been 
consolidated further in this visit. 


The leader of the revolution spoke to the press delegation accompanying the guest chair- 
man and described this visit as important and extremely successful, and said it will con- 
solidate the friendship and cooperation between our two countries, He said: On this 
occasion I would like to convey my greetings to the friendly Bulgarian people. In conclu- 
sion the leader of the revolution said that understanding and joint cooperation will be 
reflected in the joint statement which will be issued about this visit.

[Text] March 15, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Following the cordial visit by Todor Zhivkov, 
first secretary of the Bulgavian Communist Party and president of the State Council, a 
joint Libyan Arab-Bulgarian communique was issued in both Tripoli and Sofia yesterday 
evening. The following is the text: 


Upon an official invitation by Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the grand al-Fatah 
revolution, His Excellency President Todor Zhivkov the first secretary of the Bulgarian 
Communist Party and president of the State Council of the People's Republic of Bulgaria, 
paid a cordial and official visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah from 
March 11-14, 1980, 


The Bulgarian president was warmly received by Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and the Libyan Arab 
people, reflecting the solidarity of existing friendship between the two peoples. The 
Bulgarian president visited several economic projects and historic sights and acquainted 
himself with the achievements of the Libyan Arab peoples. He was met most hospitably 
everywhere he went. 


The two leaders held talks in a cordial atmosphere of understanding and friendship, e- h 
leader in turn acquainted the other of the achievements attained by his country. They 
discussed existing bilateral relations and ways of developing and strengthening them, 
They exchanged points of view on international affairs of joint interest paying special 
concern to the Palestinian question, 


President Zhivkov paid tribute to the great achievements attained by the Libyan Arab 
people towards the objectives of the grand al-Fatah revolution, This is shown by the 
implementation of direct democracy and the achievement of people's authority. He 
praised the role played by the Jamahiriyah in the achievement of Arab unity and in the 
struggle against imperialism, neo-colonialism, racism and Zionism, 


The Bulgarian preside1.t condemned imperialist and (?reactionary) threats against the 
Jamahiriyah. He asserted Bulgaria’s stand by the Libyan Arab people in the face of 
such threats. 


The Libyan Arad side expressed appreciation for the support offered by Bulgaria and the 
socialist bloc to the Jamahiriyah against colonialist and reactionary aggression. 

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi expressed his great appreciation for the Bulgarian people's success 
in adopting advanced socialism and a consistent and constructive policy aiming to achieve 
peace, security and cooperation not only in the Balkan region, but in Europe and the 
world at large. Colonal al-Qadhdhafi expressed his appreciation of the significance of 
the 1,300th anniversary of the establishment of the Bulgarian state, an event to be cele- 
brated by Bulgaria next year. 


The two leaders spoke of their gratefulness for the contimed promotion of bilateral 
relations begun with an earlier communique signed in Tripoli on 25 March 1976 to 
promote bilateral cooperation and mutual friendship.

They expressed their eagerness to increase and develop joint cooperation beneficial to 
the two sides. They stressed the importance of maintaining contacts with the objective 
of strengthening bilateral relations and to conduct talks at definite intervals and at 
all levels to discuss issues concerning the two sides. 


The two leaders reviewed the implementation of economic, scientific and technological 
cooperation agreements concluded in Sofia on 20th June 1978 to promote cooperation from 
1981 until 1985. This agreement, they pointed out, constitutes a sound basis for in- 
ereasing cooperation between the two countries. A positive review was carried out 
involving the activities of the Libyan Arab-Bulgarian committee for economic, scien- 
tific and technological cooperation. The two heads of state pointed out that agree- 
ments and cooperation have increased remarkably during the last few years and that the 
rapid progress of the Jamahiriyah and Bulgarian people would offer ample opportunities 
to increase this cooperation both in quality and quantity. With this in mind, they 
requested the Libyan Arab-Bulgarian committee for economic, scientific and technologi- 
cal cooperation and all planning bodies to draw up a specific programme to expand this 
cooperation in line with the two countries! development plans as of 1981 until 1985 and 
to give new impetus and a new stable framework for economic relations with special 
emphasis on the following fields of cooperation: Agriculture, irrigation, industriali- 
sation of agricultural products, construction and industrial buildup, storage, other means 
of trade, the setting up of public health establishments, the supply of transport equip- 
ment, tourism, communication, exchange of technical expertise and training. The objec- 
tive of this cooperation is to bring trade between the two countries to required standard 
by 1985. The two leaders reviewed the current international situation, expressing their 
satisfaction at having identical views concerning the main international issues. They 
expressed their determination to spare no efforts in bringing about peace and security 
al? over the world, disarmament, and detente and to end the arms race and tension. 


The two leaders expressed their deep concern towards increasing international tension 
recently, and the policies adopted by imperialist and reactionary forces escalating the 
arms race, ami their continuous interference in the internal affairs of other countries, 
They declared their determination to contime the struggle in cooperation with progres- 
sive anti-colonialist and peace loving forces in order to reinforce defence and maintain 
the security ani peace of the world, 


They stressed the right of peoples to defend their freedom and support their own choices, 
The two sides referred to the necessity of taking active measures for disarmament, and 
maintain the principles of equality in rights and security. 


They showed particular interest in the dangerous developments that took place recently 
in the Arab region, and declared their condemnation of the Stable David accords, and the 
separate agreements which the Egyptian regime signed with the Zionist entity, and the 
normalization of relations between them, which poses danger to the basic interests of 
the Arab nation. They condemned the tripartite talks held by the Egyptian regime, the 
Zionist entity and the United States on so-called Palestinian self-autonomy, for giving 
a form of legality to (Israeli) occupation and settlement in the Arab lands being 
occupied by the Zionist entity. ‘

They expressed their concern towards the imperialist attempts to divide the Arab people 
and lead them through the path of surrender, They affirmed their belief that the Arab 
progressive forces will foil all these attempts. 


The two leaders expressed their belief that the just and peaceful settlement of the 
Palestinian cause cannot be achieved except through the full and unconditional with- 
drawal of (Israel) from all occupied lands and without the realization of the 
legitimate rights of tne Palestinian Arab people, including their right of self- 
determination and the establishment of their independent state in their land, They 
also expressed their belief that no sett”°ement could be reached in the area without 
the participation of all concerned parties including the Palestinian people. 


The two sides praised the stands of the front of confrontation and steadfastness which 

was established in Tripoli and its anti-imperialist and anti-Zionist resolutions. They 
expressed their hope that Arab efforts be directed towards the implementation of these 

resolutions. 


They expressed their belief that the positioning of the imperialist forces in the Arab 
Gulf and the Indian Ocean and the military threats constitute threats not only to the 
freedom and independence of the peoples of the Arab area and Southeast Asia, but also 
to the peace and security of the world as a whole, They strongly denounced the designs 
of the USA and its construction of aggressive regimes in the Arab region and Southwest 
Asia in order to strike at national liberation movements and exploit the natural 
resources of the countries of the region, 


The two sides denounced the brutal campaign launched by imperialism exploiting the 
events of Afghanistan to find a foothold in the Arab region. In this context they 
denounced the imperialist attempts to intervene in the internal affairs of Southwest 
Asia, They affirmed their respect for the will of the Afghan people and they affirmed 
their support for their independent progressive revolution. 


The two leaders expressed their determination to support the just struggle waged by the 
people of Namibia and Zimbabwe and greeted the victory of the Zimbabwean Patriotic 
Front in the elections, They declared opposition to a11 forms of manoeuvers by 
imperialism and racism to obstruct the establishment of a free independent state in 
Zimbabwe, They furthermore insisted on the need for the unconditional withdrawal of 
the South African forces from the land they illegally occupy in Namibia and for the 
need for the implementation of UN resolutions in this respect to achieve the inde- 
pendence of this African state. They denounced the racist policy pursued by the South 
African regime towards its independent neighbouring state. They announced their 
determination for the need to liquidate colonialism and racism, 


They stressed the necessity to seek a solution for the issue of the Western Sahara 
according to the United Nations resolutions, including the right of its people to self- 
determination, They also condemned imperialist intervention in the affairs of the 
African Continent, especially against its independent states to obstruct their progres- 
sive advance and national independence, 


Both sides support the initiatives of Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia which aim at developing 
the links of neighbourhood, and cooperation among the South Asian countries calling for 
peace and security in the region,

The two sides welcomed the steps taken by the socialist countries to ease military 
tension and disarmament in Europe, and they gave special interest to the necessity of 
applying the spirit of the final decisions of the European Cooperation and Security 
conference which was held in Helsinki, as a long-term programme for ali countries that 
participated. They expressed their hope that the forthcoming conference of Berlin 
would be held in a constructive atmosphere, and achieve the expected results. In this 
course they expressed their concern towards the attempts made by the reactionary forces

The two leaders agreed to transform the Mediterranean Sea into a peaceful zone of

The two leaders expressed the importance of the non-aligned movement in lessening

The communique attached importance for the need to reconstruct international relations 
in the mass media in order to liquidate media monopolies and to disseminate true in- 
formation about the countries concerned, 


The Libyan Arab side praised the role played by the socialist block in the maintenance 
and consolidation of peace, disarmament, harmony, and in promoting international 
cooperation, the just Arab cause together with that of the newly independent and 
developing countries and of national liberation movements, 


The two sides expressed strong belief that the basis and unity of action amongst 
progressive Arab forces and their friendship and cooperation with the socialist bloc 
has been of great significance, They are, therefore, determined to participate in 
developing cooperation between the socialist bloc and developing and non-aligned 
countries, 


The two sides thought highly of the results of the visit by President Zhivkov of 
Bulgaria to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and. noted its contribution 
to the improvement and consolidation of bilateral cooperation. 


President Todor Zhivkov of Bulgaria, first secretary of the Central Committee of the 
Bulgarian Communist Party and president of the State Council of the People's Republic 
of Bulgaria, expressed his gratitude to Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi and the Libyan 
Arab people for the generous hospitality he received during his visit. He called on 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi to visit him officially in return. The invitation was accepted, 
put no date has been appointed as yet.

{[Text] Tripoli, 26 March, JAMAHIRTYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
leader of the revolution affirmed that he is confident that the people can lead them- 
selves by themselves, 


He said: "The people's authority cannot turn to chaos. If the masses are sure that 
they own authority and they are free, then they will respect this authority ani 
shoulder the responsibility of freedom, and will behave in a way that will not harm 
their interests and future and will take wise and positive resolutions," 


The leader said: "The peoples will has been positively assured through the initiative 
of the masses of the people*s congresses which changed the administrative map of the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah by perfect people's resolutions, This was 
strengthened by the amalgamation of municipalities and branch municipalities," 


In his speech today in which he incited the masses to affirm am strengthen their 
masses, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi greeted the people’s congresses which reached this 

degree of awareness and responsibility and which can determine for themselves and 
take very important decision, The speech was transmitted by radio and television. 


He affirmed that this is the answer to any one who is doubtful about the people's will 
because the people are ruled without a government and that people's power was estab- 
lished without an outsider and superviser, the people being the supervisers and the 
masters of themselves, 


He elaborated that there is no need for any mandate on the masses or to speak on their 
behalf he affirmed that the masses realized their interests and took the necessary 
measures which suit them, He added that the more positive and correct resolution are 
adopted by the people's congresses, the more the people's will and the masses confidence 
are consolidated, 


He explained that in the case where the masses adopted wrong decisions the result would 
be negative and would weaken the popular will, a matter which forces the masses to seek 
a leadership, and thus dictatorship returns, 


He said: "I always trust the masses, The decisions of the inhabitants of the 
municipalities that integrated are nothing but evidence of the popular will which 
is enforced and the people's authority which is confirmed day by day," 


He added that these decisions refute and dismiss the justifications of the governments 
which dominate their peoples under the pretext that they are not capable of governing 
themselves and that they need dictators to govern them all the time, In this respect 
he explained that these styles date back to the pre-Jamahiriyah period, being violent 
and oppressive styles which will eventually bring about the revolt of the masses in a 
popular revolution to be instigated by the revolutionary committees everywhere. He 
went on to say that if the revolution succeeded in Libya then it would encourage the 
peoples to make a revolution thus putting an end to the era of dictatorship and 
starting the era of the masses,
