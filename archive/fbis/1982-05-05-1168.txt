LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI OFFERS SYMPATHY ON BEN YAHIA DEATH 


LD050818 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 5 May 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 5 May (JANA) -- The brother leader telephoned the president of Algeria, 
Chadli Bendjedid yesterday. 


The leader of the revolution offered President Chadli Bendjedid condolences and sympathy 
from the Libyan Arab people on the death of the late Mohamed Seddik Ben Yahia, minister 
of state for foreign affairs, whose aircraft crashed the night before last close to the 
Iranian-Turkish border. 


AL-QADHDHAFI SCORES REPORTED MOROCCO-EGYPT TALKS 


LD042008 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 4 May 82 


[Text] The brother commander of the revolution made a statement to JANA following his 
meeting with Brother Yasir ‘Arafat in Tripoli today. He said the reports about a visit 
to Egypt by the Moroccan foreign minister and the open talk about attempts to restore 
relations between the two countries come as a great surprise indeed, unless the Moroccan 
king has been entrusted with the role of the executed traitor Anwar as-Sadat and unless 
he has chosen for himself a similar fate. 


Should it take place, such a step will mean that the king of Morocco has committed an act 
of high treason, that he has recognized the enemy through Egypt and that he too has 
bartered Palestine for the Sinai. However ignorant one might be of the world and particu- 
larly the Arab world, one cannot fail to realize that the Arab nation has blacklisted 

Egypt only because of its recognition of the enemy, who still occupies Palestine, makes 
its people destitute and ended the state of war with it, anc for no other reason. The 
apparent [shakli] evacuation from the Sinai, which in reality is still under Israel's 
domination, does not remove the reason that led the Arabs to boycott Egypt. 


In conclusion, I cannot believe that the king of Morocco would permit himself to do so 

in front of the Palestinian people who are persecuted every day and in front of his people 
and the other peoples of the Arab nation, and to violate the Arab League Charter, con- 
travene the resolutions of the Baghdad conference, inherit As-Sadat's high treason, 

expose himself to the same fate as As-Sadat and rob his country of all its qualifications 
to host any Arab summit conference or play any Arab role. 


Morocco's tendency to grant military bases to the United States and enter the Camp David 
[agreement] through Egypt force Libya to reconsider the position it declared on 11 June 1981. 


The brother commander of the revolution today received Brother Yasir ‘Arafat, PLO Executive 
Committee chairman, in the presence of Brig ‘Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of 
the armed forces, and Brother ‘Abu Tariq, PLO representative in the Jamahiriyah.
