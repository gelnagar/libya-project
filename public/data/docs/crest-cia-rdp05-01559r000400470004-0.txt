LIBYA UPGRADES AIR DEFENSES WITH NEW MISSILE SITE ON GULF
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp05-01559r000400470004-0
https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-cia-rdp05-01559r000400470004-0

Summary (AI): A June 1986 Washington Post report (catalogued in CIA CREST) says Gadhafi upgraded Libya's air defenses with a second SA-5 missile site at Benghazi on the Gulf of Sidra, with Soviet help. A companion Post dispatch from Tripoli says Gadhafi appeared dazed and drew a dwindling audience in a June 12 television speech marking the 1970 closing of Wheelus Air Base.
• Second SA-5 site at Benghazi on the Gulf of Sidra was partially operational; the original Surt site had all 12 launchers back in service.
• The April 15, 1986 US night raid found Libyan defenses unprepared; Libyan runway lights were left on during the bombing.
• Gadhafi's June 12, 1986 speech was delivered from a secret television studio, with no audience, marking the anniversary of the 1970 Wheelus closing.
• Libya's population was cited as 3.5 million.
• Syria sent a delegation of air defense experts to Libya after the first Navy attacks in the Gulf of Sidra.
