published 19 June 1956 

NIE 36, 5-56 has generally held up well and remains useful as 
a basic estimative analysis of Libya and US interests therein. 
However, it has to some extent been outdated by the recent British 
decision to make drastic reductions in its military and financial 
commitments in Libya and other base areas and by the repercussions 
of the Suez crisis. It should therefore be used only in conjunction 
with SNIE 36.5-57, in which these and other later developments are 
taken into account,
