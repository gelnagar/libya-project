MEMORANDUM FOR THE CRISIS PRE-PLANNING GROUP (CPPG) 

SUBJECT: Plans to Counter Libyan Threats in the U.S. (S) 

At the CPPG meeting on Monday, January 13, the Chairman tasked 
the Operations Sub-Group (OSG) of the TIWG to make a 
recommendation on whether or not to take action against the 
People's Committee for Libyan Students (PCLS) in McLean, 
Virginia. The view of the CPPG was that a failure to take action 
against the PCLS damages our political position on sanctions 
against Qadhafi. (S) 

In response to this tasking, the OSG reviewed current FBI 
operations and activities aimed at the PCLS and proffers the 
following views and recommendations to the CPPG: 

25X11 

-- Our policy of sanctions against Qadhafi does, however, offer 
an opportunity for better control over the operations of the 
PCLS. Accordingly, the OSG recommends the following course 
of action: 

- No new student visas should be issued. Since we have 
little other choice, those here on valid student visas 
should be allowed to complete their course of study. 

- The Department of Treasury should place restrictions/ 
controls on funds being moved from Libya for use by 
Libyan students here in the U.S. 

- The PCLS should be required to register the intended 
use for all funds transferred into or out of its 

accounts. Specific limits should be placed on 
transactions in the PCLS accounts.
