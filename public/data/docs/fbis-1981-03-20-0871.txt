LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES UNIVERSITY GRADUATES 


LD192148 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 19 Mar 81 


[Text] The brother commander attended an educational ceremony held at the Al-Fatah 
University this evening for the distribution of graduation certificates to the 1978-80 
graduates who have gained their MA degrees. 


The commander of the revolution delivered a speech at the ceremony. He congratulated 

the brother graduates, saying that whenever a new group of students graduates we feel 

an increase in our independence in numerous fields. The battle of education, he said, is 
the primary battle man fights to triumph over the problems that face him. He affirmed 
that without education and the unmasking of its laws there can be no civilization or 
progress. 


The commander of the revolution said: We are approaching a cultural revolution which 
will demolish the traditional forms of education and the traditional educational institu- 
tions which were inherited from the reactionary society. 


Addressing the students and graduates he said: The battle demands from you to use all 
the weapons you possess so that we may triumph in this battle as we did in all the pre- 
vious battles. By our triumph in this cultural battle, the commander said, we will be 
able to build a framework for a new kind of education compatible with the new jamahiri 
society--a society in which we hope all man's ambitions will be realized and man will 
attain the stage of creativity and che unleashing of his capabilities, for then he will 
be a free man. 


The brother commander of the revolution pointed out that education as it is today, with 
its present institutions, is contrary to the structure of the new jamahiri society, which 
has evolved as a result of the constant escalation of the revolution and as a result also 
of the new changes that have taken place in the political, economic and social fields 

and which have, in the end, toppled the old society. That old society, he said, was 
based on exploitation and repression which in turn flourished at the expense of the 
freedom of man and society.

With the destruction of the traditional educational framework, he said, a new type of 
education with new frameworks in line with the nature of the new society, the society 

of the masses, will be built. The brother commander added: This battle is yours, and 
fighting it is your responsibility. He who fails to fight in this battle, he said, is 

a coward. This is because we are actually marching forward toward the establishment 

of the jamahiriyah in which all matters are resolved in a manner which servies the interests 
of the popular masses and in which we retrieve all that was lost for many long centuries. 


He pointed out that the battle was not one of rifles and guns, fought by soldiers in 
traditional military uniforms, but one of education and knowledge. The responsibility 
for fighting this battle and offering sacrifices for it and achieving victory in it, 
he said, is that of the students and graduates. For it is no less grave than many 

of the basic battles fought on the road of building anew society and the demolition of 
the old society. 


Addressing the students and graduates, he said: All I care about is that this battle 

is fought and to explain to you its idea and plan. Its resrunsibility falls on your 
shoulders because it concerns you, for you are in a better position to know what are 

its objectives and how to tackie them. With your success in this battle, he said, the 
traditional present educational institutions will disappear--institutions such as 

colleges and their like--and will be replaced by a new framework which will realize 

the objectives of the new society. The traditional curricula, which are inherited, will 
alsc disappear and will be replaced by a new revolutionary technological curriculim through 
which, he said, we will be abled to solve the problems facing us, for science is there to 
serve man and to solve his problems. Otherwise, there is no use for science. 


ACCUSATIONS OF LIBYAN SUPPORT OF TERROR DENOUNCED 


LD191515 Kuwait AR-RA'Y AL-'AMM in Arabic 17 Mar 81 p 7 


{'Azzam Yunis interview with the director of the Libyan External Information Department, 


Dr Khalifah al-'Azbi--date and place not specified] 


[Excerpts] Dr Khalifah al'Azbi, director of the Libyan Jamahiriyah External Information 
Department, said that the trend of Libyan information is pan-Arab and unionist and that 
information coordination between the Jamahiriyah and Syria is a matter of course within 
the framework of comprehensive unity between two effective members of the Steadfastness 
and Confrontation Front. 


Dr al'Azbi said that the Jamahiriyah is the target of a vicious, intensive and systematic 
campaign led by the Egyptian regime with the aim of accusing Libya of terrorism. The 

world must realize that there is a vital difference between revolution and terror. In the 
world's biased view the daily Zionist bombing of Lebanese towns and villages and Palestinian 
camps is a legitimate action. Real terror in fact is the intimidation of peoples, the 
building up of air forces and naval forces, and threatening people with starvation, he added. 


Dr al-'Azbi asserted that rightist and fascist elements in the Italian Parliament are 
the ones leading a campaign of distortion against Libya, a campaign which is in the interests 
of neither peoples and for which the Italian people are responsible. 


Dr al-'Azbi said that the Jamahiriyah is the target of an intensive, systematic and 
coordinated colonialist and reactionary campaign aiming at one thing: namely to accuse 
Libya of terrorism. We refuse to be accused of terrorism and the world must understand 
that there is a vital difference between terror and revolution. The liberation of 
Palestine is not terrorism and the liberation of Namibia and Zimbabwe is not terrorism. 
Either real terrorism is terrorizing peoples with aircraft, navies, and maneuvers as 
weil as violating the airespace of peaceful countries.

I must point out in this regard, he said, that the United States is daily violating our 
airspace and territorial waters. Additionally, the United States threatens people with 
starvation. This is really terrorism. 


Dr al-‘Azbi added: Brother Colonel al-Qadhdhafi has often made this clear and denied 

that Libya is a scene of bases for terrorism. In a recent interview with the Italian paper 
GIORNALE DI SICILIA, Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud challenged the Italian security departments 
to provide any material evidence that the Jamahiriyah has any connection with what is 
happening in Iialy. Nevertheless, the Italian press continues to accuse us of terror--an 
accusation which is part of the irresponsible international bias.
