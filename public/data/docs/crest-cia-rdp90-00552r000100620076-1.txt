Summary (AI): A 1982 Los Angeles Times article (OCR-garbled) reports CIA Director Casey telling a House committee that the CIA did not approve Libyan terrorist training activities of former agents Edwin Wilson and Frank Terpil. Rep. Boland's committee was investigating CIA involvement with Libya; the article also names Shackley and Cline as retired officials. Little about Tripoli itself.
• Casey told the House intelligence committee on a Tuesday that the CIA did not approve Libyan terrorist training activities of former agents.
• The article says Casey declined to meet with the committee but provided Boland typed notes.
• Boland's committee had a four-month investigation; it also mentions Libya exporting steroid devices to the Soviet Union (OCR-garbled).
