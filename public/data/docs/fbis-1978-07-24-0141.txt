DJIBOUTI, SEYCHELLES PRESIDENTS ARRIVE, MEET AL-QADHDHAFI 
LD232158Y Tripoli JANA in English 1453 GMT 23 Jul 78 LD 


(Text ] Tripoli, July 23, JANA--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi received last night Presi- 
dent Hassan Gouled Aptidon of the French Territory of the Afars and the Issas and Presi- 
dent France Albert Rene of Seychelles. 


Present at the meeting were Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir and Lt Col Mustafa al-Kharrubi 
and the secretaries of foreign affairs and industry.

Both presidents had arrived in Tripoli yesterday evening on an official visit to the 
Jamahiriyah, due to last several days. 


The two guests were given an official reception attended by Col Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, 
Lt Col Mustafa al-Kharrubi and Mr 'Abu al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, chairman of the General 
People's Committee, other secretaries in the Secretariat, and diplomats, 


In a communique received by JANA's correspondent President Gouled praised the good 
relationship existing between the Jamahiriyah and Djibouti which he described as a 
strong, fraternal relationship based on friendship and cooperation. 


He said that if the Africans aim at forming a force, they should act and cooperate to 
create this force, to confront colonialism and reaction. They should hold mutual con- 
sultations on the necessity to create cuch a force. He stressed that they would not 
accept any foreign intervention on this subject and that this force should remain within 
the framework of African unity. 


The president of Seychelles also issued a communique received by JANA's correspondent 
on the relations between the Jamahiriyah and Seychelles. 


He said that Seychelles is grateful to the Jamahiriyah for its attitude in supporting 
Seychelles until they achieve independence. He added that they are now inspired by 
the policies of the Jamahiriyah, and that they appreciate the courageous attitudes 

of the Jamahiriyah against colonialism and reaction, 


President Rene expressed his happiness, considering it a great honor to visit his 
second home, the Jamahiriyah. 


Commenting on the plan to build an African force he said: "We would accept this proposal 
provided the force would be purely African with no power given to any foreign state, 

We would reject this force if it is inspired by foreign or colonialist countries. We 
want this force to be ruled by the Africans themselves within the framework of the 
Organization of African Unity. Africa is the sole authority responsible for its self- 
determination and its self-defense."

Talks Begin

[Text] At the Foreign Secretariat headquarters in Tripoli, the official talks between 
the Socialist Peoples Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah [SPLAJ] and the People's Republic of 

Congo began yesterday. On the SPLAJ side the talks were headed by brother 'Abd al-'Ati 
al-'Ubaydi, chairman of the General People's Committee, and on the Congolese side by 

Col Louis Sylvain Goma, the Congolese prime minister. The Arab side included the brother 
secretaries of agriculture and trade, the under secretaries of the Housing Secretariat and 
the director of the Technical Cooperation Department at the Foreign Secretariat. The 
congolese side included the ministers of trade, economy, construction and the environment. 


The JANA correspondent has learned that the talks concerned joint cooperation between the 
two countries in the field of agriculture and the timber industry. Joint companies were 
discussed and ways of strengthening the existing cooperation between the two countries 
were studied,
