LIBYA

AL-UBAYDI MEETS WITH OAU SECRETARY GENERAL 


LDO71522 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 7 Jul 82 


[Text] Brother ‘Abd al-Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the Popular Committee of the People's 
Foreign Liaison Bureau, conferred this morning with Edem Kodjo, secretary general of the 
OAU, who arrived in Tripoli last night. At this meeting they discussed preparations for 
the 39th session of the OAU Foreign Ministers Council to be held from 26 July to 2 August, 
and the 19th African summit from 5 to 8 August in Tripoli, as well as African issues and 
topics submitted for discussion at these meetings. 


Edem Kodjo has acquainted himself with the preparations for the next African summit. This 
morning he visited the people's hall and the residence of the delegations which will take 
part in the conference. Edem Kodjo expressed his appreciation for the efforts and the 
great preparations being undertaken for making the conference a success. He expressed 
his conviction that these will contribute positively to the success of the conference, 
which will be a new starting point for the continent. 


BULGARIAN FOREIGN TRADE MINISTER PAYS VISIT 


Delivers Al-Qadhdhafi Message 
LDO71656 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 7 Jul 82 
[Text] Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the armed forces, yesterday 
received the Bulgarian foreign trade minister, who handed him a written personal message 
for the brother leader of the revolution from Todor Zhivkov, chairman of the Bulgarian 
State Council. 
Signs Trade Protocol 


LD071554 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 7 Jul 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Jul (JANA) -- The secretary of the General People's Committee for 

Economy and Light Industries and Bulgarian Foreign Trade Minister Khristo Khristov this 
morning signed a protocol for joint cooperation between the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah and the Republic of Bulgaria. The protocol includes backing for forms of 
cooperation between the two friendly peoples in various technical, economic and trade fields. 


Departs Tripoli 
LD071556 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 7 Jul 82 


[Excerpt] Tripoli, 7 Jul (JANA) -- Bulgarian Foreign Trade Minister Khristo Khristov 

left Tripoli this afternoon with his accompanying delegation. The Bulgarian minister hailed 
the outcome of his visit to the Jamahiriyah and said: We have agreed to support cooperation 
in the agricultural and economic fields and to increase the volume of trade exchange between 
the two friendly countries.
