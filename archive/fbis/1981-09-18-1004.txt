LIBYA

PDRY LEADER ARRIVES, ISSUES STATEMENT 15 SEP 


LD161252 Tripoli JANA in Arabia 0700 GMT 16 Sep 81 


[Excerpts] Benghazi, 16 Sep (JANA)--The secretary general of the Yemeni Socialist Party 
Central Committee and chairman of the Presidium and prime minister of the PDRY, ‘Ali 
Nasir Muhammad, arrived in Beninah Airport yesterday evening on a visit to Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


Brother 'Ali Nasir Muhammad made a statement to a JANA correspondent at Beninah Airport 
in Benghazi: 


He expressed his pleasure at visiting Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah which 

is in the framework of the intensive communications between the brothers to examine 

the latest developments resulting from the critical aggressive escalation by imperialism, 
Zionism and reaction, which is aimed at hitting the progressive and patriotic forces 

and weakening their steadfastness, and restoring imperialist domination of the destinies 
of our Arab nation. 


Brother 'Ali Nasir Muhammad stressed the necessity of supporting the struggle of the 
Arab peoples at this critical stage in which the scope of aggressive tendencies and of 
the U.S. imperialist and Zionist conspiratorial plans are widening. This is embodied 

in the continued occupation of Arab territories and repeated attacks on Beirut and 
southern Lebanon by the Zionists with the aim of liquidating the Palestinian revolution 
and the Lebanese national movement, and also in the insistence on implementing the 

Camp David conspiracies, and in increasing the U.S. imperialist military presence in 
Egypt, Sudan and Oman, the most critical results of which were the provocative maneuvers 
near the PDRY and Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah borders and the treacherous 
attack on the two Libyan aircraft inside Libyan Arab territorial skies and waters. 


Brother ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad also pointed to the cooperation and friendship alliance 
between Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, the PDRY and Ethiopia and said that 
it forms a good starting point for closing the ranks of the progressive and patriotic 
forces in the Arab homeland and Africa and for enhancing the dynamism of their struggle 
against imperialism and its supporters.
