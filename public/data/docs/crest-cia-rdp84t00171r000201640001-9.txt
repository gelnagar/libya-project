Summary (AI): A 2 September 1983 NPIC imagery briefing attachment on the disposition of Libyan and Libyan-backed forces in northern Chad, listing units and air defence assets at places such as Faya Largeau, Ounianga Kebir, Gouro, Ogouie and Aozou. Nothing here concerns Tripoli or domestic Libyan repression.
• Libyan forces in northern Chad included elements of four mechanized infantry battalions, one motorized infantry battalion, two tank companies, and six heavy-lift battalions.
• Air defence assets included two deployed SA-6 batteries, one probable SA-9 battery, and three ZSU-23/4 guns (two probable).
• Combat aircraft in northern Chad included six Mirage F-1, 23 Fitter (Su-22), 15 SF.260 light-strike aircraft, and five Hind.
• 1983-09-02: NPIC reports Libyan force dispositions and air defence deployments in northern Chad during the conflict.
