LIBYA

AL-CADHDHAFI INTERVIEWED BY LUXEMBOURG TV 


LD261254 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 26 Mar 81 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 26 Mar (JANA)--Tripoli, during the past 2 days Luxembourg television 
has carried the interview which correspondent (Christian Mallard) had with the brother 
leader. 


With regard to the situation in Chad and the possibility of French intervention, the leader 
explained that fear stems from French intervention and not Libyan intervention. He said 
Libya only entered Chad on request to end the war, and did actually end it. The Libyan 
forces will be withdrawn shortly if military and political threats against Chad are 
removed: However, we fear that Egyptian-Sudanese military concentrations along the eastern 
borders of Chad and the mobilization once again of Sudanese gangs against Chad could hinder 
it. The brother leader added that this is obstructing the withdrawal of Libyan forces from 
Chad and we fear might obstruct it further. 


The leader replied to a question about his assessment of the future of the world and the 
possibility of a confrontation between the two major powers. He said that the future of 
the world rests on two choices--either total collapse or peace. This means that con- 
frontation is not inevitable and can be avoided. However, now there are causes which warn 
of danger, the most important of which is the Arab question, where it is feared that the 
Jews could cause a third world war the way they caused the First and Second World Wars. 
The blind American support for the Israelis could also lead to a confrontation between 
the two major powers. Today we are seeing practical steps along this road by the 
establishment of American military bases in Egypt, Somalia, Oman and Sudan, which means 
that the American forward confrontation lines have advanced to become parallel with the 
European front, which ends at Turkey and Greece. 


The leader in his interview with Luxembourg television added that the second reason for 
this danger is the failure to disarm, the continuation of nuclear armament and the build- 
ing of nuclear medium-range missile bases in central Europe. The French attempts to 
reestablish a military presence in Africa are bound to contribute to increasing tension 
in the world once again. The leader stressed that giving Egypt a military role in the 
region is bound to exacerbate conditions inthe region. This in effect means that the 
line of confrontation has extended from Europe to Africa. 


Regarding the role of the Islamic revolutions in the Jamahiriyah and Iran, the brother 
leader explained that the Islamic world is now being headed by these two revolutions to 
liberate it from alliances and colonialism, since colonialism has imposed backwardness 
on the Islamic world through crusade-like motives. 


Asto the influence of the Jamahiriyah in international politics and the world balance, the 
brother leader stressed that Libya is a neutral country of the first order and has basic 
influence on the balance of power in the Mediterranean, the Middle East and the whole 
world.

FOREIGN LIAISON SECRETARY MEETS ARAB AMBASSADORS 


LD261406 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 26 Mar 81 


[Text] The brother secretary of the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison this morning received the ambassadors of the sister Arab countries in the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


During this meeting the brother secretary talked with the Arab ambassadors about the 
Jamahiriyah's stands vis-a-vis the developments taking place in the Arab arena. He spe- 
cifically mentioned the dangerous move of the Sudanese regime of restoring its diplomatic 
relations with the ruling regime in Egypt, which is considered a blatant violation of the 
resolutions of the Baghdad summit. The brother secretary of the People's Bureau for 
Foreign Liaison drew attention to the fact that the exchange of ambassadors between the 
two regimes in Egypt and Sudan is considered a traitorous step and another operation in 
an attempt to confirm the capitulationist solutions. He also drew attention to the fact 
that this step is considered a dangerous precedence and a depature from the Arab and 
Islamic summits’ resolutions. 


He explained that the Jamahiriyah submitted the subject of the exchange of ambassadors 
between Egypt and Sudan to the Arab League Council in Tunis. He noted that the council 
had adopted a middle-of-the-road resolution in face of the traitorous and treacherous 
behavior. 


During this meeting he talked to the Arab ambassadors about the gravity of the situation 

in southern Lebanon and the repeated Israeli and isolationist attacks it was being sub- 
jected to. He urged that a unified Arab stand be taken to defend Lebanon. [In this respect 
he stressed the readiness of the Jamahirivah to extend the necessary assistance and to 
help to ensure full protection for the sons of the Lebanese people in the south. 


In his talk he dealt with the forthcoming meeting of the Jerusalem committee, which will 
be chaired by the king of Morocco. He criticized this meeting because of the involvement 
of Hasan II in suspicious and arranged meetings with the Zionist terrorist Shim'on Peres, 
a matter which confirms the futility of the meetings of this committee in the shadow of 
a regime that has links with the enemy of the Arab nation.

SOLDIERS RETURNING FROM CHAD WELCOMED IN BANGHAZI 


Popular Celebrations 
LD270030 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 26 Mar 81 


[Excerpts] Yesterday evening Banghazi welcomed in a revolutionary and festive atmosphere 
our soldiers who had supported freedom in Chad, defeated colonialism and reaction and 
restored peace and stability to the fraternal Chadian people. [he supporters of freedom 
were welcomed by the popular masses, vanguards of the armed people, including male and 
female students from schools and institutes, a popular cavalry force, units from various 
sections of the armed forces and a considerable number of mujahidin. 


Veterans decorated the chests of the supporters of freedom, the soldiers of the Great 
1 September Revolution, with roses, when they arrived at the Benina airport. A guard of 
honor was also there to welcome them. 


Well-wishers mixed with the returning troops of the Great 1 September Revolution in a 

huge popular march, during which they chanted slogans reaffirming the popular determina- 
tion to move forward on the road of the Great 1 September Revolution, defend its principles, 
hold high our flags everywhere and call for the adoption of the eternal humanitarian 

theses [of the Green Book]. The popular masses reaffirmed their solidarity with the 

leader of the Great 1 September Revolution, the champion of freedom and creator of the 

era of the masses. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Address 
LD270938 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 27 Mar 81 


[Excerpts] Banghazi, 27 Mar (JANA)--The popular masses in the city of Banghazi welcomed 


with roses some of the Libyan military units withdrawn from Chad after taking part in 
ending the bloody civil war ignited by agents of colonialism and after establishing peace 
and security in the land of this sister country. 


Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Al-Fatah Revolution, said in a speech: 
Courageous soldiers, today you are returning to the same barracks from which you moved 
abroad to carry out the second death order which I issued to you after the first death 
order which I issued to you on the dawn of 1 September 1969. 


He pointed out that this is the second time that forces have gone outside their borders, 
successfully carried out their mission and returned to their country, leaving the land to 
its people. The first time was when the Egyptian Army under the command of Jamal ‘Abd 
an-Nasir fought alongside the 26 September Revolution and alongside the Yemen-Arab people, 
backed the republican regime, and defeated the agent reactionary monarchy forever, and then 
these forces returned to Egypt. 


The brother leader stressed that the courageous liberating soldiers, after offering 

sacred sacrifices, completed a task which the whole of Africa and the major powers failed 

to perform. He pointed out that France abandoned Chad at its darkest hour because its moti 
vation was colonial and all the African countries together were not moved by the massacre to 
which the people of Chad were exposed. The brother leader stressed that after the Chad 
national government under the leadership of Frolinat, the Chad national liberation movement, 
turned to its neighbor Libya to end the tragedy of the people of Chad, there was no alter- 
native other than to use force. 


The brother leader addressed the returning soldiers by saying: All of us must be ready to 
return to the fight at any time if the enemies once again try to threaten the security 

of Chad, which has become closely linked with the security of Libya. The brother leader 
added: You have decided the battle in the interest of the people of Chad and in the interest 
of peace and freedom in Africa, and you have defeated the reactionaries and their colonial 
and Zionist masters.
