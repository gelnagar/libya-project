LIBYA

ARMED FORCES CHIEF RECEIVES USSR'S SKORIKOV 


LD241642 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 24 Nov 82 
[Text] Tripoli, 24 Nov (JANA) -- Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis, commander in chief of the 
armed forces, this morning received Air Marshal Skorikov, chief of the Main Staff of the 


Soviet Air Force, who is currently on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah.
