AT-TURAYKI STATEMENTS ON AS-SADAT'S DEATH, BUSH 


LD111950 Tripoli JANA in English 1806 GMT 11 Oct 81 


[Text] Damascus, 11 Oct (JANA) -- The liaison secretary for external affairs, Dr ‘Ali 
at-Turayki, said the execution of As-Sadat by the Egyptian people is another lesson to be 
learned by American imperialism following its disastrous experiences in Iran. He said an 
agreement signed with hirelings is totally rejected and cannot be implemented by the people. 


In a statement made to journalists in Damascus last night, Dr at-Turayki said: As-Sadat 
signed the treasonous Camp David accords with the U.S. and Israel, but the Egyptian people 
eliminated him, and any other such person will be eliminated. 


On the anti-Jamahiriyah statements made by U.S. Vice President George Bush, who was earlier 
director of the CIA, Dr at-Turayki said the leader and entire people of Libya are not 
interested in obtaining a certificate of good conduct from America. Such acknowledgement, 
he said, can only be accepted from the Arab nation and liberated peoples. 


He said American accusations are an honour for the Libyan Arab people, because American 
imperialism has made a habit of describing revolutionaries and freedom fighters as 
terrorists, and only thanks traitors such as As-Sadat. He said Sudan's ruler Numayri is 
facing the same fate meted out to As-Sadat. 


On recent American military moves in the region, Dr at-Turayki said these moves will only 
make the Egyptian people more convinced not only in the correctness of their decision in 
executing As-Sadat, but also in foiling As-Sadat's capitulationist stance. When this stance 
is foresaken, Egypt is to reoccupy its rightful commanding role from within the Arab nation. 


U.S. Plans to 'Contain' 
LD111936 Tripoli JANA in English 1818 GMT 11 Oct 81 
[Text] Washington, 11 Oct (JANA) -- The U.S. vice president, George Bush, formerly the 
director of the Central Intelligence Agency, has made terse, aggressive statements against 


the Jamahiriyah's people and their revolution's leader Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. 


Bush reiterated American allegations designed to picture Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and the 
entire Libyan people as terrorists. 


In statements made at the Press Club in Washington, the American vice president said Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi constitutes a basic threat to Israel, Sudan and Egypt and that he is allegedly 
prepared to spark off a third world war. 


These statements made by Bush following As-Saaai's death confirm predictions that the 
United States is moving ahead with plans to contai: the situation in Egypt after the 
disappearance of one of its main agents who was prepared to serve aggressive American 
interest in the region. 


Bush, like many other American officials and political and military observers in the country, 
feels that the Jamahiriyah is a strategic obstacle hindering the implementation of terroristic 
U.S. schemes.

HAIG'S STATEMENTS ON EGYPT, CHAD "HOSTILE" 


LD121729 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 12 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 12 Oct (JANA) -- U.S. Secretary of State Alexander Haig has made 
hostile statements against the Libyan Arab people, using the Libyan people's humane 
support for the people of Chad as an excuse for aggravating tension and for moving in a 
provocative manner to invade Egypt and to consolidate the U.S. military and political 
presence in Sudan and Somalia. The Egyptian news agency reported last night the text of 
the statements which Haig made to the U.S. NBC television network. In these statements

region; his death makes it imperative that we reassert our interests in Egypt and

regimes.

Through his statements the U.S. secretary explains the U.S. attitude not only toward
