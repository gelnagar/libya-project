Summary (AI): A 1981 Daily Telegraph article reports that a former U.S. Green Beret, Luke Thompson, said he met British mercenaries in Tripoli in July 1977 who were helping Gaddafi's armed forces, and that they made rot-gut whisky to get around the alcohol ban. The men had been recruited by former CIA agent Edwin Wilson.
• Thompson said British mercenaries in Libya made their own whisky, which was popular because it got around the alcohol ban.
• Thompson said the pay offered was $6,500 a month with a $10,000 bonus at the end of a year.
• Wilson's London company was called Brilhurst, with an office in Hans Road, Knightsbridge.
• Wilson safe houses were reportedly in Sussex.
• 1977-07: About 17 British mercenaries recruited by Edwin Wilson were summoned to a meeting with Libyan intelligence chief Abdullah Sanussi at the Ministry of Trade and placed under Capt. Hadjazi's operational control.
