LIBYA

LEADERS CONFER WITH VISITING IRAQI OFFICIAL 
LD1L4L434Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 14 Dec 78 LD 


[Text] Libyan Foreign Secretary Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki conferred at the 
Foreign Secretariat this morning with Na'im Haddad, member of the Regional Command 
of the Ba'th Party and of the Iraqi Revolution Command Council, who is now in Libya. 
They reviewed the current Arab situation and international issues of mutual interest. 
During the meeting, which was attended by Iraq's ambassador to Libya, ways and means 
of guaranteeing the strengthening of relations and the development of cooperation 
between Libya and Iraq in all areas were discussed. 


Jallud Given Iraqi President's Message 
LD141656Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 14 Dec 78 LD 


[Text] Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud this morning received at the People's 
Palace brother Na'im Haddad, member of the Regional Command of the Ba'th Party and 
member of the Iraqi Revolution Command Council, who handed Staff Major Jallud a message 
addressed to brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 1 September revolution, 
from his brother, Iragi President Ahmad Hasan al-Bakr, concerning current developments 
in the Arab situation.
