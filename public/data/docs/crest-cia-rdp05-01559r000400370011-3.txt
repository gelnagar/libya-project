Summary (AI): A August 1975 cable from the U.S. Embassy in Beirut reports excerpts of an interview Muammar Qaddafi gave to the Beirut French-language daily L'Orient-Le Jour after a reporters' trip to Tripoli. He discusses Lebanon's Maronite Christians, religion, and the Palestinians. Nothing here concerns a specific place in Tripoli.
• The interview centred mainly on the Christian, especially Maronite, role in Lebanon.
• Qaddafi said there is no religious conflict in Lebanon and that the Maronites are 'our brothers, like other Arab Christians.'
• Qaddafi described himself as 'a believer and an Arab nationist' and said he was 'more Palestinian than they are.'
• 1975-08-24: L'Orient-Le Jour publishes an exclusive interview with Qaddafi, resulting from a recent trip to Tripoli by a delegation of its reporters
