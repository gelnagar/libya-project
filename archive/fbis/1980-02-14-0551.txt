JANA: U.S. MARINE CONTINGENT ARRIVES IN OMAN 
LD131500 Tripoli JANA in English 1418 GMT 13 Feb 80 LD 
[Text] Washington, February 13, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The American military 
administration has started to invade the Arab Gulf region, Eighteen hundred American 
Marines have arrived in the Sultanate of Oman on board four amphibious ships, 
An American military source today confirmed the arrival of these American troops in the 
Arab Sea, According to AGENCE FRANCE-PRESSE (AFP), the unit is equipped with war 
machinery including 19 helicopters, "AM-60" tanks, landing craft and tow and Dragon 


anti-tanks missiles, 


AFP referred to a source close to the American Defence Department that these troops are 
to remain in the area, indefinitely. 


This aggressive measure by the U.S.A. is part of the conspiracy to strike at the 
liberation movements in the region and to fight the progressive countries opposed to the 
imperialist American policy against the freedom of the peoples,

The Islamic Iranian Revolution is a cornerstone of the era of the masses, This era has 
commenced its forward march, heralding the end of serfdom from those who control wealth, 
authority and arms, and transferring those means to the people through people!s con- 
gresses and committees, There is no democracy except through people's congresses, an 
armed people and a society based on partnership, The Iranian Revolution!s cornerstone 

is Islam, Its purpose is freedom and its religious leadership is a model for the 

new Islamic state, as called for in the Holy Koran, 

With reference to the verses of the Holy Koran "be alert, youth and elderly" and "pre- 
pare as much as you could of your strength to face the enemy" the strength here is the 


whole strength and the alertness is not only limited to the regular army but it means 

that the youth and the elderly should fight and the whole people should be armed, The 
parliamentary state, the professional army, capitalism, and the system of one ruling party, 
or a number of parties, all these are the by-preducts of Christianity in its stage of 
lecadence, and of the Western bourgeoisie in the period of colonialism, 


We have high hopes in the Iranian Revolution under your heroic leadership and your belief 
in the establishment of the state of new Islam, the liberation of Palestine and Alaqsa 
mosque and in supporting weak peoples whoever they may be, Finally, I assure you and 

the great Iranian people of our genuine stand with you in this stage of revolutionary 
change from an Islamic republic until the establishment of people's power, I also 
believe in our strategic alliance against our common enemy, Victory is inevitable, May 
peace and the mercy of God befall upon you.
