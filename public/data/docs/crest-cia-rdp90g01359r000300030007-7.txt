Central Intelligence Agency 
Office of the Deputy Director for Intelligence 

27 June 1986 

NOTE TO: DCI 
DDCT 

SUBJECT: After Action Reporting on US Attack 
on Libya 

You have seen several complaints from our 
analysts that they have not received any after 
action reports from the pilots and others 
involved in the attack on Libyan targets. | STAT 

At some point in time you probably should 
hear that briefing, but both of the officers 
are now back in their commands. If you are 
interested in some detail, I would be glad to 
give you a few words summarizing the briefing. 

STAT 

Richard J. Kerr 
DDI
