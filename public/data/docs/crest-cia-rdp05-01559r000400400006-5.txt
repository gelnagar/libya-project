• A four-sided Arab summit was expected in Damascus early the following month.

((TEXT)) ( (QUESTION) ) BROTHER LEADER AS A LEADER OF THE ARAB 
REVOLUTION IS IT NOW AMONG YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES TO ASSUME THE 
LEADERSHIP OF THE REVOLUTIONARY FORCES GROUPED UNDER THE BANNER OF 
THE PAN-ARAB COMMAND? 
( (AL-QADHDHAFI)) YES, THE PAN-ARAB COMMAND IS RESPONSIBLE FOR THE 
REVOLUTIONARY FORCES IN THE ARAB WORLD AND THIS IS A COLLECTIVE 
LEADERSHIP. 
((QUESTION) ) BROTHER LEADER, SANA CORRESPONDENT: IT IS KNOWN THAT 
THE LIBYAN JAMAHIRIYAH IS THE BASTION OF ARAB LIBERATION IN THE ARAB 
MAGHREB AND THAT SYRIA IS THE BASTION OF STEADFASTNESS AND 
CONFRONTATION IN THE ARAB EAST. A FOUR-SIDED ARAB SUMMIT CONFERENCE 
WHICH WILL BE HELD IN DAMASCUS, EARLY NEXT MONTH. TO WHAT EXTENT 
DO YOU EXPECT THIS SUMMIT TO CONTRIBUTE TO THE CONFRONTATION OF THE 
AXIS OF REACTIONARY CONSPIRACY LED BY ‘ARAFAT AND HUSAYN, ESPECIALLY 
AS THEY AHVE ANNOUNCED THAT DIRECT TALKS BETWEEN THE 'ARAFAT-JORDAN 
DELEGATION AND ISRAEL WILL BEGIN IN THE COMING MONTHS. AT THE SAME 
TIME, TO WHAT EXTENT DO YOU EXPECT THIS FOUR-SIDED SUMMIT TO PLAY A 
ROLE IN ESTABLISHING ARAB UNITY AND IN CARRYING OUT THE SLOGAN, 
LIBERATION FROM THE SOUTH TO THE SOUTH, WHICH HAS LATELY BEEN RAISED 
IN THE LIBYAN JAMAHIRIYAH? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) THE FOUR-SIDED MEETING, IRRESPECTIVE OF WHERE 
AND WHEN IT IS HELD, IS A BASIS FOR ACHIEVING UNITY AMONG THESE 
FOUR COUNTRIES. UNITY AND WHAT IT WILL ACHIEVE NEEDS NO DEFINITION. 
IT IS IN ITSELF A DECISIVE AND HISTORIC RESPONSE TO HOSTILE PLANS, 
WHETHER THOSE OF ‘ARAFAT, HUSAYN, AMERICA, OR OTHERS. 
((QUESTION)) ARE THERE CONDITIONS IN: WHICH LIBYAN FORCES MIGHT 
BE FORCED TO CROSS LONGITUDE 16 IN THE SOUTH? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) WE DO NOT RECOGNIZE ANY LONGITUDE OR LATITUDE, 
16 OR 60. WHO MADE THIS LINE AND WHO HAS THE RIGHT TO DRAW UP THESE 
LINES IN CHAD? AT ANY RATE, LIBYA HAS NO FORCES IN CHAD, BUT IF 
FOREIGN FORCES INTERVENE THERE LIBYA WILL PAY NO HEED TO LONGITUDE 
OR LATITUDES. 
((QUESTION)) LEADER, IT IS SUGGESTED IN SOME QUARTERS THAT YOU HAD 
AN INFLUENCE IN THE AFFIARS IN SUDAN. IS THIS TRUE AND IF SO, WHAT 
KIND OF INFLUENCE? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI, IN ENGLISH)) ALL THE LIBYANS AND SUDANESE ARE 
BROTHERS AND ((WORDS INDISTINCT)) CONSIDERED ONE LAND, ONE COUNTRY, 
ONE PEOPLE, ONE NATION. AS SUCH, WE DEPEND ON EACH OTHER AND WE 
SUPPORT EACH OTHER. AND THE LIBERATION OF SUDAN AND THE LIBERTY OF 
SUDAN AND THE LIBERATION OF PEOPLES WHO LIVE IN THE OTHER ARAB 
COUNTRIES ARE ONE. WE NEED THEM AND THEY NEED US. WE SUPPORT THEM 
AS THEY SUPPORT US. THEY ARE ARABS, THEY ARE ONE PEOPLE. WE 
REJECTED TO SAY IT IS INTERVENTION IN THE OTHERS' AFFAIRS, BETWEEN 
ARABS. OUR INTERNAL AFFAIRS IS ONE AND THE SUDANESE ARE ARAB 
BROTHERS. WE LOVE THEM AND SUPPORT THEM. 
((QUESTION)) IN THE NAME OF GOD, THE MERCIFUL, THE COMPASSIONATE, 
((WORD INDISTINCT)) I READ YOU HAD A MEETING WITH B. KREISKY 
((FORMER AUSTRIAN CHANCELLOR -- FBIS)) YESTERDAY. WOULD YOU PLEASE 
TELL US WHAT YOU TALKED ABOUT AND IF ANYTHING CONCRETE WAS DECIDED? 
THANKS YOU. 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) HE VISITED SEVERAL COUNTRIES, SUCH AS AMERICA,

INDIA, AND THE GULF. WE TALKED FOR 2 HOURS ABOUT INTERNATIONAL 
PROBLEMS. WE SHARE CONCERN FOR WORLD PEACE. WE ARE DEEPLY CONCERNED 
OVER WORLD PEACE, ESPECIALLY OVER THE MORAL DETERIORATION OF THE 
GULF WAR WHICH HAS TURNED INTO A WAR AGAINST CIVILIANS, A WAR OF 
ATROCITIES AGAINST CITIZENS. THIS UGLY DETERIORATION IS 
UNACCEPTABLE TO US. THE WORLD SHOULD DO ALL IT CAN TO HALT THE WAR 
OF THE CITIES. 
((QUESTION)) ((WORD INDISTINCT)) FROM THE SPANISH NEWS AGENCY: 
YOU HAVE ACHIEVED UNITY WITH MOROCCO. ARE YOU PREPARED TO OFFER 
MILITARY SUPPORT TO MOROCCO IN CASE OF PROBLEMS WITH SPAIN OVER 
CEUTA AND MELILLA? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) FIRST, IT IS UNLIKELY THAT THERE WILL BE A WAR 
OVER CEUTA AND MELILLA. BUT NATURALLY WE ABCK MOROCCO. CEUTA AND 
MELILLA ARE TWO ARAB CITIES AND THERE IS NO JUSTIFICATION FOR 
KEEPING THEM THIS LONG. AND I DO NOT THINK THAT SPAIN IS READY 
TO GO TO WAR FOR THE SAKE OF ARAB CITIES. 
((QUESTION)) ((WORD INDISTINCT)) FROM MOROCCO: YOUR EXCELLENCY, 
COLONEL, A CONFERENCE ((WORD INDISTINCT)) WAS HELD RECENTLY IN THE 
CAPITAL OF THE ARAB-AFRICAN UNION ((WORD INDISTINCT)) AND 
IT ADOPTED A NUMBER OF RESOLUTIONS, SOME OF WHICH WERE MADE PUBLIC 
WHILE OTHERS REMAINED (?IN TRUST WITH THE LEADERSHIP). THE 
CONFERENCE WAS DOMINATED BY THE SPIRIT OF OUJDA -- THAT AGREEMENT 
WHICH CAUSED A GREAT CONTROVERSY IN THE WORLD AND TRIBAL 
REACTIONS IN ARAB AND AFRICAN CAPITALS. WHAT WERE THE FEELINGS OF 
THE REVOLUTIONARY GROUPS WHICH CONSTITUTE THE GENERAL COMMAND 
REFERRED TO CONSIDERING THAT THE ARAB LIBYAN-MOROCCAN UNITY IS ONE 
OF THE MOST IMPORTANT TIES IN OUR MODERN TIMES. SECOND QUESTION: 
THE BELIEF PREVAILED FOLLOWING THE EVENTS WHICH BROUGHT DOWN 
NUMAYRI'S REGIME ON 6 APRIL, THE SWEEPING AWAY OF THE RULING MILITARY 
MACHINE BY THE POPULAR MASSES IN THE SUDAN, THAT U.S. IMPERIALISM 
MIGHT RESORT TO MOVING ITS ((WORD INDISTINCT)) TACTICS TO ABSORB 
THE ANGER OF THE MASSES BY MEANS OF ((WORDS INDISTINCT)). WHAT IS 
THE ATTITUDE OF GENERAL SIWAR AL-DHAHAB TOWARD THIS GAME? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) FIRST, DURING THE MEETING OF THE PAN-ARAB 
COMMAND OF THE REVOLUTIONARY FORCES NO CERTAIN REGIONAL OR LOCAL 
PROBLEMS WERE DISCUSSED, RATHER THE ISSUES OF LIBERATION AND UNITY 
AT THE LEVEL OF THE ARAB HOMELAND WERE RAISED. BUT YOU KNOW THAT 
SOME ARAB GROUPS HAVE RESERVATIONS CONCERNING THE ARAB-MOROCCAN 
UNITY ((AS HEARD)). THEIR RESERVATIONS CONCERN MOROCCO. WE REPLIED 
TO THOSE RESERVATIONS, THESE QUESTIONS. YOU KNOW SOME ARAB (?FORCES) 
ANNOUNCED THEIR STANDS FOLLOWING OUJDA AND THEY HAVE RESERVATIONS. 
THERE IS NOTHING NEW IN THIS RESPECT. THOSE WHO HAD RESERVATIONS 
CONTINUE TO RAISE THEM. 
((WORDS INDISTINCT)) EXAMPLE CREATED BY NUMAYRI ALSO HAS A 
LESSON IN IT. I DO NOT THINK THAT THERE IS ANYBODY IN OR OUTSIDE 
THE SUDAN WHO STILL TRUSTS AMERICA AND ITS ALLIANCES. IT HAS 
BECOME CLEAR THAT THE PEOPLES' WILL IS STRONGER THAN THE WILL OF 
AMERICA.
