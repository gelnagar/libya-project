LIBYA

Government Reacts to Jordan’s Severance of Ties 


King Husayn’s Policies Scored 
LD231844 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
23 Feb 84 


[Text] Yesterday, the Jordanian regime announced the severance 
of its political relations with the Jamahiriyah on the pretext of a 
demonstration staged by the Arabs residing in the Jamahiriyyah 
outside the Jordanian Embassy in Tripoli. The demonstration 
was held to protest the capitulatory trend in Jordanian policy, 
which has become more definite since the recent visit of King 
Husayn to Washington and his meetings with the U.S. and 
Egyptian presidents. The Egyptian regime has had full relations 
with the Zionist enemy for several years, based on an agreement 
the Arab world unanimously branded as treason. Egypt was 
boycotted by the Arabs as a result of the Baghdad summit, where 
Husayn was one of the witnesses. 


As background to the Jordanian decision to sever relations with 
the Jamahiriyah, we present the following report:

bypassed and neglected. This time the meeting was a tripartite

new plot. A responsible senior American official had stated that
