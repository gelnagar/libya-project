LIBYA

Lebanon's Al-Jumayyil Arrives 16 December 
LD161424 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
16 Dec 83 


[Text] Lebanese President Amin al-Jumayyil arrived in Tripoli 
at noon today on a visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah. He was welcomed at Tripoli International Airport 
by Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, several members 
of the General People’s Congress, members of the Arab diplo- 
matic corps in Libya, and some officers of the Libyan Arab 
Armed Forces

LD162251 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2ZIISGMT 16 Dec 83 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution received Lebanese 
President Amin al-Jumayyil this afternoon. During their long 
meeting, they studied the (7difficult) Lebanese situation, the 
circumstances prevailing in Lebanon, and the fierce Zionist- 
imperialist struggle to tear Lebanon apart asa first step in efforts 
to harm the Arab naiion and expose it to danger. 


it was stressed at the meeting, which was attended by Staff 
Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, that there is a joint desire 
to cooperate and consult, and that decisive solutions which will 
finally guarantee stability on sound, democratic foundations 
must be achieved at the local level in Lebanon. 


Also stressed was the need to stop aggression — as represented 
in the shelling conducted by the U.S. and NATO navy and air 
force. The need to work earnestly at the Arab and international 
levels to secure the withdrawal of foreign occupation forces was 
similarly highlighted, as was the need to make it clear that 
Lebanon, with its Arab people and Arab soil, will not agree to 
the presence of any invading forces on its soil 


Lebanese President Amin al-Jumayyil left Tripoli this evening 
after a short visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahir-
