LIBYA

POSTPONEMENT OF ARAB LEAGUE MEETING REQUESTED 
NC221254 Cairo MENA in Arabic 1214 GMT 22 Mar 79 NC 


[Text] Cairo, 22 March--Libya officially applied to the Arab League today to postpone 
the Arab League Council meeting scheduled to be held in Mogadiscio this Saturday. 

The application came in a memorandum which the Arab League Secretariat received from 
the Libyan Government. A source in the Afab League Secretariat has stated that the 
Secretariat has conveyed the text of the Libyan memorandum to the Arab governments. 

The source added that the league charter provides for holding two ordinary sessions 

of the Arab League Council each year, one in March and one in September, that an appli- 
cation for a postponement may be submitted to the council when it meets, and that the 
council itself will decide whether to accept or reject the application,

Meets Arab Envoys 


LD221900 Tripoli JANA in English 1654 GMT 22 Mar 79 LD

today at the Foreign Secretariat the ambassadors of sister Arab countries with whom 

he discussed the consequences of the capitulatory treaty due to be signed by the 

defeated Egyptian president and the racist Zionist enemy. Dr at-Turayki also considered 
with them the measures required for confronting the conspiracy which aims at dealing 

a blow to the Palestinian revolution and the existence of the Arab nation. Dr at-Turayki
