LIBYA

PETROLEUM SECRETARY ADDRESSES PEOPLE'S CONGRESS 


LD031210 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0924 GMT 3 Jan 82 


[Speech by Petroleum Secretary ‘Abd as-Salam az-Zaq'ar at the morning session of the 
General People's Congress held at the Green Hope Hall in Tripoli -- live] 


[Text] In the name of God, the merciful, the compassionate. Brothers, members of the 
congress: I would like to ask your permission to speak about the subject of oil and to 
have a brief look at the oil in the defunct regime. [as heard] 


The paper before you explains what has occurred in the oil market in the past few months. 

It is clear to all that the oil market has deteriorated in the last part of 1981. This 
occurred as a result of an imperialist plot led by the United States and using its lackeys 
to resist the progressive states in OPEC headed by the Jamahiriyah. Naturally the 
Jamahiriyah, through its revolutionary and firm stances in the face of defeatist solutions 
regarding the Palestinian question and the causes of liberation in the world has been the 
target of U.S. imperialism, which is trying to fight it through its most important resource, 
namely oil. This plot consists of flooding the world market with the oil of reactionary 
countries. This glut has been estimated at more than 3.5 million barrels per day. It 

is a glut resulting from overproduction by Saudi Arabia and some other organization members. 
There is an additional glut estimated at 3.5 million barrels resulting from oil stock- 
piling by those countries using the world's energy resources. This stockpiled oil was 

used to flood the market in the second half of 1981. 


The members of the organization have tried to pressure Saudi Arabia and other countries 

to reduce production so that the other members of the organization, particularly the 
Jamahiriyah, Nigeria and Algeria, would not be harmed the way they have been. We were 
harmed by the refusal of Saudi Arabia and others to reduce production or to try to deal 
with the situation within the organization. The organization has not been able to resolve 
the issue because of the intransigence of some of its members and their lack of desire to 
reach a sclution. In the last two conferences, in August and October, the Jamahiriyah 

put forward a fundamental solution to this problem. It demanded that the countries that 
flooded the market with oil rid of the glut by reducing their production and that the 
remainder of the countries that were not harmed, such as the Jamahiriyah and Nigeria, to 
[words indistinct] as the production in the Jamahiriyah has reached the minimum technical 
level estimated for its fields, which on some days is approximately 600,000 barrels or less. 


In fact, our own revenues, our plans and our assistance to friendly and sisterly countries 
have been harmed as a result of this hostile imperialist stance imposed on our people. 

In September 1981 the leader of the revolution made clear the dimensions and the objectives 
of this plot against the national economy. He made it clear that this action was aimed 
at making the Jamahiriyah lose its markets in order to harm our country and weaken its 
political position and so that the U.S. imperialists could pounce upon us and restore 
their bases. This was and still is the aim. For this reason the brother leader made 

it clear that the Libyan Arab people have two choices: either to face the plot and to 
challenge it because they respect the principles of freedom, justice and support for the 
oppressed and the repressed, or to give up and to sell their oil at the cheapest price 

in submission to the will of the imperialists and the reactionary agents and to retreat 
from the victories we have achieved in the past 10 years in curtailing the activities of 
the foreign companies, gaining control of our oil resources and rzising prices from $1.80 
to $40. 


Vv. 4 Jan 82 Q2 NORTH AFRICA 


If we were to relinquish our gains we should continue to consider the increasing costs 
to consumers and move away from production and productivity and subsequently lose all 
these gains [sentence as heard]. 


These are the main points of the paper that has been submitted to the basic People's 
Congresses, and as I have already briefly said, the Jamahiriyah has been exposed to a 
plot to reduce its prices. The Jamahiriyah has challenged this plot. However, the 
Libyan Arab people, as the brother leader of the revolution has stated, should understand 
the dimensions of the plot, which is not limited to economics but has military dimensions 
as well, given the attack by the American aircraft and U.S. 6th Fleet against our waters 
and territory in August 1981. Therefore, there are two paths before us: either to face 
challenge with counterchallenge, to stand steadfast and to reduce our commitments and 
expenditures in order that this wealth is not lost and is not sold at the cheapest prices, 
or to tighten our belts, to reduce our commitments and our consumption and to face the 
situation seriously and courageously in order to be able to surmount this crisis and 
repulse the military and economic aggression directed against our people. [sentence as 
heard] This is what is called for in this paper. 


I believe that the People's Congresses, which are the solid foundation of the people's 
authority and truly represent the people, have made clear their views and their inclin- 
ation to challenge this imperialist plot to reduce consumers’ costs. They have decided 

to move away from overspending and waste in order to allow the oil sector and those respon- 
sible for it the opportunity to resist the pressures of the monopolistic companies and 

the plots of reactionary U.S. imperialism. This is what the paper has called for, and 

this is what is demanded. I believe that the People's Congresses have a national con- 
science and a genuine understanding of what is before them, and I believe that they have 
adopted the necessary resolutions in support of our national steadfastness. 


Peace be with you. 


IRANIAN OIL DELEGATION PROMISES TECHNICAL AID 


LD031854 Tripoli JANA in English 1829 GMT 3 Jan 82 


{[TExt] Tripoli, 3 Jan (JANA) -- The secretary of the General People's Committee for Oil 
and members of the People's Congress from the National Oil Company met with an Iranian 
oil delegation. 


The secretary presented a detailed survey of the National Oil Company and its role in 
the administration and service of the oil sector with the help of the people's authority. 


The Iranian delegation expressed condemnation of the American consp” -ies against the 
Jamahiriyah and stressed the support and the solidarity of the Iranian Islamic revolution- 
aries with the Jamahiriyah and its confrontation of U.S. imperialism and Zionism. 


The delegation also expressed readiness to provide all technical aid to the Jamahiriyah 
in their struggle against American imperialism. 


AL-QADHDHAFI CABLES ISLAMIC, CHRISTIAN COUNTRIES 


LD031638 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 1 Jan 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 1 Jan (JANA) -- The brother leader has sent a cable to the heads of 
Islamic and Christian countries on the occasion of the new year. The text is as follows: 


V. 4 Jan 82 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


As we are celebrating the elapsing of 1981 years since the birth of Christ, Jesus the 
son of Mary, peace be on him, and the advent of the year 1982 on his birthday, we should 
not drink wine; we should recite the book of God during this night in order to know what 
God said through his prophets. 


This is what Jesus said: "Whosoever shall say to his brother, raca, shall be in danger 
of the council; but whosoever shall say ‘thou fool’ shall be in danger of hell fire. If 
thou rememberest that thy brother hath aught against thee, first be reconciled to thy 
brother, and then come and offer thy gift. Agree with thine adversary quickly, while thou 
art in the way with him, lest at any time the adversary deliver thee to the judge. I say 
unto you, whosoever shall smite thee on thy cheek, turn to him the other also. And 
whosoever shall compel thee to go a mile, go with him. And if any man will sue thee at 
the law, and take away thy coat, let him also have thy cloak. But I say unto you, love 
your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you, and pray for 
those who spitefully use you and persecute you. For if ye love them who love you, what 
reward have ye? And if ye salute your brethren only, what do ye more than others?" 
[sentence as received] 


I say to you on this occasion: How could we compare all this with the actions of Christ's 
followers today. They are the ones who smite the people on their faces, who sue people 
over their coats [as received], and who curse people and hate them; it is the others who 
are praying for Christ's followers. 


The following words are the best I can say to you and remind you of on the occasion of the 
new year: "In the beginning was theword." Colonel Mu'ammar Al-Qadhdhafi. 


BENGHAZI CHADIANS THANK AL-QADHDHAFI FOR AID 


NC040735 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 4 Jan 82 


[Text] The Chadian popular masses residing in Benghazi have lauded the Libyan Arab 
people's support for and backing of the Chadian people in returning security and stability 
to Chad. In their telegram to the brother leader, the Chadian popular masses have 
expressed the Chadian people's appreciation for the humanitarian aid which the Libyan 
Arab people have extended to emphasize the Chadian people's freedom.

Merger Talks 
LDO21518 Tripoli JANA in English 1402 GMT 2 Jan 82 
[Text] Tripoli, 2 Jan (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY) -~ A Syrian labour and popular delegation 
headed by Tawfiq Salhah, member of the Arab Ba'th Party's regional command and Mr Yusuf 


Ju'aydani, minister of labour and social affaris arrived in Tripoli yesterday evening to 
attend the General People's Congress due to start its proceedings this morning.

Mr Salhah told our correspondent that his visit to the Jamahiriyah comes for furthering 
the dialogue between the two sisterly countries, and to arrive at a final formula for the 
merger between Syria and Libya. 


He said the country's two people are fighting the same enemy: world imperialist, led by 
the U.S. and Israel. 


He said that Syrian and Libyan Arab peoples are also struggling to foil all treacherous 
attempts directed against the Arab nation's vital causes.
