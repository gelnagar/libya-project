LIBYA

Libya Blames Tunisians for Pipeline Explosion 
LDI31333 Kuwait KUNA in English 1036 GMT 13 Jan 84 


[Text] Tunis, Jan 13 (KUNA) — Libya has said that the blast 
which occurred tn the trans- Tunisia-Algerian pipeline “was per- 
petrated by Tunisians, and a Tunisian car was used to implement 
the plan,” a press report said here Friday 


The 4S’ S AB AH newspaper said that Libya sent a memorandum 
in response to the note which Tunisia had presented to the Libyan 
Government, saying that the blast last week at Qulayb al-Anz 
check point, two km from the Libyan border, was carried out by 
Tunisians who penetrated Libyan territories then returned to the 
Tunisian border from another side to give the impression that the 
operation was implemented by Libya-based attackers. 


The memorandum, according to the paper, has expressed Libya's 
readiness to carry out joint border patrols and to participate in 
mending the damages caused by the blast “not because of a guilty 
feeling, but to help the Tunisian economy.” 


The memorandum affirmed that “such an incident is harmful to 
the good and developed bilateral relations.”
