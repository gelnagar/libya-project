Summary (AI): A May 1984 CIA cable reports that 'Abd al-Qadir Baqdadi, the Libyan blamed for the fatal shooting of British policewoman Yvonne Fletcher, was under arrest in Tripoli without formal charges. It also says Col. Abd al-Rahman Shaybi was sent to London by Qadhafi to stop People's Bureau staff from defecting, threatening their families.
• Baqdadi was under arrest in Tripoli but had not been formally charged with any crime.
• As many as eight Libyans barricaded in the People's Bureau in London reportedly wanted political asylum in Britain.
• Libyan Intelligence Service learned of the planned defections and Qadhafi dispatched Shaybi to London to stop them.
• 1984-04: Fatal shooting of British policewoman Yvonne Fletcher outside the Libyan People's Bureau during the siege; the shooter is reported as 'Abd al-Qadir Baqdadi (1 killed)
• 1984: Shaybi sent to London by Qadhafi to prevent defections from the People's Bureau; families of those inside threatened with being held if they defected
