[Question] What has the Bureau of Employmnet done to protect Jordanian 
workers abroad? 


[Answer] The Bureau of Employment began to supervise the movement of workers 
to foreign countries in cooperation with the embassies concerned at the 
beginning of last year. We are now obtaining work visas directly from the 
embassies concerned for workers who are working abroad. An agreement has 
been reached with some of the Arab embassies in this connection and there 

are ongoing contacts with other embassies to regulate this ubject. 


In addition, the Ministry of Labor has taken a positive step to protect the 
interests of Jordanians working abroad by the appointment of two labor 
attaches in Jordanian embassies abroad to supervise Jordanian workers there. 
Two [attaches] have been appointed in Kuwait and the UAE, and we intend to 
appoint others in the countries in which there are Jordanians working. 


The Bureau of Employment has also been concerned with seeing to it that the 


workers are receiving fair wages. Brokers and agents have also been pro- 
hibited from contracting with workers in any way whatsoever.

LIBYAN COOPERATION PROTOCOL-- Amman, 3 Jul--Today the Libyan Arab Develop- 
ment Institute and Jordan's Royal Scientific Society signed a scientific 
and technological cooperation protocol. The protocol stipulates the 
promotion of scientific and technological research cooperation in develop- 
ment plans, the exchange of scientific and technological information as 
well as visits by experts and officials in the field. According to the 
protocol, joint research programs and cooperation will be carried out in 
the field of technology transfer in development plans. The two parties 
will also conduct research and studies on the better utilization of Arab 
and local natural resources. The protocol also provides for the formation 
of a joint committee to meet alternatively in Tripoli and Amman to follow 
up the implementation of the protocol and to propose new fields of coopera- 
tion between the two countries. Following the signing of the protocol, 
the Jordanian and the Libyan sides held a working session to discuss 
projects which will be implemented in the future and which include the 
carrying out of economic, solar energy, industrial and environmental 
studies and research. [Text] [Amman JNA in Arabic 1415 GMT 3 Jul 79 JN] 


AGRICULTURAL PROJECT LOANS--Frankfurt--JNA--The Jordanian and West German 
governments signed two loan agreements in Frankfurt yesterday for DM14 
million. The first agreement provides a DMl10-million loan to the Agricul- 
tural Loan Organization which will provide loans to Jordanian farmers for 
developing and improving the agricultural methods being used to make better 
use of their land and increase its agricultural productivity. The second 
agreement provides for a DM4-million loan by the German government to the 
Jordanian Cooperative Organization as indirect loans to the agricultural 
cooperatives in the kingdom to improve and increase the productivity of 
the farmers who are members of the cooperatives. The agreement was signed 
for the Jordanian government by Dr Hanna ‘Awdah, chairman of the National 
Planning Council, who is currently on a visit to West Germany. [Text]
