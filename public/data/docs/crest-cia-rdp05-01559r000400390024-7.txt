[Text] Al-Bayyan al Awwal, Dhulhijja 24, Oct, 10, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- As part of 
the continuous inciting for the strengthening of people's authority and people's democratic 
‘process, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the revolution, yesterday attended the 
lst session of the Basic People's Congress (BPC) in its 3rd regular session at Al-Bayyan 
al Awwal city. 

The leader in his speech addressed the BPC's which are holding their 3rd regular session 
prior to the General People's Congress (GPC), through Al-Bayyan al Awwal's (BPC). 

In his speech, the leader, stressed two main points. First, the democratic way in which 
the BPC puts the items for discussion on the agenda especially the general agenda by which 
the masses of the BPC decide the democratic and foreign policies. 

In this respect, the leader said that from now on the masses will put forward the agenda 
in order to further stress that the Libyan people are free and the masters of themselves 
without a ruler or a government. 

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the people's committees will be one of the sources for formulating 
the agenda through the files which are to be tabled to the people's congresses in all fields. 
He said the people's committees will assume such a role because as they are also responsible 
for implementing the resolutions of the people's congresses, these congresses might ask them 
to issue laws or certain rules that would help in implementing the resolutions of the 
people's congresses. 

The leader said the role of the people's congresses secretariats is to table the files, 
observations and requests of the people's committees to members of the Basic People's 
Congresses in the Jamahiriyah so as to clarify its views regarding the issues dealt with 
in these files and observations. 

Dealing with the method in which future internal issues are to be included on the general 
agenda, the leader said: "Any internal issue adopted by any Basic People's Congress shall 
be referred to the General Secretariat of the General People's Congress, which shall in 
turn circulate it to all Basic People's Congresses throughout the Jamahiriyah for debate 
in a true democratic method. 

The function of the General People's Congress would be to unify points of view and formulate 
the decisions of the Basic People's Congresses. 

Regarding the second point expressed by masses in demonstrations and public gatherings 
regarding issues imposed on us by the enemy, the leader said: "We should decide on how to 
face death and our fate. We should prepare for making sacrifices and showing resoluteness 
to stop the Zionist death wagon that is intent on destroying the Arab nation." 

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed the seriousness of such issues and the responsibility of the 
people towards them, adding that the Zionist death wagon that destroyed Palestine, Lebanon 
and Egypt will overrun Syria and the rest of the Arab nation and that the people should 

seriously consider confrontation because they will be responsible for all gains or losses. 

He said: "In the era of the masses, there is no hero who can single-handedly take such a 
grave decision. Only the entire body of the masses can decide their fate and take the 
decision that can safeguard it."
