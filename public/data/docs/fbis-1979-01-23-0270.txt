LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI WELCOMES GUINEAN PRESIDENT SEKW TOURE 
LD222104 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1815 GMT 22 Jan 79 LD 


[Excerpt ] Tripoli--Guinean President Ahmed Sekou Toure this evening arrived in Tripoli 
on a visit to the Jamahiriyah lasting several days. 


The esteemed guest was given an official reception at Tripoli International Airport where 
brother Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi led the welcoming party. Among those also present 
were Maj tAbd as-Salam Jallud, Lt Col Mustafa al-Kharrubi, and the secretaries and 
members of the diplomatic corps. 


Al-Qadhdhafi, Toure Begin Talks 
LD231028 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 22 Jan 79 LD 


(Text ] Arab Libyan-Guinean talks began last night. The talks were led by Col Mu!'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi and President Ahmed Sekou Toure, Taking part in these talks were: Staff 
Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud; Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, foreign secretary; 
Engineer Nuri al-Fayturi al-Madani, secretary of communications; Muhammad !Ali at-Tabu, 
secretary of agriculture; the assistant secretary for political affairs and the director 
of the Department of Cooperation at the Foreign Secretariat, 


On the Guinean side the talks were attended by: Dr Lansana Beavogui, member of the 
Political Bureau and prime minister; Mamdy Keita, member of the Political Bureau and 
minister of culture and education; Fily Sissoko, minister of foreign affairs; N'faly 
Sangare, minister of planning and economic cooperation; Saikou Barry, minister of foreign 
commerce and F, Camara, minister for Islamic affairs, 


Col Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi delivered a speech in which he welcomed the Guinean guest, 

hailing the good relations between the two fraternal peoples--the Arab Libyan and the 
Guinean, Colonal Al-Qadhdhafi saluted the two Islamic revolutions in the Jamahiriyah 
and in Guinea for their noble stands toward the just causes, The colonel praised the 
steps which the cooperation between the two countries has achieved in various fields, 


Ahmed Sekou Toure also spoke, He hailed the firm relations between the Arab Libyan 

and Guinean peoples, He said any victory achieved in the Jamahiriyah is a victory for the 
Guinean revolution and its people, He hailed the vast achievements realized by the 

two revolutions everywhere in the Jamahiriyah and Guinea, He also hailed the cooperation 
between the two countries, pointing to the existence of potentials to achieve more 
progress by widening the scope of cooperation in various fields, 


President Ahmed Sekou Toure stressed the importance of the role being played by the two 
revolutions and their joirt struggle in the service of the African and Islamic causes, 


The talks were adjourned for evening prayers and were resumed afterward. They continued 
until about 2100, 


A closed meeting was held between Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and the guest, President 
Ahmed Sekou Toure, following the end of the talks,
