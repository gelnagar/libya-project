LIBYA

AL-'UBAYDI_ SPEAKS TO PRESS ON FOREIGN POLICY 


LDO90010 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 8 Jan 81 


[Text] Brother ‘Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the People's Committee of the People's 
Bureau for External Liaison, said at a press conference held tonight in Tripoli that the 
methods of dealing with peoples and the strengthening of our relations with them will be 
through the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for External Liaison and through 
people's bureaus abroad and the embassies of the state accredited to the Jamahiriyah. He 
explained that the Jamahiriyah is certain that this form of our people's experiment will 
consolidate our relations with sisterly and friendly peoples and will further promote these 
relations. He said it is regrettable that some states do not understand this experiment 

and that we stand ready to explain our views on this experiment, which is an irrevocable 
decision of the people. 


Answering a question on whether the Jamahiriyah would treat reciprocally countries which do 
not recognize people's bureaus he said that we will be ready for dialogue and willing to 
explain the objectives of these bureaus before resorting to reciprocal treatment. On re- 
garding the joint Libyan-Chadian communique, he said that it was the right of every two 
countries to agree to an action program and they were free to make their own decisions. 

He said that there was no neédd for the interference of any other state in such matters 

and confirmed once against the support of the Libyan people to its brother the Chadian 
people and its national government. 


On the resolution of the General People's Congress concerning support of national libera- 
tion movements, he said that this resolution stemmed from the belief of the Libyan people 

in the freedom of man everywhere, confirming that the Jamahiriyah would continue to support 
these movements which defend the freedom and dignity of man everywhere. Brother al-'Ubaydi 
welcomed the invitation of the Tunisian prime minister, Mohamed Mzali, concerning the revival 
of Libyan-Tunisian relations and expressed the Jamahiriyah's readiness to consolidate 
cooperation and relations with Tunisia in order to deprive the colonialist countires of the 
chance to drive a wedge between our two peoples. 


He spoke about Arab-American relations during the era of the new president, Reagan, and 
said that improvement of relations with the United States will be through its understanding 
of our fateful problems, without bias in favor of the Zionist enemy. He added that our 
relations with the American people were good and that the majority of the American people 
understood the truth about our stand.
