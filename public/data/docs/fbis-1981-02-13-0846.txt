LIBYA

JALLUD ARRIVES IN CHAD; PUBLIC RECEPTION REPORTED 


LD122026 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1915 GMT 12 Feb 81 


[Text] Ndjamena, 12 Feb (JANA)--Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud arrived at 
Ndjamena airport this evening on a visit to Chad. There was a popular and an official 
welcome for Staff Major Jallud. Heading those who welcomed him were Brother Abdel Kader 
Kamougue, deputy head of the national government in Chad [title as received], Brother Ahmat 
Acyl, Chadian foreign minister, members of the Chadian Government, and some heads of 
political missions accredited to Chad. 


The JANA correspondent reports that the Chadian capital today witnessed a great and popular 
march in which various sections of the youth of fraternal Chad took part as a welcome for 
brother Staff Major Jallud, and during which they stressed the fateful cohesion between 

the two fraternal peoples, the Libyan Arab people and the Chadian people.

The marchers proceeded through the streets ol! Ndjamena chanting "Long live the two 
revolutions," determined to face up to all colonialist attempts to encroach upon the 
independence and unity of the Chadian people and the alliance between the two fraternal 
peoples, the Libyan and Chadian peoples.
