LIBYA

KUWAITI FINANCE MINISTER ATIQI ARRIVES 8 SEP 
LD082013 Tripoli JANA in English 1701 GMT 8 Sep 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 8 Sep JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Kuwaiti financial minister, ‘Abd 
al-Rahman Atiqi, arrived in Tripoli this morning on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


The Kuwait minister was received at Tripoli International Airport by Muhammad az- 
Zarruk Rajab, the secretary of the General People's Committee for Treasury, its under- 
secretary, the director of the Libyan Arab Foreigm Bank and a representative from the 
Protocol Department at the Foreign Secretariat. 


In a statement to our correspondent, Mr Atiqi commended the friendly relations between 
the Jamahiriyah and his country. This cooperation, he added, stems from an awareness 

and understanding of the joint efforts to be made at all levels during this difficult 

stage through which the Arab nation is passing. 


He added that the Jamahiriyah and Kuwait have played a key role to this effect and that 
this visit would promote this role in the service of the Arab nation.
