Tripoli Reports on ‘Invasion’ 
NC260912 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 
26 Oct 83 


[Text] The United States, Britain |as heard], and six allied states 
in the Caribbean staged an armed barbaric invasion of Grenada 
yesterday. At 0550 local time yesterday, an extensive landing of 
invaders took place at the international airport area, which is 
under construction on the island. Heavy clashes broke out 
between the invaders and the island's defenders who put up 
strong resistance because of their belief in their freedom and fate. 


The head of the U.S. Administration, who is power hungry, 
announced the news of this barbaric invasion of Grenada without 
concealing the evil intentions and disgraceful actions to break up 
the revolution there against Grenada’s will. Unequivocally he 
declared that the United States invaded Grenada with the par- 
ticipation of fascist governments in Antigua, Barbados, Domi- 
nica, Jamaica, Saint Lucia, and Saint Vincent. The United 
States, Britain, and the fascist governments carried out this 
barbaric operation of invading Grenada using an aircraft carrier 
and several warships that appeared at dawn along the coast of 
the island. 


The U.S. Administration paved the way for this invasion, which 
is inconsistent with the simplest human rights and violates all 
international laws and charters, by a meeting including the heads 
of fascist states and governments of the so-called Caribbean 
union. These governments agreed without hesitation to join their 
forces with the U.S. invaders who landed in Grenada and 
directed their guns and incendiary shells against the struggling 
people of this small island. It was reported that the Armed Forces 
and the People’s Militia in Grenada arc still bravely resisting the 
U.S. invading forces which landed on the island at dawn yester- 
day. The radio|not further identified] reported that the resistance 
men shot down nine U.S. helicopters and killed hundreds of the 
invading forces
