Summary (AI): An FBIS London translation excerpt of a Qadhafi speech, broadcast by Tripoli domestic radio, in which he denounces Sudan's Numayri and Egypt, warns the US against intervening in the Arab region, and says Libya has no forces in Chad. It concerns foreign policy and gives no specific Tripoli locations.
• The excerpt is from a Tripoli domestic Arabic broadcast dated 28 March 1985, filed by FBIS London.
• Qadhafi accuses Egypt and Sudan of asking the US for help and warns that any US intervention will lead to a confrontation with America.
• Qadhafi says Sudan offered its airports at Khartoum, Juba, Kasala and Gadarif for transferring Jews to Israel.
• Qadhafi denies French claims that Libyan forces are advancing into southern Chad, saying Libya has no forces in Chad.
• 1985-03-28: Qadhafi says the revolution is escalating in Khartoum and that Numayri cannot find enough prisons for demonstrators; he announces support for the Sudanese opposition.

TO AMERICA THAT ANY PEACEFUL OR VIOLENT REACTIONS INSIDE THE ARAB

HOMELAND, OR EVEN INSIDE THEIR OWN CAPITALS -- AND IF AMERICA 
ACCEPTS THIS CALL AND THROWS ITSELF IN THE CONFLICT INSIDE THE 
ARAB NATION, THEN WE WILL CONFRONT AMERICA TOO, AND WHEN WE CONFRONT 
AMERICA ON THE ARAB LAND THIS WILL BE A GLORIOUS BATTLE, WE HAVE
