Saudi Prince Bandar’s Remarks on Israel Repudiated 
LD121933 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 12 Apr 84 


[Text] Washington, 12 Apr(JANA)-— Prince Bandar ibn Sultan, 
Saudi ambassador to Washington, stated during a reception 
organized in his honor by the Washington Press Club that the 
Arabs were ready to establish what he called “peace with Israel” 
on the condition that it give back the occupied Arab lands. He 
also said that the plan of U.S. President Reagan for the Middle 
East and the Fes summit resolutions were the sole peace plans. 


We believe that Prince Bandar ibn Sultan is not entitled to speak 
for and on behalf of the Arabs now that he promises that the 
Arabs can recognize Israel and also decides that the Reagan and 
the Fes plans are the sole fundamental peace plans. 


Prince Bandar can express his personal views and personal 
opinion, but he has no right to speak en behalf of the Arabs in a 
cause for which the Arab nation has offered scores of martyrs, 
and is still (?eager) to offer more so that Palestine may be 
liberated inch by inch. This nation realizes the nature of the 
Arab-Zionist conflict, and realizes that this conflict must be 
decisvely settled by one of the two sides remaining [as received]. 


Arab People’s Congress Body Studies ‘Zionist Threats’ 
LD130904 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 13 Apr 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 April — The Delegates Council at the Perma- 
nent Secretariat of the Arab People’s Congress has studied the 
growing Zionist threats against Syria during the past few days. 


At its meeting here yesterday evening, the Delegates Council 
warned that the Zionist enemy and his American allies, having 
suffered a true defeat in Lebanon, were now coordinating plans 
and concentrating capabilities against Syria in preparation for 
dealing a blow to its frontline and overturning the balances in the 
region for the good of imperialism and the Zionist enemy. 


The Council called on the national Arab masses and forces and 
on the international organizations to be on the alert and to work 
for checking the growing Zionist threats and to bolster political 
and material solidarity with Syria and with the national Leb- 
anese movement and the Palestine resistance. 


The Delegates Council continued preparations for an Arab and 
international movement against the Zionist crime and for the 
assertion of the legitimate rights of the Palestinian Arab people 
to self-determination and the setting up of their independent state 
over their land. 


The Council also considered the plan of movement on the Arab 
level with a view to contacting the various parties to the Arab 
liberation movement and to consult with them over the escalation 
of the struggle against imperialism, Z,onism and reaction in a 
manner which would bolster further the victory achieved by the 
alliance of Syria with the national Lebanese masses and forces
