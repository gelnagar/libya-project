LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES IRAN'S ‘AZIZI_ 


LD090054 Tehran IRNA in Englisch 2003 GMT 8 Feb 83 


[Text] Tehran, Feb.8, IRNA -- Deputy Foreign Minister Ahmad ‘Azizi submitted a message 
of President Khameni'i to the Libyan president, Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi in Tripoli, Libya, 
Monday. During the meeting, ‘Azizi expressed happiness that the meeting was being held 
on the day that the great operations of the Islamic forces had started against the 
Iraqi aggressors. He continued that this victory not only belonged to Iranian com- 
batants but to all the Muslime and oppressed of the world. 


"The deputy foreign minister said that the Wal-Fajr operations were very serious and 
would continue until ultimate victory. Hoping for the health of the imam, President 
Khamene'i, and other authorities of the Islamic Republic and expressing delight over 
the visit of the Iranian delegation, President al-Qadhdhafi said that Libya's stand 
towards the Islamic Republic was a stand of unity, revolutionary and fundamentally 
unchangeable. 


The Libyan president continued that Libya was beside the Iranian nation and revolution 
before the overthrow of the shah's regime, and the revolutionary government of Libya 
was the only one which did not establish relations with the defunct shah's regime. 


In conclusion, ‘Aziz stressed the importance of the joint session of foreign ministers 
of Libya and Syria to be held in Tehran, hoping that it would achieve positive 

results with other member countries of the Steadfastness Front for taking an anti- 
oppression and anti-Zionist stand.

Al-'Ubaydi Meets ‘Azizi 


LDO71039 Tripoli JANA in English 0840 GMT 7 Feb 83 


{Text] Tripoli, Rabi’ Thani 23, Feb 7, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The foreign liaison

KUNA CITES JALLUD ON POLISARIO, MOROCCO 


LDO41541 Kuwait KUNA in Arabic 1409 GMT 4 Feb 83 


[Text] Rabat, 4 Feb (KUNA) -- Libya's second strongman, Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud, was 
quoted today as having said his country has not recognized the SDAR [Saharan Democratic 
Arab Republic] and never will. 


The Moroccan opposition paper, AL-BAYANE, said today that Jallud declared his country 
will fight (?Polisario) until the end if it betrays pan-Arab unity. 


Jallud expressed the hope that the clouds of discord between his country and Morocco 
will disappear soon. He added: "We rely on the peoples of the Arab Maghreb to lead the 
Arab nation to the shores of safety." 


The paper pointed out that a change has been t ‘king place in the Libyan attitude toward 
the Saharan question and called for exploiting this change for the benefit of the Arab 


countries. 


JANA: IRAQI OFFICIAL SURVIVES ATTEMPT ON LIFE 


LD081942 Tripoli JANA in English 1822 GMT 8 Feb 83 
[Text] Tehran, Rabi’ Thani 24, Feb 8, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- Reports from Tehran 
said today that Muhammad Samir, deputy-chief of the Iraqi Intelligence Service, had 
escaped an assassination attempt on his life. He sustained minor injuries and [is] 
still receiving treatment in the hospitals.
