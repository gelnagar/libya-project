Summary (AI): A December 1984 CIA Directorate of Intelligence assessment describes Libya supplying weapons (pistols, rifles, silenced pistols, RPG-7s, Katyusha rockets, explosives) to terrorist groups abroad. Most of the document is redacted and nothing specific to Tripoli sites is named.
• Libya reportedly supplied at least 30 silenced pistols to anti-Arafat Fatah rebels in 1983.
• Libya reportedly received nearly one half billion dollars worth of armaments from the Soviet Union, Eastern Europe, China and North Korea in 1984.
• Libya purchased over 2200 tons of small arms and ammunition from Spain in the first half of 1984, per press reports.
• The Irish authorities seized 5 tons of weapons from the S.S. Claudia, which had sailed from Tripoli in early 1973.
• Brazil approved sale of 20,000 9mm Taurus MT-12A submachineguns to Libya in mid-1984.
