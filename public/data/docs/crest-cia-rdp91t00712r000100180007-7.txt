Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery analysis reports heavy-lift transporter and armour activity at Tajura Barracks and Vehicle Storage Area in Libya, linked to Libyan operations in Chad, plus defensive reinforcement at Ounianga Kebir old airfield in Chad. Coordinates are given for the Tajura site.
• At least 34 empty heavy-lift transporters, 21 of them in convoy, remained in the northern section of the Tajura installation.
• At least 39 armoured vehicles were discernible at Ounianga Kebir airfield.
• Five SF.260 light-strike aircraft and three Hip helicopters were present at Ounianga Kebir.
• The imagery analyst said the Tajura activity may be related to ongoing Libyan operations in Chad.
• Tajura coordinates given as 34-49-50N 013-27-30E.
• 1983-08-16: High level of heavy-lift transporter activity; at least five probable tanks loaded; elements of one tank battalion and one mechanized infantry battalion parked outside vehicle storage buildings.
• 1983-08-16: At least ten 130mm M-46 field guns and a BMP-equipped mechanized infantry battalion arrived; at least 39 armoured vehicles discernible in defensive positions around the airfield.
