LIBYA

JABIR MEETS PALESTINIAN FACTION LEADERS 


LDO30404 Tripoli JANA in English 1800 GMT 2 Apr 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada Thani 19, April 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- Commander in 
chief of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, at midday today 
received a number of the leaders of the factions within the Palestinian resistance: 
Ahmad Jibril, secretary general of Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine-Gen- 
eral Command; "Isam al-Qadi, regional secretary of As-Sa'iqah (the organisation of 
vanguards of the popular liberation war) and Dr Samir Ghawshah, secretary general of 
Popular Front of Palestinian Struggle. 


CONFERENCE ON GREEN BOOK TO START 7 APR 


LDO31639 Tripoli JANA in English 1427 GMT 3 Apr 83 


[Excerpts] Al-Bayan al-Awal, Jumada Thani 20, April 3, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- On 
April 7, the first international conference on Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi's thought (Green 
Book) will take place in Jamahiriyah. 1,000 intellectuals and researchers will take 
part in conference in addition to political leaders from various countries in their 
capacity as intellectuals. 


The conference is to take place at the University of Qar Yunis in the city of Al-Bayan 
al-Awal underthe auspices of and the participation of the teacher, thinker Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi who provides fundamental and final solutions to all human problems. 


The starting day of the conference coincides with the anniversary of the students’ 
revolution which brought out the creative abilities of the students in Jamahiriyah, 
thus forming an important revolutionary force part of the real forces of the revolution 
and in compliance with the first part of the Green Book. The conference is to be held 
in response to the wishes of many leading intellectuals in order to explore further the 
universal dimensions of this pioneering humanitarian thought. At the Caracas symposium 
in November 1981, the international intellectuals who participated insisted upon the 
importance of holding such a conference to provide conducive opportunity in order to 
bring out the contents of this thought which stimulates human knowledge and makes it 

an eternal thought, permanently inspiring. 


The conference is an indication of the wide universal dimension of the third universal 
theory which has begun to have practical influence in the world as a whole. While the 
participation of a large crowd of distinguished intellectuals from Europe, North 
America, Latin America, Africa, Asia, and Australia point out that scientific interest 
and towering intellectual belief in this theory is not limited to the Third World coun- 
tries and that it extends to all parts of the world from East to West, to North to 
South. 


Hundreds of journalists, reporters, writers, and politicians, international and local, 
will cover this great international conference of thought which was in response to 
those journalists’ and intellectuals" wishes. 


All arrangements and preparations have been made for their journey to Jamahiriyah and 
to cover the conference giving international aspect to it.
