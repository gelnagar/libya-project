Summary (AI): A 1985 New York Post article reports on the book "The Specialist" by "Gayle Rivers," which describes an alleged 1982 Libyan plot to bomb a New York subway train in midtown Manhattan. The Sunday Times of London disputed the book's account and identified the author as Raymond Brooks, an arms dealer; the publisher stood by the book.
• The book The Specialist was to be published in Britain by Sidgwick & Jackson and in the US by Stein and Day, with a $60,000 promotion budget.
• The US hardcover was priced at $16.95 with about 50,000 copies about to reach shelves.
• The alleged plot involved the Puerto Rican Rastafarians, the Jewish Defense League, the Black Liberation Army and the Islamic League.
• The Sunday Times said the author had failed to make the grade as an SAS territorial reservist.
• 1982: Alleged Libyan-backed plan, described in the book The Specialist, to assemble explosives from several terrorist groups on a subway train for detonation in midtown Manhattan; the Sunday Times disputed this account.
