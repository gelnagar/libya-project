Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a February 1990 interview in which Qadhdhafi discusses Arab and African politics, including Mubarak's OAU chairmanship, the Libyan-Chadian problem, Israeli influence in Africa, and Syria-Iraq relations. It contains no information about Tripoli sites, security, or repression.
• Qadhdhafi says Libya faces continuous U.S. aggression and that the Libyan-Chadian problem should be resolved during Mubarak's tenure.
• Qadhdhafi claims Israel helped turn African countries against the Arabs, citing Senegal, Mali, Niger, Chad and Ethiopia.
• Qadhdhafi says he mediated between Syria and Iraq after the October War and that their 1978-era summit reconciliation was later undone by the Iran-Iraq war.

((TEXT)) IBRAHIM NAFI': CONCERNING ALL THE DISCUSSIONS WHICH 
TOOK PLACE ON THE ARAB AND AFRICAN SITUATION, PRESIDENT HUSNI 
MUBARAK, IN HIS CAPACITY AS A CHAIRMAN OF THE ORGANIZATION OF 
AFRICAN UNITY ((OAU)) IN ITS CURRENT SESSION, HAS TRIED TO TAKE SOME 
‘POSITIVE STEPS TOWARD ENDING THE DISPUTES AND CONFLICTS BY PEACEFUL 
MEANS. HOW CAN WE REINFORCE AND ENHANCE THIS MOVE THROUGH FUTURE 
STEPS? 

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: PRESIDENT MUBARAK HAS A VERY ACTIVE, DYNAMIC 
PERSONALITY. ALSO, HE IS LOYAL TOWARD ANY OF HIS UNDERTAKINGS. 
HOWEVER, HIS CHAIRMANSHIP OF THE OAU CAME DURING A PERIOD WHICH 
WITNESSED A NUMBER OF OPEN FRONTS, WHICH NEED CLOSE ATTENTION. BUT 
HE IS PREOCCUPIED WITH EGYPT'S PROBLEMS ON THE ONE HAND, AND THE 
PROBLEMS CONCERNING JOINT ARAB ACTION THROUGH THE ARAB COOPERATION 
COUNCIL, THE PROBLEMS OF PALESTINE, THE INTIFADAH, THE EMIGRATION, 
THE NO-PEACE, NO-WAR STATE OF AFFAIRS, AND THE UNITY OF EUROPE ON 
THE OTHER HAND. ADD TO ALL THIS THE PROBLEMS OF THE MEDITERRANEAN

ALL THE AFRICANS OUR ENEMIES. WE HAVE TO ADMIT THE ISRAELIS' 
ACHIEVEMENTS IN THIS REGARD IN SENEGAL, MALI, NIGER, CHAD, AND 
ETHIOPIA. OF COURSE, THEIR FOCUS IS ON THE COUNTRIES WHICH BORDER 
THE ARAB HOMELAND, SO THAT THEY WILL HARASS THE SUDAN, EGYPT, 
ALGERIA, AND LIBYA, AND CORDON OFF THE ARAB HOMELAND BY ZIONIST 
ACTION. THIS ZIONIST CONSPIRACY HAS PLAYED AN EFFECTIVE ROLE IN THE 
AFRICAN'S PREJUDICE AGAINST THE ARABS. THERE IS NO AFRICAN-ARAB 
PROBLEM WHATSOEVER; BUT THERE IS A CONSPIRACY AGAINST THE AFRICAN 
AND THE ARAB AMONG ZIONIST IMPERIALISM, RACISM, AND THE CRUSADERS. 

IBRAHIM NAFI': IS THE LATEST ON THE EFFORTS OF RECONCILIATION OR 
MEDIATION BETWEEN THE PALESTINIANS AND SYRIA, AND SYRIA AND IRAQ, AN 
IMPORTANT ISSUE THAT THE ARAB CITIZEN SHOULD KNOW ABOUT? 
_ COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: IT IS TRULY REGRETTABLE THAT THINGS SHOULD 
HAVE GONE THIS FAR, TO THE EXTENT OF RESORTING TO MEDIATION BETWEEN 
SYRIA AND IRAQ, BECASUE THERE HAVE NOT BEEN SOLID REASONS TO CREATE 
ANY DISAGREEMENT BETWEEN THE TWO. ON THE CONTRARY, BOTH ARE RULED 
BY ONE PARTY. THERFORE, THIS PARTY SHOULD MAKE IT A PRIORITY NOT TO 
ABANDON ITS INTERESTS IN THE TWO COUNTRIES, SO AS TO REMAIN 
CONSISTENT WITH THE DEMAND FOR UNITY OF WHICH THE ARAB YOUTHS ARE 
CONVINCED. SO, IF THE PARTY ACHIEVES VICTORY IN THE ARAB COUNTRIES, 
UNITY WILL COME SPONTANEOUSLY. IF IT ACHIEVES VICTORY IN SYRIA AND 
IRAQ, FOR EXAMPLE, THEN WHY NOT ESTABLISH UNITY SPONTANEOUSLY 
BETWEEN THEM. I THINK THIS REFUTES THE BA'THIST ARGUMENTS. THE 
BA'THISTS SHOULD HAVE PROVEN THEIR CREDIBILITY. BUT, INSTEAD OF 
THIS, WE ARE NOW TALKING ABOUT A MEDIATION TO BRIDGE THE GAP BETWEEN 
SYRIA AND IRAQ; WHILE THE LAND IS COMMON, THE PEOPLE ARE ONE, THE 
PARTY IS ONE, AND THE GRAVE THREAT FACING THEM IS THE SAME--THAT IS 
ZIONISM AND THE EXPANSIONIST AMBITIONS IT CONTEMPLATES IN THE ARAB 
NATION. FOR MY PART, I HAVE EXERTED A TREMENDOUS EFFORT. I WENT TO 
IRAQ. I ALSO WENT TO SYRIA AFTER THE OCTOBER WAR. I TRIED TO 
RECONCILE THEM, AND MY EFFORTS LED TO THEIR MEETING IN A SUMMIT. 
THEN, THERE WAS THE IRANIAN-IRAQI WAR. IRAQ SAID SYRIA HAS HATCHED 
A PLOT AGAINST IT. SO, AFTER IT WAS RECONCILIATION, IT BECAME A 
CONSPIRACY. THE TRUTH IS THAT THE CONSPIRACY IS THAT OF THE ENEMY. 
THIS LED ONCE MORE TO UNJUSTIFIED BOYCOTT. HOWEVER, WE ARE SPARING 
NO EFFORTS NOW TO MEND THE SITUATION FOR THE SAKE OF THE UNITY OF 
THE ENTIRE ARAB NATION.
