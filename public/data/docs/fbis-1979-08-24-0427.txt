PLANE ON DOMESTIC FLIGHT HIJACKED, LANDS IN CYPRUS 

NC241012 Nicosia Domestic Service in Greek 1000 GMT 24 Aug 79 NC 


[Text] A Libyan airliner that was hijacked this morning while on a domestic flight 
landed at Larnaca Airport at 1130 for refuelling. Originally the Cypriot authorities 
refused to allow the plane to land and it then headed for Beirut. After a while the 
plane returned to Cyprus airspace and reported that it was going to make a forced 
landing because, as the pilot reported, the plane ran out of fuel. Following this, 
the plane was allowed to land for refuelling. The Libyan plane is a Boeing 727 and 
it carries about 60 passengers and a crew of 9. So far the identity of the hijackers 
has not become known. Police and military forces are on the alert at Larnaca Airport. 


Surrender of Hijacker
