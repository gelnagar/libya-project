[Text] A few days ago, before the ministers of foreign affairs of Arab 
countries met in Tunisia to look into Tunisia's complaint against Libya,

must be condemned, Libyan intervention must also be condemned." 


The conversation between the two leaders was not confined to this point. 
It turned into rebuke and accusations concerning the Moroccan Sahara. 
[They talked about] who was supporting the Polisario and inciting them 
against Morocco, and about who was extending his influence and his actions 
to Chad, Ethiopia and other areas of Africa and the Middle East. 


The discussion stopped short of becoming an Algerian-Libyan dispute. To 
preserve good bilateral relations, neither one of the two parties wants 
this discussion to turn into bickering, at least not at this time.
