ADEN PACT INFORMATION MINISTERS CONCLUDE MEETING 


LD290816 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0215 GMT 29 Nov 81 


[Text] The information ministers of the countries of the Aden Pact for Friendship and 
Cooperation Between the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, the PDRY and the 
Ethiopian Socialist Republic concluded their me:tings in Tripoli yesterday evening. At 

the conclusion of the meeting, a joint communique was published, in which the items of the 
joint communique was published, in which the items of the joint communique of the political 
committee of the Aden Pact at its extraordinary meeting in Addis Ababa were confirmed. 


The communique stressed the necessity of increasing cooperation and coordination between 
information apparatus in the three countries and the continued exposing of colonialist 
(?plans) aimed at (?poisoning) public opinion and at a military imperialist invasion. 

It also stressed the importance of arousing the masses to (?overcome) the instruments of 
domination, exploitation and [word indistinct] and to strengthen the confidence of the masses 
in themselves and their (?awareness) in confronting colonialism, imperialism and reaction, 
and to support the people's struggle against imperialism, colonialism and Zionism. 


The communique stressed that the information media are a progressive instrument used by 

the masses to confront their enemies and the information apparatus in the three countries 
is an instrument to mobilize all active forces in supporting freedom and progress every- 
where. The communique added that any agreement was reached on coordinating the informa- 
tion efforts between the three countries to abort imperialist plans and to confront hostile 
plans, including the U.S. military maneuvers in the Arab region, and coordinating the 
stands of the three countries in the international arena and bolstering good neighborli- 
ness, peace and cooperation in accordance with the charters and principles of the regional 
and international organizations to which they belong.

AL-QADHDEAFI PHONES AL-ASAD ON DAMASCUS "CRIME" 
LD301904 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1835 GMT 30 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 30 Nov (JANA) -- A telephone contact took place today between the brother 
commander of the revolution and Syria President Hafiz al-Asad. They discussed the current 
situation in the Arab region, the failure of the Fes Arab summit conference and the need to 
continue tc confront the imperialist-Zionist offensive against the Arab nation. 


They also discussed yesterday's barbaric, bestial crime committed by the criminal elements 
of the Muslim Brotherhood at Al-Azbakiyah quarter in Damascus, in which many children, women 
and elderly were killed. The brother commander strongly denounced this barbaric act by the 
reactionary plotters, members of the Muslim Brotherhood, who used religion as a cover for 
committing bestial and barbaric acts. 


The brother commander of the revolution affirmed that this senseless against the 
innocent people of Syria reflects the bloody and criminal characteristics of all the sub- 
versive activities and moves made by this agent clique, which are aimed at harming the 


steadfastness of the Syrian people in the face of the Zionist enemy, imperialism and reaction.

MIRGHANI SUGGESTS CHAD-SUDAN BORDER COMMITTEE 


JN301104 Khartoum SUNA in English 1043 GMT 30 Nov 81 


[Text] Khartoum, 30 Nov (SUNA) -- Foreign Minister Muhammad Mirghani stated that Sudan 
asked Chadi President Goukouni Oueddei to form a joint Chadi-Sudano committee assigned 
to solve all problems of borders between the two countries. 


Mirghani said that Sudan's demand came after President Goukouni asked Libyan troops to 
evacuate Chadi territories. 


He proclaimed that Sudan asked the current chairman of OAU to assign a part of the pan- 
African peace-keeping force of Chad to patrol the Sudan, Chadi borders. 


He reaffirmed that Sudan exerts efforts for restoring stability in Chad which would not 
prevail unless national reconciliation was achieved and a just, free and democratic 


elections took place. 


He concluded that Sudan does not support any partner of Chadi dispute but it will boost 
the one who Chadi people selects.

NUMAYRI ADDRESSES NEW CABINET, SOCIALIST UNION 


JN281619 Khartoum SUNA in Arabic 1440 GMT 28 Nov 81 


[Text] Khartoum, 28 Nov (SUNA) -- President Numayri today addressed the joint session 

of the newly formed Cabinet and the Socialist Union Politburo. The president asserted 
there is a wide scope for serving the nation and that honorable work in all fields and 

at all levels has the same value and importance. He expressed his gratitude and 
appreciation for all those officials who have shouldered their responsibilities for some 
time and who have made efforts and contributions in accordance with their ability. He 
also said that the needs for change do not mean that there were shortcomings in all fields 
and cannot be justified in all cases by a lack of skill or by bad performance. 


President Numayri described the current stage as that of self-criticism in order to 
change an old and familiar line in which all the authority was confined to Khartoum 
instead of the entire Sudanese territory. He said after the ruler issues a decision he 
must not be prevented from following it up. He should not act alone and all information 
must be available to him before making his decision. The responsibility for facing 
dissipation must not be confined to complaints so that the officials responsible for pro- 
duction cannot think that the way to face their problems is to shed useless tears. 
Efficiency in leadership does not mean monopolizing leadership. Public office is not a 
means of protecting officials. Good leadership means honest men who do not misuse their 
tongues. Leaders should not form cliques, elite as pleasure-seeking groups, or pretend 
to be wisemen. 


Speaking about the requirements of the next phase, President Numayri called on all 
officials to shoulder their responsibilities and to play a vital role in all fields. He 
also called for integration among the political, legislative, executive and popular 
bodies to overcome the difficulties. He said his choice of his Cabinet members was based 
on the fact that he wanted them to be the revolutionary vanguards against dissipation, 
negligence and low production in all positions and fields. 


President Numayri said the country is experiencing a pressing economic crisis. He said, 
however, that this crisis is not difficult to solve because the causes are obvious and 
are mainly due to the instability between what we import, mainly oil, and what we export, 
such as agricultural products. There is a decrease in exports and an increase in imports. 
He said we have no choice but to double our efforts to increase production. We should 
encourage labor, propose means to increase our exports and to reduce imports. 


President Numayri asserted that the new Cabinet is a link in reorganizing the govern- 
ment's departments to realize the principle of decentralization. He referred to reducing 
the number of ministries from 18 to 13 and to reducing portfolios from 44 to 33 as a 
result of the establishment of the regional governments. The number of military attaches 
abroad has been reduced by seven, consulates abroad have also been reduced by seven and 
some embassies have been closed. The president said the reduction in government expendi- 
ture will not affect production and basic services to citizens. Production will be raised 
to satisfy the local market and to export the surplus. Dissipation and waste must end. 
All negligent persons will be punished. The president called for adhering to and 
implementing this program.
