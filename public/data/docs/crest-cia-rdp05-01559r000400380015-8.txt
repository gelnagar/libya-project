Summary (AI): An FBIS London flash reports a 78-minute recorded press conference by Muammar al-Qadhdhafi, held in Tripoli on 20 October 1977 and broadcast on Tripoli domestic radio. He discusses the role of the People's Congresses, support for the Palestinian people, and Libya's poor relations with the United States.
• Press conference recorded and held in Tripoli on 20 October 1977, broadcast by Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic at 1322 GMT on 22 October
• Qadhdhafi said relations with the United States were bad and that the US had put Libya on its blacklist because of Libyan support for the Palestinians
• Qadhdhafi said support for the Palestinian people was a foregone conclusion and warned it could invite aggression against the Jamahiriyah
• 1977-10-20: Qadhdhafi holds a recorded press conference (78 minutes) on the People's Congresses, Palestinian support and foreign loans; broadcast by Tripoli domestic service on 22 October 1977
