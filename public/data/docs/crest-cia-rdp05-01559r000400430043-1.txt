((EXCERPTS)) ((AL-QADHDHAFI CONTINUES)) ONE UPON A TIME 
LIBYA WAS EMBROILED IN A CONFLICT BETWEEN UGANDA AND 
TANZANIA. WE CONSIDER THAT WRONG. TIME HAS NOW PASSED 
SINCE THAT HAPPENED BUT WE CONSIDER IT WRONG. HOWEVER, WE 
SUPPORTED THE REVOLUTION IN UGANDA AND THE REVOLUTION 
SUCCEEDED. WE STILL STAND BY THAT. I MEAN, ANY POPULAR 
REVOLUTION AGAINST REACTION AND IMPERIALISM SHOULD BE 
SUPPORTED BY US. HOWEVER, WHEN IT'S A STATE AGAINST A 
STATE, TO BE ALIGNED WITH ONE STATE AGAINST ANOTHER STATE, 
EVEN IF THERE WAS A DIFFERENCE -- ONE A REACTIONARY STATE 
AND THE OTHER ((WORD INDISTINCT)) STATE -- AS FAR AS WE ARE 
CONCERNED, WE AS ARABS, AS AFRICANS, WE SHOULD NOT EMBROIL 
OURSELVES IN CONFLICTS SUCH AS THESE. 

WE SHOULD OFFER A HELPING HAND TO THE AFRICANS AND COMBAT 
THE ZIONIST INFLUENCE WHICH IS NOW INFILTRATING AFRICA, 
USING OUR INTERVENTION IN SOME OF THESE CONFLICTS. THANK 
GOD WE HAVE COURAGE AND SELF-CONFIDENCE WHICH ENABLES US TO 
CRITICIZE OURSELVES PUBLICLY, DECLARING OUR MISTAKES AND 
SAYING THAT WE HAVE NOW UNDERSTOOD THOSE MISTAKES AND THAT 
WE WILL WORK TO EXTEND A HELPING HAND TO OUR AFRICAN 
BROTHERS. 

((QUESTION) ) THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY IN GENERAL, AND 
THE ARAB AND ISLAMIC PEOPLES IN PARTICULAR, FELT OPTIMISTIC 
’ WITH THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE RECENT CEASE-FIRE AGREEMENT 
IN THE IRAQ-IRAN WAR WHICH PUT AN END TO THE BLOODY 8-YEAR 
WAR WHICH RAGED BETWEEN IRAQ AND IRAN LEAVING THOUSANDS OF 
DEAD MUSLIM BROTHERS IN IRAQ AND IRAN. HOW DO YOU SEE, 
BROTHER COLONEL, THE REGION'S FUTURE FROM THE ASPECT OF ITS 
SECURITY AND DEVELOPMENT, IN LIGHT OF THE CURRENT 
INTERNATIONAL EFFORTS TO ESTABLISH PEACE IN THE GULF REGION? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) WHEN THE MATTER BECOMES ONE OF BORDERS, 
WE DO NOT EXPECT IT TO BE SETTLED QUICKLY, BECAUSE BORDERS 
ARE AN INHERITED PROBLEM. I REGRET THAT SINCE THE CEASE- 
FIRE AND THE HALTING OF THE WAR, THE ISSUE OF BORDERS HAS 
BEEN RAISED: WHERE DOES THE BORDER LIE? IS IT IN. THE MIDDLE 
OF THE SHATT AL-ARAB RIVER, OR IS IT ON ITS EASTERN BANK? 

IN FACT I BECAME PESSIMISTIC WHEN I HEARD THAT THIS BORDER 
ISSUE HAD BEEN RAISED. 

BUT I WAS PLEASED WITH THE CEASE-FIRE, BECAUSE I MYSELF 
HAD EXERTED INTENSIVE EFFORTS WITH BOTH SIDES, ESPECIALLY 
THE IRANIAN SIDE, TO END THIS WAR. AND WHEN IRAN ACCEPTED 
THE CEASE-FIRE, THE IRANIAN PRESIDENT SENT ME A LETTER, 
WHICH WAS BROADCAST AND YOU, OF COURSE, HEARD IT. HE SAID 
THAT THIS CAME IN RESPONSE TO MY PERSISTENT AND REPEATED 
CALLS FOR THEM TO HALT THE WAR AND ACCEPT THE SECURITY 
COUNCIL RESOLUTION THAT THEY HAD ACCEPTED THE CEASE-FIRE. I 
THANKED HIM FOR THIS RESPONSE TO OUR REPEATED CALLS. BUT 
NOW I FEAR THERE MIGHT BE ACTS OF BLACKMAIL, IMMORAL ACTIONS 
THAT MIGHT OCCUR DURING THE NEGOTIATIONS. 

INDEED I WAS DEPRESSED AND FELT PESSIMISTIC PARTICULARLY 
WHEN THE BORDER ISSUE WAS RAISED -- THAT IT SHOULD BE DEALT

((QUESTION) ) BROTHER COLONEL, THE HOSTILE WESTERN MEDIA 
PRACTICE HEGEMONY AND DISTORT THE FACTS AND THE ARAB'S 
IMAGE. IN OPPOSITION TO THIS, HOW DO YOU VISUALIZE THE 
DRAWING UP OF A UNIFIED ARAB INFORMATION POLICY, CAPABLE OF 
CONFRONTING THESE PRACTICES AND DEFENDING THE COMPREHENSIVE 
CAUSES OF THE ARAB NATION? THANK YOU. 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) A LONG TIME AGO I DEMANDED ...((CHANGES 
THOUGHT)) WHEN KING KHALID VISITED US, I SAID AT THE DINNER 
BANQUET THAT WE ARABS MUST SPEAK IN ONE LANGUAGE TO THE 
WORLD. WE MAY SPEAK VARIOUS LANGUAGES AMONG OURSELVES AS A 
RESULT OF THE INTERACTIONS, CONTRADICTIONS, AND ALLIANCES 
EXISTING BETWEEN THE ATLANTIC OCEAN AND THE GULF, BECAUSE WE 
ARE ONE PEOPLE AND ONE HOMELAND IN WHICH THERE ARE KINGDOMS, 
REPUBLICS, JAMAHIRIYAHS, SULTANATES, EMIRATES, AND VARIOUS 
ALLIANCES. THIS PUTS US, OF COURSE, IN CONTINUOUS 
INTERACTION UNTIL ALL OF THESE MATTERS ARE SETTLED AND THE 
CONTRADICTIONS AND OPPOSITIONS ARE REMOVED. BUT WITH THE 
WORLD, AS ARABS WE SHOULD SPEAK IN ONE TONGUE WITH THE 
UNITED STATES, THE USSR, EUROPE, AND AFRICA -- THIS INCLUDES 
THE INFORMATION MEDIA. BUT THIS CALL, OF COURSE, REMAINED A 
MERE CALL. I STILL INSIST THAT WE SHOULD SPEAK IN ONE 
TONGUE. BUT OF COURSE THERE IS A DIFFICULTY: THE DIVERSITY 
IN THE POLITICAL WILLS AND THERI CONTRADICTORY NATURE MAKES 
EVERYONE EXPRESS THIS CONTRADICTION IN THE MEDIA. ((PASSAGE 
OMITTED REITERATING THIS THEME) )
