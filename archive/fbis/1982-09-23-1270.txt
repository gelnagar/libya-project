LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI THREATENS TO WITHDRAW FROM UN 
LD221816 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1615 GMT 22 Sep 82 
[Text] Tripoli, 22 Sep (JANA) -- The leader of the revolution has sent the following 
letter to UN Secretary General Javier Perez de Cuellar: 
Mr UN Secretary General: 


I address this letter to you without requesting that the United Nations take any action 
on Lebanon and Palestine or other countries unjustly occupied by forces of aggression. 
I only request that this letter be circulated to the members of the assembly. 

I wish to affirm my lack of confidence in the efforts of the UN Organization in my 
capecity as a citizen, as a revolutionary and as a politically responsible official in 
this contemporary world. I have decided to convey my conviction to my people, and to 
all peoples who are not occupying a permanent seat on the falsely named Security Coun- 
cil. Indeed, I will call on my country to leave this organization and will do my utmost 
to incite all the small nations also to leave this assembly. I will work in earnest to 
form a new organization for the united smaller peoples, even if this might lead to a new 
polarization in the world, which would increase the number of polarizations to three or 
four instead of two, unless the veto right is abolished or extended to UN member nations 
and the UN General Assembly acquires power over the Security Council by relying on 
majority vote and not on the veto. 


[his conviction and that decision have come as an inevitable result of the occupation, 
devastation and annihilation to which my Arab countries have been subjected without any 
effective action by the United Nations to stop or check all that and of the continuing, 
constant and very serious threat to the security and future of these countries. The 
so-called UN Security Council has not been able to check that either. 


My national, pan-Arab and human conscience cannot permit me to keep silent after 20,000 
Arab citizens, civilian Palestinian and Lebanese women, men and children were killed and 
30,000 others wounded before the very eyes and within the hearing of the world within a 
few weeks, and after the recent barbaric massacre at the Sabra and Shatila camps, which 
claimed the lives of thousands of innocent women, old people and children, and which is 


unparalleled in contemporary human history for barbarism, violence and ugliness. 


You yourself are suffering from the clear impotence of the United Nations. You have no 
confidence in its ability as you have repeatedly said in your statements. 


Mr Secretary General, I am sorry to have to express to you and to the world the bitter- 
ness I feel here in my country at the fact that the second historic attempt which was 
made at San Francisco in 1945 to create an international body to prevent war and 
preserve peace has completely failed, as did the first attempt, the League of Nations. 


We the Arabs are in a better position to give this black testimony and declare this bad 
result with sorrow and agony. This is because our countries have been aggressed upon 
without the United Nations being able to implement any of the articles in its charter -- 
a fact which truly convinced us that the United Nations has failed and that its most 
important arm, the UN Security Council, has lost its function.
