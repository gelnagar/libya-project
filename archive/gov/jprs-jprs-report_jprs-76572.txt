OPPOSITION LEADER'S APPEAL=-The "free" return to hie country of former 
Mauritanian president Mokhtar Ould Daddah “would restore the dignity and 
credibility lacking in Nouakchott." This statement was made yesterday in 
Paria by the founder of the National Unity Movement (MUN, in opposition to 
the regime of Colonel Ould Haidallah), Ahmed Moilid. Moilid, who has resided 
in Rabat since the overthrow of former Mauritanian chief of state Ould Daddah 
by a coup on 10 July 1978, said that Colonel Ould Haidalla, currently presi- 
dent of the Supreme National Military Committee (CMSN), “is an invention of 
the POLISARLO Front and is totally in line with the policy of the Libyan 
chief of state, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. Recommending “the complementary 
nature of the Rabat-Nouakchott-Dakar axis" because of the “historic coexis- 
tence of bonds linking the three countries," Moilid also came out in favor 

of a military presence in Mauritania, "providing the people with security,” 
he said. The exiled leader branded the “defeatiem" of the current govern- 
ment and called on the Mauritanian people "to mobilize against the enemies 

of the nation.” "We must save Mauritania at a decisive time in its history,” 
said Moilid, who also denounced the “persecution of opponents by the Nouak- 
chott government.” [Text] [Dakar LE SOLEIL in French 9 Sep 80 p 18] 11,464
