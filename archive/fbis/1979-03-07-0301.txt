LIBYA

COMMENTARY ON REASONS FOR CARTER'S MIDEAST TRIP 


LDO70816 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 6 Mar 79 LD 


{Political commentary: "What Is Behind the Tour of the U.S, President to the Area?"] 


{Excerpts] The visit of U.S, President Carter to the Egyptian capital and Tel Aviv 
Thursday is not merely a U.S, demonstration to celebrate the final chapter of the 
farce of pushing through humiliating, capitulationist solutions in the Arab area, It 
is part of the U.S, plan which aims at redrawing the aggressive and colonialist 
strategy after it suffered cracks and collapsed as a result of the growing popular 
uprisings which predict the emergence of the time of the masses, particularly in the 
Jamahiriyah and Iran, 


The United States had sensed that the current of popular revolution based on progressive 
Islamic concepts and the philosophy of the era of the masses and its values and high 
ideals, which surged forward from the land of the Jamahiriyah, is a roaring current, 

It surges forward forcefully against all the reactionary symbols which hide behind the 
walls of repression and terror and shelter under colonialist care and protection, 
Therefore, and in almost 1 and 1/2 months, the United States has sent two of its leading 
officials to the area, U.S roving ambassador Alfred Atherton and U.S, Secretary of 
Defense Harold Brown, If the first one was entrusted with the task of bringing the 
reactionary regimes and the Zionist enemy within a close alliance framework under the 
auspices of international imperialism, Brown was sent to study the military needs of 
these regimes on the assumption that the United States and its weapons are capable 

of confronting the masses and their surging revolution, But Brown had forgotten the 
lesson which the United States had violently learned in the Jamahiriyah, Iran, 

Ethiopia, Southeast Asia and Angola, 


All U.S. reports sent to the White House have been unanimous in that the events of 

the raging popular revolution in the area call for intense pressure by U.S, diplomacy 
to make the Zionists and the defeated Egyptian president speed up the conclusion of 
the agreement of surrender and humiliation so that the U.S, agents may free themselves 
for the task of safeguarding and protecting U.S, interests and confronting the popular 
revolution which threatens the reactionary regimes with destruction and downfall, And 
from this platform stemmed Carter's call for the holding of the Camp David meeting 
between the Egyptian prime minister and the racist, terrorist Dayan,

The U.S. and Zionist sides have reached a new agreement, and this time they have imposed 
the solution on the defeated Egyptian president even in his absence from the meeting. 

He is probably convinced that the contradiction between himself and the Zionists is 
marginal if compared with the danger of popular revolution which threatens the monopolist 
and colonialist interests and the reactionary regimes in whose footsteps he follows. 

This will also free him and free the Zionists for playing their suspect roles in the 

Arab homeland and the African Continent. 


The colonialist farce considered it necessary that the episodes of negotiations which 
were numerous and varied in dimensions for almost 1 and 1/2 years should bring the farce 
to an end by crowning it with Carter's journey Thursday to the Egyptian capital and Tel 
Aviv amid the hails and acclaims of the Egyptian propaganda media. This medium depicts 
him as the man who holds the interests of the Egyptian people dear to him. 


But can the Egyptian Arab people remove from their minds 25 years or more of open U.S. 
hostility to Egypt and the Arab nation? Can they forget that the United States with its 
human, material and military support for the Zionists was the one which spilled the blood 
of well over 80,000 Egyptian martyrs, let alone the martyrs who fell in the Golan, 
Lebanon and the martyrs of the Palestinian revolution? Can they forget the martyrs of 
the Bahr al-Baqgar school, the Abi Za'tbal factories, Hulwan and the battles of attrition 
along the banks of the canal? U.S. President Carter comes to Egypt with hands stained 
with the blood of the Egyptian people, He comes to Egypt at the same time that reports 
confirmed that the Zionist enemy is preparing to launch a new aggressive war against the 
patriotic and progressive forces in Lebanon and against the Palestinian resistance, 


What is this so-called peace which Carter wants to bring? Since when has the United 
States been keen on cherishing peace when it was a basic party to the crime? It took 
part alongside the enemy in all the battles witnessed in the arena of Arab-Zionist 
struggle. If the United States imagines that it can decorate its picture with its fool- 
ish policy of imposing a capitulationist solution at the expense of the rights of the 
Palestinian Arab people and the honor of the Egyptian people, and at the expense of com- 
plete Arab sovereignty over all Arab territory, it is totally wrong. Indeed, it is 
digging its grave with its own hands. This conspiratorial solution will be the basic 
incentive for the outbreak of the comprehensive popular revolution in all parts of the 
Arab homeland which will uproot the colonialist presence and its interests and agents 

in the area. 


The colonialist forces realize that the only party which negotiates with the Zionists is 
fragile and supple and prone to collapse. It is isolated from the Egyptian masses and 
the entire Arab nation. This has always been the case with the United States. It has 
always backed a losing horse. It has always allied itself with the forces of reaction 
and repression which in the long run will lose in the face of popular revolutions. 


As for the defeated Egyptian president, as the leader of the revolution brother Col 
Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi has stated to the AFRIQUE-ASIE weekly, if he signs the agreement 
of humiliation and dishonor he would thus be going over the red line. He would be 
cutting all links with the whole Arab nation including the most conservative regimes. 
There might be here and there some people who may show sympathy for him and others who 
may give him support, but all of them are afraid to follow him because they know for 
certain that they will clash with mighty forces which will fight them mercilessly. 
This is because the matter here involves the sacred future of the Arab nation,

AT-TURAYKI ADDRESSES CONFERENCE OF ARAB FOREIGN MINISTERS 
LD061746 Tripoli JANA in English 1647 GMT 6 Mar 79 LD 
{"Speech by Foreign Secretary"--JANA headline] 


[Text] Kuwait, March 6 JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The foreign secretary said that the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah had made serious attempts in order to restore 
peace and achieve unity between the two parts of Yemen, 


Addressing the conference of the Arab foreign ministers which concluded its works in 
Kuwait last night, the foreign secretary said that the events in both Yemens and the 
whole arena are but another chain in the link of imperialist-Zionist schemes to crush 
any revolution in the Arab area and that war between brothers is detrimental to the 

Arab nation as a whole, Dr at-Turayki stressed that imperialism and its allies should 
not be given the opportunity to trigger off a war among Arab sister states, pointing out 
that imperialism intends to defeat the Arab people through every possible means and force 
them to carry out its capitulatory policies. This he said is a planned and fixed policy 
aimed at creating more trouble in order to distract the Arab people from the real danger 
represented in the (Israeli) enemy and the U.S. which are trying to control the Arab 
world, 


He said that Yemen should be left alone without any foreign intervention in its internal 
affairs, and "all of us should not spare any efforts to unify the two Yemens as a step 
towards an Arab unity which the Jamahiriyah is endeavouring to achieve", The foreign 
secretary affirmed that the struggle against the Zionist enemy is part of the Arab 
people's struggle against imperialism, the ally of the Zionist enemy, which is trying 

to find other puppets in the Arab region to carry out its policy aimed at humiliating the 
Arab people and using their region to serve its greeds and interests. He pointed out 
that Yemen has a dense population and a strategic location which has always been a target 
for imperialist forces which kept weakening Yemen, beginning with inciting tribal chiefs 
and ending up with exerting pressure on the Yemeni people. 


Dr at-Turayki praised the Iranian revolution which brought about one of the worst defeats 
of imperialism. 


NIGERIAN PRESIDENT'S ENVOY ARRIVES 6 MAR 


LD061022 Tripoli JANA in English 0846 GMT 6 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 March, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Brigadier Omar Ali Shinkafi, the 
personal envoy of the Nigerian president to Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of 
the Great First of September Revolution, has arrived in Tripoli, The Nigerian envoy 
was received at Tripoli International Airport by a representative from the Foreign 
Secretariat and the Nigerian ambassador to Tripoli.
