Agreements Signed 
LD082233Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 8 Jul 78 LD 


[Text] Brother Musa Abu Furaywah, secretary of planning, and Soltan Ali Keshtmand, 

Afghan planning minister, signed two agrcements. The first agreement deals with cooperation 
in the spheres of economy, trade and technology, and the second with cooperation in the 
cultural, scientific and information spheres. 


The two sides agreed on establishing a joint company for cooperation in the agricultural 
and industrial fields, as well as establishing Islamic cultural centers in Afghanistan. 
They also agreed on the exchange of news between the two countries and the establishing 
of offices for the Libyan and Afghan news agencies in both countries. The two sides 
also agreed on the necessity of encouraging trade and trade exchange and the opening of 
trade offices to introduce the preducts of both countries.

[Text] Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud left for Algeria at noon today on a brief 
visit during which he will meet with Algerian President Houari Boumediene. 


JANA has learned that during his meeting with the Algerian president, Jallud will talk 
about issues affecting the Arab nation and the current Arab situation. The meeting comes 
within the framework of consultations on Arab issues among the countries of the stead- 
fastness and confrontation frunt. 


AFRICAN MINISTERS MEET AT LIBYAN EMBASSY IN SUDAN 


LD100146Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 9 Jul 78 LD 


[Text] A meeting was held on the evening of 9 July at the Jamahiriyah Embassy in Khartoum, 
Attending were the foreign ministers of the Jamahiriyah, Algeria, Mozambique, Angola, 
Benin, Madagascar, Sao Tome and Principe, Guinea-Bissau and People!s Congo. 


JANA's Khartoum correspondent says the meeting discussed the situation and recent develop- 
ment in Africa. It also discussed coordination among the ministers at the African foreign 
ministers! conference currently being held in Khartoum to adopt resolutions and recommenda- 
tions that will insure the unity of the continent and the consolidation of its struggie 
against colonialism, imperialism and Zionism. 


Meanwhile, the Jamahiriyah foreign secretary held a meeting with the foreign ministers of 
Cameroon, Burundi, Mauritania and Sudan in order to coordinate pocitions concerning the 
issues on the agenda of the 3lst session of the African foreign ministers! conference. 

He discussed with them the ways and means necessary for the consolidation of bilateral 
relations in the interest of Africa's unity and for its standing up to all plots hatched 
by colonialism and Zionism.
