LIBYA

LIBYA INFORMS CUSTOMERS OF OIL PRICE REDUCTIONS 


NC281236 Paris AFP in English 1148 GMT 28 Dec 81 


[Text] Nicosia, 28 Dec (AFP) -- Libya has informed customers of oil price reductions coming 
into force on (?January) [word indistinct] the MIDDLE EAST ECONOMIC SURVEY (MEES) said today. 


Top quality Brega and Zueitina oil will come down 50 cents to $37 a barrel, the floor 
price set by the oil exporting countries at their recent Abu Dhabi conference. Sider and 
Sirtica are to cost $36.5 a barrel or 18 cents below the Abu Dhabi floor rate. Lower 
quality Sarir will be reduced to $35.75 a barrel or 55 cents under the floor price.
