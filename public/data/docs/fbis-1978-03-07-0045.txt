SED'S LAMBERZ HOLDS TALKS WITH JALLUD IN TRIPOLI 
LDO061652Y East Berlin ADN International Service in German 1457 GMT 6 Mar 78 LD 


(Text] Tripoli--The member cf the General Secretariat of the General People's Committee 
of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, today 
received Werner Lamberz, the special envoy of the general secretary of the SED Central 
Committee, Erich Honecker. 


In detailed and cordial talks, political, economic, cultural and scientific-techrical 
cooperation between the GDR and Libya were discussed. A further subject of discussion 
was the joint struggle of the two countries against imperialism, colonialism and neo- 
colonialism, eSpecially in the Arab world and in Africa. Werner Lamberz and 'Abd as- 
Salam Ahmad Jallud stressed the usefulness of trusting political consultations between 
leading personages of the two states and discussed measures that further the common aims 
of peace, independence and social progress. 


Taha ash-Sharif fbn Amir, liaison secretary of the General People's Committee; Paul 
Markowski, member of the SED central committee; and Ambassador Freimut Seidel also 
took part in the meeting. 


Death of Lamberz Announced 
LDO71232Y¥ Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 7 Mar 78 LD 


{Text} Tripoli, 7 March, JANA--A painful accident occured last night when a helicopter 
carrying Werner Lamberz, member of the Politburo and secretary of the Central Committee 
of the Socialist Unity Party [SED] of the GDR and an accompanying delegation crashed 
near Tripoli. The helicopter was also carrying Taha ash-Sharif ibn 'Amir, liaison 
secretary, and brother Ahmad Bushagur, director of protocol at the Foreign Secretariat. 


All the eleven occupants of the helicopter died in the crash. On the East German side, 
they were: Werner Lamberz, member of the Politburo and secretary of the SED Central 
Committee; Paul Markowski, member of the SED Central Committee; Armin Ernst, interpreter; 
and Mr Achim Spremberg, a photographer.
