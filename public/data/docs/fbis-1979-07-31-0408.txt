CUBAN VICE PRESIDENT GARCIA FRIAS ARRIVES 27 JUL 
LD272133 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 27 Jul 79 LD 
[Text] Guillermo Garcia Frias, member of the Political Bureau and vice president of the 
Councils of State and Ministers in Cuba, arrived tonight in Tripoli for a visit to the 


Jamahiriyah lasting several days, during which he will have talks on bilateral relations 
and the sixth summit conference of the nonalined to be held late in September in Havana, 


Guillermo praised relations between the two countries and said that his visit comes within 
the context of consultations between the two countries on the nonalined summit and be- 
coming acquainted with the direct democracy experience in Libya and benefitting from 

this experience rich with humanitarian dimensions. He said in a statement to JANA: We 

in Cuba follow with admiration and appreciation all the great successes scored by our 
friends the Libyan Arab people led by revolutionary Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi.

TURKISH DEPUTY PRIME MINISTER CETIN ARRIVES 27 JUL 
LD272110 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 27 Jul 79 LD 


[Text] Hikmet Cetin, Turkish deputy prime minister and minister of state for economic 
affairs, arrived in Tripoli tonight for a visit to the Jamahiriya lasting several days. 
During this time he will attend meetings of the Libyan-Turkish joint economic commission. 
The Turkish official in a statement to JANA confirmed the depth of friendship and links 
between the Libyan and the Turkish peoples, pointing to the development of cooperation 
between the two countries in the various fields. 


Committee Meetings Begin 
LD281941 Tripoli JANA in English 1755 GMT 28 Jul 79 LD 


(Text] Tripoli, July 28, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Meetings of the fifth session of the 
Libyan-Turkish joint economic committee started this morning in the Foreign Secretariat's 
assembly hall. 


Engineer Muhammad al-Mangqush, secretary of the General People's Committee for Housing, 
delivered a speech welcoming the Turkish deputy prime minister and the minister of state for 
economic affairs and the accompanying delegation, and praising the progress of Libyan- 
Turkish relations. He said that these relations have notably increased in scope, covering 
the technical and scientific cooperation as well as the social security field and labour 
force. 


Hikmet Cetin, the Turkish deputy prime minister and minister of state :i'or economic 

affairs, delivered a speech praising the achievements realized by the Jamahiriyah in various 
fields. He said that the ties of friendship and cooperation between the Libyan and Turkish 
people originate from common historic and religious ties. 

He pointed out that bilateral cooperation is getting stronger day after day in all fields. 
The meeting decided to form several subcommittees which are expected to meet tomorrow.
