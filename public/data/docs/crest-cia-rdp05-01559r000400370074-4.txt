Summary (AI): A garbled 1976 CIA-reading-room news item (ANNA wire, Malta) reports that Colonel Muammar al-Qadhdhafi, chairman of the Libyan Command Council, pledged Libyan support to Malta in its fight to end foreign bases there. The text is heavily OCR-damaged and the details are only partly legible.
• Report dated Malta, 23 May 1976, datelined by ANNA news agency.
• The speech was delivered on 22 May 1976 to Maltese citizens.
• Qadhdhafi reportedly said Libya would turn Malta into an independent country; the rest of the text is illegible.
• 1976-05-22: Qadhdhafi delivered a speech to crowds of Maltese citizens pledging Libyan support for Malta's campaign to end foreign bases on the island.
