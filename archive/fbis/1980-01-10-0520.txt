LIP YAN CHARGE IN BEIRUT RECEIVES NAYIF HAWATIMAH 
LDO71834 Tripoli JANA in English 1643 3M? 7 Jan 80 LD 


{Text] Beirut, Jan 7, JAMAHRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Mr (Muhammad 'Abd al-Qassim) charge d'affaires 
of the Jamahriyah's embassy in Beirut yesterday received Nayif Hawatimah, secretary general 
of the Democratic Front fo tne Liberation of Palestine. Our correspondent in Beirut 

said that the meeting contimed for an hour during which they reviewed the results of the 
meeting between the leadership of the Great 1 September Revolution and the resistance leaders 
in Tripoli and developments of the Arab and Palestinian situations in the light of the 
(Israeli) imperialist offensive against the Palestinian people and their struggle for 

the restoration of their land and usurped right. The charge d'affaires explained the 
Jamahiriyah's stance in supporting the Palestinian revolution and that the Great 1 September 
Revolution was and will always be a real support to the Palestinian people through their 
struggle for liberating their homeland.

[Text] Tripoli, Jarmary 8, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The foreign secretary held contacts 
with the foreign ministers of each of Saudi Arabia, Syria, Kuwait, Yemen Arab Republic, 
United Arab Emirates and Tunisia on the first Arab-African ministerial conference which 
will be held on the Jamahiriyah's soil from 14th-16th January 1980. 


The JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY learnt that the Arab foreign ministers have affirmed the 
importance of holding the conference in time and the importance of Arab-African coopera=-
