• Intelligence reports said Qaddafi supported some 30 insurgent, radical or terrorist groups worldwide.

• Libyan exiles opposing Qaddafi reportedly received support from Egypt, Iraq, Morocco, Saudi Arabia, Sudan and Tunisia.
