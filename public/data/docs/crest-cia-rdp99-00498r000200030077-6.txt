Summary (AI): A Washington Post article (13 February 1982) reports that Douglas Schlachter, an aide to former CIA agents Edwin Wilson and Frank Terpil, pleaded guilty to conspiring to supply explosives and train commandos for Libyan leader Muammar Qaddafi. Wilson was said to be living in Tripoli, and Terpil was reported in Beirut; both are fugitives.
• Wilson and Terpil were indicted in April 1980 and again in August 1981 with Schlachter over a 1976 contract to supply explosives, high technology weapons and training to the Libyan government.
• Schlachter faces a maximum of seven years in prison or $110,000 in fines, or both; no sentencing date had been set.
• The CIA said in January 1982 it could find no documentary evidence of an official relationship between Schlachter and current or former agency officials.
• 1982-02-12: Douglas Schlachter pleaded guilty to conspiracy to supply explosives and train commandos for Qaddafi, and to violating federal explosives shipment laws.
