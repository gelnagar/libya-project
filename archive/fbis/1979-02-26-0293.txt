PRIME MINISTER OF MAURITIUS ENDS VISIT 


1D222016 Tripoli JANA in English 1750 GMT 22 Feb 79 LD 


(Text ] Tripoli, February 22, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Mauritian delegation led by 
Prime Minister Dr. Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam left Tripoli this morning ending a four- 
day visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah,. 


Dr. Ramgoolam was given an official send-off at Tripoli International Airport by the 
head of the General People's Committee, Mr. 'Abd al'Ati al-'Ubyadi, the secretaries and 
undersecretaries and the directors of various departments at the Foreign Secretariat.

Joint Statement 
LD222216 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 22 Feb 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--A joint statement was issued this evening at the end of the visit of 
Seewoosagur Ramgoolam, the prime minister of Mauritius, to the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah. Here is the text: 


At the invitation of brother 'Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, chairman of the General People's 
Committee of the Jamahiriyah, Dr Seewoosagur Ramgoolam, the chairman of the Council of 
Ministers of Mauritius and the head of the senior ministerial delegation, visited 

the Jamahiriyah from 19 to 22 February. 


During this visit brother 'Abd al'-Ati al-'Ubaydi held talks with Dr Seewoosagur Ramgoolam 
in the presence of the delegations of both countries which dealt with bilateral relations 
and African, Arab and international issues, 


In the sphere of bilateral relations the two sides once again stressed the fraternal and 
historic ties between the peoples of both countries and reviewed the development of these 
relations in all spheres and stressed their commitment to work to realize further the 
growth of cooperation between the two countries. 


In the African sphere, the two sides have noted that the African Continent is being 
exposed to new colonial and racists assaults and have stressed their determination to 
contime to fight colonialism and racism until the whole African continent is liberated, 
In this connection the two sides expressed their indictment of the policy of the colonial 
forces striving to dominate the African Continent. They demanded the imperative with- 
drawal of foreign forces from the African Continent and the liquidation of colonial bases. 


The two sides also stressed the need to liberate the African peoples who are still 
suffering from hegomony, foreign monopolies and racist domination and their belief that 
armed struggle against colonialism and racism is the sole road for the freedom of the 
people, In this connection they contime to support and back all African liberation 
movements, whether those fighting racist colonialism in the south of the continent or 
those who are still struggling against the domination of foreign forces. In this con- 
nection the two sides have stressed their belief that a just and fair solution for the 
Western Sahara question should be found which takes into consideration the wishes of 
its citizens. 


Regarding the Ugandan-Tanzanian conflict, the two sides expressed their anxiety about 

the fighting taking place among the brothers which only serves the enemies of the continent 
and on this occasion they have addressed a warm appeal to both sides to immediately halt 
the fighting and to peacefully solve the dispute between them within the framework of 

the African family in accordance with the principles and the charter of the OAU- 


In the Arab sphere, the two sides praised the efforts of the Arab-African group for 
cultural and economic progress and the defeat of colonialism, They stressed that Arab- 
African unity is bound to contribute to the realization of economic and political freedom 
of all the Arab and African peoples and to be beneficial to the peoples of the Third 
World because it is based on foundations linking the two groups historically, geograph- 
ically and culturally. 


Regarding the Palestinian question,the two sides condemned any attempt to ignore the 
right of the Palestinian people to solve their question and consider that any solution 
Which the Palestinian people are not full party to is unsatisfactory.

They also stressed their determination to contime to.support the struggle of the 
Palestinian people to regain their usurped rights including their right to return to 
their homeland and to live in a free and independent state. 


In the international sphere the two sides stressed their commitment to the aims and 
charter of the United Nations and to the resolutions and the principles of nonalinement, 
and they called for the amendment to the UN Charter to realize justice and equality 
among all states. They also called for the establishment of a new world economic system 
based on justice and equality. 


At the end of the visit, His Excellency the prime minister of Mauritius, Seewoosagur 
Ramgoolam, expressed his profound admiration for the great achievements realized by the 
Great 1 September Revolution under the leadership of brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
that seek to provide prosperity and the establishment of social justice in the Jamahiriyah. 
He also expressed his deep appreciation and gratitude for the warm fraternal welcome which 
he and the accompanying delegation enjoyed during their presence in the Jamahiriyah. 

The prime minister of Mauritius asked brother ‘Abd al-'Ati al-' Ubaydi to convey to 
brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi a renewal of the invitation to visit Mauritius. He 

also renewed the invitation to brother chairman of the General People's Committee to 

visit Mauritius. This invitation has been accepted and the date is to be fixed later on. 


Issued in Tripoli, 22 February 1979.

Ends Visit

(Text ] Tripoli, February 26, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Nigerian President Olusegun Obasanjo's 
personal envoy, Mr. Umario Ali Shinkafi, left Tripoli yesterday evening after a short 
one day visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


Present at the Nigerian envoy's send-off at Tripoli International Airport were the acting 
director of African affairs at the Foreign Secretariat, the Nigerian ambassador to the 
Jamahiriyah and a delegate from the protocol directorate at the Foreign Secretariat.
