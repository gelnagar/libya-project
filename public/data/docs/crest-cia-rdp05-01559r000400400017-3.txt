[Excerpts] In the name of God, the merciful, the compassionate. 
Every soul shall taste of death; you shall surely be paid in full 
your wages on the day of resurrection. [Koranic verse] God has 
spoken the truth. 

The Secretariat of the General People’s Congress announces to 
the Libyan Arab people with sorrow the death of one of the 
devoted strugglers who gave generously for the sake of their 
homeland —- Muhammad ‘Abd al-Salam Abu Minyar al- 
Qadhdhafi — who died this morning, Wednesday 18 Shaban 
1394 after the death of the Prophet corresponding to 8 May 1985. 
He was 100 years old. 

The deceased was known for his contribution to the Libyan jihad 
movement against the Italian invasion, as he joined the muja- 
hidin right from the very beginning of the invasion and partici- 
pated in many battles including the battle of Al-Girbabiyah. 
Throughout his life, he carried the scars of the wounds he 
sustained in these battles. 

While we express to the leader of the revolution Brother Colonel

NATO Naval Maneuvers in Mediterranean Expose Aims 
LD080925 Tripoli JANA in English 0823 GMT 8 May 85 

{Text] Tripoli, 18 Shaban, 8 May, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — NATO Forces began military manoeuvres in the 
Mediterranean on May 3 which are to end on May 7. Ships 
belonging to various NATO member states will take part in these 
military exercises and Egypt has put its ports at the disposal of 
these Western ships in the region. Other NATO forces including 
units from the U.S.A., Britain, Italy, Holland and France began 
yesterday and are due to last for ten days where ships carrying 
nuclear missiles will take part. 

The Mediterranean is considered to be a very important region 
for many parties as 800 different ships pass through it daily which 
make up for third of the world oil supplies and one sixth of world 
trade. Foreign powers and later NATO powers in recent years 
considered the Mediterranean Sea as an important area for their 
military activities. The United States of Amcrica keeps 400,000 
tonnes of shipping in the Mediterranean a tenth of America’s 
naval power. 

The American Fleet in the Mediterranean is led by the nuclear 
aircraft carrier Nimitz. These American ships threatened the 
people of the region whether they be in Lebanon or Libya and 
the continued American nuclear presence threatens the whole of 
the Mediterranean. 

NATO forces have an undisclosed number of nuclear submarines 
in the Mediterranean where they threaten the security of this 
vital region which many have called for turning into a sea of 
peace and a region of cooperation and confrontation [as received] 
between its people. Although the NATO manoeuvres in cooper- 
ation with certain Mediterranean countries shows the aims of 
NATO states to impose their domination of the Mediterranean 
and turning into an observation point theatening the security of 
the people of the region. {sentence as received]
