Summary (AI): A Damascus domestic radio broadcast on 27 May 1976 reported that Libyan leader Muammar al-Qadhdhafi denounced the Egyptian-Israeli Sinai agreement as a betrayal of Egypt and the Arab nation. The OCR-garbled text says his remarks were addressed to Libyan students and the Egyptian students union in Cairo. This is foreign policy with no Tripoli-specific content.
• Qadhdhafi reportedly called the Sinai agreement a disgrace to Egypt and the Arab nation.
• The speech was addressed to Libyan students and the Egyptian students union in Cairo and was published by the Libyan National Bureau in Cairo.
• Qadhdhafi reportedly said Egypt must concentrate its efforts on liberating its territory and rejected capitulation.
• 1976-05-27: Damascus domestic radio broadcast reports Qadhdhafi's criticism of the Sinai agreement, from a speech to Libyan students in Cairo
