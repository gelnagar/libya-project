LIBYA

LIBYA CHALLENGES HAIG TO SUPPORT 'ALLEGATIONS' 


LD130210 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Aratic 0015 GMT 13 Nov 81 


[Text] U.S. Secretary of State Alexander Haig announced in a most impertinent manner 
that the Jamahiriyah might be behind yesterday's alleged assassination attempt on the 
U.S. charge d'affaires in Paris. The U.S. secretary of state made this allegation 
before the U.S. Senate Foreign Relations Committee yesterday. 


A responsible source at the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison described Haig's accusation of the Jamahiriyah being behind the assassination 
attempt as another link in the feverish campaign of terrorism which is led by the 
United States against the Great al-Fatih Revolution and its historical leadership. 


The source added that this accusation affirms the United States" unbridled imagination 
in fabricating accusations and imagining alleged actions as being carried out by the 
Jamahiriyah through the world. Such imagination can be expected only from children 
who are miles away from responsibility. 


The source said that this allegation confirms the continuous and intentional attempt of 

the U.S. administration to misinform the U.S. public and the U.S. Senate by giving false 
information about matters outside Libya which has repeatedly stated its keeness on building 
bood relations with the American people. The source said that Jamahiriyah people who 

have decided to confront U.S. aggression and terrorism in defense of its land and 
territorial waters could not resort to assassination as a political means, which the U.S. 
administration has long experience with through the CIA. 


The People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison challenges General Haig to produce one single 
piece of evidence to support his unfounded allegations. 


JANA EDITOR COMMENTS ON CHAD DEVELOPMENTS 


LD130932 Tripoli JANA in English 0807 GMT 13 Nov 81 
[Quotation marks as received] 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 Nov (JANA) -- President Goukouni Queddei, president of the Chadian 
national government, in a press conference here last night [as received], accused 
Sudan of interfering in Chad's internal affairs by backing Hissein Habre's forces in 
the battles which have been raging in eastern Chad for the last 48 hours. 


The French news agency said that Chadian president affirmed that some Sudanese elements 
infiltrated Habre's northern forces and took part in those battles. 


President Goukouni revealed the intervention of the Sudanese forces in the battles 
alongside Habre's forces against the government forces, while the Libyan Arab forces 
withdrew from all the above mentioned areas in eastern Chad after they have accomplished 
their historic and humanitarian role in realizing and consolidating freedom and peace 

in Chad. This role was undertaken in response to the legitimate Chadian national 
government, and as soon as the Libyan forces completed their withdrawal from those areas, 
the security situation collapsed and the flames of war erupted between the government 
forces on the one side, and the Sudanese and Habre's forces on the other side.

JANA's political editor commented on this swift development of events: 


"It has been confirmed for the world, after it was confirmed for the Chadian President 
Goukouni himself, that the Sudanese forces intervened in Chad after the withdrawal of 
Libyan Arab forces and the full and brave accomplishment of their historic role. 


This proves the role of the Great Al-Fatih Revolution which strengthened the freedom of 
the Chadian people and restored peace and security to Chad, and Africa too. 


Thys, all imperialism's pretexts have been exposed. [word indistinct] is opposed to 
Africa's freedom and is anti-Semitic, anti-Arab and anti-Islam. This hostility is shown 
through its deviousness, spreading of lies and the overturning of facts throughout the 
period of the presence of the Libyan Arab (?Armed) Forces in Chad to defend the freedom 
of the Chadian people who was crushed by the (?civil war and) were torn apart by the 
{word indistinct] imperialist plots, and whose country was looted by the enemies. 


Our armed forces have achieved the goal of the Chadian people and responded to their 
aspirations and their needs and saved them from the long civil war. This was in response 
to the call of the Chadian Government and Frolinat [Chadian National Liberation] Front. 


Today the Libyan Arab Armed Forces stand truly neutral and impartial towards all Chadian 
factions and will not side with one against another. 


The world now bears witness to the importance and the effectiveness of the Libyan Arab 
forces and its practical role as the safety valve which secured the freedom of the Chadian 
people. 


Now they have withdrawan from the eastern sector, the situation has suddenly deteriorated 
and the world began to appreciate the reality of the historic Libyan stand." 


The political editor added: "The joint African peace keeping force should shoulder its 
full responsibility in confronting this invasion of Chad in which the Sudanese forces are 
taking part. The Jamahiriyah is pleased and proud to have fulfilled its role, and (?more 
so) in responding to President Goukouni's request to withdraw after it completely fulfilled 
its duty in a way highly appreciated by the whole world."
