OPPOSITION PAPER SHUT DOWN--Both the Arabic and French editions of the newspaper 

AL BAYANE, organ of Ali Yata's (Moroccan) Party of Progress and Socialism, have 
been indefinitely suspended since Wednesday. The newspapers’ editorial board 

that says it does not know the reasons for thie measure. The official authorities 
responsible for the ban have not given any explanation. On Saturday Ali Yata 

said in hie editorial: “Yes, we can be proud of our democratic achievements." 

The last issue, removed from Kiosks on Wednesday, criticized the judiciary over 

the eviction of 1,000 peasants and expressed moderate criticiem of the government's 
agricultural policy. In Fez three students have been sentenced to 2 months’ impri- 
sonment, and that increases the number of young people convicted for “public dis- 
turbances" to 51. The Rabat Faculty of Sciences remains closed on the government's 
orders. [Text] [10241227 Paris L'HUMANITE in French 20 Feb 81 p 9)
