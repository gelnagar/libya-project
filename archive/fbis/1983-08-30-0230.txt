LIBYA

IRNA Interviews Foreign Secretary al-’Ubaydi 


LD262052 Tehran IRNA in English 1937 GMT 26 Aug 83 


[Text] Damascus, Aug 26, IRNA — Libyan Foreign Minister 
*Abd al-’Ati al-’"Ubaydi speaking to IRNA Friday said “We 
believe that Islamic Revolution of Iran will overcome all conspir- 
acies hatched by the United States and its allies in the region.” 
He also emphasised the strengthening of Libya-Iran solidarity. 


The interview was held on the occasion of Iran’s Foreign Minister 
*Ali Akbar Velayati’s visit to Libya. 


Al-’Ubaydi commenting on the results of Velayati’s visit said 
that meetings were held in an atmosphere of mutual under- 
standing and cooperation with the aiming of achieving both 
countries’ common objectives in political field. He said talks 
centred on Iran-Libyan resistance and ways and means of 
thwarting plots of their common enemies particularly those of 
U.S. imperialism. 


Commenting on Iraqi imposed war on Iran, Al-’Ubaydi said that 
if Iraqi was sincere it would have never started war with Iran. 
Saddam, he said, was serving interests of the U.S. against Islamic 
Revolution. Al-’Ubaydi said that Saddam did not fight against 
Iran during defunct Shah's regime. 


He also said that the Islamic Republic since the culimination of 
Islamic Revolution has served Islam well, noting that it immedi- 
ately severed relations with the apartheid Governent of South 
Africa and the Zionist regime, and put all means at its disposal 
at the service of Islam. 


Commenting on the issue of Palestine and division within Al- 
Fatah ranks, Al-’Ubaydi said Libya repeatedly has announced 
that as long as Arab lands are under Zionist occupation there 
would be no peace in the region. He said that what was happening 
now was more to the benefit of Zionism and imperialism who 
intended to dominate the region. 


He said Iran and Libya as supporters of Syria were an obstacle 
against imperialism’s pressures. He said all compromising ways 
concerning Palestine should be eliminated, and aid should be 
granted to Palestinian and Lebanese combatants fighting in 
Al-Biqa’ valley to prepare ground for an overall war against 
Zionists. 


Referring to the iseue of Chad, Al-’Ubaydi said that Chad was 
a neighbor of Libya and its security influences and is important 
to Libya’s security. The interference of countries including the 
US., France, Zaire, Egypt and Sudan fanned the flames of wider 
conflict for an essentially domestic issue between opponent 
groups there. 


Libyan foreign minister said that Libya supported National 
Unity Government of Chad. 


JANA Editor Rejects Mitterrand Statement on Chad 
LD271941 Tripoli JANA in English 1431 GMT 27 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulgada 19, August 27, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — French President Francois Mitterrand 
discussed last Thursday with “Le Monde” French newspaper 
French military intervention in Chad and gave justifications 
which do not correspond to the reality as well as contradictory 
explanations justifying their intervention. According to what the 
French president told “‘Le Monde” to justify the intervention of 
French forces in Chadian territory, he claimed that France 
intervened in Chad because it feared Libya's dissemination of 
Islam and revival of the Islamic spirit in Africa. 


JANA’s political editor commented on the French president's 
statements by saying: 


“France's words have been blatantly contradicted by its deeds. 
France has claimed that its intervention in Chad was aimed at 
putting an end to American intervention there. However military 
intervention did not stop American intervention as is claimed by 
France. Thus, on the grounds of stopping American intervention 
in Chad France has resorted to blackmailing and has at the same 
time implemented a dishonest policy. 


The Statement by the French president to “Le Monde” newspa- 
per has taken a new dimension as Mitterrand explained political 
stands on the ground of religious crusade stands... From this point 
of view Mitterrand showed the crusaders’ fanatical spirit and 
attacked Islam in Africa as a result of Libyan arab move to 
support the causes of Islam and Muslims. Mitterrand has turned 
the war into a crusade and has shown its hostility towards Islam 
in Africa.
