LIBYA

TRIPOLI COMMENTS ON MOVE TOWARD ISLAMIC STATE IN IRAN 
LD221932 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 22 Jan 79 LD 
(unattributed commentary] 


[Excerpts Iran is currently preparing itself for the birth of its nascent Islamic 
State which will rise over the ruins of the filthiest reactionary fascist regime in 
history. By their giant revolution, the heroic Iranian people inflicted defeat on 

that regime and toppled it on its foundation, 


Huge masses of people are now marching on Tehran where they are gathering in a popular 
rally which epitomizes the greatness and solidarity of the popular will which has by 
its revolution triumphed over the corrupt Pahlavi regime, 


The masses of the Iranian people have sacrificed thousands of martyrs at the altar of 
freedom throughout the lean years of the Pahlavi era. The results of their revolu- 
tionary uprising are as big as their Struggle which astounded the world and rendered 
the biggest imperialist power incapable of even rescuing its expelled agent, 


By their revolutionary uprising lasting over a year, the Iranian masses have provided 
the strongest practical proof that there is no way for humanity to solve its problems 
and dilemmas other than through the golden age, the age of the masses, 
Along the road to the age of the masses, the revolution of the Iranian people today 
marches with strong steadfast steps armed with the ambitions and aspirations of its 
masses toward the establishment of its Islamic democratic State, which is the strategic 
objective of the Iranian people's revolution,
