Summary (AI): A 1982 NPIC briefing attachment reports two Libyan Air Force crashes: a probable Mirage III/V lost on takeoff at Sebha airfield in central Libya, and a possible Fitter that crashed and burned while landing at Ghurdabiyah airfield, near Surt. Neither site is in Tripoli.
• The Libyan Air Force recently lost a probable Mirage III/V and a possible Fitter in two separate crashes.
• Ghurdabiyah airfield is about 9 NM south of Surt on the Mediterranean coast.
• Coordinates given: Sebha 26-59-36N 014-28-01E; Ghurdabiyah 31-03-34N 016-35-49E.
• 1982-05: Probable Mirage III/V crash on takeoff at Sebha airfield
• 1982-05: Possible Fitter crash and fire while landing at Ghurdabiyah airfield
