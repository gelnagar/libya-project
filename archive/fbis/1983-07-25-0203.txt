LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Remarks Departing for Nouakchott 
LD221445 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
22 Jul 83 


[Text] As he was leaving Tripoli International Airport on his way 
to Nouakchott, the brother commander of the revolution made a 
statement to JANA in which he said: | am determined to unify 
the Arab nation and to rally its scattered parts and pool its 
resources to destroy the Zionist death train fueled by American 
energy. He who fails to respond to this call, he said, will be writing 
his own death sentence
