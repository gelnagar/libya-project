LIBYA

ITALIAN FOREIGN MINISTER MALFATTI DISCUSSES FISHING, OIL 
AU25204'55 Rome ANSA in Inelish 1945 GMT 25 Oct 79 AU 


[Text] Tripoli, October 25 (ANSA)--Italian Foreign Minister Franco Maria Malfatti and 
the number two man in the Libyan Jamahiriyahan Government, Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad 
Jallud, met here today for a two-hour talks on international issues and the situation in 
the Middle East in particular. 


Malfatti reiterated the Italian position and said that the Middle East problem must be 
solved taking into consideration three principles: No territories should be annexed 
through force, every state has the right to live within safe and recognized borders, 
and recognition of the legitimate rights of the Palestinian people to have a homeland. 


Speaking of the nonaligned movement, the foreign minister said that Italy considers the 
role played by several countries "extremely important" for the maintenance of peace in 
the world. 


Today Italy and the Libyan Jamahiriyah signed a fishing agreement which calls for the 
creation of joint companies and was made possible after the Jamahiriyah dropped its 
demand for sharing fishing catches which will be divided in accordance with the two 
countries! respective stocks. 


With respect to Libyan oil sales to Italy, the Jamahiriyah confirmed that despite the 
different market conditions, Tripoli will stick to its quota of seventeen million tons 
of crude earmarked for Italy (?this year). 


Tripoli also confirmed that it will stick to its commitment to supply twenty million 
additional tons of oil to be shared out between 1980 and 1983.
