[Text] Tripoli, June 4, SPA — Saudi Crown Prince ‘Abdallah 
the first deputy premier and commander of the National Guard 
arrived here today from Riyadh on a visit to Libya. 


On his arrival, he was greeted by Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud, 
member of the General Secretariat of the General People’s 
Congress and hig*> ranking Libyan officials. 


The crown prince left Riyadh earlier today on a tour that will 
also take him te Syria, Iraq and Jordan. 


General ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud expressed his joy over the crown 
prince's visit and said he was optimistic the visit would achieve 
positive results 


In a statement to reporters, General Jallud said “we are attaching 
great importance to the visit which has a special significance.” 


“We hope the visit will be a turning point with regard to bilateral 
relations between the two countries,” he added.
