LIBYA

DEFENSE MEETING OIL EMBARGO CALL, WALKOUT REPORTED 


LD241424 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 24 Jul 81 


[Text] The Libyan delegation to the emergency meeting of the Arab Defense Council with- 
drew from the meeting hall during yesterday's session in protest against the presence of 
a delegation representing the Sudanese regime, which is in alliance with the Egyptian 
regime. The presence of a delegation representing the Sudanese regime constitutes a 
dangerous violation of the Arab defense strategy. The Sudanese regime will pass Arab 
defense secrets to the Egyptian regime which will pass them on to the Zionist enemy. 


The secretary of the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liason called 
on the Arab countries to impose an oil embargo on the United States as a consequence to 
its aggressive stands and continued support for the Zionist enemy. In his speech, 
delivered yesterday evening at the Arab council meeting which started yesterday evening 
in Tunis, he stressed that the United States unconditionally supports the Zionist enemy 
and pointed out the need for putting pressure on the United States by all means, 
including the oil weapon, to force it to change its position, which is biased in favor 

of the Israelis. He also called on the Arab countries to deploy all capabilities to pro- 
vide air and sea protection for Lebanon against the ferocious Israeli attacks. The secre- 
tary of the People's Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liason announced that 
the Jamahiriyah is ready to place all its capabilities at the disposal of the Joint Arab 
Defense Council in this respect. 


The Arab Defense Council ended its emergency meeting in the early hours of this morning 

in Tunis. The council issued a statement supporting Palestinian resistence and condemning 
the Zionist entity. The Joint Arab Defense Council comprises the foreign and defense 
ministers of Arab countries.

JANA INTERVIEWS SUDANESE OPPOSITION LEADER 


LD261535 Tripoli JANA in English 1434 GMT 26 Jul 81 


[Text] London, 26 Jul (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The leader of the Sudanese opposition, 
Husayn Zharif al-Hindi, affirmed that the Sudanese people has been launching repeated 
uprisings against the fascist regime in Sudan. These have escalated in the past year 
and spread to most parts of the country. The widespread strikes which included the 

rail workers, he said, paralysed the country. Various forms of oppression, terror, guns 
and tanks were used against the angry masses, who stood fast courageously. Mr Hindi 
added that the people were not intimidated and are still exerting every effort to bring 
the downfall of the regime. 


Speaking with our correspondent in London Hindi said: "Sudan is governed by the security 
system which exercises every form of torture and oppression against the nationals. All 
the people in Sudan, workers, farmers, professionals and the intelligentsia, stand 
together against the regime."

The Sudanese opposition, including all its factions, he said, is unanimously agreed to 
support the popular revolution until the downfall of the treacherous Numayri regime. 

The Sudanese peoples are determined to persist in their endeavour in the face of all the 
oppression being exercised. 


On the deteriorated economic situation in Sudan and Numayri's propagandist claim of the 
existence of development schemes, Hindi commented: "Sudan is bankrupt and has no credit 
to cover the costs of a single project. It is now dependent upon foreign loans and is 
incapable of increasing the payment installments of these loans. It is also failing to 
up date its debt timetable and the regime has no income whatsoever." 


Hindi continued to say that there were no economic projects for the welfare of the people 
and that those projects which have been set up have only served a limited mercenary class 
with commissions and various bribes. He pointed out that even the projects which had been 
set up earlier had ceased to operate, such as Al-Juzirah project which was the biggest 
project in Africa. The quality of production here, he stressed, has deteriorated this 
year to a very low level. 


Sudan has no resources it can export, Hindi continued, which has led to an incredible 
lowering of living standards. Markets are controlled by the monopolist merchant class 
which is extorting extremely high profits. 


As far as health or educational services, Hindi said, these are nominal and not worth 
mentioning. "All claims to development schemes are nothing but lies. What there is are 
poverty, misery and deprivation. The Sudanese people cannot find the basic necessities 
of life." 


Mr Hindi affirmed the depressing situation to be the same whether in the north or south 
of the country. The south is especially affected as hundreds are dying from famine and 
drought. He said the regime has banned the media from reporting on the miserable situ- 
ation in the south. 


On the Numayri regime's attempts to lift the Arab boycott imposed on Egypt and its call 
for efforts to be made to return Egypt to the Arab fold, he said Numayri should think 

in the first instance of returning Sudan to the Arab fold. He added: "How can Arab 
countries contemplate returning Egypt to the Arab fold when the regime there adheres 

to the Camp David accords, and when Egyptian territories are still occupied by Zionists? 
Furthermore, Egyptian and Sudanese territories are being used as military bases for colo- 
nialist powers. 


"In fact the Egyptian and Sudanese regimes are inextricably linked to world imperialism, 
and are implementing colonialist schemes in the region. 


"All conspiracies currently directed against the Arab nation are part of the Camp David 
scheme adopted by the Egyptian and Sudanese regimes. 


"Returning to the Arab fold means a public denunciation of the Camp David accords. On 
the other hand, any other Arab country that attempts to support these two regimes in their 
current state will face immediate ostracism." 


On the repercussions of the Egyptian-Sudanese alliance, Hindi said: "The alliance is a 
colonialist-Zionist conspiracy aimed at providing Israel with water from the River Nile."

SUNA INTERVIEWS DEFENSE MINISTER ON CHAv, SECURITY 


JN251935 Xhartoum SUNA in English 1725 GMT 25 Jul 81 


[Text] Kampala, 25 Jul (SUNA)--Ugandan Vice President Paulo Muwanga has attributed the 
turbulent political situation in Chad to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi of Libya whom he charged 
to being instrumental to creating troubles in various parts of the African Continent. 

In an exclusive interview with SUDAN NEWS AGENCY Muwanga said the political situation in 
Chad, I believe is caused by president Al-Qadhdhafi. I can only hope that president 
Al-Qadhdhafi would think it fit to contribute in future to the solution of African 
problems other than creating new ones, Muwanga said. 


Vice President Paulo Muwanga has denied categorically reports quoting Red Cross sources 
that fierce fighting flared up at Arua and Yambe in the West Nile Province during the 
past three weeks. He also dismissed as groundless the already spreading rumours about 
the unification of different dissident groups under the commandship of Moses Ali. 


The reports of fighting in West Nile Province are similar rumours to those the world 
community has been treated to...they have no foundation Muwanga said. 


Muwanga affirmed that the withdrawal of Tanzanian troops has not harmed the security 
situation in Uganda. He indicated that following the departure of the last Tanzanian 
soldier at the end of past month, the security situation has not only continued to be 
peaceful but it has exploded the myth that without Tanzanian troops Ugandan National 
Liberation Army could not hold on its own. 


He said Ugandan Army has received valuable assistance and he praised Sudan for its 
contribution in this regard and commended President Numayri for his deep concern over 
the issues affecting that security situation in Uganda. He quoted President Numayri 
words on the Ugandan security that as long as Uganda is peaceful with all its neighbours 
the fear of security situation in Uganda worsening withdrawal of the Tanzanian troops is 
more imaginary than true. [sentence as received] 


MUWANGA ANNOUNCES RELEASE OF EX-AMIN SOLDIERS 


EA242048 Kampala Domestic Service in English 1900 GMT 24 Jul 81 


[Excerpt] The first batch of more than 1,400 ex-Amin soldiers, who were detained at 
Luzira prison, will be released tomorrow. This was announced today by the vice president 
and minister of defense, Mr Paulo Muwanga, when he was addressing detainees at Luzira 
prison. The decision to release them was announced by President Milton Obote on 27 May 
during ceremonies to mark the first anniversary of his return from exile. 


Mr Muwanga told the detainees that the government is not interested in keeping anyone in 
prison especially at this time when all Ugandans should join hands and rebuild the 
country. 


He appealed to them to forget the past and join the ranks of law-abiding fellow 
Ugandans in the task of rehabilitation and reconstruction of the country's shattered 
economy. Amid applause, the vice president told the detainees that they would be given, 
among other things, 2,000 shillings each. He warned them not to join the bandits who 
are there to disrupt peace and unity in the country. He also advised them to work hard 
for themselves and forget the practice of divisionisnm.
