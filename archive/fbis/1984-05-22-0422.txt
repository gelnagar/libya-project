LIBYA

People Ready To ‘Transfer Battle’ to U.S., UK 
LD211756 Tripoli JANA in English 1710 GMT 21 May 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Sha‘ban 21, may 21, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR newspaper published 
by the revolutionary committees has warned today the American 
and British Governments and their agent reactionary regimes of 
the consequences of their imperialist threats and provocations 
against the Jamahiriyah confirming that the revolutionary com- 
mittees are in constant readiness to executive the death sentence 
and transfer the battle into these countries which feed terrorism. 


The paper reviewed in the editorial article headed “He Who 
Warns is Excused,” the terrorist plans conpired by these colonial 
and reactionary regimes against the Jamahiriyah. It pointed in 
this respect to what the British newspaper MAIL ON SUNDAY 
published last week, that the British Government was studying a 
military plan of aggression against the Jamahiriyah involving 


naval and air forces under the cover of rescuing the British 
citizens and this plan was presented in fact to Thatcher by the 
British war ministry last Sunday. 


The paper added that the Hashemite royal regime and ‘Arafat's 
clique are completely engaged in preparing assassination and 
sabotage operations in the Jamahiriyah and they are training 150 
Palestinians for this purpose in a camp near Az-Zarga by four 
Jordanian brigadiers and a senior Palestinian officer from ‘Ara- 
fat’s clique and the director of ‘Arafat’s Fatah organization office 
in Amman. 


The paper explained that in addition to this and what had 
happened two weeks ago when a terrorist group infiltrated the 
Jamahiriyah to carry out operations of assasination and sabotage 
of the people’s production and marketing establishments did not 
raise the anger of the American Government and its artificial 
zeal, of course, on international law and similar terms use by its 
propaganda media. [sentence as received] The paper said that 
only one thing which had raised the anger of the American 
Government and let it to cry for the international law, peace and 
security was the decision of the Basic People’s Congresses on 
their emergency meeting about chasing and liquidating all ter- 
rorist elements of stray dogs who hang around brothels of the 
Western colonial countries and reactionary Arab countries and 
are trained under supervision of the agents of these countries on 
infiltration into the Jamahiriyah and sabotage its civilized 
achievements and undermining the people’s authority. 


AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR went on its editorial article to 
say: Any aggressive action against the Libyan Arab people, and 
what is being prepared in fact today secretly and openly, is not 
terrorism in the view of the American Government, but, when 
the Libyan Arab people defend their authority and revolutionary 
achievements and decide to eliminate all those who obstruct their 
civilized march, this then becomes terrorism in the eyes of the 
Amerian black house. 


The paper confirmed: Any rational person cannot give an ear 
to what the American officials, who have been for long involved 
in contradictory statements, do and say. The American Govern- 
ment seizes every opportunity to claim its deep concern about 
international law, while it is the first to breach it when the matter 
is related to its colonial ambitions, suffice it to point out that the 
American Government refused in the last few days to obey the 
U.N. World Court of Justice decision, in The Hague condemning 
the American acts against Nicaragua, which called Reagan’s 
government to stop immediately the mining of the ports in this 
country which pursues a free national policy. 


The paper asserted that America turns things upside down when 
it explains things and looks at them with bias from the point of 
view of its imperialist selfish interests. It considers aggressive 
designs against the Jamahiriyah, revolutionary regimes and 
world liberation movements a legal act, while the act by these 
regimes and revolutionary movements to defend themselves and 
extend moral and material support to each other, an act of 
terrorism that should be penalized in every way from launching 
a propaganda campaign and imposing comprehensive economic 
sanctions to direct military intervention.

The paper explained that when the Libyan Arab people decided 
in its Basic People’s Congresses to chase and liqudate all terrorist 
elements of stray dogs everywhere who aimed at the people’s 
authority, were fully aware of the fact that this decision would 
not please the leader of international terrorism because in fact it 
embraces, trains and supplies with money and weapons these 
stray saboteurs to use them at the end to achieve its colonial aims 
and dreams of returning to the Libyan Arab territory to establish 
its military bases and dominate it and allow American cowboys 
to wander about on the streets of Tripoli with arrogance and 
delusive of obeyed masters, the same way they were before the 
rise of the Great Al-Fatih Revolution. The Libyan Arab people 
were fully aware that the deciison of the Basic People’s Con- 
gresses to chase and liquidate all stray terrorist elements would 
not please the American Government and colonial allies because 
America has been leading widescale terrorist and aggressive 
moves involving Britain, right wing ‘Arafat’s clique, the Jor- 
danian and Sudanese regimes and some other Arab reactionary 
regimes to attack the people’s authority in the Jamahiriyah which 
has become the lighthouse of guidance for the weak people who 
strive for ultimate salvation and a strong fortress for all fighters 
who confront imperialists, oppressors and people’s hangmen. 


The paper confirmed that when the Libyan Arab people decided 
through its Basic People’s Congresses to chase and liquidate the 
fugitive and stray terrorist elements who aimed to hit the civilized 
achievements realised by streams of sweat were applying in fact, 
an important clause of international law provisions, the people’s 
right to defend their freedom, territories and achievements. 


AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR concluded its editorial by saying that 
the revolutionary committees warn the American Government, 
Britain and their agent reactionary regimes of the consequences 
of imperialist threats and provocations and deliberate propa- 
ganda against the Jamahiriyah reaffirming that they are in 
constant readiness to execute the death sentence and transfer the 
battle inside these countries which feed terrorism and cut off its 
limbs and run over its injured bodies as he who warns is excused. 


At-Turayki on Support for Lebanon’s Al-Jumayyil 
NC220951 Beirut Domestic Service in Arabic 0900 GMT 
22 May 84 


[Text] In a statement he made tc the AN-NAHAR newspaper, 
Libyan Secretary of the General People’s Committee for Foreign 
Liaison Dr ‘Ali Abd as-Salam at-Turayki has said that his 
country supports President al-Jumayyil and his government and 
appreciates the national decision he and the Lebanese nationalist 
forces made when they abrogated the 17 May agreement. 


Dr at-Turayki added: We shall work with all our strength to 
achieve peace and stop the bloodshed in Lebanon. He said: We 
constantly tell our friends and allies — the Lebanese nationalist 
and political forces with whom we have relations — of the need 
to cooperate with President al-Jumayyil and to achieve national 
reconciliation on a firm and just basis so that all of Lebanon can 
stand as one rank in the face of the Zionist occupation of a large 
part of Lebanese territory.
