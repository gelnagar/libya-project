LIBYA

AL-'UBAYDI WELCOMES FRANCE'S CHEYSSON 31 MAY 


LD311936 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1720 GMT 31 May 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 31 May (JANA) -- French External Relations Minister Claude Cheysson arrived 
in Tripoli this afternoon. He was welcomed on arrival by the secretary of the people's 
committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison, ‘Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi. 


Cheysson is accompanied by a number of ministry officials. 


In a statement to JANA, Cheysson said: We hope to have very normal relations with the 
jamahiriyah, relations between two independent countries that value their independence. 

The French minister expressed pleasure at his visit, stressing his country's readiness 

to promote the existing cooperative relations between the two countries and to work for new 
prospects for these relations. 


Meeting Held 
LD312346 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 31 May 82 
[Text] The secretary of the people's committee of the People's Foreign Liaison Bureau met 
with French External Relations Minister Claude Cheysson last night. They discussed bilateral 
relations, including the development of cooperation, and examined a number of issues of common 


interest. 


COOPERATION AGREEMENT SIGNED WITH IRELAND 31 MAY 


LD010734 Tripoli JANA in English 1800 GMT 31 May 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 31 May (JANA) -- The jamahiriyah and Ireland today initialled an economic, 
social and cultural agreement at the end of the Irish agriculture minister's visit to the 
jamahiriyah. The agreement was initialled for Libya by the general people's committee 
secretary of education and for the Irish side by the agriculture minister. The agreement 
calls for the development of bilateral cooperation in various fields.
