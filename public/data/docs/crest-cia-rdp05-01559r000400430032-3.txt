Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a 2 May 1988 Al-Sharq al-Awsat interview with Qadhdhafi, held at al-'Aziziyah Barracks in Tripoli. He appeals to Libyans and Arabs abroad to return, says there is no opposition, and describes recent releases of prisoners and decisions on political freedoms.
• Qadhdhafi said there is no opposition in Libya and that newspapers may be published only if not financed from abroad.
• He said a few dozen prisoners who exploited religion in politics or conspired with foreigners were still held, and that all foreigners had been released.
• He said Libya had demolished prisons and borders and that Arabs could enter without a passport or visa.
• He said he personally opposed the death sentence and hard labour.
• 1988-04-30: Qadhdhafi interviewed by Talhah Jibril and Arab journalists at al-'Aziziyah Barracks in Tripoli; he appealed for Arabs and Libyans abroad to return and said recent decisions had released prisoners, including those who had plotted.
