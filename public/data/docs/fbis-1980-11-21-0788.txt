LIBYA

IRAN'S RAFSANJANI ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 20 NOV 
LD201806 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 20 Nov 80 


[Text] Hojjat ol-Eslam Rafsanjani, speaker of the Iranian Consultative Assembly, and a 
delegation accompanying him, arrived this evening at Tripoli international airport on a 
visit to the Jamahiriyah. He was welcomed by the secretary of the General People's 
Congress, secretary for the affairs of the people's congresses and the Libyan ambassador 
to Iran. 


Airport Statement 
LD202100 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 20 Nov 80 


[Text] The speaker of the Iranian Islamic Consultative Assembly praised the attitude of 
the great 1 September revolution to the Iranian Islamic revolution. He said: This 
attitude has always been positive, constructive and useful, and we have met cn the part 
of our brothers in the Jamahiriyah with all support for and comprehensive solidarity with 
our Islamic revolution. 


In a statement to JANA at Tripoli international airport the speaker of the Consultative 
Assembly expressed his satisfaction and that of the delegation accompanying him at visiting 
the Jamahiriyah. He said: Today, we feel at home and among our kinfolk. He expressed 
confidence that his visit and that of the delegation accompanying him to the Jamahiriyah 
will have good results for the consolidation of the strong relations between the two Islamic 
revolutions in various fields, and support for the unity of the ranks of the Islamic 
progressive forces everywhere, and the elimination of the colonialist-Christian-Zionist- 
reactionary alliance in the region.
