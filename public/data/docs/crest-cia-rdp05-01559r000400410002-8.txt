He defends Palestinian armed struggle, warns of war with the US, promises to "tip the balance" in the Mediterranean, and talks of suicide squads and US economic measures. The document says nothing about specific Tripoli sites.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya might go to war at any time to remove state terrorism, which he said would count as self-defence under Article 51 of the UN Charter.
• He said he would accept Palestinian camps on Libyan territory if the aim was liberating occupied Palestine.
• He said the US had frozen Libyan assets and that this was being studied.
• He said suicide squads had been set up by popular masses at a congress in a municipality, reportedly in the central area of Libya.
• He referred to a Libyan decision of 28 March 1985 at the Al-Nasir Base about tipping the Mediterranean balance.
