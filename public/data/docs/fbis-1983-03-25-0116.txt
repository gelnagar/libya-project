AL-QADHDHAFI_ CONGRATULATES HASSAN ON THRONE DAY 


LD250826 Rabat Domestic Service in Arabic 1300 GMT 24 Mar 83 


{"Text" of cable from Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-OQadhdhafi to King Hassan on occasion 
of throne day -- date not given] 


[Text] Your Majesty King Hassan II: 


On the occasion of the National Day of your sister country, it pleases me to convey to 
you, in the name of the people of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, sin- 
cerest congratulation coupled with my best wishes. I ask God to repeat the occasion with

Moroccan people. 


SDAR_ PRESIDENT ABDELAZIZ ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 


LD230712 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0015 GMT 23 Mar 83 


[Text] Mohamed Abdelaziz, president of the SDAR and secretary general of the Polisario, 
and Abdelkader Talebomar, member of the Political Bureau of the Polisario, and an accomp- 
anying delegation arrived in Tripoli yesterday evening. 


Brother Mohamed Abdelaziz made a statement to the JANA reporter at Tripoli International 
Airport in which he said: We come to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahirivah 

at a time when we are linked by one destiny and one objective. We are linked by 
revolutionary ties and we fight for revolutionary aims against the common enemies of 

the Arab nation -- namely Zionism, imperialism, and reaction. . 


He said: We are in need of such meetings to define the course of our aims and pan-Arab 
aspirations. He noted the determination of the revolutionary forces to extricate them- 
selves from the current Arab reality, which is weak and complacent. He said: In our 
struggle for the freedom of our people, we rely on the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah and on the aid and support it gives us. 


He added: The SDAR is a fact. We have influence in the region and are considered a 
fundamental element in northwest Africa. He said that the Moroccan royalist regime still 
clings to its hostile stand. It is a stand which is against the Saharan people and 
which serves imperialism, its aims, and its expansionist plans. 


JANA: UN IGNORES COMPLAINT BY CHAD'S HABRE 


LD250007 Tripoli JANA in English 1436 GMT 24 Mar 83 


[Text] New York, Jumada Thani 10, March 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The complaint 
filed by Hissein Habre to the U.N. Security Council against the Jamahiriyah, at the 
equest of the U.S. Administration, was met with indifference. Our correspondent in New 
York said that the Security Council's members did not give any attention to Habre's 
representative's trivial remarks made before the council. 


During this very short session, the representatives of Egypt, Habre and Senegal attacked 
the Jamahiriyah. The Egyptian representative's speech before the council was hostile to 
the Jamahiriyah in the same way that the U.S. Administration's hostility towards the 
Jamahiriyah is usually expressed. 


The Jamahiriyah permanent representative to the U.N. refuted in his speech the allega- 
tions expressed by the three representatives and were in line with the U.S. hostile 
attitude towards the Libyan people.

IRANIAN DELEGATION ARRIVES FOR ANNIVERSARY 


LD240943 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0735 GMT 24 Mar 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Mar (JANA) -- A large popular delegation from the Islamic Republic 
of Iran, headed by Hojjat ol-Eslam Hashemi Rasuli, director of the office of Imam 
Khomeyni, arrived last night in Tripoli on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah within the framework of supporting and reinforcing cooperation and 
relations between the Jamahiriyah and Iranian revolutions. 


The fraternal Iranian popular delegation, headed by Hojjat ol-Eslam Rasuli, will attend 
the Libyan Arab People's 13th anniversary celebrations marking the expulsion of colonial 


British bases from Libyan Arab soil. 


STUDENTS PREPARE MARCH ON FOREIGN LIAISON BUREAU 


LD242032 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1940 GMT 24 Mar 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Mar (JANA) -- The General Congress of Jamahiriyah Students held an 
emergency meeting at Al-Fatih University this evening which was attended by the 
secretaries of the students congresses in the municipalities, the secretaries of the 
students popular committees at educational institutions, universities, and high schools, 
and the secretaries of the students congresses abroad. 


During the emergency meeting, members of the popular committee for students liaison 

and popular students committee for foreign liaison were elected so that the students 
could participate in the march on the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison along with 

other vocational congresses. 


At the end of the emergency meeting, the General Congress of Jamahiriyah Students issued 
a statement affirming the adherence of the masses of students to the direct popular 
authority and affirming also the implementation of the resolutions of the Basic 

People's Congresses as formulated by the General People's Congress with regard to the 
march on the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison by all the vocational congresses. 


The statement urged all the other vocational congresses to speed up the formation of 
their committees for marching on the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison.
