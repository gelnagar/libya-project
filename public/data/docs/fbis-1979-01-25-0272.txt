AL-QADHDHAFI COMMENTS ON KURDS, ERITREA, SAHARA 
LD241423 Paris LE MONDE in French 21-22 Jan 79 p 3 LD 


[Unattributed report; "The Kurds Constitute a Nation Which Has Been Partitioned" ] 


[Text] The Kurds constitute a nation which has been partitioned among surrounding 
countries, Libyan head of state Colonel Al-Qadhdhafi stated on 18 January. 


Asked about the rise of nationalism in the Arab world during an interview broadcast by 
Radio France Internationale, he said that you "could talk about nationalism with reference 
to Kurds in the same way you could talk about nationalism in Ireland," 


"However," the Libyan president emphasized, "minorities which do not belong to a nation-- 
like the Circassians in the Arab East--must be willing to exercise their duties and rights 
within the framework of the nation to which they belong even though they are not of Arab 
origin," 


"However," he stated, "this does not apply to the Kurds, They constitute the Kurd nation 
in the Middle East where they have a geographical position," 


The Libyan head of state also referred to his appeals in support of an end to hostilities 
between the Eritreans and the Ethiopians and came out "against a solution imposed by force," 


As far as West Sahara is concerned, Colonel Al-Qadhdhafi condemned the "invasion" of 
the territory and "the fact that the Saharan people have been treated like a flock of 
sheep by the Moroccan Royal Army."

Joint Communique Issued 
LD241912 Tripoli JANA in English 1753 GMT 24 Jan 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, January 24, Jamahiriyah News Agency--The president of the Republic of 
Guinea, Ahmed Sekou Toure, left Tripoli this afternoon after ending a two-day visit to 
the Jamahiriyah,. 


The Guinean president was seen off officially and publicly, and Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
the leader of the great lst of September revolution and secretary-general of the General 
People's Congress, was at foremost of those bidding him farewell. Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam 
Jallud, Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, Lt Col Mustafa al-Kharrubi, Maj Al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydis, 
head and members of the General People's Committee, under secretaries, the Jamahiriyah 
ambassador accredited to Guinea, the Guinean ambassador and members of the Guinean Embassy 
in the Jamahiriyah were present, 


The following joint communique has been issued after the visit: 


At the invitation of Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the great lst of September 
revolution and secretary general of the General People's Congress, in the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, His Excellency Ahmed Sekou Toure, president of the 
People's Revolutionary Republic of Guinea visited the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah during the period from 23-25 Safar, 1399 Hijra, corresponding to 22-24 
January 1979, at the head of a high-level delegation, 


During the visit, the two leaders met and held talks on the bilateral relations between 
the sisterly countries, and the Islamic, African, Arab and international issues, 


On the level of bilateral relations, the two leaders reaffirmed the brotherly and 
historical ties between the peoples of the two countries and expressed their satis- 
faction towards the development of these relations in all fields.

They also affirmed their adherence to work for achieving further cooperative relations 
between them to set an example to be followed as well as a true expression of mutual 
confidence between the two leaders and peoples. 


On the Islamic level, the two leaders reaffirmed their determination to support all 
Islamic causes, to stand by Islamic nations who are struggling against exploitation 
and oppression and to construct a society in which justice and equality prevail as 
preached by holy Islam, 


In this respect, they affirmed the continuation of their support for Muslims in the world. 


Q& the African level, the two leaders note that the continent is facing an imperialist, 
racist onslaught again, and they affirmed their determination to continue struggle wtil 
the whole African continent is liberated from the forces of evil, racism and imperialism. 


In this framework, the two leaders expressed their condemnation of the policy of the 
imperialist forces which aim at reintroducing their direct supremacy on the African 
continent. They called for the necessity of evacuation of imperialist forces from the 
continent and the liquidation of imperialist bases there. 


The two leaders also affirmed the importance of liberating African peoples from influence, 
monopolies and oppressive racism. They believe that armed struggle against imperialism 
and racism is the only way to freedom of African peoples, therefore they decided to 
continue supporting and consolidating all African liberation movements whether those that 
are against racist, settling imperialism in the sourthem part of the continent or those 
that are still struggling against the supremacy of foreign forces, In this respect, the 
two sides affirm their total and firm support for the right of the peoples who are 
struggling for self-determination against imperialism and racist regimes, 


Upon reviewing the provocations of the racist forces against the neighbouring African 
countries of the occupied regions in the south of the continent, they affirmed their 
absolute support for the stand of these countries. They also decided to support them for 
steadfastness against the violations they face on their lands. They also praise, on this 
occasion, the heroic stand of each of the republics of Angola and Mozambique. 


On the Arab level, the two leaders praised the efforts being made by the Arab and African 
communities to promote cultural and economic progress, thus thwarting imperialism, 


They stressed that the concern of the Arab-African wity was to work towards the fulfill- 
ment of economic and political freedom for all the Arab and African peoples. This will 
benefit all the peoples of the Third World because it is based on linkage between the 
two communities, historically, geographically, and through their civilizatims. 


The two leaders condemned any effort aimed at excluding the Palestinian people from 
dealing with their own cause. The two sides also asserted their determination to support 
and reinforce the Palestinian people to restore their own land and all their rights. 


On the international level, the two leaders affirmed their adherence to the principles of 
the United Nations, the resolutions and recommendations of the Islamic conference, and 

the conferences of the non-aliged countries, They call for the need to amend the Charter 
of the United Nations to bring about justice and equality among all cowmtries.

They also call for the need for a new international economic system which recognizes 
the rights of the Third World countries on the basis of justice and equality. 


At the end of the visit, the Guinean President Ahmed Sekou Toure expressed his appre- 
ciation of the great lst of September revolution which aims at providing prosperity 
and setting up social justice in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


President Sekou Toure directed an official invitation to Col Mu'tammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
the leader of the great lst of September revolution and secretary-general of the 
General People's Congress, to visit the revolutionary People's Republic of Guinea. 
The invitation was accepted but its date will be decided later,
