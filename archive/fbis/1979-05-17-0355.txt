LIBYA

NIGER PRESIDENT KOUNTCHE ENDS VISIT 15 MAY 
LD151901 Tripoli JANA in English 1751 GMT 15 May 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, May 15, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--President Seyni Kountche of Niger ended 
his visit to the Jamahiriyah this afternoon, 


An official send-off was given to the Niger president at Tripoli International Airport led 
by Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, a number of secretaries of general peoples com- 
mittees and the ambassadors of the two countries, 


In a statement to our representative, President Kountche said that his visit was extremely 
fruitful, and that he held talks with Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 
First of September Revolution, and Staff Major Jallud, on strengthening bilateral relations 
and on reviewing current international issues in general and Arab and African issues in 
particular, 


President Kountche concluded his statement by expressing his appreciation for the warm 
welcome and hospitality he had received from the Libyan Arab people and their leadership. 


JAPANESE FRIENDSHIP SOCIETY DELEGATION ARRIVES 


LD161228 Tripoli JANA in English 0810 amr 16 May 79 LD 


(Text] Tripoli, 16 May, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Japanese-Arab friendship delegation 
arrived yesterday in Tripoli headed by a member of the Central Committee of the Japanese 
Socialist Party and representatives from the Arab-Japanese friendship society on a five- 
day visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, 


The delegation was received at Tripoli International Airport by the secretary general and 
members of the Arab-Japanese friendship society and a representative from the foreign 
liaison bureau, 


In a statement to our correspondent the head of the delegation praised the development of 
the relations between the Japanese and the Libyan Arab people, 


He said that the delegation's visit comes within the framework of the consolidation of 
relations between the Arab and the Japanese peoples.
