Summary (AI): A 1984 CIA memo from the Acting National Intelligence Officer for Africa discusses how Sudan's President Nimeiri may view Qadhafi's new "moderate" stance after Libya's accords with Morocco and France. It raises the possibility that Nimeiri might seek accommodation with Qadhafi to end Libyan backing for rebels in southern Sudan. Nothing here concerns places in Tripoli.
• Libya reached an accord with Morocco (King Hassan) that Nimeiri saw as unprecedented.
• A French-Libyan agreement on Chad could again plunge Chad into internal conflict once French troops leave.
• Libyan-backed rebels were active in southern Sudan.
• The US reportedly had virtually no warning of the French-Libyan agreement.
