LIBYA

STATEMENT ON UAE LEADER'S 1 JUL VISIT TO TRIPOLI 


LDO11500 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 1 Jul 81 


["Text" of statement issued in Tripoli on 1 July following visit to Libya by UAE President 
Shaykh Zayid ibn Sultan Al Nuhayyan] 


[Excerpts] At the invitation of his brother, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, commander of the 
Great 1 September Revolution, UAE head of state Shaykh Zayid ibn Sultan Al Nuhayyan paid a 
visit to Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah accompanied by an important delegation 
consisting of Shaykh Rashid al-Mu'alla, member of the Higher Council and ruler of Umm 
al-Quwain, Presidential Adviser Ahmad as-Suwaydi and Minister of State for Foreign Affairs 
Rashid ‘Abdallah. . 


During the visit discussions were held on the international situation in general and the 
situation in the Arab region in particular. The leaders of the two countries affirmed their 
absolute faith in the unity of the Arab nation and the need to mobilize its capabilities 

to face the challenges of Zionism and imperialism. 


His Highness Shaykh Zayid ibn Sultan Al Nuhayyan expressed his support for and appreciation 
of the initiative made by the brother commander of the Great 1 September Revolution for 
unifying the Arab and Islamic nation's capabilities, for rising above bilateral squabbles 
and for deploying all the capabilities of the Arab and Islamic nations in the service of 
the common battle against the common enemy. 


His Highness Shaykh Zayid appeals to leaders of the Arab and Islamic nations to respond 
sincerely to the sincere initiative which aims at building an Arab and Islamic power 
capable of safeguarding the dignity of the Arabs and Muslims and protecting the Islamic 
sanctities. 


Within this context, the two countries' leaders address an Islamic appeal to the brothers 
in Iraq and Iran to stop the shedding of Islamic blood and to declare a truce on the 
occasion of the blessed month of Ramadan. 


As to the Western Sahara, the two sides stressed the importance of settlement of this 
problem directly by the two parties involved in the conflict for the realization of 
self-determination. 


The commander of the revolution affirmed his support for the Arab Gulf Cooperation Council. 
He also praised the role played by Shaykh Zayid within this council, through Arab unity. 


The two sides studied the grave effects and losses incurred by the oil-producing countries 
because of the oil glut in the markets. In this regard, they affirmed the need for drawing 
up a unified policy of prices and production within the framework of OPEC to be adhered to 
and respected by all member countries. 


During the meetings Shaykh Zayid expressed his appreciation of the initiative by the brother 
commander in calling for Arab solidarity and the casting aside of marginal squabbles among 
the brothers for the sake of confronting the plots hatched against our Arab nation. 


Shaykh Zayid ibn Sultan Al Nuhayyan left the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
this morning. He was seen off by the brother commander of the revolution.

U.S. STATE DEPARTMENT POSITION ON OAU CONDEMNED 


LD011814 Tripoli JANA in English 1445 GMT 1 Jul 81 


{[Text] Tripoli, 1 Jul (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)-~Dean Fisher, spokesman of the U.S. State 
Department, has criticised the Organisation of African Unity (0.A.U.), for the latter's 
condemnation of U.S. relations with the racist South African regime at its recent summit in 
Nairobi. He also criticised the 0.A.U. for its support for Jamahiriyah and the latter's 
presence in Chad. Fisher, furthermore, criticised the summit's decision to hold the next 
summit in Libya in 1982. 


Mr. Ibrahim al-Beshari, secretary of the Administrative Committee for Revolutionary 
Information in the Jamahiriyah, has responded to Fisher's criticism of the African countries, 
with the following statement: 


One--the American administration is embroiled in backing fascism and racism throughout the 
world, particularly the racist South African regime and the Zionist racist regime. The 
African unanimous condemnation of relations between the U.S.A. and the apartheid regime of 
South Africa is an historic international condemnation. 


TIwo--Fisher's statement is an interference in the internal affairs of African countries 
which are independent sovereign states, and members of an international organization, the 
0.A.U. 


Three—-The summit decision to hold the next conference in Libya affirms Libya's dis- 
tinguished and great standing in Africa, whether America liked it or not. 


Four--Fisher's arguments on terrorism are invalid because the United States is the major 
terror machine in the world and her interference in African affairs is a part of its 
terrorising of the world's peoples. 


Five--The United States appoints herself as an international policeman which corrects 
others’ conduct, while the U.S. international code of conduct is confirmed as colonialism 
and terrorist. 


Six--the African support for Libya's efforts toward achieving peace in Chad reflects Africa's 
awareness and determination to solve her own problems and affirms that the Africans appreci- 
ate Libya's sacrifices for Chad. This in itself angered the United States which looks at 
Chad as an area of U.S. influence. 


Seven--The U.S. administration has failed in influencing the African countries to take an 
anti-Libyan stance, despite all its contacts with African governments and messages sent by 
Reagan to several African heads of states to this effect. 


All these U.S. moves have failed, because the African now recognised the reality of the 
colonialist, terrorist and racist policy of the U.S.A.
