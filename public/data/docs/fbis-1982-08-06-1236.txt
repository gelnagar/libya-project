LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFT, MACHEL SIGN FRIENDSHIP TREATY 


LD051850 Tripoli JANA in English 1718 GMT 5 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 16, August 5, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Colonel Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi and President Samora Machel of Mozambique today signed a joint agreement of 
friendship and cooperation between the Jamahiriyah and Mozambique. 


On the Mozambique side, it was attended by the ministers of foreign affairs, justice and 
information, who are accompanying President Machel on his visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


Trade, Military Agreements 
EA060820 Maputo Domestic Service in Portuguese 0430 GMT 6 Aug 82 


[Text] Presidents Aamora Machel and Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi yesterday in Tripoli signed a 
general agreement on cooperation between the People's Republic of Mozambique and the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


The agreement, which lays down general guidelines on cooperation between the two countries 
in a number of fields, was complemented with other accords, namely, a trade agreement, a 
military cooperation agreement and an agreement on the constitution of an investments 
enterprise. 


A Radio Mozambique correspondent reported that sources close to the Mozambique presiden- 
tial delegation described the official visit and the agreements that were signed as 
positive and friendly. The signing of the agreements marked the end of the official visit 
by President Samora Machel to Libya. 


Meanwhile, Field Marshal Samora Machel is still in Tripoli to attend the 19th summit 
meeting of the OAU heads of state. 


As for tue holding of the OAU summit meeting, our correspondent said that the heads of 
State and govermment present in the Libyan capital are promoting intense diplomatic 


activity to attain the necessary quorum of about 34 countries. 


President Samora Machel yesterday met the heads of state of Madagascar, Algeria, Congo 
and Tanzania and the Sao Tome and Principe foreign minister.
