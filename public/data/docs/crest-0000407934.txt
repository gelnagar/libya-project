• The alleged Kikhia abduction would be the first act of Libyan terrorism since 1990 and the first confirmed operation against a Libyan dissident overseas since 1987, per the report's comment.
• Qadhafi asked the Pope to intervene on his behalf in the Pan Am 103 affair in early December 1993.
• France and Belgium rejected Libyan proposals in late November 1993 to try the Pan Am 103 suspects in their countries.
• The French judge investigating the UTA bombing rejected a Libyan offer to cooperate, citing its lack of specificity.
