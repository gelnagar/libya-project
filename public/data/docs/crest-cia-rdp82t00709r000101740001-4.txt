Summary (AI): A June 1982 CIA briefing note reports the first complete SA-8 surface-to-air missile battery identified in Libya, at a SAM support facility in Tarabulus (Tripoli) with a SAM training school to the east-southeast. Many passages are redacted, so the exact site location is unclear.
• First complete SA-8 SAM battery identified in Libya; first identification of SA-8 equipment at an operational SAM facility.
• Battery consisted of four SA-8 TLAR vehicles, one probable SA-8 resupply vehicle and one probable BTR-60PA command vehicle.
• The Tarabulus SAM support facility also supports an SA-6 regiment.
• Document gives coordinates 32-46-30N 013-12-10E for the facility.
• 1982-06: Complete SA-8 firing battery identified for the first time in Libya: four transporter-erector-launcher-radar vehicles, one probable resupply vehicle and one probable BTR-60PA command vehicle.
