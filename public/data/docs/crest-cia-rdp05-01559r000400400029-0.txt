Summary (AI): An FBIS Okinawa cable reports that Japan's NHK aired a six-minute satellite interview with Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi on 3 June 1985. He says Libya finds it difficult to obtain nuclear weapons, is not pursuing them as policy, and opposes nuclear weapons worldwide. The interview also covers Libya's acceptance of Japanese Red Army member Kozo Okamoto and Libya's stance in the Iran-Iraq war.
• Interview conducted in English with Japanese subtitles, about one hour before broadcast via satellite relay.
• Qadhdhafi said: 'It is difficult for Libya to obtain nuclear weapons. And it is not our policy to do so.'
• Subtitles reportedly rendered the indistinct portion as: 'But to use them against Israel may be a different story.'

FYI: NHK INTERVIEWS AL-QADHDHAFI ON NUCLEAR WEAPONS 

0W031312 

TOKYO NHK TELEVISION NETWORK IN JAPANESE AT 1218 GMT ON 3 JUNE 
CARRIES A 6-MINUTE INTERVIEW WITH LIBYAN CHIEF OF STATE MU'AMMAR 
AL-QADHDHAFI, CONDUCTED "AN HOUR EARLIER" BY NHK NEWSCASTER 

TARO KIMURA VIA SATELLITE RELAY. 

IN THE INTERVIEW, CONDUCTED IN ENGLISH WITH JAPANESE TRANSLATION 
SHOWN IN SUBTITLES, AL-QADHDHAFI REPLIES TO KIMURA'S QUESTIONS ON 
WHY LIBYA ACCEPTED KOZO OKAMOTO, A JAPANESE RED ARMY TERRORIST 
RELEASED BY THE ISRAELIS, WHAT LIBYA WILL DO TO COUNTER ISRAEL'S 
ACQUISITION OF NUCLEAR WEAPONS; AND WHICH SIDE LIBYA SUPPORTS IN THE 
TRAN-IRAQ WAR. 

ASKED IF HE WOULD OBTAIN NUCLEAR WEAPONS IF THEY ARE OBTAINABLE, 
AL-QADHDHAFI SAYS: “IT IS DIFFICULT FOR LIBYA TO OBTAIN NUCLEAR 
WEAPONS. AND IT IS NOT OUR POLICY TO.DO SO. WE ARE AGAINST 
NUCLEAR WEAPONS IN THE WORLD, AND WE CALL TO DESTROY ALL THE 
NUCLEAR BOMBS IN THE WORLD. BUT TO USE THIS NUCLEAR ((WORDS 
INDISTINCT))." ACCORDING TO JAPANESE SUBTITLES, THE INDISTINCT 
PORTION READS: "BUT TO USE THEM AGAINST ISRAEL MAY BE A DIFFERENT 
STORY." 

RECEPTION IS GOOD, BUT AL-QADHDHAFI'S ENGLISH IS VERY POOR.
