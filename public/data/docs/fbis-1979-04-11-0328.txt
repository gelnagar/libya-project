AL-AHRAM REPORT ON 7 APRIL KHALIL STATEMENT 


LD101719 [Editorial Report LD] Cairo AL-AHRAM in Arabic on 8 April carries a report on 
Prime Minister Khalil's 7 April statement to People's Assembly committees and the ensu- 
ing debate. The report states: 


"Dr Jamal al-'Utayfi (independent) said: Despite our differences in views I have no doubt 
bout the patriotism of the Egyptian negotiator whom we must support with all our strength 
because the coming stage is going to be hard and very difficult. In the past stage the 


Egyptian negotiator has achieved most of what we have asked of him, such as the question 
of the formation of the UN emergency forces. We have asked that this force should not be 
composed of permanent Security Council members. Now we call for the gradual normaiiza- 
tion of relations with the stages of withdrawal. We also demand further clarification 
regarding Egypt's Arab commitment under this treaty and what is intended by the legal 
interpretation of the treaty's provision concerning this matter. This is in view of 

the fact that this provision places a serious responsibility on the Egyptian side. 


"Interjecting, Dr Khalil said: I can see that you are taking a pessimistic view of this 
article. I have been asked about Egypt!s stand should Syria seek to liberate the Golan 
Heights by force. I said we would not consider it an aggressive war but a defensive war 
because the liberation of land is a defensive action." 


Khalil later went on to state, in response to another deputy: 


"Concerning normalization of relations, the prime minister said: I hope that you will 
have no fear at all regarding normalization of relations. The exchange of diplomatic 
representatives will be carried out in a gradual manner, in accordance with the stages 
of withdrawal. 


"We have concealed nothing in the negotiations with Israel. We have spoken clearly. 
For example, a point was raised regarding whether if Syria agreed to enter into negti- 
ations with Israel and then the Israeli side became intransigent and, as a consequence, 
Syria went to war against Israel in order to liberate the Golan, such a war would be 
considered defensive. I replied: Yes, and it would be subject to the joint Arab 
defense pact." 


DEBATES PEACE 


422 Cairo Domestic Service ti: "abic O GMT 10 Apr 79 NC 

The People's ssembly, meeting alternately under the chairmanship of Speaker 

Sufi Abu Talib and Deputy Speaker as-Sayyid 'Ali as-Sayyid, today resumed its debate 
of the draft peace treaty between Egypt and Israel, its annexes, complementary agreements 
and the letters exchanged in this respect. 


The first speaker was member Hafiz Badawi, who lauded the Egyptian fighter's role in the 
glorious October War, which paved the way for the peace agreement, and which put an end 
to the bloody conflict in the Middle East. He supported the spirit and letter of the 
treaty. 


Engineer Ibrahim Shukri, leader of the Socialist Labor Party, declared his party's support 
of the treaty. He said that the party agrees to the treaty with complete awareness. 
Engineer Ibrahim Shukri reviewed the contents of the treaty and the circumstances of its 
conclusion and said that he personally ascertained that there were no secret documents. 
He added: What we have achieved is only the beginning of the road which we must cross 
with assurance. He called for exploiting the positive aspects and said that the normali- 
zation of relations with Israel is a natural course if we want to have a peace treaty. 

If some people view the subject with suspicion, the Socialist Labor Party views it as 

the minimum requirement for implementing the treaty. Noting the Arab stand, he said that 
the current reaction cannot benefit the cause and is not in its interest. He said that 
some Arab rulers want Egypt to be under trusteeship. 


Mustafa Kamil Murad, leader of the Liberal Socialist Party, declared his party's unanimous 
support for the treaty which, he said, is a step toward a comprehensive solution, not a 
bilateral agreement since it was in implementation of UN Security Council Resolutions 242 
and 338. He said that the treaty invites the Arab states to join the negotiations and 
preserves the Palestinian people's rights. He said that the presence of the Egyptian 
forces in Sinai in implementation of the treaty is, in his own opinion as a former mili- 
tary man, adequate. He noted that the Egyptian negotiator was sincere, clear, precise 

and loyal. 


Member Mahmud Abu Wafiya spoke about the Soviet Union's objective in the area and said 
that the Soviet Union is seeking to create a class struggle in Egypt. He explained 
that the peace agreement has put an end to the question of settlements in Sinai from 
which Israel would withdraw. In itself this is a gain for the Arab nation.

ISRAELI PROTEST OVER REMARKS ON SYRIA 
Cairo DPA in Arabic 2032 GMT 10 Apr 79 NC 


[Text] Cairo, 10 Aprile-Egyptian Prime Minister Dr Mustafa Khalil today rejected Israel's 
protests about his recent remarks to the Egyptian People's Assembly and described these 
protests as completely unjustified. In his remarks Dr Khalil had said that Egypt would 
under certain circumstances help Syria to recover the Golan Heights by force if Israel 

was not ready to return them through negotiations. 


In a statement to DPA, Dr Khalil affirmed that the postponement of the visits of Israeli 
Deputy Prime Minister Yiga'el Yadin and Israeli Defense Minister 'Ezer Weizman to Cairo 
has nothing to do with Israel's recent protest but is due to the Jewish Passover holiday. 
Dr Khalil also affirmed that the Egyptian people will vote in a referendum to be held 

n 19 April on whether or not they support the peace treaty with Israel. 


It should be recalled that the exchange of the instruments of the ratification of the 
treaty between Egypt and Israel, in accordance with which the treaty will become operative, 
will take place on 22 April in the Sinai Peninsula, la_er than had been previously 

lecided. Dr Khalil said that the U.S. Embassy in Cairo had shown him a protest letter 
addressed to Egypt from the Israeli Government. Dr Khalil asked that this letter should 
not pass [tamurr] without him clarifying his stand. 


An Egyptian Government spokesman has stated that Dr Khalil told the People's Assembly 
on Saturday that it is legitimate for Syria to liberate its Israeli-held territory if 
Israel remains obdurate and opposed to negotiations or restoration of territories. He 
added that this could be a Syrian defensive measure and in this case Egypt would stand 
by Syria in accordance with the Arab defense pact. The spokesman explained that there 
would be no justification for Egypt to support Syria if Israel showed a desire to hold 
negotiations while Syria was opposed to such negotiations. 


Dr Khalil told DPA that he believes that Egyptian and Israeli differences of opinion 
regarding his remarks will not obstruct the peace process.

have investments in Egypt totaling about 10 billion dollars, I do not think that will 
be withdrawn, 


SPIEGEL: What justifies this optimism of yours? 


An-Nasir: Eight of the $10 billion are invested in such a way that it will be hard to 
withdraw them, so there will remain $2 billion--this is one fifth of the amount, 


SPIEGEL: Even the withdrawal of $2 billion would hit Egypt. 


An-Nasir: The decisions of Baghdad clearly indicate that only such Egyptian firms 
will be involved that are trading with Israel. 


SPIEGEL: Are there many? 

An-Nasir: Egyptian-Israeli trade will take place within very narrow limits, Egypt, a 
developing country, will need most urgently money and technology. For credits, Israel 
probably is not the right address, 

SPIEGEL: The Israelis hope, however, to sell you their technology, 


An-Nasir: We would prefer the technology of the West. 


SPIEGEL: Egyptian tourism expert Moustafa Niasi conducted negotiations in Tel Aviv on 
un agreement, Will tourism soon develop between the two countries? 


An-Nasir: Yes, Israelis will come to us who want to see our antiquities, and Egyptians 
will visit religious places in Israel, 


SPIEGEL: Are you not afraid that foreign companies will discontinue supplying the 
Arab world from Cairo?

An-Nasir: This will not involve many companies, First, the Egyptian market itself is 
interesting and lends itself to expansion, and second, foreign companies prefer a 
politically stable country like Egypt. Nobody will renounce us, 


SPIEGEL: Will private Arab investors not withdraw their money from Egypt out of dis- 
appointment over the agreement? 


An-Nasir: I think this is rather improbable. You know that we Arabs easily get excited, 
but we also calm down again just as quickly. 


SPIEGEL: How long will it take until the average Egyptian will profit from peace? 
An-Nasir: I reckon with a starting period of about 2 years. 

SPIEGEL: Does Egypt expect additional development aid from Bonn? 

An-Nasir: President as-Sadat met with the full understanding of Chancellor Schmidt, 
We do not anticipate any special service specifically from the Federal Republic; 
rather, we harbor the justified hope for a joint action of the European community.
