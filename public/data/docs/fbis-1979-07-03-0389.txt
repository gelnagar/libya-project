{Interview granted by Libyan Chairman Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to Louis Faris "during recent 
visit to Damascus"] 


[Text] [Question] Mr Chairman, what are the objectives of your current tour, and which 
countries will you be visiting? 


[Answer] We will try to visit all the Arab countries in the East. Before coming to 
Syria I visited Algeria. As for the objectives, they are to try to bring the Arab nation 
together and boost its steadfastness in the face of the major J.S.-backed Zionist on- 
slaught against the Arab nation. We must continue to tighten the noose around the enemy 
until he dies. The Arab nation mst now make up for the loss of Egypt and step up 

(the siege) against the enemy, the Egyptian regime and all the forces hostile to the 
Arabination. We must try to change the Arab League Charter. I suggested that Tunis or 
Kuwait be the league headquarters. My proposal was accepted, and Tunis has become the 
league's headquarters. Tunisia is now enthusiastic about having the league's headquarters 
on its territory and about the Arab League becoming a reality and playing an effective 
role, unlike the sickly role it used to play when 1t was based in Egypt.

To satisfy this aim the Arab League Charter must be revised. This is one of the major 
tasks I wanted to carry out during this tour; that is, to reach an Arab agreement to 
restore accord within the Arab League. We also want a regular Arab summit to be held 
annually and an Arab League secretary general to be appointed periodically--not annually 
but periodically. I would support the proposal that the first secretary general be from 
Tunisia and that the first Arab summit be held in Tunis. 


I would also like to see the restoration of the Arab military industries organization, 
which was dissolved following the fall of Egypt. We will revive it on an Arab basis. 
One of the major tasks of this tour also concerns the question of Lebanon, of southern 
Lebanon, of the steadfastness of Lebanon, of reconstructing the south, protecting the 
Palestinian resistance and trying to mend the serious rift with the Lebanese front. The 
Lebanese people have suffered a great deal and made sacrifices for the sake of the 
Lebanese cause. The Arab nation must help Lebanon in this national problem, Yes, these 
are the things that I wish to achieve during my tour. 


[Question] How do you evaluate the unity process between Syria and Iraq, and does Libya 
intend to join that unity? 


[Answer] There is no doubt that Libya is a pro-unity state; it is a genuine supporter of 
unity. The geographical factor plays a big role inthematter., Libya is in the western 
part of the homeland, while Iraq is in the far eastern part, as is Syria. I was one 

of the first to favor and advocate an immediate unity between Syria and Iraq. I am 
prepared to take serious steps on this road. I feel that my fervent calls for Iraqi- 
Syrian unity are, thank God, now being answered, We are prepared to place all our 
capabilities at the disposal of this unity so that it will succeed and will lead toa 
genuine and even immediate unity between Syria and Iraq. This is a must in order to 
face the serious imbalance which imperialism and Zionism have succeeded in creating 
through imposing surrender on Egypt. 


[Question] What are the possibilities of Libya joining the Syrian-Iraqi unity? 


[Answer] I have already told you that Libya is a pro-unity country and looks forward to 
unity. The Libyans have no objection or reservation whatever with respect to the estab- 
lishment of Arab unity, but the geographical factor does not allow it. Geographically 
unity between Syria and Iraq seems quite possible. Unity between Libya, Tunisia and 
Algeria is geographically possible. Proceeding from this premise, it would be some- 
thing truly great if we could unite Libya with Iraq. 


[Question] Much has been said about Libya's attitude toward Syrian-Iraqi unity. Allow 
me to insist on hearing your real opinion on this subject. 


[Answer] We were the first to call for this unity and to applaud it. We will work 
earnestly for its achievement. 


[Question] The self-rule talks between Egypt and Israel have begun while Jordan, Libya 
and all the rest of the Arab countries are opposing this plan. What is your role in 
this, and how are your relations with Jordan? How do you evaluate the Jordanian role 
at this state? 


[Answer] Our relations with Jordan are, of course, good. They are brotherly relations. 
There is great cooperation between the two countries.

We call on Jordan to shoulder the responsibility of the Palestinian resistance's, but 
we will not leave Jordan to shoulder the burden and consequences of this responsibility 
alone, We will support Jordan and stand with it in shouldering the responsibilities, 
burden and consequences of the Palestinians! return to their bases. This is anhistoric 
mission and a national and historical duty which Jordan must shoulder. We must stand 
with Jordan in order to be able to shoulder it. 


Therefore, steps between the Palestinians and Jordanians must be achieved on this 


basis, particularly since they are closer to each other where reality and positions are 
concerned, 


As for the question of self-rule and what is being said in this regard, I firmly believe 
that the present position of the West Bank and Gaza Strip under occupation is much better 
in self-rule terms than the self-rule being proposed now. There is self-rule under the 
occupation, and it is much better than the formula now proposed. In other words, if 

the self-rule now being negotiated between the Egyptian Government, the Israelis and the 
Americans is achieved, it will be a step backward. Self-rule does exist now, and what 
they are trying to propose is a big trick. It is a step backward even for the international 
stance that supports the establishment of a Palestinian state. Recently President Tito, 
who is a great leader and one of the founders of the nonalined movement, visited Libya. 
He made a speech in which he said that "the Palestinian people must return to their home 
and establish their own independent Palestinian state. The whole world agrees on the 
establishment of an independent Palestinian state, and not on the establishment of the 
proposed self-rule, which is a step backward and runs counter to the world's unanimeus 
stand on the need for the establishment of 4 Palestinian state. 


[Question] How do you visualize the outcome of your efforts to thwart the self-rule 
plan? 


[Answer] One of the tasks I will carry out during my present tour is to reach 

an agreement with the Arabs--except with those who have surrendered--on how to resist 
this plan, after understanding exactly what is happening now. I hope to be able to foil 
this conspiracy, because it is a major and serious conspiracy. 


[Question] You have encouraged the bolstering of Palestinian national unity. How do you 
view this matter now, and how are you supporting the Palestinian revolution? What are 
in your opinion, the encouraging and the negative factors in this regard? 


[Answer] You are probably aware of the document signed in Tripoli among the Palestinian 
fronts regarding the question of unity. I believe that the situation since the document 
was signed has changed. I mean that the Palestinian situation now is better than before, 
but unity has not yet reached it: proper status. One of the most important topics discussed 
at the Tripoli conference of the Palestinian fronts was the need to reorganize the PLO 
Executive Committee so that it is formed of leaders of the Palestinian fronts. The 
Executive Committee should compromise them under Yasir ‘Arafat's leadership. That is 

the true position to be achieved so that the horse can be put before the cart. Now the 
Cart is before the horse, (Chairman al-Qadhdhafi here laughed loudly)
