AL-QADHDHAFI ON EGYPT IAN-ISRAELI MOVES 
LD271549 Tripoli JANA in English 1414 GMT 27 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, February 27, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The leader of the revolution 
addressed the following cable to the kings, princes and presidents of Arab states: 


Brothers, kings, princes and presidents of Arab states, no doubt the Arab nation was 
shocked as it saw the (Israeli) ambassador present his credentials officially to a head 

of an Arab state, namely Egypt, while an Arab ambassador presents his credentials officially 
to the president of the enemy state, 


This shock was clearly expressed by the Sudanese Government which did not think that the 
Egyptian president would go as far as this in his treasonous course, That is the govern- 
ment which had maintained its links with the As-Sadat government until he fell in the 
shameful predicament, and followed his path until everything turned upside down. But 

you may not hate something which might turn out to be to your benefit. 


Brothers, As-Sadat's actions after several preparations led to very serious consequences 
for the region and for the future of the Arab nation from the ocean to the Gulf. These 
serious issues include: 


1--The presentation of the Egyptian ambassador!s credentials in Jerusalem indicated that 
the two sides agreed that Jerusalem is a Jewish capital. This had been approved after 
the ambassador himself was accepted, in spite of As-Sadat's previous declarations about 
Jerusalem, 


2--The huge military aid given to the two sides by the USA following the normalization 

of relations meant that the two sides have now become a military, political and economic 
alliance, This was being confirmed every week. It meant more than an alliance, that 

the intention is clearly towards the control of the oilfields through this dirty alliance 
and, moreover, the control of the religious holy shrines in the Middle East, from 
Jerusalem to Mecca and from Hebron to Khaiber and Medina, 


3-- Following the treaties and declarations and political and military manoeuvres in the 
Arab Gulf and Arab Peninsula as well as in the rest of the offended Arab world, following 
the birth of this illegitimate Egyptian-(Israeli) alliance supported by the USA, which 
had alleged to be civilized and branded Arabs as backward like the Africans, it has now 
become clear that the Arab nation is to be divided for the benefit of others, 


4--As-Sadat has convinced himself and his new allies that Egypt is not Arab and that, 

like (Israel), it is alien. Aliens tend to befriend each other, This is the slogan 
As-Sadat is advocating with the enemies of the Arab nation, This proved there is a genuine 
intention to establish an alliance against Arabs in order to rob them of their national 
resources particularly energy, to gradually destroy the Arab nation until the Arabs them- 
selves become aliens, astray, far from civilization.

As-Sadat expressed this openly by saying: We Egyptians and (Israelis) meet as civilized 
peoples. Even if Arabs did not submit and even if their oil and holy shrines are not 
seized, this new alliance is designed to terrorize them until they give in and act 
according to the enemy!s wishes. 


5--What we should deduct from all this is: 


That As-Sadat!s policies should not be attributed to the Arabs and should not be consid- 
ered as Arab, This would be a fatal mistake, These policies should be looked upon as 
designed by a man who convinced himself and his allies that he is an enemy of the Arabs 
and a brother of the (Israelis). To the whole world this is as clear as daylight. 
However it cannot blind the sight but blinds the heart. 


As to our belief in Egypt's true Arabism, its belonging and common destiny, it is firm 
in our hearts and in the hearts of the Egyptians and could not be shaken by the treasons 
of As-Sadat which have gone as far as apostasy and absurdity and are seen as such by the 
very enemies, 


Brothers, unless the Arab nation rallies its top political, military and economic levels, 
levels which can legitimately speak and act on behalf of the Arab nation, whether this 
right is assumed or granted by delegations, while observing the historic national and 
religious responsibility for the present and future of the nation, I repeat, unless the 
nation rallies to face this threat immediately and courageously, this situation could 
become really grave because the events could follow a different trend such as: 


1--Addressing the Arab masses directly for them to defend their right to a dignified life 
on their land, should the officials fail to face the threats facing these masses and 
jeopardizing their future, 


2--Uprisings and revolts are a national reaction in defence of the masses! survival and 
dignity and in revenge for the martyrs of the glorious past, 


3--Resorting to defensive, imperative, instinctive and individualistic actions by all 
Arabs, with all those who believe. The threats of the enemies and that of the allies 
are both dangerous and evil. But this is the least harmful. This is clear and urgent, 
There is no justification whatsoever for any Arab state after this hlack day, the 26th 
of January [as received] 1980 to keep Egyptian embassies which we know so far are still 
keeping all their staff in well-known Arab capitals, with only the ambassadors missing. 
The same applies to the airlines which are serving the (Israelis) from Tel Aviv to Cairo 
and still flying to Arab capitals, 


Moreover, the general andprivate investments have now been integrated in Egyptian banks 
with the enemy's financial investments, an integration achieved with Arab funds which 
were not available to the enemy until the Arab investments were merged with the enemy's 
in Egyptian banks. The same applies to tourism which has not hitherto stopped between 
well-known Arab countries and Egypt.

The mixing of Arab tourists with the (Israelis) in Egypt, in their entertainment places, 
in Egyptian hotels and in the means of transport which will inevitably carry the 
(Israelis) with the Arabs, is in itself a break of the psychological barrier, which 
As-Sadat promised you to remove. 


So. Is that the end of us? At the end, despite the acute harm caused by the relinquish- 
ment of the Arab nation's rights, despite the potential dangers against the future of 
this nation which the enemies are plotting against, despite the huge losses suffered and 
the great victories won by the enemy and in spite of the humiliation which has hurt the 
Arab nation and its holy shrines, despite all this, a mighty nation is one which gains 
strength from the acuteness of its suffering. From the challenges of defeats it wrenches 
victories, Allah is greater than the great power which has created (Israel). And Allah 
is mightier than the mightiest force in the world today. 


Immortality to the Arab nation and its martyrs in Palestine.
