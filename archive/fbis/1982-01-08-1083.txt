LIBYA

OFFICIALS MEET WITH IRANIAN DELEGATION 6 JAN 


LD080956 Tehran IRNA in English 0916 GMT 8 Jan 82 


[Text] Tehran, 7 Jan (IRNA) -- The Iranian oil delegation currently visiting Libya met 
and conferred with the Libyan petroleum minister, 'Abd as-Salam Az-Zaq'ar, on Wednesday 
[6 Jan]. During this meeting the Libyan minister thanked the Islamic Republic for dis- 
patching the Iranian oil expert group while Iran was itself involved in the war imposed 
by Saddam. 


Also different aspects concerning mutual co-operations were discussed when the delegation 
met and conferredwiththe Libyan minister of heavy industries. 


V. 8 Jan 82 Q 2 NORTH AFRICA 


It should be recalled that following the departure of the U.S. oil group from Libya, a 
ten-man oil delegation from the Islamic Republic of Iran was dispatched to Libya on 
Saturday, Jan 2. This delegation consisted of Iranian oil experts.

JANA REPORTS SUDANESE FRONT'S APPEAL TO MASSES 


LD071502 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1330 GMT 7 Jan 82 


[Text] The Sudanese Socialist Popular Front [Al-Jabhah ash-Sha'biyah al-Ishtirakiyah 
as-Sudaniyah] has issued a statement calling on the honorable men and officers in the 
Sudanese Armed Forces and the police to consolidate themselves with the popular masses in 
one front, with the aim of destroying the regime of terrorism, domination and treason 
and establishing the authority of the masses. 


In its statement, the front asked the masses of Sudanese people to close their ranks at 
universities, schools, farms and production centers, and set up revolutionary bodies and 
popular committees in order to topple the agent regime in Sudan with a new revolutionary 
will, epitomizing with their spirit of rejection and defiance the inevitable fact that 
the only revolutionary change is that which stemsfrom a popular base. 


In its statement, the front creeted all progressive and revolutionary forces which stand 

on the side of the Arab people in Sudan in their just struggle for freedom, socialism 

and unity, foremost of these forces being the people of the Jamahiriyah and the 

of their revolution, revolutionary struggler Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, who is the defender 
of freedom and the popular masses. 


The front said that the masses of our people in Sudan, who are struggling against the 
fascist regime in Sudan and its repressive practices, believe that the only revolutionary 
change is that which stems from the popular-mass base and that the true change which heralds 
high hopes and the elevation of the popular struggle is that whichinvolves the establishment 
by the masses of their popular authority, the authority to topple the reactionary 

bourgeois agent regime in Sudan. This, the front explained, can only be achieved through 
changing the Sudanese trade unions into labor trade unionist councils [majalis naqabiyah 
‘ummaliyah] practicing direct democracy in all economic institutions and establishments, 
coupled with the occupation of these institutions and establishments, assuming their 
ownership and running them by themselves through people's production committees. 


The statement of the Sudanese Socialist Popular Front pointed out that the current stage 

in the Arab homeland, the African Continent and the Third World is one of violent 

political upsurges and armed revolutions. It said that as they wage their battle against 
the agent regime, which was openly and in the manner of agentry embraced programs 

of Zionist, imperialist and reactionary interests, our masses should close their ranks 

not only to defend themselves but also to wage an armed liberation war for the establishment 
of the people's authority.
