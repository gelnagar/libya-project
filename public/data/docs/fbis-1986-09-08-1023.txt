LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Denies Link to Karachi Hijack Attempt 
NC060557 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0500 GMT 
6 Sep 86 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution met with Indian Prime 
Minister Rajiv Gandhi and Pakistani President Ziaul Haq in 
Harare yesterday. During his meeting with the two officials, the 
brother leader of the revolution refuted the tendentious allega- 
tions of U.S. imperialism which try to link the Great Jamahiriyah 
to the hijacked Pan Am aircraft in Karachi. 


Official Blames CIA, Mosad 
JN060628 Damascus Domestic Service in Arabic 
0415 GMT 6 Sep 86 


[Text] Yesterday evening the Pakistani security forces launched 
an attack on the hijackers of the U.S. Pan American airliner 
which was hijacked at Karachi airport yesterday morning. News 
agencies said fire was exchanged between the hijackers and the 
security forces during the attack. Reuter cited eyewitnesses as 
saying that the clash led to the deaths of about 15 and the 
wounding of scores of passengers as well as the deaths of 2 
hijackers. The agency added that at least 30 ambulances rushed 
to the scene and began moving casualties. 


The Libyan Relations Bureau in Damascus has vehemently 
denied any Libyan connection whatsoever with the hijacking of 
the U.S. airliner and said: We are surprised that the name of 
the Libyan Jamahiriyah has been linked to this case. In a 
statement to SANA, the bureau's charge d'affaires in Damascus 
said that the reports carried by news agencies are clear and 
cannot deceive anyone, indicating that the Jamahiriyah is the 
first victim of terrorism. Therefore, he added, Libya is against 
terrorism and against the killing of innocent people and 
defenseless elderly people, women, and children aywhere in the 
world. 


Concluding, the Libyan official said: The coincidence of the 
hijacking of the U.S. civilian plane with the rabid campaign led 
by Reagan’s administration against the Jamahiriyah people 
diplomatically, militarily, and economically emphasizes that the 
incident was fabricated by the U.S. Central Intelligence Agency 
and its protege the Mosad and that it was aimed at justifying a 
new U.S. aggression against the Libyan Jamahiriyah. He drew 
the attention of world public opinion to the seriousness of this 
hostile U.S. policy and to the clear attempts to justify it.
