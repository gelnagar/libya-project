"Closed Meeting’ Held 


JN150645 Damascus Domestic Service in Arabic 0415 GMT 15 May 82 


[Text] Tripoli — Presidents Hafiz al-Asad and Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi held a closed 
meeting at 2030 yesterday. The meeting lasted until 2100. They later were joined by 
"Abd al-Halim Khaddam, deputy prime minister and foreign minister; Faruq ash-Shar', 
minister of state for foreign affairs; Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud and ‘Abd al-'‘Ati 
al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the people's committee for foreign liaison. 


Discussion during the meeting dealt with the regional situation in light of the 
imperialist and Zionist schemes hostile to the Arab nation and the attempts to Arabize 
the Camp David conspiracy. They also discussed efforts to generalize the Camp David 
conspiracy in the Arab arena through dragging other Arab parties into it and the 
resumption of relations with the Egyptian regime. 


Stress was put on the need for the Arab countries to remain committed to the ninth Arab 
summit conference resolutions and to consider the resumption of relations with the 
Egyptian regime, which is chained by the Camp David shackles and the capitulatory peace 
treaty with the Zionist enemy, as a violation of the Arab summit conferences' resolu- 
tions and some form of recognition of the racist Zionist entity. 


The two sides stressed their determination to confront imperialist-Zionist schemes and 
any Arab deviation from pan-Arab commitments.

Tripoli Details Talks 


LD151940 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 15 May 82 


[Excerpts] Syrian President Hafiz al-Asad left the jamahiriyah this afternoon. 


Talks which lasted for many hours were held throughout the Syrian president's stay in the 
jamahiriyah. The talks were attended on the Libyan side by Staff Maj Abd as-Salam Ahmad 
Jallud; Brigadier Mustafa al-Kharrubi, chief of staff of the armed forces, and ‘Abd al-‘Ati 
al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the general people's committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison. On the Syrian side were ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam, deputy prime minister, and Faruq 
ash-Shar', minister of state for foreign affairs. 


All aspects of the situation in the Arab region were debated, particularly the Arabization 
of the stable David agreements and the attempts aimed at trapping and involving a number 
of Arab states in stable David under the slogan that Egypt had retrieved the Sinai. This 
was an attempt by the reactionary states to mislead Arab public opinion and to cover the 
price which Egypt had paid in return for the Sinai -- the wager which Egypt made with the 
Zionist enemy for normalizing relations, for giving up its Arab and international commit- 
ments and for forfeiting the Palestinian issue. Instead of putting pressure on Egypt to 
return to the Arabs, certain Arab reactionary regimes began to endorse Egypt's actions of 
recognizing the enemy and of selling the Palestinian issue in an attempt to pressure the 
Arab rejectionist forces to be in the orbit of the capitulatory solutions. 


At these meetings, they also asserted that certain Arab states' rapprochement with Egypt 
is reneging on the resolutions of the Baghdad summit. They considered that reconciliation 
with Egypt is an attempt by the reactionary regimes to lessen the pressure on Egypt and to 
get rid of the consequences of stable David, consequences which are represented in the 
relationship between Egypt and the Zionist enemy. 


Within this framework, both sides warn the Arab countries persisting on this already 
planned course that seeks to realize strategic harmony between Israel, the United States 
and Arab reaction and against the Palestinian cause and the higher interests of the Arab 
nation that these attempts taking place conflict with the resolutions of the Baghdad 
summit. The two sides call on the Arab nation to adhere to the Baghdad resolutions. 


The two sides also studied the attempts seeking to Arabize the war which the Iraqi regime 
has waged against the Islamic revolution in Iran in implementation of the imperialist, 
Zionist and reactionary plans to bring the downfall of the Islamic revolution in Iran, the 
revolution which has been in cohesion with the Arabs, and to liberate Palestine. This 

war is destroying Iraq's capabilities and is removing it from the area of Arab-Zionist 
struggle. Therefore, the two sides agreed to resist this plan which is aimed at covering 
up the pan-Islamic crime committed by the Iraqi regime. 


The Arab masses cannot be deceived by such attempts, attempts aimed at making it an 
Arab-Iranian war. 


The two sides affirmed the need to warn the Arab masses of the seriousness of this plan 
and the seriousness of this war. The pressing need to convene the next pan-Arab summit 
of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front also was examined. Contacts are now being 
made with the other front members to set the date of the meeting. The two sides also 
agreed to implement the resolutions of the popular congresses and the political leader- 
ship in Syria with regard to unity between the two countries.

PRC ENVOY ARRIVES, SPEAKS ON RELATIONS 


LD161404 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 16 May 82 


[Text] The secretary of the people's committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison met this morning with Xuan [as heard], personal envoy of the PRC Government. 
Discussions during the meeting covered ways and means to consolidate and develop rela- 
tions in various spheres and to exchange views on international issues of joint interest. 


The Chinese Government envoy and members of his delegation arrived in Tripoli early this 
morning on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. He made a state- 
ment to a JANA correspondent, in which he said the visit comes with a view to enhancing 
cooperation between our two countries. The development of relations between us serves 
not only the interests of our two peoples but also world peace. The Libyan people are 
heroic, known for their struggle against challenges. He added that the jamahiriyah has 
contributed to the struggle of the Palestinian people for the recovery of their occupied 
territory. The jamahiriyah and China are both facing challenges from the big world 
powers. They also are facing threats and they are fighting side by side to safeguard 
their dignity and national sovereignty. For Libya to be strong and respected under the 
leadership of Colonel al-Qadhdhafi serves not only the Libyan people but also the 
interests of the Chinese people.
