LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES POLISARIO, SDAR LEADERS 
LD160618 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 2315 GMT 15 Aug 79 LD 


{Text] The brother leader of the revolution had talks with brothers Mohamed Tabdul Aziz, 
secretary general of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Saguia el Hamra and Rio de 
Oro [POLISARIO], and Mohaned Ould Lamine, Saharan Democratic Arab Republic prime minister, 
on recent developments in the Sahara following the signing of the peace treaty between 
Mauritania and the POLISARIO. This took place when the leader met the two Saharan leaders. 


TALKS WITH CENTRAL AFRICAN EMPIRE BEGIN 15 AUG 
LD151426 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1230 GMT 15 Aug 79 LD 


[Text] Talks between the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the Central 
African Empire opened this morning at the Communications and Maritime Transport Secre- 
tariat in Tripoli. They were attended by ‘Eng Nuri al-Fayturi aleMadani, secretary of 
the General People's Committee for Communications and Maritime Transport, and Jose K. 
Maythongol, minister of transport and commerce in the Central African Empire. They 
were attended also by the general secretary of the secretariat and the ambassador of 
the Central African Empire to the Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


The talks deal with developing cooperation between the Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the 
Central African Empire in the sphere of transport and communications. A technical 
committee in the sphere of civil aviation has been set up to work out the basis for 
cooperation in this sphere.
