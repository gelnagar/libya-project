LIBYA

Beirut Explosions ‘Direct Answer’ to U.S Policy 
LD231606 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
23 Oct 83 


[Text] Early this morning the headquarters of the U.S. military 
leadership in Beirut was destroyed by two cars full of explosives. 
The ecight-story headquarters was completely destroyed and the 
windows of the neighboring buildings were smashed. The build- 
ing contained at least 300 persons when the explosion took place. 
Helicopters from the aircraft carrier Eisenhower hurried to the 
site of the explosion to participate in the rescue oerations. Since 
the beginning of the rescue operations, the number of the corpses 
to be found continues to increase. More than 120 US. corpses 
have been found and more than 20 French corpses. 


This incident is the second of its kind against the illegal military 
presence of the U.S. forces in Lebanon which raided the positions 
of the Islamic and patriotic forces in Lebanon a few days ago 
using land, naval, and air arms. A few months ago, the US. 
Embassy was exposed to a similar incident in Beirut. It seems 
that the U.S. estimates of the situation in Lebanon ignore several 
factors and criteria which contribute to the Lebanese crisis, 
especially the feelings and dignity of the Lebanese in their 
rejection of all forms of U.S., Zionist, and European foreign 
intervention — especially if this intervention is in favor of a side 
which is not loyal to the Arab Lebanon
