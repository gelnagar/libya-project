LIBYA

Military Leaders Meet With Palestinian Officials 
LDO11016 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT | Jan 84 


[Text] Tripoli, | Jan (JANA) — Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam 
Ahmad Jallud and Brigadier General Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, 
commander in chief of the armed forces, last night had a meeting 
with Brothers Abu Salih, member of the commanding committee 
of the new revolutionary movement of Fatah, and Ahmad Jibril, 
general secretary of the Popular Front for the Liberation of 
Palestine General Command, who arrived in Tripoli at midday. 


JANA Editor Views Egypt’s Role in Arab World 
LD311522 Tripoli JANA in English 1432 GMT 31 Dec 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Thani 26, December 31, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY The political editor of the JAMAHIR- 
IYAH NEWS AGENCY released the following commentary on 
current contacts between some Arab regimes and the Egyptian 
regime which is committed to the treacherous Camp David 
accords: 


Official contacts between some Arab countries and the Egyptian 
regime, it should be pointed out, are an (?insult) to the Arab 
masses. The reason behind this is the Egyptian regime’s illegit- 
imate relationship with the Zionist enemy. Furthermore, these 
contacts are a blatant violation of the resolutions of the Baghdad 
summit, i.e. boycotting Egypt. 


Looking at the question from a different perception we find that 
for some Arab countries signing the Baghdad agreement was a 
tactical step. These regimes, no matter what excuses are given 
now, have never been genuine with regard to the Arab folk policy. 


Nevertheless, their justifications should not mislead us. Such 
contacts mean an implicit recognition of the expansionist enemy. 
Not a single Arab would forget that the bloody Zionist flag is 
being hoisted in the sky of Cairo. 


The present regime in Egypt has gone many steps further than 
what As-Sadat did. While the cursed As-Sadat went to the 
enemy,the present regime brought them to Cairo. Egyptian [word 
indistinct] has been surrendered to the forces of NATO. 
Presidential places are opened to Zionist terrorists. Markets are 
openly brought in Egyptian markets. [as received] Israeli tourists 
are seen in the streets of towns. 


The regime of Cairo has not committed itself even to a verbal 
promise to abrogate this treacherous agreement. On the contrary, 
it is defending it and dragging new policies to accept its con- 
ditions. Egyptians officials supported the U.S.-Zionist strategic 
alliance accords. A few days ago they met with ‘Arafat as the 
new link in the building of submission. 


If these Arab heads accept the conditions of Zion, the Arab 
masses will not. The year 1984, we have no doubt, shall be the 
year of resolution in the Arab world. A storm will break out 
putting down all heads of capitulation
