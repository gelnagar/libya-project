SAHARAN COMMUNIQUE ON MOROCCAN GENERAL'S DEATH 


LD271502 Tripoli JANA in English 1424 GMT 27 Jan 83 


[Text] Algiers, Rabi Thani 12, Jan 27, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The Saharan Information 
Ministry issued a communique yesterday saying that General Ahmed Dlimi, commander-in- 
chief of the Moroccan forces, was killed in a carefully planned assassination plot.
