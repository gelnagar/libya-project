[Text] Tripoli, 3 Feb 79, JANA--Following the visit of brother 'Abd al-Fattah Isma'il, 
chairman of the Presidium of the PDRY People's Supreme Council, to the Jamahiriyah, a 
joint communique was published, whose text is as follows: 


At the invitation of Col Mu'ammar al-Fattah Isma'il, general seoretary of the Yemeni 
Socialist party, and chairman of the Presidium of the PDRY People's Supreme Council, 
paid a state visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiryah from 27 Jamary 
to 3 February 1979. 


Brother 'Abd al-Fattah Isma'il and the accompanying delegation were accorded a warm 
welcome, expressing the deep ties linking the two fraternal peoples of the Jamahiriyah 
and the PDRY. A meeting took place between brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, and 
brother 'Abd al-Fattah Isma'il, during which there was a review of fateful Arab issues, 
the current international situation and matters of interest to both countries. In an 
atmosphere of fraternity and understanding the Libyan and PDRY sides held a series of 
meetings, during which they discussed bilateral issues and stressed with satisfaction 
the continuous growth of the strong cooperation between the two fraternal countries. 
The two sides exchanged views and reviewed the experiences of the two revolutions 
regarding the revolutionary progressive changes being conducted in both countries in 
realization of the interest of the [word indistinot] masses. Both sides stressed their 
respective determination to further develop this cooperation between them in a deeper 
and more comprehensive manner, which will benefit the two fraternal peoples and increase 
their influence in the front of steadfastness and confrontation, and in the struggle 
being waged against the Zionists, the colonialist forces and their aggressive schemes 
in the Arab homeland.

{"Text" of decision No 2 for 1979 on reorganizing the Libyan governmental secretariat: 
issued on 1 February 1979 by the Libyan General Secretariat of the General People's Congress) 


(Text ] The General Secretariat of the General People's Congress, implementing the decisions 
of the third ordinary session of the people's congresses which met from 21 October 1978 to 
20 November 1978, and implementing the decisions drafted by the General People's Congress in 
its fourth session for 1978 which set up general people's committees in the municipalities. 
has drawn up the following decision: 


Article I 


The Secretariats in the Socialist People's Arab Libyan Jamahiriyah are to be reorganized in 
the following manner: 


1--The Secretariat of Justice 

2--The Secretariat of Health 

3-The Secretariat of Petroleum 

4.~The Secretariat of Agrarian Reform and Land Reclamation 
5--The Secretariat of Housing 

6--The Secretariat of Economy 

7--The Secretariat of the Treasury 

8--The Secretariat of Heavy Industries 

9--The Secretariat of Light Industries 

10--The Secretariat of Education 

1l--The Secretariat of Information 

12--The Secretariat of Electricity 

13--The Secretariat of Communications and Maritime Tramsport 
14--The Secretariat of Municipalities 

15--The Secretariat of Jamahiriyah Sport 

16--The Foreign Secretariat 

17--The Secretariat of Planning 

18--The Public [al-ammah] Service Secretariat 

19--The Secretariat of Social Security. 


V. 6 Feb 79 NORTH AFRICA 


Article II 

The secretariats are to be rearranged, their designations outlined, and public estab- 
lishments and departments set up, abolished and transfered from one secretariat to 
another by decisions of the General People's Committee without being restricted to the 
provisions of the laws in force, 


Article III 


The existing secretariats, not mentioned in Article I of this decision, on the issuing 
of this decision are to be merged with the secretariats as follows: 


A--The Secretariat of the Interior with the Secretariat of Justice; 


B--The Secretariats of Agriculture and Dams and Water Resources with the Secretariat 
of Agrarian Reform and Land Reclamation; 


C--<The Secretariats of Trade, Nutrition and Marine Wealth with the Secretariat of 
Economy ; 


D--The Secretariat of Youth with the Secretariat of Jamahiriyah Sport; 


E--The Secretariat of Social Affairs and Social Security with the Secretariat of Social 
Security; 


F--The Secretariat of Industry with the Secretariat of Light Industries; 
G--The Secretariat of Labor and Civil Service with the Secretariat of Public Service. 
Article IV 


All public authorities and establishments with the exception of the following are to 
be abolished: 


The universities and institutes, the municipalities, the central apparatus for general 
administration control, the auditing office, the Jamahiriyah security authority, Libya's 
central bank, the atomic energy establishment, the agricultural bank, the industrial 

and real estate bank, the national petroleum establishment. 


Article V 

Until the decisions stipulated in the next article are issued, the present secretaries 
will continue to run the secretariats given in Article III, their public authorities and 
establishments according to the statutes and laws in force, 

Article VI 

The General Public Committee is to issue the necessary decision for the transfer of 


employees which will result from the provisions of this decision, The employees are to 
keep the same grade, seniority and salaries they now hold, 


V. 6 Feb 79 NORTH AFRICA 


Article VII 


This decision is to be published in the official gazette and to come into force from 
the day it is issued.

Secretariat of Interior Abolished 
LD051104 Tripoli JANA in English 1448 GMT 4 Feb 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 4 February, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Secretariat of the Interior 
has been abolished in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahirlyah after the role of 
this secretariat ceased to exist by the achievement of the people's authority and the 
beginning of the era of the masses, 


It is known that the basic role undertaken by interior ministries in existing regimes 

is protecting rulers from revolutions by their people, spying on the people and striking 
the counter movements resisting their regimes, so these rulers have absolute control of 
power and wealth over the rest of the people through the Ministry of the Interior. 


The abolition of the Interior Secretariat in the Jamahiriyah confirms the well-established 
people's authority so that its protection is no longer a monopoly of a secretariat or 
particular departments but apopular responsibility that should be undertaken by every 
citizen. The responsibility of people's security is therefore the responsibility of the 
people's committees, in the same way as all other sectors are being run by people at 

all levels of planning, control and implementation.
