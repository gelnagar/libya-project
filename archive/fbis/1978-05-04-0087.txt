The two sides condemn the cooperation between the racist regimes in South Africa and 
occupied Palestine and consider this coope i in opposition to human values and the 
right of man to freedom. 


The two sides express their great satisfaction with and total support for the resolutions 
of the 30th session of the ministerial council of the African foreign ministers, which

The two sides condemn imperialist attempts to create pacts and military blocs between 
imperialism and the states of the continent, for this endangers the security and safety

The two sides call on African states not to join such military blocs, in accordance with 
the principles of nonalinement. 


The two sides strongly condemn imperialist interference in the affairs of the African 
continent, aimed at internationalizing African disagreements and problems so as to serve as 
an outlet for imperialism and as an excuse for its intervention, thus returning to the de-

the OAU to find the right solutions to their problems, 


The two sides also stress the exceptional ability of the African people to unite against 
the attempts to divide them, and they call on progressive forces to work to create a 
united front to confront the foreign forces intervening in Africa. 


In this connection they bless the step taken by the SPLAJ, Sudan and Niger to make a 
success of the reconciliation meeting among the brothers in Chad, which were crowned by 
the Sabha conference on 21 February 1978 and the meetings of all parties on 23 and 24 
March 1978 in Sabha and Benghazi. 


In regard to the Arab sphere, the two sides call for Arab and African cohesion and unity. 
They stress the importance of the joint role played by the Arab-African group for world 
cultural and economic progress to liberate and free it from imperialist hegemony and mono- 
polies, Joint cooperatian between these two groups is beneficial to the peoples of the 
Third World in general, and through historic and geographical relations the Arab and 
African groups will be able to play an effective and influential role in the world arena 
and in various spheres. 


When they studied what befell the Palestinian issue as a result of the arrogance and 
pomposity of the racist Zionists, the two sides confirmed their determination and insis- 
tence on backing and supporting the Palestinian people to regain their land and all their 
usurped rights by escalating the legitimate struggle which this people is waging. 


In this regard, the two sides condemn every initiative aimed at banishing the Palestinian 
people from handling their cause, for no one has the right to handle it in the name of the 
Palestinians. 


Regarding the world sphere, while the two sides stress their commitment to the charter and 
principles of the United Nations, they consider it necessary to amend the charter of the 
organization so that it wouldrealize justice and equality among all nations, small and big, 
and demand the abolition of the "veto." They also call for finding a new world economic 
system which would recognize the right of participation of the countries of the Third 
World on the basis of justice and equality.

TURKISH=LIBYAN AGREEMENTS, PROTOCOL SIGNED IN TRIPOLI 


TAO217':2¥ Ankara Domestic Service in Turkish 1600 GMT 2 May 78 TA 


[Text] A commercial and financial cooperation agreement envisaging Libyan loans to 
Turkey and the increase of Turkish exports to Libya was signed in Tripoli today. The 
agreement was signed on behalf of Turkey by Minister of State Hikmet Cetin, who had gone 
to Libya for bilateral talks, and on behalf of Libya by Secretary of Housing Muhammad 
al-Manqush, 


RT correspondent Omer Dilek reports that according to the agreement, Turkey is to receive 
a loan of $100 million from Libya. Turkey's export volume to Libya will be increased to 
$85 million annually. According to the agreement, Turkey will export 230,000 tons of 

wheat, 15,000 tons of olive oil, 10,000 tons of tomato sauce and other agricultural products 
to Libya this year, The agreement will be valid for 5 years. 


A banking agreement and a protocol envisaging cooperation in the agricultural and industrial 
fields were also signed between Turkey and Libya in Tripoli today. According to the 
banking agreement, the Libyan Arab Foreign Bank will open an account with the aim of speed- 
ily transferring to Turkey the savings of Turkish workers employed in Libya. Through this 
account, Turkish workers will be able to send foreign currency to Turkey. According to 

the protocol, it was decided to form joint companies in the fields of wheat, sugar and 
animal fodder in Turkey and in the field of [word indistinct] in Libya. According to the 
protocol, a joint Turkish-Libyan construction and renovation firm to be set up in Libya 
will construct a dockyard in Libya. The protocol also envisages ship services between 
Tripoli, the capital of Libya, and Izmir to transport passengers, equipment and goods, 

ne ship services envisaged to be established soon will also have refrigerated depots. 


Meanwhile, Cetin today visited Libyan Prime Minister [title as heard] Abd as-Salam Jallud 
and gave him a message from Prime Minister Bulent Ecevit. Cetin will leave Tripoli for 
Sofia this evening to participate in Ecevit's official visit to Bulgaria.
