LIBYA

Libya Seeks Exocets for Use Against U.S. Ships 
NC301545 (Clandestine) Radio Free Lebanon in Arabic 
1445 GMT 30 Apr 87 


[Text] The French magazine L'Express cites sources in NATO 
intelligence as saying that Libyan missions in Europe have been 
instructed to buy Exocet missiles for use against U.S. ships in the 
Mediterranean.
