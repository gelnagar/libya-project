LIBYA

JANA on ‘Lies’ About Role of Multinational Force 
LD1I91504 Tripoli JANA in English 1431 GMT 19 Sep 83 


[Text] Beirut, Dhulhijah 12, Sept 19, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The leader of the Lebanese fascist-Phalangist 
league Amin al- Jumayyil has issued blatant lies regarding the 
role of the so-called multinational force in Lebanon. He also 
showed that the Phalangist understanding of relations with the 
crusading force is diametrically opposed to the normal perception 
of this role by public opinion. This Phalangist perception is based 
on the desire to escalate war against the Muslims and to 
transform Lebanon into a bridgehead for Zionist aggression. 
Al-Jumayyil’s desire has always been the annihilation of all 
Muslims in Lebanon and to direct a crusading blow at Syria and 
all Muslims 


Al-Jumayyil, true to the spirit of all criminal fascists, only sees 
what he wants to see. He has thus steadily and cleverly avoided 
reference to the bombardment by the U.S. Sixth Fleet of posi- 
tions held by Muslim and patriotic forces. He has failed to 
mention the overflights of U.S. warplanes based on the aircraft 
carrier “Eisenhower” and the British jets that bombed peaceful 
Muslim inhabitants. He has chosen to turn a blind eye to the 
despicable role of the French forces in stoking the fire of the 
crusade in Chad and Lebanon. Al-Jumayyil has always been a 
liar, and the relationship between the multinational force and the 
crusade in Lebanon is both direct and fundamental 


JANA Says Fifth Plane Downed by Lebanon Front 
LD1I91922 Tripoli JANA in English 1841 GMT 19 Sep 83 


[Text] Damascus, Dhulhijah 12, Sept 19, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY JANA correspondent in Damascus has 
reported that the forces of the National Salvation Front shot 
down today another aircraft of the air force of the fascist Pha- 
langist government. So far five aircraft of the Lebanese Air Force 
were downed. JANA correspondent in Damascus said that a 
statement by the front is expected to be issued on the current 
fighting in Sug al-Gharb.
