LIBYA

LIBYAN ROLE IN PAKISTANI AIRLINER AFFAIR REPORTED 


JANA Chrono logy 
LD140952 Tripoli JANA in English 0843 GMT 14 Mar 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 14 Mar (JANA)--The Pakistani plane carrying detainees whose release has 
been demanded by the hijackers is due to arrive in Tripoli today, 14 March, Saturday. The 
Jamahiriyah has agreed to receive the plane for solely humanitarian reasons and out of 
concern for the lives ofthe innocent passengers. 


The Pakistani Government was in constant contact withthe Libyan Arab People's Bureau in 
Pakistan and the People's Committee of the Foreign Liaison Bureau to secure their coopera- 
tion in settling the problem of the hijacked plane. The Pakistani Government sent a 
memorandum to the Libyan Arab People's Bureau in Islamabad on the 2nd of March saying: 

The Pakistani authorities request the Jamahiriyah to endeavour for the safety of the 
passengers aboard the hijacked plane. 


On the 3rd of March, the Pakistani charge d'affaires, accompanied by the Pakistani aviation 
general director and the head of the Pakistani negotiating team, met the secretary of 

the Libyan Arab People's Bureau in Kabul and requested the prompt intervention of the 
Jamahiriyah for the release of the passengers. In his request, he asserted that the 
Jamahiriyah is a sister country of Pakistan and is led by the great Islamic leader, 
Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi who enjoys high esteem in the entire Islamic world. He added that 

the help rendered by the Jamahiriyah to Pakistan in this respect would have the most pro- 
found effect inthe whole world and particularly in Pakistan. The charge d'affaires asserted 


that he was entrusted by his government to submit this request. 


On the fourth of March, Pakistan expressed to the Libyan People's Bureau in Islamabad its 
appreciation for the Jamahiriyah's promise to put its endeavour for the settlement of the 
case of the Pakistani hijacked plane. fas received] 


On the 6th of the same, the Pakistani foreign office called the secretary of the Libvan 
People's Bureau in Islamabad together with ambassadors of France, Czechoslovakia, Iran, 
India, China, America and Turkey and informed them of the Pakistani Government's request 
and hence sought their help in solving the problem of the hijacked plane with view to the 
rescue of the lives of the passengers.

The Pakistani Foreign Office expressed its appreciation and gratitude to the help rendered 
by the Jamahiriyah in this respect especially the stand made by the secretary of the 
Libyan People's Bureau in Kabul who offered himself as a hostage in exchange for the 
release of the passengers, and which was rejected by the hijackers. In the same meeting, 
the Pakistani Foreign Department requested more efforts from the Jamahiriyah in solving 
the problem of the hijacked plane and rescuing the lives of its passengers. 


On the 8th of March, the American foreign department [as received] contacted the Libyan 
Arab People's Bureau in Washington, and requested the possibility of the Jamahiriyah's 
intervention to secure the release of the passengers of the hijacked plane. The American 
foreign department expressed the hope that the Pakistani plane will be allowed to land in 
Libya and that efforts would be made for the release of its passengers a number of whom 
are American. 


The Pakistani ambassador to the Jamahiriyah requested from the secretary of the People's 
Committee for Foreign Liaison to have the Jamahiriyah exert its efforts and endeavours for 
the release of the passengers of the hijacked Pakistani plane. 


This humanitarian stand by the Jamahiriyah which aims at putting an end to the tragedy 
of the hijacked plane, confirms its humanitarian policy which opposes terrorism and the 
exposure of innocent lives to danger. 


Refusal of Pakistani Prisoners 
NC141527 Paris AFP in English 1523 GMT 14 Mar 81 


[Text] Islamabad, 14 Mar (AFP)--The Libyan Government today announced it had refused to 
allow the Pakistani prisoners freed at the demand of hijackers to enter Libya, an official 
source said here. This last-minute about-turn put into question the fate of more than 100 
hostages being held aboard a Pakistan International Airl.aes plane in Damascus. An 
official spokesman here said the Libyan refusal, which came after an airliner carrying 54 
prisoners released from Pakistani jails left Aleppo in northern Syria and Tripoli, had 
“totally shocked the Pakistan Government". This meant that the plane would probably have 
to return to Syria, observers said. 


MINUTES OF LIBYA-INDIA COMMISSION MEETING SIGNED 


BK131544 Delhi ISI Diplomatic Information Service in English 1527 GMT 13 Mar 81 


[Text] The agreed minutes of the third session of the Indo-Libyan Joint Commission were 
signed on 2 March in Tripoli by Doctor Charanjit Chanana and Libyan Cochairman of the Joint 
Commission Jum'ah al-Arbash, minister of electricity. The agreed minutes laid stress 

on the active participation of India in many sections of the new Libyan 5-year plan 
starting from 1981. 


Special emphasis is laid on Indian participation in power generation and transmission 
lines, construction of railway lines, roads, airports and townships. Indian companies 
bidding for industrial units in the light as well as the heavy section, management and 

the setting up of agricultural farms in the desert, management by Indian companies of 
industrial and power units, the setting up of training centres in the industrial field and 
increasing the volume of Indo-Libyan trade. It is expected that during the current year 
the value of projects in Libya won by India companies will increase from $1.5 billion to 
$2.5 billion. Indo-Libyan trade is to be expanded and Air India start a service to 
Tripoli.

Doctor Charanjit Chanana called on Colonel al-Qadhdhafi on 2 March. He was taken by 

special plane to Sebha in the south of Libya where the Libyan leader was attending the 
fourth anniversary celebrations of the declaration of the peoples rule. During the 

meeting Colonel al-Qadhdhafi paid tribute to the distinguished leadership of Prime Minister 
Mrs Indira Gandhi and remarked that after An-Nasir she is the champion of the cause of the 
Third World. The Libyan leader also expressed satisfaction at the work done so far by 
Indian companies in Libya. Minister Chanana apprised him of the work done by the joint 
commission and his government and the Indian Government's determinaticn to further strength- 
en economic cooperation with Libya. 


During his stay Doctor Chanana also called on and had fruitful talks with the ministers of 
oil, heavy industry, light industry and planning besides having lengthy talks with the 
minister of electricity. Libya has agreed to give India 1 million tonnes of oil during 
1981 and in case the type of oil is not suitable for our refineries, to help us in third 
countries to get the end products which we required and for which we had proposed Libya to 
sell its lighter crude. 


LIBERIA'S DOE SENDS MESSAGE TO AL-QADHDHAFI 


LD150738 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 15 Mar 81 


[Text] The brother leader has received a written message from Samuel Doe, head of state 
and chairman of the People's Redemption Council of Liberia stating: Out of our strong 
desire to establish historic relations between our two countries, I convey to you, 
dear elder brother, on behalf of the Liberian people and myself, an invitation to share 
with us the celebration of the first anniversary of our popular revolution 


I affirm to you, elder brother, that your presence among us during our people's celebra- 
tion of this anniversary will have a great good effect on our people. Please accept this 
invitation from the Liberian people to share our celebrations with us. 


Please accept the Liberian people's most sincere appreciation of your great efforts to 
defend freedom in Africa, and to spread prosperity and peace in Africa.
