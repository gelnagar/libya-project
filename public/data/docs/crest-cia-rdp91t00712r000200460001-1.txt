Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery note on Libyan military equipment dispersal at garrisons near Tubruq (Tobruk) and Ras al-Hilal, areas far from Tripoli. It describes tank, artillery and rocket units dispersed at the SA-6 support facility, the Tubruq Military Facility West, and a facility labelled a terrorist training facility, possibly a reaction to the U.S. presence in the Mediterranean.
• Tubruq is approximately 70 NM west-northwest of the Libya-Egypt border.
• Ras al-Hilal is approximately 125 NM east of Benghazi and 200 NM west of the Libya-Egypt border.
• The Tubruq SA-6 facility had at least 30 medium tanks and at least 12 vehicles/trucks parked in front of sheds.
• Ras al-Hilal housed 18 RM-70 MRLs and about 45 support vehicles dispersed in a wooded area.
• Coordinates given: 32-04-47N 023-53-30E; 32-04-47N 023-52-31E; 32-55-02N 022-10-16E.
• 1983-11: Libyan military equipment dispersal observed at garrisons in the Tubruq and Ras al-Hilal areas, possibly a reaction to the U.S. presence in the Mediterranean.
