JANA REPORTS SPEAKERS, ACTIVITIES OF ARAB CONGRESS 


Jallud Speech on Chad 
LD161754 Tripoli JANA in English 1659 GMT 16 Jan 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 Jan (JANA)--Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud addressed the 2nd 

session of the Arab People's Congress (A.P.C.) stressing the outstanding concept of the 
A.P.C. which represents an introduction to a large scale awakening of the masses. Such 
action, he added, conforms with our ideological beliefs and our faith in the masses and 
the role of the Arab popular movement. He said: "If tragedy lies in the absence of the 
masses, the solution, therefore lies in their presence. Through their spontaneous action, 
the masses were the major weapons of "Abd an-Nasir when fighting reaction, colonialism 

and Zionism. We in Libya, as revolutionaries and believers in the masses and the people's 
authority, work towards restoring the masses’ role in the popular movement and giving 

it its ideological dimensions. 


The Arab masses should have a strategy and an ideology according to which they fight and 
struggle. The action should be of certain characteristics, indications and effects and 

not mere tactics and limited official movements, or a tool in the hands of a certain 

regime. Even though it encompasses the Arab progressive regime, the Steadfastness 

Front should not be linked to the masses’ movements and the Arab People's Congress (A.R.C.). 


The colonialist and fascist forces will conspire against their historic action because 
the success of the Arab masses to take over control of their own destiny and to take 
their responsibilities directly. 


"The A.P.C. should not turn into another picture of Arab solidarity but should mobilize 
the masses towards and overall popular uprising. Only then, the Arab nation starts its 
cultural revival." 


"We in the Jamahiriyah expect the A.P.C. and the Arab trade unions and vocational 
organization to play their role in the strategic counteroffensive against colonialist 
military bases." 


"France can not out-wit us and move her agents in Africa, so if she wants to talk to us, 
she should address us directly." 


"The fuss made of the Libyan presence in Chad is only a cover and a pretext for the French 
troops to go to Central Africa, Senegal, Gabon and Sudan to join the Egyptian forces 
there.

"When we went to Chad to help the legitimate government we prepared ourselves to face all 
possibilities and conspiracies." 


"We respect the will of the Chadian people and we do not, therefore, impose any sort of 
ties on them, but our intervention was according to an official request and a joint treaty. 
Consequently, the only side which has the right to terminate our help to Chad is the 
legitimate government there." 


"We are aiming to restore security and peace in Chad and Africa and hope the Chadian people 
will start rebuilding their country through the help of the neighboring African countries 
as well as the U.N. Organization." 


"What logic is that which prevents us from coming to the aid of a legimate government. I 
am surprised at that logic which bans any help to a legitimate government and allows in- 
vasion and occupation. As-Sadat has criticized France for its delays in intervening in 
Chad and also criticized U.S.A. for not intervening in Iran, while Egypt announced she 
will send troops to Somalia and France is still occupying part of the African Continent." 


"We can not accept the situation in which the Libyan people are attacked from Chad, and 
can not possibly accept imposing a regime on Chad, hostile to the Libyan people." 


“Any sabotage or aggression against the Chadian people coming from Chad's neighboring 
countries will be punished." 


"We have expected the thanks because we stopped the rebellion in Chad and put an end to 
the civil war there that may have spread throughout Africa." 


Speakers, Decisions 
LD161926 Tripoli JANA in English 1709 GMT 16 Jan 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 Jan (JANA)--The Arab People's Congress Permanent Secretariat submitted 
its annual report to its Secretary General Mr. Omar al-Hamidi on the organization's 
activities, yesterday. The report stressed the necessity for national struggle and 
solidarity among Arab radical forces as well as unifying different factions of the Arab 
liberation movement and for strengthening ties with world liberation, democratic and so- 
cialist forces. The report also stated the Secretariat's activities on the Palestine 
cause, as well as its activities on Lebanon, Egypt and Arab Gulf and Jazirah. 


At this session, the head of South Yemen delegation delivered a speech on behalf of President 
‘Ali Nasir, in which he said that the holding of this session at this critical 

and complex stage of the Arab situation, due to the vicious imperialist attack on the 

region, is a necessity and of vital importance. He pointed out that the Arab region became 

a first priority in the colonialist countries’ plans because of the formers’ oil resources. 
They line up these fleets in the Arabian Sea, the Gulf, and the Mediterranian Sea, set up 
their military bases in Oman, Egypt and Somalia and place their rapid deployment forces 

on full alert. 


He also accused the imperialist forces of creating obstacles for North and South Yemen, 
to slow the process of the two countries’ unity. 


Mr. Abu 'Ali Mustafa, deputy-secretary general of the Popular Front for the Liberation 
of Palestine P.F.L.P., delivered a speech in which he hailed the Libyan people's pro- 
gressive stands against imperialism and its allies in the region, and their support of 
the Palestinian revolution.

The holding of this session, the PFLP leader said, coincides with the recent escalation 
of the Palestinian masses’ resistance against the (Israeli) oppressive measures. 


The secretary~general of the Lebanese Christian-democratic organization greeted the 
Syrian-Libyan merger and called upon the Lebanese Arab masses to affiliate to it to 
further strengthen the stand against imperialist schemes. He also hailed the transforma- 
tion of the Libyan émbassies abroad into people's bureaus to by-pass the traditional 
diplomatic relations. 


The representative of the African National Congress delivered a speech on behalf of the 
African delegations in which he condemned the colonialist plots against Africa. He also 
expressed his relief at the ending of the civil war in Chad and affirmed that Chad has 
become a sovereign independent country. He pointed out that the colonialist countries 
are trying to regain control over some of the Third-World countries especially in Africa 
to exploit the natural resources of these countries and to hit the national liberation 
movements in Palestine, Namibia, South Africa, Western Sahara, El Salvador and Bolivia. 
At the end of his speech, he called for wiping out the racist regimes in South Africa 
and occupied Palestine. 


The representatives of the Italian Communist Party delivered his party's message in which 
he pledged his unconditional support for the Arab nation's control over the cause of 
Palestine. He also affirmed the support of the Italian progressive forces for the 
Palestinian cause, and stressed the importance of solidarity among the world progressive 
forces especially in the Mediterranian region to combat the colonialist presence in the 
region which takes the form of military bases. 


The congress approved the annual report submitted by the Permanent Secretariat. 


Four committees were set up, one is the Political Committee, to discuss the political 
report and the final statement, another in the Organizational Committee to draft the 
organizations bye-laws and the budget for the people's struggle fund, the third is the 
Charter Committee and the fourth is a committee responsible for suggestion about the 
activities of the 1981. 


DAILY ATTACKS FRENCH, AMERICAN AFRICAN POLICIES 


LD162022 Tripoli JANA in English 1855 GMT 16 Jan 81 


(Text) Tripoli, 16 Jan (JANA)--Under the heading ‘Another Harlot Called France’ the 
Jamahiriyah daily published in Tripoli said that France the younger sister to American 
imperialism, plays its imperialistic role following the distribution of world tasks 

among the United States, being the imperialist leader, and its junior partners, to 
confront the dangers of a world revolution and to alleviate the budget of right wing coups 
planned by the United States. 


It added that France is attempting to turn back the wheels of history, in an attempt to 
wrest the historical victory achieved by the Chadian people, after a hemorrhage which 
lasted 20 years, by using its lackeys in Central Africa, as a bridgehead to invade the 
liberated and unified Chad, thus exposing the interests of the French people to danger. 
The newspaper referred to the French military buildup and massing of troops in Central 
Africa in order to bolster her colonialist role in the heart of Africa, which is hostile 
to the triumph of the Chadian people, and a threat to its security and unity.

It said France does not play this dirty role in Africa by alone [as received]. American 
and reactionary states stand behind her spearheaded by the lackey Egyptian regime in a 
desperate attempt to find a new solution for the severely deteriorating world capitalist 
crisis, by going back and resorting to forms of old imperialism, so that America could 
devote itself to confronting the world radical forces and explosive revolutionary areas 
in the third world which directly threaten vital American interests.

MORE EMBASSIES TRANSFORMED INTO PEOPLE'S BUREAUS 


Tehran Embassy 
LD081236 Tehran Domestic Service in Persian 1030 GMT 8 Jan 81 


[Text] According to the report of the Central News Bureau, the employees of the Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah Embassy in Tehran today held a ceremony changing that country's embassy 
into the People's Bureau of the Arab Jamahiriyah of the Libyan people. Mr Sa'd Mudbir, 
the country's ambassador in Tehran, was chosen as the secretary of the committee of the 
people's bureau. The employees also elected five members of the administrative committee 
of the people's bureau, three of whom were from among the employees. The other two 
members are outside the staff of the people's bureau in Tehran, At the moment they are 
in Libya, but according to the popular decision they are dutybound to accept this 

post. 


According to our correspondent's report, these ceremonies were held at the premises of 

the Libyan People's Bureau in the presence of Iranian guests and correspondents, and 
began with the chanting of some verses from the glorious Koran. Then one of the people 

of Libya who has come to Tehran explained the way the Libyan People's Bureau will be run 
and the goals of the people's bureau, saying: According to the Koranic versewhich says 
"the affairs should be dealt with through consultation", today the Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
Embassy will be changed into a people's bureau. This decision arises from the fact that 
the people's bureau represents the people of Libya; its duty is to strengthen relations 
between the two nations of Libya and the Islamic Republic of Iran. The transformation of 
the embassy into a people's bureau expresses the wishes of the people of Libya to establish 
friendly relations with the people of the world, He added: We are interested in the 
Islamic Republic of Iran and also call the rest of the people of the world to popular 
revolutions, while the Western propaganda organizations distort our work and abuse it. 

The work of this bureau is to establish relations between the Libyan nation and other 
nations through the nations. [as heard]

Canberra Embassy 
OW141421 Melbourne Overseas Service in English 1230 GMT 14 Jan 81 


[Text] Libya has recalled its diplomatic staff from Canberra and announced the closure 
of its embassy. The diplomats have been replaced by four elected representatives who 
have been established in what they call a Libyan People's Bureau. A spokesman for the 
bureau would continue to carry out the functions of an embassy but would not seek 
diplomatic status. Libya has over the past 15 months replaced many of its embassies 
throughout the world with people's bureaus.
