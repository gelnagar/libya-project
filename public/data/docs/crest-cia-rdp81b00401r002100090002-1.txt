Summary (AI): CIA talking points on Libya (9 November 1978) say Qadhafi's foreign behaviour has moderated out of expediency, citing Egyptian threat, discomfort with Soviet ties, US sanctions, and domestic insecurity after the 1975 Muhayshi coup attempt. Also includes an arms inventory table and notes on Algeria after Boumediene's illness.
• Soviet advisors in Libya reportedly rose from 700 to 1,200-1,500 between July 1976 and late 1978.
• Estimated value of Soviet-bloc weapons contracts with Libya, 1974-1978: $2.6 billion.
• Libyan inventory listed 700 Soviet, 800 Polish and 600 Czechoslovak medium tanks, and 205 Soviet and 110 French combat-capable aircraft.
• Libya's inventory included 39 Soviet SAM battalions and 8 French SAM batteries, 40 Soviet surface-to-surface missile launchers, and 3 Soviet submarines.
• Algerian succession: National Assembly president becomes chief of state for a maximum of 45 days, followed by a special party congress and presidential election.
• 1975: Unsuccessful coup attempt by Umar Muhayshi, after which Qadhafi became suspicious of the officer corps
