Summary (AI): A Wall Street Journal article (31 July 1980) reports that Billy Carter lied twice to Justice Department investigators about cash he received from Libya, and that he had registered as a foreign agent of Libya. It covers the related Senate inquiry, the sale of Boeing 727s and C-130 aircraft to Libya, and the Vesco allegations.
• Billy Carter said he received $220,000 in two payments from Libya, plus gifts valued at $2,850 and two free trips to Libya.
• The State Department blocked export of C-130 planes to Libya because of Libyan involvement.
• A Senate subcommittee was investigating whether Libya tried to influence U.S. policy through Billy Carter and others.
• The House Judiciary Committee was considering a resolution of inquiry into the Billy Carter affair.
• 1980-07-14: Billy Carter registered as a foreign agent of Libya
• 1978-09: Billy Carter's first trip to Libya, reported by the White House as described in State Department cables
