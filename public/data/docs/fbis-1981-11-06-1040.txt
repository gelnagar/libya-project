PLO LEADER MAKES STOPOVER IN TRIPOLI 


LD061108 Tripoli JANA in English 0819 GMT 6 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 Nov (JANA) —- Yasir ‘Arafat, PLO Executive Committee chairman, and 
Brigadier Sa'd Sayil (Abu al-Walid), Central Committee member of the Palestine Liberation 
Movement (Fatah), last night stopped over at Tripoli international airport. 


Yasir ‘Arafat made a statement to our correspondent in which he said "We must recognize 
that we are facing an imperialist-Zionist onslaught on the Arab region, particularly 
after the announcement of the Zionist-imperialist alliance.

He added, "We must take into account the Zionist concentrations in the south where terror- 
ist Begin said a war would break out against the Palestinian revolution, the Lebanese 
national movement and Syria, this, in addition to manoeuvres being conducted by the Rapid 
Deployment Force, American naval units in Bahrain and the Mediterranean and forces from 
Egypt, Sudan, Somalia and Oman. These manoeuvres pose a direct and serious threat to 

the Arab nation and are a practical manifestation of America's declaration of a strategic 
alliance between itself and Zionism. This alliance does not only influence the present 
generations but all future generations as well. 


I am now touring the Arab region before the Fes summit in order to adopt a united Arab 
stand in confronting the challenges facing the Arab nation." 


LIBYA, ROMANIA SIGN COOPERATION AGREEMENT 


LD051728 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 5 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 5 Nov (JANA) -- A cooperation agreement was signed in Tripoli this morn- 
ing between the Socialist Peoples Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah [SPLAJ] and Romania. It pro- 
vides for cooperation inconstruction and also participation by Romanian companies in the 
implementation of some of the Jamahiriyah's transformation plan projects. The agreement 
was signed for the SPLAJ by the secretary of the General People's Committee for Housing 
and for Romania by the minister state secretary in the Ministry of Individual Construction, 
Ion Stanescu. 


LIAISON SECRETARY RECEIVES POLISARIO OFFICIAL 


LDO12054 Tripoli JANA in English 1908 GMT 1 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 1 Nov (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY) -- The People's Committee secretary at the 
external liaison bureau this morning conferred with Sid Ahmed El Batal, member of the 
Polisario Front Executive Committee and member of the Saharan Republic's Revolutionary 


Command Council. Discussions centered on recent developments in the Western Sahara issue.
