LIBYAN AMBASSADOR TO PAKISTAN DENIES HE RESIGNED 


NC291836 Abu Dhabi EMIRATES NEWS AGENCY in Arabic 1815 GMT 29 Jan 80 NC 


[Text] Abu Dhabi, 29 January--In a statement to the EMIRATES NEWS AGENCY today, Libya's 
Ambassador to Pakistan Muhammad Bashir Megheirbi denied press reports that he has resigned 
and decided to live in Abu Dhabi. He described these reports as completely incorrect, 

He added that he is still his country's ambassador in Islamabad and that he is now paying 
a private visit to the Emirates, where he previously served as his country's ambassador 
for 5 years, 


Embassy Statement 
LD301900 Tripoli JANA in English 1625 GMT 30 Jan 80 LD 


[Text] Kuwait, January 30, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The embassy of the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in Kuwait today denied allegations published yesterday 
in the Kuwaiti press that the Jamahiriyah's ambassador to Pakistan had resigned, 


The press attache at the embassy issued a statement denying these allegations and 
describing them as untrue. He said Mr Bashir Megheirbi is acting as ambassador to 
Pakistan but was recalled in protest against the execution of the former Prime Minister 
Zulfikar Ali Bhutto by the Pakistani authorities, The statement which was broadcast 
and published in the Kuwaiti press today, confirmed that Mr Megheirbi is now in the 
United Emirates states on a private visit, 


DELEGATION EXPLAINS WALKOUT FROM SUDANESE PARTY CONGRESS 

LD311055 Kuwait AS -SIYASAH in Arabic 28 Jan 80 p 16 LD 

[Marwan Hazin dispatch: "Libyan Delegation Withdraws From Third National Congress of 
Sudanese Socialist Union; Libyan-Sudanese Crisis Threatens Severance of Relations 


Because Egyptian and American Military Attaches Visited Sudanese -Libyan Border; 
Sudanese Foreign Ministry Denies Egyptian Attache's Visit"] 


{Excerpts ] Khartoume-The Libyan delegation to the Third National Congress of the 
Sudanese Socialist Union led by Muhammad Abu alQasim az Zuway, assistant secretary 
general of the People's General Congress, has announced its withdrawal from the 
Sudanese congress, 


V. 31 Jan 80 : NORTH AFRICA 


At an extraordinary press conference at the Palace of Friendship, where the Sudanese 
Congress is meeting, Az=Zuway said: The withdrawal decision was made as a result of the 
visit made by the American military attache, agcompanied by the Egyptian military attache, 
to the Sudanese area of (Al-Fashir). He added that at a time when a highelevel Libyan 
Jamahiriyah delegation is participating in congress meetings with the Sudanese people, 

the Sudanese information media came out with a strange report about a visit by the two 
military <ttaches to an area on the Libyan-Sudanese border. 


The Libyan official emphasized the seriousness of this visit, which he considered "tanta- 
mount to a direct provocation directed against the Jamahiriyah and falling within the cone 
text of a Sudanese-Egyptian alliance under an American umbrella." He said: This has taken 
place at a time when we are aware of the extent of relations between the Egyptian and 
Israeli regimes--and of the concentration of massive armies on our Libyan border. 


Muhammad azeZuway, who is No 3 in Libya, emphasized that the visit testifies to the existence 
of military coordination between Sudan and Egypt with America's blessing. Naturally, he 
said, such coordination is directed against neighboring states such as Libya. 


He added: We regard this action as a hostile and provocative act at a time when we are exe 
tending a friendly hand to Sudan. The report came as a surprise to us. We regret having to 
decide to withdraw from the current congress. 


He asserted that the visit took place on 25 January--that is, 3 days ago--at the same time 
as the Libyan delegation was arriving in Sudan. He said that the strange thing is that the 
SUNA NEWS AGENCY and Sudanese radio and television carried the report without hesitation. 
The Libyan official said: We have asked the Sudanese ambassador in the Libyan capital 
(Tripoli) for an explanation of this behavior but so far we have received no reply. He 
added: If anything, this proves the existence of military coordination between the three 
countries (Sudan, Egypt and America). 


He explained that the withdrawal decision has no connection with the recent report of 
Sudanese military facilities to the United States. He said: In any case, that is an 
internal matter which concerns Sudan and we have no right to interfere in it. He 
differentiated between the two reportse=-the report on the two attaches visit and the report 
on Sudanese facilities for the United States--by saying: Libya considers the report on 

the visit a hostile act and premeditated coordination among the three aforementioned 

parties against the Jamahiriyah. The Libyan official said: We hope that Sudan will issue 

a statement explaining the question of the visit so that relations will not be affected. 

He then recalled the reports on Egyptian concentrations on the border with Libya and also 
the secret annexes to the Camp David agreement which, he said, include a hostile act against 
the Arab nation and progressive forces like Libya. He said that Egypt has confirmed that 

it has assumed the role of policeman in the area, 


Later on, the Sudanese Foreign Ministry issued a statement explaining that the Egyptian 
military attache did not visit the aforementioned area and that the American military 
attache visited it alone. 


So far, there has been no Libyan reaction to the Sudanese explanation.
