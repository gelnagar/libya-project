LIBYA

Coverage of 8 May Attack on Al-Qadhdhafi Headquarters 


JANA Promises KUNA Details 
LD081740 Kuwait KUNA in Arabic 1732 GMT 8 May 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 8 May (KUNA) — This evening JANA declined 
to give any details about incidents reported to have taken place 
in Tripoli earlier today. However, it said in a telex contact with 
KUNA that it will provide KUNA with details later this evening. 


Reports from Rome spoke earlier about shooting in the Libyan 
capital earlier today. 


Spokesman Blames ‘Arafat Loyalists 
NCO081739 Paris AFP in English 1735 GMT 8 May 84 


[By Marie Jioannidis] 


[Text] Paris, May 8 (AFP) — Libyan forces today repulsed an 
armed commando attack on Libyan leader Colonel Mu‘ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi’s combined headquarters and residence here, kill- 
ing the attackers, diplomatic sources in Tripoli said contacted by 
telephone. 


The attack, which Libyan sources said was carried out by 13 
people who entered the country from neighbouring Tunisia on 
Sunday, began at around 0630 GMT near the Al-‘Aziziyah 
barracks in central Tripoli, the sources said. Shooting could be 
heard around the heavily guarded barracks, which Col al- 
Qadhdhafi uses both as a residence and a headquarters, for over 
four hours and either 15 or 16 artillery explosions were heard, 
they added. It was not known whether Col al-Qadhdhafi was in 
the barracks at the time of the attack. 


A Libyan spokesman interviewed on a French radio station here 
blamed the abortive attack on “a few Libyans and Palestinians 
loyal to Abu lyad [Salah Khalaf,” a close associate of Palestine 
Liberation Organization (PLO) Chairman Yasir ‘Arafat. The 
spokesman, Ibrahim Bihari, said the attackers had taken up 
positions in a building before being “liquidated” by members of 
Libya’s Revolutionary Committees. Libya is at odds with Mr 
‘Arafat’s Fatah organization, the main grouping within the 
divided PLO. 


The shooting here stopped at between 1130 GMT and 1230 
GMT, when normal traffic resumed in Tripoli, the diplomatic 
sources said. The attack came only hours after the Libyan news 
agency JANA, in a report monitored here, reported that one 
member of a terrorist group had been killed and two injured in 
an incident near the Tunisian border on Sunday. The person 
killed in that incident was identified on Libyan television yester- 
day as Ahmad Ahwas, a Libyan said to be wanted by Interpol 
and carrying a Sudanese diplomatic passport. The passport and 
an international arrest warrant against a Mr Ahwas were dis- 
played during the Libyan television broadcast. The identity 
photograph on the passport was not shown, although that on the 
arrest warrant could be seen.
