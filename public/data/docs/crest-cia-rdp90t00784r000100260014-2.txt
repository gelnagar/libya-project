Summary (AI): A June 1982 NPIC briefing note reports the first identification in Libya of a complete SA-8 surface-to-air missile battery at the Tarabulus (Tripoli) SAM support facility, which also supports an SA-6 regiment. The battery had four SA-8 transporter-launcher-radar vehicles plus a probable resupply vehicle and a probable BTR-60PA command vehicle.
• Coordinates given: 32-46-30N 013-12-10E.
• The facility also supports an SA-6 regiment.
• This is the first identification of SA-8 equipment at an operational SAM facility.
• The document is an NPIC/IEG briefing note, control no. 2-15650/82, marked SECRET NOFORN.
• 1982-06-28: First identification of a complete SA-8 SAM battery in Libya: four TLAR vehicles, one probable resupply vehicle, one probable BTR-60PA command vehicle.
