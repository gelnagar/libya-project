LIBYA

AL@QADHDHAFI RECEIVES TELEGRAM FROM BREZHNEV 
LD271126 Tripoli JANA in English 0814 GMT 27 May 79 LD 
(Text] Tripoli, May 27, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The revolution's leader received a 
telegram from Soviet Communist Party chairman and president of the Supreme Soviet Presidium, 
Mr. Leonid Brezhnev, on the occasion of Africa liberation day. 
Dr. Brezhnev affirmed in his telegram that the complete liquidation of remnants of 
colonialism and racism in the African Continent has entered its final Stage, and that the 


day when the phenomenon of colonialism is abolished is not far away. 


Mr. Brezhnev hailed the African peoples successes in their struggle for freedom, independence 
and social progress. 


Mr. Brezhnev affirmed in his telegram to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi the Soviet Union!s solidarity 
with the African peoples struggle in Zimbabwe and Namibia and work for liquidating the 
racist regime in South Africa, 


Mr. Brezhnev condemned the new racists*® practices in South Africa and aggression against 
liberation forces in neighbouring African countries. 


Mr. Brezhnev finally wished African peoples further successes in their struggle for liberating 
Africa from colonialism and racism, 


He also wished them economic and social progress under the shadow of world peace, 


V. 30 May 79 NORTH APRICA 


POLISARIO OFFICIAL ARRIVES FOR TALKS WITH MAURITANIA 


LD26196 Tripoli JANA in English 1849 GMT 26 May 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, May 26, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The assistant secretary general of the 
Front for the Liberation of Al*Saguia al-Hamra and Rio de Oro, Mr Bachir Mustapha Rakibi, 
arrived in Tripoli International Airport today at oon en a several day visit to the 
Jamahiriyah. 


Present at his welcome in the airport were representatives of the General People's Congress 
External Liaison Office and Foreign Secretariat's Protocol Directorate. 


Rakibi made a statement to the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY's correspondent in which he praised 
just stands taken by the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah toward the Al-Saguia 
aleHamra and Rio de Oro, 


He said: "We are here to continue negotiations between the Islamic Republic of Mauritania 
and the Saharan Republic on the Mauritanian handover of Saharan Republic territories to the 
popular front."
