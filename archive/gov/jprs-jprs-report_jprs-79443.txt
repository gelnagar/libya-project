Step on the Path to Trouble 


Fikri al-Jazzar, member of the Labor Party Parliamentary Committee, then said: 
The government request for confiscation of today's edition of AL-SHA'B is nothing 
but a step on the path to trouble that God wants us to overcome and vanquish. 


AL-SHA'B has been a target from the day they tried to stop it by fabricating 
the so-called newsprint crisis. When they failed, they tried to stop it with 
pressures on the party deputies in the People's Assembly to resign from the 
party. When they failed in this step, colleague Ahmad Firghali was expelled 
from the assembly so that his erxpulsion might intimidate a number of the party 
deputies in the assembly and make them resign from the party. But their third 
endeavor failed and so they have resorted to the direct method of confiscating 
the paper. 


The advice I wish to offer the chairman of the Democratic National Party is to 
let AL-SHA'B continue publishing so that it can act as an outlet. I beg him 
to let AL-SHA'B continue publishing so as to protect the system because we are 
a part of the system and we defend it. What I mean by the system here is the 
constitutional republican system and the state's legitimate institutions. 


Fikri al-Jazzar then dealt with the U.S. aggression against Libya, saying tliat 
all the Arab countries have condemned it because the dogfight between the U.S. 
and Libyan planes took place over the waters of the Gulf of Sidrah, which is 
within the territorial borders of the Libyan people. It is not right for 
Egypt's voice to be alien amidst the Arab consensus condemning the aggression.

Difficult Situations Create Honorable Men 


Muhammad al-Sayyid ‘Abd al-'Aziz, a member of the party youth, then said: How 
difficult are the conditions and days we are going through! But these condi- 
tions and days create honorable men and true patriotism. 


They have tried to disband the Lawyers Union Council but they have not dissolved 

Egyptian nationalism in the hearts of the lawyers. They then canceled Ahmad 

Firghali's membership in the People's Assembly. But they have not dropped Fir- 

ghali from the people's hearts. They then confiscate AL-SHA'B. Now that they 

have confiscated AL-SHA'B, will the Egyptian people welcome Menachem Begin, the 
murderer of the Arab people in Palestine and Lebanon? Their endeavors are | 
futile because the people will march forward, regardless of what they do.
