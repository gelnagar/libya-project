{Interview with Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi by Kurt Sei-

[Text] NEUE KRONEN-ZEITUNG: Brother Mu‘am- 
mar, let us start with the topical events and Israel's 
course of action in the occupied territories. How do you 
evaluate the Palestinians’ revolt and its effects on the 
development in the Middle East? 

Al-Qadhdhafi: Undoubtedly, the whole world has now 
become aware of the danger that Zionism represents. 
Today all those who support Zionism, for example, most 
of all the United States, would have to be called to 
account, just like those who supported Hitler and fas- 
cism in the past. The Zionists constantly use the world’s 
sensitivity toward the crimes of fascism against the Jews 
to justify their own criminal policy. They go so far as to 
want to interfere in other countries and to decide, for 
example, who shall be president in Austria, because 
Austria is a small country. 

If Waldheim were really guilty, he and (French Presi- 
dent) Mitterrand would have to be placed on trial. 
Mitterrand was minister in a government that commit- 
ted terrible crimes in Algeria, even a genocide with more 
than | million people killed. Mitterrand was responsible 
for the murder of many patriots in the prisons. Do only 
crimes against Jews count, while crimes against Arabs do 
not? What kind of justice is this? 

ARAB AFRICA 

NEUE KRONEN-ZEITUNG: In the past you vehe- 
mently criticized PLO leader ‘Arafat. How do you judge 
today his part in the topical events? 

Al-Qadhdhafi: This is the revolution of a new genera- 
tion.... I do not want to say any more. 

Peace Movement in Israel Positive [subhead] 

NEUE KRONEN-ZEITUNG: In Israel a new generation 
in the oppositional peace movement is making itself 
heard. Are these not forces of hope which should be 
promoted and encouraged? 

Al-Qadhdhafi: It has always been my point of view that 
Israel has no right to exist, but that there is only one 
Palestinian people—Arab, Christian, and Jewish Pales- 
tinians. It is surely possible that these peace-loving 
groups you have mentioned will be part of such a 
Palestinian state. The others should return to where they 
have come from, that is to say, to the Arab states. It is not 
at all in the interest of the Jews that there is a Jewish 
state. It brings them into a position of hostility toward 
other peoples. The Jews who are killed by the Palestin- 
ians are not killed because they are Jews. They would not 
be harmed if they had remained in the Soviet Union or 
in France. 

NEUE KRONEN-ZEITUNG: Libya’s policy is some- 
times hard for us to understand. In this context, terror is 
also mentioned and you are called the “‘most dangerous 
man in the world.” Do you feel that you misunderstood? 

Al-Qadhdhafi: People are not against me because there is 
terrorism. Libyans never hijacked a plane or took hos- 
tages; we reject terrorist crimes, because we are putting 
up a just struggle for the liberation of the peoples. Our 
opponents call this terrorism in order to make us suspect, 
and so they can terrorize us. Real terrorists do not meet 
with such hostility as shown toward us. 

NEUE KRONEN-ZEITUNG: Reagan will not be Pres- 
ident for much longer. Will you then be better disposed 
toward the United States? 

Al-Qadhdhafi: I believe that relations between the 
United States and the Arabs will undoubtedly improve 
after Reagan leaves. We do not hate the Americans, but 
we are against their policy. | do not believe that the next 
President will commit the same follies and horrible 
crimes against us as Reagan has.

rial has disappeared from Germany. Are you building 
nuclear bombs?

that the material was not shipped to Libya or Pakistan.

NEUE KRONEN-ZEITUNG: Regarding the intercon-

lamic fundamentalism.” What is your attitude toward it? 
Your country is also strictly Islamic. Al-Qadhdhafi:

Palestine and what has been made of the Jewish religion—

Newspaper Reports Efforts To Reunite PLO 
JN291731 Abu Dhabi AL-ITTIHAD in Arabic 
28 Feb 88 p 1 

[Text] AL-ITTIHAD has learned that during the past few 
days Libya has resumed its contacts with the Palestinian 
revolution factions in Damascus and the Fatah Move- 
ment in Tunisia in order to convince them of the 
importance of holding an expanded meeting in Tripoli 
next month. Its task will be to discuss the means for 
expediting the reunification of the PLO in light of 
regional and international political developments, the 
escalation of the popular uprising in the occupied terri- 
tories, and the need to support and develop it. 

Palestinian sources in Damascus told AL-ITTIHAD that 
Brigadier General Abu Bakr Yunis, chief of staff of the 
Libyan Armed Forces, will arrive in Damascus in the 
next few weeks in order to convince Syria to support this 
meeting and to work to render it a success. He will also 
hold talks with Palestinian organizations and officials 
who have not responded to the Libyan invitation. 
Among those who have not responded are Al-Sa‘iqah, 
Khalid al-Fahum, and ‘Abd al-Muhsin Abu Mayzar. The 
sources said they expect the new Libyan endeavors to 
succeed after the PFLP-GC and the Palestine Popular 
Struggle Front, which had previously rejected such a 
meeting, changed their stand and agreed to the Libyan 
endeavors.
