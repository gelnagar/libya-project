Summary (AI): A December 1983 NPIC imagery note reports increased physical security at Qadhafi's headquarters and living complex at the Tarabulus Barracks Brigade and Battalion HQ in northwest Tripoli, with roadblocks, dispersed SA-8 launchers, a ZSU-23/4, ZPU-4 guns and at least nine tanks/APCs. The barracks is described as heavily fortified with tank bunkers and underground storage.
• Garrison units included one T-72 tank battalion, one BM-21 multiple rocket launcher battalion, one BMP-equipped mechanized infantry company, and one air defence unit with SA-8, SA-9 and antiaircraft guns.
• Coordinates given: 32-52-28N 013-10-07E.
• Document classified SECRET NOFORN, from the National Photographic Interpretation Center (NPIC), control no. 2714445/83.
• 1983-12-16: Increased physical security observed at Qadhafi's headquarters: at least six roadblock barriers, concrete blocks in maze fashion at four entrances, two deployed SA-8 launchers (deployed since mid-summer 1983), one ZSU-23/4, several ZPU-4 guns, at least nine tanks and APCs.
