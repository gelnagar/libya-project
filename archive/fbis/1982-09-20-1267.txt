CONDEMNS ARAB INACTION IN FACE OF MASSACRES

political commentator] 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Sep (JANA) -- It is being confirmed day by day that the Zionists are 


determined to annihilate the Arab race from the face of the earth without distinguishing 
among the Syrians, Lebanese, Palestinians or Egyptians, or among women, children or the 


elderly.

This determination has been confirmed by the horrible massacres being committed by the 
invading Zionists in Lebanon and in the face of which the world stands shocked. Arabs 
from the ocean to the gulf stand still like rabbits and rats awaiting the arrival of the 
Zionist train of death, which is approaching at high speed, to crush their bones and 
tear up their bodies, turning them into a (?sea) of blood. 


At a time when the Zionists are raping Arab women and slaughtering innocent children, 
leaving hundreds of bodies swimming in pools of blood in the streets of west Beirut as

their palaces, drunk with happiness that they are still on their thrones. They leave 
Beirut with its ruined streets and buildings and its men, women and children to confront 
the bullets of the enemy and the bayonets pointed at their chests with spite, hatred, 
savagry and criminal terrorism by the invaders, who have become accustomed to shedding 
blood. 


What has the Fes summit done? What has the pig of the peninsula done through his con- 
tinuing contacts with the U.S. administration? Today's massacres are the shameful culmin- 
ation of their rush to make Washington their Qiblah and a place of pilgrimage instead of 
Beirut, which is being burned by the Zionists and destroyed by the train of death, and 
which is being made the battlefield on which to settle the conflict. 


But cowardice does not make victory. The cowards are unable to move, and here are thousands 
of victims whose blood is being shed as a result of this cowardice. These wretched and 
feeble policies, if maintained, definitely will enable the train of death to advance 
forcefully toward their positions, where they sit and issue mere feeble statements. 


The collective massacres performed by the neocrusaders in Lebanon today are the best proof 
of the impotent stands expressed by the Fes summit. The vital forces in the Arab world 
should rise to the challenge to shoulder their responsibility and destroy all these trivial 
samples, those fading molds, hated types and bad symbols which are deep in their agent 
role, thus turning their bodies and the bodies of all honorable men into a dam to confront 
the Zionist train of death.

massacre which is still being perpetrated in Beirut since beginning on Friday night, and 
said that the president, who is in Paris, is following the development of events.

never [word indistinct] in this century and that the crime committed by the sinful hand

EQUATORIAL GUINEA 


PRESIDENT OBIANG TO VISIT FRANCE 22-23 SEPTEMBER 


AB171800 Paris AFP in French 1723 GMT 17 Sep 82 


[Text] Paris, 17 Sep (AFP) -- Equatorial Guinean President Teodoro Obiang Nguema 
Mbasogo will make an official working visit to France from 22 to 23 September, it was 
learned on Friday in Paris from an authoritative French source. 


The president of Equatorial Guinea will be received on Thursday, 22 September, by 
President Francois Mitterrand, the same source specified. This will be President Obiang 


Nguema Mbasogo's first official visit to France. 


ZALRE 


AB171346 Paris AFP in English 1247 GMT 17 Sep 82 


[Text] Lusaka, 17 Sep (AFP) -- Zaire's Shaba Province Governor Mandungu Bula Nyati 
has assured Zambia that his country has ordered an immediate withdrawal of its troops 
who had seized and occupied Zambian territory at Chiengi in Luapula Province. 


The decision on the pullout was reached at an emergency meeting in Lubumbashi, Zaire, 
yesterday between Mr Nyati and a member of the Central Committee for Zambia's Copperbelt 
Province, Alex Shapi, who is also chairman of the joint Zambia-Zaire Permanent Commission. 


Before travelling to Lubumbashi in southern Zaire yesterday, Mr Shapi announced that 
Zaire had raised its flag on Zambian soil and massed troops at Chiengi.
