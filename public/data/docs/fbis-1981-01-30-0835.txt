LIBYA

MILITARY OFFICIAL COMMENTS ON DEFENSE, AID TO CHAD 


LD300026 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 29 Jan 81 


[Excerpts ] The commander of urban militarization, Major al-Khuwayldi al-Humaydi, has 
addressed students at the Tripoli secondary school and Dahman secondary boys school. 
He concentrated on the question of urban militarization and the future horizons for 
building a popular force capable of defense, deterrence and attack. 


In his lecture, which he delivered on Thursday morning at the people's hall, Al-Humaydi 
spoke of the historic role played by the students in instigating Libyan society toward 
revolution and change. 


Maj al-Khuwayldi al-Humaydi also stressed the need for supporting and aiding the Chadian 
people so that freedom will be established in another part of the African Continent-~-a 
purpose for which the great 1 September revolution was fighting. 

He added that France and those behind it must realize that the fierce winds of freedom, 
which have swept the jungles of Africa, are capable of uprooting imperialism. 


He affirmed that the Libyan Arab people are not prepared to listen to the buzzing of 
foreign aircraft on their borders whatever their directions. 


Major al-Humaydi then spoke of the humiliating conditions in which the Arab nation was 
living at present and the Arab rulers’ abandonment of their principal cause the Palestinian 
cause. He affirmed that the liberation of Palestine cannot be achieved by statements 
issued at summit conferences but by the declaration of war against the enemy and deter- 
mination to liberate the land and Arab dignity. 


GOUKOUNI CONDEMNS FRENCH MOVES AGAINST CHAD 


LD291502 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 29 Jan 81 


[Text] The premier [as heard] of the lawful government and head of state of Chad, Goukouni 
Oueddei, has condemned the suspicious French moves and positions against the Chadian 
people. At a press conference he held in At-Ta'if yesterday, he said that France 

cannot impose anything on Chad. He stressed that the unity of the masses between 

Chad and the Jamajhiriyah will be achieved by a decision dictated by the will of the 

two brotherly people. 


He accused the French president of imposing his control on Chad. In this connection 
Goukouni Oueddei stressed that his country cannot possible cooperate with France while 
the latter is leading imperialistic attempts against the sovereignty of Chad and the 
ambitions of its people. 


The Chadian head of state also accused Giscard d'Estaing of breaking up the national 
unity in Chad and of mobilizing French forces to impose rebellious agents which the 
people decided to get rid of once and for all. 


Goukouni declared to reporters that the Libyan presence in Chad is lawful and took 

place at Chad's request, and that it constitutes no danger at all. He said that this 
presence will remain until overall peace is established throughout Chad, pointing out in 
this connection that foreign forces are threatening Chad's borders.

CHADIAN PRESIDENT ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 29 JAN 


LD292336 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 29 Jan 81 


[Text] Goukouni Oueddei, head of the legitimate government and state in Chad, arrived in 
Tripoli yesterday [29 January] evening on a state visit to the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah. He was welcomed at the Tripoli international fair by Staff Maj ‘Abd 
as-Salam Jallud, members of the Secretariat of the General People's Congress, secretaries 
of the popular committees and members of the political corps in the Jamahiriyah.
