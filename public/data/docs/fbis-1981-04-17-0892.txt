LIBYA

OFFICIAL DENOUNCES U.S. INTERVENTION IN GULF AREA 


LD162304 Kuwait KUNA in English 1546 GMT 16 Apr 81 


[Text] Paris, 16 Apr (KUNA)--A prominent Libyan leader denounced Thursday U.S. plans 
for direct intervention in the Gulf region and Middle East in general. 


Saed Hafyana currently on a visit here told KUNA that the U.S. deployment force was aimed 
at ensuring the superiority of the United States over the Arabian Gulf countries. He 
warned the United States to stay away from the Gulf region and added "We know our problems 
and can solve them without any outside intervention be it military, political or economic." 
Indeed there is a great danger threatening the Gulf security but Arab nations were 
competent enough to maintain their independence and security, he added. 


However, he admitted that the main problem was Egypt and the ‘Camp David plot'. He 
expressed concern over certain Arab countries who are normalising relations with 
President as-Sadat's regime. He condemned As-Sadat for offering military bases on 
Egyptian soil. 


Hafyana also accused Sudan of violating the decisions taken at Baghdad summit and 

added that the Sudanese Government was moving into the footsteps of As-Sadat. He called 

on Arab nations to take drastic measures against Sudan which had joined Egypt in collaborating 
with Israel. 


Referring to Libyan President Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi's proposed visit to Italy, Hafyana 
said it was aimed at strengthening peace and security in the Mediterranean region and 
added that no external pressure could influence this objective.
