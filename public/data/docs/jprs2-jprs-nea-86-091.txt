EGYPT 

COMMUNIST FIGURE ANALYZES OPPOSITION 
Damascus AL-BA'TH in Arabic 30 May 86 pp 5, ll 

[Interview with Egyptian Communist Party Politburo Member Michel Kamil: 
“The Egyptian Opposition, Between Theory and Practice;" place and date not 
specified] 

[Text] Comrade Michel Kamil, member of the Egyptian Communist Party's Polit- 
buro, is vastly experienced, being one of those progressive Egyptian strug- 
glers who have carried on their “revolutionary struggle” ever since the Fifties. 
Along with other eminent figures, he unhesitatingly paid tic price of the 
struggle in gloomy prisons, underwent the worst kinds of torture, and willingly 
suffered exile in carrying out his mission. 

This interview with Michel Kamil is particularly important in the local, Arab 
and international context because it is so open. It basically concentrates 
on the Egyptian opposition's theoretical and practical situation and on 

the heated incidents taking place in Egypt, particularly the uprising by the 
central security forces, the murder of Sulayman Khatir, the workers’ strikes, 
the extension of the emergency law, the Egyptian regime's attempt to get 

out of its predicament by Arabizing Camp David, and the growing tendency 
towards subservience towards imperialism and Ziotism, in addition to American 
and Zionist threats against resolute Syria and aggression against Libya. 

In the interview, which lasted more than 2 hours, Kamil diagnosed the crisis 
and outlined various outlets and objective alternatives. Here we give you 
the interview word for word, without any retouching. 

The Difference Between the Opposition and the Masses 

[Question] How would you compare the Egyptian opposition's theoretical and 
practical situation to the spontaneous mass resurgence taking place in Egypt 
recently? 

[Answer] It is true that we are experiencing a certain dissimilarity and 
contradiction, as manifested in the “resurgence” of the mass movement in 

Egypt (even though it has spontaneous features), along with the official oppo- 
sition forces' “backsliding"” to join, and even forge an alliance with, this 

mass movement. There is increasing isolation and schism between the leaders 

of the Egyptian opposition and the mass movement, something which involves not 
only us but also pertains to the various opposition forces and their rapproch- 
ement with the middle class cadres and the backbone of the Egyptian national 
opposition. This schism exists not only between the opposition parties and 

the masses, but also between these parties' leaders and their own cadres and 
Supporters, to the point where these leaders themselves have been forced to 
resolve this situation. We have noticed that the latest political report by 
the NPU contained phrases which demonstrated a critical Overview of NPU 
policies and Practices, as well as clear references to t
[…]
m internal erosion. Thus these opposition forces gave 
wide dissemination to the slogans “national agreement” and “democratic 
accord,” and invited all the Egyptian parties, including the ruling NPU, 
to take part in this model “alliance” in order to confront the dangers 
which threatened everyone, authorities and opposition alike, and which 
therefore, in their view, threatened to sweep them all away. 

However, on the other hand, the events of the past 3 months have proven that 
the regime was too hasty in taking steps and following policies which 

were totally contradictory to those flights of fancy presented by the oppo- 
sition. Police repression against the legitimate struggle of the working 
class and the masses has increased in ferocity, more arrests are being 

made, and crimes of brutal torture have become everyday practice. Just 

as happened during the January 1977 experiment, al-Sadat's subsequent push 
towards total commitment to imperialism, his taking refuge with Israel, and 
the signing of the Camp David agreement, we find this same experience 

being repeated with the Mubarak regime's forceful rush towards protecting 
itself, and we therefore see many repressive measures, such as the following. 

For the first time, an American nuclear aircraft carrier, accompanied by 

five battleships, was permitted to pass through the Suez Canal into the Medi- 
terranean; this potentially polluting passage was part of the American plans 
for aggression against Syria and Libya. Furthermore, the Egyptian regime 

has stepped up its hostile, squalid propaganda campaigns against the remaining 
citadels of the Arab nationalist movement. Egyptian Foreign Minister 'Ismat 
'Abd-al-Majid has issued statements supporting the results of the Tokyo 
conference of the seven industrial nations, particularly those concerning 
“alleged terrorism.” The Egyptian regime's sale to Israel of Egyptian 
weaponry, including 60 French Gazelle planes assembled in Egypt, has been 
revealed; most of these weapons, except for the planes, have been sent 

on to South Africa. The Egyptian regime does not deny this fact, justify- 
ing it on the grounds that these weapons were sold and sent to Israel, and 
not to South Africa, as if dealing with the racist Zionist state so close to 
our borders and so immediately threatening is more excusable than dealing 
with South Africa. 

In addition, recently leaked reports indicate that negotiations are in pro- 
gress between the Egyptian regime and the American administration over grant- 
ing “military bases" in Sinai and on the Libyan borders, in return for fin- 
ancial compensation in the form of “rent”, on the pretext of alleviating 

the military debt and interest burden so as to try to solve the economic 
crisis. 

Spreading Camp David Throughout the Arab World 

[Question] In your opinion, what role has been “assigned" to the Mubarak 
regime to facilitate American and Zionist aggression against the Arab world? 

[Answer] The Egyptian regime's role in American aggression against the re- 
gion is no secret. The regime carries out its assigned role covertly, to 

a certain extent, fearing the reaction of the Egyptian popular masses. Even 
so, at this time, the part which President Husni Mubarak is playing in 
reviving the ‘Amman agreement, attempting to propagate the Camp David 
agreements on the official Arab level, and providing the “Zionized Phal- 
angist Lebanese forces" with weapons, has become clear and well-known. Just 
recently it was officially announced that an Egyptian ship carrying large 
quantities of Egyptian weapons had arrived at the Lebanese port of Juniyah. 

The National Economy Cannot Be Built With Subsidies and Subservience 

[Question] Mubarak and Peres called on the recent seven-zember Tokyo Summit 
to adopt a Marshall Plan. What is your opinion of this? Why are some 
Egyptian opposition forces calling for Saving the Egyptian national economy? 

[Answer] All these delusions of “guidance” proposed by some Egyptian 
opposition forces have collapsed under the pressure of the re
[…]
nary trend, particularly because it has always kept 
pace with the existing mass movement, and also because this revolutionary 
trend within those parties has always coexisted with the course of events 
in the mass movement, thus leading to the development of new cadres within 
the mass movement who we hope will join ranks with the more experienced 
leaders. This is the year of life--it is a historical imperative that 

the new cadres pursue their true course and that these opposition forces 
escape from their present dilemma. 

The Masses' Fruitful Coming-of-Age 

[Question] One aspect of the crisis in the Egyptian opposition came to 
light recently in the propositions of “mass dryness" and “building up 
the national economy with foreign aid,” although the course of events 
proved the exact opposite. What is your reply to those propositions, 
which are an attempt to cover up and smooth over the crisis? 

[Answer] We feel that the opposition forces' crisis is essentially a 
leadership crisis, not a crisis of “mass dryness." 

Serious incidents might occur sometimes without arousing any mass reaction, 
but should we hold the masses accountable? or the forces which are presumably 
the masses’ leaders? 

The fact is that the present situation in Egypt holds ample evidence of 

a fruitful mass coming-of-age and an anger towards the regime, which became 
obvious after the bombing of the PLO base in Tunisia, the hijacking of 

the Egyptian plane, Israel's act of piracy against the Libyan plane, and 
the murder of Sulayman Khatir. Scientifically and logically, it must be 
said that no mass movement can be expected to carry on firmly and steadily 
without leaders. It is a known fact that any mass movement experiences 
waves of expansion and contraction. The primary role of the leaders is 

to give continuity to a spontaneous movement, and to elevate it from a 
spontaneous movement to an organized, purposeful political movement. 

It might be said, and we believe it might be partly true, that “mass aware- 
ness" comes before “leaders' awareness.” This might seem odd, but it is a 
fact if we put it in its proper context. The masses have gained, and there 

has become lodged in their consciousness, specific concepts and convictions 
which constitute an advanced form of awareness, an awareness acquired from 
the facts of life, their class situation, and their sufferings. 

As for the leaders, if we look at the problem purely in the sense of awareness, 
i.e. the comprehension of purely political concepts and the ability to analyze 
and debate, then some might feel that this is a true awareness. However, 

it does not express mass interests, nor is it in the interest of the emerging 
exploited struggling mass forces. 

In this case, therefore, mass awareness might achieve partial, temporary 
gains, but will never achieve radical, strategic goals. Herein lies the role 
of leadership as we understand it: leadership should clearly express mass 
interests and prog
[…]
ppened is that the invitation is now being extended to all 
Egyptian parties and forces indiscriminately. There is utter confusion, with 
revolutionary forces being mixed up with counter-revolutionary forces, and 
arbitrary lines of separation being drawn between them. 

With regard to the opposition front's program, we have suggested the formu- 
lation of a national program which could achieve the goals of social change. 
The other forces have now begun offering one of two different concepts; 
oddly enough, they combine the two into one idea. On the one hand, they 
call for setting up an intermediate phase within the existing regime, 

and for eliminating parasitism instead of the regime, so as to guide it 

and improve its economic performance. On the other hand, they propose a 
general national program for all forces without exception, including those 
who support subservience and imperialist brokerage and those who have 

a direct interest in continuing this subservience. This is our stand, 
which shows clearly just how much we differ. You can judge for yourself. 

The Hysterical Attack On Syria 

[Question] What do you think of Syria's stand on American-Zionist threats 
and its other fundamental, firm, nationalist stands? 

[Answer] First of all, in order to evaluate Syria's honorable, resolute role, 
you need only follow the American administration's strange, hysterical attack, 
which is basically directed at Syria itself, even though it began with 
attacks on Granada, Libya, and other places. 

The hysterical American-Zionist series of attacks is aimed at two spots 
which represent the principal bases of defiance against imperialism and its 
plots for hegemony--Syria in the Arab world, and Nicaragua in Latin America. 
This is not to dismiss imperialist plans to strike at all bases of per- 
severance and challenge to American hegemony, but right now the imperialists 
are obviously concentrating on trying to get rid of the thorn of “resolute 
Syria," which disturbs the sleep of all the colonialists. 

Strategic Parity 
[Question] In spite of all the threats against Syria, which is determined 
to achieve strategic parity with the Zionist enemy, what should the Arab 

national liberation forces do, in the face of foreign threats against 
Syria, to realize their own potentials? 

9 

[Answer] First of all, Syria is capable of offering up the greatest sacri- 
fices in order to protect this main fortress of perseverance. Naturally, 
Strategic parity with the Zionist enemy, as proposed by Syria, is the main 
thing upsetting imperialist and Zionist circles. 

At present, the main trend in American-Zionist aggression is to try to 
upset the parity which Syria is striving for. 

Syrian perseverance is a major factor in broader Arab perseverance, and 
guarantees the return of some type and degree of Arab solidarity. This per- 
severance is sure to thwart imperialist-Zionist regional plotting. 

All indications point to the danger of an Israeli-American attack against 
Syria, a danger which is virtually certain, because all available facts con- 
firm it. On the one hand, there is the international milieu, in which imperi- 
alist forces have to a certain extent succeeded in mobilizing European public 
opinion along racist, anti-Arab lines. However, we believe that this success 
is only temporary. 

In addition, there is the "Israeli factor," which wants to assert itself as 
a strike force in the region, now that the direct American military inter- 
vention in Libya has shaken things up. Israel relies on this theory, by 
means of which it has been able to attract huge financial contributions from 
Europe and the United States in order to serve its expansionist goals. 

Furthermore, there is Arab fragmentation, which imperialism is trying to 
exploit before the Arabs return to their natural state of cohesiveness and 
nationalist sentiments are rekindled. 

Therefore, we call on all Arab nationalist forces to stand firm with Syria 
in every way, not just through political support but by giving the Syrian 
people and regime all kinds of political, material and moral support, 
particularly since Syria's future will be important in determining the 
future of the Arab national liberation movement as a whole.
