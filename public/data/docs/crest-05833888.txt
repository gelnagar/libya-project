INTENTIONS AND ATTITUDES OF LIBYAN REVOLUTIONARY COMMAND COUNCIL TOWARDS THE WEST, ARAB UNITY, AND THE GOVERNMENT OF LIBYA
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/05833888
https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-05833888

Summary (AI): A 30 October 1969 CIA intelligence cable reports the Libyan Revolutionary Command Council's intentions on Wheelus Air Base, Western arms and oil companies, Arab unity, a new constitution, and government appointments. It says the RCC plans to make Tripoli the capital and to station five or six thousand army and air force troops at Wheelus.
• The RCC will adhere to its treaty obligations regarding Wheelus Air Base and will keep obtaining military supplies and training from the West.
• The RCC will not touch American oil companies.
• The RCC rejected proposals to standardize Libya's military supplies with those of the UAR.
• Union with the UAR is considered totally out of the question by the RCC.
• All political parties will be banned under the new constitution, which was drafted entirely by the RCC.
• Cyrenaica is more peaceful and normal than Tripolitania, according to the RCC's assessment of opposition.
• 1969-10-16: Qadhafi gave a Tripoli speech on Wheelus Air Base; the RCC says the views expressed do not represent the entire RCC.
• 1969-10-24: Editorial in the government-owned daily Al-Thawra, prepared by the Prime Minister's office and not approved by the RCC, criticised the West.
• 1969-10-28: Tripoli Radio announced the Council of Ministers had cancelled the partnership agreement between LIPETCO and the Chappaqua Oil Company.
