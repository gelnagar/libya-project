LIBYA

LIAISON BUREAU EXPLAINS ABSENCE FROM FES MEETING 


LD300300 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 29 Aug 82 


[Text] A source at the Bureau for Foreign Liaison yesterday made the following statement 
on the reason for the Jamahiriyah's absence from the meeting now taking place in Fes: 


The reason for the Jamahiriyah not attending the Fes conference springs from its 
conviction that this meeting does not serve the Arab cause in any way. Indeed, it is the 
final part of a three-sided conspiracy whose participants are the Zionist military machine, 
the U.S. administration and Arab reaction. 


The United States used the Zionist military force to kill Palestinians and Lebanese and 
then tried to institute a political coverup for this barbaric Zionist action by using the 
UN veto and ordering Arab regimes to accept the Palestinians who were driven out of Beirut. 
It was then the turn of reaction to legitimize what the Zionists and the United States 

had done. They chose Fes as the place from which the deliver the final blow to the 
Palestinian issue, this time with an Arab fist in the form of an Arab summit conference. 


V. 30 Aug 82 G2 NORTH AFRICA 


The Jamahiriyah called on the Arabs at the beginning of the Zionist invasion of Lebanon 
to meet to agree on unified Arab action, namely, to repel the aggression. However, they 
kept silent or made excuses and avoided the issue. And now they meet, but for what and 
for whom? The forces of the Resistance were destroyed and their remnants expelled and 
scattered because they opposed the Zionist aggression. This has become a living reality 
and its political effect is being consolidated. The political map of Lebanon is being 
arranged in such a manner as to maintain the agents of Zionism in office. The Zionists 
have now begun talking about expelling those Lebanese who do not swear allegiance to the 
Zionists or their agents. 


The presence of a representative of the Egyptian regime in the lobbies of this con- 
ference confirms that it and subsequent meetings of the Arab kings and presidents are 

a continuation of the Fes summit and the final link in a conspiratorial chain. Any form 
of participation of the progressive Arab states in these meetings would be a blot against 
them and would constitute participation in this conspiratorial chain. 


The Bureau for Foreign Liaison appeals to the progressive Arab states not to take part 
in these meetings, which seek nothing less than a warrant to recognize the enemy, a 
warrant bearing the name of the king of Saudi Arabia. It is the first item on the 
agenda of these meetings. 


AL-QADHDHAFI ON MOROCCAN INVITATION TO EGYPT 


LD292008 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1845 GMT 29 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 29 Aug (JANA) -- The brother leader of the revolution has sent a 
telegram to the heads of state of the pan-Arab Steadfastness and Confrontation Front. 
The telegram concerns the invitation extended by the Moroccan king to the representative 
of the Egyptian regime to go to Morocco to be near the place of the convening of the 
Arab foreign ministers conference, which means the participation of this representative 
in the meetings of the Arab foreign ministers. 


The Moroccan king's invitation also means an attempt to normalize the relationship 
between the Egyptian regime and the other Arab countries as a prelude to making them 
accept Egyptian capitulation as a fait accompli. 


The brother leader in his telegram pointed out that the participation of the foreign 
ministers of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front in these meetings means a viola- 
tion of the resolutions of the Baghdad conference and contradiction of the commitment 
of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front to uphold a complete boycott of the 
Egyptian regime. This boycott is necessary because the Egyptian regime signed the 

Camp David agreements, allied itself with the Zionists and the United States and 
betrayed the Arab nation.

LEADER OPENS REVOLUTIONARY COMMITTEES MEETING 


LD280152 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 27 Aug 82 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution last night opened the fifth meeting of the 
revolutionary committees for accomplishing pan-Arab tasks in confronting the challenge 
to the Arab nation from the evil trio of reaction, imperialism and Zionism. The meeting 
is being held under the slogan: For pan-Arab tasks. This slogan is the aim and the 
action. There is now only once choice before the living forces of the Arab nation -- 

to demolish the dead positions in the pan-Arab body so as to allow the Arab masses to 
surge forward and build a jamahiri popular socialist Arab society capable of imposing 

an Arab existence on Arab territory. 


This emergency meeting is being held at a time when the Arab nation is facing a serious 
threat to its very existence. There is no one better qualified to upset the equation 
than those revolutionary forces who are encouraging the masses throughout the Arab 
homeland to take possession of their resources and make their own decisions. Arab 
reality (?calls) on the revolutionary forces to surge forward to the revolutionary 
action stage, (?burn) reactionary bridges and colonialism and launch a revolutionary 
pan-Arab counterattack to impose the will of the masses, expel the enemy, demolish 

the thrones of reaction and destroy the pawns of colonialism.

becoming president of Lebanon; the involvement of the Saudi rulers in this through their

News agencies tell us of the continued U.S. attempts to impose capitulatory projects

All this rot has been accumulating in the Arab homeland since the complete fall of Egypt

of Lebanon, the extermination of its people and the destruction of its towns and villages, 
schools, and places of worship. 


Zionist preparations are currently underway to slay the remainder of the Lebanese 
patriotic joint movement now that its avowed enemy, the bloodthirsty Bashir al-Jumayyil, 
has been installed as president of Lebanon through a U.S.-Zionist plan ari implementation 
and through Saudi backing in the absence of the pan-Arab progressive revolutionary 

forces and lack of confrontation plans. The Arab citizen desperately needs to keep 

at bay the shadow of terror and fear. He must eliminate the apathy, indifference and 
frustration through which this nation has been living and witnessing. 


Zionist and imperialist brigandage continues in the Arab homeland. Numerous Arab capitals 
will fall after the fall of Palestine, Cairo and Beirut.
