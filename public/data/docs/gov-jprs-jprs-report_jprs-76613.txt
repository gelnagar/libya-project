LIBYAN OFFICIAL INTERVIEWED ON CURRENT CONCERNS 


Palermo GLORNALE DI SICILIA in Italian 8 Jul 80 p 15 


[Interview with Mohamed Tabet, a leader of the Libyan People's Revolutionary 
Committee, by Francesco Marotta date and place not given) 


|Text] During the 1] years which have passed since Colonel Qadhdhafi came 

to power, Libya has undergone profound political, economic and social changes, 
which have made it one of the leading countries of the Arab world and of the 
Third World, The Libyan experiment of an "Islamic socialism" together with 
that form of direct democracy which finds its expression in the People's 
Revolutionary Committees created in 1973 has been viewed with sympathy by 
some elements of the European left which consider it a third way between 
Western capitalism and Soviet socialism. The important role of Tripoli in an 
area of the world such as the Middle East, where there are concentrated the 
most serious points of crisis of the international equilibrium, increases 

the importance of knowing Libya's position on the major problems of the 
moment. This occasion is given to us by the presence now in Sicily of 
Mohamed Tabet, one of the principal exponents of the Libyan People's Revo- 
lutionary Committees, to whom we posed a series of questions. 


[Question] What is the current state of relations between Libya and Italy? 


[Answer] Relations with Italy are good and we are willing to improve them 
by broadening the possibilities for economic cooperation. The presence of 
Italian firms in Libya is appreciated by our people and is considered a 
great contribution to the industrial and agricultural development of our 
country. 


[Question] What is your position on the problem of fishing, which interests 
Sicily particularly? 


[Answer] On the question of fishing, there are no p ‘oblems on our side. 
Everything depends on you. We are ready to negotiate an agreement. In 
Libya, a dozen Sicilian companies are already at work and we would like to 
broaden the possibilities for cooperation, As early as 1977, we signed an 
agreement for cooperation with the Sicilian region. This document has not 
been put into effect and not through our fault, We are still willing and 
interested in implementing it if the regional authorities meet us halfway 
and, if necessary, we are ready to initiate new talks to bring it up to date.

[Question] Italian and European public opinion has been impressed by the 
recent murders of certain Libyan opposition members who were living abroad, 
What is at the origin of these forms of violence? 


[Answer] This is a problem which concerns only the Libyan people, because in 
Libya all the power is in the hands of the people, The people consider the 
opposition members as thieves who have carried abroad the riches of the 
country. Those who have accepted the invitation to return to their home- 
land have not been punished and now they are respected and they live freely 
and have jobs. The objective of the Libyan people is to give complete 
freedom to all. Those who did not accept the invitation to return to Libya 
have been judged by the same Libyan people who requested their physical 
elimination, 


[Question] What is Libya's position on the question of the Middle East in 
the light also of the increased tension in relations with Egypt? 


[Answer] The relations between the Libyan and the Egyptian peoples are 
relations of brotherhood; concerning our relations with Sadat, we are 
strongly opposed to his objectives, which are neoimperialistic, We want 

the Mediterranean to be a peaceful sea, Peace is the prime objective of the 
Libyan people, who want the welfare of all the nations of this area. For 
this reason, we are opposed to the presence of military fleets, especially 
American ones, in the Mediterranean. Sadat wants to grant sea and land 

bases to the United States to dominate the Egyptian people and the Arab 
nations adjacent to Cairo, The American presence in the Middle East and in 
the Mediterranean has the aim of dominating the Arab peoples to exploit them. 
Imperialism is the great disease of the United States which was born in the 
process of chasing from their land and oppressing the Indians of America. 

The same thing happened in the Middle East, with the Palestinian people being 
chased from their land by the Jews who had come from the United States, 
Rurope and the USSR. The Middle Eastern crisis will be resolved when these 
lands are returned to the Palestinians who were born there. Only in this 
way can a Palestinian state be created, a state in which even the Jews of 
Arab origin can live in peace. Israel's objective and that of the United 
States is to destroy the Arab people and we will always be opposed to this 
and we will support the PLO, which only wants to get back the lands which 
belong to the Palestinian people. We consider the Camp David agreements 
useful only to American and Israeli interests and a betrayal of Palestinian 
interests. Now all the Arab peoples have the task of destroying these 
agreements and even the Egyptian people are committed in the struggle to cause 
the fall of the Sadat regime which is the artisan of these agreements. 


[Question] What importance do the rumors of opposition between Libya and 
Arafat, spread recently by certain press organs, have? 


[Answer] We support the Palestinian people in all areas in carrying out their 
objective to liberate Palestine. The reports of opposition between us and 
Arafat are inventions of Israeli propaganda and the proof of the contrary is 
in the welcome which the Libyan people paid to Arafat when he came to 
Tripoli.

[Question] What is Libya's strategy within OPEC? 


[Answer] O11 is an economic asset which concerns all the peoples of the 
world and they must all do their share to protect this asset and to act in 
such a way that it can be used peacefully by all. 


[Question] What is your opinion of the Iranian Islamic revolution and of the 
question of the American hostages? 


[Answer] We consider the Iranian revolution to be a revolution with a pro- 
found human responsibility which aims to increase the freedom of men and 
fight colonialism, The question of the hostages concerns only the Iranian 
people, they alone must decide, 


[Question] What is your position on the Afghan crisis and on the struggle of 
the Afghan rebels? 


[Answer] The Afghan crisis is a problem which concerns only the Afghan people 
who must have the last word. 


[Question] Some European press organs have spoken of Libyan support of 
terrorist groups, including the Italian ones. What do you think of these 
accusations? 


[Answer] These reports are slander against Libya by Israeli and American 
propaganda, as are false the rumors of attempts against Qadhdhafi, who enjoys 
excellent health and is in power with the full support of the Libyan people. 
Libya aids all the peoples struggling for independence from colonialism and 
supplies aid to all the movements of liberation. Freedom, well-being and peace 
for all the peoples of the world are the fundamental objectives of the poli- 
tical revolution,
