LIBYA

Revolution in South Pacific Causes U.S. ‘Panic’ 
LD250903 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 25 May 87 


[Text] Tripoli, 25 May (JANA) — In a commentary reflecting 
the views of the U.S. Administration, the Voice of America has 
stated that Libya has stepped up its efforts to provoke what it 
calls a wave of sabotage in the South Pacific. It also alleged that 
Libya has been training and equipping antigovernment groups 
there. 


Vv. 26 May 87 


Commenting on this, the JANA political editor said 


We are not surprised by the crooked logic of the U.S. Govern- 
ment, which is well-versed in the art of distorting facts and ideas 
Everyone knows the United States has assumed the role of 
defender of the South Pacific and the future of its countries only 
to protect the 500 U.S. military bases scattered throughout the 
region. It is also seeking to tighten its grip over the world’s 
strategic centers, access routes, and the capitalist empire of its 
monopolist firms in the area. The sanie is true of the French 
Government, which falsely raises the alarm about threats to the 
Pacific and its countries. In fact, its true fear is for the fate of its 
15 bases and the future of its nuclear tests in Pacific waters. 
Today France stands on the threshold of its 85th nuclear test. 
While warning of the threat to the Pacific, France fully realizes 
that the only threat is to its imperialist presence in the region, 
and on the island of New Caledonia in particular 


The JANA political editor states that those using their mass 
media and official outlets to spread stories of what they call 
Libyan influence on one occasion, the Libyan danger on another, 
and the Libyan role on a third, are actually showing their genuine 
panic in the face of the great | September revolution’s constant 
instigation of the peoples in the South Pacific to realize the basic 
danger lying in wait for them. In essence, it threatens their waters 
by attempting to confiscate their will, consolidate the old impe- 
rialist presence there, continue the process of transforming the 
region's peoples into rats for nuclear tests, and to use their lands 
as a warehouse for the monopolist octopus of th«ir exploitative 
capitalist firms. 


What increased the horror of this was the immediate and wide- 
spread effect the Libyan revolution caused. The response shown 
by the region's liberation forces to this stimulus was seen in the 
growing popular rejection of imperialist designs and plans. It also 
took the form of a developing revolutionary movement, which has 
begun to seize the initiative on the road to revolution and liber- 
ation. 


It remains for us to state one important fact: Libya's role in 
instigating the peoples of the world toward revolution and victory 
over the instruments of evil and the forces opposed to freedom 
will continue to move forward. This is not the result of a phased, 
tactical stand, it is a firm established principle forming one of the 
pillars of the humanitarian great | September revolution. It was 
sponsored by the Libyan Arab people in the document pro- 
claiming people's power, in which they committed themselves to 
the defense of freedom anywhere in the world.
