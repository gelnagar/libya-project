SALAH KHALAF ON ALGIERS MEETING, NEW ARAB FRONT 
GF131922 Kuwait AL-WATAN in Arabic 12 Feb 83 ppl, 9 
[Dispatch by ‘Amid al-Shanti from Algiers] 

[Excerpts] Salah Khalaf, alias Abu Iyad, Fatah Central Committee member, 
has affirmed to AL-WATAN that the preparatory meetings currently taking 
place in Algiers, including the third meeting of the secretaries general 

of the Palestinian revolution factions, have revealed the depth of democracy 
in the revolution's ranks. The Palestinian revolution is a gigantic revo- 
lution which rejects mandate, he said. 

A Palestinian source has said Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has 
proposed mediation between Libya and Fatah and that he asked PDRY leader 
"Ali Nasir Muhammad to mediate between the two sides. 

The source said the PLO representative in Aden, ‘Abbas Zaki, conveyed this 
proposal to Yasir ‘Arafat, who is currently in Algiers to attend the 16th 
session of the Palestine National Council, which will begin tomorrow, Monday. 

The Palestinian source said there is a proposal to form a new Arab front 
which will include the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front countries and 
some other Arab countries under the name: "The Front for the Defense of 
the Arab World" [Jabhat al-Difa' 'Al al-'Alam al-'Arabi]. 

AL-WATAN has learned that the rightist Lebanese Phalangist Party has begun 
contacts with the Palestinian resistance to lay down the new bases for the 
new Palestinian-Lebanese relations. 

A Palestinian official has held Israel responsible for the explosion of the 
Palestinian Research Center in Lebanon and said this sabotage was aimed 
at exploding the contacts between the Phalangists and the PLO.
