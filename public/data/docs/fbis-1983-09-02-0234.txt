LIBYA

Ceremonies Mark Anniversary of Al-Fatih Revolution

LDO! 2045 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1902 GMT 


| Sep 83 


[Speech by Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of Libyan 
revolution, in Tripoli on | September at celebration parade 
marking | 4th anniversary of Great Al- Fatih Revolution — live] 


[Excerpts] In the name of God. On this memorable day, this great 
historical day, | September, we greet the Free Unionist Officers 
and the valiant troops who brought about the revolution, the 
revolution of the troops who in 1969 that overthrew the reaction- 
ary monarchy and the colonial bases and liberated Libyan terri- 
tory from reaction and colonialism. [crowd chants slogans] 
Greetings to the Free U nionist Officers and greetings to the brave 
troops who created the revolution on | September 1969. 


From the land of the soldiers revolution under the command of 
the Free Unionist Officers, the shackles of the masses have been 
smashed and the free masses surged forward after the Swaia 
speech to undermine the obsolete monarchist, reactionary society 
and to build the Jamahiry society founded on the power of the 
people by means of popular congresses and popular committees. 
The credit — all the credit — for these bewildering historical 
changes on Libyan territory, which have become a world phe- 
nomenon and which laid the foundation stone of the era of the 
masses, belongs to the courage of the Free Unionist Officers and 
ther vahant soldiers [words indistinct} 


On this day we greet the fraternal and friendly delegations which 
came from all over the world to share with the Libyan people 
their celebration of the | 4th anniversary of the Great At-Fatih 
Revolution. At the forefront of the fraternal and friendly del- 
egations we greet our Brother Khouna Ould Haydala, president 
of the Mauritanian Republic. We greet the guests who have come 
from friendly states, from the Soviet Union and from the socialist 
community. | have said that there is a list of fraternal guests from 
the Arab countries participating with you today in this eternal 
anniversary. At the forefront of these Arab delegations is our 
Brother Ould Haydala, and the African guests with our friend 
Ratsiraka at their head, the guests of the socialist community 
with the Soviet Union at their head. [applause] We also greet in 
particular the delegations of the Palestinian people, led by the 
chairman of the PNC. [crowd chants slogans about Palestine} 


Also taking part with us in this tribute to the great | September 
are African youth, thousands of youths from Africa [applause] 
who decided that their youth festival, their great African festival 
on Libyan territory, coincides with the Great Al-Fatih Rev- 
olution anniversary, so that thousands of youths from Africa 
could participate in the tribute to the great | September with the 
Libyan people. [applause] If this means anything, it means that 
the Libyan people enjoy wide support in the African Continent 
and in other world continents. The fact that thousands of African 
youths are present today in Libyan territory is sufficient support 
and backing for the Libyan people from the active force in Africa, 
the force of the future in Africa. It is the youth of Africa who 
will build the future of Africa and draw up the map of Africa. 
They stand today in Libya in support of the Libyan people. 
[applause} 


Free brothers, today, seen and heard by the fraternal Arab 
peoples, especially those at our borders, we feel joy and happiness 
and we celebrate freedom day, the day of emancipation of the 
popular masses, the day of liberation of the Libyan land from 
reaction and colonialism, the day of the building of socialism and 
the power of the people, the construction of the new Jamahiry 
society. While we are celebrating at the Green Sqaure in Tripoli, 
in the Jamahiriyah, we bitterly regret, brothers, that darkness 
should once again engulf dear Egypt. Today Egypt is subjugated 
by the invading American imperialist crusading forces which 
desecrate its land, desecrate the tomb of ‘Abd an-Nasir. The 
American forces today tear up the flags of ‘Abd an-Nasir. They 
desecrate the Arab land of Egypt, which must be liberated, 
brothers. [applause and slogans] 


Brothers, we feel that our freedom is incomplete and that our 
existence is threatened and our dignity humiliated. We are an 
inseparable part of the Arab people from the ocean to the Gulf. 


The battle, brothers, has not ended. What has been liberated is 
but one of the struggle posts; only one part of the occupied 
homeland has been cleansed. Freedom on the Libyan land is 
threatened unless Arab freedom and that of the Arab homeland 
triumph. 


The battle between us and colonialism, brothers, has not ended. 
Before us lie sacrifices; before us lie pan-Arab responsibilities 
that we have to shoulder with patience, sacrifice, and courage. 
This is not an act of volunteering; it is self-defense. When the 
Palestinian people lost their freedom and their land, the Arab 
nation began to suffer, and it has continued to suffer until now 
and will continue to suffer from colonialism which usurped 
Palestine. 


The tragedy did not stop with the Palestinian people who were 
made homeless in 1948; the occupation did not stop at the borders 
of Palestine and [word indistinct] in 1948. Colonialism which 
occupied Palestine has now spread the area of its occupation; it 
occupied Lebanon and it occupied Egypt and imposed capit- 
ulation on them. It is now gathering its strength to destroy and 
tear up Syria and then to proceed across Syria to the Arab east 
and across Egypt to the Arab Maghreb, to destroy the Arab 
existence and replace it with an alien Zionist empire. 


Egypt's surrender harmed Arab confrontation and weakened the 
ability of the Arab nation to confront the enemy. 


Brothers, Egypt has committed the greatest historic sin against 
the Arab nation, and is still committing it, still practicing pros- 
titution. As-Sadat, who sold Egypt and enabled Zionism to be 
established in Egypt and in Palestine, who announced in front of 
the world that “this is the land of Israel and this is the people of 
Israel,” denied Palestine and the Palestinian people. This crime 
was not confined only to As-Sadat, who was doomed and went to 
hell. It was not confined only to Egypt, which surrendered and is 
humiliated. It has hit the entire Arab nation. Egypt, even after 
the death of As-Sadat, is still practicing prostitution, and is still 
practicing national! adultery against the Arab nation. 


Brothers, we say this today for the Egyptian Army on the Libyan 
borders to hear and for the Egyptian people to hear. Egypt has 
committed the greatest sin: Egypt has betrayed the Arab 
nation. The Egyptian people might get upset when they hear me 
say that Egypt has committed political and national adultery and 
iS prostituting itself against the Arab nation. But we say to the 
Egyptian people that if they do not want Egypt to be described 
with these mean words ard dirty characteristics, they must save 
Egypt from the regime of Husni Mubarak, from the gang of 
Husni Mubarak, the gang of Arab [word indistinct} Egypt must 
revolt and break [words indistinct] and burn the land under the 
feet of the invading imperialist American forces which are now 
stampeding across the land of Egypt. 


At the hands of the new fascists who made Egypt into an Arab 
E! Salvador, great Egypt is now a shooting gallery for the 
American forces. American B-52 bombers, with their cight 
engines, come from their bases in America to drop bombs on 
Egyptian land, then they go back. Egypt as a whole is made into 
a shooting gallery, similar to the base of [word indistinct} relative 
to the (Mallaha) base at that time. Egypt as a whole has become 
a shooting gallery for American forces. What greater insult can 
there be for Egypt? American aircraft come from their bases in 
America to drop their bombs on Egyptian land, then they go 
back. This is a restoration of American prestige — the 
Americans, for whom ‘Abd an-Nasir was a fierce enemy who 
made them timid and who prevented them from filling the 
vacuum in the Arab world. ‘Abd an- Nasir said Arab nationalism 
should fill the vacuum, Arab unity should fill he vaccum, Arab 
power should fill the vacuum. We will continue to use the slogans 
of ‘Abd an- Nasir, because we believe that the Arab unity should 
fill the vacuum, that Arab nationalism should fill the vacuum, 
that Arab power should fill the vacuum. 


But now they complain about ‘Abd an-Nasir. When they bring 
their aircraft to drop their bombs on Egyptian land, this is a 
restoration of the prestige of American imperialism. It wants to 
hit and destroy the land from which ‘Abd an-Nasir arose. This 
is what the Americans do. The fascist military gang which has 
made Egypt into an Arab El Salvador must listen to these words. 

These villians who felt no shame after transforming Egypt into 
a company for exploitation, a market for exploitation by imperi- 

alism, also transformed it into a military base. They insulted the 
Egyptian people and the Arab land and transformed it into a 
shooting gallery for American forces.
