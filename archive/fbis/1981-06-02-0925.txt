LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES SFRY DEFENSE MINISTER 


LD011014 Tripoli JANA in English 0845 GMT 1 Jun 81 
{Text] Tripoli, 1 Jun (JANA)--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi last night received General 
Nikola Ljubicic, federal secretary of national defense in Yugoslavia, currently visiting 
the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah.
