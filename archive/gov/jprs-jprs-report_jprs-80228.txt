Paris AL-NAHAR AL-‘ARABI WA AL-DUWALI in Arabic No 236, 9-15 Nov 81 pp 14-16 
[Interview with Sultan Qabus ibn Sa'id by 'Ali Hashim in Muscat; date not specified] 


[Text] Sultan Qabus ibn Sa'id, Sultan of Oman, denied the presence of American 
forces or any other foreign forces on Omani land. In his reply to the questions 
of AL-NAHAR AL-'ARABI WA AL-DUWALI, he confirmed that there was agreement among 
the Gulf states on the contents and the concepts of the Omani working paper on the 
question of security in the region. 


On the eve of the second Gulf Summit meeting, to be held 10 November in Riyadh, the 
sultan stressed that any meeting held between the Gulf leaders, especially when 
held in frankness and with objectivity, is considered an important event. He con- 
sidered the establishment of the tripartite alliance among Libya, South Yemen, and 
Ethiopia as a sign confirming the aggressive intentions and greediness of the 
Soviet Union towards the region. He said that he has continuously supported the 
idea of full cooperation between Egypt and the Arab states. He then expressed his 
support for the proposals for solving the Middle East crisis of Saudi Crown Prince 
Fahad ibn ‘Abdul ‘Aziz "because they embody his concept for a just and comprehen- 
sive peace settlement." 


On the possibility of exchangirg diplomatic representation between the Arab Gulf 
states and the Eastern Bloc states, he said that such exchanges must adhere to the 
principle of peaceful coexistence, which stipulates good neighborliness and non- 
interference in the internal affairs of others. Finally he expressed the readiness 
of the states of the region to mediate between Iraq and Iran "if the parties of the 
dispute ask for it."
