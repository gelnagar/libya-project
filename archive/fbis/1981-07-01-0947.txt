LIBYA

UAE HEAD OF STATE ARRIVES, MAKES STATEMENT 30 JUN 


LDO10100 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 30 Jun 81 


[Text ] Shaykh Zayid ibn Sultan Al Nuhayyan, head of the state of the UAE, arrived in 

the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 30 June. He was welcomed by the brother 
leader of the revolution. On his arrival Shaykh Zayid made a Statement to JANA in which 
he said: We appreciate what brother Col Mu 'ammar al-Qadhdhafi did for the sake of inter- 
Arab cohesion and solidarity so that Arabs may stand as one in both easy and difficult 
times. We hope, Shaykh Zayid added, that the initiative of the brother colonel will be 

a step forward for inter-Arab solidarity, because the Arab nation will recover its glory 
only through solidarity. He expressed hope of seeing all Arabs agree to the brother 
colonel's call because that represents [words indistinct] of the Arab nation.
