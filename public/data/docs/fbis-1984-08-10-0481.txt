LIBYA

U.S. Called Responsible for Gulf of Suez Mines 
LD092308 Tripoli Voice af Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2015 GMT 9 Aug 84 


[Text] The statements made by Abu Ghazalah, the Egyptian 
regime’s defense minister and those made by the U.S. Defense 
Department indicate that it was the U.S. Government that 
planted the mines in the Gulf of Suez and the Red Sea to impose 
its domination over the region and intensify the American mili- 
tary presence there. It also indicates that there is collusion 
between the Egyptian regime and the American Government in 
this respect. On this subject, we present this analysis: 


Reports have been coming about the explosion of a number of 
mines in the Red Sea and particularly in the Gulf of Suez and 
the damaging of 14 ships and the sinking of a Nigerian oil tanker 
which is the [word indistinct] energy. 


Despite the so-called American investigation of these mines, all 
indications point to the fact that the American Government has 
used these explosions to deploy its forces in the Red Sea and the 
Gulf of Suez and consequently to dominate this economically and 
strategically important international sea route because it links 
the Indian Ocean and the Arabian Sea with the Mediterranean, 
and thus is the gateway between the East and West. 


Political observers affirm that the U.S. Government was behind 
the planting of the mines in the Red Sea and the Gulf of Suez in 
preparation for its direct military intervention in this important 
part of the greater Arab homeland on the pretext of what it likes 
to call the protection of international navigation and to consoli- 
date its imperialist designs in the Arab area. 


It has been reported that the Spanish Government has placed the 
military base of Rota, which is near Cadiz in Andalusia, at the 
disposal of the American military forces deployed to the Red Sea

and the Gulf of Suez on the pretext of clearing the mines from 
the region. It has been reported that 4 helicopters and 200 
soldiers are being sent, with other forces to follow shortly to the 
region. 


This is as far as America is concerned. As to the British role, four 
British naval units have moved toward the Gulf of Suez. These 
units had earlier been participating in a NATO maneuver. For 
its part, France has announced it wants to strengthen its presence 
in Djibouti, and hence it was concerned with what was taking 
place in the Red Sea. This French affirmation was made after a 
meeting between the French president and the president of 
Djibouti who is now in France. The newspaper AL-KHALIJ has 
denounced this military preparation by the American Pentagon 
and its Western allies for intervention in this Arab region on the 
pretext of clearing the mines. The paper says that this proves the 
exploitation by these treacherous terrorist powers which often 
engineer crises themselves and try to show themselves as saviors. 


The contradiction that has characterized the statements of the 
Egyptian officials concerning the question of the planting of the 
mines in the Red Sea prove that this operation resulted from prior 
planning by both sides. The Egyptian regime’s defense minister, 
‘Abd al-Halim Abu Ghazalah, has stated that Egypt has not 
reached agreement with the United States over the date of arrival 
of the American minesweeper helicopters. While Abu Ghazalah 
affirmed that Egypt had asked the U.S. Government to send the 
minesweeper helicopters, he said also that there are no mines in 
the Gulf of Suez and the Red Sea. On the other hand, Western 
circles have accused Iran of planting mines in the Red Sea and 
the Gulf of Suez. But the Iranian prime minister has denied these 
allegations, saying that Iran has nothing to do at all with this 
matter. 


There are two factors that affirm that it was the United States 
that planted the mines: First, the planting of mines is an 
operation which requires reconnaissance and sophisticated 
equipment, and second, the United States has a military presence 
in the region through its forces in Egypt. 


The Kuwaiti paper AR-RA‘Y AL-‘AMM has accused the United 
States, the Zionist enemy and the imperialist West of being the 
only beneficiaries of this sordid game in order to escalate the 
situation in the Arab region. It said that an observer cannot but 
pause before the suspect movements of the Western fleets toward 
the Arab region and the Horn of Africa. The United States is 
now repeating in the Red Sea and the Gulf of Suez what it had 
done before in the habors of Nicaragua, where it planted mines 
which led to human and economic losses. That aggressive action 
was overwhelmingly denounced throughout the world.
