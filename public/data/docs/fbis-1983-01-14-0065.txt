LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES PLO LEADERS 12 JANUARY 


LD131047 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0845 GMT 13 Jan 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 Jan (JANA) -- The leader of the revolution received the Palestinian 
resistence unit leaders yesterday evening. They are: Secretary General of the PFLP 
George Habash; member of the PFLP Politburo and official in charge of the occupied 
territory ‘Abd ar-Rahim Falluh; Secretary General of the PFLP-General Command Ahmad 
Jibril; spokesman for the DFLP Fadl Sharur; member of the DFLP Politburo Jamil Hilal; 
Secretary General of the Popular [Liberation] War Vanguards-As-Sa'iqah forces 

"Isam al-Qadi; member of the pan-Arab command of che Syrian Ba'th Party and member of 
PLO Pan-Arab Committee Muhammad Khalifah; Secretary General of the Palestinian Popular 
Struggle Front Samir Ghawshah; and Secretary General of the [Popular] Struggle Front 
"Ali Haytham ‘Abd al-Qadir. 


PDRY DEFENSE MINISTER WELCOMED BY JABIR 


LD131028 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0845 GMT 13 Jan 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 Jan (JANA) -- PDRY Defense Minister Salih Muslih Qasim arrived in 
Tripoli yesterday evening. The commander in chief of the Libyan Armed Forces, Brigadier 
General Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, welcomed the Yemeni defense minister and his accompanying 
delegation on their arrival at Tripoli International Airport. 


Received by Al-Humaydi 
LD141022 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1620 GMT 13 Jan 83 


[Excerpt] Tripoli, 13 Jan (JANA) -- The brother Major al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi, aide 
to the chief of the general staff of the armed forces, received this afternoon brother 
Salih Muslih Qasim, the minister of defense of the PDRY; and the delegation accompanying 
him. After the meeting, the Yemeni minister told JANA that his visit came in the 
context of the strengthening of the fraternal relations between the two fraternal 
peoples of the Jamahiriyah and the PDRY. He said that he was carrying a message from 
President ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, the secretary general of the Central Committee of the 
PDRY Socialist Party and chairman of the Presidiumof the Supreme People's Council of 
Democratic Yemen, to his brother Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the 
Great 1 September Revolution, concerning the situation in our Arab region, especially 
the imperialist-Zionist-reactionary plots to destroy the Palestinian revolution, the 
Lebanese Nationalist Movement, and the progressive Arab regimes. 


SAUDI ENVOY REPORTEDLY TO RESUME DUTIES 
LD131554 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1515 GMT 13 Jan 83 


[Text] Tripoli 13 Jan (JANA) -- The Saudi news agency, quoting a responsible source, 
reports that the Saudi ambassador to the Socialis’ People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
will return to his previous post as soon as is po ble. 


It is known that it was Saudi Arabia which took the initiative of withdrawing its 
ambassador from the Jamahiriyah, which does not believe in the policy of breaking off 
diplomatic relations with states, particularly fraternal Arab states, unless th: point 
at issue is a case of treason or linkage with the Zionist enemy, as happened with the 
As-Sadat regime in Egypt.
