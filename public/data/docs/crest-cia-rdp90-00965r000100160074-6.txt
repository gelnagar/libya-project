Summary (AI): A 1981 Washington Post article, as excerpted in a declassified CIA file, reports on an alleged scheme by American arms dealers to supply Libya under Qaddafi with M16 rifles and ammunition, some reportedly bound for the PLO. The text is garbled by OCR, so details are partial. It concerns Libyan arms procurement, not Tripoli itself.
• Washington Post article dated 27 July 1981, citing a New Yorker account by Edward Loyd.
• Alleged plan to funnel nearly 80,000 M16 rifles and 22 million rounds of ammunition, taken from the American arsenal left behind in Vietnam, to Libya.
• Shipments were to be handed over to the Palestine Liberation Organization.
• The arms deals were valued at a minimum of $23 million and may involve about 230,000 M16 rifles.
• The PBI file reportedly says at least 10 other people were trying to sell arms to Libya.
