LIBYA

GENERAL PEOPLE'S COMMITTEES ASSUME POWER 2 MAR 
LD022225 Tripoli JANA in English 1800 GMT 2 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 2 March, JANA--The thorough and complete establishment of the people's 
authority in Libya has been accomplished as people's committees have replaced today the 
government everywhere. 


The people's congresses which include all the people's masses have replaced the Revolu- 
tionary Command Council, and the people's committees have replaced the Secretariat of the 
Interior which came to an end. Thus one of the governments of the international community 
vanishes by the masses’ legitimacy through the establishment of the Jamahiriyah which means 
the rule of the people. The army will also vanish by the establishment of the armed people. 


With regard to the wealth it has been distributed among the people where the house belongs 
to its occupier and wage workers have been transformed into partners and the land belongs 
to nobody.

JANA EXPLAINS DEMOCRATIC REFORMS 4 MARCH 


LDO41338 Tripoli JANA in English 0920 GMT 4 Mar 79 LD 


{"Authority Separated From Revolution"--JANA headline] 


[Text] Tripoli, Mar 4, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Authority was separated from the revolution 
last Friday when Colonel Mu/ammar al-Qadhdhafi and his colleagues in the revolution!s 
leadership gave up their posts in the General People's Congress to concentrate, with 
revolutionary committees under the leadership of Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of 
the Great First of September Revolution, on revolutionary work amongst the masses to achieve 
the revolution's goals. 


Colonel Mufammar al-Qadhdhafi had earlier said once and again that what had happened on the 
first of September 1969 should not be construed as a mere military coup aimed at seizing 
power..e.. It was not, moreover, a small-scale reform movement, but a revolution to topple 
all worn-out political, economic and social ideas whose downfall was inevitable, and to 
establish new concepts emanating from and conforming to the spirit of the age, which looked 
to the future with honour. 


The principles and goals of the Great First of September Revolution were crystallised in the 
Third World theory enunciated by the revolution's leader in his Green Book of which two 
chapters have been published. The first chapter deals with the problem of democracy, whereas 
the second chapter deals with economic problems. The third chapter, due to be published 
soon, deals with societal problems. 


The solution to the problem of democracy lies in the abolition of various forms of present 
government in the world to be replaced by people's authority. Present forms of government 
monopolise authority or exercise it for the people and in the people's absence while the 
people are relegated to spectator status. This is an insult to the people's dignity and 

a permanent accusation of its (?incapacity) to rule itself by itself and decide upon its 

own affairs as it sees fit, not as its deputies deem appropriate. People's authority, then, 
is the alternative to one man's, a class, a party's, a sect's or a tribe's rule, since this 
means people's self rule through people's congresses and committees.

As for economic problems, these are solved through efforts to destroy all aspects of 
exploitation and monopoly [word indistinct] individuals or classes of society's wealth, 
thus leading to the dicta "land is no one's property" and "the house belongs to its 
occupant" and "partners, not wage earners" and "in necessity lies freedom", These dicta 
will witness the establishment of new socialism which will end all shackles of oppression, 
cruelty, exploitation and political and economic hegemony. Such important issues, which 
transform all established yet outmoded political and economic systems and herald the new 
era of the masses where authority, wealth and arms are in the hands of the masses, calls 
for a continuous effort and ceaseless struggle not at the summit of authority but amidst 
the masses and their broad bases. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi has willed himself to the revolution's cause ever since he was a 
secondary school student. He has now completely devoted himself, with staff Major ‘Abd 
as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, Lieutenant-Colonel Mustapha 
al-Kharrubi and Major al-Khuwaylidp al-Humaydi (members of the former General Secretariat 
of the General People's Congress) and revolutionary committees for revolutionary work 
within the masses agitating them for further exercise in authority and guiding them 
towards the achievement of these great goals. 


As concerns the army, in implementation of the document announcing people's authority, 
defence of the homeland is the responsibility of every citizen. No one person should 
defend another...in order to achieve this end, the process of training all citizens on 
carrying arms is progressing smoothly. At one stage, a society with an armed people will 
be achieved and the traditional regular army will disappear, removing ore of the major 
sources terrorising people, together with authority and wealth. 


Through the process of separating authority from the revolution, all traditional forms of 
rule would have been eradicated in the Jamahiriyah under which the state is split into head 
of government, army and peoplé.e.s. Instead of this, a people directly ruling itself 
through 178 people's congresses would take over, including professional congresses. These 
people's congresses in the Jamahiriyah are the source of all authority, decisions and plans 
governing all aspects of life whether internally or externally, and whether long or short- 
term. 


In implementation of the direct people's democracy theory drawn out and defined by 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi in the Green Book's first chapter, the decisions and recommendations 
of people's congresses are to be transferred through these congress! secretaries to the 
General People's Congress. The General People's Congress is no more than a higher draft- 
ing committee which reviews and coordinates all action referred to it and comes out with 
laws, resolutions and recommendations. People's committees, which run secretariats 
(ministries) and other utilities, will then execute these laws, resolutions and 
recommendations to the letter. By this method, people's committees formed directly from 
the masses of people's congresses, will have replaced the government's administration in 
executing resolutions and recommendations of people's congresses and drafted by the 
General People's Congress.
