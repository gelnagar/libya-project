Meetings With Jabir 
LD221800 Tripoli JANA in English 1644 qmT 22 Jul 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, July 22, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The commander in chief of the armed for- 
ces today received in his office the Turkish Defence MinisterMr Neset Akmandor and his ac- 
companying delegation who are currently visiting the Jamahiriyah. 


The meeting was attended by theGeneral People's Committee secretaries of planning and hous- 
ing and the Turkish ambassador in the Jamahiriyah. 


On the other hand, Brigadier Jabir and Mr Akmandor held a meeting which was attended by 
the Libyan Arab and Turkish delegations. The meeting centered on discussion of topics of 
mutual interest to both countries.

Meeting With Jallud 
Lp202110 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 20 Jul 79 LD 


(Text ] Staff Major Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud this evening met Dom Mintoff, Maltese prime 
minister, who arrived in Tripoli this afternoon for a short visit to the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. The meeting, attended by the brother secretary for liaison, was 
devoted to discussing ways of strengthening the relations of friendship‘and cooperation be- 
tween the two countries, and of a number of current international issues of common interest. 


Dom Mintoff left Tripoli this evening for home. He was seen off at Tripoli International 
Airport by the brother secretary for liaison, the brother director of protocol at the 
Foreign Secretariat, and by the Maltese ambassador to the Jamahiriyah.

LD222206 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 22 Jul 79 ID 


[Text] The delegation of the Portuguese Council for Peace and Cooperation headed by Costa 
Gomes, the former Portuguese president, arrived in Tripoli this evening on a visit to the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah which will contimue for several days. Costa 
Gomes expressed the solidarity of the progressive and democratic forces in Portugal with 
the Libyan Arab people and the just struggle of the Arab nation against the Zionist enemy 
and for the liberation of the occupied Arab areas and the vestoration of the legitimate 
rights of the Palestinian people so that peace and justice will be realized in the Arab 
area,

GUINEAN PRESIDENT TOURE ARRIVES, MAKES STATEMENT 
LD231024 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 GMT 23 Jul 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--Guinean President Ahmed Sekou Toure arrived in Tripoli this morning on 
a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


The senior Guinean guest was welcomed at Tripoli International Airport by Muhammad 
Bilqasim Zuwayy, deputy general secretary of the General People's Congress, a number 
of the brother members of the General People's Congress and a representative from the 
Protocol Department at the Foreign Secretariat, 


The president made a statement to a JANA representative in which he expressed his joy at 
this visit and in which he praised the popular experiment being applied in the Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah, where total power, wealth and arms have come into the hands of the 
people, He also praised the great achievements scored by the Libyan Arab people in the 
shadow of the Great First of September revolution in all political, economic and social 
spheres, He stated: We, in Guinea, are following with great interest and admiration the 
achievements of the revolution in the Libyan Arab Jamaahriyahin various spheres, 


At the end of his statement, the Guinean president conveyed his own and his people's 
greetings to the Libyan Arab people,
