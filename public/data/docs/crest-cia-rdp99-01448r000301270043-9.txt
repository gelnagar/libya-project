Summary (AI): A 1987 Los Angeles Times article reports that NSC staff proposed a joint U.S.-Egyptian invasion of Libya in 1985, which Secretary of State Shultz and others blocked. The plan followed the June 1985 TWA Flight 847 hijacking, which was thought to be backed by Kadafi, and was never presented to Reagan for approval.
• The plan proposed Egyptian troops capturing half of Libya's territory under cover of U.S. close air support, according to officials.
• Joint Chiefs of Staff said the plan could eventually involve up to six U.S. combat divisions.
• Veliotes, then U.S. ambassador in Cairo, was called home to help argue against the plan.
• Mubarak made clear that any Egyptian military action against Libya would be for Egyptian reasons only.
• 1985-06: TWA Flight 847 hijacked by terrorists thought to be backed by Kadafi; a joint U.S.-Egyptian invasion plan of Libya was drafted afterward
