LIBYA

Demonstrations Reported at Jordanian Embassy 
LD182129 Tripoli JANA in English 1845 GMT 18 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada al Awal 14, Feb 18, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — wiasses of Palestinians and other Arabs 
demonstrated here this morning to condemn the visit by the head 
of the Egyptian regime and King Husayn of Jordan to Washing- 
ton and their meeting of conspiracy with the head of the U.S. 
Administration Ronald Reagan to undermine the Palestinian 
cause. 


Demonstrators carried slogans which denounced the stands of 
both the Egyptian and Jordanian regimes accusing them of 
openly conspiring against the causes of the Arab nation and 
bargaining with the Palestinian cause. They also burned the flag 
of Jordan in front of the Jordanian Embassy in Tripoli, in protest 
over this visit undertaken with the purpose of announcing the 
surrender of both regimes to American policies and their accep- 
tance of U.S. plans of conspiracy aimed at undermining the 
Palestinian cause and establishing a pseudo-Palestinian entity on 
the West Bank. 


In a statement issued following the demonstration, the partici- 
pants addressed their condemnation to the heads of both the 
Egyptian and Jordanian regimes for their conspiratorial activi- 
ties in conjunction with the U.S. Administration aimed at imple- 
menting Reaga’s project of surrender. The statement reaffirmed 
that their visit to Washington confirms their yielding to U.S. 
policies and is aimed at extending the scope of the Camp David 
conspiracy t« liquidate the Palestinian cause. The masses also 
warned against the consequences resulting from the adoption of 
capitulationist policies and stressed that the dynamic forces in 
the Arab nation will not stand with hands tied in the face of the 
actions of treason aimed at imposing Zionist-imperialist hege- 
mony over the Arab nation. 


The angry masses called for the downfall of Yasir ‘Arafat and 
for bringing him to trial before the Arab people accused of 
neglecting the Palestinian revolution and the Arab nation, and 
of adopting capitulationist tendencies as well as betraying the 
struggles of and sacrifices of the Palestinian people. 


The statement said: Wecall upon the popular forces and all the 
honorable people in our Arab nation — especially in Palestine, 
Egypt and Jordan — to stand in the face of traitors and con- 
spirators. These forces will follow up all the symbols of treason 
in the Arab homeland as they did with As-Sadat.
