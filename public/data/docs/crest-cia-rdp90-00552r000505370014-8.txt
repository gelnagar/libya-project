It cites the April 5 La Belle discotheque bombing in West Berlin and says the attacks were aimed to minimize Libyan civilian casualties.

• 1986-04-05: Terrorist bomb exploded in a nightclub frequented by American servicemen (La Belle discotheque); Sgt. Kenneth Ford and a young Turkish woman killed, about 230 wounded.
