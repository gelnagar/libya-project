in Arabic 6 Oct 85 pI 

[AL-AHRAM “exclusive” dispatch: “Joint Libyan-Soviet 
Naval Exercises”’] 

[Text] Tripoli — Libyan naval forces have carried out joint 
exercises with the Soviet Navy in the Gulf of Sirte; the exercises 
ended last week. These exercises were the third joint naval 
maneuvers which Libya has carried out with the Soviet Navy this 
year. The first exercises took place last February and the second 
took place last May.

U.S. Abstained on UN Vote To Please Zionists 
LD051943 Tripoli JANA in English 1740 GMT 5 Oct 85 

[Text] New York, Muharram 21, October 5, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The American Administration abstained 
today when votes were taken on a resolution condemning the 
Israeli attack against the Tunisian capital. This was a further 
evidence that America is only concerned with pleasing the Zion- 
ists and realising their expansionist policies in the Arab region. 

The Security Council adopted a resolution by 14 votes in favour 
of the resolution condemning the Israeli aggression against 
Tunis, but the United States delegate abstained. 

The resolution urged the member-states of the United Nations 
to take deterrent measures against the Zionists so as to prevent 
them in future from committing similar acts against the territo- 
rial sovereignty of other countries. The resolution asserted the 
right of Tunisia to compensation for the damage it suffered 
following the attack.
