LIBYA

U.S. FLEET MANEUVERS SAID AIMED AT ‘INTIMIDATION’ 


LD101744 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 10 Mar 81 


[Text] The U.S. 6th Fleet is carrying out maneuvers in the Mediterranean Sea within the 
American plans bent on aggression, adventure, intimidation of peoples and continuing to 
violate Arab rights. 


At present two aircraft carriers and eight naval guard ships in addition to a squadron 

of aircraft to guard the fleet are opposite the central part of the Jamahiriyah. These 
maneuvers come within the framework of the evil American military movement, aimed at 
dominating the region and flexing its power and muscles in order to intimidate the region's 
peoples and subjugate them to its colonial hegemony. 


We recall that last year, and at the beginning of this year, the U.S. 6th Fleet carried 

out a number of provocative maneuvers in the Mediterranean Sea, including the joint 
maneuvers lasting 2 weeks last November with the forces of the Egyptian regime and exten- 
sive naval and air maneuvers on 21 March last year between the islands of Cyprus and Crete. 
Last year America also strengthened this fleet with more military ships, aircraft, equip- 
ment, tanks and members of the Rapid Deployment Force, 


The aim of these provocative Americar moves is to dominate peoples and to bleed them of 
their wealth and resources. At the same time they constitute a fundamental danger to 

the security and stability of peoples struggling for freedom. They are nothing but masked 
interventions in the internal affairs of the countries which reject American colonial 
hegemony and presence close to their territories or coasts. 


AL-QADHDHAFI FOR 'CLAIMS UF PEOPLES,' NOT TERROR 


LD102112 Rome Domestic Service in Italian 1800 GMT 10 Mar 81 


[Text] In an interview to be aired by the second television channel this evening, 
Libyan leader Al-Qadhdhafi again rejects accusations levelled against Tripoli of support- 
ing international terrorism and the Red Brigades in Italy. Al-Qadhdhafi again makes dis- 
tinction between what he terms just claims of the peoples--listing the Basque Fatherland 
and Liberty Group [ETA] in the Basque region as an example--and the purely terrorist 
organizations such as the Red Brigades. Libya, Al-Qadhdhafi maintains, supports the first 
and not the second. As regards the relations between Italy and Libya, Al-Qadhdhafi states, 
among other things, that they could develop considerably if he were to visit our country. 
To organize such a visit, he concludes, it would be enough to agree on the right of the 
Libyan people to compensation for war damage. 


JANA EDITOR COMMENTS ON ECONOMIC AID TO CHAD 


LD101418 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 10 Mar 81 


[Text] British television claims the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has not 
given any assistance to Chad to help it out of its economic situation. JANA's political 
editor has refuted these false claims which the British radio service and other colonial 
organs have been reiterating. He stressed that in addition to the military assistance 
given by the Jamahiriyah, which enabled the people of Chad to triumph over their enemies 
and to confirm their freedom and will, the Jamahiriyah spared nothing from the Chadian 
people, who were torn by the civil war and suffered profoundly from French colonialism. 
Without any doubt, the joint history molded by the blood of the Libyan and Chadian martyrs 
demands that the Libyan Arab people give every assistance to the fraternal people of Chad 
at all times.

Now Libya is participating in the battle of rebuilding and reconstruction in Chad, 
while the colonialist states object to extending any humanitarian assistance to the 
people of Chad and devote their organs and agents in Africa to hostility toward the 
people of Chad and their revolution. 


In waging this battle, the Jamahiriyah has begun to give the necessary assistance to 
rebuild the National Army of Chad, to revive joint economic projects, to set up other 
economic projects in the spheres of banking, agricultural development, stockbreeding, 
industrial development, transportation and communications. More assistance and parti- 
cipation now are being extended to reform the public services. 


In his commentary, the political editor said: The people of Chad now fully realize 
who are their genuine friends, who offered martyrs on their land, and who are the 
enemies who mobilized their organizations and agents to put down the revolution and 
become hostile to them, and who caused the killing of thousands of innocent people 
and the destructions of scores of villages and towns. 


The editor pointed out that Libya's assistance to the people of Chad is not the object 
of out-bidding or [word indistinct] but is a duty dictated by bonds of blood, history 
and religion. The Libyan people will continue to fulfill their duty to strengthen 

the people of Chad and to lead them to future wide horizons and to rebuild life in 

the*r land, despite the misleading propaganda and the colonial doubt-creating campaigns.

PRC MINISTER VISITS TUNISIA--Tunis, 2 Mar (TAP)--"With the continuous strengthening 

of friendly relations between China and Tunisia, the trade between our two countries 
has recorded a rapid development on the basis of mutual advantages and equality" 

said Mr Li Kiang, Chinese minister of foreign trade upon his arrival on Sunday in 

Tunis for an official visit. The Chinese minister said that thanks to the exchange 

of visits and views on problems concerning the two countries, trade between Tunisia 
and China will keep on developing. [Text] [Tunis TAP in English 1407 GMT 2 Mar 81 LD]
