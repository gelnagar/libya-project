LIBYA

REPORTAGE ON REVOLUTION ANNIVERSARY ACTIVITIES 


Military Parade 
LDO20702 Tripoli JANA in English 1718 GMT 1 Sep 81 


[Text] Triploi, 1 Sep (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The Libyan Arab people and the whole 
militant revolutionary forces in the Arab region and the whole world today celebrate 

the 12th anniversary of the Great <l-Fatah Revolution. On the dawn of 1969 the revolutionary 
marching masses set off to topple a shaky monarchy and a puppet regime. 


At the height of the celebrations of the 12th anniversary of the Great al-Fatah Revolution, 
a grand military parade was held this morning at Al-Khadra Square of units representing 

the various forces of the armed people which responded to the U.S. aggression and 

firmly deterred the imperialist aggressors. The military parade was attended by Colonel 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the revolution, and units from the land, naval and air 
armed forces took part in this parade. 


The supreme commander of the armed forces announced the start of the parade at the 
Al-Khadra Square (Green Square) and signalled the start of the parade of the different 
units representing the land, naval and air military academies and detachments of the 
military high schools. Units representing different detachments of T-72, T-62 and 
T-55 tank units took part in this parade, as well as others representing the missiles 
units, war operations, army administration and supplies units and air force formations 
including MIG-23, SU-series of fighter-bombers including the SU-22 and helicopters. 


The military parade was also attended by Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in 
chief of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces; Lieutenant Mustafa Kharubi; Major Khweildi 
Hamidi, commander of urban militarization; the chiefs of staff of the four sections 
of the armed forces; and a large number of officers. 


Present at the parade were the Madagascar Republic president; the Chadian head of state 
and president of the Chadian provisional government, Goukouni Oueddei; chairman of the 
P.L.O. Executive Committee and supreme commander of the Palestinian revolution, Yasir 
‘Arafat; member of the Sandinista National Front Command in Nicaragua, Daniel Ortega; 

the Lebanese National Movement's leadership; the Palestinian revolution's leadership; 
delegations from Lebanon, the Soviet Union, Yugoslavia, Hungary, Czechoslovakia, Bulgaria, 
Afghanistan, Democratic Geimany, North Korea, Romania, Guinea, Mauritania, Rwanda, Turkey, 
Mauritius, Senegal, Benin; and the African youth organization. Also present were mayors 
from the occupied Arab lands, the delegations taking part in the international conference 
of solidarity with the Jamahiriyah people and heads of diplomatic legations accredited 

to Jamahiriyah. 


After the supreme commander of the armed forces gave the go-ahead for the start of the 
great military parade led by the bearers of the masses’ banner and the bearers of the 
armed forces’ banner, magnificently arranged detachments of students from the military 
academy began parading before the main podium followed by detachments of graduates 
from the military academy for girls, students from military secondary schools, 

women volunteers, and infantry and frogmen detachments. These were followed be 
detachments of the air defence, the vanguard of the armed people, and some units of 
commandos and paratroops. 


While this parade of various detachments was going on, formations of MIG-25 and -23 
planes, Ilyushin 39 and Sukhoi planes, Tupolev strategic bombers and helicopters 
were giving impressive aerial displays.

Then followed a parade of the various mechanized units of the armed forces headed by 
mechanized signal units and reconnaissance vehicles while anti-submarine helicopters 
were hovering over the area. 


The parade also included new vehicles equipped with armour-penetrating missiles and 
armour-penetrating portable guns, amphibious armoured transport vehicles, military 
transport vehicles equipped with guns and missiles, rocket launchers, some units of 
self-propelled field artillery, units of the engineering corps and various anti-aircraft 
missiles, high altitude surface-to-air missiles, four barreled anti-aircraft artillery, 
surface-to-surface missiles and long range missiles. These were followed by units of 
the administrative affairs and supply, T-72 and T-62 tanks and other various units of 
tanks conveyed on tanks carriers. 


The Libyan Arab Navy also took part in the military parade and conducted a naval 
display at Tripoli Naval Port. The military parade held in Al-Khadra Square lasted 
4 hours.
