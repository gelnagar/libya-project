Summary (AI): A August 1974 CIA operations center cable reports that Qadhafi, in an interview published in the Beirut and Libyan press, hinted that the US may have prompted Egypt's recent moves against Tripoli. The cable says the remark may cover an embarrassing inter-Arab feud or be a retort to Sadat's claim that Libya was being used in an imperialist plan to divide the Arab East from the Arab West.
• Qadhafi said 'perhaps America or some other party' had instigated Egypt's actions to suit its own purposes; he warned reconciliation with Egypt would then be impossible.
• Sadat had said the week before that Libya was being used unwittingly in an imperialist plan to divide the Arab East from the Arab West, a reference to Tripoli's new cooperation with the Soviets.
• The interview's publication violated the previous week's propaganda truce between Libya and Egypt.
• 1974-08-13: Qadhafi publishes interview hinting at US instigation of Egyptian actions against Libya; cable reports it
