ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS PERMANENT SECRETARIAT ENDS 4-DAY MEETING 
LD101050Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 10 Jan 79 LD 


{Summary] Tripoli--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress concluded 
its 4 days of meeting last night. It passed a number of resolutions, including the 
approval of the report of the secretary general about the activity of the Permanent 
Secretariat during 1978. It also put on record its appreciation of the efforts of the 
secretary general during that period. It underlined the need to increase efforts for 
the implementation of the resolutions of the Arab People's Congress and the Permanent 
Secretariat, and to increase the effectiveness of the Permanent Secretariat in the 
course of the struggle of our nation against the imperialist, Zionist and reactionary 
schemes. It approved the plan of action for 1979 and the timetable for the execution 
of this plan, and it also approved the internal constitution which regulates the running 
of the secretariat. 


The secretary general of the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress, 'Umar 
al-Hamaidi, praised the efforts by members of the secretariat during that period and 
urged the escalation of the struggle at a time when the forces of imperialism and 
reaction were trying to harm the Arab nation and its dignity. 


He stressed that the Stable David agreements are aimed at changing the political map 
in the Arab homeland and the cultural reality in the region. 


Mohamed Lamine, member of the POLISARIO Politburo, attended the final session.

Congress Issues Political Statement 


LD101839Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 10 Jan 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress issued a poli- 
tical statement at the conclusion of its first session for 1979. It stressed that the 

U.S, imperialist -Zionist-As-Sadat plan is trying hopelessly to achieve a new link in the 
links of intrigue against our Palestinian Arab people and Arab nation by signing a separate 
peace treaty and the recognition of the Zionist entity by the agent in Egypt. 


The statement also stressed that all these intrigues hatched by the trio which is hostile 
to our people are an attempt to gain time to push through the new link of intrigue in 
the light of the rising resistance put up by our Arab people's masses and national and 
progressive forces and in the light of the activities of the Steadfastness and Confron- 
tation Front, the rise of the National Joint Action Charter between Syria and Iraq and 
the Arab official unanimity in the Baghdad summit in opposing the two Camp David agree- 
ments. 


The statement lauded the steadfastness of the Palestinian resistance and Arab Syria in 
the face of the imperialist-Zionist-As-Sadat plan. It also praised the struggle of the 
Palestinian resistance against the Zionist enemy and its isolationist allies. The state- 
ment stressed the cohesion of the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 
with the struggle of the Algerian revolution and the struggle waged by the PDRY against 
imperialism and reactionary plans. 


The statement lauded the Arab people in North Yemen, Oman, Sudan and Morocco who resist 
the Camp David plot. The statement warned against the dangerous role being played by 

the agent gendarme, As-Sadat, "in the provocative military operations on the borders of 
the Jamahiriyah to kindle a war between the two regions which will serve only imperialism 
and Zionism," 


The statement lauded the steadfastness of the Egyptian Arab people and their national 
forces which are embarking on attempts to topple As-Sadat!s regime and to expose the 
imperialist -Zionist-reactionary plans in Egypt and which are trying to restore Egypt 
to its leading place at the heart of the Arab nation along the path of the 23 July 
revolution, which the late President Jamal 'Abd an-Nasir exploded. 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress lauded the struggle of the 

Muslim Arab liberation movements in the Arab homeland, the stances of the solidarity by 

the socialist camp and the stance of the political, democratic, trade union and workers! 
organizations in support of the Arab people on all occasions." It also lauded the struggle 
of the national liberation movements in Africa, Asia and Latin America, stressing the unity 
of struggle of the peoples of the world in facing imperialism, Zionism and reaction. 


The statement hailed with pride and affection the struggle of the Iranian people and 
their popular uprising, which is considered a unique picture of the determination of the 
peoples to challenge their enemies to achieve their aspirations in liberation, democracy 
and sovereignty over their resources, 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress pointed out at the end of its 
statement that it has begun to adopt the necessary measures to convene an international 
conference of solidarity with the Arab people to expose and confront the imperialist- 
Zionist plens and to back and endorse the legitimate national rights of the Palestinian 
people in.order to implement the decision of the Arab People's Congress held in Damascus, 
which called for this conference,

ISLAMIC CONFERENCE SECRETARY GENERAL ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 
LD101510Y Tripoli JANA in English 1437 GMT 10 Jan 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, January 10, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The secretary-general of the Islamic 
Conference Dr Amadou Karim Gaye arrived in Tripoli last night on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. 
Dr Gaye was invited in Tripoli by the foreign secretary to discuss the conditions of 
Filipino Muslims, He is due to attend the meeting of the four-party ad-hoc committee 

on the Filipino Muslims and to study the question of the committee on Jerusalem, 


Moreover, he is expected to look into the latest developments of the Arab cause following 
the recent events, in the light of the Islamic Foreign Ministers Conference which was 
held in New York after the signing of the "Stable David" agreements, 


This conference had taken place at the Jamahiriyah's initiative. 


Dr Amadou Karim Gaye gave a statement to JANA's reporter stressing that the "Stable David" 
agreements violated the charter and resolutions of the Islamic Conference. 


"The Islamic community believes that no peace can be achieved without a solution to the 
problem of Jerusalem," he said. 


Dr Gaye pointed out that any unilateral measure to solve the problem of Jerusalem which 
excludes the Islamic countries can never lead to a settlement to this cause, the prime 
cause of the Muslims and "this is a violation of the charter of the organization," 


He stressed that the organization would not accept a solution to the cause of the Palestinian 
people which would contradict its goals or rail to win the approval of all the Islamic 
countries. 


Dr Gaye asserted that the real solution lies in the liberation of all the occupied Arab 
territories and the restoration of the full rights of the Palestinian Arab people. 


Dr Amadou Karim Gaye, secretary-general of the Islamic Conference was received at the 
airport by Foreign Secretary Dr 'Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki; the foreign under-secretary; 
the assistant under-secretary for political affairs; md the head of the Islamic department 
at the Foreign Secretariat.
