LIBYA

LEADERS MEET WITH PLO'S ‘ARAFAT 24 APRIL 


Al-Qadhdhafi Meeting 
LD241414 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 24 Apr 82 
[Text] The brother leader of the revolution today received Brother Yasir ‘Arafat, 
chairman of the PLO Executive Committee. The meeting was attended by Brother Abu Iyad 
{Salah Khalaf], member of the Fatah Central Committee, and Brother Abu Tariq, PLO 


representative in the jamahiriyah. 


Brother Yasir ‘Arafat arrived in Tripoli this morning on a visit to the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


VOP Report 


NC250805 (Clandestine) Voice of Palestine in Arabic 0530 GMT 25 Apr 82 


[Text] Brother leader Abu ‘Ammar [Yasir ‘Arafat] is continuing his official visit, 

started yesterday afternoon, to the Libyan Jamahiriyah in response to an invitation 

from Libya's Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. The brother commander in chief met yesterday 
afternoon with the Libyan president. [as heard] The meeting was attended by Fatah 
Central Committee member Abu Iyad and the brother representative of the Palestine liberation 
organization in the Libyan Jamahiriyah. 


During this meeting, a general review was made of the latest developments in the current 
situation in the area, particularly the recent Zionist air aggression against a number 

of Lebanese areas and the threats and military concentrations in southern Lebanon. They 
also discussed the escalating popular uprising of the masses of our people in the occupied 
homeland in the face of the Zionist military occupation, its aggressive projects and the 
unholy aggression against the blessed Al-Aqsa Mosque. A number of issues of mutual concern 
also were discussed. 


Jabir Reception 
LD241928 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1908 GMT 24 Apr 82 
[Text] Tripoli, 24 Apr (JANA) -- Brig Abu Bakir Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the 
armed forces, today received Brother Yasir ‘Arafat, chairman of the PLO Executive 


Committee. The meeting also was attended by Brother Abu Iyad, member of Fatah Central 
Committee, and by the PLO representative in the jamahiriyah.

TALKS BEGIN WITH POLAND'S SIWICKI 21 APRIL 


LD222238 Tripoli JANA in English 1411 GMT 22 Apr 82 

[Text] Tripoli, 22 Apr (JANA) -- The Libyan-Polish joint talks have started in Tripoli 
last night headed by Brigadier Mustafa al-Kharrubi, general chief of staff of the Libyan 
Arab Armed Forces, on the Libyan side and by General Florian Siwicki, member of the 
Military Council for National Salvation, deputy defence minister and chief of staff of 
the Polish Armed Forces. 


At-Talhi Meeting 
LD222234 Tripoli JANA in English 1633 GMT 22 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 22 Apr (JANA) -- Mr. Jadallah ‘Aziz at-Talhi, secretary of the general 
people's committee, this morning received General Florian Siwicki, member of the Polish 
Military Council for National Salvation, first deputy minister of defence and chief of 
staff of the Polish Armed Forces. They both discussed furthering relations and bilateral 
cooperation in various sectors. 


Delegation's Departure 
LD232326 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 23 Apr 82 


[Text] Gen Florian Siwicki, member of the Military Council of National Salvation, chief 
of staff of the Polish Armed Forces and deputy minister of national defense in the Polish 
People's Republic, and his accompanying delegation left Tripoli this evening after a 
several days visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. The Polish guest 
and his delegation were seen off at Tripoli international airport by Brig Mustafa 
al-Kharrubi, chief of general staff, and a number of officers of the Libyan Arab Armed 
Forces.
