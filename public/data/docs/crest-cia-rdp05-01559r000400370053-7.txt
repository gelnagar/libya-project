Summary (AI): Garbled FBIS/CIA cable transcribing Qadhafi's 10 April 1976 speech at a Misrata rally, where he said the march showed support for the revolution and that disturbances at Libyan universities were a popular revolution settled in favour of revolutionary forces. A second item reports his 11 April Tripoli speech on the Arab Libyan people's strength, though the text is heavily garbled.
• Qadhafi's Misrata speech on 10 April 1976 was reported by Tripoli Arna and carried by FBIS London on 11 April 1976.
• The rally was described as a mammoth procession from several areas of the Republic.
• Qadhafi said that the forces of the revolution had controlled the universities because they constitute the vast majority.
• The second item, dated 11 April 1976 from Tripoli Arna in English, quotes Qadhafi on the Libyan Arab people's strength and on agricultural labour and factory construction; text is garbled.
• 1976-04-10: Qadhafi addressed a popular march and rally in Misrata, saying disturbances at Libyan universities had been settled in favour of revolutionary forces and warning enemies of the revolution.
