Memorandum of Conversation
Washington , June 2, 1976, 9:18–10:00 a.m.

President Ford

Dr. Henry A. Kissinger, Secretary of State

Brent Scowcroft, Assistant to the President for National Security Affairs

[Omitted here is discussion unrelated to Libya.]

Kissinger: Sadat wants to overthrow Qadhafi. [less than 1 line not declassified]

President: They have wanted to for a long time. What do they want from us?

Kissinger: He wants us to screen the Soviets from moving into Libya, aerial photos of Libya, and contact with the Sixth Fleet. They don’t. . . .

[Omitted here is discussion unrelated to Libya.]
