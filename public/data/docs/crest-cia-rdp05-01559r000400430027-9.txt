Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of Qadhdhafi's 7 April 1988 speech at Ra's Jadir on the Tunisian border, where he declared the abolition of borders with Tunisia and opened free movement, followed by Libyan-Tunisian cooperation agreements. The document is mainly about Libya's foreign relations with Tunisia, not Tripoli itself.
• Qadhdhafi declared the abolition of man-made borders between Libya and Tunisia, allowing Tunisians to enter Libya without visa or passport from Ra's Jadir to Ghudamis.
• The agreements cover social welfare, a draft cooperation accord, joint exploitation of mineral resources in the Jarf al-Gari region, and a social and economic protocol.
• The same issue of FBIS-NES reports Egyptian and Saudi news items unrelated to Libya.
• 1988-04-07: Qadhdhafi spoke at the students' revolution anniversary event, declared the abolition of the man-made borders with Tunisia, and had the border gate torn down.
• 1988-04-06: Libya and Tunisia signed cooperation agreements on social welfare, a draft cooperation accord, a mineral resources accord for the Jarf al-Gari region, and a social and economic protocol.
• 1988-04-07: Tunisian ministers Mahmoud Mestiri (foreign affairs) and Habib Ammar (interior) attended the border gate event and met Qadhdhafi.
