LIBYA

Secretariat Urges Strikes at U.S. Interests 
LD101607 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1555 GMT 10 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 10 February — The permanent Secretariat of the 
Arab People’s Congress has affirmed the need for all Arab forces 
and masses to act and to shoulder immediately their responsibil- 
ity for facing up to the flagrant American aggression in Lebanon 
by all means possible, including striking at American interests in 
the Arab homeland. 


This affirmation was contained in a statement issued by the 
delegates’ council of the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab 
People’s Congress following its meeting yesterday evening. The 
statement noted the dangerous escalation of aggression by the 
American Administration against the Arab people in Lebanon, 
using the U.S. naval fleet and air force to direct intense fire,

causing damage and devastation to many Lebanese villages and 
civilians. 


The statement saluted the steadfastness of the Lebanese 
National Salvation Front against the aggression and the great 
victories it achieved, forcing the oppressive American forces to 
flee to the sea. The statement affirmed that the current situation 
in Lebanon demands from all Arab forces and masses that they 
assume their responsibilities immediately and provide full sup- 
port to the Lebanese National Salvation Front and Syria to 
enable them to rout the treacherous American aggression in the 
Arab homeland. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Offers Condolences at USSR Embassy 
LD121541 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
12 Feb 84 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution this morning paid a 
visit to the Soviet Embassy in Tripoli, where he offered his sincere 
condolences on the death of the late Soviet president, Yuriy 
Andropov, general secretary of the CPSU and chairman of the 
Supreme Soviet Presidium. The leader offered his condolences to 
the Soviet ambassador and members of the embassy. He wrote 
the following in the condolences register: 


In the name of God, the compassionate, the merciful: 

Every soul will encounter death. We would like to express our 
deep sorrow, because we have lost another dear friend, the friend 
President Yuriy Andropov, and on this occasion we would like to 
offer the most sincere condolences to our Soviet friends. On this 
sad occasion, we would like to affirm our unwavering determina- 
tion to continue constructing strategic friendship with the peoples 
of the Soviet Union and record our firm resolve to develop 
relations with them in all spheres. We are from God and unto 
Him we return. 


[Signed] Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi
