LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Sees Off Syria’s Al-Asad 
LD271255 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
27 Aug 84 


[Excerpt] Syrian President Hafiz al-Asad today left Banghazi 
after a visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 
The brother leader of the revolution led those seeing off President 
Hafiz al-Asad and his accompanying delegation at Baninah 
International Airport. 


GUNT Official Accuses Habre Regime of Mass Killings 
LD280808 Tripoli Voice af Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
0115 GMT 28 Aug 84 


[Text] The Chadian Transitional Government of National Unity 
[GUNT] secretary of state for combating the crimes of Hissein 
Habre has affirmed that the rebellious clique in N‘djamena has 
been carrying out campaigns of mass annihiliation of the people 
of southern Chad. It has distributed poisoned food, killing hun- 
dreds of them. It also practices methods of starvation and repres- 
sion against them. He pointed out that these terrorist campaigns 
are being carried out on the orders of French imperialism in 
revenge for the southern people’s support of the legitimate gov- 
ernment. He also pointed out that the Habre clique has been 
stealing aid sent to the Chadian people.
