Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery briefing board reports that a new airfield is under construction at Bani Walid in northwest Libya, about 75 nautical miles southeast of Tripoli. It is described as the sixth major airfield under construction in Libya.
• Runway approximately 3,000 meters long, expected to be hard-surfaced and oriented north-northeast/south-southwest once complete.
• Footings for a possible hangar lie at the east-southeast edge of the runway; a rock crushing plant is southeast of the airfield.
• Other major airfields under construction in Libya at the time: Maktura, Matan as Sarra, Al Wigh, Al Jufrah, and Ghadames East.

AIRFIELD CONSTRUCTION 
BANI WALID AIRFIELD. LIBYA 

25XK1 

1. (S/D) SIGNIFICANCE: A SIXTH MAJOR AIRFIELD IS UNDER 
CONSTRUCTION IN LIBYA. 

2. (S/D) REMARKS: A NEW AIRFIELD, BANI WALID AIRFIELD 
(GRAPHIC T OF 2), WITH A RUNWAY APPROXIMATLEY 3,000 METERS 
LONG, IS UNDER CONSTRUCTION IN NORTHWEST LIBYA. THE AIRFIELD 
IS IN THE EARLY STAGES OF CONSTRUCTION AND WILL PROBABLY HAVE A 
HARD-SURFACED, NORTH-NORTHEAST/SOUTH-SOUTHWEST RUNWAY WHEN 
COMPLETED. FOOTINGS FOR A POSSIBLE HANGAR ARE AT THE 
EAST-SOUTHEAST EDGE OF THE RUNWAY. A ROCK CRUSHING PLANT IS 
SOUTHEAST OF THE AIRFIELD. 

(S/D) THE AIRFIELD IS AT THE SOUTHWEST EDGE OF BANI WALID AND 
APPROXIMATLEY 75 NM SOUTHEAST OF TARABULUS. THIS IS THE SIXTH 
KNOWN MAJOR AIRFIELD PRESENTLY UNDER CONSTRUCTION IN LIBYA 
(GRAPHIC 2 OF 2). AIRFIELDS, IN ADDITION TO BANI WALID, ARE 
UNDER CONSTRUCTION AT MAKTURA, MATAN AS SARRA, AL WIGH, 

AL JUFRAH, AND GHADAMES EAST.
