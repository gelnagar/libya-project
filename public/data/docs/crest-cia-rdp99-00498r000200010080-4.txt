Summary (AI): A 1980 New York Times article reprinting conclusions of a Senate subcommittee report on Billy Carter's dealings with Libya. It says Libyan officials cultivated him with trips, an oil commission arrangement, and about $220,000 in payments, aiming to benefit Libyan policy and gain influence.
• Libyan payments to Billy Carter totaled $220,000 according to the subcommittee.
• Billy Carter's oil commission arrangement was with the Charter Crude Oil Company.
• The Department of Justice rejected the view that the Charter arrangement was an ordinary commercial deal.
• The Foreign Agents Registration Act was applied in this case to require public disclosure.
