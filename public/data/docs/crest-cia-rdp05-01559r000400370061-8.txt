[Text] The leader of the revolution, Brother Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, tonight 
attended a huge popular rally which was held at Al-Asabi!' to commemorate the famous 
Jandubah battle waged by our ancestors against the legions of the fascist Italian 
invasion, ; 

When the leader of the revolution arrived at the ceremony, the huge crowd which had 
leen gathering there since early morning received him with resounding cheers for the 
continuity of the revolution and the leader in order to achieve the aims of the masses 

in freedom, socialism and unity. 

Amid the cheers of the masses for the long life of the great revolution and its leader 
and detonator, Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the revolution deli- 
vered a speech, At the beginning of the speech he paid homage to the souls of the 
martyrs of the Libyan Arab struggle and said: The immortal battle of Janduban remained 
forgotten for over 60 years, until the decisive battle for the Libyan Arab people's 
freedom took place. This decisive battle is the Al-Fatih [1 September] battle which 
recalled the Jandubah battle and other battles of the Libyan struggle. 

He asserted that the great Al-Patih is considered a new opening for freedom, a new 
opening for industrialization, agrarian reform and progress in this land. He said: 
There are scores of battles like Jandubah in which our fathers and forefathers fought 
and were martyred--battles which were concealed and deliberately forgotten by the 
defunct regime, which indicates that the battles of Al-Jihad were activated only after 
the great Al-Fatih. 

He contended that there was a conspiracy against the existence and history of this 
people--a conspiracy which was engineered and led by the vagabonds in the defunct 
regime whose era came to an end after the morning of the great Al-Fatih dawned. He 
said that concealing the battles of Al-Jihad in the defunct regime means that the 
Idbyan people were not free to express their existence and history. He added that 
the masseS who are today commemorating the Jandubah battle are the masses who pro- 
ceeded freely after the great Al-Fatih to confirm their presence and to reformulate 
their history. 

At the end of his address, the leader of the revolution saluted the masses of the 
Libyan Arab people in Ghiryan and the neighboring areas who, by commemorating the 
the battles of Al-Jihad, confirmed their conscious commitment to the history of our 
people and the procession of their struggle along the course of freedom, socialism 
and unity.
