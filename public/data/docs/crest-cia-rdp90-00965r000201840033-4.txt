Summary (AI): A 1985 New York Times article reports that President Reagan approved a covert CIA plan to weaken Qaddafi's regime, which House Intelligence Committee members questioned and which Reagan ordered investigated after leaks. It is foreign-policy background with no specific places in Tripoli.
• Reagan approved a covert plan aimed at weakening the regime of Col. Muammar el-Qaddafi, according to Congressional sources and Administration officials.
• The House Intelligence Committee had questioned the plan, which reportedly called for the CIA to help countries opposing Qaddafi.
• The Administration was reported to have told Libyan diplomats in 1981 to leave Libya, after which American companies and personnel were advised to leave.
• An executive order issued by Reagan bars the CIA from engaging in assassinations.
• Reagan ordered an investigation into the release of secret intelligence reports on Qaddafi quoted in The Washington Post account.
