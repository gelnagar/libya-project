Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a February 1990 interview in Egypt's Al-Ahram, in which Qadhdhafi discusses water scarcity, Israeli control of regional rivers, Egyptian-Libyan rapprochement and the Jamahiriya system. It has no places, events or people in or near Tripoli.
• Qadhdhafi said the 'current battle is a battle for water' and warned of Israeli control of the Jordan, Litani and Yarmuk rivers.
• He described the Jamahiriya as government by the masses, with members of the basic people's organisations from age 18.
• Libya and Tunisia had removed their border, with free movement without passports, per Qadhdhafi.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya and Egypt were one people divided by colonialism.

IS REPRESENTED BY THE SHORTAGE OF WATER IN MANY AREAS IN THE WORLD. 
THIS SERIOUS OBSTACLE MIGHT AFFECT THE LIFE OF MANY PEOPLES IN THE

CURRENT BATTLE IS A BATTLE FOR WATER IN THE FIRST PLACE. MEANWHILE, 
THE ARABS TALKED ABOUT POLITICAL, MILITARY, AND FEDAYEEN BATTLES. I 
TOLD THEM THAT THE MILITARY ISRAELI ACTIVITIES IN THE REGION ARE

HEART OF AFRICA; IT CAN BE CONTROLLED IN ETHIOPIA, UGANDA, AND 
SOUTHERN SUDAN. IN FACT, THE ISRAELIS ARE EMBARKING ON ATTEMPTS IN 
ETHIOPIA AND UGANDA. WE RECALL THAT DURING OBOTE'S RULE, THE 
ZIONIST CONTROL WAS VERY STRONG IN UGANDA. THE OBJECTIVE IS TO 
SUFFOCATE EGYPT. 

I ALSO SAID IN MY LECTURES AT THE LIBYAN UNIVERSITIES THAT THEY 
((THE ISRAELIS)) WILL FEAR EGYPT DESPITE ITS PROFESSION OF PEACE. 
THEY REALIZE THAT DANGER WILL COME FROM EGYPT, EVEN IN THE NEXT 
GENERATION. CONSEQUENTLY, THEY WANT TO LAY HANDS ON THE NILE 
SOURCES TO THREATEN THE LIVES OF THE EGYPTIANS. THIS MEANS THAT THE 
SOURCE THE NILE, WHICH IS THE LIFEBLOOD IN EGYPT AND SUDAN, IS NOT 
IN OUR HANDS AND CAN BE CONTROLLED FROM OUTSIDE THE ARAB HOMELAND. 
AND THE TIGRIS AND THE EUPHRATES, ON WHICH IRAQ AND SYRIA THRIVE, 
ARE NOT IN OUR HANDS; THEY ARE IN THE HANDS OF TURKEY. AND TURKEY 
MIGHT BE OCCUPIED BY A HOSTILE FORCE; AND POSITIONS MIGHT CHANGE. A 
PROBLEM EMERGED FROM THE ATATURK DAM THAT STOPPED THE FLOW OF WATER 
TO SYRIA AND IRAQ. THIS SHOWS THAT THESE RIVERS ARE NOT IN OUR 
HANDS. THE BUILDING OF THE ETHIOPIAN DAM, THE ZIONIST AMBITIONS IN 
THE NILE, AND THE PROBLEM IN SOUTHERN SUDAN AIM AT CONTROLLING THE 
NILE SOURCES. AND THE ISRAELIS ARE NOW PUMPING WATER DAY AND NIGHT 
FROM THE JORDAN RIVER TO THE NEGEV AND PUMPING AL-LITANI INTO THE 
AL-HULAH AND LAKE TIBERIAS. I ASKED THE JORDANIANS, AND THEY SAID 
WE CANNOT EXPLOIT THE JORDAN RIVER. THE ISRAELIS HAVE COMPLETELY 
CONTROLLED IT. THE BATTLE IS A REAL BATTLE FOR WATER. NOW, AFTER 20 
YEARS, THE ARABS HAVE REALIZED THE REALITY OF THE BATTLE AND WE ARE 
BEGINNING TO FEEL THE SAME ARAB DESTINY. 

PRAISE BE TO GOD, THE MENTALITY HAS CHANGED NOW. HOWEVER, I AM 
AFRAID THAT ARAB OASES WILL DRY UP IN THE ARABIAN PENINSULA, 
SOUTHERN EGYPT, LIBYA, AND SUDAN BECAUSE THEY MIGHT BE USED UP 
QUICKLY, PARTICULARLY IF FOREIGN COMPANIES ARE PRESENT IN THESE 
OASES. SUCH COMPANIES WANT TO MAKE PROFITS QUICKLY; THEY DO NOT 
CARE ABOUT THE FUTURE. 

I ASKED THE ARAB AGRICULTURE MINISTERS WHEN THEY MET IN LIBYA TO 
SAFEGUARD THE ARAB OASES BY USING WIND-DRIVEN PUMPS RATHER THAN 
CONVENTIONAL ENGINES, AND BY KEEPING FEUDALISM AND FOREIGN COMPANIES 
AWAY FROM THESE OASES IN ORDER TO PREVENT THE USE OF ONLY ONE WELL 
IN IRRIGATING MANY FARMS.

((TEXT)) IBRAHIM NAFI': IN SIMPLE WORDS, HOW DOES BROTHER COL. 
AL-QADHDHAFI EXPLAIN THE LIBYAN TERM “JAMAHIRIYAH"? 

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: IT MEANS GOVERNMENT BY THE MASSES. ANYONE 
BECOMING 18 YEARS OLD, WHETHER MAN OR WOMAN, BECOMES A PARTNER IN 
POWER; THAT IS, BECOMES A MEMBER IN THE BASIC PEOPLE'S ORGANIZATION. 
HE WILL BE THE DECISIONMAKER AND THE MASTER. LAWS IN LIBYA ARE 
ISSUED BY THE ENTIRE LIBYAN PEOPLE. THE JAMAHIRIYAH BASIC PEOPLE'S 
CONGRESSES MEAN THAT THE STATE IS RUN BY THE MASSES, THAT POWER 
COMES FROM THE MASSES, AND THAT THERE IS NO POWER ABOVE THAT OF THE 
PEOPLE AND THE PEOPLE'S CONGRESSES AND COMMITTEES. IT IS USEFUL 
THAT THE FOLLOWING SLOGANS APPEAR IN THE ARABIC DAILY AL-AHRAM

((TEXT)) IBRAHIM NAFI': HOW ABOUT THE PERFORMANCE OF THE 
MINISTERIAL COMMITTEE FOR DEVELOPMENT PLANNING, WHICH WAS FORMED 
LAST MONTH? 

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: IT IS PERFORMING ITS DUTIES SUCCESSFULLY. 

IBRAHIM NAFI': WHAT ABOUT THE OUTCOME OF THE RECENT TALKS 
BETWEEN YOU AND PRESIDENT MUBARAK? CAN IT BE CONSIDERED A NEW STEP 
FORWARD? 

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI:; CERTAINLY. 

IBRAHIM NAFI': DO YOU EXPECT THIS STEP TO PRODUCE CLEAR, 
IMMEDIATE, AND EFFECTIVE RESULTS FOR THE TWO PEOPLES? 

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: THE SERIOUS WORK WHICH MAKES THE FUTURE MAY 
NOT PRODUCE SPEEDY DIVIDENDS. HOWEVER, THERE ARE ACCOMPLISHMENTS 
WITH IMMEDIATE RESULTS, SUCH AS THE OPENING OF BORDERS, MOVEMENT OF 
COMMODITIES AND PERSONNEL, AND RESIDENCE. THIS DEPENDS ON DEMAND 
AND SUPPLY IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE NEEDS OF THE TWO COUNTRIES. WE 
AGREED TO FREE MOVEMENT. THIS PRACTICE HAS SPREAD THROUGHOUT THE

IBRAHIM NAFI': BUT IS THERE AN AGREEMENT OVER STRATEGIC ACTION? 

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: YES. 

TBRAHIM NAFI'; FRANKLY, HOW DO YOU VIEW THE FUTURE OF THE 
CURRENT UNDERSTANDING AND RAPPROCHEMENT BETWEEN EGYPT AND LIBYA? 
PERHAPS, AS MUCH AS THE AVERAGE MAN IS WATCHING THESE DEVELOPMENTS

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: THIS WAS POSSIBLE IN THE PAST. HOWEVER, I 
BELIEVE THAT WE ARE EXPERIENCING A STAGE IN WHICH WE HAVE MANY 
LESSONS TO LEARN FROM. WE HAVE BECOME FED UP WITH EXPERIENCES AND 
FLUCTUATIONS. WE EVEN FOUGHT EACH OTHER IN AN IMPROPER MANNER. 
PERHAPS WE CAN SEE OUR HARVEST FROM THE FLUCTUATIONS, SURPRISES, AND 
PROBLEMS. WE REAPED NOTHING BUT THE WASTE OF TIME, SCORN OF OTHERS, 
BACKWARDNESS, FAILURE OF ACHIEVEMENTS, AND ALL THAT HAS BEEN 
ACCOMPLISHED HAS BEEN RUINED. MEANWHILE, THE ENEMY IS WATCHING US 
HAPPILY. WE HAVE GROWN UP AND BECOME MATURE. 

IBRAHIM NAFI': BROTHER COLONEL, I BELIEVE WE HAVE COVERED ALL 
RAISED QUESTIONS AND QUERIES. DO YOU HAVE ANYTHING TO SAY TO THE 
EGYPTIAN PEOPLE? 

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: FIRST, I WANT TO THANK THE EGYPTIAN PEOPLE 
FOR THE HEARTFELT WELCOME IN WHICH ALL THE PEOPLE OF ASWAN TOOK 
PART. THEY SHOUTED MY NAME, AND DID NOT FEEL I WAS A STRANGER AMONG 
THEM. WE BELIEVE WE ARE ONE PEOPLE WHO HAVE BEEN DIVIDED BY 
COLONIALISM INTO LIBYA, WHICH WAS OCCUPIED BY THE ITALIANS, AND 
EGYPT, WHICH WAS OCCUPIED BY THE ENGLISH. HOWEVER, WE THE LIBYANS 
AND EGYPTIANS WILL REMAIN ONE PEOPLE. IT WAS THE ITALIANS WHO 
INSTALLED THE BARBED WIRES. REGRETTABLY, FOR A WHILE WE RESPECTED 
THE WISH OF THE COLONIALISTS. WE SHOULD HAVE BROKEN AND DEMOLISHED 
IT. I TELL OUR BROTHERS IN EGYPT: WE ARE THE SONS OF ONE NATION. 
LIBYA IS AN EXTENSION OF EGYPT, AND EGYPT IS AN EXTENSION OF LIBYA. 
THE UNITY OF SOIL AND PEOPLE IS INEVITABLE. THIS IS NOT OPTIONAL. 
ANY PROBLEMS RAISED BETWEEN US ARE DOOMED TO FAILURE. ONLY OUR 
UNITY WILL TRIUMPH. I THANK AL-AHRAM FOR MAKING THIS OPPORTUNITY 
AVAILABLE TO ME.
