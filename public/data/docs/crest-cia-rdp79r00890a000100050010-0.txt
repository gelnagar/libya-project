• Libya was a federation of Tripolitania, Cyrenaica and the Fezzan, consolidated by the United Nations in December 1951.
• The government depended on British financial assistance.
• The king had no male heir.
• The US had no formal agreement to use Wheelus and had paid no rental fee.
• 1953-06: King Idris summarily removed three top administrators the previous week, and Prime Minister Mahmoud Muntasser was reported on the verge of resignation.
• 1953-03-28: Libya was admitted to the Arab League.
