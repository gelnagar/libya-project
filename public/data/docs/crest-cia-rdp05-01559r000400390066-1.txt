Summary (AI): An FBIS transcript of a March 1985 Qadhdhafi anniversary speech, broadcast by Tripoli domestic radio, in which he praises the Hizbullah-era pressure that pushed Israel and the US out of Lebanon and calls for an armed people in place of regular armies. It is Libyan foreign-policy rhetoric with no specific places or events in Tripoli.
• The speech was broadcast by Tripoli domestic radio and reported by FBIS London on 29 March 1985.
• Qadhdhafi calls for an armed people to replace the regular army and for a people's liberation war against Zionism and imperialism.
• The speech calls for resistance from South Lebanon to South Palestine under the slogan 'Liberation from the South to the South'.
• The speech attacks Arab rulers as failed and 'agent regimes' and urges incitement of Arab masses from the ocean to the Gulf.

((TEXT)) THE ISRAELIS SAID ARROGANTLY THAT THEY WOULD 
NOT GET OUT OF LEBANON WITHOUT IMPOSING CONDITIONS WORSE THAN THE 
CONDITIONS OF STABLE DAVID. THEY WERE ESCALATING WITH EGYPT AS 
A BASE, AND THEY WANTED TO FORCE INSULTS AND OTHER 
HUMILIATING AGREEMENTS ON THE ARAB NATION, ONE COUTNRY AFTER ANOTHER. 
BUT THEY WERE STOPPED BY THE PEOPLE'S REVOLUTION AND THE POPULAR 
RESISTANCE. THEY WERE FACED BY PEOPLE WHO ARE FIGHTING BECAUSE THEY 
WANT TO, AND NOT AN ARMY WHO TAKES ORDERS. THEY SAID THEY 
WOULD NOT GET OUT WITHOUT IMPOSING A NEW HUMILIATION TO BE ADDED 
TO THE HUMILIATION OF STABLE DAVID WHICH NOW COVERS EGYPT 
FROM HEAD TO FOOT. THE ISRAELIS ARE NOW RUNNING AWAY FROM 
SOUTH LEBANON AND THE WORLD KNOWS THEY ARE COWARDS. 
BUT WE ARE NOT ONLY REVIEWING EVENTS; WE ALSO WANT TO LEARN. 
THE LESSON IS THAT AMERICA WAS KICKED OUT OF LEBANON WHEN AN 
INDIVIDUAL ARAB WAS ABLE TO KILL 300 AMERICANS. THEY MADE 
SOME CALCULATIONS IN THE PENTAGON, AND SAID: ONE PERSON IN A 
CAR FULL OF EXPLOSIVES WILL DIE, BUT 300 AMERICANS WILL ALSO 
DIE. THEREFORE, WE CANNOT RESIST THIS NATION. WHAT RESTORED 
THEIR REASON WAS THAT WHEN ONE ARAB DIED, 300 AMERICANS DIED WITH 
HIM. BEFORE THAT THERE WERE SCANDALS. AN ISRAELI POW FROM THE 
ISRAELI REGULAR RMY WAS EXCHANGED FOR 1,000 POWS OF THE CLASSICAL 
ARAB ARMY. AMERICA REGAINED ITS REASON BECAUSE OF THE STRIKES 
IT RECEIVED IN LEBANON. THIS IS WHAT RESTORED ITS REASON. IT 
WITHDREW IT FORCES LIKE LOCUSTS BY HELICOPTERS, AND SOME OF 
THEM HAD TO SWIM IN THE SEA IN ORDER TO REACH ONE OF THE NAVAL 
VESSELS. THIS IS AMERICA, THE SUPERPOWER | 
THIS GIVES US CONFIDENCE IN OURSELVES AND IN THE JAMAHIRIYAH 
THEORY. AN ARMED PEOPLE WILL NEVER BE DEFEATED, BUT 
REGULAR ARMIES ARE UNRELIABLE. ((CHEERS) ) 
CONSEQUENTLY, IT IS THE DUTY OF THE LIBYAN MILITARY TO TRAIN THE 
PEOPLE IN THE USE OF ARMS SO THAT AN ARMED PEOPLE MAY COME 
[NTO BEING AND THE REGULAR ARMY MAY DISAPPEAR. WE CANNOT CONFRONT 
AMERICA WITH A LIBYAN REGULAR ARMY, BUT WE CAN CONFRONT IT WITH 
AN ARMED PEOPLE. AN ARMED PEOPLE CANNOT BE DEFEATED. THE ISRAELIS 
WENT MAD, THE AMERICANS WENT MAD; THEY USED ALL THEIR MEANS; THEY 
USED ALL THEIR MEANS OF TOTAL DESTRUCTION TO BRING THE PEOPLE OF 
LEBANON TO THEIR KNEES AND TO HUMILIATE THEM BUT THEY HAVE FAILED 
AND IN THE END THEY HAVE FLED FROM LEBANON. 
AND SO WE MUST LEARN THIS LESSON AND ADOPT AS A RULE THE 
JAMAHIRIY THEORY AND A PEOPLE'S LIBERATION WAR BY AN ARMED 
PEOPLE. WE WILL CONFRONT ZIONISM AND IMPERIALISM WITH AN ARMED 
PEOPLE. AS FOR THE REGULAR ARMIES, THEY LEFT THEIR PLACE IN THE 
RESISTANCE IN HUMILIATION AFTER THE DEFEATS THEY SUFFERED. 
SO MANY WARS WERE WAGED BY ARAB REGULAR ARMIES AND THE RESULT 
WAS DEFEAT. AND NOW HERE IS A THE FIRST WAR WAGED BY A SMALL ARAB 
PEOPLE WITH MODEST WEAPONS, BUT THEY DEFEATED THE ZIONIST WAR 
MACHINE THAT BROUGHT FEAR TO THE ARAB REGULAR ARMIES; CEASE-FIRE 
WAS IMPOSED ON THESE ARMIES AFTER 2 OR 3 DAYS BECAUSE THEY 
COULD NOT CONTINUE THE WAR. THE ISRAELIS ARE NOW BEGGING THE 
WORLD, SAYING: WE WANT TO STOP THE FIGHTING, BUT THE ARMED PEOPLE 
SAY NO. THERE [S NO END TO THE FIGHTING.

WHAT IS REQUIRED NOW IS CONTINUAL RESISTANCE FROM THE SOUTH 
OF LEBANON TO THE SOUTH OF PALESTINE; IT MUST NOT STOP, MONTH 
AFTER MONTH, YEAR AFTER YEAR, UNTIL COMPLETE LIBERATION. 
((APPLAUSE)) OUR SLOGAN NOW, THE SLOGAN THAT WE MUST HOLD ALOFT 
NOW, IS: LIBERATION FROM THE SOUTH TO THE SOUTH| UNDER THIS 
SLOGAN WE MUST BRING OUR WEAPONS AND OUR FIGHTING FORCES, ALL OUR 
DYNAMIC RESOURCES INTO THIS GLORIOUS BATTLE. 
AS FOR THE ARAB RULERS, THEY ARE ALSO LIKE THE CLASSICAL ARMIES. 
THEY HAVE REGISTERED THEIR END; THEY HAVE NOTED THAT THEY HAVE 
FAILED, THAT THE GOVERNMENTAL SYSTEM HAS FAILED; THE ARAB RULERS 
OF VARIOUS KINDS -- FROM THE INHERITANCE TO THE. FAMILY AND 
TRIBE -- HAVE FAILED. THOSE WHO SAYS THE INHERITED SYSTEM IS BETTER, 
HAVE AN INHERITED SYSTEM; THOSE WHO SAY THAT THE PRESIDENTAL, 
THE REPUBLICAN, OR THE ELECTED SYSTEM IS BETTER, HAVE SUCH A 
REPUBLICAN SYSTEM; THOSE WHO SAY THE SULTANATE IS BETTER, 
HAVE A SULTANATE SYSTEM; THOSE WHO SAY SHAYKHDOMS, HAVE GOT 
SHAYKHDOMS; THOSE WHO SAY A PRINCEDOM ((IMARAH)) IS BETTER, THANKS 
BE TO GOD HAS A PRINCEDOM. ALL KINDS ARE AVAILABLE; THEY HAVE 
ALL FAILED. THEY COULD NOT MAKE ANY PROGRESS IN DEVELOPMENT NOR IN 
LIBERATION. IN THE END THEY RUN TO THE AMERICAN WHITE HOUSE AND 
SAY BEFORE THE WORLD: HERE I AM AT YOUR SERVICE. THE PLACE OF 
PILGRIMAGE HAS BECOME THE AMERICAN WHITE HOUSE AND NOT MECCA. THIS 
MECCA IS A DECEPTION BUT THEY GO TO IT; IT IS FOR SIMPLE MINDED 
PEOPLE. FOR THE ARAB RULERS THEIR PLACE OF PILGRIMAGE GAS 
BECOME THE AMERICAN WHITE HOUSE. 
THE RULERS HAVE NOW BEEN EXPOSED, SO THEY MUST HIDE. 
WE MUST NOW MAKE PREPARATIONS AND INCITE THE ARAB MASSES FROM THE 
OCEAN TO THE GULF, SO THAT THEY WILL BURY THESE ROTTEN BODIES 
LIVING AMONG US, AND WHICH BROUGHT NOTHING TO THE MASSES BUT SHAME 
AND DISGRACE BEFORE THE WORLD. THESE ROTTEN AND AGENT REGIMES 
DESERVE EVERY HUMILIATION AND DISCREDIT. IT IS AN IMPUDENCE FOR 
THESE REGIMES TO REMAIN IN POWER AFTER HAVING NOTED THEIR HISTORIC 
END. THEY HAVE FAILED HISTORICALLY ON ALL FRONTS; THEY HAVE 
EXHAUSTED WHAT THEY HAD; THEY HAVE NO ALTERNATIVE IN THE FACE OF 
THIS FAILURE BUT TO PURSUE A NEW PHASE OF TAKING REVENGE AGAINST THE 
MASSES. REVENGE AGAINST THE MASSES HAS BEGUN IN EGYPT, IN THE 
SUDAN, IN IRAQ, NEARLY IN EVERY PART OF THE ARAB HOMELAND. BECAUSE 
THE REGIMES HAVE FAILED TO CONFRONT THE ENEMY, THEY ARE 
TAKING REVENGE AGAINST THE MASSES. IT IS LIKE A MAN WHO FAILS 
TO SOLVE HIS PROBLEMS AND TURNS AGAINST HIS WIFE; YOU FIND HIM 
HAVING FAMILY PROBLEMS; HE DIVORCES HIS WIFE OR HITS HER. THIS 
SHOWS THAT HE HAS PROBLEMS OUTSIDE, AT WORK, OR SOMEWHERE 
ELSE WHICH HE FAILED TO RESOLVE. IN FACT THE ARAB RULERS ARE NOW 
PLAYING THE ROLE OF SUCH A MAN, BECAUSE THEY HAVE FAILED TO 
SOLVE THEIR PROBLEMS.
