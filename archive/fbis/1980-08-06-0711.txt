LIBYA

PAPER PRAISES LIBYA'S USE OF BILLY CARTER 
LD051304 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 5 Aug 80 


[Text] The Saudi ASH-SHARQ AL-AWSAT, published in London, has stated that Libya has 
succeeded in what the Arabs have failed to achieve over 30 years. It says that for the 
first time an Arab state has been able to penetrate the American administration and has 
been able to set up an instrument of pressure inside America. The paper, in its analysis 
of the relationship between the Jamahiriyah and the brother of the U.S. President Carter, 
has underlined the success of the Jamahiriyah to (?use) the brother of the American 
President in the service of its interests in the United States.
