At-Turayki Comment 
ID121642 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 12 Dec 79 ID 


[Text] Dr 'Aii #Abd as@Salam ateTurayki, the foreign secretary, has refuted the claims of 
some Fatah leaders that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has closed the 

PLO office in Tripoli. During his meeting with Arab ambassadors in Tripoli this morning 
he stressed that the campaign being waged by these leaders was nothing but an attempt to 
cover up their suspicious attitudes and contacts. The foreign secretary pointed out 

that the stands of the Great 1 September Revolution, which was considered the main and 
real mainstay of tre Palestinian revolution, stemmed from its revolutionary and patriotic 
principles and from the Palestinian issue being a national issue, and that the Palestinian 
revolution was sacred while individuals were not. 


The Arab ambassadors told the foreign secretary of the appreciation of their countries for 
the patriotic stand of the Jamahiriyah, and on their part confirmed the incorrect claims 
of some of Fatah*s leaders. They declared that they were in contact with the movement’s 
office in Libya, which was operating normally.

ECONOMIC, TECHNICAL PROTOCOL SIGNED WITH GREECE 
LD122053 Tripoli JANA in English 1801 GMT 12 Dec 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, December 12, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The secretary of the General 
People's Committee for Planning Mr, Musa Abu Furaywah, and the Greek minister of 
finance this morning signed a protocol for economic and technical cooperation between 
the Jamahiriyah and Greece, 


The protocol includes the establishment of joint corporations in different fields and 
the setting up of a commercial sea route that links the (?ports) of Libya and Greece, 
as well as Greek companies to train Libyans in several fields and supervise the running 
of vocational training institutes,
