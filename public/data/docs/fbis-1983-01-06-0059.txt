[Unattributed article: "Greece To Close Libya-Targeted Spy Base] 


{Excerpts} The socialist government in Greece wants to close an American air base in 
Greece which it says is not being used for NATO-related purposes, but for spying on 
non-NATO countries, particularly Libya. 


The Hellenikon Air Base, on the eastern outskirts of Athens, was established by the 
Americans under a 1953 agreement which allowed the U.S. to set up four bases in the 
country as staging and supply posts for NATO and U.S. air and naval forces. The three 
other bases involved are Nea Makri, north-east of Athens, and Heraklion and Suuda Bay 
in Crete. 


The Greek Government savs that the Hellenikon base should be closed down because it 
is being used for the unauthorised surveillance of countries not in the Warsaw Pact 
which are friendly to Greece.

The fact that the Americans do send spy planes to overfly Libya was proved later in the 
year when, on September 2d, Libva shot down an American reconnaissance aircraft over 
the Benghazi Region. The point of embarkaticn of this aircraft has never been revealed, 
indeed the Americans still deny the whole incident, but it showed that the U.S. sends

U.S. Air Force's EC-135 electronic intelligence gatherers have been regularly intercep- 
ted by Libyan fighters snooping around Libva’s coasts. These planes are known to ope- 
rate out of Hellenikon, hence the Greek Government's concern.
