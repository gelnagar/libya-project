Summary (AI): A January 1982 memo from the CIA's National Intelligence Officer for Near East and South Asia to the DCI and DDCI comments on a Libya paper for an NSC meeting. It argues that the claim that US actions have suspended Qadhafi's terrorist activities against high US officials is too categorical given the available evidence.
• Memo DDI 485-82, dated 21 January 1982, prepared for an NSC meeting on Libya at 1530 hours that day.
• The memo says a US note to Qadhafi and other US actions may have interrupted Libyan plans, but evidence is inadequate to show terrorist activities against high US officials were suspended.
• Attachment titled 'Status Report on Libya' is referenced but not included in the text.
