Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing note on the Libya-Chad conflict says Libyan and dissident forces controlled northern Chad, while French and Chadian government forces held the south and had flown combat aircraft into N'Djamena airfield. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• Libyan/dissident forces control northern Chad; the central portion's status is undetermined; French/government forces control southern Chad.
• Four Mirage F.1 and six Jaguar strike aircraft, plus a C-135F tanker, arrived at N'Djamena airfield.
• Six Transall C-160 transports were at the airfield, two of them arrived.
• Mirage aircraft were armed with Matra Magic AAMs.
• 1983-08: French combat aircraft and a mobile Crotale SAM battery arrived to support government forces in southern Chad.
