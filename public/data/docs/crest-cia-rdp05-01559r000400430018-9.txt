Summary (AI): A FBIS translation of a February 1988 Tripoli Television broadcast of a speech by Qadhdhafi to the Tripoli Municipality People's Congress. He explains how basic people's congresses (about 140 in Tripoli, grouped into eight branches) feed resolutions up to the General People's Congress, using agricultural production as the example.
• Tripoli has eight branches of people's congresses and about 140 basic people's congresses.
• The General People's Congress was to receive 13 municipal papers, one from each municipal congress.
• Qadhdhafi cites the agricultural production body, the People's Committee of Agriculture in Tripoli, and joint foreign companies (Romanian, Polish) as examples of issues reviewed.
• The speech states that matters such as agricultural policy, justice, public sports, social security and utilities end at the municipal congress.
• 1988-02-13: Qadhdhafi addresses a meeting of the Tripoli Municipality People's Congress, broadcast by Tripoli Television at 1845 GMT.
