Summary (AI): A CIA Directorate of Intelligence memo (11 April 1986) assesses how Qadhafi would likely respond to a US military strike, including possible attacks on his residences, on Jamahiriyah Guard facilities, and on his headquarters. It warns that a strike would push radicals to expand terrorism against US interests.
• The document says Qadhafi is determined to use the crisis with Washington to inflict a political defeat on the US.
• A strike on facilities housing the Jamahiriyah Guard, described as Qadhafi's coup protection force, would probably reduce Libya's inclination to target US officials in Libya.
• Washington was not yet prepared to target Qadhafi directly, partly to avoid accusations of attempted assassination.
• Radicals are described as Qadhafi's only reliable source of domestic support.
• The document was prepared by the Maghreb Branch, Arab-Israeli Division, Office of Near East and South Asian Analysis.
• 1986-04: Assessment that a US strike on Qadhafi's residences would shake him and that a strike on Jamahiriyah Guard facilities would likely be seen as part of a broader campaign to subvert his regime.
