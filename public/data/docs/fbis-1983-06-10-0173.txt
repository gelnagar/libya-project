LIBYA

JANA on Anniversary of U.S. Eviction From Libya 
LDO092116 Tripoli JANA in English 1732 GMT 9 Jun 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Shaban 27, June 9, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The 11th of June marks the 13th anniversary of 
evicting the American military bases and removing the land- 
marks of American aggression and terrorism. 


The Libyan Arab people will receive this anniversary with big 
festivals and celebrations throughout Jamahiriyah. The distin- 
guishing feature of these festivities will be a determined chal- 
lenge to deter American terrorism and aggression and to affirm 
the Libyan people’s resoluteness in being ready to sacrifice their 
lives and realise victory in every battle and confrontation against 
imperialism and its rotten agents. 


JANA’s political editor has written the following on this occa- 
sion: 


The 1 1th of June will be a reminder of the biggest defeat inflicted 
by the Libyan Arab people on America and the most prominent 
victory for this people’s will against imperialist bases, which 
threatened the people’s destiny and freedom. 


The 13th anniversary of the evacuation of the American bases 
from Libyan soil will be celebrated by all Libyans who are very 
proud of themselves for being able to defeat America’s arro- 
gance. This historic event was the work of the determined Libyan 
Arab people. 


The celebration is again a challenge to U.S. imperialism and local 
puppets. 


The celebrations means that people would further sacrifice their 
lives and make life hell for U.S. imperialism. America conspired 
against the Libyan people, flagrantly attacked them and her fleet 
has been provocatively patrolling the Mediterranean opposite the 
Libyan shores. 


America’s ambitions and terroristic intentions are limitless. 
Our free people who had defeated the USA have again faced U.S. 


fleets and deterred a possible U.S. intervention in the Gulf of 
Sidra. 


The USA will not threaten the Libyan people once more and we 
will not allow her to step on this (?sacrifice) as the whole people 
will not hesitate to immediately and strongly retaliate against 
every attempt of threat or aggression and are ready to sacrifice 
and use every possible means to struggle against the U.S. aggres- 

sion, terrorism, racism and new crusade. The celebrations of our 
saint ih entioumad’ aemaendas Ui bases in 
Libya confirm the genuine and true mettle of the Libyan people 
and reflect the challenging spirit against the USA and its fleets, 
plots and defeatist symbols and agent satellites which move 
according to U.S. instructions. 


Be damned all those who lost their dignity, honour and con- 
fidence in their people and listened instead to the instructions of 
their masters in the Black House who violated all international 
norms, charters and human rights.
