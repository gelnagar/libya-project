Summary (AI): A New York Times article (catalogued 1981-12-07, though the header reads 7 December 1982) reports Colonel Qaddafi denying a plot to assassinate President Reagan, while the State Department said it had evidence of such plans. Qaddafi also threatened to fight America and said US policy was going to harm American companies.
• Qaddafi said the US had tried to assassinate him and to poison his food.
• Qaddafi challenged the US to investigate and said Zionist propaganda was behind accusations against Libya in America.
• Qaddafi said Libyan support goes only to movements struggling for a just cause, like the PLO.
• Senators Byrd and Baker had been briefed by the Secret Service before the CBS 'Face the Nation' program.
• The State Department statement was the first official report from a government agency on the matter.
• 1981-12-06: Qaddafi denied in an interview, televised from his Tripoli office, that he had plotted to assassinate President Reagan or anyone else; the State Department said it had strong evidence of Qaddafi-planned murders of American officials at home and overseas.
