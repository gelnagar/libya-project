Summary (AI): An FBIS wire relays a Tripoli radio broadcast of a message from Libyan leader Muammar al-Qadhdhafi to Egyptian President Sadat protesting Egypt's arrest of Libyan Al-Masmudi, who was reportedly a guest of the Egyptian government. The message is Libya's foreign policy, with no Tripoli places or sites involved.
• The message was broadcast by Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic at 2315 GMT on 8 March 1976.
• Qadhdhafi said the arrest of Al-Masmudi harmed Egypt's prestige and called on Sadat to free him quickly.
• The family of Al-Masmudi was in contact with Tripoli.
• 1976-03-08: Qadhdhafi message to Sadat protesting the Egyptian arrest of Al-Masmudi, broadcast by Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland
