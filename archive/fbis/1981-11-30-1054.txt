LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI MARKS RETURN OF TROOPS FROM CHAD 


GF281655 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1036 GMT 28 Nov 81 


[Speech by Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi at rally in Sabha marking return of Libyan 
troops from Chad -- live] 


[Excerpts] [cheers and commotion] Oh brave and liberating soldiers and officers: 
[applause] It is a great honor for your country that you return to it today proud and 
with high morale, having carried out a strategic mission that has gained international 
glory for your people and country and raised Libya's standing. By your historic and 
strategic mission, you have honored all Libyan men and women, all Arabs and Africa. You 
have achieved glory for every one of you and glory for the Libyan Arab Armed Forces and 
for the Libyan people. 


On this day, we have the right to explain to the world how this historic and strategic 
mission carried out by our armed forces took place. Let us go back to the past and clarify 
the Libyan people's relationship with the Chadian national and popular liberation move- 
ment, Frolinat, which was established to regain the rights of oppressed people in Chad. 
Since its inception, Frolinat has been an expression of the state of oppressed people in 
Chad, the Muslim tribes, the Arab tribes and the Muslim non-Arab tribes which lived as 
second or third class citizens during the era of French colonialism in Chad and during the 
fascist rule installed by French colonialism in Chad after its departure -- from the 
Tombalbaye era to the Felix Malloum era. Persecution and racial discrimination were 
practiced against Muslims in Chad, who constitute the overwhelming majority. Racial 
discrimination in its ugliest form was also practiced against ethnic Arabs in Chad. 


The Muslim population was the victim of a deliberate extermination process -- starvation, 
deliberate neglect and also murder. There was also a plan to wipe out ethnic Arabs in 
Chad. French colonialism and the fascist regimes that succeeded it were responsible for 
this plan. The Chadian National Liberation Movement, Frolinat, emerged in these circum- 
stances to wrest the rights of the persecuted Muslims in Chad and to save Chad from the 
clutches of the fascist minority installed by French imperialism, enabling it to exercise 
control over the Chadian people. Libya was the support for Frolinat. Frolinat continued 
to fight for almost 20 years, relying on the Libyan people's support. 


When the sultan of the (Sabu) tribes, Sultan (Wajay), was banished, he did not find a 

haven in the world and in the whole of Africa except in Libya. He spent the rest of his 
life in Libya. Sultan (Wajay) was the spiritual father of Frolinat. Frolinat was able 

to reach Ndjamena, the Chadian capital, thanks to the Libyan people's unlimited and con- 
sistent support. Felix Malloum, head of the military government and junta and friend of 
French colonialism, fled to Nigeria in the face of our men's advance, and he is still there. 


As a result of Frolinat's political and military efforts supported by Libya's indirect 
political and military efforts, Goukouni Oueddei, the head of Frolinat, was installed as 
head of the Chadian Transitional Government at the Lagos conference in Nigeria with 
Libya's strong support. Frolinat's leaders, who were fighting with Goukouni Oueddei, 
were appointed ministers in this government. 


Our role at that time ended and we closed the Chadian file. However, civil war broke 
throughout Chad and spread until it reached the borders of Libya -- until it reached 
(Sud), inside Libya, and the borders of Niger, Nigeria, Cameroon, the Central African 
Republic and Sudan. The mutiny spread to almost all the countries of central Africa.

Chad's territorial integrity and national unity were threatened. More than 5,000 
Chadian civilians were killed in this civil war. Guillotines were erected in a 

number of populated cities. Innocent citizens who refused to join this or that 

side were guillotined. 


When the Chadian people were being exterminated close to our borders and peace and 
security collapsed near our southern borders, Goukouni Oueddei came to us and asked for 
direct intervention. He addressed us in the name of all Chadian men sand women; in 

the name of innocent children, the men and women who were unable to carry arms; in the 
name of our neighborliness; in the name of Islam; in the name of Africa to put an 

end to this bloody tragedy in Chad because, as Goukounisaid, “You alone in this region 
have the power to end this fierce war in Chad." 


At the same time, we had no desire whatsoever to go into Chad. We believed that Frolinat's 
assumption of power in Ndjamena, thanks to our long support for it, had freed us of our 
obligations toward Chad. However, in the face of this plea and insistence and for the 

sake of the Chadian people, Africa and security on our borders, we told Goukouni 

Oueddei that we would enter Chad only legally, in a legitimate manner that permitted 

our armed forces, as foreign forces, to cross their borders and enter the territory of 
another country. It is not easy or permissible for the armed forces of one country to 

enter the territory of another country without legal support acceptable to the world. 
Consequently, he agreed sign a Libyan-Chadian joint defense and security treaty. 


At this time last year I issued an order to the vanguards of an armored division to 
enter Chad. A land and air bridge was formed to transport an armored division. This 
bridge extended over 4,000 km. It crossed the Libyan desert, the high Tibesti moun- 
tains and the Chadian desert and then crossed jungles, rivers, swamps and towns. 
During this operation called "Swift Lightning," an armored division was transvorted 


to the heart of Africa. [applause] [Chants of "We will not forget Palestine, we lib- 


erated Ndjamena"] These forces fought in climatic conditions, some of which were 
similar to Libya's and some totally different. They fought on territory, some of 
which is similar to Libyan territory and some totally different. In any case, these 
forces fought with great efficiency that was acknowledged by the enemies. [prolonged 
chants ] 


This armored division -- of which only token unites are in Sabha today while the rest 

of this division is now inside Libyan territory -- has gained a fighting ability on 
various types of terrain and in various climates. [chants of "popular revolution against 
reactionaries." ] 


The special forces -- the commandos -- fought in a difficult conditions that compelled 
them to change the nature of their fighting and to carry out tasks different from 
their special tasks. [chants] 


Tank crews fought and carried out tasks that might have differed from their usual 
tasks. Missile battery crews fought and used the missile batteries creatively in 
deployments that are not the task of missile batteries, particularly in Ndjamena 
when conditions were very difficult. 


The administrative organization of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces demonstrated a high 
level of logistic ability. They extended bridges across the desert, mountains, the 
desert once again, the jungle, lakes, rivers and towns. The armed forces on the 
battlefront received their supplies from rear bases in Libyan territory without 
shortages or confusion. The fighting and administrative branches of the Libyan 

Arab Armed Forces underwent a memorable historic and (?logistical) test. Men trained 
as staff officers were tested in real war and proved worthy of wearing the insignia 
of staff officers. [applause] 


All military mozilization lessons taught in military colleges were tested by junior 
officers. They were tested on the battle fronts and proved the usefulness of military 
studies at Libyan military schools and also proved the ability of junior officers to 
apply all military lessons learned in military schools without having to attend 
courses after military college. [applause]

This armored division supported by all types of equipment penetrated deep in western, 
eastern and central Chad after having liberated northern Chad. The officers commanding 
these axes were able, through the instructions of the principal operations room in 
Tripoli -- where Maj ‘Abd as-Salem Jallud, who was charged with the command of the 
operations room, could directly communicate with the axes leaders on the battlefield 
from Batha to Lake Chad [applause] -- the staff officers commanding these axes were able 
to make changes in field plans. They referred these plans back to the operations room 
in Tripoli to obtain immediate approval for the change. Notification was given for the 
change in plan and approval for it obtained from the operations room within minutes and 
the plan was immediately implemented. The plan thus made it possible to bring about 
radical changes in the axes, in attacking the targets, in the choice of targets and in 
the timing of the attack. They acquired practical experience in addition to the 
theoretical experience they received at staff colleges in the Soviet Union, Yugoslavia 
and in foreign countries. 


This division spread out in every part of Chad and entered every town and crossed every 
lake, swamp and river, mountain and desert. The shock troops were carried on tanks and 
rocket artillery and the infantry were directly supported by the air force and the 
administrative organization. [applause] We are proud on this day to have a high level 
division which engaged in various battles in various circumstances -- topographical and 
climatic and fought an enemy who has a different nature, fighting in an organized manner 
and in the form of guerrilla warfare, and entering unknown territory and not hesitating 
to carry out this duty to gain glory for itself and for the Libyan people. The enemy 
suffered more than 3,000 dead in these battles. Our forces gave 300 martyrs, that is 

to say, 2 percent. From the military point of view, the percentage loss is very small. 


We tried all our different types of weapons -- weapons we mobilized with this division -- 
to make sure of their effectiveness. We used the Topolev strategic bombers. They 
carried out 67 missions in support of this ivision’s assault. [applause and cheers] 
They carried out 10 reconnaissance-photographic strategic missions deep along the 

borders of Sudan, Central Africa and inside had so that we could be sure that the 

area in which our forces were fighting was not threatened by any foreign intervention. 
Ultimately, the Topolev carried out 86 assault and photographic missions during the 
operation of that division in Chad. 


The Topolev aircraft took off from their bases in Libya to carry out these tasks in the 
heart of Africa and returned to their bases in Libya without landing in Chad. [applause] 
The F-1 aircraft — the Mirage fighter-bomber -- also took off from a base in Libya and 
carried out 58 assault and reconnaissance missions. They played a major role in the 
liberation of the northern capital of Chad. 


The MiG-23 -- MF, the developed version -- carried out 85 missions from hangers inside 
Chad. The aircraft formations you saw today -- the 260 aircraft -- carried out 1,500 
missions [words indistinct]. [applause] They were used in a concentrated manner -- 
perhaps I can say that they were used in a foolish manner. This is because these aircraft 
are not expensive and don't require complicated technical matters like an ordinary 
fighter and bomber aircraft. They don't require bases as they are capable of landing 

on all types of ground. They were used to bomb enemy concentrations and the government 
forces of the agent of Sudan in Tibah and [word indistinct] on the border of Sudan. 

They were used in a concentrated manner until they destroyed all his formations aimed 

at the Libyan forces and were at [word indistinct] and surrounding areas. The (?M)124 -—- 
flying fortresses -- were also tested; helicopters which carry open-range and guided 
missiles; the armed helicopters, were all used in a concentrated manner. They carried 
out 134 missions during various operations. [applause] Other types of strategic aircraft 
were used for administrative purposes. With them strategic bridges were built during 

the entry into Chad and when the forces were returning. Their technical capability was 
proved.

Many were wounded. We said 300 officers and men were martyred during these operations. 
This is a very small number in comparison with the magnitude of the forces which partici- 
pated. There were 700 soldiers wounded with various kinds of wounds; but all have been 
treated andare well. Only 50 of them are still being treated abroad. The rest and slight 
injuries and were treated in field hospitals of the mobilized division and in hospitals 

in the support bases inside Libyan territory. [applause] 


By way of these strategic land and air bridges more than 40,000 tons [as heard] was moved 
in a very short time. This astonished the world. How was this division transported in 

a short time to the heart of Africa and how did this division manage to fight in the heart 
of Africa in unknown territory and against an unknown enemy and how was it able to return 
in 10 days to Libyan territory? I say there is a mechanical capability in Libya which even 
the U.S. forces do not have; the Libyan Arab Armed Forces have this capability. [applause] 
This is because there is a plan to develop air and land transport means, particularly the 
land transport means -- they have been developed locally and now we can transport a large 
consignment on a minimum number of vehicles and in the shortest possible time. 


When we say this, brothers, we want to show the world the defensive capability of Libya, 
and show them that this is our responsibility -- military and political, in order to deny 
all tendentious, false and stupid accusations which say the Soviet Union is behind this 
action or that this strategic action could not have been carried out by the effort of a 
state such as Libya because it needs a superpower. But now we have shown them that this 
effort was Libyan; that Libya has and that the Soviet Union has nothing to do with the 
operation in Chad. [applause] When this operation was carried out, the Soviet Union, 
being a friendly country, supported us politically in the international areas only, bearing 
in mind that Libya purchases its strategic weapons from the Soviet Union and that a number 
of these soldiers and officers were trained in the Soviet Union. As for the operation in 
Chad, neither the Soviet Union nor anyone else has anything to do with it. [applause] 

It was a surprise to the Soviet Union and to other countries of the world. 


This was an action carried out by a free people who shoulder their responsibility before 
the world and shoulder all consequences resulting from any action decided by the Libyan 
people, out of their own free will. [applause] The Libyan people are free people and 

have made great sacrifices for their freedom and, to occupy their rightful place under the 
sun and on the ground, they do not accept being the lackey of any power. At the same time, 
the Libyan people befriend those who want to befriend them and are hostile to those who 
are hostile to them. [applause and cheers] 


These forces which returned the favor to the people of Chad will remain a mobilized division. 
An order will be issued for its formation as a rapid deployment force in opposition to the 
U.S. Rapid Deployment Force. [applause and cheers] All strategic military means will 

be constantly placed at the disposal of this division so that it may perform the tasks of 
of the rapid deployment force everywhere in order to help the nations fighting for freedom. 
[applause and cheers} If the U.S. Rapid Deployment Force is fighting the nations in order 
to kill freedom it is our duty now that Libya has become the international headquarters of 
resisting colonialism and imperialism in the world, in accordance with the resolution 
adopted on 1 September of last year by the international conference on solidarity with the 
Libyan people, to form a rapid deployment force to fight for freedom and to fight on the 
side of the peoples. Its task will be in total opposition to that of the U.S. Rapid 
Deployment Force.

Brothers, the world cannot at all object to this action so long as it permits any 

state capable of forming forces for intervention to do so. Therefore, we too, taking 
advantage of this U.S. precedent, declare the formation of a rapid deployment force to 
fight on the side of peoples and for freedom. [applause and cheers] The United States, 
brothers, has formed a force and called it a rapid deployment force. When it was asked: 
Where can these forces operate, the United States said that the U.S. Rapid Deployment 
Force has a right to intervene anywhere in the world to protect U.S. interests. This 

is an encroachment of the entire land by those who have power. We too, therefore, according 
to our own capability which cannot be compared with that of the United States as the 
United States is the number one superpower in the world, have the right to form a rapid 
deployment force so that it may be ready to intervene anywhere we undertake to fight 

on the side of freedom. 


When the people's authority was established in Libya, it was decided that Libyans are 
committed to freedom and are prepared to defend it in Libya and anywhere else. Your 

return has shown the world that Libya is not an aggressive power and does not seek to 
invade any country. It fights for freedom. 


The Libyan forces, however, have withdrawn; fighting is taking place now, alas, between 
the forces of Gcukouni and the forces of Habre which are supported by Numayri's forces. 
But the Libyan forces did not leave Abeche, Adre, Iriba, (Kursi) and Tina until after 

they forced agent Numayri to recognize the government of Chad and the presidency of 
Goukouni Oueddei, which until very recently he refused to do. He did not recognize this 
government, but the blows which were dealt to his interventions at the Chad borders forced 
him to recognize the Government of Chad -- kneeling, humble and base. 


Anyone can look at Numayri's past and recent statements. He is sure to find that in his 
past statements, he did not recognize the Chad Government at all nor did he recognize 

the government of Goukouni Oueddei. But in his recent statements, following the recent 
battles at (Gunayda), Iriba and Adre, he recognized the Government of Chad and Goukouni 
Oueddei. But why did he recognize them after he was dealt blows on the borders of 

Chad, when since for over a year he was preparing for a large-scale campaign, believing 
that in this way he could topple the Chadian Government and set up a regime subservient 
to him? Indeed, in the past 2 months, on the eastern borders of Chad, which were left 
for some national Chadian forces, since the Libyan forces took up positions in their rear, 
1,000 Sudanese Government and rebel Chadian soldiers were brought in and quickly occupied 
Adre, Iriba, (Riba) and Guereda. The Libyan forces were behind that line. The Libyan 
forces were informed and [word indistinct] asked for their assistance. Units of this 
force, supported by aircraft formations, were deployed. These are the forces I mentioned 
which can land at any time and place and which have been developed locally and are armed 
with missiles, bombs and machineguns which were not included in their original design. 
Some 600 government and rebel soldiers were killed during the last 2 months; 4 Libyan 
officers led by a free officer were martyred and 12 Libyansoldiers were killed; 600 enemy 
soldiers were killed. 


Thus, Numayri came to realize that all his attempts were doomed to fail as song as they 
were directed against the Libyan forces. For over a year, Numayri has been preparing 
for his operation, believing that he could do what the Libyans had done. But there is 
a big difference between the two and there is no room for comparison in this matter. 

He took a year to prepare while we took only 2 days. 


The border towns of (Gulbus) aad Tina and logistics on the Chadian borders were intensively 
strafed from the air until the campaign he was preparing for 1 year was crushed. 

It was completely crushed. Then Numayri kneeled before the Libyan forces and recognized 
the Government of Chad. Perhaps that was the first time ever in his statements that he 
recognized the Government of Chad and the president of Chad and President Goukouni and 

said that they should be helped and that fighting against them should stop. He did 

this only after the Libyan forces broke the nose of agent Numayri.

In conclusion, brothers, we must greet our Chadian brothers who bestowed all trust on 
their Libyan brothers and permitted Libyan forces to enter all parts of Chad. The Libyan 
forces operated for 1 whole year in complete freedom, thanks to the trust bestowed on 
them by the Chadian people and the Chadian leadership. 


This is a testimony for all the world: Trust is vested in foreign forces to move about 
freely. Had these forces been other than Libyan forces, the Chadian people would not 
have given them this trust and would not have welcomed them. The Libyan forces were 
welcomed wherever they went in Chad. The road before them was strewn with roses. 
[applause] Chadian women sung their praise as they scored victories. [applause] They 
scored victories everywhere in Chad. This is because the Libyan forces fought on the 
side of the Chadian people and for the sake of freedom of the Chadian people. 


We, brothers, consider this as a medal pinned to the chest of every Libyan man and women. 
It is an historical honor for us to see our brothers inChad place their fulltrust in 
us throughout the year we operated in Chad. 


The families of those Libyans martyred in Chad should feel proud of their sons who were 
killed in Chad, in the battlefield of heroism and manliness, in the battlefields of honor 
and freedom. 


There are many who are killed in petty traffic accident::. There are those who are 
effiminates and absconders from carrying arms\ when, as you see here, women carry arms 

just like men. There are deserters from military service, who ran away from the battlefield 
of honor, dignity and prestige. They ran away from a life worth living. They live in 
Europe like stray dogs on the crumbs of the tables of Europeans, pursued by the curses 

of Libyans, the free Libyans, and die there either by physical liquidation or otherwise. 
Those killed in Chad deserve that their families feel proud of their martyrdom. [applause] 


Brothers, our meeting with you today adds to our pride in ourselves and in our defense 
power. We declare we will not retreat in the building of our military defense power. 

We also declare we will not retreat -- regardless of how much the threats and challenges 
increase around us -- from intervening on the side of the peoples if the United States 
intervenes on the side of the antipeople governments. These forces will intervene on the 
side of the peoples, because [word indistinct] fighting on any part of the Arab homeland; 
this is our soil and our homeland; but now and before our eyes the U.S. Armed Forces and 
the Israeli forces protected by the United States are violating the sanctities of the 
Arab homeland and are treading these sanctities underfoot. The U.S. strategic B-52 
bombers take off from their bases in the United States to bomb the Arab territories 

(?7at the Libyan borders). U.S. soldiers land in the sacred Arab homeland, Oman and Somalia. 


Brothers, today we urge and incite the Arab nation to fight and to have popular mobilization 
to face the U.S. invasion and to liquidate the agents of the United States, who are 

building bridges for the advance of U.S. forces instead of resisting these forces. But 

the building of this force has begun on Libyan land; the building of the rapid deployment 
force has begun; these forces, should the United States intervene in the Arabian Gulf, 

will intervene on the side of the Arab people in the Gulf. We will transport them to 

the Gulf to fight against the United States. If any people of the Arab nation from the 
ocean to the Gulf ask for our help, these forces will immediately be transported to fight 

on the side of any Arab people. The struggle is continuing.

ADDITIONS TO PEOPLE'S CONGRESS POLITICAL REPORT 


The following additions to the item entitled "Arab People's Congress Draft Political 
Report," published in the 23 November Middle East and Africa DAILY REPORT, page Q 6, 
provide the "text" of the article: 


Page 11, add after eighth graf: "III. Substitutes for the Camp David agreements: 

Ever since the Camp David agreements met with a popular and official Arab rejction, 

U.S. imperilaism and its allies have never ceased to maneuver on two levels. The first 
level aims at entrenching the agreements and their results in Egypt and in the Arab area; 
the element of time was used to impose a fait accompli. The second level aims at looking 
for a substitute or something that complements the agreements in a way that does not 
embarrass the Arab regimes linked to the United States. At the same time, the political 
precepts on this level had been and still are to help Washington in the success of its 
work on the first level. 


"The European initiative is perhaps the best example of this. The fact is that they 
first began to refer to it right up until the present. After the Fahd initiative and 
the death of As-Sadat, this initiative shrunk to the point of an understanding between 
the EEC and Saudi Arabia about a common denominator which they see as a solution to the 
Middle East problem. This means more concessions from Saudi Arabia and from Arab reation 
which rotates in its orbit. We can say that along with the field of substitutes for the 
Camp David agreements in the present stage, the Saudi initiative also remains. There- 
fore, we should conduct an objective and sober discussion of this initiative, its role 
and functions in the plans now circulating in the Arab arena." 


Page 13, add after first graph: "Saudi policy has never ceased to give its backing and 

aid to the various regimes which are openly allied with the United States, irrespective 

of the positions of these regimes toward the Camp David agreements or toward the general 
Arab-Israeli conflict. We clearly see this in its unchanging and strong support for 

the regimes in Sudan, Somalia, Oman, Morocco, and others -- regimes which are hostile to 
the aspirations and hopes of our Arab people and which have a greater affinity with the 

Egyptian regime in its alliance with Israel. 


"In addition to all this, Saudi Arabia adopts a hostile and hateful stance toward all 
the groups of the national liberation movement in the world regardless of the support 
these groups have given to the Palestinian cause and the Arab liberation movement. On 
the contrary, on more than one occasion, Saudi Arabia stood alongside international 
Zionism in its hostility to those groups and it supported their enemies. The Iranian 
example is perhaps the latest. The Fahd initiative falls into the framework of all 
this and to be more precise, it comes as the result of a special Saudi-U.S. relationship 
at a time when Washington desperately needs to safeguard its gains as a result of the 
Camp David agreements and to perpetuate these agreements at the Egyptian level while 
at the same time searching for a way in which they could be applied at the pan-Arab 
level."
