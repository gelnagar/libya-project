LIBYA

USSR REPORTEDLY PREPARING TO REPLACE AL-QADHDHAFI 
LD221335 Rome L'ESPRESSO in Italian 13 Jul 80 pp 38-44 
[Giancesare Flesca dispatch: "Even the Colonel Is Mortal") 


[Excerpts] Cairo--We were in Cairo, in a rather delapidated villa in Heliopolis, the 
new suburb that has grown up on the outskirts of the capital. In the courtyard, watched 
ver by battle-equipped policemen and burly bodyguards, a brood of lively children were 
scratching around with the chickens and a pair of sheep. "“As-Sadat is generous to us. 
Soon he will give us a new headquarters in the center of Cairo." The present one is 
too shoddy in the opinion of Mustafa al-Bakri, who has been chairman of the Libyan 
National League in Egypt for only 2 months. Its only advantage is that a great deal 

f material can be hidden in the building's basement, "and you know what I am talking 
about...." 


"Hitherto," Al-Bakri added, "We have not needed weapons. However, apart from the 
Libyan regime, about whose existence we would rather say nothing, our action has been 
purely political. We were about to do something stupid 2 months ago: Everything was 
ready, evon in detail, for striking out at Al-Qadhdhafi's killers in three European 
capitals--London, Rome and Athens. At the last moment we abandoned the idea. We dc 
not want to be confused with the colonel's gangsters." Even the attacks on the oil 
wells (it took a month to extinguish the blazes) were not the work of the "league," 
but of a clandestine group calling itself "7 April" and apparently based in Rome and 
Paris. 


The hostile tribes in Libya--the same ones as always, such as the Brassa and ! Ubaydah 
tribes--are now almost ready for war, and even within the colonel's own tribe, the 
Qadhdhafa tribe, dissidence is growing. A well-loved cousin of the family clan, (Sayed 
Qadhdhaf Addam) is now plotting openly against him. Even the clerics are becoming 
restless, and their relations with the regime have not improved since the arrest of 

80 shaykhs including the very popular Tahir az-Zawawi. 


But all this is not enough to eliminate him. The opposition knows this and pins its 
hopes on the armed forces, especially the air force--"the only branch of the armed 
forces in which there are still officers capable of thinking for themselves, since 
the purges." With these officers and other enemies of the colonel living in the 
country, Al-Bakri believes that he has established good contacts. In his opinion the 
soldiers who count are ready to overthrow Al-Qadhdhafi (some want to kill him right 
away, others after a proper trial) but want to retain power for 10 years, However, 

a central problem remains: the Russians. How is Al-Qadhdhafi to be killed if the 
Russians, who hold the keys to the arsenal, do not permit it? 


The league representatives replied: "Being cleverer than the Americans, who have not 
benefited much from the Iranian lesson, the Soviets are already preparing an alternative 
to the colonel, whom they have always mistrusted. The alternative's name cannot be 
revealed, and he wears a general's uniform beneath which beats a socialist's heart. 

We are ready to support him, though our sympathies are with the West. For that matter, 
we will even lend a hand to poor old Jallud, who was imprisoned again and tortured 

3 months ago for no reason, just to show him who is in charge...."
