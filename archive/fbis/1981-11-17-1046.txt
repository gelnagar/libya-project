LIBYA

PEOPLE'S CONGRESS SECRETARIAT RESUMES MEETINGS 


Discusses Fahd Plan 
LD162234 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 16 Nov 81 


[Text] The Arab General People's Congress Secretariat resumed its meetings in Tripoli 
this evening. Discussions during the meeting covered ways and means of countering 

the capitulationist plans, particularly Fahd's plan, and of bringing Egypt back to the 
Arab fold so that it can play its leading :ole after the destruction of the capitula- 
tionist Camp David policy. 


._The participants agree on the need to tackle and face up to the regime of Numayri 

who challenged the Arab will and followed As-Sadat in his betrayal of the Arab nation. 
They affirmed the need to put Numayri on trial as soon as possible and to expose his 
traitorous role. 


The JANA correspondent reports that during the meeting, the necessary documents were 
prepared for submission to the emergency meeting of the Arab People's Congress that 
will begin in Tripoli on 18 November at the Science College Theater. 


V. 17 Nov 81 date NORTH AFRICA 


Addresses Arab Nation 
LD170932 Tripoli JANA in English 0839 GMT 17 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 17 Nov -- The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress sent 
the following address to the Arab nation: 


"The eighties of the 20th century take the Arab nation back to the age of imperialist 
domination and exploitation. This follows decades of struggle for liberation from 
imperialism and Zionism for which hundreds of thousands have been martyred everywhere. 
They died for the sake of achieving national independence and ushering in the era of 
Arab oil. But, now, reactionary forces in the Arab world have taken hold of the reins. 
U.S. Forces in Egypt, Sudan, Somalia and Oman consolidate U.S. occupation of the Arab 
home land. 


Within the framework of the U.S.-Israeli strategic alliance the Fahd initiative emerges 
to place another serious challenge before the Arab nation. 


The imperialist U.S. forces with their fleet and armies have entered the Arab region 
under the pretext of carrying out military exercises, to openly declare domination 
of the region. 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress calls on the Arab masses and 
progressive popular organizations to move and counter the conspiracy aimed at destroying 
the Palestinian revolution and threatening national existence. It asserts that the 
emergency meeting of the Arab People's Congress which will be held in Tripoli on 
November 19-21 will be devoted to studying the situation and finding suitable solutions. 
The progressive popular forces will outline their commitment at this session. Factions 
of the Arab liberation movement are urged to come to the Aral congress to define their 
stands freely and courageously. 


LIBERATION MOVEMENTS HOLD MEETING IN TRIPOLI 


Condemn U.S. Terrorism 


LD170950 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0215 GMT 17 Nov 81 


[Text] The Arab and world liberation movemen:s represented in the Jamahiriyah have 
praised the vanguard stands of the Great Fatih Revolution and its resolute challenge to 
American military provocations and recklessness. In a cable sent to the brother leader 
at the end of their meeting in Tripoli last night, the liberation movements stressed 
that the struggle being waged by the people of the world against world imperialism 
headed by America will be decided in favor of the peoples and the revolutionary forces. 


They made it clear that the military provocations carried out by America in cooperation 

with its agents in the Arab region and in the Horn of Africa and the Caribbean only 

confirm the (?mad) power waved about by America to terrorize the people of the world and 

to plunder their resources. The liberation movements pointed out that at a time when 

ail kinds of American weapons of destruction are being released in these regions, Fahd's 
plan -- which aims at dismembering the liberation movement factions and at diverting the 
attention of progressive forces from carrying out their struggle duties, particularly 

the Great Fatih Revolution which supports the peoples struggling for freedom and progress -- 
is being put forward. 


The liberation movements again confirmed their solidarity with the people of the Jamahiriyah, 
and stand by their side in the struggle against American terrorism and arrogance and the 
agent reaction, and their complete readiness to defend the Great Fatih Revolution and 

its achievements. 


V. 17 Nov 81 Q 6 NORTH AFRICA 


Issue Statement 
LD171000 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 17 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 17 Nov (JANA) -- Representatives of liberation movements in the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah held a meeting last night in Tripoli. The meeting 
reviewed the present situation in the Arab region and the dangers surrounding it, 
especially after the aggressive military maneuvers which America is carrying out jointly 
with Egyptian, Sudanese, Somali and Omani forces in the region. 


At the end of the meeting, the liberation movements issued a statement: 


The United States is carrying out provocative military maneuvers in Egypt, Sudan, Somalia 
and Oman, which are considered to be a threat to world peace and the interests of the 
peoples of the region. The aim of these maneuvers is to terrorize the peoples and to 
strike at the progressive and revolutionary Arab resistance which is struggling against 
Zionism, reaction and the suspicious plans starting with Camp David and ending with 
Fahd's plan. 


The liberation movements and the political parties represented in Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. having comprehended the situation and the surrounding dangers 
and having realized that the protection of the Great Fatih Revolution is the responsi- 
bility of every revolutionary, held a special meeting to unify their efforts to confront 
this danger. They also studied the American maneuvers in the Caribbean and the efforts 
to threaten the Cuban revolution. 


They declare the solidarity of the liberation movements participating in the meeting with 
the Great Fatih Revolution and their support for it in all spheres in confronting the 
reactionary American danger in the event of mobilization, and they express themselves 

to be a partner in confronting the American imperialist danger. 


This meeting is considered to be a political declaration by these forces and a commitment 
to defend the Great Fatih Revolution, the steadfast bastion, in the face of the 


imperialist plots directed against it. 


LIBYA-USSR ECONOMIC COOPERATION PROTOCOL SIGNED 


LD161824 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 16 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 Nov (JANA) -- The protocol of the seventh session of the joint Libyan- 
Soviet committee for economic, scientific, technical and commercial cooperation was 

signed this morning. It was signed by the secretary of the General People's Committee 

for Electricity and, on the Soviet side, by F. Mordvinov, deputy chairman of the USSR 
State Committee for Foreign Economic Relations.
