1971 against As-Sadat's policies of dialogue with world imperialism and, especially, 
American imperialism particularly in the economic, cultural, political and military 
spheres. 


It also expressed backing for the Egyptian people's struggle against conspiracies de- 
signed to inflict starvation, ruin the national economy, and exploit the toil of workers 
in favour parasitic capitalist and family interests which have links with world capi- 
talisn.
