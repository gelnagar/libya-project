LIBYA

GUINEAN PRESIDENT TOURE ARRIVES ON VISIT 31 JUL 
ID302046 Tripoli JANA in English 1845 qmT 30 Jul 79 ID 


(Text ] Tripoli, July 30 (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--Guinea's President Ahmed Sekou Toure 
arrived in Tripoli this afternoon on a several day visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


The Guinean president was given an official welcome in Tripoli international airport. 


Participating in the welcoming ceremony were the commander of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, 
Brigadier Abu Baxr Yunis Jabir, Lieutenant-Colonel Mustafa al-Kharrubi, and the commander 
of the popular resistance, Major al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi. 


Also participating in the ceremony were the assistant secretary general of the General 
People's Congress, Mr Muhammad Abu al-Qasim az-Zuwayy, the secretary and members of the General 
People's Committee, the directors of departments, the ambassador of Guinea in the Jamahir- 
iyah, the Jamahiriyah's ambassador in Guinea and the Guinean community in Tripoli. 


Upon the disembarkation of the guest president from the aircraft, a 21 gun salute was 
given in his honour, 


The guest then shook hands with those present at the ceremony and later headed, accomp- 
anied by Brigadier Jabir, for the honour podium where the Jamahiriyah and Guinea's nat- 
ional anthems were played. 


President Sekou Toure was then introduced to the rest of his well-wishers and later headed 
in a big motorcade to the guest palace. 


Meeting With Al-Qadhdhafi 
ID311550 Tripoli JANA in English 1423 GMT 31 Jul 79 ID 


{Text] Tripoli, July 31, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader 
of the Great First of September Revolution, yesterday received Ahmed Sekou Toure, presi- 
dent of Guinea and higher offical [as received] of the revolutionary authorities and sec- 
retary general of the Guinean Democratic Party, and his delegation currently visiting the 
Jamahiriyah. 


The meeting was attended by Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, the general-commander of the 
armed forces, Lieutenant Colonel Mustafa al-Kharrubi and Major al-lhuwaylii al-Humaydi, 
commander of the people's resistance, secretaries of liaison, public services and head 
of the mission of honour accompanying the Guinean president.
