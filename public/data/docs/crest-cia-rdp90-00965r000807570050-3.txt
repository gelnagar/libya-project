• Qaddafi said Libya supported the Palestinian guerrilla struggle but was not responsible for tactical decisions on attacks.
• Qaddafi said there would be no peace in the Mediterranean and no American airlines or trade if Libya were attacked.
• Qaddafi said that he had 'passed the stage of being frightened' and invited guests to tour the complex.
• The Reagan administration reportedly considered a CIA effort to subvert received assessments of possible US reaction to the airport attacks.
