Summary (AI): A 1986 CIA cover memo (several identical copies sent to US officials) forwards an assessment, "Libya: What if Qadhafi is Ousted?", concluding that Qadhafi's removal would probably produce a regime less supportive of terrorism, but successors would still meddle regionally and an extremist regime could be a greater threat. It contains no Tripoli-specific details.
• The memo is dated 4 August 1986 and was prepared for CPPG principals after a meeting on Libya.
• The attached assessment is titled 'Libya: What if Qadhafi is Ousted?'
• The memo warns that an extremist-controlled regime could pose a greater threat to US interests than Qadhafi.

MEMORANDUM FOR: Mr. Donald Gregg

SUBJECT: Libya After Qadhafi 
( ee 

The recent meeting on Libya indicated that the attached 
assessment, "Libya: What if Qadhafi is Ousted?" would be of 
interest to you and other CPPG principals. The study concluded 
that Qadhafi's removal probably would result in a regime less 
supportive of worldwide terrorism. Even so, we cannot expect 
Qadhafi's successors to refrain from meddling in regional affairs 
or to support US policies in the Middle East and Africa. 
Moreover, we cannot rule out the possibility of an extremist- 
controlled regime taking control that could be an even greater 
threat to US interests than Qadhafi. 25x11
