HASSAN'S REMARKS ON RECOGNITION OF ISRAEL SCORED 


LD292036 Tripoli JANA in English 1400 GMT 29 Oct 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Muharram 12, October 29, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The political editor 
of JANA issued the following statement: The pronouncements of King Hassan II of Morocco 
in Washington about the Arab struggle with enemy being a legal rather than cultural one 
is still being met with wrath and anger of the Arab masses throughout the nation. In 
doing so Hassan II presented himself to Washington's politicians as an obedient stooge 
willing to serve imperialism, aid the Zionist movement and its aims by being an organ of 
Zionism and provingto be one of its known agents emulating its corrupt and renegade 
figures in the Arab world. 


Thus, with such insolence, Hassan II wanted to defame the Arab nation's struggle and to 
overturn all historic facts which affirm that the struggle against the Zionist enemy is 
civilizational conflict between the Arab nation and Zionism. There could be no 
co-existence between them. Hassan II departed from his written text and told the 
journalists: "I affirm that our presence in Washington, if we want to be logical, leads 
us to say that we want to live in peace with a recognised Israel. Otherwise, we would 
not have been here." 


Undoubtedly, the main outcome is that Hassan II has not yet learnt from history. But let 
Hassan II remember that his fate will be similar to that of traitor As-Sadat and traitors 
King ‘Abdallah, Nuri Sa'id and Idris Sanussi who thought that our great Arab nation would 
accept imperialist schemes aimed at imposing Zionism on the Arab homeland. 


The clear and official recognition of the Zionist enemy by Hassan II and his intentional 
distortion of truth about the history of the Arab nation and his ignoring its national 
struggle and cultural conflict with the Zionist enemy, comes as declared stand on a 
professional tradesmen in politics and of a king who has linked himself through his 
reign with U.S. imperialism and Zionist circles which the Moroccan Jewish community 
form one of its important links.

The undeclared content of this stand is inspired from the strong ties of the puppet 

king with U.S. imperialist plots and his policies which meet with the interest of the 
Zionist enemy. Through the axes opened by the capitulatory Camp David accords, Hassan II 
tried to say it all with the American President whose hands are still stained with the blood 
of the civilians massacred in Sabra and Shatila. 


He intended to show his loyalty to the U.S.A. and the Zionist enemy as obedient servant 
and subjugated slave in an ideal way and geared himself to serve their interests and 
objectives in the area challenging the whole Arab nation and distorting its history with 
countless heroic acts and sacrifices. 


In this respect, he alleged that the conflict between the Arab nation and the Zionist 
enemy is that of law and rights and that it is no longer a conflict of power, culture 


or history. 


JABIR, HUNGARIAN DEFENSE MINISTER HOLD TALKS 


LD311614 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 31 Oct 82 


[Text] A meeting was held this morning between Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief 
of the Arab Libyan Armed Forces, and Gen Lajos Czinege, Hungarian minister of defense, 

who is currently visiting the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. The meeting 

was attended by members of the delegation accompanying the guest minister and by a number 

of officers of the Arab Libyan Armed Forces. 


Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, when welcoming the Hungarian minister, pointed out that 

the meeting is one of a series held between the two countries contributing to deepening 
joint understanding and relations between the two peoples, which have witnessed tangible 
development in all spheres since the Great Fatah. 


Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir said the vicious assault by imperialism on the forces of freedom 
and progress in the world, and which is now taking place in the Arab region, and our courageous 
challenge to this aggression, is proof of our solid stand vis-a-vis the cause of freedom 

and progress. The brigadier noted the unlimited military and political support by U.S. 
imperialism for the Zionist train of death which is sweeping over the whole pan-Arab 
existence from the Atlantic to the Gulf. It swept over and occupied Palestine, it 
temporarily took Egypt from the Arab region, and recently it swept over Lebanon at artillery 
fire distance from Damascus. All this places on the shoulders of the Jamahiriyah and its 
revolutionary forces pan-Arab responsibilities to halt the Israeli train of death driven 

by an American energy. The Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, and all the living forces of its 
nation, are struggling to deter this aggression. In doing so it allies itself with all 
forces of progress in the world, foremost, the socialist countries, headed by the Soviet 
Union, the natural and historic ally of the Arab nation and which strengthened the struggle 
of the Arab nation throughout the years of struggle under the leadership of Jamal 

‘Abd an-Nasir. 


Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir pointed out that the socialist countries should comprehend 
this stage, the stage of the progressive Arab forces, struggle against imperialism, 
Zionism and reaction, and also the repercussions of this on the cause of freedom and 
progress in the world. U.S. imperialism is aiming to make the Arab region a region of 
influence and occupation tied to its strategy. It uses the Israelis as a spearhead to 
plunge into the Arab body, and uses Arab reaction as a tool to bridle the potential of 
the Arab mases and to prevent them from massing their material and moral resources to 
confront the Zionist entity from which the train of death is released and behind which 
stands imperialism.

Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir added that any look at the map of the Arab homeland without an 
examination of the revolutionary progressive vision leads friendly forces to adopt postures 
which are not of an equivalent deterrence to the imperialist-Zionist aggression against the 
Arab lands. Subsequently, the ferocity of the imperialist aggressive actions are increased 
and the burden of repelling the aggression falls on the shoulder of the Arab progressive 
forces, which are headed by and (?vanguarded by) the Jamahiriyah. 


Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir made a speech in which he stressed that the Jamahiriyah has 
decided to courageously challenge the Zionist train of death to stop it, because it threatens 
the existence of the Arab nation and destroys its hopes over its own land. This choice is 

a necessity and not just a duty. He pointed out that the struggle against this sweeping 
danger of the three heads -- imperialism, Zionism and reaction -- is producing new complica- 
tions for the Arab strugglers, hence the Arab masses look to all their friends to support 
their struggle against the common enemy. They consider the cause of freedom to be indivisable 
and the struggle against the Arab nation to be a common objective. 


The Hungarian minister noted the growing relations between Arab Libyan and Hungarian peoples. 
He pointed out that Hungary will stand with the Arab people in confronting the imperialist- 
Zionist aggression because it represents a common danger. The Hungarian minister expressed 
his appreciation for the warmth of the hospitality extended him.
