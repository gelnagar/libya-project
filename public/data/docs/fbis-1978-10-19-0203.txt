LIBYA

MAURITANIA'S SALECK MAKES PRESS STATEMENT BEFORE LEAVING 


ID182152Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 18 Oct 78 LD 


[Excerpt] Before leaving Tripoli today Moustapha Ould Mohamed Saleck, chairman of the 
Military Committee for National Recovery and head of the Mauritanian Government, made 
a statement to the press in which he said: It gives me pleasure to express, in my 
own name and in the name of the delegation accompanying me, the feelings of joy and 
happiness that we have felt since our arrival in the land of the great 1 September 
revolution, because we felt that we were among our own families amd friends, 


Ve. 19 Oct 78 ~* NORTH AFRICA 


Brother Mustapha Saleck praised the great role that the Libyan Arab people play on the 
Pan-Arab and international levels. In this connection he praised the aid that the 
great 1 September revolution is giving to the fraternal Mauritanian people. He rea- 
ffirmed that the 10 July corrective movement in Mauritania was staged in order to 
Support right and fight falsehood. It also brought an appreciation of the role being 
played on the Arab and international levels by the great 1 September revolution and a 
realization that Mauritania shares the same interests with Libya. He said: We realize 
the prominent role that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah can play in 
halting the bloodshed in the western region of the Arab homeland. We also believe in 
the necessity of creating a suitable atmosphere for the establishment of fruitful co- 
operation among the countries of the western part of the Arab homeland in the service 
of the peoples of these countries and as a contribution to the realization of compre- 
hensive Arab unity. 


Brother Saleck reaffirmed that the 10 July movement is determined to work cont inuously 
to establish peace and understanding in place of the fighting among the brothers of 
this region, He said that the Mauritanian Islamic Republic fully realizes its heavy 
responsibility to contribute to achieving a final solution and that it will spare no 
effort to meet that responsibility.
