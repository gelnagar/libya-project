Summary (AI): A 1986 Libyan TV broadcast of an interview with Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, conducted by Soviet journalists in Tripoli on 23 April, in which he discusses the April 1986 US air raids, NATO and US fleet maneuvers, and Vietnam. It mentions the US raid only in general terms and gives no Tripoli-specific places, sites or casualty figures.
• Interview recorded 23 April 1986 in Tripoli by a team of Soviet journalists from TV, TASS, Novosti, Pravda and Izvestiya.
• Qadhdhafi says the US 6th Fleet could have been attacked and that Libya stopped short of destroying the US wireless station at Lampedusa, Italy.
• He claims Libya shot down F-111 bombers and that US pilots died after parachuting.
• He refers to a Nonaligned Movement delegation that visited Libyan hospitals and saw dead children and wounded.
• 1986-04-15: US air raids on Libya (referred to by Qadhdhafi as the aggression); he says Libyan forces downed or drove off raiding aircraft, without giving Tripoli-specific sites
