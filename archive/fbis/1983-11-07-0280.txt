LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Asks King Fahd To Postpone Summit 
LDO51406 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
5 Nov 83 


[Text] JANA has learned that within the framework of contacts 
and consultations between the brother commander of the rev- 
olution and King Fahd Ibn ‘Abd al-‘Aziz, the king of Saudi 
Arabia, the brother commander has asked King Fahd to postpone 
the call for the forthcoming Arab summit conference in Riyadh 
until after the Arab situation becomes more clear, to avoid failure 
of the conference and to insure that it results in decisive fateful 
decisions that can face up to the collapse and deterioation the 
Arab world is witnessing as a result of the ferocious crusading 
offensive 


Talks With Czechoslovakia Begin in Tripoli 
LD061609 Tripoli JANA in English 1503 GMT 6 Nov 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Safar 1, November 6, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The joint Libyan Arab-Czechoslovak committee 
started its meetings in Tripoli this morning to discuss cooperation 
between the Jamahiriyah and Czechoslovakia in all fields. This 
morning's meeting was attended by the General People’s Com- 
mittee secretary of marine transport and communications and on 
the Czechoslovak side by the Minister of Foreign Trade Bohumil 
Urban. Discussions during the meeting centered on methods of 
bolstering bilateral cooperation. Agreement was also reached on 
the formation of three technical subcommittees to discuss 
bilateral cooperation in commerce and cable and wireless com- 
munications.
