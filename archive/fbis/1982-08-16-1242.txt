PEOPLE'S CONFERENCE CALLS FOR MILITANT ACTION 


LD131406 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 13 Aug 82 


[Excerpt] The masses of the Arab Palestinian People's Conference held an emergency session 
in (Al-Bayan al-Awal) last night. All Palestinian organizations and Arab revolutionary 
forces in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah took part in this session. 


The masses of the Arab Palestinian People's Conference discussed at this session the 
current grave Arab situation and adopted a number of resolutions in which they called for 
the pooling of the Arab revolutionaries within a military framework to carry out a serious 
action that would shake the seats of the traitorous Arab rulers. 


The masses of the conference also decided at the session to form revolutionary committees 
in the occupied territories and in the Arab states and to open camps for the training of 
Arab revolutionaries to save the Arab people from the dangerous turning point the Arab 
rulers have created to tear apart the Arab nation; to set up clandestine military cells 

to strike at the imperialist interests that have implanted the Zionist enemy in the Arab 
homeland and furnished it with the means of aggression; to set up a unified Arab army under 
the leadership of Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 1 September Revolution, 
and to assert three no's of Khartoum and stand against the evil plans aimed at our Arab 
masses. 


The masses of the Arab Palestinian People's Conference also have decided to set up a pan- 
Arab congress that would include the Arab revolutionaries to confront the challenges our 
Arab nation is experiencing and to check the invasions to which they are being subjected; 
to reject all capitulatory solutions and not count on personalities regardless of their 
reputation; to bring about compulsory military training for all factions of the Palestinian 
people and to expose the role of Arab reaction represented by the rulers who have agreed 

to receive the Palestinian fighters so that they would be thousands of miles from the 
battle ground; to implement the resolutions of the Palestinian People's Conferences which 
were endorsed by the General People's Congress; and to implement the resolutions of the 
first meeting of the Palestinian Arab revolution forces.
