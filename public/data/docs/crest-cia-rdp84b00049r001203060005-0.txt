SUBJECT: Topic For Discussion at DCI Breakfast Meeting with Secretary 
Haig on 25 August 1981 

Worldwide Libyan Activities of Concern to the U.S. Government 

The Qadhafi government in Libya is continuing a wide range of 
activities which are opposed to, and in many cases are a direct threat 
to, U.S. interests in the Middle East and other areas of the world. 
Among the primary activities of current concern are: 

- Continued support for international terrorist groups and 
national liberation movements in Africa, the Middle East, and other 
areas. 

- The new trilateral treaty among Libya, Ethiopia, amd PDRY, 
which poses a threat to Egypt, Sudan, Somalia, North Yemen, Saudi 
Arabia, and Oman. 

- Qadhafi's support to radicals in Central America[ 25X1

interest to Qadhafi.

- Continued Libyan military occupation of Chad.

- Continued Libyan opposition to, and attempted sabotage of,

- Qadhafi's assumption, in the coming year, of the Chairmanship
