LIBYA

PLANNED U.S. MANEUVERS ‘MIGHT TRIGGER' WAR 


LD171626 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 17 Mar 82 


[Text] The U.S. Navy secretary said yesterday in a statement that the U.S. 6th Fleet in 
the Mediterranean will hold maneuvers in the Gulf of Sidra off Libyan shores during the 
next 6 months. The radio political affairs editor comments: In 1981 when the Gulf of 
Sidra was subjected to an attack by U.S. aircraft, the Reagan administration was seeking 
to drive the Arab Libyan people into a military confrontation against the United States 
[words indistinct]. But the Reagan administration failed to achieve its objective. 

For this reason, it has tried to use pressure that might lead to confrontation, but 
through other means, the means of economic blockade. 


Observers following U.S. policy on the jamahiriyah -- from the breaking of diplomatic 
relations to the military attack against the Gulf of Sidra, including the attempt to impose 
a total economic blockade against the Arab Libyan people, and ending with the recent 
statement by the U.S. Navy secretary on the military maneuvers that the U.S. administration 
intends to conduct in the Gulf of Sidra, as well as the information campaign launched by 

the Reagan administration against Arab Libyan people -- believe that these steps are nothing 
but preparations for an invasion of Libya and conditioning world opinion for such an 
invasion. 


The Reagan administration has made several aggressive moves against the Arab Libyan people,’ 
but the forthcoming military maneuvers in the Gulf of Sidra. are a blatant aggressive 

move against the Arab Libyan people. Observers say this is a clear attempt to invade the 
jamahiriyah. This is shown by all preparations for the maneuvers which, as stressed 

by the White House press spokesman [as heard] will take place during the next 6 months, 


and by the fact that they are to be conducted in the Gulf of Sidra amid allegations by 
U.S. circles that the waters of Sidra are international waters rather than Libyan terri- 
torial waters. 


It is worth noting that if such a U.S. intention toward Libya is true -- and this makes 
observers anticipate another U.S. involvement like that in the Vietnam war or worse -- 
then the jamahiriyah must prepare to confront a war that would be imposed on it, which 
might trigger a third world war, as well as various economic, geographic and international 
changes. 


Moreover, since the maneuvers the United States intends to conduct in the Gulf of Sidra 
would be inside Libyan territorial waters -- which would then mean an open attack against 
the territory of an independent state -- the jamahiriyah, because of its position in the 
framework of a strategic alliance with the world forces of revolution and progress which 
use the jamahiriyah as an international sanctuary to fight colonialism, Zionism, racism 
and reaction, will therefore not be alone in the confrontation in defense of its 
territorial integrity. 


The U.S. administration would then bear the full responsibility for whatever conse- 
quences might result and would place the security and peace of the region and the 


world in (?danger). 


AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES ARAB STUDENTS‘ MEETING 


LD171926 Tripoli JANA in English 1804 GMi 17 Mar 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 17 Mar (JANA) -- The revolution's leader has greeted the first ideo- 
logical symposium of Arab students studying in jamahiriyah currently being held here.

Addressing the symposium this afternoon, the leader opened his remarks by saying: "You 
have stepped along the correct path. You were a neglected quantity, much like the 
150 million Arabs living from the Atlantic to the Gulf: quantity devoid of quality." 


He added: "The life which I mean is not the life of eating and drinking, but that of 
this nation's existence facing danger from the big powers, for America is determined 
to expand the Zionist entity and to back it so as it becomes a substitute for the Arab 
nation's entity. The issue during this state is that of a threatened national 
existence in which life loses its value. The important thing is not material life, 
for we can live as poor but free. Such a life would be of value." 


The leader stressed that the Zionist enemy's design is aimed at the land and against the 
Arab people. He said: "Your gathering is the starting point along the correct path. 

In this gathering, a programme of national endeavour should be drawn. This gathering 
should not be a fleeting meeting but a nationalist gathering which would succeed in 
creating a movement fully independent of the present state of association with the 
Zionists, Americans and reaction. The present Arab actuality is sick and must be 
destroyed.... The new generation must sweep away all the stockpiled debris in the Arab 
arena and combust it with the flames of people's revolution." 


He said: "The Arab people should attain power and enter the battle affirming our 
existence, for the Arab people today are excluded from the battle. Thus, Arab youth 
should set a nationalist programme for liberating the Arab nation and for its unity." 


He stressed: "The solution is to create a comprehensive and wide ranging nationalist 
movement of conquest which would remove the borders and barriers and push the masses 
into the arena of action and battle, so as to resist imperialism, reaction and 
instruments of domination." 


He pointed out: "Without such an orientation, the Arab nation is doomed to slavery 
and Zionism, and this nation could be obliterated to be replaced by the manufactured 
Zionist entity. This nation could become a destitute nation due to the Zionist march. 
History affirms this, for there were nations which were obliterated in the world, 

such as the red Indians." 


The leader warned that the Arab nation is passing through a grave stage, which could 
lead to the end of the Arab nationality. He said: "Despite this, there is now 
potential to unite our forces and to work for confronting the enemy." 


FRENCH FAMILY CONVICTED OF SPYING PARDONED 


NC171859 Paris AFP in English 1823 GMT 17 Mar 82 


[Text] Valenciennes, France, 17 Mar (AFP) -- A French woman and her two sons, sentenced 
to life in prison in Libya for spying, have been pardoned by Libyan President [as 
received] Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, it was learned here today. 


Denise Dupont, 58, Alain Dupont, 25, and Jean-Claude Dupont, 21, were arrested in 
Tripoli in April, 1980, convicted of "spying for American imperialism and Zionism," 
and sentenced last February 20 to life in prison. 


News of the pardon was transmitted in Paris by officials of the Libyan Embassy [as 
received] to the mayors of the towns of Thiant and Haulchin, where the Duponts lived 
before going to Libya.
