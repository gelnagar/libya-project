MEETINGS BETWEEN U.S., TUNISIAN OFFICIALS REPORTED 
1D222208 Tripoli JANA in English 1843 GMT 22 Feb 80 ID 


{Text] Tunis, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCYe«The puppet regime in the protectorate of Tunisia 
continues its consultations with officials of the Carter administration to get more support 
in confronting the popular uprising throughout the country. 


The Tunisian Foreign Minister Mohamed Fitouri met with the U.S. ambassador to the United 
Nations who arrived in Tunis yesterday coming from occupied Palestine, said Radio Tunis. 
The radio did not disclose issues discussed, but observers say the meeting was centered 
around the U.S, support to its puppet and his junta. 


This is confirmed by a report submitted by the Tunisian ambassador to Washington to Habib 
Bourguiba in which he expressed his satisfaction toward the U.S. stand. Other reports said 
that Hedi Nouira also met with the American official and discussed with him the deteriorating 
situation in Tunisia as a result of the popular uprising.

BULGARIAN FOREIGN MINISTER*S LETTER TO AT-TURAYKI CITED 
1D241058 Tripoli JANAin English 0821 GMT 24 Feb 80 ID 


([Text] Tripoli, Feb 24, JAMAHIRTYAH NEWS AGENCY--The foreign minister of Bulgaria said in 
the letter sent to Foreign Secretary Dr. ‘Ali Abd as-Salam at-Turayki that the French 
invasion of Tunisia is aimed at world revolutionary and progressive forces, especially 
the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, 


He said Bulgaria is closely following the aggressive activities of the United States and 

its allies in NATO which are aimed at realising U.S. political and military hegemony and 

the quelling of popular revolutions especially in Iran and the Arab region. He said attempts 
at using internal events which have recently taken place in Punisia is a Clear example of 
Bulgaria's fears, He added that the invasion is part of an international colonialist con- 
spiracy which includes the Arab world, the Mediterranean and Africa, 


He said colonialist anti-revolutionary forces which support capitulationist policies, are 
trying to create a premeditated atmosphere of tension regarding the Jamahiriyah. This 
action, he added carries great dangers regarding the Arab region's security and peace, 
because Libya's struggle against the capitulationist policy and the U.S, and its allies 
attacks is plain for all to see, 


The Bulgarian foreign minister affirmed his country’s support for the policies of the grand 
Al-Fatah revolution against Zionist and colonialist schemes,
