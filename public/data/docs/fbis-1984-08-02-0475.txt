LIBYA

‘Terrorist Reagan’ Prevents Olympic Reporting 
LDO11553 Tripoli JANA in English 1438 GMT | Aug 84 


[Text] Tripoli Dhulgidah 4, August | JAMAHIRIYA NEWS 
AGENCY — (Mulud Araybi Shalgo) head of the Libyan press 
delegation to the Olympic Games of Los Angeles has denounced 
the refusal of the American Government to allow the Libyan 
Arab press delegation to report the Olympic Games. He 
described the American measure as repressive and terrorist one 
as a part of a series of American hostility stands against the 
people of the Jamahiriyah. He said in an interview with the 
Jamahiriyah sports newspaper due to be published tomorrow that 
“the government of terrorist Reagan has no other attention or 
business but the Jamahiriyah”. He went on to say, “It has taken 
a new hostile position against us by refusing our three pressmen 
delegation to enter America to participate in reporting the games 
and following this international sports events”. [as received] 


He added, “We hoped as sport reporters to follow closely the 
Olympic Games and write about it as an important international 
event and an important historical opportunity confirming the 
noble human relations between peoples but the American Gov- 
ernment on motives of hatred and inciting spirit of entity against 
the Libyan Arab people had stopped that from happening”. He 
explained, “It was not strange for this government to take an 
action like this because it is the most reactionary, underdevei- 
oped, right wing, terrorist and anti-liberty and progress govern- 
ment”. 


He confirmed this decision comes as part of the mounting 
aggressive terrorist activities carried out by the American Gov- 
ernment and supervised by Reagan himself against the people of 
the Jamahiriyah. 


He said, “The decision to refuse the Libyan Arab press mission 
entry to America was another terrorist act to be added to the 
terrorist actions carried out by the government of Reagan against 
the Jamahiriyah”’. 


(Mulud Shalgo), head of the Libyan press delegation explained, 
“The government of terrorist Reagan knows perfectly well we 
were sports reporters and when it claimed that we were not, it 
was a lie, and they knew that it was a lie because we were sports 
reporters and had an official identification confirming that we 
are members of sports press community in the Jamahiriyah”. He 
pointed in this respect that “I am personally a sports journalist 
and editor of the Jamahiriya sports newspaper published by the 
Secretariat of the General People’s Committee for Sports in the 
Jamahiriyah and my colleague Ibn Khalal is a photographer with 
the same newspaper and the other colleague, Khalifa “Abd 
as-Sadiq is an editor of the sports page of the daily newspaper 
AL-FAJR AL-JADID. 


(Mulud Araybi Shalgo) pointed out that the Libyan press del- 
egation had carried out lengthy efforts with the American 
Embassy in Bonn to allow the Libyan press delegation to enter 
America. He said, “In spite of showing invitation cards addressed 
to us by the Organizing Committee of Los Angeles Olympics to 


attend the games, these efforts were rendered useless”. He made 
it clear that the government of terrorist Reagan by this action 
did not give any weight or attention to morality and human 
standards and respect of international conventions and laws and 
had violated the Olympic Charter and norms of the Olympic 
Games. 


He called in this respect the International Olympic Committee, 
pressmen association and other international organizations and 
associations to condemn this deliberate preplanned inhumane 
stand taken against the Libyan journalists and denounce these 
terrorist practices. 


The head of the Libyan press delegation said, “This disgraceful 
American terrorist action was preplanned because America had 
enough time to make sure that if we were sports reporters or not, 
but for deliberate aggressive motives the government of Reagan 
had taken this action aimed at inciting world public opinion 
against the Great Al-Fatih Revolution by spreading hatred alle- 
gations and unfounded claims to accuse the Jamahiriyah of 
terrorism”. [sentence as received] 


The head of the Libyan press delegation concluded his interview 
with the Jamahiriyah sports newspaper due to be published 
tomorrow raised questions by asking, “By what right or logic the 
government of terrorist Reagan forbids the Libyan sports press 
mission from participating in this Olympics? This is not the 
Olympics of America and was not established for America 
organized for it, but it is an international game and every state 
has the right to participate in it and it is a great opportunity for 
understanding, dialogue, love and peace presented by the inter- 
national community to its members, all members and not for only 
one of them” [sentence as received] 


Libyan-Italian Joint Committee Agreements Reviewed 
LDO11102 Tripoli JANA in English 0935 GMT | Aug 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulgidah 4, August 1, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The secretary of the People’s Committee 
for Foreign Liaison People’s Bureau met yesterday with Giulio 
Andreotti, the Italian foreign minister. The meeting reviewed 
what the Libyan Arab-Italian Joint Economic and Social Corn- 
mittee has agreed on. During the meeting, it was confirmed on 
the need of giving bilateral cooperation between the countries 
special attention in order to achieve the common interest of the 
Libyan Arab and Italian peoples. 


Andreotti Issues Departure Statement 
LDO11107 Tripoli JANA English 0941 GMT | Aug 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulgidah 4, August 1, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Giulio Andreotti, Italian foreign minister, 
left Tripoli last night ending a visit to the Jamahiriyah during 
which he headed the Italian delegation in the meetings of the 
fourth [as received] session of the Libyan Arab-Italian Joint 
Committee for Economic and Social Cooperation. The commit- 
tee ended these meetings last night in Tripoli. Giulio Andreotti 
gave a press release to JANA correspondent in which he praised 
the results of his visit to the Jamahiriyah and the agreements 


signed to open the day for more intensive efforts and extending 
cooperation relations between the two countries to include differ- 
ent fields. 


He said, “My visit to the Jamahiriyah is an important one due 
to the political results achieved. We carried out a series of talks 
about a number of international issues of common interest”. 


Arab, African Delegations Arrive for Session 
LD020002 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2315 GMT | Aug 84 


[Text] Delegations to the work of the seventh session of the 
Permanent Committee on Arab-African Cooperation, due to 
begin tomorrow in Tripoli, continue to arrive. The following 
arrived this evening: Peter Onu, OAU secretary general; Dr 
as-Sadiq Rashid, director of the Social Union, Human and 
Environmental Settlements in the African Economic Committee; 
Arondo Saryasa, leader of the Angolan delegation; Brother 
Mohamed al-Amin Ould Yahya, Mauritanian delegate to the 
Arab League; and Brother Ahmad ash-Shihabi, deputy 
chairman of the Economic Department of the PLO. 


The following also arrived this evening at Tripoli International 
Airport: Augustine Stevens, minister of state for foreign affairs 
in Sierra Leone; Dr Asamoah, Ghana foreign minister; Dr 
Edward Soma, director general of FAO; Brother Muhammad 
{name indistinct], deputy under secretary at the Syrian Foreign 
Ministry; and Mohamed Ali Hammad, Somali minister of state 
for foreign affairs. Also arriving in Tripoli this evening brother 
Yaqub al-Kindi, foreign under secretary in the United Arab 
Emirates; and Yami Al-Hadu, director of the office of Brother 
Ahmad Mahtar Mbow, UNESCO secretary general, to repre- 
sent him at the meeting of the Permanent Committee on Arab- 
African Cooperation; and the ambassador of Ethiopia to Paris.
