QADDAFI IS SAID TO VOICE THREAT AGAINST REAGAN
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp90-00965r000100160038-6
https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-cia-rdp90-00965r000100160038-6

Summary (AI): A Washington Post column (13 October 1981) reports that Qaddafi vowed to go ahead with plans to assassinate President Reagan after US jets shot down two Libyan jets, and describes Libyan-linked killings of exiles and ties to ex-CIA agents Edwin Wilson and Frank Terpil. The CIA denies planning any assassination of Qaddafi.
• Qaddafi reportedly planned new assassination plots against Sadat 'every three to six months', per one intelligence source.
• A Libyan group called 'Free Unionist Officers' vowed to attack US interests anywhere and seek the physical liquidation of Americans.
• The CIA reportedly planned to use a poisoned dart against Qaddafi, per the column; the CIA denies planning or discussing any assassination attempt.
• Wilson is alleged to have arranged an assassination attempt on another Qaddafi critic via three anti-Castro Cubans who once worked for the CIA.
• 1981-08: US fighters shot down two Libyan jets over the Mediterranean; weekend afterward Qaddafi reportedly discussed plans to assassinate Reagan in a phone call with Ethiopian leader Mengistu Haile Mariam
• 1980-04: Qaddafi warned Libyan expatriates to come home or suffer physical elimination; at least 10 anti-Qaddafi exiles murdered since then in Italy, West Germany, Britain, Greece and the United States (10 killed)
• 1981-07: Edwin Wilson, hiding in Tripoli, made a secret visit to Rome to meet US officials
