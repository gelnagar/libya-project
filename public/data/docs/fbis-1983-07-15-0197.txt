LIBYA

JANA Editor Denies Libyans Captured in Chad 
LD1 50436 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 14 Jul 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 14 Jul (JANA) — The renegade Hissein Habre 
claimed that a number of Libyans have been taken prisoner and 
that those alleged prisoners were captured during the battles 
between the clique of renegade Hissein Habre and the Chadian 
National Liberation Army. 


The political editor of JANA has commented on these allega- 
tions: 


The mutinious clique’s allegations that Libyans have been taken 
prisoner by that clique are a mere hypocricy and fabrication. The 
refutation of these allegations made by the correspondents cov- 
ering the fighting between the national liberation forces and the 
forces of Hissein Habre’s clique were contradictory, which con- 
firms that they have intentionally lied to cover up the successive 
defeats they have sustained at the hands of the Transitional 
National Union Governmentforces.
