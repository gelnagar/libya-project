LIBYA

TRIPOLI REPORTS ABC INTERVIEW WITH AL—QADHDHAFI 


LDO71558 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 7 Dec 81 


[Text] The leader of the revolution described the allegations and falsifications spread 
by the American administration against Libya and the accusations about sending alleged 
squads to America to assassinate President Reagan as a terrorist policy by the American 
administration against the Jamahiriyah. The leader, in an interview last night relayed 
directly by the American television network ABC, said: We reject American claims and 
accusations and we challenge Reagan's administration to give proof or evidence of its 
claims and falsifications. 


The leader stressed that we reject assassination methods and we do not accept the 
assassination of anyone. It is not in our nature, character or behavior to assassinate 
people. However, this is America's behavior and several times it tried and carried out 
a number of attempts to get rid of Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi. He stressed that these 
blatent attempts were debated in the United States by CIA, the Congress, the American 
Government and President Reagan. It has become a fact well known to the world. 


The brother leader made it clear that he is not happy with the assassination of any 
person, but he is happy when a person who pursues a wrong policy disappears. He said: 

I am not against As-Sadat as As-Sadat, I am not against Reagan as Reagan, but I am 
against their policies. As-Sadat was a bad person. He was a man of war, for he stopped 
the war on the eastern front and started it on the western front. The region is rid 

of him, because of his murder. Many things vanished and were buried with him. Regarding 
Reagan, I believe that all peoples are against him, even American citizens themselves, 
for one American citizen fired at him because he hated him. He is a man of war who is 
driving the world toward a major catastrophe and his administration is the worse 
administration we have witnessed in Arerica. 


In reply to a question about the motives that drive America to spread these false 
accusations against the Jamahiriyah, the leader made it clear that America wants us to 
turn into slaves. We refuse to be slaves for America and, hence, it places us in the 
catagory of one of its enemies. He said: We would like to be a free and nonaligned 
state. However, America would like to dominate the world and would like to divide it 
into masters and slaves. He expressed his belief that the Zionist propaganda and the 
ruler of Sudan, with some of the elements who deserted from military service, were 
behind the spread of false information, upon which the American administration based 
its accusations and falsifications against us. 


The brother leader ridiculed President Reagan because he believes such information and 
allegations. He stressed that Reagan believes this because he is stupid and ignorant. 
The one who supplied him with this information was the lying Numayri who is running away 
from his people and who is asking for Reagan's protection, as are some Libyan students 
who are escaping from military service and who find it to be in their interest to 
spread such lies and misleading information. He said: We are prepared to investigate 
this information and to expose the liar. Reagan is the one who is lying. He stressed 
that America daily, militarily, psychologically and economically exercises pressure 

and terrorism against us. This is a terrorist policy against us and we are prepared to 
face it. We will face it with all the peoples who suffer from American pressures and 
terrorism, whether in the Arab homeland or in the whole world. 


8 Dec 81 Q7 NORTH AFRICA 


The brother leader referred to the American terrorist aggression in the Gulf of Sidra. 
He stressed that the Jamahiriyah once again will challenge such an aggression if it is 
committed against us, from the concept of defending ourselves, our freedom and honor. 
Regarding the joint American-Egyptian military maneuvers, the leader of the revolution 
stressed that the Arab people do not consider them maneuvers but an aggression against 
Arab territory. 


The brother leader replied to a question about the possible improvement of Libyan-Arab- 
American relations. He expressed his belief that there is no possibility of changing 
all this until the American people themselves change Reagan and his bad administration. 
The brother leader advised the American people to work to overthrow Reagan the way they 
overthrew Nixon to make it possible to establish good relations not only with the 
Libyan-Arab people but also with the peoples of the world. He stressed that President 
Reagan does not deserve to lead America as a major power because he is stupid and behaves 
like a child. 


The brother leader spoke about the decision of the American oil company Exxon to 
terminate its services in Libya. He stressed thet this company's decision came as a 
result of pressures by the Reagan administration and in response to the request of this 
administration. He said that the Jamahiriyah prepared for all contingencies to face any 
situation in this respect. 


He explained that such a posture by arerica is considered proof that Reagan's policy is 
working for the destruction of the interests of the companies and American people. He 
pointed out that Exxon benefited from its work in Libya in the same way that Libya 
benefits from the work of this company. However, Reaganis working for the destruction 
of the interests of this company and the interests of the other American companies and 
for the destruction of the interests of the American people themselves. He said: The 
American people and the American Congress should comprehend the gravity of this wrong 
policy which is endeavoring to destroy the interests of the people in Libya. He 
stressed that Reagan is bent on forcing American companies to leave Libya. 


At the end of this interview with ABC, the brother leader repeated his call tc investigate 
the lies and allegations made by Reagan's administration. Ye expressed the Jamahiriyah's 
readiness to accept arbitration to find the truth about thes: lies and fabrications and 
the dimensions of this hostile policy against the people of the Jamahiriyah.

[Text] Tripoli, 7 Dec (JANA) -- The interview carried out by the American T.V. network 


ABC with Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qa~hdhafi and broadcast live last night via satellite has 
created further repercussions in America and throughout the world.

Our corres;jondent in Washington said many Americans were wondering why the U.S.

The correspondent said ABC T.V. received many phone calls following the interview calling

American Government to produce evidence in support of its accusations against Libya. 

ABC said if this evidence is not forthcoming, American public opinion will then realise 
that these allegations were made to infiltrate a totaly unfounded danger to serve other 
purposes.

In a separate report also broadcast later by ABC, the T.V. station said the American 
authorities have declined to produce evidence to substantiate the anti-Libyan accusations 
proving that the American story has no base in reality. 


Our correspondent further added that the majority of Washington political observers noticed 
after following the interview two important points. One was the good intention of the 
Jamahiriyah to establish good relations with the American people and that Libya was free 
from the accusations made by the Reagan administration since this administration did not 
possess any logical proof to substantiate these allegations... The second point was 

that Reagan's administration is exploiting thts concentrated campaign alleging the existence 
of a looming danger facing top American officials. The campaign is seen to have two 
purposes. 


1. Diverting the American people's anger following the failure of the administration's 
political, economic, social and military policies by fabricating an unfounded danger to 
the nation's security. 


2. This alleged danger is being exploited to justify its intention of strengthening the 
CIA and opening the United States to its terroristic and spying activities. 


Other observers pointed out that Israeli intelligence is concentrating all its efforts to 
intensify this campaign to divert the American people's enmity against Israel towards a new 
and fabricated enemy, Libya and Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. 


The U.S. broadcasting and T.V. network attached prominent attention to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's 
interview. The Arabic language radio station "the Voice of America" today quoted Colonel 
al-Qadhdafi's description of a Libyan hit team sent to America to assassinate President 
Reagan as a fabrication and that the U.S. administration received its information from 
suspect sources, such as Numayri, Zionist propaganda and Libyan students abroad who have 
dodged military service. 


"The Voice of America" went on to express Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's astonishment at U.S. 
fears from such a small country as Libya, specially as the U.S. is a great power with 
more than 200 million people. It also cited Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's description of 
President Reagan as naive and a liar. It said: "Colonel al-Qadtdhafi called for a com- 
prehensive investigation to be carried out on the issue to arrive at the truth and inform 
the American people's U.S. Congress and the Libyan Arab people.... 


"Colonel al-Qadhdhafi moreover, called on the Americans to rid themselves of Reagan as 
they had done before with President Reagan [as received]. 


"Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed his opposition to terror and accused the U.S. itself of 
exercising military, political and pschological terrorist actions against Libya. But, 
Libya will resist this U.S. onslaught as it had done in the Gulf of Sidra...." 


"The Voice of America" included Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's comment on the decision by the U.S. 
company Exxon to stop its services in Libya. He is reported to have attributed the move 
to pressure exerted by the U.S. administration against Libya. He expressed Libya's 
readiness, however, to meet all possible contingencies should the rest of the U.S. 
companies follow suit. 


The newspaper the New York TIMES included a lengthy article on the interview in its 
front page today. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi is quoted as saying: "We are certain we did not send anyone to 
assassinate Reagan or any other person in the world.... 


"President Reagan is naive and unqualified to lead America as a great power. America must 
rid itself of his administration and force his downfall as it had done with Nixon...."

TRIPOLI SAYS FIERCE BATTLES RAGE IN CHAD 


LD071242 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1200 GMT 7 Dec 81 


[Text] Fierce battles are now raging between the Northern Forces and the Chad forces in 
the towns of Abeche and Oum Hadjer. Oum Hadjer is expected to fall later today. It was 
learned from Ndjamena this morning that the forces from the lst Army have joined the 
North Army and that other [word indistinct] elements also have joined the Northern Forces. 
An intense exchange of fire took place between the forces of the Revolutionary Democratic 
Council and the lst Army on the one side and the forces sent from Ndjamena on the other. 
The exchange of fire took place between these sides in the town of Mongo. 


Now here is the latest news: Before noon today, the Chad Government forces succeeded in 
forcing the Northern Forces to retreat and drove them back inside Abeche after the Northern 
Forces tried to capture Oum Hadjer.

AIR TRANSPORTATION AGREEMENT -- An agreement for air transportation between Jamahiriyah 
and Rwanda was signed 10 November at the headquarters of the public administration for 
civil aviation. The agreement allows the aviation companies in both countries to make 
regular air trips between the two countries and for regular operations systems and customs 
exemptions. [Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 10 Nov 81 LD] 


TALKS WITH USSR, PAKISTAN -- The meetings of the seventh session of the joint Arab Libyan- 
Soviet committee for economic, scientific and technical cooperation between the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the Soviet Union began this morning in Tripoli. The 
committee decided today to set up a number of technical committees to study bilateral 
cooperation and to prepare protocols of cooperation to be signed at the end of the committee's 
meetings. The Joint Arab-Libyan-Pakistani committee for economic and social cooperation 

this morning held a meeting to study cooperation between Libya and Pakistan. During these 
meetings, the committee will study the already implemented agreements between the two 


countries and will prepare protocols of cooperation to be signed at the end of the committee's 
meetings. [Text] [Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 11 Nov 81 LD]
