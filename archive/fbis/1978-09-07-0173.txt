foreign nationals from Israel, including Israeli residents with foreign passports, 
[Israeli Interior Ministry decided to forbid the residents’ participation in the tour.

LABOR PARTY ESTABLISHES ARAB SOCIAL-POLITICAL CLUB 


TAO61441Y Jerusalem Domestic Service in Hebrew 1400 GMT 6 Sep 78 


[Text] The Labor Party's Arab Department has established a body called the social-political 
club for the party's Arab members. The idea is for this body to operate like the commercial 
and industrial clubs, 


The director of the Arab Department, Ra'anan Kohen, told our correspondent that this is the 
first body of its kind for self-employed and other educated people established by any party. 


The body's first meeting will take place at Bet Berl in about 2 weeks and the conclusions 
drawn from the Camp David summit will be discussed there with the participation of 


Shim'on Peres.

Text}; Last night, Prime Minister Ja'far Sharif-Emami said in a speech at a dinner 
eception given in honor of Japanese Prime Minister Takeo Fukuda: 


A new national life movement began in Iran half a century ago. It began after centuri 
of isolation and seclusion from important world intellectual and scientific currents. 
For the past 16 years, in particular, it has acquired greater impetus am speed. Iran 
has taken rapid strides toward industrialization, the creation of the necessary infra 
structure and the training of an efficient manpower. Iran's economic growth during 
these years bears comparison only with your country!s growth during the 2 decades of 
1950's and 1960's, 


Jf course, this rapid movement toward industrialization has been coupled with material 
shortcomings and failings and, recently, with social unrest. But, by overcoming 
difficulties, we are determined to open the way to a brilliant future for our country's 
future generation with an even tempo by drawing on past experience and by relying on 
our moral and national traditions. Along this path, our two countries! economic coopera- 
is of special importance to us. This cooperation has expanded in a remarkable 
in recent years; and, among other things, it is noteworthy that the volume of 


exchanged by the two countries in the past 10 years has increased by 10 and the 


from your country by 15. 


In the field of industrial cooperation, Sharif-Emami referred to the Bandar-e Shahpur 
petrochemical complex project as an outstanding example of the widening cooperation 
between the two countries. 


In reply, while expressing pleasure with his trip to Iran, Takeo Fukuda recalled the two 
countries! relations and the age-old Iranian-Japanese contacts. On the subject of the 
two countries!’ present relations, he said: Our economies complement each other: Iran 
is the main guarantor of Japan's oil, which is a substance which cannot be ignored in 
our economic development. In return, Japan is Iran's biggest commercial emporium. 


Takeo Fukuda emphasized the Japanese Government!s tremendous enthusiasm for the ever- 
expanding relations and cooperation between the two countries, and, after praising Iran'; 
all-round progress and two countries! role in safeguarding world peace, expressed the wish 
for an increasing strengthening of the cohesion between the two countries, which have 


devoted themselves to this continent's peace, progress and strength. 


The first round of talks between Iran and Japan took place yesterday in the presence of 
the two countries' prime ministers. An exchange of views took place on the expansion 
of trade and economic relations and the extension of various types of cooperation,
