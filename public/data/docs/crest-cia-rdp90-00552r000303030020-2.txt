Summary (AI): Chicago Sun-Times clippings from September 1980 report that a leaked secret CIA memo called Rev. Jesse Jackson a Libyan oil agent, which Jackson denied. The White House said the government had no evidence he was a Libyan agent. The story is about U.S. politics, with Libya as background only.
• Jackson wrote to the Libyan Embassy in Washington on behalf of the Wallace Oil Co. of Tuskegee, Ala., a black-owned oil concern.
• Jackson said he sent several letters to the Libyan government supporting opening trade routes between Libya and black America.
• Billy Carter registered with the Justice Department as a Libyan agent on July 14 under protest.
• American companies do $4 billion in business with Libya each year, according to the clippings.
• The CIA reportedly apologized to Jackson and opened an investigation into the leak.
• 1980-03-31: CIA Director Stansfield Turner reportedly passed the secret memo on Jackson to national security adviser Zbigniew Brzezinski
