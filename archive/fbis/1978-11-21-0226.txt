LIBYA=HUNGARY JOINT ECONOMIC COMMITTEE MEETS IN TRIPOLI 


LD210022Y Tripoli JANA in English 1632 GMT 20 Nov 78 LD 

+ 
[Text] Tripoli, 20 November, JANA--The Libyan Arab-Hungarian joint committee on 
economic and social cooperation contimed it's meeting this morning at the Foreign 
Secretariat in Tripoli, to discuss methods of promoting cooperation between the 
Jamahiriyah and Hungary, in all fields, 


According to sources at the cooperation department at the Foreign Secretariat, the 
committee will set a comprehensive program to implement the agreements reached during 
the recent visit of Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, to Hungary, 


The Libyan Arab delegation to these meetings includes representatives from the foreign, 
transport, trade, electricity, dams and water resources, education, agriculture and 
health secretariats,
