LIBYA

Tripoli Says USSR To Help Build Nuclear Power Station 
LD201448 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
20 Oct 84 


[Text] JANA has learned that serious talks are now in progress 
between the Secretariat for Energy in the Socialist Peoples 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the State Committee for Eco- 
nomic Affairs in the USSR, aimed at initiating cooperation in 
constructing the first electronuclear power station in Libya, 
which will be comprised of two ordinary pressured water reactors 
with a total capacity of 880 MW. The station will increase energy 
in the Jamahiriyah and will link all electric power stations now 
in operation with the nuclear station. 


The secretary for energy has stated that the Jamahiriyah nuclear 
program aims at the use of atomic energy for peaceful purposes 
and is in line with the Green Book, which calls for progress, 
emancipation, and peace. It is also in line with the desire to spread 
the green color, the color of development and life, throughout the 
world. We have made it a point to devote all our technical 
capabilities and resources and nuclear program to peaceful pur- 
poses. 


He said: We will begin implementation of the first electro- 
nuclear power station which will produce 880 MW of electricity.
