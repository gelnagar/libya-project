Summary (AI): A 1983 New York Post column (Rowland Evans and Robert Novak, as OCR suggests) reports that a leak about Muammar Qaddafi's plot against neighboring Sudan, and his possible retaliation, endangered U.S. agents and the handling of the story at the White House. The OCR text is fragmentary and mostly about U.S. politics rather than Tripoli itself.
• The column says a leak risked the lives of U.S. agents after Libyan leader Khadafy's plot against Sudan became known.
• The leak reportedly came probably from Pentagon officials, according to the text.
• The text mentions a U.S. AWACS radar plane dispatch to Egypt to monitor the situation.
• The text also refers to Pope John Paul II assassination investigation and Italian police and intelligence.
