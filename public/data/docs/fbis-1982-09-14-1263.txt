JANA EDITOR VIEWS 'FES SUMMIT OF TREASON’ 


LD122108 Tripoli JANA in English 1824 GMT 12 Sep 82 
[Quotation marks as received] 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulqidah 24, September 12, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The French news 
agency, commenting on the Fes summit and its decision to recognise the Zionist enemy, has 
pointed out that most Arab countries have clearly changed their tone towards the Israelis. 
by deciding to recognise the Zionist entity which they fought against since 1948. 


JUNA's political editor commented on the Fes decisions, to be added to the list of Arab 
reactionary treasonous acts. He said "The Fes summit resolutions, through collective 
recognition of the Zionist enemy, has affirmed the futility and wretchedness of Arab 
policy towards the Arab nation's enemies, 


This summit has shown that as though all the “Arabs" have abandoned the struggle by signing 
the document of recognising the Zionist enemy. [as received] Undoubtedly, this is a treason 
against the Arab nation from the Atlantic to the Gulf and a treacherous stab in the 

back against the pan-Arab national entity, which is struggling against the Zionist enemy 

for regaining the right of a people, made homeless, oppressed, tortured and killed. 


From the point of view of the Arab masses, the Fes summit is a summit of treason aimed 
at breaking the Arab nation's will and determination to continue the fight and to destroy 
the Zionist-American death wagon. 


This summit has followed the worst path in revealing this treason. It showed that its 
steps have the same content as those of American-Zionist schemes. Most Arab kings and 
presidents have also publicly expressed their desire to live in peace with the enemy, 

to unceasingly work under the American umbrelia and to show their active preparedness to 
strangle the Palestinian cause, 


A blatant example of this is the Fes summit decision to financially compensate those 
Palestinians who do not want to go back. This means that the Palestinian people who were 
forcibly made homeless and whose homes were usurped by the enemy and who went to the 

West Bank, Gaza and elsewhere, should give all their rights in their homeland in exchange 
for some money,

"This has beena Zionist demand for which Zionism has exerted all terrorist means to 
realize. Today, the Arab rulers in Fes not only have accepted this demand but also 
declared their readiness to be base brokers towards the most honourable and noble Arab 
cause after the Zionists failed to realize this demand since 1948. 


Thus, the Fes summit of treason has reaffirmed the meeting of interests of Zionism and 
Arab reaction, under the American umbrella, and categorically proven that America's 
friends are the Israelis' friends". 


JANA COMMENTS ON U.S. MANEUVERS IN ARABIAN SEA 


LD112216 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 11 Sep 82 


[Text] Washington, 11 Sep (JANA) -- During October, the U.S. administration will begin 
Rapid Deployment Force [RDF] maneuvers in the northwest section of the Arabian Sea. 


In addition to the RDF, units from the forces of the family of Al Sa'ud and forces of 
the agent Qabus are to take part. 


The U.S. administration is seeking the approval of Arab regimes to surrender further 
facilities for the use of their territories in the service of the strategic aims of the 
U.S. Pentagon.
