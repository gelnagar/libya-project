LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI GETS "URGENT CABLE' FROM PFLP HEAD 


LD092242 Tripoli JANA in English 1848 GMT 9 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 20, August 9 -- Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Colonel Mu'ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi, leader of the revolution, had received an urgent cable from Mr Nayif Hawatimah, 
secretary general of the Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine, on the latest 
developments in west Beirut. 


JOINT COMMUNIQUE ISSUED ON MACHEL VISIT 


LDO70904 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0815 GMT 7 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Aug (JANA) -- The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and Mozambique 
have condemned the imperialist, reactionary and Zionist conspiracies carried out by the 
Zionist entity with the support and approval of the U.S. administration against the Arab 
Palestinian and Lebanese peoples. 


The two sides, in a joint communique issued yesterday in Tripoli and Maputo at the end 

of the visit by Mozambique President Samora Machel to the Jamahiriyah, reaffirmed anew 

their support for the Arab Palestinian and Lebanese peoples in their resistance against 

the mass annihilation process being carried out by the Zionist enemy forces. The two 

sides strongly condemned the imperialist conspiracies, with the alliance of reaction, 

racism and Zionism, to strike at the OAU, to put an end to the unity of the African 
Continent, to obstruct the latter's efforts to achieve liberation from colonialism in all 
its forms and from racism and to realize prosperity and progress for the African peoples. 
They reaffirmed their determination to continue their efforts to confront these conspiracies. 


They appealed to all African states to take a decisive stand in confronting these con- 
spiracies in order to deprive the enemies of Africa of any opportunities, to foil their 
attempts and to preserve the unity of the African peoples. The two sides condemned the 
hostile policies of the racist regimes in southern Africa that are supported by U.S. 
imperialism against the peoples of South Africa, Namibia and the African confrontation 
states. 


Maputo Report 


EA080646 Maputo Domestic Service in Portuguese 0500 GMI 8 Aug 82 


[Text] Libya has expressed its readiness to join the frontline states in their firm 
commitment to the struggle for the total liberation of southern Africa, according to 
a Mozambique-Libya joint communique issued in Tripoli after President Samora Machel's 
visit to that country. 


The two states denounced the maneuvers aimed at imposing neocolonial solutions on the 
people of Namibia. 


The communique expresses the solidarity of Mozambique and Libya with the struggles of 
Latin Americans, particularly the peoples of El Salvador. 


The two sides congratulated Cuba, Nicaragua and Grenada-for their efforts to consolidate 
national independence and their revolutionary gains.
