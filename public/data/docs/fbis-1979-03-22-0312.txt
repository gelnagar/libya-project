TRIPOLI MARCHERS CALL FOR OVERTHROW OF AS-SADAT 
LD221052 Tripoli JANA in English 0802 GMT 22 Mar 79 LD 


(Text] Tripoli, 22 March, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--A huge people's march took place 

in Tripoli yesterday morning in denunciation of the defeatist capitulatory course and the 
shameful, humiliating and treacherous agreements being concluded by the defeated 

Egyptian president with the racist Zionist enemy under the umbrella of American imperialisn, 
and its ally, Zionist racism, 


Thousands of people in the Jamahiriyah joined a huge march from Green Square in Tripoli 
passing through Martyr Mohammad Mugaryif Street to the American Embassy. There the 
masses shoute* loud slogans in condemnation of the defeated Egyptian president and the 
agreements which have been signed, together with those still to be signed, with the 
Zionists. They announced that the (?Arab) nation will tear the agreements, as it is able 
to confront all the conspiracies plotted against it by American imperialism and 

its agent, the defeated president. 


The demonstrators raised green flags and the flag of Palestine, pictures of Colonel 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, and placards denouncing the attempts of the defeated Egyptian 
president which aim at harming the Arab cause and occupied Palestine. The placards 
carried such slogans as "The Capitulatory Humiliating Agreements and the Stamp of Shame 
on the Arab Nation," and the "Revolutionary Committees in the Arab Countries Will Lead the 
Arab Jamahiriyah to the Power of the People.” The voices of the demonstrators were also 
raised addressing the Egyptian people: “Let the brave take heed from Iran." The march 
then proceeded to the People's Palace where several speeches were delivered which, in turn, 
denounced the capitulatory course of the defeated president and the agreements being 
concluded under the supervision of American imperialism. The speakers called upon the 
peoples of the Arab nation to stand together against the capitulatory course so as to 
foil the agreements. They called upon the Arab people in Egypt to revolt and overthrow 
the defeated agent and thereby return to Egypt its right Arab visage and its national role 
in the Arab nation,

ECONOMIC, TECHNICAL PROTOCOL SIGNED WITH YUGOSLAVIA 
LD202002 Tripoli JANA in Mnglish 1805 GMT 20 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, March 20, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The secretary of the General People's 
Committee for Planning, Mr Musa Abu Furaywah and Mr Vuko Dragasevic, the member of the 
Yugoslav Federal Executive Council and chairman of the Committee on Cooperation with 

the Developing Countries have signed this afternoon a protocol on technical, scientific 
and economic cooperation between the Socialist People's Liban Arab Jamahiriyah and 
Yugoslavia, 


Ve 22 Mar 79 NORTH AFRICA 


Another protocol was signed on the mutual recognition of the two cowmtries! certifi- 
cates and academic degrees and their equivalents during the signing ceremony which 

took place at the Foreign Secretariat in Tripoli. Mr. Abu Furaywah delivered a 

short speech. He stressed that the agreement will greatly reinforce relations be- 

tween the two friendly peoples. "By signing this agreement we would have reached a 
leap forward with respect to the reinforcement of bilateral relations," he said. He 
added that the progress achieved during the meetings of the joint committee embodied the 
two countries! wish to promote cooperation. 


In turn, Mr. Dragasevic spoke stressing that the protocol is an important way to con- 
solidate relations between the Jamahiriyah and Yugoslavia, "We attach great importance 
to the good relations existing between our two countries and will endeavour to imple- 
ment this protocol," he said, adding that cooperation between the two countries will 
develop beyond the context of the agreement. "We also look forward to a bright future 
for the relations between the two friendly peoples," he said. 


Mr. Dragasevic expressed his satisfaction at the results achieved and the talks he 
conducted with Libyan Arab officials. He said, "During the talks we have come to 
realize the determination of officials in the Jamahiriyah to promote and reinforce 
relations. " 
The signing of this protocol in Tripoli today concluded the meetings of the fourth 
session of the Libyan Arab-Yugoslav Committee on Bilateral Cooperation in the 
Technical, Scientific and Economic F lds.
