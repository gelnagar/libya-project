LIBYA

CONGRESS LEADER SEEKS WAYS TO 'DAMAGE U.S, INTERESTS! 
LDO20010Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 1 Nov 78 LD 


[Excerpts] Tripoli--Umar al-Hamidi, secretary general of the Permanent Secretariat of 

the Arab People's Congress, explained that the Permanent Secretariat is a popular democratic 
organization embracing more than 200 political, popular and trade union organizations of 

the Arab homeland, 


At a press conference he gave at the Permanent Secretariat in Tripoli today, he said that 
the work of the secretariat stemmed from the rejection of political, moral and military 
defeat and the determination to liberate Palestine and all occupied Arab territories, 

He said that in the light of those two objectives, the Permanent Secretariat is working 
to create a progressive popular front committed to those objectives. 


He said that the Palestine armed struggle was one aspect of national revolution and that 
it was the responsibility of the Arab People's Congress to provide all the necessary 
conditions for steadfastness of the Palestine people and revolution and for all honest 
Arab strugglers in Egypt, Oman, Lebanon and the Western Sahara. He said that the 
Secretariat completed a draft charter which will serve as a national [word indistinct] 
for the creation of a progressive popular front,

On economic relations between Arab countries and the United States which supports the 
Zionist gangs, the secretary general said the Secretariat asked a group of experts to 
prepare a study on U.S.-Arab economic relations and to define the correct method of 
damaging U.S. interests. An economic conference will be held in the light of the study 
within the next few months. He said the secretariat is not against Americans, but against 
the U.S. regime. 


He said that the Permanent Secretariat believed that the Eritrean question should be 
Solved by peaceful means, and the people of Sahara should be supported in their call for 
self-determination.
