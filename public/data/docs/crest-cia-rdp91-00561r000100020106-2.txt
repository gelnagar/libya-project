Summary (AI): A 1984 New York Times article reports that the FBI surveilled the Libyan Mission to the UN from 1981, suspecting Libya funded black activists in the U.S. to support Qaddafi and oppose Reagan's re-election. The mission's former head, Ali A. Treiki, had just become Libya's Foreign Minister.
• FBI surveillance of the Libyan Mission to the UN was reportedly aimed at determining whether Libya illegally gave money to black activists in the U.S.
• Surveillance of the mission began in 1981 after authorities received information about a possible Libyan plan to assassinate (reported as a plan targeting) Kirkpatrick; text is partly garbled.
• Telephone wiretaps reportedly showed Libyans encouraging support among black activist groups to defeat Reagan's re-election campaign.
