LIBYA

Bulgaria’s Zhivkov, Delegation Arrive 6 Mar 
LD061518 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
6 Mar 84 


[Excerpts] A meeting was held midday today between the brother 
leader of the revolution and Todor Zhivkov, general secretary of 
the Central Committee of the Communist Party and chairman 
of the State Council in the People’s Republic of Bulgaria. 


The meeting was attended by Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad 
Jallud; Brigadier Abu Bakar Yunis Jabir, the commander-in- 
chief of the Armed Forces; and Major al-Khuwayldi al- 
Humaydi. The meeting was also attended by members of the 
delegation accompanying the Bulgarian chairman, which 
included Petur Mladenov, member of the Political Bureau of the 
Central Committee of the Bulgarian Communist Party and 
foreign minister; Aleksandur Petkov, chairman of the National 
Agro-Industrial Union, and Khristo Khristov, minister of foreign 
trade. 


Todor Zhivkov arrived in Tripoli this morning ona 3-day friendly 
and official visit to Jamahiriyah. 


The Bulgarian leader expressed his satisfaction and joy for 
visiting Jamahiriyah and for meeting the brother leader of the 
revolution. In a statement he made to JANA he said that the 
existing relations between our two countries are friendly, and 
developing continuously. In addition we intend to develop them 
to serve the interests of the two friendly peoples and to realize 
our joint aims for the establishment of peace in the world. 
Chairman Todor Zhivkov stressed that Jamahiriyah and Bul- 
garia stand against imperialism and nuclear blackmail and 
struggle for international peace and the establishment and the 
strengthening of relations among the peoples. He said: 1 am 
confident that my meeting with Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader 
of the revolution, who enjoys a prestigious positions in the Bulgar- 
ian people's and world quarters, will give a new impetus for the 
deepening and exiending relations between the two friendly 
peoples. 


At the end of his statement the chairman of the State Council of 
Bulgaria delivered the greetings of the Bulgarian people to the 
Libyan Arab people wishing them further progress and happi- 
ness. 


The Bulgarian leader is accompanied by a delegation which 
includes in its membership Petur Mladenov, member of the 
Political Bureau of the Central Committee of the Bulgarian 
Communist Party and foreign minister; Aleksandur Petkov, 
chairman of the National Agro-Industrial Union, Khristo Khris- 
tov, minister of foreign trade; Milan (Milanov), adviser of the 
Chairman of the State Council's office, and Aleksandur 
(?Kariyev), minister of construction and development [title as 
heard] and a number of the Bulgarian state and party officials.

Further Report on Al-Qadhdhafi 5 March Speech 
LD070343 Tripoli JANA in English 0831 GMT 6 Mar 84 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, Jumada Athani |, March 6, JAMAHIR- 
IYAH NEWS AGENCY — In his important speech yesterday 
evening the revolution’s leader said that as of today: “Libyan 
troops positioned in the Al-Biga’ valley must either join the 
fighting with the Lebanese people against the Israelis — if there 
is a need for them — or return to Syrian territory or the the 
Libyan Arab territory after having accomplished their mission. 


Colonel Al-Qadhdhafi confirmed the Libyan people’s solidarity 
with the Lebanese Muslim and Christian people and said that 
the Libyan people must make more efforts and sacrifices with 
their brothers in Lebanon. 


He also said that the Arab character of Lebanon has today been 
confirmed with the abrogation of the May 17 agreement which 
deprived Lebanon from its Arab character and was aimed at 
linking all Lebanon, land, air and sea to the Zionist and Ameri- 
can camp. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi confirmed that the abrogation of the May 
17 agreement has restored Lebanon to the Arab fold. “Therefore, 
we must give aid to the Lebanese people and stand by their side 
in defence against the Israeli forces which are accupying 
southern Lebanon until these are evicted and Palestine is liber- 
ated. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: We have decided to continue the 
struggle and to make more sacrifices. Libyans must come for- 
ward to volunteer or join the camps of training and fighting on 
the side of the Palestinian resistance, the Lebanese patriotic 
movement and the Lebanese people. We must also come forward 
to volunteer and join the training camps on the side of the 
Sudanese, the Syrian, the Palestinian and the Lebanese people. 
We do not believe that borders should separate Libya and Egypt, 
Libya and Sudan, Libya and Syria, Syria and Lebanon, Lebanon 
and Palestine. This is our Arab land, one land, one people and 
one nation and we will exert all efforts to liberate and unite it.

Libyan Role in Abrogation of 17 May Treaty Cited 
LD062342 Tripoli JANA in English 1743 GMT 6 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada Thani 1, Mar 6, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The Lebanese Cabinet held a historic 
session yesterday during which it announced the abrogation of 
the agreement which was signed on May 17 1983. By abrogating 
the May 17 agreement, the Arab masses have, once again, 
emerged triumphant over the forces of imperialism, Zionism and 
reaction. 


The Lebanese Arab people, furthermore, inflicted a humiliating 
defeat on the crusade American forces and forced them out of 
Lebanon to American warships off Lebanese shores. It is worth 
noting that the Lebanese people’s victory was not the result of 
political meeting and manoeuvring nor did it come about through 
U.N. lobbies or its organizations or through reactionary schemes, 
but was rather through sacrifices of the Arab dynamic forces. 


What has been accomplished today crowned the alliance between 
the Great Al Fatih Revolution, Syria, the Lebanese National 
Salvation Front, the New Revolutionary Movement within Fatah 
and the Islamic revolution in Iran in addition to the sacrifices of 
the Great Al Fatih Revolution and the Libyan heroes who fell in 
Lebanon and whose blood had mingled with the blood of their 
Syrian, Lebanese and Palestinian brothers. 


The triumph of the Lebanese people and the abrogation of May 
17 treaty came to prove, once again, what the Great Al Fatih 
Revolution had, following the evacuation of Palestinian forces 
from Beirut and the aftermath massacres of Sabra and Shatila 
said that the battle in Lebanon is a fiational one and that the 
Zionists and crusaders could be defeated through people’s war. 
It proved what Al Fatih had predicted that 1984 is the year of 
challenge and the defeat for the enemies of the Arab nation. 


The Jamahiriyah has warned against the dangers of the Zionist 
invasion and the presence of NATO forces in Lebanon and that 
such new crusade campaign is aimed at the very existence of the 
Arab nation. 


In this context, the historic leadership of the Great Al Fatih 
Revolution took the initiative and made many calls for the 
liberation of Lebanon and confirmation of its Arab identity.
