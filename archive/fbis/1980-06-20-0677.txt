EGYPTIAN REVOLUTIONARIES IN LIBYA CONDEMN AS-SADAT'S MOVES 
LD200914 Tripoli JANA in English 0813 GMT 20 Jun 80 


(Text ] Tripoli, June 20, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Egyptian revolutionary committees 
in Tripoli and Banghazi released a statement in which they strongly denounced the Egyptian 
president's declaration of the state of emergency on the border of the Jamahiriyah. The 
statement said that this decision was taken in implementation of the orders of Washington 
and Tel Aviv. The statement said, that the Egyptian revolutionary committees are the 
spearhead of struggle against the defeated traitor As-Sadat. The committees have been 
following what takes place in Egypt including the provocations, treason and humiliation 
represented in the foreign imperialist presence and the reestablishment of the colonialist 
bases in Egypt. 


Our people in Egypt stood in support of their historic leader Jamal 'Abd an-Nasir who 
exerted all efforts to eradicate colonialism and liberate the land. He gave his life for 
these objectives. 


The statement added that the Egyptian military buildup on the Jamahiriyah borders was 
ordered by the U.S.A. to be implemented by the Egyptian president who worships Carter and 
Begin. The statement went on to say that the Egyptian revolutionary committees (?condemn) 
any attack against the Libyan Arab people and their pioneer revolution as an attack against 
the Arab nation because the Libyan revolution is the real tool of achieving the 
comprehensive unity from the (?ocean) to the Gulf. 


The statement said: Our Arab army in Egypt, faithful soldiers of An-Nasir: You fought 
for the sake of your Arab nation. Do not accept humiliation, and do not obey the orders 
of the defeated president which are in fact the ordersof the U.S. White House and the 
Zionist Knesset. Don't accept the aggression against your Libyan Arab people. How would 
you fight your own people. It will be shameful to obey these orders and remain as slaves 
to U.S.A. and Israel. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES MESSAGE FROM PFLP'S HABASH 


LD200320 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 19 Jun 80 


[Excerpts] The brother leader has received a cable of support and solidarity from Dr 
George Habash, the secretary general of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine 
(PFLP) in which we affirmed the front's solidarity with the Great 1 September Revolution in 
the face of the military concentrations and the declaration of a state of emergency by the 
defeated Egyptian regime along the borders of Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
[SPLAJ]. The following is the text of the cable: 


V. 20 Jun 80 I 4 NORTH AFRICA 


Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafis:: A greeting of revolution and struggle. In the name 
of the Political Bureau of the PFLP and its Central Committee and in the name of all 
fighters and cadres of the PFLP, we wish to declare our full solidarity with the Great 
1 September Revolution in the face of the military concentrations and the declaration 
of state of emergency by the traitor As-Sadat regime on the borders of SPLAJ,. 


Your firm stand in the face of the treason of agent As-Sadat and his compliance with the 
designs of imperialism, Zionism and reaction in the region prompted American imperialism 
to use with all its strength and various means, including in particular, the regime of 
agent As-Sadat to confront the Great 1 September Revolution. 


The PFLP stands with you in the same trench against this dangerous escalation of 
aggression by the regime of traitor As-Sadat. We are fully confident that the masses of 
the Libyan heroic people are capable of thwarting the aggression and facing all plots, 
whatever the sacrifices. 


[Signed] George Habash, secretary general of PFLP, 19 June 1980

KIPRIANOU, DENKTAS. WELCOME LIBYAN MEDIATION EFFORT 
LD190126 Tripoli JANA in English 1749 GMT 18 Jun 80 


[Text] Tripoli, June 18, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--President Spyros Kiprianou of Cyprus 
and Rauf Denktas, Turkish community leader in Cyprus, both welcomed a move by the 
Jamahiriyah to solve the Cyprian problem. Mr Denktas’ said he is ready to attend a 
conference held in Tripoli for this purpose. Mr Denktas has paid a visit to the 
Jamahiriyah and met with Major 'Abd as-Salaam Jallud and the foreign secretary. 


UN Secretary General Informed 
LD191330 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1230 GMT 19 Jun 80 


[Text] The foreign minister of Cyprus today conveyed to the brother foreign secretary 
the gratitude of President Kiprianou to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
for its efforts toward solving the Cyprus problem. The foreign minister of Cyprus 
confirmed the preparedness of President Kiprianou to meet Mr.R.Denktas in Libya within 
the framework of a conference to solve the Cyprus problem, The foreign secretary has 
acquainted the UN secretary general of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah's 
efforts to solve the Cyprus problem.
