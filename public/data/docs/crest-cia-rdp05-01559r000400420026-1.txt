Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a November 1987 Tripoli TV broadcast in which Qadhdhafi says he will boycott the Amman Arab summit, which he calls a U.S.-ordered gathering about the Gulf war. It also contains an Egyptian arms-fair preview and a Moroccan note on an Iraqi envoy. Nothing here places a site in Tripoli.
• Qadhdhafi says Libya will not attend the Amman summit because it was set by the United States, not the Arabs.
• Qadhdhafi proposes an Arab summit agenda covering the fight against the US and France, the liberation of Palestine, an Arab federation, and an Arab atomic bomb.
• Qadhdhafi says NATO and the US attacked Libya and struck Libyan cities, and that the houses in ruins in front of him were damaged in those raids.
• The Cairo arms fair was to be held 9-13 November 1987.
• 1987-11-02: Qadhdhafi, speaking at a meeting with Arab journalists in Tripoli, announces he will not attend the Amman Arab summit and calls for a rival Arab summit.
