The statement expressed strong condemnation of the Camp David accords and of all 
attempts to liquidate the Palestine cause, to impose capitulation on the Arab nation

The statement praised the unionist step taken by Syria and Libya. 


The two sides also strongly condemned the new treasonous role played by the King of 
Jordan, who tried to succeed in this role under Arab cover by means of the Amman 
meeting. They also condemned his unsuccessful attempt to undermine the PLO's 
leading role and to replace the legitimate Palestinian representation, so as to 
permit him to play a new al-Sadat role in the Arab region by liquidating the central 
cause of the Arab struggle. 


The statement denounced U.S. imperialism's aggressive policy in the region and its 
attempts to spread aggressive military bases and to deploy more of its naval forces, 
which threaten the Arab region's safety and security. The statement called for the 
removal of these bases and for the elimination of the imperialist presence through- 
out the region. 


The statement condemned the Iraqi regime's war against the Iranian revolution and 
stressed that this war contradicts the real interests of the Arab and Iranian peoples, 
as well as their nationalist and liberation objectives. It asserted that this war 
serves imperialist and Zionist interests in the region and weakens the Arab nation's 
ability to confront the Zionist enemy and the imperialist, Zionist and reactionary 
conspiracies in the Arab homeland.

FOREIGN MINISTERS CONFERENCE TO CONVENE--Kuwait, 20 Dec (KUNA)--Diplomatic sources 
were Saturday quoted as saying that contacts undertaken by the Algerian foreign 
minister during his tour of Arab countries have (?resulted) in a preliminary agree- 
ment to restore unity of the Arab front. AL-QABAS newspaper further quoted the 
sources as saying that an agreement was reached on holdingaconference for the Arab 
foreign ministers. All Arab countries with the exception of Egypt will attend the 
meeting due to be held during the Islamic foreign ministers conference scheduled in 
Saudi Arabia on January 17, the sources confirmed. They also stressed that all Arab 
countries have agreed to attend the conference which is expected to be held on 

the eve of the Islamic ministers’ meeting. They further added that contacts are 
currently being held among Arab capitals to set an agenda for the meeting. The 
diplomatic sources predicted that visits will be soon exchanged between Arab offi- 
cials within the framework of coordination before the convention. [Text] [LD201205 
Kuwait KUNA in English 1008 GMT 20 Dec 80)

SYRIANS HELPED LIBYANS IN CHAD--Western observers believe that Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's 
success in capturing the Chad capital Ndjamena was due to military aid given by Syria. 
Observers affirm that Damascus secretly sent 40 senior Syrian officers to Tripoli 
shortly before the recent offensive in Chad. Most of these were staff officers, 
because Libya has few men sufficiently trained and capable of organizing lines of 
communication for the military units. The observers also disclose that some Syrian 
field officers also acted as advisers to the Libyan officers during the attack on 
Ndjamena. The Syrians who were sent to Libya were selected with great care; the 

name of every officer whom it was decided to send to Libya had to be submitted 

to Syrian President Hafiz al-Asad's office for approval. [AL-—JAZIRAH London Bureau 
Report) [LD301239 Riyadh AL-JAZIRAH in Arabic 25 Dec 80 p 1] 


SYRIA DENIES REPORTED CHIRAC PROTEST--Paris--The Syrian ambassador in Paris yester- 
day denied reports that he had received a protest message from Jacques Chirac, head 
of the Gaullist movement and mayor of Paris, on the so-called Syrian shelling of the 
Lebanese town of Zahlah. The Syrian ambassador told the SANA correspondent in Paris 
that reports published by the French newspaper LE MONDE on a protest by Chirac were 
baseless. The ambassador requested the director fo the Middle East department in 
the French Foreign Ministry to publish this Syrian denial. [Text] [JN250716 Damas-

All the NCC members praised the historic role played by His Majesty King Husayn in
