LIBYA

MATERIALS ON AL-QADHDHAFI MOSCOW VISIT, SIDE TRIPS 


Commentator on USSR Visit 
LD292342 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 29 Apr 81 


[Text] JANA's political commentator says that the meeting in Moscow between the leader 

of the revolution and the Soviet leaders, and particularly with President Leonid Brezhnev, 
general secretary of the CPSU Central Committee, reflected the reality of interaction at 

the level of civilization between the Arab nation and the Soviet peoples, despite the 
ideciogical differences stressed by Soviet leader Brezhnev in the speech delivered at the 
dinier given by the CPSU Central Committee in honor of the brother leader. The political 
commentator underlines the importance of the visit of the brother leader to the Soviet Union, 
its deep significance and its positive results in the interest of the member states of the 
Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation, on whose behalf the leader spoke at the Kremlin, 
and also in the interest of the peoples struggling for freedom and quality. 


In this respect, the political commentator stresses the success of the visit, which came 

at a grave phase when colonialist and reactionary forces are busy building bridgeheads for 
invading imperialist forces to cross into the Arab countries of Egypt, Sudan, Somalia, Oman 
and Morocco. It also came at a time when the imperialist-reactionary threat against the 
peoples of the Jamahiriyah and Chad is taking shape, while military activity is being openly 
stepped up against the territorial integrity of Lebanon, and while the Palestianian people 
are being annihilated at the hands of the aggressive Zionists supported by the United 
States.

Viewpoints between the two sides were identical, particularly in connection with the 
need to consolidate the Arab position politically and militarily, as well as on the need 
to limit strategic weapons, by withdrawal of warships carrying nuclear warheads from the 
Mediterranean, the prevention of the building of foreign military bases in the Arab Gulf 
region, and the confrontation of imperialist attempts aimed at small and developing 
countries and at creating zones of influence worldwide, 


The political commentator underlines the warm welcome accorded to the brother leader 

during this visit, hailing the special importance attached by the Soviet leaders to the 
leader's visit. His encounter with Leonid Brezhnev reflected the warmth of the friend- 
ship between the Libyan and Soviet peoples and the dealings on an equal footing between 

the two countries. The talks held betweenthe leader and Soviet President Brezhnev, with 
the participation of the members of the two delegations, took place in a spirit of complete 
friendship and understanding and led to positive results. 


The commentator particularly stresses that the talks concentrated on the politicl, inter- 
national, economic and technical areas. The minutes of an agreement on bilateral coopera- 
tion in economic and technical cooperation were signed. An assessment was also made of 
the international military situation as indicated by the activity of the delegation ac- 
companying the brother leader, and the latter's meetings with Soviet officials. The 
political commentator sheds light on the nature of the leader's visit to the Soviet Union 
and its positive significance regarding complete sympathy for just causes. He stresses 
that the visit was a victory for the will of the people of the Western Sahara and will 
strengthen their struggle. 


The political commentator points out that there was an agreement between the Libyan and 

Soviet sides to strengthen and consolidate the struggle waged by the Saharan people for 

freedom and to foil colonialist and reactionary plans aimed at impeding their legitimate 
ambitions and aspirations to impose their free will. 


The leader's visit to the Soviet Union also represents a victory for the cause of freedom 
in Chad, a cause which the Libyan people support with all their strength, proceeding from 
their principles positions. The Soviet Union attached a particular importance to this: 
The Soviet leaders expressed to the brother leader the Soviet Union's preparedness to 
stand by the side of the Libyan and Chadian people in the event of any indication of an 
attempt to destabilize security and stability in Chadian territory. 


The leader's visit to the Soviet Union and his encounters with the Soviet leaders 
strengthened the common objectives of the two sides in their determination to confront 
aggression against the Arab territories, to fight colonialism, Zionism and reaction, 

and to strive to build better human relations on a strong basis of justice, equality 

and mutual respect. The visit also strengthened the desire to ensure continuous friend- 
ship and to develop cooperation. 


At the same time, the talks and their positive results deepened the feelings in the Arab 
people that friendly relations with the Soviet Unionhave never been at the expense of 

any of their social and political values concerning their independence, their national 
drive for unity, their positive neutrality and their social and spiritual choices. The 
political commentator underlines that the leader's encounter with the Soviet leaders 
consolidatedand emphasized these facts, he adds that the visit contributed to the deepen- 
ing of the meaning of positive neutrality and nonalignment, and reflected the consistent 
position of the Jamahiriyah on this issue. 


The encounter also effectively contributed to emphasizing the principled positions of 

the Jamahiriyah on the struggles of peoples. Inparticular, the problem of Afghanistan 
was discussed, and the Jamahiriyah stressed its respect for the struggle of the Afghan 
people and the need for efforts to ensure the independence and neutrality of Afghanistan.

The leader's visit to the Soviet Union emphasized the importance of cooperation, especi- 
ally in the current international circumstances which are becoming more and more complicated 
due to the existence of colonialist aggressive policies, enemies of liberation movements and 
plunderers of people's natural resources. 


The political commentator hails the great meeting in Moscow yesterday between the leader 

of the revolution and the Muslim masses, stressing the deep ties revealed by this meeting 
and the respect and appreciation of Muslims for the Great 1 September Revolution and its 
positions of support for the cause of Islam and Muslims. The Muslim masses enthusiastically 
welcomed the leader of the revolution, showing the leader love and appreciating the zeal 

of the Great 1 September Revolution to improve conditions for Muslims and to realize their 
objectives and aspirations. 


The visit of the leader to Muslim cemeteries, his prayers to God to bless the Muslim dead 
and his participation in prayers on the occasion of the burial of the dead deeply impressed 
the Muslim masses in the Soviet Union.
