LIBYA

MOZAMBIQUE PRESIDENT ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 1 AUG 


LD031330 Tripoli JANA in English 1657 GMT 1 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 12, August 1, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Mozambique's president, 
Samora Machel, arrived here this afternoon to attend the meetings of the OAU. 


He was warmly met by the leader of the revolution who received him at Tripoli International 
Airport, together with the secretary of the General People's Congress, members of the 
General People's Committee, ambassadors accredited to the Jamahiriyah and a number of 
officers of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces. 


The Mozambique president spoke to JANA's correspondent at the airport of the deep 
relations between the Mozambique and Libyan Arab people at all levels and said he 

will hold talks in Jamahiriyah as part of bilateral efforts to consolidate friendship 
and cooperation, joint struggle between the 2 peoples against imperialism and for the 
freedom of African peoples. President Samora Machel highly valued the Great al-Fateh 
Revolution stands in support of African people's struggle against all forms of hegemony. 


He concluded by saying that he believes Africans will succeed in dealing with the 
different problems which face the African Continent and find solutions for these so as 
progress may be realized for African peoples.
