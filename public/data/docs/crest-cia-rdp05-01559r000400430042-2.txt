Summary (AI): An FBIS London transmission (7 September 1988) excerpts the final question and Qadhafi's answer from an Algiers TV interview, in which he discusses a Palestinian state and PLO structure and sends greetings to the Algerian people. It has no specific Tripoli places, events or people.
• Qadhafi says a Palestinian instrument of struggle should bring together all Palestinians, rather than a state or government in the abstract.
• He proposes that PLO offices be turned into Palestinian embassies and the resistance become a Palestinian Liberation Army.
• The passage notes that freedom of movement between Libya and Algeria was to begin 1 September.

((EXCERPTS)) ((QUESTION)) THIS IS THE LAST QUESTION. 
BROTHER COLONEL, SINCE ITS INCEPTION THE LIBYAN ARAB 
REVOLUTION HAS BEEN EAGER TO PROVIDE MATERIAL AND MORAL 
SUPPORT FOR NATIONAL LIBERATION MOVEMENTS, PRIMARILY THE 
PALESTINIAN REVOLUTION. HOW DO YOU PERCEIVE THE SITUATION 
IN THE LIGHT OF THE NEW DEVELOPMENTS AND THE PLAN OF ACTION 
FOR THE UPRISING TO CONTINUE AND FOR THE PALESTINIAN PEOPLE 
TO REALIZE THEIR STATE UNDER THE PLO'S LEADERSHIP? 
((QUESTION AS HEARD)) THANK YOU. 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) I BELIEVE THAT THERE SHOULD BE AN 
INSTRUMENT ((ADAT)) FOR THE PALESTINIAN REVOLUTION THAT 
WOULD BRING TOGETHER ALL PALESTINIANS. I DO NOT REJECT THE 
ESTABLISHMENT OF A PALESTINIAN STATE OR GOVERNMENT, BUT IT 
MUST BE AN INSTRUMENT OF STRUGGLE, NOT OF ((WORD 
INDISTINCT)) AND SUBMISSION. IT MUST COME FROM THE HEART OF 
THE PALESTINIAN REVOLUTION, WHICH IS THE WEST BANK AND GAZA 
STRIP. THE REVOLUTIONARIES THERE MUST EXPRESS THEMSELVES TO 
THE WORLD BY ESTABLISHING THIS INSTRUMENT, AND THE 
PALESTINIAN RESISTANCE INSIDE AND OUTSIDE SHOULD TURN INTO A 
PALESTINIAN LIBERATION ARMY. ALL PLO OFFICES MUST BE TURNED 
INTO PALESTINIAN EMBASSIES. IT MUST BECOME AN INSTRUMENT OF 
FIGHTING AND STRUGGLING TO LIBERATE PALESTINE FROM THE RIVER 
((JORDANN -- FBIS)) TO THE ((MEDITERRANEAN -- FBIS)) SEA, 
AND MUST BE NOTHING OTHER THAN THIS. 

((QUESTION)) ALLOW ME TO EXPRESS OUR DEEP GRATITUDE. 
WOULD YOU ADDRESS THE ALGERIAN PEOPLE ON THIS OCCASION? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) I AM GRATEFUL TO YOU. THE ALGERIAN 
PEOPLE ARE MY PEOPLE AND BROTHERS. I AM NO STRANGER TO 
THEM TO SEND THEM GREETINGS FROM AFAR. WE LIVE TOGETHER 
WITH OUR HEARTS AND SWORDS SIDE BY SIDE. ((PASSAGE 
OMITTED ON ALGERIAN REVOLUTION; GREETINGS TO THE ALGERIAN 
PEOPLE; FREEDOM OF MOVEMENT BETWEEN THE TWO COUNTRIES TO 
BEGIN 1 SEPTEMBER) )
