LIBYA

RENE TOLD TROOPS READY 'TO DEFEND' SEYCHELLES 


LD021456 Tripoli JANA in English 1423 GMT 2 Dec 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 2 Dec (JANA) -- The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has 
informed President Albert Rene of the Seychelles that the Jamahiriyah is quite ready to 
send troops to defend Seychelles against any U.S. threats or provocations. 


The message said that the Libyan forces are at the disposal of the people of Seychelles 
in countering any U.S. imperialist danger that might harm their freedom, violate their 
security and shake their stability. These forces will return to the Jamahiriyah as soon 
as their mission is fulfilled, the message added. 


JANA: CIA DOCUMENT TO NATO ATTACKS LIBYA 


LD022002 Tripoli JANA in English 1826 GMT 2 Dec 81 


[Text] Washington, 2 Dec (JANA) -- The terrorist American agency, the CIA, is continuing 
its attacks on the Jamahiriyah. Last week it presented NATO with a secret report urging 
European countries to stand against Libya and in support of its criminal imperialist 
scheme which aims at impairing the Libyan Arab-European relations in order to serve the 
greedy American interests. 


The Italian newspaper (?LA STAMPA) reported in the 30th November edition that what 
worried NATO countries was the CIA's denunciation of Libya's decision to form a rapid 
“intervention” force, a force unique of its kind in Africa. The CIA report had 
deliberately distorted the true intentions behind the formation of this force, the sole 
objective of which is to help freedom fighting countries against American imperialism. 
This report is part of the campaign of hatred and conspiracy launched by American 
administration against the Jamahiriyah, a campaign which relies on lies, falsifications 
and ideological deception, and exposes American terrorist intentions aimed at forcing 
anti-American systems to change their position. This anti-Jamahiriyah policy springs 
from imperialist arrogance and muscle flexing to terrorise peaceful nations. It also 
follows a series of American enemy positions against the Libyan Arab people in this 
attempt across international fields to reject the American imperialist presence. 


AMERICAN-ARAB SOCIETY CABLES AL-—QADHDHAFI 


LD022042 Tripoli JANA in English 1845 GMT 2 Dec 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 2 Dec (JANA) -- Mohammad al-Mahdi, chairman of the American-Arab 
Society, has sent a calbe to the revolution's leader acquainting him of the American 
administration's vile conspiracies against the Jamahiriyah.

He informed the leader that the American administration's conspiracies include criminal 
plans aimed at the revolution's leader. He said that criminal American elements were 
employed to execute these plans, which reveal to public opinion the extent of the 

Reagan administration's criminal conduct in its relations with other countries following 
policies disliked by Washington. 


Al-Mahdi denounced the CIA murderers’ treacherous conduct in assassination schemes, 
organizing coups in various countries. 


SHARON OFFERS AFRICANS HELP AGAINST LIBYA 


NC040817 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 4 Dec 81 


[Text] A member of the Zionist Knesset has revealed that Zionist War Minister terrorist 
Ari'el Sharon has offered two African states the help of the Zionist air force in order 
to commit an aggression against the Jamahiriyah. Zionist Knesset Member (Pierre Filez) 
explained before the Knesset the day before yesterday that this offer was made during 
the trip which Sharon made to a number of African states 2 weeks ago.
