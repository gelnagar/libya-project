LIBYA

Mubarak’s ‘Fabrications’ on High Dam Plot Rapped 
LD041127 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0915 GMT 4 Oct 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 4 Oct (JANA) — The head of the Egyptian regime 
has alleged that he has information regarding what he termed a 
Libyan attempt to bomb the High Dam. These allegations came 
in an interview with the head of the Egyptian regime and 
published by the Egyptian government-owned AL-AKHBAR in 
its issue of 2 days ago. The JANA political editor comments on 
these allegations and fabrications: 


The statements made by the head of the Egyptian regime are no 
more than allegations and fabrications, and fall within the search 
for pretexts to perpetrate aggression against the Jamahiriyah. 
This makes us always vigilant in confronting and foiling the 
conspiracies hatched against us in Washington, which does not 
approve of the Jamahiriyah’s policy, independent stance, rejec- 
tion of American domination, and defense of the pan-Arab issues 
of the Arab nation. 


Someone ought to point out the true aim of the head of the 
Egyptian regime in making such allegations and his objective for 
reiterating such absurdities. He makes such statements to divert 
attention from the deteriorating internal situation in Egypt and 
from the gruelling crises experienced by the Egyptian people — 
a matter which came to a head in the form of anger and wrath 
expressed by the demonstrators at Kafr al-Dawwar, Alexandria, 
the village of Bani Ghalib, and elsewhere — and ultimately he 
wants to prove to the politicians in Washington that he is 
qualified to carry out the tasks entrusted to him against the 
Jamahiriyah. 


Certainly the false accusations made by Husni Mubarak against 
the Jamahiriyah were met with a wave of ridicule and mockery. 
This is because what has been uttered by the head of the Egyptian 
regime lacks credibility. The Egyptian people themselves are 
certain of the intentions of the regime and of what this regime is 
hatching against them. This is because the people realize that it 
is impossible to accept the regime’s allegations and accusations 
against the Jamahiriyah. 


The High Dam, built by Egyptian construction workers, is a 
source of pride for the whole Arab nation, and is a great national 
project of which we are very proud. To say that Libya is planning 
to bomb this national project is proof the Egyptian regime, known 
for its bias to the Zionist enemy and for ties with the suspect 
American plans, is suffering a lack of political maturity. We even 
go so far as to say that what has been uttered by the head of the 
Egyptian regime is an ethical decline and a declared bankruptcy. 
Therefore the Egyptian regime had nothing with which it could 
conceal its intentions and readiness to participate in the imple- 
mentation of the American anti-Jamahiriyah plan. 


The statement made last night by U.S. State Department 
spokesman Alan Romberg in Washington and his support for the 
allegations of the Egyptian regime is strong proof that the policy 
of the Egyptian regime is being managed in Washington and not 
in Cairo, and that the accusations made by the head of the 


Egyptian regime are American accusations against the Jamahir- 
iyah issued in Egyptian language, and whose motives and aims 
are very well known to us, of course. 


JANA Views Egyptian Withdrawal From Arab Union 
LD031644 Tripoli JANA in English 1500 GMT 3 Oct 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Muharram 8, October 3, JAMAHIRYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The so-called Egyptian People’s Assembly 
approved last Monday night the decision of the regime of Husni 
Mubarak to withdraw from the Union of Arab Republics. [The] 
JANA political editor commented on the approval of the so- 
called Egyptian People’s Assembly of the withdrawal from the 
Union of Arab Republics. He wrote: “This approval is an open 
falsification of the will of forty million Egyptian citizens and 
cannot be regarded except that the Egyptian regime provides an 
evidence to the masses of the Arab nation that it is a renegade 
regime working towards stabbing the aspirations and ambitions 
of the Arab nation for unity. It is [an] unjust step favouring the 
enemies of this nation. Of course, a shameful act like this does 
not need an explanation or analysis because it represents the 
desires of the regime and exposes his separatist face. At the same 
time, it encourages us more to boycott this regime which has 
chosen this step to become a regional regime which its interests 
meet with the claimants of separatism and regionalism and 
American imperialism and the Zionist enemy. 


Husni Mubarak, who hides behind false slogans, has exposed 
himself completely. He did not accept at any time since he took 
office to talk about cancellation of [the] Camp David agreement 
but had been throwing obstacles openly in the way of any honest 
call for leaving the shed of Camp David and disengaging from 
the Zionist enemy which is still occupying the Egyptian territory 
of Tabah and imposed on the Egyptian Army conditions under- 
mining the sovereignty of the Egyptian people. Husni Mubarak 
{word indistinct] to withdraw from the Union of Arab Republics 
which came by the will of the Egyptian, Libyan Arab and Syrian 
Aran peoples. 


He refused to withdraw from the agreements of humiliation and 
shame which chained the Egyptian people and affected humili- 
ation to the Arab nation and heroic struggle. Of course, Husni 
Mubarak has chosen the [word indistinct] action not the act 
which services the Arab nation”.
