Istanbul Synagogue Said Used by Israeli Intelligence 
JN070534 Paris Radio Monte Carlo in Arabic 
0500 GMT 7 Sep 86 


[Excerpts] The final casualty toll in the Istanbul synagogue blast 
yesterday reached 23 dead and 4 injured, one seriously. When 
the explosion occurred, 31 people were inside the synagogue. 
[passage omitted on reaction to attack]

Jewish synagogue had been used as a center for Israeli intel- 
ligence.

[Text] Tripoli, Muharam 6, Shahr al Fateh 10, Jamahiriyah 
News Agency The 8th conference of non-aligned states in 
Harare adopted a number of resolutions concerning the aiding of 
the great Jamahiriyah and supporting her and condemning 
America. 


rhe heads of states and governments expressed their deep con- 
cern at recent events in the Mediterranean resulting from the 
provocative repeated attacks against the Great Socialist People’s 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. Such attacks pose a danger to peace 
and security of the region and the world as a whole. 


The heads of states and governments referred in this context to 
the contents of the statements issued by the Non-Aligned Move- 
ment’s [word indistinct] co-ordination office concerning this mat- 
ter during its meetings in New York on Al Nawar|[February]and 
Marikh [March] 26, 1986 and also the contents of its 
extraordinary meeting in New Delhi on Al Tayr [April] 15, 1986 
following the barbaric American aggression against the help and 
co-operation of Britain. 


The heads of states and governments stressed their strong con- 
demnation of this unjustifiable barbaric American-NATO 
aggression which is tantamount to an act of state-sponsored 
terrorism, a violation of international law and the U.N. charter. 


The heads of states and governments demanded America’s 
immediate halting of these hostile acts including the military 
manoeuvres regarded as a violation of the sovereignty of the 
Great Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and its terri- 
torial integrity, and which also subject peace and security in the 
Mediterranean region to danger and pose an obstacle in the way 
of efforts being made to turn the Mediterranean into a region of 
peace and security. 


The heads of states and governments demanded that America 
pay full and immediate reparation to the Great Socialist People’s 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah for the human and material losses it 
suffered. 


The heads of states and governments declared their full support 
to the Great Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and 
their solidarity with her in defending herself, sovereignty and 
territorial integrity. 


The heads of states and governments declared that the air attack 
on the home of the leader of the revolution was aimed at his and 
his family’s reputation, is a dangerous precedent in international 
relations and a vicious crime by all political and moral standards. 
This while the heads of states and governments take into consid- 
eration the responsibility of the U.N. Security Council in preserv- 
ing international peace and security 


They expressed regret at the prevention of this council from 
issuing a resolution condemning this flagrant aggression commit- 
ted by America against the Great Jamahiriyah 


The heads of states and governments of the non-aligned states 
decided to support including the issue of the American aggression 
against the Great Jamahiriyah in the agenda of the 41st session 
of the U.N. General Assembly because of the dangerous nature

of the issue and its negative and dangerous consequences on

The heads of states and governments meeting in Harare dis- 
cussed decisions taken by the American Administration against 
the Great Jamahiriyah which come in the shape of imposing an 
economic embargo on her and freezing her assets in America. 
They condemned these measures as a form of economic arms- 
twisting to achieve political aims. 


The heads of states and governments demanded America to 
immediately cancel these measures and declared the solidarity 
of the Non-Aligned Movement with Great Jamahiriyah against 
these measures which are aimed at squeezing her economic and 
social development plans, and endangering her sovereignty and 
the independence of her people. 


The heads of states and governments of the Non-Aligned Move- 
ment called upon all states to take appropriate practical steps in 
supporting Great Jamahiriyah in overcoming these tyrannical 
decisions. 


JANA’s political editor hailed these important resolutions and 
said: “These resolutions clearly show that world public opinion

the Great Jamahiriyah which enjoys this tremendous interna- 
tional support and solidarity by one hundred and one states of the 
world with two billion people of the world’s population, aside 
from the socialist states and China, or America which has shown 
its moral, political and military decline before the non-aligned 
states which makes up two thirds of the giobe. JANA political 
editor adds: The terrorism which America practices against the 
people of the Great Jamahiriyah and which was aimed at the 
leader and his family last Al Tayr [April] has completely isolated 
America and put her in the docks, indeed America has become 
isolated even amongst its own Western allies and also amongst 
her agents.
