JANA REPORTS REACTION TO YOUNG'S RESIGNATION 
1D162001 Tripoli JANA in English 1759 GMT 16 Aug 79 ID 
[Text] Paris, Aug 16, JAMAHIRIVAH NEWS AGENCY--The resignation of Andrew Young, the 
U.S. permanent representative at the UN, provoked strong reactions in the international 
organization and political circles in Washington, particularly among Third World countries 
where Young enjoys a special popularity, 
Many thought that President Carter used Young as a scapegoat for American-Zionist relations, 
Andrew Young was a faithful companion of the late American black leader Martin Inther King. 
Political observers in Washington see Andrew Young's resignation, whether or not it was 
forced, as a hard blow to President Carter, 14 months before the 1980 presidential 


elections, 


GOVERNMENT DECIARES 17 AUG AS JERUSALEM DAY 


1D170844 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 17 Aug 79 Db 
[Text] Tripoli--The Socialist People!ts Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has decided to declare 
today as Jerusalem Day in response to the wish expressed by the Islamic revolution in 
fran, This day will be an initial stand for the liberation of the Islamic soil and holy 
Jerusalem, 
JANA has learned that speakers at the mosques today will deal with the question of the 
liberation of holy Jerusalem and emphasize that its liberation and the liberation of 
Palestine is the duty of Muslims and that this is what they should fight for,
