Summary (AI): A 1956 CIA background note on US aid to Libya under the base agreement: $40 million in economic aid over 17 years (about $5 million turned over as of January 1956), technical assistance of about $7 million, relief gifts over $6 million, and US military expenditure in Libya of about $10 million a year. The OCR is poor, so some figures are uncertain.
• Under the Libyan base agreement, the US agreed to provide Libya with $40 million in economic aid over a 17-year period.
• As of January 1956, about $5 million (OCR uncertain) of the economic aid had been turned over.
• Technical assistance program allotments through June 1956 total about $7 million.
• Several gifts of relief supplies (1952 through 1955) are valued at over $6 million.
• US military expenditure in Libya totals about $10 million annually.
• Among future US plans is Defense Department consideration of a Libyan request (text illegible).
