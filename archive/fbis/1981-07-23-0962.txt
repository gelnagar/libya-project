LIBYA

SUDAN TO BE REJECTED AT ARAB DEFENSE MEETING 


LD230854 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 23 Jul 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 23 Jul (JANA)--JANA has learned that the leaders of the pan-Arab Front 

for Steadfastness and Confrontation have instructed their delegations to the emergency 
meeting of the joint Arab defense council to be held in Tunis tomorrow [as heard] to 

reject the presence of the Sudanese delegation and not to talk about the military plan 
while it is present. The information would be conveyed to the-Israelis by the Sudanese 
delegation through the Egyptian regime's machinery, as has been done in the past, and 

since the Sudanese regime has revealed its true leanings by formally declaring its recogni- 
tion of Israel through its alliance and link with the Egyptian regime.

AL-BASHSHARI INTERVIEWED ON ANTI-LIBYAN CAMPAIGNS 


GF222010 Kuwait AL-WATAN in Arabic 22 Jul 81 p 11 


[Exclusive interview granted to correspondent ‘Atif ‘Awdah by Ibrahim Muhammad al-Bashshari, 
Secretary of the Administrative Committee for Revolutionary information and Libyan" 
"information minister," inTripoli--no date given] 


[Excerpts] Question: The Western media are launching a continuous campaign against the 
Jamahiriyah aimed at distorting the Libyan role. Is there any plan to counter this campaign? 


Answer: Before his visit to Jerusalem, Western media used to describe As-Sadat as the weak 
man. After his ominous visit, he became the hero of the Western press. This is because 

of the submission of the western media to monopolies, privilege, multinational companies 
and intelligence services that serve U.S. imperialism. The campaign we are being subjected 
to is the result of our brave stand in supporting liberation movement and the people's 
struggle for their independence and freedom. These campaigns aim at distorting the anti- 
terrorist Libyan role and at limiting our anti-imperialist activity. They are also aimed 
at stunting our revolutionary role and intimidating us. The United States confuses 
terrorist movements with liberation movements. If not, why did stupid Haig, Reagan's 
secretary of state, describe the Palestinian people's struggle asterrorism? If so, what 

is Israel doing, and what can we call the actions it is carry out? 


Therefore, the campaign is linked to our stand. If our stand changes, the Western media's 
way of dealing with us will change. Our move to counter this campaign depends on all 
honorable people in the world. We have a number of cultured friends in the West who explain 
the Jamahiriyah's viewpoint and reveal the truth. 


Question: The Libyan media are talking about threats the Jamahiriyah is facing. What is 
the source of these threats and how can they be countered? 


Answer: The U.S. is aiding an Egyptian-Sudanese aggression attempt against the Libyan 
people. In this regard, the Egyptian president is massing his troops along the Libyan 
border and the Sudanese president is massing his troops along Chad's western border under 
the cover of a broad U.S. campaign to fight terrorism. The goal is aggression against 
Libya to return it to the U.S. sphere of domination following Libya's strike against U.S. 
bases after the September revolution. 


If any aggression is committed against us by Egypt, Sudan or the United States--taking 
into consideration that we stopped the military confrontation with Egypt and have 
transformed the western region into educational and agricultural areas instead of 
camps--Liby2 will become another Vietnam. 


V. 23 Jul 81 Q 5 NORTH AFRICA 


If the United States decides to commit any aggression against us, it will be the loser 
and will pay heavily for its terrorism. In this regard, we are not surprised at Numayri's 
Statement to the HERALD TRIBUNE, in which he said he insists on killing the colonel 
[Al-Qadhdhafi] and that he is ready to place all his resources at the disposal of a 
professional to commit this crime. 


Question: What is Libya's stand toward the Gulf Cooperation Council? 


Answer: Defending the Gulf is the responsibility of the Gulf countries. The actual 
threat being posed to the region is the U.S. threat, aided by 

the Rapid Deployment Force. We support the idea of asserting the freedom of the 
Gulf people and preserving Gulf security in any way deemed appropriate, without any 
foreign intervention of whatever origin. We also share the opinion that there is a 
premeditated U.S. attempt to intimidate the area with an imaginary danger. This is 

being done only to give the United States a pretext for intervening in the region in one 
way or another.
