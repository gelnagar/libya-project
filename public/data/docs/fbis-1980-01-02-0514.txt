PALESTINIANS ISSUE STATEMENT ON PLO-LIBYAN RELATIONS 
LD301505 Tripoli JANA in English 0813 GMT 30 Dec 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, 30 Dec, JANA--Leaders of the Palestinian resistance movement held a 
press conference yesterday at Al-Shati Hotel in Tripoli, At the beginning of the confer- 
ence, the following statement was read: 


"Within the framework of the natural and strategic alliance between the Great 1 September 
Revolution and the Palestinian revolution, sensing the patriotic responsibility and real- 
izing the nature of the Arab nation's struggle with its enemies who include imperialists 
Zionists, reactionary and capitulationist forces, the following general secretaries of 
the Palestinian organizations: 


Ahmed Jabril, secretary general of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine- 
General Command; Nayif Hawatimah, secretary general of the Democratic Front for the 
Liberation of Palestine; George Habash, secretary general of the Popular Front for the 
Liberation of Palestine; Samir Ghawshah, secretary general of the Front for Popular 
Struggle; and Majid Muhsin, secretary of As-Sa'iqah organization in Lebanon, paid a visit 
to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah between 25 and 29 December 1979. 


The delegation held several meetings with the Great 1 September Revolution's leadership, 
and conferred with Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi., 


Discussions during these meetings centered on the Palestinian cause, the Arab situation 
in all its dimensions, the position taken by Arab and Palestinian revolutionary forces 
and methods of developing their capabilities so as to enhance their effectiveness in 
confronting the conspiracy aimed at disuniting our nation, the role of revolutionary and 
progressive forces in agitating the Arab masses toward waging the armed struggle for libe- 
ration and return to the usurped homeland, 


Points of view converged regarding the use of armed struggle and a long-stretched popular 
liberation war as the only correct means of liberating Palestine. Both sides believe 
that these methods and increased fedayeen operations from all fronts should be the Arab 
revolution's strategy in general, and the Palestinian revolution's in particular, 


In spite of the clarity of various repercussions and stages of the conspiracy waged 
against the Arab nation, revolutionary and progressive forces in the Arab world are called 
upon to concert all efforts for foiling the Stable David conspiracy, the treasonous As - 
Sadat-Israel treaty, and the self-rule conspiracy, because these represent some of the 
stages of the grand conspiracy which the enemies of our nation are trying to impose on 

us, Departing from the communique issued by the factions of the Palestinian revolution 
and the leadership of the Great 1 September Revolution released on 15 June 1979 and in 
affirmation of the right and determination of the Palestinian Arab people to armed 
struggle for the liberation cf Palestine, and in establishing the call for the libera- 
tion and initiative included in the speech of Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 
Great 1 September Revolution on the occasion of the festival of solidarity with Palestine 
on 29 November 1979, and in a bid to escalate the Palestinian armed struggle, the follow- 
ing has been agreed:

Palestine; 


2. It is necessary to launch fedayeen operations from all Arab fronts and to enable

3. <Any attempt to divert the Palestinian armed revolution to a political movement should

7- Absolute faith in the masses and striving to liberate them and mobilise their capabil- 
ities for liberation and the revolution. Also, the uplifting of the Arab and Palestinian 
masses from the state of injustice in which they might exist so that they are able to carry

Arab masses. No struggle can be carried out in their name because these masses are the 
real driving force for liberation. Any serious effort to draw a strategy for patriotic

8. The alliance between the Great 1 September Revolution and the Palestinian revolution

9. The fabrication of any dispute with the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah

10. The political committee of the PLO in the Jamahiriyah will be formed from the factions 
of the Palestinian revolution. 


11. The unity of the Palestinian peovle in the occupied lands and beyond should be

Palestinian people. 


12. The role of the front ci steadfastness and confrontation is important, and it will

Zionist-reactionary conspiracy.

The leaders of the Palestinian resistance affirmed in their press conference that the 
Jamahiriyah has been supporting the Palestinian revolution with funds and arms and has 
been a basic supporter against hostile designs and attempts at liquidation, 


They also affirmed that it is the duty of the Palestinian people and their revolution 

and the nationalist forces in Lebanon to acknowledge their gratitude notwithstanding the 
fact that it is a national duty of the Libyan Arab people and their leadership to back 
them, With regard to the allegations that the PLO's office in Tripoli was closed and 
surrounded, the leaders of the Palestinian resistance movement said that these allegations 
are groundless. They affirmed that the organization's office is carrying out its full 
functions towards Palestinian people and has not and will not be touched as long as the 
Great 1 September Revolution's stands towards the Palestinian people are clear, They said 
that it would have been better, instead of circulating these allegations, if other Arab 
countries were denounced strongly for not allowing the opening of PLO offices on their 
territories until now. A case in point is Saudi Arabia, which for the last fifteen years 
has refused the PLO permission to open an office on its territories, The Palestinian 
resistance leaders wonder why this position is not condemned and why matters are weighed 
in two different scales, one for Arab revolutionary forces and other for Arabs who support 
America. 


Regarding the speech delivered by Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to mrk the international 
day of solidarity with the Palestinian people, the Palestinian resistance leaders said 
Arab and Libyan mass media organs are to blame for not conveying the full facts to the 
Arab people and world, 


What was in fact conveyed, they said, were unintegrated excerpts of the speech. They 
added that, notwithstanding Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's standing in regard to the Palestinian 
revolution, they sensed a clearly revolutionary outlook in this speech tinged with pain 
regarding the present outlook of the Arab nation and Palestinian revolution, 


They said the speech incorporated many important topics, What really drew our attention, 
they added, was Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's warning to the Palestinians to beware of the con- 
certed Arab and international campaign which seeks to tame the revolution,

The Palestinian resistance leaders added: "Why the campaign against this speech at a time

Tiey said the struggle against enemies necessitates the solution of secondary end, mostly, 
imaginary disputes within the Arab revolutionary movement. We should, they added, divert

literal dragging of As-Sadat into the arms of American imperialism, the sponsoring by 
Morocco's King Hassan of various preparations for the final treasonous contacts with the 
enemy and their tragic repercussions, and the arrests carried out by King Husayn of 
hundreds of Palestinians, including members of the Palestine National Council, 


The Palestinian resistance leaders called on all revolutionaries to raise their voices in 
unison against these events and work hard for the solution of any outstanding problems 
that might exist amongst revolutionary Arab forces. 


They said the Great September Revolution is now carrying out a unique experiment in 
revolutionising the masses. They added: “We await the results of this experiment as 
we do of all human experience. How then could we not but learn from an experiment 
which emanates from the core of the Arab nation's reality?" 


They said Colonel Muammar al- Qadhdhafi'ts speech, which was delivered to mark the 
(?international) for solidarity with Palestine, is a true expression of sorrow and pain 
regarding the nation's reality. They therefore called for a closer and more sincere examination 
of the speech, 


The Palestinian leaders affirmed their belief in armed struggle as the sole and correct 
path for gaining recognition of Arab rights and liberating the usurned lands. All other 
methods of action are mere attempts to transform it from a revolutionary movement 
depending on armed struggle, to a mere political movement. 


They said such a transformation will only be achieveu at the expense of the Arab nation's 
vital principles and interests. 


The Palestinian leaders denied that their visit to the Jamahiriyah is viewed as a 
gesture of reconciliation, They said the visit falls within the framework of 
continuous consultations between the Great 1 September and Palestinian revolutions, 
as confirmed the communique by the emergency meeting held last July between the 
leaderships of both revolutions,
