QADHDHAFIL BACKS DOWN IN CONFRONTATION WITH TRIBAL LEADERS 
Cairo AL-AHRAM in Arabic 11 Apr 80 p 5 


[Article: "Struggle Between Qadhdhafi and Libyan Tribal Leaders; Qadhdhafi 
Compelled To Free 181 Tribal Leaders; Tribesamen Pressure Army"| 


[Text] A report received from Libya says: “President Mu'ammar Qadhdhafi 
has issued an order to abolish the tribal system which has prevailed among 
the Bedouin tribes throughout Libya's history. These tribes are the 
al-Bara'isah, the al-Hasah, the al-'Awafir, the al-'Ubaydat, the al-Harabi, 
the al-Araqah, the al-Shalawiyah, the al-Manafah, the al-Shawa'ir, the 
al-Darsah, and the al-Shahbiyat. Officers and enlisted men of the Libyan 
armed forces as well as all the civil servants and workers in the Libyan 
Government are from these tribes. 


"This decision came as a result of Qadhdhafi's determination to apply the 
principles of the Green Book, which aim at breaking down every unit and 
grouping among the Libyan people. 


"The tribes have rebelled against Qadhdhafi's decision, which abolishes 
their traditions and the unity which has existed among the tribes for 
yundreds of years. 


"A big conference was held in al-Jabal al-Akhdar which brought together all 
the tribal leaders and shaykhs and the secretaries of the people's committees 
from the provinces of Sirt, al-Khums, Benghazi and Darnah. Those attending 
the conference discussed the damage which has resulted from this decision 

and the danger it poses to the unity of the tribes and the harmony and 
homogeneity among them. The conference issued resolutions summoning all 
members of these tribes, all officers and enlisted men in the Libyan armed 
forces, all Libyan students studying in Europe and America who are working 

on people's committees in Libya's foreign embassies, and all oilfield 

workers to return to the tribes without delay. 


"Libyan intelligence reported the conference resolutions to Qadhdhafi, who 
issued a decision calling for the arrest of 181 leaders and shaykhs from 
the ten [sic, eleven listed in the first paragraph] Libyan tribes and the 


41 





people's committees who attended the conference. He charged them with 
violating the charter of the Libyan revolution. He also decided to execute 
any officer or enlisted man from these tribes who seeks to leave his post 
in the Libyan armed forces, 


"The arrest of the tribal leaders brought about a major aplit in the 
Libyan Army and caused grumbling among army officers and enlisted men 
who belong to these tribes. In addition, the Bedouins who work in the 
oilfields and in government agencies declared open rebellion. 


"Qadhdhafi held a meeting with all the officers of the armed forces, Present 
at this meeting were Commander-in-Chief Lt Col Abu-Bakr Yunis, Prime Minister 
"Abd-al-Salam Jallud and Commander of Libyan Military Intelligence Mustafa 
al-Kharubi. Qadhdhafi justified the abolition of the tribal system by 

saying that it is a primitive and backward system which conflicts with the 
concepts of the revolution and hinders the anticipated leap forward in 
Libya's awakening in its modern age. 


"After intense discussion, the conference decided to immediately release 
the tribal leaders and secretaries of the people's committees and allow 

the officers and enlisted men who had responded to the call of their tribes 
to return to their posts in the army without punishment. The conference 
also decided to rescind Qadhdhafi's decision calling for the abolition of 
the tribal system in Libya and not to return to it in the future. 


"Now, all the Libyan tribes are delighted at having forced Qadhdhafi to 
rescind all his decisions against the tribes. It should be mentioned that 
the ten Libyan tribes represent all Libya's pepulation. The armed forces 
and all workers in the state consist of members of these tribes."

[Text] AL-AKHBAR has received the following important 
statement from the Libyan Citizens Grouping. The 
statement was issued on the anniversary of the April 
massacres carried out by Qadhdhafi in Libya last year. 
The document says that April has become the month of 
massacres, murders and savage crimes of torture. The 
massacres began with the murder of Libyan journalist 
Muhammad Mustafa Ramadan in London. The text of the 
statement follows: 


In the name of the most important of the rights gained by the human race 
through its Long march, the right to live in security within a state whose 
most important duty is to achieve this goal, we direct this message to the 
peoples of the world. We say unanimously to them that we have lost this 
right to security at the hands of the president of our state, whose main 
preoccupation has become the invention of new ways in which to terrori.e 
the citizens and punish them indiscriminately. 


There can be no doubt that the most repulsive condition which a people can 
endure is to discover that the gangs knocking on the doors of the citizens 
during the night are not bands of criminals, in which case they could 
register complaints with the security authorities, but gangs which are 
under the direct authority of the president and receive their orders 
directly from him. He calls these bands the "revolutionary committees," 
or sometimes the "red gangs." Perhaps a statement describing the methods 
which the Libyan president, Mu'ammar Qadhdhafi, has employed from the 

time he staged the coup in 1969 until February 1980, with preparations 
now being made for a collective purge of many members of the Libyan people, 
who will go to the gallows during April, the month set aside as the annual

Qadhdhafi has employed to govern the Libyan people will force everyone 

to assess the situation which causes us to reach out for help and assistance, 
Because what we are being subjected to is the threatened annihilation of 

an entire people, These measures which Colonel Qadhdhafi has employed to 
deal with our people provide the best illustration of what we are enduring. 
The situation in brief is as follows: 


The First of the Crimes 


1, On 1 September, the royal regime in Libya was overthrown and the 
People's Tribunal was formed. The tribunal then began trying the key 
members of the royal regime, A few months later, Qadhdhafi claimed that 
he had uncovered a plot to overthrow his regime. He formed a special 
military tribunal which began trying certain of the prominent members of 
the Revolution Command Council who had played a principal role in the rise 
of the revolution movement. The Libyan people began hearing rumors about 
the methods of torture and interrogation foreign to their peace-loving 
nature. Then the first purge took place against a group of RCC members, 
including Defense Minister Adam al-Hawwaz, Interior Minister Musa Ahmad 
and other officers who had been members of the revolutionary movement. 

The trials were a sham without any semblence of justice. Sentences were 
handed down ranging from 10 years in prison to life imprisonment and death. 


Purging of the Army 


2. Mu'ammar Qadhdhafi began to feel uneasy about his grip on the RCC 
following the first purge. It became a matter of course for attempts 

to overthrow the regime of Mu'ammar Qadhdhafi to be discovered at any time. 
Most of these attempts were fabricated by this ruler in order to reach 
certain elements in the army and purge them early, so that even when all 
the evidence proved that some of the accused officers were innocent, they 
were nevertheless stripped of military rank and discharged from the army. 
Some of these men spent 4 years in prison without trials before being 
released. ihe Libyan people have heard about and seen many of their countrymen-- 
including officers, enlisted men and civilians--who have been maimed in 
torture operations. Some lost their teeth, some suffered paralysis in 

one of their legs, some lost their hearing, and some, such as Colonel 
al-Majiri and Captain al-Barghuthi, even lost their lives. In most cases 
a group of civilians were charged along with the military group, as occurred 
when the poet Rashid al-Zubayr al-Sanusi was charged and sentenced to life 
imprisonment. Ahmad Bu-Rujayl, a [contractor] and son of Yusaf Bu-Rajayl, 
friend of the freedom fighter “Umar Mukhtar, was sentenced under similar 
circumstance. The Libyan people believed that these plots could be real, 
but they rejected the inhumane methods which were being used against their 
fellow citizens. At first, Qadhdhafi did not want to show his cards all 

at once because a plan had been laid to purge the Libyan people in stages 
without regard for the people as a whole. Thus, he claimed that he was 
unaware of what had been going on in the torture cells, and a committee 
was formed to investigate the acts of torture and the deaths of numerous

Campaign of Murder 


3. <A campaign of disguised murders began against certain prominent in- 
dividuals in the army. Revolution Council member Captain al-Maqrif was 
murdered, and it was claimed that he died in an automobile accident. 
Officer al-Harathi was murdered, and again an automobile accident was 
blamed. Capt ‘Atiyah al-Kasih was murdered, another alleged victim of 

an automobile accident. And then an individual by the name of Miftah 
al-Hindbany, a man well known for his courage and nationalist positions 
and one of the civilians who had cooperated with the colonel at the 
beginning of the revolution, was murdered. Once again, an automobile 
accident was given as the cause of death. Tripoli Central Prison, the 
old prison built during the time of the Fascist executioner Mussolini, 
began once more to know lines of mothers, wives and cildren coming to 
visit the arrested and condemned individuals. Sometimes they were 
permitted to do so, while at other times they were turned away. A state 
of grief gradually began to overcome the Libyan people. Is it believeable 
that these plots were real? The Fazzan conspiracy, the al-Abyar con- 
spiracy, the al-Hawwaz conspiracy, the Military Hospital conspiracy. 
Dozens--or rather, hundreds--of the Libyan people's young men are thrown 
into the prisons based on sentences handed down in trials at which no 
lawyer is present. And in spite of their severity, these sentences are 
replaced with more extreme sentences issued directly by President of the 
Revolution Qadhdhafi. During this period, many special unjust laws were 
put into force, including the Law of the People's Tribunal, the Law for 
the Protection of the Revolution, and the Law for the Prohibition of 
Party Activities. It is worth noting, perhaps, that the People's Tribunal 
Law gives the judge the right to draw up a legal provision while a case is 
being tried making any theretofore legal act a crime. In other words, 

the judge in the People's Tribunal has been given the legislative power 

in addition to the judicial power so that the defendant cannot escape the 
punishment intended for him. 


Meaning of the Popular Revolution 


4. The first assault against the Libyan people under the pretext of 
purging the main supporters of the previous regime came to an end. The 
conspirators against the Revolution Command Council had been purged. 
Mu'ammar Qadhdhafi's second assault against the Libyan people began in 

1974 with his announcement of what he termed the Popular Revolution. The 
aggression that took place against the people is something which no 
reasonable mind can comprehend. During that February, Qadhdhafi went to 
his native town of Zuwarah, which is located near the Tunisian border, and 
delivered a speech in which he attacked every man who held any specific 
ideology--in other words, belonged to a party--and assailed the (phenomenon

The Third Attack 


5. Then in 1975 the third attack on the Libyan people came with a claim 
that a plot had been uncovered. Large numbers of young army officers who 
had recently graduated were arrested, and several of the remaining members 
of the Revolution Command Council were charged. After 2 years of imprison- 
ment and horrible torture, they were brought before a special expedited 
military tribunal, and the resulting sentences ranged from life imprison- 
ment to death. The military prosecution contested a group of the sentences, 
and they were raised from life imprisonment to death. In order to expand 
the purging operation against the people to a broader scale, a mob of 
disreputable people were sent into the university precinct during the 
student union election in order to provoke the students. The mob attacked 
the students, and because the police were not aware that these elements 

had been sent by Mu'ammar Qadhdhafi himself, they apprehended them by order 
of the public prosecutor and put them in jail. On the following day,

however, an order was issued to the warden of the jail to release these

After about a week, another group from the colonel's intelligence organiza- 
tion disguised as university students provoked the students. When they 
began to be repulsed by the students, who were prepared this time, the 
students were surprised to find that the intelligence agents were carrying 
pistols to defend themselves and had drawn the weapons in the university 
precinct. While these men looked like students, they were actually agents 
from the colonel's intelligence organization. The students got revenge for 
this provocation by staging a huge demonstration. They clashed with the 
police, and the Socialist Union in Benghazi was set on fire. This was 

the colonel's goal: to lure the students into this trap so that he could 
make an example of them. In the event, large numbers of students were 
apprehended, from secondary students all the way up to university students. 
These incidents took place in 1976. 


In 1977, a group of young officers were brought before a special military 
tribunal which pronounced death sentences on 21 officers and sentenced the 
rest to life imprironment. 





On 2 April 1977, 21 officers were executed by firing squad. These officers 
ranged in age from 21 to 27. Then on 7 April, a prison vehicle carrying 
three students left Tripoli Central Prison at 0500 en route to Benghazi, 
which is 1,000 kilometers away. It reached its destination at 1500, and 
the students were then hung in the main square in front of the Socialist 
Union in Benghazi. Their bodies remained hanging from 1500 until 2000. 


On the same day, a Libyan businessman by the name of ‘Umar al-Sadiq al- 
Warfali was executed along with an Egyptian man at the port of Benghazi. 
These men had been charged with planning to blow up the port, but no 
evidence was offered to the people to back up this charge, and no citizens 
were present at their trial. Traffic was rerouted by the place where the 
gallows had been erected in order to force the citizens to view the i.no- 
cent victims, and the victims were also shown on television that night. 

Do not ask about the terror which struck the women, children and young 
people who stood staring in disbelief at the suspended bodies of the 
martyrs. 


The April Massacres 


6. Colonel Qadhdhafi's main goal became to spread terror and fear in the 
minds of the citizens on a continuous basis. He turned the day of the 
massacre in April into an occasion for celebrating that event with those 

of his aides who remained. He named one of the squares "7 April Square" 
and put up placards menacing the citizens with the warning that every April 
will be a time for revenge. There is a sign in the square on the Baninah 
Airport Road in Benghazi which announces that "7 April is a day to settle 
accounts with reactionism." Similar signs can be found on al-Funduq Road 
and in front of Sports City in Benghazi, all of them specifying the month 
of April as the annual time for revenge on the citizens.

The seriousness of this threat has been confirmed by events. With the 
beginning of February in 1978=-i.e., the year following the execution of 
the officers and students--a group of innocent citizens were apprehended. 
Care was taken to make sure that they came from different classes. Thus 
they included contractors, teachers, businessmen, students, functionaries 
and officers. Military intelligence took custody of these individuals 
and began a campaign of physical and psych. logical torture against them. 
They were finally released some 6 months after rumors had been circulated 
they they would be executed in April. Most of these citizens emerged 
from their ordeal mentally and physically impaired. This was purposefully 
done so that these citizens would tell about what they had been subjected 
to in the way of forms of torture foreign to the nature of the peace- 
loving Libyan people, with the result that the state of terror and fear 
which had begun to reign over many of the citizens would be heightened. 





Another purpose of this operation was to pave the way for a horrible 
measure which Colonel Qadhdhafi wanted to put into effect. This measure 
was the appropriation of the private property of the citizens, including 
real estate, businesses and contracting firms. In fact, during the same 
year, the unparalleled ruler of the Libyan people issued a law which 
nationalized the citizens’ real estate. It permitted ownership of one 
dwelling only, solely for the use of the owner and his family. Then he 
put together a group of unidentifiable and disreputable persons and began 
sending them out in raucous gangs to stage what he termed a "march" on 
the private companies and businesses of the citizens and seize them, 
allegedly on behalf of the people! This was based on the claim that 
business, according to Qadhdhafi, is an exploitative and predatory phe- 
nomenon, as are the contracting firms. He called the businessmen and 
contractors "thieves," and "robbers," and he repeated these terms con- 
tinuously whevever he held a televised news conference. In other words, 
he was not content with divesting the people of their possessions, but 
launched a sustained attack against their dignity. 


7. In April 1979, Qadhdhafi prepared another operation to terrorize the 
Libyan people in the city of Benghazi. Saboteurs under his authority set 

off a bomb in the house of a young man named Tawfiq Bin-Sa'ud, a [contractor] 
in Benghazi. This martyr died immediately after the incident, and Qadhdhafi's 
intelligence service claimed that the young man had been setting the timing 
on the bomb so that it would go off in Qadhdhafi's motorcade. A wide-scale 
operation of arrests was launched against all the people, and exhaustive 
investigations were conducted in order to spread terror in the minds of 

the citizens and gather as much information as possible about the citizens’ 
private lives. 


The pretext given for the arrest and interrogation of each citizen was that 
the intelligence service had found that individual's name on a list in the 
possession of the martyr Tawfiq ibn Sa'ud. However, many people did not 
examine this list to find out the truth of this claim, and those who did 
found that it was a typed list of names including their own. The takings 
for April 1979 consisted of a large number of arrested individuals, some

held under no specific charge along with as many as 100 other prisoners. 


8. Following the operation to divest the citizens of their private property, 
Qadhdhafi formed public sector companies and business firma belonging to 

the state. He laid another trap for the people in order to purge another 
group. Many ordinary citizens and functionaries came forward to partici- 
pate because there was no other field in which to pursue any activity to 

earn a living, since this ruler had slammed all the doors of livelihood 

in the faces of the citizens. 


The year 1980 arrived, and on the same date as before, 10 February, Qadhdhafi 
sent his terrorist groups--which |! named the "Revolutionary Committees" 
and which receive their orders directly from him--to conduct dawn raids on 
the houses of the citizens. The pretext given for these raids was that 
certain individuals were collecting commissions from foreign companies 
while working in public sector enterprises. If they were not employed in 
public sector enterprises, the charge was that they had smuggled their 
personal assets abroad. And if they fell into neither of these categories, 
they were accused of criticizing and damaging the country's position. The 
important fact, however, is that with the arrival of April each year, a 
bloody purge is conducted against large numbers of the Libyan people. 


Appeal to the Peoples of the World 


At this point, we pause a moment to implore the peoples of the world and 
the various information media which constitute world public opinion to 
focus their attention on what is befalling the Libyan people and to 
monitor the methods which are being used this year with those who are now 
under arrest and the manner in which apprehensions and interrogations are 
being conducted. We summarize these things as foilows: 


A. Apprehension: Colonel Qadhdhafi issues orders directly to the groups 
subordinate to him, which consist of civilians from morally degenerate 
classes along with other groups of mercenaries who wear special uniforms 
consisting of green tunics with face masks of the same color, since their 
leader is the author of the Green Book. These men are given lists of 
names and they go to each individual's house late at night and knock on 
the door. When the individual they are looking for comes out, they tell 
him they want to talk to him for a few minutes. They lure him close to 
their vehicle, and then push him inside and take off through the streets 
at an insane rate of speed. They deliver their captive to the place of 
detention, leaving his wife and children behind him in a state of fear and 
unparalleled anxiety knowing that their loved one has been seized in the 
annual April roundup. They are well aware of what the month of April 
means for any individual whose name happens to be drawn.

The Falaqah and Electricity 


B. Interrogation: The organization of executioners conducts interroga- 
tions using the “falaqah" [beating the soles of the feet with a stick], 
electric shock and threats to bring shame on the prisoner. This 

year, something new was added: the prisoner must confess what he is asked 
to confess or else his wife and children are killed. But this is not all. 
After the desired confession is obtained, the accused is brought forward 
while still under arrest and made to confess before a television camera 
that he is a criminal and a thief and anything else he is told to say by 
the executioners organization under the leadership of President Mu'ammar 
Qadhdhafi. Just try to imagine a man being seen on television by his wife 
and children confessing that he is a criminal and a thief before he is 
even brought to trial or questioned by a single law official. Try to 
envision this affront to dignity, this utter disregard for humanity, and 
this destruction of the ideals of man. 





The Gallows Provide Him Relaxation 


Those who think that what is occurring in Libya is an internal action with 
which no one on the outside should interfere may believe that this problem 
is merely a political dispute between a rviler and a group of people on 
account of certain issues, or perhaps a pover struggle. However, this is 
not the case. 


The true situation is this: authority has been seized by a person who 
possesses a bizarre criminal and agressive disposition which is closer 

to a sickness than anything else. This person is only at rest when he 
sees the gallows and the prisons and the weeping of the defeated and 
conquered. Qadhdhafi has said this to the world on more than one occasion: 
"There is no government in Libya. There is no president. Rather, there 
are revolutionary committees, and I (i.e., Qadhdhafi) am the leader of 
these revolutionary committees." Perhaps the best confirmation of the 
illegitimacy of this murderer's authority is the fact that he marched on 
the Libyan embassies--which constitute the state's interface with other 
states--and turned them into popular offices, thereby breaking all inter- 
national laws and conventions 


We, the Libyan people, implore all other peoples of the world to support 
us in this ordeal and to reject with every possible means the April 
massacre which this executioner named Mu'ammar Qadhdhafi will undertake 
this year. Hundreds of victims and innocent persons are awaiting behind 
bars, and behind them are their families and children reaching out to 
every conscientious person in this world and imploring him to support 

them to the utmost of his ability, in keeping with humanitarian duty which 
is the basic source of all noble values in this life. 


My brother humanitarian, the lists of victims have begun to be made in 
earnest, and we want you to stand behind us in order to put an end to 
them. But before we conclude this appeal to your conscience, we offer

Family Receives the Body 


One Sunday at the beginning of February of this year, an educated young 
man well known among his countrymen for his high moral standards returned 
from London. He was a [contractor] from the city of Tripoli by the name 
of ‘Amir al-Tahir al-Daghisi. As soon as he landed at the airport he 

was taken in custody by people from the murderer's organizations who took 
him in for interrogation. The following Wednesday, hie family was 
summoned to receive his body. He had been “hot to death. 


It should be noted that Qadhdhafi is now no longer ashamed to commit such 
outrages against the Libyan people. On the contrary, he held a conference 
with his revolutionary committees and called for a physical purge of his 
adversaries at home and abroad, whom he did not identify. His newspapers 
carried confirmation of these remarks. An article in the sixth iseue of 
the newspaper AL-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR of 18 February 1980 entitled "Awaiting 
the Zero Hour" evinced a readiness to carry out this physical purge. 
After all this, we do not believe that what is happening in Libya can be 
considered an internal action. Rather, it is a hostile action aimed at 
the life and dignity of man, and is completely devoid of any political 
content. 


The Libyan people--who are unarmed, are friendly to all peoples of the 
world, and are surrounded by mercenaries and evil people--call on these 
peoples to stand behind them and reject with every possible means the 
annual massacres which are occurring, beginning in February and continuing 
through April. These massacres are carried out with a savagery which re- 
minds one of the celebrations held by barbarian peoples when the blood of 
their human sacrifices was shed. May humanity always triumph, and may 

the executioners of the peoples be defeated.
