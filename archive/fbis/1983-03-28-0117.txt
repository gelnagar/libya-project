LIBYA

ENVOY OF GHANA'S RAWLINGS DEPARTS AFTER TALKS 


LD261228 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 25 Mar 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 25 Mar (JANA) -- Captain Kojo Sikata, specialadviser of the 
Ghanaian Provisional National Defense Council [PNDC] and personal envoy of 

Flight Lieutenant Jerry Rawlings, chairman of the PNDC, and the accompanying 
Ghanaian delegation which included Dr Kwesi Botwe, minister of finance and economic 
planning, and Appiah Korang, minister of fuel and power, has left Tripoli this 
afternoon after a visit to Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah during 

which he held a series of talks on strengthening cooperation between the Jamahiriyah 
and Ghana. 


In a statement to JANA at Tripoli international airport Capt Kojo praised the people 
of the Jamahiriyah's achievements in various fieldsthanks to the Great Al-Fatin 
Revolution. He also expressed his deep satisfaction with the results of his and 
the accompanying delegation's visit to the Jamahiriyah. I was entrusted by Flt 

Lt Jerry Rawlings to deliver a message to the brother leader pertaining to the 
bilateral cooperation between the two revolutions. [passage indistinct]

UN GROUP PROTESTS U.S. RESTRICTION ON STUDENTS 


LD261620 Tripoli JANA in English 1400 GMT 26 Mar 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada Thani 12, March 26, JAMAHIRIYAH ‘EWS AGENCY -- The 
Jamahiriyah delegation at U.N.E.S.C.0O. objected against wine repressive resolution 
taken by the U.S. Administration which prohibits the Jamahiriyah students from 
continuing their education in the U.S. in advanced technology. 


The written protest which was distributed to U.N.E.S.C.0.'s permanent delegates 
asserted that this racist decision constitutes a flagrant violation of the declara- 
tion of human rights and U.N.E.S.C.0."s own charter. It added that it discloses 
the barbarism and decline of the U.S. Administration and its negligence of the 
natural rights of man to acquire knowledge. 


The Libyan U.N.E.S.C.O. delegate said that the Arab group who met upon the request 
of the Jamahiriyah delegation has decided to inform the U.S. U.N.E.S.C.O. 
delegation of its own objections.
