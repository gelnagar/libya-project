LIBYA

Armed Forces Placed at Disposal of Junblatt, Syria 
LD180044 Tripoli Voice af Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2315 GMT 17 Sep 83 


[Text] JANA has learned that the leader of the revolution, the 
commander in chief of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, has 
issued an order placing the Libyan Arab forces deployed in 
Al-Biga’ at the disposal of Walid Junblatt, leader of the Progres- 
sive Socialist Party. The order also places the Libyan Arab 
Armed Forces and all their military (?equipment) at the disposal 
of the Syrian command. The leader of the revolution has called 
on Arabs to follow Libya's example. 


The leader of the revolution also sent a telegram to Arab kings 
and presidents. Here is its text:
