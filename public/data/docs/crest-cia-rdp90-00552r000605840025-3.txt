Nothing specific to Tripoli sites appears.
• Schlachter is a 38-year-old Virginian held by U.S. Marshals and faces more than 25 years in prison if convicted.
• Prosecutors requested bail of $500,000 at an arraignment in federal court.
• Schlachter's lawyer said he had documents suggesting he was taking instructions from and reporting to CIA and Office of Naval Intelligence personnel while in Libya.
• The CIA denied official involvement in Wilson's private activities but acknowledged some officials were involved with Wilson.
• 1978: Wilson and Terpil struck a deal to supply the Libyan Government with explosives and train terrorists
