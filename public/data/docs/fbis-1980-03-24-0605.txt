LIBYA

NIGER PRESIDENT KOUNTCHE WELCOMED TO LIBYA BY JALIWD 
1D221418 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 22 Mar 80 ID 


[Text] President of Niger, Seyni Kountche arrived at noon today in Tripoli on a visit 
to the Jamahiriyah. He was welcomed at the airport by Maj 'Abd as-Salam Jallud, the 
secretaries of the General People's Committee, the secretary general of the OAU, Edem 
Kodjo, diplomats and members of the Niger Embassy. 


In a statement to JANA, the president explained that the visit was within the framework 
of direct contacts to strengthen ties of friendship and brotherhood between the Libyan 
and Niger peoples. He stressed that his meetings in Libya would be a means of reviewing 
fraternal relations, the stage they had reached and ways of strengthening them in the 
interests of the two brotherly peoples. The president of Niger is accompanied by a 
delegation which includes Minister of Economic Affairs, Trade and Industry Mai Maigana, 
the minister of foreign affairs and cooperation, Daouda Diallo, and Interior Under 
ecretary Malam Moussa.

'Closed Meeting’

[Text] Tripoli, 22 March--A closed meeting was held this evening between Staff Maj 
‘Abd al-Salam Ahmad Jallud and Seyni Kountche, the president of the Niger Supreme 
Military Council, who arrived in Tripoli at noon today on a visit. The meeting was 
also attended by Foreign Secretary Dr. 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki. 


BULGARIAN BUILDING MINISTER ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 
1D232028 Tripoli JANA in English 1725 GMT 23 Mar 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, March 23, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Bulgarian minister of construc- 
tion, Mr.Ivan Sakarev, arrived in Tripoli today for a several day visit to the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. During the visit the Bulgarian minister will hold 
talks with Libyan Arab officials aimed at strengthening relations between the two 

peoples in both countries. The president of the Petrochemical Workers Unions of Bulgaris 
also arrived on the same plane. He is due to attend the fifth international anti-oil 
monopolies conference which is expected to be convened here in Tripoli between 26-30 
March.
