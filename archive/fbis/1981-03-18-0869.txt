LIBYA

FOREIGN LIAISON BUREAU STATEMENT ON MAURITANIA 


LD180054 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 17 Mar 81 


[Text] The People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison yesterday issued a statement in connection 
with the attempt which was carried out by elements supported by Morocco to overthrow 
the Mauritanian Government. The statement said: 


The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has been following with great concern news 
of the abortive coup which was attempted by some fascist elements in collusion with the 
hireling and imperialist-backed regime in Morocco. While strongly denouncing this 
treacherous attempt, which was aimed at the freedom and independence of fraternal 
Mauritania and attempted to undermine its independence and security, the Jamahiriyah 
reaffirms its stand most strongly alongside the Mauritanian people and their government 
and its support for it. 


It declares that any aggression against Mauritania is aggression against the Jamahiriyyah. 
It would also like to record the Jamahiriyyah people's appreciation for the fraternal 
Mauritanian people and congratulate them for the awakening they have shown by rallying 
around their leadership in this trial and their strong opposition to this conspiracy and 
their success in nipping it in the bud.

LD180052 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 17 Mar 81

the Central Committee and PDRY minister of interior, who conveyed to him a message from

Military Committee for National Salvation. 


AL-QADHDHAFI ANNOUNCES FORMATION OF CAVALRY FORCE 


LD180436 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 17 Mar 81 


[Text] The brother leader has announced the ‘ormation of the popular cavalry force as 

a step along the road of the emergence of the people's militarization. He explained in 

a meeting with hundreds of cavalrymen who took part in the popular cavalry festival, 

held to mark the 4th anniversary of the emergence of the people's authority and the birth 
of the Jamahiriyah, that thousands of cavalrymen will join the banner of this force in 
all parts of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. They will be trained in 

the use of all types of weapons according to their abilities and standards. The care of 
the cavalrymen and their horses will cover all regions of the Jamahiriyah. 


The leader said that in this way a new popular armed force has come into being to defend 
the revolution, realize its aims and protect the people's authority. The force will 
consist of revue units with combat horses with weapons; in the use of which the force 

will be trained. The JANA reporter learned that Col Ali al-Fayturi has been entrusted with 
the task of forming and supervizing this force. 


The cavalrymen who took part in the popular festival of horsemanship, held at the cavalry 
school on the read to Tripoli international airport, sent a message to the brother leader 
in which they expressed their loyalty and readiness to sacrifice themselves for the sake 
of the Great 1 September Revolution and strengthening the people's authority. 


The cavalrymen staged a march of support for what the leader stated at the meeting. 
They expressed pride and happiness over the giant achievements which have been realized 
on the soil of the Jamahiriyah and which have astounded the world. 


It is worth noting that a grand popular horsemanship festival will be held in Benghazi in 
celebration of the llth anniversary of the expulsion of British bases from Libyan Arab 


soil.
