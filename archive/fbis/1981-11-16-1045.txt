TRIPOLI REPORTS ON TROOP WITHDRAWAL, CHAD EVENTS 


LD151422 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 15 Nov 81 


[Text] A responsible source at the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison made the following statement: 


Today, Sunday, 15 November 1981, the last Libyan Arab soldier arrived back from Ndjamena 
and, thus, the Chadian capital has been completely evacuated of the Libyan Arab forces 
which liberated it a year ago. Tomorrow, Monday, the last Libyan Arab soldier will 
leave the town of Abeche, capital of eastern Chad. 


Preparations are underway in a [word indistinct] way in Sabha to welcome the Libyan 
Arab forces coming back from Chad, after performing their historic and humanitarian 
role by ending the devastating civil war, bolstering the freedom of the Chadian people 
and firmly establishing the bases of peace and security in Chad.

LD151244 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1200 GMT 15 Nov 81 


[Text] The Sudanese forces and the forces of Hissein Habre occupied three towns in 

eastern Chad today after the withdrawal of the Libyan Arab forces. They are Guereda, 

Adre and Iriba. The forces of the Chadian Government retreated 30 kilometers in the 
direction of Abeche after the occupation of the three towns. The Sudanese forces and 

che forces of Hissein Habre are moving in the direction of (Umm Zuwayr). It has been 

learned that fierce fighting is going on at the outskirts of that town between the government 
forces and the Sudanese forces and the forces of Hissein Habre. 


OIL SECRETARIAT SAYS ESSO ‘ABANDONS' INTERESTS 


LD141744 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1720 GMT 14 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 14 Nov (JANA) -- The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
Secretariat for Oil today issued the following statement: 


The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah Secretariat for Oil announces that on 
4 November 1981, Esso Standard (Libya) Limited and Esso (Sirte) Limited served notice on 
the abandonment of all their interests in the Jamahiriyah. 


The Secretariat for Oil replied to the two said companies that their actions were incompatible 
with oil law No 25/55M and its amendments, were also incompatible with the general provisions 


of the law, and that their action was tantamount to a failure by each of them to carry out 
their legal commitments and was a unilateral abrogation of their contractual ties. 


The Secretariat for Oil reserved and continue toreserve its full rights under the law. 


TRIPOLI: HAIG CHARGES ON PARIS ATTEMPT RETRACTED 


LD141932 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1815 GMT 14 Nov 81 


[Text] The United States retracted its charges against the Jamahiriyah. U.S. Secretary 
of State Alexander Haig alleged that Libya has behind the attempt on the life of the 
U.S. charge d'affaires in Paris the day before yesterday. 


The U.S. State Department last night officially admitted its falsification of facts and 
the leveling of false charges against the Jamahiriya. An official statement made by the 
department's spokesman said that there was no tangible proof that Libya played a role in 
the attempt on the life of the American diplomat.

COMMENTARIES ASSAIL BRIGHT STAR MANEUVERS 


LD152358 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 15 Nov 81 
[Unattributed commentary] 


[Excerpts] If the activities of the American military forces outside the United States 
fall within the framework of mere maneuvers, as alleged by the Washington administration, 
there would have been no need to carry out these maneuvers where they are now taking place. 
From the technical viewpoint, a maneuver is carried out to test the forces’ strength 

and effectiveness and the degree of coordination among the various units. From this 
technical point, the maneuver can yield the desired results if it is carried out on the 
territory of the state which wants to test its forces. 


But because the U.S. purpose for sending its forces to the region is something quite 
different from the technical maneuvers and completely divorced from maneuvers, the United 
States is not carrying out this military activity on its vast territory but on the 
territory of the Arab homeland. 


The U.S. purpose is to occupy this homeland, that is, maintain military forces in per- 
manent bases inside Arab territory, to be terrorist garrisons mobilized for movement in 
any direction inside the Arab homeland to try to liquidate and suppress any popular 
uprising and to try finally and comprehensively to make the Arabs submit. The United 
States has interests inside the Arab homeland which it exploits in order to pour into 
its treasury tens of billions of dollars every year. 


Although the United States has long relied on its Zionist base in occupied Palestine to 
play the role of guard for U.S. interests, the fact is that the United States senses that 
this Zionist base, which is surrounded by enemies and which lacks future guarantees, is 
going to be outlived by the huge reserve of the Arab strategic resources, and the United 
States has begun to tend to rely directly on its military forces to guard U.S. interests. 
Furthermore, the United States, which sees that the progressive revolutionary tide in the 
region is rising in a manner which affirms the nearness of the historic transformation, 
wants to surround and contain this tide by terrorist centers and by muscle-flexing and 
military bases. It seems that the United States believes that when it carries out this 
work by itself it will achieve its goal, since it is known to be a great power and has 
unlimited destructive capabilities. 


In addition to all these motives which prompt the United States to try to occupy the Arab 
homeland, there is yet another motive which the U.S. secretary of state called the uni- 
versal role of the American soldier. The U.S. secretary of state was referring to his 
country's interventions in the world and was declaring that his country would not allow 
its soldiers to become stale in their barracks, or soft because of lack of war since 

that was the cause of the collapse of the Roman Fmpire, according to Alexander Haig. 


At one time, the hesitating official Arab reality used to confine itself to negative 
stands: pretending that it was with Palestine, but behind the scenes it signed the plans 
of liquidating its cause. The Arab masses used to condemn that capitulating attitude. 

But the position has now become worse and more serious. Again the hesitant official 

Arab reality in some Arab countries has become the bridge over which the U.S. forces cross 
openly and in daylight to occupy the Arab homeland; it has become an armor in the 

American machinery. If the United States has concluded the strategic alliance with the 
Zionist enemy -- the alliance in accordance with which the United States is preparing 

to enter the war on the side of the Zionist enemy against the Arab nation -- the United 
States has regarded this alliance as one side of a pincer movement; the second side is 

the agent regimes who have caused their countries to fall into the hands of the American 
occupation. The United States wants to close the two sides of the pincers on the force 

of the revolution of this nation to stifle and annihilate it and so that U.S. neocolonialism 
may be free in the region.

Warning to Arab States 
LD141926 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1700 GMT 14 Nov 81 
[‘Ali Salim commentary] 


[Text] The American Bright Star maneuvers began today in the territories of four Arab 
countries: Egypt, Sudan, Somalia and Oman. The maneuvers will last 1 month. 


U.S. forces have been arriving the past few days in Egyptian ports and airports. Five 
thousand soldiers from the 24th Division of the Rapid Deployment Force have landed. M-60 
tanks and N-113 armored troop transporters were brought in by a ship which sailed from 
Georgia on 25 October. Huge C-50 and C-131 aircraft carried paratroopers and their equip- 
ment as well as a number of other armored vehicles to be used in these maneuvers. 


The major and most important phases of the Bright Star maneuvers, which are being held under 
the supervision of Robert Kingston from his headquarters at Ra's Banas, take place in the 
Egyptian western desert close to the eastern borders of the Jamahiriyah, where 6 huge 
B-52-type bombers flying direct from the United States will drop their bombs. Other troops 
later will move to Sudan and Somalia where the American forces' activities will include 
street combat training. One thousand U.S. Marines will land in Oman during a 24-hour period. 


While the United States claims that the purpose of the maneuvers is to raise the standard 
of its soldiers' combat capabilities, and while the regimes participating in the maneuvers 
justify their participation with the pretext of benefitting from U.S. high arms technology, 
there are some important points which cannot be overlooked in any evaluation of the real 
purpose of these maneuvers: 


1. The timing of the maneuvers: They are taking place following an organized and well- 
studied escalation of propaganda campaigns against the Jamahiriyah; 


2. The precise selection of the venues of the maneuvers: The common borders of the 
Jamahiriyah with Egypt and Sudan could prompt the planners of the course of the maneuvers 
and those entrusted with their execution to carry out a surprise aggression against the 
Jamahiriyah, which persistently opposes American policies in the Arab region and exposes 
the traitorious role of some of the capitulationist regimes -- a matter which does not 
meet with the approval of the United States or its agents in the area. 


Furthermore, these maneuvers are taking place at a time when the position of the United 
States is becoming critical throughout the Arab region, particularly following the death 
of As-Sadat and the unmasking of the false dreams he promised his people and those regimes 
which boarded his bandwagon. 


Thus, strategically, these maneuvers are a warning to the peoples of these countries. 
Primarily, it is easy for the United States to bring its forces to their lands, even in the 
absence of its puppets, since its huge B-52 bombers can travel nonstop from the United 
States to the Arab homeland.
