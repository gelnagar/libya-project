LIBYA

VIEWS OF AUSTRIA'S KREISKY ON MIDEAST REPORTED 


LD252110 Tripoli JANA in English 1907 GMT 25 Feb 81 


[Text] Vienna, 25 Feb (JANA)--The Austrian Chancellor Kreisky has expressed great interest 
in meeting the leader of the Great Al-Fatah Revolution in the very near future. Ina 
statement to JANA he said that this visit has not been decided yet but it would be decided 
upon next week. 


Chancellor Kreisky refuted the Egyptian regime's allegation that he had mediated between 

the Egyptian ruler and the Palestinians. He said "I deny this. As-Sadat's view is 

completely contradictory to Arafat's and it is impossible to bring them close". He added: 
"As-Sadat believes that the Palestine Liberation Organization does not have genuine inde- 
pendence while the view of the P.L.O. is that it is the sole representative of the Palestinian 
people." Chancellor Kreisky went on to say that As-Sadat is currently in a difficult position 
and is isolated because of the separate agreements. He does not represent the cause of all 
Arabs and everybody knows that he is isolated because of his policy in the Arab world." 


Concerning his party's stance toward the Sahrawi people's cause, he said that "the majority 
in the party support the Polisario and Austrian Government will take a supporting stand of 


the Sahrawi Republic if the United Nations took such a decision". 


Kreisky also declared that the North-South dialogue would be held in Mexico and group 
fifteen countries at summit level. The date would also be fixed soon. 


On the Iraqi-Iranian war, the Austrian chancellor said that the Iraqi Army was the one to 
start the war and it occupied parts of Iran. The United Nations should find a solution to 
this problem which might come with the withdrawal of the Iraqi troops, he added.
