LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI REMARKS ON LEBANON, U.S., TERRORISM 


LD131502 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1230 GMT 13 May 81 


[Text] The brother leader of the revclution has reaffirmed that the deterioration of the 
situation in Lebanon is the result of the continuous Zionist aggression. In an interview 
with Spanish television, which was screened last night, he said: I clearly see an Israeli 
act of aggression on an independent state--Lebanon. He added that the conditions which 

the Israelis are demanding from the army which they want to attack--that it should not 
defend itself--are unprecedented. The leader of the revolution referred to the installation 
of the missiles to confront the enemy, and the reaction this has created. He pointed out 
that the task of these missiles is to provide air cover for the forces which are confronting 
the aggression. He said that Israel wants to attack these forces and at the same time 

wants these forces to remain without air cover. The brother leader reaffirmed that it is 
unbelie ible that the Israelis will do anything without orders and approval from the United 
States. 


In reply to a question whether he believed the Palestinians would ever regain their land 
following the announcement of the Egyptian regime that it will never fight Israel, the 
leader of the revolution reaffirmed that the Palestinian people have the right to fight 
in order to liberate their land whether Egypt surrendered to the enemy or not. In the 
long run, the Palestinian people will be able to liberate their land. 


During the interview the leader of the revolution spoke about the humane attitude which 

the Libyan Arab people adopted toward their brothers the people of Chad, and their help to 
the latter in achieving their freedom. He reaffirmed anew that we sent our forces to Chad 
in accordance with a joint defense agreement and at the request of the national government 
in Chad. Our armed forces were able to put an end to the war that went on for 20 years. 
The leader added: Now that the war is over, peace and security have returned to Chad. The 
leader reaffirmed that this action for peace by the Libyan Army deserves the Nobel Peace 
Prize. 


With regard to the U.S. administration's decision to close down the Libyan Arab People's 
Bureau in Washington and the expulsion of its staff, the brother leader said: This has 
disappointed us in that the new U.S. administration has proven that it is more stupid than 
the Carter administration. By this measure, it appears that the U.S. Government does not 
know others [as heard] and has no idea what international politics is all about. The 
brother leader pointed out: We see no serious reason why the U.S. administration should 
make such a decision. He added: No doubt we are honored not to have relations with a 
country which has been cursed by all peoples of the world. He said: The U.S. Government, 
in making this decision, has violated the principles and the Charter of the United Nations, 
as the aims of this charter are geared toward bolstering friendly relations between the 
peoples of the world in order to consolidate peace.

He pointed out that the explusion of the members of the Libyan Arab People's Bureau in 
Washington will have a negative reflection on the world, no doubt, [as heard] and in 
the long run the United States has condemned itself by taking such an action. It 
appears that the United States does not want to see any free or neutral state in the 
world but wants states to be either slaves or enemies of the United States. We refuse 
to be slaves for anyone. 


The brother leader expressed his belief that this U.S. policy will seriously harm U.S. 
interests in the region in the future, as the future in the Arab homeland belongs to 
the force that supports Libya, and that this future will be in the hand of this force. 
He said: I believe that the United States has never learned the lessons of Iran, 
Ethiopia, Vietnam, and Kampuchea. 


The leader reaffirmed Libya's rejection of terrorism and said: We are against 
terrorism. This is quite clear. He pointed out that there is a deliberate confusion 
between the struggle waged by peoples for national liberation and terrorism or terrorist 
actions. The leader pointed out that the aim behind this deliberate confusion is to 
obstruct the struggle of the peoples for liberation. He said: We wholeheartedly sup- 
port struggle when the aims of the latter are just and when there are justifications 

for such a struggle. He added: We have our own concept of what is terrorism. I have 
said on many occasions that the pinnacle of terrorism lies in manufacturing nuclear 
weapons, the establishment of military bases on others’ territories and deliberately 
making peoples go hungry. The zenith of this terrorism lies in the economic, military 
and political pressures which are the making of the colonialist states. The leader 
stressed the need for distinguishing between this and individual terrorist actions 

such as the taking of innocent hostages, kidnapping and threatening with the use of 
arms--such actions are carried out by individuals and well-known terrorist organiza- 
tions. He said that there is a psychological and social atmosphere in the world which 
motivates such kind of terrorism. For my part, I consider it to be more legitimate 

that we should discuss the possibility of removing the psychological and social tensions 
from which the world is suffering. 


WESTERN ENVOYS TOLD OF MOROCCAN 'CAMPAIGN' 


LD121550 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 12 May 81 


[Text] Within the contacts and meetings of the People's Foreign Liaison Bureau these 
days for the purpose of uncovering and exposing the tendentious campaigns waged by the 
agent Moroccan regime, a meeting took place last night at the headquarters of the 
People's Foreign Liaison Bureau which was attended by ambassadors from Western coun- 
tries. 


The posture of the Jamahiriyah vis-a-vis the political campaign waged by the agent 
regime in Morocco in a large number of the world's countries, trying to augment its 
hostile position against the people of the Jamahiriyah and their prominent stands, was 
explained at this meeting. 


The ambassadors were given explanations of the proposal by the brother leader on the 
emergence of unity between Mauritania and the Saharan republic, the establishment of an 
alliance among the Jamahiriyah, Algeria, Mauritania and the Saharan republic to face 
the threats and plans to which the region of North Africa is being exposed and the 
involvement of the Moroccan king in these plans to disturb the region's security. The 
People's Foreign Liaison Bureau told the ambassadors that the agent Moroccan regime, 
while it claims to support the Palestinian people's right to self-determination, denies 
this right to the Saharan people, whose right has been recognized and confirmed by 
international organizations such as the United Nations, the OAU and others.

It was stressed to the ambassadors that the dispute between the Jamahiriyah and the 
Moroccan regime was a dispute over principles, that there was no dispute between the two 
fraternal peoples and that the king of Morocco was playing a role supplementary to that 
of the ruler of Egypt. The Jamahiriyah has asked the ambassadors to explain the posture 
of the Jamahiriyah to friends and brethren in their countries, acquainting them with 
this move of the agent Moroccan regime and that the Jamahiriyah will work with those 

who are genuine to expose and isolate this regime. 


AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS CONGRATULATIONS TO MITTERRAND 


LD120103 Iripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 11 May 81 


[Text] The brother leader has sent a cable of congratulations to the French president- 
elect, Francois Mitterrand, congratulating him for the trust placed in him by the 
majority of the French people and for his triumph in the French presidential elections. 
In his cable, the leader said: Your election rightly indicates that the French people 
were not pleased with the policy of Giscard d'Estaing, which was characterized by 
hostility toward the African peoples who are aspiring for freedom. Mcreover, it was 
also characterized by a return to the old concept of colonialism and military interven- 
tion for the purpose of dominating people, particularly in Africa. 


The brother leader added that the socialists are the progressive forces and that we are 
in accord with chem in the aim of building socialism to achieve social justice. 


In his cable the brother leader called on the new French president to sign a deeper 
understanding of the Palestinian issue to support the right of the Palestinian Arab 
people to self-determination; to take important steps to consolidate relations with 
Libya, which had beenthe bridge of the Arab-French friendship; and to consolidate rela- 
tions with the Arab homeland, which has numerous common interests with the French 
people--relations which were harmed by Giscard d'Estaing's colonialist, irresponsible 
policy, which is under American domination. 


MITTERRAND VICTORY IN FRENCH ELECTION WELCOMED 


LD102016 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 10 May 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 10 May (JANA)--The JANA political editor has welcomed the news of 

the victory of Francois Mitterrand in the French presidential election. The editor 

said: The fall of P'Estaing [as received] means the fall of the policy of colonialist domination in 
Africa and the Arab homeland which D'Estaing pursued and which led to the tarnishing of 

the reputation of France. The editor added: The victory of socialist Mitterrand 

establishes an opportunity of understanding among socialists, through a fruitful 

dialogue, because capitalist D'Estaing always followed an infamous policy. 


EXILE ATTRIBUTES STUDENT DEATH TO ‘KILLER SQUAD’ 


NC140900 Paris AFP in English 0845 GMT 14 May 81 


[Text] Rabat, May 14 (AFP)--An 18-year-old Libyan student murdered in England last 
April was assassinated by one of Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi's killer squads, 
exiled opposition leader Yousouf Lamgarief said here Wednesday. Mr Lamgarief, a former 
ambassador to India, said that Sanoussi Latiwish was in contact with Libyan opposition 
supporters in Britain at the time of his death. (Mr Latiwish was found murdered in a 
field outside Cambridge last April. British police have said they did not believe he 
had been killed for political reasons.) 


The student's father, an army chief of staff under the deposed monarchy who remained 
in favour after Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's 1969 coup, died in an Athens hotel in January 
this year. General Latiwish, who was detained in Libya briefly at the end of last 
year following reports of an army mutiny in Tobruk, was allowed to leave the country 
for medical reasons.

Greek authorities said that the general died of a heart attack, and his body was flown home 
for burial. His son said [as received] flew to Libya for his father's burial and charged 
publicly that his father had been assassinated, Mr Lamgarief said.
