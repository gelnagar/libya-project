Ministerial Conference on Desertification Ends 
ABO091735 Dakar PANA in English 1711 GMT 9 Nov 85 

[Text} Dakar, 9 Nov (PANA)— The Second Ministerial-Confer- 
ence on Desertification ended on Saturday by adopting about 30 
major projects to accelerate efforts against desertification. 

The conference gave fnntidals to the current chairman (Senegal) 
to take necessary measures in obtaining material and. financial 
support frorm different sources in order to facilitate immediate 
implementation of the projects. 

Emphasizing the need for South-South cooperation, the con- 
ference also resolved that it should be complemented by. North- 
South cooperation. 

On the continuous surveillance of the ecosystems, the conference 
adopted projects on making the existing meteorological network 
more dynamic and strengthening it, operating a teletransmission 
system in various Comides [Conference Ministerielle sur la 
Desertification — Ministerial Conference on Desertification] 
countries, compiling an inventory of studies on climatic systems 
in different regions, and establishing a terrestrial mechanism for 
the surveillance of the ecosystems. 

Others include the effective utilization of high technology for 
evaluating of activities related to desertification and on the 
establishment of an organisation and a management formula for 
different meteological networks, as well as a followup 
mechanism. 

On the management and utilisation of water resources aimed at 
controling drought and desertification, the conference adopted 
projects which include the establishment of schemes for the 
management and utilisation of the waters of major hydrographic 
systems, and an inventory study project of the sites selected form 
using the surface waters of the hydrographic system of Lake 
Chad. 

Other projects include a study on the possibility of multiplying 
biological techniques in water conservation efforts, research on 
water stockage and evaporation control technics, model for the

use of deep and superficial layers common to the region, and the 
establishment for river-basin agriculture i in arid and semi-arid 
Sahelian regions. 

On the economy of wood products the conference adopted pro- 
jects on the study and development of vegetal formations, 
research and popularization of substitute energies, rehabilitation 
of degraded areas and creation of funds from resources generated 
by the exploitation of forests to ensure that forestry operations 
are implemnted. 

Other projects include the adoption of a price policy which best 
reflects the value of forests products and the design of a study 
looking into the possibility of energy exebanaes among the 
Comides countries. . 

Regarding soils and salinisation, the conference adopted projects 
which include the establishment of an inventory in each state of 
the affected areas. This will be the basis of a map which will make 
it possible to identify and monitor the extent and gravity of the 
problem and to determine in which areas. urgent actions are to 
be undertaken. 

The ministers also adopted projects aimed at making efforts to 
inform the popualtions about the dimension of the phenomenon 
and thereby increase their awareness of the possible limitations 
represented by the salinisation of poorly irrigated lands. 

On the. bush and. burnt lands, the conference adopted a project 
aimed at establishment village committees for the control of bush 
fires. It is expected that these committees will serve to safeguard 
stocks of organic matter and soil fertility. They will likewise 
guarantee more effective management or forest ecosystem. 

The ministers expressed in their final report the wish to hold the 
next conference scheduled in 1988 in one of the North African 
countries. 

Twenty-five countries participated in the conference from Alge- 
ria, Benin, Burkina Faso, Cape Verde, Congo, Ivory Coast, 
Gambia, Senegal, Ghana, Guinea-Bissau and Guinea-Conakry. 
Others are Mali, Morocco, Mauritania, Niger, Sierra Leone, 
Chad, Togo, Tunisia, Sudan and Egypt. Cameroon, Central 
African Republic and Libya were participating for the first time.
