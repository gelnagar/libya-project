LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS LETTER TO ARAB SUMMIT 


LD302016 Tripoli JANA in English 1845 GMT 30 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulqidah 11, August 30, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Colonel Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi leader of the Great al-Fatah Revolution has sent a letter to the presidents 
of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front on the Arab summit scheduled on 6th September. 


The intended summit is complementary to Fes summit. Unfortunately it is to take place when 
it had become too late for such a move. Arab reaction is banking on this summit to condone 
Zionist aggression, which resulted in the killing of thousands of people and driving out the 
Palestinian fighters from Lebanon. 


Furthermore, the conference is to give its blessing to Arab reaction's efforts in backing 
Zionist schemes. 


The recognition of Israel will be discussed at this summit in two different proposals, so 
that if one fails, the other will be carried out. 


The return of the Egyptian regime to Arab ranks will also be discussed at this summit. By 
doing so, the past will be forgotten, and so will the blow dealt to the Arab nation by the 
Egyptian regime. At this summit, the Iraq-Iran war, which was started on behalf of the 
U.S.A. by the Iraqi regime against the Iranian Islamic regime, will also be repudiated. 


The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has decided not to take part in this summit 
and has rejected the conspiracy. The Jamahiriyah believes its participation means sub- 


mission to imperialist-Zionist-reactionary schemes. 


AL-QADHDHAFI ATTENDS NAVY CELEBRATIONS 


LD302230 Tripoli JANA in English 1803 GMT 30 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulqidah 11, August 30, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The revolution's 
leader and supreme commander of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
this morning attended celebrations to mark the delivery by the Libyan Arab naval forces 

of vessels including missile boats, as part of festivities marking the 13th anniversary 

of the Great al-Fateh Revolution. The ceremonies were also attended by the Commander-in- 
Chief of ‘the Libyan Arab Armed Forces Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir.

JABIR ADDRESSES CADET GRADUATION CEREMONY 


LD270928 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 27 Aug 82 


[Excerpt] Darnah, 27 Aug (JANA) -- Within the framework of the celebrations of the 
13th anniversary of the Great al-Fatih Revolution, a ceremony was held yesterday morning 
for the graduation of the first group of air cadets at Ai-Bunbah Secondary College. 


The graduation ceremony, held in Al-Bunbah Hall, was attended by Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis 
Jabir, commander in chief of the general staff and air force chief of staff, and by a 
number of officers, noncommissioned officers and Libyan Arab Armed Forces personnel. 

A number of cadets held a parade on the occasion. 


The commander in chief of the armed forces, Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, addressed the 
graduates. After saluting them, he said: It gives me pleasure, while we are celebrating 
the 13th anniversary of the Great al-Fatih Revolution, to attend the first graduation 
ceremony of air cadets at the secondary college at Al-Bunbah, the very place that gave 
birth to fighters like ‘Umar al-Mukhtar, the symbol of the national struggle. He 
stressed that the descendents of ‘Umar al-Mukhtar will continue the battle of liberation 
throughout our entire Arab nation. He added that on such a day we supply this country 
with youths trained in flying helicopters in order to consolidate the fighting ability 

of our armed forces. 


In his speech, he stressed that we are proceeding on the correct path drawn up by the 
leader of the revolution, the path that leads to the achievement of people's authority 
and to the final liberation from all forms of subjugation and enslavement, the path that 
leads to the establishment of the armed people. 


In his speech, he saluted the officers and noncommissioned officers of the air force 

for their efforts, which led to the graduation of this group, and stressed that he still 
must make great and unlimited efforts to develop this secondary college so it can fully 
perform its role. 


Attends 29 August Event 
LD291116 Tripoli JANA in English 0848 GMT 29 Aug 82 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, Dhulqidah 10, August 29, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- A graduation 
ceremony was held yesterday evening at Maatiga Air Force Base to mark the graduation of 
the third batch of pilots and technicians from the air force secondary school. The 
graduation ceremony, part of celebrations marking the 13th anniversary of the Great 
al-Fateh Revolution, was attended by the Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces Brigadier 
Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, the Chief-of-Staff Brigadier Mustafa al-Kharrubi, the chiefs of 
staff of the army, navy and air force, armed forces officers and a large gathering of 
citizens. 


The commander in chief [as received] of the air force school delivered a speech in which 
he said: "We are happy on this, the 13th anniversary of the Great al-Fateh Revolution, 
to celebrate a new and big accomplishment of the armed forces by graduating the third 
batch of pilots and technicians from the air force secondary school."

He said the graduation of the third batch of cadets is proof of the success of this experi- 
ment of establishing the air force secondary school, an idea first put forth by the supreme 
conmander of the armed forces, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. He said the graduation of 
the two previous batches has proved a success for this military institution, as graduates 
are now piloting the most modern aircraft. He said it is a great honour for the air force 
secondary school to graduate the first batch of girl students who responded to the call 

of the supreme commander of the armed forces for bringing closer the establishment of the 
principle of the armed people. 


Among those who were given diplomas in yesterday's graduation were several Algerian pilots 
and technicians who enrolled in the air force high school to affirm patriotic links and 
the Great al-Fateh revolution's unionist principles.

KING HASSAN RECEIVES SAUDI, SYRIAN MINISTERS 


LD301654 Rabat MAP in English 1250 GMT 30 Aug 82 
[Text] Casablanca, [no date as received] (MAP) -- King Hassan II of Morocco received here 
Saturday evening Prince ‘Abdallah ibn ‘Abd al-'Aziz, Saudi crown prince and first deputy 
president of the Ministers' Council and commander of the Saudi National Guard. 
The sovereign received afterwards Syrian Foreign Minister ‘Abd al-Halim Khaddam. 
Moroccan State Minister for Foreign Affairs M'hamed Boucetta attended this audience.
