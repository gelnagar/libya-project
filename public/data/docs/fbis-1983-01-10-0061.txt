LIBYA

PLO'S HABASH, HAWATIMAH, LEBANESE COMMUNIST ARRIVE 


LD100101 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0015 GMT 9 Jan 83 


[Text] George Habash, secretary general of the PFLP; Nayif Hawatimah, the secretary 
general of the Democratic Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine [DPFLP]; 
George Hawi, the secretary general of the Lebanese Communist Party, and their accom- 
panying delegations have arrived in Tripoli yesterday evening on a visit to the 
Jamahiriyah. 


In a statement to a JANA reporter, Brother George Habash affirmed that he is visiting 
Libya to discuss with the brothers in the Jamahiriyah the circumstances through which 
the Arab nation, the Palestinian revolution and the Lebanese patriotic movement are 
passing and the imperialist-Zionist onslaught that is aimed at the Arab nation. 


Brother George Habash said that the efforts that the leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi is 
making in this respect through his call to the leaders of the Steadfastness and 
Confrontation Front and the secretaries general of the Palestinian revolutionary groups 
and the Lebanese patriotic movement groups are worthy of serious attention in order to 
announce a practical reply that our nation's masses are awaiting after all that happen- 
ed in Lebanon. 


On his part, Brother Nayif Hawatimah said that we come to the Jamahiriyah to discuss 
the real current conditions of the Arab nation after the Zionist invasion of Lebanon. 
He affirmed this by saying: In the Palestinian revolution, we are resoleved along 
with the great Al-Fatih revolution to armed struggle and to the muster of all Arab 
energies to inflict a defeat on the enemies and liberate the land. 


In his statement to the JANA reporter, Brother George Hawi affirmed that the visit is 
aimed at strengthening the struggle of the Lebanese people to expel the Zionist inva- 
ders and restore the freedom of the Lebanese people and continue the revolution of the 
Palestinian people in the recovering of their full rights.
