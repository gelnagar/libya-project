ARAB OPPOSITION MOVEMENTS ISSUE STATEMENT 


LD061130 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 6 Feb 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 Feb (JANA) -- The pan-Arab conference of the Arab homeland opposition 
movements and popular organizations concluded its work last night. 


The conference, held in the Jamahiriyah in response to the pan-Arab initiative of the 
brother leader of the revolution to draw a new combative strategy to confront the 
psychologically and militarily defeated Arab reality, issued a statement. The follow- 
ing is the text of the statement: 


The conference of the Arab homeland opposition forces, popular organizations, and move- 
ments of armed struggle held in Tripoli 1-5 February 1983 was attended by 350 
members representing 135 organizations from various arenas in the Arab homeland and the 
Mahjar [Arabs residing abroad] in response to the pan-Arab initiative of Brother 
Colonel Mu'ammar al-qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 1 September Revolution. Its aim was 
to draw up a new combatant plan to confront the psychologically and militarily defeated 
Arab reality, to enable the Arab nation to repulse the Zionist death train that is 
supplied with American technology and financed by Arab reaction, and to start the 
counterattack. 


At the conference the participants democratically debated and studied the following 
agenda: 


1. Tee crisis of the pan-Arab liberation movement. 

2. Defining the political line. 

3. A program of action for confrontation. 

The conferees decided on the following: 

1. Concerning the crisis of the Arab liberation movement, the conference has decided 
that the pan-Arab liberation movement is suffering a sharp and grave crisis in the 
areas of ideology, organization, democracy, and in the behavior of the masses. Because 
of the gravity of this crisis, the conferees have decided to call a specialized 


seminar of patriotic, progressive, and revolutionary Arab personalities to discuss this 
crisis in all its aspects.

The results of this seminar are to be submitted to an upcoming special conference, 
which will study these results and draw up practical plans to enable the Arab nation 
to emerge from the crisis, to fulfill its combatant tasks, to wrest the initiative 
from the imperialist-Zionist-reactionary enemy, and to fulfill the tasks of this stage 
of liberation, democracy, unity, and socialism. 


The conference recommends that the permamemt secretariat of the Arab peoples confer- 
ence make preparations to follow up this seminar. 


2. The political line: The conference has decided on the following political line: 


1. The Arab nation's conflict with the Zionist enemy is a conflict of existence, not 
one of borders and not of the possibility of a settlement or coexistence between them. 
The existence of one is diametrically opposed to the existence of the other. 


2. Employing the rifle and armed struggle is the only path for the liberation of 
Palestine and all occupied Arab territories. The popular liberation war, the mobili- 
zation of the Arab masses, and building genuine democracy are the most important 
principles that should be supported and developed. 


The conference stresses the immortal precept of the pan-Arab hero Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir: 
What is taken by force can only be restored by force. The conference also adheres to 
the slogan: No peace, no negotiations, no recognition of the Zionist enemy, and no 
relinguishing the pan-Arab cause. 


3. The conference specifically points to imperialism, Zionism, and reaction as the 
archenemy of the Arab nation; the nature and aims of this enemy are diametrically 
opposed to the nature and the aims of the forces of liberation and progress in the 
Arab homeland and there is no option but to have a confrontation with them. 


4. Proceeding from the fact that the Palestine question is a pan-Arab cause from 
which hundreds of thousands of Palestinians, Egyptians, Syrians, Lebanese and others 
from the sons of the Arab nation had been martyed and in view of the pan-Arab respon- 
sibility, the conference declares its rejection of all capitulationist projects that 
seek to recognize the Zionist entity, its rejection of the Camp David accords and 

the attempt taking place to extend them, its rejection of European initiatives and 
the Reagan plan, and its rejection and condemnation of the Fes summit conference 
resolutions. It also stresses its rejection of the autonomous administrative project 
and civil administration that the Zionist enemy is trying to impose. The conference 
has decided on a struggle using all means to foil these. The conference calls for a 
confrontation of all attempts aimed at finding an alternative or a partner to the PLO, 
the sole legitimate representative of the Palestinian people. 


In this respect the conference s.resses its rejection of the attempts of Arab reaction 
to give proxy to the Jordanian regime or the Egyptian regime. The conference declares 
that the main purpose of the Arab masses' struggle is the complete liberation of 
Palestinian national soil and the establishment of a democratic state on it. 


It stresses the unity of the PLO and the rights of the Palestinian people to return, 
to self-determination, and to the unconditional establishment of an independent 
national state. It also stresses its support for an independent Palestinian charter 
to face reactionary pressures aimed at removing the organisation from its vanguard 
combatant position in the pan-Arab liberation movement, and on adherence to the 
civilized and humanitarian posture of the Arab nation, which does not discriminate 
between religions and creeds. The conference, in facing the racist, fascist Zionism 
that occupies Palestine and threatens pan-Arab existence, declares it condemnation of 
any contacts with Israeli organizations and individuals, and calls for stopping these 
contacts regardless of the slogans they raise and the concepts they put forward so 
long as they approve and recognize the Zionist usurper of the land of Palestine.

3. Program of Action. 


The conference stresses that the Zionist invasion of Lebanon has exposed the reality 
of the tragic Arab conditions from the ocean to the Gulf, the treachery of the re- 
actionary regimes, the impotence of the national regimes, and the failure of the 
popular organizations to confront the escalating imperialist-Zionist-reactionary :: ::::. 
assault, a matter which is threatening the pan-Arab existence — not only the 
Palestinian question -- as an objective result of the division, regionalism, lack of 
democracy, absence of Arab unity, absence of the masses, and deprivation of their 
authority. Arab opposition is, therefore, called upon to change this reality, to 
lead the masses to authority and not to remain as a democratic justification for the 
regimes. The conference has decided the following: 


1. To adopt the form of armed struggle in the arenas and positions that guarantee 
the effectiveness of this form, providing that the pan-Arab liberation movement ex- 
tends material and moral support to this form; and to impel it to become a reality 
facing the agent regimes and the Zionist existence in Arab territories. 


2. To form pan-Arab fedayeen companies. 


3. To struggle with all means against U.S. military bases and the ‘Rapid Deployment 
Forces in the Arab homeland, in Morocco, Egypt, Sudan, Somalia, Oman, the Peninsula, 
the Gulf, and Lebanon. 


4. To step up a pan-Arab fund for popular struggle financed by progressive states, 
popular and vocational organizations, movements, parties, and individuals in the 
Arab homeland. 


5. The pan-Arab liberational movement, under the leadership of and prompted by 

the popular masses, must isolate and eliminate capitulationist policies, practices, 
and ideas using various means to foil them including demonstrations, sit-ins, popular 
boycotts, economic and culture means, and by preventing visits and meetings connected 
with revisionism and capitulationism. 


6. In order to implement this form of struggle the revolutionary opposition forces in 
various Arab countries must be expanded and their combat programs developed through 
all possible forms of coalitions [jabhawiyah] to achieve unity and coordination be- 
tween them in confronting imperialism, Zionism, and reaction, to intensify popular 
rejection of reactionary policies; to escalate the struggle for reform on the basis 

of serious struggle for democratic freedoms and social progress in the Arab homeland 
and against the motives for division and the forces of regionalism, in thought and in 
practice; and to push forward and to achieve Arab unity. 


The conference has also decided the following: 


1. To call on the steadfastness and confrontation forces to draw up an Arab confron- 
tation plan, with the necessary elements, against Zionist invasion. 


2. To devote all resources to support the stance of the Syrian Arab Republic in its 
confrontation and challenges to the Zionist enemy and capitulationist solutions; to 
salute the sacrifices of the Syrian Arab people and their Armed Forces for the cause 
of the Arab nation in Palestine and Lebanon. 


3. To support the struggle of the Lebanese patriots in their continuous confrontation 
for the liberation of Lebanon and to force an unconditional Israeli withdrawal. The 
conference salutes the enormous sacrifices of the Lebanese patriotic masses for the 
Palestinian revolution and the pan-Arab cause. 


The conference also backs the PLO, the sole legitimate representative of the Palestin- - 
ian people, in its struggle against all capitaluationist schemes for the liberation 
of the whole Palestinian soil.

The conference salutes the Palestinian Arab people in their continuous struggle, 
especially the population of the occupied territory as it glorifies the heroic sacrifices 
that have been made and are being made against the Zionist occupation. 


The conference delcares its principled stand on the side of the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah in its struggle against imperialism, cionism, and reaction, and capitu- 
lationist solutions. 


It declares its stand on the side of Democratic Yemen against all plots being hatched 
by imperialism and reaction against it. 


The conference announces its work to mobilize the masses on the national and pan-Arab 
levels against Arab reaction in order to foil its plans and halt its vicious assault, 
a matter that calls for coalition and wider support among all Arab sides opposed to 
reaction. The conference has, therefore, decided to extend all forms of backing for 
the Egyptian national forces and the national and pan-Arab forces in Iraq, Jordan, and 
Sudan, to the National Salvation Front in Somalia, and the Popular Liberation Front 

of Saqiyah al-Hamra and Rio de Oro, to stress their right to self-determination and to 
support the national and pan-Arab forces in the Gulf and Arabian Feninsula. 


The conference has decided to stand with the Moroccan progressive forces in their 
struggle against the reactionary regime of the agent king for democracy and social 
progress. 


The conference salutes the struggle of the masses and its pan-Arab and progressive forces 
in their struggle against reaction and imperialism, extends backing and support for the 
Popular Front for the Liberation of Oman and to all the national pan-Arab forces in 

the Arab homeland, because the overthrow of any reactionary regime in the Arab arena 
will contribute to correcting the imbalance of power between us and reaction. 


The conference delcares its support and stand on the side of the popular revolution in 
Iran against the aggressive war waged by Saddam Husayn's regime, which has obstructed 


Iraq's and Iran's resources from contributing to repelling the imperialist-Zionist- 
reactionary assault. 


The conference salutes and appreciates the efforts of backing and solidarity extended by 
the countries of the socialist community headed by the Soviet Union. It also salutes the 
democratic and liberational forces in the world for their stand with regard to the 
struggle with the Arab nation, and in particular with regard to the Palestinian and 
Lebanese question. 


The conference also salutes the efforts of Arabs living ‘broad for the service of the 
causes of the Arab nation and calls for support for the ganizations that work for this. 


The conference has decided to work to support the Arab people's conference and its 
agencies on the path of developing its formation to include all the forces opposed to 
capitulationist solutions, and to attain the establishment of the popular progressive 
pan-Arab front in accordance with its charter. 


The conference delegates the implementation of these decisions to the permanent 
secretariat of the Arab people's conference, and the forces of the pan-Arab 
liberation movement. 


IRAQI ‘OPPONENTS’ ISSUE STATEMENT IN TRIPOLI 


LDO71010 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 7 Feb 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Feb (JANA) -- The Iraqi national, pan-Arab, and revolutionary forces 
yesterday held a comprehensive meeting in Tripoli. 


V. 8 Feb 83 Q5 NORTH AFRICA 


The meeting of the forces opposed to Saddam Husayn at-Tikriti's regime crowned a series 
of meetings held during the period of convening the pan-Arab conference of opposition 
movements, popular organizations, and the armed struggle movements in the Arab homeland, 
which ended its work the day before yesterday. 


The text of the statement issued by the Iraqi national, pan-Arab, and revolutionary 
forces reads as follows: 


In order to bring down the fascist agent regime in Iraq and to set up a democratic 
regime to solve the dilemmas under which the Iraqi people are suffering... 


Owing to the perils threatening the Arab national existence and the importance of 
restoring the Iraqi arena back to the pan-Arab struggle; and, as bringing down this 
fascist agent regime has become a revolutionary, national, and pan-Arab responsibility, 
the responsibility of the Iraqi masses, represented by their energetic forces; and, out 
of the Iraqi national, pan-Arab, and revolutionary forces’ sense of their national and 
pan-Arab role... 


The national parties, forces, and personalities have held a series of meetings, crowned 
by a comprehensive meeting on 6 February 1983 in Tripoli held at the time of the con- 
vocation of the conference of opposition parties and political forces in the Arab home- 
land, at which their will and the will of the Iraqi popular masses triumphed. 


These national parties, forces, and personalities are: 


The Arab Socialist Movement

They decided the following: 

1. They made a final decision to unite asa broad national front which will remain open 
to Islamic revolutionary forces, and to national forces and personalities hostile to 
imperialism, Zionism, reaction, and America. 

2. They agreed on a political program for the front, defined as follows: 

A. To bring down the fascist agent regime in Iraq. 


B. To set up a democratic, popular, and wionist regime. 


C. To allow the Iraqi people to freely choose the system they want once the fascist 
agent regime has been toppled. 


D. To grant genuine autonomy to Iraqi Kurdistan and to secure the rights of the Turkman 
and other minorities.

To achieve a united front within 1 month of the signing of this statement.
