LIBYA

U.S. Attack on Country via Chad Thought ‘Likely’ 
LD231842 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 23 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 23 Mar (JANA) — Reports attributed to official 
sources have been circulating in a number of Arab and African 
capitals about a likely U.S. attack on the Socialist People’s 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. These reports say the attack will take 
place ‘n coordination with Egypt, Sudan, the Zionist enemy, and 
France, and will begin with a military occupation of Chad 
followed by attacks on strategic targets in Libya with a view to 
destroying them. 


Some Arab and African countries have informed Libya of this 
through official secret channels. 


Diplomats of Multinational Force Countries Threatened 
LD240809 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 0215 GMT 24 Mar 84 


[Text] An organisation calling itself Islamic Jihad Organization 
{Munathamah al-Jihad al-Islami] has announced that it will 
avenge the thousands of Muslims killed in Lebanon at the hands 
of the American, British, French and Italian forces which were 
part of the so-called multinational force. Dispatches arriving 
yesterday reported that the embassies of the four countries in 
Egypt, Indonesia, and in some of the Arab and Islamic countries 
received messages about this matter through the post from the 
Islamic Jihad Organization. 


The messages said that the establishments and the interests of 
the four countries will be targets to be hit at any time and at 
place. Moreover, the members of the diplomatic missions and 
their families will be subjected to punishment. 


A number of banks and airline companies of the four countries 
in a number of Arab and Islamic countries received similar 
messages. The messages underlined the heinous massacres and 
the bloody crimes committed by the forces of the Western powers 
against the thousands of men, women, children, and the elderly 
in Lebanon.
