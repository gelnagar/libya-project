Summary (AI): This is a 1985 translated transcript of Qadhdhafi's press conference in Tripoli. He discusses the Arab-African Union with Morocco, the Sudanese uprising against Numayri, Chad, and Egypt. It has no specific local facts about Tripoli.
• The press conference was held on 10 April 1985 and was broadcast by Tripoli Voice of the Greater Arab Homeland.
• Qadhdhafi discussed economic integration between Morocco and Libya within the Arab-African Union.
• Qadhdhafi commented on the Sudanese popular revolution that overthrew Numayri and on Egypt's Mubarak.

((TEXT)) ((AL-QADHDHAFI)) BY ALL THE PARTIES, FRONTS, 
ORGANIZATIONS, AND DETACHMENTS STRUGGLING FOR THE UNITY AND FREEDOM 
OF THE ARAB NATION IN THE ARAB HOMELAND. 
((QUESTION)) THE ARAB-AFRICAN UNION HAS TAKEN (?MANY) STEPS 
IN THE ORGANIZATIONAL FIELD AND HAS SET UP BODIES. WE WOULD LIKE 
TO LOOK TO THE NEAR FUTURE AND ASK YOU TO TELL US HOW YOU SEE 
THE ECONOMIC INTEGRATION BETWEEN THE TWO PARTIES OF THE 
ARAB-AFRICAN UNION PARTICULARLY IN THE PRODUCTION SECTOR, A 
MATTER WHICH WILL STOP BOTH MOROCCO AND THE JAMAHIRIYAH RELYING 
ON THEIR REQUIREMENTS BEING MET FROM ABROAD. 
((AL-QADHDHAFL).) COOPERATION IS OUTSIDE THE SUBJECT OF THE 
PAN-ARAB COMMAND OF THE REVOLUTIONARY FORCES BUT NEVER MIND I 
WILL ANSWER IT. I HAVE GREAT HOPES THAT THIS UNION WILL PAVE THE 
WAY FOR A UNION WHICH WILL EMBRACE THE ENTIRE ARAB NATION. THIS 
UNION HAS REMOVED A BIG OBSTACLE. WE DID NOT BELIEVE THAT IT WAS 
POSSIBLE FOR ARAB REGIMES WHICH ARE DISSIMILAR TO UNITE. THIS 
WAS POSSIBLE. THE GREAT POTENTIALS OF BOTH MOROCCO AND LIBYA 
WHICH WILL BE UTILIZED WITHIN THIS UNIONIST FRAMEWORK WILL ENABLE 
BOTH COUNTRIES, GOD WILLING, TO MAKE A CONSIDERABLE LEAP ALONG 
THE PATH OF PROGRESS AND RATIONALIZE THEIR RESOURCES WHEN THIS 
UTILIZATION TAKES PLACE WITHIN THE UNIONIST FRAMEWORK BETWEEN THE 
TWO COUNTRIES. THE IMPORTANT BASIS OF THE UNIONIST ACTION, 
PARTICULARLY IN THE ECONOMIC ASPECT; WILL BE LAID DOWN AT THE 
ANTICIPATED MEETING OF THE PRESIDENTIAL COUNCIL OF THE UNION. 
((QUESTION)) IN THE VERSION TRANSMITTED BY JANA, IT APPEARS 
THAT IN YOUR SPEECH AT THE FINAL MEETING OF THE PAN-ARAB COMMAND 
OF THE REVOLUTIONARY FORCES YOU, THE BROTHER LEADER, CALLED ON 
THE ARAB NATION TO FORM SUICIDE UNITS AND ALSO CALLED ON THE ARAB 
NATION TO TRANSFORM ITSELF INTO SUICIDE DETACHMENTS. IS THIS 
CORRECT? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) IT LOOKS AS IF THERE IS SOME CONTRADICTION 
BETWEEN THE ARABIC VERSION AND THE TRANSLATION. MY CALL WAS THAT 
THE ARAB NATION'S STRUGGLE INSIDE THE ARAB HOMELAND IS LEGITIMATE 
FOR THE LIBERATION AND UNITY OF THE AAB NATION. 
((QUESTION)) ((WORDS INDISTINCT)) SIWAR AL-DHAHAB, IS IT NOT 
PREMATURE TO CALL WHAT HAS HAPPENED IN SUDAN A POPULAR REVOLUTION, 
ESPECIALLY SINCE WE KNOW THAT SIWAR AL-DHAHAB FOLLOWS THE SAME LINE 
AS THAT PURSUED BY NUMAYRI? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) ON THE CONTRARY: WHAT ENABLED SIWAR 
AL-DHAHAB TO ATTAIN POWER IS THE POPULAR REVOLUTION. ((SENTENCE 
INDISTINCT)) (2NUMAYRI) CARRIED OUT A MILITARY COUP D'ETAT AGAINST A 
REGIME WHICH WAS CONSIDERED A LIBERAL REGIME, AND ESTABLISHED 
MILITARY AUTHORITY OVER (?HIS PEOPLE). AS FOR THE MOVEMENT BY SIWAR 
AL-DHAHAB IT IS THE OPPOSITE -- IT HAS OVERTHROWN A MILITARY 
FASCIST REGIME TO ESTABLISH A POPULAR AUTHORITY. 
((QUESTION)) DID YOU LOOK FAVORABLY ON THE MEETING IN BAMAKO 
BETWEEN GOUKOUNI OUEDDEI AND HISSEIN HABRE AND DID YOU ADVISE 
GOUKOUNI OUEDDEI TO GO TO BAMAKO TO MEET HISSEIN HABRE? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) THE MEETING DID NOT TAKE PLACE AT ALL. THE 
PRESIDENT OF MALI MADE A LAUGHING STOCK OF HABRE. WE SUPPORT PEACE 
AND UNITY IN CHAD. WE SUPPORT SOLVING THE PROBLEM OF CHAD BY

((QUESTION)) ((WORDS INDISTINCT)) EXCUSE ME, I DO NOT BELIEVE 

THAT I GOT AN ANSWER TO THE QUESTION WHICH I ASKED EARLIER. I 

WOULD LIKE TO ASK THAT SAME QUESTION AGAIN IF I MAY. DURING THE 
COURSE OF YOUR OPENING REMARKS YOU SUGGESTED (( INTERRUPTED) ) I MAY 
HAVE BEEN ((WORD INDISTINCT)) WITH MY QUESTION PERHAPS I 

SHOULD ASK IT AGAIN. SINCE I DID NOT ASK IT CORRECTLY -- PERHAPS 

I ASKED IT INCORRECTLY. I BELIEVE, LEADER, THAT YOU WERE SAYING 
THAT THE NATIONAL COMMAND WOULD DEAL WITH COUNTRIES THAT INTERFERE 
IN THE ARAB NATION IN THEIR OWN HOMELAND. AND I ((WORD INDISTINCT) ) 
BY WHAT YOU MEAN BY THAT AND I WOULD LIKE YOU ((INTERRUPTED AGAIN) ) 
ARE YOU TALKING, LEADER, ABOUT EXPORTING FORCE TO THOSE NATIONS THAT 
YOU BELIEVE ((WORD INDISTINCT)) THE ARAB NATION? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) I BELIEVE THAT THE EXPLANATION I GAVE AT THE 
BEGINNING IS SUFFICIENT. 

((QUESTION)) BROTHER LEADER, THE EGYPTIAN REGIME, IMMEDIATELY 

AFTER THE DECLARATION OF THE POPULAR REVOLUTION IN SUDAN AND THE 
DOWNFALL OF THE LACKEY NUMAYRI, BEGAN TO MAKE EMPTY STATEMENTS AND 
DECLARE THAT IT HAS SOME RELATIONSHIP WITH THE NEW SITUATION IN THE 
SUDAN, ALTHOUGH WE KNOW THAT WHAT HAS HAPPENED IN THE SUDAN IS A 
POPULAR REVOLUTION WAGED BY THE MASSES ((INTERRUPTED)) ALSO THE 

HEAD OF THE EGYPTIAN REGIME ANNOUNCED WHAT HE TERMED AS THE THREAT 
OF THE JAMAHIRIYAH AND COL AL-QADHDHAFI PERSONALLY, ALTHOUGH HE -- 
THE AGENT HUSNI MUBARAK -- WILL NOT ALLOW INTERFERENCE IN SUDAN'S 
INTERNAL AFFAIRS, AND THE SECURITY OF SUDAN IS LINKED TO THE SECURITY 
OF EGYPT. WHAT IS YOUR INTERPRETATION OF THIS? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) HUSNI MUBARAK IS CO NTINUING ALONG THE SAME WRONG 
PATH PURSUED EARLIER BY SADAT AND FARUQ: TO LOOK DOWN ON THE 
SUDANESE AND REGARD THEM AS BELONGING TO THE EGYPTIANS -~- SLAVES FOR 
THE EGYPTIANS, AS IF KHARTOUM IS A CAPITAL CITY OF A 

GOVERNORATE WHICH IS ATTACHED TO CAIRO. THIS IS WHAT MADE THE 
SUDANESE PEOPLE REBEL. IT SUFFICES THAT THE STATEMENTS MADE BY 
HUSNI MUBARAK WERE ANSWERED BY THE SUDANESE PEOPLE THEMSELVES 

THE DAY BEFORE YESTERDAY WHEN ANGRY DEMONSTRATIONS MARCHED IN THE 
STREETS OF KHARTOUM AND OTHER SUDANESE TOWNS SHOUTING: HUSNI MUBARAK 
IS A TRAITOR| DOWN WITH AMERICA| THE DEMONSTRATIONS INSULTED HIM 
JUST AS THEY INSULTED AMERICA AND NUMAYRI. THIS MEANS THAT THE 
SUDANESE PEOPLE REGARD HUSNI MUBARAK A TRAITOR AND RESPONSIBLE FOR 
THE TRAGEDIES SUFFERED BY THE SUDANESE PEOPLE OWING TO THE 
CONNECTION BETWEEN THE SUDANESE AND THE LACKEY EGYPTIAN REGIME. 

THIS IS NOT AGAINST THE EGYPTIAN PEOPLE OF AGAINST THE UNITY BETWEEN 
EGYPT AND SUDAN.
