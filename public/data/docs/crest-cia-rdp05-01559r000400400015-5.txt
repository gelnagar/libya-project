• Qadhafi said the Secretary of the People's Committee for Communications in Benghazi should not claim ignorance of maritime, air, land transport or telecommunications matters, since he sat on the committee and attended all its meetings.

THE COMMITTEE FOR COMMUNICATIONS. FOR HE CAN TELL US EVERYTHING 
SINCE HE WAS PRESENT AT ALL THE MEETINGS AND PARTICIPATED 
EQUALLY IN THE ADOPTION OF DECISIONS.
