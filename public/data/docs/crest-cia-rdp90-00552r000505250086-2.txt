Summary (AI): A Human Events article argues that Jack Anderson's August 1981 columns on alleged CIA plots against Qaddafi, and U.S. press reports, may have provoked Libyan assassination squads targeting President Reagan. It covers Soviet media amplification of the claims. Nothing specific to Tripoli sites.
• Two Libyan aircraft were shot down by U.S. forces in August 1981, per the article.
• Anderson cited a top-secret draft CIA planning document dated May 9, 1981, 'Draft Covert Operations Planning Document Africa-Middle East'.
• Soviet TASS and Radio Moscow publicized the Anderson claims.
• Boland said on July 28 that the Executive Order prohibits U.S. intelligence assassinations.
• 1981-08-25: Jack Anderson column appeared in many U.S. newspapers claiming CIA plotters planned to poison Qaddafi's food or inject him with a poison dart
• 1981-12-06: Qaddafi, in an ABC interview, claimed the U.S. government was preparing to assassinate him and poison his food
