LIBYA

JANA: Brazilian Parliamentarians on U.S. Presence 
LDOS51933 Tripoli JANA in English 1822 GMT 5 May 83 


[Text] Brasilia, Rhyh 23, May 5, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — A number of Brazil’s members of parliament have 
expressed astonishment at the presence of U.S. military planes 
in the country, coinciding with the detention of the Libyan planes 
which were heading for Nicaragua. 


They asked the chairman of the parliamentary Foreign Affairs 
Committee to clarify the information given by the government 
on the Libyan planes question, for the information did not reveal 
that an American Hercules military plane was parking near the 
Libyan planes with 13 CIA agents aboard it. 


The parliamentarians expressed displeasure with the govern- 
ment's stand on the issue. They [words indistinct] to a deliberate 
attempt to divert attention from the presence of the U.S. planes. 
This has further convinced them that the U.S. was applying 
pressure on the government copcerning the Libyan planes, which 
were carrying aid to the Nicaraguan people.
