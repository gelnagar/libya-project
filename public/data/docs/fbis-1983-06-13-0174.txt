Al-Qadhdhafi Sends Message to Saudi King Fahd 
LD110556 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 2315 GMT 10 Jun 83 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution, yesterday sent a cable 
to King Fahd ibn 'Abd al-’Aziz, king of the Kingdom of Saudi 
Arabia on the occasion of leaving Saudi territory, the text of 
which is as follows: 


Brother Fahd ibn ‘Abd ai-’Aziz, king of the Kingdom of Saudi 
Arabia. While we leave our second homeland, I express to you 
great appreciation and gratitude for the fraternal welcome and 
hospitality accorded us by you and your brothers. 


Our meeting these days comes at a crucial time in the history of 
the Arab nation and in extremely critical circumstances when 
the existence of all of us is exposed to [words indistinct} the 
Zionist train of death which is driven by American energy has 
begun to threaten our Arab homeland from the ocean to the Gulf. 


I express full satisfaction with the results of the important talks 
which we held and which lay a sound foundation for (? a unified) 
Arab action to confront the enemy. 


In conclusion, I repeat appreciation and wish the Arab people in 
the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia continued progress and prosperity. 
Forward, and the struggle continues for unity and liberation. 
Your brother, Colonel Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi.

revolution has returned to the Jamahiriyah after concluding his

Ivory Coast Call to Reagan Termed ‘State of War’ 
LD111104 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1850 GMT 10 Jun 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 10 Jun (JANA) — Ivory Coast President Felix 
Houphouet-Boigny made a statement to The Washington Post 
today in which he called on the Reagan administration to con- 
front the Jamahiriyah’s policy in Africa, the policy that stems 
from the heart of the OAU’s principles and objectives, alleging 
that Libya’s adoption of this policy is causing the spread of 
instability. 


The Ivory Coast president called on African and Arab govern- 
ment to put a stop to this policy. He said that this will definitely 
be achieved if the United States and the Western powers support 
the confrontation of Libya. 


President Houphouet-Boigny said officiaily that his government 
and some African states have done all they were able to stop 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi from assuming the chairmanship of the 
OAU. 


He added: Al-Qadhdhafi backs extremist governments, therefore 
the United States and friendly states should move with whatever 
means they have to confront this. He also said that during his 
discussion with President Reagan he proposed that the United 
States and the Western powers should apply diplomatic and 
financial pressure on Libya instead of mounting secret military 
operations against the Libyan leader as in the past.
