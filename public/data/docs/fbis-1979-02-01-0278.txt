LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI, PDRY'S ISMA'IL DISCUSS RELATIONS 


LD311046 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1000 GMT 31 Jan 79 LD 


[Text] Last night a round of talks was held between the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah and the PDRY under the chairmanship of brother Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi and 
brother ‘Abd al-Fattah Isma'il at the People's Palace in Tripoli. The talks dealt with 
a review of bilateral relations and the strengthening of cooperation between the 
Jamahiriyah and the PDRY and also the conditions in the Arab homeland in particular and 
in the world in general. A technical committee has been set up to discuss conperation 
in various spheres between the two sister regions.

MINISTER SAYS LIBYA HELPS ALL REVOLUTIONS 


1D311745 Kuwait AR-RA'Y AL-'AMM in Arabic 30 Jan 79 p 18 LD 


['Abd al-Hamid 'Abd al-'Attar report on interview with Libyan Information and Culture 
Minister Muhammad Abu al-Qasim az-Zuwayy in Kuwait--date not given] 


[Text] Libyan Secretary of Information Muhammad Abu al-Qasim az-Zuwayy has praised 
Kuwaiti-Libyan relations, saying that they stand on solid ground and are based on similar 
backgrounds. 


He said that Kuwait and Libya went through difficult times before the emergence of oil 
in that both peoples belong to one and the same terrain, namely the desert. The secre- 
tary said that the foreign policies of the State of Kuwait and the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah are quite similar in their support for the Palestinian people's 
struggle and the peoples! struggle for freedom, independence and sovereignty. The 
secretary of information emumerated the points of similarity in the policy of Kuwait and 
his country toward the major powezs and the Third World countries. 


In reply to a question by AR-RA'Y AL-'AMM regarding Libya's relations with the Arab 
countries, he said that Libya's relations with most Arab countries are excellent. 

But our relations with Egypt and Morocco remain a strumbling block to Arab solidarity. 

Our relations with Egypt will remain as they are until As-Sadat's initiative is finished 
once and for all and until the Arab nation's view regarding so-called Israel is one again-- 
one and the same, Racist countries, including Israel, mist disappear. Israel is 
established on aggression and the oppression of the Palestinian people. 


The Libyan secretary of information recalled Libya's intensive efforts to prevent the 
Egyptian president from completing his initiative. But, he said, the Egyptian president 
contimied to insist and so the division of the Arab nation became more acute and Israel 
became more intransigent and insistent on pursuing its expansionist and racist attitudes 
toward the Arab world. 


Muhammad az-Zuwayy then spoke about Libya's relations with Morocco, the second country 
he considers to be a stumbling block to Arab solidarity. He said: Our differences with 
Morocco stem from its policy toward the Saharan people--a policy which is contrary to 
the principle of the people's right to self-determination. Mauritania and Morocco have 
shared the Western Sahara between them regardless of the Saharan people's will. 


The Libyan information secretary said that his country will contimue to pursue its well- 
known policy, which is based on the need for the Saharan people to obtain the right to 
self-determination, 


Replying to a question regarding reports that Libya exports revolutions and arms to many 
troublespots in the world, Muhammad az-Zuwway said: "We are proud to be so accused. We 
assert to the whole world that we provide material, moral and political support to 

every liberation revolution in the world, but we do not interfere in other countries! 
internal affairs. Libya has helped and is contimuing to help Muslim rebels in the Philip- 
pines, the people of Zimbabwe and the revolution in Iran, But I would like to make it 
clear that the present sweeping revolution in Iran against the regime cannot possibly 

be entirely dependent on foreign support. If we said it was, we would be denying the 
Iranian people's struggle and their blood which has been shed in all parts of Iran."

The Libyan information secretary said that Al-Qadhdhafi was the first to predict the 
outbreak of the Iranian revolution, The Iranian revolution is one of the world's 
strongest and deepest revolutions. It is even more so than the Libyan revolution itself, 


Referring to the claim that the Iranian revolution involves a threat of a comminist 
advance, Muhammad az-Zuwayy said that this revolution is free of any currents foreign 

to Islam. He said that the slogans raised by the rebels are quite clear, although there 
might be certain elements trying to infiltrate the revolution. But there is no fear for 
this revolution, Muhammad az-Zuwayy expressed the hope that the Iranian people will 
achieve their goal of liberating themselves from what they have been suffering for a long 
time, 


Speaking about the present Arab situation and its effects on the future of the mumber 
one Arab problem--the Palestine problem--Az-Zuwayy said that the present Arab situation 
calls for prolonged and thorough consideration. He said; We mist strengthen our insis- 
tence and struggle to free the Arab nation from Zionism, which is oppressin us, 

But we mist not confuse Israel as a state and Judaism as a religion. Judaism is a 
heavenly religion just like Islam and Christianity. The Arab people believe in coex- 
istence with the Jews but not with Zionism because Zionism is racist and is against 
religions. 


The Libyan information secretary said: This generation to which we belong may not be 
able to achieve the goal of liberating the Palestinian Arab territories, but this should 
be no means prevent us from working sincerely together in order to lay the foundation 
for the coming generation. History proves that carrying on the struggle from one 
generation to the next is a natural thing. What is unnatural is that we should 

lose patience and despair and completely surrender, as the Egyptian president has done, 


Muhammad az-Zuwayy asserted that with their great potential the Arab people can do a 
great deal for the sake of the Palestinian peoblem if they purge their ranks of the 
elements hampering their struggle. 


Asked about the Libyan Jamahiryah's relations with the two superpowers, the Libyan infor- 
mation secretary said: Our relations with the United States depend entirely on this 
superpower's relations with Israel. This is the reason why our relations often seem 
gloomy. However, ve believe that contact with the American people must contime and be 
strengthened so that through such contact we can change its views toward racist states 

in the world, including Israel. 


Regarding Libya's relations with the Soviet Union, Az-Zuwayy said they are strong because 
the Soviet Union is still giving support to the Arab people, particularly to the Pales- 
tinian people, For this reason we reiterate here what the late President Jamal 'Abd 
an-Nasir said: "We are antagonistic toward whoever professes enmity toward us and 
befriend whoever befriends us."
