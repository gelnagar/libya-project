Summary (AI): A June 1974 CIA cable (COMINT, classified) reports that Libyan leader Gadhafi held talks in Tripoli with Fatah leader Yasir Arafat and PFLP head Ahmed Jibril, with Libya providing substantial aid to the Palestinian fedayeen. The subject is foreign policy about the Palestinian cause, not Tripoli sites or local repression.
• Gadhafi believes the PFLP-GC deserves increased Libyan aid, while he strongly opposes negotiations with Israel.
• Jibril reportedly sought assurances of continued Libyan support for cross-border raids; Arafat wanted Libya to moderate its opposition to his efforts to send a Palestinian delegation to the Geneva peace talks.
• The cable states there is no evidence Libya agreed to step up its support to Jibril's group.
• 1974-06-20: Gadhafi held talks in Tripoli with Fatah leader Yasir Arafat and met separately with Ahmed Jibril, head of the PFLP-GC, per the cable referencing a 20 June 1974 conference in Tripoli.
