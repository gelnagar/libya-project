[Excerpt] Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Sa'ud al-Faysal arrived in Tripoli at noon today 
on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. He was welcomed at the Tripoli international airport

ALGERIAN, LIBYAN, PLO LEADERS REPORTEDLY TO MEET 
JN211233 Doha QNA in Arabic 1210 GMT 21 Dec 79 JN 


[Text] Damascus, 21 December--The QNA correspondent in Damascus has learned from 
informed sources that the mediation recently undertaken by Algeria and Syria to put an 
end to the Libyan-Palestinian disagreement has led to positive results in the interest 
of the Palestinian cause and Arab solidarity. 


He has also learned that a tripartite summit meeting will be held in Algiers at the end 
of next week between Algerian President Chadli Bendjedid, Libyan leader Mu'ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi and PLO Executive Committee chairman Yasir 'Arafat., The informed sources 

say that during this meeting a discussion will be held on ways to restore calm to 
Libyan-Palestinian relations as was the case before their aggravation at the outset of 
this month,

Jibril Arrival Statement 
LD250956 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 25 Dec 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--Brother Ahmad Jibril, general secretary of the Popular Front for the 
Liberation of Palestine-General Command, arrived in Tripoli last night for a visit to 
Libya. The official responsible for Palestinian affairs of the Arab Liaison Office 
welcomed Jibril at the airport, 


Ahmad Jibril made a statement to a JANA correspondent in which he praised the positions 
of the Great 1 September Revolution on the struggle of the Palestinian people to regain 
their usurped land, He said that the 1 September Revolution, under the leadership of 
the brother colonel constituted a hard obstacle in the face of the plotters against the 
Arab cause. 


Jibril added in his statement that all Arab peoples from the Atlantic to the Arabian Gulf 
are partners in the destiny of our nation and that the size of the participation of the 
national and patriotic regions in this revolution should be more general and more compre- 
hensive in order to face the plots being hatched by Arab reaction and imperialism 

against the Arab people, 


Brother Jibril stressed that the Arab struggle is the only path to liberate the usurped 
land.

JALLUD ADDRESSES EUROPEAN REVOLUTIONARY COMMITTEES MEETING 
LD261436 Tripoli JANA in English 1407 GMT 26 Dec 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Dec 26, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud 
yesterday evening attended the first conference of revolutionary committees of Europe. 
The conference had been inaugurated earlier in the morning in the Nasr ad-Din Gummi Hall 
in Tripoli. 


Staff Major Jallud delivered a speech during his attendance in which he stressed the 
importance of the conference. He said not only was it important for the advancement of 
scientific education but also that the conference acted as a means of sharply defining 
the functions of revolutionary forces. He said the revolution is no mere joy party 
put a serious full-time undertaking. 


He added that the Great 1 September Revolution had demolished the myth of omipotence 
surrounding the great powers. It had decided to make itself a force to be reckoned 
with, Staff Major Jallud added: ‘We will do away with mterialist philosophies, 
because peoples are not measured by their material capacities." He also said that the 
1969 revolution had been embarked upon as a means for achieving the people's revolution. 
This, in turn, had resulted in the establishment of people's authority which was 
acknowledged by the Libyan Arab people in Sabhah, 


He likened the revolution to a surgical operation of society which only the most ardent 
and faithful could undertake. 


He said the revolution is being constantly escalated and in need of ardent believers. 
He quoted Koranic verse in which God has said no matter how small the forces of goodness 
are, they will finally prevail even over the overwhelming forces of evil.

He stressed the necessity of guidance in preparing revolutionary forces, He said that in 
the early march of Islam, only a few people were able to change the course of history in 
Asia and Africa because they had associated the creed of Islam to life. Islam is a 
constant struggle for freedom and a way of life. So, he added, is the revolution, 


Staff Major Jallud said the true revolutionary is one whose initial struggle commences 
with himself. He who conquers himself can later convince others. The revolution 

depends on honesty wherever man prevails. He said in the battles awaiting us, only 
those who are true believers can remain steadfast. It is therefore for this reason, he 
added, that we should prepare ourselves as revolutionaries to carry out our responsibili- 
ties.
