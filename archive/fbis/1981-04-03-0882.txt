LIBYA

CUBAN CONSTRUCTION MINISTER ARRIVES FOR TALKS 


FL311206 Havana Domestic Service in Spanish 1030 GMT 31 Mar 81 


[Text] Cuba's minister in charge of construction abroad, Levi Farah, has arrived in 
the Libyan capital to begin talks with local officials on bilateral cooperation in this 
field. Levi Farah arrived from Angola, where he exchanged views with construction 
officials there to strengthen Cuban participation in the construction work being done in 
that country.
