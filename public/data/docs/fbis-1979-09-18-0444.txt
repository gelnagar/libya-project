STATEMENT ISSUED ON SARKIS MESSAGE TO AL-QADHDHAFI 
LD142140 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1615 GMT 14 Sep 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 14 September, JANA--The Foreign Secretariat has issued a statement 
which says that brother Muammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great First of 

September Revolution, has received a message from Lebanese President Ilyas Sarkis. The 
statement says that the Jamahiriyah agrees with Lebanon on the need for an urgent 

Arab summit to discuss the issue of Lebanon from all aspects, beginning with its 
distant and near causes and ending with the Jsraeli policy, in order to draw up a 
comprehensive Arab plan and define the role of each Arab state in a joint Arab battle, 


The statement adds that the Jamahiriyah appreciates the role of Lebanon and the dangers 
surrounding it, sees Lebanon as having borne more than any other Arab country despite 
the weakness of its capabilities, and believes that Lebanon should not be left to 
carry alone the burden of the military battle against the enemy,

COMMUNIQUE ISSUED ON IRANIAN DELEGATION!S VISIT 
LD141728 Tripoli JANA in English 1631 GMT 14 Sep 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, September 14 (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--A communique on the visit of the 
delegation of the Iranian Islamic Republic Party to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah was issued in Tripoli yesterday. 


The communique said: "Following an invitation by the Foreign Liaison Bureau at the General 
Secretariat of the General People's Congress and within the framework of continuous consul- 
tation between the Great lst of September Revolution and the Iranian Islamic revolution, a 
delegation from the Iranian Islamic Republic Party visited the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah and attended celebrations of the 10th anniversary of the Great lst of September 
Revolution," 


"The delegation was received by Colonel Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the revolution, 
Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud, Muhammad az-Zuwayy, assistant secretary general of the 
General People's Congress and head of the Foreign Liaison Bureau." 


The communique said that during those meetings, the delegation conducted lengthy and deep 
negotiations during which the spirit of brotherhood, and full understanding has prevailed. 


The communique added that negotiations included various issues, The foremost one was the 
complete liberation of the Palestinian soil and all Islamic lands, particularly holy 
Jerusalem, and the return of the Palestinian people to their homeland and the establishment 
of their independent and legal authority. and to spread its national sovereignty over the 
whole of Palestine. 


The two sides agreed that a complete Islamic unity is a necessity and it is the only way 
of liberation and dignity for all Muslims, 


The communiqus added that human and material efforts of all Muslims, in addition to their 
Islamic civilization, should be employed to serve human liberty everywhere on earth and 
to guarantee the world's peace, security and happiness, 


The two sides agreed on exchanging experiences, visits and people!s delegations as well as 
the formation of a joint committee between the Islamic Republic Party and the General 
Secretariat of the General People's Congress to coordinate masses action between the two 
brotherly peoples through holding its meetings regularly in the two countries, 


The Islamic delegation expressed its great admiration for the huge military parade on the 

day of celebration of the tenth anniversary of the Great First of September Revolution, 

It affirmed that the military power possessed by the Jamahiriyah is in fact a power to the 
Islamic nation, the Third World and the peoples who struggle for their freedom and independence. 


The communique also praised the agricultural progress as well as the great progress in 
housing, educational and health fields achieved by the Great First of September Revolution,

It also praised the direct people's democratic experience of the Jamahiriyah people 
in which they preach [to] the world's nations to achieve true democracy and social 
justice, 


The Libyan Arab side praised the Islamic revolution which broke out in Iran under 

the leadership of the great struggler Imam Ayatollah Khomeyni, as it is considered 

a true people's Islamic revolution in this age, and which forms, together with the 
Great First of September Revolution, a leading start for an Islamic revival consisting

Congress to visit Iran.

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES BLACK AMERICAN DELEGATION 
LD161632 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1525 GMT 16 Sep 79 LD 


(Text] Tripoli, 16 September--During his meeting this morning with members of the 
black American delegation, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi reiterated the support of the 
Great First of September Revolution for the honorable struggle of blacks to end the 
racial discrimination being practiced against them and to attain freedom and equality 
with other human beings. 


The members of the popular black American delegation told the brother colonel that they 
feel proud because of the cultural role the Great First of September Revolution has 
been playing and that they will work to entrench the historic ties of struggle between 
the revolution and blacks, 


They also expressed their belief in the human concepts contained in the Green Book 
and stated that they support these concepts with all their strength. 


The delegation praised the major changes that have taken place on the material and 
moral levels and affirmed that they will convey to the 30 million American blacks 

the concepts of the Great First of September Revolution which support freedom and the 
struggle against racial discrimination everywhere. The delegation presented the 
brother colonel with the medal of Martin Luther King, the struggler for the freedom 
of blacks.
