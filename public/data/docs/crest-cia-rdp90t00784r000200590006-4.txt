Summary (AI): A February 1983 NPIC briefing note reports SA-2 surface-to-air missile equipment being loaded onto a Libyan El Temsah-class roll-on/roll-off ship at Tripoli port, with the final destination unknown. It also lists earlier Libyan RO/RO transshipments to Ethiopia, Algeria, the Polisario, the Congo and the Central African Republic.
• Ship type: Libyan El Temsah class roll-on/roll-off (RO/RO) ship.
• At least five SA-2 missile transporters placed on the RO/RO deck; three SA-2 launchers on the quay.
• Final destination of the equipment is unknown.
• Earlier Libyan RO/RO transshipments: Ethiopia, Algeria, through Algeria to the Polisario, through the Congo to the Central African Republic, and within Libya.
• Coordinates given: 32-54-25N 013-11-15E.
• 1983-02: SA-2 surface-to-air missile equipment being loaded on a Libyan El Temsah class RO/RO ship; at least five SA-2 missile transporters on the deck and three SA-2 launchers on the quay.
