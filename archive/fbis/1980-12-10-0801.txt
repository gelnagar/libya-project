LIBYA

U.S. SAID TO HAVE INFLUENCED LIBERIAN DECISION 


LD051538 Tripoli JANA in English 1450 GMT 5 Dec 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 5 Dec (JANA)--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the great 
Al-Fatah revolution, received an important delegation from Liberia headed by its foreign 
minister and including the ministers of planning and state affairs, which arrived in 

the Jamahiriyah to express apologies on postponing the visit of the Liberian president 
to the Jamahiriyah which was due on 30 November last. 


The delegation briefed the leader on the circumstances lived by Liberia in the last 
few days which caused the postponing of the visit. The delegation delivered a written 
message to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi from the Liberian president. It reads as follows: 


"My dear friend and brother, I find myself unfortunately obliged to advise you that I 
suddenly found myself in a position which does not allow me to visit your sisterly 
country which was due on 30 November last. I would like to put to you the reason be- 
hind the cancellation of the visit. Unexpected obstacles in our economic situation 
have affected our oil imports that forces to postpone this visit but will send [passage 
as received] a special delegation headed by Mr Gabriel Matthews, the foreign minister, 
on a good will visit to brief you on the reality of the situation, hoping, dear brother, 
that you will receive him and give him your full confidence. I also hope that a date 
for another friendly visit will be fixed. The said delegation will discuss with the 
popular bodies in the Jamahiryah means of enhancing the close cooperation between our 
countries and peoples to consolidate our sacred African causes. While I repeat my 
apologies, I hope, dear friend, that you will accept my warmest respects and apprecia- 
tion. The struggle (?continues for) the people's interests". 


V. 10 Dec 80 a NORTH AFRICA 


"The Voice of America" has on 2 December announced that the U.S. under secretary 

for African affairs who was on a visit to Monrovia has said that U.S. pressures 

were the reason behind postponing the visit of the Liberian president to the 
Jamahiriyah, thus exposing his country's hostile intentions towards Africa and the 
peoples of the world. And in the same time, in his letter to the leader, the Liberian 
president exposed the falsehood of U.S. media and answered the colonialist intervention 
in Africa. 


AT-TURAYKI MEETS WITH NIGERIA'S SHAGARI 9 DEC 


LD101028 Tripoli JANA in English 0947 GMT 10 Dec 80 


[Text] Tripoli 10 Dec (JANA)--President Shehu Shagari of Nigerian Republic received 
yesterday Dr. ‘Ali ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, the foreign secretary who is currently 
visiting Nigeria. The foreign secretary conveyed, during this meeting to the Nigerian 
president, the due attention being realised by the leader of the revolution Colonel 
Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to support the efforts of the two countries for achieving peace 
and stability in Chad. Dr. at-Turayki confirmed the efforts being exerted by the 
Jamahiriyah to stand with the Chadian people affirming upon the necessity for pursuing 
the joint efforts to achieve stability and peace in Chad. 


President Shagari commended Jamahiryah's efforts to accomplish peace and security 

in this African country, confirming Nigeria's continuous support to the legitimate 
government in Chad and the cooperation with the Jamahiriyah to achieve peace in this 
country. He referred those chaos in the country will allow the colonialists to 
re-interfere in African continent. 


Meanwhile the foreign secretary met yesterday with Nigerian foreign minister, where 
the two sides discussed the present issues in African continent and imperialist moves 
to destabilize the area. The two sides also discussed matters related to bilateral 
relations and mutual joint causes. 


KURDS CABLE SUPPORT FOR AL-QADHDHAFI STANDS 


LD100010 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 9 Dec 80 


[Text] The Kurdish masses in various localities have paid a tribute to the stand 

of the brother leader of the revolution in support of the causes of justice, right and 
peace. In cables they have sent to the leader they hav tressed that his explanations 
regarding the Kurdish question and the establishment of a Kurdish state on Kurdistan 
territory had a great effect on the Kurdish Muslim people who have endured centuries 
of tyranny, oppression and fanaticism. The Kurds and Arabs exiled from Iraq who 

have joined the Kurdish Is'amic Army in their cables to the brother leader emphasized 
that the Kurdish people who have suffered from repression, deprivation and 

suppression of freedom see in the explanations of the leader on the Kurdish question 

a direct and decisive reply to the attempts to obliterate the history and struggle of 
the Kurdish Muslim people. 


Kurds in Turkey who joined the Kurdish Islamic Army also stressed that the explanations 
of the leader of the revolution show full understanding of the realities which are 

being experienced by the Kurdish people and their just cause. The Kurds saluted the 
heroic stance of the leader of the great 1 September revolution who is never too 
frightened to speak the truth. 


Kurds belonging to the Kurdish Islamic Army declared their readiness to stand on the 
side of the Libyan Arab people in fighting the agent and reactionary forces in the 
Arab region and in defending the achievements of the great 1 September revolution.
