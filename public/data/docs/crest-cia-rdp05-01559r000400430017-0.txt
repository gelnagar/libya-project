1 Feb 88 

[Text] Abu Dhabi, | Feb (MENA)}—Soviet Ambassador 
to Cairo Gennadiy Kirillovich Zhuravlev has attacked 
Israeli Prime Minister Yitzhaq Shamir’s stand on the 
international peace conference, describing it as intransi- 
gent and noting that both Egypt and Jordan have explic- 
itly declared their rejection of the autonomy for which 
Shamir is calling. 

The Soviet ambassador has affirmed that President 
Husni Mubarak’s initiative seeks to resolve the Palestin- 
ian issue within the framework of an international con- 
ference, a step the Soviet Union supports. 

In a statement to the Abu Dhabi-based newspaper AL- 
ITTIHAD, published today, the Soviet diplomat said 
that his country has always backed proposals for a 
peaceful settlement in the Middle East. He noted that 
current events in the occupied Arab territories necessi- 
tate a speedy, peaceful settlement in the region.

NC311603 Cairo MENA in Arabic 1450 GMT 
31 Jan 88 

[Text] Cairo, 31 Jan (MENA}—The workers of Egypt 
and Albania have declared their full support for the 
struggle of the Palestinian people under the leadership of 
the PLO, their sole legitimate representative. 

In a joint communique issued today at the end of a visit 
by an Albanian trade union delegation led by Sotir 
Kocallari, the two countries’ workers called on Iraq and 
Iran to immediately halt the war between them and solve 
their problems by peaceful means. 

The communique, which was signed on behalf of Egypt 
by Ahmad Ahmad al-‘Amani, chairman of the General 
Federation of Labor Unions, underlined the two parties’ 
solidarity with the Arab peoples that are struggling in 
defense of freedom and independence. The two parties 
condemned all racist practices by the racist regime in 
South Africa and declared their support for the Namibia 
people’s struggle. 

The communique noted that the two parties agreed to 
bolster and strengthen the cordial relations between 
them, to cooperate in fields of mutual interest, and to 
exchange expertise, information, delegations, and visits. 

The Albanian delegation, which left for Athens today, 
arrived in Cairo on 21 January. This is the first visit to 
Egypt by an Albanian trade union delegation in 20 years. 
During the visit, the delegation met with Consultative

land, which is free from imperialist hegemony, to hold 
the first international conference of the American Indian 
nation. It is being held to liberate that nation. This

the unity of the people who are fighting for freedom. It 
stresses that freedom is a common cause for all the 
masses. We would also like to point out now that the 
so-called free world is merely a myth, a lie, and a perfidy. 

The representatives of the American Indian nation rep- 
resent all Indian tribes; and behind them, the black 
nation. They have gathered here in order to speak out 
about their sacred and just cause. The free world is being

What the American Indian nation is subjected to and

The ugly racial segregation, under which blacks are living

of peace.

but because Allah created them with a black color, and 
because the Yankee invaders hunted and captured their 
fathers and forefathers in their original homeland, the 
African Continent, and brought them like animals to
