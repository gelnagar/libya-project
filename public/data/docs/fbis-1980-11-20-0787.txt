LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI_ SCORES AS-SADAT, U.S. AGGRESSION 


LD200954 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0245 GMT 20 Nov 80 


[Excerpts] The brother leader last night met the masses of the popular conferences through 
TV and radio and the Voice of Arab Homeland to explain the agendas of the popular conferences 


in their third session of 1980.

He explained that the Libyans must realize the aggressions confronting them when dis- 
cussing the agenda of their popular conferences, particularly when they come to discuss 
military issues and resources issues, pointing out that the conflict in the world is 
now taking place over such wealth. 


The brother leader referred to the aggressive military provocations along our borders, 
stressing that there is no escape from general mobilization to confront these provocations 
which might at any moment turn into flagrant aggressive action aimed at our wealth 

and freedom. Moreover, he emphasized that no one can represent another in defending, 
confronting and defeating aggression. 


The brother leader stressed that the Libyans must prepare the strength that will enable 
them to defeat the enemy which is now carrying out military exercises in Egypt in pre- 
paration for an attack on Arab oil, pointing out that the true conflict which is now 
taking place in the world is the conflict over oil. 


The brother leader said that greediness, viciousness and insolence cannot be confronted 
except by force with which to strike against colonialism. He emphasized that our 
military preparedness is not to launch the war today but the war of tomorrow when it is 
imposed on us, because we have no aggressive intention to go outside our borders, but 
we must be prepared to defend ourselves. 


The brother leader stressed the need to train women in the use of weapons and prepare 

for the coming confrontation in the general mobilization. He said that the militarization 
of the educational institutions does not mean a deprivation of education and learning, 
emphasizing the fact that there is no interruption of education at all; education must 
continue in the schools which are militarized in accordance with their laid down curriculum. 


The brother leader dealt with the point of avoiding confrontation with the Egyptian Army. 
He explained that if As-Sadat and America impose war on us, then we must confront them. 
But on our part we are not advocates of war with Egypt nor of a confrontation with the 
Egyptian Army. He said that if As-Sadat attacks us then we are prepared for this, but 
there is no reason fur soldiers to face one another with guns. He said As-Sadat is 
carrying out continuous provocations and that the Libyan people will at some time reach 
a point where they can no longer accept such provocations or defiance even if it is 
America who is defying them. He added that the maneuvers which the American forces are 
carrying out along the Libyan borders are the highest degree of defiance, affirming that 
we should show the Egyptian Army that we have no confrontation with it but we have 
confrontation with As-Sadat and America. 


The leader emphasized that what is being said about Egypt also applies to another 
neighboring Arab state, namely there is no call for placing our army along the border 
with Tunisia or the borders of Egypt because we not not confronting the Tunisian people 
nor the Egyptian people; moreover, we are not confronting the Tunisian Army nor the 
Egyptian Army. 


With regard to our relations with the outside world, the brother leader underlined the 
need to define our cooperation with countries by taking into consideration that some of 
them are brothers, and others are friends and allies, particularly when fixing the prices 
of oil and marketing it.

The brother leader spoke about the backing of external information. He pointed out that 
Europe and America are (?fighting) the Libyans with newspaper articles, radios, films, 
plays and stories, so much so that they depict the Libyan people as a terrorist, bar- 
baric and savage people threatening peace in the world maintaining that they had killed 
[name indistinct] and Musa as-Sadr and all those who had died. He added that the 
American and Zionist intelligence and the Egyptian Government and the reactionary 
governments publish newspapers in Arabic, English, French and Italian to attack the 
Libyan people. The brother leader explained that if the Libyan people wished to defend 
themselves and fight on these fronts, then let them fight on these fronts as long as the 
fighting is on paper which is better than fighting with heads and blood. 


The leader then explained the Jamahiriyah's backing of the liberation movements in the 
world, pointing out that the struggle for freedom is a legitimate struggle that needs to 
be backed and supported (?pointing to) the enactment of a law in this conne: tion that 
would bind us to the backing of liberation movements. 


With regard to the determination of foreign policy, the leader called for the need to 
enlighten the masses on the question of the continental shelf and urged that the case of 
the continental shelf with Malta be submitted to the International Court. Moreover, 
international agreements must also be explained to the masses to endorse tliem, as well 
as the treaty to defense, friendship and alliance with the Transitional National Govern- 
ment in Chad. 


He pointed to the Jamahiryah's demands for compensation for damages caused to the Libyan 
people as a result of the aftermath of World War II. He said that they will be discussed 
within the articles of foreign policy. 


OFFICIAL RECEIVES CREDENTIALS OF CSSR_ AMBASSADOR 


LD161846 Tripoli JANA in English 1631 GMT 13 Nov 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 Nov (JANA)--The secretary of the Foreign Liaison Bureau at the General 
People's Congress, Mr. Ahmad Shahati, this morning received the credentials of the new 
Czechoslovakian ambassador to the Jamahiriyah. Mr. Shahati said in a speech welcoming 

the new ambassador that it is important that relations of friendship are developed be- 
tween the Libyan Arab and Czechoslovak people. 


He said the Libyan Arab people are now in full control of authority, wealth and arms and 

is working for the establishment of friendship and cooperation with all justice and peace- 
loving peoples. He said such action will forthwith be more positive after the recent 
transformation of Libyan embassies abroad into people's bureaus run by people's committees. 


The new Czechoslovak ambassador on his part lauded existing relationship of friendship 
and cooperation between the Libyan Arab and Czechoslovak peoples and said he will work 
for the further development of bilateral cooperations.
