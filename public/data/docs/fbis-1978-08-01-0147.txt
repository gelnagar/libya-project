LIBYA

PDRY FINANCE MINISTER ARRIVES WITH MESSAGE FOR AL-QADHDHAFI 
LD312230Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 31 Jul 78 LD 


(Summary] Fadl Muhsin ‘Abdallah, PDRY minister of finance, arrived in Tripoli this 
evening on a few days visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


"Brother Fadl Muhsin ‘Abdallah made a statement to the JANA correspondent in which he 
expressed his pleasure at visiting his second homeland, the Jamahiriyah. He said: I 

am conveying the greetings of brother 'Ali Nasir Muhammad, chairman of the PDRY 
Presidential Council and Council of Ministers, to brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the 
leader of the 1 September revolution and the heroic Libyan Arab people." 


JANA EDITOR COMMENTS ON STATEMENTS BY MUBARAK 
LD311120Y Tripoli JANA in English 0944 GMT 31 Jul 78 LD 
[Text] Tripoli, July 31, JANA--JANA's political editor has commented on the declarations 


which were made recently by the Egyptian vice president, Husni Mubarak, in Tehran in which 
he openly accused (Israel) of being an obstacle on the road to peace. 


JTANA's political editor said: "Egypt is now sure through its vice president that (Israel) 
eel 


is not seeking peace but land", and in that connection we agree with him. He also said 
that Husni Mubarak's declarations affirmed what we used to maintain all along, that 
Israel) is an alien entity in the area and its existence is against peace and that it 


was founded by means of usurping land and the expulsion of the Arab people from 
this land, 


JANA's political editor further said that the Egyptian Government should adopt a positive 
stand after realizing that the Zionist entity was based on aggression, Egypt should also 
be aware that the solution is to re-evaluate Egyptian policy in order to regain its 
occupied territories by military force and not by direct negotiations.

AL-QADHDHAFI LECTURES STUDENTS, OFFTCERS IN MISRATAH 


LD271814Y Tripoli JANA in English 1513 GMT 27 Jul 78 LD 


{Excerpt] Misratah, July 27, JANA--Yesterday evening the supreme commander of the Libyan 
Arab Armed Forces, Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, met the students of the college in 
Misratah. The meeting was attended by Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, the general 
commander of the armed forces, the chief of staff of the air forces, the commander of 
the college and officers of the college. 


On his arrival at the college, the officers and students shouted: "Long live the revo- 
lution and long live its leader, Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi gave an ideological lecture in which he explained the passages 

in the Green Book and particularly the part about authority, wealth and arms being in 
the hands of the people. He also talked about the historical transformations which 
the Jamahiriyah was experiencing. Such a transformation, he said, was unique, because 
Libya was the first Jamahiriyah in history. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi explained the fundamental transformation achieved by the revolu- 
tion which would affect all fields, including the military establishment, because when 
the firearms, the arms of wealth and the arms of authority were possessed by a certain 
groups, it represented terrorism for the rest of society. 


He affirmed that defending the country was the responsibility of every citizen and it 
was not the responsibility of one group. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added that transforming the hired workers into partners, and 
putting the wealth and arms in the hands of the people was the real solution and would 
lead to the ideal society which humanity was seeking.
