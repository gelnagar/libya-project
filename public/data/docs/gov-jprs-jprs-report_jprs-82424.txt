[Text] A decree, No 46, by the commander-in-chief of the armed forces, on 
the terms under which Arab volunteers can serve in the Libyan armed forces. 


The commander-in-chief of the armed forces: After studying law No 40 of 
1974 pertaining to service in the armed forces, law No 35 of 1977 pertain- 
ing, to reorganization of the armed forces, and law No 5 of 1978 amending 
certain statutes of the military law; and following the historic announce- 
ment that Arab volunteers would be accepted in the Libyan Arab armed forces 
in order to mobilize Arab forces for the battle for peace and liberation, 
the following decree has been issued: 


Article l 

The Libyan Arab armed forces will accept as volunteers soldiers from Arab 
countries who have completed their service in their own countries in ac- 
cordance with the following conditions, and in compliance with the condi- 
tions of acceptance for military service as stipulated in law No 40 of 1974 
pertaining to service in the armed forces which do not contradict tie provi- 


sions of this decree: 


1. The prospective volunteer must be of Arab nationality and must not be 
married to a non-Arab woman. 


2. this service not have been terminated due to bad conduct or ill health. 
3. He must never have been convicted of a felony or breach of security. 


4. No more than 3 years must have elapsed since his discharge from service 
in his own country. 


5. He must be physically fit for work at the time he volunteers. 


6. His skills must be needed by the armed forces.

Prospective volunteers will submit their applications to the Libyan brother-

the prospective volunteers will be carefully examined. The extent to which
