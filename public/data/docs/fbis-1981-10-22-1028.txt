LIBYA

OFFICIALS DENY CALLING ARAB LEAGUE MEETING 


LD211920 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1848 GMT 21 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 Oct (JANA) -- A source at the People's Committee of the Foreign Liaison 
Bureau today made the following clarification: News agencies have carried a report which 
said that the Socialist Peoples Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has called an emergency Arab League 
meeting to discuss it's dispute with the Sudan. 


The People's Committee of the Foreign Liaison Bureau would like to reaffirm that Libya 
has not and will not ask for an emergency meeting of the Arab League Council to discuss 
the problem between the Jamahiriyah and Sudan. If there were a border problem, then this 
would be between Sudan and Chad, and the Socialist Peoples Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has 

no connection with such a problem. Any disputes or problems regarding the border between 
Chad and Sudan concern those states. 


However, if some people want to interpret the Sudanese-Chadian border problem as a 
confrontation between the Libyan and Sudanese forces then this is very remote from the 

truth as the Libyan forces in Chad, in accordance with agreements between the two countries, 
are there to maintain peace and order in this fraternal neighboring country. 


ARAB LEAGUE CHIEF KLIBI ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 


LD211434 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 21 Oct 81 


[Text] Chedli Klibi, secretary general of the Arab League, arrived in Tripoli this morning 
for a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. In a statement to the 

JANA correspondent Brother Klibi expressed pleasure at visiting the land of the great 
Fatih. He said the purpose of his visit to the Jamahiriyah is to discuss and review the 
current Arab situation and the delicate stage through which our Arab nation is going as 

it hopes for the desired glory of uniting its will and achieving glory, pride, progress 

and prosperity.

AMBASSADORS INFORMED OF NUMAYRI'S ‘THREATS’ 


African Ambassadors 
LD211954 Tripoli JANA in English 1702 GMT 21 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 Oct (JANA) -- The people's Committee of the Foreign Liaison Bureau 
yesterday evening summoned African ambassadors accredited to the Jamahiriyah and informed 
them about the threats issued by Sundan's ruler against the Jamahiriyah and their impli- 
cations of premeditated intentions of aggression on the people of the Jamahiriyah. ‘Abd 
al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the people's Committee of the Foreign Liaison Bureau, 
briefed the African ambassadors on the Jamahiriyah's stand vis-a-vis these threats and 
informed them that the Jamahiriyah was not planning to attack Sudan or Egypt. However, 
he said that faced with these statement threats [as received] by Sudan's ruler to take 
the battle into the Jamahiriyah and his open declaration to carry out terrorist opera- 
tions in Libya, the Jamahiriyah finds itself compelled to counter any act of aggression 
and deter any attempt of aggression against it from Sudan. 


Mr al-'Ubaydi said to the African ambassadors to the Jamahiriyah that the Jamahiriyah 
will confront any attempt aimed at undermining the freedom and security of the people 
and that the essence of our differences with the Sudanese regime is due to the regime's 
flagrant violation of the African summit resolution which stressed clearly the consoli- 
dation of the Chadian people's freedom. The Sudanese regime is continuously trying to 
undermine Chad's freedom. Numayri's regime has intentionally defied the Arab summit 
resolutions and aligned itself with the Americans and Camp David. Furthermore, he openly 
supports Hussein Habre's sabotage activities against Chad. He added that Numayri's 
regime ignored the African and Arab summit resolutions using a small pretext and argu- 
ment as a cover for his hostile moves against the Jamahiriyah. He is going ahead with 
nis challenge to the African countries and in trivialising their approach for consoli- 
dating peace and security in Chad. 


Mr al-'Ubaydi went on to say that the Jamahiriyah is committed to the African resolution, 
but at the same time (?it is) concerned about the security of Chad and considers it an 
African duty. 


He also briefed the ambassadors on the U.S. attempt to increase tension in the area and 
encourage Sudan to follow suit by convincing it of the so-called Libyan threats. Numayri, 
he said, has become the prisoner of his illusion. 


Asian Ambassadors 
LD212046 Tripoli JANA in English 1907 GMT 21 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 Oct (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY) -- The People's Committee of the Foreign 
Liaison Bureau this afternoon summoned the ambassadors of Asian countries accredited to 
the Jamahiriyah and briefed them on the threats issued by Sudan's ruler against the 
Jamahiriyah and the implications of premediated intentions of aggression on the people 

of the Jamahiriyah. 


The general secretary of the Liaison Affairs at the People's Foreign Liaison Bureau 
acquainted the ambassadors with the stand of the Jamahiriyah towards these threats and 
informed them that the Jamahiriyah had no intention of attacking Sudan or any other 
neighbouring country. He said that faced with these blatant threats which reached the 
stage of threatening assassination by Sudan's regime, the Jamahiriyah has no choice 
but to take decisive and deterrent measures against any attempted acts of terrorism 
the ruling regime in Sudan might carry out.

Latin American Ambassadors 
LD212044 Tripoli JANA in English 1911 GMT 21 Oct 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 Oct (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY) -- The Peoples Bureau of Foreign 

Liaison today summoned the Latin American ambassadors accredited to Jamahiriyah as part of 
acquainting brotherly countries with the aggressive path followed by Sudan's ruler towards 
Jamahiriyah, his flagrant threats to transfer the battle into Jamahiriyah and his declara- 
tion of carrying out terrorist operations against the Libyan Arab people. 


The general secretary of Liaison Affairs at the People's Bureau of Foreign Liaison explained 
Jamahiriyah's stance towards the Sudanese ruler's blatant threats against Jamahiriyah 

people and the American provocations aimed at heightening tension, inciting the Sudanese 
regime in this direction and making it live in a world of illusions regarding an alleged 
Libyan danger threatening Sudan's security. 


He said, "The Sudanese regime is still deliberately ignoring Arab and African decisions. 
It bases its claims on false pretexts to cover up its aggressive moves towards Jamahiriyah 
people at American imperialist prompting."
