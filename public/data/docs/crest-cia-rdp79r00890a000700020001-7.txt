Summary (AI): A February 1956 NSC briefing reports that the new Soviet ambassador in Tripoli offered Libya wheat, "unlimited" economic aid and arms without strings, and that Libya's prime minister Ben Halim hinted he might accept. It also notes that a transfer of the US 7th Air Force headquarters from Tripolitania was under review.
• Soviet offer included 40 to 50 thousand tons of wheat and 'unlimited' economic aid, including 'free arms'.
• Generalov reportedly approached the Libyan petroleum commissioner about Soviet oil concessions that might carry rights to airstrips and radio stations.
• Ben Halim denied that a Soviet oil concession request had been made.
• Transfer of the US 7th Air Force headquarters from Tripolitania was under review.
• 1956-01-06: Soviet Ambassador Generalov arrived in Tripoli.
• 1956-01-13: Generalov presented credentials to King Idriss and made a 'no strings' economic aid offer.
