LIBYA

JALLUD LECTURES TEACHERS ON REVOLUTION 
1D101536 Tripoli JANA in English 1450 GMT 10 Jul 79 D 
[Text] Tripoli, July 10, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY4-Staff-Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud 


said that the transformation cannot take place through emotional beliefs but through 
the full ideological beliefs in the dicta of the revolution, 


During the intellectual lecture given by Staff-Major Jallud to the members of the second 


constitutional session of the Jamahiriyah teachers, he said that the states that we have 
to pass through in order to secure the goals of the revolution at the local, Arab and 
Islamic levels, require an increase in the number of believers in the revolution and not 
mere reliance on the historic leadership but on the whole masses. 


Staff-Major Jallud pointed out that urban militarization is the only way to confirm the 
people!ts authority, achieve democracy and grant the people self-rule, 


He added that the historic leadership is aiming at achieving and consolidating the dicta 
"power, wealth and weapons in the people!s hand," 


He affirmed that once the masses are in such a position this would deprive fascism and 
dictatorship of any opportunities and confirm the power of the masses, 


Staff-Major 'Abd as-Salam, Jallud stressed that the task of the people's revolutionary 
forces is to assert and apply the revolutionary dicta, to overcome the old remnants 
and work for a continuous movement of the masses, 


He said: "The revolutionary vanguard achieves victories for the masses, as opposed to 
the parties vanguard whose victories are only beneficial to their own parties." 


He pointed out that the Great First of September Revolution seeks a renaissance of the 
Arab nation's civilisation and its acts for the triumph [words indistinct ].

He said that this noble aim requires the action of all the revolutionary forces and the 
believers in the unity of the Arab nation as a source of strength and a restoration of 
Islam's past glories, He said: "Our success depends on our ability to create cadres of 
believers which learn, read and argue about the revolution," 


Staff-Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud pointed out that the revolution should be carried 
out in practice in the teaching of subjects and that a unionist approach should be used 

in the teaching of history, geography and other subjects. He said: "We cannot create a 
generation of unionists without changing the present education syllabuses." 


He noted that the first step taken by (Israel) was to inform the defeated Egyptian 
president of the need to change the Egyptian education curricula so that anything touching 
upon the true aims and nature of (Israel) would be deleted, He added that America has 
presented the necessary funds to finance the operation whose real objective, he said, was 
to mislead the Egyptian new generation as to the nature of (Israeli) intentions, 


Staff-Major Jallud asserted the need to believe completely in the dicta of the revolution 
because they are meant for the happiness of man, for the attainment of his liberty, and 
the free provision of his needs. The revolutionary process is innate, he said. 


In conclusion Staff-Major Jallud explained that the construction of an ideology should 
begin with the teacher and pupil considering the fact that more than one third of the 
Jamahiriyah's population are students. He said: "Our educational views differ from the 
rest of the world as our revolution is distinguished by humanitarian principles." Staff- 
Major Jallud affirmed that the importance the revolution devotes to the creation of the 
ideologist is the correct way to build the personality of man, Man is considered to be 
the fulcrum of any progress and hence the central element in the process of progress and 
development, 


NICARAGUAN DEIEGATION ARRIVES, HOIDS TALKS 
1D101422 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 10 Jul 79 ID 


[Text ] The head of the Foreign Liaison Bureau received in his office in Tripoli this 
morning a delegation from the provisional government in Nicaragua. The recent develop- 
ments in Nicaragua and the victories scored by the Sandinista front against the fascist 
regime of Somoza were discussed during this meeting, The members of the delegation exposed 
the aggressive alliance between Somoza!ts facsist regime and the usurping enemy in Palestine. 
The delegation of the revolutionary government in Nicaragua, which arrived in Tripoli last 
night, is composed of an official of the Sandinista front for military affairs, a member 

of the consultative council and the front!s official for foreign relations. 


A delegation from the Foreign Liaison Bureau of the General People's Congress has left 
to take part in the conference of solidarity with the people of Nicaragua to be held in 
Caracas, the capital of Venezuela, the day after tomorrow, Thursday. A conference of 
solidarity with the people of Nicaragua and the liberation movements in Latin America 
was held in Benghazi in January.

AT-TALHI RECEIVES SAHARAN PRIME MINISTER LAMINE 
LD101404 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 10 Jul 79 LD 
[Text] Engineer Jadallah 'Azzur at-Talhi, secretary of the General People's Committee, 
received in his office this morning Mohamed Lamine, prime minister of the Saharan 
Democratic Arab Republic, who is on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah. The meeting was attended by the head of the Arab Liaison Bureau of the 
secretariat of the General People's Congress.
