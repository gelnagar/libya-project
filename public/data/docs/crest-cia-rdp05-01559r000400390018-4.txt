Summary (AI): A July 1982 US Embassy Rome cable summarizes a La Repubblica interview with Qadhafi. He denied supporting terrorism, warned the US against naval maneuvers in the Gulf of Sidra, said he would accept Israel only if a democratic Palestinian state existed, and blamed US pressure for blocking an Italy visit and hurting Libya's economy.
• Cable reference: Rome 16388, classified Confidential/Limited Official Use, sent 14 July 1982.
• Qadhafi said the US asked Italy not to receive him on an Italy visit; he said Libya was officially informed of this.
• Qadhafi said any naval maneuvers in the Gulf of Sidra would result in war.
• Qadhafi said there was no true revolutionary force in the Arab world except Libya, South Yemen, Polisario, and the progressive Lebanese and Palestinian left.
• Embassy comment: no moderation in tone compared with past interviews.
• 1982-07-14: La Repubblica publishes front-page interview with Colonel Qadhafi

SUBJECT: INTERVIEW WITH QADHAFI 

1. SUMMARY. THE LEFT OF CENTER ROME DAILY "LA 
REPUBBLICA" CARRIES A FRONT PAGE INTERVIEW WITH 
COLONEL QADHAFI IN ITS YULY 14 EDITION. HIGHLIGHTS 
OF THE INTERVIEW INCLUDE QADHAFI’S DENIAL THAT HE 
SUPPORTS INTERNATIONAL TERRORISM, HIS SEVERITY TOWARD 
NEARLY ALL ARAB GOVERNMENTS IN PLACE, AND HIS WARN- 
ING TO THE UNITED STATES CONCERNING FURTHER MANEU- 
VERS IN THE GULF OF SIDRA. QADHAFI EXPRESSES HIS 
WILLINGNESS TO ACCEPT ISRAEL AS SOON AS A DEMOCRATIC 
STATE RECOGNIZING PALESTINIAN RIGHTS EXISTS IN PALES- 
TINE. HE BLASTS THE U.S. FOR SABOTAGING HIS PRO- 
POSED VISIT TO ITALY AND ADMITS THE U.S. EMBARGO IS 
HURTING HIM ECONOMICALLY. IN "LA REPUBBLICA’S" VIEW, 
QADHAFI HAS TEMPERED SOMEWHAT HIS LANGUAGE 

TOWARD THE WEST. WE QUESTION THIS JUDGMENT. 

THE PAPER FURTHER EXPRESSES ITS OPPOSITION TO 

AN EMBARGO THAT WOULD TURN LIBYA INTO A-SUPER- 

POWER BATTLEGROUND. FOLLOWING IS A PRECIS OF 

KEY POINTS. END SUMMARY. 

2. LIBYAN-ITALIAN RELATIONS 

AS CONCERNS A POSSIBLE VISIT ON MY PART TO ITALY, 
THE UNITED STATES WAS RESPONSIBLE FOR SABOTAGING 
IT. IN FACT, "AMERICA FORMALLY ASKED ITALY NOT 

TO RECEIVE ME. WE HAVE BEEN OFFICIALLY INFORMED 
OF THIS." (NOTE: IN RESPONSE TO THE UOQURNALIST’S 
DIRECT QUESTION WHETHER. ITALY HAD INFORMED LIBYA

3. TERRORISM 

I HAVE NEVER SUPPORTED TERRORIST ORGANIZATIONS, 
BUT I DON’T DENY MY SUPPORT FOR REVOLUTIONARY 
MOVEMENTS. THE WEST IS BEST AOVISED TO IMPOSE 
ITS ECONOMIC AND MILITARY EMBARGOS AGAINST 
ISRAEL, "THE EVIL OF THE EARTH." 

4. ARAB GOVERNMENTS 

THERE IS NO TRUE REVOLUTIONARY FORCE IN THE ARAB 

“WORLD AT THIS TIME EXCEPT LIBYA, SOUTH YEMEN, 
POLISARIO, THE PROGRESSIVE LEBANESE AND THE 
PALESTINIAN LEFT. ALL THE OTHERS ARE REACTIONARY, 
HOSTILE TO LIBERTY AND THE RIGHTS OF MEN. 

S. LIBYAN-SOVIET RELATIONS 

THE SOVIETS HAVE BEEN LITTLE HELP TO LEBANON IN 
ITS HOUR OF NEED. CONFRONTED BY DIRECT ATTACK BY 
THE UNITED STATES AGAINST LIBYA, HOWEVER, THE 
SOVIET UNION WOULD NOT STAND IDLE. SOVIET A0- 
VISERS ARE IN LIBYA, BUT FOR THAT MATTER SO ARE 
AMERICAN CIVILIANS, WHO ARE IN FACT MORE NUMEROUS 
THAN THE SOVIETS. 

6. U.S.-LIBYAN RELATIONS 

NEITHER LIBYA NOR THE U.S. OCCUPY ANY PART OF 

ONE ANOTHER’S TERRITORY. THEREFORE, THERE IS NO REASON 
WHY WE CAN’T OPEN A DIALOGUE. THERE IS A STANDING 
WILLINGNESS ON MY PART TO MEET PRESIDENT REAGAN 

AND NEGOTIATE WITH THE U.S. NONETHELESS, ANY 

NAVAL MANEUVERS IN THE GULF OF SIORA WOULD RESULT 

IN WAR. LIBYA WILL DEFEND ITS TERRITORY. 

7. LIBYAN-ISRAELI RELATIONS 

SHOULD A TRULY DEMOCRATIC STATE COME INTO EXISTENCE 
IN PALESTINE, ONE IN WHICH JEWS AND PALESTINIANS 
LIVE TOGETHER, I WOULD BE PLEASED TO RECOGNIZE SUCH 
A STATE.

8. COMMENT 

"LA REPUBBLICA" EDITORIALIZES THAT THE TONE OF QADHAFI’S 
REMARKS IS SOMEWHAT MORE TEMPERED THAN IT HAS BEEN IN 
THE PAST, EXCEPT AS REGARDS ISRAEL. THE PAPER FURTHER 
EXPRESSES ITS OPPOSITION TO AN EMBARGO THAT WOULD 

TURN LI8YA INTO A FIGHTING GROUND BETWEEN THE TWO 
SUPERPOWERS. HOWEVER, WE FAIL TO OISCERN IN THE 
INTERVIEW ANY MODERATION IN QADHAFI’S TONE WHEN COM- 
PARED TO PAST INTERVIEWS THAT PERIODICALLY APPEAR 

IN THE ITALIAN PRESS.
