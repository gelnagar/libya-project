LIBYA

CRUDE OIL PRICE INCREASED $2,77 ABOVE OPEC CEILING 
NC151823 Paris AFP in English 1758 GMT 15 Oct 79 NC 


{[Excerpt] London, Oct 15, (AFP) --Libya has raised the price of its crude oil above 
the ceiling of 23.5 dollars a barrel agreed by the Organisation of Petroleum Exporting 
Countries (OPEC) in Geneva at the end of June, oil industry sources said today, 


They said Iran, despite denials in Tehran over the weekend, has also increased its 
export prices following the example set last week by Kuwait, another OPEC country, in 
the wake of a similar move by Mexico, which is not a member of the organisation, 


By raising its selling prices by 2,77 dollars a barrel across the board, Libya became 
the first of the 13 OPEC countries to break through the agreed OPEC ceiling, the sources 
said, They said some Western oil companies had been informed about the latest increases 
by Libya and Iran today, 


The best quality of Libyan crude has been selling at 23,5 dollars a barrel since July l, 
Under the latest adjustments, effective as from Oct 1, this quality will henceforth 
cost 26.27 dollars.
