JAMAICAN PRIME MINISTER ENDS VISIT TO LIBYA 
LD021541 Tripoli JANA in English 1444 GMT 2 Jun 80 
[Text] Tripoli, June 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Jamaican prime minister left Tripoli 
this morning at the end of an official visit to the Jamahiriyah during which he held 
talks on promoting bilateral relations. In a statement to JANA, he expressed his satis- 
faction with the results he achieved during his stay in the Jamahiriyah. He also praised 


the direct democracy practised in the Jamahiriyah which he witnessed during a meeting 
of Al-Andalus [as received] basic people's congress in Tripoli. 


Vv. 4 Jun 80 NORTH AFRICA 


LIBYAN, SOVIET AGRICULTURAL OFFICIALS HOLD TALKS 
LDO32044 Tripoli JANA in English 1740 GMT 3 Jun 80 


[Text] Tripoli, June 3, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The secretary of the general people's 
committee for land reclamation and agrarian reform held a meeting this morning with the 
Soviet deputy minister for agriculture, The meeting discussed the bilateral cooperation 

and dealt with the running of the joint committees in (the) agriculture field. The visiting 
Soviet deputy minister visited today Al-Fatah University where he praised the progress 
achieved by the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in various fields.
