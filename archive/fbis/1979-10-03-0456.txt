AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES BENGHAZI SYMPOSIUM ON 'GREEN BOOK! 
LD022052 Tripoli JANA in English 1754 GMT 2 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Oct 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The leader of the Great First of September 
Revolution Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that revolution which broke out all over 
the world, including those against feudalism and the [words indistinct] radical society, 

are all attempts by man to find solutions to his economic, political and social problems, In 
his speech in the second session of the International Symposium on the Green Book at Ghar 
Yunis University in Benghazi last night, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that the Green Book is the 
inevitable outcome, after the failure of past solutions, which led man to revolution, the 
{word indistinct] being (?the) last one. These great revolution experiments were tried by 
man to solve these problems for which he paid dearly in order to achieve progress and 
prosperity for humanity as a whole. 


Political Parties Ineffective 
LD022053 Tripoli JANA in English 1800 GMT 2 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] Benghazi, Oct 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu!'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of 
the Great First of September Revolution, affirmed that the parties are not capable of 
solving the problem of the struggle for power. Amr evidence to this is the current struggle 
for power in countries which have a party parliamentary system. 


The solution, he said, lies in abolishing all traditional ruling tools and handing authority 
over to the masses, 


In his speech at the International Symposium on the Green Book in Benghazi, he said that 
the masses are still confused but will be victorious in the end when the people!s authority 
is established and the ideal solution propagated by the Green Book, 


The leader wondered should the people be politically aware of this fact, why then couid 
they not rule themselves, and should the people be encapable of that, why would we take 
for granted that they are politically aware. On the one hand we say the masses are not 
politically aware and should be ruled and on the other we say that they are politically 
aware and should give their votes to a certain party. This in itself is deceit, which 
could only be accepted before the dicta "no democracy without people's congresses", 
"representation is fraud" and "authority of the people" had been revealed.

a formal solution for the political problem until the economic problem is solved. The 

workers cannot but agree with what the emyioyer proposes during the basic people's congress

Governments Cause Wars 
LD022055 Tripoli JANA in English 1814 GMT 2 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] Benghazi, Oct 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The leader of the Great First of September 
Revolution Colonel Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi explained that although the Jamahiriyah had neither 
a government nor a ruling system, it has an international character and direct relations 
with the peoples, 


He said: Then the causes of wars would disappear because the [words indistinct] wars to 
serve their personal goals. For example, an American President launches a war against 
Vietnam to promote his electoral campaign; another might impose a settlement in the Middle 
East to serve a similar end. 


Speaking last night during the Benghazi Green Book International Seminar, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi 
said that the Second World War was not due to hostilities between the German people and the 
peoples attacked by Hitler. He stressed that a real democracy is taking place in the 
Jamahiriyah because there can be no democracy without people's congresses, Thus the 

problems of democracy were all solved and there is no need to look for other alternatives.

FOREIGN SECRETARY MEETS WITH VARIOUS LEADERS AT UN 
LD022010 Tripoli JANA in English 1818 GMT 2 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] New York, Oct 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The foreign secretary met Dr, Ahmed 
Karim Jay, secretary general of the Islamic conference here yesterday. They discussed 
the French invasion of Central Africa and the oppression inflicted upon Muslims in the 
Philippines, as well as issues to be dealt with during the extraordinary meeting of 
Islamic countries! foreign ministers which will be held in New York on October 8th, 


Dr, Jay reiterated the Islamic conference's condemnation of the French intervention, 
describing it as a precedent threatening African peace and security, since it 
constitutes an interference in the affairs of an independent state, Dr. Jay said that 
the secretariat of the conference is still following the inflictions of Muslims in 

the Philippines, 


The foreign secretary met with Mr, Adam Kudjo, the administrative secretary general of 
the OAU, with whom he discussed the possibility of convening an extraordinary conference 
of African foreign ministers on the French invasion of Central Africa, 


Mr, Kudjo reassured the foreign secretary of the OAU's strong condemnation of the 
interference in the internal affairs of an OAU member state, He said that this invasion 
violates the organisation's principles and charter and that it is the right of the 
people in Central Africa to change and choose the regime they want, Other countries 
have no right to impose what they want on this people, 


The foreign secretary met with the Congolese minister of foreign affairs and cooperation, 
with whom he discussed the issues dealt with at the U.N. 34th session and the situation 
in Africa, 


The Congolese minister reaffirmed his country's strong condemnation of the French 
invasion of Central Africa, which constitutes an illegal interference in the affairs 
of an independent state, 


He said his country would not tolerate this action, which is unacceptable and a 
violation of the charters of the United Nations, the OAU and the nonaligned movement, 


The foreign secretary met with the head of the Maltese delegation to the U.N., 
discussing with him bilateral relations and the agenda of the U.N, 34th session, 


The Maltese official expressed his denunciation of any kind of foreign intervention 
in a U.N. member state,
