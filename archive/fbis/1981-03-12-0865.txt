LIBYA

U.S. FLEET MANEUVERS SAID TO REFLECT 'ANXIETY' 


LD111436 Tripoli Domestic Servicein Arabic 1230 GMT 11 Mar 81 


[Excerpt] In strategic places in the Mediterranean Sea and along areas facing the central 
part of Jamahiriyah, the U.S. 6th Fleet began naval and air maneuvers yesterday. This 
American move, if it indicates anything, is an indication that the achievements of the 

Arab people in the Jamahiriyah have began to cause real anxiety in American colonial 
quarters. This military move is resorted to from time to time to intimidate the peoples, 
threaten their freedom and achievements, prevent their growth and stifle their aspirations. 


U\S. REPORTEDLY GIVES ‘SPY AIRCRAFT' TO MOROCCO 


LD111356 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 11 Mar 81 


[Text] Informed diplomatic sources have mentioned that America has handed the Moroccan 
regime a spy aircraft to be used against the Polisario revolutionaries. This step comes 
within the framework of colonial alliance against the struggle of the people of the Sahara 
and to protect the Moroccan regime against the popular revolution. 


AL-QADHDHAFI STRESSES OPPOSITION TO ALL TERRORISM 


LD111634 Tripoli JANA English 1432 CMT 11 Mar 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 11 Mar (JANA)--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great Al- 
Fatah Revolution, affirmed that the Red Brigades and Baader Meinhof are two similar 
terrorist organizations under different names. 


V. 12 Mar 81 ) NORTH AFRICA 


In an interview on the Italian T.V., Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that the Basque organization 
calls for separating the region from Spain, a position which Libya does not support, but 
rather believes in the unity of the Spanish soil. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added that the Basque issue is a Spanish internal affair and Libya 
does not interfere in the internal affairs of other countries. He stressed Libya's 


strong opposition to all forms of terrorism. 


TURKISH DEPUTY PRIME MINISTER ENDS VISIT 11 MAR 


LD111433 Tripoli JANA in English 0821 GMT 11 Mar 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 11 Mar (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--Mr Turgut Ozal, the Turkish deputy prime 
minister and the state minister of economic affairs and the delegation accompanying him, 
yesterday left Tripoli international airport after a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah. The Turkish minister made a statement to JANA's correspondent praising 
the giant achievements made in the Jamahiriyah thanks to the Great Al-Fatah Revolution and 
he said that during his visit he held positive talks on bilateral relations and ways of 
developing them in various fields. 


The secretary of the General People's Committee of Economy and Mr Turgut Ozal, the Turkish 
deputy prime minister and the state minister of economic affairs, yesterday signed the 
minutes for bilateral cooperation. These minutes include cooperation inthe economic, 
industrial, agricultural, and commercial fields and the exchange of expertise. 


AL-OADHDHAFI MEETS NICARAGUANS; DOCUMENTS SIGNED 


LD112152 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 11 Mar 81 


[Text] The brother leader has received the Nicaraguan minister of foreign affairs and 
the minister of the International Reconstruction Fund, who are currently visiting the 
Jamahiriyah. 


The two ministers stressed the role of the Jamahiriyah in supporting the cause of freedom 
and instanding alongside the peoples struggling for freedom and deliverance from foreign 
domination. The two ministers expressed to the brother leader the appreciation of the 
people of Nicaragua for the stand of the Great 1 September Revolution in supporting their 
struggle for the sake of the triumph of their popular revolution, which overthrew the 
regime of the fascist clique who dominated and terrorized the people of Nicaragua. They 
affirmed that, since achieving freedom, their country now marches along the road of 
realizing the jamahiri system and that the Third World theory is now being carefully and 
profoundly taught in Nicaragua. 


The secretary of the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison and 
the Nicaraguan foreign minister this evening signed the protocol of an agreement for 
cooperation between the Jamahiriyah and the Republic of Nicaragua. They also signed an 
agreement for setting up a joint company between the Jamahiriyah and the Republic of 
Nicaragua. They also signed an agreement for setting up a joint company between the 
Jamahiriyah and Nicaragua to operate in the agricultural field.
