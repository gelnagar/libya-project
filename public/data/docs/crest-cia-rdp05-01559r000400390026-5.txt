JANA REPORT ON AL-QADHDHAFI 7 OCT SPEECH 

LD081048 Tripoli JANA in English 0805 GMT 8 Oct 82 

(Text] Tripoli, Dhulhijja 1, October 8, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Colonel Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great Al Fateh Revolution addressed the grand rally held last 
night in the village of Omar Mukhtar, at Al-Jabal al-Akhdar municipality in celebration of 
the 12th anniversary of the explusion of the remnants of Italian fascists. 

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi started his speech by greeting the father of martyrs the hero Omar 
Mukhtar who fought against the colonialists and mobilized the jihad movement throughout 
Libyan soil. 

He said,. "In every part of the free Jamahiriyah, we decided today to commemorate "Vengeance 
Day". And we come to this particular place as an expression of our gratitude to the 
historic role played by Omar Mukhtar in resisting colonialism. While celebrating 
"Vengeance Day", the day when we drove the last Italian occupationist fascists out of 
Libyan soil, we have to acknowledge the role of Omar Mukhtar who polarized the jihad 
movement all over Libyan soil and take him as a good example to follow not in command and 
leadership only, but also in the life of every citizen of this people and this nation". 

The leader said "When the death wagon faced Libyan territory exactly as it is now facing 
Arab territories, Omar Mukhtar left his position as Koranic teacher at Zawiya and did not 
act to become leader but acted out of a sense of patriotic duty by carrying a gun to 
confront the death wagon. 

Yet meanwhile, Idriss Senoussi escaped to Egypt when the death wagon arrived in the country, 
leaving the Libyans and proving his cowardice and utter despicability and non-adherence to 
Libyan soil". 

He added: "Libya gained victory, expelling the Italians and toppling Idriss Senoussi. 

The Libyan people were thus rendered free and became aware of the truth about Idriss Senoussi 
who ruled Libya and contributed to its backwardness. He oppressed our people, established 
dictatorship and oppressive police rule and allowed the establishment of British, American 
and French bases. During his rule, Italian colonialism established itself here, killing 

our grandfathers and fathers and destroying the country”. 

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added that the Senoussi family had sold Libya for the Italian lira 
and had not thought that this people will one day be a victor and will expose it to the 
world. 

' He added: "We are celebrating one of these occasions on which we expose those who deceived 
the Libyan people and imposed themselves as rulers on the Libyan people". 

"Omar Mukhtar did not impose himself on the Libyan people until his martyrdom. He did not 
claim leadership or presidency but carried the gun in face of the invaders and with him 
joined all the Libyans from all parts of the country". 

On the "Vengeance Day", the revolution's leader said: ''We have driven out over 20,000 
Italians occupying Libyan soil. 20,000 Italians are equal to the number of Israeli troops 
who occupied and stayed in Lebanon usurping the farms, stores, facilities and economy and 
meddling with Lebanon's affairs appointing the president, the prime minister and the 
commanders of the Lebanese Army, as the Italians did in Libya before the revolution".

He said: "Up till 1970, agricultural land, stores and cars were taken over by the Italians. 

He said Italy then nominated members of Parliament and paid vast sums of money so that they 
would protect its interests. He added that the Council of Ministers issued laws partial to 
the Italian coloniser who destroyed agricultural land, transforming it to buildings, and 
smuggling money under cover of these Cabinet-sanctioned laws. 

The revolution's leader showed disdain for the remnants of the bygone regime who have fled 
abroad and who talk in the name of Libya and the so-called Libyan front and Libyan opposition. 

He said: "These stray dogs composed of ex-premiers who are traitors and hirelings... what 
did they do when they earlier headed governments and when Libya was an Italian and English 
colony?... They were dogs of the Italians and not one of them handed in his resignation 

because of Italy's colonisation of Libya. All the laws they had signed were in the interests 
of the Italian colonisers..." 

\ 
He added: "Did any one of them resign when the five American and British bases were 
established on Libyan soil? When the likes of those despicable hireling escapees talk 
abroad in the name of Libya, they demean the Libyan people because they sold out Libya. 

The Libyan people absolutely reject the idea of being ruled again, after having realised 
their own authority and established the Jamahiri (mass) system, by hireling remnant escapees 
who had conspired against them and owed their allegiance to a corrupt regime that had served 
colonialism and ignored the Libyan people..." 

He said: "There shall be no mercy for the agents of America. The escaped hirelings, 
enemies of the Libyan people, shall not escape from this people. 

He said: "It will be crazy for the base leaders and governments of the eradicated era who 
were installed under the banner of the U.S. bases and Italian colonization and were bred on 
humiliation treason, treachery and denial of Libyan soil, to have ambitions of ruling Libya 
once again after the Libyan people established their people's authority and announced the 
first Jamahiriyah in history where the revolutionary committees movement was born from among 
the ranks of the masses to become an international force. He added that those base people 
are way behind the era of the masses because they are from the monarchy which still believes 
that the world is still passing through the era of kings. 

He said: "The current monarchies in Jordan, Morocco and Saudi Arabia are museums. Upon 
entering them, we feel we are entering a strange museum of the Middle Ages... 

Look at how the king in the Saudi Kingdom cuts off the heads of the people at the market- 
place? -Look at how the people are treated like sheep and ruled by method of succession..." 

The leader pointed to the lavish life-style of the Saudi rulers and said that instead of 
these. life-styles being imported by ships to them from abroad, they should build factories 
and farms. 

The leader strongly attacked the followers of the eradicated regime. He said: "The Libyan 
people did not ask for them when the revolution erupted. Theydid not go out to the streets 
to support them. They supported the revolution instead... 

Those who spoke about Libya and its people in order to acquire money from their American 
and Israeli masters and their other masters in the Egyptian, Saudi and Moroccan regimes, 
have acquired money from regimes opposed to the Libyan people, revolution, Jamahiriyah, 
socialism, unity and liberation". 

He continued: "These regimes are agents of America and Israel, enemies of the Arab nation 
that are creating the death wagon to trample all Arab countries, each and every individual 
one of them. It has already done so in Palestine, Lebanon and Egypt, and is now on its 
way to other Arab countries...

He said: "These regimes wish to see Libya, its people, revolution, industry and agriculture

He added: "If the United States chooses to antagonise Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and his 
colleagues, we shall challenge America anywhere. We shall go to the Bahamas Islands and 
meet with America there. We shall emerge there if the person being sought is Al-Qadhdhafi. 
Yet those who are being sought are the Libyan people and the Arab nation. They are seeking 
to destroy the Arab nation through a worldwide U.S. colonialist strategy. Libya is only 
one these positions that is resisting colonialism." 

Col. Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi also pointed in his speech to those who committed crimes against 
the Libyan people, stole their money and then escaped abroad. He said: "The Libyan people 
shall recover their fortune from these people and try them either in absentia or otherwise. 

The door to repentance is, however, open to those who are now aware that they were living 

in an era of exploitation, oppression and corruption and are now living in the era of soc- 
jalism and social justice and that wealth belongs equally to the Libyan people." He said 

the door to repentance is open to those who know that a new era has begun in Libya. 

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added that the door to repentance is wide open to all those who want 
to return to Libya to live honourably as the rest of the Libyans. Those who link their 
fate with the reactionaries, Israelis and Americans should face that fate on the hands of 
Libyan Arab people. He added that the files of the despicable stooges and the dirty 
skeletons who used to lick the shoes of the Italians and America's dogs [words indistinct] 
base, those who believe that America is able to protect them should know that America will 
not protect them as it could not protect As-Sadat on October 6 when he was killed before 
the eyes of America and under the surveillance of its satellites, CIA and the Rapid 
Deployment Force. 

Moreover, America could not protect Haile Selassie from the wrath of the Ethiopian people 
nor could it protect the shah of Iran who America considered as cornerstone of its policies 
in the Middle East. The same can be said about Lon Nol in Cambodia and his colleagues in 
South Vietnam. 

Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi announced that the Jamahiriyah is fighting America in Somalia 
and is putting all its capabilities in the fierce fight between the Democratic Salvation 
Front and America. He said America wants to change this Moslem African Arab country into 
an American base from which it launches wars on the Arab countries, strike a siege on the 
Arab countries and controls Bab el Mandab [spelling of place as received} and the Red Sea 
so that it threatens Mecca and Medina and brings the Arab Peninsula oil under the mercy of 
America, its is when Somalia becomes a zone of American colonialism and influence. 

He added that the Somali people led by the Democratic Salvation Front is now waging a war 
against the Americans in Somalia towards liberating Somalia before the U.S. bases are 
established. Colonel al-Qadhdhafi went on that the Jamahiriyah is fighting with all its 
capabilities alongside the Democratic Salvation Front against America which backs Siad 
Barre. 

He said: "The Libyan Arab people has the right to self-defence and to declare war on 
its enemies, inside and outside the country. 

The Libyan people is determined to liquidate the enemies of the revolution inside and 
outside the country to consolidate its freedom and defend its gains and achievements.

He stressed that the awakening of the peoples has denied America many of its vital and

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi reminded that traitor As-Sadat had sold the Egypt of Abdul an-Nasir

The revolution's leader said the most massive marine and air lift in the 20th century was

He expressed hope that the day will come when the Americans will be expelled from Egypt

perform this holy duty". : 

He noted that Egypt has not been an independent country. Since 1973, Egypt has been

to the Jamahiriyah. 

The revolution's leader said there is no escape from the reality of total mobilisation to 
face the death wagon which has become a reality to the Arabs who at Fes recognised the 
Zionist enemy. 

He continued: This enemy has, however, refused to recognise the Arabs... This means that 
the Arab nation from the Atlantic Ocean to the is open for seizure by the Israelis. 
They are intent on devastating the Arab nation and are arming themselves for that purpose..." 

He added that the Zionist enemy's plan calls for the emigration of 12 million Jews for 

occupation and settlement in Arab lands in the Nile, Euphrates, Tigris, Jordan, Litani, 
and Yarmuk with Jerusalem as the capital of their state...

He added that the "Egyptian regime is extremely weak and incapable of answering the

Col. al-Qadhdhafi praised the heroic struggle waged by the Palestinian Resistance against

In the speech delivered yesterday evening to mark the 12th anniversary of the expulsion 
of remnants of Italian fascism, Col. al-Qadhdhafi also praised the heroic resistance 
of the socialist union and "Al-Murabitun" forces. 

He said: "The Palestinian Resistance was the land mine that was hindering the enemy... 
They wanted to contain and destroy it so that they would advance unopposed, to occupy 
and destroy other Arab countries... The Arabs are supposed to thank the Palestinians

The leader asked: "Who was defending Beirut...? It was the Palestinians. The day the

the Palestinians left and the massacres that took place there after the evacuation of 

the Palestinian Resistance..." 

The leader continued, "Didn't the Israelis say that they did not want to enter Beirut 
for humanitarian reasons... The truth was not because of humanitarian reasons but for 
Palestinian reasons. They were afraid of the Palestinian Resistance in West Beirut.... 

When the Resistance left, they entered Beirut with their tanks, destroyed and massacred

He said: "We cannot accept this fate at all... this situation is totally unacceptable 
and we as men and women should fight it." 

Col. al-Qadhdhafi said the Lebanese groups who participated in the fighting in Lebanon 
against the invasion forces were the "Al-Murabitun" and the socialist union... 

Col. al-Qadhdhafi said Libyan men and women should fight, and that jihad (holy war) 
is an obligation on every one of us, because the Israelis are surely coming and there 

is no way out except waging a holy war. 

He said these matters will be debated by the people's congresses because the death 
wagon theatens all Arabs who can only resist it or be trampled by it.
