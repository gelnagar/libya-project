LIBYA

NICARAGUAN FOREIGN MINISTER ARRIVES 9 APR 


LD091828 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1805 GMT 9 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 9 Apr (JANA) -- Nicaraguan Foreign Minister Miguel d'Escoto arrived in 
Tripoli this evening for a visit to the jamahiriyah, during which he will hold contacts and 
talks on the strengthening of cooperation and relations between the two friendly countries. 
In a statement to a JANA correspondent, Miguel d'Escoto stressed the solidarity of the 
Nicaraguan people with the Arab Libyan people in their confrontation with American terror 
plots. He also said the true solution to the Palestinian issue would be to return the 
Palestinian people to the occupied lands. He praised the strong relations linking the 

PLO, the sole representative of the Palestinian people, with the Nicaraguan people. 


Al-'Ubaydi Meeting 
LD101342 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 10 Apr 82 
[Text] Brother 'Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the people's committee of the Foreign 
Liaison Bureau, this morning met with Nicaraguan Froeign Minister Miguel d'Escoto, who 
is currently visiting the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. During this meeting, 
they discussed relations between the two friendly countries and means for consolidating 


them and exchanged views on various international issues. 


HUNGARIAN CHIEF OF STAFF ARRIVES 10 APR 


LD101340 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 10 Apr 82 


[Text] Hungarian Deputy Defense Minister and Chief of Staff Istvan Olah arrived in Tripoli 
this morning. He and his accompanying delegation were welcomed at Tripoli international 
airport by Brig Mustafa al-Kharrubi, chief of staff of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, 

chiefs of staff and officers of the armed forces. 


Talks Held 
LD110916 Tripoli JANA in English 0848 GMT 11 Apr 82 
[Text] Tripoli, 11 Apr (JANA) -- Talks were carried out yesterday evening between the 
Libyan Arab and Hungarian sides. The meeting was headed on the Libyan side by Brigadier 
Mustafa al-Kharrubi, the armed forces chief of staff, and on the Hungarian side by Istvan 
Olah, Central Committee member, deputy defense minister and armed forces chief of staff. 


Jabir Reception 


LD111744 Tripoli JANA in English 1653 GMT 11 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 11 Apr (JANA) -- The General Commander of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces 
Brigadier Abu Bakr Unis Jabir this morning received Istvan Olah, Central Committee member 
and Hungary's deputy defense minister and chief of general staff. The meeting was also 
attended by the Libyan Arab Chief of Staff Brigadier Mustafa al-Kharrubi.
