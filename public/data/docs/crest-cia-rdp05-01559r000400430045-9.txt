((EXCERPTS ((AL-QADHDHAFI CONTINUES) ) THE ALGERIAN 
CHARTER IS A PLAN OF ACTION WHICH WE MUST IMPLEMENT AS OF 
TODAY. HENCEFORTH IT MUST BE IMPLEMENTED IN LIBYA AND 
ALGERIA BECAUSE IT IS A POLITICAL AND ECONOMIC PLAN. AS 
FOR THE GREEN BOOK, IT IS A THEORY; A THEORY WHICH PERHAPS 
MAY ONLY BE IMPLEMENTED AFTER 100 YEARS. IT IS A THEORY 
WHICH ((WORDS INDISTINCT)). IT IS A THEORY WHICH ADVOCATES 
THE CREATION OF A NEW ERA, THE JAMAHIRI ERA. POWER WOULD 
BELONG TO THE PEOPLE; WEALTH WOULD BELONG TO THE PEOPLE; 
EVERYTHING WOULD BELONG TO THE PEOPLE. GENERALLY SPEAKING, 
THE GREEN BOOK IN ITS THREE CHAPTERS IS CLEAR. IT IS 
SOMETHING ELSE THAT THE GREEN BOOK WAS NEVER INTENDED TO BE 
IMPLEMENTED. IT MUST BE READ ONLY. TO BE READ IN LIBYA AND 
ALGERIA AND MAYBE ANOTHER GENERATION WILL IMPLEMENT IT. 

EVEN IN LIBYA WE HAVE MADE GREAT STRIDES WITHOUT BEING ABLE 
TO IMPLEMENT THE GREEN BOOK. IT IS (?NOT) EVEN NECESSARY TO 
IMPLEMENT IT IN LIBYA ((WORD INDISTINCT)). GENERALLY 
SPEAKING, IT IS A THEORY AND IDEOLOGY. 

AS FOR THE CHARTER, WE CAN IMPLEMENT IT AS OF TODAY. 
THERE IS NO OBJECTION TO IT. I WOULD SAY: LET THE ALGERIAN 
CHARTER BE CIRCULATED TO ALL LIBRARIES AND CULTURAL CENTERS 
IN LIBYA, IN ALL POPULAR CIRCLES ((WORD INDISTINCT)). IT IS 
A BRILLIANT PLAN OF ACTION. ((REPETITIVE PASSAGE OMITTED) ) 

((QUESTION) ) BROTHER COLONEL, WE EXPECET A MAGHREBAN 
SUMMIT BEFORE THE END OF THIS YEAR. WHAT CAN WE EXPECT 
FROM THIS SUMMIT FROM THE ASPECT OF THE NEW IMPETUS IT WILL 
GIVE? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) IT IS NOT IMPORTANT WHETHER IT IS HELD 
OR NOT; WHAT IS IMPORTANT IS THAT THE WORKING COMMITTEES 
ACCOMPLISH THEIR WORK. THE SUMMIT GAVE THE GREEN LIGHT AND 
ENTRUSTED THESE COMMITTEES TO DRAW UP VISUALIZATIONS FOR THE 
ESTABLISHMENT OF MAGHREBAN UNITY. IF THEY ACCOMPLISH 
ANYTHING THAT REQUIRES THAT WE MEET, WE WILL MEET. THERE IS 
NO LONGER A PROBLEM FOR US TO MEET. SHOULD WE ONE DAY 
CONTACT EACH OTHER, THEN THERE IS NO BIG PROBLEM; WE CAN 
‘MEET AT THE HOME OF ANYONE OF US. WE ARE BROTHERS AND WE 
ARE CLOSE TO EACH OTHER, AND THANK GOD, THERE ARE NO 
PROBLEMS. WE TALK ON THE PHONE CONTINUALLY AMONG OURSELVES 
AND WE CAN MEET AT ANY TIME -- WE, THE FIVE. IN OTHER 
WORDS, IT DOES NOT INTEREST US IF A SUMMIT IS HELD OR NOT; 
WHAT CONCERNS US IS THAT THE COMMITTEES ACCOMPLISH THEIR 
WORK. THIS IS WHAT MATTERS. 

((QUESTION) ) THE RELATIONS OF COOPERATION AND 
COMPLEMENTARITY BETWEEN THE TWO FRATERNAL PEOPLE, THE 
ALGERIAN AND LIBYAN PEOPLES, HAVE CONTINUED TO DEVELOP IN 
LINE WITH THEIR EXPECTATIONS AND IN AN EMBODIMENT OF THE 
WILL OF THE ALGERIAN AND THE LIBYAN REVOLUTIONS, ESPECIALLY 
SINCE THE HISTORIC MEETING BETWEEN YOU AND PRESIDENT CHADLI 
BENDJEDID AT AIN AMENAS IN 1986. CAN YOU, BROTHER COLONEL, 
EVALUATE THE STEPS THAT HAVE BEEN ACCOMPLISHED AND THE SCOPE 
FOR THE ACHIEVEMENT OF THE STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES OF THE ARAB

NATION? 
((AL-QADHDHAFI)) IN GENERAL, AS A RESULT OF THE MEETINGS, 
THE DIALOGUE, AND THE SERIOUS INTERACTIONS BETWEEN THE 
ALGERIAN AND THE LIBYAN REVOLUTIONS WE ARE NOW ABOUT TO 
PRESENT A DRAFT FOR UNITY AND THE CREATION OF ONE STATE 
BETWEEN LIBYA AND ALGERIA. THIS IS RECORDED IN THE DRAFT OF 
THE UNIONIST CONSTITUTION FOR A ONE STATE -- AND TUNISIA 
MIGHT JOIN US IN SETTING UP THIS STATE, BECAUSE OF THE 
EVENTS OF 7 NOVEMBER. TUNISIA WAS BACKWARD. AND HAS NOW 
BECOME PROGRESSIVE, AND CONSEQUENTLY, THERE IS NO OBSTACLE, 
NO REASON, TO MAKE TUNISIA STAND ALOOF FROM JOINING IN THE 
FOUNDING OF THE UNIONIST STATE. 

THE THREE OF US MIGHT SET UP A UNIONIST STATE AND BE AT 
THE SAME TIME IN A MAGHREBAN UNITY THAT BRINGS TOGETHER 
MOROCCO AND MAURITANIA, AND SEE THE FATE OF THE SAHARA 
((AL-QADHDHAFI LAUGHS)): SHALL WE SAY IT IS A STATE, PART OF 
THE REGION? 

THUS THIS STRUGGLE HAS INDEED INFLUENCED WHAT WE SEE NOW: 
THE BROTHERS IN ALGERIA INFORMED US THAT ON 1 SEPTEMBER 1989 
A CELEBRATION WILL BE HELD ON THE ARTIFICIAL BORDERS CREATED 
OF COURSE BY THE FRENCH, THE ENGLISH, AND THE ITALIANS, TO 
ABOLISH THE BORDERS AND THE NEED FOR PASSPORTS, AND TO MOVE 
WITH AN IDENTITY CARD. THIS GAVE US GREAT PLEASURE, AND WE 
CONSIDER THIS A SALUTE AND A PRESENT FROM THE 
REVOLUTIONARIES OF ALGERIA TO THE AL-FATIH ANNIVERSARY. 
((PASSAGE OMITTED: POINTING OUT THAT THE ALGERIAN, 
PALESTINIAN, AND LIBYAN REVOLUTIONS ALL BEGAN ON THE FIRST 
DAY OF THE MONTH)
