Summary (AI): A July 1980 cable from the U.S. Embassy in Vienna reports on a Gaddafi speech of June 11, 1980 (marking the evacuation of American bases in Libya), in which he called for the overthrow of several Arab governments and for the killing of Libyans who work with American authorities. It also mentions a Libyan People's Bureau note and reports of Syrian commando groups abroad. Nothing here is specific to Tripoli sites.
• Speech called for the Sadat government in Egypt to be overthrown
• Speech said committees had carried out judgments against enemies in London, Italy and Beirut
• Gaddafi told the Revolutionary Committees to stop activities and not carry out death sentences until reconfirmed by Revolutionary Courts, except against Libyans working with American authorities
• Embassy received the speech copy from the Libyan People's Bureau; Austria had not formally recognised the bureau
• 1980-06-11: Gaddafi speech on the occasion of the evacuation of American bases in Libya, calling for the overthrow of the Sadat government and other regimes, and for killing of Libyans working with American authorities
• 1980-06-11: Demonstration in front of the Libyan People's Bureau in London, attacked in a Libyan People's Bureau note from Vienna
