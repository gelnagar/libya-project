LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS MESSAGE TO ALGERIAN PRESIDENT 


LD032332 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 3 Apr 82 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has sent a cable to Algerian President 
Chadli Bendjedid on the occasion of the convening of the first joint union meeting 
between the general popular committee in the jamahiriyah and members of the Algerian 
Government. Here is the text of the cable: 


To the dear brother, President Chadli Bendjedid; 


Algeria's playing host to the first unified session of the executive machinery in the 
two fraternal regions and your decision to personally open and oversee the session 
delights us. We salute you and your patriotic government and the great Algerian 
people. We join hands to gain strength for completing the building of unity, the 
cornerstone of which is being laid today on Algerian soil, the land of the 1 million 
martyrs -- which is worthy of this historic act. This act is considered an earnest 
and guaranteed turning point toward the great objective and the great hope, namely, to 
realize Arab unity. 


Today, you have transformed words into deeds. You have fulfilled your promise and 
you gave an example of sincerity to this nation by leaving the vicious circle and 
translating words and wishes into action. 
Forward...and the struggle will continue until comprehensive unity is realized. 
{[Singed] Your Brother, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi
