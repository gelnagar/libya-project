LIBYA

‘Strong Pratest’ Note Given to UK Over Arrests 
LD130927 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0845 GMT 13 Mar 84 


(“The People’s Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison Delivers a Strong Protest to the British Government, 
corrected as follows: — JANA headline] 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 Mar (JANA) — The People’s Committee of 
the People’s Bureau of Foreign Liaison has delivered a strongly 
worded protest to the British Government over the arrest by the 
British authorities yesterday of 23 Libyan citizens who are in 


NORTH AFRICA Q1 


Britain as tourists, students or patients seeking medical 
treatment. The committee considers this action as conducive to 
the destruction of relations between the two countries. 


The Libyan Arab protest was handed to the British charge to the 
Jamahiriyah, when he was summoned to the People’s Bureau for 
Foreign Liaison yesterday for the purpose. 


In its protest note, the committee demands the immediate release 
of all the Libyans arrested by the British authorities. It also 
demands an official apology over the arrest of a member of the 
People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison, who was on a mission at the 
Libyan Arab People’s Bureau in London, and his detention at 
London airport. It also demands an official apology for the 
British authorities’ storming and searching the office of one of 
the staff of the Libyan Arab People’s Bureau in London. 


The People’s Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison angrily denounces the involvement of the name of the 
Jamahiriyah with such acts, and categorically rejects all the 
so-called British warnings as being totally unacceptable. The 
People’s Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison 
pointed out to the British charge d’affaire that these serious acts 
of provocation against the citizens of the Jamahiriyah constitute 
an alignment by Britain on the side of the enemies of the Libyan 
Arab people, and as such will be met with the necessary mea- 
sures. 


The Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah considers the 
charges leveled against it a pretext to cover the British attitude 
of encouraging the stray dogs that committed a crime against the 
rights of the Libyan Arab people. 


JANA Reports Sudanese Liberation Front Statement 
LD111107 Tripoli JANA in English 0828 GMT 11 Mar 84 


[Text] London, Jumada Thani 6, March 11, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The Sudanese Popular Liberation Move- 
ment issued a statement in London affirming its determination 
to continue the armed struggle to rid the Sudanese people from 
the hegemony of hireling Numayri and his clique, and to estab- 
lish socialist-democratic-popular rule. 


In its statement, the front affirmed that the Sudanese masses in 
the south had joined its ranks to carry arms against the Numayri 
regime and its mercenaries. It said that the revolutionaries now 
control vast regions in the south, including the towns of Akobo, 
Wat, Nashir and [words indistinct]. 


The front accused hireling Numayri and his clique of robbing, in 
association with foreign companies, the Sudanese people of their 
resources and transforming Sudanese territory into training 
ranges and staging posts for the intervention of Western colonial- 
ist forces in the African region.

Malagasy Foreign Minister Holds Tripoli Talks 
LD101915 Tripoli JANA in English 1821 GMT 10 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada Thani 5, March 10, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — A meeting was held at noon today between 
the People’s Committee at the People’s Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison and the foreign minister of Democratic Madagascar, Mr 
Jean Bemananjara. 


Discussions during the meeting centered on methods of enhanc- 
ing cooperation and developing bilateral relations between the 
Jamahiriyah and the Democratic Malagasy republic. The meet- 
ing also discussed the situation in Africa in light of the American 
imperialist threats which are undermining security and stability 
in the continent, together with the threats posed by the racist 
South African regime to the frontline states.
