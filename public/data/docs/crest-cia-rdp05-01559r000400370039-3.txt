Summary (AI): An FBIS wire relays a Tripoli ARNA report that Qadhdhafi told a delegation of Darsa tribesmen from eastern Libya that the revolution had expelled foreign presence and was building industrial and agricultural bases. It has no specific Tripoli locations or events.
• Qadhdhafi said the revolution had removed about 20,000 plainclothes 'fascist' army personnel of the expelled Italian community, as well as the British and American bases.
• The Darsa tribe delegation was from eastern Libya.
• Darsa shaykhs expressed solidarity with Qadhdhafi and the revolution.
• 1976-02-16: Qadhdhafi received a delegation of Darsa tribesmen from eastern Libya (reported by ARNA from Tripoli on 17 Feb 1976; 'yesterday' refers to 16 Feb)

((TEXT)) TRIPOLI, LIBYA, 17 FEBRUARY, ARNA~- COLONEL . 
MU*AMMAR AL~ QADHDHAFI SAID YESTERDAY THE LIBYAN REVOLUTION HAD 
RIG THE COUNTRY QF THE 20,000 STRONG PLAINCLOTHED FASCIST ARMY 
(OF THE EXPELLED ITALIAN COMMUNITY DURING THE DEFUNCT MONARCHICAL 
REGIME) BESIDES THE BRITISH AND AMERICAN BASES. 

THE LEADER OF THE REVOLUTION TOLD A DELEGATION OF TRIBESMEN 
OF DARSA TRIBE, EASTERN LIBYA, THAT THE REVOLUTION, AFTER 
ELIMINATING FOREIGN PRESENCE, HAD STARTED BUILDING INDUSTRIAL 
AND AGRICULTURAL BASES TO ACHIEVE SELF-SUFFICTENCY AND REMOVE 
BACKWARDNESS. : .

USe WE MUST NOT FRUSTRATE THEIR HOPES, HE DECLARED.

WITH COLONEL AL-— QADHOHAFI AND THE REVOLUTION AND HAILED ITS 
ACHIEVEMENTS IN THE SHORT PERIOD OF ITS STRUGGLE.
