LIBYA

NIGERIAN OFFICIAL ARRIVES, SPEAKS ON CHAD 
LD172220 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 2015 GMT 17 May 79 LD 


[Text] Shehu Musa Yar'adua, chief of staff of the Nigerian Armed Forces and deputy chair. 
man of the Supreme Military Council, arrived today in Tripoli, He was welcomed at the 
airport by 'Ali at-Turayki, foreign secretary, ‘Abd al-Majid al-Qa'ud, secretary of 
liaison, and Jum'ah al-Arbash, secretary of electricity, 


Shehu Musa told a JANA reporter: We have diligently tried with the countries which took 
part in the Kano conference to attain a settlement to the problems of Chad, In spite 

of all the problems which have arisen subsequently, Nigeria and the Jamahiriyah con- 
tinue their efforts to reach a solution to what is taking place in Chad, He added: We 
will never agree to any interference in the affairs of Chad by any foreign party.
