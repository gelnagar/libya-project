LIBYA

7 APRIL ORGANIZATLON THREATENS NEW OPERATIONS AGAINST QADHDHAFI 
London SAWT LIBYA in Arabic Apr 80 p 17 


[Article: "7 April Organization claims responsibility for exploding 
oil wells") 


[Text] A secret organization which calls itself "The 7 April Organiza- 
tion’ announced its responsibility for the explosion of three oil wells 
during the period from mid-February to mid-March in Zaltan in the southern 
part of the province of Ajdabiya. The fires set in the third well were 
still not under control one month after the first explosion. 


The organization said that the purpose of these explosions is to put 
pressure on the American oil companies so that they will cease supporting 
the continuation in power of client al-Qadhdhafi. The purpose is also 
to convince them that their oil interests will not be safe in the future 
if al-Qadhdhafi stays in power. 


The statement of the 7 April Organization indicates the determination 
of the organization to carry out a number of other military operations 
against elements of the tyrannical government and against al-Qadhdhafi's 
agents, t’ avenge the martyrs of April who were hung by al-Qadhdhafi and 
the officers who were executed by firing squad following the army insur- 
gence on 25 August 1975. The organization's operations are also "in 
response to the perpetration of crimes and acts of violence and liquida- 
tion against our people.” 


American State Department sources had indicated that three dangerous 
explosions took place in the oil fields in Libya in the past two months, 
and that the cause of this series of explosions and the identity of the 
organization which planted the bombs were unknown. 


Mr Reed Adar, the American specialist in extinguishing oil field fires, 
led the famous squad of petroleum firefighters in Houston (Texas) to the 
site of the bombings in an attempt to extinguish the fires and prevent 
their getting out of control.
