ALBERIAN PRESIDENT BENDJEDID ARRIVES 1 APR 
LD011912 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 1 Apr 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--Brother Chadli Bendjedid, president of the Algerian republic, arrived 

in Tripoli this evening on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, 

The Algerian president was accorded a great welcome by the people at Tripoli international 
airport, 


President Bendjedid is accompanied by a high-level Algerian delegation which includes 
Mohamed Saleh Yahiaoui, member of the Political Bureau and the party's coordinator; 
Seddik Ben Yahia, member of the Political Bureau and foreign affairs minister; and Col 
Abdallah Belhouchet, member of the Political Bureau and inspector general of the people's 
national army, 


In a statement to JANA President Chadli Bendjedid said that the purpose of his visit to 
his second homeland, Libya, is to exchange views on the issues of the Arab nation at 
this stage in view of the existence of grave conspiracies being hatched against this 
nation, particularly against the Palestinian issue, 


President Chadli Bendjedid emphasized that the aims of the Great 1 September Revolution 
and the Algerian revolution are the same and so is their destiny, He said we must exert 
joint efforts in the interest of our Arab nation, our unity, Arabism and Islam, In con- 
clusion the Algerian president expressed his profound appreciation and that of his accom- 
paying delegation for the sincere, warm, fraternal welcome by the masses of the Arab 
people in Libya.

Statement Issued 


LDO21008 Tripoli JANA in English 0904 GMT 2 Apr 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, April 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--A press statement was issued at the 
end of the visit made by the foreign minister of the Islamic Republic of Mauritania 
to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


V. 2 Apr 80 NORTH AFRICA 


The statement stressed commitment to the Arab objectives and determination to continue 
the struggle to foil all capitulatory plans designed to liquidate the Palestinian cause. 
It also condemned the normalization of Egyptian-(Israeli) relations and called for the 
implementation of the strict Arab boycott against the Egyptian regime. 


The joint statement pledged support for the Arab people of the Western Sahara through 
their struggle for self-determination and solidarity with the African peoples fighting 
for independence and liberation. Condemning the apartheid regime of South Africa, the 
statement stressed the importance of Arab-African cooperation and dialogue and urged for 
stepping up such meetings for the interest of the African and Arab peoples. 


AFRICAN OIL WORKERS FEDERATION FORMED 
LD292142 Tripcld JANA in English 1819 GMT 29 Mar 80 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, March 29, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--African organisations, unions and 
syndicates participating in the fifth international oil antimonopolies conference announced 
in a statement issued yesterday the formation of the general federation of African oil 
workers. These organisations also formed a preparatory committee which includes the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, Nigeria, Algeria, Congo, Mali, Benin, Senegal, 
Guinea, Mauritania and Madagascar. The committee, whose headquarters is in the Jamahiriyah, 
has been entrusted with preparations for the organisation's founding conference. The 
statement issued last night also lauded the victories of Zimbabwe's people and declared 
solidarity with the Palestinian people's just struggle.
