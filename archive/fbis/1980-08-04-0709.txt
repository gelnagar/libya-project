BRITONS REPORTEDLY DETAINED FOR MINOR OFFENSES 
LDO31540 London SUNDAY TELEGRAPH in English 3 Aug 80 p 2 


{Text] Scores of Britons have been detained in Libya and others refused entry at air- 
ports while opposition to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's government grows inside the country. 


Western diplomatic sources in Tripoli report that up to 100 Britons are in prison, 
mainly for minor offences. Some are being held temporarily while their visas are 
investi gated. 


The moves, which have also affected other Europeans, are thought to be linked partly to 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's efforts to stamp out underground opposition and silence powerful 


critics living in exile. 


JANA Denial 


LD031542 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 3 Aug 8¢ 


{Text] Tripoli, 3 August (JANA)--The British SUNDAY TELEGRAPH has published a news 
item to the effect that a large number of Britons were detained in Libya. This 

news item, which is baseless, shows the level to which this newspaper has stooped, 
There are no British detainees in Libya and there is no government in Libya so as to 
have opposition: There is people's authority, a people ruling itself and which is 
enjoying real freedom. There is no regime called the Al-Qadhdhafi regime; there is a 
regime of people's authority. The attempts by Britain to cover up its suppression 
of Libyans in Britain by spreading such false claims shall not succeed,

LIBYA DENIES GIVING AID TO THAI MUSLIM SEPARATISTS 
LD021520 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1415 GMT 2 Aug 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 2 Aug (JANA)--AFP reports the paper THAI RAT as saying that a group 
of Muslim separatists has admitted receiving assistance from the Jamahiriyah to set up 
an independent Islamic state in the south of Thailand. This report is false and un- 
founded, The Jamahiriyah has no connection with the movement and has never given it 
any assistance.

MAURITANIA TO PROTEST MOROCCAN RAT TO UN, OAU 


ABO11925 Paris AFP in French 1900 GMT 1 Aug 80 


{Text] Nouakchott, 1 Aug (AFP)--Mauritania has decided to file a vigorous protest to 
the UN Security Council, the OAU and the nonaligned movement in the wake of the Moroccan 
aggression against the village of Bou Lanouar (96 km northwest of Nojadhibou). This 

was announced on Friday in a Mauritanian Council of Ministers communique, Mauritania, 
it is to be recalled, accuses the Moroccan Air Force of bombing military facilities in 
Mauritania on Tuesday. 


CMSN Meeting 
ABO11035 Nouakchott Domestic Service in French 0630 GMT 1 Aug 80 


[Excerpt] The Permanent Committee of the Military Committee of National Salvation met 
on Thursday, 31 July 1980, under the chairmanship of Lt Col Mohamed Khouna Ould Haydala, 
president of the Military Committee of National Salvation, head of state and government. 
During this meeting the Permanent Committee of the Military Committee of National 
Salvation held very long discussions on the tense situation in the country following 
the aggression perpetrated by the Moroccan Air Force against the Mauritanian town of 
Bou Lanouar. This aggression, which crowns the treacherous manoeuvres and plots against 
our country by Moroccan expansionism and which is a flagrant violation of the principle 
of international law, only strengthens our determimation to defend our territorial in- 
tegrity, our independence and our sovereignty against the aggressive blows of neo- 
colonialism and its agents.

The chairman of the Military Committee for National Salvation, head of state and govern- 
ment gave the council details of the attack of the Moroccan Air Force on the Mauritanian 
village of Bou Lanouar on the evening of 29 July 1980. 


The council expressed strong indignation at this treacherous aggression on the sanctity 

of our soil and the independence of our country. It has decided to place the issue before 
the international bodies, particularly the UN Security Council, the OAU, the Arab League 
and the organization of nonaligned states, to express strong protest and draw the attention 
of world public opinion to the adverse consequences which such irresponsible actions can 
have on the peace and security of the area, 


The council appeals to all the country!s forces to mobilize and close ranks to confront 
the aggression from whatever quarters and foil any maneuvers engineered against the 
sovereignty and independence of our people,
