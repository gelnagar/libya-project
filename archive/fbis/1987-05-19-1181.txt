LIBYA

Minister Arrives as Presidential Envoy 
LD181700 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 
1600 GMT 18 May 87 


[Text] Ethiopian Minister of Culture Girma Yilma arrived in 
Tripoli today as the personal envoy of Ethiopian President Men- 
gistu Haile Mariam to the brother leader of the revolution. 


The personal envoy of the Ethiopian president stated that the 
purpose of his visit is to develop relations between the two friendly 
countries. 


Received by Al-Ondhdhafi 
LD190909 Tripoli Voice af Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 0115 GMT 19 May 87 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has received Girma 
Yilma, culture minister and personal envoy of the Ethiopian 
president, who has conveyed a written message to him. 


Ethiopian Culture Minister Girma Yilma arrived in Tripoli 
yesterday as an envoy of Ethiopian President Mengitsu Haile 
Mariam to the brother leader of the revolution. The personal 
envoy of the Ethiopian president stated that his visit comes within 
the framework of developing relations between the two friendly 
countries.
