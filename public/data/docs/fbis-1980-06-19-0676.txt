The declaration of the state of emergency in the eastern desert and along the borders 
with Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, together with deploying the principal 


units of the Egyptian forces toward the Libyan borders are tantamount to a declaration 
of a state of war.

with the chief of staff of the Egyptian forces through the Egyptian minister of state

prelude to a premeditated aggressive plan against the Libyan people. The lies and feeble

after the Egyptian people and army have discovered that the promises of heaven and prosperity

The Egyptian people are suffering more deprivation and misery--indeed, they have lost 
their honor and dignity and even their daily bread. 


The Egyptian people havefound themselves in isolation from their nation and their Islamic 
world. Because of As-Sadat, the Egyptian people became enemies of themselves and allies 
of the enemies of Arabism and Islam--the Zionists and imperialists. 


In the face of this premeditated and planned action for aggression, as manifested in this 
declaration of a state of war, Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah wants to place 
the Egyptian people, the Egyptian Army, the Arab nation, the Islamic world and the world 
at large face-to-face with these acts of playing with fire being committed by As-Sadat. 


The Libyan people, the Egyptian people snd the Egyptian Army are called upon to foil the 
efforts of their common enemy As-Sadat, and to assert their everlasting alliance in deed. 
At the same time, the Foreign Secretariat affirms the following: 


A. Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah reserves its legitimate right to self- 
defense as supported by all international laws, conventions and traditions, foremost of 
which is the UN Charter. It has the capability, the strength and the right to strike 
at the aggressor, thwart it and destroy it. As-Sadat must bear full responsibility for 
this, and also bear full responsibility for creating tension and playing with fire. 


B. The Foreign Secretariat holds the United States responsible for the aggression and 
for the declaration of state of war because As-Sadat is not the master of his decision. 
He is only a slave and an obedient agent of the United States. When he plans aggression 
and declares a state of war, he does so to implement an American decision. 


The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah Foreign Secretariat wishes to hold the 
world witness that the United States is the one who instigates, pushes and encourages 
As-Sadat to commit aggression against the Libyan people.

STEADFASTNESS FRONT TO MEET, DISCUSS EGYPT 22 JUNE 
1D182006 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1910 GMT 18 Jun 80 


[Summary] Tripoli--The foreign secretary today contacted the foreign ministers of the 
Steadfastness and Confrontation Front to make arrangements for the emergency meeting, 
which the front!s political committee will hold on Sunday, to study the situation in 
the wake of the declaration of a state of emergency by the defeated (President 
as-Sadat of Egypt) along the borders of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


Within this framework, the Foreign Secretariat has acquainted the heads of the diplo- 
matic missions accredited to Libya with the dimensions of the plot which the defeated 
Egyptian president is hatching to attack the Libyan people in implementation of the 
plans drawn up in the alliance of the Stable David agreements, 


The foreign secretary also sent cables today to the UN secretary general, the president 
of the Security Council, the Arab League secretary general, the (?chairman) of the OAU, 
the (?president) of the Islamic Conference Organization, and the chairman of the non- 
aligned conference explaining the (?actions) being taken by the defeated Egyptian 
president and which constitute a threat to stability in the region, and escalation of 
tension and a violation of international charters and norms, 


The foreign secretary stressed that the Libyan people reserve their right to defend 
themselves under the UN Charter and that they are capable of doing so and that the 
defeated must bear all the consequences, 


FOREIGN MINISTRY BRIEFS DIPLOMATS ON EGYPTIAN 'CONSPIRACY! 


1D191000 Tripoli JANA in English 0918 GMT 19 Jun 80 


[Text ] Tripoli, June 19, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Jamahiriyah Foreign Secretariat 
contacted the foreign ministers of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front to pre- 
pare for an extraordinary meeting of the front!s Political Committee, to discuss the 
Egyptian declaration on the state of emergency along the Jamahiriyah borders, 


The Foreign Secretariat also briefed the heads of the diplomatic missions to Libya, on 
the conspiracy which is being schemed by the Egyptian president against the Libyan 
people as part of the implementation of the Stable David accords, 


V. 19 Jun 80 NORTH AFRICA 


The Foreign Secretariat explained in the brief to the diplomatic mission, the dimensions 
of the conspiracy and the gravity of the situation. 


On the other hand, the foreign secretary sent cables to the UN secretary general, the 
secretary general of the Arab League, the secretary of the Organization of African Unity, 
the secretary of the Islamic Conference and the chairman of the non-aligned movement, in 
which he explained about the gravity of the situation due to the declaration of the 
defeated Egyptian president which threatens the peace and security in the area and 
violates international charters and regulations. 


The foreign secretary affirmed that the Libyan people have the legitimate right to 

defend themselves and are backed by international charters, foremost the UN Charter. 

The Libyan people, the secretary added, have the strength to foil the aggression and hold 
the defeated president responsible for all the consequences.

{Text] Tripoli, 17 June--JANA's political editor comments on the defeated Egyptian 
president's declaration of a state of emergency on the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah borders. 


The U.S. peace has pushed As-Sadat to war to kill more Egyptians. At all times--since 
the disgraceful Kilometer-101 tent, until signing theStable David treaty with the Zionist 
enemy=--the United States and its agent As-Sadat, who has transformed Egypt into an 
American colony, have been talking about peace. But what do they mean by their peace, 
and where has it led? It has transformed Egypt from an independent country into an 
American colony abounding with U.S. experts, men and military bases which disgraced the 
Egyptian homeland and are now threatening the security and peace of the Arab peoples and 
shaking stability in this part of the world. They are also threatening the independence 
of the African peoples and posing a threat to the Islamic peoples. 


The so=callied peace has made Egypt subservient to Zionist politics, a pawn for the 
terrorist Begin. The alleged peace has--and this is the important thing--turned the 
confrontation between Arabs and Zionists, between Egyptians and Israelis, into one between

one between an Arab and his brother.

Egyptians to fight the Arabs on behalf of the United States and the Zionist enemy. But 

the Libyan people, and with them the Arab nation which has rejected the treason and disgrace
