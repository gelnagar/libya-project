AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES THANKS FROM PHILIPPINES PRESIDENT

{Text] Tripoli, Dec 4, JANA--The leader of the ist of September revolution and secretary- 
general of the General People!s Congress, Colonel Mu!lammar al-Qadhdhafi received a written

PARTICIPATION IN NONALINED COMMITTEE ON NUCLEAR ENERGY PLANNED 


LDO4O938Y Tripoli JANA in English 1648 GMT 3 Dec 7/8 LD 


([Text] Tripoli, 3 Dec, JANA--The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah is participating 
in the meetings of the coordination committee for the international conference for the 
peaceful use of nuclear energy. The meeting is to be held on the 4th and 5th of this month 
in Belgrade, 


This committee, which emanated from the non-aligned foreign ministerts meetings, includes 
three Jamahiriyah and fourteen other members, 


The committee will discuss during its meetings the coordination of attitudes among non- 
aligned countries for the forthcoming nuclear energy conference for peaceful purposes to 
be held next year,
