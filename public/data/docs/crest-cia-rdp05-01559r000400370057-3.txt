AL-QADHDHAFI DISCUSSES LEBANON IN RALLY SPEECH 
Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1915 GMT 18 Apr 76 JN 

[Text ] Palestinian, Lebanese, and Syrian ‘Arab brothers who work in Tripoli have staged 

a march to the RCC Headquarters. They expressed their support for the Great 1 September 
Revolution and its leader, the trustee of Arab nationalism. They expressed their support 
for the honest and effective backing which the Libyan Arab Republic has given to the 
progressive forces in Lebanon. 

The trustee of Arab nationalism made a speech which he devoted to what is happening in 
Lebanon. He began the speech by greeting the Palestinian, Lebanese, and Syrian Arab 
brothers. He said: What is happening in Lebanon is not far removed from us since what 
is happening in Lebanon is part of the battle which the nation is facing. The Lebanese 
Arab people have surmounted the wall of fear and confronted the enemies of the masses as 
their brothers in Libya did on 1 September 1969. He said: What has happened in Lebanon 
4s a healthy phenomenon and a true expression of the nature of the battle between the 
Arab masses and their enemies. The Arab nationalist forces would have expressed this 
same thing if they had had the oppprtunity. 

Te trustee of Arab nationalism referred to the attempts being carried out in Lebanon, 

such as the cease-fire, the disarming of the nationalist forces, the attempt to achieve 

a reconciliation between the unionists and isolationists, and the interpretation of the 
events in Arab Lebanon in a way inconsistent with reality. He asserted that these attempts 
are destined to fail because they run contrary to the inevitability of history and because 
they are aimed at foiling the popular revolution which the nationalist progressive forces 
in Lebanon have kindled. This revolution may expand or ignite other parts of the Arab 
homeland in view of the nature of the Lebanese arena, which has a great influence. 

Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi added: Any act hostile against the Lebanese nationalist 
progressive forces will in fact harm the Palestinian question, which is not a local but a 
pan-Arab and world question. In this connection he addressed the Palestinian, Syrian, and 
Lebanese Arab brothers, saying: The stand of the Libyan Arab Republic is clear to all. 

We see no justification for a cease-fire with the enemy, who is still occupying the whole 
of Palestine and parts of territories of fraternal Syria and Egypt. 

He added: What is happening in Lebanon is a reflection of the restlessness of the Arab 

people over the attempts to deceive the masses and to impose capitulationist solutions 
on them.
