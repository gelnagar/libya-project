LIBYA

Yugoslav Foreign Minister Arrives 22 September 
LD221847 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1530 GMT 22 Sep 83 


[Text] Tripoli 22 Sep (JANA) — The yugoslav foreign minister 
and the accompanying delegation arrived in Tripoli this 
afternoon for a visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jama- 
hiriyah in the framework of the relations of fruitful cooperation 
existing between the two friendly countries. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Explains Views 
LD230931 Tripoli Voice af Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 0115 GMT 23 Sep 83 


[Text] Yesterday the brother leader received the Yugoslav for- 
eign minister, who conveyed to him the Yugoslav viewpoint with 
regard to the U.S. intervention and the aggravation of the 
situation in the Middle East and the Meditewrranean — a matter 
which threatens the outbreak of a third world war and demands 
that the nonaligned countries stand together to confront the 
imperialist U.S. assault. 


At this meeting, the leader explained that a solution to the 
Lebanese crisis cannot be achieved without the withdrawal of 
foreign forces from this Arab country. The Arab nation is 
capable of resolving the problem of one of its countries. 
Therefore, no one from outside the Arab nation has a right to 
interfere in an Arab issue, regardless of excuses. 


The leader explained that allegations that this intervention came 
at the request of the Lebanese president lack logic because this 
Lebanese president has no legitimacy, nor does he represent the 
Lebanese people. He represents only one faction, the Phalangists. 
His election took place under cover of Israeli bayonets. The 
parliament which he alleges elected him came to the end of its 
term, and when it met to elect him it had no quorum. 


The brother leader pointed out that this U.S. imperialist escala- 
tion is only the beginning of a 10th crusade which has as its target 
the Muslims in Lebanon and also the Arab homeland. 


With regard to Chad, the leader explained that the solution to 
this problem must be evident and can be realized through the 
withdrawal of foreign forces from this country and through 
understanding among the conflicting factions in Chad in order 
to end this 18-year-old civil war. He also pointed out that 
President Goukouni Oueddei is the legitimate president of Chad. 


The views of the two countries are identical with regard to the 
situations in Lebanon and Chad. The positive role played by the 
nonaligned countries in consolidating the march of world peace 
and stability was underlined. The brother leader also underlined 
the Jamahiriyah’s attitude on the deteriorating situations in both 
Lebanon and Chad.
