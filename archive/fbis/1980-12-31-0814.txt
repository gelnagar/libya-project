LIBYA

EGYPTIAN OPPOSITION LEADER EXPECTS 'UPHEAVAL' 


LD311008 Tripoli JANA in English 0900 GMT 31 Dec 80 


[Text] Kuwait, 31 Dec (JANA)--The former Egyptian chief of staff and secretary general 
of the Egyptian Patriotic Front Saad Eddin al-Shazli predicted a strong public upheaval 
against As-Sadat's regime which had transformed Egypt into a military base. 


In a long interview vy AL QABAS Kuwaiti newspaper, published yesterday he said that 
As-Sadat is trying to deceive the Arab people by saying he is providing facilities to 
the Americans where in fact he is giving them military bases to be empioved against the 
Arab people to protect the American interests in the region, a fact which is well 
understood by the Egyptian Army. He pointed out that the Egyptian front, is in contact 
with national opposition elements and organization inside Egypt and believes that 
As-Sadat's regime can only be undermined by the Egyptian Arab people. He stressed the 
strength and continous escalation of the opposition movement inside Egypt which is to 
unite with other secret organization for the aim of overthrowing As-Sadat. He declared 
that the Egyptian Patriotic Front will soon join the national front to strengthen Arab 
opposition to Camp David.
