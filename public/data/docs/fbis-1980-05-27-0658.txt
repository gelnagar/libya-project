LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI HITS COLONIALIST INFLUENCE IN AFRICA 


LD251550 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 25 May 80 


[Text] The leader of the revolution has confirmed that the intention of the colonialist 
states is to maintain their interests in Africa in order to keep Africa as a farm for 
Europe and a mine for its factories of Europe. They intend to maintain the colonialist 
tradition, that is to keep the Africans as slaves and the Europeans as masters. 


He said during a meeting this morning with the African ambassadors and representatives 
of liberation movements in the Jamahiriyah on the occasion of Africa Day that colonialist 
policies are aimed at keeping Africa as an exploited continent and keeping the Africans 
backward. The leader explained that when the OAU was established a small number of its 
countries enjoyed independence but now the majority are independent. What distinguishes 
this anniversary is the independence of the people of Zimbabwe who were able to extract 
their independence by force. The leader said although we are proud of the large number 
of independent states in Africa we have some observations regarding this independence, 
that is that colonialist states insisted on making the independence of African states 

a fictitious one. For example, the African French-speaking group is still regarded 

by France as colonies belonging to it. To confirm this, France follows a tradition of 
inviting those states every year for a meeting headed by France in order to ensure 

that they behave in accordance with French policy. The leader added that the continu- 
ation of this tradition will abolish the independence of the French-speaking states 

and that the response of those countries to this tradition is submission to the French 
will. The leader pointed out that this tradition is shameful to us, the African 
leaders, and makes a large group of the cammtinent's independent states subordinate to 

a colonialist state. The leader asked what would have happened had the English-speaking 
states' subordination to Britain continued? This would mean that Africa was not inde- 
pendent. The leader explained we all know that this relationship and this subordination 
does not benefit African states; they benefit the important colonialist states. The 
leader added that the colonialist states are doing everything to maintain this bad 
situation and said keeping the language of colonialism as the official language of those 
states is aimed at maintaining the interests of colonialist states. Therefore, when we 
mark the 17th anniversary of Africa Day we do mot recognize the French language or the 
English language and this is a start to the elimination of this subordination of 
African countries to colonialist states. Here we only recognize our original African 
languages: Arabic, Hausa, Swahili and Amharic. 


The leader said if the national interests of the African countries subjected to this 
tradition created by France had benefitted we would not have objected to it. But we 
see that those countries are still needy and poor and require assistance. The leader 
explained if France was involved in helping French-speaking states, we should think 
about limiting the aid and services of the African Cooperation Fund and the Arab Bank 
for Economic Development in Africa--the bodies in which Libya is a major contributor 
with about 200 million dinars--to countries which France did not undertake to help. 
But, the leader said, we know that France is cheating Africa as Africa was cheated 
by the Israelis in order to expand their racialist entity in South Africa.

The leader pointed out that among the regrettable negative observations is that an 
African state has recognized the racialist regime in Palestine and revived the racialist 
regime in the southern part of the continent. It is Egypt. He explained that there is 
no difference in recognizing the Israelis and the racialists in South Africa, and 

there is no difference in saying that Palestine belongs to the Israelis or that South 
Africa belongs to the racialists. 


Consequently, what will be our attitude as Africans toward an African country which re- 
cognizes the racialist regime in Africa? He said: No doubt we will expell it from the 
OAU and boycott it. Thus we should think seriously about expelling the Egyptian regime 
from the OAU because it wants to (?represent) the racialists. The leader said nonex- 
pulsion of the Egyptian regime from the OAU and thinking about the reestablishment 

of relations with the Israelis would make the Libyan people seriously reconsider their 
attitude. We will submit the matter to popular conference in order to allow the Libyan 
people to decide who should receive aid. He pointed out that any resolution taken by 
the Libyan people would have a serious effect on bilateral aid and that provided through 
establishments--a matter which would influence the economies of certain African states 
who depend on this aid, especially if we take into consideration the fact that Libya is 
the northern gate to Africa and it has tens of joint ventures and is a major partici- 
pant in economic development establishments in Africa, with tens of embassies in those 
countries. Add to this the revolutionary influence of Libya in Africa and the world. 


The leader then said that the Libyan people would also define its attitude to the 
continuation of French leadership of the French-speaking African states. The continua- 
tion of this would make us think about transferring all Libyan representation to Paris 
for this is its natural place. Consequently, the French Embassy in Tripoli should 

be regarded as representing the interests of those countries in Libya. 


The leader welcomed the withdrawal of the invading French forces from Chad but expressed 
regret at the continued civil war in Chad and at the stationing of French forces with- 
drawn from Chad in other African states such as Central Africa, Cameroon, Gabon, the 
Ivory Coast, Senegal and Djibouti. This will enable those forces to return to Chad 
whenever they wished. 


The leader said that Libya would do all possible to establish peace in Chad if asked 

to do so officially. He explained that Libya is capable of this. The leader greeted 

the African countries enjoying the freedom of decision. Despite the fact that they 

speak French, they are the progressive African states and with other non-French speaking 
states give the true face of African dignity. The leader said we are proud of the victory 
achieved by the Saharan people, of the increased recognition of Saharan Arab Democratic 
Republic which we as revolutionaries regard as a revolutionary explosion in a region still 
suffering from feudalism, exploitation and colonialism. This is regarded from the 
traditional point of view as a matter of self-determination, the right of every group 
small or big. 


The leader ended his speech by saying: Let Africa remain for the Africans! Africa has 
no alliance other than with itself! Hands off Africa! Long live the original African 
languages--Arabic, Hausa, Swahili and Amharic!

[Text] Sebha, May 25, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--A large popular rally was stated in 
the city of Sebha today to express support for the third meeting of the revolutionary 
committees and contimiity in the endeavor to destroy the society of oppression and ex- 
Ploitation and set up the new socialist order.

Marching through the main streets and squares of the city, the demonstrators carried 
green flags and placards affirming the combined pursuit by the masses and revolutionary 
forces of fighting the remants of theold regime and constructing the new socialist 
order. 


Tne rally issued the following statement: 


"In confirmation of the eternal humane dicta, in the belief in the vanguard precepts 
which promise the people of the world final emancipation from the suppression of 
Wage-earning and exploitation, and in feeling a sense of responsibility towards the 
great historic action for which the revolutionary forces have dedicated themselves in 
its loyalty to the ideology of the rebel Colonel Mu'ammer al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 
grand Al-Fatah revolution and the Libyan Arab peoples who believe in their revolution 
and Arabism, we, the marching force in this tumultuous rally whose destiny and life has 
been linked to that of the people's revolution, assert the following: 


1. In confirmation of the creative humane dicta in the third universal theory, we un- 
swervingly assert our determination to work unceasingly, locally and abroad, to imple- 
ment the dicta. 


2. We stress that the people's revolution is the sole means for human emancipation from 
oppression and subjugation. It is the aspired revolution of tomorrow. This tum] tuous 
rally, seen in the city which witnessed the declaration of people's authority and the 
establishment of the first Jamahiriyah in histroy, affirms its persistence in the 
policy destroying the society of exploitation and slavery in order to set up the new 
socialist order, the society of equality and production. 


3. We assert the recommendations issued during the third meeting of the revolutionary 
committees that physical elimination is the ultimate and decisive action in the dialec- 
tics of revolutionary conflict, if the previous measures of stripping the adversary of 
political, economic and social weapons have not ended their activities. In this way 

we can achieve the society of the peoples who are leaders md masters who hold authority, 
wealth and arms. It is the society of freedom am equality where there are no masters 
and slaves. 


To the front! Al-jfatah forever! Committees everywhere! 
[Signed] The people of the city of Sebha. 
22 MAY 'SPONTANEOUS MARCH', MASSES! STATEMENT REPORTED 


LD231345 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 23 May 80 


[Text] The popular masses in Tripoli came out last night in a huge spontaneous march 
which passed through the streets of Tripoli. The masses expressed their strong deter- 
mination to continue to strip the exploiting and bourgeois elements of their economic 
weapons and to crush exploitation in society for the sake of achieving the new social- 
ist society. 


The masses participating in the march issued a statement in which they stressed the need 
to strip the counterforces of their effectiveness in hindering the masses! movement, 

and stressed that savings in excess of one's needs were obtained from the wealth of 
society and at the expense of other's needs. 


V. 27 May 80 I4 NORTH AFRICA 


The statement emphasized the eternal cohesion of the masses with the leader of their 
revolution and their fateful commitment to the precepts of the Great 1 September Revolution. 
The statement pointed out that the stripping of the exploiting and bourgeois elements of 
their economic weapons will hasten the formulation of the free and happy socialist society 
advocated by the Green Book and in which what will be achieved will be the repletion of 
material and moral needs and in which everything will be liberated from the domination and 
subjugation by the society of exploitation to which the revolution had put an end. 


In the conclusion the statement of the masses declared that hoarding in excess of one's 
needs is a transgression against the wealth of society, that individuals in the society of 
the masses can only store away whatever they need for their own requirements, and that 
squandering the wealth of other members of the new socialist society is not permitted unless 
each of them renders public services more than others and produces more than others. [This 
is followed by recording of voices chanting in praises of Al-Fatah and the 1 September 
Revolution].
