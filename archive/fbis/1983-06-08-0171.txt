LIBYA

Cooperation Agreement Signed With CSSR 
LDO41521 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1455 GMT 4 Jun 83 


[Text] Tripoli 4 Jun (JANA) — The minutes of the agreement 
for bilateral cooperation between the Jamahiriyah and 
Czechoslovakia were signed this morning at the People’s Bureau 
for External Liaison. The agreement minutes provides for devel- 
oping cooperation in the economic, social, cultural, health, infor- 
mation, and sport fields. It also provides for the intensification of 
contacts between the popular organizations in both countries to 
consolidate relations of friendship between the two friendly 
peoples.

Ceremony Honors Military Delegation to CAR 
LD061716 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
6 Jun 83 


[Text] This morning Major al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi, assistant 
chief of the Libyan Armed Forces General Staff, attended a 


celebration held to honor the members of the Libyan Arab 
Armed Forces delegation which has recently returned from the 
Central African Republic after successfully and efficiently com- 
pleting its tasks within the context of cooperation between the 
two friendly countries 


During the celebration Brother Maj al-Humaydi distributed 
medals of military duty to the officers and soldiers of this group 
in appreciation for their great role in training and raising the 
qualification of the armed forces in the CAR, thus enabling them 
to defend the freedom of the friendly people in the CAR. Brother 
Maj al-Humaydi spoke on the occasion, which was attended by 
a large number of officers, NCO’s, and soldiers of the Libyan 
Arab Armed Forces. He praised its success in fulfulling its task 
in the friendly country 


The president of the CAR had greeted the members of this 
military group with pride and praised its role in implementing 
the ties of friendship between the two friendly peoples. A big 
celebration was held in Bangui in honor of the group on the 
occasion of the end of its mission 


Chadian Opposition Leader Denounces U.S. Stand 
LDOS0452 Tripoli JANA in English 1359 GMT 4 Jun 83 


[Text] Paris, Shaban 23, June 4, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY The Chadian National Unity Government, led by 
Goukouni Oueddei, yesterday denounced the American stand on 
the Chadian question 


The Foreign Minister Mahamat Nour Adam denounced in Paris 
yesterday the White House spokesman’s statement on Thursday 
in which the latter alleged that Libya was getting ready to 
intervene in Chad and that there was already a Libyan military 
buildup on the Chadian border 


He added that Washington's statement is a direct interference in 
Chad's interna! affairs 


Sudanese Refusal To Aid Libyan Aircraft Scored 
LDOS1858 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1740 GMT 5 Jun 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 5 June (JANA) — The authorities of the underling 
Numayri refrained from providing any information to facilitate 
the flight of the aircraft of the Libyan Arab delegation while 
flying through Sudanese airspace en route to Addis Ababa, thus 
endangering the safety of international aviation in the area 


This ill-mannered behavior confirms the evil spirit which domi- 
nates the behavior of the regime of the underling Numayri and 
which reveals his ill and hostile intentions 


This behavior is also considered a blatant deliberate violation of 
the laws of air navigation and grossly flouts all the laws and codes 
of conduct which were laid down by the International Civil 
Aviation Organization
