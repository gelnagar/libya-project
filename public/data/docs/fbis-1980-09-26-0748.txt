LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI, PDRY PRESIDENT HOLD CLOSED MEETING 


LD251029 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 25 Sep 80 


[Excerpt] A closed meeting was held last night between the brother leader and Brother ‘Ali 
Nasir Muhammad, secretary general of the Central Committee of the Yemeni Socialist Party, 
chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme People's Council and PDRY prime minister. The 


meeting extended into the early hours of this morning. 
Press Statement 
LD252228 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1615 GMT 25 Sep 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 25 Sep (JANA)--A press statement on the visit of Chairman ‘Ali Nasir 
Muhammad, chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme People's Council and chairman of the 
Council of Ministers of the People's Democratic Republic of Yemen was issued this evening. 
Here is the text: 


In response to the pan-Arab call made by brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 
great Fatah revolution, on the occasion of the 1lth anniversary of the great Fatah revolu- 
tion, and in view of the need at the present stage of the history of the Arab nation to 
escalate the revolutionary struggle aginst colonialism, Zionism and reaction, and in view 
of the pioneering role of the great Fatah revolution and the glorious 14 October revolu- 
tion, Brother ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme People's 
Council and chairman of the Council of Ministers of the People's Democratic Republic 

of Yemen, paid a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah from 24 to 25 
September 1980. 


During this visit a deep and fully responsible study was made of the requirements of the 
current stage of the Arab nation and its revolutionary movement. The two sides agreed 
that Arab unity is the historic and revolutionary reply to the strategic dismemberment of 
the Arab nation, the result of the plans of Zionism, colonialism and reaction. They con- 
firm the following: 


1. The determination of the two revolutions in Libya and Democratic Yemen to move the 
Arab nation to the stage of counterattack in confronting the Zionist and imperialist 
presence and that they shoulder this historic responsibility and its struggle consequences. 


2. The two sides agree on the need to draw up a fixed timetable to achieve the unity of 
the two parts of Yemen in accordance with the Tripoli deciaration in order that Yemen will 
be able to join the nucleus unity state of Syria and Libya.
