AL-AHRAM: DPRK TO WITHDRAW PILOTS FROM LIBYA 
NC220933 Paris AFP in English 0911 GMT 22 Jan 80 NC 
[Text] Cairo, Jan, 22 (AFP)--North Korea is to withdraw its pilots from Libya following 
reports of possible Libyan military action against Egypt, the Cairo daily AL-AHRAM said 


today. The North Koreans have been training local pilots in Korean fighters sold to 
Libya, the daily reported,

REVOLUTIONARY COMMITTEES PUBLISH NEW ORGAN 
LD181434 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 18 Jan 80 LD 


[Text] The first issue of the newspaper AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR [The Green March], organ of 
the revolutionary committees, was published today with analytical and intellectual 
articles on a number of important issues. Im a front-page article the paper emphasized 
that information has become an instrument for misleading and misinforming the popular 
masses everywhere about what is going on around them. It said that AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR 
is the organ of the revolutionary committees, which discovered through the Green Book 
the false democracy prevaiiing in the world and the truth about the societies of 
exploitation and organized themselves into revolutionary committees to detonate the 
revolutior--the revolution of the future, The paper added that the publication of 
AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR is good news and a pioneering action for lovers of freedom, since 
this is the first time that a voice has emerged to analyze and present the issues ina 
true revolutionary spirit, because it is the voice of freedom and progress under the 
guidance of the revolutionary committees, 


PALESTINIAN DETAINEES CONTINUE HUNGER STRIKE 
JN211842 Baghdad INA in Arabic 1830 GMT 21 Jan 80 JN 


[Text] Beirut, 21 Jamuary--Today the Palestinians who are detained in the prisons of 
the Libyan town of Al-Bayda continued their open hunger strike for the fifth day 
running, in protest against their illegal detention. Reporting this today, WAFA says 
that the Palestinian detainees are Ahmad al-Barwani, Dr Muhammad Nur, Dr Muhammad 
Awdah, Abd al-Munim Hamdan, Fathi al-Kawamili and Mahmud al-Kurdi,.

LD211616 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 21 Jan 80 LD 
(Unattributed commentary ] 


[Excerpts] The reports that leaked out about the callup by the subdued Egyptian regime 
of reserve forces for active duty in the Egyptian Army and about the cover of secrecy 
that shrouded this callup arouse a great deal of surprise and astonishment, They also 
prove that the subdued Egyptian regime has placed itself at the disposal of the United 
States to perform the role of the imperialist policeman in the region, threatening its 
security and the forces of liberation and revolution which refuse to submit to the 
American-Zionist will.

the regular forces is that the Egyptian forces have been withdrawn from the Canal front 
with the Zionist racist enemy, that this front has now been totally relaxed, that there 
is, actually, no front with the enemy, that the Egyptian regime declared that the 
October War was the last war, that the subdued Egyptian regime has entered into an 
alliance with the Zionists and that the negotiations with the terrorist Begin have not 
been deadlocked to give the impression that the Egyptian regime was seeking to put ona 
show of force for the benefit of the enemy, What also provokes astonishment is that 
the Camp David agreements provide for settling all secondary disputes between the 
subdued and the enemy by means of direct negotiations and not by the threat of use of 
force, and that the subdued regime dares do no such thing against the enemy now that 
the enemy has gained geographical and strategic advantages in Sinai and the Negev 
through the capitulation of the traitorous Egyptian president,
