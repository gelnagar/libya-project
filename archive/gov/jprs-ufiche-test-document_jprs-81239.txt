AGRICULTURAL PROJECTS INSPECTED--The Guide of the Revolution Inspects the al- 
Rajmah Agricultural Project Grazing Lands: Tripoli, 25 Rajab, 19 May (Jamahiriyah 
News Agency)--Yesterday the guide of the revolution made an inspection visit to 
the grazing lands of the al-Rajmah agricultural project, which is incorporated 
into the huge grazing project extending from soutt. of al-Abyar to the plain of 
Benghazi in the north and from the al-Fatih project in the east to the Wadi 

al-Bab one in the west. This great expanse makes it possible to raise one million 
head of livestock. The guide of the revolution also inspected the project which 
was Carried out by the revolutionary committee at the Qar Yunis Economic 

Faculty in 1980 with the guide of the revolution's participation in planting 
grapevines in several regions. That agricultural experiment has been pro- 

fitable and has borne fruit in spite of the aridity of the soil. The guide of 

the revolution was accompanied on this inspection visit by Messrs [as published] 
Abu-Zayd Umar Durdah, secretary of the General People's Committee for Agrarian 
Reform and Land Reclamation. [Text] [Paris BULLETIN QUOTIDIEN DE L'AGENCE
