Summary (AI): A CIA cable dated 12 September 2012 reports that a U.S. facility in Benghazi had been under heavy fire since about 0430 local time, with mortars and RPGs, and that Libya's Prime Minister's Office ordered military reinforcements to help defend it. Mortar fire reportedly came from a nearby racetrack, and gun trucks set up defensive positions around the facility to assist evacuation.
• As of 0630 local time on 12 September 2012, the U.S. facility in Benghazi had been under heavy fire since approximately 0430.
• The Libyan Prime Minister's Office ordered military reinforcements to assist in defending the U.S. facility.
• Reports said attackers had MANPADS (not further identified).
• Gun trucks set up a defensive posture around the U.S. facility; it was not known whether they belonged to Libyan militias or the Libyan military.
• 2012-09-12: Attack on a U.S. facility in Benghazi with mortars and RPGs from about 0430; Libyan government sent military reinforcements; reports of MANPADS among attackers (unconfirmed).
