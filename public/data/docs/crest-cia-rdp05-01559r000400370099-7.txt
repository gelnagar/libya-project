Summary (AI): An FBIS transcript of a Qadhafi speech broadcast from Tripoli on 2 September 1976 (domestic Arabic service) covers the revolution's achievements and a hard line against Egypt's Sadat, including a threat to sever relations and an assertion that Libya would not move troops to the Egyptian border. It names no Tripoli sites, prisons or killings.
• Qadhafi said Libya had supplied the Egyptian army with 1,000 artillery electronic devices, 107 tanks and 30 Mirage planes during the 1973 October War.
• Qadhafi claimed the revolution produced 39 factories in less than 7 years and over 250,000 houses.
• Qadhafi said the popular conferences had voted against severing relations with Egypt.
• The transcript is dated 02 September 1976 and was filed by FBIS Athens.
• 1976-09-02: Qadhafi speech on the revolution's achievements and on relations with Egypt, broadcast on domestic Arabic service
