BILLY CARTER USED BY BRZEZINSKI AS LIBYA GO-BETWEEN
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp90-00552r000100990092-3
https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-cia-rdp90-00552r000100990092-3

Summary (AI): A 1980 Washington Post article (reprinted in CIA reading room) reports that the White House disclosed National Security Adviser Zbigniew Brzezinski used Billy Carter, the President's brother, as a go-between with Libya in November 1979 to help free the American hostages in Iran. Mobs in Tripoli had burned the US Embassy there five days after that meeting.
• Billy Carter received $220,000 from the Libyan government in January and April 1980, described as payments on a $500,000 loan.
• Brzezinski met Billy Carter and Houderi on 27 November 1979, 23 days after the hostages were seized in Tehran.
• Qaddafi reportedly sent a message to Khomeini asking for the hostages' release after the Tripoli embassy burning.
• Billy Carter's meeting with Brzezinski was arranged June 10 and held June 11, 1980; Lloyd Cutler then referred him to lawyers.
• Senate Judiciary Republicans sought a full committee inquiry; a House resolution was introduced by 76 members.
• 1979-11-27: Brzezinski met Billy Carter and Libyan chargé Ali Houderi in Washington, about 23 days after the Tehran hostages were seized; Libya later asked Khomeini to release the hostages.
• 1979-11-32: Mobs in Tripoli burned the American Embassy, five days after the Brzezinski-Carter-Houderi meeting.
