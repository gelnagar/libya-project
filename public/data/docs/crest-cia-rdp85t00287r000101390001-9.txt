Summary (AI): A June 1980 CIA memo to the Intelligence Branch assesses that Qadhafi's assassination campaign against Libyan dissidents abroad is continuing, mainly in Western Europe, and that US-based dissidents may be targeted. It describes hit-team methods and the role of Libyan People's Bureaus, with a list of ten Libyans killed by June 1980. None of the incidents is in Tripoli.

SUBJECT : Libyan Assassination Teams: Some Patterns 

l. We have reason to doubt that Libya's Qadhafi has 
ordered a halt to the ongoing series of assassinations of 
Libyan dissidents living abroad. While Western Europe has 
been the major arena for these incidents, we believe that 
Libyans living in the US will also be targetted, in part 
as proof that no one is safe from Qadhafi's vengeance. 

2. Preliminary analysis of the European murders 
suggests that there are two types of Libyan hit teams 
operating against dissident targets. In the majority 
of killings, it appears that professional assassins 
(presumably belonging to the Libyan Intelligence Service) 
have been the perpetrators. In several cases, however, 
the killings appear to have been the work of amateurs, 
possibly students or others with little formal training 
in covert warfare. The fact that two of the victims 
were killed by strangulation and decapitation suggests 
amateurs rather than professional killers. 

3. A common modus operandi has been for a two-man 
hit squad to kill the victim. The hit teams probably 
are relying on locally-recruited Libyans or members of 
the so-called "People's Bureaus" to do the spotting. 
The Peoples' Bureau probably also arranges for the ex- 
traction of the hit team once an operation is complete.

4. The West European cases lead us to expect that 
the Peoples' Bureau in the US would be responsible for 
spotting and assessing Libyan dissidents living here, 
with a hit squad being sent in to commit the actual 
assassinations. In addition to whatever weapons the 
Peoples' Bureau here already possesses, the Libyan gov- 
ernment would not hesitate to use the diplomatic pouch 
to bring in more. Thus the assassins are unlikely to 
attempt to enter the US armed. 

5. The whole concept of the "Peoples' Bureaus" 
has caused an inordinate amount of difficulty for govern- 
ments worldwide in trying to define the exact status of 
Peoples Bureau "diplomats." At times the Libyans claim 
to have diplomatic immunity; at others, such as the re- 
cent case in the US, they have claimed to be in a non- 
diplomatic status and therefore not capable of being 
PNGed. Libyan assassins are probably capitalizing on 
this confusion and traveling on Peoples Bureau passports. 

6. An argument can be made that all persons traveling 
on Peuples' Bureau passports are to some degree accomplices 
of these assassination plots, although the vast majority of 
them probably are not involved in actual assassinations. 
Peoples Bureau personnel entering the US from a European 
location (especially London, Rome, Bonn, Geneva, or Malta) 
where there is a recent history of assassination attempts 
deserve special attention however. Unfortunately we cannot 
yet determine whether the hit teams are traveling singly or 
in small groups. 

7. Additionally, some assassins might be traveling 
on regular Libyan passports. Again, those possessing 
passports recently stamped in European countries deserve 
special attention. We have no evidence of assassins trav- 
eling on non-Libyan passports, although the Libyan Intelli- 
gence Service can fabricate such documents. We cannot 
confirm that non-Libyan nationals have been used to carry 
out any of the recent assassinations, nor that non-Libyans 
have been targetted. 5
