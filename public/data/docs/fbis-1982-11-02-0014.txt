[Text] (Barbai) (northern Chad) Muharram 14, Oct. 31, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Poli- 
tical and military officials who had earlier formed the interim Chadian Government of 
National Unity, have now established a temporary political administration called the 
national peace government. The 15-men government is headed by Goukouni Oueddei and 
Oueddei's deputy is the father of Abdel Kader Kamougue, commander of the southern forces. 


The government of national peace was formed during a meeting held in the northern Chadian 
region of (Barbai) on October 27 and 28. 


Following the meeting, a press statement was issued saying those in attendance studied 
recent developments in Chad and affirmed the determination to carry out unified poli- 

tical and military action under the new governmertt whose members are to be revealed at 
a later date. 


The press statement outlined the following freedom fighting tasks: 


1. Fighting the unjust and terroristic regime of the forces of the north in a bid to 
bring back peace and unity to Chad. 


2. Unification of the fighting forces under a national army independent from political 
organisations. 


3. Organising all Chadian citizens opposed to the creation of a dictatorial government 
in Chad. 


Those convened in the meeting also decided on establishing a general staff command to 
coordinate military operations. 


The press statement affirmed that the national peace government under Goukouni Oueddei 
is intent on realising peace and democracy in Chad. The new government also appealed 
to the Chadian people to strive for unity and show support for the new administration 
to put an end to the tragedy caused by the Hissein Habre clique and their allies.

The press statement also appealed to all African and friendly countries not to recognise 
the blood-stained authority of Hissein Habre, and to stand beside the Chadian people in 
their struggle on all levels. 


It affirmed that the Chadian people support their legitimate officials in accordance with 
the 18th Organisation of African Unity's summit resolutions in Nairobi and the meeting 
of African heads of state in Tripoli last August. 


Those convened in the meeting further affirmed their adherence to the Lagos agreement 
for realising true peace and justice in Chad. 


On the other hand, the new chief-of-staff sent a message to the Chadian Army in which 
he praised the wide scale rejection by the armed forces of the northern forces’ dominance 
and their opting for the legitimate national peace government's officials. 


He also reiterated the determination of the army to defeat the enemies of the Chadian 
people and liberate Chad from slavery and dictatorship. 


He added, addressing the army: "You will be the Chadian liberation forces who will be 

the backbone for the launching of settlement activities and executing the main objective, 
which is to grant the Chadian people their basic rights, after facing the threat of mass 
liquidation and the horrors of fascism at the hands of the northern forces. Hissein 

Habre has placed the Chadian people under the regime of fear, a regime backed by the 
enemies of a free Africa and which refused all proposals for a settlement or reconciliation 
in order to realise security and stability for the Chadian people." 


He also confirmed the Chadian people's determination to step up the struggle against 
Habre's forces who symbolize violence and who should be crushed by fiercer violence 
because the time has come to put an @nd to the barbaric crimes and inhuman actions. 


The national liveration forces are a creation of the people's will and will from now on 
say no to injustice, dictatorship and U.S. imperialism, he added. 


The chief-of-staff then warned the northern forces against continuing their massacres, 
theft, extortion and illegal seizure of the citizens properties and said that from now 
on, the objective should be one, that of liberating Chad, securing freedom, democracy 
and justice and preserving by all means the independence of the Chadian people. 


He added: "You will be the fighters for freedom in order to restore and ensure the 
democratic system and wipe the tears from the eyes of the widows and orphans who have 
not been spared by the northern forces. In the light of this unity we shall achieve 
victory." 


Mr. Goukouni Oueddei had delivered a speech at the beginning of the meeting, following 

his appointment at the head of the government, in which he reiterated the firm determination 
to restore freedom and crush the enemies of the Chadian people who have shed the blood 

of its innocent children.
