Summary (AI): Transcript of a May 1980 ABC News interview with Muammar Qaddafi, in which he defends Libya's threats against exiles, the expulsion of American diplomats, oil-as-weapon remarks and arms purchases. It gives the Libyan regime's stated positions but no specific Tripoli sites.
• Qaddafi said the Revolutionary Committees held a conference at Benghazi University that decided to liquidate exiles who cooperate with foreign intelligence services.
• Qaddafi said Libya's People's Bureaus are not diplomatic missions.
• Qaddafi said Libya might cut oil supplies to the US and that the US support of Israel was one reason.
• Qaddafi said Libya bought large amounts of arms from the Soviet Union, and that the US encourages Egypt's Sadat to launch war against Libya.
• Clark cited about 2000 remaining Americans in Libya.
• 1980-05: Qaddafi says Revolutionary Committees, not he, would liquidate Libyan exiles who stay abroad; Libya deported 25 Americans and arrested two others on spying charges; the US expelled four Libyan People's Bureau members from Washington.
