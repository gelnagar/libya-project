COMMENTARY ON NEED FOR PALESTINIAN NATIONAL UNITY 
LD 72054 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 7 Dec 79 LD 
[Unattributed commentary ] 


[Excerpts] The Palestine question was never one of a conflict over borders between two 
countries. It is one of imperialist-Zionist usurpation and aggression which drove an 
entire people out of their homeland and brought Jewish settiers from their homelands to 
settle in this land, In such a case, compromises cannot retrieve the Palestinian Arab 
rights. 


To prevent the wheels of history from turning backward, to assure that the cause is not 
lost and the historic rights of the Palestinian Arab people are not aborted through the 
plots of capitulation, and to thwart the attempts to curb, domesticate and tame the 
resistance and force it to adapt to the corrupt Arab conditions and ride on the bandwagon 
of political solutions, the Palestinian masses must mobilize politically and militarily 
and organize revolutionarily in a manner that will guarantee for them the right to make 
Palestinian decisions, continue the armed struggle and fight the logic of custodianship, 
subservience and reconciliation with Arab conditions. 


Organization of all the Palestinian masses, wherever they may be, is the only way to 
rectify the Arab position so as to serve the Palestinian cause and not work against it. 
The Palestinian masses throughout the Arab homeland must be organized so that the home- 
land will protect the Palestine revolution and provide it with the fuel that will keep 
it burning. 


The Palestinian masses alone are responsible for the creation of national unity in their 

popular manner through the formation of the revolutionary committees, popular congresses { 
and general people's congresses, and the creation of popular leaderships that can imple- 
ment the will of the Palestinian masses and thus make popular decisions for the liberation 

of Palestine and impose their revolutionary logic over the Arab arena, 


Since the Great 1 September Revolution considers the Palestine question its principal 
cause, because of its revolutionary standi for the pan-Arabization of the battle for the 
liberation of Palestine, the leader of the revolution has opened the way to all sorts of 
Palestinian mass movements inside Libya so that the Palestinian masses may organize and 
prepare for liberation and for the continuation of the armed struggle and the rejection 
of the inaction which engulfs the current Arab state of affiars.

FORMATION OF PALESTINIAN REVOLUTIONARY COMMITTEES URGED 
LDO091935 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 9 Dec 79 LD 
[Station commentary] 


[Excerpts] Despite the work of more than 30 years, and particularly of the past 14 years, 
the Palestinian liberation struggle is still unable to attain the minimm of its strategic 
objectives. The Palestinian strategy has adopted the liberation of the land as a general 
base and the gun and armed struggle as the only means and legitimacy for the attainment 
of these objectives. 


When these two basic factors were betrayed by the resolution adopted at the 10th Arab 
summit conference providing for the withdrawal of Palestinian guns in the last area open 
to armed struggle in southern Lebanon, the shadow of this shortcoming manifested itself 
violently in attempts to arouse in the Palestinian popular masses and the entire Arab 
nation a spirit of revolution and pride despite the grave attempts to divert the Palestine 
revolution from its course. 


In the face of that resolution or Arab stance--if we can call it so that--which is directed 
against the last stronghold of Palestinian confrontation with the enemy and their most 
effective sphere of tackling the plot of capitulation and its traitorous course, the 

Great 1 September Revolution issued its call to the masses of the Palestinian people to 
take their popular stand out of the sphere of passive rejection into the sphere of 
revolutionary interaction and the point of explosion that is aware of the dangers of the 
stage and the responsibility of the masses! presence in the battle. 


The response of the Palestinian masses in the Jamahiriyah rose to the level of the 
committed revolutionary stand of the Great 1 September Revolution and the sincere proud 
call which offered to the masses of the Palestinian people an alternative to a state of 
affairs that was almost overtaken by events and devoured by their serious developments 
through a mere resolution. 


The call of the Great 1 September Revolution has come to the aid of the Palestinian masses 
in their hard stance or grave stance, if we can call it that, against this Arab resolution. 
It was a call for the formation by the Palestinian masses of revolutionary committees of 
their own and popular conferences of their own--the most idea example of what their 
liberation course should be, and what their stance of struggle should be in the face of 
the enemies of their existence and the plots set up in the way of their revolution. 


The Great 1 September Revolution's concern to safeguard the guns in the hands of the 
Palestinian fighters was the main factor and motive behind its call to the Palestinian 
masses to change their revolutionary presence in a manner that would secure for their 
revolution the guarantees of strength and dignity and for the course of their liberation 
struggle the means of continuity toward their strategic ‘objectives of liberation and 
return. 


The Great 1 September Revolution has honored its pledge to the masses of the Palestinian 
people and its commitment to their cause and armed revolution. Likewise, the bases of 
the great Palestinian people have lived up to the expectations and have manifested their 
extreme concern to safeguard the course and tools of their revolution on the path of 
liberation and return.

masses in Jamahiriyah block the way in the face of all those who aspire to dominate their

They undoubtedly provide all the guarantees and security valves for the Palestine

LIBYA ASKS PLO REPRESENTATIVE TO LEAVE 'WITHIN 24 HOURS! 
LDO91324 Kuwait KUNA in Arabic 1305 GMT 9 Dec 79 LD 


[Text] Aigiers, 9 December--The Libyan authorities have asked PLO representative in 
Tripoli Sulayman ash-Shurafa to leave the Jamahiriyah within 24 hours. 


This was disclosed to the KUWAITI NEWS AGENCY by PLO representative in Algeria Faruq 
Yunis, who added that Ash-Shurafa will be leaving for Tunis today. 


The Palestinian official expressed his belief that the deterioration of relations between 
Libya and the Palestinians will weaken the Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation, 
which includes Syria, Algeria, the PDRY and the Jamahiriyah and which was formed to 
combat President Anwar as-Sadat's peace policy toward Israel. 


Differences between the two sides escalated following the PLO's announcement that elements 
manipulated by the Libyan intelligence media have for a few days been besieging the PLO 
and Fatah offices in Libya with a view to occupying them. But the Libyan authorities 
denied this and said that those besieging the offices were Palestinians who had formed 
revolutionary committees in response to Al-Qadhdhafi's call. 


Faruq Yunis appealed to the "fraternal Libyan leadership to put and end to the siege 
around the premises over which the Palestinian flag is hoisted." He reiterated the PLO!s 
statement that elements "belonging to the Libyan authorities” were responsible for 
organizing the siege. Faruq Yunis added: "We are now passing through a grave stage 
fraught with plots from all sides, and consequently it is not right for the Libyan 
Jamahriyah to take such a grave turn." 


Yunis added that Palestinian-Libyan relations have always been "stronger than all conflicts 
and that the Palestine revolution and the Libyan revolution are members of the Front of 
Steadfastness and Confrontation. We believe this matter will only weaken the front, which 
has assumed the role of thwarting the Camp David capitulationist plot with full force."
