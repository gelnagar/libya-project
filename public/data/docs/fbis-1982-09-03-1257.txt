LIBYA

JANA CITES REAGAN TV SPEECH ON MIDDLE EAST 


LD022238 Tripoli JANA in English 1920 GMT 2 Sep 82 


[Text] Washington, Dhulqidah 14, September 2, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Ronald Reagan, 
the American President, announced that the USA is opposed to the establishment of an 
independent Palestinian state over the occupied West Bank and Gaza. He added that Camp 
David accords and the Security Council Resolution No. 242 providing for the non-political 
rights of the Palestinian refugees constitute the cornerstone in the policy to be pursued 
by his administration in the next stage of the conflict in the Arab region. 


Reagan suggested the establishment of a Palestinian self-rule government over the West 
Bank and Gaza on condition that it is linked to Jordan. Concerning occupied Jerusalem, 
Reagan said Jerusalem should be united but its final status should be decided by 
negotiations between the Arabs and Zionists. 


Reagan continued in the speech made today and broadcast on American television networks, 

that the USA will oppose any proposal from any party at any stage of the negotiations if 
such proposal threatens the security of the Zionist entity. He described this position 

as a solid commitment representing his own personal commitment as well. 


The American President expressed his personal admiration of the policy of aggression 
pursued by the Zionist enemy. He said he personally admires the wars waged by the 
Zionists during the last 34 years against the Arab nation. 


Reagan opened his speech by saying that this day is worthy of our pride as it registers 
the end of the evacuation of the PLO from Lebanon. Reagan said: "I am very pleased the 
U.S. Marines carried out the eviction of the Palestinians from Beirut successfully." 

He said: "The PLO in the area is determined by two factors, one is the strategic 
importance of the area to the USA and the second is the U.S. commitment to Israel's 
security." 


He added, "The Palestinians and the Arabs should recognize the Zionist entity." This 
statement states clearly the future policy of the USA in the Arab region He only 
mentioned the Palestinian people's rights in the context of the autonomy rule.
