Summary (AI): A 1983 New York Times article reports that the Reagan administration sent four AWACS surveillance planes to Egypt over fears of a Libyan military move against Sudan, and that the carrier Nimitz moved toward Egypt and Libya. It concerns Libya's regional conflicts and US foreign policy, not Tripoli itself.
• Four AWACS planes with electronic surveillance and communications gear were sent to Egypt.
• Officials said Libya was moving Soviet-built aircraft to southern Libya and bases in Chad, prompting the AWACS decision.
• The USS Nimitz, the only carrier attached to the Sixth Fleet, maneuvered away from Lebanon toward Egypt and Libya.
• In August 1981 two US Navy jets shot down two Libyan planes over the Gulf of Sidra.
• The officially stated cover story was joint training exercises.
