LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI, DELEGATION LEAVE FOR PRC, DPRK 
Stopover in Sofia 
LD231814 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1708 GMT 23 Oct 82 


[Text] Sofia, 23 Oct (JANA) -- The brother leader and an accompanying delegation stopped 
over in the Bulgarian capital this afternoon en route to the People's Republic of China, 
where he will pay an official visit lasting several days. He then will pay a working 
visit to the Democratic People's Republic of Korea. 


A big welcoming reception was staged at Sofia airport for the brother leader. Welcoming 
him were: the prime minister, member of the Bulgarian Communist Party Central Committee 
Politburo [as received], members of the Politburo, ministers of the Bulgarian Government, 
high-ranking army officials, and the Arab community which received the leader with 

chants of appreciation and welcome. 


The brother leader left the Jamahiriyah at noon today on a working visit to the People's 
Republic of China and to the Democratic People's Republic of Korea in response to official 
invitations from the heads of both countries. 


The brother leader is accompanied by a delegation including Brothers Brig Gen Mustafa 
al-Kharrubi, chief of the staff of the armed forces, ‘Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary 
of the General People's Committee for Foreign Liaison, Musa Abu Furaywah, secretary of 
the General People's Committee for Economy and Light Industries, Abu Zayd ‘Umar Durdah, 
secretary of the General People's Committee for Agrarian Reform and Land Reclamation, 
Al-Bukhari Salim Hudah, secretary of the General People's Committee for Communications 
and Maritime Transport, and a number of experts and technicians from various committees 
and public institutions. 


Stopover in USSR 
LD2418290 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1750 GMT 24 Oct 82 
[Excerpt] Moscow, 24 Oct (JANA) -- The leader of the revolution tomorrow will start an 
official and friendly visit to the People's Republic of China, which will last a 


few days. 


This afternoon the leader andhis accompanying delegation stopped over in Irkutsk, southern 
Siberia, in the USSR on his way to Beijing. 


The leader was met by the secretary of the Central Committee of the regional communist 
party, the first deputy of the regional head of government and the secretary and members 
of the Libyan Arab People's Bureau in the Soviet Union. 


JANA CRITICIZES HASSAN DELEGATION, REAGAN POLICY 


LD241946 Tripoli JANA in English 1645 GMT 24 Oct 82 


[Text] Washington, Muharram 7, Oct 24, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- in a dogfight-like 
atmosphere to surrender and acquire the defeatist spirit under the slogans of peace and 
just solution, King Hassan and the Fes delegation presented Reagan with a crystal dove. 


The White House then said that this gift is a reward for Reagan's efforts to realise 

what he calle peace in the Middle East. The reality for Arab and international public 
opinion is that Reagan, through his policies and actions, is adopting a hostile policy 
towards the Arab nation and is implementing programmes of conspiracy and terrorism against 
it as he stands by the Zionist enemy and supervises in person the implementation of 
sabotage plans in Asia and Africa.
