DISPOSITION OF LIBYAN FORCES IN CHAD (SANITIZED)

https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-cia-rdp84t00171r000201410001-4

Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing attachment shows the disposition of Libyan ground and air forces in northern Chad, including units at Faya Largeau, Ounianga Kebir, Ogoui and Aozou. It has no Tripoli-specific content.
• Libyan forces in Chad reportedly included elements of three mechanized infantry battalions, one probable motorized infantry battalion, two tank companies, three heavy-lift battalions, ten 130mm M-46 field guns, two 122mm D-30 howitzer batteries and one BM-21 rocket launcher battery.
• Libyan combat aircraft in Chad reportedly included seven Mirage F-1, 23 Fitter (Su-22), 20 SF.260 light-strike aircraft, nine Hind attack helicopters and three Hip helicopters.
• Air defence assets reportedly included five SA-9 systems and ZSU-23/4 radar, with flat face and spoon rest early warning radars.
• 1983-07: Libyan mechanized and motorized infantry units introduced in late July, escalating the conflict with Chadian government forces; Libyan ground units pushed south to Faya Largeau.
