LIBYA

POLITICAL EDITOR COMMENTS ON HAIG STATEMENT 


LD301948 Tripoli JANA in English 1810 GMT 30 May 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 30 May, (JAMAHTRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The U.S. secretary of state expressed 
anxiety on what he called Libyan military activity in Lebanon and described the support 
by Al-Fatah Revolution for the Palestinian revolution as an act of terrorism. Our 
political edit>r commented on General Haig's statement saying: "This American statement 
reveals the child-like, misconceived and arrogant American mentality."
