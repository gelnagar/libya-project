LIBYA

SOVIET ECONOMIC OFFICIAL DEPARTS TRIPOLI 
1D271658 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 27 Dec 79 ID 
[Excerpt] Tripoli--V. Mordvinov, deputy chairman of the Soviet State Committee for 
Foreign Economic Relations and head of the Soviet side to the joint Arab LibyanrSoviet 
committee, left Tripoli early this morning at the end of a wisit to the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, during which bilateral relations in various fields were discussed.
