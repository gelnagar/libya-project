LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI APPEALS FOR END TO IRAQI-IRANIAN FIGHTING 
Messages to Khomeyni, Husayn 
LD232020 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1800 GMT 23 Sep 80 


{[Text] Tripoli, 23 Sep (JANA)--The brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has addressed two 
cables, one to Ayatollah Khomeyni and one to the Iraqi President Saddam Husayn, following 
the escalation of the armed clashes between the Iranian and the Iraqi forces, in which 

he expressed his concern and regret for the fighting which is now raging betwen the forces 
of the two countries. He said that the fighting now raging between the Muslims has aroused 
our concern and much regret since this fighting serves the interests of no one but America 
and the Israelis, and that its outcome will only be in the interest of the common enemy. 


In his cables the brother colonel added that the lives which are being wasted and the 
weapons which are being destroyed on both sides could have been saved for the liberation 
of Jerusalem, the qiblah of the Muslims and the capital of Palestine. He went on to say 
that there will be no martyrs in this battle since both the killer and the killed will go 
to hell. The brother colonel appealed to the Iraqi president and to Imam Khomeyni to put 
an end to the fighting between the Muslims and to save all the capabilities of both Muslim 
countries for the battle of honor against the Zionist enemy. 


Messages to Islamic Leaders 
LD240900 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 24 Sep 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Sep (JANA)--The brother leader has sent messages to the kings and 
presidents of the Islamic states calling for urgent intervention to halt the fighting 
between Iraq and Iran, for maintaining the unity of the Islamic nation and for providing 
all means for the battle against the enemy. 


In his message the leader stressed that this fighting will only benefit the enemies of 
the Islamic nation and only serves colonialist interests. The brother leader also 
appealed for the necessary immediate intervention to put a halt to this fighting, to 
work for a settlement of the dispute through peaceful means and to hold urgent contacts 
in this connection. 


Appeal to Castro 


LD240902 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 24 Sep 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Sep (JANA)--The foreign secretary of the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah has held urgent contacts with the secretariat of the Islamic Conference 
Organization regarding a speedy intervention to end the fighting between the Muslim 
brothers in Iran and Iraq. 


Contacts also took place with Cuban President Fidel Castro, in his capacity as the present 
chairman of the nonaligned group movement, to immediately intervene to halt the fighting 
taking place between the two nonaligned states, Iraq and Iran, and to work to solve the 
dispute between them through peaceful means within the framework of the spirit and 
principles of the nonaligned movement. 


V. 24 Sep 80 NORTH AFRICA 


BENIN PRESIDENT ARRIVES 23 SEP FOR VISIT 


LD232138 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1800 GMT 23 Sep 80 


[Summary] Tripoli, 23 Sep (JANA)--Benin President Mathieu Kerekou arrived in Tripoli this 
evening on a visit to SPLAJ [Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyan]. He was welcomed 

at the airport by Col Muammar al-Qadhdhadi, Capt al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi, Lt Col Mustafa 
al-Kharrubi and others. The president is accompanied by a delegation including ministers 

of foreign affairs, housing and agriculture. 


In a statement to JANA, President Kerekou hailed SPLAJ achievements and the merger be- 
tween Libya and Syria. He said that Africa is exposed to an imperialist invasion by U.S. 
imperialism and its allies. The visit is aimed at foiling imperialist plans.

"ARAFAT, DELEGATION ARRIVE IN TRIPOLI 23 SEP 
LD231010 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 23 Sep 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 23 Sep (JANA)--Brother Yasir ‘Arafat, chairman of the PLO Executive 
Committee, arrived in Tripoli yesterday accompanied by a delegation which includes secre- 
taries of the Palestinian resistance factions. They are Nayif Hawatimah, secretary general 
of the Democratic Front for the Liberation of Palestine [DPFLP]; Dr Samir Ghawshah, secre- 
tary general of the Palestinian Popular Struggle Front; Abu Mahir al-Yamani, member of 

the Political Bureau of the Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine [PELP]; Fadhl 
Shurur, member of the Political Bureau of the Popular Front-General Command; and 'Isam 
al-Qadi, secretary general of the As-Sa'iqah Front. 


Brother ‘Arafat made a statement to the JANA representative at Tripoli international air- 

port in which he said: Our visit to the Jamahiriyah comes at a time when the Arab Jamahiriyah 
is celebrating the declaration of the establishment of the unity merger between our people 

in Syria and in the Jamahiriyah. He added: We in the Palestinian revolution consider 

this unity to be an important turning point in the present circumstances when our Arab 

nation and the Palestinian revolution is facing dangerous cultural challenges and when the 
enemy is directing its land, sea and air forces in the ugliest assault against the Lebanese 
and Palestinian people in southern Lebanon using the most modern weapons--weapons banned 
internationally--and when it is increasing its brutality and terrorism against our people 

in Palestine and the occupied Arab lands. 


Brother Yasir ‘Arafat stressed that this unity gives us new hope in confronting these 
dangerous colonialist-Zionist challenges. He said: We are confident that from this pan- 
Arab starting point we will achieve victory against the enemies of our nation and will 
foil the defeatist Camp David agreements, and that our Palestinian Arab people will defeat 
the dirty, tripartite self-rule plots hatched by Carter, Begin and As-Sadat.
