CZECH SELF-PROPELLED GUN/HOWITZER TARABULUS ARMY BARRACKS PRINN, LIBYA (SANITIZED)

Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery note dated 31 August 1983 reports five Czechoslovak 152mm M-1978 self-propelled guns/howitzers stored in front of a vehicle shed at the Tarabulus (Tripoli) Army Barracks at Prinn, Libya. The weapon was newly observed in Libya, and the equipment was probably shipped to Tripoli port by a Krakow-class merchant ship seen at Ploce, Yugoslavia.
• The Czechoslovakian 152mm self-propelled gun/howitzer M-1978 was newly observed in Libya; this was the first observation of the weapon outside Czechoslovakia.
• Coordinates given for the barracks: 32-50-46N 013-14-13E.
• The barracks is 5 nautical miles southeast of Tarabulus port facilities.
• The document is a NPIC attachment (Z-13969/83), copy NPIC/IEG, dated 31 August 1983.
• The document is partly sanitized and much of the text is redacted or garbled by OCR.
• 1983-08-31: Imagery observed five Czechoslovak 152mm M-1978 self-propelled guns/howitzers in front of a vehicle storage shed.
• 1983: A Czechoslovak Krakow-class merchant ship at port facilities, with five guns/howitzers on railcars observed.
