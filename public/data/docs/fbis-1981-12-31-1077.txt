LIBYA

SAUDI ARABIA DECIDES TO RESTORE RELATIONS 


LD310802 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0750 GMT 31 Dec 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 31 Dec (JANA) -- The Popular Bureau for Foreign Liaison in Tripoli 
today issued the following statement: 


Out of their anxiousness to cast away differences, repair the rift in Arab relations 
and unify Arab action against the common enemy, the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah and the Saudi Arabian Kingdom have decided to restore diplomatic relations 
between them.
