FRG'S FOREIGN MINISTER ENDS VISIT 20 JUN 
LD201524 Tripoli JANA in English 1436 GMT 20 Jun 79 LD 
[Text] Tripoli, June 20, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The foreign minister of the Federal 
Republic of Germany, Herr Hans-Dietrich Genscher, left Tripoli this morning after a 
twoeday visit to the Jamahiriyah, 
He was seen off at Tripoli International Airport by Foreign Secretary Dr ‘Ali 'Abd 
as-Salam at-Turayki, some heads of departments at the Foreign Secretariat, the 
ambassador of the Federal Republic of Germany to the Jamahiriyah and the Jamahiriyah's 
charge d'affaires to Federal Germany, 
Herr Genscher had arrived in Tripoli on Monday evening, on a visit to the Jamahiriyah 
during which he held talks with Dr at-Turayki on bilateral relations and means of 
promoting them, as well as the current international issues, 
Meanwhile the sub-committees for cooperation between the two countries held several 
meetings which dealt wich the framework of cooperation and the possibility of develop- 
ing it between the Jamahiriyah and the Federal Republic of Germany,

AT-TURAYKI RECEIVES LETTER FROM KUWAIT FOREIGN MINISTER 


LD211856 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 21 Jun 79 LD 


[Text] Foreign Secretary Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki has received a letter from 
Sabah al-Ahmad al-Jabir as-Sabah, Kuwaiti deputy prime minister and foreign minister, 
stressing that the passage of enemy naval units through the Suez Canal constitutes 
defiance of the feelings of our Arab nation and a stab at our long struggle. He also 
expressed complete confidence that this will be a motive for us to close ranks and 
stand in solidarity so as to confront all dangers facing our nation,

Brother Sabah al-Ahmad al-Jabir as-Sabah also stressed that this defiance will only in- 
crease the determination of our nation to regain all its rights, and that commitment to 

the Baghdad conference resolutions constitutes a shield for the interests of the Arab nation 
and support for its struggle. Kuwait is determined to go ahead in implementing these 
resolutions, in view of the support for the Arab joint action this entails, so as to 

insure the unity of the Arab stand in confronting the dangers and challenges threatening 

the Arab nation.
