LD090147 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
0015 GMT 9 Jan 84 


{Text} AFP, REUTER, American television, TASS, and (?the 
SYRIAN NEWS AGENCY) have had an interview with the 
brother leader of the revolution in which he spoke about a number 
of Arab and international issues. In the interview the leader 
touched upon the subject of the withdrawal of American troops 
and the Multinational Force from Lebanon and their 
replacement by forces from smaller countries under the aegis of 
the United Nations, stressing in this respect the preparedness of 
the Jamahiriyah to send troops to Lebanon to prevent fighting 
among the Lebanese after the withdrawal of these forces. 


In this press interview the leader aiso dealt with the deployment 
of the U.S. nuclear cruise missiles in the Comiso base in southern 
Italy, pointing out that we will face this in solidarity with the 
Italian people, who reject the presence of these missiles, which 
constitute a danger to the existence of the Italian people in the 
event of war. 


The leader also discussed the situation that prevailed in fraternal 
Tunisia in recent days, revealing that the U.S. Administration 
had been bracing itself for intervention in Tunisia during the 
events. He stressed that monitoring (?services) intercepted com- 
munications ordering 6th Fleet units to move toward Tunisian 
shores to exploit the situation and to entrench US. forces in the 
Arab Maghreb as they did in Lebanon. 


The brother leader also spoke about the recent developments 
within the Fatah organization and ‘Arafat's visit and meeting 
with the head of the Egyptian regime, stressing that ‘Arafat is 
considered finished, at least as far as Fatah is concerned. 


The brother leader of the revolution touched upon Libyan- 
American relations and the hostility harbored by the current 
U.S. Administration toward the Arab Libyan people, explaining 
that the whole world is praying for peace and for America to get 
rid of Reagan, because he is going to cause a disaster for America 
due to his aggressive ‘leanings and his militarist policy. 


Jallud on ‘New Fatah,’ ‘Arafat Leadership 
LDO72321 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2115 GMT 7 Jan 84 


[Excerpts] On the 19th anniversary of the Palestinian revolution, 
Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud kas saluted the new 
revolutionary Fatah movement. He said the emergence of the 
movement was a courageous and great event which will be 
immortalized by the coming generations of Palestinians and 
Arabs. 


In a speech at a popular rally held yesterday evening in Damas- 
cus, he explained that the new Fatah revolutionary movement 
made change possible and also the burial of the cycle of defeat, 
treason, and surrender. Maj Jallud also saluted the Palestinian 


alliance which embraces the PFLP-GC, the Palestine Liberation 
Front, and the As-Sa‘igah organization with the Fatah rev- 
Olutionary movement. 


Staff Maj Jallud warned of attempts which are taking place to 
encircle the revolutionary uprising within Fatah and to contain 
it in order to prevent this historical event from taking its course 
as a historical transformation in favor of the Palestinian rev- 
olutionary movement and the pan-Arab liberation movement. He 
stressed that the people of the Jamahiriyah and the revolutionary 
committees movement oppose these attempts. They believe this 
historic action will take its course and lead to (? rejuvenating) the 
Palestinian and Arab revolutions and the pan-Arab unionist 
movement. 


Staff Maj Jallud declared that the new Fatah revolutionary 
movement is Fatah and that the leadership of the uprising in 
Fatah is the legitimate leadership of Fatah. He called on the rest 
of the misguided Fatah cadres to join the brothers in the uprising 
and (?strengthen) the militant course of Fatah and the Palestin- 
ian revolution. He said that the 19th anniversary of the Palest:n- 
ian revolution is the anniversary of rejuvenating the revolution, 
the anniversary of the militant tide, the anniversary of holding 
on to the rifle and to martyrdom and to upholding the vow to 
liberate Palestine from the river to the sea. 


Staff Maj Jallud refuted the attempt to separate the Central 
Committee from Yasir ‘Arafat and refuted the statement by 
some people that the Fatah Central Committee is not (?controled) 
by Yasir ‘Arafat. He said ‘Arafat did not go to Egypt or meet 
with Husni Mubarak until after consulting the Central Commit- 
tee and obtaining its approval. He stressed the need for the other 
progressive Palestinian groups to decide their stand toward the 
reactionary leadership in Fatah and said they should make up 
their minds over the need to topple the right-wing leadership in 
its entirety and to overthrow the capitulationist-reactionary- 
rightist trend. He said they must shoulder their historic respon- 
sibility before the Palestinian masses and before the Palestinian 
martyrs and before the Arab nation and its honorable fighiers. 


Staff Maj Jallud stressed the need to agree to a plan to conduct 
struggle against the Zionist-imperialist enemy. He denounced 
the treacherous aerial aggression by Zionist warplanes against 
defenseless citizens in Ba‘labakk which caused death and injury 
to many. Hesaid: In the Jamahiriyah, we feel pain for this ugly 
massacre which the Zionist enemy has committed. He stressed 
the imperative need to put up resolute opposition to this Zionist 
brigandry with all the means of resistance. 


Staff Maj Jallud noted the genuine mass upsurge demonstrated 
in the steadfast stand by Syria and the stand of the Fatah uprising 
and the Palestinian alliance. He explained that this upsurge is 
the decisive answer to the circle of treason and to the agreements 
of surrender and to Zionism and imperialism. 


He said that the movement of popular and mass upsurge began 
with the outbreak of the courageous uprising, together with the 
alliance and with the stand of Syria and the Jamahiriyyah 
alongside this uprising. 


Jallud warned against (?Zionist-imperialist) reactionary 
attempts to benefit from the meeting between ‘Arafat and 
Mubarak to break the isolation of the Egyptian regime and to 
pan-Arabize Camp David. H- warned that 1984 will be the year 
of the masses and the year of their wrath — the year of the 
masses’ revenge against capitulationist and defeatist regimes and 
the year of masses deluge and the year of (? revenge). 


Iranian Official Meets With Al-‘Ubaydi 
LDO81222 Tehran Domestic Service in Persian 1030 GMT 
8 Jan 84 


[Text] Having ended his visit to Syria, the political under secre- 
tary of the Foreign Ministry, leading a delegation, arrived in 
Libya, where he met and conferred with ‘Abd al-‘Ati al-“Ubaydi, 
the secretary of foreign liaison. In the course of the meeting the 
Islamic Republic's stands and viewpoints in connection with its 
refusal to attend the Islamic summit conference in Morocco and 
the region's current developments were explained. The two sides 
also discussed and exchanged views on plots hatched by America 
and other powers aimed against returning Egypt to the Islamic 
and Arab world, conspiratorial attempts made within the context 
of the Jordan-Egypt-Iraq axis against the Islamic Republic of 
Iran in line with the promotion of Camp David and efforts to 
compromise the PLO. During the 2-hour meeting, they also 
discussed the problems faced by Lebanon and the Iragi-imposed 
war on the Islamic Republic of Iran, with both sides emphasizing 
maximum cooperation between the Islamic Republic of Iran and 
the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah.
