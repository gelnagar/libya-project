Summary (AI): A 1981 Daily Telegraph article reports an American former Green Beret, Luke Thompson, saying he met British mercenaries in Libya who ran a homemade whisky operation while serving Gaddafi's forces, recruited by Edwin Wilson. The meeting with Libya's intelligence chief was in Tripoli in July 1977 at the Ministry of Trade.
• Mercenaries reportedly made their own whisky in Tripoli because alcohol was banned.
• Wilson's London company was called Brilburst, with an office in Hans Road, Knightsbridge.
• Pay offered was $5,300 a month with a $10,000 bonus at the end of a year.
• London was reportedly a pivotal stop for Wilson's operation, used for supplies, orders and hand-carrying cash.
• 1977-07: About 17 mercenaries summoned to a meeting at the Ministry of Trade with Libyan intelligence chief Abdullah Sanussi, who placed them under Cant. Hadjazi's command.
