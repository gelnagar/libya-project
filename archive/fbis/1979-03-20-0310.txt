{Text ] Tripeli 17 March, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 
Great First of September Revolution, received a message from the Chad President Felix 
Malleum following the conclusion of the Chad reconciliation conference held at Kano in 
Nigeria, in which he expressed his appreciation for the efforts and effective participa- 
tion of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah delegation in Kano's conference, 
In his message to Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, Malloum said that a settlement was reached, 
thanks to the efforts made by the Libyan Arab delegation, for a sound basis for under- 
standing in Chad. Malloum's message comes after reaching an accord in the Kano confer- 
ence, in which the Jamahiriyah and political forces in Chad participated. The agreement 
stipulates a cease-fire in Chad, the disarmament of Ndjamena, and withdrawal of forces 
100 kilometers from the capital. 


The accord also stipulates the formation of a committee for controlling the cease-fire, 
which includes the Jamahiriyah and participant African countries in the Kano conference, 
as well as the formation ef a neutral force to undertake the maintenance of peace and 
stability during the transitional period, and disolution of existing organizations in 
Chad: the republic's Presidency and government, the Defence and Security Council, the 
National Union's Council and the formation of a transitional government including repre- 
sentatives from all Chad parties. The accords also stipulate the issuing of a general 
pardon which includes all political prisoners, hostages and exiles. Meanwhile, politi- 
cal observers affirm that these accords will not completely solve problems in Chad. 

They also believe that the popular forces in Chad will free themselves the slavery ef 
authority [as heard].

[Text] Paris, March 19, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--In an interview with AFRIQUE-ASIE magazine 
published today, Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the Great First of 

September Revolution, called upon France to stop interfering in the internal affairs of 
Chad and put an end to its occupation there. France, he said, should withdraw her forces 
from Chad and let the Chadian people solve their own problems.

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi stressed that there were no Libyan forces inside Chad and added: 
"Tt is regrettable that French soldiers should die while attempting to stop the victory 
of the Chadian revolution against the ruling regime and the French forces behind it, a 
victory which I foresee as inevitable." 


Colenel al-Qadhdhafi asked: "What would happen if one of the other parties called for 
military intervention from an ally?" 


Referring to the many reports on the Jamahiriyah's support for the revolution in Chad, 
he said: "In the wake of the lst of September Revolution, we have assisted the revolu- 
tion in Chad which represents a nation rebelling against the racism of fascist masters 
and those seeking to control and exploit the country's natural resources for the 
interests of neo-colonialism." Colonel al-Qadhdhafi explained the basis of this 
revolution, saying that it is an armed struggle by the oppressed against exploitation, 
by the poor against the rich, and by slaves against masters. 


Colonel aleQadhdhafi spoke of the intensive efforts exerted by the Jamahiriyah to 

bring about peace and a constructive and permanent solution to Chad based on respect 
for all the rights and duties of all parties involved. He said: "This peace has not 
been achieved due to the maneuvres of those eager to suppress the revolution in order 
to safeguard their control over the country. Hence the collaboration between Habre and 
Malloum. We were quite sure then that they would disagree and end up by destroying

Chadian territories. However we knew that the problem was overcome." [sentence as

Chad. He said: "We shall reach a peaceful solution to the border dispute concerning 
Aozou, once a popular and stable government is formed in Chad. I am fully confident 
that a solution will be found in keeping with the interests of our people, for there 
is no preblem without a solution between us." 


The leader of the Great First ef September Revolution, Colonel Mu*ammar al-Qadhdhafi,

REPORT ON JALLUD'S STATEMENT ON ALGIERS TALKS 
LD181522 Tripoli JANA in English 1420 GMT 18 Mar 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Mar 18, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud 
returned to Tripoli yesterday evening after concluding a short visit to Algeria. 


Present at the farewell of Major Jallud in Algiers! Dar El Beida Airport was Politburo 
member and Algerian National Liberation Front party coordinator, Colonel Mohamed Salal 
Yahiaoui, and the Jamahiriyah's ambassador to Algeria. 


Upon his arrival from Algiers, Staff Major Jallud made a statement to newsmen. He said 
his visit came within the context of brotherly relations binding both revolutions...the 
Great First of September and Algerian Revolutions. This meeting, he said, is necessitated 
by the current extraordinary circumstances which the Arab nation is experiencing. 


He said the Great First of September and Algerian Revolutions represent the progressive 
and revolutionary assets of the Arab nation. The Arab masses, he said, look up to these 
two revolutions for guidance after the shameful accord between the defeated Egyptian 
president and the Zionists concluded under American imperialist pressure, incentive and 
(?bribery). 


He said: "In order that these treasonous accords become a reality, the conspirators 
will try to strike revolutionary movements, because they represent the forces capable of 
foiling this conspiracy..." 


He added: "Revolutionary and progressive forces in the Arab world should not recoil, but 
should conduct quick and wide-ranging contacts amongst themselves, because they are the 
Pulsating forces of the Arab nation. It is within this context, therefore, that our two 
allied revolutions meet..." 


Major Jallud said: "Colonialism will attempt to strike revolutionary centers in the 
Arab nation through the escalation of Arab reaction's conspiratorial role. This it will 
attempt as a means of diverting the attention of these revolutionary forces from their 
Progressive role of leadership of the Arab masses and to block them from fulfilling 
their patriotic role of exposing colonialism and reaction's conspiratorial schemes 
against the future and existence of the Arab nation." 


He added: "We should, as revolutionary Arabs, expose this conspiracy, and our revolutionary 
forces should gain the initiative and proceed on all fronts to crush it..." "Revolutionary 
forces," he said, "are the sharp edge and final word in determining our fateful causes..." 


Major Jallud said: "If these revolutionary forces and Arab masses move, then they will be 
able to crush all conspiracies against them and trample over all capitulationist and 
treasonous accords..." 


Major Jallud said: "If everyone was satisfied with mere condemnations and protests 
printed in newspapers or in statements and communiques to that effect, ournation will 

not forgive us and history will show no mercy...We, as revolutionary forces, are required 
to act. Therefore, we have hastened contact with our closest allies and brethren in 


the Algerian revolution..."

JANA CITES FRENCH MAGAZINE ON EGYPT'S PLANNED AGGRESSION 


LD191544 Tripoli JANA in English 1430 GMT 18 Mar 79 LD 


["Defeated President Plans Aggression Against the Jamahiriyah"--JANA headline] 


[Text] Paris, March 19, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The French magazine AFRIQUE-ASIE said 
that the defeated president of Egypt had recently held a meeting with his deputy, Husni 
Mubarak, and his minister of defence, Kamal Hasan 'Ali, in order to plan an aggression 
against the Jamahiriyah. A week prior to his meeting, the magazine said, the Egyptian de- 
fence minister made several inspection visits to the Egyptian borders with the Jamahiriyah 
to observe the Egyptian military build up there. This new Egyptian build up, AFRIQUE- 
ASIE pointed out, is much larger than the one of the 1977 summer when the defeated 
Egyptian president gave orders to his air force to bombard Jamal 'Abd an-Nasir Base and 
other neighboruing areas on the Jamahiriyah borders. 


Quoting the B.B,C. correspondent in the Egyptian capital, the magazine said that squadrons 
of Egyptian fighters reinforced by 600 tanks will stay in that area permanently. Egypt- 
ian forces on the Jamahiriyah's border, the magazine said, were transferred from the front 
line with the (Israeli) enemy, following the defeat of the Egyptian president.
