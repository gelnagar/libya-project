JANA: AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES AFRICAN ENVOYS ON NATIONAL DAY 
LD260949 
TRIPOLI JANA IN ARABIC 0700 GMT 26 MAY 85 
((TEXT)) TRIPOLI, 26 MAY (JANA) -- YESTERDAY EVENING THE 
BROTHER LEADER OF THE REVOLUTION RECEIVED HEADS OF THE AFRICAN 
DIPLOMATIC MISSIONS ACCREDITED IN LIBYA ON THE OCCASION OF 
AFRICA DAY. 
IN A BRIEF ADDRESS TO THE HEADS OF THE AFRICAN DIPLOMATIC 
MISSIONS THE LEADER SPOKE ABOUT HIS RECENT VISIT TO CENTRAL 
AFRICA WHICH INCLUDED RWANDA, BURUNDI, AND SUDAN AND HE APOLOGIZED 
FOR NOT BEING ABLE TO ACCEPT THE INVITATION FROM PRESIDENT 
OF GABON EL HADJ OMAR BONGO. 
THE LEADER NOTED THAT THE COUNTRIES OF CENTRAL AFRICA DO 
NOT SUFFER FROM DROUGHT AND THAT THEY HAVE VAST UNUTILIZED WATER 
RESOURCES. THE LEADER SAID: IT IS REGRETTABLE THAT AT THE TIME 
I WAS VISITING THESE COUNTRIES TO DISCUSS AFRICAN ISSUES, THE 
PRESIDENT OF ZAIRE WAS IN OCCUPIED PALESTINE MEETING WITH THE 
RACIST ZIONISTS -- ALLIES.OF THE WHITE RACISTS IN SOUTH AFRICA. 
THE LEADER POINTED OUT THAT BOTH THE PRESIDENTS OF RWANDA 
AND BURNIDI HAVE POINTED TO THE NEED FOR THE ARABS TO CONTACT 
MOBUTU BEFORE ADOPTING ANY DEFINITE STANCE AGAINST HIM FOR RESTORING 
HIS RELATIONS WITH THE ZIONIST OCCUPIERS AS A RESULT OF A 
MISUNDERSTANDING SINCE HE ((MOBUTU -- FBIS)) BELIEVED THAT THE 
ARABS BEGAN TO ACCEPT THE ESTABLISHMENT OF RELATIONS WITH THE 
ZIONIST ENEMY IN THE WAKE OF EGYPT'S SUBMISSION AND ITS DEFEAT 
DURING THE RAMADAN WAR AHD THE FORCIBLY IMPOSED REOCGNITION OF IT 
AT THE TENT OF SHAME 101 ((KILOMETER 101 -- FBIS)). HE ALSO 
POINTED TO THE STATEMENTS MADE BY THE FOREIGN MINISTER OF ZAIRE 
WHO SAID THAT MOBUTU WENT TO OCCUPIED PALESTINE TO DEFEND THE ARAB 
CAUSE AND THE MINISTER HAS REQUESTED PEOPLE NOT TO BE HASTY IN 
JUDGING ZAIRE. THE LEADER SAID THAT ON THE ADVICE OF THE BROTHER 
PRESIDENTS OF RWANDA AND BURUNDI, THE ARAB LEAGUE SECRETARY 
GENERAL WILL VISIT ZAIRE AND WE HOPE THAT THIS ARAB ENDEAVOR 
WILL RECEIVE A RESPONSE FORM THE PRESIDENT OF ZAIRE TO 
RECONSIDER HIS DECISION TO RESTORE RELATIONS WITH THE ZIONIST 
ENEMY. THE LEADER HAS ASSERTED THAT AS ARABS WE ARE 
CONFRONTING AN ENEMY WHO THREATENS OUR EXISTENCE AND WE SHALL 
NOT ACCEPT FROM ANY RGEIME -- PARTICULARLY FROM OUR AFRICAN 
BROTHERS -- THE ESTABLISHMENT OF RELATIONS WITH THE ZIONIST ENEMY 
AND WE CANNOT COMPROMISE ON THIS QUESTION. 
THE LEADER THEN SAID THAT AFRICA DAY THIS YEAR COMES WHEN 
THE PALESTINIAN PEOPLE ARE FACING A NEW TRAGEDY. WHAT THE ZIONISTS 
HAVE FAILED TO ACHIEVE IS NOW BEING ACHIEVED BY ARABS AGAINST 
PALESTINIANS. AT A TIME WHEN THE WORLD IS STILL AFFECTED BY THE 
MASSACRES OF SABRA AND SHATILA AT THE HANDS OF SHARON, AND BEFORE 
THIS WOUND HAS HEALED, THESE MASSACRES ARE BEING REPEATED BY 
ARAB SHARONITES.

THE LEADER SAID: THE PALESTINE SALVATION FRONT HAS ASSURED ME 
THAT THE JUSTIFICATIONS WHICH WERE CITED BY THOSE RESPONSIBLE FOR 
THESE MASSACRES WHICH ARE BEING COMMITTED NOWADAYS AGAINST THE 
PALESTINIAN CAMPS ARE UNTRUE AND THAT WHAT IS BEING SAID IS NO 
MORE THAN A JUSTIFICATION FOR COMMITING THIS UGLY CRIME. 

THE LEADER WENT ON TO SAY: THIS HAS PROMPTED US TO WONDER IF 
YASIR ‘ARAFAT WAS NOT RIGHT IN HIS SEARCH FOR A LAND TO ESTABLISH 
A PALESTINIAN STATE ON ANY PART OF PALESTINE EVEN IF IT IS ON 
THE DONKEY'S BACK SINCE THE PALESTINIANS ARE BEING SLAUGHTERED 
EVERYWHERE. THE LEADER HAS STRESSED THAT THIS DOES NOT 

MEAN THAT WE ACCEPT THE ZIONIST PRESENCE ON THE ARAB LAND. EVEN 
IF A PALESTINIAN STATE IS ESTABLISHED AND RECOGNIZES THE ZIONIST 
ENTITY, WE WILL NOT RECOGNIZE THIS ENTITY. 

THE LEADER SAID THAT IN ADDITION TO THE IMPOSSIBILITY 

OF THE COEXISTENCE OF A PALESTINIAN STATE AND A ZIONIST ENTITY, 
THE WAR BETWEEN THE ARABS AND THE ZIONISTS IS A WAR OF EXISTENCE 
PARTICULARLY AFTER THE ZIONISTS ARMED THEMSELVES WITH THE ATOM 
BOMB AND DEPLOYED NUCLEAR MISSILES IN THE NEGEV AND ON THE 
GOLAN. 

THE LEADER ADDED: I CANNOT BUT SIDE WITH THE PALESTINIANS 
WHETHER THEY ARE JUSTIFIED OR NOT. THIS STANCE IS BASED ON 
PRINCIPLE AND THE MASSES OF THE SOCIALIST PEOPLE'S LIBYAN ARAB 
JAMAHIRIYAH HAVE NO OPTION IN THE FACE OF WHAT IS TAKING PLACE 
BUT TO STAND BY THE PALESTINIANS AND INCITE THEM TO RESIST AND NOT 
SURRENDER THE CAMPS. 

IN HIS ADDRESS THE LEADER CALLED ON THE PALESTINIANS 

TO STAY ON LIBYAN SOIL WITH THEIR CAMPS AND DETACHMENTS AND TO 
CONTINUE FIGHTING FROM LIBYA TO LIBERATE PALESTINE. THE LEADER 
ADDED: LET IT BE KNOWN THAT LIBYA SUPPORTS THE ANNEXATION OF 
LEBANON TO SYRIA SO THAT THE TWO BECOME ONE COUNTRY, BUT THIS 
SHOULD NOT BE REALIZED OVER THE CORPSES OF PALESTINIANS. 

IN CONCLUSION THE LEADER ASKED THE HEADS OF THE AFRICAN 
DIPLOMATIC MISSIONS TO CONTACT THEIR LEADERS TO STAND BY THE 
PALESTINIANS IN THE TRIBULATIONS THEY ARE FACING THESE DAYS. 
(ENDALL) 260700 DYKES/AM 26/1018Z MAY
