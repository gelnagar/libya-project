LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Contacts Machel on Defense Treaty 
LD251520 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 25 May 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 May (JANA) — Leader of the Great Al-Fatih 
Revolution, Colonel Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi has contacted 
President of Mozambique Samora Machel. Col al-Qadhdhafi 
condemned the racial attack upon Mozambique, stressing the 
Jamahiriyah’s readiness to implement the joint defense treaty 
between the two countries against the racist regime in South 
Africa. 


Col al-Qadhdhafi stressed that the radical solution is to work for 
the total elimination of the racist regime and to liberate South 
Africa from domination so the Azania state may emerge from 
the ruins of the racist regime — as the Zimbabwe Republic 
emerged from the ruins of the racist regime in Rhodesia, and as 
Arab Palestine will once more emerge from the ruins of Israel.
