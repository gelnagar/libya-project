LIBYA

COMMENTARY ON MEDIA REACTION TO AL-QADHDHAFI SERMON 
LD220137 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 21 Oct 80 


[Excerpts] The Arab, Islamic and world media's attention was claimed by a speech 
delivered by the brother leader at the congregation of prayers in the Zuwaylah region. 


The media underlined the leader's words that the Islamic holy places in Mecca and Medina 
have now fallen under American occupation and that one of the foremost Islamic duties is 
a jihad to liberate Mecca. The media also highlighted the leader's assertion that 
pilgrimage in the future would be meaningless if the American occupation of God's house 
continued and that pilgrimage would only have one meaning, namely jihad to liberate the 
house of God. Anyone who ignored this fact and turned away from jihad by merely 
performing the traditional pilgramage rites, would be undertaking a superficial worship 
other than that which was willed by God. 


As for the Saudi media, it tried to avoid the essence of the speech and to deal with 
peripheral questions in order to divert the attention of citizens from the real substance 
of the speech. The media concentrated its attention on the services provided for the 
pilgrims and the good treatment they received as if this was not the duty of the Saudi 
Government, anyway. It went further by saying that pilgrims, including Libyans, were 
deeply grateful to the king and his wise government. This is glossing over the truth 
which will deceive no one. Instead of telling the truth to the Arab and Islamic masses 
about the American air invasion of the skies of the peninsula, it resorted to deception 
by talking about the helicopter which was used to organize traffic. 


Thus, a fundamental contradiction is clearly manifested between the revolution and its 
political and ideological implications which call for freedom and stand against the 
capitalist, reactionary and fascist forces and the reactionary regimes which protect 
and worship imperialism. Thus, also appears the historical contradiction between the 
revolutionary forces, the forces of good and life in the Arab nation and the right wing 
and reactionary forces which in vain call themselves Islamic organizations and falsely 
allege zeal for the interests of Islam and Muslims while taking refuge under the 
umbrella of the United States and relying on a decrepit wall of myths.
