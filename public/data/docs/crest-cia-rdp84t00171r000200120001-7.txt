Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery briefing note dated 4 August 1983 reports that Libya recently moved medium tanks from Libya into northern Chad areas held by Libyan-backed dissident forces. The convoy was seen northwest of Faya Largeau, and the equipment likely came from Sebha in west-central Libya. Nothing here concerns Tripoli.
• Convoy contained six medium tanks (two not on graphic), four POL/water trucks, four cargo trucks and six Land Rover-type vehicles.
• The Kaelble heavy-lift transporter is a West German-produced vehicle used extensively by Libya and previously observed with Polisario forces in Western Sahara.
• At least five T-54/55 tanks had left Sebha armored storage area, about 570 NM north-northwest of Faya Largeau.
• 1983-08: Convoy with six medium tanks on Kaelble heavy-lift transporters, plus supply trucks and vehicles, identified on U-2 imagery heading toward Faya Largeau.

DEPLOYMENT OF TANKS FROM LIBYA TO CHAD (SWN/NF) 

25X1 

1. SIGNIFICANCE: MEDIUM TANKS HAVE RECENTLY BEEN DEPLOYED 
FROM LIBYA TO AREAS OF NORTHERN CHAD CURRENTLY CONTROLLED 
BY LIBYAN-SUPPORTED DISSIDENT FORCES. (SWN/NF) 

2. REMARKS: A CONVOY CONTAINING SIX MEDIUM TANKS (TWO NOT ON 
’ GRAPHIC) ON KAELBLE HEAVY-LIFT TRANSPORTERS WAS IDENTIFIED 
ON (U-2) IMAGERY OF THIS CONVOY 20X1 
WAS APPROXIMATELY 100 NM NORTHWEST OF FAYA LARGEAU AT 
18-36-48N 017-33-53E. OTHER VEHICLES IN THIS CONVOY, WHICH 
WAS HEADED TOWARD FAYA LARGEAU, INCLUDED FOUR POL/WATER 
TRUCKS, FOUR CARGO TRUCKS, AND SIX LAND ROVER-TYPE VEHICLES 
IMAGERY OF REVEALED PROBABLE 25xX1 
ELEMENTS OF THIS SAME CONVOY AT OGOUI, APPROXIMATELY 175 NM 
NORTHWEST OF FAYA LARGEAU. (SNF) 

THE KAELBLE HEAVY-LIFT TRANSPORTER IS A WEST 
GERMAN-PRODUCED VEHICLE USED EXTENSIVELY BY LIBYA. IT HAS 
BEEN OBSERVED WITH POLISARIO FORCES IN WESTERN SAHARA. 
(SWN/NF) 

THE EQUIPMENT IN THE CONVOY WAS PROBABLY DEPLOYED FROM THE 
SEBHA AREA IN WEST-CENTRAL LIBYA. NUMEROUS BOXES AND 
CRATES WERE SCATTERED NEAR SEVERAL T-54/55 TANKS AT SEBHA 

ARMORED STORAGE AREA ON IMAGERY OF SUGGESTING THAT 25X1 
AMMUNITION OR SUPPLIES WERE BEING LOADED. AT LEAST FIVE OF 
THESE TANKS HAD DEPARTED BY SEBHA IS 25X11 
APPROXIMATELY 570 NM NORTH-NORTHWEST OF FAYA LARGEAU.
