Rawlings Letter Delivered 
LD182212 Tripoli JANA in English 1859 GMT 18 Feb 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Feb (JANA) -- The revolution's leader today received Mr Chris Bukari Atim, 
member of the Provisional National Defence Council [PNDC] of the Republic of Ghana, who 
handed the leader a letter from Flt Lt Jerry Rawlings, president of the PNDC. 


In his letter, Flic Lt Rawlings [words indistinct] strong revolutionary relations between 
the Libyan Arab and Ghanian peoples and their development in the interests of the peoples 
and of Africa. He added: "We in Ghana regard you as an example to be emulated in being 
brave and strong in facing Africa's (?enemies). We will stand together [words indistinct] 
our relations in various [word indistinct] because the consolidation of our relations would 
give Africa a solid force. The Provisional National Defence Council in Ghana desires the 
development of relations between the Ghanian and Libyan peoples and looks forward to 
carrying out this work at the Arab and African (?levels). 


Flt Lt Rawlings stressed in his letter to the leader: "World history affirms to the 
peoples that the road to freedom is the road of change through revolutionary regimes. The 
3lst December 1981 revolution in Ghana and the Great lst September Revolution are ready 

to work together to confront imperialism and racism." 


HAIG STATEMENTS REVEAL ‘DASTARDLY' U.S. POLICY 


LD181748 Tripoli JANA in English 0907 GMT 18 Feb 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Feb (JANA) -- General Alexander Haig, U.S. secretary of state, revealed 
the dastardly policy being perpetrated by the Reagan administration against the jamahiriyah. 
In a press conference held in Morocco, he spoke in a hostile, vengeful and rancorous manner 
against the jamahiriyah and expressed annoyance at its increasingly important role in 
siding with people struggling to gain their liberty.

General Haig claimed that the jamahiriyah incites trouble and turmoil and its activities 

to be a cause for concern, accused Libya of terrorism and his official and private statements 
deliberately incriminated Libya in masterminding incidents actually fabricated by the American 
administration in an attempt to conceal the U.S. terrorist role in the region. [sentence as 
received] 


Haig is well aware that Libya knows full well the nature of the U.S. policy which it 

attempts to expose. Thus, it is possible to forgive this tyrant if he charts the U.S. policy 
in a way which guarantees its successful implementation whether directly or through lackey 
surrogates and infidel tyrants. 


By refuting U.S. allegations, the jamahiriyah, the subject [words indistinct] in this context 
and the country in question, is considered by various observers as a strategic obstacle to 
U.S. designs, whether in the Arab region, Africa or the Caribbean region. 


In the meantime, the jamahiriyah is gaining a particular importance in confronting these 

designs and acts as a formidable obstacle against U.S. designs aimed at imposing hegemony 
and subjugation of people. Thus, the reasons behind Haig's virulent attack on the jamahiriyah 
becomes obviously clear to the world at large. 


JANA REPORTS ‘ESCALATING' SOMALI ARMY MUTINY 


LD181740 Tripoli JANA in English 0850 GMT 18 Feb 82 


[Text] Mogadishu, 18 Feb (JANA) -- The ruling regime in Somalia sent military reinforcements 
to the northern province where the mutiny inside the army barracks is escalating. News says 
that tens of soldiers were reported killed or injured as a result of the clashes which took 
place inside these barracks between pro- and anti-regime forces. 


The sources added that most of the 8th Army has turned against the regime following the 
execution of 84 military personnel. The town of Boho Tella came under the control of the 
Democratic Salvation Front. An urgent message from Mogadishu said that the barracks of 
Jabili, Horaya Coul and Arabi have been seized by the rebel forces.

LD182356 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1930 GMT 18 Feb 82 

[Text] Tripoli, 18 Feb (JANA) -- Kamal Abu Shamah, member of the Algerian National Party
