Summary (AI): A 1981 Fayetteville Observer article reports that a Cumberland County, N.C. man, James "Jimmy" Dean, allegedly recruited Eugene Tafoya to shoot Libyan dissident Faisal Zagallai at Fort Collins, Colorado, in October 1980, with prosecutors saying Libyan leader Qaddafi ordered the killing and former CIA agent Edwin Wilson, living in Libya, was behind it.
• Prosecutors say Dean paid Tafoya $8,000 in London after the shooting.
• Zagallai was shot in October 1980 (the article's date reads 1989 in OCR, likely a typo), in Fort Collins, Colorado.
• Wilson was indicted in Los Angeles in 1980 for allegedly illegally exporting explosives to Libya and was believed to be living in Tripoli.
• The 1977 training squad was promised $4,500 monthly plus bonuses.
• 1980-10: Faisal Zagallai, 35, a Libyan dissident and Colorado State University graduate student, was shot twice in the head with .22-caliber slugs and lost an eye; Eugene Tafoya was charged with attempted murder and conspiracy.
• 1977-07: A training program for Libyan terrorists, reportedly organized and financed by Edwin Wilson with one active-duty and nine former Green Berets, began after Patry Loomis contacted Luke Thompson.
