LIBYA

Shultz Accusations on Support for Terrorism Rebutted 
LDO52338 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1930 GMT 5 Apr 84 


[Excerpts] New York, 5 Apr (JANA) — US. Secretary of State 
George Shultz has reiterated the U.S. Administration's allega- 
tions that states that reject America’s policies practise terrorism. 
At a meeting in Washington the night before last with officials 
from America, Europe and Japan, the U.S. secretary of state 
claimed that the Jamahiriyah, Iran, Syria and the DPRK are 
backing terrorism. He also urged America's allies to take posi- 
tions hostile to these states. 


These statements on the part of the U.S. secretary of state stem 
from the U.S. Administration's hostile policies toward peoples 
that refuse to acquiesce to domination and who follow an inde- 
pendent line toward Washington. They express the nature of the 
aggressive objectives of the American imperialist line, which is 
being carried out throughout the world in a barbaric manner 
rejecting the right of peoples to defend their freedom. 


The US. secretary of state has leveled these blatant accusations 
against the Jamahiriyah, Syria, the DPRK and Iran because he 
realizes fully that these states have shown that they constitute an 
unbroken thorn in the American flank, and that he who practises 
terrorism in reality is not one of these states: America is the 
first imperialist state that has raised terrorism to the level of state 
policy. The Reagan administration wants to behave in the world 
as an international policeman, alone. Furthermore, it sends its 
fleets and aircraft squadrons to threaten small peoples. It deploys 
deadly missiles in Europe and it is behind all the hideous massa- 
cres perpetrated by the Zionists against the Arabs. It is the one 
that commits infamous and disgraceful acts against insurgents 
in Latin American countries, and back puppet regimes in Sudan, 
Egypt and Somalia. This is indeed real terrorism, and America 
is the sole state that openly practises organized international 
terrorism. 


NORTH AFRICA Q1 


SFRY’s Mamula Meets Again With Yunis Jabir, Departs 
LDO51948 Tripoli JANA in English 1408 GMT 5 Apr 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Rajab 5, April 5, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — Talks between Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, 
commander in chief of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, and 
Admiral Branko Mamula, the federal secretary for national 
defence of Yugoslavia, who is visiting Jamahiriyah were resumed 
last night in Tripoli. Members of the two delegations also 
attended the meeting. 


Departs 5 Apr

5 Apr 84
