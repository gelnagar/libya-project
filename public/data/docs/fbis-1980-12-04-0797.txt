LIBYA

TRIPOLI REPORTS ON SENEGAL-MAURITANIA TENSION 


LD031404 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 3 Dec 80 


[Text] Relations between Mauritania and Senegal have gravely deteriorated over the 
past 2 days after the ruling regime in Senegal massed its forces along the Mauritanian- 
Senegalese border. 


The Jamahiriyah Arab News Agency has quoted those arriving from Senegal to Nouakchott 
as having said that the number of Senegalese concentrated along the Mauritanian border 
was between 5,000-6,000 soldiers with full military equipment. It is known that the 
Senegalese regime has suspended the movement of goods between the two countries.
