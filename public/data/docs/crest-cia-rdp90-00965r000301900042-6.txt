Summary (AI): A 1983 New York Post column (Evans and Novak) says a leak about a Libyan plot against Sudan exposed U.S. agents in Libya and angered President Reagan, who feared Qaddafi would retaliate. The OCR text is partly garbled and mentions no specific Tripoli sites.
• The leak reportedly risked the lives of U.S. CIA agents or pseudo-U.S. agents in Libya.
• U.S. AWACS radar planes were dispatched to Egypt to monitor the situation in Sudan.
• Reagan said on Feb. 16 (1983) that the AWACS shift was 'not an unusual happening.'
• The column also refers to a separate CIA probe of possible Soviet complicity in the attempted assassination of Pope John Paul II.
