Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a Jeune Afrique interview with Muammar al-Qadhdhafi (published 8 October 1976) covering his political ideas, arms purchases, relations with Egypt, the Soviet Union and the United States, and alleged plots. It contains no specific places in or near Tripoli.
• Qadhdhafi said that a Libyan Army of 30,000 men was far from the figure of 2,000 Soviet-supplied tanks cited by the interviewer, and called satellite figures inaccurate.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya ordered and paid cash for eight American C-130 transport aircraft, but the US stopped the deal shortly before delivery.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya had sent 300 tanks and 60 aircraft to Egypt and Syria during the October 1973 war.
• Qadhdhafi said the Scud missiles shown on 1 September 1976 were improved Scuds but not long enough to reach Israel.
• Qadhdhafi said the Libyan people's congresses and committees were the basis of democracy and rejected the referendum as oppression.
