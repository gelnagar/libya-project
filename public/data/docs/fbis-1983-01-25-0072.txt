LIBYA

REPORT ON AL-QADHDHAFI ROMANIA, BULGARIA VISITS 


LD242252 Tripoli JANA in English 1823 GMT 24 Jan 83 


{Text] Tripoli, Rabi’ Thani 9, Jan 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The leader of the 
revolution arrived back here midday today ending an official and friendly 7-day visit 
to Bulgaria and Romania. 


He had talks with President Todor Zhivkov, secretary general of the Central Committee 
of the Bulgarian Communist Party and head of state of the People's Republic of 
Bulgaria and Nicolae Ceaucescu, the secretary general of the Communist Party and 
president of the Socialist Republic of Romania. The talks centered on bolstering the 
relations of friendship and cooperation between the Jamahiriyah people on one hand and 
the peoples of Bulgaria and Romania on the other. 


The visit yielded positive results in the long-term cooperation and contributed in con- 
solidating the front of struggle against imperialism and Zionism in defence of peace 
and progress. 

Two friendship and cooperation treaties were concluded with Bulgaria and Romania. 

Two long-term programmes for comprehensive cooperation have been also signed between 
the Jamahiriyah separately with Bulgaria and Romania as well as agreements on economic, 
trade, scientific and technical cooperation.
