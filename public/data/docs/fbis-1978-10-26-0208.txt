AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS MESSAGE TO AL-BAKR, AL-ASAD 
LD251420Y Tripoli Lomestic Service in Arabie 1220 GMT 


(Text) Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi sent a cable to > Iraqi president and the 
Syrian president, the text of which is as follows: 


Brother President Ahmad Hasan al-Bakr, Brother President Hafiz al-Asad; 


The meeting between you takes place on the level of national responsibility, I personally 
feel satisfied and happy about this. You are aware how anxious I was for such a meeting 
ind I have great hopes for its results, Hope does not derive from the meeting itself, but 
from the results f the meeting, We will not Just be satisfied with a meeting between the 
leaders of Syria and Iraq. The national imperative and the historic reply to the victory 
scored by Zionism against Arab nationalism by Egypt's downfall and removal from the 
national battle is unity between Syria and [raq, politically and militarily, 


As two presidents who have overcome all differences and personal and party complications 
you can help the Syrian and Iraqi peoples, as a priority task, to transcend borders and 
regionalism, through their determination which is the determination of the great Arab 
nation, I can categorically declare that the masses of the Arab nation salute and 
applaud this meeting because they are awaiting from it 


a national reaction appropriate 
to the level of danger facing the nation, I am certain that the National Front of 
Steadfastness and Confrontation and the Jamahiriyah in particular will be a strong 
backing for your work, 

The unionists in the Jamahiriyah are interested in the unity of any two or more peoples 
2f the Arab nation even if the people of the Jamahiriyah are not among them, May Baghdad 
remain the focal point of glory, and may Damascus remain the throbbing heart of Arabdom, 
forward, Your brother Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi.
