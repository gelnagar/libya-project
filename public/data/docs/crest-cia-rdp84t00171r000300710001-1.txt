Summary (AI): NPIC imagery briefing note on a probable drone support facility at Abu Kammash on Libya's northwest coast, near the Tunisian border. It is far from Tripoli and has no Tripoli-specific content.
• First identification of a probable drone support facility in Libya, from imagery of Abu Kammash.
• Facility is wall and double-fence secured and contains two large hangar-type buildings, one helicopter landing pad and one possible ground launch position.
• One probable Agusta A-109 helicopter was present, probably serving as the launch/recovery vehicle for the drone.
• The facility was not present on earlier imagery but was nearing completion on later imagery.

2. REMARKS: A PROBABLE DRONE SUPPORT FACILITY WAS IDENTIFIED FOR 
THE FIRST TIME IN LIBYA ON JMAGERY OF ABU KAMMASH 25X1 
PROBABLE DRONE SUPPORT FACILITY IS BOTH WALL AND DOUBLE-FENCE 
SECURED, AND CONTAINS TWO LARGE HANGAR-TYPE BUILDINGS, ONE 
HELICOPTER LANDING PAD, AND ONE POSSIBLE GROUND LAUNCH 
POSITION. ALSO PRESENT WAS ONE PROBABLE AGUSTA A-109 
HELICOPTER WHICH PROBABLY SERVES AS THE LAUNCH/RECOVERY 

VEHICLE FOR THE DRONE. THE FACILITY WAS NOT PRESENT ON 25X11 
BUT WAS NEARING COMPLETION ON IMAGERY OF 25X1
