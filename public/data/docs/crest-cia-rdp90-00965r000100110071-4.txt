Summary (AI): A Washington Post column (Jack Anderson and Dale Van Atta, 24 September 1986) reports that Abdel Moneim Huni, a former Gadhafi ally and one of the officers who helped seize power in 1969, called from exile in Cairo for his former army colleagues to overthrow Gadhafi. Huni had split with Gadhafi in 1975 and reportedly met Reagan administration officials secretly in Washington the previous January.
• Huni was once vice president, interior minister and intelligence chief, and was Gadhafi's No. 3 man before splitting in 1975.
• Thousands of leaflets bearing Huni's call were distributed throughout Libya.
• Huni made a secret trip to Washington in January 1986 and conferred with Reagan administration officials; talks were described as inconclusive.
• Huni timed his proclamation for 30 August, just before the 17th anniversary of the 1 September revolution.
• 1986-08-30: Huni issued a declaration from exile calling on army officers and soldiers to force Gadhafi from power and announced the formation of a 'Libyan Committee for National Salvation'.
• 1986-04: After the U.S. air raid on Libya in April, Gadhafi sent an urgent message to Huni in Cairo offering him a return to the ruling military council.
• 1976: Gadhafi reportedly tried to have Huni assassinated in Cairo.
• 1975: Huni split from the regime in disgust over corruption and persecution of dissidents and minorities.
