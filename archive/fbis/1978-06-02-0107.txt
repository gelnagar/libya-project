[Text] Cuba and Libya signed a trade cooperation agreement at the conclusion of the 
talks held in Tripoli by governmental delegations of the two countries. The agreement, 
which calls for expansion of trade and includes Cuban sugar exports to Libya, was signed 
by Foreign Trade Minister Marcelo Fernandez Font and his Libyan counterpart, Abu Bakr 
'Ali ash-Sharif.
