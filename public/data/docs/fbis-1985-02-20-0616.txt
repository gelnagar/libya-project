LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Mauritanian Message 
LD162338 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2115 GMT 16 Feb 85 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution tonight received Dr 
Louleid Ould Weddad, the personal envoy of Mauritanian pres 

ident Colonel Maayouia Ould Sidi Ahmed Taya, chairman of the 
Military Committee for National Salvation and head of state of 
the Republic ofMauritania, who conveyed to him a written 
message from the Mauritanian president. 


SFRY’s Culafic Conducts Visit to Tripoli 


Received by Jabir 
LD191522 Tripoli JANA in English 1427 GMT 19 Feb 85 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada Awal 29, Feb. 19, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir has today 
received the Yugoslav member of the collective Presidency and 
the federal secretary for internal affairs, Dobroslav Culafic, 
currently on a visit to Jamahiriyah. 


Received by Al-Qadhdhafi 
LD200954 Tripoli JANA in English 0832 GMT 20 Feb 85 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada Awal 30, February 20, JAMAHIRYAH 
NEWS AGENCY The leader of the revolution received 
yesterday Mr Dobroslav Culafic the member of the Presidency 
council and the federal secretary for internal affairs in Yugosla 
via who handed the leader, during this reception, a sword as a 
symbolic present 


Mr Dobroslav expressed his complete satisfaction with regard to 
the development of the strategic cooperation relations between 
Yugoslavia and Jamahiriyah. He also expressed his appreciation 
on the level that these relations have reached in various fields 


V. 20 Feb 85
