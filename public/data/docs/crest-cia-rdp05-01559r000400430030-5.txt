Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a live Tripoli Television broadcast of Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi addressing a terrorism seminar in Tripoli on 13 April 1988. He recalls the April 1986 U.S. air raid, saying bombs were aimed at his bedroom, tent, office and Ibn Ashur Street in Benghazi, and he names Bab al-Aziziyah as a target.
• Speech was broadcast live by Tripoli Television at 1645 GMT on 13 April 1988.
• Qadhdhafi says he is not a president and that the seminar was held at the permanent headquarters of the Permanent Secretariat of the Progressive Socialists Organization.
• Speech says Libya is against terrorism but argues it is caused by colonialism and foreign bases.
• Speech says that if Libya invaded Chad it would be considered terrorism.
• Document also contains unrelated FBIS items about Chad appointments and Israeli training of Chadian troops in Zaire.
• 1986-04-15: Speaker refers to the U.S. air and naval aggression two years earlier, directed at the Jamahiriya's base, Ibn Ashur Street and Benghazi.
• 1986-03: Speech refers to the shooting down of a number of U.S. aircraft on the Line of Death.
