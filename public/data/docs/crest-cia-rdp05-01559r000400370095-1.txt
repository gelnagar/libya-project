Summary (AI): An FBIS London transmission reprints a Le Monde interview with Colonel Qadhafi from 1 September 1976. He discusses terrorism, alleged training camps in Libya, Sudan and Numayri, and Libyan mediation in Lebanon. Nothing specific to Tripoli sites.
• Qadhafi rejected accusations by Egypt, Tunisia and Sudan that Libya trained their citizens in camps in Libya for subversion.
• Qadhafi said Libya would accept commissions of inquiry to check the camps.
• Qadhafi said he regretted helping Numayri regain power in July 1971, because Numayri executed two coup leaders whom Libya had detained after forcing their aircraft to land in Libya.
• Qadhafi said Libya wanted Syria to withdraw its army from Lebanon and to promote a compromise under Ilyas Sarkis.
