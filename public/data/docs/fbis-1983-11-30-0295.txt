LIBYA

Jabir, Bulgarian Chief of Staff Hold Talks 
1.D270750 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0708 GMT 27 Nov 83 


{Excerpt} Tripoli Libyan Arab-Bulgarian talks began last 
night between Brigadier General Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, com- 
mander in chief of the Armed Forces, and Colonel General 
Atanas Semerdshiev, chief of the Bulgarian General Staff and 
deputy minister of defense
