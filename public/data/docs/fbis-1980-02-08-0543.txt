LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI STRESSES SOLIDARITY WITH TUNISIAN UPRISING 
LD071525 Tripoli JANA in English 1415 GMT 7 Feb 80 LD 


[Text ] Banghazi, February 7, JAMAHLRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--At a banquet in honour of the 
Venezuelan | president last night, the leader of the grand Al-Fatah revolution, Colonel 
iitammar al-Qadhdhafi said that a neighbouring country was exposed to invasion by a 

major power. "I refer to the invasion of Tunisia by the French forces," he added. He 
continued: "Therefore it becomes clear to us that France has set up a colonialist 
bridge spreading from Tunisia to Chad and as far as Central Africa. This isin addition 


its pilfering of the riches of the African peoples, including the Tunisians"
