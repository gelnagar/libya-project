LIBYA

U.S. 'SPY AIRCRAFT' DOWNED 2 SEPTEMBER 


LD061358 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1338 GMT 6 Oct 82 

[Text] The Air Defense at 1959 on Thursday, 2 September, in the Banghazi region downed 
an American spy aircraft dispatched by the U.S. 6th Fleet in the direction of Banghazi, 
exploiting with trickery and deception the international corridor that ensures the safety 
and protection of civilian flights. 


The disclosure of the incident has been postponed until now in order to ensure the study 
of the remains and the identity of the wreckage of the aircraft. 


Wreckage Shown on TV 
LDO70934 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0920 GMT 7 Oct 82 
[Text] Tripoli, 7 Oct (JANA) -- Last night, Jamahiriyah television showed a film of the 
U.S. spy plane that was brought down by the Jamahiriyah's air defenses on 2 September. 


AL-FAJR AL-JADID, published by JANA, also carried pictures of the U.S. spy plane wreckage. 


ARAB DELEGATION TO VISIT MOSCOW 8 OCTOBER 


LD061640 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1558 GMT 6 Oct 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 Oct (JANA) -- The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 
issued a statement today which states: At the invitation of the board of the USSR Afro-Asian 
People's Solidarity Organization, a popular Arab delegation comprising a number of important 
political and social figures in the Arab homeland, led by the general secretary of the 
Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress, will visit Moscow on 8 October. 


The purpose of the visit is to meet Soviet leaders in order to discuss the current situation 
in the Arab homeland and the world, and also Arab-Soviet relations and the necessity of 
developing these relations so they serve the common interests in challenging imperialist, 
Zionist and reactionary schemes. 


SOVIET-ARAB FRIENDSHIP GROUPS CABLE AL-QADHDHAFI 


LD061611 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1555 GMT 6 Oct 82 

[Text] Tripoli, 6 Oct (JANA) -- The leader of the revolution has received a cable from 
the Soviet-Arab friendship societies in which they stressed their determination to 
strengthen ties of friendship and cooperation between the Libyan-Arab and Soviet peoples. 
The Soviet-Arab friendship societies condemned, in their cable, the barbaric Zionist 
invasion of Lebanon and the abhorrent massacre perpetrated by the Zionists against the 
old, women, and children in the Sabra and Shatila camps.
