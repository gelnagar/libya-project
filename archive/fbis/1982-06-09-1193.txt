LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ASSURED OF AL-ASAD'S STEADFASTNESS 


LD090742 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 9 Jun 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 9 Jun (JANA) -- The brother leader of the revolution held an urgent 
telephone conversation with President Hafiz al-Asad and is now assured of the steadfastness 
of the resistance and the joint forces and of Syria's steadfastness with them. 


President al-Asad assured the brother ieader of the revolution of the firmness of the 
situation and of the high morale of the fighting Arab forces. Moreover, contacts are 
continuing on all ievels among the members of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front. 


Al-'Ubaydi Meets Syrian Envoy 
JNO82020 Damascus Domestic Service in Arabic 1915 GMT 8 Jun 82 


[Text] ‘Abd al-‘Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison in 
the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, met in Tripoli today with the director of 
the Syrian relations bureau in Tripoli. Al-'Ubaydi conveyed greetings from Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 1 September Revolution, to President Hafiz al-Asad and 

pledged Libya's stand alongside Syria, the Palestinian Resistance and the Lebanese people 
against the Israeli aggression and the imperialist-Zionist plots. Al-‘Ubaydi pointed out 
that reports disseminated by hostile news agencies do not reflect the views of the Libyan 
leadership. 


AL-QADHDHAFI PRAISES VOLUNTEERS' RAPID RESPONSE 


LD081428 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 8 Jun 82 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution saluted those who volunteered for the jamihiri 
guard for their response to the call and for immediately joining the training. The leader 
applauded this rapid response to raise the banner of the jamahiri guard without any 

material return or selfish aspirations for positions and titles, a matter which confirms 

the ability of our people to continue to offer sacrifice and to proceed forward in escalating 
the people's revolution to confront all possibilities. 


MEETING WITH ALGERIA CONCERNS PARLIAMENTARY UNITY 


LD081426 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 8 Jun 82 


[Text] Within the framework of preparations for the joint meeting between the General 
People's Congress and the Algerian People's National Assembly and in accordance with the 
agreement between the brother leader and Algerian President Chadli Bendjedid to achieve 
unity between the two Arab peoples of Libya and Algeria, a joint meeting was held this 
morning in Tripoli which included the members of the General People's Congress General 
Secretariat and the delegation of the Algerian People's National Assembly. Fixing the 
date of the joint meeting between the General People's Congress and the Algerian People's 
National Assembly during this year in the jamahiriyah was discussed during this meeting. 
The agenda of this historic unionist meeting also was discussed in order to implement the 
resolutions and recommendations of the basic people's congresses to achieve Arab unity.

OAU OFFICIAL ARRIVES TO DISCUSS SUMMIT PLANS 


LD0O72116 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 7 Jun 82 


[Text] The deputy secretary general of the OAU arrived in Tripoli this afternoon with an 
accompanying delegation to hold meetings concerning final preparations for convening the 
African summit in the jamahiriyah next August. 


Technical commissions at the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison are now busy making final 
preparations likely to ensure the success of this important African encounter due to be 
held on the land of the Great 1 September Revolution.
