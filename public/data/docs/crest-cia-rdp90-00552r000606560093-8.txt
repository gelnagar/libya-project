Summary (AI): A 1981 Washington Post clipping reports that Eugene Tafoya, a former Green Beret accused of wounding Libyan dissident Faisal Zagallai in Fort Collins, Colorado, in October 1980, is protesting "hearsay evidence" in letters to Denver newspapers. It links the case to former CIA operative Edwin P. Wilson, who denies training terrorists. Nothing here is set in Tripoli.
• Tafoya, 47, is accused of wounding Zagallai at Zagallai's apartment in Fort Collins in October 1980.
• Tafoya's letter, dated Sept. 7 1981, was given to the Rocky Mountain News and The Denver Post by his attorneys.
• Tafoya was denied a lower bond partly because he is believed to have substantial holdings in secret Swiss bank accounts.
• Wilson's telex number surfaced during a search of Tafoya's New Mexico home.
