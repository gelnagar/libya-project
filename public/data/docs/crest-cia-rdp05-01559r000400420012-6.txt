to Algeria of the Dutch foreign minister, Algerian-Dutch politi-

at favouring national reconciliation and the restoration of peace 
in Lebanon. 

The two sides also examined the evolution of the conflict between 
Iraq and Iran and initiatives likely to find a political solution for 
this conflict. 

Concerning regional issues, the two sides examined the Western 
Sahara conflict in its political and military dimension and its 
consequences on security, stability and construction of the Great 
Maghreb. 

It is worth recalling that Hans van den Broek arrived Sunday 
here for a three day official visit at the invitation of Foreign 
Minister Ahmed Taleb Ibrahimi.

{Text] Geneva, March 21 (AFP) — Libyan leader Colonel 
Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi was cut off mid-sentence Friday in a 
television link-up between Tripoli and a conference on terrorism 
here organised by the International Organisation for Progress. 

He had just started to answer the first question from delegates 
after his speech when the sound was cut. He sat waiting for it to 
be restored and then got up and left the Tripoli studio. 

The questioner, an American teacher, had asked why Libya had 
not taken its case to the International Court of Justice after the 
US. air raids on Tripoli and Banghazi on April 15 last year. 

During his speech the colonel called on smaller countries to 
boycott the United States and Israel, the seats of world terrorism, 
and accused the U.S. Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) of 
creating the acquired immune deficiency syndrome (AIDS) virus 
as a biological weapon. 

He warned Europe could expect attacks “as long as it was an 
accomplice to terrorist acts in the Middle East, notably against 
the Palestinians, the number one victims of terrorism.” 

He also attacked France for its policies in Chad, New Caledonia, 
Martinique, Mayotte and Reunion, warning it “would have to 
reckon with the terrorist acts perpetrated by these oppressed 
peoples.” 

The three-day conference of some 50 U.S. and European del- 
egates was to adopt a final resolution on Saturday.
