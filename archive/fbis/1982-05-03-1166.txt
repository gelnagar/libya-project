LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ISSUES 'LABOR DAY' APPEAL 


LD020924 Tripoli JANA in English 0832 GMT 2 May 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 2 May (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY) -- The revolution's leader Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi yesterday issued the following, appeal to the workers of the world on the 
occasion of "labour day": 


"The great historic transformations that have taken place, thanks to the popular mass 
movement, were intended to place matters in their natural frame work by putting the workers 
in a position to benefit from the fruits of revolution. What, in fact, has happened is 

the opposite. The situation has remained as it had always been. Employers and bureaucracies 
still exploit the efforts of workers. They have been chea ing this vanquished sector of 
society throughout the world and misleading them into thiniing that the first of May is their 
very own anniversary. In fact this occasion only marks the victory of the bourgeoisie over 
the rights of workers. Every year on this occasion, the workers are benumbed by all sorts 

of statements and speeches. This occasion is in fact a day of rejoicing for governments 

and employers. 


"The first of May has contributed towards entrenching social oppression, injustice and 
exploitation where the rich have become richer and the poor poorer. Current social 
injustices throughout the world point to the reality that the absence of workers from 
performing their true role contributes towards the confirmation of the cruel basis of 
these societies. 


"For the phenomenon of unemployment and the chaining of workers to bureaucratic procedures 
to prevent them from gaining a living for their families is the epitomy of deceit. Workers, 
no matter how much their lot is improved, remain as some sort of slaves. 


"The solidarity of the working forces is aimed at reaching that great dream of liberation 
from all forms of oppression, exploitation and slavery. Workers still reel under the slavery 
of wage-earning. What workers struggle should be directed at is liberation from the slavery 
of wage-earning by marching on factories and production sites. They should also smash 
exploitative and bureaucratic administrations and set up a popular workers administration 

so that -ckers can get their fair share of production and become partners and no more 
wage-earners. 


The true anniversary for the world's workers is September 1, 1978. On that day workers 
throughout Libya s- ‘ed factories and production sites removing government bureaucracy and 
private exploita sist administration, and setting up a popular administration. The Libyan 
workers thus endea ages of slavery and became partners in production instead of wage earners. 
On September 1, 1978, the struggle of the world's workers was crowned by a world accomplish- 
ment that has been the product of the struggle of the oppressed. This accomplishment is a 
true challenge to the forces of exploitation, and spells the end of all those who practised 
cruelty and persecution since the Middle Ages by sapping the efforts of workers throughout 
the’ world.

"The practice of marking the first of May each year as labour day with the blessing of 
employers and governments can only be seen as the greatest robbery in history in which the 
workers struggle has been stolen from them and invested in favour of those who exploit them. 


"Trade unions have become part of government's machinery, trading in manpower and using 
this inhuman role for the benefit of governments, employers and trade unionist leaders. 
This situation clearly points to the necessity of eradicating trade unions that have now 
become an obstacle to workers, preventing them from controlling their capabilities and 
ending ages of slavery, backwardness and exploitation. 


"On this day we call on the workers of the world who are suffering from deceit, exploitation, 
oppression and slavery, to rebel against crude social relations by seizing factories and 
production units to control their rights over production and form their people's conzresses 
and committees. "No democracy without people's congressess... committees everywhere." 


"The outbreak of the workers revolution shall sweep the world, destroying forces of 
exploitation and oppression and raising the banner of the dictum ‘partners in production, 
not wage-earners’ and guided by the second chapter of the Green Book." 


JANA COMMENTATOR DENIES JALLUD-GOUKOUNI MEETING 


LD302012 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1920 GMT 30 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 30 Apr (JANA) -- The JANA political commentator comments on the secret 
foreign report cited in this week's issue of the ECONOMIST which refers to a meeting between 
Brother Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and Goukouni Oueddei, president of the Transi- 
tional Government in Chad: 


First, Brother Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud has not met with Goukouni Oueddei, president 
of the Transitional Government of Chad since their last meeting during Staff Maj 'Abd 
as-Salam Jallud's visit to Ndjamena last October. Since that time, no meeting has taken 
place between Staff Major Jaliud and Chadian President Goukouni Oueddei. 


Second, the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, whose forces participated in returning 
security, stability and tranquillity to Chad, enabled supplies of food and medicines to be 
distributed among those Chadians in need and provided the required economic assistance to 
enable our Chadian brothers to rebuild their country following the civil war and its after- 
math, has no intention whatsoever of assisting any party in the ongoing civil war in Chad. 


Third, the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah maintains complete neutrality toward 
the conflicting parties in Chad and will not interfere, even through a non-Chadian neutral 
force or through a peacekeeping force, in Chadian affairs. 


Fourth, through its experience and understanding of the Chadian problem, the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, more than others, sees that this question cannot be solved 


from the outside. 


PDRY FOREIGN MINISTER ARRIVES FOR TALKS 29 APR 


LD301000 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 30 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 30 Apr (JANA) -- PDRY Foreign Affairs Minister Salim Salih Muhammad and 
the delegation accompanying him arrived in Tripoli last night on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


In a statement to a JANA correspondent, Salim said this visit comes in light of the new 
developments in the Arab situation, the escalation of the imperialist onslaught on our region 
and the various plots these circles are launching against the Steadfastness and Confrontation 
Front.

Therefore, it was inevitable that the level of coordination between our two countries, 
members of this front, be raised. During this visit, he said he will be discussing 
matters relating to these developments and the means to confront them as well as the 
joint coordination between our two countries and the development of relations between 
our two peoples. 


Arab Situation Discussed 
LD301824 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 30 Apr 82 
[Text] Tripoli, 30 Apr (JANA) -- The brother secretary of the people's committee of 
the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison conferred today with Brother Salim Salih Muhammad, 
PDRY foreign minister, who arrived in Tripoli this evening. 
During the meeting, they discussed the latest developments in the Arab situation, the 
escalation of the imperialist onslaught as witnessed by the Arab area and ways of 
repelling it. They also discussed bilateral relations and the need to strengthen and 
coordinate their stands to strengthen the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front in its 
confrontation with the imperialist onslaught and the imperialist and U.S. schemes in 
the Arab area.
