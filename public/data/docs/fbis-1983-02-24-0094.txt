LD231736 Tripoli JANA in English 1432 GMT 23 Feb 83 


{Text] Tripoli, Jumada 11, Feb 23 JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- President Husni Mubarak 
of Egypt alleged that Libyan aircraft had violated Egypt's airspace and that he had 
dispatched planes to intercept them since they carried neither U.S. nor Israeli 
identification. 


Speaking at a press conference yesterday Mubarak (?warned of) the repercussions 

if such incidents are repeated. It is difficult not to shake cynically upon hearing 
such comments since Egyptian territory has already been desecrated by Israelis 

and Americans. It would have been advisable for the Egyptian leader to have 

ordered his airforce to stop the U.S. AWACS and Israeli Kfir aircraft. If Libyan 
aircraft had actually entered Egyptian airspace this would have been an honour for 
Egypt. Instead U.S. and Zionist planes which had killed Egyptians in Abu Za'bal, Bahr 
al-Baqar, Suez Canal, and the delta continue to operate freely in Sinai, Cairo, 

and Alexandria. However, Libyan aircraft have defended Egyptian airspace in the 
October war and the Egyptian people will celebrate when the Libyan planes return to 
protect Egypt's airs pace.

IMPLEMENTATION OF UNION AGREEMENTS URGED 
LD231112 Tripoli JANA in English 0840 GMT 23 Feb 83 


["Statement by Revolutionary Command on 25th Anniversary of First Arab Unity in 
Modern History" -- JANA headline] 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada 11, Feb 23, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- It is of great 
significance that the revolutionary forces and the popular masses are commemorating 
this historic event at a time when Arab national existence is being threatened. The 
unity between Egypt and Syria under the leadership of the leader Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir 
had destroyed the Baghdad Pact, the Eisenhower plan and foiled his imperialist 
theory -- the theory of filling the vacuum in the Middle East and North Africa which 
was accepted by the government of the lackey Idriss as-Sanusi. 


The Egyptian-Syrian unity gave impetus to the rise of the masses and set a revolution- 
ary unionist tide into action which culminated in the outbreak of the July 14 
revolution in Iraq, September 26 revolution in Yemen, the Algerian revolution, 

and the revolution of South Yemen. 


The first Arab unity inspired a cultural revival in the Arab nation. Its impact 

shook the Arab states to their very foundation. Similarly, the separation, with 
its equally strong impact, led to the development of the first cells of the free 

unionist revolutionary committees which sparked off the Great Al-Fatih Revolution 
in response to the separation and the ensuing defeat. 


This national unionist revolution has raised the banners of unity once again and 
drafted the plans of the new unity, the unity of the masses (Jamahiriyah). It 
has sincerely borne the responsibility entrusted upon it by the architect of the 
first Arab unity, the leader Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir. 


The leadership of the Great Al-Fatih Revolution would like to take this opportunity 
to announce the following to the Arab masses from the ocean to the Gulf: 


Firstly: Unity is a revolutionary task and therefore the great Al-Fatih 
Revolution considers it a first priority. 


Secondly: The Great Al-Fatih Revolution considers unity to be a question of 
principle and belief, but given the present deterioration and capitulations, 

the Great Al-Fatih Revolution sees Arab unity as the historic and necessary answer 
to this defeatist Arab situation in the face of the Zionist death wagon. 


Thirdly: It announces its readiness to immediately start unity procedure with any 
progressive Arab regime or faction unconditionally, with the exception that this 
unity be based on political and social interests of the masses and that all the 
capabilities of this United States should be channelled for the liberation of 
Palestine. 


It calls upon the Algerian people and military President Chadli Bendjedid, leader 

of the million martyrs' revolution, to immediately proceed with this unity. It 
calls upon our Tunisian brothers and militant President Habib Bourguiba to implement 
the Jarbah declaration. 


It announces its commitment to the Arab republics union which was set up as a 
result of the referendum among the peoples of Egypt, Syria, and Libya as well as 
its commitment to the Tripoli charter that includes Egypt, the Sudan, Syria, and 
Libya.
