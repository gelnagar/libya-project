LIBYA

SYRIAN BA'TH PARTY OFFICIAL ARRIVES 28 SEP 


LD280708 Tripoli JANA in English 1420 GMT 27 Sep 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 28 Sep (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--Member of the Syrian Ba'th Party's 
National Command, Mr Muhammad Haidar, arrived in Tripoli this morning to attend the 
General People's Congress extraordinary session tomorrow. Mr Haidar affirmed to our 
correspondent the Syrian masses’ solidarity with the Libyan people in the common quest 
for unity. The Syrian party official expressed honour for being able to participate in 
the General People's Congress proceedings which are being held to ascertain unity between 
the Jamahiriyah and Syria whose establishment is the hope of all Arabs. 


1 Oct 80 NORTH AFRICA 


PALESTINIAN LEADERS LEAVE TRIPOLI 28 SEP 
LD281952 Tripoli JANA in English 1629 GMT 28 Sep 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 28 Sep (JANA)--Leaders of the Palestinian resistance movement left 
Tripoli yesterday evening after ending their visit to the Jamahiriyah. They were: 

Nayif Hawatimah, secretary general of the Democratic Front for the Liberation of 
Palestine; Ahmad Jibril, secretary general of the Popular Front-General Command; 

Dr Samir Ghawshah, secretary general of the Popular Struggle Front; Issma al-Qadi, 
secretary general of As-Saiqah; Abu Mahir al-Yamani, Political Bureau member of the 
Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine; and Fadil Shrour, Political Bureau member 
of the Popular Front General Command. 


PEOPLE'S GENERAL CONGRESS ADOPTS RESOLUTIONS 
LD290756 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0245 GMT 29 Sep 80 


[Text] The People's General Congress concluded its extraordinary session yesterday 
evening at the People's Hall in Tripoli with a reading of the resolutions adopted by 

the various basic popular conferences and drafted by the general meeting of the popular 
conferences, popular committees, trade unions and vocational organizations--the People's 
General Congress. The following are the resolutions: 


1. The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah [SPLAJ] borders will be open to all 
Arabs. The SIFLAJ land will be the land of all Arabs. The Arab nationality will be the 
nationality of all the inhabitants of the SPLAJ and duel nationality will be abolished. 


2. The SPLAJ will be considered a frontline state. All capabilities will be devoted 
for the battle of the Arab unity and liberation with the speeding up of the creation of 
the armed people for the sake of the realization of actual confrontation 


3. Merger will be established between the Libyan Arab people and the Syrian Arab people 
provided that authority shall be vested in the people and no authority shall be vested 
in the states and the merger shall be the nucleus for an overall Arab unity and for the 
establishment of the Jamahiriyah Arab socialist free and united society in the whole 
Arab lands with authority in it vested in the people. 


4. The speech of commander of the revolution brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi on the 
occasion of the llth anniversary of the revolution will be considered the program of 
work for the coming stage locally and on the pan-Arab field. 


5. Views of the conferences which represented a small proportion and which disagreed 
with the majority of the conferences will be taken into consideration in respect of all 
the resolutions they have taken.
