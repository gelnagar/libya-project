LIBYA

UN Security Council Session on ‘U.S. Threat’ Asked 
LD211004 Tripoli JANA in English 0854 GMT 21 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada Thani 17, Mar 21, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jama- 
hiriyah yesterday requested an emergency session of the Security 
Council to discuss the tense and serious situation emerging from 
threats by the U.S. of intended aggression against Libya. 


The memorandum sent by the General People’s Committee for 
Foreign Liaison Bureau to the Security Council stated that the 
sending of AWACS together with a formation of U.S. fighter 
bombers to the Arab area is a well planned and direct aggression 
against the Jamahiriyah from Egyptian and Sudanese territories, 
particularly if that is seen within the context of the statement by 
U.S. State Department’s official spokesman in which he con- 
firmed that these aircraft would participate in joint operations 
currently taken by Egypt and sudan. 


The General People’s Committee for Foreign Liaison Bureau in 
its memorandum to the Security Council stressed that what is 
being undertaken by the U.S. and Arab area is violation for the 
United Nation’s charter and threat to security and peace in the 
area and the world. 


It also pointed out that what is currently being prepared by the 
U.S. the American style in preparing for aggression which was 
previously used against the freedom of countries and people 
opposing domination. [sentence as received] The latest example 
in this respect was the invasion of Grenada. 


The committee stressed that what happened in Omdurman is a 
play planned in advance as justification for taking aggressive acts 
against the Jamahiriyah and allegations meant to involve her in 
an internal incidents facing the Sudanese regime as well as 
pretext to bring the aggressive American forces to the Arab area 
to exercise its pressure threats, and aggression on the Libyan 
Arab people after U.S. defeat in the Lebanon. 


The memorandum emphasized that the Jamahiriyah has got the 
right to legitimate self defence against any aggression or threat 
aimed at its freedom and dignity.
