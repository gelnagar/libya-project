Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of two Tripoli TV broadcasts (3 January 1988) shows Qadhdhafi addressing students at several Tripoli-area universities and colleges, stressing science, self-reliance and anti-Western and anti-Israeli rhetoric. It mentions a visit to a secondary school in the Abu Salim municipal branch and to the Hay al-Andalus municipal branch.
• Broadcast by Tripoli Television Service in Arabic, 3 January 1988, 2000 GMT.
• Qadhdhafi argued that Libyan graduates in electronics or pharmacology would have no work at home, and that Libya needed science for its battles.
• Qadhdhafi said the United States, Europe and Israel were attacking the Arab nation, and that the EEC was forming a dangerous bloc.
• The document is a Foreign Broadcast Information Service (FBIS) London message, two parts.
• 1988-01-03: Tripoli TV broadcast of Qadhdhafi addressing newly accepted university students; the broadcast was recorded earlier and aired on 3 January 1988 at 2000 GMT.
• 1988-01-02: Qadhdhafi met secondary certificate students at the Abu Salim municipal branch school.
