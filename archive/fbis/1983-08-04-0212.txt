LIBYA

U.S. Military Experts To Train Habre Forces 
LD041024 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0925 GMT 4 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 4 Aug (JANA) — U.S. Defense Department 
sources have announced that a number of American military 
experts have arrived in Ndjamena under the pretext of training 
Hissein Habre’s forces in the use of the antiaircraft missiles that 
the United States has sent to the rebel Habre clique as part of 
the continuous arms shipments that the Reagan administration 
has decided to employ in Chad against the forces of the Legiti- 
mate Government of National Unity. 


Bardai Radio revealed earlier that black U.S. soldiers partici- 
pated in the fighting on the side of the Ndjamena rebel forces 
during the battles that took place around Abeche and Faya- 
Largeau. 


Informed sources in Paris said today that the U.S. military 
experts will themselves use the Redeye antiaircraft missiles. Thus 
the Reagan administration will have entered as a direct party 
into the fighting which is raging between Chadians; this falls 
within the framework of the American plan to internationalize 
the conflict in Chad and implement the aims of this plan to serve 
the imperialist strategy in Chad. One must understandwhat 
Washington is concocting by its flagrant military intervention in 
Chad’s internal affairs. It is becoming clear that Washington's 
aim includes making Chad yield to an aggression against Libya. 


We must be aware of the fact that the American danger is being 
escalated in a flagrant manner that confirms the Reagan admin- 
istration’s desire to exacerbate the situation and create hotbeds 
of tension presaging an explosion. It must moreover be noted that 
the repeated American charges alleging that Libya is intervening 
in Chad are flagrant provocations aimed at giving the United 
States a pretext for direct invervention in Chad, a fact which has 
been clearly confirmed to us. 


Thus it is becoming evident in political spheres that a superpower, 
namely the United States, has played an ugly role in a clear 
blackmail operation to carry out military intervention under the 
screen of false claims that it is seeking stability and security in 
Chad. It is now removing its mask itself to confirm the use of 
gunboat policy and force for the purpose of subversive hegemony 
in Africa. 


Al-‘Ubaydi Denies Libya Bombing Chadian Town 
LD031548 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 3 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 3 Aug (JANA) — Brother ‘Abd al-‘Ati al- 
‘Ubaydi, secretary for foreign liaison, received African ambas- 
sadors accredited to the Jamahiriyah in Tripoli this morning. 


The brother secretary for foreign liaison informed the African 
ambassadors about the situation in Chad and the false claims of 
the rebel Habre’s gang that Libyan aircraft are carrying out air 
raids against the city of Faya Largeau. 


He firmly denied that these false claims have any foundation, 
and stressed that they are an attempt to distort the Jamahiriyah’s 
position, which calls for an end to foreign intervention in that 
country’s affairs, especially the overt intervention by America 
and France. Its stance also calls for achieving security and peace 
through national reconciliation among the conflicting groups of 
the people of Chad. 


He explained that these false accusations by the Ndjamena rebel 
gang are aimed at disturbing the continuous consultations that 
the Jamahiriyah is holding with the OAU to arrive at a complete 
cease-fire in Chad. In this respect he pointed out that the 
Jamahiriyah has previously officially asked the OAU and Pres- 
ident Mengistu Haile Mariam, current OAU chairman, to send 
a committee to investigate the facts and confirm the falsity of the 
claims that the rebels are spreading from Ndjamena against the 
Jamahiriyah about its interference in Chad's affairs 


Brother al-‘Ubaydi said that the Jamahiriyah has asked the 
OAU to stop the overt U.S.-French intervention and to condemn 
it, because it will lead to the internationalization of the conflict 
in Chad. The Jamahiriyah has also asked the organization to 
work for achieving peace in Chad through national reconcili- 
ation. 


During this meeting, the brother secretary of foreign liaison 
informed the African ambassadors accredited to the Jama- 
hiriyah about the movements of the U.S. Sixth Fleet off Libyan 
shores, saying that these movements constitute a clear 
provocation and threats of aggression against the Libyan Arab 
people.

Public Works Minister ‘Confirms’ Habre Dead 
LDO31959 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1936 GMT 3 Aug 83 


[Text] Bardai, 3 Aug (SANA) — The minister of public works in 
the so-called government of Habre has confirmed the death of 
Hissein Habre yesterday in the town of Faya-Largeau 


These confirmations came in statement made by the minister of 
public works, who was captured in the battles which raged in the 
town of Oum Chalouba and which fell in the hands of the forces 
of the Legitimate Government of National Unity 


The captured minister declared that Libya did not take part in 
the battles which took place between the forces of the Legitimate 
Government of National Unity, led by Goukouni Oueddei, and 
the forces of Hissein Habre.

ANSA: Diplomatic Spokesman Discusses Chad 
AU031958 Rome ANSA in English 1920 GMT 3 Aug 83 


[Text] — Rome, August 3 — The Libyan diplomatic office here 
today issued a “categorical” denial of any form of Libyan 
intervention in Chad, but warned that the north African state 
will “safeguard its security” if the international community does 
not halt U.S. and French military intervention in support of Chad 
President Hissein Habre. 


The spokesman for the Libyan Jamahiriyah’s People’s Office 
(embassy) here, ‘Ali “Attiya, told ANSA that imperialist coun- 
tries (the United States and France) and their “reactionary” 
allies in Africa (Egypt, Sudan, and Zaire) are planning to extend 
what was hitherto a “conflict internal to Chad” to the whole 
region 


“The Libyan Jamahiriyah feels directly attacked,” Attia added, 
appealing to all “peace-loving peoples’’, including Italy, to pre- 
vent the conflict’s enlargement. 


Otherwise, he said, “there will be very ugly consequences for 
international relations, peace, and world stability”. 


According to the spokesman, the Tripoli government will con- 
tinue with its neutral stance over Chad, entrusting its hopes for 
a peaceful solution to the civil war in the Organization of African 
Unity 


‘Attiya told ANSA that reports of Libyan air attacks on Faya 
Largeau in northern Chad are “completely false’ and invented 
by “imperialist propaganda to justify intervention in Africa.” 


He added that those who claim Libyans are present in Chad must 
produce evidence, charging Chad officials with trying to pass off 
peasants from the northern part of the country as Libyan soldiers. 


The spokesman would say nothing about a Libyan news agency 
report claiming Habre’s death yesterday, but he pointed out that 
Habre has not denied the report in person and has not been seen 
in public. He added that Tripoli is hoping Chad will get a new
