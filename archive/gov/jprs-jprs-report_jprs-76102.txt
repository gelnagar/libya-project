[Memorandum to the Arab League by Ramadan al-Saltani, representative of the 
Libyan National Democratic Movement, dated 26 February 1980] 


[Text] The following is the text of the important memorandum 
submitted by the Libyan National Democratic Movement to the 
emergency meeting held by the Arab League on 27 February in 
Tunis to review Tunisia's complaint concerning the treacher- 
ous act of aggression committed against the city of Qafsah 
by the ruler of Libya. 


In the name of God the Merciful 

Distinguished secretary general of the Arab League 
Distinguished members of the Arab delegations 
Tunis 


The Libyan National Democratic Movement is pleased to send its most sincere 
greetings to you, along with its prayers that God grant you success and wisdom 
in everything you undertake to serve Arab causes and help our nation to avoid 
the dangers it is exposed to in these critical and precarious times. 


The Movement hopes that this emergency meeting will result in adopting what 

the times demand in terms of a serious and wholesome approach, as well as clear 
and decisive positions in support of the truth and the overall interests of all 
of the peoples of the Arab world. 


The Libyan National Democratic Movement is leading the popular opposition to 
the regime of Col Qadhdhafi which is based on coercion, repression, and sav- 
agery, and which has been inflicting disasters upon our people for 10 long, 
dark, and dreadful years. The Movement first of all wishes to register its 
strong condemnation of the barefaced and treacherous aggression which the 
Qadhdhafi regime undertook against the security and sovereignty of the fellow- 
Arab land of Tunisia, with which the Libyan people--throughout the course of 
history--have maintained close ties of friendship and good neighborly relations. 
The Movement also wishes to convey to you the feelings of regret and anger, felt

by all Libyans, concerning the heavy losses and numerous victims caused by 
thie act of aggression. The Movement also firmly condemns the attempts of 
the tribal military regime to scheme and create trouble, not only among us 
and our fellow-Arabe in Tunisia, but also among all of our other fellow- 
Arabs, neighbors, and friends, 


The Political Bureau of the Libyan National Democratic Movement also wishes 
to express ite alarm and great fears over the possibility of an increasing 
drift by the tribal military establishment which dominates Libya into acts 
of irresponsibility, adventure, troublemaking and aggression against others, 
and into efforts to create conditions which encourage the enemies of the Arab 
nation to engage in more conspiracies and attempts to impose their influence 
and impede the Arab nation's aspirations to achieve growth and break out of 
underdevelopment and subservience. 


On thie occasion, all of the Libyan people consider it important that your 
attention be drawn to the terrible things and dreadful catastrophes going on 
in their country due to actions perpetrated by the regime of backwardness, 
violence, and savagery. 


What is going on in Libya today defies all imagination. The repressive regime 
of Qadhdhafi is continuing its role of sabotaging the country's potential, 
destroying public facilities and utilities, doing away with institutions, 
equandering the wealth of the people, and wasting all of Libya's potential 

for growth, properity, and continued progress. Qadhdhafi is imposing upon 
all of our citizens a form of despotic mob rule, and numerous government 
agencies of which are dishing out to them a type of humiliation, persecution, 
and degradation, the likes of which they have never seen before, not even 
during the time when they were ander the domination of the Fascist Italian 
colonialists. 


Ae we have already mentioned in a previous statement addressed to you, hundreds 
of our citizens--simple people, enlightened people, cultured people, religious 
teachers, students, and qualified cadres--have been led to Qadhdhafi's jails 
and prisons. They are still in his torture chambers today and are receiving 
the most vile treatment that this terrorist regime has been able to think of. 


QGadhdhafi has confiscated the possessions of our citizens, has taken away from 
them their means of earning an honest living, has closed in their faces the 
doors to opportunities for a decent livelihood, and has changed them into 
masses of paupers and destitute people who are able to exist only on handouts 
and donations which the ruler "generously" gives to them for the purpose of 
further humiliating and degrading then. 


Qadhdfi has wasted the country's capabilities and has squandered its wealth 
on his gigantic purchases and accumulation of weapons, as well as on his 
improvised and unsuccessful agricultural and industrial projects. The sole 
purpose of these projects was to create reasons for cheap propaganda and mis- 
leading and demagogic slogans, and to give a decorative facade to his regime. 
The only result of all of this has been an increase in the bank accounts of

middlemen, mercenaries, and agente of the regime, and in the aid to the coun- 
tries and regimes which are the lackeys of world imperialiam and which are

Even our country's historic features and time-honored name have not escaped 
Qadhdhafi'e malice and attacks. He has tried to obliterate the name "Libya"

In his frengy, he has embarked upon destroying residential areas, changing the

Then he turned to the area of education, which was the most valuable thing

illiteracy. He decreased the number of daily school hours and forced students

By means of his tyrannical laws and abnormal, arbitrary decisions he purged 
the government administrative apparatus of all of its most competent and quali-

government agencies and departmerts, and this drove the remaining people, who

and to avoid staying in the civil service. 


Qadhdhafi destroyed the labor movement. Sometimes he threw the union leaders 

into jail, and at other times he kicked them out of office, expelled them, and 
deprived them of exercising their trade-union rights. He also took away from 
them their right to create a sound labor union organization and he transformed

On the national level, under the pretexts of his unionist slogans, he has

In the name of siding with the causes of liberation and supporting peoples

STUDENT 1976 REBELLION AGAINST QADHDHAFI COMMEMORATED 
London SAWT LIBIA in Arabic Mar 80 p 8 


(Statement by the "Britain Branch" of the National Federation of Libyan 
Students “Concerning the January Slaughter") 


[Text] On 12 January 1976, the suspect fascist Qadhdhafi regime revealed 
its bloody, repressive nature when its soldiers violated the sanctity of 
Libyan universities, to open fire on and plant bayonets into the chests of 
the vanguards of students who have led the students' great insurgence against 
this client regime, and who have thereby expressed the people's anger at the 
regime's repression, aggression, subjugation, and corruption. 


On that specific date, hundreds of our innocent colleagues were wounded, the 
blood of many of them was spilled, and many of them died. They sacrificed 
their Lives for freedom and for their country, and in defense of their people, 
the nobleness of knowledge, and their honor and ideals. 


Libyan students had always taken their positions in the front ranks since 
they consider themselves to be messengers of knowledge, the hope of the 
nation and its resource for the future. They did this whenever their nation's 
causes were exposed to danger or those in power went beyond the limits of 
national interests, whenever the skies of our country were covered with clouds 
of autocracy, and whenever a perverted ruler assaulted the sanctity of the 
people's freedoms and the sovereignty of the law. 


For this reason it was natural that this military putsch [Article appears to 
use word putsch to avoid use of word revolutionary] regime should have, as 
one of its basic tasks, the destruction of the "National Federation of Libyan 
Students." This is the federation that organized the ranks of the students 
and which leads their legitimate national struggle. There was also no doubt 
that this reactionary military putsch regime, would have, as one of its basic 
aims, the persecution of those elements which were active within the student 
federation and their liquidation by various means--starting with kicking them 
out of school, arresting them wholesale, throwing them into prisons, holding 
show trials which sentenced an elite group of our colleagues to long terms in 
jail or to life sentences, and, finally, setting up gallows. The only crime 


48 





of these young elements was that they were inspired by a spirit of knowledge 
and education, that they opposed the practices of the regime, that they 
exposed its deficiencies, and that they firmly stood against all of the 
aggression, tyranny, despotism and terror of the regime against our country 
and our people. 


Twelve January 1976 was the day of the decisive battle waged by the repress- 
ive fascist regime against the National Federation of Libyan Students. The 
victims and martyrs we offered will be the spark of a student revolt which 
will never end until the last of fortress of the regime falls. This is the 
basis for the national students' struggle which is firm in spite of all of 
the regime's wickedness, means of repression and terror. There is no doubt 
that our people will achieve their final salvation, that the banners of free- 
dom will fly over their land, and that they will once again have Ce, 
peace, and dignity. 


The blood of our martyrs, who fell under the daggers and bullets of 
Qhadhdhafi's soldiers, and who were hanged at “Martyrs' Square" in the city 
of Benghazi--this blood of our heroic martyrs, ‘Umar Dabbub and Muhammad 
Ibn Sa'ud, will not be forgotten until we avenge them. 


Our brave colleagues, who have held their heads high for years inside the 
torture chambers of Qadhdhafi's prisons, will always remain on the minds of 
our people. 


On the occasion of this painful anniversary, the anniversary of the slaughter 
of January 1976, we must all, wherever we are, inside the huge prison or out- 
side of it, take one minute to stand up and ask God's mercy for the souls of 
our heroic martyrs, ‘Umar Dabbub and Muhammad Ibn Sa'ud. We must also stand 
up and show respect and esteem for our brethren, the brave men who are inside 
Qadhdhafi's jails and prisons of terror. We must repeat the holy oath before 
our fighting people that our struggle for freedom will continue in all arenas 
along with the forces of our steadfast National Democratic Movement, until 
the fall of the regime of repression, terror, and savagery; until our nation 
achieves victory and salvation; and until the bloodthirsty regime of Qadhdhafi 
falls. 


May our great nation crush the tyrannical gangs of Qadhdhafi, and his commit- 
tees of mobs. 


Freedom and victory to the great eternal Libya! 
Glory and peace to our heroic righteous martyrs! 


Long live Libya! Long live the National Federation of Libyan Students!

the court which Qadhdhafi forms, from time to time, from a group of corrupt

On the 20th of this month, Qadhdhafi's court will hold a session in order to

LIFE STORY OF QADHDHAFI REGIME'S VICTIM AL-DAGHIS OUTLINED 
London SAWT LIBIA in Arabic Mar 80 p 10 


(Statement by the Committee for the Defense of Political Prisoners and Public 
Freedoms in Libya, dated 1 March 1980] 


[Text] The Committee for the Defense of Political Prisoners and Public Free- 
doms in Libya has been observing, with a great deal of alarm, the bloody events 
which have been continually taking place on the Libyan scene and the zeal of 
Qadhdhafi's evil and savage regime in pursuing @ policy of tyranny and terror 
against the citizens of our Libyan nation. 


Now Qadhdhafi's regime has added still another crime to the list of acts of 
physical liquidation which it has relied upon as an official policy and which 
was announced personally by Qadhdhafi in a public speech. This crime was the 
murder of attorney ‘Amir al-Tahir al-Daghis after he was arrested at his place 
of residence in the city of Tripoli on Sunday, 24 February 1980. The body of 
the martyr was handed over to his relatives in a sealed box. ‘They were allowed 
to see only his face for the purpose of identifying him. His funeral proces- 
sion was held on Thursday, 28 February, that is, only 4 days after his date of 
arrest. 


Moreover, the authorities of the regime claimed that the martyr had committed 
suicide in prison. This is something which, of course, can deceive nobody, in 
view of the fact that people knew the deceased to be a level-headed person who 
was firm in his convictions and who had a balanced personality. 


Before this horrible act of physical liquidation, the martyr had been summoned 
for investigation in December of 1979. He was interrogated concerning the 
extent of his relationship with an Areb country which the Qadhdhafi regime 
claims to be plotting the overthrow of the ruling regime in Libya. But the 
martyr totally denied that he had any relationship, direct or indirect, of 
this type. 


When the truth of his statements and testimony was confirmed, Khalifah Hanish, 
who had conducted the investigation and who is one of Qadhdhafi's cousins, used 
the carrot-and-stick method and demanded tnat al-Daghis cooperate with him in 
committing espionage against this particular Arab country. When al-Daghis 


52 








refused this cheap offer, Hanish ordered him not to leave the country. This 
was @ step taken in preparation to physically liquidate hin, 


The martyr's first contact with the regime goes back to the fourth day after 
the military coup in September 1969. At that time contact was made with him 
since he was one of the nationally known personalities whose cooperation the 
new regime desired to have. His answer was clear, and it was that he was not 
interested in this cooperation. Ever since that time, the military regime 
Classified al-Daghis as being one of those in opposition to it, 


In 1973, the martyr was arrested and imprisoned for a period of more than 4 
months during the time of the so-called “people's revolution," when there 
took place the arrest and imprisonment of hundreds of educated people and 
nationalists. During this time, laws were abolished and books and libraries 
were burned. 


Freedom fighter ‘Amir al-Tahir al-Daghis was born in the city of Tripoli in 
1933. He pursued his university education at the University of Cairo where 
he received a law degree in 1959. While he was studying in Egypt, he was 
exposed to the intellectual and political trends there. Then he returned 
to Libya after his graduation, became one of the founders of the [Libyan] 
Arab Socialist Ba'th Party, and was elected secretary-general of the party. 
In 1961 he was arrested along with 158 of his colleagues. They were all 
tried on charges of attempting to overthrow tle monarchy. After the martyr 
was sentenced, he spent more than 2 years in prison. 


The martyr continued to fight against the deteriorating situation in his 
country. He became a prominent national figure in the eyes of all, and was 
the object of esteem and respect on the part of all sincere and patriotic 
groups and trends. 


In the year 1964, he participated in the publication of the newspaper AL-AYYAM 
which promoted national consciousness in Libya. When the previous regime began 
to sense the danger of the political line represented by this newspaper, the 
authorities stopped its publication and arrested the martyr along with a group 
of his colleagues. But al-Daghis continued to exert his efforts and use his 
energies in the realm of patriotic action until the time of the war of June 
1967 which shook the whole Arab world. In his role as a freedom fighter, al- 
Daghis lost no time in contributing his militant efforts in organizing and 
directing the wave of popular anger and protest against the consequences of 
the painful June War. 


After the coup which swept away the monarchy in Libya in September 1969, the 
new rulers tried to enlist him in their ranks in order to give national legit- 
imacy to their coup. However, martyr al-Daghis had his own views which reject 
the principle of military coups as a means of solving national problems. 


Also, martyr al-Daghis was honest with himself and with his assessment of the 
state of affairs. From the very beginning, he avoided any form of cooperation

with the new military regime, Since that time, the martyr devoted himself 
wholly to his private business as 4 writer of contracts and his whole interest 
in life became his family and the bringing up of his children, 


In spite of this, Qadhdhafi's regime did not forgive the fact that al-Daghis 
had refused to cooperate with it. Go it avenged iteelf against him by means 
of the horrible crime of taking his life, making his five children orphans, 

making his wife a widow, and inflicting an irreplaceable lose on the nation. 


In the face of this dreadful event, the Committee for the Defense of Political 
Prisoners and Public Freedoms in Libya calle upon the persecuted citizens of 
our nation to act seriously and decisively to stop this bloody tyrant by means 
of a powerful and courageous shakeup which will restore radiance to Libya's 
face, 


The committee calls upon the Federation of Arab lawyers, the Federation of 
Arab Jurists, the Association of lawyers of Arab North Africa, the Inter- 
national Federation of Democratic Jurists, and the International Committee 
of Jurists to investigate the matter of the assassination of their colleague, 
the martyr al-Daghis, and to lodge a strong protest against the repressive 
practices and criminal plans of the Qadhdhafi regime. 


Furthermore, we implore all peoples and international organizations, world 
public opinion, and all supporters of the causes of human rights in the world, 
to lend support to the Libyan people in their struggle to put 4 stop to the 
bloody campaigns of terror which are being carried out by Qadhdhafi and his 
regime's henchmen both inside Libya and outside of it, and to unmask all of 
the organizations and institutions which have descended to the level of sup- 
porting this regime to promote their own materialistic and selfish interests 
at the expense of the blood of innocent people and the tragedies of the Libyan 
nation. 


Finally, the Committee offers its sincere condolences to the family of the 
deceased and to all sincere patriotic forces in the country. May our right- 
eous martyrs have immortality and may their souls have God's mercy.

MARTYDOM OF ‘AMIR AL-DAGHIS DESCRIBED 
London SAWT LIBIA in Arabic Mar 80 p 9 
[Article: “The Martyrdom of ‘Amir al-Daghis, the National Freedom Fighter"] 


(Text) The “Libyan National Democratic Movement," with a great deal of sorrow 
and regret, announces to the Libyan people the death of the prominent national 
freedom fighter, Mr ‘Amir al-Tahir al-Daghis, who surrendered his soul to his 
Creator after the vile torture he suffered at the hands of the henchmen of the 
bloody tyrant, Mu'ammar 41-Qadhdhafi . 


The bloodthirsty Khalifah (Hanish), commander of the security forces, had 
ordered the arrest of attorney ‘Amir al-Daghis on 24 February. Al-Daghis was 
taken to the al-'Agigiyah Camp where interrogation of the freedom fighter be- 
gan concerning his patriotic attitude and hie relations with the opposition. 
Al-Daghis was also interrogated regarding his ties with one of the Arab Rejec- 
tionist countries which Qadhdhafi accuses of supporting his opposition and of 
attempting to arrange a coup d'etat against his regime. And when the execu- 
tioners did not get anything out of al-Daghis, they took him into the torture 
chamber where they subjected him to pain beyond his human level of endurance. 
So he surrendered his life to his Creator on Wednesday, 27 February. 


The body of ‘Amir the martyr was delivered to his relatives in a tightly sealed 
box, and Qadhdhafi's intelligence agents closely supervised the burial without 
opening the box, having told ‘Amir's family that he had committed suicide in 
prison! 


The martyrdom of this outstanding freedom fighter, Mr ‘Amir al-Tahir al-Daghis, 
at the hands of Qadhdhafi's bloodthirsty assassins, is a great tragedy and a 
momentous happening which can only fill our souls with sorrow and our hearts 
with bitterness and more hatred and anger against this regime. This regime 
seems determined to liquidate the good freedom fighting elements on the scene 
in a desperate attempt not only to defend its existence, but also to deprive 
the nation of ite elite men so that the Libyan people's struggle will not have 
a chance to bring about “a good national alternative." This is a task related 
to the plans of the imperialist countries which are anxious that there be no 
"good alternative” in case of the downfall of their evil lackeys who are in 
power at the present time. 


But this “task,” which Qadhdhafi is attempting to carry out in the interests 
of conspiratorial international forces, does not appear to be easy to achieve. 
The Libyan national scene is a broad one, and it is full of heroes and men who 
will not give in because of the death of the martyr ‘Amir al-Daghis. The mar- 
tyrdom of dozens, or even hundreds, of the great leaders of the national move- 
ment will not sap their strength, nor will it undermine their willpower or 
steadfastness in the arenas of honor and glorious national struggle.

There are thousands of others Like the martyr ‘Amir a)-Daghis, who are now 
firmly and bravely standing in their positions and who are relentlessly 
continuing the struggle. 


Our great nation of Libya is not sterile. It has been generous throughout 
the course of history. It is capable of producing millions of men and heroes 
who would take their oath in front of their lord, people, and nation that 
they would never lay down their arma until victory is achieved. 


On that day, the plans of imperialiem and the efforte of their evil lackey 
shall meet with diamal failure. On that day aleo, righteous Libyans will 
rejoice at what has happened, and the screen will finally be lowered once 
and for all] on the catastrophe which has come upon our nation and which our 
people have suffered from so much. 


The freedom fighters of the Libyan National Democratic Movement observe with 
humility and reverence the death of their great martyr, ‘Amir al-Daghis. They 
ask that God have mercy on his pure and chaste soul and that his kinfolk and 
his people show patience as well as determination and steadfastness. On this 
sad occasion, the freedom fighters declare and take an oath, in the name of 
everything noble, lofty, and sacred, that the blood of the martyr ‘Amir will 
not go in vain, and that their reply will be stern, decisive, and above the 
Level of the crime committed by the bloodthirsty killer, Mi'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
against our people and our great nation. 


"Do not think of those killed in the service of God as dead. Rather, think of 
them as alive, with their Lord, and blessed by Him [Koranic verse] ." 


"Among the faithful there are men who have been true to their pledge to God. 
Some of them have passed away, and others are waiting to meet their Lord and

ACCOUNT OF CAREER OF A QADHDHAFI AGENT GIVEN 
London SAWT LIBIA in Arabic Mar 80 pp 24-26 
[Article: “How This Agent Began, and How He Ended Up") 


{Text} One of the most prominent agents who has now been enlisted in the 
service of the Qadhdhafi regime's intelligence network, for the purpose of 
operating abroad, is Mr Ahmad al-Salihin al-Huni. He was the last minister 
of information under the former monarchy and is the owner of the newspaper 
AL-'ARAB [the Arabs] which is currently published in London! 


Ahmad al-Salihin is about 50 years of age, was born in Egypt, and did not go 
to Libya until after its declaration of independence and the establishment 
of national rule in the early 50's. 


In view of the circumstances of his origin, which enabled him to receive only 
a very small degree of education, he was entrusted with 4 small administrative 
job in the ministry of agriculture in the province of Fazzan. It would not 
have been possible for him to get even this job, had it not been for the 
assistance of the Sayf-al-Nasr family which ruled the province at that time 
and which took a liking to him. 


About 2 years after he took this job, in the middle of the year 1956, to be 
exact, the authorities in the province of Fazzan discovered that this Ahmad 
al-Salihin was engaging in the collection of information about “the personal 
activities” of the authorities and high officials of the province. He was 
sending the information to the royal administrative offices in Tubrug and 
Tripoli in an attempt to curry favor and gain access to palace officials. 

He also wanted to draw attention to the fact that he was personally prepared 
to work and offer services which were in demand in this domain, that is, the 
domain of spying on people and gathering information for those who hold posi- 
tions of power and influence. 


Mr Sayf al-Nasr ‘Abd al-Jalil, the head of the Provincial Executive Council, 
was the one who had brought Ahmad al-Salihin from [the town of) Abu-al-Matamir 
in Egypt, and he was the one who had made it possible for him to get a govern- 
ment job in spite of the fact that he lacked the necessary qualifications. 

Mr Sayf al-Nasr considered himself to be al-Salihin's benefactor and considered

himself to be the one to whom 4l-Salihin was indebted. For that reason, 
‘Abd al-Jalil's reaction was very strong and severe. When the matter of 
al-Salihin's activity was discovered, ‘Abd al-Jalal did not hesitate to 
take the decision to fire him from his job in the provincial government. 
‘Abd al-Jalil forced Ahmad al-Salihin to pack his bags and go to the prov- 
ince of Tripoli to seek assistance and help in getting some other job from 
those to whom he had been sending the secret reporta~--the officials of the 


royal palace. 


In fact, these officials did intercede in al-Salihin's favor. He was appointed 
to @ position, similar to the one he had before, in the ministry of agriculture 
in the province of Tripoli. Thus Ahmad a1-Salihin was now close to the palace, 
close to the headquarters of the federal government, and also close to foreign 
embassies. Before long, this man became one of the more prominent agents who 
was working with palace circles, state security agencies, and the United States 
Information Agency. 


Ahmad al-Salihin would offer requested information to each party, individually, 
and would receive payment from all of them. At the same time, he was making 
contacts and consolidating his relationships with those in positions of influ- 
ence, and he was exploiting this in order to improve his own personal and pro- 
fessional situation. 


At the beginning of 1963, o11 became a fact of life in Libya, and American 
companies had managed to export it in very large quantities. At this point, 
the United States had really begun to totally extend its influence into polit- 
ical life in Libya and, in fact, had started to pull the rug from under the 
feet of the declining empire, that is, from under the feet of the English. 


American moves to spread American influence and total hegemony over the country 
took the form of pressure on King Idris to replace his government with one 
headed by Dr Muhyi al-Din (Fakini). 


Before his appointment as head of the government, (Fakini) was Libya's perma- 
nent representative in the UN. The Americans had discovered that he was a 
person who could be used and relied upon during the new phase beginning in 
Libya--a phase of abundance outpouring of oil and of securing American vital 
interests. 


Just as soon as his new government was formed, Dr Muhyi al-Din (Fakini) 
announced his decision to appoint Ahmad al-Salihin as the director general of 
the ministry of information!! Ahmad al-Salihin's star began to rise and to 
shine. He became a prominent personality not only with regard to investigative 
agencies, information gathering and spying on citizens, but also with regard to 
the CIA, the American Embassy and the services of American information offices 
in both Tripoli and Benghazi. 


Just a few years later, Ahmad al-Salihin became minister of information and 
culture. This marked the beginning of the era of buying off newspapers and 
writers, and the institutionalization of prostitution and political corruption 
in the media and in the domain of intellectual and cultural life.

In apite of all of his negative characteristics and all of the complexes and 
perversions involved in hia personality, 4l-Salihin possessed a good deal of 
frankness and courage when he talked to those with his close associates, 


Al-Salihin never kept secret from any of them that he had risen to high posi- 
tions of authority through the help of his American friends, and that he had 

a friendly and close relationship with the American ambassador, David (Newsome). 
And he told everybody with complete confidence that he would very soon be the 
head of the government. 


When he assumed his “responsibilities” as minister of information, Ahmad al- 
Salihin did not neglect to care for his own financial situation. Within a 
very short time, he became the owner of 4 number of villas and of an extensive 
area of land plots suitable for building and construction in good locations in 
the city of Tripoli. He was also able to increase his capital in foreign banks 
to the point where it reached the figure of 4 million dollars. This he managed 
to do by means of radio and television projects which were planned and imple- 
mented during his administration. 


He took @ large advance bribe from the English company (Adrus) to which he 
entrusted the project of furnishing the Institute of Music. Before that, he 
had already accepted a similar large bribe for the project of building the 
institute, which has not yet been built to date. 


He was also a partner with the “honorable deputy,” Ahmad al-Hajj ‘Ali al- 
Mahmudi, and al-Tahir al-Sagari , in forming a contracting company which, 
thanks to his “special efforts," was awarded the contract to build the 
Central Hospital in the city of Sabha. 


But it seems that things did not turn out as he had planned. American inter- 
ests, after that, began to neglect al-Salihin as they had neglected a number 
of others, and the tables turned completely. King Idris departed, and the 
military took over the reins of power. 


Al-Salihin, like other ministers who had served under the monarchy, was put 
in jail after 1 September, and he stayed in jail for several months. Never- 
theless, he did not lose hope. Instead, he began to dream of getting another 
position under the new [pro-American] military administration. 


Al-Salihin appeared on television only a few days after the success of the 
military coup. He was calm and collected, all smiles, and was talking about 
the evils of the monarchy which he himself had served as one of its ministers. 
He said publicly, and without the slightest embarrassment: "The soldiers of 
the [pro-American] coup are young men who have faith in their God, and their 
God has shown them the way”! 


After that, al-Salihin was released from jail completely unharmed. In fact, 
he actually was given a new position under the new regime. It must be an 
irony of fate! The level of his position was no higher than the position in
