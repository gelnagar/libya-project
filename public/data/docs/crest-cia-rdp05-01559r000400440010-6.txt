[Excerpt] Cairo, AL-SHARQ AL-AWSAT bureau— 
Knowledgeable sources have said that Egyptian Presi- 
dent Husni Mubarak is expected to visit Libya after he 
returns from his European trip. He is expected to hold 
important discussions with Libyan leader Col. Mu’am- 
mar al-Qadhdhafi on recent Arab and international 
developments. 

The same sources noted that an Egyptian-Sudanese 
meeting may be held at the summit level in the Libyan 
capital during President Mubarak’s visit, since Lt. Gen.

Mubarak’s visit. 

This anticipated meeting comes as part of Libyan 
attempts to reconcile Egypt and Sudan and clear the air 
in their bilateral relations. The sources noted that 
arrangements for this meeting had been discussed 
through direct diplomatic contacts among Egypt, Libya, 
and Sudan. 

Sudan had contacted Col. al-Qadhdhafi on an urgent 
basis following the sudden crisis with Egypt at the 
beginning of this month. [passage omitted]

Al-Qadhdhafi Marks Spanish Occupation of Tripoli 

LD2607195491 Tripoli JANA in English 1433 GMT 
26 Jul 91 

[Excerpts] Tripoli, Nasser [July] 26, JAMAHIRIYA 
NEWS AGENCY—The leader of the revolution Colonel 
Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi confirmed that the aggressive 
attack carried out by Spain on the city of Tripoli in 1510 
was not the first attack and will not be the last attack. In 
the significant speech he delivered, last evening, in the 
ceremony which was mounted in Tripoli, on the occa- 
sion of marking the Spanish occupation of Tripoli, he 
said a thousand years before this attack, another attack 
took place. The Vandals launched an attack from the 
same place in Europe, from Spain and Italy. They 
attacked the city and massacred its people. [passage 
omitted] 

Col. Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi said that America was 
seeking any justification for aggression against Libya or 
Iraq or any other Arab country. It always produces a 
thousand false pretext as a justification to carry out its 
aggression over the Arab nation. Col. al-Qadhdhafi 
stated: We are not idiots. Humanity is not idiot, it 
realises the aggression recurs without the existing pre- 
texts. He indicated that if there were some justifications 
now, what were the justifications then when Spain occu- 
pied Tripoli in 1510? What were the justifications for the 
Italian occupation of Libya in 1911? What were the 
justifications for the [Roman] aggression over Carthage 
in 218. [passage omitted] 

Col. Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi said that Europe always 
looked at the Arab homeland as a whole single target, 
which it could not occupy part of unless it occupied the 
other parts. Col. al-Qadhdhafi added: What we must 
understand from this lesson is: even the Israelis—who 
are a European assault, occupying Palestine—and Pales- 
tine was not occupied from the east or from the south but 
was occupied from the north. [sentence as received] 
{passage omitted] He added that the Israelis now con- 
sider the international conference as nonsense and that 
they are manufacturing atom bombs and nuclear mis- 
siles with 4,000 miles range indicating that such range 
will reach the region of North Africa, the Arabian 
Peninsula, Iraq, Sudan, and Mauritania.

Col. al-Qadhdhafi said that Jews and Christians were the 
Crusaders now and they are imperialism and Zionism: 
Those will never be satisfied with us unless we become 
slaves flowing them. This is their character. The leader 
talked in his important speech about what is called: the 
peace conference and the recognition of the Israelis, 
asserting: The war plants still produce atom bombs. The 
enemies continue to build their air and naval forces, day 
and night. Millions of immigrant Jews continue to flock 
into Palestine from all over the world and years later will 
attack Egypt, Syria, and Jordan, and establish their state 
from the Euphrates to the Nile. They cannot settle down 
until they drink from the Nile and Euphrates because 
they have no water of their own, this is inevitable. 

Col. Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi said that the Israelis did 
not care much whether the Arabs recognize them or not, 
because they continued to manufacture atom bombs 
relentlessly. He asserted that the U.S. did not intervene 
with the Israelis as it has done with Iraq, to destroy their 
weapons: The American Security Council [as received] 
did not issue a resolution stripping the Israelis of their 
atom bombs. Only the Arabs can strip the Israelis by 
force, and this does not come about except with Arab 
unity. The leader added: If Arab unity is not reached, the 
fate of the Arabs, everywhere, no matter how friendly 
they were by demanding peace, is the same as the fate of 
the Libyans on Thursday 25 Nasser 1510. [passage 
omitted] 

Col. Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi asserted: We will strike 
Italy and Spain if any attack or aggression against Libya 
was launched from them, despite our sentiments, feel- 
ings and wish for peace and more friendship with them. 
He indicated that if those nations were seeking to live in 
security and peace, then they must close all the bases of 
aggression against the Arab nations, do not assist aggres- 
sion, and not make their ports stations for the fleets of 
aggression, for ever. 

The leader said: From now on, all those nations must 
bear all the harm, the reaction and the counter attack 
from those upon which the aggression is launched 
whether they be Libyans or non Libyans. We are hostile 
to whoever is hostile to us and peaceful with all who seek 
peace with us, after that we do not care how many 
children and women die in Italy or Spain or their homes 
demolished, as our children and women die and our 
homes being demolished. Their children and their 
women must die and their homes must be demolished 
because whoever starts aggression is more tyrannical. 

The leader asserted the need to amass all potentials to 
build the economic and defensive capability, fortify the 
coast and plant the Libyan land with mines and thorns so 
that no one can set his feet upon it, and that is inevitable. 
He asserted that the Libyan Arab people wished to live in 
peace and security and develop its land but the invaders 
do not want that for us. They come to us all of a sudden 
in order to occupy our land as they occupied Tripoli on 
such a day 481 years ago. He pointed out that the
