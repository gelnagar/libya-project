TRIPOLI: SAUDI ARABIA DOES NOT WANT ARAB SUMMIT 


LD230044 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 22 Jun 82 


[Text] JANA has learned that Brother Yasir ‘Arafat, chairman of the PLO Executive Committee, 
has officially informed the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah that he had asked the 

Arab League to call an urgent, immediate Arab summit. In this regard, both Lebanon and Syria 
asked the league to convene an Arab summit, and Algeria, the PDRY, Kuwait, Qatar, Mauritania 

and Tunisia agreed. 


However, the secretary general of the Arab League seems to be unenthusiastic about convening 
a summit meeting and to be obeying the orders of Saudi Arabia, which does not want an Arab 
summit during this grave moment for the Arabs so that the Zionist enemy may be abie to 
entrench itself, achieve the objectives of its invasion of Arab territory, impose its con- 
ditions for the liquidation of the Palestinian Resistance, deal a blow to the Lebanese 
Nationalist Movement and encircle Syria.

mission to the Arab region: 


While the Zionist enemy continues his barbaric destruction of Lebanon and practices the 
ugliest forms of killing and terrorism and the whole world learns with certainty that America 
with its aircraft, tanks, fleets and various weapons of destruction is on the side of the 
enemy, the American administration boasts about human rights and sends one of its advisers, 
known to be American Zionism's instrument in the region, to find solutions and formulas 

that are, in fact, a natural continuation of the Camp David plot.

America, known for its international terrorism, must stop practicing the ugliest forms 

of international terrorist methods that threaten to destroy aidwipe out humanity. The

AL-—QADHDHAFI ON LEBANON, U.S. MANEUVERS, REAGAN 


AU211557 Vienna PROFIL in German No 25, 21 Jun 82 pp 24-25 


[Report on Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi's interview with a visiting Austrian "political 
scientists delegation" at his "headquarters" in Libya -- date not given] 


[Excerpts] On the war in Lebanon: 


"Libya is wondering why the Americans and Europeans continue to support Zionism, 
considering that it has been proved that Zionism is a shocking factor for world peace, 
and that the Israelis are barbarians and demagogues, against world peace and against 
mankind in general. As we know, they have now shown their true face, because in their 
latest attacks they have not distinguished between military and civilian targets. They 
have bombed civilians, killed children and old people and razed to the ground culture 
and civilization. Ultimately, this certainly will lead to a third world war as the 
Israelis caused World Wars I and II. However, I would very much like to know how Europe 
and America would react if an Arab nation had committed these barbaric acts. Europeans 
and Americans still have not realized that the Israelis are an evil to the whole world 
and that they will certainly touch off a world catastrophe. They have no morals and are 
barbarians. They want to destroy the whole world so as to remain alone." 


On Libya's role in the Lebanon conflict: 


"We will be in solidarity with the Palestinian people until they have regained their 
rights. However, God willing, the Europeans and Americans will pay the price, the price 
for their support of Israel. They will pay this price because the Israelis are conjur- 
ing up a third world war.... Hence, Europe and America must simply dissociate them- 
selves from these barbarians and cut off any military and economic support for Israel." 


On the U.S. intention to again hold maneuvers in the Gulf of Sidra in September: 

"That would mean world war..and it is certainly possible that a second world power would 
also intervene in this war -- the Soviet Union...we will offer resistance to aggressors, 
colonialists and conquerers..." 


On chances for Arab unity: 


"As long as the currently existing regimes are in power, I don't see any chance what- 
ever. Only when all these systems are abolished will Arab unity become real." 


On the Libyan model as the foundation for Arab unity: 


"The system of the Jamahiriyah is designed not only for the Arab states, but is world- 
wide." 


On his current assessment of U.S. President Ronald Reagan: 

"He has changed a little, no doubt. At first, he was a 100-percent ignoramus as far as 
international relations are concerned. Today, he grasps at least 25 percent of the 
world problems." 

On his visit to Austria in March 1982: 

"The visit to Austria was highly successful and paved the way for our future visits to 


other Western countries... In this context, I am highly satisfied with the endeavors of 
the Austrian chancellor to this end."

PAPER CALLS FOR COMPENSATION FOR WAR DAMAGES 


LD212028 Tripoli JANA in English 1844 GMT 21 Jun 82 


[Texg] Tripoli, Sha'ban 29, June 21, JANA News Agency -- Today’s issue of AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR 
newspaper emphasized Jamahiriyah's right for compensation for war damages, losses and their 
destructive effects on the environment. 


This came in a long article which debated the issue of world war damages in Jamahiriyah, 
international accords and laws, the declaration of permanent sovereignty over its natural 
resources and Jamahiriyah inability to take part in the organization and exploitation of 

its total resources arising from land mines planted in land and sea by imperialist countries. 


The paper pointed to these countries responsibility to remove these mines from Libyan lands 
and waters and to the 1973 charter, article 2, which stipulates the right of every country 
to exercise its full and permanent sovereignty including the right of use and exploitation 
of all its natural wealth and resources and its economic activities. Also, the right of 
all individuals or groups countries [as received] for cooperation to remove all obstacles 
hindering the exploitation and use of these resources. The presence of mines in Libyan 
territory are causing excessive damages and should be removed. Compensation should follow 
according to international laws and rights as well as on human basis and considerations.
