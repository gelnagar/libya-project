LIBYA

UGANDAN MINISTER ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI FOR TALKS 
LD282108 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1730 GMT 28 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--Paulo Muwanga, Ugandan minister of internal affairs, arrived in Tripoli 
this afternoon at the head of a delegation. During his visit to the Jamahiriyah he will 
hold talks on strengthening relations between the two countries. In a statement to JANA, 
he underlined the depth of the historical ties that bind the peoples of the Jamahiriyah 
and 'Iganda, adding that the visit would seek to deepen these ties further. 


Muwanga, attacking world imperialist circles, said that they were trying to fish in 
troubled waters in their efforts to cast doubt on relations between the peoples of the 
two countries. He was welcomed at Tripoli international airport by the secretary of the 
General People's Committee for Justice, the Ugandan ambassador and officials from the 
African and Protocol Departments of the Foreign Secretariat, 


CUBAN ECONOMIC OFFICIAL ENDS LIBYA VISIT 
LD281228 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 28 Nov 79 LD 
[Text] Tripoli--Angel Gomez, first deputy minister of the State Committee for Economic 
Cooperation in Cuba, and his accompanying delegation left Tripoli this morning after 
attending the meetings of the second session of the Libya-Cuban joint committee for 
economic, technical and scientific cooperation, which ended yesterday. 
He was seen off at Tripoli airport by the brother secretary of the Popular Committee for 
the Provisions Commodities, a representative of the cooperation administration at the 
Foreign Secretariat, and the Cuban ambassador to the Jamahiriyah. 

Results of Visit 
LD281250 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 28 Nov 79 LD 
[Text] Tripoli--Yesterday the meetings of the second session of the joint Libyan-Cuban 
committee for technical and scientific cooperation ended. At the conclusion of the meet- 
ings a minute on agreement on cooperation between the Jamahiriyah and Cuba in the fields 
of maritime transport, housing, agriculture, health and sports was signed.
