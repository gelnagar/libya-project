LIBYAN ENVOY DISCUSSES AL-QADHDHAFI INITIATIVE 
NC151736 Beirut Domestic Service in Arabic 1130 GMT 15 Oct 81 


[Text] Foreign Minister Fu'ad Butrus this morning met with the secretary of the. 
Libyan Brotherhood Bureau, Salih al-Duruqi, and discussed the nature of the 
Libyan initiative on the Lebanese issue which was announced by Col Mu‘ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi. After the meeting al-Duruqi replied to correspondents’ questions 
as follows: 


[Begin recording] [al-Duruqi] We discussed the question of Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's 
initiative on a Libyan role to save Lebanon from its ordeal. We discussed the 
details. We wanted to get the opinion of the minister and the Lebanese authority 
on this matter. 


[Question] What was the minister's opinion in this regard? 


[Answer] We know that the Lebanese authority supports any Arab role in the 

interest of Lebanon and the Arab homeland in general. His excellency the minister 
welcomed it very much, and gave some guidance on the subject of our contacts. As 
you know, we have not yet submitted the details of the initiative. We have post- 


poned this until we complete our contacts with all the parties and the legitimate 
authority. 


[Question] Can we know in brief whether the initiative means the dispatch of a 
Libyan envoy to Lebanon to hold contacts with the parties, or wiil this take 
place through your person? 


[Answer] It is possible at a later stage that an envoy will come or that a summit 


meeting will take place. This depends on the opinion of the Lebanese authority 
and the rest of the parties. 


[Question] Is it possible to learn the practical nature of this initiative? 


[Answer] The aim, I think, is clear. It is to bring about a decisive end to the 
Lebanese problem by benefiting from all the previous initiatives. We are seeking 
to find a concrete solution to the Lebanese problem, which we think affects the 


whole Arab homeland. Any delay in finding a solution will have a negative effect 
on all the Arabs. 


39 








[Question] Is it possible to learn the Libyan visualization of the solution? 
[Answer] As I have already stated, we have not worked out the details. 
[Question] Will the contacts you are holding include all the parties in Lebanon? 


[Answer] Yes. We are holding contacts and we shall hold contacts with any side 
that has an opinion which can benefit us in crystallizing the details and the 
features, which we shall try to carry out with the help of all the parties. 


[Question] Are there contacts with the members of the Arab Followup Committee 
[words indistinct]? 


[Answer] Perhaps we told this to his excellency the minister. We consider our- 


selves in one way or another as members of the Followup Committee, because the 
Followup Committee stems from an Arab summit conference which was held within the 
framework of the Arab League. We are members of the league and attended this 
conference. Therefore, we consider ourselves represented in it. We support any 
move. The success or failure of the committee does not affect nor does it con- 
tradict our initiative. 


[Question] Is there a specific date on which the Libyan initiative will begin 
to take effect? 


[Answer] There is no specific date. This depends on the completion of all the 
contacts and on providing the suitable atmosphere for this. [end recording]

BRIEFS 


WOUNDED TO MOSCOW FOR TREATMENT--Some 45 wounded and disabled members of the 
Lebanese Nationalist Movement left for Moscow yesterday for treatment in USSR 
hospitals. The wounded and disabled were transported by a special Soviet air- 
line plane and were accompanied by Dr ‘Ali Bazzi. ‘Ali Musawi, member of the 
Nationalist Movement Secretariat, said that a second batch of wounded will. leave 
for Moscow within 15 days. He said that the 7-month visa granted to the wounded 
people can be renewed depending on their condition. [Unattributed report] 
[Excerpts] [PM011153 Beirut AL-SAFIR in Arabic 25 Sep 81 p 5] 


U.S. ARMS CONSIGNMENT ARRIVES--A consignment of U.S. military equipment to the 
Lebanese Army arrived at Beirut port today. It consists of 44 armored troop 
carriers with their spare parts. A new consignment of French weapons that 
includes five armored vehicles will arrive at Beirut port on Tuesday. [Excerpts]
