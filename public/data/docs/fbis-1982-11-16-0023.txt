AL-QADHDHAFI INTERVIEWED ON ROLE IN MIDEAST 


GF101504 Kuwait AL-WATAN in Arabic 9 Nov 82 p 13 


{Interview granted by Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to AL-WATAN's Belgrade correspondent Yasin 
ar-Rawashdah in Belgrade -- date not given] 


[Text] [Question] Can you tell us briefly about the Libyan-Yugoslav talks held in 
Belgrade? How would you assess these talks? 


[Answer] They are positive talks reflecting the level of relations between the two 
countries to the extent of strategic cooperation. 


[Question] Let us, then, speak about the anxiety in the Arab region. The unity of 
Arab countries is one of the fundamental conditions necessary for resolving the Middle 
East crisis. What role can Libya play in this unity and in resolving this crisis?

[Answer] I agree that Arab unity is the key to resolving the Middle East crisis. 

For this reason, Libya is looking forward to playing a cohesive role aimed at uniting 
the Arab nation. Our determination to achieve Arab unity is certain either through the 
current political and social factors or by creating new factors. For the sake of 
achieving Arab unity, Libya is shouldering its responsibilities in this direction and 
will play a practical role in this regard. Anyway, in all circumstances, the presence 
of a Zionist state armed with all types of weapons of massive destruction, including 
nuclear weapons, represents a threat to the entire region and to international peace. 


After the latest expansion at the expense of the Lebanese people, the destruction of 
cities and the killing of thousands of innocent people, it has become clear that the 
racist state should be eliminated. In this case, the Arab people will be in alliance 
with the Jewish people to crush the racist Zionist state. There should be a serious 
stance by all the countries of the world toward the United States in particular to 
prevent it from supporting the racist Zionist state, which threatens peace with weapons 
and armament. 


The Jews are victims of Zionism and the Jews who died in Lebanon were in fact innocent 
victims. Zionism has led them to this hellfire. They are now being killed because of 
the heroic Resistance and the Nationalist Front in Lebanon. Naturally, they will die 
as long as they are being sent to these hopeless battles. Thus, the Jews are in need 
of being liberated from the fist of Zionism and from the racist Zionist state. If the 
racist Zionist state is eliminated, a democratic state for Palestinians and Jews can be 
established in the Middle East. 


[Question] One of the factors affecting Arab unity and the situation in the Middle East 
is the Iranian-Iraqi war, which causes division among the Arabs. What is your opinion 
regarding the possibility of ending this war? 


[Answer] In general, we and other countries have made efforts from the beginning with 
both Iran and Iraq, urging them not to resort to force in resolving the problems between 
them. After the eruption of the war, efforts to stop this war and return to negotia- 
tions continued. Neither of the two sides responded to these efforts and thus, the war 
reached the point where it will be impossible to return to the starting point. The 
results of war have made a return to the prewar point a very difficult matter. The 
problem has become so complicated that it will be very difficult to return to the start- 
ing point and resume mediation. Regrettably, the result of this war has become the most 
sensible thing, despite the bitter cost of it. 


{Question] Libya, as an African state hosting the forthcoming African summit confer- 
ence, must be concerned with the unity of the continent's countries. What are the 
steps Libya will carry out to ensure the success-of the conference and achieve African 
unity? 


[Answer] During this conference we want to concentrate on the economic issues, improve 
the social standard of the African citizen and solve the problems of African emigrants, 
African workers in Europe, Britain and the United States and problems of blacks in the 
United States, who are inhumanely treated and who live in bad conditions. The contin- 
ent is responsible for extricating those African workers from these conditions through 
the OAU.

In addition, we will try to resolve the political problems among the African countries 
and to settle the border disputes. We will also urge the wealthy African countries to 
offer assistance to the poor countries of the continent. We, as Africans, will contri- 
bute to the support of the Nonaligned Movement, whose hopes are the same as Africa's. 


[Question] What about the role of the Nonaligned Movement? 


[Answer] The Nonaligned Movement should play a more effective role in order to 
accomplish a minimum limit of protection for the movement's members against imperial- 
ist threats, such as the U.S. threats in the Gulf of Sidra and other places, such as 
its support for fascism in El Salvador, racism in Africa, Zionist arrogance in the 
Middle East and the massacres that were perpetrated by Zionism with the use of U.S. 
arms and in which there were 50,000 victims, all of whom were children, women and old 
men of the Lebanese and Palestinian peoples. 


When I speak about nonalignment I am speaking about the neutral, nonaligned and active 
countries, such as Yugoslavia and Libya, which can play an active role in the movement 
to fill the space that really exists between the two camps. The nonaligned countries 
should form a deterrent -- even a military one -- so that the superpowers’ retreat will 
not to be at their expense and to prevent the spillage of problems and wars into this 
shaky region, which is nonaligned and should remain nonbelligerent and a deterrent to 
prevent them from playing with fire in this region. 


We believe that the international peace of the past several years was not accomplished 
by ethics or negotiations, but is due to the deterrent that presently exists among the 
superpowers. 


[Question] The Jamahiriyah as a developing African and Arab state is influenced by the 
world situation and international situations certainly affect it. What is your opin- 
ion? 


[Answer] Of course, it is not possible to isolate any society from reacting to and 
arguing with the other societies. Naturally it is not possible to isolate Libya from 
the world. But Libya has its own characteristics. It reached the jamahiriyah system 
and achieved a specific leap in resolving the problem of authority, the problem of 
democracy, the problem of resources, the economic problem and the problem of the state 
in general. It has become a jamahiriyah, and the first jamahiriyah in the world. 


Libya has become the cornerstone in the era of masses, the era in which the rule of the 
masses who govern themselves directly and run themselves automatically will prevail. 

It is this society in which governments, parties, armies and rulers disappear to be 
replaced by the masses, whereby the people will perform the tasks. At that time, just 
peace will be achieved in the world, a permanent peace among peoples. The masses will 
replace the armies. The peoples have no aggressive ambitions against each other and 
the armed people will be in a defensive stance to deter aggression. 


The armed people are never, ever defeated. International peace will be achieved for 
the first time in the history of humanity. 


We see that the accomplishment of the jamihiriyah system in the world -- which Libya 
is preaching -- and the [implementation of the] third international theory, the popular 
committees and popular conferences, all these will solve the problems of the world.

Thus, Libya has its own characteristics which makes it bear many burdens that I wish 
the world could understand. 


We offer to the world a holy message; the message of peace, final liberation and the 
liberation of man everywhere. So will the world understand us? 


EMERGENCY AID DISPATCHED TO TUNISIA 5 NOV 


LD051246 Tripoli JANA in English 0919 GMT 5 Nov 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Muharram 19, Nov 5, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Major Khweildi Hamidi, 
assistant chief-of-staff of the Libyan Arab Armed Force, this morning supervised the 
dispatch of trucks carrying emergency aid offered by the Jamahiriyah people to the 
brotherly Tunisian people who suffered a national disaster as a result of the floods 
and torrential rain swept certain Tunisian regions. The Tunisian general commissioner 
in the Jamahiriyah attended the departure of the caravan together with the secretary of 
the General People's Committee for Social Security and the acting secretary of the 
People's Committee for Foreign Liaison Bureau. 


This solidarity gesture is in line with the national stands which reflect the natural 
unity among the Arab nation as a whole and between Tunisia and the Jamahiriyah in 
particular. 


This also materialize the objectives of the Great al-Fateh Revolution to achieve the 
overall Arab unity. 


Mohamed Jenifane, the Tunisian commissioner in the Jamahiriyah, expressed the thanks 
and appreciation of President Habib Bourguiba of Tunisia and the Tunisian people to 
Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi leader of the Great Al-Fateh Revolution and the Libyan 
Arab people for the emergency aid to reduce the sufferings and miseries resulted from 
this natural disaster. This humanitarian stand, he added, reflects one aspect of the 
rapid response between both brotherly people, this feeling which he said he felt during 
direct contacts with the Libyan masses.
