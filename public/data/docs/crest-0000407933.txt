Summary (AI): A CIA Counterterrorist Center commentary assesses that Libyan dissident Mansur Kikhia was likely abducted by Libyan operatives in Cairo in December 1993. It also notes Qadhafi's public vow to crush exiled oppositionists and his call for the assassination of Munim al-Huni.
• Kikhia arrived in Cairo on 29 November 1993 to attend a meeting of the Arab Organization for Human Rights (1-2 December).
• Kikhia visited family in Alexandria before returning to Cairo to meet his brother on 11 December.
• The commentary says there is strong circumstantial evidence that the Libyan government abducted Kikhia.
• Qadhafi accused Libyan oppositionists of working for US intelligence in several speeches over the prior two months.
• 1993-12-12: Mansur Kikhia disappeared from his Cairo hotel room; his insulin was left behind. He had planned to leave for Paris that day.
• 1993-12-12: Qadhafi publicly vowed to crush Libyan oppositionists in exile and called for the assassination of Munim al-Huni, an Egypt-based oppositionist.
