LIBYA

Resolutions Reduce Peoples Bureaus Abroad 
LD231700 Tripoli JANA in English 1630 GMT 23 May 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Sha‘ban 23, May 23, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY has 
learnt in accordance with the Basic People’s Congresses’ res- 
olutions about reduction of Libyan Arab People’s Bureaus 
abroad, the People’s Committee for Foreign People’s Bureau has 
in fact implemented this decision. 


Students in Italy Form Revolutionary Committee 
LD232120 Tripoli JANA in English 1806 GMT 23 May 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Sha*ban 23, May 23, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — In accordance with dicta “no revolutionary outside 
revolutionary committees” and “revolutionary committees 
everywhere,” the revolutionary force of the Libyan Arab stu- 
dents studying courses on electricity and light industry affairs in 
the city of Foligno in Italy met and formed a revolutionary 
committee. 


The revolutionary committee has confirmed in a cable to the 
leader of the revolution their commitment to preach the thought 
of the Third Universal Theory which heralds the liberation of 
humanity from all forms of oppression and terror that control the 
destinies of the people. 


It also confirmed their readiness to carry out the death sentence 
in defence of the civilized achievement realized by the Great 
al-Fatih Revolution and to destroy agents and hired people.
