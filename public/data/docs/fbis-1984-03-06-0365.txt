LIBYA

Masses Celebrate Abrogation of 17 May Agreement 
LD060310 Tripoli JANA in English 2027 GMT 5 Mar 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada al ULLA 30, March 5, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Giant popular marches were carried out 
in Tripoli this evening to celebrate the resouding victory of the 
free popular struggle in Lebanon which led to the abrogation of 
the May 17 1983 agreement between the Zionist enemy, Leb- 
anese Government and America. 


The masses surged forth in human chains, upon hearing the 
decision of abrogating the submissive agreement, in Tripoli’s 
streets and squares and headed towards the international confer- 
ences’ headquarters. 


The masses expressed joy for the patriotic victory that restored 
confidence to the Arab masses, enhanced the popular struggle 
for the inevitability of victory for the revolution and burned all 
the treacherous and humiliating papers and agreement signed by 
the symbols of treachery and submission to betray and stab the 
Arab nation’s bitter struggle against its enemies.

Al-Qadhdhafi Speaks on Abrogation 
LD060312 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1915 GMT 5 Mar 84 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 5 Mar (JANA) — The brother leader of the 
revolution delivered an important speech to the people who went 
out in huge popular masses this evening in Tripoli in jubilation 
over the abrogation of the humiliating 17 May agreement. 


The leader began his speech by saying that we were firmly 
committed to defeating the 17 May agreement, which is consid- 
ered a second stable David. Here last May we decided to struggle 
to burn this humiliating agreement. On that day an alliance 
began with the Lebanese natioal government, the Palestinian 
resistance and the Syrian Arab Republic in an effort to burn this 
agreement, which is considered stable David No 2. 


The leader continued saying: Today the Lebanese Government 
was forced to abrogate the 17 May agreement. He confirmed that 
the rifle is what abrogated and burned the 17 May agreement, 
and that the noble Arab blood is what erased the 17 May 
agreement in spite of America, in spite of the Israelis, in spite of 
the Phalangists, and in spite of the cowardly feeble Arab reac- 
tionarism. 


He stressed that the abrogation of the 17 May agreement was a 
clear victory for the holy alliance of the Islamic revolution in 
Iran, the people’s revolution in Libya, the Syrian Arab Republic 
and the National Salvation Front representing the Lebanese 
people. [passage indistinct] 


The leader of the revolution declared that as of today the Libyan 
forces stationed in the Al-Biqa’ valley should either join the fight 
of the Lebanese people against the Israelis, if there is a need for 
them, or return to Syrian territory or to the Libyan Arab territory 
after having accomplished their mission. 


The leader of the great 1 September Revolution said: We 
informed the rulers of the Arab nation last May that they had to 
define their stand toward the humiliating 17 May agreement, 
which usurped another Arab country after Egypt and annexed it 
in the service of the enemy and his strategy against the Arab 
nation. But they bowed their heads, with the exception of Syria, 
which stood with us and with the patriotic forces and the Iranian 
revolution against this agreement. [passage indistinct] 


The brother leader of the revolution affirmed that the humiliat- 
ing 17 May agreement (?was devised) by the United States. It 
was written by the U.S. Administration in favor of the Zionist- 
imperialist camp, and consequently the reactionaries did not take 
any stand against the 17 May agreement. The leader affirmed 
that the Lebanese Government signed the agreement on the order 
of the United States. 


He expressed great regret over the fact that no statement was 
made by the Arab governments, which reflected the lowest limit 
of arabism toward the agreement. 


The leader greeted the victory of the Lebanese masses and 
patriotic forces in abrogating the submissive agreement. He said

that the defeat is a defeat of the Phalangists, the Israelis, the 
Americans, and the defeatists who wanted to use the 17 May 
agreement to curse us and the patriotic revolutionary movement 
in the Arab homeland. But today we are savoring their misfor- 
tunes and we challenge those who thought that the 17 May 
agreement could not be abrogated. 


The leader of the revolution announced that the next battle will 
start with the Zionist enemy, who is occupying Palestine now 
after the shattering defeat of the Atlantic treaty and the enemy 
camp in Lebanon, and after Lebanon has been cleansed with 
blood. He pointed out that Israeli planes made crazy raids in an 
attempted reaction to the abrogation of the 17 May agreement. 
He said that despite the Israelis and the United States who made 
them, we say that these planes cannot be stronger than the U.S. 
planes that bombed the Druze and the Lebanese national move- 
ment in an attempt to destroy our revolution and stop our march 
toward the sea. Our national forces have reached the sea, and the 
US. troops have escaped to the sea, and this agreement has been 
burned. 


He stressed that a shattering defeat has been inflicted on the 
enemies by expelling them from Lebanon. They are running 
away like rats to the sea, taking refuge in the ships. He said that 
the U.S. soldiers, as a consequence of this defeat, fled before the 
Druze and the Shi‘ites, who are fighting with the rifle. 


He asserted that the humiliating 17 May agreement has been 
burned in front of the U.S. destroyer battleship New Jersey, 
which was used as a challenge by Reagan. He said that Reagan’s 
crooked nose has been rubbed in the dust by the rifle and the 
popular revolution. 


He again stressed that we must get prepared for the next battle 
with the Zionist enemy in order to expel him from southern 
Lebanon and liberate Palestine inch by inch, from the river top 
the sea and from Lebanon to Egypt. He said that the masses of 
the Arab nation are determined to continue the march until the 
Zionist camp is destroyed. We say to America that the battle has 
become overt between us and her and that we have decided to 
purge our land, either to become martyrs or expel the Zionists 
and the Americans and the reactionaries from our Arab land. 


He said that the United States had gone berserk in the face of 
the march and cohesion of the women and men of the Druze and 
Shi‘ites with the Palestinian resistence in Lebanon. The brother 
leader of the revolution stressed that the U.S. battleship New 

Jersey and the F-16 and F-14 planes failed to stop the march of 
the Druze, the Shi‘ites, the Lebanese national forces and Pal- 
estinian resistence. He also stressed that whatever the Israelis did 
and however mad they went, and however much they tried to shell 
homes, towns and villages to terrorize the Arab nation, the 
masses of the Arab nation will remain determined to continue 
the march until they destroy the Zionist camp. 


He stressed to the submisive Egyptian ruler and to the rulers of 
the Arab nations and to the Phalangists who are conspiring with 
the Zionist enemy, that the Arab nation is stronger than America 
and the Israelis because it possess the right of self-defense on its 
own soil.
