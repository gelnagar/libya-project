JANA POLITICAL EDITOR ATTACKS AS-SADAT 'THREATS' 


LD052118 Tripoli JANA in English 1827 GMT 5 Apr 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, April 5, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The defeated Egyptian president has 
issued threats against the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and Syria, and an 
ugly verbal attack against Saudi Arabia. In an interview published by the U.S. newspaper 
WASHINGTON POST ami distributed by the goverrment-controlled Egyptian news agency, the 
defeated president said if Libya becomes the source of serious problems, then Egypt will 
discipline it.
