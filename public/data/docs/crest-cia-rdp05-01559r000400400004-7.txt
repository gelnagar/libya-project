Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a live 7 April 1985 Tripoli Domestic Service broadcast of Qadhdhafi's speech at al-Fatih University on the anniversary of the students' uprising. The excerpt is mostly about Sudan's ouster of Numayri and attacks on the US, Egypt and Saudi Arabia; it does not describe Tripoli sites or local repression.
• Speech was broadcast live by Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic, 1120 GMT, 7 April 1985.
• Qadhdhafi praised the Sudanese people and General Suwar al-Dhahab after Numayri's overthrow and said the US had cut short Numayri's visit.
• The FBIS document is an out-of-turn excerpt, take 1 of 2, referencing an earlier FYI message LD071333.
• 1985-04-07: Qadhdhafi delivers a live speech at Al-Fatih University on the 9th anniversary of the students' uprising, broadcast on Tripoli Domestic Service; excerpt focuses on Sudan.

TAKE 1 OF 2 -- OUT-OF-TURN EXCERPT: AL-QADHDHAFI ON SUDAN 

LDO7 1506 

TRIPOLI DOMESTIC SERVICE IN ARABIC 1120 GMT 7 APR 85 

((SPEECH BY LIBYAN LEADER MU'AMMAR AL-QADHDHAFILAT THE AL-FATIH 

UNIVERSITY IN TRIPOLI ON THE 9TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE STUDENTS' 

UPRISING -- LIVE)) 

((EXCERPT)) ((PASSAGE OMITTED)) PERSECUTION MUST BE ELIMINATED; 

WE MUST ELIMINATE OPPRESSION; EXPLOITATION IS SHORT-LIVED. YOU 

HAVE SEEN FORCES THAT NEVER DREAMED THAT THEY WOULD BE TOPPLED; 

THE APPARANT NUMAYRI, MAY GOD BLESS HIS SOUL, WHO IS NOW IN THE 

DUSTBIN OF HISTORY, DAMNED AND UNWANTED, GOOD RIDDANCE, WAS 

GLOATING AND ERECTING ((WORD INDISTINCT)) IN THE STREETS. HE RELIED 

ON HIS MASTER CROUCHING IN THE DEFEATED CAPITAL ((CAIRO -- FBIS)). 

HE RELIED ON REAGAN. ((SHOUTING OF SLOGANS)) HE USED TO BELIEVE 

THAT FASCISM WOULD LAST INDEFINITELY. THAT POLICE RULE WOULD LAST, 

THAT RELYING ON AN ALIEN WOULD CREATE ((WORD INDISTINCT)) AND 

DO NOT LEARN LESSONS. HE DID NOT LEARN A LESSON FROM AL-SADAT; 

HE USED TO RELY ON AL-SADAT ((WORDS INDISTINCT)). HE DID NOT SAY: 

THIS HEAD OF WHICH I AM AN APPENDANT HAS BEEN CUT OFF. NO, HE 

CARRIED ON ATTACHING HIMSELF TO ANOTHER HEAD, SOWING IT WITH A 

NEEDLE; HE ATTACHED HIMSELF TO HUSNI MUBARAK. AND NOW HUSNI 

MUBARAK, BEHIND NUMAYRI, IS CONTACTING SUWAR AL-DHAHAB TELLING HIM: 

WE GOT RID OF NUMAYRI, IT IS A GOOD THING THAT YOU HAVE GOT 

RID OF THIS DISASTER. THIS IS THE MAN WHOSE BOOT NUMAYRI WAS 

LICKING. SAUDI ARABIA, ON HEARING THAT NUMAYRI WANTED TO GO THERE, 

CLOSED ITS AIRSPACE. YESTERDAY HE WAS RELYING ON SAUDI ARABIA 

AND THOUGHT THAT IT WOULD SUPPORT HIM. IT TOLD HIM: YOUR OWN 

PEOPLE HAS REBELLED AGAINST YOU AND HAS THROWN YOU OUT; IT CLOSED 

ITS AIRSPACE FOR 1 HOUR INFORMING THE WHOLE WORLD ABOUT THIS BECAUSE 

IT THOUGHT THAT HE WAS FLYING THERE. 

AS FOR AMERICA HE WAS THERE. AS SOON AS THEY HEARD THAT THE 

SUDANESE PEOPLE WAS IN CONTROL THEY WERE THE ONES TO CUT SHORT HIS 

VISIT. THE AMERICANS WERE THE ONES TO THROW HIM OUT. 

THEY HEARD THAT THE PEOPLE WAS DEFINITELY IN CONTROL IN SUDAN 

AND THEY TOLD HIM: RIGHT, NOW YOU MUST LEAVE. WE USED TO BE WITH 

YOU WHEN YOU WERE IN POWER. 

WHEN HE WAS IN POWER HOW MANY TIMES DID WE ADDRESS OURSELVES TO 

HIM SAYING: LOOK AT THE FATE OF THE SHAH OF IRAN. HAS AMERICA 

BEEN OF ANY USE TO HIM? AND IDRISS AL-SANOUSSI -- WE UNDERTOOK 

THE REVOLUTION WHILE HE WAS ABROAD IN EUROPE; DID AMERICA 

OR BRITAIN BRING IN THEIR FORCES TO BRING HIM BACK? FROM THE START 

HE PACKED UP HIS SUITCASES AND WENT TO AMERICA WHICH COULD NOT BRING

TO ALL THE CHEAP PEOPLE LIKE NUMAYRI TO THE EAST AND WEST OF US 

WE ADDRESS OUR WORDS AND TELL THEM THAT YOUR FATE WILL BE LIKE THIS,

NOT FEAR AMERICA AT ALL. ((CHANTS)) 

GREETINGS TO THE SUDANESE PEOPLE, TO THOSE HEROIC BRAVE 

PEOPLE WHO, DESPITE MASSACRES, REPRESSION, AND PRISONS, HAVE RECORDED 

A VICTORY FOR THE POPULAR MASSES' REVOLUTION. 

THE BROTHER GENERAL 'ABD AL-RAHMAN SUWAR AL-DHAHAB AND HIS 

COLLEAGES ARE TO BE THANKED FOR RESPONDING TO THE WILL OF THE 

SUDANESE PEOPLE. THEY HAVE IMPOSED NOTHING ON THE SUDANESE PEOPLE. 

ALL THEY DID WAS TO RESPOND ONLY TO THE WILL OF THE PEOPLE AND 

JOIN THE PEOPLE. THUS, IN HIS FIRST PROCLAMATION, SUWAR AL-DHAHAB 

DID NOT SAY: WE HAVE STAGED THE REVOLUTION OR WE HAVE DECIDED TO 

STAGE A REVOLUTION. HE SAID: WE HAVE DECIDED TO JOINT AND ALIGN 

WITH THE PEOPLE. 

IT WAS THE PEOPLE WHO OCCUPIED THE RADIO STATION, WHO ACTUALLY 

CLOSED THE BRIDGES OF ((WORD INDISTINCT)), THE WHITE NILE, WHO 

BESEIGED ALL THE STRATEGIC TARGETS AND AREAS, AND WHO ACTUALLY 

CAME OUT INTO THE STREETS, BOTH MEN AND WOMEN. NOTHING REMAINED 

FOR A NATIONAL PATRIOTIC MAN LIKE 'ABD AL-RAHMAN SUWAR AL-DHAHAB 

OTHER THAN TO JOIN AND ALIGN WITH THE PEOPLE, WHO DECLARED THAT 

THEY WERE AGAINST NUMAYRI AND THE POLICY OF NUMAYRI. 

I WANT TO SAY TO OUR BROTHERS IN SUDAN: WHY HAS NUMAYRI FALLEN 

AND REACHED THIS ABYSS WITH SUCH SPEED, AND WHICH WE PREDICTED 

A FEW DAYS AGO? THE END OF NUMAYRI BECAME INEVITABLE. EVEN WHEN I 

TALKED TO THE SUDANESE PEOPLE, ON SATURDAY EVE AND TOLD THEM THAT 

THEIR RENEDEVOUZ WAS IN THE MORNING AND THE MORNING WAS NOT TOO 

FAR. INDEED, THE MORNING BROUGHT THE END OF NUMAYRI AND HIS GANG. 

((APPLAUSE AND CHANTS) ) 

WHY HAS NUMAYRI MET SUCH AN ABOMINABLE END, THIS DISGRACEFUL 

END? THERE ARE INTERNAL AND EXTERNAL REASONS FOR THIS. THE FIRST 

NAIL IN NUMAYRI'S COFFIN, THE FIRST COLLAPSE IN THE POLICY OF 

NUMAYRI WAS HIS ATTACHMENT TO THE VANQUISHED CAPITAL AND HIS 

VISIT TO THE VANQUISHED CAPITAL AND HIS MARCH UNDER THE ISRAELI 

FLAG FLUTTERING IN THE YVANQUISHED CAPITAL AND HIS TAKING PART IN 

THE FUNERALOF THE INTERNED AL-SADAT -- WHO WAS KILLED BY THE 

EGYPTIAN PEOPLE -- SIDE BY SIDE WITH THE JEW BEGIN. NUMAYRI WALKED 

SIDE BY SIDE WITH BEGIN IN THE FUNERAL OF THE INTERNED AL-SADAT. 

THIS IS SOMETHING WHICH WE WILL NEVER FORGIVE HIM FOR. HE HAS NOW 

PAID THE PRICE FOR THIS.
