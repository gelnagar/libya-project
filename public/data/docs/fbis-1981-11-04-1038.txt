LIBYA

ATTACK ON AL-QADHDHAFI PLANNED FOR 10-15 NOV 


LD032332 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2300 GMT 3 Nov 81 
% 


[Text] Confirmed information published by several Arab and foreign news agencies, newspapers 
and radios reveals that an offensive plan aimed at the leader of the revolution will be 
executed between 10 and 15 November. This will coincide with the provocative military 
maneuvers which the forces will carry out west of Egypt. 


This information, which has been confirmed by the Greek papers RIZOSPASTIS, AVRIANI and 

AVGI, by the Kuwaiti AL-WATAN, the magazine AT-TALI'AH, published in Arabic in Paris, and by 
the Syrian AL-BA'TH, and by many Arab and foreign news agencies and radios quoting diplomatic 
circles, says that the plan is principally aimed at Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi by 

striking at all the places where the leader of the revolution is believed to be. 


According to the confirmed information published by world information media over the past 
2 days, preliminary measures will precede the operation, starting with strategic bombing 


by B-52's at a certain area inside Libyan borders. 


U.S. PLANS TO INTERVENE IN SUDAN, OMAN, SOMALIA 


LD031738 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 3 Nov 81 


[Text] UPI has reported that the U.S. Rapid Deployment Force formed an air bridge between 

the United States and Egypt to transport forces taking part in joint maneuvers. The agency 
reports that a number of U.S. transport aircraft yesterday arrived in Egypt carrying military 
and communications equipment to be used in the maneuvers. It points out that this airlift 
involves about 450 trips by C-141 transport and the huge C-5 aircraft. 


The U.S. State Department yesterday announced that Somalia and Oman have officially joined 
Egypt and Sudan to take part in the military maneuvers being held by the U.S. forces in 
the Arab region starting next week for 1 month. 


The Voice of America radio service has mentioned that these maneuvers will be taking place with 
each country separately and a few thousand U.S. forces will be taking part in them in 
addition to U.S. air support in which strategic U.S. B-52 bombers will participate. 


It is clear that the sea landing on the shores of Oman and Somalia, and the parachute landing 
in Sudan will not be (?an exercise) landing, as U.S. officials point out. However, the U.S. 
forces to be landed will stay to secure the regimes of Sudan, Oman and Somalia, since these 
regimes serve U.S. and Zionist interests. Information and reports stress that America will 
militarily intervene in the three countries under cover of these maneuvers.
