LIBYA

PALESTINIAN CONGRESS ELECTS OFFICIAL 


LD270932 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 27 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli--The masses of the Palestinian Arab people's general congress elected 

a secretary, assistant secretary and members of the secretariat of the congress during 

a meeting held by the congress in Tripohi last night. They are as follows: Dr Yahya Jabr, 
general secretary; 'Abd ar-Rahman Shahin, assistant secretary; Kamil Issa, member; 
Muhammad Surur, member; Mas'ud al-Qadar, member. 


V. 28 May 80 NORTH AFRICA 


YAR CHIEF OF STAFF ENDS VISIT 23 MAY 
LD231738 Tripoli JANA in English 1623 GMT 23 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli, May 23, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Brigadier 'Abd al-'Aziz al-Boti, North 
Yemeni chief of staff, left Tripoli today at the end of a short visit to the Jamahiriyah. 
In a statement to JANA correspondent, Brigadier al-Boti expressed his admiration for the 
Libyan Armed Forces! equipment and training. 


OIL SECRETARY RECEIVES DPRK ECONOMIC MINISTER 
LD241902 Tripoli JANA in English 1713 GMT 24 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli, May 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The oil secretary this morning conferred 
with the North Korean minister of external economic affairs and his accompanying dele- 
gation. Discussions centered on aspects of bilateral cooperation and the exchange of 
views regarding cooperation in the oil industry. 


Departure of DPRK Official 
LD251910 Tripoli JANA in English 1639 GMT 25 May 80 


[Text] Tripoli, May 25, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The minister of external economic 
affairs in the Democratic People's Republic of Korea, Kong Chin-tae, left this morning 
after ending a visit of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah during which 

he held talks on the consolidation of bilateral friendship and cooperation in all fields.
