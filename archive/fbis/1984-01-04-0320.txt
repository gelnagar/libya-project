LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Discusses Tunisian Events With Mzali 
LDO41051 Tripoli JANA in English 0941 GMT 4 Jan 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi al Awal 30, Jan4, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — There has been a telephone conversation between 
the leader of the revolution and Mohamed Mzali, the Tunisian 
prime minister. A similar telephone conversation took place 
between ‘Abd al-‘Ati al-‘Ubaydi, secretary of the people’s com- 
mittee for foreign liaision bureau, and Beji Caid es-Sebsi, the 
Tunisian foreign minister. 


The contacts were about the current events in Tunisia, and it has 
been agreed to coordinate and cooperate between the two coun- 
tries in these unfortunate circumstances. 


JANA learned that a Libyan delegation will arrive in Tunisia 
tomorrow according to what has been agreed during these con- 
tacts. 


Army Chief Jabir Receives Two PFLP Officials 
LD040802 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 4 Jan 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 4 Jan(JANA)— Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, 
commander-in-chief of the Armed Forces, has received the 
Brothers Taysir Qubba‘ah and Salah Salah, members of the 
Political Bureau of the Popular Front for the Liberation of 
Palestine.
