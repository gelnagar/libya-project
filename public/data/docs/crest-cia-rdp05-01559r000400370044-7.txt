Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a Libyan news agency (ARNA) report says Colonel Muammar al-Qadhdhafi sent Soviet leader Leonid Brezhnev a congratulatory message on his re-election as party secretary general at the 25th Party Congress. It concerns Libyan foreign policy and has no specific Tripoli locations.
• The message was carried by Tripoli ARNA in English at 1659 GMT on 14 March 1976.
• Qadhdhafi wrote that he congratulated Brezhnev on the confidence the Congress showed in him and on the success of the 25th Congress.

TRIPOLI ARNA IN ENGLISH 1659 GMT 14 MAR 76 LD : 

((TEXT)) TRIPOLI, LeAsRey MARCH 14, ARNA=- COLONEL MU* AMMAR 
AL- QADHDHAF1, CHAIRMAN OF THE REVOLUTIONARY COMMAND COUNCIL» HAS 
SENT THE FOLLOWING MESSAGE TO MR. LEONID BREZHNEV, THE SECRETARY 
GENERAL OF THE SOVIET COMMUNIST PARTY: | 

"IN THE NAME OF THE PEOPLE GF THE LIS8YAN ARAB REPUBLIC AND IN 
THE NAME OF THE REVOLUTIONARY COMMAN CQUNCIL, 1 HAVE THE PLEASURE 
TO CONGRATULATE YOU, NOT FOR YOUR RE-ELECTION AS SECRETARY GENERAL 
OF THE PARTY, FOR THIS TS A HARD TASK AND A HEAVY BURDEN, BUT I 
CONGRATULATE YOU FOR THE CONFIDENCE OF THE CONGRESS IN YOU WHICH 
CALLED FOR YOUR UNDERTAKING THE CONTINUATION OF YOUR POSITION. 

WT ALSO CONGRATULATE YOU FOR THE SUCCESS OF THE 25TH CONGRESS 
WHICH INCARNATED THE MAGNIFICENT ACHTEVEMENTS OF THE PEOPLES OF 
THE SOVIET UNION UNDER THE LEADERSHIP OF YOUR PARTY. 

WT TAKE THIS OCCASION TO LOOK FORWARD TO MARCH AHEAD ON THE 
PATH OF THE HISTORICAL FRIENDSHIP BETWEEN THE ARABS AND THE 

SOVIETS AND YOUR CONTINUATION IN THE LEADERSHIP OF THE PARTY 

MAKES THIS HOPE FEASIBLE SHORTLY, BY GOD*S WILL. 

"ACCEPT MY REGARDS. COLONEL MU‘AMMAR AL— QADHDHAFI. 141659
