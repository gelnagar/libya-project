Summary (AI): A State Department routing slip and interagency memo on a restricted Libya Stair-Step Plan, a series of graduated U.S. military operations near Libya, discussed at a November 1982 meeting. The OCR is heavily garbled, and the document describes only planning and review procedures, not Tripoli sites or events.
• Interagency Group No. 14 meeting on the Libya Stair-Step Plan was scheduled for Monday, late November 1982 at 3:45.
• The NSC memo recommends that Steps 4-5 plans go to SIG-level principals, and Steps 6-8 go through JRC channels, with a formal proposal ten days in advance when possible.
• Steps 1-3 of the plan reportedly drew no hostile reaction, so the fleet could continue similar operations.
• The stair-step matrix lists graduated operations in the air, sea and missile categories, with latitude bands around 30-33 N, and is marked Secret.
