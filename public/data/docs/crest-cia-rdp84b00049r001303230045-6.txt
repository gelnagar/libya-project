Summary (AI): A 1982 CIA briefing summarizes Libyan subversion, military training and political activity across sub-Saharan Africa, country by country. Nothing in it concerns Tripoli itself.
• Libya provided $150 million in 1981 for military construction in Sudan, under an August 1981 treaty with secret military annexes.
• Libya trained and equipped a 3,000-man dissident Somali group in Ethiopia and trained Somali dissidents at Sabha, Libya.
• Around 100 official Libyans were based at the Libyan People's Bureau in Cotonou, Benin.
• Libyan cargo planes averaged about two a week to Ghana after the December 1981 coup, ostensibly carrying foodstuffs and medical supplies.
• Libya backed coup attempts in Sudan in 1975 and 1976 and in Niger in 1976 (Niger coup attempt 1976).
