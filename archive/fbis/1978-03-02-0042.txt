LIBYA

U.S. REPORTEDLY ATTEMPTS TO INTIMIDATE LIBYA 
LD231616Y Tripoli JANA in English 1514 GMT 23 Feb 78 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, February 23, JANA--JANA Political Editor writes: It becomes clearer 
daily that the American Government is the main terrortst power in the world as the 
leader of colonialism and the enemy of all nations. Her deeds are at variance with what 
she preaches. Thus, she is the first to violate human rights through her odious attempt 
to dominate smaller states. 


This can be shown in the statement issued by the State Department denouncing the assassina- 
tion of Yusuf Siba'i. The statement has called for the stepping up of international 
cooperation to fight terrorism, yet as is expected of those totally immersed in terrorist 
activities, it has overlooked the raid of As-Sadat's special forces on Larnaca Airport. 

It goes without saying that the reason for the State Department silence on As-Sadat!s 
outragious aggression against Cyprus is that As-Sadat is one of their agents and a tool 

in spreading terrorism within Egypt, the Arab nation and Africa. 


To condemn As-Sadat would be to condemn the United States and Israel, the conspirators 
and initiators of the abortive Larnaca operation. Had it not been for this conspiracy 
the State Department would not have hesitated in condemning the terrorism unleashed 

by one state against another member state in accordance with UN resolutions and the UN 
Charter which the U.S.A, has endorsed. 


Moreover, the state department is so impudent as to attempt to intimidate the Jamahiriyah 
in order to stop here from giving her full and unconditional support to the liberation 
movements struggling for freedom, independence and their right to self-determination 
throughout the world. Nothing infuriates the United States more than the support tne 
Fatah revolution offers to the Palestinian people and their cause in defence of their 
right to return to their homeland and establish an independent state. 


During President 'Abd an-Nasir, the United States was enemy number one to the Arab 
nation and especially to Egypt. But now that As-Sadat has capitulated he has become 
their closest friend; his treason is looked upon as courage and his aggression on Cyprus 
as not liable to condemnation. In order to stop its aggressive campaign against the 
Jamahiriyah, the United States expects the Jamahiriyah to follow As-Sadat's step and 
renounce all the principles and values laid down by the Fatah revolution. Should the 
Jamahiriyah refuse to capitulate and continue her support of the Palestinian and 
African people struggling together with all the Third World states against colonialism 
and for the protection of their natural resources, the United States would alter its 
values and carry out the most horrendous acts of terrorism to initimidate Jamahiriyah, 
which attempt is doomed to failure.

The American colonialist mentality has not understood the true nature of the great 
Fatah revolution and the Jamahiriyah people's belief in justice which, through by God's 
help, [as received] is invincible. The history of the U.S. is full of crimes against 
nations as shown by the American Congress committee reports which exposed the crimes 
committed by the American administration in Vietnam, Cambodia, Laos and Latin America, 
besides the American role in toppling the elected regime in Chile. 


In his speech on the anniversary of the evacuation of American troops from the soil of 
the Jamahiriyah, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that "terrorist practices today are similar 
to those of the 19th century. We have to draw our conclusions to avoid similar pit- 
falls as our history has indicated that no power on earth can ever stifle the people's 
resistance in Jamahiriyah, nor could the barbaric campaigns repress the people who are 
determined to defend their homes and their freedom; such was the case in Derna, which 
witnessed a valiant resistance in June 1805. [no closing quote as received] Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi indicated that the Libyans were at present exposed to a new wave of 
international terrorism designed to subdue and humiliate them. 


The American Navy of 1977 is the sane as that of 1805. The reactionary conspiracies 
hatched by the imperialist stooges in the past are identical with today's, but the 
Jamahiriyah people who have adamantly resisted imperialism in the past are strong 

enough to thwart any new campaigns. Colonel al-Qadhdhafi stressed that the legitimate 
struggle of the Jamahiriyah, Tunisia, Algeria and Morocco were considered by imperialists 
as acts of piracy. In fact these people were waging a sacred war for the protection 

of their homes and lands. He affirmed that the Arabs who are struggling to restore their 
usurped land (Palestine) are not pirates as alleged by the West. Only those who occupy 
land by force are the real terrorists and pirates. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi denied American-Zionist allegations that the Arab nation was waging 
a terrorist war. He said: "We have no objections to the question of terror being 
brought before the United Nations as we are the first to fight terrorism. We appeal to 
the UN to intensify its efforts to abolish terrorism, specifically by the fleets of 
imperialist countries, who threaten smaller states. He affirmed that the Zionist 
invasion, supported by the superpowers, against Palestine in 1967 and in 1973 against 
other Arab countries is the best example of international terror.
