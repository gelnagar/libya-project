November 25, 1981 

SECRET/SENSITIVE 

Senior Interdepartmental Group Memorandum No. 14 

PARTICIPANTS: See Attached List 
DATE AND TIME: November 25, 3:30 p.m. 
PLACE: Room 7219, State Department 

SUBJECT: SIG Meeting on Libya 

Summary of Conclusions 

Under Deputy Secretary Clark's chairmanship, a restricted 
SIG convened at 3:30 p.m., November 25, to take up the question 
of U.S. policy toward Libya. Options 5 through 9 of the IG op- 
tions paper were discussed and agreed to, subject to minor in- 
sertions and changes requested by Defense and the Joint Chiefs 
of Staff. These changes will be made and a revised paper pre- 
pared by COB Friday, November 27. 

At 4:15 p.m., the restricted session was expanded by the 
addition of OMB, Treasury, STR, Commerce, Justice and Energy. 
Options 1 through 4 were discussed. Both Justice and Energy 
pointed to difficulties in implementation. No vote on these 
options was taken. All present were instructed to submit 
revisions or new material for inclusion in the paper by noon 
Friday, November 27. Silence would signify concurrence. The 
target for moving the subject to the NSC was set in the week of 
November 30. 

NSC Director Allen suggested that the subject might be 
brought to the President's attention first through a small 
briefing, perhaps using NSPG time, and later through a full NSC 
meeting or meetings. He emphasized the need to maintain strict 
confidentiality. 

Chairman Clark emphasized to all that the options as stated 
were subject to modification to suit changing circumstances.
