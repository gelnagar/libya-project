Summary (AI): A 1982 National Photographic Interpretation Center note reports construction of a new launch pad at the Jarmah missile launch site (OTRAG) in Libya, plus a new support building and a water reservoir extension. The site is in the southern desert, far from Tripoli.
• Coordinates given: 26-33-05N 013-09-50E.
• Erector-launcher normally parked at the launch position was removed to facilitate construction.
• Part of the launch position was paved with concrete.
• A new support building was constructed near the centre of the site.
• Water reservoir in the southwest portion of the site was being extended.
• 1982-08-13: New launch pad constructed on the previously identified launch position; erector-launcher had been removed; a new support building was built near the site centre and a water reservoir extension was under way.
