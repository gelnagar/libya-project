LIBYA

\ AL-MABRUK COMMENTS ON OIL PRICES, DECLINE OF DOLLAR 
LD140019Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 15 Dec 78 LD 


[Text] Brother 'Izz ad-Din al-Mabruk, the petroleum secretary, confirmed that maintain- 
ing the dollar as the pricing unit for oil will not benefit producers even if the next 
conference decides to increase oil prices because the continued decline of the dollar will 
swallow the increase. The petroleum secretary explained at a press conference in Abu 
Dhabi that the dollar, even if it rises a little, will decline again because the U.S. 
balance of payments has a growing deficit and inflation is on the increase, Brother 'Izz 
ad-Din al-Mabruk said industrialized countries refuse to link the rise in the price of oil 
to increases in the price of consumer goods and raw materials. He pointed out that in 
addition to not being able to overcome inflation, the industrialized countries had pur- 
posely allowed the dollar to decline in order to reduce the oil producers! incomes at the 
same time they were asking those producers not to raise the price of oil.
