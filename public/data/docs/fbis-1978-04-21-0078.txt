In his statement to the magazine AL-WATAN AL-!ARABI , the brother colonel spoke of the

In reply to a question about the Zionist aggression against southern Lebanon, the brother 
colonel said that the Zianist aggression against southern Lebanon was undoubtedly directed 
against the Palestinian people and resistance. But this took place in the territory of 
an Arab state. This led to complications for a number of the Arab countries which tried 
to send troops and material to the Palestinian resistance to deal with the aggression. 

But these forces and military equipment had to pass through the borders and across the 
territory of a country adjoining the scene of the fighting. 


Speaking of the latest developments in some parts of the African Continent and the internal 
problems and frightful imperialist schemes they are being subjected to, the brother colonel 
said: We have exerted great efforts to solve these problems from within the African 
Continent. This is because we believe that Africa belongs to the Africans and that the 
solution of their domestic problems should be found by their own hands. It was because 

of this that the Jamahiriyah joined hands with its neighbors Niger, Sudan and Chad to 

solve the problem of the Chad and put an end to the civil war there.
