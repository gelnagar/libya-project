LIBYA

SPOKESMAN FAULTS U.S. STATEMENT ON CHAD SITUATION 


LD110846 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 11 Jan 81 


[Text] A spokesman for the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison has stated that the United 
States is the last to be able to talk about matters and issues affecting the essence of 
relations between the Libyan and Chadian peoples. 


The spokesman was thus comuenting on the U.S. statement issued in the wake of the Libyan 
Arab-Chadian joint statement. He said that the United States was trying to divert the 
attention of world public opinion from its military bases in the various parts of Africa, 
Asia and the Middle East as well as in Latin America and also from its fleets which roam 
regional waters to frighten peoples. 


The United States, he said, should withdraw its fleets and bases from these areas before 
permitting itself to talk about what it calls the Libyan military presence in Chad. 


12 Jan 81 NORTH AFRICA 


AL~"UBAYDI DISCUSSES CHAD WITH AFRICAN ENVOYS 


LD110900 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 11 Jan 81 


[Text] Brother ‘Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, the secretary of the popular committee of the 
People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison, yesterday evening met with the African ambassadors to 
the Jamahiriyah. He spoke to them about the clamor being raised by imperialist circles over 
the Libyan Arab-Chadian joint statement. He said that these circles are trying to impede the 
efforts being exerted to rebuild Chad and establish peace and security in that fraternal 
country. 


Al-'Ubaydi said that the Jamahiriyah is not seeking to threaten peace and security in the 
area, as some try to do, but is seeking to bolster the peace, security and stability of the 
Chadian people. 


He also affirmed the Chadian people's right and that of the Jamahiriyah to establish bi- 
lateral relations in accordance with the UN and OAU Charters. 


He explained the contents of the Libyan Arab<hadian joint statement to the African ambassadors 
and warned that France's attempts to interfere in African affairs and to restore its domina- 
tion cannot be ignored or idly tolerated. 


He asked why no clamor is raised when France sends its troops anew to Africa with a view to 
reimposing its domination on it; why a clamor is raised only when the Jamahiriyah rushes 

to the aid of Chad in accordance with the resolutions of the Lagos conference and the 
Freetown summit and to bolster the efforts of the legitimate government in crushing the 
rebellion, he asked. 


TOGOLESE CABLE OPPOSES LOME SUMMIT ON CHAD ISSUE 


LD120834 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0245 GMT 12 Jan 81 
[Text] The brother commander has received the following cable from Togolese President Eyadema: 


Dear commander: The President of Sierra Leone, in his capacity as the current OAU chairman, 
has conveyed to me the need for holding an emergency meeting at Lome next Monday, 12 January 
1981, of the Chad reconciliation committee, which had signed the Lome cease-fire agreement 

on 28 February 1980, to discuss the latest developments in Chad, but I have rejected his 
request. Similar requests were made by a number of other African presidents who stressed the 
need for holding such a conference in Lome. However, I have rejected all their requests. I 
told them that I am prepared to welcome them in Togo as guests, sharing the Togolese people's 
celebration of their national independence anniversary on 23 January. I categorically reject 
the holding of any meetings in Togo on the question of Chad. 


Brother commander: The Togolese people and I personally view with admiration and pride your 
efforts to increase cooperation between our two friendly peoples. 


Finally, dear brother, accept my utmost appreciation and greetings on the occasion of the 
new year. 


[Signed] General Eyadema, president of the Republic of Togo
