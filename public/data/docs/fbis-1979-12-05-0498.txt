Prime Minister Departs 
LDO22210 Tripoli JANA in English 1635 GMT 2 Dec 79 LD 


(Text] Tripoli, Dec 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Mauritanian prime minister and vice 
president of the military Committee for National Salvation Mr Mohammed Ould Khouna 
Heydalla and his accompanying delegation left Tripoli today at noon after ending a two 
day visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


Present at Mr Heydalla's send-off in Tripoli intemational airport were Staff-Major 'Abd 
as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, members of the General People's Committee and Mauritania's 
ambassador to the Jamahiriyah. 


In a statement to our correspondent Mr Heydalla expressed appreciation for the hospitality 
with [which] his accompanying delegation and himself were received throughovt the visit to 
the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


He said the talks he conducted will have the highest positive effects on future relations 
between two brotherly peoples and that his visit fell within the framework of strengthening 
existing relations between Mauritania and Libya.

PETROLEUM MINISTER CALLS FOR OIL PRICE RISE AT CARACAS 

LDO41356 Kuwait KUNA in Arabic 1255 GMT 4 Dec 79 LD 

[Text] Kuwait--The Libyan Secretary of Petroleum 'Izz ad-Din al-Mabruk today said that 
OPEC will raise its prices at the conference it will be holding in Caracas on 17 December. 


He called for the increase to be big, however, he did not mention the percentage of the 
expected increase or the percentage to be sought by Libya. 


Al-Mabruk who is here to attend a conference of the Arab petroleum ministers, told jour- 
nalists that the major world oil companies "are exploiting" the present oil situation in 
the world because of the shortage of Iranian oil supplies and have begun selling oil on 

the free markets at prices reaching $45 per barrel. 


The price per barrel set by OPEC last June varies between $18 and 23.5. However, some 
members of the organization, among them Libya, have broken through the price ceiling. 


Al-Mabruk called on OPEC to take a decision in the Caracas conference aimed at absorbing 
the difference between the companies! prices and the OPEC prices. He stressed that a 
decision to increase prices will be taken at the conference; however, he made it clear 
that Libya does not favor a gradual increase in prices.
