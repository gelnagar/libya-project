LIBYA

"ARAFAT, DELEGATION ARRIVE IN TRIPOLI 22 SEP 


NC221634 (Clandestine) Voice of Palestine in Arabic to the Arab World 1615 GMT 
22 Sep 80 


[Text] Brother commander Abu ‘Ammar arrived in Tripoli this morning leading a Palestin- 
ian delegation, including the secretaries general of the Palestinian revolution groups, 
at the official invitation of Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. 


Our correspondent has learned that Abu ‘Ammar will hold talks with Al-Qadhdhafi and 
other Libyan officials on the current Arab situation in general and the developments 
in the Palestinian question in particular as well as the issue of Syrian-Libyan unity. 


PEOPLE'S CONGRESS EXTRAORDINARY SESSION ISSUES RESOLUTION 

LD221845 Tripoli JANA in English 1630 GMT 22 Sep 80 

[Text] Tripoli, 22 Sep (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The secretariat of the General People's 
Congress has issued the following resolution calling for an extraordinary session: 
Following the resolutions and recommendation of the people's congress extraordinary 


meeting on September 2, it has been decided: 


1. To convene the General People's Congress at the People's Hall in Tripoli to draft 
the basic people's congresses [as received] resolutions. 


2. This resolution is to be published in the official newspaper and is in force as 
from date of issue.

LIBYA TO REDUCE OIL PRODUCTION FROM 1 OCT 
LD221508 Tripoli JANA in English 1418 GMT 22 Sep 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 22 Sep (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah is to cut its oil production by 10 percent. According to the sources of 
oil secretariat the reduction of oil output will start from October l. 


AL-QADHDH.\FI ADDRESSES POLITICAL INSTRUCTORS 
LD180900 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 18 Sep 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Sep (JANA)--Last night our brother the leader met political instructors. 
At this meeting the leader explained that the decision taken by the Libyan people to 

turn Libya into a state of confrontation was a serious decision and will help Libya 

perform its total pan-Arab role in confronting the enemy and in restoring the honor and 
dignity of the Arab nation and in wresting its freedom. 


At this meeting the leader stressed that unity between Libya and Syria is the unity of 
the working masses and that the revolutionary forces in the two countries must work for 
the establishment of this unity and for confronting the forces which are hostile to the 
unity and the masses. 


Our brother leader emphasized the need to confront regionalism and destroy its basis. 

He said unity between Libya and Syria is a sign for the destruction of regionalism so 
that the Arab countries can join this unity one after the other, since we have opened the 
gate of the unionist era which is the starting point for the liberation of the land. The 
regionalists will realize that their regionalism is a shield that will not give them 
protection.
