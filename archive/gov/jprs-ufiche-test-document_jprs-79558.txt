MEA SIGNS AIRBUS DEAL--Middle East Airlines has signed a $350 million contract 
for five wide-bodied European-built Airbus airliners, with an option on 14 
others. The contract was signed by MEA Chairman Assad Nasr and Airbus Indus- 
trie Generali Manager Roger Beteille in Paris on October 30. The Beirut-based 
airline will equip the Airbuses with Pratt and Whitney engines. The deal 
includes spare engines and spare parts. Delivery will begin in the spring of 
1984. The aircraft, which will seat 207 persons, including 24 first-class and 
183 economy class passengers, will be more economical in terms of fuel costs 
than the Boeing 707s which form the bulk of MEA's present fleet. The signing 
of the deal was originally due to take place earlier this year, but was post- 
poned as a result of the losses inflicted on the airline by security problems 
in Lebanon (AN-NAHAR ARAB REPORT & MEMO, May 11, October 10). [Text] [Paris

U.S. THREAT--Addis Ababa, 16 Nov (JANA)--The Political Committee of the 
tripartite friendship and cooperation treaty between Ethiopia, Democratic 
Yemen and the Jamahiriyah will start its meeting here today. The three 
countries are represented in the committee by the secretary of the People's 
Committee of the foreign liaison bureau from the Jamahiriyah and the two 
ministers of foreign affairs for both Democratic Yemen and Ethiopia. The 
meeting is aimed at discussing the threats of the U.S. military manoeuvres 
currently taking place in the Arab region. Mr al-'Ubaydi, the Libyan 
foreign liaison secretary, said at his arrival in Addis Ababa, that the 
U.S.A. is trying to draw the attention of threats that do not exist in 

the region, in order to gain control over the Arab region and Africa, and 
to achieve its colonialist goals. He added if there is any threat to 

peace in the region, then its coming from America which undermines people's 
freedom. [Text] [LD161512 Tripoli JANA in English 1418 GMT 16 Nov 81] 


OMAN MESSAGE TO CONGRESS--Tripoli, 16 Nov (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The 
permanent secretariat of the Arab People's Congress received a cable from 

the Central Executive Committee of the Popular Front for the Liberation of 

Oman on the occasion of the emergency session of the Arab People's Congress. 

The text is as follows: "The emergency session of the Arab People's Congress 

in the Jamahiriyah is being held at a time when the Arab nation is witnessing 

a conflict between the revolutionary forces on the one hand and imperialist, 
Zionist and reactionary regimes in Sudan, Solamia and Oman on the other hand. 

The imperialist onslaught is gathering special momentum in the region with 

U.S. military manoeuvres operating freely in the region.... The U.S.A. is deter- 
mined to subjugate the Arab nation and impose its hegemony on its resources. 
Furthermore, these military exercises, in addition to constituting a direct 
threat to progressive Arab regimes on whose borders the manoeuvres are taking 
place, threaten security and stability in the region. They represent an act 

of invasion against these countries and a prelude to actual occupation of 

Arab oil fields, especially those in the Arab Gulf Region and the Arab Peninsula. 
The Arab masses who reject subjugation and capitulationist settlements outlined 
by al-Sadat draw attention to your conference to urge you to take a staunch 

stand against the attack and to abort all capitulationist settlements in the 
Arab region." [Text] [LD160954 Tripoli JANA in English 0852 GMT 16 Nov 81]
