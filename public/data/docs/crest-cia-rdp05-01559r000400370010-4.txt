Summary (AI): A garbled 1975 FBIS-style wire from London reports that Colonel Muammar al-Qadhdhafi met with Arab Socialist Union (ASU) leaders attending a cultural course in Tripoli on 10 August 1975. He criticized capitalism and communism on economic grounds, and Major Abd al-Salam Jallud was present.
• The report was transmitted from London and cites an Arabic-language broadcast at 1030 GMT on 10 August.
• Qadhdhafi argued that capitalism forces markets for arms and that communism deprives people of ownership and incentives to work.
• The document is heavily garbled by OCR, so some wording is uncertain.
• 1975-08-10: Qadhdhafi met with ASU leaders attending the first cultural course of 1975 and spoke on economic issues, criticizing capitalist and communist systems.
