SUBJECT » Additional Materials on Libyan Activities for DCI 

"1. The attached memorandum from the DCI indicates that we are likely 
to have an NSC meeting on Libya next week. For such a meeting, he requests 
a fully rounded picture of Libya's mischief making around the world. 

2. As you are aware, early this week I requested information on Libyan 
activities in the Middle East, South Asia, and Africa. A copy of that material 
is attached. 

. 3, Please ask your people to provide similar material on any Libyan 
activity in other parts of the world such as Latin and South America, Europe, 
and the Far East. My. perception is that there is less activity in those areas 
than in the areas already covered, but we need to provide the Director a 

_ comprehensive examination. : 

4. Because the timing of an NSC meeting next week is anyone's guess, we. 
ought to plan on having the materials no later than noon Tuesday, 16 February. 
We must recognize, however, that we could receive notification of an NSC meeting 
as early as Tuesday, which means the Director would want his.material as soon 
as possible, maybe even by Monday evening. I will let you know as soon as I 
receive word when the meeting is to be held. 

5, It would be useful. if one of your Libyan experts could draft for the 
Director some talking points based on the full picture of Libyan activity 
around the world. He wants, to be sure, the details as already provided and 
as to be provided on the various regions, but ultimately it all has to be 
pulled together in a single statement. Please provide such a draft for the 
Director at the time the other material comes forward.
