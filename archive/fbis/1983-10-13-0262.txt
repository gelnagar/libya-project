LIBYA

Al-Oadhdhafi Receives Mauritanian President 
LD1 21502 Tripoli JANA in English 1427 GMT 12 Oct 83 


{Text} Tripoli, Muharram 6, Oct JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The leader of the revolution yesterday received Lt 
Colonel Mohamad Khouna Ould Haydala, chairman of the 
Military Committee of National Salvation and president of the 
Islamic Republic of Mauritania 


Bureau on French Citizens’ ‘Delayed’ Departure 
LD122124 Kuwait KUNA in English 1832 GMT 12 Oct 83 


[Text] Paris, Oct 12 (KUNA) — The Libyan People’s Bureau in 
Paris said Wednesday the departure of the 37 French citizens 
from Tripoli last Sunday has been “delayed” due to routine 
identify checking. A communique released by the bureau said 
that “if the departure of the 37 French citizens from Tripoli has 
been somewhat delayed, that was mainly due to an administra- 
tive decision taken by the revolutionary committees, which are 
temporarily controlling the airport to enable a better checking of 
papers and identities.” “That is a worldwide practice and it is 
normal for a foreigner to comply with the rules and regulations 
of the country he is in and be subject to some administrative 
measures,” it said 


The Libyan People’s Bureau denied the “false and malevolent 
rumours” mentioning that the French citizens might be held 
hostages by the Libyan authorities. The communique affirmed 
that the Libyan authorities “have never taken the decision to hold 
the French citizens hostages, and even less to resort to any kind 
of blackmail.” 


it accused the French press of changing “lies into truth” and said 
that there has been lately a “denigration campaign against Libya 
orchestrated by the French secret services and carried out by the 
French media.”
