23 February 1982 

MEMORANDUM FOR: National Intelligence Officer for Near East 
and South Asia 

FROM : Special Assistant to the Director for 
Interdepartmental Affairs 

SUBJECT : Upcoming NSC Meeting on Libya 

1. We just received official notice that the twice-postponed NSC meeting 
on Libya has been rescheduled for 3:00 on Thursday, 25 February. 

2. In anticipation of such a meeting last week, we pulled together for 
the Director a book which responded to his desire to have a world-wide look 
at Libyan activities and trouble-making. In addition, you supplied the 
Director comments on the status of the interagency papers on sanctions. We 
need to check with the Director to ascertain what, if any, other material 
he would like to have for this meeting. I request that you act as the 
Director's action officer for this meeting to prepare for him whatever he 
needs. I have made an appointment for 3:00 tomorrow for you, 29X1 
and I to discuss preparations with the Director. 

3. I am fairly confident that the Director will wish to be armed with 
an opening statement that addresses both the Libyan problem and his views on 
the interagency deliberations. You will remember that DDI had provided him 
some draft talking points on Libyan trouble-making. That may be sufficient 
for his needs, but we will have to double-check that with him. In any case, 
he will need some sort of statement on the Libyan problem. We can settle 
on the actual time for submitting the material to the Director, but I strongly 
suspect that he will want to have his draft statement by close of business 
on Wednesday. I have in hand last week's package, should you need to review 
any of the material. You should ask DDI to update, if necessary, any of the 
background items provided the Director last week.

23 February 1982 

MEMORANDUM FOR: Acting National Intelligence Officer for USSR and 
Eastern Europe 

FROM : Special Assistant to the Director for Interdepartmental 
Affairs 
SUBJECT : Upcoming NSC Meeting on Poland 

1. This memorandum is to confirm you are the action officer for Friday's 
NSC meeting on Poland. We are scheduled to meet with the Director at 2:30 
tomorrow to review preparations for this meeting. 

2. We have already discussed to some degree what the Director will probably 
want to have on hand, i.e., at a minimum an opening statement which addresses 
the current situation in Poland, the impact of U.S. sanctions on Poland and the 
USSR, and the probable impact and consequences of further U.S. sanctions or 
efforts to penalize the Soviets, including the impact and reactions of our 
allies. We can confirm this with the Director tomorrow. 

3. As we discussed, because Harry, _|has been the one to attend recent 29X1 
SIG meetings on this subject, it will be useful for him to weigh in with his 
own thoughts in support of the Director's participation in the NSC meeting.
