Summary (AI): An NPIC briefing note dated January 1982 reports the first identification of Scud and FROG-7 tactical missile units at Bin Ghashir, a probable tactical surface-to-surface missile facility about 7 nautical miles south of Tripoli. Equipment may have come from the Tarabulus military facility Kulliyat, about 6 nautical miles north.
• At least 21 Scud MAZ-543 transporter-erector-launchers (TEL) identified at Bin Ghashir.
• Five Scud resupply semitrailers (three on graphic) and two probable FROG-7 TELs present.
• Eight FROG-7 resupply vehicles and numerous support vehicles present.
• One Scud TEL and two support vehicles were operationally deployed in a field immediately to the west.
• Coordinates given as 32-45-30N 013-12-30E.
• The document is a NPIC briefing note, attachment to Z-10136/82, classified SECRET.
• 1982-01: Elements of at least two Scud brigades and one FROG-7 battalion identified at Bin Ghashir probable TAC SSM facility for the first time.
