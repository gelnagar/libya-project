Summary (AI): A December 1981 White House directive (NSDD 16) sets U.S. economic and security steps against Libya, including passport invalidation for travel there, an oil and trade embargo, a demarche over terrorist plotting, and a Task Force on Libya. It names no Tripoli sites.
• NSDD 16 dated December 10, 1981, marked SECRET/SENSITIVE.
• Directs the Secretary of State to invalidate U.S. passports for travel to Libya.
• Directs the Treasury to prepare a U.S. embargo of Libyan oil imports and of exports to Libya.
• Establishes a State Department and White House co-chaired Task Force on Libya.
• Orders the Secretary of Defense and Joint Chiefs to develop military responses to assassination attempts, hostage-taking, or attacks after Americans withdraw.

December 10, 1981 lists the President's economic and security 
decisions for Libya.

Task Force on Libya. The Task Force will coordinate, implement, 
and make recommendations of additional options to the President.

DIRECTIVE NUMBER 16 

ECONOMIC AND SECURITY DECISIONS FOR LIBYA 

After consultation with the National Security Council I have 
directed that the following steps be taken to reduce the 
threat that Libya poses to U.S. national security: 

1. The Secretary of State should send a private 
demarche to Libya reemphasizing an earlier warning concerning 
its plotting of terrorist actions agai nist U.S. citizens or 
facilities. 

2. The Secretary of State should request in the name 
of the President that U.S. corporations with American citizens 
in Libya and U.S. corporations that trade with Libya make a 
voluntary withdrawal of those Americans and a minimization 
of sales and purchases to and from Libya. 

Be The Secretary of State shall invalidate U.S. 
passports for travel to Libya. 
4, During the withdrawal of Americans and in the 

context of Libyan terrorist planning against U.S. targets, 
the Secretary of the Treasury shall initiate and coordinate 
preparations for: 

as U.S. embargo of imports of Libyan oil. 
b. U.S. embargo of exports to Libya. 
5. The Secretary of Defense and the Joint Chiefs of 

Staff shall undertake to develop and evaluate military 
measures suitable for execution in response to: 

a. An unsuccessful assassination attempt while 
Americans are withdrawing. 

b. A successful assassination attempt while 
Americans are withdrawing. 

Ce An attack after Americans have been taken 
hostage. 
da. An attack following the withdrawal of Americans.

6. The Secretary of Defense in coordination with the 
Secretary of State shall assure that military measures be 
proportional to provocation under international law, military 
feasibility, collateral damage and political implications. 

7. Appropriate briefings shall be provided to Congres- 
sional leadership and other groups as required. 

8. The Secretaries of State and Defense as well as the 
Assistant to the President for National Security Affairs and 
the Director of Central Intelligence shall report to the National 
Security Council on diplomatic and military steps required to 
deter Soviet military involvement in light of Presidential 
decisions. 

9's The State Department and the White House shall co-chair 
a Task Force on Libya. Duties of the Task Force shall include: 
a. Implementation of the above decisions. 
b. Recommendation of additional options to the 
President. 

One of the first assignments of the Task Force is to 
decide what actions should be taken now if a mandatory withdrawal 
of U.S. business personnel from Libya is to be implemented.
