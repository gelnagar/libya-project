LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI EXPLAINS NATURE OF PEOPLE'S CONGRESSES 
LDO31844 Tripoli JANA in English 1620 GMT 3 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] Benghazi, October 3, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel al-Qadhdhafi last night 
took part in the evening session of the international seminar currently held at Ghar 
Younis University, Benghazi. 


Elaborating on a communication made by Dr Henry Clement from the U.S.A., he stated that 
people's congresses include all the people and are not elected by the masses, as 
thought by the speaker. The people divide themselves into people's congresses which 
involve all citizens without exceptions, 


On the speaker's view that it is impossible to bring about radical changes, Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi said that on the contrary everything is radically changeable and that this 
is obvious in the situation in Libya. "Libya changed from a kingdom to a Jamahiriyah. 
The workers changed from wage-workers to partners, and government system made way to 
the rise of the people's authority. All these changes are radical", he said, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi explained that the idea of the impossibility of radical change is 
a product of Western culture to which the speaker belonged, He added that the whole 
Western culture is now staggering before the continuous changes taking place in the 
wor ld, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi went on to comment on the paper of Dr Issam Naaman, a lecturer at 
Beirut University, who gave a lecture about the possibility for one sector of a society 
to impose its hegemony over the others through its unions, 


He said: "All over the world, trade unions rule their members in a dictatorial way. 
This is a current practice in all trade unions, which exist under the prevailing 
regimes the world over, The situation is totally different in a society of the masses 
where people's congresses, in vocational bodies, decided and selected a people's 
committee to run the administration, 


The people's congress of vocational bodies discuss its own affairs, The members of the 
people's congress of vocational bodies are all members in the basic people's congresses, 


This is why trade unions in the society of the masses differ radically from all other 
traditional unions". 


Concerning Dr Issam's view on the importance of awareness in the practice of democracy 
and the relation between awareness and education, the colonel said: "The illiterate 
can have knowledge through experience, which can enable him to make sound decisions, 
while a university educated person can act ignorantly, not being able to make sound 
decisions regarding his future", 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added that the third part of the Green Book has brought the 
solution to such a problem as he said: "Knowledge or education does not represent the 
organized method, Classified subjects which the student is bound to learn in printed 
books, sitting on rows of seats, are the prevalent form of knowledge today all over 
the world and are going against freedom, Compulsory education, which the states 
throughout the world imposed on their youths, is one of the styles of repression of 
freedom,"

Amongst the other lectures discussed were those given by Dr Enayatollah from the

Dr Enayatollah's lecture dealt with economic development in a new socialist society,

AT-TURAYKI MEETS FOREIGN COUNTERPARTS AT UN 

PDRY Foreign Minister 
LDO032114 Tripoli JANA in English 1841 GMT 3 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] New York, Oct 3, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The foreign secretary held a meeting 
today with Mr. Salim Muhammad Muti, foreign minister of Democratic Yemen, at the U.N. 
headquarters in New York. They discussed the latest developments on the Arab level, 
the items on the agenda of the thirty-fourth session of the U.N. General Assembly and 
the French invasion of Central Africa. The Yemeni foreign minister confirmed to the 
foreign secretary that Democratic Yemen strictly opposes the French intervention, 
considers it as a violation of the principles of the United Nations Organisation and 
(?sees) in it an intervention in the affairs of a member-state of the U.N., the 
Organization of African Unity and the non-alignment movement, 


Cuban Foreign Minister 
LD032043 Tripoli JANA in English 1750 GMT 3 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] New York, October 3, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Dr., 'Ali at-Turayki, foreign 
secretary, held a meeting yesterday with his Cuban counterpart and the president of 
the non-aligned movement, during which the outcome of the Havana September non- 
aligned summit was reviewed, especially the resolution dealing with the Palestinian 
question and condemning the capitulatory treaty signed by the defeated president 
and (Israel). 


The two sides agreed to take the necessary steps to implement the resolution, They 
have also discussed the French invasion of Central Africa, because she is a member 
of non-aligned movement, 


The Cuban foreign minister affirmed his country's rejection of any intervention in 
Africa, for this would threaten the continent's security and independence, 


The question will be raised again during the non-aligned foreign minister meetings 
in New York tomorrow,
