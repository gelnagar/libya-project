LIBYA

SAUDIS DELAY PLANE'S OVERFLIGHT PERMISSION 


LD250812 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 25 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 25 Aug (JANA) -- The secretary of the General Peoples’ Committee for 
Agrarian Reform and Land Reclamation, Brother Abu Zayd ‘Umar Durdah, left for the YAR 
last night. 


Brother Abu Zayd ‘Umar Durdah and his accompanying delegation were scheduled to leave 
Tripoli the night before last but the Saudi authorities delayed granting their aircraft 
permission to fly through Saudi airspace. 


CENTRAL AFRICAN REPUBLIC MINISTER ARRIVES 


LD241526 Tripoli JANA in English 1440 GMT 24 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulqidah 5, August 24 -- Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The foreign minister 
of the Central African Republic arrived here last night on a visit to the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah.
