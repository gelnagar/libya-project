LIBYA

INA: INTELLIGENCE MEN "HARASS? PALESTINIANS 
JNO5i941 Baghdad INA in Arabic 1925 GMT 5 Dec 79 JN 


{Text] Baghdad, 5 December--The Voice of the Palestinian Revolution broadcasting from 
Algiers has said that for days now Libyan intelligence men have been besieging the PLO 
offices in Libya and harassing the Palestinians there. The radio said that elements 
from Libyan intelligence besieged the PLO offices without reason and that those working 
in the offices, headed by the PLO representative, rejected this measure and declared 

that they would not leave their offices and would not allow anybody to break into them. 
They said that they were ready to die inside their offices instead. The radio also said 
that some other elements attacked the houses of Palestinians in Libya and arrested a nunm- 
ber of them.
