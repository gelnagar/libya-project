AR-RA'Y INTERVIEW WITH LIBYAN FOREIGN SECRETARY 
JN190913 Amman AR-RA'Y in Arabic 19 Jan 80 pp 1, 14 IN 


[Text] Damaseus--Nasr al-Majali dispatch--Libyan Foreign Secretary Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam 
at-Turayki has said that Libya maintains special ani extremely fraternal relations with 
Jordan. He also said that Libya will support Jordan's pan-Arab stand in defense of the 
Arab nation and its crucial issues. 


Dr at-Turayki added: His Majesty King Husayn and Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi are in 
complete agreement on the single Arab stand to be adopted to meet all challenges and 
to fight all plots against the Arab nation. 


The Libyan foreign secretary affirmed that visits and meetings will continue between 

the two fraternal countries in the interest of the Arab nation. He said that the letters 
exchanged between King Husayn and Al-Qadhdhafi aimed at entrenching Arab understanding 
and solidarity. 


Dr at-Turayki, who was answering questions pvt to him by AR-RA'Y following the press 
conference he held in Damascus yesterday to outline the results of the foreign ministers 
conference of the Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation, said that the next ‘rab 
summit conference in Amman will give joint Arab action a further push forward. He 
stressed that strong steps will be taken to strengthen intrinsic Arab paver on the 
military, economic, manpower and political levels. 


Dr at-Turaki said that intrinsic Arab power is currently being built on an individual 
basis in some Arab countries, but that the next step will be for unified Arab action. 

Dr at-Turayki expresséd the hope that decisions will be taken on the summit level, after 
the amendment of the Arab League Charter, so the Arab citizens will see their effective 
tangible results. 


Dr at-Turayki said that the next Arab summit in Amman will set forth to the Arabs their 
true and real responsibilities, because the Arab plans which will be submitted to the 
conference will be clear, integral plans. 


Asked about Libyan-Palestinian relations, Dr at-Turayki said: There is absolutely no 
dispute between the PLO and Libya. For example, the Great 1 September Revolution in 
Libya was a real reaction to the Arab defeat in the 1967 war. Our revolution's objective 
has always been the restoration of Jerusalem and Palestine. In its foreign and domestic 
policy, Libya emphasizes the Palestine question and the support for the Palestinian 
people. Tibya has always been the main supporter of the Palestine question on the 
international level. 


The Libyan secretary declined to say if there is any intention to reopen Fatah offices 
in Libyan cities. However, he said: We will contime to be loyal to the cause of 
this fraternal people. 


Regarding the request to postpone the Islamic Conference scheduled to meet in Islamabad 
on 26 Jarmary and the reaction if the Muslims insisted on going ahead with the con- 
ference, Dr at-Turayki said: The Islamic Conference Organization was formed after 

the attempt to burn the Al-Aqsa mosque. Therefore, the organization was formed 
essentially to support the question of Palestine and Jerusalem, which is both an 

Arab and Islamic issue. We do not want the organization to deviate from its basic 

aims. Islamic action mst not be diverted from the central issue, which is the 
Palestine question.

Dr at-Turayki added: If we are indeed concerned about Islam, then our main efforts at 
this time must be devoted to eliminating the real danger; namely, the Zionist enemy 
which is desecrating holy Arab Muslim territory. 


Dr at-Turayki attacked the United States for encouraging the holding of the Islamic 
Conference. He said the United States is playing an obvious game which we mst seriously 
resist. 


Dr at-Turayki contimed: The Isiamic Conference is planned for 26 January, the dav when 
the As-Sadat regime and the Zionist entity will begin normalizing their relations. Here 
lies the danger and the obstacles which will prevent the conference from achieving its 
objective, because the world and Islamic public will be preoccupied with the normaliza- 
tion of Egyptian-Israeli relations. The conference will thus be a fiasco and its reso- 
lutions will be of no importance. 


As to what the position would be if the Islamic countries and other Arab states insisted 
om holding the conference as scheduled, Dr at-Turayki said: Delegations will be sent 

to those Islamic and Arab countries to discuss the matter with them and to try to persuade 
them to postpone the decision on the time and place for the conference. 


Dr at-Turayki expressed the hope that the Islamic and Arab countries will respond posi- 
tively to the request to postpone the conference. 


Dr at-Turayki attacked the Egyptian regime for surrendering to capitulatory conferences. 
He said the Egyptian regime has sold Jerusalem, Palestine and Egypt itself. Dr at-Turayki 
recalled the report submitted by Hasan at-Tuhami [special adviser to President as-Sadat] 
on armed Muslim terrorist groups in Egypt and said: This report was passed to Israeli 
intelligence so it would cooperate with the Egyptian rulers in crushing these groups. 


Dr at-Turayki said that Islam has gained world influence after the Iranian Islamic Revo- 
lution, and that the position of the member states of the Front of Steadfastness and 
Confrontation on this issue is well-lkmowm. Dr at-Turayki asserted that these states 
cannot possibly side with "America's Islam.” 


Replying to a question about Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's request that U.S. President Carter 
change his Middle East policy, a request which was spurned by White House sources, Dr 
at-Turayki said: We in Libya stick to that position. It is up to the United States to 
change its policy in the Arab region. This will be in the interest of the American 
people themselves. The United States mst choose between the friendship of the Araos 
and that of Zionism. 


Replying to another question on the Arab oil states' attitude toward the frontline states 
in light of the increasing oil prices, Dr at-Turayki said: We must be clear on the sub- 
ject of oil and its prices. We should not fall victim to Western propaganda about Arab 
wealth. The uproar on the oil price increase is being created by Western circles which 
have enormous economic interests in order to exploit their world influence. 


He added: The oil-producing countries will place all their resources, including oil, 
at the disposal of the frontline states. We will support Jordan and Syria. It must 
be real support within an overall clear Arab strategy and for the sake of real action.

Dr at-Turayki asserted that supporting Jordan and Syria as frontline states is a duty 
dictated by the pan-Arab interest. Dr at-Turayki called on all the Arab oil-producing 
countries to support Jordan and Syria so they can continue to face aggression, threats 
and pressure. He said that supporting these two countries is a pan-Arab responsibility 
which must be carried out without fail. 


JOINT STATEMENT ISSUED ON GUYANESE PRIME MINISTER'S VISIT 
LD201348 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 GMT 20 Jan 80 LD 


[Joint statement issued 19 January on the visit of the Guyanese prime minister to the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah] 


{Excerpts] Tripoli, 20 Jarmary--At the invitation of Brother Jadallah Azzuz at-Talhi, 
secretary of the General People's Committee, the prime minister of the cooperative 
Republic of Guyana, Linden Forbes Sampson Burnham, paid a cordial and official visit 
to Libya from 15 to 19 Jarmary, 1980. 


In an atmosphere of amity and mtual understanding, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
commander of the Great 1 September Revolution, received Guyanese Prime Minister Burnham 
and they exchanged views on bilateral relations and international questions of common 
interest. 


During the meeting, discussions were held on the Third World theory and the people's 
assumption of authority in Libya. 


Guyanese Prime Minister Burnham expressed his admiration for this theory and the 
achievements it has brought about in Libya in the various political, ecoomic, social 
fields. 


For his part, the Guyanese prime minister acquainted Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi with 
the socialist experiment in Guyana which aims at giving the people the maximum measure 
of participation in running their own affairs and in insuring the just distribution of 
the commmnity's resources. 


During the visit, the signing took place of a number of documents on basic methods 
for cooperation between the two friendly countries in the fields of agriculture and 
fishing, as well as of the minutes of protocol for cooperation in other fields. 


With regard to the current international situation, the views of the two sides were 
identical on many of the important problems. The two sides reaffirmed their commit- 
ment to contimue the struggle against imperialism and colonialism in all their forms, 
as well as against racism, including Zionism and apartheid. 


They also pledged to work unceasingly to bring about a just and balanced international 
economic system. 


The two sides expressed their dissatisfaction with the rigid stand adopted by countries 
that have practiced colonialism toward the just demands of the developing countries 
for the establishment of a new world economic system. They agreed that the consolida- 
tion of the national independence of the Third World peoples necessitates a close 
economic cooperation among the developing countries. 


V. 23 Jan 80 18 NORTH AFRICA 


The two sides paid special attention to the grave developments in the Arab region and 
denounced the Camp David and other agreements concluded between the Egyptian regime and 
the Zionist enemy--agreements which only bring great harm to the rights and interests 
of the Arab nation and amount to a squandering of the rights of the Palestinian people. 
The two sides affirmed that the Camp David agreements conflict with the UN resolutions 
and those of the nonalined summit conference. 


The two sides expressed their support for the struggle of the Palestinian people against 
Zionist expansionist designs, including Zionist aggressions against southern Lebanon. 
They also affirmed their support for the Palestinian revolution, and all forms of the 
struggle waged by the Palestinian people and their liberation movement, for the 
retrieval of their rights to self-determination and the establishment of their inde- 
pendent state in Palestine. 


With regard to the situation in Africa the two sides reiterated their commitment to 
support all the necessary steps needed for the elimination of the vestiges of imper‘alism 
and racist exploitation on the continent. They also expressed their belief that in spite 
of the latest developments in Zimbabwe, international vigilance should still contime 

to follow developments there. 


The two sides denounced in particular the continued intervention by South Africa in the 
domestic affairs of Zimbabwe. They also denounced the suppressive racist regimes in 
South Africa and Namibia and the imperialist plots and designs which aim at protecting 
their interests in these two countries. They renewed their support for the SWAPO libera- 
tion movement as the sole legitimate representative of the Namibian people and the just 
demands of the oppressed people of South Africa. 


The two sides affirmed their support for the struggle of the people of Western Sahara 
and their legitimate right to self-determination in accordance with the resolutions of 
the United Nations and the OAU. 


The two sides noted with satisfaction the recent developments in some Caribbean and 
Latin American countries which are seeking to consolidate their national independence 
and have control over their natural resources. 


The two sides denounced the policy of hegemony in the area which is aimed at obstructing 
the legitimate aspirations of the area's peoples for progress and prosperity. The two 
sides also expressed their support for the Puerto Rican people's right to independence 
and self-determination. 


They also expressed their support for the people of Belize and their right to self- 
determination, sovereignty and territorial integrity. The two sides denounced the 
dictatorial regime in Chile and reiterated their firm support for the Chilean people's 
struggle against the oppression and tyramny of the suppressive fascist regime. 


The two sides reviewed with satisfaction the results of the Sixth Nonalined Summit 
Conference which was held in Havana in September 1979, and agreed that the nonaline- 
ment movement has played a positive and effective role in international affairs and in 
consolidating and preserving international peace and security.

The two sides affirmed that adherence to the principles and policies of the nonalined 
movement provides the smaller nations with the best guarantees for their independence, 
and also provides a stand against the revival of a competition and race between the 
big powers for the imposition of their influence on the world. The two sides expressed 
their satisfaction at the role played by the nonalined movement in calling for a new 
world economic system. In this regard, they noted that cooperation, solidarity and 
joint action by the member countries, which characterize the political aspect of the 
nonalined movement, should also cover economic relations. For this reason, the two 
sides consider that the resolution providing for collective self-reliance by the member 
countries of the nonaliined movement constitutes an important step toward the establish- 
ment of a stronger unity among the nonalined countries and cmtributes toward their 
economic progress. And in this respect, the two sides agreed that the economic aspects 
of their bilateral relations should be pursued with inte-est and should be constantly 
reviewed. 


The two sides renewed their full support for the United Nations and its principles 
as contained in its charter and for its contribution to the safeguarding of inter- 
national peace and security. They agreed to continue to cooperate within the various 
UN bodies and to work to make the measures which aim at upholding the principle of 
equality in the adoption of resolutions among all the organization's members, irre- 
spective of their size and strength, more effective. 


At the end of the visit, Prime Minister Burnham invited Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to 
visit wiyana, which he gratefully accepted. The time of the visit will be fixed at 
a later date.

JAMAICAN SECURITY MINISTER ARRIVES IN LIBYA 
FL211912 Kingston Domestic Service in English 1730 GMT 21 Jan 80 FL 


[Text] National Security Minister Dudley Thompson arrived in Tripoli, Libya, last 
night on a mission believed to be associated with securing economic aid for Jamaica. 
Mr Thompson, a former foreign affairs minister, left Jamaica unannounced following 
last week's private cabinet meeting at which Prime Minister Manley reported on the 
outcome of his trip to the International Monetary Fund in Washington. No information 
has been officially released on the decisions taken by cabinet.

Mr Thompson was met at the Tripoli international airport by the Libyan Minister for 
Light Industries and by a representative of that country's Protocol Department. Mr 
Thompson's is the second visit of a senior government official to the Libyan Jamahiriyah 
in 4 months. Prime Minister Manley led a delegation to that country in October last 
year. 


Meanwhile, two other Caribbean leaders have just concluded visits to Libya. Prime Minister 
Maurice Bishop of Grenada was given a rousing welcome when he arrived in Tripoli on 
Saturday and Guyanese Prime Minister Forbes Burnham has just signed a joint communique 

with Libyan leaders charting cooperation between the two countries over the next few years.
