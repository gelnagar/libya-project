Summary (AI): A Washington Star article (30 July 1980) reports that former CIA employee Francis E. Terpil, charged with acting as an unregistered foreign agent for Libya and conspiring to murder a Libyan exile, has a trial date set for November 5 in Washington. It also discusses a Time magazine photo placing him next to Billy Carter at a Libyan revolution anniversary event.
• Terpil and Wilson were indicted in April 1980 in Washington on charges including acting as unregistered agents for Libya, conspiracy to commit murder, and unlawful export of U.S. munitions.
• Terpil was arrested in New York in December 1979 on state weapons charges.
• Terpil admitted attending the 10th anniversary celebration of the Libyan revolution in 1979 but denied sitting next to Billy Carter.
• Judge Pratt delayed the case from early October to allow more preparation.
• 1980-11-05: Trial date set for Francis E. Terpil on charges of acting as unregistered agent for Libya and conspiracy to commit murder (trial scheduled for November 5)
• 1976-07: Alleged conspirators plotted in mid-1976 to kill Umar Abdullah Muhayshi, a former member of the Libyan ruling council who had defected to Egypt
