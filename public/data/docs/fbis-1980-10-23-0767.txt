LIBYA

ARAB-AFRICAN PREPARATORY COMMITTEE ISSUES STATEMENT 
LD181416 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 18 Oct 80 


[Text] The preparatory committee of the Arab-African conference yesterday concluded 

its meetings in Tripoli. The committee was making preparations for the forthcoming 
Arab-African conference. The committee issued a statement. It reviewed the present 
conditions through which the Arab and African regions and the plots to which the liberation 
movements in Africa and the Arab homeland are being exposed to prevent them realizing 

their aims. The statement called for all efforts to be exerted to back the unity of 

the struggle of Africa and the Arab nations on the basis of being aware of the importance 

of their cooperation and solidarity in the battle raging against colonialism and its agents.
