LIBYA KILLER SQUAD CONCERNS REAGAN
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp95b00915r000500110022-1
https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-cia-rdp95b00915r000500110022-1

Summary (AI): A December 1981 press package (CIA reading room copy) reports that a U.S. intelligence report said a five-person Libyan assassination squad had entered the US to kill President Reagan or his advisers. It also carries Libya's denial, calling the reports products of a "CIA fantasy farm."
• Secret Service protection ordered for Baker, Edwin Meese III and Michael K. Deaver after an informant's warning
• Informant, not American, named five alleged members of a Libyan assassination squad; credibility unverified
• Reports said the team may have crossed from Canada into the US
• Libyan government said the US claims were disinformation and that Western media operate as an annex of the CIA
• Reports linked the alleged plot to anger over the August US Navy jet downing of Libyan reconnaissance planes over the Gulf of Sidra
• 1981-12-04: White House asks US news organizations to restrain reporting on security measures for President Reagan after reports of a Libyan-trained assassination team in the US
• 1981-12-05: Libyan government (spokesman Khalifa Azzabi) denies the assassination reports, calling them products of a CIA fantasy farm
