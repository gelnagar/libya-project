Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery briefing note from August 1983 reports Libyan SA-6 air defence batteries and other units deployed at Aozou airfield in northern Chad during the Libya-Chad conflict. Aozou is far from Tripoli, so this bears only on Libyan military foreign operations.
• At least two SA-6 batteries were operationally deployed immediately northwest and southeast of Aozou airfield.
• Partial coverage showed seven SA-6 TEL, two straight flush radar, one SA-6 transloader and at least 12 support vehicles.
• Other air defence equipment included an SA-9 unit and a ZSU-23/4 unit.
• Aozou is about 50 nm south of the Libya-Chad border; coordinates given as 22-38-35N 017-41-53E.
• 1983-08: Imagery shows at least seven SA-6 transporter-erector launchers, two straight flush radars, one transloader and at least 12 support vehicles; also an SA-9 unit, a ZSU-23/4 unit, a tank company and an artillery battery north of the runway. This is the furthest south Libyan SA-6 units have deployed in recent operations.
