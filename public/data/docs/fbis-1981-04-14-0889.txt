LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI CALLS FOR MAURITANIA-SAHARAN MERGER 


LD131317 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 13 Apr 81 


[Text] An important meeting was held this morning and was attended by the brother com- 
mander of the Great 1 September Revolution; Brother Sidi Ahmed Ould Bneijara, vresident of 
the Islamic Republic of Mauritania; Brother Mohamed Abdelaziz, the secretary general of the 
Polisario; Brother Walid Junblatt, leader of the Lebanese National Movement; and Brother 
Ahmad Jibril, from the Palestine revolution. 


During this important meeting the brother commander spoke in the name of the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in its capacity as current chairman of the Steadfastness 
and Confrontation Front and called for establishing unity between the Mauritanian Islamic 
Republic and the Sarahan Arab Republic. 


He also called for the formation of an alliance between the Jamahiriyah, Algeria, Mauritania 
and the Saharan Republic. 


The brother commander has addressed an invitation to the Lebanese National Movement, 
Mauritania and the Saharan Republic to join the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front. 


He also called for expelling the Sudanese regime from the Arab League and the Islamic 
Congress because Sudan's recognition of the As-Sadat regime and consequently its recog- 
nition of the Zionist enemy constitute uu alignment against the Palestinian people and 
the Palestine question. 


TRIPOLI REPORTS DEMONSTRATION AGAINST SUDAN REGIME 


LD131422 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 13 Apr 81 


[Text] The Sudanese masses in the Jamaiiriyah today staged a demonstration denouncing and 
condemning the treacherous stands of the ruler of Sudan and his humiliating fall in the lap 
of the United States and the agent reactionary forces. The Sudanese masses staged their 
demonstration in Tripoli this morning. They called for the downfall of the ruling regime 
in Sudan, they carried banners condemning and denouncing his suspect alliance with the 
ruler of Egypt and exposing his agentry. The Sudanese masses also denounced the agent 
regime's subservience and its handing over of Sudanese territory for use by American 

troops and bases. 


The participants in the demonstration also carried banners proclaiming their solidarity 
with the Great 1 September Revoluton and its pan-Arab march against imperialism and its 
agents. 


The Sudanese masses then issued a statement, which reads as follows: From the land of 
the unique people who crowned their struggle with the establishment of the world's first 
Jamahiriyah--the nucleus of true Arab unity and the hope of mankind, and its happy dream; 
in the name of the beloved Sudanese people and their future generations, we have ordained 
this tumultuous demonstration to denounce and condemn the utterances made by the midget, 
butcher Numayri at his feeble press conference in Khartoum. Our shoulders are weighted 
down by huge tasks and burdens dictated by the current crucial and complicated era, which 
is now passing through an extremely dangerous juncture as a result of the ferocious 
imperialist offensive against the Arab nation and of the ferocious attempts being made 

to contain the Arab nation with military bases and rapid deployment forces. 


The statement calls on the Sudanese revolutionaries and all honorable revolutionaries 
to join the effective positive movement which will lead to the removal of the choking 
nightmare that has crouched on the chest of our poor people for 12 continuous years.

The statement adds: You are fully aware of the crimes committed by the ruling clique against 
the Sudanese Arab citizens, and the other events that took place during a decade when 

the Sudanese citizens were robbed of the most fundamental manifestations of true freedom 

and democracy and when their true Arab values and ideals were violated. The best sons of 

the homeland were made homeless, their honorable revolutionaries were imprisoned, their 
honor was violated and their wealth was squandered on bribes, favoritism and corruption-- 

so much so that Sudan became one of the world's poorest countries and its foreign debts 
amounted to billions of dollars. 


The statement affirms that the agent regime has kept the Sudanese people~-known for their 
firm stand on uprisings and the making of revolutions, and who embraced the Arab League 
and the OAU, and who were founding members of the movement of positive neutrality and 
nonalignment--has kept the Sudanese people away from all these fields, has shaken their 
identity inside the Arab League, turned them into a losing member of the OAU, and ended 

by making them aligned. The land of the Sudanese people now abounds with foreign troops 
and subversive weapons and Sudan has succumbed to foreign influence. Sudan's policy is now 
administered from outside and the Sudanese have no say in it. Sudan's external policy 

has been humiliated and is now that of an agent regime. It betrays the pan-Arab cause. 
Sudan's domestic policy abounds with malpractice in development and regional rule. Sudan 
is filled with blood and destruction. 


The Sudanese participants in the demonstration add in their statement that the hour of 
jihad has struck for all those who stand by that regime or cover it up, because this regime 
has betrayed its nation and territory. The statement goes on: The times of ordeal and 
hardship were the touchstone for the will of the peoples and their ability to write history 
and to redraft realities in a manner which would realize their ambitions and progressive 
inclinations. Either they rise to the level of these hardships and impose their will and 
existence or they will fade out, be buried and end. 


The statement affirms that flouting the gains and destiny of the Sudanese people by 
Numayri and his clique must be stopped immediately--not by hopes and dreams but by armed 
popular revolution. Free revolutionaries inside and outside Sudan rally together! You 
will triumph over all the agents! Rally so that you may topple this regime and uproot 

it and thus pave the way for the creation from its ashes of a socialist, unionist, jamahiri 
regime opposed to imperialism, reaction and agents. 


The statement holds the masses of the Arab nation and the free African people responsible 
for condemning the U.S. bases and securing their withdrawal from Sudanese territory, and 
for boldly and strongly opposing them. It also holds them responsible for denouncing the 
Numayri regime's decision to restore full relations with the defeatist As-Sadat regime. 

he statement also holds all the Arab regimes responsible for enforcing all the sanctions 
imposed against the As-Sadat traitorous regime and the agent Numaryi regime, which is akin 
to it. The statement calls for the agent regime's immediate expulsion from the Arab 

League and the halting of all economic aid to it. It also holds them responsible for 
exposing the dimensions of the plot being hatched by the Egyptian and Sudanese regimes, 
with the cooperation of the American-Zionist circles. 


At the end of the demonstration, the Sudanese masses observed a moment of silence in 
memory of the martyrs who fell in Sudan at the hands of the agent traitorous Numayri regime. 


The masses addressed cables to the ruler of Sudan, the ruler of Egypt and the U.S. 
President in which they affirmed their determination to continue their struggle against 
the agent regime in Sudan. They also affirmed the Sudanese people's determination to 
reject the American bases in Sudan.

The Sudanese masses denounced the Sudanese regime's alliance with the ruler of Egypt by 
restoring relations with him to implement the suspect imperialist designs against the 

Arab nation. In their cable the Sudanese masses also denounced the Egyptian military 
concentrations on the Sudanese-Chadian borders and affirmed that this is a mean attempt 

to strike at the revolution in Chad and a flagrant provocation of the people of Jamahiriyah. 


JOINT LIBYAN-MAURITANIAN COMMUNIQUE ISSUED 13 APR 


LD140228 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 13 Apr 81 


[Text] A joint Libyan-Arab Mauritanian communique was issued this evening following the 
visit to Libya of Brother Sidi Ahmed Ould Bneijara, the Mavritanian prime minister, who 
held talks dealing with bilateral cooperation and ways uf strengthening it in accordance 
with the wishes of the two fraternal peoples and in accordance with their efforts to 
realize their plans for progress and prosperity. 


The talks dealt with developments in Arab and African issues and with the current inter- 
national situation. In the joint communique the two sides affirmed the need to continue 
the struggle to abort the Camp David agreement. They expressed full support for the 
Palestinian people's just position and for the right of these people to return to their 
homeland, to achieve self-determination and to establish their own independent state on 
their complete national soil. The two sides also expressed their continuing support for 
the Palestinian people in their struggle for the complete liberation of all Palestinian 
territories. 


The two sides affirmed their absolute support for the struggle of the fraternal Egyptian 
people and their patriotic and progressive forces to overthrow the As-Sadat regime. They 
also stressed the need to raise and unify the energies and to consolidate their alliance 
to confront the imperialist onslaught against the Arab nation and to eliminate all the 
aggressive military bases established in the Arab homeland by imperialism. 


The two sides also denounced the disregard of the expansionist Moroccan regime for the 
sovereignty, unity and safety of the territory of the Islamic Mauritanian Republic. They 
strongly denounced the series of cunning conspiracies which the regime continues to hatch 
in order to strike at the Mauritanian people and to destroy their security and stability. 
The latest conspiracy was the attempt to overthrow the national government on 19 March 
1981. The sides also denounced the traitorous role played by Hassan II, which is to 
serve Zionism and imperialism and to strike at the liberation movements on the African 
Continent. 


The two sides expressed grave concern over the situation in the Western Sahara, which has 
resulted from foreign occupation. They reaffirmed their support for the struggle of the 
fraternal Saharan people, under the leadership of Polisario, to achieve their independence, 
freedom and self-determination. They condemned the Moroccan regime's increasing attempts 
to ignite the flame of war in the area and they condemned its stubborness and violation 
of all international documents and resolutions of the OAU, the Nonaligned Movement and 
the United Nations.[passage indistinct] The two sides expressed hope that the fraternal 
Sudanese people will stand up to this conspiracy, which seeks to separate the Sudanese 
people from the Arab nation to make them join the Camp David group. They also affirmed 
their full support for the [Transitional] Government of National Unity [word indistinct] 
in Chad, under the leadership of President Goukouni Oueddei, and their condemnation for 
the imperialist and secessionist forces which are trying to harm the victories of the 
Chadian people. In this connection, they strongly condemned the Egyptian military presence 
in Sudan, which is aimed at undermining security and stability in Chad and at conspiring 
against the freedom and national unity of the Chadian people.

The two sides assessed the situation in Lebanon and expressed their solidarity with the 
fraternal Lebanese people. They condemned the repeated Zionist attacks on Lebanese terri- 
tory. They stressed their support for the independence and Arabism of Lebanon and the 
unity of its territory, which is being threatened by Zionism and imperialism. They 
affirmed their support for the patriotic and progressive forces in Lebanon and condemned 
the isolationist-reactionary-Zionist alliance. 


The two sides also affirmed their complete support for the heroic struggle of the fraternal 
African peoples of Namibia and South Africa against imperialism and racial discrimination. 
They expressed their support for the struggle of these peoples to achieve their freedom 
and independence and to eliminate colonialism and racism once and for all on the continent. 
They denounced the conspiracies and intrigues hatched by the imperialist states aimed at 
dividing the African states and hampering the effectiveness of the OAU. 


They affirmed their belief in and attachment to Arab-African cooperation and also to the 
strengthening of the relations of fraternity and cooperation between the states adjoining 
the Sahara. The two sides affirmed their adherence to the principles of the Nonaligned 
Movement and their resolve to strengthen its role in the struggle for effective participa- 
tion in consolidating international peace and security. 


The two sides expressed satisfaction with the positive results of this visit and affirmed 
the importance of continuing the meetings between the officials of the two fraternal 
countries at various levels.
