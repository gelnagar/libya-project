BILLY CARTER USED BY BRZEZINSKI AS LIBYA GO-BETWEEN
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp90-00552r000100990091-4
https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-cia-rdp90-00552r000100990091-4

Summary (AI): A Washington Post article (23 July 1980) reports that National Security Adviser Zbigniew Brzezinski met Billy Carter and Libyan charge Ali Houderi in November 1979 to seek Libyan help in freeing the American hostages in Iran, and that Billy Carter received $220,000 from Libya. It concerns US-Libya relations and the Billy Carter affair rather than Tripoli sites.
• Billy Carter received $220,000 from the Libyan government in two checks conveyed through Houderi, in January and April 1980, described as payments on a $500,000 loan.
• The White House said Brzezinski initiated the November 1979 meeting by calling on Billy Carter for help.
• A source disputed this and said Billy Carter asked Brzezinski to meet.
• Qaddafi reportedly sent a message to Ayatollah Khomeini asking for the hostages' release.
• 1979-11-27: Brzezinski, Billy Carter and Libyan charge Ali Houderi meet in Washington, 23 days after the hostages were seized in Tehran
• 1979: Mobs in Tripoli stormed the American Embassy there, five days after the Brzezinski-Carter-Houderi meeting
