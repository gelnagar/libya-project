LIBYA

AL- QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES BASIC CONGRESSES 28 APRIL 


LD282222 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 28 Apr 82 


[Text] Within the framework of encouraging the popular masses to exercise authority, the 
leader of the revolution spoke this evening on television and radio to the masses of the 
people's basic congresses in central Tripoli and central Al-Fatih. The leader stressed that 
the seriousness of debate in the congresses is an indication of the level reached in the 
experiment of people's authority toward its final consolidation. The seriousness of the 
debate indicates the extent of the feeling of responsibility of the masses with regard to 
the authority which they have, in effect, begun to exercise. 


The leader stressed that the jamahiriyan's economic position is not difficult at all despite 
the U.S. blockade -- supported by the Saudi reactionary conspiracy which has flooded the 
world market with huge quantities of gasoline in its efforts, within the scope of the U.S. 
conspiracy, to weaken the economic position of other petroleum countries that have rejected 
the U.S. domineering attitude. 


The leader affirmed that we know how to make difficult decisions in difficult circumstances. 
If those circumstances arise, we will make the necessary decision, however severe, to 
confront and defeat the enemies’ conspiracies because the basis of all our decisions is 

that our integrity should remain safeguarded and our heads held high. The leader stressed 
that our petroleum wealth is bolstered every day with new discoveries that will have 
definite roles in bolstering our economic steadfastness and in realizing our position of 
resistance to the enemies' conspiracies. 


The basic people's congresses continue their meetings of the first ordinary session for 
this year. 


JALLUD COMMENTS ON AFRICAN ECONOMIC SITUATION 


LD281900 Tripoli JANA in English 1830 GMT 28 Apr 82 
{All quotation marks as received] 


[Text] Tripoli, 28 Apr (JANA) -- Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud has stressed that Africa's 
economic and social problems could only be solved by the Africans and through African 
administration. He said the African Continent "is capable through cooperation and sincere 
determination, of solving these problems and most properly deploying its resources in 
realising transformations serving African man and causes." 


This came in a speech at the African planning and economy ministers’ meeting held here 
yesterday. Staff Major Jallud called on Africen countries to confront imperialists 
schemes aimed at dominating and robbing their resources.

He stressed: "Imperialism will again try to halt development of African countries' 
independence from political independence to economic social and cultural independence. 
The advanced countries with imperialist tendency have stood against developing peoples’ 
aspirations. 


He added: "Despite the emergency of these stands characterised by selfish interests 
and old policies, this should not prevent the developing and African countries from 
forcefully and determinedly continuing the development of their political independence." 


He said: "The advanced industrial countries have placed all types of obstacles and 
rejected all attempts to create a just world economic order... these countries want to 
keep the backward world forever backward and to confine independence in Africa to raising 
a flag and having a president. They do not want independence to acquire its political, 
economic, cultural and social meaning. 


He went on: "These countries want us to remain as raw-material-exporting countries. 

While they continue to drain our resources at cheapest of prices and to make our countries 
consumer markets for their products... the advanced industrial countries are not serious 
about establishing a just world economic order, which will allow the advanced countries 

to continue their progress while also letting the backward world to overcome its back- 
wardness and start the stage of development." [as received] 


He affirmed: "The Western imperialist countries are not serious about transferring 
technology to Third World and African countries." He noted that the prevailing money 
system was set by the imperialist states to serve their imperialist policy." 


Staff Major Jallud stressed the necessity to change current economic and monetary prac- 
tices, establish a new just economic order, end technological monopoly, realise a just 
distribution of the monetary liquidity, find initiatives for trade exchange between 
developing and industrial countries. 


America and the rest of industrial countries are dominating finance establishments and 
loans, and the loans of the World Bank which are granted to developing countries on 
political, not economic basis. Countries which America and West Europe approve of are 
granted these loans. Others which are independent of U.S. hegemony, refuse U.S. bases 
and its new colonialism do not benefit from these loans. 


He then stressed the necessity of Africa's and Third World countries’ developing and 
promoting their economy and exploiting their wealth. These should also get ready for 

a struggle to realise these important objectives, especially prepare themselves to fight 
imperialist countries. He then discussed U.S. imperialist policies and [word indistinct] 
he told participants that the world is facing [word indistinct] reactionary and right 
wing administration led by Reagan, Haig and Weinberger, and stressed that it is opposed 
to all democratic forces whether in America, the West or the rest of the world. This 
administration, he said, is back to the feudal system, adopts its terrorist repressive 
methods and is the enemy of every progressive sign of peoples’ aspirations. 


U.S. administration is waging a war on small peoples and Africa. It has shown its 
hostility towards Africa by resuming relations with the racist South African regime 
and lifting economic sanctions on its regime. At the same time it is using South 
African racists to aggress African people. 


Staff Major Jallud added: "The current terrorist aggression backed by America, on 
Angola and Mozambique will move on to Zambia, Libya, Chad and Congo. This most 
reactionary and feudal minded American administration blatently backs two racist 
regimes, the South African racist regime and the barbaric racist one in Palestine. 
It also endeavors to make each of these regimes a special gendarme after it lost its 
other gendarme: the shah of Iran."

He affirmed: '"'The American administration led by Reagan daily challenged African sentiments 
ever since it came to power. It inaugurated its rule by strengthening cooperation with the 
racist South African regime and undermining international decisions concerning independence 
of South-West Africa. It is now waging an economic war against and creating social and 
economic problems for Third World countries." 


He stressed: "The American administration is waging a war against small peoples and 
against [word indistinct]. This reactionary administration wants to create economic chaos 
in the world and believes it will be immune from such [word indistinct] go but President 
Reagan now finds himself being burnt with the fire he himself kindled. It is expected that 
American balance of payment deficit would reach 125 billion dollars and unemployment 10 
million people." 


Staff Major Jallud exposed the ugliest of American administration policy. He said: "This 
administration does not only want to starve and destroy African and Third World peoples but 
also wants its closest allies in Western Europe to be totally subservient to it." 


He warned that American administration policy "is threatening to kindle a third world war 
and social and political chaos within the three years remaining for this administration's 
term of office. He said: "This is the most dangerous period the world is going through 
since World War II. 


Before the rise of the Great Al Fateh Revolution, industrial countries used to set the 

price of raw materials, manufacture, search for and sell them, and developing countries 

did not have a say in these deals. Al Fateh Revoluion has led the revolution of countries 
rich in raw materials and oil, corrected the existing prices and enabled these countries 

to master their wealth, production and prices. This administration and the rest of 
capitalist countries opposed to all rights want to thwart these victories through 

political decisions, force and very low prices for raw materials. Some countries’ income 

has thus suffered serious set backs at a time when price of manufactured goods in capitalist 
countries is increasing by 18 to 20 per cent." 


Concluding his speech, Staff Major Jallud re-affirmed the necessity to change the current 
world economic order calling upon Third World peoples to struggle to transform it in 
order to benefit from a just monetary distribution. 


COOPERATION DISCUSSED WITH TUNISIAN MINISTER 


LD281622 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 28 Apr 82 


[Excerpts] The secretary of the People's Committee for Foreign Liaison met at noon today 
with Tunisian Foreign Minister Beji Caid es-Sebsi. They discussed consolidation of bilateral 
cooperation within the framework of economic unity. They also discussed improving relations 
to new levels of cooperation and implementing the agreements signed when the leader of the 
revolution visited Tunisia. 


The Tunisian minister arrived in Tripoli this morning. He explained in a statement to JANA 
that his visit comes within the framework of liaison and consultation, the foundations of 
which were laid down by Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and President Bourguiba. This visit, he 
said, will enable us to raise bilateral relations to the level of their historic (?links) 
and the aspirations of the two fraternal peoples for more progress and true solidarity.

COMMENTARY ON 'FALSE CELEBRATIONS' OVER SINAI 


LD281416 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 27 Apr 82 
{Station commentary] 


[Excerpts] Any act that a regime would like to regard as a victory cannot be regarded as 
such just because this regime issued a republican or royalist decree hailing the action 
as a victory. Official celebrations of this matter called a victory. despite the spot- 
lights and arranged propaganda campaigns, are nevertheless colorless. It isn't difficult 
at all to detect a sense of shame dominating all those who hold the celebration because 
they are ordered by the ruler to display a so-called joy on the occasion which the regime 
regards as a victory. 


A slave cannot become victorious simply by breaking only one of the many fetters around 
his wrist. This slave would, however, become victorious if he broke all the chains, 
corporal and moral, and then became his own master. A prisoner doesn't become a free 
man by being transferred from solitary confinement to an air-conditioned room inside the 
prison. He becomes truly free when he comes out to breathe the fresh air over the soil 
of his homeland. 


Nations do not feel that what they are celebrating is a victory when, in fact, it is not 
a real victory. Oppressed nations feel victorious only when they overcome their weakness 
and become strong nations. If their sovereignty is not complete, they feel victory only 
when they complete their sovereignty in actual fact. And when they have an occupied soil, 
they feel victory only when they recover this land and impose their own total sovereignty 
over it. 


If a regime in the age of falsehood and lies and fabrications comes forward to claim in 
front of a nation that it has realized a victory for this nation, the nation in question 
knows that what has been achieved is the very pinnacle of defeat. A regime like this 

cannot make its lie stick or make the nationdance for joy because in living nations there 
are spots of awareness and centers of feelings. People have brains which evaluate actions, 
differentiate between right and wrong and know the difference between victories and defeats. 


The war between the Arab nation and the Zionist enemy is a war of two poles apart for they 
will never meet, will never understand each other or have any possibility of coexistence 
on Arab soil. The struggle is between Arab nationalism and those Zionist invaders brought 
by imperialism from various countries of the world into Palestine and established for 

them on this soil a racist entity artificial and alien to the area. 


The ruler has the power to issue a republican or a royal decree in which he tells people 

to consider white as black and black as white. Through his radios, he also can issue 
republican or royal decrees telling people to praise the United States and accept coexistence 
with the Zionists because in the opinion of the rulers this is the fate of the Arabs, 
because their enemy, as these rulers are claiming, is more powerful and possesses more 
deadly weapons. The ruling regime possesses the power to issue republican or royal decrees 
ordering people to treat dis,race and defeat as victory and pride, but since when has the 
glorious history of nations been compiled by the decrees of dictatorial regimes? And 

since when have acts been regarded as victories just because the ruling regime wants them 
to carry this description? And since when have honorabledeeds been the creation of hired 
propaganda organs and suspect and mercenary pens? 


The Zionist enemy occupies an Arab land and no Arab action can be regarded as a true 
victory of the Arab nation unless this action is the liberation of all occupied Arab soil 
and first of all the soil which was occupied in 1948.

On this basis, the return of the West Bank and the Gaza Strip or any other Arab soil to the 
Arab nation while our soil, which the invaders nowadays call the land of Israel, remains 
under occupation will never be a victory for us but in actual fact a victory for the Zionist 
enemy who wants to obtain Arab recognition of his artificial entity to serve as a legal 
preparation for spreading out later on tu the Euphrates in the east and the Nile in the west. 


What is the meaning of the enemy's statement that if he felt that the Arabs are not abiding 
by his conditions, he will swoop down on them and seize their land? What does this mean? 

It means that all treaties which the ruling regime concluded with the enemy are not the means 
to peace and stability in the area, and that the Camp David accords, which they claim are 

a ground for peace, are in actual fact a time bomb which threatens to explode at any moment. 
Anything the Arabs might receive, regardless of its high or low value, is not worthwhile 

if its price is recognizing the Zionist enemy, liquidating the Palestinian issue and accept- 
ing a secondary role in the area while leaving the basic role and substantial weight in the 
region to a force alien to the area, Zionism. This force addresses the world as one who 
gives the orders and possesses might and sway over the Arab area, acting on the basis that 
the Arabs are a group of lower creatures whose extermination cannot be regarded even as 

a violation of the right of man or the sanctity of the human being. 


The proper, just and lasting solution to the conflict in the area is that occupied Arab 
soil should return in full to its Arab owners. This solution will be imposed by force no 
matter how long it takes to do so. The Zionist invaders will return to their homelands 
from which they came in the same way the French invaders returned from Algeria to France 
after about 130 years of colonization and the same way the Italian invaders returned from 
Libya to Italy after scores of years of tyranical fascist colonization. In Palestine, 
there will then be an Arab state consisting of all its sons who belong to Arab nationalism 
irrespective of their faiths. Only in this situation will celebrations be regarded as 
victory celebrations, but in any other situations all celebrations are false, untrue and 
cheap.
