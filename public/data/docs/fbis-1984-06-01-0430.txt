LIBYA

AL-ANBA‘ Reports U.S. Secretly Training Libyans 
LD311633 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1615 GMT 31 May 84 


[Text] Kuwait, 31 May (JANA) — Kuwaiti AL-ANBA‘ newspa- 
per today reports that a group of absconding Libyans have been 
trained at special camps in the United States on Soviet-made 
aircraft similar to those possessed by Libya. These planes were 
altered and equipped by the U.S. to enable them to be infiltrated 
into Libya to strike at important vital targets. The paper adds 
that an American paper recently reported the crash of a Soviet 
MIG-23 aircraft at the training field at one of the air bases in 
the United States.
