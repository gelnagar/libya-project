JANA: PDRY Security Agreement Minutes Signed 
LD061643 Tripoli JANA in English 1407 GMT 6 May 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Rajab 24, May 6, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The Democratic Yemen Republic and the Social- 
ist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah yesterday signed the 
minutes of their security agreement and the exchange of exper- 
tise in this field. 


It was signed by the secretary of the General People’s Committee 
for Justice and the Yemeni minister of interior and member of 
the Central Committee, Mr. Muhammad ‘Abdallah al-Batani 
[name as received]. 


NORTH AFRICA Q! 


Al-Batani Departs 6 May 
LD062150 Tripoli JANA in English 1756 6 May 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Rajab 24, May 6, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — Mohammad ‘Abdallah al-Batani, member of the 
Central Committee and minister of interior in the Yemen Peo- 
ple’s Democratic Republic, and the delegation accompanying 
him left here this afternoon after a short visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


Mr. Al-Batani praised the immense accomplishments attained in 
the Jamahiriyah through the Great Al-Fatih Revolution. 


In a statement to our own correspondent he said that during his 
visit he has witnessed a marked development in the Jamahiriyah 
at all levels which will certainly benefit the Libyan Arab people 
and the Arab nation.
