LIBYA

REVOLUTIONARY COMMITTEES SESSION CALLED 


LD252210 Tripoli JANA in English 1750 GMT 25 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulqidah 6, August 25, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The revolutionary 
force is to convene the fifth session of the revolutionary committees at 7 pm tonight. 
This is an emergency session called for to shoulder nationalist historic tasks, which are 
truly revolutionary in character and effective during this grave stage facing the Arab 
nation. The nation is witnessing a vile crusade, savage Zionist onslaught and flagrant 
impotent of the reactionary Arab regimes, which enabled the Zionist enemy to invade 
Lebanon and American imperialism to (?conquer) [word indistinct] the Arab homeland turning 
them into staging posts for aggression against this nation. 


The revolutionary force is to devote this emergency fifth session to specify the very 
serious nationalist tasks, conquer the arena of revolutionary action to accomplish these 
tasks in confronting the imperialist-Zionist-reactionary tide, and to confront the new 
crusaders who are conspiring against this nation's existence, future and bitter struggle 
for freedom.
