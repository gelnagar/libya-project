QAFSAH RADIO: ASSASSINATION ATTEMPT WOUNDS NOUIRA 
LD262336 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 26 Feb 80 LD 
[Excerpt] Qafsah Radio, monitored in southern Italy, interrupted its ordinary programs 
to broadcast the report that Hedi Nouira, prime minister of the protectorate of 
Tunisia, was wounded when an attempt was made on his life by revolutionaries operating 
in the capital. He had to be rushed to Paris for treatment, 
The broadcast said that Tunisian revolutionaries in the past few days planned to blow 
up some installations in the capital, especially the radio station and party head- 
quarters, The Tunisian authorities admitted such an operation when they caught one of 


the revolutionaries redhanded, 


An AFP dispatch to Paris reported the wounding of Nouira, describing his condition as 
serious. 


PDRY FOREIGN MINISTER ARRIVES, MAKES STATEMENT 


LD251206 Tripoli JANA in English 0814 GMT 25 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Feb 25, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Politbureau candidate-member and 
Foreign Minister of the Yemen Democratic Republic Mr, Salim Salih Muhammad arrived in 
Tripoli this afternoon on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 
Present at Mr. Muhammad's reception in Tripoli international airport were the foreign 
secretary, the Yemen's ambassador to the Jamahiriyah, and the secretariat's Arab 
department director. 


V. 27 Feb 80 NORTH AFRICA 


In a statement made to our correspondent the Yemeni foreign minister expressed delight 
for his visit to the Jamahiriyah. He said the Democratic Yemen Republic and the 
Jamahiriyah are linked by brotherhood and common struggle against colonialism, Zionism 
and their agents, he said: "I convey the greetings of our people to the Libyan Arab 
people, hoping that our relations will witness further progress and development so that 
we can realize the goals and expectations of the Arab nation". 


In sending his statement, he stressed the importance of joint meetings and con- 
sultations in a bid to confront the dangers of the colonialist-Zionist reactionary 
scheme and conspiracies against the Arab nation's just causes, which require the 
mobilisation of all efforts and the unity of Arabs,

IRANIAN ENVOY CONCLUDES TALKS ON FURTHERING COOPERATION 
LD251650 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 25 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli--Dr 'Ali Shams Ardakani, the ambassador of the Iranian Islamic 
Republic to Kuwait, left Tripoli at noon today after paying a visit to the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah during which relations between the two Islamic 
revolutions in Jamahiriyah and Iran and the means to deepen and develop these re- 
lations were discussed, 


The Iranian ambassador made a statement to JANA stressing the efforts by the two 
Islamic revolutions in Jamahiriyah and Iran to strengthen strategic relations between 
them and to stand united in the face of colonialism and the enemies of Islam, 


Brother Ardakani stressed that the understanding and sincerity he noted during his 
meeting in Jamahiriyah will definitely contribute to the consolidation and 
progress of these relations, 


The Iranian ambassador was seen off at Tripoli international airport by brother head 
of the department of Turkey and Iran at the Foreign Secretariat,

JANA ON MCHENRY'S REMARKS ON TUNISIAN INDEPENDENCE 
LD251339 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 25 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Voice of America has reported that U.S, Ambassador Donald McHenry has stated 
that the United States wants Tunisia to remain independent, and that he warned against 
any foreign interference in Tunisian affairs, He is also reported to have said that 
the United States will view any foreign interference in Tunisia's affairs very 
seriously, The JANA political affairs editor has commented on this, saying that Libya 
also views any foreign interference in Tunisia very seriously,
