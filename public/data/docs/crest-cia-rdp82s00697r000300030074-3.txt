Summary (AI): A 1974 UN Law of the Sea conference working paper, co-sponsored by Libya among eleven other states, proposes a draft article letting coastal states control customs, fiscal, immigration and sanitary matters in a contiguous zone beyond the territorial sea. It says nothing specific about Tripoli or domestic repression.
• Document is A/CONF.62/C.2/L.78, dated 23 August 1974, from the Second Committee of the Third UN Conference on the Law of the Sea.
• Co-sponsors listed include the Libyan Arab Republic along with Egypt, India, Iran, Kuwait, Liberia, Mexico, Morocco, Oman, Qatar, UAE and Yemen.
• The draft article concerns control in an economic and contiguous zone to prevent and punish infringement of customs, fiscal, immigration or sanitary regulations.
