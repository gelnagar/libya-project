Summary (AI): A 1981 Los Angeles Times article reports that Libyan agents tried to recruit American bomb experts from California for a terrorist training center in Tripoli, offering about $100,000. Most refused after intelligence warnings, though some reportedly accepted and went on to train terrorists.
• Libyan recruiters reportedly sought about 30 California bomb experts for a terrorist training center in Tripoli.
• Recruiters reportedly offered McCree $100,000, with money paid in advance.
• U.S. intelligence sources reportedly warned the recruited Americans that they might never come back alive.
• Some American bomb experts reportedly accepted the Libyan offer and supplied explosives and trained terrorists.
• McCree expressed concern that trained terrorists could attack the 1984 Los Angeles Olympics.
• 1977: Two men claiming to represent the Libyan government approached bomb squad officer Sgt. Arleigh McCree at a Beverly Hills hotel with an offer, reportedly about $100,000, to recruit American bomb experts.
• 1981: Ted Perez of the Ontario Fire Department was approached by an American recruiter on Libya's behalf and refused.
