Wilson and Frank E. Terpil. This is about U.S. intelligence matters tied to Libya rather than Libya's own internal affairs or places in Tripoli.
• Casey and other CIA officials testified before the House Intelligence Committee behind closed doors for three hours.
• Tentative CIA conclusions include no official CIA involvement in Libyan terrorist training, no official contact with Wilson or Terpil after their indictment, and no official CIA involvement in recruiting Green Berets for Libya projects.
