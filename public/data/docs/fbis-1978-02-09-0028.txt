[Text] Tripoli, February 8, JANA--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadndhafi, the leader of the First 
of September Revolution, last night announced that selections of the basic people's con- 
gress will begin throughout the country from Thursday. 


In an address on radio and television Colonel al-Qadhdhafi announced that the leadership 
committees of the people's congresses were also being upgraded. 


He said the selection process will be supervised by the revolutionary committees since 
these committees were not official or government as official departments do not exist 
in the Jamahiriyah at all as a result of the people's power introduced in the country, 
he said. 


He said that it was the people who had formed the people's congresses hence it would 

be the people who would select their leaderships. People, he added, had set up the 
people's administrative committees resvonsible before the people's congresses and had 
selected the revolutionary committees. Col. al-Qadhdhafi remarked on the results of the 
experiment in the past period by saying that it was based on principles of no democracy 
without people's congresses, committees all over the country, no representation on behalf 
of the people and rerresentation is a farce. [sentence as raceived] 


All these, he said, is irrevocable and basic, within the framework sovereignty of the 
peopie, represented by the people's conferences, the people's committees, and trade 
unions (syndicates). 


He added we can modify our experiment until it reaches near perfection so that the whole 
world might take it as a model and follow it. 


Col. al-Qadhdhafi stressed that Libyan men and women were equal in their political and 
by human rigrts, and that natural differences between the two sexes should not affect 
their equality in political, human and social rights such as education. There is no 
difference between teachers, students and employees of both sexes.

The viewpoints of the two sides were identical on the following points: 

1. Bilateral relations: The two sides examined the circumstances which affected the

C. To reopen the embassies of the two countries in Tripoli and Khartoum and to take

3. The two sides blessed the reconciliation efforts between the Chad Government and
