Al-Qadhdhafi Banquet Speech 
LD302046 Tripoli JANA in English 0932 GMT 30 Sep 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, 30 Sep, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY=-Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of 
the First of September Revolution, said that the Arab nation had all the material and 
spiritual-moral capacities which entitle it to be respected in the world. 


In his speech, given yesterday at a banquet in honour of King Khalid who is currently visit- 
ing the Jamahiriyah, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that the Arab nation's position does 
not allow it to follow in the rear of the superpowers and that a great nation like that could 
not prostrate to any oppressor on this earth. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "The Arab nation itself is one of the world's powers. Hence we 
should use our nation's material and moral force to bring out its power and to force the world 
to respect it. 


This great national goal, he added, is the dream of all Arab unionists, revolutionaries and 
nationalists for which they have fought. Their only aim was to ensure the Arab nation's 
triumph, to liberate occupied Arab territories, to unite the Arab nation and to place its 
capacities at the Arabs! disposal, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi affirmed that Arab unionists and revolutionaries do not want the Arab 
nation to be a milking cow and would not be transformed into a mine supplying the enemy's 
factories nor into a farm feeding the enemy. 


Colonel Mufammar al-Qadhdhafi pointed out the dangerous circumstances under which the Arab 
nation is currently passing following (Israel's) seizure of the Egyptian front, one of the 
nation's fronts. 


He said this front which used to be one of the front lines facing the enemy is now under 
(Israeli) control and has become an anti-Arab front, which undoubtedly constitutes a great 
loss to the Arab nation. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added in a speech he made at a banquet given in honour of King Khalid 
of Saudi Arabia that the dangerous rift in the Arab ranks and capitulation of one of the 
nation's front lines should not lead us to capitulation or despair for we believe we are 
right and the enemy's wrong and those with rights to wrench would eventually win. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that the Egyptian front, being within the Arab lines, poses a greater 
threat then that of the enemy in occupied Palestine which is surrounded by the Arab nation. 


Should this siege, he added, be maintained and should the Arab nation continue to be prepared 
for the enemy, the day would eventually come when (Israel) would fall. Unfortunately. 

the enemy has been relieved now that the Suez Canal, vital to it, is reopened after the 

Arab nation's struggle closed it. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi went on: "The danger of the capitulatory Egyptian front has begun to 
appear now as it is attacking the Arab nation. Not long ago President as-Sadat, who is 
(Israel's) tool against the Arab nation, was happy to open its front to the enemy to 
intensify tension in the Arab Maghreb region. 


Colonel Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi commended Bahrain's attitude towards the defeated Egyptian 
regime's false allegations.

CENTRAL AFRICAN FRONT LEADER ARRIVES, MAKES STATEMENT 
LD291148 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 28 Sep 79 LD 


[Text] Ange Patasse, leader of the Popular Liberation Front of Central Africa, arrived in 
Tripoli tonight from Paris, where he has sought refuge since yesterday at the Arab Libyan 
Bureaus. He was welcomed at Tripoli International Airport by a representative of the Foreign 
Secretariat. 


In a statement to JANA, he praised the attitude of brother Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi toward the 
French military invasion of Central Africa. He praised the role of the Jamahiriyah and its 
opposition to this invasion. He also praised the Jamahiriyah's refusal to grant political 
asylum to Emperor Bokassa. 


Patasse went on to add that the Jamahiriyah!'s stand opposed to the French military invasion 
encouraged us to seek political asylum here. He went on to condemn the French military 
invasion of the territory of Central Africa and said that the French military forces at present 
were besieging Central Africa. 


Patasse appealed to all African countries and all progressive forces throughout the world to 
demand the withdrawal of the French forces immediately and unconditionally from Central Africa. 
He said: I draw the attention of African countries to the fact that the problem today in the 
Central African Republic [CAR] concerns not onlythe CAR but also all independent countries in 
the continent. He said it was a U.S, attempt to restore colonialism in Africa, He explained 
that France sought by its invasion to exploit the natural resources of Central Africa, in 
particular those of energy. 


Patasse praised the stand of the president of Benin, Mathieu Karakou, which was opposed to 
the French invasion. He called on the Organization of African Unity to adopt firmer attitudes 
toward the invasion and to work for submitting the case to the United Nations.
