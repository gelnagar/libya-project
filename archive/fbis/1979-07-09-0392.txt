PRESS STATEMENT ISSUED ON VISIT OF IRAN'S MONTAZERI 
LD061128 Tripoli JANA in English 0946 GMT 6 Jul 79 LD 
{"Press release on the visit of Shaykh Montazeri to the Jamahiriyah"-~JANA headline ] 


[Text] Tripoli, July 6, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY«-The following press statement was 
released yesterday on the visit of Shaykh Mohammed Montazeri to the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah which reads as follows; 


"Within the framework of the strategic alliance between the Great First of September 
Revolution and the Iranian Islamic revolution which reflects the dimensions of rapproche- 
ment and integration between the peoples of the two countries, in a way which serves 
their objectives and aspirations for Islamic unity and a unified stand against their 
enemies, a people's delegation from the cadres of the Iranian Islamic revolution headed 
by Shaykh Mohammad Montazeri, the member of the Islamic Republican Party's Central 
Committee, paid a visit to the Jamahiriyah between 3-6 July, During the visit the 
delegation met with Staff-Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud; Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis 
Jabir; 'Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, the secretary-general of the General People's Congress; 
and Ahmad ash-Shihati, the head of the Foreign Liaison Bureau, These meetings were 
attended by Sa'd Mujabir, the editor of AL-USBU' AS-SIYASI and AL-USBU! AL-THAQAFI, 


The delegation held a political meeting at the Foreign Liaison Bureau at the General 
Secretariat of the General People's Congress during which the two sides considered methods 
of promoting and reinforcing the bonds of brotherhood and relations of common struggle 
between the two revolutions, through the exchange of visits by various delegations at 
peoples! levels, to increase coordination and to enable the Iranian delegations to 

observe the experiment currently implemented in the Jamahiriyah to establish the people's 
democracy which sets an example to the world, 


The exchange of peoples! delegations will also give the Libyan Arabs the opportunity to 
find the facts about the glories of the Islamic revolution in Iran and to establish 
direct contacts between the two brotherly peoples, 


Moreover it has been agreed that people's delegations from the Iranian Islamic revolution 
and the Jamahiriyah would participate in future in each other's religious bairams and 
national festivals,

During their meeting, the two sides stressed their rejection of the treaty of shame and 
humiliation which was signed by the defeated ruler of Egypt and their condemnation of 
all the parties which collaborated with him either openly or in disguise as well as 
those who contributed and still contribute in dissolving the cause of the Palestinian 
Arab people. 


The two gides praised the steadfastness and confrontation front and the efforts exerted 

by the leader of the Great First of September Revolution, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 

to foil the As-Sadatist-Zionist-imperialist scheme in the region, through the mobilization 
of Arab efforts and capabilities and the reinforcement of and guidance to the ranks 

of the Palestinian revolution, in preparation for the battle of full liberation of 

every inch of unsurped Arab Islamic land. 


The two parties also asserted that any provocation of any of the two revolutions by the 
imperialist-Zionist-reactionary forces would be interpreted as an attempt to strike at 
the Islamic revolutionary forces which should be confronted. 


The two sides affirmed that the two revolutions, with all their political and economic 
potentials as well as their geographic position, are capable of teaching hard lessons to 
the enemies of the Islamic and Arab nation. 


Whilst endeavouring to establish the basis of mutual cooperation and employing the 
efforts of both revolutions for the benfit of their peoples and of all the human, 

Islamic and Arab peoples. The two parties declare their support to all the revolutionary 
liberation forces of the world. They stand by the oppressed of the earth, at the service 
of Islam and humanity and denounce all the military, economic and political pressures 
exerted by reaction, Zionism and imperialism. 


At the end of these talks, which were held in an atmosphere of brotherhood marked by 
frankness and a sense of responsibility, agreement was reached on the setting up of 

a joint programme reflecting the agreement in viewpoints with respect to the issues 

considered and fully coordinating between them. 


Furthermore, Shaykh Montazeri had the opportunity to speak at a political education 
session at the "Al-Fatah" camp. He spoke to foreign and Arab reporters at a press 
conference and defined to them and underlying principles of the Islamic revolution in 
Iran and its support for the world's liberation movement. 


Shaykh Montazeri invited the head of the Foreign Liaison Bureau at the secretariat 
of the Arab People's Congress to visit Iran. The invitation was accepted with thanks, 
at a date to be set later".
