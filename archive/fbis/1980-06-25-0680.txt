LIBYA

FORMER AL-QADHDHAFI ASSOCIATE REPORTS SHOOTING 
NC 250729 (Clandestine) Voice of Lebanon in Arabic to Lebanon 0615 GMT 25 Jun 80 
{Radio's Pari: correspondent's dispatch] 


[Excerpt] Last night French television, Channel 1, broadcast an interview with a 

military official who was close to Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. This man, who is called 

Mubi, had left Libya in order to escape the elimination campaign being carried out by 

Libya against opponents. He arrived in Paris 2 days ago. Mubi, who is of Chadian origin, 
said an aide of the Libyan leader fired at Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, hitting him in the shoulder. 
He added that he had seen the wounded colonel more than once. He also said that Libya had 
shrouded the subject in complete secrecy in order to prevent the report from being leaked 
abroad. 


PFLP-GC LEADER JIBRIL ARRIVES, MAKES STATEMENT 


LD241716 Tripoli JANA in English 1408 GMT 24 Jun 80 


(Text] Banghazi, June 24, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The secretary of the Popular Front for 
the Liberation of Palestine-General Command, Mr Ahmad Jibril, arrived in Banghazi yester- 
day on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. In a statement to our 
correspondent on the current situation in the Arab region following the Egyptian president's 
decision to declare the state of emergency on the eastern borders of the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and his military buildup, he said that the Egyptian president's 
moves are not surprising, and since his capitulation to the enemy, he performed actions 
against the wishes of the Arab nation and her masses. He added that the Libyan Revolution 
uncovered the Egyptian president and his course even before 1972, and warned the Arab nation 
of his dangerous course and capitulation designs among which is the signing of Camp David 
accords. 


Mr Jibril declared that the Egyptian president after accomplishing the main part of the 
imperialist design of Camp David accords turned into a repressive police [as received] in 
the region. Currently he is assigned with new tasks against the national and revolutionary 
forces in the Arab region and Africa. 


Mr Jibril referred to As-Sadat's declaration of a state of emergency and his military build- 
up along the eastern borders with the Jamahiriyah, describing this move as part of the 
American-(Israeli) colonialist scheme which aims to distract the Egyptian people from the 
internal crisis and As-Sadat's failure to fulfill his promises to [words indistinct] the 
Egyptian president's measures against Libya, he said, is one side of a U.S. conspiracy 
against Al-Fatah revolution and all progressive forces in the region and Africa. The 
squadrons of U.S. fighters on the Egyptian soil is an evidence to this. We should not 
rule out the presence of (Israeli) units on the borders with Libya supporting [words 
indistinct], the Egyptian regime in carrying out a further reckless action against the 
Libyan Arab people directed towards restoring the U.S. colonial domination on the whole 
of the Arab region. 


Mr. Jibril affirmed that the Egyptian president was mistaken if he believed his adventure 
would be carried out smoothly, for he should know that Al-Fatah revolution is strong and 
its strength is based on the will and determination of the Libyan people. He concluded by 
pledging the solidarity of the Palestinian revolution's factions with great Al-Fath revolu- 
tion and stressed that they will fight side by side with the Jamahiriyah against the 
puppet Egyptian regime.

TEXT OF BREZHNEV GREETINGS MESSAGE TO AL-QADHDHAFI 
LD242152 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GmT 24 Jun 80 


[Text] The brother leader has received a greetings message from Leonid Brezhnev, general 
secretary of the CPSU and chairman of the USSR Supreme Soviet Presidium, on the 10th 
amiiversary of the evacuation of the U.S. military bases. Here is the text: 


To Lt Col Multammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 1 September Revolution: On the occasion 
of the 10th anniversary of the evacuation of the American bases in Libya, we offer our 
warm greetings to you personally and to the people of the Socialist People's Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah. 


On the day the last American soldier left Libya, an important stage ended in the liberation 
struggle of the Libyan people for strengthening their national independence and against 

the presence of foreign forces and Western military bases which represented important 
imperialist centers in Libya. At this time when imperialism is trying to return to its 
former positions and to deploy its military present in the Middle East, we find Libya has 
enthusiastically ended the military activities of imperialist powers inits territory, thus 
giving anew boost tothe peoples! struggling aginst the establishment of new bases in the 
Arab homeland and in regions close to it in Asia and Africa.
