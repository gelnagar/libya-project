Summary (AI): A March 1986 FBIS routing sheet and Nicosia cable say the FBIS bureau in Cyprus took precautions after a Libyan-Cypriot Friendship Society demonstration outside the Embassy on 26 March 1986 and a specific Libyan threat. The document concerns Cyprus, not Tripoli, and gives no Tripoli-specific facts.
• FBIS Nicosia bureau staggered shift hours for the next 10 days in response to a specific Libyan threat (Ref A, DIRECTOR 795322).
• Bureau staff had been briefed on the Libyan threat the previous week.
• Management said it would keep irregular hours and requested extra police patrols of staff residences.
• 1986-03-26: Libyan-Cyprus Friendship Society demonstration outside the embassy; passed without incident; FBIS bureau took alert precautions

CONFIDENTIAL 261355Z MAR 86 STAFF

TO: PRIORITY FBIS. 

SUBJECT: LIBYAN ALERT PRECAUTIONS 

REFS: A. DIRECTOR 795322 
B. DIRECTOR 794187 

1. ASSUME YOU HAVE SEEN REFS, PARTICULARLY REF A. 
2. LIBYAN-CYPRUS: FRIENDSHIP SOCIETY HELD DEMONSTRATION 

‘OUTSIDE EMBASSY 26 MARCH. IT PASSED WITHOUT INCIDENT. 

BUREAU WAS ALERTED BY EMBASSY AND NECESSARY PRECAUTIONS, 
AS OUTLINED IN OUR E&E PLAN, WERE TAKEN WITHOUT INTERRUPTION 
OF WORK. 

3. BUREAU STAFFERS HAD ALREADY BEEN BRIEFED ON LIBYAN 
THREAT LAST WEEK. BUT IN LIGHT OF SPECIFIC THREAT 
CONTAINED IN REF A, WE WILL STAGGER SHIFT HOURS FOR NEXT 10 
DAYS SO EDITORS ARRIVALS-DEPARTURES FROM HOMES/BUREAU ARE 
MORE IRREGULAR. MANAGEMENT WILL ALSO KEEP IRREGULAR HOURS. 
IF EXTRA PATROLS OF RESIDENCES ARE LAID ON BY LOCAL POLICE, 
WE HAVE REQUESTED THAT FBIS STAFFERS BE PROVIDED THIS SERVICE. 
IF MORE SPECIFIC INFORMATION BECOMES AVAILABLE, WE WILL 
RECONSIDER SITUATION, BARRING NEED TO CLOSE RUREAU, THIS 
WILL PROBABLY ENTAIL MINIMAL STAFFING, SOMF SHIFTING OF 
COVERAGE, AND POSSIBLY DISPERSING OFF-DUTY STAFFERS TO SAFFR 
AREAS ON ISLAND, WE DON’T THINK THERE IS ANY MORE WE CAN DO 
AT THIS TIME. BUT ARE OPEN T? 1 ES SUGGESTIONS, IF ANY. 

ALL CONF TDERTEAL.
