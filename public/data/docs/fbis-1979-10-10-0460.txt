AT-TURAYKI, NONALINED MINISTERS DISCUSS 'FRENCH INTERVENTION! 
1D051644 Tripoli JANA in English 1414 Gmf 5 Oct 79 ID 


{Text] New York, Oct 5, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The foreign ministers of non-aligned 
countries held an emergency session yesterday at the UN headquarters on the request 
of the Jamahiriyah to discuss the French invasion of the Central African empire. 


At the commencement of the session Dr. ‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, the Jamahiriyah 
foreign secretary, said that the invasion was a threat to the non-aligned movement, 
a violation of the principles and charter of the U.N., and of the Organization of 
African Unity (OAU). 


He pointed out that the French intervention was not the first of its kind, For many 
years France has been intervening directly in the affairs of the continent, using 
weapons and mercenaries to change their aggressive regimes, conspiring against these 
countries and using them as military bases, 


He clarified: "It is the right of the peoples to carry out the changes they desire, 
It is not acceptable to have foreign intervention, which is a violation of the 
sovereignty of the independent member countries of the U.N, We in the Jamahiriyah 
do not defend individuals, but we reject the principle of foreign intervention in 
the affairs of an independent country which enjoys complete sovereignty, toppling 
its regime and appointing some Paris resident to take over," 


Dr. at-Turayki called upon the non-aligned countries to condemn the invasion, so as 
to reinforce the movement of non-alignment and its confrontation of schemes designed 
against its unity. 


Nigeria drafted a motion condemning the invasion, calling for immediate measures to be 
taken to stop the invasion and the withdrawal of the Prench forces,
