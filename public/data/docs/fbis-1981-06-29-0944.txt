LIBYA

JALLUD, DELEGATION BEGIN TALKS IN MOSCOW 27 JUN 


LD271334 Tripoli JANA in English 0948 GMT 27 Jun 81 


[Text] Moscow, 27 Jun (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--Talks began today between Staff Major 
Jallud's delegation and its Soviet counterpart. Participants on the Arab Libyan side 
included some members of the Ceneral People's Committee, officials from the Secretariats 
of Housing, Electricity, Energy, Heavy and Light Industries. The Soviet side included 
the first deputy of the prime minister and the ministers of industry, planning together 
with the deputy foreign minister and a number of Soviet officials. 


SUDANESE ANTIREGIME ORGANIZATION SAID FORMED 


LD281454 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 28 Jun 81 


[Text] The formation of the organization of the officers of the people's armed forces 
for national salvation [hay'at dubbat quwwat al-sha'b al-musallah li inaqadh al-watan] 
was announced in Khartoum today. A statement issued by the organization and circulated 
in Khartoum last night said that the organization truly symbolizes the will of the 
national officers, noncommissioned officers and men and affirms the Sudanese people's 
determination to topple and uproot the regime. 


The statement affirmed that the one and only objective of the establishment of this 
organization is to save the country from blind subservience to U.S. imperialism and to 
check the danger threatening Sudan. The statement called on the Sudanese masses--workers, 
merchants, farmers, intelligentsia and students--to be fully vigilant, to reject the 
trickery of the Numayri regime and to work to topple the regime. 


The statement affirmed that the path pursued by the Numayri regime has held to the 
complete collapse of Sudan's economy, its dependence on crumbs from the tables of U.S. 
imperalism, the squandering of the country's national independence and the transformation 
of Sudan into a base for American occupation armies. 


The statement also affirmed that the continuation of the popular uprising in the face of 
Numayri's regime and the closing of ranks will bring nearer the day of deliverance and 
the downfall of this agent regime.
