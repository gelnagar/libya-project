((TEXT)) THE BROTHER LEADER SAID THAT THE GREATNESS OF THE ARAB 
NATION LIES IN THIS MEETING. WE REALIZE THAT OUR NATION IS IN 
DANGER. WE NO LONGER THINK IN TERMS OF WHETHER YOU BELIEVE IN GOD 
OR YOU DO NOT, WHETHER YOU SAY YOUR PRAYERS OR YOU DO NOT, 
WHETHER YOU BELIEVE IN SCIENTIFIC SODIALISM OR YOU DO NOT. ‘THESE ARE 
VARIOUS TRENDS IN THIS NATION. THIS IS THE COLLECTIVE SPIRIT OF 
THIS NATION. IT IS A NATION THAT HAS SPIRIT AND SUBSTANCE. AFTER 
THIS MEETING I HAVE NOTHING BUT PROFOUND ESTEEM FOR YOU, UNDILUTED 
ESTEEM FOR EACH AND EVERY ONE OF YOU AND FOR EACH OF YOUR 
DETACHMENTS. THIS IS BECAUSE UNTIL VERY RECENTLY I USED TO REGARD 
A MEETING IN WHICH ALL ARAB REVOLUTIONARIES WOULD GATHER WITH ALL 
THEIR VARIOUS FACTIONS AROUND ONE TABLE AND PRODUCE ONE DECISION 
AND SIGN ONE PAPER AS SOMETHING BORDERING ON THE IMPOSSIBLE. THAT 
USED TO BE MY ASSESSMENT; ONE WHICH WAS APPARENTLY INCORRECT, THANK 
GOD. 
THE BROTHER LEADER SAID: ON OUR PART I WOULD LIKE TO ASSURE YOU 
THAT YOU WILL NOT BE DISAPPOINTED IN US AS INDIVIDUALS, AS A LAND, 
AND AS A PEOPLE WHOSE GREAT SACRIFICES, DESPITE LIMITED 
CAPABILITIES AND SMALL NUMBERS, YOU ARE WELL AWARE OF. BUT THIS 
PEOPLE HAS ALWAYS BEEN AN EXPRESSION OF THE GREATNESS OF THE ARAB 
NATION. 
I SWEAR TO YOU ON MY FAMILY HONOR AND MY MILITARY HONOR AS 
WELL, AND ON MY REVOLUTIONARY AND PAN-ARAB COMMITMENT, AND ON THE 
HONOR AND THE COMMITMENT OF MY COLLEAGUES IN THE MOVEMENT OF THE 
FREE UNIONIST OFFICERS AND THE REVOLUTIONARY OFFICERS WHO JOINED 
AFTER THE EMERGENCE OF THE REVOLUTION AND THE REVOLUTIONARY 
COMMITTEES, THAT WE WILL NOT DESERT YOU OR DISAPPOINT YOUR HOPE 
IN US. WE WILL BE WITH YOU WITH ALL WHAT WE POSSESS BOTH IN GOOD 
AND HARD TIMES. WE WOULD LIKE TO STRESS THAT WE HAVE NOT THE LEAST 
FEAR OF THE ENEMY OR THE REACTIONARIES. WE SIT TOGETHER WITH YOU 
WHILE WE TREAD WITH OUR FEET THE THRONES OF REACTION IN THIS PLACE 
WHICH USED TO HOUSE KINGS AND PRINCES. WE WERE NOT AFRAID EVEN WHEN 
WE WERE VERY JUNIOR OFFICERS AND ORDINARY SOLDIERS. THIS 
INDICATES THAT WE HAVE GONE BEYOND THE STAGE OF BEING AFRAID OF 
REACTION. THE THRONE AND SCEPTER OF HUSAYN OR ANY OTHER DID NOT 
FRIGHTEN US AND DOES NOT FRIGHTEN US NOW. WE SIT HERE WHILE THE 
CROWN OF IDRIS AL-SUNUSI IS DESTROYED UNDER OUR FEET AND SHOES. 
ALSO, NONE OF US HAS AN IOTA OF FEAR OF AMERICA. IT SUFFICES THAT 
WE EMBARKED ON THE REVOLUTION AND EXPELLED AMERICA, WHICH HAD FIVE 
HUGE MILITARY BASES, ONE OF WHICH HAD ATOMIC BOMBS -- THAT BASE 
WAS WHEELUS BASE IN TRIPOLI -- AND THE BRITISH BASE. LIBYA WAS 
NATURALLY OPEN TO THEIR FORCES WHO USED TO COME AND GO WITHOUT 
PERMISSION. THERE USED TO BE 20,000: ITALIAN COLONIALISTS OCCUPYING 
TRIPOLI AND ITS OUTSKIRTS. 
THEREFORE, WE DO NOT THE LEAST FEAR OF AMERICA. AMERICA MAY 
HAVE BEEN DELUDED BY AGENTS TO THINK THAT THE ARABS ARE AFRAID OF 
IT, LIKE THE VERSE IN THE KORAN WHICH SAYS: "MEN SAID TO THEM: A 
GREAT ARMY IS GATHERING AGAINST YOU AND FRIGHTENED THEM; BUT IT 
ONLY INCREASED THEIR FAITH."

OFFICIALLY ANNOUNCED TO THE WORLD THAT THE BAY OF SIDRA WILL BE 
DEFENDED TO THE DEATH AND WILL BE TURNED INTO A BLOODY BAY IF 
AMERICA ENTERS IT. THIS SHOWS THAT WE ARE NOT AFRAID OF AMERICA. 
THE BROTHER LEADER ADDED: WHEN THE AWACS PLANES CAME TO EGYPT 
AND SUDAN WE OFFICIALLY ANNOUNCED TO THE WORLD THAT THE ORDERS 
WERE ISSUED TO THE LIBYAN AIR FORCE THAT IF THE AWACS PLANES 
INTERFERED IN ANY WAY IN THE LIBYAN SKIES AND LIBYAN SOIL THEY 
WERE INSTRUCTED TO ATTACK THEM WHEREVER THEY WERE IN ANY SKY. 
AFTERWARD THEY WITHDREW THEM. AND IN LEBANON, WE HAVE SEEN HOW 
THEY WITHDREW IN HUMILIATION. 
THE BROTHER LEADER AFFIRMED THAT THE BOLD BLOWS OF THE ARAB NATION 
IN LEBANON DESTROYED NOT WHAT THE HIRELING AL-SADAT USED TO CALL 
"THE PSYCHOLOGICAL BARRIER TO RECOGNIZING AND ACCEPTING THE 
ISRAELIS" BUT THEY DESTROYED THE FEAR BARRIER -- IF ANYONE EVER 
FEARED THE ISRAELIS; IT TURNED OUT THAT THE ISRAELIS WERE COWARDS 
AND PAPER TIGERS. 
THE BROTHER LEADER SAID: WE DECLARE TO OUR NATION AND TO THE 
WORLD THAT WE DO NOT FEAR IMPERIALISM OR ZIONISM OR THEIR WAR 
MACHINE. WE IN THE HEART OF THE ARAB HOMELAND WANT TO DECLARE TO 
CONTEMTIBLE REACTION THAT IT IS TRULY BASE; AND REACTION HAS 
JOINED HANDS WITH AMERICA AND IS SECRETLY IN ALLIANCE WITH THE 
ZIONIST ENEMY. 
WE ARE MEETING HERE IN ONE OF THE ARAB TOWNS OPENLY AND WE INCLUDE 
IN OUR PROGRAM THE QUESTION OF DESTROYING REACTION AND ITS ALLIES. 
THIS IS A SIGNIFICANT BLOW. WE WANT THEM TO UNDERSTAND AND SAH: 
THERE IS SOMEONE WHO IS CHALLENGING US INSIDE THE ARAB HOMELAND. 
OUR MEETINGS ARE PUBLIC AND ON TELEVISION AND ON RADIO. THEY 
INCLUDE PROGRAMS WHICH SAY: WE WILL DESTROY REACTION, ZIONISM 
AND IMPERIALISM. 
THE BROTHER LEADER SAID REACTION THINKS THAT THERE IS NO ONE 
WHO CAN CONFRONT IT. THEY THINK THAT ALL OF YOU WILL GO TO THEM 
AND WILL MARCH BEHIND THE OIL AND SAY TO THEM: WE ARE WITH YOU 
AND WE WILL STOP THE ACTS OF VIOLENCE AGAINST YOU AND AGAINST 
YOUR ALLIES. PLEASE GIVE US SOME ALMS. 
LET THEM GO TO HELL TOGETHER WITH THEIR OIL. WE SHOULD SET 
FIRE TO THE OIL; IT SHOULD NOT GO TO AMERICA AND WE DO NOT WANT IT.
