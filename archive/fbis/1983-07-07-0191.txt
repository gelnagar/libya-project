LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Denies Intervention in Chad 
PM041 200 Paris Le Matin in French | Jul 83 pp 2, 3 


{Interview with Libyan head of state Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
by Marc Kravetz in Tripoli — date not given] 


[Text] Le Matin: Colonel, you are accused on all sides of 
rekindling the Chadian fire and supplying, if not men, at least 
the togistical support and weapons which enabled Goukouni 
Oueddei to seize Faya-Largeau. You obviously reject those 
accusations, but it is nonetheless difficult to believe you when you 
state, as you do in you letter to the Ethiopian head of state, who 
is OAU chairman, that Libya is adopting a position of strict 
neutrality and noninterference toward Chad. 


Al-Qadhdhafi: 1! say that because it is the truth, and | repeat 
that there is not a single Libyan soldier in Chad. Indeed, | have 
written to the OAU chairman to ask for the formation of a 
commission of enquiry which will visit the scene and establish 
that we are telling the truth. We are particularly anxious for that 
commission to be formed and we want it to visit Faya-Largeau 
as soon as possible. 


Le Matin. You say there are no Libyan soldiers. That is not 
incompatible with the other accusation made against you: that 
you are supplying air cover for Goukouni’s offensive. 


Al-Qadhdhafi: We did not intervene in that battle. We do not 
intend to intervene. We will not intervene a second time in Chad. 
We were as surprised as the rest of the world to learn of the attack 
on Faya-Largeau. 


Le Matin. Nonetheless, you supplied Goukouni’s weapons — 
modern and particularly efficient weapons, judging from what | 
have read in the past few days. 


Al-Qadhdhafi: If there are any modern weapons on the battle- 
field it is those supplied to Ndjamena by France. As for Libyan 
weapons, they are found in both camps. Hissein Habre is fighting 
with Libyan weapons. So is Goukouni. But they are not very 
modern weapons. They date from about 10 years ago, from the 
days when we supplied equipment to Frolinat, within which 
Habre and Goukouni were then allies. We are not sending 
modern weapons to Chad, as you say, partly because we know 
that they are likely to be used against us some day. 


Le Matin. Observers also noted that Goukouni’s offensive came 
at a time when some of Hissein Habre’s forces are immobilized 
on the other side by a border war waged against them by the 
Nigerians from a region reputed to be favorable to you. 


Al-Qadhdhafi: We have aboslutely nothing to do with the 
border conflict between the Chadians and Nigerians over a few 
small islands in Lake Chad.

pening in Chad?
