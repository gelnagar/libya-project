an  important  role  in  these  countries '  balances  of  payments ,  in  addition 
to  meeting  the  needs  of  the  families  of  the  migrating  workers.  On  the

been  gaining  in  significance,  especially  from  the  viewpoint  of  comprehen¬ 
sive  Arab  development. 

One  of  these  negative  effects  is  the  difference  in  wages  in  the  Arab 
countries  and  the  subsequent  rise  of  wages  in  the  labor  exporting 
countries.  This  rise  in  wages  has  been  accompanied  by  a  rise  in  the 
cost  of  living  in  these  countries  and  this  is  something  that  increases 
the  deficits  of  these  countries  and  obstructs  implementation  of  their 
development  projects  whose  costs  have  also  risen.
