Statement Issued 
LD212258 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 2115 GMT 21 Feb 85 


[Text] A statement was issued today in Tripoli at the conclusion 
of a meeting between Brothers Walid Junblatt, leader of the 
Progressive Socialist Party, and Ibrahim Qulaylat, leader of the 
Independent Nasirites Al-Murabitun movement. The statement 


Says 


Within the framework of the pan-Arab work and in unification 
of the pan-Arabist forces’ efforts, the following has been agreed 


To firmly adhere to the historical alliance between the 
Progressive Socialist Party and the Al-Murabitun Movement 
founded with the revolution of 1958, which was led by the great 
leader Kamal Junblatt. 


To translate this historic alliance into tangible action 
through the unity of pan-Arab stances and in the form of joint 
struggle action to confront the imminent dangers in the Lebanese 
and Arab arenas, and in a manner that will serve the pan-Arab 
courses and stances 


They declare to their cadres, strugglers and their masses their 
firm: commitment to (?improve) the materialization of the pan- 
Arab, national, and Islamic positions 


They have decided to work together with all conviction, serious- 
ness and commitment to realize these objectives. 


Signed: Walid Junblatt, leader of the Progressive Socialist 
Party;I brahim Qulaylat, leader of the Al-Murabitun Movement
