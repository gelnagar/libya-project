LIBYA

More on Al-Qadhdhafi on Mubarak-‘ Arafat Meeting 
LD231956 Tripoli JANA in English 1415 GMT 23 Dec 83 


[Text] Tripoli Rabi al Awal 17, Dec 23, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY —- The leader of the revolution condemned 
Yasir ‘Aratat’s meeting with the president of the Egyptian 
regime. In a speech delivered yesterday evening, to a women’s 
gathering at the basic people’s congress in central Tripoli, he said 
“The exciting thing today was ‘Arafat's meeting with the Egyp- 
tian President. It is known that Egypt, after it recognized the 
enemy, was blacklisted, according to the decision of the Arab 
summit meeting in Baghdad and was boycotted by the Arabs till 
it withdraws its recognition of the Zionist enemy”. 


The leader explained that the problem between us and the Israelis 
is the Palestinian problem. The issue of Palestine’s occupation. 
If Yasir ‘Arafat is the defender of the Palestinian cause and 
meets with the Egyptian president who recognizes what is called 
Israel, then everything has turned upside down 


The leader posed a few questions saying, if ‘Arafat the Palestin- 
ian and representative of the Palestinian cause makes it up with 
the president of a regime which recognizes the enemy, what are 
we the rest of the Arabs to do? Aren't we all working for the 
Palestinian cause? 


He added when Abu ‘Ammar no longer represents the Palestin- 
ian people and Palestinian cause and renounces the Palestinian 
cause and the Arab fold. [sentence as received] He is considered 
a traitor like the rulers of Egypt hence it is of no consequence if 
a traitor meet a traitor, in agreement with the enemy and having 
sold out the cause. If Abu ‘Ammar is still patriotic and not a 
traitor and he is still the defender of the Palestinian cause, this 
means boycotting Egypt. It becomes meaningless if each one of 
us went to meet Husni Mubarak 


The leader questioned further “[quotation mark as received] Why 
did we boycott Egypt... [JANA ellipses] We boycotted Egypt for 
Palestine..{[JANA ellipses] and now the leader of Palestine is in 
Egypt 


What do we do then? We do no longer have any justification for 
boycotting Egypt, as the representative of the Palestinian cause, 
for whom we boycotted Egypt, is in Egypt, meeting the president 
of the Egyptian regime.

{Text} Jamahiriyah: The secretary of the People’s Bureau for 
Foreign Liaison this morning summoned the ambassador of 
Jordan to the Jamahiriyah, to protest Jordan's action, which 
departs from the resolutions of the daghdad summit, signing of 
an economic agreement with the government of the Egyptian 
regime which recognizes the Zionist enemy
