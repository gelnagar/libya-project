Upper Volta’s Captain Zongo Visits Tripoli 
LDO91521 Tripoli JANA in English 1426 GMT 9 Jan 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Thani 5, Jan 9, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — Captain Henri Zongo, member of the National 
Revolutionary Council and member of the cabinet of Upper 
Volta, arrived last night in Tripoli on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


Brigadier Mustafa al-Kharrubi, the chief of staff of the Armed 
Forces, met Captain Zongo at the international airport of Tripoli. 


JANA Reports Fire on Tunisian-Algerian Pipeline 
LD101023 Tripoli JANA in English 0918 GMT 10 Jan 84 


[Text] Tunis, Rabi Thani 6, Jan 10, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The Tunisian minister of defence announced 
yesterday afternoon that a fire broke out early morning yesterday 
in the Algerian-Tunisian oil pipeline at a point inside Tunisian 
territory called Hanshir al-Bassasah. This pipeline connects the 
Algerian Ain Aminas [spelling of name as received] oil fields and 
Skhira point in the Gabes Gulf. 


According to a statement by the Tunisian Ministry of Defence 
broadcast by Tunisian radio and distributed by the TUNIS 
AFRICAN NEWS AGENCY the fire was put out after it 
caused a halt in oil flow from Ain Aminas in Algeria.
