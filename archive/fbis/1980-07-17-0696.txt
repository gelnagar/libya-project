LIBYA

KUWAITI PAPER: 'SPY NETWORK® UNCOVERED IN PEOPLE®S BUREAU 
LD170848 Kuwait KUNA in Arabic 0719 GMT 17 Jul 80 


[Text] Kuwait--A Kuwaiti paper reports today that Libya has uncovered a spy network working 

for the United States at its diplomatic mission in Washington. In a dispatch from the U.S. 
capital, which it did not attribute to any source, AS-SIYASAH notes that all members of the net- 
work are among employees at the "the Libyan People's Bureau" mission. It says that the two 
leaders of the network are Libyan diplomats, and that the rest are secretaries and employees. 
However, the paper did not name the two diplomats, nor their position. It also gives no dee 
tails as to the number, names or nationality of the other members, 


AS-SIYASAH says that the network was uncovered a few days ago and that it is expected that 
Libya will reveal the details of this network when the two Libyan diplomats return home to 
appear before apeople!s court. The paper does not say for which U.S. Government department the 
network was working. 


The paper adds that the two Libyan diplomats were running the spy network and that last March 
they passed secret information to U.S, authorities which helped them foil the task of four 
Libyan diplomats who were charged with executing opponents of the regime of Libyan leader 
Mu%ammar al-Qadhdhafi in the United States. 


The U.S, Government deported the four Libyans last April although the Libyan Government had 
denied U.S. charges that they were planning to assassinate the opponents of the Libyan Government 
in the United States, particularly among the student ranks. The paper says that the secretaries 
involved in this network photographed certain documents and retyped others. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES MESSAGE FROM FIDEL CASTRO 


LD170856 Tripoli JANA in English 0823 GMT 17 Jul 80 


[Text] Tripoli, July 17, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu*%ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of 
the great First of September Revolution, received a message from Dr. Fidel Castro, chairman of 
the nonealigned movement present session, in which he condemned the (Israeli) terrorist acts 
against the Arab citizens and the arbitrary measures against the Palestinian people. 


Dr. Castro also condemned in the message "Camp David" accords and the U.S. hostile stand towards 
the Arab cause and the Palestinian people. Dr, Castro finally called upon the non-aligned 
countries to pledge their solidarity with the Palestinian people!s struggle.
