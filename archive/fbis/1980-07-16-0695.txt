LIBYA

AT-TURAYKI COMMENTS ON RELATIONS WITH BILLY CARTER 


NC151820 Paris AFP in English 1807 GMT 15 Jul 80 


[Excerpt] Nicosia, July 15 (AFP)--Libya established relations with U.S. President 

Jimmy Carter's brother Billy to maintain contact with the American people and to tell 
the United States that its Middle East policies are "erroneous," Libyan Foreign Minister 
‘Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki said here today. 


V. 16 Jul 80 NORTH AFRICA 


"We are trying to inform the American people of the erroneous politics of their government 
towards the Middle East, and the contact with Billy Carter enters into this context," 
Mr at-Turayki told a news conference. 


Libya "maintains contacts at the level of the people with a good many Americans to 
establish a reciprocal understanding between the two people and to inform the American 
people of the real situation in the Middle East," he said.
