• Qaddafi's home is inside the Bab al-Aziziya barracks, a nearly 20-acre compound; his bunker was reportedly under the central part of the complex.
• Reports from Western diplomats say at least seven assassination attempts against Qaddafi, most by military men, were reported in recent years.
• Around 1,000 to 2,000 'revolutionary guards' were reportedly inserted into the armed forces; about 1,400 tanks and 450 combat planes reportedly remained stored.
• Some 5,000 to 6,000 Soviet and East bloc military advisers were reported in Libya.
• 1985-11: Reported assassination attempt against Qaddafi in which provincial governor Col. Hassan Eshqal was killed in gunfire, per Western diplomats (reported Jan 1986). (1 killed)
• 1986-04-15: US air raid on Qaddafi's compound; his infant adopted daughter Hana was reported killed and two sons wounded. (1 killed)
• 1986-04-15: Bombs fell in residential neighbourhood; at least 18 reported dead and 60 wounded. (18 killed)
