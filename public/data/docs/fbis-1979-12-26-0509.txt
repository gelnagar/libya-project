LIBYA

OFFICIAL DENIES REPORT ON MEDIATION OF RIFT WITH PLO 
LD231456 Tripoli JANA in English 1434 GMT 23 Dec 79 LD 
[Text] Tripoli, Dec 23, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--A spokesman for the Arab Liaison Bureau 
at the General Secretariat of the General Peoples Congress denied the existence of any 
mediation between Libya and the Palestinian resistance, 
In a statement to our correspondent, he said that all news about this mediation is 
groundless. He said that the so-called dispute between Libya and the Palestinian resistance 
is in fact a dispute between the Palestinian masses and Fatah's leadership. 


He affirmed that the Socialist Peoples Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah will be the true supporter 
of the armed Palestinian struggle for the restoration of all usurped rights.

AMBASSADOR TO JORDAN DISCUSSES PALESTINIAN ISSUE 
JN240846 Amman AR-RA'Yin Arabic 24 Dec 79 pp 1, 13 JN 
[By Ahmad Salamah ] 


[Text] Libyan ambassador in Amman Salih as-Sanusi has asserted that serious attempts are 
being exerted to contain the Libyan-Palestinian crisis. 


At a press conference the ambassador held at the Libyan embassy in Amman yesterday evening, 
he said that the Libyan and Palestinian revolutions are in harmony and have identical views. 
He added that the Libyan revolution is committed to supporting and backing the Palestinian 
resistance both morally and materially. The Libyan revolution, he said, will not change its 
stand, which holds the PLO to be the sole legitimate representative of the Palestinian people. 


The Libyan ambassador asserted that recent differences, which have been distorted and 
exploited by hostile media, were merely differences of opinion. The Libyan revolution 
intends to uphold and support the Palestinian struggle. In his reply to journalists! 
questions, the Libyan ambassador said: I assure you that the existing crisis between 
Libya and the Palestinian revolution is only a transient one and will be contained through 
the efforts exerted by the Libyan and Palestinian leadership and the Arab brothers. 


Asked about the Palestinian revolutionary committees proposed by Libya, which constitute a 
direct intervention in the internal affairs of the Palestinian revolution, the Libyan 
ambassador said: The purpose of proposing these committees is to support the Palestinian
