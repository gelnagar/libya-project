Summary (AI): A 1975 wire report from Tripoli (via Libyan Arab News Agency) says Qadhafi sent Zaire's President Mobutu a message denouncing an alleged American plot to assassinate Mobutu, and stressing that Libya considers aggression against Zaire as aggression against Libya. It concerns foreign policy, not Tripoli places or local repression.
• Message was presented by the Libyan ambassador in Kinshasa.
• Qadhafi said Libya considers aggression against Zaire as aggression against itself.
• Message covered the African and world situation and bilateral cooperation.
• 1975-07-27: Libyan news agency reports Qadhafi's reply to Mobutu, denouncing an alleged American plot to assassinate Mobutu and provoke chaos in Zaire.
