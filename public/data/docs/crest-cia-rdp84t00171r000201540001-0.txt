Summary (AI): An August 1983 CIA imagery briefing attachment describing Libyan military units deployed in northern Chad at Faya Largeau, Ounianga Kebir, Gouro, Ogoui and Aozou. It covers Libya's foreign military activity and says nothing about Tripoli itself.
• Imagery showed elements of four mechanized infantry battalions, two probable motorized infantry battalions, two tank companies and three tank platoons in northern Chad.
• Air assets included six Mirage F-1 and 23 Fitter (Su-22) fighter/bombers, 19 SF.260 light-strike aircraft and six Hind attack helicopters.
• Air defence equipment included two SA-6 batteries (not operational), three SA-9 TELs and three ZSU-23/4 SPAAGs.
• Document is dated 25 August 1983 and was prepared by the National Photographic Interpretation Center (NPIC/IEG).
• 1983-08-25: NPIC imagery shows Libyan military forces deployed at five locations in northern Chad.
