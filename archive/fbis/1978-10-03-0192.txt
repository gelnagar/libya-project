LIBYA

JALLUD RECEIVES ERITREAN LIBERATION FRONT DELEGATION 


LDO21844Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 2 Oct 78 LD 


[Text] Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud at noon today at Ash-Sha'b palace received 
brother Ahmad Muhammad Nasir, chairman of the Revolutionary Council of the Eritrean Liber- 
ation Front, and his accompanying delegation. The meeting was attended by the head of the 
Arab Liaison Bureau of the Secretariat of the General People's Congress. 


FOREIGN SECRETARY AT-TURAYKI LEAVES FOR NEW YORK 
LDO11752Y¥ Tripoli JANA in English 1704 GMT 1 Oct 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, October 1, JANA--The foreign secretary, Dr 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, left 
Tripoli this afternoon to New York heading the Jamahiriyah delegation in the 33rd U.N. 
session. He will deliver the Jamahiriyah speech in this session on October 6, 


The foreign secretary will conduct talks and contacts with several foreign ministers of 
sisterly and friendly countries regarding bilateral cooperation and issues of common interest 
and the agenda of the U.N. session. 


The foreign secretary will also head the Jamahiriyah delegation to the extraordinary meeting 
of the non-aligmed countries! foreign ministers which will be held at the U.N. headquarters 
to discuss the "Stable David" accords and the concessions given by the Egyptian president 
which absolutely contradicted the resolutions of non-+aligned countries! foreign ministers 
conference adopted in Belgrade last July. 


ARAB-AMERICAN PEOPLE'S DEBATE TO BE HELD 9-13 OCT 
LDO021522Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 2 Oct 78 LD 


[Excerpts] The Foreign Liaison Office at the General Secretariat of the People's General 
Congress has issued the following press statement on the occasion of the holding of the first 
Arab-American peoples! debate in Tripoli from 9 to 13 October 1978: 


The Great 1 September Revolution, which pursues fixed and unchanging positions toward the 
causes of freedom and peace in the world, believes in the usefulness of dialogs with all 
peoples in the world for building bridges of friendship founded on mutual respect and con- 
structive fruitful cooperation for the good of the peoples. 


In line with the concept of nonalinement and positive neutrality embraced by the Great 1 
September Revolution--the Great 1 September Revolution which is part of the Arab liberation 
movement--the Foreign Liaison Office since late in 1977 has been in touch with certain 
popular organizations in the United States, organizations with impact and influence in U.S. 
society, This was done in coordination and consultation with the popular organizations 

in the Arab homeland with a view to holding an Arab-American dialog at popular level to 
discuss political, cultural, economic and information issues of interest to the Arab and 
American peoples. 


V. 3 Oct 78 NORTH AFRICA 


The preliminary committee of the conference held several meetings to discuss the topics to 
be raised during the dialog. It was agreed that both the Arab and American sides would 
submit a number of theses dealing with U.S. policy in the Middle East and its likely trends; 
the importance of Arab-U.S. economic cooperation; scientific, cultural and intellectual 
exchange; the role of the information media in the field of Arab-U.S. relations. 


A number of Arab and American popular organizations will participate in the dialog in 
addition to a number of specialists in the topics. Observers from various popular organ- 
izations in Europe, South America and Asia will attend, and so will many world writers 
and journalists. Delegations to the conference are due to arrive on 5 October. 


In announcing the holding of this popular Arab-American dialog, the liaison office expects 
this first conference to achieve position results serving the interests of both the Arab 
and the American peoples and the causes of peace and liberation based on justice in the
