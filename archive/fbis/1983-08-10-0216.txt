LIBYA

Foreign Secretary Briefs Envoys on U.S. ‘Threats’ 
LD091934 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
1715 GMT 9 Aug 83 


[Text] Secretary of the People’s Committee of the People’s 
Bureau for Foreign Liaison ’Abd al-’Ati al- Ubaydi at noon today 
met the Arab ambassadors accredited to the Jamahiriyah to 
acquaint them with the developments of the situation in the light 
of the American threats to Libya and the dangers that will arise 
from American military exercises being held in certain Arab 
countries. At his meeting with the Arab ambassadors, Al-Ubaydi 
stressed that the American exercises in Egypt, Sudan, Oman, and 
Somalia are aimed at occupying neighboring Arab countries, and 
particularly the Jamahiriyah, which refuses to allow the Arab 
homeland to be opened to any superpower. 


He said that we in the Jamahiriyah are facing a great power with 
which we have no border or security problem other than its 
attempt to make our people yield to the American will and 
compel them to pursue the line of the colonialist policy that it 
pursues. He added that America is preparing aggression against 
Libya and will use the Rapid Deployment Force stationed in 
Egypt, Sudan, and Chad for this purpose. 


Brother al-Ubaydi underlined to the Arab ambassadors the 
contents of the message that the brother leader sent to Arab kings 
and presidents explaining the extent of the plot hatched by 
America aimed at occupying the Arab homeland and the ending 
the Arabs’ existence as a entity. He said that this requires a clear 
stance in view of the fact that the threat to the Jamahiriyah’s 
security is a threat to the security of the other Arab countries. 


Brother al-Ubaydi stressed that keeping silent about what is 
happening in the Arab region is not good logic since the American 
maneuvers affect all Arab states. 


He expressed full appreciation of the stance of the Arab countries 
that which have affirmed their support and that stand by the 
Libyan Arab people, namely North Yemen, South Yemen, Syria, 
and Algeria. He called on the rest of the Arab countries to adopt 
a firm stance against these maneuvers and to take a joint Arab 
action to check them. 


The secretary of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison gave a 
clear picture of the Arab ambassadors about what is happening 
in Chad, the civil war that started 20 years ago, and its present 
accompaniment of the escalation of foreign interventions by 
America and France, which represents a direct threat to the 
security of the Jamahiriyah and the security of Africa. 


He affirmed that the Jamahiriyah continues to support the Chad 
liberation front, Frolinat, so as to assert a historic and cultural

fact, pointing out that Habre, who rebelled against the lawful 
government in Chad under Goukouni Which came to power in 
accordance with the national reconciliation conference in 
Lagos,is now allying himself with America, France, and Zaire,

The brother secretary of the People’s Committee of the People’s 
Bureau for Foreign Liaison said that the allegations about the 
Jamahiriyah’s intervention in the Chad war were used as a 
pretext for the intervention of France and America and their 
agents in Africa. He explained that the American threats, which 
are coupled with the presence of the American Rapid Deploy- 
ment Force and aircraft carriers, and the American military 
concentrations on Libya’s northern and southern borders consti- 
tute a flagrant threat to our security and to the security of the 
whole of Africa, and indeed to the security of Arab region. 


He said that these open threats by America made us request that 
the UN Security Council hold an emergency session. He 
expressed his confidence that a strong Arab stance will be 
adopted at the Security Council to condemn these foreign inter- 
ventions and open threats to commit aggression against the 
Jamahiriyah. 


JANA Lists U.S. Ships, Planes ‘Directed’ at Libya 
LD092215 Tripoli JANA in English 1946 GMT 9 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 30, August 9 JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The intensification of the U.S. military build-up 
continues in regular stages as further warships, frigates, recon- 
naisance planes and jet fighters continue to arrive in preparations 
for America’s aggression against the Libyan people under the 
cover of providing aid for Habre’s forces in Ndjamena and 
protecting its so-called strategic interests in the region. 


The U.S. military arsenal is being deliberately directed at Libya 
as the aircraft carrier “Eisenhower” is stationed two hundred 
miles north west of Benghazi on the the Libyan coast together 
with a nuclear submarine, with seven surveillance ships and a 
guided rocket frigate. 


On Sudan’s western border with Libya there are currently also 
two U.S. planes, AWACS, F-16 and F-15 together with four 
refuelling aircraft and a reconnaisance plane. Furthermore, U.S. 
strategic reconnaisance planes are carrying out continuous mili- 
tary missions on a 24 hour basis over the Libyan, Egyptian, 
Sudanese and Chadian borders. One of these reconnaisance 
planes takes off from an Egyptian airbase and the other from a 
Sudanese air base. A further group of four strategic recon- 
naisance planes carry out a continuous vigil over the Jamahiriyah 
coastline. Those planes are based in Greece, Cyprus and Egypt. 


As far as the airplanes at Egyptian bases these include two 
AWACS reconnaisance planes and six fuel planes, 24 F-16 and 
40 military helicopters. 


An R-C-35 aircraft specialised in reconnaisance is currently 
monitoring movements in Chad from Sudanese territories and a 
satellite station has also been installed in Ndjamena in addition 
to rocket launchers placed on the Sudanese border.
