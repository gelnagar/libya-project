LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RESPONDS TO PRESS ON CURRENT ISSUES 


LD161348 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 16 Mar 81 


[Text] The brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, commander of the Great 1 September 
Revolution, has affirmed that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah is opposed 
to plane hijacking. Replying to a question about the hijacking of the Pakistani plane 

at a press conference he held in Tripoli yesterday and which was attended by a large 
number of world press and news agency corresponsents, he said: We do not know who the 
hijackers were and what the purpose of the hijacking operation was. We also did not know 
whether those coming from Pakistan were actually political prisoners or ordinary people. 
We also did not know the reason for their imprisonment. 


Asked about the difference between revolutionary violence and terrorism, the brother 
commander pointed out that we support revolutionary violence such as the struggle of 
the Palestinians for their cause and other causes similar to that of Palestine. The 
brother commander gave examples of groups practising terrorism, affirming that the 
Jamahiriyah stands against all types of terrorism. 


The brother commander explained the Jamahiriyah's principled stand of support for the 
Palestinian strugglers who are fighting for the sake of their freedom, the cause of their 
people and the liberation of their land, as well as for their just cause. He pointed 

out that we support all the peoples struggling for their freedom and independence because 
we believe in just causes, the causes of the peoples. 


Asked about the situation in El Salvador, the brother commander said: We support the 
struggle of the people of El Salvador against dictatorship and the influence of the 
United States, which supports dictatorship. 


The brother commander pointed out at his press conference that the Jamhiri [of the masses] 
unity between Libya and Chad is in line with the OAU Charter. Those who criticize this 
unity, he said, are wrong. The brother commander also affirmed that the unity of the two 
countries depends on the agreement of the peoples of the two countries and their willpower. 


It is the right of peoples to fight their enemies, he said, and if the Libyan people have 
enemies then it is the right of the Libyan people to fight them and rid themselves ot 
them. He pointed out that imperialism, Zionism, reaction and che enemies of mankind, 
freedom and peace, the enemies of humanity, are ali enemies of the Libyan people. 


The brother commander said that it is the duty of every Arab to fight for the sake of 
the liberation of Palestine, adding: I am personally prepared to do so. 


The brother commander said: We will not amend or change our policy toward the United 
States until the U.S. Goveriment changes its pro-Israeli attitude. He pointed out that all 
the world's peace- and freedom-lcving forces condemn the American stand, which supports 

the Zionist enemy. The brother commander added: The U.S. policy, which is against the 
Arab nation, concerns not only the Jamahiriyah but the entire Arab nation.

[Text] Tripoli, 16 Mar (JANA)--The Great Al-Fatah Revolution's leader Colonel Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi held a press conference yesterday in Tripoli. 


In the conference, he said the Jamahiriyah had no knowledge of the Pekistani airplane 
hijack operation, who commanded the operation and whether the released Pakistani prisoners 
were indeed political detainees or ordinary criminals. 


He added that the Jamahiriyah opposed terror and hijacking ani said there was a difference

their freedom and their cause and what they were therefore performing was revolutionary 
violence, not terror.

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi denied the presence in Libya of East German, Cuban or Soviet technical 
advisers. He added: "We only have Russian and French personnel who have come to train 
Libyans on the use of arms bought from both countries". 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the Jamahiriyah opposes the acquisition of nuclear weapons and 
calls for the destruction of all such weapons world-wide and the peaceful use of nuclear 
energy. 


On the new U.S. administration, he said: "We still do not know President Reagan and hope 
that relations between us will be good during his term of office".
