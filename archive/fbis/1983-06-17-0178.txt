LIBYA

JANA: Al-Qadhdhafi Drops Western Sahara Issue 
LD162012 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 16 Jun 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 Jun (JANA) — After his return from his visit 
to several sister Arab countries in the Arab East, the leader of 
the revolution made a statement in which he stressed the continu- 
ation of the Jamahiriyah’s pan-Arab role in an (?all-out) pan- 
Arab mobilization to repel the Zionist death wagon and to ensure 
effective participation in this pan-Arab action so as to confirm 
the unity of the Arab nation and preserve its existence. 


The statement said: Libya discharged its duty in full with regard 
to Saguia el Hamra and Rio de Oro until they were liberated 
from years of Spanish colonization through the struggle of the 
Polisario and until the issue was placed in the hands of the OAU 
under the leadership of Ethiopia. Now that the OAU is respon- 
sible for tackling this issue, and now that relations between 
Morocco and Algeria have been restored, Libya declares that 
there has not been a Moroccan-Libyan problem or difference; the 
main problem is the pan-Arab problem. Since Libya today plays 
a major role in the general pan-Arab mobilization to confront the 
historic enemy, Libya will continue to rouse the Arab nation and 
marshal its potential so as to destroy the enemy, dropping all 
considerations that are not up to the level of the Arab fate, which 
the Zionist death wagon threatens
