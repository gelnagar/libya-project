Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a June 1985 NHK television interview with Qadhdhafi, recorded in Tripoli, in which he discusses Kozo Okamoto, Israel's nuclear weapons, the Iran-Iraq war and Scud missiles. It contains no specific facts about places or events in Tripoli.
• Interview was conducted in Tripoli and broadcast by NHK on 3 June 1985.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya, as a small country, cannot manufacture nuclear weapons and that it is not Libyan policy to obtain them.
• Qadhdhafi denied supplying Iran with Scud missiles, saying no one has any evidence.
• Qadhdhafi said he accepted Okamoto because he is a hero to Palestine.

((TEXT)) ((KIMURA)) COLONEL AL-QADHDHAFI, THANK YOU VERY MUCH 

FOR JOINING OUR PROGRAM IN SUCH EARLY MORNING OVER THERE AND DURING 

THE TIME OF HOLY RAMADAN. COLONEL, WE BELIEVE YOU ARE A WORLD 

LEADER, BUT WHY COULD THAT WORLD LEADER ACCEPT A TERRORIST LIKE 

KOZO OKAMOTO IN YOUR COUNTRY? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) OKAY, THANK YOU VERY MUCH FOR THIS INTERVIEW AND 

FOR THE WORDS THAT YOU SAID. ANYHOW, I HAVE NO MORE INFORMATION 

ABOUT THIS PERSON, KOZO. 

((KIMURA)) AMERICANS BLAME YOU AS A GUARDIAN OF TERRORISTS. 

I MEAN, ARE YOU A GUARDIAN OF TERRORISTS? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI, LAUGHING)) OF COURSE, NOT. I SUPPORT THE 

FIGHT FOR THE FREEDOM AND JUST CAUSE IN THE WORLD. AND AMERICA, 

OF COURSE, IS AGAINST FREEDOM, AGAINST THE FREEDOM OF ALL THE PEOPLE 

IN THE WORLD BECAUSE AMERICA IS AN IMPERIALIST POWER WHO WANTS TO 

DOMINATE ALL THE WORLD. AND I OPPOSE THIS IMPERIALIST POLICY IN 

THE WORLD AND SUPPORT THE STRUGGLE OF ALL THE FIGHTERS OF THE FREEDOM 

IN THE WORLD. THEREFORE, AMERICA AND ZIONISM CREATED THIS PROPAGANDA 

AGAINST ME BECAUSE I SUPPORT THE PALESTINIAN PEOPLE TO STRUGGLE TO 

RESTORE HIS HOMELAND, PALESTINE, AND TO OPPOSE THE IMPERIALISTS' 

POLICY IN THE WORLD OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA. THEREFORE, 

AMERICA AND ZIONISM -- BOTH OF THEM -- CREATED THIS BLACK PROPAGANDA 

AGAINST ME. I AM AGAINST TERRORISM AT ALL. 

((KIMURA)) WE RECEIVED A REPORT THAT ISRAEL ALREADY POSSESSED 

A NUCLEAR WEAPON. AND WHAT DO YOU THINK THE ARAB COUNTRIES SHOULD DO 

TO COUNTER THIS? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) NOW THE SITUATION IS VERY SERIOUS BECAUSE 

ISRAELIS, OUR ENEMIES, NOW OBTAINED THIS NUCLEAR WEAPON AND IT IS 

AGAINST US, OF COURSE. NOW WE HAVE THE RIGHT TO DO SO. BUT LIBYA, 

A SMALL COUNTRY, CANNOT MANUFACTURE THIS NUCLEAR WEAPON. BUT I 

SPOKE ABOUT AND SPEAK ABOUT ALL THE ARAB COUNTRIES TO DO TOGETHER TO 

FACE THIS SITUATION. AND WE ARE VERY SORRY TO BE OBLIGED TO SPEAK 

ABOUT THIS SERIOUS THING. WE WANT TO AVOID ANY WAR AND TO AVOID ANY 

MANUFACTURING THESE NUCLEAR WEAPONS. 

((KIMURA)) WILL YOU OBTAIN ONE IF IT IS OBTAINABLE? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) IT IS DIFFICULT FOR LIBYA TO OBTAIN NUCLEAR 

WEAPONS, AND IT IS NOT OUR POLICY TO DO SO. WE ARE AGAINST NUCLEAR 

WEAPONS IN THE WORLD AND WE CALL TO DESTROY ALL THE NUCLEAR BOMBS 

IN THE WORLD. BUT TO USE THIS NUCLEAR ENERGY IN (?PEACEFUL) PURPOSE. 

((AS HEARD)) ((JAPANESE SUBTITLES RENDER THIS SENTENCE AS: 

"BUT TO USE THEM AGIANST ISRAEL MAY BE A DIFFERENT STORY." -- FBIS)) 

((KIMURA)) BUT THERE IS ANOTHER WAR GOING ON IN YOUR PART OF THE 

WORLD, AND I UNDERSTAND YOU ARE VISITING IRAQ VERY SOON. BUT ALSO 

YOU HAVE SUPPORTED IRAN UNTIL NOW. HAVE YOU CHANGED YOUR POLICY, 

OR WHICH DO YOU SUPPORT -- IRAN OR IRAQ -- AT THE TIME? 

((AL-QADHDHAFI)) FIRST OF ALL, MY ANALYSIS OF THIS WAR IS: 

MEANINGLESS. IT HAS NO MEANING IN FACT, AND WE ARE VERY, VERY SORRY 

TO SEE THESE TWO NEIGHBOR COUNTRIES INVOLVED IN THIS MEANINGLESS 

WAR. WE DID OUR BEST TO STOP THIS WAR BUT WE FAILED. ANYHOW, 

WE SUPPORT REVOLUTION AND WE CAN'T FIGHT AGAINST REVOLUTION. WE 

BELIEVE THE REVOLUTION IN IRAN HAS LIBERATED THIS AREA FROM THE 

INFLUENCE OF THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA, AND IRAN NOW IS ON 

THE SIDE OF THE ARAB CAUSE AND AGAINST IMPERIALISM AND AGAINST

((KIMURA)) THERE IS A REPORT THAT YOU HAVE SUPPLIED IRAN WITH

((KIMURA)) TO MAKE A LITTLE SUPPELEMENTARY EXPLANATION, IN THE 
PORTION DEALING WITH EX-CONVICT OKAMOTO, I ASKED AL-QADHDHAFI A 
LITTLE PERSISTENTLY WHY HE HAD ACCEPTED OKAMOTO INTO LIBYA. HE 
REPLIED: I ACCEPTED HIM BECAUSE HE IS A HERO TO PALESTINE. 

I THEN ASKED HIM: IN CASE HE COMMITS ANOTHER TERRORIST ACT FOR 
THE CAUSE OF PALESTINE, WILL YOU APPROVE OF IT? IN REPLY, HE SAID: 
IT IS NOT A MATTER FOR ME TO APPROVE OR ANYTHING LIKE THAT. IT IS 
A PALESTINIAN QUESTION.
