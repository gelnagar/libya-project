LIBYA THREATENS USE OF FORCE IF COMPENSATION DEMANDS NOT MET 


Paris BULLETIN QUOTIDIEN DE L'AGENCE JAMAHIRYA-PRESS in French 6 Jul 82 
pp 1-2 


[Article: "Sequels to the Second World War"'] 


[Text] TRIPOLI, 5 July--Today the newspaper AL-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR once more 
recalled the damages and tradgedies caused by mines placed in Libyan 
territory during the Second World War, which the Libyan people suffered 
from. 


The paper contacted citizens who were victims of the after-effects of World 
War II as they were engaging in farming activities. The paper published 


photographs of these atrocities and of the victims of mine explosions. 


The paper wrote on this subject: World War II has been over for a long time, 
but it is not over on Arag Libyan territory. Mines placed by both sides all 
over Libya still constitute a serious threat. Libyans are threatened with 
death if they want to plant something or prospect for natural resources like 
petroleum etc., or build a highway or an airport. 


The popular socialist Libyan Arab Jamahiriya has repeatedly asked countries 
which waged war and placed mines on its territory to provide it with maps 
enabling it to find these mines and destroy them. But unfortunately, these 
countries--who have caused great damage in Libya, which was not part of the 
conflict--have pretended to ignore Libya's request. By acting in this way, 
they are practicing terrorism against the peaceful Libyan people. Is the 
placing of a bomb in an airplane, restaurant or airport a more terrorist 
and hateful act than placing these mines throughout Libyan territory? 


The appeal for the formation of a tribunal to study the complaints of 
victims remains valid, as does the request for compensation for damages 
caused by the colonialists to the Libyan people, who have paid for someone 
else's war. 


If those responsible to not agree to our requests, we will have no choice 
but to force them to, and bring war to their territory or to wherever they 
are. Someday they will have to pay.
