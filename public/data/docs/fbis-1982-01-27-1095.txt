LIBYA

EGYPTIAN MILITARY 'MUTINY' IN ALEXANDRIA 


LD271218 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1200 GMT 27 Jan 82 
[Text] At this very moment, a serious mutiny is taking place at Jiyanklis base at Alexandria. 
Some military units have moved toward the base in an attempt to end the military mutiny 


which is still continuing. No other details have been received. 


SUDAN REGIME BEGINS ‘CAMPAIGN OF ARRESTS' 


LD262332 Tripoli JANA in English 1906 GMT 26 Jan 82 


[Text] Khartoum, 26 Jan (JANA) -- An urgent report from the Sudanese capital has indicated 
that the regime today began a wide scale campaign of arrests among army personnel and former 
members of the political organization. Among the detainees are a number of provincial 
governors and a large number of workers and students. This indicates that the situation in 
Sudan is heading for grave developments. 


A state of paralysis descended on Khartoum and other cities today. The French news agency 
reported that queueing for petrol reached unprecedented scale because of petrol shortages. 


ARAB TRADE ORGANIZATION PROTESTS U.S. POLICIES 


LD261522 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 26 Jan 82 


[Text] The Arab vocational trade union organization today handed a note of protest to 

the Belgian ambassador, as the Belgian Embassy looks after U.S. interests in the Jamahiriyah, 
condemning and denouncing the fascist policy pursued by the U.S. administration against 

the Arab nation. 


The trade union organization saluted the stances of the Great 1 September Revolution in 
resisting the U.S. imperialist conspiracies which aim at harming the efforts of the Arab 
nation. 


The Arab trade union organization also denounced the Zionist enemy's decision to annex the 
Golan Heights, reaffirming that this decision is false and null and void. It also denounced 
the U.S.-Zionist plan in southern Lebanon and the attempts to liquidate the Palestinian 
revolution and the Lebanese National Movement. The organization saluted the stand taken 

by the Syrian Arab people against the imperialist-Zionist conspiracies.
