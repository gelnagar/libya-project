LIBYA

OFFICTAL WELCOMES VISITING SOVIET FRIENDSHIP DELEGATION 


LD261356Y Tripoli JANA in English 0938 GMT 26 Jan 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, January 25, JANA--Head of the Foreign Liaison Bureau of the General 
People's Congress Ahmad ash-Shahati yesterday welcomed the visiting Soviet delegation 
of the Libyan-Soviet Friendship Society. 


He lauded the relations between the two countries as well as the stands of the First 
of September Revolution towards national and international issues. 


The vice president of the Libyan-Soviet Friendship Society, in reply to Ash-Shahati's 
address, praised the role of the First of September Revolution regarding important and 
dangerous international issues and its stands against the colonialist~Zionist plans. 


A member of the society, Omar Hamdi, also spoke about its activities while the Soviet 
ambassador to the Jamahiriyah lauded the solidarity between the Jamahiriyah and Soviet 
people against international neo-colonialism. 


He expressed pleasure at the growing relations between the people of the Soviet Union 
and the Arab nation in general and those of the Jamahiriyah in particular.
