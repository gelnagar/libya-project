LIBYA

AL-HUMAYDI COMMENTS ON 'FAILURE" OF FES SUMMIT 


LD262148 Tripoli JANA in English 1922 GMT 26 Nov 81 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 26 Nov (JANA) -- Major al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi, commander of city 
militarization, today met with the students of the Teachers" Institute barracks in Gasr 
bin Gastrir. The students presented him with written a pledge of loyalty to the revolu- 
tion's leader, stressing their adherence to the Great September Revolution and denouncing 
imperialist provacations. 


He stressed,"The mass society will not be defeated because it represents the revolu- 

tionary school led by the thought of Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great lst September 
Revolution, as exemplified by the etermal humanitarian dicta of the Green Book. These 

dicta heralded the era of the masses to save humanity from oppression, exploitation, 
slavery, wage labour and domination." 


Major al-Humaydi added, "Students represent the conscious and intellectual force which 
could master all forms of weapons. Training on these weapons is now included in the 
syllabus of schools, institutes and universities. The Libyan Arab people is the only 
Arab people which truly practices freedom through people's congresses and committees, 
unions, associations and leagues." 


He went on: "The Libyan people is the only people countering America, leader of world 
terrorism, because it is the people of a deep rooted civilisation and historical 
glories. One of its aims is to realize the rise of the armed people, an event which 
will be the definitive victory over the people’s and freedom's enemies." 


Referring to the region's recent events, he said, "America, leader of international 
terrorism, is leading a campaign against the Great lst September Revolution because 
this revolution represents the living conscience of the Arab nation, its honour, 
steadfastness and spirit of challenge to plots and because it is leading the dynamic 
forces sturggling for liberation from the imperialists, and is always saying no to 
surrender and to summit conferences the decisions of which reach Amercia before the 
capitals of the people concerned. The Fes summit embodies treason because the Arab 
nation's in no need for a conference of their excellencies and majesties expressing the 
desire of America. The Arab nation needs an Arab conference for liberation." 


He added, "The Arab nation needs achievements to lift it from backwardness. The 

Al Fatah Revolution's achievements are an example to be followed. The world probably 
realizes the extent of the giant achievements realized under the Great lst September 
Revolution. Perhaps the latest revolutionary achievement to affirm this is the inaugura- 
tion of the technological university by the revolution’s leader.” 


He went on, "The resolutions of Arab summits have become laughable for the world, instead 
of being a source of respect and fear, because they do not represent the Arab nation's 
conscience. The Fes summit had failed. This failure is a success for the Great Al Fatah 
Revolution's political line and a success for the revolutionary stands declared by 

the revolution's leader in his recent speech at the Arab People's Congress. The failure 
of the summit also affirms that those who attended did not express the Arab people's 
will for liberation and victory. The failure of the Fes summit is a success for the 
sincere revolutionary forces and the Arab cause which will boost its hope in victory. 

The Arab nation is in no need to hear Arab summit resolutions because they are a consol- 
idation of authoritarianism and the continued presence of the imperialist in Arab lands 
as well as boost for divisions, subservience and imperialist circles. The Arab people 
has no need for hypocritical embraces and exchange of jaundiced smiles. The Arab people 
is, in fact, in need for capturing power, wealth and weapons to lead the world in 
accordance with its will and potential."

the society of freedom where the people is the master, the possessor of power, wealth and 
weapons as applied in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah."
