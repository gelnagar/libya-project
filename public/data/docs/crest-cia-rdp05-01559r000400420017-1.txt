Summary (AI): A May 1987 Tripoli TV broadcast records Qadhdhafi chairing a meeting with the General People's Committee and People's Congress secretaries, scolding them for inaction since 7 April and announcing revolutionary courts to enforce laws. He also argues that Libyan industry is failing and outlines a plan for state, collective and partner ownership of factories.
• Qadhdhafi said the General People's Committee had not met since 7 April 1987 and the General People's Committee for Education had not met either.
• Revolutionary courts have been set up in every municipality and municipal section to try those who violate laws, including municipal guards.
• Qadhdhafi said the 'Rule 9' programme aimed to train about 5,000 people under age 40 and move them from the civil service into production sectors.
• Qadhdhafi said Libyan industry was declining, that factories had sold raw materials, and that heavy industries (petrochemical, steel, chemical) should be state-owned with foreign labour.
• Qadhdhafi said the next academic year would not start until a new education system was ready.
• 1987-05-23: Qadhdhafi chairs meeting with General People's Committee, People's Committee on Municipalities secretaries and General People's Congress Secretariat; orders revolutionary courts set up in every municipality to enforce laws
