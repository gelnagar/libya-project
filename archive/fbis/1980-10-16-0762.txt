LIBYA

COMMUNIQUE OF ALL-ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS ON IRAN-IRAQ WAR 
LD121348 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 GMT 12 Oct 80 


{[Text] Tripoli, 12 Oct (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The standing secretariat of the All-Arab 
People's Conference issued a communique after a meeting of the delegates council which 
ended Tuesday in Tripoli. Here is the text of the communique; 


In the context of the imperialist-Zionist-reactionary scheme, the shah of Iran played 

the role assigned him as U.S. gendarme, and established the closest ties with the Zionist 
enemy, so that, when the iranian peoples, under the leadership of Imam Khomeyni, triumphed 
and the Islamic Iranian revolution destroyed one of the strongest entities representing an 
agent reactionary dictatorship in the region, the Arab nation considered this victory 

not only a victory for Iran but a victory for all the peoples of the area. In fact, the 
first decision of the Iranian revolution was to break off diplomatic relations with the 
Zionist enemy and to embrace the Palestinian revolution. It also broke off relations with 
the Sadatist regime. 


Hence, assessment of Arab-Iranian relations must take into consideration the supreme Arab 
interests, and must be put on the map of international Arab relations, and nobody «an 
monopolize such an assessment or unilaterally try to remedy the situation, all the more 
because the Iranian revolution is only 1 and 1/2 years old and can in no way be made 
accountable for the errors of the former Shah regime against the Arab nation, nor can it 
be blamed for the retreat of Arab officials before the shah of Iran. 


At any rate, and with due regard to all the Arab national rights, the settlement of any 
dispute with the Iranian revolution should have come through dialogue and arbitration, 
and in no circumstances can a recourse to war take place at a time when the Arab nation is 
preoccupied with its confrontation with the imperialist-Zionist-reactionary plan, and 
when the Palestinian question is the core of the Arab struggie between the Arab nation 
and its allies--foremost of whom is the Iranian revolution--and the enemies led by 
imperialism and Zionism. 


Furthermore, if we take into consideration the fact that the Arab regimes had retreated 
before the shah of Iran ..en he occupied the Gulf islands in 1971, how can one believe the 
claim that the same regimes are today waging a nationalist liberation war against the Iranian 
revolution? We consider that using the pretext of problems dating back to the shah era and 
official agreements concluded with him, and denounced at that time by the (Arab) nationalist 
progressive forces, does not justify today a recours? to war against the Islamic revolution, 
an ally of the Arab nation. 


Therefore, the standing secretariat of the All-Arab People's Conference condemns the war 
now being waged between Iraq and Iran, and considers that this war serves only the interests 
of imperialism, Zionism and reaction, and that in no way can war be the means of dealing 
with our allies, whatever differences may exist between us. 


At a time when Israel occupies all of Palestine and parts of Egypt, Syria and Lebanon, 

in the midst of an imperialist and international conspiracy and Arab surrender; at a 

time when the Sadatist regime and Arab reaction are covering up this crime, the Arab nation 
badly needs the potential of the Iraqi people, particularly their Arab armed forces, in 
order to wage the war of liberation, dignity and honor in Palestine where the army of the 
Iranian Islamic revolution will be its ally, fighting with it against our common enemies 
imperialism and Zionism. Consequently, the declaration of war (against Iran), and its 
continued destructive effects, serves only the interests of imperialism, Zionism and 
reaction.

There should therefore be--as a matter of urgency--recourse to reason, and all measures 
must be taken to stop bloodshed, heal the rift, and preserve the common interests of the 
peoples of the region. Arab and Islamic leaders must assume their responsibilities and 
intervene to remedy the situation in a way that preserves Arab rights and strengthens 
the Islamic Revolution in Iran and its progressive role. The capabilities of two allied 
peoples, who are the same target of a common enemy, are being destroyed while the world 
watches, because these losses adversely affect Arabs and Muslims alike, and the only 
beneficiary will be imperialism, Zionism and reaction. Can one remsin silent over this 
situation, whatever the considerations may be? 


We call on the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front, the Arab League and the Islamic 
Conference to undertake a courageous and decisive initiative to halt the aggression, heal 
the wounds of war and enable the two fraternal peoples to overcome this trial. And here 
(?arbitrator) is what God said: If two groups of believers come to fight each other, make 
peace between them; but if one of them turns into a tyrant toward the other then fight the 
tyrant until it reverts to the commandments of God. [Koran quotation]
