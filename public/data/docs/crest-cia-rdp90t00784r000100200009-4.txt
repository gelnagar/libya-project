Summary (AI): A 1982 NPIC imagery note reports an Iranian merchant ship moored at a military transshipment quay in Tripoli port and an Iranian 747 cargo aircraft at Tripoli airfield, suggesting continued Libyan military support to Iran. Earlier 1980-1981 activity at these sites was linked to arms shipments to Iran.
• Iranian ship probably the Iran Nehad (Arya Nur class) moored at the military transshipment quay at Tripoli port.
• Iranian 747 heavy-lift cargo aircraft with civil markings observed at Tripoli airfield.
• Similar activity in 1980 and 1981 was associated with major arms shipments to Iran.
• No military cargo loading was discernible at either location in the 1982 imagery.
• 1981-06: Iranian merchant ship Iran Ershad loaded T-54/55 tanks and M-46 130mm field guns
• 1980-10: Iranian civil aircraft observed
