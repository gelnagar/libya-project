A plan approved last month by Presi-

Although a mix of cunning, charisma,

power for 17 years, U.S. intelligence ana- 
lysts believe the Libyan leader's grip now 
is slipping. But U.S. and Western Euro- 
pean diplomats and intelligence analysts 
conceded that despite growing political,

“He's in trouble, but it is too early to
