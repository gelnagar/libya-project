LIBYA

PLO’s Qaddumi Arrives, Praises Al-Qadhdhafi 
LD171713 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 
1600 GMT 17 May 87 


[Text] Brother Faruq Qaddumi, chairman of the PLO’s Political 
Department, arrived in Tripoli this afternoon on a visit to the 
Great Jamahiriyah. 


He was accompanied by Brothers Jawid al-Ghusayn, chairman 
of the Palestinian National Fund Council, and Abu ‘Ali Mustafa 
and Yasir ‘Abd Rabbuh, members of the organization’s Execu- 
tive Committee. 


Brother Qaddumi praised the tremendous efforts made by the 
brother leader of the revolution to unify the Palestinian resistance 
factions. 


In a statement to JANA, he said: The work being done by 
Brother Colonel al-Qadhdhafi has been prominent in backing 
this unifying trend and in achieving this outstanding step toward 
unified Palestinian action and stronger efforts. He added: The 
purpose of this trip is to reinforce relations with our brothers in 
the Great Jamahiriyah, beside whom we stand to face up to 
imperialism and Zionism and their agents in the region.
