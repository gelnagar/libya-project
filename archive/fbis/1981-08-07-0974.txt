LIBYA

OFFICIAL COMMENTS ON CIA ANTI-AL-QADHDHAFI PLOT 


AB061422 Nouakchott Domestic Service in French 1400 GMT 5 Aug 81 


[Text] Mr (Hudayri Faqih), the head of the Libyan Arab Fraternity in Nouakchott has 
strongly condemned the false campaign of aggression the United States launched against 
his country. The diplomat, who organized an interview with the national press yesterday, 
accused Washington of preparing a plan of direct aggression against Libya, in connivance 
with the regime of As-Sadat and the Zionists.

The military facilities which As-Sadat granted to the American Rapid Development Force 
unit based in Egypt, the current Americannaval maneuvers in Libyan territorial waters and 
other preparations going on fall well within the framework of this imperialist conspirarcy 
against our country, he explained. He also stated that as published on 3 July by 
NEWSWEEK magazine, the CIA has a plan aimed at physically eliminating the leader of the 
revolution, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. He added that the American press has also under- 
taken a virulent lying campaign aimed at discrediting the emancipatory and anticolonial 
role of Libya in Africa and in the Middle East [words indistinct]. 


Mr (Hudayri Faqih) stated that his country vigorously protests these sordid maneuvers 
and is looking for the support and understanding of all peace and justice-loving countries 
to foil this Zionist plan. 


This interview also afforded the Libyan diplomat the opportunity to repeat the protest 
made against these (?contradictions) last June by the Libyan leader, Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
who called on the Arab countries to unite to face the Zionist aggression. On this 
occasion Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi had delcared his readiness to forget all misunder- 
standings with the other Arab countries and to work to unite the efforts as well as the 
economic and human potentialities of the Arabs for this purpose. He added that this was 
why he had reestablished relations with (?Egypt), Saudi Arabia and Morocco. 


Mr (Faqih) concluded his discussion by talking about the relations of cooperation between 
Libya and Mauritania. The head of the Bureau of the Libyan Arab Fraternity in Nouakchott 
stated that these relations are excellent and that they will be (?further strengthened). 

We will work relentlessly to increase the pace of our relations, we will diversify and 
amplify our bilateral cooperation which concernsall fields including the economic, cultural 
and social fields, he added.
