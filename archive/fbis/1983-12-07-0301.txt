LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Message From Al-Asad 
LD061412 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arable 1330 GM1 
6 Dec 83 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has received a written 
message from the Syrian president, Hafiz al-Asad, dealing with 
the latest developments in the Arab region following the escala- 
tion of the situation by the Americans and the Zionists 


In his message, President al-Asad pointed to the dangers inherent 
in the American intervention in Lebanon. He noted the risk of it 
developing into direct military aggression against Syria and the 
Arab nation, particularly in the wake of the American-Israeli 
strategic pact, which not only links American interests to those 
of the Zionists, but also places the great American potential at 
their disposal, allowing them to seriously threaten the peace, 
security, and interests of the Arab nation 


The Syrian president affirmed his country’s determination to 
safeguard its independence and defend Arab rights against any 
possible aggression and all Zionist expansionist designs 


The message was conveyed to the brother leader of the revolution 
by Munir Barikhan, head of the Syrian Arab Relations Bureau 
in the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah
