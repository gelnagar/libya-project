[Report on speech by Ma'dmmar al-Qadhdhafi, chairman of the Revolution Command Council, 
on 30 March at a gathering to mark the anniversary of Al-Kardun battle] 

[Text] Brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 1 September revolution, 
speaking to the masses which gathered in (Kilkizan) last night to mark the anniversary 
of Al-Kardun battle, exposed the campaign of lies and fabrications of the agent 
regimes in Tunisia and Egypt. The LAR, the brother colonel said, has so far not 
decided to put an end to these two regimes, U1bya is capable of doing so with a 
single blow through the masses of the Arab people in Egypt and Tunisia without the 
need for taking any other action, But we know that the two regimes are doomed. 

In order to prove the extent of the lies and fabrications made by the ruling regimes 
in Egypt and Tunisia, he declared that the LAR was asking the Arab League secretary 
general and Amnesty International or any other neutral authority to go to Tunisia and 
Egypt and investigate the charges of their governments, Any impartial committee will 
find that the LAR did not send anyone to assassinate any Tunisian or Egyptian and that 
there is no Libysn plan for assassinations, 

The brother colonel then provided proof that what the Tunisian Government had said 
was false and nothing but lies, He outlined the following sequence of events which 
led to the Tunisian Government's false allegations: 

1. The government lied to the ailing old man President Bourguiba as he was preparing 
to receive a French television delegation, telling him that there was a Iibyan-Alge- 
rian plot to assassinate hin, 

2. Bourguiba believed this and told French television of the plot. 

3. later they said that it would be better to exclude Algeria and say instead that 
it was only a Libyan plot. 

“4, At noon on the same day, the Tunisian security director held a press conference 
in which he said the plot was .Libyan and was aimed at assassinating several Tunisian 
political personalities. 

5. On the evening of the same day, Hedi Nouira said there was no plot but that Libya 
had sent a group of people in order to kill a Tunisian political personality and that 
this personality was not President Bourguiba, 

6. On the following day, Nouira said that the political personality concerned could 
even be a non-Tunisian, | , 

The brother colonel said that this Sequence of events goes to prove that the story 
is untrue and full of lies. ‘The brother colonel said that the LAR had good inten- 
tions toward the Tunisian Government, It offered it a chance to participate in the 
Abu Kammash continental shelf project. It also decided to give Tunisia some aid. 
But we were, nevertheless, later surprised by a campaign of lies, 

The brother colonel said: I am sure that President Bourguiba has been deceived and 
misled by his enemies, They have given him false information, It appears that these 
enemies wanted to create a battle with the IAR, or that the United States instructed 
them to do so, They are wrong to think that the United States would support them, 
The United States has abandoned the governments of Southeast Asia in the face of the 
march of these countries! masses,
