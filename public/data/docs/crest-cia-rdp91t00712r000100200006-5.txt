Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery briefing note on Libyan military deployments in Chad (Ounianga Kebir, Faya Largeau) and heavy-lift transport activity at Tajura Barracks in Libya. Most of the document is redacted, and the Tajura activity is the only Libya-side item.
• At Ounianga Kebir, Libyan forces include one mechanized infantry battalion with BMP APCs and one probable tank battalion still deployed around the airfield.
• Six of ten 130mm M-46 field guns have arrived and are deployed north of the Ounianga Kebir airfield.
• At Faya Largeau airfield, an increase in empty and loaded heavy-lift transporters suggests transshipment of mechanized infantry battalion elements.
• One probable ZSU-23/4 self-propelled antiaircraft gun was identified west of Faya Largeau airfield, the first identification of probable antiaircraft guns in that area.
• At least 12 trucks arrived near Salal military garrison, probably due to the presence of French forces.
• 1983-08: High level of heavy-lift transport activity; at least 70 heavy-lift transporters in convoy at the installation entrance, about 30 in the same area earlier.
