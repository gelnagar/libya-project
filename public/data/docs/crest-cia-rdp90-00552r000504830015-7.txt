Summary (AI): A 1981 Los Angeles Times article (as reproduced in a CIA reading-room copy) reports that Libyan agents sought about 30 California bomb experts to train terrorists at a training center in Tripoli; the recruits who refused were warned not to go. Some experts reportedly accepted the offer and supplied explosives to Libya. The OCR text is partly garbled.
• About 30 California area bomb experts were reportedly sought by Libyan agents for a terrorist training center in Tripoli.
• U.S. intelligence sources reportedly warned the recruits that Libya would not let them return if they went.
• Some California bomb experts reportedly accepted the Libyan offer and supplied explosive materials to Libya.
• 1977: A West Coast bomb expert was approached for the Libyan assignment in 1977, according to the article.
