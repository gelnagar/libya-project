LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SET TO VISIT AUSTRIA 10 MAR 


LD090750 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 9 Mar 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 9 Mar (JANA) -- A source at the people's committee for the Foreign 
Liaison Bureau announced in Tripoli last night that all Libyans living abroad, whether 
because of their job specialization or for other reasons, will be given the opportunity 
to meet with the leader of the revolution, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, during his official 
cordial visit to Austria beginning on the tenth of this month. 


Through this meeting, Libyan brothers living abroad will be able to discuss and examine the 
circumstances and problems which have prevented them from returning to the homeland, In 
this way, they will find ways to overcome and to deal with their problems in order to 
facilitate their return to jamahiriyah so that they can participate in their country's 
[word indistinct] development and changes. 


JANA has learned that agreement has been reached with the Austrian authorities to provide 
all necessary arrangements for Libyans living abroad to enter Austria without any 
difficulties so that they will be able to meet the leader and present their problems with 
total frankness and clarity. 


JANA COMMENTS ON SAUDI ARABIA POLICIES 


LD062124 Tripoli JANA in English 1835 GMT 6 Mar 82 


{Text] Tripoli, 6 Mar (JANA) -- The political editor of the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY 
condemned the role led by the Saudi rulers, by order of America, in the Arabian Gulf 
region. 


He said: Saudi Arabia is exercising all forms of pressure on Arabian Gulf countries in 
order to assume a position of total hegemony under which no other voice can be heard... 
Saudi Arabia sees this hegemonial role as a vital part of its strategy of responding to 
American goals and demands in the region. This ugly role overtly creates excuses for U.S. 
intervention and is designed to make others succumb to U.S. strategy. The Saudi rulers 
are fighting to the death for maintaining this hireling role and defending U.S. (?policy). 
They are not interested in defending the interests of the poverty-stricken and oppressed 
Saudi people. Their only worry is their own narrow personal interests, pursuits and 
pleasures. Therefore, the satisfaction of American interests is the core of the policies 
pursued by the U.S.-educated Saudi rulers who speak either for Washington or Tel Aviv. 


These policies are in direct contradiction with Arab goals, including the liberation of 
Palestine and the regaining of occupied Arab lands. The most extreme manifestation of 
this policy is the extortion and overt pressures exerted by the Saudi rulers in a bid to 
prepare the political ground for a capitulationist settlement which the Americans 

will approve of. 


Saudi Arabia is thus attempting to impose hegemony over Arabian Gulf countries so that 

oil cannot be used as a weapon in serving Arab interests. The Saudi rulers are horrified 
at the idea that Arabian Gulf oil might be used as an effective weapon in the Arab nation's 
civilisational struggle with Israel. 


Stemming from its aggressive strategy, especially in the oil-rich Arabian Gulf, the 
United States has been ceaselessly working for letting its regional agents act as 
policemen, ready to deal with any movement that seeks to undermine American interests...

Scorns Production Cut 
LDO71418 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 7 Mar 82 


[Text] A statement issued by the Saudi Royal Court yesterday announced that Saudi Arabia 
has decided to cut its oil production this month, March, to 7.5 million barrels daily. 


The JANA political editor commented on the so-called Saudi Arabian decision: Most certainly, 
Saudi Arabia has lost all credibility with itself and with the others. Lying has become a 
daily behavior and practice aimed at diverting attention from the efforts it exerts to 

serve American stragegy and to bolster the strategic oil stockpiles of the U.S. 
administration. 


This is not the first time Saudi Arabia announces such an illusionary cut. Last September, 
it announced that it had decided to reduce its oil production from 10.5 million barrels 
daily to 9.5 million barrels daily. Later, on 1 Noverber 1981, it alleged that it had 
decided to cut production to 8.5 million barrels per day. 


The new Saudi statement on cutting oil production to 7.5 million barrels per day, the 
editor added, is due to Saudi Arabia's fear of Arab and Islamic reactions. Under a thick 
blanket of lies and allegations, the newly announced cut is intended to absorb these 
reactions. 


He affirmed that Saudi Arabia's oil production should not exceed 2 million barrels a day, 
which is sufficient to meet its needs, particularly as the revenue from the oil is not 
spent on the Saudi people but is distributed among the princes. 


The JANA political editor added that Saudi Arabia's measures, which intended to create 

a world oil glut, was in response to U.S. demands and the interests of Israel and its 
armament. All the Saudi rulers care about, he said, is to appease the United States. 

The Saudi flooding of the oil markets with Saudi oil is meant to achieve two objectives: 

to solve the economic crisis from which the U.S. administration suffers and to increase the 
income of the princes. 


The Saudi rulers care nothing about anything else. They don't care if the Libyan people, 
the Algerian people, the Nigerian people or any other people suffer as a result. This 
is a fact which rises above all argument. All statements to the contrary are only part 
of the policy of lies and fabrications pursued by Saudi Arabia to appease the United 
States and to help extend the arm of Zionism.
