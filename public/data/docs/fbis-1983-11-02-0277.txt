LIBYA

Tripoli Describes March on Fatah Offices 
LD301508 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
IO Oct 83 


[Text] The Palestinian news agency, WAFA, which is under the 
deviating leadership of Fatah, yesterday published, and has been 
quoted by some other news agencies and radios, a report in which 
it claimed that the security machinery in Jamahiriyah adopted 
an alleged measure against the representatives of Fatah move- 
ment in the Jamahiriyah who have been deposed by the the 
Palestinian masses. The agency also claimed that the representa- 
tives of the movement's followers of ‘Arafat, on the way toward 
the offices, were harrassed and have been deported by force. It 
is definite that these claims made by the deviating leadership in 
Fatah headed by ‘Arafat are pure fabrications and plots — which 
the dissenters have been accustomed to created whenever 
blows repeatedly fall on them and they are pushed back in the 
Palestinian arena in the face of an angry revolutionary sweep. 


Our reply to these claims is very clear. We consider them a 
blatant attempt to divert the attention of public opinion from the 
real revolutionary reaction taking place inside the Fatah move- 
ment and a deliberate line to distort the truth and the core of this 
spontaneous revolutionary move carried out by the Palestinian 
masses alone and that, naturally, will cover all Arab arena. Yasir 
‘Arafat and his group will shortly be aware of this from all data 
produced by this raging move. 


Exactly what happened in the Jamahiriyah and which was 
completely distorted by the agent leadership in Fatah is a purely 
Palestinian action. The Palestinian masses on 2! October 1983 
marched on Fatah office in the Jamahiriyah. The members of 
the Palestinian liberation movement Fatah declared that they 
joined the new revolutionary movement under the leadership of 
Abu Musa [Sa‘id Musa Muragha] The security machinery in the 
Jamahiriyah carried out its duty in full in controlling the march 
of the Palestinian masses and provided full and comprehensive 
protection to the members of these office deposed by the Palestin- 
ian masses. Credit is due to this machinery, which protected 
‘Arafat's elements against the anger of the sweeping Palestinian 
masses, which were calling for their heads for their participation 
in the treacherous role. From this appears the total falsehood of 
the claims and lies produced by the deviated leadership of Yasir 
‘Arafat in order to harm the duties and the task of the Libyan 
security machinery, and subsequently to harm the posture of the 
Jamahiriyah, which views the actions of the Palestinian masses 
as purely a Palestinian decision. 


British Exercises in Egypt Show Mubarak Treachery 
LD291747 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
29 Oct 83 


[Station commentary] 


[Excerpts] An angry popular demonstration was held this morn- 
ing in Darnah to denounce the exercises of the British forces on 
Egyptian territory close to the Libyan Arab border. These British 


exercises confirm once again that Husni Mubarak has fully sold 
himself to the Western colonialist powers and has become a 
chain-led slave unfit to be head of a state like Egypt, which has 
become an American- Western colonly over which the American 
Administration has stretched its long arm 


These exercises, like the American ones, are considered an insult 
to the struggle of the Arab people of Egypt and an insolent 
encroachment by the imperialists on the Arab people from ocean 
to the Gulf. They also firmly confirm the extent to which Husni 
Mubarak is prepared to go to accept the notion of aggression 
being planned by imperialists against the Jamahiriyah. He, like 
the traitor As-Sadat, represents a cheaply bought president 
implementing the hostile plots against the Libyan Arab people 
to intimidate and pressure them to halt the revolutionary tide 
which they represent in the world against American imperialism 
and its aggressive plans in the world. 


Husni Mubarak, when he opens Egyptian territory to British 
forces and those like them, especially the American forces, is 
stabbing the Egyptian people in the back and is challenging their 
will in rejecting aggression against their brethren in Libya, who 
backed them up and with whom with their weapons and aircraft 
destroyed the Bar Lev line. The Egyptian people should rebel and 
cleanse their land of the traitors who betrayed their history and 
their national heritage and opened the land of Arab Egypt to the 
hordes of the imperialist invaders who are returning to Egypt to 
gloat over its misfortune after the defeat and eviction in the Suez 
battle, and to take revenge on “Abd an-Nasir’s Egypt which 
rubbed their faces in the mud and dwarfed them before the world. 
The Arab masses must feel the danger sweeping them along as 
a result of the treachery of the agent regimes in Egypt and must 
wipe away the disgrace which stained Arab history.
