CONFRONTATION FRONT STATEMENT ON ZAIRE, ISRAEL 


LD220928 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 22 May 82 


[Text] The Steadfastness and Confrontation Front issued a statement yesterday evening on 
Zaire's decision to resume political relations with the racist Zionist regime. The state- 
ment reads: 


The Steadfastness and Confrontation Front strongly denounces the decision by the ruling 
regime in Zaire to restore its political relations with the racist Zionist enemy -- an 

act hostile to the Arab nation, a retrogradation and an obstacle to the joint Arab-African 
struggle against imperialism, racism and Zionism. This decision is also a violation of the 
African summit conferences’ resolutions on boycotting racist and Zionist regimes as the 
common enemy of the African people and the Arab nation. 


Zaire's departure from the African unanimity through its alliance with racist Zionism, 

the statement adds, is, in fact, an alliance with the sinister racist regimes in South 
Africa and occupied Palestine and a racist act committed against the Arabs who boycott and 
resist the racist regimes in southern Africa and fight side by side with Africans in their 
struggle against racism. 


The statement affirms that the racist regimes whether in South Africa or in occupied 
Palestine organically are bound together and strategically, militarily and materially 
are allied in their efforts to destroy the Arab and African peoples. 


The statement calls on the African peoples and countries to take a firm stand against the 
Zaire regime in alliance with racism and Zionism.

The statement cautions that racist Zionism, allied with the white racist regime in South 
Africa, tas been trying by all means to reinfiltrate Africa after this cancerous tumor 
was removed. 


The statement also calls on the Zairian people to reject the decision by the Zaire regime 
to strike an alliance with the Zionist entity, warning that the next step will be a Zaire 
alliance with the racist regime in Pretoria. The statement calls on the Zairian people to 
topple the regime in their country, which is hostile to their Arab brothers. It affirms 

that the Zaire Government's decision to restore political relations with the Zionist enemy 
will have only one result: to hasten the fall of the ruling regime in Zaire, which has 

disregarded all human values and Africa's desperate fight against imperialism and Zionism. 


The statement adds that the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front finds itself forced to 
disregard the facist Zaire regime and to seek direct alliance with the people of Zaire 
inside and outside the country in order to abort the policy of agency and subservience to 
imperialism and its tools pursued by the ruling regime. 


In conclusion, the statement warns against using the attitude of an agent, weak and defeated 
Arab regime, which has been boycotted by the Arabs and politically isolated from the African 
peoples and the Nonaligned Movement member countries, as a pretext for the resumption of 
political relations with the Zionist regime and for according it recognition. The statement 
points out that Zaire's act:on will not cause Africa and the Arab nation to retreat or 
withdraw from their noble principle and common historical struggle against racism, that 

will continue until the liberation of South Africa, Namibia and occupied Palestine.

LD212346 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 21 May 82 


[Text] The JANA reporter has learned that the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah

result of the decision of the Zaire regime to reestablish its relations with the Zionist 
and racist entity. 


AL-QADHDHAFI SAYS 'LIBERATE' HOLY PLACES 


LD212352 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 21 May 82 
[Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi statement -- no date given —- read by announcer] 


[Text] On the occasion of the anniversary of the prophet's midnight journey to the seven 
heavens, I call on Muslims everywhere to liberate the holy mosque and the Al-Aqsa Mosque -- 
the mosque from which the prophet, may peace be upon him, started on his midnight flight 
and the mosque to which he returned from his journey to heaven. 


I also call for placing holy Mecca and Al-Madinah al-Munawwarah under the Islamic 
administration of all Islamic nations. It is not permissible for the house of God to 
remain under the political control of any one state. The house of God is for all believers. 
Therefore, we must earnestly start action right now to remove the Saudis" political 
domination from the sacred house of God, even if this is to be accomplished by way of 

jihad.

As God willed, the pilgrims must come to the ancient house from all directions and with- 
out the need for permission or license from anyone, whoever he may be. The places which 
lead to the holy house of God should be accessible and unrestricted to all Muslims. 
Also, they should not be under the control of any state or regime. The pilgrims from 
all corners of the Islamic world must come to the house of God without the restrictions 
of passports or entry visas. Anyone who stops them or prevents them from entering 
should be looked upon as one would look upon a highwayman. As for those who have 
rejected God and would keep men from the way of God and from the sacred mosque which we 
have made open to all men: equal is the dweller and the visitor country and we shall 
cause any whose purpose therein is profanity or wrong doing to taste of most grievous 
penalty. [Koranic verse] 


And as the prophet, may peace be upon him, traveled to Al-Madinah al-Munawwarah to Mecca 
and the ancient house to enter it as a triumphant conquerer and to cleanse it of 
idolators and the idols, we must set out from every direction and from every Muslim 
country to conquer Mecca and liberate the ancient house from the desecration of the 
Saudis and then we must set out to liberate Jerusalem and the Al-Aqsa Mosque, whose 
precinctswe did bless. 


CHADIAN DELEGATION ARRIVES FOR CONSULTATIONS 


LD212114 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1850 GMT 21 May 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 21 May (JANA) -- A delegation of senior Chad officials arrived in the 
Socialist People's Libyan Jamahiriyah, carrying a message to the leader of the revolu- 
tion, to hold consultations with the historic leadership of the Great 1 September 
Revolution on new developments in Chad. 


The delegation includes the minister of state for interior and security, El Hadj Mahamat 
Abba Said; the national education minister, Acheikh ibn Oumar; and the first deputy 


commander of the general command of the Chad Army (Rakhis Manani). 


CHADIAN PRESIDENT ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 23 MAY 


LD231304 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 23 May 82 


[Text] Chad President Goukouni Oueddei, head of the Transitional National Union Govern- 
ment in Chad, arrived in Tripoli at noon today. He was welcomed at Tripoli International 
Airport by Brig ‘Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, general commander of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, 
members of the general people's committee and a number of military officers. The 
president is accompanied by a delegation which includes Adoum Togoi, minister of 

defense; Youssef Saleh, director of the Political Bureau at the presidency; and a number 
of government officials. 


President Goukouni made a statement to JANA, in which he expressed appreciation for the 
fraternal welcome accorded him and the accompanying delegation. He also confirmed the 
seriousness of the political and military situation in Chad and that the Government of 
Chad is watching this situation. He said Chad witnessed fierce wars over several years, 
causing many economic problems. Regarding the political situation in Chad, he said 
internal differences among various tendencies prevent the National Union Government from 
controlling its programs and confronting the bad situation. In addition, the military 
situation is cause for concern. 


Jabir-Goukouni Meeting 


LD232018 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 23 May 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 23 May (JANA) -- Brig ‘Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the 
Libyan Armed Forces, this evening met with President Coukouni Oueddei, head of the 
Transitional National Union Government of Chad. 


V. 24 May 82 Q5 NORTH AFRICA 


Present at the meeting were: Chadian Defense Minister Adoum Togoi; Minister of State for 
the Interior and Security Mahamat Abba Said; andMinister of National Education, Youth and 
Sports Acheikh ibn Oumar. 


During the meeting, President Goukouni Oueddei explained to Brig ‘Abdu Bakr Yunis Jabir 

the deteriorating situation in Chad and the languid stand by the African forces, stressing 
that the nonseriousness of these forces is being established every day. He added that the 
African forces remain indifferent and do nothing at all in the face of the escalation of civil 
war and the collapse of security and stability of many Chadian villages and towns.
