REAGAN PRESS CONFERENCE STATEMENTS CRITICIZED 


LD171654 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 17 Jun 81 


[Text] Washington, 17 Jun (JANA)--U.S. President Reagan has alleged that the barbaric 
Zionist raid on the nuclear reactor in Iraq was in self-defense. He said at his press 
conference in the White House yesterday that the Israelis had cause for concern over the 
Iraqi nuclear installations, as he put it. He added that he would have preferred that 
the matter had been left for the United States to exercise its influence with France in 
order to halt the flow of nuclear technology to Iraq. He alleged that the Israelis 

did not constitute a threat to the Arabs. 


The JANA political commentator said that the U.S. President's statement proves to the world 
that the United States could not condemn any action by the Israelis, regardless of what this 
action might be and regardless of its consequences. The statement has also shown that the 
raid was the result of the desire of the United States and the Zionists to destroy Arab 
capabilities and prevent the Arab nation from using its own capabilities and seeking 
development and progress. 


As the brother leader has stressed to the Arab nation on several occasions, we cannot 

under any circumstances distinguish between the United States and the Israelis. He who 
befriends the United States must know that he is a close friend of the Israelis, the enemies 
of the Arab nation. 


JABIR ADDRESSES TROOPS RETURNING FROM CHAD 


LD180200 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 17 Jun 81 


[Text] Another combat unit has returned to the soil of the homeland. This unit is one of 
the Libyan Arab Armed Forces units that carried out the second order of death issued by the 
leader of the revolution to fight alongside the Chadian people to enabie them to win 
freedom and spread security and peace in Chad. 


Brig Gen Abu Bakr Unis Jabir, the commander in chief of the armed forces, welcomed this unit 
at a popular reception held in the (?Surt) District yesterday afternoon after it participated 
in achieving the mission of peace in Chad. This unit was accompanied by delegations of 
Chadian youth and women of Frolihat in expression of the unity of the Libyan and Chadian 
peoples. 


Brig Gen Abu Bakr Unis Jabir, the commander in chief of the armed forces, made a speech 

at the reception in which he praised the heroic role played by the Libyan Arab Armed Forces 
in confronting the imperialist and reactionary conspiracy against the freedom of the Chadian 
people. He said this unit of fighters took part in implementing the order of death in 
response to the appeal of the leader of the revolution to go to Chad. It participated in 
putting an end to this conspiracy, which also was aimed at encircling the Great 1 September 
Revolution. 


Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir affirmed that the role played by our armed forces in Chad and 
their support for the Chadian people in winning their freedom and defeating their enemies 
is a role that has been praised by friends and enemies alike. Truly it is one of the 
responsibilities of the revolution and contributes to achieving its aspirations and aims. 
He said that the task accomplished successfully and gallantly by Liby > combatants in Chad 
embodies the revolutionary determination of all Libyan fighters and affirms their belief in 
the leader, as they responded to his appeal and adhered to the implementation of the slogan 
of the revolutionary force. That the true revolutionary is the revolutionary who takes 
to the battlefield with a combatant will for the sake of defending the revolution and the 
people's achievements.

He added that the Libyan military units in Chad have successfully carried out their 
mission. They confronted all the reactionary and colonialist forces. They bravely faced

Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir affirmed what the leader of the revolution said: That

MILITARIZATION LEADER COMMENTS ON ARMING POPULACE 


LD171553 Beirut AS-SAFIR supplement in Arabic 11 Jun 81 pp 1-2 


[Report on an interview with Maj al-Khuwayldi al-Humaydi, commander of the operation for the 
militarization of Libyan towns, at the headquarters of the militarization of towns in 
Tripoli--date not given] 


[Excerpts] Maj al-Khuwayldi al-Humaydi is one of the free unionist officers who, together 
with Mu'ammaral-Qadhdhafi, staged the 1 September 1969 revolution in the Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah. He is the commander of the operation for the militarization of the towns 
(tajyish al-Mudun) and the person in charge of implementing the "armed people" motto. 

We met Al-Humaydi at the headquarters of the militarization of towns in As-Sayyidi Street 
in Tripoli in order to hear directly from him the aims of the Libyan experiment for the 
militarization of towns and the application of the motto "arms in the hands of the 
people." 


The first quéstion was about the historical principles on which the experiment of the armed 
people in the Jamahiriyah is based and the relationship of this experiment with Libya's 
recent history as well as with the experiments for the militarization of towns and 

the establishment of groups of supporters in other countries. 


Major al-Humaydi emphasized that there was no connection between the Libyan experiment in this 
field and the experiments of other peoples. "Although we tried to benefit from the 
experiments of other peoples, we have not imitated any of them so far. Take the Swiss 
experiment for example--we only hear about it and are not very familiar with it and we 

have not tried to benefit from it."

He added: What we are doing is only the beginning of an integrated study and action. 
Our aim is not to imitate other peoples. Our aim is to put a rifle in the hands of 
every citizen so that the weapon and its serial number will be embossed on the 
citizen's identity card like his blood group and so that every citizen will have a 
military identity. 


Before all else our experiment derives its origin from our history, the history of the 
Libyan struggle. It is part ef this struggle. We have found old documents in which the 
Libyans called for the formation of popular resistance regiments. 


Al-Humaydi went on to say: For this reason, the Revolution Council issued a decision 
in 1972 providing for the establishment of the popular resistance. 


Our aim from this experiment is the nonrecurrence of what happened in 1911 when Italian 
fascist colonialism invaded the Libyan shores. We say that there shall be no colonialism 
in the wake of the 1 September revolution. Inviewof the Jamahiriyah's vast territory, 
the revolution is the most important factor in the light of the presence of many foes 

to the east and west who harbor enmity toward Libya because it speaks the truth. 


The commander of the militarization of the towns said that Libya is the target of the 
Egyptian and Sudanese rulers, Israel and even Europe, and of America, basically. Its 
resources are also a target because present-day wars are economic wars just as they 
were in the past. 


Al-Humaydi went on to say: These resources, the revolution, the small population and 
the vast territorial expanse require us to train all Libyans [in the use of arms }. 

This is also consistent with another military logic--namely, arms development. Wars 
are no longer border wars. Now there exist cross-border missiles, nuclear missiles, 
nuclear submarines and very long-range fighters and bombers. In our case, the regular 
army, even if it became 1 million strong, would be ineffective because modern weapons 
move more quickly and have a longer range than the regular army. 


Al-Humaydi explained: We in the Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah are trying to protect all the 
revolution’s achievements with all weapons, for what is the use of having a factory and 
nothing to defend it with? 


Therefore, the development now taking place in Libya must be accompanied by development 

on the level of defense, just as right goes with might. Might creates right and protects 
it. It is might which creates and protects Arab right. Israel created a falsehood 

and it is protecting this falsehood withits might. It wants to impose its existence and 
its own conditions. If we remain in our present state of deterioration, we might reach 
the point where Israel would dictate its wishes to us as individuals, issue instructions 
to us as to how to behave every day and tell us what to do and what not to do inside 

our own homes, as Brother al-Qadhdhafi said in one of his speeches. 


Referring to recent history, Al-Humaydi pointed out that the Libyan Army was not an army 
in the real sense of the word when the revolution took place. The police and the mobile 
forces had advanced weapons and helicopters. Despite this, we were able to paralyze the 
“Wheelus" base to the west of Tripoli with determination and the scant arms we had. 

We told the Americans that any plane to take off from the base would be subjected to 
fire from our guns and that if they tried to bombard us the consequences would be worse. 
America complied with the warning and we realized from the very first moment that the 
United States was a coward and only cared about its own interests. 


Therefore, Washington, which was unable to confront and tame us in 1969, is now trying 
to avenge itself. The expulsion of the members of the Libyan People's Bureau in 
Washington is only part of this operation of vengeance for what we had done to it and 
to its bases in 1969.

He added: Preparations must be completed for any futuie battles which imrerialism might 
impose on us. We regard the wsult of the recent Arab foreign ministers’ emevgency 
meeting in Tunis on the current situation between Syria and Israel as mere "theatrics." 
All that the Arab League members did was say: "We are with Syria," knowing that war 
could break out in a matter of hours or days. We would have liked the other Arabs to 
follow in our footsteps and demonstrate their solidarity with Syria and the Palestinian 
resistance by taking practical steps even if it means sending units of their republican 
or royal guards to the borders of Palestine. 


He added: “If you want to prevent a war you must prepare for it. By not preparing for 
war, you will be encouraging war. This is a fact which prevails in the relations among 
the big powers." 


Although there is no direct foreign enemy threatening Libyan territory at present, 

the state of relations between Libya and its neighbors makes it incumbent upon the 
revolution command that it remain vigilant and prepared for a war which might be launched 
aginst it by these neighbors, "Egypt included, although this pains us. We, in the 

final analysis, say that it is a challenge. Our dignity is above all other considerations." 


"If only the Arabs were sincere, they would stop talking about the liberation of Palestine. 
What would they liberate Palestine with? By fighting among themselves or with olive ° 
branches and doves of peace?" 


"If we really want to liberate Palestine, it will certainly not be liberated by badges 
of military rank, wars similar to the 1967 and 1973 wars or the Jerusalem Committee, which 
is chaired by a plotter against Jerusalem. 


"The rifle is the only thing that can prevent the Arabs from drowning. Numayri is 

now with As-Sadat. Who will punish As-Sadat for his treason? The Palestinian rifle 
must be pointed at some Arab leaders before it is pointed at Israel. It must be 
directed at the heads of treason. Numayri would not have been able to receive As-Sadat 
had this rifle been pointed at its proper target first." 


Al-Humaydi added: "With the advent of 1990, approximately one-third of the Libyan 
people--the students--will have joined the banks of the armed people. These are the 
people of the future. Since 1980, the gate of every school has been leading to the 

gate of the Military College of Military Formations. Im our country education is 
compulsory. Therefore, every student entering a school will be compelled to receive 
military training. The school is the door to the militarization of the people. We 
expect the Libyan people to turn into fighters within a few years. Schools represent 
the basis for training the Libyans, male and female, in the use of arms. The basic role 
of these students will bethe defense of the country. 


"As far as Israel is concerned," Al-Humaydi says, "we do not believe that the skill and 
efficiency of the Israeli soldier is the effective factor inflicting defeats on the 
Arabs. The question is more psychological than anything else. Israel has been able 

to make the Arabs believe that they are less competent than it is--so much so that 

the Arab soldier has begun to doubt himself and his abilities. We in the Jamahiriyah 
are acting to prepare the soldier in time of peace and create a battle atmosphere for 
him during maneuvers. Our object is to give the Libyan citizen good combat proficiency. 
For this reason we are trying to give him good training and good command so that he 
will be up to the standard of the Israeli soldier in the future." 


Major al-Humaydi adds bitterly: "But there is an Arab problem which is facing us in this 
field. This problem is that this action of ours is being attacked in the interest of 
Israel. There are Arab regimes which join Israel and Washington in accusing us of 
terrorism and of overarming ourselves. They also are attacking our relationship with 
Moscow. This problem goes beyond the Arab area to Africa as well.

In this long interview, a question was asked about fears that the militarization of the 
people in Libya would turn the people into a fascist or a nazi people, as happened in 
Germany and Jtaly between World Wars One and Two. Al-Humaydi said: '"Facism in Italy 
and Germany was the facism of an individual. We here have raised the issue historically 
and the object is not to turn the people into a facist people. The object is to prevent 
the recurrence of what happened in 1911 and also 1977, when As-Sadat moved his forces 

to our border. Thousands of Egyptians died in the 1977 incidents. As-Sadat is now 
advocating another operation. The proof of this is the death of his defense minister 
and 13 other generals about 3 months ago. the generals were in command of all the 
Egyptian corps, We do not believe that they were going on a picnic in a military 
helicopter at the Siwah Oasis, which is close to our border." 


He added: by this we do not mean that we are preparing to fight Egypt. It must be 
recalled first of all that it was the popular congresses which decided on disengagement 
with the Egyptian Army and consequently the withdrawal of our forces from the border. 
However, if the battle is imposed on us by As-Sadat, we shall fight. If Numayri imposes 
a battle on us, we shall also fight. We are primarily preparing to participate in 

the Arab cause. If no battle is imposed on us for the sake of this cause, we shall 
impose it. We shall give the Libyans training in arms and send them in every direction. 
We are now preparing for a battle which Begin, Reagan or France might impose.
