AL-QADHDHAFI OPPOSES IDEA OF FRENCH TROOPS IN LEBANON

Summary (AI): A May 1976 Tripoli radio (Voice of the Arab Homeland) transcript reports Qadhdhafi's press conference in Malta, where he opposed French troops in Lebanon, rejected Maltese military alliances, and pledged Libyan support for Malta's neutrality and economic independence. Nothing here concerns places in Tripoli itself.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya would stand with the Maltese people if Malta moved toward military alliances.
• Qadhdhafi said he was astonished by France's plan to send troops to Lebanon and would consider it military aggression.
• Qadhdhafi proposed a Mediterranean cooperation bloc as an alternative to aggressive blocs.
• Qadhdhafi said the Libyan Arab Republic is free of foreign bases and would help Malta economically to remove foreign bases.
• 1976-05-23: Qadhdhafi held a press conference with local press and foreign journalists in Malta and later met Maltese workers and a Maltese delegation; he opposed French troops in Lebanon and foreign bases in Malta.
