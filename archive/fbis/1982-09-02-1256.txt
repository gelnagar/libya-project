LIBYA

MILITARY PARADE MARKS REVOLUTION ANNIVERSARY 


LDO12308 Tripoli JANA in English 2029 GMT 1 Sep 82 


{Text] Tripoli, Dhulqidah 13, September 1, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Colonel al-Oadhdhafi, 
supreme commander of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, this afternoon arrived at the Al-Khadra 
Square to attend the big military parade now in progress there to mark the 13th anniversary 
of the Great al-Fateh Revolution. Also present is Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, 
commander-in-chief of the armed forces; Brigadier Mustafa Kharrubi, chief of staff of the 
armed forces; and Major al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi, commander of city militarization.

gathering of Libyan Arab people.

Al-Qadhdhafi Speech 
LDO12300 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1848 GMT 1 Sep 


[Head of state Col Mu‘ammar al-Qachdhafi speech on the 1 September Revolution anniversary 
in Martyrs Square in Tripoli -- Live] 


{Excerpits] Brothers: At this historic hour, we stand to salute the Free Unionist fficers 
and their brave soldiers who created the Great al-Fatih and transferred the authority 

from the reactionary dictatorship and the [word indistinct] foreign bases to the hands 

of the people in the interest of the popular masses, who now have the slogan of having 

the authority, the arms and resources in their hands. 


Brothers, its pleases us to greet our guests, friends and brothers from all parts of the 
world, who have come to salute Al-Fatih day with the free people, with the great Libvan 
people. They are headed by the leaders of the Salvadoran revolution, the revolution 
which needs no introduction, the revolution which is destroying the strongholds of 
fascism in El Salvador in Latin America and which is challenging the might of imperialist 
America right at its borders. The people of £1 Salvador, under the leadership of its 
revolutionaries, are challenging the United States and the might of America. The people 
of El Salvador are giving a lesson, brothers, to the timid peoples who have not yet 
managed to rid themselves of the complex of fear of the great powers. The people of 

El Salvador are an example of the peoples’ struggle for freedom, after the struggle of 
the heroic Vietnamese people and after the struggle of the people of Nicaragua.
