Summary (AI): A State Department cable from the Tripoli embassy reports a speech by Muammar Gadhafi to students at a Tripoli college, in which he warned Libyans abroad opposing the revolution and commented on improving relations with Tunisia. The OCR is heavily degraded, so several phrases are uncertain.
• Gadhafi said Libyans currently residing abroad who oppose the revolution must return to the Jamahiriya or be doomed, with a final warning that those who ignore it have only themselves to blame.
• Gadhafi said a new page had turned in Tunisia-Libya relations following the removal of a Tunisian cabinet minister.
• The cable is marked LIMITED OFFICIAL USE and was sent from the Tripoli embassy to the Secretary of State in Washington.
• 1980-04-28: Gadhafi spoke to groups of students at a college in Tripoli, warning Libyans residing abroad who oppose the regime and saying elements of the 'bygone regime' and 'exploitation' must either return to the Jamahiriya or be doomed; he also said the quarrel with Tunisia had ended following the removal of Hedi Nouira's cabinet minister.
