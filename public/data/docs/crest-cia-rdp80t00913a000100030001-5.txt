• The report states that 14 AW radar sites had been identified in Libya by its date, up from three in September 1973; eight of the 11 new sites are within 110 nautical miles of the Egyptian-Libyan border.
• 13 of the Libyan sites were occupied, and no deceptive techniques were observed at Libyan sites.
• The Tarabulus AW Radar Facility (site 114) is one of four occupied TALL KING facilities in Libya.
• Electronics identified include TALL KING, BAR LOCK, SIDE NET, SPOON REST, FLAT FACE, SQUAT EYE, SCORE BOARD and R-400 radars.

AIR WARNING RADAR FACILITIES IN EGYPT, THE SINAI, AND LIBYA 

INTRODUCTION 

1. This report provides detailed information on air warning (AW) radar sites in Egypt, the 
Sinai, and Libya identified on KEYHOLE imagery obtained It updates 
portions of NPIC report and contains two location maps and a table. 

2. Two large-scale maps (Figures | and 2) show the locations of the AW radar sites. The site 
numbers on the maps are keyed to Table | which lists the facilities and gives a general description of and 
the electronics order-of-battle for each site. The five occupied TALL KING facilities in Egypt and the four 
in Libya are indicated on the maps. 

DESCRIPTION 
Egyptian Sites 
3. ( 102 AW radar sites had been identified in Egypt. This was an 
increase of 18 from the 84 sites identified in September 1973. Ten of the sites identified in September 1973 
had been removed by and 28 new sites had been identified. The majority of the new 

Egyptian AW radar sites have been built along the Red Sea coast and the border with Libya. 

4. 58 of the Egyptian sites were occupied. Deceptive techniques 
(dummy equipment and mounds) have been observed at ten of the sites. Nine of these sites (Abu Suweir 
Airfield AW Radar Facility, Al Mansurah AW Radar Facility, Beni Suwayf AW Radar Facility 1, Birma 
Airfield AW Radar Facility, Gebel El Basur AW Radar Facility, Helwan AW Radar Facility, Jabal Abu 
Marwah AW Radar Facility, Luxor Airfield AW Radar Facility 2, and Matruh AW Radar Facility TALL 
KING) contain dummy mounds and equipment interspersed with functional equipment and/or mounds. 
Zafaranah AW Radar Facility, however, is not operational; it consists entirely of dummy equipment. The 
dummy equipment and mounds observed at these sites are indicated on Table |. 

Libyan Sites 

5. The number of AW radar sites identified in Libya increased from three in September 1973 
to 14 by| Eight of the 11 new sites identified since September 1973 are within 110 
nautical miles of the Egyptian-Libyan border. | 13 of the Libyan sites were occupied. 

No deceptive techniques have been observed at the Libyan sites.
