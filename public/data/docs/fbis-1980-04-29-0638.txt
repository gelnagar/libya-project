an attempt to destroy their Islamic Revolution ranging from the freezing of the funds

to a policy of besieging the Iranian people economically and to attempts to isolate the 
Iranian people on the international scene.

Iranian people committed by the United States. The action of U.S. imperialism is con- 
sidered a grave precedent and is a blatant and direct military aggression against the 
Iranian people. It is considered a flagrant challenge to all the peoples of the area 
and to the peace-loving peoples of the world. The United States has violated the UN 
Charter,

2. Transferring the UN headquarters from New York to another country.

Writing in Tel Aviv MA'tARIV on page five, Shmu%el Schnitzer says that even though "a leader 

of a nation should hesitate greatly before he decides to take an action which involves not 
only danger to human lives but might also create a chain of reactions the scope of which cannot

OAU MEETING DISCUSSES ENERGY PROBLEM PROPOSALS 


AB260740 Paris AFP in English 0640 Gmt 26 Apr 80 


[Text] Lagos, April 26 (AFP)--Energy problems and the demands of non-oil-producing 
countries dominated discussions here during the fifth day of the preparatory ministerial 
conference for next week's economic sumnit of the Organization of African Unity (OAU). 


Members of the conference's drafting committee examined the Economic Commission for 
Africa's (ECA) project for providing a solution to energy problems. 


The continent!s non-oil-producing nations have asked Africa's producers--Nigeria, Libya, 
Algeria and Gabon as members of the Organization of Petroleum Exporting Countries and 
Egypt and Angola as individual oil producers--to allow them preferential tariffs and 
payment facilities (such as settlement in local currency rather than foreign exchange). 


However, the oil producers, and more particularly the OPEC members, countered by point- 
ing out that the exporters organization had set up a special fund to help the non-oil 
producers, 


The project, which will be submitted first to a plenary session of the Council of Ministers 
and subsequently to the summit, calls for both long- and short-term plans for the supply 
of hydrocarbons and new efforts to develop hydroelectric power, 


The draft calls for the setting up of an African energy commission under the OAU umbrella 
and the creation of an African fund for energy development, 


On the sidelines of the debates, several countries, including Nigeria, Zaire and Angola, 
have been meeting to conclude plans for the introduction of an African economic community. 


Differences have arisen over the timetable to be followed to set it up; although no one 
has challenged the principle involved in creating a continental common market. Some 
countries are proposing that regional and subregional groupings such as the 16-member 
Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) should be developed further before 
the OAU moves forward to a pan-African economic grouping.

[Excerpts] The first economic summit of the Organization of African Unity, OAU, opens 
in Lagos today. It will last 2 days. Some of the resolutions for the summit conference 
were adopted at the preliminary session of the ministerial meeting, which ended in the 
federal capital yesterday. These include the draft proposal for an economic community. 
Another resolution adopted by this meeting of ministers is the representation of Africa 
in international economic organizations. 


The heads of states and governments are also expected to discuss the report on the 
conflict in Chad, as well as on the efforts being made to resolve it. A Chadian leader, 
Mr Goukouni Oueddei, is expected to attend the summit meeting. 


Liberia will not be represented at the summit. Explaining this at a news briefing, the 
assistant secretary general of the OAU» Mr Peter Onu, said that the presence of a 
Liberian delegation at the summit would create some problems.
