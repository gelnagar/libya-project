LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI MEETS WITH PDRY LEADER 24 NOV 


LD242130 Tripoli JANA in English 1913 GMT 24 Nov 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 24 Nov (JANA) -- The leader of the revolution and ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, 
the secretary general of the Socialist Party Central Committee, president of the Supreme 
People's Council and president of the Ministerial Council in the Yemen Democratic 
Republic, held a meeting this afternoon during which they discussed the American imperial- 
ist terrorist aggressions and the provocative moves towards revolutionary systems. 


The Yemeni leader left the Jamahiriyah (?late) this afternoon ending his short visit to 
the country. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES KURDISH LEADER'S MESSAGE 


LD211504 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 21 Nov 81 


[Text] The leader of the revolution has received a message from Jalal at-Talbani, head 
of the Kurdish National Unity Party. Talban's message expressed the absolute solidarity 
of the party with the people of the Jamahiriyah in their courageous stance against 
colonial and reactionary challenges. At-Talbani expressed the readiness of the party 
and cadres to fight side by side with the people of the Jamahiriyah at any time against 
any imperialist-reactionary aggression aimed at the freedom of the Libyan people.
