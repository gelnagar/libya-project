JALLUD COMPARES LIBYAN, IRANIAN REVOLUTIONS 
LD122134 Tripoli JANA in English 1615 GMT 11 May 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, May 11, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--In an interview with AL-USBU! AS-SIYASI 
yesterday Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud affirmed the Islamic revolution in Iran 
to be literally a revolution of the people. For, this has been achieved by the 35 million 
Iranians from the beginning of the revolutionary movement until the downfall of the 

shah's regime, 


This alone signifies the greatness of this Islamic people's revolution, He said: "In 
the Jamahiriyah, the revolution was staged by an organized military movement, namely 
that of the Pree Unionist Officers--the leadership then developed into a people's revo- 
lution, The two revolutions are, therefore, peoples! revolutions with each one having 
its own point of entry. There are great similarities between the two revolutions." 


Staff-Major Jallud went on to cite these aspects of similarity with the Great First of 
September Revolution and its intellectual characteristic. This is the characteristic 
that has prompted tne confrontation," he said,

"This is missed by some Western writers waiting on the Great First of September Revolution 
and its leader, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi. They have concentrated upon the hard 

blews the revolution has dealt imperialism, such as ending American and British bases 

in the country, leading the revolution of the OPEC countries, awakening the third world, 
i.e. to transform its political independence so as to include social, cultural and 
economic spheres, actual participation in the movement of struggle against imperialism 
supporting the Palestinian resistance which did not possess medium and heavy arms until 
after the Great First of September Revolution, supporting the Ethiopian revolution 

against Haile Selassie, and its stand with the revolutionaries of Chad, Philippines, 
Saghia al Hamra and Rio de Oro," 


Staff Major Jallud commented that in spite of the writers who concentrate on this side 
of the role of the Great First of September Revolution and stop here, there are others 
who have said that this was not really the danger posed by the Libyan revolution. The 
danger they say, lies in the confrontation. 


Staff Major Jallud pointed out that the challenge and threat which the First of September 
Revolution [word indistinct] lie in its role of [word indistinct] Islamic revival. This 
revival is the beginning of a (word indistinct] which is threatening the status quo 

of the world and Western civilization and which heralds a great Eastern civilization, 
"The victory of the Iranian revolution,” he said, “and Khomeyni's return have shown 


the speculations around the First of September Revolution to be correct in that the 
Islamic tide will grow stronger, that (?Western) civilization is bound to fall, that the 
Islamic revolution in Iran is the first victory of Islamic challenge brought about by 
the First of September revolution, and that it is the victory of Islamic civilization 
against the Christian-Jewish alliance fighting for Western civilization," 


Staff Major Jallud went on, "I wish the Arabs had pondered upon Carter's words when 
he visited occupied Palestine and said that (Israel) did not only safeguard Western 
interests in the region but [words indistinct] Western civilization." 


He said that the first (?revolution) to come which the First of September Revolution 
heralded erupted in Iran. He stressed that the two revolutions agree on the importance 
{word indistinct] source of moral commitment and on the role of faith in struggle and 
on the emphasis placed by Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and his revolution on faith 

and the 'jihad' which, Staff Major Jallud added, were not acceptable factors. However, 
faith and the 'jihad' are now being actually practised in Iran. "The cry of 'God is 
Great' the sign of 'Al-Shahada' and the ' jihad' alone can move the Muslim. We have 
therefore called the Palestinian revolutionaries '‘mujahidin' because we believe that 
the word jihad moves the Palestinian who is fighting (Israel) and America. This same 
powerful influence of faith [words indistinct] behind the Islamic revolution in Iran," 


Furthermore, Staff Major Jallud asserted that the two revolutions correspond to each 
other in their faith in the role of the masses ( jamahir). 


"In the Jamahiriyah," he said,""we believe in the capability of the people's masses 

and in the role they are able to play. (?However) Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi is the 
sole revolutionary leader in the world that comes from the proletariat, other leaders 
mostly have bourgeois backgrounds. In Iran, toe, they believe in the masses who have 
sparked off the revolution and brought the downfall of the shah."

He also drew the parallel between the stands of the Jamahiriyah and the Iranian people 
towards the sha. 


Staff Major Jallud said: "When the shah celebrated 2,500 years of Persian imperial 

rule and considered the spread of Islam a period of colonialism which has ended in 

Iran, the heads of all the states, including the Arabs, calling [as received] for the 
long life of the shah. They crowded round the tables and feasted on food brought by 

the shah from Maxim's in Paris. We were the only ones to refuse the invitation, 
cynically ridiculing the shah and those who accepted the invitation and went. We 

were the only one said the revolution is approaching, the shah will inevitably fall. 

We said all this when all the others were reiterating that the shah was the destiny of 
the Iranian people just as Haile Selassig was the destiny of the Ethiopians. We all know 
the logic of history and believe that the will of the people is derived from the will of 
god, which supercedes all other wills, Hence, the meeting point of our two revolutions, 
the faithful in the role of the masses and their capabilities, is central and significant." 


He went on: “The two revolutions agree on the progressive outlook of Islam and on the 
similarity between the Iranian and Jamahiriyah people in their belief that Islam is a 
permanent and overall revolution--political, social and economic. The also believe 

in the people's authority, the state of the faithful people's congresses, the invincible 
armed people and the [word indistinct] land is no one's property and he who produces 

is the one who consumes." 


He added that the [Iranians believe, like the Jamahiriyah people do, that Muslims 
should have their own characteristics, It is no coincidence that the flag of the 
Iranian revolution is green, the colour of paradise, fertility and freedom. 


He pointed out that the concept of workers in Iran is the same as that of producers 
in the Jamahiriyah. 


itaff Major Jallud went on: "The party which the Iranian revolution has set out to

people's rule, 


"When I used to talk about people's authority, people's congresses and committees, the

Muslim people in Iran and not the Muslim Iranian people. This confirms the conformity 
between the two Islamic revolutions. 


"The First of September Revolution is the closest revolution to the one in Iran and to 
its leaders and factions, for the source of the two revolutions is the same: The 
Koran. This was reaffirmed by Ayatollah Khomeyni's message to Colonel Mu‘ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi. Believe that Al-Sunna is what had been performed by the prophet Mohammed

As Iranians are mature people they are not taken in by manoeuvres, They have expressed

Jamahiriyah. 


"The interest Ayatollah Khomeyni has shown in our delegation has not been g_ven to any 
others. We had meetings with him which lasted hours as well as with other leaders of 
the revolution including Ayatollah Montazeri, Ayatollah Taleqani, the leadership of the

Republic Party. On some occasions these meetings lasted until the early hours of the 
morning. ' 


Referring to the Iranian people's interest in the Jamahiriyah and its Great First of 
September Revolution, he said: “Our intellectual meetings were characterized by serious 
discussions and objectivity." 


Staff Major Jallud expressed his great appreciation of the Iranian people's maturity 
especially that of women who are proud of their Islamic personality and who have refused 
to follow Western trends. He said that the Islamic Iranian society protects women ani 
offers them suitable work opportunities. This is exactly what Colonel al-Qadhdhafi is 
emeavouring to achieve. The two revolutions agree on procedural aspects. After the 
outbreak of the First of September Revolution, high salaries were reduced, minimum 

wages were increased more than once, and housing projects were speedily carried out. 

All this is taking place in Iran at present. 


Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud referred to the reactiomry presentation of religion 


in the past which caused some thinkers to consider it "the opium of the people." He 
said: "Marx and Engels judged religion through people's religious practice ami not by

Salvation. But they evaluated religion through the practices of the clergy. We say 
that religion is the way to the salvation of the oppressed. 


In Iran mullahs who supported the shah amd exploited their posts at the cost of the 
people are corsidered the corrupt of the earth. These should be tried before others 
because they--as mullahs--ought to have been the first to lead the revolution and 
confront the shah's dictatorship." 


Staff Major Jallud described the visit of the Jamahiriyah people's delegation to Iran 

as highly successful. He said: "The delegation did not find any difficulties in its
