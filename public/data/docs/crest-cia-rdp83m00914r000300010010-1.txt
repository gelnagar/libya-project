If we decide to move further and undertake economic 
actions like (1) a unilateral boycott of U.S. imports of oil 
from Libya; (2) a broadened ban on exports to Libya, we can 
expect the following concerns to be expressed by the U.S. 
public: 

-- First, fear that the U.S. will pay for these 
actions at the marketplace. In particular, there is likely

domestic consumers hardship. Memories of the long gas lines

others will profit commercially at U.S. expense. This 
will be especially acute if wide publicity is given to the
