LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI HOLDS PRESS CONFERENCE IN BENGHAZI 
LD131904 Tripoli JANA in English 1629 GMT 13 Jan 80 LD 


{(Text] Tripoli, Jan 13, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The revolution's leader Colonel Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi said the people's revolution is the revolution of tomorrow and that the era 
of the Jamahiriyah will be realised through it, after which the people will resume direct 
authority and all forms of dictatorial rule, whether sectarian, party of parliamentary 
systems, are abolished. 


This came in the press conference held by Colonel al-Qadhdhafi in Benghazi which was 
attended by representatives of major Arab and international newspapers. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said strikes and unrest currently witnessed throughout the world 

will soon develop into a workers revolution. This will happen because the workers feel 
they are. oppressed and that a considerable amount of their labour is compromised as they 
only receive wages and do not share in the profits of their production. He said the 

Green Book is the workers! salvation from exploitation and wage slavery. It also points 

the way to peoples oppressed by governments to eradicate all forms of parliamentary rule and 
other modes of indirect representation. 


As to the leader's role after the revolution's separation from authority, Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi said authority had become the monopoly of the masses after the declaration 
of people's authority. 


The role of the masses, together with revolutionary committees, is now that of popular 
agitation against all unjust political, economic and social relations, the entrenchment 
of people's authority and heralding the dawning of the masses! era both domestically 
and abroad. 


On the relationship between the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and "Fatah" 
organisation, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "If any misunderstanding exists between us, it 
is on the part of 'Fatah', so 'Fatah' should dispel this misunderstanding". 
He denied the existence of any mediation efforts between the Jamahiriyah and "Fatah" 
' there is no dispute from the Jamahiriyah's point of view. He said the Great 1 
otexber Revolution itself took the initiative by supporting "Fatah" with arms and money, 
° 2 it in its commando operations and permitting it to use Libya's radio stations and 
tc open an office in the Jamahiriyah. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the Al-Fatah revolution will contime to grant support and aid 
to Palestinians who believe in armed struggle as the only means of liberating their land. 


He said the speech he had delivered to mark the international day for solidarity with 
Palestine was erystal clear in its appeal to the Palestinian revolution not to modify 

its stance so that it may fit the reality of current Arab affairs, and for freedom in 

its movements unhindered by any pressures exerted by Arab regimes so that the Palestinian 
liberation movement may remain truly revolutionary. 


On therelationship between the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the United 
States, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the Jamahiriyah will reconsider its policy vis-a-vis 
the U.S. if America does not dramatically change its attitude towards the Arab world and 
Palestine.

He said the Jamahiriyah has not worked against the American people whose government has 
placed the Jamahiriyah on its black-list. 


Commenting on the forthcoming U.S. Presidential elections, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi: said 
President Carter promised that, if reelected, he will alter American foreign policy 
regarding the Palestine issue. This promise gives Carter a final chance to reconsider 
his policy vis-a-vis the Arabs. 


As to the acceptance of a U.S. charge d'affaires in the Jamahiriyah, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi 
said the Jamahiriyah is not opposed to the idea as long as he is accompanied by an American 
people's bureau in Libya. 


This matter, he said, is in the hands of Americans residing in the Jamahiriyah because 
they could occupy the U.S. embassy and transform it to a people's bureau. 


In response to another question regarding the disruption of economic relations with China, 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed to the decision taken by basic people's congresses and 
reformulated by the General People's Congress severing economic and commercial links with 
China because of its military support including the supply of 90 jet fighters to the 
defeated Egyptian regime. 


He said the only front with which the defeated Egyptian president will use these weapons 
is the Libyan front, because his sole enemy after capitulation to (Israel) is the Arabs. 


On oil, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said oil prices will continue to climb and production decrease 
as long as the prices of industrial goods exported to poorer countries keep on spiralling. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the basic people's congresses have decided on establishing a 
200 million Libyan dinars fund for aid to developing countries in surmounting problems 
resulting from oil price rises. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the Jamahiriyah's relations with Iran are good. He said the 
Al-Fatah revoiution actively agitated for revolution in Iran by forwarding aid and political 
(backing) before its eruption. 


In response to a question regarding the exchange of ambassadors between both countries, 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said such a measure would be a mere formality and that relations 
between the two revolutions have extended far beyond these formalities. 


On the Al-Fatah revolution's unionist efforts, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said people's authority 
will impose itself and in the final analysis achieve Arab unity. 


As to finding a solution for the dispute between "POLISARIO" and Morocco, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi 
said no negotiated settlement will be reached so long as King Hassan of Morocco persists 
in oppressing the Saharan people and annexing them by force to Morocco. 


He said the people of Seguia al-Hamra and Rio de Oro are in a state of revolution and that 
their problem is not that of negotiations. 


In response to a question regarding the occupation by students of some Libyan embassies 
and their transformation into people's bureaus, he said such occupations will continue.

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the process by which some embassies which had already been 
transformed into people's bureaus had taken place lies in the fact that the host 
countries had thousands of Libyan students in them. 


As for his declining to attend the fifth session of the Arab people's congress, Colonel 
aleQadhdhafi said people*s authority is now clearly defined for the Libyan Arab people 
and satisfactorily implemented by them. Colonel al-Qadhdhafi felt that he no longer had 
a role to play in the congress after the revolution!s separation from authority. 


Colonel al#Qadhdhafi, however, made some comments regarding the basic people's congresses. 
He said these congresses were supposed to draw out their own agendas and send them to the 
General People*s Congress for harmonisation with other agendas and reformulation. 


He said this process was not entirely adopted for administrative reasons. 


As to the appointment of some new secretaries to the General People's Committee, Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi said future appointments or dismissals of these secretaries will be made by 
the basic people*s congress, in harmony with people's authority. 


On the inactive participation of women in the Jamahiriyah's political life, Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi said there are no barriers which hold women from practising their rights. 
He said 50 percent of all revolutionary committees are composed of women, especially 
women students, bevause students are actively participating in the revolution more than 
in the exercise of authority. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi ended the press conference by criticizing the Western news media for 
reporting malicious and negative news regarding the Jamahiriyah. 


He said journalists coming to Libya should realistically report what they see in the 
Jamahiriyah and not depending on reports circulated by Zionist sources which aim at 
falsifying the facts and using them in its own interests.
