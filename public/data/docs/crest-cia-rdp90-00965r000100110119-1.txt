Summary (AI): A 1986 Jack Anderson column (Washington Post, 27 April 1986) argues that European governments that criticized the U.S. raid on Libya overlooked Qaddafi's record of backing terrorism and assassinations of dissidents in Italy, Germany, and Greece. It does not concern Tripoli sites or events directly.
• Column says Italy hosted the largest number of Libyan assassination attempts according to an intelligence team's conclusion.
• Column says Italian authorities later made a separate peace with terrorists allowing transit through Italy.
• Column says France refused to let U.S. planes overfly French territory during the 1986 raid.
• Column says Qaddafi's regime funded groups including the Basque Fatherland, the Provisional IRA, and the Baader-Meinhof Gang.
• 1984-07: Two young Libyan dissident students shot in their Athens apartment by assassins, according to a July 1984 cable (2 killed)
• 1984-01: Threatening letters received in West Berlin offices of AP and AFP, signed by 'The Armed Arab Struggle', linked by police to Libyan involvement
• 1981-02: Libyan assassination attack at Rome's Fiumicino Airport wounding a number of people, per intelligence summary
