JANA: AL~QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES MUSIC STUDENTS IN TRIPOLI 

LD132023 

TRIPOLI JANA IN ARABIC 1400 GMT 13 JUN 85 

((TEXT)) TRIPOLI, 13 JUN (JANA) -- THE BROTHER LEADER OF THE 

REVOLUTION MET WITH TEACHERS AND STUDENTS OF THE ART AND MUSIC 

DEPARTMENT OF AL-FATEH UNIVERSITY TEACHING COLLEGE IN TRIPOLI 

THIS MORNING. 

DURING THIS MEETING THE BROTHER LEADER TALKED ABOUT THE ROLE 

WHICH THIS DEPARTMENT SHOULD PLAY PROMOTING THE REVOLUTION OF THE 

ARAB HOMELAND, REALIZING ARAB UNITY, AND CONFRONTING THE CHALLENGES 

AND DESTRUCTIVE OPERATIONS TO WHICH OUR NATION IS EXPOSED BY ZIONISM 

AND WORLD IMPERIALISM HEADED BY THE UNITED STATES. 

THE BROTHER LEADER EXPLAINED THAT THIS DEPARTMENT AND THE 

SUBJECTS IT STUDIES CAN BE A MEANS FOR POSSIBLE AGITATION THROUGH 

MUSIC, PLAYS, OR ART. HE SAID ART AND MUSIC ARE VERY IMPORTANT IN 

THE PROCESS OF AGITATION BECAUSE WE INCITE THE MASSES TO REBEL IN 

THE ARAB HOMELAND, TO DESTROY THE BORDERS, TO REMOVE THE ZIONIST 

ENTITY, TO CONFRONT AMERICA AND TO PRODUCE, TO PROGRESS, AND TO 

OCCUPY ITS RIGHTFUL PLACE ON EARTH AND UNDER THE SUN. 

THE BROTHER LEADER STRESSED THE IMPORTANCE OF DEVELOPING THE 

DEPARTMENT OF ART AND MUSICAL TRAINING TO ENABLE IT TO ACHIEVE THE

DESIRED AIMS, ESPECIALLY BECAUSE THE NATURE OF THE PERIOD WE ARE

THE BROTHER LEADER SAID THAT AT PRESENT WE ARE PASSING THROUGH 
STAGE OF HISTORIC WITHDRAWAL, COLLAPSE, AND VICIOUS CHALLENGES 

AND WE NEED TO RAISE OUR MORALE. THIS REQUIRES DEVOTING ALL 
ARTISTIC AND CREATIVE POTENTIALS IN THE PROCESS OF RAISING 

MORALE AND TO SUCCEED IN BATTLE. 

THE BROTHER LEADER THEN TURNED TO THE CLAMOR RAISED BY WESTERN 
INFORMATION MEDIA ABOUT THE BURNING OF WESTERN MUSICAL INSTRUMENTS. 
HE EXPLAINED THAT THESE INFORMATION MEDIA DID NOTHING WHEN THE 
ZIONISTS BURNED WHOLE VILLAGES IN PALESTINE AND LEBANON AND DID 
NOTHING ABOUT THE ANNIHILATION OF PALESTINIAN CAMPS IN BEIRUT BY 
THE AMAL GANG AND ITS LEADERSHIP AND THOSE COLLABORATING WITH 

THEM. BUT THEY STIRRED AN ENORMOUS CLAMOR ABOUT THE BURNING OF 
THESE WESTERN MUSICAL INSTRUMENTS. 

IN THIS CONTEXT THE BROTHER LEADER POINTED OUT THAT 

IMPERIALISM, ZIONISM, AND THE HOSTILE FORCES OF EVIL BEAR AN OLD 
GRUDGE AGAINST THE ARAB NATION WHICH IS CLINGING TO THE SIGNS 

OF EXPANSION IN THE FACT OF IMPERIALIST AND COLONIALIST DESIGNS 

IN THE WORLD. HE STRESSED THAT LIBYA TROUBLES THEM GREATLY BECAUSE 
IT IS AN INCITING VOICE AND IS ANXIOUS ABOUT THE RESISTANCE AND 
EXPOSES THEIR PLANS AGAINST THE ARAB NATION. 

THE BROTHER LEADER EXPLAINED THAT THE BURNING OF INSTRUMENTS 
INDICATES THAT THERE IS A RISING AMONG THE OPPRESSED PEOPLES AND IT 
SYMBOLIZES THE REJECTION OF WHAT IS IMPOSED ON THESE PEOPLES. HE 
SAID COLONIALISM MUST BE REJECTED IN ALL ITS ASPECTS.
