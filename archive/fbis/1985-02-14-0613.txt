LIBYA

JANA Comments on U.S. Halt in Arms Sales to Arabs 
LD132242 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1845 GMT 13 Feb 85 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 February — The American newspaper, THE 
WASHINGTON POST has reported recently that the U.S. 
Government has decided to halt all sales of new and sophisticated 
weapons to Arab countries and that this decision will remain in 
force for a period of 4-6 weeks at least, on the pretext of what the 
Reagan government has called the completion of studies of 
American strategic and security interests in the region. 


Commenting on this American decision, the JANA political 
editor said that the U.S. Government's decision represents some 
sort of disdain for the mentality of the countries that deal with 
the United States. For the United States invents pretexts to make 
these countries understand that their demands for the purpose of 
American arms cannot be at the expense of the Zionists because 
they are its strategic allies. 


We wonder what effect this embargo of arms sales to the Arabs 
by Washington for such a short limited period of time would have 
on the Zionist enemy. Washington has been supplying this racist 
entity with a constant and permanent flood of arms, while it 
calculates in minutes, hours, and weeks the question of selling 
arms to the Arabs. 


We wonder: Does the imposition of this arms embargo for a 
short period of time constitute, in Washington's opinion, a threat 
to the Zionist enemy or does Washington have other motives? 
[sentence as received] We are completely baffled as to how the 
American mentality could resort to such tricks and obvious 
methods, hoping that we could be convinced by them. Washing- 
ton attaches great importance to the interests of their strategic 
ally and spares nothing in supplying him. 


The Zionist lobby in American official circles has succeeded in 
making the Reagan government voluntarily allocate huge sums 
of money amounting to $1.8 billion for the Zionist entity in the 
form of military aid in 1986. 


Naiurally, the U.S. Government did not think for one moment 
of touching this resolution, while at the same time it disdains the 
Arab mentality. It has thus halted the sale of any new arms to
