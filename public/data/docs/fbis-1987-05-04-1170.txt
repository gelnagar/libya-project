LIBYA

PLO’s Al-Wazir, DFLP’s Hawatimah Arrive 3 May 
LD032115 Tripoli Television Service in Arabic 
2030 GMT 3 May 87 


[Text] Brothers Khalil al-Wazir, or Abu Jihad, member of the 
FatahCentral Committee and deputy commander in chief of the 
Palestinian Revolution Forces, and DFLP Secretary General 
Nayif Hawatimah arrived in Tripoli this afternoon on a visit to 
the Great Jamahiriyah. 


Brother Nayif Hawatimah stressed that his trip to the Great 
Jamahiriyah comes as the Great Jamahiriyah’s efforts, through 
the pan-Arab initiatives of the brother leader of the revolution, 
continue to take on greater authority. This work continued 
through the talks in Tripoli under the auspices of the brother 
leader of the revolution, and through the unifying document that 
those meetings produced. 


In a statement toa JANA correspondent at Tripoli International 
Airport, Brother Nayif Hawatimah emphasized that the Great 
Jamahiriyah’s efforts and the personal work of the brother leader 
played a very large role in the marvelous outcome of the Algiers 
PNC session. Brother Khalil al-Wazir praised the Great Jama- 
hiriyah’s work, as well as that of the brother leader, which 
together brought positive results to the PNC during its latest 
meeting in Algiers.

Speaking to the JANA correspondent, he said the PNC session 
was a gift to our Palestinian people and to our Arab nation, and 
that it is a step of responsibility and commitment toward the

which all Arab resources will be mobilized to face the on the Arab 
nation. 


Hawatimah Previews Talks 
JNO31723 Paris Radio Monte Carlo in Arabic 
1700 GMT 3 May 87 


[Text] DFLP Secretary General Nayif Hawatimah arrived in 
Tripoli today at the invitation of the Libyan command. In a 
statement, he said his talks with Colonel Mu‘ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi will focus on the appropriate ways of adopting a 
unified stand to confront the counterattack that Washington, Tel 
Aviv, Egypt, Morocco, and Jordan have launched to pressure the 
PLO now that it has achieved national unity. 


Statement on Australian Stand on Pacific Issued 
LD031929 Tripoli JANA in English 
1820 GMT 3 May 87 


[Statement of the People’s Committee for the People’s Foreign 
Liaison Bureau on the Australian Stance Towards Libya’s Pres- 
ence in the Pacific” — JANA headline} 


[Text] Tripoli, May 3 Jamahiriyah News Agency — The People’s 
Committee for the People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau held its 
ordinary meeting today, 3 May 87 and discussed items on its 
agenda, amongst them the statements made by the prime minis- 
ter and foreign minister of Australia on the Libyan Arab pres- 
ence in the South Pacific. 


The People’s Committee for the People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau 
would like, on this occasion, to refer to the statements it has 
already issued in this respect and reiterates that the Libyan 
presence is always aimed in this region or others in the world to 
establish relations with various countries and peoples without 
interference in their internal affairs and for fruitful cooperation 
in accordance with the rules and customs of international law for 
the benefit of international peace and security. 


Minutes for Cooperation Document Signed With Malta 
LD022239 Tripoli Television Service in Arabic 
2030 GMT 2 May 87 


[Text] The minutes of a meeting to implement a cooperation 
agreement between the Great Jamahiriyah and the Republic of 
Malta were signed this morning. The minutes were signed on the 
Libyan Arab side by Dr Fathi ibn Shatwan, secretary of the 
General People’s Committee for Industry, and for Malta by that 
country’s minister of industry, Karmenu Vela. 


The minutes contained practical measures for implementing the 
accord concluded between the two countries on the right of 
property, work, residence, and movement of citizens from both 
states in either country. The minutes stipulated that citizens of

both countries have the right to move from one state to the other 
with personal identity cards. The minutes also allows a force of 
Maltese technicians to work in the Great Jamahiriyah. 


In addition, the two sides agreed to set up a number of joint 
projects in the fields of marine fishing and the sponge, tobacco, 
and clothing industries. The two parties noted the significance of 
the volume of bilateral trade. 


The Libyan Arab delegation was received this morning by Mal- 
tese Prime Minister Kermenu Mifsud Bonnici. After the meet- 
ing, the Maltese prime minister made a statement to a 
correspondent from the Great Jamahiriyah radio stressing the 
importance of intensifying the bonds of friendship and cooper- 
ation between the two friendly peoples for their mutual benefit.
