MEMORANDUM FOR: Deputy Director of Central Intelligence

SUBJECT: Suggested Discussion Item on Malta-Libyan Military 

Relations for Your Meeting with SECDEF 

Libya seems to be successfully pressuring Malta to accept a Libyan 
military presence on the island. 

Libya is taking advantage of Malta's desire to expand its economc 
relationship with Libya (which offers it an export market, 
investments in Malta, oi] and the absorption of otherwise unemployed 
Maltese into the Libyan economy) and to avoid alienating an irritable 
and unpredictable close neighbor. 

These considerations led to a Malta-Libyan Friendship and Assistance 
Treaty in 1984, which contained a secret protocol on military 
cooperation. 

Since then Libya has obtained Maltese assent to: 

- The presence of two Libyans at the Valletta Civil Air Control 
center to provide Tripoli information on air activity in the 
Mediterranean. 

- The probable dispatch to Malta of a team to survey possible sites 
for Libyan EW and GCI radars. 

- The presence of an aircraft with the probable similar mission of 
locating the best radar sites. 

- The dispatch last summer of a military training team with as many 
as 70 men to train a Maltese paramilitary group controlled by 
former Prime Minister Dom Mintoff.
