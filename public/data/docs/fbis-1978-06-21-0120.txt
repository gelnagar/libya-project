MILITARY INDUSTRIALIZATION ORGANIZATION ESTABLISHED 
LD201404Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 2 Jun 7£ LD 


{Text ] JANA has learned that a General Authority for Military Industrialization attached to 
the General Command of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces has been set up. The establishment of 
this authority is considered a new addition to the achievements of the Great 1 September 
Resolution--dependence on our own resources in the field of weapons and military equipment 
production and a new [word indistinct] in the sphere of military industrialization. The 

aim of the General Authority for Military Industrialization is to build an advanced military 
industry foundation and to draw up plans to halt a world monopoly of weapons.
