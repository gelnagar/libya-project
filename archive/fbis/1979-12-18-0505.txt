TRANIAN CENTRAL BANK PRESIDENT ARRIVES 16 DEC

[Text] Tripoli, 16 December--The president of the Iranian Central Bank arrived in 
Tripoli this evening on a visit to Libya, The visit is designed to strengthen fraternal 
cooperation relations between the two Muslim peoples. He was welcomed at Tripoli inter- 
national airport by the governor of the Central Bank of Libya and a representative of 
the Protocol Section of the Foreign Secretariat, 
OPENING OF LIBYA-USSR ECONOMIC SESSION REPORTED 


1D171840 Tripoli JANA in English 1635 GMT 17 Dec 79 ID 


[Text] Tripoli, December 17, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The fifth session of the Libyan 
Arab-Soviet Committee for Economic, Commercial, Educational and Technical Cooperation 
commenced today at the Foreign Secretariat in Tripoli.

Foreign secretary of the General People's Committee for Electricity, engineer Jumla 
al-Arbash, delivered an opening speech in which he welcomed the members of the Soviet 
delegation and pointed to the achievements of continued bilateral cooperation in the 
various fields, The present session, he said, will undoubtedly play a major role in 
developing the existing cooperation, 


The vice chairman of the Soviet State Committee for Foreign Economic Relations and head 
of the Soviet delegation praised the achievements attained by the Great September Revolu- 
tion, He cited the fruitful and continued cooperation between the two countries to be 
indicative of the good relations that tie their respective peoples, 


He said, "The desire by both sides to develop this cooperation will have a great effect 
in finding new ways for strengthening and developing it," 


Several subcommittees were formed to study and research this bilateral cooperation,
