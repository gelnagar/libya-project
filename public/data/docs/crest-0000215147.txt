Summary (AI): A 1977 CIA assessment of the Egyptian-Libyan border standoff after the July 1977 fighting. It covers stalled disengagement talks, Egyptian military buildup near the border, and Libyan forces in the eastern border area. Nothing here concerns places in or near Tripoli.
• Egyptian plans reportedly included a ground invasion aimed at capturing Tobruk and limited special forces strikes against Tripoli and Benghazi, to spark an internal uprising against Qadhafi.
• Libyan forces in the eastern border area were listed with combat troops, tanks, and SA-2/3 and SA-6 battalions.
• Egyptian forces in the Western Military District included SA-6 battalions and about 280-365 tanks in the table.
• 1977-07-23: Fighting between Egypt and Libya ended; Egyptians subsequently moved an armored division and all SA-6 battalions from the Suez Canal area to the western command.
