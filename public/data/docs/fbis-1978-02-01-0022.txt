JANA EDITOR CRITICIZES U,S, STAND ON TERRORISM 


LD010322Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GM? 31 Jan 78 LD 


(Text ] Cyrus Vance made a statement declaring that the Jamahiriyah, Iraq and the PDRY 
are on a list of countries which the United States intends to take measures against because 
these countries are helping Palestinians whom he described as terrorists. 


JANA'S political edito:, commenting on this repeated American threat which encompasses all 
aspects of international terrorism, writes: 


We give assistance to the Palestinians because they are struggling to regain their usurped 
land and their lost rights. America itself contributed to their loss and continues to help 
the usurper, 


The political editor of the agency goes on to say: While it cries through statements by 
her officials on various levels to describe the seekers of f.ceedom as terrorists, America 
wants to forget Or pretend to forget and wants to let the world forget its terrorist 
practices in all parts of the earth and within the United States itself. 


The political editor of the agency has stressed that the world will not forget and history 
will recall that the United States is a leader in terrorism whether by her aggressive 
practices in Southeast Asia and the tragedies inflicted on the small peaceful peoples, 

and her practices against the peoples of Latin America and the interference of her 
intelligence services in the internal affairs of these countries and the control of her 
monepolist companies over the natural resources [words indistinct]. The world shall net 
forget those who were killed in all parts of the world at the instigation of the terrorist 
American authorities themselves and its intelligence services. Also no one will forget 
the conditions of the poor, the Negroes and the minorities in America herself and their 
continued repression. The true aspects of Americar terrorism continue to appear in 

clear pictures everywhere. 


Possibly the most brilliant example of this is the presence of the American 6th Fleet in 
the Mediterranean Sea and its other fleets in all areas. Possibly the series of scandals 
which exposed the interference of the American intelligence and its control of the various 
information media through agents and bigging devices show the control they have over the 
American people themselves. Finally, most of those who are being killed daily in all 
parts of the world are being killed with American weapons of destruction. Then of what 
terrorism do the masters of terrorism speak?
