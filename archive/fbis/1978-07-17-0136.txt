LIBYA

JALLUD LEAVES FOR TALKS WITH INDIA'S DESAI 
LD170022Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 16 Jul 78 LD 
[Text] Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud left for India this evening for an official 
visit of several days. He will have talks with Indian Prime Minister Morarji Desai and 
other Indian officials on relations between the Jamahiriyah and India and how to consolidate 
them. He is accompanied by the secretaries of planning, electricity and housing. 
He was seen off by Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir and by the Indian charge d'affaires in Tripoli.
