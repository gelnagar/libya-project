LIBYA

SENEGALESE PRESIDENT MEETS WITH AT-TURAYKI 


LD172134Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1700 GMT 17 Jul 78 ID 


{Text] Khartoum--Senegalese President Leopold Sedar Senghor this afternoon received 
‘Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, foreign secretary, who is leading the Jamahiriyah delega- 
tion to the meetings of African foreign ministers, During the course of the meeting, 
bilateral relations, the situation in Africa and the agenda of the 15th African summit 
were discussed,
