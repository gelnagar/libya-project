Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery briefing attachment (November 1984) reports Libyan forces reoccupying at least five areas in Chad, including Faya Largeau, Ouniananga Kebir, Gouro, Ogoui and Fada airfields. It describes armored, mechanized infantry and air units deployed there. None of these places is in or near Tripoli.
• Attachment to NPIC/PEG memo Z-11971/84, dated November 1984.
• Faya Largeau airfield: at least 80 trucks, four Hind attack helicopters and one Hip reported.
• Ogoui airfield: at least 12 heavy-lift transporters and 40 trucks arrived; two tanks were the only equipment observed there.
• No French forces were observed at Salal, Moussoro, Mao or Ati.
• 1984-11: Libyan forces reoccupied at least five areas in Chad, including Faya Largeau, Ouniananga Kebir, Gouro, Ogoui and Fada.
