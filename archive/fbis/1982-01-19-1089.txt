[Text] Tripoli, 18 Jan (JANA) -- The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 
issued the final statement at the end of its first meeting of 1982 yesterday. 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress held its first meeting of 1982 
in Tripoli between 14-17 January to review: 


The current Arab and international political situation; 
Organizational affairs; 
Evaluating the previous period of 1981; 
Developing the Permanent Secretariat; 
Reviewing the secretariat's 1982 budget; 
-- To study the implementation of the secretariat's 1982 activities. 


The following documents were also issued at the end of the meeting: 


A political statement 
Organizational measures 
- A final statement 


The statement reads as follows: 


Firstly, the escalation of the imperialist-Zionist-reactionary challenges to the Arab 
masses were characterised by the following: 


1 -- The intensification of the U.S. imperialist military presence in the Arab world, 
which is aimed at surrounding the Arab masses and supporting the local reactionary 
regimes especially after the execution of traitor As-Sadat. 


2 -- The continuous reactionary oppressive measures against the masses uprising, such 
as the uprising in Sudan against the fascist reactionary regime there. 


3 -- The escalation of the Zionist [words indistinct] the Palestinian revolution and 
the oppression against Palestinian masses inside the occupied territories. 


4 -- The strengthening of the strategic alliance between the U.S.A. and the Zionist 
entity against the Arab nation which led to the annexation of the occupied Golan Heights, 
a move that was considered a challenge to world conventions. 


5 -- The escalation of the U.S. threats and provocations against the Libyan people and 
their leadership. Such threats have come to the point of imposing economic sanctions 
against the Jamahiriyah in an attempt to paralyse her from pursuing her revolutionary 
efforts and support for freedom causes in the Arab world, Africa, Asia and Latin America. 


Secondly, as a counter action to the imperialist-Zionist-reactionary challenges, the 
Permanent Secretariat issued the following resolution, stressing the need for: 


1 -- Consolidation of armed struggle and opening the door widely for Arab masses’ 
capabilities for the national cause. 


2 -- Exposing the reactionary government pacts and suspicious links with U.S.

U.S.A. and dealing a blow to U.S. interests in the area. This can be done by: 


-- Urging the Arab masses to boycott U.S. products. 
-- Urging them to boycott the U.S. air and maritime means of transport. 


-- Mobilising the Arab masses to urge their governments to impose strict official political

[Words indistinct] urging the Arab masses to intensify these efforts in order to foil the

5 -- To support the uprisings of the Palestinian Arab people in occupied Palestine and 
consolidate the steadfastness of the Syrian masses inthe Golan Heights, because these 
represent popular opposition to the Zionist enemy's presence and all schemes aimed to win 
recognition for it. 


6 -- To support the Sudanese people's uprising which should be bolstered by political and 

mass media action to bring the downfall of the hireling Numayri regime. This calls for the 
speedy implementation of the Permanent Secretariat resolution to try Numayri before an Arab 
people's court for treason against the Arab nation and for acting as an agent of international 
imperialism. 


7 -- To support the armed people's uprisings in Somalia and Oman and intensify political and 
media nobilization to achieve further solidarity amongst the people which wouid be capable 
of countering Arab reaction and fascism and international imperialism. 


8 -- To back Syria's steadfastness under its militant leadership in countering reactionary 
Zionist and imperialist conspiracies, especially as Syria is considered to be the forefront 
[word indistinct] base confronting the Zionist enemy. 


-- To bolster Syria's alliance with the Palestinian revolution and the Lebanese National 
Movement. 


9 -- To consolidate the Palestinian revolution and its armed resistance under the leadership 
of the PLO and intensify its confrontation with the Zionist enemy. 


10 -- To support the Lebanese Arab people and their national movement in preserving Lebanon's 
unity and Arab character and thus achieve solidarity with the Palestinian revolution's 
struggle and Syria, and resist the Zionist enemy's attempts to isolate south Lebanon and 

turn it into a Zionist entity. 


11 -- To promote solidarity with the Libyan Arab people to confront U.S. imperialist 
conspiracy against them. 


-- To expose the reactionary U.S. alliance aimed at isolating the Jamahiriyah economically, 
and U.S. provocations against the Great al-Fateh Revolution and its revolutionary leadership. 


12 -- To speed up the merger between Syria and the Jamahiriyah into a united jamahiri (of the 
masses), democratic and progressive state. 


-- To speed up the implementation of Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi's recommendation at the 
final session of the G.P.C. regarding the setting up of a union including Syria, the 
Jamahiriyah and Algeria which would be a preliminary step towards a comprehensive unity 

to counter imperialism, Zionism and reaction.

SOUTHERN REGION OFFICIAL EXPLAINS SUMMONING 


JN141107 Khartoum, SUNA in Arabic 1235 GMT 14 Jan 82 


[Text] Juba, 14 Jan (SUNA) -- Staff Maj Gen Qasam Allah ‘Abdallah Rasas, chief of the 
transitional higher executive council of the southern region, told the southern poli- 
ticians summoned to Khartoum, who returned to Juba, that their summoning had no rela- 
tionship whatsoever with their opinions on whether to divide the southern region. 
Rasas said they were summoned because of the suspicion with which the organization 
they formed was regarded since this organization contravenes legitimacy in the state. 


Rasas asserted this during his meeting with these politicians the day before yesterday.
