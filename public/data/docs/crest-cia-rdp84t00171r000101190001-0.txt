Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC imagery analysis describes a probable dissident support camp at Matan as Sarra Military Camp No. 2 in southeastern Libya, about 60 nautical miles north of the Libya-Chad border, near an airfield. The camp held rocket launchers, trucks, vehicles and tents, and is judged a likely staging base for forces entering Chad. This is far from Tripoli.
• Camp is about 60 nautical miles north of the Libya-Chad border, at coordinates 21-38-38N 021-50-07E.
• Camp is 2 nautical miles south of Matan as Sarra airfield.
• Camp has been occupied since at least the date of imagery and held the first identified BM-11/21 MRLs in the Matan as Sarra area.
• Analysts judged the camp and airfield likely served as a staging base for forces entering Chad.
• 1983-07-15: Imagery identified a probable dissident support camp with at least four probable BM-11/21 multiple rocket launchers, 20 large cargo trucks, 25 smaller vehicles and 27 tents.
