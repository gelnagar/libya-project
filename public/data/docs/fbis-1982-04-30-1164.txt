LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES TUNISIAN FOREIGN MINISTER 


LD291946 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 29 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 29 Apr (JANA) -- The brother leader of the revolution today received the 
minister of foreign affairs of the sisterly Tunisian Republic, Beji Caid es-Sebsi. 


USSR AMBASSADOR DELIVERS BREZHNEV MESSAGE 

LD291904 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1832 GMT 29 Apr 82 

[Text] Tripoli, 29 Apr (JANA) -- The brother leader of the revolution today received 
the Soviet ambassador to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamihiriyah, who delivered 
a written message from Leonid Brezhnev, general secretary of the CPSU Central Committee.
