LIBYA

AT-TURAYKI COMMENTS ON CHAD DURING VISIT TO BENIN 


LD132010 Tripoli JANA in English 1841 GMT 13 Jan 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 Jan (JANA)--Dr. Ali at-Turayki, the foreign liaison secretary, who 
visited Benin yesterday, stated that his visit to the Republic of Benin falls within the 
framework of the consultations taking place between the progressive African states. 


In a press conference he gave at Cotonou airport, Dr. at-Turayki said that the visit was 
also to co-ordinate the positions of these states regarding the imperialist campaigns in 
Africa headed by France with the help of some of the reactionary and puppet regimes. 

He also mentioned the way imperialism in Africa plundered its riches and natural resources 
and humiliated its peoples stressing that the imperialist prosperity was built at the 
expense of the poor peoples of this continent who were deprived of their daily bread. 


He also denounced the attempts made by the imperialist countries, especially France, to 
bring back the African Continent under the power and influence of imperialism, emphasising 
the total opposition of the African peoples to these attempts and that was what the Chadian 
people expressed when they forced French imperialists out of their country once and for 

all after a struggle that lasted more than twenty years.

Dr. at-Turayki added "Chad has forever left the circle of those who strangled the 
Chadian people's freedom and imposed their domination on them for many years". He 
asked: "Where are these people who are ostensibly shedding tears for the Chadian 
people, as far as the presence of the imperialist military bases in more than one 
place in the African Continent is concerned?" 


Ending his statement, he warmly appealed to the progressive states to participate 
energetically in rebuilding Chad, which was destroyed by colonialist, imperialist and 
reactionary conspiracy.
