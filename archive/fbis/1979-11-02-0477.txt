LIBYA

DEPUTY ADDRESSES UN COMMITTEE ON U.S, POLICY, DISARMAMENT 
LD011230 Tripoli JANA in English 0945 GMT 1 Nov 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Nov 1 , JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The United States is still practising 
an aggressive policy against the Arab nation, particularly against the Palestinan people, 
through its military, human, economic and moral support to the Zionism it fosters, The 
Jamahiriyah's deputy representative at the United Nations Mr Udu Aburuwein said, Ina 
statement before the first committee he affirmed that the U.S. weapons are being used 

to plunder Palestinian lands, to firmly establish occupation and destroy the villages of 
south Lebanon, “America, he continued, practises international terrorism through its 
threats of intervention within the territorial waters of other states and by sending its 
marine and airforce into the territorial limits of pacifist country, [quotation marks 

as received] He pointed out that the U.S, still menaces oil-producing countries to 
occupy Oil wells and impose the prices it wants while at the same time refusing to con- 
sider the price of it own exports or that of the wheat it uses as a means of pressure 
and terrorism against poor countries, He stressed that the American treat of inter. 
vention against the sovereignty of some states is a breach of the U.N, charter which 
calls for the respect of the basic rights of the peoples to choose the political, social 
and economic system they want, 


He mentioned that the Western media talk too much about the energy crisis, they try 

to put the blame for it on the oll-producing countries against whom they try to set 

the world public opinion, This, he said, is also a violation of the U.N, charter which 
confirms the right of the peoples to use their resources in accordance with thelr own 
interest, 


Furthermore,he underlined that military bases on the soil of other states are a source 
of fear and a threat to countries of the area as well as to the states where the bases 
are, These are also a means of pressure used to orientate the policies of the states 
concerned, and this too is considered as an interference in internal affairs and a menace 
to international peace and security, He emphasized that the elimination of military 
bases and the evacuation of the existing ones is likely to strengthen security and 
restore confidence, 


He called upon the member states to recognize the legitimacy of the struggling peoples 
still under colonial domination and racist regimes and to back up the national libera- 
tion movements, 


He warned that unless the prerogatives given to the permanent members of the Security 
Council--<for example the right of veto--are used correctly, disarmament is likely not 
to be achieved, He went on to say that among other dangerous factors which hamper 
disarmament are the threat to use force against other states, the interference in 
their internal affairs and the fact that international relations are not based on 
justice and democracy, as well as the division of the world into alliances and the 
existence of distrust between the nations, All this, he stated, does not speed up 
the process of disarmament, 


Regarding the tenth extraordinary session of the General Assembly on disarmament, 

he said that the discussions of its final document to press ahead with disarmament 
must be implemented, He pointed out that this is @ joint responsibility, the big 
part of which is borne by the states with nuclear weapons and that these states shall 
have to go in for more negotiations over disarmaments,

He advised that priority be given to nuclear arms and pledged the Jamahiriyah's support 
for the creation of areas free from nuclear weapons in Europe, Africa, Asia.and the 
Middle East, 


He insisted that the Mediterranean must be a lake of peace without foreign fleets and 
bases," Nuclear weapons capacity for the Zionists, he affirmed, is no longer a secret, 
Not only does this threaten the Palestinian people but the international community as 
awhole, "It jeopardizes the world peace," he warned, [quotation marks as received] 


He said that evidence and numerous secret reports revealed the existence of Zionist 
nuclear armament which the world must heed and take strong action against, The scandal 
of the robbery of uranium, hc continued, can no longer behidden and it is high time 

the U.N, was supplied with all the inquiries about these facts and their discovery so 
that we know the truth about them and find out a way to prevent such actions to be 
repeated, 


At the end of his address the Jamahiriyah's deputy representative at the United Nations 
said that the declaration put forward by Czechoslovakia regarding international coopera. 
tion for disarmament deserves consideration and should be backed "if we are to engage 

on the right path towards disarmament," 


He rounded his communication saying that the world is in need for a better guidance 
to develop its national resources and use them for social and economic prosperity,
