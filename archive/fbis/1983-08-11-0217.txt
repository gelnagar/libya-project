LIBYA

GUNT Spokesman Reports Recapture of Faya-Largeau 
LD101432 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
10 Aug 83 


[Text] Abderahman Moussa, representative of the legitimate 
Chad Union Government to France, has announced that Faya- 
Largeau fell this morning to the forces of the Chad popular 
revolution and is under their control. In an urgent dispatch 
midday today, AFP reported the confirmation by Abderahman 
Moussa of the caputure of Faya-Largeau by the forces of the 
popular revolution. He said: We are in fuil control of Faya- 
Largeau, and the results of the battles against the mutinous 
forces of Hissein Habre will be announced later. 


Tripoli: Goukouni Oueddei Led Attack on Faya-Largeau 
LD101654 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 
10 Aug 83 


[Excerpt] Abderahman Moussa, representative of the legitimate 
Chad Union Government in France, has announced that the twon 
of Faya-Largeau fell this morning to the forces of the Chad 
popular revolution and is under their control. Abderahman

Oueddei led the attack on Faya-Largeau and that the forces of 
the Chadian popular revolution chased and wiped out the rem- 
nants of the rebel Habre forces. 


Tripoli Radio on Bright Star Exercises 
LD101445 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
10 Aug 83 


[Text] The American Rapid Deployment Forces today start huge 
month-long provocative exericses in the territories of Arab coun- 
tries, Egypt, Sudan, Somalia, and Oman. Thousands of Ameri- 
can soldiers, squadrons of B-52 bombers, F-16 fighters, AWACS 
aircraft, and squadrons of helicopters are to take part in them in 
addition to tanks, antiaircraft guns, naval ships, and destroyers 
These “Falling [Afil; play on words] Star” exercises are consid- 
ered preparations for a future American occupation of Arab 
territory and are part of the series of occupations of the Arab 
homeland which began in 1948. They are also considered a 
profound humiliation of the Arab nation and a grave violation of 
its independence. They also directly threaten the progressive 
Arab regimes and forces, in particular Libya. They are blatant 
intervention by foreign forces, allies of the Zionist enemy, in the 
Arab homeland.
