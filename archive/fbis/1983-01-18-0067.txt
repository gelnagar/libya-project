LIBYA

OIL SECRETARIAT ISSUES STATEMENT, URGES UNITY 


LD172028 Tripoli JANA in English 1846 GMT 17 Jan 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi Thani 3, Jan 17 JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The Oil Secretariat 

of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah issued a statement on the deterio- 
rating oil prices in the world's market pointing out that the Jamahiriyah is so con- 
cerned about oil prices because it led and still leads the battle of oil prices for 

the interest of the peoples of the oil producing countries from the viewpoint of 
unwavered and principled stand. As an outcome of this battle, the oil producing 
countries were able to control and fix oil prices, marketing, and shares given to the 
oil monopolies which used to explore, produce, market, fix prices and shares of profits 
for the country which owns the oil fields. The oil secretariat would like to point out 
the following: 


1 -- The Jamahiriyah has reduced oil production and put technical control not over 
each company but went beyond that to limit the production of eachwell separately. 
Such controls are endorsed by OPEC and not implemented by other oil countries. 


2 -- The worsening of the oil market is referred to the absence of production control. 
Production in other oil producing countries is regulated according to the interests 

of the capitalist countries. Some of these producing countries doubled their pro- 
duction which caused a damage in their oil reserves and created what is now called 
oil glut and a stagnation in the world's oil market and enabled the capitalist 
monopolies and countries to build large oil reserves to undermine the oil market.

3 -- The Jamahiriyah has repeatedly warned of that danger at the OPEC meetings in 
the recent years and even vefore the current deteriorating state of the oil market. 


4 -- The Jamahiriyah has called and calls upon the OPEC countries to restore produc- 
tion with reference to historic production levels and reduce production to absorb the 
oil glut in the world's oil market. 


5 -- The Jamahiriyah adheres to the OPEC basic prices and is ready to reconsider the 

qualitative differentials which are not static and controlled by the world's economic 
situation and the oil markets. This would be the case if ail the oil producing coun- 

tries adhered to limit and reduce production to a certain level that absorbs the oil 

glut with consideration to the "historic levels of productiion" of the oil producing 

countries. 


6 -- Jamahiriyah puts all its resources towards maintaining the unity of OPEC and 

stop it from collapse with a stress onacommitment to the gains achieved by OPEC 

to the peoples of its member countries and confrontation of the campaigns of blackmail 
and exploitation waged by the capitalist monopolies.
