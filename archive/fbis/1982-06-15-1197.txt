LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES ARMED FORCES OFFICERS 


LD142252 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 14 Jun 82 


[Text] Brother Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, supreme commander of the armed forces, 
met hundreds of officers on the 12th anniversary of the evacuation of American forces 
from Libyan Arab soil and explained to them, in the wake of the strategic maneuvers 
carried out by the armed forces in the Gulf of Sidra 2 days ago, the political and 
military situation in and around Lebanon. He affirmed to them the impossibility of 
any direct military action from Libya on the northern front for geographical reasons, 
stressing that had it not been for these reasons Libya would have participated in 
transporting its potential to the battle and would not have stopped until the enemy 
had been defeated. 


As far as such action is concerned, he said, its potential is available only in the 
confrontation countries geographically adjacent to occupied Palestine, namely, Egypt, 
Jordan, Saudi Arabia, Syria and Lebanon.

MALTA FOREIGN MINISTER SIGNS AGREEMENT, DEPARTS 


LD071958 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 7 Jun 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Jun (JANA) -- An economic and technical cooperation agreement between 
the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the Republic of Malta was signed this 
evening. 


The agreement contains the coordinated foreign policy stand of the two countries and 
guarantees the neutrality of the Mediterranean, making it a sea of security and peace. 


The two sides also agreed to support the Libyan Arab-Maltese recipient [qabidah] company 
and its projects. 


The agreement was signed by the secretary of the people's committee of the People's 
Bureau for Foreign Liaison and on the Maltese side by Foreign Affairs Minister Alex 
Sceberra Trigona. 


The Maltese foreign minister left Tripoli this evening.

JANA SCORES SAUDI STAND ON ‘'ZIONIST AGGRESSION' 


LD110908 Tripoli JANA in English 0817 GMT 11 Jun 82 


[Text] Riyadh, Sha'ban 18 June 11, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The Saudi regime has 
affirmed its close links with American imperialism and other imperialist countries while 
the Zionist enemy continues its savage invasion of Lebanon. 


The government news agency in Riyadh announced today that the regime's foréign minister, 
Sa'ud al-Faysal, headed this morning for Washington to meet with Reagan. He will also 
visit some European countries, including Britain. 


This tour affirms the Saudi regime's continued conspiratorial role against the 

Palestinian Revolution and its encouragement for the Zionist aggression. The Saudi 

rulers are using the tour as a propaganda ploy, to pretend that they are attempting to 

stop the Zionist invasion. In fact, this is a flimsy pretext by a regime which is strongly 
opposed to Arab national interests and openly hostile to the Arab nation's causes. 

This regime's conspiratorial role and strong links with U.S. imperialism are clearly 
evidenced by its continued strengthening of America's strategic oil reserves and deployment 
of Saudi finance and the [words indistinct] smuggled money in American banks and companies.
