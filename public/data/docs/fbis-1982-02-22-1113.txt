LIBYA

ANTI-IMPERIALIST GROUP HOLDS MEETING IN TRIPOLI 


LD202156 Tripoli JANA in English 1735 GMT 20 Feb 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 Feb (JANA) -- The meetings of the Preparatory Committee for the 
International Platform [Forum] Against Imperialism, Zionism, Racism and reaction starts 
in Tripoli today. 


The meetings will be attended by delegations representing some countries and international 
organisations. 


The committee will focus its discussion on the current situation in different parts of 
the world and imperialist-Zionist activities against various people's freedom. 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great al-Fateh Revolution said in his 
address to the International Conference for Solidarity with the Libyan people on 

3lst August 1981 that "According to the declaration of people's authority in March 1977, 
the Libyan people who committed themselves to defend causes of freedom, will accept with- 
out hesitation the jamahiriyah's transformation to a centre for international resistance 
against imperialism, Zionism, racism and reaction. The Libyan people will take this 
responsibility courageously and honourably." 


The conference called upon liberation movement representatives and all participants to 
discuss Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's initiative and to take the necessary steps in this respect. 


The foreign minister of Madagascar and his delegation and the Algerian delegation arrived 
here on Friday [19 Feb] to take part in the committee meeting. The representatives of 

the Cypriot Socialist Party, the Greek Socialist Party and the Soviet Afro-Asian Solidarity 
Committee also arrived in Tripoli yesterday. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Makes Speech 
LD211248 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1125 GMT 21 Feb 82 


[Speech by Head of State Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi at opening session of Preparatory 
Committee of International Forum Against Imperialism, Zionism, Racism and Reaction in 
Tripoli 21 February -- live] 


[Text] Honorable members of the preparatory committee, I welcome you and praise you 

for shouldering your struggle duty by meeting here today to prepare for the International 
Forum Against Imperialism, Racism and Reaction, in accordance with the resolution of the 
World Conference of Solidarity With the Libyan People which was held [date indistinct] 
1981 to express solidarity with the Libyan people against the direct American military 
aggression. 


Gentlemen, for more than 180 countries and over 240 organizations worldwide to choose 
Libya as an international forum for combating imperialism, racism and reaction is a 
great honor which makes our people feel extremely proud. It is also a medal of courage 
and pride pinned on the chest of every Libyan man and woman. In addition to that, it 

is « great trust placed in the Libyan people by these peoples and organizations. It is 
also a heavy responsibility for Libyan men and women, which they must carry courageously 
and heroically.

In their declaration of the establishment of their historic authority, the Libyan people ae 
recorded their adherence to freedom and their readiness to fight for it in their own land 

and to provide protection for the oppressed fighters for freedom elsewhere in the world. The 
Libyan people have become the only sovereign people, for they rule themselves by themselves. 
The Libyan people have set up a jamahiri society which is extensively self-run, without a 
government or proxy for the masses, Hence the Libyan people deserve to have their land 

become an international forum for combating imperialism, racism and reaction. [applause] 


American imperialism and its agent -- reaction -- have most insolently and arrogantly 
revealed their unjust and aggressive policy against the independence of Libya and the 
sanctions of its borders in a flagrant defiance of Libyans' national choices in their own 
national territory. This oppressive challenge by the arrogant and power maniacs such as 
that trivial midget, the American President, and his small minded servants who are still 
even more trivial and disgraceful, and the racist Zionists and cowardly reactionaries, will 
most certainly be countered, and I promise you here most solemnly, by a stubborn resistance. 
One million Libyans, the majority of whom are students, workers, peasants and civil servants 
of both sexes, will leave their schools and turn their factories and farms and even their 
offices and homes into trenches for resistance. [prolonged applause] 


The barbaric military campaigns which these maniacs, the enemies of peoples, are preparing 
for, will founder on the rock of the popular resistance of the armed people and will end 
in complete failure on the Libyan front. 


Honorable members of the preparatory committee: I have given you this assurance so that 
your confidence in my country will become stronger and so you may rest assured that it 

is prepared to shoulder the responsibility of becoming an international forum to combat 
imperialism, racism and reaction and that it is worthy of all this international trust and 
this honor. 


Now I will explain to you my views, which I hope will help you in preparing for this great 
new act of struggle: = 


1. The first result of the selection of Libya as the world forum against imperialism, reaction 
and racism is that it should be the leader of this resistance. But in my analysis, and to 

be objective, it must be said that the socialist camp led by the USSR is most certainly 

capable of resisting the United States and its allies in defense of that same socialist 

camp. In other words, the Warsaw Pact is the deterrent that would face NATO in the event 

of direct confrontation. Hence, my country and the other peoples represented here do not 

claim for themselves the leadership of the world resistance including that of the socialist 
camp. We will, however, undoubtedly stand by our friends. 


2. For Libya to become an international forum for world resistance means that Libya will be 
the commander of this resistance, but not of the whole resistance as such. This means 

that the fight against imperialism will be the task of all the oppressed people who join 
this international forum and thereby indicate the start of the cohesion of their struggle 
under its banners. 


3. This means the birth of the unity of struggle of all the peoples exposed to aggresion 

of imperialism and its tools: racism and reaction. It must be clearly understood that 

this also means that from the day of the establishment of this forum, the struggle of these 
peoples wiil assume a new legitimate character: the legitimacy of unity on all fronts 

and in all fields. More clearly and more precisely, this means the fighting in El Salvador, 
for example, will become an international struggle among all the peoples and organizations 
in this forum and the rightist fascist forces supported by American imperialism.

In practice, this means all the peoples and organizations which attended the Coni2rence 

on Solidarity with the Libyan People last August and decided to set up this forum must 

send fighters and reinforcements to the people of El Salvador. The same thing applies 

to Guatemala, Chile, Palestine, Namibia, South Africa and the Sahara, where actual fighting 
is raging now, and where the national forces are clashing with the tools of imperialism, 
Zionism, racism and rightism. This also means that any aggression by imperialism or any 

of its tools against any of these independent peoples participating in this forum shall 
constitute aggression against all these peoples and organizations, and consequently they 

must send fighters and reinforcements immediately to fight on the side of the people attacked. 


The peoples and organizations constituting this forum must prepare themselves for direct 
participation in checking the likely aggressions against LIbya, Nicaragua, Cuba, Angola, 
Syria and Lebanon. These are all independent peoples but their independence is being 
threatened by imperialism and racism. 


The peoples and organizations of this forum should also support each other in facing any 
problem or crisis that might develop against one of them, some of them or all of them, -- 
be it political or economic -- resulting from the aggressive policy of imperialism or its 
tools. 


Honorable members of the preparatory committee. We would have liked to see China with us 
here, for it is qualified, as a member of the Third World, to play an effective and deter- 
rent role in checking the imperialist and racist forces. China was set to play this role 
for the good of the national liberation of peoples and organizations in the days of Mao 
Aedong and Zhou Enlai. China is in a significant position because it falls within the 
sphere of Afro-Asian solidarity which is in alliance with Latin America. If China were 

to place its weight on the side of the struggle of these peoples, it would have given it 

a great effectiveness. Hence, it is wise and useful to leave the door open for China to 
join this forum when it decides to sever its ties once and for all with the imperialist 
forces who are opposed to the peoples of this forum. 


Honorable members of the preparatory committee, if your forthcoming conference, for which 
you are now preparing, were to adopt these principles, then a new era would be born and 

a new world would be created -- a world created by the forces of peoples who are determined 
to live free and, for the sake of achieving this objective, to inflict a final defeat on 
imperialism, reaction and racism. The routing of evil which is embodied in imperialism 
and its tools, becomes certain if we succeed in creating this new world. March forward. 
The struggle will continue.
