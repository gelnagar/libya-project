Summary (AI): A September 1975 FBIS/CIA transcript of Muammar al-Qadhdhafi's speech at Martyrs' Square in Tripoli marking the 1 September revolution anniversary. He hailed Uganda's Idi Amin and Guinea-Bissau's Luis Cabral, warned that any military counter-movement would be crushed, and pledged support for Palestinian militant groups.
• The speech was broadcast live by Tripoli Domestic Service starting 1636 GMT on 1 September 1975.
• Qadhdhafi said the transitional stage toward popular democracy would last for decades and would not be a dictatorship.
• He said no sick, ignorant, rancorous or reactionary persons would hold leadership positions in trade unions or mass organisations.
• The speech was released in several takes (CIA cable MSG 1877, 1911, 1957, 1852, 1878 from Amman).
• 1975-09-01: Qadhdhafi gave a live-broadcast speech at a public rally marking the 6th anniversary of the 1 September revolution, warning that any post-revolution military counter-movement would be crushed as a fascist movement and pledging support for the Palestinian armed struggle.
