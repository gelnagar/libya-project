LIBYA

Sandinist Front Delegation Arrives on Visit 
LD180928 Tripoli JANA in English 0825 GMT 18 May 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Shaban 5, May 18, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — Mr. Carlos Munez member of the National Com- 
mand of the Sandinista Front in Nicaragua arrived here yester- 
day accompanied by a large delegation on a visit to the 
Jamahiriyah within the framework of enhancing the relations of 
friendship and cooperation between the Jamahiriyah and Nica- 
ragua.
