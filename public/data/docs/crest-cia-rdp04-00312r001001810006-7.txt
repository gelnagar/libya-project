Summary (AI): Jack Anderson column reporting, from Justice Department files, that American operatives (led by ex-CIA agent Frank Terpil) trained and equipped Qaddafi's terrorist squads and supplied booby-trapped devices in the 1970s. It mentions a Libyan intelligence palace workshop, but no specific Tripoli site is named beyond a former royal winter palace.
• Justice Department files describe American operatives training Libyan killers in booby-trap construction, disguised as a mine-clearing operation.
• Terpil offered to smuggle a U.S. Redeye missile into Libya.
• One order of 100,000 timers bought for $4.50 to $6 per unit was sold to the Libyans at a grossly inflated price.
• Qaddafi's brother was involved in the London deal.
• 1976-07: Explosive devices delivered by Americans to a Libyan colonel in Tobruk; one device detonated, killing three Libyan soldiers and wounding others, per Justice Department files. (3 killed)
• 1976-08: Americans established a bomb workshop code-named 'Swanee' in the former royal winter palace.
• 1976-06: Terpil closed a deal with Qaddafi's brother in London.
