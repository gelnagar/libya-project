SUDANESE FOREIGN MINISTER ENDS VISIT 13 MAY 


LD132303 Tripoli JANA in English 1900 GMT 135 May 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 May, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Sudanese vice president and 
foreign minister, Mr ar-Rashid at-Tahir Bakr, left Tripoli today at noon, Leading 
those seeing Mr Bakr off at Tripoli International Airport was Brigadier Abu Bakr 
Yunis Jabir. 


Also participating in the farewell were the foreign secretary, Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam 
at-Turayki, and the General People's Committee secretary of education, Dr Muhammad 
Ahmad ash-Sharif, 


Mr ar-Rashid Bakr had arrived yesterday evening in Tripoli from Morocco after 
participating in the tenth Islamic foreign ministers' conference held in Fez, 


In a statement made to the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY's correspondent, he praised results 
achieved by the conference and said: "The Islamic countries' unanimity was made clear 
through a solution adopted by the conference which rejected all attempts aimed at 
entrenching Zionist occupation of Arab lands and which do not recognise the Palestine 
people's inalienable rights of return to their homeland, establishment of an independent 
Palestinian state, and the necessity of liberating all occupied Arab lands," 


He said: "our Arab and Islamic nation affirmed through earlier resolutions their 
insistence on carrying out the struggle against the enemy,"

He added: "The Arab nation reaffirms the failure of attempts made by colonialism and 
other hirelings to weaken its morale." He said: "These attempts will strengthen the 
Arab nation's belief in its values and challenge any conspiracy planned by our enemies." 


Ending his statement, the Sudanese vice-president said: "The Arab nation is capable of 
confronting the challenge and gaining victory. It is also capable of regaining its 
rights from the Zionist entity which is ceaselessly working for expansion and entrench- 
ment of its occupation." 


The foreign secretary, Dr 'Ali at-Turayki and Mr ar-Rashid Bakr had earlier this morning 
held a meeting in the Foreign Secretariat in Tripoli. The meeting was attended by the 
General People's Committee secretary of education, Dr Muhammad Ahmad ash-Sharif.

COOPERATION AGREEMENT SIGNED WITH EASY GERMANY

[Text] Tripoli, 12 May, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCYe-The General People's Committee secree 
tary for light industries, Dr "Umar al-Maqsi, and the demociatic German minister for 
electrical engineering and electronics, Mr Otfried Steger, this morning signed a coe 


operation agreement in the Foreign Secretariat. 


The agreement stipulates cooperation in cement manufacturing, textiles, communications, 
railways, sea transport, the fields of agricultural and economics. 


The agreement also includes the exchange of technical expertise between the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the German Democratic Republic. 


The signing of this agreement came during the third meeting of the Libyan ArabeGerman 
democratic joint committee.
