ALGERIA 

Al-SHA‘B Cited on ‘Difficulty’ of Convening Summit 
LD032139 Algiers APS in Arabic 
1700 GMT 3 May 86 

[Text] Algiers, 3 May (APS) — The Al-SHA‘B newspaper pub- 
lished a commentary today in its “Opinion” feature under the 
headline “Failure or Aborting?” which said that there is indeed 
a great difficulty in agreeing on an emergency Arab summit to 
discuss the American aggression against the Libyan Jamahiriyah 
despite the decision to hold it in Morocco. It has become clear 
that there are Arab sides that have adopted an attitude that 
obstructed the good progress toward the meeting of the Arab 
kings and presidents to discuss and study an extraordinarily 
serious issue aimed against the honor and dignity of the whole 
Arab nation. The paper went on to say the reality of the Arab 
world encompasses several issues. Some were dealt with and 
others remained unsolved because of the attitudes of some arab 
states or because of external attitudes. It pointed out that those 
Arab countries were all too well aware of these Arab issues when 
the American aggression took place. They could have called for 
an Arab summit before this time, considering that these states 
are earnestly concerned for Arabism and the honor of the Arab 
nation. ; 

The paper pointed out that through such behavior, all saw how 
the European states exploited this to support the U.S. aggression 
in words and deeds, beginning with emergency meetings and 
theendorsement of many serious measures against Libya, the 
victim, in front of the shameful Arab silence, — silence behind 
which Arab sides moved to preserve Arab disunity and pushed 
away any chance for the return of Arab fraternity and consensus. 
The paper asked, how can one explain the agreement in principle 
to hold the meeting and fix the place, and then postpone or cancel 
everything in the shadow of the division of the Arab world, and 
split into blocs and small alliances, working gradually for the 
weakening and removal of the importance of the Arab summit 
issue, which has lost its warmth and importance as a result. 

The paper confirmed that this Arab procrastination and indiffer- 
ence toward the American-European hatred for the Arab nation 
will push the Arab nation further toward weakness and subordi- 
nation in all fields. It pointed out that all the conditions and the 
requirements for agreement are there — at a time when other 
nations which have nothing in common other than circumstantial 
interests forgive each others’ sins and make important decisions 
at various levels. The paper went on to say that the truth is that 
it is important to agree on an Arab summit to discuss the 
American aggression on sister Libya, considering that it is an 
extraordinary and serious event that concerns all Arab peoples 
and states, in preparation for an other Arab summit concerning 
all the Arab issues. 

The paper concluded by saying: we still continue to hope while 
we are alive that an Arab summit will be held to discuss all that 
concerns the nation or at least for the sake of meeting to prove to 
the Western world that the Arabs can at least meet.
