LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Klibi Message of Support 
LD220901 Tripoli JANA in English 0827 GMT 22 Aug 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 25 Dhulgidah, 22 August JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The leader of the revolution received a 
message of support to the agreement of union between the 
Jamahiriyah and Morocco from the secretary-general of the 
Arab League, Chedli Klibi. It reads as follows: 


Colonel Mu‘ammer Al-Qadhdhafi, leader of Al-Fatih of the 
Great September Revolution; The agreement of the union signed 
between the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the 
Moroccan Kingdom is a positive step towards realizing more 
cooperation among our countries, towards bringing our peoples
