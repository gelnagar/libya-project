LIBYA

RAUL CASTRO, GIAP DECORATED BY AL-QADHDHAFI 
FLO51637 Havana Domestic Service in Spanish 1632 GMT 5 Nov 79 FL 


[Text ] Army Gen Raul Castro, first vice president of the Cuban Councils of State and 
Ministers, has been decorated in Tripoli, the Libyan capital, with the order of 
military star and military work hero medal. The second secretary of the Communist 
Party of Cuba and minister of the Revolutionary Armed Forces received the decorations 
from Libyan revolution leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhaff; The Cuban leader has ended a 
3-day visit to Libya which he made in the company of Gen Vo Nguyen Giap, vice 

premier and minister of defense of Vietnam, at the invitation of Qadhdhafi. 


General Giap also has decorated by the leader of the Libyan revolution with the 
1 September and military training orders and military work hero medal. 


During their stay in Libya, Army Gen Raul Castro and Gen Vo Nguyen Giap met with Mu! ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi and Cmdr ‘Abd as-Salam Jallud. Raul and Giap also visited an air 
base near Tripoli that formerly belonged to the United States and laid floral 

wreaths at the monument to martyrs of the 1 September 1969 revolution in that fraternal 
Arab country. 


As we have reported, the Cuban delegation which includes, among others, Commander of the 
Revolution Ramiro Valdes, vice president of the Council of State, had arrived in Libya 
from Algiers where it participated in the 25th anniversary of the beginning of the 
Algerian revolution. The visitors were seen off at Tripoli's international airport by 
Col Abu Bakr Yunis, commanding general of the Libyan Armed Forces. 


GIAP, RAUL CASTRO LEAVE TRIPOLI, ISSUE STATEMENT 
1D051948 Tripoli JANA in English 1451 GMT 5 Nov 79 ID 


[Text] Tripoli, November 5, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--General Vo Nguyen Giap, the 
Vietnamese vice premier, minister of national defence and leader of the people's 
struggle, and General Raul Castro, the Cuban first vice president in the Council of 
State, and in the Council of Ministers, minister of the Revolutionary Armed Forces, 
second secretary of the Central Committee of the Central Committee of the Cuban Party 
and chief of the armed forces, left Tripoli today after visiting the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah.

The two guests were given an official farewell at Tripoli international airport headed 
by Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, chief of staff of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces. 
Others included chiefs of staff, officers from the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, the Cuban 
ambassador, the ambassadors of the socialist countries accredited to the Jamahiriyah, 
and a representative from the protocol department at the Foreign Secretariat. 


In a statement to a reporter of the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY the two guests praised the 
giant achievements realized by the Libyan Arab people under the Great First of September 
Revolution. They also hailed the victories and gains the Jamahiriyah has won in all 
international circles and praised the pioneer role of the revolution in the struggle 
against colonialism and racism and international imperialism and her support to all the 
oppressed peoples and liberation movements of the world. 


In their statement the guests referred to the talks they conducted during the visit 
which dealt with Third World issues. They stressed that viewpoints of the Jamahiriyah, 
Cuba and Vietnam were identical on all the issues considered. 


General Giap insisted that the Arab nation would inevitably win in its just struggle 
against the forces of Zionism, racism and international imperialism. He also declared 
that the Vietnamese people will always remain the ally of the peace-loving peoples 
fighting imperialism and its colonialist expansion conspiracies in Africa, Latin America 
and all parts of the world. Victory will always reward the peoples! steadfastness in the 
face of the forces hostile to humanity. 


In conclusion, the two guests expressed their appreciation and gratitude for the warm 
people's reception and the generous hospitality they were met with.
