LIBYA

T-TURAYKI MAKES PRESS STATEMENT ON MAGHREB, INTER-ARAB ISSUES 


LD211215 Casablanca LE MATIN in French 11 May 79 p 8 LD 


(Mustapha Sehimi report: "The Fes Islamic Conference" ] 


[Excerpt ] Yesterday work continued in plenary session and in committee. In addition, 
Libyan foreign minister made a statement to the press. 


In fact, replying to questions put to him by representatives of the national press, 
the Libyan foreign minister made a number of statements. 


Asked about Libya's attitude toward tension in the Maghreb, Dr at-Turayki stated that 
his country supports the UN and OAU resolutions, the meaning of which is perfectly clear 
in this respect. 


Furthermore, he expressed his support for the appeal launched by his majesty the king 
in favor of complete autonomy for the Palestinians in their liberation struggle, free 
from any domination by any Arab state. 


Asked about the attitude adopted by Sudan, which in the end decided not to vote for 
Egypt's exclusion from the Islamic conference, he emphasized that Sudan remains an 
Arab country, maintains solidarity with fraternal countries and is a supporter of the 
cause of Islamic solidarity. 


As for the fate of Imam Musa as-Sadr, Dr at-Turayki stated that this matter no longer 
concerns his country.

‘as we have officially stated," he said, "Imam as-Sadr left Libya for Italy several 
months ago. He has disappeared from that country. This matter no longer concerns 
Libya and it is up to Lebanon, of which the imam is a national, to initiate inquiries 
if necessary.” 
In conclusion the Libyan foreign minister expressed his support for the creation of a 
pan-Islamic military force. "A committee of experts," he stated, "met in London 
recently and adopted a number of proposals on the subject. n any case, I intend to 
submit this matter to the Islamic conference.”
