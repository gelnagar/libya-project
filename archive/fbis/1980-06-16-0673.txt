UK EMBASSY IN TRIPOLI FIREBOMBED 13 JUNE 

LD142230 LONDON PRESS ASSOCIATION in English 1854 GMT 14 Jun 80 

[By David Healey, PRESS ASSOCIATION diplomatic staff] 

[Text] A firebomb was thrown into the British Embassy in Tripoli, Libya, on Friday night 
it was confirmed tonight by the Foreign Office in London. The bomb was a small one and 


caused only superficial damage to a wooden entrance door. 


The incident came only hours after the British Government had ordered the expulsion of 
the head of the Libyan diplomatic mission in London, Mr Musa Kousa,. 


He was told to leave within 48 hours after saying he approved of the latest threat by 
the Libyans to murder Libyan exiles opposed to the Al-Qadhdhafi regime. There were no 
reports of any injuries following the firebomb attack on the British Embassy and the 
fire brigade arrived within atout 10 minutes and dealt with the blaze. 


However, the British ambassador, Mr Michael Edes, today protested to the Libyan 
authorities and requested more protection for the embassy. 


Mr Kousa is thought to be still in Britain tonight. He is expected to leave tomorrow.

Republic of Chad was signed this morning at the Foreign Secretariat in Tripoli. The

Libyan Arab people who have stood with them in their battles against colonialism and 
fascism, which were controlling the Chadian people, and for the Jamahiriyah!s continuing 
efforts to establish peace in Chad through the national reconciliation conference.

JANA EDITOR DENIES REPORT OF 'SECRET AIRPORT! 
LD141918 Tripoli JANA in English 1730 GMT 14 Jun 80 


(Text] Tripoli, June 14, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The political editor of the JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY denied a report fabricated by a Paris-based magazine JEUNE AFRIQUE in which 
it said Libya has built a secret airport near the border with Mali and Mauritania. 


The political editor said the report is both silly and a total lie fabricated by a 
magazine well-known for its connections with two Western intelligence organisations. 
He said the magazine has consistently fought against freedom, progress and Islam. He 
added that it is known for its services to colonial interests in Africa, defence of 
colonial countries, justification of exploitation, dictatorship and reaction and anti- 
progressive antifreedom stances.
