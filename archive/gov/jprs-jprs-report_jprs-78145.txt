{Text] Cairo, 11 May (MENA)=--The Supreme State Security Prosecution, under the 

chairmanship of Counselor Raja’ al-'Arabi, is about to conclude its investigation 
into the cases of two sabotage groups, which have been seized in the past few weeks, 
The groups comprise 28 persons who are now under interrogation. 


The rejectionist elements abroad dispatched these sabotage groups to Egypt to corry 
out a number of bomb-throwing incidents as part of the terrorist plans being con- 
cocted against Egypt from abroad to undermine its national march. 


The State Security Intelligence Department arrested Faruq ‘Ali Nasif, whose nom de 
guerre is Abu Salim, together with a group which included Khalil ‘Abd al-Qadir, 
whose nom de guerre is Abu Khalid; Yusuf al-Abyad; and Abu Muslim al-Sayyid, whose 
nom de guerre is Ahmad al-Ghamri. The leader of the group confessed that this 
group was established and trained abroad to carry out sabotage operations inside 
Egypt. Someone called ‘Abbas Hilmi Hasan, whose nom de guerre is ‘Adil and who is 
a first lieutenant in the democratic front for the liberation of Palestine in 
Lebanon, trained this group in the use of weapons and the preparation of explosives 
in the front's camps in Beirut. He has a baccalaureate in commerce from Baghdad 
University and is married to an Iraqi woman. 


Last December, the rejectionist front dispatched Khalil ‘Abd al-Qadir to Egypt to 
gather information on some places and utilities that could become a target for 
sabotage operations. He returned to Beirut in February. He came again to Cairo 

on 1l March to prepare for the sabotage operations. Meanwhile, he was sending 
cables from Cairo to the organization's command in Beirut, and was receiving cables 
from this command. The intelligence departments has been monitoring all these 
cables. 


During this period, Khalil ‘Abd al-Qadir recruited two people to help him in imple- 
tL onting the sabotage operations. These two were also arrested. 


foward the end of March, the Iraqi wife of terrorist ‘Abbas Husayn [name as 
received] arrived in Egypt. She was caught with a quantity of explosives hidden 
in her suitcase. The explosives were hidden inside soap bars. The fuses were 
hidden inside a cigarette box closed by cel ophane.
