[Text] Washington, Shawal 27, August 6, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The US. Administration has ordered its 
agents in Egypt and Zaire to extend the scope of their military 
intervention in Chad according to the requirements of the U.S. 
imperialist plan to recruit Chad and stage an aggression against 
Libya through Chadian territory. 


Agent Mobutu who is currently visitng Washington announced 
the decision to send a further 700 Zairian soldiers to Chad 
making the total of the invading Zairian forces 2,700 in addition 
to 6 fighter jets. The American Government had earlier 
announced that it will cover the cost of all the Zairian forces 
spending in Chad and will give other military, economic and 
financial aid to this (7agent) regime. 


U.S. President Reagan praised in person what he called 
Mobutu’s courage and his military intervention in Chad and 
considered him a true friend serving American policies and 
objectives in the region. At the same time the U.S. government 
has ordered his employee at the state department, Husni 
Mubarak to take the initiative to expand his military moves in 
Chad and to back the stand of Washington's agents in Ndjamena 
who are also receiving huge quantities of military supplies from 
France. Kamal Hassan’s statement on this issue points out that 
Husni Mubarak’s regime will increase its military involvement 
in Chad. 


This Egyptian hostile move can be interpreted as a declared 
intention on the part of the Egyptian regime to participate in the 
war on the side of rebel Habre’s forces i.e. direct participation in 
the implementation of the aggressive plan aimed at attacking 
Libya.
