ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS ENDS, ISSUES STATEMENT 
LD261644Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 26 Jun 78 LD 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 26 June--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 
ended its meeting yesterday evening at the conference hali of Ash-Shati'! Hotel in 
Tripoli after 4 days of deliberations. 


The Permanent Secretariat issued a number of resolutions at the end of its meetings 
yesterday. 


After reviewing the work of the Preparatory Committee dealing with the trial of the 
Egyptian president and of the Information Committee, the Permanent Secretariat decided 
that the trial of the Egyptian president shall begin immediately in light of the measures 
adopted by the secretariat, which has already completed the formation of the trial 
bodies: the prosecution panel and the court panel, 


In this regard, the Permanent Secretariat has decided to hold a world press conference 

in Damascus to announce the bill of indictment against the Egyptian president, who is 
charged with high treason. The trial will take place in Baghdad at a date to be fixed by 
the permanent secretariat. 


The Permanent Secretariat has decided to entrust the chief of the delegations of Syria, the 
PLO and the Lebanese Nationalist Movement to submit working papers on the practical mea- 
sures which are likely to bolster the national and progressive Arab forces in Syria and 
within the ranks of the Palestine revolution and the Lebanese Nationalist Movement. 


The Permanent Secretariat decided to provide urgent support for the Egyptian national 
movement by participating in some of the political activities of the Egyptian opposition. 


The secretariat also decided to hold a celebration marking the 23 July Revolution 
anniversary at a pan-Arab level. The celebration commemorating this Egyptian revolution 
will be held in Damascus and will be organized by the secretariat and Syria. Preparations 
for it will be made by all the parties to the secretariat.which will issue a political 
document on the occasion evaluating the historic significance of the 23 July Revolution 
and its effect on the current national struggle. The docwment will explain the effects of 
the Egyptian president's apostasy and his betrayal of the objectives of this revolution 
and the Arab revolution. The document broadcast on the eve of 23 July by the radios of 
Damascus, Baghdad, Aden, Algiers and Tripoli. 


Following its sixth session, the Permanent Secretariat also decided to accept an invi- 
tation to visit the USSR to consolidate the close, strong relations between the USSR 
and the Arab national liberation movement,

Continuing its efforts to consolidate the unity of the Arab vocational and trade unionist 
organizations, the Permanent Secretariat decided to bolster the unity of the Arab Women's 
Federation. 


The Permanent Secretariat affirmed its strong denunciation and condemnation of the 

growing imperialist role of the French Government in Africa and its aggression against the 
Arab people of the Western Sahara and its direct interference in Zaire and Chad. It 
called on all the national and progressive Arab and world forces to offer all material 

and moral aid to the Saharan people who are being subjected to a plot designed to 
annihilate them at the hands of reaction, 


The Permanent Secretariat greeted the Libyan people's courage in the face of the im- 
perialist plots in Africa and affirmed its support of the Arab national and progressive 
forces for the Jamahiriyah in its confrontation of imperialist moves. 


The Permanent Secretariat decided to bolster the pan-Arab struggle in the Arab Gulf 
countries against the growing dangers to the Arab character of the Arab Gulf and to 
strengthen the struggle of the national and progressive Arab forces in this area, 
particularly that of the popular front for the liberation of Oman. 


The Permanent Secretariat decided to promote relations with the world progressive and 
democratic forces through contacting the international political and progressive and 
democratic parties and organizations and explaining pan-Arab issues to them regularly. 


At the end of its meeting, the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress issued 
the following statement: 


The permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress met from 21 to 25 June 1978 in 
Tripoli, Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. In this Sixth Session, the 
Permanent Secretariat reviewed the political Arab and international situations, It 
completed its work in an atmosphere of open democracy, thereby demonstrating the will 
of the masses of our Arab nation to counter all chalienges of destiny. 


This atmosphere manifested itself most clearly in the resolutions that were unanimously 
adopted, particularly those pertaining to the completion of preparations for the stages 
of the trial of the Egyptian president which, it was decided, should begin within the 
next few Gays with a largescale press conference to be held in Damascus where the bill 
of indictment will be announced. The trial itself will be held in Baghdad. 


The court and prosecution panels have already been chosen from political and legal Arab 
strugglers from the various countries of the Arab homeland, 


The Permanent Secretariat considers it its duty to assert to the masses of the Arab nation 
that the trial of the Egyptian president, who is the symbol of treason, is in fact a 

trial of all traitors who departed from the will of the nation and who deal with the 
Zionist enemy in whatever form. 


The Permanent Secretariat reviewed conditions in the Arab homeland and adopted the relevant 
resolutions with a view to strengthening the capabilities of the Arab revolutionary 
struggle and rallying all resources of the Arab nation and its capabilities of struggle 
both materially and morally. The battle of destiny the Arab nation is fighting against 
imperialism and Zionism stems from its determination to achieve all its social and pan-Arab 
rights and from its intention to continue the struggle against settlement and capitula- 
tion in all its forms because it will lead to recognition of the Zionist entity.
