LIBYANS GAVE BILLY EXTRA LOAN
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp09s00048r000100020096-6
https://archive.org/details/cia-readingroom-document-cia-rdp09s00048r000100020096-6

Summary (AI): A 1981 newspaper article (Wilmington Sunday News Journal, by Joe Trento, reprinted in CIA reading room) reports that Billy Carter received loans and payments from the Libyan government and discussed machine-gun deals with former CIA agent Frank Terpil. It describes Justice Department and FBI handling of the case and the Carter-Terpil contacts.
• Federal investigators determined the Libyans had 'loaned' Billy Carter $200,000 and given him $20,000 more to cover expenses of a Libyan delegation visit to the US.
• Reports said Carter received an additional $200,000 from the Libyan government, by a check written on a London bank; NSA knew of it in May 1980 but proof was withheld on national security grounds.
• Carter was under investigation for failure to register as a Libyan agent amid allegations of gun-running and recruiting terrorists for Libya.
• Terpil was convicted in absentia and given a 50-year sentence in New York in 1980 on charges connected with gun-running.
• The ban on military exports to Libya was never lifted, per the article.
• 1979: Billy Carter visited Libya; he met Frank Terpil at a dinner and watched a parade in Tripoli on his second visit.
• 1980-01: Billy Carter told a Justice Department official and an FBI agent that Terpil had recently contacted him about getting machine guns for Libya.
