Summary (AI): A August 1974 CIA cable reports that Libyan Prime Minister Jallud made an unexpected trip to Cairo, possibly to smooth bilateral problems after Sadat publicly accused Libya of stationing Mirage aircraft in Egypt in violation of a French arms embargo. No place in Tripoli is named as a site.
• Sadat publicly said Libyan Mirage aircraft had been stationed in Egypt for over a year, in violation of a French arms embargo against Middle East combatants.
• Talks between Qadhafi and Sadat the previous weekend did not go well; both sides agreed to stop public back-biting.
• Paris reportedly had to request an explanation and apologies from the Libyan side over the Mirage issue.
• 1974-08-23: Jallud made an unexpected trip to Cairo after a week of travel to Rome and Paris, with a brief stop in Tripoli
