LIBYA

‘Collapse’ of Israeli-Lebanese Agreement Hailed 
LD292337 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2115 GMT 29 Feb 84 


[Station commentary] 


[Excerpts] The collapse of the Zionist-Phalangst 17 May 
agreement, whose official death announcement is now only a 
question of time, has come about as a result of huge sacrifice and 
the blood of caravans of martyrs which the Lebanese Arab people 
did not hesitate to offer for the sake of Lebanon’s Arabism. The 
bitter struggle these Arab people waged against the Zionist 
crusaders caused them to flee to the Al-Awali River and to their 
warships in the sea off the Lebanese Arab shores. 


This bitter struggle brought about the collapse of the Zionist- 
Phalangist 17 May agreement, despite all the American and 
Zionist warnings and threats against abrogation of this ill- 
omened agreement, which would have turned this Arab land into 
a Zionist protectorate in the full sense of the word, and an 
advanced base for U.S. imperialism which aims at full dom- 
ination of the Arab region by all means. 


The collapse of the 17 May Zionist-Phalangist agreement is not 
a victory for the Lebanese national forces alone; it is also a victory 
for the revolutionary forces in the Arab homeland, foremost of 
which stands the Jamahiriyah, which from the very first day of 
the signing of this agreement of humiliation called for the 
declaration of a jihad to end it without any delay. 


The collapse of the 17 May Zionist-Phalangist agreement her- 
alds the start of a great mass uprising throughout the Arab 
homeland which will destroy the dictators, the reactionaries and 
the infiltrators who ally themselves with the enemies of the Arab 
nation. Yes, it heralds the fall of Husayn, Husni [Mubarak], 
‘Arafat and his deviationist clique, and those like them 


The collapse of the 17 May Zionist-Phalangist agreement her- 
alds the greatest tiding: the collapse of the ill-omened, 
traitorous Camp David accord which fettered Egypt and isolated 
it from its Arab nation and brought shame and disgrace to it, 
turning it into a Zionist colony and a free ground for those whom 
God has cursed in His glorious Koran. This agreement robbed 
Egypt of its independence and caused it to submit in hursiliation 
to the will of a foreign racist entity whose population does not 
exceed 3 million. What a farce! 


If the Lebanese Arab people, who are small in number, have 
succeeded by their struggle, blood and the caravans of sacrifices 
to bring about the collapse of the traitorous 17 May agreement 
and inflict defeat on the Zionists and the crusaders, then the 
Egyptian Arab people, who executed the vanquished As-Sadat, 
have no alternative today but to follow the example of the 
Lebanese Arab people and rise to break the fetters with which 
As-Sadat’s heir is trying to shackle them further, and to tear up 
the traitorous Camp David accord which brought disgrace to the 
Arab people of Egypt.
