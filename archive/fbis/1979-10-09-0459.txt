LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES FESTIVAL ON 'REVENGE DAY! 
LD081854 Tripoli JANA in English 1430 GMT 8 Oct 79 LD 


{"Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi's Speech at the Great People's Festival in Hatiyat 
Tagrift"--JANA headline] 


[Text] Tripoli, October 8, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
leader of the Great First of September Revolution, stated that the arrogance of that 
despicable colonist Gaerciana has been wiped out from Tagrift at the hands of steadfast 
Libyan mujahidin, who were ready to die rather than give up their rights. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that such solidarity and steadfastness demonstrate that those 
in the right, no matter how weak they may be, ultimately win. 


He stated that the losses which befell the fascist Italian enemy in Hatiyat Tagrift 
will always be the doom of any aggressor against the peoples. He added: "The stead- 
festness of our fathers and forefathers is the kind that asserts the struggle of people 
everywhere", 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, speaking at the great people's festival held yesterday evening in 
Hatiyat Tagrift on the occasion of 'Revenge Day', said: "We pray for mercy for the 
martyrs who died for the liberty of the Libyan Arab people, which ultimately leads to 

the liberty of the entire Arab nation, At the same time we earnestly curse the agressors, 
the Italians, and all those who betrayed their country." 


He affirmed that, in spite of the Libyan people's poverty and their limited resources 
as opposed to the great numbers and capabilities of the Italian fascists, this people, 
who were the owners of the land, were able to defeat the mighty enemies, tear off and 
trample over silk-made Italian flag, 


The leader of the revolution pointed out that the Mujahidin (?and the) martyrs of 
Tagrift did not only fight the Italian Army but also fought armies of traitors. He 
said that the agents who came to this country under the Italian flag fighting by the 
side against the country-men and their betrayal of their Arabism, were defeated and 
cursed by the dead and the living. The treason was over as well as the false glory of 
the traitors, The money their [as received] received from the Italians for fighting 
the mujahidin was dried up. 


He added that the traitors did not think that this small nation under Italian domination 
could be victorous one day and erect their martyrs' memorials in the same old battle- 
field. They did not realize also that they will be cursed by future generations. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that those traitors had joined the side of the 
Italians because they were short-sighted. They neither believed in victory nor in this 
people's capability to win over its enemy. As to the mujahidin who believed in victory 
and in their right over their land and that Tagrift is the land they possessed and were 
entitled to die on and defend against every aggressor--they are the ones we greet today 
while we curse the traitors and cowards.

We commemorate and celebrate the 'Revenge Day', the day of victory over the invadors 
after long years of occupation, the final victory when the Italian colonialist were 
expelled from Libyan soil, for we want to learn a historic lesson since history repeats 
itself. The lesson is that whoever sells out his homeland, dignity and freedom cheaply 
will be cursed by the coming generations, es will be those who followed the same course 
and joined the ranks of the enemy. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi went on to say that aggression might take place again. Therefore, 
everyone of us must remind his children that traitors will be cursed, while the martyrs 
who faced the battle will secure freedom, happiness for their followers. 


He went on to say that those who offered their life for freedom during the Tagrift 
battle are now undoubtedly in heaven. He said the Mujahidin who survived this battle 
and others deserve to be honoured and respected. They deserve a comfortable life 
because they fought for our freedom and happiness. 


Eyen in the future we and the future generations will continue to honour those who 
confronted the enemies and fought not only for their own freedom but for that of others, 
and for the martyrs who deserve us to pray for them to be granted mercy and forgiveness. 


He said: "Throughout our history we shall prove this and teach our future generations 
never to betray the principles they maintained throughout their history and always to 

set chase to the traitors and humiliate those who join their ranks. He said that on 

this occasion there were some surviving traitors who felt the (?shame) of having 
sympathized with the traitors who sold out their homeland and joined the enemy. On the 
other hand there were others who on the same occasion felt proud and dignified because they 
had stood by the heroes who sacrificed their lives on the soil of Tagrift and are now 

in heaven and enjoy dignity, respect and pride. 


Colonel Mu'Ammar al-Qadhdhafi continued: "When we commemorate the mujahidin to pay our 
respects to them, and when we recall the traitors to denounce them, we should not 

forget a third group. This group did not join the Italians in their fighting, nor was 
it part of the courageous mujahidin who held steadfast on the soil of Tagrift in the face 
of the aggressor. It was that cowardly group that was neither treasonous nor joined 
the fighting. It retreated from Zalla to take cover in Al-Horouj, leaving Garciani to 
lead his armies to surprise the peaceful peoples of Hatiya Tagrift, instead of stabbing 
him in the back as they should have done". 


He added: "We also recall another similar group, who with their arms and equipment 
stationed near the place of battle, preferred to remain afar instead of taking part. 
This detestable abstention was motivated by a love of leadership. They did not wish 
to die as martyrs, but, nevertheless, they wished the mujahidin'to engage in battle so 
that the leadership would be theirs afterwards. In other words, they sacrificed the 
people for the sake of the leadership." 


He also affirmed that those who avoided taking part in the battle and jumped to leader- 
ship at the expense of the gujahidinm @i@not realize that the people will one day reveal 
the true history of those traitors who did not know their treason would inflict shame 
and humilitation upon their grandchildren. 


He added: This is a useful lesson in the history of our people, a lesson which the 
martyrs recorded on this land with their blood and sweat on the day of Tagrift. The 
cowards and traitors were dead as well as the martyrs. The first left shame and the 
latter left pride and dignity. Khalifa Az-Zawi did as well as Sa'd at-Tibouli but both 
are greatly different.

The leader of the Great First September Revolution Council Mu!'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
added: "The Libyan people was weak before the invading army; it was colonialized 
and dispersed. There it is today, free, powerful, strong and unified, leading the 
Arab nation towards freedom , dignity and liberation. 


He said that 'Revenge Day! coincided with the 7th October, the day when the invading 
Italian fleet pounded the Libyan coast in 1911 and which, sixty years later, marked 

the final expulsion of 20 thousand Italians from Libyan soil. On the 7th October 1970, 
the biggest air and sea link between Tripoli and Rome was set up to evacuate the 
Italians from Libya in the same way they were previously sent from latter to the former. 


The date was not chosen, but came as a coincidence, an evidence of God's will to show 
the world that any aggressor, no matter for how long his power, was bound to end in 
failure. On this day in 1970 the Italians, who had come to Libya as invadors, were 
defeated and expelled. 


The leader of the Great First of September Revolution, Colonel Mu'tammar al-Qadhdhafi 
said "Then the Italians withdrew their forces from Burga and formed a mighty army in 
other areas since they had occupied all areas except those under the leadership of 
Qnar Muktar. They wanted to use this army to attack Galo, Ugla and Kafra after they 
had attacked Agdabiah, Aqeila and An-Nufleh", 


He went on to say: "But this arny was confronted with the Tagrift battle, during 
which it suffered great losses and was pushed to the sea, having failed to pursue the 
fighting. Was it not for the treason of the leaders of fighting tribes and the un- 
faithful Libyans who joined the enemy ranks, Italy would not have invaded Libya in 
1928." 


Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi pointed out: "The Tagrift battle should have marked the 
beginning of the end of Italian occupation as the Qardhabiah battle did--had it not 
been for the traitors and leaders who sought refuge anywhere, even outside the home- 
land. These leaders prevented the mujahidin from reaping the fruits of their struggle, 
since all the wonderful victories were wasted away. 


The area between AbuNijaym and Ajdabia up to Jalo and Jafra oasis was a pocket of 
resistance. It was a desert inhabited by the lions which Italians feared. This is 
why it was the target of their attacks in 1928. The leader of the revolution went on 
to say: "We thank all, for after these tragedies brought to us by colonialism and 
after the forced disperse, we were back. Those who were expelled to Chad and Egypt 
returned to their homes and land. There are some veterans still alive. They can see 
now how the situation has changed. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi concluded: "Those who are with us tonight of the mujahidin who 
fought in Tagrift on the first of Ramadan, (February 15th) 1928 with their ancient 
guns, could not have known they would witness the unveiling of the memorial of freedom 
and victory at the very sight of the Tagrift battle or see the Libyans as strong free 
peoples. Little did they expect to see in their lifetime Libyan tanks in Tagrift, 
driven by Libyans, and others flying the most sophisticated combat planes in the skies 
of Tagrift."

ECONOMIC IMPACT OF SOVIET ARMS PURCHASES ASSESSED 
LD060930 Cairo Voice of the Arabs in Arabic 0600 GMT 6 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] SAWT LIBYA, the organ of the Libyan National Movement [al-Harakat al-Wataniyah 
al-Libiyyah], has stated that Libya is suffering intense difficulties as a result of 
the huge arms deals it is concluding with the Soviet Union--which greatly exceed its 
actual needs--and also because of loans it is obtaining from some Arab and foreign 
countries to finance useless schemes, 


The London-based periodical, quoting a source in the Libyan Treasury Secretariat, adds 
that Libya's payments to the Soviet Union for the arms deals have reached more than 

1 billion Libyan dinars per year, It states that the weapons being received by Libya 
are not being used, but are being stored in the desert or in camps where they will rust.
