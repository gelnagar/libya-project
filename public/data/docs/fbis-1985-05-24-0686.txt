LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi To Visit Iraq; Seeks End to Gulf War 
LD241013 Kuwait KUNA in English 0912 GMT 24 May 85 


[Text] Kuwait, May 24 (KUNA) — Libyan leader Mu‘ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi will visit Iraq “within the coming two weeks” to 
work out a new initiative to end the Iraq-Iran war, daily AL 
QABAS reported Friday. 


The daily said Al-Qadhdhafi will be accompanied by a high 
ranking Saudi Arabian official 


Al-Qadhdhafi earlier this week made a surprise visit to Saudi 
Arabia and had talks with the King Fahd. AL-QABAS said in 
his talks with Saudi officials, the Libyan leader suggested [word 
indistinct] on a fresh move to end the Gulf war. 


Al-Qadhdhafi has been on unfriendly terms with Iraqi President 
Saddam Husayn since Iraq and Iran went into war five years ago 
However, Al-Qadhdhafi has recent been diplomatically engaged 
in attempts to(?mend) ferces with some of his Arab adversaries 


Jallud, Syria’s Khaddam Discuss Palestinian Camps 
LD241012 Tripoli JANA in English 0901 GMT 24 May 85 


[Text] Tripoli, Ramadan 5, May 24. JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY Contacts continued all day yesterday between 
Staff Major ‘Abd al-Salam Ahmad Jallud and ‘Abd al-Halim 
Khaddam, the Syrian vice president related to what facing the 
Palestinian camps of barbaric shelling carried out by Amal 
movement and the need to put an end to the vicious acts facing 
the inhabitants of the Palestinian camps.
