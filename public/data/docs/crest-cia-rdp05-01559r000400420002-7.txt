Summary (AI): A January 1986 Tripoli broadcast of a speech by Qadhdhafi to World Islamic Call preachers from West Africa, calling for jihad against France and the United States over Chad and urging attacks on French bases in Africa. It also includes the preachers' reply and a JANA denial of a reported Libyan attack in Chad. No specific Tripoli sites or killings are named.
• Qadhdhafi said France should be made to feel that if it attacks Libya, Muslims should attack France everywhere.
• Qadhdhafi claimed the U.S. aims in Africa were to change regimes in Sudan, Libya, and Nigeria.
• The World Islamic Call Society preachers' message referred to a U.S. aggression during April, meaning the April 1986 U.S. strike.
• JANA denied that the Libyan Army attacked the Kalait region in Chad.
• 1986-01-12: Qadhdhafi addressed visiting World Islamic Call preachers from West Africa and called for jihad against French and U.S. bases in Africa.
