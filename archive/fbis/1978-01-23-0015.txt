LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES MESSAGE FROM KING HUSAYN 
LD211028Y Tripoli JANA in English 0819 GMT 21 Jan 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, January 21, JANA:--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the revolution leader 
yesterday received a message from King Husayn of Jordan. The message stressed the 
steadfast stand of Jordan against the surrender solutions and negotiations of disgrace 
between the Zionists and the Egyptian regime. The message said Jordan's stand from 

the very beginning was firm in support of the Palestinian issue and we had stressed 

this stand at all Arab and internationai forums. 


It said our stand emanates from the national satisfaction and is derived from the long 


history of struggle of the united Arab nation ever since the beginning of the Palestinian 
issue.
