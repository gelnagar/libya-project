LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Discusses Chad, Arab Issues 
PM211545 Paris Le Monde in French 21 Jul 83 pp |, 3 


{Interview with Libyan head of state Mu’ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
date not given] 


by Eric Rouleau in Tripoli 


[Text] Tripoli — “I have invited President Mitterrand to come 
to Libya and | am prepared to visit France not only to eliminate 
the misunderstandings and problems which have clouded our 
bilateral relations but also to develop and extend them in all 
spheres; we are awaiting the Paris reply to our proposal 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, conciliatory or intransigent, ironical or 
falsely naive in turn, expressed himself with olympian calm 
Faithful to his bedouin tastes, he receives his visitors in a huge 
tent erected on...the lawn which surrounds the presidential pal- 
ace. Dressed in a well-cut uniform, his curly hair cut short, his 
relaxed and at times shining face contrasted with the cutting 
remarks he made about the French Government 


Question: What proposal would you make to President Mitter- 
rand to enable France to play a positive and effective role in 
solving the Chadian conflict? 


“Absolutely nothing on this subject,” the leader of the Libyan 
Jamahiriyah (“state of the masses’) said in an icy tone. “I do not 
think that France has any role in Chad or elsewhere on the same 
continent. Africa is for the Africans, just as Europe is for the 
Europeans. The anachronistic colonial concepts which inspire 
your government are hateful to our peoples and will eventually 
destroy French interests in the Third World. We agree that 
France should develop relations of friendship and cooperation 
with all states. But we will never agree to it acting as guardian 
and interfering in Chad's internal affairs, especially by supplying 
military support to one of the two parties to the conflict.” 
Question: You are doing that by arming and equipping the 
other side 
Answer: That is untrue! 

In response to our more than skeptical expression, Col al- 
Qadhdhafi abandoned his tone of denial and changed his tactics 
“Well, suppose | supply arms to the National Unity Transition 
Government [|GUNT] of which Mr Goukouni Oueddei is presi- 
dent. In so doing | have served the cause of Chad's legitimate 
government a coaltion of 11 political, ethnic, tribal and 
religious groups which cover virtually the whole territory, the 
Muslim north and the Christian south, the west as well as the 
east. The GUNT was formed democratically under OAU aus- 
pices following a successful national reconciliation, and it was 
recognized by the whole world community, including France. It 
is established on national territory and its army controls half of 
the country. The latter is fighting Hissein Habre, a criminal 
officer who was formerly defense minister in that same govern- 
ment, and who occupied Ndjamena, the capital, by force of arms 
And it is this usurper which France is supporting while the world 
press is storming against us Africans, neighbors and brothers of
