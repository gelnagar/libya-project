Summary (AI): An NPIC briefing note from January 1982 reports Libyan Foxbat (MiG-25) aircraft deployed to Ghat airfield North in southwest Libya, far from Tripoli. It also notes the normal home base at Ibn Nafa and earlier deployments to Ghurdabiyah and Sebha.
• Eleven Foxbat aircraft deployed to Ghat Airfield North, with five on the south parking apron, two Foxbat A and one Foxbat B on the parallel taxiway, and three on the southwest end of runway 06/24.
• The Foxbat were parked in a defensive dispersal pattern, suggesting they were not in transit.
• SF.260 aircraft strength at the airfield was 16, slightly below the normal 25 to 35.
• This was the third deployment of Foxbat from their normal home base at Ibn Nafa airfield.
• 1982-01: 11 Foxbat aircraft identified at the airfield for the first time, parked in a defensive dispersal pattern; 16 SF.260 aircraft present versus a normal 25 to 35.

FOXBAT DEPLOYMENT 
GHAT AIRFIELD NORTH, LIBYA 

20X1 

1. (S/D) SIGNIFICANCE: FOXBAT ARE IDENTIFIED FOR THE FIRST TIME 
AT THIS AIRFIELD. 

2. (S/D) REMARKS: A TOTAL OF 11 FOXBAT HAVE DEPLOYED TO THIS 
FIELD. FIVE FOXBAT A (4 ON GRAPHIC) ARE ON THE SOUTH PARKING 
APRON, PLUS TWO FOXBAT A AND ONE BOXBAT B ARE ON THE PARALLEL 
TAXIWAY, AND THREE FOXBAT A ARE ON THE SOUTHWEST END OF RUNWAY 
06/24 (NOT ON GRAPHIC). THE FOXBAT ARE PARKED IN A DEFENSIVE 
DISPERSAL PATTERN SUGGESTING THAT THEY ARE NOT IN TRANSIT. 
SF.260 AIR ORDER-OF-BATTLE IS SLIGHTLY LOW WITH 16 PRESENT; 25 
TO 35 ARE NORMALLY HERE. 

(S/D) THIS IS THE THIRD DEPLOYMENT OF FOXBAT FROM THEIR NORMAL 

HOME BASE AT IBN NAFA AIRFIELD ) SINCE 25X1 
DEPLOYMENTS OF FOXBAT HAVE OCCURED TO 25XK1 

GHURDABIYAH AIRFIELD AND TO 25X1 

SEBHA AIRFIELD 

(S/D) GHAT AIRFIELD NORTH IS IN THE SOUTHWEST SECTION OF LIBYA 
NEAR THE TOWN OF SARDALAS, APPROXIMATELY 30 NM FROM THE ALGERIA 
BORDER. THE AIRFIELD NORMALLY SUPPORTS AN SF.260 UNIT.
