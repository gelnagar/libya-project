LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES MESSAGE FROM BENDJEDID 


LD282118 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 28 Oct 81 
[Text] Tripoli, 28 Oct (JANA) -- The leader of the revolution this evening received 
Abdelhamid Almuhayri and Lakhdar Ibrahimi, members of the Algerian FLN Central Committee. 
Almuhayri handed the leader of the revolution a message from President of the Algerian 
Republic Chadli Bendjedid.
