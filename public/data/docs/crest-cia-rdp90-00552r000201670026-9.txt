Summary (AI): A 1985 Washington Times article reports that President Reagan ordered a leak investigation into a Washington Post account of a covert CIA plan to topple Qaddafi. It covers Libyan military buildup and Qaddafi's support for insurgent and terrorist groups abroad, with no specific Tripoli sites mentioned.
• The anti-Qaddafi plan was approved in a presidential 'finding' issued earlier in the year, according to the Washington Post.
• U.S. officials said Libya had purchased an estimated $20 billion worth of Soviet-made military equipment, including 2,400 tanks and 500 warplanes.
• Qaddafi said in a speech in 1985 marking the 14th anniversary of the closing of Wheelus Air Base that Libya could 'export terrorism, liquidation and arson to the heart of America.'
• Libya's government said pro-Qaddafi forces would seek the 'physical liquidation' of American leaders, beginning with Reagan.
• A 1984 CIA-led vulnerability assessment and a special national intelligence estimate on Libya were cited.
