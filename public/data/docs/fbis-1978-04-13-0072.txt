LIBYA

CHAD PRESIDENT CABLES THANKS TO AL -QADHDHAFI 
LD122325Y Tripoli JANA in English 1840 GMT 12 Apr 78 LD 


{[Text] Tripoli, April 12, JANA--Colonel Mu'tammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Al-Fatah 
(One] September Revolution, has received a cable from President Felix Malloum of Chad 
in which the president thanked him for the warm hospitality given to him and his 
accompanying delegation during their visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah, President Malloum said: This visit has offered us the opportunity to 
exchange views on problems which concern our two countries. I am sure that friendly 
relations and brotherly links between our two countries will be strengthened, 


MAURITANIAN PRESIDENT TO VISIT 13 APRIL 
LD122212Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1743 GMT 12 Apr 78 LD 


(Text] Tripoli--JANA has learned that the president of the Islamic Republic of Mauritania, 
Mokhtar Ould Daddah, will tomorrow begin an official visit to the Socdalist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah that will go for several days.
