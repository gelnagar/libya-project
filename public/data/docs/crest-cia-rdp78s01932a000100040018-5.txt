Summary (AI): A April 1974 cable reports that Libyan officials expected mass demonstrations in Tripoli supporting Qadhafi, organized by the Arab Socialist Union and popular committee leaders, to show his domestic detractors that he remained in command.
• The Arab Socialist Union was described as the country's only legitimate political party.
• The rallies were described as part of Qadhafi's attempts to shed his leadership role, often reminiscent of the late President Nasser's public appeals.
• The cable cites an earlier Tripoli message (Tripoli 405, 4 April 1974).
• 1974-04-09: Planned mass demonstrations in support of Qadhafi, organized by the Arab Socialist Union and popular committee leaders
