It is a processing status message, with little detail on the content.
• The speech lasted 140 minutes in total.
• Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland broadcast the recording at 0035 GMT on 3 September 1976.
• The first 120 minutes were processable at the time of the cable; BBC was processing the remaining 30 minutes with only one monitor available.
• 1976-09-02: Qadhdhafi gave a 140-minute speech at the anniversary rally in Tripoli; the additional 30-minute portion was devoted virtually entirely to Egypt.
