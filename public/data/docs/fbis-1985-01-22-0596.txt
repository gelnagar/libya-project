LIBYA

Gambian President Arrives for Talks 19 Jan 
LD192211 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1900 GMT 19 Jan 85 


[Text] Tripoli, 19 January (JANA)— Gambian President Dawda 
Jawara arrived this evening in Tripoli on a 3-day visit to the 
Jamahiriyah. Staff Major ‘Abd al-Salam Ahmad Jallud headed 
those who welcomed him and the accompanying delegation at 
Tripoli International Airport. Also taking part in the welcoming 
party were members of the General Popular Committee and 
heads of the diplomatic missions in the Jamahiriyah. 


President Dawda Jawara made a statement to JANA in which 
he said: I am happy to be visiting the Jamahiriyah for the 
second time. We are linked by good relations and we look forward 
to meeting the brother leader of the revolution with whom we will 
review a number of issues of common interest, foremost of which 
is the Palestinian issue.

Al-Qadhdhafi ‘Ready’ To Settle Chadian Conflict 
AB211816 Paris AFP in French 1634 GMT 21 Jan 85 


[Text] Paris, 21 Jan (AFP) — Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
leader of the Libyan revolution, has stated that he wants to settle 
the Chadian problem and says he is still ready “to support any 
peaceful settlement that will bring all the opposing parties 
around the same table for negotiaitons.” 


In an interview granted to the Arabic periodical KOL EL-ARAB 
to be published in Paris on Wednesday, he stressed: “We shall 
not allow anybody to fight us from Chad or use that country to 
launch attacks against us. Furthermore, we are opposed to the

installation of a regime hostile to Libya’s security and the secu- 
rity of our borders.” 


Recalling that Libya has maintained good relations with the 
Chadian people, he stressed that Libyans “are in the same trench 
as the government of Goukouni Oueddei,” adding that President 
Hissein Habre “who is hostile to us, is presently besieged from 
all sides and I think he is going to fall.” 


Talking about his last meetings with French President Francois 
Mitterrand and Spanish Prime Minister Filipe Gonzalez, the 
Libyan head of state described them as “blows to the United 
States and to Ronald Reagan in particular...Arabs being able to 
ally themselves provisionally with secondary enemies in order to 
confront the big enemies.” The Libyan leader felt that these 
meetings were necessary because they strengthened relations 
between Libya and the socialist regimes in Western Europe. 
These meetings, he said, “are meant to promote cooperation 
between these regimes and the Arabs, and positively reflects on 
security in the Mediterranean.” 


Asked about a possible meeting with PLO leader Yasir ‘Arafat, 
Col Al-Qadhdhafi stated that he “no longer needs him” but 
added that “‘Arafat would be welcomed any time in Libya as a 
Palestinian Arab citizen.” 


On the conflict between Palestinians, he indicated that “the 
panoply of fronts and leaders do not serve the Palestinian cause.” 
He added: “I call for and encourage the emergence of an 
iron-fisted leader who would annihilate all the archaic structures 
[of the PLO] and would unify the resistance army against the 
Zionist enemy.” 


FLN, Jamahiriyah Sides Agree During Talks 
LD182045 Algiers Domestic Service in French 1830 GMT 
18 Jan 85 


[Excerpts] The FLN Party delegation led by Mr Messaadia has 
ended its visit to Libya. It was a 3-day visit highlighted by 
political talks between the officials of the Permanent Secretariat 
of the Central Committee and Colonel al-Qadhdhafi. The talks 
centered on bilateral cooperation, its strengthening, and on Arab 
scene developments, particularly in the Maghreb. The two sides 
agreed on a certain number of issues of common interest. 


Al-Turayki Meets With PDRY Foreign Minister 
LD201520 Tripoli JANA in English 1419 GMT 20 Jan 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada Awal |, January 20, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Dr ‘Ali al-Turayki, secretary of the Gen- 
eral People’s Committee for Foreign Liaison Bureau, met last 
night with Dr ‘Abd al-‘Aziz al-Dali, foreign minister of the 
PDRY and the accompanying delegation. During the meeting 
discussions were focused on the bilateral relations and several 
other international issues of mutual concern. In addition the two 
officials discussed the smuggling operation of Ethiopian Jews 
(Falashas) to occupied Palestine with the assistance of Numayri's 
regime.
