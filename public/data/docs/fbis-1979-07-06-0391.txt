[Text] Commerce and Industry Minister Azzeddine Jassous has returned to Morocco after 

an official visit t the Soviet Union during which he signed a Moroccan-Soviet trade 
agreement. Jassous stated that the Soviet Union is considered one of the leading custom- 
ers and suppliers of Morocco, He added that the exchange between Morocco and the Soviet 
Union has tangibly developed, reaching a volume worth 30 billion centimes. In the fishing 
sector, the trade exchange has also registered noticeable growth. 


He said the recent Moroccan-Soviet trade agreement is automatically renewable every 5 
years. It also provides a legal framework for all subsequent agreements.
