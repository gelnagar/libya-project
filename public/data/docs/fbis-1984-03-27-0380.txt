LIBYA

Habre Forces Said Losing Control in Southern Chad 
LD270949 Tripoli JANA in English 0833 GMT 27 Mar 84 


[Text] Bardai, Jumada Thani 23, Mar 27, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Bardai radio said tension dominated the 
south of Chad as the forces of renegade Habre lost control of 
many towns and villages in the area to the forces of the legitimate 
united government headed by Oueddei 


The radio said in a report broadcasted yesterday forces of 
National Liberation Army launched an attack on Lombou, south 
of Chad, in which they destroyed 10 armoured trucks, killed 77 
soldiers and captured military equipment 


Meanwhile, green commandos of the Liberation Army launched 
an attack on (Al Douniyan), a big cotton market, and killed 7 of 
Habre’s soldiers. It also attacked Bakourou and (Nikaba) where 
it killed a number of rebel forces. 


The radio added that the liberation army also carried out attacks 
on Habre forces in (Bangjiyar Wet Logone) and inflicted heavy 
human and materia! damage on it 


‘Revolutionary Forces’ in UK Send Message to Leader 
LD262022 Tripoli JANA in English 1814 GMT 26 Mar 84 


[Text] London, Jumada Thani 22, March 26, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY The revolutionary forces in the British 
arena confirmed its commitment to spreading the Green Book 
thought and to confront people's enemies, wherever they are. 


They stressed in a cable sent to revolution leader that the 
revolutionary forces would finally settle the dispute in favour of 
the masses by removing obstacles hindering the people from 
carrying out the historical march, whereby it would possess 
authority, wealth and weapons. 


Omani Opposition Affirms Support for Al-Qadhdhafi 
LD251454 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
25 Mar 84 


[Text] The Popular Front for the Liberation of Oman has sent a 
cable to the brother leader of the revolution. It says: We are 
following with profound interest the revolutionary developments 
in the sister Arab Sudanese region, the great victories scored by 
the popular revolution in the southern Sudanese region, and the 
extensive popular uprisings threatening to topple forever Numay- 
ri’s regime in Khartoum. It is natural for the Camp David pact 
alliance to come to support this agen: regime after they found 
themselves in a bad position and after the huge defeat suffered 
by the Reagan administration and the NATO alliance in the 
Lebanese arena under the fierce blows of the Lebanese national 
movement and through the effective participation and backing 
by the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, Syria, and 
the Palestinian revolution. 


At the same time, we salute your huge revolution your great 
steadfastness, your firm and courageous stance by the side of the 
forces of the Arab and world revolutionary forces, and your 
challenge to world imperialism, Zionism, and reaction. We 
express to you in the name of the struggling Omani people and 
their revolution our condemnation and denunciation of the 
Numayri agent regime and his imperialist masters. We express 
to you our full and absolute support for your courageous stances 
against imperialism, Zionism, and their reactionary allies. The 
Great Al-Fatih Revolution has opened great prospects and a 
streadfast bastion for the Arab and world revolution. We in the 
Omani revolution and (?Omanis) of the Progressive National 
Movement affirm to you our stand shoulder to shoulder with you 
and our challenge together to the imperialist-reactionary assault 
for liberation and Arab unity of the struggling nation. March 
progressively forward. The Arab masses are with you until we 
achieve the goals of our Arab nation and its pan-Arab aspira- 
tions.
