LIBYA

"ASSASSINATION' OF CITIZEN IN UK PROTESTED 


LD220759 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 22 Aug 84 


[Text] Tripoli 22 Aug (JANA) -- A spokesman for the Popular Committee of the People's 
Bureau for Foreign Liaison has stated the following: 


Following the assassination of the Libyan Arab citizen 'Ali Muhammad al-Jahur in London 
on Monday 20 August 1984, the Popular Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign 
Liaison summoned the Italian charge d'affaires in his capacity as representative of 

the state looking after the British interests in Libya and expressed to him its protest 
and strong denunciation of the criminal act, the assassination of the Libyan citizen 
‘Ali Muhammad al-Jahbur, reaffirming that the circumstances in which this criminal act 
was carried out do not rule out the British authorities’ involvement and collusion. 

The Popular Committee of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison also warned against 
the consequences of any criminal act against the safety of the Libyan citizens who are 
in Britain and placed full responsibility for that on the British authorities.
