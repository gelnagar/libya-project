Summary (AI): A 1985 Washington Post piece (reprinted in a CIA reading-room file) says Morocco handed anti-Qaddafi exile Omar Muhayshi to Qaddafi, and that he was beaten to death on the airport runway in Tripoli after arriving. The article relies on US intelligence sources and is not firmly established.
• Muhayshi was reportedly offered a $1 million bounty by Qaddafi to an ex-CIA agent to arrange his murder, per the article.
• Article says Muhayshi was granted refuge in Morocco and that Libyan aid to Polisario guerrillas was cut off as part of the quid pro quo.
• Article says Egypt was the only reliably safe haven in the region for exiles.
• 1984-11: Muhayshi reportedly handed over by Morocco to Qaddafi as part of a deal over Libyan aid to Polisario; the article says he was killed soon after arriving in Tripoli (article says 'last year' relative to 1985).
• 1975-08-11: A plot by two Revolutionary Command Council members and 20 military officers was aborted; Muhayshi fled to Tunisia and most others were executed the following March.
