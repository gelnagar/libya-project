LIBYA

JANA EDITOR DENIES REPORT OF COUP ATTEMPT 


LD100908 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 10 Jan 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 10 Jan (JANA) -- The French television service TF last night carried 
a report claiming that a coup attempt had taken place against Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and 
that the Libyan leader had arrested his secretary. Some news agencies carried this 
report and attributed it to the SUDANESE NEWS AGENCY. 


JANA's political editor comments on this false report. He points out that it is not 
true and is without any foundation. The report comes within the courseof the black 
lies and campaigns waged by U.S. imperialism against Libya and spread by its agents 
and by the hired Western information media. He says this report is false and the 
fabrication of criminal intentions. 


The editor makes it clear that the climate and the soil for spreading these coup reports 
and the possibility of them occurring is not the Libyan climate and not Libyan soil. 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi is not a king or a president or a ruler so that a coup can take 
place against him; he is the leader of a revolution, inciter of it and maker of its 
thought. The Libyan people who took over ownership of power, resources and the weapons 
can not turn against themselves and squander their gains by handing over control of 
their affairs to a party, president or ruling group such as the corrupt group terrorizing 
Sudan and leading its people into a tragic situation of disorder and confusion. These 
Western information media could have carried a true picture of the situation in Sudan 
and showed the inhuman criminal ways imposed by the ruling regime in Sudan on the 
Sudanese people. But according to the customary collusion of these media with the agent 
regimes, they ignored the tragedy of the Sudanese people as they did and are doing with 
the tragedies of the struggling peoples in South Africa, El Salvador and Nicaragua and 
others. 


POLISH MINISTER ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 8 JAN 


LDO90900 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 9 Jan 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 9 Jan (JANA) -- Gen Tadeusz Hupalowski, member of the Polish Military 
Council for National Salvation and minister of administration, local economy and 
protection of the environment, arrived in Tripoli last night. 


Delivers Jaruzelski Letter 
LD091450 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 9 Jan 82 


[Text] The leader of the revolution today received Gen Tadeusz Hupalowski, member of 

the Polish Military Council for National Salvation and minister of administration, 

local economy and environmental protection of the socialist Polish Republic. The Polish 
minister handed a written letter from General Jaruzelski, chairman of the Polish Military 
Council for National Salvation, to the leader of the revolution, regarding the relations 
of cooperation and friendship between the two countries and the development of the 
situation in Poland following the thwarting of imperialist attempts aimed at the 
freedom of the Polish people.

General Jaruzelski expressed his satisfaction about the Polish [as heard] people's stand 
and said: I am addressing you in your capacity as a great leader and a friend of the 
Polish people. We highly value your supportive stand vis-a-vis the people of Poland 

which has been subjected to threats and a blockade supported by U.S. imperialism. General 
Jaruzelski added in his letter to the brother leader of the revolution: The Polish people 
are in need of your complete support and understanding of its current situation within 

the framework of the constantly strengthening relations of friendship and solidarity 
between the Arab Libyan and the Polish peoples. General Jaruzelski expressed his and the 
Polish people's gratitude for the supportive stand declared by the brother leader in his 
speech at the closing session of the General People's Congress, in which he asserted the 
Jamahiriyah's firm stand with the Polish people and condemned the economic blockade and 
the starvation policy practiced by America against Poland with the aim of subjugating 

it, and the Libyans' readiness to offer economic support in the form of urgent foodstuffs 
for the Polish people. 


Makes Departure Statement 
LD101422 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 10 Jan 82 


[Text] Gen Tadeusz Hupalowski, member of the Polish Military Council of National Salva- 
tion and minister of administration, local economy and environmental protection of the 
socialist Polish Republic, left Tripoli today following a visit to the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, during which he delivered to the brother leader of the revolution 
a written message from General Jaruzelski, chairman of the Polish Military Council of 
National Salvation, on cooperation between the two countries and the development of the 
situation in Poland. 


In a statement to JANA, the Polish member of the Military Council lauded the Jamahiriyah 
for its support toward the Polish people and for its assistance which emphasizes the 
depth of relations between the two peoples. He expressed his conviction that Polish-Arab 
Libyan relations will witness an expansion in various fields of cooperation. He added 
that his meeting with the leader of the revolution was held in a friendly atmosphere and 
that the results of his visit to the Jamahiriyah were positive. 


The Polish official accused the United States of being behind the events in Poland, 
affirming that the policy of creating internal sectarian strife, starvation and economic 
blockade is the basic means used by the U.S. administration to strike at progressive and 
socialist regimes. The situation in Poland gradually is returning to normal and the 
coming stage will see a new impetus in industry and the economy.
