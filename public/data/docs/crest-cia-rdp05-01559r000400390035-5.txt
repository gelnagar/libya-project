Summary (AI): A State Department cable from the Rome embassy reports on Qadhafi's October 7, 1983 speech in Misurata, in which he demanded that Italy pay double in colonial-era damages. The speech is not about Tripoli itself, so the relevance is background only.
• Qadhafi claimed approximately 40 billion dollars in damages from Italy.
• Italian ambassador in Libya was not present at the speech, according to newspapers and the Libyan Foreign Ministry.
• Press linked the tone to Libyan anger over Italian troops in the Lebanese MNF and planned US cruise missile installation in Sicily.
• 1983-10-07: Qadhafi gave a speech on the 14th anniversary of the expulsion of Italian residents from Libya, demanding Italy pay double in colonial-era damages and saying an open file now existed between the two governments.

SUBJECT: QADHAFI"S SPEECH IN MISURATA 

REF: ROME 23861 

1. (LOU) ITALIAN DAILIES OF 10/8 GIVE COVERAGE TO 
LIBYAN LEADER QADHAFI'S OCT. 7 SPEECH ON THE 14TH ANNI- 
VERSARY OF THE EXPULSION OF ITALIAN RESIDENTS IN LIBYA. 
FOLLOWING IS A PRECIS OF KEY REMARKS ON ITALIO-LIBYAN 
RELATIONS. WE SURMISE THE FULL TEXT WILL BE CARRIED ON 
FBIS. 

-- THE NEWLY INSTALLED SOCIALIST “GOVERNMENT IN ITALY 
SHOULD PAY DOUBLE IN ORDER TO RESOLVE THE PROBLEM OF 
DAMAGES COMMITTED AGAINST THE LIBYAN PEOPLE DURING THE 
COLONIAL PERIOD. THIS WOULD OPEN A NEW CHAPTER IN BI- 
LATERAL RELATIONS. 

-- AS OF 1983, ITALY HAS NEVER ADMITTED ITS RESPONSI- 
BILITIES FOR THE PAST. 

-- TWICE THE UNITED NATIONS HAS APPROVED RESOLUTIONS 
RECOGNIZING THE JUSTICE OF OUR CLAIMS.

-- "IF WE DON'T ARRIVE AT AN AGREEMENT WITH THE GOOD 
(SOCIALIST GOVERNMENT) ALONG THE LINES TRACED BY THE 
UNITED NATIONS, FAMILIES THAT HAVE LOST HUMAN LIVES AND 
GOODS CAN ACT IN THE MANNER THEY SEE FIT IN ORDER TO 
OBTAIN FROM ITALY THAT WHICH IS THEIR DUE. WE HOPE 
THINGS WON'T COME TO THIS POINT. STILL FROM THIS DAY ON, 
AN OPEN FILE EXISTS BETWEEN OUR TWO GOVERNMENTS AND WE 
WILL DO ALL POSSIBLE TO OBLIGE THE ITALIAN GOVERNMENT TO 
PAY A PRICE." 
2. (LOU) THE NEWSPAPERS JUDGE QADHAFI'S TONE PARTICULAR- 
LY HARD AND NOTE THAT HIS SPEECH WAS GIVEN IN MISURATA, 
WHERE AN IMPORTANT BATTLE TOOK PLACE IN 1923 BETWEEN 
LIBYANS AND ITALIANS. ‘THE PRESS ALSO NOTES THAT QADHAFI 
IS CLAIMING APPROXIMATELY 40 BILLION DOLLARS IN DAMAGES 
AND THAT THIS CLAIM, ALONG WITH LIBYAN UNHAPPINESS OVER 
THE PARTICIPATION OF ITALIAN TROOPS IN THE LEBANESE MNF, 
CLOSE TIES BETWEEN THE U.S: AND ITALY AND THE FORTHCOMING 
INSTALLATION OF CRUISE MISSILES IN SICILY, ADD FLAMES TO 
QADHAFI'S FIRE. 
3. (CONF) NEWSPAPERS REPORT, AND MFA CONFIRMS, THAT THE 
ITALIAN AMBASSADOR IN LIBYA WAS NOT PRESENT FOR THE 
SPEECH. MFA IS AWAITING AMBASSADOR QUARONI'S ANALYSIS 
OF THE SPEECH AND WILL PROVIDE US WITH A READOUT NEXT 
WEEK. RABB
