LIBYA

TRIPOLI WARNS OF POSSIBLE U.S. INTERVENTION IN IRAN 
LD150048 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 14 Nov 79 LD 
[Station commentary] 


[Excerpts] There is no doubt that U.S. imperialism, when it brought the deposed shah into 
its territory, must have realized the risks involved and the provocation it would cause to 
the Iranian Muslim people, whc adamantly demand that the shah be tried for his ugly crimes 
against the Iranian popular revolution for plundering the wealth of the Iranian people and 
for impeding their progress. 


Why did the United States deliberately welcome the deposed and internationally wanted shah 
when it must have realized that it would cause a popular reaction which might assume a 
violent form against U.S. interests? A reply to this question may be found in the U.S. 
design which aims at artificially creating circumstances and events which afford the best 
means for reestablishing U.S, influence, even by direct military intervention, in that 
vital and sensitive part of the world which embraces Iran and the Arab Gulf region, which 
is regarded the main oil source of the world. 


The deliberate action by the United States to welcome the deposed shah and to give him 
shelter when it knows very well this would be regarded as an affront to the dignity of the 
Muslim people of Iran, who might react violently against the U.S. interests, is no more than 
an attempt to create a justification for direct U.S, military intervention in Iran on the 
pretext of rescuing the 60 American hostages in the U.S, Embassy in Tehran and for ree 
establishing its domination over that oil-rich region, 


U.S. Defense Department sources announced that a number of U.S. naval units, including an 
aircraft carrier, yesterday headed toward the Arab Gulf area to hold maneuvers along with 
British naval units. Earlier in the week the Defense Department announced that it was 
carrying out intensive crash training of some infantry units and paratroopers in methods 
of attacking specific places and points in any state of the world. This confirms the 
aggressive intentions of the U.S, administration to intervene in Iran on the pretext of 
saving its embassy employees.

Anyone who has followed U.S. statements and actions throughout this year cannot fail to 
observe that the United States has threatened military intervention in that region on the 
grounds of protecting the oil supply, as was stated by Mr Brown. It coupled tts threats 
with an increased military presence in the Indian Ocean and the Arabian Sea, where it 
decided to establish a new fleet, using as its base the island of Diego Garcia, 


And so, if U.S. imperialism became precipitate and intervened militarily in Iran on the 
pretext of protecting its embassy employees, it would not only threaten the Iranian popular 
revolution but also Arab national security. his would of necessity require that the Arab 
nation and the Iranian Muslim people stand united in one trench of struggle against U.S. 
imperialism. 


The Arab Guif represents the Arab homeland's eastern wall, and at whe same time a bridge which 
links the Arab nation with the Iranian nation. This wall should be strong and impregnable 

so that it can repulse the attempts of foreign military intervention on the pretext of 
protecting Arab oil supplies to the United States and so that it becomes a protective shield 
for the Islamic popular revolution in Ira. 


U.S., UK GULF NAVAL EXERCISE SIGN OF IMPENDING ATTACK 
LD141915 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 14 Nov 79 LD 
{Text] The U.S. aircraft carrier Midway and seven other naval units of the U.S. fleet are 
proceeding toward the Arabian Gulf to take part in naval exercises jointly with Brit in. 
A spokesman of the U.S. Defense Department explained yesterday that the exercises would 
involve a mock battle. 
The exercises are considered a direct threat to the Arab nation and to world peace and see 
curity, confirming the imperialist role of the United States and its allies, which flouts 
the most elementary international conventions. Observers believe these maneuvers are a 
signal for U.S. preparations to mount an attack against the Arab oilfields, especially after 
the decision of the Islamic revolution in Iran to cut off oil to the United States.
