LIBYA

YUGOSLAV VICE PRESIDENT IKONIC ARRIVES 17 APRIL 


LD172130 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 17 Apr 82 


{[Text] Tripoli, 17 Apr (JANA) -- Branislav Ikonic, vice president of the Yugoslav Federal 
Executive Council, and an accompanying delegation, arrived in Tripoli today on a visit to 
the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


The Yugoslav deputy premier made a statement to a JANA correspondent stressing the deep, 
friendly relations between the two countries. He said: My visit comes within the framework 
of developing and consolidating relations between the jamahiriyah and Yugoslavia in all 
spheres. 
Economic Talks Held 

LD180904 Tripoli JANA in English 0829 GMT 18 Apr 82 
[Text] Tripoli, 18 Apr (JANA) -- The General People's Committee secretary of the economy 
and light industry, Mr Musa Abu Furaywah, yesterday conferred with the vice president of the 
Yugoslav Federal Executive Council, Mr Branislav Ikonic, and his accompanying delegation 
who arrived in Tripoli earlier yesterday. 
Discussions centred on methods of bolstering bilateral relations and the implemented parts 
of joint economic, social, technical and scientific cooperation agreements signed between 
both countries. 

Departs for Home 18 April 
LD182340 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 18 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Apr (JANA) -- Yugoslav Vice President Branislav Ikonic left Tripoli this 
evening at the end of his visit to the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


HUNGARIAN CONSTRUCTION MINISTER ARRIVES 16 APRIL 


LD171450 Tripoli JANA in English 0920 GMT 17 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 17 Apr (JANA) -- The Hungarian construction minister, Kalman Abraham, 
arrived in Tripoli yesterday noon on a visit to the jamahiriyah. 


HUNGARIAN MILITARY OFFICIALS SIGN PROTOCOL 


LD161424 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 16 Apr 82 


[Text] Libyan Arab-Hungarian talks were concluded last night in Tripoli. The final session 
was attended by Brig Gen Mustafa al-Kharrubi, chief of staff of the armed forces, and 

Istvan Olah, Politburo member, first deputy defense minister and Hungarian chief of staff, 
and their delegations. 


A protocol on bilateral cooperation was concluded in Tripoli yesterday. Brig Gen 
Mustafa al-Kharrubi and Istvan Olah signed the protocol. 


Istvan Olah and the accompanying delegation left Tripoli at noon today and were seen off at 
Tripoli Airport by Brig Gen Mustafa al-Kharrubi. 


TALKS HELD WITH HUNGARIAN HOUSING MINISTER 


LD180912 Tripoli JANA in English 0835 GMT 18 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 18 Apr (JANA) -- The General People's Committee secretary for housing, 
Engineer Muhammad al-Manqush, yesterday received the Hungarian construction minister, 

Dr. Kalman Abraham. Discussions centered on cooperation in construction and the participa- 
tion of Hungarian companies in the implementation of several projects in the jamahiriyah.

MALIAN FOREIGN MINISTER BRINGS MESSAGE 


AB161820 Nouakchott Domestic Service in French 1430 GMT 16 Apr 82 


[Text] Col Maayouia Ould Sid Ahmed Taya, member of the Military Council for National 
Salvation [CMSN], prime minister and minister of national defense, received in audience 

this morning Alioune Blondin Beye, the Malian minister of foreign affairs, who arrived 
yesterday evening in Nouakchott. Present at the audience were Mohamed El Moktar Ould Zamel, 
the minister of mines and energy and interim foreign and cooperation minister, as well 

as the ambassador of Mali to Nouakchott. At the end of the audience, the Malian minister 
stated that he came to deliver to the prime minister a message from President Moussa Traore 
addressed to Lt Col Mohamed Khouna Ould Haydala, the cahirman of the CMSN and head of state. 


[Begin recording] [Beye] I would first like to thank the prime minister for granting 

me this audience with so much affability. I came to deliver to him a message from President 
Moussa Traore addressed to President Khouna Ould Haydala now on official visit abroad. I 
had the opportunity to hold long discussions with him and had ample time to convey a 
fraternal message from President Moussa Traore, a message which, as is customary, 

concerns regular consultations on the historic ties between the two heads of state, 
bilateral issues and matters concerning the subregion and so on. 


[Question] Generally, how is cooperation among the Sahel countries developing? 


[Answer] Well, you know that cooperation between Mali and Mauitania has contributed much 
to it. Remember, just a few (?weeks) ago, the Mauritanian capital hosted a (?historic) 
summit which will henceforth go down in (?annals) as the summit of the organization of 
Sahelian countries. You are also aware that this offered another opportunity to the two 
heads of state to hold bilateral talks during which they examined ways and means to 


strengthen this cooperation. You also know that not very long ago, I myself was cochairman @ 
of the joint Mali-Mauritania commission which met here and reached important decisions for t 
development of this fruitful cooperation which is dear to our hearts. [end recording]
