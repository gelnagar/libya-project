Summary (AI): A 1976 Tripoli radio broadcast reports Qadhdhafi distributing 352 farm title deeds at Wadi Zamzam and giving a speech attacking party rule and calling for purges of "rightist" students in universities. A second item quotes the interior minister saying security forces are no longer an instrument of terror. Nothing here places a specific site in Tripoli.
• Qadhdhafi said enemies of the revolution were 'liquidated in fact by the masses' and that revolutionary committees of teachers and students were formed to control universities.
• Qadhdhafi said that Libya had no party or parliament and that a people's assembly could not be represented by 100 deputies.
• Interior Minister al-Humaydi said security forces are now 'no more an instrument of terror and suppression'.
• Libya repatriated Tunisian nationals in April 1976 in reply to Tunisian pressure, according to al-Humaydi.
• 1976-04-19: Qadhdhafi distributed title deeds to 352 farms to workers in the Wadi Zamzam agricultural projects.
