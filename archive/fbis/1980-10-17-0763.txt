LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI URGES CARTER, REAGAN TO REMOVE U.S. BASES 
LD162218 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 16 Oct 80 


[Text of cable from Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 1 September revolution, 
to U.S. President Jimmy Carter, Democratic Party candidate for the U.S. presidency, 
and Ronald Reagan, Republican Party candidate; date letter sent not given--read by 
announcer } 


[Text] We would have liked to maintain friendship with the United States, especially in 
view of the fact that our past is free of a colonialist background, contrary to the back- 
ground between the Arab nations and those countries that colonized parts of them such as 
France, Italy and Britain. As to America, there is no such colonialist background be- 
tween it and us. The strategic attempt at establishing friendship between the Arab 
nation and America started from the time that the late President Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir came 
to power. Indeed, Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir used to express the aspirations of the Arab nation 
but America wasted that chance and adopted unfriendly attitudes toward that initiative, 

a matter which led to the loss of this historic opportunity for friendship. 


Then ‘Abd an-Nasir turned to the Soviet Union and established a useful and respectable 
friendship with it free of colonialist ambitions and military bases. Proof of this lies 
in the fact that when Egypt, after ‘Abd an-Nasir, wanted to undo its relations with the 
Soviet Union, the matter was carried out with ease and the Soviet Union did not resist 
it, and we have not found any colonialist traces to this friendship in Egypt. 


Libya, while raising the pan-Arab banner after the death of Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir, was 
grateful for the fact that the evacuation of American forces from Libya was peaceful. 


We carried out another strategic initiative, following Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir's first 
strategy, with the objective of establishing friendship based on mutual respect between 
Libya and the United States and subsequently between the Arab nation and America. 


But America reacted to this initiative with the same hostile spirit with which it had 
reacted to Jamal ‘Abd an-Nasir and began to waste this initiative. It adopted several 
aggressive, unilateral measures against Libya, represented in a chain of various boy- 
cotts, and then adopted very hostile political and propaganda attitudes toward Libya. 
We find that the same American policy has pushed the positions of the United States and 
Libya and the Arab and the American nations to a very serious stage and America alone 
bears the responsibility for pushing matters to this point. 


American policy has brought us to a stage where there is no room for the possibility of 
submitting friendship initiatives as in the past. Therefore, we are now searching for 
the possibility of avoiding an armed confrontation between the Arabs and the Americans. 
This can be avoided only if the United States alone stops the military steps it has 
adopted, which threaten the independence of the Arab homeland, by withdrawing its bases 
from the island of Al-Masirah, Muscat and Oman, and Somalia, by withdrawing its listening 
and early warning aircraft from Saudi Arabia, by ending its occupation of Egypt and by 
removing its naval and air concentrations from the borders of Libya on the Mediterranean. 
Otherwise, an armed clash and the outbreak of war in its legal meaning will be possible 
at any moment because we have the national obligation of defending our Arab homeland and 
exercising the legitimate right to self-defense.

Should a war break out--and this is possible--it would be one imposed by the United 
States and it would be a war for the recovery of the independence of the Arab home- 
land and to repulse occupation from it, because we did not aggress against the United 
States; we did not fight Americans in their land; we did not establish bases for our- 
selves inside America; we did not violate the airspace of the United States; we did not 
fly our aircraft in its skies; we did not violate its territorial waters; and we did 
not harm the Arab territorial integrity; it is the one which violates Arab airspace and 
which flies armed aircraft in Arab airspace. 


According to these facts, we have the right to self-defense and we have all the legiti- 
mate justifications internationally, humanly and nationally for the defense of our ex- 
istence and independence and to fight the American forces on our Arab national soil. 


Political tricks cannot fool us as they fool the naive people you deal with and whose 
days are numbered, and with those like them with whom you have dealt in the past. You 
have not learned from the shah of Iran, Lon Nol, Idris as-Sanussi and Cao Ky. The 
result of this dealing led to their downfall on the one hand and America's loss on the 
other. The trick is that the Arab nation should be forced to pay the price of the super- 
powers' policies at the expense of its independence on such matters as the Afghan pro- 
blem, the problem of the Iraq-Iran war and the energy problem. All this is rejected. 


If America wants to confront the Soviet Union in Afghanistan, for instance, it should 
confront it directly. We shall not accept the Arab homeland becoming part of the bar- 
tering by which America wants to establish its new colonialist constitution and to cover 
up its colonialist ambitions in the Arab homeland. America should understand that Arab 
oil is for the Arabs and that it is the only source of wealth inside the barren desert 
in which they live. It is their sole source for living; it is the security for their 
foodstuffs. Their existence depends upon this wealth. Accordingly, the Arabs have every 
right to care for their sole source of living and to benefit from it to the utmost 
degree possible and to invest it to the optimum benefit, and consequently to die for its 
sake. However, America ignores this fact and regards Arab oil as a vital matter to U.S. 
security alone without understanding that it is a matter of life and death for the 
Arabs, its owners. 


If the United States suffered harm, as it says, as a result of how we dispose and control 
oil, then by the same token we too would be entitled to interfere with the right of the 
United States to use and control the atom as this is also a vital question and a source 
of danger to us. And if the use of oil by its owners represents a danger to others, the 
use of the atom also represents a danger to nonusers, and if the Arabs are not free to 
use their oil, they also have the right to say that America is not free to use the atom 
because it is a source of danger to them. The false and selfish logic of America should 
disappear for ever and should be replaced by reason and the recognition of equality among 
mankind and nations so that peace may prevail, which is more important, and so that the 
exchange of interests between nations will be based on consent and common benefit and 

not on usurpation and terror. 


The second matter is the American military protection of Israeli occupation in Palestine. 
Regarding this matter we would like to highlight the following facts: It is unreasonable 
for a superpower to adopt a biased attitude in favor of one of the sides in the Middle 
East region. For example, in the Zionist-Arab conflict the United States should have 
adopted a neutral attitude; rather, it should have stood by the oppressed and displaced 
Palestinian people. America cannot claim to be neutral at all.

There is no better indication of its bias than its enabling of the Israelis to occupy 
Arab land up to now; instead of seeing it stop arming them, we find it increasing its 
support with arms and all means of power every year in order to confirm their occupation 
of the Golan, part of Syria, the West Bank of Jordan, and Sinai and Gaza which are part 
of the Egyptian land, and enabling them by force of arms to work for the annihilation of 
the Palestinian and Lebanese peoples by means of raids by land, sea and air. 


From here I want to say that it is the duty of the Arabs--and they are forced to fight 

at any hour--to liberate their occupied land. All the time America protects the Israelis 
it is preventing the Arabs from liberating their land. Consequently, if the Arabs were 
able to recover any part of occupied Palestine, the United States would immediately 
intervene in this war considering this as occupation of Israeli land and ignoringthe fact that, 
after Palestine, the Israelis occupied new Arab land in 1967 and 1973 without anybody 
intervening to prevent this occupation. Naturally, an American military intervention, 
should it happen, will open the gates for the intervention of other foreign military 
forces from outside the region, a matter which will make us immediately face a third 
world war, responsibility for which will rest with the Israelis for their occupation of 
Palestine and other Arab territories, and with the Americans for their protection of this 
occupation and their intervention in all the dangerous fronts from Al-Masirah Island to 
Oman, Muscat, Somalia, Palestine, Egypt, Lebanon, the Gulf and the peninsula. By this, 
they will force the Arabs to fight them and we will be fighting on our soil American 
forces which have crossed the Atlantic and Indian Oceans, the Arabian Sea, the 
Mediterranean, the Red Sea and which have reached us. This, for-us, is legitimate and 
sacred defense. 


The legitimacy for this fighting, which will take place at any moment since it will be 
self-defense, is the reason whichwill not make ushesitate in calling for it and going 
through it even if this should lead to a second Vietnam in the world or to a third world 
war. The one responsible will be the aggressor--not the one that defends its existence 
in its land. With this clarification we see America at a crossroads at this period: 

it either takes the path of peace and withdraws its forces from the Arab homeland and 
from its maritime, aerial and territorial borders and leaves Arab oil to the Arabs and 
adopts neutrality in the Middle East, or it pursues the path of war and aggression. 


Out of love for peace and the desire to save the world and in order to clear our conscience 
we address this historic message to you. 


If you also love peace and care about saving the world we believe that your electoral 
program should rely on a new, peaceful U.S. policy based on these facts that we have 
mentioned and which you should take to the American Arabs, the Jewish Americans, the 
blacks and the whites of America and the red Indians, so that they can rally around you 
and give you their votes. Then we shall support you and stand by you. 


I believe that with this message I have provided you with a good service, a service which 
shows you matters as they are and from the point of view of the Arab nation and its 
revolutionary forces which now lead the battle for liberation, progress, dignity and 
unity and which will in the end triumph without doubt. Those who ignore it will be 
defeated. 


These facts may not have been clear to you in the past because America and its successive 
governments has dealt with the naive, the agents, the bribe-takers and the ignorant and 
has ignored the real, revolutionary force which has influenced the making of the future.

I believe this is the real cause of the false picture of the Arab homeland and which has 
made America (?do the forbidden) in its dealings with the Arab nation. 


Peace be upon the one who follows the right path. 


[Signed] Col Mu'ammar Al-Qadhdhafi
