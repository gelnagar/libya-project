LIBYA

LOME CONFERENCE STATEMENT, RESPONSES REPORTED 


LD152200 Tripoli JANA in English 1946 GMT 15 Jan 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 15 Jan (JANA)--African leaders present in Lome on the occasion of Togo's 
national day have issued a statement which reads in part: 


"The presidents have reviewed the events in Chad, particularly the agreement between the 
Jamahiriyah and the Government of National Unity in Chad concerning the establishment of 
unity between the two countries. Feeling that unity requires the consent of the peoples 
concerned, through their governmental apparatus, being concerned towards the increasing 
of tension and lack of security in the region, and also being concerned, on the other 
hand, towards the danger of military intervention in the region by foreign forces, they 
stress the following: 


1. The Lagos conference is appropriate to realize genuine and lasting peace and security 
in Chad which has sovereignty, independence and tranquility. 


2. They declare that the agreement of unity between the Socialist Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
and the Provisional Government of National Unity violates the spirit of the Lagos confer- 
ence. They stress that a government elected in complete freedom and democracy, and not 
the Provisional Government of National Unity, is the only party capable of commiting the 
Chadian nation to sign basic agreements on a wide scale. They ask the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and the Provisional Government of National Unity to regard the 
agreement as null and void and ask other sides which have forces and military advisors on 
Chadian territories to immediately withdraw them. 


3. All the member states of the Organization of African Unity, especially those adjoining 
Chad, are requested not to allow any foreign troops or Chadian rebels to use their soil 
as bases to attack the Republic of Chad. 


4. They have decided to dispatch a special commission, with the help of the United 
Nations, if need be, and also a peacekeeping African force, including Benin, Congo, Guinea 
and Togo which will be positioned on the entire Chadian soil and whose duty is to see 

to the implementation of the Organization of African Unity's resolutions. 


5. They send a warning that any attempts to hold elections without the supervision of 
the Organization of African Unity is futile. 


6. They delegate the secretary general of the Organization of African Unity to organize 
free and fair elections under the supervision and control of the organization before 
the end of April. 


Libyan Response 


LD151455 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 15 Jan 81 


[Statement issued by the People's Committee of the People's Bureau for External 
Liaison--no date given] 


[Text] The People's Bureau for External Liaison has examined the statement issued by 
certain African heads of state following consultations during their attendance at Togo's 
celebrations of its national day. The People's Bureau expresses its satisfaction with 
most of the paragraphs contained in the statement with the exception of paragraph 2 which 
is considered an unjustifiable interference in the affairs of Chad. 


V. 16 Jan 81 NORTH AFRICA 


The withdrawal of Libyan forces from Chad can only be requested by the party which asked 
for the entry of these forces. The Libyan forces entered Chad in accordance with a 
treaty and in accordance with a request by the president of the people of Chad, who has 
all the legitimacy because it was he who formed the Chad Liberation Movement--Frolinat— 
and it was he who led the liberation war for 20 years. It was he who, through armed 
struggle, reached the capital of Chad and overthrew the agent government of Maloum. 
Goukouni has all the legitimacy which enabled him to ask for the Libyan forces and to 
retain them. He alone can ask for the withdrawal of these forces. Libya will only 
respond to Goukouni's request and will withdraw its forces when requested. Goukouni 
QOueddei is the president of Chad and head of the Chad Liberation Movement. He has the 
revolutionary legitimacy and the lawful legitimacy that enables him to request the with- 
drawal of the Libyan forces after having performed their task with honor and bravery, 
after losing martyrs and after driving away and defeating the imperialist and reactionary 
attempts aimed at making Chad a base for aggression, and after participating in the 
achievement of security and stability in Chad and putting an end to the tragedies ex- 
perienced bythe Chadian people for many years. 


If Goukouni Oueddei, who waged a long war of liberation and is the one who represents 
the Chadian people, if he does not have the legitimacy, how then could the other African 
heads of state have authority and legitimacy? Many of the heads of African states were 
leaders of liberation movements similar to the Frolinat liberation movement. President 
Goukouni has the legal legitimacy and the revolutionary legitimacy which he derived from 
a lengthy struggle. 


The People's Bureau for External Liaison stresses that the political unity between the 

two countries will not be accomplished except after a referendum by the people of Chad, 
and that the statement issued by the African heads of state in Lome on the unity between 
the two countries is illogical and unlawful. The African heads of state failed to express 
(?such a view) when they discussed this topic. The OAU Charter urges the unity of the 
states of the continent and emphasiz*s that unity among African countries is a goal and 
efforts must be made to achieve it. How then could the African heads of state permit 
themselves to contradict and oppose what the charter stipulates and to oppose the aims 

of the OAU? If the African heads of state had not made such an error, it would have been 
possible to accept the statement. 


The People's Bureau for External Liaison expresses its satisfaction with the third 
paragraph of the statement in which the African heads of state acknowledge that Hissein 
Habre is a mere rebel rejected by Africa after he had been rejected by the people of Chad. 
The bureau also expresses its appreciation for the call made by the African heads of 
state to the neighboring countries of Chad not to permit exploitation of the territories 
of these countries as a base for any aggressive action directed against the people of 
Chad,

FOREIGN SECRETARY ‘'UBAYDI VISITS ETHIOPIA 


LD152252 Tripoli JANA in English 0920 GMT 15 Jan 81 


[Text] Addis Ababa, 15 Jan (JANA)--Abt al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, the secretary of the People's 
Committee and the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison, met in Addis Ababa Dr Kalk Jadel 
[as received], Ethiopia's foreign minister and member of the Central Committee of the 
Ethiopian People's Party. 


During this meeting, which the secretary of the Libyan Arab People's Committee at the 
People's Bureau in Ethiopia attended, discussions revolved around bilateral relations and 
adequate ways of increasing cooperation in the best interest of the two peoples. Al-'Ubaydi 
presented the Ethiopian minister with a thorough account of the democratic experiment the 
Libyan Arab people are conducting in the Jamahiriyah through the people's congresses 

and committees. He also clarified the purpose behind the transformation of the Jamahiriyah's 
Embassies abroad into popular and brotherly bureaus, run by people endeavouring to strengthen 
relations between all the peoples and rejecting all forms of traditional diplomacy, in 

accord with the spirit of the radical solutions put forward in the third universal theory. 


ARAB CONGRESS SECRETARIAT HOLDS PRESS CONFERENCE 


LD151656 Tripoli JANA in English 1440 GMT 15 Jan 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 15 Jan (JANA)--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 
said that the present ordinary session of the congress confirms the national progressive 
character of the organization. For the first time all Arab political forces and trade 
unions are taking part. 


The permanent secretariat pointed out at a press conference in Tripoli yesterday that the 
consolidation and understanding created among Arab forces and their agreement to one 
charter and programme in the struggle for national liberation, democracy and progress are 
directed against U.S. imperialist domination and bases which constitute a direct threat 
to the freedom and security of the region, as well as to Africa and the Mediterranean. 


The secretariat added that the Iraqi Ba'th Party and the right wing reactionary forces 

have failed in their attempts which were aimed at the Arab People's Congress. These forces 
fear the success of this organization which is a serious weapon in the Arab masses" hand 
against the interests of these forces. 


The permanent secretariat condemned French intervention in the internal affairs of African 
countries, especially Chad, which consolidates the interest of U.S. imperialsm in the 
region. The secretariat pointed to previous French intervention in the Western Sahara, 
Mauritania and Zaire, and the current naval, land and air attack against Central Africa. 


The permanent secretariat announced that a memorandum was sent to the Islamic countries’ 
foreign ministers conference which is to be held next Saturday in at Ta'if and Saudi Arabia 
[as received]. In this memorandum the secretariat elaborated on the repeated (Israeli)- 
U.S. backed aggressions against the Islamic holy places in Jerusalem. 


In the memorandum, the secretariat called for the practical application of the "jihad call." 
"Jihad" it said, cannot be experienced through statements and leaflets or theory or a 
neutral stand toward the Palestinian people and their enemies.

the Arab and Islamic assets from foreign banks and multi-national companies. 

The memorandum also pointed out the U.S. military presence in some Arab and Is!amic countries. 


JALLUD OPENS ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS SESSION 


LD161024 Tripoli JANA in English 0816 GMT 16 Jan 81 


{[Text] Tripoli, 16 Jan (JANA)--Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud opened yesterday 
morning the debates of the (?second ordinary) session of Arab People's Congress at the 
people's hallin Tripoli. Delegations representing vocational and trade union organizations 
Arab and other liberation movements, in addition to some friendly popular delegation are 
taking part in this session. 


Among those attending the opening session were ‘Ali Bilkhayr, the assistant secretary of 
the General Arab Congress, and ‘Abdallah Sahmul, the secretary of the people's committees 
affairs and assistant secretary of the People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison. 


The secretary general of the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab Peoples Congress stressed 
in his inaugural speech the importance of the meeting which came as the people's reply 
to the combination of humiliating circumstances that Arab nation was passing through. 


He said that the moral value of the conference symbolized the unity between the Arab masses 
and progressive forces in the Arab world, and that the realization of Arab unity as the 
ultimate aim which the Arab nation strived to achieve, stressing that the Arab People's 
Congress would continue its endeavours to achieve this unity in spite of all imperialist 
and reactionary attempts. 


He concluded his speech by saying that the congress coincided with the birthday of the 
immortal leader, ‘Abd al-Jamal an-Nasir, and hailing the Egyptian people in their struggle 
against the defeatist and puppet regime.
