Summary (AI): A 1981 New York Times article reports Secretary of State Alexander Haig saying the U.S. opposes Americans working for Libya's government but cannot stop it, since the U.S. is a free society. It also reports that American and Western pilots and technicians were recruited by a former CIA employee to fly and maintain Libyan aircraft, and to fly troops and supplies to Chad.
• Haig said Americans breaking the law would be prosecuted, but that taking a job for a large sum of money should not be affected by law where no law is violated.
• Reported recruitment of American and Western pilots and technicians by a former CIA employee to service Libyan Air Force planes.
• Reported flights of transport planes carrying Libyan troops and supplies to Chad.
• Haig testified before the House Foreign Affairs Committee; this was his first public comment on the matter.
