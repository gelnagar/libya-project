LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Holds Press Conference in Tripoli 
NC021312 Paris AFP in English 1244 GMT 2 May 84 


[Text] Tripoli, May 2 (AFP) Libyan leader Mu‘ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi warned countries sheltering “Libyan terrorists,” 
sought by Interpol at Libyan request, he will strike them “where 
it hurts.’ Each country has its “sensitive point,” he said. 


He added at a press conference, without giving details, that he 
would resume contact with the Irish Republican Army (IRA) 
fighting the British in northern Ireland, and warned against 


attempts to impose an economic embargo on Libya. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi also said the 9,000 Britons in Libya would 
not be affected by the crisis following Britain’s decision to break 
relations with Libya. But, he added, Britain would be responsible 
for what could happen to them. 


Col al-Qadhdhafi, speaking four days after Libyan diplomats 
returned here following the British decision, repeated charges 
against British police activities at the Libyan People’s Bureau in 
London. He accused them of planting the arms and explosives 
they said they found during a search of the Bureau — formerly 
the Embassy — on Monday. 


Britain broke diplomatic relations after the fata! shooting of a 
British policewoman and the wounding of 10 other people during 
a demonstration outside the Bureau on April 17. 


Col al-Qadhdhafi today denied any Libyan responsibility in the 
shooting, against opponents of his regime. Libya had earlier 
rejected British charges that the shots came from the Bureau, 
saying British police themselves were responsible. 


Col al-Qadhdhafi today warned European countries against 
yielding to U.S. pressure for economic measures against Libya, 
saying they should not make sacrifices in the name of the 
Americans. 


The Europeans “should not be dragged behind the Americans in 
a new world war,” he said. The first Soviet attack would be 
directed at Europe, he added, citing the deployment of U.S. 
missiles in Western Europe.
