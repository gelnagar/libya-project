LIBYA

PEOPLE'S COMMITTEE CALLS FOR ‘ALL-OUT WAR' 


LD111344 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1237 GMT 11 Jun 82 
[Statement issued by the general people's committee for foreign liaison on 11 June] 


{Text] Our Palestinian people and the Lebanese people are being subjected to a ferocious, 
barbaric attack, the aim of which is to kill and destroy them at a time when the UN 
Security Council and the major powers have been unable to check the treacherous aggression 
of the imperialists and Zionists. This aggression is directed against Syria's steadfast- 
ness in order to force it into capitulation and into handing Lebanon as a whole over to 
the forces opposed to the Arab nation, who are in alliance with the enemy. 


In view of this, the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah calls at this critical 
stage through which the Arab nation is passing and when its face is being decided for the 
holding of an urgent conference in Libya or at the Arab League headquarters to discuss 
one topic: how to resist and check the aggression. The meeting should be held within 

24 hours. 


[The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah also calls for transforming the war into 

an all-out war extending from the Gulf to the ocean; the placing of all military, economic 

and oil resources at the disposal of Syria, the [Palestinian] Resistance and the Nationalist Movement ; 
the rejection of any cease-fire until the Zionist enemy withdraws unconditionaily from Lebanon 

and the rejection of the Zionist enemy's humiliating conditions; and the proclamation 

of holy jihad and a popular liberation war. 


If the Arab rulers prove themselves unable to shoulder their historic responsibility 

in the face of this aggression and to check it, then the Arab masses have no alternative 
but to seize authority in order to face up to their destinies and to check the aggression 
themselves. 


The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah declares that all the resources of the 
Libyan people have been placed at the disposal of Syria, the Palestinian Resistance and 
the Lebanese Nationalist Movement. 


The Libyan people are prepared to join the holy war side by side with Syria, the [Palestinian] 
Resistance and the Lebanese Nationalist Movement. They affirm that the Arabs are capable of 
defeating the Zionist enemy in Lebanon and that the checking of this aggression will be 

the start of the liberation of Palestine. 


[fhe people's committee for foreign liaison greets the Syrian Steadfastness and Confrontation 
ront, the Palestinian Resistance and the Lebanese Nationalist Movement, 


LEBANON, SYRIA AGREE TO URGENT ARAB SUMMIT 
LD111254 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1237 GMT 11 Jun 82 


[Text] telephone contacts took place today between the commander of the revolution and 
Syrian President Hafiz al-Asad and Lebanese President Ilyas Sarkis. They agreed to 
holding an urgent summit conference. 


LD111352 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1237 GMT 11 Jun 82 


[Text] The People's Bureau for Foreign Liaison today summoned the ambassadors of the Arab 
countries in the jamahiriyah and spoke to them about the importance of holding an urgent 
summit conference to face up to the Zionist aggression on Lebanon and the Palestine 
Revolution as called for by the jamahiriyah today.
