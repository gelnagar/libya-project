LIBYA

JANA: CHAD HARASSES LIBYAN DIPLOMATS IN NDJAMENA 


LDO81744Y Triopoli JANA in English 0810 qmT 8 Jan 78 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, January 8, JANA--The Chad regime continues its harassment and provocations 
against the Jamahiriyah diplomatic mission in Chad, JANA learnt. The latest Chad provo- 
cation had been the searching of the diplomatic bags of the Jamahiriyah sent by the Foreign 
Secretariat in Tripoli to the Jamahiriyah Embassy in Ndjamena, the Chadian capital. The 
last suth search was made on January five in violation of all norms and rules of diplomatic 
conduct and political relations between states.
