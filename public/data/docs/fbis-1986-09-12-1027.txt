JANA: ‘Kneeling Husni’ Receives ‘Zionist Terrorist’ 
LD112351 Tripoli Voice af Greater Arab Homeland 
in Arabic 2200 GMT 11 Sep 86 


[Text] The Zionist terrorist Shim‘on Peres, the enemy's prime 
minister, arrived in Alexandria to attend his first meeting with 
the Egyptian president Kneeling [al-Barik] Husni. The Egyptian 
security authorities adopted stringent security measures in Alex- 
andria where the treason talks will take place. The aircraft of the 
terrorist Peres, the prime minister of the Zionist enemy, landed 
at a military airport near Alexandria. He then took a helicopter 
to[name indistinct] Palace, where the treason talks will take place 
between the hireling Egyptian regime and the Zionists. 


JANA’s Arab affairs editor commented on the meeting of trea- 
son, which is taking place in Egypt. He said: 


Yesterday Kneeling Husni subservicntiy received the terrorist 
Shim‘on Peres thereby to receive orders concerning his shameful 
role in the conspiracy against the sons of the Arab nation and in 
striking the freemen of Egypt who reject imperialist and Zionist 
hegemony.

{From the “Question and Answer” program presented by uniden-

[Excerpts] Brother Arabcitizens: This program is from you and 
for you. It is from you because it raises questions that you think

[Question] Who are the actual enemies of the Arabs? Please give 
a brief synopsis of the deteriorating Arab conditions, the political, 
economic, cultural, military and social conditions that are con- 
stantly changing. 

[Answer] Our great Arab homeland extends from the Ocean to 
the Arab Gulf, covers approximately 14 million square km, has 
a population of 180 million Arabs, and is rich in material and 
moral capabilities: oil, mineral wealth, livestock, plants, and 
cultural — a homeland that was the cradle of divine religions: 
Judaism, Christianity, and Islam. [passage omitted] 


Three enemies have rallied against this great Arab homeland to 
prevent the great Arab people from benefiting from these 
resources and building the great Arab humanitarian culture. 
This great Arab homeland constitutes a cultural nucleus for a 
new world force known as the Third World.
