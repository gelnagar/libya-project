LIBYA

Tripoli Reports Al-Qadhdhafi’s Arrival on Crete 
NC151123 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1105 GMT 
15 Nov 84 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution arrived this morning 
on the island of Crete on a visit to Greece in response to an 
invitation by Greek Prime Minister Andreas Papandreou. JANA 
has learned that during the visit a meeting will take place 
between the brother leader of the revolution and French Presi- 
dent Francois Mitterrand in the presence of the Greek prime 
minister. Meanwhile, French President Mitterrand and Greek 
prime minister Andreas Papandreou arrived on the island of 
Crete. 


SFRY’s Ljubicic Ends Visit, Seen Off by Jabir 
LD141805 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1700 GMT 
14 Nov 84 


[Excerpt] Major General Nikola Ljubicic, member of the 
Presidency of the Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia, left 
Tripoi this evening following a visit of several days to the Socialist 
People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis 
Jabir, commander-in-chief of the Libyan forces, was at the 
forefront of those who saw him off at Tripoli international 
airport.
