LIBYA

"ARAFAT, AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESS RALLY FOR PALESTINE 


‘Arafat Address 
LD142330 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1915 GMT 14 May 81 


[Speech by Chairman of the PLO Executive Committee Yasir ‘Arafat at a rally held in 
Tripoli to commemorate the 15 May 1948 anniversary of the “usurpation of Palestine"--live] 


[Excerpts] In the name of God, the merciful, the compassionate. Brother Colonel 
Mu'ammar [applause], brothers, [chants] comrade leaders of the Palestinian revolution 
[applause], my people, oh fraternal and heroic Libyan people: [applause] 


15 May 81 Q 2 NORTH AFRICA 


It is by no means a coincidence, dear brothers, that we hold this meeting on the 15 May 
anniversary, this painful anniversary, but we the revolution of the Palestinians know 
through the muzzle of the gun carried by this fighter in the Palestinian revolution 
[applause] what the meaning of this anniversary is--not an anniversary of (?sadness) but 
an anniversary of a covenent, the anniversary of an oath we make before the masses of 
our Arab nation [applause] that this revolution shall continue its struggle and [word 
indistinct] carrying its rifle until the liberation of Palestine [applause], until we 
raise this flag, the flag of the Palestinian revolution, over the hills of Jerusalem 

and say that Jerusalem is Arab and shall remain so. [applause] 


Yes, dear brothers, there shall be no capitulation [words indistinct] but the ones who 
have the right to say to us [word indistinct] are those heroes in the bastion of 
(?steadfastness) in southern Lebanon [applause]. No to capitulation and no to kneeling! 
From here we declare full mobilization for the Palestinian revolution, and all should 
‘oin the Palestinian revolution. [applause and chants] 


Yes it pleases me to hear this slogan and how much it pleases me to hear this slogan, 
this chanting repeated, but how much wounded Palestine smiles when the rifles are 
raised and hands are raised carrying rifles. [applause] Yes, from here [words 
indistinct] general mobilization to include all Palestinians [chants], and every 
Palestinian who does not respond to this general mobilization is negligent. Yes, dear 
brothers, I say this at this time, dear brothers, and I hope that we will be a little 
above chanting--yes to chanting, because it moves the masses--but not through chanting 
alone will Palestine be liberated. [applause] 


I say, dear brothers, at this [words indistinct] we must know the meaning of the 
imperialist attack, the meaning of the American attack in this region. It is by no 
means a coincidence in our region that one (?finds) in the Mediterranean the 6th Fleet, 
in the Red Sea, the Gulf, in the Indian Ocean the 5th and [word indistinct] and the 7th 
Fleet. The Rapid Deployment Force has been announced. This talk is by no means a 
coincidence, brothers. This (?role) is planned. America is planning [chants against 


the United States] to impose its domination over this region, brothers. And when I say 
this is a bad time for Arabs this is not being poetic and [words indistinct]. We must 
admit that America and the Zionist enemy were able to bring down the fortified Egyptian 
bastion through As-Sadat's capitulation. The big Egyptian bastion fell through 
As-Sadat's capitulation. We must know what it means to provide facilities and bases 

at Ra's Banas in Egypt. [applause] 


But I know this Egyptian people, which has provided 86,000 martyrs and wounded men since 
1948 in the defense of Palestine and the Arab nation, [applause] 


Now we must know this fact, then move to what is happening in the Sudan, and then to 
what is happening in Somalia. At Berbera there is an American base, at [words indis- 
tinct] on Masirah island in Oman. In addition to the facilities, I can give figures, 
and not in a (?demagogic manner). Listen, in addition, to the facilities granted in 
the Gulf to the 5th and the 7th Fleets, which were joined later, after Carter's 
announcement, by units of the British fleet, We must know these facts. 


America is the head of terrorism in the world, the organized international terrorism 
which is now happening. What is now happening in southern Lebanon? What is now 
happening in southern Lebanon at the hands of this Zionist enemy, which uses the most 
modern American weapons--including those banned internationally? This is not called 
{word indistinct] in the dictionary, brothers. It is called international [words 
indistinct] carried out by the [words indistinct]. This is the real terror. We must 
understand that the tons of bombs dropped on Palestinian camps and Lebanese cities and 
villages is terror--is official, organized terror.
