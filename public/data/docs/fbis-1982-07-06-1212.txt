LIBYA

AL-QADHBHAFI CABLES PALESTINIAN LEADERS 


LD040030 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 3 Jul 82 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution yesterday [3 July] sent a telegram to the 
leaders of the Palestinian Resistance, the text of which follows: 


V. 6 Jul 82 Q2 


Brother leaders of the gallant Palestinian Resistance: 

It is disgraceful and unacceptable at any price to give the enemy the right to negotiate 
over our existence, whether in Beirut or in Lebanon as a whole, because this encroachment — 
if it is allowed to take place -- will give the enemy the right to hunt you down anywhere 

in the world. 


Whatever means of tyranny the United States might use to strengthen the Israelis, the latter 
are not entitled to do what they are doing today. It is nothing but aggression. I advise 
you to commit suicide rather than to accept disgrace. Your suicide will immortalize the 
cause of Palestine for future generations. Your blood is the fuel of the revolution, which 
has become inevitable from the sea to the Gulf. 


Negotiations can take place only between you and the Lebanese people. In the present situa- 
tion it is unacceptable that it take place under Israeli occupation. The priority today 

is to fight the enemy until he unconditionally leaves all of Lebanon. Let suicide be the 
priority; it is the road to victory. What we hear today, despite all the circumstances, is 
the ultimate in farce and disgrace. 


There is a decision which, if taken by you, no one can prevent. It is the decision to die. 
Let this be. "If a wound hath touched you, be sure a similar wound hath touched the others" 
[Koranic verse]. They are suffering like you are. Disgrace, maledictions and dishonor to 

the Arab rulers — agents of Zionism and slaves of the United States. After this moment, 

one ought to talk to their (?wives), for they might prove to be better. Libya places all 

its resources at the disposal of Syria and the resistance. Immortality to the martyrs of the 
Palestinian people and the Muslims of Lebanon. Glory be to the rifle. The struggle continues. 


[Signed] Your brother, Col Muammar al-Qadhdhafi 
‘Arafat Replies 


LD060048 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 5 Jul 82 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has received a cable from Brother Yasir ‘Arafat, 
chairman of the PLO Executive Committee. Brother ‘Arafat's cable is a reply sent by the 
leader of the revolution to the leaders of the Palestinian Resistance. 


In his cable, Brother ‘Arafat confirmed the steadfastness of the Palestinian Revolution and 
the forces of the Lebanese Nationalist Movement in the face of the barbaric Zionist- 
American invasion which is backed by Arab reaction. Brother ‘Arafat explained that this 
invasion is aimed at the elimination of the Palestinian Resistance. 


Brother ‘Arafat said the world bears witness to the decision which the Palestinian 
Resistance has made. He said the world also bears witness to the bravery and steadfastness 
of the heroes besieged by the forces of invasion. He said the invasion has the unlimited 
support of the American administration. Brother ‘Arafat said this American support has 
reached the point of controlling Arab information as we have not heard of a single demon- 
stration from the ocean to the Gulf except for those taking place in our own occupied land. 


Brother ‘Arafat added in his message to the leader: No one has responded to this American- 
Zionist siege to which the Palestinians and the Lebanese are subjected. Those steadfast 
Palestinian heroes and the heroes of the Nationalist Movement expected -- while waging the 
longest Arab war against Zionism -- that at least the aircraft of the Arab nation would fly 
to cover their sky, protect their women and children and lift the siege around them in this 
advanced trench of our Arab nation. He warned that should this trench fall -- God forbid; 
it will not fall; it will stand fast and triumph -- Arab regimes would fall after it. 


V. 6 Jul 82 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


Finally Brother ‘Arafat confirmed the steadfastness of the Palestinian Resistance in 
the face of the forces of the Zionist invasion and expressed his confidence in the triumph 
of the Palestinian people against the Zionist enemy. ; 


LIAISON BUREAU STATEMENT ON AT-TA'IF RESULTS 


LD031718 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 3 Jul 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 3 Jul (JANA) -- The People's Foreign Liaison Bureau has issued a 
statement about the results produced by the meetings of the six-member committee set up 
by the emergency meeting of the Arab League Council [as received]. Here is the text of 
the statement: 


The People's Foreign Liaison Bureau has studied the statement of the six-member committee 
set up by the emergency meeting of the Arab League Council which met in At-Ta'if, the 
condemnation of the Zionist invasion and the call to contact influential countries, foremost 
the United States, on the issue. In order to comment on this statement and its contents 
which appeared in a manner indicating weakness and retreat, the People's Foreign Liaison 
Bureau notes the following: 


1. Instead of starting to plan to repulse the invasion and move immediately to liberate 
the land and defend our dignity, the aforementioned six-member committee was satisfied 
with condemning this invasion and merely expressing its sympathies with the Palestinian 
people. By such a posture the committee has not exceeded the traditional formulas which 
the United Nations and other international organizations have customarily used in 
addressing international public opinion, formulas which contain terms of condemnation, 
indictment, denunciation and disapproval. Being satisfied with formulating slogans 
indicting the Zionist invasion the committee confirms its feebleness and inability to 

do anything. Hence its actions resulted in the epitome of this feebleness: resolutions 
which are utterly ridiculous. 


2. The decisions to contact America, first strategic ally of the Zionist enemy, the 
direct partner in the invasion and sole defender of this enemy at the United Nations, 
confirms the extent of the Saudi rulers’ domination over and ability to direct the work 

of this committee to serve the aggression's plans which aim at imposing American influence 
on the Arab region, liquidating the Palestine question and browbeating the rejectionist 
Arab countries and forcing them to accept capitulationist solutions. 


3. The People's Committee of the People's Foreign Liaison Bureau which considers America 
a direct party to the aggression against the Arab nation and the prime instigator of the 
barbaric, brutal Zionist invasion against Lebanon, believes that the only language to be 
used with the United States is total boycott and striking at its vital interests wherever 
they may be. 


4. The People's Foreign Liaison Bureau considers that a committee which produces 
resolutions of such a caliber can only express the links and directives which control 
the policies of Al Sa'‘ud. 


We challenge the Saudi rulers who, with all their oil wealth and huge financial deposits, 
raise false, horse-trading and misleading slogans, to shift even one of the enemy's tanks 
from its position inside sister Lebanon where the resistance is beleagured and where 
innocent children and women are being killed daily at the hands of the Zionist invaders.
