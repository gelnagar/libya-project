Summary (AI): A January 1986 State Department cable from the US embassy in Sanaa reports that Qaddafi phoned Yemeni President Salih on January 5, 1986, and that Salih reaffirmed Yemen's support for Libya under the joint Arab defense agreement. The cable adds that Salih was reportedly seeking Libyan financial aid to replace Saudi promises that had not been delivered.
• Al-Thawra's January 6 edition carried the report of the call
• Salih reportedly sought Libyan financial aid to replace Saudi assistance promised but not provided over three years
• The newspaper article was described as intended for the consumption of the Libyan embassy
• Gaddafi's call was reportedly his third to Sanaa in one week
• 1986-01-05: Qaddafi telephoned Yemeni President Salih; Al-Thawra reported Salih confirmed Yemen's support for Libya against threats it faces
