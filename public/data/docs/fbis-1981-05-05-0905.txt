LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI VIEWS 'COLONIALIST, ZIONIST ADVANCE' 


LD050330 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 231 MT 4 May 81 


[Text] At his meeting yesterday [4 May] with cadets of the military academies, the 
brother leader referred to the grave deterioration which the Arab homeland is experiencing. 
He pointed out that if this deterioration continues, the Arab nation will suffer a fatel 
defeat similar to that which the American Indians suffered after their land was invaded 
by the Europeans. Arab regimes are contributing to the onset of such a catastrophe by 
their dreadful capitulation to the imperialist onslaught. America has set up a strategic 
umbrella under which the Israelis are advancing with the aim of establishing an empire 

to replace the Arab nation. The Israelis are proceeding to realize their dreams. They 
have made Jerusalem their capital, have taken over most of the water sources and are on 
their way to the oil wells. There is no barrier between the enemy and this region. The 
enemy has occupied most of the rivers and America has reached strategic areas such as the 
Suez Canal, the Red Sea, the Bab el-Mandes and Al-‘Aqabah. Moreover, the Palestinian 
resistance has lost the Jordanian arena, adjacent to the occupied territory. 


The leader pointed out that the West Bank was once outside the occupation, but is now 
being annexed to the occupied territory. Moreover, we also find Iraq, with all its 
military and economic power, fighting the Iranian revolution on behalf of America. 


The brother leader said that in Lebanon the enemy wants to create a fait accompli of 
Israeli domination. Israeli planes are daily entering Lebanese airspace without any 
regard to the United Nations or the Arab nation. He added that Lebanon is headed for 
Israeli domination. South Lebanon is already under Israeli domination, and the Palestinian 
resistance, having been liquidated in Jordan, is now being destroyed in Lebanon. What 
is taking place in Lebanon is tantamount to the besieging of Syria. A plot is being 
hatched against Syria; the battle is getting fiercer in the direction of Syria. All the 
military operations are designed to reach Syria, as Lebanon does not constitute any 
obstacle to the Israelis. The leader stressed that there is great concern for the 
Palestinians, as they are confined to a limited area at a time when their forces should 
be deployed all along the Arab confrontation line with the enemy. 


The leader pointed out that the downfall of Syria would be a disxster for the Arab 
nation. He noted that the United States is building bridgeheads everywhere. It has 
established bases in Egypt, Sudan, Somalia and Oman. U.S. forces, he said, are advancing 
without any opposition. The leader added: We have to launch a counterattack on all 
fronts and by all methods.

We also have to speed up the completion of unity between Syria and Libya, as this is a 
strategic step and a historic action; it will be the foundation stone for the building 
of the anti-imperialist attack force and for the defense cf what remains of the Arab 
homeland and the existence of the Arab nation. We must also work for unity of action 
with Algeria. For the first ime a counteraction is necessary to confront a real danger 
threatening the existence of che Arab nation. 


The Arabs’ capitulation before the colonialist and Zionist advance, he added, encourages 
the enemy to go ahead with i-s plan for the creation of a Zionist empire. Therefore, 
all material support must be given to and volunteers must join the Somali national 
deliverence front, as its battle is part of the Arab confrontation battle with the U.S. 
forces. The Omani liberation movement must be supported, so as to give impetus to the 
revolution there. The Polisario must be supported, so as to expand the scope of the 
revolution and make it difficult for the enemy to carry out its plans. The Palestinian 
revolution must be supported and given all potentials. The Palestinian resistance must 
accept volunteers. The Arabs must volunteer to join the resistance so that it will con- 
stitute a threat to the Zionist settlements. The Lebanese National Movement must be 
supported and supplied with volunteers and arms so that it can become the strongest group 
in the Lebanese arena, in support of the Palestinian resistance. The Steadfastness and 
Confrontation Front must also be expanded; it must take the offensive and embrance all 
Arab forces which stand up to the enemy. 


The leader stressed that the military in the Jamahiriyah is different from those of the 
traditional regimes. The traditional military defends causes which our military does 
not. If the former knew the goals of these causes, it might refuse to die for them. In 
other words, the traditional military has no real cause for which to die. If the 
political regimes which they defend protect exploitation, then all the potentials of 
their respective countries are utilized in service of the forces of exploitation of the 
masses. The local forces of exploitation are linked with the international forces of 
exploitation. The leader pointed out that in the jamahiri regime, where government ends, 
there is no instrument that rules and utilizes the army for its own goals. This is be- 
cause the people have replaced it; the people have the right to utilize everything for 
the service of the masses. The military man in the jamahiri regime must have a high 
degree of awareness of all issues. Traditional militarism has disappeared and been re- 
placed by the armed people. The military must be aware of political issues; conscription 
cannot be exercised against them as is the case in the traditional and reactionary 
regimes. 


USSR SAID TO PLEDGE MORE SUPPORT FOR SYRIA, LIBYA 


LD041428 Kuwait KUNA in Arabic 1330 GMT 4 May 81 


[Text] Beirut, 4 May (KUNA)--The Libyan leader, Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, has obtained a 
Soviet pledge for increased support for his country and Syria after he affirmed to the 
Soviets that Libya and Syria were determined to fight Israel with or without Soviet help, 
the Lebanese newspaper AS-SAFIR reports today, adding that the pledge was obtained 
during Al-Qadhdhafi's recent visit to the USSR, where he had political talks with the 
Soviet leaders concerning developments in the Middle East. 


AS-SAFIR also reports that "the Libyan leader returned home after reaching agreement 
with the Soviet leaders on vital strategic matters of importance to both countries." 
However, the paper does not give any further details on these matters and confines it- 
self to saying that "there was a general feeling of the necd for an offensive policy 
instead of the defensive policy which had enabled the Egyptian President Anwar as-Sadat 
to allow the establishment of American bases in his country and offer to join NATO and 
amass troops along the borders with Libya." 


"Al-Qadhdhafi's visit to Moscow," the paper writes, "was on behalf of the Steadfastness 
and Confrontation Front, which includes Syria, Libya, Algeria, the PDRY and the PLO."

AS-SAFIR, which is described as being close to Libya, also says that the results of 
Al-Qadhdhafi's visit "will become evident shortly." According to the Lebanese paper, 
Al-Qadhdhafi has affirmed that his country will confront "the American-Israeli war in 
defense of the Arab nation with or without Soviet help." 


Al-Qadhdhafi asked the Soviet leaders to define their "exact" position on the minimum 
support the Arabs can rely upon in their war against Israel.
