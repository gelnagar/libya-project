Summary (AI): This is a June 1986 White House memo scheduling a Crisis Pre-Planning Group meeting on Libya to review political and economic steps against Libya, with an agenda covering intelligence on internal stability, terrorism, diplomatic efforts, and economic sanctions. It names the participating officials but does not describe any site in Tripoli.
• Agenda items: intelligence update on Libya's internal situation and intentions for further terrorist activity; status of allied anti-terrorism efforts; allied cooperation on economic sanctions.
• Economic sanctions agenda included the June 30 deadline, a refined product ban, a residual supplier initiative (Libyan oil boycott), and follow-up of Tokyo Summit initiatives such as closing People's bureaus.
