MOROCC( 
MINISTER, AIDES INSPECT CONDITIONS IN TAN TAN 
LDO11824 Rabat Domestic Service in Arabic 1300 GMT 1 Feb 79 LD 


[Excerpt] Mr Mohamed Benhima, minister of state in charge of the interior, returned 
from Tan Tan yesterday, after spending a full day there. He contacted the inhabitants 
and the authorities who accorded his excellency an enthusiastic welcome. The whole popu- 
lation of Tan Tan highly appreciated his majesty's gesture of sending the minister of 
State and a number of his aides to inspect conditions in the town and to contact its 
inhabitants after the treacherous attack on it. 


At a rally there the minister of state conveyed his majesty's concern and the smypathy 

of the Moroccan people to the inhabitants, and also the condemnation by public opinion of 
the treacherous attack Tan Tan's inhabitants had been exposed to. The crowds in Tan Tan 
prayed for the innocent victims--martyrs of the aggression. The committee, led by the 
minister, mentioned the losses sustained by the citizens and the town, and took all the 
necessary on-the-spot measures to face the situation resulting from the aggression. 


COMMENTARY ON RELATIONS WITH ALGERIA REPORTED 
LDO11334% Rabat MAP in English 1209 GMT 1 Feb 79 LD 


[Text] Rabat, Feb 1 (MAP)--Moroccan Radio and Television (RTM) broadcast here Wednesday 
the following commentary: 


"There are two contradictory attitudes which prevail in the Maghreb. There is the atti- 
tude of aggression against the sovereignty and the Moroccan territory. [sentence as 
received] All possible means have been deployed to worsen the situation. Attacks have 
been committed against the Moroccan territory with the use of modern and :ntensive arms. 
Morocco is accused of parachuting arms and exploding bombs in Algerian tervitory while 
Algeria is trying to turn public opinion against Mcrocco, even at the prics of causing 
grave divisions within the Arab nation and the African Continent. That is the first 
attitude," stated RTM, 


"The second is that of the people who have faith in the integrity of its territory, 
which was recovered in peace and legitimacy. These people are constantly in the defen- 
sive position being made to face a continued offensive. Their policy is of moderation, 
avoiding all that may aggravate the conflict, proclaiming its will for peace and re- 
conciliation,” added RTM, 


"These are the two attitudes present, it was clearly stated in the communique published 
by the Council of Ministers. Morocco continues to adhere to the positive factors of 
the crisis, avoiding mishaps which would prevent peace. Morocco for its part is doing 
everything in its power to avoid conflicts that would create irreparable damage," con- 
cluded RTM,
