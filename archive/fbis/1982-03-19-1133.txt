LIBYA

U.S. COULD PROVOKE BLOODSHED IN GULF OF SIDRA 


LD19114 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0015 GMT 19 Mar 82 
[Station commentary] 


[Excerpts] The special forces, the revolutionary forces within the Arab nation, have the 
duty to incite the Arab popular masses to rise in a popular revolution so that these masses 
May attain authority. Otherwise, the end will be grave and tragic. 


Could there be a worse end than the anninilation of the Arab entity and the destruction 
of the Arab nationalism so that scums of the earth would come with their foreign entity to 
replace the Arabs and Zionism would replace Arab nationalism? 


The United States and the imperialist forces have decided to bolster the Zionist enemy not 
only to consolidate his occupation of the Arab territories occupied to date but also to 
undermine Arab nationalism. Thus, the aggression is directed against the Arab land and 
Arab nationalism alike. 


In face of this, the old glories would be useless in checking the aggression. Unless new 
glories are achieved and they stand up in the face of the present aggression, the aggressors 
will attain their objectives against Arab nationalism.

Should our enemy succeed this time in defeating us, it will be difficult to redress the 
situation. For this reason, the revolutionary forces in the Arab nation should save 

the Arab nation from suffering another defeat and should drive the Arab masses into 
fighting a battle in which triumph over the enemy would be guaranteed because it would be 
based on the solid ground of Arab unity which devotes all capabilities of the Arab nation 
for the fateful war against the forces laying in wait for the Arabs with a view to 
annihilating them. 


The United States has most arrogantly announced that it will stage war maneuvers in Arab 
territorial waters, waters which belong to an Arab country and constitute geographically, 
historically, naturally and legally an extension of its territorities. The United States 
would not have gone this far in taking the Arabs so lightly had it seen the Arabs united 

as one hand and one striking force. It would not have gone blusteringin this manner against 
the Arab homeland and would not have aligned itself so strongly with the Zionist enemy had 
it known that its interests in the Arab worla would be harmed. 


However, it is now the duty of the Arab revolutionary forces to tell America with the 
language of armed forces that the arrogant American provocation will lead to a third world 
war because the Libyan Arab people, the free people who possess their authority, wealth 
and arms and who lead the special pan-Arab unitary progressive force of the Arab nation, 
have decided to turn the Gulf of Sidra into a red gulf with the blood of the invaders and 
their own blood if the U.S. administration, in its folly, should go to the extent of pro- 
voling the Libyan Jamahiriyah people within their territorial waters. 


The revolutionary force, the force of life and the future of the Arab nation, should pre- 
pare itself henceforth for fighting decisive battles in defense of the Arab existence 
and Arab dignity, for the liberation of the occupied Arab territory and for ending the 
imperialist expansionist ambitions. 

U.S. Maneuvers Denounced 


LD182332 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 18 Mar 82 


{From the "Reading in the News" program] 


[Excerpts] The announcement by the U.S. secretary of the navy that the U.S. 6th Fleet 
will be staging maneuvers in the Gulf of Sidra during the next few months has aroused wide- 
scale denunciation in all Arab and international spheres. 


This provocative terroristic announcement by the U.S. administration against the Arab 
Libyan people comes as a continuation to the terrorism U.S.-imperialsim has been pursuing 
against the jamahiriyah in an attempt to force the Great 1 September Revolution and its 
leadership to abandon their fight against U.S. imperialism and to stop exposing its 

plots and designs against the Arab homeland, the progressive forces and the liberation 
movements of the world. 


The U.S. administration has begun to devote all its time to hatching plots against the 
jamahiriyah as the brother commander of the revolution stated in his speech at the last 
extraordinary session of the General People's Congress, when he said the head of the U.S. 
administration Reagan has nothing on his mind other than Libya and that it is his 

main preoccupation. The U.S. National Security Council meets only to discuss Libya and 
Reagan speaks of nothing but Libya.

IRAN PLEDGES SUPPORT AGAINST U.S. AGGRESSION 


LD181524 Tripoli JANA in English 1430 GMT 16 Mar 82 


[Text] Tehran, 18 Mar (JANA) -- Mr. ‘Ali Tabataba'i, spokesman for the Iranian Foreign 
Office, this morning announced that Iran pledges support for her brothers in the 
jamahiriyah against the expected U.S. military aggressive move in the Libyan Gulf of Sidra. 
The spokesman told our correspondent that Iran strongly denounces the U.S. military 
manoeuvres in the Libyan territorial waters and considers them as hostile policy aims to 
subjugate the countries of the Mediterranean basin. 


AL-QADHDHAFI ASKS KREISKY TO MEDIATE WITH U.S, 


PM191023 London AL-=MAJALLAH in Arabic 20-26 Mar 82 p 10 


[From the "AL-MAJALLAH correspondents" feature] 


[Excerpt] Informed diplomatic sources in Vienna have confirmed to AL~MAJALLAH that 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi has asked Austrian Chancellor Kreisky to mediate between him and the 
United States and a number of West European states, Al-Qadhdhafi's move is seen as an 
attempt to break the Libyan regime's international isolation. 


The sources say this is not the first time that Al-Qadhdhafi has sought mediation between 
him and the United States. He previously asked Yugoslavia, Turkey and other states to 
mediate but these mediations failed and Libya's relations with the United States remain 
tense as a result of the colonel's policies and actions aimed at destabilizing the Middle 
East and Africa, 


The sources also say that Kreisky will mediate although he already knows that his mediation 
will fail.

COLONEL AL-QADHDHAFI MEETS WITH STUDENTS 18 MAR 


LD182300 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 18 Mar 82 


[Text] Within the framework of his tour to enhance the movement of the revolutionary 
committees, the brother commander today met with the students of all schools and secondary 
educational institutions in the areas of Al Khums and (Zlaytah). 


During his meetings with the students, the commander affirmed the need to widen the base 
of the revolutionary committees to bring in new revolutionary forces and thus widen the 
movement of the revolutionary committees and bolster their role. 


He also affirmed the importance of forming a pan-Arab revolutionary wide movement with 

the task of inciting the popular masses in the Arab homeland and staging a popular revolution 
to settle the battle once and for all in the interest of the masses that are absent from 

the battle and whose absence has enable the Zionist enemy to advance in the Arab arena 
without meeting any resistance, supported in his advance by American imperialism which has 
been using the reactionary agent regimes as a bridge over which the enemy passes and 

advances through the Arab arena. 


The brother commander said the reactionary regimes have proven their bankruptcy. They 
have enabled the Zionist enemy to advance and penetrate internally without doing anything 
to check his advance and penetration. He also warned of a genuine threat to the Arab 
nation. 


The brother commander affirmed the need for the masses to assume authority so that they 
can check the enemy and keep the threat away from this nation. He said the value of life 
lies in man's enjoyment of true freedom with a high sense of morality, affirming the 
freedom of the masses. 


The commander laid on the shoulders of the revolutionary forces in the Arab homeland the 
task of blowing up all the tragedies and contradictions that cloud the skies of the Arab 
homeland, stressing the Arab nation is a great nation and the farce taking place is not 
taking place with its free will but through the rigging of its will. This nation, he said, 
needs someone to revive its will. In this regard, the commander affirmed the role of the 
revolutionary committees in the Arab arena. He pointed out that the popular masses in the 
Arab homeland have alone endured losses because of the actions of the agent reactionary 
regimes and the advance and penetration of the enemy with the support of the United States. 
The commander said 150 million Arabs are absent from the battle and are relying on a handful 
of regular soldiers. 


In his meetings with the students, the commander affirmed the role of the Great 1 September 
Revolution. He said Libya is the springboard for the revolutionary work; it is the 
staunch Arab fortress and the Arab center for bolstering the movement of the Arab man.
