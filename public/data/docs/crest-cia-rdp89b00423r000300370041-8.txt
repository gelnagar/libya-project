SUBJECT: Libyan Activities in North Africa — 

1. Attached is a brief rundown of recent Libyan activities in 
North Africa as you requested. It was compiled by DDI/NESA. I would 
just like to add a few additional thoughts on this problem. 

2. I am increasingly disturbed at the continuing pace of 
destabilizing events which stem from Qadhafi. Fortunately the majority 
of his actions are usually poorly conceived and have limited affect. 
Nonetheless it is a constant irritant and a continuing threat to the 
general stability of the region. 

3. As in the case of Syria, I doubt that the Soviets are 
specifically directing Qadhafi's activities--and indeed many of them the 
Soviets do not even like. But Qadhafi remains a card for the Soviets--a 
foothold--and the destabilizing effects of his activities can and do 
serve Soviet interests in a general sense. Qadhafi, of course, pays 
cash for the Soviet arms he gets, and it is his oil money which enables 
him to carry out such an ambitious program.
