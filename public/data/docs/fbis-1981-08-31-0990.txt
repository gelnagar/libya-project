Ak-Qadhdhafi Speech 28 Aug 
LD282218 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1808 GMT 28 Aug 81 


[Speech by Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi at the 28 August opening session of the international 
conference of solidarity with the Libyan people in Tripoli--live] 


[Text] Gentlemen, it gives me pleasure to stand up here to salute the delegates of more 
than 80 states of the international community who have come here today in support of and 


in solidarity with the Libyan people. I welcome the delegates of more than 245 international 
organizations. 


Your solidarity with the Libyan Arab people means that the Libyan people are on the 

side of right and that their enemies are on the side of wrong. It means that America is 
unjust and that it has transgressed against the Libyan people. It means that its agents 
in occupied Palestine, Egypt, Sudan and elsewhere are unjust and are aggressors against 
the Libyan people and other nations of the area. 


The convening of this international conference of representatives of nations, governments, 
political bodies and leading figures in the world who are famous in politics, culture 
and scientific research is an international testimonial to the Libyan people by those 
nations, governments, political bodies and independent leading personalities in the world. 


Gentlemen, this international conference of solidarity is the strongest proof and testimony 
and the greatest evidence of the justice of the policy that is followed by the Libyan 


people under the banner of the revolution and of the broad support from all the corners 


of the world for the unchanging and courageous stand of the Libyan people toward colonialism, 
Zionism and hireling reaction. 


Gentlemen, this international conference of solidarity with the Libyan people is the international and 
practical reply to the false accusations that the filthy imperialists and their agents 
have levied against this nation. Gentlemen, what excuse is left for the imperialists or 
their agents--the enemies of the Libyan people--after this international conference in 
support of the Libyan people, held in the name of the governments, peoples and parties 

of the world and their important personalities? It seems that what is left for the 
imperialists is either to be ashamed of themselves or to face international disgrace. 
Through this international conference in support of the Libyan people against the United 
States--the head of international terrorism and the source of aggression--the governments 
of Egypt and Sudan and the Zionists who have become CIA officials--the imperialists will 
(2?be convicted) before the international community. 


The Libyan people are satisfied to prove through this international conference and its 
resolutions, before their enemies and before the entire world, that their policy is right 
and prudent. You have provided them with support thit is more powerful than firearms, 
which the enemies have provided for use against their enemies, and this support means 
that international public opinion is on the side of the Libyan people. There is no doubt 
that this is so, because the Libyan people exercise the right of self-defense. They 

are thus repelling the repeated cold and hot attacks against them. You realize that the 
Libyan people's victory is a victoryfor the forces of peace, freedom and justice. It is 
an assertion of the right of peace, freedom and justice. It is an assertion of the 

right of the small nations to have a place in the sun, to enjoy stability and respect in 
the world.

Through this conference, gentlemen, you are supporting the Libyan people's policy, which 

is hostile to imperialism everywhere and which is hostile to Zionism and reaction. The 
Libyan people, with this support, will continue without hesitation along the path that 

is hostile to imperialism, reaction and Zionism and all forms of racial discrimination. 

While reaffirming to their enemies the correctness of their views and the vindication 

that is provided by this international support for them, they will counter the black 
propaganda that the aggressors use to justify their aggression. Through your support, 

it has become obvious to all that the Libyan people's revolutionary line is anti-imperialist, 
antiracist and antireactionary. It is the line that is supported by all the peace-loving 
and freedom-loving forces in the world. 


The aggression that is directed against the Libyan people under the leadership of America 
is a true threat to the security of the nations that follow a democratic, peaceful and 
independent policy. The imperialists do not recognize the independence of any nation. 

But they try to dominate nations, and they are not (?deterred) except by force. The 

Libyan people want to regain their national wealth in order to spend it on their increasing 
needs and to participate in aiding the poor nations. The poor nations are grateful to 

the Libyan people for their participation in the international funds and banks that are 
devoted to helping the underdeveloped nations. The Libyan people want to have absolute 
sovereignty over all their material and moral resources in order to establish through 

free will a truly democratic system based on direct popular power. The Libyan people 

want to live in a climate of security, stability and peace so that they can direct all 
their efforts toward a peaceful transformation and progress. In view of this international 
support by the forces that are hostile to imperialism racism and hireling reaction, the 
Libyan people will undertake to double the struggle against these enemies. When these 
forces display solidarity with the Libyan people, they do so in order to champion all the 
issues of the nations that are exposed to imperialist tyranny and whose existence is 
threatened inside their own borders. 


The victory of the just cause of the Libyan people is a victory for all just causes, and 
it leads to strengthening international peace and repelling armed expansion and the 
(20pen) interference to subvert from within. The just cause of the Libyan people is this: 
They should live in freedom, peace and stability inside their land, air and sea borders 
and build, progress and be peacefully transformed into a new jamahiri society. They 
should place their material and moral resources in the service of the just struggle 
everywhere so that freedom and peace may triumph. [applause] 


This important international conference, which includes the living forces in the world 
that are famous and occupy a prominent place, the forces that are custodians of the 
civilization of man and his immortal values, such as freedom, peace, respect and progress, 
the forces that have strength to defend these high ideals, the forces that are courageous 
in resisting imperialism, racism and hireling reaction--this international conference also 
includes delegates and spokesmen of the international liberation movements, the freedom- 
loving nations, international political bodies and important personalities. It includes 
delegates from well over 80 states and from more than 245 international political 
organizations and is capable of having an effective influence over international 

politics and of exposing the truth about imperialism, racism and raaction. It is 

capable of resisting them and of bringing about their defeat. 


Gentlemen, we now have to establish a permanent structure for this solidarity. We should 
now engage in a counterbattle against the insolent attack of imperialism and its tools 

in the world against the Libyan people and the otiier peoples of the world who are victims 
of aggression, such as the people of El Salvador, Chile, Namibia, Angola, Palestine, 
Lebanon, South Africa, Egypt, Sudan, Somalia, Oman and Ireland.

We must intensify the fight against the U.S. Government and work to undermine its military 
bases in West Europe, Greece and Turkey aad to prevent it from establishing new bases in 
Egypt, Sudan and Somalia. We must work to clear the Mediterranean and the Indian Ocean 
of the fleets belonging to the imperialist powers. 


Gentlemen, we now have to surge forward to liberate the nations in the United Nations 
organization by moving the headquarters of this organization from the U.S. capital [as 
heard] and to persuade all the nations that are united in that international organization 
to hasten to deprive the United States of the right of veto. It has become a sword 
hanging over the people's legitimate rights [applause] whenever anything is submitted to 
the Security Council. It has become a weapon and has been added to the arsenal that 
America presents to the Zionist camp for use in the destruction of Palestinian camps and 
Lebanese towns. It is used to promote the continuing occupation of Arab land, to terrorize 
the nations of the Middle East and to deny them of progress. This has happened to the 
gallant Syrian people and it has happened in Iraq [applause]. The United States must one 
day face the call for its expulsion from the United Nations and its banishment as an 
outlaw and a member that is suffering from power madness. [applause] 


Some governments have placed themselves in the service of the filthy and irresponsible 
plots of the ClA--such as the hireling government of Egypt, which lacks legality, continues 
to dominate the Egyptian people with the strength of its U.S. backing and the overt and 
covert persecution of the Egyptian people and allied itself with evil Zionism and sold 
itself to the devil; the Sudanese Government, which is so enthusiastic about imperialism 
and Zionism, from whom it has been seeking assistance in its last hours of survival; the 
Government of Somalia, which is swaying under the strikes of the National Salvation Front; 
and the governments of El Salvador and Chile. We must work on an international level 

to indict these base and hireling governments for their crimes, to expose them and to 
deprive them of any dealings and to establish a practical alliance with their peoples in 
order that they may get rid of them. [applause] 


Gentlemen, we should show solidarity with the Egyptian people against neo-Nazism [applause], 
with the Sudanese people against As-Sadat [applause], with the Somali people against 

Ja'far Numayri [applause], with the Somali people against fascism and even with the 
American people themselves against Zionist containment. [applause] We should show 
solidarity with the persecuted races, such as red Indians and black Americans. [applause] 


Gentlemen, in accordance with the declaration of people's power issued by the popular 
congresses in March 1977, in which the Libyan people have committed themselves to defend 
freedom everywhere and thereby rise to the level of the international support for them, 
the Libyan people will, without hesitation, honor your request if you ask them to let the 
Jamahiriyah be a center for international resistance against imperialist colonialism, 
hireling reaction and racism in all its forms. [applause and chanting] I do not doubt 
that they will bear this internationalist responsibility with courage and dignity. 


Peace be with you. [applause and chanting]

and we shall fight even better in the future. 


Report on 30 Aug Speeches 
LD310825 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0745 GMT 31 Aug 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 31 Aug (JANA)--The international conference for solidarity with the 
Jamahiriyah people resumed its meetings yesterday evening and heard speeches from the 
heads of the following organizations: The Peronist movement of Argentina; the African 
Trade Unions organization; the Venezuelan socialist forces; the Greek trade unions and 
associations; the El Salvador liberation front; the Socialist International organization; 
the Portuguese Communist Party; and Uruguayan organizations. 


The speakers expressed support for the commander of the revolution's call in his opening 
speech for the establishment of a center to fight the imperialist, reactionary and racist 
forces whatever their forms and for the removal of the United Nations [headquarters] from 
the United States. They also denounced the serious American imperialist stand vis-a-vis 
the just causes of the peoples struggling for freedom and independence and stressed the 
need to purge the Mediterranean and the Pacific of American imperialist bases.

Message to Angola 
LD301520 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 30 Aug 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 30 Aug (JANA)--The international conference for solidarity with the 
Jamahiriyah people sent a message to Jose Eduardo dos Santos, chairman of the Labor 
Party of the Popular Movement for the Liberation of Angola and president of the People's 
Republic of Angola [PRA]. The following is the text of the message: 


The Secretariat of the International Conference for Solidarity With the Arab People and 
Their Central Cause--Palestine, which is convening in Tripoli on 27 August~--the eve of 

the opening of the international conference for solidarity with the Jamahiriyah people-- 
condemns the serious escalation, aggression and attempts to occupy the southern regions 

of the PRA by the racist forces of the racial discrimination regime of South Africa with the approval 
of the U.S. Administration of Reagan. It strongly denounces this invasion and open aggression 
on the borders and sovereignty of an independent state, the PRA, and this disregard and 
flouting of the UN Charter and resolutions, coming on the celebration of Namibia day--a 

fact which makes it a double defiance of world public opinion and international unity. This 
invasion and aggression threatens Southern Africa with an all-out war and poses a threat to 
world peace. 


We affirm our solidarity with the unarmed and innocent victims of aggression of the 
apartheid regime in Angola and Namibia. We express our full admiration and support for 
the peace-loving people of Angola and their revolutionaries and for the Labor Party of 
the Angolan popular liberation movement in their firm stand against the treacherous 
aggression of the racist regime of South Africa and in support of the just struggle of 
the Namibian people for national independence. 


The secretariat stresses the importance and serious significance of facing up to the 
current imperialist aggression, led by the United States in the form of concentrated 
military operations against the peoples in Angola, Southern Africa, Libya, Lebanon, 
Palestine, the Middle East and Latin America. 


We affirm the growing cooperation and solidarity of all the world anti-imperialist 
national democratic forces, as well as the support of the international conference for 
solidarity with the Jamahiriyah Arab people and the international African conference 
which will be held in Luanda from 6 to 9 September. 


The secretariat appeals to all anti-imperialist and democratic forces represented by 

the Tripoli conference and all world peace forces to act quickly offering moral, politi- 
cal and military support for the PRA, and thereby support freedom peace and justice in 
the world. 


[Signed] The International Secretariat for Solidarity With Arab People and Their Central 
Cause--Palestine
