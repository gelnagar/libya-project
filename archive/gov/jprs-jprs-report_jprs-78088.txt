LIBYA FINANCES COMPLEX FOR HANDICAPPED 
Beirut AL=<SAFIR in Arabic 18 Mar 81 p ® 


[Article: “Some $4 Million From the Jamahiriyah for the ‘Complex of the 
September Revolution for the Development of Human Capabilities'") 


[Text] The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has offered $4 million 

to the Institutions for Social Care in Lebanon (Islamic Orphanage) for the pur- 
pose of constructing the “Complex of the September Revolution for the Development 
of Human Capabilities.” This complex will be constructed in the ‘Armun shrine 
area and will consist of four specialized institutions for the blind, deaf, 
physically handicapped and mentally retarded. A complex for the development of 
human capabilities operated by these institutions is located in the same area. 


To commemorate the occasion, the directorship of the institutions held a celebra- 
tion at its headquarters at 1030 yesterday worning. Present at the celebration 
were the secretary of the Office of the Arab Brotherhood, Salih al-Daruqi; 
Peoples Committee members ‘Abd-al-Qadir Ghawqah and Ramadan al-Samu'i; president 
of the Committee for Care, Dr Fu'ad al-Bazgari, along with members Sami al-Huss, 
Muhammad al-Jarudi, Sa'd-al-Din Farukh, Mahoud Qamuriyah, Dr Bashir Bilani and 
Dr Bahii Tabbarah; general director of the Institutions for Care, Muhammad 
Barakat; and a sumber of employees and administrators. 

















The celebration took place in a hall at the center o° which was a three-dimensional 
model of the complex, which was designed by Engineer Rabi' ‘Ammash. Mr Barakat 
gave an overview of the complex, which will include four institutions specializing 
in different types of handicaps. Each institution will be independent with respect 
to its education
