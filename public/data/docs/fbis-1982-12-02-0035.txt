FORMER CHADIAN MINISTER SCORES GENEVA CONFERENCE 


LD020830 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 2 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 2 Dec (JANA) -- (Mohamed Nour Adam), minister of state for foreign 
affairs in the Transitional National Union Government in Chad, has strongly condemned 
"the Geneva conference," which was held under false and feeble slogans. He stressed 
that the aim of this conference, in which representatives from the Reagan administra- 
tion, the French Government, and from certain Arab reactionary regimes took part, is 
to collect funds to help Hissein Habre and save him from falling, and to purchase 
weapons to kill the Chadian people who reject him. 


In a statement to reporters last night, he said that the Transitional National Union 
Government had lodged a formal protest with the UN secretary general against this con- 
ference, which was held under the veil of contributing to the rebuilding of Chad. 


The Chadian minister of state for foreign affairs has stressed again that the only 
legitimate government the Chadian people support and recognize is the Transitional 
National Union Government led by Goukouni Oueddei, and which was formed from all 11 
Chadian factions under the supervision of the Organization of African Unity (OAU) and 
in accordance with the resolutions of the Nairobi summit. 


He added that Hissein Habre does not represent the legitimate government in Chad and 
had no popular ground. On this basis, the OAU has refused to accredit him as the law- 
ful president of Chad. He said that the Transitional National Union Government con- 
tinues to enjoy the support and recognition of the overwhelming majority of the 
African states and the international community in view of the legitimacy of this 
government. 


He stressed in his statement that the occupation of the capital by Hissein Habre 
represents nothing as far as the people of Chad and their legitimate government are 
concerned, pointing out that Habre's control does not go beyond Ndjamena, and this 
occupation is due to the military backing he is receiving from imperialism and Arab 
reaction, particularly the regime of the lackey Numayri. 


He explained to the reporters in his statement that the legitimate government in Chad 
controls more than two-thirds of the country and the Chadian people have intensified 
their resistance to Hissein Habre, the fact that made him spread and disseminate 
unfounded rumors. 


The minister of state for foreign affairs of Chad has repeated that Habre, who usurped 
power in the country with the help of the imperialist circles, does not control any- 
thing at present but a mountain of sand, and that the forces of the Transitional 
National Union Government are in control of the northern, southern, and central parts 
of Chad. Ndjamena is surrounded from all sides by the forces of the legitimate govern- 
ment and the revolutionaries of the Chadian people. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES GHANAIAN LEADER'S ENVOY 


LD251750 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 25 Nov 82 


[Text] This morning, the brother leader of the revolution received Mr Paul V. Obeng, 
coordinating secretary of the Ghanaian Provisional National Defense Council [PNDC] and 
personal envoy of Flt Lt Jerry Rawlings, chairman of the PNDC. 


V. 2 Dec 82 Q2 NORTH AFRICA 


Mr Obeng handed a private message to the brother leader from Flt Lt Jerry Rawlings in 
which the latter explained the current situation in Ghana and the conspiracies hatched 
by imperialism against the Ghanaian revolution and the Ghanaian people. 


The meeting was attended by the minister of foreign affairs and oil in Ghana [as heard], 
the Ghanaian ambassador to the Jamahiriyah, and the secretary of the Arab Libyan 
People's Bureau in Ghana.
