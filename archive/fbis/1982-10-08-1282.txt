LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI DELIVERS ‘VENGEANCE DAY' SPEECH 


LDO72208 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1715 GMT 7 Oct 82 


[Speech by Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi at a "Vengeance Day" rally in Al-Jabal al-Akhdar -- live] 


[Excerpt] Those [Libyans abroad] who repent are welcomed back; those who do not repent 

and continue to wage war against the Libyan people [words indistinct]. It is our right to 
chase them everywhere. If America [words indistinct], to Hell with America. We do not 
recognize America [word indistinct], not its culture, and we consider it the devil on earth. 
It must be resisted, and we are working to resist America. [words indistinct]. 


We are now in a fierce fight with America in Somalia. America wants to turn this African 
Muslim Arab country into an American base from which it would fight and surround the Arab 
countries and control Bab al-Mandeb and the Red Sea and threaten Mecca and Medina. The 

oil in the Arabian Peninsula would be at the mercy of America when Somalia falls under 
American imperialism and influence. The Somali people, under the leadership of the 
Democratic Salvation Front, are now waging war against America and the agents of America 

in Somalia. They want to liberate Somalia before the American bases are established. We 
are now fighting with all our resources with the Democratic Salvation Front against America. 
We are with the Democratic Salvation Front, and America is with Siad Barre. [chanting] 


If the work done by the revolutionary committees in exposing the enemies of the Libyan 
people who openly joined the enemy [words indistinct] in America that it is a terrorist act. 
The Libyan people should prove that they are responsible for that act and for any similar 
act that eliminates the enemies of the Libyan people abroad. They should also propose 

that the people's conferences hold open discussion on this matter, so that America can 

hear with us now the ambassadors of 44 states who write reports to their governments saying 
that this matter -- the elimination of Libyan people's enemies abroad -- should be discussed 
by all people's conferences, which encompass the entire Libyan people. America will hear 
if the Libyan people decide to eliminate their enemies abroad. If anybody declares war 
against the Libyan people, it is the right of Libyan people, in self-defense, to declare 
war. If you are fighting us in Europe and America, how do you expect us not to fight you 

in [word indistinct]? They receive money from America, destroy Libya [words indistinct]. 

Do they expect that their Libyan people would not fight them? They will fight them in 
America and even on Mars, as I have told you. [words indistinct] 


We must eliminate them. From now on, this cannot be restricted to the revolutionary 
committees alone. The revolutionary committees are only one of the tools. Every Libyan 

who travels abroad -- if he is a Libyan loyal to Libyan soil -- wants Libya to be free, 

wants the authority to be in his hands. He is then responsible for the elimination of 

its enemies wherever they are. Whether you are having treatment or are a tourist or are 
visiting or studying, you are part of the Libyan people, which this act is against 

Therefore, you are fighting everywhere. This should be presented to the people's conferences 
for open discussion, so that America will hear that the Libyan people are entirely resolute 
about eliminating their adversaries, who get money from America, and are laughing at 

America. So far America has not been able to find anybody that supports it inside Libya. 


Then America will judge for itself and say that the Libyan people are content. 


V. 8 Oct 82 Q 2 NORTH AFRICA 


As long as the revolutionary committees, the people's conferences and the entire Libyan 
people decide a matter such as this, what can America sa to the world? Would it say 

the Libyan people are content? Naturally it does not hesitate. This socially, culturally 
and humanly decayed country, however benevolent it was, will never say the people are 
content. Why -- because it judged the Palestinian people as content. 


America refused to negotiate with the PLO, which even America's agents the Saudis and the 
Moroccans acknowledge is the sole legitimate representative of the Palestinian people. Even 
the dog Philip Habib, who travels inthe Middle East like adog, refuses to negotiate with 
the Palestinians. Why? Because, naturally, his origin is Lebanese, and he is an agent 
of America. Why does an agent of America refuse to negotiate with the Palestinians? 
Because America does not acknowledge the Palestinians, and it considers them terrorists. 
An entire people, giving sacrifices every day for the sake of their occupied land -- the 
whole world admits that in 1948 Palestine was completely occupied by the Zionists -- 

has been deprived since that day and are fighting to return to their land. America 

says this is terrorism -- a people fights for a sacred cause, a people with no piece 

of land on which to live, fighting to gain a piece of land [word indistinct]. America 
says this is terrorism. [Words indistinct] we do not bother at all with any utterance 
from America. If it says they are terrorists, this means they are on the right path. 

As long as it says the revolutionary committees are terrorist, it means that the revo- 
lutionary committees are terrorist, it means that the revolutionary committees are the 
real revolutionary forces that shape civilization, support freedom and incite the masses 
for the final emancipation, because America fears the peoples and their control. 


The peoples themselves made America lose Iran. The awakening of the Iranian people 
themselves deprived America of that large sphere of influence and deprived it of its 
first agent, the shah of Iran. The awakening of the peoples in Ethiopia itself deprived 
America and made it lose Ethiopia, on which America depended in Africa, and made it lose 
its first agent, Haile Selassie. The awakening of the peoples in Southeast Asia -- in 
Vietnam and Kampuchea -- humbled America and forced it leave these countries. The 
peoples marched on it. The Americans left their huge aircraft, 130 aircraft, at the 
airport, and the American soldiers fled. The Vietnamese people sold America's aircraft 
at auction in Vietnam. These were the American aircraft the soldiers left, leaving 

even their [words indistinct]. It is not a novelty, (?with bare feet) they came to 

the shore for the warships to pick them up. This is fact. 


This is a fact and a disgrace for America. [Words indistinct] peoples are the real 
enemy of America, and for this reason America takes under its patronage anyone working 
against the people. A Libyan fugitive agent goes to it saying: I am working against 
the Libyan people. 


What is the power of the people? What are the popular congresses? The popular committees? 
The revolutionary committees? What is the Jamahiriyah? Wealth and arms are in the 

hands of the people! America does not accept these things. In other words, the people 
become strong, a people of the Arab nation become armed, having wealth, power. If this 
process grows from one people to another, the Arab nation will become master of its wealth, 
weapons and power. The arab nation would then say no to America, no to Israel. 

[Words indistinct] anyone that destroys the will of any people is taken under American 
patronage. 


JANA Report 


LD062324 Tripoli JANA in English 2000 GMT 7 Oct 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulhijja 20, Oct 7, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Colonel Mu'ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi leader of the Great Al-Fateh Revolution addressed the grand rally held tonight 
in the village of Omar Mukhtar, at Al-Jabal al-Akhdar municipality in celebration of 

the 12th anniversary of the expulsion of the remnants of Italian fascists. 


V. 8 Oct 82 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi started his speech by greeting the father of martyrs the hero Omar 
Mukhtar, who fought against the colonialists and mobilized the jihad movement throughout 
Libyan soil. He said: "In every part of the free Jamahiriyah, we decided today to 
commemorate ‘Vengeance Day’. And we come to this particular place as an expression of 

our gratitude to the historic role played by Omar Mukhtar in resisting colonialism. 

While celebrating ‘Vengeance Day’, the day when we drove the last Italian occupationist 
fascists out of Libyan soil, we have to acknowledge the role of Omar Mukhtar who polarized 
the jihad movement all over Libyan soil and take him as a good example to follow not in 
command and leadership only, but also in the life of every citizen of this people and 
this nation." 


The leader said: "When the death wagon faced Libyan territory exactly as it is now facing 
Arab territories, Omar Mukhtar left his position as Koranic teacher at Zawiya and did not 

act to become leader but acted out a sense of patriotic duty by carrying a gun to confront 
the death wagon. Yet meanwhile, Idriss Senoussi escaped to Egypt when the death wagon 
arrived in the country, leaving the Libyans and proving his cowardice and utter despicability 
and ron-adherence to Libyan soil." 


He added: "Libya gained victory, expelling the Italians and toppling Idriss Senoussi. 
The Libyan people were thus rendered free and became aware of the turth about Idriss 
Senoussi, who ruled Libya and contributed to its backwardness. He oppressed our people, 
established dictatorship and oppressive police rule and allowed the establishment of 
British, American and French bases. During his rule, Italian colonialism established 
itself here, killing our grandfathers and fathers and destroying the country." 


Colonel al-Qadhdafi added that the Senoussi family had sold Libya for the Italian lira and 
had not thought that this people will one day be a victor and will expose it to the world. 
He added: "We are celebrating one of these occasions on which we expose those who deceived 
the Libyan people and imposed themselves as rulers on the Libyan people." 


"Omar Mukhtar did not impose himself on the Libyan people until his martyrdom. He did not 
claim leadership or presidency but carried the gun in face of the invaders and with him 
joined all the Libyans from all parts of the country." 


On the "Vengeance Day", the revolution's leader said: "We have driven out over 20,000 
Italians occupying Libyan soil. 20,000 Italians are equal to the number of the Israeli 
troops who occupied and stayed in Lebanon usurping the farms, stores, facilities and 

economy and meddling with Lebanon's affairs appointing the president, the prime minister 

and the commanders of the Lebanese Army, as the Italians did in Libya before the revolution." 


He said: "Up till 1970, agricultural land, stores and cars were taken over by the Italians." 
He said Italy then nominated members of Parliament and paid vast sums of money so that 

they would protect its interests. He added that the Council of Ministers issued laws partial 
to the Italian coloniser who destroyed agricultural land, transforming it to buildings, 

and smuggling money under cover of these Cabinet-sanctioned laws. 


The revolution's leader showed disdain for the remnants of the bygone regime who have fled 
abroad and who talk in the name of Libya and the so-called Libyan front and Libyan 
opposition. He said: "These stray dogs composed of ex-premiers who are traitors and 
hirelings... what did they do when they earlier headed governments and when Libya was an 
Italian and English colony?... They were dogs of the Italians and not one of them handed 
in his resignation because of Italy's colonisation of Libya. All the laws they had signed 
were in the interests of the Italian colonisers...." 


V. 8 Oct 82 Q4 NORTH AFRICA 


He added: "Did any one of them resign when the five American and Britsh bases were estab- 
lished on Libyan soil? When the likes of those despicable hireling escapees talk 
[words indistinct]. They demean the Libyan people because they sold out Libya. 


The Libyan people absolutely reject the idea of being ruled again, after having realised 
their own authority and established the jamahiri (mass) system, by hireling remnant escapees 
who had conspired against them and owed their allegiance to a corrupt regime that had served 
colonialism and ignored the Libyan people..." 


He said: "There shall be no mercy for the agents of America. The escaped hirelings, 
enemies of the Libyan people, shall not escape from this people. 


He said: "It will be crazy for the base leaders and governments of the eradicated era 
who were installed under the banner of the U.S. bases and Italian colonization and were 
bred on humiliation treason, treachery and denial of Libyan soil, to have ambitions of 
ruling Libya once again after the Libyan people established their people's authority 

and announced the first jamahiriyah in history where the revolutionary committees movement 
was born from among the ranks of the masses to become an international force. He added 
that those base people are way behind the era of the masses because they are from the 
monarchy, which still believes that the world is still passing through the era of kings. 


He said: "The current monarchies in Jordan, Morocco and Saudi Arabia are museums. Upon 
entering them, we feel we are entering a strange museum of the Middle Ages.... Look at how 
the king in the Saudi kingdom cuts off the heads of the people at the market-place. Look 
at how the people are treated like sheep and ruled by method of succession...." 

The leader pointed to the lavish life-style of the Saudi rulers and said that instead of 
these life-styles being imported by ships to them from abroad, they should build factories 
and farms.... 


The leader strongly attacked the followers of the abdicated regime. He said: "The Libyan 
people did not ask for them when the revolution erupted. They did not go out to the 
streets to support them. They supported the revolution instead.... 


Those who spoke about Libya and its people in order to acquire money from their American 
and Israeli masters and their other masters in the Egyptian, Saudi and Moroccan regimes, 
have acquired money from regimes opposed to the Libyan people, revolution, Jamahiriyah, 
socialism, unity and liberation." He continued: ''These regimes are agents of America 

and Israel, enemies of the Arab nation that are creating the death wagon to trample all 

Arab countries, each and every individual one of them. It has already done so in Palestine, 
Lebanon and Egypt, and is now on its way to other Arab countries...." He said: "These 
regimes wish to see Libya [words indistinct] people, revolution, industry and agriculture 
all erased. They wish to see Libya once again kneeling to colonialism...." 


He added: “If the United States chooses to antagonise Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and his 
colleagues, we shall challenge America anywhere. We shall go to the Bahamas islands and 
meet with America there. We shall emerge there if the person being sought is Al-Qadhdhafi. 
Yet those who are being sought, are the Libyan people and the Arab nation. They are 
seeking to destroy the Libyan people and the Arab nation. They are seeking to 

destroy the Arab nation through a worldwide U.S colonialist strategy. Libya is only one 
of these positions that is resisting colonialiva." 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi also pointed in his speech to those who committed crimes 

against the Libyan people, stole their money and then escaped abroad. He said: "The 
Libyan people shall recover their fortune from these people and try them either in absentia 
or otherwise.

The door to repentance is, however, open to those who are now aware that they were living 
in an era of exploitation, oppression and corruption and are now living in the era of 
socialism and social justice and that wealth belongs equally to the Libyan people." He 
said the door to repentance is open to those who know that a new era has begun in Libya....
