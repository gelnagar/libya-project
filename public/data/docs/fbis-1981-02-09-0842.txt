LIBYA

AL~QADHDHAFI_ ADDRESSES STUDENTS UNION ON CHAD 
LD082002 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 1230, GMT 8 Feb 8] 


[Speech by Chief of State Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to a Students Union meeting at 
Qaryunis University--recorded] 


[Excerpts] Brothers: In the name of God, I greet the Jamahiriyah Students Union on the 
occasion of its fifth session. I also greet the delegations from the Arab countries-- 
Syria, Tunisia, Jordan, Lebanon, Iraq, Sudan and Yemen. I also greet the delegations 

of the friendly countries--the USSR, Cuba, Bulgaria, Romania, Gree e, Chile--as well 


as those of the International Union of Students and the World Youth Organization. 


I see in this conference a practical epitomization of the popular authority that was 
established in the Jamahiriyah, for this meeting is actually a general c nference--a 
conference of delegates and secretaries of the Student popular committees--which in 
turn is a microcosm of the people's authority. 

The governments which now control vocational trade unions and federations with a view 
to subduing the masses to the wishes of the ruling authorities are now going through 
their last throes of death in many parts of the world under the blows of the popular 
masses, which are breaking the chains of the governments which seek to dominate and 
impose their will on them. 


I affirm that the student sector is capable of carrying out liberation and popular 
revolution. Students consitute the revolutionary vanguard at all times and in all 
places. On this occasion, I wish to honor the memory of 
in the battles of revolution and freedom. [applause] 


the Libyan students who fell 


Undoubtedly, the workers, peasants and all popular toiling masses constitute the vanguard 
of the popular revolution, the revolution of tomorrow. But the students have the added 
advantage of being a vigilant as well as a popular toiling sector. Thus it is the 

sector best equipped to lead the popular revolution, the revolution of tomorrow. The 
best proof of this lies in the fact that the Students form the majority, the overwhelming 
majority, in the revolutionary committees in Libya. [applause] Suffice it to say also 
that the great 1 September revolution was a revolution of students. [applause] The 
students formed the first revolutionary committees. They were the ones who stormed the 
army barracks and who responded to the calls of their fellow soldiers. 


One of the important issues which concerns not only the students but also the whole 
country is that of the need to change the curriculum. The current c l 

inherited from the traditional society, the reactionary society, and consequently it is 
also reactionary. 


We are one of those developing countries faced with the problems of backwardness which is 
using revolution to conquer that backwardness. Hence it is essential that the conventional 
curriculum be crushed by the popular revolution and replaced by a technological-scientific 
one which would contribute to solving the problems of backward life and enable us to 

create an advanced life.

Another important issue is that of transforming the student power, which is considered 
unproductive, into a productive power. It is yet another weapon to be used in the fight 
against backwardness and for progress. 


Yet another important issue is that of militarization as a means leading to the creation 
of an armed people. Such ideas would seem Strange when raised in a backward reactionary 
sectety, a consumer society dominated by a spirit of slavery, a society whose members 

do not feel themselves responsible for protecting life. This is one of the ideas that 
would be looked upon with surprise and would be difficult to apply. 


Had it not been for the intensive revolutionary work which in a short time succeeded in 
changing the conditions in Libya, had it not been for this great effort made by the 
revolutionary forces of the society, the people's assumption of authority would not have 
reached its present level and there would not have been any possibility for raising such 
bold and important ideas, which call for changing the curriculum and the lives of students 
and making things that used to seem strange to the individual a part of his basic 
responsibilities, so that he may become free. 


This trend, undoubtedly, is opposed to the trends hostile to progressiveness--trends 
which prevail in most parts of the world that abhor freedom. 


There is yet another trend. It is one of bringing pressure to bear on the popular masses. 
It is one of banning arms and preventing the masses from exercising their rights, checking 
them, restraining them, suppressing their will, dominating them--all this under false 
slogans. 


The right trend which leads to the emanicipation of man and the triumph of freedom is 
that which lays responsibility on the shoulder of man--for slaves cannot have responsibility; 
only free men can. 


Militarization has become essential for the defense of freedom--not only because Libya is 
rich with oil, or because its area is large and therefore it needs no more land, or because 
its population is small but has weight in the Strategic balance--not because of these 

alone, but also because Libya is the only Jamahiriyah in the world. It consists of free 
people who declared to the world that they will stand by all struggling peoples everywhere-- 
peoples fighting for freedom--and will actually struggle until these peoples rid themselves 
of their oppressors and enslavers. [applause and chants] 


Militarization and the creation of barracks have thus become a persistent necessity for 
the defense of freedom and self in the face of the challenges posed by the forces opposed 
to freedom and the triumph of peoples, forces which want to encircle the Jamahiriyah 

and destroy the era of the masses at its outset. They want to silence the voices which 
have begun to shake the world, shouting slogans which are extremely crucial, like giving 
power, wealth and weapons to the people. [applause and shouts] 


Therefore, we must see how crucial and necessary these issues are--these issues which 
are being discussed by the popular basic congresses and the popular students congresses. 
They are also included in the agenda of the fifth congress of the general congress of 
the Jamahiriyah students. 


Yesterday, the Libyan people lent support to the people of Chad to liquidate aggression 
and en’ the racist war of extermination which reaction and colonialism had fed with a 
considerable supply of weapons in an attempt to conquer the Chadian people by force, to 
impose war on Libya, to lay siege around Libya and to impose on the people of Chad a 
regime which later could be used to serve the interests of colonialism and reaction in 
Africa.

When Libya responded to the request of the revolutionary government in Chad--the government 
which was formed by the national liberation movement of Chad--colonialism activated all 

its filthy naive and bribed agents of Africa--enemies of their own peoples. Africa became 
a puppet theater manipulated by the colo..ialist states across the sea by invisible threads. 
African leaders have become like dummies who move involuntarily and reiterate what their 
colonialist master decides. I mean her: the hireling African rulers, not the free ones. 
The man who knows who the dummies in A‘ rica are knows who their master is and who makes them 
move from across the sea, 


I regret that these people are part of this continent, which has been transformed into a 
puppet theater, and a large number of its heads became dummies manipulated by the colonizer, 
who dreams of colonialist empires or which the sun would never set, and of overseas colonies, 
and who foolishly and absurdly claims that these states have interests and important security 
considerations overseas. Every state has security considerations at its borders. We cate- 
gorically reject and all people with us reject this absurd claim that a state can have a 
security consideration in an area which does not touch its borders. The Chad crisis is to 
Libya a security issue because Chad is at Libya's borders. It has nothing to do with 
France's security or that of any other country, or with the security of America or even 

the security of As-Sadat, the dummy. 


Nevertheless, we closed the borders and did not interfere in the struggle which was rsoing 
on in Chad for a long time. But recently we responded to an urgent request from the 
revolutionary government in Chad to intervene on its side and end the colonialist, reac- 
tionary and racist war. Afterward, the colonialist world and its underling the reactionary 
world stood and tried to distort a noble operation because of which Libya deserves the 
Nobel Peace Prize--not condemnation. This is because the war had gone on for so many 
years. But now stabilitv and reassurance have returned to the people who were shattered 


by that racist war. 


All the colonialist and réactionary states of the world added fuel to the fire. They did 
not help to put out this fire. The people of Chad and Africa paid the price of this fire. 
But the Libyan people, who were in alliance with the people of Chad under the declaration 
of Frolinat were the ones who put an end to this tragedy. [applause] But despite this, 
they resort to lies and falsehoods as a pretext for fighting Libya and confronting it, 
claiming that this expansion was a Soviet expansion. 
This cunning propaganda might be believed by some simpletons and ignorant people in the 
world. But we declare that the responsibility is 100 percent a Libyan responsibility. 

The Soviet Union has no connection with what is happening in Chad. [applause] The Soviet 
Union has nothing to do with what is going on in Chad. (?The enemies) want to confront 
the people of Chad and the Libyan people--but they must reveal courageously, if they have 
any courage, their imperialist and sinister intentions. They are searching for absurd 
excuses which no mind can believe. The Soviet Union did not help Libya in the Chad opera- 
tion, not even with one transport plane inside Libyan territory. The entire war and ad- 


ministrative effort was by the Libyan armed forces and the Libyan people only. [applause] 


I say that the Soviet Union is not party to this action. We stress the responsibility 

of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah for this action, because Libya is entitled 
to shoulder its responsibility before the world because it is a noble and honorable action 
which realized peace and put an end to the massacre which was going on in Chad. On the 
other hand, it has secured the borders of Libya, and it safeguarded Libya against all the 
colonialist attempts which sought, through this war, to dominate this country.
