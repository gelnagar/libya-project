TRIPOLI: U.S... PROMISES EGYPT TO HELP SEIZE OIL WELLS 
LD111650Y¥ Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 11 Jan 78 LD 


[Text] Press reports from Washington have confirmed that America had explained to the 
Government of Egypt that its economic problem cannot possibly be solved except by occupy- 
ing the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and seizing its oil wells. The reports 
said that America had assured the Egyptian Government that it would render full military 
support to achieve this end.

vacate the As-Sallum area quickly when thev are requested to do so. 


The press reports say that the following day the Egyptian minister visited the Egyptian 
forces on the borders with the Jamahiriyah accompanied by the enemy defense minister,

movement of the Egyptian forces in all those positions. 


AMMAN AR-RA'Y INTERVIEWS AMBASSADOR IN AMMAN 
JNO60947Y Amman AR-RA'Y in Arabic 6 Jan 78 pp 1,10 JN 
{Muhammad Abu Ghawsh report] 


{Text] Salih as-Sanusi, Libyan ambassador in Amman, has declared Libya's appreciation of 

and support for Jordan's patriotic stands as expressed by His Majesty King Husayn on many 
occasions. These stands were recently demonstrated by the rejecticn of Begin's plan and 

the settlements which harm the rights of the Palestinian people and their national sovereignty. 


In a statement to AR-RA'Y, the Libyan ambassador said that President Mu'tammar al-Qadhdhafi's 
message to His Majesty King Husayn the day before yesterday expressed appreciation of and 
support for Jordan's patriotic stands. 


Speaking on the visits to be exchanged between high-ranking officials from the two countries, 
the Libyan ambassador said that Libya again extends its invitation to King Husayn to visit 
Libya. He said that the visit has been accepted by his majesty the king and the date will 

be fixed at the appropriate time. The ambassador added that officials from the two countries 
will exchange visits as well. 


In reply to a question about the resumption of Libyan aid to Jordan, the ambassador said: 

As long as Jordan continues to be on the confrontation line with the Zionist enemy, it is 
entitled to comprehensive aid not only from Libya, but also from all the Arab countries 

to enable it to shoulder its responsibilities and consolidate its patriotic stands. 
Naturally, economic aid and the question of financing Jordan's development projects fall 
within this framework. Consultations regarding this aid are taking place between the appro- 
priate authorities, he added. He said: We are proceeding in this from responsible pan-Arab 
principles which the patriotic stands of our Arab brothers.

On the future of the Jordanian-Libyan relations, the ambassador said: Our relations 

are excellent. I am personally anxious to consolidate and develop these relations. In 
this regard he referred to his meetings with the prime minister and with Jordanian 
officials who were eager to develop and boost cooperation between the two fraternal 
countries. He added: I am fully confident that the future of our relations is bright 
and that there is a cause for optimism. President al-Qadhdhafi's message to His Majesty 
King Husayn has confirmed Libya's readiness to develop these relations and to raise them 
to an advanced stage. 


In appraising Arab relations and their future and Israel's proposed plans, the Libyan 
ambassador said: I would like to stress that the Arabs in general agree on the basic 
strategic concept, namely, that the occupied Arab territories are Arab land for which 

we need not reaffirm our Arabism. Our sovereignty over this land is not an issue subject 
to bargaining. it is regretable that the Arab differences go against this concept and 
revolve around peripheral differences when there should be understanding and dialog. 


The Libyan ambassadcr said acrimoniously: It is regrettable and painful to hear people 
reiterating that Israel will withdraw and will submit concessions. We reject this mis- 
leading Israeli proposal because Israel--the aggressor and usurper--has nothing to ccncede, 


Regarding the extent of Libya's commitment to support th: Arab clause and the need for the 
Arabs to face their responsibilities in the battle of survival and challenge, the Libyan 
ambassador said: Libya considers the Arab cause tv be a cantral, basic and crucial cause. 
We base our relations with the other countries on their connection to our just cause, their 
support for Arab rights and their support for the aspirations and hopes of the Palestinian 
people. The ambassador added: Libya considers the Arab cause to be a general cause in 
which all Arab countries should be invcelved. This is a responsibility that should be 
shouldered by every Arab citizen and nobody has the right to exempt himself from this. 
Nobody has the right to deal separately with the cause or to abandon his patriotic duties 
and pan-Arab responsibilities. 


The ambassador continued by saving that Libya is endeavoring to beef up unified Arab action 
against the Zionist danger. It is also working to mend the Arab rift. Libya is happy to 
see that its good offices have succeeded in creating understanding between Iraq and Syria, 
because all Arab understanding serves Arab interests. 


As for sharing the sufferings of our brothers living under Israeli occupation, the Libyan 
ambassador said: We consider any accomplishment or gain to be incomplete until we enable 
our brothers who are standing fast in the face of Israeli occupation to breathe the fresh 
air of freedom.
