Summary (AI): Translated excerpt of a 28 March 1985 Qadhdhafi speech, broadcast on Tripoli domestic radio, announcing a Pan-Arab command for revolutionary forces and denying Libyan advances into Chad. It is about Libyan politics and foreign policy and mentions no specific Tripoli sites.
• Speech was broadcast by Tripoli domestic Arabic radio, per the REF line (LD282203 TRIPOLI DOMESTIC ARABIC).
• Qadhdhafi said an agreement had been reached and an inspection carried out, then claimed Libyan forces were advancing into Chad, which he called imaginary.
• The speech called for a Pan-Arab command for leading revolutionary forces in the Arab homeland and for building socialism.
• The speech referred to a defeated alliance seeking refuge in America, which it called the bitter enemy.
• 1985-03-28: Qadhdhafi speech announcing that a Pan-Arab command for leading revolutionary forces would hold its first meeting the next day; claims Libyan forces were falsely reported advancing into Chad.
