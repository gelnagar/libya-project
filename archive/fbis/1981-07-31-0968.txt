LIBYA

U.S. DiSTORTION OF ANTI-AL-QADHDHAFI PLAN REPORTED 


LD310606 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 30 Jul 81 


[Text] The American paper the Washington POST, which has close links with VIA departments, 
said on 28 July that the American terrorist subversive operation which had caused a 
scandal in the White House was not directed against Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, as it alleges, 
but against Mauritania. 


Commenting on the report published by the American paper, the JANA political editor said: 
This is a further attempt by the American administration to distort the facts with a view 
to concealing the truth of its aggressive directions and its flagrant stance against the 
leader of the revolution. But it is a fully exposed attempt and does not require much 
trouble to reveal its hypocrisy, lies and so forth. The entire world's public opinion 
was aware of it in advance. 


He said that the United States cannot possibly absolve itself of its clear involvement in 
this terrorist operation. Its agent and representative, the leader of the Sudan, 
preceded it in all arenas by revealing this at the press conference he held in Alexandria 
during his recent meeting with his partner in treason, the ruler of Egypt. It is now 
trying through its various propaganda media and through its representatives and agents to 
deny its criminal terrorist plan against the leader of the revolution. By so doing it 

is formally asserting that it had laid down its terrorist plan against the leader of the 
revolution, which it intended to execute by means of its tools and traitorous symbols 

in the region. 


The entire world's public opinion is now convinced that the United States, which has 
treacherously murdered a large number of revolutionary leaders, does not hesitate in 
practicing terrorism, which it has carried out with relish. This is to enable it to lay 
down a terrorist plan that is directed at the leader of the revolution, who has resisted 
the United States and exposed to the world its terrorist stands, shameful political 
conduct and conspiratorial policy against the freedom of nations to the world.
