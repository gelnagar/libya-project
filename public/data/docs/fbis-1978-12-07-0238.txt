AL-QADHDHAFI ADDRESSES TRADE UNION DELEGATION 


LD061906Y Tripoli JANA in English 1638 GMT 6 Dec 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Dec 6, JANA--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the lst of September 
revolution and secretary-general of the General People's Congress, last night met members 
of the production workers trade union. 


During this meeting Colonel al-Qadhdhafi urged the union to encourage production workers 
to seize power over establishments, firms and production centres in order to liberate 
themselves from the slavery of being hired hands ami to implement the dictum: "partners 
and not hired hands." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi explained that this sacred move by the production workers of the Jama- 
hiriyah is a victory of freedom which brings happiness to mankind. This will enable 
oppressed production workers to regain their rights and to acquire a respectable place in 
the new socialist society. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi pointed out that not only would this sacred move benefit the produc- 
tion sector but also all classes of people whose interests are linked directly or indirectly 
with the production sector.

TANZANIAN, UGANDAN OFFICIALS HOLD TALKS IN LIBYA 
LD051316Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 5 Dec 78 LD 


[Text] Mohamed Ali Foum, Tanzanian deputy foreign minister, will arrive in Tripoli 
this eveningon avisit to the Jamahiriyah within the framework of the efforts by the 
Jamahiriyah to solve the dispute between Uganda and Tanzania. 


Lt Col (Bairl), under secretary of the Ugandan Foreign Ministry, arrived in Tripoli 
yesterday on a visit to the Jamahiriyah within the framework of these efforts. 


It is recalled that the Jamahiriyah is continuing its serious efforts to reach a solution 
for the dispute between the two sister countries, Tanzania and Uganda, out of its con- 
viction of the Africans! capability to solve their own problems without outside inter- 
ference or trusteeship and its confidence that African wisdom can take the initiative 
from the enemies of the continent, which needs to direct all its capabilities against 

its racist enemies, who are backed by colonialism. 


These efforts are manifested in a number of cables sent by brother Col Mu! ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi to Presidents Julius Nyerere and Idi Amin, and the visit of brother Muhammad 
Abu al-Qasim az-Zuwayy, secretary of information and culture, as a personal envoy of the 
brother colonel to these sister countries. 


JABLONSKI STOPS OVER IN LIBYA WHILE RETURNING HOME 


ID060950Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 5 Dec 78 LD 


[Text] Henryk Jablonski, secretary general of the Polish United Workers! Party and 
chairman of Council of State, this evening made a stopover in Benghazi on his return 

to his country. The Polish leader expressed his happiness at the welcome accorded 

to him for the second time during his transit through the Jamahiriyah, saying: Please 
convey my warm greetings to Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the revolution, for 

whom our people harbor much esteem and affection. He stressed the need to strengthen 
friendship and cooperation in order to bolster relations between the two friendly countries. 


After a rest Jablonski went into the town of Benghazi. He is on his way back to Poland 
after visiting a number of African states.

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES HUSAYN ENVOY, MESSAGE 


LDO61800Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 6 Dec 78 LD 


[Text] Col Mu'tammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the revolution, today received brother 
‘Abd al-Hamid Sharaf, chief of the Jordanian Royal Court and King Husayn's personal 
envoy to the brother colonel. Brother ‘Abd al-Hamid Sharaf conveyed to the brother 
colonel a message from King Husayn connected with the development of relations between 
Jordan and the Palestinians. 


‘Abd al-Hamid Sharaf, chief of the Royal Jordanian Court, arrived in Tripoli this after- 
noon on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. In a statement to a JANA correspondent, 'Abd al-Hamid 
Sharaf expressed his pleasure at visiting the Jamahiriyah and said: I am carrying a 
message from King Husayn to brother Col Mu'ammar aleQadhdhafi connected with the current 
Arab situation and the means of strengthening Arab resistance and the consolidation of 
relations among the Arab countries on a fraternal basis in light of the resolutions of 

the Baghdad conference and in a spirit of sublime pan-Arab interest.
