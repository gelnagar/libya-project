LIBYA

DER SPIEGEL: AL-QADHDHAFI DISCUSSES LIQUIDATION POLICY 


DW171607 Hamburg DER SPIEGEL in German 16 Jun 80 p 120 


{Unattributed interview with Libyan head of state Mufammar al-Qadhdhafi, date and 
Place of interview not given] 


[Text] Question: In the 10 years of Libyan revolution the "weapon of terror" has never 
been used against Libyan dissidents. On the contrary, you always preferred to ignore your 
adversaries abroad. Suddenly you give them an ultimatum and order them, under threat 

of murder, to return to Libya. Why? 


Al-Qadhdhafi: The persons asked to return are not dissidents. They want to cement the 
society of the past, they are the remnant of a society of exploitation, corruption and 
slavery, and those putting up resistance to the power of the masses will be persecuted 
mercilessly, neutralized and eliminated. 


Question: In Europe it is claimed that your policy merely serves the purpose of covering 
up internal difficulties. They say that the army has been calling your foreign policy into 
question since the end of last year. Besides, broad circles are against your new system 
which practically boils down to the abolition of profit. 


Al-Qadhdhafi: I resent these contentions. There are no such things as internal problems. 
The revolution will triumph. The people are beginning to eliminate the remnants of the past. 


Question: For the Libyans living abroad you have set 11 June as the last dateline for 
their return. Why did you pick that date? And what will happen after 11 June. 


Al-Qadhdhafi: I have granted that respite to spare these people the vengeance of the 
revolutionary forces. After 11 June I will be unable to guarantee their physical integrity. 


Question: Do you mean to say by this that all of them will be eliminated? 


Al-Qadhdhafi: What attitude can I adopt, anyway? When I passed my call on to them I spoke 
without any ambiguity: Come before 11 June or all of you will be liquidated! 


Question: Hence, the road is clear for the revolutionary committees? 


Al-Qadhdhafi: Of course. The revolutionary fighters will fulfill their program. They 
will chase all these people to their destruction. 


Question: What is it, anyway, that you fear from these dissidents? Who are they? What 
do they have in mind, and why are they wanted so badly by the revolutionary committees? 


Al-Qadhdhafi: This involves a class of people which exploits Libya. It is the enemy of 
the people, the enemy of the freedom of the workers. This class does not allow the soil 
to belong to everyone. It does not agree with the distribution of arms to the whole 
population. 


Question: What would happen if these people decided to return to Libya? 
Al-Qadhdhafi: If they decided to do that, I would still be capable of protecting them. 


Question: Don't you feel you are a misunderstood prophet now, after your foreign policy 
failures?

Al-Qadhdhafi: I do not deny that these failures are the result of the coincidence of 
a number of circumstances. But they will serve very much to accelerate the process of 
unity of the Arab peoples, 


Question: The West is afraid of an Islamic-Arab rebirth, Is this anxiety justified? 


Al-Qadhdhafi: I condemn any military expansion, I believe, in contrast, that our 
reawakening is bound to lead to a moral and civic rebirth. Do not forget that I am the 
harbinger of peace for all of mankind. 


Question: So eventheoretically it is not necessary to think about a new Poitiers? It 
was there that Charles Martell halted the Arab invasion in 732. 


Al-Qadhdhafi: There will be no new Poitiers. There is no call whatsoever to repeat that 
adventure, 


Question: The harbinger of peace, therefore, will not turn into a bellicose conqueror? 


Al-Qadhdhafi: We are working toward giving the world a new, universal civilization and 
not just an Arab one. Consequently it is just as easy to recognize our friends as well 
as to discover our enemies, On our side are the people's masses who are longing for 
peace and fighting against slavery. Those who resist us are.allthose elements which 
suppress freedom and try to practice slavery. 


Question: Why, then, are you not asking the revolutionaries of the whole world to come 
to Libya to live and work? 


Al-Qadhdhafi: We will invite them very soon, 


Question: Why did you reform the Libyan currency and set 1,000 dinars (DM 6,000) per 
family for the exchange? 


Al-Qadhdhafi: The currency restriction was decided on to dissolve the fortunes of the 
captialists for good, Among other things the elimination of money is the ultimate goal 
of our revolution, Some day work by man will serve exclusively to satisfy his needs, 
Trade relations will no longer be necessary. And one thing you may believe me for sure: 
On that day man will really find his happiness! 


Question: How do you make this idealistic ideology compatible with the war potential 
with which you equip Libya anyway, and even with nuclear armament, as is claimed time and 
again? 


Al-Qadhdhafi: This is nothing but gossip and slander. We have no intention whatever of 
acquiring nuclear weapons, But we do intend to use nuclear energy for peaceful purposes, 


Question: But you do confirm that you possess a nuclear weapon? 

Al-Qadhdhafi: We have defense options which you cannot even imagine, All our neighbors 
are weak...So why should one use this weapon? The extreme weakness of our neighbors 
perhaps is our greatest opportunity. 

Question: Now, do yeu have the atomic bomb or don!t you? 


Al-Qadhdhafi: I will merely tell you in confidence that Libya has signed all treaties 
prohibiting the use of this weapon; I cannot tell you more,

[Text] Tripoli, June 17, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY=--The USA terrorizes Libyan citizens 
abroad. The U.S. authorities threatened and terrorized the Libyan envoy in the United

PROTOCOL SIGNED WITH TURKISH CYPRUS; DENKTAS DEPARTS 
TA171405 (Clandestine) Bayrak Radio in Turkish to Cyprus 1300 GT 17 Jun 80 


[Text] President Rauf Denktas, who concluded his official visit to Libya, left for 
London today. In Libya Denktas held his last contact with the leader of the Call to 
Islam Association, (Dr Sharif). Ina statement after the meeting, Denktas said that 
steps aimed at solving the important problems of the Turkish Federated State of Cyprus 
will be initiated as a result of the great interest shown by the association, He added 
that the association will send experts to the federated states to conduct investigations. 
Meanwhile, a protocol has been signed between the Turkish Federated State of Cyprus and 
the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. The protocal envisages cooperation in 
the fields of economy, trade, industry, tourism, communications and civil aviation, 
According to the protocol technical, cultural and social cooperation will be further 
developed. 


LIBYA, MALI SIGN ECONOMIC, INFORMATION PROTOCOL 
ID171519 Tripoli JANA in English 1429 GMT 17 Jun 80 


[Text] Tripoli, June 17, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The foreign secretary and his counter- 
part of Mali last night signed a protocol for bilateral cooperation in economic and 
information sectors, It includes developing Libyan Arab-Mali joint ventures for meat 

and livestock, establishing a joint agricultural body and completing the procedures to 
establish the joint bank. Programmes for cultural exchange and initial agreement on 
trade and labor were also included in the protocol. The Libyan Arab-Mali joint committee 
will. hold itsnext meeting in Bamako.
