NICARAGUAN DELEGATION ENDS VISIT 30 OCT 
LD302004 Tripoli JANA in English 1836 GMT 30 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, October 30, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Nicaraguan delezation headed by 
Mr Henry Ruiz, member of the Nicaraguan Government Committee for National Redressment, 
have left Tripoli this evening after a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab 
Jamihiriyah during which it conducted talks with the Libyan Arab brothers on the bilateral 
relations between both countries, the ways of strengthening them in the interest of 

both peoples and other issues of common concern, 


In a statement to a JAMAIHIRIYAH 1.ZWS AGENCY correspondent at the airport Mr Henry Ruiz 
affirned the depth of links that connect the two revolutions and the two peoples indicating 
that the victory of revolution in Nicaragua and the truth of the Great First of September 

Rev lution and her belief in nations' freedom is what has given relations their distinguished 
ard ideal form, He added: "Our relation has become strong and firm and the visit of our 
delegation to the land of the Great First of September Revolution is an affirmation of 

the depth of these relations which we seek to strengthen and consolidate, He also pointed 

to the appreciation, respect and esteem of the Nicaraguan people for the great and 

valuable aids presented by the Libyan Arab people to the Nicaraguan people in their 
revolution against the fascist Somoza regime.

In his statement Mr Ruiz praised the heroic stands of the Great First of September 
Revolution which applies the slogans of freedom that it called for and firmly counters 

the challenges of imperialism and racism as well as positively and effectively supporting 
the national liberation movements. He expressed the admiration of the leaders of the 
Sandinist Revolution and the Nicaraguan people for the leader Colonel Mu'ammar al- 
Qadhdhafi and the people of the Jamahiriyah, as well as their great appreciation and 
esteem for the victories and creative achievements realized by the Great First of September 
Revolution, for the freedom of man and for the establishment of the prosperous socialist 
society. 


Mr Ruiz was seen off at Tripoli International Airport by the secretary of the General 
People's @ommittee for Social Security and representatives of the Cooperation and Proto- 
col Departments at the Foreign Secretariat, as well as the Cuban [as received] ambassador 
to the Jamahiriyah.

LD302010 Tripoli JANA in English 1759 GMT 30 Oct 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, October 30, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The secretary general of Saguia el 

Hamra and Rio de Oro Popular Liberation Front, Mohammad Abdelaziz, and Mohammad al-Amin, 
prime minister of the Saharan Arab Democratic Republic left Tripoli this afternoon after 
a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, 


In a statement to JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY the front's secretary general affirmed the im- 
portance of relations between the Libyan and the Saharan peoples, indicating that time 
and positions adopted strengthen this relation and consolidate it. He praised the firm 
and courageous stands of the Great First of September Revolution towards the different 
causes and her decisive confrontation of all imperialist attempts and designs seeking 

to plunder the nations' resources and restore colonialist domination of them, 


He also said: "Our people in the Sahara and Rio de Oro confront any unholy alliance 
representing all forces hostile to the peoples' aspirations, especially in the Arab 
homeland and Africa." At the canclusion of his statement Mr Abdelaziz greeted the Lib- 
yan Arab people and the leader of the Great First of September Revolution describing 
Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, as "a hero, freedom-fighter and great revolutionary who 
is genuinely andhonorably seeking to liberate man and attain his happiness," 


Mr Abdelaziz was seen off at Tripoli International Airport by a representative from the 
Arab Liaison Bureau and the front!s representative in Tripoli.
