LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI VIEWS ROLE OF UNITED NATIONS ON ANNIVERSARY 
LD271709 Tripoli JANA in English 1435 GMT 27 Oct 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Oct 27, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY: Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said the so- 
called United Nations is an organisation of disparaging governments only. He said 
"We look forward to the day when an organisation is established binding all peoples 
and nations of the [word indistinct] world." 


In a reception held in People's Palace in Tripoli to commemorate the 34th anniversary 
for the U.N.'s establishment, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "Such an organisation can 
only be established after the abolition of governments and the establishment of peoples 
authority." He added: In order to achieve the envisaged entity, the United Nations 
Organisation should be reorganized to act in harmony with its charter and so that all 
members countries should achieve equality, especially in the Security Council." 


He stressed the need for abolishing the right of veto and permanent membership so that 
all nations are treated equally both in membership and in votes, 


He pointed out that the mandatory council of the UN has (?been a) burden to the UN, so 
this council should be abolished too and the colonialist countries should cede control 
of areas not included in the mandate, such as the Reunion Island, which is colonised 
by (?France), Northern Ireland which is controlled by the U.K., Mauritius Islands 
controlled by the USA, and the settlers in Palestine, (?south) Africa, and 

Zimbabwe should go back to where they came from, 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi elaborated in his speech that there are a number of UN member 
countries where the ruling regimes have been changed by other members of the UN, This 
is considered a [word indistinct] violation of the UN Charter on which the UN was 

set up, he said. 


We cannot say that humanity has not achieved any progress after World War I and II, 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi added, but the United Nations in its present state is not what 
we need. Yet it is still better than the previous League of Nations, The concern 
which small countries show towards the dictatorship exercised upon them at the U.N, 
Security Council is because of the power of permanent members of the Security Council 
enjoyed by France, Britain and the United States, which are still colonialist 
countries and have the veto right, 


Colonel Multammar al-Qadhdhafi then praised the personal efforts of the U.N. secretary- 
general and said: "We have to put our faith in the capacity of small nations in 
creating a better world, and we have to admit that there are real and serious inter- 
national problems we have failed to solve, some of which are causing suffering and 
misery to several peoples of the world. Such problems, he said, need a collective 
effort to alleviate suffering,

He gave the example of the tragedy of the Palestinian people who are now living as refugees out- 


side their homeland, as well as the tragedy of the African peoples of Zimbabwe, South 
Africa, Namibia, Mozambique, Zambia and Botswana, who are exposed to barbaric military 
attacks. 


Once more we confirm our faith in the capacity of peoples to defeat and put an ermd to 

such tragedies and those wno cause them, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said, on the same level 

as the Vietnamese people who achieved victory and sacrificed a lot before the United Nations 
was able to solve their problem." 


The leader of the Great First of September Revolution Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
concluded that there is another final problem disregarding its political implications. 
It is the tragedy of a huge number of refugees in Southeast Asia caused by political 
and social interactions and changes in the region. 


He added: In order to face the tragedy which has befallen our women, children and elderly, 


we should increase our efforts." In the letter of congratulations sent to the U.N. secretary 
general, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said: "This is not a customary letter with soft words 
and undertones. This is because we are anxious to see an effective organization." Ending 


the letter, Colonel al-Qadhdhafi expressed hope that a day will come when all of humanity 
will speak the same language. 


The celebration was attended by the liaison and foreign secretaries, under-secretaries 
and heads of departments in the Foreign Secretariat, heads of Arab and foreign diplomatic 
legations accredited to the Jamahiryah, and theresident representative of the United 
Nations in the Jamahiriyah. 


The resident U.N. representative in the Janahriyah read the message sent by the U.N. 
secretary general on "U.N. Day" and pointed out that celebrations marking the U.N.'s 
34th anniversary come at a time when freedoms and human rights are still being violated, 
and when conflicts [words indistinct] are becoming stronger among nations. The U.N. 
representative praised the achievements accomplished in the Jamahiriyah by the Great 
First of September Revolution in the political, economic and social fields. 


NICARAGUAN SANDINIST FRONT DELEGATION ARRIVES 
LD271746 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1430 GMT 27 Oct 79 LD 


(Text ] Tripoli--This afternoon a delegation of the Sandinist Front for the Natioral 
Liberation of Nicaragua led by Henry Ruiz Hernandez arrived in Tripoli on a visit to the 
Jamahiriyah. They were welcomed at Tripoli international airport by Bashir Hawdah, secretary 
of the general people's committee for land reclamation and development, and representatives 
from the cooperation department, the two Americas department and the protocols office of 

the foreign secretariat. 


In a statement to JANA, Henry Ruiz praised the support of the Great First September 
Revolution for the Sandinist revolution in Nicaragua from its beginning to its victory 

over the fascist Somoza regime. He said the visit was within the framework of strengthening 
and developing relations between the First September and Sandinist revolutions. He saii 
the Jamahiriyah's support for our revolution had a great influence on the triumph of the 
Sandinist in Nicaragua, which was a triumph for all progressive forces in the world.
