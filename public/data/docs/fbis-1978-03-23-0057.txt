LIBYA

FIRST GROUP LEAVES TO FIGHT IN SCUTH LEBANON 
LD221422¥ Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 22 Mar 78 LD 


(Text ] A JANA correspondent has learned that the first group of volunteers--Libyans, 
Arab brethren and Moslems residing in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah 
who had registered their names--left the Jamahiriyah yesterday on their way to the 
battlefield in southern Lebanon to join their brother Palestinians and Lebanese pro- 
gressive nationalists in their fierce battles against the racist Zionist enemy. The 
correspondent has learned that this group of volunteers totals 200 men. 


In addition, a central committee has been set up in the Jamahiriyah to collect contrib- 
utions for the Palestinians and the Lebanese who are facing the Zionist aggression in 
southern Lebanon. The committee will be under the supervision of the competent offices 
of the General Secretariat of the General People's Congress, the unions, and the popular 
associations. Moreover, subcommittees on the level of municipalities in the Jamahiriyah 
have been formed to collect all kinds of contributions, including blood donations, and 
to register volunteers. 


These steps fall within the framework of Libya's actions on the Arab and international 
level to support the Palestinian and Lebanese brothers in southern Lebanon, and they 
are in accordance with the recommendations of the participants in the popular march 
which was organized in Tripoli on 19 March in support of the steadfastness of the joint 
Lebanese- Palestinian national front in repelling the Zionist enemy. 


AL~QADHDHAFI: LIBYA PREPARING AFRICAN SUMMIT 


LD221448Y Kuwait AR-RA'Y Al-'AMM in Arabic 17 Mar 78 p 1 LD 


[Text] In an expanded interview with AR-RA'Y Al-'AMM, Libyan leader Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, 
secretary general of the General People's Congress, has stated that Somali President 
Mohamed Siad Barre has agreed to withdraw his forces from the Ogaden Region, which is 
disputed by Somalia and Ethiopia. 


The colonel said that the Somali president has agreed to this within the framework of 
proposals for a peaceful settlement of the conflict between those two countries. The 
Libyan leader said that he held closed meetings with the Somali president for 2 days, 
during which an understanding was reached on a plan for joint action in the Horn of 
Africa. 


Chairman al-Qadhdhafi told an AR-RA'Y AL~'AMM delegation that contacts are currently 
taking place in order to convene an urgent African summit conference to discuss the 
situation in the Horn of Africa with a view to taking the region out of the sphere of 
international conflict, so that a solution that insures the interests of all parties 
concerned can be reached.
