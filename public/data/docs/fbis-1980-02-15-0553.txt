LIBYA

ARRIVAL OF PDRY DELEGATION IN TRIPOLI REPORTED 
LD142026 Tripoli JANA in English 1920 GMT 14 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Feb 14, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Mr 'Ali Nasir Antar the defence minister 
and member of the Political Bureau of the Socialist Party of South Yemen arrived in 
Tripoli today. 


He was given an official reception at his arrival which was attended by Brigadier ‘Abu 
Bakr Yunis Jabir the general commander of the armed forces, a number of army officers, the 
S. Yemeni ambassador to the Jamahiriyah the general manager of Tripoli airport and a 
representative from the Protocol Department of the Foreign Secretariat. 


He said our visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah comes within the 
framework of the consolidation of brotherly relations between our people who are 
struggling for the unity of the Arab nation and against imperialism, Our visit stems from 
our joint belief in the continued exchange of views to achieve and strengthen the unity 

of Arab revolutionary force. 


He affirmed that the viewpoints are identical particularly regarding struggle against the 
Zionist enemy and the treasonous conspiracy of Stable David which deny our Palestinian 
Arab people their independent state. He said: Carry the greetings of our Yemeni Arab 
people to their brother the Libyan Arab people. 


Colonel 'Ali Ahmad Nasir Antar was accompanied by a delegation including 'Abd al-Ghani 
tAbd al-Qadir member of the Central Committee and minister of industry, head of oil and 
mining department and Mr Taha Hilal director of national oil company. 


FOREIGN SECRETARY RETURNS TO TRIPOLI FROM CHAD 


LD141056 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 GMT 14 Feb 80 LD 


(Text ] Tripoli--The foreign secretary returned to Tripoli last night at the end of his 
visit to Ndjamena where he held a series of meetings with Chadian President Oueddei 
Goukouni and his vice president at which they discussed relations between the fraternal 
countries and the means that would insure the implementation of the Lagos agreement for 
national reconciliation in Chad, 


The foreign secretary also held a meeting with the Chadian prime minister and with the 
ministers of defense and the interior at which they reviewed the situation in Chad and 
the means of strengthening relations between the two fraternal countries, 


The Chadian leaders stressed to the foreign secretary their total appreciation to the 
brother leader of the revolution and to the people of the Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah for 
the assistance and backing which they rendered to the people of Chad to achieve national 
reconciliation and the liberation of Chad from foreign colonialism, The Chadian leaders 
also underlined their determination to implement the Lagos agreement and to work for the 
realization of the unity of Chad, peace and security in it, and their determination to 
achieve the evacuation of the colonialist forces from Chad in accordance with the Lagos 
agreement,

The foreign secretary stressed to the Chadian leaders the concern of the Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah and its people for the unity, safety and security of Chad and stressed that it 
would spare no effort in supporting the fraternal people of Chad so that they could achieve 
their national unity and secure the withdrawal of the colonialist forces from their 
national territory.
