Summary (AI): Text of President Carter's August 1980 report to a Senate Judiciary subcommittee on his brother Billy Carter's dealings with Libya, including the 1978 and 1979 trips, the Iran hostage appeal routed through Billy, and the Foreign Agents Registration Act inquiry. It covers U.S. policy differences with Libya but names no Tripoli sites in detail.
• Billy Carter's September 1978 Libya trip was followed by three U.S. Embassy Tripoli cables commenting on it.
• In November 1979 Billy Carter helped arrange a meeting between Brzezinski and Libya's People's Bureau chief to seek Libyan help on the hostages in Iran.
• The Foreign Agents Registration Act investigation concerned Billy Carter's relationship with Libya.
• Libyan Foreign Secretariat publicly said on Nov. 22, 1979 that hostages should be released.
• 1978-09: Billy Carter visited Libya with a group of Georgia businessmen and state officials.
• 1979-11: U.S. Embassy in Tripoli attacked by a Libyan mob, per Carter's account of his Dec. 6 meeting with Libyan People's Bureau chief Ali el-Houdari.
