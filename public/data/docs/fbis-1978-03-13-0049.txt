JANA POLITICAL EDITOR PRAISES FEDAYEEN OPERATION 
LD121603¥ Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1415 GMT 12 Mar 78 LD 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 12 March--JANA Political Editor has saluted the heroic operation 
carried out by the Palestinian fedayeen in the depth of the occupied territory, thus 
debunking the myth of Israeli security and supremacy and its ability to escape punishment. 


We have always said, the editor added, that all doors must be opened before the just, 
legitimate Palestinian struggle, and all capabilities must be placed at its disposal to 
enable it to strike violently, deep inside the enemy lines, because we know that the 
enemy society is an artificial one, a broken society founded on oppression, blackmail, 
exploitation and the domination of the overwhelming majority by a small minority. Hence 
striking the enemy from within will make him panic and will expose his true face and 
inflict on him defeat with a minimum of cost and men. 


The editor added: As we salute the hepoes of the bold fedayeen operation, we are aware 
that the enemies of the Arab nation will call it an act of terrorism, but we say to them 
that the operation was an act of retaliation by the Palestinians against their Zionist 
enemies, beginning with Begin, who is the one primarily responsible for the Dayr Yasin 
massacre. [passage indistinct] 


Terrorism, the JANA editor said, is what the Zionist enemy did when it killed and 
Slaughtered the Palestinian people and drove them out of their homes, amd when they 
attacked Arab villages and towns adjoining Palestine, and when they shot down the Libyan 
civilian plane.

MOR OCCO 
PRIME MINISTER ELABORATES ON TRADE WITH USSR 
LD112303Y Rabat MAP in French 1530 GMT 11 Mar 78 LD 


[Text] Rabat--Prime Minister Ahmad Osman was interviewed by Moroccan radio-television 
on the Mor:ecan-Soviet agreements signed during his current visit to Moscowe 


"TMese cooperation agreements in the sphere of phosphates and in that of product 
exchanges," Mr Osman said, "are of great importance and constitute the most important 
contract made between a gieal power and a country of the Third World," The prime 
minister added: "Among the four agreements signed, it is worth mentioning the long 

term agreement on economic and technical cooperation in phesphates between the Kingdom 
of Morocco and the USSR, as weil as the special protocol relating to spending regulations 
linked to the execution of the first stage in the exploitation of the Miskala phosphate 
deposits, which could have an annual production capacity of 12 million tons, 


"These agreements," Mr Osman said, "are based on certain basic principles and cover a 
period of 30 years. During this period each of the two sides is free to act so as to 
guarantee its rights. Thus, the royal phosphates office (0.C.P.) is called upon to 
provide itself with a part of thenecessary equipment from its own resources, should 


the Soviet offer not be satisfactory, " 


The prime minister in addition noted that another agreement concerning the exchange 
of products has been made for 30 years, It provides for the delivery to the USSR of 
phosphates and phosphate byproducts, and the long term provision of Morocco with 
chemical products, nitrate fertilizers and primary materials such as oil.

The third agreement, Mr Osman said, relates to the financing of the first stage in 
the development of the Miskala deposits, as well as the financing of studies for this 
project, with a credit of about $20 million granted at favorable conditions. The 
fourth agreement concerns the exchange of products during the period 1978-1980. 


Speaking about the discussions he had on Friday morning with the Soviet leaders, 

Mr Osman said they embraced several bilateral and international issues. "We examined 

in depth issues relating to our trade relations, and I would like to underline that 

the trade agreement which will be concluded next year must be renewed and strengthened. 
This is also the wish of the Soviet side. We also examined the extent of the agree- 
ments we concluded, as well as certain international issues concerning our two countries, 
such as the Middle East," he said in conclusion.
