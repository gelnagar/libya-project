SAUDI KING KHALID LEAVES FOR GENEVA 1 OCTOBER 
LDO011258 Tiyadh SNA in Arabic 1030 GMT 1 Oct 79 LD 


[Excerpt] Benghazi, 1 October--King Khalid ibn 'Aba@ al-'Aziz left Benghazi at 1230 Saudi 
time for Geneva today at the end of his 2-day official visit to the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. He was seen off at the airport by Libyan leader Col Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, members of the Libyan Revolution Command Council, senior Libyan civilian 
and military officials and Saudi Ambassador to Libya Shaykh 'Abdallah al-Fadl. 


Jallud!s Comments 
LDO11052 Riyadh SNA in Arabic 0945 GMT 1 Oct 79 LD 


[Excerpts] Benghazi, 1 October--Maj 'Abd as-Salam Jallud, member of the Revolution 
Command Council inthe Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, has reiterated that the 
Arab nation possesses the capability, resources and confidence in the future to thwart all 
opportunities for its enemies and to emerge strong and great from its ordeals. The Arab 
nation is always capable of doing so because of its history, faith and struggle, he added. 


In an interview with SNA, Major Jallud expressed his hope that the Saudi-Libyan meetings will 
end in satisfactory results compatible with the urgent issues that have imposed themselves 

on the Arab area, and that they will thus serve the interest of Islam and the Muslims. We 
rely to a great extent, he said, on the kingdom and its role for preserving the Arab 
character of the Gulf and for strengthening the position of Islam. 


Major Jallud said that King Khalid and Col Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi might discuss the 
development of the Baghdad conference resolutions, adding that the Baghdad conference has 
decided to hold an annual summit in November. Since the meeting between the king and 
al-Qadhdhafi takes place before the convocation of the second Baghdad conference, anything 
the two leaders agree to will constitute a major contribution aimed at rectifying and 
clarifying the movement and vision of the future,

AT-TURAYKI ADDRESSES GROUP OF 77 SESSION IN NEW YORK 

LD292126 Tripoli JANA in English 1856 GMT 29 Sep 79 LD 

(Text ] New York, September 29, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Foreign Secretary Dr. 'Ali !Abd 
as-Salam at-Turayki addressed a Group of 77 meeting which was held yesterday at U.N, 


headquarters and dealt with the present international economic situation and its effect 
on developing countries, 


Dr. at-Turayki referred to the attempt by the advanced industrial countries, particularly 
France, to control the resources of the developing countries by various means, including 
military invasion. He mentioned in particular France!s recent invasion of Central 
Africa, a clear evidence of a return to the era of imperialism. 


Ve. 2 Oct 79 : NORTH AFRICA 


Dr. at-Turayki denied the allegations of industrial countries suggesting that the economic 
crisis is due to the rise in the price of oil. He clarified that the current economic 
crisis is in fact the result of weaknesses in the existing system on the control and 
exploitation of the resources of developing countries, He said that the oil exporting 
countries are victims of the international economic system and not a cause to the crisis, 
Referring to the Mexican suggestion concerning the establishment of an international energy 
policy, the foreign secretary explained that, in spite of the goodwill expressed in this 
suggestion, it will harm the developing countries as the advanced countries will use it to 
break up the Group of 77. 


He added that Mexico as an oil exporting country should join OPEC and discuss this sug- 
gestion. He stressed the necessity of negotiations among the developing countries and 

that they should be alert in the face of all attempts aiming at dividing them because their 
strength lies in their solidarity. He warned the developing countries against the attempts 
of the advanced countries to exploit them, 


JANA REPORTS AT-TURAYKI UN PRESS CONFERENCE 
LD292124 Tripoli JANA in English 1814 GMT 29 Sep 79 LD 


[Text] New York, September 29, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Foreign Secretary Dr. 'Ali 
'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki said the Arab countries have totally refused the capitulation 

treaty signed by the Egyptian regime and (Israel) under U.S. pressure, because the Arab 

nation finds capitulations and defect unacceptable, 


In a press conference held in the U.N. headquarters, the foreign secretary said recent 
developments have increased tension in the region as a result of American intervention and 
its efforts to impose capitulatory settlement plans, such as the one signed by the defeated 
Egyptian President under U.S. pressure. 


He said America's on the Egyptian regime is a losing one, and that evidence to support 

this belief is file, especially recent events in Iran and Nicaragua, [sentence as received] 
Dr. at-Turayki said the struggle between Zionist ideology and the Arabs is civilisational, 
and that Arabs are only against capitulation, not peace. 


What is needed, Dr. at-Turayki said, is a just peace which would grant Palestinians their 
legitimate rights and that there will be no peace as long as these rights are denied. 


The foreign secretary said the Jamahiriyah's people have established relations with the 
American people, but that struggle for the Palestinian people's rights is the essence 
of our relation with the United States. 


Dr. at-Turayki said France's action is extremely serious because it entails the invasion 
of an independent country by a member of the U.N. Security Council, and the setting up of 
a person who lived in Paris as president of the invaded country. This, he said, is in 
complete contravention of the Organization of African Unity's principles and we cannot 
remain silent when such acts are committed. 


He said any people has the right of changing its regime, but France does not have the right 
of military intervention which has placed the region in a dangerous situation, 


He said this situation is extremely serious, especially when a great power pressures 
another nation defeated in war into signing such accords,

Dr. at-Turayki said the capitulation treaty has been totally refused by all Arab coun- 
tries and especially the confrontation states of Syria, Jordan and the Palestine 
Liberation Organization. The foreign secretary said the United States is betting on 
the Egyptian regime's capability of making other Arab countries participate in this 
farce, yet the Baghdad conference proved the contrary and isolated Egypt, not only 
from the Arab regime, but also from the Islamic world through expelling it from the 
Islamic conference. 


Dr. at-Turayki said the African summit conference of Monrovia and the nonalined con- 
ference of Havana strongly condemned these accords and that these resolutions are vie- 
wed as a direct condemnation of U.S. policy in the region. The U.S., Dr. at-Turayki 
said, should therefore review its Middle Eastern policy and be cautioned on any attempt 
to impose solutions by force and (fear). The enemy will not achieve peace. On the con- 
trary, he said, it would create danger for peoples of the region. 


Dr. at-Turayki said the colonialism which Africa got rid of is trying to stage a come- 
back. France, he said, is trying to establish colonial alliances to serve its interests 
and spread its hegemony in Africa. 


But Africa, he said, has refused this policy and the Khartoum African summit conference 
was the best reply to France's ambitions. 


Dr. at-Turayki said France even used mercenaries in its bid to establish a colonial 
alliance. He gave examples of mercenary incursions in Benin and the Comoros Islands, 
which were staged to oust the ruling regimes there. 


Dr. at-Turayki said as a result of the failure of these incursions, France then resorted 
to overt and direct military intervention, as had happened recently in central Africa. 


On the situation in Africa, Dr. at-Turayki said this is similar to the current situa- 
tions in the. Arab region. He said the racist regime in Palestine is not dissimilar to 
racist regimes in South Africa and Zimbabwe. 


He said colonialism created Zionism in occupied Palestine and racist regimes in Africa. 


He said Africa has now gained considerable political weight in international organi- 
sations by virtue to 50 African states which are now members of the U.N, 


Dr. at-Turayki said the military presence in Africa will no doubt end and France's pre- 
sence in the Reunion Island will also end. 


He said available accounts indicate that France is preparing to invade Benin, Congo, 
Guinea and Angola, and that evidence of this planning was found in documents captured 
after the invasion of Benin. 


Dr. at-Turayki wondered why the world community is not discussing Vietnam's intervention 
in Cambodia and France's invasion of Central Africa. 


He said ever since the death of President Pompidou, the French Government has been pur- 
suing a policy of intervention in Africa.

The foreign secretary said the Jamahiriyah's people is against any foreign intervention 
in Africa, whatever its source or nature. 


He said France used its military bases in the Gabon and Chad for intervention in Central 
Africa, confirming fears that these bases not only threaten host countries, but also 
neighboring nations. These nations therefore have the right of fighting the presence of 
these bases by all means available, 


The foreign secretary pointed out that the people of the Jamahiriyah demand an immediate 
end to French presence in Africa, because France was waging war of aggression against 
other countries and the live example for such aggression was the invasion of Central 
Africa. Mr. Bob Dinar who invaded Benin was in complete agreement with the Elysee. 


Concerning the Jamahiriyah's stand towards the Western Sahara, the foreign secretary 
said: "The POLISARIO started and (?grew) with the aid of the Jamahiriyah and the agree- 
ment signed in Algiers (?is) exactly similar to the one signed in Tripoli with the 
Mauritanian foreign minister, " 


The foreign secretary said that the Jamahiriyah's policy has not changed towards the 
POLISARIO since it started conducting the struggle against Spanish imperialism and to 
achieve self-determination, 


Answering a question concerning energy, the foreign secretary sais: "The energy cannot 
be discussed separately from other raw materials." 


The foreign secretary said that the black Americans have the right to concern themselves 
with African affairs because the black Americans came from an African origin and were 
harmed by the American policy in Arab region and in Africa. 


The foreign secretary indicated that the millions of dollars are paid to (Israel) by the 
American Government are lost by the American people including the blacks. Moreover, the 
dismissal of Andrew Young following his meeting with a Palestinian was a clear indication 
that the Americans have not understood that they have interests in the Arab world and 
Africa. 


He added: "The time is ripe for a change in American policy in favor of their people and 
for understanding the lessons of Iran and Nicaragua," 


Concerning the influence of the Zionist enemy on the American policy he said: "We as 
Arabs and Muslims are not against the Jews. On the contrary, we, as Semites, are nearest 
to the Jews, but we are against the Zionists influencing American policy." 


He indicated that terrorist Begin's influence in the Congress was stronger than Carter's. 


Answering a query on the slogans used by the American administration on human rights, he 
said: "These slogans are false, They discriminate between the rights of one people and 
those of others. Otherwise why don't we hear anything about the two million Palestinians 
and about the millions of refugees suffering from racial discrimination in South Africa?" 


(?Finally the) foreign secretary said: "There are attempts aimed at harming OPEC and they 
will be discussed in Caracas next December",
