LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI URGES MILITARY ACTION, OIL EMBARGO 


LD061924 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1900 GMT 6 Aug 82 


{[Text] Tripoli, 6 Aug (JANA) -- The brother leader has made the following statement to 
JANA: Israeli aggression has reached an extremely intolerable degree of insolence and 
ferociousness. The Arabs have no alternative, whatever the calculations at the proposed 
summit or without it, other than to send immediately 10 military divisions [firgqah]j tc 
Syria supported by 500 aircraft and to begin fighting with the same might and ferocity 
across Lebanon, Jordan and Syria to expel the enemy who has become reckless and who has 
violated the sanctity of all the Arab territories under occupation now. 


In the case of any political or materiel American support to him, the flow of Arab oil must 
be stopped immediately and ail Arab assets must be withdrawn from the banks which are under 
Zionist control. 


Libya is prepared to provide one division [firgah] and a number of squadrons of aircraft 
should suchaplan materialize.

[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 18, August 7, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The leader made the 
following statement to the Jamahiriyah News Agency: 


“Israeli aggression has reached an unbearable level of fierceness and impertinence. 
Regardless of calculations at the proposed summit, the only solution left to Arabs is to 
send ten military units at once to Syria, backed by 500 aircraft and begin the fighting 
with the same fierceness and force throughout Lebanon, Jordan and Syria to evict the enemy

of any U.S. political or material aid, Arab oil supply should be stopped immediately.... 
All Arab assets should be withdrawn from U.S. banks dominated by Zionists.... Libya is 
ready to send one of these units and flights of aircrafts when this plan is ready to be 
implemented and I am ready to lead these forces in person leaving Libya forever." 


LIBYA CONDITIONALLY AGREES TO ATTEND ARAB SUMMIT 


LD082012 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1928 GMT 8 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 8 Aug (JANA) -- The General-People's Committee for Foreign Liaison issued 
a statement today in connection with the call for an Arab summit conference. The text: 


At a time when the call is renewed for holding an Arab summit conference in Tunis, the 

Arab League headquarters, on Yemeni initiative, we agree to attend the conference on one 
condition which is clear, specific and irrevocable, otherwise there will be no participa- 
tion at all. It is that the conference must not be a conspiracy to arrange the evacuation 
of the Resistance from Lebanon by an Arab decision nor must it be for achieving the political 
aims of the aggression nor for accepting the conspiracy of the reactionaries headed by 

Saudi Arabia, America and Zionism against the Palestinian Revolution and against the 
Palestinian and Lebanese peoples. We accept if its sole topic is to discuss how to resist 
the aggression and how to dispatch 10 military divisions [firqah] supported by air squadrons 
to Syriato immediately begin fighting with the same ferocity and force across Lebanon, Jor- 
dan and Syria to expel the enemy and to force him to withdraw, and to use all political 

and economic weapons headed by oil and Arab deposits.

AL-QADHDHAFI: U.S. KNEW ABOUT ISRAELI INVASION 


LD062224 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 6 Aug 82 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution affirmed that the United States had prior 
knowledge of the Zionist invasion of Lebanon and supported it forcefully. 


In a statement to newsmen last night [6 Aug], he said: I draw the attention of the 
American people to the fact that their interests in the area will be destroyed if the 
Zionist invasion of Lebanon continues. 


ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS SECRETARIAT MEETS 7 AUG 


LDO71912 Tripoli JANA in English 1823 GMT 7 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 18, Aug 7 -- Jamahiriyah News Agency -- The Permanent 
Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress held a meeting today at noon in Tripoli that 
included members of the council of delegates and the leaders of several Arab popular and 
vocational organizations. 


The Secretariat discussed the situation in the Arab nation, the escalation of popular 
action to confront the Zionist invasion of Lebanon, and the expulsion and extermination 
of the Lebanese and Palestinian people. 


The meeting also reviewed the political statements issued yesterday by the Palestine 
Liberation Organization (PLO) and discussed the Secretariat's two-item agenda calling 
first on Arab vocational and popular organizations to hold an emergency meeting in 
Tunisia in a bid to respond to U.S. provocations and challenges to the joint Arab will, 
and to send delegations to Arab and foreign countries to explain the dimensions of the 
conspiracies against the Arab nation. 


Arab Action ‘Demanded’ 
LD081624 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1500 GMT 8 Aug 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 8 Aug (JANA) — The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's 

Congress has affirmed the need to have decisive and urgent steps to stop the barbaric 
Zionist invasion of Lebanon and to deter the new nazism which is threatening world peace 
and security. The Secretariat demanded, in a statement issued following the meeting of 
the delegates’ council of the Arab People's Congress yesterday, that the Arab countries 
mass their military forces and their material capabilities in the confrontation arena to 
face the continuous Zionist intransigence in exterminating our Palestinian and Lebanese 
Arab peoples. 


The Secretariat warned all defeatists and conspiraturs against the Arab nation that 
their interests will be destroyed by the Arab nation. it demanded that all Arab 
political parties and organizations and all trade unions and vocational organizations 
shoulder their responsibilities immediately by amassing the forces of the Arab people 
from the ocean to the Gulf, and by organizing ranks to enter the battle with dignity in 
defense of our nation's existence, and its honor, to demonstrate and take to the streets 
without getting permission from anyone in order to impose the move of the Arab govern- 
ments to sever relations immediately with the United States of America, to start 
boycotting its goods and to use the Arab oil as a basic weapon in the battle, to with- 
draw Arab assets from U.S. banks and not to expose Arab oil itself to the danger of 
destruction by the Arab nation.

In conclusion, the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People’s Congress called on all 
popular organizations and vocational and trade unions to attend the emergency meeting 

in Tunis which is usually attended by the executive bureaus of these organizations, due
