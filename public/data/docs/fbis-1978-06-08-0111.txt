LIBYA

INA: JALLUD IN PARIS ON UNDISCLOSED MISSION 
JNO71258Y Baghdad INA in Arabic 1240 GMT 7 Jun 78 JIN 


[Text] Paris, 7 June--It has been learned trom informed sources in the French capital 
Paris that Libyan Maj 'Abd as-Salam Jallud, member of the General Secretariat of the 
General People's Congress, has been in France for 48 hours on an unannounced visit. The 
Libyan Embassy in Paris and the French Foreign Ministry have declined to confirm or 

deny this report. 


Informed sources here say that Major Jallud's visit is related to Libyan-French relations, 
which began to cool noticeably following the French military intervention in Zaire and 
Chad. 


FOREIGN SECRETARY AT-TURAYKI CRITICIZES PARIS C UNFERENCE 
LD061632Y Tripoli JANA in English 1535 GMT 6 Jun 78 LD 


[Text] Niamey, June 6, JANA--Dr 'Ali at-Turayki, the foreig secretary and president of 
the current session of the African countries foreign ministers council, has affirmed: 
"The Organisation of African Unity regards the European conference in Paris as an inter- 
vention in Africa's internal affairs. The imperialist countries have no right to discuss 
African problems." 


Dr at-Turayki, in a statement to Niamey radio, added: "The aim of this meeting is to 
bring back imperialist control over Africa. The imperialist intervention has complicated 
and created tension in Africa." He affirmed: "The imperialist countries are the prime 
agitators of the problems in Africa." 


Dr at-Turayki said: "The stability of Africa and the African countries will mean the 

end of imperialism. That is why it resorted to sending mercenaries as happened in Benin 
and Sao Tome. When they failed it resorted to direct intervention using its armed forces 
and planes," 


Dr at-Turayki said: "If imperialism, with its armed forces and planes, is concerned 
about Africa, as it pretends, then it should send its forces to Namibia and South Africa 
to fight the racist regimes."
