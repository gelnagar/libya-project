Summary (AI): A 1975 CIA-reading-room cable transcribing Qadhdhafi's 45-minute speech at the opening of the Second Arab Youth Festival in Tripoli. He calls for a single Arab strategy for unity and liberation, criticizes Arab diplomacy after the 1973 war, and warns about Iranian intervention in Dhofar.
• The festival drew some 2,000 young men and women from several countries; only Egypt, Jordan, Bahrain and Lebanon were absent.
• Qadhdhafi greeted the PFLP-General Command for a Jerusalem operation timed to the festival.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya would fight a popular liberation war in southern Arabia if Iranian intervention in Dhofar was not ended.
• Qadhdhafi said the merger with Egypt had been replaced by mergers with the United States and possibly Israel, and dismissed the Djerba Declaration with Tunisia.
