Summary (AI): A January 1984 NPIC imagery note reports the first deployment of the Twin Plate mobile troposcatter communications system in Libya, with four R-410 relay sets at the Tarabulus (Tripoli) Air Defense Barracks. The sets were parked in a separately secured area alongside a command post bunker, suggesting an air defence command post and communications centre.
• Four R-410 (Twin Plate) troposcatter relay sets were observed at Tarabulus Air Defense Barracks; three were in travel mode and one partially erected.
• The area also held seven R-405 vans (not all annotated), one R-409, and 47 unidentified radio van trucks, indicating an operational communications unit.
• The Twin Plate system had been seen at the Tarabulus SAM depot in Libya but not previously seen deployed there.
• Analysts judged the facility probably functions as an air defence command post and communications centre.
• Coordinates given: 32-52-05N 013-21-44E.
• 1984-01: First observed deployment of Twin Plate mobile troposcatter communications system in Libya; four R-410 relay sets, three in travel mode and one partially erected.
