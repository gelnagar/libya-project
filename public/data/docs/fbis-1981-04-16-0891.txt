LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI VIEWS FOR AGRICULTURAL ADVISERS 


LD141414 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 14 Apr 81 


{Text] The leader of the revolution attended a meeting of the professional conference of 
agronomists. In his address to the agronomists, the brother leader said: We badly need 
revolutionaries, revolutionary agricultural advisers, and revolutionary doctors. He 
pointed out that students rebelled on 7 April 1976, and the workers rebelled in September 
1978 by turning from hired hands to partners. 


The leader pointed out that there are other professional quarters, but their duties are 
not up to the standard of the duties of the engineer, the agricultural adviser and the 
doctor. The leader said: Regrettably, up to now there was no revolutionary adviser and 
no revolutionary doctor. He added that this slogan was raised but was not applied. The 
leader attributed this lack of a revolutionary adviser to the lack of a revolutionary person 
in this medium. The leader said: The revolutionary does not come about by a decision, 
but by revolution and by applying it. The leader explained that the opportunity is now 
available to the revolutionary agricultural adviser, for wherever he goes he will find 
huge agricultural projects and that there are problems facing the peasants as a result of 
the complexity of agricultural processes. The revolutionary adviser should have all the 
means which would enable peasants and farmers to reach the required standard. 


The leader said: Agricultural production bolsters the freedom of the peoples and confirms 
their independence. Real freedom is economic freedom--which is confirmed by providing 
food and supplies. He stressed that America intimidated the world with wheat. There 
are continuing battles caused by America challenging the peoples. Despite the human, 
water and soil potentials in the Arab homeland, which could enable it to export its pro- 
ducts to the world, regrettably we find that the Arab nation is not yet aware of this 
great target. The enemy is gathering more of the sources of soil and water in the Arab 
homeland. He said: (?Through) the revolution an extensive process of reclamation took 
place. (?Clouds were mobilized), new water sources were discovered and dams were built. 
Despite this, because of the lack of revolutionary agricultural advisers the peasants are 
unable to better exploit the huge potentials available.
