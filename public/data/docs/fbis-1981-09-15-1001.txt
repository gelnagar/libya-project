LIBYA

OIL, MINERAL, CHEMICAL WORKERS CONFERENCE ENDS 


LD142048 Tripoli JANA in English 1843 GMT 14 Sep 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 14 Sep (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The fifth general conference of the Arab 
Union of Oil, Mineral and Chemical Workers ended its 5-day meetings in Tripoli last night. 


The conference, which was attznded by 14 Arab organizations and 18 international organiza- 
tions, approved the union's future work plan on trade unions, cultural, media, economic and 
social aspects. 


This work plan stressed the need to back the trade union movements in their continuous 
struggle to put into practice the slogan "Arab Oil for Arab Masses". The plan also stressed 
the need to be in permanent contact with Arab oil workers in order to confront all 
imperialist conspiracies and schemes facing the Arab homeland. 


The conference condemned the attempts aimed at breaking up the unity of the Organization of 
Petroleum Exporting Countries. 


In its final session, the conference issued a political communique stressing the importance 
of the effective role to be played by Arab workers in the oil and mineral sectors. The 
communique also praised the vanguard role of Arab workers and revolutionary forces in 
confronting and challenging the imperialist Zionist vicious offensive, and their ability 

to incite the working masses to respond to this offensive in order to settle the conflict 
once and for all in the interest of the Arab nation, 


The conference issued the following resolutions: 


1. Arab oil is the bulwark of the Arab nation's economy in time of peace and an effective, 
strategic weapon in the fateful battle. 


2. The current circumstances through which the Arab nation is passing necessitate quicken- 
ing the pace of unity of the Arab masses. 


3. The conference affirms its support for the Palestinian people's struggle under the 
PLO's leadership. It further calls for the opening of all Arab fronts for Palestinian 
fedayeen operations, and emphasizing the patriotic nature of the battle. 


4. The conference supports Syria's steadfastness against Zionism and capitulationist 
solutions drawn out by the Camp David alliance. 


5. The conference affirms the Arab nature and territorial integrity of Lebanon, condemns 
all conspiracies aimed at the country's division and salutes all Lebanese patriotic forces 
for supporting the Palestinian resistance movement. 


6. The conference affirms the solidarity of oil workers with the Libyan Arab people's 
steadfastness against American imperialism and salutes the armed vanguards of the Libyan 
Arab people which repulsed the American attack, It also salutes the resolutions made by 

the international conference for solidarity with the Jamahiriyah people, and the transforma- 
tion of Libya into a bastion of struggle against colonialism and world imperialism, 


7. The conference regrets the continuation of war between Iran and Iraq in spite of efforts 
exerted b: friendly countries to stop the war which casts negative effects on the Arab 


nation's central cause, Palestine.

8. The conference expresses its total support for the popular front for the liberation 
of Oman's struggle against the Qabus regime, and calls on the world's progressive forces 
to support this struggle. 


9. The conference backs the efforts exerted by African and Arab revolutionary forces 
for the solution of the Western Saharan people's problem, and granting the Saharan 
people the right of self-determination. 


10. The conference condemns the Numayri regime's oppressive practices directed against 
the struggle of honest Sudanese workers and the imprisonment of their progressive leaders. 


ll. The conference declares the alliance of Arab workers with world liberation movements, 
condemns racist oppression directed against the struggle of the South African and 
Namibian peoples and backs the struggle waged by Latin American peoples. 


12. The conference condemns the monopolist-imperialist conspiracies against the Arab 
nation's resources. It calls for the withdrawal of Arab funds from American and European 
banks, stopping Arab oil supplies to the U.S. and ending the preponderance of Western 
monopolisitic companies. It further believes that the creation of an oil glut on the 
world market is a dangerous conspiracy directed against the Arab nation and its future. 


On the other hand, members of the general secretariat of the Arab Federation of Federation 
of Petroleum, Mining and Petro-chemicals Workers [name of organization as received] were 
elected today. They are the Jamahiriyah, Syria, Algeria, Iraq and Palestine. 

Democratic Yemen and Lebanon were elected as reserve members.
