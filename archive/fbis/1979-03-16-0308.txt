ARAB PEOPIE'S CONGRESS ISSUES STATEMENT ON GARTER VISIT 


LD151656 Tripoli JANA in English 0830 GMT 15 Mar 79 LD 


[Text ] Beirut, 15 March, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab 
People's Congress closed its emergency session yesterday afternoon, It issued a statement, 
appealing to the masses of the Arab nation, their social, vocational, trade union and 
political organizations to take part in the Arab People's Congress and endeavour to adopt 
a mumber of resolutions. 


on the popular level it was decided that all the national vocational and trade-union 
organizations would be invited to hold emergency meetings, in the name of their masses 
opposing the conspiratorial visit [as received] and to invite all world organizations 
parallel to them to stand in solidarity with the struggle of the Arab people,

was also decided that an operation room would be set up at the Permanent Secretariat 
the Arab People's Congress to follow-up all the activities and actions taking place 
the national level; operation rooms would also be set up on the level of the 
countries whose forces are taking part in the Arab People's Congress, They would 
carry out the same duties, 


The Permanent Secretariat also decided to invite the educational, cultural and 
information establishments to contribute in the battle in which ».r nation is engaged 
and to invite the imams of mosques and church clergymen to expose, through their 
communication with the Arab masses, the conspiracy designed to strike our civilization 
and spiritual heritage. 


With regard to the masses the Permanent Secretariat resolved during its emergency 
session to organize marches and popular demonstrations simultaneously in all the Arab 
countries and in the world's countries where there are big Arab communities, The 
Secretariat declared the 2lst March a day of national rally against Carter's 
conspiratory visit and all the reactionary Zionist and imperialist schemes, It 
resolved to start calling for this day under this motto and to hold [word indistinct] 
contacts to this effect as soon as this paper is ratified, 


The statement pointed out that starting from the 2lst March a campaign will be staged 
to collect signatures for a concentrated petition denouncing the conspiracy. This 
petition will unite all Arab civizens in factories, universities, schools and 
markets, while the Arabs in this campaign will aim at millions of signatures as a 
symbol of Arab rejection of the alliance of the Egyptian president, Begin and Carter, 
Copies of these petitions are to be presented to the international organizations and 
all the friendly forces, 


It was decided that a comprehensive national strike would be declared on the ird [as 
received] March, the day of the land, All forces would stop work on this day and 
express their denunciation for the conspiratory visit--this in addition to a boycott 
of American and Egyptian planes, ships and all oil and communication routes as an 
expression of Arab rejection, 


The implementation of the Arab People's Congress's resolutions stipulating the attack 
of imperialist interests in the Arab homeland, particularly the American, was also 
decided. 


Within the context of official Arab policy it was decided that the countries which 
participated in Baghdad's ninth summit conference would be invited to hold an emergency 
meeting in order to take the measures likely to insure the implementation of all the 
resolutions passed at this summit, 


Moreover, Iraq and the National Steadfastness and Confrontation Front would be invited 
to hold an emergency meeting to carry out the resolutions concerning the confrontation 
of the consequences of Carter's agreement with the Egyptian president, Begin and take 
the necessary measures to face the new developments of the situation, 


An open memorandum would be addressed to the Arab rulers calling on them to withdraw 
their capitals and Arab savings in American banks and to use the oil weapon to confront 
the aggressive feverish American moves against our nation which were condemned at the 
Baghdad summit, 


This in addition to the boycott of American goods and means of communication,

The memorandum would call upon all Arab regimes, the masses in all Arab cowmtries to 
allow freedom of movement and expression of indignation at Carter's cmspiratory visit,

larly through demonstrations, marches and the participation in the general strike on

The statement declared that all the friendly allied forces with the Arab struggle in 
the world will be notified of all these resolutions so that they can express their 
solidarity with the Arab people in the way they find suitable. Moreover, all the 
international and Arab information trade unios, political progressive forces, bodies, 
and organizations will be notified of the Baghdad summit resolutions. These contain the 
transfer of the Arab league headquarters in Cairo, the suspension of Egypt's membership 
in the league, the withdrawal of recognition in the Egyptian regime, the implementation 
of the boycott policies on companies, institutims, and individuals in Eqypt who deal, 
directly or indirectly, with the Zionist enemy, the transfer of what remains of the 
Majahiriyah's establishments such as unions and associations in Cairo, and suspending 
all Arab economic aid to the Eqyptian regime, 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress had issued a political statement 
at the end of its extraordinary session asserting confidence in the Arab masses in 
defeating their Zionist imperialist enemies with their reactionary agents, as well as 
foiling their schemes, It also affirmed its solidarity with Palestinian Arab masses in 
the occupied land who heroicly confronted Carter's visit and the Zionist occupiers, 
hereby expressing their rejection of the shameful treacherous agreement, and the self- 
rule schemes. They also express their unwavering determination to continue the 
struggle, employing all means to liberate their usurped cowtry and regain their per- 
manent legitimate rights under the leadership of the Palestine Liberatio Organization 
P.L.0O., which is their sole legitimate representative. 


In the name of the Arab masses, the Secretariat underlined its solidarity with the 
heroic struggle of the Arab people in Lebanon, the wiity of its land and people, and 
standing against any attempt at liquidating the Palestinian resistence and the creation 
of a sectarian entity connected with (Israel). 


The Secretariat greeted the Arab masses in Eqypt and asserted the solidarity of the 
Arab masses with them in their struggle against the As-Sadat regime. They will stand 
by them in their confrontation to Carter's visit, their rejection of all the ensuing 
results and those to come in compliance with the capitulatory course of As-Sadat which 
is crowned by the treacherous agreement concluded with the Zionist enemy. They will 
stand with them, moreover, in their struggle to gain their democratic freedom 


The Secretariat affirmed its solidarity with the Steadfastmess and Confrontation Front 
and the Charter of the Joint National Action between Iraq and the steadfast Syria in 
the confrontation forefront with the Zionist enemy and its solidarity with the struggle 
of the national and progressive forces in the North Yemen under the leadership of the 
democractic national front and the Republic of Democratic Yemen which faces the con- 
spiracy of imperialism and reaction in the southern Arab Peninsula. The Secretariat 
affirmed the importance of the unity of the two Yemens and condemned the sending of 

the Egyptian regime's forces to back Qabus with the aim of dealing a blow to the 
people's -evolution in Amman and the attempt of sending forces to North Yemen to back 
Santa's regime. 


The Secretariat strongly condemned the statements of the American defence secretary and 
his threats that American imperialism will launch military operations to safeguard its 
monopolistic interests and control oil sources.

RIGHT TO ACT AGAINST ISRAELI-EGYPTIAN ALLIANCE CLAIMED 
AU152020 Rome ANSA in English 1953 GMT 15 Mar 79 AU 


[Text] Rome, March 15 (ANSA)--The Libyan Jamahiriyah today claimed the right to under- 
take "any action whatsoever" against the “alliance between Egypt and Israel" in state- 
ments here from the head of the International Relations Committee of the Libyan People's 
Congress (parliament) Hamad Shahati, 


Shahati told a press conference that the Tripoli [word indistinct] lays claim to this 
right since Egyptian President Anwar as-<Sadat is concentrating his troops along the 
Libyan frontier, He also described the peace treaty almost finalised by Tel Aviv and 
Cairo as a "further act of aggression against the Arab people", 


The peace which Israel, Egypt and the United States are trying to "impose" on the 
Middle East was not a "true peace because it does not take account of the Palestinians! 
rights", Shahati said, 


Asked about American threats to occupy the Middle Eastern oil wells if need be, Shahati 
replied laconically that it would "cost them dearly", On Italo-Libyan relations, he 
described them as "excellent" and voiced hopes for their further development, "Like 

us, the Italian people are interested in a just and lasting peace in the Mediterranean", 
he said,
