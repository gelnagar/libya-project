Summary (AI): A 1981 New York Times article reports that the State Department said it was not in the national interest for American pilots and mechanics to work for the Libyan Air Force, after reports that Americans were recruited through an operation linked to Edwin P. Wilson. It also mentions Libyan civil aircraft used to move troops and the 1980 closure of the US Embassy in Tripoli after mobs attacked it.
• The State Department said on 22 October 1981 that it was not in the national interest for American pilots and mechanics to work for the Libyan Air Force.
• Reports said American pilots flew helicopters in support of Libyan operations in Chad.
• The US withdrew its diplomatic presence from Libya in May 1980 because of Libyan support for terrorism.
