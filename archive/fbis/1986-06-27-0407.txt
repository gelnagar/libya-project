LIBYA

Eyewitnesses to Armed Forces ‘Mutiny’ Cited 
NC302229 Cairo MENA in Arabic 2150 GMT 30 Jun 86 


[Text] Tripoli, 30 Jun(MENA)— Al-Ahram says that it has been 
confirmed that there was another mutiny in the ranks of the 
Libyan Armed Forces as a result of resentment caused by the 
extremist line of Colonel al-Gadhdhafi and which finally led to 
the US. air strike against Libyan targets in April. The paper 
reports in tomorrow's edition that following the U.S. strike, the 
Libyan military felt that its position at home had become critical 
Consequently, there have been several attempted revolts, the 
most recent of which occurred several days ago. Eyewitnesses 
stated that they heard shots inside the Bab al-‘Aziziyah camp, 
Al-Qadhdhafi’s headquarters, and that eight rebels had been 
killed. 


Al-Ahram also reports: The effects of the U.S. ban on the sale of 
new civilian planes to Libya are beginning to become apparent. 
Libyan airlines has been experiencing a sharp drop in revenue 
since January, when the General People’s Committee forbade 
Libyan travel abroad. The long-serving planes were using too 
many spare parts, and the Libyan company could not purchase 
new ones as a result of the U.S. ban. Also, the price of food has 
risen sharply in Libya. A kilogram of meat now costs five dinars, 
and sufficient food is not available. Libyan authorities have tried 
to explain spiralling prices by blaming them on a new class of 
black market merchants. Authorities admit that this phenom- 
enon has been rather widespread in Libyan society of late.
