LIBYA

WORKERS DONATE 1-DAY'S WAGES TO PALESTINIANS 


LD302118 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 30 Mar 82 


{Text} Tripoli, 30 Mar (JANA) -- The masses of the vocational trade union congress at 
Tripoli harbor have decided to contribute 1 day's wages every month throughout 1982, 
beginning next April, to back the steadfastness and struggle of the Palestinian Arab 
people in Palestine and in the occupied Arab territories. This decision was made during 
the emergency meeting which the congress held tonight in support of the struggle of the 
Palestinian people against the racist Zionist forces of occupation.
