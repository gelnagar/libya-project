LIBYA

AT-TURAYKI RECEIVES CHAD REVOLUTION FIGURES 
LD061906 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 1715 GMT 6 Aug 79 LD 


[Text] At noon today the foreign secretary received at his office in Tripoli the leaders 
of the various political inclinations of the Chad revolution and the members of FROLINAT's 
unified committee for action, who are currently on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


In the course of the meeting they discussed the latest developments in the Chad issue and 
the good offices extended by the neighboring states to bring about national reconciliation 
in Chad while lessening foreign intervention, 


TURKISH DEPUTY PRIME MINISTER CETIN ENDS VISIT 3 AUG 
LD031310 Tripoli JANA in English 0847 GMT 3 Aug 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli 3 Aug, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Turkish deputy prime minister of state 
for economic affairs, Mr Hikmet Cetin, left Tripoli yesterday at the end of a few-day 
visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah during which he attended the 
meetings of the 5th session of the Turkish-Libyan Arab joint committee, 


Mr Cetin held talks which dealt with the reinforcement and development of relations 
between the two countries as well as the promotion of cooperation in the various fields. 


Mr Cetin expressed satisfaction at the results of the meetings and of his talks. He 
referred to the historic bonds tying the Libyan Arab and Turkish peoples. [In a state- 
ment to our correspondent Mr Cetin praised the tremendous progress achieved by the Great 
First of September Revolution in the various fields and at various levels. 


ARAB PEOPLE'S CONTRESS SECRETARIAT RESUMES MEETINGS 

LD031512 Tripoli JANA in English 1400 GMT 3 Aug 79 LD 

[Text] Tripoli, 3 Aug, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab 
People's Congress last night resumed the meetings of its 4th session for 1979 at the 
conference hall of Al-Shati Hotel in Tripoli. 

Discussion focused on the real perils threatening the Arab nation's existence as speakers 


insisted on the relevance of Arab unity as an important weapon in the Arab nation's fight 
against the schemes of Zionism and imperialism,

The speakers pointed out that the present stage requires a practical programme enabling 
the Permanent Secretariat to work in solidarity with the Arab masses and their national 
and progressive organizations, 


The conferees concentrated on the study of the internal regulation bill of the board of 
directors of the secretariat!s Arab people's trust, 


The bill includes the appointment of the board of directors, the conditions for member- 
ship, the function of members and the rules governing the organization of meetings and 
debates as well as the issue of the secretariat!s people, 


During this meeting.several committees were formed to facilitate the work of the [word 
indistinct ], a committee for collecting donations from various parts of the Arab world 
and another to draft the final resolutions and political statements. 


Proposals made for the preliminary follow-up of the international conference in solidarity 
with the Arab people due to be held in Lisbon, Portugal, were also considered, 


The conferees expressed great concern about the situation in Lebanon and called for the 
necessity for speedy action by the Arab nation to assume its national and militant
