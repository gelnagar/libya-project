[Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi speech at the opening session of the general conference of the

[Text] In the name of God. Gentlemen, members of the general conference of the international 
platform: I repeat what I told the preparatory committee, that Libya will be an interna- 
tional platform for resisting imperialism, racism, reaction and fascism, and this is the 
greatest honor of the Libyans and a medal of courage, a great honor and pride on the chest 

of every one of them. I hope they are worthy of this; but at the same time it is a serious 
responsibility and a heavy burden on all our shoulders. 

Gentlemen, it appears that success has crowned all the measures in transforming into action 
the resolution of the international solidarity conference with the Libyan people in 

August 1981, from the work of the preparatory committee to the success in convening this 
general conference. This shows the seriousness in the building of the framework necessary 
for the unification of the forces of struggle against Tupertabicts racism, reaction and 
fascism. 

Gentlemen, members of the general conference of the international platform: We have now 
reached the stage at which, with our strong will, we could draw up strategic plans for all 
our forces to confront imperialism, racism, reaction and fascism. No force on earth could 
prevent us from doing so since we have actually reached this phase. 

Gentlemen, what is important now is to prepare the factors of total success in drawing up 
the joint action in our battle against the common enemy. 

The convening of this international conference today is the signal of the birth of a new 
political phenomenon, introducing a high level (?body) in the (?caravan) of the international 
struggle and is a qualitative change in all the many forces that were dispersed and that 
will be fighting on the same front and against the same enemy. This is a historic day as 

it is a strategic turning point in the thinking and action of these forces, something that 
will reflect positively on the course of the battle fought by these forces and in their 
interest. Powerful and mighty efforts will jointly enter battle as from the date of the 
setting up of this international platform. This meeting is only a declaration of the 

setting up of collective defense by the forces of freedom, progress and peace in the world.

TURKEY'S OZAL BEGINS OFFICIAL CONTACTS 14 JUNE 
TA141841 Ankara Domestic Service in Turkish 1600 GMT 14 Jun 82 

[Text] State Minister and Deputy Prime Minister Turgut Ozal, who is visiting the Socialist 
People's Libyan Jamahiriyah, has began his official contacts. Ozal today met separetely 
with Housing Secretary Muhammad al-Manqush and Communications Secretary Salim Hudah. 
Cooperation between Turkey and Libya in various fields was reviewed during the talks. 

Ozal is expected to meet tomorrow with General People's Committee Secretary ‘Aziz al-Tahhi 
and certain ministers. 

The Turkey-Libya joint economic committee met today in Tripoli and began its work. The 
promotion of bilateral trade relations and the work of Turkish construction companies in 
Libya are to be discussed by two subcommittees. The Turkish delegation to the joint 

economic committee is headed by Ozal, while Al-Manqush is heading the Libyan delegation.
