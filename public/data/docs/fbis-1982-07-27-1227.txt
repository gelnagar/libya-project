AT-TURAYKI RETURNS FROM MISSION TO SYRIA 26 JULY 


LD262044 Tripoli JANA in English 1822 GMT 26 Jul 82 

[Text] Tripoli, Shawal 6, July 26, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Dr. ‘Ali ‘Abd as-Salam 
at-Turayki flew home today after a visit to Damascus during which he met with President 
Hafiz al-Asad. 


Dr. at-Turayki was assigned to this mission by Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi and President 
Chadli Bendjedid of Algeria. 


Dr. at-Turayki briefed President al-Asad on the meeting between Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and 
President Bendjedid. 


Dr. at-Turayki also briefed the Algerian officials on his visit to President al-Asad. 


JANA EDITOR CRITICIZES PLO 'RIGHTWING' MOVES 


LD261034 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 26 Jul 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 26 Jul (JANA) -- The reactionary Palestinian rightwing these days has been 
intensifying suspicious moves with the reactionary Arab regimes and the Zionist enemy -- 
moves aimed at carrying out the reactionary, Zionist and American plans seeking to entrench 
the Zionist sccupation of the Arab lands and to impose surrender on the Arab nation. Such 
moves have begun in the wake of the failure of capitulation efforts due to the firmness and 
steadfastness of the active forces of the Arab nation and the Palestinian Resistance that 
rejects all surrender plans intended for the Arab nation. 


Commenting on these dubious moves, the JANA political editor emphasizes that the methods 

and channels used by the Palestinian reactionary rightwing confirm it is nothing more than 

an instrument of imperialism and Zionism, just like the rest of the reactionary Arab regimes 
that move in the orbit of imperialism and are hostile to the Arab nation and its active forces. 


The contacts made by the Palestinian reactionary rightwing with the Zionists and the 
reactionary Arab regimes and the whispering in a number of world capitals over the reviving 

of colonialist Resolution 242, and the talk about peace moves, in light of the Zionist 
occupation and the massacre of Palestinians and Lebanese in Lebanon, confirm that this right- 
wing is now playing the role of godfather for capitulation, for abandonment of the Palestinian 
cause and for the extermination of the Palestinians. 


The political editor affirms that these instruments have been pushed by imperialism, Zionism 
and reaction to carry out the American-Zionist policies and their schemes in the Arab region, 
and, therefore, the confrontation of the American-Zionist plan must begin with the confronta- 
tion of these groups, who are playing a dirty role, andthe fringes that manifest themselves 
around the resistance movement. 


The political editor says any talks about an end to hostilities, a dialogue or peace treaty 
with the Zionist enemy, means enabling this enemy to pick up the fruit of its savage and 
barbaric invasion and encouraging it to carry on with its aggression. At the same time, it 
means showing that the Arabs can be made to capitulate by force of arms. 


This phenomenon, represented by the Palestinian reactionary rightwing, must be confronted by 
no less a violent confrontation than the one opposed to Arab reaction and other agents and 
traitors.

JANA SAYS EGYPT PRESSING FOR PLO CAPITULATION 


LD262208 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 26 Jul 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 26 July (JANA) -- The reactionary regimes revolving in the U.S. administra- 
tion's orbit have begun to act openly to bolster their relations with Palestinian reactionary 
rightists and to rely on it to spread publicity for the acceptance of agreements of treason 
and capitulation, and to create a favorable atmosphere that would turn the Zionist invasion 
of Lebanon into a fait accompli. 


The meeting [PLO Executive Committee member] Ahmad Sidqi ad-Dajani is holding with the 
Egyptian minister of state of foreign affairs, Butrus Ghali, and others in Cairo falls 
within the framework of these relations which give complete sponsorship to the imperialist 
plan aimed at enabling the Zionist enemy to impose its hegemony, to escalate its aggression 
and to widen the scope of its invasion of the Arab area. 


Any relations the Palestinian right and reactionary regimes seek to establish are no more 
than a prelude to the completion of the imposition of the capitulationist plan and to sub- 
mission to the Zionist enemy. It is the same plan the dead traitor as-Sadat accepted and 
helped to implement after completely becoming an agent and mercenary of American imperialism. 


In this respect, it should be noted with regard to the expansion of meetings between the 
Palestinian reactionary rightists and reactionary regimes that Egyptian politicians are now 
pushing the Palestinian rightists toward exercising a bigger role in making the Palestinians 
accept capitulation. 


The Egyptian role in this regard is clear and well-defined. It is consistent with the 
position of the Egyptian regime which is fully bound to the Camp David agreements and the 
humiliating condition they embody -- recognizing no right for the Palestinians.
