GUINEAN PRESIDENT SEKOU TOURE ARRIVES 
LD181644yY Tripoli JANA in English 1415 GMT 18 Apr 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, April 18, JANA--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, the leader of the great 
Al-Fatah revolution, received President Ahmad Sekou Toure, the supreme leader of the 
revolution, the general secretary of the Guinean Democratic Party and the president of 
the Republic of Guinea, who arrived in Tripoli last night on an official few-days visit 
to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


Present to meet the Guinean [word indistinct] were Staff-Major ‘Abd as-Salam and Major 
al-Khuwaylidi al-Humaydi, members of the General Secretariat of the General People's 
Congress. Also participating in the reception were the chairman and members of the 
General People's Committee, the under secretaries, the diplomatic corps in the Jamahiriyah, 
the leaders of the people's congresses and committees, a number of the armed forces and 
police officers and a large gathering of the Libyan Arab people. 


The president of the Republic of Guinea was accompanied by a large official delegatisu 
including the prime minister, the ministers of social affairs, justice, information and 
planning, the president of the National Assembly, the director of banks, the director of 
Guinean Air Lines and a number of state officials. 


President Sekou Toure was reported by a JANA correspondent to have expressed his dsep 
satisfaction for his being in the Jamahiriyeh. 


In this respect he said: "I thank God Almighty who has enabled me to visit the 
Jamahiriyah and to meet with its revolutionary leader Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi." 


He added “my cherished dream has now come true. I have been longing for visiting this 
noble country ever since the advent of the great Al-Fatah revolution. Now my dream has 
come true. I am now on the Libyan land where I can get in touch with the courageous 

people of the Jamahiriyah and the leader of its revolution, Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi." 


He expressed his deep appreciation to the heroes who broke this revolution. At the head 
of those heroes comes Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, who is not only a leader of the Jamahiriyah 
but he is also a leader of the Islamic world and all the African Continent. 


In conclusion he said: "I do not consider myself a guest in this country. Rather, I 
look upon myself as a citizen and a struggler who belongs to the people of the Jamahiriyah. 


I wish to convey my deep appreciation for every Libyan citizen in this country."

[Text] Tripoli, April 13, JANA--Yesterday morning the city of Tripoli witnessed a mass 
rally made up of hundreds of thousands of Arab Libyan citizens together with some of the 
Arab residents in the Jamahiriyah, The rally marched through the streets to the 
people's palace shouting “long live the leader of the Great September Revolution, 
Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi."
