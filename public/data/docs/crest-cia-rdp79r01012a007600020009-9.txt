Summary (AI): An Intelligence Advisory Committee post-mortem, dated 25 June 1956, on a national intelligence estimate about US interests in Libya. It finds no major intelligence deficiencies; the main problem was speculative judgment about the political struggle expected after the king's death. The document is heavily redacted and has no Tripoli-specific content.
• IAC noted the findings of NIE 36.5-56 on 19 June 1956 and approved the action set out in section II.
• Finding 1: no major intelligence deficiencies were encountered in preparing the estimate.
• The chief estimative problem was the outcome and implications for US interests of the political struggle likely to follow the death of the king.
• Section II asks the IAC agencies to levy requirements in their fields to overcome the deficiencies noted, but the specific deficiencies are redacted.
