LIBYA

LIAISON BUREAU WARNS MOROCCO OF U.S. TIES 


LD131424 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 13 Feb 82 


[Text] The People's Foreign Liaison Bureau this morning summoned the Moroccan ambassador 

to the Jamahiriyah, who was questioned on reports from news agencies and Arab and inter- 
national radio stations on the intention of Morocco to grant the United States military bases 
on its soil. The ambassador was informed that the Jamahiriyah is very annoyed by these reports; 
if they are true, this would be a serious matter. The reports make it appear that Arab 
homeland is being sold to America in an auction and, at the same time, it deprives the peoples 
of this nation their independence, for which they struggled one after the other. The people's 
Foreign Liaison Bureau also informed the Moroccan ambassador that if this is true, it is a 
serious matter and its repercussions will be very grave. 


V. 16 Feb 82 Q 2 NORTH AFRICA 


PDRY PRESIDENT ARRIVES 15 FEB FOR CONSULTATIONS 


LD151902 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 15 Feb 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 15 Feb (JANA) -- 'Ali Nasir Muhammad, secretary general of the Yemeni 
Socialist Party, this afternoon arrived in the Jamahiriyah where he was welcomed by the 
leader of the revolution. 


In a statement to JANA, ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad said: I have come to the Socialist People's 
Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah for consultations with Brother Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 
Great 1 September Revolution, on the matters of interest to our Arab nation, on developments 
in the Arab region in the light of the unlawful alliance between the Zionist enemy and the 
United States, on the role of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front and the importance 
of bolstering it in the face of the imperialists and Zionists. 


"Ali Nasir Muhammad pointed out that the important political developments in the Arab 
homeland demand concacts with the brothers in the pan-Arab front for steadfastness and 
confrontation and the adoption of a unified stance toward developments. He said it is 
important for us as we meet in the land of the Great September Revolution to express 

our satisfaction with and extreme admiration for the development of relations of struggle 
between the two fraternal countries. 


A meeting was held this evening between the brother leader of the revolution and Brother 
‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, secretary general of the PDRY Socialist Party. 


AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES GUINEAN, CYPRIOT VISITORS 


NC120902 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 12 Feb 82 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution yesterday received Lt Gen Lansane Diane, member 
of the Political Bureau of the party Central Committee and the defense minister of the 
People's Revolutionary Republic of Guinea. The brother leader of the revolution also 
received Vasos Lissaridhis, leader of the Cypriot Socialist Party. 


ANTI-OAU FORCE DEMONSTRATIONS CONTINUE IN CHAD 


LD141600 Tripoli JANA in English 1428 GMT 14 Feb 82 


[Text] Nairobi, 14 Feb (JANA) -- Popular demonstrations have continued for the second 
consecutive day in Chad's capital Ndjamena against the presence of the African force in 
the country. The demonstrators called for the withdrawal of the force as it had proved 
ineffective in carrying out its role. 


The Chadian President Goukouni Oueddei said in a speech to the demonstrators that the 
country has fallen victim to a conspiracy and renewed his government's rejection of the 
Nairobi conference resolutions regarding Chad.
