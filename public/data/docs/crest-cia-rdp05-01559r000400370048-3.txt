Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of a Buenos Aires TELAM report says Qadhafi offered Argentina unlimited cooperation, especially financial, and that a 35-member Libyan trade delegation would visit Argentina to expand meat and industrial trade. Nothing here concerns places or events in Tripoli itself.
• Libya imported 114,602 tons of beef and 80,110 tons of mutton in the first nine months of 1975.
• The Libyan trade delegation was to stay in Argentina for two weeks and was organised with the Argentine Embassy in Libya.
• Argentina's agricultural products were the main Argentine exports to Libya.
• 1976-03-19: Qadhafi said Libya was ready to extend unlimited cooperation, especially financial, to Argentina; a 35-member Libyan importers' delegation was to depart for Buenos Aires.
