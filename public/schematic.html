Sites in the record with no usable location

  • Black Horse prison (al-Hissan al-Aswad). A prison from the Italian colonial era that Abu Salim replaced after its completion in 1984. No published source places it on a map.
  • Military intelligence prison, Tripoli. A former detainee describes being held there after the May 1984 arrests. The building's location is not given.
  • 7 April prison, Benghazi. Used for interrogations before detainees were moved to Jdeida in Tripoli. It lies outside this map.
  • Secret detention centres. Amnesty International reports incommunicado detention in unnamed centres. They cannot be mapped.

Notes on positions

Dashed rings show the possible margin of error for each marker. Abu Salim's position comes from a published coordinate. The other sites are placed by district or suburb only. "Suspected" marks a facility that existed in Tripoli with no source tying it to a specific year under Gaddafi.
