Summary (AI): A 1956 CIA Board-style validity review (TAC-D-100/14) checks the Libyan section of a 1954 National Intelligence Estimate on North Africa. It finds the estimate largely accurate on Libyan internal stability, US base talks, French and British influence, and Egyptian challenges, but says it overstated Turkish competition with Egypt and missed Libya-USSR diplomatic ties.
• The document is TAC-D-100/14, dated 25 June 1956, a validity study of the Libyan section of NIE 71-54 published 31 August 1954.
• NIE 71-54 paragraph 66 over-emphasized the extent to which Turkey would compete with Egypt for influence in Libya.
• NIE 71-54 did not anticipate the establishment of diplomatic relations between Libya and the USSR or recent Soviet gestures toward Libya.
• The estimate correctly assessed prospects for passage of the US-Libyan Base Agreement, further French-Libyan difficulties, decline of British influence, and Egypt's challenge to Western position in Libya.

Validity Study of the Libyan Section of NIE 71-54: 

Probable Developments in North Africa, 

published 31 August 1954 

l. With minor exceptions, the estimates concerning Libya 
made in NIE 71-54 appear to have been valid. The fundamental 
factors noted in NIE 71-54 as affecting internal political stability 
continue to do so. The analysis of Libyan external relations cor- 
rectly assessed the prospects for passage of the’ US-Libyan Base 
Agreement, further French-Libyan difficulties, the decline of 
British influence in Libya, and Egypt's challenge to the Western 
‘position in the kingdom. 

2 NIE 71-54 (paragraph 66) did over-emphasize the extent 
to which Turkey would successfully compete with Egypt for influence 
in Libya, The estimate also did not anticipate the establishment of 
diplomatic relations between Libya and the USSR or recent Soviet 
gestures toward Libya.
