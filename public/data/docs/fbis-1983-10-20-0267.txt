LIBYA

Libyan-Soviet Committee Begins Talks 
LDI91638 Tripoli JANA in English 1438 GMT 19 Oct 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Muharram 13, Oct 19, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY — The joint Libyan-Soviet committee began its 9th 
session yesterday in the conference hall at People’s Foreign 
Liaison Bureau in Tripoli. The meeting was attended by secre- 
tary of the General People’s Committee for Electricity, and from 
the Soviet side Mr. Mulid Minon [name as received], head of the 
International Committee of Foreign Trade Relations of the 
Soviet Union 


In the first meeting the Agenda was reviewed. It consists of the 
two cooperation and the means needed to promote it in the area 
of economic engineering, petrol industries, agriculture, commu- 
nications, transport, geology, health and trade exchange and 
scientific and cultural cooperation
