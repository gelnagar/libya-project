LIBYA

JANA EDITOR COMMENTS ON ASSASSINATION RUMORS 


LD180852 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 18 Nov 81 


[Text] The leader of the revolution last night, Tuesday, received Turkish Deputy 
Premier Turgot Ozal. 


The leader also last night received Dr ‘Abd al-Wahhab Mahmud, the private envoy of Yemeni 
President ‘Ali Abdallah Salih. 


JANA's political editor has commented on the tendentious and venomous reports leaked by 
the American intelligence organs through some Western news agencies and information media, 
who practice spreading misleading information, in an ill-intentioned attempt to spread 
confusion and to deepen evil designs which have nurtured in the feverish brains of the 
bunch which governs the White House, and spread tendentious, rancorous and misleading 
rumors, the gist of which is that Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi was subjected yesterday to 

an assassination attempt, according to the claims of the American administration and 

the Western information media. 


The political editor says that such rumors which were cunningly spread and which were 
produced by a rancorous and evil spirit had been received in Tripoli with laughter, mockery 
and derision. They are considered to be 2 perfect example of the fact that those who rule 
America have the mentality of infants and act according to their sick dreams and behavior 
which are governed by the desire for crime and bloodthirstiness. 


Faced with this simple inhuman thinking which characterizes American plicy, one cannot 
help but die laughing and mocking the mentality which governs the "White House and which 
is bent on expressing the bankrupt American policy through accusations and lies -—- the 
latest of which have just appeared on the international arena. 


The cheap American intrigue is not new to the Libyan Arab people who, together with all 
the peoples who are hostile to American policy, every day witness evidence proving 
American intrigue and insolent attitudes which are being carried out along evil paths by 
despicable people who are without any taste and who are spreading American infamy with the 
lowest means and ugliest methods. 


Official's Denial to AFP 
NC171841 Paris AFP in English 1836 GMT 17 Nov 81 
[Excerpt] Paris, 17 Nov (AFP) -- A Libyan official today implicitly denied rumors cir- 


culating in Western capitals to the effect that Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi had 
been assassinated. 


"The situation is perfectly stable throughout Libya and nothing abnormal has happened," 
the official of the Foreign Information Bureau in Tripoli contacted by telephone from 
Paris added. 


Asked about the rumors, the official said, "Nothing surprises us because we are used to 
baseless rumours put out periodically against Libya by international imperialism."

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES FRG PARLIAMENTARIAN 


LD181026 Hamburg DPA in German 0958 GMT 18 Nov 81 


[Excerpt] Tripoli/Bonn, 18 Nov (DPA) -- The Libyan head of state, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
received Juergen Moellemann, foreign and security policy spokesman of the FDP Parliamentary

AL-QADHDHAFI WARNS KHALID OF ZIONIST PLANS 


LD171444 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 17 Nov 81 


[Text] The leader of the revolution has sent a message to King Khalid with copies to 
other Arab kings and presidents, calling for a more profound understanding of the imminent 
dangers to the Arab nation in light of the fact that the Zionist enemy is diligently and 
cunningly working to implement its aggressive plan for the establishment of a Zionist 

state stretching from the Euphrates to the Nile and from Jerusalem to Mecca and Al-Madinah. 


The leader has drawn attention to the fact that the Zionist enemy's plan seeks in time 
to make the northern part of Saudi Arabia and the western part of Irea -- the region of 
the joint Saudi-Iraqi-Jordanian border -- a no man's land andthat the Jewish heavy stick 
which subjugated southern Lebanon next plans to sweep over east of the Jordan. 


In his message, the leader made it clear that the security and existence of the Arab 
nation are effectively exposed to Israeli danger. This is the basic issue which should 
be studied and debated at the forthcoming Arab summit conference. Unless pan-Arab 
efforts are devoted to confronting this issue, the Arab summit will then be meaningless 
and it will be treachery to participate in it. 


GOVERNMENT VIEWS U.S. OIL BOYCOTT THREAT 


LD181038 Kuwait KUNA in Arabic 0943 GMT 18 Nov 81 


[Excerpts] Tripoli, 18 Nov (KUNA) -- Libya said today that the U.S. threats to halve 
imports of Libyan oil is an American attempt to undermine stability in Libya. It stressed 
that the suspension by the American company Exxon, one of the major world oil companies, 
of its activities in Libya will not affect the production of Libyan oil. 


An official statement by the Libyan Government says the U.S. declaration that it is 
considering imposing a boycott on its imports of Libyan oil "is part of the American 
endeavors to destabilize Jamahiriyah." 


On the other hand, the chairman of the National Libyan Oil Company, Wahid Umar [name 
indistinct], said the withdrawal of the American oil company, Exxon, is not unexpected. 
He said: "Naturally, it is sad to lose a partner of 25 years standing. However, in 
the oil industry, one party withdraws and 10 others come knocking at the door." He 
added that the withdrawal is "politically prompted" and that other oil companies are 
resisting such pressures. 


The president of OPEC, Marc N'guema, who is on an official visit to Tripoli, said that 
no oil company must be able to feel that it is able to threaten a member country of 
the Organization of Petroleum Exporting Countries, which implements the decisions of 
the organization.
