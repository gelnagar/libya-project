Al-"l baydi-Cheysson 13 April Meeting in Paris 
LD141049 Tripoli JANA in English 0907 GMT 14 Apr 83 


[Text] Paris, Jumada Thani 31, Apr 14, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY—Our correspondent here reports that in 
yesterday's meeting between ‘Abd al-"Ati al-"Ubaydi, secretary 
of the People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau, his accompanying del- 
egation, the French foreign minister, Claude Cheysson, and 
French Foreign Ministry officials, agreement was reached on a 
timetable to implement the cooperation agreement signed by the 
joint committee last month 


The French side in talks expressed the appreciation and respect 
of the French Government for Jamahiriyah’s policy and the 
Great Al-Fatih Revolution’s orientation. It stressed 
Jamahiriyah’s important role as an African [words indistinct 
member of the Nonaligned Movement
