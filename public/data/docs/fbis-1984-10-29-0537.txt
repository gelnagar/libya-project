LIBYA

Al-Turayki Meets With Burkina’s Zongo 
LD281529 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 
28 Oct 84 


[Text] Dr Ali ‘Abd al-Salim al-Turayki, secretary of the People’s 


Committee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison, this 
afternoon had a meeting with Captain Henri Zongo, member of 
the National Council of the revolution and minister of economic 
development in Burkina Faso who is on a visit to the Socialist 
People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


During this meeting, they reviewed bilateral relations and ways 
and means of promoting and strengthening relations of cooper- 
ation for the common good. They also discussed African and 
international issues of common interest, and discussed intensify- 
ing joint efforts to back up the African liberation movement. 


Captain Henri Zongo expressed the appreciation and thanks of 
the people of Burkina Faso for the support and backing that the 
people of the Jamahiriyah have extended and continue to extend 
in its confrontation of imperialist conspiracies against the aspira- 
tions and options of the people of Burkina Faso. 


He said: We appreciate the principled stance of the 1 Septem- 
ber Revolution and its leader Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi 
in supporting and backing the African liberation movements, 
confronting racist regimes, and helping the downtrodden every- 
where.
