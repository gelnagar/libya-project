LIBYA

JALLUD ADDRESSES AFRICAN ECONOMY MINISTERS 


LD272300 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 27 Apr 82 


[Text] Staff Maj ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud said the meeting of African ministers of 
planning and economy comes when the world's economic difficulties are increasing. 
Mankind faces sharp economic problems that embrace even advanced states. 


In his speech at the opening session of the eighth ministerial conference of the 
African economic committee, which began its work yesterday evening at the Green Hope 
Hall in Tripoli, he said: This meeting gives the participants strong incentive to 
adopt suitable resolutions for which the African peoples are waiting because Africa's 
problems can only be solved by Africans themselves and an African determination to face 
the problems confronting the continent.

He said this can only be accomplished through true resolve and the ability of the 
Africans to solve their own problems. It will be resolved by Africans becoming 
accustomed to utilizing their natural resources and relying on their own abilities. He 
explained that colonialism is now trying to substitute the past political colonization 
of Africa with new economic and social colonialism. 


He said all attempts at UN conferences have failed to produce a world economic system 
because the colonial powers obstruct the aspirations of the developing states. He added 
that the advanced colonial powers have been trying to perpetuate their policy of 
maintaining foreign economic domination over African economies.
