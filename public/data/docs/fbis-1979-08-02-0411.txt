ARAB PEOPLE®S CONGRESS STARTS FOURTH SESSION 1 AUG 
LDO11642 Tripoli JANA in English 1443 GMT 1 Aug 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 1 Aug, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab 
People*s Congress opened its fourth session for this year at Al-Shate Hotel in Tripoli 
last night. 


Omar Himidi, its secretary-general, opened the meeting by saying: "This session comes at 
a time which requires of us objectivity. For we must be aware of the danger surrounding 
us and assess the results of our work. We must examine what is taking place around us and 
‘ understand our opponents! stands and position so that we could launch a struggle with a 
constructive goal, a struggle which can establish the serious steps taken by the Arab 
nation to confront the imperialist-Zionist-reactionary conspiracy. 


"The Arab nation through its progressive national forces epitomized in the Arab People’s 
Congress and the Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation has been able to expose hostile 
plans to reveal the capitulatory policy adopted by the defeated Egyptian president. Through 
steadfastness and confrontation the Arab nation has succeeded to isolate the Egyptian 
regime at official and popular Arab levels and is endeavoving to tighten the grip of 
isolation on this regime at intermational level after it nas been exposed before world 
public opinion as an agent of world Zionism and American imverialism. However the fact 
remains that Carter, Begin and As-Sadat continue to carry out their plans and have even 
succeeded in transforming Egypt from a base for the Arab revolutionary movement into a 
hostile front and a lackey of world Zionism. This tripartite alliance is determined to 
realize its goals, it continued aggression on south Lebanon being a proof to that." 


Omar Hamidi went on to say: "If met with obstacles, in its attempts to liquidate the 
Palestinian cause and revolution, the alliance can always find other deviant means not 
only to make use of its own endeavours but also of those of their opponents, for these 
endeavours which have succeeded in drawing the attention of world public opinion to the 
serious situation in the Arab region andits influence on peace and nations?’ interests are 
also used through the allies of the American-Israeli-Egyptian alliance to exert pressure 
on the arab nation and force it to accept half solutions." He added: "In this stage we 
must be aware of what is going on around as as our enemies are determined to carry out 
their plans and our reply to them so far is not up to the requirements of the battle. 


"Our battle necessitates that progressive and national forces draw up a militant programme 
to achieve the following: 


"1. The Arab masses should shoulder their national responsibilities in confronting hostile 
conspiracies. 


"2, Serious endeavours to deal a blow to imperialist interests in the Arab region. 


"3, To caution against the reactionary-Zionist-imperialist movement and not to be de- 
ceived by their desigs. 


"4. To rally around and develop the two tools of the transitional Arab struggle: the 
steadfastmess and confrontation front and the Arab People*®s Congress.

"5, To carry out national duties towards the Palestinian revolution and the progressive 
national movement in Lebanon and Syria." 


Omar Hamidi pointed out that the period ofsteadfastness through which the Arab nation 
has gone thanks to its struggle and sacrifices will be wasted unless it is followed 

by a militant progremme of confrontation and steadfastness against imperialist-Zionist- 
reactionary forces, 


He then concluded his speech by saying that the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab 
People's Cong ess which was given responsibility by the masses through the resolutions 
of the first and the second congresses should follow the path of progressive national 
struggle with all its power, being completely certain that its opponents would not give 
in unless they were defeated and that an offensive 2s the best means of defence, 


Speeches were delivered by delegations of Syria, Egypt, the PLO,,the Lebanese national 
progressive movement, the international federation of Arab trade unions, the Popular 
Front for the Liberation of Oman and the Popular Front for the Liberation of Saguia 
el Hamra and Rio de Oro, 


Mr Ali al-Gharsan the head cf the Syrian delegation, expressed his thanks and appreciation 
to the Jamahiriyah for convening this meeting and for sparing no efforts to make a 

success of the conference. He spoke of the current political situation in the Arab 

region and the gravity of the hostile polts and the necessity of confronting such plots 

by a clear working programme, 


Mr Abdulrahman Khamisi also stressed the necessity of confronting the challenges facing 
the Arab nation and warned of the current situation faced by the Arab nation and of the 
role of imperialism and its allies in Europe. 


In this session Dr Abdullah Sadeh, the head of the Lebanese delegation, said this session 
should mobilize the Arab masses to confront imperialist-Zionist-reactionary plots and spoke 
of the conspiracy that Lebanon is facing. He added that the people in south Lebanon 

are subject to immense inflictions and that unless the Arab nation takes immediate 

action, Lebanon will face a major disaster. 


The participants then commemorated thedeceased Zuhayr Muhsin, former head of the PLO 
Military Department and leader of As-Sa'iqah, praying for God's mercy on hig soul. 


The Permanent Secretariat then resumed studying the agenda which included the following: 
1. The Arab and international political situation, 

2. Distribution of the Permanent Secretariat departments among members. 

3. Discussing the draft charter of the isl esinle progressive front. 

4, Concluding discussion on national (?popular syndicates). 


5. Following-up the implementation of the Permanent Secretariat's plan for 1979 and 
its resolutions, 


6. Following-up the preparation for the world conference in solidarity with the Arab 
people and their central issue: Palestine.

GUINEAN PRESIDENT SEKOU TOURE ENDS VISIT 
LDO11518 Tripoli JANA in English 1440 GMT 1 Aug 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli, 1 Aug, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Guinean President Ahmed Sekou Toure, 
left Tripoli today after a two-day visit to the Jamahiriyah during which he held talks on 
bilateral relations and issues conceming the two countries. 


He was seen off at Tripoli International Airport by Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir; Lt 
Col Mustafa al-Kharrubi; *Abd al-*Ati al-*Ubaydi, the general secretary of the General 
People's Congress; Muhammad 'Abd al-Mabrum, secretary of the General People*s Committee 
for Civil Services; a number of armed forces officers, the head of the Protocol 
Department of the Foreign Secretariat and the Guinean ambassador to the Jamahiriyah and 
his staff.

CONTRIBUTION TO UNRWA--Amman, 11 July--Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah has 
contributed $3 million to the United Nations Relief and Works Agency, UNRWA, to help cover 
preparatory education expenses UNRAW provides for Palestinian refugees. UNRWA sources say 
that Libya's contribution for this year totals $4 million. [Amman JNA in Arabic 1510 GMT
