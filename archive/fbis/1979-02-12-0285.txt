LIBYA

JANA REPORTS AL-QADHDHAFI INTERVIEW WITH LEBANESE PAPER 


LDO71850 Tripoli JANA in English 1625 GMT 7 Feb 79 LD 


[Text] Beirut, 7 Feb, JANA--The editor of the Lebanese daily AL-KIFAH AL-ARABI held 
the following press interview published last Monday with Colonel Muammar al-Qadhdhafi, 
leader of the Great lst of September Revolution and secretary-general of the General 
People's Congress: 


Question: You said the objective of revolutionary committees is to encourage the masses 
to practice power in the real sense of the word and directly in order to prevent 
fascists from infiltrating and taking over power, hence the revolution. By which gauges 
and controls could these committees protect themselves from this inflatration? 


Answer: This issue has been decided upon from the very beginning considering the nature 
of the revolutionary work, for when a group of people meets and forms a revolutionary 
committee claiming that they belong to revolutionary forces, we assume that part of this 
group are revolutionaries while the other are opportunists. Vut the revolutionary 
committee like all others does not practice power. Its role is restricted to encouraging 
people's congresses to practice power and to hold meetings in order to discuss at 

length their agenda and help those congresses by elucidating the issues at hand.

Besides, the revolutionary committees undertake to guide people's committees chosen 

by people's congresses in their implementation of the revolutions passed by these con- 
gresses. Therefore, working inside the revolutionary committees does not entitle any- 

body to any power. Hence those who continue working there are the true revolutionaries 

wh@ believe in the cause of the masses and the revolution. Opportunists who aim at achiev- 
ing personal gains through joining revolutionary committees are soon disillusioned and 
prefer to resign and abandon the revolutionary couse. Therefore from the very start these 
elements which have infiltrated inside the revolutionary committees are hit by the reality 
of these committees. 


So they drop out on their own accord, leaving revolutionary committees to true revolution- 
aries. In short, the nature of revolutionary work itself can sift these elements which 
drop out automatically without their being told they are not revolutionaries and have no 
right to join the revolutionary committees. 


Question: What is the secret in your declaration of revolutionary transformation, in 
politics, economics and religion all together? Don't you think that by this you have 
facilitated the alliance between the opposing forces of traditionalists, regionalists, 
men of religion and capitalists 


Answer: On the contrary, the declaration of the three revolutions you have mentioned all 

at once will not lead at all to the alliance of the forces whose’ interests have been damaged 
for each faction is busy receiving the blows of the revolution, which leaves it no time 

for alliance. 


All these old factions are going to be liquidated by the revolutionary society, the society 
of the masses. I would like to clarify that each of these factions is simultaneously 
exposed, in isolation of the rest, to the attack of the revolutionary forces, which renders 
it incapable of helping the other or calling it for help. 


Even if it calls for aid, none will hear its shout because the side called upon for relief 
is under the same attack. Here, I affirm that these factions are limited and weak, and the 
force of the revolutionary masses' attack will finish them off very quickly and at once. 

I have to be clear regarding the religious revolution. I do not represent Marx who staged 
a revolution against religion. On the contrary, I am leading a revolution fcr religion, 
but from within, in order to correct its deviation. We must understand the clear and great 
difference between someone who launches a revolution against religion and another whose 
revolution is for the religion and for its defence, by eradicating all the factors against 
its propagation and adhering to its teachings. This is the case with us. 


We are earnest in abolishing charlatans and religious opportunists. They alone are harmed 
by our religious revolution, and they alone are the target of our revolution. As to the 
pious believer in the true Muslim religion who knows the world of God, he will join the 
ranks of the religious revolution, for whom it had been launched. 


Question: "There is no freedom to a nation that depends on sea alone to feed itself," is a 
historic statement by Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and the Great First of September Revolution has 
attempted to achieve an agricultural revolution by offering generous facilities and numerous 
projects, and in spite of this farmers continued to retreat and shrink by turning into a 
society of merchants. Do you think that the abolition of the class of merchants, for its 
non productivity, is enough to return the Libyan Arab citizen back to the (?land) because 

it is the true wealth?

The concept of traditional armies is a defeatist one, as once the army surrenders or is 
destroyed or loses a battle, the whole c.untry must give into the enemy, which is a strange 
situation. But should the whole people be involved in the battle, it is another matter. 
Therefore, why should victory depend on a group of people whose defeat means that of the 
whole country. The armed people is a dangerous experiment that scares governments. In any 
case this experiment cannot take place under governments which rule the people. This ex- 
periment cannot be carried out unless the people practice power. Once this is done, the 
people will most certainly arm themselves. Hence an armed people cannot exist in a country 
ruled by false democracy because the government will make sure it has control over arms 

so that it could oppress them. 


Question: Some time ago you disclosed a plan devised by the Egyptian president to invade 
the Jamahiriyah and reports of different sources referred to military mobilization on 
the Egyptian-Libyan Arab borders. How true is this and what lies behind it? 


Answer: Nearly the whole Egyptian Army has been mobilized on the Libyan borders after 

it has been withdrawn from the Suez Canal by the Egyptian president. Nevertheless, there 
is no fear from it. We have direct and national relations with many officers and soldiers 
of the Egyptian Army. As a matter of fact the Egyptian president did us a favour by 
sending the Egyptian Army to our borders, thus facilitating our contacts with it. 


But I can not help but pity this army because of the very bad conditions they live in in 
the Sahara, the reason why we supply it with water, tea, cigarettes, sugar and rice. We 
are confident that this army will march from the Western Sahara into Cairo one of these 
days. 


Question: Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, how do you invisage the fature of the Steadfastness and 
Confrontation Front inthe light of the Iraqi-Syrian pact? 


Answer: The stand of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front has been reinforced 
following Baghdad's summit where the whole Arab nation stood against the measures taken 

by the Egyptian regime which was discarded amd isolated. The union between Syria and 

Iraq will most certainly strengthen the Steadfastness Front and constitutes a major 

factor in the unity of the steadfastness forces. I am the first of those eager to see the 
Syrian-Iraqi union achieved and I was the first to call for it. I exerted continuous 
efforts in 1973 to bring about this unity and Iam satisfied at what I have achieved and 

at the outcome of it all. We in the Jamahiriyah are looking forward to this unity, which 
is as important as a unity between Libya and Egypt. The unity of Iraq and Syria,is 
characterized by an important geographical factor which was lacking in the previous union 
of Egypt and Syria, and I believe that this missing geographical factor was responsible 
for the breaking up of the 1958 unity and not the regional and reactionary forces which 
existed in Egypt during the revolution and still do nowadays and which have become so weak 
that they can not harm a national action of the level of unity. 


Question: What effect do you think the Iranian revolution would have on the Arab nation, 
and do you believe that the axis policy called for by certain countries will save them 
from this effect? 


Answer: The Iranian revolution is a real volcano which erupted in the area and will lead 
to restoring balance once more. However, its effect can not be prevented, as it is only 

natural for volcanos to cause earthquakes which cannot be stopped by pacts and alliances. 
Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the Arab Gulf ami most countries surrounding Iran will definitely be 
exposed to earthquakes and new structures.

Iran's revolution is a positive phenomenon and a God-send, as well as a proof that the 
U.Se protection for its stooges is only a mirage. We thank God that the United States 
has failed to safeguard any of its stooges, from Lon Nol to the shah of Iran. Arab 
rulers should take heed and realize that the United States will not protect them from 
people's revolutions. The United States is always backing the losing horse and clings 
to its drowining men. More than once I have made it clear that the United States should 
align with the people and national forces and give up its alliances with fascist, 
repressive forces inimical to the peoples. However, Washington has always preferred 
to contradict itself by calling for human rights and concluding alliances with forces 
hostile to freedom and progress which try to crush man himself. What has the United 
States gained from this policy of hers but continual loss and failure? 


Question: Many military reports have said that the Jamahiriyah is endeavouring to 
possess nuclear weapons. How true is this? 


Answer: These reports aim at casting suspicion on the countries working to use the atom 
in energy. These reports are wicked and inimical to progress. The whole world is looking 
for an alternative to energy which is found in the atom. 


I believe that striving for this alternative is legal and the whole world should do the 
same. I should think that the right to possess science and progress cannot be objected to. 


Consequently, the authors of those reports had better direct their accusations at 
countries which manufacture atomic bombs and use them in underground and sea tests. 

These reports are ridiculous. They should be strongly attacking countries possessing 
the atomic bomb and not fabricating rumours around countries which want to use the atomic 
energy for peaceful purposes in medicine, agriculture and electricity. They are calling 
on the Third World to keep their backwardness. This is all there is to it. 


AL-KIFAH AL-'ARABI published an introduction to this interview in which the editor-in- 
chie* who made the interview pointed out that the leader of the Great lst of September 
Revolution comes out to the masses everyday encouraging them to seize state establish- 
ments in order to achieve real freedom by seizing power, wealth and arms. 


When AI-KIFAH AL-'ARABI, he said, went to Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi for this 
interview, it was aware of its importance in the succession of events currently taking 
place. The Jamahiriyah itself is undergoing three revolutions--political, economic and 
religious--while the revolution of Khomeyni is approaching final victory.
