Limited Aid 


[Question] Is the financial aid which Syria received for oil and gas 
exploration from the OAPEC countries and the World Bank sufficient? 


[Answer] Up till now the amount of aid received has been limited. At 
the present time, we are relying on concluding contracts with foreign 
companies to prospect for oil in Syrian territory, on condition that 
the Syrian Government will not suffer any financial losses if these 
companies do not find oil. If they do find oil, they can recover the 
costs by selling 40 percent of the oil which is discovered, at world 
market prices. But the rest cf the oil, that is, 60 percent of it, 
goes to Syria. 


As far as OAPEC is concerned, it has a Petroleum Services Co, with
