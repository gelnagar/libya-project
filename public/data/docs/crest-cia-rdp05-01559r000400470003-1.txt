Summary (AI): A Washington Post article from June 1986 (filed from Tripoli on June 11) reports that Gadhafi failed to make a promised personal appearance at the anniversary rally marking the takeover of Wheelus Air Base, instead addressing the nation on television. It describes his threats of suicide squads, the thin crowd at Green Square, and speculation about his whereabouts after the April 15 U.S. raid on Tripoli and Benghazi.
• Gadhafi said Libya had suicide squads as a deterrent and would fight a possible war with the U.S. lasting 10 years or more.
• Gadhafi's barracks home in Tripoli was destroyed by U.S. bombs in April 1986.
• Diplomats believed Gadhafi moved between the desert oasis of Sabah and his wife's home district near Beyda.
• Only 18 diplomats, all from Third World countries, attended at one reviewing stand; only a Moroccan delegation was in the other guest stand.
• 1986-06-11: Anniversary rally and parade for the 1970 takeover of Wheelus Air Base; Gadhafi did not appear in person and spoke on TV instead; a crowd of no more than 2,000 attended.
• 1986-04-15: U.S. bombing of Tripoli and Benghazi, referenced as the last occasion of Gadhafi's public appearances before western reporters.
