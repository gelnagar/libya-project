LIBYA

Bulgaria’s Atanasov, Delegation Arrive for Talks 
LD061338 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1215 GMT 6 Jun 87 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 Jan(JANA)— Georgi Atanasov, premier of the 
People’s Republic of Bulgaria, arrived in Tripoli this afternoon 
on a visit to the Great Jamahiriyah as part of the fruitful 
cooperation and friendship between the Libyan Arab and Bulgar- 
ian peoples. 


The brother secretary of the General People’s Committee, mem- 
bers of the General People’s Committee, officers of the Libyan 
Arab Armed Forces, and members of the diplomatic corps 
accredited to the Great Jamahiriyah welcomed the guest and his 
accompanying delegation at Tripoli International Airport 


Mr Georgi Atanasov is accompanied on this visit by Khristo 
Khristov, trade minister and chairman of the Libyan Arab- 
Bulgarian Joint Committee; Ivan Ganev, deputy foreign min- 
ister; Dimitur Dimitrov, deputy chairman of the Planning 
Commission; and a number of Bulgarian officials. 


The Bulgarian premier made a statement toa JANA correspon- 
dent on his arrival at Tripoli International Airport. He expressed 
his pleasure at [word indistinct] the Great Jamahiriyah, noting 
that he is very confident that this visit will be a good opportunity 
for exchanging views on various aspects of bilateral cooperation 
and urgent international issues. 


He added that during his delegation’s Stay in the Great Socialist 
People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah, the achievements of the 
friendly Libyan people, their life, and all that they have been able 
to accomplish will be viewed with great interest. 


Joint Talks Begin 
LDO71650 Tripoli JANA in English 1430 GMT 7 Jun 87 


[Text] Tripoli, Al Sayf [June] 7, Jamahiriyah News Agency — 
The Libyan Bulgarian talks began yesterday in Tripoli at the 
meeting hall of the General People’s Committee. 


Taking part in the talks were: the secretary of the General 
People’s Committee and the chairman of the Bulgarian Council 
of Ministers, Mr Georgi Atanasov. 


Also taking part were the secretary of the General People’s 
Committee for Industries, a number of specialists and techni- 
cians and the secretary of the Libyan Arab People’s Bureau in 
Bulgaria. 


Attending on the Bulgarian side were: Mr Khristo Khristov, 
the minister for foreign trade and the president of the mixed 
Bulgarian - Libyan Commission, Mr Ivan Ganev, the deputy 
foreign minister, Mr Dimitur Dimitrov, deputy chairman of the 
State Planning Committee, and the ambassador of Bulgaria to 
Great Jamahiriyah. 


The secretary of the General People’s Committee opened the 
talks with a speech in which he welcomed the chairman of the 
Bulgarian Council of Ministers and the delegation accompany- 
ing him. He pointed out that the visit comes within the framework

of consolidation of entrenched relations between the two friendly 
countries. 


The secretary of the General People’s Committee lauded the 
great development seen by relations between Great Jamahiriyah 
and the People’s Republic of Bulgaria. 


He pointed out that many accords and protocols were signed in 
recent years between the two countries as a result of efforts made 
by the leader of the revolution and President Todor Zhivkov and 
exchange of delegations at all levels between the two countries. 


Also, Mr Atanasov addressed the meeting starting it by convey- 
ing the regards and best wishes of the Bulgarian president to the 
leader of the revolution and good wishes to the Libyan Arab 
people for prosperity and progress. 


The chairman of the Bulgarian Council of Ministers condemned 
anew the aborted U.S..NATO barbaric aggression against the 
people of Great Jamahiriyah and reaffirmed the support of 
Bulgaria, people and Government, for the Libyan Arab people in 
its courageous resistance against American imperialist and reac- 
tionary plots. 


He lauded the development of relations between Great Jamahir- 
iyah [and] the People’s Republic of Bulgaria. He said “We 
appreciate the Libyan Bulgarian cooperation which turned, 
thanks to the will of both countries, into a comprehensive cooper- 
ation especially in social, economic and political fields. [no closing 
quotation marks as received] 


The chairman of the Bulgarian Council of Ministers expressed 
the desire of his country to develop and consolidate its relation- 
ship with Great Jamahiriyah and the strengthening of the volume 
of bilateral cooperation in various aspects of life. 


Mr Georgi Atanasov deplored anew the imperialist intervention 
in Chad. He asserted that the Socialist People’s Republic of 
Bulgaria rejects any imperialist intervention in African affairs 
whose problems and disputes should be solved through the OAU 
and a purely African framework. 


JANA learnt that two committees were set up during yesterday's 
talks. A political committee as well an economic and technical 
committee were set up and start their work as from today.

Cooperation Talks Continue 
LD080829 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0720 GMT 8 Jun 87 


[Text] Tripoli, 8 Jun (JANA) — The joint Libyan Arab- 
Bulgarian Committee for Economic and Technical Cooperation 
held an expanded meeting in Tripoli yesterday evening. The 
secretary of the General People’s Committee for Industry and 
Khristo Khristov, the Bulgarian minister of trade and chairman 
of the joint Libyan Arab-Bulgarian committee, were in atten- 
dance. 


Members of the five subcommittees established during the Lib- 
yan Arab-Bulgarian Committee talks were included in the 
meeting. The two sides discussed the clauses of the agreement 
which they signed for the political, economic, industrial, agricul- 
tural, scientific, and technical sectors in order to prepare draft 
resolutions for adoption by the subcommittees. 


The two sides also stressed the importance of finding ways to 
expand and reinforce bilateral cooperation to achieve the inter- 
ests and goals of the peoples of the Great Jamahiriyah and 
Bulgaria.

Khristov Lauds Libyan Advances 
LD091047 Tripoli JANA in English 0956 GMT 9 Jun 87 


[Text] Tripoli, Al Sayf [June] 9, Jamahiriyah News Agency — 
Mr. Khristo Khristov, the Bulgarian minister of foreign trade 
and chairman of the joint Bulgarian-Libyan Arab Committee 
holding its meetings currently in Tripoli, has paid tribute to the 
bilateral cooperation relations between Great Al-Jamahiriyah 
and Bulgaria. 


In comments made to JANA, Mr. Khristov has said that the 
above cooperation has opened up new dimensions in the cooper- 
ation in economic, agricultural and industrial spheres through 
the implementation of the cooperation agreements concluded 
between the two friendly countries. 


Meanwhile, he has lauded the great economic advancement 
achieved by the Libyan Arab people in all walks of life thanks to 
the revolution adding that the friendly Libyan Arab people 
implements the ambitious plans and programmes brought about 
by the Great Al-Fatih Revolution especially in the economic 
sector in a bid to serve its social objectives aiming at achieving 
progress and development. 


He adds that he and his delegation are pleased with the cultural 
achievements they had witnessed such as the establishment of 


industrial research centre which constitutes an industrial and 
scientific fortress aiming at achieving progress and giving 
training to the citizens in all fields in this friendly country, i.e, 
Great Al-Jamahiriyah.
