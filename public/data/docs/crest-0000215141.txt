It also covers Libyan reservist mobilization, Soviet cautiousness toward Egypt, and Libyan casualty concerns. Nothing specific to Tripoli sites is named.
• Libya reportedly recalled as many as 7,000 reservists and sent military trucks and confiscated civilian vehicles east.
• Unusual flights by Libyan Tu-22 bombers took place over Tripoli the day before the cable.
• Egyptians claimed Libya lost close to 500 soldiers, a figure the cable says was probably exaggerated.
• Soviet official response was muted, with Pravda carrying a cautious statement that avoided calling Egypt the aggressor.
• 1977-06: Egypt and Libya observe a de facto truce after fighting; Egypt had declared a unilateral cease-fire that Libya had not answered.
