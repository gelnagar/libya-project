Libya Detains Crew of Tunisian Fishing Vessel 

London AL-DUSTUR in Arabic 7 Mar 88 p 3 

[Text] The Libyan Navy surrounded a Tunisian fishing 
vessel a week ago and escorted it to the Libyan port of 
Zuwarah. When AL-DUSTUR got in touch with Libyan 
circles to ask them about the details, the answer was that 
this was merely a mistake and that things would return to 


normal. However, the Libyan authorities have not 
released the detained fishermen as of yet. Will this 
incident become a new problem in the normalization of 
Tunisian-Libyan relations?
