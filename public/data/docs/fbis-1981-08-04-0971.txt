LIBYA

U. S. CHARGE OF LIBYAN LINK IN GAMBIA COUP DENTED 


LD0313238 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 3 Aug 81 
[Unattributed commentary] . 


{lext] The U.S. administration and its various information media have continued their 
black propaganda campaigns against the Great 1 September Revolution and its leader within 
the framework of their terroristic aggressive plan, which centers on information distortion 
and the creation of confusion and the instigation of agents as a means to assassinating 

the commander of the revolution. 


James Fisher, the official spokesman of the U.S. State Department, said 4 days ago that 
the U.S. Government possesses information proving Libya's participation in last Thursday's 
coup [in Gambia], as he alleged. Thus, the responsible U.S. official has exposed the 
American aggressive intentions teward the Jamahiriyah and has uttered fabricated and false 
statements. Meanwhile, AFP has reported that there is no connection whatsoever between 
Libya and the developments in Gambia. 


Thus, the United States has unmasked its ugly face and its aggressive course--a course it is 
propagating through the propaganda campaigns, lies and fabrications which -re echoed by 

the mercenary Western press. THE MIDDLE EAST magazine, published in Loi. » has stated that 
the Reagan administration seems to have forgotten that authority in Libya in all fields is 
in the hands of the Libyan people, throug!. their popula: committees and congresses, and 
consequently that they are capable of checking all plots being hatched by the leader of 
international terrorism’ the United States. 


ARAB GROUP ISSUED CALL FOR WORLD CONFERENCE 


LD032238 Tripoli voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 3 Aug 81 


[3 Aug press statement issued by the International Secretariat for Solidarity With the 
Arab People and Their Central Issue--Palestine] 


[Text] Being aware of its responsibilities vis-a-vis imperialism, Zionism and reaction, 

the democratic progressive forces throughou. the world; and out of its concern for 
shouldering its duties in backing and supporting the Arab issues in implementation of their 
threat of aggression which the Socialist people's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah is being subjected 
to by American imperialism and its agents in the region, particularly the regimes of 
As-Sadat and Numayri, which has reached 2 degree of provocation to which the antiagression 
forces cannot renain indifferent, the International Secretariat for Solidarity With the 

Arab People and Their Central Issue--Palestine has called for the convening of a world 
conference for solidarity with the people of the Jamahiriyah from 28 to 31 August. 


The purpose of this conference is to expose this plan, which is not just against the 
struggle of the Libyan Arab people and the Arab nation, but is also against the struggle of 
peoples who reject domination and struggle for freedom everywhere. In view of the fact 
that the question of freedom and its defense is an international responsibility, and since 
there exists the people's front, which cherishes justice, peace and freedom confronting 

the front of imperialist aggression,the international secretariat urges all progressive 
and democratic forces to shoulder their responsibilities and get ready for this conference, 
which will be a world trial of the crimes imperialism is committing against a people who 
have liberated their will and are partaking in the liberation of the peoples of the world. 


[Signed] The International Secretariat for Solidarity With the Arab People and Their 
Central Issue--Palestine
