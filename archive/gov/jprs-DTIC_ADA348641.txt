The  abovementioned  five  American  signals  might  be

The  timing  of  the  media  clamor  created  by  America  with

in  the  Middle  East.  And  we  did  not  hear  from  America 
any  question  or  denunciation  of  these  Israeli  practices.

besiege  their  independent  initiatives,  and  preserve  the 
subservience  of  most  of  the  Arab  regimes  to  the  United 
States.  In  this  plan,  the  United  States  sometimes  wants 


to  use  the  weapon  of  economy  and  at  other  times  the
