LIBYA

STUDENTS PARTICIPATE IN PRODUCTION PROJECTS 
Tripoli AL-TALIB in Arabic 30 Aug 81 pp 6-8 
(Article: "Toward Achieving Self-Sufficiency for Society"] 


[Text] As confirmation of the revolution's continuous advance, sweeping away 
all the puny obstacles before it and overcoming all barriers built by the ene- 
mies of life and freedoms, as continuation of the leadership revolutionary 
course, led by the Arab masses in the Jamahiriyah, and in accordance with the 
facts, showing that our student masses, throughout our great land, have proved 
their value, efficiency and readiness to build the new socialist society, which 
the glorious First of September Revolution and the Third World theory are trying 
to emphasize throughout the world, so that happiness and freedom can be achieved 
for all the masses that reject submissive slavery, our students have made great 
strides forward, when measured against the short life of the revolution and the 
student movement in the Jamahiriyah. 


Our students in the Jamahiriyah were the ones who took part in the revolution 
right up to 2 March, a historic day in the life of mankind. They were the ones 
who achieved the students’ revolution of 7 April, producing a series of civil 
uprisings and demonstrations, boldly undertaken by our students in 61, 64 and 
65. As a decisive result of the aforementioned facts, our students are embla- 
zoned on the pages of history. They were the first students in the world to 
turn to being productive. 


To that end, student conferences in the Jamahiriyah and abroad decided that there 
was a need to participate in production, that students should not depend on 
society's other groups for productivity, and that their roles should not con- 
tinue to be negative and dependent at the expense of society, since that is 
completely rejected in the new society. Everyone shares equally in the new 
society; accordingly, the Fifth Jamahiriyah Students Conference, held at Qariyu- 
nis University in Benghazi, recommended: 


"Students' conferences have stressed the achieving of self-sufficiency for 
society. The memorandum in this regard established a way to do that. The 
Jamahiriyah's students, who are determined to do this, are offering pioneering 
experience to students of the world, to develop their role, from mere tools of 
rejection, exploited by forces battling against authority, to producers of 
material and moral change in their societies."

This recommendation was a strong cry, which the students of 7 April sent forth 
to the students of other societies to follow on their path, in order to achieve 
self-sufficiency, and to lay the foundations of the new society, in which power, 
wealth and weapons are in the masses' hands. 


The administrative committee of the General Conference of Jamahiriyah Students 
turned the recommendation over to the General People's Committee for practical 
implementation, which in turn formed a committee to study effective ways to em- 
ploy the students, in accordance with the premises upon which this sector's 
participation in implementing projects of the plan would be based. These pro- 
jects could be implemented by them on the level of each municipality, in accor- 
dance with numbers targeted for employment. 


This year, students have been employed at production sites, which are basically 
dependent on muscle power and on that type of production that is far from offices 
and air-conditioning. This is with the proviso that students, above the obliga- 
tory level at specialized technical institutes and colleges, be employed in their 
specialties, while general secondary students may work in any of various produc- 
tion sites. 


Construction Projects 


These are represented by the construction of large cisterns in Tobruk by stu- 
dents of the Tobruk secondary school, construction of dirt roads by students 
from the Darnah Teachers Institute, construction and afforestation of gardens 
by the students of the Abu Zar al-Ghafari school in Benghazi, and construction 
of new gardens in the town of al-Zawiyah, in which students of the al-Zawiyah 
secondary school and the Sarman secondary school participated. 


Afforestation 


Students of the Darnah Teachers Institute undertook the planting of Wadi Darnah 
with trees. The students of the city of Tripoli also dug holes for planting 
trees in the Tarhunah and Ghariyan agricultural projects. 


Maintenance and Cleaning 


The students of the Darnah Teachers Institute are maintaining schools and insti- 
tutes in the area, along with students from the Jamal ‘Abd al-Nassir secondary 
school in al-Fateh, and students of the Jaber Ibn Hayan school in Sarat. Stu- 
dents of the Haun secondary school in al-Jafrah are cleaning irrigation ditches 
in the agricultural project. 


Moreover, students of the Teachers Institute in Marzag are cleaning irrigation 
ditches for projects in al-Hamirah, Turaghan, Umm al-Araneb and Zuwaylah, as 
well as road maintenance and paving. 


Students of the Brak Teachers Institute, during the first stage of their employ- 
ment program, are also cleaning irrigation ditches in the Brak agricultural pro- 
ject. In Awbari, students are participating in maintaining schools and insti- 

tutes, and in maintaining the dirt roads, along with students from Sauf al-Jin, 














Zulaytan, al-Khams, Tarhunah, al-Zawiyah, al-‘Aziziyah, Ghariyan, Yafran, 
Subha and Tripoli. 


Excavating 


Excavation activities are especially represented by the digging of water 
canals or laying underground cables, whether for electricity or telephone. 


In Darnah, during the first stage, students took part in laying underground 
cables. Students in al-Fateh also participated in digging a canal to channel 
the waters of the al-Ghurayq. 


In Benghazi, during the first stage of the employment program, students dug 
and maintained protective canals for the city of Benghazi, and dug a reservoir 
for the waters of the al-Khadhra’'. 


In Marzaq, students of the Marzaq Teachers Institute are being used to dig 
canals and to extend electricity lines. In Ghadamis, students are extending 
electricity lines, and students from Suf al-Jin are taking part in digging a 
canal to connect water wells in the Zamzam project. Students from al-Khams are 
also extending water networks in the town. In the town of al-Naqat al-Khams, 
students are working to fill up the ponds and swamps. 


Stabilizing the Sand Dunes 


It was thought to use students to resist and stabilize the encroaching sands, 
by preparing earthen walls. This is a very important matter, especially in the 
southern area of the Jamahiriyah, where the advancing desert is considered more 
of a threat than anything else. 


On this basis, students of Suf al-Jin will participate in preparing earthen 
walls, and students from the city of Ajdabiya will take part in controlling 
sand dunes. 


Reports received have indicated that use of students this year is reaching a 
very excellent level, and a percentage of its success has been dependent on the 
persistence and determination of the students to become productive, apart from 
from their scholastic duties. 


Let us review a report published about the use of students in the town of al- 
Jabal al-Akhdhar. It will clarify for us the extent of the success achieved 

by the idea of employing students, and the recommendation of the student 

masses, which they themselves adopted without urging or being ordered to, since 
all those forms have been done away with in the age of the masses, in which the 
order is the people's con erences" order, power is the people's power, and imple- 
mentation belongs to the ,»eople‘s committees.

Al-Damur Martyrs Camp 


The al-Damur Martyrs production camp is located 30 kms from the city of Ghariyan. 
There are about 280 students located there, in their first period of employment. 


56 











The students are employed at this camp on two projects, Ghariyan's terraces, 
in which they are doing the stone work and the planting, and the project of 
grazing lands in Ghariyan, in which they are doing the inside and outside 
fencing. This is in addition to productive work within these projects, which 
continues for 8 hours a day. Apart from that, the students are continuously 
active all summer long. In addition to their work, there is a mixture of in- 
tellectual and vocational lectures, cultural and journalistic activities and 
popular sports activities.
