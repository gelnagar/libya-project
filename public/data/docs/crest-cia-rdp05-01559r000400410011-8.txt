Qadhdhafi describes U.S. sanctions as an economic war, claims the U.S. intends to strike Libya, and says Libya mobilized its forces when the U.S. fleet approached the Gulf of Sidra line.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya has about 1,000 planes of various types.
• Reagan declared a national emergency over the Libyan threat, enabling sanctions and an economic blockade.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya would wait for the 22 January Arab League meeting before responding.
• The interview was published in Beirut in the 20-26 January 1986 issue of Al-Nahar al-Arabi wa al-Duwali.
• 1986-01: According to Qadhdhafi, U.S. ships approached the Gulf of Sidra in January 1986; Libya mobilized its air force, navy and air defence, but the ships dispersed before crossing the line.
