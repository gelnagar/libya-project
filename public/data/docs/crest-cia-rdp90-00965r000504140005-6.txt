Summary (AI): An ABC News broadcast from April 7, 1986 reports U.S. officials linking Libya to a West Berlin discotheque bombing that killed two people, including an American soldier. It says Libyan messages to its East Berlin embassy showed knowledge of the attack, and that France expelled two Libyan diplomats. Nothing here is about Tripoli itself.
• U.S. intelligence sources said Libyan messages to its East Berlin embassy after the bombing indicated clear knowledge of the attack and praised it.
• Substantial communication between Libya and its East Berlin embassy preceded the attack, prompting U.S. concern raised with East Germany and the Soviet Union.
• France expelled two Libyan diplomats over the weekend for alleged terrorist links.
• Libyan embassies are referred to as people's bureaus.
• 1986-04-05: Bombing of a discotheque in West Berlin, one of the dead an American soldier; U.S. officials link it to Libya. (2 killed)
