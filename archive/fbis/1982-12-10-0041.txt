LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI RECEIVES CABLE FROM CHAD COMMITTEES 


LD051428 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 5 Dec 82 


[Text] Members of Chadian revolutionary and popular committees have sent a cable to 
the brother leader of the revolution on the occasion of convening their popular 
congress. It reads: We Chadians, members of the revolutionary and popular committees, 
meeting at a popular congress, extend to you our profound appreciation for the revo- 
lutionary stand and the continuous, gigantic efforts you are exerting in support of 
the cause of the people of Chad, their popular revolution and their transitional 
national government. It is our duty on this occasion to extend our profound grati- 
tude and appreciation to the fraternal Libyan Arab people for the support they have 
extended to the just cause of the people of Chad. The revolutionary vigilance of the 
Chadian and Libyan Arab peoples is capable of foiling the intrigues hatched by imper- 
ialism, Zionism, and reaction against the free peoples in Africa. 


TRIPOLI NOTES U.S. MILITARY ACTIVITY IN MIDEAST 


LD041548 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 4 Dec 82 
[Unattributed commentary] 


[Text] U.S. military activity in the Arab homeland is intensifying noticeably. In 
the next 2 days U.S. naval units will be heading for the Moroccan port of Tangier. 
Moreover, at the end of this week U.S. forces are to conduct military exercises in 
the Sultanate of Oman. We present the following report on U.S. military activity in 
the Arab homeland. 


The latest feature of U.S. activity in the Arab homeland is the miltiary exercise to 
be held at the end of this week in the Sultanate of Oman. The operation includes the 
landing of U.S. Marines on the shores of Oman under the cover of F-15 and AWACS 
aircraft and strategic B-52 bombers. A nuclear aircraft carrier is also to take part 
in this exercise. 


Observers believe the U.S. presence in the Arab homeland has noticeably increased 
since the beginning of this decade. It has taken many forms and shapes, the first of 
which is represented by the Zionist enemy. Then there are the permanent U.S. military 
bases, such as those in several Arab countries under agreements with their regimes. 
In addition, there are the concessions offered to U.S. forces in many Arab sea and 
airports. Finally, there are the military exercises inside the Arab homeland or at 
its extremities. Through everyone of these points U.S. imperialism seeks to achieve 
its aim to dominate the Arab homeland and exploit its resources. 


Since 1979, the size of the U.S. military presence in the Middle East has changed 
significantly. Before that date, the U.S. presence was limited to five naval units 
of the U.S. 6th Fleet. Now this force includes 32 naval units, including aircraft 
carriers, nuclear missile-carrying destroyers, and other naval ships nad units, in 
addition to the military bases and ports and thousands of soldiers at sea and on land. 


In its efforts to militarily dominate the Arab homeland as an alternative for its 
agent regimes -- in the event that they are popularly destroyed -- the U.S. Adminis- 
tration is seeking to establish a force acquainted with the nature of the Arab home- 
land and one that is highly prepared and proficient, by carrying out military exer- 
cises on Arab territory.
