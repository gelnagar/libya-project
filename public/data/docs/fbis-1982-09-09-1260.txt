{Text] Tripoli, Dhulqidah 18, September 6, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- AZ=ZAHF AL-AKHDAR 
newspaper today wrote the following leader: There are crowns and swords on the heads and 
shoulders of the Arab presidents and kings enough to open not Beirut only but also Iakko. 

And around the necks of their women there is enough to buy thousands of weapons and necessary

With the fall of Beirut, fell the Arab authoritarian parties which took hypocrisy as a

particular. The fall of Beirut revealed the impotence of the so-called opposition in the 
Arab countries and it was clear how close is their link with the rulers. 


Beirut should bury under its rubble all the Arab decay to start afresh in formulating an 
honourable Arab history and bring up a new Arab generation immaterial of the cost. A 
generation which is committed to clear out the darkness into which the Arab world has sunk. 


This is net something impossible nor it is difficult to defeat the enemy when all the Arab 
resources are poured together in a progressive revolutionary movement that rejects the 
presence of an invading Zionist and racist entity inside the Arab world. And as the houses 
of the poor were destroyed in Beirut this time, then the palaces of the fake leaders should 
be pulled down in the future. And because thousands of innocent children who disappeared 
under the rubble of Beirut, then the medal holders speech lovers and seat worshippers this 
time should disappear totally from the scene. [sentence as received] 


An Arab glory made by the masses should be built with the incitation of the revolutionary 
committees through their dismantling of all treacherous facades and figures and through a 
revolutionary popular theory that leads a class struggle employed for the interest of the 
workers. 


The Arab nation feels so disappointed and frustrated as a result of the consecutive pounding 
exercised against it through the summits of treason and the courts of palaces. The next 
stage is that of revolutionary action which aims to liberate Arab land and restore Arab 
dignity. This urges the revolutionary committees to lay down their practical programmes 

to be carried out with the utmost courage and ferocity and with all available financial 

and moral resources. Any meaning of lunery [as received] has disappeared under the shadow 
of the death which covers the Arab arena and sends its instruments of destruction in every 
election. We have to establisn grounds for confrontation, and to achieve that, we have 

to depend only on the Arab rifle, blood and sacrifice.
