LIBYA DEMANDS RELEASE OF NATIONALS ARRESTED IN LONDON 
LD121916 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1835 GMT 12 Jun 80 


[Text] Tripoli, 12 June, JANA--‘The Foreign Liaison office this morning called the 
British ambassador in Libya and handed him a strongly worded protest note on the action 
of British police yesterday when they gathered some mercenary elements and organized a 
false demonstration in front of the Libyan People's Bureau in London. 


The memorandum said that the irresponsible behavior of the British Goverment hams 
relations between the two peoples. It also said that if the British Goverment does not 
end such provocative actions immediately and if it does not free the Libyans arrested 
by the British police yesterday, Libya will be forced to take similar measures toward 
British subjects in Libya.
