LIBYA

People’s Foreign Liaison Committee Statement 
LD141500 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
14 May 83 


[Text] In response to the call made by the brother commander of 
the revolution on the occasion of the 13th anniversary of the 
eviction of the British imperialists from the homeland and on his 
incitation to the vocational congress to march on the People’s 
Foreign Liaison Bureau; in consolidation of the people’s author- 
ity; in affirmation of direct people’s democracy, and from the 
concept that lasting relations exist between peoples and not 
governments, the people's foreign liaison committees of the voca- 
tional congresses marched on the People’s Foreign Liaison 
Bureau this morning and set up a people’s committee to run it 
and to turn it into an advance post of revolutionary action and a 
popular administration that will carry out the resolutions of the 
basic people’s congress. The people’s committees for foreign 
liaison at the vocational congresses, which undertook the march 
on the People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau, have issued the following 
statement: 


In reliance on God; from the concept of committees everywhere; 
in consolidation of the people's authority; in affirmation of direct 
democracy which provides that the people shall govern them- 
selves by themselves through the people's congresses, which are 
vested with the absolute right to make decisions, and through the 
people's committees, which are the instruments of execution; in 
the implementation of the resolutions of the basic people’s con- 
gresses providing the People’s Foreign Liaison Bureau with all 
human and material resources that enable it to create relations 
on the level of the popular masses with all peoples of the world, 
so that the voice of the Libyan people will roar, resound and be 
heard carrying to all the peoples of the world the Libyan people's 
new human line of thinking — a line that is alive and calling for 
complete emancipation from all forms of oppression, repression 
and domination; in eptomization of the pioneering, revolutionary 
thesis that lasting human relations exist among peoples and not 
among governments, the Libyan Arab masses, through their 
vocational organizations, today march via the people’s commit- 
tees for foreign liaison at the vocational congresses that elected 
them to assme the task of implementing the resolutions govern- 
ing relations between the Libyan people and the other world 
peoples, to form the people's committee of the Foreign Liaison 
Bureau 


This committee will assume its duties from today to effect the 
abovementioned change in popular political action. In this con- 
text, the committee will be guided by the Green ideology, the 
ideology of the third universal theory which unleashes latent 
potential and summons the peoples of the world to seek liberation, 
emancipation and revolution. This theory represents an immortal 
ideology which affirms noble human principles so that man’s 
freedom is asserted, his full happiness is established, all forms of 
injustice and oppression are eliminated and a new world cultural 
rebirth is created enabling the peoples of the world to take 
command of their destinies and take hold of the reins of their
