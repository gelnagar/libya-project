Summary (AI): A New York Post analysis from late March 1986 assesses what the U.S. gained from the Gulf of Sidra naval clash with Libya, including damage to patrol boats and a Soviet-built missile site. It argues the Soviet Union distanced itself from Qadhafi and that the U.S. gained intelligence on Libyan bases.
• Operation Prairie Fire was the name given to the first phase of U.S. operations against Libya.
• The article says the Soviet Union provides Libya about $5 billion a year in military aid.
• The U.S. naval task force had been off the Libyan coast since the previous December.
• The article says there had been no Libyan terrorist strike against American or Israeli targets since the task force entered the Gulf of Sidra.
• 1986-03: High seas clash in the Gulf of Sidra during Operation Prairie Fire; Libyan patrol boats damaged and a Soviet-built missile site destroyed.
