ARAB GULF PLANNING, DEVELOPMENT SEMINAR ENDS 
JNO41503 Baghdad INA in Arabic 1345 GMT 4 Jun 80 


(Excerpt) Baghdad, 4 Jun--A seminar for the ministers of planning and 
development in the Arab Gulf and peninsula concluded its work here today. 


The seminar called for coordination among the states of the above regions 
on economic and social development plans and for the establishment of 
joint investment projects, to boleter their political and economic inde- 
pendence. 


It also recommended international seminars for planning officials in the 
Arab Gulf and peninsula, by which to exchange viewpoints when drafting 
annual and five-year plans, along with economic information, statistics 
and etudies on planning and development affairs. 


During the 3-day seminar, the planning ministers and their representa- 
tives discussed means of coordinating development plans in the Arab Gulf 
and peninsula regions, as well as reports related to the strategy and 
objectives of the pan-Arab development plan for the next 5 years and the 
planning of manpower in Iraq. 


The participants in the seminar sent a telegram to Iraqi President Saddam 
Husayn in which they hailed the great role played by Iraq in unifying the 
Arabs and condemned the aggressive attitudes against Iraq expressed by 
any party, affirming their support for Iraq in ite battle against the 
Arab nation's enemies.
