LIBYA

Sudanese Said To Establish Positions in Abeche 
LD130945 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0921 GMT 13 Dec 83 


{Text} Abeche, 13 Dec (JANA) According to informed sources 
here, heavy concentrations of Sudanese forces backed by U.S 
units have taken up positions in Abeche and the surrounding area 
with the intention of occupying the eastern region of Chad and 
then moving northward. It has now been confirmed that French 
and Zairian forces have increased their strength in Ndjamena 
and have brought in large quantities of heavy guns and transport 
vehicles in order to attack northern Chad. These reports confirm 
that all the talk about ending the current conflict among the 
warring parties there was false. It is equally clear that despite the 
efforts of the OAL, all that talk was merely a pretext for 
camouflaging a large-scale act of aggression being planned for 
the occupation of Chad and the restoration of imperialism there 
In this respect, the French have capitalized on Habre’s naivete 
and his ambition to seize power. These reports also indicate that 
the United States is anxiously seeking to impose its domination 
over the African Continent and establish a foothold there 


‘Cooperation’ Talks With Tunisia Open 
LD1 30230 Tripoli JANA in English 1817 GMT 12 Dec 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Rabi al Awal 7, Dec 12, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY Started this morning in Tripoli the 
Libyan-Tunisian talks on bolstering cooperation in the work 
force sector in implementation of the resolutions of the joint 
meeting held in Tripoli between the General People’s Committee 
and the Tunisian cabinet 


The meeting was attended by Muhammad al-Mabruk, the sec 
retary of the General People's Committee for Public Services and 
from Tunisia Mohamed Naceur, minister of social affairs in 
addition to a number of experts and specialists from the Jama 
hirtyah and Tunisia
