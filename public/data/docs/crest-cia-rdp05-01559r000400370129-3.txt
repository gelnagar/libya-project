[Question] If one day Egypt and Syria were to enter into a peace agreement with Israel,

[Question] Do you believe that Carter's accession to the U.S. presidency could have a

[Answer] Any opinion would be premature. We do know, however, that President Carter is

[Question] What advice would you give Carter--to take prompt action in the Middle East? 

[Answer] I do not believe President Carter would take my advice into consideration, 
He certainly does not lack advisers. In any case, I do not believe that America will
