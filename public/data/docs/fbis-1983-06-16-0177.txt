LIBYA

Baghdad: Al-Qadhdhafi on Support for Fatah Rebels 
JN141214 Baghdad Voice of the Masses in Arabic 
1100 GMT 14 Jun 83 


[Text] Nicosia — Libya's Al-Qadhdhafi has overtly expressed his 
support for the rebellious move that has been engineered by some 
Palestinian military elements in Lebanon's Al-Biga’ against the 
PLO leadership. Al-Qadhdhafi described the move as a clear-cut 
{word indistinct} 


In press statements published by the Cyprus-based A/-Mawgif 
4l-"Arabi, which is financed by Libya, Al-Qadhdhafi said the 
Palestinian revolution is without a leader. He asked in this 
context: Is it Abu “Ammar [Yasir ‘Arafat] or Abu Salih? He 
admitted his failure in offering anything in service of che [Pales- 
tinian| cause because, as he put it, the present Palestinian lead- 
ership 1s completely crippled 


Al-Qadhdhafi described the PLO leadership as mercurial, 
pointing out that the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front will 
convene immediately once the situation within Fatah is settled 
This is a clear indication of his support for the rebels 


The Libyan ruler called for declaring what he called a civil war 
against the regimes of Lebanon and the othe: Arab countries, 
claiming that such wars would achieve Arab unity
