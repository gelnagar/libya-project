TOBRUQ RALLY EXPRESSES SUPPORT FOR REVOLUTION 
LD202134 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2015 GMT 20 Aug 80 


[Text] As part of the popular celebrations by the masses in various parts of the 
Jamahiriyah on the occasion of the llth anniversary of the Great 1 September Revolu- 
tion, the town of Tobrug this evening witnessed a large popular demonstration through 
the streets of -ne town. People carried posters expressing the victories of our peo- 
ple, pictures of the brother leader and quotations from the Green Book. They shouted 
slogans pledging the adherence of our people to their revolution, achievements and 
gains. 


The masses of the Tobruq municipality issued a statement underlining their commit- 
ment to power for the people and the socialist changes. The statement added: On 
this immortal occasion, we declare to the whole world that we are an ancient Arab 
people who adhere to their great revolution, will defend it and hold on to its 
achievements as they represent a symbol of which we are proud and will defend with 
everything we possess to the last drop of our blood. 


The Tobruq masses held a large popular rally this evening in which speeches were de- 
livered confirming the adherence of the Tobruq masses to the Great 1 September Revo- 
lution which has achieved popular democracy. 


OPPOSITION FIGURE DELIVERS COMMUNIQUE ON COUP. ATTEMPT 

NC202244 Paris AFP in English 2240 GMT 20 Aug 80 

[Text] Rabat, Aug 20 (AFP)--A coup attempt by the 9th Libyan Army brigade, based 
at Tobruq, was confirmed in a communique given to AGENCE FRANCE-PRESSE here tonight 


by the former head of Libya's audit office, Muhammad Yosef Lamgarief, one of the 
chief leaders of Libyan opposition abroad. 


The brigade revolted on August 6, under the direction of "free officers," said the 
communique from Mr Lamgarief who, before announcing on July 31 his decision to re- 
jcin the opposition, was Libya's ambassador to India. 


"This act of courage has definitely broken the wall of fear built up by the bloody 
regime of Al-Qadhdhafi, and the path is now open for revolt by the Libyan people and 
its army," the communique said. 


"Prom now until the revolution triumphs," it added, "the historic date of August 6 
will be commemorated by all those who struggle to overthrow Al-Qadhdhafi and will 
be considered as a day of national celebration. Vive la revolution. Vive la Libye 
Libre. (Long live the Revolution. Long Live Free Libya)" 


Earlier reports had said rebellious army elements had planned to take Tobruq then 
Benghazi before marching on Tripoli. 


One report said Libyan leder sent planes to bomb the rebel unit, killing about 100 
of its members. 


The Libyan JANA NEWS AGENCY denied reports of the uprising.

AL-QADHDHAFI DISCUSSES STATUS OF CHRISTIAN ARABS 


NC201236 Beirut AS-SAFIR in Arabic 15 Aug 80 pp 8, 9, 10 


{Interview with Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi conducted by chief editor Talal 
Salman, head of Arab section Tawfiq Sardawi, and roving editor Michel Nimri, at a sea- 
side resort for officers in Libya--date not given] 


{Excerpts] Question; Well, now let us talk about the relationship between nationalism 
and Islam. In your opinion, where do Islam and nationalism meet and where do they 
divide? 


Answer; It is evident from history that every nation has had a religion at a certain 
time. Even if a nation did not have a heavenly religion, it invented a religious 
theory to depend on; such as Buddhism, Zoroastrianism, and Confucianism, Perhaps all 
of them were prophets, but we present them today as the originators of man-made 
theories. 


What is important is that a religious theory normally springs up in any nation that has 
no heavenly religion. The conclusion is that every nation has a religion. A nation, 
is on the one hand a national affiliation, and a religious affiliation on the other, 
When I say "religion," I do not necessarily mean Islam or any other heavenly religion, 
but any religious belief. Atheism is a religion. There are those who worship idols 
and those who worship God. The important thing is that every nation must have national 
and religious affiliations. Therefore, there is neither difference nor agreement 
between nationalism and religion. They are like the body and soul of a human being. 
Every nation imparts its national affiliation to its religion. When there are many 
nations with one religion, the religious factor does not last. It soon gets torn apart 
and overcome by the stronger social factor, which is the basis in the nation, 


But if you find many religions in a single nation--that is to say thet the sons of this 
nation believe in many religions--this would be an abnormal situation, as is the case 
today in the Arab homeland, It is abnormal to have a non-Muslim Arab in it. The 
status of the non-Muslim Arab is wrong, and he must be a Muslim and correct his status. 
One cannot be part of a nation and have a religion different from that of the nation. 
Nationalism and religion are two faces on the same coin, 


As for the Christians in the Arab homeland, first it is necessary to emphasize that 
Christ was sent to the Israelites. If you were an Israelite, then it is alright to be 
a Christian, The Israelites themselves, whose prophet was Moses and their religion is 
Judaism, rejected Christ and wanted to crucify him, but they crucified someone who 
resembled him, as stated in the Holy Koran, "But they killed him not, nor crucified 
him, but so it was made to appear to them...nay, God raised Him up unto Himself." 
{Koranic verse] 


The Romans, on the other hand, adopted Christianity. Christ appeared as a persecuted 
man seeking people to adopt Christianity. Indeed, Christianity spread in the Roman 
homeland--Europe. Now Europe itself is beginning to get rid of Christianity because it 
is losing interest init. Half of Europe now believes in Marxism, Its religion is 
Marxism, and its prophet is Karl Marx. In Western Europe, the new generation is 
actually atheist. Perhaps Christianity will become nonexistent in these countries in 
the future. 


The important thing is that those who are concerned with Christianity are the 
Israelites. Moses himself did not come for all the people, but for the Israelites only, 
and so did Christ.

In sending a prophet to every nation, God sought to punish the Israelites. He sent them 
laws and commandments that do not exist in Muhammad's laws. For example, he prohibited 
them from working on Saturdays. This is a punishment. Likewise, the things which are 
prohibited to us are perhaps a punishment to us because they may be permittcd for other 
creeds. Therefore, the question of religion is connected with national affiliation. 


Question: On this basis, what is the fate of the Christian caonmunities in the Arab 
homeland? There are perhaps more than 10 million Christians in the Arab homeland. 


Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi said in the same calm tone and with the same conviction: ‘They have 
adopted the wrong position and they must change their position if they are real Arabs. 


Question: But there is no doubt about their Arabism. 


Answer: ‘Then, they must adopt Islam. Otherwise, spiritually they will be placing them- 
selves in the position of the Israelites. The religion of Arab nationalism is Islan. 

As I said, Carist did not come to the Arabs but to the Israelites, and they in turn 
rejected him. How can we, the Arabs, accept him? 


Question: On this basis, how do you view Christian Europe? 


Answer: I have already told you tue reasons for the spread of Christianity in Europe 
and its future. But the Arabs are not interested in Christianity, and it is wrong to 
be an Arab and a Christian at the same time. The prophet of the Arabs is Muhammad, and 
the Koran came down in the Arabic language. 


Question: Let us in the Arab area agree on God as the basis. 


Answer: We are in agreement about God, be it in Judaism, Islam or Christianity. All 
these heavenly religions are monolithic and call for the worship of one God. But we are 
discussing certain religious laws and religious rites. I do not know why the Christian 
Arab punishes himself with Christ's laws, which are meant to punish the Israelites. Why 
should he bear the punishments that Christ brought for the Israelites? 


Question: If we view religion as the religion of the majority and the political stands 
as that of the majority, the stands of the Christian Arabs are the stands of the majority 
and as such they are completely like the Muslims. Therefore, the political stand of the 
Christian Arabs is clear; but what are the other aspects you talked about? 


Answer: Do you see, as I do, that this situation creates an abnormal state of affairs 
and many problems. I believe that this was at the root of the Lebanese problem. It is 
the duality of being an Arab and a Christian. 


Question: Before the events in Lebanon, the Christian Arab never felt any form of duality, 


Answer: The basis of this duality is that as a Christian Arab you are living with me 

in one homeland, but you are attached more toc the Vatican than you are to Mecca for 
example--that is, your soul is European and your body is Arab. This was the factor that 
caused the contradiction in Lebanon to erupt am, grow bigger and bigger, leaving the 
interested powers to exploit the situation. Israel, France, the United States and Arab 
reactionaries have all interfered. However, the root of the problem is this duality. 

If the Arab people were not divided into Muslims and Christians, no such problem would 
have existed.
