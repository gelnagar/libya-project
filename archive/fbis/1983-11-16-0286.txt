LIBYA

Ghanaian Airliner Refused Landing Permission 
LDI51551 Tripoli JANA in English 1434 GMT 15 Nov 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Safar 10, Nov 15, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY The Egyptian state news agency said that a 
Ghanaian airliner had made an emergency landing in Cairo for 
refuelling after she was refused a landing permission by Libyan 
authorities. JANA correspondent has learnt that the Ghanaian 
liner was indeed refused landing permission in Libyan airport 
after the people in charge had confirmation that the airliner was 
en route from occupied Palestine to Accra. The airliner tried to 
land in Beirut but was not able to because of the security situation 
there. The decision was in line with Libya’s national stand and 
her commitment to the regulations of the Arab Bureau for 
boycotting Israel.
