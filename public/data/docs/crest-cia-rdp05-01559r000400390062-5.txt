Summary (AI): FBIS translation of a Qadhdhafi anniversary speech, broadcast by Tripoli domestic radio in late March 1985, warning that US Rapid Deployment Forces were preparing bases across the Arab world and that Libya could resist occupation. It is political rhetoric with no places, units or repression details in or near Tripoli.
• Speech warns that the American Rapid Deployment Forces (RDF) are being prepared to use bases between the Gulf and the ocean in Arab countries.
• Speech says Libya is free internally and externally but has no guarantees of continued freedom because threats surround it.
• Speech refers to Grenada's resistance to US forces and to Egypt's economic dependence on the United States.

((TEXT)) UNFORTUNATELY, NOW WIDE AREAS OF THE ARAB HOMELAND ARE 

BEING SURVEYED AND PREPARED TO WELCOME THE AMERICAN RAPID DEPLOYMENT 

FORCES ((RDF)). DAY AND NIGHT, BASES EXTENDING BETWEEN THE GULF 

AND THE OCEAN ARE BEING PREPARED FOR THE AMERICAN RDF. 

FOLLOWING ALL THE IMMENSE SACRIFICES OFFERED BY THE ARAB NATION, 

WE FIND OURSELVES WITNESSING A NEW SETBACK FOR THE CAUSE OF 

FREEDOM IN OUR LANDS; AND IMPERIALISM, WHICH WE FOUGHT DAY AND 

NIGHT, NOW RETURNS IN OTHER FORMS. ((CHANTING)) 

ANYWAY, IMPERIALISM MAY RETURN AND MAY EVEN RETURN TO 

LIBYA -- AND NOT UNDER CONDITIONS OF A SETBACK TO THE 

REVOLUTION -- BUT EVEN WITH THE EXISTENCE OF THE REVOLUTION THIS 

COULD HAPPEN. THIS, IN FACT, IS BECAUSE THERE ARE NO GUARANTEES 

FOR THE FREEDOM OF A SMALL AREA SUCH AS LIBYA IN THE ARAB 

HOMELAND. NOW, THIS PIECE OF THE ARAB HOMELAND IS ACTUALLY FREE 

BOTH INTERNALLY AND EXTERNALLY. THERE ARE NO GUARANTEES, HOWEVER, 

FOR ITS CONTINUED FREEDOM, BECAUSE THREATS SURROUND IT FROM ALL 

SIDES. 

THE ARAB COUNTRIES SURROUNDING LIBYA COULD BE USED BY THE RDF 

AGAINST LIBYA. FURTHERMORE, A SMALL PIECE OF LAND WITH A SMALL 

PEOPLE CANNOT GUARANTEE SURVIVAL IN A WORLD DOMINATED BY THE 

LARGER AND THE SUPERPOWERS. WE MAY RESIST. EVEN GRENADA PUT UP 

RESISTANCE -- GRENADA PUT UP RESISTANCE AND CONFRONTATION. HOWEVER, 

IT CANNOT RESIST THE AMERICAN FORCES, WHICH ARE THE PRINCIPAL 

SUPERPOWER IN THE WORLD. ((CHANTS)) WE WILL RESIST WHENEVER THE 

COLONIALISTS RETURN. BUT WE CAN ONLY TAKE THE DECISION TO DIE. 

WE CANNOT DECIDE THE END OF A SUPERPOWER, A COUNTRY LIKE AMERICA, 

WHEN IT DECIDES TO OCCUPY A COUNTRY WHICH IS FREE LIKE LIBYA. 

LIBYA MAY PUT UP RESISTANCE AND ALL ITS PEOPLE COULD 

BECOME MARTYRS. BUT THERE IS ONE THING WE ARE SURE OF: WE CAN DIE, 

AND THE DECISION TO DIE LIES IN OUR HAND. BUT THE ENEMY MAY TRIUMPH 

IN THE END. 

HERE BEGINS THE QUESTION OF ARAB UNITY, WHICH IS AN EMOTIONAL 

MATTER AND NOT A SLOGAN RAISED ABOUT EXTERMISTS OR FANATICS. IN 

FACT, THE ISSUE IS ESSENTIAL FOR THE SURVIVAL OF THE ARAB NATION ON 

EARTH AND UNDER THE SUN. THE ENTIRE ARAB NATION IS EQUAL IN NUMBER 

TO THE PEOPLE OF NIGERIA, FOR EXAMPLE. CAN WE DIVIDE NIGERIA INTO 

20 STATES? NIGERIA ITSELF DOES NOT ACCEPT BEING DIVIDED INTO 2 OR 

3 STATES, NOT TO MENTION 20 STATES, KINGDOMS, REPUBLICS, AMIRATES, 

AND SULTANATES. IF WE UNITE THE ARABS, THEY WILL BECOME A STATE 

LIKE NIGERIA. 

NATURALLY, REGIONALISTS AND THE LACKIES OF COLONIALISM BRING UP 

THE GENERATIONS BY TELLING THEM THROUGH SCHOOLS AND NATIONAL 

EDUCATION THAT THIS IS A TUNISIAN NATION, THIS IS AN ALCGERIAN NATION, 

THIS IS A LEBANESE NATION. AND THEY CLOSE BORDERS. THEY SERVE 

COLONTALISM. WHILE THEY ARE IN THEIR CAPITALS THEY SERVE 

COLONIALISM. CURRICULA ARE DRAWN BY THE TRAITORS, LACKIES, AND 

AGENTS TO SERVE COLONIALISM. ANY CHILD CAN DRAW THE MAP OF THE 

REGION IN WHICH HE LIVES, BUT HE CANNOT DRAW THE MAP OF THE ARAB 

HOMELAND. THIS IS A SIMPLE BUT AN IMPORTANT MATTER. HOWEVER, WITH 

OUT NEW REVOLUTIONARY GENERATION AND THE NEW REVOLUTIONARY FORCE, 

WE ARE TRYING TO RALLY. WE HAVE ALWAYS REJECTED FAILURE. WE

ALL THE CAPABILITIES OF PRODUCTION IN EGYPT STOPPED, AND IT 

BECAME MORTGAGED TO THE UNITED STATES. THE WHEAT COMES EVERY 3 
MONTHS TO THE PEOPLE OF EGYPT FROM AMERICA, INSTEAD OF EGYPT 
EXPORTING WHEAT TO THEM. COLONIALISM CAME IN DURING SADAT'S ERA

IN EGYPT. EGYPT BECAME A CONSUMER MARKET. HOTELS WERE BUILT; 
LUXURY CARS WERE AROUND, TOGETHER WITH CAR DEALERS' OFFICES. BUT 
DO THE PEASANT, THE WORKER, AND THE MILLIONS OF TOILERS BENEFIT FROM 
HOTELS LIKE THE SHERATON, THE HILTON? THEY DO NOT KNOW THEM 

NOR CAN THEY APPROACH THEM. HOW DOES AN EGYPTIAN PEASANT OR
