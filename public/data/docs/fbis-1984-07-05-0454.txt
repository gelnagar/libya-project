LIBYA

Iranian Envoy Meets With At-Turayki 
LDO041952 Tehran IRNA in English 1755 GMT 4 Jul 84 


[Text] Tripoli, July 4, IRNA — The ambassador of the Islamic 
Republic of Iran to Libya, Mohammad Qasem Khansari, met 
and conferred with foreign secretary of Libya, ‘Ali ‘Abd as- 
Salam at-Turayki here Wednesday. 


The expansion of mutual relations and other issues were dis- 
cussed between the two parties during the meeting. 


Committee Preparing Anniversary Congress Ends Session 
LDOS1002 Tripoli JANA in English 0924 GMT 5 Jul 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 7 Shawal, 5 July, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS 
AGENCY -—- The International Committee for the Preparation 
of the International Congress, which will be held on the occasion 
of the 15th anniversary of the great 1st September revolution on 
29th-3 ith August 1984, ended its meetings yesterday in Tripoli. 


At the end of its meetings, the committee issued a communique 
deploring the American plots and terrorist plans against the 
Libyan Arab people, the revolutionary forces and liberation 
movements in the world affirming the Libyan Arab people's 
capability to confront all the plots set by imperialism and Zion- 
ism against the Arab nation. 


The communique asserted that the international congress which 
will be held in Jamahiriyah would be an international expression 
to stand by all liberation and democratic forces in the world 
against American imperialism for freedom, progress and peace 
in the Arab region and throughout the world.

ant Colonel (?Benjamine Ahiqbaine). A Sudanese People’s Lib-

mentioned attack, which occurred 29 June, one of the lackey
