Summary (AI): An FBIS transcript of a Tripoli radio broadcast of Muammar al-Qadhdhafi's 10 April 1985 press conference announcing a "Pan-Arab Command" of revolutionary forces. He rejects the U.S. State Department statement, calls the U.S. a colonialist state, and warns that any interference will bring the war to the interferer's own country, while insisting Libya itself is not responsible.
• Qadhdhafi said the Pan-Arab Command aims to liberate the Arab homeland and unite the Arab nation.
• He rejected a U.S. State Department statement as flagrant interference and said the U.S. was a colonialist and racist state.
• He said the Pan-Arab Command would not transfer violence outside the Arab homeland unless others interfered, in which case the war would be transferred to their own countries.
• He stated he was not a head of state or president of the Jamahiriya, since the Jamahiriya system has no president.
• 1985-04-10: Qadhdhafi press conference with Arab and international correspondents announcing the Pan-Arab Command of the Revolutionary Forces in the Arab Homeland

((TEXT)) ((AL-QADHDHAFI)) FIRST OF ALL I WOULD LIKE TO WELCOME 

YOU. THE REASON FOR CONVENING THIS PRESS CONFERENCE IS THE FACT 

THAT THERE IS NOW A PAN-ARAB COMMAND FOR THE ARAB REVOLUTION FORCES 

IN THE ARAB HOMELAND. I WOULD LIKE TO POINT OUT THAT A PAN-ARAB 

COMMAND TO LEAD THE ARAB REVOLUTIONARY FORCES IN THE ARAB 

HOMELAND HAS BEEN SET UP. THIS COMMAND HAS BEEN ANNOUNCED AND 

IS QUITE CLEAR; IT IS NO SECRET. 

THE AIM OF THIS COMMAND IS TO LIBERATE THE ARAB HOMELAND AND TO 

UNITE THE ARAB NATION. WE CALL ON ALL FORCES STRUGGLING FOR 

FREEDOM, JUSTICE, AND PEACE IN THE WORLD TO SUPPORT THIS ACTION. 

THE AIM OF THIS COMMAND IS LEGITIMATE AND DEFENSIVE. TI IS TO 

FUNCTION INSIDE THE OCCUPIED AND TORN ARAB HOMELAND WHICH IS IN 

NEED OF LIBERATION AND UNITY. 

I REGRET VERY MUCH TO SEE REACTION FROM SUPERPOWERS SUCH AS 

THE UNITED STATES, A REACTION WHICH IS HOSTILE AND IS CONSIDERED 

A CRUDE INTERFERENCE IN THE INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF A NATION WHICH WANTS 

TO SOLVE ITS LIBERATION AND UNIFICATION PROBLEMS. I 

REGRET THIS VERY MUCH AND CATEGORICALLY REJECT, IN THE NAME OF THE 

PAN-ARAB COMMAND OF THE REVOLUTIONARY FORCES IN THE ARAB HOMELAND, 

THIS STATEMENT WHICH IS REGARDED AS BEING A FLAGRANT 

INTERFERENCE ~- THE STATEMENT ISSUED BY THE U.S. STATE 

DEPARTMENT. THE ONLY SIGNIFICANCE OF THIS AMERICAN STATEMENT IS 

THAT IT PROVES BEFORE THE WORLD THAT THE UNITED STATES IS A 

COLONIALIST AND RACIST STATE, HOSTILE TO THE 

LIBERATION OF PEOPLES AND TO THE NATIONS WHICH WANT TO SOLVE 

THEIR PROBLEMS AND UNIFY THEIR TERRITORIES. THIS IS A DENIAL BY THE 

UNITED STATES OF THE PRINCIPLES FOR WHICH GEORGE WASHINGTON 

REBELLED AND FOR WHICH ABRAHAM LINCOLN FOUGHT. 

THIS IS A BETRAYAL OF THESE PRINCIPLES. THE OTHER SIGNIFICANCE 

IS THAT THE UNITED STATES HAS RIGHT FROM 

THE VERY BEGINNING DECIDED ITS ATTITUDE -- TO BE HOSTILE TO THE 

ASPIRATINS OF THE ARAB NATION -- AND ULTIMATELY HAS MADE ITSELF 

A TARGET OF THE PAN-ARAB COMMAND AND OF THE REVOLUTIONARY FORCES 

WHICH BELONG TO THIS COMMAND. 

I ADVISE THE UNITED STATES TO CHANGE ITS ANTI-ARAB STANCE AND 

TO BE FRIENDLY TO THIS NATION WHEN THE LATTER IS LIBERATED AND 

UNITED AT THE HANDS OF THE HANDS OF THE PAN-ARAB COMMAND OF THE 

REVOLUTIONARY FORCES. JOINT INTERESTS OF BOTH THE ARAB NATION AND 

THE AMERICAN NATION CAN BE SERVED ON THE BASIS OF THE PRINCIPLES 

OF MUTUAL RESPECT, ASSISTANCE, NONINTERFERENCE IN EACH OTHER'S 

AFFAIRS, AND RESPECT FOR EACH NATION'S CHOICES. IF THE UNITED 

STATES IS OF THE OPINION THAT ITS INTERESTS CAN BE SERVED WHEN THE 

ARAB NATION IS TORN APART, COLONIZED, AND BACKWARD, THEN THIS 

IS AN UNLAWFUL AND COLONIALIST INTEREST, AND DESTRUCTIVE TO THE 

ARAB NATION, WHICH WILL BE RESISTED BY THE PAN-ARAB COMMAND OF THE
