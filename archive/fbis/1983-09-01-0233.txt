AL-ZAHAF AL-AKHDAR Attacks U.S. ‘Aggression’ 
PM010818 London Al-Zahaf al-Akhdar in English 

27 Aug 83 p7 
{Unattributed article: Who Is To Shoot 
First?”’} 


“U.S. Aggression: 


[Text] The approach of American planes close to the Libyan 
coast, the beginning of the American-Egyptian manoeuvres on 
the eastern borders of the Jamahiriyah, the escalation of the 
military conflict in Chad and the constant rumours: all these 


are indications of the intention of the United States Administra- 
tion to enter into a military conflict with the Jamahiriyah. 


The American Administration, which for a long time has been 
searching for a pretext to attack the Jamahiriyah militarily, has 
found in the issue of Chad the opportunity to do this. Thus it is 
strengthening its presence there, helped by France and some 
reactionary regimes in Africa, especially Egypt, Zaire and the 
Sudan. 


The American Administration and the other countries which 
want to wage a war on the Jamahiriyah have exploited events in 
Chad, where a civil war has been waged for more than twenty 
years. They have imposed a military siege, the first step in the 
declaration of an aggressive war against the Jamahiriyah, which 
started this time from the southern border. 


The American Administration claims falsely that it is only giving 
material and military aid to the government of the rebel Hissein 
Habre, without intervening directly. America knows that what 
is taking place in Chad is an internal power struggle, between 
President Goukouni Oueddei and the rebel minister Hissein 
Habre. It also knows that the Jamahiriyah is not a party to this 
struggle, and that it adopted a neutral stance, until it was proven 
that the American Administration itself was involved in the 
struggle, not to mention the involvement of other foreign forces 
whether from Western Europe or Africa, on the side of one group 
in Chad against eleven other local groups. 


War Against Jamahiriyah 


The aim behind the escalation of the military and political 
situation is to wage a war against the Jamahiriyah, to threaten 
its security, its entity, and to besiege it in order to undermine its 
active and progressive role in world affairs, especially in relation 
to events in the Arab region, Africa and Central and Latin 
America, Chad and other similar issues are used by the American 
Administration and African reaction to justify their war against 
the Jamahiriyah. This war according to all indications will be a 
military one. 


Thousands of American soldiers of occupation are stationed on 
the Eastern borders of the Jamahiriyah under the cover of the 
joint American-Egyptian military manoeuvres. Tens of military 
aircraft are in readiness on the aircraft carrier Eisenhower with 
hundreds of American marines, all ready to embark ona military 
attack on the Arab Libyan coast. On the southern border, 
hundreds of soldiers and technicians, American and French, are 


based in Chad. This is an indication of the aggressive intentions 
of the American Administration against the people of the Jama- 
hiriyah. It is in preparation for a military action. 


There has never been an occasion when American land, air and 
naval forces took part in manoeuvres in this region without 
constituting the beginning of an aggressive war against the 
Jamahiriyah. This is what the American Administration is pre- 
paring for, just as it prepared for the Vietnam war and has 
prepared against the people of Nicaragua. It is war then, and the 
Arab people in the Jamahiriyah should place their fingers on the 
trigger. 


Mauritanian President Arrives in Tripoli 
LD312346 Tripoli JANA in English 1808 GMT 31 Aug 83 


[Text] Tripoli, Dhulgidah 23, august 31, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Mr Mohamed Ould Haydala, the presi- 
dent of the Mairutanian Islamic Republic arrived here this 
afternoon to take part in the celebrations of the Libyan Arab 
people’s 14th anniversary of their glorious Al-Fatih Revolution. 
He was warmly welcomed at Tripoli international airport by 
Staff Major "Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, members of the 
General People’s Committee, heads of diplomatic missions here, 
officers of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces and the Mauritanian 
youth delegation taking part in the 2nd African youth festival. 
The president was accompanied by Colonel Ahmed Ould Abd- 
allah, the Mauritanian foreign minister [name and title as 
received], Mr (Bahri Joub Mustapha), member of the military 
committee and Mr (Mohammad Abdelrahman), the director of 
presidential affairs. 


Mr Mchamed Khouna Ould Haydala made a statement to 
JANA correspondent in which he expressed his happiness to visit 
Jamahiriyah and attend the festivities of the Libyan Arab peo- 
ple’s 14th anniversary of the Great Al-Fatih Revolution. He said 
that during this visit, he will have a chance to discuss a number 
of issues of joint interest, especially on Africa and the Arab 
world. He concluded by praising cooperation relations between 
the Jamahiriyah and Mauritania which he said have been bol- 
stered in different fields for the interest of the 2 brotherly peoples. 


Soviet Delegation Arrives for Anniversary Celebrations 
LD311408 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
3] Aug 83 


[Text] Bally Yazkuliyev, deputy chairman of the USSR Supreme 
Soviet Presidium, arrived in Tripoli early this morning on a visit 
to the Jamahiriyah to take part in the celebrations of the Libyan 
Arab people on the 14th anniversary of the great | September 
revolution. Yazkuliyev expressed hope that his visit will contri- 
bute to the development of friendly relations betwen the Soviet 
and Libyan Arab people and to their consolidation and the 
backing of the joint struggle against imperialism. In a statement 
to JANA, he said that the Soviet people are following with 
interest the successes achieved by the Libyan Arab people against 
imperialism, Zionism, and reaction. He added that his visit will 
be an opportunity to acquaint himself with the great 
achievements gained by the Libyan Arab people on various levels.
