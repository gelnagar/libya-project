LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SPEAKS OF NEED FOR LIBYAN-U.S. DIALOG 
LD111602Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 11 Oct 78 LD 


[Text ] Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi attended part of the morning session of the Arab- 
people's dialog which began yesterday. Speaking at the session, the colonel 
the importance of this dialog in building bridges of understanding between the 

American and Arab nations, in the absence of which the world is losing important oppor- 

tunities and is increasing misunderstanding, which in the end leads to international 

conflict and harms the cause of peace. 


He said: This symposium must be a brick in a large structure which one day may be com- 
pleted, because it is of no interest to Arabs and Americans to contime misunderstanding 
and hostility. He said: From the start we should admit that America in general at 
present is hostile to the Arab nation, moreover hostile to a large number of the peoples 
of the world. He stressed that this was an irrefutable fact despite what is being said 
to justify it as a policy and not a hostile act. However, in any event this policy is 
hostile to the people's interests, threatening their independence and against their 
progress. The Americans must answer the question: Why are you hostile to the Arabs and 
the peoples of the globe? Because this is not our problem. We are facing a hostile 
policy placing us in a defensive position, forcing us, according to the rules of self- 
defense, to resist it. 


The colonel also discussed international terrorism. He said he is most enthusiastic 
about the need to submit the issue of terrorism to the United Nations so that the inter- 
national community may find a solution to it. However, we ought to know what terrorism 
is and to ask the peoples about the type of terrorism they are experiencing and the source 
of it. We also should draw a list of the types of terrorism which are facing humanity, 
such as military bases, naval fleets, nuclear weapons, starvation and the occupation of 
territory by force. The brother colonel said that U.S. policy in the Arab homeland is 
hostile to the progressive and liberation regimes and in return supports fascism and 
reaction. He said: This is an historic insult to the American people. He stressed that 
what was being reiterated by America. about hjman rights was a demagogic policy because 
it is practiced on behalf of human rights everywhere unless they are American or Israeli 
rights.
