Summary (AI): A 1981 Aviation Week item reports that West German private rocket firm Otrag closed its launch operations in Libya, which had been based at Seba Oasis about 560 miles south of Tripoli. Libya's leader Qadhafi did not oppose the shutdown, and Morocco had accused Libya of using the program to acquire medium-range missile technology.
• Otrag conducted a series of launches in Zaire in 1977-78 before moving to Libya.
• Morocco charged that Qadhafi was using Otrag to acquire technology for medium-range missiles; Wukasch denied this.
• Otrag's 1,500 shareholders put up an additional $10 million to continue activity through 1982.
• A planned two-stage rocket test with an Otrag-developed inertial navigation system was cancelled.
• 1981-03-01: Otrag successfully tested a low-cost single-stage launch vehicle from Seba Oasis, its fourth test overall.
• 1981-08: Otrag withdrew from Libya about four months before the December 1981 report, to avoid political problems with North African states opposed to the program.
