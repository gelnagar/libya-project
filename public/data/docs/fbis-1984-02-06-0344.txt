French Minister Cheysson Visits for Talks 
LD041525 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1432 GMT 4 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, 4 Feb(JANA) — The French minister for external 
relations, Claude Cheysson, arrived in Libya this afternoon. 


Meets With Al-Qadhdhafi 
LDO51225 Paris Domestic Service in French 1200 GMT 
5 Feb 84 


[Text] Claude Cheysson met with Colonel al-Qadhdhafi in Trip- 
oli, Libya, this morning. Their talks, delicate ones as far as the 
minister of external relations is concerned, have just ended, but 
we have no further information so far. 


Jallud Receives French Visitor

[Text] Tripoli, Jumada al Ulla 2, February 5, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud 
yesterday evening received French Foreign Minister Claude 
Cheysson, who is currently visiting the Socialist People’s Libyan 
Arab Jamahiriyah. , 


Cheysson Departs 5 Feb 
LDO52102 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1755 GMT 
5 Feb 84 


{Excerpts] Tripoli, 5 Feb (JANA) The French minister of 
external relations, Claude Cheysson, left Tripoli this evening 
after a 2-day visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


Brother ‘Abd al-‘Ati al-‘Ubaydi, secretary of the popular com- 
mittee of the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison was at Tripoli 
International Airport to see him off.
