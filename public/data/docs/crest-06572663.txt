IRAQI-LIBYAN INVESTIGATION OF URANIUM EXTRACTION POSSIBILITIES IN SOMALIA
https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/06572663

Summary (AI): A March 1999 CIA information report says Libyan and Iraqi technical specialists were investigating uranium extraction from deposits at Bur Acaba in Somalia, far from Tripoli. Libya was reported to be expanding its influence in Somalia during the breakdown of peace there.
• Delegation of 15 included seven Iraqis, four Iranians and four Libyans.
• Italian energy company ENI held a uranium concession at Bur Acaba and worked there in the early 1980s before fighting closed foreign activity.
• Initial indications were that ENI did not consider the Bur Acaba uranium to be of good quality.
• 1999-03-05: Five Libyans arrived in Somalia to evaluate uranium extraction at Bur Acaba with Somali counterparts.
• 1999-03-07: Aircraft arrived at Balidogle with a 15-person delegation of technical specialists, including seven Iraqis, four Iranians and four Libyans.
