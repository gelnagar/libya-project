Summary (AI): This is a 1977 FBIS wire relaying five takes of a Qadhafi speech broadcast by Tripoli Domestic Service on 7 October 1977 at a Libyan rally. He attacks the United States and Israel, calls for Arab unity, mentions possible Libyan air force strikes on Israeli airfields in Sinai, and says popular congresses will decide on unity with Egypt. It has no specific local places in Tripoli.
• Qadhafi says popular congresses will meet later this year to vote on unity with Egypt.
• Qadhafi says he would order Libyan Air Force squadrons to strike Israeli airfields in Sinai if he alone decided.
• Qadhafi says the United States is behind Israeli actions against the Arab nation.
• Qadhafi calls for public ownership of natural resources such as water and land.
• 1977-10-07: Qadhafi gives a speech at a rally (about 2 hours 47 minutes), broadcast by Tripoli Domestic Service
