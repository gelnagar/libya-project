LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SPEAKS ON CHAD, LOME TALKS, FRANCE 


LD142040 Tripoli JANA in English 1925 GMT 14 Jan 81 


[Text] Benghazi, 14 Jan (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 
great Al-Fatah revolution, declared today, in a meeting with the student masses in 
Benghazi, that Libyan forces in Chad were being presently reinforced to face French 
colonialist forces’ reinforcements along Chad's borders. 


The leader added "The Libyan forces entered Chad in accordance with an agreement between 
the two countries and in response to President Goukouni's call, who has all the legitimacy 
because he was the one who formed the Chad Liberation Movement (Frolinat) and who led the 
liberation war for many years. It was also he who brought armed struggle to Chad's capital 
and overthrew Maloum's puppet regime. Goukouni Oweddei has all the legitimacy enabling 
him to call for Libyan forces, and will continue to have it. He alone is capable of 
asking them to withdraw, and he alone will be positively answered by Libya which will 
withdraw its forces after they had accomplished their task with honour and bravery, after 
they have given martyrs, and after these Libyan forces have defeated the rebellion and 
ended the civil war which has lasted many years, and also destroyed imperialist and 
reactionary attempts to transform Chad into an aggressive base. 


The leader affirmed "The Frolinat movement, alongside of which Libya fought for ten years 
against imperialism, American interference, reactionary forces in Africa and the racist 
puppet regime in Chad, and which now has come to power, knows that the Libyan people is 
its historic ally and which will not abandon it in some future day."

The leader added "The Frolinat movement is an African liberation movement and an 
historical revolutionary movement hostile to colonialism just like the Algerian Libera- 
tion Front, the Popular Front for Liberation of Angola, the Mozambique Liberation Move- 
ment and the Guinea-Bissau Liberation Movement. The leader of this movement is Goukouni 
Oueddei who has the same legitimacy as any of the above mentioned movements, and has the 
same revolutionary legitimacy, and any decision adopted by the Lome conference against 
this movement's legitimacy is but ink or paper because it cannot annul quarter of a 
century's struggle waged by the Chadian people, or annul the value of the sacrifices of 
the martyrs who fell defending freedom in Chad." 


The leader declared "The Libyan forces will not leave Chad until French forces leave 
Central Africa." 


Regarding Lome's current conference, the leader said "This conference, which has no 
legitimacy, will not be able to take any decision concerning Chad, and any resolutions 
emanating from this conference will be ignored by us and will not be binding on us." 


The Libyan leader asked "Why are those who are meeting today in Lome not objecting to 
the inflow of French troops into the Central African Republic and to the coup France 
organized in that country followed by the setting-up of a government flown in to 

Bangui on French planes? Why do not they object to the inflow of French troops into the 
Republic of Cameroon, Chad's neighbour? Why do not they object to the presence of 
French troops in Gabon and Senegal? Why do not they ask for the withdrawal of the 
Senegalese forces occupying Gambia? And why do not those who are meeting today in Lome 
protest against the presence of Egyptian troops in Sudan and Tanzanian troops in 
Uganda? 


The Libyan leader added "Those meeting in Lome should have protested against these 
imperialist interventions in Africa instead of protesting against the aid the Libyans 


give to the Chadian people.” 


The Libyan leader wert on “Any decision some African heads of state meeting in Lome 
might take against Libya or the legitimate government in Chad would have dangerous 
results. In this case the Libyan people will seriously consider stopping all economic 
aid to these conspiracies." He added: "Any violation of Chadian territory will be 
considered a violation of Libyan soil, and the Libyan people will fight side by side 
with the Chadian people. We are ready to take part in any battle, we will never 
refrain from defending the Chadian people, we will not leave the bodies of our martyrs 
to the imperialists’ mercy, we will use all our military potential in the battle 
because we are the allies of the African peoples and revolutionary movements in 
Africa, and we will not hesitate to wage war against imperialism until they burn in 
the hungles of Africa along with their bases and their puppets- 


The Libyan leader affirmed "We accept the challenge and the difficult path" regarding 
relations with France, the Libyan leader again affirmed that "The Libyan people, in 
all the decisions taken at people's congresses, is still preserving France's friend- 
ship and is still protecting the common and mutual interests existing between the 
Libyan and French peoples. We are striving and we will always strive to promote our 
relations with France but this cannot be done at the expense of Libya's security, or 
at the expense of Libya's martyrs in Chad, or if the conspiracy against Libya goes on 
behind the scenes. If France interferes with the security of the Libyan people, the 
Libyan people will enter the battle with all its weapons, the oil weapon, the polit- 
ical and economic weapon, and the revolutionary weapon. France will then suffer heavy 
losses ,"

The Libyan leader added that "If France commits any foolish act against the Libyan 
people, propositions will be put forward to the people's congresses, in emergency 
sessions, to boycott France economically and in all fields". 


The leader stressed "France will be the losing party politically, economically and 
militarily in Africa and the Arab nation if it stood against Libya and intervened in 
Libya's circle of security, and the freedom loving Africans will be with us". 


The leader added "By what right does France, which is in the heart of Europe, regard 
the security of a country in the middle of Africa as related to its own security? And 
how do French soldiers fight in Central Africa in the name of France's security while 
the Libyan people do not have the right to fight in Chad defending their security? And 
how could the Libyan people not regard Chad's security as related to their own?" 


The leader went on to say "America, which is situated in the other half of the globe, 
is also regarding Chad's security as related to its own security. So how could Libya 
not regard Chad's security as related to its own?" 


The leader also warned the Egyptian and Sudanese regimes of the consequences of 
conspiring against the Chadian people." 


Reinforcements in Chad 
LD141940 Tripoli JANA in Arabic i800 GMT 14 Jan 81 


[Text] Benghazi, 14 Jan (JANA)--Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the 1 September 
revolution, this morning addressed students in Benghazi. He said that the Libyan 
forces in Chad are now being reinforced in face of French imperialist troop reinforce- 
ments on the Chad borders. He added that the Libyan forces entered Chad in accordance 
with [words indistinct] between the two countries and at the request of President 
Goukouni Oueddei, who enjoys full legitimacy because it is he who created the Chad 
Liberation Movement, Frolinat, who led the liberation war for many years and who 
brought the armed struggle to the capital of Chad and brought down the agent govern- 
ment of Maloum [words indistinct], Goukouni alone has the full legitimate right to 
{word indistinct] to invite the Libyan forces into his country, to keep them there or 
to ask them to withdraw. Only to him could Libya respond if he requested withdrawal 
of its forces after they have discharged their mission honorably and bravely and 
defeated rebellion. 


ARMY CHIEF RECEIVES PALESTINIAN, LEBANESE LEADERS 


LD150009 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 14 Jan 81 


[Text] Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, commander in chief of the armed forces, received a 
delegation representing groups of the Palestinian resistance and the Lebanese 
Nationalist Movement. 


It includes the brothers: Nayif Hawatimah, secretary general of the Democratic Front; 
Abu Ali Mustafa, assistant secretary of the Popular Front for the Liberation of 
Palestine; (Niami Jindil) member of the As-Sa'iqah political bureau; Taysir Abd 
al-Majid, member of the political bureau of the Palestinian Popular Struggle Front; 

Dr ‘Abdallah Sa'adah, from the Syrian Social Nationalist party; Kamal Yunis, member of 
the executive committee of the Lebanese Socialist Union; and Nadim Abd as-Samad, member 
of the Political Bureau of the Lebanese Communist Party.
