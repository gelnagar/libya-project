AT#TURAYKI ON OIL WEAPON, LIBYAN DIPLOMATS IN U.S. 
LD161133 Kuwait AR@RAtY ALe®AMM in Arabic 14 May 30 p 19 


[Hisham Talib report: "At#Turayxi Calls on Arabs To Use Oil as Weapon; LibyaneTunisian 
Relations Returning to Normal"] 


[Text] ibyan Foreign Secretary Dr "Abd aseSalam ateTurayki has called for Arab fronts to 
be open to Fedayeen action. Speaking at a press conference late last night before leaving 
for Islamabad, he said the Arabs must turn their attention to the Palestinian problem more 
than any other, because it represents the conflict with Zionism. He stressed that this was 
the Muslims? number one problem, to which all capabilities and weapons must be devoted.

He criticized those who shed tears for Afghan Muslims and said: Where do they stand 
toward events in Uganda and the Philippines and toward what is happening to the Muslims 
there? He wondered why no attempt is being made to discuss the attempt to invade Muslim 
Iran. 


The Libyan official once again stated his country's demand that the Western countries 
pay compensation for the losses it suffered as a result of their fighting on Libyan 
territories, saying that Libya suffered losses and destruction when in a battle in 
which it had no interest. He said that international decisions have been made giving 
Libya the right to make such a claim. He called on the Arab countries to use all 
weapons, including the oil weapons, against the Zionist enemy, who “has used the West 
to serve his ends." He stressed in this regard the need for an Arab decision to cut 
off oil to Western countries that serve Israeli interests. 


Replying to a question regarding Libyan-Tunisian relations, he referred to the historical 
ties between the two countries which, he said, developed following the "Djerba" unity 
declaration which was aborted by certain separatist elements. He said: Once those 
elements were gone, relations between the two countries went through a new stage, and 
contacts and coordination between us developed. 


Speaking about the deportation of Libyan diplomats from the United States, he said that 
the United States and Britain see danger in Libya. The deportation of Libyan diplomats 
was unjustified, he said. 


The Libyan foreign secretary then spoke about Arab-Iranian relations. He referred to 
the importance of strengthening Arab alliance with Iran, on the grounds that Iran now 
constitutes with the Arabs a joint front against Zionism. He denied that his country 
supports one Arab country against ancther and said that it is absolutely not in the 
Arab nation's interest to lose a strong ally [presumably Iran]. 


Concluding his conference, Dr At-Turayki said that Kuwaiti-Libyan relations are extremely 
close and that he wished that all Arab countries! relations could be like those between 
Kuwait and Libya in the field of joint economic projects. He described his talks with 
Minister of State 'Abd al-'Aziz Husayn as very positive.
