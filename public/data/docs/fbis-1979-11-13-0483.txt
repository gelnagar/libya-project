LIBYA

JANA COMMENTARY ON U.S, -IRANIAN SITUATION 
LD101008 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 10 Nov 79 LD 


{Text] Tripoli--Some news agencies have reported that the PLO is mediating for the 
release of the Ameridan hostages being held by Iranian students in the U.S. Embassy in 
Tehran. 


The political editor of JANA has stateé that the Palestinians have bigger and greater 
tasks. The liberation of Jerusalem from tne claws of Zionism, and, it is not their task 
to mediate in matters which concern the Americans who are the main financers and backers 
of the Zionist gangs usurping (?Arab) Palestine and they constitute the state most hostile 
to the just struggle of the Arab nation in confronting Zionism, 


The political editor has added that if such reports are true, this is a blemish of dis- 
grace on the forehead of the Palestinian revolution which must stand with all Arabs with 
the Iranian Islamic revolution in confronting the intrigues of American-Zionist 
imperialism. The political editor had warned the Palestinian revolution against falling 
into the trap being laid by American imperialism, 


Condemns Sadat's Offer 
LD101010 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 10 Nov 79 LD 
{Text ] Tripoli--JANA's political editor has commented on the offer of the vanquished 


Egyptian president to grant once again the ejected shah to come to Egypt and reside in 
it. The editor says: 


This offer made by the vanquished Egyptian president in the present circumstances, with- 
out anyone asking him for this, only confirms that the vanquished Egyptian president is 
volunteering for the service of U.S. imperialism and at the same time confirms the open 
hostility which the vanquished president harbors for the Islamic popular revolution in 

Iran which stands solidly on the side of the Palestinian Arab question. The JANA political 
editor adds that by this behavior the vanquished president is merely putting into practice 
the adage, "give us your dog to bark for us."
