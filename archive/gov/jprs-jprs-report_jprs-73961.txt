LIBYA

AGRICULTURAL PROJECT WILL ENHANCE FOOD SECURITY 
Paris AL-MUSTAQBAL in Arabic 2 Jun 79 p 42 
[Unsigned article: "First Treaty of Its Kind: Wheat in Hand" ] 


[Text] "Wheat in hand" is the title of the new agreement reached by a French 
organization (Procurement Assistance and Cooperation) with the concerned 
authorities in the Libyan Arab Jumahiriyah. According to the agreement, the 
French company will implement on behalf of Libya an agricultural project 
which will supply Libya with additional quantities of wheat through which 

the latter's food security will be strengthened. 


The title of the project, as is clear, is borrowed from the expression 

"keys in hand" which is used whenever a given organization agrees with a 
second party to construct a factory to be delivered ready for operation and 
production. However, if the expression "keys in hand" has become customary 

in the realm of industrial contracts for a great number of factories and 
industrial installations, the expression "wheat in hand" is a new expression, 
since this is the first time an agreement has been reached between two 

parties in which an agricultural project will be implemented at the production 
stage. 


It has been customary in various types of agricultural contracts to task the 
technical authority with preparing studies, supervising the implementation 
of the project, constructing the installations and equiping the facilities, 
but to leave the agricultural operation and production to the project owners. 
However, this contract requires the French company to turn over the field, 
which is to be developed, after 3 years in such a condition that 36 kantaras 
of wheat and 19 kantaras of alfalfa per hectare are being produced. 


The field is located in the heart of the Al-Sarir desert about 600 km from 
Benghazi. It is 8,500 hectares in area. The field will be irrigated by 
water from an area in the desert where 21 wells were drilled by a Bulgarian 
organization. The French company wiil equip the field with giant, wheel- 
mounted watering platforms which measure over 500 meters each. The project 
will also be equiped with all necessary ..saterials for fertilizing, seeding 
and harvesting.

Although the French company that was awarded the contract won out over the 
American firm, known as (Bull), which had also bid on it, French official 
circles are not greatly pleased. Desp'’te the fact that the operation will 
be financed through a loan from Libya--the total value of the contract is 
390 million French francs (about $90 million)--the fact that the French 
won the contract does not mean that other foreign companies have been excluded 
from participating in the deal, since the obligated company has found that 
it will be compelled to seek help from foreign companies to provide the 
equipment valued at 50 million French francs ($12 million). This includes 
the American (Falmount) Company, which will provide the watering platforms, 
and the German firm (KSB), which will provide the pumps. The French must 
seek help from foreigners either because of French firms’ inability to 
provide the required equipment within the specified time, because the price 
of the equipment has risen, or because of the unavailability of suitable 
equipment. 


Nevertheless, the French will retain 340 million French francs from the project 
to be distributed among approximately 20 medium and small industrial firms. 
Prominent among these is the engineering firm which won the contract, and 

has put together a conglomerate of companies which will participate in its 
implementation. It has already started work by sending 60 engineers who will 
live--as the French say--this adventure in the heart of the desert for 3 

whole years. 


The most prominent aspect of the project is not that it will provide Libya 

with a new agricultural area which increase the production of wheat and alfalfa, 
but rather that it will give 250 Libyans and Tunisians the opportunity to 

be trained by the French in the field and in France during the next 3 years, 

so that later they will be able to farm that particular field and to teach 
others to farm other fields.
