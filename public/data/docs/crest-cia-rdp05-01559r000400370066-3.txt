Summary (AI): A May 1976 Tanjug-relayed report quotes Libyan leader Muammar al-Qadhdhafi praising Libyan-Yugoslav relations and describing Yugoslavia as an independent, non-aligned country. The OCR is badly garbled, so some details are uncertain. The item concerns foreign policy and has no specific Tripoli sites.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya and Yugoslavia have an agreement under which the heads of government meet at least once a year, alternating between Belgrade and Tripoli.
• The agreement is described as without precedent among non-aligned countries that do not have a formal alliance.
• Qadhdhafi said Libya's relations with Yugoslavia are based on mutual respect and cooperation (partially garbled).
• 1976-05-03: Qadhdhafi, in an interview with a Yugoslav journalist, said Libyans consider Yugoslavia an independent, non-aligned country they can rely on.
