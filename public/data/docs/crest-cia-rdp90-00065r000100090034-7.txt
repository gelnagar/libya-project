Summary (AI): An FBIS routing slip and cover note (9 September 1987) reporting that FBIS provided near real-time coverage of Libyan television's coverage of the 1 September coup anniversary, including a military parade and an evening speech by Qadhdhafi. The London Bureau recorded the broadcast and sent videotapes to headquarters after Moscow television coverage. The document itself contains no details of the parade or speech.
• The coverage was recorded by the FBIS London Bureau because of commitments to cover Moscow television live.
• Videotapes were fed to Headquarters via INTERNET and Langley after the Moscow newscast.
• The routing slip is addressed to the Director of Management & Planning and the DS&T, with a note 'For the report to the DCI'.
• 1987-09-01: Anniversary of the 1969 coup, marked by a military parade and an evening speech by Qadhdhafi, broadcast on Libyan television
