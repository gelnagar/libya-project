LIBYA

Al-OQadhdhafi Receives Message From Kuwaiti Amir 
LD271352 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 
27 Jun 83 


[Text] The brother leader has received a message from Kuwaiti 
Amir Jabir al-Ahmad as-Sabah in reply to the message from the 
brother leader to Arab kings and presidents regarding the current 
Arab position and the massing of all resources to confront the 
Zionist death train. 


The Kuwaiti amir’s message says: 


1 do not want to add to the description of the current state of Arab 
affairs presented in your message to me. Our position as an Arab 
state, forming part of this great Arab homeland, is the categori- 
cal rejection of any agreement with the Zionist enemy, that the 
Arab cause is an indivisible whole and no Arab country has the 
right unilaterally to act or adopt a decision with regard to this 
cause, about which Arabs must collectively adopt a decision. 


JANA Comments on ‘Ambitions’ in Chad 
LD271507 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1425 GMT 27 Jun 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 27 Jun (JANA) — A responsible source at the 
Popular Bureau for Foreign Liaison has made the following 
comments on statements by the Egyptian regime on the situation 
in Chad: 


The internatonal community realizes that what is going on in 
Chad now is a domestic struggle for power between the rebel 
Hissein Habre and the forces of the legitimate government of 
national unity. The international community bears in mind the 
irresponsible role of the Egyptian regime and its appendage the 
Numayri regime in Sudan to enable the rebel Habre to take 
control of the situation in Ndjamena. 


The statements by the Egyptian regime are nothing but echoes 
of the fabricated accusations levelled by the Americans and the 
imperialist forces in Africa in an attempt to pave the way for their 
direct intervention in Chad. 


While denouncing once again such false accusations, the Social- 
ist Peoples Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah reaffirms its anxiety for 
the security and territorial integrity of Chad, and its determina- 
tion not to remain on the sidelines in the event of any foreign 
intervention in Chad. It reminds the international community of 
the fact that the Egyptian Armed Forces are practically occupy- 
ing Sudan against the will of its people. This Egyptian military 
presence in the territory of Sudan reflects the Egyptian expan- 
sionist ambitions, which may well involve Chad. This contradicts 
the claims by the Egyptian regime that it is anxious to guard the 
freedom of peoples and their territorial integrity.
