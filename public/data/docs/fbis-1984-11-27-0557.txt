LIBYA

Egyptian Situation ‘Exploded’ After School Shutdown 
LD261039 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 26 Nov 84 


[Text] Cairo 26 Nov (JANA) — Egypt's international situation 
has exploded since the president of the agent Egyptian regime, 


Husni Mubarak, ordered the shutdown of A!-Azhar University 
after it was stormed last night by police and Army forces. More 
than 300 students were arrested and taken in closed vehicles to 
unknown destinations. 


The international situation in Egypt appears to be more disturbed 
as a result of the tyrannic authorities’ repressive measures 
against the students of Al-Azhar yesterday when bloody battles 
raged from dawn until dusk. 


The police forces used armored trucks and vehicles and police 
dogs in storming the university compound and brutally repressed 
the students a few hours after a surprise visit by the president of 
the Egyptian regime, Husni Mubarak, to the university under 
intense police protection. 


The demonstrating students were denouncing Husni Mubarak 
for handing over the Egyptian Ra‘s Banas base to the American 
forces and for following a misleading and deceptive policy that 
increased the Egyptian people’s pains and tribulations. 


Dozens of police vehicles and armored cars and hundreds of 
central security men are currently stationed [at the university]. 
The police’s campaign of arrest and terror is being led by Interior 
Minister Ahmad Rushdi under strict instructions from the pres- 
ident of the Egyptian regime. Some neighboring areas and hotels 
close to the university compound have been turned into operation 

. centers in which plans for arrests, stormings, and repressions 
against the students are drawn. 


Eyewitnesses have stated that the present disturbed situation in 
Egypt resembles the situation that preceded the death of the 
traitor Al-Sadat to a great extent when he turned Egypt into a 
political detention camp in which he threw the most effective 
Egyptian forces, opposition people, students, lawyers, and work- 
ers and passed a series of terrorist laws that are still being 
implemented to the letter by Husni Mubarak, the most dis- 
graceful of which is the emergency law which took away the 
Egyptian people’s freedom and shackled them. 


The disgraceful deeds Husni Mubarak carried out against the 
angry students of al-Azhar University expose the essence of his 
policy which is based on deception and carrying out the orders of 
Washington and Tel Aviv. It also removed all credibility from 
his statements and postures which spew false democracy and 
protection of freedoms. The decision of Husni Mubarak’s regime 
to storm the Al-Azhar compound, shut down the university, 
arrest hundreds of students and teachers, burn some of its 
buildings, and kill some students, places Husni Mubarak’s 
regime in particular in front of the Egyptian people in its true 
nature as a repressive regime that did not produce anything new 
other than implementing predrawn and prepacked plans in 
Washington.
