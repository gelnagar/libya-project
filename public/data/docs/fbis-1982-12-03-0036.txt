LIBYA

TUNISIAN PRIME MINISTER MEETS WITH MINISTERS 


LD012020 Tripoli JANA in English 1846 GMT 1 Dec 82 


[Text] Tripoli, Safar 14, Dec 1, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Mohamed Mzali, prime 
minister of Tunisia, today met with Musa Abu Furaywah, secretary of the General People's 
Committee of Economy and Light Industry, and Fawzi al-Shakshuki, secretary of the 
General People's Committee of Planning. Also present was Juma's Fazzani, general 
commissioner of Jamahiriyah in Tunisia. 


Mr. Mzali expressed the Tunisian Government's keenness to implement the economic, social 
and cultural integration programme agreed upon last February by the revolution's leader, 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, and President Habib Bourguiba. He urged for a serious search for 
creating new practical steps to implement this programme. 


He expressed his conviction regarding the unity of the two brotherly countries and said: 
"This close cooperation between the two peoples is in line with the course of history."
