LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SPEECH MARKS SIXTH ANNIVERSARY 
LDO21849 Tripoli JANA in English 1517 GMT 2 Mar 83 


[Text] (Bayan al Awalbnhjumada) 18, March 2, Jamahiriyah News Agency -- Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi has declared that the muscle-flexing by foreign forces and threats to the 
free Libyan Arab people would only bolster this people's resoluteness, challenge and 
determination. 


He said, in a historic speech today marking the sixth anniversary of the proclamation 

of people's authority, that the show of force by the American forces, at the request 

of agents and hirelings, will not decrease our determination, challenge, and resoluteness 
however grave the possibilities are." 


He added: "Let America know that any interaction, peaceful and warlike, within the 
Arab homeland is an internal matter solely concerning the Arabs. No other side has 
the right to interfere in them. The Arab nation has the right to unite and remove the 
borders dividing it. This nation's unity does not differ from that of the USA or the 
Soviet republics or Yugoslavia or Italy's old unity." 


He said: ‘The struggle we are waging to remove the borders between us and Egypt and 
Sudan, and between any parts of the Arab homeland is ;recisely similar to the struggle 
waged by America to remove the borders between its states." 


He stressed: "We do not fear America, although we know that America is a super power 
and that there is no room for comparison between us and them. This is because we are 
practising our legitimate right on Earth and under the sun.” 


He added: "It is our right to live free over our land, and against American 
dictates.” 


The revolution leader affirmed, in his speech today, the Libyan Arab people's deter- 
mination to confront American aggression, however grave the possibilities are. He said, 
“we will march forward with challenge, determination and resoluteness to agitate for 
revolution in the Arab homeland until the Arab nation achieves victory and frees its 
will, against the dictates and desires of America, Zionism, and the agents.” 


He added, “America should study history and realize that the Arab nation's struggle 
for unity is similar to that waged by the American people in the past for unity." 


He went on, “the struggle of Americans then was not regarded as legitimate by the 
British, which it was so for the American fighters."
