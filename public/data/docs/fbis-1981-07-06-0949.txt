LIBYA

PDRY'S "ALI NASIR ARRIVES FOR VISIT 4 JUL 


LD042300 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2200 GMT 4 Jul 81 


[Text] Brother 'Ali Nasir Muhammad, chairman of the Presidium of the Supreme People's 
Council and president of the PDRY, arrived in Tripoli this evening on a visit to the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


The Yemeni president made a statement to a JANA correspondent in which he said: I am very 
happy to pay this visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah at the invitation 
of my brother Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of the Great 1 September Revolution, to 
study together the conditions and developments in the Arab and international arena, and 
particularly the sharpening of imperialist and Zionist conspiracies and movements which 

are aimed at the freedom and sovereignty of our Arab nations. 


He said: I am confident that the visit will strengthen our militant relations, which serve 
the interests of the two revolutions and the interests of our two peoples. It will enable 
us to discuss aspects of future promotion of our two countries’ relations. It will 
strengthen the bridges of militant contact between our two revolutions and the mobilization 
and mustering of our energy and efforts to consolidate the positions of the progressive 
forces in our Arab homeland and to constantly adopt a principled stand toward our brothers 
Syria, Lebanon and the Palestinian revolution, who are in the forefront against the Zionist 
enemy. At the same time we will work to strengthen the role of the Front of Steadfastness 
and Confrontation and expand the scope of its strategic alliance with the Arab and inter- 
national liberation movement and the socialist community. 


Al-Qadhdhafi Meeting 
LD060934 Tripoli JANA in English 0845 GMT 6 Jul 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 6 Jul (JANA)--Colonel Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, leader of Al-Fatah Revolution, 
and Mr ‘Ali Nasir Muhammad, president of the Presidium of the Supreme People's Council 

and president of the Democratic Republic of Yemen, both held a meeting in Tripoli last 
night. Also present were Staff Major ‘Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud, Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis 
Jabir, commander in chief of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces, and members of the delegation 
accompanying President 'Ali Nasir Muhammad. Part of the meeting was attended by Mr Taysir 
Qubba's, member of the P.F.L.P. Politburo, and Mr George Hawi, secretary general of the 
Lebanese Communist Party.

JABIR CONFERS WITH CHADIAN ARMED FORCES CHIEF 


LD041352 Tripoli JANA in English 0920 GMT 4 Jul 81 


[Text] Tripoli, 4 Jul (JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY)--The General Commander of the Armed Forces 
Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir last Thursday evening conferred with the Chadian Armed Forces 
chief of staff and his accompanying delegation. 


In a speech delivered by the Chadian official on a dinner given in his honour, he expressed 
gratitude for the Libyan Arab people's position vis-a-vis the Chadian people. He (?declared) 
the Libyan people stood beside us at the most difficult of times enabling us to attain free- 
dom, peace and security. 


He added: 'The Libyan people have proven their metal through their sacrifices and struggle 
beside us at a time when everyone forgot us.... We will not forget this and will work 
incessantly for further solidarity between the Libyan and Chadian people.’ 


JALLUD RETURNS FROM VISIT TO SFRY, USSR 2 JUL 
LD030830 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0115 GMT 3 Jul 81 


[Text] Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud yesterday concluded his cordial official visits 
to the USSR and Yugoslavia, which lasted for a week. 


During the visits, Jallud signed a protocol for cooperation in the scientific, technical, 
electrical and economic fields with Nikolay Tikhonov, Politburo member and chairman of the 
Soviet Council of Ministers. He also signed a protocol for cooperation in the scientific, 
technical, electrical, industrial and economic fields with the president of the Yugoslav 
Federal Executive Council. 


On his return to the Jamahiriyah Jallud was welcomed at the airport by Brig Gen Abu Bakr 
Yunis Jabir, the commander in chief of the armed forces.
