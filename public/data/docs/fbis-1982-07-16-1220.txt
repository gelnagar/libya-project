LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI SENDS MESSAGE TO LEADERS ON LEBANON 


LD152154 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 15 Jul 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 15 Jul (JANA) -- The brother leader of the revolution has sent a message 
to the world's presidents and kings, in which he said the Zionist military campaign to 
subjugate Lebanon and to eradicate the Palestinian issue is in truth a new terrorist 
operation that presents serious new “acts in human behavior and thought. The brother 
leader pointed out that this includes observation of a serious new precedent in inter- 
national behavior, which after today will make it possible for any army to cross its 
borders and occupy the capital of another country on the pretext of searching for its 
enemies to liquidate them. 


The brother leader added that the Zionist military invasion of Lebanon has revealed the 
basic moral collapse of the Israeli Zionist creed which seeks to build a racist and 
religious empire on Palestinian and Arab soil at the experse of the Arab existence. 


The brother leader explained that the destruction of Lebanon by the Zionist invasion, 
the burning and destruction of cities and villages in Lebanon around their inhabitants, 
happened as a result of the attitude of the United States which alone stood alongside 
and fostered the logic of aggression and used the policy of force in international 
security, and emerged as a partner in the planning and execution of this aggression. 


The brother leader noted that the U.S. administration provided the enemy with the most 

modern weapons of destruction produced by the U.S. war machine; it defended this enemy 

in international forums and tried through its agents in the area to exploit the results 
of the invasion to serve the plans of the U.S.-Israeli alliance in the Arab area and to 
make strategic gains in the international struggle. 


The brother leader drew attention to the danger threatening the area as a result of the 
U.S.-Zionist alliance, that expresses itself through military and political arrangements. 


At the end of his message, the brother leader asked that an end be put to the great 
power's complete alignment with Zionism, that recognition should be withdrawn from the 
Zionist state and that efforts should be made to expel this state from the United Nations 
because any cooperation with the racist and Zionist state will harm all internationally 
acknowledged human principles and values. At the same time, such cooperation would be 
considered hostile cooperation against the Arab nation. He also called for a halt to 
emigration to occupied Palestine, because this is seen as a contribution to invading the 
area, and for each state to repatriate its emigrants. He called for the need to speed 
up the establishment of the democratic Palestinian state and to support and back all the 
fighters for establishing such a state, because this is considered to be in accordance 
with a pressing international demand and to be a basic condition for establishing a 
lasting and just peace in the area. 


AL-QADHDHAFI INTERVIEW WITH ITALIAN NEWSPAPER 


LD160914 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 16 Jul 82 


[Text] The widely ciruclated Italian paper LA REPUBBLICA has conducted an interview 
with the brother leader Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi on current international issues and 
on the Zionist invasion of Lebanon. 


V. 16 Jul 82 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


The brother leader stressed that the Zionists have no right to invade Lebanese 
territories or to make arrangements between two Arab sides -- the Lebanese and the 
Palestinian. This is sheer aggression -- a great catastrophe -- and must be resisted. 
They should not be allowed to negotiate and regulate by force relations between Arab 
sides. The brother leader added that relations between the Palestinians and the Lebanese 
cannot possibly be determined under the Zionist occupation of Lebanon. If the world 
allows this, then it is the right of any state where people are working against it in 
another state, to take its military force, if it is capable of doing so, and invade 

and besiege that state and oust them by force. This is nothing more than flagrant 
aggression that must be resisted. 


Replying to a question about the reason for the Zionist invasion of Lebanon at this 
particular time, the leader replied: There is an agreement among the Saudi rulers, the 
Iraqi regime and the Palestinian right for such action at this time when the Iraqi regime 
is about to collapse. 


Regarding relations with Western Europe, the brother leader said: We want useful, equal 
and normal relations. This requires that the Europeans understand us and that we under- 
stand them. This cannot possibly happen except through direct contacts. At present, 
they understand us only through Zionist-controlled propaganda organs. The brother leader 
pointed out that the task of Zionism is to create hostility between the Arabs and Europe 
and to undermine relations in its own interest. The same applies to U.S. policy, which 
is aimed at undermining relations between Europe and the Arabs and at exploiting this for 
its own interest. 


Replying to a question about a possible solution to the situation in Lebanon and in the 
Middle East, the brother leader replied: In Lebanon, the aggressing Zionist forces must 
be fought until they withdraw. As for a solution to the Middle East problem, it lies 

in disarming the Zionists and returning those who came to Palestine after 1948 to the 
homeland from which they came and establishing a democratic Palestinian state. 


The brother leader added that the Zionists are threatening world peace and that they 

are evil on earth. The Palestinian people are the victims of the Zionist aggression 

and the Arab nation is a victim of the Zionist aggression, which is backed by the United 
States. 


With regard to the damages caused the Libyan Arab people as a result of the Italian 
colonization, the brother leader said: The Libyan people must be compensated for the 
damages they sustained as a result of the Italian colonization. 


Regarding the oil embargo the U.S. administration has imposed on the Libyan people, 

he said America decides relations in its own interest. From the material of view, our 
sole aim is to find and develop a substitute for oil and to extract it sparingly so 

it will last as long as possible. The Libyan people -—- this people who have become 
free for the first time without a government -- want freedom. 


With regard to the jamahiri [of the masses] regime, the brother leader said it is a 

new regime and the beginning of a new era, just like the French Revolution which ushered 
in the era of the republics. Now begins the era of the popular masses and Libya is the 
cornerstone of this new era. The jamahiri regime is the common denominator for all 

the peoples of the world. Current events show that the world masses are marching toward 
power and want to establish the jamahiri regime.
