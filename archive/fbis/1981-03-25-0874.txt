LIBYA

ARAB LEAGUE CALLED ON TO EXPEL SUDAN REGIME 


LD251304 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 25 Mar 8] 


[Text] The Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah submitted a memorandum to the 

ordinary session of the Council of the Arab League, currently being held in Tunis at the 
foreign minister level, calling for the expulsion of the Sudanese regime from the Arab 

League because it restored diplomatic relations with the Egyptian regime. This is considered 
a violation of the resolutions of the Badhdad summit conference and--because of the 

Sudanese regime's allying itself with the United States and granting it military bases on 
Sudan's territory--it is a matter which exposes the Arab nation and its freedom to danger. 


RADIO REPORTS SUDANESE OPPOSITION STATEMENT 


LD241450 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 24 Mar 81 


[Text] The Sudanese opposition reported that Numayri has unveiled his true face and has 
shown it with complete arrogance and insolence as a lackey of the American camp. He even 
kneels humbly in front of fallen agents such as As-Sadat. In a press statement issued 
yesterday in London, the Sudanese opposition pointed out that Numayri often denied any 
relationship with colonialism and pretended to sever relations with the Egyptian president 
while at the same time he indulged and threw himself in the lap of colonialism. Arab 
monies and assistance have evaporated into the quagmires of his entourage while the 
Sudanese people became more miserable, hungrier and more destitute, with lines stretching 
for miles to buy a loaf of bread or a pinch of sugar. 


In its statement, the Sudanese opposition said the way the Sudanese people are living 

is not strange; there is no economy in the Sudan. Numayri's authority is that of a despot 
indulging in problems which are not the concern of the Sudanese citizen. The statement 
stressed that there is no solution other than the overthrow of this rotten regime which 
spins rumors to find a cover to embrace the American forces on Sudanese territory. 


In its statement the Sudanese opposition reviewed the justifications offered by Numayri 


to restore relations with the regime of the Egyptian president and to throw himself into 
the lap of American colonialism. In this respect it called on the Arab nation to kick 


this hypocrite out of its ranks to join all the fallen hypocrites and Zionists.
