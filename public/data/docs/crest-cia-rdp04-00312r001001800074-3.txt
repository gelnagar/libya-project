Summary (AI): A 1981 press article (reproduced in the CIA reading room) describing American former intelligence and Green Beret personnel who allegedly trained Libyan terrorists and supplied explosives to Qaddafi's regime from 1976 to 1979. It names Edwin Wilson, Frank Terpil and associates and describes a Tripoli-based operation at a palace complex and a beachfront hotel.
• The article says more than 20 Americans, including five former CIA employees, were allegedly connected to the Libyan operation, with perhaps as many as 60 Americans overall according to State Department sources.
• Explosives and electronic equipment shipped from J.S. Brower and Associates in California were packed in black 50-gallon drums marked Industrial Solvent.

• The article says Libyan commandos were believed responsible for the previous year's execution of Libyan exiles in Europe.
• 1976-09: First American recruits for the Libyan operation informed the CIA about the operation in September 1976, according to the article; the operation reportedly continued until late December 1979.
• 1976-04: In spring 1976 Terpil and Wilson traveled to Pomona, California, to arrange electronic equipment and explosives shipments for Libya; the first shipment of explosives reportedly went to Tripoli in drums marked Industrial Solvent.
