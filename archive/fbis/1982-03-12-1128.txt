LIBYA

TRIPOLI COMMENTS ON U.S. BAN ON LIBYAN OIL 


LD112240 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2115 GMT 11 Mar 82 
{[Unattributed commentary] 


[Excerpts] The U.S. administration has imposed a total ban on its imports of Libyan oil. 
It has also ordered a ban on all its exports of oil technological equipment to the 
jamahiriyah. These measures were made public at a press conference held by the spokesman 
of the U.S. State Department yesterday afternoon. Justifying this decision by the U.S. 
administration, the spokesman alleged that the jamahiriyah is spreading terrorism 
throughout the world and is taking hostile stands toward those regimes allied with the 
United States, such as Somalia and Sudan. 


The anti-jamahiriyah U.S. administration decision has aroused numerous reactions. 
Secretary General of OAPEC ‘Ali 'Atiqah denounced the U.S. administration's decision to 
stop imports of oil from the jamahiriyah and has called for facing up to the U.S. deci- 
sion and denouncing it. He was speaking in a statement to the QATARI NEWS AGENCY. He 
said this matter should not be allowed to pass easily because Libyan oil is an indivisible 
part of Arab oil and Libya is an indivisible pit of the Arab nation. 


We do not want here to relate the serial of U.S. aggression against the Libyan people and 
their great revolution, since the start of their great revolution on 1 September 1969. 

It suffices, however, to point to the preludes to the present decision. On 17 February 
1982, U.S. Secretary of State Alexander Haig revealed the filthy terroristic policy of 
the United States at a press conference in Morocco. He expressed his dismay at the 
growing role played by the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah in the cause of 
freedom and in support for the peoples struggling for freedom. 


Political observers believe that these anti-jamahiriyah measures by the U.S. administra- 
tion are no more than a prelude to further terroristic aggressive measures that might 
include armed aggression against the Libyan Arab people and their revolution. 


The brother leader of the revolution described the attitude of the U.S. administration 
toward Libyan Arab oil and its plotting with the Saudi agent regime as a desperate U.S. 
attempt to subdue the peoples, to undermine the Palestine question and to impose a 
solution on the Palestinians and deprive Libya of money and arms to support the Palestine 
revolution. He affirmed that the Libyan Arab people will not submit to Arab reaction 
or the U.S. administration. He said it remains for our Arab nation's masses to face up 
to U.S. aggression by escalating their struggle against it, against its cancerous 
Zionist entity in our region and against Arab reaction. This is the road which the 
masses of our Arab nation, led by their living revolutionary forces, should follow 
against the U.S. Zionist aggression which is supported by Arab reaction, now that all 
masks covering the ugly face of the U.S. administration have been shed -- the U.S. 
administration whose chief, Reagan, said Israel was a strategic treasure for the United 
States in the Arab region.

COMMENTARY ATTACKS 'RECKLESS' U.S. DECISION 


LD120200 Trupoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0015 GMT 12 Mar 82 
(Unattributed commentary] 


[Text] The Reagan administration's reckless and frivolous decision to impose a total ban 

on its imports of Libyan oil and on the exportation of U.S. oil equipment and technology to 
the jamahiriyah under false pretexts and claims that arouse nothing but disgust and loathing, 
such as the charge that the jamahiriyah aids terrorism, has met with nothing more than 
mockery from the Libyan people and has come as no surprise. The U.S. animosity toward the 
jamahiriyah people has gone way beyond such economic pressure measures with the direct 
military clash in the Gulf of Sidra. Yet it did not make the Libyan people bat an eye or 
shake with fear under the pressure of the enormous military and material might of U.S. 
imperialism. 


For the last 2 years, the Libyan Arab people had taken all possibilities of U.S. aggression 
into consideration and have been expecting clashes at every moment while preparing them- 
selves to face up to the United States in all political, economic and military fields, 
mobilizing all their capabilities and resources for the purpose, and affirming total mass 
mobilization, thus epitomizing the principle of the armed people; and also while developing 
their economic capabilities and potentials to create new economic subsitutes as an alternative 
for oil. They have done all this out of their sure conviction that the values, ideals and 
principles put forward by the jamahiriyah and the fact that it has been acting as an inter- 
national platform for fighting imperialism, Zionism, reaction and racism were bound one 

day to clash with the political line propounded by terrorist Reagan which aims at crushing 
the peoples' freedoms, combat‘ ing the liberation and revolution movements and bolstering 
the agent regimes that are hostile to their peoples and that are subservient to U.S. 
imperialism. 


Thus our people have not been surprised by this U.S. oil ban and the U.S. economic war. 

The United States is wrong if it imagines that its measures will make our people kneel after 
starving them. First, our people are fully confident in their economic and military 
capabilities which will enable them to stand fast. And second and foremost, our people 

are fully confident of their enormous morale capabilities and are determined to confront 
U.S. imperialism even if tht means the total annihilation of the entire Libyan people. 


The real reasons for this declared U.S. war against the jamahiriyah people do not lie in 

the allegations made by U.S. imperialism -- allegations that have become exposed and well- 
known to world public opinion, such as the allegation that Libya suports terrorism. Bruno 
Kreisky yesterday said that there was no evidence to corroborate the truth of these allega- 
tions. The truth of the matter is that U.S. imperialism wants our people to lose their 
independence and to retreat from their ideological and principled choices and to bow their 
head and kneel in front of the United States. But the Libyans would rather die than do 

any such thing. 


Our people are unlike the reactionary regimes which open their lands and skies to the 

U.S. forces. Here in the jamahiriyah we have authority of the people who decide matters 
in the open without any representation or proxy. We have no ruler or government to decide 
to bow humbly before the United States for the sake of safeguarding their own narrow 
selfish interests. 


Because of our people's insistence on maintaining their independence and adhering to their 
political, social and economic ideological choices and because of their refusal to kneel 
before the United States, U.S. imperialism has been taking all these provocative, hostile 
measures against our people.
