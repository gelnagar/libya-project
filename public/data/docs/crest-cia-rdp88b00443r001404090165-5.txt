Summary (AI): A September 1983 CIA assessment on the Lebanon fighting in the Shuf and Alayh districts says Libyan and Iranian fighters, along with Palestinian forces and Syrian support, backed the Druze. It describes Libyan fighters reinforcing a Druze position and Libyan financial help to Druze leader Junblatt. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• Assessment dated 12 September 1983 on participation of Syrian, Palestinian, Iranian and Libyan forces in Lebanon fighting.
• Libyan and Iranian participation was described as probable or reported, not confirmed; the assessment estimates no more than 1,000 Palestinian and likely Libyan and Iranian fighters.
• Libyan fighters were moving to reinforce a Druze position, probably in the contested Alayh area.
• A small number of Iranian forces with armored vehicles reportedly deployed in Alayh and the Shuf.
• 1983-09: Druze fighting with Palestinian, Libyan and Iranian fighters supporting attacks on Druze targets such as Bhamdun and Sug al Gharb.
