Summary (AI): A 1947 CIA intelligence report says the French had established themselves in the Fezzan (southern Libya) and would likely push to incorporate it into Algeria, with arguments based on conquest, geography, ethnicity, and British colonial policy. It also says France may trade support for Italy's return to Libya in exchange for keeping the Fezzan. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• French arguments for retaining the Fezzan: right of conquest, geographical, ethnic, and British colonial policy with Arab desire to be administered by the French.
• France may advocate Italy's restored control over Libya in exchange for Italy allowing France to keep the Fezzan.
