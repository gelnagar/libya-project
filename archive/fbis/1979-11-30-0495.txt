LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI CALLS FOR HEIGHIENED ARMED STRUGGLE 
LD300042 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 2315 GMT 29 Nov 79 LD 


[Summary] This evening Colonel al-Qadhdhafi attended a big popular rally in Tripoli 

on the occasion of second anniversary of the world day of solidarity with the Arab 

people of Palestine, Addressing the rally, he said that the Palestinian revolution should 
not allow any restrictions to be placed upon it and should observe no law other than 

that of the vevolution, 


"He said that fidati action should be launched from all Arab fronts, It should proceed 
from Libya toward Palestine, and eventhrough Egypt, from the Sudan through the Red Sea; 
from Saudi Arabia, which has direct borders with the occupied land; from Jordan; from 
Syria through the Golan; and from Lebanon," 


He urged the Palestinian masses to smash all obstacles standing in the path of fidali 
action and called on those Palestinians in Libya to form revolutionary committees, popular 
congresses, a general Palestinian congress and a popular Palestinian leadership. He 

also called on the various Palestinian fronts to merge under the leadership of a single 
Palestinian popular leadership. 


"He stressed that, while not belittling the importance of the outstanding sacrifices 
made inside the occupied territory which occur daily, he would like to call on the 
Palestinian organizations outside the occupied land to threaten navigation in the Bab El 
Mandeb and the Suez Canal, through which come the enemy!s weapons and goods. He said 
the Arab Suez Canal must ke sabotaged because it is now in the possession of the regime 
of the treacherous As-Sadat and has become the life artery which feeds the enemy, 
whereas it used to be closed to the enemy during the time of 'Abd an-Nasir. 


"He said this would be a legitimate action in the eyes of the world and one the Palestinians 
would be entitled to undertake, because the Suez Canal has been used against the cause of 
the Palestinian people and against their [word indistinct] and existence, He asked, 

Why are the Palestinians failing to cut off this artery which is nourishing the body of 

the enemy after the departure 'Abd an-Nasir? He explained that the traitorous Egyptian 
president, having tied his fate to that of his imperialist masters and having become more 
Israeli than the Israelis themselves, cannot issue an order to stop the enemy's civil 

and military ships which strike the Lebanese and Pa@lestinians at Tyre and Sidon and freely 
move fiom the Red Sea to the Mediterranean, 


"Concerning the use of Arab oil, he stressed that if it was not used to serve the cause 

of Palestine, to destroy the enemy and to deter the enemy's allies, it should be sabotaged 
also because Arab oil would be worthless if it merely served the interests of the enemy 
and his allies and was used to fuel the very aircraft that bomb Palestinian camps." 


He also emphasized that dependence on passive political action alone would not bring the 
day of liberation closer and thus return would become a delusion, 


"He warned the Palestine revolution against the danger of being influenced by the 
deteriorating state of Arab affairs so that the revolution will not lose its effective- 
ness or be turned into a government like other Arab governments. He pointed out that the 
Palestine revolution, during the latest Arab summit, had to go along with the Arab 
reality and accept at least not to undertake fida!li activities across Lebanese borders,

"Addressing the Palestinian masses, he said: If you complied with Arab reality, then the 
Palestine revolution would fail and the Zionist occupation of Palestine would be entrenched. 
The leader of the Great 1 September revolution called on them not to pay any attention to 
any Arab borders, Arab governments, Arab decisions or Arab summits."
