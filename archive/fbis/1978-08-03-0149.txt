LIBYA

JALLUD RECEIVES CHAD LIBERATION FRONT LEADER 
LDO21330Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 2 Aug 78 LD 
[Text] Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud received in his office at noon today 
Chairman of Chad National Liberation Front Goukouni Quedesi. The meeting was attended 


by Engineer ‘Abdullah as-Salih, secretary of industry. 


JALLUD DEPARTS FOR OFFICIAL VISIT TO PRC 


LDO22352Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 2 Aug 78 LD 


[Excerpt] Brother Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Jallud left tonight for Peking on an 

official visit lasting several days at the invitation of the Chinese Government. 

Staff Major Jallud will be accompanied during the visit by an economic delegation 

and the foreign, electricity and housing secretaries. 

MALDIVIAN FOREIGN MINISTER ARRIVES IN TRIPOLI 

LDO21346Y Tripoli JANA in English 0916 GMT 2 Aug 78 LD 

{Text}? Tripoli, August 2, JANA--Mr Fathulla Jameel, foreign minister of the Republic 

of Maldives, arrived at Tripoli international airport yesterday afternoon on a visit 

to the Jamahiriyah to last several days. 

The visiting minister was received by an under-secretary at the Foreign Secretariat.
