LIBYA

ANTILANDING MANEUVER PART OF ANNIVERSARY CELEBRATIONS 


LD101440 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 10 Jun 80 


[Text] Within the framework of our people!s celebrations of the 10th anhiversary of 
evicting U.S. bases from the homeland, the Libyan Arab Armed Forces carried out a 
maneuver this morning with live ammunition in the Gulf of Sirte in the Mediterranean 
Sea. The navy, air defense and land forces took part in the maneuver, The maneuver 
plan was formulated on the bagis of confronting a sea and air landing by a projected 
aggressor at an area of the Libyan coast. The forces taking part in the maneuver were 
able to destroy totally the projected landing operation, The bombers hit their targets 
with accuracy along the coast, the naval units also completely destroyed their targets 
in the open sea.

ARAB PEOPLE'S CONGRESS URGES SUPPORT FOR PFLO 
LD102212 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1730 GMT 10 Jun 80 


(Text ] Tripoli--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress, in a statement 
it issued here today on the occasion of the 15th ammiversary of the outbreak of the 
Omani revolution, has called for the need to back amd support the Omani revolution in 
its struggle for the downfall of the regime of the agent Qabus who has turned Omani 
territories into a large base for the United States, 


In its statement, the Permanent Secretariat said that the agent Qabus is defying the 
Arab nation and is adding a new betrayal to his record--which is full of betrayal--by 
Signing a new agreement with U.S. imperialism, the documents of which were exchanged 
on 5 June in Muscat and which stipulate the granting of new facilities to the U.S. for- 
ces at all the installations. 


The statement stressed that the U.S. presence in Oman does not so much threaten the 
Security and safety of the Gulf region as expose international security and peace to 
danger. The statement pointed out that the concentration of U.S. military forces in 
the Arabian Sea and in the Gulf of Oman and which are using the territories of Oman 

and its territorial waters as staging bases, are, in addition to being a force for the 
suppression of and a threat to the national revolutionary forces in the region, an 
indivisible part of the U.S.-Zionist plan directed against the Arab nation, and of which 
the tripartite Camp David constitutes a spearhead, 


The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress called in its statement for the 
breaking off of political and economic relations with the regime of the agent Qabus 

and to work for its expulsion from the Arab League and the application against it of all 
sanctions that have been taken against the regime of the Egyptian president. 


It also called for intensified military, political and material backing for the Popular 
Front for the Liberation of Oman so that it can topple the agent regime in Muscat and 
wrest independence for our Omani people.
