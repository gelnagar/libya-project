AL-JAMAHIRIYAH COMMENTS ON EXECUTIONS IN EGYPT 


PM210915 Tripoli AL-JAMAHIRIYAH in Arabic 16 Apr 82 p 2 
{Editorial: "Arabs Executed Al-Islambuli"] 


[Excerpts] Shame on us for failing to save the lives of Kahlid al-Islambuli and his four 
comrades. Khalid al-Islambuli simply fulfilled the general popular will. It would have 
been natural if he had been killed at the time of the military parade, but he escaped death 
then and we have not given him the opportunity to live. We are, therefore, partners in the 
crime of his murder. 


Khalid was a young Egyptian who saw, heard and felt that his people rejected the United 

States and Zionism and that his rejection resulted in the imprisonment and torture of 
thousands who struggled in defense of the homeland's glory and the nation's honor. When 

he saw all Egypt being sent to detention camps in September 1982, from Siraj ad-Din Pasha 

to ‘Umar at-Talmasani to Pope Shanudah, he must have wondered, with alarm: What next? 

He saw the head of the regime before him so he cut that head off. That was the ultimate 

act of heroism by an Egyptian Arab Muslim officer. He left it to us to complete the remaining 
acts of heroism. 


Instead of completing his great deed, we began to raise despicable questions while at the 
same time "enjoying" the departure of As-Sadat. 


Wasn't it a U.S. plan to get rid of the man who had burned himself out? Why weren't the 
other parties to the plot killed? These are questions propagated by the U.S., Israeli, 
Egyptian and perhaps Arab intelligence to undermine the heroism of the martyr and to cast 
doubt on our will and our resolve. 


Have the Camp David agreements been abrogated? Have the decisions of absolute subservience 
to the West, better known as the open-door laws, been cancelled? Have the laws restricting 
freedom been abolished? No, on the contrary, much more has been added to them. Thousands 
more have been detained. A state of emergency has been declared. More declarations have 
been made, stressing that the Camp David agreement and the "peace treaty" are here to stay, 
as if they are facts of nature. The martyrdom of Khalid al-Islambuli and his comrades 
provide bloody proof that some things have yet to fall. 


The martyrdom of Al-Islambuli and his comrades has given the Americans and the Zionists 
bloody proof that the downfall of the symbol does not mean final collapse and it has told 
the Arabs that they can only address themselves to Camp David. It gave the Egyptians 

a sense of guilt which will stay with them for generations. 


Unless the dreamers come out of their deep sleep before it is too late, the bload of 
Al-Islambuli and his comrades will not be forgotten. Unless we generate their kind of 
blood in our veins and complete the march, vengeance will be wreaked on us all.

PAPER CALLS SAUDIS ‘REAL ENEMY OF ARAB CAUSE' 


LD171550 Tripoli JANA in English 1432 GMT 17 Apr 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 17 Apr (JANA) -- The Libyan paper AL-MUNTIJUN published here today said 
the escalating revolution of the Arab citizens of the occupied lands has embarrassed the 
royal Saudi family, proponents of laxity and capitulation. 


In an article on the Saudi-rulers' call for a strike, it said that by exposing themselves 
through revolutionary action to the gunfire of the Israeli Army, the inhabitants of the 
occupied lands have uncovered the suspect role of the Saudi princes, allies of the U.S. 
administration. 


It said: "The revolt of our Palestinian Arab people will gain victory over the enemies 

of life and the Saudi princes because they are the real enemies of the Arab cause and 

of religion, morals and values.... The Palestinian revolution should continue because its 
flame shall liquidate all Zionist, imperialist and Saudi enemies...." 


PILGRIMS' ARREST SHOWS 'DEVIATION' OF AL SA'UDS 


LD171358 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 17 Apr 82 


[Text] On 5 November 1980, Saudi Arabia authorities arrested a Libyan and an Egyptian 
who were attending pilgrimage rites, despite the guaranteed conditions of security and 
safety for every pilgrim in the holy land. The Saudi authorities attacked the mass of 
pilgrims and arrested Libyan citizen Ali Mansur Sa'd Abu Turkiyah and Egyptian citizen 
Abd ar-Rahman ‘Awad Attiyah. They are still in custody despite all efforts exerted by 
the general secretary of the Islamic Conference Organization for their release or to find 
the reason for their arrest. The Saudi authorities imposed a ban on visits to the two 
men. 


This state of affairs is regarded as an example of the deviation of the Al Sa'ud family 
from Islamic jurisprudence, which confirms that this family's practices are based on 
repression and blackmail and have gone against God's decree on carrying out the pilgrimage, 
which demands that it should be a safe occasion during which Muslims from all over the 
world can meet each other and consult about issues of interest to Muslims and Islam. The 
unfortunate situation created by the conditions under which the pilgrimage rites are 
carried out has prompted the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah always to demand 
that the holy places in the Arabian Peninsula should not be subject to any side which 
could claim guardianship and authority over them because the tyrannical practices of the 
Al Sa'ud family have proved that it deviated from the path of truth. The holy Koran 
frankly states that God made the Mecca holy shrine a place of refuge and sanctuary for 
the people.
