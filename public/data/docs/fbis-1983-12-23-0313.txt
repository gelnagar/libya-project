LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Condemns Mubarak-‘Arafat Meeting 
LD231028 Tripoli JANA in English 0838 GMT 23 Dec 83 


(Text] Tripoli, Rabi al Awal 18, Dec 23, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY —- The leader of the revolution condemned 
Yasir ‘Arafat's meeting with the president of the Egyptian 
regime and described it as provoking...[JANA ellipsis] He further 
explained that Egypt after recognising the enemy was black- 
listed and boycotted till it has withdrawn its recognition. 


The leader went on saying that the problem between us and the 
Zionist enemy is the issue of Palestine. If ‘Arafat, the defender 
of the cause, meets with the Egyptian president who has recog- 
nised what is called Israel, then everything has been turned 
upside down. 


The leader's condemnation came in a speech delivered yesterday 
evening to the women’s gathering at the basic people’s conference 
at central Tripoli.
