JORDANIAN MINISTER OF STATE ARRIVES 5 JANUARY 
LD051634 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1415 GMT 5 Jan 80 LD 
(Text ] Tripoli--Jordanian ‘Minister of State Hasan Ibrahim arrived in Tripoli this 
afternoon on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. He was welcomed at the airport by Foreign 
Secretary 'Ali at-Turayki, the Jordanian ambassador and others, 

Meets Jallud 

LD062240 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 6 Jan 80 LD 


{Text ] Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud this morning received Hasan Ibrahim, the 
Jordanian minister of state for foreign affairs, who is now on a visit to the Jamahiriyah, 


Following the meeting, which was attended by Foreign Secretary at-Turayki, Hasan Ibrahim 
made a statement to JANA in which he affirmed that the meeting was very fruitful, He 
said: I have delivered to brother Jallud a message to the brother commander of the 
revolution from his brother King Husayn, 


He also said that a review was made of the latest developments in the Arab situation and 
matters of interest to the Arab nition and matters of common interest to the two countries. 


Dr ‘Ali at-Turayki this morning received the Jordanian minister of state for foreign 
affairs and they reviewed together the situation in the Arab region and cooperation 
between the Jamahiriyah and Jordan, Also discussed were matters of interest to both 
countries, 


The meet ing was attended by head of the Arab desk at the Foreign Secretariat and the 
Jordanian ambassador to the Jamahiriyah,. 


JANA EDITOR COMMENTS ON SPEECH BY FATAH'S SALAH KHALAF 


LD081056 Tripoli JANA in English 0800 GMT 8 Jan 80 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, January 8, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Salah Khalaf known as Abu Iyad, 
a (?PLO) leader of Fatah movement, has launched a rude attack against the Libyan Arab 
people and the leadership of the Great 1st of September Revolution, 


Vv. 8 Jan 80 I2 NORTH AFRICA 


In a speech made in Beirut yesterday on the occasion of the martyrs day and in the presence 
of Yasir ‘Arafat, Abu Iyad denied the financial and military support offered by the 
Jamahiriyah people andtheir historic leadership to Fatah movement. Commenting on this 
attack, JANA political editor said that it is not in the interests of any loyal freedom 
fighter eager to actually liberate his homeland and not merely talk about it to 
orchestrate a battle with a progressive revolutionary state such as Libya, a genuine ally 
of the Palestinian resistance, The editor went on "the creation by Fatah in particular 
of a battle with the Jamahiyriyah can only indicate a move towards the stable David 
parties, the defeated Egyptian president himself has paved the way to join stable David 
by creating a battle with Libya in order to cover up the truth about his capitulation 

to the enemy under the auspices of the United States. This move has led to his isolation 
from the Arab nation and the establishment of alternative ties with (Israel) and American 
imperialism." 


The editor affirmed that Fatah's fabrication of battles against progressive revolutionary 
countries indicates a new trend through which this movement wants to reach an alliance 
which began on the path of the defeated Egyptian president. [Few words indistinct ] 
struggle and not to diplomacy. The editor went on to say that Fatah movement should 
remember that the Libyan military and material aid which it denies today was the basic 
factor for the survival of this movement, Fatah's force was built by the Libyan aid 

and Fatah's leaders know this. 


In conclusion JANA editor pointed to the resolution of the basic people's congresses 

to break off relations of the Libyan people with this movement. He said this resolution 
stems from the eagerness of the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiyriyah to see the 
Palestinian revolution firm in its goals and means represented in the liberation of all 
the usurped lands through an armed struggle only.
