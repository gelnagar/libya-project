Summary (AI): A January 1988 FBIS translation of a Tripoli Television broadcast in which Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi addresses students in Tripoli on 21 January 1988, urging them to join revolutionary committees and reject reliance on others, and warning against U.S. influence over the Gulf of Sidra and Aozou. Most of the document covers Egypt, Yemen and other Arab news unrelated to Libya.
• Speech delivered at a 21 January 1988 meeting with students in Tripoli, broadcast by Tripoli Television Service (LD212336, 2040 GMT).
• Al-Qadhdhafi said the U.S. had expelled Libyan students studying the atom.
• He referred to the Gulf of Sidra and Aozou as Libyan territory the U.S. and others wanted to control.
• He cited the 11 June anniversary of the evacuation of U.S. bases as Eviction Day.
• The speech mentioned U.S. bases formerly at Al-Mallahah, Al-Watiyah, Tajura, Sidra, and Bir al-Usta Milad.
• 1988-01-21: Al-Qadhdhafi addressed a meeting of students in Tripoli, calling for revolutionary committee membership and mobilization, and criticizing U.S. policy and the Aozou dispute with Chad.
