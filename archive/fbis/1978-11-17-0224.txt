[Text] Washington, Nov 16 (MAP)--"President as-Sadat is still leyal to the Rabat 

peace summit,” King Hassan II of Morocco told Wednesday here at the National Press Club, 
"The Camp David framework" cannot re-establish peace in the Middle East without the 
participation of Jordan, Saudi Arabia, the maximum ef Arab countries, and especially 

the Palestinians, the king added, 


As for the Arab economic boycott of Egypt, Hassan IIT said: "It is more an intellectual 
position than an effective one, for the Arab world cannot live in war against Egypt." 


When asked to advise the shah of Iran, the king said: "Elections will enable him (the 
shah) to judge the qualities of his adversaries". 


On Moroccan-Algerian relations Hassan compared Alguria to a noisy apartment neighbor, 
held Algiers responsible for the crisis in north west Africa. The Moroccan military 
situation in the Sahara "is good", and "never" would a Saharan confederation be allowed, 
he said. However, “our friends should give us arms fer self-defense", the king added. 


As for Ceuta and Mellila, still claimed by Morocco, Hassan said he aims to negotiate 
their return with Spain in the framework of peace, goed neighbourhood, friendship and 
understanding,
