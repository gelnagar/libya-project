• As of 2030 hours local on 12 September 2012, no known security incidents or clashes in Tripoli affected U.S. or other foreign missions.
• A vehicle with a black flag was detained around the U.S. Embassy (NFI).
• National Guard patrols of roads to and from embassies and vital government buildings ran only until early morning 12 September 2012.
• 2012-09-11: Attack against the U.S. Consulate in Benghazi; separate U.S. facility also attacked.
• 2012-09-12: Demonstrations in support of the attacks in Benghazi.
