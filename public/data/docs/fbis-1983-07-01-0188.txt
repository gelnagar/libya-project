LIBYA

JANA on US. ‘Sorties’ Near Libyan Airspace 
LD301732 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1700 GMT 30 Jun 83 


[Text] Tripoli 30 Jun (JANA) — Yesterday American military 
aircraft carried out provocative sorties near the Libyan airspace. 
The American aircraft took off from the American aircraft 
carrier Eisenhower which is now carrying out provocative terror- 
ist movements opposite the Libyan shores. 


The American CBS television network said the American Air 
Force is carrying out special activity opposite the Libyan shores. 
Martin, the CBS correspondent, said in his dispatch to the 
network that the aim of Washington’s new defiance is to flex its 
muscle. 


TASS said that the American Pentagon is carrying out military 
blackmail against the Jamahiriyah accompanied by fabricated 
statements of the American State Department accusing Libya of 
so-called itnerference in the affairs of Chad. 


RFL on Intercept Incident 
NC301755 (Clandestine) Radio Free Lebanon in English 
1747 GMT 30 June 83 


[Text] We start our news tonight with a report just in saying that 
planes from the U.S. aircraft carrier Eisenhower in the Mediter- 
ranean have intercepted two Libyan planes which tried to 
approach their mother ship. Further details are not available. 


JANA: US.-Egyptian Threats 
LDO10928 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0800 GMT | Jul 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 1 Jul (JANA) — The Reagan administration is 
intensifying the tone of its provocations and intimidation against 
the Libyan people. This administration cannot think of anything 
else other than to threaten Libya and provoke it with the utmost 
hostility, prompted by a criminally evil psychology. 


The U.S. Administration has once again revealed its intention of 
carrying out provocative actions against the Libyans when U.S. 
military aircraft took off from the U.S. aircraft carrier Eisen- 
hower 2 days ago and carried out provocative flights near the 
Libyan Arab airspace in the Gulf of Sidra. 


By such ugly actions it has proved to the world that it is an 
aggressive state plotting against a small people who rejected US. 
hegemony and insisted on playing an independent role in inter- 
national events for the freedom of peoples and the consolidation 
of the pillars of security and peace in the world. 


The Reagan administration is adopting stances of the utmost 
hostility toward Libya and views the stances of its agents and 
their ailing psychology as a lever to threaten Libya and to hinder 
its world role in asserting the freedom of the anti-imperialist 
peoples.
