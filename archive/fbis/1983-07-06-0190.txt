LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Interviewed on Lebanon, Chad 
LDO51501 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
0200 GMT 5 Jul 83 


{Interview with Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi by 
unidentified correspondent, given to Moroccan radio and televi- 
sion networks “prior to his departure from Morocco” — 
recorded} 


[Excerpts] [Correspondent] | would like to ask you about the 
current issues in the Arab arena — developments, events, and 
questions resulting from [words indistinct] withdrawal of the 
Zionist forces from Lebanon, and [words indistinct}. 


[Al-Qadhdhafi} Libya's attitude was clear in the talks we had 
during our visit to Morocco. When the capitulationist agreement 
was announced, angry popular demonstrations were staged 
throughout the Jamahiriyah. Libya considers the Lebanese- 
Israeli agreement as graver than the Stable David agreement 
signed by the defunct As-Sadat. This agreement totally robs 
Lebanon of its independence. Not only that, it does so to the [word 
indistinct] Zionism. By this agreement Lebanon becomes part of 
the Zionist land used to safeguard the Zionists and their security, 
and to strike against the Arab countries, particularly Syria. By 
this agreement, Lebanon has lost its independence. This 
agreement is rejected because it was imposed on Lebanon under 
the conditions of Israeli occupation. Hence, from a legal point of 
view, it is null and void. As you may recall, Brother King Hassan 
has said: This agreement has extremely serious consequences 
not for Lebanon alone but for the entire Arab nation as well. If 
affects the future of the entire Arab nation. Consequently, the 
entire Arab nation must meet and tackle the grave situation 
resulting from the agreement. 


Clearly, this agreement's provisions cancel out Palestine, for it 
says that Israel's borders extend to so and so. The agreement also 
cancels out the Palestinian people and affirms the Israeli people. 
It cancels the start of war between Lebanon and the Israelis. 
Hence southern Lebanon, about half of Lebanon, becomes a 
security zone in which Lebanon has no right to act except with 
the permission of the Israelis. Even if a Lebanese civilian aircraft 
wants to fly over the area, it first has to obtain the permission of 
the Israeli occupation authorities. It is total capitulation. 


I call on the popular masses in Morocco to express their support 
for the king’s point of view and for the king's position which says 
the agreement is very grave and will have serious consequences 
for the Arab nation. We would not be surprised to see demonstra- 
tions staged in Morocco as has happened in the Jamahiriyah 
when the masses symbolically set fire to the agreement.
