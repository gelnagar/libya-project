It has no specific places in or near Tripoli.
• Subcommittee majority concluded the $220,000 from Libya was a gift, not a loan.
• Billy Carter said $220,000 was part of a half-million dollar loan he was negotiating; he had told Lisker in June that $20,000 was a repayment of expenses from hosting a Libyan delegation in Atlanta in early 1979.
• Brzezinski asked Billy Carter to arrange a White House meeting with the Libyan diplomat on Nov. 27 about the American hostages in Iran.
• The subcommittee report to the full Senate was due by Oct. 4.
