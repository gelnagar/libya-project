Holds Talks 
LDO52214Y Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 5 Jul 78 LD 


{Text] The first session of official talks was held this morning between the Libyan and 
Afghan sides on the consolidation of cooperation between the two countries in the economic, 
cultural and technical spheres, 


The Libyan Arab side was led by brother Musa Abu Furaywah, secretary ol planning, and 
the Afghan side by Soltan Ali Keshtmand, minister of planning. 


At the start of the meeting brother Musa Abu Furaywah, secretary of planning, made a 
speech in which he welcomed the Afghan minister of planning and the accompanying dele- 
gation. He said: Your coming to the Jamahiriyah indicates the strength of the relations 
which link our two countries. He confirmed that the Jamahiriyah is doing its best to 
consolidate its relations with Islamic countries. The secretary of planning added: Our 
meeting today is considered an important meeting between the two revolutions in the 
Jamahiriyah and Afghanistan. The Libyan Jamahiriyah is considered the strong prop for the 
peoples struggling in the cause of their freedom. 


The Afghan minister of planning then made a speech in which he lauded the good relations 
which link the Jamahiriyah and Afghanistan. He said: These relations are based on firm 
religious foundations. The Afghan minister lauded the progress the September revolution 
has brought about in all spheres. He added: The revolution in Afghanistan is working 
hard, at the same stride as the 1 September revolution, to eliminate the relics of the 
former reactionary regime and to realize progress for the Afghan people, He pointed out 
that the visit would be an effective tool to strengthen our relations with the Jamahiriyah 
in the economic fields. 


JANA reports that during the first session of the talks a joint Libyan-Afghan committee 
was formed to study the aspects of cooperation between the two countries. 


CONGRESS SECRETARIAT ISSUES COMMUNIQUE ON AS-SADAT TRIAL 


LD051602Y¥ Tripoli JANA in English 1429 GMT 5 Jul 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, July 5, JANA--The Permanent Secretariat of the Arab People's Congress 
has issued a communique in which it explained the measures taken up till now to imple- 
ment the decisions of the Arab People's Congress which was held in Tripoli at the end 
of last year. The text of the communique is as follows: 


Vv. 6 Jul 78 NORTH AFRICA 


Pursuant of the decisions of the Arab People's Congress held in Tripoli, in the Socialist 
People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah from 5-8 December 1977, which was attended by more than 
two hundred political, popular and trade unionist organizations from all parts of the 
Arab world and representatives of Arabs abroad, and which took a number of decisions to 
deal with the political situations prevailing in the Arab area, such as: to confront 
the policy of capitulation in the face of the enemy, adopted by the Egyptian president; 
to denounce his visit to occupied Jerusalem with its consequent results; and to assign 
the Permanent Secretariat of the congress to put As-Sadat on trial for the crimes he has 
committed against the Egyptian people, the Arab nation-and the Palestinian cause, the 
Permanent Secretariat has taken charge to set up a pan-Arab tribunal for the Egyptian 
president. 


After several sessions the Permanent Secretariat had prepared the bill of indictment, 
formed the prosecution case, and prepared a list of witnesses. In its last meeting in 
Tripoli from 21-25 June 1978, the Permanent Secretariat decided to carry out the trial 
and: 


1. To hold an international press conference to which would be invited the Arab and 
international media in Damascus during the second half of July 1978 at a date to be de- 
cided in coordination with the Syrian side, in order to announce the indictment, 


2. That the court would start its sessions in Baghdad during the first half of August 
1978. 


This court, established by the Arab popular forces, will be concerned with trying the 
anti-progressive procedure of capitulation led by the agent of Zionism and imperialism, 
the Egyptian president. Therefore, all peace-loving, progressive democratic forces in 
the world are invited to support this trial, which will realize freedom of opinion, will 
place, as clear as daylight, in front of the Arab and international public opinion the 
crimes committed against the Arab nation, and will win back basic freedoms for the Arab 
individual in Egypt, who now faces campaigns of repression and the most dreadful methods 
of dictatorship.
