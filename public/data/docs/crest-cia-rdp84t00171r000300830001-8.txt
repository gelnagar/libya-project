Summary (AI): A 1983 National Photographic Interpretation Center briefing note reports that Al Jufrah airfield in central Libya, reportedly Soviet-assisted, has become operational and capable of handling advanced jet aircraft. It lies about 260 nautical miles southeast of Tripoli, so it is not a Tripoli site.
• GCI site and GCA site (one Eye and two Spot radar) newly occupied at Al Jufrah.
• 12 Foxbat-capable aircraft bunkers in the WNW fighter dispersal area; 12 medium bomber revetments in the bomber dispersal area.
• Two large aircraft hangars near the centre of the airfield complex nearing completion.
• Analysts said GCI sites are not previously identified at Libyan airfields but are common at Warsaw Pact airfields, indicating Soviet involvement.
• 1983-12: Al Jufrah airfield assessed as operational and capable of handling advanced jet aircraft; GCI and GCA radar sites, bunkers, hangars and missile storage under construction or completed.
