LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Receives Austria’s Kreisky 27 Dec 
LD280009 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
2IISGMT 27 Dec 83 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution today received the 
former Austrian chancellor, Bruno Kreisky, who is currently 
visiting the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamuahiriyah 


The former Austrian chancellor earlier in the evening attended 
part of the debates of the masses of the basic popular congress at 
Sug al-Jum’‘ah on issues raised during the current session 


Kreisky followed from a short distance the earnest character of 
these debates, which stems from the spirit of responsibility in 
making decisions, and which manifests the direct popular democ 
racy and affirms the power of the people
