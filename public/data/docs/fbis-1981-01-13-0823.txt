LIBYA

-RIPOLI REPORTS ACTIVITIES OF IRAQI FRONT 


LD121420 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 12 Jan 81 

[Text] Baghdad--The Iraqi National Islamic Liberation Front on 22 December attacked the 
premises of Al-Mawsil television station in Iraq, killing a number of guards and disrupt- 
ing transmission. They also captured two Egyptian officers with the rank of captain. 


The front also attacked the offices of the director of public security at Al-Kazimiyah, 
killing seven soldiers of the Iraqi regime. 


The forces of the Iraqi National Islamic Liberation Front have taken coutrol of Al-Hajj 
‘Imran district, which overlooks Kirkuk east of Al-Mawsil. Kirkuk is the oil center. 


PAPER ATTACKS FRENCH ‘IMPERIALIST INTERFERENCE’ 


LD121444 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 12 Jan 81 
[Press review] 


[Excerpts] Under the headline "Await Napoleon's Return," today's issue of AZ-ZAHF 
AL-AKHDAR writes: The freedom of Africa and the sanctity of its territories and indepen- 
dence and its right to exploit its own wealth and save itself “rom poverty and backward- 
ness are all sacred matters which, if violated, demand an immediate rush to carry arms 
and mobilize the African masses in the face of the flagrant imperialist interference. 


The imperialist powers tread upon all human values and international traditions and 
charters and even the most fundamental international norms of respect for the will of 
peoples and noninterference in their internal affairs. They have brandished their air and 
land weapons including French military aircraft, tanks, rockets and artillery in an overt 
military campaign aimed at swarming central Africa with the French military presence and 
turning it into a strong arsenal to be used as a springboard for aggression against the 
neighboring African peoples. 


The article urges the African revolutionary forces to arouse the African peoples’ concern 
for their security and independence and thus make them rush like a roaring torrent to 
check all forms of imperialist interference in the continent's affairs. 


Elsewhere, AZ-ZAHF AL-AKHDAR publishes an article on French military concentrations and 
interference in Africa. The article reiterates that the open French military interference 
in the internal affairs of the continent is not new. If one recalls the black dossier in 
Africa of French actions against the peoples of the African Continent, one can readily 
realize this fact. 


The writer asks: In the face of these truly agonizing events, we can rightly ask: What 
independence has remained for Africa? Does Africa belong to France or to the Africans?

MORE EMBASSIES CONVERTED INTO PEOPLE'S BUREAUS 


Sri Lanka Embassy 
BKO71415 Hong Kong AFP in English 1333 GMT 7 Jan 81 


[Text] Colombo, 7 Jan (AFP)--The Libyan Embassy here was today converted into a people's 
bureau administered by a committee of Libyans. A statement issued by the committee said 
this was in response to a call by the Libyan leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to change the 
Libyan embassies into people's bureaus to be administered by people's committees. 


Similar changes have taken place in Libyan embassies in several other capitals over the 
past few months. From today the people's bureau will be administered by a people's 
committee chosen by the Libyan revolutionary power of the Libyan Jamahiriya, the state- 
ment said. 


The charge d'affaires of the embassy has been appointed to the five-member committee and 
he has been placed in charge of cultural affairs. The other four committee members came 
from abroad a few days ago. It was not immediately known whether the diplomats in the 
former embassy would return home. 


“The establishment of the people's bureau in Sri Lanka, which expressed the people's 
diplomatic system, will play a positive part in further strengthening the already exist- 
ing ties between the two countries", the statement said.
