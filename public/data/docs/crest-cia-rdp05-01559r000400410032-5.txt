[News conference given by Libyan leader Mu‘ammar al-

(Text} (Question in English] The United States is saying that 
Libya may be behind the recent wave of attacks. What is Libya’s 
response to that? 

[Answer in Arabic with simultaneous English translation] This is 
an old story which the world has been listening to for a long time, 
and the world has been [word indistinct] for some time. The world 
has no evidence or any proof of this old story. It is merely to 
justify aggression against an independent state to make it lose its 
independence. 

{Question} Are you worried about the probability of the U.S. 6th 
Fleet returning? 

[Answer] We have now finished preparing military plans for 
confrontation in light of recent U.S. anti-Libyan statements. The 
United States will be militarily defeated and will fail for the 
second time. This is an issue which has already been settled: If 
an aggression is committed against us then we will spread 
violence against the United States and against its civilian and 
military targets in all the continents of the world. This issue has 
already been decided. But the important thing is that from now 
on we will call on all the peoples of the world to expel the United 
States from the United Nations'and from the UN Security 
Council. It should have no right to occupy a permament seat in 

the UN Security Council because a country which occupies a’ 

permanent seat in the UN Security Council should preserve 
world peace whereas the United States is a crazy country which 
threatens world peace; it now has no right to occupy a permanent 
seat in the UN Security Council. Efforts should be made to expel 
it from the UN Security Council unless the American people 
bring down Reagan and the whole crazy U.S. Administration. 

[Question in English] Who could be behind these attacks against 
US. targets? Could Libya be behind the attacks? 

[Answer] The enemies of the United States — all peoples on earth 
— are behind these actions.

[Question] Does this include Libya? 

[Answer] There is no doubt that the United States has turned 
Libya into an enemy but we have not yet issued orders for such 
action. When we issue such an order — if a new aggression is 
committed against us — then we will issue such an order to all 
international forces which helped us and to the forces of the 
Pan-Arab Command to start violence against the United States 
everywhere in the world. If a new aggression is committed then 
all the threats which I have told you of in previous news con- 
ferences will be implemented, including making the Mediterra- 
nean into a war zone — which will include southern Europe — 
and sea and air transport in the Mediterranean will be stopped. 

[Question] Can you explain to us what the United States aims to 
achieve by its political, economic, and military attack on the 
Arab region in general and Libya in particular? 

{Answer] Its aim is to subjugate the Arab nation and to make 
Israel into the strongest power in the region. It wants to subjugate 
the Arab homeland to neocolonialism and to continue its siege of 
the USSR. Its strategy requires that all the regions which stand 
in the way should be subjugated to U.S. influence. Fortunately 
this brings us and the USSR together because we are fighting for 
our independence; but at the same time we are doing the USSR 
a service because we are fighting its enemy which is advancing 
at our expense toward the USSR. 

(Question by TASS correspondent in Russian followed by Arab 
translation] Mikhail Gorbachev presented a new initiative for 
establishing peace in the Mediterranean through the withdrawal 
of the USSR and USS. fleets. What is your position on this 
initiative? Why in your opinion did the U.S. reject this initiative? 

[Answer] We supported it from the beginning; we support this 
proposal. This initiative indicates the USSR’s peaceful intentions 
and our friend Gorbachev’s wish to remove the danger of war 
from the world. This shows the difference between a superpower 
which feels its responsibilities toward world peace and which is 
the opposite of the irresponsible behavior of the U.S. Administra-. 
tion — a behavior devoid of any feeling of responsibility toward 
world peace. Togther with the peoples of the Mediterranean we 
will support the USSR proposal of withdrawing the superpower 
fleets from the Mediterranean. As for why the United States 
rejected it, it is known that it is primarily to protect the Zionist 
entity [words indistinct] and to punish the Mediterranean Arab 
countries. Therefore, to safeguard imperialist interests, the 
United States does not care about world peace. 

[Question in English] Will the Soviets defend you militarily if the 
6th Fleet returns? Will the Soviets defend you militarily? 

[Answer] Generally we are friends with the USSR and there is 
an agreement between us and the USSR to consult each other 
during serious crises to coordinate our efforts. 

[Question in English] In case of a U.S. attack, do you think the 
USSR will give military support to Libya?
