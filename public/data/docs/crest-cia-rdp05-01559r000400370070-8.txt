Summary (AI): A garbled 1976 CIA-reading-room translation reports Qadhdhafi meeting the masses of the 1 September University in Tripoli on the morning of 13 May 1976. He spoke of a group that tried to damage the university and education, and of the 7 April "popular revolution" crushing its enemies.
• The speech was reported at 1730 GMT on 13 May 1976 from a domestic broadcast.
• Qadhdhafi said the 7 April movement was a popular revolution, not a fascist coup.
• The text says the university should graduate specialists needed in various fields.
• 1976-05-13: Qadhdhafi addressed students, faculty and workers in the university courtyard, referring to the 7 April 1976 events as a popular revolution that crushed enemies of the university and education.
