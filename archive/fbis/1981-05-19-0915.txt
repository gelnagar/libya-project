LIBYA

PEOPLE'S BUREAU RELATIONS WITH U.S. EXPLAINED 


LD190732 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 18 May 8] 


[Text] Sources at the People's Committee of the People's Foreign Liaison Bureau have stated 
that the people's committee which was recently set up by the student masses to run the 
Libyan People's Bureau in the United States and to work for laying the foundations of the 
pillars of cooperation between the Libyan Arab and American peoples has been accredited by 
the students’ conferences in the Jamahiriyah and by the People's Committee of the People's 
Foreign Liaison Bureau. These sources have explained that members of the people's com- 
mittees of the people's bureaus are not diplomats, because after the establishment of the 
people's authority in the Jamahiriyah there was no longer government or foreign ministry in 
the Jamahiriyah, but rather a people's committee responsible for foreign affairs; it is 

the Liaison between the Libyan people and other peoples. 


The sources have pointed out that members of the people's bureau deported by the American 
Government were not diplomats and were not appointed by anyone, but were chosen by the 
Libyan people themselves. The sources have pointed out that if the United States does not 
believe in the fact that sound relations are those which are established directly between 
peoples, then the American Government thinks in a way that is behind the thinking of the 
era of the masses which the Libyan people are experiencing. The sources have explained that 
we in the Jamahiriyah, following the establishment of people's authority in 1977, have gone 
beyond the stage of traditional relations which is represented by the president, the 
government, the ambassador and the embassy. The sources stressed that common ties among 
the peoples of the world and the intermingling of interests are the basis on which re- 
lations among all peoples must be built, and that we in the Jamahiriyah welcome the 
establishment of popular relations with all the peoples of the world within the framework 
of mutual respect and common interests. 


AL-QADHDHAFI HOSTS BANQUET FOR OAU CHAIRMAN 


LD171024 Tripoli Voice’of Arab Homeland in Arabic 0115 GMT 17 May 81 


[Text] The brother commander yesterday met with Dr Siaka Stevens, the president of Sierra 
Leone and the current OAU chairman. The meeting was followed by a plenary meeting attended 
by members of both delegations and OAU Secretary General Adem Kojo. 


The brother commander helda banquet in honor of the OAU chairman last night, which was 
attended by Adem Kojo and his aides. Also present were Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, 
the commander in chief of the armed forces and members of the General People's Committee 
and members of the diplomatic and political corps accredited to the Jamahiriyah. 


V. 19 May 81 Q 2 NORTH AFRICA 


Speaking at the dinner banquet, the brother commander welcomed the current OAU chairman, 
the OAU secretary general and his aides. He said: I welcome the brother president 

as president of Sierra Leone and as chairman of the OAU. I thank him for cutting short 
his holiday and accepting the invitation to visit our country together with the OAU secre- 
tary general and his sides. I believe that his presence among us today, together with the 
secretary general and his aides, has great significancce and extreme importance. 


I wish to express my satisfaction with the talks we had today in the presence of the OAU 
secretary general concerning African issues, which are of interest to the entire 

African Continent. I am pleased to note that our views are identical on the common 
African issues. We allocated sufficient time in our talks today to a number of issues of 
vital importance to Africa, the commander said. He added: The most important to these 
issues were the questions of Chad and the Sahara, on which our views were fully identical. 


I thank the OAU chairman for the appreciation he expressed during the talks for Libya's 
positive role in ending the 20-year-old war in Chad. The OAU chairman noted that because 
of Libya's efforts, peace, security and stability have returned to Chad after 20 years of 
fighting and civil war. Dr Stevens also expressed the OAU's appreciation for Libya's 
efforts to achieve peace in Chad at a time when all other efforts of the Chadian National 
Government to obtain the help of other African forces failed to end the war. 


Dr Stevens has been notified that the Libyan forces which established peace and stability 
in Chad have now begun to withdraw and are being replaced by Chadian units. Thus, the 
Libyan forces will be gradually withdrawn as soon as national Chadian units are formed 

to replace them. The brother commander said: In our talks today, the OAU secretary 
general and his aides expressed great appreciation for this plan, which will help Chad to 
build its own forces and to gradually replace Libyan forces. For our part, we affirmed 
that this should not mean that we oppose the entry of any other African forces into Chad. 
However, we came to the conclusion in our talks that it is too late for this and that it 
is not necessary for other African countries to take the trouble of sending forces to 
Chad now that security and order prevail there and now that Libyan forces have begun to 
withdraw following the formation of Chadian national forces. 


The brother commander added: We agreed in our talks on the importance of holding a limited 
African summit soon, with the participation of specific countries, the Chadian dossier 

once and for all. Libya is prepared to attend this final, limited conference, which will 
end the Chadian problem and close its dossier for good. 


We expressed our appreciation for any joint African effort to help the Chadian people rebuild 
after the devastations of the civil war. This reconstruction effort now falls entirely 
on the shoulders of the African country of Libya. 


We also noted our awareness of the deteriorating conditions in the Western Sahara. If 
conditions continue as they are now, then a more serious deterioration may take place in 
that area, thereby widening the sphere of the war. We came to the conclusion that the only 
way to tackle this problem is through a peaceful effort to end this tragedy. This is the 
same path already followed by a large number of African countries which have re ognized 

the SDAR, including Sierra Leone and Libya. Other African countries should follow this 
path so that the SDAR may acquire official membership in the OAU. 


The brother commander pointed out that the efforts of the 27 OAU member states which have 
recognized the SDAR are positive efforts that will bring about peace. The recognition 

of the Saharan Republic by such a large number of countries means that it has secured 

the necessary support to become an OAU member. In view of this, the Moroccan Government 
is fighting an illegal war because it is fighting against an independent country which 
has won the right to become an OAU member. 


V. 19 May 81 Q 3 NORTH AFRICA 


Without a solution to this issue, more fighting will occur and the war will certainly 
escalate, resulting in serious deterioration in the Arab Maghreb region. This is because 
the Saharan people are determined to continue fighting until they attain their independence. 


The brother commander said: We reached complete agreement on all issves. For our part, 
we appreciate the efforts exerted by President Stevens in his capacity as OAU chairman to 
solve African problems and to increase African solidarity and unity. The experience and 
wisdom of President Stevens has made a positive contribution to the solution of African 
issues. Tangible successes have been achieved during his chairmanship, with the help of 
the OAU secretary general. 


We also discussed the importance of granting wider powers to the OAU secretary general 
so that he can increase his effectiveness. 


The brother commander added: I express my satisfaction with the development of friendship 
and cooperation between Libya and Sierra Leone. We declare that we have no reservations 
regarding the development of our relations with Sierra Leone. Following this visit, our 
relations will witness new development. All of this is due to Dr Stevens’ visit to his 
second country--Libya. Once more, I express my gratitude to you, Mr President, for pro- 
viding us with this opportunity to meet. Thank you. 


Dr Siaka Stevens, the current OAU chairman and president of Sierra Leone, also spoke during 
the banquet. He expressed his gratitude for the welcome and hospitality shown to him. 

He added: We must acknowledge that you have brought peace, calm and security to Chad. In 
this connection, your efforts are in accord with those exerted by the OAU to establish 
peace in Chad. 


The aim of the OAU at present, he said, is to reinforce and complete the road you have 
begun so that a greater degree of peace and stability can exist in this country. I am 
confident that our talks and our summit meeting will help to establish peace and end the 
problem at its roots and thereby end all existing disputes. 


I have visited about 13 African countries. I have also visited China and I had planned 
for some time to visit your country. I am extremely happy with my visit now. You are an 
OAU member and you work for peace as does the OAU. For this reason, we agree that through 
cooperation we will achieve peace and end conflicts. 


During our meeting this afternoon, I told you that there is a great opportunity to esta- 
blish peace. We are all working to provide the Chadian people with the opportunity to elect 
the legitimate government that will govern them. 


With regard to the Saharan issue, the current OAU chairman said: I can say that the 
Polisario has the support which entitles it to become an OAU member. Efforts to accept 
the Polisario as a full OAU member at the OAU conference in Nairobi will begin within 
the next 2 months. 


Dr Stevens added: I can say that the OAU has succeeded in persevering despite the numerous 
problems it has faced. It will, in the end, achieve what it is seeking. We express our 
thanks to all African countries, and particularly to those African countries which have 
offered help and which have tried their best to insure the success of the OAU. I wish 

your great country all success. I hope that the African peoples and members of the OAU 
will work to establish cooperation among themselves for the good of all. 


AL-QADHDHAFI SPEAKS ON PALESTINE USURPATION DAY 


LD151926 Tripoli JANA in English 1646 GMT 15 May 81 


[For another version--live but from poor reception--of the speech below by Col Mu'ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, see the 15 May DAILY REPORT, page Q 3] 


[Text] Tripoli, 15 May (JANA)--The leader of the revolution has affirmed that it was the 
Palestinian who was fighting and dying while the rest of the Arabs were feeling ashamed 
because they did not fight. 


V. 19 May 81 Q4 NORTH AFRICA 


In a speech he delivered yesterday before the Arab masses participating in the mass rally 
held on the occasion of the thirty third anniversary of Palestine usurpation, the leader 

said: "The Palestinian people is sacrificing its children everyday, the Lebanese people 

is faced with threat and destruction, and the threat is also directed against Syria, the 

last trench in the northern front." 


He added, "We announce to America, and we are free to do so, that any weapons the 
Palestinian resistance can use we are ready to supply them and buy them even if their 
weight is in gold." 


The leader of the revolution also indicated that the Zionist enemy for thirty three years 
has been achieving successive successes in the application of its strategic plan to esta- 
blish a Zionist state in the heart of the Arab homeland. The leader warned that "if the 

enemy's advance intensifies in front of an Arab retrogression, as it is the case now, we 

will witness, in coming years, a bad situation affecting even the Arab existence itself." 
He added, "The question is no longer a question of independence or lack of independence, 

but a question of existence of nonexistence." 


He went on to say, "Arab existence on the Arab homeland is really threatened now by the 
U.S. bases and American protection which constitutes a strategic umbrella for a Zionist 
advance which adopts a step-by-step manoeuvre." He indicated that “after thirty years, 
the Zionist enemy reached Jerusalem and made it its so-called capital, and it means that 
it continues to apply this strategy under the U.S. umbrella, which umbrella also succeeded 
in getting a foot on Arab soil and setting up bases in Egypt, Sudan, Oman, Somalia and 
other parts of the Arab world where camps are ready for U.S. troops, and where their 
airports, harbours, streets and even their houses are U.S. property." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi called upon the Palestinian revolutionary command to mobilize all 
Palestinian forces all over the world, materially and morally, and open the door wide to 
have the honour of fighting and dying for Palestine. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said that the Lebanese Government should define its stand clearly, 

either accept Arab forces to come to defend Lebanon or accept Israeli forces over its soil. 
“With its present attitude, the Lebanese Government makes the man of the street wonder 

why the Arabs take such a negative stand towards an Arab League member."' Colonel al-Qadhdhafi 
called upon the Lebanese Government to allow Arab troops in Lebanon and then the Arab rulers 
will reveal their real identity whether they are with the Arab nation of against it. 


"The aim of the Israelis is to deal a blow to the Arab nation. They have occupied part 

of Lebanon on their way to Syria and Jordan. Egypt's turn will come later as the Israeli 
withdrawals from Arish and Sinai are only metaphorical, as Egyptian forces are not allowed 
to replace Israeli troops." 


The leader continues, "The Syrian missiles in dispute should cover the skies of southern 
Lebanon to make it impossible for the Israeli planes to penetrate into the Lebanese air- 
space." 


"The Libyans are ready to deploy missiles on the Lebanese territory for that purpose. We 
make this offer again to the Lebanese Government, as we already extended this offer over 
two years ago." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi wondered why the Israeli planes have the right to strike against Syrian 
forces along the borders of Syria, "while we are denied the right to destroy Sa'd Haddad's 
positions and forces?" The Arab planes should start daily raids on Haddad's enclave at 
least and the Arab forces should form an umbrella to the Palestinian resistance to enable 

it liberate Palestine and destroy the Israeli settlements there. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said thac most Arabs thought talks were useless, against the Israeli 
escalated attacks and air raids on Palestinian camps, Lebanese cities and Arab troops.

“We are, however, forced into talks, even though we share the view of those who are 
tired of talks." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said in his speech before the Arab masses who took part in the 

33rd anniversary of Palestinian usurpation: "We heard the speech of the leader of the 
Palestinian revolution, Brother Abu ‘Ammar [Yasir ‘Arafat], because he has the right to 
speak. It is the Palestinisn who fights and dies and does not believe in talks. But 
we, the other Arabs, feel ashamed as we did not fight. It is the Palestinian people who 
fight and their children die daily, and behind them stand the Lebanese people who were 
exposed to threat and destruction. It is also directed against Syria, the last trench 
in northern front. 


The leader added, "The Palestinians, more than anyone else, have the right to speak 
loudly, because every day they fight with their lives in their hands. It is very 
important to hear the voice of the Palestinian resistance leader in such hours, when 
the Israelis stage raids against the Lebanese cities and Palestinian forces and camps. 
Because the stronger this voice is the stronger the fighters will be. Those who talk 
and do nothing should be ashamed of themselves, and it is a shame that the rest of the 
Arabs do not join in the fight." 


"I have the utmost pleasure, and in this particular time, to have ‘Abu Ammar, Abu Iyad 
[Salah Khalaf] and Abu Tariq [Sulaymen ash-Shurafa', Fatah representative in Libya] from 
Fatah's command with us here together with the rest of the leaders and secretaries of 
the P.F.L.P., D.F.L.P., P.F.L.P.-General Command and As-Sa'qah." 


"The Libyans in particular and the Arabs in general, consider this gathering with the 
leaders of Palestinian people as very important and resembles a real answer to the 
malicious sides which rejoiced at the existence of a crisis between Libya and Fatah." 
He affirmed "there should be no crisis between Al-Fateh Revolution and the Palestinian 
revolution, because no contradiction can be found between them, and there should be no 
contradiction." 


The leader wondered why there was a so-called crisis in the past, and said, "Maybe there 
was a hidden hand behind everything that happened." He added, "The Palestinians should 
not = blamed by the Arabs, and we will not betray the Palestinian leadership unless it 
is j yed by the Palestinian masses, because the leaders of the Palestinian people 
cant ve against the revolution, against the revolutionary activity, or against the 
popular masses, people's congresses, or the revolutionary committees." He also 
indicated "the Fatah movement is special because it is not a political party, so it 
would be unlikely and unusual that it contradicts with the movement of the Palestinian 
people's congresses and revolutionary committees." 


The leader stressed the importance of increasing popular congresses between the 
Jamahiriyah and the Palestinian people, for all the world know that behind the 
Palestinian leadership, there is a people who refuse to be conquered. Through these 
congresses the Palestinian people will make its existence felt by its enemies and through 
this existence they will fight and refuse to be occupied. For he knows that this 
leadership is guiding a people and that the Palestinian occupied land has a people that 
exist and by that, we mean not just regionally, in any Arab country or just anywhere, 

but these congresses, about which our views were almost identical as to their 

importance people's congresses and revolutionary committees. 


The leader wondered what the world's position would be towards the establishment of 
hundreds of Palestinian peoples congresses and revolutionary committees, given that it 
already recognized the resolutions of the Palestinian National Council. He said: "This 
does not mean the absence of the Palestinian Liberation Organization. If there was a 
discussion on this issue a formula would be reached for the creation of people's 
congresses and revolutionary committees under the umbrella of the P.L.O. which we talk 
about everywhere and support, and show to the world as the sole representative of the 
Palestinian people so that no other merchant after As-Sadat comes and does what As-Sadat 
is doing now in the conspiracy of autonomy to have the Palestinian people represented 

by a group of traitors."

He went on to say "as Arabs we are all agreed and committed to affirm the character of 
the PLO at least in the phase of liberation struggle until Palestine is liberated, and 
after this the form of the regime is not important because these matters come in their 
own time, but temporarily now we stress the importance of the PLO and consider it the 
militant essence of the Palestinian." He added "so long as all Palestinians are 
supporting the PLO inside the occupied land and outside, no one has the right to exclude 
it and we must preserve it, support and back it internationally so that world knows that 
this organization is the legitimate sole representative of the Palestinian people." 


The leader continued: "The evidence is that there is no contradiction between the PLO 
and the free will of the Palestinian masses to form their own people's congresses and 
committees. In Libya now, there are Palestinian people's congresses and revolutionary 
committees in the same time we have here with us the command of the PLO. He who is in

"Our stand was and still is quite clear. We are with the Palestinian masses and their 
freedom. We allow no oppression to be exercised on them, at home and abroad. It is 
enough that they are humiliated and repressed inside the occupied land. Let them be 
free in unoccupied Arab lands such as Libya." 


"Therefore, the Palestinian masses have to exercise their freedom in this arena, they 
should be allowed to express their view in support of the P.L.O. and Palestinian 
resistance command." 


"I do not believe that the freedom of masses is a threat on their cause. On the 
contrary, when the masses are free, they win their cause. On the other hand, people 
who do not control their destinies eventually lose all their causes". 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi then called upon the Palestinian masses to back financially and 
morally their leadership when the masses, he said become free, then, they can organize 
themselves in people's congresses." He added "the Palestinian should not listen to any 
one, but to judge things according to their conscience and in the interests of their 
cause. They should not put themselves under Arab rulers’ control as this would mean 

the liquidation of the Palestinian cause. The Great al-Fateh Revolutionwill always 
stand in support of the Palestinian masses inside and outside the occupied territories". 


He added: No one has the right to interfere in the Palestinian internal affairs at this 
particular stage, or to oppose the approach or the arrangements they choose. 


"We will always stand on the side of the Palestinians all the way along until victory or 
death", he said. 


The leader asserted that there is a real danger facing the Arab nation and that the 
enemy had been aware of this Arab retreat and helplessness especially after the losing 
war waged by Iraq against Iran. The war against the Iranian revolution is being 
financed on behalf of America, the leader explained, asserting that such matter causes 
a big Arab loss. 


The leader added that enemy became aware of this strategic disorder and surprised us 
with conditions unheard of before in the long history of man. And today the enemy sent 
a reconnaissance plane for gathering information about the Syrian territories and 
forces, which the Syrians shot down. 


The enemy may decide to invade Syria or attack its forces in Lebanon with the aim of

[Text] The commander in chief of the armed forces, Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir this 
morning received Brother Qunswah, member of the Regional Command of the Arab Ba'th 

Party and regional secretary general of the Ba'th in Lebanon. He also received ‘Abd 
al-Rahim Murad, the secretary general of the Arab National Union in Lebanon. He also 
received Brother Abu al-Hul, member of the Fatah Central Committee and brother Abu Tariq, 
{Sulayman ash-Shurafa'], director of the PLO office in the Jamahiriyah.
