Summary (AI): A 1983 NPIC briefing attachment maps Libyan ground and air forces deployed in northern Chad (Faya Largeau, Ounianga Kebir, Ogoui, Aozou) during the Libyan-backed fighting with Chadian government forces. Nothing here concerns Tripoli itself.
• Libyan forces in Chad as of August 1983 included elements of three mechanized infantry battalions, one probable motorized infantry battalion, two tank companies, three heavy-lift battalions, ten 130mm M-46 field guns, two 122mm D-30 howitzer batteries and one BM-21 rocket launcher battery.
• Libyan combat aircraft in Chad included seven Mirage F-1, 23 Fitter (Su-22), 20 SF.260 light-strike aircraft, nine Hind attack helicopters and three Hip helicopters.
• Air defence around Aozou included five SA-9 and three ZSU-23/4, with flat face and spoon rest early warning radars about 27 NM away.
• 1983-07: Libyan mechanized and motorized infantry units introduced into Chad in late July, after which Libyan ground forces pushed as far south as Faya Largeau.
