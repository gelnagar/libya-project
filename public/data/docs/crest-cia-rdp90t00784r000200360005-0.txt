Summary (AI): An NPIC imagery briefing note (16 September 1982) reports that 16 Libyan-origin tanks and heavy-lift trailers recently arrived at a Polisario camp at Oued Tatrat in Algeria, probably shipped from Libya via the Mers el Kebir naval base and shipyard. The subject is Libyan military support to the Polisario, far from Tripoli.
• Sixteen medium tanks and three MAZ-537 heavy-lift transporter trailers were newly identified at Oued Tatrat Polisario Camp No.1.
• Oued Tatrat lies 620 nm southwest of Mers El Kebir and 33 nm east of the Algerian-Western Sahara border.
• Heavy haze and oblique imagery prevented earlier identification of the equipment.
• Collateral reference: NPIC Z-11178/82, Arms Transshipment Activity, 16 Aug 1982.
• 1982-08: Libyan roll-on/roll-off cargo ship offloaded 16 T-54/55 medium tanks and four MAZ-537 heavy-lift transporter trailers, each carrying an MRL, probably later transported to Oued Tatrat.
