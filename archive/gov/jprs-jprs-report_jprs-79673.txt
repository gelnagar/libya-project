Foreign Ministry "Currents" 


Second, the cabinet: In the French cabinet, the strongest force that is sympa- 
thetic to the Arabs, that understands their issues and that is eager to maintain 
a strong French-Arab relationship is the Ministry of External Relations. This 
ministry contains a large number of officials and diplomats who are sympathetic 
to the Arabs. You even find in the ministry several Arab currents: There is a 
current that supports Saudi Arabia, the Gulf and Iraq, a pro-Palestinian current, 
a pro-Algeria current, a pro-Morocco current, a pro-Libya current, a pro-Lebanon 
current, a pro-Egypt current and so forth. Claude Cheysson, the minister of 
external relations (60 years old), is considered to a large degree "a friend of 
the Arabs" even though he is not hostile to Israel. Cheysson has broad experience 
in Third World affairs generally and in Arab-world affairs in particular as a 
result of the years of his work within the framework of the EEC. He believes 
that strengthening relations with the Arab world is a "vital" and indispensable 
issue for France. Cheysson is realistic and is like Mitterrand in this respect.

Ministers Sympathetic to Arabs 


The present French cabinet includes a number of prominent ministers who are 
sympathetic to the Arabs. The minister closest to the Arabs is Michel Jobert, 
the minister of state for foreign trade. Jobert is a leftist Gaullist who was 
minister of external relations under Pompidou's presidency during the 1973 war. 
At the time, Jobert clashed with Dr Kissinger and worked to foil a U.S. scheme 
aimed at forming a U.S.-European front to fight and strike OPEC. Jobert believes 
in the necessity of cooperation with the Arabs and the oil countries not only 

at the political level but in all the spheres of interest to both sides. He is 
frank and daring. One of the reasons for including him in the cabinet was the 
eagerness to reassure the Arabs. In addition to Jobert, there is Jean-Pierre 
Chevenement, the French minister of state for scientific research and technology. 
He is one of the leaders of the current supporting the Arabs and the Palestinians
