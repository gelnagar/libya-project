LIBYA

ARAB COUNCIL CONDEMNS PRESENCE OF U.S. FLEET 


LD161944 Tripoli JANA in English 1416 GMT 16 Mar 83 


{Text] Tripoli, Jumada Thani 2, Mar 16, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY -- The Council of 
Representatives at the Secretariat of the Arab People’s Congress [APC] has condemned 
the aggressive presence of the U.S. Sixth Fleet in Alexandria and which is geared to 
terrorize the Libyan Arab people. The council denounced the shameful stands of the 
Egyptian regime by welcoming the U.S. fleet destroyers and aircraft carrier Nimitz. 


During the meeting held by the A.P.C. council here last night to prepare for the lst 
meeting of the A.P.C. Secretariat in 1983 in Tripoli between 19-21 March, the council 
called upon the Egyptian revolutionary force and the Arab masses to live up to their 
responsibility and face the imperialist infiltration of the U.S. military forces which 
exercise a dirty role to suppress the freedom of the Arab people and crush the 
nationalist and progressive spirit in the Arab world. 


It condemned the blatant interference of the U.S. forces in the Arab region reflected 
in all forms of terrorism and aggression and unlimited support for the Zionist eneay. 


Tripoli Reports Protests 
LD162344 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 16 Mar 83 


[Text] The storm of public anger and protestation in the Jamahiriya against the visit 
to Alexandria by components of the U.S. 6th Fleet, components which were repeatedly and 
openly used to provoke the Libyan Arab people, continues. For the third consecutive 
day, public demonstrations [word indistinct] in (Gatran), Suq Al-Khamis, Barqin, 
(?7Al-Shati'), [word indistinct] and Tauorga expressing anger, protest, and defiance at 
the aggressive provocations of the U.S. 6th Fleet. Statements issued after the angry 
demonstrations emphasized that the official reception in Alexandria by the Egyptian 
regime for the American nuclear aircraft carrier Nimitz and a number of other members 
of the U.S. 6th Fleet, which had engaged in repeated terrorist aggression against the 
Jamahiriya, is a provocative [word indistinct] act against the Libyan Arab people. The 
masses emphasized in their statements that by this dirty act the Egyptian regime has 
made Egypt a military base for American imperialism that threatens the Arab nation and 
its free will. The masses participating in the angry demonstrations reiterated their 
insistence to confront the arrogant provocations of the American administration which 
is trying to damage the giant achievements of the Libyan Arab people through the Great 
Al-Fatih Revolution.

TRADE OF OIL FOR FRENCH WARPLANES REPORTED 


LD152000 Kuwait KUNA in English 1919 GMT 15 Mar 83 


[Text] Paris, March 15 (KUNA) -- Libya anxiously trying to strengthen its air defence 
system has agreed to supply oil to France in return for 20 French Mirage 2000 and 
"Super Etendard’ planes, it was reported here Tuesday. The decision was taken by Libya 
after secret diplomatic endeavours between Paris and Tripoli proved successful and the 
two countries convened here last week a meeting of their joint commission for economic, 
technical, cultural and scientific cooperation. 


The meeting which was attended by the French minister of foreign trade and Libya 
planning minister Fawzi al-Shakshuki was described by political observers here as a 
"dialogue" between the two countries to improve relations which were strained following 
Libyan intervention in Chad, the closure of French Embassy in Tripoli and a series of 
charges and countercharges against each other since the beginning of 1980. 


Official sources here believe that the French oil companies ‘CFP and Elf-Aquitaine' 
have already entered into negotiations with Libyan oil authorities to conclude a one 
year deal to import Libyan oil. If everything goes well between the two countries, the 
sources added, the French authorities would soon make it known public that it was 

ready to supply Mirage and Super Etendard planes to Libya. 


COOPERATION PROTOCOL SIGNED WITH ROMANIA 


LD162342 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 16 Mar 83 


[Text] A protocol on cooperation between the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab 
Jamahiriyah and the Socialict Republic of Romania in the fields of communications and 
maritime and air transportation was signed this morning in Tripoli. The protocol 
includes an agreement on cooperation between the two countries in the fields on roads, 
railways, telecommunications, training and joint investments. The protocol was signed 
for Libya by the brother secretary of communications and maritime transport, and for 
Romania by Vasile Bulucea, minister of transportation and telecommunications. The 
protocol comes within the framework of implementing the agreements on joint cooperation 
between the two friendly countries, which were agreed upon during the visit to the 
Socialist Republic of Romania by the brother leader of the revolution last January.

ITALIAN RELATIONS JEOPARDIZED BY STATEMENT 
LD151149 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0900 GMT 15 Mar 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 15 Mar (JANA) -- The Italian newspaper EPOCO has published a state- 
ment attributed to the Italian foreign minister, Emilio Colombo, which smacks of 

the American reactionary fabrications about an alleged Libyan threat to the Sudan 

and Egypt. The Italian minister made intimations which are harmful to the developing 
relations between the two countries. He sought to dwell on a matter which in essence 
consists of mere allegations. The intentions behind these allegations are clear, 
particularly since they originate from a superpower which constantly provokes the 
Libyan people politically, economically, and militarily. 


This official stand, expressed by the Italian minister, is no more than a blind 
repetition of the same pretexts and allegations leveled by the United States against 
Libya. 


One cannot but regret to see such an attitude on the part of a man who is expected 
to scrutinize, verify, and deal with facts and the reality which prompted the other, 
alleging parties to admit that there was no Libyan threat to either the Sudan or 
Egypt or, for that matter, to any other country -- not to mention the fact that the 
Italian minister himself had been informed of the Libyan denials of any intention to 
interfere in anybody's affairs or commit aggression against anyone. 


Emilio Colombo is quoted as having said that we have witnessed a period of tension 
between the United States and Libya because of some military moves and initiatives of 
an unspecified nature by Tripoli. Colombo, furthermore, said that the United States 
took some precautionary measures in the form of the deployment of AWACS aircraft and 
the movement of the aircraft carrier Nimitz. 


These utterances by the Italian foreign minister are contrary to the facts and 
constitute flagrant accusations which are in form and content akin to the American 
allegations and accusations which aim at justifying aggression against the Libyan 
Arab people who are defending their freedom and who desire to live in peace and 
security. 


We denounce these statements which pursue a line contrary to the one we had hoped to 
see and which is necessary for the maintenance of close relations of cooperation 
between Libya and Italy. We believe that the Italian people cannot but support our 
denunciation.
