LIBYA

Al-Onadhdhafi Receives Viewage From India’s Gandhi 
LD2Z80H01 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland in Arabic 
0015 GMT 28 Now 83 


[Text] The brother leader of the revolution has received a reply 
from Indira Gandhi, current chairman of the Nonaligned Move- 
ment. In her message, she expressed anxiety at the serious threat 
to peace in the eastern Mediterranean posed by the concentration 
A weapons, troops, and warships there [words indistinct} 


She confirmed that the Zionist invasion of Lebanon has made 
the situation more dangerous, pointing out that she has contacted 
many world leaders to urge them to use their influence in order 
to achieve the immediate and unconditional withdrawal of Israeli 
forces from Lebanon, as the withdrawal of these forces will 
facilitate the departure of all remaining troops. 


Indira Gandhi expressed satisfaction with and appreciation of the 
efforts made by the brother leader of the revolution for the 
freedom and unity of Lebanon, confirming that her country will 
always support the unity, sovereignty and nonaligned status of 
Lebanon 


Indira Gandhi ended her message by describing our struggle as 
directed against both Israeli aggression and the injustice and 
{word indistinct] which it commits, repeating that the cuurent 
situation in Lebanon will serve only the interests of the aggres- 
sors
