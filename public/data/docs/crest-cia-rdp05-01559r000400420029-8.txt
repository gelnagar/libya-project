Summary (AI): A December 1987 FBIS translation of a Tripoli TV broadcast of Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi's speech at the opening of the General People's Congress (22 Nov 1987) in Tripoli's People's Hall, on people's congresses, "freedom declaration," the Green Book, and Libyan bases. It mentions Wheelus Air Base (now Mellaha/Mitiga) only in historical context.
• The General People's Congress consisted of 5 General Secretariat secretariats, 65 municipal secretariats, 130 branch secretaries and assistants, 39 municipal congress secretaries, 11 General People's Committee members, and 130 people's committees, per the chairman.
• Qadhdhafi said the revolution was a declaration of freedom and that the Green Book is not sacred.
• Qadhdhafi said the Gulf of Sidra and the Aozou strip should not be conceded to the 'enemy alliance'; he said oil pipelines and ports run to the Gulf of Sidra.
• Qadhdhafi referred to the Al-'Adam base (former British base near Tubruq) in the same passage.
• 1987-11-22: Opening session of the General People's Congress addressed by Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, broadcast live by Tripoli Television.
