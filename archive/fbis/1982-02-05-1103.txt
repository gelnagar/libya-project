LIBYA

OFFICIAL SUMMONS GREEK ENVOY OVER AIR INCIDENT 


LD041424 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1330 GMT 4 Feb 82 


[Text] This morning [as heard] 'Abd al-'Ati al-'Ubaydi, secretary of the people's 
committee of the People's Foreign Liaison Bureau, summoned the Greek charge d'affaires 
to the Jamahiriyah. He explained to the charge d'affaires the dimensions of the terror- 
ist act which the U.S. administration engineered and which comprised two U.S. Phantom-14 
military aircraft intercepting the Libyan passenger airliner in Greek airspace during 
its normal flight No 152 from Tripoli to Athens. 


The secretary of the people's committee of the People's Foreign Liaison Bureau stressed 
that this terrorist act constitutes a threat to safety and security in the Mediterranean 
region. He recalled the fact that Papandreou's government declared that the presence of 
the U.S. bases in Greece constitutes a danger to the life of the Greek and neighboring 
peoples. 


SUDANESE TEXTILE WORKERS DECLARE GENERAL STRIKE 


NC050737 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0600 GMT 5 Feb 82 


[Text] Textile workers in Sudan yesterday declared a general strike as part of the 
popular uprising in Sudan against Numayri's hardline regime. The strike affected most 
of the textile plants in the country. The workers formed national committees to super- 
vise the strike and to establish coordination with the rest of the national forces.
