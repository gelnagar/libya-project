Validity Study of SNIE 36, 5-59 

The Libyan Situation 

Published 24 February 1959 

SNIE 36, 5-59 dealt mainly with the internal political. situation 
in Libya and Libya's foreign policy. It remains valid in most 
particulars. It did not, of course, include consideration of the 
implications of oil development, as the principal strikes had 

not been made at the time of publication of the estimate.
