LIBYA

USSR’s Demchenko Arrives on Visit 20 May 
LD201937 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1900 GMT 20 May 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 20 May (JANA) — Vladimir Demchenko, deputy 
chairman of the RSFSR Council of Ministers and chairman of 
the Soviet-Libyan Friendship Society, arrived in Tripoli this 
evening on a visit to the Jamahiriyah. 


In a statement toa JANA reporter, he expressed his appreciation 
for the prominent role the Jamahiriyah is playing in the struggle 
against U.S. imperialism and Zionism. He stressed the solidarity 
of the Soviet Union with the people of the Jamahiriyah in their 
opposition to all U.S. imperialist plans. 


He also expressed the hope that his visit would contribute to the 
promotion of the relations of friendship and cooperation between 
the two friendly peoples.
