LIBYA

AT-TURAYKI MEETS WITH TURKISH AMBASSADOR 


LD291640 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 29 Dec 81 


{Text] Tripoli, 29 Dec (JANA) -- The liaison secretary for foreign affairs, Dr Ali ‘Abd 
as-Salam at-Turayki, today received the Turkish ambassador. The meeting was held within 
the framework of efforts being made by Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah to 
reconcile Turkey and Greece, to maintain relations between the two countries at a strong 
and cohesive level and to maintain the historic ties between the Turkish and Greek peoples 
in a manner that will perserve peace and security in the Mediterranean region.
