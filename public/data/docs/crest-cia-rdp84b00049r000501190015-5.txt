Summary (AI): A 1982 White House memo to Secretary of State Shultz forwards Secretary Weinberger's "stair-step" plan for increasing U.S. military presence near Libya, and summarizes past U.S. naval exercises in the Gulf of Sidra and Libyan protests. Nothing here is specific to Tripoli.
• The stair-step plan proposed graduated increases in U.S. military activity near Libya, with steps from limited airspace restrictions in July 1982 to open-ocean missile exercises south of 32°30'N by December 1982.
• COMSIXTHFLT conducted two to three open-ocean missile exercises a year.
• A 1979 U.S. Navigation and Overflight Policy Paper said the U.S. should avoid actions seen as acquiescence in illegal claims.
• The memo asks that the issue be handled on a strict need-to-know basis.
• 1973-10: Libya's UN representative declared the Gulf of Sidra south of 32°30'N an integral part of Libya under its complete sovereignty.
• 1980-06-14: Open ocean missile exercise with a NOTAM covering the area south of 32°30'N; Libya delivered a protest note.
• 1980-09-21: Open ocean missile exercise with NOTAM south of 32°30'N; Libya protested and declared its own forces would exercise in the same area; parts of the U.S. exercise were cancelled.
