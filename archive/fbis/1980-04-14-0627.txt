LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI APPEALS TO IRAQ, IRAN TO AVOID CONFRONTATION 
1D111642 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1400 GMT 11 Apr 80 D 


[Text] Tripoli--Brother Col Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi has sent a message to Presidents Saddam 
Husayn and Abolhasan Bani-Sadr, Here is the text: 


I appeal to you in the name of Islam, which made the believers brothers, and in the name of 
the common history of the two fraternal nations--Arab and Persian--to make every effort 


possible to end confrontation between the two states. 


A clash between the Iranian Revolution and any detachment of the Arab revolution only 
serves the colonialists and the Zionists--the real enemies of both nations, 


The necessary and legitimate confrontation is that of the United States and the Israelis 
and their dirty allies, the reactionary agents, Until our envoys reach Baghdad and Tehran 
I beg you to exercise the virtue of self-restraint. Hold your anger and do not provide the 
enemies with the opportunity to rejoice at our misfortunes, 
[Signed] Your brother Mu'tammar al-Qadhdhafi.
