[Text] The brother leader of the revolution [Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi] last night held a 
dinner banquet in honor of Gustav Husak, the general secretary of the Czechoslovak 
Communist Party Central Committee and the CSSR president. Present at the dinner banquet 
were Staff Maj 'Abd as-Salam Ahmad Jallud and Brig Gen Mustafa al-Kharrubi, as well as 
the CSSR deputy premier and the delegation accompanying the visiting president. The 
banquet was also attended by the heads of the Arab and foreign diplomatic missions in 
the Jamahiriyah. 


During the banquet, speeches were exchanged between the brother leader and the CSSR 
»resident. The brother leader delivered a speech in which he welcomed CSSR President 
Gustav Husak and the delegation accompanying him. The brother leader expressed his 
gratitude for the development of relations between the Jamahiriyah and the CSSR. He 
said: The relations between Libya and Czechoslovakia are excellent. This visit will 
further develop these relations and we pin much hope on it, because it will contribute 
to the strengthening of this friendship and the development of the existing cooperation 
between the Libyan and the Czechoslovak peoples. 


The brother leader said: We still carry nice memories about your people, your country 
and your government from our visit to your country 3 years ago. He said: We appreciate 
this visit, which comes at a time when the imperialist plots are intensifying against 
Libya and the socialist countries. This visit is considered a practical example of the 
solidarity of Czechoslovakia with the Libyan people at a time when they are facing direct 
armed aggression from the United States. 


The brother leader said: The confrontation with imperialism and its ferocious onslaught 
will be the subject of interest in our talks during this visit. He said: This armed 
aggression by a big power has convinced us that the strengthening of cooperation and 
cohesion among the progressive forces in the world is unavoidable. 


He said: Imperialism, which is against freedom, peace and progress, has begun acting 
openly and pursuing an extremely insolent policy. We announce that we have decided to 
confront it, and that the strengthening of cohesion between our people and the socialist 
camp is an important step against the plot. 


The brother leader affirmed that this will not only strengthen the peoples of 
Czechoslovakia and Libya, but all the peoples who are facing the imperialist onslaught 
and the racist aggression in Palestine and Africa. He said: Tne enhancement of our 
cohesion will strengthen the struggle of the peoples of El Salvador, Chile, Nicaragua, 
Palestine, Namibia and other peoples who are struggling against imperialism and racism. 
The strengthening of relations with the socialist bloc, specifically between 
Czechoslovakia and Libya, is, in fact, a strengthening of the friendship and relations 
between the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front and the socialist bloc and 
Czechoslovakia. It is also a strengthening of your relations with the peoples of the 
Arab nation. The 1 September Revolution enjoys the profound support of the peoples of 
the Arab nation and Libya is considered their representative and the country that 
expresses their aspirations. 


The brother leader said: We would like to assure you tonight of our readiness to 
further develop this friendship and cooperation.

Revolution, Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, President Gustav Husak, General Secretary of the 
Czechoslovak Communist Party [CPCZ] Central Committee and president of the CSSR paid 

a friendly official visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah from 7 to 10 
September 1981. The Czechoslovak president was accorded an official and popular welcome, 
which was evidence of the strength of friendship between the two friendly countries and 
peoples. 


The Czechoslovak president and accompanying delegation visited some economic, agricultural, 
and industrial projects and acquainted themselves with the achievements of the Great 

1 September Revolution. Official talks were held between President Gustav and Col Mu'ommar 
al-Qadhdhafi, Participating in the talks on the Libyan side were:

attention to what is going on in the Arab arena. 


Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi informed President Gustav Husak of the recent provocation

The two sides stressed their countries' solidarity with the struggle of the Palestinian 
Arab people under the leadership of the PLO, the sole legitimate representative of the 
Palestinian people, and declared that lasting and just peace in the region cannot be 
achieved except by liberating all the occupied Arab territories and by guaranteeing the 
legitimate rights of the Palestinian people, including their rights to return to their 
homes, to determine their own fate and to set up their independent state. 


The Czechoslovak side viewed with great admiration the efforts being exerted by the 
Steadfastness and Confrontation Front to foil the imperialist, Zionist and reactionary 
plots to pass capitulatory solutions. In this reenect the Czechoslovak side appreciates

underlined the importance of cooperation and friendship between the Arab people and the 
peoples of the socialist community and they expressed their determination to further 
deepen these relations in realization of the common aims in the service of the just 
causes, progress and peace. 


The two sides expressed support for an immediate halt to Israeli attacks against the 
people of Lebanon, and for ensuring Lebanon's independence, national sovereignty and 
territorial safety.

The two sides stressed that they continue to stand against attempts to internationalize 
the Lebanese crisis, a matter which contradicts the interests of the Arab people and 
peace in the Arab region. 


The tw> sides strongly condemn the barbaric Israeli raid on the Iraqi nuclear establish- 
ments, considering it a violation of the basic rules of international relations in 

the UN Charter. The two sides expressed concern over U.S. neocolonialist policy which 
is represented in the intensified military presence in the Mediterranean and the Arabian 
Gulf on the pretext that these are vital areas of interest: This is a matter which 
constitues a danger not only to the independence and security of the peoples of this 
region but also a threat to world peace and security. 


The two sides also stressed that European security is firmly linked to the security of 
the Mediterranean. 


The two sides condemned attempts by the industrial imperialist states to impose economic 
domination on the developing countries with the aim of undermining these countries’ 
development plars and depriving them of economic and social progress for their peoples, 
and also preventing them from obtaining (satisfactory) prices for their national pro- 
ducts and natural resources. 


The two sides rejected the imperialist policy of economic discrimination, embezzlement 
and exploitation and also all forms of economic pressure. 


The two sides discussed the situation in Africa and stressed their support for the 
liberation movement, especially in Namibia under the leadership of SWAPO, the sole 
legitimate representative of the Namibian people. The two sides condemned the aggres- 
sion by the racist regime in South Africa on the territories of Angola and other 
neighboring African countries. 


The two sides supported what was achieved by the African countries at the Nairobi 
conference in connection with the problem of the Western Sahara and the recognition 
of the right of Saharan people to self-determination in accordance with UN principles 
and OAU resolutions. 


The two sides support the stability of peace and political life in Chad, and also the 
efforts being made in this direction by the OAU, as expressed by the resolutions of 

the OAU summit in Nairobi. They welcomed the establishment of security and stability 

in Chad. In this respect the Czechoslovak side commended the effective role which the 
Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah played in eliminating the imperialist and 
reactionary plans and in stopping the hemorrage to which the people of Chad were exposed. 


The two sides supported the efforts being exerted to end the war between Iraq and Iran 
and they reasserted their stand that the continuation of this war between the two 
countries is contrary to their interests. It also exposes the security and stability 
of the region to extreme danger and it gives colonialism the chance to reestablish 

its presence in this region. 


The two sides stressed their solidarity with the revolution of the people of Afghanistan 
and the support the efforts being made to find a settlement of the situation in Afghanistan. 
They also strongly condemn the interference of imperialist forces in the internal affairs 
of the region, particularly in Afghanistan. 


The two sides stressed their solidarity with the efforts of the peoples of Southeast Asia 
for peace and security and for checking the imperialist military presence in that 

region, and also its constant attempts to interfere in the internal affairs of Vietnam, 
Kampuchea and Laos.

When reviewing the situation in Europe, the two sides underlined the extreme danger which 
is represented in the NATO decisicn regarding the production and (?deployment) of medium-

The two sides strongly condemn the U.S. Administration's decision to embark on the pro-

The two sides support the international initiatives calling for comprehensive disarmament: 
In this respect they point out the Czechoslovak initiative which was adopted by the UN

The two sides underlined the important positive role being played by the Nonaligned

for peoples who are struggling for liberation and progress. TI yo sides consider the 
Nonaligned Movement to be an important element in the stability . the international 
Situation. The two sides stressed their adherence to the principles and aims of the 
United Nations and also their appreciation of the role the United Nations is playing in

The two sides evaluated the development of the current economic and trade relations 

and they expressed satisfaction with their continuous growth. They also agreed on wider

President Gustav Husak expressed profound thanks for the great hospitality accorded him

Jamahiriyah, and he addressed an invitation to Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi to visit 
Czechoslovakia; the invitation was accepted with thanks and its date is to be fixed later.
