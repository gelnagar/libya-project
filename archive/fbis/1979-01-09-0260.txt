LIBYA

AT-TURAYKI RECEIVES GAMBIAN FOREIGN, ECONOMIC MINISTERS 
LD0$2116Y Tripoli JANA in English 1740 GMT 8 Jan 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 8 January, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS ACGENCY--The Foreign Secretary Dr ‘Ali 

‘Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, received the Gambian Minister of External Affairs Mr Lamin 

K. Jabang who is accompanied by the minister of economic planning. Both are attending 

the meetings of the Libyan Arab-Gambian joint committee for economic and social cooperation, 


Attended by the director of the African department at the Foreign Secretariat and the 
ambassadors of both countries, the meeting discussed aspects of bilateral cooperation and 
the strengtheniig of relations and reviewed African issues and matters of common interests. 


Mr Lamin K, Jabang praised the efforts exerted by the Jamahiriyah to support African 
countries as well as the interest shown by the Jamahiriyah in reinforcing bilateral 
relations with Gambia in all fields,

LIBYAN, HUNGARIAN FOREIGN MINISTERS! SPEECHES REPORTED 
LDO72112Y Tripoli JANA in English 1840 GMT 7 Jan 79 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, Jan 7, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--Libyan Arab-Hungarian discussions to 
strengthen bilateral relations and cooperation started today at the Foreign Secretariat 

in Tripoli, Dr 'Ali 'Abd as-Salam at-Turayki, the foreign secretary, headed the Libyan 
Arab delegation, while Mr Frigyes Puja, foreign minister, who arrived in Tripoli yesterday, 
headed the Hungarian side, 


Dr at-“urayki delivered a speech at the start of the session in which he greeted the 
Huncarian foreign minister and his accompanying delegation. 


He said: "Our bilateral relations have undergone marked development during the past 
years through bilateral contacts, the last of which was Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's visit to 
Hungary last year, and which scored significant results through the discussions between 
Colonel al-Qadhdhafi and the Hungarian president." 


The foreign secretary pointed out that the relations between the two countries "are not 
ordinary relations but strategic ones, endorsed by the two peoples. Nor are they temporary 
relations, but continuous, not merely between the Jamahiriyah and Hungary but those of 

Arab progressive forces, represented by the Jamahiriyah, in the Arab nation, Africa and 
Asia."

He added: "Tripoli's conference of the steadfastness and confrontation front had affirmed 
these relations, while these discussions, which are a continuation of previous ones, will 
be an important stage in strengthening bilateral relations and cooperation, We, as two 
countries, are seriously seeking to develop these relations, consolidating them in the 
interest of the two friendly peoples." 


The foreign secretary expressed his hope "that the visit of the Hungarian foreign minister 
to the Jamahiriyah is another step contributing to the development of these relations, 

and enabling him to get acquainted with the achievements realised by the Libyan Arab 
people and to observe the democratic experience, the establishment of people's power 

and the achievement of prosperity and growth on the land of the Jamahiriyah." 


The Hungarian foreign minister replied in a speech expressing his joy at visiting the 
Jamahiriyah, and the warm welcome he was accorded on his arrival in Tripoli. 


He said: "The Hungarian Government and people highly appreciate the results of Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi's visit to Hungary, and that his talks affirmed the possibility of 
expanding cooperation and reinforcing relations between the two countries. Colonel 
al-Qadhdhafi's visit to Hungary had strongly reinforced the bilateral relations," 


The Hungarian foreign minister went on to say; "It is our duty to benefit from these 

possibilities in reinforcing relations to confront our mutual enemy, because our enemy 
is the same, and our friend is the same; it is therefore in the interest of our two 
peoples that he should cooperate with the progressive forces in the world," 


Concluding his speech, the Hungarian foreign minister expressed his country's desire to 
develop cooperation with the Jamahiriyah in all fields,
