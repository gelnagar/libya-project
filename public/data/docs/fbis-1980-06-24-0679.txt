STEADFASTNESS FRONT DENOUNCES EGYPTIAN 'AGGRESSION! 
LD231416 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 23 Jun 80 


[Text] The Political Committee of the National Front for Steadfastness and Confrontation 
this morning ended its emergency session at the Hotel al-Shati!? in Tripoli. The session 
was attended by the brother Socialist People's Arab Jamahiriyah [SPLAJ] foreign secretary, 
Deputy Syrian Prime Minister and Foreign Minister "Abd alwHalim Khaddan, Algerian Foreign 
Minister Mohamed Ben Yahia, PDRY Foreign Minister Salim Salih Muhammad, head of the PLO 
Political Committee Faruq Qaddumi and PLO official spokesman Abd al=-Muhsin Abu Mayzar, 


The committee has issued a statement which states: 


At the invitation of the brother SPLAJ foreign secretary, the Political Committee of the 
National Front for Steadfastness and Confrontation convened in an emergency session in Tripoli 
from 21 to 22 June 1980 to discuss the threats of the Egyptian regime against the SPLAJ in 

the form of the declaration of a state of emergency and military aggressive moves along the 
borders with the SPLAJ. 


The committee studied the current situation resulting from these aggressive provocative threats. 
The committee has established the truth of the analyses made by the front's summit con- 
ferences that the Stable David plot is continuing within the framework of a plan which U.S, 
imperialism and the Zionist entity are implementing using the AseSadat regime against the 

Arab nation in an attempt to subdue it by destroying its national and progressive achievements 
and by controlling.its destiny, looting its wealth and placing the Arab area under the 

umbrella of U.S, imperialism and Zionism, 


Following the As#Sadat regime®s betrayal of the cause of the Egyptian people and that of the 
Arab nation and its transformation into an obedient tool in the hands of the new aggressive 
alliance, the plan continued to deal blows and plot against the steadfastness front and the 
escalation of the struggle of the Palestinian Arab people in occupied Palestine using all 
terrorist methods including the forcing of the Palestine people out of their lands, the 
committing of acts of suppression and the murdering of sons and strugglers of the Palestinian 
people. 


The plan culminated in the transfer of the main thrust of the Egyptian armed forces from the 
frontline with the Zionist enemy, where they should be, to the Libyan frontier as a prelude 
to a premeditated aggressive scheme against the Arab people of Libya and the African 
liberation movement. 


Within the framework of this plan, AseSadat handed over the Arab land of Egypt to be turned 
into an airenavaleland base for America and Zionism,

The joint maneuvers staged by the various parties to the Stable David accord, coupled 

by intensive U.S. military aid to the Egyptian regime consisting of the most modern 
military hardware and the cooperation and coordination in the military field between 

the various parties to the Stable David accord in the establishment of aggressive military 
bases and the carrying out of provocations and flexing of muscles, provide the best proof 
of the gravity of this aggressive alliance and its serious threat to the security and 
safety of the area and the world, 


It has become evident that in this manner, the Egyptian regime has been trying to break 
the seige of isolation imposed on it on the Arab, Islamic and world level, It has 

also been trying to divert the eyes of the Egyptiin people away from the worsening 
problems from which Egypt has been suffering economically, politically and socially and 
to preoccupy the Egyptian Army and blind it to the indignation and grumbling that was 
mounting against the policy of the Egyptian regime, 


In view of all this, the committee wishes to reaffirm its denunciation and condemnation 
of As-Sadat!s aggressive move at the order of U.S. imperialism and in coordination with 
the Zionist enemy and declares the following: Adherence to the provisions of the 
declaration of the principles and objectives and institutions of the National Front of 
Steadfastness and Confrontation, Paragraph 6, Article 3, which provides that aggression 
on any member of the front is tantamount to an aggression against all members, 


The Political Committee announces the determination of the front!s members to check the 
plot in all its forms and extensions, and their firm stand on the side of the SPLAJ 
people with a view to checking and routing the agvrossion, 


The Political Committee strongly denounces the naval and air military provocations 
committed by the United States against the Libyan Arab people. It also denounces the 
American-Egyptian joint maneuvers and the flexing of muscles and the establishment of 
aggressive military bases in the region in accordance with the conspiratorial plan of 
the Stable David, 


The Political Committee is confident that the Arab people and army of Egypt, who have 
offered great sacrifices for the sake of their own national causes and those of their 
Arab nation, can never accept becoming a tool for the realization of the objectives of 
U.S. imperialism and Zionism, It is also confident that the plot which the Stable David 
parties are planning and seeking to implement will be foiled by the Arab nation, For 
aggression against the people of SPLAJ is tantamount to aggression against one of the 
bastions of steadfastness and confrontation and an additional treacherous stab added to 
the bloody successive stabs directed against this nation and is a mean attempt to silence 
the honorable pan-Arab and progressive voices which continue to rise in determined 
rejection of the capitulationist solutions of the Palestinian question and to call on 
the Arab nation to continue its struggle to destroy the plots and intrigues that are 
being matched against its historic past, present and future generations, 


The Political Committee wishes to draw attention again to the gravity of the course 
pursued by the ruler of Egypt, thereby implementing the policy of the Stable David 
parties, It urges the fraternal people of Egypt and its valiant army to put an end to 
this regime which has become a source of dangers which threaten not only the security 
and interests of the Egyptian people, but of the entire Arab nation.

Statement on Camp David 
LD231632 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 23 Jun 80 


[Text] The Political Committee of the National Front of Steadfastness and Confrontation 
has issued a statement about the escalation of the Stable David aggression against the 
Palestinian people. The following is the text of the statement: 


During the session of the Political Committee of the National Front of Steadfastness and 
Confrontation in Tripoli on 21 June 1980, the participants discussed the escalation by 

the Stable David parties, led by American imperialism, of their aggression against the 
Palestinian people in the form of the intensification of the expansionist colonization 
operations and the campaigns of repression, expulsion and officially organized terrori- 
zation carried out by the Zionist occupation authorities, including mass murder of members 
of our Palestinian Arab people and their national leaders within the framework of the 
implementation Of the plot of autonomous rule and the driving of the legitimate owners 
from their occupied homeland. 


The committee affirmed the fateful organic cohesion of the front's members with the 
Palestinian Arab people inside and outside the occupied homeland against the autonomous 
plot and the schemes of the Stable David parties. 


The committee decided to provide support for the steadfastness of the Palestinian Arab 
people in all fields to enable them to achieve their objectives of liberation, returr 

and the establishment of their independent state in Palestine under the leadership of 

the PLO, their sole legitimate representative. 


While greeting the heroic steadfastness of the Palestinian people in the occupied homeland 
and their continuing and escalating revolution against occupation and its designs, the 
Political Committee calls on the world public opinion and all the forces of liberation, 
progress, democracy and peace in the world to declare their effective solidarity with 

the Palestinian people and their leadership and to work for putting an end to the official 
organized Zionist terrorism.
