COMMENTARY ATTACKS U.S. MOTIVES ON LEBANON 


LD030826 Tripoli Voice of Arab Homeland Arabic 0115 GMT 3 May 81 
[Unattributed commentary ] 


[Excerpt] While the Lebanese crisis, in its early stages, was shrouded with ambiguity, 
the objectives behind the intensification of the situation there have now become 
officially clear. For the present, these objectives are confined to an American-Zionist 
plan aimed at the liquidation of the Palestine resistance through the partitioning of 
Lebanon. 


The U.S. secretary of state recently announced the setting up of a special team to watch 
and follow the developments in Lebanon. This American expression of concern has come, of 
course, following the escalation of the Syrian role against the Zionist enemy in Lebanon. 
An official of the U.S. State Department recently revealed that U.S. President Reagan and 
his secretary of state knew in advance of the Zionist aggressive plan and its objectives 

in Lebanon. The Kuwaiti paper AL-ANBA', reporting this, said that Reagan and his secretary 
of state have given the green light for the Zionist military intervention in southern 
Lebanon to create favorable conditions for American intervention in Arab affairs and 
particularly in Lebanon's domestic affairs. 


By examining recent news reports, observers would reach this same conclusion even if it 

was not revealed by the American officials: The American military presence has been daily 
mounting in the Arab area, and with the help of the regimes of the Arabs of America, it has 
been taking more important positions in the region. Indeed, nothing remained except for 
the United States to create, through the Lebanese crisis, a pretext for justifying to the 
world its intervention in the Arab homeland. Otherwise what is the reason for the 
declaration of a state of high alert in the U.S. State Department on the pretext of 
following the situation in Lebanon? 


Also, what is the reason for the British Embassy's call in Beirut to its citizens to 
think seriously of leaving Lebanon immediately. Although such a call is not of prime 
importance, on the face of it, it does prove that the British Embassy has information 
which points to an expected explosion. 


JANA COMMENTS ON ABU GHAZALAH'S WASHINGTON VISIT 


LDO31010 Tripoli JANA in English 0805 GMT 3 May 81 


[Text] Paris, 3 May (JANA)--General ‘Abd al-Halim Abu Ghazalah, the Egyptian defence 
minister, said that the fact that the Jamahiriyah possesses double the amount of arms 
than Egypt, poses it as a potential threat to Egypt. Speaking at an interview with the 
newspaper, ‘the Washington POST', General Abu Ghazalah said Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi, who 
visited Moscow this week, possesses sufficient finance as well as equipment and contacts 
with a super power. Furthermore, in a speech quoted by the official Egyptian MIDDLE EAST 
NEWS AGENCY, the defence minister confirmed that Egypt will be sanctioning the setting up 
of other U.S. military bases in addition to Ra's Banas base. A joint U.S.-Egyptian 
communique issued last Thursday indicated the need for speeding up the export of arms to 
Egypt which include F-16 planes and M-60 tanks. The communique also discusses a five- 
year programme to develop the Egyptian Armed Forces. 


Our political editor comments: This is how the Egyptian defence minister exposed his 
government's policy and denied As-Sadat's own statements. He pointed out that the five- 
year plan mentioned by As-Sadat will not achieve prosperity for the Egyptian people, nor 
will it provide more food for the poor and deprived or improve their make-shift housing 
Situation.

In fact, the five-year plan aims at amassing advanced arms of all types and includes a 
widescale project for destruction and destabilizing peace and security in Africa. We 
recall As-Sadat's statements to the Egyptian magazine, OCTOBER, in which he said all 
hatred between us and Israel is gone forever, and that there will be no war following 
the October war. We also remember this assertion that America and Britain are not 
imperialist countries and animosity against them should, therefore, cease. 


We wonder at the reason behind these advanced arms, the concentrated contacts with the 
U.S. administration and the joint military communiques. We wonder at the visits by the 
U.S. fleet to Egyptian ports. Does As-Sadat intend to throw his nets for Mediterranean 
fish or does he want to catch the whales of the Egyptian Sudanese Nile, which has become 
part of the As-Sadat empire? Any two sensible people will concede that this stock-piling 
of arms by As-Sadat, which he claims will not be used against Israel, U.S.A. or Britain, 
are intended to be used against the Libyan Arab people and their oil wealth. All the 
evidence and reports substantiate this strategy beyond doubt. 


A revolutionary outlook demands that everything should be placed in its right perspective 
so that those who are unaware of the truth of the situation should be enlightened. The 


revolutionaries cannot be taken by surprise. 


AL~QADHDHAFI RETURNS FROM USSR, SFRY VISITS 


LD011328 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 1 May 81 


[Excerpts] The commander of the revolution has concluded his successful visits to the 
USSR, Yugoslavia and Bulgaria. The visits took place during crucial times when the Arab 
nation is facing a ferocious imperialist offensive and when agent regimes have been 
falling in the laps of imperialism and colonialism and Arab territories have been opened 
before American forces, foreign bases and Zionist aggressions. 


The brother commander returned to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah at noon 
today, where he was welcomed at Tripoli international airport by Brother Staff Maj 'Atd 
al-Salam Ahmad Jallud, Lt Col Mustafa al-Kharrubi, Maj al-Khuwayldi al-Humaydi and the 
brother secretary and members of the General People's Committee. 


The brother commander left Belgrade airport this morning at the end of his visit to 
Yugoslavia. He was given a huge popular and official sendoff. Cvijetin Mijatovic, 
president of the SFRY and Brig Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, the commander in chief of the 

Libyan Armed Forces, were at hand to see him off. He was also seen off by members of the 
Federal Executive Council, the Libyan students and officials of the Libyan People's 
Bureau and the Arab community in Yugoslavia. 


The »rother commander of the revolution met this morning with Cvijetin Mijatovic at the 
place where the brother commander was staying in Belgrade. 


The brother commander sent a cable to Cvijetin Mijatovic on the occasion of the conclusion 
of his visit to Yugoslavia. The following is the text of the cable. 


As I leave your friendly country, I have the pleasure of expressing to you, to the 
Presidium and to the friendly Yugoslav people and government my ew for the great 
hospitality, cordiality and understanding we received. 


I am confident that the results we achieved in our talks will make a great contribution 
to bolstering cooperation between our two peoples. Our treatment of international issues 
will also make a contribution toward advancing the Nonaligned Movement and its role in 
promoting international peace. 


Please accept my utmost respects and appreciation. 


[Signed] Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi.

AL-WATAN INTERVIEWS AL-GADHDHAFI ON USSR TALKS 


LD011137 Kuwait AL-WATAN in Arabic 29 Apr 81 pp l1, 11 


[Shakir al-Jawhari report: "Al-Qadhdhafi Tells AL-WATAN in Moscow: ‘What Is Required 
Are Practical Soviet Stances in Lebanon; Moscow Is Apprehensive of the Gulf Cooperation 
Council'"] 


[Text] Moscow--Libyan Leader Mu'ammar al-Qadhdhafi has emphasized the need to strengthen 
the relations between the Arab states and the Soviet Union. However, he has told AL-WATAN 
that the stances of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front states and the Soviet Union 
are not identical. He said that the Soviet Union is a major power and our stances cannot 

be completely identical to its stances. The Libyan leader disclosed to AL-WATAN that Soviet 
leader Leonid Brezhnev and the Soviet leadership have finally understood Libya's stand in 
Chad. Moscow has declined to comment on the military aid which the Libyan Government has 
given so far to Chadian President Goukouni Oueddei. The Libyan leader said that the imperi- 
alist and colonialist confrontation by the United States, France and certain Arab regimes 
with the Libyan role in Chad and the Chadian National Government has prompted Moscow to 
reconsider its calculations because it has realized that Libya and Chad represent a serious 
threat to imperialist interests in the African Continent. 


Replying to a question as to whether the joint statement which is to be issued on his talks 
with Soviet leader Leonid Brezhnev will include a reference to the situation in Chad, he 

said that the discussions had not touched on this matter up to the time he gave this interview 
in the evening of the day before yesterday [27 April] after concluding the first session of 
discussions. 


Asked about the requests he has presented to the Soviet leadership in the name of the 
Steadfastness and Confrontation Front states, the Libyan leader said: I asked President 
Brezhnev to develop the Soviet Union's political and military attitude toward the 
Steadfastness and Confrontation Front. However, he refused to reveal the nature of the 
requests but confined himself to saying that what is requested of the Soviets are practical 
stances in Lebanon. He did not define the nature of these stance 


He said that he communicated to the Soviet leadership a request from PLO Executive Committee 
Chairman Yasir ‘Arafat to clarify the proposal made by Soviet leader Leonid Brezhnev for 
convening an international conference for a comprehensive settlement in the Middle East. He 
pointed out that ‘Arafat did not understand exactly what Brezhnev had meant by his proposal. 


He then referred to the different viewpoints between Libya and the Soviet Union in relation 
to the rejection of the two Camp David agreements and the Egyptian-Israeli conciliation 
[sulh] treaty. He said that Moscow rejects the two agreements and the treaty because they 
have led to a separate settlement between Egypt and the Zionist entity whereas Libya rejects 
a separate and a comprehensive political settlement alike. The Libyan leader emphasized that 
he will continue to assume the leadership of the Steadfastness and Confrontation Front until 
a new summit of the front states is convened. 


He pointed out that, in his capacity as current leader of the front, he has sent a number 

of letters--one to the Arab League requesting in the name of the front that Sudan be expelled 
from the league and that the same measures that have been applied to Egypt be applied to 
Sudan; another message to the Islamic Conference member states asking that Sudan's member- 
ship in the conference be suspended just as Egypt's membership has been suspended; and a 
third message to King Husayn informing him before visiting Moscow that he will not implement 
a former agreement between them--an agreement endorsed by the steadfastness and confrontation 
states’ summit held in Damascus--providing for Libyan funding of a Soviet arms deal with 
Jordan.

He said that he has informed King Husayn in his message that he will not abide by the 
agreement because King Husayn reneged on his obligations and did not permit the 
Palestinian resistance to return to Jordan and to operate against the Zionist entity 
from Jordanian territories. He added: Before we buy arms for Jordan, we must know in 
which direction the arms are to be used. 


Regarding the efforts being made to reconcile Muscat and Aden, Al-Qadhdhafi said that 
he is surprised at the talk about reconciliation because no reconciliation should take 
place with those who offer bases and facilities to the American forces. He added that 
Libya is diligently working to arm the Popular Front for the Liberation of Oman and to 
have it resume its military operations against the Omani Government. 


Replying to a question on the amount of response his request [talab] to the Soviet 
leadership has received--the request on the situation of the Arab Gulf in che light of 
the move to form American and British Rapid Deployment Forces and the condition made hy 
Kuwaiti Foreign Minister Shaykh Sahab al-Ahmad, he said that Kuwait, Libya and the 
Soviet Union are agreed on rejecting the idea of these forces but Moscow 1s apprehensive 
about the formation of the Gulf States Cooperation Council. 


He revealed to AL-WATAN that the Soviet Union has asked him officially to endeavor to 
persuade the Iranian leadership to change its hostile attitude toward the Afghan Govern- 
ment under the leadership of President Babrak Karmal and that Moscow is not satisfied 
with the Iranian attitude toward Afghanistan. However, he did not say how and when he 
will make this endeavor.
