TRIPOLI DEMONSTRATION AT U.S. EMBASSY REPORTED 
LD021317 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1255 GMT 2 Dec 79 LD 


[Text] This morning Tripoli witnessed an overwhelming student demonstration in soli- 
darity with the Iranian students and the revolution of the Iranian Muslim people in 
the fact of the imperialist colonialist provocations and ambitions. [chants faintly 
heard in the background] 


The demonstrations moved out from As-Sahah al-Khadra! Square and marched toward the 

U.S. Embassy in Tripoli. The demonstrators carried placards calling for the downfall 

of U.S. imperialism and affirming the inevitable victory of the Iranian Muslim Revolution 
against the imperialist and reactionary plots. 


Upon the arrival of the demonstrating students at the U.S. Embassy, they cheered for 
the downfall of attempts to impose hegemony by the United States and the allies and 
lackeys of imperialism. They also affirmed their solidarity with the Iranian people. 
They declared the Libyan Arab students! support for their brothers, the Iranian stu- 
dents, in their demand for the handing over of the fleeing shah to the Iranian people 
to put him on trial. 


The demonstrators set fire to effigies of the agent shah and called for the downfall 
of the defeated Egyptian regime and its defeated president who is in collusion with 
the butchers and executioners of peoplés. 


Demonstrators! Communique 
LD021538 Tripoli JANA in English 1500 GMT 2 Dec 79 LD 
[Excerpt] Tripoli, Dec 2, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The demonstrators issued a communi- 
que in which they affirmed Libyan student support for Iranian students until they 


achieve victory against colonialists ard their allies. 


The communique said America knows perfectly well the extent of the Muslim Iranian 
people's insistence for gaining freedom. The communique also said the U.S. is aware 
through its experience in Vietnam of the highly effective character of popular liber- 
ation movements. 


The communique affirmed the Libyan students! solidarity with world liberation movement 
and their support for those striving for freedom. 


The communique called upon peoples of the world to follow the Libyan Arab people!s 
lead where the masses now wield authority, dispose of wealth and carry arms, and where 
the people are masters of their own fate,

AL- QADHDHAFTI REPORTEDLY TO REQUEST RELEASE OF U.S. HOSTAGES 
PAQ11937 Madrid EFE in Spanish 1900 GMT 1 Dec 79 PA 


[Text] Milan (Italy), 1 Dec (EFE)--Libyan leader Mu!ammar al-Qadhdhafi will hand a 
message to an Iranian delegation now in Tripoli asking the Ayatollah Khomeyni to re- 
lease the hostages in the U.S. Embassy in Tehran. 


This was announced by the chief of the revolution himself during an exclusive inter- 
view with Italian reporter Oriana Fallaci that will be published tomorrow in CORRIERE 
DELLA SERA of Milan. 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi voiced his conern over recent military movements by the United 
States and said "if all this could really be the beginning of a third world war, we 
must use all our power to stop it from happening." 


"I am trying to convince the Iranians," he pointed out, "to release the hostages. 

A group of persons very close to Khomeyni has arrived in Tripoli. I will give them 
a personal message for the imam asking for the release of the hostages, because the 
situation is becoming increasingly dangerous." 


Colonel al-Qadhdhafi said he was "personnally against" the U.S. Embassy incidents, 
although he said "it is necessary to understand that revolutions are sometimes chaotic." 


"In Iran," he added, "there is not even a government. The students are acting without 
government control. The embassy incident is not a normal conflict between states and 
cannot be judge according to traditional criteria, and even less can it be confronted 
with traditional methods." 


In any event, the Libyan leader told Oriana Fallaci, "when officials of an embassy 
commit acts not in line with their duties that are harmful to the host country, re- 
actions like those of Tehran can be unleashed." 


Mutammar al-Qadhdhafi then pointed out that the tension in the Islamic world is an 
"international revolution against the United States." All this is happening, he added, 
"because all people, not only Muslims, hate North America." 


Despite admitting that everything happening in Iran is the exclusive business of the 
Iranians, Al-Qadhdhafi cautioned that "in the event of a U.S. attack, we will not re- 
main standing with our hands tied." 


On the other hand, the Libyan leader recognized the present close relationship between 
Libya and USSR, to the point of not discounting Tripoli's eventual membership in the 
Warsaw Pact. 


The possibility of an OPEC boycott of the dollar was also mentioned in the interview, 
with the Libyan leader voicing support for it. The "antidollar rejection front," he 
explained, would be comprised of Iran, Iraq, Algeria, Libya and also Saudi Arabia, 
Kuwait and the Arab Emirates.

"There is no doubt the moment has come to utilize petroleum as a political weapon, 
and we are in that [word indistinct]," he stated, threatening that if the United 
States freezes Libyan furds deposited in US. banks, "we would react vigorously, even 
with a petroleum blockade." 


In Europe, Qadndhafi added, only the nations supporting the U.S. policy would be in- 
cluded in a sanction of this type. 


Ending on the subject of terrorism, Al-Qadhdhafi said it is "the result of capitalist 
society." When queried about the origin of Soviet arms in the hands of the Red 
Brigades, he said: "We are not responsible for how the Palestinians use the arms 

we give them. We deliver arms to them because we believe in their cause and consider 
it our duty to help them. What happens after that is not my business." 


TRIPOLI RADIO REPORTS BAHAMAS REJECTS SHAH'S ENTRY 
LD030028 Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 1 Dee 79 LD 


[Text] Monte Carlo Radio reported Saturday that the Bahaman authorities have expressed 
their refusal to receive the deposed shah in their territory after it was reiterated 
recently that he is likely to leave the United States for the Bahamas Islands. 


It is known that last week Mexico in its turn declared its categorical »efusal to allow 
the shah to return to its territory. 


Reports Departure From U.S. 
LDO22004 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1230 GMT 2 Dec 79 LD 


[Text] The deposed shah today left the United States for an unknown destination. 
Press reports say it is likely that the shah may proceed to Egypt after the Bahaman 
authorities refused to receive him. 


On the other hand, the Iranian students who occupy the U.S. Embassy in Tehran con- 
firmed that the hostages would be presented for trial if the deposed shah was not 
handed over to Iran. At a press conference they held yesterday, the students said 
that the hostages were under their protection and that no harm would befall them. 
Nevertheless, if any danger to the sovereignty of Iran loomed the hostages would be 
subject to death [t!dam]. During the press conference the Iranian students showed 
new documents proving that the American Embassy had not been a diplomatic mission but 
a den of spies. 


The Islamic Revolution Guards in Iran have organized training sessions for Iranian 
youth on arms and the defense of the Islamic Revolution should America carry out an 
aggression against it. Yesterday the Iranian radio requested the army to open the 
camps for all the Iranian people so that they may acquaint themselves with modern 
weapons and military matters. 


AL-QADHDHAFI CALLS ON PALESTINIANS TO DESTROY SUEZ CANAL 

NC301149 Paris AFP in English 1145 GMT 30 Nov 79 NC 

[Text] Paris, Nov 30, (AFP)--Libyan leader Mulammar al-Qadhdhafi today called on the 
Palestinian organisations to destroy the Suez Canal and threaten shipping in the Bab 
El Mandab Straits leading into the Red Sea. 

Colonel al-Qadhdhafi, quoted by the Libyan news agency monitored here, said "the canal 


must be destroyed because it has become, through the traitor (Egyptian President Anwar) 
As-Sadat, an artery which supplies the (Zionist) enemy with weapons and goods."

The Palestinians should also destroy Arab oil wells, unless they were used for the 
benefit of the Palestinians, colonel al-Qadhdhafi said. Otherwise, the oil "can only 
serve to supply the enemy and his allies." 


Palestinian action on any other front than the Suez, Bab El Mandeb and oil fronts 
would be "contrary to revolutionary [word indistinct] and inappropriate", he said. 


The Palestinian masses should have freedom of action in all Arab countries, the 
Colonel added, in a speech marking the second anniversary of an international 
Patestinian solidarity day. 


"The Palestinian masses in Libya are freetoact," he said. "They can take revolution- 
ary action in the way they prefer." He urged those in Libya to set up revolutionary 
comiittees and unify the offices of various Palestinian nationalist groups.
