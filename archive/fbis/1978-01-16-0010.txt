LIBYA

PAPER CRITICIZES AS-SADAT, 'LACKEY NUMAYRI'! 
LD131534Y¥ Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1015 GMT 13 Jan 78 LD 


[Excerpts] Tripoli--Under the title: Another plot against the Libyan people, AL-ISBU' 
AS-SIYASI in its issue today writes: 


As-Sadat paid a visit to Sudan and renewed his alliance of intrigue with his lackey 
Numayri,; he renewed his pact and his alliance of intrigue. The ruler of Egypt during 

his meetings with Numayri was joined by the vanquished Lt Gen Muhammad ‘Abd al-Ghani 
al-Jamasi, Egyptian minister of war and war production. They claimed that there was a 
Libyan-Soviet plot being hatched against Sudan as a coverup for a forthcoming aggression 
being planned by the ruler of Egypt against the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 
Moreover, the threads of intrigue followed hell-bent by the spy ruler of Egypt have entered 
a grave stage and the Egyptian information media began to bless agression against the 
Libyan people. 


It appears that this spy has not been deterred by the severe blow dealt him by the Libyan 
people so this time he needs a harsher blow. If the first beating did not bring him back 

to his senses and did not make him understand the truth about the Libyan people, then 

we tell him: Go ahead and try and see what awaits you. As-Sadat by his behavior has proved 
that he is a dog who loves only the one who throttles him, for he does not get fed up of 
bowing to 'Ezer Weizman, the Zionist minister of war. Weizman has spent his life killing 
Egyptians and now devotes his time to personally humiliating As-Sadat. 


We address the Egyptian people and the Egyptian Army to warn them against the destructive 
abyss which this Zionist spy is leading them toward, because after losing them their 
freedom and dignity and surrendering their lands, today he is taking them their freedom 
and dignity and surrendering their land; today he is taking them into a hostile adventure 
against a sister people the consequences of which will be very dark for the Egyptians. 

We address the sons of the Egyptian people to save themselves from this tyrant who is 
leading them like a flock of sheep to a deadly end. Once again we say that any hostile 
move by this tyrant in Egypt will receive our severe retaliation and we shall let nim 
know who the Libyan people are arid how retaliation is taken against the plotters. 


V. 16 Jan 78 NORTH AFRICA 


JANA REPORTS ARRIVAL OF U.S. SENATOR FOR VISIT 


LD160942Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 Gv? 16 Jan 78 LD 


[Text] Tripoli, 16 January--U.S. Senator James McClure arrived in Tripoli last night 
on a visit of ten days at the invitation of the foreign liaison bureau of the People's 
General Congress, He was welcomed at Tripoli international Airport by the head of the 
bureau and members of his staff, 


McClure told JANA that the basic aim of his visit was to bolster relations between the 
Libyan Arab people and the American people and exchange views with the general secretariat 
of the People's General Congress.
