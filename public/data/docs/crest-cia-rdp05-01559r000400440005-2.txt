Summary (AI): An FBIS translation of part four of a February 1990 interview with Qadhdhafi on Arab-Arab affairs. He discusses Ethiopia, Somalia and Sudan and warns that ethnic and national strife could spread across the Arab world. It contains nothing specific to Tripoli.
• Qadhdhafi says the Green Book's predictions about nationalities have come true in the Soviet Union and will continue throughout the world.
• He compares Ethiopia to Chad, describing it as a state made up of various nationalities torn apart by national strife.
• He denounces Garang's call for a 'revolutionary Sudan' that is neither Arab nor Islamic.
• He argues that if the separatist design succeeds in Sudan it will spread to Egypt, Syria, Iraq and the Arab Maghreb.

((TEXT)) IBRAHIM NAFI': WHAT ABOUT ETHIOPIA AND SUDAN? 

COL. AL-QADHDHAFI: ACCORDING TO THE GREEN BOOK, NATIONALITIES 
FINALLY TRIUMPH. ANY COUNTRY WITH VARIOUS NATIONALITIES WILL BE 
TORN APART. THIS IS THE AGE OF THE MASSES. THE GREEN BOOK'S 
PREDICTIONS, WHICH HAVE COME TRUE IN THE SOVIET UNION, WILL CONTINUE 
THROUGHOUT THE WORLD. THE SO-CALLED ETHIOPIA IS LIKE CHAD. IT IS 
NOT ONE NATION. IT IS MADE UP OF VARIOUS NATIONALITIES. NOW, IT IS 
BEING TORN APART BY NATIONAL STRIFE. YOU KNOW THAT THE MAP OF THE 
WORLD WILL BE FINALLY DRAWN BY NATIONAL STRIFE. THERE ARE DIFFERENT 
PEOPLES IN ETHIOPIA. THERE ARE THE PEOPLE WHO CARRIED OUT THE 
REVOLUTION, THERE ARE THE ERITREAN PEOPLE, THERE ARE THE PEOPLE OF 
OGADEN, AND SO ON. IN SOMALIA, THE PROBLEM IS THAT OF POWER 
STRUGGLE. THIS IS THE AGE OF THE MASSES, AND THE MASSES ARE CAPABLE 
OF WRESTING POWER. THEY DO NOT LIKE TO BE RULED BY ONE PARTY OR BY 
AN ARMY OR A PARLIAMENT. THEY DO NOT EVEN LIKE TO BE RULED BY THE 
PROLETARIAT. THROUGH SUCH CONFLICTS, THE PARTIES THAT HAVE

THE GULF TO THE ((PACIFIC)) OCEAN IS BASED ON RACE AND FATEFUL 
AFFILIATION. ARABISM, WHICH RUNS THROUGH THE VEINS OF EVERY MAN IN 
THIS AREA, HAS MADE HIM AN ARAB IN TERMS OF AFFILIATION AND DESTINY. 
IN ADDITION, THE RACIAL UNITY CONSTITUTES THE BACKBONE. IT IS VERY 
STRANGE THAT ALL THIS IS BEING RECONSIDERED. DEFINITELY IF THEY 
SUCCEED IN SUDAN, THEY WILL MOVE TO EGYPT. IF THEY SUCCEED IN 
EGYPT, THEY WILL MOVE TO SYRIA. THEN, THEY WILL MOVE TO IRAQ AND 
THE ARAB MAGHREB. WHEN IDENTITY BECOMES BASED ON BEING AN ARAB, 
MUSLIM, CHRISTIAN, OR MARONITE, THE ENTIRE ARAB ENTITY WOULD 
COLLAPSE. THEREFORE, THERE MUST BE A SERIOUS STAND ALONGSIDE SUDAN. 
SUCH A STAND, FOR EGYPT, IS CONSIDERED SELF-DEFENSE. WHEN THE 
AFRICANS TRY TO ACHIEVE INDEPENDENCE AND FORM A NEW GROUP IN SOUTH 
SUDAN, THIS WOULD CONSTITUTE AN INFLUENCE AREA FOR A BIG POWER. IT 
IS MORE ADEQUATE FOR THEM TO REMAIN WITHIN A UNIFIED SUDAN. I MEAN 
THE ISSUE SHOULD BE DEALT WITH SERIOUSLY--BY MEANS OF PEACE OF WAR-- 
BECAUSE IT IS DANGEROUS.
