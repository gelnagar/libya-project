LIBYA

JALLUD RETURNS TO TRIPOLI FROM VISITS TO PRC, DPRK, PAKISTAN 
LD181210Y Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 18 Aug 78 LD 


[Excerpt] Tripoli--Yesterday evening Staff Major 'Abd as-Salam Jallud returned to Tripoli 
from Pakistan on the completion of his official 2-week visit to the PRC, the DPRK and 
Pakistan.
