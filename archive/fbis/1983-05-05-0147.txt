LIBYA

Al-Qadhdhafi Demands ‘Full Cargo’ on Planes 
LDO50033 Tripoli Voice of Greater Arab Homeland In Arabic 
2315 GMT 4 May 83 


[Text] Brother Leader Colonel Mu’ammar Al-Qadhdhafi, leader 
of the Great | September Revolution, yesterday evening 
[Wednesday | called the Brazilian ambassador to the Jamahiriyah 
and told him that he is not a traditional president and has nothing 
to do with the ambassadors of foreign countries. Their contacts 
are with the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison which the [word 
indistinct] Arab people has commissioned [words indistinct]. 


Brother colonel [words indistinct] in view of the subject of the 
formation of Libyan aircraft and the Brazilian Government 
treatment of it was insulting to the Libyan people and constituted 
interference in its internal affairs. Also, the behavior of the 
Brazilian Government was far from reasonable and acceptable 
limits. So, accordingly, the brother colonel informed the 
Brazilian ambassador to tell his government that SPLAJ [Social- 
ist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah] firmly refuses to receive 
the cargo from the aircraft formation carried on any means of 
transportation other than the Libyan aircraft which are in Brazil, 
and that the Libyan aircraft formation was ordered not to leave 
Brazil's Manaus airport in particular, except with its full cargo. 
The Brazilian Government has the right to do what it wishes, 
within its borders, concerning the aircraft formation and crew, 
and Libya reserves its right to do what it wishes as well. In 
addition to that, the People’s Bureau for Foreign Liaison will 
issue a Statement to the shipping and air companies not to commit 
themselves to transport the cargo of the Libyan aircraft forma- 
tion, because Libya will not receive it, except on the aircraft 
formation which carried them. The brother colonel added during 
his meeting with the ambassador that since Brazil hastaken the 
side of imperialism against the Nicaraguan people and isstanding 
against the freedom of the peoples and against the freedom of the 
Libyan people, then it is only right that the aircraft formation be 
allowed to return to the Jamahiriyah carrying its full cargo. 


It is known that Brazil used crew from Iraq and 16 Americans 
in opening the Libyan aircraft and searching and emptying them. 
Also, the Brazilian Government refused to return the cargo on 
the Libyan aircraft and asked to rent transport from other 
countries. 


JANA on Aims, Possible Results of Shultz Tour 
LD042146 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 1750 GMT 4 May 83 


[Text] Tripoli, 4 May (JANA) — U:S. Secretary of State George 
Shultz is continuing a tour in the Arab region, through which the 
U.S. Adminsitration aims to impose its control and hegemony on 
the region. 


The U.S. Administration sent its secretary of state to the Arab 
region in a desperate attempt to mend its aggressive policy's 
deterioration and collapse at the hands of Latin American peo- 
ples and the defeats dealt it by the peoples of Central America.
