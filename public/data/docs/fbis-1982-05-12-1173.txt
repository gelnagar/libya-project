LIBYA

JANA CITES 'SECRET' MUBARAK-FAHD MEETING 


LD120854 Tripoli JANA in English 0800 GMT 12 May 82 


[Text] Beirut, 12 May (JANA) -- AM-MUBARIT Lebanese magazine revealed yesterday that a 
secret meeting took place between Egyptian President Mubarak and Saudi Crown Prince Fahd 
in Muscat during Mubarak's latest visit to the city. 


The magazine said brief talks were held between Mubarak and Fahd, attended by King Qabus, 
and stressed it is not their first meeting as both met just before the assassination of 
traitor As-Sadat. 


Following As-Sadat's execution, both met last March to conclude a strategic agreement 
between Egypt and Saudi rulers to be implemented after the alleged Zionist withdrawal 
from Sinai. 


The strategic plan stipulates that Saudi Arabia be a platform for any political moves 

in the region in an attempt to spread Saudi family's power over developments in the 
region. This will also put pressure on pro-Saudi Arab regimes to resume relations with 
Egypt, gain approval of the capitulatory Fahd plan which would be achieved through a Fes 
or Riyadh summit and economic pressure, on some Arab countries. 


Saudi Arabia in turn would put pressure on the PLO to ease its opposition to a resumption 
of relations with Egypt. 


The U.S. administration has played a role in this new move adopted by Saudi family which 
includes the formation of an axis, involving Jordan, Morocco, Somalia, Sudan, Oman, 
Egypt and Saudi Arabia. 


This axis would confront Steadfastness and Confrontation Front countries. 


SOVIET DEPUTY AGRICULTURE MINISTER ARRIVES 


LD112034 Tripoli JANA in English 1748 GMT 11 May 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 11 May (JANA) -- The Soviet deputy agriculture minister and joint head 
of the Libyan Arab-Soviet joint agricultural committee and his accompanying delegation 
arrived here today. 


The guest informed JANA's correspondent that his visit is within framework of consolidating 
and developing bilateral cooperation and strengthening cooperation between the two peoples 
in various fields, particularly in agriculture.
