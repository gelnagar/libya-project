LIBYA

POLISARIO OFFICIAL MAKES TRIPOLI ARRIVAL STATEMENT 
LD101056 Tripoli JANA in English 0941 GMT 10 Dec 79 LD 
[Text] Tripoli, Dec 10, JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY--The Secretary General of the Liberation 
Front of Saguia el Hamra and Rio de Oro Mr Mahamed Abdul Aziz arrived in Tripoli last 
night on a visit to the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah. 


He and his delegation were welcomed by the head of the Arab Liaison Bureau and a repre- 
sentative of the Protocol Department at the Foreign Secretariat. 


In a statement to the JAMAHIRIYAH NEWS AGENCY Mr Abdul Aziz expressed his pleasure at this 
visit, praising the support and effective help offered by the Jamahiriyah to the just 
struggle of the Saharan people.
