Summary (AI): A January 1989 Tripoli TV broadcast of a Qadhdhafi speech to a Basic People's Congress in al-Jabal al-Akhbar municipality, in which he calls for abolishing several state bodies, including the Jamahiriya Security Service, the Jamahiri Sports committees and the JANA news agency. The speech contains no Tripoli site-specific facts.
• Qadhdhafi said the Jamahiriya Security Service and the Bureau of Investigations have overlapping functions and that the Security Service must be abolished because complaints against it are mounting.
• He said the Jamahiriya Security Service competes with the Foreign Ministry and that its violations are increasing.
• He announced plans to abolish the JANA news agency, which he said had 400 employees and should be reduced to 19.
• He said the Jamahiri Sports committees had failed and that about 1,900 civil servants working on them should go home.
• He said about 198,000 cars had been imported and that the companies importing cars should be abolished.
