TUNISIA, LIBYA SIGN ECONOMIC AGREEMENTS 
LD151512 Tunis TAP in English 1453 GMT 15 Sep 81 


[Text] Tunis, 15 Sep (TAP)--A Tuniso-Libyan trade agreement was signed on Monday 
in Tunis by Tunisia's national economy Minister Abdelaziz Lasram and his Libyan 
counterpart, Abu Zayd "Umar Durdah, at the end of a working visit the Libyan 
official is paying in Tunisia. 


Completing the agreements concluded between the two countries in 1973, 1977 and 
1979, this agreement relates to the fields of industry, tourist-trade, transport 
and communications and electricity. 


In the industrial field, the agreement stressed both sides' interest in the 
achievement of joint industrial projects. Specialized commissions are to be 
created to examine the possibilities of achieving industrial [words indistinct] 
notably in matters of phosphate fertilizers, industry and petrochemical industries. 


As regards tourist trade, Tunisia undertook to train a great number of Libyan 
tourist cadres in Tunisian specialized schools. 


The agreement also provides for the organization of the Tunisian skilled workers' 
emigration to Libya and for the creation of a joint company to facilitate the 
travelling vf both countries’ tourists and the obtainment of visas for the two 
countries' nationals. 


In the field of transport and communications, the agreement provides for more 
flights between Tunisia and Libya, for the creation of a commission meant to 
reactivate the project of joint maritime company and for the reestablishment and 
strengthening of telephone lines between the two countries. 


As regards the field of electricity, the agreement provides [word indistinct] the 
connection of both countries electrical networks. The cost of this operation 
is estimated at 40 million Tunisian dinars (1 Tunisian dinar equals 2 dollars).
