Benghazi is not in the Tripoli area, and the note mentions Tripoli port only as the usual place for Soviet arms shipments.
• Ship is a Soviet Kapitan Kushnarenko-class merchantship.
• Nine Fishbed type C crates were on deck; two possible Fishbed component crates in the fifth hold.
• Note says Libyans have not purchased Fishbed for some time, opting for the more sophisticated Flogger aircraft.
• Coordinates given for Banghazi: 32-07-14N 020-03-07E.

ARMS TRANSSHIPMENT — 
BANGHAZL PORT ES, LIBYA 

1. (S/D) SIGNIFICANCE: SOVIET ARMS TRANSSHIPMENT IS UNDERWAY. 

2. (S/D) REMARKS: A SOVIET KAPITAN KUSHNARENKO-CLASS MERSHIP WITH 
FISHBED AIRCRAFT CRATES ON BOARD IS AT BANGHAZI PORT 
FACILITIES. THIS SHIP, WHICH IS OFF-LOADING CARGO, HAS NINE 
FISHBED TYPE C CRATES ON DECK AND TWO POSSIBLE FISHBED 
COMPONENT CRATES IN THE FIFTH HOLD. A FLOATING CRANE, WITH ITS. 
BOOM EXTENDED OVER THE CARGO HATCHES, IS MOORED ALONG THE 
STARBOARD SIDE OF THE SHIP. HOWEVER, NO AIRCRAFT-ASSOCIATED 
CRATES ARE DISCERNIBLE ON THE QUAY OR IN THE PORT i 
KAPITAN KUSHNARENKO-CLASS WAS PRESENT ON > BUT 
HEAVY CLOUDS PRECLUDED IDENTIFICATION OF ANY DECK CARGO. 

(S/D) THE FISHBED CRATES MAY BE DESTINED FOR SOME COUNTRY OTHER 
THAN LIBYA. SOVIET ARMS SHIPMENTS TO LIBYA ARE NORMALLY 
PROCESSED AT TARABULUS PORT FACILITIES . IN 
ADDITION, THE LIBYANS HAVE NOT PURCHASE BED FOR SOME TIME, 

OPTING FOR THE MORE SOPHISTICATED FLOGGER AIRCRAFT.
