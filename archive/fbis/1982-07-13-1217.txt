LIBYA

JANA COMMENTS ON U.S., SAUDI ROLES IN LEBANON 


LD130922 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 13 Jul 82 


[Text] Tripoli, 13 Jul (JANA) -- According to news reports, Algerian President Chadli 
Bendjedid and Saudi King Fahd have contacted Syrian President Hafiz al-Asad, asking him 
to accept moving the Palestinians from Beirut to Damascus and Fahd has tried to convince 
President al-Asad to aceept moving the Palestinian Resistance to Syria on the pretext 

of rescuing Palestinian citizens and the Lebanese people from the Zionist invading forces. 


The JANA political editor comments: 


Evidence, facts and convictions that have emerged and become clear to Arab citizens are 
that the Zionists are being backed militarily, politically and economically by the United 
States to occupy more Arab territory and capture its resources, to destroy the Palestinian 
Resistance and to crush the will to fight and the challenge of the Arab fighters. Hence, 
serious acceptance to move the Palestinian Resistance from Beirut to Damascus will not 
alter anything in the American-Zionist aims because the logic and the goal of the Zionist 
enemy is to pursue the Palestinian Resistance wherever it may be, whether in Beirut or 

in Damascus or in any other Arab capital. 


In the same way the Zionists chased the Palestinian forces from southern Lebanon to the 
capital of Lebanon itself, it is with the same aggressive logic the Zionists will pursue 

the Palestinians to Damascus. Accepting this Zionist logic will give the enemy the right 

to strike at and invate any Arab capital in which the Palestinian Resistance is to be found. 
The series of events makes it clear to us that the sole Arab posture towards this aggres- 
sive Zionist logic is to confront the Zionist invaders, to fight them and to move the 
battle to real fronts, to break the siege of Beirut and to crush and drive back the 

Zionist invaders from all Lebanese territory. Let the reply be: to fight the enemy from 
trench to trench, street to street and town to town; this is the only position the Arabs 
must adopt in confronting the Zionist racist presence.

The political editor continues: King Fahd ibn ‘Abd al-'Aziz Sa'ud (?is definitely) 
directly responsible for realizing the political aims and for conducting the orchestra on 
behalf of America and the Zionists in turning the PLO into a political entity devoid of 
all power. He is delegated [the task] of distorting Syria's steadfastness and his request 
for the moving of the Palestinian Resistance from Beirut falls within this framework. 


The political editor says, moreover, that Fahd personally, in agreement with the Zionists 
and the separatists, is responsible for the emergence of a crusade regime [as received] 
in Lebanon to confront the Muslims who challenged the Zionist invasion.
