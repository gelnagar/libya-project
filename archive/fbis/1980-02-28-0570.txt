LIBYA

AL-QADHDHAFI EXPRESSES SATISFACTION WITH GISCARD'S REMARKS 


LD271657 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 1600 GMT 27 Feb 80 LD 


[Text] In a statement to JANA, the brother leader of the revolution expressed his satis- 
faction at the statement made by French President Giscard d'Estaing last night over 
French television in which he confirmed the withdrawal of the French forces from Tunisia 
and asserted that what happened in Tunisia was purely an internal development. 


The brother leader also welcomed the French president's call to make the Mediterranean 
a sea of peace and stability. He affirmed that Libya will be the first to support such 
acall. He said that this statement in itself is sufficient to stop the deliberation 
on French intervention in Tunisia since this problem is now over. Moreover, he said, 
the possibilities ofa grave confrontation with France, which did exist, are over, He 
added that there is no longer any objection to the French president's visit to the Arab 
Gulf countries, which he has planned.

In his statement the leader of the revolution stressed thai the confrontation with the 
Tunisian regime is also over with the collapse of Hedi Nouira, who is considered a 

symbol of this regime, provided that he is replaced by the opposition government for the 
restoration of the fraternal relations between the two brotherly countries. He emphasized 
that the opposition government would work to open for the Tunisian people the road to 
progress and the unity of the peoples of the Arab nation and to keep foreign influence 
away from ‘them, 


The leader of the revolution concluded his statement by saying: If this is not achieved 
then I fear the escalation of the confrontation--this confrontation which is led by the 
revolutionary committees--with the Tunisian regime.

AL-HAWADITH: TERRORIST GROUPS TO LIQUIDATE AL-QADHDHAFI FOES 


LD271633 London AL-HAWADITH in Arabic 22 Feb 80 p 22 LD 


(Unattributed report: "!Mafia! for the Liquidation of Al-Qadhdhafi's Opponents Abroad" ] 


(Text ] Last week an unidentified man described as Libyan-looking set fire to a bookshop 
in Park Lane, London, because it had been distributing a bulletin entitled "AL-JIHAD"® 
published by the Libyan opposition. A number of distributors of this bulletin have also 
been threatened by unidentified persons. It has been said that a sum of money (estimated 
at 7,000 pounds sterling) has been paid to a newspaper distributor to dispose of the 
Arabic newspapers and magazines which criticize Colonel al-Qadhdhafi's regime. 


According to information reaching the Libyan opposition, Al-Qadhdhafi!'s intelligence 

has agreed with several groups of international terrorists (Mafia) to liquidate Libyan 
opposition leaders, The revolutionary committees will provide these gangs with the 
necessary information to carry out their plans, This is being done in accordance with 

a recommendat ion--adopted by the revolutionary committees and published in one of the 
regime's newspapers called AL-JIHAD--providing for the physical liquidation of the regime's 
opponents in Europe, It is the first such decision that an offial authority has taken 
publicly and published in itsnewspapers, saying that it will liquidate its opponents 
abroad.
