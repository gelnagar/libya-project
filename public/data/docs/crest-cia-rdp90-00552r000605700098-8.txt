Summary (AI): A Washington Post article reports on the trial in Fort Collins, Colorado, where Libyan dissident Faisal Zagallai identified former Green Beret Eugene Tafoya as the man who shot him in the head in October 1980, an attempted assassination the prosecution says was directed by Qaddafi's regime. Zagallai describes his student opposition activities and his fear of Qaddafi's threats.
• Prosecutors claim the 1980 shooting was an attempted assassination directed by Qaddafi's regime; the defense says Tafoya went to deliver a CIA warning.
• In February 1980, Qaddafi reportedly ordered all Libyan students abroad to return or face execution.
• Zagallai said he lost his Libyan scholarship in 1979 and bought a pistol for protection.
• Zagallai admitted he probably cocked his gun but insisted he never fired it.
• 1980-10-19: Faisal Zagallai was shot twice in the head in his apartment by a man posing as a job recruiter, identified at trial as Eugene Tafoya.
• 1976: Zagallai said he participated in taking over the Libyan Embassy in the United States.
• 1973: After Zagallai helped organize a student conference in Libya, six students were reportedly hanged by the regime. (6 killed)
