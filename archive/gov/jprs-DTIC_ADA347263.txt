LIBYA

Tripoli  Real  Estate  Chief  Calls  For  Adherence  to 
Property  Laws 

45040059a  Tripoli  AL-SHA  TT22  Dec  87  p  3 

[Interview  with  Mukhtar  Mahfuz,  chairman  of  Socialist 
Real  Property  Registration  Department,  Tripoli  Munic¬ 
ipality:  “An  Appeal  to  our  Brother  Citizens  To  Record 
and  Document  Their  Real  Properties”] 

[Text]  In  a  press  interview  which  AL-SHATT  carried 
out,  Mr  Mukhtar  Mahfuz,  chairman  of  the  Socialist  Real 
Property  Registration  Department  of  Tripoli  municipal¬ 
ity,  talked  about  a  number  of  important  issues  which 
concern  Libyan  Arab  citizens,  especially  since  these 
issues,  which  were  presented  in  this  conversation,  are 
related  to  a  number  of  sensitive  considerations  which 
have  a  connection  to  the  life,  future  and  family  of  the 
citizen.
