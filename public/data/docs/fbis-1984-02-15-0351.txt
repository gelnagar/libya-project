LIBYA

JANA Comments on Mubarak, Husayn U.S. Visit 
LD142210 Tripoli JANA in English 1807 GMT 14 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada al Ulla 11, Feb 14, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The Reagan administration undertook to 
guarantee the survival of King Husayn’s regime as well as insure 
the continuation of the same line of policy based on peaceful 
coexistance with the Zionist enemy and serving the imperialist 
interests in the region. 


The head of the U.S. Administration last night pledged his 
obligations towards the security and safety of the monarchist 
regime in Jordan and said this commitment will not change 


For his part, King Husayn said: “Our aims and objectives ar¢ 
identical,” and stressed to the American President the Jordanian 
monarchy’s commitment to continue the hostile anti-Syrian 
policy. King Husayn did not ask for a guarantee of U.S. protec 
tion of his regime in fear of the Zionist enemy, but of Syria as he 
told U.S. officials who praised his regime for siding with Iraq in 
its war against Iran and his acceptance of plans and initiatives 
formulated by the Reagan administration. 


A senior American official said that King Husayn told Reagan 
of his satisfaction with the rapproachment between the Iraqi and 
Egyptian regimes, and with the recent U.S. initiatives on Iraq 
which aim to strengthen Iraq’s shaky position in the war against 
the Islamic revolution in Iran in return for Iraq's vassalage to 
U.S. policy in the area. 


King Husayn of Jordan and President Mubarak of Egypt joined 
in asking the U.S. Administration for more military and eco 
nomic aid to rescue the deteriorating situation in both countries, 
and also joined their voices to ask for U.S. protection against the 
wrath of their people for their capitulationist policies in Egypt 
and Jordan. 


The Reagan administration considers the Jordanian king and the 
Egyptian president stooges who respond to U.S. policies and 
objectives in the region, and that the U.S. will be able to use both 
of them in the service of its hostile aims and plans in order to 
achieve the results desired in the strategic cooperation agreement 
between the U.S. and the Zionist enemy. This reactionary axis 
will be linked to the U.S. wagon as a partner in carrying out 
Reagan’s capitulatory plan, which was endorsed by Yasir ‘Arafat 
enthusiastically so as to reach his dream of building his state on 
the West Bank and Gaza Strip 


Mubarak’s and King Husayn’s visit to Washington and their 
reception by Reagan in another conspiracy against the Arab 
nation cannot be viewed except from one angle; namely, a visit 
devoted to undermine the Arab nation and serve the U.S.- Zionist 
plan against the interests and objectives of this nation

NEWS AGENCY Brigadier Abu Bakr Yunis Jabir, com- 
mander in chief of the Libyan Arab Armed Forces met here 
tonight with Ethiopian President Mengistu Haile Mariam. They 
discussed the cooperative relations between both friendly coun-

The secretary general of the National Democratic Party in 
brotherly Morocco indicated that the unity of the Arab Maghreb 
countries is the foundation stone of total Arab unity. He added 
that the Jamahiriyah and Moroccan Kingdom are the wings of 
the Arab Maghreb, and the great Arab Maghreb cannot take off 
without its wings. This fact in itself imposes upon the Maghreb 
to unite and join efforts with all honesty and loyalty in order to 
become the drive force for the rest of the Arab nation 


The Moroccan minister, in his speech to the General People’s 
Congress, referred to the brotherly relations between the Jama- 
hiriyah and Morocco, especially after the historic meeting last 
summer between the leader of the revolution and King Hassan 
Il. He added that this meeting established the basis for develop- 
ing relations between the brotherly states in various fields. Both 
countries express their strong willingness and determination to 
go a step forward toward achieving the total unity of the Arab 
Maghreb and the entire Arab nation. The Moroccan minister 
noted the meeting of the General People’s Committee and 
Moroccan cabinet last January in Rabat, and talked about the 
positive outcome of the meeting which, he added, was an indica- 
tion that both countries were willing to work toward unity as 
drawn up by the leader and King Hassan II. 


He ended his speech by wishing success to the congress in its 
working session, which will be on a level with the Libyan Arab 
people’s ambitions and at a level with the hopes of our Arab 
nation and its future expectations for a radiant and prosperous 
tomorrow with the flags of freedom, democracy, dignity and 
honour flying above. 


Tunisian Official Speaks 
LD150035 Tripoli JANA in English 1743 GMT 14 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada al Ulla 11, Feb 14, JAMAHIRIAH 
NEWS AGENCY Mohammed Treiki, director of the 
research centre at the Tunisian Dostour Party, delivered a speech 
at the General People’s Congress morning session in which he 
conveyed the greetings of President Habib Bourguiba and of the 
Tunisian people to the revolution’s leader, Colonel! Mu‘ammar 
al-Qadhdhafi, and the Libyan people. 


He said: “lam very happy to be here among you and to closely 
watch the work of the General People’s Congress, which will in 
due course enable me to convey the real picture to the Tunisian 
people about the course which the Libyans have chosen to 
manage their own affairs, and consequently build their bright 
future in this part of the Arab world 


He highly admired the dynamic and serious work of the sec- 
retaries of the Basic People’s Congress at this congress and the 
honest way by which they passed on the resolutions and rec- 
ommendations of the people of the basic congress to this foreign 
and domestic affairs and building socialism [sentence as 
received]. He added that the resolutions of the People’s Con- 
gresses reflect the will of the Libyan people as well as their 
responsibilities and determination. He added that the Libyan and 
Tunisian Arab people are working together towards realizing 
their common aims for a better future and a prosperous life
