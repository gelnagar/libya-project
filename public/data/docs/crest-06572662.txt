Summary (AI): A 1999 CIA information report says a delegation including four Libyan nuclear technicians, along with Iraqi and Iranian colleagues, visited Somalia in March 1999 to assess uranium ore deposits. The document concerns Somalia and has no specific places in or near Tripoli.
• Four Libyan technicians were among the 15-member delegation that arrived in Mogadishu around 8 March 1999.
• The team visited uranium reserve areas including Ali Gelle, Buurhakaba, Bur Heybe, Bur Eyle and Bur Qalin.
• The team considered shipping ore via Merca port but rejected it due to lack of control, favouring a coastal point between Gesira and Dhanane.
• The team visited the old ENI uranium mine at Bur Acaba, about 180 km west of Mogadishu; the Rahawayn Resistance Army (allied with Ethiopia) blocked ore movement to Merca.
• The delegation reportedly offered four million US dollars for 6,000 tons of raw uranium ore.
• 1999-03-08: Delegation of 15 nuclear scientists and technicians (7 Iraqis, 4 Iranians, 4 Libyans) arrived as guests of the Somali self-declared president to assess uranium ore quality.
