LIBYA

OFFICIAL RECEIVES CONGOLESE FOREIGN MINISTER 


LD240814 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0715 GMT 24 Jun 82 

[Text] Tripoli, 24 Jun (JANA) -- Brig Gen Abu Bakr Yunis, commander in chief of the 
armed forces, has received Pierre Nze, Congolese minister of foreign affairs, who 
delivered a message from the Congolese president to the leader of the revolution.
