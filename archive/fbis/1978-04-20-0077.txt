[Text] Official talks between the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah and .the 
Republic of Guinea began this evening at the people's palace in Tripoli. The Libyan 
Arab side is led by Col Mu'ammar al-Qadhadhafi, leader cf the Great 1 September 
Revolution, while the Guinea side is led by Ahmad Sekou Toure, leader of the Guinea

brother secretaries of agriculture, industries, planning and foreign affairs. The 
Guinean delegation includes Lansana Beavogui, prime minister, speaker of the National 
Assembly, and the ministers of social affairs, justice, information, planning, coopera- 
tion and banking. 


WPC CHAIRMAN CHANDRA CONFERS WITH LIBYAN OFFICIALS 


LD150142Y Tripoli Voice of the Arab Homeland in Arabic 2315 GMT 14 Apr 78 LD 


[Text] A press statement was released yesterday evening at the end of the talks which 
took place in the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah between the WPC, headed 

by Romesh Chandra, chairman, and the Permanent Secretariat of the Arab people's conference, 
headed by brother 'Umar al-Hamidi, secretary general of the Secretariat. The two sides 
exchanged views on issues and matters of interest to the nationalist, progressive and 
peace-loving forces in the world, particularly the situations in the Arab area and Africa. 


The WPC members acquainted themselves with the activity of the Permanent Secretariat 

and the conferences, which stem from the Arab people's conference, and the energetic 
efforts of the Permanent Secretariat to implement its resolutions, especially the 
establishment of a progressive Arab front, laying down the necessary plans to confront 
the imperialist-Zionist-reactionary conspiracy, and mobilizing the forces of Arab public 
opinion to abort those plans and reply to all attempts aimed against the struggle of the 
Arab nation. ; 


WPC Chairman Chandra affirmed the WPC's support for these efforts, which are aimed at 
strengthening peace based on justice and which oppose the aggressive plans against the 
Arab area. Mr Chandra gave some information on WPC activity in regard to matters concern- 
ing the Arab-Zionist conflict. He affirmed the WPC's complete and continuing support for 
efforts to permit the Palestinian Arab people to recover their national rights and uphold 
their right to self-determination and the WPC's support for all efforts aimed at liberating 
the occupied Arab territory. He condemned the expansionist policy of the Zionist enemy, 
affirming in the name of the WPC the need for immediate and uncondit‘onal withdrawal of 
the Zionist forces from southern Lebanon. Romesh Chandra once again affirmed that the 
WPC has always supported efforts to unite all Arab forces struggling against imperialism, 
Zionism and reaction. 


The two sides agreed on the need to continue contacts between the two sides to draw up 
a program for joint cooperation for the benefit of mankind. 


Brother Ahmad ash-Shahhati, the secretary general of the Progressive Socialist Organization 
in the Mediterranean, also met with Romesh Chandra. Questions of cooperation between the 
organization and the WPC were discussed.
