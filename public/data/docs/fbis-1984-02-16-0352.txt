General People’s Congress Meeting Continues 


Al-Qadhdhafi: No Female Military Training 
LD151343 Tripoli Domestic Service in Arabic 0915 GMT 
15 Feb 84 


[Speech by Colonel Mu‘ammar al-Qadhdhafi at morning session 
of General People’s Congress meeting in Tripoli — live} 


[Excerpt] A number of draft laws submitted by the leaderships, 
that is, the secretariat of the General People’s Congress and the 
General People’s Committee, have been defeated at the basic 
popular congresses. 


There are differences; it is feared there are differences on the 
draft law for an armed people. The draft law for an armed people 
has suffered a heavy defeat, and it cannot be issued in this form 
because the people have rejected the setting up of an armed 
people at the [local or basic] popular congresses and preferred the 
statu quo, that is, conscription, mandatory military service. 


This law cannot then be issued; no armed people but armed men, 
and I hope we will find them. This requires an end to the program 
of military training in all the girls schools and girls institutes. 
Therefore, starting the first of next month, | will issue an order 
to the Armed Forces to stop the general military training in girls 
schools and girls institutes and make this effort available for the 
training of boys. Women will be ineligible [for military service] 
because, since you have said to us that military service must be 
optional, there there is no reason for training them. 


Support For Lebanon 
LD151457 Tripoli JANA in Arabic 0700 GMT 14 Feb 84 


[Text} Tripoli, 14 Feb(JANA)— Yesterday evening the General 
People’s Congress sent the following cable to the fighters against 
the 10th crusade in Arab Lebanon. The cable was read at the end 
of yesterday's evening session: 


NORTH AFRICA Q! 


We, the members of the Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jama- 
hiriyah General People’s Congress, salute your heroic 
steadfastness against the invading crusaders and Zionists. By 
your steadfastness, you have proved to the world that this nation’s 
determination to fight will not waver however ferocious the 
imperialist offensive against our nation becomes. By your heroic 
struggle, you have restored honor to our nation just as Saladin 
did when he expelled the invading crusaders from the land of 
Arab Palestine after 70 years of occupation. 


As we salute your bold spirit of sacrifice, which is the rock on 
which the arrogance of American imperialism and its allies will 
founder, we wish to affirm to you and to nobody besides your 
brothers in the Jamahiriyah stand by you with all their force, just 
as they have always done, loyal to the chaste blood of Libyans 
that flowed on the soil of Arab Lebanon throughout your heroic 
struggle against the fascists, the Zionist invasion, the leader of 
world terrorism, and its oppressive fleets. 


We will spare no effort in supporting you with men and arms until 
the invaders are routed and their banners are smeared in the mud 
and they leave Arab Lebanon in disgrace and shame chased by 
the curses of all free men in this world. 


Glory to our martyrs and defeat to the aggressors! Forward, and 
the struggle will continue! 


[Signed] The General People’s Congress, Socialist People’s Lib- 
yan Arab Jamahiriyah 


Stand on Lebanon Incidents 
LD151533 Tripoli JANA in English 0852 GMT 15 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada al-Ulla 12, Feb 15, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The General People’s Congresses in the 
Socialist People’s Libyan Arab Jamahiriyah yesterday evening 
reviewed what was decided by the masses of the basic people’s 
congresses with regard to the issue of Lebanon incidents and the 
statement of Libyan Arab People’s stand on this issue. The 
decision taken by the congresses’ masses reads as follows: 


1) The people’s congresses salute the efforts taken by the leader 
of the revolution and bless the steps and efforts made by the 
Jamahiriyah with regard to the Lebanese problem and the Arab 
and the Islamic issues. 


2) Support and aid to the Lebanese national movement and to the 
free Muslim power in Lebanon with all means of support which 
would enable them to repulse colonialist countries. 


3) To morally and materially support Syria and to support the 
new revolutionary movement in Fatah so as to repulse the Zionist 
invasion of the Arab nation. 


4) Affirmation of the decisions taken by the people’s congresses 
as regard action to achieve unity, unification of Palestinian 
factions and factions of Lebanese resistance as well as calling on 
the Arabs to mobilise all their military and economic resources 
against the Israeli and U.S. enemy.

5) No recognition of the Lebanese regime and no support for it 
and rejection of the capitulatory document concluded between 
the Lebanese regime and the Zionist enemy. 


6) Condemnation of those rulers who fell short of backing Leba- 
non and instigating the revolutionary forces in the Arab world as 
means to overthrow the symbols of treason and [word indistinct] 
and transfer the battle to the regimes [words indistinct] and to 
confront the U.S. enemy. 


Sanctions Against Zaire, Liberia 
LD160024 Tripoli JANA in English 1910 GMT 15 Feb 84 


[Text] Tripoli, Jumada al Ulla 12, Feb 15, JAMAHIRIYAH 
NEWS AGENCY — The General People’s Congress resumed 
its deliberations this morning to formulate the resolutions of the 
basic people’s congresses in their 3rd regular session of 1984. In 
the morning session the congress listened to the decisions of the 
basic people’s congresses regarding the Jamahiriyah’s stands 
towards the African countries which restored relations with the 
Zionist enemy and those considering it. The decisions taken by 
the congresses are as follows: 


1. To freeze reiations with Zaire and Liberia. 


2. To work through the O.A.U. and exert pressure on those two 
countries so as to reconsider their attitude towards the Zionist 
enemy. 


3. To coordinate with Arab countries and draw a plan for Arab 
effective action in Africa in order to convince the African states 
not to restore relations with the Zionist enemy and explain the 
racist nature of the Zionist enemy and South Africa. 


4. To make political moves through international institutions and 
the O.A.U. to bring back the Jamahiriyah’s investments in the 
holding companies in various countries. 


5. The congresses affirm their previous decision by renewing 
support and backing for the revolutionary liberation movements 
in Africa.
