AL-QADHDHAFI REITERATES 11 JUNE DEADLINE 


LD091301 Rome L'UNITA in Italian 7 Jun 80 p 17 


(Unattributed report: "Al-Qadhdhafi Threatens Expatriates! 'Liquidation!"] 


[Text] Rome--Libyan leader Al-Qadhdhafi has confirmed in an interview with a Milan 
weekly the 11 June deadline as the "last chance" for "dissident" Libyans living abroad to 
return home and the threat of their "elimination" if they do not. As is known, several 
Libyan expatriates have already been assassinated in Rome, London, Bonn, Athens and 
Beirut. 


Ignoring the protests prompted by these assassinations, Al-Qadhdhafi said that after 

11 June he "will be unable to guarantee anyone's physical immunity." In an interview 
conducted in a tent in the Libyan desert, Al-Qadhdhafi said outright that whoever ignores 
the ultimatum "will be eliminated." "The revolutionary committees," the Libyan leader 
added, "will continue with their plans. They will seek them all out and liquidate then." 
Al-Qadhdhafi also confirmed that Libya intends to ask Italy, the FRG and Britain again 
for "compensation for war damage."

HASSAN SUPPORTS IDEA OF EUROPEAN ROLE IN MIDEAST PEACE 


LD061631 Casablanca LE MATIN in French 31 May 80 pp 1, 3, 4 


[Apparent text ofan interview given by King Hassan of Morocco to representatives of Radio

[Excerpts] The Near East Problem 


Question (Jean-Claude Paris, French Television Channel III): Sir, last week Saudi Crown 
Prince Fahd stated in a NEWSWEEK interview that his country was prepared to sign a certain 
kind of peace agreement in the Near East. Did Prince Fahd, whom you received this week, 
explain to you how Saudi Arabia could insure peace in the Near East? What role could 
Morocco, Europe and France play in this respect? Furthermore, is the idea of Near East 
peace without guarantees by the superpowers--the United States and the USSR--realistic? 


Answer: I can assure you that you are really unlucky, because if you were to put that 
question to me tomorrow I would be able to answer it; since Prince Fahd is to stay here 
for a few days! rest we have, as it were, cut our agenda for discussions into several 
parts. I am certainly meeting him tonight for discussions concerning this question, and 
I am extremely sorry that we have all missed such an opportunity and this is no ruse, 


Question (Jean-Claude Paris): A great deal has been said recently--at least since efforts 
failed to implement the Camp David agreements in full by 26 May--about a European proposal, 
In your view, what should be the aim of such a proposal? 


Answer: First, to go back to the first point, without disclosing Crown Prince Fahd's 
innermost thoughts of which I know nothing, any peace agreement must, in any case, be 
based on the following three points: withdrawal, the Palestinian people!s rights and the 
Jerusalem problem. This is a constant. The solution which Price Fahd is considering 

but which I have not yet studied with him can be applied only within the framework of this 
three-part plan, In any case, it is dependent on this plan, 


In my opinion, Europe has a role to play because of the three blocs it is the least 
committed. The Western bloc--the American bloc--is committed to the Israeli cause, and 
the Soviet and socialist bloc seems to be more committed to the Arab cause, or anyway to 
that of the Arab hardliners, There remains Europe because of the very fact that it is 
old Europe, the crossroads of civilizations, ofmigrations and of human knowledge. Well, 
there remains this Europe which could maybe create a platform--I would not say neutral 
but, let us say, less biased. Perhaps Europe will manage to reconcile things which have 
hitherto seemed irreconcilable, mamely, recognition of the Palestinian people's rights, a 
Palestinian entity and recognition of the state of Israel by that Palestinian entity 
itself. 


When? How? I cannot tell you, but I believe that Europe has such a role to play, and I 
believe that it must start playing it as soon as possible in its own interest.

For if we glance at a map of the world we will see that most of the fuses are geographi- 
cally concentrated in this region--between the Mediterranean and the Gulf. And it is 
certain that the Middle East grenade--the Arab-Israeli conflict--is more likely to explode 
than any of the grenades on which we are sitting. I believe that Europe has a role to 
play. It must start playing it as soon as possible, 


Question (Jean-Claude Paris): Do you believe that the United States and the Soviet Union 

would let Europe play such a role? Will the two superpowers--I am especially thinking of 

the United States, where an election is impending--not try to prevent it from playing such 
a role? Would it not be a setback for President Carter if Europe managed to do something 

which he himself has failed to do? 


Answer: I am not asking Europe to become both the promoter of and the sole godfather to any 
general peace conference, However, it could initiate such a meeting, give help and tell the 
two big blocs: I have done my work, now let us meet, the three of us, either in Geneva or 
elsewhere, to conclude it. I have never said that Europe should work on its own from 

start to finish, 


What Islam Really Is 


Question (Rene Collinet from Paris-Normandie): Your Majesty, what do you think about what 

has been called more or less everywhere the "Islamic revival", especially insofar as it has 
taken place through the intermediary of Ayatollah Khomeyni in Shi'ite Iran? (LE MATIN note: 
A question asked at the very moment when a muezzin started calling the faithful to prayer), 


Answer: What is rather strange is that everybody more or less follows in Montesquieu's 
footsteps, askingt "Are you a Persian?" It as only in 1979-1980 that people said: "Ah, 
there is Islam and there are Muslims," This was too good to be true since, in the final 
analysis, it was not Islam's awakening. It was not a question of increased interest in 
Islam on the part of other religions. It was merely: "Look, gentlemen, what Islam is 
like," And instead of making observers look at some other country, another society and 
other mores, a country called Iran has been brought to their attention, 


A country which has set up Islamic courts, as if Islam provided for Islamic courts which pass 
sentence, execute and personify organized disorder, when Muslim society was the first 
organized society in both religious and secular spheres. In any case, this becomes obvious 
when you read the Prophet's life and also and especially if you realize the speed with which 
Islam spread to neighboring countries. This was due to the fact that Islam's conquests were 
not dependent on faith only, Islam also brought law, legislations and a "way of life." 

And some people clearly want to see Islam awake’on the basis of satanic rather than Muslim 
practices. 


Attitude Toward Afghanistan Unchanged 


Question (Pierre De Voluy, Radio Monte Carlo): Your Majesty, now that 5 months have 
elapsed since the Soviet intervention in Afghanistan and some days since the Islamabad 
conference, what in your opinion has been the Muslim World's reaction to the former 
event, and what attitude has the Muslim world adopted to it?

Answer: It may well be that some difference has been noticed between the working of 

the recent Islamabad resolutions and the wording of the resolutions adopted at the 
prededing conference, However, what I can tell you is that the Muslim attitude to 
Afghanistan's occupation has remained unchanged. Meanwhile, of course, the landing of 
an American commando in Iran has been used by the Iranians as a pretext for saying: "We 
also want to condemn American because noMuslim country should be attacked in this way." 
Some other factors have also affected resolutions and recommendations. But be that as it 
may, when you look at the Afghan problem really closely, you can see that nothing has 
changed in either structure of texture as far as the Muslim countries! attitude to the 
occupation of Aftghanistan by force is concerned. 


Moroccan Proposal Adopted 


Meanwhile many things have occurred, We have been especially pleased that a Moroccan 
proposal has been adopted. We submitted it as far back as the first Islamabad conference, 
We suggested that, in any case, no verbal condemnation would liberate Afghanistan. At 
that time, we proposed that a commission be appointed to go to the Kremlin and try to 
engage in dialogue, taking the prestige of all concerned into account. Since wounds 
were still fresh at the time of the first conference, our proposal failed to get overall 
support, However, we are pleased that it has received such support now, and we feel sure 
that a dialogue with the Soviet Union will first of all make a difference as far as our 
attitude to a Muslim country and to the USSR is concerned. But the two issues should not 
be confused. This should be explained to the USSR, I believe that it is always easier 
to convince anyone through dialogue than through a monologue, All this means that the 
attitude of Muslim countries has remained unchanged, Whenever any Muslim country is 
invaded, the Muslim family must join forces and defend it.

Nothing Is Easier in the Sahara Than To Drive a Stake in the Ground and Proclaim It 
Liberated Territory 


Answer: By their absence perhaps, but not because of any ban. But you know, nothing 
could be easier in the Sahara than to drive a stake into the ground. I could take you 
with me tomorrow and tell you: "This is such and such a Moroccan border village which 

is in Algeria. You see, here I am." Nothing could be simpler, and nothing looks as

Question (Jacques Paoli, Radio Monte Carlo): Your Majesty, you have told us that you 
are confident with respect to the development of the situation relating to the West Sahara 
problem, but in your view is Morocco still in a state of war? 


The USSR Has Entered Into War Against Us 


Answer: Yes, we are at war because we have been attacked. We are in a state of war and 
not guerrilla warfare. The weapons which are used against us exceed the framework of 
guerrilla warfare and, in any case, this has not prevented me from saying in one of my 
statements to the press that, even though the USSR has not declared war on us formally, 
it is at war with us. When I met the Soviet ambassador I enlarged on this statement by 


saying: "Usually, when a country sells arms to another country it is always with the 
following proviso: 'We hope and genuinely expect that these arms will not be used against 
countries which have traditionally been our friends and allies and we are selling them to 
you on this condition....'" Such a clause, I told the ambassador, must certainly have 
been signed by you and Al-Qadhdhafi whenever you sold him arms. He has not abided by it, 
and you have done nothing to make him abide by it. In my view, therefore, you are in- 
directly at war with us.

Answer: You know, nobody can say that he is safe from something. As individuals we are 
attacked daily. The ecologists will tell you that. Whether it be on an individual basis 
or in a country or society, the problem is not to be safe. The problem is to have enough 
antibodies to resist these attacks. 


Every day the world is attacked by the transistor radio which is much more harmful on 
the ideological plane than a MIRVed nuclear missile whatever argument it puts forward. 
In my opinion Morocco will be safe from any destabilization as long as it decides to re- 
main Morocco, knowing that it must evolve and espouse the philosophy or I would not say 
every century--unfortunately history is counted no longer in centuries but in decades-- 
each decade, but in its way with its own gentus. If it does and continues to do this, I 
do not think that this country can -ver be destabilized.

That will not make it safe. It must not be kept too aseptic because it would fall to the 
first virus. However, it will not be brought to its knees. I say this sincerely and 
proudly as a Moroccan, leaving aside my position. 


Question (Robert Bonifay): What is the first virus called? 
It Takes a Great Deal of Courage To Accept the Virus of Freedom 


Answer: The first virus is freedom: From the moment you accept freedom you have to accept 
all its implications. This was true from the moment that there were no exit or entry visas 
in my country. From the moment doors were open to all audiovisual communications. From 
the moment when I did not restrict all radio sets to one network and one wavelength. From 
the moment when I allowed my workers to go to the Netherlands and see the squatters riot- 
ing in the city on the day of the queen's coronation and allowed them to vote and return. 


Therefore, I accept the first virus. However, at the same time my instinct, my history 
and my authenticity give me all the antibodies with which to fight it. 


I can assure you that it takes a great deal of courage to accept the first virus of free- 
dom. However, if you want to be a great country you can only be one in freedom.

cally very important if we succeed in building it. 


North-South Dialogue Will Always Remain Distorted 
North-South dialogue will, in my opinion, remain imbalanced however much goodwill the 


interlocutors have, because the problem does not consist in submitting files or sitting
